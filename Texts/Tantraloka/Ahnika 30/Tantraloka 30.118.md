## Mūla

रुद्रशक्तीति वेदार्णं स्याद्रुद्रदयितेऽथ मे ।
पापं दहदहेत्येषा द्वादशार्णा चतुष्पदी ॥ ^mula

rudraśaktīti vedārṇaṃ syādrudradayite'tha me |
pāpaṃ dahadahetyeṣā dvādaśārṇā catuṣpadī || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 03 - Chapter Thirty, part 2#^pg80|p. 62]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=80>))
>
> (The third Vidyā begins with) the syllable of Māyā (HRĪM), which is followed by three words consisting of eight syllables (altogether), namely, PARE BRAHME CATURVIDYE, and one of five syllables, that is, YOGADHĀRINI. (This is followed by) three words - ĀAN, ANTARĀAN, PARAMĀAN - in the vocative and terminating with the letter E, said to consist of ten syllables. (Then comes) RUDRAŚAKTI, which consists of the four syllables, (followed by another) four words consisting of twelve syllables (altogether), namely, RUDRADAYITE ME PĀPAM DAHA DAHA. (Then comes) the pair SAUMYE SADĀŚIVE, consisting of a group of six (syllables). (They are followed by) four syllables and a half, which are the Point (HŪM), the Arrow (PHAT) and SĀVAHĀ. (This) Vidyā, numbering fifty and a half syllables, 1 is that of the Supreme Goddess (Parameśvarī), that removes (the sins of transgressing) the Rule. (with 30.116–120ab)
