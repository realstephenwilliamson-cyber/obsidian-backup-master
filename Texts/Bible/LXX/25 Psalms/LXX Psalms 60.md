# LXX Psalms 60

← [[LXX Psalms 59]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 61]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 061]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 061]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**60:1** For the end, among the Hymns of David.  ^60-1

**60:2** O God, hearken to my petition; attend to my prayer.  ^60-2

**60:3** From the ends of the earth have I cried to thee, when my heart was in trouble: thou liftedst me up on a rock, thou didst guide me:  ^60-3

**60:4** because thou wert my hope, a tower of strength from the face of the enemy.  ^60-4

**60:5** I will dwell in thy tabernacle for ever; I will shelter myself under the shadow of thy wings. Pause.  ^60-5

**60:6** For thou, O God, hast heard my prayers; thou hast given an inheritance to them that fear thy name.  ^60-6

**60:7** Thou shalt add days to the days of the king; *thou shalt lengthen* his years to all generations.  ^60-7

**60:8** He shall endure for ever before God: which of them will seek out his mercy and truth?  ^60-8

**60:9** So will I sing to thy name for ever and ever, that I may daily perform my vows.  ^60-9
