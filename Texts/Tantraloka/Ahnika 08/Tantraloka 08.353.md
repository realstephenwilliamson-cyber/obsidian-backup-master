## Mūla

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 10 - Chapter Eight, part 8#^pg361|p. 343]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=361>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 8.353cd–354ab.
>
> (Fettered) phenomenal existence (bhava) is said to extend from Māyā down to (the hell) Avīci, while the absence of phenomenal existence (abhava) is said to extend from Ananta up to the end of the authority (adhikāra) over the pure qualities of iva (in the pure principles up to transcendental Śiva). This should also be eliminated. (with 8.352cd–353ab)
>
> For even here, Śiva commissions (beings to aid) those he has seen fit to grace; (but although that is good), it is preferable for even such (work) to come to an end. The absence of phenomenal existence (does not therefore imply liberation, it is called this) because it does not exist at the lower level. (with 8.353cd–354ab)
