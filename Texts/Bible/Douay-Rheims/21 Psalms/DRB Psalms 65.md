# Psalms 65 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 64|← Psalms 64]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 66|Psalms 66 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 65|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**65:1** Unto the end, a canticle of a psalm of the resurrection. Shout with joy to God, all the earth, ^65-1

**65:2** Sing ye a psalm to his name; give glory to his praise. ^65-2

**65:3** Say unto God, How terrible are thy works, O Lord! in the multitude of thy strength thy enemies shall lie to thee. ^65-3

**65:4** Let all the earth adore thee, and sing to thee: let it sing a psalm to thy name. ^65-4

**65:5** Come and see the works of God; who is terrible in his counsels over the sons of men. ^65-5

**65:6** Who turneth the sea into dry land, in the river they shall pass on foot: there shall we rejoice in him. ^65-6

**65:7** Who by his power ruleth for ever: his eyes behold the nations; let not them that provoke him he exalted in themselves. ^65-7

**65:8** O bless our God, ye Gentiles: and make the voice of his praise to be heard. ^65-8

**65:9** Who hath set my soul to live: and hath not suffered my feet to be moved: ^65-9

**65:10** For thou, O God, hast proved us: thou hast tried us by fire, as silver is tried. ^65-10

**65:11** Thou hast brought us into a net, thou hast laid afflictions on our back: ^65-11

**65:12** Thou hast set men over our heads. We have passed through tire and water, and thou hast brought us out into a refreshment. ^65-12

**65:13** I will go into thy house with burnt offerings: I will pay thee my vows, ^65-13

**65:14** Which my lips have uttered, And my mouth hath spoken, when I was in trouble. ^65-14

**65:15** I will offer up to thee holocausts full of marrow, with burnt offerings of rams: I will offer to thee bullocks with goats. ^65-15

**65:16** Come and hear, all ye that fear God, and I will tell you what great things he hath done for my soul. ^65-16

**65:17** I cried to him with my mouth: and I extolled him with my tongue. ^65-17

**65:18** If I have looked at iniquity in my heart, the Lord will not hear me. ^65-18

**65:19** Therefore hath God heard me, and hath attended to the voice of my supplication. ^65-19

**65:20** Blessed be God, who hath not turned away my prayer, nor his mercy from me. ^65-20
