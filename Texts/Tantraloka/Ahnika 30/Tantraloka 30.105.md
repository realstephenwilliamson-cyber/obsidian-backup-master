## Mūla

गुरुणा तत्त्वविदा किल शिष्यो यदि मोक्षमात्रकृतहृदयः ।
मोक्षैकदानचतुरा दीक्षा सेयं परोपनिषदुक्ता ॥ ^mula

guruṇā tattvavidā kila śiṣyo yadi mokṣamātrakṛtahṛdayaḥ |
mokṣaikadānacaturā dīkṣā seyaṃ paropaniṣaduktā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 03 - Chapter Thirty, part 2#^pg77|p. 59]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=77>))

> If the disciple has set his heart solely on liberation, this is the initiation that a teacher who (profoundly) knows reality (needs to administer) – capable by itself of dispensing liberation, it is said to be the supreme secret.
