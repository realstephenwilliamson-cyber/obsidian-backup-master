## Mūla

तस्मादस्य न कर्मास्ति कस्यापि सहकारिताम् ।
मलः करोतु तेनायं ध्वंसमानत्वमश्नुते ॥ ^mula

tasmādasya na karmāsti kasyāpi sahakāritām |
malaḥ karotu tenāyaṃ dhvaṃsamānatvamaśnute || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI

> Therefore, he neither has any Karma, nor does Impurity (continue to) serve as an (attendant) concomitant cause of anything, and so it is in the process of being destroyed.
