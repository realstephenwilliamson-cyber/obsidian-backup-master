## Mūla

ज्योतिर्ध्वनिसमीरकृतः सा मान्त्री व्याप्तिरुच्यते परमा ।
सकलाकलेशशून्यं कलाढ्यखमले तथा क्षपणकं च ॥ ^mula

jyotirdhvanisamīrakṛtaḥ sā māntrī vyāptirucyate paramā |
sakalākaleśaśūnyaṃ kalāḍhyakhamale tathā kṣapaṇakaṃ ca || ^iast

> Having heard the inarticulate (ayvakta) (sound) (that rises up) from the location of (Śakti’s) heart between the breasts and ends at the lips (1) (in her) throat, (2) in the middle between these two Wheels (during sex), (3) when (after orgasm,) arousal has subsided, (they attain tranquillity, and all the energies of Sound) come to rest. And the supreme Bhairava of Sound, eight-fold in this way, (who manifests) there made of light, resonance and wind, is said to be the supreme pervasion of the one who recites Mantra. (with 29.158cd–160ab)
>
> Kṣayāntastha (HĀḤ (?)), and Kaṇṭhyoṣṭhya (HSRKṢMVYŪṀ). Such is this pervasion from the Half-Moon to the Transmental. (with 29.160cd–161ab)

*Lead-in:* अत्र च किं तत् भैरवाष्टकं का च मान्त्री व्याप्तिः?--इत्याशङ्क्य आह--

> सकलाकलेशशून्यं कलाढ्यखमले तथा क्षपणकं च ॥
> अन्तःस्थं कण्ठ्योष्ठ्यं चन्द्राद्व्याप्तिस्तथोन्मनान्तेयम् ।

एषां च शक्तिशक्तिमत्सामरस्यवेलायामुदयात् तदन्यतरव्यपदेशायोगात् नपुंसकत्वमेव उचितमिति तल्लिङ्गेन निर्देशः ॥
