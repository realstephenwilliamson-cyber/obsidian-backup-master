## Mūla

अतिथिं सोऽनुगृह्णाति तत्कालाभिज्ञमागतम् ।
तथा सुफलसंसिद्ध्यै योगिनीसिद्धनायकाः ॥ ^mula

atithiṃ so'nugṛhṇāti tatkālābhijñamāgatam |
tathā suphalasaṃsiddhyai yoginīsiddhanāyakāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 03 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 1#^pg33|p. 16]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=33>))
>
> Just as one who has saved (upātta) money for a long time throws a party (utsava), and doing so, respectfully welcomes a guest (atithi) who knows the time (it is to take place and so) has come (although uninvited); similarly, the leaders of the Yoginīs and Siddhas, who even though they exert themselves in order achieve the good fruits (they desire), grace one who has come knowing that that is the time (for their assembly, even if he has not been invited). (with 28.23cd–25ab)
