# Jeremiah 37 (Douay-Rheims)

*Jeremias 37 in the Douay-Rheims*

[[DRB Jeremiah 36|← Jeremiah 36]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Jeremiah 38|Jeremiah 38 →]]

**37:1** Now king Sedecias the son of Josias reigned instead of Jechonias the son of Joakim: whom Nabuchodonosor king of Babylon made king in the land of Juda. ^37-1

**37:2** But neither he, nor his servants, nor the people of the land did obey the words of the Lord, that he spoke in the hand of Jeremias the prophet. ^37-2

**37:3** And king Sedecias sent Juchal the son of Selemias, and Sophonias the son of Maasias the priest to Jeremias the prophet, saying: Pray to the Lord our God for us. ^37-3

**37:4** Now Jeremias walked freely in the midst of the people: for they had not as yet cast him into prison. And the army of Pharao was come out of Egypt: and the Chaldeans that besieged Jerusalem, hearing these tidings, departed from Jerusalem. ^37-4

**37:5** And the word of the Lord came to Jeremias the prophet, saying: ^37-5

**37:6** Thus saith the Lord the God of Israel: Thus shall you say to the king of Juda, who sent you to inquire of me: Behold the army of Pharao, which is come forth to help you, shall return into their own land, into Egypt. ^37-6

**37:7** And the Chaldeans shall come again, and fight against this city, and take it, and burn it with fire. ^37-7

**37:8** Thus saith the Lord: Deceive not your souls, saying: The Chaldeans shall surely depart and go away from us: for they shall not go away; ^37-8

**37:9** But if you should even beat all the army of the Chaldeans that fight against you, and there should be left of them some wounded men: they shall rise up, every man from his tent, and burn this city with Are. ^37-9

**37:10** Now when the army of the Chaldeans was gone away from Jerusalem, because of Pharao’s army, ^37-10

**37:11** Jeremias went forth out of Jerusalem to go into the land of Benjamin: and to divide a possession there in the presence of the citizens. ^37-11

**37:12** And when he was come to the gate of Benjamin, the captain of the gate, who I was there in his turn, was one named Jerias, the son of Selemias, the son of Hananias: and he took hold of Jeremias the prophet, saying: Thou art fleeing to the Chaldeans. ^37-12

**37:13** And Jeremias answered: It is not so, I am not fleeing to the Chaldeans. But he hearkened not to him: so Jerias took Jeremias and brought him to the princes. ^37-13

**37:14** Wherefore the princes were angry with Jeremias, and they beat him, and cast him into the prison that was in the house of Jonathan the scribe: for he was chief over the prison. ^37-14

**37:15** So Jeremias went into the house of the prison, and into the dungeon: and Jeremias remained there many days. ^37-15

**37:16** Then Sedecias the king, sending, took him: and asked him secretly in his house, and said: Is there, thinkest thou, any word from the Lord? And Jeremias said: There is. And he said: Thou shalt be delivered into the hands of the king of Babylon. ^37-16

**37:17** And Jeremias said to king Sedecias: In what have I offended against thee, or thy servants, or thy people, that thou hast cast me into prison? ^37-17

**37:18** Where are your prophets that prophesied to you, and said: The king of Babylon shall not come against you, and against this land? ^37-18

**37:19** Now therefore hear, I beseech thee, my lord the king: let my petition be accepted in thy sight: and send me not back into the house of Jonathan the scribe, lest I die there. ^37-19

**37:20** Then king Sedecias commanded that Jeremias should be committed into the entry of the prison: and that they should give him daily a piece of bread, beside broth, till all the bread in the city were spent: and Jeremias remained in the entry of the prison. ^37-20
