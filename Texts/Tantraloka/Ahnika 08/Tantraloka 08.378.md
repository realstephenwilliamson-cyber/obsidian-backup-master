## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 10 - Chapter Eight, part 8#^pg373|p. 355]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=373>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 8.378cd–379ab.
>
> Śiva Beyond Tranquillity (śāntyāfīta) Above is the Enclosure of the Point (bindu), which is brilliantly luminous. There is a lotus there which shines like the moon. Śiva Beyond Tranquillity (śāntyāfīta) is there. He is adorned by this power, whom he embraces, surrounded by the entourage of the Forces (kalā), that is, (Nivṛtti) Cessation and the rest. He is encircled by innumerable Rudras and their powers, and tens of millions of worlds. (with 8.377–378)

## Viveka

> [!viveka]- Viveka in English (Dyczkowski, vol. V, pp. 355–356)
> ![[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 10 - Chapter Eight, part 8#Viveka on 8.377–378]]

> ऊर्ध्वे बिन्द्वावृतिर्दीप्ता तत्र तत्र पद्मं शशिप्रभम् ।
> शान्त्यतीतः शिवस्तत्र तच्छक्त्युत्सङ्गभूषितः ॥
> निवृत्त्यादिकलावर्गपरिवारसमावृतः ।
> असंख्यरुद्रतच्छक्तिपुरकोटिभिरावृतः ॥

ऊर्ध्वे इति, सुशिवावरणात् । "तच्छक्ति"इति शान्त्यतीता । तदुक्तम् --

ङिवृत्तिश्च प्रतिष्ठा च विद्या शान्तिस्तथैव च ।

परिवारः स्मृतस्तस्य शान्त्यतीतस्य सुव्रते ॥

तस्य वामे तु दिग्भागे शान्त्यतीता व्यवस्थिता ॥"(स्व. १० ।१६२१)
