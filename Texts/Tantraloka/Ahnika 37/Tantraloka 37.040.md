## Mūla

स्थाने स्थाने मुनिभिरखिलैश्चक्रिरे यन्निवासा
यच्चाध्यास्ते प्रतिपदमिदं स स्वयं चन्द्रचूडः ।
तन्मन्येऽहं समभिलषिताशेषसिद्धेर्न सिद्ध्यै
कश्मीरेभ्यः परमथ पुरं पूर्णवृत्तेर्न तुष्ट्यै ॥ ^mula

sthāne sthāne munibhirakhilaiścakrire yannivāsā
yaccādhyāste pratipadamidaṃ sa svayaṃ candracūḍaḥ |
tanmanye'haṃ samabhilaṣitāśeṣasiddherna siddhyai
kaśmīrebhyaḥ paramatha puraṃ pūrṇavṛtterna tuṣṭyai || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 11 - Chapter Thirty-Seven, part 1#^pg261|p. 243]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=261>))
>
> (There,) in every place, all (the great) sages made their dwellings. (Indeed,) Śiva Himself presides over every step (within it). I believe there is no country greater than Kashmir to achieve all the accomplishments (one may) desire, and satisfy one whose condition is full (and perfect).
