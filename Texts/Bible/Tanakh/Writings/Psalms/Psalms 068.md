## Chapter 68

**68:1** [^1]For the leader. Of David. A psalm. A song. ^68-1

**68:2** God will arise,  
His enemies shall be scattered,  
His foes shall flee before Him. ^68-2

**68:3** Disperse them as smoke is dispersed;  
as wax melts at fire,  
so the wicked shall perish before God. ^68-3

**68:4** But the righteous shall rejoice;  
they shall exult in the presence of God;  
they shall be exceedingly joyful. ^68-4

**68:5** Sing to God, chant hymns to His name;  
extol Him who rides the clouds;  
the LORD is His name.  
Exult in His presence— ^68-5

**68:6** the father of orphans, the champion of widows,  
God, in His holy habitation. ^68-6

**68:7** God restores the lonely to their homes,  
sets free the imprisoned, safe and sound,  
while the rebellious must live in a parched land. ^68-7

**68:8** O God, when You went at the head of Your army,  
when You marched through the desert, *selah* ^68-8

**68:9** the earth trembled, the sky rained because of God,  
yon Sinai, because of God, the God of Israel. ^68-9

**68:10** You released a bountiful rain, O God;  
when Your own land languished, You sustained it. ^68-10

**68:11** Your tribe dwells there;  
O God, in Your goodness You provide for the needy. ^68-11

**68:12** The Lord gives a command;  
the women who bring the news are a great host: ^68-12

**68:13** “The kings and their armies are in headlong flight;  
housewives are sharing in the spoils; ^68-13

**68:14** even for those of you who lie among the sheepfolds  
there are wings of a dove sheathed in silver,  
its pinions in fine gold.” ^68-14

**68:15** When Shaddai scattered the kings,  
it seemed like a snowstorm in Zalmon. ^68-15

**68:16** O majestic mountain, Mount Bashan;  
O jagged mountain, Mount Bashan; ^68-16

**68:17** why so hostile, O jagged mountains,  
toward the mountain God desired as His dwelling?  
The LORD shall abide there forever. ^68-17

**68:18** God’s chariots are myriads upon myriads,  
thousands upon thousands;  
the Lord is among them as in Sinai in holiness. ^68-18

**68:19** You went up to the heights, having taken captives,  
having received tribute of men,  
even of those who rebel  
against the LORD God’s abiding there. ^68-19

**68:20** Blessed is the Lord.  
Day by day He supports us,  
God, our deliverance. *Selah*. ^68-20

**68:21** God is for us a God of deliverance;  
GOD the Lord provides an escape from death. ^68-21

**68:22** God will smash the heads of His enemies,  
the hairy crown of him who walks about in his guilt. ^68-22

**68:23** The Lord said, “I will retrieve from Bashan,  
I will retrieve from the depths of the sea; ^68-23

**68:24** that your feet may wade through blood;  
that the tongue of your dogs may have its portion of your enemies.” ^68-24

**68:25** Men see Your processions, O God,  
the processions of my God, my king,  
into the sanctuary. ^68-25

**68:26** First come singers, then musicians,  
amidst maidens playing timbrels. ^68-26

**68:27** In assemblies bless God,  
the LORD, O you who are from the fountain of Israel. ^68-27

**68:28** There is little Benjamin who rules them,  
the princes of Judah who command them,  
the princes of Zebulun and Naphtali. ^68-28

**68:29** Your God has ordained strength for you,  
the strength, O God,  
which You displayed for us ^68-29

**68:30** from Your temple above Jerusalem.  
The kings bring You tribute. ^68-30

**68:31** Blast the beast of the marsh,  
the herd of bulls among the peoples, the calves,  
till they come cringing with pieces of silver.  
Scatter the peoples who delight in wars! ^68-31

**68:32** Tribute-bearers shall come from Egypt;  
Cush shall hasten its gifts to God. ^68-32

**68:33** O kingdoms of the earth,  
sing to God;  
chant hymns to the Lord, *selah* ^68-33

**68:34** to Him who rides the ancient highest heavens,  
who thunders forth with His mighty voice. ^68-34

**68:35** Ascribe might to God,  
whose majesty is over Israel,  
whose might is in the skies. ^68-35

**68:36** You are awesome, O God, in Your holy places;  
it is the God of Israel who gives might and power to the people.  
Blessed is God. ^68-36

[^1]: The coherence of this psalm and the meaning of many of its passages are uncertain.
