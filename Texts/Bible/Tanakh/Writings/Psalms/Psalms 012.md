## Chapter 12

**12:1** For the leader; on the *sheminith*. A psalm of David. ^12-1

**12:2** Help, O LORD!  
For the faithful are no more;  
the loyal have vanished from among men. ^12-2

**12:3** Men speak lies to one another;  
their speech is smooth;  
they talk with duplicity. ^12-3

**12:4** May the LORD cut off all flattering lips,  
every tongue that speaks arrogance. ^12-4

**12:5** They say, “By our tongues we shall prevail;  
with lips such as ours, who can be our master?” ^12-5

**12:6** “Because of the groans of the plundered poor and needy,  
I will now act,” says the LORD.  
[^1]“I will give help,” He affirms to him.[^2] ^12-6

**12:7** The words of the LORD are pure words,  
silver purged in an earthen crucible,  
refined sevenfold. ^12-7

**12:8** You, O LORD, will keep them,  
guarding each from this age[^3] evermore. ^12-8

**12:9** On every side the wicked roam  
when baseness is exalted among men.[^4] ^12-9

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
