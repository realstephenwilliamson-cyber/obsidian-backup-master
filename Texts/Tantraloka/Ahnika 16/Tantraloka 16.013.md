## Mūla

वामे चापरया साकं नवात्मा दक्षगं परम् ।
त्रिशूले दक्षिणे मध्यशृङ्गस्थो रतिशेखरः ॥ ^mula

vāme cāparayā sākaṃ navātmā dakṣagaṃ param |
triśūle dakṣiṇe madhyaśṛṅgastho ratiśekharaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 05 - Chapter Sixteen, part 1#^pg25|p. 5]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=25>))
>
> Bhairavasadbhāva, together with Parā, is in middle of the middle trident. Navātmā, together with Aparā, is on the left (prong), and the other (couple) is on the right one. Ratiśekhara, along with Parāparā, is on the middle prong of the right trident. Bhairavasadbhāva, along with Parā, is on the right prong, (and Navātmā with Aparā on the left prong). Navātmā, along with Aparā, is on the middle prong of the left trident, and Sadbhairava with Parā is on the other left prong (and Ratiśekhara together with Parāparā is on the right prong). (with 16.12cd–15ab)
