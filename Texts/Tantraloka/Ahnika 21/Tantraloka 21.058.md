## Mūla

प्रायश्चित्तैस्तथा दानैः प्राणायामैश्च शोधनम् ।
कृत्वा विधिमिमां चापि दीक्षां कुर्यादशङ्कितः ॥ ^mula

prāyaścittaistathā dānaiḥ prāṇāyāmaiśca śodhanam |
kṛtvā vidhimimāṃ cāpi dīkṣāṃ kuryādaśaṅkitaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 12 - Chapter Twenty-One#^pg294|p. 274]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=294>))
>
> Or else, if the teacher (feels that he is) not properly (and fully inspired and) energized (dīpta), he may execute this procedure, and so too perform the initiation for (his disciple), free of (all) doubts once he has purified (himself) with rites of reparation (prāyaścitta), the offering of gifts (dāna) and practiced (various forms of) breath control (prānāyāma). (with 21.57cd–58)
