## Mūla

अज्ञाचार्यमुखायातं निर्वीर्यं मन्त्रमेष यत् ।
जप्तवान्स गुरुश्चात्र नाधिकार्युक्तदूषणात् ॥ ^mula

ajñācāryamukhāyātaṃ nirvīryaṃ mantrameṣa yat |
japtavānsa guruścātra nādhikāryuktadūṣaṇāt || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 13 - Chapter Twenty-Two#^pg308|p. 288]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=308>))
>
> (The reason for this) is because he has repeated a Mantra devoid of vitality, that has come from the mouth of an ignorant teacher. Moreover, (that) teacher has no authority (to teach and impart Mantras), due to the aforementioned defect. Thus, (a true teacher) may initiate (him again only after) having first purified him. (with 22.34–35ab)
