## Mūla

इतीयं सद्य उत्क्रान्तिः सूचिता मालिनीमते ।
स्वयं वा गुरुणा वाथ कार्यत्वेन महेशिना ॥ ^mula

itīyaṃ sadya utkrāntiḥ sūcitā mālinīmate |
svayaṃ vā guruṇā vātha kāryatvena maheśinā || ^iast

## Translation

>
> The Great Lord has alluded (sūcitā) in the Mālinīmata that this (initiation, which brings about) an immediate exit (from the body), can be performed (equally well) by oneself (alone), or by a teacher.
