## Mūla

यथास्वमाधरौत्तर्यविचित्रात्संस्कृतस्तथा ।
लीनः प्रबुद्धो मन्त्रत्वं तदीशत्वमथैति वा ॥ ^mula

yathāsvamādharauttaryavicitrātsaṃskṛtastathā |
līnaḥ prabuddho mantratvaṃ tadīśatvamathaiti vā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 12 - Chapter Ten, part 3#^pg398|p. 379]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=398>))
>
> Those who have been purified by the power of meditation (bhāvanā) and the like taught in the Vaiṣṇava and other schools dissolve (into their respective levels of consciousness). According to whether their own (condition) is of a higher or lower order, they attain either the state of a Mantra (perceiver) or that of a Lord of Mantra when they awake. However, those Dissolution Deconditioned (perceivers) who are devoid of freedom and have been forcefully brought under the sway of delusion wake up to a life of transmigratory existence due to the latent traces of their (past Karma).' (with 10.135cd–137)
