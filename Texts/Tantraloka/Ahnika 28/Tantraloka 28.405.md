## Mūla

भाषा न्यायो वादो लयः क्रमो यद्यदेति शिष्यस्य ।
सम्बोधोपायत्वं तथैव गुरुराश्रयेद् व्याख्याम् ॥ ^mula

bhāṣā nyāyo vādo layaḥ kramo yadyadeti śiṣyasya |
sambodhopāyatvaṃ tathaiva gururāśrayed vyākhyām || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X

> The teacher should undertake (his) explanation making use of (every) means to awaken the disciple, (such as the use of) 1) language, 2) logic, 3) discussion (vada),), 4) repetition (laya), and 5) the ordering (krama) (of the subject matter).
