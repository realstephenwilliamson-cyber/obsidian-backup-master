## Mūla

अनन्तः प्रथमस्तेषां कपालीशस्तथापरः ।
अग्निरुद्रो यमश्चैव नैर्-ऋतो बल एव च ॥ ^mula

anantaḥ prathamas teṣāṁ kapālīśas tathāparaḥ |
agnirudro yamaś caiva nairṛto bala eva ca || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, July 2023 – September 2023
> p. 71 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=76>))
>
> 1) Ananta (Endless) is the first amongst them. Kapālīśa (Lord of the Skull) comes after (him followed by) Agnirudra (Rudra of Fire), Yama, Nairṛta (Nephast), Bala (Strength), Śīghra (Speedy), Nidhīśvara (Lord of Treasures), Sarvavidyādhipa (King of All Knowledge) is another, Śambhu (Tranquil) and (finally) Vīrabhadra (Auspicious Hero) whose light is (like) a smokeless fire.[^32] When these eleven are pure, (all) hundred (Rudras) are considered to be pure.[^33] (with 5.13–15ab)

[^32]: MV 5/13-14 quoted in TĀv ad 8/181 (180cd-181ab), which is a paraphrase of it.
[^33]: MV 5/11-15ab is quoted in TĀv ad 8/438-445 (437cd-445ab).
