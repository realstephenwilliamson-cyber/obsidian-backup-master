# Job 37 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Job 36|← Job 36]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Job 38|Job 38 →]]

**37:1** “At this also my heart trembles and leaps out of its place. ^37-1

**37:2** Listen, listen to the thunder of his voice and the rumbling that comes from his mouth. ^37-2

**37:3** Under the whole heaven he lets it loose, and his lightning to the corners of the earth. ^37-3

**37:4** After it his voice roars; he thunders with his majestic voice, and he does not restrain the lightnings^[Heb *them*] when his voice is heard. ^37-4

**37:5** God thunders wondrously with his voice; he does great things that we cannot comprehend. ^37-5

**37:6** For to the snow he says, ‘Fall on the earth’; and the shower of rain, his heavy shower of rain, ^37-6

**37:7** serves as a sign on everyone’s hand, so that all whom he has made may know it.^[Meaning of Heb of 37.7 uncertain] ^37-7

**37:8** Then the animals go into their lairs and remain in their dens. ^37-8

**37:9** From its chamber comes the whirlwind and cold from the scattering winds. ^37-9

**37:10** By the breath of God ice is given, and the broad waters are frozen fast. ^37-10

**37:11** He loads the thick cloud with moisture; the clouds scatter his lightning. ^37-11

**37:12** They turn round and round by his guidance to accomplish all that he commands them on the face of the habitable world. ^37-12

**37:13** Whether for correction or for his land or for love, he causes it to happen. ^37-13

**37:14** “Hear this, O Job; stop and consider the wondrous works of God. ^37-14

**37:15** Do you know how God lays his command upon them and causes the lightning of his cloud to shine? ^37-15

**37:16** Do you know the balancings of the clouds, the wondrous works of the one whose knowledge is perfect, ^37-16

**37:17** you whose garments are hot when the earth is still because of the south wind? ^37-17

**37:18** Can you, like him, spread out the skies, hard as a molten mirror? ^37-18

**37:19** Teach us what we shall say to him; we cannot draw up our case because of darkness. ^37-19

**37:20** Should he be told that I want to speak? Did anyone ever wish to be swallowed up? ^37-20

**37:21** Now, no one can look on the light when it is bright in the skies, when the wind has passed and cleared them. ^37-21

**37:22** Out of the north comes golden splendor; around God is awesome majesty. ^37-22

**37:23** The Almighty^[Traditional rendering of Heb *Shaddai*]—we cannot find him; he is great in power and justice, and abundant righteousness he will not violate. ^37-23

**37:24** Therefore mortals fear him; he does not regard any who are wise in their own conceit.” ^37-24
