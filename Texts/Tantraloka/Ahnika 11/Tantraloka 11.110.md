## Mūla

मणाविन्द्रायुधे भास इव नीलादयः शिवे ।
परमार्थत एषां तु नोदयो न व्ययः क्वचित् ॥ ^mula

maṇāvindrāyudhe bhāsa iva nīlādayaḥ śive |
paramārthata eṣāṃ tu nodayo na vyayaḥ kvacit || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 03 - Chapter Eleven, part 2#^pg115|p. 95]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=115>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 11.110cd–111ab.
>
> (All things are) manifestations (bhāsa) within Śiva, like blue and other (colours) in the rainbow (that shines) within a gem. In reality, they neither come into being anywhere nor cease to exist.
