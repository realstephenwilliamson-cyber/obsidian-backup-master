# LXX Psalms 10

← [[LXX Psalms 09]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 11]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 011]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 011]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**10:1** For the end, a Psalm of David. In the Lord I have put my trust: how will ye say to my soul, Flee to the mountains as a sparrow?  ^10-1

**10:2** For behold the sinners have bent *their* bow, they have prepared their arrows for the quiver, to shoot privily at the upright in heart.[^1]  ^10-2

**10:3** For they have pulled down what thou didst frame, but what has the righteous done?  ^10-3

**10:4** The Lord is in his holy temple, as for the Lord, his throne is in heaven: his eyes look upon the poor, his eyelids try the sons of men.  ^10-4

**10:5** The Lord tries the righteous and the ungodly: and he that loves unrighteousness hates his own soul.  ^10-5

**10:6** He shall rain upon sinners snares, fire, and brimstone, and a stormy blast *shall be* the portion of their cup.  ^10-6

**10:7** For the Lord *is* righteous, and loves righteousness; his face beholds uprightness.[^2]  ^10-7

[^1]: 10:2 *Gr.* in dark moon, or little moonlight, *q. d.* luce malignâ.
[^2]: 10:7 *Gr.* righteousnesses.
