## Chapter 19

**19:1** And you are to intone a dirge over the princes of Israel, ^19-1

**19:2** and say:  
What a lioness was your mother  
Among the lions!  
Crouching among the great beasts,  
She reared her cubs. ^19-2

**19:3** She raised up one of her cubs,  
He became a great beast;  
He learned to hunt prey—  
He devoured men. ^19-3

**19:4** Nations heeded [the call] against him;  
He was caught in their snare.  
They dragged him off with hooks  
To the land of Egypt. ^19-4

**19:5** When she saw herself frustrated,  
Her hope defeated,  
She took another of her cubs  
And set him up as a great beast. ^19-5

**19:6** He stalked among the lions,  
He was a great beast;  
He learned to hunt prey—  
He devoured men. ^19-6

**19:7** He ravished their widows,[^1]  
Laid waste their cities;  
The land and all in it were appalled  
At the sound of his roaring. ^19-7

**19:8** Nations from the countries round about  
Arrayed themselves against him.  
They spread their net over him,  
He was caught in their snare. ^19-8

**19:9** With hooks he was put in a cage,  
They carried him off to the king of Babylon  
And confined him in a fortress,  
So that never again should his roar be heard  
On the hills of Israel. ^19-9

**19:10** Your mother was like a vine in your blood,[^2]  
Planted beside streams,  
With luxuriant boughs and branches  
Thanks to abundant waters. ^19-10

**19:11** And she had a mighty rod[^3]  
Fit for a ruler’s scepter.[^4]  
It towered highest among the leafy trees,[^5]  
It was conspicuous by its height,  
By the abundance of its boughs. ^19-11

**19:12** But plucked up in a fury,  
She was hurled to the ground.  
The east wind withered her branches,  
They broke apart and dried up;  
And her mighty rod was consumed by fire. ^19-12

**19:13** Now she is planted in the desert,  
In ground that is arid and parched. ^19-13

**19:14** Fire has issued from her twig-laden branch  
And has consumed her boughs,  
She is left without a mighty rod,  
A scepter to rule with.  
This is a dirge, and it has become a [familiar] dirge. ^19-14

[^1]: Emendation yields “ravaged their castles.”
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain; emendation yields “in a vineyard.”
[^3]: Heb. plural.
[^4]: Heb. plural.
[^5]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
