# LXX Job 37

← [[LXX Job 36]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Job 38]] →

*Job · [[LXX]]*

**37:1** At this also my heart is troubled, and moved out of its place.  ^37-1

**37:2** Hear thou a report by the anger of the Lord's wrath, and a discourse shall come out of his mouth.  ^37-2

**37:3** His dominion is under the whole heaven, and his light is at the extremities of the earth.  ^37-3

**37:4** After him shall be a cry with a *loud* voice; he shall thunder with the voice of his excellency, yet he shall not cause men to pass away, for one shall hear his voice.  ^37-4

**37:5** The Mighty One shall thunder wonderfully with his voice: for he has done great things which we knew not;  ^37-5

**37:6** commanding the snow, Be thou upon the earth, and the stormy rain, and the storm of the showers of his might.  ^37-6

**37:7** He seals up the hand of every man, that every man may know his own weakness.  ^37-7

**37:8** And the wild beasts come in under the covert, and rest in *their* lair.  ^37-8

**37:9** Troubles come on out of the secret chambers, and cold from the mountain-tops.  ^37-9

**37:10** And from the breath of the Mighty One he will send frost; and he guides the water in whatever way he pleases.  ^37-10

**37:11** And *if* a cloud obscures *what is* precious *to him*, his light will disperse the cloud.  ^37-11

**37:12** And he will carry round the encircling *clouds* by his governance, to *perform* their works: whatsoever he shall command them,  ^37-12

**37:13** this has been appointed by him on the earth, whether for correction, *or* for his land, or if he shall find him *an object* for mercy.  ^37-13

**37:14** Hearken to this, O Job: stand still, and be admonished of the power of the Lord.  ^37-14

**37:15** We know that God has disposed his works, having made light out of darkness.  ^37-15

**37:16** And he knows the divisions of the clouds, and the signal overthrows of the ungodly.  ^37-16

**37:17** But thy robe is warm, and there is quiet upon the land.  ^37-17

**37:18** Wilt thou establish with him *foundations* for the ancient *heavens? they are* strong as a molten mirror.  ^37-18

**37:19** Wherefore teach me, what shall we say to him? and let us cease from saying much.  ^37-19

**37:20** Have I a book or a scribe by me, that I may stand and put man to silence?  ^37-20

**37:21** But the light is not visible to all: it shines afar off in the heavens, as that which is from him in the clouds.  ^37-21

**37:22** From the north *come* the clouds shining like gold: in these great are the glory and honour of the Almighty;  ^37-22

**37:23** and we do not find another his equal in strength: *as for* him that judges justly, dost thou not think that he listens?  ^37-23

**37:24** Wherefore men shall fear him; and the wise also in heart shall fear him.  ^37-24
