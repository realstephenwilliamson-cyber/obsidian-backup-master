## Chapter 105

**105:1** Praise the LORD;  
call on His name;  
proclaim His deeds among the peoples. ^105-1

**105:2** Sing praises to Him;  
speak of all His wondrous acts. ^105-2

**105:3** Exult in His holy name;  
let all who seek the LORD rejoice. ^105-3

**105:4** Turn to the LORD, to His might;[^1]  
seek His presence constantly. ^105-4

**105:5** Remember the wonders He has done,  
His portents and the judgments He has pronounced, ^105-5

**105:6** O offspring of Abraham, His servant,  
O descendants of Jacob, His chosen ones. ^105-6

**105:7** He is the LORD our God;  
His judgments are throughout the earth. ^105-7

**105:8** He is ever mindful of His covenant,  
the promise He gave for a thousand generations, ^105-8

**105:9** that He made with Abraham,  
swore to Isaac, ^105-9

**105:10** and confirmed in a decree for Jacob,  
for Israel, as an eternal covenant, ^105-10

**105:11** saying, “To you I will give the land of Canaan  
as your allotted heritage.” ^105-11

**105:12** They were then few in number,  
a mere handful, sojourning there, ^105-12

**105:13** wandering from nation to nation,  
from one kingdom to another. ^105-13

**105:14** He allowed no one to oppress them;  
He reproved kings on their account, ^105-14

**105:15** “Do not touch My anointed ones;  
do not harm My prophets.” ^105-15

**105:16** He called down a famine on the land,  
destroyed every staff of bread. ^105-16

**105:17** He sent ahead of them a man,  
Joseph, sold into slavery. ^105-17

**105:18** His feet were subjected to fetters;  
an iron collar was put on his neck. ^105-18

**105:19** Until his prediction came true  
the decree of the LORD purged him. ^105-19

**105:20** The king sent to have him freed;  
the ruler of nations released him. ^105-20

**105:21** He made him the lord of his household,  
empowered him over all his possessions, ^105-21

**105:22** to discipline his princes at will,  
to teach his elders wisdom. ^105-22

**105:23** Then Israel came to Egypt;  
Jacob sojourned in the land of Ham. ^105-23

**105:24** He made His people very fruitful,  
more numerous than their foes. ^105-24

**105:25** He changed their heart[^2] to hate His people,  
to plot against His servants. ^105-25

**105:26** He sent His servant Moses,  
and Aaron, whom He had chosen. ^105-26

**105:27** They performed His signs among them,  
His wonders, against the land of Ham. ^105-27

**105:28** He sent darkness; it was very dark;  
did they not defy His word?[^3] ^105-28

**105:29** He turned their waters into blood  
and killed their fish. ^105-29

**105:30** Their land teemed with frogs,  
even the rooms of their king. ^105-30

**105:31** Swarms of insects came at His command,  
lice, throughout their country. ^105-31

**105:32** He gave them hail for rain,  
and flaming fire in their land. ^105-32

**105:33** He struck their vines and fig trees,  
broke down the trees of their country. ^105-33

**105:34** Locusts came at His command,  
grasshoppers without number. ^105-34

**105:35** They devoured every green thing in the land;  
they consumed the produce of the soil. ^105-35

**105:36** He struck down every first-born in the land,  
the first fruit of their vigor. ^105-36

**105:37** He led Israel[^4] out with silver and gold;  
none among their tribes faltered. ^105-37

**105:38** Egypt rejoiced when they left,  
for dread of Israel[^5] had fallen upon them. ^105-38

**105:39** He spread a cloud for a cover,  
and fire to light up the night. ^105-39

**105:40** They asked and He brought them quail,  
and satisfied them with food from heaven. ^105-40

**105:41** He opened a rock so that water gushed forth;  
it flowed as a stream in the parched land. ^105-41

**105:42** Mindful of His sacred promise  
to His servant Abraham, ^105-42

**105:43** He led His people out in gladness,  
His chosen ones with joyous song. ^105-43

**105:44** He gave them the lands of nations;  
they inherited the wealth of peoples, ^105-44

**105:45** that they might keep His laws  
and observe His teachings.  
Hallelujah. ^105-45

[^1]: I.e., the Ark; cf. Ps. 78.61; 132.8.
[^2]: Or “Their heart changed.”
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^4]: Lit. “them.”
[^5]: Lit. “them.”
