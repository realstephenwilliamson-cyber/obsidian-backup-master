### Vardhamanācharita

## Asaga

Vardhamanacharita 

1 23 

#### 123. Pen Pictures (A.K. Warder)

I n which in the evening the moonlight, white from the foam of ambrosia, e ntering through a round window 

Was tasted on the jewel led mosaic 

by a delighted kitten desiring milk I. 35 

In the summer when the woods are twisted by the flames of forest fires; remaining on a mountain in the fierce rays of the sun, 

His body dried up though his firmness does not waver for a moment here; 

The sage's endurance of heat proclaims his resignation. 

xv. 106 A.K. Warder
