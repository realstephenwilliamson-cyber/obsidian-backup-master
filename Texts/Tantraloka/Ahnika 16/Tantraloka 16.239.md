## Mūla

शोध्यतत्त्वे समस्तानां योनीनां तुल्यकालतः ।
जननाद्भोगतः कर्मक्षये स्यादपवृक्तता ॥ ^mula

śodhyatattve samastānāṃ yonīnāṃ tulyakālataḥ |
jananādbhogataḥ karmakṣaye syādapavṛktatā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 07 - Chapter Sixteen, part 3#^pg124|p. 104]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=124>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 16.238cd–239ab.
>
> When the purifying mantra is deposited in this way, the net of (the disciple’s) fetters dissolves away by union (yoga) with its (purifying) rays (brought about) by the might of the teacher’s visualization of it. (Thus the disciple) is made to take birth simultaneously in all the wombs within the reality to be purified. By thus experiencing (them all), when his Karma is exhausted, (he attains) liberation. (with 16.238–239)
