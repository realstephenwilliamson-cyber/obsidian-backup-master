## Mūla

सहस्रादिकहोमोऽपि तृप्त्यै वित्तानुसारतः ।
सति वित्तेऽपि लोभादिग्रस्तो बाह्यप्रधानताम् ॥ ^mula

sahasrādikahomo'pi tṛptyai vittānusārataḥ |
sati vitte'pi lobhādigrasto bāhyapradhānatām || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 08 - Chapter Fifteen, part 7#^pg277|p. 259]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=277>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 15.422cd–423ab.
>
> Then the teacher should make (the gesture of) the deer (mrgīmudrā) with the middle finger, ring finger and thumb. He should then offer libation to the main (Mantra) with sesamum seeds and one hundred (oblations of) clarified butter made with the small ladle. In the same way, (he should offer) one sixth (the number of libations of the times he has repeated) the limbs and faces, and a tenth of (the repetitions of) the remaining Mantras also. (One can offer) even a thousand or more oblations also to satisfy (the Mantra deities), according to the money one has (to do this). (with 15.418cd–420ab)
>
> One who, although he has money, is consumed by greed and the like, perceiving that external (matters) are the most important, consciousness takes second place, and so he does not receive a descent of the power (of grace). (with 15.420cd–421ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* ननु यदि शतिकेनैव होमेन तर्पणं सिध्येत्, तत् किं वित्तक्षयकारिणा सहस्रादिकेन?--इत्याशङ्क्य आह--

> सति वित्तेऽपि लोभादिग्रस्तो बाह्यप्रधानताम् ॥
> प्रथयंश्चिद्गुणीभावाच्छक्तिपातं न विन्दति ।

बाह्यप्रधानता प्रथयन्निति बाह्येधनादावाग्रहात् । यथोक्तम्--

"विभवे सति यो लोभान्न कुर्याद्विधिविस्तरम् ।

नासौ फलमवाप्नोति जातुचित्परमेश्वरि ॥"
