## Mūla

कुशेध्म पञ्चगव्यं च शर्वाग्रे विनियोजयेत् ।
वामामृतादिसंयुक्तं नैवेद्यं त्रिविधं ततः ॥ ^mula

kuśedhma pañcagavyaṃ ca śarvāgre viniyojayet |
vāmāmṛtādisaṃyuktaṃ naivedyaṃ trividhaṃ tataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 04 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 2#^pg97|p. 80]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=97>))
>
> The fuel, made of kuśa grass and the five products of the cow, should be placed before Śarva (i.e. Śiva). Then one should offer, mixed with the Nectar of the Left etc.,' the three kinds of food offering, namely, blood, alcohol, and various drinks. Then he should offer oblation (to the fire) with the meat of a great bull (mahākṣma) and a ram, along with sesame seeds, or else sesame seeds together with clarified butter, or else with rice or wheat grains, drenched with the Five Nectars, together with pieces of sugar. (with 28.171–173)
