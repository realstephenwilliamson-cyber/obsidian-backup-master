## Song of praise and prayer for Jerusalem

*A Song of Ascents. Of David.*

**122:1** I was glad when they said to me, “Let us go to the house of the LORD!” ^122-1

**122:2** Our feet are standing within your gates, O Jerusalem. ^122-2

**122:3** Jerusalem—built as a city that is bound firmly together. ^122-3

**122:4** To it the tribes go up, the tribes of the LORD, as was decreed for Israel, to give thanks to the name of the LORD. ^122-4

**122:5** For there the thrones for judgment were set up, the thrones of the house of David. ^122-5

**122:6** Pray for the peace of Jerusalem: “May they prosper who love you. ^122-6

**122:7** Peace be within your walls and security within your towers.” ^122-7

**122:8** For the sake of my relatives and friends I will say, “Peace be within you.” ^122-8

**122:9** For the sake of the house of the LORD our God, I will seek your good. ^122-9
