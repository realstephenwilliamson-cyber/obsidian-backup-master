### Frag. 1 (= 4Q277 1 ?)

**1** [… the garments] in which he has not ministered in the holy ^f1-1

**2** […] and he shall gird (?) the garments and shall slaugh[ter] ^f1-2

**3** [the] heifer before him, and he shall ‹collect› its blood in a vessel of clay with which ^f1-3

**4** [one has not brought offeri]ngs on the altar, and sprinkle some of the blood with [his] ﬁnger seven ^f1-4

**5** [times] straight in the direction of the tent of meeting. And he shall cast the cedar, ^f1-5

**6** [the hyssop and sca]rlet into the midst of its (the heifer’s) burning. ^f1-6

**7** [… and he shall coll]ect the ashes of the heifer ^f1-7

**8** [… they shall pla]ce it to store it ^f1-8

**9** [for the lustral water …] The priest shall clothe himself ^f1-9
