# Πρίσκα

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · noun

**Dodson:** Prisca, Priscilla — Prisca, Priscilla, the former being the more correct and formal name, the latter a diminutive and more familiar; a Roman lady, probably of good birth, wife of the Jewish Christian Aquila. (Strong's 4251; headword Πρίσκα, ης, ἡ)
