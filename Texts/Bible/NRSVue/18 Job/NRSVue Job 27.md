# Job 27 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Job 26|← Job 26]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Job 28|Job 28 →]]

## Job maintains his integrity

**27:1** Job again took up his discourse and said: ^27-1

**27:2** “As God lives, who has taken away my right, and the Almighty,^[Traditional rendering of Heb *Shaddai*] who has made my soul bitter, ^27-2

**27:3** as long as my breath is in me and the spirit of God is in my nostrils, ^27-3

**27:4** my lips will not speak falsehood, and my tongue will not utter deceit. ^27-4

**27:5** Far be it from me to say that you are right; until I die I will not put away my integrity from me. ^27-5

**27:6** I hold fast my righteousness and will not let it go; my heart will not reproach me as long as I live. ^27-6

**27:7** “May my enemy be like the wicked, and may my opponent be like the unrighteous. ^27-7

**27:8** For what is the hope of the godless when God cuts them off, when God takes away their lives? ^27-8

**27:9** Will God hear their cry when trouble comes upon them? ^27-9

**27:10** Will they take delight in the Almighty?^[Traditional rendering of Heb *Shaddai*] Will they call upon God at all times? ^27-10

**27:11** I will teach you concerning the hand of God; that which is with the Almighty^[Traditional rendering of Heb *Shaddai*] I will not conceal. ^27-11

**27:12** All of you have seen it yourselves; why then have you become altogether vain? ^27-12

**27:13** “This is the portion of the wicked with God and the heritage that oppressors receive from the Almighty:^[Traditional rendering of Heb *Shaddai*] ^27-13

**27:14** If their children are multiplied, it is for the sword, and their offspring have not enough to eat. ^27-14

**27:15** Those who survive them the pestilence buries, and their widows make no lamentation. ^27-15

**27:16** Though they heap up silver like dust and pile up clothing like clay, ^27-16

**27:17** they may pile it up, but the just will wear it, and the innocent will divide the silver. ^27-17

**27:18** They build their houses like nests, like booths made by sentinels of the vineyard. ^27-18

**27:19** They go to bed with wealth but will do so no more; they open their eyes, and it is gone. ^27-19

**27:20** Terrors overtake them like a flood; in the night a whirlwind carries them off. ^27-20

**27:21** The east wind lifts them up, and they are gone; it sweeps them out of their place. ^27-21

**27:22** It^[Or *He* (that is, God)] hurls at them without pity; they flee from its^[Or *his*] power in headlong flight. ^27-22

**27:23** It^[Or *He* (that is, God)] claps its^[Or *his*] hands at them and hisses at them from its^[Or *his*] place. ^27-23
