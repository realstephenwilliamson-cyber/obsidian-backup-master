## Mūla

एतेषामनिवेद्यैव न किंचिदपि भक्षयेत् ।
देवद्रव्यं गुरुद्रव्यं चण्डीद्रव्यं च वर्जयेत् ॥ ^mula

eteṣām anivedyaiva na kiṁcid api bhakṣayet |
devadravyaṁ gurudravyaṁ caṇḍīdravyaṁ ca varjayet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2025 – April 2025
> p. 37 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=51>))
>
> **The Teaching of the Rules and Concluding Ablution**
>
> The wise man always (eats) after having (first) worshipped the gods, fire, teachers and goddesses, and should not eat anything without having offered them food (first).[^148] (with 8.131cd–132ab)

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2025 – April 2025
> p. 37 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=51>))
>
> (Moreover) he should avoid (touching) the sacrificial substances (offered) to the god, teacher and the goddess (*caṇḍī*).[^149] The knower of mantra should not act fruitlessly even for a moment. He should be devoted to the practice of yoga and also, or else, to the practice of mantra. (with 8.132cd–133)

[^148]: 15/538 (534) is a paraphrase of MV 8/132 (not quoted by Jayaratha).
[^149]: See TĀ 15/534-535ab.
