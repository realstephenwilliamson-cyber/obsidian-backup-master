Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 9.07 - Parashat Huqqat#^z3-182a|Parashat Ḥuqqat, at 3:182a]]

*Volume 3, folio 182a* · Chukat

**Chukat 6:44** R. Simeon said: ‘What is “under the sun”? This is the moon; and whoever attaches himself to the moon without the sun, his labour is “under the sun” assuredly; and this was the original sin of the world; and hence it says, “What profit is there to man in all his labour, to wit, to the first Adam and all who have followed him.’ ^chukat-6-44

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Chukat 7:45–8:52.*

**Chukat 9:53** LET AARON BE GATHERED UNTO HIS PEOPLE, ETC. R. Hiya adduced here the verse: “Wherefore I praised the dead which are already dead”, etc. (Eccl. 4, 2). ^chukat-9-53

**Chukat 9:54** How could King Solomon praise the dead more than the living, seeing that only he is called “living” who walks in the way of truth in this world, while the wicked man who does not walk in the way of truth is called “dead”? ^chukat-9-54
