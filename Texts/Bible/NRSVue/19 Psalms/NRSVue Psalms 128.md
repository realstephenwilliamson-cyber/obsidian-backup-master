## The happy home of the faithful

*A Song of Ascents.*

**128:1** Happy is everyone who fears the LORD, who walks in his ways. ^128-1

**128:2** You shall eat the fruit of the labor of your hands; you shall be happy, and it shall go well with you. ^128-2

**128:3** Your wife will be like a fruitful vine within your house; your children will be like olive shoots around your table. ^128-3

**128:4** Thus shall the man be blessed who fears the LORD. ^128-4

**128:5** The LORD bless you from Zion. May you see the prosperity of Jerusalem all the days of your life. ^128-5

**128:6** May you see your children’s children. Peace be upon Israel! ^128-6
