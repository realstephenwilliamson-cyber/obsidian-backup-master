## Mūla

पित्रा स शब्दगहने कृतसंप्रवेश-
स्तर्कार्णवोर्मिपृषतामलपूतचित्तः ।
साहित्यसान्द्ररसभोगपरो महेश-
भक्त्या स्वयंग्रहणदुर्मदया गृहीतः ॥ ^mula

pitrā sa śabdagahane kṛtasaṃpraveśa-
starkārṇavormipṛṣatāmalapūtacittaḥ |
sāhityasāndrarasabhogaparo maheśa-
bhaktyā svayaṃgrahaṇadurmadayā gṛhītaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 11 - Chapter Thirty-Seven, part 1#^pg271|p. 253]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=271>))
>
> He was introduced to (Sanskrit) grammar (śabdagahana) by his father. The drops sprinkled by the waves of the ocean of logic (tarka) purified his stainless mind, and he dedicated himself to the enjoyment of the intense aesthetic delight (rasa) of literature, gripped by devotion to Maheśa, by virtue of the (spiritual) inebriation that spontaneously takes hold (of those such as him).

## Viveka
