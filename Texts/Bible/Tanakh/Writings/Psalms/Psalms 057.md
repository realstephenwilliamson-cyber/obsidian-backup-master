# Psalms 057

Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 057]] · Septuagint (Brenton): [[LXX Psalms 56]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

Compare: [[NRSVue Psalms 057|NRSVue]] (Heb 57:1 = superscription; Heb 57:2–12 = NRSVue 57:1–11)

## Chapter 57

**57:1** For the leader; *al tashḥeth*.[^1] Of David. A *michtam*; when he fled from Saul into a cave. ^57-1

**57:2** Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me,  
for I seek refuge in You,  
I seek refuge in the shadow of Your wings,  
until danger passes. ^57-2

**57:3** I call to God Most High,  
to God who is good to me. ^57-3

**57:4** He will reach down from heaven and deliver me:  
God will send down His steadfast love;  
my persecutor reviles. *Selah*. ^57-4

**57:5** As for me, I lie down among man-eating lions  
whose teeth are spears and arrows,  
whose tongue is a sharp sword. ^57-5

**57:6** Exalt Yourself over the heavens, O God,  
let Your glory be over all the earth! ^57-6

**57:7** They prepared a net for my feet to ensnare me;[^2]  
they dug a pit for me,  
but they fell into it. *Selah*. ^57-7

**57:8** [^3]My heart is firm, O God;  
my heart is firm;  
I will sing, I will chant a hymn. ^57-8

**57:9** Awake, O my soul!  
Awake, O harp and lyre!  
I will wake the dawn. ^57-9

**57:10** I will praise You among the peoples, O Lord;  
I will sing a hymn to You among the nations; ^57-10

**57:11** for Your faithfulness is as high as heaven;  
Your steadfastness reaches to the sky. ^57-11

**57:12** Exalt Yourself over the heavens, O God,  
let Your glory be over all the earth! ^57-12

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Cf. Mishnaic Heb. kefifah, a wicker basket used in fishing.
[^3]: With vv. 8–12, cf. Ps. 108.2–6.
