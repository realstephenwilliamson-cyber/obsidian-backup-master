# Song of Songs 8

← [[Song of Songs 7]] · [[Tanakh]]

Compare: [[NRSVue Song of Songs 08|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 8

**8:1** If only it could be as with a brother,  
As if you had nursed at my mother’s breast:  
Then I could kiss you  
When I met you in the street,  
And no one would despise me. ^8-1

**8:2** I would lead you, I would bring you  
To the house of my mother,  
Of her who taught[^1] me—  
I would let you drink of the spiced wine,  
Of my pomegranate juice. ^8-2

**8:3** His left hand was under my head,  
His right hand caressed me. ^8-3

**8:4** I adjure you, O maidens of Jerusalem:  
Do not wake or rouse  
Love until it please! ^8-4

**8:5** Who is she that comes up from the desert,  
Leaning upon her beloved?  
Under the apple tree I roused you;  
It was there your mother conceived you,  
There she who bore you conceived you. ^8-5

**8:6** Let me be a seal upon your heart,  
Like the seal upon your hand.[^2]  
For love is fierce as death,  
Passion is mighty as Sheol;  
Its darts are darts of fire,  
A blazing flame. ^8-6

**8:7** Vast floods cannot quench love,  
Nor rivers drown it.  
If a man offered all his wealth for love,  
He would be laughed to scorn. ^8-7

**8:8** “We have a little sister,  
Whose breasts are not yet formed.  
What shall we do for our sister  
When she is spoken for? ^8-8

**8:9** If she be a wall,  
We will build upon it a silver battlement;  
If she be a door,  
We will panel it in cedar.” ^8-9

**8:10** I am a wall,  
My breasts are like towers.  
So I became in his eyes  
As one who finds favor. ^8-10

**8:11** Solomon had a vineyard  
In Baal-hamon.  
He had to post guards in the vineyard:  
A man would give for its fruit  
A thousand pieces of silver. ^8-11

**8:12** I have my very own vineyard:  
You may have the thousand, O Solomon,  
And the guards of the fruit two hundred! ^8-12

**8:13** O you who linger in the garden,[^3]  
A lover[^4] is listening;  
Let me hear your voice. ^8-13

**8:14** “Hurry, my beloved,  
Swift as a gazelle or a young stag,  
To the hills of spices!” ^8-14

[^1]: Emendation yields “bore”; cf. 6.9; 8.5.
[^2]: Lit. “arm.”
[^3]: Heb. plural. Meaning of verse uncertain.
[^4]: Heb. plural. Meaning of verse uncertain.
