## Chapter 131

**131:1** A song of ascents. Of David.  
O LORD, my heart is not proud  
nor my look haughty;  
I do not aspire to great things  
or to what is beyond me; ^131-1

**131:2** but I have taught myself to be contented  
like a weaned child with its mother;  
like a weaned child am I in my mind.[^1] ^131-2

**131:3** O Israel, wait for the LORD  
now and forever. ^131-3

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
