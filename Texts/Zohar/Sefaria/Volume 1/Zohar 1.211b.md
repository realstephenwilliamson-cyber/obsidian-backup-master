# Zohar 1.211b

← [[Zohar 1.211a]] · [[Zohar (Soncino)]] · [[Zohar 1.216a]] →

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 3.06 - Parashat Va-Yiggash#^z1-211b|Parashat Va-Yiggash, at 1:211b]]

*Volume 1, folio 211b* · Vayigash, Vayechi

*Passage Vayigash 12:131 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 1.211a#^vayigash-12-131|Vayigash 12:131]].*

**Vayigash 12:132** AND JACOB BLESSED PHARAOH. R. Jose cited in connection with this the verse: *I have compared thee, O my love, to a steed in Pharaoh’s chariot* (S. S. 1, 9). ^vayigash-12-132

**Vayigash 12:133** Observe’, he said, ‘that there are chariots of the left that belong to the other side, and there are chariots of the right that are under the aegis of supernal holiness; the latter are of grace, the former of severity. ^vayigash-12-133

**Vayigash 12:134** When the Holy One, blessed be He, executed justice on the Egyptians, every form of punishment which He inflicted on them was after the very pattern of those chariots, and after the very pattern of that other side: as that side slays and takes men’s souls, so did the Holy One, blessed be He, as it says: “and the Lord slew all the first-born in the land of Egypt” (Ex. 13, 15), and so with all the other punishments executed on the Egyptians. This is the implication of the words, “I compared thee, O my beloved, etc.”, to wit, “I made thee equal to the other side in the power to slay.” And what does Scripture say in regard to the future? “Who is this that cometh from Edom, with crimson garments from Bozrah? etc. (Is. 63, 1). ^vayigash-12-134

**Vayigash 12:135** AND ISRAEL DWELT IN THE LAND OF EGYPT, IN THE LAND OF GOSHEN; AND THEY GOT THEM POSSESSION THEREIN, AND WERE FRUITFUL, AND MULTIPLIED EXCEEDINGLY , and they got them possession, to wit, as a permanent possession, inasmuch as it belonged to them; and they were fruitful, and multiplied, assuredly so, seeing that they were relieved of all vexation and they enjoyed all the luxuries of the world. Blessed be the Lord for evermore. Amen and Amen![^1] ^vayigash-12-135

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Vayechi 1:1–1:4.*

[^1]: The first four and a half pages of this section (211b-216a) are declared by all the commentators to be an interpolation, containing much erroneous doctrine.
