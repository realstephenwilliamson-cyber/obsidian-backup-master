# Sirach Prologue (NRSVue)

— · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Sirach 01|Sirach 1 →]]

**Prol.:1** Many great teachings have been given to us through the Law and the Prophets ^P-1

**Prol.:2** and the others^[Or *other books*] that followed them, ^P-2

**Prol.:3** and for these we should praise Israel for instruction and wisdom. ^P-3

**Prol.:4** Now those who read them must not only themselves understand them ^P-4

**Prol.:5** but must also as lovers of learning be able through ^P-5

**Prol.:6** both speaking and writing to help the outsiders. ^P-6

**Prol.:7** So my grandfather Jesus, who had devoted himself especially ^P-7

**Prol.:8** to the reading of the Law ^P-8

**Prol.:9** and the Prophets ^P-9

**Prol.:10** and the other books of our ancestors ^P-10

**Prol.:11** and had acquired considerable proficiency in them, ^P-11

**Prol.:12** was himself also led to write something pertaining to instruction and wisdom, ^P-12

**Prol.:13** so that by becoming familiar also with his book^[Gk *with these things*] those who love learning ^P-13

**Prol.:14** might make even greater progress in living according to the law. ^P-14

**Prol.:15** You are invited, therefore, ^P-15

**Prol.:17** to read it ^P-17

**Prol.:16** with goodwill and attention ^P-16

**Prol.:18** and to be indulgent ^P-18

**Prol.:19** in cases where we may seem ^P-19

**Prol.:20** to have rendered some phrases imperfectly, despite our diligent labor in translating. ^P-20

**Prol.:21–22** For what was originally expressed in Hebrew does not have exactly the same effect when translated into another language. ^P-21

**Prol.:23** Not only this book, ^P-23

**Prol.:24** but even the Law itself, the Prophets,^[Gk *prophecies*] ^P-24

**Prol.:25** and the rest of the books ^P-25

**Prol.:26** differ not a little when read in the original. ^P-26

**Prol.:27** In the thirty-eighth year of the reign of Euergetes, ^P-27

**Prol.:28** when I came to Egypt and stayed for some time, ^P-28

**Prol.:29** I found a copy affording^[Other ancient authorities read *found opportunity for*] no little instruction. ^P-29

**Prol.:30** It seemed highly necessary that I should myself devote some diligence and labor to the translation of this book. ^P-30

**Prol.:31–32** During that time I have applied my skill day and night ^P-31

**Prol.:33** to complete and publish the book ^P-33

**Prol.:34** for those living abroad who wish to gain learning and are ^P-34

**Prol.:35** preparing ^P-35

**Prol.:36** to live according to the law. ^P-36
