## Mūla

विज्ञानाकलपर्यन्तमात्मतत्त्वमुदाहृतम् ।
ईश्वरान्तम्च विद्याह्नम्शेषम्शिवपदम्विदुः ॥ ^mula

vijñānākalaparyantam ātmatattvam udāhṛtam |
īśvarāntam ca vidyāhnam śeṣam śivapadam viduḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, October 2022 – December 2022
> p. 24 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=31>))
>
> It is said that the principle of the Self (extends) up to (the level of) the Dissolution Deconditioned (perceivers), the one called Knowledge up to that of Īśvara. (The wise) know that the rest is Śiva's plane.[^67]

[^67]: MV 2/47 is quoted in TĀv ad 11/34cd-35. TĀ 11/34cd-35 is a paraphrase of MV 2/46cd-47.
