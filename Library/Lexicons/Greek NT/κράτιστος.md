# κράτιστος

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · adjective

**Dodson:** most excellent — most excellent, an official epithet, used in addressing a Roman of high rank, and in the second century one of equestrian (as distinguished from senatorial) rank. (Strong's 2903; headword κράτιστος, η, ον)
