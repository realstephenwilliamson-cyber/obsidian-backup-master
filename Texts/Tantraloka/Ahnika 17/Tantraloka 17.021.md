## Mūla

तस्यैव तत्स्वतन्त्रत्वं यातिदुर्घटकारिता ।
संबोधरूपे तत्तस्मिन् कथं संबोधना भवेत् ॥ ^mula

tasyaiva tatsvatantratvaṃ yātidurghaṭakāritā |
saṃbodharūpe tattasmin kathaṃ saṃbodhanā bhavet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 08 - Chapter Seventeen#^pg170|p. 150]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=170>))
>
> (The forms) in which that (Light) manifests, as the object of worship and meditation etc., are (expressions of its) freedom, that can do the impossible. = (with 17.20cd–21ab)
>
> Thus, how can here be an invocation within that which is invocation (itself)? (How can an awakening be brought about within that (reality) which is awakened consciousness by nature?) It is not the luminosity of the light that is present in the act of illumination (it is the object of illumination that is illumined, not the light). (with 17.21cd–22ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* अत एव पूज्यत्वादेरमुख्यत्वात् नात्राह्वानाद्युपयोगः?--इत्याह--

> संबोधरूपे तत्तस्मिन् कथं संबोधना भवेत् ॥
> प्रकाशनायां वै न स्यात्प्रकाशस्य प्रकाशता ।

तत् तस्मात् पूज्यत्वादेस्तत्स्वातन्त्र्यविजृम्भामात्रत्वात् तस्मिन् संबोधनक्रियाकर्तृत्वात्मनि शिवे संबोधना कथं भवेत् संबोध्यमानता अस्य न न्यायया । नहि परकर्तृकायां प्रकाशनायां प्रकाशस्य प्रकाशतैव स्यात्, किन्तु प्रकाश्यमानता- -इत्यर्थः ॥
