## Mūla

कजं मध्ये तदर्धेन शूलशृङ्गाणि तानि तु ।
शूलाङ्कं मण्डलं कल्प्यं कमलाङ्कं च पूरणे ॥ ^mula

kajaṃ madhye tadardhena śūlaśṛṅgāṇi tāni tu |
śūlāṅkaṃ maṇḍalaṃ kalpyaṃ kamalāṅkaṃ ca pūraṇe || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 04 - Chapter Thirty-One, part 1#^pg111|p. 93]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=111>))
>
> ‘(In one type of mandala, there is a) lotus in the middle (of it). (In the other type,) the prongs of the trident are half the size of that (lotus). These (constitute) the mandala that is marked by a trident. One marked by a lotus should be fashioned to complete 1.
