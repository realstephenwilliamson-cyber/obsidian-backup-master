# LXX Proverbs 29

← [[LXX Proverbs 28]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Proverbs 30]] →

*Proverbs · [[LXX]]*

**29:1** A reprover is better than a stiff-necked man: for when the latter is suddenly set on fire, there shall be no remedy.  ^29-1

**29:2** When the righteous are praised, the people will rejoice: but when the ungodly rule, men mourn.  ^29-2

**29:3** When a man loves wisdom, his father rejoices: but he that keeps harlots will waste wealth.  ^29-3

**29:4** A righteous king establishes a country: but a transgressor destroys *it*.  ^29-4

**29:5** He that prepares a net in the way of his own friend, entangles his own feet in it.  ^29-5

**29:6** A great snare *is spread* for a sinner: but the righteous shall be in joy and gladness.  ^29-6

**29:7** A righteous man knows how to judge for the poor: but the ungodly understands not knowledge; and the poor man has not an understanding mind.  ^29-7

**29:8** Lawless men burn down a city: but wise men turn away wrath.  ^29-8

**29:9** A wise man shall judge nations: but a worthless man being angry laughs and fears not.  ^29-9

**29:10** Bloody men hate a holy *person*, but the upright will seek his soul.  ^29-10

**29:11** A fool utters all his mind: but the wise reserves his in part.  ^29-11

**29:12** When a king hearkens to unjust language, all his subjects are transgressors.  ^29-12

**29:13** When the creditor and debtor meet together, the Lord oversees them both.  ^29-13

**29:14** When a king judges the poor in truth, his throne shall be established for a testimony.[^1]  ^29-14

**29:15** Stripes and reproofs give wisdom: but an erring child disgraces his parents.  ^29-15

**29:16** When the ungodly abound, sins abound: but when they fall, the righteous are warned.  ^29-16

**29:17** Chasten thy son, and he shall give thee rest; and he shall give honour to thy soul.  ^29-17

**29:18** There shall be no interpreter to a sinful nation: but he that observes the law is blessed.[^2]  ^29-18

**29:19** A stubborn servant will not be reproved by words: for even if he understand, still he will not obey.  ^29-19

**29:20** If thou see a man hasty in *his* words, know that the fool has hope rather than he.  ^29-20

**29:21** He that lives wantonly from a child, shall be a servant, and in the end shall grieve over himself.  ^29-21

**29:22** A furious man stirs up strife, and a passionate man digs up sin.  ^29-22

**29:23** Pride brings a man low, but the Lord upholds the humble-minded with honour.  ^29-23

**29:24** He that shares with a thief, hates his own soul: and if any having heard an oath uttered tell not of it,  ^29-24

**29:25** *they* fearing and reverencing men *unreasonably* have been overthrown, but he that trusts in the Lord shall rejoice. Ungodliness causes a man to stumble: but he that trusts in his master shall be safe.[^3]  ^29-25

**29:26** Many wait on the favour of rulers; but justice comes to a man from the Lord.  ^29-26

**29:27** A righteous man is an abomination to an unrighteous man, and the direct way is an abomination to the sinner.  ^29-27

[^1]: 29:14 *Heb.*'for ever.' See Am. 1. 11; Mic. 7. 18; in the Greek.
[^2]: 29:18 *Or,*'most blessed.'
[^3]: 29:25 Possibly, 'in the Lord.' See 2 Pet. 2. 1.
