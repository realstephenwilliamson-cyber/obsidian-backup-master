## Chapter 112

**112:1** Hallelujah.  
Happy is the man who fears the LORD,  
who is ardently devoted to His commandments. ^112-1

**112:2** His descendants will be mighty in the land,  
a blessed generation of upright men. ^112-2

**112:3** Wealth and riches are in his house,  
and his beneficence lasts forever. ^112-3

**112:4** A light shines[^1] for the upright in the darkness;  
he is gracious, compassionate, and beneficent. ^112-4

**112:5** All goes well with the man who lends generously,  
who conducts his affairs with equity. ^112-5

**112:6** He shall never be shaken;  
the beneficent man will be remembered forever. ^112-6

**112:7** He is not afraid of evil tidings;  
his heart is firm, he trusts in the LORD. ^112-7

**112:8** His heart is resolute, he is unafraid;  
in the end he will see the fall of his foes. ^112-8

**112:9** He gives freely to the poor;  
his beneficence lasts forever;  
his horn is exalted in honor. ^112-9

**112:10** The wicked man shall see it and be vexed;  
he shall gnash his teeth; his courage shall fail.  
The desire of the wicked shall come to nothing. ^112-10

[^1]: Or “He shines as a light.”
