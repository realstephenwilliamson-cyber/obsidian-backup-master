## Mūla

ज्ञप्त्यात्मेति कथं कर्मनियत्यादि प्रतीक्षते ।
कर्मकालनियत्यादि यतः सङ्कोचजीवितम् ॥ ^mula

jñaptyātmeti kathaṃ karmaniyatyādi pratīkṣate |
karmakālaniyatyādi yataḥ saṅkocajīvitam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 05 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 3#^pg121|p. 104]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=121>))
>
> How can the grace (anugraha), which is the intuitive insight (jñapti) into one’s own unfolding (vikasvara) nature (in its fullness), be dependent? on Karma and the constraint (it exerts) etc. (with 28.233cd–234ab)
>
> The very life of Karma, the constraint (it exerts), time etc., is the contracted (state of consciousness of the individual soul), so how can this serve as a cause of grace, the nature of which in the destruction of (that) contraction? Moreover, grace is divided into intense (fvra) and gradual. (All) this has been explained at length previously, so what is the use of saying the same things again? (with 28.234cd–236ab)
