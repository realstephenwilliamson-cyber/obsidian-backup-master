## Mūla

शिवशक्त्योर्न भेदोऽस्ति शक्त्युत्थास्तु मरीचयः ।
तासामानन्दजनकं मद्यं शिवमयं ततः ॥ ^mula

śivaśaktyorna bhedo'sti śaktyutthāstu marīcayaḥ |
tāsāmānandajanakaṃ madyaṃ śivamayaṃ tataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 03 - Chapter Fifteen, part 2#^pg73|p. 55]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=73>))
>
> This is so as wine is iva, and all Mantras are born from Śiva. There is no difference between Śiva and his power, from which (all) the rays (of the senses) are born, and wine, which is Śiva, makes them blissful. Thus, (he who performs this ablution) is competent to (penetrate) consciousness in its full, awakened form. (with 15.73cd–75ab)

## Viveka

> यतः शिवमयं मद्यं सर्वे मन्त्राः शिवोद्भवाः ॥
> शिवशक्त्योर्न भेदोऽस्ति शक्त्ययुस्थास्तु मरीचयः ।
> तासामानन्दजनकं मद्यं शिवमयं ततः ॥
> प्रबुद्धे संविदः पूर्णे रूपेऽधिकृतिभाजनम् ।

अधिकृतिभाजनमिति प्रबुद्धपूर्णसंविद्रूपतया केवलीभवेत्-- इत्यर्थः ॥

ननु अन्येषामयमाशयः--यत्

"आदौ स्नानं प्रकुर्वीत सर्वकिल्बिषनाशनम् ।"
