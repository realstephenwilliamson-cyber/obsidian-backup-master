## Mūla

मात्रा ह्रस्वाः पञ्च दीर्घाष्टकं द्विस्त्रिःप्लुतं तु ॡ ।
एकाशीतिमिमामर्धमात्राणामाह नो गुरुः ॥ ^mula

mātrā hrasvāḥ pañca dīrghāṣṭakaṃ dvistriḥplutaṃ tu ḹ |
ekāśītimimāmardhamātrāṇāmāha no guruḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 13 - Chapter Six, part 6#^pg444|p. 429]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=444>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 6.226cd–227ab.
>
> It is said that the time (that is, duration) of the thirty-three consonants is half a measure (ardhamātra) (each). The five short vowels (last for) one measure (each) (mātrā), the eight long ones, two (each), and the prolated Ḷ, three. (with 6.225cd–226ab)
>
> These are the eighty-one half measures (mātrā) taught (to us by) our teacher. (with 6.226cd)
