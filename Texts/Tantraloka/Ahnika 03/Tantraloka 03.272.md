## Mūla

इत एव प्रभृत्येषा जीवन्मुक्तिर्विचार्यते ।
यत्र सूत्रणयापीयमुपायोपेयकल्पना ॥ ^mula

ita eva prabhṛtyeṣā jīvanmuktirvicāryate |
yatra sūtraṇayāpīyamupāyopeyakalpanā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. II
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 02 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v02 12 - Chapter Three, part 9#^pg412|p. 396]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 02.pdf#page=412>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 3.272cd–273ab.
>
> From this point onwards we will discuss (the nature of) this (state of) liberation in life (jīvanmukti), (which is such) even though this notion that there is a means and a goal (persists) there in a subtle form (sūtraṇayā). In the previous chapter there was (no question of) any formation of division (bheda), for (at the level) free of means, who is liberated, how and where? (with 3.272–273)
