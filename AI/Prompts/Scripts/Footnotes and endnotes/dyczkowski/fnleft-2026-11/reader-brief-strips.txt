READING THE START OF FOOTNOTES (Dyczkowski's Tantraloka translation, 3 Nov 2026). Reading only: write nothing but your own output file.

You get images in /home/claude/fnleft/sheets/. Most are SHEETS: up to ten strips cut from printed pages, each under a grey bar
"CASE iNNN". Each strip holds the line where one footnote begins, with a line or two above it. A few cases instead have the lower
part of a whole page (files named vNN-pKKKK-low.png).

Your task file lists, for each case: the case id, its image, a footnote number, and the first words of that footnote AS A COMPUTER
READ THEM. The number is the vault's label: often NOT the printed number (the print may say 37 where the label says 1087). Find the
footnote by its first words; use the number only as a hint. A strip may show two or three footnotes; pick the one whose text
begins with (or soon reaches) the first words given.

For each case, copy EXACTLY what is printed between the small raised footnote number and those first words.
- Often that is nothing.
- Sometimes it is a source abbreviation (a siglum such as YSū, ŚDṛ, BhGī, SpKā, TĀ, TĀv, SvT, MVT, JY, KauśīBr), a verse
  number such as "142cd", a word or several words, a quotation mark, a bracket, a dash or another mark. Anything printed there
  counts, even if it looks like the natural start of the note: the first words you are given begin AFTER that spot.
- The first words may contain small reading errors or "…" where the computer lost a word further on; ignore that.
- Copy diacritics exactly (ā ī ū ṛ ṣ ś ṇ ṭ ḍ ṃ ṁ ḥ ñ ṅ), and curly quotes as printed (‘ ’ “ ”).
- Do not guess. If the print at that spot is unclear, write "unclear". If you cannot find the footnote, found = false.

Output: JSON Lines at the path in your task, one line per case, in the order of your list:
{"id": "i000", "found": true, "between": "", "printed_number": "37", "note": "at most 12 words"}
("between": "" = nothing printed; otherwise the exact characters, or "unclear".)
Open each image once with the Read tool (several cases share a sheet). Never write the divine name (the four-letter name of God)
in any form. At the end check that every case has exactly one line and that each line parses as JSON. Reply with one line:
counts of cases, found, nothing, something, unclear.
