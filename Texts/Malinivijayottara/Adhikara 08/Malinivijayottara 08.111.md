## Mūla

कुशास्तरणपरिधिविष्टराणां च कल्पनम् ।
सर्वमस्त्रेण कुर्वीत विद्यामो.म्ह्रीमिति न्यसेत् ॥ ^mula

kuśāstaraṇaparidhiviṣṭarāṇāṁ ca kalpanam |
sarvam astreṇa kurvīta vidyām o.m hrīm iti[^127] nyaset || ^iast

*Muktabodha's e-text of the KSTS edition (M00160) has:* kuśāstaraṇaparidhiviṣṭarāṇāṃ ca kalpanam | sarvamastreṇa kurvīta vidyārmohīmiti nyaset ||

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2025 – April 2025
> p. 31 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=45>))
>
> (All the preparatory purificatory rites) of the sacrificial hearth (*kuṇḍa*), including the digging, sprinkling, beating (of the ground to flatten it), the smearing, the cross, the enclosure (*akṣavāṭa*), the fashioning of the carpet of *kuśa* grass, the enclosing trench (*paridhi*), and the seat of *kuśa* grass (*viṣṭara*), (as well as) the installation of the thunderbolt, should be done with the Weapon.[^126] (with 8.110–111cd)

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2025 – April 2025
> p. 32 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=46>))
>
> One should deposit the (Mālinī) Vidyā OṀ HRĪṀ. Having deposited the Śiva (Mātṛkā with an initial) OṀ and worshipped both again, he should bring the fire on a copper or earthenware plate. Having meditated (on it thinking that it is) Śiva's seed, it should be cast into the womb (yoni) of the Vidyā.[^128] (with 8.111cd–113ab)

[^127]: kh: *vidyāmaindrīm*
[^126]: TĀ 15/402-404ab (399-401ab) comment on MV 8/110-11ab.
[^128]: MV 8/111d-113ab is quoted in the TĀv ad 15/405cd-406ab (402cd-403ab). 'Having moved three times around outside the pit with the syllable OṀ (*dhruva*).' This line (quoted by Jayaratha just after this verse is not found in the edition of the MV.
