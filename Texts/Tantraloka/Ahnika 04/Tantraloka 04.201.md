## Mūla

अन्तरिन्धनसंभारमनपेक्ष्यैव नित्यशः ।
जाज्वलीत्यखिलाक्षौघप्रसृतोग्रशिखः शिखी ॥ ^mula

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. III
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 03 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v03 09 - Chapter Four, part 8#^pg317|p. 304]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 03.pdf#page=317>))
>
> The fire (of consciousness) burns constantly and intensely, without need of any other fuel, its awesome flame pouring forth from all the senses. Phenomena, entering this fire of consciousness, intensifying its glorious splendour, become the means to offer oblation. (with 4.201–202)
