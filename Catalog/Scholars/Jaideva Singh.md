# Jaideva Singh

Scholar of Kashmir Shaivism (1893–27 May 1986, died in Varanasi); he studied for many years with Swami Lakshman Joo and translated the Vijñānabhairava, the Śiva Sūtras, the Spanda-Kārikās and the Parātrīśikā-vivaraṇa; he was chief producer at All India Radio and was appointed chairman of the Uttar Pradesh Sangeet Natak Akademi in 1973 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaideva_Singh)). The page does not state what post he held when he died, so the chairmanship is the last one found.
