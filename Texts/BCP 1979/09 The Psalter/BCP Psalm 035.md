# Psalm 35

← [[BCP Psalm 034]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 036]] →

*Judica, Domine* · Book One · Seventh Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 035]]

**35:1** Fight those who fight me, O LORD; \*  
attack those who are attacking me. ^35-1

**35:2** Take up shield and armor \*  
and rise up to help me. ^35-2

**35:3** Draw the sword and bar the way against those who pursue me; \*  
say to my soul, "I am your salvation." ^35-3

**35:4** Let those who seek after my life be shamed and humbled; \*  
let those who plot my ruin fall back and be dismayed. ^35-4

**35:5** Let them be like chaff before the wind, \*  
and let the angel of the LORD drive them away. ^35-5

[p. 630] ^pg630

**35:6** Let their way be dark and slippery, \*  
and let the angel of the LORD pursue them. ^35-6

**35:7** For they have secretly spread a net for me without a cause; \*  
without a cause they have dug a pit to take me alive. ^35-7

**35:8** Let ruin come upon them unawares; \*  
let them be caught in the net they hid;  
let them fall into the pit they dug. ^35-8

**35:9** Then I will be joyful in the LORD; \*  
I will glory in his victory. ^35-9

**35:10** My very bones will say, "LORD, who is like you? \*  
You deliver the poor from those who are too strong for them,  
the poor and needy from those who rob them." ^35-10

**35:11** Malicious witnesses rise up against me; \*  
they charge me with matters I know nothing about. ^35-11

**35:12** They pay me evil in exchange for good; \*  
my soul is full of despair. ^35-12

**35:13** But when they were sick I dressed in sack-cloth \*  
and humbled myself by fasting. ^35-13

**35:14** I prayed with my whole heart,  
as one would for a friend or a brother; \*  
I behaved like one who mourns for his mother,  
bowed down and grieving. ^35-14

**35:15** But when I stumbled, they were glad and gathered together;  
they gathered against me; \*  
strangers whom I did not know tore me to pieces and would not stop. ^35-15

**35:16** They put me to the test and mocked me; \*  
they gnashed at me with their teeth. ^35-16

[p. 631] ^pg631

**35:17** O Lord, how long will you look on? \*  
rescue me from the roaring beasts,  
and my life from the young lions. ^35-17

**35:18** I will give you thanks in the great congregation; \*  
I will praise you in the mighty throng. ^35-18

**35:19** Do not let my treacherous foes rejoice over me, \*  
nor let those who hate me without a cause wink at each other. ^35-19

**35:20** For they do not plan for peace, \*  
but invent deceitful schemes against the quiet in the land. ^35-20

**35:21** They opened their mouths at me and said, \*  
"Aha! we saw it with our own eyes." ^35-21

**35:22** You saw it, O LORD; do not be silent; \*  
O Lord, be not far from me. ^35-22

**35:23** Awake, arise, to my cause! \*  
to my defense, my God and my Lord! ^35-23

**35:24** Give me justice, O LORD my God,  
according to your righteousness; \*  
do not let them triumph over me. ^35-24

**35:25** Do not let them say in their hearts,  
"Aha! just what we want!" \*  
Do not let them say, "We have swallowed him up." ^35-25

**35:26** Let all who rejoice at my ruin be ashamed and disgraced; \*  
let those who boast against me be clothed with dismay and shame. ^35-26

**35:27** Let those who favor my cause sing out with joy and be glad; \*  
let them say always, "Great is the LORD,  
who desires the prosperity of his servant." ^35-27

[p. 632] ^pg632

**35:28** And my tongue shall be talking of your righteousness \*  
and of your praise all the day long. ^35-28
