## Mūla

अर्घपात्राम्बुविप्रुड्भिः स्पृष्टं सर्वं हि शुध्यति ।
शिवार्ककरसंस्पर्शात्काऽन्या शुद्धिर्भविष्यति ॥ ^mula

arghapātrāmbuvipruḍbhiḥ spṛṣṭaṃ sarvaṃ hi śudhyati |
śivārkakarasaṃsparśātkā'nyā śuddhirbhaviṣyati || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 04 - Chapter Fifteen, part 3#^pg119|p. 101]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=119>))
>
> Anything touched by the drops of water in the sacrificial vessel is purified. What purification could there be other than (the one that comes about by) contact with the rays of the sun that is Śiva?
