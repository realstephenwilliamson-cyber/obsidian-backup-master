## Mūla

अनेनैव विधानेन पुंस्तत्त्वात्तु कलान्तिकम् ।
त्रयोदशविधं ज्ञेयं रुद्रवत्प्रलयाकलाः ॥ ^mula

anenaiva vidhānena pumstattvāt tu kalāntikam |
trayodaśavidham jñeyam rudravatpralayākalāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, July 2022 – September 2022
> p. 29 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Malini (journal), July-September 2022.pdf#page=31>))
>
> One should know by this same procedure that (each of the principles ranging) from the principle of the Individual Soul[^18] to the Force (of Obscuration) (kalā) is thirteenfold. The (savedya) Dissolution Deconditioned (Pralayākala)[^19] (subjects are each) elevenfold.

[^18]: Read with the KSTS edition of the MV as required by the metre, *pumstattvāt* tu for *pumstattvāt* *.
[^19]: Although the reading here and in TĀv ad 10/124cd-127ab is *pralayākalaḥ*, read with the KSTS edition of the MV the plural *pralayākalāḥ*. Vasudeva prefers to retain the singular, although all the other perceivers listed are plural.
