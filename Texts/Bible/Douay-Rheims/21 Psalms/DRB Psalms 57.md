# Psalms 57 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 56|← Psalms 56]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 58|Psalms 58 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 57|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**57:1** Unto the end, destroy not, for David, for an inscription of a title. ^57-1

**57:2** If in very deed you speak justice: judge right things, ye sons of men. ^57-2

**57:3** For in your heart you work iniquity: your hands forge injustice in the earth. ^57-3

**57:4** The wicked are alienated from the womb; they have gone astray from the womb: they have spoken false things. ^57-4

**57:5** Their madness is according to the likeness of a serpent: like the deaf asp that stoppeth her ears: ^57-5

**57:6** Which will not hear the voice of the charmers; nor of the wizard that charmeth wisely. ^57-6

**57:7** God shall break in pieces their teeth in their mouth: the Lord shall break the grinders of the lions. ^57-7

**57:8** They shall come to nothing, like water running down; he hath bent his bow till they be weakened. ^57-8

**57:9** Like wax that melteth they shall be taken away: fire hath fallen on them, and they shall not see the sun. ^57-9

**57:10** Before your thorns could know the brier; he swalloweth them up, as alive, in his wrath. ^57-10

**57:11** The just shall rejoice when he shall see the revenge: he shall wash his hands in the blood of the sinner. ^57-11

**57:12** And man shall say: If indeed there be fruit to the just: there is indeed a God that judgeth them on the earth. ^57-12
