*Part I · Lesson 111* · [[Likutei Moharan]]

## 1

“*Rosh B’nei Yisrael* (The head of the Children of Israel)” ([[Torah 21 - Ki Tisa#^30-12|Exodus 30:12]]). The first letters spell *ReBY* (my teacher).

Conversely, the first letters of “*R’sha’im Bachoshekh Yidamu* (The wicked shall be silent in the darkness)” ([[I Samuel 02#^2-9|1 Samuel 2:9]]) spell *ReBY*. \<This hints that their Torah is taught in darkness.>
