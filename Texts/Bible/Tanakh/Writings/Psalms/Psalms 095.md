## Chapter 95

**95:1** Come, let us sing joyously to the LORD,  
raise a shout for our rock and deliverer; ^95-1

**95:2** let us come into His presence with praise;  
let us raise a shout for Him in song! ^95-2

**95:3** For the LORD is a great God,  
the great king of all divine beings. ^95-3

**95:4** In His hand are the depths of the earth;  
the peaks of the mountains are His. ^95-4

**95:5** His is the sea, He made it;  
and the land, which His hands fashioned. ^95-5

**95:6** Come, let us bow down and kneel,  
bend the knee before the LORD our maker, ^95-6

**95:7** for He is our God,  
and we are the people He tends, the flock in His care.  
O, if you would but heed His charge this day: ^95-7

**95:8** Do not be stubborn as at Meribah,  
as on the day of Massah, in the wilderness, ^95-8

**95:9** when your fathers put Me to the test,  
tried Me, though they had seen My deeds. ^95-9

**95:10** Forty years I was provoked by that generation;  
I thought, “They are a senseless people;  
they would not know My ways.” ^95-10

**95:11** Concerning them I swore in anger,  
“They shall never come to My resting-place!” ^95-11
