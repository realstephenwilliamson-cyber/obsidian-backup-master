## Mūla

न्यस्येत्क्रमेण सर्वांङ्गं तेनैवास्य च संस्पृशेत् ।
उक्तं दीक्षोत्तरे चैतज्ज्वालासंपातशोभिना ॥ ^mula

nyasyetkrameṇa sarvāṃṅgaṃ tenaivāsya ca saṃspṛśet |
uktaṃ dīkṣottare caitajjvālāsaṃpātaśobhinā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 08 - Chapter Fifteen, part 7#^pg298|p. 280]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=298>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 15.460cd–461ab.
>
> Then (the teacher) should worship on his right hand, by means of the left, the wheel of Mantras, energized (dīpta) and full of all the Paths, that burn up all the fetters. (He should) place that hand on the head of (his) disciple, onto whose body the series of paths have been deposited, and he should deposit in due order (the Mantra) on each limb (of his body), and touch it with that same (hand). (with 15.456cd–458ab)
>
> And (so) this is said in the Dīkṣottara, (where we read that) the disciple becomes a regular initiate (samayin) when (the teacher) has given (and laid upon him) Śiva’s hand, beautiful with the descent (saṁpāta) of the flames (of the fire of Mantra). He is united, while he is still alive, with the Īśvara principle, and is (rendered) fit to study (the scriptures). (with 15.458cd–459)
