## Mūla

महेश्वरत्वं संवित्त्वं तदत्यक्ष्यद् घटादिवत् ।
परिच्छिन्नप्रकाशत्वं जडस्य किल लक्षणम् ॥ ^mula

maheśvaratvaṃ saṃvittvaṃ tadatyakṣyad ghaṭādivat |
paricchinnaprakāśatvaṃ jaḍasya kila lakṣaṇam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. II
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 02 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v02 07 - Chapter Three, part 4#^pg222|p. 206]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 02.pdf#page=222>))
>
> If the Great Lord were to have a body of (but) one (fixed) form, he would have to give up His lordship and nature as consciousness and be (insentient,) like a (common) jar. (with 3.100cd–101ab)
>
> Indeed, circumscribed (and conditioned) manifestation (paricchinna-prakāśatva) is the characteristic of (all that is) insentient. Consciousness differs from the insentient because (it is not an object, and so) is not delimited (na parimīyate) (by anything). (with 3.101cd–102ab)
