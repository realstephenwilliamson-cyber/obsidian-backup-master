# ἵστημι

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · verb

**Dodson:** I cause to stand, stand — trans: (a) I make to stand, place, set up, establish, appoint; mid: I place myself, stand, (b) I set in balance, weigh; intrans: (c) I stand, stand by, stand still; met: I stand ready, stand firm, am steadfast. (Strong's 2476; headword ἵστημι)
