# Psalm 142

← [[BCP Psalm 141]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 143]] →

*Voce mea ad Dominum* · Book Five · Twenty-ninth Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 142]]

**142:1** I cry to the LORD with my voice; \*  
to the LORD I make loud supplication. ^142-1

**142:2** I pour out my complaint before him \*  
and tell him all my trouble. ^142-2

**142:3** When my spirit languishes within me, you know my path; \*  
in the way wherein I walk they have hidden a trap for me. ^142-3

**142:4** I look to my right hand and find no one who knows me; \*  
I have no place to flee to, and no one cares for me. ^142-4

**142:5** I cry out to you, O LORD; \*  
I say, "You are my refuge,  
my portion in the land of the living." ^142-5

**142:6** Listen to my cry for help, for I have been brought very low; \*  
save me from those who pursue me,  
for they are too strong for me. ^142-6

**142:7** Bring me out of prison, that I may give thanks to your Name; \*  
when you have dealt bountifully with me,  
the righteous will gather around me. ^142-7
