## Mūla

रथ्यान्तरे मूत्रपुरीषमध्ये चण्डालगेहे निरये श्मशाने ।
सचिन्तको वा गतचिन्तको वा ज्ञानी विमोक्षं लभतेऽपि चान्ते ॥ ^mula

rathyāntare mūtrapurīṣamadhye caṇḍālagehe niraye śmaśāne |
sacintako vā gatacintako vā jñānī vimokṣaṃ labhate'pi cānte || ^iast

## Translation

> ‘(Wherever the liberated man dies, be it) in the middle of a street, amongst dung and urine, in the house of an outcaste, in hell, in a cemetery, the man of knowledge, whether he thinks or is free of thought, attains liberation, at the end (of his life) 9150.
