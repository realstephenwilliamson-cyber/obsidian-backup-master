# Psalm 34

← [[BCP Psalm 033]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 035]] →

*Benedicam Dominum* · Book One · Sixth Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 034]]

**34:1** I will bless the LORD at all times; \*  
his praise shall ever be in my mouth. ^34-1

**34:2** I will glory in the LORD; \*  
let the humble hear and rejoice. ^34-2

**34:3** Proclaim with me the greatness of the LORD; \*  
let us exalt his Name together. ^34-3

[p. 628]

**34:4** I sought the LORD, and he answered me \*  
and delivered me out of all my terror. ^34-4

**34:5** Look upon him and be radiant, \*  
and let not your faces be ashamed. ^34-5

**34:6** I called in my affliction and the LORD heard me \*  
and saved me from all my troubles. ^34-6

**34:7** The angel of the LORD encompasses those who fear him, \*  
and he will deliver them. ^34-7

**34:8** Taste and see that the LORD is good; \*  
happy are they who trust in him! ^34-8

**34:9** Fear the LORD, you that are his saints, \*  
for those who fear him lack nothing. ^34-9

**34:10** The young lions lack and suffer hunger, \*  
but those who seek the LORD lack nothing that is good. ^34-10

**34:11** Come, children, and listen to me; \*  
I will teach you the fear of the LORD. ^34-11

**34:12** Who among you loves life \*  
and desires long life to enjoy prosperity? ^34-12

**34:13** Keep your tongue from evil-speaking \*  
and your lips from lying words. ^34-13

**34:14** Turn from evil and do good; \*  
seek peace and pursue it. ^34-14

**34:15** The eyes of the LORD are upon the righteous, \*  
and his ears are open to their cry. ^34-15

**34:16** The face of the LORD is against those who do evil, \*  
to root out the remembrance of them from the earth. ^34-16

[p. 629]

**34:17** The righteous cry, and the LORD hears them \*  
and delivers them from all their troubles. ^34-17

**34:18** The LORD is near to the brokenhearted \*  
and will save those whose spirits are crushed. ^34-18

**34:19** Many are the troubles of the righteous, \*  
but the LORD will deliver him out of them all. ^34-19

**34:20** He will keep safe all his bones; \*  
not one of them shall be broken. ^34-20

**34:21** Evil shall slay the wicked, \*  
and those who hate the righteous will be punished. ^34-21

**34:22** The LORD ransoms the life of his servants, \*  
and none will be punished who trust in him. ^34-22
