## Mūla

उद्यद्गौराङ्कुरविकसितैः श्यामरक्तैः पलाशै-
रन्तर्गाढारुणरुचिलसत्केसरालीविचित्रैः ।
आकीर्णा भूः प्रतिपदमसौ यत्र काश्मीरपुष्पैः
सम्यग्देवीत्रितययजनोद्यानमाविष्करोति ॥ ^mula

udyadgaurāṅkuravikasitaiḥ śyāmaraktaiḥ palāśai-
rantargāḍhāruṇarucilasatkesarālīvicitraiḥ |
ākīrṇā bhūḥ pratipadamasau yatra kāśmīrapuṣpaiḥ
samyagdevītritayayajanodyānamāviṣkaroti || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 11 - Chapter Thirty-Seven, part 1#^pg265|p. 247]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=265>))
>
> The ground strewn at every step with saffron flowers, with their blue-red (Śyāmarakta i.e. violet) petals blooming on the white sprouts emerging (from their stem), (adorned) on the inside with beautiful rows of stamens (quivering) playfully with their radiant deep red colour, make a (veritable) garden fit for the worship of the three goddesses (Parā, Parāparā, and Aparā).
