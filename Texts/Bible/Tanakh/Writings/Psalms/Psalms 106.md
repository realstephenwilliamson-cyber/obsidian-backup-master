## Chapter 106

**106:1** Hallelujah.  
Praise the LORD for He is good;  
His steadfast love is eternal. ^106-1

**106:2** Who can tell the mighty acts of the LORD,  
proclaim all His praises? ^106-2

**106:3** Happy are those who act justly,  
who do right at all times. ^106-3

**106:4** Be mindful of me, O LORD, when You favor Your people;  
take note of me when You deliver them, ^106-4

**106:5** that I may enjoy the prosperity of Your chosen ones,  
share the joy of Your nation,  
glory in Your very own people. ^106-5

**106:6** We have sinned like our forefathers;  
we have gone astray, done evil. ^106-6

**106:7** Our forefathers in Egypt did not perceive Your wonders;  
they did not remember Your abundant love,  
but rebelled at the sea, at the Sea of Reeds. ^106-7

**106:8** Yet He saved them, as befits His name,  
to make known His might. ^106-8

**106:9** He sent His blast against the Sea of Reeds;  
it became dry;  
He led them through the deep as through a wilderness. ^106-9

**106:10** He delivered them from the foe,  
redeemed them from the enemy. ^106-10

**106:11** Water covered their adversaries;  
not one of them was left. ^106-11

**106:12** Then they believed His promise,  
and sang His praises. ^106-12

**106:13** But they soon forgot His deeds;  
they would not wait to learn His plan. ^106-13

**106:14** They were seized with craving in the wilderness,  
and put God to the test in the wasteland. ^106-14

**106:15** He gave them what they asked for,  
then made them waste away. ^106-15

**106:16** There was envy of Moses in the camp,  
and of Aaron, the holy one of the LORD. ^106-16

**106:17** The earth opened up and swallowed Dathan,  
closed over the party of Abiram. ^106-17

**106:18** A fire blazed among their party,  
a flame that consumed the wicked. ^106-18

**106:19** They made a calf at Horeb  
and bowed down to a molten image. ^106-19

**106:20** They exchanged their glory  
for the image of a bull that feeds on grass. ^106-20

**106:21** They forgot God who saved them,  
who performed great deeds in Egypt, ^106-21

**106:22** wondrous deeds in the land of Ham,  
awesome deeds at the Sea of Reeds. ^106-22

**106:23** He would have destroyed them  
had not Moses His chosen one  
confronted Him in the breach  
to avert His destructive wrath. ^106-23

**106:24** They rejected the desirable land,  
and put no faith in His promise. ^106-24

**106:25** They grumbled in their tents  
and disobeyed the LORD. ^106-25

**106:26** So He raised His hand in oath  
to make them fall in the wilderness, ^106-26

**106:27** to disperse[^1] their offspring among the nations  
and scatter them through the lands. ^106-27

**106:28** They attached themselves to Baal Peor,  
ate sacrifices offered to the dead. ^106-28

**106:29** They provoked anger by their deeds,  
and a plague broke out among them. ^106-29

**106:30** Phinehas stepped forth and intervened,  
and the plague ceased. ^106-30

**106:31** It was reckoned to his merit  
for all generations, to eternity. ^106-31

**106:32** They provoked wrath at the waters of Meribah  
and Moses suffered on their account, ^106-32

**106:33** because they rebelled against Him  
and he spoke rashly. ^106-33

**106:34** They did not destroy the nations  
as the LORD had commanded them, ^106-34

**106:35** but mingled with the nations  
and learned their ways. ^106-35

**106:36** They worshiped their idols,  
which became a snare for them. ^106-36

**106:37** Their own sons and daughters  
they sacrificed to demons. ^106-37

**106:38** They shed innocent blood,  
the blood of their sons and daughters,  
whom they sacrificed to the idols of Canaan;  
so the land was polluted with bloodguilt. ^106-38

**106:39** Thus they became defiled by their acts,  
debauched through their deeds. ^106-39

**106:40** The LORD was angry with His people  
and He abhorred His inheritance. ^106-40

**106:41** He handed them over to the nations;  
their foes ruled them. ^106-41

**106:42** Their enemies oppressed them  
and they were subject to their power. ^106-42

**106:43** He saved them time and again,  
but they were deliberately rebellious,  
and so they were brought low by their iniquity. ^106-43

**106:44** When He saw that they were in distress,  
when He heard their cry, ^106-44

**106:45** He was mindful of His covenant  
and in His great faithfulness relented. ^106-45

**106:46** He made all their captors kindly disposed toward them. ^106-46

**106:47** Deliver us, O LORD our God,  
and gather us from among the nations,  
to acclaim Your holy name,  
to glory in Your praise. ^106-47

**106:48** Blessed is the LORD, God of Israel,  
From eternity to eternity.  
Let all the people say, “Amen.”  
Hallelujah. ^106-48

[^1]: Cf. Targum, Kimhi.
