## Who shall abide in God’s sanctuary?

*A Psalm of David.*

**15:1** O LORD, who may abide in your tent? Who may dwell on your holy hill? ^15-1

**15:2** Those who walk blamelessly and do what is right and speak the truth from their heart; ^15-2

**15:3** who do not slander with their tongue and do no evil to their friends nor heap shame upon their neighbors; ^15-3

**15:4** in whose eyes the wicked are despised but who honor those who fear the LORD; who stand by their oath even to their hurt; ^15-4

**15:5** who do not lend money at interest and do not take a bribe against the innocent. Those who do these things shall never be moved. ^15-5
