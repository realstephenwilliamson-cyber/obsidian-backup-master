## Mūla

अन्योऽपि कश्चन जनः शिवशक्तिपात-
संप्रेरणापरवशस्वकशक्तिसार्थः ।
अभ्यर्थनाविमुखभावमशिक्षितेन
तेनाप्यनुग्रहपदं कृत एष वर्गः ॥ ^mula

anyo'pi kaścana janaḥ śivaśaktipāta-
saṃpreraṇāparavaśasvakaśaktisārthaḥ |
abhyarthanāvimukhabhāvamaśikṣitena
tenāpyanugrahapadaṃ kṛta eṣa vargaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 11 - Chapter Thirty-Seven, part 1#^pg277|p. 259]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=277>))
>
> (Indeed,) any other person (who came to him) possessing (spiritual) power of his own, helplessly impelled by a descent of Śiva’s power, he made a locus (of Śiva’s) grace, for he had not been taught to be averse to refusing a (sincere) request.
