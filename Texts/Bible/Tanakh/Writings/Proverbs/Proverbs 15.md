# Proverbs 15

← [[Proverbs 14]] · [[Tanakh]] · [[Proverbs 16]] →

Compare: [[NRSVue Proverbs 15|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 15

**15:1** A gentle response allays wrath;  
A harsh word provokes anger. ^15-1

**15:2** The tongue of the wise produces much knowledge,  
But the mouth of dullards pours out folly. ^15-2

**15:3** The eyes of the LORD are everywhere,  
Observing the bad and the good. ^15-3

**15:4** A healing tongue is a tree of life,  
But a devious one makes for a broken spirit. ^15-4

**15:5** A fool spurns the discipline of his father,  
But one who heeds reproof becomes clever. ^15-5

**15:6** In the house of the righteous there is much treasure,  
But in the harvest of the wicked there is trouble. ^15-6

**15:7** The lips of the wise disseminate knowledge;  
Not so the minds of dullards. ^15-7

**15:8** The sacrifice of the wicked is an abomination to the LORD,  
But the prayer of the upright pleases Him. ^15-8

**15:9** The way of the wicked is an abomination to the LORD,  
But He loves him who pursues righteousness. ^15-9

**15:10** Discipline seems bad to him who forsakes the way;  
He who spurns reproof will die. ^15-10

**15:11** Sheol and Abaddon lie exposed to the LORD,  
How much more the minds of men! ^15-11

**15:12** The scoffer dislikes being reproved;  
He will not resort to the wise. ^15-12

**15:13** A joyful heart makes a cheerful face;  
A sad heart makes a despondent mood. ^15-13

**15:14** The mind of a prudent man seeks knowledge;  
The mouth of the dullard pursues folly. ^15-14

**15:15** All the days of a poor man are wretched,  
But contentment is a feast without end. ^15-15

**15:16** Better a little with fear of the LORD  
Than great wealth with confusion. ^15-16

**15:17** Better a meal of vegetables where there is love  
Than a fattened ox where there is hate. ^15-17

**15:18** A hot-tempered man provokes a quarrel;  
A patient man calms strife. ^15-18

**15:19** The way of a lazy man is like a hedge of thorns,  
But the path of the upright is paved. ^15-19

**15:20** A wise son makes his father happy;  
A fool of a man humiliates his mother. ^15-20

**15:21** Folly is joy to one devoid of sense;  
A prudent man walks a straight path. ^15-21

**15:22** Plans are foiled for want of counsel,  
But they succeed through many advisers. ^15-22

**15:23** A ready response is a joy to a man,  
And how good is a word rightly timed! ^15-23

**15:24** For an intelligent man the path of life leads upward,  
In order to avoid Sheol below. ^15-24

**15:25** The LORD will tear down the house of the proud,  
But He will establish the homestead of the widow. ^15-25

**15:26** Evil thoughts are an abomination to the LORD,  
But pleasant words are pure. ^15-26

**15:27** He who pursues ill-gotten gain makes trouble for his household;  
He who spurns gifts will live long. ^15-27

**15:28** The heart[^1] of the righteous man rehearses his answer,  
But the mouth of the wicked blurts out evil things. ^15-28

**15:29** The LORD is far from the wicked,  
But He hears the prayer of the righteous. ^15-29

**15:30** What brightens the eye gladdens the heart;  
Good news puts fat on the bones. ^15-30

**15:31** He whose ear heeds the discipline of life  
Lodges among the wise. ^15-31

**15:32** He who spurns discipline hates himself;  
He who heeds reproof gains understanding. ^15-32

**15:33** The fear of the LORD is the discipline of wisdom;  
Humility precedes honor. ^15-33

[^1]: For leb as a source of speech, see note to Eccl. 5.1.
