## Mūla

मुक्तौ च देहे ब्रह्माद्याः षडधिष्ठानकारिणः ।
तस्यान्ते तु परा देवी यत्र युक्तो न जायते ॥ ^mula

muktau ca dehe brahmādyāḥ ṣaḍadhiṣṭhānakāriṇaḥ |
tasyānte tu parā devī yatra yukto na jāyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 12 - Chapter Six, part 5#^pg413|p. 398]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=413>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 6.191cd–192ab.
>
> (It is said in this scripture that) Brahmā and the rest preside over (adhiṣthānakārin) the six conditions (of the body and soul), namely, infancy, youth, maturity, death, rebirth, and liberation, respectively. (with 6.190cd–191ab)
>
> The Supreme Goddess (Parā Devī) is at the end of this (process,) where one who is conjoined (to Her) is not born (again). Just by knowing this alone, one can dispense grace and initiation. (with 6.191cd–192ab)
