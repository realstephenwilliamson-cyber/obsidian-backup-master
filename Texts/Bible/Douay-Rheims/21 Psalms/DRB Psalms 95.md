# Psalms 95 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 94|← Psalms 94]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 96|Psalms 96 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 95|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**95:1** A canticle for David himself, when the house was built after the captivity. Sing ye to the Lord a new canticle: sing to the Lord, all the earth. ^95-1

**95:2** Sing ye to the Lord and bless his name: shew forth his salvation from day to day. ^95-2

**95:3** Declare his glory among the Gentiles: his wonders among all people. ^95-3

**95:4** For the Lord is great, and exceedingly to be praised: he is to be feared above all gods. ^95-4

**95:5** For all the gods of the Gentiles are devils: but the Lord made the heavens. ^95-5

**95:6** Praise and beauty are before him: holiness and majesty in his sanctuary. ^95-6

**95:7** Bring ye to the Lord, O ye kindreds of the Gentiles, bring ye to the Lord glory and honour: ^95-7

**95:8** Bring to the Lord glory unto his name. Bring up sacrifices, and come into his courts: ^95-8

**95:9** Adore ye the Lord in his holy court. Let all the earth be moved at his presence. ^95-9

**95:10** Say ye among the Gentiles, the Lord hath reigned. For he hath corrected the world, which shall not be moved: he will judge the people with justice. ^95-10

**95:11** Let the heavens rejoice, and let the earth be glad, let the sea be moved, and the fulness thereof: ^95-11

**95:12** The fields and all things that are in them shall be joyful. Then shall all the trees of the woods rejoice ^95-12

**95:13** Before the face of the Lord, because he cometh: because he cometh to judge the earth. He shall judge the world with justice, and the people with his truth. ^95-13
