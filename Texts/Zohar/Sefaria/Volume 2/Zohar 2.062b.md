# Zohar 2.062b

← [[Zohar 2.062a]] · [[Zohar (Soncino)]] · [[Zohar 2.063a]] →

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 4.06 - Parashat Be-Shallah#^z2-62b|Parashat Be-Shallaḥ, at 2:62b]]

*Volume 2, folio 62b* · Beshalach

*Passage Beshalach 28:388 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 2.062a#^beshalach-28-388|Beshalach 28:388]].*

**Beshalach 28:389** For the Lord dispenses favour (*rozeh eth*) to them that fear Him, and those who fear Him are those “that wait for His mercy”, that is, who are entirely dependent on Him for their daily bread.’ ^beshalach-28-389

**Beshalach 28:390** R. Jesse the Elder never used to prepare his meal before praying for it. He was wont to say: ‘Let us ask the King first!’ Then he would wait for some time and say: ‘The time has arrived for the King to give us food; prepare the meal!’ This is the way of those who fear the Lord and are afraid of sin. ^beshalach-28-390

**Beshalach 28:391** As for the ungodly, it is not so with them, for their ways are crooked: “Woe unto them that rise up early in the morning, that they may follow strong drink” (Isa. 5, 11). But “The Lord taketh pleasure… in those that wait for His mercy”; and herein are the sons of faith different from others. Hence it is written: “The people shall gather a certain rate every day in his day, that I may prove them whether they will walk in my Torah or not.” ^beshalach-28-391

**Beshalach 28:392** The peculiar expression “in his day” indicates that the sons of faith are known by their “daily” walking in the straight way of the Torah. R. Isaac found the same truth in the following verse: “The righteous eateth to the satisfaction of his soul” (Prov. 13, 25), which he interpreted to mean: “The righteous has his meal only after he has satisfied his soul with prayer and study.” ^beshalach-28-392

**Beshalach 28:393** Said R. Simeon: ‘Observe that before the Holy One gave Israel the Torah He tried them to see who would be a son of faith and who would not be. How did He try them? By the manna. All those who were found to be sons of faith were signed with the sign of the crown of Grace by the Holy One Himself; and from those who were not found to be thus, this supernal crown was withheld. ^beshalach-28-393

**Beshalach 28:394** It has been said at that hour Israel was perfected below according to her prototype above, for it is written, “and they came to Elim, where were twelve wells of water and threescore and ten palm trees” (Ex. 15, 27). Now the Holy Tree[^1] spreads to twelve boundaries on the four quarters of the earth, and to seventy branches closely intertwined, so that what was above should have here its counterpart below. ^beshalach-28-394

**Beshalach 28:395** At that hour holy dew dropped down from the Hidden Most Ancient One and filled the head of the Lesser Countenance, the place which is called “Heaven”. From this dew of the supernal holy light the manna descended, and in so doing dispersed itself into flakes and became solidified “as thin as the hoar frost on the ground” (Ex. 16, 14). ^beshalach-28-395

**Beshalach 28:396** And all the sons of the Faith went out, gathered it, and praised the Holy Name for it. The manna diffused the scents of all the spices of the Garden of Eden, through which it had passed in descending. Each one found in the manna the taste he most desired; and as he ate he blessed the Supernal Holy King for His goodness, and was himself blessed with understanding of the Supernal Wisdom. Therefore that generation was called “the generation of knowledge”. ^beshalach-28-396

**Beshalach 28:397** These were the sons of Faith, and to them was given the privilege of contemplating and comprehending the holy Torah. ^beshalach-28-397

**Beshalach 28:398** But of those who were not truly faithful it is written, “and the people roamed about (*shatu*) and gathered (the manna)” (Num. 11, 8). The word “*shatu*” (roamed about) indicates that these people allowed “stupidity” (*shatuta*) to enter into them, because they were not sons of Faith. ^beshalach-28-398

[^1]: *Tifereth*.
