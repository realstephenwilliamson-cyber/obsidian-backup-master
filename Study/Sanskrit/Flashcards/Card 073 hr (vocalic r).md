# हृ hṛ

**Card 73 of 108** (printed: Card 3-13 · Alpha 101) · consonant + vowel

## Front

- **Glyph:** हृ
- **Example:** हृदय

> [!note]- Back
> - **Pronunciation:** As in ‘hṛdaya’ (heart)
> - **root:** ‘to take away/carry/remove’
> - **Class:** short syllable · This is the only time ṛ is not placed below the letter.
