# LXX Psalms 87

← [[LXX Psalms 86]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 88]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 088]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 088]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**87:1** A song of a Psalm for the sons of Core for the end, upon Maeleth for responsive *strains*, of instruction for Æman the Israelite.[^1]  ^87-1

**87:2** O Lord God of my salvation, I have cried by day and in the night before thee.  ^87-2

**87:3** Let my prayer come in before thee; incline thine ear to my supplication, O Lord.  ^87-3

**87:4** For my soul is filled with troubles, and my life has drawn nigh to Hades.  ^87-4

**87:5** I have been reckoned with them that go down to the pit; I became as a man without help;  ^87-5

**87:6** free among the dead, as the slain ones cast out, who sleep in the tomb; whom thou rememberest no more; and they are rejected from thy hand.[^2]  ^87-6

**87:7** They laid me in the lowest pit, in dark *places*, and in the shadow of death.  ^87-7

**87:8** Thy wrath has pressed heavily upon me, and thou hast brought upon me all thy billows. Pause.  ^87-8

**87:9** Thou hast removed my acquaintance far from me; they have made me an abomination to themselves; I have been delivered up, and have not gone forth.  ^87-9

**87:10** Mine eyes are dimmed from poverty; but I cried to thee, O Lord, all the day; I spread forth my hands to thee.  ^87-10

**87:11** Wilt thou work wonders for the dead? or shall physicians raise *them* up, that they shall praise thee?[^3]  ^87-11

**87:12** Shall any one declare thy mercy in the tomb? and thy truth in destruction?  ^87-12

**87:13** Shall thy wonders be known in darkness? and thy righteousness in a forgotten land?  ^87-13

**87:14** But I cried to thee, O Lord; and in the morning shall my prayer prevent thee.  ^87-14

**87:15** Wherefore, O Lord, dost thou reject my prayer, *and* turn thy face away from me?[^4]  ^87-15

**87:16** I am poor and in troubles from my youth; and having been exalted, I was brought low and into despair.[^5]  ^87-16

**87:17** Thy wrath has passed over me; and thy terrors have greatly disquieted me.  ^87-17

**87:18** They compassed me like water; all the day they beset me together.  ^87-18

**87:19** Thou hast put far from me *every* friend, and mine acquaintances because of *my* wretchedness.[^6]  ^87-19

[^1]: 87:1 *Gr.* understanding.
[^2]: 87:6 *Alex.* om. ἐῤῥιμμένοι, cast out.
[^3]: 87:11 See *Heb.*
[^4]: 87:15 *Heb.* and *Alex.* soul.
[^5]: 87:16 See 2 Cor. 4. 8.
[^6]: 87:19 *Alex.* friend and neighbour.
