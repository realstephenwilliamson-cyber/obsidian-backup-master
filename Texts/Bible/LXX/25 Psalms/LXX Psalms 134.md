# LXX Psalms 134

← [[LXX Psalms 133]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 135]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 135]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 135]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**134:1** Alleluia. Praise ye the name of the Lord; praise the Lord, *ye his* servants,  ^134-1

**134:2** who stand in the house of the Lord, in the courts of the house of our God.  ^134-2

**134:3** Praise ye the Lord; for the Lord is good: sing praises to his name; for *it is* good.  ^134-3

**134:4** For the Lord has chosen Jacob for himself, *and* Israel for his peculiar treasure.  ^134-4

**134:5** For I know that the Lord is great, and our Lord is above all gods;  ^134-5

**134:6** all that the Lord willed, he did in heaven, and on the earth, in the sea, and in all deeps.  ^134-6

**134:7** Who brings up clouds from the extremity of the earth: he has made lightnings for the rain: he brings winds out of his treasures.  ^134-7

**134:8** Who smote the first-born of Egypt, both of man and beast.  ^134-8

**134:9** He sent signs and wonders into the midst of thee, O Egypt, on Pharao, and on all his servants.  ^134-9

**134:10** Who smote many nations, and slew mighty kings;  ^134-10

**134:11** Seon king of the Amorites, and Og king of Basan, and all the kingdoms of Chanaan:  ^134-11

**134:12** and gave their land *for* an inheritance, an inheritance to Israel his people.  ^134-12

**134:13** O Lord, thy name *endures* for ever, and thy memorial to all generations.  ^134-13

**134:14** For the Lord shall judge his people, and comfort himself concerning his servants.  ^134-14

**134:15** The idols of the heathen are silver and gold, the works of men's hands.  ^134-15

**134:16** They have a mouth, but they cannot speak; they have eyes, but they cannot see;  ^134-16

**134:17** they have ears, but they cannot hear; for there is no breath in their mouth.  ^134-17

**134:18** Let those who make them be made like to them; and all those who trust in them.  ^134-18

**134:19** O house of Israel, bless ye the Lord: O house of Aaron, bless ye the Lord:  ^134-19

**134:20** O house of Levi, bless ye the Lord: ye that fear the Lord, bless the Lord.  ^134-20

**134:21** Blessed in Sion be the Lord, who dwells in Jerusalem.  ^134-21
