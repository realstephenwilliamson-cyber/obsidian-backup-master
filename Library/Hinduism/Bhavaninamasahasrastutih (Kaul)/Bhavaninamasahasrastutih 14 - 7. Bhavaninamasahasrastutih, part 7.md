AEAT Svacchandā 592 The deity of spontaneous will.

The truth, that the transcendent reality of Paramasiva is, is known to the world by the power of creation etc. called Parā Śakti. Freedom is spontaneity of will and not its limitation towards fulfilling of a desired object, which is commonly said to be an enjoyment. Spontanegus activity or Kriyā is possible only when there is no limited or particular desire. There is no motive or cause, on the part of Śiva in creation. Śakti being synonymous to Śiva, is really the way of understanding Śiva (or consciousness). When consciousness is free and full, it is natural of it to overflow in creative activity, just as it is natural for a child to burst into playing. Parā Sakti is, therefore, this spontaneous will which is the state of desirelessness

or perfection.
