### 5.1–8: Community purity

*[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 05#^5-1|Text]], p. 329* · Chapters 5–8 clarify how the “ekklēsia” should maintain its purity, addressing particular concerns raised in a letter Paul received from members of the Corinthian assemblies. ^5-1

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 05#^5-1|1]]:** *Sexual immorality* (Gk “porneia”) . . . *among pagans*, Paul scolds the Corinthian community for tolerat-ing behavior that even idolaters would condemn; on associating sexual immorality with idolatry see, e.g., [[Ezekiel 06#^6-9|Ezek 6.9]]; [[Ezekiel 23#^23-37|23.37]]; [[Romans 01#^1-14|Rom 1.14–27]]; *Sib. Or.* 3.37–8. *Living with his father’s wife*, Roman (Gaius, *Inst*. 1.63) and Jewish Law ([[Torah 29 - Achrei Mot#^18-7|Lev 18.7–8]]; Philo, *Spec. Laws* 3.20–28; Pseudo-Phocylides, *Sent*. 179; 11Q19 66.12; *m. Ker*. 1.1) prohibited sexual relations between stepson and stepmother. ^5-1-2

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 05#^5-2|2]]:** *Arrogant*, see 4.6n. *Mourned*, over the punishment for sin ([[Ezra 10#^10-6|Ezra 10.6]]; [[Nehemiah 01#^1-4|Neh 1.4]]). *Removed*, expelled ([[Torah 48 - Shoftim#^17-7|Deut 17.7]], quoted in v 13), see [[Ezra 07#^7-26|Ezra 7.26]]; [[Ezra 10#^10-8|10.8–44]]; [[Matthew 18#^18-15|Mt 18.15–18]]; [[1 Timothy 01#^1-18|1 Tim 1.18–20]]; 1QS 7.1–2. ^5-2

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 05#^5-3|3–4]]:** Paul “in absentia” convicts the sinner. ^5-3

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 05#^5-5|5]]:** *Hand this man over*, the community must abandon him to his sinful ways. *Satan*, (see [[1 Corinthians 07#^7-5|1 Cor 7.5]]; [[2 Corinthians 12#^12-17|2 Cor 12.17]]; [[1 Timothy 01#^1-20|1 Tim 1.20]]), lit., “the *satan*,” God’s adversarial agent ([[Job 02#^2-6|Job 2.6]]; [[LXX Sirach 21#^21-27|Sir 21.27]]; 11QPsa 19.15). In rabbinic literature, Satan preys on human weakness and prosecutes sinners in the heavenly court (*b*. *Git*. 52a; *Pesiq*. *Rab*. 185b–186a); in Enochic literature, he is the angel whose minions torture the bodies of lusting sinners to purify their souls in preparation for the last judgment, when he will destroy the unrepentant (*1 En.* 53.3; 67.7–13). Some rabbinic literature also envisions the fires of Gehinnom (hell) purifying sinners’ souls (*b. Rosh Ha-Shanah* [[Rosh Hashanah 16#16b|16b]]–17a). *Destruction of the flesh*, the sinner will experience bodily decay preceding the *day of the Lord*. *Saved*, rescued from condemnation. *Day of the Lord*, see 1.8n.; 3.13. ^5-5

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 05#^5-6|6]]:** *Boasting*, see 1.29n.; 4.7. *Leavens*, that which puffs up or causes *boasting* ([[Hosea 07#^7-4|Hos 7.4]]; [[Matthew 16#^16-6|Mt 16.6]]; [[Galatians 05#^5-9|Gal 5.9]]; *Mek*. *Beshalah* 3 on [[Torah 16 - Beshalach#^14-11|Ex 14.11]]). ^5-6

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 05#^5-7|7–8]]:** *Clean out*, removing leaven in preparation for Passover ([[Torah 15 - Bo#^12-15|Ex 12.15]]; [[Torah 15 - Bo#^13-7|13.7]]). *Unleavened*, evoking the bread eaten at Passover with the *paschal lamb*, interpreted as Christ *sacrificed* at Passover (see [[Torah 15 - Bo#^12-8|Ex 12.8]],[[Torah 15 - Bo#^12-21|21]]; [[John 01#^1-29|Jn 1.29]]; [[Acts 08#^8-32|Acts 8.32]]; [[1 Peter 01#^1-19|1 Pet 1.19]]; [[Revelation 05#^5-6|Rev 5.6]]). *Festival*, the communal meal of the “ekklēsia” (see *2 En*. 68.9). *Old yeast*, a metaphor for evil (*b. Ber*. [[Berakhot 17#17a|17a]]). ^5-7

### 5.9–13: Eschewing evil

*[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 05#^5-9|Text]], p. 330* ^5-9

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 05#^5-9|9]]:** This *letter* is no longer extant; parts may be contained in 2 Cor. See 7.1. *Not to associate*, banish sexual violators from the community and shun them (see [[Torah 29 - Achrei Mot#^18-29|Lev 18.29]]; [[Psalms 001#^1-1|Ps 1.1–2]]; 101). *Sexually immoral*, see 5.1n. ^5-9-2

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 05#^5-11|11]]:** *Eat*, table fellowship includes only those who maintain their holy status (Josephus, *J.W.* 2.129–33; 1QS 6.2–5,16–17,20–21 vs. [[Mark 02#^2-16|Mk 2.16]]; see “Food and Table Fellowship,” p. 650). Vice lists are common ways of characterizing sinners (e.g., 6.9–10; [[Jeremiah 07#^7-9|Jer 7.9]]; [[Ezekiel 18#^18-11|Ezek 18.11–13]]; [[Hosea 04#^4-2|Hos 4.2]]; [[LXX Wisdom of Solomon 14#^14-24|Wis 14.24–27]]; [[Mark 07#^7-21|Mk 7.21–23]]; [[Romans 01#^1-29|Rom 1.29–31]]; [[2 Corinthians 12#^12-20|2 Cor 12.20–21]]; [[Galatians 05#^5-19|Gal 5.19–21]]; Philo, *Sacr*. 5 \[32\];1 QS 4.9–11; *Apoc*. *Abr*. 24.6–25.3; *1 En.* 10.20; 91.6–7; *Jub*. 7.20–21; 21.21; 23.14). ^5-11

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 05#^5-12|12–13]]:** *Inside*, believers only judge other insiders. Judgment of *those outside* is God’s purview (see 5.3–4n.). Paul quotes [[Torah 47 - Re'eh#^13-6|Deut 13.5]]; 17.7 and alludes to other passages (regarding idolatry and other misdeeds: [[Torah 48 - Shoftim#^19-19|Deut 19.19]]; [[Torah 49 - Ki Teitzei#^21-21|21.21]]; [[Torah 49 - Ki Teitzei#^22-21|22.21]],[[Torah 49 - Ki Teitzei#^22-24|24]]; [[Torah 49 - Ki Teitzei#^24-7|24.7]]) to justify expulsion for immoral behavior. ^5-12
