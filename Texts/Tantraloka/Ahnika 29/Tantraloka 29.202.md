## Mūla

न्यस्येच्छिखान्तं पतति तेनात्रेदृक् क्रमो भवेत् ।
प्रोक्षितस्य शिशोर्न्यस्तप्रोक्तशोध्याध्वपद्धतेः ॥ ^mula

nyasyecchikhāntaṃ patati tenātredṛk kramo bhavet |
prokṣitasya śiśornyastaproktaśodhyādhvapaddhateḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 12 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 4#^pg378|p. 361]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=378>))
>
> It is said in the venerable Ratnamālā that (the teacher) should deposit (the Mālinī alphabet,) beginning with N and ending with PH and burning with radiant light, up to the topknot (of his disciple). Due to that, (the disciple) falls (possessed onto the ground). The procedure (krama) here (in this case) is such (as follows). (with 29.201cd–202ab)
>
> The teacher should visualize how the energy, with which he is one (tanmaya), well energized (saṁdīptā), (travels through the disciple), whose body is erect, from the feet up and comes to the top of (his) head, burning away (as it does so) the fetters of the disciple who has been sprinkled (with consecrated water), and on whom the path that needs to be purified, described previously, has been deposited. (with 29.202cd–203)
