*Volume 3, folio 154a* · Beha'alotcha

*Passage Beha'alotcha 17:97 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 3.153b#^beha-alotcha-17-97|Beha'alotcha 17:97]].*

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Beha'alotcha 17:98.*

**Beha'alotcha 17:99** [^1] R. Abba said: ‘Every time the tabernacle was set up by the hands of men there was a day of universal joy, and the sacred Oil was poured into the lamps, and these all shed their light abroad, and those who brought this about won redemption for themselves in this world and life in the next. Thus “righteousness delivereth from death”.’ ^beha-alotcha-17-99

**Beha'alotcha 18:100** [^2]MAKE UNTO THEE TWO TRUMPETS OF SILVER, ETC. R. Simeon adduced here the verse: “And when the *Hayoth* (living creatures) went, the wheels went hard by them; and when *the Hayoth* were lifted up from the bottom the wheels were lifted up” (Ezek. 1, 19). ‘The *Hayoth*’, he said, ‘are borne along by the supernal power; ^beha-alotcha-18-100

**Beha'alotcha 18:101** and so were the movements of the tribes below who bore on their standards the likenesses of the *Hayoth*, that of Lion, Eagle, Ox, Man. Angels attended each of the standards. ^beha-alotcha-18-101

**Beha'alotcha 18:102** The first standard bore the likeness of Lion and was attended by Michael, who had under him two chieftains, Zophiel and Zadkiel. ^beha-alotcha-18-102

**Beha'alotcha 18:103** When these set out numerous armed hosts moved in unison on the right-hand side whilst the sun on the left illumined them. ^beha-alotcha-18-103

**Beha'alotcha 18:104** The Lion put forth his right hand and summoned to himself all his hosts, to wit, three hundred and seventy thousand lions, and they all assembled round him. ^beha-alotcha-18-104

**Beha'alotcha 18:105** When this Lion roars all the firmaments and all their hosts and legions quiver and shake. The Fiery River blazes forth and sinks a thousand and five hundred stages to the lower Gehinnom. Then all the sinners in the Gehinnom shake and tremble and burn in the fire. So Scripture says: “The lion hath roared, who will not fear?” (Amos 3, 8). ^beha-alotcha-18-105

**Beha'alotcha 18:106** A second roar he emits, which is taken up by his entourage of three hundred and seventy thousand lions. Then he puts forth his left hand, when all the “masters of Rigour” here below are seized with fear and are bowed down underneath that hand. So we read: “Thy hand shall be on the neck of thy enemies” (Gen. 49, 8). ^beha-alotcha-18-106

**Beha'alotcha 18:107** Each of the *Hayoth* had four wings formed of white flaming fire, ^beha-alotcha-18-107

**Beha'alotcha 18:108** as well as four faces turned towards the four cardinal points, all illumined by the white light of the sun; the one turned to the east was illumined with a joyous light, the one towards the west with a concentrated light, the one towards the north was within the penumbra of the sun. ^beha-alotcha-18-108

**Beha'alotcha 18:109** These contained three groups.[^3] One numbered seventy-four thousand and six hundred. These were of the higher grades. There were, besides, a long succession of lower grades. These were innumerable. ^beha-alotcha-18-109

**Beha'alotcha 18:110** The second group contained fifty-four thousand and four hundred besides those of the lower grades, who were innumerable. The third group, which followed behind, contained fifty-seven thousand and four hundred. ^beha-alotcha-18-110

[^1]: The following passage does not appear in our Hebrew text.
[^2]: The first 10 lines of the Hebrew text are not found in our translation.
[^3]: Corresponding to the three tribes under each standard.
