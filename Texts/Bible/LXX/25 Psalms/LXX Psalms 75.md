# LXX Psalms 75

← [[LXX Psalms 74]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 76]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 076]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 076]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**75:1** For the end, among the Hymns, a Psalm for Asaph; a Song for the Assyrian.  ^75-1

**75:2** God is known in Judea; his name is great in Israel.  ^75-2

**75:3** And his place has been in peace, and his dwelling-place in Sion.[^1]  ^75-3

**75:4** There he broke the power of the bows, the shield, and the sword, and the battle. Pause.  ^75-4

**75:5** Thou dost wonderfully shine forth from the everlasting mountains.  ^75-5

**75:6** All the simple ones in heart were troubled; all the men of wealth have slept their sleep, and have found nothing in their hands.  ^75-6

**75:7** At thy rebuke, O God of Jacob, the riders on horses slumbered.  ^75-7

**75:8** Thou art terrible; and who shall withstand thee, because of thine anger?  ^75-8

**75:9** Thou didst cause judgment to be heard from heaven; the earth feared, and was still,  ^75-9

**75:10** when God arose to judgment, to save all the meek in heart. Pause.  ^75-10

**75:11** For the inward thought of man shall give thanks to thee: and the memorial of his inward thought shall keep a feast to thee.  ^75-11

**75:12** Vow, and pay *your vows* to the Lord our God; all that are round about him shall bring gifts, *even* to him that is terrible,  ^75-12

**75:13** and that takes away the spirits of princes; to him that is terrible among the kings of the earth.  ^75-13

[^1]: 75:3 *Or,* Salem, see the Hebrew.
