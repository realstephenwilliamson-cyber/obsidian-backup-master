# Uḍḍāmareśvaratantra — Chapter 8

[[Uddamaresvaratantra 07|← Chapter 7]] · [[Uddamaresvaratantra|← index]] · [[Uddamaresvaratantra 09|Chapter 9 →]]

athānyat sampravakṣyāmi nārīṇāṃ garbhadhāraṇam

^s1

padmabījaṃ gavyapayasā saha yā narī pibati sā garbhavatī bhavati satyam eva ādityavāre nimantrayet candravāre bhakṣayet

^s2

pūrvadigbhāgasthitaṃ śarīṣamūlaṃ gavyaghṛtena saha ṛtusamaye bhakṣayet sā saṃvatsareṇa garbhavatī bhavati

^s3

anapatyā ca yā narī kapitthaṃ bhakṣayet sadā

^s4

abhimantrya tu mantreṇa sāpi putravatī bhavet

^s5

ekavṛkṣataṭe nārī snānaṃ kṛtvābhimantrayet

^s6

ādivandhyāpi deveśi bhaved garbhavatī hi sā

^s7

devaḥ śivo bhaved yatra nadīsaṅgam asaṃnidhau

^s8

tasyāṃ nadyāṃ diśi svāhā vandhyā putravatī bhavet

^s9

vidhir atrocyate kapilāgomayenātha bhūmiṃ saṃlipya yatnataḥ

^s10

snātvā vidhiprakāreṇa maṇḍalaṃ kārayet tataḥ

^s11

caturasraṃ catuṣkoṇaṃ tanmadhye vartulaṃ smṛtam

^s12

tanmadhye vilikhet paścād aṣṭapattraṃ sakarṇikam

^s13

madhye tu pūjayed devaṃ pattre śaktiṃ prapūjayet

^s14

nirvraṇamaṇḍalasyāgre kamalaṃ sthāpayed budhaḥ

^s15

śikhipattranakhavarṇena likhet

^s16

bhṛṅgatā kulasaptaparvatamṛttikā [... au5 Zeichenjh] svamalāmṛttikāvalmīkamṛttikānimbamūlamṛttikā suvarṇarajatatāmrakāṃsyasahasramūlasarvatīrthāni samudrāḥ saritaḥ sarvāḥ sarvauṣadhayaḥ sarvadevatāḥ sarvasiddhayaḥ sarvayoginyaḥ sarve girayaḥ sarve nāgāḥ pañcajātyāni phalāni pañcaprakārākṣatāni pañca suvarṇapuṣpāṇi sthiracittena mantritakalaśe parikalpayet

^s17

atha mantraḥ kṣaṃ kṣīṃ kṣūṃ kṣaiṃ kṣauṃ kṣaḥ

^s18

anena mantreṇa yā narī vidhānena niratavarjitā abrahmacāriṇī devī vandhyā pañcapativarjitā

^s19

aputrā labhate putrān durbhagā subhagā bhavet

^s20

anenaiva vidhānena kanyā prāpnoti satpatim

^s21

mantrauṣadhīprayogāś ca ye cānye cūrṇakīrṇakāḥ

^s22

navābhicāritāḥ kūrāḥ śuddhadehā bhavanti te

^s23

ye cānye vighnakartāraś caranti bhuvi rākṣasāḥ

^s24

te sarve pralayaṃ yānti satyaṃ devi mayoditam

^s25

sakṛd uccarite mantre mahāpuṇyaṃ prajāyate

^s26

brahmahatyādayo doṣāḥ kṣayaṃ yānti na saṃśayaḥ

^s27

atha prathamopāya ucyate nāgakesara 10 māṣakaṃ gavyaghṛtena sahartusnānadivase pibet

^s28

tadanantaraṃ bhartrā saha rātrau saṃyogaṃ kuryāt

^s29

sā avaśyam eva garbhavatī bhavati

^s30

atha dvitīyopāyaḥ śvetajīrakam 17 māṣamātrakaṃ rātrau mṛnmayapātre jalena saha nirāvaraṇasthāne samagrarātrau sthāpayet

^s31

agnim adivase śilāyāṃ piṣṭvā paryuṣitajalena yā strī ṛtusnānadine pītvā rātrau bhartrā saha saṃyogaṃ kuryād avaśyaṃ sā garbhavatī bhavati

^s32

atha tṛtīyopāyaḥ dakālvadmadīpi 10 māṣakaṃ gavyadugdhena saha yā ṛtusnānadivase pītvā rātrau bhartrā saha saṃyogaṃ kuryāt sā avaśyam eva garbhavatī bhavati

^s33

iti tṛtīyopāyaḥ

^s34

uoṃ ghaṇṭākarṇāya svāhā imaṃ saptadhā japtvā grāme nagare vā praviśet tatra viśiṣṭaṃ bhojanaṃ prāpnoti

^s35

anyac ca bho alla me siddhā anenāṣṭottaraśataṃ japet

^s36

śirīṣamūlamṛdaḥ kṣetrasya catuṣkoṇeṣu mokṣayet tadā śaśakamūṣakavarāhacatuṣpādaprabhṛtīnāṃ mukhabandhanaṃ bhavati

^s37

adhunā sampravakṣyāmi mantrān me phaladāyakāḥ

^s38

ye sidhyati daśānyūnaṃ mantrasādhanamuktidāḥ

^s39

śatrumitrodāsīnasādhyasiddhasya lakṣaṇam

^s40

mantrākṣarāṇi likhitvā sādhakasya tasya yadāpi ca prathamavargākṣaro bhavati tadā mitraṃ dvitīyavargākṣaro bhavati tadā siddhaḥ

^s41

tṛtīyavargasya yadā bhavati tadā sādhyaḥ caturthavargākṣaro bhavati tadodāsīnaḥ pañcamavargākṣaro yadā bhavati tadā śatrur jñātavyaḥ

^s42

etān bhedān jñātvā mantraśodhanam ārabhet tadā sādhakānāṃ sukhāvaho bhavati atha kalpavṛkṣaṣaṇḍamūlāni yāni prakṣālitāni gavyadadhimiśritāyāṃ rājikāyāṃ saṃskāryāṇi

^s43

tato niyamapūrvakaṃ bhakṣayet

^s44

nirvyādhiyogeṣv amuṃ svamukhe niyojayet

^s45

vidhānam asyā bravīmīti devī

^s46

atha mantraḥ uoṃ namaḥ ṣaṇmukhāya śaktihastāya mayūravāhanāya auṣadhīkena dehi me bhava svāhā

^s47

anena mantreṇa caturdaśyāṃ śucir bhūtvā mayūraśikhām utpāṭayet tadā mahāprabhāvayuktā bhavati

^s48

gavyaghṛtena saha saṃgṛhyeta tadā indriyabalo bhavati

^s49

pañcamalena svarṇakāro bhavati

^s50

anyac ca śvetakaravīramūlaśvetagirikarṇikāmūlahevacanāṅbhīkṛtajātāñjalī pañcamalasamāyuktā khāne pāne pradātavyā maraṇāntaṃ vaśīkaraṇam

^s51

anyac ca śvetagirikarṇikāmūlaṃ svavīryeṇa saha svakīyapañcamalaharavīryaśvetārkamūlam etāni hastarkṣe puṣyarkṣe vā ekīkṛtya kumārikāhastābhyāṃ mardayitvā aṣṭamyāṃ caturdaśyāṃ vā gajamadena saha haste guṭikāṃ kārayet

^s52

yasyai ekāpi dīyate sā vaśyā bhavati nānyathā

^s53

śuklapakṣe 'pi sarpāṇāṃ dīyate te sarpā api vaśyā bhavanti śrīmahābhairavasya vaco yathā kuṅkumena saha dīyate tadā vai gajo vaśībhavati

^s54

rocanayā kuṅkumena saha yadā tilakaṃ kriyate tadā sā strī pṛṣṭhalagnā bhramati

^s55

gurudārebhyaḥ tilako na darśayitavyaḥ

^s56

rājadvāre tathā nyāye vivāhe yuddhe jayāvahe trailokyamohanam etat

^s57

triphalā 5 māṣāḥ nimba 1 māṣa kadamba 2 māṣa nīpa 3 māṣa tirāitā 4 māṣa karañja 5 māṣa bhṛṅgarāja 6 māṣa mayūraśikhā 7 māṣāḥ etāni samabhāgāni sūkṣmacūrṇāni kārayet

^s58

tadanantaraṃ madhunā saha peṣayet

^s59

bhojanaṃ ca yathāhāraṃ kurute nātra saṃśayaḥ

^s60

etac cūrṇaṃ surebhyo 'pi durlabham

^s61

rātricūrṇaṃ śirīṣavalkalacūrṇaṃ ca gavyaghṛtena saha yasyai vanitāyai ṛtusnānadivase pānārthaṃ dīyate sā strī vandhyāpi garbhavatī bhavati nātra saṃśayaḥ

^s62

etac cūrṇaṃ kapitthaphalena saha ṛtusamaye aputravatī bhakṣayati sā strī putram āpnoti

^s63

etac cūrṇaṃ śvetakaṅkolīmūlaṃ lakṣmaṇācūrṇaṃ ca samaṃ kṛtvā kuṅkumakvāthena sahartusamaye sadā bhakṣaṇārthaṃ dīyate tadā tasyāḥ śarīraśuddhir bhavati

^s64

paścād ṛtusamayopari pañca dināni bhakṣayet tadā sā garbhadhāraṇakṣamā bhavati nātra saṃśayaḥ

^s65
