## Mūla

चार एकत्र नह्यत्र श्वासप्रश्वासचर्चनम् ।
समानेऽपि तुटेः पूर्वं यावत्षष्ट्यब्दगोचरम् ॥ ^mula

cāra ekatra nahyatra śvāsapraśvāsacarcanam |
samāne'pi tuṭeḥ pūrvaṃ yāvatṣaṣṭyabdagocaram || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 13 - Chapter Six, part 6#^pg435|p. 420]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=435>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 6.209cd–210ab.
>
> In this way, one imagines two years in (one) cycle (cāra) of the Equal Breath (samāna) together in one place. Here (in this case), there is no deliberation on inhalation and exhalation. (with 6.208cd–209ab)
>
> The wise compute within the Equal Breath (samāna) also (all) the measures of time, ranging from one fuṭi to sixty years, (explained) previously, present in a single (phase of its) very subtle motion. (with 6.209cd–210ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* ननु किमत्र वर्षद्वयमेवोदियादुतान्यदपि ? -- इत्याशङ्क्याह --

> समानेऽपि तुटेः पूर्वं यावत्षष्ट्यब्दगोचरम् ॥
> कालसंख्या सुसूक्ष्मैकचारगा गण्यते बुधैः ।

सुसूक्ष्मत्वे हेतुः "एकचारगा"इति । पूर्वं हि तुट्याद्युदयश्चारद्वये प्रोक्तः -- इत्येतदपेक्षया तत्र स्थौल्यं संभाव्यमिति भावः ॥ २०९ ॥
