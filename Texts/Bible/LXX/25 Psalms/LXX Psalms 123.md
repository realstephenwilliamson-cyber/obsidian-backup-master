# LXX Psalms 123

← [[LXX Psalms 122]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 124]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 124]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 124]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**123:1** A Song of Degrees. If it had not been that the Lord was among us, let Israel now say;  ^123-1

**123:2** if it had not been that the Lord was among us, when men rose up against us;  ^123-2

**123:3** verily they would have swallowed us up alive, when their wrath was kindled against us:  ^123-3

**123:4** verily the water would have drowned us, our soul would have gone under the torrent.  ^123-4

**123:5** Yea, our soul would have gone under the overwhelming water.  ^123-5

**123:6** Blessed be the Lord, who has not given us for a prey to their teeth.  ^123-6

**123:7** Our soul has been delivered as a sparrow from the snare of the fowlers: the snare is broken, and we are delivered.  ^123-7

**123:8** Our help is in the name of the Lord, who made heaven and earth.  ^123-8
