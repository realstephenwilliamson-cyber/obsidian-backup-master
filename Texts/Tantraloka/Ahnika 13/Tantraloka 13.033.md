## Mūla

दर्शनाय पुमर्थैकयोग्यतासचिवं धियः ।
आरभ्य सूते धरणीपर्यन्तं तत्र यच्चितः ॥ ^mula

darśanāya pumarthaikayogyatāsacivaṃ dhiyaḥ |
ārabhya sūte dharaṇīparyantaṃ tatra yaccitaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 05 - Chapter Thirteen, part 1#^pg161|p. 141]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=161>))
>
> (The Sārikhyā would object that ignorance) is said to be the absence of insight (adarśana). It is the instrumental cause (that stimulates) fundamental Nature (pradhāna) (which is the pure non-finite objectivity that), in a state of (constant) transformation, (evolves into finite objects). This takes place in conjunction with both the (mistaken) association (saṁkīrṇa) (of the subjectivity of the individual soul with Nature, which is binding,) and (the discernment of their) separation (vaiviktya) (from one another, which is liberating). In order to reveal (this), (prompted by its) disposition (yogyatā), with which it is associated (to act) solely for the benefit of the soul, (Nature) generates (all things,) beginning with the Intellect right up to the Earth. (with 13.32–33)
