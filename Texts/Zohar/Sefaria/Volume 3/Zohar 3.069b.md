*Volume 3, folio 69b* · Achrei Mot

*Passage Achrei Mot 39:235 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 3.069a#^achrei-mot-39-235|Achrei Mot 39:235]].*

**Achrei Mot 40:236** IN THE SEVENTH MONTH ON THE TENTH DAY OF THE MONTH. The allusion of the “tenth” is as we have explained. ^achrei-mot-40-236

**Achrei Mot 40:237** We have learnt that on this day all joy and all illumination and all forgiveness depend on the Supernal Mother from whom issue all springs. Then all the lights shine with glad brightness until all is firmly established, and all judgements are also bathed in light and punishment is not inflicted. ^achrei-mot-40-237

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Achrei Mot 40:238.*

**Achrei Mot 40:239** It is written: “Howbeit (*ach*) on the tenth day of this seventh month is the day of atonement, and ye shall afflict your souls” (Lev. 23, 27). What is the force here of the word *ach* (only)? ^achrei-mot-40-239

**Achrei Mot 40:240** When used in connection with the Passover (Ex. 12, 15) we derive from it the lesson that on half of the day preceding the Passover the eating of leaven is permitted and on half it is forbidden.[^1] Shall we say that here also it teaches that half the day eating is permitted and half forbidden?’ R. Simeon replied: ‘It goes here with the words “ye shall afflict your souls”, and signifies that the real affliction is only in the second half of the day.’ ^achrei-mot-40-240

**Achrei Mot 40:241** FOR ON THIS DAY HE SHALL ATONE FOR YOU. R. Eleazar said: ‘We should expect here, “l shall atone for you”. The “he”, however, signifies that the Jubilee sends forth streams to water and replenish all on this day, and this “for you”, to purify you from all sins, so that judgement should have no power over you.’ ^achrei-mot-40-241

**Achrei Mot 40:242** R. Judah said: ‘Happy are Israel in that God took pleasure in them and sought to purify them so that they might belong to his Palace and dwell therein.’ ^achrei-mot-40-242

**Achrei Mot 41:243** R. Judah cited here the verse: “A song of ascents. Out of the depths have I cried unto thee, O Lord” (Ps. CXXXI). ‘We have learnt’, he said, ‘that when God was about to create man, He consulted the Torah and she warned Him that he would sin before Him and provoke Him. ^achrei-mot-41-243

**Achrei Mot 41:244** Therefore, before creating the world God created Repentance, saying to her: “I am about to create man, on condition that when they return to thee from their sins thou shalt be prepared to forgive their sins and make atonement for them”. Hence at all times Repentance is close at hand to men, and when they repent of their sins it returns to God and makes atonement for all, and judgement is suppressed and all is put right. ^achrei-mot-41-244

**Achrei Mot 41:245** When is a man purified of his sin?’ R. Isaac said: ‘When he returns to the Most High King and prays from the depths of his heart, as it is written, “From the depths I cried unto thee”.’ ^achrei-mot-41-245

[^1]: v. T. B. Pesahim, 28b.
