# Hebrews 3 (NRSVue)

## Moses a servant, Christ a son

**3:1** Therefore, holy brothers and sisters, partners in a heavenly calling, consider Jesus, the apostle and high priest of our confession, ^3-1

**3:2** who was faithful to the one who appointed him, just as Moses also “was faithful in all^[Other ancient authorities lack *all*] God’s^[Gk *his*] house.” ^3-2

**3:3** Yet Jesus^[Gk *this one*] is worthy of more glory than Moses, just as the builder of a house has more honor than the house itself. ^3-3

**3:4** (For every house is built by someone, but the builder of all things is God.) ^3-4

**3:5** Now Moses was faithful in all God’s^[Gk *his*] house as a servant, to testify to the things that would be spoken later. ^3-5

**3:6** Christ, however, was faithful over God’s^[Gk *his*] house as a son, and we are his house if we hold firm^[Other ancient authorities add *to the end*] the boldness and the pride inspired by hope. ^3-6

## Warning against unbelief

**3:7** Therefore, as the Holy Spirit says, “Today, if you hear his voice, ^3-7

**3:8** do not harden your hearts as in the rebellion, as on the day of testing in the wilderness, ^3-8

**3:9** where your ancestors put me to the test,^[Other ancient authorities read *tempted me, tested me*] though they had seen my works ^3-9

**3:10** for forty years. Therefore I was angry with that generation, and I said, ‘They always go astray in their hearts, and they have not known my ways.’ ^3-10

**3:11** As in my anger I swore, ‘They will not enter my rest.’ ” ^3-11

**3:12** Take care, brothers and sisters, that none of you may have an evil, unbelieving heart that turns away from the living God. ^3-12

**3:13** But exhort one another every day, as long as it is called “today,” so that none of you may be hardened by the deceitfulness of sin. ^3-13

**3:14** For we have become partners of Christ, if only we hold our first confidence firm to the end. ^3-14

**3:15** As it is said, “Today, if you hear his voice, do not harden your hearts as in the rebellion.” ^3-15

**3:16** Now who were they who heard and rebelled? Was it not all those who left Egypt under the leadership of Moses? ^3-16

**3:17** And with whom was he angry forty years? Was it not those who sinned, whose bodies fell in the wilderness? ^3-17

**3:18** And to whom did he swear that they would not enter his rest, if not to those who were disobedient? ^3-18

**3:19** So we see that they were unable to enter because of unbelief. ^3-19
