## Chapter 104

**104:1** Bless the LORD, O my soul;  
O LORD, my God, You are very great;  
You are clothed in glory and majesty, ^104-1

**104:2** wrapped in a robe of light;  
You spread the heavens like a tent cloth. ^104-2

**104:3** He sets the rafters of His lofts in the waters,  
makes the clouds His chariot,  
moves on the wings of the wind. ^104-3

**104:4** He makes the winds His messengers,  
fiery flames His servants. ^104-4

**104:5** He established the earth on its foundations,  
so that it shall never totter. ^104-5

**104:6** You made the deep cover it as a garment;  
the waters stood above the mountains. ^104-6

**104:7** They fled at Your blast,  
rushed away at the sound of Your thunder, ^104-7

**104:8** —mountains rising, valleys sinking—  
to the place You established for them. ^104-8

**104:9** You set bounds they must not pass  
so that they never again cover the earth. ^104-9

**104:10** You make springs gush forth in torrents;  
they make their way between the hills, ^104-10

**104:11** giving drink to all the wild beasts;  
the wild asses slake their thirst. ^104-11

**104:12** The birds of the sky dwell beside them  
and sing among the foliage. ^104-12

**104:13** You water the mountains from Your[^1] lofts;  
the earth is sated from the fruit of Your work. ^104-13

**104:14** You make the grass grow for the cattle,  
and herbage for man’s labor  
that he may get food out of the earth— ^104-14

**104:15** wine that cheers the hearts of men,  
oil that makes the face shine,[^2]  
and bread that sustains man’s life. ^104-15

**104:16** The trees of the LORD drink their fill,  
the cedars of Lebanon, His own planting, ^104-16

**104:17** where birds make their nests;  
the stork has her home in the junipers. ^104-17

**104:18** The high mountains are for wild goats;  
the crags are a refuge for rock-badgers. ^104-18

**104:19** He made the moon to mark the seasons;  
the sun knows when to set. ^104-19

**104:20** You bring on darkness and it is night,  
when all the beasts of the forests stir. ^104-20

**104:21** The lions roar for prey,  
seeking their food from God. ^104-21

**104:22** When the sun rises, they come home  
and couch in their dens. ^104-22

**104:23** Man then goes out to his work,  
to his labor until the evening. ^104-23

**104:24** How many are the things You have made, O LORD;  
You have made them all with wisdom;  
the earth is full of Your creations. ^104-24

**104:25** There is the sea, vast and wide,  
with its creatures beyond number,  
living things, small and great. ^104-25

**104:26** There go the ships,  
and Leviathan that You formed to sport with. ^104-26

**104:27** All of them look to You  
to give them their food when it is due. ^104-27

**104:28** Give it to them, they gather it up;  
open Your hand, they are well satisfied; ^104-28

**104:29** hide Your face, they are terrified;  
take away their breath, they perish  
and turn again into dust; ^104-29

**104:30** send back Your breath, they are created,  
and You renew the face of the earth. ^104-30

**104:31** May the glory of the LORD endure forever;  
may the LORD rejoice in His works! ^104-31

**104:32** He looks at the earth and it trembles;  
He touches the mountains and they smoke. ^104-32

**104:33** I will sing to the LORD as long as I live;  
all my life I will chant hymns to my God. ^104-33

**104:34** May my prayer be pleasing to Him;  
I will rejoice in the LORD. ^104-34

**104:35** May sinners disappear from the earth,  
and the wicked be no more.  
Bless the LORD, O my soul.  
Hallelujah. ^104-35

[^1]: Lit. “His.”
[^2]: Lit. “to make the face shine from oil.”
