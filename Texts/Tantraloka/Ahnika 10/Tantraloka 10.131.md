## Mūla

शिवस्वाच्छन्द्यमात्रं तु भेदायैषां विजृम्भते ।
इत्याशयेन संपश्यन्विशेषं सकलादिह ॥ ^mula

śivasvācchandyamātraṃ tu bhedāyaiṣāṃ vijṛmbhate |
ityāśayena saṃpaśyanviśeṣaṃ sakalādiha || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 12 - Chapter Ten, part 3#^pg395|p. 376]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=395>))
>
> In the case of the Consciousness Deconditioned (perceivers), even this degree of division is absent. The unfolding of Śiva’s freedom alone serves to differentiate them from one another. (with 10.130cd–131ab)
>
> Observing that they differ from Conditioned (perceivers), it is with this intention that the Omnipresent Lord has not said that the Dissolution Deconditioned (perceivers) etc. have thirteen etc. aspects. (with 10.131cd–132ab)
