# LXX Psalms 45

← [[LXX Psalms 44]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 46]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 046]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 046]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**45:1** For the end, for the sons of Core; a Psalm concerning secret things.  ^45-1

**45:2** God is our refuge and strength, a help in the afflictions that have come heavily upon us.  ^45-2

**45:3** Therefore will we not fear when the earth is troubled, and the mountains are removed into the depths of the seas.  ^45-3

**45:4** Their waters have roared and been troubled, the mountains have been troubled by his might. Pause.  ^45-4

**45:5** The flowings of the river gladden the city of God: the Most High has sanctified his tabernacle.  ^45-5

**45:6** God is in the midst of her; she shall not be moved: God shall help her with his countenance.[^1]  ^45-6

**45:7** The nations were troubled, the kingdoms tottered: he uttered his voice, the earth shook.  ^45-7

**45:8** The Lord of hosts is with us; the God of Jacob is our helper. Pause.  ^45-8

**45:9** Come, and behold the works of the Lord, what wonders he has achieved on the earth.  ^45-9

**45:10** Putting an end to wars as for the ends of the earth; he will crush the bow, and break in pieces the weapon, and burn the bucklers with fire.  ^45-10

**45:11** Be still, and know that I am God: I will be exalted among the nations, I will be exalted in the earth.  ^45-11

**45:12** The Lord of hosts is with us; the God of Jacob is our helper.  ^45-12

[^1]: 45:6 *Alex.* and *Heb.* before, *or,* toward the morning.
