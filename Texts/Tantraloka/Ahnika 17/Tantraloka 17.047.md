## Mūla

संस्काराणां चतुष्केऽस्मिन्ये मन्त्राः कथिता मया ।
तेषु कर्मपदात्पूर्वं धरातत्त्वपदं वदेत् ॥ ^mula

saṃskārāṇāṃ catuṣke'sminye mantrāḥ kathitā mayā |
teṣu karmapadātpūrvaṃ dharātattvapadaṃ vadet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 08 - Chapter Seventeen#^pg181|p. 161]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=181>))
>
> In the mantras I have said (are used) in these four (purifying) rites of passage, (the teacher) should say the words “Earth principle” (dharātattva) before the word that denotes the object (of the rite) (karmapada).
