# Likutei Moharan I 216

← [[Likutei Moharan I 215]] · [[Likutei Moharan]] · [[Likutei Moharan I 217]] →

*Part I · Lesson 216* · [[Likutei Moharan]]

## 1

Know! the philosophers call nature *Em Kol Chay* (Mother Nature). We, with our prayers, nullify nature. For nature necessitates such-and-such, and through prayer [the course of] nature is changed.

This is the aspect of the Eighteen Blessings of prayer, not counting the blessing for [overcoming] atheists. For with the *ChaY* (18) blessings we nullify nature, *Em Kol ChaY,* subjugating and nullifying the atheists and heretics.
