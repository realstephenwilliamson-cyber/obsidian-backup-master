### Frag. 1 col. i (= 4Q436 1 i - ii)

**1** [… you have removed from me,] and in stead of it you have given [a p]ure [heart;] ^f1c1-1

**2** [the evil inclination you have removed …] you have [placed] in [my] heart; the lewdness of eyes ^f1c1-2

**3** [you have removed from me, and you have looked at …] your […] …; stubbornness ^f1c1-3

**4** [you sent away from me, and you turned it into humility; angry rage you removed from me] and you gave me a spirit of patience; ^f1c1-4

**5** [arrogance and haughtiness you discarded from me …] to me, the spirit of deceit you destroyed ^f1c1-5
