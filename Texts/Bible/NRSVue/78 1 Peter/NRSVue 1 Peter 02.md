## The living stone and a chosen people

**2:1** Rid yourselves, therefore, of all malice and all guile, insincerity, envy, and all slander. ^2-1

**2:2** Like newborn infants, long for the pure, spiritual milk, so that by it you may grow into salvation— ^2-2

**2:3** if indeed you have tasted that the Lord is good.^[Or *kind*] ^2-3

**2:4** Come to him, a living stone, though rejected by mortals yet chosen and precious in God’s sight, and ^2-4

**2:5** like living stones let yourselves be built^[Or *you yourselves are being built*] into a spiritual house, to be a holy priesthood, to offer spiritual sacrifices acceptable to God through Jesus Christ. ^2-5

**2:6** For it stands in scripture: “See, I am laying in Zion a stone, a cornerstone chosen and precious, and whoever believes in him will not be put to shame.” ^2-6

**2:7** This honor, then, is for you who believe, but for those who do not believe,^[Other ancient authorities read *obey*] “The stone that the builders rejected has become the very head of the corner,” ^2-7

**2:8** and “A stone that makes them stumble and a rock that makes them fall.” They stumble because they disobey the word, as they were destined to do. ^2-8

**2:9** But you are a chosen people, a royal priesthood, a holy nation, God’s own people,^[Gk *a people for his possession*] in order that you may proclaim the excellence of him who called you out of darkness into his marvelous light. ^2-9

**2:10** Once you were not a people, but now you are God’s people; once you had not received mercy, but now you have received mercy. ^2-10

## Live as servants of God

**2:11** Beloved, I urge you as aliens and exiles to abstain from the desires of the flesh that wage war against the soul.^[Or *one’s life*] ^2-11

**2:12** Conduct yourselves honorably among the gentiles, so that, though they malign you as evildoers, they may see your honorable deeds and glorify God when he comes to judge.^[Gk *God on the day of visitation*] ^2-12

**2:13** For the Lord’s sake be subject to every human authority,^[Or *every authority ordained for humans*] whether to the emperor as supreme ^2-13

**2:14** or to governors as sent by him to punish those who do wrong and to praise those who do right. ^2-14

**2:15** For it is God’s will that by doing right you should silence the ignorance of the foolish. ^2-15

**2:16** As servants of God, live as free people, yet do not use your freedom as a pretext for evil. ^2-16

**2:17** Honor everyone. Love the family of believers.^[Gk *Love the brotherhood*] Fear God. Honor the emperor. ^2-17

## The example of Christ’s suffering

**2:18** Slaves, be subject to your masters with all respect,^[Or *fear*] not only those who are good and gentle but also those who are dishonest. ^2-18

**2:19** For it is a commendable thing if, being aware of God, a person endures pain while suffering unjustly. ^2-19

**2:20** If you endure when you are beaten for doing wrong, what credit is that? But if you endure when you do good and suffer for it, this is a commendable thing before God. ^2-20

**2:21** For to this you have been called, because Christ also suffered for you, leaving you an example, so that you should follow in his steps. ^2-21

**2:22** “He committed no sin, and no deceit was found in his mouth.” ^2-22

**2:23** When he was abused, he did not return abuse; when he suffered, he did not threaten, but he entrusted himself to the one who judges justly. ^2-23

**2:24** He himself bore our sins in his body on the cross,^[Or *carried up our sins in his body to the tree*] so that, having died to sins, we might live for righteousness; by his wounds^[Gk *bruise*] you have been healed. ^2-24

**2:25** For you were going astray like sheep, but now you have returned to the shepherd and guardian of your souls.^[Or *lives*] ^2-25
