## All lands summoned to praise God

*A Psalm of thanksgiving.*

**100:1** Make a joyful noise to the LORD, all the earth. ^100-1

**100:2** Serve the LORD with gladness; come into his presence with singing. ^100-2

**100:3** Know that the LORD is God. It is he who made us, and we are his;^[Or *and not we ourselves*] we are his people and the sheep of his pasture. ^100-3

**100:4** Enter his gates with thanksgiving and his courts with praise. Give thanks to him; bless his name. ^100-4

**100:5** For the LORD is good; his steadfast love endures forever and his faithfulness to all generations. ^100-5
