## Chapter 115

**115:1** Not to us, O LORD, not to us  
but to Your name bring glory  
for the sake of Your love and Your faithfulness. ^115-1

**115:2** Let the nations not say,  
“Where, now, is their God?” ^115-2

**115:3** when our God is in heaven  
and all that He wills He accomplishes. ^115-3

**115:4** [^1]Their idols are silver and gold,  
the work of men’s hands. ^115-4

**115:5** They have mouths, but cannot speak,  
eyes, but cannot see; ^115-5

**115:6** they have ears, but cannot hear,  
noses, but cannot smell; ^115-6

**115:7** they have hands, but cannot touch,  
feet, but cannot walk;  
they can make no sound in their throats. ^115-7

**115:8** Those who fashion them,  
all who trust in them,  
shall become like them. ^115-8

**115:9** O Israel, trust in the LORD!  
He is their help and shield. ^115-9

**115:10** O house of Aaron, trust in the LORD!  
He is their help and shield. ^115-10

**115:11** O you who fear the LORD, trust in the LORD!  
He is their help and shield. ^115-11

**115:12** The LORD is mindful of us.  
He will bless us;  
He will bless the house of Israel;  
He will bless the house of Aaron; ^115-12

**115:13** He will bless those who fear the LORD,  
small and great alike. ^115-13

**115:14** May the LORD increase your numbers,  
yours and your children’s also. ^115-14

**115:15** May you be blessed by the LORD,  
Maker of heaven and earth. ^115-15

**115:16** The heavens belong to the LORD,  
but the earth He gave over to man. ^115-16

**115:17** The dead cannot praise the LORD,  
nor any who go down into silence. ^115-17

**115:18** But we will bless the LORD  
now and forever.  
Hallelujah. ^115-18

[^1]: With vv. 4–11, cf. Ps. 135.15–20.
