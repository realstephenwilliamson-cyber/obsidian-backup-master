## Mūla

स तत्फलत्यागकृतं विशिष्टं फलमश्नुते ।
अनया परिपाट्या यः समस्तां कर्मसंततिम् ॥ ^mula

sa tatphalatyāgakṛtaṃ viśiṣṭaṃ phalamaśnute |
anayā paripāṭyā yaḥ samastāṃ karmasaṃtatim || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 05 - Chapter Nine, part 3#^pg118|p. 101]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=118>))
>
> (However,) he who there (in that case) also abandons the latent impression of the fruit (of his action) by the firmness (of his intention to do so) enjoys a special fruit, generated by abandoning it. (with 9.108cd–109ab)
>
> He who abandons the entire stream of Karma by disassociating himself from it (anahaṁyutayā) is, by this process, a Vijñānakevalin (akala), (who is free of Karma) even though (his consciousness is still) contracted. (with 9.109cd–110ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* एतदेव प्रकृते योजयति --

> अनया परिपाट्या यः समस्तां कर्मसंततिम् ॥
> अनहंयुतया प्रोज्झेत् ससङ्कोचोऽपि सोऽकलः ।

अनयेति -- फलत्यागपर्यन्तरूपया, समस्तामिति -- शुभामशुभां च, अनहंयुतयेति -- तदनासक्ततया -- इत्यर्थः, अकल इति -- विज्ञानाकलः ॥ १०९ ॥
