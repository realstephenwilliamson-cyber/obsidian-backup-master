# Psalm 2 (NRSVue)

## God’s promise to his anointed

**2:1** Why do the nations conspire and the peoples plot in vain? ^2-1

**2:2** The kings of the earth set themselves, and the rulers take counsel together, against the LORD and his anointed, saying, ^2-2

**2:3** “Let us burst their bonds apart and cast their cords from us.” ^2-3

**2:4** He who sits in the heavens laughs; the LORD has them in derision. ^2-4

**2:5** Then he will speak to them in his wrath and terrify them in his fury, saying, ^2-5

**2:6** “I have set my king on Zion, my holy hill.” ^2-6

**2:7** I will tell of the decree of the LORD: He said to me, “You are my son; today I have begotten you. ^2-7

**2:8** Ask of me, and I will make the nations your heritage and the ends of the earth your possession. ^2-8

**2:9** You shall break them with a rod of iron and dash them in pieces like a potter’s vessel.” ^2-9

**2:10** Now therefore, O kings, be wise; be warned, O rulers of the earth. ^2-10

**2:11** Serve the LORD with fear; with trembling ^2-11

**2:12** kiss his feet,^[Cn: Meaning of Heb of 2.11b and 12a is uncertain] or he will be angry, and you will perish in the way, for his wrath is quickly kindled. Happy are all who take refuge in him. ^2-12
