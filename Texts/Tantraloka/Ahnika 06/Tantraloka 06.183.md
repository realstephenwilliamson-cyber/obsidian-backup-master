## Mūla

कालशक्तिस्ततो बाह्ये नैतस्या नियतं वपुः ।
स्वप्नस्वप्ने तथा स्वप्ने सुप्ते सङ्कल्पगोचरे ॥ ^mula

kālaśaktistato bāhye naitasyā niyataṃ vapuḥ |
svapnasvapne tathā svapne supte saṅkalpagocare || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 12 - Chapter Six, part 5#^pg407|p. 392]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=407>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 6.183cd–184ab.
>
> This deliberation (carcanā) on the wonderful diversity of action is established in the pure will alone, and is the power of Time. Thus, in the outer world, (the power of Time) has no fixed form. (with 6.182cd–183ab)
>
> We observe that even a tiny particle of time appears to be immense when we dream of dreaming, in a dream, in deep sleep, or when (immersed) in the sphere of thought, or in deep meditation, or when discerning (and contemplating) the process of withdrawal and creation of the universe. (with 6.183cd–184)
