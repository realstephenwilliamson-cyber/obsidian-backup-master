# Romans 3 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Romans 02|← Romans 2]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Romans 04|Romans 4 →]]

Compare: [[Romans 03|BSB and SBLGNT]]

**3:1** What advantage then hath the Jew, or what is the profit of circumcision? ^3-1

**3:2** Much every way. First indeed, because the words of God were committed to them. ^3-2

**3:3** For what if some of them have not believed? shall their unbelief make the faith of God without effect? God forbid. ^3-3

**3:4** But God is true; and every man a liar, as it is written, That thou mayest be justified in thy words, and mayest overcome when thou art judged. ^3-4

**3:5** But if our injustice commend the justice of God, what shall we say? Is God unjust, who executeth wrath? ^3-5

**3:6** (I speak according to man.) God forbid: otherwise how shall God judge this world? ^3-6

**3:7** For if the truth of God hath more abounded through my lie, unto his glory, why am I also yet judged as a sinner? ^3-7

**3:8** And not rather (as we are slandered, and as some affirm that we say) let us do evil, that there may come good? whose damnation is just. ^3-8

**3:9** What then? Do we excel them? No, not so. For we have charged both Jews, and Greeks, that they are all under sin. ^3-9

**3:10** As it is written: There is not any man just. ^3-10

**3:11** There is none that understandeth, there is none that seeketh after God. ^3-11

**3:12** All have turned out of the way; they are become unprofitable together: there is none that doth good, there is not so much as one. ^3-12

**3:13** Their throat is an open sepulchre; with their tongues they have dealt deceitfully. The venom of asps is under their lips. ^3-13

**3:14** Whose mouth is full of cursing and bitterness: ^3-14

**3:15** Their feet swift to shed blood: ^3-15

**3:16** Destruction and misery in their ways: ^3-16

**3:17** And the way of peace they have not known: ^3-17

**3:18** There is no fear of God before their eyes. ^3-18

**3:19** Now we know, that what things soever the law speaketh, it speaketh to them that are in the law; that every mouth may be stopped, and all the world may be made subject to God. ^3-19

**3:20** Because by the works of the law no flesh shall be justified before him. For by the law is the knowledge of sin. ^3-20

**3:21** But now without the law the justice of God is made manifest, being witnessed by the law and the prophets. ^3-21

**3:22** Even the justice of God, by faith of Jesus Christ, unto all and upon all them that believe in him: for there is no distinction: ^3-22

**3:23** For all have sinned, and do need the glory of God. ^3-23

**3:24** Being justified freely by his grace, through the redemption, that is in Christ Jesus, ^3-24

**3:25** Whom God hath proposed to be a propitiation, through faith in his blood, to the shewing of his justice, for the remission of former sins, ^3-25

**3:26** Through the forbearance of God, for the shewing of his justice in this time; that he himself may be just, and the justifier of him, who is of the faith of Jesus Christ. ^3-26

**3:27** Where is then thy boasting? It is excluded. By what law? Of works? No, but by the law of faith. ^3-27

**3:28** For we account a man to be justified by faith, without the works of the law. ^3-28

**3:29** Is he the God of the Jews only? Is he not also of the Gentiles? Yes, of the Gentiles also. ^3-29

**3:30** For it is one God, that justifieth circumcision by faith, and uncircumcision through faith. ^3-30

**3:31** Do we, then, destroy the law through faith? God forbid: but we establish the law. ^3-31
