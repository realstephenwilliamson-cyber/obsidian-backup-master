## Mūla

विस्पष्टं यद्वेद्यजातं जाग्रन्मुख्यतयैव तत् ।
यत्तु तत्राप्यविस्पष्टं स्पष्टाधिष्ठातृ भासते ॥ ^mula

vispaṣṭaṃ yadvedyajātaṃ jāgranmukhyatayaiva tat |
yattu tatrāpyavispaṣṭaṃ spaṣṭādhiṣṭhātṛ bhāsate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 13 - Chapter Ten, part 4#^pg474|p. 455]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=474>))
>
> When objectivity manifests very clearly (and distinctly) (vispaṣṭa) in the imagination (utprekṣā), a dream, an intention (saṁkalpa), memory, when drunk (unmada), or other such perceptions (dṛṣṭi), waking is the predominant state (within dreaming). (with 10.249cd–250ab)
>
> The dream state is said (to prevail whilst dreaming) when the objectivity within thought (vikalpāntarga) is not clearly (and distinctly) manifest (avispaṣta), (whereas) the sustaining (consciousness which is the objectivizing agent) (adhiṣthātr) underlying it is. (with 10.250cd–251ab)

अपिर्भिन्नक्रमः । तेन जाग्रदविशिष्टाधिष्ठात्रपि विकल्पान्तरगमविस्पष्टं वेद्यजातं पुनस्तत्रोत्पेर्क्षादौ यद्भासते तत्स्वप्नपदमुच्यते स्वप्नस्वप्नरूपत्वान्मुख्यः स्वप्नः -- इत्यर्थः ॥ २५० ॥
