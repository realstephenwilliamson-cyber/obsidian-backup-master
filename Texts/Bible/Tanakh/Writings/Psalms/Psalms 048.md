## Chapter 48

**48:1** A song. A psalm of the Korahites. ^48-1

**48:2** The LORD is great and much acclaimed  
in the city of our God,  
His holy mountain— ^48-2

**48:3** fair-crested, joy of all the earth,  
Mount Zion, summit of Zaphon,[^1]  
city of the great king. ^48-3

**48:4** Through its citadels, God has made Himself known as a haven. ^48-4

**48:5** See, the kings joined forces;  
they advanced together. ^48-5

**48:6** At the mere sight of it they were stunned,  
they were terrified, they panicked; ^48-6

**48:7** they were seized there with a trembling,  
like a woman in the throes of labor, ^48-7

**48:8** as the Tarshish fleet was wrecked  
in an easterly gale.[^2] ^48-8

**48:9** The likes of what we heard we have now witnessed  
in the city of the LORD of hosts,  
in the city of our God—  
may God preserve it forever! *Selah*. ^48-9

**48:10** In Your temple, God,  
we meditate upon Your faithful care. ^48-10

**48:11** The praise of You, God, like Your name,  
reaches to the ends of the earth;  
Your right hand is filled with beneficence. ^48-11

**48:12** Let Mount Zion rejoice!  
Let the towns[^3] of Judah exult,  
because of Your judgments. ^48-12

**48:13** Walk around Zion,  
circle it;  
count its towers, ^48-13

**48:14** take note of its ramparts;  
go through[^4] its citadels,  
that you may recount it to a future age. ^48-14

**48:15** For God—He is our God forever;  
He will lead us evermore.[^5] ^48-15

[^1]: A term for the divine abode.
[^2]: See 1 Kings 22.49.
[^3]: Or “women.”
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^5]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
