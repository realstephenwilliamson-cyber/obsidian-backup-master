## Mūla

गुप्तागुप्तविधानादियागचर्याक्रमेण सम्पूर्णम् ।
अनुयागः किल मुख्यः सर्वस्मिन्नेव कर्मविनियोगे ॥ ^mula

guptāguptavidhānādiyāgacaryākrameṇa sampūrṇam |
anuyāgaḥ kila mukhyaḥ sarvasminneva karmaviniyoge || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 03 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 1#^pg40|p. 23]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=40>))
>
> (It is said) everywhere (in the scriptures) that one should convene the assembly of the ancillary sacrifice in an excellent manner on a day a parvan (occurs). It is (rendered) full (and complete) by the sexual practice (caryākrama) of the primary sacrifice (performed according to) the prescribed procedure (for both) the secret (rites) and those that are not secret. Amongst all the rites performed in this (context), the ancillary sacrifice is indeed the main one. Thus (the officiant) intent on it should make an effort to determine the time the ancillary sacrifice (should be performed). (with 28.40cd–42ab)

## Viveka

> सर्वत्र च पर्वदिने कुर्यादनुयागचक्रमतिशयतः ॥
> गुप्तागुप्तविधानादियागचर्याक्रमेण सम्पूर्णम् ।
> अनुयागः किल मुख्यः सर्वस्मिन्नेव कर्मविनियोगे ॥
> अनुयागकाललाभे तस्मात्प्रयतेत तत्परमः ।

मुख्य इति--आदियागो हि पूजोपकरणभूतद्रव्योपहरणरूपत्वादेतदङ्गम्--इति भावः, तेन पर्ववेलायामनुयाग एव भरः कार्यः--इति तात्पर्यम् ॥
