## Mūla

यतश्चात्मप्रथा मोक्षस्तन्नेहाशङ्क्यमीदृशम् ।
नावश्यं कारणात्कार्यं तज्ज्ञान्यपि न मुच्यते ॥ ^mula

yataścātmaprathā mokṣastannehāśaṅkyamīdṛśam |
nāvaśyaṃ kāraṇātkāryaṃ tajjñānyapi na mucyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. I
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 01 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v01 10 - Chapter One, part 9#^pg348|p. 334]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 01.pdf#page=348>))
>
> There is so no such doubt (possible) here (with regards to our teachings, namely,) that even one who has (complete) knowledge (jñānin) of that (one ultimate reality) may not be liberated, (because, although knowledge is the cause of liberation, it may not bring about its result every time), as a cause does not necessarily give rise to its effect (immediately and in every case). (This objection is groundless,) because liberation is (in fact nothing but) the experience of one’s own authentic identity (ātmaprathā) (and not merely its effect).
