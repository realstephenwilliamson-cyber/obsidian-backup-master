## Mūla

एतावान्मृतिभोगो हि मर्मच्छिन्मूढताक्षगा ।
ध्वान्ताबिलत्वं मनसि तच्चैतेषु न विद्यते ॥ ^mula

etāvānmṛtibhogo hi marmacchinmūḍhatākṣagā |
dhvāntābilatvaṃ manasi taccaiteṣu na vidyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 06 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 4#^pg145|p. 128]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=145>))

> Such is the experience (bhoga) of death, namely, the severance of the weak vital points (in the body) (marman), the loss of sensory consciousness, and total darkness in the mind. That does not take place within these (yogis).
