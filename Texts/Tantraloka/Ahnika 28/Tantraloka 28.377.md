## Mūla

नृत्तादौ विषये प्राप्ता पूर्णानन्दत्वमश्नुते ।
ईर्ष्यासूयादिसङ्कोचकारणाभावतोऽत्र सा ॥ ^mula

nṛttādau viṣaye prāptā pūrṇānandatvamaśnute |
īrṣyāsūyādisaṅkocakāraṇābhāvato'tra sā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 07 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 5#^pg187|p. 170]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=187>))
>
> The consciousness, full to overflowing with bliss, (of each one) individually, attaining in this way oneness when dance and the like is its object, enjoys a state of complete (unobstructed) bliss.! (with 28.376cd–377ab)
>
> It is because the causes of the contraction (of consciousness), such as jealousy, envy and the like, are absent, that consciousness is here expanding without obstacles, and conjoins (each spectator to his or her own essential) bliss. (with 28.377cd–378ab)
