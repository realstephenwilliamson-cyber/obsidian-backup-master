“I offer worship to the *yōginīs* who protect everyone as children in their wombs (*nigarbha yōginyaḥ*), whose chakra grants us all protection (*sarvarakṣākarē cakrē*); and to Tripuramālinī, the sequence of these states that all people experience, who governs this chakra.”

Perform the *krōm mudrā* of Sarvamahāṅkuśā.
