# Nina Mirnig

Professor of Cultural and Intellectual History of Premodern South Asia at the University of Vienna since 2025, after ten years at the Austrian Academy of Sciences' Institute for the Cultural and Intellectual History of Asia; she works on early Śaivism, Śaiva tantric death rites and the inscriptions of early medieval Nepal ([University of Vienna](https://stb.univie.ac.at/en/department/team/nina-mirnig); [Austrian Academy of Sciences](https://www.oeaw.ac.at/en/m/mirnig-nina)).
