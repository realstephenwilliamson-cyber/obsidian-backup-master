*The Hymn to Mālinī - Mālinīstava* 

जय त्वं मालिनीदेवि निर्मले मलनाशिनि |
ज्ञानशक्तिः प्रभुदवि बुद्धिस्त्वं तेजवर्धनी ॥ (२३८) 

*jaya tvaṃ mālinīdevi nirmale malanāśini |* 

*jñānaśaktiḥ prabhurdevi buddhistvaṃ tejavardhanī || (38)* 

Be victorious, O goddess Mālinī, you who are pure! The destroyer of impurity! O goddess, (you) are the lord (prabhu), the power of knowledge, you are the intellect and you increase the radiant energy (tejas) (of consciousness). 

जननी सर्वभूतानां संसारेऽस्मिन् व्यवस्थिता ।
माता वीरावली देवी कारुण्यं कुरु वत्सले ॥ (३९) 

*jananī sarvabhūtānāṃ saṃsāre'smin vyavasthitā |* 

*mātā vīrāvalī devī kāruṇyaṃ kuru vatsale || (39)* 

(You) are the mother of all living beings residing in this world of transmigratory existence. (You are) the mother, the goddess Vīrāvalī. O dear one, be compassionate! 

ए जयति परमतत्वनिर्वाणसंभूततेजोमयीं । १ 

*aiṁ jayati paramatattvanirvāṇasaṃbhūtatejomayīṃ | 1* 

AIṀ! (The goddess who) is the radiant energy (tejas) (sambhūta) from nirvāṇa, the Supreme Principle, is victorious! 

ई निःसृता व्यक्तरूपा परा ज्ञानशक्तिस्त्वमिच्छा क्रिया | २ 

*īṁ niḥsṛtā vyaktarūpā parā jñānaśaktistvamicchā kriyā | 2* 

ĪṀ! (You are) Parā (the Supreme One) who has emerged (out of the godhead) in manifest form; you are the power of knowledge, will and action. 

ओं ऋरज्विरेखा पुनः सुप्रनागेन्द्रवत्कुण्डलाकाररूपा प्रभुर्नादशक्तिस्त्वं संगीयसे । ३ 

*oṁ ṝjvirekhā punaḥ suptanāgendravatkuṇḍalākārarūpā* 

*prabhurnādaśaktistvaṃ saṃgīyase | 3* 

OṀ! (Your form is originally like) a straight line then it is in the form of a (round) earring, (coiled) like the sleeping serpent king. You are praised as the lord, the energy of Sound (nādaśakti). 

*[p. 4]* ^pg4

हां भासुराकाररूपा सुरूपा शिवा उ्येष्ठनामा च वामाथ रौद्री अनाख्याम्बिका बिन्दुरूपावधूतार्धचन्द्राकृतिस्त्वं त्रिकोणा एकार -एेकार - ईकार - अंकारसंयोजितैकत्र यत् तत्त्वमापद्यते । ४
*hāṁ bhāsurākārarūpā surūpā śivā jyeṣṭhanāmā ca vāmātha raudrī anākhyāmbikā bindurūpāvadhūtārdhacandrākṛtistvaṃ trikoṇā ekāra - aikāra* 

*- īkāra - aṃkārasaṃyojitaikatra yat tattvamāpadyate | 4* 

HĀṀ! (Your) form is brilliantly luminous (bhāsurā) and beautiful. (You) are Siva and (the goddess) called Jyeṣṭhaas well as Vāmā, Raudrī, and Ambikā (who is) Anākhyā. (Your) form is a (dimensionless) Point (bindu), You are Avadhūtāand your form is the Half Moon. (You are the female) Triangle (trikoṇā) which, conjoined together in one place with the letters E, AI, Ī and AṀ, attains to the (ultimate) principle. 

ए व्युत्क्रमेणोत्थिता तत्स्वरूपा भगाकारावस्थायिनी । ५ 

*aiṁ vyutkrameṇotthitā tatsvarūpā bhagākārāvasthāyinī | 5* 

AIṀ! Of that nature (you) have arisen (out of the godhead) in the reverse order (vyutkrameṇa) and abide in the form of the Yoni. 

अं अः आदितत्वोद्धवा । & 

*aṁ aḥ āditattvodbhavā | 6* 

AṀ! AḤ! (You are the goddess who has) come forth from the First Principle. 

हीं आदिक्षान्तयोनिस्वरूपा च श्रीकण्टसंमोहिनी । ७ 

*hrīṁ ādikṣāntayonisvarūpā ca śrīkaṇṭhasamohinī | 7* 

HRĪṀ! (You who are) the source (Yoni of the letters) beginning with A and ending with KṢA infatuate Śrīkaṇṭha (A). 

फं रुद्रमाता तथानन्तशक्तिः सुसृक्ष्मा त्रिमूर्त्यामरेशार्षिनी ।८
*phreṁ rudramātā tathānantaśaktiḥ susūkṣmā trimūrtyāmareśārṣinī |8* PHREṀ! (You are) Rudra's mother and so too, the power of Ananta (the Endless One -A), Susūkṣmā (the Extremely Subtle -I), Trimūrti (the Trinity –Ī), Amareśā (the Mistress of the Immortals –U) and Arghinī (She who Offers Lustrations –Ū). 

*[p. 5]* ^pg5

हकारौकार एकत्र संबोधनी बिन्दुस्वरूपिणी । ९ 

*hakāraukāra ekatra saṃbodhanī bindusvarūpiṇī | 9* 

(HOṀ!) (You are) the Point, the (awakening) invocation (saṃbodhanā) together (in one place) (ekatra) within the letter HA and the letter O. 

क्रां ह ~ र - ई कारनिन्द्रड्किता योगिनी सिद्धयोगेश्वरी । १० 

*krāṁ ha - ra - ī kārabindvaṅkitā yoginī siddhayogeśvarī | 10* 

KRĀṀ! (You are) the yogini Siddhayogeśvarī marked with HA, RA, the letter I and the Point. 

हौ योगमायातत्वा भारभूतिः । ११ 

*hrauṁ yogamāyātattvā bhārabhūtiḥ | 11* 

HRAUṀ! (You are) Bhārabhūti (Ṛ), the reality (tattva) of Yogamāyā. 

हीं तिथीशात्सिका । १२ 

*hrīṁ tithīśātmikā | 12* 

HRĪṀ! (You) are Tithīśā (Ṝ). 

कां स्थाणुभूता हराख्या च ण्टीशभौतीशसद्यात्मिकानुग्रहेशार्चिता । १३
*kāṁ sthāṇubhūtā harākhyā ca jhaṇṭīśabhautīśasadyātmikānugraheśārcitā |* *13* KĀṀ! (You are) Sthāṇu (the Pillar) (Ḷ) and are called Hara (Ḹ). You are worshipped by Jhaṇṭīśa (E), Bhautīśa (AI), Sadyātmika (O) and the Lord of Grace (Anugraheśa) (AU). 

हओ क्ररदेहानुगा त्वं महासेनसंभोगिनी । १४ 

*hrūauṁ krūradehānugā tvaṃ mahāsenasaṃbhoginī | 14* 

HRŪAUṀ! You are the companion of the one whose body is Krūra (i.e.Ṁ) and enjoy sexual pleasure with Mahāsena (Ḥ). 

दसूओं षोडशान्तामृता बिन्दुसंदोहिनी शाम्भवी श्वेतनिष्यन्ददेहप्लुता । १५
*hsrūauṁ ṣoḍaśāntāmṛtā bindusadohinī śāmbhavī śvetaniṣyandadehaplutā |* *15* HSVŪAUṀ! (You are) the nectar at the end of the sixteen. (You are the goddess) Śāmbhavī who milks the (lunar) Drop (bindu) and (your) body is flooded with (its) white secretions. 

*[p. 6]* ^pg6

*aiṁ aśeṣasamyakparānandanirvāṇasaukhyaprade | 16* 

AIṀ! (O goddess who) bestows the entire joy of nirvāṇa which is right (samyak) and supreme bliss!

*hrīṁ devi bhairavi | 17*

*saḥ parāmālini malayāvali | 19* 

SAḤ! O Malayan range (malayāvalī)! (You are) Parā, (the goddess) Mālinī.

*sauḥ tribhuvanāvali | 20*

*ḍaralakasahamryūṁ phetkāragambhīraghoreśvari | 3 6* 

ḌARALAKASAHAMRYŪṀ! O Goddess (you who by your) howl are deep and formidable!

*tāṁ tāriṇi | 3 8*

खख ख आकाशलिङ्गोदितमहामातृशाकिनि । ३९ 

*khakhaphreṁ kha ākāśaliṅgoditamahāmātṛśākini | 3 9* 

KHAKHAPHREṀ! O Great Mother Śakini, within the Void who has arisen out of the Liṅga of Space!

*caraṇavandino'haṃ kāruṇyādanukampasva māmiti | 1 2 6* 

BHAIṀ! BHAIṀ! BHAIṀ! O Bhairavi! I am Bhairava who has come to take refuge in you. I desire the Command. O Bhairavi who are the Liṅga of Space! Forgive (me my) sin! O Śivā! O Uma! O mother of the universe! O Kālikā! You who live in the abyss of the cave of the Lotus of Kula! By extolling (stavana) (the glories of) Mālinī to you, I praise (your) feet and laud (your) Rule. Out of compassion be gracious towards me!
