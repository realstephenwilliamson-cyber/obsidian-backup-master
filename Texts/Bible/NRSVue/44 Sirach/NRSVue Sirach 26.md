# Sirach 26 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Sirach 25|← Sirach 25]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Sirach 27|Sirach 27 →]]

## The joy of a good wife

**26:1** Happy is the husband of a good wife; the number of his days will be doubled. ^26-1

**26:2** A courageous wife brings joy to her husband, and he will complete his years in peace. ^26-2

**26:3** A good wife is a good portion; she will be granted as a portion to the man who fears the Lord. ^26-3

**26:4** Whether rich or poor, his heart is content, and at all times his face is cheerful. ^26-4

## The worst of evils: A wicked wife

**26:5** Of three things my heart is frightened, and of a fourth I am in great fear:^[Syr: Meaning of Gk uncertain] slander in the city, the gathering of a mob, and false accusation—all these are worse than death. ^26-5

**26:6** But it is heartache and sorrow when a wife has a rival, and a tongue-lashing makes it known to all. ^26-6

**26:7** A bad wife is a chafing yoke; taking hold of her is like grasping a scorpion. ^26-7

**26:8** A drunken wife arouses great anger; she cannot hide her shame. ^26-8

**26:9** The haughty stare betrays a sexually immoral wife; her eyelids give her away. ^26-9

**26:10** Keep strict watch over a headstrong daughter, or else, when she finds liberty, she will make use of it. ^26-10

**26:11** Be on guard against her impudent eye, and do not be surprised if she sins against you. ^26-11

**26:12** As a thirsty traveler opens his mouth and drinks from any water near him, so she will sit in front of every tent peg and open her quiver to the arrow. ^26-12

## The blessing of a good wife

**26:13** A wife’s charm delights her husband, and her skill puts flesh on his bones. ^26-13

**26:14** A silent wife is a gift from the Lord, and nothing is so precious as her self-discipline. ^26-14

**26:15** A modest wife adds charm to charm, and no scales can weigh the value of her self-control. ^26-15

**26:16** Like the sun rising in the heights of the Lord, so is the beauty of a good wife in her well-ordered home. ^26-16

**26:17** Like the shining lamp on the holy lampstand, so is a beautiful face on a stately figure. ^26-17

**26:18** Like golden pillars on silver bases, so are shapely legs upon steadfast feet. ^26-18

## Other ancient authorities add verses 19–27:

**26:19** *My child, keep sound the bloom of your youth, and do not give your strength to strangers.* ^26-19

**26:20** *Seek a fertile field within the whole plain, and sow it with your own seed, trusting in your fine stock.* ^26-20

**26:21** *So your offspring will prosper, and, having confidence in their good descent, will grow great.* ^26-21

**26:22** *A prostitute is regarded as spittle and a married woman as a tower of death to her lovers.* ^26-22

**26:23** *A godless wife is given as a portion to a lawless man, but a pious wife is given to the man who fears the Lord.* ^26-23

**26:24** *A shameless woman constantly acts disgracefully, but a modest daughter will even be embarrassed before her husband.* ^26-24

**26:25** *A headstrong wife is regarded as a dog, but one who has a sense of shame will fear the Lord.* ^26-25

**26:26** *A wife honoring her husband will seem wise to all, but if she dishonors him in her pride she will be known to all as ungodly. Happy is the husband of a good wife, for the number of his years will be doubled.* ^26-26

**26:27** *A loud-voiced and garrulous wife is like a trumpet sounding the charge, and every person like this lives in the anarchy of war.* ^26-27

## Three depressing things

**26:28** At two things my heart is grieved, and because of a third anger comes over me: a warrior in want through poverty, intelligent men who are treated contemptuously, and a man who turns back from righteousness to sin—the Lord will prepare him for the sword! ^26-28

## The temptations of commerce

**26:29** A merchant can hardly keep from wrongdoing, nor is a tradesman innocent of sin. ^26-29
