## Mūla

ननु संवित्पराम्रष्ट्री परामर्शमयी स्वतः ।
परामृश्या कथं ताथारूप्यसृष्टौ तु सा जडा ॥ ^mula

nanu saṃvitparāmraṣṭrī parāmarśamayī svataḥ |
parāmṛśyā kathaṃ tāthārūpyasṛṣṭau tu sā jaḍā || ^iast

> Surely, (someone may object that this cognitive) consciousness (saṁvid) is a reflecting perceiver that is itself (svatah) (intrinsically and spontaneously) reflective awareness. How can it be an object of awareness? If it were to be emitted in that form (it would be) insentient (not consciousness).

## Viveka

> परामृश्या कथं ताथारूप्यसृष्टौ तु सा जडा ॥

ताथारूप्येति परामृश्यत्वस्येत्यर्थः ॥ ८ ॥
