# Psalm 138

← [[BCP Psalm 137]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 139]] →

*Confitebor tibi* · Book Five · Twenty-eighth Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 138]]

**138:1** I will give thanks to you, O LORD, with my whole heart; \*  
before the gods I will sing your praise. ^138-1

**138:2** I will bow down toward your holy temple  
and praise your Name, \*  
because of your love and faithfulness; ^138-2

**138:3** For you have glorified your Name \*  
and your word above all things. ^138-3

**138:4** When I called, you answered me; \*  
you increased my strength within me. ^138-4

**138:5** All the kings of the earth will praise you, O LORD, \*  
when they have heard the words of your mouth. ^138-5

**138:6** They will sing of the ways of the LORD, \*  
that great is the glory of the LORD. ^138-6

**138:7** Though the LORD be high, he cares for the lowly; \*  
he perceives the haughty from afar. ^138-7

**138:8** Though I walk in the midst of trouble, you keep me safe; \*  
you stretch forth your hand against the fury of my enemies;  
your right hand shall save me. ^138-8

**138:9** The LORD will make good his purpose for me; \*  
O LORD, your love endures for ever;  
do not abandon the works of your hands. ^138-9
