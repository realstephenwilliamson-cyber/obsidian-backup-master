## Chapter 4

**4:1** Hear the word of the LORD,  
O people of Israel!  
For the LORD has a case  
Against the inhabitants of this land,  
Because there is no honesty and no goodness  
And no obedience to God in the land. ^4-1

**4:2** [False] swearing, dishonesty, and murder,  
And theft and adultery are rife;  
Crime follows upon crime! ^4-2

**4:3** For that, the earth is withered:  
Everything that dwells on it languishes—  
Beasts of the field and birds of the sky—  
Even the fish of the sea perish. ^4-3

**4:4** “Let no man rebuke, let no man protest!”  
For this your people has a grievance against [you],  
O priest![^1] ^4-4

**4:5** So you shall stumble by day,  
And by night a prophet[^2] shall stumble as well,  
And I will destroy your kindred.[^3] ^4-5

**4:6** My people is destroyed because of [your] disobedience!  
Because you have rejected obedience,  
I reject you as My priest;  
Because you have spurned the teaching of your God,  
I, in turn, will spurn your children. ^4-6

**4:7** The more they increased, the more they sinned against Me:  
I will change their dignity to dishonor. ^4-7

**4:8** They feed on My people’s sin offerings,  
And so they desire its iniquity. ^4-8

**4:9** Therefore, the people shall fare like the priests:  
I will punish it for its conduct,  
I will requite it for its deeds. ^4-9

**4:10** Truly, they shall eat, but not be sated;  
They shall swill,[^4] but not be satisfied,  
Because they have forsaken the LORD  
To practice[^5] ^4-10

**4:11** lechery.  
Wine[^6] and new wine destroy  
The mind of ^4-11

**4:12** My people:  
It consults its stick,[^7]  
Its rod[^8] directs it!  
A lecherous impulse has made them go wrong,  
And they have strayed[^9] from submission to their God. ^4-12

**4:13** They sacrifice on the mountaintops  
And offer on the hills,  
Under oaks, poplars, and terebinths  
Whose shade is so pleasant.  
That is why their[^10] daughters fornicate  
And their daughters-in-law commit adultery! ^4-13

**4:14** I will not punish their daughters for fornicating  
Nor their daughters-in-law for committing adultery;  
For they themselves turn aside[^11] with whores  
And sacrifice with prostitutes,  
And a people that is without sense[^12] must stumble. ^4-14

**4:15** If you are a lecher, Israel—  
Let not Judah incur guilt—  
Do not come to Gilgal,[^13]  
Do not make pilgrimages to Beth-aven,[^14]  
And do not swear by the LORD![^15] ^4-15

**4:16** Ah, Israel has balked  
Like a stubborn cow;  
Therefore,  
The LORD will graze him  
On the range, like a sheep.[^16] ^4-16

**4:17** [^17]Ephraim is addicted to images—  
Let him be. ^4-17

**4:18** They drink to excess—  
Their liquor turns against them.  
They “love” beyond measure—  
Disgrace is the “gift” ^4-18

**4:19** Which the wind is bringing;[^18]  
They shall garner shame from their sacrifices. ^4-19

[^1]: For failing to reprove; but meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Emendation yields “your children”; cf. v. 6 end.
[^3]: Lit. “mother.”
[^4]: For this meaning of hiznah cf. v. 18.
[^5]: Cf. 12.7.
[^6]: Emendation yields “New grain”; cf. 7.14; 9.1–2.
[^7]: I.e., its phallus, meaning “its lust.”
[^8]: I.e., its phallus, meaning “its lust.”
[^9]: See note b at 1.2.
[^10]: Heb. “your,” here and through v. 14.
[^11]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^12]: Cf. vv. 11–12.
[^13]: One who participates in the debaucheries of the open-air shrines is not fit to visit a temple building.
[^14]: Lit. “House of Delusion,” substituted for Bethel (cf. Amos 4.4).
[^15]: I.e., you are not fit to profess His religion; see Jer. 12.16.
[^16]: Instead of giving them fodder in return for their work; cf. Isa. 30.23–24.
[^17]: Meaning of vv. 17–19 uncertain in part.
[^18]: Lit. “has bound up in the corners of its garment”; see note at Mal. 3.20.
