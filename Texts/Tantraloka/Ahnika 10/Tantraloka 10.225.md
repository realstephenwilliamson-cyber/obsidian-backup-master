## Mūla

शिवाभेदाच्च किं चाथ द्वैते नैकट्यवेदनात् ।
अनया च दिशा सर्वं सर्वदा प्रविवेचयन् ॥ ^mula

śivābhedācca kiṃ cātha dvaite naikaṭyavedanāt |
anayā ca diśā sarvaṃ sarvadā pravivecayan || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 13 - Chapter Ten, part 4#^pg454|p. 435]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=454>))
>
> The other (perceivers), that is, the Mantreśas (mantrādhināthādi) and the rest (are in reality only instruments of consciousness, but if they are sometimes considered to be) causes, it is because of their close proximity (saṁnidhi) (to consciousness), (as well as their) oneness with Śiva (śivābheda), and finally because in (the state of) duality (in which we reside, we) experience (them) as being closer (to us on the plane of duality). (with 10.224cd–225ab)
>
> He who is constantly analysing all things at all times in accord with this teaching becomes, in a short time, the Lord of the Wheel of Consciousness, and so Bhairava Himself. (with 10.225cd–226ab)
