## Mūla

तादृशीं ये तु नो रूढां संवित्तिमधिशेरते ।
अक्रमात्तत्प्रसिद्ध्यर्थं क्रमिको विधिरुच्यते ॥ ^mula

tādṛśīṃ ye tu no rūḍhāṃ saṃvittimadhiśerate |
akramāttatprasiddhyarthaṃ kramiko vidhirucyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII

>
> The gradual (kramika) (external ritual) procedure is taught to those who do not come to abide in this kind of (firmly) stable (rūdhā) consciousness (saṁvitti) immediately (akramāt), in order that that (they may) attain it.

## Viveka

> तादृशीं ये तु नो रूढां संवित्तिमधिशेरते ।
> अक्रमात्तत्प्रसिद्ध्यर्थं क्रमिको विधिरुच्यते ॥
