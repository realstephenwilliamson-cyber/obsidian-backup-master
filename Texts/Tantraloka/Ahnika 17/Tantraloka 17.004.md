## Mūla

इतिप्रतीतिदार्ढ्यार्थं बहिर्ग्रन्थ्युपकल्पनम् ।
बाहू कर्मास्पदं विष्णुर्मायात्मा गलसंश्रितः ॥ ^mula

itipratītidārḍhyārthaṃ bahirgranthyupakalpanam |
bāhū karmāspadaṃ viṣṇurmāyātmā galasaṃśritaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 08 - Chapter Seventeen#^pg160|p. 140]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=160>))
>
> The Impurity (mala) (of Māyā), which is the manifestation of that (reality) in a form that is other than it is, is called the Knot. In order to strengthen the perception (of it) in this way, outer knots are fashioned accordingly. (with 17.3cd–4ab)
>
> The arms are the locus of action. Viṣṇu, whose nature is Māyā, is located in the throat. The topknot that flows downwards (represents the Impurity that engenders the state of limitation) of the individual soul. It is for this reason that (the knots are) imagined in this way. (with 17.4cd–5ab)
