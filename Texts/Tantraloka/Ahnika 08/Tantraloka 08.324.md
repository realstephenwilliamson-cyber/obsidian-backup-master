## Mūla

तेन कलादिधरान्तं यदुक्तमावरणजालमखिलं तत् ।
निःसंख्यं च विचित्रं मायैवैका त्वभिन्नेयम् ॥ ^mula

tena kalādidharāntaṃ yaduktamāvaraṇajālamakhilaṃ tat |

## Translation

>
> Thus, the entire net of obscuring coverings which, as explained, are (the series of principles from) Force (kala)) up to Earth, are countless and diverse, while Māyā itself is one and undivided.
