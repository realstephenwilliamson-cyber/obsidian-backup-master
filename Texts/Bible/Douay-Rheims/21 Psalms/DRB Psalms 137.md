# Psalms 137 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 136|← Psalms 136]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 138|Psalms 138 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 137|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**137:1** I will praise thee, O lord, with my whole heart: for thou hast heard the words of my mouth. I will sing praise to thee in the sight of his angels: ^137-1

**137:2** I will worship towards thy holy temple, and I will give glory to thy name. For thy mercy, and for thy truth: for thou hast magnified thy holy name above all. ^137-2

**137:3** In what day soever I shall call upon thee, hear me: thou shall multiply strength in my soul. ^137-3

**137:4** May all the kings of the earth give glory to thee: for they have heard all the words of thy mouth. ^137-4

**137:5** And let them sing in the ways of the Lord: for great is the glory of the Lord. ^137-5

**137:6** For the Lord is high, and looketh on the low: and the high he knoweth afar off. ^137-6

**137:7** If I shall walk in the midst of tribulation, thou wilt quicken me: and thou hast stretched forth thy hand against the wrath of my enemies: and thy right hand hath saved me. ^137-7

**137:8** The Lord will repay for me: thy mercy, O Lord, endureth for ever: O despise not the work of thy hands. ^137-8
