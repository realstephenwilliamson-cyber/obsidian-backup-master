## Mūla

ज्ञातज्ञेया धातृपदस्था अपि सन्तो
ये त्वन्मार्गात्कापथगास्तेऽपि न सम्यक् ।
प्रायस्तेषां लैङ्गिकबुद्ध्यादिसमुत्थो
मिथ्याबोधः सर्पवसादीपजकल्पः ॥ ^mula

jñātajñeyā dhātṛpadasthā api santo
ye tvanmārgātkāpathagāste'pi na samyak |
prāyasteṣāṃ laiṅgikabuddhyādisamuttho
mithyābodhaḥ sarpavasādīpajakalpaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 12 - Chapter Fourteen, and Appendices to Chapters Eleven and Twelve#^pg372|p. 350]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=372>))
>
> Vidyādhipati has said something similar in the Mānastotra (where we read). ‘Those who, (although they) have taken initiation into the path of Your teachings, come to disagree (with them) because they are (still) under the sway of delusion (moha), are certainly not in contact with the sun of Your (awakened consciousness). Are lotuses closed by the rays of the sun? Even though they have known what is to be known and reside on the plane of the Benefactor, those who abandon Your path for bad ones have not been properly (illumined). (Instead,) they are mostly occupied with false knowledge born from the notions of those who carry sectarian marks and the like. They are like lamps fed by serpent fat. Just as copper penetrated by (alchemical) mercury does not return to its original state, similarly those who live on the surface of the earth that have drunk (that divine) nectar are (never) tormented again by thirst or hunger for it.’ (with 14.9cd–12)
