# Proverbs 16

← [[Proverbs 15]] · [[Tanakh]] · [[Proverbs 17]] →

Compare: [[NRSVue Proverbs 16|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 16

**16:1** A man may arrange his thoughts,  
But what he says depends on the LORD. ^16-1

**16:2** All the ways of a man seem right to him,  
But the LORD probes motives. ^16-2

**16:3** Entrust your affairs to the LORD,  
And your plans will succeed. ^16-3

**16:4** The LORD made everything for a purpose,  
Even the wicked for an evil day. ^16-4

**16:5** Every haughty person is an abomination to the LORD;  
Assuredly,[^1] he will not go unpunished. ^16-5

**16:6** Iniquity is expiated by loyalty and faithfulness,  
And evil is avoided through fear of the LORD. ^16-6

**16:7** When the LORD is pleased with a man’s conduct,  
He may turn even his enemies into allies. ^16-7

**16:8** Better a little with righteousness  
Than a large income with injustice. ^16-8

**16:9** A man may plot out his course,  
But it is the LORD who directs his steps. ^16-9

**16:10** There is magic on the lips of the king;  
He cannot err in judgment. ^16-10

**16:11** Honest scales and balances are the LORD’s;  
All the weights in the bag are His work. ^16-11

**16:12** Wicked deeds are an abomination to kings,  
For the throne is established by righteousness. ^16-12

**16:13** Truthful speech wins the favor of kings;  
They love those who speak honestly. ^16-13

**16:14** The king’s wrath is a messenger of death,  
But a wise man can appease it. ^16-14

**16:15** The king’s smile means life;  
His favor is like a rain cloud in spring. ^16-15

**16:16** How much better to acquire wisdom than gold;  
To acquire understanding is preferable to silver. ^16-16

**16:17** The highway of the upright avoids evil;  
He who would preserve his life watches his way. ^16-17

**16:18** Pride goes before ruin,  
Arrogance, before failure. ^16-18

**16:19** Better to be humble and among the lowly  
Than to share spoils with the proud. ^16-19

**16:20** He who is adept in a matter will attain success;  
Happy is he who trusts in the LORD. ^16-20

**16:21** The wise-hearted is called discerning;  
One whose speech is pleasing gains wisdom. ^16-21

**16:22** Good sense is a fountain of life to those who have it,  
And folly is the punishment of fools. ^16-22

**16:23** The mind of the wise man makes his speech effective  
And increases the wisdom on his lips. ^16-23

**16:24** Pleasant words are like a honeycomb,  
Sweet to the palate and a cure for the body. ^16-24

**16:25** A road may seem right to a man,  
But in the end it is a road to death. ^16-25

**16:26** The appetite of a laborer labors for him,  
Because his hunger[^2] forces him on.[^3] ^16-26

**16:27** A scoundrel plots[^4] evil;  
What is on his lips is like a scorching fire. ^16-27

**16:28** A shifty man stirs up strife,  
And a querulous one alienates his friend. ^16-28

**16:29** A lawless man misleads his friend,  
Making him take the wrong way. ^16-29

**16:30** He closes his eyes while meditating deception;  
He purses his lips while deciding upon evil. ^16-30

**16:31** Gray hair is a crown of glory;  
It is attained by the way of righteousness. ^16-31

**16:32** Better to be forbearing than mighty,  
To have self-control than to conquer a city. ^16-32

**16:33** Lots are cast into the lap;  
The decision depends on the LORD. ^16-33

[^1]: Lit. “Hand to hand”; meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Lit. “mouth.”
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
