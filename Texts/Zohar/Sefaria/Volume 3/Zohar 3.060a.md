*Volume 3, folio 60a* · Achrei Mot

*Passage Achrei Mot 9:67 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 3.059b#^achrei-mot-9-67|Achrei Mot 9:67]].*

**Achrei Mot 10:68** R. Eleazar thereupon arose and began with the text: “And the Lord spoke unto Moses after the death of the two sons of Aaron” (Lev. 16, 1). ‘This verse’, he said, ‘seems superfluous, seeing that the text proceeds, “And the Lord said to Moses, speak unto Aaron thy brother”, which should properly be the beginning of the section. ^achrei-mot-10-68

**Achrei Mot 10:69** The explanation is this. When God gave the incense to Aaron, he desired that no other man should use it in his lifetime. He said to him: “Thou desirest to increase peace in the world. By thy hand shall peace be increased above. Therefore the incense of spices shall be delivered to thy hand from now onwards, and in thy lifetime no other shall use it.” Nadab and Abihu, however, thrust themselves forward to offer it. ^achrei-mot-10-69

**Achrei Mot 10:70** We have learnt that Moses worried himself to find out what was the cause of their misfortune. Hence God spoke to him after their death and said to him: “The cause is that they ‘drew near to the Lord’, and thrust themselves forward in the lifetime of their father.” Now if the sons of Aaron brought this upon themselves through thrusting themselves forward in the lifetime of their father, what should I deserve if I thrust myself forward before my father and R. Pinchas and the rest of the Companions?’ Hearing this, R. Pinchas came up to him and kissed him and blessed him. ^achrei-mot-10-70

**Achrei Mot 11:71** R. Simeon then discoursed on the verse: “Behold it is the litter of Solomon, threescore mighty men are round about it”, etc. (S.S. 3, 7). He said: ‘The “litter” here is the throne of glory of the King, Solomon, the “king to whom all peace belongs”. Threescore mighty men are round about it, clinging to its sides as emissaries from stern judgement; they are called the sixty rods of fire wherewith that Youth[^1] is girt. ^achrei-mot-11-71

**Achrei Mot 11:72** On its right hand is a flashing sword, on its left hand coals of fire with seventy thousand consuming flames. Those threescore are armed with deadly weapons from the armoury of the supernal Might of the Holy One, blessed be He. ^achrei-mot-11-72

**Achrei Mot 11:73** They all “handle the sword and are expert in war”: they are ever ready to execute judgement and are called the “lords of wailing and howling”. ^achrei-mot-11-73

**Achrei Mot 11:74** And from whence do they receive all this? “From fear”: that is, from the place called “the fear of Isaac”. And when? “In the night”: at the time when they are commissioned to execute judgement. ^achrei-mot-11-74

**Achrei Mot 11:75** We have learnt that a thousand and five hundred bearers of arms with authority to smite are attached to the side of those mighty ones. In the hands of him who is called “Youth” there are four mighty keys. Sea monsters go forth beneath the ship ^achrei-mot-11-75

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Achrei Mot 11:76.*

[^1]: Metatron.
