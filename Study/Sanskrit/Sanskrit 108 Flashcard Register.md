# Sanskrit 108 Flashcard Master Register

> [!abstract] Overview
> A complete inventory of the 108 Sanskrit Flashcards (The card images were deleted on 15 Oct 2026 at Stephen's request; the two image columns are kept, empty, so the card views still read the table.) It catalogs all 108 cards across three progressive learning tiers:
> 1. **Cards 1–27:** Vowels (*Svara*) & Mātrās
> 2. **Cards 28–60:** Simple Consonants (*Vyañjana*) organized by mouth position (*sthāna*)
> 3. **Cards 61–108:** Conjuncts (*Saṃyuktākṣara*), Ligatures, and Sacred Bījas
>
> This register powers the **Daily Sanskrit Letter Cycle** on `Home.md` and in your daily reflection notes.

**Body places** come from Abhinavagupta's own list in [[Tantraloka 15.117|TĀ 15.117]]–[[Tantraloka 15.120|15.120]] and Jayaratha's comments on it (Dyczkowski vol. VIII, pp. 78–79). Pairs go right then left; for teeth and lips, lower then upper (Jayaratha). The root text names the hip and "thigh and so on" for the legs; foot, toes and toenails are Jayaratha's reading of "and so on". It lists seven tissues for "the nine" last letters; breath (*prāṇa*) for ha and sheath (*kośa*) for kṣa come from the verse Jayaratha quotes there. Vowel signs take their vowel's place; syllables, conjuncts and word cards list each letter's place.

## Integration with Tantrāloka (Āhnika 3)
In Kashmir Shaivism, the 50 letters of the Sanskrit alphabet (*Varṇamālā*) are not conventional human signs; they are the pulsations of **Mātṛkā** (the Goddess as Sound).

## Devī Mātṛkā-śarīra: where each letter goes

Checked against [[Tantraloka 15.117|Tantrāloka 15.117–120]] (root text and Jayaratha). This replaces the crash-session draft, which had several places wrong (a on the crown, aṃ on the palate, aḥ on the throat, lips and teeth swapped, bha on the navel).

> [!matrika]+ Head and face: the sixteen vowels (15.117cd–118ab)
> - **अ a** forehead (*lalāṭa*) · **आ ā** mouth (*vaktra*)
> - **इ i / ई ī** right / left eye · **उ u / ऊ ū** right / left ear
> - **ऋ ṛ / ॠ ṝ** right / left nostril · **ऌ ḷ / ॡ ḹ** right / left cheek
> - **ए e / ऐ ai** lower / upper teeth (*rada*) · **ओ o / औ au** lower / upper lip (*oṣṭha*)
> - **अं aṃ** crown lock (*śikhā*) · **अः aḥ** tongue (*jihvā*)

> [!matrika]+ Arms, legs and trunk: the twenty-five stops (15.118cd–119)
> - **क ka – ङ ṅa** right shoulder, arm, hand, fingers, fingernails (*skandha, bāhu, kara, aṅguli, nakha*)
> - **च ca – ञ ña** the same on the left
> - **ट ṭa – ण ṇa** right hip, thigh, foot, toes, toenails (*kaṭi, ūru*, and with Jayaratha *pāda, aṅguli, nakha*)
> - **त ta – न na** the same on the left
> - **प pa / फ pha** right / left side (*pārśva*) · **ब ba** back (*pṛṣṭha*) · **भ bha** belly (*jaṭhara*) · **म ma** heart (*hṛd*)

> [!matrika]+ The tissues: the last nine letters (15.119d–120ab)
> - **य ya** skin (*tvak*) · **र ra** blood (*rakta*) · **ल la** flesh (*māṃsa*) · **व va** sinews (*sūtra*, which Jayaratha glosses as *snāyu*)
> - **श śa** bone (*asthi*) · **ष ṣa** fat (*vasā*) · **स sa** semen (*śukra*)
> - **ह ha** breath (*prāṇa*) · **क्ष kṣa** sheath (*kośa*): these two only in the verse Jayaratha quotes
> - These are spread through the whole body rather than set at one point; on the drawing, shade or outline the whole figure.
