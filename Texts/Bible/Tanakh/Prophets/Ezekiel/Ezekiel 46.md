## Chapter 46

**46:1** Thus said the Lord GOD: The gate of the inner court which faces east shall be closed on the six working days; it shall be opened on the sabbath day and it shall be opened on the day of the new moon. ^46-1

**46:2** The prince shall enter by way of the vestibule outside the gate, and shall attend at the gatepost while the priests sacrifice his burnt offering and his offering of well-being; he shall then bow low at the threshold of the gate and depart. The gate, however, shall not be closed until evening. ^46-2

**46:3** The common people[^1] shall worship before the LORD on sabbaths and new moons at the entrance of the same gate. ^46-3

**46:4** The burnt offering which the prince presents to the LORD on the sabbath day shall consist of six lambs without blemish and one ram without blemish— ^46-4

**46:5** with a meal offering of an *ephah* for the ram, a meal offering of as much as he wishes for the lambs, and a *hin* of oil with every *ephah*. ^46-5

**46:6** And on the day of the new moon, it shall consist of a bull of the herd without blemish, and six lambs and a ram—they shall be without blemish. ^46-6

**46:7** And he shall provide a meal offering of an *ephah* for the bull, an *ephah* for the ram, and as much as he can afford for the lambs, with a *hin* of oil to every *ephah*. ^46-7

**46:8** When the prince enters, he shall come in by way of the vestibule of the gate, and he shall go out the same way. ^46-8

**46:9** But on the fixed occasions, when the common people come before the LORD, whoever enters by the north gate to bow low shall leave by the south gate; and whoever enters by the south gate shall leave by the north gate. They shall not go back through the gate by which they came in, but shall go out by the opposite one.[^2] ^46-9

**46:10** And as for the prince, he shall enter with them when they enter and leave when they leave. ^46-10

**46:11** On festivals and fixed occasions, the meal offering shall be an *ephah* for each bull, an *ephah* for each ram, and as much as he wishes for the lambs, with a *hin* of oil for every *ephah*. ^46-11

**46:12** The gate that faces east shall also be opened for the prince whenever he offers a freewill offering—be it burnt offering or offering of well-being—freely offered to the LORD, so that he may offer his burnt offering or his offering of well-being just as he does on the sabbath day. Then he shall leave, and the gate shall be closed after he leaves. ^46-12

**46:13** Each day you shall offer a lamb of the first year without blemish, as a daily burnt offering to the LORD; you shall offer one every morning. ^46-13

**46:14** And every morning regularly you shall offer a meal offering with it: a sixth of an *ephah*, with a third of a *hin* of oil to moisten the choice flour, as a meal offering to the LORD—a law for all time. ^46-14

**46:15** The lamb, the meal offering, and oil shall be presented every morning as a regular burnt offering. ^46-15

**46:16** Thus said the Lord GOD: If the prince makes a gift to any of his sons, it shall become the latter’s inheritance; it shall pass on to his sons; it is their holding by inheritance. ^46-16

**46:17** But if he makes a gift from his inheritance to any of his subjects, it shall only belong to the latter until the year of release.[^3] Then it shall revert to the prince; his inheritance must by all means pass on to his sons. ^46-17

**46:18** But the prince shall not take property away from any of the people and rob them of their holdings. Only out of his own holdings shall he endow his sons, in order that My people may not be dispossessed of their holdings. ^46-18

**46:19** Then he led me into the passage at the side of the gate to the sacred chambers of the priests, which face north, and there, at the rear of it, in the west, I saw a space. ^46-19

**46:20** He said to me, “This is the place where the priests shall boil the guilt offerings and the sin offerings, and where they shall bake the meal offerings, so as not to take them into the outer court and make the people consecrated.”[^4] ^46-20

**46:21** Then he led me into the outer court and led me past the four corners of the court; and in each corner of the court there was an enclosure. ^46-21

**46:22** These unroofed[^5] enclosures, [each] 40 [cubits] long and 30 wide, were in the four corners of the court; the four corner enclosures had the same measurements. ^46-22

**46:23** [On the inside,] running round the four of them, there was a row of masonry, equipped with hearths under the rows all around. ^46-23

**46:24** He said to me, “These are the kitchens where the Temple servitors shall boil the sacrifices of the people.” ^46-24

[^1]: I.e., those other than the priests, the Levites, and the prince; lit. “the people of the land.”
[^2]: Lit. “straight before him.”
[^3]: Cf. Lev. 25.10.
[^4]: See note e at 44.19.
[^5]: So Mishnah Middoth 2.5; emendation yields “small.”
