## Chapter 150

**150:1** Hallelujah.  
Praise God in His sanctuary;  
praise Him in the sky, His stronghold. ^150-1

**150:2** Praise Him for His mighty acts;  
praise Him for[^1] His exceeding greatness. ^150-2

**150:3** Praise Him with blasts of the horn;  
praise Him with harp and lyre. ^150-3

**150:4** Praise Him with timbrel and dance;  
praise Him with lute and pipe. ^150-4

**150:5** Praise Him with resounding cymbals;  
praise Him with loud-clashing cymbals. ^150-5

**150:6** Let all that breathes praise the LORD.  
Hallelujah. ^150-6

[^1]: Or “as befits.”
