## Chapter 11

**11:1** Then a spirit lifted me up and brought me to the east gate of the House of the LORD, which faces eastward; and there, at the entrance of the gate, were twenty-five men, among whom I saw Jaazaniah son of Azzur and Pelatiah son of Benaiah, leaders of the people. ^11-1

**11:2** [The LORD] said to me, “O mortal, these are the men who plan iniquity and plot wickedness in this city, ^11-2

**11:3** who say: ‘There is no need now to build houses; this [city] is the pot, and we are the meat.’[^1] ^11-3

**11:4** I adjure you, prophesy against them; prophesy, O mortal!” ^11-4

**11:5** Thereupon the spirit of the LORD fell upon me, and He said to me, “Speak: Thus said the LORD: Such are your thoughts, O House of Israel; I know what comes into your mind. ^11-5

**11:6** Many have you slain in this city; you have filled its streets with corpses. ^11-6

**11:7** Assuredly, thus says the Lord GOD: The corpses that you have piled up in it are the meat for which it is the pot; but you shall be taken out of it. ^11-7

**11:8** You feared the sword, and the sword I will bring upon you—declares the Lord GOD. ^11-8

**11:9** I will take you out of it and deliver you into the hands of strangers, and I will execute judgments upon you. ^11-9

**11:10** You shall fall by the sword; I will punish you at the border of Israel. And you shall know that I am the LORD. ^11-10

**11:11** This [city] shall not be a pot for you, nor you the meat in it; I will punish you at the border of Israel. ^11-11

**11:12** Then you shall know that I am the LORD, whose laws you did not follow and whose rules you did not obey, acting instead according to the rules of the nations around you.” ^11-12

**11:13** Now, as I prophesied, Pelatiah son of Benaiah dropped dead. I threw myself upon my face and cried out aloud, “Ah, Lord GOD! You are wiping out the remnant of Israel!” ^11-13

**11:14** Then the word of the LORD came to me: ^11-14

**11:15** “O mortal, [I will save] your brothers, your brothers, the men of your kindred,[^2] all of that very House of Israel to whom the inhabitants of Jerusalem say, ‘Keep far from the LORD; the land has been given as a heritage to us.’ ^11-15

**11:16** Say then: Thus said the Lord GOD: I have indeed removed them far among the nations and have scattered them among the countries, and I have become to them a diminished sanctity in the countries whither they have gone. ^11-16

**11:17** Yet say: Thus said the Lord GOD: I will gather you[^3] from the peoples and assemble you out of the countries where you have been scattered, and I will give you the Land of Israel. ^11-17

**11:18** And they shall return there, and do away with all its detestable things and all its abominations. ^11-18

**11:19** I will give them one heart and put a new spirit in them;[^4] I will remove the heart of stone from their bodies and give them a heart of flesh, ^11-19

**11:20** that they may follow My laws and faithfully observe My rules. Then they shall be My people and I will be their God. ^11-20

**11:21** But as for them whose heart is set upon their detestable things and their abominations, I will repay them for their conduct—declares the Lord GOD.” ^11-21

**11:22** Then the cherubs, with the wheels beside them, lifted their wings, while the Presence of the God of Israel rested above them. ^11-22

**11:23** The Presence of the LORD ascended from the midst of the city and stood on the hill east of the city. ^11-23

**11:24** A spirit carried me away and brought me in a vision by the spirit of God to the exile community in Chaldea. Then the vision that I had seen left me, ^11-24

**11:25** and I told the exiles all the things that the LORD had shown me. ^11-25

[^1]: I.e., the exiles will not return.
[^2]: I.e., the exiles.
[^3]: I.e., the exiles.
[^4]: Heb. “you.”
