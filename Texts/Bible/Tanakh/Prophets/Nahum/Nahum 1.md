# Nahum 1

[[Tanakh]] · [[Nahum 2]] →

Compare: [[NRSVue Nahum 01|NRSVue]] (NRSVue 1:15 = Heb 2:1)

## Chapter 1

**1:1** A pronouncement on Nineveh: The Book of the Prophecy of Nahum the Elkoshite. ^1-1

**1:2** The LORD is a passionate, avenging God;  
The LORD is vengeful and fierce in wrath.  
The LORD takes vengeance on His enemies,  
He rages against His foes. ^1-2

**1:3** The LORD is slow to anger and of great forbearance,  
But the LORD does not remit all punishment.  
He travels in whirlwind and storm,  
And clouds are the dust on His feet. ^1-3

**1:4** He rebukes the sea and dries it up,  
And He makes all rivers fail;  
Bashan and Carmel languish,  
And the blossoms[^1] of Lebanon wither. ^1-4

**1:5** The mountains quake because of Him,  
And the hills melt.  
The earth heaves[^2] before Him,  
The world and all that dwell therein. ^1-5

**1:6** Who can stand before His wrath?  
Who can resist His fury?  
His anger pours out like fire,  
And rocks are shattered because of Him. ^1-6

**1:7** The LORD is good to [those who hope in Him],  
A haven on a day of distress;  
He is mindful of those who seek refuge in Him. ^1-7

**1:8** And with a sweeping flood  
He makes an end of her place,[^3]  
And chases His enemies into darkness. ^1-8

**1:9** Why will you plot against the LORD?  
He wreaks utter destruction:  
No adversary[^4] opposes Him twice! ^1-9

**1:10** For like men besotted with drink,  
They are burned up like tangled thorns,  
Like straw that is thoroughly dried.[^5] ^1-10

**1:11** [^6]The base plotter  
Who designed evil against the LORD  
Has left you. ^1-11

**1:12** Thus said the LORD:  
“Even as they[^7] were full and many,  
Even so are they over and gone;  
As surely as I afflicted you,  
I will afflict you no more.”[^8] ^1-12

**1:13** And now  
I will break off his yoke bar from you  
And burst your cords apart. ^1-13

**1:14** The LORD has commanded concerning him:[^9]  
No posterity shall continue your name.[^10]  
I will do away with  
The carved and graven images  
In the temples of your gods;  
I will make your grave  
Accord with your worthlessness.[^11] ^1-14

[^1]: Lit. “bud.”
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain; emendation yields “those who oppose Him.”
[^4]: Cf. Ugaritic ṣrt.
[^5]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^6]: Vv. 11–14 would read well after 2.1.
[^7]: I.e., the days of your affliction.
[^8]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^9]: Heb. “you.”
[^10]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^11]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
