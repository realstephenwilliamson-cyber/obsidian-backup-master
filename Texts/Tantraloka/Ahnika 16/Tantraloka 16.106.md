## Mūla

जलाद् ध्यन्तं सार्धयुग्मं मूलं त्र्यंगुलमित्यतः ।
द्वादशांगुलताधिक्याद्विधिरेष परापरः ॥ ^mula

jalād dhyantaṃ sārdhayugmaṃ mūlaṃ tryaṃgulamityataḥ |
dvādaśāṃgulatādhikyādvidhireṣa parāparaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> This, the intermediate (parāpara) procedure (is such) because it (extends for an) extra twelve fingers, as the principles from Water up to the intellect occupy two fingers and a half and Nature (prakṛti) three.
