*Part I · Lesson 201* · [[Likutei Moharan]]

## 1

There is a hint in the *Targum* that on Pesach we are to pray loudly. As is brought concerning the verse ([[Lamentations 2#^2-7|Lamentations 2:7]]), “They made a sound in the House of the Lord, as on the day of the assembly”—as the sound of the people who pray on the Festival of Pesach. Study the *Targum* there.

The first letters of “*TZedakah Tatzil Mimavet* (Charity saves from death)” ([[Proverbs 10#^10-2|Proverbs 10:2]]) spell *MaTZoT*.

Disbursing charity generously is a *segulah* (preternatural remedy) for epilepsy, God forbid. [This is hinted at by] the first letters of “*Pizar Natan L’evyonim* (He has given generously to the poor)” ([[Psalms 112#^112-9|Psalms 112:9]]), which spell *NoPheL* (an epileptic).
