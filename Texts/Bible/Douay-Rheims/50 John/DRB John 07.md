# John 7 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB John 06|← John 6]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB John 08|John 8 →]]

Compare: [[John 07|BSB and SBLGNT]]

**7:1** After these things Jesus walked in Galilee; for he would not walk in Judea, because the Jews sought to kill him. ^7-1

**7:2** Now the Jews’ feast of tabernacles was at hand. ^7-2

**7:3** And his brethren said to him: Pass from hence, and go into Judea; that thy disciples also may see thy works which thou dost. ^7-3

**7:4** For there is no man that doth any thing in secret, and he himself seeketh to be known openly. If thou do these things, manifest thyself to the world. ^7-4

**7:5** For neither did his brethren believe in him. ^7-5

**7:6** Then Jesus said to them: My time is not yet come; but your time is always ready. ^7-6

**7:7** The world cannot hate you; but me it hateth: because I give testimony of it, that the works thereof are evil. ^7-7

**7:8** Go you up to this festival day, but I go not up to this festival day: because my time is not accomplished. ^7-8

**7:9** When he had said these things, he himself stayed in Galilee. ^7-9

**7:10** But after his brethren were gone up, then he also went up to the feast, not openly, but, as it were, in secret. ^7-10

**7:11** The Jews therefore sought him on the festival day, and said: Where is he? ^7-11

**7:12** And there was much murmuring among the multitude concerning him. For some said: He is a good man. And others said: No, but he seduceth the people. ^7-12

**7:13** Yet no man spoke openly of him, for fear of the Jews. ^7-13

**7:14** Now about the midst of the feast, Jesus went up into the temple, and taught. ^7-14

**7:15** And the Jews wondered, saying: How doth this man know letters, having never learned? ^7-15

**7:16** Jesus answered them, and said: My doctrine is not mine, but his that sent me. ^7-16

**7:17** If any man do the will of him; he shall know of the doctrine, whether it be of God, or whether I speak of myself. ^7-17

**7:18** He that speaketh of himself, seeketh his own glory: but he that seeketh the glory of him that sent him, he is true, and there is no injustice in him. ^7-18

**7:19** Did Moses not give you the law, and yet none of you keepeth the law? ^7-19

**7:20** Why seek you to kill me? The multitude answered, and said: Thou hast a devil; who seeketh to kill thee? ^7-20

**7:21** Jesus answered, and said to them: One work I have done; and you all wonder: ^7-21

**7:22** Therefore, Moses gave you circumcision (not because it is of Moses, but of the fathers;) and on the sabbath day you circumcise a man. ^7-22

**7:23** If a man receive circumcision on the sabbath day, that the law of Moses may not be broken; are you angry at me because I have healed the whole man on the sabbath day? ^7-23

**7:24** Judge not according to the appearance, but judge just judgment. ^7-24

**7:25** Some therefore of Jerusalem said: Is not this he whom they seek to kill? ^7-25

**7:26** And behold, he speaketh openly, and they say nothing to him. Have the rulers known for a truth, that this is the Christ? ^7-26

**7:27** But we know this man, whence he is: but when the Christ cometh, no man knoweth whence he is. ^7-27

**7:28** Jesus therefore cried out in the temple, teaching, and saying: You both know me, and you know whence I am: and I am not come of myself; but he that sent me, is true, whom you know not. ^7-28

**7:29** I know him, because I am from him, and he hath sent me. ^7-29

**7:30** They sought therefore to apprehend him: and no man laid hands on him, because his hour was not yet come. ^7-30

**7:31** But of the people many believed in him, and said: When the Christ cometh, shall he do more miracles, than these which this man doth? ^7-31

**7:32** The Pharisees heard the people murmuring these things concerning him: and the rulers and Pharisees sent ministers to apprehend him. ^7-32

**7:33** Jesus therefore said to them: Yet a little while I am with you: and then I go to him that sent me. ^7-33

**7:34** You shall seek me, and shall not find me: and where I am, thither you cannot come. ^7-34

**7:35** The Jews therefore said among themselves: Whither will he go, that we shall not find him? will he go unto the dispersed among the Gentiles, and teach the Gentiles? ^7-35

**7:36** What is this saying that he hath said: You shall seek me, and shall not find me; and where I am, you cannot come? ^7-36

**7:37** And on the last, and great day of the festivity, Jesus stood and cried, saying: If any man thirst, let him come to me, and drink. ^7-37

**7:38** He that believeth in me, as the scripture saith, Out of his belly shall flow rivers of living water. ^7-38

**7:39** Now this he said of the Spirit which they should receive, who believed in him: for as yet the Spirit was not given, because Jesus was not yet glorified. ^7-39

**7:40** Of that multitude therefore, when they had heard these words of his, some said: This is the prophet indeed. ^7-40

**7:41** Others said: This is the Christ. But some said: Doth the Christ come out of Galilee? ^7-41

**7:42** Doth not the scripture say: That Christ cometh of the seed of David, and from Bethlehem the town where David was? ^7-42

**7:43** So there arose a dissension among the people because of him. ^7-43

**7:44** And some of them would have apprehended him: but no man laid hands on him. ^7-44

**7:45** The ministers therefore came to the chief priests and the Pharisees. And they said to them: Why have you not brought him? ^7-45

**7:46** The ministers answered: Never did man speak like this man. ^7-46

**7:47** The Pharisees therefore answered them: Are you also seduced? ^7-47

**7:48** Hath any one of the rulers believed in him, or of the Pharisees? ^7-48

**7:49** But this multitude, that knoweth not the law, are accursed. ^7-49

**7:50** Nicodemus said to them, (he that came to him by night, who was one of them:) ^7-50

**7:51** Doth our law judge any man, unless it first hear him, and know what he doth? ^7-51

**7:52** They answered, and said to him: Art thou also a Galilean? Search the scriptures, and see, that out of Galilee a prophet riseth not. ^7-52

**7:53** And every man returned to his own house. ^7-53
