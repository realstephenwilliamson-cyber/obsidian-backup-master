## Mūla

यावानस्य हि संतानो गुरुस्तावत्स कीर्तितः ।
सम्यग्ज्ञानमयश्चेति स्वात्मना मुच्यते ततः ॥ ^mula

yāvānasya hi saṃtāno gurustāvatsa kīrtitaḥ |
samyagjñānamayaśceti svātmanā mucyate tataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. I
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 01 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v01 12 - Chapter One, part 11#^pg425|p. 411]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 01.pdf#page=425>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 1.234cd–235ab.
>
> A teacher is said to be as great as is his (spiritual) family (saṁtāna), and if (that) is made of right knowledge (samyagjñāna), he therefore liberates himself (when a member of this spiritual family attains liberation). Thus (the saying) that starts (with the words): ‘the man of knowledge liberates his own spiritual family’ is fully proved both by reason (yukti) and scripture (āgama), for such is the extent of (just) one (true) sage. (with 1.235–236)
