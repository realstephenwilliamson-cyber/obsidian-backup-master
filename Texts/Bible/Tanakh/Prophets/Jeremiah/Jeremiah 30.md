# Jeremiah 30

## Chapter 30

**30:1** The word which came to Jeremiah from the LORD: ^30-1

**30:2** Thus said the LORD, the God of Israel: Write down in a scroll all the words that I have spoken to you. ^30-2

**30:3** For days are coming—declares the LORD—when I will restore the fortunes of My people Israel and Judah, said the LORD; and I will bring them back to the land that I gave their fathers, and they shall possess it. ^30-3

**30:4** And these are the words that the LORD spoke concerning Israel and Judah: ^30-4

**30:5** Thus said the LORD:  
We have heard cries of panic,  
Terror without relief. ^30-5

**30:6** Ask and see:  
Surely males do not bear young!  
Why then do I see every man  
With his hands on his loins  
Like a woman in labor?  
Why have all faces turned pale? ^30-6

**30:7** Ah, that day is awesome;  
There is none like it!  
It is a time of trouble for Jacob,  
But he shall be delivered from it. ^30-7

**30:8** In that day—declares the LORD of Hosts—I will break the yoke from off your neck and I will rip off your bonds. Strangers shall no longer make slaves of them; ^30-8

**30:9** instead, they shall serve the LORD their God and David, the king whom I will raise up for them. ^30-9

**30:10** But you,  
Have no fear, My servant Jacob  
—declares the LORD—  
Be not dismayed, O Israel!  
I will deliver you from far away,  
Your folk from their land of captivity.  
And Jacob shall again have calm  
And quiet with none to trouble him; ^30-10

**30:11** For I am with you to deliver you  
—declares the LORD.  
I will make an end of all the nations  
Among which I have dispersed you;  
But I will not make an end of you!  
I will not leave you unpunished,  
But will chastise you in measure. ^30-11

**30:12** For thus said the LORD:  
Your injury is incurable,  
Your wound severe; ^30-12

**30:13** No one pleads for the healing of your sickness,[^1]  
There is no remedy, no recovery for you. ^30-13

**30:14** All your lovers have forgotten you,  
They do not seek you out;  
For I have struck you as an enemy strikes,  
With cruel chastisement,  
Because your iniquity was so great  
And your sins so many. ^30-14

**30:15** Why cry out over your injury,  
That your wound is incurable?  
I did these things to you  
Because your iniquity was so great  
And your sins so many. ^30-15

**30:16** Assuredly,  
All who wanted to devour you shall be devoured,  
And every one of your foes shall go into captivity;  
Those who despoiled you shall be despoiled,  
And all who pillaged you I will give up to pillage. ^30-16

**30:17** But I will bring healing to you  
And cure you of your wounds  
—declares the LORD.  
Though they called you “Outcast,  
That Zion whom no one seeks out,” ^30-17

**30:18** Thus said the LORD:  
I will restore the fortunes of Jacob’s tents  
And have compassion upon his dwellings.  
The city shall be rebuilt on its mound,[^2]  
And the fortress in its proper place. ^30-18

**30:19** From them shall issue thanksgiving  
And the sound of dancers.  
I will multiply them,  
And they shall not be few;  
I will make them honored,  
And they shall not be humbled. ^30-19

**30:20** His children shall be as of old,  
And his community shall be established by My grace;  
And I will deal with all his oppressors. ^30-20

**30:21** His chieftain shall be one of his own,  
His ruler shall come from his midst;  
I will bring him near, that he may approach Me  
—declares the LORD—  
For who would otherwise dare approach Me? ^30-21

**30:22** You shall be My people,  
And I will be your God. ^30-22

**30:23** Lo, the storm of the LORD goes forth in fury,  
A raging tempest;  
It shall whirl down upon the head of the wicked. ^30-23

**30:24** The anger of the LORD shall not turn back  
Till it has fulfilled and completed His purposes.  
In the days to come  
You shall perceive it. ^30-24

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: I.e., on the mound of ruins left after its previous destruction.
