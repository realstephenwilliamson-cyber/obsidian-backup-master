## Mūla

भोक्तर्यात्मनि तेनेयं भेदरूपा व्यवस्थितिः ।
इति स्वकार्यप्रसवे सहकारित्वमाश्रयन् ॥ ^mula

bhoktaryātmani teneyaṃ bhedarūpā vyavasthitiḥ |
iti svakāryaprasave sahakāritvamāśrayan || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 05 - Chapter Nine, part 3#^pg113|p. 96]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=113>))
>
> The subject who experiences the various and diverse consequences (of Karma) considers within himself that this is what he must experience. This state of duality is due to this. (with 9.100cd–101ab)
>
> (Karma) takes its support (from Impurity) as an attendant cause, in order to give rise to its effects, (and so) Impurity (can be said to be) the cause of Karma as that which makes its power (to bestow its fruits) manifest. (with 9.101cd–102ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* अतश्च नास्य स्वसंपत्तावुपादानं सहकारि वा कारणं मलः, किन्तु कार्यकरणे हस्तावलम्बनप्रायः -- इत्याह --

> इति स्वकार्यप्रसवे सहकारित्वमाश्रयन् ॥
> सामर्थ्यव्यञ्जकत्वेन कर्मणः कारणं मलः ।

नहि सङ्कोचं विनास्य विचित्रफलदाने किंचित्सामर्थ्यमभिव्यज्यते - इति भावः ॥ १०१ ॥
