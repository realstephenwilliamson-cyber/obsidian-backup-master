## Chapter 23

**23:1** Job said in reply: ^23-1

**23:2** Today again my complaint is bitter;  
My strength is spent[^1] on account of my groaning. ^23-2

**23:3** Would that I knew how to reach Him,  
How to get to His dwelling-place. ^23-3

**23:4** I would set out my case before Him  
And fill my mouth with arguments. ^23-4

**23:5** I would learn what answers He had for me  
And know how He would reply to me. ^23-5

**23:6** Would He contend with me overbearingly?  
Surely He would not accuse me! ^23-6

**23:7** There the upright would be cleared by Him,  
And I would escape forever from my judge. ^23-7

**23:8** But if I go East—He is not there;  
West—I still do not perceive Him; ^23-8

**23:9** North—since He is concealed, I do not behold Him;  
South—He is hidden, and I cannot see Him. ^23-9

**23:10** But He knows the way I take;  
Would He assay me, I should emerge pure as gold. ^23-10

**23:11** I have followed in His tracks,  
Kept His way without swerving, ^23-11

**23:12** I have not deviated from what His lips commanded;  
I have treasured His words more than my daily bread. ^23-12

**23:13** He is one; who can dissuade Him?  
Whatever He desires, He does. ^23-13

**23:14** For He will bring my term to an end,  
But He has many more such at His disposal. ^23-14

**23:15** Therefore I am terrified at His presence;  
When I consider, I dread Him. ^23-15

**23:16** God has made me fainthearted;  
Shaddai has terrified me. ^23-16

**23:17** Yet I am not cut off by the darkness;  
He has concealed the thick gloom from me. ^23-17

[^1]: Lit. “My hand is heavy.”
