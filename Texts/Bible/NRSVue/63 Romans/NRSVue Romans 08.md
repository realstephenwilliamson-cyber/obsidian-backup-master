## Life in the Spirit

**8:1** Therefore there is now no condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus. ^8-1

**8:2** For the law of the Spirit^[Or *spirit*] of life in Christ Jesus has set you^[Gk *you* is singular; other ancient authorities read *me* or *us*] free from the law of sin and of death. ^8-2

**8:3** For God has done what the law, weakened by the flesh, could not do: by sending his own Son in the likeness of sinful flesh and to deal with sin,^[Or *and as a sin offering*] he condemned sin in the flesh, ^8-3

**8:4** so that the just requirement of the law might be fulfilled in us, who walk not according to the flesh but according to the Spirit.^[Or *spirit*] ^8-4

**8:5** For those who live according to the flesh set their minds on the things of the flesh, but those who live according to the Spirit^[Or *spirit*] set their minds on the things of the Spirit.^[Or *spirit*] ^8-5

**8:6** To set the mind on the flesh is death, but to set the mind on the Spirit^[Or *spirit*] is life and peace. ^8-6

**8:7** For this reason the mind that is set on the flesh is hostile to God; it does not submit to God’s law—indeed, it cannot, ^8-7

**8:8** and those who are in the flesh cannot please God. ^8-8

**8:9** But you are not in the flesh; you are in the Spirit,^[Or *spirit*] since the Spirit of God dwells in you. Anyone who does not have the Spirit of Christ does not belong to him. ^8-9

**8:10** But if Christ is in you, then the body is dead because of sin, but the Spirit^[Or *spirit*] is life because of righteousness. ^8-10

**8:11** If the Spirit of him who raised Jesus from the dead dwells in you, he who raised Christ Jesus^[Other ancient authorities read *Christ* or *the Christ* or *Jesus Christ*] from the dead will give life to your mortal bodies also through^[Other ancient authorities read *on account of*] his Spirit that dwells in you. ^8-11

**8:12** So then, brothers and sisters, we are obligated, not to the flesh, to live according to the flesh— ^8-12

**8:13** for if you live according to the flesh, you will die, but if by the Spirit you put to death the deeds of the body, you will live. ^8-13

**8:14** For all who are led by the Spirit of God are children of God. ^8-14

**8:15** For you did not receive a spirit of slavery to fall back into fear, but you received a spirit of adoption. When we cry, “Abba!^[Aramaic for *Father*] Father!” ^8-15

**8:16** it is that very Spirit bearing witness^[Or *15a spirit of adoption, by which we cry, “Abba! Father!”16The Spirit itself bears witness*] with our spirit that we are children of God, ^8-16

**8:17** and if children, then heirs: heirs of God and joint heirs with Christ, if we in fact suffer with him so that we may also be glorified with him. ^8-17

## Future glory

**8:18** I consider that the sufferings of this present time are not worth comparing with the glory about to be revealed to us. ^8-18

**8:19** For the creation waits with eager longing for the revealing of the children of God, ^8-19

**8:20** for the creation was subjected to futility, not of its own will, but by the will of the one who subjected it, in hope ^8-20

**8:21** that the creation itself will be set free from its enslavement to decay and will obtain the freedom of the glory of the children of God. ^8-21

**8:22** We know that the whole creation has been groaning together as it suffers together the pains of labor, ^8-22

**8:23** and not only the creation, but we ourselves, who have the first fruits of the Spirit, groan inwardly while we wait for adoption, the redemption of our bodies. ^8-23

**8:24** For in^[Or *by*] hope we were saved. Now hope that is seen is not hope, for who hopes for what one already sees? ^8-24

**8:25** But if we hope for what we do not see, we wait for it with patience. ^8-25

**8:26** Likewise the Spirit helps us in our weakness, for we do not know how to pray as we ought, but that very Spirit intercedes^[Other ancient authorities add *for us*] with groanings too deep for words. ^8-26

**8:27** And God,^[Gk *And the one*] who searches hearts, knows what is the mind of the Spirit, because the Spirit^[Gk *he* or *it*] intercedes for the saints according to the will of God. ^8-27

**8:28** We know that all things work together^[Other ancient authorities read *God makes all things work together* or *in all things God works*] for good for those who love God, who are called according to his purpose. ^8-28

**8:29** For those whom he foreknew he also predestined to be conformed to the image of his Son, in order that he might be the firstborn within a large family.^[Gk *among many brothers*] ^8-29

**8:30** And those whom he predestined he also called, and those whom he called he also justified, and those whom he justified he also glorified. ^8-30

## God’s love in Christ Jesus

**8:31** What then are we to say about these things? If God is for us, who is against us? ^8-31

**8:32** He who did not withhold his own Son but gave him up for all of us, how will he not with him also give us everything else? ^8-32

**8:33** Who will bring any charge against God’s elect? It is God who justifies. ^8-33

**8:34** Who is to condemn? It is Christ^[Other ancient authorities read *Christ Jesus*] who died, or rather, who was raised, who is also at the right hand of God, who also intercedes for us. ^8-34

**8:35** Who will separate us from the love of Christ? Will affliction or distress or persecution or famine or nakedness or peril or sword? ^8-35

**8:36** As it is written, “For your sake we are being killed all day long; we are accounted as sheep to be slaughtered.” ^8-36

**8:37** No, in all these things we are more than victorious through him who loved us. ^8-37

**8:38** For I am convinced that neither death, nor life, nor angels, nor rulers, nor things present, nor things to come, nor powers, ^8-38

**8:39** nor height, nor depth, nor anything else in all creation will be able to separate us from the love of God in Christ Jesus our Lord. ^8-39
