## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, July 2022 – September 2022
> p. 31 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Malini (journal), July-September 2022.pdf#page=33>))
>
> **The Signs of Rudra's Power**
>
> The penetration of Rudra's power is always well established there (in such a teacher), and when it occurs within (him), these are the signs of that one can perceive. There, this is the first sign, namely,[^32] sound unwavering devotion to Rudra. The second is the accomplishment of Mantras, which brings about immediate conviction (pratyaya). The third sign is considered to be (smṛta) dominion over all the principles. The (wise) say that the fourth sign is the successful accomplishment of (all) projects one has undertaken. One should know that the fifth is the gift of poetry (kavitva) which is beautiful (adorned as it is with all) the figures of speech, and knowledge of the purport of all the scriptures arises within him (spontaneously) without cause.[^33] (with 2.13–16)

[^32]: This sentence, i.e., MV 2/14ab, is quoted in TĀv ad 3/292-293ab (291cd-292), 13/73 and 13/117cd-119ab. MV 2/4b is quoted in 13/214d.
[^33]: MV 2/16cd is quoted in TĀ 4/45cd-46ab and explained in the following verse. It is also quoted in 15/15cd and explained in a different perspective there in the following verse.
