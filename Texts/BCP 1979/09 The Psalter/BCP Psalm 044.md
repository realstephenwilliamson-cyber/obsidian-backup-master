# Psalm 44

← [[BCP Psalm 043]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 045]] →

*Deus, auribus* · Book Two · Ninth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 044]]

**44:1** We have heard with our ears, O God,  
our forefathers have told us, \*  
the deeds you did in their days,  
in the days of old. ^44-1

**44:2** How with your hand you drove the peoples out  
and planted our forefathers in the land; \*  
how you destroyed nations and made your people flourish. ^44-2

**44:3** For they did not take the land by their sword,  
nor did their arm win the victory for them; \*  
but your right hand, your arm, and the light of your countenance,  
because you favored them. ^44-3

**44:4** You are my King and my God; \*  
you command victories for Jacob. ^44-4

**44:5** Through you we pushed back our adversaries; \*  
through your Name we trampled on those who rose up against us. ^44-5

**44:6** For I do not rely on my bow, \*  
and my sword does not give me the victory. ^44-6

**44:7** Surely, you gave us victory over our adversaries \*  
and put those who hate us to shame. ^44-7

[p. 646]

**44:8** Every day we gloried in God, \*  
and we will praise your Name for ever. ^44-8

**44:9** Nevertheless, you have rejected and humbled us \*  
and do not go forth with our armies. ^44-9

**44:10** You have made us fall back before our adversary, \*  
and our enemies have plundered us. ^44-10

**44:11** You have made us like sheep to be eaten \*  
and have scattered us among the nations. ^44-11

**44:12** You are selling your people for a trifle \*  
and are making no profit on the sale of them. ^44-12

**44:13** You have made us the scorn of our neighbors, \*  
a mockery and derision to those around us. ^44-13

**44:14** You have made us a byword among the nations, \*  
a laughing-stock among the peoples. ^44-14

**44:15** My humiliation is daily before me, \*  
and shame has covered my face; ^44-15

**44:16** Because of the taunts of the mockers and blasphemers, \*  
because of the enemy and avenger. ^44-16

**44:17** All this has come upon us; \*  
yet we have not forgotten you,  
nor have we betrayed your covenant. ^44-17

**44:18** Our heart never turned back, \*  
nor did our footsteps stray from your path; ^44-18

**44:19** Though you thrust us down into a place of misery, \*  
and covered us over with deep darkness. ^44-19

**44:20** If we have forgotten the Name of our God, \*  
or stretched out our hands to some strange god, ^44-20

**44:21** Will not God find it out? \*  
for he knows the secrets of the heart. ^44-21

[p. 647]

**44:22** Indeed, for your sake we are killed all the day long; \*  
we are accounted as sheep for the slaughter. ^44-22

**44:23** Awake, O Lord! why are you sleeping? \*  
Arise! do not reject us for ever. ^44-23

**44:24** Why have you hidden your face \*  
and forgotten our affliction and oppression? ^44-24

**44:25** We sink down into the dust; \*  
our body cleaves to the ground. ^44-25

**44:26** Rise up, and help us, \*  
and save us, for the sake of your steadfast love. ^44-26
