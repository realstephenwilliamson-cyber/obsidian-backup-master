## Mūla

मानतैव तु सा प्राच्यप्रमातृपरिकल्पिता ।
उच्छलन्त्यपि संवित्तिः कालक्रमविवर्जनात् ॥ ^mula

mānataiva tu sā prācyapramātṛparikalpitā |
ucchalantyapi saṃvittiḥ kālakramavivarjanāt || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. II
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 02 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v02 08 - Chapter Three, part 5#^pg250|p. 234]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 02.pdf#page=250>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 3.128cd–129ab.
>
> It is called subjectivity on that leve (of Māyā on which consciousness is obscured by objectivity), because it is fit (to become such, and can moreover distinguish one object from another). This (level) corresponds to that of the means of knowledge (mentally) conceived (parikalpita) by the (supreme) subject, that precedes it (and on which it depends). (with 3.127cd–128ab)
>
> Even when consciousness pours out of itself in the form of subject and object etc., free as it is of the sequence of time (kālakrama), it is in an emergent (uditāā) (state which is) full (pārnā) (and all-inclusive). (with 3.128cd–129ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* ननु परा संविदेव यदि मानादिरूपेणापि प्रस्फुरेत् तत्तस्या अस्तमय एव किं न स्यात् ? -- इत्याशङ्क्याह—

> उच्छलन्त्यपि संवित्तिः कालक्रमविवर्जनात् ॥
> उदितैव सती पूर्णा मातृमेयादिरूपिणी ।

मातृमेयादिरूपत्वेन बहिरुल्लसन्त्यपि संवित्तिः शकृद्विभातोऽयमात्मा"इति न्यायेन अवभासनक्रियाविच्छेदाभावात्कालानवच्छेदादुदितैव संविन्मात्ररूपत्वाप्रच्यावात् नियमेन अनस्तमितरूपा -- इत्यर्थः । अत एव पूर्णा स्वात्ममात्रविश्रान्तिरूपत्वादनन्यापेक्षिणी -- इत्यर्थः ॥ १२८ ॥
