## Mūla

अनुगृह्य शिवः साक्षन्मन्त्रेशत्वे नियुक्तवान् ।
ते स्वगोचरमासाद्य भुक्तिमुक्तिफलार्थिनाम् ॥ ^mula

anugṛhya śivaḥ sākṣanmantreśatve niyuktavān |
te svagocaram āsādya bhuktimuktiphalārthinām || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2022 – June 2022
> p. 46 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=22>))
>
> **The Mantras**
>
> Thus in this way, everywhere in the universe the one hundred and eight Rudras, beginning with Aṅguṣṭhamātra and the rest, were by virtue of their competence (*yogyatā*) graced by Śiva, who having done so, appointed them directly to the rank of Lords of Mantra. (with 1.37–38ab)

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2022 – June 2022
> p. 46 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=22>))
>
> These, having assumed their own sphere (of authority) (*gocara*), dispense to those like Brahmā, who aspire to liberation and worldly benefits (*bhoga*), (their appropriate) fruit accord to their own power. They too subsequently instruct the seers (*ṛṣi*) and these great lords, the Manus, the knowledge of what should be adopted and discarded, revealed by Śiva. (with 1.38cd–40ab)
