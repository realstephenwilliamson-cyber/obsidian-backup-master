# Psalm 33 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 032|← Psalm 32]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 034|Psalm 34 →]]

## The greatness and goodness of God

**33:1** Rejoice in the LORD, O you righteous. Praise befits the upright. ^33-1

**33:2** Praise the LORD with the lyre; make melody to him with the harp of ten strings. ^33-2

**33:3** Sing to him a new song; play skillfully on the strings, with loud shouts. ^33-3

**33:4** For the word of the LORD is upright, and all his work is done in faithfulness. ^33-4

**33:5** He loves righteousness and justice; the earth is full of the steadfast love of the LORD. ^33-5

**33:6** By the word of the LORD the heavens were made and all their host by the breath of his mouth. ^33-6

**33:7** He gathered the waters of the sea as in a bottle; he put the deeps in storehouses. ^33-7

**33:8** Let all the earth fear the LORD; let all the inhabitants of the world stand in awe of him, ^33-8

**33:9** for he spoke, and it came to be; he commanded, and it stood firm. ^33-9

**33:10** The LORD brings the counsel of the nations to nothing; he frustrates the plans of the peoples. ^33-10

**33:11** The counsel of the LORD stands forever, the thoughts of his heart to all generations. ^33-11

**33:12** Happy is the nation whose God is the LORD, the people whom he has chosen as his heritage. ^33-12

**33:13** The LORD looks down from heaven; he sees all humankind. ^33-13

**33:14** From where he sits enthroned he watches all the inhabitants of the earth— ^33-14

**33:15** he who fashions the hearts of them all and observes all their deeds. ^33-15

**33:16** A king is not saved by his great army; a warrior is not delivered by his great strength. ^33-16

**33:17** The war horse is a vain hope for victory, and by its great might it cannot save. ^33-17

**33:18** Truly the eye of the LORD is on those who fear him, on those who hope in his steadfast love, ^33-18

**33:19** to deliver their soul from death and to keep them alive in famine. ^33-19

**33:20** Our soul waits for the LORD; he is our help and shield. ^33-20

**33:21** Our heart is glad in him because we trust in his holy name. ^33-21

**33:22** Let your steadfast love, O LORD, be upon us, even as we hope in you. ^33-22
