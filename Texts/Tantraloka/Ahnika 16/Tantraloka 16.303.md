## Mūla

कर्मणां शोधनं कार्यं बुभुक्षोर्न शुभात्मनाम् ।
यः पुनर्लौकिकं भोगं राज्यस्वर्गादिकं शिशुः ॥ ^mula

karmaṇāṃ śodhanaṃ kāryaṃ bubhukṣorna śubhātmanām |
yaḥ punarlaukikaṃ bhogaṃ rājyasvargādikaṃ śiśuḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 07 - Chapter Sixteen, part 3#^pg155|p. 135]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=155>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 16.302cd–303ab.
>
> (Only) the bad actions (karman), past, present and future of one who desires worldly benefits should be purified, not the (good) auspicious ones. (However,) the good actions (should also be purified) in the case of a disciple who, on the contrary, having abandoned worldly benefits such as a kingdom, (residence) in heaven and the like, desires (only) other-worldly (lokottara) benefits. (with 16.302cd–304ab)

## Viveka

> यः पुनर्लौकिकं भोगं राज्यस्वर्गादिकं शिशुः ॥
> त्यक्त्वा लोकोत्तरं भोगमीप्सुस्तस्य शुभेष्वपि ।
