## Mūla

अस्ति चातिशयः कश्चित्तासामप्युत्तरोत्तरम् ।
यो विवेकधनैर्धीरैः स्फुटीकृत्यापि दर्श्यते ॥ ^mula

asti cātiśayaḥ kaścittāsāmapyuttarottaram |
yo vivekadhanairdhīraiḥ sphuṭīkṛtyāpi darśyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 13 - Chapter Ten, part 4#^pg435|p. 416]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=435>))

> The wise, who are rich in discrimination, have clearly explained that of those (three Goddesses), the previous with respect to what follows possesses a certain (superior) excellence.
