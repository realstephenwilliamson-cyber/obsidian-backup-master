## Prayer for protection from enemies

*To the leader. A Psalm of David.*

**64:1** Hear my voice, O God, in my complaint; preserve my life from the dread enemy. ^64-1

**64:2** Hide me from the secret plots of the wicked, from the scheming of evildoers, ^64-2

**64:3** who whet their tongues like swords, who aim bitter words like arrows, ^64-3

**64:4** shooting from ambush at the blameless; they shoot suddenly and without fear. ^64-4

**64:5** They hold fast to their evil purpose; they talk of laying snares secretly, thinking, “Who can see us?^[Syr Jerome: Heb *them*] ^64-5

**64:6** Who can search out our crimes?^[Cn: Heb *They search out crimes*] We have thought out a cunningly conceived plot.” For the human heart and mind are deep. ^64-6

**64:7** But God will shoot his arrow at them; they will be wounded suddenly. ^64-7

**64:8** Because of their tongue he will bring them to ruin;^[Cn: Heb *They will bring him to ruin, their tongue being against them*] all who see them will shake with horror. ^64-8

**64:9** Then everyone will fear; they will tell what God has brought about and ponder what he has done. ^64-9

**64:10** Let the righteous rejoice in the LORD and take refuge in him. Let all the upright in heart glory. ^64-10
