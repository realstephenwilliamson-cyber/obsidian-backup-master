## Mūla

मेरोः पश्चिमतो गन्धमादो यस्तस्य पश्चिमे ।
केतुमालं कुलाद्रीणां सप्तकेन विभूषितम् ॥ ^mula

meroḥ paścimato gandhamādo yastasya paścime |
ketumālaṃ kulādrīṇāṃ saptakena vibhūṣitam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 04 - Chapter Eight, part 2#^pg137|p. 119]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=137>))

> (The land of) Ketumāla is to the west of Gandhamāda, which is to the west of Meru. It is adorned with seven principal mountains (kulādri).
