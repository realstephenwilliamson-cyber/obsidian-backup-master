# Psalm 114 (NRSVue)

## God’s wonders at the exodus

**114:1** ^[Psalms 114–115 are a single psalm in the earliest witnesses]When Israel went out from Egypt, the house of Jacob from a people of strange language, ^114-1

**114:2** Judah became God’s^[Heb *his*] sanctuary, Israel his dominion. ^114-2

**114:3** The sea looked and fled; Jordan turned back. ^114-3

**114:4** The mountains skipped like rams, the hills like lambs. ^114-4

**114:5** Why is it, O sea, that you flee? O Jordan, that you turn back? ^114-5

**114:6** O mountains, that you skip like rams? O hills, like lambs? ^114-6

**114:7** Tremble, O earth, at the presence of the LORD, at the presence of the God of Jacob, ^114-7

**114:8** who turns the rock into a pool of water, the flint into a spring of water. ^114-8
