# Psalm 118 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 117|← Psalm 117]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 119|Psalm 119 →]]

## A song of victory

**118:1** O give thanks to the LORD, for he is good; his steadfast love endures forever! ^118-1

**118:2** Let Israel say, “His steadfast love endures forever.” ^118-2

**118:3** Let the house of Aaron say, “His steadfast love endures forever.” ^118-3

**118:4** Let those who fear the LORD say, “His steadfast love endures forever.” ^118-4

**118:5** Out of my distress I called on the LORD; the LORD answered me and set me in a broad place. ^118-5

**118:6** With the LORD on my side I do not fear. What can mortals do to me? ^118-6

**118:7** The LORD is on my side to help me; I shall look in triumph on those who hate me. ^118-7

**118:8** It is better to take refuge in the LORD than to put confidence in mortals. ^118-8

**118:9** It is better to take refuge in the LORD than to put confidence in princes. ^118-9

**118:10** All nations surrounded me; in the name of the LORD I cut them off! ^118-10

**118:11** They surrounded me, surrounded me on every side; in the name of the LORD I cut them off! ^118-11

**118:12** They surrounded me like bees; they blazed^[Gk: Heb *were extinguished*] like a fire of thorns; in the name of the LORD I cut them off! ^118-12

**118:13** I was pushed hard,^[Gk Syr Jerome: Heb *You pushed me hard*] so that I was falling, but the LORD helped me. ^118-13

**118:14** The LORD is my strength and my might; he has become my salvation. ^118-14

**118:15** There are glad songs of victory in the tents of the righteous: “The right hand of the LORD does valiantly; ^118-15

**118:16** the right hand of the LORD is exalted; the right hand of the LORD does valiantly.” ^118-16

**118:17** I shall not die, but I shall live and recount the deeds of the LORD. ^118-17

**118:18** The LORD has punished me severely, but he did not give me over to death. ^118-18

**118:19** Open to me the gates of righteousness, that I may enter through them and give thanks to the LORD. ^118-19

**118:20** This is the gate of the LORD; the righteous shall enter through it. ^118-20

**118:21** I thank you that you have answered me and have become my salvation. ^118-21

**118:22** The stone that the builders rejected has become the chief cornerstone. ^118-22

**118:23** This is the LORD’s doing; it is marvelous in our eyes. ^118-23

**118:24** This is the day that the LORD has made; let us rejoice and be glad in it.^[Or *in him*] ^118-24

**118:25** Save us, we beseech you, O LORD! O LORD, we beseech you, give us success! ^118-25

**118:26** Blessed is the one who comes in the name of the LORD.^[Or *Blessed in the name of theLORDis the one who comes*] We bless you from the house of the LORD. ^118-26

**118:27** The LORD is God, and he has given us light. Bind the festal procession with branches, up to the horns of the altar.^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^118-27

**118:28** You are my God, and I will give thanks to you; you are my God; I will extol you. ^118-28

**118:29** O give thanks to the LORD, for he is good, for his steadfast love endures forever. ^118-29
