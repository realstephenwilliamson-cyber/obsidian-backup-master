## Mūla

परा परापरा चेयमपरा च सदोदिता ।
सृष्टिसंस्थितिसंहारैस्तासां प्रत्येकतस्त्रिधा ॥ ^mula

parā parāparā ceyamaparā ca sadoditā |
sṛṣṭisaṃsthitisaṃhāraistāsāṃ pratyekatastridhā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 03 - Chapter Five, part 1#^pg43|p. 28]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=43>))
>
> It is also the (goddesses) Parā (Supreme), Parāparā (Middling) and Aparā (Lower), who are perpetually active (sadodita) (in every phase of the cycle of manifestation). (with 5.25ab)
>
> Each one (of these three) is threefold, according to whether it is in a state of creation, persistence or withdrawal. (with 5.25cd)
