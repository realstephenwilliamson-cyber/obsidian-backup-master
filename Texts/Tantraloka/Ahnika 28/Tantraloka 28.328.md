## Mūla

इत्यादिमृतिभोगोऽयं देहे न त्यजनं तनोः ।
यस्त्वसौ क्षण एवैकश्चरमः प्राणनात्मकः ॥ ^mula

ityādimṛtibhogo'yaṃ dehe na tyajanaṃ tanoḥ |
yastvasau kṣaṇa evaikaścaramaḥ prāṇanātmakaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 06 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 4#^pg158|p. 141]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=158>))
>
> There (in that case), the obscuration of the senses, the difficulty in breathing, that runs counter (to their operation) and the like, is the experience of death (that takes place) in the body; it is not the (actual) quitting of the body. (with 28.327cd–328ab)
>
> That single final instant after which this body becomes (lifeless) like a piece of wood or wall is (that of the residual) life force (prāṇana). The fraction of the (tiny) part of time (when the soul) abandons the body separates it from the body. Thus, it is free of the (sensations of) pleasure and pain etc. of that body. Whatever (the dying man) recollects in that (moment,) due to a previous latent impression (of his Karma) awakened by (the power of) destiny, practice, an intense descent of the power (of grace) or the like, is the nature he attains, be it one that is happy, suffers, (or) is deluded. (with 28.328cd–331)
