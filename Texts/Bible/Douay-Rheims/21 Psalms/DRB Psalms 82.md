# Psalms 82 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 81|← Psalms 81]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 83|Psalms 83 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 82|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**82:1** A canticle of a psalm for Asaph. ^82-1

**82:2** O God, who shall be like to thee? hold not thy peace, neither be thou still, O God. ^82-2

**82:3** For lo, thy enemies have made a noise: and they that hate thee have lifted up the head. ^82-3

**82:4** They have taken a malicious counsel against thy people, and have consulted against thy saints. ^82-4

**82:5** They have said: Come and let us destroy them, so that they be not a nation: and let the name of Israel be remembered no more. ^82-5

**82:6** For they have contrived with one consent: they have made a covenant together against thee, ^82-6

**82:7** The tabernacles of the Edomites, and the Ismahelites: Moab, and the Agarens, ^82-7

**82:8** Gebal, and Ammon and Amalec: the Philistines, with the inhabitants of Tyre. ^82-8

**82:9** Yea, and the Assyrian also is joined with them: they are come to the aid of the sons of Lot. ^82-9

**82:10** Do to them as thou didst to Madian and to Sisara: as to Jabin at the brook of Cisson. ^82-10

**82:11** Who perished at Endor: and became as dung for the earth. ^82-11

**82:12** Make their princes like Oreb, and Zeb, and Zebee, and Salmana. All their princes, ^82-12

**82:13** Who have said: Let us possess the sanctuary of God for an inheritance. ^82-13

**82:14** O my God, make them like a wheel; and as stubble before the wind. ^82-14

**82:15** As fire which burneth the wood: and as a flame burning mountains: ^82-15

**82:16** So shalt thou pursue them with thy tempest: and shalt trouble them in thy wrath. ^82-16

**82:17** Fill their faces with shame; and they shall seek thy name, O Lord. ^82-17

**82:18** Let them be ashamed and troubled for ever and ever: and let them be confounded and perish. ^82-18

**82:19** And let them know that the Lord is thy name: thou alone art the most High over all the earth. ^82-19
