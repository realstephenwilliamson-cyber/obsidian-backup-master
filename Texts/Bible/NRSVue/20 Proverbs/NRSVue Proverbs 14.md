# Proverbs 14 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Proverbs 13|← Proverbs 13]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Proverbs 15|Proverbs 15 →]]

**14:1** The wise woman^[Heb *Wisdom of women*] builds her house, but the foolish tears it down with her own hands. ^14-1

**14:2** Those who walk uprightly fear the LORD, but one who is devious in conduct despises him. ^14-2

**14:3** The talk of fools is a rod for their backs,^[Cn: Heb *a rod of pride*] but the lips of the wise preserve them. ^14-3

**14:4** Where there are no oxen, there is no grain; abundant crops come by the strength of the ox. ^14-4

**14:5** A faithful witness does not lie, but a false witness breathes out lies. ^14-5

**14:6** A scoffer seeks wisdom in vain, but knowledge is easy for one who understands. ^14-6

**14:7** Leave the presence of a fool, for there you do not find words of knowledge. ^14-7

**14:8** It is the wisdom of the clever to understand where they go, but the folly of fools misleads. ^14-8

**14:9** Fools mock at the guilt offering,^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] but the upright enjoy God’s favor. ^14-9

**14:10** The heart knows its own bitterness, and no stranger shares its joy. ^14-10

**14:11** The house of the wicked is destroyed, but the tent of the upright flourishes. ^14-11

**14:12** There is a way that seems right to a person, but its end is the way to death.^[Heb *ways of death*] ^14-12

**14:13** Even in laughter the heart is sad, and the end of joy is grief. ^14-13

**14:14** The perverse get what their ways deserve, and the good, what their deeds deserve.^[Cn: Heb *from upon him*] ^14-14

**14:15** The simple believe everything, but the clever consider their steps. ^14-15

**14:16** The wise are cautious and turn away from evil, but the fool throws off restraint and is careless. ^14-16

**14:17** One who is quick-tempered acts foolishly, and the schemer is hated. ^14-17

**14:18** The simple are adorned with^[Or *inherit*] folly, but the clever are crowned with knowledge. ^14-18

**14:19** The evil bow down before the good, the wicked at the gates of the righteous. ^14-19

**14:20** The poor are disliked even by their neighbors, but the rich have many friends. ^14-20

**14:21** Those who despise their neighbors are sinners, but happy are those who are kind to the poor. ^14-21

**14:22** Do not those who plan evil err? Those who plan good find loyalty and faithfulness. ^14-22

**14:23** In all toil there is profit, but mere talk leads only to poverty. ^14-23

**14:24** The crown of the wise is their wealth, but folly is the garland^[Cn: Heb *is the folly*] of fools. ^14-24

**14:25** A truthful witness saves lives, but one who utters lies is a betrayer. ^14-25

**14:26** In the fear of the LORD one has strong confidence, and one’s children will have a refuge. ^14-26

**14:27** The fear of the LORD is a fountain of life, so that one may avoid the snares of death. ^14-27

**14:28** The glory of a king is a multitude of people; without people a prince is ruined. ^14-28

**14:29** Whoever is slow to anger has great understanding, but one who has a hasty temper exalts folly. ^14-29

**14:30** A tranquil mind gives life to the flesh, but jealousy makes the bones rot. ^14-30

**14:31** Those who oppress the poor insult their Maker, but those who are kind to the needy honor him. ^14-31

**14:32** The wicked are overthrown by their evildoing, but the righteous find a refuge in their integrity.^[Gk Syr: Heb *in their death*] ^14-32

**14:33** Wisdom is at home in the mind of one who has understanding, but it is not^[Gk Syr: Heb lacks *not*] known in the heart of fools. ^14-33

**14:34** Righteousness exalts a nation, but sin is a reproach to any people. ^14-34

**14:35** A servant who deals wisely has the king’s favor, but his wrath falls on one who acts shamefully. ^14-35
