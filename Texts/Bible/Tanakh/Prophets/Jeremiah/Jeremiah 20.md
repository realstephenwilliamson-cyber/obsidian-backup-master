## Chapter 20

**20:1** Pashhur son of Immer, the priest who was chief officer of the House of the LORD, heard Jeremiah prophesy these things. ^20-1

**20:2** Pashhur thereupon had Jeremiah flogged and put in the cell[^1] at the Upper Benjamin Gate in the House of the LORD. ^20-2

**20:3** The next day, Pashhur released Jeremiah from the cell.  
But Jeremiah said to him, “The LORD has named you not Pashhur, but Magor-missabib.[^2] ^20-3

**20:4** For thus said the LORD: I am going to deliver you and all your friends over to terror: they will fall by the sword of their enemies while you look on. I will deliver all Judah into the hands of the king of Babylon; he will exile them to Babylon or put them to the sword. ^20-4

**20:5** And I will deliver all the wealth, all the riches, and all the prized possessions of this city, and I will also deliver all the treasures of the kings of Judah into the hands of their enemies: they shall seize them as plunder and carry them off to Babylon. ^20-5

**20:6** As for you, Pashhur, and all who live in your house, you shall go into captivity. You shall come to Babylon; there you shall die and there you shall be buried, and so shall all your friends to whom you prophesied falsely.” ^20-6

**20:7** You enticed me, O LORD, and I was enticed;  
You overpowered me and You prevailed.  
I have become a constant laughingstock,  
Everyone jeers at me. ^20-7

**20:8** For every time I speak, I must cry out,  
Must shout, “Lawlessness and rapine!”  
For the word of the LORD causes me  
Constant disgrace and contempt. ^20-8

**20:9** I thought, “I will not mention Him,  
No more will I speak in His name”—  
But [His word] was like a raging fire in my heart,  
Shut up in my bones;  
I could not hold it in, I was helpless. ^20-9

**20:10** I heard the whispers of the crowd—  
Terror all around:  
“Inform! Let us inform against him!”  
All my [supposed] friends  
Are waiting for me to stumble:  
“Perhaps he can be entrapped,  
And we can prevail against him  
And take our vengeance on him.” ^20-10

**20:11** But the LORD is with me like a mighty warrior;  
Therefore my persecutors shall stumble;  
They shall not prevail and shall not succeed.  
They shall be utterly shamed  
With a humiliation for all time,  
Which shall not be forgotten. ^20-11

**20:12** O LORD of Hosts, You who test the righteous,  
Who examine the heart and the mind,  
Let me see Your retribution upon them,  
For I lay my case before You. ^20-12

**20:13** Sing unto the LORD,  
Praise the LORD,  
For He has rescued the needy  
From the hands of evildoers! ^20-13

**20:14** Accursed be the day  
That I was born!  
Let not the day be blessed  
When my mother bore me! ^20-14

**20:15** Accursed be the man  
Who brought my father the news  
And said, “A boy  
Is born to you,”  
And gave him such joy! ^20-15

**20:16** Let that man[^3] become like the cities  
Which the LORD overthrew without relenting!  
Let him hear shrieks in the morning  
And battle shouts at noontide— ^20-16

**20:17** Because he did not kill me before birth  
So that my mother might be my grave,  
And her womb big [with me] for all time. ^20-17

**20:18** Why did I ever issue from the womb,  
To see misery and woe,  
To spend all my days in shame! ^20-18

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: I.e., “Terror all around”; cf. v. 10.
[^3]: Emendation yields “day.”
