## The two ways

**1:1** Happy are those who do not follow the advice of the wicked or take the path that sinners tread or sit in the seat of scoffers, ^1-1

**1:2** but their delight is in the law of the LORD, and on his law they meditate day and night. ^1-2

**1:3** They are like trees planted by streams of water, which yield their fruit in its season, and their leaves do not wither. In all that they do, they prosper. ^1-3

**1:4** The wicked are not so but are like chaff that the wind drives away. ^1-4

**1:5** Therefore the wicked will not stand in the judgment nor sinners in the congregation of the righteous, ^1-5

**1:6** for the LORD watches over the way of the righteous, but the way of the wicked will perish. ^1-6
