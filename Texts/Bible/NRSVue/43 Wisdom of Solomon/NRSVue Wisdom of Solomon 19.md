# Wisdom of Solomon 19 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Wisdom of Solomon 18|← Wisdom of Solomon 18]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · —

## The Red sea

**19:1** But the ungodly were assailed to the end by pitiless anger, for God^[Gk *he*] knew in advance even their future actions: ^19-1

**19:2** how, though they themselves had permitted^[Other ancient authorities read *had changed their minds to permit*] your people to depart and hastily sent them out, they would change their minds and pursue them. ^19-2

**19:3** For while they were still engaged in mourning and were lamenting at the graves of their dead, they reached another foolish decision and pursued as fugitives those whom they had begged and compelled to leave. ^19-3

**19:4** For the fate they deserved drew them on to this end and made them forget what had happened, in order that they might fill up the punishment that their torments still lacked ^19-4

**19:5** and that your people might experience^[Other ancient authorities read *accomplish*] an incredible journey, but they themselves might meet a strange death. ^19-5

## God guides and protects his people

**19:6** For the whole creation in its nature was fashioned anew, complying with your commands, so that your children might be kept unharmed. ^19-6

**19:7** The cloud was seen overshadowing the camp and dry land emerging where water had stood before, an unhindered way out of the Red Sea and a grassy plain out of the raging waves, ^19-7

**19:8** where those protected by your hand passed through as one nation, after gazing on marvelous wonders. ^19-8

**19:9** For they ranged like horses and leaped like lambs, praising you, O Lord, who delivered them. ^19-9

**19:10** For they still recalled the events of their sojourn, how instead of producing animals the earth brought forth gnats, and instead of fish the river spewed out vast numbers of frogs. ^19-10

**19:11** Afterward they saw also a new kind of bird, when desire led them to ask for luxurious food, ^19-11

**19:12** for, to give them relief, quails came up from the sea. ^19-12

## The punishment of the Egyptians

**19:13** The punishments did not come upon the sinners without prior signs in the violence of thunder, for they justly suffered because of their wicked acts, for they practiced a more bitter hatred of strangers. ^19-13

**19:14** Others had refused to receive strangers when they came to them, but these made slaves of guests who were their benefactors. ^19-14

**19:15** And not only so, but while punishment of some sort will come upon the former for having received strangers with hostility, ^19-15

**19:16** the latter, having first received them with festal celebrations, afterward afflicted with terrible sufferings those who had already shared the same rights. ^19-16

**19:17** They were stricken also with loss of sight—just as were those at the door of the righteous man—when, surrounded by yawning darkness, all of them tried to find the way through their own doors. ^19-17

## A new harmony in nature

**19:18** For the elements changed^[Gk *changing*] places with one another, as on a harp the notes vary the nature of the rhythm, while each note remains the same.^[Meaning of Gk uncertain] This may be clearly inferred from the sight of what took place. ^19-18

**19:19** For land animals were transformed into water creatures, and creatures that swim moved over to the land. ^19-19

**19:20** Fire even in water retained its normal power, and water forgot its fire-quenching nature. ^19-20

**19:21** Flames, on the contrary, failed to consume the flesh of perishable creatures that walked among them, nor did they melt^[Cn: Gk *nor could be melted*] the crystalline, quick-melting kind of heavenly food. ^19-21

## Conclusion

**19:22** For in everything, O Lord, you have exalted and glorified your people, and you have not neglected to help them at all times and in all places. ^19-22
