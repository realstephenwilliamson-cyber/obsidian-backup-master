# Psalm 80 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 079|← Psalm 79]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 081|Psalm 81 →]]

## Prayer for Israel’s restoration

*To the leader: on Lilies, a Covenant. Of Asaph. A Psalm.*

**80:1** Give ear, O Shepherd of Israel, you who lead Joseph like a flock! You who are enthroned upon the cherubim, shine forth ^80-1

**80:2** before Ephraim and Benjamin and Manasseh. Stir up your might, and come to save us! ^80-2

**80:3** Restore us, O God; let your face shine, that we may be saved. ^80-3

**80:4** O LORD God of hosts, how long will you be angry with your people’s prayers? ^80-4

**80:5** You have fed them with the bread of tears and given them tears to drink in full measure. ^80-5

**80:6** You make us the scorn^[Syr: Heb *strife*] of our neighbors; our enemies laugh among themselves. ^80-6

**80:7** Restore us, O God of hosts; let your face shine, that we may be saved. ^80-7

**80:8** You brought a vine out of Egypt; you drove out the nations and planted it. ^80-8

**80:9** You cleared the ground for it; it took deep root and filled the land. ^80-9

**80:10** The mountains were covered with its shade, the mighty cedars with its branches; ^80-10

**80:11** it sent out its branches to the sea and its shoots to the River. ^80-11

**80:12** Why then have you broken down its walls, so that all who pass along the way pluck its fruit? ^80-12

**80:13** The boar from the forest ravages it, and all that move in the field feed on it. ^80-13

**80:14** Turn again, O God of hosts; look down from heaven and see; have regard for this vine, ^80-14

**80:15** the stock that your right hand planted.^[Heb adds from 80.17*and upon the one whom you made strong for yourself*] ^80-15

**80:16** It has been burned with fire; it has been cut down; may they perish at the rebuke of your countenance. ^80-16

**80:17** But let your hand be upon the one at your right hand, the one whom you made strong for yourself. ^80-17

**80:18** Then we will never turn back from you; give us life, and we will call on your name. ^80-18

**80:19** Restore us, O LORD God of hosts; let your face shine, that we may be saved. ^80-19
