# Wisdom of Solomon 4 (Douay-Rheims)

*Wisdom 4 in the Douay-Rheims*

[[DRB Wisdom of Solomon 03|← Wisdom of Solomon 3]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Wisdom of Solomon 05|Wisdom of Solomon 5 →]]

**4:1** O how beautiful is the chaste generation with glory: for the memory thereof is immortal: because it is known both with God and with men. ^4-1

**4:2** When it is present, they imitate it: and they desire it when it hath withdrawn itself, and it triumpheth crowned for ever, winning the reward of undefiled conflicts. ^4-2

**4:3** But the multiplied brood of the wicked shall not thrive, and bastard slips shall not take deep root, nor any fast foundation. ^4-3

**4:4** And if they flourish in branches for a time, yet standing not fast, they shall be shaken with the wind, and through the force of winds they shall be rooted out. ^4-4

**4:5** For the branches not being perfect, shall be broken, and their fruits shall be unprofitable, and sour to eat, and fit for nothing. ^4-5

**4:6** For the children that are born of unlawful beds, are witnesses of wickedness against their parents in their trial. ^4-6

**4:7** But the just man, if he be prevented with death, shall be in rest. ^4-7

**4:8** For venerable old age is not that of long time, nor counted by the number of years: but the understanding of a man is grey hairs. ^4-8

**4:9** And a spotless life is old age. ^4-9

**4:10** He pleased God and was beloved, and living among sinners he was translated. ^4-10

**4:11** He was taken away lest wickedness should alter his understanding, or deceit beguile his soul. ^4-11

**4:12** For the bewitching of vanity obscureth good things, and the wandering of concupiscence overturneth the innocent mind. ^4-12

**4:13** Being made perfect in a short space, he fulfilled a long time: ^4-13

**4:14** For his soul pleased God: therefore he hastened to bring him out of the midst of iniquities: but the people see this, and understand not, nor lay up such things in their hearts: ^4-14

**4:15** That the grace of God, and his mercy is with his saints, and that he hath respect to his chosen. ^4-15

**4:16** But the just that is dead, condemneth the wicked that are living, and youth soon ended, the long life of the unjust. ^4-16

**4:17** For they shall see the end of the wise man, and shall not understand what God hath designed for him, and why the Lord hath set him in safety. ^4-17

**4:18** They shall see him, and shall despise him: but the Lord shall laugh them to scorn. ^4-18

**4:19** And they shall fall after this without honour, and be a reproach among the dead for ever: for he shall burst them puffed up and speechless, and shall shake them from the foundations, and they shall be utterly laid waste: they shall be in sorrow, and their memory shall perish. ^4-19

**4:20** They shall come with fear at the thought of their sins, and their iniquities shall stand against them to convict them. ^4-20
