# Psalm 67

← [[BCP Psalm 066]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 068]] →

*Deus misereatur* · Book Two · Twelfth Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 067]]

**67:1** May God be merciful to us and bless us, \*  
show us the light of his countenance and come to us. ^67-1

**67:2** Let your ways be known upon earth, \*  
your saving health among all nations. ^67-2

**67:3** Let the peoples praise you, O God; \*  
let all the peoples praise you. ^67-3

**67:4** Let the nations be glad and sing for joy, \*  
for you judge the peoples with equity  
and guide all the nations upon earth. ^67-4

**67:5** Let the peoples praise you, O God; \*  
let all the peoples praise you. ^67-5

**67:6** The earth has brought forth her increase; \*  
may God, our own God, give us his blessing. ^67-6

**67:7** May God give us his blessing, \*  
and may all the ends of the earth stand in awe of him. ^67-7
