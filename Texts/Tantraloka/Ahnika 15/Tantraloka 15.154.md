## Mūla

कुर्वन्ति ते शिवा एव तान्पूर्णान्प्रति किं फलम् ।
विनापि ज्ञानयोगाभ्यां क्रिया न्यासार्चनादिका ॥ ^mula

kurvanti te śivā eva tānpūrṇānprati kiṃ phalam |
vināpi jñānayogābhyāṃ kriyā nyāsārcanādikā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 04 - Chapter Fifteen, part 3#^pg116|p. 98]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=116>))
>
> (Thus,) those who are engaged in this kind of worship (arcana) in order to penetrate (and become one with) the universe (viśva) that is full (and unlimited) Śiva are themselves Śivas. For them, complete (and perfect as they are), what (other) reward (could there be)? (with 15.153cd–154ab)
>
> (Thus) ritual, which comprises the deposition (of Mantras) (nyāsa), worship (arcana) and the rest, grants, even without (cultivating) knowledge and (practicing) Yoga, the supreme fruit (of liberation) because, (performed) in this way, it imparts the realization of oneness. (with 15.154cd–155ab)
