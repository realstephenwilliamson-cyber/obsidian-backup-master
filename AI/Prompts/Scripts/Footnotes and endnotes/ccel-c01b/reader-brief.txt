PLACING FOOTNOTES ON THEIR PAGE (reading task; change nothing but your own output file)

The text is from a converted book (Schaff's Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers, CCEL edition). Each case gives the FOOTNOTE TEXT that the
conversion put at the foot of one page (page A), as raw Markdown (links look like [[Target|label]]; numbers at the start of footnotes
are footnote numbers; a bare 3-digit number may be the 1888 print-page number). The conversion sometimes put some of the NEXT page's
footnotes (page B) at the end of page A's text, and sometimes jumbled the order (a Greek word before its own number, and the like).

You get two images: the lower part of page A and of page B. Compare the text with what each page prints, and decide:
- "move": the text from a certain point to its very end is printed on page B (not on A), and everything before that point is printed on A.
  Give that point as "start_quote": the first 4 to 10 words of the part that belongs to B, copied EXACTLY from the raw text (same
  characters, brackets, asterisks and link markup), so that a program can find it. It must occur once in the case's text.
- "mixed": some of the text is printed on B but it is not one block at the end (pieces of A and B alternate). Say which in "note".
- "none": all of the text is printed on page A.
- "unclear": you cannot tell (say why).
Output one JSON object per case, as JSON Lines, at the path given in your task:
{"case": "h00", "decision": "move", "start_quote": "...", "note": "at most 25 words"}
Never write the divine name (the four-letter name of God) in any form; write "the divine name". Open the images with the Read tool.
Check that every case has one line and each parses as JSON. Reply with one short line of counts.
