# Lamentations 5 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Lamentations 04|← Lamentations 4]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · —

## A plea for mercy

**5:1** Remember, O LORD, what has befallen us; look, and see our disgrace! ^5-1

**5:2** Our inheritance has been turned over to strangers, our homes to aliens. ^5-2

**5:3** We have become orphans, fatherless; our mothers are like widows. ^5-3

**5:4** We must pay for the water we drink; the wood we get must be bought. ^5-4

**5:5** With a yoke^[Symmachus: Heb lacks *With a yoke*] on our necks we are hard driven; we are weary; we are given no rest. ^5-5

**5:6** We have made a pact with^[Heb *have given the hand to*] Egypt and Assyria to get enough bread. ^5-6

**5:7** Our ancestors sinned; they are no more, and we bear their iniquities. ^5-7

**5:8** Slaves rule over us; there is no one to deliver us from their hand. ^5-8

**5:9** We get our bread at the peril of our lives, because of the sword in the wilderness. ^5-9

**5:10** Our skin is black as an oven from the scorching heat of famine. ^5-10

**5:11** Women are raped in Zion, young women in the towns of Judah. ^5-11

**5:12** Princes are hung up by their hands; no respect is shown to the elders. ^5-12

**5:13** Young men are compelled to grind, and boys stagger under loads of wood. ^5-13

**5:14** The old men have left the city gate, the young men their music. ^5-14

**5:15** The joy of our hearts has ceased; our dancing has been turned to mourning. ^5-15

**5:16** The crown has fallen from our head; woe to us, for we have sinned! ^5-16

**5:17** Because of this our hearts are sick; because of these things our eyes have grown dim: ^5-17

**5:18** because of Mount Zion, which lies desolate; jackals prowl over it. ^5-18

**5:19** But you, O LORD, reign forever; your throne endures to all generations. ^5-19

**5:20** Why have you forgotten us completely? Why have you forsaken us these many days? ^5-20

**5:21** Restore us to yourself, O LORD, that we may be restored; renew our days as of old— ^5-21

**5:22** unless you have utterly rejected us and are angry with us beyond measure. ^5-22
