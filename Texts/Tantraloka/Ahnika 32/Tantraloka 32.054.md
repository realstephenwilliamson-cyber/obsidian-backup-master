## Mūla

सूपविष्टः पद्मके तु हस्ताग्रांगुलिरश्मिभिः ।
पराङ्मुखैर्झटित्युद्यद्रश्मिभिः पृष्ठसंस्थितैः ॥ ^mula

sūpaviṣṭaḥ padmake tu hastāgrāṃguliraśmibhiḥ |
parāṅmukhairjhaṭityudyadraśmibhiḥ pṛṣṭhasaṃsthitaiḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 06 - Chapter Thirty-Two#^pg180|p. 162]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=180>))
>
> 1) (The yogi,) well seated in the lotus posture, (experiences) the inner state (of rest when he engulfs and withdraws outer objectivity) with the (lunar) rays located on the back of the fingers on the forepart of (his) hands, that emerge all of a sudden, turned away (from him). This (first form of the Seal of the) Skyfarer is (the Goddess of the Moon) Śaśāṅkinī, who is (also appropriately) called ‘Retraction’ (saṁkocā). (with 32.54–55ab)
>
> 8) Looking downwards, endowed with sound (inner) vision (samyagdrṣti), the two hands contracted (and bent), as if throwing himself suddenly into a deep abyss by penetrating with audacity (and forcefully into consciousness) (sāhasa) - this is the Skyfarer who, called Vīrabhairava, develops consciousness. (KhecarT) has thus been described in eight ways in the venerable Bhargāṣtakaśikhākula. (with 32.54–62)
