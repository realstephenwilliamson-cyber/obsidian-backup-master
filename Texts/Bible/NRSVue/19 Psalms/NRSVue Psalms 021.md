## Thanksgiving for victory

*To the leader. A Psalm of David.*

**21:1** In your strength the king rejoices, O LORD, and in your help how greatly he exults! ^21-1

**21:2** You have given him his heart’s desire and have not withheld the request of his lips. *Selah* ^21-2

**21:3** For you meet him with rich blessings; you set a crown of fine gold on his head. ^21-3

**21:4** He asked you for life; you gave it to him—length of days forever and ever. ^21-4

**21:5** His glory is great through your help; splendor and majesty you bestow on him. ^21-5

**21:6** You bestow on him blessings forever; you make him glad with the joy of your presence. ^21-6

**21:7** For the king trusts in the LORD, and through the steadfast love of the Most High he shall not be moved. ^21-7

**21:8** Your hand will find out all your enemies; your right hand will find out those who hate you. ^21-8

**21:9** You will make them like a fiery furnace when you appear. The LORD will swallow them up in his wrath, and fire will consume them. ^21-9

**21:10** You will destroy their offspring from the earth and their children from among humankind. ^21-10

**21:11** If they plan evil against you, if they devise mischief, they will not succeed. ^21-11

**21:12** For you will put them to flight; you will aim at their faces with your bows. ^21-12

**21:13** Be exalted, O LORD, in your strength! We will sing and praise your power. ^21-13
