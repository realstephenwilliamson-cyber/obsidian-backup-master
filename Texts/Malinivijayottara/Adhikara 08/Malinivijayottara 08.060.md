## Mūla

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2025 – April 2025
> p. 17 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=31>))
>
> **The Square at Śuddhavidyā between the Uvula and Cavity of Brahmā**
>
> One should meditate (in this way) on the square base (*catuṣkikā*) which is above the knot and below the three prongs. They say that it is the principle of (Pure) Knowledge and that it has two coverings.[^66] *One should meditate (anucintayet) on that principle in the middle (between) the Cavity of Brahmā (kakha) and the uvula.*[^67] (with 8.60–61ab)

[^64]: kh: *chadanadvayeti*
[^66]: Read with MS Kh *chadanadvaya-* for *chadanatraya-.*
[^67]: MV 8/61 is quoted inTĀv ad 15/297cd-303ab.
