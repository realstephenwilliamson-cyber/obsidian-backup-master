## Mūla

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 10 - Chapter Eight, part 8#^pg367|p. 349]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=367>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 8.362cd–363ab.
>
> They say that above the Enclosure of Śiva is the one called the Enclosure of Liberation (mokṣāvaraṇa), within which (the scriptures) describe eleven Rudras. Beyond this is the Enclosure of Dhruva (dhruvāvaranṇa), that has only one world. Above the Enclosure of Dhruva is the Enclosure of Desire (icchāvaraṇa). Those Śivas who are there reside within the abode of the Lord’s will. It said to have (only) one world. (with 8.361cd–363)
