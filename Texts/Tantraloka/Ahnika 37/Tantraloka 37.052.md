## Mūla

तस्मिन् कुबेरपुरचारिसिंतांशुमौलि-
सांमुख्यदर्शनविरूढपवित्रभावे ।
वैतस्तरोधसि निवासममुष्य चक्रे
राजा द्विजस्य परिकल्पितभूरिसंपत् ॥ ^mula

tasmin kuberapuracārisiṃtāṃśumauli-
sāṃmukhyadarśanavirūḍhapavitrabhāve |
vaitastarodhasi nivāsamamuṣya cakre
rājā dvijasya parikalpitabhūrisaṃpat || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 11 - Chapter Thirty-Seven, part 1#^pg268|p. 250]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=268>))
>
> (Thus,) on the bank of the Vitastā, sanctified by the sight before it (of the temple) of Sītāhśumauli, (to the north of which is) Kubera’s abode, the king, imagining the great benefits (sampat) (of doing so), made a dwelling place there for this brahmin (called Atrigupta).
