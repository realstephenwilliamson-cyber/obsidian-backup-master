## Mūla

बीजभावोऽथाग्रहणं साम्यं तूष्णींस्वभावता ।
मुख्या मातृदशा सेयं सुषुप्ताख्या निगद्यते ॥ ^mula

bījabhāvo'thāgrahaṇaṃ sāmyaṃ tūṣṇīṃsvabhāvatā |
mukhyā mātṛdaśā seyaṃ suṣuptākhyā nigadyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 14 - Chapter Ten, part 5#^pg480|p. 461]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=480>))
>
> The wise consider common sleep (laukikī nidrā) to be deep sleep because they are the same (in three essential respects). (They are both) the seed state (of other forms of consciousness), (they are both equally) devoid of perception, and are silent. (with 10.259cd–260ab)
>
> This state called deep sleep is said to be the primary plane (daśā) of the perceiver. (with 10.260cd)
