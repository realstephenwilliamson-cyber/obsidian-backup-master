## Mūla

अवज्ञां विदधीतेति शंभुनाऽऽज्ञा निरूपिता ।
स्वधनेन दरिद्रस्य कुर्याद्दीक्षां गुरुः स्वयम् ॥ ^mula

avajñāṃ vidadhīteti śaṃbhunā''jñā nirūpitā |
svadhanena daridrasya kuryāddīkṣāṃ guruḥ svayam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 02 - Chapter Fifteen, part 1#^pg49|p. 31]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=49>))
>
> The teacher should not disdain (the disciple) who has received a descent of the power (of grace and be averse to) initiating him. This is how Śambhu(nātha) has explained (Śiva’s) command. (with 15.35cd–36ab)
>
> If the disciple is poor, the teacher should personally (cover the costs) of the initiation, even if (the teacher himself is poor and can provide) only water and dūrva grass. Or else (in certain cases), the disciple can beg (for the money to cover the cost of) the initiation. (with 15.36cd–37ab)
