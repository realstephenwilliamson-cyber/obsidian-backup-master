## Mūla

यदा त्वेकेन शुद्धेन तदन्तर्भावचिन्तनात् ।
न पृथक् शोधयेत्तत्त्वनाथसंश्रवणात्परम् ॥ ^mula

yadā tvekena śuddhena tadantarbhāvacintanāt |
na pṛthak śodhayettattvanāthasaṃśravaṇātparam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 08 - Chapter Seventeen#^pg184|p. 164]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=184>))
>
> If when one (Path) has been purified and (the teacher) thinks that the others are contained within it, he should not for that reason purify (them) separately (one by one). After hearing (the name of) the Lord of (that) principle (tattvanātha), (he should offer) a full (oblation), and having extracted the soul (of the disciple from the reality level that has been purified) and cast it upwards, (the teacher) should conjoin it within (his own) Self. (with 17.54–55ab)
