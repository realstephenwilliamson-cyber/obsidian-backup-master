# Psalm 19

← [[BCP Psalm 018]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 020]] →

*Cæli enarrant* · Book One · Fourth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 019]]

**19:1** The heavens declare the glory of God, \*  
and the firmament shows his handiwork. ^19-1

**19:2** One day tells its tale to another, \*  
and one night imparts knowledge to another. ^19-2

**19:3** Although they have no words or language, \*  
and their voices are not heard, ^19-3

**19:4** Their sound has gone out into all lands, \*  
and their message to the ends of the world. ^19-4

**19:5** In the deep has he set a pavilion for the sun; \*  
it comes forth like a bridegroom out of his chamber;  
it rejoices like a champion to run its course. ^19-5

[p. 607] ^pg607

**19:6** It goes forth from the uttermost edge of the heavens  
and runs about to the end of it again; \*  
nothing is hidden from its burning heat. ^19-6

**19:7** The law of the LORD is perfect and revives the soul; \*  
the testimony of the LORD is sure and gives wisdom to the innocent. ^19-7

**19:8** The statutes of the LORD are just and rejoice the heart; \*  
the commandment of the LORD is clear and gives light to the eyes. ^19-8

**19:9** The fear of the LORD is clean and endures for ever; \*  
the judgments of the LORD are true and righteous altogether. ^19-9

**19:10** More to be desired are they than gold, more than much fine gold, \*  
sweeter far than honey, than honey in the comb. ^19-10

**19:11** By them also is your servant enlightened, \*  
and in keeping them there is great reward. ^19-11

**19:12** Who can tell how often he offends? \*  
cleanse me from my secret faults. ^19-12

**19:13** Above all, keep your servant from presumptuous sins;  
let them not get dominion over me; \*  
then shall I be whole and sound,  
and innocent of a great offense. ^19-13

**19:14** Let the words of my mouth and the meditation of my heart be acceptable in your sight, \*  
O LORD, my strength and my redeemer. ^19-14
