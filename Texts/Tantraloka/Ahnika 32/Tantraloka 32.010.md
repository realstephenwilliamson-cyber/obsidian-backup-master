## Mūla

तत्र पूर्णेन रूपेण खेचरीमेव वर्णये ।
बद्ध्वा पद्मासनं योगी नाभावक्षेश्वरं क्षिपेत् ॥ ^mula

tatra pūrṇena rūpeṇa khecarīmeva varṇaye |
baddhvā padmāsanaṃ yogī nābhāvakṣeśvaraṃ kṣipet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 06 - Chapter Thirty-Two#^pg159|p. 141]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=159>))
>
> Mudrā is of four kinds, according to whether it is (made with) the body, the hands, speech or mind. There (in that regard), I will describe only the Skyfarer in a complete form (that includes all these four varieties). (with 32.9cd–10ab)
>
> (It is said) in the Śrīpūrvaśāsana (that is, in the Mālinīvijayottara): ‘Assuming the lotus posture, the Yogi should direct (his mind), the Lord of the Senses, into the navel. In the form of a staff, he should lead it along until (it reaches) the three Voids (in the head). After restraining it there, he should rapidly impel (it) with the three Voids (above the head). Having formed this (Mudrā), (the yogi) flies into the (supreme) Void.’ (with 32.10cd–12ab)
