## Mūla

अभिषिक्ताविमावेवं सर्वयोगिगणेन तु ।
विदितौ भवतस्तत्र गुरुर्मोक्षप्रदो भवेत् ॥ ^mula

abhiṣiktāvimāvevaṃ sarvayogigaṇena tu |
viditau bhavatastatra gururmokṣaprado bhavet || ^iast

## Translation

> (These two,) consecrated in this way, are known (and honoured) by all the host of yogis. There, (out of the two), it is the teacher who bestows liberation.
