# Psalm 58 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 057|← Psalm 57]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 059|Psalm 59 →]]

## Prayer for vengeance

*To the leader: Do Not Destroy. Of David. A Miktam.*

**58:1** Do you indeed decree what is right, you gods? Do you judge people fairly? ^58-1

**58:2** No, in your hearts you devise wrongs; your hands deal out violence on earth. ^58-2

**58:3** The wicked go astray from the womb; they err from their birth, speaking lies. ^58-3

**58:4** They have venom like the venom of a serpent, like the deaf adder that stops its ear, ^58-4

**58:5** so that it does not hear the voice of charmers or of the cunning enchanter. ^58-5

**58:6** O God, break the teeth in their mouths; tear out the fangs of the young lions, O LORD! ^58-6

**58:7** Let them vanish like water that runs away; like grass let them be trodden down^[Cn: Meaning of Heb uncertain] and wither. ^58-7

**58:8** Let them be like the snail that dissolves into slime, like the untimely birth that never sees the sun. ^58-8

**58:9** Sooner than your pots can feel the heat of thorns, whether green or ablaze, may he sweep them away! ^58-9

**58:10** The righteous will rejoice when they see vengeance done; they will bathe their feet in the blood of the wicked. ^58-10

**58:11** People will say, “Surely there is a reward for the righteous; surely there is a God who judges^[Or *there are gods who judge*] on earth.” ^58-11
