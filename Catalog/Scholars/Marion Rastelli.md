## Free online

- [IKGA (Austrian Academy) staff page](https://www.oeaw.ac.at/en/ikga/team/directorate/rastelli-marion): Links to CV PDF and publications-list PDF (https://www.oeaw.ac.at/fileadmin/Institute/IKGA/PDF/team/Publications_Rastelli.pdf), and to two open-access edited volumes: 'Tantric Communities in Context' (2019, https://austriaca.at/9783700186731) and 'Studies in Hinduism IV' (2007, http://hw.oeaw.ac.at/3680-4inhalt). Her monographs (1999, 2006) are order-only.
- [Publications list PDF (Oct 2022 version)](https://www.oeaw.ac.at/fileadmin/Institute/IKGA/PDF/team/Publications_Rastelli_2022.pdf): Bibliography 1999-2022 (about 60 items: 6 books, 40+ articles, reviews); a few entries carry URLs to free versions (Tantric Communities in Context on austriaca.at; her chapter in 'Yoga in Transformation' 2018 as open-access PDF; articles on akademicka.pl and menadoc.bibliothek.uni-halle.de). Most entries have no free link.
- [Tantric Communities in Context (Austrian Academy of Sciences Press, 2019), open access](https://verlag.oeaw.ac.at/produkt/tantric-communities-in-context/605619): Co-edited by Rastelli with Mirnig and Eltschinger; e-book PDF at 0,00 EUR, all chapters Open Access with free PDF download. Full-volume PDF also seen on austriaca.at in search results (https://austriaca.at/0xc1aa5572%200x003c3854.pdf).
