## Mūla

तीव्रमन्दादिभेदेन शक्तिपातं तथाविधम् ।
इत्येष समयी प्रोक्तः श्रीपूर्वे करकम्पतः ॥ ^mula

tīvramandādibhedena śaktipātaṃ tathāvidham |
ityeṣa samayī proktaḥ śrīpūrve karakampataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 12 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 4#^pg374|p. 357]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=374>))
>
> The (degree of) paralysis (of his disciple’s) hand (that takes place on these occasions), beginning with the grasping of the blindfold up to the undertaking (samādāna) of (the rite) of the (cleaning) stick for the teeth, is indicative there (of the degree) of the corresponding descent of the power (of grace,) according to whether it is intense or weak etc. (with 29.196–197ab)
>
> Thus, it is because of the shaking (of his) hand that he is said in Śrīpūrva (to have become) a regular initiate. (with 29.197cd)
