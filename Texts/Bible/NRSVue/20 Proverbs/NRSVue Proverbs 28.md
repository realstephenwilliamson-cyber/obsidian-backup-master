# Proverbs 28 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Proverbs 27|← Proverbs 27]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Proverbs 29|Proverbs 29 →]]

**28:1** The wicked flee when no one pursues, but the righteous are as bold as a lion. ^28-1

**28:2** When a land rebels it has many rulers; but with an intelligent^[Heb *intelligent knowledgeable*] person, honesty endures. ^28-2

**28:3** A poor person who oppresses the poor is a beating rain that leaves no food. ^28-3

**28:4** Those who forsake the law praise the wicked, but those who keep the law struggle against them. ^28-4

**28:5** The evil do not understand justice, but those who seek the LORD understand it completely. ^28-5

**28:6** Better to be poor and walk in integrity than to be crooked in one’s ways even though rich. ^28-6

**28:7** Those who keep the law are wise children, but companions of gluttons shame their parents. ^28-7

**28:8** One who augments wealth by exorbitant interest gathers it for another who is kind to the poor. ^28-8

**28:9** When one will not listen to the law, even one’s prayers are an abomination. ^28-9

**28:10** Those who mislead the upright into evil ways will fall into pits of their own making, but the blameless will have a goodly inheritance. ^28-10

**28:11** The rich is wise in self-esteem, but an intelligent poor person sees through the pose. ^28-11

**28:12** When the righteous rejoice, there is great glory, but when the wicked prevail, people go into hiding. ^28-12

**28:13** No one who conceals transgressions will prosper, but one who confesses and forsakes them will obtain mercy. ^28-13

**28:14** Happy is the one who is never without fear, but one who is hard-hearted will fall into calamity. ^28-14

**28:15** Like a roaring lion or a charging bear is a wicked ruler over a poor people. ^28-15

**28:16** A ruler who lacks understanding is a cruel oppressor, but one who hates unjust gain will enjoy a long life. ^28-16

**28:17** If someone is burdened with the blood of another, let that killer be a fugitive until death; let no one offer assistance. ^28-17

**28:18** One who walks in integrity will be safe, but whoever follows crooked ways will fall into the Pit.^[Syr: Heb *fall all at once*] ^28-18

**28:19** Anyone who tills the land will have plenty of bread, but one who follows worthless pursuits will have plenty of poverty. ^28-19

**28:20** The faithful will abound with blessings, but one who is in a hurry to be rich will not go unpunished. ^28-20

**28:21** To show partiality is not good, yet for a piece of bread a person may do wrong. ^28-21

**28:22** The miser is in a hurry to get rich and does not know that loss is sure to come. ^28-22

**28:23** Whoever rebukes a person will afterward find more favor than one who flatters with the tongue. ^28-23

**28:24** Anyone who robs father or mother and says, “That is no crime,” is partner to a thug. ^28-24

**28:25** The greedy person stirs up strife, but whoever trusts in the LORD will be enriched. ^28-25

**28:26** Those who trust in their own wits are fools, but those who walk in wisdom come through safely. ^28-26

**28:27** Whoever gives to the poor will lack nothing, but one who turns a blind eye will get many a curse. ^28-27

**28:28** When the wicked prevail, people go into hiding, but when they perish, the righteous increase. ^28-28
