## Mūla

विशेषाणां यतोऽवश्यं दशा प्रागविशेषिणी ।
क्षुभितं शब्दतन्मात्रं चित्राकाराः श्रुतीर्दधत् ॥ ^mula

viśeṣāṇāṃ yato'vaśyaṃ daśā prāgaviśeṣiṇī |
kṣubhitaṃ śabdatanmātraṃ citrākārāḥ śrutīrdadhat || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 09 - Chapter Nine, part 7#^pg281|p. 262]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=281>))
>
> (This is so) because particulars are necessarily preceded by a generic, non-specific state (aviśeṣinīdaśā). (with 9.283ab)
>
> When the subtle element of sound is disturbed, it becomes the Space element, which sustains the diverse forms of sound. This is because sound is space, which is (the space) that makes room (avakāśa) for the superimposition (adhyāsa) within it of the entities denoted by words (vācya). When that is disturbed by conjunction with the subtle element of touch, it becomes the Air element (vāyu), which is therefore both sound and touch. (with 9.283cd–285ab)
