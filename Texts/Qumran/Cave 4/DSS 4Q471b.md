### Frags. 1 - 3 (= 1QHa xxvi top; 4Q427 7 i + 9; cf. 4Q491c 1)

**1** [… who has been] despised like [me … for in the] ^f1-1

**2** holy [dwelling] is ev[erything precious to me, and who suffers] evil like [me …] ^f1-2

**3** like me together with [the holy ones …] and who will be lik[e me … is not] ^f1-3

**4** like my instruction […] ^f1-4

**5** who is like me among the gods […] ^f1-5

**6** who can measure [what issues] from my lips, who [will summon me with the tongue … I am] ^f1-6

**7** friend of the king, companion of the ho[ly ones, and not shall come to me … and who] ^f1-7

**8** can be compared to [my glory,] for I, [with the gods is my position and my glory is with the sons of the king. Not] ^f1-8

**9** with pure gold … […] ^f1-9

**10** [Blank?] Sing, [favoured ones, …] ^f1-10
