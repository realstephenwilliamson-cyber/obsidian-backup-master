# LXX Psalms 144

← [[LXX Psalms 143]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 145]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 145]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 145]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**144:1** David's *Psalm of* praise. I will exalt thee, my God, my king; and I will bless thy name for ever and ever.[^1]  ^144-1

**144:2** Every day will I bless thee, and I will praise thy name for ever and ever.[^2]  ^144-2

**144:3** The Lord is great, and greatly to be praised; and there is no end of his greatness.  ^144-3

**144:4** Generation after generation shall praise thy works, and tell of thy power.  ^144-4

**144:5** And they shall speak of the glorious majesty of thy holiness, and recount thy wonders.  ^144-5

**144:6** And they shall speak of the power of thy terrible *acts*; and recount thy greatness.  ^144-6

**144:7** They shall utter the memory of the abundance of thy goodness, and shall exult in thy righteousness.  ^144-7

**144:8** The Lord is compassionate, and merciful; long-suffering, and abundant in mercy.  ^144-8

**144:9** The Lord is good to those that wait *on him*; and his compassions are over all his works.  ^144-9

**144:10** Let all thy works, O Lord, give thanks to thee; and let thy saints bless thee.  ^144-10

**144:11** They shall speak of the glory of thy kingdom, and talk of thy dominion;  ^144-11

**144:12** to make known to the sons of men thy power, and the glorious majesty of thy kingdom.  ^144-12

**144:13** Thy kingdom is an everlasting kingdom, and thy dominion *endures* through all generations. 13a The Lord is faithful in his words, and holy in all his works.[^3][^4]  ^144-13

**144:14** The Lord supports all that are falling, and sets up all that are broken down.  ^144-14

**144:15** The eyes of all wait upon thee; and thou givest *them* their food in due season.  ^144-15

**144:16** Thou openest thine hands, and fillest every living thing with pleasure.  ^144-16

**144:17** The Lord is righteous in all his ways, and holy in all his works.  ^144-17

**144:18** The Lord is near to all that call upon him, to all that call upon him in truth.  ^144-18

**144:19** He will perform the desire of them that fear him: and he will hear their supplication, and save them.  ^144-19

**144:20** The Lord preserves all that love him: but all sinners he will utterly destroy.  ^144-20

**144:21** My mouth shall speak the praise of the Lord: and let all flesh bless his holy name for ever and ever.  ^144-21

[^1]: 144:1 *Lit.* to the age, and to the age of the age.
[^2]: 144:2 *Lit.* to the age, and to the age of the age.
[^3]: 144:13 *Gr.* a kingdom of all ages.
[^4]: 144:13 *Gr.* in every generation and generation.
