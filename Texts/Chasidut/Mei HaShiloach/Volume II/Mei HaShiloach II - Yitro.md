## Teaching 14

**Honor your father and your mother:** It is found in the holy Zohar (Kedoshim, 2) that "father and mother" are hinting to the thought of the mind and the desire of the heat. And this is what God, may He be blessed, commanded us - to honor them. Such that if a person knows about himself that he is very refined and has no egotistical interests, he should not push away his thought and desire, but rather honor them.
