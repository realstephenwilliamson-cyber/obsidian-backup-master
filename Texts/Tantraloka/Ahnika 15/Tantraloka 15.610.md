## Mūla

ज्ञानप्राप्त्यभ्युपायत्वात्समयास्ते प्रकीर्तिताः ।
एवं संश्राव्य समयान्देवं संपूज्य दैशिकः ॥ ^mula

jñānaprāptyabhyupāyatvātsamayāste prakīrtitāḥ |
evaṃ saṃśrāvya samayāndevaṃ saṃpūjya daiśikaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 10 - Chapter Fifteen, part 9#^pg365|p. 347]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=365>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 15.612cd–613ab.
>
> They are called pledges (samaya) because they must be observed, because (by means of them one) is connected (saṁgama) with the supreme reality, and because they are the means to attain knowledge. (with 15.609cd–610ab)
>
> Once the teacher has told (the neophant) the rules in this way and has worshipped the Lord, he should dismiss Him into the tranquil space of His own consciousness, by dissolving away (therein the mantra of His) ‘sacred body’ (mūrti). (with 15.610cd–611ab)
