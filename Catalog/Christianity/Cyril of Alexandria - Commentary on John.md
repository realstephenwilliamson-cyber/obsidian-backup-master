**Checked against the files** on 22 Oct 2026: vol. I's title page (PDF 9) reads "Commentary on the Gospel according to S. John by S. Cyril, Archbishop of Alexandria. Vol. I, S. John I–VIII. Sold by James Parker & Co., Oxford … MDCCCLXXIV"; vol. II's (PDF 7, 11) "Library of Fathers of the Holy Catholic Church … London: Walter Smith (late Mozley), 1885", "Vol. II". The translators are named in vol. II's preface by H. P. Liddon (PDF 13–19, dated Christ Church, All Saints 1885): vol. I by the late P. E. Pusey, whose father Dr Pusey wrote most of its preface; vol. II by the Rev. Thomas Randell of St John's College, commissioned in 1880 to complete the work. Vol. I has 756 PDF pages, vol. II 764 (Books VI–XII, beginning with the fragments of the lost Books VII and VIII); vol. II also carries a table of the contents of both volumes, vol. I's own having been faulty (Liddon, PDF 17). Internet Archive scans of library copies (`commentaryongosp01cyri`, `commentaryongosp02cyri`). The year is left empty because the two volumes differ (1874 and 1885; both are in the file names).

**Text layer:** the Internet Archive's 2009 OCR, searchable, with OCR errors; it loses the Greek. The Markdown copies were made from a fresh OCR of the page images (Tesseract, English and Greek models), so the Greek words in the prefaces and notes are readable there; expect a few slips in the English and in the marginal references.
