# Isaiah 54

Compare: [[NRSVue Isaiah 54|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 54

**54:1** Shout, O barren one,  
You who bore no child!  
Shout aloud for joy,  
You who did not travail!  
For the children of the wife forlorn  
Shall outnumber those of the espoused  
—said the LORD. ^54-1

**54:2** Enlarge the site of your tent,  
Extend the size of your dwelling,[^1]  
Do not stint!  
Lengthen the ropes, and drive the pegs firm. ^54-2

**54:3** For you shall spread out to the right and the left;  
Your offspring shall dispossess nations[^2]  
And shall people the desolate towns. ^54-3

**54:4** Fear not, you shall not be shamed;  
Do not cringe, you shall not be disgraced.  
For you shall forget  
The reproach of your youth,  
And remember no more  
The shame of your widowhood. ^54-4

**54:5** For He who made you will espouse you—  
His name is “LORD of Hosts.”  
The Holy One of Israel will redeem you—  
He is called “God of all the Earth.” ^54-5

**54:6** The LORD has called you back  
As a wife forlorn and forsaken.  
Can one cast off the wife of his youth?  
—said your God. ^54-6

**54:7** For a little while I forsook you,  
But with vast love I will bring you back. ^54-7

**54:8** In slight anger, for a moment,  
I hid My face from you;  
But with kindness everlasting  
I will take you back in love  
—said the LORD your Redeemer. ^54-8

**54:9** For this to Me is like the waters[^3] of Noah:  
As I swore that the waters of Noah  
Nevermore would flood the earth,  
So I swear that I will not  
Be angry with you or rebuke you. ^54-9

**54:10** For the mountains may move  
And the hills be shaken,  
But my loyalty shall never move from you,  
Nor My covenant of friendship be shaken  
—said the LORD, who takes you back in love. ^54-10

**54:11** Unhappy, storm-tossed one, uncomforted!  
I will lay carbuncles[^4] as your building stones  
And make your foundations of sapphires. ^54-11

**54:12** I will make your battlements of rubies,  
Your gates of precious stones,  
The whole encircling wall of gems. ^54-12

**54:13** And all your children shall be disciples of the LORD,  
And great shall be the happiness of your children; ^54-13

**54:14** You shall be established through righteousness.  
You shall be safe from oppression,  
And shall have no fear;  
From ruin, and it shall not come near you. ^54-14

**54:15** [^5]Surely no harm can be done  
Without My consent:  
Whoever would harm you  
Shall fall because of you. ^54-15

**54:16** It is I who created the smith  
To fan the charcoal fire  
And produce the tools for his work;  
So it is I who create  
The instruments of havoc. ^54-16

**54:17** No weapon formed against you  
Shall succeed,  
And every tongue that contends with you at law  
You shall defeat.  
Such is the lot of the servants of the LORD,  
Such their triumph through Me  
—declares the LORD. ^54-17

[^1]: Lit. “Let the cloths of your dwelling extend.”
[^2]: I.e., the foreigners who had occupied regions from which Israelites had been exiled; cf. 2 Kings 17.24.
[^3]: Other Heb. mss. and the ancient versions read “days.”
[^4]: Taking pukh as a byform of nophekh; so already Rashi.
[^5]: Meaning of verse uncertain.
