## Mūla

भो भोः शक्र त्वया स्वस्यां दिशि विघ्नप्रशान्तये ।
सावधानेन कर्मान्तं भवितव्यं शिवाज्ञया ॥ ^mula

bho bhoḥ śakra tvayā svasyāṁ diśi vighnapraśāntaye[^114] |
sāvadhānena karmāntaṁ bhavitavyaṁ śivājñayā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2025 – April 2025
> p. 30 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=44>))
>
> (The disciple) should then cause an unbroken stream (of liquid) to fall from the small jar (*vārdhanī*), and after that (the teacher) should rotate it around the large jar (*kalaśa*), saying this to the lords of the regions:
>
> “O! O! Śakra, by Śiva's command you should attend with care on your quarter (of space) up to the end of the rite, so that (demonic) obstacles (there) may be quelled.” (with 8.105–106)

[^114]: k: *vighnopaśāntaye*
