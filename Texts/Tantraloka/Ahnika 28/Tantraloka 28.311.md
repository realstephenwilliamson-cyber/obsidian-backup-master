## Mūla

अपि चेति ध्वनिर्जीवन्मुक्ततामस्य भाषते ।
सचिन्ताचिन्तकत्वोक्तिरेतावत्संभवस्थितिम् ॥ ^mula

api ceti dhvanirjīvanmuktatāmasya bhāṣate |
sacintācintakatvoktiretāvatsaṃbhavasthitim || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 06 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 4#^pg150|p. 133]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=150>))
>
> The word ‘also’ suggests his condition as one who is liberated in life, and that (his) condition can only possibly be such, (whatever be the circumstance,) by the expression ‘whether he thinks or is free of thought’.
