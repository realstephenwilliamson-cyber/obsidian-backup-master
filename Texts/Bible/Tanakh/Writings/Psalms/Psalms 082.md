## Chapter 82

**82:1** A psalm of Asaph.  
God stands in the divine assembly;  
among the divine beings He pronounces judgment. ^82-1

**82:2** How long will you judge perversely,  
showing favor to the wicked? *Selah*. ^82-2

**82:3** Judge the wretched and the orphan,  
vindicate the lowly and the poor, ^82-3

**82:4** rescue the wretched and the needy;  
save them from the hand of the wicked. ^82-4

**82:5** They neither know nor understand,  
they go about in darkness;  
all the foundations of the earth totter. ^82-5

**82:6** I had taken you for divine beings,  
sons of the Most High, all of you; ^82-6

**82:7** but you shall die as men do,  
fall like any prince. ^82-7

**82:8** Arise, O God, judge the earth,  
for all the nations are Your possession. ^82-8
