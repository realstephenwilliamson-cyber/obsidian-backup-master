## Mūla

पूर्वापरं तदेवेह मध्ये शूलं तु तद्बहिः ।
कारयेत त्रिभिः सूत्रैरेकैकं वर्तयेत च ॥ ^mula

pūrvāparaṃ tadeveha madhye śūlaṃ tu tadbahiḥ |
kārayeta tribhiḥ sūtrairekaikaṃ vartayeta ca || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 05 - Chapter Thirty-One, part 2#^pg132|p. 114]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=132>))
>
> ‘(The upper) eastern and (lower) western (part of the prong in the middle) should be fashioned (in such a way that) the middle (part of it) is depressed by four finger-breadths. Such is the prong here in the middle. Outside that (on its two sides), one should fashion with three threads each (three circles), and draw (within them) three lotuses on the tip of the trident. (These lotuses have a radius of) twelve finger-breadths (set at a distance of) four finger-breadths (from one other). Those on the right and left (both) have three and eight petals (respectively), and the one in the middle, twelve. (Thus making) three wheels (cakratraya). (Then) a circle (vātapura) consisting of a lotus eight finger-breadths (wide) with (eight) petals, called (the Lotus of) Wisdom, is (drawn) at the base of the trident. (with 31.113cd–116abc)
