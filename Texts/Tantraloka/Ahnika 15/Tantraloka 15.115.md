## Mūla

तत्स्थानं यत्र विश्रान्तिसुन्दरं हृदयं भवेत् ।
यागौकः प्राप्य शुद्धात्मा बहिरेव व्यवस्थितः ॥ ^mula

tatsthānaṃ yatra viśrāntisundaraṃ hṛdayaṃ bhavet |
yāgaukaḥ prāpya śuddhātmā bahireva vyavasthitaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 03 - Chapter Fifteen, part 2#^pg95|p. 77]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=95>))
>
> Therefore, in order to attain worldly accomplishment or liberation, the place which is best suited to perform the rites of adoration, the repetition of Mantras and meditation, is where the heart (of consciousness) is beautiful with the rest (it enjoys in its own essential nature) (viśrāntisundara). (with 15.114cd–115ab)
>
> Once he whose soul is pure has reached the location of the sacrifice (yāgauka) he should, whilst (still) outside it, perform the common (sāmānya) deposition, so that the external sacrifice may be successful. (with 15.115cd–116ab)
