# Hosea 8 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Hosea 07|← Hosea 7]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Hosea 09|Hosea 9 →]]

## Israel’s apostasy

**8:1** Set the trumpet to your lips! One like a vulture^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] is over the house of the LORD, because they have broken my covenant and transgressed my law. ^8-1

**8:2** They cry to me, “My God, we know you!”^[Gk Syr: Heb adds *Israel*] ^8-2

**8:3** Israel has spurned the good; the enemy shall pursue him. ^8-3

**8:4** They made kings but not through me; they set up princes but without my knowledge. With their silver and gold they made idols for their own destruction. ^8-4

**8:5** Your calf is rejected, O Samaria. My anger burns against them. How long will they be incapable of innocence? ^8-5

**8:6** For it is from Israel, an artisan made it; it is not God. The calf of Samaria shall be broken to pieces. ^8-6

**8:7** For they sow the wind, and they shall reap the whirlwind. The standing grain has no heads; it shall yield no meal; if it were to yield, foreigners would devour it. ^8-7

**8:8** Israel is swallowed up; now they are among the nations as a useless vessel. ^8-8

**8:9** For they have gone up to Assyria, a wild ass wandering alone; Ephraim has bargained for lovers. ^8-9

**8:10** Though they bargain with the nations, I will now gather them up. They shall soon writhe under the burden of kings and princes. ^8-10

**8:11** When Ephraim multiplied altars to expiate sin, they became to him altars for sinning. ^8-11

**8:12** Though I write for him the multitude of my instructions, they are regarded as a strange thing. ^8-12

**8:13** Though they offer choice sacrifices,^[Cn: Meaning of Heb uncertain] though they eat flesh, the LORD does not accept them. Now he will remember their iniquity and punish their sins; they shall return to Egypt. ^8-13

**8:14** Israel has forgotten his Maker and built palaces, and Judah has multiplied fortified cities, but I will send a fire upon his cities, and it shall devour his strongholds. ^8-14
