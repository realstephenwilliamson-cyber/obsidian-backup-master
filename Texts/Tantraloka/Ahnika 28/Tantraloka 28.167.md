## Mūla

योगिनीक्षेत्रमातॄणां बलिं दद्यात्ततो गुरुः ।
पञ्चगव्यं चरुं दन्तकाष्ठं शिष्यैः समन्ततः ॥ ^mula

yoginīkṣetramātṝṇāṃ baliṃ dadyāttato guruḥ |
pañcagavyaṃ caruṃ dantakāṣṭhaṃ śiṣyaiḥ samantataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 04 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 2#^pg96|p. 79]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=96>))

> After this, the teacher should make an animal offering (bali) all around to the Yoginīs and to the Mothers (who protect) the place, and to the disciples, the five products of the cow, the sacrificial pap (caru), and the stick for the teeth.
