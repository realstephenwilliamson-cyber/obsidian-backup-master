## Mūla

एकादशाक्षरं वर्म पुरुष्टुतमिति स्मृतम् ।
तारो द्विजिह्वः खशरस्वरयुग्जीव एव च ॥ ^mula

ekādaśākṣaraṃ varma puruṣṭutamiti smṛtam |
tāro dvijihvaḥ khaśarasvarayugjīva eva ca || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 02 - Chapter Thirty, part 1#^pg49|p. 31]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=49>))
>
> , VAJRIṆE VAJRADHARĀYA SVĀHĀ, preceded by the syllable OM, is the Armour (varman) called Puruṣṭuta (Praised by Many), consisting of eleven syllables. (with 30.39cd–40ab)
>
> The Saviour (OM), Two Tongues (J), Space (M), conjoined with the vowel Arrow (U), and the Living Being (S), (along with emission H,) are the Eye (netra), which is light, and said to be (a Mantra) common to all (Śaiva schools). (with 30.40cd–41ab)
