## Mūla

सिद्धातन्त्रे मण्डलानां शतं तत्पीठ उच्यते ।
यत्तन्मध्यगतं मुख्यं मण्डलानां त्रयं स्मृतम् ॥ ^mula

siddhātantre maṇḍalānāṃ śataṃ tatpīṭha ucyate |
yattanmadhyagataṃ mukhyaṃ maṇḍalānāṃ trayaṃ smṛtam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 04 - Chapter Thirty-One, part 1#^pg88|p. 70]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=88>))
>
> The mandala is said to begin with one and end with a hundred. Thus, it is said in the Siddhātantra that in that section (tatpītha), there are a hundred mandalas. Amongst them, three mandalas are clearly considered to be the main (mukhya) ones, namely, one with a (single) trident in the middle, one with three tridents, and one with nine tridents. (with 31.7cd–9ab)
