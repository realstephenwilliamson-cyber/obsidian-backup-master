# Mishneh Torah (Touger)

The *Mishneh Torah* in Rabbi Eliyahu Touger's translation (Moznaim), one note per chapter (1,003 notes, 15,685 halakhot), following Sefaria's structure. Sefaria describes it as a "Legal code written by Maimonides in the 12th century, groundbreaking in its organization and scope." Each halakhah is a block (`^3-9` is chapter 3, halakhah 9), so a link can point to it; Touger's notes are the footnotes. At the top of each chapter, collapsed boxes list the Talmud passages and the verses Sefaria links to its halakhot; verse and Talmud references in the text are links.
