## Mūla

इत्ययं करणस्कन्धोऽहङ्कारस्य निरूपितः ।
त्रिधास्य प्रकृतिस्कन्धः सात्त्वराजसतामसः ॥ ^mula

ityayaṃ karaṇaskandho'haṅkārasya nirūpitaḥ |
tridhāsya prakṛtiskandhaḥ sāttvarājasatāmasaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 08 - Chapter Nine, part 6#^pg237|p. 220]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=237>))
>
> We have described one aspect of the ego, namely, (the ego) as an instrument of cognition (karanaskandha). (Now we come) to its material aspect (prakrtiskandha), which is three-fold, namely, sāttvika, rājasika and tāmasika.
