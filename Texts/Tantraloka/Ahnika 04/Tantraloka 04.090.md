## Mūla

प्राप्ते च द्वादशे भागे जीवादित्ये स्वबोधके ।
मोक्षः स एव कथितः प्राणायामो निरर्थकः ॥ ^mula

prāpte ca dvādaśe bhāge jīvāditye svabodhake |
mokṣaḥ sa eva kathitaḥ prāṇāyāmo nirarthakaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. III
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 03 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v03 04 - Chapter Four, part 3#^pg118|p. 105]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 03.pdf#page=118>))
>
> It is said in the venerable Vīrāvali: ‘By dissolving the one-pointed mind into Śivas nature, which is just (pure awakened) consciousness (bodhamātra), the Moon (of apāna) and the Sun (of prāṇa) dissolve away. (Then, when) the Sun of Life (jīvāditya)“ which is one’s own consciousness, reaches the twelfth part (of the End of the Twelve), that is said to be liberation.” Breath control is useless. Breath control, which (merely) inflicts pain on the body, should not be practiced. He who knows (this) secret is both liberated and liberates (others).’" (with 4.89–91)
