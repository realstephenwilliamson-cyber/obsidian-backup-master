# LXX Zechariah 06

← [[LXX Zechariah 05]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Zechariah 07]] →

*Zechariah · [[LXX]]*

**6:1** And I turned, and lifted up mine eyes, and looked, and, behold, four chariots coming out from between two mountains; and the mountains were brazen mountains.  ^6-1

**6:2** In the first chariot *were* red horses; and in the second chariot black horses;  ^6-2

**6:3** and in the third chariot white horses; and in the fourth chariot piebald *and* ash-coloured horses.  ^6-3

**6:4** And I answered and said to the angel that talked with me, What are these, my lord?  ^6-4

**6:5** And the angel that talked with me answered and said, These are the four winds of heaven, *and* they are going forth to stand before the Lord of all the earth.  ^6-5

**6:6** *As for the chariot* in which were the black horses, they went out to the land of the north; and the white went out after them; and the piebald went out to the land of the south.  ^6-6

**6:7** And the ash-coloured went out, and looked to go and compass the earth: and he said, Go, and compass the earth. And they compassed the earth.[^1]  ^6-7

**6:8** And he cried out and spoke to me, saying, Behold, these go out to the land of the north, and they have quieted mine anger in the land of the north.  ^6-8

**6:9** And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,  ^6-9

**6:10** Take the things of the captivity from the chief men, and from the useful men of it, and from them that have understood it; and thou shalt enter in that day into the house of Josias the son of Sophonias that came out of Babylon.[^2]  ^6-10

**6:11** And thou shalt take silver and gold, and make crowns, and thou shalt put *them* upon the head of Jesus the son of Josedec the high priest;  ^6-11

**6:12** and thou shalt say to him, Thus saith the Lord Almighty; Behold the man whose name is The Branch; and he shall spring up from his stem, and build the house of the Lord.[^3]  ^6-12

**6:13** And he shall receive power, and shall sit and rule upon his throne; and there shall be a priest on his right hand, and a peaceable counsel shall be between *them* both.[^4]  ^6-13

**6:14** And the crown shall be to them that wait patiently, and to the useful men of the captivity, and to them that have known it, and for the favour of the son of Sophonias, and for a psalm in the house of the Lord.[^5]  ^6-14

**6:15** And they *that are* far from them shall come and build in the house of the Lord, and ye shall know that the Lord Almighty has sent me to you: and *this* shall come to pass, if ye will diligently hearken to the voice of the Lord your God.  ^6-15

[^1]: 6:7 *Or,* one said.
[^2]: 6:10 *Heb.* proper names. See *A. V.*
[^3]: 6:12 *Gr.* from beneath him.
[^4]: 6:13 *Lit.* virtue.
[^5]: 6:14 *Gr.* of it.
