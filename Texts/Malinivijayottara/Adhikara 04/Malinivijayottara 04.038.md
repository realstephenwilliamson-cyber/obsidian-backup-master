## Mūla

भुञ्जानस्तत्फलं तेन हीयते न कथंचन ।
सुसिद्धः स तु बोद्धव्यः सदाशिवसमः प्रिये ॥ ^mula

bhuñjānas tatphalaṁ tena hīyate na kathaṁcana |
susiddhaḥ sa tu boddhavyaḥ sadāśivasamaḥ priye || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, April 2023 – June 2023
> p. 22 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=63>))
>
> O beloved, one who, wherever and however he may be, enjoying the fruit of that (yoga) and does not fall from that at all, should be known to be well accomplished (susiddha) and equal to Sadāśiva.[^22] (with 4.35cd–38)

[^22]: MV 4/37cd-38 is quoted in TĀv ad 13/331, where Jayaratha explains MV 4/33-41, focusing on the four types of yogis listed in MV 4/33.
