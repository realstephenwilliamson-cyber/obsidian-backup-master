# LXX Psalms 140

← [[LXX Psalms 139]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 141]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 141]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 141]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**140:1** A Psalm of David. O Lord, I have cried to thee; hear me: attend to the voice of my supplication, when I cry to thee.  ^140-1

**140:2** Let my prayer be set forth before thee as incense; the lifting up of my hands *as* an evening sacrifice.  ^140-2

**140:3** Set a watch, O Lord, on my mouth, and a strong door about my lips.[^1]  ^140-3

**140:4** Incline not my heart to evil things, to employ pretexts for sins, with men who work iniquity: and let me not unite with their choice ones.[^2][^3][^4]  ^140-4

**140:5** The righteous shall chasten me with mercy, and reprove me: but let not the oil of the sinner anoint my head: for yet shall my prayer also be in their pleasures.[^5]  ^140-5

**140:6** Their mighty ones have been swallowed up near the rock: they shall hear my words, for they are sweet.  ^140-6

**140:7** As a lump of earth is crushed upon the ground, our bones have been scattered by the *mouth of* the grave.  ^140-7

**140:8** For mine eyes are to thee, O Lord God: I have hoped in thee; take not away my life.  ^140-8

**140:9** Keep me from the snare which they have set for me, and from the stumbling-blocks of them that work iniquity.  ^140-9

**140:10** Sinners shall fall by their own net: I am alone until I shall escape.  ^140-10

[^1]: 140:3 *Lit.* a door of fortification.
[^2]: 140:4 *Gr.* pretend pretences.
[^3]: 140:4 *Gr.* in.
[^4]: 140:4 *Gr.* I will not.
[^5]: 140:5 *q. d.* lest I be injured by them.
