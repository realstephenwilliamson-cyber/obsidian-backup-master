### 13.1–5: Understanding evil occurrences

*[[NRSVue Luke 13#^13-1|Text]], p. 143* ^13-1

**[[NRSVue Luke 13#^13-1|1]]:** *Galileans whose blood Pilate had mingled*, the event is unknown; on Pilate’s ruthlessness see, e.g., Philo, *Leg. Gai*. 37–38; Josephus, *J.W*. 2.9.2–4; *Ant*. 18.2.2; 18.3.1–2; 18.4.1–2. *With their sacrifices*, Galileans participated in Temple worship. Tragic death does not indicate a sinful life. ^13-1-2

**[[NRSVue Luke 13#^13-4|4]]:** *Tower of Siloam*, another unknown accident. Siloam, southwest Jerusalem ([[Isaiah 08#^8-6|Isa 8.6]]; [[Nehemiah 03#^3-15|Neh 3.15]]; [[John 09#^9-7|Jn 9.7]]). ^13-4

### 13.6–9: Unfruitful fig tree

*[[NRSVue Luke 13#^13-6|Text]], p. 143* · ([[Matthew 21#^21-18|Mt 21.18–19]]; [[Mark 11#^11-12|Mk 11.12–14]]). ^13-6

**[[NRSVue Luke 13#^13-7|7]]:** *Cut it down*, see 3.9. ^13-7

**[[NRSVue Luke 13#^13-8|8]]:** *One more year*, limited time left to bear good fruit. ^13-8

### 13.10–17: Sabbath healing

*[[NRSVue Luke 13#^13-10|Text]], p. 144* · (see 4.31–37n.). ^13-10

**[[NRSVue Luke 13#^13-14|14]]:** *Leader of the synagogue*, see 8.41n. *Sabbath*, see 6.1–11 and notes. ^13-14

**[[NRSVue Luke 13#^13-15|15]]:** *Untie his ox*, a “qal vahomer” (from lesser to greater) argument. ^13-15

**[[NRSVue Luke 13#^13-16|16]]:** *Daughter of Abraham*, Jewish woman ([[LXX 4 Maccabees 15#^15-28|4 Macc 15.28]]; [[LXX 4 Maccabees 17#^17-6|17.6]]; [[LXX 4 Maccabees 18#^18-20|18.20]]; cf. [[Luke 19#^19-9|Lk 19.9]]). ^13-16

**[[NRSVue Luke 13#^13-17|17]]:** *Entire crowd was rejoicing*, people celebrate the healing; they find no violation of halakhah. ^13-17

**[[NRSVue Luke 13#^13-18|13.18–19]]:** **Parable of the mustard seed** ([[Matthew 13#^13-31|Mt 13.31–32]]; [[Mark 04#^4-30|Mk 4.30–32]]). ^13-18

**[[NRSVue Luke 13#^13-19|19]]:** *Became a tree*, an exaggeration. *Made nests in its branches*, see [[Daniel 04#^4-9|Dan 4.12]],[[Daniel 04#^4-18|21]], suggesting an empire. ^13-19

### 13.20–21: Parable of the yeast

*[[NRSVue Luke 13#^13-20|Text]], p. 144* · ([[Matthew 13#^13-33|Mt 13.33]]). ^13-20

**[[NRSVue Luke 13#^13-21|21]]:** *Yeast*, see 12.1n. *Mixed* (Gk “enegkrypsen”), lit., “hid.” *Three measures*, 50–60 pounds. Another parable of hyperbole, contrast, growth, and transformation of domestic images (mustard seed, yeast). ^13-21

### 13.22–30: Who will be saved

*[[NRSVue Luke 13#^13-22|Text]], p. 144* · ([[Matthew 07#^7-13|Mt 7.13–14]],[[Matthew 07#^7-22|22–23]]; [[Matthew 08#^8-11|8.11–12]]; [[Matthew 19#^19-30|19.30]]; [[Mark 10#^10-31|Mk 10.31]]). ^13-22

**[[NRSVue Luke 13#^13-23|23]]:** *A few be saved*, consistent with the biblical view of the righteous remnant (e.g., [[Isaiah 06#^6-13|Isa 6.13]]; [[Isaiah 10#^10-20|10.20–22]]; [[Jeremiah 23#^23-23|Jer 23.23]]); also a concern of much apocalyptic literature (see esp. *4 Ezra* \[2 Esd\]), but not rabbinic literature, which takes a generous view of salvation (e.g., *m. Sanh*. 10.1). ^13-23

**[[NRSVue Luke 13#^13-24|24]]:** *Will not be able*, restricted access to salvation. ^13-24

**[[NRSVue Luke 13#^13-28|28]]:** *Weeping and gnashing of teeth*, see [[Matthew 08#^8-12|Mt 8.12]]; [[Matthew 13#^13-42|13.42]],[[Matthew 13#^13-50|50]]; [[Matthew 22#^22-13|22.13]]; [[Matthew 24#^24-51|24.51]]; [[Matthew 25#^25-30|25.30]]. *Abraham and Isaac and Jacob*, unity with the ancestors in the world-to-come. ^13-28

**[[NRSVue Luke 13#^13-29|29]]:** *People will come*, perhaps originally referring to the ingathering of the exiles, a sign of the messianic age ([[Isaiah 60#^60-4|Isa 60.4]]; [[Zechariah 10#^10-6|Zech 10.6–10]]; [[Psalms 107#^107-3|Ps 107.3]]); for Luke, the Gentile mission. *Eat in the kingdom*, the messianic banquet (see 11.3n.). ^13-29

### 13.31–33: Herod’s threat

*[[NRSVue Luke 13#^13-31|Text]], p. 145* ^13-31

**[[NRSVue Luke 13#^13-31|31]]:** See “Pharisees in Luke,” p. 122. *Here*, Galilee and Perea (see 3.1n.). ^13-31-2

**[[NRSVue Luke 13#^13-32|32]]:** *Go and tell*, Jesus presumes Pharisees have access to Herod. *Fox*, an insult. ^13-32

**[[NRSVue Luke 13#^13-33|33]]:** *Prophet*, Jesus accepts this designation. ^13-33

### 13.34–35: Lament over Jerusalem

*[[NRSVue Luke 13#^13-34|Text]], p. 145* · ([[Matthew 23#^23-37|Mt 23.37–39]]; see also [[Luke 18#^18-41|Lk 18.41–42]]). ^13-34

**[[NRSVue Luke 13#^13-34|34]]:** *City that kills the prophets*, see 6.23; 11.47–50. The lament anticipates Stephen’s death (Acts 7). *Gather*, perhaps referring to the ingathering of the exiles (see v 29n.). ^13-34-2

**[[NRSVue Luke 13#^13-35|35]]:** *House*, Temple, see [[Jeremiah 22#^22-5|Jer 22.5]]. *Blessed is the one who comes*, [[Psalms 118#^118-26|Ps 118.26]], from the Hallel Psalms (113–118), recited on festivals, including Passover; see [[Luke 19#^19-37|Lk 19.37–38]]. The psalm does not, however, specify the identity of *the one*. ^13-35
