## Mūla

शिवतत्त्वमतः प्रोक्तमन्तिकं सर्वतोऽमुतः ।
अत एव प्रयत्नोऽयं तत्प्रवेशे न विद्यते ॥ ^mula

śivatattvamataḥ proktamantikaṃ sarvato'mutaḥ |
ata eva prayatno'yaṃ tatpraveśe na vidyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 13 - Chapter Ten, part 4#^pg447|p. 428]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=447>))
>
> The more the separation (bhid) between the moments (of perception) diminishes, the closer consciousness approaches, until it finally ends in iva. Thus the Śiva principle is said to be the closest of them all. (with 10.214–215ab)
>
> Therefore, no effort is required to penetrate into Him. The application of effort (yatnayoga) is in proportion to the distance (between us and Him). (with 10.215cd–216ab)
