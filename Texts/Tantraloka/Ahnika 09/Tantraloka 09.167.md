## Mūla

तथापि मालिनीशास्त्रदृशा तां संप्रचक्ष्महे ।
कलादिवसुधान्तं यन्मायान्तः संप्रचक्षते ॥ ^mula

tathāpi mālinīśāstradṛśā tāṃ saṃpracakṣmahe |
kalādivasudhāntaṃ yanmāyāntaḥ saṃpracakṣate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 06 - Chapter Nine, part 4#^pg161|p. 144]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=161>))
>
> Even though it is clearly apparent that (the nature of this causal relationship is) variously (conceived) in other scriptures, we will (limit ourselves) to explain it from the point of view of the Mālinīśāstra. (with 9.166cd–167ab)
>
> (The group of principles ranging) from the Force (of limited agency) up to Earth, that is within Māyā and will be described (further ahead), differs for each individual soul. (This is clear from the fact) that, for example, the joy and suffering (each one experiences are) different. (with 9.167cd–168ab)
