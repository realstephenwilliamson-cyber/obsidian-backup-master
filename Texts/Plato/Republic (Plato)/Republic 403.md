There is no contradiction between this and the half-serious proposal of the Laws to use supervised drinking-bouts as a safe test of character (Laws 641).] we said that they must abstain. For a guardian is surely the last person in the world to whom it is allowable to get drunk and not know where on earth he is. Yes, he said, it would absurd that a guardian^[γε emphasizes what follows from the very meaning of the word. Cf. 379 B, 389 B, 435 A.] should need a guard. What next about their food?
