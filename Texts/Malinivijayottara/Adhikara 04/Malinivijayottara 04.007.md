## Mūla

क्रियाज्ञनविभेदेन सा च द्वेधा निगद्यते ।
द्विविधा सा प्रकर्तव्या तेन चैतदुदाहृतम् ॥ ^mula

kriyājñanavibhedena sā ca dvedhā nigadyate |
dvividhā sā prakartavyā tena caitad udāhṛtam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, April 2023 – June 2023
> p. 16 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=57>))
>
> (Thus) in order to achieve yoga with seed (sabījayoga)[^6] (just knowledge of) the characteristics of mantras is (not) sufficient. There is no competence (adhikāritā) (to practice) Śaiva Yoga without initiation. [n. 7] Moreover, (initiation) is said to be of two kinds in accord with the distinction between (the practice based on the performance of ritual) action and (that conducive to the development of the) knowledge (of ultimate principles). Thus, this has been said because that (initiation) should be performed in the two (corresponding) ways. Śiva's initiation not only confers entitlement (to the fruits of) yoga (and its practice), it also (confers the) authority (to practice) mantras as well as liberation (itself).”[^8] (with 4.6–8)

[^6]: Sabijayoga corresponds to sabijasamādhi in Patañjali's Yoga (YSū 1/46 *tā eva sabijaḥ samādhiḥ*). According to the *Yogasūtrabhāṣya* the term denotes the four kinds of meditative attainments (*samāpatti*) related to gross and subtle external objects (ibid. *tāś catasraḥ samāpattayo bahirvastubijā iti samādhir api sabijaḥ*). MV 4/6cd. This important line is paraphrased by Abhinavagupta in 8/193ab (192cd). He quotes it directly twice in 15/5ab and in 16/291ab (16/291), as does Jayaratha in TĀv ad 1/231cd-232ab (232), 4/42cd-43ab, 8/191- 193 (190cd-193ab), and intro. to 8/(210-212) (209cd-212ab). See note to comm. on 1/232cd-233ab.
[^8]: MV 4/4, 5cd-8 quoted in TĀ 16/288cd-292 (289-293ab) with no variants with respect to Somadeva's edition. MV 4/8 is quoted in TĀ 15/5cd-6ab; MV 4/8d is quoted in TĀv introduction to TĀ 2/41.
