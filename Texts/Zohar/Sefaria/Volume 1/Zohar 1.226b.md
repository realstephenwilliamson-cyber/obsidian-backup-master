# Zohar 1.226b

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 3.07 - Parashat Va-Yhi#^z1-226b|Parashat Va-Yḥi, at 1:226b]]

*Volume 1, folio 226b* · Vayechi

*Passage Vayechi 33:347 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 1.226a#^vayechi-33-347|Vayechi 33:347]].*

**Vayechi 33:348** After twelve months the whole is at rest; the body reposes in the dust and the soul is clad in its luminous vestment. The spirit regales itself in the Garden of Eden, and the higher soul (*neshamah*) ascends to the place where all delights are concentrated; and all three come together again at certain times. ^vayechi-33-348

**Vayechi 33:349** Alas for men that they look not to their foundation, and neglect the precepts of the Torah. For some of these precepts fashion a glorious garment above, and some a glorious garment below, and some a glorious garment in this world; and man requires them all. And they are made literally out of his days, as we have explained. ^vayechi-33-349

**Vayechi 33:350** R. Judah the Elder one day saw in a dream his own image illumined and radiating brightly in all directions. ‘What is that?’ he said; and the answer came: ‘It is thy garment for thy habitation here’; whereupon he was in great joy. ^vayechi-33-350

**Vayechi 33:351** R. Judah said: ‘Every day the spirits of the righteous sit in rows in the Garden of Eden arrayed in their robes and praise God gloriously, as it is written: “Verily the righteous shall praise thy name the upright shall sit before thee.” ‘ ^vayechi-33-351

**Vayechi 33:352** R. Abba said: ‘When Jacob “bowed down to Him that is over the bed”, as we have explained, and knew that he had reached the highest grade, and that his grade was on high with that of his fathers, and that he was the consummation of the whole, his heart was strengthened and he rejoiced in God’s favour towards him. Hence it says, “And Jacob strengthened himself.” ‘ ^vayechi-33-352

**Vayechi 34:353** R. Judah said: ‘We learn in the Mishnah that judgement is pronounced on the world at four seasons: at Passover, in respect of produce; at Pentecost, in respect of fruit-trees; on New Year, when “all the denizens of the world pass before Him like a flock of sheep”; and on Tabernacles, when the rainfall is determined. This we have esoterically explained as follows. Passover is the time for the decision with regard to cereals, because on Passover Israel began to enter into the holy portion of the Almighty and to remove from themselves the leaven which symbolizes the powers who are appointed over the idol-worshipping nations and who are called “strange gods”. ^vayechi-34-353

**Vayechi 34:354** On Pentecost judgement is passed in respect of the fruit of the tree: this is the great and mighty tree which rears itself aloft. ^vayechi-34-354

**Vayechi 34:355** On New Year all pass before Him like a flock of sheep, because New Year (lit. head of the year) is the head of the King. ^vayechi-34-355

**Vayechi 34:356** On Tabernacles judgement is pronounced in respect of water, because this festival is the beginning of the right hand of the King, and therefore the rejoicing of water is universally diffused.’ ^vayechi-34-356
