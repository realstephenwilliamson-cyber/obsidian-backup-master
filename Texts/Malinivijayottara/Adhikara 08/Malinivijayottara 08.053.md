## Mūla

तदम्बुना समापूर्य षड्भिरङ्गैः समर्प्य च ।
अमृतीकृत्य सर्वाणि तेन द्रव्याणि शोधयेत् ॥ ^mula

tadambunā samāpūrya ṣaḍbhiraṅgaiḥ[^58] samarpya ca |
amṛtīkṛtya sarvāṇi tena dravyāṇi śodhayet || ^iast

> After having filled (the sacrificial vessel) with water, offered it with the six limbs (of the mantra, and thus) transformed it into nectar, all the (sacrificial) substances should be purified with it.
