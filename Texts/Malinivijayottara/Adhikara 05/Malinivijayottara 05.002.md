## Mūla

अवीचिः कुम्भीपाकश्च रौरवश्च तृतीयकः ।
कूष्माण्डभुवने शुद्धे सर्वे शुद्धा न संशयः ॥ ^mula

avīciḥ kumbhīpākaś ca rauravaś ca tṛtīyakaḥ |
kūṣmāṇḍabhuvane śuddhe sarve śuddhā na saṁśayaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> p. 69 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=74>))
>
> **1) Kālāgni and the Netherworlds**
>
> *Initially one should purify the world of Kālāgni with effort*.[^29] Once the (nether) world of Kūṣmāṇḍa has been purified, Avīci and Kumbhīpāka along with Raurava, the third (hell), are all undoubtedly purified. (with 5.1cd–2)

[^29]: MV 5/1d TĀv ad 8/ (438-445) (437cd-445ab).
