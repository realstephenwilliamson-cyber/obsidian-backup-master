# Isaiah 25 (Douay-Rheims)

*Isaias 25 in the Douay-Rheims*

[[DRB Isaiah 24|← Isaiah 24]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Isaiah 26|Isaiah 26 →]]

**25:1** O Lord, thou art my God, I will exalt thee, and give glory to thy name: for thou hast done wonderful things, thy designs of old faithful, amen. ^25-1

**25:2** For thou hast reduced the city to a heap, the strong city to ruin, the house of strangers, to be no city, and to be no more built up for ever. ^25-2

**25:3** Therefore shall a strong people praise thee, the city of mighty nations shall fear thee. ^25-3

**25:4** Because thou hast been a strength to the poor, a strength to the needy in his distress: a refuge from the whirlwind, a shadow from the heat. For the blast of the mighty is like a whirlwind beating against a wall. ^25-4

**25:5** Thou shalt bring down the tumult of strangers, as heat in thirst: and as with heat under a burning cloud, thou shalt make the branch of the mighty to wither away. ^25-5

**25:6** And the Lord of hosts shall make unto all people in this mountain, a feast of fat things, a feast of wine, of fat things full of marrow, of wine purified from the lees. ^25-6

**25:7** And he shall destroy in this mountain the face of the bond with which all people were tied, and the web that he began over all nations. ^25-7

**25:8** He shall cast death down headlong for ever: and the Lord God shall wipe away tears from every face, and the reproach of his people he shall take away from off the whole earth: for the Lord hath spoken it. ^25-8

**25:9** And they shall say in that day: Lo, this is our God, we have waited for him, and he will save us: this is the Lord, we have patiently waited for him, we shall rejoice and be joyfull in his salvation. ^25-9

**25:10** For the hand of the Lord shall rest in this mountain: and Moab shall be trodden down under him, as straw is broken in pieces with the wain. ^25-10

**25:11** And he shall stretch forth his hands under him, as he that swimmeth stretcheth forth his hands to swim: and he shall bring down his glory with the dashing of his hands. ^25-11

**25:12** And the bulwarks of thy high walls shall fall, and be brought low, and shall be pulled down to the ground, even to the dust. ^25-12
