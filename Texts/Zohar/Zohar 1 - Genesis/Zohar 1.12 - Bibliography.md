# Bibliography

This bibliography includes works cited and utilized by the translator, except for standard rabbinic texts and most reference works. Readers seeking further resources on the *Zohar* can consult *The Library of Gershom Scholem on Jewish Mysticism: Catalogue*, edited by Joseph Dan, Esther Liebes, and Shmuel Reem.
