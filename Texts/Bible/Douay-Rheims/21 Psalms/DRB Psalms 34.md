# Psalms 34 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 33|← Psalms 33]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 35|Psalms 35 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 34|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**34:1** For David himself. Judge thou, O Lord, them that wrong me: overthrow them that fight against me. ^34-1

**34:2** Take hold of arms and shield: and rise up to help me. ^34-2

**34:3** Bring out the sword, and shut up the way against them that persecute me: say to my soul: I am thy salvation. ^34-3

**34:4** Let them be confounded and ashamed that seek after my soul. Let them be turned back and be confounded that devise against me. ^34-4

**34:5** Let them become as dust before the wind: and let the angel of the Lord straiten them. ^34-5

**34:6** Let their way become dark and slippery; and let the angel of the Lord pursue them. ^34-6

**34:7** For without cause they have hidden their net for me unto destruction: without cause they have upbraided my soul. ^34-7

**34:8** Let the snare which he knoweth not come upon him: and let the net which he hath hidden catch him: and let the net which he hath hidden catch him: and into that very snare let them fall. ^34-8

**34:9** But my soul shall rejoice in the Lord; and shall be delighted in his salvation. ^34-9

**34:10** All my bones shall say: Lord, who is like to thee? Who deliverest the poor from the hand of them that are stronger than he; the needy and the poor from them that strip him. ^34-10

**34:11** Unjust witnesses rising up have asked me things I knew not. ^34-11

**34:12** They repaid me evil for good: to the depriving me of my soul. ^34-12

**34:13** But as for me, when they were troublesome to me, I was clothed with haircloth. I humbled my soul with fasting; and my prayer shall be turned into my bosom. ^34-13

**34:14** As a neighbour and as an own brother, so did I please: as one mourning and sorrowful so was I humbled. ^34-14

**34:15** But they rejoiced against me, and came together: scourges were gathered together upon me, and I knew not. ^34-15

**34:16** They were separated, and repented not: they tempted me, they scoffed at me with scorn: they gnashed upon me with their teeth. ^34-16

**34:17** Lord, when wilt thou look upon me? rescue thou soul from their malice: my only one from the lions. ^34-17

**34:18** I will give thanks to thee in a great church; I will praise thee in a strong people. ^34-18

**34:19** Let not them that are my enemies wrongfully rejoice over me: who have hated me without cause, and wink with the eyes. ^34-19

**34:20** For they spoke indeed peaceably to me; and speaking in the anger of the earth they devised guile. ^34-20

**34:21** And they opened their mouth wide against me; they said: Well done, well done, our eyes have seen it. ^34-21

**34:22** Thou hast seen, O Lord, be not thou silent: O Lord, depart not from me. ^34-22

**34:23** Arise, and be attentive to my judgment: to my cause, my God, and my Lord. ^34-23

**34:24** Judge me, O Lord my God according to thy justice, and let them not rejoice over me. ^34-24

**34:25** Let them not say in their hearts: It is well, it is well, to our mind: neither let them say: We have swallowed him up. ^34-25

**34:26** Let them blush: and be ashamed together, who rejoice at my evils. Let them be clothed with confusion and shame, who speak great things against me. ^34-26

**34:27** Let them rejoice and be glad, who are well pleased with my justice, and let them say always: The Lord be magnified, who delights in the peace of his servant. ^34-27

**34:28** Any my tongue shall meditate thy justice, thy praise all the day long. ^34-28
