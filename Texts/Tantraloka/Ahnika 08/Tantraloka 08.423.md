## Mūla

वामेशरूपसूक्ष्मं शुद्धं विद्याथ शक्तितेजस्विमितिः ।
सुविशुद्धिशिवौ मोक्ष ध्रुवेषिसंबुद्धसमयसौशिवसंज्ञाः ॥ ^mula

vāmeśarūpasūkṣmaṃ śuddhaṃ vidyātha śaktitejasvimitiḥ |
suviśuddhiśivau mokṣa dhruveṣisaṃbuddhasamayasauśivasaṃjñāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 11 - Chapter Eight, part 9#^pg400|p. 382]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=400>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 8.423cd–424ab.
>
> 1) Vāmā (and the rest), 2) 158, 3) (the Enclosure of) Form, 4) the Subtle (Enclosure), 5) the Pure One, 6) that of Knowledge (Vidyā), 7) Śakti 8) Tejasvin (Energetic), 9) (the Enclosure) of Knowledge (Miti), 10) the Perfectly Pure and 11) Śiva, and 12) the ones called Mokgṣa (Liberation), 13) Dhruva (the Immobile), 14) Iṣi (Intention), 15) Saṁbuddha (the Awakened Ones), 16) Samaya (the Rule) and 17) Sauśiva. The Quiescent (śĀānta) that consists of seventeen worlds is linked to the three worlds of (the principles of) Vidyā (Pure) Knowledge, Īśvara and Sadāśiva. (with 8.423–424ab)
