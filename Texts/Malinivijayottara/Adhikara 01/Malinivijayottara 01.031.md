## Mūla

तत्त्रिधा तैजसात्तस्मात्मनोऽक्षेशमजायत ।
वैकारिकात्ततोऽक्षाणि तन्मात्राणि तृतीयकात् ॥ ^mula

tat tridhā taijasāt tasmāt mano 'kṣeśam ajāyata |
vaikārikāt tato 'kṣāṇi tanmātrāṇi tṛtīyakāt || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2022 – June 2022
> p. 45 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=21>))
>
> That (ego) is of three kinds. From the energetic (rājasika ego) originated the mind (manas), which is the lord of the senses; then from (its original sāttvika) transformation, the senses; and the subtle elements (of sensory sensations), from the third (tāmasika kind).[^67]

[^67]: Abhinavagupta refers to this verse in TĀ 9/276cd-277ab and Jayaratha quotes it in TĀv ad 9/272cd-273ab.
