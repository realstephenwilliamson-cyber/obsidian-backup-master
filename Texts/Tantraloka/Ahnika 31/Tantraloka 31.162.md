## Mūla

रेखाद्वयं पातयेत यथा शूलं भवत्यपि ।
अधोभागादिभिश्चोर्ध्वं तत्र रेखा प्रपद्यते ॥ ^mula

rekhādvayaṃ pātayeta yathā śūlaṃ bhavatyapi |
adhobhāgādibhiścordhvaṃ tatra rekhā prapadyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 05 - Chapter Thirty-One, part 2#^pg150|p. 132]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=150>))
>
> In the middle, above and below, one should cast two lines, two finger-breadths (apart from one another), in such a way that the trident is also (fashioned) there. Once the lower (and middle) parts have been made equal with the upper (one), a line is formed there. Then, the two threads above (should be cast) in the same way. (with 31.161cd–163ab)

## Viveka

> [!viveka]- Viveka in English (Dyczkowski, vol. XI, pp. 132–133)
> ![[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 05 - Chapter Thirty-One, part 2#Viveka on 31.161–163]]

> मध्ये चोर्ध्वं ततः कुर्यादधस्तादंगुलद्वयम् ॥
> रेखाद्वयं पातयेत यथा शूलं भवत्यपि ।
> अधोभागादिभिश्चोर्ध्वं तत्र रेखा प्रपद्यते ॥
> समीकृत्य ततः सूत्रे ऊर्ध्वे द्वे एवमेव तु ।

एवं स्थानत्रये अंगुलद्वयान्तरालं द्वयोः पार्श्वयोः रेखाद्वयं कुर्यात् येन सर्वतः साम्येन अधोमध्यभागाभ्यां सह ऊर्ध्वं समीकृत्य रेखा प्रपद्यते, ततस्तथैव द्वे ऊर्ध्वे सूत्रे पातयेत यथा सदण्डं शूलं संपद्यते ॥
