# Sirach 49 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Sirach 48|← Sirach 48]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Sirach 50|Sirach 50 →]]

## Josiah and other worthies

**49:1** The name^[Heb: Gk *memory*] of Josiah is like blended incense prepared by the skill of the perfumer; his memory^[Heb: Gk *it*] is as sweet as honey to every mouth and like music at a banquet of wine. ^49-1

**49:2** He did what was right by reforming the people and removing the lawless abominations. ^49-2

**49:3** He kept his heart fixed on the Lord; in lawless times he made godliness prevail. ^49-3

**49:4** Except for David and Hezekiah and Josiah, all of them were great sinners, for they abandoned the law of the Most High; the kings of Judah came to an end. ^49-4

**49:5** They^[Heb *He*] gave their power to others and their glory to a foreign nation, ^49-5

**49:6** who set fire to the chosen city of the sanctuary and made its streets desolate, as Jeremiah had foretold.^[Gk *by the hand of Jeremiah*] ^49-6

**49:7** For they had mistreated him, who even in the womb had been consecrated a prophet, to pluck up and ruin and destroy and likewise to build and to plant. ^49-7

**49:8** It was Ezekiel who saw the vision of glory, which God^[Gk *He*] showed him above the chariot of the cherubim. ^49-8

**49:9** For he also mentioned Job, who held fast to all the ways of justice.^[Heb Compare Syr: Meaning of Gk uncertain] ^49-9

**49:10** May the bones of the Twelve Prophets send forth new life from where they lie, for they comforted the people of Jacob and delivered them with confident hope. ^49-10

**49:11** How shall we magnify Zerubbabel? He was like a signet ring on the right hand, ^49-11

**49:12** and so was Jeshua son of Jozadak; in their days they built the house and raised a temple^[Other ancient authorities read *people*] holy to the Lord, prepared for everlasting glory. ^49-12

**49:13** The memory of Nehemiah also is lasting; he raised our fallen walls and set up gates and bars and rebuilt our ruined houses. ^49-13

## Retrospect

**49:14** Few have^[Heb Syr: Gk *No one has*] ever been created on earth like Enoch, for he was taken up from the earth. ^49-14

**49:15** Nor was anyone ever born like Joseph;^[Heb Syr: Gk adds *the leader of his brothers, the support of the people*] even his bones were cared for. ^49-15

**49:16** Shem and Seth and Enosh were honored,^[Heb: Gk *Shem and Seth were honored by people*] but above every other created living being was Adam. ^49-16
