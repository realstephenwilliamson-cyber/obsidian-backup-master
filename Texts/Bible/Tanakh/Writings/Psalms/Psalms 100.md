## Chapter 100

**100:1** A psalm for praise.[^1]  
Raise a shout for the LORD, all the earth; ^100-1

**100:2** worship the LORD in gladness;  
come into His presence with shouts of joy. ^100-2

**100:3** Acknowledge that the LORD is God;  
He made us and we are His,[^2]  
His people, the flock He tends. ^100-3

**100:4** Enter His gates with praise,  
His courts with acclamation.  
Praise Him!  
Bless His name! ^100-4

**100:5** For the LORD is good;  
His steadfast love is eternal;  
His faithfulness is for all generations. ^100-5

[^1]: Traditionally “for the thanksgiving offering.”
[^2]: So qere; kethib and some ancient versions “not we ourselves.”
