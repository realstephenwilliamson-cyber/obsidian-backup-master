## Chapter 13

**13:1** For the leader. A psalm of David. ^13-1

**13:2** How long, O LORD; will You ignore me forever?  
How long will You hide Your face from me? ^13-2

**13:3** How long will I have cares on my mind,  
grief in my heart all day?  
How long will my enemy have the upper hand? ^13-3

**13:4** Look at me, answer me, O LORD, my God!  
Restore the luster to my eyes,  
lest I sleep the sleep of death; ^13-4

**13:5** lest my enemy say, “I have overcome him,”  
my foes exult when I totter. ^13-5

**13:6** But I trust in Your faithfulness,  
my heart will exult in Your deliverance.  
I will sing to the LORD,  
for He has been good to me. ^13-6
