# siddhāntasārapaddhati

**Catalog number:** M00531  
**Author:** bhojadeva ascribed to  
**Manuscript:** NGMCP 5-743/vi <zaivatantra> 203 Reel no: B 28/19  
**Published:** Muktabodha Indological Research Institute, India

## Description

From an old palm leaf manuscript from Nepal which though undated indicates the texts early date.  If the text was indeed compiled in the 11th century, then this is a rare example of an early śaiva liturgical manual.
