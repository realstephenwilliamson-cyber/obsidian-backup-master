# LXX Sirach 07

← [[LXX Sirach 06]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Sirach 08]] →

*Sirach · [[LXX]]*

**7:1** Do no evil, so shall no harm come unto thee.  ^7-1

**7:2** Depart from the unjust, and iniquity shall turn away from thee.  ^7-2

**7:3** My son, sow not upon the furrows of unrighteousness, and thou shalt not reap them sevenfold.  ^7-3

**7:4** Seek not of the Lord preeminence, neither of the king the seat of honour.  ^7-4

**7:5** Justify not thyself before the Lord; and boast not of thy wisdom before the king.  ^7-5

**7:6** Seek not to be judge, being not able to take away iniquity; lest at any time thou fear the person of the mighty, and lay a stumblingblock in the way of thy uprightness.  ^7-6

**7:7** Offend not against the multitude of a city, and then thou shalt not cast thyself down among the people.  ^7-7

**7:8** Bind not one sin upon another; for in one thou shalt not be unpunished.  ^7-8

**7:9** Say not, God will look upon the multitude of my oblations, and when I offer to the most high God, he will accept it.  ^7-9

**7:10** Be not fainthearted when thou makest thy prayer, and neglect not to give alms.  ^7-10

**7:11** Laugh no man to scorn in the bitterness of his soul: for there is one which humbleth and exalteth.  ^7-11

**7:12** Devise not a lie against thy brother; neither do the like to thy friend.  ^7-12

**7:13** Use not to make any manner of lie: for the custom thereof is not good.  ^7-13

**7:14** Use not many words in a multitude of elders, and make not much babbling when thou prayest.  ^7-14

**7:15** Hate not laborious work, neither husbandry, which the most High hath ordained.  ^7-15

**7:16** Number not thyself among the multitude of sinners, but remember that wrath will not tarry long.  ^7-16

**7:17** Humble thy soul greatly: for the vengeance of the ungodly is fire and worms.  ^7-17

**7:18** Change not a friend for any good by no means; neither a faithful brother for the gold of Suphir.  ^7-18

**7:19** Forego not a wise and good woman: for her grace is above gold.  ^7-19

**7:20** Whereas thy servant worketh truly, entreat him not evil, nor the hireling that bestoweth himself wholly for thee.  ^7-20

**7:21** Let thy soul love a good servant, and defraud him not of liberty.  ^7-21

**7:22** Hast thou cattle? have an eye to them: and if they be for thy profit, keep them with thee.  ^7-22

**7:23** Hast thou children? instruct them, and bow down their neck from their youth.  ^7-23

**7:24** Hast thou daughters? have a care of their body, and shew not thyself cheerful toward them.  ^7-24

**7:25** Marry thy daughter, and so shalt thou have performed a weighty matter: but give her to a man of understanding.  ^7-25

**7:26** Hast thou a wife after thy mind? forsake her not.  ^7-26

**7:27** Honour thy father with thy whole heart, and forget not the sorrows of thy mother.  ^7-27

**7:28** Remember that thou wast begotten of them; and how canst thou recompense them the things that they have done for thee?  ^7-28

**7:29** Fear the Lord with all thy soul, and reverence his priests.  ^7-29

**7:30** Love him that made thee with all thy strength, and forsake not his ministers.  ^7-30

**7:31** Fear the Lord, and honour the priest: and give him his portion, as it is commanded thee: the firstfruits, and the trespass offering, and the gift of the shoulders, and the sacrifice of sanctification, and the firstfruits of the holy things.  ^7-31

**7:32** And stretch thine hand unto the poor, that thy blessing may be perfected.  ^7-32

**7:33** A gift hath grace in the sight of every man living; and for the dead detain it not.  ^7-33

**7:34** Fail not to be with them that weep, and mourn with them that mourn.  ^7-34

**7:35** Be not slow to visit the sick: for that shall make thee to be beloved.  ^7-35

**7:36** Whatsoever thou takest in hand, remember the end, and thou shalt never do amiss.  ^7-36
