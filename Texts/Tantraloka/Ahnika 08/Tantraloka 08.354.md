## Mūla

इष्टा च तन्निवृत्तिर्ह्यभवस्त्वधरे न भूयते यस्मात् ।
पत्युरपसर्पति यतः कारणता कार्यता च सिद्धेभ्यः ॥ ^mula

iṣṭā ca tannivṛttirhyabhavastvadhare na bhūyate yasmāt |
patyurapasarpati yataḥ kāraṇatā kāryatā ca siddhebhyaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 10 - Chapter Eight, part 8#^pg362|p. 344]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=362>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 8.354cd–355ab.
>
> For even here, Śiva commissions (beings to aid) those he has seen fit to grace; (but although that is good), it is preferable for even such (work) to come to an end. The absence of phenomenal existence (does not therefore imply liberation, it is called this) because it does not exist at the lower level. (with 8.353cd–354ab)
>
> The Lord’s condition as the cause (kāraṇatā) (of office) and task (kāryatā) (to be carried out) retreats from perfected souls (siddha). Thus, when Śiva is attained as the one called ‘beyond phenomenal existence’ (atibhava), (both come to an end,) as does the covering (kañcuka) (of Māyā) (that obscures them), and these two (bhava and abhava) are completely aloof (atimadhyasthau) (from carrying out any task or function). (with 8.354cd–355ab)

*Lead-in:* तन्निवृत्तिमेव व्याचष्टे --

> पत्युरपसर्पति यतः कारणता कार्यता च सिद्धेभ्यः ॥
> कञ्चुकवच्छिवसिद्धौ तावति भवसंज्ञयातिमध्यस्थौ ।

"कारणता"नियोक्तृत्वम्, "कार्यता"नियोज्यत्वम्, शिद्धेभ्यः"इति मुक्ताणुभ्यः, "कञ्चुकवत्"इति मायीयावरणवत् । तदपि हि तथा निवर्तत एव -- इति भावः । यतस्तौ शिवसिद्धौ अतिभवरूपत्वात् "अतिमध्यस्थौ"अत्युदासीनौ नियोज्यनियोक्तृतादिक्षोभशून्यौ भवतः -- इत्यर्थः ॥ ३५४ ॥
