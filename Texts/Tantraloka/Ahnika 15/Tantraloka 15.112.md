## Mūla

साध्यं तदस्य दार्ढ्येन सफलत्वाय कल्पते ।
उक्तं श्रीसारशास्त्रे च निर्विकल्पो हि सिध्यति ॥ ^mula

sādhyaṃ tadasya dārḍhyena saphalatvāya kalpate |
uktaṃ śrīsāraśāstre ca nirvikalpo hi sidhyati || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 03 - Chapter Fifteen, part 2#^pg94|p. 76]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=94>))
>
> A person whose heart is pure should cause (the Deity who is) the goal to be achieved (sādhya) to be reflected there (within it). Its stability (dārḍhya) (there) serves to make (his practice) fruitful. (with 15.111cd–112ab)
>
> It is said in the venerable Sāraśāstra that it is the person who is free of thought that is successful, whereas those (whose minds are full of) thoughts suffer (kliśyante), even if (all) has been done in accord with the procedure (kalpokta). (with 15.112cd–113ab)
