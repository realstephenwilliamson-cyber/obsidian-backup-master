# Zohar 3.009b

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 7.03 - Parashat Va-Yiqra#^z3-9b|Parashat Va-Yiqra, at 3:9b]]

*Volume 3, folio 9b* · Vayikra

*Passage Vayikra 20:128 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 3.009a#^vayikra-20-128|Vayikra 20:128]].*

**Vayikra 20:129** Now the offering of a poor man is highly esteemed before God, because he brings two offerings: one the actual sacrifice, and the other his own flesh and blood, because, though he has nothing to eat himself, he yet brings an offering. A poor man can bring a little flour and make atonement; ^vayikra-20-129

**Vayikra 20:130** for just as his own flesh and blood were burning (with hunger), so the flour is heated with the oil smeared on it. Here, too, we learn that any man may bring an offering on a baking-pan or a frying-pan because it is heated in the same way as he heated his flesh and blood with his evil passions and set all his limbs on fire. The essence of the offering is that it is analogous to the sin, and that a man should offer to God his desires and passions, for this is more acceptable than all. ^vayikra-20-130

**Vayikra 20:131** Blessed are the righteous that they bring this offering every day. Yet withal, the actual offering is better, because it brings blessings on all worlds.’ ^vayikra-20-131

**Vayikra 21:132** He further discoursed on the verse: “Blessed be the Lord out of Zion, who dwelleth at Jerusalem, hallelukah” (Ps. 135, 21). He said: ‘Is the Lord blessed from Zion? Is He not blessed from the recondite supernal stream? What it means, however, is that the Lord is blessed when the Moon is illumined by the Sun and they draw near one another. ^vayikra-21-132

**Vayikra 21:133** Or again, it may mean that the place from which it is known that the Holy One, blessed be He, is blessed is Zion, as it is written: “For there the Lord commanded the blessing, etc.” (Ps. 133, 3).’ Said R. Jesse to him: ‘Blessed art thou that thou hast attained to all this, and blessed are all those that study the Torah.’ ^vayikra-21-133

**Vayikra 22:134** IF HIS OBLATION BE A SACRIFICE OF PEACE OFFERINGS. R. Judah discoursed here on the verse: “And God said, Let there be a firmament in the midst of the waters” (Gen. 1, 6). ‘When God created the world’, he said, ‘He created seven firmaments above, and in each one stars and constellations and ministers to serve, ^vayikra-22-134

**Vayikra 22:135** and Chariots one above the other to take upon themselves the yoke of the kingship of their Master. Some have six wings, some four wings; some have four faces, some two faces, and some one face; some are of fire, some of water, and some of air. ^vayikra-22-135

**Vayikra 22:136** The firmaments envelop one another like the skins of an onion, and every firmament trembles from the fear of its Master by whose command it moves or stops. Over all is the Holy One, blessed be He, who sustains all in His power and might. Similarly there are seven earths below, one higher than the other, the Land of Israel being the highest of all and Jerusalem being the highest point in the whole inhabited world. ^vayikra-22-136
