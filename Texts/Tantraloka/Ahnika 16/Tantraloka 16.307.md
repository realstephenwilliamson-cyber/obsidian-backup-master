## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 07 - Chapter Sixteen, part 3#^pg156|p. 136]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=156>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 16.306cd–307ab.
>
> The best of teachers, who is engaged in the initiation that consists (of offering) sacrificial substances, should make offerings to the fire with clarified butter and sesame seeds etc., saying “I purify his karma”. In (the course of) initiation consisting of knowledge, that (disciple’s karma) is purified by the intent (sandhi) of the teacher who is deeply rooted (rūḍha) in his own consciousness and by whose power (balāt) its destruction takes place. If (only the disciple’s) inauspicious actions are purified, the good (actions) bear fruit in various degrees, depending on the degree of their own excellence, (in this or that place) in the midst of the path. (with 16.304cd–307ab)
>
> Once the enjoyment of the fruits accrued by the gradual maturing (and digestion) of auspicious (good Karma) is complete, and future Karma has thereby been destroyed, (the disciple) is present on that (reality level and world) to which he has been conjoined (by the teacher who initiates him there). (with 16.307cd–308ab)
