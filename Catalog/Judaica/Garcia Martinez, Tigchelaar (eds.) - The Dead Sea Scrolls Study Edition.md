The PDF is Brill's own file (Acrobat Distiller 4.0, 28 July 1999), 1,386 pages, the two volumes in one.

Transcription and translation face each other: the Hebrew or Aramaic on the left-hand (even) pages, the English on the right. The English is kept as notes in `Texts/Qumran` ([[Qumran]]): one note per manuscript, in the order of the edition, with a block for every numbered line, so that a reference such as 1QS 3.13 can be linked to `[[DSS 1QS#^3-13]]`. The Hebrew and Aramaic are only in the PDF. Notes converted on 9 November 2026.

Two other copies of the same edition were in Downloads and are not in the vault: a PDF re-saved by calibre from this file (1,388 pages), and an EPUB made in 2017 whose Hebrew is stored with its letters in reverse order. This file is the better of the three.
