## Mūla

मानसीयमितस्त्वन्याः पद्माद्या अष्ट मुद्रिकाः ।
मातृव्यूहकुले ताः स्युरस्यास्तु परिवारगाः ॥ ^mula

mānasīyamitastvanyāḥ padmādyā aṣṭa mudrikāḥ |
mātṛvyūhakule tāḥ syurasyāstu parivāragāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 06 - Chapter Thirty-Two#^pg178|p. 160]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=178>))
>
> Having reached the nose, the two eyes, the heart, the two breasts, the two testicles and the penis, this (mudrā) is present (in all) the body. One should ascertain the abode of transmigration and the abode of its absence by the upward movement (uccārena) (of Mudrā). This is the mental (form of the Seal of the Skyfarer). (with 32.50cd–52a)
>
> Apart from (the Skyfarer,) there are eight (other) mudras, including (the Seal of the) Lotus and the rest, that are amongst its entourage, (each belonging) to a family of the group (of eight) Mothers. The form of the entire body (as a whole) is the phoneme KṢ (kūṭākṣara). (It is said) in the Gahvaratantra that this seal is Bhairava’s Great Seal (mahāmudrā). (with 32.52bcd–53)
