# Psalm 117

← [[BCP Psalm 116]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 118]] →

*Laudate Dominum* · Book Five · Twenty-fourth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 117]]

**117:1** Praise the LORD, all you nations; \*  
laud him, all you peoples. ^117-1

**117:2** For his loving-kindness toward us is great, \*  
and the faithfulness of the LORD endures for ever.  
Hallelujah! ^117-2
