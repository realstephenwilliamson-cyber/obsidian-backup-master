# Psalm 120

← [[BCP Psalm 119]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 121]] →

*Ad Dominum* · Book Five · Twenty-seventh Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 120]]

**120:1** When I was in trouble, I called to the LORD; \*  
I called to the LORD, and he answered me. ^120-1

**120:2** Deliver me, O LORD, from lying lips \*  
and from the deceitful tongue. ^120-2

**120:3** What shall be done to you, and what more besides, \*  
O you deceitful tongue? ^120-3

**120:4** The sharpened arrows of a warrior, \*  
along with hot glowing coals. ^120-4

**120:5** How hateful it is that I must lodge in Meshech \*  
and dwell among the tents of Kedar! ^120-5

**120:6** Too long have I had to live \*  
among the enemies of peace. ^120-6

**120:7** I am on the side of peace, \*  
but when I speak of it, they are for war. ^120-7
