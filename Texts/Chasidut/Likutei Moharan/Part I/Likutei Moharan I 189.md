*Part I · Lesson 189* · [[Likutei Moharan]]

## 1

One has to be very vigilant against sadness and sluggishness. For the bite of the serpent is mainly sadness and sluggishness, as in ([[Isaiah 65#^65-25|Isaiah 65:25]]), “Dust shall be the serpents food.” “Dust” corresponds to sadness and sluggishness which come from the element of dust, as is brought in the writings. \<Study above, *Likutey Moharan* I, 56:end.>
