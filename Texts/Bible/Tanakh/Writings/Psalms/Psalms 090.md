## Chapter 90

**90:1** A prayer of Moses, the man of God.  
O Lord, You have been our refuge in every generation. ^90-1

**90:2** Before the mountains came into being,  
before You brought forth the earth and the world,  
from eternity to eternity You are God. ^90-2

**90:3** You return man to dust;[^1]  
You decreed, “Return you mortals!” ^90-3

**90:4** [^2]For in Your sight a thousand years  
are like yesterday that has passed,  
like a watch of the night. ^90-4

**90:5** You engulf men in sleep;  
at daybreak they are like grass that renews itself; ^90-5

**90:6** at daybreak it flourishes anew;  
by dusk it withers and dries up. ^90-6

**90:7** So we are consumed by Your anger,  
terror-struck by Your fury. ^90-7

**90:8** You have set our iniquities before You,  
our hidden sins in the light of Your face. ^90-8

**90:9** All our days pass away in Your wrath;  
we spend our years like a sigh. ^90-9

**90:10** The span of our life is seventy years,  
or, given the strength, eighty years;  
but the best of them[^3] are trouble and sorrow.  
They pass by speedily, and we are in darkness.[^4] ^90-10

**90:11** Who can know Your furious anger?  
Your wrath matches the fear of You. ^90-11

**90:12** Teach us to count our days rightly,  
that we may obtain a wise heart. ^90-12

**90:13** Turn, O LORD!  
How long?  
Show mercy to Your servants. ^90-13

**90:14** Satisfy us at daybreak with Your steadfast love  
that we may sing for joy all our days. ^90-14

**90:15** Give us joy for as long as You have afflicted us,  
for the years we have suffered misfortune. ^90-15

**90:16** Let Your deeds be seen by Your servants,  
Your glory by their children. ^90-16

**90:17** May the favor of the Lord, our God, be upon us;  
let the work of our hands prosper,  
O prosper the work of our hands! ^90-17

[^1]: Or “contrition.”
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^4]: Or “fly away.”
