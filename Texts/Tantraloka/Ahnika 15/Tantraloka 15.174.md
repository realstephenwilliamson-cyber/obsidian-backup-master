## Mūla

घोरान्ध्यहैमननिशामध्यगाचिरदीप्तिवत् ।
भक्ष्यो हंसो न भक्ष्योऽसाविति विप्रतिपत्तिषु ॥ ^mula

ghorāndhyahaimananiśāmadhyagāciradīptivat |
bhakṣyo haṃso na bhakṣyo'sāviti vipratipattiṣu || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 04 - Chapter Fifteen, part 3#^pg126|p. 108]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=126>))
>
> The Great Lord has revealed (this practice) even in the scriptures of the fettered, like a flash of light in the midst of a winter’s (haimana) night of dreadful, blinding darkness. (with 15.173cd–174ab)
>
> Only one who is purified by Śiva’s nondual (practice) triumphs over the (many) Vedic texts, which (frequently) disagree with one another, as happens (for example, where it is said in some places that) wild goose (haṁsa) can be eaten (and in others that) it cannot be eaten. (with 15.174cd–175ab)

*Lead-in:* ननु पाशवे शासने शुद्ध्यशुद्ध्योर्विभाग उक्तः, इह तु तदविभाग इति किमनयोः कल्पयोर्युज्येत?--इत्याशङ्क्य आह--

> भक्ष्यो हंसो न भक्ष्योऽसाविति विप्रतिपत्तिषु ॥
> स्मार्तीषु विजयत्येको यः शिवाभेदशुद्धिकः ।

भक्ष्य इति--यत् श्रुतिः--

"हंसो वृको वृषदंशस्ते एन्द्राः ।"इति ।

अभक्ष्य इति--यत्स्मृतिः--

"कलविङ्कं प्लवं हंसं चक्राह्वं ग्रामकुक्कुटम् ।"

इति अभक्ष्यप्रकरणे । विप्रतिपत्तिष्विति--श्रुतेः ॥
