## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 13 - Chapter Six, part 6#^pg455|p. 440]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=455>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 6.235cd–236ab.
>
> The letter KṢ consists of three half measures. There is a measure (for the two letters K and), and another half measure is in the pause (between the two). Once it has been included in the calculation, one has to divide each half finger-breadth into seven, and (to make a) half measure, (and attribute to each, six of these parts). The letter KṢ, as stated before, includes (symbolically) all the conjunctions (between letters) (sarnyoga), and (so) is present in all (Mantras). Arising at the beginning and end of all the letters and in their conjunctions, (it symbolizes the) all-pervasive Lord. (with 6.234cd–236)
