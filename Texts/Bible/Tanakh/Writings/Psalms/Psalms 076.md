## Chapter 76

**76:1** For the leader; with instrumental music.  
A psalm of Asaph, a song. ^76-1

**76:2** God has made Himself known in Judah,  
His name is great in Israel; ^76-2

**76:3** Salem became His abode;  
Zion, His den. ^76-3

**76:4** There He broke the fiery arrows of the bow,  
the shield and the sword of war. *Selah*. ^76-4

**76:5** You were resplendent,  
glorious, on the mountains of prey. ^76-5

**76:6** The stout-hearted were despoiled;  
they were in a stupor;  
the bravest of men could not lift a hand. ^76-6

**76:7** At Your blast, O God of Jacob,  
horse and chariot lay stunned. ^76-7

**76:8** O You! You are awesome!  
Who can withstand You  
when You are enraged? ^76-8

**76:9** In heaven You pronounced sentence;  
the earth was numbed with fright ^76-9

**76:10** as God rose to execute judgment,  
to deliver all the lowly of the earth. *Selah*. ^76-10

**76:11** The fiercest of men shall acknowledge You,  
when You gird on the last bit of fury.[^1] ^76-11

**76:12** Make vows and pay them to the LORD your God;  
all who are around Him shall bring tribute to the Awesome One.[^2] ^76-12

**76:13** He curbs the spirit of princes,  
inspires awe in the kings of the earth. ^76-13

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
