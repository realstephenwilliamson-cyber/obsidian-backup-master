## Mūla

अनुत्तरं परं धाम तदेवाकुलमुच्यते ।
विसर्गस्तस्य नाथस्य कौलिकी शक्तिरुच्यते ॥ ^mula

anuttaraṃ paraṃ dhāma tadevākulamucyate |
visargastasya nāthasya kaulikī śaktirucyate || ^iast

> The Supreme Abode – the Absolute (Anuttara) – is called Akula, whereas the emission (visarga) of that Lord is called Kaulikī śakti.
