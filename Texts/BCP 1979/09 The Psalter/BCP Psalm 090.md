# Psalm 90

← [[BCP Psalm 089]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 091]] →

*Domine, refugium* · Book Four · Eighteenth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 090]]

**90:1** Lord, you have been our refuge \*  
from one generation to another. ^90-1

[p. 718] ^pg718

**90:2** Before the mountains were brought forth,  
or the land and the earth were born, \*  
from age to age you are God. ^90-2

**90:3** You turn us back to the dust and say, \*  
"Go back, O child of earth." ^90-3

**90:4** For a thousand years in your sight are like yesterday when it is past \*  
and like a watch in the night. ^90-4

**90:5** You sweep us away like a dream; \*  
we fade away suddenly like the grass. ^90-5

**90:6** In the morning it is green and flourishes; \*  
in the evening it is dried up and withered. ^90-6

**90:7** For we consume away in your displeasure; \*  
we are afraid because of your wrathful indignation. ^90-7

**90:8** Our iniquities you have set before you, \*  
and our secret sins in the light of your countenance. ^90-8

**90:9** When you are angry, all our days are gone; \*  
we bring our years to an end like a sigh. ^90-9

**90:10** The span of our life is seventy years,  
perhaps in strength even eighty; \*  
yet the sum of them is but labor and sorrow,  
for they pass away quickly and we are gone. ^90-10

**90:11** Who regards the power of your wrath? \*  
who rightly fears your indignation? ^90-11

**90:12** So teach us to number our days \*  
that we may apply our hearts to wisdom. ^90-12

**90:13** Return, O LORD; how long will you tarry? \*  
be gracious to your servants. ^90-13

[p. 719] ^pg719

**90:14** Satisfy us by your loving-kindness in the morning; \*  
so shall we rejoice and be glad all the days of our life. ^90-14

**90:15** Make us glad by the measure of the days that you afflicted us \*  
and the years in which we suffered adversity. ^90-15

**90:16** Show your servants your works \*  
and your splendor to their children. ^90-16

**90:17** May the graciousness of the LORD our God be upon us; \*  
prosper the work of our hands;  
prosper our handiwork. ^90-17
