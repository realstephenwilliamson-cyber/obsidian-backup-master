## Mūla

द्वितीया शिव(शक्ति)रूपैव सर्वज्ञानक्रियात्मिका ।
तस्यामवहितो योगी किं न वेत्ति करोति वा ॥ ^mula

dvitīyā śiva(śakti)rūpaiva sarvajñānakriyātmikā |
tasyāmavahito yogī kiṃ na vetti karoti vā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 13 - Chapter Ten, part 4#^pg441|p. 422]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=441>))
>
> There, the first (of these) moments is (completely) pure and full. The second is Śakti, who is universal knowledge and action. (with 10.206cd–207ab)
>
> What does the yogi who pays attention to this not know or do? (with 10.207cd)
>
> The venerable Kallaṭa has said the same (in the Tattvārthacintāmaṇi): “one attains omniscience and omnipotence when the moment passes (tuṭipāta)”, whereby is meant the second moment. (with 10.207cd–208)
