## Mūla

स तया संप्रबुद्धः सन्योनिं विक्षोभ्य शक्तिभिः ।
तत्समानश्रुतीन्वर्णांस्तत्संख्यानसृजत्प्रभुः ॥ ^mula

sa tayā saṁprabuddhaḥ san yoniṁ vikṣobhya śaktibhiḥ |
tatsamānaśrutīn varṇāṁs tatsaṁkhyān asṛjat prabhuḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> p. 44 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=44>))

> Awakened by that (will) and having aroused the matrix (yoni) with (his) powers, the lord emitted an equal number of phonemes, with the same sound (as those). O (Goddess) praised by (all) the Heroes, embraced by them,[^21] they bestow as (their) fruits (the fulfilment of every) desire of the very best adepts, not otherwise.[^22] (with 3.27–28)

[^21]: Quoted in 16/211b.
