## Mūla

साहसानुप्रवेशेन कुञ्चितं हस्तयुग्मकम् ।
अधोवीक्षणशीलं च सम्यग्दृष्टिसमन्वितम् ॥ ^mula

sāhasānupraveśena kuñcitaṃ hastayugmakam |
adhovīkṣaṇaśīlaṃ ca samyagdṛṣṭisamanvitam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 06 - Chapter Thirty-Two#^pg183|p. 165]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=183>))
>
> 8) Looking downwards, endowed with sound (inner) vision (samyagdrṣti), the two hands contracted (and bent), as if throwing himself suddenly into a deep abyss by penetrating with audacity (and forcefully into consciousness) (sāhasa) - this is the Skyfarer who, called Vīrabhairava, develops consciousness. (KhecarT) has thus been described in eight ways in the venerable Bhargāṣtakaśikhākula. (with 32.54–62)
