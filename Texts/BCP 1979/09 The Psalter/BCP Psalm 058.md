# Psalm 58

← [[BCP Psalm 057]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 059]] →

*Si vere utique* · Book Two · Eleventh Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 058]]

**58:1** Do you indeed decree righteousness, you rulers? \*  
do you judge the peoples with equity? ^58-1

**58:2** No; you devise evil in your hearts, \*  
and your hands deal out violence in the land. ^58-2

**58:3** The wicked are perverse from the womb; \*  
liars go astray from their birth. ^58-3

[p. 665]

**58:4** They are as venomous as a serpent, \*  
they are like the deaf adder which stops its ears, ^58-4

**58:5** Which does not heed the voice of the charmer, \*  
no matter how skillful his charming. ^58-5

**58:6** O God, break their teeth in their mouths; \*  
pull the fangs of the young lions, O LORD. ^58-6

**58:7** Let them vanish like water that runs off; \*  
let them wither like trodden grass. ^58-7

**58:8** Let them be like the snail that melts away, \*  
like a stillborn child that never sees the sun. ^58-8

**58:9** Before they bear fruit, let them be cut down like a brier; \*  
like thorns and thistles let them be swept away. ^58-9

**58:10** The righteous will be glad when they see the vengeance; \*  
they will bathe their feet in the blood of the wicked. ^58-10

**58:11** And they will say,  
"Surely, there is a reward for the righteous; \*  
surely, there is a God who rules in the earth." ^58-11
