## Mūla

धरातत्त्वाविभेदेन यः प्रकाशः प्रकाशते ।
स एव शिवनाथोऽत्र पृथिवी ब्रह्म तन्मतम् ॥ ^mula

dharātattvāvibhedena yaḥ prakāśaḥ prakāśate |
sa eva śivanātho'tra pṛthivī brahma tanmatam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 12 - Chapter Ten, part 3#^pg415|p. 396]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=415>))
>
> Now will be explained how these fifteen aspects, which have previously been described as a whole (in the Mālinīvijayottara), relate to the Earth principle as a whole. The Light which shines at one with the Earth principle is Lord Śiva. As (the Veda) declares: ‘Brahman is the earth’. The Great Lords of Mantra are those who by Śiva’s will are intent on attaining the accomplishments (siddhi) associated with the Earth Principle. (with 10.167–169)
