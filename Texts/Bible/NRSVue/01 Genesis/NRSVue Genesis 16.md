## The birth of Ishmael

**16:1** Now Sarai, Abram’s wife, bore him no children. She had an Egyptian slave whose name was Hagar, ^16-1

**16:2** and Sarai said to Abram, “You see that the LORD has prevented me from bearing children; go in to my slave; it may be that I shall obtain children by her.” And Abram listened to the voice of Sarai. ^16-2

**16:3** So, after Abram had lived ten years in the land of Canaan, Sarai, Abram’s wife, took Hagar the Egyptian, her slave, and gave her to her husband Abram as a wife. ^16-3

**16:4** He went in to Hagar, and she conceived, and when she saw that she had conceived, she looked with contempt on her mistress. ^16-4

**16:5** Then Sarai said to Abram, “May the wrong done to me be on you! I gave my slave to your embrace, and when she saw that she had conceived, she looked on me with contempt. May the LORD judge between you and me!” ^16-5

**16:6** But Abram said to Sarai, “Your slave is in your power; do to her as you please.” Then Sarai dealt harshly with her, and she ran away from her. ^16-6

**16:7** The angel of the LORD found her by a spring of water in the wilderness, the spring on the way to Shur. ^16-7

**16:8** And he said, “Hagar, slave of Sarai, where have you come from and where are you going?” She said, “I am running away from my mistress Sarai.” ^16-8

**16:9** The angel of the LORD said to her, “Return to your mistress, and submit to her.” ^16-9

**16:10** The angel of the LORD also said to her, “I will so greatly multiply your offspring that they cannot be counted for multitude.” ^16-10

**16:11** And the angel of the LORD said to her, “Now you have conceived and shall bear a son; you shall call him Ishmael,^[That is, *God hears*] for the LORD has given heed to your affliction. ^16-11

**16:12** He shall be a wild ass of a man, with his hand against everyone, and everyone’s hand against him, and he shall live at odds with all his kin.” ^16-12

**16:13** So she named the LORD who spoke to her, “You are El-roi,”^[Perhaps *God of seeing* or *God who sees*] for she said, “Have I really seen God and remained alive after seeing him?”^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^16-13

**16:14** Therefore the well was called Beer-lahai-roi;^[That is, *well of the living one who sees me*] it lies between Kadesh and Bered. ^16-14

**16:15** Hagar bore Abram a son, and Abram named his son, whom Hagar bore, Ishmael. ^16-15

**16:16** Abram was eighty-six years old when Hagar bore him^[Heb *Abram*] Ishmael. ^16-16
