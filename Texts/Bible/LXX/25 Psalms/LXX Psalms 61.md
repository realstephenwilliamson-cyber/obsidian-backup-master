# LXX Psalms 61

← [[LXX Psalms 60]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 62]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 062]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 062]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**61:1** For the end, a Psalm of David for Idithun.  ^61-1

**61:2** Shall not my soul be subjected to God? for of him is my salvation.  ^61-2

**61:3** For he is my God and my Saviour; my helper, I shall not be moved very much.[^1]  ^61-3

**61:4** How long will ye assault a man? ye are all slaughtering as with a bowed wall and a broken hedge.  ^61-4

**61:5** They only took counsel to set at nought mine honour: I ran in thirst: with their mouth they blessed, but with their heart they cursed. Pause.  ^61-5

**61:6** Nevertheless do thou, my soul, be subjected to God; for of him *is* my patient hope.  ^61-6

**61:7** For he *is* my God and my Saviour; my helper, I shall not be moved.  ^61-7

**61:8** In God *is* my salvation and my glory: *he is* the God of my help, and my hope is in God.[^2]  ^61-8

**61:9** Hope in him, all ye congregation of the people; pour out your hearts before him, for God is our helper. Pause.  ^61-9

**61:10** But the sons of men are vain; the sons of men are false, so as to be deceitful in the balances; they are all alike *formed* out of vanity.[^3]  ^61-10

**61:11** Trust not in unrighteousness, and lust not after robberies: if wealth should flow in, set not your heart upon it.  ^61-11

**61:12** God has spoken once, *and* I have heard these two things, that power is of God;  ^61-12

**61:13** and mercy is thine, O Lord; for thou wilt recompense every one according to his works.  ^61-13

[^1]: 61:3 *Or,* any more.
[^2]: 61:8 *Gr.* on.
[^3]: 61:10 *Gr.* altogether.
