## Mūla

निरंशे शिवतत्त्वे तु कथमुत्क्रान्तिसंगतिः ।
यथा धरादौ वाय्वन्ते भृग्वम्ब्वग्न्युपवासकैः ॥ ^mula

niraṃśe śivatattve tu kathamutkrāntisaṃgatiḥ |
yathā dharādau vāyvante bhṛgvambvagnyupavāsakaiḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 12 - Chapter Fourteen, and Appendices to Chapters Eleven and Twelve#^pg390|p. 368]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=390>))
>
> (The Ūrmikaulārnavatantra declares): ‘If God is omnipresent, (pray) tell (me) — where will He go once (he has) quit (the body)? And if He is not omnipresent, He would then be like (any common object such as) a jar. This yogic practice of (voluntarily) leaving (the body) is said to take place part by part (ekadeśena). How can (any such) exiting reasonably apply to Śiva, who is devoid of parts?’ (with 14.33cd–35ab)
>
> Just as the Self is conjoined to (the elements) beginning with Earth and ending with Air by (committing suicide by throwing oneself off) a precipice, (into) water (or) fire and fasting (respectively), in just the same way (the yogi’s Self is conjoined) into (elemental) Space by the path (vartanā) of quitting (the body voluntarily). (with 14.35cd–36ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* भोगोपायता चास्याः कथं स्यात् ? -- इत्याशङ्क्याह --

> यथा धरादौ वायवन्ते भृग्वम्ब्वग्न्युपवासकैः ॥
> आत्मनो योजनं व्योम्नि तद्वदुत्क्रान्तिवर्तना ।

तेन यथा भृग्वादिभिः क्रमेण धरादौ तत्त्वचतुष्टये भोगस्तथाप्यनया आकाशतत्त्वे इति । कारणगुणप्रक्रमेण हि कार्येण भवितव्यम् -- इति भावः ॥
