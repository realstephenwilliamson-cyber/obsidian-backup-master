In the preceding pages the Khecarīcakra with its three concentric mandalas and the central ādimandala has been described. We will now turn our attention to the symbolism of this fifth cakra which is mainly concerned with the Goddess’ different manifestations and functions, the Navātman and the method of meditation to be followed by the sādhaka in order to achieve identity with the Supreme.
