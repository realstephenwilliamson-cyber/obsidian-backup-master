## Mūla

हृदयाख्ये महाकुण्डे जाज्वलन् स्फीततां व्रजेत् ।
तस्य शक्तिमतः स्फीतशक्तेर्भैरवतेजसः ॥ ^mula

hṛdayākhye mahākuṇḍe jājvalan sphītatāṃ vrajet |
tasya śaktimataḥ sphītaśakterbhairavatejasaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 03 - Chapter Five, part 1#^pg40|p. 25]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=40>))
>
> The fire of the mighty Bhairava, blazing intensely in the great sacrificial hearth called the Heart, aroused by (rubbing together) the fire sticks of that meditation, swells. (with 5.22cd–23ab)
>
> He should contemplate the (three) abodes of subject, means and object of knowledge of that possessor of power, at one (with each other and the Lord, the supreme subject), His abounding energy, Bhairava’s fiery effulgence (tejas). (with 5.23cd–24ab)

*Lead-in:* नन्वेतावतैव कथमेवं स्यात् ? -- इत्याशङ्क्याह --

> तस्य शक्तिमतः स्फीतशक्तेर्भैरवतेजसः ॥
> मातृमानप्रमेयाख्यं धामाभेदेन भावयेत् ।

यतः "तस्य"स्वातन्त्र्यशालिनो भैरवात्मनः परस्य प्रकाशस्य मितप्रमात्रादिधामत्रयम् "अभेदेन भावयेत्"तत्साद्भूतमनुसंदध्यात् । येन पारिमित्यतिरस्कारेण परप्रमात्रैकात्म्यमुदियात् ॥ २३ ॥
