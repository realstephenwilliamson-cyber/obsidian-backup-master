# Psalms 4 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 03|← Psalms 3]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 05|Psalms 5 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 04|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**4:1** Unto the end, in verses. A psalm for David. ^4-1

**4:2** When I called upon him, the God of my justice heard me: when I was in distress, thou hast enlarged me. Have mercy on me: and hear my prayer. ^4-2

**4:3** O ye sons of men, how long will you be dull of heart? why do you love vanity, and seek after lying? ^4-3

**4:4** Know ye also that the Lord hath made his holy one wonderful: the Lord will hear me when I shall cry unto him. ^4-4

**4:5** Be angry, and sin not: the things you say in your hearts, be sorry for them upon your beds. ^4-5

**4:6** Offer up the sacrifice of justice, and trust in the Lord: many say, Who sheweth us good things? ^4-6

**4:7** The light of thy countenance O Lord, is signed upon us: thou hast given gladness in my heart. ^4-7

**4:8** By the fruit of their corn, their wine and oil, they are multiplied. ^4-8

**4:9** In peace in the selfsame I will sleep, and I will rest: ^4-9

**4:10** For thou, O Lord, singularly hast settled me in hope. ^4-10
