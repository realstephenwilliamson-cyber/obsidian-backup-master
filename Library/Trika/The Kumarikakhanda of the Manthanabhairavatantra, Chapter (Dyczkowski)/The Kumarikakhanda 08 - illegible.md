Benefits of the Hymn and Admonition to Secrecy 

The *Mālinīstavarāja* was brought down (to earth) by Ādinātha.<sup>151</sup> It is the great voice (*mahāvāṇī*)<sup>152</sup> (inwardly) impelled by the Command and impelled by bliss in order to graciously confer the Command<sup>153</sup> so that (one may acquire) authority. (40-41ab) 

He who reads it in front of the Transmission (*krama*), in the Rule (*samaya*) of the Transmission of the Child,<sup>154</sup> will (first achieve) accomplishment (*siddhi*) from the Transmission<sup>155</sup> and then the eight yogic powers (*guṇa*), including the capacity to become small at will (*aṇimā*) and the rest, are his.<sup>156</sup> By just uttering it (he makes) contact with the plane of bliss. (41cd-42) 

(If) one is one-pointedly attentive (to the recitation of this hymn), (at all) three times (of the day) or (just) one time (a day), a divine penetration (of energy) (*divyāveśa*) takes place by the authority (*adhikāra*) of the contemplation (*saṁkhyā*) of the seed - syllables (uttered during the recitation of this hymn).<sup>157</sup> (And one obtains) the wealth which is the grace of the Command and the Accomplishment of Speech (*vācāsiddhi*).<sup>158</sup> There can be no doubt about it! (43ab-44ab)

Thus one progressively (*krameṇa*) obtains the manifold teaching concerning mantra and (thus) achieves the condition (*gati*) within the Supreme (transcendental) Principle that is tranquil and invisible. (44cd-45ab) 

By recalling it once one attains the entire transmission (*krama*) of mantras and Vidyās, just twice, complete knowledge and thrice, the plane of the Mother.<sup>159</sup> (45cd-46ab) 

It should be kept hidden from the disciples of others, devoid of the lineage (*pāramparya*). It should not be given to the deceitful and the proud. (46cd-47ab) 

The Goddess Bursts out of the Liṅga and graces the God 

Then after the great soul, Bhairava had praised the Great Goddess in this way, the Supreme Goddess broke open the Liṅga and emerged (from it).<sup>160</sup> And she who resides in the Liṅga and whose form is the Liṅga<sup>161</sup> spoke to Siddhanātha.<sup>162</sup> (47cd-48ab) 

"O Śambhu! Out of (our) prior affection and due to (your steadfast) attitude (*bhavana*) and friendship, I am giving you the certain knowledge (*niścaya*), namely, Kula and Kaula that can (only be) grasped by means of Being (*bhāvagrāhya*)." (Thus) by mind and by speech, out of love, she taught (the teachings concerning) the great Kaula reality (*mahākaulika*). (48cd-50ab) 

When he was invited (in this way) by the goddess, he came in friendship (*mitratvamāgata*) and so this Siddhanātha is known here by the name Mitra (Friend).<sup>163</sup> (50cd-51ab) 

(The god said:) "O (goddess) Kaulikā, you who are a part of me! Here from today onwards it is you who are the master (*svāmitvaṁ tava*)." And Kaulīśa (also), who recites mantras and is the bearer of the Command of contemplation (*bhavanājñā*), was endowed with authority. (51cd-52ab) 

(This is the fourth) chapter called *Concerning the Mālinīstavarāja*. It belongs to the primordial descent into the great sacrifice of the Churning Bhairava in the tradition (*anvaya*) consisting of seventy million (verses) that has emerged on the Path of Meru which (extends) for a hundred and twenty-five thousand (verses). It has been brought (down to earth) into the first sacred seat along the path of the Seat of Knowledge (*vidyāpīṭha*) which is the ultimate purport of the Subsequent Six (Thousand Verses) of the Pure Division (*vimalabhedottara-ṣaṭka*) in the division beginning with \`Kā' in the venerable scripture of twenty-four thousand (verses) (known as) the *Svāminīmata* which is the Supreme Lord's command as uttered within the Transmission of the Mother (*avvākrama*).
