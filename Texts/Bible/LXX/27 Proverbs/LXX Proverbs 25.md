# LXX Proverbs 25

← [[LXX Proverbs 24]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Proverbs 26]] →

*Proverbs · [[LXX]]*

**25:1** These are the miscellaneous instructions of Solomon, which the friends of Ezekias king of Judea copied out.[^1]  ^25-1

**25:2** The glory of God conceals a matter: but the glory of a king honours business.  ^25-2

**25:3** Heaven is high, and earth is deep, and a king's heart is unsearchable.  ^25-3

**25:4** Beat the drossy silver, and it shall be made entirely pure.  ^25-4

**25:5** Slay the ungodly from before the king, and his throne shall prosper in righteousness.  ^25-5

**25:6** Be not boastful in the presence of the king, and remain not in the places of princes;  ^25-6

**25:7** for *it is* better for thee that it should be said, Come up to me, than that *one* should humble thee in the presence of the prince; speak of that which thine eyes have seen.  ^25-7

**25:8** Get not suddenly into a quarrel, lest thou repent at last.  ^25-8

**25:9** Whenever thy friend shall reproach thee, retreat backward, despise *him* not;  ^25-9

**25:10** lest thy friend continue to reproach thee, so thy quarrel and enmity shall not depart, but shall be to thee like death. 10a Favour and friendship set *a man* free, which do thou keep for thyself, lest thou be made liable to reproach; but take heed to thy ways peaceably.  ^25-10

**25:11** *As* a golden apple in a necklace of sardius, so *is it* to speak a *wise* word.  ^25-11

**25:12** In an ear-ring of gold a precious sardius is also set; *so is* a wise word to an obedient ear.  ^25-12

**25:13** As a fall of snow in the time of harvest is good against heat, so a faithful messenger *refreshes* those that send him; for he helps the souls of his employers.  ^25-13

**25:14** As winds and clouds and rains are most evident *objects*, so is he that boasts of a false gift.  ^25-14

**25:15** In long-suffering is prosperity to kings, and a soft tongue breaks the bones.  ^25-15

**25:16** Having found honey, eat *only* what is enough, lest haply thou be filled, and vomit it up.  ^25-16

**25:17** Enter sparingly into thy friend's house, lest he be satiated with thy company, and hate thee.  ^25-17

**25:18** *As* a club, and a dagger, and a pointed arrow, so also is a man who bears false witness against his friend.  ^25-18

**25:19** The way of the wicked and the foot of the transgressor shall perish in an evil day.  ^25-19

**25:20** As vinegar is bad for a sore, so trouble befalling the body afflicts the heart. 20a As a moth in a garment, and a worm in wood, so the grief of a man hurts the heart.  ^25-20

**25:21** If thine enemy hunger, feed him; if he thirst, give him drink;  ^25-21

**25:22** for so doing thou shalt heap coals of fire upon his head, and the Lord shall reward thee *with* good.  ^25-22

**25:23** The north wind raises clouds; so an impudent face provokes the tongue.  ^25-23

**25:24** *It is* better to dwell on a corner of the roof, than with a railing woman in an open house.  ^25-24

**25:25** As cold water is agreeable to a thirsting soul, so is a good message from a land far off.  ^25-25

**25:26** As if one should stop a well, and corrupt a spring of water, so *is it* unseemly for a righteous man to fall before an ungodly man.  ^25-26

**25:27** *It is* not good to eat much honey; but it is right to honour venerable sayings.  ^25-27

**25:28** As a city whose walls are broken down, and which is unfortified, so is a man who does anything without counsel.  ^25-28

[^1]: 25:1 Possibly genuine, q. d. beyond doubt.
