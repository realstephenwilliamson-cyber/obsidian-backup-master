# LXX Psalms 127

← [[LXX Psalms 126]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 128]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 128]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 128]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**127:1** A Song of Degrees. Blessed are all they that fear the Lord; who walk in his ways.  ^127-1

**127:2** Thou shalt eat the labours of thy hands: blessed art thou, and it shall be well with thee.[^1]  ^127-2

**127:3** Thy wife shall be as a fruitful vine on the sides of thy house: thy children as young olive-plants round about thy table.  ^127-3

**127:4** Behold, thus shall the man be blessed that fears the Lord.  ^127-4

**127:5** May the Lord bless thee out of Sion; and mayest thou see the prosperity of Jerusalem all the days of thy life.  ^127-5

**127:6** And mayest thou see thy children's children. Peace be upon Israel.  ^127-6

[^1]: 127:2 *Alex.* fruits of thy labours.
