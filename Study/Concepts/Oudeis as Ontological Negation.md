# Oudeis as Ontological Negation

> [!abstract] Definition
> The grammatical and philosophical re-reading of the Greek pronoun *oudeis* (οὐδείς, "no one") in [[John 14#^14-6|John 14:6]] (*"No one comes to the Father except through me"*), interpreting it not as an exclusive gate keeping certain people out, but as an ontological statement that "no-one-ness" (the death of the self-contained ego) is the prerequisite for union with the Divine.

## Exegetical Move
In [[Gospel of Recognition text 2026-08-05|The Gospel of Recognition]]:
- Institutional reading: "Only Christians who profess the creed enter; non-Christians do not."
- Mystical/Ontological reading: "The entity that arrives at the Father is not a self-aggrandizing ego (*oudeis*—nobody). As long as you remain a 'somebody' claiming private possession of spirituality, you cannot enter."
- The "me" (*ego eimi*) through which one passes is not a historical human personality, but the Cosmic Logos, the non-dual ground of reality itself.
