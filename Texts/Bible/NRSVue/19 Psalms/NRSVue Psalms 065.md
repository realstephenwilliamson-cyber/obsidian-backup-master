## Thanksgiving for earth’s bounty

*To the leader. A Psalm of David. A Song.*

**65:1** Praise is due to you, O God, in Zion, and to you shall vows be performed, ^65-1

**65:2** O you who answer prayer! To you all flesh shall come. ^65-2

**65:3** When deeds of iniquity overwhelm us, you forgive our transgressions. ^65-3

**65:4** Happy are those whom you choose and bring near to live in your courts. We shall be satisfied with the goodness of your house, your holy temple. ^65-4

**65:5** By awesome deeds you answer us with deliverance, O God of our salvation; you are the hope of all the ends of the earth and of the farthest seas. ^65-5

**65:6** By your^[Gk Jerome: Heb *his*] strength you established the mountains; you are girded with might. ^65-6

**65:7** You silence the roaring of the seas, the roaring of their waves, the tumult of the peoples. ^65-7

**65:8** Those who live at earth’s farthest bounds are awed by your signs; you make the gateways of the morning and the evening shout for joy. ^65-8

**65:9** You visit the earth and water it; you greatly enrich it; the river of God is full of water; you provide the people with grain, for so you have prepared it. ^65-9

**65:10** You water its furrows abundantly, settling its ridges, softening it with showers, and blessing its growth. ^65-10

**65:11** You crown the year with your bounty; your wagon tracks overflow with richness. ^65-11

**65:12** The pastures of the wilderness overflow; the hills gird themselves with joy; ^65-12

**65:13** the meadows clothe themselves with flocks; the valleys deck themselves with grain; they shout and sing together for joy. ^65-13
