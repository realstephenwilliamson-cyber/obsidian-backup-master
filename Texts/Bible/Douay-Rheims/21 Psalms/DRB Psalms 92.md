# Psalms 92 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 91|← Psalms 91]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 93|Psalms 93 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 92|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**92:1** The Lord hath reigned, he is clothed with beauty: the Lord is clothed with strength, and hath girded himself. For he hath established the world which shall not be moved. ^92-1

**92:2** Thy throne is prepared from of old: thou art from everlasting. ^92-2

**92:3** The floods have lifted up, O Lord: the floods have lifted up their voice. The floods have lifted up their waves, ^92-3

**92:4** With the noise of many waters. Wonderful are the surges of the sea: wonderful is the Lord on high. ^92-4

**92:5** Thy testimonies are become exceedingly credible: holiness becometh thy house, O Lord, unto length of days. ^92-5
