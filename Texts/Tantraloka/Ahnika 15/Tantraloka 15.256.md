## Mūla

गुरवस्त्वाहुरित्थं यन्न्यासद्वयमुदाहृतम् ।
मुमुक्षुणा तु पादादि तत्कार्यं संहृतिक्रमात् ॥ ^mula

guravastvāhuritthaṃ yannyāsadvayamudāhṛtam |
mumukṣuṇā tu pādādi tatkāryaṃ saṃhṛtikramāt || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 05 - Chapter Fifteen, part 4#^pg172|p. 154]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=172>))
>
> The teacher (Śambhunātha) has said that he who aspires to liberation should perform in this way the two aforementioned depositions, in accord with the process of reabsorption, that is, beginning with the feet.
