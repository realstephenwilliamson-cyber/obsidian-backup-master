## Chapter 21

**21:1** For the leader. A psalm of David. ^21-1

**21:2** O LORD, the king rejoices in Your strength;  
how greatly he exults in Your victory! ^21-2

**21:3** You have granted him the desire of his heart,  
have not denied the request of his lips. *Selah*. ^21-3

**21:4** You have proffered him blessings of good things,  
have set upon his head a crown of fine gold. ^21-4

**21:5** He asked You for life; You granted it;  
a long life, everlasting. ^21-5

**21:6** Great is his glory through Your victory;  
You have endowed him with splendor and majesty. ^21-6

**21:7** You have made him blessed forever,  
gladdened him with the joy of Your presence. ^21-7

**21:8** For the king trusts in the LORD;  
Through the faithfulness of the Most High  
he will not be shaken. ^21-8

**21:9** Your hand is equal to all Your enemies;  
Your right hand overpowers Your foes. ^21-9

**21:10** You set them ablaze like a furnace  
when You show Your presence.[^1]  
The LORD in anger destroys them;  
fire consumes them. ^21-10

**21:11** You wipe their offspring from the earth,  
their issue from among men. ^21-11

**21:12** For they schemed against You;  
they laid plans,  
but could not succeed. ^21-12

**21:13** For You make them turn back[^2]  
by Your bows aimed at their face. ^21-13

**21:14** Be exalted, O LORD, through Your strength;  
we will sing and chant the praises of Your mighty deeds. ^21-14

[^1]: Or “at the time of Your anger.”
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
