# Psalms 60 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 59|← Psalms 59]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 61|Psalms 61 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 60|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**60:1** Unto the end, in hymns, for David. ^60-1

**60:2** Hear, O God, my supplication: be attentive to my prayer, ^60-2

**60:3** To thee have I cried from the ends of the earth: when my heart was in anguish, thou hast exalted me on a rock. Thou hast conducted me; ^60-3

**60:4** For thou hast been my hope; a tower of strength against the face of the enemy. ^60-4

**60:5** In thy tabernacle I shall dwell for ever: I shall be protected under the covert of thy wings. ^60-5

**60:6** For thou, my God, hast heard my prayer: thou hast given an inheritance to them that fear thy name. ^60-6

**60:7** Thou wilt add days to the days of the king: his years even to generation and generation. ^60-7

**60:8** He abideth for ever in the sight of God: his mercy and truth who shall search? ^60-8

**60:9** So will I sing a psalm to thy name for ever and ever: that I may pay my vows from day to day. ^60-9
