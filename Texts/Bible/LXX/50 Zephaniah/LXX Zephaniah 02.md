# LXX Zephaniah 02

← [[LXX Zephaniah 01]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Zephaniah 03]] →

*Zephaniah · [[LXX]]*

**2:1** Be ye gathered and closely joined together, O unchastened nation;  ^2-1

**2:2** before ye become as the flower that passes away, before the anger of the Lord come upon you, before the day of the wrath of the Lord come upon you.  ^2-2

**2:3** Seek ye the Lord, all ye meek of the earth; do judgment, and seek justice, and answer accordingly; that ye may be hid in the day of the wrath of the Lord.  ^2-3

**2:4** For Gaza shall be utterly spoiled, and Ascalon shall be destroyed; and Azotus shall be cast forth at noon-day, and Accaron shall be rooted up.  ^2-4

**2:5** Woe to them that dwell on the border of the sea, neighbours of the Cretans! the word of the Lord is against you, O Chanaan, land of the Philistines, and I will destroy you out of *your* dwelling-place.  ^2-5

**2:6** And Crete shall be a pasture of flocks, and a fold of sheep.  ^2-6

**2:7** And the sea coast shall be for the remnant of the house of Juda; they shall pasture upon them in the houses of Ascalon; they shall rest in the evening because of the children of Juda; for the Lord their God has visited them, and he will turn away their captivity.  ^2-7

**2:8** I have heard the revilings of Moab, and the insults of the children of Ammon, wherewith they have reviled my people, and magnified themselves against my coasts.  ^2-8

**2:9** Therefore, *as* I live, saith the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, Moab shall be as Sodoma, and the children of Ammon as Gomorrha; and Damascus *shall be* left as a heap of the threshing-floor, and desolate for ever: and the remnant of my people shall plunder them, and the remnant of my nation shall inherit them.  ^2-9

**2:10** This is their punishment in return for their haughtiness, because they have reproached and magnified themselves against the Lord Almighty.  ^2-10

**2:11** The Lord shall appear against them, and shall utterly destroy all the gods of the nations of the earth; and they shall worship him every one from his place, *even* all the islands of the nations.  ^2-11

**2:12** Ye Ethiopians also are the slain of my sword.  ^2-12

**2:13** And he shall stretch forth his hand against the north and destroy the Assyrian, and make Nineve a dry wilderness, *even* as a desert.  ^2-13

**2:14** And flocks, and all the wild beasts of the land, and chameleons shall feed in the midst thereof: and hedgehogs shall lodge in the ceilings thereof; and wild beasts shall cry in the breaches thereof, and ravens in her porches, whereas her loftiness was *as* a cedar.  ^2-14

**2:15** This is the scornful city that dwells securely, that says in her heart, I am, and there is no longer any *to be* after me: how is she become desolate, a habitation of wild beasts! every one that passes through her shall hiss, and shake his hands.  ^2-15
