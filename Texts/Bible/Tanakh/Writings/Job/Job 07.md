## Chapter 7

**7:1** Truly man has a term of service on earth;  
His days are like those of a hireling— ^7-1

**7:2** Like a slave who longs for [evening’s] shadows,  
Like a hireling who waits for his wage. ^7-2

**7:3** So have I been allotted months of futility;  
Nights of misery have been apportioned to me. ^7-3

**7:4** When I lie down, I think,  
“When shall I rise?”  
Night drags on,[^1]  
And I am sated with tossings till morning twilight. ^7-4

**7:5** My flesh is covered with maggots and clods of earth;  
My skin is broken and festering. ^7-5

**7:6** My days fly faster than a weaver’s shuttle,  
And come to their end without hope.[^2] ^7-6

**7:7** Consider that my life is but wind;  
I shall never see happiness again. ^7-7

**7:8** The eye that gazes on me will not see me;  
Your eye will seek me, but I shall be gone. ^7-8

**7:9** As a cloud fades away,  
So whoever goes down to Sheol does not come up; ^7-9

**7:10** He returns no more to his home;  
His place does not know him. ^7-10

**7:11** On my part, I will not speak with restraint;  
I will give voice to the anguish of my spirit;  
I will complain in the bitterness of my soul. ^7-11

**7:12** Am I the sea or the Dragon,[^3]  
That You have set a watch over me? ^7-12

**7:13** When I think, “My bed will comfort me,  
My couch will share my sorrow,” ^7-13

**7:14** You frighten me with dreams,  
And terrify me with visions, ^7-14

**7:15** Till I prefer strangulation,  
Death, to my wasted frame. ^7-15

**7:16** I am sick of it.  
I shall not live forever;  
Let me be, for my days are a breath. ^7-16

**7:17** What is man, that You make much of him,  
That You fix Your attention upon him? ^7-17

**7:18** You inspect him every morning,  
Examine him every minute. ^7-18

**7:19** Will You not look away from me for a while,  
Let me be, till I swallow my spittle? ^7-19

**7:20** If I have sinned, what have I done to You,  
Watcher of men?  
Why make of me Your target,  
And a burden to myself? ^7-20

**7:21** Why do You not pardon my transgression  
And forgive my iniquity?  
For soon I shall lie down in the dust;  
When You seek me, I shall be gone. ^7-21

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Or “when the thread runs out.”
[^3]: See note at 3.8.
