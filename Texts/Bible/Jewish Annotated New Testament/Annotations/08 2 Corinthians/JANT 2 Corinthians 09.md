### 9.1–15: Second letter concerning the collection

*[[NRSVue 2 Corinthians 09#^9-1|Text]], p. 365* ^9-1

**[[NRSVue 2 Corinthians 09#^9-1|1]]:** Ch 9 starts the appeal afresh. ^9-1-2

**[[NRSVue 2 Corinthians 09#^9-2|2]]:** *Macedonia*, compared to 8.1, now hears Paul’s boasting that *Achaia has been ready since last year*. ^9-2

**[[NRSVue 2 Corinthians 09#^9-3|3]]:** *Brothers*, also v 5, probably those mentioned in 8.18,22–23. ^9-3

**[[NRSVue 2 Corinthians 09#^9-5|5]]:** *Bountiful gift . . . voluntary gift* both translate Gk “eulogia”; see 8.1n. ^9-5

**[[NRSVue 2 Corinthians 09#^9-6|6]]:** *One who sows bountifully will also reap bountifully*, cf. [[Hosea 10#^10-12|Hos 10.12]]; the idea that people reap as they sow appears in rabbinic literature (e.g., *b. Sukk*. 49b: “He who does charity and justice is regarded as though he had filled all the world with kindness”). ^9-6

**[[NRSVue 2 Corinthians 09#^9-7|7]]:** *God loves a cheerful giver*, see [[Proverbs 22#^22-9|Prov 22.9]]. ^9-7

**[[NRSVue 2 Corinthians 09#^9-8|8]]:** *Abundance* carries with it the responsibility of sharing. *Enough* (Gk “autarkeia”), see [[1 Timothy 06#^6-6|1 Tim 6.6]] (“contentment”). The word connotes the Hellenistic philosophical ideal of self-sufficiency. In contrast, Paul, adopting an idea familiar from Judaism (see 1.8–9n.), states that God is the basis for such sufficiency. See 12.9n. ^9-8

**[[NRSVue 2 Corinthians 09#^9-9|9]]:** See [[Psalms 112#^112-9|Ps 112.9]]. ^9-9

**[[NRSVue 2 Corinthians 09#^9-10|10]]:** *Seed . . . food*, [[Isaiah 55#^55-10|Isa 55.10]]. *Harvest of your righteousness*, [[Hosea 10#^10-12|Hos 10.12]]. ^9-10

**[[NRSVue 2 Corinthians 09#^9-11|11–12]]:** Paul’s working tire-lessly on the collection may be motivated by his apocalyptic vision of Gentiles pouring into Jerusalem ([[Isaiah 60#^60-4|Isa 60.4–7]]; [[Micah 4#^4-1|Mic 4.1–2]]), rendering overflowing *thanksgiving to God*. ^9-11

**[[NRSVue 2 Corinthians 09#^9-13|13]]:** *Testing*, see 2.9n. *Grace of God*, see 8.1n. ^9-13
