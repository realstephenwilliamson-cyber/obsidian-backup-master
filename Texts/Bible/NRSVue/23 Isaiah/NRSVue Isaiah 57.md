# Isaiah 57 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Isaiah 56|← Isaiah 56]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Isaiah 58|Isaiah 58 →]]

## Israel’s futile idolatry

**57:1** The righteous perish, and no one considers why; the devout are taken away, while no one understands that it is due to evil that the righteous are taken away. ^57-1

**57:2** Those who walk uprightly enter into peace and rest on their couches. ^57-2

**57:3** But as for you, come here, you children of a sorceress, you offspring of an adulterer and a prostitute.^[Heb *an adulterer and she prostitutes herself*] ^57-3

**57:4** Whom are you mocking? Against whom do you open your mouth wide and stick out your tongue? Are you not children of transgression, the offspring of deceit— ^57-4

**57:5** you who burn with lust among the oaks, under every green tree; you who slaughter your children in the valleys, under the clefts of the rocks? ^57-5

**57:6** Among the smooth stones of the valley is your portion; it is they who are your lot; to them you have poured out a drink offering; you have brought a grain offering. Should these acts cause me to relent? ^57-6

**57:7** Upon a high and lofty mountain you have set your bed, and there you went up to offer sacrifice. ^57-7

**57:8** Behind the door and the doorpost you have set up your symbol, for in deserting me^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] you have uncovered your bed; you have gone up to it; you have made it wide; and you have made a bargain for yourself with them; you have loved their bed; you have gazed on their nakedness.^[Or *phallus*; Heb *hand*] ^57-8

**57:9** You journeyed to Molech^[Or *the king*] with oil and multiplied your perfumes; you sent your envoys far away and sent them down to Sheol. ^57-9

**57:10** You grew weary from your many wanderings, but you did not say, “It is no use!” You found your desire rekindled, and so you did not weaken. ^57-10

**57:11** Whom did you dread and fear so that you lied and did not remember me or give me a thought? Have I not kept silent and closed my eyes,^[Gk Vg: Heb *silent even for a long time*] and so you do not fear me? ^57-11

**57:12** I will announce your verdict, and the objects you made will not help you. ^57-12

**57:13** When you cry out, let your collection of idols deliver you! The wind will carry them off; a breath will take them away. But whoever takes refuge in me shall possess the land and inherit my holy mountain. ^57-13

## A promise of help and healing

**57:14** It shall be said, “Build up, build up, prepare the way; remove every obstruction from my people’s way.” ^57-14

**57:15** For thus says the high and lofty one who inhabits eternity, whose name is Holy: I dwell in the high and holy place and also with those who are contrite and humble in spirit, to revive the spirit of the humble and to revive the heart of the contrite. ^57-15

**57:16** For I will not continually accuse, nor will I always be angry, for then the spirits would grow faint before me, even the souls that I have made. ^57-16

**57:17** Because of their wicked covetousness I was angry; I struck them; I hid and was angry, but they kept turning back to their own ways. ^57-17

**57:18** I have seen their ways, but I will heal them; I will lead them and repay them with comfort, ^57-18

**57:19** creating for their mourners the fruit of the lips.^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] Peace, peace, to the far and the near, says the LORD, and I will heal them. ^57-19

**57:20** But the wicked are like the tossing sea that cannot keep still; its waters toss up mire and mud. ^57-20

**57:21** There is no peace, says my God, for the wicked. ^57-21
