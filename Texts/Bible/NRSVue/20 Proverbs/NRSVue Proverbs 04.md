# Proverbs 4 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Proverbs 03|← Proverbs 3]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Proverbs 05|Proverbs 5 →]]

## Parental advice

**4:1** Listen, children, to a father’s instruction, and be attentive, that you may gain^[Heb *know*] insight, ^4-1

**4:2** for I give you good precepts: do not forsake my teaching. ^4-2

**4:3** When I was a son with my father, tender and my mother’s favorite, ^4-3

**4:4** he taught me and said to me, “Let your heart hold fast my words; keep my commandments and live. ^4-4

**4:5** Get wisdom; get insight: do not forget nor turn away from the words of my mouth. ^4-5

**4:6** Do not forsake her, and she will keep you; love her, and she will guard you. ^4-6

**4:7** The beginning of wisdom is this: get wisdom, and whatever else you get, get insight. ^4-7

**4:8** Prize her highly, and she will exalt you; she will honor you if you embrace her. ^4-8

**4:9** She will place on your head a fair garland; she will bestow on you a beautiful crown.” ^4-9

## Admonition to keep to the right path

**4:10** Hear, my child, and accept my words, that the years of your life may be many. ^4-10

**4:11** I have taught you the way of wisdom; I have led you in the paths of uprightness. ^4-11

**4:12** When you walk, your step will not be hampered, and if you run, you will not stumble. ^4-12

**4:13** Keep hold of instruction; do not let go; guard her, for she is your life. ^4-13

**4:14** Do not enter the path of the wicked, and do not walk in the way of evildoers. ^4-14

**4:15** Avoid it; do not go on it; turn away from it and pass on. ^4-15

**4:16** For they cannot sleep unless they have done wrong; they are robbed of sleep unless they have made someone stumble. ^4-16

**4:17** For they eat the bread of wickedness and drink the wine of violence. ^4-17

**4:18** But the path of the righteous is like the light of dawn, which shines brighter and brighter until full day. ^4-18

**4:19** The way of the wicked is like deep darkness; they do not know what they stumble over. ^4-19

**4:20** My child, be attentive to my words; incline your ear to my sayings. ^4-20

**4:21** Do not let them escape from your sight; keep them within your heart. ^4-21

**4:22** For they are life to those who find them and healing to all their flesh. ^4-22

**4:23** Keep your heart with all vigilance, for from it flow the springs of life. ^4-23

**4:24** Put away from you crooked speech, and put devious talk far from you. ^4-24

**4:25** Let your eyes look directly forward and your gaze be straight before you. ^4-25

**4:26** Keep straight the path of your feet, and all your ways will be sure. ^4-26

**4:27** Do not swerve to the right or to the left; turn your foot away from evil. ^4-27
