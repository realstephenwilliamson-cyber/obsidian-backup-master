## The joy of living in Zion

*Of the Korahites. A Psalm. A Song.*

**87:1** On the holy mount stands the city he founded; ^87-1

**87:2** the LORD loves the gates of Zion more than all the dwellings of Jacob. ^87-2

**87:3** Glorious things are spoken of you, O city of God. *Selah* ^87-3

**87:4** Among those who know me I mention Rahab and Babylon; Philistia, too, and Tyre, with Cush—“This one was born there,” they say. ^87-4

**87:5** And of Zion it shall be said, “This one and that one were born in it,” for the Most High himself will establish it. ^87-5

**87:6** The LORD records, as he registers the peoples, “This one was born there.” *Selah* ^87-6

**87:7** Singers and dancers alike say, “All my springs are in you.” ^87-7
