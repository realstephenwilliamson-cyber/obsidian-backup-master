## Mūla

प्रभामण्डलके खे वा सुलिप्तायां च वा भुवि ।
त्रिशूलार्कवृषान्दिक्स्था मातरः क्षेत्रपं यजेत् ॥ ^mula

prabhāmaṇḍalake khe vā suliptāyāṃ ca vā bhuvi |
triśūlārkavṛṣāndiksthā mātaraḥ kṣetrapaṃ yajet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 04 - Chapter Fifteen, part 3#^pg130|p. 112]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=130>))
>
> (First of all,) one should sacrifice to the Trident, the Sun, (Śiva’s) Bull, the Mothers who reside in the directions, to the guardian of the site, and the Yoginīs, with separate Mantras (for each one), consisting of the syllable OM, the word ‘salutation’ (namah), and the name (of the being addressed). (This should be done) in the circle of light, in the sky, or on a well-smeared (piece) of ground. Or else, (in the brief form of initiation, the Mantras are replaced) by a single utterance (of a Mantra) in which (all these beings are) addressed (collectively), with the words ‘outer entourage’ (bāhyaparivāra). The Mantra (used) for worship (arcana) consists of the syllable OM (tāra), the name in the dative, and the word ‘salutation’ (namah). (with 15.181–183ab)
