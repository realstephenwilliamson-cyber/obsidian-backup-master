# LXX Psalms 08

← [[LXX Psalms 07]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 09]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 008]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 008]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**8:1** For the end, concerning the wine-presses, a Psalm of David.  ^8-1

**8:2** O Lord, our Lord, how wonderful is thy name in all the earth! for thy magnificence is exalted above the heavens.  ^8-2

**8:3** Out of the mouth of babes and sucklings hast thou perfected praise, because of thine enemies; that thou mightest put down the enemy and avenger.  ^8-3

**8:4** For I will regard the heavens, the work of thy fingers; the moon and stars, which thou hast established.  ^8-4

**8:5** What is man, that thou art mindful of him? or the son of man, that thou visitest him?  ^8-5

**8:6** Thou madest him a little less than angels, thou hast crowned him with glory and honour;  ^8-6

**8:7** and thou hast set him over the works of thy hands: thou hast put all things under his feet:  ^8-7

**8:8** sheep and all oxen, yea, and the cattle of the field;[^1]  ^8-8

**8:9** the birds of the sky, and the fish of the sea, the *creatures* passing through the paths of the sea.  ^8-9

**8:10** O Lord our Lord, how wonderful is thy name in all the earth!  ^8-10

[^1]: 8:8 *Gr.* cows.
