## Mūla

ततो रजांसि देयानि यथाशोभानुसारतः ।
सिन्दूरं राजवर्तं च खटिका च सितोत्तमा ॥ ^mula

tato rajāṃsi deyāni yathāśobhānusārataḥ |
sindūraṃ rājavartaṃ ca khaṭikā ca sitottamā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 04 - Chapter Thirty-One, part 1#^pg101|p. 83]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=101>))
>
> The best colours, each for one of the goddesses, is red lead (sindūra), (dark blue or black) lapis lazuli (rājavarta); and the whitest chalk. The Goddess Parā is (white) like the moon. The Goddess Parāparā is red. The other one is Aparā, who is the black (kālī), terrific, and fierce Yogini (candayoginī). (with 31.39cd–41ab)
