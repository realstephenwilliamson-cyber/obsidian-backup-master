# Psalms 40 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 39|← Psalms 39]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 41|Psalms 41 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 40|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**40:1** Unto the end, a psalm for David himself. ^40-1

**40:2** Blessed is he that understandeth concerning the needy and the poor: the Lord will deliver him in the evil day. ^40-2

**40:3** The Lord preserve him and give him life, and make him blessed upon the earth: and deliver him not up to the will of his enemies. ^40-3

**40:4** The Lord help him on his bed of sorrow: thou hast turned all his couch in his sickness. ^40-4

**40:5** I said: O Lord, be thou merciful to me: heal my soul, for I have sinned against thee. ^40-5

**40:6** My enemies have spoken evils against me: when shall he die and his name perish? ^40-6

**40:7** And if he came in to see me, he spoke vain things: his heart gathered together iniquity to itself. He went out and spoke to the same purpose. ^40-7

**40:8** All my enemies whispered together against me: they devised evils to me. ^40-8

**40:9** They determined against me an unjust word: shall he that sleepeth rise again no more? ^40-9

**40:10** For even the man of peace, in whom I trusted, who ate my bread, hath greatly supplanted me. ^40-10

**40:11** But thou, O Lord, have mercy on me, and raise me up again: and I will requite them. ^40-11

**40:12** By this I know, that thou hast had a good will for me: because my enemy shall not rejoice over me. ^40-12

**40:13** But thou hast upheld me by reason of my innocence: and hast established me in thy sight for ever. ^40-13

**40:14** Blessed by the Lord the God of Israel from eternity to eternity. So be it. So be it. ^40-14
