## Mūla

शासनस्थान्पुराजात्या न पश्येन्नाप्युदीरयेत् ।
न च व्यवहरेत्सर्वाञ्छिवाभेदेन केवलम् ॥ ^mula

śāsanasthānpurājātyā na paśyennāpyudīrayet |
na ca vyavaharetsarvāñchivābhedena kevalam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 10 - Chapter Fifteen, part 9#^pg354|p. 336]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=354>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 15.578cd–579ab.
>
> One should not (take) initiation and the like and go to sacred sites (āyatana) with (fickle) thoughts (vikalpa); nor should one seek to find a reason for (one’s own) laxity in practicing the stated (teaching). One should not see, refer to, or treat those who are established followers of the teachings according to their prior caste status. (Rather, one should treat them) all only as one with Śiva. (with 15.575–576)
