## Mūla

दैशिकायत्त एव स्याद्भोगे मुक्तौ च सर्वथा ।
दीक्षा च केवला ज्ञानं विनापि निजमान्तरम् ॥ ^mula

daiśikāyatta eva syādbhoge muktau ca sarvathā |
dīkṣā ca kevalā jñānaṃ vināpi nijamāntaram || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 07 - Chapter Sixteen, part 3#^pg154|p. 134]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=154>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 16.298cd–299ab.
>
> However, he who (on the contrary) is devoid of insight, Yoga and the like, is like a blind man (walking) on the road; he depends completely on the teacher, for (both) worldly benefits and liberation. (with 16.298cd–299ab)
>
> It was said before on the basis of (both) reasoning and scripture, that initiation is liberating by itself alone, even without one’s own inner knowledge. (with 16.299cd–300ab)
