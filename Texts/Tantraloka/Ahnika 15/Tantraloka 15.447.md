## Mūla

तस्य पातः शुभः प्राचीसौम्यैशाप्योर्ध्वदिग्गतः ।
अशुभोऽन्यत्र तत्रास्त्रहोमोऽप्यष्टशतं भवेत् ॥ ^mula

tasya pātaḥ śubhaḥ prācīsaumyaiśāpyordhvadiggataḥ |
aśubho'nyatra tatrāstrahomo'pyaṣṭaśataṃ bhavet || ^iast

## Translation

> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 15.449cd–450ab.
>
> It is auspicious for that (stick) to fall to the east, north, northeast, west or vertically (ārdhvadik). It is inauspicious (if it falls) elsewhere. There (in that case) also, one hundred and eight oblations should be (offered) with the Weapon.
