## The genealogy of Jesus the Messiah

**1:1** An account^[Or *A book*] of the genealogy^[Or *birth*] of Jesus the Messiah,^[Or *Jesus Christ*] the son of David, the son of Abraham. ^1-1

**1:2** Abraham was the father of Isaac, and Isaac the father of Jacob, and Jacob the father of Judah and his brothers, ^1-2

**1:3** and Judah the father of Perez and Zerah by Tamar, and Perez the father of Hezron, and Hezron the father of Aram, ^1-3

**1:4** and Aram the father of Aminadab, and Aminadab the father of Nahshon, and Nahshon the father of Salmon, ^1-4

**1:5** and Salmon the father of Boaz by Rahab, and Boaz the father of Obed by Ruth, and Obed the father of Jesse, ^1-5

**1:6** and Jesse the father of King David. And David was the father of Solomon by the wife of Uriah, ^1-6

**1:7** and Solomon the father of Rehoboam, and Rehoboam the father of Abijah, and Abijah the father of Asaph,^[Other ancient authorities read *Asa*] ^1-7

**1:8** and Asaph^[Other ancient authorities read *Asa*] the father of Jehoshaphat, and Jehoshaphat the father of Joram, and Joram the father of Uzziah, ^1-8

**1:9** and Uzziah the father of Jotham, and Jotham the father of Ahaz, and Ahaz the father of Hezekiah, ^1-9

**1:10** and Hezekiah the father of Manasseh, and Manasseh the father of Amos,^[Other ancient authorities read *Amon*] and Amos^[Other ancient authorities read *Amon*] the father of Josiah, ^1-10

**1:11** and Josiah the father of Jechoniah and his brothers, at the time of the deportation to Babylon. ^1-11

**1:12** And after the deportation to Babylon: Jechoniah was the father of Salathiel, and Salathiel the father of Zerubbabel, ^1-12

**1:13** and Zerubbabel the father of Abiud, and Abiud the father of Eliakim, and Eliakim the father of Azor, ^1-13

**1:14** and Azor the father of Zadok, and Zadok the father of Achim, and Achim the father of Eliud, ^1-14

**1:15** and Eliud the father of Eleazar, and Eleazar the father of Matthan, and Matthan the father of Jacob, ^1-15

**1:16** and Jacob the father of Joseph the husband of Mary, who bore Jesus, who is called the Messiah.^[Or *the Christ*] ^1-16

**1:17** So all the generations from Abraham to David are fourteen generations; and from David to the deportation to Babylon, fourteen generations; and from the deportation to Babylon to the Messiah,^[Or *the Christ*] fourteen generations. ^1-17

## The birth of Jesus the Messiah

**1:18** Now the birth of Jesus the Messiah^[Or *Jesus Christ*] took place in this way. When his mother Mary had been engaged to Joseph, but before they lived together, she was found to be pregnant from the Holy Spirit. ^1-18

**1:19** Her husband Joseph, being a righteous man and unwilling to expose her to public disgrace, planned to divorce her quietly. ^1-19

**1:20** But just when he had resolved to do this, an angel of the Lord appeared to him in a dream and said, “Joseph, son of David, do not be afraid to take Mary as your wife, for the child conceived in her is from the Holy Spirit. ^1-20

**1:21** She will bear a son, and you are to name him Jesus, for he will save his people from their sins.” ^1-21

**1:22** All this took place to fulfill what had been spoken by the Lord through the prophet: ^1-22

**1:23** “Look, the virgin shall become pregnant and give birth to a son, and they shall name him Emmanuel,” which means, “God is with us.” ^1-23

**1:24** When Joseph awoke from sleep, he did as the angel of the Lord commanded him; he took her as his wife ^1-24

**1:25** but had no marital relations with her until she had given birth to a son,^[Other ancient authorities read *her firstborn son*] and he named him Jesus. ^1-25
