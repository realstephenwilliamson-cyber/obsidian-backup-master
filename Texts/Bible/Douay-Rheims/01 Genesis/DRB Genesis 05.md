# Genesis 5 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Genesis 04|← Genesis 4]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Genesis 06|Genesis 6 →]]

**5:1** This is the book of the generation of Adam. In the day that God created man, he made him to the likeness of God. ^5-1

**5:2** He created them male and female; and blessed them: and called their name Adam, in the day when they were created. ^5-2

**5:3** And Adam lived a hundred and thirty years, and begot a son to his own image and likeness, and called his name Seth. ^5-3

**5:4** And the days of Adam, after he begot Seth, were eight hundred years: and he begot sons and daughters. ^5-4

**5:5** And all the time that Adam lived came to nine hundred and thirty years, and he died. ^5-5

**5:6** Seth also lived a hundred and five years, and begot Enos. ^5-6

**5:7** And Seth lived after he begot Enos, eight hundred and seven years, and begot sons and daughters. ^5-7

**5:8** And all the days of Seth were nine hundred and twelve years, and he died. ^5-8

**5:9** And Enos lived ninety years, and begot Cainan. ^5-9

**5:10** After whose birth he lived eight hundred and fifteen years, and begot sons and daughters. ^5-10

**5:11** And all the days of Enos were nine hundred and five years, and he died. ^5-11

**5:12** And Cainan lived seventy years, and begot Malaleel. ^5-12

**5:13** And Cainan lived after he begot Malaleel, eight hundred and forty years, and begot sons and daughters. ^5-13

**5:14** And all the days of Cainan were nine hundred and ten years, and he died. ^5-14

**5:15** And Malaleel lived sixty-five years, and begot Jared. ^5-15

**5:16** And Malaleel lived after he begot Jared, eight hundred and thirty years, and begot sons and daughters. ^5-16

**5:17** And all the days of Malaleel were eight hundred and ninety-five years, and he died. ^5-17

**5:18** And Jared lived a hundred and sixty-two years, and begot Henoch. ^5-18

**5:19** And Jared lived after he begot Henoch, eight hundred years, and begot sons and daughters. ^5-19

**5:20** And all the days of Jared were nine hundred and sixty-two years, and he died. ^5-20

**5:21** And Henoch lived sixty-five years, and begot Mathusala. ^5-21

**5:22** And Henoch walked with God: and lived after he begot Mathusala, three hundred years, and begot sons and daughters. ^5-22

**5:23** And all the days of Henoch were three hundred and sixty-five years. ^5-23

**5:24** And he walked with God, and was seen no more: because God took him. ^5-24

**5:25** And Mathusala lived a hundred and eighty-seven years, and begot Lamech. ^5-25

**5:26** And Mathusala lived after he begot Lamech, seven hundred and eighty-two years, and begot sons and daughters. ^5-26

**5:27** And all the days of Mathusala were nine hundred and sixty-nine years, and he died. ^5-27

**5:28** And Lamech lived a hundred and eighty-two years, and begot a son. ^5-28

**5:29** And he called his name Noe, saying: This same shall comfort us from the works and labours of our hands on the earth, which the Lord hath cursed. ^5-29

**5:30** And Lamech lived after he begot Noe, five hundred and ninety-five years, and begot sons and daughters. ^5-30

**5:31** And all the days of Lamech came to seven hundred and seventy-seven years, and he died. ^5-31

**5:32** And Noe, when he was five hundred years old, begot Sem, Cham, and Japheth. ^5-32
