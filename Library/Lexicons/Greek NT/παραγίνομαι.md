# παραγίνομαι

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · verb

**Dodson:** I appear, come, arrive at — (a) I come on the scene, appear, come, (b) with words expressing destination: I present myself at, arrive at, reach. (Strong's 3854; headword παραγίνομαι)
