# II Chronicles 27

← [[II Chronicles 26]] · [[Tanakh]] · [[II Chronicles 28]] →

Compare: [[NRSVue 2 Chronicles 27|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 27

**27:1** Jotham was twenty-five years old when he became king, and he reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem; his mother’s name was Jerushah daughter of Zadok. ^27-1

**27:2** He did what was pleasing to the LORD just as his father Uzziah had done, but he did not enter the Temple of the LORD; however, the people still acted corruptly. ^27-2

**27:3** It was he who built the Upper Gate of the House of the LORD; he also built extensively on the wall of Ophel. ^27-3

**27:4** He built towns in the hill country of Judah, and in the woods he built fortresses and towers. ^27-4

**27:5** Moreover, he fought with the king of the Ammonites and overcame them; the Ammonites gave him that year 100 talents of silver and 10,000 kor of wheat and another 10,000 of barley; that is what the Ammonites paid him, and [likewise] in the second and third years. ^27-5

**27:6** Jotham was strong because he maintained a faithful course before the LORD his God. ^27-6

**27:7** The other events of Jotham’s reign, and all his battles and his conduct, are recorded in the book of the kings of Israel and Judah. ^27-7

**27:8** He was twenty-five years old when he became king, and he reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem. ^27-8

**27:9** Jotham slept with his fathers, and was buried in the City of David; his son Ahaz succeeded him as king. ^27-9
