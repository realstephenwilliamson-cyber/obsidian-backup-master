## Mūla

एवं प्रसङ्गान्निर्णीतं प्रकृतं तु निरूप्यते ।
शिवहस्तविधिं कृत्वा तेन संप्लुष्टपाशकम् ॥ ^mula

evaṃ prasaṅgānnirṇītaṃ prakṛtaṃ tu nirūpyate |
śivahastavidhiṃ kṛtvā tena saṃpluṣṭapāśakam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 09 - Chapter Fifteen, part 8#^pg309|p. 291]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=309>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 15.474cd–475ab.
>
> (This) has thus been explained by the way. (Now we go back to) discussing the main point. After having performed the prescribed rite of Śiva’s Hand, and thereby burnt away the (disciple’s) fetters, (the teacher) should make that disciple bow (with reverence) to the large jar, the jar onto which the Weapon (PHAT) has been deposited, the fire, and his own Self, until, by means of meditation, he ultimately (attains) rest (within Śiva). After that, one should throw an offering (outside the sacrificial surface) to the ghosts and the Mothers. (with 15.472–474ab)
