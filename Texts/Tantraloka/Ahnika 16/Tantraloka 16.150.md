## Mūla

कलाचतुष्कवत्तेन तस्मिन्वाच्यं विधित्रयम् ।
एवं षड्विधमध्वानं शोध्यशिष्यतनौ पुरा ॥ ^mula

kalācatuṣkavattena tasminvācyaṃ vidhitrayam |
evaṃ ṣaḍvidhamadhvānaṃ śodhyaśiṣyatanau purā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 06 - Chapter Sixteen, part 2#^pg87|p. 67]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=87>))
>
> The four (Forces (kalā)) beginning with Cessation (nivṛtti) each occupy four, forty-six, twenty-two, and twelve (fingers’ space, respectively). The Force Beyond Quiescence (śāntyatīta) pervades (the others). The other two (procedures), intermediate and supreme, (are formed) by adding twelve (fingers’ space), and (then) twelve (more, respectively,) within the second Force. The procedure concerning the four Eggs is alluded to here (in the Mālinīvijayottara) with the word ‘etc.’ Thus, (this implies that) the three procedures within (that domain) should be said to be (formed) like (those of the) four Forces. (with 16.147cd–150ab)
>
> Having first deposited in this way the Path of six kinds that is to be purified (śodhya) onto the body of the disciple, (the teacher) should deposit onto it (the Mantra) which, because it is the main one amongst (all of them), is considered to be the purifier (with 16.150cd–151ab)
