## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 07 - Chapter Sixteen, part 3#^pg118|p. 98]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=118>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 16.227cd–228ab.
>
> There are three Parts on each of the first pairs of fingers, and four Parts on each (of the following) eight. There are eleven Parts, ten, one, one, six, twelve, five, five, two, two, one, and one finger measures, respectively. Counting them (all together,) there are eighteen. The other (remaining nineteenth) Part (is not counted separately,) because of its pervasive (presence). This is the inferior measure of the goddess (Parāparā), when only the purifying mantra is deposited. (with 16.228–229)
