## The majesty of God’s rule

**93:1** The LORD is king; he is robed in majesty; the LORD is robed; he is girded with strength. He has established the world; it shall never be moved; ^93-1

**93:2** your throne is established from of old; you are from everlasting. ^93-2

**93:3** The floods have lifted up, O LORD, the floods have lifted up their voice; the floods lift up their roaring. ^93-3

**93:4** More majestic than the thunders of mighty waters, more majestic than the waves^[Cn: Heb *majestic are the waves*] of the sea, majestic on high is the LORD! ^93-4

**93:5** Your decrees are very sure; holiness befits your house, O LORD, forevermore. ^93-5
