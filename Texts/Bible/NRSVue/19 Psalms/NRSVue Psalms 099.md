# Psalm 99 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 098|← Psalm 98]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 100|Psalm 100 →]]

## Praise to God for his holiness

**99:1** The LORD is king; let the peoples tremble! He sits enthroned upon the cherubim; let the earth quake! ^99-1

**99:2** The LORD is great in Zion; he is exalted over all the peoples. ^99-2

**99:3** Let them praise your great and awesome name. Holy is he! ^99-3

**99:4** Mighty King,^[Cn: Heb *And a king’s strength*] lover of justice, you have established equity; you have executed justice and righteousness in Jacob. ^99-4

**99:5** Extol the LORD our God; worship at his footstool. Holy is he! ^99-5

**99:6** Moses and Aaron were among his priests, Samuel also was among those who called on his name. They cried to the LORD, and he answered them. ^99-6

**99:7** He spoke to them in the pillar of cloud; they kept his decrees and the statutes that he gave them. ^99-7

**99:8** O LORD our God, you answered them; you were a forgiving God to them but an avenger of their wrongdoings. ^99-8

**99:9** Extol the LORD our God, and worship at his holy mountain, for the LORD our God is holy. ^99-9
