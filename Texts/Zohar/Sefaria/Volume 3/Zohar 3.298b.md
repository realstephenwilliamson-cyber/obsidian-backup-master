# Zohar 3.298b

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 9.16 - Commentary on Ha'azinu, continued#^z3-298b|Commentary on Ha’azinu, continued, at 3:298b]]

*Volume 3, folio 298b* · Ha'Azinu

*Passage Ha'Azinu 12:231 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 3.298a#^ha-azinu-12-231|Ha'Azinu 12:231]].*

**Ha'Azinu 12:232** Further, there is a deep mystery here, since God said that “these” things are attached to His name, and just as the Holy One, blessed be He, cannot forget His name, which is all, so He does not forget Israel, who are attached to His very name.’ R. Hiya thereupon became excited and said: ‘In truth that is the thing. Blessed be God that I reminded you, so that now I know what it was and who said it. ^ha-azinu-12-232

**Ha'Azinu 12:233** I ran four miles that day and could not find out who it was.’ R. Isaac said: ‘I heard it because I went into a cave where R. Eleazar was resting.’ ^ha-azinu-12-233

**Ha'Azinu 13:234** REMEMBER THE DAYS OF OLD, CONSIDER THE YEARS OF MANY GENERATIONS. R. Abba said: ‘The days of old” are the six Days in which God made the world; and these Days know and are acquainted with all the years of the world, and all generations up to this generation to which we ourselves belong. ^ha-azinu-13-234

**Ha'Azinu 13:235** ASK THY FATHER AND HE WILL SHOW THEE: this is the Holy One, blessed be He, who will reveal to thee the hidden depths of Wisdom, to wit, that when those six Days constructed the universe they did so only for thy sake that thou mightest come and perform the Law, as we have learnt, that God made a condition with creation that if when Israel came they would accept the Torah it should stand, and if not that it should revert to chaos. ^ha-azinu-13-235

**Ha'Azinu 13:236** Therefore God assigned other peoples to Chieftains and Overseers, but of you it is written, FOR THE LORD’S PORTION IS HIS PEOPLE, JACOB IS THE LOT OF HIS INHERITANCE, since He assigned them to no Potentate or Angel or Chieftain, but took them for his own portion. ^ha-azinu-13-236

**Ha'Azinu 13:237** Where did He find them? IN A DESERT LAND AND IN A WASTE HOWLING WILDERNESS, as it is written, “Terah the father of Abraham… and I took your father Abraham”, etc. (Joshua 24, 2, 3); and from there He guided Israel in every generation, never separating from them.’ AS AN EAGLE THAT STIRRETH UP HER NEST. ^ha-azinu-13-237

**Ha'Azinu 14:238** R. Jose said: ‘There is no creature so devoted to her young as the eagle, being as kind to them as it is cruel to others. ^ha-azinu-14-238

**Ha'Azinu 14:239** THE LORD ALONE DID LEAD HIM AND THERE WAS NO STRANGE GOD WITH HIM: He alone, as it is written, “And the Lord went before them”, etc. (Ex. 13, 21), and they were not led by an angel or any other Chieftain such as are called “strange god”.’ ^ha-azinu-14-239

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Ha'Azinu 14:240.*

**Ha'Azinu 15:241** O THAT THEY WERE WISE, THAT THEY UNDERSTOOD THIS. R. Jose said: ‘All the verses of this section are reproofs addressed by Moses to Israel, with the exception of the Holy Name which he disclosed at the beginning of his discourse.’ R. Abba said: ‘Even his reproofs are included in the holy name, since there is no word in the Torah which is not comprised in the holy name. ^ha-azinu-15-241

**Ha'Azinu 15:242** That the name of the Holy One, blessed be He, however, might be inscribed in this section, we have had to wait till here, where it is indeed written, “O that they were wise, that they understood this (*zoth)”,* meaning that if Israel knew how *zoth* takes hold of judgement to punish sinners “they would consider their latter end”, and take heed to be faithful to her. ^ha-azinu-15-242
