## Mūla

भिन्नक्रमौ निपातौ च त्यजतीति च सप्तमीम् ।
व्याचक्षते तच्च सर्वं नोपयोग्युक्तयोजने ॥ ^mula

bhinnakramau nipātau ca tyajatīti ca saptamīm |
vyācakṣate tacca sarvaṃ nopayogyuktayojane || ^iast

> All those who (explain these) verses without having understood this are (wasting their time) clutching at straws. Some say that the position of the relative pronoun (yat) and the demonstrative (tat) is reversed (vyatyayam), some (read the text) in another way, some (say that) the two particles (vā and api) are out of sequence, and that (the diction) ‘he abandons’ (functions as) a locative absolute. All this is of no use (whatsoever) to the present discussion. (with 28.364–365)

## Viveka

> [!viveka]- Viveka in English (Dyczkowski, vol. X, pp. 163–164)
> ![[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 07 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 5#Viveka on 28.364–365]]

> अज्ञात्वैतत्तु सर्वेऽपि कुशकाशावलम्बिनः ।
> यत्तदोर्व्यत्ययं केचित्केचिदन्यादृशं क्रमम् ॥
> भिन्नक्रमौ निपातौ च त्यजतीति च सप्तमीम् ।
> व्याचक्षते तच्च सर्वं नोपयोग्युक्तयोजने ॥

यत्तदोर्व्यत्ययमिति--यं यं भावमेति तं तं स्मरन्निति । अन्यादृशमिति--पाठत एव । भिन्नक्रमाविति--स्मरन्वापीति । सप्तमीति--अन्त्ये क्षणे कलेवरं त्यजति सतीति ॥ ३६५ ॥
