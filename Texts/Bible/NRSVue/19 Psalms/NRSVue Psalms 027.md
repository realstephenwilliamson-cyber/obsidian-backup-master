## Triumphant song of confidence

*Of David.*

**27:1** The LORD is my light and my salvation; whom shall I fear? The LORD is the stronghold^[Or *refuge*] of my life; of whom shall I be afraid? ^27-1

**27:2** When evildoers assail me to devour my flesh—my adversaries and foes—they shall stumble and fall. ^27-2

**27:3** Though an army encamp against me, my heart shall not fear; though war rise up against me, yet I will be confident. ^27-3

**27:4** One thing I asked of the LORD; this I seek: to live in the house of the LORD all the days of my life, to behold the beauty of the LORD, and to inquire in his temple. ^27-4

**27:5** For he will hide me in his shelter in the day of trouble; he will conceal me under the cover of his tent; he will set me high on a rock. ^27-5

**27:6** Now my head is lifted up above my enemies all around me, and I will offer in his tent sacrifices with shouts of joy; I will sing and make melody to the LORD. ^27-6

**27:7** Hear, O LORD, when I cry aloud; be gracious to me and answer me! ^27-7

**27:8** “Come,” my heart says, “seek his face!” Your face, LORD, do I seek. ^27-8

**27:9** Do not hide your face from me. Do not turn your servant away in anger, you who have been my help. Do not cast me off; do not forsake me, O God of my salvation! ^27-9

**27:10** If my father and mother forsake me, the LORD will take me up. ^27-10

**27:11** Teach me your way, O LORD, and lead me on a level path because of my enemies. ^27-11

**27:12** Do not give me up to the will of my adversaries, for false witnesses have risen against me, and they are breathing out violence. ^27-12

**27:13** I believe that I shall see the goodness of the LORD in the land of the living. ^27-13

**27:14** Wait for the LORD; be strong, and let your heart take courage; wait for the LORD! ^27-14
