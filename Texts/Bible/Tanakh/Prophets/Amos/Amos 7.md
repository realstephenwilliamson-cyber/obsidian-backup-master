## Chapter 7

**7:1** This is what my Lord GOD showed me: He was creating [a plague of] locusts at the time when the late-sown crops were beginning to sprout—the late-sown crops after the king’s reaping.[^1] ^7-1

**7:2** When it had finished devouring the herbage in the land, I said, “O Lord GOD, pray forgive. How will Jacob survive? He is so small.” ^7-2

**7:3** The LORD relented concerning this. “It shall not come to pass,” said the LORD. ^7-3

**7:4** This is what the Lord GOD showed me: Lo, my Lord GOD was summoning to contend by[^2] fire which consumed the Great Deep and was consuming the fields. ^7-4

**7:5** I said, “Oh, Lord GOD, refrain! How will Jacob survive? He is so small.” ^7-5

**7:6** The LORD relented concerning this. “That shall not come to pass, either,” said my Lord GOD. ^7-6

**7:7** This is what He showed me: He was standing on a wall checked with a plumb line[^3] and He was holding a plumb line.[^4] ^7-7

**7:8** And the LORD asked me, “What do you see, Amos?” “A plumb line,”[^5] I replied. And my Lord declared, “I am going to apply a plumb line[^6] to My people Israel; I will pardon them no more. ^7-8

**7:9** The shrines of Isaac shall be laid waste, and the sanctuaries of Israel reduced to ruins; and I will turn upon the House of Jeroboam with the sword.” ^7-9

**7:10** Amaziah, the priest of Bethel, sent this message to King Jeroboam of Israel: “Amos is conspiring against you within the House of Israel. The country cannot endure the things he is saying. ^7-10

**7:11** For Amos has said, ‘Jeroboam shall die by the sword, and Israel shall be exiled from its soil.’” ^7-11

**7:12** Amaziah also said to Amos, “Seer, off with you to the land of Judah! Earn your living[^7] there, and do your prophesying there. ^7-12

**7:13** But don’t ever prophesy again at Bethel; for it is a king’s sanctuary and a royal palace.” ^7-13

**7:14** Amos answered Amaziah: “I am not a prophet,[^8] and I am not a prophet’s disciple. I am a cattle breeder[^9] and a tender of sycamore figs. ^7-14

**7:15** But the LORD took me away from following the flock, and the LORD said to me, ‘Go, prophesy to My people Israel.’ ^7-15

**7:16** And so, hear the word of the LORD. You say I must not prophesy about the House of Israel or preach about the House of Isaac; ^7-16

**7:17** but this, I swear, is what the LORD said: Your wife shall play the harlot[^10] in the town, your sons and daughters shall fall by the sword, and your land shall be divided up with a measuring line. And you yourself shall die on unclean soil;[^11] for Israel shall be exiled from its soil.” ^7-17

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain. The king’s reaping of fodder apparently occurred near the end of the rainy season, and whatever the locust destroyed after that could not be replaced for another year.
[^2]: Emendation yields “flaming.”
[^3]: Or “destined for the pickax”; meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^4]: Or “pickax”; meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^5]: Or “pickax”; meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^6]: Or “pickax”; meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^7]: Lit. “eat bread.”
[^8]: I.e., by profession.
[^9]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain; emendation yields “sheep breeder”; cf. the next verse and 1.1.
[^10]: Emendation yields “be ravished”; cf. Lam. 5.11.
[^11]: Cf. Hos. 9.3 and note.
