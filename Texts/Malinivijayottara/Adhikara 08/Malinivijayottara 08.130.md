## Mūla

एतदेवान्यथाभूतं दुःस्वप्न इति कीर्त्यते ।
पक्वमांसाशनाभ्यङ्गगर्तादिपत नादिकम् ॥ ^mula

etad evānyathābhūtaṁ duḥsvapna[^145] iti kīrtyate |
pakvamāṁsāśanābhyaṅgagartādipata nādikam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2025 – April 2025
> p. 37 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=51>))
>
> One that is contrary to this is said to be a bad dream (*duḥsvapna*). (Examples are dreaming of) eating cooked meat, being smeared with unguents, falling into a precipice and the like. After having performed the rites of expiation taught in the Tantras[^146] (the disciple) attains the condition of a 'twice born' (brahmin).[^147] (with 8.130–131ab)

[^145]: k: *duḥkhasvapnamitīti*
