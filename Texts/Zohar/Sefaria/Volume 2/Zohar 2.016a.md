*Volume 2, folio 16a* · Shemot

*Passage Shemot 31:266 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 2.015b#^shemot-31-266|Shemot 31:266]].*

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Shemot 32:267–32:273.*

**Shemot 32:274** He said that when Israel went into exile all the souls of their progenitors gathered in the cave of Machpelah and cried: “Old man, the afflictions of thy children are terrible; they have to do the work of slaves and a heathen nation makes their lives unbearable.” ^shemot-32-274

**Shemot 32:275** Immediately the spirit of Jacob was awakened. He asked permission to go to Egypt, and he went. Then the Holy One summoned all His celestial hosts and their leaders and they all accompanied Jacob and his sons. The tribes went down to Egypt with their father when they were alive and again when they were dead. Said R. Abba: ‘Then the words were fulfilled: “As a father pitieth his children ” (Ps. 103, 13).’ ^shemot-32-275

**Shemot 33:276** R. Judah bar Shalom was once walking together with R. Abba. They arrived at a place where they decided to spend the night. After taking a meal they lay down to sleep, putting their heads on some raised ground under which was a grave. Before falling asleep they heard a voice from the grave, crying: ‘Twelve years have I been sleeping here, and only now do I wake, for I now see the image of my son.’ ^shemot-33-276
