# Psalm 148 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 147|← Psalm 147]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 149|Psalm 149 →]]

## Praise for God’s universal glory

**148:1** Praise the LORD! Praise the LORD from the heavens; praise him in the heights! ^148-1

**148:2** Praise him, all his angels; praise him, all his host! ^148-2

**148:3** Praise him, sun and moon; praise him, all you shining stars! ^148-3

**148:4** Praise him, you highest heavens and you waters above the heavens! ^148-4

**148:5** Let them praise the name of the LORD, for he commanded and they were created. ^148-5

**148:6** He established them forever and ever; he fixed their bounds, which cannot be passed.^[Or *he set a law that cannot pass away*] ^148-6

**148:7** Praise the LORD from the earth, you sea monsters and all deeps, ^148-7

**148:8** fire and hail, snow and frost, stormy wind fulfilling his command! ^148-8

**148:9** Mountains and all hills, fruit trees and all cedars! ^148-9

**148:10** Wild animals and all cattle, creeping things and flying birds! ^148-10

**148:11** Kings of the earth and all peoples, princes and all rulers of the earth! ^148-11

**148:12** Young men and women alike, old and young together! ^148-12

**148:13** Let them praise the name of the LORD, for his name alone is exalted; his glory is above earth and heaven. ^148-13

**148:14** He has raised up a horn for his people, praise for all his faithful, for the people of Israel who are close to him. Praise the LORD! ^148-14
