## Mūla

तद्विधिः श्रुतिपत्रेऽब्जे मध्ये देवं सदाशिवम् ।
ईशरुद्रहरिब्रह्मचतुष्कं प्राग्दिगादितः ॥ ^mula

tadvidhiḥ śrutipatre'bje madhye devaṃ sadāśivam |
īśarudraharibrahmacatuṣkaṃ prāgdigāditaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 15 - Chapter Twenty-Four#^pg371|p. 351]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=371>))
>
> The procedure for that (is as follows). (The teacher should first) worship in the centre of a lotus with four petals the god Sadāśiva, and (on the petals, clockwise) beginning from the east, the group of four, namely, Iśvara, Rudra, Viṣṇu and Brahmā. (with 24.18–19)
