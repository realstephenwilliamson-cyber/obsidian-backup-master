# 1 Enoch 31 (Charles)

[[1 Enoch 030|← 1 Enoch 30]] · [[1 Enoch|index]] · [[1 Enoch 032|1 Enoch 32 →]]

**31:1** And I saw other mountains, and amongst them were ⌜groves of⌝ trees, and there flowed forth from them nectar, which is named sarara and galbanum. ^31-1

**31:2** And beyond these mountains I saw another mountain ⌜to the east of the ends of the earth⌝, 〚whereon were aloe trees〛, and all the trees were full **of stacte**, being like almond trees. ^31-2

**31:3** And when one **burnt** it, it smelt sweeter than any fragrant odour. ^31-3
