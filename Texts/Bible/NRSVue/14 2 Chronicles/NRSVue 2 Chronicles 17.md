# 2 Chronicles 17 (NRSVue)

## Jehoshaphat’s reign

**17:1** His son Jehoshaphat succeeded him and strengthened himself against Israel. ^17-1

**17:2** He placed forces in all the fortified cities of Judah and set garrisons in the land of Judah and in the cities of Ephraim that his father Asa had taken. ^17-2

**17:3** The LORD was with Jehoshaphat because he walked in the earlier ways of his father;^[Gk: Heb *his father David*] he did not seek the Baals ^17-3

**17:4** but sought the God of his father and walked in his commandments and not according to the ways of Israel. ^17-4

**17:5** Therefore the LORD established the kingdom in his hand. All Judah brought tribute to Jehoshaphat, and he had great riches and honor. ^17-5

**17:6** His heart was courageous in the ways of the LORD; furthermore, he removed the high places and the sacred poles^[Or *Asherahs*] from Judah. ^17-6

**17:7** In the third year of his reign he sent his officials Ben-hail, Obadiah, Zechariah, Nethanel, and Micaiah to teach in the cities of Judah. ^17-7

**17:8** With them were the Levites Shemaiah, Nethaniah, Zebadiah, Asahel, Shemiramoth, Jehonathan, Adonijah, Tobijah, and Tob-adonijah; and with these Levites the priests Elishama and Jehoram. ^17-8

**17:9** They taught in Judah, having the book of the law of the LORD with them; they went around through all the cities of Judah and taught among the people. ^17-9

**17:10** The fear of the LORD fell on all the kingdoms of the lands around Judah, and they did not make war against Jehoshaphat. ^17-10

**17:11** Some of the Philistines brought Jehoshaphat presents and silver for tribute, and the Arabs also brought him seven thousand seven hundred rams and seven thousand seven hundred male goats. ^17-11

**17:12** Jehoshaphat grew steadily greater. He built fortresses and storage cities in Judah. ^17-12

**17:13** He carried out great works in the cities of Judah. He had soldiers, mighty warriors, in Jerusalem. ^17-13

**17:14** This was the muster of them by ancestral houses: Of Judah, the commanders of the thousands: Adnah the commander, with three hundred thousand mighty warriors, ^17-14

**17:15** and next to him Jehohanan the commander, with two hundred eighty thousand, ^17-15

**17:16** and next to him Amasiah son of Zichri, a volunteer for the service of the LORD, with two hundred thousand mighty warriors. ^17-16

**17:17** Of Benjamin: Eliada, a mighty warrior, with two hundred thousand armed with bow and shield, ^17-17

**17:18** and next to him Jehozabad with one hundred eighty thousand armed for war. ^17-18

**17:19** These were in the service of the king, besides those whom the king had placed in the fortified cities throughout all Judah. ^17-19
