# LXX Psalms 54

← [[LXX Psalms 53]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 55]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 055]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 055]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**54:1** For the end, among Hymns of instruction by David.  ^54-1

**54:2** Hearken, O God, to my prayer; and disregard not my supplication.  ^54-2

**54:3** Attend to me, and hearken to me: I was grieved in my meditation, and troubled;  ^54-3

**54:4** because of the voice of the enemy, and because of the oppression of the sinner: for they brought iniquity against me, and were wrathfully angry with me.  ^54-4

**54:5** My heart was troubled within me; and the fear of death fell upon me.  ^54-5

**54:6** Fear and trembling came upon me, and darkness covered me.  ^54-6

**54:7** And I said, O that I had wings as *those* of a dove! then would I flee away, and be at rest.  ^54-7

**54:8** Lo! I have fled afar off, and lodged in the wilderness. Pause.  ^54-8

**54:9** I waited for him that should deliver me from distress of spirit and tempest.  ^54-9

**54:10** Destroy, O Lord, and divide their tongues: for I have seen iniquity and gainsaying in the city.  ^54-10

**54:11** Day and night he shall go round about it upon its walls: iniquity and sorrow and unrighteousness *are* in the midst of it;[^1]  ^54-11

**54:12** and usury and craft have not failed from its streets.  ^54-12

**54:13** For if an enemy had reproached me, I would have endured it; and if one who hated *me* had spoken vauntingly against me, I would have hid myself from him.  ^54-13

**54:14** But thou, O man likeminded, my guide, and my acquaintance,  ^54-14

**54:15** who in companionship with me sweetened *our* food: we walked in the house of God in concord.  ^54-15

**54:16** Let death come upon them, and let them go down alive into Hades, for iniquity is in their dwellings, in the midst of them.  ^54-16

**54:17** I cried to God, and the Lord hearkened to me.  ^54-17

**54:18** Evening, and morning, and at noon I will declare and make known *my wants*: and he shall hear my voice.  ^54-18

**54:19** He shall deliver my soul in peace from them that draw nigh to me: for they were with me in many *cases*.  ^54-19

**54:20** God shall hear, and bring them low, *even* he that has existed from eternity. Pause. For they suffer no reverse, and *therefore* they have not feared God.[^2]  ^54-20

**54:21** He has reached forth his hand for retribution; they have profaned his covenant.  ^54-21

**54:22** They were scattered at the anger of his countenance, and his heart drew nigh them. His words were smoother than oil, yet are they darts.[^3]  ^54-22

**54:23** Cast thy care upon the Lord, and he shall sustain thee; he shall never suffer the righteous to be moved.[^4]  ^54-23

**54:24** But thou, O God, shalt bring them down to the pit of destruction; bloody and crafty men shall not live out half their days; but I will hope in thee, O Lord.  ^54-24

[^1]: 54:11 *Or,* it, *sc.* iniquity.
[^2]: 54:20 *Or,* they have nothing to give in exchange, *q. d.* to redeem their souls. Compare Ps. 48. 7,8; Mark 8. 37.
[^3]: 54:22 Compare *Heb.* and *A. V.*
[^4]: 54:23 *Or,* nourish.
