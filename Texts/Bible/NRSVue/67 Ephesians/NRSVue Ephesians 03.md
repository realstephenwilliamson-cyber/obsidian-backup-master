## Paul’s ministry to the gentiles

**3:1** This is the reason that I, Paul, am a prisoner for^[Or *of*] Christ Jesus^[Other ancient authorities lack *Jesus*] for the sake of you gentiles, ^3-1

**3:2** for surely^[Or *if indeed*] you have already heard of the commission of God’s grace that was given me for you ^3-2

**3:3** and how the mystery was made known to me by revelation, as I wrote above in a few words,^[Or *wrote before in brief*] ^3-3

**3:4** a reading of which will enable you to perceive my understanding of the mystery of Christ. ^3-4

**3:5** In former generations this mystery^[Gk *it*] was not made known to humankind, as it has now been revealed to his holy apostles and prophets by the Spirit: ^3-5

**3:6** that is, the gentiles have become fellow heirs, members of the same body, and sharers in the promise in Christ Jesus through the gospel. ^3-6

**3:7** Of this gospel I have become a servant according to the gift of God’s grace that was given me by the working of his power. ^3-7

**3:8** Although I am the very least of all the saints, this grace was given to me to bring to the gentiles the news of the boundless riches of Christ ^3-8

**3:9** and to make everyone see^[Other ancient authorities read *to bring to light*] what is the plan of the mystery hidden for ages in^[Or *by*] God, who created all things, ^3-9

**3:10** so that through the church the wisdom of God in its rich variety might now be made known to the rulers and authorities in the heavenly places.^[Gk *heavenlies*] ^3-10

**3:11** This was in accordance with the eternal purpose that he has carried out in Christ Jesus our Lord, ^3-11

**3:12** in whom we have access in boldness and confidence through faith in him.^[Or *the faith of him*] ^3-12

**3:13** I pray, therefore, that you^[Or *I*] may not lose heart over my sufferings for you; they are your glory. ^3-13

## Prayer for the readers

**3:14** For this reason I bow my knees before the Father,^[Other ancient authorities add *of our Lord Jesus Christ*] ^3-14

**3:15** from whom every family^[Gk *fatherhood*] in heaven and on earth takes its name. ^3-15

**3:16** I pray that, according to the riches of his glory, he may grant that you may be strengthened in your inner being with power through his Spirit ^3-16

**3:17** and that Christ may dwell in your hearts through faith, as you are being rooted and grounded in love. ^3-17

**3:18** I pray that you may have the power to comprehend, with all the saints, what is the breadth and length and height and depth ^3-18

**3:19** and to know the love of Christ that surpasses knowledge, so that you may be filled with all the fullness of God. ^3-19

**3:20** Now to him who by the power at work within us is able to accomplish abundantly far more than all we can ask or imagine, ^3-20

**3:21** to him be glory in the church and^[Other ancient authorities omit *and*] in Christ Jesus to all generations, forever and ever. Amen. ^3-21
