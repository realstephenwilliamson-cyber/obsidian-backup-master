## Chapter 135

**135:1** Hallelujah.  
Praise the name of the LORD;  
give praise, you servants of the LORD ^135-1

**135:2** who stand in the house of the LORD,  
in the courts of the house of our God. ^135-2

**135:3** Praise the LORD, for the LORD is good;  
sing hymns to His name, for it is pleasant. ^135-3

**135:4** For the LORD has chosen Jacob for Himself,  
Israel, as His treasured possession. ^135-4

**135:5** For I know that the LORD is great,  
that our Lord is greater than all gods. ^135-5

**135:6** Whatever the LORD desires He does  
in heaven and earth,  
in the seas and all the depths. ^135-6

**135:7** He makes clouds rise from the end of the earth;  
He makes lightning for the rain;  
He releases the wind from His vaults. ^135-7

**135:8** He struck down the first-born of Egypt,  
man and beast alike; ^135-8

**135:9** He sent signs and portents against[^1] Egypt,  
against Pharaoh and all his servants; ^135-9

**135:10** He struck down many nations  
and slew numerous kings— ^135-10

**135:11** Sihon, king of the Amorites,  
Og, king of Bashan,  
and all the royalty of Canaan— ^135-11

**135:12** and gave their lands as a heritage,  
as a heritage to His people Israel. ^135-12

**135:13** O LORD, Your name endures forever,  
Your fame, O LORD, through all generations; ^135-13

**135:14** for the LORD will champion His people,  
and obtain satisfaction for His servants. ^135-14

**135:15** [^2]The idols of the nations are silver and gold,  
the work of men’s hands. ^135-15

**135:16** They have mouths, but cannot speak;  
they have eyes, but cannot see; ^135-16

**135:17** they have ears, but cannot hear,  
nor is there breath in their mouths. ^135-17

**135:18** Those who fashion them,  
all who trust in them,  
shall become like them. ^135-18

**135:19** O house of Israel, bless the LORD;  
O house of Aaron, bless the LORD; ^135-19

**135:20** O house of Levi, bless the LORD;  
you who fear the LORD, bless the LORD. ^135-20

**135:21** Blessed is the LORD from Zion,  
He who dwells in Jerusalem.  
Hallelujah. ^135-21

[^1]: Others “against you.”
[^2]: With vv. 15–20, cf. Ps. 115.4–11.
