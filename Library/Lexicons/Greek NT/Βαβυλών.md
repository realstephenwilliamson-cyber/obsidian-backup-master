# Βαβυλών

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · noun

**Dodson:** Babylon — (a) Babylon, the ancient city on the Euphrates, to which the people of Jerusalem, etc., were transported, (b) hence allegorically of Rome, from the point of view of the Christian people. (Strong's 897; headword Βαβυλών, ῶνος, ἡ)
