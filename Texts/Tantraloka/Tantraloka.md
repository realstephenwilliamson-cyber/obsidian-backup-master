# Tantrāloka of Abhinavagupta, with the Viveka of Jayaratha (Āhnikas 1–37)

Split from Muktabodha's Devanagari e-texts (catalog M00092 and M00093), which follow the KSTS edition. 5,845 verse notes, one per numbered verse as the edition numbers them. Each has the verse in Devanagari (block `^mula`) and IAST (block `^iast`), a Translation section that links to the page of Dyczkowski's translation (see below), and Jayaratha's comment in reading order: the root lines he is commenting on are quoted where they fall. Where he comments on several verses at once, the others link to one note of the group, usually a later one (437 of the 2,105 links point back to the previous verse). In 114 of these links the note linked to has only the quoted verses, sometimes with a one-line lead-in, and no comment. Link to a verse as `[[Tantraloka 01.001]]`, or to its text as `[[Tantraloka 01.001#^mula]]`.

In October 2026 the root text was checked against the printed KSTS pages and corrected where the e-text differs: 174 verses were repaired in structure and 488 words corrected. Evident misprints in the print were not followed. [[Tantraloka verse-page map]] gives the page of every verse and lists the exceptions; the record is [[Phase 2 changes (6 Oct 2026)]].

## Translation and word analysis

- **Dyczkowski's translation:** each verse note links, under *Translation*, to the page of Mark S. G. Dyczkowski's eleven-volume translation (Library › Trika › Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation) where that verse's Sanskrit is printed. The pages were found by matching the transliterated verse lines in the scans against these notes: 5,829 of 5,845 verses were found exactly; the other 16 show the range of pages between their neighbours. Page numbers are the ones printed in these scans. The separate contents PDF follows a slightly different printing for volume I, chapter 10 and chapters 28–29, so its page numbers there don't match these scans.
- **Word analysis:** 2,726 verses from the Digital Corpus of Sanskrit (āhnikas 1–9, 11–12, 16–21 and 26) have a word-by-word note (lemma, grammar). The link is just under the IAST text. Index: [[Tantraloka words (DCS)]].

## Check these

The e-text is uneven, so the split relies on its layout and numbering. Worth checking against the printed KSTS volumes:

- **Numbers the e-text skips:** 1.76. Those verses are not missing from the text; the numbering jumps.
- **Misprinted numbers corrected:** 6.193 (printed as 6-93), 6.234 (printed as 6-34), 10.98 (printed as 10-18), 10.151 (printed as 10-159), 16.228 (printed as 16-28).
- **Verses the transcription ran into Jayaratha's prose** (30), separated by rule, so the boundary may be off by a word: 1.38, 1.185, 1.221, 1.223, 1.250, 1.277, 1.330, 3.93, 3.105, 3.186, 3.257, 4.46, 6.116, 7.33, 7.71, 8.56, 8.255, 9.201, 10.212, 11.62, 13.283, 14.31, 15.46, 15.113, 15.162, 16.231, 28.287, 28.371, 29.219, 30.96.
- **Numbers the e-text uses twice:** 1.136, 8.399, 10.131, 13.281. The second line printed with that number is kept in the commentary, where it falls.
- **Repeated passage.** Āhnika 3: the e-text repeats verses 84–96 with their commentary; the repeat is kept inside the commentary of 3.96.
- **Verses with an unusual number of lines** (152, recounted 15 Oct 2026): mostly long metres, where a line may sit with the neighbouring verse. Their notes carry `check: line count` in the frontmatter, so a search for `[check: line count]` lists them.
- Chapters begin with the second half of a verse begun at the end of the previous chapter; it is filed with the verse the edition numbers it under, or under the chapter's closing.
