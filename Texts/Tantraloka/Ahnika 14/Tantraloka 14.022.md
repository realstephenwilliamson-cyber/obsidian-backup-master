## Mūla

तिरोभूतः परेतासुरपि बन्धुसुहृद्गुरून् ।
आलम्ब्य शक्तिपातेन दीक्षाद्यैरनुगृह्यते ॥ ^mula

tirobhūtaḥ paretāsurapi bandhusuhṛdgurūn |
ālambya śaktipātena dīkṣādyairanugṛhyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 12 - Chapter Fourteen, and Appendices to Chapters Eleven and Twelve#^pg378|p. 356]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=378>))
>
> (In the second case,) one who is obscured, even if dead (paretāsu), may be graced by a descent of the power (of grace), by means of initiation and the like, with the assistance of his relatives, friends and teachers.
