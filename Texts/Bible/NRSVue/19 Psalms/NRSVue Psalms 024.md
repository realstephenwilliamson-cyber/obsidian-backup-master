# Psalm 24 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 023|← Psalm 23]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 025|Psalm 25 →]]

## Entrance into the temple

*Of David. A Psalm.*

**24:1** The earth is the LORD’s and all that is in it, the world, and those who live in it, ^24-1

**24:2** for he has founded it on the seas and established it on the rivers. ^24-2

**24:3** Who shall ascend the hill of the LORD? And who shall stand in his holy place? ^24-3

**24:4** Those who have clean hands and pure hearts, who do not lift up their souls to what is false and do not swear deceitfully. ^24-4

**24:5** They will receive blessing from the LORD and vindication from the God of their salvation. ^24-5

**24:6** Such is the company of those who seek him, who seek the face of the God of Jacob.^[Gk Syr: Heb *your face, O Jacob*] *Selah* ^24-6

**24:7** Lift up your heads, O gates! and be lifted up, O ancient doors, that the King of glory may come in! ^24-7

**24:8** Who is the King of glory? The LORD, strong and mighty, the LORD, mighty in battle. ^24-8

**24:9** Lift up your heads, O gates! and be lifted up, O ancient doors, that the King of glory may come in! ^24-9

**24:10** Who is this King of glory? The LORD of hosts, he is the King of glory. *Selah* ^24-10
