## Mūla

कियतोऽपि तदत्यन्तं योग्यं यागेऽत्र जीववत् ।
अनेन नययोगेन यदासत्तिविदूरते ॥ ^mula

kiyato'pi tadatyantaṃ yogyaṃ yāge'tra jīvavat |
anena nayayogena yadāsattividūrate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 04 - Chapter Fifteen, part 3#^pg121|p. 103]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=121>))
>
> Therefore, whatever is close to consciousness here (in this world) because it is graced by consciousness, even if slightly, it is completely fit (to be an offering) here in this sacrifice, as is (for example) a living being. (with 15.164cd–165ab)
>
> On the basis of this principle, something is indicated as being suitable or unsuitable (to be offered in the) sacrifice to the degree in which it comes closer or becomes more distant from consciousness. (with 15.165cd–166ab)
