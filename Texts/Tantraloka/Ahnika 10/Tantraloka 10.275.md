## Mūla

प्रसंख्याता प्रचयतस्तेनेयं प्रचयो मता ।
नैतस्यामपरा तुर्यदशा सम्भाव्यते किल ॥ ^mula

prasaṃkhyātā pracayatasteneyaṃ pracayo matā |
naitasyāmaparā turyadaśā sambhāvyate kila || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 14 - Chapter Ten, part 5#^pg489|p. 470]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=489>))
>
> Free of the (transcendental) detachment of the subject in deep sleep (rūpaka), this, (the Fourth state), tends towards fullness (pārnatonmukhī). Yogis (call) contemplative union (samāpatti) within it ‘Beyond Form’ (Rūpātīta) (which is the conscious subject). Tending towards fullness as it is, the man who meditates with intuitive insight (prasaṁikhyātr), at one with it, sees the universe gathered (within it into a single whole) (pracayatah). Thus this, (the Fourth State) is called ‘Accumulation’ (pracaya). (with 10.273cd–275ab)
>
> No second Fourth state (i.e. Fourth-fourth) is possible in this (state). Consciousness (saṁvid) is not objectively perceptible (vedyā). It only manifests as the conscious sentience (vittva) (of the perceiver). (with 10.275cd–276ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* नन्वस्यामपि परस्परसाङ्कर्याज्जाग्रदादिवच्चातूरूप्यं किं संभवेन्न वा ? -- इत्याशङ्क्याह --

> नैतस्यामपरा तुर्यदशा सम्भाव्यते किल ॥
> संविन्न किल वेद्या सा वित्त्वेनैव हि भासते ।

तुर्यं हि परा संवित्, सा च वेदित्रेकस्वभावेति वेद्यदशासंस्पर्शोऽप्यस्या न स्यात्तत्कथमियं वेद्यवेदकोभयरूपतामश्नुवीत येनोभयदशाधिशायि तुर्यतुर्यमपि स्यात् ॥ २७५ ॥
