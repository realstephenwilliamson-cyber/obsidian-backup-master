# Psalm 133

← [[BCP Psalm 132]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 134]] →

*Ecce, quam bonum!* · Book Five · Twenty-eighth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 133]]

**133:1** Oh, how good and pleasant it is, \*  
when brethren live together in unity! ^133-1

**133:2** It is like fine oil upon the head \*  
that runs down upon the beard, ^133-2

**133:3** Upon the beard of Aaron, \*  
and runs down upon the collar of his robe. ^133-3

**133:4** It is like the dew of Hermon \*  
that falls upon the hills of Zion. ^133-4

**133:5** For there the LORD has ordained the blessing: \*  
life for evermore. ^133-5
