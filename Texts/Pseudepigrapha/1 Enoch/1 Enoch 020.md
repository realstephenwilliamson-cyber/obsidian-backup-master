## XX. Names and Functions of the Seven Archangels.

**20:1** And these are the names of the holy angels who watch. ^20-1

**20:2** Uriel, one of the holy angels, who is over the world and over Tartarus. ^20-2

**20:3** Raphael, one of the holy angels, who is over the spirits of men. ^20-3

**20:4** Raguel, one of the holy angels who †takes vengeance on† the world of the luminaries. ^20-4

**20:5** Michael, one of the holy angels, to wit, he that is set over the best part of mankind 〚and〛 over chaos. ^20-5

**20:6** Saraqâêl, one of the holy angels, who is set over the spirits, who sin in the spirit. ^20-6

**20:7** Gabriel, one of the holy angels, who is over Paradise and the serpents and the Cherubim. ^20-7

**20:8** Remiel, one of the holy angels, whom God set over those who rise. ^20-8
