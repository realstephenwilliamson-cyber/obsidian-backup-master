## Mūla

ओं औं हस्त्रयमित्येतद्विद्यामायाकलात्रये ।
अनुस्वारविसर्गौ च विद्येशेश्वरतत्त्वयोः ॥ ^mula

oṃ auṃ hastrayamityetadvidyāmāyākalātraye |
anusvāravisargau ca vidyeśeśvaratattvayoḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 02 - Chapter Thirty, part 1#^pg23|p. 5]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=23>))
>
> The three, OṀ, AUṀ and HAḤ are in the three (principles of) Knowledge, (absence of) Māyā, and (absence of) the Force (of limited agency) (kalā), (respectively). AṀ and AḤ are within the principles of Vidyeśa and Īśvara.
