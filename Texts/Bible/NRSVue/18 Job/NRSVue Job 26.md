## Job replies: God’s majesty is unsearchable

**26:1** Then Job answered: ^26-1

**26:2** “How you have helped one who has no power! How you have assisted the arm that has no strength! ^26-2

**26:3** How you have counseled one who has no wisdom and given much good advice! ^26-3

**26:4** With whose help have you uttered words, and whose spirit has come forth from you? ^26-4

**26:5** The shades below tremble, the waters and their inhabitants. ^26-5

**26:6** Sheol is naked before God, and Abaddon has no covering. ^26-6

**26:7** He stretches out Zaphon^[Or *the North*] over the void and hangs the earth upon nothing. ^26-7

**26:8** He binds up the waters in his thick clouds, and the cloud is not torn open by them. ^26-8

**26:9** He covers the face of the full moon and spreads over it his cloud. ^26-9

**26:10** He has described a circle on the face of the waters, at the boundary between light and darkness. ^26-10

**26:11** The pillars of heaven tremble and are astounded at his rebuke. ^26-11

**26:12** By his power he stilled the Sea; by his understanding he struck down Rahab. ^26-12

**26:13** By his wind the heavens were made fair; his hand pierced the fleeing serpent. ^26-13

**26:14** These are indeed but the outskirts of his ways, and how small a whisper do we hear of him! But the thunder of his power who can understand?” ^26-14
