# Job 21 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Job 20|← Job 20]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Job 22|Job 22 →]]

## Job replies: The wicked often go unpunished

**21:1** Then Job answered: ^21-1

**21:2** “Listen carefully to my words, and let this be your consolation. ^21-2

**21:3** Bear with me, and I will speak; then after I have spoken, mock on. ^21-3

**21:4** As for me, is my complaint addressed to mortals? Why should I not be impatient? ^21-4

**21:5** Look at me and be appalled, and lay your hand upon your mouth. ^21-5

**21:6** When I think of it I am dismayed, and shuddering seizes my flesh. ^21-6

**21:7** Why do the wicked live on, reach old age, and grow mighty in power? ^21-7

**21:8** Their children are established in their presence and their offspring before their eyes. ^21-8

**21:9** Their houses are safe from fear, and no rod of God is upon them. ^21-9

**21:10** Their bull breeds without fail; their cow calves and never miscarries. ^21-10

**21:11** They send out their little ones like a flock, and their children dance around. ^21-11

**21:12** They sing to the tambourine and the lyre and rejoice to the sound of the pipe. ^21-12

**21:13** They spend their days in prosperity, and in peace they go down to Sheol. ^21-13

**21:14** They say to God, ‘Leave us alone! We do not desire to know your ways. ^21-14

**21:15** What is the Almighty,^[Traditional rendering of Heb *Shaddai*] that we should serve him? And what profit do we get if we pray to him?’ ^21-15

**21:16** Is not their prosperity indeed their own achievement?^[Heb *in their hand*] The plans of the wicked are repugnant to me. ^21-16

**21:17** “How often is the lamp of the wicked put out? How often does calamity come upon them? How often does God^[Heb *he*] distribute pains in his anger? ^21-17

**21:18** How often are they like straw before the wind and like chaff that the storm carries away? ^21-18

**21:19** You say, ‘God stores up their iniquity for their children.’ Let it be paid back to them, so that they may know it. ^21-19

**21:20** Let their own eyes see their destruction, and let them drink of the wrath of the Almighty.^[Traditional rendering of Heb *Shaddai*] ^21-20

**21:21** For what do they care for their household after them, when the number of their months is cut off? ^21-21

**21:22** Will any teach God knowledge, seeing that he judges those who are on high? ^21-22

**21:23** One dies in full prosperity, being wholly at ease and secure, ^21-23

**21:24** his loins full of milk and the marrow of his bones moist. ^21-24

**21:25** Another dies in bitterness of soul, never having tasted of good. ^21-25

**21:26** They lie down alike in the dust, and the worms cover them. ^21-26

**21:27** “Oh, I know your thoughts and your schemes to wrong me. ^21-27

**21:28** For you say, ‘Where is the house of the prince? Where is the tent in which the wicked lived?’ ^21-28

**21:29** Have you not asked those who travel the roads, and do you not accept their testimony, ^21-29

**21:30** that the wicked are spared in the day of calamity and are rescued in the day of wrath? ^21-30

**21:31** Who declares their way to their face, and who repays them for what they have done? ^21-31

**21:32** When they are carried to the grave, a watch is kept over their tomb. ^21-32

**21:33** The clods of the valley are sweet to them; everyone will follow after, and those who went before are innumerable. ^21-33

**21:34** How then will you comfort me with empty nothings? There is nothing left of your answers but falsehood.” ^21-34
