### Col. i

**1** [And] he crowned the mountains with pro[duce and] poured down food upon them, and he satiated every living being with good fruit. «All those who do my will /will eat and be satisﬁed/», says yh[w]h ^1-1

**2** «and bless my [holy] name». «But see, they have done what is evil in my eyes», says yhwh, «and opposed God with their d[ee]ds». ^1-2

**3** And yhwh judged them according to [all] their ways, and according to the thoughts of the [evil] inclination of their heart, and he thundered against them with [his] might, [and] all ^1-3

**4** the foundations of the ea[rth sh]ook, [and wa]ter broke forth from the abysses, all the sluice-gates of the heaven were opened and all the abysse[s] overﬂowed [with] mighty waters, ^1-4

**5** and the sluice-gates of the heaven p[ou]red out rain. And he destroyed them in the ﬂood […] … them all … […] ^1-5

**6** This is why everything the[re was on] dry land was [wiped out], so that man, [animals and every] bird, every winged being, [di]ed. And the gi[ant]s did not escape ^1-6

**7** and […] and God made [… and] he placed the rainbow [in the clouds to] remember the covenant ^1-7

**8** [… and never again will] the water of the ﬂood [come] for [destruction, or will] the turmoil of the waters [be op]ened ^1-8

**9** […] /they made/ and clouds […] for water ^1-9

**10** […] … […] ^1-10
