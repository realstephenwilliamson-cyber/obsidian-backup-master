The Outer 10-Cornered Figure: Maṇipūra Chakra

aim hrīm śrīm hsaim namaḥ

aim hrīm śrīm hsklīm namaḥ

aim hrīm śrīm hssauḥ namaḥ

sarva mantrāsanāya namaḥ

In the Maṇipūra Chakra, the sounds *ha* and *sa* are joined together. Here, in the union between Shiva and Shakti, Shiva is in yoga. He is not emitting seed, but his desire is so powerful that his lingam has become vertically erect, touching his navel. That is where the Seat of Fire<sup>347</sup> is supposed to be. The Seat of Fire is the outer 10-cornered 347  The Maṇipūra Chakra.

figure, which relates to the individual (the inner 10-cornered figure, discussed next, relates to the cosmos). So *hsaim*, *hsklīm* and *hssauḥ* are all related to the individual. 

Before you proceed to the cosmic figure, you must go through this link.

Collectively, the *nyāsa* for these first four centers—Mūlādhāra, Svādhiṣṭhāna, Maṇipūra and Anāhata—is known as the “placement of the four seats” (*caturāsana nyāsa*). Remember, *nyāsa* means paying attention to your body. Holding your mind in a certain place on your body, keeping your awareness fixed there—that is called *nyāsa*.

The first seat is called *dēvī ātma āsanāya namaḥ*. Devi resides in the Mūlādhāra

Chakra, and I reside in the Mūlādhāra Chakra. I am in the security center, and Devi is likewise in the security center.

The second seat is the *śrī cakrāsanāya namaḥ*. I am in the sex center and so is

Devi in the sex center. When I am in the Svādhiṣṭhāna Chakra, Devi is in there, too. When I am looking for sensations, Devi is also looking for sensations.
