## The security of God’s people

*A Song of Ascents.*

**125:1** Those who trust in the LORD are like Mount Zion, which cannot be moved but abides forever. ^125-1

**125:2** As the mountains surround Jerusalem, so the LORD surrounds his people from this time on and forevermore. ^125-2

**125:3** For the scepter of wickedness shall not rest on the land allotted to the righteous, so that the righteous might not stretch out their hands to do wrong. ^125-3

**125:4** Do good, O LORD, to those who are good and to those who are upright in their hearts. ^125-4

**125:5** But those who turn aside to their own crooked ways, the LORD will lead away with evildoers. Peace be upon Israel! ^125-5
