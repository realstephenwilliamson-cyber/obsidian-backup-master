# Psalm 25 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 024|← Psalm 24]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 026|Psalm 26 →]]

## Prayer for guidance and for deliverance

*Of David.*

**25:1** To you, O LORD, I lift up my soul. ^25-1

**25:2** O my God, in you I trust; do not let me be put to shame; do not let my enemies exult over me. ^25-2

**25:3** Do not let those who wait for you be put to shame; let them be ashamed who are wantonly treacherous. ^25-3

**25:4** Make me to know your ways, O LORD; teach me your paths. ^25-4

**25:5** Lead me in your truth and teach me, for you are the God of my salvation; for you I wait all day long. ^25-5

**25:6** Be mindful of your mercy, O LORD, and of your steadfast love, for they have been from of old. ^25-6

**25:7** Do not remember the sins of my youth or my transgressions; according to your steadfast love remember me, for the sake of your goodness, O LORD! ^25-7

**25:8** Good and upright is the LORD; therefore he instructs sinners in the way. ^25-8

**25:9** He leads the humble in what is right and teaches the humble his way. ^25-9

**25:10** All the paths of the LORD are steadfast love and faithfulness, for those who keep his covenant and his decrees. ^25-10

**25:11** For your name’s sake, O LORD, pardon my guilt, for it is great. ^25-11

**25:12** Who are they who fear the LORD? He will teach them the way that they should choose. ^25-12

**25:13** They will abide in prosperity, and their children shall possess the land. ^25-13

**25:14** The friendship of the LORD is for those who fear him, and he makes his covenant known to them. ^25-14

**25:15** My eyes are ever toward the LORD, for he will pluck my feet out of the net. ^25-15

**25:16** Turn to me and be gracious to me, for I am lonely and afflicted. ^25-16

**25:17** Relieve the troubles of my heart, and bring me^[Or *The troubles of my heart are enlarged; bring me*] out of my distress. ^25-17

**25:18** Consider my affliction and my trouble, and forgive all my sins. ^25-18

**25:19** Consider how many are my foes and with what violent hatred they hate me. ^25-19

**25:20** O guard my life and deliver me; do not let me be put to shame, for I take refuge in you. ^25-20

**25:21** May integrity and uprightness preserve me, for I wait for you. ^25-21

**25:22** Redeem Israel, O God, out of all its troubles. ^25-22
