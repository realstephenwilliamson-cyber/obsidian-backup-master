## Chapter 4

**4:1** [^1]He made an altar of bronze 20 cubits long, 20 cubits wide, and 10 cubits high. ^4-1

**4:2** He made the sea[^2] of cast metal 10 cubits across from brim to brim, perfectly round; it was 5 cubits high, and its circumference was 30 cubits. ^4-2

**4:3** Beneath were figures of oxen set all around it, of 10 cubits, encircling the sea; the oxen were in two rows, cast in one piece with it. ^4-3

**4:4** It stood upon twelve oxen: three faced north, three faced west, three faced south, and three faced east, with the sea resting upon them; their haunches were all turned inward. ^4-4

**4:5** It was a handbreadth thick, and its brim was made like that of a cup, like the petals of a lily. It held 3,000 *bath*. ^4-5

**4:6** He made ten bronze lavers for washing; he set five on the right and five on the left; they would rinse off in them the parts of the burnt offering; but the sea served the priests for washing. ^4-6

**4:7** He made ten lampstands of gold as prescribed, and placed them in the Great Hall, five on the right and five on the left. ^4-7

**4:8** He made ten tables and placed them in the Great Hall, five on the right and five on the left. He made one hundred gold basins. ^4-8

**4:9** He built the court of the priests and the great court, and doors for the great court; he overlaid the doors with bronze. ^4-9

**4:10** He set the sea on the right side, at the southeast corner. ^4-10

**4:11** Huram made the pails, the shovels, and the basins. With that Huram completed the work he had undertaken for King Solomon in the House of God: ^4-11

**4:12** the two columns, the globes, and the two capitals on top of the columns; and the two pieces of network to cover the two globes of the capitals on top of the columns; ^4-12

**4:13** the four hundred pomegranates for the two pieces of network, two rows of pomegranates for each network, to cover the two globes of the capitals on top of the columns; ^4-13

**4:14** he made the stands and the lavers upon the stands; ^4-14

**4:15** one sea with the twelve oxen beneath it; ^4-15

**4:16** the pails, the shovels, and the bowls.[^3] And all the vessels made for King Solomon for the House of the LORD by Huram his master were of burnished bronze. ^4-16

**4:17** The king had them cast in molds dug out of the earth, in the plain of the Jordan between Succoth and Zeredah. ^4-17

**4:18** Solomon made a very large number of vessels; the weight of the bronze used could not be reckoned. ^4-18

**4:19** And Solomon made all the furnishings that were in the House of God: the altar of gold; the tables for the bread of display; ^4-19

**4:20** the lampstands and their lamps, to burn as prescribed in front of the inner Sanctuary, of solid gold; ^4-20

**4:21** and the petals, lamps, and tongs, of purest gold; ^4-21

**4:22** the snuffers, basins, ladles, and fire pans, of solid gold; and the entrance to the House: the doors of the innermost part of the House, the Holy of Holies, and the doors of the Great Hall of the House, of gold. ^4-22

[^1]: Cf. 1 Kings 7.23–50.
[^2]: I.e., a large basin.
[^3]: Or “forks.”
