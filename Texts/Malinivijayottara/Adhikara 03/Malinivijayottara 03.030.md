## Mūla

अनन्तस्यापि भेदस्य शिवशक्तेर्महात्मनः ।
कार्यभेदान्महादेवि त्रैविध्यं समुदाहृतम् ॥ ^mula

anantasyāpi bhedasya śivaśakter mahātmanaḥ |
kāryabhedān mahādevi traividhyaṁ samudāhṛtam || ^iast

> **The Three Energies Aghorā, Ghorā and Ghoratarā**
>
> Although the differentiation of Śiva's power, the great soul, is infinite, even so, O Great Goddess, it is said to be of three kinds due to its (various) diverse effects.
