## Chapter 40

**40:1** The LORD said in reply to Job. ^40-1

**40:2** Shall one who should be disciplined complain against Shaddai?[^1]  
He who arraigns God must respond. ^40-2

**40:3** Job said in reply to the LORD: ^40-3

**40:4** See, I am of small worth; what can I answer You?  
I clap my hand to my mouth. ^40-4

**40:5** I have spoken once, and will not reply;  
Twice, and will do so no more. ^40-5

**40:6** Then the LORD replied to Job out of the tempest and said: ^40-6

**40:7** Gird your loins like a man;  
I will ask, and you will inform Me. ^40-7

**40:8** Would you impugn My justice?  
Would you condemn Me that you may be right? ^40-8

**40:9** Have you an arm like God’s?  
Can you thunder with a voice like His? ^40-9

**40:10** Deck yourself now with grandeur and eminence;  
Clothe yourself in glory and majesty. ^40-10

**40:11** Scatter wide your raging anger;  
See every proud man and bring him low. ^40-11

**40:12** See every proud man and humble him,  
And bring them down where they stand. ^40-12

**40:13** Bury them all in the earth;  
Hide their faces in obscurity. ^40-13

**40:14** Then even I would praise you  
For the triumph your right hand won you. ^40-14

**40:15** Take now behemoth, whom I made as I did you;  
He eats grass, like the cattle. ^40-15

**40:16** His strength is in his loins,  
His might in the muscles of his belly. ^40-16

**40:17** He makes his tail stand up[^2] like a cedar;  
The sinews of his thighs are knit together. ^40-17

**40:18** His bones are like tubes of bronze,  
His limbs like iron rods. ^40-18

**40:19** He is the first of God’s works;  
Only his Maker can draw the sword against him. ^40-19

**40:20** The mountains yield him produce,  
Where all the beasts of the field play. ^40-20

**40:21** He lies down beneath the lotuses,  
In the cover of the swamp reeds. ^40-21

**40:22** The lotuses embower him with shade;  
The willows of the brook surround him. ^40-22

**40:23** He can restrain the river from its rushing;  
He is confident the stream[^3] will gush at his command. ^40-23

**40:24** Can he be taken by his eyes?  
Can his nose be pierced by hooks? ^40-24

**40:25** Can you draw out Leviathan by a fishhook?  
Can you press down his tongue by a rope? ^40-25

**40:26** Can you put a ring through his nose,  
Or pierce his jaw with a barb? ^40-26

**40:27** Will he plead with you at length?  
Will he speak soft words to you? ^40-27

**40:28** Will he make an agreement with you  
To be taken as your lifelong slave? ^40-28

**40:29** Will you play with him like a bird,  
And tie him down for your girls? ^40-29

**40:30** Shall traders traffic in him?[^4]  
Will he be divided up among merchants? ^40-30

**40:31** Can you fill his skin with darts  
Or his head with fish-spears? ^40-31

**40:32** Lay a hand on him,  
And you will never think of battle again. ^40-32

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Lit. “Jordan.”
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
