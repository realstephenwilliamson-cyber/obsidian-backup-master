# Job 15

← [[Job 14]] · [[Tanakh]] · [[Job 16]] →

Compare: [[NRSVue Job 15|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 15

**15:1** Eliphaz the Temanite said in reply: ^15-1

**15:2** Does a wise man answer with windy opinions,  
And fill his belly with the east wind? ^15-2

**15:3** Should he argue with useless talk,  
With words that are of no worth? ^15-3

**15:4** You subvert piety  
And restrain prayer to God. ^15-4

**15:5** Your sinfulness dictates your speech,  
So you choose crafty language. ^15-5

**15:6** Your own mouth condemns you—not I;  
Your lips testify against you. ^15-6

**15:7** Were you the first man born?  
Were you created before the hills? ^15-7

**15:8** Have you listened in on the council of God?  
Have you sole possession of wisdom? ^15-8

**15:9** What do you know that we do not know,  
Or understand that we do not? ^15-9

**15:10** Among us are gray-haired old men,  
Older by far than your father. ^15-10

**15:11** Are God’s consolations not enough for you,  
And His gentle words to you? ^15-11

**15:12** How your heart has carried you away,  
How your eyes have failed[^1] you, ^15-12

**15:13** That you could vent your anger on God,  
And let such words out of your mouth! ^15-13

**15:14** What is man that he can be cleared of guilt,  
One born of woman, that he be in the right? ^15-14

**15:15** He puts no trust in His holy ones;  
The heavens are not guiltless in His sight; ^15-15

**15:16** What then of one loathsome and foul,  
Man, who drinks wrongdoing like water! ^15-16

**15:17** I will hold forth; listen to me;  
What I have seen, I will declare— ^15-17

**15:18** That which wise men have transmitted from their fathers,  
And have not withheld, ^15-18

**15:19** To whom alone the land was given,  
No stranger passing among them: ^15-19

**15:20** The wicked man writhes in torment all his days;  
Few years are reserved for the ruthless. ^15-20

**15:21** Frightening sounds fill his ears;  
When he is at ease a robber falls upon him. ^15-21

**15:22** He is never sure he will come back from the dark;  
A sword stares him in the face. ^15-22

**15:23** He wanders about for bread—where is it?  
He knows that the day of darkness has been readied for him. ^15-23

**15:24** Troubles terrify him, anxiety overpowers him,  
Like a king expecting a siege.[^2] ^15-24

**15:25** For he has raised his arm against God  
And played the hero against the Almighty. ^15-25

**15:26** He runs at Him defiantly[^3]  
[^4]With his thickly bossed shield. ^15-26

**15:27** His face is covered with fat  
And his loins with blubber. ^15-27

**15:28** He dwells in cities doomed to ruin,  
In houses that shall not be lived in,  
That are destined to become heaps of rubble. ^15-28

**15:29** He will not be rich;  
His wealth will not endure;  
His produce shall not bend to the earth.[^5] ^15-29

**15:30** He will never get away from the darkness;  
Flames will sear his shoots;  
[^6]He will pass away by the breath of His mouth. ^15-30

**15:31** He will not be trusted;  
He will be misled by falsehood,  
And falsehood will be his recompense. ^15-31

**15:32** He will wither before his time,  
His boughs never having flourished. ^15-32

**15:33** He will drop his unripe grapes like a vine;  
He will shed his blossoms like an olive tree. ^15-33

**15:34** For the company of the impious is desolate;  
Fire consumes the tents of the briber; ^15-34

**15:35** For they have conceived mischief, given birth to evil,  
And their womb has produced deceit. ^15-35

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Lit. “with neck.”
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^5]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^6]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
