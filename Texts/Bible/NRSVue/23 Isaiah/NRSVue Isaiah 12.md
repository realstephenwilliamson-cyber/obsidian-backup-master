# Isaiah 12 (NRSVue)

## Thanksgiving and praise

**12:1** You will say on that day: “I will give thanks to you, O LORD, for though you were angry with me, your anger turned away, and you comforted me. ^12-1

**12:2** Surely God is my salvation; I will trust and will not be afraid, for the LORD^[Q ms Heb mss Gk Syr Vg Tg: MT *for Yah, theLORD*] is my strength and my might; he has become my salvation.” ^12-2

**12:3** With joy you will draw water from the wells of salvation. ^12-3

**12:4** And you will say on that day: “Give thanks to the LORD; call on his name; make known his deeds among the nations; proclaim that his name is exalted. ^12-4

**12:5** Sing praises to the LORD, for he has done gloriously; let this be known in all the earth. ^12-5

**12:6** Shout aloud and sing for joy, O royal^[Or *O inhabitant of*] Zion, for great in your midst is the Holy One of Israel.” ^12-6
