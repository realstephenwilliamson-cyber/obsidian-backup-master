## Mūla

निजानन्दे प्रमात्रंशमात्रे हृदि पुरा स्थितः ।
शून्यतामात्रविश्रान्तेर्निरानन्दं विभावयेत् ॥ ^mula

nijānande pramātraṃśamātre hṛdi purā sthitaḥ |
śūnyatāmātraviśrānternirānandaṃ vibhāvayet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 03 - Chapter Five, part 1#^pg56|p. 41]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=56>))
>
> (The yogi) is initially established in the Heart, that is, the pure subjective aspect alone (pramātraṁśa), which is the Innate Bliss (Nijānanda) of repose, in the pure emptiness (śūnyatāmātra) (of deep sleep). (1) When the exhaled (upward moving) breath (prāṇa) emerges, he should contemplate (vibhāvayet) the Bliss of Stillness (Nirānanda)
