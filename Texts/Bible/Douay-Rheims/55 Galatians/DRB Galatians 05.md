# Galatians 5 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Galatians 04|← Galatians 4]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Galatians 06|Galatians 6 →]]

Compare: [[Galatians 05|BSB and SBLGNT]]

**5:1** Stand fast, and be not held again under the yoke of bondage. ^5-1

**5:2** Behold, I Paul tell you, that if you be circumcised, Christ shall profit you nothing. ^5-2

**5:3** And I testify again to every man circumcising himself, that he is a debtor to the whole law. ^5-3

**5:4** You are made void of Christ, you who are justified in the law: you are fallen from grace. ^5-4

**5:5** For we in spirit, by faith, wait for the hope of justice. ^5-5

**5:6** For in Christ Jesus neither circumcision availeth any thing, nor uncircumcision: but faith that worketh by charity. ^5-6

**5:7** You did run well, who hath hindered you, that you should not obey the truth? ^5-7

**5:8** This persuasion is not from him that calleth you. ^5-8

**5:9** A little leaven corrupteth the whole lump. ^5-9

**5:10** I have confidence in you in the Lord: that you will not be of another mind: but he that troubleth you, shall bear the judgment, whosoever he be. ^5-10

**5:11** And I, brethren, if I yet preach circumcision, why do I yet suffer persecution? Then is the scandal of the cross made void. ^5-11

**5:12** I would they were even cut off, who trouble you. ^5-12

**5:13** For you, brethren, have been called unto liberty: only make not liberty an occasion to the flesh, but by charity of the spirit serve one another. ^5-13

**5:14** For all the law is fulfilled in one word: Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself. ^5-14

**5:15** But if you bite and devour one another; take heed you be not consumed one of another. ^5-15

**5:16** I say then, walk in the spirit, and you shall not fulfill the lusts of the flesh. ^5-16

**5:17** For the flesh lusteth against the spirit: and the spirit against the flesh; for these are contrary one to another: so that you do not the things that you would. ^5-17

**5:18** But if you are led by the spirit, you are not under the law. ^5-18

**5:19** Now the works of the flesh are manifest, which are fornication, uncleanness, immodesty, luxury, ^5-19

**5:20** Idolatry, witchcrafts, enmities, contentions, emulations, wraths, quarrels, dissensions, sects, ^5-20

**5:21** Envies, murders, drunkenness, revellings, and such like. Of the which I foretell you, as I have foretold to you, that they who do such things shall not obtain the kingdom of God. ^5-21

**5:22** But the fruit of the Spirit is, charity, joy, peace, patience, benignity, goodness, longanimity, ^5-22

**5:23** Mildness, faith, modesty, continency, chastity. Against such there is no law. ^5-23

**5:24** And they that are Christ’s, have crucified their flesh, with the vices and concupiscences. ^5-24

**5:25** If we live in the Spirit, let us also walk in the Spirit. ^5-25

**5:26** Let us not be made desirous of vain glory, provoking one another, envying on another. ^5-26
