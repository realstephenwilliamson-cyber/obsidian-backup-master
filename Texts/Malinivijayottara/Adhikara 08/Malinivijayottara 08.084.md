## Mūla

तेनापि खेचरीं बद्ध्वा त्यजत्येवं महीतलम् ।
ततोऽभिमन्त्र्य धान्यानि महास्त्रेण त्रिसप्तधा ॥ ^mula

tenāpi khecarīṁ baddhvā tyajaty evaṁ mahītalam |
tato 'bhimantrya dhānyāni mahāstreṇa trisaptadhā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2025 – April 2025
> p. 24 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=38>))
>
> (One can also) meditate on the assembly of powers (*śakticakra*) by the procedure (in which the deities) are worshipped individually (*pṛthagyāgavidhānena*).[^88] By that (means also), once formed (the gesture of) the Skyfaring (Goddess), he quits in the same way the surface of the earth.[^89] (with 8.83cd–84ab)

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2025 – April 2025
> p. 25 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=39>))
>
> **The sacrifice to the site(vāstuyāga)**
>
> Then, after having consecrated grains of wheat with the great Weapon twenty-one times, he should scatter them in all the directions, meditating on them as being like a burning fire. Having meditated on that abode (of the sacrifice) as being free of obstacles and gathered (the grains) together, he should place (them) in the northeastern quarter.[^90] (with 8.84cd–85)

[^88]: See TĀ 15/365.
[^89]: TĀ 15/365cd-366ab) (365) is an explanation of MV 8/83cd-84ab (not quoted by Jayaratha).
[^90]: This is where Gaṇeśa is worshipped. MV 8/85d is quoted in TĀv ad 15/377cd-378ab (376cd-377). See TĀ 15/369cd-370ab (369).
