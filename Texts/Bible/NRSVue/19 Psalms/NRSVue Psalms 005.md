## Trust in God for deliverance from enemies

*To the leader: for the flutes. A Psalm of David.*

**5:1** Listen to my words, O LORD; attend to my sighing. ^5-1

**5:2** Listen to the sound of my cry, my King and my God, for to you I pray. ^5-2

**5:3** O LORD, in the morning you hear my voice; in the morning I plead my case to you and watch. ^5-3

**5:4** For you are not a God who delights in wickedness; evil will not sojourn with you. ^5-4

**5:5** The boastful will not stand before your eyes; you hate all evildoers. ^5-5

**5:6** You destroy those who speak lies; the LORD abhors the bloodthirsty and deceitful. ^5-6

**5:7** But I, through the abundance of your steadfast love, will enter your house; I will bow down toward your holy temple in awe of you. ^5-7

**5:8** Lead me, O LORD, in your righteousness because of my enemies; make your way straight before me. ^5-8

**5:9** For there is no truth in their mouths; their hearts are destruction; their throats are open graves; they flatter with their tongues. ^5-9

**5:10** Make them bear their guilt, O God; let them fall by their own counsels; because of their many transgressions, cast them out, for they have rebelled against you. ^5-10

**5:11** But let all who take refuge in you rejoice; let them ever sing for joy. Spread your protection over them, so that those who love your name may exult in you. ^5-11

**5:12** For you bless the righteous, O LORD; you cover them with favor as with a shield. ^5-12
