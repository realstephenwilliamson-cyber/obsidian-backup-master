# LXX 1 Paralipomenon 08

← [[LXX 1 Paralipomenon 07]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX 1 Paralipomenon 09]] →

*1 Paralipomenon · [[LXX]]*

**8:1** Now Benjamin begot Bale his first-born, and Asbel his second *son*, Aara the third, Noa the fourth,  ^8-1

**8:2** and Rapha the fifth.  ^8-2

**8:3** And the sons of Bale were, Adir, and Gera, and Abiud,  ^8-3

**8:4** and Abessue, and Noama, and Achia,  ^8-4

**8:5** and Gera, and Sephupham, and Uram.  ^8-5

**8:6** These *were* the sons of Aod: these are the heads of families to them that dwell in Gabee, and they removed them to Machanathi:  ^8-6

**8:7** and Nooma, and Achia and Gera, he removed them, and he begot Aza, and Jachicho.  ^8-7

**8:8** And Saarin begot *children* in the plain of Moab, after that he had sent away Osin and Baada his wives.  ^8-8

**8:9** And he begot of his wife Ada, Jolab, and Sebia, and Misa, and Melchas,  ^8-9

**8:10** and Jebus, and Zabia, and Marma: these *were* heads of families.  ^8-10

**8:11** And of Osin he begot Abitol, and Alphaal.  ^8-11

**8:12** And the sons of Alphaal; Obed, Misaal, Semmer: he built Ona, and Lod, and its towns:  ^8-12

**8:13** and Beria, and Sama; these *were* heads of families among the dwellers in Elam, and they drove out the inhabitants of Geth.  ^8-13

**8:14** And his brethren *were* Sosec, and Arimoth,  ^8-14

**8:15** and Zabadia, and Ored, and Eder,  ^8-15

**8:16** and Michael, and Jespha, and Joda, the sons of Beria:  ^8-16

**8:17** and Zabadia, and Mosollam, and Azaki, and Abar,  ^8-17

**8:18** and Isamari, and Jexlias, and Jobab, the sons of Elphaal:  ^8-18

**8:19** and Jakim, and Zachri, and Zabdi,  ^8-19

**8:20** and Elionai, and Salathi,  ^8-20

**8:21** and Elieli, and Adaia, and Baraia, and Samarath, sons of Samaith:  ^8-21

**8:22** and Jesphan, and Obed, and Eliel,  ^8-22

**8:23** and Abdon, and Zechri, and Anan,  ^8-23

**8:24** and Anania, and Ambri, and Ælam, *and* Anathoth,  ^8-24

**8:25** and Jathin, and Jephadias, and Phanuel, the sons of Sosec:  ^8-25

**8:26** and Samsari, and Saarias, and Gotholia,  ^8-26

**8:27** and Jarasia, and Eria, and Zechri, son of Iroam.  ^8-27

**8:28** These *were* heads of families, chiefs according to their generations: these dwelt in Jerusalem.  ^8-28

**8:29** And the father of Gabaon dwelt in Gabaon; and his wife's name was Moacha.  ^8-29

**8:30** And her first-born son was Abdon, and Sur, and Kis, and Baal, and Nadab, and Ner,  ^8-30

**8:31** and Gedur and his brother, and Zacchur, and Makeloth.  ^8-31

**8:32** And Makeloth begot Samaa: for these dwelt in Jerusalem in the presence of their brethren with their brethren.  ^8-32

**8:33** And Ner begot Kis, and Kis begot Saul, and Saul begot Jonathan, and Melchisue, and Aminadab, and Asabal.  ^8-33

**8:34** And the son of Jonathan *was* Meribaal; and Meribaal begot Micha.  ^8-34

**8:35** And the sons of Micha; Phithon, and Melach, and Tharach, and Achaz.  ^8-35

**8:36** And Achaz begot Jada, and Jada begot Salæmath, and Asmoth, and Zambri; and Zambri begot Mæsa;  ^8-36

**8:37** and Mæsa begot Baana: Rhaphæa, *was* his son, Elasa his son, Esel his son.  ^8-37

**8:38** And Esel *had* six sons, and these *were* their names; Ezricam his first-born, and Ismael, and Saraia, and Abdia, and Anan, and Asa: all these *were* the sons of Esel.  ^8-38

**8:39** And the sons of Asel his brother; Ælam his first-born, and Jas the second, and Eliphalet the third.  ^8-39

**8:40** And the sons of Ælam were mighty men, bending the bow, and multiplying sons and grandsons, a hundred *and* fifty. All these *were* of the sons of Benjamin.  ^8-40
