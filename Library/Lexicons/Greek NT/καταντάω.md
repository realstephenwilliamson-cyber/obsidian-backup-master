# καταντάω

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · verb

**Dodson:** I come down, arrive at, reach — (a) I come down, either from high land to lower (or actually to the sea-coast), or from the high seas to the coast; hence met: I arrive at, reach (my destination), (b) of property: I come down (descend) by inheritance to an heir. (Strong's 2658; headword καταντάω)
