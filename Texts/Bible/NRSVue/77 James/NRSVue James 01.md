# James 1 (NRSVue)

— · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue James 02|James 2 →]]

Compare: [[James 01|BSB and SBLGNT]] · [[DRB James 01|Douay-Rheims]] · [[JANT James 01|JANT notes]]

## Salutation

**1:1** James, a servant of God and of the Lord Jesus Christ, To the twelve tribes in the dispersion: Greetings. ^1-1

## Faith and wisdom

**1:2** My brothers and sisters, whenever you face various trials, consider it all joy, ^1-2

**1:3** because you know that the testing of your faith produces endurance. ^1-3

**1:4** And let endurance complete its work, so that you may be complete and whole, lacking in nothing. ^1-4

**1:5** If any of you is lacking in wisdom, ask God, who gives to all generously and ungrudgingly, and it will be given you. ^1-5

**1:6** But ask in faith, never doubting, for the one who doubts is like a wave of the sea, driven and tossed by the wind. ^1-6

**1:7–8** For the doubter, being double-minded and unstable in every way, must not expect to receive anything from the Lord. ^1-7

## Poverty and riches

**1:9** Let the brother or sister of humble means boast in having a high position ^1-9

**1:10** and the rich in having been humbled, because the rich will disappear like a flower in the field. ^1-10

**1:11** For the sun rises with its scorching heat and withers the field; its flower falls, and its beauty perishes. It is the same way with the rich; in the midst of a busy life, they will wither away. ^1-11

## Trial and temptation

**1:12** Blessed is anyone who endures temptation. Such a one has stood the test and will receive the crown of life that the Lord^[Gk *he*; other ancient authorities read *God*] has promised to those who love him. ^1-12

**1:13** No one, when tempted, should say, “I am being tempted by God,” for God cannot be tempted by evil and he himself tempts no one. ^1-13

**1:14** But one is tempted by one’s own desire, being lured and enticed by it; ^1-14

**1:15** then, when desire has conceived, it engenders sin, and sin, when it is fully grown, gives birth to death. ^1-15

**1:16** Do not be deceived, my beloved brothers and sisters. ^1-16

**1:17** Every generous act of giving, with every perfect gift, is from above, coming down from the Father of lights, with whom there is no variation or shadow due to change.^[Other ancient authorities read *variation due to a shadow of turning*] ^1-17

**1:18** In fulfillment of his own purpose he gave birth to us by the word of truth, so that we would become a kind of first fruits of his creatures. ^1-18

## Hearing and doing the word

**1:19** You must understand this, my beloved brothers and sisters: let everyone be quick to listen, slow to speak, slow to anger, ^1-19

**1:20** for human anger does not produce God’s righteousness.^[Or *justice*] ^1-20

**1:21** Therefore rid yourselves of all sordidness and rank growth of wickedness, and welcome with meekness the implanted word that has the power to save your souls. ^1-21

**1:22** But be doers of the word and not merely hearers who deceive themselves. ^1-22

**1:23** For if any are hearers of the word and not doers, they are like those who look at themselves^[Gk *at the face of his birth*] in a mirror; ^1-23

**1:24** for they look at themselves and, on going away, immediately forget what they were like. ^1-24

**1:25** But those who look into the perfect law, the law of liberty, and persevere, being not hearers who forget but doers who act—they will be blessed in their doing. ^1-25

**1:26** If any think they are religious and do not bridle their tongues but deceive their hearts, their religion is worthless. ^1-26

**1:27** Religion that is pure and undefiled before God the Father is this: to care for orphans and widows in their distress and to keep oneself unstained by the world. ^1-27
