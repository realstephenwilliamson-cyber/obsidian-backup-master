## Chapter 32

**32:1** Of David. A *maskil*.[^1]  
Happy is he whose transgression is forgiven,  
whose sin is covered over. ^32-1

**32:2** Happy the man whom the LORD does not hold guilty,  
and in whose spirit there is no deceit. ^32-2

**32:3** As long as I said nothing,  
my limbs wasted away  
from my anguished roaring all day long. ^32-3

**32:4** For night and day  
Your hand lay heavy on me;  
my vigor waned  
as in the summer drought. *Selah*. ^32-4

**32:5** Then I acknowledged my sin to You;  
I did not cover up my guilt;  
I resolved, “I will confess my transgressions to the LORD,”  
and You forgave the guilt of my sin. *Selah*. ^32-5

**32:6** Therefore let every faithful man pray to You  
upon discovering [his sin],[^2]  
that the rushing mighty waters  
not overtake him. ^32-6

**32:7** You are my shelter;  
You preserve me from distress;  
You surround me with the joyous shouts of deliverance. *Selah*. ^32-7

**32:8** Let me enlighten you  
and show you which way to go;  
let me offer counsel; my eye is on you. ^32-8

**32:9** Be not like a senseless horse or mule  
whose movement must be curbed by bit and bridle;[^3]  
far be it from you![^4] ^32-9

**32:10** Many are the torments of the wicked,  
but he who trusts in the LORD  
shall be surrounded with favor. ^32-10

**32:11** Rejoice in the LORD and exult, O you righteous;  
shout for joy, all upright men! ^32-11

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain; others “in a time when You may be found.”
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain; for this rendering cf. Ibn Ezra.
