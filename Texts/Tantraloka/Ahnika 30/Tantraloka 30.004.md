## Mūla

आधारशक्तौ ह्रीं पृथ्वीप्रभृतौ तु चतुष्टये ।
क्ष्लां क्ष्वीं वं क्षमिति प्राहुः क्रमाद्वर्णचतुष्टयम् ॥ ^mula

ādhāraśaktau hrīṃ pṛthvīprabhṛtau tu catuṣṭaye |
kṣlāṃ kṣvīṃ vaṃ kṣamiti prāhuḥ kramādvarṇacatuṣṭayam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 02 - Chapter Thirty, part 1#^pg21|p. 3]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=21>))
>
> (The Mantra) HRĪM is within the power of the foundation (of the Trika Trident). They say that the group of four syllables, KṢLĀM. is within the four, (the principles) beginning with Earth, respectively. HAM is in the channel (of the Trident). (with 30.4–5a)
