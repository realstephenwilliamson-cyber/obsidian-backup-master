# Proverbs 27 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Proverbs 26|← Proverbs 26]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Proverbs 28|Proverbs 28 →]]

**27:1** Do not boast about tomorrow, for you do not know what a day may bring. ^27-1

**27:2** Let another praise you and not your own mouth, a stranger and not your own lips. ^27-2

**27:3** A stone is heavy, and sand is weighty, but a fool’s provocation is heavier than both. ^27-3

**27:4** Wrath is cruel, anger is overwhelming, but who is able to stand before jealousy? ^27-4

**27:5** Better is open rebuke than hidden love. ^27-5

**27:6** Well meant are the wounds a friend inflicts, but profuse are the kisses of an enemy. ^27-6

**27:7** The sated appetite spurns honey, but to a ravenous appetite even the bitter is sweet. ^27-7

**27:8** Like a bird that strays from its nest is one who strays from home. ^27-8

**27:9** Perfume and incense make the heart glad, but the soul is torn by trouble.^[Gk: Heb *the sweetness of a friend is better than one’s own counsel*] ^27-9

**27:10** Do not forsake your friend or the friend of your parent; do not go to the house of your kindred in the day of your calamity. Better is a neighbor who is nearby than kindred who are far away. ^27-10

**27:11** Be wise, my child, and make my heart glad, so that I may answer whoever reproaches me. ^27-11

**27:12** The clever see danger and hide, but the simple go on and suffer for it. ^27-12

**27:13** Take the garment of one who has given surety for a stranger; seize the pledge given as surety for foreigners.^[Vg and 20.16: Heb *for a foreign woman*] ^27-13

**27:14** Whoever blesses a neighbor with a loud voice, rising early in the morning, will be counted as cursing. ^27-14

**27:15** A continual dripping on a rainy day and a contentious wife are alike; ^27-15

**27:16** to restrain her is to restrain the wind or to grasp oil in the right hand.^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^27-16

**27:17** Iron sharpens iron, and one person sharpens the wits^[Heb *face*] of another. ^27-17

**27:18** Anyone who tends a fig tree will eat its fruit, and anyone who takes care of a master will be honored. ^27-18

**27:19** Just as water reflects the face, so one human heart reflects another. ^27-19

**27:20** Sheol and Abaddon are never satisfied, and human eyes are never satisfied. ^27-20

**27:21** The crucible is for silver, and the furnace is for gold, so a person is tested^[Heb lacks *is tested*] by being praised. ^27-21

**27:22** Crush a fool in a mortar with a pestle along with crushed grain, but the folly will not be driven out. ^27-22

**27:23** Know well the condition of your flocks, and give attention to your herds, ^27-23

**27:24** for riches do not last forever, nor a crown for all generations. ^27-24

**27:25** When the grass is gone, and new growth appears, and the herbage of the mountains is gathered, ^27-25

**27:26** the lambs will provide your clothing, and the goats the price of a field; ^27-26

**27:27** there will be enough goats’ milk for your food, for the food of your household and nourishment for your female servants. ^27-27
