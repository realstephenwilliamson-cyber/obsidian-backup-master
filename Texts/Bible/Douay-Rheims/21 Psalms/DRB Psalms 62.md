# Psalms 62 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 61|← Psalms 61]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 63|Psalms 63 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 62|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**62:1** A psalm of David when he was in the desert of Edom. ^62-1

**62:2** O God, my God, to thee do I watch at break of day. For thee my soul hath thirsted; for thee my flesh, O how many ways! ^62-2

**62:3** In a desert land, and where there is no way, and no water: so in the sanctuary have I come before thee, to see thy power and thy glory. ^62-3

**62:4** For thy mercy is better than lives: thee my lips shall praise. ^62-4

**62:5** Thus will I bless thee all my life long: and in thy name I will lift up my hands. ^62-5

**62:6** Let my soul be filled as with marrow and fatness: and my mouth shall praise thee with joyful lips. ^62-6

**62:7** If I have remembered thee upon my bed, I will meditate on thee in the morning: ^62-7

**62:8** Because thou hast been my helper. And I will rejoice under the covert of thy wings: ^62-8

**62:9** My soul hath stuck close to thee: thy right hand hath received me. ^62-9

**62:10** But they have sought my soul in vain, they shall go into the lower parts of the earth: ^62-10

**62:11** They shall be delivered into the hands of the sword, they shall be the portions of foxes. ^62-11

**62:12** But the king shall rejoice in God, all they shall be praised that swear by him: because the mouth is stopped of them that speak wicked things. ^62-12
