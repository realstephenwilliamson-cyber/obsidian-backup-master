## Chapter 79

**79:1** A psalm of Asaph.  
O God, heathens have entered Your domain,  
defiled Your holy temple,  
and turned Jerusalem into ruins. ^79-1

**79:2** They have left Your servants’ corpses  
as food for the fowl of heaven,  
and the flesh of Your faithful for the wild beasts. ^79-2

**79:3** Their blood was shed like water around Jerusalem,  
with none to bury them. ^79-3

**79:4** We have become the butt of our neighbors,  
the scorn and derision of those around us. ^79-4

**79:5** How long, O LORD, will You be angry forever,  
will Your indignation blaze like fire? ^79-5

**79:6** Pour out Your fury on the nations that do not know You,  
upon the kingdoms that do not invoke Your name, ^79-6

**79:7** for they have devoured Jacob  
and desolated his home. ^79-7

**79:8** Do not hold our former iniquities against us;  
let Your compassion come swiftly toward us,  
for we have sunk very low. ^79-8

**79:9** Help us, O God, our deliverer,  
for the sake of the glory of Your name.  
Save us and forgive our sin,  
for the sake of Your name. ^79-9

**79:10** Let the nations not say, “Where is their God?”  
Before our eyes let it be known among the nations  
that You avenge the spilled blood of Your servants. ^79-10

**79:11** Let the groans of the prisoners reach You;  
reprieve those condemned to death,  
as befits Your great strength. ^79-11

**79:12** Pay back our neighbors sevenfold  
for the abuse they have flung at You, O Lord. ^79-12

**79:13** Then we, Your people,  
the flock You shepherd,  
shall glorify You forever;  
for all time we shall tell Your praises. ^79-13
