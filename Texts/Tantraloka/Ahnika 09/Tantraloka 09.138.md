## Mūla

एतत्कार्ममलं प्रोक्तं येन साकं लयाकलाः ।
स्युर्गुहागहनान्तःस्थाः सुप्ता इव सरीसृपाः ॥ ^mula

etatkārmamalaṃ proktaṃ yena sākaṃ layākalāḥ |
syurguhāgahanāntaḥsthāḥ suptā iva sarīsṛpāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 05 - Chapter Nine, part 3#^pg135|p. 118]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=135>))
>
> This is said to be Karmic impurity with which the Dissolution Deconditioned (pralayākala) subjects are associated. Like sleeping serpents, they reside deep in the abyss of the cave (of Māyā). Then, when their latent (karmic) impressions are awakened, they reap their appropriate consequences and transmigrate repeatedly in this (universe) ranging from (the world of) Brahmā down to (that of) plants. (with 9.138–139)
