# ἁμαρτάνω

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · verb

**Dodson:** I sin — originally: I miss the mark, hence (a) I make a mistake, (b) I sin, commit a sin (against God); sometimes the idea of sinning against a fellow-creature is present. (Strong's 264; headword ἁμαρτάνω)
