## Chapter 2

**2:1** The word of the LORD came to me, saying, ^2-1

**2:2** Go proclaim to Jerusalem: Thus said the LORD:  
I accounted to your favor  
The devotion of your youth,  
Your love as a bride—  
How you followed Me in the wilderness,  
In a land not sown. ^2-2

**2:3** Israel was holy to the LORD,  
The first fruits of His harvest.  
All who ate of it were held guilty;  
Disaster befell them  
—declares the LORD. ^2-3

**2:4** Hear the word of the LORD, O House of Jacob,  
Every clan of the House of Israel! ^2-4

**2:5** Thus said the LORD:  
What wrong did your fathers find in Me  
That they abandoned Me  
And went after delusion and were deluded? ^2-5

**2:6** They never asked themselves, “Where is the LORD,  
Who brought us up from the land of Egypt,  
Who led us through the wilderness,  
A land of deserts and pits,  
A land of drought and darkness,  
A land no man had traversed,  
Where no human being had dwelt?” ^2-6

**2:7** I brought you to this country of farm land  
To enjoy its fruit and its bounty;  
But you came and defiled My land,  
You made My possession abhorrent. ^2-7

**2:8** The priests never asked themselves, “Where is the LORD?”  
The guardians of the Teaching ignored Me;  
The rulers[^1] rebelled against Me,  
And the prophets prophesied by Baal  
And followed what can do no good. ^2-8

**2:9** Oh, I will go on accusing you  
—declares the LORD—  
And I will accuse your children’s children! ^2-9

**2:10** Just cross over to the isles of the Kittim and look,  
Send to Kedar and observe carefully;  
See if aught like this has ever happened: ^2-10

**2:11** Has any nation changed its gods  
Even though they are no-gods?  
But My people has exchanged its glory  
For what can do no good. ^2-11

**2:12** Be appalled, O heavens, at this;  
Be horrified, utterly dazed!  
—says the LORD. ^2-12

**2:13** For My people have done a twofold wrong:  
They have forsaken Me, the Fount of living waters,  
And hewed them out cisterns, broken cisterns,  
Which cannot even hold water. ^2-13

**2:14** Is Israel a bondman?  
Is he a home-born slave?  
Then why is he given over to plunder? ^2-14

**2:15** Lions have roared over him,  
Have raised their cries.  
They have made his land a waste,  
His cities desolate, without inhabitants. ^2-15

**2:16** Those, too, in Noph and Tahpanhes[^2]  
Will lay bare[^3] your head. ^2-16

**2:17** See, that is the price you have paid  
For forsaking the LORD your God[^4]  
While He led you in the way.[^5] ^2-17

**2:18** What, then, is the good of your going to Egypt  
To drink the waters of the Nile?  
And what is the good of your going to Assyria  
To drink the waters of the Euphrates? ^2-18

**2:19** Let your misfortune reprove you,  
Let your afflictions rebuke you;  
Mark well how bad and bitter it is  
That you forsake the LORD your God,  
That awe for Me is not in you  
—declares the Lord GOD of Hosts. ^2-19

**2:20** For long ago you[^6] broke your yoke,  
Tore off your yoke-bands,  
And said, “I will not work!”[^7]  
On every high hill and under every verdant tree,  
You recline as a whore. ^2-20

**2:21** I planted you with noble vines,  
All with choicest seed;  
Alas, I find you changed  
Into a base, an alien vine! ^2-21

**2:22** Though you wash with natron  
And use much lye,  
Your guilt is ingrained before Me  
—declares the Lord GOD. ^2-22

**2:23** How can you say, “I am not defiled,  
I have not gone after the Baalim”?  
Look at your deeds in the Valley,[^8]  
Consider what you have done!  
Like a lustful she-camel,  
Restlessly running about,[^9] ^2-23

**2:24** Or like a wild ass used to the desert,  
Snuffing the wind in her eagerness,  
Whose passion none can restrain,  
None that seek her need grow weary—  
In her season, they’ll find her! ^2-24

**2:25** Save your foot from going bare,  
And your throat from thirst.  
But you say, “It is no use.  
No, I love the strangers,[^10]  
And after them I must go.” ^2-25

**2:26** Like a thief chagrined when he is caught,  
So is the House of Israel chagrined—  
They, their kings, their officers,  
And their priests and prophets. ^2-26

**2:27** They said to wood, “You are my father,”  
To stone, “You gave birth to me,”  
While to Me they turned their backs  
And not their faces.  
But in their hour of calamity they cry,  
“Arise and save us!” ^2-27

**2:28** And where are those gods  
You made for yourself?  
Let them arise and save you, if they can,  
In your hour of calamity.  
For your gods have become, O Judah,  
As many as your towns! ^2-28

**2:29** Why do you call Me to account?  
You have all rebelled against Me  
—declares the LORD. ^2-29

**2:30** To no purpose did I smite your children;  
They would not accept correction.  
Your sword has devoured your prophets  
Like a ravening lion. ^2-30

**2:31** O generation, behold[^11] the word of the LORD!  
Have I been like a desert to Israel,  
Or like a land of deep gloom?  
Then why do My people say, “We have broken loose,  
We will not come to You any more?” ^2-31

**2:32** Can a maiden forget her jewels,  
A bride her adornments?  
Yet My people have forgotten Me—  
Days without number. ^2-32

**2:33** How skillfully you plan your way  
To seek out love!  
Why, you have even taught  
The worst of women your ways. ^2-33

**2:34** Moreover, on your garments is found  
The lifeblood of the innocent poor—  
You did not catch them breaking in.[^12]  
Yet, despite all these things,[^13] ^2-34

**2:35** You say, “I have been acquitted;  
Surely, His anger has turned away from me.”  
Lo, I will bring you to judgment  
For saying, “I have not sinned.” ^2-35

**2:36** How you cheapen yourself,  
By changing your course!  
You shall be put to shame through Egypt,  
Just as you were put to shame through Assyria. ^2-36

**2:37** From this way, too, you will come out  
With your hands on your head;[^14]  
For the LORD has rejected those you trust,  
You will not prosper with them. ^2-37

[^1]: Lit. “shepherds”; cf. 3.15; 23.1 ff.
[^2]: Cities in Egypt. The Egyptians, like the Assyrians, will prove a disappointment; cf. v. 36.
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^4]: Lit. “that is what your forsaking the Lord your God is doing to you.”
[^5]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^6]: For the form, cf. shaqqamti, Judg. 5.7; others “I.”
[^7]: Following the kethib; qere “transgress.”
[^8]: I.e., of Hinnom; cf. 7.31–32; 32.35.
[^9]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^10]: I.e., other gods.
[^11]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^12]: In which case there might have been an excuse for killing them; cf. Exod. 22.1.
[^13]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^14]: A gesture of wild grief; cf. 2 Sam. 13.19.
