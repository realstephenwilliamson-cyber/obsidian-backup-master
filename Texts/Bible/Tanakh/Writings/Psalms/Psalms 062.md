# Psalms 062

Compare: [[NRSVue Psalms 062|NRSVue]] (Heb 62:1 = superscription; Heb 62:2–13 = NRSVue 62:1–12)

## Chapter 62

**62:1** For the leader; on *Jeduthun*. A psalm of David. ^62-1

**62:2** Truly my soul waits quietly for God;  
my deliverance comes from Him. ^62-2

**62:3** Truly He is my rock and deliverance,  
my haven; I shall never be shaken. ^62-3

**62:4** How long will all of you attack[^1] a man,  
to crush[^2] him, as though he were  
a leaning wall, a tottering fence? ^62-4

**62:5** They lay plans to topple him from his rank;  
they delight in falsehood;  
they bless with their mouths,  
while inwardly they curse. *Selah*. ^62-5

**62:6** Truly, wait quietly for God, O my soul,  
for my hope comes from Him. ^62-6

**62:7** He is my rock and deliverance,  
my haven; I shall not be shaken. ^62-7

**62:8** I rely on God, my deliverance and glory,  
my rock of strength;  
in God is my refuge. ^62-8

**62:9** Trust in Him at all times, O people;  
pour out your hearts before Him;  
God is our refuge. *Selah*. ^62-9

**62:10** Men are mere breath;  
mortals, illusion;  
placed on a scale all together,  
they weigh even less than a breath. ^62-10

**62:11** Do not trust in violence,  
or put false hopes in robbery;  
if force bears fruit pay it no mind. ^62-11

**62:12** One thing God has spoken;  
two things have I heard:  
that might belongs to God, ^62-12

**62:13** and faithfulness is Yours, O Lord,  
to reward each man according to his deeds. ^62-13

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
