# Dissolution of the Questioner

> [!abstract] Definition
> The apophatic mystical transition where the Divine Absolute does not provide propositional answers to human suffering or existence, but instead dissolves the presuppositions and autonomy of the egoic questioner.

## The Paradigm of Job
In [[Gospel of Recognition text 2026-08-05|The Gospel of Recognition]]:
- When the voice speaks out of the Whirlwind in Job 38–41, it balances no moral ledger and offers no justification for Job's misery.
- Instead, the uncontainable majesty of the Absolute overwhelms the boundaries of Job's separate selfhood.
- Job's final realization—*"Therefore I abhor myself, and repent in dust and ashes"* ([[Job 42#^42-6|Job 42:6]])—is not moral debasement, but ego-dissolution: to stand in the presence of the Infinite, one must become "no one."
