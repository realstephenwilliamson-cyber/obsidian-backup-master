# Sirach 4 (Douay-Rheims)

*Ecclesiasticus 4 in the Douay-Rheims*

[[DRB Sirach 03|← Sirach 3]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Sirach 05|Sirach 5 →]]

**4:1** Son, defraud not the poor of alms, and turn not away thy eyes from the poor. ^4-1

**4:2** Despise not the hungry soul: and provoke not the Boor in his want. ^4-2

**4:3** Afflict not the heart of the needy, and defer not to give to him that is in distress. ^4-3

**4:4** Reject not the petition of the afflicted: and turn not away thy face from the needy. ^4-4

**4:5** Turn not away thy eyes from the poor for fear of anger: and leave not to them that ask of thee to curse thee behind thy back. ^4-5

**4:6** For the prayer of him that curseth thee in the bitterness of his soul, shall be heard, for he that made him will hear him. ^4-6

**4:7** Make thyself affable to the congregation of the poor, and humble thy soul to the ancient, and bow thy head to a great man. ^4-7

**4:8** Bow down thy ear cheerfully to the poor, and pay what thou owest, and answer him peaceable words with mildness. ^4-8

**4:9** Deliver him that suffereth wrong out of the hand of the proud: and be not fainthearted in thy soul. ^4-9

**4:10** In judging be merciful to the fatherless as a father, and as a husband to their mother. ^4-10

**4:11** And thou shalt be as the obedient son of the most High, and he will have mercy on thee more than a mother. ^4-11

**4:12** Wisdom inspireth life into her children, and protecteth them that seek after her, and will go before them in the way of justice. ^4-12

**4:13** And he that loveth her, loveth life: and they that watch for her, shall embrace her sweetness. ^4-13

**4:14** They that hold her fast, shall inherit life: and whithersoever she entereth, God will give a blessing. ^4-14

**4:15** They that serve her, shall be servants to the holy one: and God loveth them that love her. ^4-15

**4:16** He that hearkeneth to her, shall judge nations: and he that looketh upon her, shall remain secure. ^4-16

**4:17** If he trust to her, he shall inherit her, and his generation shall be in assurance. ^4-17

**4:18** For she walketh with him in temptation, and at the first she chooseth him. ^4-18

**4:19** She will bring upon him fear and dread and trial: and she will scourge him with the affliction of her discipline, till she try him by her laws, and trust his soul. ^4-19

**4:20** Then she will strengthen him, and make a straight way to him, and give him joy, ^4-20

**4:21** And will disclose her secrets to him, and will heap upon him treasures of knowledge and understanding of justice. ^4-21

**4:22** But if he go astray, she will forsake him, and deliver him into the hands of his enemy. ^4-22

**4:23** Son, observe the time, and fly from evil. ^4-23

**4:24** For thy soul be not ashamed to say the truth. ^4-24

**4:25** For there is a shame that bringeth sin, and there is a shame that bringeth glory and grace. ^4-25

**4:26** Accept no person against thy own person, nor against thy soul a lie. ^4-26

**4:27** Reverence not thy neighbour in his fall: ^4-27

**4:28** And refrain not to speak in the time of salvation. Hide not thy wisdom in her beauty. ^4-28

**4:29** For by the tongue wisdom is discerned: and understanding, and knowledge, and learning by the word of the wise, and steadfastness in the works of justice. ^4-29

**4:30** In nowise speak against the truth, but be ashamed of the lie of thy ignorance. ^4-30

**4:31** Be not ashamed to confess thy sins, but submit not thyself to every man for sin. ^4-31

**4:32** Resist not against the face of the mighty, and do not strive against the stream of the river. ^4-32

**4:33** Strive for justice for thy soul, and even unto death fight for justice, and God will overthrow thy enemies for thee. ^4-33

**4:34** Be not hasty in thy tongue: and slack and remiss in thy works. ^4-34

**4:35** Be not as a lion in thy house, terrifying them of thy household, and oppressing them that are under thee. ^4-35

**4:36** Let not thy hand be stretched out to receive, and shut when thou shouldst give. ^4-36
