## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 11 - Chapter Twenty#^pg253|p. 233]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=253>))
>
> (The teacher who is) one-pointedly engaged in repeating Mantra, (should imagine that on the palm of his) right hand is an intensely flaming triangle, which is the abode of Fire (R), to be burning all around with the phonemes of Fire (R), fanned by the abode of the Wind (Y). Taking hold of some seed, he should imagine that it is burning (there in his) hand and within (his) heart. The seed, burning with the fire of an uninterrupted stream (dhoranī) of PHATṬs and energized by the Fire (of the letter R), becomes sterile and incapable of germination. (with 20.2–4)
