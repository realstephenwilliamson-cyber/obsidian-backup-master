# Psalm 125

← [[BCP Psalm 124]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 126]] →

*Qui confidunt* · Book Five · Twenty-seventh Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 125]]

**125:1** Those who trust in the LORD are like Mount Zion, \*  
which cannot be moved, but stands fast for ever. ^125-1

**125:2** The hills stand about Jerusalem; \*  
so does the LORD stand round about his people,  
from this time forth for evermore. ^125-2

**125:3** The scepter of the wicked shall not hold sway over the land allotted to the just, \*  
so that the just shall not put their hands to evil. ^125-3

[p. 782]

**125:4** Show your goodness, O LORD, to those who are good \*  
and to those who are true of heart. ^125-4

**125:5** As for those who turn aside to crooked ways,  
the LORD will lead them away with the evildoers; \*  
but peace be upon Israel. ^125-5
