# Psalm 112

← [[BCP Psalm 111]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 113]] →

*Beatus vir* · Book Five · Twenty-third Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 112]]

**112:1** Hallelujah!  
Happy are they who fear the Lord \*  
and have great delight in his commandments! ^112-1

**112:2** Their descendants will be mighty in the land; \*  
the generation of the upright will be blessed. ^112-2

**112:3** Wealth and riches will be in their house, \*  
and their righteousness will last for ever. ^112-3

**112:4** Light shines in the darkness for the upright; \*  
the righteous are merciful and full of compassion. ^112-4

**112:5** It is good for them to be generous in lending \*  
and to manage their affairs with justice. ^112-5

**112:6** For they will never be shaken; \*  
the righteous will be kept in everlasting remembrance. ^112-6

**112:7** They will not be afraid of any evil rumors; \*  
their heart is right;  
they put their trust in the Lord. ^112-7

**112:8** Their heart is established and will not shrink, \*  
until they see their desire upon their enemies. ^112-8

**112:9** They have given freely to the poor, \*  
and their righteousness stands fast for ever;  
they will hold up their head with honor. ^112-9

**112:10** The wicked will see it and be angry;  
they will gnash their teeth and pine away; \*  
the desires of the wicked will perish. ^112-10
