## Chapter 14

**14:1** [^1]For the leader. Of David.  
The benighted man thinks,  
“God does not care.”[^2]  
Man’s deeds are corrupt and loathsome;  
no one does good. ^14-1

**14:2** The LORD looks down from heaven on mankind  
to find a man of understanding,  
a man mindful of God. ^14-2

**14:3** All have turned bad,  
altogether foul;  
there is none who does good,  
not even one. ^14-3

**14:4** Are they so witless, all those evildoers,  
who devour my people as they devour food,  
and do not invoke the LORD? ^14-4

**14:5** There they will be seized with fright,  
for God is present in the circle of the righteous. ^14-5

**14:6** You may set at naught the counsel of the lowly,  
but the LORD is his refuge. ^14-6

**14:7** O that the deliverance of Israel might come from Zion!  
When the LORD restores the fortunes of His people,  
Jacob will exult, Israel will rejoice. ^14-7

[^1]: Cf. Ps. 53.
[^2]: Lit. “There is no God”; cf. Ps. 10.4.
