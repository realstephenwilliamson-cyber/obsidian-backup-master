# Psalm 149

← [[BCP Psalm 148]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 150]] →

*Cantate Domino* · Book Five · Thirtieth Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 149]]

**149:1** Hallelujah!  
Sing to the LORD a new song; \*  
sing his praise in the congregation of the faithful. ^149-1

**149:2** Let Israel rejoice in his Maker; \*  
let the children of Zion be joyful in their King. ^149-2

**149:3** Let them praise his Name in the dance; \*  
let them sing praise to him with timbrel and harp. ^149-3

**149:4** For the LORD takes pleasure in his people \*  
and adorns the poor with victory. ^149-4

**149:5** Let the faithful rejoice in triumph; \*  
let them be joyful on their beds. ^149-5

**149:6** Let the praises of God be in their throat \*  
and a two-edged sword in their hand; ^149-6

**149:7** To wreak vengeance on the nations \*  
and punishment on the peoples; ^149-7

**149:8** To bind their kings in chains \*  
and their nobles with links of iron; ^149-8

**149:9** To inflict on them the judgment decreed; \*  
this is glory for all his faithful people.  
Hallelujah! ^149-9
