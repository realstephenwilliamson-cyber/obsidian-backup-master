## Mūla

तस्यात्मजश्चुखलकेति जने प्रसिद्ध-
श्चन्द्रावदातधिषणो नरसिंहगुप्तः ।
यं सर्वशास्त्ररसमज्जनशुभ्रचित्तं
माहेश्वरी परमलंकुरुते स्म भक्तिः ॥ ^mula

tasyātmajaścukhalaketi jane prasiddha-
ścandrāvadātadhiṣaṇo narasiṃhaguptaḥ |
yaṃ sarvaśāstrarasamajjanaśubhracittaṃ
māheśvarī paramalaṃkurute sma bhaktiḥ || ^iast

> His son was Narasiṁhagupta, well known amongst men by the name of Cukhalaka. Supremely adorned with devotion to Maheśvara, his intellect was pure like the moon, and his mind shone brightly by virtue of his (constant) immersion in the aesthetic essence (rasa) of all the scriptures.
