## Mūla

मध्यशृङ्गं वर्जयित्वा सर्वः पूर्वोदितो विधिः ।
ततो यदुन्मुखं खण्डचन्द्रयुग्मं पुरोदितम् ॥ ^mula

madhyaśṛṅgaṃ varjayitvā sarvaḥ pūrvodito vidhiḥ |
tato yadunmukhaṃ khaṇḍacandrayugmaṃ puroditam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 04 - Chapter Thirty-One, part 1#^pg93|p. 75]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=93>))
>
> The teacher, who knows how to apply the same procedures in accord with varying circumstances (žhavedin), should carefully lay hold of the second thread of Brahmā, that extends along the western part (of the mandala), below the two sides (on the south and north). Facing west, the teacher, making use of that thread, (should repeat) all the previously described procedure, with the exception (of that part of it which concerns) the prong in the middle. (with 31.22cd–24ab)
>
> Then, making use of the two previously mentioned half-moons that are facing (each other), (the teacher should) make a good (and beautiful) inner alignment of the ‘pillow’ with these two (between them), and with the two threads in front of them, along with the two (prongs, above and below the two tridents on the southern and northern sides), there are (also) the two prongs in the middle (between the prongs). (with 31.24cd–25)
