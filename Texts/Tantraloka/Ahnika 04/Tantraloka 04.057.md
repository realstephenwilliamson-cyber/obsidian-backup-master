## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. III
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 03 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v03 03 - Chapter Four, part 2#^pg86|p. 73]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 03.pdf#page=86>))
>
> ‘The causes (of liberation) are considered to be countless because they are impelled by (Śiva’s) own will. Sometimes (the Lord graces the individual soul) by means of the Yoga of devotion, (ritual) action (karman) or knowledge (vidyā) also, by instructing (in the ways of) righteousness (dharma) and wisdom (jñāna), or by means of mantras or initiation also. By these and numerous other ways, the Supreme Lord, the governor (pati) of all the universe, graces those subject to transmigration.’ (with 4.55–57ab)
>
> ‘The knowledge which is the (realization of) universal oneness within Kula (the sphere of manifestation) (kulasāmānya) and bestows (liberation, which is) the Kaula accomplishment (kaulikasiddhi), can be attained, O Dear One, by awakening the Circle of the Mothers (mātṛmaṇḍala), purification, austerity, meditation, yoga, the repetition of Mantra, knowledge, worship with Mantra (mantrārādhana) and (the observance of) vows.’ (with 4.57cd–58)
