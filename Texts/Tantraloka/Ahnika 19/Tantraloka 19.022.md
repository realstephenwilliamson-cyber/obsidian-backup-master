## Mūla

शिष्यदेहे नियोज्यैतदनुद्विग्नः शतं जपेत् ।
उत्क्रम्योर्ध्वनिमेषेण शिष्य इत्थं परं व्रजेत् ॥ ^mula

śiṣyadehe niyojyaitadanudvignaḥ śataṃ japet |
utkramyordhvanimeṣeṇa śiṣya itthaṃ paraṃ vrajet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 10 - Chapter Nineteen#^pg237|p. 217]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=237>))
>
> The prescribed procedure (vidhi), according to the venerable Dīkṣottara, is this: the Swan (H) encapsulated by (two) Fires (R), the Man (M), below which is Rudra (Ū) along with the Point (Ṁ) (above). Once applied this to the body of the disciple, (the teacher), unperturbed, should repeat (this Mantra) one hundred times. (Then), causing the disciple to exit (his body) upwards, in the twinkling of an eye, he goes in this way to the supreme (abode). (with 19.21–22)
