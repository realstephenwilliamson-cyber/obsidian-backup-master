# Jeremiah 34

← [[Jeremiah 33]] · [[Tanakh]] · [[Jeremiah 35]] →

Compare: [[NRSVue Jeremiah 34|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 34

**34:1** The word which came to Jeremiah from the LORD, when King Nebuchadrezzar of Babylon and all his army, and all the kingdoms of the earth and all the peoples under his sway, were waging war against Jerusalem and all its towns: ^34-1

**34:2** Thus said the LORD, the God of Israel: Go speak to King Zedekiah of Judah, and say to him: “Thus said the LORD: I am going to deliver this city into the hands of the king of Babylon, and he will destroy it by fire. ^34-2

**34:3** And you will not escape from him; you will be captured and handed over to him. And you will see the king of Babylon face to face and speak to him in person;[^1] and you will be brought to Babylon. ^34-3

**34:4** But hear the word of the LORD, O King Zedekiah of Judah! Thus said the LORD concerning you: You will not die by the sword. ^34-4

**34:5** You will die a peaceful death; and as incense was burned for your ancestors, the earlier kings who preceded you, so they will burn incense[^2] for you, and they will lament for you ‘Ah, lord!’ For I Myself have made the promise—declares the LORD.” ^34-5

**34:6** The prophet Jeremiah spoke all these words to King Zedekiah of Judah in Jerusalem, ^34-6

**34:7** when the army of the king of Babylon was waging war against Jerusalem and against the remaining towns of Judah—against Lachish and Azekah, for they were the only fortified towns of Judah that were left. ^34-7

**34:8** The word which came to Jeremiah from the LORD after King Zedekiah had made a covenant with all the people in Jerusalem to proclaim a release[^3] among them— ^34-8

**34:9** that everyone should set free his Hebrew slaves, both male and female, and that no one should keep his fellow Judean enslaved. ^34-9

**34:10** Everyone, officials and people, who had entered into the covenant agreed to set their male and female slaves free and not keep them enslaved any longer; they complied and let them go. ^34-10

**34:11** But afterward they turned about and brought back the men and women they had set free, and forced them into slavery again. ^34-11

**34:12** Then it was that the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah from the LORD: ^34-12

**34:13** Thus said the LORD, the God of Israel: I made a covenant with your fathers when I brought them out of the land of Egypt, the house of bondage, saying: ^34-13

**34:14** “In the seventh year[^4] each of you must let go any fellow Hebrew who may be sold[^5] to you; when he has served you six years, you must set him free.” But your fathers would not obey Me or give ear. ^34-14

**34:15** Lately you turned about and did what is proper in My sight, and each of you proclaimed a release to his countrymen; and you made a covenant accordingly before Me in the House which bears My name. ^34-15

**34:16** But now you have turned back and have profaned My name; each of you has brought back the men and women whom you had given their freedom, and forced them to be your slaves again. ^34-16

**34:17** Assuredly, thus said the LORD: You would not obey Me and proclaim a release, each to his kinsman and countryman. Lo! I proclaim your release—declares the LORD—to the sword, to pestilence, and to famine; and I will make you a horror to all the kingdoms of the earth. ^34-17

**34:18** I will make the men who violated My covenant, who did not fulfill the terms of the covenant which they made before Me, [like] the calf which they cut in two so as to pass between the halves:[^6] ^34-18

**34:19** The officers of Judah and Jerusalem, the officials, the priests, and all the people of the land who passed between the halves of the calf ^34-19

**34:20** shall be handed over to their enemies, to those who seek to kill them. Their carcasses shall become food for the birds of the sky and the beasts of the earth. ^34-20

**34:21** I will hand over King Zedekiah of Judah and his officers to their enemies, who seek to kill them—to the army of the king of Babylon which has withdrawn from you. ^34-21

**34:22** I hereby give the command—declares the LORD—by which I will bring them back against this city. They shall attack it and capture it, and burn it down. I will make the towns of Judah a desolation, without inhabitant. ^34-22

[^1]: For the idiom see note at 32.4.
[^2]: Lit. “burnings.”
[^3]: Others “liberty.”
[^4]: I.e., of servitude. Lit. “After a period of seven years”; cf. Deut. 14.28 and 15.1.
[^5]: Or “who sells himself.”
[^6]: Cf. Gen. 15.9–10, 17–21.
