## Mūla

मूर्तियागं चरेत्तस्य विधिर्योगीश्वरीमते ।
पवित्रारोहणे श्राद्धे तथा पर्वदिनेष्वलम् ॥ ^mula

mūrtiyāgaṃ carettasya vidhiryogīśvarīmate |
pavitrārohaṇe śrāddhe tathā parvadineṣvalam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 04 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 2#^pg59|p. 42]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=59>))
>
> Therefore, one should celebrate the Worship of the Embodiments (mūrtiyāga), having made a teacher who is well versed in the knowledge (of the scriptures) the main (officiant and object of worship). The prescribed procedure (vidhi) is found in the Yogīśvarīmata. (with 28.75cd–76ab)
>
> The Worship of the Embodiments (mūrtiyāga) should be performed when the sacred thread is offered (to the deity), when rites for the ancestors (śrāddha) (are performed), on the days when a parvan (occurs), when a solar or a lunar eclipse and the like occur, so too during the common (laukika) parvans (that are not the Kaula ones), during a festival (utsava), a marriage and the like, when a brahminical sacrificial rite (yajñakarman) (takes place), during an initiation and the installation (of a Liñga etc), when the pledges are cleansed (viśodhana) (of the sins of transgression), and (finally,) in order to (get what one) desires. (The Worship of the Embodiments) is of five kinds. (with 28.76cd–78)
