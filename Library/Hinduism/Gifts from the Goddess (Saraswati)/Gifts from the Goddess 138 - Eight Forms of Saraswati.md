*[p. 399]* ^pg428

“I offer worship to the secret *yōginīs* (*rahasya yōginyaḥ*), whose chakra eliminates all illness and disease (*sarva rōgahara cakrē*); and to Tripurāsiddhā Cakrēśvarī, who embodies the achievements possible in all these three states, who governs this chakra.”

Perform the *hskphrēm mudrā* of Sarvakhēcarī.

Weapons of the Devi

In between the eighth and seventh mandalas—that is, between the central triangle and the eight-triangle figure of Sri Yantra—we find the four weapons wielded by Lalitā Tripurasundarī, the Red Goddess.
