## Mūla

नरशक्तिसमुन्मेषि शिवरूपाद्विभेदितम् ।
यन्न्यक्कृतशिवाहन्तासमावेशं विभेदवत् ॥ ^mula

naraśaktisamunmeṣi śivarūpādvibheditam |

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 06 - Chapter Five, part 4#^pg169|p. 154]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=169>))
> Dyczkowski's numbering differs here; his markers: (115-116) (114cd-116ab), (117) (116cd-116ef).
>
> 2) This (same) Liṁga, manifest-cum-unmanifest, is the vivid penetration (into immanent consciousness) (samāveśa) that takes place as the cosmic paths (of manifestation) unfold within the (microcosmic) body. (This penetration) is associated with objectivity (idaṁbhāva) in an (active) unfolding state, (but is) enveloped in subjectivity (ahantācchādita). (It corresponds to) the intermediate level (parāpara) that is the vitality of Mantra. Unfolding as Man and Śakti, it is distinct from (the Liñga) which is iva. (with 5.114cd–116ab)
>
> 3) The manifest Liga is of the nature of consciousness, (but even so) is as if separate (from it). Its form the particularized vibration (of consciousness) (viśeṣaspanda), it is that (state in which) penetration (samāveśa) into Śiva’s subjectivity (akantā) (as one’s own identity) has been suppressed. (with 5.116cd)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* न केवलं व्यक्ताव्यक्तमेव लिङ्गमस्याः स्फारो यावत् व्यक्तमपि -- इत्याह --

> यन्न्यक्कृतशिवाहन्तासमावेशं विभेदवत् ॥
> विशेषस्पन्दरूपं तद् व्यक्तं लिङ्गं चिदात्मकम् ।

यन्नाम गुणीकृतपराद्वयरूपाहन्तापरामर्शम्, अत एव "विभेदवत्"बहीरूपतया स्फुरत् विशेषस्पन्दरूपम् -- इति विमर्शनं तद् व्यक्तं लिङ्गमुच्यत इत्यर्थः । एवमपि चिदात्मकम्, अन्यथा हि एतन्न किञ्चिद्भवेदिति भावः ॥ ११६ ॥
