# Psalms 042

Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 042]] · Septuagint (Brenton): [[LXX Psalms 41]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

Compare: [[NRSVue Psalms 042|NRSVue]] (Heb 42:1 = superscription; Heb 42:2–12 = NRSVue 42:1–11)

## Chapter 42

**42:1** For the leader. A *maskil* of the Korahites. ^42-1

**42:2** Like a hind crying for water,[^1]  
my soul cries for You, O God; ^42-2

**42:3** my soul thirsts for God, the living God;  
O when will I come to appear before God! ^42-3

**42:4** My tears have been my food day and night;  
I am ever taunted with, “Where is your God?” ^42-4

**42:5** When I think of this, I pour out my soul:  
how I walked with the crowd, moved with them,[^2]  
the festive throng, to the House of God  
with joyous shouts of praise. ^42-5

**42:6** Why so downcast, my soul,  
why disquieted within me?  
Have hope in God;  
I will yet praise Him  
for His saving presence.[^3] ^42-6

**42:7** O my God, my soul is downcast;  
therefore I think of You  
in this land of Jordan and Hermon,  
in Mount Mizar, ^42-7

**42:8** where deep calls to deep  
in the roar of Your cataracts;[^4]  
all Your breakers and billows have swept over me. ^42-8

**42:9** By day may the LORD vouchsafe His faithful care,  
so that at night a song to Him may be with me,  
a prayer to the God of my life. ^42-9

**42:10** I say to God, my rock,  
“Why have You forgotten me,  
why must I walk in gloom,  
oppressed by my enemy?” ^42-10

**42:11** Crushing my bones,[^5]  
my foes revile me,  
taunting me always with, “Where is your God?” ^42-11

**42:12** Why so downcast, my soul,  
why disquieted within me?  
Have hope in God;  
I will yet praise Him,  
my ever-present help, my God. ^42-12

[^1]: Lit. “watercourses.”
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Several ancient versions and Heb. mss. connect the first word in v. 7 with the end of 6, reading yeshu‘ot panai we’Elohai, “my ever-present help, my God,” as in vv. 12 and Ps. 43.5.
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^5]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
