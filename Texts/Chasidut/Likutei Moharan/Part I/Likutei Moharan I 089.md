# Likutei Moharan I 089

← [[Likutei Moharan I 088]] · [[Likutei Moharan]] · [[Likutei Moharan I 090]] →

*Part I · Lesson 89* · [[Likutei Moharan]]

## 1

“But You made him slightly wanting from *elohim* (divinity), and with glory and majesty *teatreihu* (You crown him).” ([[Psalms 008#^8-6|Psalms 8:6]])

Behold, it is known that whatever a person lacks, whether spiritually or physically—that want is in the Divine Presence, which is the aspect of *Elohim* (the Lord).

This is: **But You made him wanting—**[This want] is certainly “**slightly [wanting] from *elohim*.**” In other words, the want is certainly “from *Elohim*”—i.e., in the Divine Presence.

But when a person knows this, that the want is above and below, he will certainly feel great sorrow and sadness, and so will not be able to worship God with joy. He will therefore have to answer for himself: “What am I, and what is my life? For the King Himself informs me of His want. Is there any greater glory than this?” This brings him to great joy, and his intellect is restored. This is:

**and with glory and majesty *teatreihu*—**In other words, on account of his “glory and majesty,” that the King Himself informs him of the want. \<And as a result:>

***teATReihu—*** \<This refers to a crown, as in ([[I Samuel 23#^23-26|1 Samuel 23:26]]), “Shaul and his men *oATRim* (were encircling) David”—i.e.,> new intellect.
