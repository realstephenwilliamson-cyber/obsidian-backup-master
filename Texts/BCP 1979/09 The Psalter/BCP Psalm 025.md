# Psalm 25

← [[BCP Psalm 024]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 026]] →

*Ad te, Domine, levavi* · Book One · Fifth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 025]]

**25:1** To you, O LORD, I lift up my soul;  
my God, I put my trust in you; \*  
let me not be humiliated,  
nor let my enemies triumph over me. ^25-1

**25:2** Let none who look to you be put to shame; \*  
let the treacherous be disappointed in their schemes. ^25-2

**25:3** Show me your ways, O LORD, \*  
and teach me your paths. ^25-3

**25:4** Lead me in your truth and teach me, \*  
for you are the God of my salvation;  
in you have I trusted all the day long. ^25-4

**25:5** Remember, O LORD, your compassion and love, \*  
for they are from everlasting. ^25-5

[p. 615] ^pg615

**25:6** Remember not the sins of my youth and my transgressions; \*  
remember me according to your love  
and for the sake of your goodness, O LORD. ^25-6

**25:7** Gracious and upright is the LORD; \*  
therefore he teaches sinners in his way. ^25-7

**25:8** He guides the humble in doing right \*  
and teaches his way to the lowly. ^25-8

**25:9** All the paths of the LORD are love and faithfulness \*  
to those who keep his covenant and his testimonies. ^25-9

**25:10** For your Name's sake, O LORD, \*  
forgive my sin, for it is great. ^25-10

**25:11** Who are they who fear the LORD? \*  
he will teach them the way that they should choose. ^25-11

**25:12** They shall dwell in prosperity, \*  
and their offspring shall inherit the land. ^25-12

**25:13** The LORD is a friend to those who fear him \*  
and will show them his covenant. ^25-13

**25:14** My eyes are ever looking to the LORD, \*  
for he shall pluck my feet out of the net. ^25-14

**25:15** Turn to me and have pity on me, \*  
for I am left alone and in misery. ^25-15

**25:16** The sorrows of my heart have increased; \*  
bring me out of my troubles. ^25-16

**25:17** Look upon my adversity and misery \*  
and forgive me all my sin. ^25-17

**25:18** Look upon my enemies, for they are many, \*  
and they bear a violent hatred against me. ^25-18

[p. 616] ^pg616

**25:19** Protect my life and deliver me; \*  
let me not be put to shame, for I have trusted in you. ^25-19

**25:20** Let integrity and uprightness preserve me, \*  
for my hope has been in you. ^25-20

**25:21** Deliver Israel, O God, \*  
out of all his troubles. ^25-21
