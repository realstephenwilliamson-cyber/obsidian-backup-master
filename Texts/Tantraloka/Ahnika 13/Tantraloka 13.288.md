## Mūla

वैष्णवादौ हि या भक्तिर्नासौ केवलतः शिवात् ।
शिवो भवति तत्रैष कारणं न तु केवलः ॥ ^mula

vaiṣṇavādau hi yā bhaktirnāsau kevalataḥ śivāt |
śivo bhavati tatraiṣa kāraṇaṃ na tu kevalaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 09 - Chapter Thirteen, part 5#^pg323|p. 301]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=323>))
>
> The devotion of the followers of Viṣnu and the like, who are devoid of (any) contact at all with the great accomplishment (mahāsiddhi), which is identity with Śiva (śivībhāva), does not come from Śiva, who is aloof (from all Karma and the like, to which those gods are subject). (with 13.287cd–288ab)
>
> Although the cause there (of their devotion) is indeed Śiva, it is not (Śiva Himself directly who is) aloof (from all bondage) and stainless. Rather, it is (Śiva) who, assuming a limited (divine form such as Viṣṇu), depends on Karma and the rest. (with 13.288cd–289ab)

*Lead-in:* ननु समनन्तरमेवेदमुक्तं यच्छैव्या शक्त्यानधिष्ठितं न किञ्चिदपि स्यादिति, तदिदानीं वैष्णवादौ भक्तिः शिवान्नेति कथमिवाभिधीयते? -- इत्याशङ्क्याह --

> शिवो भवति तत्रैष कारणं न तु केवलः ॥
> निर्मलश्चापि तु प्राप्तावच्छित्कर्माद्यपेक्षकः ।

तत्रेति विष्ण्वादिविषयायां भक्तौ केवलत्वं निर्मलत्वं च व्यतिरेचयितुमपि त्वित्याद्युक्तम् । प्राप्तावच्छिदिति विष्ण्वादिरूपोपग्रहावच्छिन्नतया परिगृहीतसङ्कोचकलङ्कः -- इत्यर्थः ॥ २८८ ॥
