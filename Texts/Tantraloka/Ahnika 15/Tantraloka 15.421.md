## Mūla

प्रथयंश्चिद्गुणीभावाच्छक्तिपातं न विन्दति ।
उक्तं स्वच्छन्दतन्त्रे तद्दीक्षितोऽपि न मोक्षभाक् ॥ ^mula

prathayaṃścidguṇībhāvācchaktipātaṃ na vindati |
uktaṃ svacchandatantre taddīkṣito'pi na mokṣabhāk || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 08 - Chapter Fifteen, part 7#^pg278|p. 260]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=278>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 15.423cd–424ab.
>
> One who, although he has money, is consumed by greed and the like, perceiving that external (matters) are the most important, consciousness takes second place, and so he does not receive a descent of the power (of grace). (with 15.420cd–421ab)
>
> It is said in the Svacchandatantra that ‘even though he is initiated, he is not liberated. (with 15.421cd)
>
> Surely (one may ask), what fruit (does a miser) get from the rites performed in the course of (his) initiation? (with 15.421ef)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* इति । ननु एतावतैव शक्तिपातं न विन्दति इत्यत्र किं प्रमाणम्?-- इत्याशङ्क्य आह--

> उक्तं स्वच्छन्दतन्त्रे तद्दीक्षितोऽपि न मोक्षभाक् ॥

इह तावत् कृतमकृतं न भवतीति अविवादः । तत् सलोभादेर्दीक्षायां कृतस्य कर्मणः किं फलं स्यात् ?-- इत्याशङ्कते--

> ननु यत्तस्य दीक्षायां कृतं कर्मास्य किं फलम् ।
