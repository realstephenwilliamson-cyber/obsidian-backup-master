## Chapter 74

**74:1** A *maskil* of Asaph.  
Why, O God, do You forever reject us,  
do You fume in anger at the flock that You tend? ^74-1

**74:2** Remember the community You made Yours long ago,  
Your very own tribe that You redeemed,  
Mount Zion, where You dwell. ^74-2

**74:3** Bestir Yourself[^1] because of the perpetual tumult,[^2]  
all the outrages of the enemy in the sanctuary. ^74-3

**74:4** Your foes roar inside Your meeting-place;  
they take their signs for true signs. ^74-4

**74:5** [^3]It is like men wielding axes  
against a gnarled tree; ^74-5

**74:6** with hatchet and pike  
they hacked away at its carved work. ^74-6

**74:7** They made Your sanctuary go up in flames;  
they brought low in dishonor the dwelling-place of Your presence. ^74-7

**74:8** They resolved, “Let us destroy them altogether!”  
They burned all God’s tabernacles in the land. ^74-8

**74:9** No signs appear for us;  
there is no longer any prophet;  
no one among us knows for how long. ^74-9

**74:10** Till when, O God, will the foe blaspheme,  
will the enemy forever revile Your name? ^74-10

**74:11** Why do You hold back Your hand, Your right hand?  
Draw it out of Your bosom![^4] ^74-11

**74:12** O God, my King from of old,  
who brings deliverance throughout the land; ^74-12

**74:13** it was You who drove back the sea with Your might,  
who smashed the heads of the monsters in the waters; ^74-13

**74:14** it was You who crushed the heads of Leviathan,  
who left him as food for the denizens of the desert;[^5] ^74-14

**74:15** it was You who released springs and torrents,  
who made mighty rivers run dry; ^74-15

**74:16** the day is Yours, the night also;  
it was You who set in place the orb of the sun; ^74-16

**74:17** You fixed all the boundaries of the earth;  
summer and winter—You made them. ^74-17

**74:18** Be mindful of how the enemy blasphemes the LORD,  
how base people revile Your name. ^74-18

**74:19** Do not deliver Your dove to the wild beast;  
do not ignore forever the band of Your lowly ones. ^74-19

**74:20** Look to the covenant!  
For the dark places of the land are full of the haunts of lawlessness. ^74-20

**74:21** Let not the downtrodden turn away disappointed;  
let the poor and needy praise Your name. ^74-21

**74:22** Rise, O God, champion Your cause;  
be mindful that You are blasphemed by base men all day long. ^74-22

**74:23** Do not ignore the shouts of Your foes,  
the din of Your adversaries that ascends all the time. ^74-23

[^1]: Lit. “Lift up Your feet.”
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^5]: Or “seafaring men”; meaning of Heb. uncertain.
