# Sirach 19 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Sirach 18|← Sirach 18]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Sirach 20|Sirach 20 →]]

**19:1** The one who does this^[Heb: Gk *A worker who is a drunkard*] will not become rich; one who despises small things will fail little by little. ^19-1

**19:2** Wine and women lead intelligent men astray, and the man who consorts with prostitutes is reckless. ^19-2

**19:3** Decay and worms will take possession of him, and the reckless person will be snatched away. ^19-3

## Against loose talk

**19:4** One who trusts quickly has a shallow mind, and one who sins does wrong to himself. ^19-4

**19:5** One who rejoices in wickedness^[Other ancient authorities read *heart*] will be condemned,^[Other ancient authorities add *but one who withstands pleasures crowns his life.6One who controls the tongue will live without strife,*] ^19-5

**19:6** but one who hates discussion lacks understanding. ^19-6

**19:7** Never repeat a conversation, and you will lose nothing at all. ^19-7

**19:8** With friend or foe do not report it, and unless it would be a sin for you do not reveal it, ^19-8

**19:9** for someone may have heard you and watched you and in time will hate you. ^19-9

**19:10** Have you heard something? Let it die with you. Be brave; it will not make you burst! ^19-10

**19:11** Having heard something, the fool suffers birth pangs like a woman in labor with a child. ^19-11

**19:12** Like an arrow stuck in a person’s thigh, so is gossip in the belly of a fool. ^19-12

**19:13** Question a friend; perhaps he did not do it; or if he did, so that he may not do it again. ^19-13

**19:14** Question a neighbor; perhaps he did not say it; or if he said it, so that he may not repeat it. ^19-14

**19:15** Question a friend, for often it is slander; so do not believe every word. ^19-15

**19:16** A person may make a slip without intending it. Who has not sinned with his tongue? ^19-16

**19:17** Question your neighbor before you threaten him, and make room for the law of the Most High.^[Other ancient authorities add *and do not be angry.18The fear of the Lord is the beginning of acceptance, and wisdom obtains his love. 19The knowledge of the Lord’s commandments is life- giving discipline, and those who do what is pleasing to him enjoy the fruit of the tree of immortality.*] ^19-17

## True and false wisdom

**19:20** The whole of wisdom is fear of the Lord, and in all wisdom there is the fulfillment of the law.^[Other ancient authorities add *and the knowledge of his omnipotence.21When a slave says to his master, “I will not act as you wish,” even if later he does it, he angers the one who supports him.*] ^19-20

**19:22** The knowledge of wickedness is not wisdom, nor is there prudence in the counsel of sinners. ^19-22

**19:23** There is a cleverness that is detestable, and there is a fool who merely lacks wisdom. ^19-23

**19:24** Better are the God-fearing who lack understanding than the highly intelligent who transgress the law. ^19-24

**19:25** There is a cleverness that is exact but unjust, and there are people who abuse favors to gain a verdict.^[Other ancient authorities add *and the one who justifies is wise in judgment.*] ^19-25

**19:26** There are villains bowed down in mourning, but inwardly they are full of deceit. ^19-26

**19:27** They hide their faces and pretend not to hear, but when no one notices, they will take advantage of you. ^19-27

**19:28** Even if lack of strength keeps them from sinning, they will nevertheless do evil when they find the opportunity. ^19-28

**19:29** People will be known by their appearances, and sensible people will be known when first met face to face. ^19-29

**19:30** People’s attire and hearty laughter and the way they walk proclaim things about them. ^19-30
