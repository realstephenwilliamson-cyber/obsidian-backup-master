# Psalm 59

← [[BCP Psalm 058]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 060]] →

*Eripe me de inimicis* · Book Two · Eleventh Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 059]]

**59:1** Rescue me from my enemies, O God; \*  
protect me from those who rise up against me. ^59-1

**59:2** Rescue me from evildoers \*  
and save me from those who thirst for my blood. ^59-2

**59:3** See how they lie in wait for my life,  
how the mighty gather together against me; \*  
not for any offense or fault of mine, O LORD. ^59-3

[p. 666] ^pg666

**59:4** Not because of any guilt of mine \*  
they run and prepare themselves for battle. ^59-4

**59:5** Rouse yourself, come to my side, and see; \*  
for you, LORD God of hosts, are Israel's God. ^59-5

**59:6** Awake, and punish all the ungodly; \*  
show no mercy to those who are faithless and evil. ^59-6

**59:7** They go to and fro in the evening; \*  
they snarl like dogs and run about the city. ^59-7

**59:8** Behold, they boast with their mouths,  
and taunts are on their lips; \*  
"For who," they say, "will hear us?" ^59-8

**59:9** But you, O LORD, you laugh at them; \*  
you laugh all the ungodly to scorn. ^59-9

**59:10** My eyes are fixed on you, O my Strength; \*  
for you, O God, are my stronghold. ^59-10

**59:11** My merciful God comes to meet me; \*  
God will let me look in triumph on my enemies. ^59-11

**59:12** Slay them, O God, lest my people forget; \*  
send them reeling by your might  
and put them down, O Lord our shield. ^59-12

**59:13** For the sins of their mouths, for the words of their lips,  
for the cursing and lies that they utter, \*  
let them be caught in their pride. ^59-13

**59:14** Make an end of them in your wrath; \*  
make an end of them, and they shall be no more. ^59-14

**59:15** Let everyone know that God rules in Jacob, \*  
and to the ends of the earth. ^59-15

**59:16** They go to and fro in the evening; \*  
they snarl like dogs and run about the city. ^59-16

[p. 667] ^pg667

**59:17** They forage for food, \*  
and if they are not filled, they howl. ^59-17

**59:18** For my part, I will sing of your strength; \*  
I will celebrate your love in the morning; ^59-18

**59:19** For you have become my stronghold, \*  
a refuge in the day of my trouble. ^59-19

**59:20** To you, O my Strength, will I sing; \*  
for you, O God, are my stronghold and my merciful God. ^59-20
