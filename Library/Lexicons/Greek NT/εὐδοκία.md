# εὐδοκία

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · noun

**Dodson:** good-will, favor — (a) good-will (good-pleasure), favor, feeling of complacency of God to man, (b) good-pleasure, satisfaction, happiness, delight of men. (Strong's 2107; headword εὐδοκία, ας, ἡ)
