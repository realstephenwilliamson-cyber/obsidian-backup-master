# 1 Timothy 2 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB 1 Timothy 01|← 1 Timothy 1]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB 1 Timothy 03|1 Timothy 3 →]]

Compare: [[1 Timothy 02|BSB and SBLGNT]]

**2:1** I desire therefore, first of all, that supplications, prayers, intercessions, and thanksgivings be made for all men: ^2-1

**2:2** For kings, and for all that are in high station: that we may lead a quiet and a peaceable life in all piety and chastity. ^2-2

**2:3** For this is good and acceptable in the sight of God our Saviour, ^2-3

**2:4** Who will have all men to be saved, and to come to the knowledge of the truth. ^2-4

**2:5** For there is one God, and one mediator of God and men, the man Christ Jesus: ^2-5

**2:6** Who gave himself a redemption for all, a testimony in due times. ^2-6

**2:7** Whereunto I am appointed a preacher and an apostle, (I say the truth, I lie not,) a doctor of the Gentiles in faith and truth. ^2-7

**2:8** I will therefore that men pray in every place, lifting up pure hands, without anger and contention. ^2-8

**2:9** In like manner women also in decent apparel: adorning themselves with modesty and sobriety, not with plaited hair, or gold, or pearls, or costly attire, ^2-9

**2:10** But as it becometh women professing godliness, with good works. ^2-10

**2:11** Let the woman learn in silence, with all subjection. ^2-11

**2:12** But I suffer not a woman to teach, nor to use authority over the man: but to be in silence. ^2-12

**2:13** For Adam was first formed; then Eve. ^2-13

**2:14** And Adam was not seduced; but the woman being seduced, was in the transgression. ^2-14

**2:15** Yet she shall be saved through childbearing; if she continue in faith, and love, and sanctification, with sobriety. ^2-15
