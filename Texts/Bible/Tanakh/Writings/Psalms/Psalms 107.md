## Chapter 107

**107:1** “Praise the LORD, for He is good;  
His steadfast love is eternal!” ^107-1

**107:2** Thus let the redeemed of the LORD say,  
those He redeemed from adversity, ^107-2

**107:3** whom He gathered in from the lands,  
from east and west,  
from the north and from the sea. ^107-3

**107:4** Some lost their way in the wilderness,  
in the wasteland;  
they found no settled place. ^107-4

**107:5** Hungry and thirsty,  
their spirit failed. ^107-5

**107:6** In their adversity they cried to the LORD,  
and He rescued them from their troubles. ^107-6

**107:7** He showed them a direct way  
to reach a settled place. ^107-7

**107:8** Let them praise the LORD for His steadfast love,  
His wondrous deeds for mankind; ^107-8

**107:9** for He has satisfied the thirsty,  
filled the hungry with all good things. ^107-9

**107:10** Some lived in deepest darkness,  
bound in cruel irons, ^107-10

**107:11** because they defied the word of God,  
spurned the counsel of the Most High. ^107-11

**107:12** He humbled their hearts through suffering;  
they stumbled with no one to help. ^107-12

**107:13** In their adversity they cried to the LORD,  
and He rescued them from their troubles. ^107-13

**107:14** He brought them out of deepest darkness,  
broke their bonds asunder. ^107-14

**107:15** Let them praise the LORD for His steadfast love,  
His wondrous deeds for mankind; ^107-15

**107:16** for He shattered gates of bronze,  
He broke their iron bars. ^107-16

**107:17** There were fools who suffered for their sinful way,  
and for their iniquities. ^107-17

**107:18** All food was loathsome to them;  
they reached the gates of death. ^107-18

**107:19** In their adversity they cried to the LORD  
and He saved them from their troubles. ^107-19

**107:20** He gave an order and healed them;  
He delivered them from the pits.[^1] ^107-20

**107:21** Let them praise the LORD for His steadfast love,  
His wondrous deeds for mankind. ^107-21

**107:22** Let them offer thanksgiving sacrifices,  
and tell His deeds in joyful song. ^107-22

**107:23** Others go down to the sea in ships,  
ply their trade in the mighty waters; ^107-23

**107:24** they have seen the works of the LORD  
and His wonders in the deep. ^107-24

**107:25** By His word He raised a storm wind  
that made the waves surge. ^107-25

**107:26** Mounting up to the heaven,  
plunging down to the depths,  
disgorging in their misery, ^107-26

**107:27** they reeled and staggered like a drunken man,  
all their skill to no avail. ^107-27

**107:28** In their adversity they cried to the LORD,  
and He saved them from their troubles. ^107-28

**107:29** He reduced the storm to a whisper;  
the waves were stilled. ^107-29

**107:30** They rejoiced when all was quiet,  
and He brought them to the port they desired. ^107-30

**107:31** Let them praise the LORD for His steadfast love,  
His wondrous deeds for mankind. ^107-31

**107:32** Let them exalt Him in the congregation of the people,  
acclaim Him in the assembly of the elders. ^107-32

**107:33** He turns the rivers into a wilderness,  
springs of water into thirsty land, ^107-33

**107:34** fruitful land into a salt marsh,  
because of the wickedness of its inhabitants. ^107-34

**107:35** He turns the wilderness into pools,  
parched land into springs of water. ^107-35

**107:36** There He settles the hungry;  
they build a place to settle in. ^107-36

**107:37** They sow fields and plant vineyards  
that yield a fruitful harvest. ^107-37

**107:38** He blesses them and they increase greatly;  
and He does not let their cattle decrease, ^107-38

**107:39** after they had been few and crushed  
by oppression, misery, and sorrow. ^107-39

**107:40** He pours contempt on great men  
and makes them lose their way in trackless deserts; ^107-40

**107:41** but the needy He secures from suffering,  
and increases their families like flocks. ^107-41

**107:42** The upright see it and rejoice;  
the mouth of all wrongdoers is stopped. ^107-42

**107:43** The wise man will take note of these things;  
he will consider the steadfast love of the LORD. ^107-43

[^1]: Viz., of death.
