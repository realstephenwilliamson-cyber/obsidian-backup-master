## Mūla

तथाहि मानसं यत्नं तावत्समधितिष्ठति ।
अहंरूढ्या परे देहे यावत्स्याद् बुद्धिसञ्चरः ॥ ^mula

tathāhi mānasaṃ yatnaṃ tāvatsamadhitiṣṭhati |
ahaṃrūḍhyā pare dehe yāvatsyād buddhisañcaraḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 06 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 4#^pg145|p. 128]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=145>))
>
> (The yogi) takes as his support (and governs) the exertion (of his) mind (manas) to the extent that is required, so that by the firm rooting of (his) ego (ahaṁ) in the other body, (his) intellect (buddhi) is transferred into it in such a way that the aggregate of (his) vital breaths that depends on it is also transferred (with it), so that thereby the aggregate of senses awaken in the other body. (with 28.296–297)
