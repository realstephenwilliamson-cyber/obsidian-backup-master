## Chapter 8

**8:1** For the leader; on the *gittith*.[^1] A psalm of David. ^8-1

**8:2** O LORD, our Lord,  
How majestic is Your name throughout the earth,  
You who have covered the heavens with Your splendor![^2] ^8-2

**8:3** From the mouths of infants and sucklings  
You have founded strength on account of Your foes,  
to put an end to enemy and avenger.[^3] ^8-3

**8:4** When I behold Your heavens, the work of Your fingers,  
the moon and stars that You set in place, ^8-4

**8:5** what is man that You have been mindful of him,  
mortal man that You have taken note of him, ^8-5

**8:6** that You have made him little less than divine,[^4]  
and adorned him with glory and majesty; ^8-6

**8:7** You have made him master over Your handiwork,  
laying the world at his feet, ^8-7

**8:8** sheep and oxen, all of them,  
and wild beasts, too; ^8-8

**8:9** the birds of the heavens, the fish of the sea,  
whatever travels the paths of the seas. ^8-9

**8:10** O LORD, our Lord, how majestic is Your name throughout the earth! ^8-10

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain; or “You whose splendor is celebrated all over the heavens!”
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^4]: Or “the angels.”
