## Mūla

संकेते पूर्वपूर्वांशमज्जने प्रतिभाभिदः ।
आद्योद्रेकमहत्त्वेऽपि प्रतिभात्मनि निष्ठिताः ॥ ^mula

saṃkete pūrvapūrvāṃśamajjane pratibhābhidaḥ |
ādyodrekamahattve'pi pratibhātmani niṣṭhitāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 03 - Chapter Eleven, part 2#^pg89|p. 69]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=89>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 11.78.
>
> Degrees of creative intuition (pratibhā) (develop) as each (lower) prior aspect of (a linguistic) convention (saṁketa) (operating in relation to objectivity) merges into the one that follows after (at a higher level of subjectivity). (This process continues up to) the primal (ādya) phonemes which, inwardly merged (within consciousness), are (the energies of its pure reflective awareness), free of Māyā. (with 11.77cd–78ab)
>
> Those who are well-established in this creative intuition (pratibhā) which, abounding with (this) primal (phonemic consciousness), is great, certainly attain eloquence and poetic genius in all respects. (with 11.78cd–79ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* अत एव सर्वतोमुखं कवित्वं वक्तृत्वादावप्रतिहतः प्रसरोऽपि कदाचिद्भवेत् -- इत्याह --

> आद्योद्रेकमहत्त्वेऽपि प्रतिभात्मनि निष्ठिताः ॥
> ध्रुवं कवित्ववक्तृत्वशालितां यान्ति सर्वतः ।

"आद्योद्रेकमहत्त्वे"इति, "आद्येन"प्राथमिकेन "उद्रेकेण"उच्छलत्तया महत्त्वं यस्य तस्मिन् परसंविद्धामसविधवर्तिनि -- इत्यर्थः ॥ ७८ ॥
