## Mūla

अनुग्राह्यास्त्वया शिष्याः शिवशक्तिप्रचोदिताः ।
उक्तं ज्ञानोत्तरे चैतद् ब्राह्मणाः क्षत्रिया विशः ॥ ^mula

anugrāhyāstvayā śiṣyāḥ śivaśaktipracoditāḥ |
uktaṃ jñānottare caitad brāhmaṇāḥ kṣatriyā viśaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 14 - Chapter Twenty-Three#^pg323|p. 303]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=323>))
>
> (The teacher should address Śiva saying): “(these are) the disciples who, impelled by Śiva’s power, are worthy of being graced by You.” (with 23.17cd–20ab)
>
> “And that is said in the Jāāānottara (where we read that): ‘Brahmins, kṣatriyas, vaiśyas,” eunuchs, women, śūdras, as well as others who wish (to receive) that (Śaiva teaching), should not be examined (as to their competence) when (they are given) initiation; (rather that) should be examined when knowledge is being given (to them).’ The teacher who is said to be the rooted in knowledge (jñānamūla) should encourage one who performs the seven sacrifices (saptasatrin) (regardless of other considerations). (with 23.20cd–22ab)
