# gorakṣaguṭikā

**Catalog number:** M00291  
**Author:** gorakṣanātha  
**Manuscript:** NGMCP 2120 reel number: A62/3  
**Published:** Muktabodha Indological Research Institute, India

## Description

The Goraksaguṭikā (the Magic Pill of Gorakṣa) is attributed to Gorakṣa (alias Gorakhnātha) the famous founder of Haṭha Yoga. The colophons state that it is part of the Kalpadrumavivekinii and that it is a commentary (bhyāsa) on Yoga. It is framed in the format of a Tantra as a dialogue between śiva and Gaurī. Although Gorakṣanātha was a yogi, as are his followers the Naatha Yogis today, and so should not be concerned with ritual practice, in actual fact the Nātha tradition has developed an extensive liturgy centered on the worship of its Gurus. This short tract contains a series of hymns (stotra) praising Gorakṣanātha and myths that narrate their origins.
1) The first chapter (taraṅga) is dedicated to the birth and exploits of Gorakṣanātha. The following eight chapters contain
2) Gorakṣadvādaśanāmastotra
3) Gorakṣakavaca
4) Gorakṣahṛdaya
5) Gorakṣanāthakīlakoddhāraṇa
6) Gorakṣanāthapaṃcaratnastavarāja
7) Rakṣāṣṭakastotra
8) Gorakṣanāthāṣṭottaraśatanāmastotra
9) Gorakṣanāthasahasranāmastotra
