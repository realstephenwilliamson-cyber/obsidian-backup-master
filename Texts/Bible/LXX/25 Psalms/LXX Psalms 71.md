# LXX Psalms 71

← [[LXX Psalms 70]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 72]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 072]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 072]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**71:1** For Solomon. O God, give thy judgment to the king, and thy righteousness to the king's son;  ^71-1

**71:2** *that he may* judge thy people with righteousness, and thy poor with judgment.  ^71-2

**71:3** Let the mountains and the hills raise peace to thy people:  ^71-3

**71:4** he shall judge the poor of the people in righteousness, and save the children of the needy; and shall bring low the false accuser.  ^71-4

**71:5** And he shall continue as long as the sun, and before the moon for ever.  ^71-5

**71:6** He shall come down as rain upon a fleece; and as drops falling upon the earth.  ^71-6

**71:7** In his days shall righteousness spring up; and abundance of peace till the moon be removed.  ^71-7

**71:8** And he shall have dominion from sea to sea, and from the river to the ends of the earth.  ^71-8

**71:9** The Ethiopians shall fall down before him; and his enemies shall lick the dust.  ^71-9

**71:10** The kings of Tharsis, and the isles, shall bring presents: the kings of the Arabians and Saba shall offer gifts.  ^71-10

**71:11** And all kings shall worship him; all the Gentiles shall serve him.  ^71-11

**71:12** For he has delivered the poor from the oppressor; and the needy who had no helper.  ^71-12

**71:13** He shall spare the poor and needy, and shall deliver the souls of the needy.  ^71-13

**71:14** He shall redeem their souls from usury and injustice: and their name *shall be* precious before him.  ^71-14

**71:15** And he shall live, and there shall be given him of the gold of Arabia: and *men* shall pray for him continually; *and* all the day shall they praise him.  ^71-15

**71:16** There shall be an establishment on the earth on the tops of the mountains: the fruit thereof shall be exalted above Libanus, and they of the city shall flourish as grass of the earth.  ^71-16

**71:17** Let his name be blessed for ever: his name shall endure longer than the sun: and all the tribes of the earth shall be blessed in him: all nations shall call him blessed.[^1]  ^71-17

**71:18** Blessed is the Lord God of Israel, who alone does wonders.  ^71-18

**71:19** And blessed is his glorious name for ever, even for ever and ever: and all the earth shall be filled with his glory. So be it, so be it.  ^71-19

**71:20** The hymns of David the son of Jessæ are ended.  ^71-20

[^1]: 71:17 *Gr.* before.
