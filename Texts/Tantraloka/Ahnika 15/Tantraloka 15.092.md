## Mūla

हृत्पद्मदलसन्धीनामुपसन्दोहकाष्टता ।
जालन्धरं च नैपालं कश्मीरा गर्गिका हरः ॥ ^mula

hṛtpadmadalasandhīnāmupasandohakāṣṭatā |
jālandharaṃ ca naipālaṃ kaśmīrā gargikā haraḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 03 - Chapter Fifteen, part 2#^pg81|p. 63]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=81>))
>
> The eight upasaṁdohas are the junctures between the petals of the (lotus of) the heart. They are Jālandhara, Nepal, Kashmir, Gargikā, Hara, which is in the direction (of the land) of the barbarians (mleccha), Dvāravrtti, Kurukṣetra and Kheṭaka.' (with 15.92–93ab)
