## Mūla

साङ्गा चैव परा शक्तिर्न्यासः प्रोक्तोऽथ षड्विधः ।
यामलोऽयमतो न्यासः सर्वसिद्धिप्रसिद्धये ॥ ^mula

sāṅgā caiva parā śaktirnyāsaḥ prokto 'tha ṣaḍvidhaḥ |
yāmalo 'yamato nyāsaḥ sarvasiddhiprasiddhaye || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, October 2024 – December 2024
> p. 27 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=26>))
>
> Now, the deposition is said to be sixfold. (It consists of) the Icon (of Mālinī) with its faces, power, the three Vidyās, the group of eight beginning with Aghorī, the five limbs (of the Vidyā) and the supreme power with (her) limbs. Thus, this double (*yāmala*) deposition (first male then female) serves to attain all accomplishments (*siddhi*).[^46] (with 8.44–45)

[^46]: See TĀ 15/254-255.
