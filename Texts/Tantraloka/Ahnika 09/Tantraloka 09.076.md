## Mūla

ज्ञानं चाक्षुषरश्मीनां तथाभावे सरत्यपि ।
स एव च मलो मूर्तः किं ज्ञानेन न वेद्यते ॥ ^mula

jñānaṃ cākṣuṣaraśmīnāṃ tathābhāve saratyapi |
sa eva ca malo mūrtaḥ kiṃ jñānena na vedyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 04 - Chapter Nine, part 2#^pg96|p. 79]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=96>))
>
> (Impurity), whether it has a form or not, cannot veil noetic consciousness (jñāna), which (is formless), even though (the fettered soul) continues to transmigrate (saraty api). That is so with the rays of vision (that can be covered by a veil). (with 9.75cd–76ab)
>
> That same Impurity has a form (and does not). Why is it not perceived by consciousness (jñāna) that is all-pervasive? Thus, why should not everyone be omniscient? (with 9.76cd–77ab)
