## Chapter 136

**136:1** Praise the LORD; for He is good,  
His steadfast love is eternal. ^136-1

**136:2** Praise the God of gods,  
His steadfast love is eternal. ^136-2

**136:3** Praise the Lord of lords,  
His steadfast love is eternal; ^136-3

**136:4** Who alone works great marvels,  
His steadfast love is eternal; ^136-4

**136:5** Who made the heavens with wisdom,  
His steadfast love is eternal; ^136-5

**136:6** Who spread the earth over the water,  
His steadfast love is eternal; ^136-6

**136:7** Who made the great lights,  
His steadfast love is eternal; ^136-7

**136:8** the sun to dominate the day,  
His steadfast love is eternal; ^136-8

**136:9** the moon and the stars to dominate the night,  
His steadfast love is eternal; ^136-9

**136:10** Who struck Egypt through their first-born,  
His steadfast love is eternal; ^136-10

**136:11** and brought Israel out of their midst,  
His steadfast love is eternal; ^136-11

**136:12** with a strong hand and outstretched arm,  
His steadfast love is eternal; ^136-12

**136:13** Who split apart the Sea of Reeds,  
His steadfast love is eternal; ^136-13

**136:14** and made Israel pass through it,  
His steadfast love is eternal; ^136-14

**136:15** Who hurled Pharaoh and his army into the Sea of Reeds,  
His steadfast love is eternal; ^136-15

**136:16** Who led His people through the wilderness,  
His steadfast love is eternal; ^136-16

**136:17** Who struck down great kings,  
His steadfast love is eternal; ^136-17

**136:18** and slew mighty kings—  
His steadfast love is eternal; ^136-18

**136:19** Sihon, king of the Amorites,  
His steadfast love is eternal; ^136-19

**136:20** Og, king of Bashan—  
His steadfast love is eternal; ^136-20

**136:21** and gave their land as a heritage,  
His steadfast love is eternal; ^136-21

**136:22** a heritage to His servant Israel,  
His steadfast love is eternal; ^136-22

**136:23** Who took note of us in our degradation,  
His steadfast love is eternal; ^136-23

**136:24** and rescued us from our enemies,  
His steadfast love is eternal; ^136-24

**136:25** Who gives food to all flesh,  
His steadfast love is eternal. ^136-25

**136:26** Praise the God of heaven,  
His steadfast love is eternal. ^136-26
