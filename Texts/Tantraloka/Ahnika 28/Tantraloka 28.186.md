## Mūla

शेषं त्वगाधे वार्योघे क्षिपेन्न स्थापयेत्स्थिरम् ।
अथ नैमित्तिकविधिर्यः पुरासूत्रितो मया ॥ ^mula

śeṣaṃ tvagādhe vāryoghe kṣipenna sthāpayetsthiram |
atha naimittikavidhiryaḥ purāsūtrito mayā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 04 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 2#^pg97|p. 80]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=97>))
>
> This is considered (traditionally) to be the rite of the sacred thread, which (is celebrated) equally by (all) the four (levels of initiates). There is no vow higher than this, or what praise can be uttered (of it that can suffice)? (All) the remnant should be thrown into a deep stream of water. One should not make permanent offerings. (with 28.185–186ab)
>
> Now we will talk about the occasional rites I mentioned briefly before. There (on these occasions,) the wise should worship the deity in a special way, and perform the Sacrifice to the Assembly, in accord with the procedure taught previously. (with 28.186cd–187)
