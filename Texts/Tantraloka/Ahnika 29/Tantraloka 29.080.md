## Mūla

क्रमो नाम न कश्चित्स्यात्प्रकाशमयसंविदि ।
चिदभावो हि नास्त्येव तेनाकालं तु तर्पणम् ॥ ^mula

kramo nāma na kaścitsyātprakāśamayasaṃvidi |
cidabhāvo hi nāstyeva tenākālaṃ tu tarpaṇam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 10 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 2#^pg292|p. 275]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=292>))
>
> There is no (temporal) succession of any sort within consciousness, which is Light. (This is so because) consciousness is (never) absent. Thus, the offering of libation is timeless (and has no fixed time).
