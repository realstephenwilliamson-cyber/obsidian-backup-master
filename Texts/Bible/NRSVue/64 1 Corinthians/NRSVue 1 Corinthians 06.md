## Lawsuits among believers

**6:1** When any of you has a grievance against another, do you dare to take it to court before the unrighteous, instead of taking it before the saints? ^6-1

**6:2** Do you not know that the saints will judge the world? And if the world is to be judged by you, are you incompetent to try trivial cases? ^6-2

**6:3** Do you not know that we are to judge angels, to say nothing of ordinary matters? ^6-3

**6:4** If you have ordinary cases, then, do you appoint as judges those who have no standing in the church? ^6-4

**6:5** I say this to your shame. Can it be that there is no one person wise enough to decide between brothers and sisters? ^6-5

**6:6** Instead, brothers and sisters go to court against one another, and this before the unbelievers. ^6-6

**6:7** In fact, to have lawsuits at all with one another is already a defeat for you. Why not rather be wronged? Why not rather be defrauded? ^6-7

**6:8** But you yourselves wrong and defraud—and brothers and sisters at that. ^6-8

**6:9** Do you not know that wrongdoers will not inherit the kingdom of God? Do not be deceived! The sexually immoral, idolaters, adulterers, male prostitutes,^[Meaning of Gk uncertain] men who engage in illicit sex,^[Meaning of Gk uncertain] ^6-9

**6:10** thieves, the greedy, drunkards, revilers, swindlers—none of these will inherit the kingdom of God. ^6-10

**6:11** And this is what some of you used to be. But you were washed, you were sanctified, you were justified in the name of the Lord Jesus Christ^[Other ancient authorities lack *Christ*] and in the Spirit of our God. ^6-11

## Glorify God in body and spirit

**6:12** “All things are permitted for me,” but not all things are beneficial. “All things are permitted for me,” but I will not be dominated by anything. ^6-12

**6:13** “Food is meant for the stomach and the stomach for food,”^[The quotation may extend to the word *other*] and God will destroy both one and the other. The body is meant not for sexual immorality but for the Lord and the Lord for the body. ^6-13

**6:14** And God raised the Lord and will also raise us by his power. ^6-14

**6:15** Do you not know that your bodies are members of Christ? Should I therefore take the members of Christ and make them members of a prostitute? Never! ^6-15

**6:16** Do you not know that whoever is united to a prostitute becomes one body with her? For it is said, “The two shall be one flesh.” ^6-16

**6:17** But anyone united to the Lord becomes one spirit with him. ^6-17

**6:18** Shun sexual immorality!^[Or *prostitution*] Every sin that a person commits is outside the body, but the sexually immoral person^[Or *the one who hires a prostitute*] sins against the body itself. ^6-18

**6:19** Or do you not know that your body is a temple^[Or *sanctuary*] of the Holy Spirit within you, which you have from God, and that you are not your own? ^6-19

**6:20** For you were bought with a price; therefore glorify God in your body. ^6-20
