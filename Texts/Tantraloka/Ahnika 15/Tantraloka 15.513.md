## Mūla

रजतादि तथा संवित्संस्कारे द्विजतान्तरे ।
योनिर्न कारणं तत्र शान्तात्मा द्विज उच्यते ॥ ^mula

rajatādi tathā saṃvitsaṃskāre dvijatāntare |
yonirna kāraṇaṃ tatra śāntātmā dvija ucyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 09 - Chapter Fifteen, part 8#^pg330|p. 312]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=330>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 15.515cd–516ab.
>
> Just as in the course of processing (pāka) the substances (that produce) gold and the like, silver and other (precious metals appear first), similarly in the course of purifying consciousness, the condition of a ‘twice-born’ (appears) as an intermediate stage. (with 15.512cd–513ab)
>
> (Birth in a particular) womb is not the cause there (in that case, whereby one is a Brahmin). It is said that (a true) Brahmin is one who is peaceful by nature (irrespective of his caste). (with 15.513cd)
