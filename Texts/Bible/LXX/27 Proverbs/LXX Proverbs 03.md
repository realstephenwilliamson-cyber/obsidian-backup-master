# LXX Proverbs 03

← [[LXX Proverbs 02]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Proverbs 04]] →

*Proverbs · [[LXX]]*

**3:1** *My* son, forget not my laws; but let thine heart keep my words:  ^3-1

**3:2** for length of existence, and years of life, and peace, shall they add to thee.  ^3-2

**3:3** Let not mercy and truth forsake thee; but bind them about thy neck:  ^3-3

**3:4** so shalt thou find favour: and do thou provide things honest in the sight of the Lord, and of men.[^1][^2]  ^3-4

**3:5** Trust in God with all thine heart; and be not exalted in thine own wisdom.  ^3-5

**3:6** In all thy ways acquaint thyself with her, that she may rightly direct thy paths.[^3]  ^3-6

**3:7** Be not wise in thine own conceit; but fear God, and depart from all evil.  ^3-7

**3:8** Then shall there be health to thy body, and good keeping to thy bones.  ^3-8

**3:9** Honour the Lord with thy just labours, and give him the first of thy fruits of righteousness:  ^3-9

**3:10** that thy storehouses may be completely filled with corn, and that thy presses may burst forth with wine.  ^3-10

**3:11** *My* son, despise not the chastening of the Lord; nor faint when thou art rebuked of him:  ^3-11

**3:12** for whom the Lord loves, he rebukes, and scourges every son whom he receives.  ^3-12

**3:13** Blessed is the man who has found wisdom, and the mortal who knows prudence.  ^3-13

**3:14** For it is better to traffic for her, than for treasures of gold and silver.  ^3-14

**3:15** And she is more valuable than precious stones: no evil thing shall resist her: she is well known to all that approach her, and no precious thing is equal to her in value.  ^3-15

**3:16** For length of existence and years of life are in her right hand; and in her left hand are wealth and glory: 16a out of her mouth proceeds righteousness, and she carries law and mercy upon her tongue.  ^3-16

**3:17** Her ways are good ways, and all her paths are peaceful.  ^3-17

**3:18** She is a tree of life to all that lay hold upon her; and she is *a* secure *help* to all that stay themselves on her, as on the Lord.  ^3-18

**3:19** God by wisdom founded the earth, and by prudence he prepared the heavens.  ^3-19

**3:20** By understanding were the depths broken up, and the clouds dropped water.  ^3-20

**3:21** *My* son, let *them* not pass from *thee*, but keep my counsel and understanding:[^4]  ^3-21

**3:22** that thy soul may live, and that there may be grace round thy neck; 22a and it shall be health to thy flesh, and safety to thy bones:  ^3-22

**3:23** that thou mayest go confidently in peace in all thy ways, and that thy foot may not stumble.  ^3-23

**3:24** For if thou rest, thou shalt be undismayed; and if thou sleep, thou shalt slumber sweetly.[^5]  ^3-24

**3:25** And thou shalt not be afraid of alarm coming upon thee, neither of approaching attacks of ungodly men.  ^3-25

**3:26** For the Lord shall be over all thy ways, and shall establish thy foot that thou be not moved.  ^3-26

**3:27** Forbear not to do good to the poor, whensoever thy hand may have *power* to help *him*.  ^3-27

**3:28** Say not, Come back another time, to-morrow I will give; while thou art able to do *him* good: for thou knowest not what the next day will bring forth.  ^3-28

**3:29** Devise not evil against thy friend, living near thee and trusting in thee.  ^3-29

**3:30** Be not ready to quarrel with a man without a cause, lest he do thee some harm.  ^3-30

**3:31** Procure not the reproaches of bad men, neither do thou covet their ways.  ^3-31

**3:32** For every transgressor is unclean before the Lord; neither does he sit among the righteous.  ^3-32

**3:33** The curse of God is in the houses of the ungodly; but the habitations of the just are blessed.  ^3-33

**3:34** The Lord resists the proud; but he gives grace to the humble.[^6]  ^3-34

**3:35** The wise shall inherit glory; but the ungodly have exalted *their own* dishonour.  ^3-35

[^1]: 3:4 *Alex.*+ 'and write them on the table of thine heart.'
[^2]: 3:4 See Rom. 12. 17.
[^3]: 3:6 *Gr.* divide. See 2 Tim 2. 13.
[^4]: 3:21 See Heb. 2. 1.
[^5]: 3:24 *Gr.* sit down.
[^6]: 3:34 See 1 Pet. 5. 5.
