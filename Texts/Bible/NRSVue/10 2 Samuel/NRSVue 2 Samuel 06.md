# 2 Samuel 6 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue 2 Samuel 05|← 2 Samuel 5]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue 2 Samuel 07|2 Samuel 7 →]]

## David brings the ark to Jerusalem

**6:1** David again gathered all the chosen men of Israel, thirty thousand. ^6-1

**6:2** David and all the people with him set out and went from Baale-judah to bring up from there the ark of God, which is called by the name of the LORD of hosts who is enthroned on the cherubim. ^6-2

**6:3** They carried the ark of God on a new cart and brought it out of the house of Abinadab, which was on the hill. Uzzah and Ahio,^[Or *and his brother*] the sons of Abinadab, were driving the new cart ^6-3

**6:4** with the ark of God,^[Compare Gk: Heb *which was on the hill with the ark of God*] and Ahio^[Or *and his brother*] went in front of the ark. ^6-4

**6:5** David and all the house of Israel were dancing before the LORD with all their might, with songs^[Q ms Gk: MT *fir trees*] and lyres and harps and tambourines and castanets and cymbals. ^6-5

**6:6** When they came to the threshing floor of Nacon, Uzzah reached out his hand to the ark of God and took hold of it, for the oxen lurched. ^6-6

**6:7** The anger of the LORD was kindled against Uzzah, and God struck him there,^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] and he died there beside the ark of God. ^6-7

**6:8** David was angry because the LORD had burst forth with an outburst upon Uzzah, so that place is called Perez-uzzah^[That is, *bursting out against Uzzah*] to this day. ^6-8

**6:9** David was afraid of the LORD that day; he said, “How can the ark of the LORD come into my care?” ^6-9

**6:10** So David was unwilling to take the ark of the LORD into his care in the city of David; instead, David took it to the house of Obed-edom the Gittite. ^6-10

**6:11** The ark of the LORD remained in the house of Obed-edom the Gittite three months, and the LORD blessed Obed-edom and all his household. ^6-11

**6:12** It was told King David, “The LORD has blessed the household of Obed-edom and all that belongs to him because of the ark of God.” So David went and brought up the ark of God from the house of Obed-edom to the city of David with rejoicing, ^6-12

**6:13** and when those who bore the ark of the LORD had gone six paces, he sacrificed an ox and a fatted calf. ^6-13

**6:14** David danced before the LORD with all his might; David was girded with a linen ephod. ^6-14

**6:15** So David and all the house of Israel brought up the ark of the LORD with shouting and with the sound of the trumpet. ^6-15

**6:16** As the ark of the LORD came into the city of David, Michal daughter of Saul looked out of the window and saw King David leaping and dancing before the LORD, and she despised him in her heart. ^6-16

**6:17** They brought in the ark of the LORD and set it in its place, inside the tent that David had pitched for it, and David offered burnt offerings and offerings of well-being before the LORD. ^6-17

**6:18** When David had finished offering the burnt offerings and the offerings of well-being, he blessed the people in the name of the LORD of hosts ^6-18

**6:19** and distributed food among all the people, the whole multitude of Israel, both men and women, to each a cake of bread, a portion of meat,^[Vg: Meaning of Heb uncertain] and a cake of raisins. Then all the people went back to their homes. ^6-19

**6:20** David returned to bless his household. But Michal the daughter of Saul came out to meet David and said, “How the king of Israel honored himself today, uncovering himself today before the eyes of his servants’ maids, as any vulgar fellow might shamelessly uncover himself!” ^6-20

**6:21** David said to Michal, “It was before the LORD, who chose me in place of your father and all his household, to appoint me as prince over Israel, the people of the LORD—I will dance before the LORD. ^6-21

**6:22** I will make myself yet more contemptible than this, and I will be humbled in my own eyes, but by the maids of whom you have spoken, by them I shall be held in honor.” ^6-22

**6:23** And Michal the daughter of Saul had no child to the day of her death. ^6-23
