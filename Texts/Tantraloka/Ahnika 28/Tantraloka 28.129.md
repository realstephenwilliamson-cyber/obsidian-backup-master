## Mūla

पवित्रकविधिः कार्यः शुक्लपक्षे तु सर्वथा ।
पूरणं शक्तियोगेन शक्त्यात्म च सितं दलम् ॥ ^mula

pavitrakavidhiḥ kāryaḥ śuklapakṣe tu sarvathā |
pūraṇaṃ śaktiyogena śaktyātma ca sitaṃ dalam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 04 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 2#^pg85|p. 68]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=85>))
>
> The form (and nature of this sacred thread) ruipa),), taught in many scriptures, the Lord has described in just one word in the venerable Kulāgama (called) Ratnamālā. The procedure concerning the sacred thread, which completes all (ritual) procedures, should be done (sometime) during the moment in time (mentioned) here, and always% during the bright lunar fortnight. (with 28.127cd–129ab)
>
> (A rite is) completed (pūrana) (and made fruitful) by conjoining (yogena) (spiritual) energy (to it). And the white half (of the lunar month) is made of (this spiritual) energy. Thus, it is because the period of time in which the sun moves southwards is of the same kind (as that of the bright lunar fortnight) that it is said to be that (of this) procedure. (with 28.129cd–130ab)
