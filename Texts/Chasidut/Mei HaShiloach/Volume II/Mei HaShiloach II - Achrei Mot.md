## Teaching 3

***And you shalt not pass through any of your offspring to Molekh*:** That is that idolaters worship with this power, such that there is no greater love than that of a father for a child, whereas the idolaters want to show that they are not subservient to this power. But God, may He be blessed, forbade worship through this power with which the idolaters say they have a great power from it by leaving over and delivering this power to their deity. However, God, may He be blessed, marked it in the world, that He wants this love that a father loves his child. And likewise, any idolatry of the idolaters is worshipping the power of love or the power of fear.

**Torah:** [[Torah 29 - Achrei Mot#^18-21|Leviticus 18:21]]
