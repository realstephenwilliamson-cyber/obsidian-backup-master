# John 10 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB John 09|← John 9]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB John 11|John 11 →]]

Compare: [[John 10|BSB and SBLGNT]]

**10:1** Amen, amen I say to you: He that entereth not by the door into the sheepfold, but climbeth up another way, the same is a thief and a robber. ^10-1

**10:2** But he that entereth in by the door is the shepherd of the sheep. ^10-2

**10:3** To him the porter openeth; and the sheep hear his voice: and he calleth his own sheep by name, and leadeth them out. ^10-3

**10:4** And when he hath let out his own sheep, he goeth before them: and the sheep follow him, because they know his voice. ^10-4

**10:5** But a stranger they follow not, but fly from him, because they know not the voice of strangers. ^10-5

**10:6** This proverb Jesus spoke to them. But they understood not what he spoke to them. ^10-6

**10:7** Jesus therefore said to them again: Amen, amen I say to you, I am the door of the sheep. ^10-7

**10:8** All others, as many as have come, are thieves and robbers: and the sheep heard them not. ^10-8

**10:9** I am the door. By me, if any man enter in, he shall be saved: and he shall go in, and go out, and shall find pastures. ^10-9

**10:10** The thief cometh not, but for to steal, and to kill, and to destroy. I am come that they may have life, and may have it more abundantly. ^10-10

**10:11** I am the good shepherd. The good shepherd giveth his life for his sheep. ^10-11

**10:12** But the hireling, and he that is not the shepherd, whose own the sheep are not, seeth the wolf coming, and leaveth the sheep, and flieth: and the wolf catcheth, and scattereth the sheep: ^10-12

**10:13** And the hireling flieth, because he is a hireling: and he hath no care for the sheep. ^10-13

**10:14** I am the good shepherd; and I know mine, and mine know me. ^10-14

**10:15** As the Father knoweth me, and I know the Father: and I lay down my life for my sheep. ^10-15

**10:16** And other sheep I have, that are not of this fold: them also I must bring, and they shall hear my voice, and there shall be one fold and one shepherd. ^10-16

**10:17** Therefore doth the Father love me: because I lay down my life, that I may take it again. ^10-17

**10:18** No man taketh it away from me: but I lay it down of myself, and I have power to lay it down: and I have power to take it up again. This commandment have I received of my Father. ^10-18

**10:19** A dissension rose again among the Jews for these words. ^10-19

**10:20** And many of them said: He hath a devil, and is mad: why hear you him? ^10-20

**10:21** Others said: These are not the words of one that hath a devil: Can a devil open the eyes of the blind? ^10-21

**10:22** And it was the feast of the dedication at Jerusalem: and it was winter. ^10-22

**10:23** And Jesus walked in the temple, in Solomon’s porch. ^10-23

**10:24** The Jews therefore came round about him, and said to him: How long dost thou hold our souls in suspense? If thou be the Christ, tell us plainly. ^10-24

**10:25** Jesus answered them: I speak to you, and you believe not: the works that I do in the name of my Father, they give testimony of me. ^10-25

**10:26** But you do not believe, because you are not of my sheep. ^10-26

**10:27** My sheep hear my voice: and I know them, and they follow me. ^10-27

**10:28** And I give them life everlasting; and they shall not perish for ever, and no man shall pluck them out of my hand. ^10-28

**10:29** That which my Father hath given me, is greater than all: and no one can snatch them out of the hand of my Father. ^10-29

**10:30** I and the Father are one. ^10-30

**10:31** The Jews then took up stones to stone him. ^10-31

**10:32** Jesus answered them: Many good works I have shewed you from my Father; for which of these works do you stone me? ^10-32

**10:33** The Jews answered him: For a good work we stone thee not, but for blasphemy; and because that thou, being a man, maketh thyself God. ^10-33

**10:34** Jesus answered them: Is it not written in your law: I said you are gods? ^10-34

**10:35** If he called them gods, to whom to word of God was spoken, and the scripture cannot be broken; ^10-35

**10:36** Do you say of him whom the Father hath sanctified and sent into the world: Thou blasphemest, because I said, I am the Son of God? ^10-36

**10:37** If I do not the works of my Father, believe me not. ^10-37

**10:38** But if I do, though you will not believe me, believe the works: that you may know and believe that the Father is in me, and I in the Father. ^10-38

**10:39** They sought therefore to take him; and he escaped out of their hands. ^10-39

**10:40** And he went again beyond the Jordan, into that place where John was baptizing first; and there he abode. ^10-40

**10:41** And many resorted to him, and they said: John indeed did no sign. ^10-41

**10:42** But all things whatsoever John said of this man, were true. And many believed in him. ^10-42
