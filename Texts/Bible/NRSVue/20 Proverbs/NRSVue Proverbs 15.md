# Proverbs 15 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Proverbs 14|← Proverbs 14]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Proverbs 16|Proverbs 16 →]]

**15:1** A soft answer turns away wrath, but a harsh word stirs up anger. ^15-1

**15:2** The tongue of the wise adorns knowledge, but the mouths of fools pour out folly. ^15-2

**15:3** The eyes of the LORD are in every place, keeping watch on the evil and the good. ^15-3

**15:4** A gentle tongue is a tree of life, but perverseness in it breaks the spirit. ^15-4

**15:5** A fool despises a parent’s instruction, but the one who heeds admonition is prudent. ^15-5

**15:6** In the house of the righteous there is much treasure, but trouble befalls the income of the wicked. ^15-6

**15:7** The lips of the wise spread knowledge; not so the minds of fools. ^15-7

**15:8** The sacrifice of the wicked is an abomination to the LORD, but the prayer of the upright is his delight. ^15-8

**15:9** The way of the wicked is an abomination to the LORD, but he loves the one who pursues righteousness. ^15-9

**15:10** There is severe discipline for one who forsakes the way; one who hates a rebuke will die. ^15-10

**15:11** Sheol and Abaddon lie open before the LORD; how much more human hearts! ^15-11

**15:12** Scoffers do not like to be rebuked; they will not go to the wise. ^15-12

**15:13** A glad heart makes a cheerful countenance, but by sorrow of heart the spirit is broken. ^15-13

**15:14** The mind of one who has understanding seeks knowledge, but the mouths of fools feed on folly. ^15-14

**15:15** All the days of the poor are hard, but a cheerful heart has a continual feast. ^15-15

**15:16** Better is a little with the fear of the LORD than great treasure and trouble with it. ^15-16

**15:17** Better is a dinner of vegetables where love is than a fatted ox and hatred with it. ^15-17

**15:18** Those who are hot-tempered stir up strife, but those who are slow to anger calm contention. ^15-18

**15:19** The way of the lazy is overgrown with thorns, but the path of the upright is a level highway. ^15-19

**15:20** A wise child makes a glad father, but the foolish despise their mothers. ^15-20

**15:21** Folly is a joy to one who has no sense, but a person of understanding walks straight ahead. ^15-21

**15:22** Without counsel, plans go wrong, but with many advisers they succeed. ^15-22

**15:23** To make an apt answer is a joy to anyone, and a word in season, how good it is! ^15-23

**15:24** For the wise the path of life leads upward, in order to avoid Sheol below. ^15-24

**15:25** The LORD tears down the house of the proud but maintains the widow’s boundaries. ^15-25

**15:26** Evil plans are an abomination to the LORD, but gracious words are pure. ^15-26

**15:27** Those greedy for unjust gain make trouble for their households, but those who hate bribes will live. ^15-27

**15:28** The mind of the righteous ponders how to answer, but the mouth of the wicked pours out evil. ^15-28

**15:29** The LORD is far from the wicked, but he hears the prayer of the righteous. ^15-29

**15:30** The light of the eyes rejoices the heart, and good news refreshes the body. ^15-30

**15:31** The ear that heeds wholesome admonition will lodge among the wise. ^15-31

**15:32** Those who ignore instruction despise themselves, but those who heed admonition gain understanding. ^15-32

**15:33** The fear of the LORD is instruction in wisdom, and humility goes before honor. ^15-33
