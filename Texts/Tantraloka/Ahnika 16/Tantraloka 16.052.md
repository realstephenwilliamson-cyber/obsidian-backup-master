## Mūla

परोक्षेऽपि पशावेवं विधिः स्याद्योजनं प्रति ।
प्रवेशितो यागभुवि हतस्तत्रैव साधितः ॥ ^mula

parokṣe'pi paśāvevaṃ vidhiḥ syādyojanaṃ prati |
praveśito yāgabhuvi hatastatraiva sādhitaḥ || ^iast

> The procedure that in this way serves to conjoin a fettered soul who is not present (to a particular reality level or Śiva) is also the same. (with 16.52ab)
>
> 8) The sacrificial animal (vīrapaśu) that has been introduced into the sacrificial surface, killed, (its union with iva) accomplished (sādhita) (with yoga and Mantras) there itself, and is pleasing to the circle (of goddesses) (cakrajuṣta) (to whom it is offered as their sacrificial meal), is said to be ‘heroic’. (with 16.52cd–53ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* एतदेव परोक्षदीक्षायामप्यतिदिशति--

> परोक्षेऽपि पशावेवं विधिः स्याद्योजनं प्रति ।
> प्रवेशितो यागभुवि हतस्तत्रैव साधितः ॥
> चक्रजुष्टश्च तत्रैव स वीरपशुरुच्यते ।

तत्रैवेति--यागभुवि । जुष्टः उपभुक्तः ॥
