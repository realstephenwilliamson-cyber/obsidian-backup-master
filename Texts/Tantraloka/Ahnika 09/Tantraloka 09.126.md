## Mūla

कृषिकर्म मधौ भोगः शरद्यन्या च सा तनुः ।
अनुसन्धातुरेकस्य संभवस्तु यतस्ततः ॥ ^mula

kṛṣikarma madhau bhogaḥ śaradyanyā ca sā tanuḥ |
anusandhāturekasya saṃbhavastu yatastataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 05 - Chapter Nine, part 3#^pg127|p. 110]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=127>))
>
> It is evident here (in this world) also that action and its fruit are present (and experienced) in different bodies. Thus, (for example, the farmer) sows his crops in the spring and reaps them (bhoga) in the autumn, (when his) body differs from (the one he had before). (with 9.125cd–126ab)
>
> Insofar as the subject who links together experience (anusaṁdhātṛ) is one and the same, it is he alone who accrues the varied fruit of that, his past action. (with 9.126cd–127ab)

*Lead-in:* नन्वेवं कृतनाशाकृताभ्यागमचोद्यमापतेत् ? -- इत्याशङ्क्याह --

> अनुसन्धातुरेकस्य संभवस्तु यतस्ततः ॥
> तस्यैव तत्फलं चित्रं कर्म यस्य पुरातनम् ।

येनैव मया प्रागुप्तंं स एवाहमद्य भोक्ष्ये, इत्यनुसंधानैक्यात् कर्मफलयोर्वैयधिकरण्याभावात् न कृतस्य नाशो नापि अकृतस्याभ्यागमः, इति युक्तमुक्तम् -- अन्यान्यदेहस्थे कर्मफले इति ॥ १२६ ॥
