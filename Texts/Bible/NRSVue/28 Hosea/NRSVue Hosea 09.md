# Hosea 9 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Hosea 08|← Hosea 8]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Hosea 10|Hosea 10 →]]

## Punishment for Israel’s sin

**9:1** Do not rejoice, O Israel! Do not exult^[Gk: Heb *To exultation*] as other nations do, for you have prostituted yourself, departing from your God. You have loved a prostitute’s pay on all threshing floors. ^9-1

**9:2** Threshing floor and wine vat shall not feed them, and the new wine shall fail them. ^9-2

**9:3** They shall not remain in the land of the LORD, but Ephraim shall return to Egypt, and in Assyria they shall eat unclean food. ^9-3

**9:4** They shall not pour drink offerings of wine to the LORD, and their sacrifices shall not please him. Such sacrifices shall be like mourners’ bread; all who eat of it shall be defiled, for their bread shall be for their hunger only; it shall not come to the house of the LORD. ^9-4

**9:5** What will you do on the day of appointed festival and on the day of the festival of the LORD? ^9-5

**9:6** For even if they escape destruction, Egypt shall gather them; Memphis shall bury them. Nettles shall possess their precious things of silver;^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] thorns shall be in their tents. ^9-6

**9:7** The days of punishment have come; the days of recompense have come. Israel will cry out,^[Or *will know*] “The prophet is a fool; the man of the spirit is mad!” Because of your great iniquity, your hostility is great. ^9-7

**9:8** The prophet is a sentinel for my God over Ephraim, yet a hunter’s snare is on all his ways and hostility in the house of his God. ^9-8

**9:9** They have deeply corrupted themselves as in the days of Gibeah; he will remember their iniquity; he will punish their sins. ^9-9

**9:10** Like grapes in the wilderness, I found Israel. Like the first fruit on the fig tree, in its first season, I saw your ancestors. But they came to Baal-peor and consecrated themselves to a thing of shame and became detestable like the thing they loved. ^9-10

**9:11** Ephraim’s glory shall fly away like a bird—no birth, no pregnancy, no conception! ^9-11

**9:12** Even if they bring up children, I will bereave them until no one is left. Woe to them indeed when I depart from them! ^9-12

**9:13** Once I saw Ephraim as a young palm planted in a lovely meadow,^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] but now Ephraim must lead out his children for slaughter. ^9-13

**9:14** Give them, O LORD—what will you give? Give them a miscarrying womb and dry breasts. ^9-14

**9:15** Every evil of theirs began at Gilgal; there I came to hate them. Because of the wickedness of their deeds I will drive them out of my house. I will love them no more; all their officials are rebels. ^9-15

**9:16** Ephraim is stricken, their root is dried up, they shall bear no fruit. Even though they give birth, I will kill the cherished offspring of their womb. ^9-16

**9:17** Because they have not listened to him, my God will reject them; they shall become wanderers among the nations. ^9-17
