## Chapter 9

**9:1** When Solomon had finished building the House of the LORD and the royal palace and everything that Solomon had set his heart on constructing, ^9-1

**9:2** the LORD appeared to Solomon a second time, as He had appeared to him at Gibeon. ^9-2

**9:3** The LORD said to him, “I have heard the prayer and the supplication which you have offered to Me. I consecrate this House which you have built and I set My name there forever. My eyes and My heart shall ever be there. ^9-3

**9:4** As for you, if you walk before Me as your father David walked before Me, wholeheartedly and with uprightness, doing all that I have commanded you [and] keeping My laws and My rules, ^9-4

**9:5** then I will establish your throne of kingship over Israel forever, as I promised your father David, saying, ‘Your line on the throne of Israel shall never end.’ ^9-5

**9:6** [But] if you and your descendants turn away from Me and do not keep the commandments [and] the laws which I have set before you, and go and serve other gods and worship them, ^9-6

**9:7** then I will sweep[^1] Israel off the land which I gave them; I will reject[^2] the House which I have consecrated to My name; and Israel shall become a proverb and a byword among all peoples. ^9-7

**9:8** And as for this House, once so exalted,[^3] everyone passing by it shall be appalled and shall hiss.[^4] And when they ask, ‘Why did the LORD do thus to the land and to this House?’ ^9-8

**9:9** they shall be told, ‘It is because they forsook the LORD their God who freed them from the land of Egypt, and they embraced other gods and worshiped them and served them; therefore the LORD has brought all this calamity upon them.’” ^9-9

**9:10** At the end of the twenty years[^5] during which Solomon constructed the two buildings, the LORD’s House and the royal palace— ^9-10

**9:11** since King Hiram of Tyre had supplied Solomon with all the cedar and cypress timber and gold that he required—King Solomon in turn gave Hiram twenty towns in the region of Galilee. ^9-11

**9:12** But when Hiram came from Tyre to inspect the towns that Solomon had given him, he was not pleased with them. ^9-12

**9:13** “My brother,” he said, “what sort of towns are these you have given me?” So they were named the land of Cabul,[^6] as is still the case. ^9-13

**9:14** However, Hiram sent the king one hundred and twenty talents of gold. ^9-14

**9:15** This was the purpose of the forced labor which Solomon imposed: It was to build the House of the LORD, his own palace, the Millo,[^7] and the wall of Jerusalem, and [to fortify] Hazor, Megiddo, and Gezer. ^9-15

**9:16** (Pharaoh king of Egypt had come up and captured Gezer; he destroyed it by fire, killed the Canaanites who dwelt in the town, and gave it as dowry to his daughter, Solomon’s wife.) ^9-16

**9:17** So Solomon fortified Gezer, lower Beth-horon, ^9-17

**9:18** Baalith, and Tamar[^8] in the wilderness, in the land [of Judah], ^9-18

**9:19** and all of Solomon’s garrison towns, chariot towns, and cavalry towns—everything that Solomon set his heart on building in Jerusalem and in the Lebanon, and throughout the territory that he ruled. ^9-19

**9:20** All the people that were left of the Amorites, Hittites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites who were not of the Israelite stock— ^9-20

**9:21** those of their descendants who remained in the land and whom the Israelites were not able to annihilate—of these Solomon made a slave force, as is still the case. ^9-21

**9:22** But he did not reduce any Israelites to slavery; they served, rather, as warriors and as his attendants, officials, and officers, and as commanders of his chariotry and cavalry. ^9-22

**9:23** These were the prefects that were in charge of Solomon’s works and were foremen over the people engaged in the work, who numbered 550.[^9] ^9-23

**9:24** As soon as Pharaoh’s daughter went up from the City of David to the palace that he had built for her, he built the Millo.[^10] ^9-24

**9:25** Solomon used to offer burnt offerings and sacrifices of well-being three times a year on the altar that he had built for the LORD, and he used to offer incense on the one that was before the LORD. And he kept the House in repair.[^11] ^9-25

**9:26** King Solomon also built a fleet of ships at Ezion-geber, which is near Eloth[^12] on the shore of the Sea of Reeds in the land of Edom. ^9-26

**9:27** Hiram sent servants of his with the fleet, mariners who were experienced on the sea, to serve with Solomon’s men. ^9-27

**9:28** They came to Ophir; there they obtained gold in the amount of four hundred and twenty talents, which they delivered to King Solomon. ^9-28

[^1]: Lit. “cut.”
[^2]: Lit. “dismiss from My presence.”
[^3]: Targum and some other ancient versions read “and this House shall become a ruin.”
[^4]: An action performed at the sight of ruin to ward off a like fate from the observer; cf. note at Jer. 18.16.
[^5]: See 6.38–7.1.
[^6]: Perhaps taken to mean “as nothing.”
[^7]: A citadel.
[^8]: So kethib, cf. Ezek. 47.19, 48.28; qere Tadmor.
[^9]: Their names are not listed in the text.
[^10]: A citadel.
[^11]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^12]: Elsewhere called Elath.
