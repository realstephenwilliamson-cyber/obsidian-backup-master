## Mūla

अज्ञो वस्तुत एवेति तत्त्यक्त्वेत्थं विधिं चरेत् ।
न तिरोभावशङ्कात्र कर्तव्या बुद्धिशालिना ॥ ^mula

ajño vastuta eveti tattyaktvetthaṃ vidhiṃ caret |
na tirobhāvaśaṅkātra kartavyā buddhiśālinā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 13 - Chapter Twenty-Two#^pg310|p. 290]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=310>))
>
> Even once a true teacher has been found, if he does not know the state (of the disciple’s) aptitude to receive the knowledge (of the scriptures) (and wants to ascertain it), the disciple is obliged to show him his own competence (to do so). Nevertheless, even if (the disciple) says nothing or says (something) that is contrary (to the teaching) (viparyaya), (the teacher, reflecting that) he is in fact ignorant (by nature) (vastutaḥ), abandoning that (inquiry), should apply the procedure in this way. (with 22.37cd–39ab)
>
> The wise (teacher) should not doubt here (in this case) that (his disciple is) in a state of obscuration (and so is not fit to receive initiation). Obscuration consists of the desire for lower (doctrines) and not reflection (in search of) a means to higher ones. (with 22.39cd–40ab)

## Viveka

> न तिरोभावशङ्कात्र कर्तव्या बुद्धिशालिना ॥
> अधःस्पृक्त्वं तिरोभूतिर्नोर्ध्वोपायविवेचनम् ।

तिरोभूतत्वे हि अस्य ऊर्ध्वोपायविवेके स्पृहैव न भवेत्--इतिभावः ॥
