## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 05 - Chapter Sixteen, part 1#^pg22|p. 2]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=22>))

> A group of five wheels numbering six, eight, twelve, sixteen and twenty-four (spokes, respectively, is taught in the scripture called Śrīpūrva. (Wheels) with thirty-two and sixty-four (spokes) are (described) in the venerable Traisirasamata? The relationship between innumerable wheels is described in the venerable Siddhā and elsewhere. (with 16.2cd–4)
