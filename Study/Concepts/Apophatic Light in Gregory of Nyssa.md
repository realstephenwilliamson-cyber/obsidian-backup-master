# Apophatic Light in Gregory of Nyssa

> [!abstract] Definition
> St. Gregory of Nyssa’s mystical doctrine in *The Life of Moses* (2.162–163), demonstrating that the ultimate vision of the Divine Absolute consists in entering the "radiant darkness" (*gnophos*) where God transcends all discursive knowledge and conceptuality.

## The Three Stages of Moses' Ascent
In *The Life of Moses*:
1. **Light:** Encountering God in the Burning Bush (turning away from earthly delusion).
2. **Cloud:** Moses enters the guiding cloud (moving beyond sensory knowledge toward contemplation).
3. **Darkness:** Moses climbs Mount Sinai into the thick darkness where God was. The higher the mind ascends, the more it realizes that God's essence is beyond comprehension.

## Johannine Harmony
In [[Gospel of Recognition text 2026-08-05|The Gospel of Recognition]]:
- Reconciles [[John 01#^1-5|John 1:5]] (*"The light shines in the darkness, and the darkness has not overcome it"*) with the apophatic tradition.
- Divine darkness is not the absence of light, but an excess of brilliance that blinds the mortal eye, requiring the surrender of intellectual pride.
