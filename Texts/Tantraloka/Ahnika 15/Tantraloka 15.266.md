## Mūla

स शिवः शिवतैवास्य वैश्वरूप्यावभासिता ।
तथाभासनयोगोऽतः स्वरसेनास्य जृम्भते ॥ ^mula

sa śivaḥ śivataivāsya vaiśvarūpyāvabhāsitā |
tathābhāsanayogo'taḥ svarasenāsya jṛmbhate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 05 - Chapter Fifteen, part 4#^pg177|p. 159]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=177>))
>
> This consciousness, which is the Great Light and is (perpetually) expanding, is Śiva. (His) Śiva nature (śivatā) itself is His manifestation as all things. (with 15.265cd–266ab)
>
> Thus, His union (yoga) in this way with the shining (of the Great Light of consciousness and its manifestation as all things) unfolds spontaneously (svarasena). (with 15.266cd)
