## Mūla

सुखहेतौ सुखे चास्य सामान्यादभिसन्धितः ।
निर्विशेषादपि न्याय्या धर्मादिफलभोक्तृता ॥ ^mula

sukhahetau sukhe cāsya sāmānyādabhisandhitaḥ |
nirviśeṣādapi nyāyyā dharmādiphalabhoktṛtā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 05 - Chapter Nine, part 3#^pg122|p. 105]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=122>))
>
> Everyone generally intends to be happy and acquire the cause of happiness. Thus, it makes sense that they should all without exception (wish to) enjoy the fruits of the virtue (dharma) and the like (of others). It is also (everyone’s) common intention not to suffer or acquire a cause of suffering, and so one does not accrue evil actions (adharma) (from others). (with 9.115–116)
