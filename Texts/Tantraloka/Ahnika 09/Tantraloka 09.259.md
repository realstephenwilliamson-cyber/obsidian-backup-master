## Mūla

पुनर्गुणः क्रिया त्वेषा वैयाकरणदर्शने ।
क्रिया करणपूर्वेति व्याप्त्या करणपूर्वकम् ॥ ^mula

punarguṇaḥ kriyā tveṣā vaiyākaraṇadarśane |
kriyā karaṇapūrveti vyāptyā karaṇapūrvakam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 08 - Chapter Nine, part 6#^pg260|p. 243]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=260>))
>
> (According to the Vaiśeṣikas,) walking, raising the hand and the like are the main (forms of) action. Moreover, (they maintain that) perception is a quality (of the soul). The grammarians, however, (rightly) consider this to be action (also). (with 9.258cd–259ab)
>
> (The Naiyāyika’s) view is that perception requires an instrument, because (every) action invariably does so. (However, he maintains) that the act of grasping (ādāna) does not. (This view) is clearly a consequence (of his) blindness! (with 9.259cd–260ab)
