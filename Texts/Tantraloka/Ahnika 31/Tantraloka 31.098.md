## Mūla

शान्तिरूपा कला ह्येषा विद्यारूपा परा भवेत् ।
अपरा तु प्रतिष्ठा स्यान्निवृत्तिस्तु परापरा ॥ ^mula

śāntirūpā kalā hyeṣā vidyārūpā parā bhavet |
aparā tu pratiṣṭhā syānnivṛttistu parāparā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 05 - Chapter Thirty-One, part 2#^pg126|p. 108]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=126>))
>
> She is the (sphere of) energy (kalā) which is Śānta (the Tranquil One). Parā is the (energy of) Vidyā, Aparā is Nivṛtti (Cessation), and Parāparā Pratiṣthā (the Foundation).""
