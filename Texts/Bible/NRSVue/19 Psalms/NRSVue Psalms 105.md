# Psalm 105 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 104|← Psalm 104]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 106|Psalm 106 →]]

## God’s faithfulness to Israel

**105:1** O give thanks to the LORD; call on his name; make known his deeds among the peoples. ^105-1

**105:2** Sing to him, sing praises to him; tell of all his wonderful works. ^105-2

**105:3** Glory in his holy name; let the hearts of those who seek the LORD rejoice. ^105-3

**105:4** Seek the LORD and his strength; seek his presence continually. ^105-4

**105:5** Remember the wonderful works he has done, his miracles and the judgments he has uttered, ^105-5

**105:6** O offspring of his servant Abraham,^[Or *Israel*] children of Jacob, his chosen ones. ^105-6

**105:7** He is the LORD our God; his judgments are in all the earth. ^105-7

**105:8** He is mindful of his covenant forever, of the word that he commanded for a thousand generations, ^105-8

**105:9** the covenant that he made with Abraham, his sworn promise to Isaac, ^105-9

**105:10** which he confirmed to Jacob as a statute, to Israel as an everlasting covenant, ^105-10

**105:11** saying, “To you I will give the land of Canaan as your portion for an inheritance.” ^105-11

**105:12** When they were few in number, of little account and strangers in it, ^105-12

**105:13** wandering from nation to nation, from one kingdom to another people, ^105-13

**105:14** he allowed no one to oppress them; he rebuked kings on their account, ^105-14

**105:15** saying, “Do not touch my anointed ones; do my prophets no harm.” ^105-15

**105:16** When he summoned famine against the land and cut off every supply of bread,^[Heb *staff of bread*] ^105-16

**105:17** he had sent a man ahead of them, Joseph, who had been sold as a slave. ^105-17

**105:18** His feet were hurt with fetters; his neck was put in a collar of iron; ^105-18

**105:19** until what he had said came to pass, the word of the LORD kept testing him. ^105-19

**105:20** The king sent and released him; the ruler of the peoples set him free. ^105-20

**105:21** He made him lord of his house and ruler of all his possessions, ^105-21

**105:22** to instruct^[Gk Syr Jerome: Heb *to bind*] his officials at his pleasure and to teach his elders wisdom. ^105-22

**105:23** Then Israel came to Egypt; Jacob lived as an alien in the land of Ham. ^105-23

**105:24** And the LORD made his people very fruitful and made them stronger than their foes, ^105-24

**105:25** whose hearts he then turned to hate his people, to deal craftily with his servants. ^105-25

**105:26** He sent his servant Moses and Aaron, whom he had chosen. ^105-26

**105:27** They performed his signs among them and miracles in the land of Ham. ^105-27

**105:28** He sent darkness and made the land dark; they rebelled^[Cn Compare Gk Syr: Heb *they did not rebel*] against his words. ^105-28

**105:29** He turned their waters into blood and caused their fish to die. ^105-29

**105:30** Their land swarmed with frogs, even in the chambers of their kings. ^105-30

**105:31** He spoke, and there came swarms of flies and gnats throughout their country. ^105-31

**105:32** He gave them hail for rain and lightning that flashed through their land. ^105-32

**105:33** He struck their vines and fig trees and shattered the trees of their country. ^105-33

**105:34** He spoke, and the locusts came, and young locusts without number; ^105-34

**105:35** they devoured all the vegetation in their land and ate up the fruit of their ground. ^105-35

**105:36** He struck down all the firstborn in their land, the first issue of all their strength. ^105-36

**105:37** Then he brought Israel^[Heb *them*] out with silver and gold, and there was no one among their tribes who stumbled. ^105-37

**105:38** Egypt was glad when they departed, for dread of them had fallen upon it. ^105-38

**105:39** He spread a cloud for a covering and fire to give light by night. ^105-39

**105:40** They asked, and he brought quails and gave them food from heaven in abundance. ^105-40

**105:41** He opened the rock, and water gushed out; it flowed through the desert like a river. ^105-41

**105:42** For he remembered his holy promise and Abraham, his servant. ^105-42

**105:43** So he brought his people out with joy, his chosen ones with singing. ^105-43

**105:44** He gave them the lands of the nations, and they took possession of the wealth of the peoples, ^105-44

**105:45** that they might keep his statutes and observe his laws. Praise the LORD! ^105-45
