## Mūla

स्थानभेदो विचित्रश्च स शास्त्रे संख्ययोज्झितः ।
श्रीवीरावलिहृदये सप्त स्थानानि शक्तिकमलयुगम् ॥ ^mula

sthānabhedo vicitraśca sa śāstre saṃkhyayojjhitaḥ |
śrīvīrāvalihṛdaye sapta sthānāni śaktikamalayugam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 03 - Chapter Fifteen, part 2#^pg87|p. 69]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=87>))
>
> This being so, the innumerable variety of types of (sacred) places (sthānabheda) found in the scriptures, whether internal or external, concern (only) those who desire to have the fruits of this or that assembly (of divine forms) (cakra) (that reside in them). (with 15.101cd–102ab)
>
> According to the Vīrāvalihrdaya, there are seven (sacred) places (sthāna). (They are) Power (śakti), the Two Lotuses, the two cemeteries called the Path of the Gods and the Crossroad, the Lonely (Secret) Place, and the Solitary Tree (śñnyavrkṣa). From the point of view of their etymology, secondary (guṇasthiti) and metaphorical sense (upacāra), (their names all) denote consciousness. The sacrifice that may take place within a mandala (cakra), and within the body presided over by that (consciousness), is external. (with 15.102cd–104ab)
