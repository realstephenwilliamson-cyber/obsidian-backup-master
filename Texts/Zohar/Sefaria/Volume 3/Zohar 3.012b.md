*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Vayikra 26:192–26:199.*

**Vayikra 27:200** [^1] When R. Hizkiah was studying with R. Isaac, they once rose at midnight to study the Torah. R. Isaac discoursed on the verse: “Behold, bless ye the Lord, all ye servants of the Lord, which by night stand in the house of the Lord” (Ps. CXXXIV, l). ‘This verse’, he said, ‘is a tribute to all true believers. And who are the true believers? Those who study the Torah and know how to unify the Holy Name in the fitting manner. ^vayikra-27-200

[^1]: The first 49 lines of the Hebrew text on this Page do not appear in the translation as explained in the previous footnotes.
