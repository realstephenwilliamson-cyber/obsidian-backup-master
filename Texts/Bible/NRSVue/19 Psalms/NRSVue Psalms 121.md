## Assurance of God’s protection

*A Song of Ascents.*

**121:1** I lift up my eyes to the hills—from where will my help come? ^121-1

**121:2** My help comes from the LORD, who made heaven and earth. ^121-2

**121:3** He will not let your foot be moved; he who keeps you will not slumber. ^121-3

**121:4** He who keeps Israel will neither slumber nor sleep. ^121-4

**121:5** The LORD is your keeper; the LORD is your shade at your right hand. ^121-5

**121:6** The sun shall not strike you by day nor the moon by night. ^121-6

**121:7** The LORD will keep you from all evil; he will keep your life. ^121-7

**121:8** The LORD will keep your going out and your coming in from this time on and forevermore. ^121-8
