## Mūla

इत्थमैक्यसमापत्तिदानात्परफलप्रदा ।
साधकस्यापि तत्सिद्धिप्रदमन्त्रैकतां गतम् ॥ ^mula

itthamaikyasamāpattidānātparaphalapradā |
sādhakasyāpi tatsiddhipradamantraikatāṃ gatam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 04 - Chapter Fifteen, part 3#^pg116|p. 98]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=116>))
>
> (Thus) ritual, which comprises the deposition (of Mantras) (nyāsa), worship (arcana) and the rest, grants, even without (cultivating) knowledge and (practicing) Yoga, the supreme fruit (of liberation) because, (performed) in this way, it imparts the realization of oneness. (with 15.154cd–155ab)
>
> For the adept (sādhaka) (who desires worldly benefits) also, the Mantra that grants his accomplishment (siddhi) becomes one with the universe (he contemplates in this way), and (every) obstacle (to its realisation) ceasing (thereby), it quickly bestows its own accomplishment. (with 15.155cd–156ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* न केवलमियं मुमुक्षोरेव अभीष्टं फलं प्रददाति, यावत् बुभुक्षोरपि--इत्याह--

> साधकस्यापि तत्सिद्धिप्रदमन्त्रैकतां गतम् ॥
> विश्वं व्रजदविघ्नत्वं स्वां सिद्धिं शीघ्रमावहेत् ।

व्रजदविघ्नत्वमिति सर्वस्य संविदेकरूपत्वात् ॥
