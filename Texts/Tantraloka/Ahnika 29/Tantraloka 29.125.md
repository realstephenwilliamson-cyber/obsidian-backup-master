## Mūla

तत्रैष सम्प्रदायस्तस्मात्संप्राप्यते ज्ञानम् ।
तदिदमलेख्यं भणितं वक्त्राद्वक्त्रस्थमुक्तयुक्त्या च ॥ ^mula

tatraiṣa sampradāyastasmātsaṃprāpyate jñānam |
tadidamalekhyaṃ bhaṇitaṃ vaktrādvaktrasthamuktayuktyā ca || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 11 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 3#^pg333|p. 316]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=333>))
>
> The Great Lord has said that the Main Wheel is the Mouth of the Yoginī. This tradition (sarnpradāya) is there, and from that knowledge is obtained. (with 29.124cd–125ab)
>
> It is said that this (knowledge) cannot be set to writing, but passes from mouth to mouth, and for the aforestated reason, the mouth is the principal Wheel. How can one’s own consciousness be expressed in writing? (with 29.125cd–126ab)
