## Mūla

मलो नाम किल द्रव्यं चक्षुःस्थपटलादिवत् ।
तद्विहन्त्री क्रिया दीक्षा त्वञ्जनादिककर्मवत् ॥ ^mula

malo nāma kila dravyaṃ cakṣuḥsthapaṭalādivat |
tadvihantrī kriyā dīkṣā tvañjanādikakarmavat || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. I
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 01 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v01 12 - Chapter One, part 11#^pg427|p. 413]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 01.pdf#page=427>))
>
> (The dualist Śaivasiddhāntin maintains that spiritual) Impurity (mala) is a material substance (dravya) (that covers the soul) as does, for example, a cataract the eye, and that initiation is the action that removes it, just as the act of applying collyrium and the like (removes cataracts). Further on we shall refute that (view), which is contrary to both reason and scripture, and explain the (true) condition of (āṇava) Mala, Karma and Māyā. (with 1.239–240)
