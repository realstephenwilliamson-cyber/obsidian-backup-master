## Mūla

उदानवह्नौ विश्रान्तो महानन्दं विभावयेत् ।
तत्र विश्रान्तिमभ्येत्य शाम्यत्यस्मिन्महार्चिषि ॥ ^mula

udānavahnau viśrānto mahānandaṃ vibhāvayet |
tatra viśrāntimabhyetya śāmyatyasminmahārciṣi || ^iast

> (4) Then, intent on devouring (and assimilating into his consciousness) the differentiated manifestation (kalanā) of the flux of the means and object of knowledge, (the yogi), resting in the fire of the Rising Breath (udāna), should contemplate (and generate)? the Great Bliss (Mahānanda). (with 5.47cd–48ab)
>
> Having attained a state of rest there, he becomes tranquil within this great fire. (with 5.48cd)

## Viveka
