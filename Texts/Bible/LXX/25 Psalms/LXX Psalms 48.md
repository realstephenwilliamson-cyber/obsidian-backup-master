# LXX Psalms 48

← [[LXX Psalms 47]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 49]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 049]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 049]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**48:1** For the end, a Psalm for the sons of Core.  ^48-1

**48:2** Hear these words, all ye nations, hearken, all ye that dwell upon the earth:  ^48-2

**48:3** both the sons of mean men, and sons of *great* men; the rich and poor *man* together.[^1]  ^48-3

**48:4** My mouth shall speak of wisdom; and the meditation of my heart shall bring *forth* understanding.  ^48-4

**48:5** I will incline mine ear to a parable: I will open my riddle on the harp.[^2]  ^48-5

**48:6** Wherefore should I fear in the evil day? the iniquity of my heel shall compass me.[^3]  ^48-6

**48:7** They that trust in their strength, and boast themselves in the multitude of their wealth—  ^48-7

**48:8** A brother does not redeem, shall a man redeem? he shall not give to God a ransom for himself,  ^48-8

**48:9** or the price of the redemption of his soul, though he labour for ever,  ^48-9

**48:10** and live to the end, *so* that he should not see corruption.  ^48-10

**48:11** When he shall see wise men dying, the fool and the senseless one shall perish together; and they shall leave their wealth to strangers.  ^48-11

**48:12** And their sepulchres are their houses for ever, *even* their tabernacles to all generations: they have called their lands after their own names.  ^48-12

**48:13** And man being in honour, understands not: he is compared to the senseless cattle, and is like to them.  ^48-13

**48:14** This their way is an offence to them: yet afterwards men will commend their sayings. Pause.  ^48-14

**48:15** They have laid *them* as sheep in Hades; death shall feed on them; and the upright shall have dominion over them in the morning, and their help shall fail in Hades from their glory.  ^48-15

**48:16** But God shall deliver my soul from the power of Hades, when he shall receive me. Pause.  ^48-16

**48:17** Fear not when a man is enriched, and when the glory of his house is increased.  ^48-17

**48:18** For he shall take nothing when he dies; neither shall his glory descend with him.  ^48-18

**48:19** For his soul shall be blessed in his life: he shall give thanks to thee when thou dost well to him.  ^48-19

**48:20** *Yet* he shall go in to the generation of his fathers; he shall never see light.[^4]  ^48-20

**48:21** Man that is in honour, understands not: he is compared to the senseless cattle, and is like them.  ^48-21

[^1]: 48:3 *Gr.* earth-born.
[^2]: 48:5 *Gr.* problem.
[^3]: 48:6 *Gr.* shall.
[^4]: 48:20 *Gr.* as far as.
