## Mūla

तस्यैव भाविविधिवत्तत्त्वपाशवियोजने ।
पुत्रकत्वं स च परे तत्त्वे योज्यस्तु दैशिकैः ॥ ^mula

tasyaiva bhāvividhivattattvapāśaviyojane |
putrakatvaṃ sa ca pare tattve yojyastu daiśikaiḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 08 - Chapter Fifteen, part 7#^pg303|p. 285]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=303>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 15.466cd–467ab.
>
> (Then,) when he has been separated from the fetters of the principles, in accord with the procedure that will be (explained further ahead); he becomes a spiritual son (putraka), and should be conjoined by the teacher to the supreme reality. He alone knows the types of invocations (jāti); the true nature of the repetition (of Mantra) (japa), (the offering of) oblation and the rest. The adept (sāādhaka) who has been consecrated (abhiṣikta) is initially (conjoined with Śiva) by means of the jar of Nirvāṇa, and then subsequently, by the one called Īśvara (to Sadāśiva). At the end of (his Karmic) experience (bhoga), he dissolves into the supreme (reality). (with 15.464–466ab)
