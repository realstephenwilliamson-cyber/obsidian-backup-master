## Mūla

पादाधः पञ्चभूतानी व्याप्त्या द्व्यङ्गुलया न्यसेत् ।
धरातत्त्वं च गुल्फान्तमवादीनि ततः क्रमात् ॥ ^mula

pādādhaḥ pañcabhūtānī vyāptyā dvyaṅgulayā nyaset |
dharātattvaṁ ca gulphāntam avādīni tataḥ kramāt || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, October 2023 – December 2023
> p. 17 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapter 6 (2023).pdf#page=5>))
>
> [^6]*The five (gross) elements (should be deposited) from the soles of the feet,* (each one) extending for two fingers. The Earth (principle) extends up to the end of the ankle (for four fingers) and then (the twenty-three principles) beginning with Water in due order. (They each take up two fingers space) and end six fingers above the navel. [n. 7] (Then) one should think of the group of six principles (extending) from the principle of the individual soul up to the principle of the Force (of limited agency).[^8] (with 6.2–3)

[^6]: MV 6/2a is quoted in TĀv ad 16/146-147ab. MV 6/2cd-6ab are paraphrased and commented in 16/101-105. There Abhinavagupta does not refer to this quarter verse. The reading *tadvattadupariṣṭāt* is corrupt. However, by referring to TĀ 16/102 it is clear that the sense is *nābher ūrdhvam* 'above the navel'.
[^8]: They each occupy a space of three fingers, that is, eighteen in all, and so extend up to the neck. We may safely assume that the principles corresponding to the five kaṣcukas up to Māyā are in the common sequential order extending 'up to the principle of the Force (of limited agency), that is, Puruṣa (individual soul) > Niyati (causal and karmic necessity) > Kāla (time) > Rāga (Attachment) > Vidyā (limited noetic subjectivity) > Kalā (limited agency). However, in the context of the deposition of the Worlds below in MV 6/11ab-18ab corresponding to TĀ 16/114-131, the sequential order of the principles from Puruṣatattva to Māyā are not the same. See there and notes.
