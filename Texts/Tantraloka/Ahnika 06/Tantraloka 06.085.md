## Mūla

वेद्यवेदकसाम्यं तत् सा रात्रिदिनतुल्यता ।
वेद्ये विश्रान्तिरधिका दिनदैर्घ्याय तत्र तु ॥ ^mula

vedyavedakasāmyaṃ tat sā rātridinatulyatā |
vedye viśrāntiradhikā dinadairghyāya tatra tu || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 10 - Chapter Six, part 3#^pg325|p. 310]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=325>))
>
> Sometimes the repose in (outer objective) reality (vastu) and the relish (of consciousness) within oneself are equally long. This equality of the knower (vedaka) and the known (vedya) is equivalent to the equality of Night and Day. (with 6.84cd–85ab)
>
> When (consciousness) rests more in the object, this makes the Day there longer, and rest in one’s own Self shorter. Conversely, when the desire for one’s own nature awakens, the opposite takes place, that is, repose in the object diminishes. (with 6.85cd–86)
