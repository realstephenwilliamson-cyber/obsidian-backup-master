## Chapter 108

**108:1** A song. A psalm of David. ^108-1

**108:2** [^1]My heart is firm, O God;  
I will sing and chant a hymn with all my soul. ^108-2

**108:3** Awake, O harp and lyre!  
I will wake the dawn. ^108-3

**108:4** I will praise You among the peoples, O LORD,  
sing a hymn to You among the nations; ^108-4

**108:5** for Your faithfulness is higher than the heavens;  
Your steadfastness reaches to the sky. ^108-5

**108:6** Exalt Yourself over the heavens, O God;  
let Your glory be over all the earth! ^108-6

**108:7** [^2]That those whom You love may be rescued,  
deliver with Your right hand and answer me. ^108-7

**108:8** God promised in His sanctuary[^3]  
that I would exultingly divide up Shechem,  
and measure the Valley of Sukkoth; ^108-8

**108:9** Gilead and Manasseh would be mine,  
Ephraim my chief stronghold,  
Judah my scepter; ^108-9

**108:10** Moab would be my washbasin;  
on Edom I would cast my shoe;  
I would raise a shout over Philistia. ^108-10

**108:11** Would that I were brought to the bastion!  
Would that I were led to Edom! ^108-11

**108:12** But You have rejected us, O God;  
God, You do not march with our armies. ^108-12

**108:13** Grant us Your aid against the foe,  
for the help of man is worthless. ^108-13

**108:14** With God we shall triumph;  
He will trample our foes. ^108-14

[^1]: With vv. 2–6, cf. Ps. 57.8–12.
[^2]: With vv. 7–14, cf. Ps. 60.7–14.
[^3]: Or “by His holiness.”
