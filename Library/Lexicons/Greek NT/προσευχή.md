# προσευχή

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · noun

**Dodson:** prayer, a place for prayer — (a) prayer (to God), (b) a place for prayer (used by Jews, perhaps where there was no synagogue). (Strong's 4335; headword προσευχή, ῆς, ἡ)
