## Mūla

क्रमेण प्राणचारस्य ग्रास एवोपजायते ।
प्राणग्रासक्रमावाप्तकालसङ्कर्षणस्थितिः ॥ ^mula

krameṇa prāṇacārasya grāsa evopajāyate |
prāṇagrāsakramāvāptakālasaṅkarṣaṇasthitiḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 02 - Chapter Seven#^pg28|p. 10]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=28>))
>
> “In this way, the movement of the vital breath (prāṇacāra) of the yogi whose breathing has been controlled with effort is progressively consumed (grāsa).” (with 7.21cd–22ab)
>
> The state in which time is dragged (into and consumed within consciousness) (kālasaṁkarṣaṇasthiti) obtains (in consonance) with the process by which the breath is consumed. (When this process is complete,) consciousness is one alone and (perfectly) full (integral and all-embracing) (pūrnā), by setting aside (vyapohana) the relative distinctions (of discursive conceptual) knowledge (jñānabheda). (with 7.22cd–23ab)
