## Mūla

तत्त्वमार्गविधानेन ज्ञातव्यः परमार्थतः ।
पदमन्त्रकलादीनां पूर्वसूत्रानुसारतः ॥ ^mula

tattvamārgavidhānena jñātavyaḥ paramārthataḥ |
padamantrakalādīnāṁ pūrvasūtrānusārataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2024 – April 2024
> p. 33 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Malini (journal), January-April 2024.pdf#page=35>))
>
> **Conclusion**
>
> Although the phonemes are of two kinds,[^40] it is said that (there is also) a division into six kinds.[^41] That should be known as it truly is by (referring) to the procedure concerning the Path of the Principles.[^42] (with 6.27cd–28ab)

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2024 – April 2024
> p. 33 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Malini (journal), January-April 2024.pdf#page=35>))
>
> The three (procedures) with regards to the Parts, Mantras and Forces etc. should be fashioned in the manner expounded with regards to the principles and phonemes, in accord with the (teachings of the) previous *(sūtra).*[^43] (with 6.28cd–29ab)

[^40]: According to whether they belong to the sequence of Mātṛkā or Mālinī.
[^41]: Both of these sequences are divided into varṇa, mantra and pada.
[^42]: MV 9/27cd-28ab quoted as TĀ 16/144.
[^43]: MV 6/28cd-29ab quoted in TĀ 16/146. The teachings of 'the previous (sūtra)' i.e., MV 6/3-28ab are presented in TĀ 16/101-110, 132ff, and 234-236ab. They have all been quoted in the notes above in the relevant places.
