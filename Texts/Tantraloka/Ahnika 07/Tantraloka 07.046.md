## Mūla

पदमन्त्रेषु सर्वेषु यावत्तत्पदशक्तिगम् ।
शक्यते सततं युक्तैस्तावज्जप्यं तु साधकैः ॥ ^mula

padamantreṣu sarveṣu yāvattatpadaśaktigam |
śakyate satataṃ yuktaistāvajjapyaṃ tu sādhakaiḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 02 - Chapter Seven#^pg51|p. 33]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=51>))
>
> Adepts who are (deeply) engaged (in practice) should always (apply themselves) as much as they can to what is to be repeated in all Mantras with parts (padamantra), present in (both) its parts and the power (of the vital breath together).
