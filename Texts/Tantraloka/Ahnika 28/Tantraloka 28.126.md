## Mūla

कुलस्य तस्य चरमे दिने पूर्णत्वमुच्यते ।
दक्षिणायनषण्मासकर्तव्यत्वमतो विधौ ॥ ^mula

kulasya tasya carame dine pūrṇatvamucyate |
dakṣiṇāyanaṣaṇmāsakartavyatvamato vidhau || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 04 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 2#^pg83|p. 66]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=83>))
>
> (Others) call the (period of) time when the sun moves southwards and to the north, Kula and Akula, respectively. (According to them,) the last (full moon) day of this Kula (period) is said to be (Kula’s) full moon. (with 28.125cd–126ab)
>
> Thus, the rite of the sacred thread should be performed during the six months the sun moves southwards, so as to illumine (its) dark path. (with 28.126cd–127ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* अतश्चेदमप्युक्तं भवति--इत्याह--

> दक्षिणायनषण्मासकर्तव्यत्वमतो विधौ ॥
> पवित्रके प्रकाशत्वसिद्ध्यै कृष्णस्य वर्त्मनः ।

कृष्णस्येति--तमोरूपसमयलोपाद्यात्मनः ॥
