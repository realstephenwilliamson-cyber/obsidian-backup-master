## Mūla

तद्दिनप्रक्षये विश्वं मायायां प्रविलीयते ।
क्षीणायां निशि तावत्यां गहनेशः सृजेत्पुनः ॥ ^mula

taddinaprakṣaye viśvaṃ māyāyāṃ pravilīyate |
kṣīṇāyāṃ niśi tāvatyāṃ gahaneśaḥ sṛjetpunaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 11 - Chapter Six, part 4#^pg388|p. 373]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=388>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 6.155cd–156ab.
>
> At the end of his Day, the universe dissolves into Māyā, and at the end of the Night, which is the same length (as the day), Gahaneśa creates it again. To obtain the length of a day of Māyā, multiply the duration of (a Day of) Unmanifest (Nature) (prakṛti) by ten times a trillion (parārdha). Such also is its Night, which is (the period of) dissolution (pralaya). (with 6.155–156)
