# Zohar 3.081b

← [[Zohar 3.081a]] · [[Zohar (Soncino)]] · [[Zohar 3.082a]] →

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 8.03 - Parashat Qedoshim#^z3-81b|Parashat Qedoshim, at 3:81b]]

*Volume 3, folio 81b* · Kedoshim

*Passage Kedoshim 3:23 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 3.081a#^kedoshim-3-23|Kedoshim 3:23]].*

**Kedoshim 3:24** Therefore a man should rejoice with his wife at that hour to bind her in affection to him, and they should both have the same intent. When they are thus united, they form one soul and one body: one soul through their affection, and one body, as we have learnt, that if a man is not married he is, as it were, divided in halves, and only when male and female are joined do they become one body. Then God rests upon “one” and lodges a holy spirit in it: ^kedoshim-3-24

**Kedoshim 3:25** And such are called “the sons of God”, as has been said. ^kedoshim-3-25

**Kedoshim 4:26** YE SHALL FEAR EVERY MAN HIS MOTHER AND HIS FATHER, ETC, The fear of mother and father is here put side by side with the keeping of the Sabbath. Said R. Jose: ‘It is all one; he who fears one keeps the other. ^kedoshim-4-26

**Kedoshim 4:27** Why is the mother here placed before the father? As we have learnt, because she has not so much power as the father.’ ^kedoshim-4-27

**Kedoshim 4:28** R. Isaac connected this with the preceding words, “ye shall be holy”: when a man comes to sanctify himself together with his wife, it is the female who deserves the greater credit for that sanctification, and therefore the mother is placed first here.’ ^kedoshim-4-28

**Kedoshim 4:29** R. Judah pointed out that in another place the father is placed before the mother, the object being to indicate that both contributed equally to producing the son. ^kedoshim-4-29

**Kedoshim 4:30** The verse continues: AND YE SHALL KEEP MY SABBATHS, to show that one precept is of equal weight with the other.[^1] ^kedoshim-4-30

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Kedoshim 4:34–4:37.*

[^1]: The first 3 lines of the Hebrew text do not appear in our translation.
