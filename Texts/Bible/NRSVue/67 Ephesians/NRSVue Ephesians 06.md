# Ephesians 6 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Ephesians 05|← Ephesians 5]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · —

Compare: [[Ephesians 06|BSB and SBLGNT]] · [[DRB Ephesians 06|Douay-Rheims]] · [[JANT Ephesians 06|JANT notes]]

## Children and parents

**6:1** Children, obey your parents in the Lord,^[Other ancient authorities lack *in the Lord*] for this is right. ^6-1

**6:2** “Honor your father and mother”—this is the first commandment with a promise— ^6-2

**6:3** “so that it may be well with you and you may live long on the earth.” ^6-3

**6:4** And, fathers, do not provoke your children to anger, but bring them up in the discipline and instruction of the Lord. ^6-4

## Slaves and masters

**6:5** Slaves, obey your earthly masters with respect^[Or *fear*] and trembling, in singleness of heart, as you obey Christ, ^6-5

**6:6** not with a slavery performed merely for looks, to please people, but as slaves of Christ, doing the will of God from the soul. ^6-6

**6:7** Render service with enthusiasm, as for the Lord and not for humans, ^6-7

**6:8** knowing that whatever good we do, we will receive the same again from the Lord, whether we are enslaved or free. ^6-8

**6:9** And, masters,^[In Gk the same word is used for *master* and *Lord*] do the same to them. Stop threatening them, for you know that both of you have the same Lord^[In Gk the same word is used for *master* and *Lord*] in heaven, and with him there is no partiality. ^6-9

## The whole armor of God

**6:10** Finally, be strong in the Lord and in the strength of his power; ^6-10

**6:11** put on the whole armor of God, so that you may be able to stand against the wiles of the devil, ^6-11

**6:12** for our^[Other ancient authorities read *your*] struggle is not against blood and flesh but against the rulers, against the authorities, against the cosmic powers of this present^[Gk lacks *present*] darkness, against the spiritual forces of evil in the heavenly places.^[Gk *heavenlies*] ^6-12

**6:13** Therefore take up the whole armor of God, so that you may be able to withstand on the evil day and, having prevailed against everything, to stand firm. ^6-13

**6:14** Stand, therefore, and belt your waist with truth and put on the breastplate of righteousness ^6-14

**6:15** and lace up your sandals in preparation for the gospel of peace. ^6-15

**6:16** With all of these,^[Or *In all circumstances*] take the shield of faith, with which you will be able to quench all the flaming arrows of the evil one. ^6-16

**6:17** Take the helmet of salvation and the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God. ^6-17

**6:18** Pray in the Spirit at all times in every prayer and supplication. To that end, keep alert and always persevere in supplication for all the saints. ^6-18

**6:19** Pray also for me, so that when I speak a message may be given to me to make known with boldness the mystery of the gospel,^[Other ancient authorities lack *of the gospel*] ^6-19

**6:20** for which I am an ambassador in chains. Pray that I may declare it boldly, as I must speak. ^6-20

## Personal matters and benediction

**6:21** So that you also may know how I am and what I am doing, Tychicus will tell you everything. He is a dear brother and a faithful minister in the Lord. ^6-21

**6:22** I am sending him to you for this very purpose, to let you know how we are and to encourage your hearts. ^6-22

**6:23** Peace be to the brothers and sisters and love with faith, from God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ. ^6-23

**6:24** Grace be with all who have an undying love for^[Or *Grace immortal be with all who love*] our Lord Jesus Christ.^[Other ancient authorities add *Amen*] ^6-24
