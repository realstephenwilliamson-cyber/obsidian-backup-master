## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2023 – March 2023
> p. 52 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=52>))
>
> **The Five Limbs of the Vidyās (vidyāṅga)**
>
> The mantra Vidyāṅgahṛdaya (the Heart of the Limbs of the Vidyā is made) with five OṀs. O beloved, the Brahmaśiras (the Head of Brahmā), consisting of the eleven syllables (mantra), is said to be OṀ AMṚTE TEJOMĀLINI, adorned with the syllables SVĀHĀ. The Śikhā (the Topknot amongst) the group of (the limbs of the) Vidyā should be known to be Rudrāṇī,[^52] consisting of eight syllables. It is VEDAVEDINI HŪṀ PHAṬ, and has the syllable OṀ at the beginning. VAJRINE VAJRADHARĀYA SVĀHĀ, preceded by the syllable OṀ is the Armour (varman) called Puruṣṭuta (Praised by Many) consisting of eleven syllables.[^53] (with 3.61cd–64)

[^51]: g: *yutā śikheti*
[^52]: One could also take Rudrāṇī to be a vocative, in which case the translation would be: ‘O Rudrāṇī, the Śikha (the Topknot amongst) the group of (the limbs of the) Vidyā should be known to consist of eight syllables.’ As Śikhā is feminine and is indeed considered to be a power (*śakti*), it may well be called Rudrāṇī, who is Rudra’s power.
[^53]: TĀ 30/37-40ab (except 38ab) is a lightly modified quote of MV 3/61cd-64. TĀ 30/37cd = MV 3/61cd, TĀ 30/38 is a paraphrase of MV 3/62cd-63ab; TĀ 30/39ab = MV 3/63ab (read with MS G: yutā śikhā); TĀ 30/39cd-40ab (reads *svāhetyoṅkārapūrvakam* for *svāhāntaṁ praṇavādikam*) MV 3/64.
