## Chapter 118

**118:1** Praise the LORD, for He is good,  
His steadfast love is eternal. ^118-1

**118:2** Let Israel declare,  
“His steadfast love is eternal.” ^118-2

**118:3** Let the house of Aaron declare,  
“His steadfast love is eternal.” ^118-3

**118:4** Let those who fear the LORD declare,  
“His steadfast love is eternal.” ^118-4

**118:5** In distress I called on the LORD;  
the Lord answered me and brought me relief. ^118-5

**118:6** The LORD is on my side,  
I have no fear;  
what can man do to me? ^118-6

**118:7** With the LORD on my side as my helper,  
I will see the downfall of my foes. ^118-7

**118:8** It is better to take refuge in the LORD  
than to trust in mortals; ^118-8

**118:9** it is better to take refuge in the LORD  
than to trust in the great. ^118-9

**118:10** All nations have beset me;  
by the name of the LORD I will surely cut them down.[^1] ^118-10

**118:11** They beset me, they surround me;  
by the name of the LORD I will surely cut them down. ^118-11

**118:12** They have beset me like bees;  
they shall be extinguished like burning thorns;  
by the name of the LORD I will surely cut them down. ^118-12

**118:13** You[^2] pressed me hard,  
I nearly fell;  
but the LORD helped me. ^118-13

**118:14** The LORD is my strength and might;[^3]  
He has become my deliverance. ^118-14

**118:15** The tents of the victorious[^4] resound with joyous shouts of deliverance,  
“The right hand of the LORD is triumphant! ^118-15

**118:16** The right hand of the LORD is exalted!  
The right hand of the LORD is triumphant!” ^118-16

**118:17** I shall not die but live  
and proclaim the works of the LORD. ^118-17

**118:18** The LORD punished me severely,  
but did not hand me over to death. ^118-18

**118:19** Open the gates of victory[^5] for me  
that I may enter them and praise the LORD. ^118-19

**118:20** This is the gateway to the LORD—  
the victorious[^6] shall enter through it. ^118-20

**118:21** I praise You, for You have answered me,  
and have become my deliverance. ^118-21

**118:22** The stone that the builders rejected  
has become the chief cornerstone. ^118-22

**118:23** This is the LORD’s doing;  
it is marvelous in our sight. ^118-23

**118:24** This is the day that the LORD has made—  
let us exult and rejoice on it. ^118-24

**118:25** O LORD, deliver us!  
O LORD, let us prosper! ^118-25

**118:26** May he who enters be blessed in the name of the LORD;  
we bless you from the House of the LORD. ^118-26

**118:27** The LORD is God;  
He has given us light;  
bind the festal offering to the horns of the altar with cords.[^7] ^118-27

**118:28** You are my God and I will praise You;  
You are my God and I will extol You. ^118-28

**118:29** Praise the LORD for He is good,  
His steadfast love is eternal. ^118-29

[^1]: Meaning of ’amilam in this and the following two verses uncertain.
[^2]: I.e., the enemy.
[^3]: Others “song.”
[^4]: Or “righteous.”
[^5]: Or “righteousness.”
[^6]: Or “righteous.”
[^7]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
