# Likutei Moharan I 116

← [[Likutei Moharan I 115]] · [[Likutei Moharan]] · [[Likutei Moharan I 117]] →

*Part I · Lesson 116* · [[Likutei Moharan]]

## 1

One who gives charity is spared from sinning.

This is because whoever shows compassion for God’s creations, is himself shown compassion from Heaven ([[Shabbat 151#151b|Shabbat 151b]]). Yet, when anyone lacks *daat* (holy knowledge), it is forbidden to show him compassion ([[Berakhot 33#33a|Berakhot 33a]]). Therefore, since [Heaven] shows him compassion, it must be that he is given *daat*.

He is thereby spared from committing a sin. For a man does not sin unless a spirit of folly has entered him ([[Sotah 3#3a|Sotah 3a]]). But when he has *daat,* he is spared from sinning. Amen, so may it be His will.
