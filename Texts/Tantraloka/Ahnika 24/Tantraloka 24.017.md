## Mūla

साध्योऽनुमेयो मोक्षादिः प्रत्ययैर्यदतीन्द्रियः ।
दीक्षोत्तरे च पुर्यष्टवर्गार्पणमिहोदितम् ॥ ^mula

sādhyo'numeyo mokṣādiḥ pratyayairyadatīndriyaḥ |
dīkṣottare ca puryaṣṭavargārpaṇamihoditam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 15 - Chapter Twenty-Four#^pg370|p. 350]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=370>))
>
> The liberation and the like (of the deceased), which is the goal (sādhya), is beyond the senses, and (so) can (only) be inferred (as having taken place) by means (of outer) validating signs. (with 24.17ab)
>
> Here (in this regard), the Dīkṣoftara teaches (another rite, that involves) the offering (and elimination of the constitutive elements of the subtle body, that is) the City of Eight (puryaṣtaka). (with 24.17cd)
