# Emmanuel Levinas

A French philosopher (born in Kaunas, 1906; died in Clichy, 1995) who taught at the École normale israélite orientale in Paris and became its director, then taught at the University of Poitiers from 1961, at Paris-Nanterre from 1967 and at the Sorbonne from 1973 until he retired in 1979 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmanuel_Levinas)).
