## Mūla

त्रितयत्वं प्रकुर्वीत तत्त्ववर्णोक्तवर्त्मना ।
इत्थं भूतशरीरस्य गुरुणा शिवमूर्तिना ॥ ^mula

tritayatvaṁ prakurvīta tattvavarṇoktavartmanā |
itthaṁ bhūtaśarīrasya guruṇā śivamūrtinā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2024 – April 2024
> p. 33 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Malini (journal), January-April 2024.pdf#page=35>))
>
> The three (procedures) with regards to the Parts, Mantras and Forces etc. should be fashioned in the manner expounded with regards to the principles and phonemes, in accord with the (teachings of the) previous *(sūtra).*[^43] (with 6.28cd–29ab)

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2024 – April 2024
> p. 33 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Malini (journal), January-April 2024.pdf#page=35>))
>
> Such (is the procedure with regards to this present) elemental body (*bhūtaśarīra*), by means of which the teacher, who is (the living) icon of Śiva (himself), should perform the initiation that bestows all desired fruits. (with 6.29cd–30ab)

[^43]: MV 6/28cd-29ab quoted in TĀ 16/146. The teachings of 'the previous (sūtra)' i.e., MV 6/3-28ab are presented in TĀ 16/101-110, 132ff, and 234-236ab. They have all been quoted in the notes above in the relevant places.
