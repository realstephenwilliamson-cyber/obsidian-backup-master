*Part I · Lesson 202* · [[Likutei Moharan]]

## 1

The more limited a person’s intellect, the more respect one needs to show him. This is because the more limited his intellect, the more he craves honor. Thus, as we can see, women tend to be very particular about their respect and they very much want honor. The reason is that “woman’s *daat* is undeveloped” ([[Shabbat 33#33b|Shabbat 33b]]), thus it is very limited.
