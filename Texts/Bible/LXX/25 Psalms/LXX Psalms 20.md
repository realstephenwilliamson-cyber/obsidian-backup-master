# LXX Psalms 20

← [[LXX Psalms 19]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 21]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 021]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 021]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**20:1** For the end, a Psalm of David.  ^20-1

**20:2** O Lord, the king shall rejoice in thy strength; and in thy salvation he shall greatly exult.  ^20-2

**20:3** Thou hast granted him the desire of his soul, and hast not withheld from him the request of his lips. Pause.  ^20-3

**20:4** For thou hast prevented him with blessings of goodness: thou hast set upon his head a crown of precious stone.  ^20-4

**20:5** He asked life of thee, and thou gavest him length of days for ever and ever.  ^20-5

**20:6** His glory is great in thy salvation: thou wilt crown him with glory and majesty.  ^20-6

**20:7** For thou wilt give him a blessing for ever and ever: thou wilt gladden him with joy with thy countenance.  ^20-7

**20:8** For the king trusts in the Lord, and through the mercy of the Highest he shall not be moved.  ^20-8

**20:9** Let thy hand be found by all thine enemies: let thy right hand find all that hate thee.  ^20-9

**20:10** Thou shalt make them as a fiery oven at the time of thy presence: the Lord shall trouble them in his anger, and fire shall devour them.  ^20-10

**20:11** Thou shalt destroy their fruit from the earth, and their seed from *among* the sons of men.  ^20-11

**20:12** For they intended evils against thee; they imagined a device which they shall by no means be able to perform.  ^20-12

**20:13** For thou shalt make them *turn their* back in thy latter end, thou wilt prepare their face.  ^20-13

**20:14** Be thou exalted, O Lord, in thy strength: we will sing and praise thy mighty acts.  ^20-14
