# κτίσις

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · noun

**Dodson:** creation, creature, institution — (often of the founding of a city), (a) abstr: creation, (b) concr: creation, creature, institution; always of Divine work, (c) an institution, ordinance. (Strong's 2937; headword κτίσις, εως, ἡ)
