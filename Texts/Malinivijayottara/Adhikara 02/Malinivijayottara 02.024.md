## Mūla

सार्धमेतच्छतम्प्रोक्तम्भेदानामनुपूर्वशः ।
सम्क्षेपाद्विस्तरादस्य परिसम्ख्या न विद्यते ॥ ^mula

sārdham etac chatam proktam bhedānām anupūrvaśaḥ |
samkṣepād vistarād asya parisamkhyā na vidyate || ^iast

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, July 2022 – September 2022
> p. 33 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Malini (journal), July-September 2022.pdf#page=35>))

> This, in brief, is said to be the (set of) one hundred and fifty kinds (of penetration). Extensively, (they are) innumerable.
