The Mahānayaprakāśa by Arṇasimha 

In Praise of the Goddess of Consciousness 

vibhāti sarvagā sarhvin nānātvena sadoditā ॥ 1 || 

Although transcending all, (the one) consciousness, undefinable, eternal, ever manifest and omnipresent, shines as the diversity (of the many things of this world). (1)

In reality neither many nor just a single form, signless (aniketa) and free of fear, I salute that (divine consciousness that is) the Void (kha). (2)

The Lineage of the Masters (gurukrama), radiant (with the energies) that range from Khecarī to Śākinī, is known as the Teaching of the Goddess (devīnaya), that extends from Emanation to the Inexplicable. (20)

Although (continuing to be absolute and) supreme, (the one reality), which is the current of the Five Currents (pañcavāha), well-established as the wheel (of energies), is solely intent on consuming (grasana) the unfolding expansion of subject and object. (22)
