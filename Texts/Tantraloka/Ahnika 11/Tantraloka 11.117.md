## Mūla

उक्तं चैतत्पुरैवेति न भूयः प्रविविच्यते ।

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 03 - Chapter Eleven, part 2#^pg117|p. 97]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=117>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 11.117cd–118ab.
>
> The Lord is acclaimed to be all things (viśvarūpa) in this way. Nothing separate (from Him), including Earth and the rest (of the Principles), could logically exist. (All) this has been said before; it is useless to belabour the point. The many defects (advanced up to here) of the (view that there is an) outer (independent objective) reality (are sufficient to refute it), so let us bring (this discussion) to rest (pravyaramyata). (with 11.116–117)

## Viveka

> तदित्थं परमेशानो विश्वरूपः प्रगीयते ।

> उक्तं चैतत्पुरैवेति न भूयः प्रविविच्यते ।
> भूयोभिश्चापि बाह्यार्थदूषणैः प्रव्यरम्यत ॥

प्रव्यरम्यतेति, प्रविरतं पर्यवसितं समाप्तमिति यावत् ॥ ११७ ॥
