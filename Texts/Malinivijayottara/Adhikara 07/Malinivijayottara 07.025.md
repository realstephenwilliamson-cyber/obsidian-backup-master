## Mūla

वामाङ्गुष्ठाग्रसंलग्नं पाशः प्रसृतकुञ्चितः ।
हले मुष्टिर्यथा वामो दक्षहीनस्तथाङ्कुशः ॥ ^mula

vāmāṅguṣṭhāgrasaṃlagnaṃ pāśaḥ prasṛtakuñcitaḥ |
hale muṣṭiryathā vāmo dakṣahīnastathāṅkuśaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, July 2024 – September 2024
> p. 27 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=6>))
>
> **15. Pāśa (the Fetter)**
>
> The two index fingers of the right and left (hands) that are on the back of the (down turned) fist are connected to the left thumb. (This is the gesture of) the Fetter that (alternately) extends and is contracted. (with 7.24cd–25ab)

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, July 2024 – September 2024
> p. 28 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=7>))
>
> **16. Aṅkuśa (the Elephant Gaud)**
>
> The left fist, without the right, is as it is (when making the gesture of) the plough. Such is (the gesture of) the Elephant Gaud. (with 7.25cd–25cd)
