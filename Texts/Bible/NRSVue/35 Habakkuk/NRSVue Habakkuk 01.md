# Habakkuk 1 (NRSVue)

— · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Habakkuk 02|Habakkuk 2 →]]

**1:1** The oracle that the prophet Habakkuk saw. ^1-1

## The prophet’s complaint

**1:2** O LORD, how long shall I cry for help, and you will not listen? Or cry to you “Violence!” and you will not save? ^1-2

**1:3** Why do you make me see wrongdoing and look at trouble? Destruction and violence are before me; strife and contention arise. ^1-3

**1:4** So the law becomes slack, and justice never prevails. The wicked surround the righteous; therefore judgment comes forth perverted. ^1-4

**1:5** Look at the nations and see! Be astonished! Be astounded! For a work is being done in your days that you would not believe if you were told. ^1-5

**1:6** For I am rousing the Chaldeans, that fierce and impetuous nation, who march through the breadth of the earth to seize dwellings not their own. ^1-6

**1:7** Dread and fearsome are they; their justice and dignity proceed from themselves. ^1-7

**1:8** Their horses are swifter than leopards, more menacing than wolves at dusk; their horses charge. Their horsemen come from far away; they fly like an eagle swift to devour. ^1-8

**1:9** They all come for violence, with faces pressing^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] forward; they gather captives like sand. ^1-9

**1:10** At kings they scoff, and of rulers they make sport. They laugh at every fortress and heap up earth to take it. ^1-10

**1:11** Then they sweep by like the wind; they transgress and become guilty; their own might is their god! ^1-11

**1:12** Are you not from of old, O LORD my God, my Holy One? You^[Or *We*] shall not die. O LORD, you have marked them for judgment, and you, O Rock, have established them for punishment. ^1-12

**1:13** Your eyes are too pure to behold evil, and you cannot look on wrongdoing; why do you look on the treacherous and are silent when the wicked swallow those more righteous than they? ^1-13

**1:14** You have made people like the fish of the sea, like crawling things that have no ruler. ^1-14

**1:15** He brings all of them up with a hook; he drags them out with his net; he gathers them in his seine, so he rejoices and exults. ^1-15

**1:16** Therefore he sacrifices to his net and makes offerings to his seine, for by them his portion is lavish, and his food is rich. ^1-16

**1:17** Is he then to keep on emptying his net and destroying nations without mercy? ^1-17
