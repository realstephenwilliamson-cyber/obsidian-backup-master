## Mūla

वृत्तिभागीति तद्देशं शब्दं गृह्णात्यलं तथा ।
यस्त्वाह श्रोत्रमाकाशं कर्णसंयोगभेदितम् ॥ ^mula

vṛttibhāgīti taddeśaṃ śabdaṃ gṛhṇātyalaṃ tathā |
yastvāha śrotramākāśaṃ karṇasaṃyogabheditam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 09 - Chapter Nine, part 7#^pg294|p. 275]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=294>))
>
> According to us, the cause of the sense of hearing is the ego. It operates in the various places (where it is projected) (fatra tatra), thus it is capable (alam) of sensing the sound which is in that place (i.e. in the drum). (with 9.299cd–300ab)
>
> Some say that when the echo (śabdaja) of the (original) sound reaches the space confined (bhedita) in the (cavity of the) ear, which is in contact with the (inner) ear, it gives rise to the sensation of sound (śabdabuddhi). However, how can (this explanation) account for the fact that when one hears even dim drumming, one senses that this sound comes from a distance? (with 9.300cd–302ab)
