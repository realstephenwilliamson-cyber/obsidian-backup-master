## Mūla

सुशुद्धः सन्पुनः कुर्यादित्याज्ञा परमेशितुः ।
अथ त्रिशिरसि प्रोक्तो लिख्यते तद्विधिः स्फुटः ॥ ^mula

suśuddhaḥ sanpunaḥ kuryādityājñā parameśituḥ |
atha triśirasi prokto likhyate tadvidhiḥ sphuṭaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 04 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 2#^pg90|p. 73]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=90>))
>
> If he omits (the performance of the rite of) the sacred thread, the wise man must repeat the reparatory (Mantras,) (and then,) well purified, he should perform (the rite) again. This is the Supreme Lord’s command. (with 28.145cd–146ab)
>
> Now we will write clearly about the procedure for that as it is taught in the Triśiras. (with 28.146cd)
>
> (The procedure) taught here is that of the Śaiva (scripture) that (teaches) three realities to be known, consisting of five (groups of) five. Alternatively, (it can be said to be the teaching of the groups) divided into ten and eighteen (scriptures) belonging to the six currents (of revelation). (with 28.146cd–147)

> अथ त्रिशिरसि प्रोक्तो लिख्यते तद्विधिः स्फुटः ॥
