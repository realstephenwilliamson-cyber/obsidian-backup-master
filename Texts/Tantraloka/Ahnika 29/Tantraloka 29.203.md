## Mūla

ऋजुदेहजुषः शक्तिं पादान्मूर्धान्तमागताम् ।
पाशान्दहन्तीं संदीप्तां चिन्तयेत्तन्मयो गुरुः ॥ ^mula

ṛjudehajuṣaḥ śaktiṃ pādānmūrdhāntamāgatām |
pāśāndahantīṃ saṃdīptāṃ cintayettanmayo guruḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 12 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 4#^pg379|p. 362]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=379>))
>
> The teacher should visualize how the energy, with which he is one (tanmaya), well energized (saṁdīptā), (travels through the disciple), whose body is erect, from the feet up and comes to the top of (his) head, burning away (as it does so) the fetters of the disciple who has been sprinkled (with consecrated water), and on whom the path that needs to be purified, described previously, has been deposited. (with 29.202cd–203)
