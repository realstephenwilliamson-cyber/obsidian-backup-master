## Mūla

प्रवर्तन्तेऽधिकाराय करणानीव देहिनाम् ।
कृतविद्याव्रतः पश्चाद्दीक्षाव्याख्यादि सर्वतः ॥ ^mula

pravartante'dhikārāya karaṇānīva dehinām |
kṛtavidyāvrataḥ paścāddīkṣāvyākhyādi sarvataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 14 - Chapter Twenty-Three#^pg336|p. 316]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=336>))
>
> This is the Vow of the Observance of the Vidyā (vidyāvrata), taught in the Devyāyāmala, so that (the newly consecrated teacher) may activate the vitality of (his) mantras (mantravīrya), which is a state of oneness (with them). As is said in the Spandaśāsana: ‘seizing that strength (bala), Mantras, endowed with the power of omniscience, perform their functions, as do the senses of the embodied.’ (with 23.39b–41ab)
>
> (A teacher who) has completed the Vow of the Observance of Mantra should thereafter do (all that is required) in all respects, such as (impart) initiation and explain (the scriptures) to qualified disciples, (but) never to those who are not. (with 23.41cd–42ab)
