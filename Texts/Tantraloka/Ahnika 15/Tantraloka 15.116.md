## Mūla

न्यासं सामान्यतः कुर्याद्बहिर्यागप्रसिद्धये ।
मातृकां मालिनीं वाऽथ द्वितयं वा क्रमाक्रमात् ॥ ^mula

nyāsaṃ sāmānyataḥ kuryādbahiryāgaprasiddhaye |
mātṛkāṃ mālinīṃ vā'tha dvitayaṃ vā kramākramāt || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 03 - Chapter Fifteen, part 2#^pg96|p. 78]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=96>))
>
> Once he whose soul is pure has reached the location of the sacrifice (yāgauka) he should, whilst (still) outside it, perform the common (sāmānya) deposition, so that the external sacrifice may be successful. (with 15.115cd–116ab)
>
> Mātrkā and Mālinī (should be deposited on the body), either one or both, in due order or otherwise, in the sequence of emanation or waning away or both, individually and collectively, for both. (with 15.116cd–117ab)

*Lead-in:* तदेव आह—

> मातृकां मालिनीं वाऽथ द्वितयं वा क्रमाक्रमात् ॥
> सृष्ट्यप्ययद्वयैः कुर्यादेकैकं सङ्घशो द्विशः ।

क्रमाक्रमादिति--आदौ मातृका, ततो मालिनी, तद्विपर्ययो वा । अप्ययः संहारः । एकैकमिति--सृष्ट्याद्यन्यतमरूपतया । सङ्घश इति-- सृष्ट्यादित्रैरूप्येण । द्विशो--मातृकामालिनीगतत्वेन ॥
