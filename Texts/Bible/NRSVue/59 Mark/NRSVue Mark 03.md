# Mark 3 (NRSVue)

Compare: [[Mark 03|BSB and SBLGNT]] · [[DRB Mark 03|Douay-Rheims]] · [[JANT Mark 03|JANT notes]]

## The man with a withered hand

**3:1** Again he entered the synagogue, and a man was there who had a withered hand. ^3-1

**3:2** They were watching him to see whether he would cure him on the Sabbath, so that they might accuse him. ^3-2

**3:3** And he said to the man who had the withered hand, “Come forward.” ^3-3

**3:4** Then he said to them, “Is it lawful to do good or to do harm on the Sabbath, to save life or to kill?” But they were silent. ^3-4

**3:5** He looked around at them with anger; he was grieved at their hardness of heart and said to the man, “Stretch out your hand.” He stretched it out, and his hand was restored. ^3-5

**3:6** The Pharisees went out and immediately conspired with the Herodians against him, how to destroy him. ^3-6

## A multitude at the seaside

**3:7** Jesus departed with his disciples to the sea, and a great multitude from Galilee followed him; ^3-7

**3:8** hearing all that he was doing, they came to him in great numbers from Judea, Jerusalem, Idumea, beyond the Jordan, and the region around Tyre and Sidon. ^3-8

**3:9** He told his disciples to have a boat ready for him because of the crowd, so that they would not crush him, ^3-9

**3:10** for he had cured many, so that all who had diseases pressed upon him to touch him. ^3-10

**3:11** Whenever the unclean spirits saw him, they fell down before him and shouted, “You are the Son of God!” ^3-11

**3:12** But he sternly ordered them not to make him known. ^3-12

## Jesus appoints the twelve

**3:13** He went up the mountain and called to him those whom he wanted, and they came to him. ^3-13

**3:14** And he appointed twelve^[Other ancient authorities add *whom he also named apostles*] to be with him and to be sent out to preach ^3-14

**3:15** and to have authority to cast out demons. ^3-15

**3:16** So he appointed the twelve:^[Other ancient authorities lack *So he appointed the twelve*] Simon (to whom he gave the name Peter), ^3-16

**3:17** James son of Zebedee and John the brother of James (to whom he gave the name Boanerges, that is, Sons of Thunder), ^3-17

**3:18** and Andrew, and Philip, and Bartholomew, and Matthew, and Thomas, and James son of Alphaeus, and Thaddaeus, and Simon the Cananaean, ^3-18

**3:19** and Judas Iscariot, who handed him over. ^3-19

## Jesus and Beelzebul

**3:20** Then he went home, and the crowd came together again, so that they could not even eat. ^3-20

**3:21** When his family heard it, they went out to restrain him, for people were saying, “He has gone out of his mind.” ^3-21

**3:22** And the scribes who came down from Jerusalem said, “He has Beelzebul, and by the ruler of the demons he casts out demons.” ^3-22

**3:23** And he called them to him and spoke to them in parables, “How can Satan cast out Satan? ^3-23

**3:24** If a kingdom is divided against itself, that kingdom cannot stand. ^3-24

**3:25** And if a house is divided against itself, that house will not be able to stand. ^3-25

**3:26** And if Satan has risen up against himself and is divided, he cannot stand, but his end has come. ^3-26

**3:27** But no one can enter a strong man’s house and plunder his property without first tying up the strong man; then indeed the house can be plundered. ^3-27

**3:28** “Truly I tell you, people will be forgiven for their sins and whatever blasphemies they utter, ^3-28

**3:29** but whoever blasphemes against the Holy Spirit can never have forgiveness but is guilty of an eternal sin”— ^3-29

**3:30** for they had said, “He has an unclean spirit.” ^3-30

## The true kindred of Jesus

**3:31** Then his mother and his brothers came, and standing outside they sent to him and called him. ^3-31

**3:32** A crowd was sitting around him, and they said to him, “Your mother and your brothers^[Other ancient authorities add *and sisters*] are outside asking for you.” ^3-32

**3:33** And he replied, “Who are my mother and my brothers?” ^3-33

**3:34** And looking at those who sat around him, he said, “Here are my mother and my brothers! ^3-34

**3:35** Whoever does the will of God is my brother and sister and mother.” ^3-35
