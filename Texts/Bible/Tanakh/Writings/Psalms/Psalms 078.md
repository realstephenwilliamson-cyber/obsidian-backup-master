## Chapter 78

**78:1** A *maskil* of Asaph.  
Give ear, my people, to my teaching,  
turn your ear to what I say. ^78-1

**78:2** I will expound a theme,  
hold forth on the lessons of the past, ^78-2

**78:3** things we have heard and known,  
that our fathers have told us. ^78-3

**78:4** We will not withhold them from their children,  
telling the coming generation  
the praises of the LORD and His might,  
and the wonders He performed. ^78-4

**78:5** He established a decree in Jacob,  
ordained a teaching in Israel,  
charging our fathers  
to make them known to their children, ^78-5

**78:6** that a future generation might know  
—children yet to be born—  
and in turn tell their children ^78-6

**78:7** that they might put their confidence in God,  
and not forget God’s great deeds,  
but observe His commandments, ^78-7

**78:8** and not be like their fathers,  
a wayward and defiant generation,  
a generation whose heart was inconstant,  
whose spirit was not true to God. ^78-8

**78:9** Like the Ephraimite bowmen  
who played false in the day of battle, ^78-9

**78:10** they did not keep God’s covenant,  
they refused to follow His instruction; ^78-10

**78:11** they forgot His deeds  
and the wonders that He showed them. ^78-11

**78:12** He performed marvels in the sight of their fathers,  
in the land of Egypt, the plain of Zoan. ^78-12

**78:13** He split the sea and took them through it;  
He made the waters stand like a wall. ^78-13

**78:14** He led them with a cloud by day,  
and throughout the night by the light of fire. ^78-14

**78:15** He split rocks in the wilderness  
and gave them drink as if from the great deep. ^78-15

**78:16** He brought forth streams from a rock  
and made them flow down like a river. ^78-16

**78:17** But they went on sinning against Him,  
defying the Most High in the parched land. ^78-17

**78:18** To test God was in their mind  
when they demanded food for themselves. ^78-18

**78:19** They spoke against God, saying,  
“Can God spread a feast in the wilderness? ^78-19

**78:20** True, He struck the rock and waters flowed,  
streams gushed forth;  
but can He provide bread?  
Can He supply His people with meat?” ^78-20

**78:21** The LORD heard and He raged;  
fire broke out against Jacob,  
anger flared up at Israel, ^78-21

**78:22** because they did not put their trust in God,  
did not rely on His deliverance. ^78-22

**78:23** So He commanded the skies above,  
He opened the doors of heaven ^78-23

**78:24** and rained manna upon them for food,  
giving them heavenly grain. ^78-24

**78:25** Each man ate a hero’s meal;  
He sent them provision in plenty. ^78-25

**78:26** He set the east wind moving in heaven,  
and drove the south wind by His might. ^78-26

**78:27** He rained meat on them like dust,  
winged birds like the sands of the sea, ^78-27

**78:28** making them come down inside His camp,  
around His dwelling-place. ^78-28

**78:29** They ate till they were sated;  
He gave them what they craved. ^78-29

**78:30** They had not yet wearied of what they craved,  
the food was still in their mouths ^78-30

**78:31** when God’s anger flared up at them.  
He slew their sturdiest,  
struck down the youth of Israel. ^78-31

**78:32** Nonetheless, they went on sinning  
and had no faith in His wonders. ^78-32

**78:33** He made their days end in futility,  
their years in sudden death. ^78-33

**78:34** When He struck[^1] them, they turned to Him  
and sought God once again. ^78-34

**78:35** They remembered that God was their rock,  
God Most High, their Redeemer. ^78-35

**78:36** Yet they deceived Him with their speech,  
lied to Him with their words; ^78-36

**78:37** their hearts were inconstant toward Him;  
they were untrue to His covenant. ^78-37

**78:38** But He, being merciful, forgave iniquity  
and would not destroy;  
He restrained His wrath time and again  
and did not give full vent to His fury; ^78-38

**78:39** for He remembered that they were but flesh,  
a passing breath that does not return. ^78-39

**78:40** How often did they defy Him in the wilderness,  
did they grieve Him in the wasteland! ^78-40

**78:41** Again and again they tested God,  
vexed[^2] the Holy One of Israel. ^78-41

**78:42** They did not remember His strength,  
or the day He redeemed them from the foe; ^78-42

**78:43** how He displayed His signs in Egypt,  
His wonders in the plain of Zoan. ^78-43

**78:44** He turned their rivers into blood;  
He made their waters undrinkable. ^78-44

**78:45** He inflicted upon them swarms of insects to devour them,  
frogs to destroy them. ^78-45

**78:46** He gave their crops over to grubs,  
their produce to locusts. ^78-46

**78:47** He killed their vines with hail,  
their sycamores with frost.[^3] ^78-47

**78:48** He gave their beasts over to hail,  
their cattle to lightning bolts. ^78-48

**78:49** He inflicted His burning anger upon them,  
wrath, indignation, trouble,  
a band of deadly messengers. ^78-49

**78:50** He cleared a path for His anger;  
He did not stop short of slaying them,  
but gave them over to pestilence. ^78-50

**78:51** He struck every first-born in Egypt,  
the first fruits of their vigor in the tents of Ham. ^78-51

**78:52** He set His people moving like sheep,  
drove them like a flock in the wilderness. ^78-52

**78:53** He led them in safety; they were unafraid;  
as for their enemies, the sea covered them. ^78-53

**78:54** He brought them to His holy realm,[^4]  
the mountain His right hand had acquired. ^78-54

**78:55** He expelled nations before them,  
settled the tribes of Israel in their tents,  
allotting them their portion by the line.[^5] ^78-55

**78:56** Yet they defiantly tested God Most High,  
and did not observe His decrees. ^78-56

**78:57** They fell away, disloyal like their fathers;  
they played false like a treacherous bow. ^78-57

**78:58** They vexed Him with their high places;  
they incensed Him with their idols. ^78-58

**78:59** God heard it and was enraged;  
He utterly rejected Israel. ^78-59

**78:60** He forsook the tabernacle of Shiloh,  
the tent He had set among men. ^78-60

**78:61** He let His might[^6] go into captivity,  
His glory into the hands of the foe. ^78-61

**78:62** He gave His people over to the sword;  
He was enraged at His very own. ^78-62

**78:63** Fire consumed their young men,  
and their maidens remained unwed.[^7] ^78-63

**78:64** Their priests fell by the sword,  
and their widows could not weep. ^78-64

**78:65** The Lord awoke as from sleep,  
like a warrior shaking off[^8] wine. ^78-65

**78:66** He beat back His foes,  
dealing them lasting disgrace. ^78-66

**78:67** He rejected the clan of Joseph;  
He did not choose the tribe of Ephraim. ^78-67

**78:68** He did choose the tribe of Judah,  
Mount Zion, which He loved. ^78-68

**78:69** He built His Sanctuary like the heavens,  
like the earth that He established forever. ^78-69

**78:70** He chose David, His servant,  
and took him from the sheepfolds. ^78-70

**78:71** He brought him from minding the nursing ewes  
to tend His people Jacob, Israel, His very own. ^78-71

**78:72** He tended them with blameless heart;  
with skillful hands he led them. ^78-72

[^1]: Lit. “killed.”
[^2]: Or “set a limit to.”
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^4]: Or “hill” with Septuagint and Saadia.
[^5]: Inverted for clarity.
[^6]: I.e., the Ark; cf. Ps. 132.8.
[^7]: Lit. “had no nuptial song.”
[^8]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
