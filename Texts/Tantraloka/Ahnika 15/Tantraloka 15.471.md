## Mūla

एतत्तन्त्रे समय्यादिक्रमादाप्तोत्तरक्रियः ।
आचार्यो न पुनर्बौद्धवैष्णवादिः कदाचन ॥ ^mula

etattantre samayyādikramādāptottarakriyaḥ |
ācāryo na punarbauddhavaiṣṇavādiḥ kadācana || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 09 - Chapter Fifteen, part 8#^pg308|p. 290]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=308>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 15.473cd–474ab.
>
> A teacher who has attained (the authority and knowledge to perform) more advanced rites has become so gradually, (passing through the levels of initiation,) beginning with that of regular initiate in this (particular Śaiva) Tantra. He could (most certainly) never be a Buddhist or a Vaiṣṇava.
