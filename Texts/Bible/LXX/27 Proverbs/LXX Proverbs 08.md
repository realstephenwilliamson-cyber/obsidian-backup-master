# LXX Proverbs 08

← [[LXX Proverbs 07]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Proverbs 09]] →

*Proverbs · [[LXX]]*

**8:1** Thou shalt proclaim wisdom, that understanding may be obedient to thee.  ^8-1

**8:2** For she is on lofty eminences, and stands in the midst of the ways.  ^8-2

**8:3** For she sits by the gates of princes, and sings in the entrances, *saying*,  ^8-3

**8:4** You, O men, I exhort; and utter my voice to the sons of men.  ^8-4

**8:5** O ye simple, understand subtlety, and ye that are untaught, imbibe knowledge.[^1]  ^8-5

**8:6** Hearken to me; for I will speak solemn *truths*; and will produce right *sayings* from my lips.  ^8-6

**8:7** For my throat shall meditate truth; and false lips are an abomination before me.  ^8-7

**8:8** All the words of my mouth are in righteousness; there is nothing in them wrong or perverse.  ^8-8

**8:9** They are all evident to those that understand, and right to those that find knowledge.  ^8-9

**8:10** Receive instruction, and not silver; and knowledge rather than tried gold.  ^8-10

**8:11** For wisdom is better than precious stones; and no valuable substance is of equal worth with it.  ^8-11

**8:12** I wisdom have dwelt *with* counsel and knowledge, and I have called upon understanding.  ^8-12

**8:13** The fear of the Lord hates unrighteousness, and insolence, and pride, and the ways of wicked men; and I hate the perverse ways of bad men.  ^8-13

**8:14** Counsel and safety are mine: prudence is mine, and strength is mine.  ^8-14

**8:15** By me kings reign, and princes decree justice.  ^8-15

**8:16** By me nobles become great, and monarchs by me rule over the earth.  ^8-16

**8:17** I love those that love me; and they that seek me shall find *me*.  ^8-17

**8:18** Wealth and glory belong to me; yea, abundant possessions and righteousness.  ^8-18

**8:19** *It is* better to have my fruit than *to have* gold and precious stones; and my produce is better than choice silver.  ^8-19

**8:20** I walk in ways of righteousness, and *am* conversant with the paths of judgment;  ^8-20

**8:21** that I may divide substance to them that love me, and may fill their treasures with good things. 21a If I declare to you the things that daily happen, I will remember *also* to recount the things of old.  ^8-21

**8:22** The Lord made me the beginning of his ways for his works.  ^8-22

**8:23** He established me before time *was* in the beginning, before he made the earth:[^2]  ^8-23

**8:24** even before he made the depths; before the fountains of water came forth:  ^8-24

**8:25** before the mountains were settled, and before all hills, he begets me.  ^8-25

**8:26** The Lord made countries and uninhabited *tracts*, and the highest inhabited parts of the world.  ^8-26

**8:27** When he prepared the heaven, I was present with him; and when he prepared his throne upon the winds:[^3]  ^8-27

**8:28** and when he strengthened the clouds above; and when he secured the fountains of the earth:  ^8-28

**8:29** and when he strengthened the foundations of the earth:[^4]  ^8-29

**8:30** I was by him, suiting *myself to him*, I was that wherein he took delight; and daily I rejoiced in his presence continually.[^5]  ^8-30

**8:31** For he rejoiced when he had completed the world, and rejoiced among the children of men.  ^8-31

**8:32** Now then, *my* son, hear me: blessed is the man who shall hearken to me, and the mortal who shall keep my ways;[^6]  ^8-32

**8:34** watching daily at my doors, waiting at the posts of my entrances.  ^8-34

**8:35** For my outgoings are the outgoings of life, and *in them* is prepared favour from the Lord.  ^8-35

**8:36** But they that sin against me act wickedly against their own souls: and they that hate me love death.  ^8-36

[^1]: 8:5 For the use of ἄκατος and πανοῦργος in this book, see Appendix.
[^2]: 8:23 *Gr.* before the age.
[^3]: 8:27 *Or,* marked out.
[^4]: 8:29 See Appendix.
[^5]: 8:30 *Or,* arranging all things.
[^6]: 8:32 appendix has Alexandrine text as: “And blessed are they that keep my ways.”
