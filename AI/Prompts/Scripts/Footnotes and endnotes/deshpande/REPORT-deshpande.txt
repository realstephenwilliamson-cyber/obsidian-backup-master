## Result

81 calls in notes 05–11 are links `[[Abhinavagupta 12 - Appendix - Notes and References#^n-<chapter>-<note>|<raised number>]]`; the appendix has the 81 block ids (only endnotes that a link uses get one). 11 appendix paragraphs that ran two endnotes together were split where the second begins. Endnote numbers were not added (Stephen, 18 Oct). Words unchanged; removed are only the OCR's marks where the raised number is printed (’ ” ' " ? ® * ™ ° $ ! and their escapes).

## Why images

The raised numbers are about 6 px high at 150 dpi: ABBYY reads them as “ or *, Tesseract as ’ or nothing, and an automatic detector (`calls.py`) found quote marks and specks as often as calls. So five subagents read every body page (PDF 14–164, three bands each, `bands.py`) and listed each raised number with the words before it (`calls-read-from-images/`). 104 numbers; 4 were verse labels (PDF 119) and were left out. Where a number was blurred, the reader gave the number that fits the sequence and marked it uncertain.

## Method

- `anchors.py`: the endnotes and their chapters from the PDF layer's labels ("1.", "2." …; chapters from the number resets), aligned with the appendix note's words (difflib); the paragraph where each endnote begins, accepted only if its first words match the layer's (96 of 99 anchored).
- `link.py`: per chapter, numbers repeated or unreadable are dropped and only the longest increasing run is kept; each call is found in its page's Markdown by the words before it; a stray mark at the call is removed only if it holds no letters, and printed punctuation after the call is kept; nothing on heading lines. Then the block ids and splits in the appendix.
- `checklinks.py`: markers, headings, frontmatter, words (links and ids set aside), removed characters, every link with exactly one target id. After the apply, a runner job confirmed 81 ids registered and 81 links resolving.
