# Zohar 3.229b

← [[Zohar 3.223b]] · [[Zohar (Soncino)]] · [[Zohar 3.230a]] →

*Volume 3, folio 229b* · Pinchas

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Pinchas 73:453–75:461.*

**Pinchas 75:462** [^1]Now Phineas stood before the stern judgement of Isaac to shield the world and to close the breach, and therefore Phineas here corresponds to Isaac,[^2] and here the Left was united with the Right. HE TURNED BACK MY ANGER, ETC. We have here mentioned the three chiefs of Gehinnom, “Destruction”, “Anger”, and “Wrath”. When he saw the wrath stretching forth from the side of Isaac, he invested himself with [the might of] Isaac and seized thaat wrath like a man who takes hold of another and turns him back. ^pinchas-75-462

[^1]: The first 36 lines of the Hebrew text do not appear in our translation.
[^2]: The reference is to the fact that there is here in the text an extra *yod* in the name *Pinhas*, which makes it numerically equivalent to *Yizhak* (viz. 198).
