# ψυχή

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · noun

**Dodson:** the soul, life, self — (a) the vital breath, breath of life, (b) the human soul, (c) the soul as the seat of affections and will, (d) the self, (e) a human person, an individual. (Strong's 5590; headword ψυχή, ῆς, ἡ)
