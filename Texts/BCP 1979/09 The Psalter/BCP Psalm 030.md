# Psalm 30

← [[BCP Psalm 029]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 031]] →

*Exaltabo te, Domine* · Book One · Sixth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 030]]

**30:1** I will exalt you, O LORD,  
because you have lifted me up \*  
and have not let my enemies triumph over me. ^30-1

**30:2** O LORD my God, I cried out to you, \*  
and you restored me to health. ^30-2

**30:3** You brought me up, O LORD, from the dead; \*  
you restored my life as I was going down to the grave. ^30-3

**30:4** Sing to the LORD, you servants of his; \*  
give thanks for the remembrance of his holiness. ^30-4

**30:5** For his wrath endures but the twinkling of an eye, \*  
his favor for a lifetime. ^30-5

**30:6** Weeping may spend the night, \*  
but joy comes in the morning. ^30-6

**30:7** While I felt secure, I said,  
"I shall never be disturbed. \*  
You, LORD, with your favor, made me as strong as the mountains." ^30-7

**30:8** Then you hid your face, \*  
and I was filled with fear. ^30-8

**30:9** I cried to you, O LORD; \*  
I pleaded with the Lord, saying, ^30-9

**30:10** "What profit is there in my blood, if I go down to the Pit? \*  
will the dust praise you or declare your faithfulness? ^30-10

[p. 622]

**30:11** Hear, O LORD, and have mercy upon me; \*  
O LORD, be my helper." ^30-11

**30:12** You have turned my wailing into dancing; \*  
you have put off my sack-cloth and clothed me with joy. ^30-12

**30:13** Therefore my heart sings to you without ceasing; \*  
O LORD my God, I will give you thanks for ever. ^30-13
