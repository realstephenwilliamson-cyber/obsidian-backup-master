## Chapter 116

**116:1** I love the LORD  
for He hears[^1] my voice, my pleas; ^116-1

**116:2** for He turns His ear to me  
whenever I call. ^116-2

**116:3** The bonds of death encompassed me;  
the torments of Sheol overtook me.  
I came upon trouble and sorrow ^116-3

**116:4** and I invoked the name of the LORD,  
“O LORD, save my life!” ^116-4

**116:5** The LORD is gracious and beneficent;  
our God is compassionate. ^116-5

**116:6** The LORD protects the simple;  
I was brought low and He saved me. ^116-6

**116:7** Be at rest, once again, O my soul,  
for the LORD has been good to you. ^116-7

**116:8** You[^2] have delivered me from death,  
my eyes from tears,  
my feet from stumbling. ^116-8

**116:9** I shall walk before the LORD  
in the lands of the living. ^116-9

**116:10** I trust [in the LORD];  
out of great suffering I spoke[^3] ^116-10

**116:11** and said rashly,  
“All men are false.” ^116-11

**116:12** How can I repay the LORD  
for all His bounties to me? ^116-12

**116:13** I raise the cup of deliverance  
and invoke the name of the LORD. ^116-13

**116:14** I will pay my vows to the LORD  
in the presence of all His people. ^116-14

**116:15** The death of His faithful ones  
is grievous in the LORD’s sight. ^116-15

**116:16** O LORD,  
I am Your servant,  
Your servant, the son of Your maidservant;  
You have undone the cords that bound me. ^116-16

**116:17** I will sacrifice a thank offering to You  
and invoke the name of the LORD. ^116-17

**116:18** I will pay my vows to the LORD  
in the presence of all His people, ^116-18

**116:19** in the courts of the house of the LORD,  
in the midst of[^4] Jerusalem.  
Hallelujah. ^116-19

[^1]: Heb. transposed for clarity; others “I would love that the Lord hear,” etc.
[^2]: I.e., God.
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^4]: Others “of you.”
