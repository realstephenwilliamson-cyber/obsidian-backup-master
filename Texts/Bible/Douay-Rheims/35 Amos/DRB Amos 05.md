# Amos 5 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Amos 04|← Amos 4]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Amos 06|Amos 6 →]]

**5:1** Hear ye this word, which I take up concerning you for a lamentation. The house of Israel is fallen, and it shall rise no more. ^5-1

**5:2** The virgin of Israel is cast down upon her land, there is none to raise her up. ^5-2

**5:3** For thus saith the Lord God: The city, out of which came forth a thousand, there shall be left in it a hundred: and out of which there came a hundred, there shall be left in it ten, in the house of Israel. ^5-3

**5:4** For thus saith the Lord to the house of Israel: Seek ye me, and you shall live. ^5-4

**5:5** But seek not Bethel, and go not into Galgal, neither shall you pass over to Bersabee: for Galgal shall go into captivity, and Bethel shall be unprofitable. ^5-5

**5:6** Seek ye the Lord, and live: lest the house of Joseph be burnt with fire, and it shall devour, and there shall be none to quench Bethel. ^5-6

**5:7** You that turn judgment into wormwood, and forsake justice in the land, ^5-7

**5:8** Seek him that maketh Arcturus, and Orion, and that turneth darkness into morning, and that changeth day into night: that calleth the waters of the sea, and poureth them out upon the face of the earth: The Lord is his name. ^5-8

**5:9** He that with a smile bringeth destruction upon the strong, and waste upon the mighty. ^5-9

**5:10** They have hated him that rebuketh in the gate: and have abhorred him that speaketh perfectly. ^5-10

**5:11** Therefore because you robbed the poor, and took the choice prey from him: you shall build houses with square stone, and shall not dwell in them: you shall plant most delightful vineyards, and shall not drink the wine of them. ^5-11

**5:12** Because I know your manifold crimes, and your grievous sins: enemies of the just, taking bribes, and oppressing the poor in the gate. ^5-12

**5:13** Therefore the prudent shall keep silence at that time, for it is an evil time. ^5-13

**5:14** Seek ye good, and not evil, that you may live: and the Lord the God of hosts will be with you, as you have said. ^5-14

**5:15** Hate evil, and love good, and establish judgment in the gate: it may be the Lord the God of hosts may have mercy on the remnant of Joseph. ^5-15

**5:16** Therefore thus saith the Lord the God of hosts the sovereign Lord: In every street there shall be wailing: and in all places that are without, they shall say: Alas, alas! and they shall call the husbandman to mourning, and such as are skillful in lamentation to lament. ^5-16

**5:17** And in all vineyards there shall be wailing: because I will pass through in the midst of thee, saith the Lord. ^5-17

**5:18** Woe to them that desire the day of the Lord: to what end is it for you? the day of the Lord is darkness, and not light. ^5-18

**5:19** As if a man should flee from the face of a lion, and a bear should meet him: or enter into the house, and lean with his hand upon the wall, and a serpent should bite him. ^5-19

**5:20** Shall not the day of the Lord be darkness, and not light: and obscurity, and no brightness in it? ^5-20

**5:21** I hate, and have rejected your festivities: and I will not receive the odour of your assemblies. ^5-21

**5:22** And if you offer me holocausts, and your gifts, I will not receive them: neither will I regard the vows of your fat beasts. ^5-22

**5:23** Take away from me the tumult of thy songs: and I will not hear the canticles of thy harp. ^5-23

**5:24** But judgment shall be revealed as water, and justice as a mighty torrent. ^5-24

**5:25** Did you offer victims and sacrifices to me in the desert for forty years, O house of Israel? ^5-25

**5:26** But you carried a tabernacle for your Moloch, and the image of your idols, the star of your god, which you made to yourselves. ^5-26

**5:27** And I will cause you to go into captivity beyond Damascus, saith the Lord, the God of hosts is his name. ^5-27
