# Psalm 49

← [[BCP Psalm 048]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 050]] →

*Audite hæc, omnes* · Book Two · Ninth Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 049]]

**49:1** Hear this, all you peoples;  
hearken, all you who dwell in the world, \*  
you of high degree and low, rich and poor together. ^49-1

**49:2** My mouth shall speak of wisdom, \*  
and my heart shall meditate on understanding. ^49-2

**49:3** I will incline my ear to a proverb \*  
and set forth my riddle upon the harp. ^49-3

**49:4** Why should I be afraid in evil days, \*  
when the wickedness of those at my heels surrounds me, ^49-4

**49:5** The wickedness of those who put their trust in their goods, \*  
and boast of their great riches? ^49-5

**49:6** We can never ransom ourselves, \*  
or deliver to God the price of our life; ^49-6

**49:7** For the ransom of our life is so great, \*  
that we should never have enough to pay it, ^49-7

**49:8** In order to live for ever and ever, \*  
and never see the grave. ^49-8

**49:9** For we see that the wise die also;  
like the dull and stupid they perish \*  
and leave their wealth to those who come after them. ^49-9

[p. 653]

**49:10** Their graves shall be their homes for ever,  
their dwelling places from generation to generation, \*  
though they call the lands after their own names. ^49-10

**49:11** Even though honored, they cannot live for ever; \*  
they are like the beasts that perish. ^49-11

**49:12** Such is the way of those who foolishly trust in themselves, \*  
and the end of those who delight in their own words. ^49-12

**49:13** Like a flock of sheep they are destined to die;  
Death is their shepherd; \*  
they go down straightway to the grave. ^49-13

**49:14** Their form shall waste away, \*  
and the land of the dead shall be their home. ^49-14

**49:15** But God will ransom my life; \*  
he will snatch me from the grasp of death. ^49-15

**49:16** Do not be envious when some become rich, \*  
or when the grandeur of their house increases; ^49-16

**49:17** For they will carry nothing away at their death, \*  
nor will their grandeur follow them. ^49-17

**49:18** Though they thought highly of themselves while they lived, \*  
and were praised for their success, ^49-18

**49:19** They shall join the company of their forebears, \*  
who will never see the light again. ^49-19

**49:20** Those who are honored, but have no understanding, \*  
are like the beasts that perish. ^49-20
