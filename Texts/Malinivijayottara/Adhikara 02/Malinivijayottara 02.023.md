## Mūla

अकिम्चिच्चिन्तकस्यैव गुरुणा प्रतिबोधतः ।
जायते यः समावेशः शाम्भवोऽसावुदाहृतः ॥ ^mula

akimciccintakasyaiva guruṇā pratibodhataḥ |
jāyate[^40] yaḥ samāveśaḥ śāmbhavo 'sāv udāhṛtaḥ[^41] || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, July 2022 – September 2022
> p. 33 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Malini (journal), July-September 2022.pdf#page=35>))
>
> The penetration (*āveśa*) that arises for one who does not think of anything, by the awakening (*prabodhataḥ*) brought about by the teacher (*guruṇā*)[^42], is said to be the one pertaining to Śiva (*śāmbhava*).

[^40]: VB *utpadyate ya āveśa*
[^41]: MS G: *udīrita*
[^42]: Abhinavagupta understands the meaning here to be 'by an intense (*guruṇā*) awakening (*prabodhataḥ*)'. See TĀ 1/171-172 for his explanation.
