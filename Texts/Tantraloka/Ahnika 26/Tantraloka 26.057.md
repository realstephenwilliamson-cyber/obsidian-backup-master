## Mūla

रक्तकर्पासतूलेच्छुस्तुल्यतद्बीजपुञ्जवत् ।
सन्ति भोगे विशेषाश्च विचित्राः कारणेरिताः ॥ ^mula

raktakarpāsatūlecchustulyatadbījapuñjavat |
santi bhoge viśeṣāśca vicitrāḥ kāraṇeritāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 17 - Chapter Twenty-Five (continued) and Chapter Twenty-Six#^pg410|p. 390]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=410>))
>
> One who wants a particular result should lay hold of (spṛśet) the particular (corresponding) cause, just as one who desires (to cultivate) saffron (rakta) and varieties of cotton (karpāsa and tūla) (requires their corresponding) seeds, which are as they are. (Thus,) the (many) particular worldly experiences (bhoga) are determined by (as many) varied causes. (with 26.56cd–57)
