# Psalm 135

← [[BCP Psalm 134]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 136]] →

*Laudate nomen* · Book Five · Twenty-eighth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 135]]

**135:1** Hallelujah!  
Praise the Name of the LORD; \*  
give praise, you servants of the LORD. ^135-1

**135:2** You who stand in the house of the LORD, \*  
in the courts of the house of our God. ^135-2

**135:3** Praise the LORD, for the LORD is good; \*  
sing praises to his Name, for it is lovely. ^135-3

**135:4** For the LORD has chosen Jacob for himself \*  
and Israel for his own possession. ^135-4

**135:5** For I know that the LORD is great, \*  
and that our Lord is above all gods. ^135-5

**135:6** The LORD does whatever pleases him, in heaven and on earth, \*  
in the seas and all the deeps. ^135-6

**135:7** He brings up rain clouds from the ends of the earth; \*  
he sends out lightning with the rain,  
and brings the winds out of his storehouse. ^135-7

**135:8** It was he who struck down the firstborn of Egypt, \*  
the firstborn both of man and beast. ^135-8

**135:9** He sent signs and wonders into the midst of you, O Egypt, \*  
against Pharaoh and all his servants. ^135-9

**135:10** He overthrew many nations \*  
and put mighty kings to death: ^135-10

**135:11** Sihon, king of the Amorites,  
and Og, the king of Bashan, \*  
and all the kingdoms of Canaan. ^135-11

**135:12** He gave their land to be an inheritance, \*  
an inheritance for Israel his people. ^135-12

[p. 789] ^pg789

**135:13** O LORD, your Name is everlasting; \*  
your renown, O LORD, endures from age to age. ^135-13

**135:14** For the LORD gives his people justice \*  
and shows compassion to his servants. ^135-14

**135:15** The idols of the heathen are silver and gold, \*  
the work of human hands. ^135-15

**135:16** They have mouths, but they cannot speak; \*  
eyes have they, but they cannot see. ^135-16

**135:17** They have ears, but they cannot hear; \*  
neither is there any breath in their mouth. ^135-17

**135:18** Those who make them are like them, \*  
and so are all who put their trust in them. ^135-18

**135:19** Bless the LORD, O house of Israel; \*  
O house of Aaron, bless the LORD. ^135-19

**135:20** Bless the LORD, O house of Levi; \*  
you who fear the LORD, bless the LORD. ^135-20

**135:21** Blessed be the LORD out of Zion, \*  
who dwells in Jerusalem.  
Hallelujah! ^135-21
