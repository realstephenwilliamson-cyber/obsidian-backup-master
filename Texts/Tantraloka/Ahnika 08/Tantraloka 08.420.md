## Mūla

कामादिसप्तविंशकमागन्तु तथा गणेशविद्येशमयौ ।
इति पाशेषु पुरत्रयमित्थं पुरुषेऽत्र भुवनषोडशकम् ॥ ^mula

kāmādisaptaviṃśakamāgantu tathā gaṇeśavidyeśamayau |
iti pāśeṣu puratrayamitthaṃ puruṣe'tra bhuvanaṣoḍaśakam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 11 - Chapter Eight, part 9#^pg399|p. 381]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=399>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 8.420cd–421ab.
>
> Here, in the individual soul, the worlds are sixteen. These are 1) the group of Contentments, consisting of Ambā and the rest, and 3) (the goddesses) from Tārā onwards, 3) the group of (yogic) accomplishments, beginning with the power to become small at will (animā), 4) the teachers, 5) disciples of the teachers and 6) the sages, 7) the (eight channels), namely, Iqdā, and the rest, 8) the group of eight that constitutes the subtle body, 9) the world of the (sixteen) permutations, from smell (up to the mind), 10) the eight qualities of the intellect, 11) the ego, 12) the qualities of the objects of the senses, 13) the (ten) qualities (of the gross body), 14) the three worlds in the fetters of twenty-seven adjuncts, desire (kāma) and the rest, and those of 15) Gaṇeśa and 16) Vidyeśa. (with 8.418cd–420)
