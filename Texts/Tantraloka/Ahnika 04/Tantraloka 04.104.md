## Mūla

तदद्वयायां संवित्तावभ्यासोऽनुपयोगवान् ।
केवलं द्वैतमालिन्यशङ्कानिर्मूलनाय सः ॥ ^mula

tadadvayāyāṃ saṃvittāvabhyāso'nupayogavān |
kevalaṃ dvaitamālinyaśaṅkānirmūlanāya saḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> As far as that nondual consciousness is concerned, (repeated) practice (abhyāsa) is useless (as it always perfect). (Practice) serves only to uproot the doubt that (consciousness is in any way) sullied by duality.
