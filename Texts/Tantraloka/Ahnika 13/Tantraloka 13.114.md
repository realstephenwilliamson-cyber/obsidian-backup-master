## Mūla

कृत्यै मलं तथा कर्म शिवेच्छैवेति सुस्थितम् ।
यत्तु कस्मिंश्चन शिवः स्वेन रूपेण भासते ॥ ^mula

kṛtyai malaṃ tathā karma śivecchaiveti susthitam |
yattu kasmiṃścana śivaḥ svena rūpeṇa bhāsate || ^iast

> It is well established in this way that Śiva’s will requires Māyā, Karma and Impurity to carry out the three (acts of) creation, persistence and destruction. (with 13.113cd–114ab)
