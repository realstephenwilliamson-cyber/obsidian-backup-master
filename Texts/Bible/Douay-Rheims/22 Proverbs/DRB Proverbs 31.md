# Proverbs 31 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Proverbs 30|← Proverbs 30]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · —

**31:1** The words of king Lamuel. The vision wherewith his mother instructed him. ^31-1

**31:2** What, O my beloved, what, O the beloved of my womb, what, O the beloved of my vows? ^31-2

**31:3** Give not thy substance to women, and thy riches to destroy kings. ^31-3

**31:4** Give not to kings, O Lamuel, give not wine to kings: because there is no secret where drunkenness reigneth: ^31-4

**31:5** And lest they drink and forget judgments, and pervert the cause of the children of the poor. ^31-5

**31:6** Give strong drink to them that are sad: and wine to them that are grieved in mind: ^31-6

**31:7** Let them drink, and forget their want, and remember their sorrow no more. ^31-7

**31:8** Open thy mouth for the dumb, and for the causes of all the children that pass. ^31-8

**31:9** Open thy mouth, decree that which is just, and do justice to the needy and poor. ^31-9

**31:10** Who shall find a valiant woman? far and from the uttermost coasts is the price of her. ^31-10

**31:11** The heart of her husband trusteth in her, and he shall have no need of spoils. ^31-11

**31:12** She will render him good, and not evil, all the days of her life. ^31-12

**31:13** She hath sought wool and flax, and hath wrought by the counsel of her hands. ^31-13

**31:14** She is like the merchant’s ship, she bringeth her bread from afar. ^31-14

**31:15** And she hath risen in the night, and given a prey to her household, and victuals to her maidens. ^31-15

**31:16** She hath considered a field, and bought it: with the fruit of her hands she hath planted a vineyard. ^31-16

**31:17** She hath girded her loins with strength, and hath strengthened her arm. ^31-17

**31:18** She hath tasted and seen that her traffic is good: her lamp shall not be put out in the night. ^31-18

**31:19** She hath put out her hand to strong things, and her fingers have taken hold of the spindle. ^31-19

**31:20** She hath opened her hand to the needy, and stretched out her hands to the poor. ^31-20

**31:21** She shall not fear for her house in the cold of snow: for all her domestics are clothed with double garments. ^31-21

**31:22** She hath made for herself clothing of tapestry: fine linen, and purple is her covering. ^31-22

**31:23** Her husband is honourable in the gates, when he sitteth among the senators of the land. ^31-23

**31:24** She made fine linen, and sold it, and delivered a girdle to the Chanaanite. ^31-24

**31:25** Strength and beauty are her clothing, and she shall laugh in the latter day. ^31-25

**31:26** She hath opened her mouth to wisdom, and the law of clemency is on her tongue. ^31-26

**31:27** She hath looked well to the paths of her house, and hath not eaten her bread idle. ^31-27

**31:28** Her children rose up, and called her blessed: her husband, and he praised her. ^31-28

**31:29** Many daughters have gathered together riches: thou hast surpassed them all. ^31-29

**31:30** Favour is deceitful, and beauty is vain: the woman that feareth the Lord, she shall be praised. ^31-30

**31:31** Give her of the fruit of her hands: and let her works praise her in the gates. ^31-31
