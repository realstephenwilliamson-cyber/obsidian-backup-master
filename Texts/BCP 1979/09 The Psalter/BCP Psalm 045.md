# Psalm 45

← [[BCP Psalm 044]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 046]] →

*Eructavit cor meum* · Book Two · Ninth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 045]]

**45:1** My heart is stirring with a noble song;  
let me recite what I have fashioned for the king; \*  
my tongue shall be the pen of a skilled writer. ^45-1

**45:2** You are the fairest of men; \*  
grace flows from your lips,  
because God has blessed you for ever. ^45-2

**45:3** Strap your sword upon your thigh, O mighty warrior, \*  
in your pride and in your majesty. ^45-3

**45:4** Ride out and conquer in the cause of truth \*  
and for the sake of justice. ^45-4

**45:5** Your right hand will show you marvelous things; \*  
your arrows are very sharp, O mighty warrior. ^45-5

**45:6** The peoples are falling at your feet, \*  
and the king's enemies are losing heart. ^45-6

[p. 648] ^pg648

**45:7** Your throne, O God, endures for ever and ever, \*  
a scepter of righteousness is the scepter of your kingdom;  
you love righteousness and hate iniquity. ^45-7

**45:8** Therefore God, your God, has anointed you \*  
with the oil of gladness above your fellows. ^45-8

**45:9** All your garments are fragrant with myrrh, aloes, and cassia, \*  
and the music of strings from ivory palaces makes you glad. ^45-9

**45:10** Kings' daughters stand among the ladies of the court; \*  
on your right hand is the queen,  
adorned with the gold of Ophir. ^45-10

**45:11** "Hear, O daughter; consider and listen closely; \*  
forget your people and your father's house. ^45-11

**45:12** The king will have pleasure in your beauty; \*  
he is your master; therefore do him honor. ^45-12

**45:13** The people of Tyre are here with a gift; \*  
the rich among the people seek your favor." ^45-13

**45:14** All glorious is the princess as she enters; \*  
her gown is cloth-of-gold. ^45-14

**45:15** In embroidered apparel she is brought to the king; \*  
after her the bridesmaids follow in procession. ^45-15

**45:16** With joy and gladness they are brought, \*  
and enter into the palace of the king. ^45-16

**45:17** "In place of fathers, O king, you shall have sons; \*  
you shall make them princes over all the earth. ^45-17

**45:18** I will make your name to be remembered  
from one generation to another; \*  
therefore nations will praise you for ever and ever." ^45-18
