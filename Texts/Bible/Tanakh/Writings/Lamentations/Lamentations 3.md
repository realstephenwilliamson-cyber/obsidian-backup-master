# Lamentations 3

## Chapter 3

**3:1** I am the man who has known affliction  
Under[^1] the rod of His wrath; ^3-1

**3:2** Me He drove on and on  
In unrelieved darkness; ^3-2

**3:3** On none but me He brings down His hand  
Again and again, without cease. ^3-3

**3:4** He has worn away my flesh and skin;  
He has shattered my bones. ^3-4

**3:5** All around me He has built  
Misery[^2] and hardship; ^3-5

**3:6** He has made me dwell in darkness,  
Like those long dead. ^3-6

**3:7** He has walled me in and I cannot break out;  
He has weighed me down with chains. ^3-7

**3:8** And when I cry and plead,  
He shuts out my prayer; ^3-8

**3:9** He has walled in my ways with hewn blocks,  
He has made my paths a maze. ^3-9

**3:10** He is a lurking bear to me,  
A lion in hiding; ^3-10

**3:11** He has forced me off my way[^3] and mangled me,  
He has left me numb. ^3-11

**3:12** He has bent His bow and made me  
The target of His arrows: ^3-12

**3:13** He has shot into my vitals  
The shafts of His quiver. ^3-13

**3:14** I have become a laughingstock to all people,  
The butt of their gibes all day long. ^3-14

**3:15** He has filled me with bitterness,  
Sated me with wormwood. ^3-15

**3:16** He has broken my teeth on gravel,  
Has ground me into the dust. ^3-16

**3:17** My life was bereft of peace,  
I forgot what happiness was. ^3-17

**3:18** I thought my strength and hope  
Had perished before the LORD. ^3-18

**3:19** To recall my distress and my misery  
Was wormwood and poison; ^3-19

**3:20** Whenever I thought of them,  
I was bowed low. ^3-20

**3:21** But this do I call to mind,  
Therefore I have hope: ^3-21

**3:22** The kindness of the LORD has not ended,  
His mercies are not spent. ^3-22

**3:23** They are renewed every morning—  
Ample is Your grace! ^3-23

**3:24** “The LORD is my portion,” I say with full heart;  
Therefore will I hope in Him. ^3-24

**3:25** The LORD is good to those who trust in Him,  
To the one who seeks Him; ^3-25

**3:26** It is good to wait patiently  
Till rescue comes from the LORD. ^3-26

**3:27** It is good for a man, when young,  
To bear a yoke; ^3-27

**3:28** Let him sit alone and be patient,  
When He has laid it upon him. ^3-28

**3:29** Let him put his mouth to the dust—  
There may yet be hope. ^3-29

**3:30** Let him offer his cheek to the smiter;  
Let him be surfeited with mockery. ^3-30

**3:31** For the Lord does not  
Reject forever, ^3-31

**3:32** But first afflicts, then pardons  
In His abundant kindness. ^3-32

**3:33** For He does not willfully bring grief  
Or affliction to man, ^3-33

**3:34** Crushing under His feet  
All the prisoners of the earth. ^3-34

**3:35** To deny a man his rights  
In the presence of the Most High, ^3-35

**3:36** To wrong a man in his cause—  
This the Lord does not choose. ^3-36

**3:37** Whose decree was ever fulfilled,  
Unless the Lord willed it? ^3-37

**3:38** Is it not at the word of the Most High,  
That weal and woe befall? ^3-38

**3:39** Of what shall a living man complain?  
Each one of his own sins! ^3-39

**3:40** Let us search and examine our ways,  
And turn back to the LORD; ^3-40

**3:41** Let us lift up our hearts with[^4] our hands  
To God in heaven: ^3-41

**3:42** We have transgressed and rebelled,  
And You have not forgiven. ^3-42

**3:43** You have clothed Yourself in anger and pursued us,  
You have slain without pity. ^3-43

**3:44** You have screened Yourself off with a cloud,  
That no prayer may pass through. ^3-44

**3:45** You have made us filth and refuse  
In the midst of the peoples. ^3-45

**3:46** All our enemies loudly  
Rail against us. ^3-46

**3:47** Panic and pitfall are our lot,  
Death and destruction. ^3-47

**3:48** My eyes shed streams of water  
Over the ruin of my poor[^5] people. ^3-48

**3:49** My eyes shall flow without cease,  
Without respite, ^3-49

**3:50** [^6]Until the LORD looks down  
And beholds from heaven. ^3-50

**3:51** My eyes have brought me grief  
Over all the maidens of my city. ^3-51

**3:52** My foes have snared me like a bird,  
Without any cause. ^3-52

**3:53** They have ended my life in a pit  
And cast stones at me. ^3-53

**3:54** Waters flowed over my head;  
I said: I am lost! ^3-54

**3:55** I have called on Your name, O LORD,  
From the depths of the Pit. ^3-55

**3:56** Hear my plea;  
Do not shut Your ear  
To my groan, to my cry! ^3-56

**3:57** You have ever drawn nigh when I called You;  
You have said, “Do not fear!” ^3-57

**3:58** You championed my cause, O Lord,  
You have redeemed my life. ^3-58

**3:59** You have seen, O LORD, the wrong done me;  
Oh, vindicate my right! ^3-59

**3:60** You have seen all their malice,  
All their designs against me; ^3-60

**3:61** You have heard, O LORD, their taunts,  
All their designs against me, ^3-61

**3:62** The mouthings and pratings of my adversaries  
Against me all day long. ^3-62

**3:63** See how, at their ease or at work,  
I am the butt of their gibes. ^3-63

**3:64** Give them, O LORD, their deserts  
According to their deeds. ^3-64

**3:65** Give them anguish[^7] of heart;  
Your curse be upon them! ^3-65

**3:66** Oh, pursue them in wrath and destroy them  
From under the heavens of the LORD! ^3-66

[^1]: Emendation yields “whom the Lord has shepherded with.”
[^2]: Taking rosh as equivalent to resh.
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^4]: Lit. “to”; emendation yields “rather than”; cf. Joel 2.13.
[^5]: Lit. “the daughter of my”; so frequently in poetry.
[^6]: Emendation yields: 50“Until the Lord looks down from heaven/ And beholds 51my affliction. / The Lord has brought me grief.”
[^7]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
