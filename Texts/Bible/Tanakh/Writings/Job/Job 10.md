## Chapter 10

**10:1** I am disgusted with life;  
I will give rein to my complaint,  
Speak in the bitterness of my soul. ^10-1

**10:2** I say to God, “Do not condemn me;  
Let me know what You charge me with. ^10-2

**10:3** Does it benefit You to defraud,  
To despise the toil of Your hands,  
While smiling on the counsel of the wicked? ^10-3

**10:4** Do You have the eyes of flesh?  
Is Your vision that of mere men? ^10-4

**10:5** Are Your days the days of a mortal,  
Are Your years the years of a man, ^10-5

**10:6** That You seek my iniquity  
And search out my sin? ^10-6

**10:7** You know that I am not guilty,  
And that there is none to deliver from Your hand. ^10-7

**10:8** “Your hands shaped and fashioned me,  
Then destroyed every part of me. ^10-8

**10:9** Consider that You fashioned me like clay;  
Will You then turn me back into dust? ^10-9

**10:10** You poured me out like milk,  
Congealed me like cheese; ^10-10

**10:11** You clothed me with skin and flesh  
And wove me of bones and sinews; ^10-11

**10:12** You bestowed on me life and care;  
Your providence watched over my spirit. ^10-12

**10:13** Yet these things You hid in Your heart;  
I know that You had this in mind: ^10-13

**10:14** To watch me when I sinned  
And not clear me of my iniquity; ^10-14

**10:15** Should I be guilty—the worse for me!  
And even when innocent, I cannot lift my head;  
So sated am I with shame,  
And drenched in my misery. ^10-15

**10:16** It is something to be proud of[^1] to hunt me like a lion,  
To show Yourself wondrous through[^2] me time and again! ^10-16

**10:17** You keep sending fresh witnesses against me,  
Letting Your vexation with me grow.  
I serve my term and am my own replacement.[^3] ^10-17

**10:18** “Why did You let me come out of the womb?  
Better had I expired before any eye saw me, ^10-18

**10:19** Had I been as though I never was,  
Had I been carried from the womb to the grave. ^10-19

**10:20** My days are few, so desist!  
Leave me alone, let me be diverted a while ^10-20

**10:21** Before I depart—never to return—  
For the land of deepest gloom; ^10-21

**10:22** A land whose light is darkness,  
All gloom and disarray,  
Whose light is like darkness.” ^10-22

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Or “make sport of”; cf. Pal. Aram. ’afli.
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
