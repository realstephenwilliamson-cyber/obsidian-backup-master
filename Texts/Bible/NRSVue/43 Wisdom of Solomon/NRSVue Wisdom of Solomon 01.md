# Wisdom of Solomon 1 (NRSVue)

— · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Wisdom of Solomon 02|Wisdom of Solomon 2 →]]

## Exhortation to uprightness

**1:1** Love righteousness, you rulers of the earth; think of the Lord in goodness and seek him with sincerity of heart, ^1-1

**1:2** because he is found by those who do not put him to the test and manifests himself to those who do not distrust him. ^1-2

**1:3** For perverse thoughts separate people from God, and when his power is tested, it exposes the foolish, ^1-3

**1:4** because wisdom will not enter a deceitful soul or dwell in a body enslaved to sin. ^1-4

**1:5** For a holy and disciplined spirit will flee from deceit and will leave foolish thoughts behind and will be ashamed at the approach of unrighteousness. ^1-5

**1:6** For wisdom is a kindly spirit, but it will not free blasphemers from the guilt of their words, because God is witness of their inmost feelings and a true observer of their hearts and a hearer of their tongues. ^1-6

**1:7** Because the spirit of the Lord has filled the world and that which holds all things together knows what is said, ^1-7

**1:8** therefore those who utter unrighteous things will not escape notice, and justice, when it punishes, will not pass them by. ^1-8

**1:9** For inquiry will be made into the counsels of the ungodly, and a report of their words will come to the Lord, to convict them of their lawless deeds, ^1-9

**1:10** because a jealous ear hears all things, and the sound of grumbling does not go unheard. ^1-10

**1:11** Beware, then, of useless grumbling, and keep your tongue from slander, because no secret word is without result,^[Or *will go unpunished*] and a lying mouth destroys the soul. ^1-11

**1:12** Do not court death by the error of your life or bring on destruction by the works of your hands, ^1-12

**1:13** because God did not make death, and he does not delight in the destruction of the living. ^1-13

**1:14** For he created all things so that they might exist; the generative forces^[Or *the creatures*] of the world are wholesome, and there is no destructive poison in them, and the dominion^[Or *palace*] of Hades is not on earth. ^1-14

**1:15** For righteousness is immortal.^[OL adds *but unrighteousness is the acquisition of death*] ^1-15

## Life as the ungodly see it

**1:16** But the ungodly by their words and deeds summoned death;^[Gk *him*] considering him a friend, they pined away and made a covenant with him, because they are fit to belong to his company. ^1-16
