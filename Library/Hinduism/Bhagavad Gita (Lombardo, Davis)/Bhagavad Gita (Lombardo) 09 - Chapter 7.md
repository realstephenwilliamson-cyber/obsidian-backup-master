## Chapter Seven

The Blessed Lord continued:

\[1\] “With your mind absorbed in Me,
practicing *Yoga* intent on Me,
you will know Me, Son of Pritha,
wholly and beyond doubt. Listen!

\[2\] I will completely explain to you
a discriminating knowledge
that, once it has been understood,
leaves nothing more to be learned here.

\[3\] Of myriads of human beings
hardly any strive for perfection.
Of those who strive and are perfected
hardly any know Me as I am.

\[4\] Earth and water, fire and wind,
space, mind, intellect, and ego:
My material nature is
divided into these eight parts.

\[5\] That is My inferior nature,
but know that it is different from
My highest, spiritual nature
by which this universe is sustained.

\[6\] My highest nature is the womb
of all beings. Understand this!
I am the origin and the end
of the entire universe.

[p. 45] \[7\] There is nothing higher than Me,
O Arjuna, Wealth’s Conqueror.
All that exists is strung on Me
like pearls and jewels on a thread.

\[8\] I am the liquid taste of water,
the brightness of the sun and moon,
I am *Om* in all the *Vedas*,
sound in air, men’s virility.

\[9\] I am the earth’s pleasing fragrance
the brilliance in the flaring sun.
I am the life in all beings
and the ardor in ascetics.

\[10\] Know that I am the primal seed
of all beings, Son of Pritha,
the intellect of the intelligent,
the radiance of the radiant.

\[11\] I am the might of the mighty,
free from passionate desire;
and I am the lawful desire
of those beings, O Bharata.

\[12\] I am the *sattvic* qualities,
the *rajasic* and *tamasic*.
Know that these are from Me also,
not I in them, but they in Me.

\[13\] The whole universe is deluded
by the threefold modes of being
caused by the *gunas*. It knows not Me,
higher than these and eternal.

\[14\] This illusion, produced by Me
from the *gunas*, is indeed divine.
[p. 46] Only those who take refuge in Me
can pass beyond this illusion.

\[15\] Wrongdoers, the lowest of men,
do not seek Me. They are deluded,
deprived of knowledge by illusion,
attached to a demonic life.

\[16\] Among good men, there are four kinds
who honor me: those who suffer,
who seek knowledge, seek spiritual wealth,
and the wise man, O Arjuna.

\[17\] Of these four, the man of wisdom,
steadfast forever, devoted to Me,
is foremost. I am dear to him,
and he is very dear to Me.

\[18\] All of them indeed are noble,
but the wise man is thought to be
My very Self; his steadfast mind
abides in Me, the highest goal.

\[19\] The wise man, after many births,
takes refuge in Me, thinking this:
‘Vasudeva’s son is all!’
Such a great soul is hard to find.

\[20\] Men whose desires for this and that
have left them bereft of knowledge
resort to various gods and rites,
controlled by their material nature.

\[21\] Whoever wishes to honor
whatever worshipped form with faith,
I bestow upon that person
a faith that is unshakable.

[p. 47] \[22\] When one who is yoked to this faith
wants to beseech whatever form,
he receives from it his desires
because forms are ordained by Me.

\[23\] But the fruit is impermanent
for those with scant understanding.
Those worshippers go to their gods;
My worshippers surely come to Me.

\[24\] Though I am formless, the mindless think
that I have fallen into forms.
They do not know My higher being
is unsurpassed and eternal.

\[25\] Veiled over in *Yogic* magic
I am not apparent to all.
This muddled world does not see Me,
birthless and imperishable.

\[26\] I know those who have departed
and know the living, O Arjuna,
as well as beings yet to be,
but there is no one who knows Me.

\[27\] Hatred and desire rising up
and the illusion of opposites
cause all beings, O Bharata,
to become deluded at their birth.

\[28\] But those whose evil ways have ended,
those who are pure in their actions,
free from delusive duality,
worship Me with unyielding vows.

[p. 48] \[29\] Those who depend on Me and strive
for release from old age and death
comprehend *Brahman* completely,
the highest Self, and all action.

\[30\] They know I am the Supreme Being,
Supreme God, Lord of Sacrifice.
Even at the hour of death
their minds are steady, fixed on Me.”
