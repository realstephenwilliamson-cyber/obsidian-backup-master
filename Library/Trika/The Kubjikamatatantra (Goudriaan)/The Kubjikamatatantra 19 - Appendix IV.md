## Appendix IV

On mandalavidhāna: ślokas given at the beginning of paṭala 25 by EJT. The text follows the J version, unless otherwise indicated.

śrīdevy uvāca ||

1 maṇqdalānāṇm vidhānam tu, prasādam kuru bhairava |

yena jānāmy aham deva, kathayasva vidhānatah ||

śrībhairava uvāca ||

2 sāraṇm maṇdalam ākhyātaṇm, phalaṇm sāraṇm parāparam |

lāti yasmād yamātītaṇm, maṇqdalam tena kīrtitam ||

3 makāre mātarah sarvā, dakāre dqāmarīgaṇam |

lakāre lākinīvargam, maṇqdalam tena cocyate ||

4 makāre nityam ātmānam, qdakāre khecarīgaṇam |

lakāre bhūcarīvargo, maṇdalam tena kīrtitam ||

5 makāre ’nāma yo devo, dakāre śaktir iṣyate |

lakāre ṣadvidhā srṣṭir, maṇqdalam tena kīrtitam ||

6 makāram śivatattvam ca, vidyātattvam qdakāragam |

lakāram ātmatattvam tu, kīrtitaṇm tena maṇdalam ||

7 makāre tu śivam vidyād, dakāre parameśvarī |

lakāre saptakoṭyas tu, mantrāṇām parikīrtitam ||

8 makāre kāraṇāh pañca, dakāre tu parāparā |

lakāre aparāh pañca, tena maṇqdala kīrtitam ||

9 brahmā viṣṇuś ca rudraś ca, īívaraś ca sadāśivah |

ucyate maṇqalenaite, tena maṇdalam ucyate ||

10 sarve grahāś ca dikpālā, nāgā vai bhairavādayah |

tiṣthanti maṇqale līināh, sarvādhikyam ato punah ||

11 anantādyāh śivāntādhvā, ṣadvidhah samprakīrtitah |

āste maṇqdalake so hi, maṇdalaṇm vyāpakam tatah ||

12 sarvavyāpitayā nānyo, maṇqdalābhyadhiko yatah |

tena maṇdalam abhyarcya\[m\], bhuktimuktiphalārthibhih ||

13 itīṣṭaṇm maṇqdalādhyāyam, nirdiṣtam kubjikāmate |

kuryāt pradakṣiṇam devi, apasavye visarjanam ||

14 śrīmatasyāgrato devi, tad evam gurusannidhau |

suprasiddhām tato jyeṣthāṇm, suparīkṣya prayatnatah ||

15 saptāvimśatibhir bhedaih, praśnam etat kramāgatam |

ṣatprakāratrayaś cājñā, yeṣāmṇm tīvrāvalokanam ||

16 subhaktā vatsalāh śāntā, dambhamāyāvivarjitāh |

teṣāṇm maṇqdalakam kuryād, anyathā tu vilomakrt ||
