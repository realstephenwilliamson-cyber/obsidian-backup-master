## Mūla

हृदयेन चरोः सिद्धिर्याज्ञिकैः क्षीरतण्डुलैः ।
संपातं सप्तभिर्मन्त्रैस्ततः षड्भागभाजितम् ॥ ^mula

hṛdayena caroḥ siddhir yājñikaiḥ kṣīrataṇḍulaiḥ |
saṁpātaṁ saptabhir mantrais tataḥ ṣaḍbhāgabhājitam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2025 – April 2025
> p. 34 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=48>))
>
> **The Introduction of the Neophant**
>
> He should then introduce (his) disciples, who are pure, have bathed and are fasting, (into the abode of the sacrifice). Then, after having bowed to the Lord of the gods who is present in the four (things)[^135] in due order, he should give (his disciples) the five products of a cow, the sacrificial pap, and (the stick) to clean the teeth. The sacrificial pap is (prepared) successfully with sacrificial rice boiled in milk and (the utterance of the) mantra of the Heart (while it cooks).[^136] (with 8.119cd–121ab)

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2025 – April 2025
> p. 34 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=48>))
>
> (Then) the oblation (saṁpāta) is offered with (the utterance of) seven mantras[^138] and (the sacrificial pap) is then divided into six (equal) portions. (These are offered) equally to Śiva, the fire, the teacher and disciples and to the small (vārdhānī) and large jars (kumbha).[^139] (with 8.121cd–122ab)

[^135]: It appears from TĀ 15/477 (473) that these four are the big and little jars, the fire and the Self.
[^136]: See TĀ 15/437-438.
[^138]: It seems that according to TĀ 15/439 ff. these Mantras are those of the three goddesses, the three Śaktis and Mātṛsadbhāva.
[^139]: MV 8/121-122ab is quoted in TĀv ad 15/447cd-449 (444cd-446). See TĀ 15/444cd-445. There Śiva is replaced by the sthaṇḍila, that is, the sacrificial surface.
