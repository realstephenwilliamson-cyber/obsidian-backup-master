# LXX 1 Kingdoms 16

← [[LXX 1 Kingdoms 15]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX 1 Kingdoms 17]] →

*1 Kingdoms · [[LXX]]*

**16:1** And the Lord said to Samuel, How long dost thou mourn for Saul, whereas I have rejected him from reigning over Israel? Fill thy horn with oil, and come, I will send thee to Jessæ, to Bethleem; for I have seen among his sons a king for me.  ^16-1

**16:2** And Samuel said, How can I go? whereas Saul will hear of it, and slay me: and the Lord said, Take a heifer in thine hand and thou shalt say, I am come to sacrifice to the Lord.  ^16-2

**16:3** And thou shalt call Jessæ to the sacrifice, and I will make known to thee what thou shalt do; and thou shalt anoint him whom I shall mention to thee.  ^16-3

**16:4** And Samuel did all that the Lord told him; and he came to Bethleem: and the elders of the city were amazed at meeting him, and said, Dost thou come peaceably, thou Seer?[^1]  ^16-4

**16:5** And he said, Peaceably: I am come to sacrifice to the Lord. Sanctify yourselves, and rejoice with me this day: and he sanctified Jessæ and his sons, and he called them to the sacrifice.[^2]  ^16-5

**16:6** And it came to pass when they came in, that he saw Eliab, and said, Surely the Lord's anointed *is* before him.  ^16-6

**16:7** But the Lord said to Samuel, Look not on his appearance, nor on his stature, for I have rejected him; for God sees not as a man looks; for man looks at the outward appearance, but God looks at the heart.[^3][^4]  ^16-7

**16:8** And Jessæ called Aminadab, and he passed before Samuel: and he said, Neither has God chosen this one.  ^16-8

**16:9** And Jessæ caused Sama to pass by: and he said, Neither has God chosen this one.[^5]  ^16-9

**16:10** And Jessæ caused his seven sons to pass before Samuel: and Samuel said, The Lord has not chosen these.  ^16-10

**16:11** And Samuel said to Jessæ, Hast thou no more sons? And Jessæ said, *There is* yet a little one; behold, he tends the flock. And Samuel said to Jessæ, Send and fetch him, for we may not sit down till he comes.[^6][^7]  ^16-11

**16:12** And he sent and fetched him: and he was ruddy, with beauty of eyes, and very goodly to behold. And the Lord said to Samuel, Arise, and anoint David, for he is good.[^8]  ^16-12

**16:13** And Samuel took the horn of oil, and anointed him in the midst of his brethren: and the Spirit of the Lord came upon David from that day forward: and Samuel arose, and departed to Armathaim.[^9]  ^16-13

**16:14** And the Spirit of the Lord departed from Saul, and an evil spirit from the Lord tormented him.[^10]  ^16-14

**16:15** And Saul's servants said to him, Behold now, an evil spirit from the Lord torments thee.  ^16-15

**16:16** Let now thy servants speak before thee, and let them seek for our lord a man skilled to play on the harp; and it shall come to pass when an evil spirit comes upon thee and he shall play on his harp, that thou shalt be well, and he shall refresh thee.  ^16-16

**16:17** And Saul said to his servants, Look now out for me a skilful player, and bring him to me.[^11]  ^16-17

**16:18** And one of his servants answered and said, Behold, I have seen a son of Jessæ the Bethleemite, and he understands playing *on the harp*, and the man *is* prudent, and a warrior, and wise in speech, and the man *is* handsome, and the Lord *is* with him.[^12]  ^16-18

**16:19** And Saul sent messengers to Jessæ, saying, Send to me thy son David who is with thy flock.  ^16-19

**16:20** And Jessæ took a homer of bread, and a bottle of wine, and one kid of the goats, and sent them by the hand of his son David to Saul.  ^16-20

**16:21** And David went in to Saul, and stood before him; and he loved him greatly; and he became his armour-bearer.  ^16-21

**16:22** And Saul sent to Jessæ, saying, Let David, I pray thee, stand before me, for he has found grace in my eyes.  ^16-22

**16:23** And it came to pass when the evil spirit was upon Saul, that David took his harp, and played with his hand: and Saul was refreshed, and *it was* well with him, and the evil spirit departed from him.  ^16-23

[^1]: 16:4 *Gr.* is 'thy coming in peace.'
[^2]: 16:5 *Gr.* peace.
[^3]: 16:7 *Or,* face.
[^4]: 16:7 *Gr.* will not see.
[^5]: 16:9 *Gr.* in this one.
[^6]: 16:11 *Gr.* Are thy sons come to an end?
[^7]: 16:11 *Or,* the youngest.
[^8]: 16:12 *Gr.* goodly in the sight of the Lord.
[^9]: 16:13 *Gr.* leapt upon.
[^10]: 16:14 *Gr.* choked him.
[^11]: 16:17 *Gr.* man playing skilfully.
[^12]: 16:18 *Gr.* him understanding.
