# Likutei Moharan I 210

← [[Likutei Moharan I 209]] · [[Likutei Moharan]] · [[Likutei Moharan I 211]] →

*Part I · Lesson 210* · [[Likutei Moharan]]

## 1

By doing business honestly, a person fulfills [the mitzvah of] “You shall love [God]” ([[Torah 45 - Vaetchanan#^6-5|Deuteronomy 6:5]]). As our Sages, of blessed memory, said: “You shall love”—Heaven’s name should become beloved through you ([[Yoma 86#86a|Yoma 86a]]).

And when a person fulfills “You shall love,” his livelihood is without struggle or toil. As our Sages, of blessed memory, said: “A person’s livelihood is as difficult as splitting the Red Sea” ([[Pesachim 118#118a|Pesachim 118a]]). Splitting the Red Sea was in the merit of Avraham, \<who is himself the> aspect of “And you shall love,” as our Sages, of blessed memory, said: “the sea returned just before morning” ([[Torah 16 - Beshalach#^14-27|Exodus 14:27]])—this is the morning of Avraham ([[Zohar 5.05 - Parashat Terumah#^z2-170b|Zohar II, 170b]]).

Thus it is that when morning—the aspect of Avraham/\<love>—arrives, the difficult becomes relaxed. For until the morning, splitting the Red Sea was difficult for Him. From this we may conclude that by doing business honestly, a person is linked to the aspect of “And you shall love”/Avraham, and as a result he has no difficulty [earning] his livelihood.
