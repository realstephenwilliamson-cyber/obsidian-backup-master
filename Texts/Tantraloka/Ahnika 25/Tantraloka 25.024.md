## Mūla

उपायं गुरुदीक्षादिद्वारमात्रेण संश्रयेत् ।
उक्तं श्रीमन्मतङ्गाख्ये मुनिप्रश्नादनन्तरम् ॥ ^mula

upāyaṃ gurudīkṣādidvāramātreṇa saṃśrayet |
uktaṃ śrīmanmataṅgākhye munipraśnādanantaram || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 17 - Chapter Twenty-Five (continued) and Chapter Twenty-Six#^pg385|p. 365]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=385>))
>
> Indeed, the one who rescues (all) is the God of gods (Himself), and his power is beyond conception. He resorts to a means, be it the teacher, initiation or the like, solely as (His) instrument. (with 25.23cd–24ab)
