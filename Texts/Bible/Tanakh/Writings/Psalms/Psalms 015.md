## Chapter 15

**15:1** A psalm of David.  
LORD, who may sojourn in Your tent,  
who may dwell on Your holy mountain? ^15-1

**15:2** He who lives without blame,  
who does what is right,  
and in his heart acknowledges the truth; ^15-2

**15:3** whose tongue is not given to evil;[^1]  
who has never done harm to his fellow,  
or borne reproach for [his acts toward] his neighbor; ^15-3

**15:4** for whom a contemptible man is abhorrent,  
but who honors those who fear the LORD;  
who stands by his oath even to his hurt; ^15-4

**15:5** who has never lent money at interest,  
or accepted a bribe against the innocent.  
The man who acts thus shall never be shaken. ^15-5

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain; or “who has no slander upon his tongue.”
