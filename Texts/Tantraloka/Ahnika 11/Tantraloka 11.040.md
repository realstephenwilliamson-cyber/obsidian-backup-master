## Mūla

तत्त्वाध्वैव स देवेन प्रोक्तो व्याससमासतः ।
एकतत्त्वविधिश्चैष सुप्रबुद्धं गुरुं प्रति ॥ ^mula

tattvādhvaiva sa devena prokto vyāsasamāsataḥ |
ekatattvavidhiścaiṣa suprabuddhaṃ guruṃ prati || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 02 - Chapter Eleven, part 1#^pg57|p. 37]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=57>))
>
> Thus, the Lord has taught the Path of the Principles, (both) extensively and in brief, starting with (the division into) thirty-six up to the procedure for (just) one principle. (with 11.39cd–40ab)
>
> Śambhu has explained this procedure for (just) one principle (ekatattvavidhi) to the well awakened (suprabuddha) teacher and the disciple whose desire for worldly enjoyment (bhoga) has ceased. (with 11.40cd–41ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* इति न्यायेन यद्येकतत्त्वात्मना संक्षिप्तेनैव प्रकारेण दीक्षा सिद्ध्येत् तत्किमन्यैर्विस्तृतैः प्रकारैः ? -- इत्याशङ्क्याह --

> एकतत्त्वविधिश्चैष सुप्रबुद्धं गुरुं प्रति ॥
> शिष्यं च गतभोगाशमुदितः शंभुना यतः ।

गतभोगाशमिति मुमुक्षुमेव -- इत्यर्थः ॥ ४० ॥
