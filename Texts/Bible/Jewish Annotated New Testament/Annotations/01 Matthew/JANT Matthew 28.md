### 28.1–10: The empty tomb

*[[NRSVue Matthew 28#^28-1|Text]], p. 66* · ([[Mark 16#^16-1|Mk 16.1–8]]; [[Luke 24#^24-1|Lk 24.1–11]]; [[John 20#^20-1|Jn 20.1–18]]). ^28-1

**[[NRSVue Matthew 28#^28-1|1]]:** *First day of the week*, Sunday, the third day after Jesus’ death. Some Jews believed the soul departed the body after three days (*m*. *Yebam*. 16.3; *y. Moʾed Qat*. 8.5; *b. Sanh.* [[Sanhedrin 90#90b|90b]]–91a; *b*. *Shabb*. 151b; *Gen. Rab*. 100; *Sem*. 8.1). *Went to see the tomb*, women as well as men visited and/or attended tombs. ^28-1-2

**[[NRSVue Matthew 28#^28-2|2]]:** See *Acts Pil*. 13. *Great earthquake*, see 8.24n.; 27.51–53n. ^28-2

**[[NRSVue Matthew 28#^28-3|3]]:** *Clothing white as snow*, see [[Daniel 07#^7-9|Dan 7.9]]; [[Daniel 10#^10-6|10.6]]; see also [[Revelation 01#^1-14|Rev 1.14]]; [[Revelation 02#^2-18|2.18]]; on “light,” see 1QS 3.20; 1QM 13.9–10. ^28-3

**[[NRSVue Matthew 28#^28-4|4]]:** *Became like dead men*, the guards are more frightened than the women. ^28-4

**[[NRSVue Matthew 28#^28-6|6]]:** *He is not here*, many Jews believed in bodily resurrection, which would occur at the dawn of the messianic age ([[Daniel 12#^12-2|Dan 12.2]]; Josephus, *J.W.* 2.158,163; 3.374; *Ant*. 10.277–80; 16.397–98; 18.14; *Pss. Sol*. 3.12–13; 5.12–13; *1 En*. 22.13; *b. Sanh*. 90). ^28-6

**[[NRSVue Matthew 28#^28-7|7]]:** *Tell his disciples*, the women are commissioned as evangelists. *To Galilee*, 4.12; 25.32n. ^28-7

**[[NRSVue Matthew 28#^28-9|9]]:** *Jesus met them*, see [[John 20#^20-14|Jn 20.14–18]]. *Worshiped him*, Gk “proskyneō,” “kneel,” lit., “kiss towards” (in LXX for Heb “shahah,” “bow down” in worship, e.g., [[Psalms 005#^5-8|Ps 5.7]]\[LXX 5.8\]). See v 17. ^28-9

**[[NRSVue Matthew 28#^28-11|28.11–15]]:** **The guards’ report.** ^28-11

**[[NRSVue Matthew 28#^28-13|13]]:** *His disciples came by night and stole him away*, the story remained in circulation (Justin, *Dial*. 102.8). Matthew’s account is unlikely: Roman guards would not claim that *we were asleep*. ^28-13

**[[NRSVue Matthew 28#^28-15|15]]:** *To this day*, a biblical formula here referring to the time of Matthew’s authorship (11.26; 27.8; see also [[Torah 08 - Vayishlach#^35-20|Gen 35.20]]; [[Torah 46 - Eikev#^11-4|Deut 11.4]]). ^28-15

### 28.16–20: The Great Commission

*[[NRSVue Matthew 28#^28-16|Text]], p. 66* · Cf. *Acts Pil*. 14.1. ^28-16

**[[NRSVue Matthew 28#^28-16|16]]:** Like Moses, Jesus’ final instructions are given from a mountain (cf. [[Torah 53 - Ha'Azinu#^32-48|Deut 32.48]]). ^28-16-2

**[[NRSVue Matthew 28#^28-17|17]]:** *Some doubted*, doubt and fidelity to the mission of Jesus are not mutually exclusive. ^28-17

**[[NRSVue Matthew 28#^28-18|18]]:** *All authority in heaven and on earth*, the risen Christ’s domain has become the entire world (see 11.27; [[Daniel 07#^7-14|Dan 7.14]],[[Daniel 07#^7-18|18]],[[Daniel 07#^7-27|27]]; [[Ephesians 01#^1-20|Eph 1.20–23]]; [[Philippians 02#^2-9|Phil 2.9–11]]). ^28-18

**[[NRSVue Matthew 28#^28-19|19]]:** *Make disciples of all nations*, the change in Jesus’ status prompts the expansion of the mission (10.6; 15.24) to include both Jews and Gentiles (8.10; 9.17; 15.28; 18.6; 21.21; 24.13–14; 28.20). *All nations* (Gk “panta ta ethne”) can be read as “all the Gentiles.” The mission to Israel is never abrogated. *Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit*, the Trinity did not become Christian doctrine until at least the second century (*Did*. 7.1–3; Ignatius, *Magn*. 3.2; cf. [[1 Corinthians 08#^8-6|1 Cor 8.6]]; [[Ephesians 04#^4-6|Eph 4.6]]); a similar formula is found only in [[2 Corinthians 13#^13-13|2 Cor 13.13]]. This occurrence may reflect liturgical usage in Matthew’s later community, as other accounts of baptism (e.g., [[Acts 02#^2-38|Acts 2.38]]) do not use this formula. ^28-19

**[[NRSVue Matthew 28#^28-20|20]]:** *I am with you always*, the Gospel’s frame (1.23; 18.20; 28.20). By depicting Jesus as always present and mandating global evangelization, the Great Commission decreases tension over the delay of the second coming. Cf. [[Jeremiah 01#^1-19|Jer 1.19]], “I am with you,” following 1.17, “tell them everything I command you” as the prophetic commission. ^28-20
