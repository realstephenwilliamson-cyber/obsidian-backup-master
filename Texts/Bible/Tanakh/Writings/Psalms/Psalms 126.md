## Chapter 126

**126:1** A song of ascents.  
When the LORD restores the fortunes of Zion  
—we see it as in a dream[^1]— ^126-1

**126:2** our mouths shall be filled with laughter,  
our tongues, with songs of joy.  
Then shall they say among the nations,  
“The LORD has done great things for them!” ^126-2

**126:3** The LORD will do great things for us  
and we shall rejoice. ^126-3

**126:4** Restore our fortunes, O LORD,  
like watercourses in the Negeb. ^126-4

**126:5** They who sow in tears  
shall reap with songs of joy. ^126-5

**126:6** Though he goes along weeping,  
carrying the seed-bag,  
he shall come back with songs of joy,  
carrying his sheaves. ^126-6

[^1]: Lit. “we are veritable dreamers.”
