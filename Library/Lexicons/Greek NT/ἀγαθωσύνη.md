# ἀγαθωσύνη

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · noun

**Dodson:** goodness — intrinsic goodness, especially as a personal quality, with stress on the kindly (rather than the righteous) side of goodness. (Strong's 19; headword ἀγαθωσύνη, ης, ἡ)
