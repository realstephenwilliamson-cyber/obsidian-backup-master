# ἐπίγειος

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · adjective

**Dodson:** earthly — (a) on the earth, belonging to the earth (as opposed to the sky), (b) in a spiritual sense, belonging to the earthly sphere, earthly (as opposed to heavenly). (Strong's 1919; headword ἐπίγειος, ον)
