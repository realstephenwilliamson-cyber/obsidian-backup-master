## Chapter Nine

The Blessed One continued:

\[1\] “But I will tell you, a believer,
a most secret combination
of knowledge and discrimination
that will release you from all evil.

\[2\] This royal knowledge, royal secret,
is the supreme purifier,
righteous and intelligible,
both practical and eternal.

\[3\] Those who have no faith, Arjuna,
in this teaching do not attain Me
and are reborn in the cycle
of *samsara*, death and rebirth.

\[4\] The whole is pervaded by Me
in My unmanifest aspect.
Every being resides in Me;
I do not reside in beings.

\[5\] Still, beings do not reside in Me.
Behold my great and lordly *Yoga*!
My Self causes and sustains beings
but does not reside in beings.

\[6\] As the great wind goes everywhere
dwelling in space eternally,
in just this way all beings dwell
and exist in Me. Reflect on this.

[p. 54] \[7\] All beings, O Son of Kunti,
enter my material nature
when a *kalpa* ends. I send them forth
at the beginning of a *kalpa*.

\[8\] From My own material nature
I send forth again and again
this powerless multitude of beings,
empowered by my material nature.

\[9\] And none of these actions bind me,
O victorious Arjuna.
I sit aside indifferently
unattached to all these actions.

\[10\] I oversee nature’s creation
of the living and the inert.
This is the cause, Son of Kunti,
of the turning world’s existence.

\[11\] Deluded fools look down on Me
when I assume a human form,
ignorant of My higher being,
the great Lord of all beings.

\[12\] Those of vain hopes and vain actions,
of vain knowledge, undiscerning,
live an evil, demonic life
and abide in deep delusion.

\[13\] But those with great souls, Arjuna,
and of a celestial nature,
worship Me, have their minds on Me
as all beings’ eternal origin,

\[14\] Praising me perpetually,
forging ahead with firm resolve,
[p. 55] honoring Me with devotion,
ever steadfast in their worship.

\[15\] Others by knowledge-sacrifice
worship and acknowledge Me
as both the One and the Many,
multi-faced in all directions.

\[16\] I am the rite and sacrifice,
the offering, the healing herb;
I am the sacred text, the ghee,
the fire and the poured oblation.

\[17\] I am the universe’s father,
mother, founder, and grandfather;
the known, the purifier, *Om*,
the *Rig*, *Sama*, *Yajur* *Vedas*;

\[18\] The goal, supporter, lord, witness,
the home, the refuge, and the friend,
the origin, death, and foundation,
treasure house and eternal seed.

\[19\] I am heat and I am the one
who withholds and sends forth the rain.
I am death and deathlessness both,
being and non-being, Arjuna.

\[20\] Knowing the three *Vedas*, *soma* drinkers, cleansed,
   worship me with sacrifices, seeking heaven.
Attaining the pure world of Lord Indra,
   they enjoy the gods’ celestial pleasures.

\[21\] Having enjoyed the spacious world of heaven,
   merit exhausted, they enter the mortal world.
Desiring pleasure, they come and they go,
   conforming to the law of the three *Vedas*.

[p. 56] \[22\] For men who direct their minds to Me,
who worship me with no other thoughts,
constantly united with *Yoga*,
I provide and preserve all they need.

\[23\] Even those who worship other gods
and offer sacrifice in faith,
they also worship Me, Arjuna,
though not in an orthodox way.

\[24\] I am indeed the Enjoyer
and the Lord of all sacrifice.
But they do not know Me truly,
and so they fall and disappear.

\[25\] Adore the gods, go to the gods;
adore ancestors, to them go;
adore spirits, go to the spirits;
adore Me, surely come to Me.

\[26\] A leaf, a flower, fruit, water
offered to Me with devotion
by anyone whose heart is pure
is an offering I accept.

\[27\] Whatever you do, whatever you eat,
whatever sacrifice you make,
all of that, O Son of Kunti,
do as an offering to Me.

\[28\] Free at last from the bonds of action
that produce good and evil fruits,
trained in *yogic* renunciation,
your freed self will surely find Me.

\[29\] I am the same in all beings;
none are hated or prized by Me.
[p. 57] But those who are devoted to Me
are within Me, and I in them.

\[30\] Even evildoers, if they
worship Me undividedly,
must be thought of as worthy souls,
having come indeed to right resolve.

\[31\] Their souls soon become virtuous
and attain peace everlasting.
Be aware of this, O Arjuna!
No devotee of Mine is lost.

\[32\] All those who take refuge in Me,
even if born from evil wombs—
women, merchants, even outcastes—
also attain the highest goal.

\[33\] How much more so the pure *brahmins*
and the devoted royal seers!
Once in this transient, sorrowful world,
devote yourself to My worship.

\[34\] Mind on Me, devoted to Me,
worshipping Me reverently,
holding Me as your highest goal,
your very soul will come to Me.”
