## Chapter 54

**54:1** For the leader; with instrumental music. A *maskil* of David, ^54-1

**54:2** when the Ziphites came and told Saul, “Know, David is in hiding among us.”[^1] ^54-2

**54:3** O God, deliver me by Your name;  
by Your power vindicate me. ^54-3

**54:4** O God, hear my prayer;  
give ear to the words of my mouth. ^54-4

**54:5** For strangers have risen against me,  
and ruthless men seek my life;  
they are unmindful of God. *Selah*. ^54-5

**54:6** See, God is my helper;  
the Lord is my support. ^54-6

**54:7** He will repay the evil of my watchful foes;  
by Your faithfulness, destroy them! ^54-7

**54:8** Then I will offer You a freewill sacrifice;  
I will praise Your name, LORD, for it is good, ^54-8

**54:9** for it has saved me from my foes,  
and let me gaze triumphant upon my enemies. ^54-9

[^1]: Cf. 1 Sam. 23.19.
