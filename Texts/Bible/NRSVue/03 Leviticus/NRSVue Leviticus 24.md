# Leviticus 24 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Leviticus 23|← Leviticus 23]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Leviticus 25|Leviticus 25 →]]

## The lamp

**24:1** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, ^24-1

**24:2** “Command the Israelites to bring you pure oil of beaten olives for the lamp, that a light may be kept burning regularly. ^24-2

**24:3** Aaron shall set it up in the tent of meeting, outside the curtain of the covenant, to burn from evening to morning before the LORD regularly; it shall be a statute forever throughout your generations. ^24-3

**24:4** He shall set up the lamps on the lampstand of pure gold^[Heb *pure lampstand*] before the LORD regularly. ^24-4

## The bread for the tabernacle

**24:5** “You shall take choice flour and bake twelve loaves of it; two-tenths of an ephah shall be in each loaf. ^24-5

**24:6** You shall place them in two rows, six in a row, on the table of pure gold before the LORD.^[Heb *pure table*] ^24-6

**24:7** You shall put pure frankincense with each row, to be a token offering for the bread, as an offering by fire^[Or *a gift*] to the LORD. ^24-7

**24:8** Every Sabbath day Aaron shall set them in order before the LORD regularly as a commitment of the Israelites, as a covenant forever. ^24-8

**24:9** They shall be for Aaron and his descendants, who shall eat them in a holy place, for they are most holy portions for him from the offerings by fire^[Or *the gifts*] to the LORD, a perpetual due.” ^24-9

## Blasphemy and its punishment

**24:10** A man whose mother was an Israelite and whose father was an Egyptian came out among the Israelites, and the Israelite woman’s son and a certain Israelite began fighting in the camp. ^24-10

**24:11** The Israelite woman’s son blasphemed the Name in a curse. And they brought him to Moses—now his mother’s name was Shelomith daughter of Dibri, of the tribe of Dan— ^24-11

**24:12** and they put him in custody, until the decision of the LORD should be made clear to them. ^24-12

**24:13** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, ^24-13

**24:14** “Take the blasphemer outside the camp, and let all who were within hearing lay their hands on his head, and let the whole congregation stone him. ^24-14

**24:15** And speak to the Israelites, saying: Anyone who curses God shall incur guilt. ^24-15

**24:16** One who blasphemes the name of the LORD shall be put to death; the whole congregation shall stone the blasphemer. Aliens as well as the native-born, when they blaspheme the Name, shall be put to death. ^24-16

**24:17** Anyone who kills a human being shall be put to death. ^24-17

**24:18** Anyone who kills an animal shall make restitution for it, life for life. ^24-18

**24:19** Anyone who maims another shall suffer the same injury in return: ^24-19

**24:20** fracture for fracture, eye for eye, tooth for tooth; the injury inflicted is the injury to be suffered. ^24-20

**24:21** One who kills an animal shall make restitution for it, but one who kills a human being shall be put to death. ^24-21

**24:22** You shall have one law for the alien and for the native-born, for I am the LORD your God.” ^24-22

**24:23** Moses spoke thus to the Israelites, and they took the blasphemer outside the camp and stoned him to death. The Israelites did as the LORD had commanded Moses. ^24-23
