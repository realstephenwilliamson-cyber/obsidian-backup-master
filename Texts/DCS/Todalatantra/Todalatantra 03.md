# Toḍalatantra — Patala 3

[[Todalatantra 02|← Patala 2]] · [[Todalatantra|← index]] · [[Todalatantra 04|Patala 4 →]]

śrīdevī uvāca

^s1

devadeva mahādeva saṃsārārṇavatāraka

^s2

baddhayoniṃ mahāmudrāṃ kathayasva dayānidhe

^s3

śrīśiva uvāca

^s4

śṛṇu devi pravakṣyāmi baddhayoniṃ samāsataḥ

^s5

upaviśyāsane mantrī prāṅmukho vāpy udaṅmukhaḥ

^s6

gudacchidre maheśāni svaliṅgāgraṃ niveśayet

^s7

śrotre caiva tathā netre nāsāyāṃ ca tato mukhe

^s8

aṅguṣṭhādi maheśāni krameṇa yojayet sudhīḥ

^s9

nāsikāyāṃ mukhe caiva samīraṃ bahuyatnataḥ

^s10

pūrayet parameśāni kiṃcid api na recayet

^s11

śabdapratyakṣatāṃ kṛtvā varṇamālāṃ vicintayet

^s12

aṣṭottaraśataṃ mūlamantraṃ tu prajapet sudhīḥ

^s13

so 'haṃmantreṇa deveśi ānayettena vartmanā

^s14

ṣaṭcakradevatāstatra saṃtarpyāmṛtadhārayā

^s15

kṛte phalaṃ maheśāni kathayiṣyāmi te 'naghe

^s16

śrīpārvatī uvāca

^s17

vada īśāna sarvajña sarvatattvavidāṃ vara

^s18

mantramārgeṇa deveśa kālikāyāḥ sudurlabham

^s19

śrīśiva uvāca

^s20

śṛṇu devi sadānande kālikāmantramuttamam

^s21

yasyāḥ prasaṅgamātreṇa jīvanmukto bhavennaraḥ

^s22

śivādyaṃ bindunādāḍhyaṃ vāmanetrāgnisaṃyutam

^s23

siddhavidyā tv iyaṃ bhadre vidyārājñī sudurlabhā

^s24

idaṃ bījatrayaṃ cādyaṃ gaganaṃ bindubhūṣitam

^s25

vāmaśravaṇasaṃyuktaṃ dvaṃdvaṃ cāparakaṃ śṛṇu

^s26

īśānaṃ vahnisaṃyuktaṃ vāmanetrendusaṃyutam

^s27

dvitīyaṃ parameśāni sambodhanapadaṃ tataḥ

^s28

punar bījatrayaṃ kūrcaṃ māyādvaṃdvaṃ ca ṭhadvayam

^s29

dvāviṃśatyakṣaraṃ mantraṃ vidyārājñī sudurlabhā

^s30

vāgbhavādyā mahāvidyā śrīkālī devatā smṛtā

^s31

praṇavādyā mahāvidyā devatā siddhikālikā

^s32

nijabījadvayaṃ kūrcaṃ bījaikaṃ parameśvari

^s33

tryakṣarī paramā vidyā cāmuṇḍā kālikā smṛtā

^s34

etasyāḥ sadṛśī vidyā siddhidā nāsti sundari

^s35

yathā ṣaḍakṣarī vidyā tathā vidyā ca tryakṣarī

^s36

nijabījatrayaṃ bhadre śmaśānakālikā tataḥ

^s37

punar bījatrayaṃ bhadre vahnikāntāṃ samuccaret

^s38

caturdaśākṣarī vidyā triṣu lokeṣu pūjitā

^s39

dakṣiṇākālikā siddhakālikā guhyakālikā

^s40

śrīkālikā bhadrakālī cāmuṇḍākālikā parā

^s41

śmaśānakālikā devi mahākālīti cāṣṭadhā

^s42

nijabījaṃ maheśāni sambodhanapadaṃ tataḥ

^s43

punaśca kālikābījaṃ tato vahnivadhūṃ nyaset

^s44

iti cāṣṭavidhaṃ mantraṃ sarvatantreṣu gopitam

^s45

śrīdevī uvāca

^s46

śrutaṃ mahākālikāyā mantraṃ paramagopanam

^s47

tārāyā mantrarājaṃ tu śrotumicchāmi sāmpratam

^s48

yasyāḥ prasaṅgamātreṇa bhavābdhau na nimajjati

^s49

tanmantraṃ vada īśāna yadi sneho'sti māṃ prati

^s50

śrīśiva uvāca

^s51

candrabījaṃ samuccārya ādyaṃ vahnisamāgatam

^s52

vāmanetrendusaṃyuktaṃ mantrarājamimaṃ priye

^s53

ekākṣarī mahāvidyā triṣu lokeṣu pūjitā

^s54

īśānabindusaṃyuktaṃ vāmakarṇavibhūṣitam

^s55

ekākṣarī mahāvidyā mantrarājadvitīyakam

^s56

śiraṃ bindusamārūḍhaṃ vāmanetrendusaṃyutam

^s57

ādyabījaṃ dvitīyaṃ ca astramantraṃ samuccaret

^s58

praṇavādyā yadā vidyā sogratārā prakīrtitā

^s59

vidyā caikajaṭā proktā mahāmokṣapradāyinī

^s60

tārāstrarahitā tryarṇā mahānīlasarasvatī

^s61

vāgbhavādyā yadā vidyā vāgīśatvapradāyinī

^s62

śrībījādyā mahāvidyā sadā lakṣmīpradāyinī

^s63

māyādyā paramā vidyā sadā siddhipradāyinī

^s64

kūrcabījādikā caiṣā śabdarāśiprakāśinī

^s65

gaganādyā mahāvidyā nirvāṇamokṣadāyinī

^s66

prāsādādyā mahāvidyā śivasāyujyadāyinī

^s67

prāṇabījādikā caiṣā vāñchāsiddhipradāyinī

^s68

kālikādyā mahāvidyā muktidā siddhidā sadā

^s69

kālikāyāśca tārāyā ārādhanam ihocyate

^s70

prātarutthāya mantrajñaḥ sahasrāre guruṃ yajet

^s71

ṣaṭcakraṃ bhedayitvā tu cāṣṭottaraśataṃ japet

^s72

tataḥ praṇamya vidhivat snānakarma samārabhet

^s73

adyetyādi samuccārya sauramāsaṃ samuccaret

^s74

devatāprītaye paścāt snāpayecchuddhavāriṇā

^s75

oṃkāraṃ ca samuccārya gaṅge ceti samuccaret

^s76

yamuneti tataḥ paścād godāvari sarasvati

^s77

narmadeti samuccārya sindhukāverisaṃyutam

^s78

asmin jale ca saṃnidhiṃ kuru śabdamatho vadet

^s79

ānayedaṅkuśākhyena ravisaṃyuktamaṇḍalāt

^s80

mudrācatuṣṭayaṃ devi darśayed bahuyatnataḥ

^s81

rudrasaṃkhyaṃ japenmantramācchādya mīnamudrayā

^s82

sūryāyābhimukhaṃ toyaṃ niḥkṣipya ravisaṃkhyayā

^s83

mūlamantraṃ samuccārya kṣālayeccaraṇadvayam

^s84

caraṇānniḥsṛte toye trir nimajya japenmanum

^s85

mūlamantraṃ trir japtvā tu mudrayā kumbhasaṃjñayā

^s86

jalādutthāya deveśi tilakaṃ kulavartmataḥ

^s87

ātmavidyāśivais tattvair ācāmet payasā tataḥ

^s88

vahnijāyānvitā mantrā vijñeyāḥ praṇavādikāḥ

^s89

gaṅge cetyādinā devi tīrthamāvāhanaṃ caret

^s90

mūlena darbhayā bhūmau trivāraṃ nikṣipet sudhīḥ

^s91

tajjalena saptavāram ātmābhiṣekamācaret

^s92

aṅganyāsaṃ tataḥ kṛtvā vāmahaste sureśvari

^s93

gaganaṃ vāyubījaṃ ca varuṇaṃ bhūmibījakam

^s94

vahnibījaṃ binduyuktaṃ trivāraṃ japamācaret

^s95

anena manunā devi tajjalaṃ cābhimantritam

^s96

mūlamantraṃ samuccārya saptadhā tattvamudrayā

^s97

abhiṣecanamātreṇa mucyate sarvapātakaiḥ

^s98

śeṣaṃ jalaṃ maheśāni dakṣahaste samānayet

^s99

iḍayā pūrayettoyaṃ kṣālayed dehamadhyagam

^s100

tataḥ piṅgalayā devi tattoyaṃ tu virecayet

^s101

kṛṣṇavarṇaṃ tadudakaṃ pāparūpaṃ vicintayet

^s102

purato vajrapāṣāṇe phaṭkāreṇa vinikṣipet

^s103

hastaṃ prakṣālya cācamya prāṇāyāmapuraḥsaram

^s104

tarpayet kuladevaṃ ca sūryāyārghyaṃ nivedayet

^s105

devatārghyaṃ tataḥ paścād gāyatrīṃ paramākṣarīm

^s106

praṇavaṃ ca samuddhṛtya kālikāyai tato vadet

^s107

vidmahe iti saṃlikhya śmaśānaṃ ca samuddharet

^s108

vāsinīṃ ṅeyutāṃ devi dhīmahīti tato vadet

^s109

tanno ghore maheśāni prajapettu pracodayāt

^s110

gāyatrīṃ prapaṭhed dhīmān trivāraṃ jalamutkṣipet

^s111

tato japenmahāmantraṃ gāyatrīṃ paramākṣarīm

^s112

aṅganyāsaṃ tataḥ kṛtvā ācamya parameśvari

^s113

iṣṭadevīṃ maheśāni dhyātvā tu parimaṇḍale

^s114

oṃ tārāyai vidmahe iti mahogrāyai tato vadet

^s115

dhīmahīti tataḥ paścāttato devi pracodayāt

^s116

prāṇāyāmaṃ tataḥ kṛtvā cāṣṭottaraśataṃ japet

^s117

sūtrākāreṇa deveśi pūjāvidhirihocyate

^s118

svastivācanasaṃkalpaṃ ghaṭaṃ saṃsthāpya yatnataḥ

^s119

mantreṇācamanaṃ kāryaṃ sāmānyārghyaṃ tato nyaset

^s120

tajjalair dvāram abhyukṣya dvārapūjāṃ samācaret

^s121

trividhaṃ vighnam utsārya bhūtāpasaraṇaṃ tataḥ

^s122

āsanaṃ ca samabhyarcya gurudevaṃ namet sudhīḥ

^s123

karaśuddhiṃ ca tālaṃ ca trayaṃ digbandhanaṃ tataḥ

^s124

vahninā veṣṭanaṃ kāryaṃ bhūtaśuddhim athācaret

^s125

mātṛkāyāḥ ṣaḍaṅgaṃ ca kuryād āntaramātṛkām

^s126

mātṛkādhyānam uccārya bāhye tu mātṛkāṃ nyaset

^s127

pīṭhanyāsaṃ tataḥ kṛtvā prāṇāyāmaṃ samācaret

^s128

ṛṣyādikaṃ karāṅgaṃ ca varṇanyāsaṃ samācaret

^s129

ṣoḍhānyāsaṃ tato devi vyāpakaṃ tadanantaram

^s130

evaṃ samāhitamanās tattvanyāsaṃ samācaret

^s131

bījanyāsaṃ tato devi vyāpakaṃ vinyaset sudhīḥ

^s132

mūlena saptadhā dhyānaṃ mānasaiḥ pūjanaṃ caret

^s133

viśeṣārghyaṃ pīṭhapūjāṃ punardhyānaṃ sanetrakam

^s134

mudrādidarśanaṃ kāryam āvāhanaṣaḍaṅgakam

^s135

dhenvādikaṃ tataḥ prāṇapratiṣṭhāṃ mūlapūjanam

^s136

ājñāprārthanamaṅgāni kālyādīn paripūjayet

^s137

brāhmyādīnasitāṅgādīn mahākālaṃ prapūjayet

^s138

khaḍgādīn gurupaṅktiṃ ca punardevīṃ prapūjayet

^s139

balidānaṃ tato homaṃ prāṇāyāmaṃ tato japam

^s140

japaṃ samarpayeddhīmān prāṇāyāmaṃ tataścaret

^s141

etasmin samaye devi kāraṇādīn samāharet

^s142

arghyaṃ dattvā maheśāni cātmānaṃ ca samarpayet

^s143

stutiṃ ca kavacaṃ smṛtvā cāṣṭāṅgaṃ praṇamet sudhīḥ

^s144

śivo'ham iti saṃcintya saṃhāreṇa visarjayet

^s145

aiśānyāṃ maṇḍalaṃ kṛtvā cāṇḍālyucchiṣṭapūrvikām

^s146

arghyaṃ saṃdhārya śirasi candanaṃ tu lalāṭake

^s147

naivedyaṃ kiṃcit svīkṛtya viharecca nijecchayā

^s148

saṃkṣepapūjāmathavā kuryānmantrī samāhitaḥ

^s149

ādau ṛṣyādikaṃ nyāsaṃ karaśuddhistataḥ param

^s150

aṅgulīvyāpakanyāsau hṛdādinyāsa eva ca

^s151

tālatrayaṃ ca digbandhaḥ prāṇāyāmastataḥ param

^s152

dhyānaṃ mānasayāgaṃ ca arghyasthāpanameva ca

^s153

pīṭhapūjāṃ punardhyānaṃ tataścāvāhanaṃ caret

^s154

jīvanyāsaṃ tataḥ kṛtvā pūjayet paradevatām

^s155

aṅgapūjāṃ ca kālyādīn brāhmyādīṃścāṣṭabhairavān

^s156

^s157
