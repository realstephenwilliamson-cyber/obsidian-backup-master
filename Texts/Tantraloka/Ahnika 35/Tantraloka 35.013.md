## Mūla

तेनासर्वज्ञपूर्वत्वमात्रेणैषा न सिद्ध्यति ।
बहुसर्वज्ञपूर्वत्वे न मानं चास्ति किञ्चन ॥ ^mula

tenāsarvajñapūrvatvamātreṇaiṣā na siddhyati |
bahusarvajñapūrvatve na mānaṃ cāsti kiñcana || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 09 - Chapter Thirty-Five#^pg215|p. 197]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=215>))
>
> The totality of the activity of daily life (vyavahāra), whatever it be, cannot be present all together within (a single limited) perceiver. Thus, (its existence) is not justified by just being based on (a perceiver that) does not know everything. (with 35.12cd–13ab)
>
> (Thus,) there is no proof at all (to support the view that) it is based (lit ‘preceded by’) many omniscient (perceivers). (with 35.13cd)
