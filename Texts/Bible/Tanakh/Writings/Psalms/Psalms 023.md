## Chapter 23

**23:1** A psalm of David.  
The LORD is my shepherd;  
I lack nothing. ^23-1

**23:2** He makes me lie down in green pastures;  
He leads me to water in places of repose;[^1] ^23-2

**23:3** He renews my life;  
He guides me in right paths  
as befits His name. ^23-3

**23:4** Though I walk through a valley of deepest darkness,[^2]  
I fear no harm, for You are with me;  
Your rod and Your staff—they comfort me. ^23-4

**23:5** You spread a table for me in full view of my enemies;  
You anoint my head with oil;  
my drink is abundant. ^23-5

**23:6** Only goodness and steadfast love shall pursue me  
all the days of my life,  
and I shall dwell in the house of the LORD  
for many long years. ^23-6

[^1]: Others “still waters.”
[^2]: Others “the valley of the shadow of death.”
