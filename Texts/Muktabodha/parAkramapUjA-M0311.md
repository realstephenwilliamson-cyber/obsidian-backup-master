# parākramapūjā

**Catalog number:** M0311  
**Manuscript:** Last part of NGMCP manuscript no.: 5-4656 Reel No. : A 175/11  
**Published:** Muktabodha Indological Research Institute, India

## Description

Description by Mark S.G. Dyczkowski:
The text , which is unnamed, is copied is a part of a manuscript  that
begins with Aśeṣakulavallarī. I have called it Parākramapujā . It tells us that
it belongs to the tradition called Aśeṣāmbhavīmata. Parā, the goddess
worshipped here, is also called Nityā. The worship takes places both internally
and externally. The inner processes are described in detail. As usual the concern
is with the flows of vital force and Kuṇḍalinī.  The offering of wine and other
details confirm the Kaula character of the outer worship. The text supplies an
extensive  and numerous Mantras from which the close association with the
śrīvidyā school is clear, although śrīcakra is not the object of worship.
