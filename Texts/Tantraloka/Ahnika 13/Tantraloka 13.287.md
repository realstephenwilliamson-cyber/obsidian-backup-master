## Mūla

आत्मना तेन हि शिवः स्वयं पूर्णः प्रकाशते ।
शिवीभावमहासिद्धिस्पर्शवन्ध्ये तु कुत्रचित् ॥ ^mula

ātmanā tena hi śivaḥ svayaṃ pūrṇaḥ prakāśate |
śivībhāvamahāsiddhisparśavandhye tu kutracit || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 09 - Chapter Thirteen, part 5#^pg323|p. 301]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=323>))
>
> The word ‘alone’ (eva) excludes dependence (of grace) on Karma and the like. Grace (prasāda) is a state of purity (nirmalībhāva), and so is complete (and perfect) (saṁpūrṇa). By virtue of that (grace), Śiva Himself shines in all His fullness (also) as the (individual) Self (ātman). (with 13.286–287ab)
>
> The devotion of the followers of Viṣnu and the like, who are devoid of (any) contact at all with the great accomplishment (mahāsiddhi), which is identity with Śiva (śivībhāva), does not come from Śiva, who is aloof (from all Karma and the like, to which those gods are subject). (with 13.287cd–288ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* एतदेव व्यतिरेकद्वारेण निदर्शयति --

> शिवीभावमहासिद्धिस्पर्शवन्ध्ये तु कुत्रचित् ॥
> वैष्णवादौ हि या भक्तिर्नासौ केवलतः शिवात् ।

या पुनः कस्मिंश्चिदेवमुक्तयुक्त्या शिवीभावापत्तिविरहिणि वैष्णवादौ भक्तिः, नासौ केवलतः शिवात् कर्माद्यनपेक्षिणः परमेश्वरान्न भवेत् -- इत्यर्थः ॥ २८७ ॥
