## Chapter 101

**101:1** Of David. A psalm.  
I will sing of faithfulness and justice;  
I will chant a hymn to You, O LORD. ^101-1

**101:2** I will study the way of the blameless;  
when shall I attain it?  
I will live without blame within my house. ^101-2

**101:3** I will not set before my eyes anything base;  
I hate crooked dealing;  
I will have none of it. ^101-3

**101:4** Perverse thoughts will be far from me;  
I will know nothing of evil. ^101-4

**101:5** He who slanders his friend in secret I will destroy;  
I cannot endure the haughty and proud man. ^101-5

**101:6** My eyes are on the trusty men of the land,  
to have them at my side.  
He who follows the way of the blameless  
shall be in my service. ^101-6

**101:7** He who deals deceitfully  
shall not live in my house;  
he who speaks untruth  
shall not stand before my eyes. ^101-7

**101:8** Each morning I will destroy  
all the wicked of the land,  
to rid the city of the LORD  
of all evildoers. ^101-8
