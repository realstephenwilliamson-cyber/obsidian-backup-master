## Chapter 20

**20:1** For the leader. A psalm of David. ^20-1

**20:2** May the LORD answer you in time of trouble,  
the name of Jacob’s God keep you safe. ^20-2

**20:3** May He send you help from the sanctuary,  
and sustain you from Zion. ^20-3

**20:4** May He receive the tokens[^1] of all your meal offerings,  
and approve[^2] your burnt offerings. *Selah*. ^20-4

**20:5** May He grant you your desire,  
and fulfill your every plan. ^20-5

**20:6** May we shout for joy in your victory,  
arrayed by standards in the name of our God.  
May the LORD fulfill your every wish. ^20-6

**20:7** Now I know that the LORD will give victory to His anointed,  
will answer him from His heavenly sanctuary  
with the mighty victories of His right arm. ^20-7

**20:8** They [call] on chariots, they [call] on horses,  
but we call on the name of the LORD our God. ^20-8

**20:9** They collapse and lie fallen,  
but we rally and gather strength. ^20-9

**20:10** O LORD, grant victory!  
May the King answer us when we call.[^3] ^20-10

[^1]: Reference to azkara, “token portion” of meal offering; Lev. 2.2, 9, 16, etc.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Or in the light of v. 7, “O Lord, grant victory to the king; may He answer us when we call.”
