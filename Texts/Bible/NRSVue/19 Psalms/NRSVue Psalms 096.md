# Psalm 96 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 095|← Psalm 95]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 097|Psalm 97 →]]

## Praise to God who comes in judgment

**96:1** O sing to the LORD a new song; sing to the LORD, all the earth. ^96-1

**96:2** Sing to the LORD; bless his name; tell of his salvation from day to day. ^96-2

**96:3** Declare his glory among the nations, his marvelous works among all the peoples. ^96-3

**96:4** For great is the LORD and greatly to be praised; he is to be revered above all gods. ^96-4

**96:5** For all the gods of the peoples are idols, but the LORD made the heavens. ^96-5

**96:6** Honor and majesty are before him; strength and beauty are in his sanctuary. ^96-6

**96:7** Ascribe to the LORD, O families of the peoples, ascribe to the LORD glory and strength. ^96-7

**96:8** Ascribe to the LORD the glory due his name; bring an offering, and come into his courts. ^96-8

**96:9** Worship the LORD in holy splendor; tremble before him, all the earth. ^96-9

**96:10** Say among the nations, “The LORD is king! The world is firmly established; it shall never be moved. He will judge the peoples with equity.” ^96-10

**96:11** Let the heavens be glad, and let the earth rejoice; let the sea roar and all that fills it; ^96-11

**96:12** let the field exult and everything in it. Then shall all the trees of the forest sing for joy ^96-12

**96:13** before the LORD, for he is coming, for he is coming to judge the earth. He will judge the world with righteousness and the peoples with his truth. ^96-13
