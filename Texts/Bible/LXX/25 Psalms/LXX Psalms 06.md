# LXX Psalms 06

← [[LXX Psalms 05]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 07]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 006]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 006]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**6:1** For the end, a Psalm of David among the Hymns for the eighth.  ^6-1

**6:2** O Lord, rebuke me not in thy wrath, neither chasten me in thine anger.  ^6-2

**6:3** Pity me, O Lord; for I am weak: heal me, O Lord; for my bones are vexed.  ^6-3

**6:4** My soul also is grievously vexed: but thou, O Lord, how long?  ^6-4

**6:5** Return, O Lord, deliver my soul: save me for thy mercy's sake.  ^6-5

**6:6** For in death no man remembers thee: and who will give thee thanks in Hades?  ^6-6

**6:7** I am wearied with my groaning; I shall wash my bed every night; I shall water my couch with my tears.[^1]  ^6-7

**6:8** Mine eye is troubled because of my wrath; I am worn out because of all my enemies.  ^6-8

**6:9** Depart from me, all ye that work iniquity; for the Lord has heard the voice of my weeping.  ^6-9

**6:10** The Lord has hearkened to my petition; the Lord has accepted my prayer.  ^6-10

**6:11** Let all mine enemies be put to shame and sore troubled: let them be turned back and grievously put to shame speedily.  ^6-11

[^1]: 6:7 *Gr.* I have laboured.
