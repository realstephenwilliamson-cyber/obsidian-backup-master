## Mūla

तद्वन्मायापि विज्ञेया नवधा ज्ञानकेवलाः ।
मन्त्राः सप्तविधास्तद्वत्पञ्चधा मन्त्रनायकाः ॥ ^mula

tadvan māyāpi vijñeyā navadhā jñānakevalāḥ |
mantrāḥ saptavidhās tadvat pañcadhā[^20] mantranāyakāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, July 2022 – September 2022
> p. 29 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Malini (journal), July-September 2022.pdf#page=31>))
>
> One should know that Māyā (that is, the level of the apavedya Dissolution Deconditioned subjects) is also like that. The Consciousness Deconditioned (subjects) (jnānkevala) are ninefold, the Mantra (subjects) are analogously sevenfold, the Lords of Mantra (Mantreśa) are fivefold,[^21] the Lords of the Lords of Mantras (i.e. the Great Lords of Mantra) are threefold. Śiva, directly apparent, is not divided.[^22] (with 2.6–7ab)

[^20]: Printed edition: *pañca mantravināyakā iti* /
[^21]: MV 2/5d-6 is quoted in TĀv ad 10/124cd-127ab.
[^22]: MV 2/7b is quoted in TĀ 10/121cd and in TĀv ad 1/196 and 10/188cd-190ab.
