## Mūla

अभिमानदार्ढ्यबन्धक्रमेण विज्ञानसंज्ञको वेधः ।
हृदयव्योमनि सद्यो दिव्यज्ञानार्कसमुदयं धत्ते ॥ ^mula

abhimānadārḍhyabandhakrameṇa vijñānasaṃjñako vedhaḥ |
hṛdayavyomani sadyo divyajñānārkasamudayaṃ dhatte || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 13 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 5#^pg415|p. 398]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=415>))
>
> 5) (Cognitive) knowledge is eightfold, as smell and the rest (of the five senses), the intellect (buddhi), mind (saṁjña), and (the ego, which) is the sense within. (The teacher) should transmit (this from himself) into the disciple by means of the thread of his own (eight) corresponding channels (nādī), by the process of binding (the disciple to himself), by the firmness of the presumption (that he and his disciple are one). (Thus this,) the Piercing called Knowledge, brings about the immediate rise of the Sun of divine knowledge within the sky of the Heart. (with 29.263–264)
