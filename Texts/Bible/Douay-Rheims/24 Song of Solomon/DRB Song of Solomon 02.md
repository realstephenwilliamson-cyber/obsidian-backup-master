# Song of Solomon 2 (Douay-Rheims)

*Canticle of Canticles 2 in the Douay-Rheims*

[[DRB Song of Solomon 01|← Song of Solomon 1]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Song of Solomon 03|Song of Solomon 3 →]]

**2:1** I am the flower of the field, and the lily of the valleys. ^2-1

**2:2** As the lily among thorns, so is my love among the daughters. ^2-2

**2:3** As the apple tree among the trees of the woods, so is my beloved among the sons. I sat down under his shadow, whom I desired: and his fruit was sweet to my palate. ^2-3

**2:4** He brought me into the cellar of wine, he set in order charity in me. ^2-4

**2:5** Stay me up with flowers, compass me about with apples: because I languish with love. ^2-5

**2:6** His left hand is under my head, and his right hand shall embrace me. ^2-6

**2:7** I adjure you, O ye daughters of Jerusalem, by the roes, and the harts of the, fields, that you stir not up, nor make the beloved to awake, till she please. ^2-7

**2:8** The voice of my beloved, behold he cometh leaping upon the mountains, skipping over the hills. ^2-8

**2:9** My beloved is like a roe, or a young hart. Behold he standeth behind our wall, looking through the windows, looking through the lattices. ^2-9

**2:10** Behold my beloved speaketh to me: Arise, make haste, my love, my dove, my beautiful one, and come. ^2-10

**2:11** For winter is now past, the rain is over and gone. ^2-11

**2:12** The flowers have appeared in our land, the time of pruning is come: the voice of the turtle is heard in our land: ^2-12

**2:13** The fig tree hath put forth her green figs: the vines in flower yield their sweet smell. Arise, my love, my beautiful one, and come: ^2-13

**2:14** My dove in the clefts of the rock, in the hollow places of the wall, shew me thy face, let thy voice sound in my ears: for thy voice is sweet, and thy face comely. ^2-14

**2:15** Catch us the little foxes that destroy the vines: for our vineyard hath flourished. ^2-15

**2:16** My beloved to me, and I to him who feedeth among the lilies, ^2-16

**2:17** Till the day break, and the shadows retire. Return: be like, my beloved, to a roe, or to a young hart upon the mountains of Bether. ^2-17
