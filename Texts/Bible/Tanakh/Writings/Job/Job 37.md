## Chapter 37

**37:1** Because of this, too, my heart quakes,  
And leaps from its place. ^37-1

**37:2** Just listen to the noise of His rumbling,  
To the sound that comes out of His mouth. ^37-2

**37:3** He lets it loose beneath the entire heavens—  
His lightning, to the ends of the earth. ^37-3

**37:4** After it, He lets out a roar;  
He thunders in His majestic voice.  
No one can find a trace of it by the time His voice is heard. ^37-4

**37:5** God thunders marvelously with His voice;  
He works wonders that we cannot understand. ^37-5

**37:6** He commands the snow, “Fall to the ground!”  
And the downpour of rain, His mighty downpour of rain, ^37-6

**37:7** Is as a sign on every man’s hand,  
That all men may know His doings. ^37-7

**37:8** Then the beast enters its lair,  
And remains in its den. ^37-8

**37:9** The storm wind comes from its chamber,  
And the cold from the constellations. ^37-9

**37:10** By the breath of God ice is formed,  
And the expanse of water becomes solid. ^37-10

**37:11** He also loads the clouds with moisture  
And scatters His lightning-clouds. ^37-11

**37:12** He keeps turning events by His stratagems,[^1]  
That they might accomplish all that He commands them  
Throughout the inhabited earth, ^37-12

**37:13** Causing each of them to happen to His land,  
Whether as a scourge or as a blessing. ^37-13

**37:14** Give ear to this, Job;  
Stop to consider the marvels of God. ^37-14

**37:15** Do you know what charge God lays upon them  
When His lightning-clouds shine? ^37-15

**37:16** Do you know the marvels worked upon the expanse of clouds  
By Him whose understanding is perfect, ^37-16

**37:17** Why your clothes become hot[^2]  
When the land is becalmed by the south wind? ^37-17

**37:18** Can you help him stretch out the heavens,  
Firm as a mirror of cast metal? ^37-18

**37:19** Inform us, then, what we may say to Him;  
We cannot argue because [we are in] darkness. ^37-19

**37:20** Is anything conveyed to Him when I speak?  
Can a man say anything when he is confused? ^37-20

**37:21** Now, then, one cannot see the sun,  
Though it be bright in the heavens,  
Until the wind comes and clears them [of clouds]. ^37-21

**37:22** By the north wind the golden rays emerge;  
The splendor about God is awesome. ^37-22

**37:23** Shaddai—we cannot attain to Him;  
He is great in power and justice  
And abundant in righteousness; He does not torment. ^37-23

**37:24** Therefore, men are in awe of Him  
Whom none of the wise can perceive. ^37-24

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
