## Mūla

अन्योन्यतन्मयीभूतान् पूजयेच्छिवतादृशे ।
तत्र प्राणाश्रये न्यासे बुद्ध्या विरचिते सति ॥ ^mula

anyonyatanmayībhūtān pūjayecchivatādṛśe |
tatra prāṇāśraye nyāse buddhyā viracite sati || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 06 - Chapter Fifteen, part 5#^pg192|p. 174]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=192>))
>
> After worshipping the body in this way, one should worship the form (vigraha) of the vital breath, intellect and the void, (thinking) that they have become one with one another, to (ultimately) see them as one with Śiva. (with 15.295cd–296ab)
>
> There all (three) are worshipped with a single effort (ekayatna) when the deposition, grounded in the vital breath, is performed (in conjunction) with the intellect, (reflecting on how it is energized by it,) and (how that is in its turn) sustained by the Void. (with 15.296cd–297ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* कथं च एषां प्राणादीनां त्रयाणामपि विग्रहाणां पूजा कार्या?--इत्याशङ्क्य आह--

> तत्र प्राणाश्रये न्यासे बुद्ध्या विरचिते सति ॥
> शून्याधिष्ठानतः सर्वमेकयत्नेन पूज्यते ।

इह संकुचितो हि आत्मा शून्यप्रमातृतावलम्बनद्वारेण बुद्धिमधिष्ठाय प्राणे षोढा न्यासं विदध्यादिति त्रयाणामपि अपृथक्प्रयत्नं पूजा सिध्येत्--इति वाक्यार्थः ॥
