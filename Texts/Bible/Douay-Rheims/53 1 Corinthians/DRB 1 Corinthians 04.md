# 1 Corinthians 4 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB 1 Corinthians 03|← 1 Corinthians 3]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB 1 Corinthians 05|1 Corinthians 5 →]]

Compare: [[1 Corinthians 04|BSB and SBLGNT]]

**4:1** Let a man so account of us as of the ministers of Christ, and the dispensers of the mysteries of God. ^4-1

**4:2** Here now it is required among the dispensers, that a man be found faithful. ^4-2

**4:3** But to me it is a very small thing to be judged by you, or by man’s day; but neither do I judge my own self. ^4-3

**4:4** For I am not conscious to myself of any thing, yet am I not hereby justified; but he that judgeth me, is the Lord. ^4-4

**4:5** Therefore judge not before the time; until the Lord come, who both will bring to light the hidden things of darkness, and will make manifest the counsels of the hearts; and then shall every man have praise from God. ^4-5

**4:6** But these things, brethren, I have in a figure transferred to myself and to Apollo, for your sakes; that in us you may learn, that one be not puffed up against the other for another, above that which is written. ^4-6

**4:7** For who distinguisheth thee? Or what hast thou that thou hast not received? And if thou hast received, why dost thou glory, as if thou hadst not received it? ^4-7

**4:8** You are now full; you are now become rich; you reign without us; and I would to God you did reign, that we also might reign with you. ^4-8

**4:9** For I think that God hath set forth us apostles, the last, as it were men appointed to death: we are made a spectacle to the world, and to angels, and to men. ^4-9

**4:10** We are fools for Christ’s sake, but you are wise in Christ; we are weak, but you are strong; you are honourable, but we without honour. ^4-10

**4:11** Even unto this hour we both hunger and thirst, and are naked, and are buffeted, and have no fixed abode; ^4-11

**4:12** And we labour, working with our own hands: we are reviled, and we bless; we are persecuted, and we suffer it. ^4-12

**4:13** We are blasphemed, and we entreat; we are made as the refuse of this world, the offscouring of all even until now. ^4-13

**4:14** I write not these things to confound you; but I admonish you as my dearest children. ^4-14

**4:15** For if you have ten thousand instructors in Christ, yet not many fathers. For in Christ Jesus, by the gospel, I have begotten you. ^4-15

**4:16** Wherefore I beseech you, be ye followers of me, as I also am of Christ. ^4-16

**4:17** For this cause have I sent to you Timothy, who is my dearest son and faithful in the Lord; who will put you in mind of my ways, which are in Christ Jesus; as I teach every where in every church. ^4-17

**4:18** As if I would not come to you, so some are puffed up. ^4-18

**4:19** But I will come to you shortly, if the Lord will: and will know, not the speech of them that are puffed up, but the power. ^4-19

**4:20** For the kingdom of God is not in speech, but in power. ^4-20

**4:21** What will you? shall I come to you with a rod; or in charity, and in the spirit of meekness? ^4-21
