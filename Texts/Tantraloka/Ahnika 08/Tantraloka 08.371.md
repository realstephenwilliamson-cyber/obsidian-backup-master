## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 10 - Chapter Eight, part 8#^pg371|p. 353]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=371>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 8.371cd–372ab.
>
> The five Brahmās are 1) Sadyojāta, 2) Vāmadeva, 3) Aghora, 4) Tatpuruṣa and 5) Īśāna. They say that the six ancillaries (aṅga) are: the Heart, Head, Topknot, Armour, Eye and Weapon. The group of eight Sakala (Rudras) are said to be: 1) Sakala, 2) Akala (Niṣkala), 3) Śūnya, 4) Kalāḍhya (Wealthy with Energies) and 5) Khamalaṅkṛta (Adorned with Space), 6) Kṣapaṇa (Destroyer), 7) Antya (Kṣayānta) (End of Destruction) and 8) Kaṇṭhyauṣṭhya (Gutterolabial), the eighth. The ten Śivas starting from the east are: 1) Oṁkāreśa, 2) Śiva, 3) Dīpta, 4) Hetvīśa (Karaṇeśa) (Lord of the Causes), 5) Daśeśa (the Lord of the Ten), 6) Suśiva (Good Śiva), 7) Kāleśa (Lord of Time), 8) Sūkṣma (Sūkṣmarūpa) (Subtle Form), 9) Sutejas (Good Energy), and 10) Śarva. The (eighteen) Rudras are said to be 1) Vijaya (Victory), 2) Niḥśvāsa (Exhalation), 3) Svāyambhū (Self Born), 4) Agni (Fire), 5) Vīra (Hero), 6) Raurava (Roaring), 7) Mukuṭa (Crown), 8) Visara (Abundance), 9) Indu (Moon), 10) Bindu (Drop), 11) Prodgītavat (Lauded), 12) Lalita (Playful), 13) Siddharudra (Accomplished Rudra), 14) Santāna (Eternal), 15) Śarva, 16) Para (Supreme), 17) Kiraṇa (Ray) and 18) Parameśvara (Supreme Lord). (The wise) know that from all these originate the forms of knowledge (i.e. scriptures) corresponding to their names. (with 8.368cd–373ab)
