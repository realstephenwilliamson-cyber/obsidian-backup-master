## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 08 - Chapter Eight, part 6#^pg280|p. 262]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=280>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 8.249cd–250ab.
>
> Above the eight Bodies (mūrti) rise the twelve Suśivas, starting with Vāma and ending with Ekaśiva, all brilliantly energetic (sutejas) and the colour of saffron. Above that is the one called Vīrabhadra, who resides there as the Lord of the mandala. He sports with the one who has attained union (sāyujya) with him.. Next comes the Group of Eight Mahādevas, ending with Anguṣṭhamātra. (with 8.249–251ab)
