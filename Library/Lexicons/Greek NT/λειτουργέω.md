# λειτουργέω

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · verb

**Dodson:** I minister, serve publicly — I act in the public service, render service, minister, in the widest sense, of some special public religious service, but also of the service of priests and Levites. (Strong's 3008; headword λειτουργέω)
