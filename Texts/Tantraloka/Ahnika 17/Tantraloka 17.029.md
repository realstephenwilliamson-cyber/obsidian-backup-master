## Mūla

तृप्तावाहुतिहुतभुक्पाशप्लोषच्छिदादिषु ।

tṛptāvāhutihutabhukpāśaploṣacchidādiṣu |

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 08 - Chapter Seventeen#^pg174|p. 154]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=174>))
>
> The objective (karmapada) (of the rite) must be mentioned (uhana) when (some element of a rite) does not appear externally, according to its essential nature (fathātmatā), as is the case, for example, with the satisfaction (that results from offering libations,) or the burning away and severance of (inner binding) fetters by the fire that receives oblations. (with 17.28cd–29ab)
>
> When a ritual procedure has been announced and that (rite) is then to be accomplished by means of many associated secondary rites, as happens, for example, in the case of the procedures (relating to) initiation, there is no declaration (of what is being done or modification of the Mantras) (ūha) there (in such cases). (with 17.29cd–30ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* न च अवयविप्राये बहिस्तथात्मतयानवभासने कर्मण्यप्यभ्यूहनं कार्यम्--इत्याह--

> यत्रोद्दिष्टे विधौ पश्चात्तदनन्तैः क्रियात्मकैः ॥
> अंशैः साध्यं न तत्रोहो दीक्षणादिविधिष्विव ।

तदिति--उद्दिष्टविधिलक्षणं वस्तु । क्रियात्मकैरनन्तैरंशैरिति-- गर्भाधानादिभिः । दीक्षणादिविधिष्विवेति--नहि दीक्षां करोमि प्रतिष्ठां करोमि वेत्येक एवायमूहो भवितुमर्हति--इत्यर्थः ॥
