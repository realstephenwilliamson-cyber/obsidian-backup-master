# अः (a)ḥ

<span style="font-size:4em; line-height:1.2">अः</span>

**Card 27 of 108** (printed: Card 1-27 · Alpha 29) · special letter ‘visarga’ · the letter: [[Letter 16 ah (visarga)|अः (a)ḥ]]

## Front

- **Glyph:** अः
- **Variation:** a drawn sign of two arcs, one above the other (◡ over ◠); not transcribed as a character
- **Example:** योगः
- The card has no stroke order (*How to Write*).

## Back

- **Pronunciation:** out-breath
- **Class (as printed):** 1 (guttural) · hard aspiration
- **Note (no printed label):** usually an ending. (a) represents any vowel. The variation followed by p,ph is pronounced pph/ff.
- **Grid:** अं अः; in bold: अः
- **Example:** yogaḥ (union)

## Register

- **Classification:** Aspiration / Outpouring · **Sthāna:** Throat (Kaṇṭhya)
- **Body place** (*mātṛkā-nyāsa*, [[Tantraloka 15.117|TĀ 15.117]]–120): tongue (*jihvā*)
