# LXX Wisdom of Solomon 03

← [[LXX Wisdom of Solomon 02]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Wisdom of Solomon 04]] →

*Wisdom of Solomon · [[LXX]]*

**3:1** But the souls of the righteous are in the hand of God, and there shall no torment touch them.  ^3-1

**3:2** In the sight of the unwise they seemed to die: and their departure is taken for misery,  ^3-2

**3:3** and their going from us to be utter destruction: but they are in peace.  ^3-3

**3:4** For though they be punished in the sight of men, yet is their hope full of immortality.  ^3-4

**3:5** And having been a little chastised, they shall be greatly rewarded: for God proved them, and found them worthy for himself.  ^3-5

**3:6** As gold in the furnace hath he tried them, and received them as a burnt offering.  ^3-6

**3:7** And in the time of their visitation they shall shine, and run to and fro like sparks among the stubble.  ^3-7

**3:8** They shall judge the nations, and have dominion over the people, and their Lord shall reign for ever.  ^3-8

**3:9** They that put their trust in him shall understand the truth: and such as be faithful in love shall abide with him: for grace and mercy is to his saints, and he hath care for his elect.  ^3-9

**3:10** But the ungodly shall be punished according to their own imaginations, which have neglected the righteous, and forsaken the Lord.  ^3-10

**3:11** For whoso despiseth wisdom and nurture, he is miserable, and their hope is vain, their labours unfruitful, and their works unprofitable:  ^3-11

**3:12** their wives are foolish, and their children wicked:  ^3-12

**3:13** Their offspring is cursed. Wherefore blessed is the barren that is undefiled, which hath not known the sinful bed: she shall have fruit in the visitation of souls.  ^3-13

**3:14** And *blessed is* the eunuch, which with his hands hath wrought no iniquity, nor imagined wicked things against God: for unto him shall be given the special gift of faith, and an inheritance in the temple of the Lord more acceptable to his mind.  ^3-14

**3:15** For glorious is the fruit of good labours: and the root of wisdom shall never fall away.  ^3-15

**3:16** As for the children of adulterers, they shall not come to their perfection, and the seed of an unrighteous bed shall be rooted out.  ^3-16

**3:17** For though they live long, yet shall they be nothing regarded: and their last age shall be without honour.  ^3-17

**3:18** Or, if they die quickly, they have no hope, neither comfort in the day of trial.  ^3-18

**3:19** For horrible is the end of the unrighteous generation.  ^3-19
