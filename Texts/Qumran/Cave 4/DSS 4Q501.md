# 4Q501 (4QapocrLam B) · 4QApocryphal Lamentations B

> [!info]- Edition, photographs, parallels and bibliography
> M. Baillet, DJD VII, 79-80, pl. XXVIII PAM 43.617 ROC 279

*Study Edition, pp. 992–995 (the Hebrew or Aramaic on the left-hand pages, in the PDF).*

### Text

**1** […] /…/ Do not give our inheritance to foreigners, nor our produce to the sons of foreigners. Remember that ^t-1

**2** [we are the removed one]s of your people and the forsaken ones of your inheritance. Remember the sons of your covenant, the desolate, ^t-2

**3** […] the spurred ones, the wanderers, who no-one brings back, the sorely wounded, who no-one bandages, ^t-3

**4** [those bent double, who no-one *[p. 995]* rai]ses up. The wretched ones of your people have surrounded us with their lying tongue, and have turned ^t-4

**5** […] and your foliage to one born of a woman. Look and see the disgrace of the sons of ^t-5

**6** [your people for] our skin [is burning,] indignation has taken hold of us, on account of their insolent language. Do not ^t-6

**7** […] in your commandments, and let not their posterity be among {the sons of} the covenant. Blank ^t-7

**8** […] against them with the might of your strength, and avenge yourself on them. ^t-8

**9** […] and they have not placed you before them, but they act violent against the poor and needy. ^t-9
