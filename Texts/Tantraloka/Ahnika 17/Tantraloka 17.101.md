## Mūla

अभिन्नाच्छिवसंबोधजलधेर्युगपत्स्फुरत् ।
पूर्णां क्षिपंस्तत्त्वजालं ध्यायेद्भारूपकं सृतम् ॥ ^mula

abhinnācchivasaṃbodhajaladheryugapatsphurat |
pūrṇāṃ kṣipaṃstattvajālaṃ dhyāyedbhārūpakaṃ sṛtam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 08 - Chapter Seventeen#^pg204|p. 184]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=204>))

> As he casts the full oblation (into the fire), he should meditate on how the net of principles, pulsing radiantly in the form of light, is simultaneously emitted from the undivided ocean of Śiva consciousness.

## Viveka

> [!viveka]- Viveka in English (Dyczkowski, vol. IX, p. 185)
> ![[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 08 - Chapter Seventeen#Viveka on 17.101]]

*Lead-in:* कथं चात्र शुद्धतत्त्वसृष्टिं कुर्यात्?--इत्याशङ्क्याह--

> अभिन्नाच्छिवसंबोधजलधेर्युगपत्स्फुरत् ।
> पूर्णां क्षिपंस्तत्त्वजालं ध्यायेद्भारूपकं सृतम् ॥

शिवसंबोधजलधेः सृतं तत्त्वजालं ध्यायेत्--इति संबन्धः ॥ १०१ ॥
