## Mūla

ये तु विज्ञानिनस्तेऽत्र द्वेधा कम्प्रेतरत्वतः ।
तत्र ये कम्प्रविज्ञानास्ते देहान्ते शिवाः स्फुटम् ॥ ^mula

ye tu vijñāninaste'tra dvedhā kampretaratvataḥ |
tatra ye kampravijñānāste dehānte śivāḥ sphuṭam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 06 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 4#^pg147|p. 130]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=147>))
>
> Those who possess (spiritual) knowledge are here (in this context) of two kinds, according to whether (their knowledge) is a) unstable or b) otherwise. a) Those whose knowledge is unstable, clearly become Śivas when (their) body comes to an end.
