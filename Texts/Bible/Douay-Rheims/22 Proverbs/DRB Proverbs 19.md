# Proverbs 19 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Proverbs 18|← Proverbs 18]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Proverbs 20|Proverbs 20 →]]

**19:1** Better is the poor man, that walketh in his simplicity, than a rich man that is perverse in his lips, and unwise. ^19-1

**19:2** Where there is no knowledge of the soul, there is no good: and he that is hasty with his feet shall stumble. ^19-2

**19:3** The folly of a man supplanteth his seeps: and he fretteth in his mind against God. ^19-3

**19:4** Riches make many friends: but from the poor man, even they whom he had, depart. ^19-4

**19:5** A false witness shall not be unpunished: and he that speaketh lies shall not escape. ^19-5

**19:6** Many honour the person of him that is mighty, and are friends of him that giveth gifts. ^19-6

**19:7** The brethren of the poor man hate him: moreover also his friends have departed far from him. He that followeth after words only, shall have nothing. ^19-7

**19:8** But he that possesseth a mind, loveth his own soul, and he that keepeth prudence shall find good things. ^19-8

**19:9** A false witness shall not be unpunished: and he that speaketh lies, shall perish. ^19-9

**19:10** Delicacies are not seemly for a fool: nor for a servant to have rule over princes. ^19-10

**19:11** The learning of a man is known by patience and his glory is to pass over wrongs. ^19-11

**19:12** As the roaring of a lion, so also is the anger of a king: and his cheerfulness as the dew upon the grass. ^19-12

**19:13** A foolish son is the grief of his father: and a wrangling wife is like a roof continually dropping through. ^19-13

**19:14** House and riches are given by parents: but a prudent wife is properly from the Lord. ^19-14

**19:15** Slothfulness casteth into a deep sleep, and an idle soul shall suffer hunger. ^19-15

**19:16** He that keepeth the commandment, keepeth his own soul: but he that neglecteth his own way, shall die. ^19-16

**19:17** He that hath mercy on the poor, lendeth to the Lord: and he will repay him. ^19-17

**19:18** Chastise thy son, despair not: but to the killing of him set not thy soul. ^19-18

**19:19** He that is impatient, shall suffer damage: and when he shall take away he shall add another thing. ^19-19

**19:20** Hear counsel, and receive instruction, that thou mayst be wise in thy latter end. ^19-20

**19:21** There are many thoughts in the heart of a man: but the will of the Lord shall stand firm. ^19-21

**19:22** A needy man is merciful: and better is the poor than the lying man. ^19-22

**19:23** The fear of the Lord is unto life: and he shall abide in fulness without being visited with evil. ^19-23

**19:24** The slothful hideth his hand under his armpit, and will not so much as bring it to his mouth. ^19-24

**19:25** The wicked man being; scourged, the fool shall be wiser: but if thou rebuke a wise man he will understand discipline. ^19-25

**19:26** He that afflicteth his father, and chaseth away his mother, is infamous and unhappy. ^19-26

**19:27** Cease not, O my son, to hear instruction, and be not ignorant of the words of knowledge. ^19-27

**19:28** An unjust witness scorneth judgment: and the mouth of the wicked devoureth iniquity. ^19-28

**19:29** Judgments are prepared for scorners: and striking hammers for the bodies of fools. ^19-29
