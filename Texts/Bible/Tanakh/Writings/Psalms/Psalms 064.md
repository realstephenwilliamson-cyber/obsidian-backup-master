## Chapter 64

**64:1** For the leader. A psalm of David. ^64-1

**64:2** Hear my voice, O God, when I plead;  
guard my life from the enemy’s terror. ^64-2

**64:3** Hide me from a band of evil men,  
from a crowd of evildoers, ^64-3

**64:4** who whet their tongues like swords;  
they aim their arrows—cruel words— ^64-4

**64:5** to shoot from hiding at the blameless man;  
they shoot him suddenly and without fear. ^64-5

**64:6** They arm themselves with an evil word;  
when they speak, it is to conceal traps;[^1]  
they think, “Who will see them?” ^64-6

**64:7** [^2]Let the wrongdoings they have concealed,[^3]  
each one inside him, his secret thoughts,  
be wholly exposed. ^64-7

**64:8** God shall shoot them with arrows;  
they shall be struck down suddenly. ^64-8

**64:9** Their tongue shall be their downfall;  
all who see them shall recoil in horror; ^64-9

**64:10** all men shall stand in awe;  
they shall proclaim the work of God  
and His deed which they perceived. ^64-10

**64:11** The righteous shall rejoice in the LORD,  
and take refuge in Him;  
all the upright shall exult. ^64-11

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Meaning of verse uncertain.
[^3]: Reading t·amnu with some mss. (cf. Minh·at Shai) and Rashi; most printed editions tamnu, traditionally rendered “they have accom-plished.”
