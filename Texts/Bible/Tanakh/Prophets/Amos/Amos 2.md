## Chapter 2

**2:1** Thus said the LORD:  
For three transgressions of Moab,  
For four, I will not revoke it:  
Because he burned the bones  
Of the king of Edom to lime. ^2-1

**2:2** I will send down fire upon Moab,  
And it shall devour the fortresses of Kerioth.  
And Moab shall die in tumult,  
Amid shouting and the blare of horns; ^2-2

**2:3** I will wipe out the ruler from within her  
And slay all her officials along with him  
—said the LORD. ^2-3

**2:4** Thus said the LORD:  
For three transgressions of Judah,  
For four, I will not revoke it:  
Because they have spurned the Teaching of the LORD  
And have not observed His laws;  
They are beguiled by the delusions  
After which their fathers walked. ^2-4

**2:5** I will send down fire upon Judah,  
And it shall devour the fortresses of Jerusalem. ^2-5

**2:6** Thus said the LORD:  
For three transgressions of Israel,  
For four, I will not revoke it:  
Because they have sold for silver  
Those whose cause was just,  
And the needy for a pair of sandals. ^2-6

**2:7** [Ah,] you who trample the heads of the poor  
Into the dust of the ground,  
And make the humble walk a twisted course![^1]  
Father and son go to the same girl,  
And thereby profane My holy name. ^2-7

**2:8** They recline by every altar  
On garments taken in pledge,  
And drink in the House of their God  
Wine bought with fines they imposed. ^2-8

**2:9** Yet I  
Destroyed the Amorite before them,  
Whose stature was like the cedar’s  
And who was stout as the oak,  
Destroying his boughs above  
And his trunk below! ^2-9

**2:10** And I  
Brought you up from the land of Egypt  
And led you through the wilderness forty years,  
To possess the land of the Amorite! ^2-10

**2:11** And I raised up prophets from among your sons  
And nazirites from among your young men.  
Is that not so, O people of Israel?  
—says the LORD. ^2-11

**2:12** But you made the nazirites drink wine  
And ordered the prophets not to prophesy. ^2-12

**2:13** Ah, I will slow your movements  
As a wagon is slowed  
When it is full of cut grain.[^2] ^2-13

**2:14** Flight shall fail the swift,  
The strong shall find no strength,  
And the warrior shall not save his life. ^2-14

**2:15** The bowman shall not hold his ground,  
And the fleet-footed shall not escape,  
Nor the horseman save his life. ^2-15

**2:16** Even the most stouthearted warrior  
Shall run away unarmed[^3] that day  
—declares the LORD. ^2-16

[^1]: Understanding sho’afim as equivalent to shafim. Emendation yields: “Who crush on the ground / The heads of the poor, / And push off the road / The humble of the land”; cf. Job 24.4.
[^2]: Meaning of verse uncertain; alternatively: “I will slow your movements / As a threshing sledge (cf. Isa. 28.27–28) is slowed / When clogged by cut grain.”
[^3]: Lit. “naked.”
