# Zohar 3.075a

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 7.08 - Parashat Aharei Mot#^z3-75a|Parashat Aḥarei Mot, at 3:75a]]

*Volume 3, folio 75a* · Achrei Mot

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Achrei Mot 54:333–54:335.*

**Achrei Mot 55:336** [^1]THE NAKEDNESS OF THY FATHER’S WIFE SHALT THOU NOT UNCOVER. Who is meant by thy father’s wife? Said R. Simeon: ‘We have learnt: ^achrei-mot-55-336

**Achrei Mot 55:337** As long as the Matrona is with the King and giveth suck to thee, she is called “thy mother”. Now, however, that she is banished with thee and is removed from the King, she is called “thy father’s wife”. She is his wife, because he has not divorced her, although she is in exile. ^achrei-mot-55-337

**Achrei Mot 55:338** Therefore the text enjoins concerning her twice-once in reference to the time when she is still with the King and is called “thy mother”, and once in reference to the time when she is banished from the King’s palace and is called the wife of the King. Although she is far from him, thou shalt not cause her to remove from thee, lest thine enemies gain dominion over thee and she do not protect thee in the captivity. Hence “thou shalt not uncover the nakedness of thy father’s wife”. Why? Because “it is thy father’s nakedness”: because although she is removed from the King, yet he is constantly watching over her, and therefore thou must be careful before her and not sin against her.’ ^achrei-mot-55-338

**Achrei Mot 56:339** R. Simeon here quoted the verse: “For the Lord thy God walketh in the midst of thy camp to deliver thee”, etc. (Deut. 23, 14). ‘This’, he said, ‘refers to the Shekinah, which is in the midst of Israel, and especially in the captivity, to protect them continually and on all sides from all other peoples, that they should not destroy them. ^achrei-mot-56-339

**Achrei Mot 56:340** For so it has been taught, that the enemies of Israel have no power over them until Israel weaken the might of the Shekinah in face of the Chieftains who are appointed over the other nations. Then only the latter have power over them and enact cruel decrees against them. But when they return in repentance to her she breaks the power of all those Chieftains and of the enemies of Israel and avenges them on all. ^achrei-mot-56-340

[^1]: The first 18 lines of the Hebrew text are not found in our translation.
