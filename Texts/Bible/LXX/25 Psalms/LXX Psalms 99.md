# LXX Psalms 99

← [[LXX Psalms 98]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 100]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 100]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 100]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**99:1** A Psalm for Thanksgiving. Make a joyful noise to the Lord, all the earth.  ^99-1

**99:2** Serve the Lord with gladness; come before his presence with exultation.  ^99-2

**99:3** Know that the Lord he is God; he made us, and not we ourselves; *we are* his people, and the sheep of his pasture.  ^99-3

**99:4** Enter into his gates with thanksgiving, and his courts with hymns; give thanks to him, praise his name.  ^99-4

**99:5** For the Lord is good, his mercy is for ever; and his truth *endures* to generation and generation.  ^99-5
