## Mūla

अष्टौ मर्मशतान्येकचत्वारिंशच्च जायते ।
वंशैर्विषयसंख्यैश्च पद्मं युग्मेन्दुमण्डलम् ॥ ^mula

aṣṭau marmaśatānyekacatvāriṃśacca jāyate |
vaṃśairviṣayasaṃkhyaiśca padmaṃ yugmendumaṇḍalam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 05 - Chapter Thirty-One, part 2#^pg138|p. 120]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=138>))
>
> Eight hundred and forty-one junctures (marman) are produced (in this way). (with 31.134ab)
>
> The lotus (is drawn with squares) numbering the (five) objects of sense (viṣaya), and consists of two mandalas of the moon. The pedestal (is fashioned) with (squares) numbering the sentiments (i.e. six). (There) the svastika (is located,) that bestows all (one’s) desires. The door, and (so too) the platform, are (fashioned within squares) numbering the (eight) Vasus. Such is the circumambulation of the squares. (with 31.134cd–135)
