## Mūla

अतो नियतिकालादिवैचित्र्यानुविधायिनः ।
अनुग्रहस्तु यः सोऽयं स्वस्वरूपे विकस्वरे ॥ ^mula

ato niyatikālādivaicitryānuvidhāyinaḥ |
anugrahastu yaḥ so'yaṃ svasvarūpe vikasvare || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 05 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 3#^pg120|p. 103]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=120>))
>
> These (phases of) creation, persistence and re-absorption (to which the embodied soul is subject) are due to the strength of the (soul’s) Karma etc, and so (take place) in accord with the dictates of a variety (of constraints, due to the soul’s Karma) (niyati), time and the rest. (with 28.232cd–233ab)
>
> How can the grace (anugraha), which is the intuitive insight (jñapti) into one’s own unfolding (vikasvara) nature (in its fullness), be dependent? on Karma and the constraint (it exerts) etc. (with 28.233cd–234ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* अनुग्रहः पुनरस्य कर्मादि अनपेक्ष्यैव स्यात्--इत्याह--

> अनुग्रहस्तु यः सोऽयं स्वस्वरूपे विकस्वरे ॥
> ज्ञप्त्यात्मेति कथं कर्मनियत्यादि प्रतीक्षते ।
