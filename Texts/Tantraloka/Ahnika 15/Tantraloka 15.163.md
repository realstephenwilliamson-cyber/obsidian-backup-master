## Mūla

तेन शुद्धं तु सर्वं यदशुद्धमपि तच्छुचि ।
अशुद्धता च विज्ञेया पशुतच्छासनाशयात् ॥ ^mula

tena śuddhaṃ tu sarvaṃ yadaśuddhamapi tacchuci |
aśuddhatā ca vijñeyā paśutacchāsanāśayāt || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 04 - Chapter Fifteen, part 3#^pg120|p. 102]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=120>))
>
> As the pervasive Lord has said in the Śrīpūrvaśāstra with regards to the procedure (concerning the) sacrificial vessel: ‘One should not think (upakalpayet) that any substance, whatever it be (that is offered in this rite), has not been purified (by it). Purified by that, everything, even what is impure, (becomes) pure.’ (with 15.162–163ab)
>
> One should know that (whatever is said concerning) impurity (aśuddhatā) is from the perspective of the fettered souls and their doctrines. (with 15.163cd)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* ननु नीलादिवत् प्रतिभासविकारकारित्वाभावात् न शुद्ध्यशुद्धी नाम कश्चित् भावधर्मः--इत्युक्तं प्राग्बहुशः, तदशुद्धतैव का यदपसारणेनापि शुद्धता स्यात् ? --इत्याशङ्क्य आह—

> अशुद्धता च विज्ञेया पशुतच्छासनाशयात् ॥
