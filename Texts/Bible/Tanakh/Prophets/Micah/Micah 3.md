## Chapter 3

**3:1** I said:  
Listen, you rulers of Jacob,  
You chiefs of the House of Israel!  
For you ought to know what is right, ^3-1

**3:2** [^1]But you hate good and love evil. ^3-2

**3:3** You have devoured My people’s flesh;  
You have flayed the skin off them,  
And their flesh off their bones.  
And after tearing their skins off them,  
And their flesh off their bones,[^2]  
And breaking their bones to bits,  
You have cut it up as into[^3] a pot,  
Like meat in a caldron. ^3-3

**3:4** Someday they shall cry out to the LORD,  
But He will not answer them;  
At that time He will hide His face from them,  
In accordance with the wrongs they have done. ^3-4

**3:5** Thus said the LORD to the prophets  
Who lead My people astray,  
Who cry “Peace!”  
When they have something to chew,  
But launch a war on him  
Who fails to fill their mouths: ^3-5

**3:6** Assuredly,  
It shall be night for you  
So that you cannot prophesy,  
And it shall be dark for you  
So that you cannot divine;  
The sun shall set on the prophets,  
And the day shall be darkened for them. ^3-6

**3:7** The seers shall be shamed  
And the diviners confounded;  
They shall cover their upper lips,[^4]  
Because no response comes from God. ^3-7

**3:8** But I,  
I am filled with strength by the spirit of the LORD,  
And with judgment and courage,  
To declare to Jacob his transgressions  
And to Israel his sin. ^3-8

**3:9** Hear this, you rulers of the House of Jacob,  
You chiefs of the House of Israel,  
Who detest justice  
And make crooked all that is straight, ^3-9

**3:10** Who build Zion with crime,  
Jerusalem with iniquity! ^3-10

**3:11** Her rulers judge for gifts,  
Her priests give rulings for a fee,  
And her prophets divine for pay;  
Yet they rely upon the LORD, saying,  
“The LORD is in our midst;  
No calamity shall overtake us.” ^3-11

**3:12** Assuredly, because of you  
Zion shall be plowed as a field,  
And Jerusalem shall become heaps of ruins,  
And the Temple Mount  
A shrine in the woods. ^3-12

[^1]: Syntax of vv. 2–3 uncertain.
[^2]: Brought down from v. 2 for clarity.
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain; Septuagint and Syriac read “like flesh in.”
[^4]: As a sign of mourning; cf. Ezek. 24.17, 22; Lev. 13.45.
