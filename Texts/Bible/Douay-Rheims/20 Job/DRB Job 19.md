# Job 19 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Job 18|← Job 18]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Job 20|Job 20 →]]

**19:1** Then Job answered, and said: ^19-1

**19:2** How long do you afflict my soul, and break me in pieces with words? ^19-2

**19:3** Behold, these ten times you confound me, and are not ashamed to oppress me. ^19-3

**19:4** For if I have been ignorant, my ignorance shall be with me. ^19-4

**19:5** But you have set yourselves up against me, and reprove me with my reproaches. ^19-5

**19:6** At least now understand, that God hath not afflicted me with an equal judgment, and compassed me with his scourges. ^19-6

**19:7** Behold I cry suffering violence, and no one will hear: I shall cry aloud, and there is none to judge. ^19-7

**19:8** He hath hedged in my path round about, and I cannot pass, and in my way he hath set darkness. ^19-8

**19:9** He hath stripped me of my glory, and hath taken the crown from my head. ^19-9

**19:10** He hath destroyed me on every side, and I am lost, and he hath taken away my hope, as from a tree that is plucked up. ^19-10

**19:11** His wrath is kindled against me, and he hath counted me as his enemy. ^19-11

**19:12** His troops have come together, and have made themselves a way by me, and have besieged my tabernacle round about. ^19-12

**19:13** He hath put my brethren far from me, and my acquaintance like strangers have departed from me. ^19-13

**19:14** My kinsmen have forsaken me, and they that knew me, have forgotten me. ^19-14

**19:15** They that dwelt in my house, and my maidservants have counted me a stranger, and I have been like an alien in their eyes. ^19-15

**19:16** I called my servant, and he gave me no answer, I entreated him with my own mouth. ^19-16

**19:17** My wife hath abhorred my breath, and I entreated the children of my womb. ^19-17

**19:18** Even fools despise me; and when I gone from them, they spoke against me. ^19-18

**19:19** They that were sometime my counsellors, have abhorred me: and he whom I love most is turned against me. ^19-19

**19:20** The flesh being consumed. My bone hath cleaved to my skin, and nothing but lips are left about my teeth. ^19-20

**19:21** Have pity on me, have pity on me, at least you my friends, because the hand of the Lord hath touched me. ^19-21

**19:22** Why do you persecute me as God, and glut yourselves with my flesh? ^19-22

**19:23** Who will grant me that my words may be written? Who will grant me that they may be marked down in a book? ^19-23

**19:24** With an iron pen and in a plate of lead, or else be graven with an instrument in flint stone. ^19-24

**19:25** For I know that my Redeemer liveth, and in the last day I shall rise out of the earth. ^19-25

**19:26** And I shall be clothed again with my skin, and in my flesh I will see my God. ^19-26

**19:27** Whom I myself shall see, and my eyes shall behold, and not another: this my hope is laid up in my bosom. ^19-27

**19:28** Why then do you say now: Let us persecute him, and let us find occasion of word against him? ^19-28

**19:29** Flee then from the face of the sword, for the sword is the revenger of iniquities: and know ye that there is judgment. ^19-29
