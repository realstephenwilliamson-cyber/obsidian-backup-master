# Proverbs 12 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Proverbs 11|← Proverbs 11]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Proverbs 13|Proverbs 13 →]]

**12:1** Whoever loves discipline loves knowledge, but those who hate to be rebuked are stupid. ^12-1

**12:2** The good obtain favor from the LORD, but those who devise evil he condemns. ^12-2

**12:3** No one finds security by wickedness, but the root of the righteous will never be moved. ^12-3

**12:4** A good wife is the crown of her husband, but she who brings shame is like rottenness in his bones. ^12-4

**12:5** The thoughts of the righteous are just; the advice of the wicked is treacherous. ^12-5

**12:6** The words of the wicked are a deadly ambush, but the speech of the upright delivers them. ^12-6

**12:7** The wicked are overthrown and are no more, but the house of the righteous will stand. ^12-7

**12:8** One is commended for good sense, but a perverse mind is despised. ^12-8

**12:9** Better to be despised and have produce^[Cn: Heb *servant*] than to be self-important and lack food. ^12-9

**12:10** The righteous know the needs of their animals, but the mercy of the wicked is cruel. ^12-10

**12:11** Those who till their land will have plenty of food, but those who follow worthless pursuits have no sense. ^12-11

**12:12** The wicked covet the proceeds of wickedness,^[Or *covet the catch of the wicked*] but the root of the righteous bears fruit. ^12-12

**12:13** The evil are ensnared by the transgression of their lips, but the righteous escape from trouble. ^12-13

**12:14** From the fruit of the mouth one is filled with good things, and manual labor has its reward. ^12-14

**12:15** Fools think their own way is right, but the wise listen to advice. ^12-15

**12:16** Fools show their anger at once, but the prudent ignore an insult. ^12-16

**12:17** Whoever speaks the truth gives honest evidence, but a false witness speaks deceitfully. ^12-17

**12:18** Rash words are like sword thrusts, but the tongue of the wise brings healing. ^12-18

**12:19** Truthful lips endure forever, but a lying tongue lasts only a moment. ^12-19

**12:20** Deceit is in the mind of those who plan evil, but those who counsel peace have joy. ^12-20

**12:21** No harm happens to the righteous, but the wicked are filled with trouble. ^12-21

**12:22** Lying lips are an abomination to the LORD, but those who act faithfully are his delight. ^12-22

**12:23** One who is clever conceals knowledge, but the mind of a fool^[Heb *the heart of fools*] broadcasts folly. ^12-23

**12:24** The hand of the diligent will rule, while the lazy will be put to forced labor. ^12-24

**12:25** Anxiety weighs down the human heart, but a good word cheers it up. ^12-25

**12:26** The righteous are released from misfortune,^[Syr: Meaning of Heb uncertain] but the way of the wicked leads them astray. ^12-26

**12:27** The lazy do not roast^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] their game, but the diligent obtain precious wealth.^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^12-27

**12:28** In the path of righteousness there is life; in walking its path there is no death. ^12-28
