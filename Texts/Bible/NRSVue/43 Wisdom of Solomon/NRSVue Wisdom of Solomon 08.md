# Wisdom of Solomon 8 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Wisdom of Solomon 07|← Wisdom of Solomon 7]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Wisdom of Solomon 09|Wisdom of Solomon 9 →]]

**8:1** She reaches mightily from one end of the earth to the other, and she orders all things well. ^8-1

## Love for wisdom

**8:2** I loved her and sought her from my youth; I desired to take her for my bride and became enamored of her beauty. ^8-2

**8:3** She glorifies her noble birth by living with God, and the Lord of all loves her. ^8-3

**8:4** For she is an initiate in the knowledge of God and an associate in his works. ^8-4

**8:5** If riches are a desirable possession in life, what is richer than wisdom, the active cause of all things? ^8-5

**8:6** And if understanding is effective, who more than she is fashioner of what exists? ^8-6

**8:7** And if anyone loves righteousness, her labors are virtues, for she teaches self-control and prudence, justice and courage; nothing in life is more profitable for mortals than these. ^8-7

**8:8** And if anyone longs for wide experience, she knows the things of old and infers the things to come; she understands turns of speech and the solutions of riddles; she has foreknowledge of signs and wonders and of the outcome of seasons and times. ^8-8

## Wisdom indispensable to rulers

**8:9** Therefore I determined to take her to live with me, knowing that she would give me good counsel and encouragement in cares and grief. ^8-9

**8:10** Because of her I shall have glory among the multitudes and honor in the presence of the elders, though I am young. ^8-10

**8:11** I shall be found keen in judgment, and in the sight of rulers I shall be admired. ^8-11

**8:12** When I am silent they will wait for me, and when I speak they will give heed; if I speak at greater length, they will put their hands on their mouths. ^8-12

**8:13** Because of her I shall have immortality and leave an everlasting remembrance to those who come after me. ^8-13

**8:14** I shall govern peoples, and nations will be subject to me; ^8-14

**8:15** dread monarchs will be afraid of me when they hear of me; among the people I shall show myself capable and courageous in war. ^8-15

**8:16** When I enter my house, I shall find rest with her, for companionship with her has no bitterness, and life with her has no pain but gladness and joy. ^8-16

**8:17** When I considered these things inwardly and pondered in my heart that in kinship with wisdom there is immortality, ^8-17

**8:18** and in friendship with her, pure delight, and in the labors of her hands, unfailing wealth, and in the experience of her company, understanding, and renown in sharing her words, I went about seeking how to get her for myself. ^8-18

**8:19** As a child I was naturally gifted, and a good soul fell to my lot, ^8-19

**8:20** or rather, being good, I entered an undefiled body. ^8-20

**8:21** But I perceived that I would not possess wisdom unless God gave her to me—and it was a mark of insight to know whose gift she was—so I appealed to the Lord and implored him, and with my whole heart I said, ^8-21
