## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. II
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 02 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v02 04 - Chapter Three, part 1#^pg108|p. 92]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 02.pdf#page=108>))
>
> The compassionate God of the gods has revealed this in order to develop the (awakened) consciousness (bodha) of those who lack intelligence, (saying that all that exists is an image reflected within consciousness and hence is quite) real (vastu), (although) it is not also capable (nāpy alam) of being elsewhere (that is, outside the mirror of consciousness). Nor is it complete (as it lacks some aspects of the original image). (But although it) offers resistance (to merger with other reflections), it is not independent (rather its existence depends on the mirror). It is neither (fully) permanent (sthāyin) nor (totally) transitory (asthāyin). It is (produced) by the power (mahimā) of some (peculiar, higher reality) which, translucent (svaccha), (reflects all things within itself). (with 3.21–22)
