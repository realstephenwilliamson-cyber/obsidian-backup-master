## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 17 - Chapter Twenty-Five (continued) and Chapter Twenty-Six#^pg398|p. 378]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=398>))
>
> Once the teacher has understood this clearly (samyak) and is inebriated with supreme bliss, having worshipped (the Lord) in each abode of that kind (that is, within each competent disciple), he should practice the procedure. (with 26.24cd–25ab)
>
> Just as one disciple does not know the religious practice (anuṣthāna) of another, so too the teacher should act (in secrecy), for a breach of secrecy is a (culpable) defect. (The mantras) he should tell (his disciple) may be that of the triad of Goddesses, who may be pure (i.e. solitary), or in union with (their consorts). He may tell him (the Mantra of just) one goddess, pure (i.e. alone), or else in union (with her Bhairava). (with 26.25cd–27ab)
