## Mūla

भिन्नकार्याकृतिव्रातेन्द्रियचक्रानुसन्धिमान् ।
एको यथाहं वह्न्यादिषड्रूपोऽस्मि तथा स्फुटम् ॥ ^mula

bhinnakāryākṛtivrātendriyacakrānusandhimān |
eko yathāhaṃ vahnyādiṣaḍrūpo'smi tathā sphuṭam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 05 - Chapter Sixteen, part 1#^pg61|p. 41]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=61>))
>
> “Just as I am the one subject who unifies (all) the diverse products (kārya) (of consciousness), forms and assembly of the senses (indriyacakra), in the same way, I am clearly manifest (sphuṭa) in six ways (rūpa) as the fire and the rest.” Thus viewing (himself) in this way, (the teacher) should worship (amongst the six Paths) the Path by which he intends to perform the initiation, into (these) five (experiences of oneness), with the exception (of the body of) the disciple, in order to bring about (their) unification. (with 16.87–88)
