## Mūla

अङ्गादि निःसृतं पूज्यं विस्फुलिङ्गात्मकं पृथक् ।
मध्यगा किल या देवी सैव सद्भावरूपिणी ॥ ^mula

aṅgādi niḥsṛtaṃ pūjyaṃ visphuliṅgātmakaṃ pṛthak |
madhyagā kila yā devī saiva sadbhāvarūpiṇī || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 06 - Chapter Fifteen, part 5#^pg220|p. 202]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=220>))
>
> Thus, the limbs (of the deity) and the rest has come forth from that mass of consciousness endowed with the divinity (of the Goddess) present in the centre; sparks (of its light) should be worshipped separately. (with 15.330cd–331ab)
>
> The goddess in the middle is (essentially) Mātrsadbhāva, who is Kālasaṁkarṣiṇī. She is fierce, tranquil, and both together. In the Siddhātantra she is called the goddess Parā, who consists of one syllable (SAUH). (with 15.331cd–332)
