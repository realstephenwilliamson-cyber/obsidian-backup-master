## Mūla

शिवैकात्म्यविकल्पौघद्वारिका निर्विकल्पता ।
अन्यथा तस्य शुद्धस्य विमर्शप्राणवर्तिनः ॥ ^mula

śivaikātmyavikalpaughadvārikā nirvikalpatā |
anyathā tasya śuddhasya vimarśaprāṇavartinaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 05 - Chapter Fifteen, part 4#^pg179|p. 161]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=179>))
>
> This itself is said to be the stability (dārdhya) (of insight) that is taken to heart by (the adept’s) reflective awareness (as true and certain), namely, the state free of thought constructs (that arises) by means of the flux of thought constructs (concerning his) oneness with Śiva. (with 15.270cd–271ab)
>
> (If it were) otherwise, how could the nature (of the Lord,) who is pure and abides as the (very) life of reflective awareness and whose nature is to make (all things) manifest, not be contemplated? (with 15.271cd–272ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* एतदेव व्यतिरेकद्वारेण दर्शयति--

> अन्यथा तस्य शुद्धस्य विमर्शप्राणवर्तिनः ॥
> कथं नामाविमृष्टं स्याद्रूपं भासनधर्मणः ।

यदि हि एतदविकल्पात्मपरामर्शमयं शिवत्वं भावनालभ्यं न भवेत्, तत् विकल्पकलङ्कोन्मुक्तस्य सहजविमर्शात्मनः स्वप्रकाशस्य तस्य कथं नाम अविमृष्टं रूपं स्यात् सततविमृष्टं भवेत्-- इत्यर्थः ॥
