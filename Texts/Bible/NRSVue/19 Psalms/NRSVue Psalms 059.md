# Psalm 59 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 058|← Psalm 58]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 060|Psalm 60 →]]

## Prayer for deliverance from enemies

*To the leader: Do Not Destroy. Of David. A Miktam, when Saul ordered his house to be watched in*

*order to kill him.*

**59:1** Deliver me from my enemies, O my God; protect me from those who rise up against me. ^59-1

**59:2** Deliver me from those who work evil; from the bloodthirsty, save me. ^59-2

**59:3** Even now they lie in wait for my life; the mighty stir up strife against me. For no transgression or sin of mine, O LORD, ^59-3

**59:4** for no fault of mine, they run and make ready. Rouse yourself, come to my help and see! ^59-4

**59:5** You, LORD God of hosts, are God of Israel. Awake to punish all the nations; spare none of those who treacherously plot evil. *Selah* ^59-5

**59:6** Each evening they come back howling like dogs and prowling about the city. ^59-6

**59:7** There they are, bellowing with their mouths, with sharp words^[Heb *with swords*] on their lips, for “Who,” they think,^[Heb lacks *they think*] “will hear us?” ^59-7

**59:8** But you laugh at them, O LORD; you hold all the nations in derision. ^59-8

**59:9** O my strength, I will watch for you, for you, O God, are my fortress. ^59-9

**59:10** My God in his steadfast love will meet me; my God will let me look in triumph on my enemies. ^59-10

**59:11** Do not kill them, or my people may forget; make them totter by your power and bring them down, O Lord, our shield. ^59-11

**59:12** For the sin of their mouths, the words of their lips, let them be trapped in their pride. For the cursing and lies that they utter, ^59-12

**59:13** consume them in wrath; consume them until they are no more. Then it will be known to the ends of the earth that God rules over Jacob. *Selah* ^59-13

**59:14** Each evening they come back howling like dogs and prowling about the city. ^59-14

**59:15** They roam about for food and growl if they do not get their fill. ^59-15

**59:16** But I will sing of your might; I will sing aloud of your steadfast love in the morning. For you have been a fortress for me and a refuge in the day of my distress. ^59-16

**59:17** O my strength, I will sing praises to you, for you, O God, are my fortress, the God who shows me steadfast love. ^59-17
