# LXX Psalms 05

← [[LXX Psalms 04]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 06]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 005]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 005]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**5:1** For the end, a Psalm of David, concerning her that inherits.  ^5-1

**5:2** Hearken to my words, O Lord, attend to my cry.[^1]  ^5-2

**5:3** Attend to the voice of my supplication, my King, and my God: for to thee, O Lord, will I pray.  ^5-3

**5:4** In the morning thou shalt hear my voice: in the morning will I wait upon thee, and will look up.  ^5-4

**5:5** For thou art not a God that desires iniquity; neither shall the worker of wickedness dwell with thee.  ^5-5

**5:6** Neither shall the transgressors continue in thy sight: thou hatest, O Lord, all them that work iniquity.  ^5-6

**5:7** Thou wilt destroy all that speak falsehood: the Lord abhors the bloody and deceitful man.  ^5-7

**5:8** But I will enter into thine house in the multitude of thy mercy: I will worship in thy fear toward thy holy temple.  ^5-8

**5:9** Lead me, O Lord, in thy righteousness because of mine enemies; make my way plain before thy face.  ^5-9

**5:10** For there is no truth in their mouth; their heart is vain; their throat is an open sepulchre; with their tongues they have used deceit.  ^5-10

**5:11** Judge them, O God; let them fail of their counsels: cast them out according to the abundance of their ungodliness; for they have provoked thee, O Lord.[^2]  ^5-11

**5:12** But let all that trust on thee be glad in thee: they shall exult for ever, and thou shalt dwell among them; and all that love thy name shall rejoice in thee.  ^5-12

**5:13** For thou, Lord, shalt bless the righteous: thou hast compassed us as with a shield of favour.[^3]  ^5-13

[^1]: 5:2 *Lit.* understand.
[^2]: 5:11 *Gr.* fall from, *or,* by reason of.
[^3]: 5:13 *Lit.* crowned.
