# Job 33

Compare: [[NRSVue Job 33|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 33

**33:1** But now, Job, listen to my words,  
Give ear to all that I say. ^33-1

**33:2** Now I open my lips;  
My tongue forms words in my mouth. ^33-2

**33:3** My words bespeak the uprightness of my heart;  
My lips utter insight honestly. ^33-3

**33:4** The spirit of God formed me;  
The breath of Shaddai sustains me. ^33-4

**33:5** If you can, answer me;  
Argue against me, take your stand. ^33-5

**33:6** You and I are the same before God;  
I too was nipped from clay. ^33-6

**33:7** You are not overwhelmed by fear of me;  
My pressure does not weigh heavily on you. ^33-7

**33:8** Indeed, you have stated in my hearing,  
I heard the words spoken, ^33-8

**33:9** “I am guiltless, free from transgression;  
I am innocent, without iniquity. ^33-9

**33:10** But He finds reasons to oppose me,  
Considers me His enemy. ^33-10

**33:11** He puts my feet in stocks,  
Watches all my ways.” ^33-11

**33:12** In this you are not right;  
I will answer you: God is greater than any man. ^33-12

**33:13** Why do you complain against Him  
That He does not reply to any of man’s charges? ^33-13

**33:14** For God speaks time and again[^1]—  
Though man does not perceive it— ^33-14

**33:15** In a dream, a night vision,  
When deep sleep falls on men,  
While they slumber on their beds. ^33-15

**33:16** Then He opens men’s understanding,  
And by disciplining them leaves His signature ^33-16

**33:17** To turn man away from an action,  
To suppress pride in man. ^33-17

**33:18** He spares him from the Pit,  
His person, from perishing by the sword. ^33-18

**33:19** He is reproved by pains on his bed,  
And the trembling in his bones is constant. ^33-19

**33:20** He detests food;  
Fine food [is repulsive] to him. ^33-20

**33:21** His flesh wastes away till it cannot be seen,  
And his bones are rubbed away till they are invisible. ^33-21

**33:22** He comes close to the Pit,  
His life [verges] on death. ^33-22

**33:23** If he has a representative,  
One advocate against a thousand  
To declare the man’s uprightness, ^33-23

**33:24** Then He has mercy on him and decrees,  
“Redeem him from descending to the Pit,  
For I have obtained his ransom; ^33-24

**33:25** Let his flesh be healthier[^2] than in his youth;  
Let him return to his younger days.” ^33-25

**33:26** He prays to God and is accepted by Him;  
He enters His presence with shouts of joy,  
For He requites a man for his righteousness. ^33-26

**33:27** He[^3] declares[^4] to men,  
“I have sinned; I have perverted what was right;  
But I was not paid back for it.” ^33-27

**33:28** He redeemed him from passing into the Pit;  
He[^5] will enjoy the light. ^33-28

**33:29** Truly, God does all these things  
Two or three times to a man, ^33-29

**33:30** To bring him back from the Pit,  
That he may bask in the light of life. ^33-30

**33:31** Pay heed, Job, and hear me;  
Be still, and I will speak; ^33-31

**33:32** If you have what to say, answer me;  
Speak, for I am eager to vindicate you. ^33-32

**33:33** But if not, you listen to me;  
Be still, and I will teach you wisdom. ^33-33

[^1]: Lit. “once…twice.”
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: I.e., the contrite man.
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^5]: Or with kethib, “me…I.”
