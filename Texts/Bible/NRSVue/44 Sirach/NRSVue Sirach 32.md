# Sirach 32 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Sirach 31|← Sirach 31]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Sirach 33|Sirach 33 →]]

## Etiquette at a banquet

**32:1** If they make you master of the feast, do not exalt yourself; be among them as one of their number. Take care of them first and then sit down; ^32-1

**32:2** when you have fulfilled all your duties, take your place, so that you may be merry along with them and receive a wreath for your excellent leadership. ^32-2

**32:3** Speak, you who are older, for it is your right, but with accurate knowledge, and do not interrupt the music. ^32-3

**32:4** Where there is entertainment, do not pour out talk; do not display your cleverness at the wrong time. ^32-4

**32:5** A ruby seal in a setting of gold is a concert of music at a banquet of wine. ^32-5

**32:6** A seal of emerald in a rich setting of gold is the melody of music with good wine. ^32-6

**32:7** Speak, you who are young, if you are obliged to, but no more than twice and only if asked. ^32-7

**32:8** Be brief; say much in few words; be as one who knows and can still hold his tongue. ^32-8

**32:9** Among the great do not act as their equal; and when another is speaking, do not babble. ^32-9

**32:10** Lightning travels ahead of the thunder, and approval goes before one who is modest. ^32-10

**32:11** Leave in good time and do not be the last; go home quickly and do not linger. ^32-11

**32:12** Amuse yourself there to your heart’s content, but do not sin through proud speech. ^32-12

**32:13** But above all bless your Maker, who intoxicates you with his good gifts. ^32-13

## The providence of God

**32:14** The one who seeks God^[Heb: Gk *who fears the Lord*] will accept his discipline, and those who rise early to seek him^[Other ancient authorities lack *to seek him*] will find favor. ^32-14

**32:15** The one who seeks the law will be filled with it, but the hypocrite will stumble at it. ^32-15

**32:16** Those who fear the Lord will form true judgments, and they will kindle righteous deeds like a light. ^32-16

**32:17** Sinners will shun reproof and will find a decision according to their liking. ^32-17

**32:18** A sensible person will not overlook a thoughtful suggestion; an insolent^[Heb: Gk *alien*] and proud person will not be deterred by fear.^[Or *cower in fear*; other ancient authorities add *and after acting, with him, without deliberation*] ^32-18

**32:19** Do nothing without deliberation, but when you have acted do not regret it. ^32-19

**32:20** Do not go on a path full of hazards, and do not stumble at an obstacle twice.^[Heb: Gk *stumble on stony ground*] ^32-20

**32:21** Do not be overconfident on a smooth^[Or *an unexplored*] road, ^32-21

**32:22** and give good heed to your paths.^[Heb Syr: Gk *and beware of your children*] ^32-22

**32:23** Guard^[Heb Syr: Gk *Trust*] yourself in every act, for this is the keeping of the commandments. ^32-23

**32:24** The one who keeps the law preserves himself,^[Heb: Gk *who believes the law heeds the commandments*] and the one who trusts the Lord will not suffer loss. ^32-24
