## Mūla

मलैकयुक्तस्तत्कर्मयुक्तः प्रलयकेवलः ।
मलमज्ञानमिच्छन्ति संसाराङ्कुरकारणम् ॥ ^mula

mal aikayuktas tatkarmayuktaḥ pralayakevalaḥ |
malam ajñānam icchanti saṁsārāṅkurakāraṇam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2022 – June 2022
> p. 42 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=18>))
>
> **The Group of Four Selves**
>
> One should know that the Self is of four kinds. There (amongst them) is the Vijñānakevala (perceiver), who is conjoined to (just) one (form of) impurity. The Pralayakevala is conjoined with that, and (also the impurity of) Karma. (with 1.22cd–23ab)

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2022 – June 2022
> p. 42 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=18>))
>
> **Impurity**
>
> (The wise) prefer (the view that) Impurity (mala) is ignorance (not a material substance as the dualists maintain). It is the cause of (Karma, which is) the sprout of transmigratory existence (saṁsāra).[^48] (with 1.23cd)

[^48]: Quoted in TĀ 1/23ab and 9/120cd, also in TĀv ad 9/65cd-66a (1/23a) and intro to 9/98cd-99ab, and paraphrased in TĀ 13/49cd. The second sentence is quoted in TĀ 9/86b and explained in 9/88cd-89ab. MV 1/23cd-24ab quoted verbatin in TĀ 9/120cd-121ab.
