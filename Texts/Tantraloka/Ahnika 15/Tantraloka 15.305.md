## Mūla

वामा ज्येष्ठा रौद्री काली कलबलविकरिके बलमथनी ।
भूतदमनी च मनोन्मनिका शान्ता शक्रचापरुचिरत्र स्यात् ॥ ^mula

vāmā jyeṣṭhā raudrī kālī kalabalavikarike balamathanī |
bhūtadamanī ca manonmanikā śāntā śakracāparuciratra syāt || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 06 - Chapter Fifteen, part 5#^pg198|p. 180]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=198>))
>
> (The first nine are) Vāmā, Jyeṣṭhā, Raudrī, Kālī, Kalavikaraṇī, Balavikaraṇī, Balapramathinī, (Sarva)bhūtadamanī, and (in the middle), Manonmanī. (They are pervaded) here by the tranquil light of a rainbow. (The other nine are) Vibhvī, Jñānī, Kṛti (Kriyā), Icchā, Vāgīśī, Jvālinī, Vāmā, Jyeṣṭhā, and Raudrī. Beginning from the eastern petal, they all (shine resplendently) like the Fire of Time. (with 15.305–306)
