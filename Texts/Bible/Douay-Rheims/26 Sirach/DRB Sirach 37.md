# Sirach 37 (Douay-Rheims)

*Ecclesiasticus 37 in the Douay-Rheims*

[[DRB Sirach 36|← Sirach 36]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Sirach 38|Sirach 38 →]]

**37:1** Every friend will say: I also am his friend: but there is a friend, that is only a friend in name. Is not this a grief even to death? ^37-1

**37:2** But a companion and a friend shall be turned to an enemy. ^37-2

**37:3** O wicked presumption, whence camest thou to cover the earth with thy malice, and deceitfulness? ^37-3

**37:4** There is a companion who rejoiceth with his friend in his joys, but in the time of trouble, he will be against him. ^37-4

**37:5** There is a companion who condoleth with his friend for his belly’s sake, and he will take up a shield against enemy. ^37-5

**37:6** Forget not thy friend in thy mind, and be not unmindful of him in thy riches. ^37-6

**37:7** Consult not with him that layeth a snare for thee, and hide thy counsel from them that envy thee. ^37-7

**37:8** Every counsellor giveth out counsel, but there is one that is a counsellor for himself. ^37-8

**37:9** Beware of a counsellor. And know before what need he hath: for he will devise to his own mind: ^37-9

**37:10** Lest he thrust a stake into the ground, and say to thee: ^37-10

**37:11** Thy way is good; and then stand on the other side to see what shall befall thee. ^37-11

**37:12** Treat not with a man without religion concerning holiness, nor with an unjust man concerning justice, nor with a woman touching her of whom she is jealous, nor with a coward concerning war, nor with a merchant about traffic, nor with a buyer of selling, nor with an envious man of giving thanks, ^37-12

**37:13** Nor with the ungodly of piety, nor with the dishonest of honesty, nor with the held labourer of every work, ^37-13

**37:14** Nor with him that worketh by the year of the finishing of the year, nor with an idle servant of much business: give no heed to these in any matter of counsel. ^37-14

**37:15** But be continually with a holy man, whomsoever thou shalt know to observe the fear of God, ^37-15

**37:16** Whose soul is according to thy own soul: and who, when thou shalt stumble in the dark, will be sorry for thee. ^37-16

**37:17** And establish within thyself a heart of good counsel: for there is no other thing of more worth to thee than it. ^37-17

**37:18** The soul of a holy man discovereth sometimes true things, more than seven watchmen that sit in a high piece to watch. ^37-18

**37:19** But above all these things pray to the most High, that he may direct thy way in truth. ^37-19

**37:20** In all thy works let the true word go before thee, and steady counsel before every action. ^37-20

**37:21** A wicked word shall change the beast: out of which four manner of things arise, good and evil, life and death: and the tongue is continually the ruler of them. There is a man that is subtle and a teacher of many, and yet is unprofitable to his own soul. ^37-21

**37:22** A skillful man hath taught many, and is sweet to his own soul. ^37-22

**37:23** He that speaketh sophistically, is hateful: he shall be destitute of every thing. ^37-23

**37:24** Grace is not given him from the Lord: for he is deprived of all wisdom. ^37-24

**37:25** There is a wise man that is wise to his own soul: and the fruit of his understanding is commendable. ^37-25

**37:26** A wise man instructeth his own people, and the fruits of his understanding are faithful. ^37-26

**37:27** A wise man shall be filled with blessings, and they that see shall praise him. ^37-27

**37:28** The life of a man is in the number of his days: but the days of Israel are innumerable. ^37-28

**37:29** A wise man shall inherit honour among his people, and his name shall live for ever. ^37-29

**37:30** My son, prove thy soul in thy life: and if it be wicked, give it no power: ^37-30

**37:31** For all things are not expedient for all, and every kind pleaseth not every soul. ^37-31

**37:32** Be not greedy in any feasting, and pour not out thyself upon any meat: ^37-32

**37:33** For in many meats there will be sickness, and greediness will turn to choler. ^37-33

**37:34** By surfeiting many have perished: but he that is temperate, shall prolong life. ^37-34
