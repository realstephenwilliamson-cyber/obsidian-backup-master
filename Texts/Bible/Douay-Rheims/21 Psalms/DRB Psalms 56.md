# Psalms 56 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 55|← Psalms 55]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 57|Psalms 57 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 56|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**56:1** Unto the end, destroy not, for David, for an inscription of a title, when he fled from Saul into the cave. [1 Kings 24] ^56-1

**56:2** Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me: for my soul trusteth in thee. And in the shadow of thy wings will I hope, until iniquity pass away. ^56-2

**56:3** I will cry to God the most High; to God who hath done good to me. ^56-3

**56:4** He hath sent from heaven and delivered me: he hath made them a reproach that trod upon me. God hath sent his mercy and his truth, ^56-4

**56:5** And he hath delivered my soul from the midst of the young lions. I slept troubled. The sons of men, whose teeth are weapons and arrows, and their tongue a sharp sword. ^56-5

**56:6** Be thou exalted, O God, above the heavens, and thy glory above all the earth. ^56-6

**56:7** They prepared a snare for my feet; and they bowed down my soul. They dug a pit before my face, and they are fallen into it. ^56-7

**56:8** My heart is ready, O God, my heart is ready: I will Sing, and rehearse a psalm. ^56-8

**56:9** Arise, O my glory, arise psaltery and harp: I will arise early. ^56-9

**56:10** I will give praise to thee, O Lord, among the people: I will sing a psalm to thee among the nations. ^56-10

**56:11** For thy mercy is magnified even to the heavens: and thy truth unto the clouds. ^56-11

**56:12** Be thou exalted, O God, above the heavens: and thy glory above all the earth. ^56-12
