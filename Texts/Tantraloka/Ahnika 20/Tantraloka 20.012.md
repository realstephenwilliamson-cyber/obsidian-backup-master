## Mūla

यथा श्रीतन्त्रसद्भावे कथिता परमेशिना ।
श्रीपूर्वशास्त्रेऽप्येषा च सूचिता परमेशिना ॥ ^mula

yathā śrītantrasadbhāve kathitā parameśinā |
śrīpūrvaśāstre'pyeṣā ca sūcitā parameśinā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 11 - Chapter Twenty#^pg257|p. 237]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=257>))
>
> Or else (the teacher may impart the initiation) the Supreme Lord has taught in the Tantrasadbhāva that examines (the disciple’s degree of) purification by (weighing him on) scales (and so) possesses an (outer) sign of (its) accomplishment, (with 20.11cd–12ab)
>
> This same (kind of initiation) has been mentioned by the Supreme Lord in the Śrīpūrvaśāstra (Mālinīvijayottara) also, where he speaks of the gradation of the (descent of the) power (of grace), as indicated by (the signs of its attainment). They are joy, an upward leap, trembling, sleep, and the rolling of the eyes (ghūrni), as the fifth. (with 20.11cd–13)
