## Chapter Fifteen

Then the Blessed Lord said:

\[1\] “The eternal *ashvatta* tree,
with roots above, branches below,
has leaves that are the sacred hymns.
Who knows this tree knows the *Vedas*.

\[2\] Its branches spread widely below and above,
   nourished by the *gunas*. Its sprouts can be sensed,
and its roots also stretch downward far below,
   engendering action in the human world.

\[3\] The form of this tree cannot be perceived here,
   not its end, beginning, nor continuance.
Having cut this *ashvatta* tree off at the root
   with the strong, sharp ax of nonattachment,

\[4\] One must then seek as the final goal that place
   from which, having once arrived, no one returns.
It is in that primal Spirit I take refuge,
   where the primeval energy first streamed forth.

\[5\] Prideless, clear, evils of attachment conquered,
   in *Atman* constantly, desires averted,
free from duality of pleasure and pain,
   the undeluded go to that eternal place.

\[6\] Neither the sun, nor the moon,
nor does fire illumine that place,
to which having gone none return.
That place is My supreme abode.

[p. 85] \[7\] When a mere fraction of Myself
becomes a soul in the living world,
it draws to itself the sixth sense, mind,
and the others in material nature.

\[8\] When the Lord acquires a body
and when too He leaves it behind,
He takes the senses along with Him
like wind bearing perfume away.

\[9\] With hearing, sight, touch, taste, and smell
at His command, as well as mind,
the Lord as an incarnate soul
enjoys the objects of the senses.

\[10\] When departing or remaining
or while enjoying with the *gunas*,
He is not seen by the deluded
but only by those with wisdom’s eye.

\[11\] *Yogins* strive and see the Lord
situated in the *Atman*,
but mindless, unperfected souls,
though they may strive, do not see Him.

\[12\] The sun’s brilliance that illumines
every corner of the universe
and is in the moon and in fire—
know the brilliance belongs to Me.

\[13\] Alighting on earth I support
all beings with My energy,
and I cause all the plants to thrive,
becoming juicy-savored *soma*.

\[14\] Entering all breathing bodies
I become their digestive fire.
[p. 86] With the inbreath and the outbreath
I digest the four kinds of food.

\[15\] And I have entered the heart of all beings.
   Memory, knowledge, and their loss come from Me.
I am all there is to know in the *Vedas*;
   I made the *Vedanta*, am the *Vedas*’ knower.

\[16\] There are two spirits in the world,
one transient and one eternal.
All beings are the transient.
The unchanging is called the eternal.

\[17\] But there is another higher spirit
that is called the Supreme *Atman*,
who going into the three worlds
supports them as their eternal Lord.

\[18\] Since I transcend the transient,
am higher than the transient,
I am known as the Supreme Spirit
in the world and in the *Vedas*.

\[19\] Those who know without delusion
that I am the Supreme Spirit
know everything and worship Me
with all their being, Arjuna.

\[20\] This most secret of all doctrines
I have thus taught. Awakened to it,
one would be awakened truly,
all duties fulfilled, Arjuna.”
