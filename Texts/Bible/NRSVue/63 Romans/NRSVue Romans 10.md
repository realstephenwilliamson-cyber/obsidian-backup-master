# Romans 10 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Romans 09|← Romans 9]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Romans 11|Romans 11 →]]

Compare: [[Romans 10|BSB and SBLGNT]] · [[DRB Romans 10|Douay-Rheims]] · [[JANT Romans 10|JANT notes]]

**10:1** Brothers and sisters, my heart’s desire and prayer to God for them is that they may be saved. ^10-1

**10:2** For I can testify that they have a zeal for God, but it is not based on knowledge. ^10-2

**10:3** Not knowing the righteousness of God and seeking to establish their own, they have not submitted to God’s righteousness. ^10-3

**10:4** For Christ is the culmination of the law so that there may be righteousness for everyone who believes. ^10-4

## Salvation is for all

**10:5** Moses writes concerning the righteousness that comes from the law, that “the person who does these things will live by them.” ^10-5

**10:6** But the righteousness that comes from faith says, “Do not say in your heart, ‘Who will ascend into heaven?’ ” (that is, to bring Christ down) ^10-6

**10:7** “or ‘Who will descend into the abyss?’ ” (that is, to bring Christ up from the dead). ^10-7

**10:8** But what does it say? “The word is near you, in your mouth and in your heart” (that is, the word of faith that we proclaim), ^10-8

**10:9** because^[Or *namely, that*] if you confess with your mouth that Jesus is Lord and believe^[Or *trust*] in your heart that God raised him from the dead, you will be saved. ^10-9

**10:10** For one believes^[Or *trusts*] with the heart, leading to righteousness, and one confesses with the mouth, leading to salvation. ^10-10

**10:11** The scripture says, “No one who believes^[Or *trusts*] in him will be put to shame.” ^10-11

**10:12** For there is no distinction between Jew and Greek; the same Lord is Lord of all and is generous to all who call on him. ^10-12

**10:13** For “everyone who calls on the name of the Lord shall be saved.” ^10-13

**10:14** But how are they to call on one in whom they have not believed?^[Or *trusted*] And how are they to believe^[Or *trust*] in one of whom they have never heard? And how are they to hear without someone to proclaim him? ^10-14

**10:15** And how are they to proclaim him unless they are sent? As it is written, “How beautiful are the feet of those who bring good news!” ^10-15

**10:16** But not all have obeyed the good news,^[Or *gospel*] for Isaiah says, “Lord, who has believed^[Or *trusted*] our message?” ^10-16

**10:17** So faith^[Or *trust*] comes from what is heard, and what is heard comes through the word of Christ.^[Or *about Christ*; other ancient authorities read *of God*] ^10-17

**10:18** But I ask, have they not heard? Indeed they have: “Their voice has gone out to all the earth and their words to the ends of the world.” ^10-18

**10:19** Again I ask, did Israel not understand? First Moses says, “I will use those who are not a nation to make you jealous; with a foolish nation I will provoke you.” ^10-19

**10:20** Then Isaiah is so bold as to say, “I have been found by those who did not seek me; I have shown myself to those who did not ask for me.” ^10-20

**10:21** But of Israel he says, “All day long I have held out my hands to a disobedient and contrary people.” ^10-21
