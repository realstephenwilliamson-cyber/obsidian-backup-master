## Mūla

स्वातन्त्र्याद्वर्तमानैव परामर्शस्वरूपिणी ।
स च द्वादशधा तत्र सर्वमन्तर्भवेद्यतः ॥ ^mula

svātantryādvartamānaiva parāmarśasvarūpiṇī |
sa ca dvādaśadhā tatra sarvamantarbhavedyataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. III
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 03 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v03 05 - Chapter Four, part 4#^pg162|p. 149]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 03.pdf#page=162>))
>
> In the same way, this same consciousness manifests by virtue of its freedom as both internal and external (reality), even as it persists as essentially (pure) reflective awareness. (with 4.122cd–123ab)
>
> And that is twelve-fold there, as it includes everything within (itself), and is the Sun (of perception), which is the nature of the Moon (of objectivity), and abides as all things. That consciousness is in reality (paramārthataḥ) made up of twelve digits (kalā). (with 4.123cd–124)
