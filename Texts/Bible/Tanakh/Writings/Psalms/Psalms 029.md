## Chapter 29

**29:1** A psalm of David.  
Ascribe to the LORD, O divine beings,  
ascribe to the LORD glory and strength. ^29-1

**29:2** Ascribe to the LORD the glory of His name;  
bow down to the LORD, majestic in holiness. ^29-2

**29:3** The voice of the LORD is over the waters;  
the God of glory thunders,  
the LORD, over the mighty waters. ^29-3

**29:4** The voice of the LORD is power;  
the voice of the LORD is majesty; ^29-4

**29:5** the voice of the LORD breaks cedars;  
the LORD shatters the cedars of Lebanon. ^29-5

**29:6** He makes Lebanon skip like a calf,[^1]  
Sirion, like a young wild ox. ^29-6

**29:7** The voice of the LORD kindles flames of fire; ^29-7

**29:8** the voice of the LORD convulses the wilderness;  
the LORD convulses the wilderness of Kadesh; ^29-8

**29:9** the voice of the LORD causes hinds to calve,  
and strips forests bare;[^2]  
while in His temple all say “Glory!” ^29-9

**29:10** The LORD sat enthroned at the Flood;  
the LORD sits enthroned, king forever. ^29-10

**29:11** May the LORD grant strength to His people;  
may the LORD bestow on His people wellbeing. ^29-11

[^1]: Lit. “He makes them skip like a calf, Lebanon and Sirion, etc.”
[^2]: Or “brings ewes to early birth.”
