## Mūla

न जातु गोचरो यस्माद्देहान्तरविनिश्चयः ।
यत्तु बन्धुप्रियापुत्रपानादिस्मरणं स्फुटम् ॥ ^mula

na jātu gocaro yasmāddehāntaraviniścayaḥ |
yattu bandhupriyāputrapānādismaraṇaṃ sphuṭam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 06 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 4#^pg167|p. 150]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=167>))
>
> Thus, the last memory from which (proceeds) the firm decision (to assume) another (particular) body is certainly not in the sphere (of perception) of (ordinary) perceivers that are not omniscient. (with 28.341cd–342ab)
>
> The memory (when close to death) of relatives, the beloved, sons, and drinking etc. is clearly not connected with the (new) subsequent body, as it is not the last (memory) of that (previous one). (with 28.342cd–343ab)

## Viveka

> यत्तु बन्धुप्रियापुत्रपानादिस्मरणं स्फुटम् ॥
> न तद्देहान्तरासङ्गि न तदन्त्यं यतो भवेत् ।

न तदन्त्यमिति अपितु उपान्त्यक्षणवर्ती मृतिभोगोऽयम्--इति भावः ॥
