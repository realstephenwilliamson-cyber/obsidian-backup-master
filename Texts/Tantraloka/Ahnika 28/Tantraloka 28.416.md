## Mūla

शक्तिनाशान्महादोषो नरकं शाश्वतं प्रिये ।
इति श्रीरत्नमालायां समयोल्लङ्घने कृते ॥ ^mula

śaktināśānmahādoṣo narakaṃ śāśvataṃ priye |
iti śrīratnamālāyāṃ samayollaṅghane kṛte || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 07 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 5#^pg208|p. 191]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=208>))
>
> (A rite of) atonement (niṣkrti) that cancels the sin when the Rule has been transgressed is taught in the venerable Ratnamālā for those who have been initiated into a Kula (kulaja). (There the Lord says,) “(I will tell you) what I myself have previously enjoined (in the course of explaining) the procedure (vidhi) called ‘Śiva’s hand’. Having repeated the (Mantra of) the great Weapon a hundred times, he is freed (from sin). (The sole) exception (is the sin of) killing a woman. The blame (dosa)) (incurred) by killing a Śakti is (very) great. (Indeed,) O beloved, (the consequence) is eternal hell.” (with 28.415–417ab)
