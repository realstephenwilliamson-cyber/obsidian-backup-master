# Proverbs 18 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Proverbs 17|← Proverbs 17]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Proverbs 19|Proverbs 19 →]]

**18:1** The one who lives alone is self-indulgent, showing contempt for all sound judgment.^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^18-1

**18:2** A fool takes no pleasure in understanding, but only in expressing personal opinion. ^18-2

**18:3** When wickedness comes, contempt comes also, and with dishonor comes disgrace. ^18-3

**18:4** The words of the mouth are deep waters; the fountain of wisdom is a gushing stream. ^18-4

**18:5** It is not right to be partial to the guilty or to subvert the innocent in judgment. ^18-5

**18:6** A fool’s lips bring strife, and a fool’s mouth invites a flogging. ^18-6

**18:7** The mouths of fools are their ruin, and their lips a snare to themselves. ^18-7

**18:8** The words of a whisperer are like delicious morsels; they go down into the inner parts of the body. ^18-8

**18:9** One who is slack in work is close kin to a vandal. ^18-9

**18:10** The name of the LORD is a strong tower; the righteous run into it and are safe. ^18-10

**18:11** The wealth of the rich is their strong city; in their imagination it is like a high wall. ^18-11

**18:12** Before destruction one’s heart is haughty, but humility goes before honor. ^18-12

**18:13** If one gives answer before hearing, it is folly and shame. ^18-13

**18:14** The human spirit will endure sickness, but a broken spirit—who can bear? ^18-14

**18:15** An intelligent mind acquires knowledge, and the ear of the wise seeks knowledge. ^18-15

**18:16** A gift opens doors; it gives access to the great. ^18-16

**18:17** The one who first states a case seems right, until the other comes and cross-examines. ^18-17

**18:18** Casting the lot puts an end to disputes and decides between powerful contenders. ^18-18

**18:19** An ally offended is stronger than a city;^[Gk Syr Vg Tg: Meaning of Heb uncertain] such quarreling is like the bars of a castle. ^18-19

**18:20** From the fruit of the mouth one’s stomach is satisfied; the yield of the lips brings satisfaction. ^18-20

**18:21** Death and life are in the power of the tongue, and those who love it will eat its fruits. ^18-21

**18:22** He who finds a wife finds a good thing and obtains favor from the LORD. ^18-22

**18:23** The poor use entreaties, but the rich answer roughly. ^18-23

**18:24** Some^[Syr Tg: Heb *A man of*] friends play at friendship,^[Cn Compare Syr Vg Tg: Meaning of Heb uncertain] but a true friend sticks closer than one’s sibling. ^18-24
