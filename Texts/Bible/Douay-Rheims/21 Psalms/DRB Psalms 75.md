# Psalms 75 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 74|← Psalms 74]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 76|Psalms 76 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 75|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**75:1** Unto the end, in praises, a psalm for Asaph: a canticle to the Assyrians. ^75-1

**75:2** In Judea God is known: his name is great in Israel. ^75-2

**75:3** And his place is in peace: and his abode in Sion: ^75-3

**75:4** There hath he broken the powers of bows, the shield, the sword, and the battie. ^75-4

**75:5** Thou enlightenest wonderfully from the everlasting hills. ^75-5

**75:6** All the foolish of heart were troubled. They have slept their sleep; and all the men of riches have found nothing in their hands. ^75-6

**75:7** At thy rebuke, O God of Jacob, they have all slumbered that mounted on horseback. ^75-7

**75:8** Thou art terrible, and who shall resist thee? from that time thy wrath. ^75-8

**75:9** Thou hast caused judgment to be heard from heaven: the earth trembled and was still, ^75-9

**75:10** When God arose in judgment, to save all the meek of the earth. ^75-10

**75:11** For the thought of man shall give praise to thee: and the remainders of the thought shall keep holiday to thee. ^75-11

**75:12** Vow ye, and pay to the Lord your God: all you that are round about him bring presents. To him that is terrible, ^75-12

**75:13** Even to him who taketh away the spirit of princes: to the terrible with the kings of the earth. ^75-13
