# Psalm 128

← [[BCP Psalm 127]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 129]] →

*Beati omnes* · Book Five · Twenty-seventh Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 128]]

**128:1** Happy are they all who fear the LORD, \*  
and who follow in his ways! ^128-1

**128:2** You shall eat the fruit of your labor; \*  
happiness and prosperity shall be yours. ^128-2

**128:3** Your wife shall be like a fruitful vine within your house, \*  
your children like olive shoots round about your table. ^128-3

**128:4** The man who fears the LORD \*  
shall thus indeed be blessed. ^128-4

**128:5** The LORD bless you from Zion, \*  
and may you see the prosperity of Jerusalem all the days of your life. ^128-5

**128:6** May you live to see your children's children; \*  
may peace be upon Israel. ^128-6
