## Mūla

न्यासयोगेन शिष्याय दीप्यमानं महार्चिषम् ।
पाशस्तोभात्ततस्तस्य परतत्त्वे तु योजनम् ॥ ^mula

nyāsayogena śiṣyāya dīpyamānaṃ mahārciṣam |
pāśastobhāttatastasya paratattve tu yojanam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 13 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 5#^pg398|p. 381]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=398>))
>
> Having deposited the letter A nine times on (his) body, (the teacher) should then transmit it, shining with great lustre, to the disciple by depositing it (on his body). Then (the disciple’s) bonds are paralysed and so he is united with the Supreme Reality. This is the procedure (I have) seen in the Dīkṣottara, taught to me by Sambhunātha. 241cd-243ab (with 29.241cd–243ab)
