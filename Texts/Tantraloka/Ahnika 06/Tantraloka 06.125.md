## Mūla

चैत्रे मन्त्रोदितिः सोऽपि तालुन्युक्तोऽधुना पुनः ।
हृदि चैत्रोदितिस्तेन तत्र मन्त्रोदयोऽपि हि ॥ ^mula

caitre mantroditiḥ so'pi tālunyukto'dhunā punaḥ |
hṛdi caitroditistena tatra mantrodayo'pi hi || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 11 - Chapter Six, part 4#^pg366|p. 351]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=366>))
>
> It was stated earlier that the Mantras start to arise in the month of Caitra, which arises, in its turn, in the palate. Now, instead, (the year that takes its name from) Caitra arises in the Heart, and so it is here that Mantra also arises. (125cd 126ab)
