## Mūla

संपूज्य मातरं वह्नेः पितरं च विसर्जयेत् ।
चर्वादिसाधनायाग्निं समुद्धृत्य ततः पुनः ॥ ^mula

saṁpūjya mātaraṁ vahneḥ pitaraṁ ca visarjayet |
carvādisādhanāyāgniṁ samuddhṛtya tataḥ punaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2025 – April 2025
> p. 33 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=47>))
>
> Having recollected Parāparā, he should offer a full oblation again and (after) having worshipped the mother and the father of the fire, dismiss them. Then having taken fire (from the hearth, install it) again (separately) in order to prepare the sacrificial pap etc.[^130] (with 8.114cd–115)

[^130]: MV 8/115 is paraphrased and explained in TĀ 15/414-415ab (411-412ab).
