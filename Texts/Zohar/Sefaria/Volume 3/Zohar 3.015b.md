# Zohar 3.015b

← [[Zohar 3.015a]] · [[Zohar (Soncino)]] · [[Zohar 3.016a]] →

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 7.03 - Parashat Va-Yiqra#^z3-15b|Parashat Va-Yiqra, at 3:15b]]

*Volume 3, folio 15b* · Vayikra

*Passage Vayikra 32:246 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 3.015a#^vayikra-32-246|Vayikra 32:246]].*

**Vayikra 32:247** But when sin is rife in the world, the Ancient Holy One is hidden and they do not look at one another face to face, and punishment is let loose on the world and the thrones are cast down and the Ancient of Days is hidden and does not appear, so that sinners turn mercy into judgement. ^vayikra-32-247

**Vayikra 33:248** We have learnt as follows. From the side of the Mother issue emissaries of punishment who are armed with the clubs of *Geburah* (Severity), and prevail over Mercy, and then the worlds are defective and there is conflict between them. ^vayikra-33-248

**Vayikra 33:249** But when men amend their ways below, punishment is mitigated and removed, and mercy is awakened and prevails over the evil which arose from stern judgement, and then there is joy and consolation, as it is written, “And the Lord was comforted of the evil” (Ex. 32, 14). ^vayikra-33-249

**Vayikra 33:250** When judgement is mitigated, all the Crowns return to their places and the keys are restored to the Mother, and this is called repentance (*teshubah*, lit. returning), and the world is forgiven, since the Mother is in perfect joy.’[^1] ^vayikra-33-250

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Vayikra 33:251–35:257.*

[^1]: The passage which follows is written in so allusive a style that it would be hardly possible to convey its meaning by a translation. The point of it is that sin (especially the sin of unchastity) uncovers the nakedness of the Mother (*Binah)…* and repentance is the covering-up again. The word *teshubah* (returning) is also explained to mean, “causing the light from the Ancient Holy One to return to the Small of Countenance”.
