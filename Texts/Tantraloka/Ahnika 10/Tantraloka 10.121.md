## Mūla

कोऽपि भावः प्रोज्झतीति सत्यं तद्भावना फलेत् ।
श्रीपूर्वशास्त्रे तेनोक्तं शिवः साक्षान्न भिद्यते ॥ ^mula

ko'pi bhāvaḥ projjhatīti satyaṃ tadbhāvanā phalet |
śrīpūrvaśāstre tenoktaṃ śivaḥ sākṣānna bhidyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 12 - Chapter Ten, part 3#^pg390|p. 371]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=390>))
>
> Thus, in fact nothing whatsoever, even if it is objective in this way, ceases to be Śiva. It is because of this that meditation (bhāvanā) on that (particular aspect of reality) bestows its (own) fruit. (with 10.120cd–121ab)
>
> Thus, it is said in the Śrīpūrvaśāstra (i.e. the Mālinīvijayottara) that ‘Śiva, (Himself) is not directly (sākṣāt) divided’. (with 10.121cd)
