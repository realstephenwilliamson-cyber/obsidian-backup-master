# nityākaulatantra

**Catalog number:** M00316  
**Manuscript:** NGMCP Manuscript No. 2-226 Reel No. B26/21a  
**Published:** Muktabodha Indological Research Institute, India

## Description

Description by Mark S.G. Dyczkowski:
This is the only manuscript that has been recovered of this Tantra
also called Nityātantra. Although just a few badly damaged
leaves, the text which is certainly prior to the 10th century
represents an important testimony of the early worship of
Tripurāsundarī as Tripurabhairavī. This goddess is known to the
Kubjikā Tantras that precede the emergence of the well-known
śrīvidyā tradition which is centered on the worship of the goddess
Tripurāsundarī. She developed out of the pool of Nityā Yoginīs as the
most important of them, around which a group of sixteen Nityās
are configured in her distinctive yantra, the śrīcakra. This
fragment is thus of special importance for the historian of the
development of this tradition.
