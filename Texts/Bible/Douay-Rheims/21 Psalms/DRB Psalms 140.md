# Psalms 140 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 139|← Psalms 139]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 141|Psalms 141 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 140|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**140:1** I have cried to the, O Lord, hear me: hearken to my voice, when I cry to thee. ^140-1

**140:2** Let my prayer be directed as incense in thy sight; the lifting up of my hands, as evening sacrifice. ^140-2

**140:3** Set a watch, O Lord, before my mouth: and a door round about my lips. ^140-3

**140:4** Incline not my heart to evil words; to make excuses in sins. With men that work iniquity: and I will not communicate with the choicest of them. ^140-4

**140:5** The just shall correct me in mercy, and shall reprove me: but let not the oil of the sinner fatten my head. For my prayer also shall still be against the things with which they are well pleased: ^140-5

**140:6** Their judges falling upon the rock have been swallowed up. They shall hear my words, for they have prevailed: ^140-6

**140:7** As when the thickness of the earth is broken up upon the ground: Our bones are scattered by the side of hell. ^140-7

**140:8** But o to thee, O Lord, Lord, are my eyes: in thee have I put my trust, take not away my soul. ^140-8

**140:9** Keep me from the snare, which they have laid for me, and from the stumblingblocks of them that work iniquity. ^140-9

**140:10** The wicked shall fall in his net: I am alone until I pass. ^140-10
