## Mūla

तस्य स्नुषा कर्णवधूर्विधूत-
संसारवृत्तिः सुतमेकमेव ।
यासूत योगेश्वरिदत्तसंज्ञं
नामानुरूपस्फुरदर्थतत्त्वम् ॥ ^mula

tasya snuṣā karṇavadhūrvidhūta-
saṃsāravṛttiḥ sutamekameva |
yāsūta yogeśvaridattasaṃjñaṃ
nāmānurūpasphuradarthatattvam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 11 - Chapter Thirty-Seven, part 1#^pg282|p. 264]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=282>))
>
> (Ambā, Śauris) daughter-in-law, that is, Karṇa’s wife, had shaken off the condition of worldly life (sarnsāravrtti). She had only one son, who was given the name Yogeśvara (Lord of Yoga), "° who was radiant with the essence of reality, as befitted his name.
