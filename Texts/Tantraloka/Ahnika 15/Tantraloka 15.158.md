## Mūla

समस्तकारकैकात्म्यं तेनास्याः परमं वपुः ।
यष्ट्राधारस्य तादात्म्यं स्थानशुद्धिविधिक्रमात् ॥ ^mula

samastakārakaikātmyaṃ tenāsyāḥ paramaṃ vapuḥ |
yaṣṭrādhārasya tādātmyaṃ sthānaśuddhividhikramāt || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 04 - Chapter Fifteen, part 3#^pg118|p. 100]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=118>))
>
> Thus, as the act of worship (performed in this way) bestows that identity (fanmayībhāva) (with Śiva) in this way, its ultimate nature (vapus) is thus the oneness of all the factors (that constitute action). (with 15.157cd–158ab)
>
> The identification of the officiant with the foundation (where he performs the rite and the ground of the process of unification) is (achieved) by the procedure by which the location (of the sacrifice) is purified (sthānaśuddhi). (The various factors that operate together to accomplish ritual action, that is, the subject, represented by) the officiant, (the object, represented by the sacrificial substances which are) the offering (yājya), (the locative by) their location, the instrumental (by the water etc. which are the instruments of worship), the ablative (by the sacrificial vessel from which it is taken), the dative (by the deity to which the sacrifice is offered), become one with Śiva through the process of depositing (the syllabaries and Mantras). (with 15.158cd–159)
