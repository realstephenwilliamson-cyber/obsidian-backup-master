## Chapter 39

**39:1** For the leader; for *Jeduthun*. A psalm of David. ^39-1

**39:2** I resolved I would watch my step  
lest I offend by my speech;  
I would keep my mouth muzzled  
while the wicked man was in my presence. ^39-2

**39:3** I was dumb, silent;  
I was very[^1] still  
while my pain was intense. ^39-3

**39:4** My mind was in a rage,  
my thoughts were all aflame;  
I spoke out: ^39-4

**39:5** Tell me, O LORD, what my term is,  
what is the measure of my days;  
I would know how fleeting my life is. ^39-5

**39:6** You have made my life just handbreadths long;  
its span is as nothing in Your sight;  
no man endures any longer than a breath.[^2] *Selah*. ^39-6

**39:7** Man walks about as a mere shadow;  
mere futility is his hustle and bustle,  
amassing and not knowing who will gather in. ^39-7

**39:8** What, then, can I count on, O Lord?  
In You my hope lies. ^39-8

**39:9** Deliver me from all my transgressions;  
make me not the butt of the benighted. ^39-9

**39:10** I am dumb, I do not speak up,  
for it is Your doing. ^39-10

**39:11** Take away Your plague from me;  
I perish from Your blows. ^39-11

**39:12** You chastise a man in punishment for his sin,  
consuming like a moth what he treasures.  
No man is more than a breath. *Selah*. ^39-12

**39:13** Hear my prayer, O LORD;  
give ear to my cry;  
do not disregard my tears;  
for like all my forebears  
I am an alien, resident with You. ^39-13

**39:14** Look away from me, that I may recover,[^3]  
before I pass away and am gone. ^39-14

[^1]: Cf. use of t·wb in Hos. 10.1; Jonah 4.4.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
