## Mūla

तथा तथा विचित्रः स्यादवतारस्तदंशतः ।
सिद्धान्तादौ पुराणेषु तथा च श्रूयते बहु ॥ ^mula

tathā tathā vicitraḥ syādavatārastadaṃśataḥ |
siddhāntādau purāṇeṣu tathā ca śrūyate bahu || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 05 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 3#^pg132|p. 115]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=132>))
>
> Although they have (all) entered (equally) into (this same) state of union, their descent (into the world) as partial incarnations (aṁśa)) (of Rudra) is diverse, as befits the residual traces (of their past Karma) (sanskāra). (with 28.265cd–266ab)
>
> And such is what is often heard in the Purāṇas and in the Siddhāntas etc., namely, (although they are all) equally incarnations (avatāra) of Rudra, (their) Karma and worldly experience is varied. (with 28.266cd–267ab)

## Viveka

> तुल्ये रुद्रावतारत्वे चित्रत्वं कर्मभोगयोः ।
