## Mūla

मक्षिका मक्षिकाराजं यथोत्थितमनूत्थिताः ।
स्थितं चानुविशन्त्येवं चित्तं सर्वाक्षवृत्तयः ॥ ^mula

makṣikā makṣikārājaṃ yathotthitamanūtthitāḥ |
sthitaṃ cānuviśantyevaṃ cittaṃ sarvākṣavṛttayaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 06 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 4#^pg146|p. 129]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=146>))
>
> (Just as) when the king of the bees rises up (in flight), (swarms of) bees rise up after him, and when he is stationary, they follow suit (and settle down), so is it in the same way with the mind and all the functions of the senses.
