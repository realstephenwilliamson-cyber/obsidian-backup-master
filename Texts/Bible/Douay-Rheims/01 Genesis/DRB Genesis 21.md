# Genesis 21 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Genesis 20|← Genesis 20]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Genesis 22|Genesis 22 →]]

**21:1** And the Lord visited Sara, as he had promised: and fulfilled what he had spoken. ^21-1

**21:2** And she conceived and bore a son in her old age, at the time that God had foretold her. ^21-2

**21:3** And Abraham called the name of his son, whom Sara bore him, Isaac. ^21-3

**21:4** And he circumcised him the eighth day, as God had commanded him, ^21-4

**21:5** When he was a hundred years old: for at this age of his father was Isaac born. ^21-5

**21:6** And Sara said: God hath made a laughter for me: whosoever shall hear of it will laugh with me. ^21-6

**21:7** And again she said: Who would believe that Abraham should hear that Sara gave suck to a son, whom she bore to him in his old age. ^21-7

**21:8** And the child grew and was weaned: and Abraham made a great feast on the day of his weaning. ^21-8

**21:9** And when Sara had seen the son of Agar the Egyptian playing with Isaac her son, she said to Abraham: ^21-9

**21:10** Cast out this bondwoman, and her son: for the son of the bondwoman shall not be heir with my son Isaac. ^21-10

**21:11** Abraham took this grievously for his son. ^21-11

**21:12** And God said to him: Let it not seem grievous to thee for the boy, and for thy bondwoman: in all that Sara hath said to thee, hearken to her voice: for in Isaac shall thy seed be called. ^21-12

**21:13** But I will make the son also of the bondwoman a great nation, because he is thy seed. ^21-13

**21:14** So Abraham rose up in the morning, and taking bread and a bottle of water, put it upon her shoulder, and delivered the boy, and sent her away. And she departed, and wandered in the wilderness of Bersabee. ^21-14

**21:15** And when the water in the bottle was spent, she cast the boy under one of the trees that were there. ^21-15

**21:16** And she went her way, and sat over against him a great way off as far as a bow can carry, for she said: I will not see the boy die: and sitting over against, she lifted up her voice and wept. ^21-16

**21:17** And God heard the voice of the boy: and an angel of God called to Agar from heaven, saying: What art thou doing, Agar? fear not: for God hath heard the voice of the boy, from the place wherein he is. ^21-17

**21:18** Arise, take up the boy, and hold him by the hand: for I will make him a great nation. ^21-18

**21:19** And God opened her eyes: and she saw a well of water, and went and filled the bottle, and gave the boy to drink. ^21-19

**21:20** And God was with him: and he grew, and dwelt in the wilderness, and became a young man, an archer. ^21-20

**21:21** And he dwelt in the wilderness of Pharan, and his mother took a wife for him out of the land of Egypt. ^21-21

**21:22** At the same time Abimelech, and Phicol the general of his army said to Abraham: God is with thee in all that thou dost. ^21-22

**21:23** Swear therefore by God, that thou wilt not hurt me, nor my posterity, nor my stock: but according to the kindness that I have done to thee, thou shalt do to me, and to the land wherein thou hast lived a stranger. ^21-23

**21:24** And Abraham said: I will swear. ^21-24

**21:25** And he reproved Abimelech for a well of water, which his servants had taken away by force. ^21-25

**21:26** And Abimelech answered: I knew not who did this thing: and thou didst not tell me, and I heard not of it till today. ^21-26

**21:27** And Abraham took sheep and oxen and gave them to Abimelech: and both of them made a league. ^21-27

**21:28** And Abraham set apart seven ewe lambs of the flock. ^21-28

**21:29** And Abimelech said to him: What mean these seven ewe lambs which thou hast set apart? ^21-29

**21:30** But he said: Thou shalt take seven ewe lambs at my hand: that they may be a testimony for me, that I dug this well. ^21-30

**21:31** Therefore that place was called Bersabee: because there both of them did swear. ^21-31

**21:32** And they made a league for the well of oath. ^21-32

**21:33** And Abimelech, and Phicol the general of his army arose and returned to the land of the Palestines. But Abraham planted a grove in Bersabee, and there called upon the name of the Lord God eternal. ^21-33

**21:34** And he was a sojourner in the land of the Palestines many days. ^21-34
