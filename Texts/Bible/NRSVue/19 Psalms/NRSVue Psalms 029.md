## The voice of God in a great storm

*A Psalm of David.*

**29:1** Ascribe to the LORD, O heavenly beings,^[Heb *sons of gods*] ascribe to the LORD glory and strength. ^29-1

**29:2** Ascribe to the LORD the glory of his name; worship the LORD in holy splendor. ^29-2

**29:3** The voice of the LORD is over the waters; the God of glory thunders, the LORD, over mighty waters. ^29-3

**29:4** The voice of the LORD is powerful; the voice of the LORD is full of majesty. ^29-4

**29:5** The voice of the LORD breaks the cedars; the LORD breaks the cedars of Lebanon. ^29-5

**29:6** He makes Lebanon skip like a calf and Sirion like a young wild ox. ^29-6

**29:7** The voice of the LORD flashes forth flames of fire. ^29-7

**29:8** The voice of the LORD shakes the wilderness; the LORD shakes the wilderness of Kadesh. ^29-8

**29:9** The voice of the LORD causes the oaks to whirl^[Or *causes the deer to calve*] and strips the forest bare, and in his temple all say, “Glory!” ^29-9

**29:10** The LORD sits enthroned over the flood; the LORD sits enthroned as king forever. ^29-10

**29:11** May the LORD give strength to his people! May the LORD bless his people with peace! ^29-11
