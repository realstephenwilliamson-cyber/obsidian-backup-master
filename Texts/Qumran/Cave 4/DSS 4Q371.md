### Frag. 1 (+ Frags. 8 + 11; = 4Q372 1)

**1** [and he dispersed them in all the countries, and among all the peoples] he scattered them […] ^f1-1

**2** […] they did not enter [… Isra]el. They exterminated t[hem from the land …] ^f1-2

**3** […] from the place […] they did not allow them to rest [… the nations were given] ^f1-3

**4** [a place] in the valley of the vision [and …] Zion, and [t]he[y] made [… and turned] ^f1-4

**5** [Jerusalem into ruins and the mountain] of my God into a [wooded] heig[ht …] ^f1-5

**6** […] the laws of [God, and Judah as well,] ^f1-6

**7** [together with him, and he st]ood [at a crossroads,] to d[o …] ^f1-7

**8** [to be together with his two brothers. And while] all this happened, Joseph [was thrown into lands] ^f1-8

**9** [which he did not know …] among a foreign people [and they were scattered in the whole world. All] ^f1-9

**10** [their mountains were deserted by them …] and fools […] … [… and building] ^f1-10

**11** [a high place for themselves on a high mountain to arouse the jea]lousy of I[sra]el. And [t]he[y] spo[ke with words of …] ^f1-11

**12** [… the sons of Jacob,] and [t]he[y] terrif[ied (them) with] words from [th]eir [mou]ths, blas[pheming] ^f1-12

**13** [against the tent of Zion and they spoke] false words and all (kinds of) deceitful words […] ^f1-13
