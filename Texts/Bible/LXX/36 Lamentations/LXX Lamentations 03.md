# LXX Lamentations 03

← [[LXX Lamentations 02]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Lamentations 04]] →

*Lamentations · [[LXX]]*

**3:1** ALEPH. I am the man that sees poverty, through the rod of his wrath upon me.  ^3-1

**3:2** He has taken me, and led me away into darkness, and not *into* light.  ^3-2

**3:3** Nay, against me has he turned his hand all the day.  ^3-3

**3:4** He has made old my flesh and my skin; he has broken my bones.  ^3-4

**3:5** BETH. He has built against me, and compassed my head, and brought travail *upon me*.  ^3-5

**3:6** He has set me in dark places, as them that have long been dead.  ^3-6

**3:7** He has builded against me, and I cannot come forth: he has made my brazen *chain* heavy.  ^3-7

**3:8** GIMEL. Yea, *though* I cry and shout, he shuts out my prayer.  ^3-8

**3:9** DALETH. He has built up my ways, he has hedged my paths;  ^3-9

**3:10** he has troubled me, *as* a she-bear lying in wait: he is to me *as* a lion in secret places.  ^3-10

**3:11** He pursued *me* after I departed, and brought me to a stand: he has utterly ruined me.  ^3-11

**3:12** HE. He has bent his bow, and set me as a mark for the arrow.  ^3-12

**3:13** He has caused the arrows of his quiver to enter into my reins.  ^3-13

**3:14** I became a laughing-stock to all my people; and their song all the day.  ^3-14

**3:15** VAU. He has filled me with bitterness, he has drenched me with gall.  ^3-15

**3:16** And he has dashed out my teeth with gravel, he has fed me with ashes.  ^3-16

**3:17** He has also removed my soul from peace: I forgot prosperity.  ^3-17

**3:18** Therefore my success has perished, and my hope from the Lord.  ^3-18

**3:19** ZAIN. I remembered by reason of my poverty, and because of persecution my bitterness and gall shall be remembered;  ^3-19

**3:20** and my soul shall meditate with me.  ^3-20

**3:21** This will I lay up in my heart, therefore will I endure. 21a HETH. *It is* the mercies of the Lord, that he has not failed me, because his compassions are not exhausted. Pity *us*, O Lord, early *every* month: for we are not brought to an end, because his compassions are not exhausted. 21b *They are* new every morning: great is thy faithfulness. 21c The Lord is my portion, says my soul; therefore will I wait for him.  ^3-21

**3:25** TETH. The Lord is good to them that wait for him: the soul which shall seek him  ^3-25

**3:26** *is* good, and shall wait for, and quietly expect salvation of the Lord.  ^3-26

**3:27** TETH. *It is* good for a man when he bears a yoke in his youth.  ^3-27

**3:28** He will sit alone, and be silent, because he has borne *it* upon him.  ^3-28

**3:30** JOD. He will give *his* cheek to him that smites him: he will be filled full with reproaches.  ^3-30

**3:31** For the Lord will not reject for ever.  ^3-31

**3:32** CHAPH. For he that has brought down will pity, and *that* according to the abundance of his mercy.  ^3-32

**3:33** He has not answered *in anger* from his heart, though he has brought low the children of a man.  ^3-33

**3:34** LAMED. To bring down under his feet all the prisoners of the earth,  ^3-34

**3:35** to turn aside the judgment of a man before the face of the Most High,  ^3-35

**3:36** to condemn a man *unjustly* in his judgment, the Lord has not given commandment.  ^3-36

**3:37** Who has thus spoken, and it has come to pass? the Lord has not commanded it.  ^3-37

**3:38** Out of the mouth of the Most High there shall not come forth evil and good.  ^3-38

**3:39** MEM. Why should a living man complain, a man concerning his sin?  ^3-39

**3:40** NUN. Our way has been searched out and examined, and we will turn to the Lord.  ^3-40

**3:41** Let us lift up our hearts with *our* hands to the lofty One in heaven.  ^3-41

**3:42** We have sinned, we have transgressed; and thou hast not pardoned.  ^3-42

**3:43** SAMECH. Thou hast visited *us* in wrath, and driven us away: thou hast slain, thou hast not pitied.  ^3-43

**3:44** Thou hast veiled thyself with a cloud because of prayer, that I might be blind,  ^3-44

**3:45** and be cast off. AIN. Thou hast set us *alone* in the midst of the nations.  ^3-45

**3:46** All our enemies have opened their mouth against us.  ^3-46

**3:47** Fear and wrath are come upon us, suspense and destruction.  ^3-47

**3:48** Mine eye shall pour down torrents of water, for the destruction of the daughter of my people.  ^3-48

**3:49** PHE. Mine eye is drowned *with tears*, and I will not be silent, so that there shall be no rest,  ^3-49

**3:50** until the Lord look down, and behold from heaven.  ^3-50

**3:51** Mine eye shall prey upon my soul, because of all the daughters of the city.  ^3-51

**3:52** TSADE. The fowlers chased me as a sparrow, all mine enemies destroyed my life in a pit without cause,  ^3-52

**3:53** and laid a stone upon me.  ^3-53

**3:54** Water flowed over my head: I said, I am cut off.  ^3-54

**3:55** KOPH. I called upon thy name, O Lord, out of the lowest dungeon.  ^3-55

**3:56** Thou heardest my voice: close not thine ears to my supplication.  ^3-56

**3:57** Thou drewest nigh to my help: in the day wherein I called upon thee thou saidst to me, Fear not.  ^3-57

**3:58** RECHS. O Lord, thou hast pleaded the causes of my soul; thou hast redeemed my life.  ^3-58

**3:59** Thou hast seen, O Lord, my troubles: thou hast judged my cause.  ^3-59

**3:60** Thou hast seen all their vengeance, *thou hast looked* on all their devices against me.  ^3-60

**3:61** CHSEN. Thou hast heard their reproach *and* all their devices against me;  ^3-61

**3:62** the lips of them that rose up against me, and their plots against me all the day;  ^3-62

**3:63** their sitting down and their rising up: look thou upon their eyes.  ^3-63

**3:64** Thou wilt render them a recompence, O Lord, according to the works of their hands.  ^3-64

**3:65** THAU. Thou wilt give them *as* a covering, the grief of my heart.  ^3-65

**3:66** Thou wilt persecute them in anger, and wilt consume them from under the heaven, O Lord.  ^3-66
