## Mūla

स्थानवाय्वादिघर्षोत्था स्फुटतैव च पारुषी ।
तदस्यां नादरूपायां संवित्सविधवृत्तितः ॥ ^mula

sthānavāyvādigharṣotthā sphuṭataiva ca pāruṣī |
tadasyāṃ nādarūpāyāṃ saṃvitsavidhavṛttitaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. II
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 02 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v02 11 - Chapter Three, part 8#^pg383|p. 367]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 02.pdf#page=383>))
>
> The (delightful) sweetness (mādhurya) (of music), which consists of (the harmoniousness of) its undifferentiated unity, is called (its) ‘power’ (Śakti). The fully evident (articulation of words) produced by the impact of the breath on the loci (of articulation) is (by comparison) harsh. (with 3.238cd–239ab)
>
> He who is (immersed) in this (musical) sound, (close to) consciousness and similar to it, experiences in an instant a state of oneness with it (tanmayībhūti), by virtue of the character it shares in common (with consciousness) (sājātya). (with 3.239cd–240ab)
