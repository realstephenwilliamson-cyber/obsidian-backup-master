## Mūla

नह्यस्वच्छमितप्रायैर्जलैस्तृप्यन्ति बर्हिणः ।
उच्यते श्रीमतादिष्टं शंभुनात्र ममोत्तरम् ॥ ^mula

nahyasvacchamitaprāyairjalaistṛpyanti barhiṇaḥ |
ucyate śrīmatādiṣṭaṃ śaṃbhunātra mamottaram || ^iast

> (This reply however) is not good enough to quell the suffering that comes (from the intense) thirst (for the truth aroused) by the question put to us. Peacocks are not satisfied with a little dirty water! (with 9.263cd–264ab)
>
> (Now) will be stated my reply here (to this objection), taught to me by the venerable Śambhunātha, and (powerfully) strengthened by the expansion (of my) noble and pure (translucent) consciousness. (with 9.264cd–265ab)
