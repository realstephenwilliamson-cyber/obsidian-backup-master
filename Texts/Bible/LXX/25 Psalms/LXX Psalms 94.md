# LXX Psalms 94

← [[LXX Psalms 93]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 95]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 095]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 095]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**94:1** The praise of a Song by David. Come, let us exult in the Lord; let us make a joyful noise to God our Saviour.[^1]  ^94-1

**94:2** Let us come before his presence with thanksgiving, and make a joyful noise to him with psalms.  ^94-2

**94:3** For the Lord is a great God, and a great king over all gods: for the Lord will not cast off his people.[^2]  ^94-3

**94:4** For the ends of the earth are in his hands; and the heights of the mountains are his.  ^94-4

**94:5** For the sea is his, and he made it: and his hands formed the dry land.  ^94-5

**94:6** Come, let us worship and fall down before him; and weep before the Lord that made us.  ^94-6

**94:7** For he is our God; and we are the people of his pasture, and the sheep of his hand.  ^94-7

**94:8** To-day, if ye will hear his voice, harden not your hearts, as in the provocation, according to the day of irritation in the wilderness:[^3]  ^94-8

**94:9** where your fathers tempted me, proved me, and saw my works.  ^94-9

**94:10** Forty years was I grieved with this generation, and said, They do always err in their heart, and they have not known my ways.  ^94-10

**94:11** So I sware in my wrath, They shall not enter into my rest.  ^94-11

[^1]: 94:1 *Gr.* unto.
[^2]: 94:3 *Heb.* and *Alex.* omit this clause. See Ps. 93. 14.
[^3]: 94:8 Note, πειρασμοῦ is the reading of several editions.
