# Psalms 54 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 53|← Psalms 53]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 55|Psalms 55 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 54|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**54:1** Unto the end, in verses, understanding for David. ^54-1

**54:2** Hear, O God, my prayer, and despise not my supplication: ^54-2

**54:3** Be attentive to me and hear me. I am grieved in my exercise; and am troubled, ^54-3

**54:4** At the voice of the enemy, and at the tribulation of the sinner. For they have cast iniquities upon me: and in wrath they were troublesome to me. ^54-4

**54:5** My heart is troubled within me: and the fear of death is fallen upon me. ^54-5

**54:6** Fear and trembling are come upon me: and darkness hath covered me. ^54-6

**54:7** And I said: Who will give me wings like a dove, and I will fly and be at rest? ^54-7

**54:8** Lo, I have gone far off flying away; and I abode in the wilderness. ^54-8

**54:9** I waited for him that hath saved me from pusillanimity of spirit, and a storm. ^54-9

**54:10** Cast down, O Lord, and divide their tongues; for I have seen iniquity and contradiction in the city. ^54-10

**54:11** Day and night shall iniquity surround it upon its walls: and in the midst thereof are labour, ^54-11

**54:12** And injustice. And usury and deceit have not departed from its streets. ^54-12

**54:13** For if my enemy had reviled me, I would verily have borne with it. And if he that hated me had spoken great things against me, I would perhaps have hidden myself from him. ^54-13

**54:14** But thou a man of one mind, my guide, and my familiar, ^54-14

**54:15** Who didst take sweetmeats together with me: in the house of God we walked with consent. ^54-15

**54:16** Let death come upon them, and let them go down alive into hell. For there is wickedness in their dwellings: in the midst of them. ^54-16

**54:17** But I have cried to God: and the Lord will save me. ^54-17

**54:18** Evening and morning, and at noon I will speak and declare: and he shall hear my voice. ^54-18

**54:19** He shall redeem my soul in peace from them that draw near to me: for among many they were with me. ^54-19

**54:20** God shall hear, and the Eternal shall humble them. For there is no change with them, and they have not feared God: ^54-20

**54:21** He hath stretched forth his hand to repay. They have defiled his covenant, ^54-21

**54:22** They are divided by the wrath Of his countenance, and his heart hath drawn near. His words are smoother than oil, and the same are darts. ^54-22

**54:23** Cast thy care upon the Lord, and he shall sustain thee: he shall not suffer the just to waver for ever. ^54-23

**54:24** But thou, O God, shalt bring them down into the pit of destruction. Bloody and deceitful men shall not live out half their days; but I will trust in thee, O Lord. ^54-24
