# Eli Franco

Professor emeritus of Indology and former Director of the Institute of Indology and Central Asian Studies at Leipzig University (2004–2021); he leads research projects on Buddhist logic and epistemology and on the Buddhist murals of Kucha ([Austrian Academy of Sciences Press](https://verlag.oeaw.ac.at/en/person/franco-eli/226); [University of Vienna](https://stb.univie.ac.at/en/about-us/team/eli-franco/user/francoe53/inum/1083/backpid/198178)).
