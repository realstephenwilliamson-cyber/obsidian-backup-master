## Mūla

समस्तं देवताचक्रमधिष्ठातृ प्रकल्प्यते ।
अनन्तनाले धर्मादिपत्रे सद्वैद्यकर्णिके ॥ ^mula

samastaṃ devatācakramadhiṣṭhātṛ prakalpyate |
anantanāle dharmādipatre sadvaidyakarṇike || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 07 - Chapter Fifteen, part 6#^pg247|p. 229]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=247>))
>
> Cast a handful of flowers in the middle, and in the same way offer incense, perfumes, (libations of) wine (āsava) and the like in the centre of the abode of the sacrifice (yāgauka). By this (procedure, every) obstacle is quickly removed, and the sustainer of the entire assembly of the deities (devatācakra) (of the site) is conceived (and established). (with 15.375–376ab)
>
> Having done that, one should worship Ganeśa, who has arisen to quell obstacles and is located in the northeastern (quarter), with perfumes, flowers and the like. (He is on a throne) made of six (components), namely, Ananta and a lotus, (of which) the petals (are the four,) Dharma and the rest, and the calix made of true knowledge. Once worshipped, he should be dismissed. (with 15.376cd–377)
