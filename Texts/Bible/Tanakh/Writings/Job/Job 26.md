# Job 26

Compare: [[NRSVue Job 26|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 26

**26:1** Then Job said in reply: ^26-1

**26:2** You would help without having the strength;  
You would deliver with arms that have no power. ^26-2

**26:3** Without having the wisdom, you offer advice  
And freely give your counsel. ^26-3

**26:4** To whom have you addressed words?  
Whose breath issued from you? ^26-4

**26:5** The shades tremble  
Beneath the waters and their denizens. ^26-5

**26:6** Sheol is naked before Him;  
Abaddon has no cover. ^26-6

**26:7** He it is who stretched out Zaphon[^1] over chaos,  
Who suspended earth over emptiness. ^26-7

**26:8** He wrapped up the waters in His clouds;  
Yet no cloud burst under their weight. ^26-8

**26:9** He shuts off the view of His throne,  
Spreading His cloud over it.[^2] ^26-9

**26:10** He drew a boundary on the surface of the waters,  
At the extreme where light and darkness meet. ^26-10

**26:11** The pillars of heaven tremble,  
Astounded at His blast. ^26-11

**26:12** By His power He stilled the sea;  
By His skill He struck down Rahab. ^26-12

**26:13** By His wind the heavens were calmed;  
His hand pierced the Elusive Serpent.[^3] ^26-13

**26:14** These are but glimpses of His rule,  
The mere whisper that we perceive of Him;  
Who can absorb the thunder of His mighty deeds? ^26-14

[^1]: Used for heaven; cf. Isa. 14.13; Ps. 48.3.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Cf. Isa. 27.1.
