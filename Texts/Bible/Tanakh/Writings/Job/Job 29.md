# Job 29

Compare: [[NRSVue Job 29|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 29

**29:1** Job again took up his theme and said: ^29-1

**29:2** O that I were as in months gone by,  
In the days when God watched over me, ^29-2

**29:3** When His lamp shone over my head,  
When I walked in the dark by its light, ^29-3

**29:4** When I was in my prime,  
When God’s company graced my tent, ^29-4

**29:5** When Shaddai was still with me,  
When my lads surrounded me, ^29-5

**29:6** When my feet were bathed in cream,  
And rocks poured out streams of oil for me. ^29-6

**29:7** When I passed through the city gates  
To take my seat in the square, ^29-7

**29:8** Young men saw me and hid,  
Elders rose and stood; ^29-8

**29:9** Nobles held back their words;  
They clapped their hands to their mouths. ^29-9

**29:10** The voices of princes were hushed;  
Their tongues stuck to their palates. ^29-10

**29:11** The ear that heard me acclaimed me;  
The eye that saw, commended me. ^29-11

**29:12** For I saved the poor man who cried out,  
The orphan who had none to help him. ^29-12

**29:13** I received the blessing of the lost;  
I gladdened the heart of the widow. ^29-13

**29:14** I clothed myself in righteousness and it robed me;  
Justice was my cloak and turban. ^29-14

**29:15** I was eyes to the blind  
And feet to the lame. ^29-15

**29:16** I was a father to the needy,  
And I looked into the case of the stranger. ^29-16

**29:17** I broke the jaws of the wrongdoer,  
And I wrested prey from his teeth. ^29-17

**29:18** I thought I would end my days with my family,[^1]  
And be as long-lived as the phoenix,[^2] ^29-18

**29:19** My roots reaching water,  
And dew lying on my branches; ^29-19

**29:20** My vigor refreshed,  
My bow ever new in my hand. ^29-20

**29:21** Men would listen to me expectantly,  
And wait for my counsel. ^29-21

**29:22** After I spoke they had nothing to say;  
My words were as drops [of dew] upon them. ^29-22

**29:23** They waited for me as for rain,  
For the late rain, their mouths open wide. ^29-23

**29:24** When I smiled at them, they would not believe it;  
They never expected[^3] a sign of my favor. ^29-24

**29:25** I decided their course and presided over them;  
I lived like a king among his troops,  
Like one who consoles mourners. ^29-25

[^1]: Lit. “nest.”
[^2]: Others “multiply days like sand.”
[^3]: Taking yappilun as from pll; cf. Gen. 48.11.
