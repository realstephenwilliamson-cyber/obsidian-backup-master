# Sirach 35 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Sirach 34|← Sirach 34]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Sirach 36|Sirach 36 →]]

## The law and sacrifices

**35:1** The one who keeps the law makes many offerings; ^35-1

**35:2** one who heeds the commandments makes an offering of well-being. ^35-2

**35:3** The one who returns a kindness offers choice flour, ^35-3

**35:4** and one who gives alms sacrifices a thank offering. ^35-4

**35:5** To keep from wickedness is pleasing to the Lord, and to forsake unrighteousness is an atonement. ^35-5

**35:6** Do not appear before the Lord empty-handed, ^35-6

**35:7** for all that you offer is in fulfillment of the commandment. ^35-7

**35:8** The offering of the righteous enriches the altar, and its pleasing odor rises before the Most High. ^35-8

**35:9** The sacrifice of the righteous is acceptable, and it will never be forgotten. ^35-9

**35:10** Be generous when you worship the Lord, and do not skimp the first fruits of your hands. ^35-10

**35:11** With every gift show a cheerful face and dedicate your tithe with gladness. ^35-11

**35:12** Give to the Most High as he has given to you and as generously as you can afford. ^35-12

**35:13** For the Lord is the one who repays, and he will repay you sevenfold. ^35-13

## Divine justice

**35:14** Do not offer him a bribe, for he will not accept it, ^35-14

**35:15** and do not rely on a dishonest sacrifice, for the Lord is the judge, and with him there is no partiality. ^35-15

**35:16** He will not show partiality to the poor, but he will listen to the prayer of one who is wronged. ^35-16

**35:17** He will not ignore the supplication of the orphan or the widow when she pours out her complaint. ^35-17

**35:18** Do not the tears of the widow run down her cheek ^35-18

**35:19** as she cries out against the one who causes them to fall? ^35-19

**35:20** Those whose service is pleasing to the Lord will be accepted, and their prayers will reach to the clouds. ^35-20

**35:21** The prayer of the humble pierces the clouds, and it will not rest until it reaches its goal; it will not desist until the Most High responds ^35-21

**35:22** and does justice for the righteous and executes judgment. Indeed, the Lord will not delay, and like a warrior^[Heb: Gk *and with them*] he will not be patient until he crushes the loins of the unmerciful ^35-22

**35:23** and repays vengeance on the nations, until he destroys the multitude of the insolent and breaks the scepters of the unrighteous, ^35-23

**35:24** until he repays mortals according to their deeds and the works of all according to their thoughts, ^35-24

**35:25** until he judges the case of his people and makes them rejoice in his mercy. ^35-25

**35:26** His mercy is as welcome in time of distress as clouds of rain in time of drought. ^35-26
