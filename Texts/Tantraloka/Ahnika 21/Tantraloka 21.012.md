## Mūla

अदीक्षिते नृपत्यादावलसे पतिते मृते ।
बालातुरस्त्रीवृद्धे च मृतोद्धारं प्रकल्पयेत् ॥ ^mula

adīkṣite nṛpatyādāvalase patite mṛte |
bālāturastrīvṛddhe ca mṛtoddhāraṃ prakalpayet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 12 - Chapter Twenty-One#^pg273|p. 253]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=273>))
>
> (The initiation that) rescues the dead (mrtoddhāra) can be performed for one who has not been initiated, a king and the like, one who is lazy (led astray by worldly enjoyment), one who has fallen and died, a child, one who is sick, a woman, and one who is aged. (with 21.12–13)
