## Mūla

भाव्यमाना न किं सूते तत्सन्तानसदृग्वपुः ।
तत्तादृक्तादृशैर्बन्धुपुत्रमित्रादिभिः सह ॥ ^mula

bhāvyamānā na kiṃ sūte tatsantānasadṛgvapuḥ |
tattādṛktādṛśairbandhuputramitrādibhiḥ saha || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 06 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 4#^pg172|p. 155]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=172>))
>
> Although they are separated by their location (in differing bodies) and the rest because, as (the Yogasūtra) declares, the latent impressions are contiguous as they, and the memory (they elicit), are essentially one. Although what is conceived (bhāvita) (at the point of death) is not clearly evident because it is not firmly established in a (direct) experience (of it) as that, why should not the imagination (of this future state) generate (another) body that accords with (sadrk) its own continuum (of rebirths)? (with 28.351cd–353ab)
>
> That kind (of body) manifests in another world, together with (others who are) similar to his relatives, sons, friends and so on, as (happens) in a dream, due to the sequence of latent impressions (of his past Karma). (with 28.353cd–354ab)

*Lead-in:* ननु यदि नाम अस्य भावनामात्रोपनत एव देहान्तरोदयः, तत् किं नाम शोकादिवत् भावयित्रेकगोचर एव असौ स्यात्, उत सर्वजनसंवेद्योऽपि?--इत्याशङ्क्य आह--

> तत्तादृक्तादृशैर्बन्धुपुत्रमित्रादिभिः सह ॥
> भासतेऽपि परे लोके स्वप्नवद्वासनाक्रमात् ।

तत्तादृक् भावनोचितं तद्वपुर्वासनाक्रमात् स्वप्नवत् जन्मान्तरे तादृशैः प्राप्ततद्रूपानुगुणैरेव बन्ध्वादिभिः सहापि भासते सर्वजनसंवेद्यं स्यात्--इत्यर्थः ॥
