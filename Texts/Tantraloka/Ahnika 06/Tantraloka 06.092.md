## Mūla

हेये तु दर्शिते शिष्याः सत्पथैकान्तदर्शिनः ।
व्याख्यातः कृष्णपक्षो यस्तत्र प्राणगतः शशी ॥ ^mula

heye tu darśite śiṣyāḥ satpathaikāntadarśinaḥ |
vyākhyātaḥ kṛṣṇapakṣo yastatra prāṇagataḥ śaśī || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 10 - Chapter Six, part 3#^pg330|p. 315]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=330>))
>
> Let this suffice. Nor is this hostility on my part to the view of those who have come before me. When (the view) that should be rejected has been presented, disciples perceive the true path (by themselves). (with 6.91cd–92ab)
>
> The dark lunar fortnight that has been explained is where the Moon is present in the exhaled breath (prānṇa). Its nature is to nourish, and so it releases (its energies, the) lunar digits, one by one, (each) lunar day (tithi) (into the Sun and feeds it). The fifteenth tuṭi, which is close to the End of the Twelve, is the Dark Moon (amāvasyā). Here, the Sun (of the exhaled breath), exhausted, it enters the Moon (of inhalation). (with 6.92cd–94ab)
