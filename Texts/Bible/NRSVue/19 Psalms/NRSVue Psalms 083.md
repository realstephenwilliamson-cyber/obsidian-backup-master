# Psalm 83 (NRSVue)

## Prayer for judgment on Israel’s foes

*A Song. A Psalm of Asaph.*

**83:1** O God, do not keep silent; do not hold your peace or be still, O God! ^83-1

**83:2** Even now your enemies are in tumult; those who hate you have raised their heads. ^83-2

**83:3** They lay crafty plans against your people; they consult together against those you protect. ^83-3

**83:4** They say, “Come, let us wipe them out as a nation; let the name of Israel be remembered no more.” ^83-4

**83:5** They conspire with one accord; against you they make a covenant— ^83-5

**83:6** the tents of Edom and the Ishmaelites, Moab and the Hagrites, ^83-6

**83:7** Gebal and Ammon and Amalek, Philistia with the inhabitants of Tyre; ^83-7

**83:8** Assyria also has joined them; they are the strong arm of the children of Lot. *Selah* ^83-8

**83:9** Do to them as you did to Midian, as to Sisera and Jabin at the Wadi Kishon, ^83-9

**83:10** who were destroyed at En-dor, who became dung for the ground. ^83-10

**83:11** Make their nobles like Oreb and Zeeb, all their princes like Zebah and Zalmunna, ^83-11

**83:12** who said, “Let us take the pastures of God for our own possession.” ^83-12

**83:13** O my God, make them like whirling dust,^[Or *a tumbleweed*] like chaff before the wind. ^83-13

**83:14** As fire consumes the forest, as the flame sets the mountains ablaze, ^83-14

**83:15** so pursue them with your tempest and terrify them with your hurricane. ^83-15

**83:16** Fill their faces with shame, so that they may seek your name, O LORD. ^83-16

**83:17** Let them be put to shame and dismayed forever; let them perish in disgrace. ^83-17

**83:18** Let them know that you alone, whose name is the LORD, are the Most High over all the earth. ^83-18
