## Mūla

एकं सृष्टिमयं बीजं यद्वीर्यं सर्वमन्त्रगम् ।
एका मुद्रा खेचरी च मुद्रौघः प्राणितो यया ॥ ^mula

ekaṃ sṛṣṭimayaṃ bījaṃ yadvīryaṃ sarvamantragam |
ekā mudrā khecarī ca mudraughaḥ prāṇito yayā || ^iast

> One is the seed-syllable made of emanation, the vitality of which is present in all Mantras. And one is the Seal, which is the Skyfarer, that gives life to the flux of (all) the Seals.
