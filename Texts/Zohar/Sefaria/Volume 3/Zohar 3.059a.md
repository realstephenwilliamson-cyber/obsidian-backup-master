# Zohar 3.059a

← [[Zohar 3.058b]] · [[Zohar (Soncino)]] · [[Zohar 3.059b]] →

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 7.08 - Parashat Aharei Mot#^z3-59a|Parashat Aḥarei Mot, at 3:59a]]

*Volume 3, folio 59a* · Achrei Mot

**Achrei Mot 6:49** And because this incense is bound up with the supernal ointment more closely it is more esteemed by the Almighty Holy One, blessed be He, than all offerings and sacrifices. The verse continues: “Draw me, we will run after thee”. Therefore the Community of Israel says: “I am like incense and thou art like oil, draw me after thee, and we shall run, I and all my hosts.” ^achrei-mot-6-49

**Achrei Mot 6:50** “Let the King bring me to his chambers that we may all be glad and rejoice in thee”.’ ^achrei-mot-6-50

**Achrei Mot 6:51** FOR I WILL APPEAR IN THE CLOUD UPON THE MERCY SEAT. R. Judah said: ‘Happy are the righteous that God seeks to honour them. If a man rides on the horse of an earthly king he is put to death, but God let Elijah ride upon His, as it is written: “And Elijah went up in a whirlwind into heaven” (2 Kings 2, 11); and He also took Moses into the cloud, though it is written here, “in the cloud I shall appear on the mercy seat”. ^achrei-mot-6-51

**Achrei Mot 6:52** We have learnt that this was the place where the Cherubim abode. We have learnt that three times a day a miracle was accomplished with their wings. When the holiness of the King descended upon them they of themselves raised their wings and spread them out so as to cover the mercy seat. Then they folded their wings and stood rejoicing in the divine presence.’ ^achrei-mot-6-52

**Achrei Mot 7:53** R. Abba said: ‘The high priest did not see the divine presence when he entered the sanctuary, and but a cloud came down, and when it lighted on the mercy seat the Cherubim beat their wings together and broke out into song. ^achrei-mot-7-53

**Achrei Mot 7:54** What did they chant? “For great is the Lord and highly to be praised, he is to be feared above all gods” (Ps. 96, 4). When they spread out their wings they said: “For all the gods of the peoples are idols, but the Lord made the heavens” (*Ibid.* 5). And when they covered the mercy seat they said: “Before the Lord for he cometh to judge the earth, he shall judge the world with righteousness and the peoples with equity” (Ps. 98, 9). ^achrei-mot-7-54

**Achrei Mot 7:55** Then the priest used to hear their voice in the sanctuary, and he put the incense in its place with all devotion in order that all might be blessed.’ ^achrei-mot-7-55

**Achrei Mot 7:56** R. Jose said: ‘The word “equity” (*mesharim*, lit. equities) in the above quoted verse indicates that the Cherubim were male and female.’ ^achrei-mot-7-56

**Achrei Mot 8:57** R. Isaac said: ‘From this we learn that where there is no union of male and female men are not worthy to behold the divine presence.’ ^achrei-mot-8-57
