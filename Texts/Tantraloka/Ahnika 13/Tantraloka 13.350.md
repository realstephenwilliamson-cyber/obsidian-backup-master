## Mūla

प्रत्यवायो य आम्नातः स इत्थमिति गृह्यताम् ।
यो यत्र शास्त्रेऽधिकृतः स तत्र गुरुरुच्यते ॥ ^mula

pratyavāyo ya āmnātaḥ sa itthamiti gṛhyatām |
yo yatra śāstre'dhikṛtaḥ sa tatra gururucyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 10 - Chapter Thirteen, part 6#^pg361|p. 339]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=361>))
>
> The (obstructing) sin (pratyavāya) mentioned (in the scripture with the words: ‘(one should not have contact) with a fool who is devoted to (some) other teacher and attends to other scriptures’ is to be understood in the following way. (with 13.349cd–350ab)
>
> A teacher (of a particular scripture) is said to be (only) one who has been authorized (adhikrta) (to teach that particular) scripture. One who has not been authorized there (to teach that scripture) is said (here) to be (some) ‘other teacher’ (of something else). (with 13.350cd–351ab)

तत्रानधिकृत इति यथा वामे सैद्धान्तिकः ॥
