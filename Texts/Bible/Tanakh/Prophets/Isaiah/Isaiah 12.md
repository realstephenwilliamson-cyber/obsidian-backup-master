## Chapter 12

**12:1** In that day, you shall say:  
“I give thanks to You, O LORD!  
Although You were wroth with me,  
Your wrath has turned back and You comfort me, ^12-1

**12:2** Behold the God who gives me triumph!  
I am confident, unafraid;  
For Yah the LORD is my strength and might,[^1]  
And He has been my deliverance.” ^12-2

**12:3** Joyfully shall you draw water  
From the fountains of triumph, ^12-3

**12:4** And you shall say on that day:  
“Praise the LORD, proclaim His name.  
Make His deeds known among the peoples;  
Declare that His name is exalted. ^12-4

**12:5** Hymn the LORD,  
For He has done gloriously;  
Let this be made known  
In all the world! ^12-5

**12:6** Oh, shout for joy,  
You who dwell in Zion!  
For great in your midst  
Is the Holy One of Israel.” ^12-6

[^1]: Others “song.”
