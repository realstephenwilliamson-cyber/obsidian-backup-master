# Matthew 18 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Matthew 17|← Matthew 17]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Matthew 19|Matthew 19 →]]

Compare: [[Matthew 18|BSB and SBLGNT]] · [[DRB Matthew 18|Douay-Rheims]] · [[JANT Matthew 18|JANT notes]]

## True greatness

**18:1** At that time the disciples came to Jesus and asked, “Who is the greatest in the kingdom of heaven?” ^18-1

**18:2** He called a child, whom he put among them, ^18-2

**18:3** and said, “Truly I tell you, unless you change and become like children, you will never enter the kingdom of heaven. ^18-3

**18:4** Whoever becomes humble like this child is the greatest in the kingdom of heaven. ^18-4

**18:5** Whoever welcomes one such child in my name welcomes me. ^18-5

## Temptations to sin

**18:6** “If any of you cause one of these little ones who believe in me to sin,^[Or *stumble*] it would be better for you if a great millstone were fastened around your neck and you were drowned in the depth of the sea. ^18-6

**18:7** Woe to the world because of things that cause sin!^[Or *stumbling blocks*] Such things are bound to come, but woe to the one through whom they come! ^18-7

**18:8** “If your hand or your foot causes you to sin,^[Or *stumble*] cut it off and throw it away; it is better for you to enter life maimed or lame than to have two hands or two feet and to be thrown into the eternal fire. ^18-8

**18:9** And if your eye causes you to sin,^[Or *stumble*] tear it out and throw it away; it is better for you to enter life with one eye than to have two eyes and to be thrown into the hell^[Gk *Gehenna*] of fire. ^18-9

## The parable of the lost sheep

**18:10** “Take care that you do not despise one of these little ones, for I tell you, in heaven their angels continually see the face of my Father in heaven.^[Other ancient authorities add 18.11, *For the Son of Man came to save the lost*] ^18-10

**18:12** What do you think? If a shepherd has a hundred sheep and one of them has gone astray, does he not leave the ninety-nine on the mountains and go in search of the one that went astray? ^18-12

**18:13** And if he finds it, truly I tell you, he rejoices over it more than over the ninety-nine that never went astray. ^18-13

**18:14** So it is not the will of your^[Other ancient authorities read *my*] Father in heaven that one of these little ones should be lost. ^18-14

## Reproving another who sins

**18:15** “If your brother or sister sins against you,^[Other ancient authorities lack *against you*] go and point out the fault when the two of you are alone. If you are listened to, you have regained that one. ^18-15

**18:16** But if you are not listened to, take one or two others along with you, so that every word may be confirmed by the evidence of two or three witnesses. ^18-16

**18:17** If that person refuses to listen to them, tell it to the church, and if the offender refuses to listen even to the church, let such a one be to you as a gentile and a tax collector. ^18-17

**18:18** Truly I tell you, whatever you bind on earth will be bound in heaven, and whatever you loose on earth will be loosed in heaven. ^18-18

**18:19** Again, truly I tell you, if two of you agree on earth about anything you ask, it will be done for you by my Father in heaven. ^18-19

**18:20** For where two or three are gathered in my name, I am there among them.” ^18-20

## Forgiveness

**18:21** Then Peter came and said to him, “Lord, if my brother or sister sins against me, how often should I forgive? As many as seven times?” ^18-21

**18:22** Jesus said to him, “Not seven times, but, I tell you, seventy-seven^[Or *seventy times seven*] times. ^18-22

## The parable of the unforgiving servant

**18:23** “For this reason the kingdom of heaven may be compared to a king who wished to settle accounts with his slaves. ^18-23

**18:24** When he began the reckoning, one who owed him ten thousand talents was brought to him, ^18-24

**18:25** and, as he could not pay, the lord ordered him to be sold, together with his wife and children and all his possessions and payment to be made. ^18-25

**18:26** So the slave fell on his knees before him, saying, ‘Have patience with me, and I will pay you everything.’ ^18-26

**18:27** And out of pity for him, the lord of that slave released him and forgave him the debt. ^18-27

**18:28** But that same slave, as he went out, came upon one of his fellow slaves who owed him a hundred denarii, and seizing him by the throat he said, ‘Pay what you owe.’ ^18-28

**18:29** Then his fellow slave fell down and pleaded with him, ‘Have patience with me, and I will pay you.’ ^18-29

**18:30** But he refused; then he went and threw him into prison until he would pay the debt. ^18-30

**18:31** When his fellow slaves saw what had happened, they were greatly distressed, and they went and reported to their lord all that had taken place. ^18-31

**18:32** Then his lord summoned him and said to him, ‘You wicked slave! I forgave you all that debt because you pleaded with me. ^18-32

**18:33** Should you not have had mercy on your fellow slave, as I had mercy on you?’ ^18-33

**18:34** And in anger his lord handed him over to be tortured until he would pay his entire debt. ^18-34

**18:35** So my heavenly Father will also do to every one of you, if you do not forgive your brother or sister from your heart.” ^18-35
