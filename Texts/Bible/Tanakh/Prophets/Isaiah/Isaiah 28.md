## Chapter 28

**28:1** Ah, the proud crowns of the drunkards of Ephraim,  
Whose glorious beauty is but wilted flowers  
On the heads of men bloated[^1] with rich food,  
Who are overcome by wine! ^28-1

**28:2** Lo, my Lord has something strong and mighty,  
Like a storm of hail,  
A shower of pestilence.  
Something like a storm of massive, torrential rain[^2]  
Shall be hurled with force to the ground. ^28-2

**28:3** Trampled underfoot shall be  
The proud crowns of the drunkards of Ephraim, ^28-3

**28:4** The wilted flowers—  
On the heads of men bloated[^3] with rich food—  
That are his glorious beauty.  
They shall be like an early fig  
Before the fruit harvest;  
Whoever sees it devours it  
While it is still in his hand.[^4] ^28-4

**28:5** In that day, the LORD of Hosts shall become a crown of beauty and a diadem of glory for the remnant of His people, ^28-5

**28:6** and a spirit of judgment for him who sits in judgment and of valor for those who repel attacks at the gate. ^28-6

**28:7** But these are also muddled by wine  
And dazed by liquor:  
Priest and prophet  
Are muddled by liquor;  
They are confused by wine,  
They are dazed by liquor;  
They are muddled in their visions,  
They stumble in judgment. ^28-7

**28:8** Yea, all tables are covered  
With vomit and filth,  
So that no space is left. ^28-8

**28:9** [^5]“To whom would he give instruction?  
To whom expound a message?  
To those newly weaned from milk,  
Just taken away from the breast? ^28-9

**28:10** That same mutter upon mutter,  
Murmur upon murmur,  
Now here, now there!” ^28-10

**28:11** Truly, as one who speaks to that people in a stammering jargon and an alien tongue ^28-11

**28:12** is he who declares to them, “This is the resting place, let the weary rest;[^6] this is the place of re  
pose.” They refuse to listen. ^28-12

**28:13** To them the word of the LORD is:  
“Mutter upon mutter,  
Murmur upon murmur,  
Now here, now there.”  
And so they will march,[^7]  
But they shall fall backward,  
And be injured and snared and captured. ^28-13

**28:14** Hear now the word of the LORD,  
You men of mockery,  
Who govern that people[^8]  
In Jerusalem! ^28-14

**28:15** For you have said,  
“We have made a covenant with Death,  
Concluded a pact with Sheol.  
When the sweeping flood passes through,  
It shall not reach us;  
For we have made falsehood our refuge,  
Taken shelter in treachery.” ^28-15

**28:16** Assuredly,  
Thus said the Lord GOD:  
“Behold, I will found in Zion,  
Stone by stone,  
A tower of precious cornerstones,[^9]  
Exceedingly firm;  
He who trusts need not fear. ^28-16

**28:17** But I will apply judgment as a measuring line  
And retribution[^10] as weights;[^11]  
Hail shall sweep away the refuge of falsehood,  
And flood-waters engulf your shelter. ^28-17

**28:18** Your covenant with Death shall be annulled,  
Your pact with Sheol shall not endure;  
When the sweeping flood passes through,  
You shall be its victims. ^28-18

**28:19** It shall catch you  
Every time it passes through;  
It shall pass through every morning,  
Every day and every night.  
And it shall be sheer horror  
To grasp the message.” ^28-19

**28:20** The couch is too short for stretching out,  
And the cover too narrow for curling up! ^28-20

**28:21** For the LORD will arise  
As on the hill of Perazim,  
He will rouse Himself  
As in the vale of Gibeon,  
To do His work—  
Strange is His work!  
And to perform His task—  
Astounding is His task![^12] ^28-21

**28:22** Therefore, refrain from mockery,  
Lest your bonds be tightened.  
For I have heard a decree of destruction  
From my Lord GOD of Hosts  
Against all the land. ^28-22

**28:23** Give diligent ear to my words,  
Attend carefully to what I say. ^28-23

**28:24** Does he who plows to sow  
Plow all the time,  
Breaking up and furrowing his land? ^28-24

**28:25** When he has smoothed its surface,  
Does he not rather broadcast black cumin  
And scatter cumin,  
Or set wheat in a row,[^13]  
Barley in a strip,  
And emmer in a patch? ^28-25

**28:26** For He teaches him the right manner,  
His God instructs him. ^28-26

**28:27** So, too, black cumin is not threshed with a threshing board,  
Nor is the wheel of a threshing sledge rolled over cumin;  
But black cumin is beaten out with a stick  
And cumin with a rod. ^28-27

**28:28** It is cereal that is crushed.[^14]  
For even if[^15] he threshes it thoroughly,  
And the wheel of his sledge and his horses overwhelm it,[^16]  
He does not crush it. ^28-28

**28:29** That, too, is ordered by the LORD of Hosts;  
His counsel is unfathomable,  
His wisdom marvelous. ^28-29

[^1]: Ge is contracted from ge’e; cf. Ibn Ezra.
[^2]: Lit. “water.”
[^3]: Ge is contracted from ge’e; cf. Ibn Ezra.
[^4]: Emendation yields “on the bough.”
[^5]: This is the drunkards’ reaction to Isaiah’s reproof.
[^6]: I.e., do not embark on any political adventure at this time.
[^7]: I.e., embark on the political adventure.
[^8]: Or “composers of taunt-verses for that people.”
[^9]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^10]: As in 1.27; 5.16; 10.22.
[^11]: I.e., I will make judgment and retribution My plan of action; cf. 34.11; 2 Kings 21.13.
[^12]: Instead of giving victory, as at Baal-perazim and Gibeon (cf. 2 Sam. 5.19–25; 1 Chron. 14.9–16), He will inflict punishment.
[^13]: In some Near Eastern countries, wheat is actually planted rather than scattered.
[^14]: Emendation yields “threshed.”
[^15]: Taking lo as equivalent to lu.
[^16]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
