### 7.1–6: Judging others

*[[NRSVue Matthew 07#^7-1|Text]], p. 24* · ([[Mark 04#^4-24|Mk 4.24–25]]; [[Luke 06#^6-37|Lk 6.37–42]]; see [[Romans 14#^14-10|Rom 14.10]]). ^7-1

**[[NRSVue Matthew 07#^7-1|1–2]]:** See [[Psalms 018#^18-26|Ps 18.25–26]]. *Do not judge*, Rabbinic sources discuss the importance of fair judgment (*m. Avot* 2.4–5; 4.8; *m. Sot.* 1.7; *b. Taʾan.* 8a; *b. Shabb*. 127b, “He who judges his neighbor in the scale of merit is himself judged favorably”). ^7-1-2

**[[NRSVue Matthew 07#^7-3|3]]:** *Speck*: See *b. B. Bat.* 15b similarly discusses taking “a beam from between the eyes.” ^7-3

**[[NRSVue Matthew 07#^7-6|6]]:** *Dogs*, a generic, not ethnic, insult. ^7-6

### 7.7–15: Ethical guides

*[[NRSVue Matthew 07#^7-7|Text]], p. 24* ^7-7

**[[NRSVue Matthew 07#^7-7|7]]:** *Knock, and the door will be opened*, see 21.22; [[John 15#^15-7|Jn 15.7]]; [[John 16#^16-23|16.23–24]]. Vigilant prayer is the pathway to God’s rewards ([[II Kings 01#^1-10|2 Kings 1.10]],[[II Kings 01#^1-12|12–15]]; [[Isaiah 55#^55-6|Isa 55.6]]; [[Psalms 037#^37-4|Ps 37.4]]; [[Proverbs 08#^8-17|Prov 8.17]]; *b. Bek*. 44b; cf. *b. Meg*. 6b). ^7-7-2

**[[NRSVue Matthew 07#^7-9|9]]:** [[Luke 11#^11-11|Lk 11.11]] (cf. *Lev. Rab*. 34.14). ^7-9

**[[NRSVue Matthew 07#^7-11|11]]:** *How much more*, 6.26n. *Father in heaven*, 5.45n. ^7-11

**[[NRSVue Matthew 07#^7-12|12]]:** *Do to others*, see “The Concept of Neighbor,” p. 645; [[Torah 30 - Kedoshim#^19-18|Lev 19.18]]; cf. Tob 4.15; *b. Shabb*. 31a. *Law and the prophets*, see 5.17n.; 22.34–40. Matthew sees the “golden rule” as the guide for interpreting all laws; the rule does not substitute for the rest of Torah. ^7-12

**[[NRSVue Matthew 07#^7-13|13–14]]:** ([[Luke 13#^13-23|Lk 13.23–24]]). The imagery of two paths appears in Jewish sources (see [[Torah 51 - Nitzavim#^30-15|Deut 30.15]]; [[Psalms 001#^1-1|Ps 1.1]]; *2 Bar*. 85.13; *m. Avot* 2.9; *T. Ash*. 1.3,5). ^7-13

**[[NRSVue Matthew 07#^7-15|15]]:** See [[Mark 13#^13-22|Mk 13.22]]. *Beware of false prophets*, imposters claiming to be the true messengers ([[Torah 47 - Re'eh#^13-2|Deut 13.1–5]] \[HB 2–6\]; 18.15–22; [[Jeremiah 05#^5-6|Jer 5.6]],[[Jeremiah 05#^5-31|31]]; [[Jeremiah 06#^6-13|6.13–14]]; [[Jeremiah 08#^8-11|8.11]]; [[Jeremiah 23#^23-2|23.2]]; [[Ezekiel 22#^22-27|Ezek 22.27–28]]; [[Micah 3#^3-5|Mic 3.5–8]]; [[Zechariah 13#^13-2|Zech 13.2]]; [[John 10#^10-12|Jn 10.12]]; [[Acts 20#^20-29|Acts 20.29]]; *Did*. 16.3–4). Josephus, *J.W.* 6.285–88, states that false prophets contributed to the detriment and death of many Jews during the First Revolt (see “Messianic Movements,” p. 622). ^7-15

**[[NRSVue Matthew 07#^7-16|7.16–29]]:** **Ethical warnings** ([[Luke 06#^6-43|Lk 6.43–45]]). *Fruits*, their actions ([[I Samuel 24#^24-14|1 Sam 24.13]]; [[Proverbs 11#^11-30|Prov 11.30]]). The phrase is repeated (vv 16,20) for emphasis (see also 3.10; 7.22; 12.33–37; 24.5,24). ^7-16

**[[NRSVue Matthew 07#^7-19|19]]:** *Every tree*, see [[Torah 48 - Shoftim#^20-20|Deut 20.20]]; [[Luke 13#^13-6|Lk 13.6–9]]; [[James 03#^3-12|Jas 3.12]]; *Gos. Thom*. 45. *Thrown into the fire*, eschatological warning. ^7-19

**[[NRSVue Matthew 07#^7-21|21]]:** See [[Psalms 006#^6-9|Ps 6.8]]. *Will of my Father*, see 6.10n. Jesus demands proper action more than religious confession. ^7-21

**[[NRSVue Matthew 07#^7-22|22]]:** *On that day*, see 19.22,29,36, 38; 26.29; the day of judgment ([[Isaiah 02#^2-11|Isa 2.11]],[[Isaiah 02#^2-17|17]],[[Isaiah 02#^2-20|20]]; [[Hosea 01#^1-5|Hos 1.5]]; [[Joel 1#^1-15|Joel 1.15]]; [[Joel 4#^4-18|3.18]]). See *y. Rosh Ha-Shanah* 1.3; *b. Sanh.* 108a;. *Many will say to me*, Jesus as eschatological judge (cf. 16.27; 19.28; 24.29–31; 25.31–46; 26.64; 28.18; [[Romans 02#^2-16|Rom 2.16]]; [[2 Corinthians 05#^5-10|2 Cor. 5.10]]). *Did we not . . . do many deeds of power*, righteous action rather than miracles grants salvation ([[Jeremiah 14#^14-14|Jer 14.14]]; [[Luke 10#^10-20|Lk 10.20]]; 1 Cor 12–14). ^7-22

**[[NRSVue Matthew 07#^7-23|23]]:** See [[Psalms 006#^6-9|Ps 6.8]]. ^7-23

**[[NRSVue Matthew 07#^7-24|24–27]]:** ([[Luke 06#^6-47|Lk 6.47–49]]). Cf. *m. Avot* 1.17, “Let your home be a meeting place for the wise . . . .” ^7-24

**[[NRSVue Matthew 07#^7-28|28–29]]:** *When Jesus had finished*, common ending to Jesus’ discourses (11.1; 13.53; 19.1; 26.1; cf. [[Torah 21 - Ki Tisa#^34-33|Ex 34.33]]; *2 Bar*. 87.1). *Their scribes*, see 13.52n. Scribes were religious experts (9.3) who typically appealed to precedent whereas Jesus teaches on his own authority. ^7-28
