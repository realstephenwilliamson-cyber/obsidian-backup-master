## Chapter 132

**132:1** A song of ascents.  
O LORD, remember in David’s favor  
his extreme self-denial, ^132-1

**132:2** how he swore to the LORD,  
vowed to the Mighty One of Jacob, ^132-2

**132:3** “I will not enter my house,  
nor will I mount my bed, ^132-3

**132:4** I will not give sleep to my eyes,  
or slumber to my eyelids[^1] ^132-4

**132:5** until I find a place for the LORD,  
an abode for the Mighty One of Jacob.” ^132-5

**132:6** We heard it was in Ephrath;  
we came upon it in the region of Jaar.[^2] ^132-6

**132:7** Let us enter His abode,  
bow at His footstool. ^132-7

**132:8** Advance, O LORD, to Your resting-place,  
You and Your mighty Ark! ^132-8

**132:9** Your priests are clothed in triumph;  
Your loyal ones sing for joy. ^132-9

**132:10** For the sake of Your servant David  
do not reject Your anointed one. ^132-10

**132:11** The LORD swore to David  
a firm oath that He will not renounce,  
“One of your own issue I will set upon your throne. ^132-11

**132:12** If your sons keep My covenant  
and My decrees that I teach them,  
then their sons also,  
to the end of time,  
shall sit upon your throne.” ^132-12

**132:13** For the LORD has chosen Zion;  
He has desired it for His seat. ^132-13

**132:14** “This is my resting-place for all time;  
here I will dwell, for I desire it. ^132-14

**132:15** I will amply bless its store of food,  
give its needy their fill of bread. ^132-15

**132:16** I will clothe its priests in victory,  
its loyal ones shall sing for joy. ^132-16

**132:17** There I will make a horn sprout for David;  
I have prepared a lamp for My anointed one. ^132-17

**132:18** I will clothe his enemies in disgrace,  
while on him his crown shall sparkle.” ^132-18

[^1]: Lit. “eyes.”
[^2]: Cf. 1 Sam. 7.1–2; 1 Chron. 13.5–6.
