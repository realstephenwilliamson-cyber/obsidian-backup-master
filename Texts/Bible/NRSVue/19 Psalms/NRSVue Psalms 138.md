# Psalm 138 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 137|← Psalm 137]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 139|Psalm 139 →]]

## Thanksgiving and praise

*Of David.*

**138:1** I give you thanks, O LORD,^[Q mss Gk: MT lacks *OLORD*] with my whole heart; before the gods I sing your praise; ^138-1

**138:2** I bow down toward your holy temple and give thanks to your name for your steadfast love and your faithfulness, for you have exalted your name and your word above everything.^[Cn: Heb *you have exalted your word above all your name*] ^138-2

**138:3** On the day I called, you answered me; you increased my strength of soul.^[Syr Compare Gk Tg: Heb *you made me arrogant in my soul with strength*] ^138-3

**138:4** All the kings of the earth shall praise you, O LORD, for they have heard the words of your mouth. ^138-4

**138:5** They shall sing of the ways of the LORD, for great is the glory of the LORD. ^138-5

**138:6** For though the LORD is high, he regards the lowly, but the haughty he perceives from far away. ^138-6

**138:7** Though I walk in the midst of trouble, you preserve me against the wrath of my enemies; you stretch out your hand, and your right hand delivers me. ^138-7

**138:8** The LORD will fulfill his purpose for me; your steadfast love, O LORD, endures forever. Do not forsake the work of your hands. ^138-8
