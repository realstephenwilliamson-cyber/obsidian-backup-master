## Chapter 40

**40:1** For the leader. A psalm of David. ^40-1

**40:2** I put my hope in the LORD;  
He inclined toward me,  
and heeded my cry. ^40-2

**40:3** He lifted me out of the miry pit,  
the slimy clay,  
and set my feet on a rock,  
steadied my legs. ^40-3

**40:4** He put a new song into my mouth,  
a hymn to our God.  
May many see it and stand in awe,  
and trust in the LORD. ^40-4

**40:5** Happy is the man who makes the LORD his trust,  
who turns not to the arrogant or to followers of falsehood. ^40-5

**40:6** You, O LORD my God, have done many things;  
the wonders You have devised for us  
cannot be set out before You;[^1]  
I would rehearse the tale of them,  
but they are more than can be told. ^40-6

**40:7** You gave me to understand that[^2]  
You do not desire sacrifice and meal offering;  
You do not ask for burnt offering and sin offering. ^40-7

**40:8** Then I said,  
“See, I will bring a scroll recounting what befell me.”[^3] ^40-8

**40:9** To do what pleases You, my God, is my desire;  
Your teaching is in my inmost parts. ^40-9

**40:10** I proclaimed [Your] righteousness in a great congregation;  
see, I did not withhold my words;  
O LORD, You must know it. ^40-10

**40:11** I did not keep Your beneficence to myself;  
I declared Your faithful deliverance;  
I did not fail to speak of Your steadfast love in a great congregation. ^40-11

**40:12** O LORD, You will not withhold from me Your compassion;  
Your steadfast love will protect me always. ^40-12

**40:13** For misfortunes without number envelop me;  
my iniquities have caught up with me;  
I cannot see;  
they are more than the hairs of my head;  
I am at my wits’ end.[^4] ^40-13

**40:14** [^5]O favor me, LORD, and save me;  
O LORD, hasten to my aid. ^40-14

**40:15** Let those who seek to destroy my life  
be frustrated and disgraced;  
let those who wish me harm  
fall back in shame. ^40-15

**40:16** Let those who say “Aha! Aha!” over me  
be desolate because of their frustration. ^40-16

**40:17** But let all who seek You be glad and rejoice in You;  
let those who are eager for Your deliverance always say,  
“Extolled be the LORD!” ^40-17

**40:18** But I am poor and needy;  
may the Lord devise [deliverance] for me.  
You are my help and my rescuer;  
my God, do not delay. ^40-18

[^1]: Or “You, O Lord my God, have done many things—/ the wonders You have devised for us; / none can equal You.”
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^4]: Or “my courage fails me.”
[^5]: With vv. 14–18, cf. Ps. 70.
