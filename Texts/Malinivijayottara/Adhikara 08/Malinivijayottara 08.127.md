## Mūla

शुभं प्रकाशयेत्तेषामशुभे होममाचरेत् ।
ततः पुष्पफलादीनां सुवेशाभरणाः स्त्रियः ॥ ^mula

śubhaṁ prakāśayet teṣām aśubhe homam ācaret |
tataḥ puṣpaphalādīnāṁ suveśābharaṇāḥ striyaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2025 – April 2025
> p. 35 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=49>))
>
> **The Examination of the Disciple's Dreams**
>
> Having rinsed (his mouth), performed the deposition, and worshipped Śaṅkara... in the abode, pure.[^143]
>
> Having performed deposition on the disciples and, especially, himself, and performed the regular (daily) rites and the rest at dawn, (the teacher) should examine (each disciple's) dream. If it is auspicious, he reveals (the meaning) to them, whereas if it is inauspicious, he should perform a fire sacrifice (*homa*). (with 8.125–127ab)

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2025 – April 2025
> p. 36 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=50>))
>
> Auspicious dreams such those of flowers, fruits and the like, women with beautiful clothes and ornaments, escape from a calamity, ascent[^144] into auspicious lands, the drinking of liquor, decapitation, the eating of raw meat, the direct vision of deities, the smearing with faces – having seen such an auspicious (dream), he obtains the accomplishment (*siddhi*) he desires. (with 8.127cd–129)

[^143]: Most of line 8/125cd is missing.
[^144]: Read with TĀ 15/48cd *adhirohaṇam* for *avarohaṇam.*
