## Chapter 9

**9:1** All Israel was registered by genealogies; and these are in the book of the kings of Israel. And Judah was taken into exile in Babylon because of their trespass. ^9-1

**9:2** [^1]The first to settle in their towns, on their property, were Israelites, priests, Levites, and temple servants, ^9-2

**9:3** while some of the Judahites and some of the Benjaminites and some of the Ephraimites and Manassehites settled in Jerusalem; ^9-3

**9:4** Uthai son of Ammihud son of Omri son of Imri son of Bani, from the sons of Perez son of Judah; ^9-4

**9:5** and of the Shilonites: Asaiah the first-born and his sons. ^9-5

**9:6** Of the sons of Zerah: Jeuel and their kinsmen—690. ^9-6

**9:7** Of the Benjaminites: Sallu son of Meshullam son of Hodaviah son of Hassenuah, ^9-7

**9:8** Ibneiah son of Jeroham, Elah son of Uzzi son of Michri, and Meshullam son of Shephatiah son of Reuel son of Ibneiah; ^9-8

**9:9** and their kinsmen, according to their lines—956. All these were chiefs of their ancestral clans. ^9-9

**9:10** Of the priests: Jedaiah, Jehoiarib, Jachin, ^9-10

**9:11** and Azariah son of Hilkiah son of Meshullam son of Zadok son of Meraioth son of Ahitub, chief officer of the House of God; ^9-11

**9:12** and Adaiah son of Jeroham son of Pashhur son of Malchijah, and Maasai son of Adiel son of Jahzerah son of Meshullam son of Meshillemith son of Immer, ^9-12

**9:13** together with their kinsmen, chiefs of their clans—1,760, men of substance for the work of the service of the House of God. ^9-13

**9:14** Of the Levites: Shemaiah son of Hasshub son of Azrikam son of Hashabiah, of the sons of Merari; ^9-14

**9:15** and Bakbakkar, Heresh, Galal, and Mattaniah son of Mica son of Zichri son of Asaph; ^9-15

**9:16** and Obadiah son of Shemaiah son of Galal son of Jeduthun, and Berechiah son of Asa son of Elkanah, who dwelt in the villages of the Netophathites. ^9-16

**9:17** The gatekeepers were: Shallum, Akkub, Talmon, Ahiman; and their kinsman Shallum was the chief ^9-17

**9:18** hitherto in the King’s Gate on the east. They were the keepers belonging to the Levite camp. ^9-18

**9:19** Shallum son of Kore son of Ebiasaph son of Korah, and his kinsmen of his clan, the Korahites, were in charge of the work of the service, guards of the threshold of the Tent; their fathers had been guards of the entrance to the camp of the LORD. ^9-19

**9:20** And Phinehas son of Eleazar was the chief officer over them in time past; the LORD was with him. ^9-20

**9:21** Zechariah the son of Meshelemiah was gatekeeper at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting. ^9-21

**9:22** All these, who were selected as gatekeepers at the thresholds, were 212. They were selected by genealogies in their villages. David and Samuel the seer established them in their office of trust. ^9-22

**9:23** They and their descendants were in charge of the gates of the House of the LORD, that is, the House of the Tent, as guards. ^9-23

**9:24** The gatekeepers were on the four sides, east, west, north, and south; ^9-24

**9:25** and their kinsmen in their villages were obliged to join them every seven days, according to a fixed schedule. ^9-25

**9:26** The four chief gatekeepers, who were Levites, were entrusted to be over the chambers and the treasuries of the House of God. ^9-26

**9:27** They spent the night near the House of God; for they had to do guard duty, and they were in charge of opening it every morning. ^9-27

**9:28** Some of them had charge of the service vessels, for they were counted when they were brought back and taken out. ^9-28

**9:29** Some of them were in charge of the vessels and all the holy vessels, and of the flour, wine, oil, incense, and spices. ^9-29

**9:30** Some of the priests blended the compound of spices. ^9-30

**9:31** Mattithiah, one of the Levites, the first-born of Shallum the Korahite, was entrusted with making the flat cakes. ^9-31

**9:32** Also some of their Kohathite kinsmen had charge of the rows of bread, to prepare them for each sabbath. ^9-32

**9:33** Now these are the singers, the chiefs of Levitical clans who remained in the chambers free of other service, for they were on duty day and night. ^9-33

**9:34** These were chiefs of Levitical clans, according to their lines; these chiefs lived in Jerusalem. ^9-34

**9:35** The father of Gibeon, Jeiel, lived in Gibeon, and the name of his wife was Maacah. ^9-35

**9:36** His first-born son, Abdon; then Zur, Kish, Baal, Ner, Nadab, ^9-36

**9:37** Gedor, Ahio, Zechariah, and Mikloth; ^9-37

**9:38** Mikloth begot Shimeam; and they lived in Jerusalem opposite their kinsmen, with their kinsmen. ^9-38

**9:39** Ner begot Kish, Kish begot Saul, Saul begot Jonathan, Malchi-shua, Abinadab, and Eshbaal; ^9-39

**9:40** and the son of Jonathan was Merib-baal; and Merib-baal begot Micah. ^9-40

**9:41** The sons of Micah: Pithon, Melech, Taharea; ^9-41

**9:42** Ahaz begot Jarah, and Jarah begot Alemeth, Azmaveth, and Zimri; Zimri begot Moza. ^9-42

**9:43** Moza begot Binea; his son was Rephaiah, his son Eleasah, his son Azel. ^9-43

**9:44** Azel had six sons and these were their names: Azrikam, Bocheru, Ishmael, Sheariah, Obadiah, and Hanan. These were the sons of Azel. ^9-44

[^1]: With vv. 2–17, cf. Neh. 11.3–19.
