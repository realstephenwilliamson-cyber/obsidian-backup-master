## Mūla

मेलकेऽन्योन्यसङ्घट्टप्रतिबिम्बाद्विकस्वरा ।
उच्छलन्निजरश्म्योघः संवित्सु प्रतिबिम्बितः ॥ ^mula

melake'nyonyasaṅghaṭṭapratibimbādvikasvarā |
ucchalannijaraśmyoghaḥ saṃvitsu pratibimbitaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 07 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 5#^pg185|p. 168]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=185>))
>
> The consciousness, which is all things, that contracts due to the differences (bheda) between the bodies (in which it operates, returns to its) state of (ongoing) expansion (vikasvarā) during a gathering (of Siddhas and Yoginīs) (melaka), because of the (mutual) reflection (of their consciousness, that takes place) by (their) union with one another, (with 28.373cd–374ab)
>
> The flux of the rays of their own (senses), pouring forth, reflected in the (individual) consciousness (of each of those present,) as if within many mirrors, (thus) intensified (dīpta), spontaneously becomes them all (sarvāyeta). (with 28.374cd–375ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* ननु कथमेतावतैव अस्यां विकस्वरत्वं स्यात् ?--इत्याशङ्क्य आह--

> उच्छलन्निजरश्म्योघः संवित्सु प्रतिबिम्बितः ॥
> बहुदर्पणवद्दीप्तः सर्वायेताप्ययत्नतः ।

यस्य कस्यचन बहिः प्रसरन्निन्द्रियमरीचिपुञ्जः तास्वेव अनेकदर्पणप्रख्यासु योगिन्यादिसम्बन्धिनीषु संवित्सु प्रतिबिम्बितत्वात् दीप्तः सर्वतो विकासमासादयन् यत्नं विनापि सर्वायेत सर्वाकारतां यायात्--इत्यर्थः ॥
