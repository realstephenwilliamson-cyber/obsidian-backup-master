## Mūla

नित्यादिनिश्चयद्वारामविकल्पां स्थितिं श्रयेत् ।
ये तु तीव्रतमोद्रिक्तशक्तिनिर्मलताजुषः ॥ ^mula

nityādiniścayadvārāmavikalpāṃ sthitiṃ śrayet |
ye tu tīvratamodriktaśaktinirmalatājuṣaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 05 - Chapter Fifteen, part 4#^pg181|p. 163]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=181>))
>
> Thus, one should abide in a state (of awareness) free of thought constructs (vikalpa), the means to which is the conviction of (one’s own) eternal nature and the like, that destroys that (previous opposite) conviction, which is a part of transmigratory existence (saṁsāra). (with 15.274cd–275ab)
>
> It has (already) been said that those who possesses the purity which results from the most intense descent of the power (of grace) do not undergo the rites of initiation, (repeat) Mantras, or (perform) the depositions. (with 15.275cd–276ab)

## Viveka

> ये तु तीव्रतमोद्रिक्तशक्तिनिर्मलताजुषः ॥
> न ते दीक्षामनुन्यासकारिणश्चेति वर्णितम् ।

वर्णितमिति--द्वितीयाह्निके ॥
