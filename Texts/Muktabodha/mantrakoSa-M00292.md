# mantrakoṣa

**Catalog number:** M00292  
**Author:** āśāditya tripāṭhi  
**Manuscript:** NGMCP 5-470  reel number:  A 169/8  
**Published:** Muktabodha Indological Research Institute, India

## Description

The Mantrakoṣa - A Treasure Chest of Mantra - is as its name suggests a collection of Mantras and matters dealing with their recitation in the context of ritual. This late work  was written by a certain āśāditya tripāṭhi. He draws his material from several source like the Prapaṃcasāratantra which he sometimes names. The text comprises sixteen chapters. He announces at the beginning of each chapter (pariccheda) the subject he is going to expound. This is listed first followed by the names of the chapters. They are as follows:

 1)     pūjāphalakathanam ||

 2) atha pūjādravyaparibhāṣā || pūjāphalakathanam

 3)     atha pūjopacārāḥ ||  puṣpādhyāyaḥ

 4)     atha dhūpāḥ || pūjādravyakathanam

 5)     atha dīkṣā || dīkṣāsaṃkṣepakathanam

 6)     atha guruśiṣyayogyatāparīkṣā || maṃtramelanam

 7)     atha bhūtaśuddhiḥ || bhūtaśuddhikathanam

 8)     atha mātṛkānyāsaḥ || mātṛkānyāsaḥ

 9)     atha sārasvatamaṃtrāḥ || sārasvatamaṃtrakathanam

 10) atha śrīmaṃtrāḥ || ādityamaṃtrakathanam

 11) atha gaṇapati maṃtrāḥ || gaṇapatimaṃtrakathana.

 12) atha śaivamaṃtrāḥ || nṛsiṃhamaṃtrakathanam

 13) paṃcāyatanamaṃtrakathanānaṃtaraṃ nānāmaṃtrā ucyante ||

 tatrādau gāyatrīvidhānaṃ likhyate ||  nānāmaṃtrakathanam

 14) athāgneyāstraṃ likhāmaḥ | astrādimaṃtrakathanam

 15) atha yaṃtre vaktavye pūrvaṃ homīyadravyaparibhāṣā likhyate || yantrakathanam

 16) atha devapūjādau upayuktā mudrā likhyante |
