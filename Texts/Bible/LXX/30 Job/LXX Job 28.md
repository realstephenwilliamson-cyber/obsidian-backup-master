# LXX Job 28

← [[LXX Job 27]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Job 29]] →

*Job · [[LXX]]*

**28:1** For there is a place for the silver, whence it comes, and a place for the gold, whence it is refined.  ^28-1

**28:2** For iron comes out of the earth, and brass is hewn out like stone.  ^28-2

**28:3** He has set a bound to darkness, and he searches out every limit: a stone *is* darkness, and the shadow of death.  ^28-3

**28:4** There is a cutting off of the torrent by reason of dust: so they that forget the right way are weakened; they are removed from *among* men.  ^28-4

**28:5** *As for* the earth, out of it shall come bread: under it has been turned up as it were fire.  ^28-5

**28:6** Her stones are the place of the sapphire: and *her* dust *supplies* man with gold.  ^28-6

**28:7** *There is* a path, the fowl has not known it, neither has the eye of the vulture seen it:  ^28-7

**28:8** neither have the sons of the proud trodden it, a lion has not passed upon it.  ^28-8

**28:9** He has stretched forth his hand on the sharp *rock*, and turned up mountains by the roots:  ^28-9

**28:10** and he has interrupted the whirlpools of rivers, and mine eye has seen every precious thing.  ^28-10

**28:11** And he has laid bare the depths of rivers, and has brought his power to light.  ^28-11

**28:12** But whence has wisdom been discovered? and what is the place of knowledge?  ^28-12

**28:13** A mortal has not known its way, neither indeed has it been discovered among men.  ^28-13

**28:14** The depth said, It is not in me: and the sea said, It is not with me.  ^28-14

**28:15** One shall not give fine gold instead of it, neither shall silver be weighed in exchange for it.  ^28-15

**28:16** Neither shall it be compared with gold of Sophir, with the precious onyx and sapphire.  ^28-16

**28:17** Gold and crystal shall not be equalled to it, neither shall vessels of gold be its exchange.  ^28-17

**28:18** Coral and fine pearl shall not be mentioned: but do thou esteem wisdom above the most precious things.  ^28-18

**28:19** The topaz of Ethiopia shall not be equalled to it; it shall not be compared with pure gold.  ^28-19

**28:20** Whence then is wisdom found? and of what kind is the place of understanding?  ^28-20

**28:21** It has escaped the notice of every man, and has been hidden from the birds of the sky.  ^28-21

**28:22** Destruction and Death said, We have heard the report of it.  ^28-22

**28:23** God has well ordered the way of it, and he knows the place of it.  ^28-23

**28:24** For he surveys the whole *earth* under heaven, knowing the things in the earth:  ^28-24

**28:25** all that he has made; the weight of the winds, the measures of the water.  ^28-25

**28:26** When he made *them*, thus he saw and numbered them, and made a way for the pealing of the thunder.  ^28-26

**28:27** Then he saw it, and declared it: he prepared it *and* traced it out.  ^28-27

**28:28** And he said to man, Behold, godliness is wisdom; and to abstain from evil is understanding.  ^28-28
