# Isaiah 47

## Chapter 47

**47:1** Get down, sit in the dust,  
Fair Maiden Babylon;  
Sit, dethroned, on the ground,  
O Fair Chaldea;  
Nevermore shall they call you  
The tender and dainty one. ^47-1

**47:2** Grasp the handmill and grind meal.  
Remove your veil,  
Strip off your train, bare your leg,  
Wade through the rivers. ^47-2

**47:3** Your nakedness shall be uncovered,  
And your shame shall be exposed.  
I will take vengeance,  
[^1]And let no man intercede. ^47-3

**47:4** Our Redeemer—LORD of Hosts is His name—  
Is the Holy One of Israel. ^47-4

**47:5** Sit silent; retire into darkness,  
O Fair Chaldea;  
Nevermore shall they call you  
Mistress of Kingdoms. ^47-5

**47:6** I was angry at My people,  
I defiled My heritage;  
I put them into your hands,  
But you showed them no mercy.  
Even upon the aged you made  
Your yoke exceedingly heavy. ^47-6

**47:7** You thought, “I shall always be  
The mistress still.”  
You did not take these things to heart,  
You gave no thought to the end of it. ^47-7

**47:8** And now hear this, O pampered one—  
Who dwell in security,  
Who think to yourself,  
“I am, and there is none but me;  
I shall not become a widow  
Or know loss of children”— ^47-8

**47:9** These two things shall come upon you,  
Suddenly, in one day:  
Loss of children and widowhood  
Shall come upon you in full measure,  
Despite your many enchantments  
And all your countless spells. ^47-9

**47:10** You were secure in your wickedness;  
You thought, “No one can see me.”  
It was your skill and your science  
That led you astray.  
And you thought to yourself,  
“I am, and there is none but me.” ^47-10

**47:11** Evil is coming upon you  
Which you will not know how to charm away;[^2]  
Disaster is falling upon you  
Which you will not be able to appease;  
Coming upon you suddenly  
Is ruin of which you know nothing. ^47-11

**47:12** Stand up, with your spells and your many enchantments  
On which you labored since youth!  
Perhaps you’ll be able to profit,  
Perhaps you will find strength.[^3] ^47-12

**47:13** You are helpless, despite all your art.  
Let them stand up and help you now,  
The scanners[^4] of heaven, the star-gazers,  
Who announce, month by month,  
Whatever will come upon you. ^47-13

**47:14** See, they are become like straw,  
Fire consumes them;  
They cannot save themselves  
From the power of the flame;  
This is no coal for warming oneself,  
No fire to sit by! ^47-14

**47:15** This is what they have profited you—  
The traders you dealt with since youth—  
Each has wandered off his own way,  
There is none to save you. ^47-15

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain; emendation yields “And not be appeased, / Says our Redeemer, whose name is Lord of Hosts, / The Holy One of Israel.”
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain. Emendation yields “bribe.”
[^3]: Taking ‘araṣ as a variant of ‘aṣar; cf. 2 Chron. 20.37.
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
