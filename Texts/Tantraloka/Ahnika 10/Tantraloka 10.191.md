## Mūla

तन्निर्विकल्पं प्रोद्गच्छद्विकल्पाच्छादनात्मकम् ।
तदेव शिवरूपं हि परशक्त्यात्मकं विदुः ॥ ^mula

tannirvikalpaṃ prodgacchadvikalpācchādanātmakam |
tadeva śivarūpaṃ hi paraśaktyātmakaṃ viduḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 12 - Chapter Ten, part 3#^pg431|p. 412]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=431>))
>
> The six moments from the third (to the eighth) are non-discursive (nirvikalpa), and consist of the (progressive) falling away of the obscuration of discursive ideation (vikalpa). (with 10.190cd–191ab)
>
> (The wise) know these (six moments) to be Śiva’s nature, which is the Supreme Power. (with 10.191cd)
