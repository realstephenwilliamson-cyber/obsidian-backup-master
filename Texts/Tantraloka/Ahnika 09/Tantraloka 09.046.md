## Mūla

नियतिर्नास्ति वैरिञ्चे कलोर्ध्वे नियतिः श्रुता ।
पुंरागवित्त्रयादूर्ध्वं कलानियतिसंपुटम् ॥ ^mula

niyatirnāsti vairiñce kalordhve niyatiḥ śrutā |
puṃrāgavittrayādūrdhvaṃ kalāniyatisaṃpuṭam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 04 - Chapter Nine, part 2#^pg68|p. 51]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=68>))
>
> According to the Jñānottara, the principles of the Individual Self, Attachment, (limited capacity for) Knowledge, the Force (of limited agency), Time and Māyā (follow each other) in this order. There is no (principle of) Necessity, (whereas) it is said in the Vairiñca (i.e. the Svāyambhuva) that Necessity (follows) after Time. (with 9.45cd–46ab)
>
> 103A bove the triad of the soul, Attachment and (limited capacity for) Knowledge comes the couple (saṁputa) of Time and Necessity, (followed by) the Force (of limited agency) and Māyā. The sequential order according to the Kiranaśāstra has thus been stated. (with 9.46cd–47ab)
