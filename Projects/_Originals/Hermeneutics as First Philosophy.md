> # MEANING BECOMING
> 
> ### ​_For Hermeneutics as First Philosophy_
> 
> ​Motivated by the horrors of the first half of the twentieth century, Emmanuel Levinas famously calls for "Ethics as First Philosophy." The poly-crisis of the early twenty-first century, though a much more stratified horror, also demands a philosophical shift upon which the fate of humanity may rest. The issue now is not merely how we treat the Other standing before us, but how we read the very signals of reality—from the ecological limits of our planet to the conditioned reflexes of our own minds. Ethics alone can no longer be the foundation. We must turn to "Hermeneutics as First Philosophy."
> 
> ​Levinas wrote for a world that had just witnessed the too-often deadly consequences of refusing the Face of the Other in proximity, whereas our world is also one of radically mediated exploitation. On one stratum, our position as apex consumers—and yet isolated nodes—in a globalized economy renders the true upstream and downstream costs of our very existence invisible, as capitalism hides the suffering of distant Others and the planet behind a curtain of commodities. On another stratum, algorithmically manipulated political milieus  hijack our conditioned reflexes, training populations to misread the causes of their own suffering and, at best, willingly participate in their own exploitation—or, at worst, in the destruction of others.
> 
> ​It is becoming increasingly clear that our survival depends on decoding the hidden costs of our systems, dogmas, and institutions. But if we are to liberate our minds from imposed interpretations, then we must ask anew—and in a far more radical register than the twentieth century ever imagined: "What is hermeneutics?"
> 
> ​To ask this question in a radical register is to move hermeneutics out of the library and seminary and recognize it as the rigorous inquiry into meaning as such—not as some airy abstraction, but as the fundamental architecture of reality at scale. From the cosmos itself unfolding from an impossibly organized proto-state of potentials waiting to be actualized by interaction, to the citizen casting a vote or pulling a trigger as a human self motivated by trust or fear—wherever potential crystallizes through relation, meaning is at work.
