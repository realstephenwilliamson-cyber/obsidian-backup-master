# LXX Leviticus 02

← [[LXX Leviticus 01]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Leviticus 03]] →

*Leviticus · [[LXX]]*

**2:1** And if a soul bring a gift, a sacrifice to the Lord, his gift shall be fine flour; and he shall pour oil upon it, and shall put frankincense on it: it is a sacrifice.  ^2-1

**2:2** And he shall bring it to the priests the sons of Aaron: and having taken from it a handful of the fine flour with the oil, and all its frankincense, then the priest shall put the memorial of it on the altar: *it is* a sacrifice, an odour of sweet savour to the Lord.  ^2-2

**2:3** And the remainder of the sacrifice shall be for Aaron and his sons, a most holy portion from the sacrifices of the Lord.  ^2-3

**2:4** And if he bring as a gift a sacrifice baked from the oven, a gift to the Lord of fine flour, *he shall bring* unleavened bread kneaded with oil, and unleavened cakes anointed with oil.  ^2-4

**2:5** And if thy gift *be* a sacrifice from a pan, it is fine flour mingled with oil, unleavened *offerings*.  ^2-5

**2:6** And thou shalt break them into fragments and pour oil upon them: it is a sacrifice to the Lord.  ^2-6

**2:7** And if thy gift be a sacrifice from the hearth, it shall be made of fine flour with oil.  ^2-7

**2:8** And he shall offer the sacrifice which he shall make of these to the Lord, and shall bring it to the priest.  ^2-8

**2:9** And the priest shall approach the altar, and shall take away from the sacrifice a memorial of it, and the priest shall place it on the altar: a burnt offering, a smell of sweet savour to the Lord.  ^2-9

**2:10** And that which is left of the sacrifice *shall be* for Aaron and his sons, most holy from the burnt-offerings of the Lord.  ^2-10

**2:11** Ye shall not leaven any sacrifice which ye shall bring to the Lord; for *as to* any leaven, or any honey, ye shall not bring of it to offer a gift to the Lord.  ^2-11

**2:12** Ye shall bring them in the way of fruits to the Lord, but they shall not be offered on the altar for a sweet-smelling savour to the Lord.  ^2-12

**2:13** And every gift of your sacrifice shall be seasoned with salt; omit not the salt of the covenant of the Lord from your sacrifices: on every gift of yours ye shall offer salt to the Lord your God.  ^2-13

**2:14** And if thou wouldest offer a sacrifice of first-fruits to the Lord, *it shall be* new grains ground *and* roasted for the Lord; so shalt thou bring the sacrifice of the first-fruits.  ^2-14

**2:15** And thou shalt pour oil upon it, and shalt put frankincense on it: it is a sacrifice.  ^2-15

**2:16** And the priest shall offer the memorial of it *taken* from the grains with the oil, and all its frankincense: it is a burnt-offering to the Lord.  ^2-16
