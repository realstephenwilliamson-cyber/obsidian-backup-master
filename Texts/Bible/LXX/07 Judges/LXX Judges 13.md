# LXX Judges 13

← [[LXX Judges 12]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Judges 14]] →

*Judges · [[LXX]]*

**13:1** And the children of Israel yet again committed iniquity before the Lord; and the Lord delivered them into the hand of the Phylistines forty years.  ^13-1

**13:2** And there was a man of Saraa, of the family of the kindred of Dan, and his name was Manoë, and his wife was barren, and bore not.  ^13-2

**13:3** And an angel of the Lord appeared to the woman, and said to her, Behold, thou art barren and hast not born; yet thou shalt conceive a son.  ^13-3

**13:4** And now be very cautious, and drink no wine nor strong drink, and eat no unclean thing;  ^13-4

**13:5** for behold, thou art with child, and shalt bring forth a son; and there shall come no razor upon his head, for the child shall be a Nazarite to God from the womb; and he shall begin to save Israel from the hand of the Phylistines.[^1][^2]  ^13-5

**13:6** And the woman went in, and spoke to her husband, saying, A man of God came to me, and his appearance *was as* of an angel of God, very dreadful; and I did not ask him whence he was, and he did not tell me his name.[^3]  ^13-6

**13:7** And he said to me, Behold, thou art with child, and shalt bring forth a son; and now drink no wine nor strong drink, and eat no unclean thing; for the child shall be holy to God from the womb until the day of his death.  ^13-7

**13:8** And Manoë prayed to the Lord and said, I pray thee, O Lord my Lord, *concerning* the man of God whom thou sentest; let him now come to us once more, and teach us what we shall do to the child about to be born.[^4]  ^13-8

**13:9** And the Lord heard the voice of Manoë, and the angel of God came yet again to the woman; and she sat in the field, and Manoë her husband was not with her.  ^13-9

**13:10** And the woman hasted, and ran, and brought word to her husband, and said to him, Behold the man who came in *the other* day to me has appeared to me.  ^13-10

**13:11** And Manoë arose and followed his wife, and came to the man, and said to him, Art thou the man that spoke to the woman? and the angel said, I *am*.  ^13-11

**13:12** And Manoë said, Now shall *thy* word come to pass: what shall be the ordering of the child, and our dealings with him?[^5]  ^13-12

**13:13** And the angel of the Lord said to Manoë, Of all things concerning which I spoke to the woman, she shall beware.  ^13-13

**13:14** She shall eat of nothing that comes of the vine yielding wine, and let her not drink wine or strong liquor, and let her not eat anything unclean: all things that I have charged her she shall observe.[^6]  ^13-14

**13:15** And Manoë said to the angel of the Lord, Let us detain thee here, and prepare before thee a kid of the goats.  ^13-15

**13:16** And the angel of the Lord said to Manoë, If thou shouldest detain me, I will not eat of thy bread; and if thou wouldest offer a whole-burnt-offering, to the Lord thou shalt offer it: for Manoë knew not that he *was* an angel of the Lord.  ^13-16

**13:17** And Manoë said to the angel of the Lord, What *is* thy name, that *when* thy word shall come to pass, we may glorify thee?  ^13-17

**13:18** And the angel of the Lord said to him, Why dost thou thus ask after my name; whereas it is wonderful?[^7]  ^13-18

**13:19** And Manoë took a kid of the goats and its meat-offering, and offered it on the rock to the Lord; and *the angel* wrought a distinct work, and Manoë and his wife were looking on.[^8]  ^13-19

**13:20** And it came to pass when the flame went up above the altar toward heaven, that the angel of the Lord went up in the flame; and Manoë and his wife were looking, and they fell upon their face to the earth.  ^13-20

**13:21** And the angel appeared no more to Manoë and to his wife: then Manoë knew that this *was* an angel of the Lord.  ^13-21

**13:22** And Manoë said to his wife, We shall surely die, because we have seen God.  ^13-22

**13:23** But his wife said to him, If the Lord were pleased to slay us, he would not have received of our hand a whole-burnt-offering and a meat-offering; and he would not have shewn us all these things, neither would he have caused us to hear all these things as at this time.[^9]  ^13-23

**13:24** And the woman brought forth a son, and she called his name Sampson; and the child grew, and the Lord blessed him.  ^13-24

**13:25** And the Spirit of the Lord began to go out with him in the camp of Dan, and between Saraa and Esthaol.[^10]  ^13-25

[^1]: 13:5 *Gr.* iron.
[^2]: 13:5 So *Vat. i.e.* Ναζίρ but *Alex.* nearer to reading in Mat. 2. ult. Ναζίραῖον.
[^3]: 13:6 *Gr.* is.
[^4]: 13:8 See chap 6. 13,15.
[^5]: 13:12 *Heb.* משפס.
[^6]: 13:14 *Gr.* of wine.
[^7]: 13:18 See Is. 9. 6.
[^8]: 13:19 According to the *Heb.* a wonderful work. *Alex.* reads τῷ θανμαστὰ ποιοῦντι κυριῷ.
[^9]: 13:23 *Gr.* as the time is.
[^10]: 13:25 *Gr.* between Esthaol.
