# Titus 3 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Titus 02|← Titus 2]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · —

Compare: [[Titus 03|BSB and SBLGNT]]

**3:1** Admonish them to be subject to princes and powers, to obey at a word, to be ready to every good work. ^3-1

**3:2** To speak evil of no man, not to be litigious, but gentle: shewing all mildness towards all men. ^3-2

**3:3** For we ourselves also were some time unwise, incredulous, erring, slaves to divers desires and pleasures, living in malice and envy, hateful, and hating one another. ^3-3

**3:4** But when the goodness and kindness of God our Saviour appeared: ^3-4

**3:5** Not by the works of justice, which we have done, but according to his mercy, he saved us, by the laver of regeneration, and renovation of the Holy Ghost; ^3-5

**3:6** Whom he hath poured forth upon us abundantly, through Jesus Christ our Saviour: ^3-6

**3:7** That, being justified by his grace, we may be heirs, according to hope of life everlasting. ^3-7

**3:8** It is a faithful saying: and these things I will have thee affirm constantly: that they, who believe in God, may be careful to excel in good works. These things are good and profitable unto men. ^3-8

**3:9** But avoid foolish questions, and genealogies, and contentions, and strivings about the law. For they are unprofitable and vain. ^3-9

**3:10** A man that is a heretic, after the first and second admonition, avoid: ^3-10

**3:11** Knowing that he, that is such an one, is subverted, and sinneth, being condemned by his own judgment. ^3-11

**3:12** When I shall send to thee Artemas or Tychicus, make haste to come unto me to Nicopolis. For there I have determined to winter. ^3-12

**3:13** Send forward Zenas, the lawyer, and Apollo, with care, that nothing be wanting to them. ^3-13

**3:14** And let our men also learn to excel in good works for necessary uses: that they be not unfruitful. ^3-14

**3:15** All that are with me salute thee: salute them that love us in the faith. The grace of God be with you all. Amen. ^3-15
