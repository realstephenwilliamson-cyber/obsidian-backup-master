## Mūla

सर्वेतराध्वव्यावृत्तो घटतुल्योऽस्ति कुत्रचित् ।
महाप्रकाशरूपा हि येयं संविद्विजृम्भते ॥ ^mula

sarvetarādhvavyāvṛtto ghaṭatulyo'sti kutracit |
mahāprakāśarūpā hi yeyaṃ saṃvidvijṛmbhate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 05 - Chapter Fifteen, part 4#^pg176|p. 158]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=176>))
>
> The Śiva (that dualists imagine,) who is tranquil, something different from everything else, and transcends all the other paths (of emanation), is like (a common) jar. (Such a iva) does not exist anywhere. (with 15.264cd–265ab)
>
> This consciousness, which is the Great Light and is (perpetually) expanding, is Śiva. (His) Śiva nature (śivatā) itself is His manifestation as all things. (with 15.265cd–266ab)

*Lead-in:* ननु यदि एवंविधो न शिवस्तत्कतरः?--इत्याशङ्क्य आह--

> महाप्रकाशरूपा हि येयं संविद्विजृम्भते ॥
> स शिवः शिवतैवास्य वैश्वरूप्यावभासिता ।

विजृम्भते इति--विश्वेन रूपेण अवभासते--इत्यर्थः । एतावदेव हि शिवस्य शिवत्वं यत् तेन तेन रूपेण अवभासते इति उक्तं वैश्वरूप्यावभासितैव अस्य शिवतेति ॥
