# τέλειος

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · adjective

**Dodson:** perfect, full-grown — perfect, (a) complete in all its parts, (b) full grown, of full age, (c) specially of the completeness of Christian character. (Strong's 5046; headword τέλειος, α, ον)
