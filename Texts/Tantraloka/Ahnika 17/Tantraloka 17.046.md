## Mūla

पाशच्छेदं करोमीति परामन्त्रः पुनस्ततः ।
हुं स्वाहा फट् समुच्चार्य दद्यात्तिस्रोऽप्यथाहुतीः ॥ ^mula

pāśacchedaṃ karomīti parāmantraḥ punastataḥ |
huṃ svāhā phaṭ samuccārya dadyāttisro'pyathāhutīḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> (Then having uttered) first of all the mantra of Parā, (the teacher should say): “I perform for the soul of so-and-so (the rite of) severing the fetters.” (He should then repeat) the mantra of Parā again and then, having uttered HUM SVĀHĀ and PHAT, he should also offer three oblations.
