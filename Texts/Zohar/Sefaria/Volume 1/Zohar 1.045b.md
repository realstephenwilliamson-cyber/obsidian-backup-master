*Volume 1, folio 45b* · Bereshit

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Bereshit 70:140–70:146.*

**Bereshit 70:147** AND GOD SAID, LET THERE BE LIGHT R. Isaac said: ‘We learn from these words that God uprooted those shoots of which we have spoken[^1] and replanted them; hence the expression “and there was light”, implying that light had already existed.’ R. Judah confirmed this idea from the verse “light is sown for the *Zaddik*” (Ps. 97, 11), this being the one mentioned in the verse “Who aroused Righteousness (*zedek*) from the East, etc.” (Is. 41, 2). ^bereshit-70-147

**Bereshit 70:148** AND GOD SAW THE LIGHT AND DIVIDED. Said R. Isaac: ‘This implies, as we have explained, that he foresaw the works of the wicked and stored the light away.’ R. Abba said: ‘He saw its radiance flashing from one end of the world to the other, and concluded that it was better to store it away in order that sinners might not have the benefit of it.’ ^bereshit-70-148

[^1]: v. p. 131.
