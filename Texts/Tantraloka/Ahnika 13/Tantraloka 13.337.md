## Mūla

उत्तरोत्तरमुत्कर्षलक्ष्मीं पश्यन्नपि स्थितः ।
अधमे यः पदे तस्मात्कोऽन्यः स्याद्दैवदग्धकः ॥ ^mula

uttarottaramutkarṣalakṣmīṃ paśyannapi sthitaḥ |
adhame yaḥ pade tasmātko'nyaḥ syāddaivadagdhakaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 10 - Chapter Thirteen, part 6#^pg355|p. 333]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=355>))
>
> Who could be a greater victim of misfortune (daivadagdhaka) than one who sees the wealth (of a superior teacher’s) excellence and the increasingly higher levels (to which he can lead him), but even so, remains on a lower plane?
