# Psalm 54 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 053|← Psalm 53]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 055|Psalm 55 →]]

## Prayer for vindication

*To the leader: with stringed instruments. A Maskil of David, when the Ziphites went and told Saul,*

*“David is in hiding among us.”*

**54:1** Save me, O God, by your name, and vindicate me by your might. ^54-1

**54:2** Hear my prayer, O God; give ear to the words of my mouth. ^54-2

**54:3** For the insolent have risen against me; the ruthless seek my life; they do not set God before them. *Selah* ^54-3

**54:4** But surely, God is my helper; the Lord is the upholder of^[Gk Syr Jerome: Heb *is of those who uphold* or *is with those who uphold*] my life. ^54-4

**54:5** He will repay my enemies for their evil. In your faithfulness, put an end to them. ^54-5

**54:6** With a freewill offering I will sacrifice to you; I will give thanks to your name, O LORD, for it is good. ^54-6

**54:7** For he has delivered me from every trouble, and my eye has looked in triumph on my enemies. ^54-7
