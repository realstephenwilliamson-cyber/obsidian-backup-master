# Psalms 056

Compare: [[NRSVue Psalms 056|NRSVue]] (Heb 56:1 = superscription; Heb 56:2–14 = NRSVue 56:1–13)

## Chapter 56

**56:1** For the leader; on *yonath elem reḥokim*.[^1] Of David. A *michtam*; when the Philistines seized him in Gath. ^56-1

**56:2** Have mercy on me, O God,  
for men persecute me;  
all day long my adversary oppresses me. ^56-2

**56:3** My watchful foes persecute me all day long;  
many are my adversaries, O Exalted One. ^56-3

**56:4** When I am afraid, I trust in You, ^56-4

**56:5** in God, whose word I praise,  
in God I trust;  
I am not afraid;  
what can mortals[^2] do to me? ^56-5

**56:6** All day long they cause me grief in my affairs,[^3]  
they plan only evil against me. ^56-6

**56:7** They plot, they lie in ambush;  
they watch my every move, hoping for my death. ^56-7

**56:8** Cast them out for their evil;  
subdue peoples in Your anger, O God. ^56-8

**56:9** You keep count of my wanderings;  
put my tears into Your flask,  
into Your record.[^4] ^56-9

**56:10** Then my enemies will retreat when I call on You;  
this I know, that God is for me. ^56-10

**56:11** In God, whose word I praise,  
in the LORD, whose word I praise, ^56-11

**56:12** in God I trust;  
I am not afraid;  
what can man do to me? ^56-12

**56:13** I must pay my vows to You, O God;  
I will render thank offerings to You. ^56-13

**56:14** For You have saved me from death,  
my foot from stumbling,  
that I may walk before God in the light of life. ^56-14

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Lit. “flesh.”
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
