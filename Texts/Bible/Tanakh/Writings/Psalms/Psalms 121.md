## Chapter 121

**121:1** A song for ascents.  
I turn my eyes to the mountains;  
from where will my help come? ^121-1

**121:2** My help comes from the LORD,  
maker of heaven and earth. ^121-2

**121:3** He will not let your foot give way;  
your guardian will not slumber; ^121-3

**121:4** See, the guardian of Israel  
neither slumbers nor sleeps! ^121-4

**121:5** The LORD is your guardian,  
the LORD is your protection  
at your right hand. ^121-5

**121:6** By day the sun will not strike you,  
nor the moon by night. ^121-6

**121:7** The LORD will guard you from all harm;  
He will guard your life. ^121-7

**121:8** The LORD will guard your going and coming  
now and forever. ^121-8
