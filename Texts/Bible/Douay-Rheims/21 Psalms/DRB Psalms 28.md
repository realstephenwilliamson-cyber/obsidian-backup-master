# Psalms 28 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 27|← Psalms 27]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 29|Psalms 29 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 28|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**28:1** A psalm for David, at the finishing of the tabernacle. Bring to the Lord, O ye children of God: bring to the Lord the offspring of rams. ^28-1

**28:2** Bring to the Lord glory and honour: bring to the Lord glory to his name: adore ye the Lord in his holy court. ^28-2

**28:3** The voice of the Lord is upon the waters; the God of majesty hath thundered, The Lord is upon many waters. ^28-3

**28:4** The voice of the Lord is in power; the voice of the Lord in magnificence. ^28-4

**28:5** The voice of the Lord breaketh the cedars: yea, the Lord shall break the cedars of Libanus. ^28-5

**28:6** And shall reduce them to pieces, as a calf of Libanus, and as the beloved son of unicorns. ^28-6

**28:7** The voice of the Lord divideth the flame of fire: ^28-7

**28:8** The voice of the Lord shaketh the desert: and the Lord shall shake the desert of Cades. ^28-8

**28:9** The voice of the Lord prepareth the stags: and he will discover the thick woods: and in his temple all shall speak his glory. ^28-9

**28:10** The Lord maketh the flood to dwell: and the Lord shall sit king for ever. ^28-10

**28:11** The Lord will give strength to his people: the Lord will bless his people with peace. ^28-11
