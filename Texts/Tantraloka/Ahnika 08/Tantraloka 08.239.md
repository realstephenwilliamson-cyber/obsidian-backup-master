## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 08 - Chapter Eight, part 6#^pg274|p. 256]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=274>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 8.239cd–240ab.
>
> Then (beyond the worlds) of Umā and Śrīkantṭha, that are on the plane of the eight Yoga (Rudras), there are two other worlds, also (governed by) Umā and Śrīkantṭha, which will (now) be described again. There, one of these is the world of the goddess Umā, Who presides over the universe. She is the Great Goddess (Maheśvarī) who is given different names in each aeon (pratikalpa). After (this world) comes the world of Umāpati, which is surrounded by the Mothers. He is Śrīkaṇṭha, who in his supreme form is called Umāpati. (with 8.238–241ab)
