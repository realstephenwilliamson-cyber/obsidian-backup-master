# Psalm 113 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 112|← Psalm 112]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 114|Psalm 114 →]]

## God the helper of the needy

**113:1** Praise the LORD! Praise, O servants of the LORD; praise the name of the LORD. ^113-1

**113:2** Blessed be the name of the LORD from this time on and forevermore. ^113-2

**113:3** From the rising of the sun to its setting, the name of the LORD is to be praised. ^113-3

**113:4** The LORD is high above all nations and his glory above the heavens. ^113-4

**113:5** Who is like the LORD our God, who is seated on high, ^113-5

**113:6** who looks far down on the heavens and the earth? ^113-6

**113:7** He raises the poor from the dust and lifts the needy from the ash heap, ^113-7

**113:8** to make them sit with princes, with the princes of his people. ^113-8

**113:9** He gives the barren woman a home, making her the joyous mother of children. Praise the LORD! ^113-9
