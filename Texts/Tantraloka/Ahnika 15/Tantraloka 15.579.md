## Mūla

गुर्वाज्ञां पालयन्सर्वं त्यजेन्मन्त्रमयो भवेत् ।
शास्त्रपूजाजपध्यानविवेकतदुपक्रियाः ॥ ^mula

gurvājñāṃ pālayansarvaṃ tyajenmantramayo bhavet |
śāstrapūjājapadhyānavivekatadupakriyāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 10 - Chapter Fifteen, part 9#^pg355|p. 337]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=355>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 15.581cd–582ab.
>
> Observing the teacher’s command, he should abandon everything, and be one with Mantra (mantramaya) (by being fully concentrated on it). He should not dedicate himself fruitlessly to the three types of rites (obligatory, occasional and optional) without (worshipping) the scriptures, worshipping (the deities), repeating Mantra, meditation, discrimination, and (whatever) aids them. One should not kill (others)? with Mantras and magical rites (mantratantra), and not feed (others) poison. (with 15.579–580)
