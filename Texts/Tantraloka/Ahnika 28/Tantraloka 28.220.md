## Mūla

असङ्कोचस्य तन्वादिकर्ता तेनेश उच्यते ।
स वाय्वात्मा दृढे तस्मिन्देहयन्त्रे चिदात्मना ॥ ^mula

asaṅkocasya tanvādikartā teneśa ucyate |
sa vāyvātmā dṛḍhe tasmindehayantre cidātmanā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 05 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 3#^pg115|p. 98]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=115>))
>
> The sovereign power (TŚvaratā) to fashion the body in the womb belongs solely to that (initial emergence of the life force which is as yet) uncontracted (and undifferentiated). Thus it is said that it is the Lord who makes the body and the rest (of the psychophysical organism). (with 28.219cd–220ab)
>
> This (assumes) the nature of the (vital) wind that circulates within the (now) solid machine of the body, impelled (preryamāṇa) by the Self, which is consciousness, as does air within bellows (impelled by the one who uses them). Thus that (foetus) awakens, like one who, initially in (very) deep sleep, rises from it. (with 28.220cd–221)
