# Job 21

## Chapter 21

**21:1** Job said in reply: ^21-1

**21:2** Listen well to what I say,  
And let that be your consolation. ^21-2

**21:3** Bear with me while I speak,  
And after I have spoken, you may mock. ^21-3

**21:4** Is my complaint directed toward a man?  
Why should I not lose my patience? ^21-4

**21:5** Look at me and be appalled,  
And clap your hand to your mouth. ^21-5

**21:6** When I think of it I am terrified;  
My body is seized with shuddering. ^21-6

**21:7** Why do the wicked live on,  
Prosper and grow wealthy? ^21-7

**21:8** Their children are with them always,  
And they see their children’s children. ^21-8

**21:9** Their homes are secure, without fear;  
They do not feel the rod of God. ^21-9

**21:10** Their bull breeds and does not fail;  
Their cow calves and never miscarries; ^21-10

**21:11** They let their infants run loose like sheep,  
And their children skip about. ^21-11

**21:12** They sing to the music of timbrel and lute,  
And revel to the tune of the pipe; ^21-12

**21:13** They spend their days in happiness,  
And go down to Sheol in peace. ^21-13

**21:14** They say to God, “Leave us alone,  
We do not want to learn Your ways; ^21-14

**21:15** What is Shaddai that we should serve Him?  
What will we gain by praying to Him?” ^21-15

**21:16** Their happiness is not their own doing.  
(The thoughts of the wicked are beyond me!) ^21-16

**21:17** How seldom does the lamp of the wicked fail,  
Does the calamity they deserve befall them,  
Does He apportion [their] lot in anger! ^21-17

**21:18** Let them become like straw in the wind,  
Like chaff carried off by a storm. ^21-18

**21:19** [You say,] “God is reserving his punishment for his sons”;  
Let it be paid back to him that he may feel it, ^21-19

**21:20** Let his eyes see his ruin,  
And let him drink the wrath of Shaddai! ^21-20

**21:21** For what does he care about the fate of his family,  
When his number of months runs out? ^21-21

**21:22** Can God be instructed in knowledge,  
He who judges from such heights? ^21-22

**21:23** One man dies in robust health,  
All tranquil and untroubled; ^21-23

**21:24** His pails are full of milk;  
The marrow of his bones is juicy. ^21-24

**21:25** Another dies embittered,  
Never having tasted happiness. ^21-25

**21:26** They both lie in the dust  
And are covered with worms. ^21-26

**21:27** Oh, I know your thoughts,  
And the tactics you will devise against me. ^21-27

**21:28** You will say, “Where is the house of the great man—  
And where the tent in which the wicked dwelled?” ^21-28

**21:29** You must have consulted the wayfarers;  
You cannot deny their evidence. ^21-29

**21:30** For the evil man is spared on the day of calamity,  
On the day when wrath is led forth. ^21-30

**21:31** Who will upbraid him to his face?  
Who will requite him for what he has done? ^21-31

**21:32** He is brought to the grave,  
While a watch is kept at his tomb. ^21-32

**21:33** The clods of the wadi are sweet to him,  
Everyone follows behind him,  
Innumerable are those who precede him. ^21-33

**21:34** Why then do you offer me empty consolation?  
Of your replies only the perfidy remains. ^21-34
