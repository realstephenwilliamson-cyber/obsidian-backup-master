# Psalm 116 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 115|← Psalm 115]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 117|Psalm 117 →]]

## Thanksgiving for recovery from illness

**116:1** I love the LORD because he has heard my voice and my supplications. ^116-1

**116:2** Because he inclined his ear to me, therefore I will call on him as long as I live. ^116-2

**116:3** The snares of death encompassed me; the pangs of Sheol laid hold on me; I suffered distress and anguish. ^116-3

**116:4** Then I called on the name of the LORD, “O LORD, I pray, save my life!” ^116-4

**116:5** Gracious is the LORD and righteous; our God is merciful. ^116-5

**116:6** The LORD protects the simple; when I was brought low, he saved me. ^116-6

**116:7** Return, O my soul, to your rest, for the LORD has dealt bountifully with you. ^116-7

**116:8** For you have delivered my soul from death, my eyes from tears, my feet from stumbling. ^116-8

**116:9** I walk before the LORD in the land of the living. ^116-9

**116:10** ^[Some early witnesses begin a new psalm here]I kept my faith, even when I said, “I am greatly afflicted”; ^116-10

**116:11** I said in my consternation, “Everyone is a liar.” ^116-11

**116:12** What shall I return to the LORD for all his bounty to me? ^116-12

**116:13** I will lift up the cup of salvation and call on the name of the LORD; ^116-13

**116:14** I will pay my vows to the LORD in the presence of all his people. ^116-14

**116:15** Precious in the sight of the LORD is the death of his faithful ones. ^116-15

**116:16** O LORD, I am your servant; I am your servant, the child of your serving girl. You have loosed my bonds. ^116-16

**116:17** I will offer to you a thanksgiving sacrifice and call on the name of the LORD. ^116-17

**116:18** I will pay my vows to the LORD in the presence of all his people, ^116-18

**116:19** in the courts of the house of the LORD, in your midst, O Jerusalem. Praise the LORD! ^116-19
