## Mūla

तन्निषेधस्तु मन्त्रार्थसार्वात्म्यप्रतिपत्तये ।
क्षेत्रपीठोपपीठेषु प्रवेशो विघ्नशान्तये ॥ ^mula

tanniṣedhastu mantrārthasārvātmyapratipattaye |

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. III
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 03 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v03 11 - Chapter Four, part 10#^pg377|p. 364]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 03.pdf#page=377>))
>
> Moreover (the Siddhāntas prescribe) the observance of (ascetic) vows (vratacaryā) in order to achieve a state of identity (fādātmya) with (the deity, which is) the reality (artha) denoted by (one’s) Mantra. (But) that is prohibited (by the Kula Tantras), so one may (come to) realize the universal nature (sārvātmya) of (the deity, that is the) reality denoted by Mantra (mantrārtha). (with 4.258cd–259ab)
>
> Entry (praveśa) into the sacred meeting grounds (kṣetra) and the sacred seats, both primary (pīṭha) and secondary (upapīṭha), is taught (in Kula Tantras) in order to quell obstacles (to the success of one’s practice). Now, in the case of one who propitiates (a particular) Mantra (deity) and the like, (residence in such places) is taught (as a means), in order to attain that (mastery through it and the benefits it bestows). (with 4.259cd–260ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* अथ क्षेत्रादिसंप्रवेशं व्याख्यातुमाह --

> क्षेत्रपीठोपपीठेषु प्रवेशो विघ्नशान्तये ॥
> मन्त्राद्याराधकस्याथ तल्लाभायोपदिश्यते ।

"क्षेत्रं"मेलापस्थानं, "पीठं"कामरूपादि "उपपीठं"देवीकोट्टादि । "तल्लाभाय"इति तस्य मन्त्रादेः प्रियमेलापादिक्रमेण सिद्धादेर्लाभः, तन्निमित्तं वा । यदुक्तम् --

"क्षेत्रोपक्षेत्रसंदोहाद्याश्रयान्निर्मलो भवेत् ॥"

इति ॥ २५९ ॥
