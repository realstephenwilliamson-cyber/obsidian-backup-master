## Mūla

अव्यक्ताख्ये ह्याविरिञ्चाच्छ्रीकण्ठेन सहासते ।
निवृत्ताधःस्थकर्मा हि ब्रह्मा तत्राधरे धियः ॥ ^mula

avyaktākhye hyāviriñcācchrīkaṇṭhena sahāsate |
nivṛttādhaḥsthakarmā hi brahmā tatrādhare dhiyaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 11 - Chapter Six, part 4#^pg384|p. 369]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=384>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 6.150cd–151ab.
>
> Then Lord Śrīkanṇtha himself is the destroyer (of all this) directly. In this way, all the Rudras starting from Brahmā, who have dominion over what is contained within the womb of Māyā, reside within Unmanifest Root (Nature) together with Śrīkanṇṭha. (with 6.149–150ab)
>
> When Brahmā’s activities below the level of the intellect have ceased, he can then no longer be considered to be their enjoyer. (Now he) knows (jña) (the true nature of reality), his period of office is complete, and so he becomes one with Śiva. This is the intermediate form of destruction. When it ceases, it is said that (a new) creation (takes place). (with 6.150cd–151)
