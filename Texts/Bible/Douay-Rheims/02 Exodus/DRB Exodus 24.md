# Exodus 24 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Exodus 23|← Exodus 23]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Exodus 25|Exodus 25 →]]

**24:1** And he said to Moses: Come up to the Lord, thou, and Aaron, Nadab, and Abiu, and seventy of the ancients of Israel, and you shall adore afar off. ^24-1

**24:2** And Moses alone shall come up to the Lord, but they shall not come nigh; neither shall the people come up with him. ^24-2

**24:3** So Moses came and told the people all the words of the Lord, and all the judgments: and all the people answered with one voice: We will do all the words of the Lord, which he hath spoken. ^24-3

**24:4** And Moses wrote all the words of the Lord: and rising in the morning he built an altar at the foot of the mount, and twelve titles according to the twelve tribes of Israel. ^24-4

**24:5** And he sent young men of the children of Israel, and they offered holocausts, and sacrificed pacific victims of calves to the Lord. ^24-5

**24:6** Then Moses took half of the blood, and put it into bowls: and the rest he poured upon the altar. ^24-6

**24:7** And taking the book of the covenant, he read it in the hearing of the people: and they said: All things that the Lord hath spoken we will do, we will be obedient. ^24-7

**24:8** And he took the blood and sprinkled it upon the people, and he said: This is the blood of the covenant which the Lord hath made with you concerning all these words. ^24-8

**24:9** Then Moses and Aaron, Nadab and Abiu, and seventy of the ancients of Israel went up: ^24-9

**24:10** And they saw the God of Israel: and under his feet as it were a work of sapphire stone, and as the heaven, when clear. ^24-10

**24:11** Neither did he lay his hand upon those of the children of Israel, that retired afar off, and they saw God, and they did eat and drink. ^24-11

**24:12** And the Lord said to Moses: Come up to me into the mount, and be there: and I will give thee tables of stone, and the law, and the commandments which I have written: that thou mayst teach them. ^24-12

**24:13** Moses rose up, and his minister Josue: and Moses going up into the mount of God, ^24-13

**24:14** Said to the ancients: Wait ye here till we return to you. You have Aaron and Hur with you: if any question shall arise, you shall refer it to them. ^24-14

**24:15** And when Moses was gone up, a cloud covered the mount. ^24-15

**24:16** And the glory of the Lord dwelt upon Sinai, covering it with a cloud six days: and the seventh day he called him out of the midst of the cloud. ^24-16

**24:17** And the sight of the glory of the Lord was like a burning fire upon the top of the mount, in the eyes of the children of Israel. ^24-17

**24:18** And Moses, entering into the midst of the cloud, went up into the mountain: and he was there forty days, and forty nights. ^24-18
