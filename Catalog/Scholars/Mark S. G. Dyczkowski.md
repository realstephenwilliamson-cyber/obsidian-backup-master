## Free online

- [Anuttara Trika Kula - Articles (his own site)](https://www.anuttaratrikakula.org/articles/): Dyczkowski's own site. Articles page offers 5 of his essays as direct PDF downloads (Kubjika the Erotic Goddess; The Cult of the Goddess Kubjika; The Inner Pilgrimage of the Tantras; Khacakrapancakastotra and Mahanayaprakasa; Self Awareness and Egoity) plus 3 web-only articles (Swagatham to the Journey; Goddess Kubjika; Abhinavagupta's Primary Works). Site also has free lectures and one free Tantraloka lesson. The 11-volume Tantraloka translation itself is sold, not free.
- [Anuttara Trika Kula - Tantraloka translation page](https://www.anuttaratrikakula.org/tantraloka-translation/): Announcement of the completed 11-volume Tantraloka translation with purchase links; no free PDFs of the translation. Menu points to 'Free Lectures' and a free 'Tantraloka Chapter 1 Lesson 1' course.
