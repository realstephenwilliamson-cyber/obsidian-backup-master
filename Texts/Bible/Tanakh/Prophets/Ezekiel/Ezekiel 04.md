## Chapter 4

**4:1** “And you, O mortal, take a brick and put it in front of you, and incise on it a city, Jerusalem. ^4-1

**4:2** Set up a siege against it, and build towers against it, and cast a mound against it; pitch camps against it, and bring up battering rams round about it. ^4-2

**4:3** Then take an iron plate and place it as an iron wall between yourself and the city, and set your face against it. [^1]Thus it shall be under siege, you shall besiege it. This shall be an omen for the House of Israel. ^4-3

**4:4** “Then lie on your left side, and let it bear the punishment of the House of Israel;[^2] for as many days as you lie on it you shall bear their punishment. ^4-4

**4:5** For I impose upon you three hundred and ninety days, corresponding to the number of the years of their punishment; and so you shall bear the punishment for the House of Israel. ^4-5

**4:6** When you have completed these, you shall lie another forty days on your right side, and bear the punishment of the House of Judah.[^3] I impose on you one day for each year. ^4-6

**4:7** “Then, with bared arm, set your face toward besieged Jerusalem and prophesy against it. ^4-7

**4:8** Now I put cords upon you, so that you cannot turn from side to side until you complete your days of siege. ^4-8

**4:9** “Further, take wheat, barley, beans, lentils, millet, and emmer. Put them into one vessel and bake them into bread. Eat it as many days as you lie on your side: three hundred and ninety. ^4-9

**4:10** The food that you eat shall be by weight, twenty shekels a day; this you shall eat in the space of a day. ^4-10

**4:11** And you shall drink water by measure; drink a sixth of a *hin* in the space of a day. ^4-11

**4:12** “Eat it as a barley[^4] cake; you shall bake it on human excrement before their eyes. ^4-12

**4:13** So,” said the LORD, “shall the people of Israel eat their bread, unclean, among the nations to which I will banish them.” ^4-13

**4:14** Then I said, “Ah, Lord GOD, my person was never defiled; nor have I eaten anything that died of itself or was torn by beasts from my youth until now, nor has foul flesh entered my mouth.” ^4-14

**4:15** He answered me, “See, I allow you cow’s dung instead of human excrement; prepare your bread on that.” ^4-15

**4:16** [^5]And He said to me, “O mortal, I am going to break the staff of bread in Jerusalem, and they shall eat bread by weight, in anxiety, and drink water by measure, in horror, ^4-16

**4:17** so that, lacking bread and water, they shall stare at each other, heartsick over their iniquity. ^4-17

[^1]: I.e., in hostility.
[^2]: Since left and right also denote north and south (e.g., 16.46), the left side represents Israel, the northern kingdom, and the right side Judah, the southern kingdom.
[^3]: Since left and right also denote north and south (e.g., 16.46), the left side represents Israel, the northern kingdom, and the right side Judah, the southern kingdom.
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^5]: Resuming the thought of v. 11.
