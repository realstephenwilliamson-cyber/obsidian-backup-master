*Volume 3, folio 216a* · Pinchas

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Pinchas 13:84–15:106.*

**Pinchas 15:107** [^1]R. Abba and R. Jose once rose at midnight to study the Torah. As they were sitting they saw a shadow hovering over them and going to and fro in the house, and they marvelled greatly. Said R. Abba: ‘Jose, my son, I will tell you what once happened to me with the Sacred Lamp. Once we were walking in the Valley of Ono, studying the Torah. To escape the heat of the sun we sat down in the hollow of a rock, ^pinchas-15-107

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Pinchas 10:49–10:58.*

[^1]: the first 13 lines of the Hebrew text are not translated.
