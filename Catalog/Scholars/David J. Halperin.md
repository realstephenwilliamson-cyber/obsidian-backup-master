# David J. Halperin

Professor emeritus of Religious Studies at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, where he taught the history of Judaism from 1976 to 2000; his speciality is religious traditions of heavenly ascent, and he is the author of five books on Jewish mysticism and messianism as well as a novel and a book on UFOs ([Rice University, Archives of the Impossible](https://impossiblearchives.rice.edu/flash-talk-speakers/david-j-halperin); [his site](https://www.davidhalperin.net/about-david-halperin)).
