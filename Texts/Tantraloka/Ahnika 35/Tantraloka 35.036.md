## Mūla

सांख्यं योगं पाञ्चरात्रं वेदांश्चैव न निन्दयेत् ।
यतः शिवोद्भवाः सर्व इति स्वच्छन्दशासने ॥ ^mula

sāṃkhyaṃ yogaṃ pāñcarātraṃ vedāṃścaiva na nindayet |
yataḥ śivodbhavāḥ sarva iti svacchandaśāsane || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 09 - Chapter Thirty-Five#^pg231|p. 213]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=231>))
>
> It is said in the teachings of the Svacchandatantra that the Sāṁkhya, Yoga, (the Vaiṣṇava) Pāñcarātra, and Veda should not be criticized, as they all originate from Śiva.
