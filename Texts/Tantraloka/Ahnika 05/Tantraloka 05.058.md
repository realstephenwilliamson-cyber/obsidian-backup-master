## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 04 - Chapter Five, part 2#^pg78|p. 63]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=78>))
>
> There on the plane of the Upper Kundalinī is emission, beautiful with the noble pulsation (of consciousness). The (yogi) should rest there, on the plane of the Belly of the Fish. (with 5.57cd–58ab)
>
> Just as the female donkey or mare entering (at the moment of orgasm) into her own abode, (that is, the Yoni,) the temple of bliss, made of contraction and expansion, delights in her heart, similarly (the yogi) should rest (śrayet) in the Bhairava couple, established in expansion and contraction, full of the aggregate of phenomena (bhāvavrata), repeatedly destroyed and created (again and again). (with 5.58cd–60ab)
