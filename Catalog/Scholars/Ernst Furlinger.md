# Ernst Fürlinger

Ernst Fürlinger is a scholar of religious studies (Privatdozent; habilitation at the University of Vienna, 2013) and since March 2023 a research associate of the interfaculty Platform for Sustainable Development (SDGs) at the University for Continuing Education Krems, where his recent work is on climate ethics and social-ecological transformation ([Krems staff page](https://www.donau-uni.ac.at/en/university/organization/employees/person/4295058695); [CV, Nov. 2024](https://www.donau-uni.ac.at/dam/jcr:98d4de3c-579e-4592-9adf-6e0e9e562d21/CV_F%C3%BCrlinger_November2024.pdf); [Zwischenräume profile](https://zwischenraeume.co.at/team/ernst-fuerlinger/)). He took his doctorate in religious studies in Vienna in 2005 after a research stay in North India (2001–2006) that included Sanskrit study at Banaras Hindu University, worked at Krems and Vienna on Islam, Hindu Tantrism and interreligious dialogue, and from 2011 to 2019 founded and led the Centre for Religion and Globalisation in the Krems Department for Migration and Globalisation ([CV](https://www.donau-uni.ac.at/dam/jcr:98d4de3c-579e-4592-9adf-6e0e9e562d21/CV_F%C3%BCrlinger_November2024.pdf); [Zwischenräume profile](https://zwischenraeume.co.at/team/ernst-fuerlinger/)). His Academia.edu profile and the TVZ author page still give the earlier Krems department; the CV is the source followed here.
