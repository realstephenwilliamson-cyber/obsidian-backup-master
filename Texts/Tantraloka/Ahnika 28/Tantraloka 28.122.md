## Mūla

कर्तव्यः सोऽनिरोधेन यावत्सा तुलपूर्णिमा ।
तुलोपलक्षितस्यान्त्यं कार्तिकस्य दिनं मतम् ॥ ^mula

kartavyaḥ so'nirodhena yāvatsā tulapūrṇimā |
tulopalakṣitasyāntyaṃ kārtikasya dinaṃ matam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 04 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 2#^pg81|p. 64]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=81>))
>
> This (ritual) procedure can be (undertaken any time,) with no impediment, from the bright fortnight (of the month) of llṣādha (June-July) up to the full moon in Libra, that is, when the sun is in Gemini and in Cancer. The last day (of this period) is considered to be (the last day of the month of) Kārttika (October-November), which Libra implicitly denotes as (the month the sun is in Libra). Others who read Kula' (instead of Tulā – Libra) explain differently.'" (with 28.121cd–123ab)

## Viveka

> [!viveka]- Viveka in English (Dyczkowski, vol. X, p. 65)
> ![[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 04 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 2#Viveka on 28.121–123]]

> आषाढशुक्लान्मिथुनकर्कटस्थे रवौ विधिः ॥
> कर्तव्यः सोऽनिरोधेन यावत्सा तुलपूर्णिमा ।
> तुलोपलक्षितस्यान्त्यं कार्तिकस्य दिनं मतम् ॥
> कुलशब्दं पठन्तोऽन्ये व्याख्याभेदं प्रकुर्वते ।

मिथुनेत्याद्युपलक्षणम्, तेन सिंहादिस्थेऽपि । अनिरोधेनेति-- अविच्छेदेन--इत्यर्थः । तुलोपलक्षितस्येति--कार्तिके हि तुलागत एव रविर्भवेत्--इति भावः ॥
