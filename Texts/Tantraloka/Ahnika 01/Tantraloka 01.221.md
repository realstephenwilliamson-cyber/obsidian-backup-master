## Mūla

अणुर्नाम स्फुटो भेदस्तदुपाय इहाणवः ।
विकल्पनिश्चयात्मैव पर्यन्ते निर्विकल्पकः ॥ ^mula

aṇurnāma sphuṭo bhedastadupāya ihāṇavaḥ |
vikalpaniścayātmaiva paryante nirvikalpakaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. I
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 01 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v01 11 - Chapter One, part 10#^pg413|p. 399]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 01.pdf#page=413>))
>
> The duality (bheda) between individual souls (aṇu) is evident. Here the means relating to it (that resolves it) is the individual one (āṇava). Its essential nature is the certainty (which is the correct determination of) conceptual representation (vikalpaniścaya). Ultimately (it culminates in a state of consciousness that is) free of thought constructs.
