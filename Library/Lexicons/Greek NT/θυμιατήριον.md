# θυμιατήριον

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · noun

**Dodson:** altar of incense — (ordinarily: censer, but) either the altar of incense, or the shovel, on which the high-priest poured the coals, when he entered the Holy of Holies on the Day of Atonement. (Strong's 2369; headword θυμιατήριον, ου, τό)
