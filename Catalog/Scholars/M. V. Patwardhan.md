# M. V. Patwardhan

M. V. Patwardhan was a Sanskritist in Poona (Pune) who worked on Sanskrit poetics and Prakrit poetry. In the preface to his edition and translation of Jayavallabha's *Vajjālaggam* (Prakrit Text Society, 1969), signed "Prof. M. V. Patwardhan, M.A., Poona", he writes of his "teaching and administrative work in the Fergusson College, Poona" (title page and Preface, p. vii, dated Poona, 1 May 1969; read in the scanned copy on jainqq.org, 19 Oct 2026); the Works Cited of the Harvard *Dhvanyāloka* lists that edition under the same author entry as his books with J. L. Masson ([[The Dhvanyaloka 22 - Abbreviations and Works Cited#^pg757|Works Cited, p. 742]]). Daniel Ingalls's Foreword to that volume, dated August 1989, says Masson studied under him in India and reports "the sad news of Professor Patwardhan's death" shortly before the book went to press ([[The Dhvanyaloka 02 - Foreword#^pg10|Foreword]]); the exact date of death was not found, and no later position than Fergusson College was found.
