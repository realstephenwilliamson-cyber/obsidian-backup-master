## Mūla

श्रीपूर्वशास्त्रे तेनादौ तत्त्वेषूक्तं विधित्रयम् ।
अतिदिष्टं तु तद्भिन्नाभिन्नवर्णद्वये समम् ॥ ^mula

śrīpūrvaśāstre tenādau tattveṣūktaṃ vidhitrayam |
atidiṣṭaṃ tu tadbhinnābhinnavarṇadvaye samam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 06 - Chapter Sixteen, part 2#^pg84|p. 64]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=84>))
>
> Thus, the three procedures are initially expounded in the Mālinīvijayottara in relation to the principles and then transposed (with the same criteria) to the two (series of) phonemes in which (the vowels and consonants are) mixed (i.e. Mālinī) and not mixed (i.e. Mātrkā).
