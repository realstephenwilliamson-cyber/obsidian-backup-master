## Mūla

सर्वाभयाः खड्गधाराव्रतास्तत्तत्त्ववेदिनः ।
क्रमात्तत्तत्त्वमायान्ति यत्रेशोऽनन्त उच्यते ॥ ^mula

sarvābhayāḥ khaḍgadhārāvratāstattattvavedinaḥ |
kramāttattattvamāyānti yatreśo'nanta ucyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 09 - Chapter Eight, part 7#^pg324|p. 306]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=324>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 8.302cd–303ab.
>
> Those who observe the Vow of the Skull, offer their own limbs to the fire, practice severe austerities, fear nothing, and observe the Vow of the Blade of the Sword know that (reality) principle (tattva). (with 8.301cd–302ab)
>
> Gradually they reach that principle where the Lord is said to be Ananta.’ (with 8.302cd)
