## Chapter 37

**37:1** Of David.  
Do not be vexed by evil men;  
do not be incensed by wrongdoers; ^37-1

**37:2** for they soon wither like grass,  
like verdure fade away. ^37-2

**37:3** Trust in the LORD and do good,  
abide in the land and remain loyal. ^37-3

**37:4** Seek the favor of the LORD,  
and He will grant you the desires of your heart. ^37-4

**37:5** Leave all[^1] to the LORD;  
trust in Him; He will do it. ^37-5

**37:6** He will cause your vindication to shine forth like the light,  
the justice of your case, like the noonday sun. ^37-6

**37:7** Be patient and wait for the LORD,  
do not be vexed by the prospering man  
who carries out his schemes. ^37-7

**37:8** Give up anger, abandon fury,  
do not be vexed;  
it can only do harm. ^37-8

**37:9** For evil men will be cut off,  
but those who look to the LORD—  
they shall inherit the land. ^37-9

**37:10** A little longer and there will be no wicked man;  
you will look at where he was—  
he will be gone. ^37-10

**37:11** But the lowly shall inherit the land,  
and delight in abundant well-being. ^37-11

**37:12** The wicked man schemes against the righteous,  
and gnashes his teeth at him. ^37-12

**37:13** The Lord laughs at him,  
for He knows that his day will come. ^37-13

**37:14** The wicked draw their swords, bend their bows,  
to bring down the lowly and needy,  
to slaughter upright men.[^2] ^37-14

**37:15** Their swords shall pierce their own hearts,  
and their bows shall be broken. ^37-15

**37:16** Better the little that the righteous man has  
than the great abundance of the wicked. ^37-16

**37:17** For the arms of the wicked shall be broken,  
but the LORD is the support of the righteous. ^37-17

**37:18** The LORD is concerned for the needs[^3] of the blameless;  
their portion lasts forever; ^37-18

**37:19** they shall not come to grief in bad times;  
in famine, they shall eat their fill. ^37-19

**37:20** But the wicked shall perish,  
and the enemies of the LORD shall be consumed,  
like meadow grass[^4] consumed in smoke. ^37-20

**37:21** The wicked man borrows and does not repay;  
the righteous is generous and keeps giving. ^37-21

**37:22** Those blessed by Him shall inherit the land,  
but those cursed by Him shall be cut off. ^37-22

**37:23** The steps of a man are made firm by the LORD,  
when He delights in his way. ^37-23

**37:24** Though he stumbles, he does not fall down,  
for the LORD gives him support. ^37-24

**37:25** I have been young and am now old,  
but I have never seen a righteous man abandoned,  
or his children seeking bread. ^37-25

**37:26** He is always generous, and lends,  
and his children are held blessed. ^37-26

**37:27** Shun evil and do good,  
and you shall abide forever. ^37-27

**37:28** For the LORD loves what is right,  
He does not abandon His faithful ones.  
They are preserved forever,  
while the children of the wicked will be cut off. ^37-28

**37:29** The righteous shall inherit the land,  
and abide forever in it. ^37-29

**37:30** The mouth of the righteous utters wisdom,  
and his tongue speaks what is right. ^37-30

**37:31** The teaching of his God is in his heart;  
his feet do not slip. ^37-31

**37:32** The wicked watches for the righteous,  
seeking to put him to death; ^37-32

**37:33** the LORD will not abandon him to his power;  
He will not let him be condemned in judgment. ^37-33

**37:34** Look to the LORD and keep to His way,  
and He will raise you high that you may inherit the land;  
when the wicked are cut off, you shall see it. ^37-34

**37:35** I saw a wicked man, powerful,  
well-rooted like a robust native tree. ^37-35

**37:36** Suddenly he vanished and was gone;  
I sought him, but he was not to be found. ^37-36

**37:37** Mark the blameless, note the upright,  
for there is a future for the man of integrity. ^37-37

**37:38** But transgressors shall be utterly destroyed,  
the future of the wicked shall be cut off. ^37-38

**37:39** The deliverance of the righteous comes from the LORD,  
their stronghold in time of trouble. ^37-39

**37:40** The LORD helps them and rescues them,  
rescues them from the wicked and delivers them,  
for they seek refuge in Him. ^37-40

[^1]: Lit. “your way.”
[^2]: Lit. “those whose way is upright.”
[^3]: Lit. “days.”
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
