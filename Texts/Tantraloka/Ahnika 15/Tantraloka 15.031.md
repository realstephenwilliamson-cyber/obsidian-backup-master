## Mūla

समयाचारपाशं तु निर्बीजायां विशोधयेत् ।
दीक्षामात्रेण मुक्तिः स्याद्भक्त्या देवे गुरौ सदा ॥ ^mula

samayācārapāśaṃ tu nirbījāyāṃ viśodhayet |
dīkṣāmātreṇa muktiḥ syādbhaktyā deve gurau sadā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 02 - Chapter Fifteen, part 1#^pg46|p. 28]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=46>))
>
> In the case (of the initiation) ‘without seed’, (the teacher) should, on the contrary, remove all the restrictions imposed by the rules (the initiate ‘with seed’) should observe. (The disciple) is liberated solely by the initiation itself, due to (his) constant devotion to God and the teacher. The form of initiation ‘without seed’, called ‘the Immediate bestower of Nirvāṇa’, severs (and eliminates) the three bonds of past, future and present Karma (from the disciple’s soul). (with 15.31–32)
