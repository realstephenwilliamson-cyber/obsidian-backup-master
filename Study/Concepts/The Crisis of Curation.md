# The Crisis of Curation

> [!abstract] Definition
> The displacement of human, institutional, and ethical curators (editors, educators, libraries, communal elders) by algorithmic recommendation engines optimized for engagement, attention capture, and monetization, destroying the conditions for shared collective memory.

## Diagnosis
Formulated in [[Cybernetic Anamnesis text 2025-11-05|Cybernetic Anamnesis]]:
- Previous media transitions (print, radio, television) were broadcast models: heterogeneous audiences watched the same central transmission (e.g., the 1963 JFK state funeral broadcast), establishing a baseline of shared narrative emplotment.
- Under algorithmic curation, every citizen receives a hyper-personalized feed calculated to maximize individual affective stimulation.
- The crisis is not merely "bad information" (fake news), but the destruction of a common interpretive horizon: society fractures into incompatible, enclosed perceptual bubbles that cannot recognize each other's evidence.
