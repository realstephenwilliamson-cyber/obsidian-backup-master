# LXX Proverbs 11

← [[LXX Proverbs 10]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Proverbs 12]] →

*Proverbs · [[LXX]]*

**11:1** False balances are an abomination before the Lord: but a just weight is acceptable unto him.  ^11-1

**11:2** Wherever pride enters, there will be also disgrace: but the mouth of the lowly meditates wisdom.[^1]  ^11-2

**11:3** When a just man dies he leaves regret: but the destruction of the ungodly is speedy, and causes joy.[^2][^3]  ^11-3

**11:5** Righteousness traces out blameless paths: but ungodliness encounters unjust dealing.[^4]  ^11-5

**11:6** The righteousness of upright men delivers them: but transgressors are caught in their own destruction.  ^11-6

**11:7** At the death of a just man his hope does not perish: but the boast of the ungodly perishes.  ^11-7

**11:8** A righteous man escapes from a snare, and the ungodly man is delivered up in his place.  ^11-8

**11:9** In the mouth of ungodly men is a snare to citizens: but the understanding of righteous men is prosperous.  ^11-9

**11:10** In the prosperity of righteous men a city prospers:[^5]  ^11-10

**11:11** but by the mouths of ungodly men it is overthrown.  ^11-11

**11:12** A man void of understanding sneers at *his fellow* citizens: but a sensible man is quiet.  ^11-12

**11:13** A double-tongued man discloses the *secret* counsels of an assembly: but he that is faithful in spirit conceals matters.[^6]  ^11-13

**11:14** They that have no guidance fall like leaves: but in much counsel there is safety.[^7]  ^11-14

**11:15** A bad man does harm wherever he meets a just man: and he hates the sound of safety.  ^11-15

**11:16** A gracious wife brings glory to her husband: but a woman hating righteousness is a theme of dishonour. The slothful come to want: but the diligent support themselves with wealth.[^8][^9]  ^11-16

**11:17** A merciful man does good to his own soul: but the merciless destroys his own body.  ^11-17

**11:18** An ungodly man performs unrighteous works: but the seed of the righteous is a reward of truth.  ^11-18

**11:19** A righteous son is born for life: but the persecution of the ungodly *ends* in death.  ^11-19

**11:20** Perverse ways are an abomination to the Lord: but all they that are blameless in their ways are acceptable to him.  ^11-20

**11:21** He that unjustly strikes hands shall not be unpunished: but he that sows righteousness shall receive a faithful reward.  ^11-21

**11:22** As an ornament in a swine's snout, so is beauty to an ill-minded woman.  ^11-22

**11:23** All the desire of the righteous is good: but the hope of the ungodly shall perish.  ^11-23

**11:24** There are *some* who scatter their own, and make it more: and there are *some* also who gather, *yet* have less.[^10]  ^11-24

**11:25** Every sincere soul is blessed: but a passionate man is not graceful.  ^11-25

**11:26** May he that hoards corn leave it to the nation: but blessing be on the head of him that gives *it*.  ^11-26

**11:27** He that devises good *counsels* seeks good favour: but *as for* him that seeks after evil, *evil* shall overtake him.  ^11-27

**11:28** He that trusts in wealth shall fall; but he that helps righteous men shall rise.[^11]  ^11-28

**11:29** He that deals not graciously with his own house shall inherit the wind; and the fool shall be servant to the wise man.  ^11-29

**11:30** Out of the fruit of righteousness grows a tree of life; but the souls of transgressors are cut off before their time.  ^11-30

**11:31** If the righteous scarcely be saved, where shall the ungodly and the sinner appear?[^12]  ^11-31

[^1]: 11:2 See Appendix.
[^2]: 11:3 the Alexandrine text reads: “The integrity of the upright shall guide them, but the overthrow of the rebellious shall spoil them.”
[^3]: 11:3 Comp. *Heb.*
[^4]: 11:5 *Gr.* right divides.
[^5]: 11:10 See Appendix which gives the Alexandrine text as: “[note - Part of verse 10.]— but at the destruction of the wicked there is exultation.”
[^6]: 11:13 See chap 20. 27.
[^7]: 11:14 *Or,* governance.
[^8]: 11:16 *Gr.* raises.
[^9]: 11:16 *Gr.* manly.
[^10]: 11:24 *Gr.* diminished.
[^11]: 11:28 See 1 Tim. 5. 8.
[^12]: 11:31 See 1 Pet.
