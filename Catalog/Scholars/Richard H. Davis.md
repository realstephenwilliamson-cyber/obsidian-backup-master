# Richard H. Davis

Professor Emeritus and Research Professor of Religion at Bard College; his teaching and research cover classical and medieval Hinduism, Indian history, South Asian visual arts and Sanskrit, and he is working on the reception history of the Bhagavad Gita ([Bard College](https://bard.edu/faculty/richard-h-davis)).
