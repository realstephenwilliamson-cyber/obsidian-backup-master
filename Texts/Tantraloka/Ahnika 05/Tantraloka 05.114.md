## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 06 - Chapter Five, part 4#^pg168|p. 153]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=168>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 5.114cd–115ab.
>
> Herein this universe merges (lna), and here within it, it is perceived (gamyate). Such is the sign of Bhairava-consciousness in the fullness of its power. (with 5.113cd–114ab)
>
> 2) This (same) Liṁga, manifest-cum-unmanifest, is the vivid penetration (into immanent consciousness) (samāveśa) that takes place as the cosmic paths (of manifestation) unfold within the (microcosmic) body. (This penetration) is associated with objectivity (idaṁbhāva) in an (active) unfolding state, (but is) enveloped in subjectivity (ahantācchādita). (It corresponds to) the intermediate level (parāpara) that is the vitality of Mantra. Unfolding as Man and Śakti, it is distinct from (the Liñga) which is iva. (with 5.114cd–116ab)
