## Mūla

द्वावेव गर्भगाङ्गुष्ठौ विज्ञेया संनिरोधिनी ।
द्रव्यदा तु समाख्याता * * * * त्र संमुखी ॥ ^mula

dvāveva garbhagāṅguṣṭhau vijñeyā saṃnirodhinī |
dravyadā tu samākhyātā * * * * tra saṃmukhī || ^iast

*Dyczkowski prints no Sanskrit for this chapter; the text is that of the KSTS edition (Muktabodha M00160).*

> **21. Rodhā (Fixing)**
>
> The two thumbs placed within (the clenched fists) should be known to be (the gesture used to) fix (the installed deity in place) (*saṃnirodhinī*). (with 7.31ab–31ab)

> **22. Dravyadā (the Offering of the Sacrificial Substances)**
>
> ... (placed) facing before one... is said to be (the gesture of) the offering of the sacrificial substances. (with 7.31cd–31cd)
