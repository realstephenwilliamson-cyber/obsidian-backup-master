## Mūla

मन्त्राणां तृप्तये यागद्रव्यशुद्ध्यै च केवलम् ।
एवं देहं पूजयित्वा प्राणधीशून्यविग्रहान् ॥ ^mula

mantrāṇāṃ tṛptaye yāgadravyaśuddhyai ca kevalam |
evaṃ dehaṃ pūjayitvā prāṇadhīśūnyavigrahān || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 06 - Chapter Fifteen, part 5#^pg191|p. 173]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=191>))
>
> For fear of mixing (these) flowers with the ones already taken for the worship of the sacrificial vessel, one should not place (any) flower here in the sacrificial vessel to worship the deities. Only that liquid (ambu) in the sacrificial vessel, transformed into nectar, is worshipped. And that is only done to propitiate the Mantras and purify the substances required for the sacrifice. (with 15.293cd–295ab)
>
> After worshipping the body in this way, one should worship the form (vigraha) of the vital breath, intellect and the void, (thinking) that they have become one with one another, to (ultimately) see them as one with Śiva. (with 15.295cd–296ab)

## Viveka

> अर्घपात्रार्चनादत्तपुष्पसङ्कीर्णताभयात् ॥
> नार्घपात्रेऽत्र कुसुमं कुर्याद्देवार्चनाकृते ।
> अर्घपात्रे तदमृतीभूतमम्ब्वेव पूजितम् ॥
> मन्त्राणां तृप्तये यागद्रव्यशुद्ध्यै च केवलम् ।
> एवं देहं पूजयित्वा प्राणधीशून्यविग्रहान् ॥
> अन्योन्यतन्मयीभूतान् पूजयेच्छिवतादृशे ।

तदिति--अर्घपात्रे देवाद्यर्चननिमित्तं कुसुमानामकार्यत्वात् । यदुक्तम्- -

"द्रव्यमम्बु समाख्यातं कुलोच्छुष्मादिभेदगम् ।"

इति । अन्योन्यतन्मयीभूतानिति--परस्परावियोगात् ॥
