## Mūla

सर्वथैव प्रकर्तव्यं यथाविभवविस्तरम् ।
वित्ताभावे पुनः कार्यं काशैरपि कुशोम्भितैः ॥ ^mula

sarvathaiva prakartavyaṃ yathāvibhavavistaram |
vittābhāve punaḥ kāryaṃ kāśairapi kuśombhitaiḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 04 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 2#^pg87|p. 70]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=87>))
>
> The (rite of the) sacred thread should be performed fully (sarvathā) once a month, once every four months, or even (just) once a year, according to the extent of (one’s) means. (with 28.142cd–143ab)
>
> The thread may even be made of (just) vegetable fibres or braided with kusa grass if there is no money (for an expensive one). But if there is the money (and the patron says he has none), (his miserly) deceitfulness (will be) the cause of illness (here in this world) and (condemn him) to hell (in the next). (with 28.143cd–144ab)
