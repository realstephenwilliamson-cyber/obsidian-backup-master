## Mūla

शिखायां च क्षिपेत्सूत्रग्रन्थियोगेन दैशिकः ।
तस्यातद्रूपताभानं मलो ग्रन्थिः स कीर्त्यते ॥ ^mula

śikhāyāṃ ca kṣipetsūtragranthiyogena daiśikaḥ |
tasyātadrūpatābhānaṃ malo granthiḥ sa kīrtyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 08 - Chapter Seventeen#^pg159|p. 139]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=159>))
>
> After having grasped in this way the (mutual) pervasion as a unity within the five, namely, the maṇḍala, the jar (kumbha), the fire, the disciple, and his own Self, and deposited the Path onto the disciple, the teacher should cast the three impurities (mala) of Karma, Māyā and the Individual one onto the (disciple’s) arms, throat and topknot (respectively), by linking (yogena) (them to) knots (tied) in a (sacred) thread. (with 17.1cd–3ab)
>
> The Impurity (mala) (of Māyā), which is the manifestation of that (reality) in a form that is other than it is, is called the Knot. In order to strengthen the perception (of it) in this way, outer knots are fashioned accordingly. (with 17.3cd–4ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* एषां च ग्रन्थिरूपतया प्रक्षेपे कोऽभिप्रायः ?--इत्याशङ्क्याह--

> तस्यातद्रूपताभानं मलो ग्रन्थिः स कीर्त्यते ॥
> इतिप्रतीतिदार्ढ्यार्थं बहिर्ग्रन्थ्युपकल्पनम् ।

तस्य पूर्णप्रकाशात्मनः परस्य ब्रह्मणो यदतद्रूपतया भानम् संकुचितात्मतया प्रथनम्, स एव स्वरूपस्य तिरोधायकत्वान्मल इति प्रतिरोधकत्वाच्च ग्रन्थिः--इत्युच्यते । एतदेव द्रढयितुं बहिःपाशसूत्रादावेवं ग्रन्थीनामुपकल्पनम् ॥
