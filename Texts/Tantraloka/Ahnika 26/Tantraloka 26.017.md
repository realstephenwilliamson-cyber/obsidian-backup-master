## Mūla

तच्छास्त्रदीक्षितो ह्येष निर्यन्त्राचारशङ्कितः ।
न मुख्ये योग्य इत्यन्यसेवातः स्यात्तु योग्यता ॥ ^mula

tacchāstradīkṣito hyeṣa niryantrācāraśaṅkitaḥ |
na mukhye yogya ityanyasevātaḥ syāttu yogyatā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 17 - Chapter Twenty-Five (continued) and Chapter Twenty-Six#^pg396|p. 376]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=396>))
>
> (The teacher may assess that the disciple who) has been initiated into a (higher) scripture (śāstra), (but) has doubts (and is inhibited) about (observing) the conduct without restrictions (niryantra), (it teaches) is unfit (to receive and practice) the main (mantra). (If so, the disciple) may (acquire that) competence by practicing other (mantras).
