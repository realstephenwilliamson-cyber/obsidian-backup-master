# Prakāśa and Vimarśa

> [!abstract] Definition
> The foundational non-dual dyad of Kashmir Shaivism: *Prakāśa* (प्रकाश) is the luminous, self-revealing light of pure consciousness; *Vimarśa* (विमर्श) is consciousness's innate self-reflective awareness, dynamism, and freedom (*svātantrya*) to know and manifest itself.

## The Philosophical Structure
In Abhinavagupta’s *Tantrāloka* (Āhnika 1) and Utpaladeva’s *Īśvarapratyabhijñākārikā*:
- If consciousness were merely *prakāśa* (light without reflection), it would be inert and unconscious, like a crystal or mirror that reflects external objects but knows nothing of itself.
- Because it possesses *vimarśa*, consciousness reflects upon its own luminosity (*aham-vimarśa*—the pristine "I"-awareness).
- *Prakāśa* is identified with Śiva (the transcendent silence); *Vimarśa* is identified with Śakti (the creative, pulsating manifestation). They are not two entities, but the undivided nature of reality.
