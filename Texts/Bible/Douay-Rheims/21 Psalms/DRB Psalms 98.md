# Psalms 98 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 97|← Psalms 97]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 99|Psalms 99 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 98|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**98:1** A psalm for David himself. The Lord hath reigned, let the people be angry: he that sitteth on the cherubims: let the earth be moved. ^98-1

**98:2** The Lord is great in Sion, and high above all people. ^98-2

**98:3** Let them give praise to thy great name: for it is terrible and holy: ^98-3

**98:4** And the king’s honour loveth judgment. Thou hast prepared directions: thou hast done judgment and justice in Jacob. ^98-4

**98:5** Exalt ye the Lord our God, and adore his footstool, for it is holy. ^98-5

**98:6** Moses and Aaron among his priests: and Samuel among them that call upon his name. They called upon the Lord, and he heard them: ^98-6

**98:7** He spoke to them in the pillar of the cloud. They kept his testimonies, and the commandment which he gave them. ^98-7

**98:8** Thou didst hear them, O Lord our God: thou wast a merciful God to them, and taking vengeance on all their inventions. ^98-8

**98:9** Exalt ye the Lord our God, and adore at his holy mountain: for the Lord our God is holy. ^98-9
