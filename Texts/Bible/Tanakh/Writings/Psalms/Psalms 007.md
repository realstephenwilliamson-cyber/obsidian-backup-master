## Chapter 7

**7:1** *Shiggaion* of David,[^1] which he sang to the LORD, concerning Cush, a Benjaminite. ^7-1

**7:2** O LORD, my God, in You I seek refuge;  
deliver me from all my pursuers and save me, ^7-2

**7:3** lest, like a lion, they tear me apart,  
rending in pieces, and no one save me. ^7-3

**7:4** O LORD, my God, if I have done such things,  
if my hands bear the guilt of wrongdoing, ^7-4

**7:5** if I have dealt evil to my ally—  
I who rescued my foe without reward[^2]— ^7-5

**7:6** then let the enemy pursue and overtake me;  
let him trample my life to the ground,  
and lay my body in the dust. *Selah*. ^7-6

**7:7** Rise, O LORD, in Your anger;  
assert Yourself against the fury of my foes;[^3]  
bestir Yourself on my behalf;  
You have ordained judgment. ^7-7

**7:8** Let the assembly of peoples gather about You,  
with You enthroned above, on high.[^4] ^7-8

**7:9** The LORD judges the peoples;  
vindicate me, O LORD,  
for the righteousness and blamelessness that are mine. ^7-9

**7:10** Let the evil of the wicked come to an end,  
but establish the righteous;  
he who probes the mind and conscience[^5] is God the righteous. ^7-10

**7:11** I look to God to shield me;[^6]  
the deliverer of the upright. ^7-11

**7:12** God vindicates the righteous;  
God pronounces doom[^7] each day. ^7-12

**7:13** [^8]If one does not turn back, but whets his sword,  
bends his bow and aims it, ^7-13

**7:14** then against himself he readies deadly weapons,  
and makes his arrows sharp. ^7-14

**7:15** See, he hatches evil, conceives mischief,  
and gives birth to fraud. ^7-15

**7:16** He has dug a pit and deepened it,  
and will fall into the trap he made. ^7-16

**7:17** His mischief will recoil upon his own head;  
his lawlessness will come down upon his skull. ^7-17

**7:18** I will praise the LORD for His righteousness,  
and sing a hymn to the name of the LORD Most High. ^7-18

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain; others “or stripped my foe clean.”
[^3]: Or “in Your fury against my foes.”
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^5]: Lit. “kidneys.”
[^6]: Cf. Ibn Ezra and Kimhi; lit. “My Shield is upon God.”
[^7]: Others “has indignation.”
[^8]: Meaning of vv. 13–14 uncertain; an alternate rendering, with God as the main subject, is: 13If one does not turn back, He whets His sword, / bends His bow and aims it; / 14deadly weapons He prepares for him, / and makes His arrows sharp.
