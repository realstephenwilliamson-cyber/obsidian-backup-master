# Psalm 144

← [[BCP Psalm 143]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 145]] →

*Benedictus Dominus* · Book Five · Thirtieth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 144]]

**144:1** Blessed be the LORD my rock! \*  
who trains my hands to fight and my fingers to battle; ^144-1

**144:2** My help and my fortress, my stronghold and my deliverer, \*  
my shield in whom I trust,  
who subdues the peoples under me. ^144-2

**144:3** O LORD, what are we that you should care for us? \*  
mere mortals that you should think of us? ^144-3

**144:4** We are like a puff of wind; \*  
our days are like a passing shadow. ^144-4

**144:5** Bow your heavens, O LORD, and come down; \*  
touch the mountains, and they shall smoke. ^144-5

**144:6** Hurl the lightning and scatter them; \*  
shoot out your arrows and rout them. ^144-6

**144:7** Stretch out your hand from on high; \*  
rescue me and deliver me from the great waters,  
from the hand of foreign peoples, ^144-7

**144:8** Whose mouths speak deceitfully \*  
and whose right hand is raised in falsehood. ^144-8

**144:9** O God, I will sing to you a new song; \*  
I will play to you on a ten-stringed lyre. ^144-9

**144:10** You give victory to kings \*  
and have rescued David your servant. ^144-10

**144:11** Rescue me from the hurtful sword \*  
and deliver me from the hand of foreign peoples, ^144-11

**144:12** Whose mouths speak deceitfully \*  
and whose right hand is raised in falsehood. ^144-12

[p. 801] ^pg801

**144:13** May our sons be like plants well nurtured from their youth, \*  
and our daughters like sculptured corners of a palace. ^144-13

**144:14** May our barns be filled to overflowing with all manner of crops; \*  
may the flocks in our pastures increase by thousands and tens of thousands;  
may our cattle be fat and sleek. ^144-14

**144:15** May there be no breaching of the walls, no going into exile, \*  
no wailing in the public squares. ^144-15

**144:16** Happy are the people of whom this is so! \*  
happy are the people whose God is the LORD! ^144-16
