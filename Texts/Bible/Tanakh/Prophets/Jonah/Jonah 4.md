# Jonah 4

← [[Jonah 3]] · [[Tanakh]]

Compare: [[NRSVue Jonah 04|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 4

**4:1** This displeased Jonah greatly, and he was grieved. ^4-1

**4:2** He prayed to the LORD, saying, “O LORD! Isn’t this just what I said when I was still in my own country? That is why I fled beforehand to Tarshish. For I know that You are a compassionate and gracious God, slow to anger, abounding in kindness, renouncing punishment. ^4-2

**4:3** Please, LORD, take my life, for I would rather die than live.” ^4-3

**4:4** The LORD replied, “Are you that deeply grieved?” ^4-4

**4:5** Now Jonah had left the city and found a place east of the city. He made a booth there and sat under it in the shade, until he should see what happened to the city. ^4-5

**4:6** The LORD God provided a ricinus plant,[^1] which grew up over Jonah, to provide shade for his head and save him from discomfort. Jonah was very happy about the plant. ^4-6

**4:7** But the next day at dawn God provided a worm, which attacked the plant so that it withered. ^4-7

**4:8** And when the sun rose, God provided a sultry[^2] east wind; the sun beat down on Jonah’s head, and he became faint. He begged for death, saying, “I would rather die than live.” ^4-8

**4:9** Then God said to Jonah, “Are you so deeply grieved about the plant?” “Yes,” he replied, “so deeply that I want to die.” ^4-9

**4:10** Then the LORD said: “You cared about the plant, which you did not work for and which you did not grow, which appeared overnight and perished overnight. ^4-10

**4:11** And should not I care about Nineveh, that great city, in which there are more than a hundred and twenty thousand persons who do not yet know their right hand from their left, and many beasts as well!”[^3] ^4-11

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain; others “gourd.”
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Infants and beasts are not held responsible for their actions.
