# Psalms 149 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 148|← Psalms 148]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 150|Psalms 150 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 149|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**149:1** Sing ye to the Lord a new canticle: let his praise be in the church of the saints. ^149-1

**149:2** Let Israel rejoice in him that made him: and let the children of Sion be joyful in their king. ^149-2

**149:3** Let them praise his name in choir: let them sing to him with the timbrel and the psaltery. ^149-3

**149:4** For the Lord is well pleased with his people: and he will exalt the meek unto salvation. ^149-4

**149:5** The saints shall rejoice in glory: they shall be joyful in their beds. ^149-5

**149:6** The high praise of God shall be in their mouth: and two-edged swords in their hands: ^149-6

**149:7** To execute vengeance upon the nations, chastisements among the people: ^149-7

**149:8** To bind their kings with fetters, and their nobles with manacles of iron. ^149-8

**149:9** To execute upon them the judgment that is written: this glory is to all his saints. Alleluia. ^149-9
