## Mūla

गुरुश्चात्र निरोधाख्ये काल इत्थं विभौ वदेत् ।
जीवत्यस्मिन्फलान्तं त्वं तिष्ठेर्जीवावधीति वा ॥ ^mula

guruścātra nirodhākhye kāla itthaṃ vibhau vadet |
jīvatyasminphalāntaṃ tvaṃ tiṣṭherjīvāvadhīti vā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 18 - Chapter Twenty-Seven#^pg425|p. 405]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=425>))
>
> Here (in that case), at the time when the (deity) is detained (in the icon) (nirodha), the teacher should say before the pervasive Lord: “As long as he lives, may you abide (here), until (he obtains) the fruit (he desires), or until the end of (his) life.”
