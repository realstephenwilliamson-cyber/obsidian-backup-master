## Mūla

पुराध्वनि हुतीनां या संख्येयं तत्त्ववर्णयोः ।
तामेव द्विगुणीकुर्यात्पदाध्वनि चतुर्गुणाम् ॥ ^mula

purādhvani hutīnāṃ yā saṃkhyeyaṃ tattvavarṇayoḥ |
tāmeva dviguṇīkuryātpadādhvani caturguṇām || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 08 - Chapter Seventeen#^pg210|p. 190]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=210>))
>
> The number of oblations required for the Path of the Worlds should be doubled for the Path of the Principles and the Phonemes. It should be four times the number for the Path of the Parts of Mantra (pada), double that (i.e. eight) and then double that again (i.e. sixteen) for the Paths of Mantras and the Forces, respectively. For the purification of the three principles, they should be multiplied by twenty. Thus for (all) three principles, there are sixty oblations for each rite. They say that for the one principle, the oblations should be one hundred and eight. (with 17.118–120)
