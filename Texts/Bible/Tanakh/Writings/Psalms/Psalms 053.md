## Chapter 53

**53:1** [^1]For the leader; on *mahalath*.[^2] A *maskil* of David. ^53-1

**53:2** The benighted man thinks,  
“God does not care.”[^3]  
Man’s wrongdoing is corrupt and loathsome;  
no one does good. ^53-2

**53:3** God looks down from heaven on mankind  
to find a man of understanding,  
a man mindful of God. ^53-3

**53:4** Everyone is dross,  
altogether foul;  
there is none who does good,  
not even one. ^53-4

**53:5** Are they so witless, those evildoers,  
who devour my people as they devour food,  
and do not invoke God? ^53-5

**53:6** There they will be seized with fright  
—never was there such a fright—  
for God has scattered the bones of your besiegers;  
you have put them to shame,[^4]  
for God has rejected them. ^53-6

**53:7** O that the deliverance of Israel might come from Zion!  
When God restores the fortunes of His people,  
Jacob will exult, Israel will rejoice. ^53-7

[^1]: Cf. Ps. 14.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. unknown.
[^3]: Lit. “There is no God”; cf. Ps. 10.4.
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
