## Mūla

संहृत्यैकैकमिष्टिर्या सान्त्येष्टिर्द्वितयी मता ।

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 15 - Chapter Twenty-Four#^pg366|p. 346]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=366>))
>
> We will now describe the ritual procedure (vidhi) taught in the venerable Siddhātantra. (There we read): ‘(In this rite, the teacher utters) one by one, withdrawing (in the reverse order), the last (mantra), having made it first, and the first, last. (Thus,) the sacrifice (iṣṭi) which is such is the ‘the last sacrifice’ (antyeṣṭi), which is considered to be of the second (kind). O beloved, (this rite) is not (to be performed) for an adept (sādhaka) who has burnt away the (obligation to observe the) Rule by (regular) worship (pūjā), meditation (dhyāna) and the repetition (of Mantra) (japa). (For) by (his) death, this (one) (ayam) is liberated or becomes a Skyfarer. Nor should the last rites (be performed) for a teacher who possesses the truth, and who, wherever he may die, (attains a state of) pure consciousness and (so) has no head.’ (with 24.6–9ab)
