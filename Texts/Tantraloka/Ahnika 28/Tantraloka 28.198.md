## Mūla

तत्रेत्थं प्राग्यदा पश्येच्छक्त्युन्मीलितदृक्क्रियः ।
देहस्तावदयं पूर्वपूर्वोपादाननिर्मितः ॥ ^mula

tatretthaṃ prāgyadā paśyecchaktyunmīlitadṛkkriyaḥ |
dehastāvadayaṃ pūrvapūrvopādānanirmitaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 05 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 3#^pg107|p. 90]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=107>))
>
> (This detachment from the body takes place) there (in that case), if one whose (senses) of knowledge and action have been opened up by the power (of consciousness) sees in this way, right from the beginning (of each act of perception). (with 28.198ab)
>
> (A person’s) body is fashioned from a preceding succession of material causes (that is, the bodies of his ancestors), (whereas) the Self is free of alteration (vikāra) and, because it is eternal, has no (preceding) cause. (with 28.198cd–199ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* ननु अस्य कथं देहादावहम्भाव एव भ्रश्येत्, येन तत्र अनास्थापि स्यात् ? --इत्याशङ्क्य आह--

> तत्रेत्थं प्राग्यदा पश्येच्छक्त्युन्मीलितदृक्किर्यः ।

इत्थमिति--वक्ष्यमाणेन प्रकारेण ॥

तदेव आह--

> देहस्तावदयं पूर्वपूर्वोपादाननिर्मितः ॥
> आत्मा विकाररहितः शाश्वतत्वादहेतुकः ।

पूर्वपूर्वेति--पितृपितामहादयः, अत एव कृतकत्वाद्विनश्वरः ॥
