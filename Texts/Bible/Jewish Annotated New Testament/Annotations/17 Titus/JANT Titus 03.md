# Titus 3 (JANT notes)

[[JANT Titus 02|← Titus 2]] · [[Jewish Annotated New Testament|JANT index]] · —

Text: [[NRSVue Titus 03|NRSVue]] · [[Titus 03|BSB and SBLGNT]] · Introduction: [[JANT Titus - Introduction]]

**[[NRSVue Titus 03#^3-1|3.1]]:** *Rulers and authorities*, Roman governmental leaders; a warning against civil disobedience (see [[Romans 13#^13-1|Rom 13.1–7]]; [[1 Timothy 02#^2-1|1 Tim 2.1–2]]; [[1 Peter 02#^2-13|1 Pet 2.13–14]]). ^3-1

**[[NRSVue Titus 03#^3-3|3]]:** These vices are associated with Gentiles (see [[Romans 01#^1-29|Rom 1.29–31]]). ^3-3

**[[NRSVue Titus 03#^3-4|4–7]]:** A single sentence in Greek, perhaps a baptismal formula. ^3-4

**[[NRSVue Titus 03#^3-5|5]]:** *Saved us*, God’s salvation, indicated by Christ’s actions and the believer’s moral transformation (3.3), is a completed action; by contrast, in Paul’s undisputed epistles, salvation is completed by future resurrection. For salvation as moral conversion, see Philo (*Rewards* 163). *Not because of any works* reflects Pauline language ([[Romans 03#^3-28|Rom 3.28]]; [[Galatians 02#^2-16|Gal 2.16]]; see also [[Torah 46 - Eikev#^9-5|Deut 9.5]]), where Paul specifically refers to “works of the law.” *Mercy* (Gk “eleos”), a central attribute of the divine in Jewish Scriptures (see [[Torah 21 - Ki Tisa#^34-6|Ex 34.6–7]]; [[Torah 37 - Sh'lach#^14-18|Num 14.18]]; [[Psalms 086#^86-5|Ps 86.5]],[[Psalms 086#^86-15|15]] \[LXX 85.5,15\]) along with love and compassion (Heb “ḥesed”). *Water of rebirth and renewal by the Holy Spirit*, baptism. *Rebirth and renewal* connect God’s past act of creation to God’s present act of the transformation of the believer’s heart and to God’s future re-creation or bodily resurrection. See 1QH 11–12, which also connects creation to resurrection, praising God’s power and compassion. *The Holy Spirit*, God at work in the world; the Greek lacks the definite article. ^3-5

**[[NRSVue Titus 03#^3-7|7]]:** *Justified*, brought into right relationship with God. Paul stresses justification outside of the works of the law (see [[Romans 03#^3-24|Rom 3.24]]; [[Galatians 02#^2-16|Gal 2.16]]; cf. [[James 02#^2-21|Jas 2.21–25]]). ^3-7

### 3.8b–11: Community controversies

*[[NRSVue Titus 03#^3-8|Text]], p. 453* ^3-8

**[[NRSVue Titus 03#^3-8|8]]:** *Good works*, see 2.7n.; 3.5n. ^3-8-2

**[[NRSVue Titus 03#^3-9|9]]:** See [[1 Timothy 01#^1-4|1 Tim 1.4]],[[1 Timothy 01#^1-6|6–7]]. *Genealogies*, either generational lists in the Jewish Scriptures or genealogies of Jesus (Gen 10; 1 Chr 1–9; [[Matthew 01#^1-1|Mt 1.1–17]]; [[Luke 03#^3-23|Lk 3.23–38]]). *Quarrels about the law*, debates about proper interpretation and application of biblical law (see 1.14n.). ^3-9

**[[NRSVue Titus 03#^3-10|10]]:** *Admonition*, [[Matthew 18#^18-15|Mt 18.15–17]] and the DSS (1QS and CD) enjoin similar methods for addressing disputes. ^3-10

### 3.12–15: Farewell

*[[NRSVue Titus 03#^3-12|Text]], p. 454* ^3-12

**[[NRSVue Titus 03#^3-12|12]]:** *Artemas*, otherwise unknown. *Tychicus*, Paul’s companion ([[Acts 20#^20-4|Acts 20.4]]; [[Ephesians 06#^6-21|Eph 6.21]]; [[Colossians 04#^4-7|Col 4.7–9]]; [[2 Timothy 04#^4-12|2 Tim 4.12]]). *Nicopolis*, city on Greece’s western coast. ^3-12-2

**[[NRSVue Titus 03#^3-13|13]]:** *Zenas*, otherwise unknown; *the lawyer* (“nomikos”) refers to an esxpert in Jewish law in [[Matthew 22#^22-35|Mt 22.35]] and Lk (see 7.30; 10.25). *Apollos*, see [[Acts 18#^18-24|Acts 18.24–28]]; [[1 Corinthians 03#^3-4|1 Cor 3.4–6]]; [[1 Corinthians 16#^16-12|16.12]]. ^3-13

**[[NRSVue Titus 03#^3-14|14]]:** *Good works*, see 3.5n. ^3-14

**[[NRSVue Titus 03#^3-15|15]]:** One of the shortest farewell formulas in the Pauline letters. ^3-15
