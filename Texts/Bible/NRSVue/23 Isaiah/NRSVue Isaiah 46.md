# Isaiah 46 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Isaiah 45|← Isaiah 45]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Isaiah 47|Isaiah 47 →]]

**46:1** Bel bows down; Nebo stoops; their idols are on beasts and cattle; these things you carry are loaded as burdens on weary animals. ^46-1

**46:2** They stoop; they bow down together; they cannot save the burden but themselves go into captivity. ^46-2

**46:3** Listen to me, O house of Jacob, all the remnant of the house of Israel, who have been borne by me from your birth, carried from the womb; ^46-3

**46:4** even to your old age I am he; even when you turn gray I will carry you. I have made, and I will bear; I will carry and will save. ^46-4

**46:5** To whom will you liken me and make me equal and compare me, as though we were alike? ^46-5

**46:6** Those who lavish gold from the purse and weigh out silver in the scales—they hire a goldsmith, who makes it into a god; then they fall down and worship! ^46-6

**46:7** They lift it to their shoulders; they carry it; they set it in its place, and it stands there; it cannot move from its place. If one cries out to it, it does not answer or save anyone from trouble. ^46-7

**46:8** Remember this and consider;^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] recall it to mind, you transgressors; ^46-8

**46:9** remember the former things of old, for I am God, and there is no other; I am God, and there is no one like me, ^46-9

**46:10** declaring the outcome from the beginning and from ancient times things not yet done, saying, “My purpose shall stand, and I will fulfill my intention,” ^46-10

**46:11** calling a bird of prey from the east, the man for my purpose from a far country. I have spoken, and I will bring it to pass; I have planned, and I will do it. ^46-11

**46:12** Listen to me, you stubborn of heart, you who are far from deliverance: ^46-12

**46:13** I bring near my deliverance; it is not far off, and my salvation will not tarry; I will put salvation in Zion, for Israel my glory. ^46-13
