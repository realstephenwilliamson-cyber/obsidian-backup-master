# Zohar 3.038a

← [[Zohar 3.037b]] · [[Zohar (Soncino)]] · [[Zohar 3.038b]] →

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 7.05 - Parashat Shemini#^z3-38a|Parashat Shemini, at 3:38a]]

*Volume 3, folio 38a* · Shmini

**Shmini 4:41** For the Community of Israel is blessed by the priest and the Israelite is blessed by the priest, and the priest is blessed by the Supernal Priest, as it is said: “And they shall put my name upon the children of Israel and I will bless them” (Num. 6, 27). ^shmini-4-41

**Shmini 4:42** It is written: “Remember, O Lord, thy tender mercies and thy lovingkindnesses, for they have been ever of old” (*me’olam*, lit. from the world, Ps. 25, 6). “Mercies” here refers to Jacob and “lovingkindnessess’ to Abraham. God takes them from the world and raises them aloft, and makes of them a holy Chariot to shield the world. So God does with all the righteous. ^shmini-4-42

**Shmini 4:43** It may be asked, why is there no reference to Isaac here? He was left over to punish those who oppress his offspring, and is referred to in the verse, “Arouse thy might” (Ps. 80, 3); and again, “The Lord shall go forth as a mighty man, he shall stir up jealousy like a man of war” (Isa. 42, 13). R. Hiya said: ‘Jacob and Abraham we require to shield us, but Isaac’s function is to make war, and therefore we do not desire him to be associated with them. ^shmini-4-43

**Shmini 4:44** We may also explain the words “for they are from the world” thus. When God created the world He took Isaac and created with him the world. He saw that it would not be able to endure, so He supported it with Abraham. He saw that it needed further support, so He took Jacob and joined him with Isaac. Hence the world is supported by Abraham and Jacob, and they are “from the world”.’[^1] ^shmini-4-44

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Shmini 4:45–4:49.*

[^1]: The text here inserts a passage which in substance is merely a repetition of p. 37a, “R. Eleazar discoursed…” to p. 37b, “something else”.
