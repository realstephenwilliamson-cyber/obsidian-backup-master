## Chapter 11

**11:1** I fell in love with Israel  
When he was still a child;  
And I have called [him] My son  
Ever since Egypt. ^11-1

**11:2** [^1]Thus were they called,  
But they went their own way;  
They sacrifice to Baalim[^2]  
And offer to carved images. ^11-2

**11:3** I have pampered Ephraim,  
Taking them in My[^3] arms;  
But they have ignored  
My healing care. ^11-3

**11:4** I drew them with human ties,  
With cords of love;  
But I seemed to them as one  
Who imposed a yoke on their jaws,  
Though I was offering them food. ^11-4

**11:5** No!  
They return to the land of Egypt,  
And Assyria is their king.  
Because they refuse to repent, ^11-5

**11:6** A sword shall descend upon their towns[^4]  
And consume their limbs  
And devour [them] because of their designs.[^5] ^11-6

**11:7** For My people persists  
In its defection from Me;  
When it is summoned upward,  
It does not rise at all.[^6] ^11-7

**11:8** How can I give you up, O Ephraim?  
How surrender you, O Israel?  
How can I make you like Admah,  
Render you like Zeboiim?[^7]  
I have had a change of heart,  
All My tenderness is stirred. ^11-8

**11:9** I will not act on My wrath,  
Will not turn to destroy Ephraim.  
For I am God, not man,  
The Holy One in your midst:  
I will not come in fury.[^8] ^11-9

**11:10** The LORD will roar like a lion,  
And they shall march behind Him;  
When He roars, His children shall come  
Fluttering out of the west. ^11-10

**11:11** They shall flutter from Egypt like sparrows,  
From the land of Assyria like doves;  
And I will settle them in their homes  
—declares the LORD. ^11-11

[^1]: Meaning of parts of vv. 2–7 uncertain.
[^2]: Emendation yields “calves”; cf. 8.4–6; 13.2.
[^3]: Heb. “his.”
[^4]: Emendation yields “bodies,” lit. “skins”; cf. Job. 18.13.
[^5]: Emendation yields “their bones.”
[^6]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^7]: Admah and Zeboiim were destroyed with neighboring Sodom and Gomorrah; cf. Gen. 10.19; 14.2, 8; Deut. 29.22.
[^8]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
