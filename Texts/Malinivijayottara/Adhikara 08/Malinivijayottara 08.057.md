## Mūla

लम्बिकावधितश्चात्र शूलोर्ध्वं ग्रन्थिरिष्यते ।
अभित्त्वैनं महादेवि पाशजालमहार्णवम् ॥ ^mula

lambikāvadhitaś cātra śūlordhvaṁ granthir iṣyate |
abhittvainaṁ mahādevi pāśajālamahārṇavam || ^iast

> **The Shaft of the Trident**
>
> Then (beyond that) one should imagine the channel which is the staff of that (Trident) called Ananta that extends up to the uvula. There is said to be a knot above the staff.[^62] O great Goddess, without piercing through (that) great ocean of nets and fetters, a man does not attain union (*yoga*) with Śiva. (with 8.56cd–58ab)

[^62]: Read *nālordhvaṁ* for *śūlordhvaṁ*.
