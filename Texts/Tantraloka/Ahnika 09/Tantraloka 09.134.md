## Mūla

तथापि ज्ञानकाले तत्सर्वमेव प्रदह्यते ।
उक्तं च श्रीपरेऽहानादानः सर्वदृगुल्वणः ॥ ^mula

tathāpi jñānakāle tatsarvameva pradahyate |
uktaṃ ca śrīpare'hānādānaḥ sarvadṛgulvaṇaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 05 - Chapter Nine, part 3#^pg133|p. 116]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=133>))
>
> Thus, whatever one who is under the sway of delusion has (done or) failed to do, is all burnt away, when insight (dawns) (jñānakāle). (with 9.133cd–134ab)
>
> It is said in the venerable Mālinīviyajottara that he who sees all things and does not take up or reject (anything) and is powerful, should burn up (in an auspicious) moment all (the residual traces of what) he has done (that he should not have done) and what he has not done (that he should have done) present in the body. (with 9.134cd–135ab)

*Lead-in:* किमत्र प्रमाणम् ? -- इत्याशङ्क्याह --

> उक्तं च श्रीपरेऽहानादानः सर्वदृगुल्वणः ॥
> मुहूर्तान्निर्दहेत्सर्वं देहस्थमकृतं कृतम् ।

इह कश्चित् सर्वदृक् -- सर्वमेव संविद्रूपतया जानानः, अत एव विगलितभेदत्वात् अहानादानः, अत एवोल्वणः -- त्यक्तहेयोपादेयविभागः, क्षणादेव सर्वं दुष्कृतं सुकृतं च देहस्थं निर्दहेत् -- ज्ञानाग्निसात् कुर्यात् -- इत्यर्थः ॥ १३४ ॥
