## Mūla

नदीनगह्रदप्रायं यच्च पुण्यं न तन्मृतौ ।
उत्कृष्टं तन्मृतानां तु स्वर्गभोगोपभोगिता ॥ ^mula

nadīnagahradaprāyaṃ yacca puṇyaṃ na tanmṛtau |
utkṛṣṭaṃ tanmṛtānāṃ tu svargabhogopabhogitā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 05 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 3#^pg137|p. 120]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=137>))
>
> (Sacred) rivers, mountains, lakes and the like are not the best (places) to die. Those who die in those (places merely) experience the enjoyments of heaven. Indeed, those who, (although they) have attained the discernment of insight, (but) on the point of death pin their hope (āsthā) on inferior sacred places (āyatana), are here (considered to be) obscured (by ignorance). (with 28.277–278)
