# Proverbs 1 (NRSVue)

— · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Proverbs 02|Proverbs 2 →]]

**1:1** The proverbs of Solomon son of David, king of Israel: ^1-1

## Prologue

**1:2** For learning about wisdom and instruction, for understanding words of insight, ^1-2

**1:3** for gaining instruction in wise dealing, righteousness, justice, and equity; ^1-3

**1:4** to teach shrewdness to the simple, knowledge and prudence to the young— ^1-4

**1:5** let the wise, too, hear and gain in learning and the discerning acquire skill, ^1-5

**1:6** to understand a proverb and a figure, the words of the wise and their riddles. ^1-6

**1:7** The fear of the LORD is the beginning of knowledge; fools despise wisdom and instruction. ^1-7

## Warnings against evil companions

**1:8** Hear, my child, your father’s instruction, and do not reject your mother’s teaching, ^1-8

**1:9** for they are a fair garland for your head and pendants for your neck. ^1-9

**1:10** My child, if sinners entice you, do not consent. ^1-10

**1:11** If they say, “Come with us, let us lie in wait for blood; let us wantonly ambush the innocent; ^1-11

**1:12** like Sheol let us swallow them alive and whole, like those who go down to the Pit. ^1-12

**1:13** We shall find all kinds of costly things; we shall fill our houses with spoil. ^1-13

**1:14** Throw in your lot among us; we will all have one purse”— ^1-14

**1:15** my child, do not walk in their way; keep your foot from their paths, ^1-15

**1:16** for their feet run to evil, and they hurry to shed blood. ^1-16

**1:17** For in vain is the net baited while the bird is looking on; ^1-17

**1:18** yet they lie in wait—to kill themselves! and set an ambush—for their own lives! ^1-18

**1:19** Such is the end^[Gk: Heb *are the ways*] of all who are greedy for gain; it takes away the life of its possessors. ^1-19

## The call of wisdom

**1:20** Wisdom cries out in the street; in the squares she raises her voice. ^1-20

**1:21** At the busiest corner she cries out; at the entrance of the city gates she speaks: ^1-21

**1:22** “How long, O simple ones, will you love being simple? How long will scoffers delight in their scoffing and fools hate knowledge? ^1-22

**1:23** Give heed to my reproof; I will pour out my thoughts to you; I will make my words known to you. ^1-23

**1:24** Because I have called and you refused, have stretched out my hand and no one heeded, ^1-24

**1:25** and because you have ignored all my counsel and would have none of my reproof, ^1-25

**1:26** I also will laugh at your calamity; I will mock when panic strikes you, ^1-26

**1:27** when panic strikes you like a storm and your calamity comes like a whirlwind, when distress and anguish come upon you. ^1-27

**1:28** Then they will call upon me, but I will not answer; they will seek me diligently but will not find me. ^1-28

**1:29** Because they hated knowledge and did not choose the fear of the LORD, ^1-29

**1:30** would have none of my counsel and despised all my reproof, ^1-30

**1:31** therefore they shall eat the fruit of their way and be sated with their own devices. ^1-31

**1:32** For waywardness kills the simple, and the complacency of fools destroys them; ^1-32

**1:33** but those who listen to me will be secure and will live at ease without dread of disaster.” ^1-33
