# Song of Songs 2

## Chapter 2

**2:1** I am a rose[^1] of Sharon,  
A lily of the valleys. ^2-1

**2:2** Like a lily among thorns,  
So is my darling among the maidens. ^2-2

**2:3** Like an apple tree among trees of the forest,  
So is my beloved among the youths.  
I delight to sit in his shade,  
And his fruit is sweet to my mouth. ^2-3

**2:4** He brought me to the banquet room  
And his banner of love was over me.[^2] ^2-4

**2:5** “Sustain me with raisin cakes,  
Refresh me with apples,  
For I am faint with love.” ^2-5

**2:6** His left hand was under my head,  
His right arm embraced me. ^2-6

**2:7** I adjure you, O maidens of Jerusalem,  
By gazelles or by hinds of the field:  
Do not wake or rouse  
Love until it please! ^2-7

**2:8** Hark! My beloved!  
There he comes,  
Leaping over mountains,  
Bounding over hills. ^2-8

**2:9** My beloved is like a gazelle  
Or like a young stag.  
There he stands behind our wall,  
Gazing through the window,  
Peering through the lattice. ^2-9

**2:10** My beloved spoke thus to me,  
“Arise, my darling;  
My fair one, come away! ^2-10

**2:11** For now the winter is past,  
The rains are over and gone. ^2-11

**2:12** The blossoms have appeared in the land,  
The time of pruning[^3] has come;  
The song of the turtledove  
Is heard in our land. ^2-12

**2:13** The green figs form on the fig tree,  
The vines in blossom give off fragrance.  
Arise, my darling;  
My fair one, come away! ^2-13

**2:14** “O my dove, in the cranny of the rocks,  
Hidden by the cliff,  
Let me see your face,  
Let me hear your voice;  
For your voice is sweet  
And your face is comely.” ^2-14

**2:15** Catch us the foxes,  
The little foxes  
That ruin the vineyards—  
For our vineyard is in blossom. ^2-15

**2:16** My beloved is mine  
And I am his  
Who browses among the lilies. ^2-16

**2:17** When the day blows gently[^4]  
And the shadows flee,[^5]  
Set out, my beloved,  
Swift as a gazelle  
Or a young stag,  
For the hills of spices![^6] ^2-17

[^1]: Lit. “crocus.”
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Or “singing.”
[^4]: Emendation yields “declines”; cf. Jer. 6.4.
[^5]: Septuagint reads “lengthen”; cf. Jer. 6.4.
[^6]: Heb. bather of uncertain meaning; 8.14 reads besamim, “spices.”
