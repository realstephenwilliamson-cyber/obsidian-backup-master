## Mūla

शक्तिमच्छक्तिभेदेन धरातत्त्वम्विभिद्यते ।
स्वरूपसहितम्तच्च विज्ञेयम्दशपञ्चधा  ॥ ^mula

śaktimacchaktibhedena dharātattvam vibhidyate |
svarūpasahitam tac ca vijñeyam daśapañcadhā[^14] || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, July 2022 – September 2022
> p. 28 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Malini (journal), July-September 2022.pdf#page=30>))
>
> **The Division of the Principles into Fifteen Divisions**
>
> The Earth principle is differentiated by the division of (seven) energies (śakti) and (seven) power-holders (śaktimat), and (so), along with its own nature (svarūpa), it should be known to be fifteen-fold.[^15]

[^14]: 1 to 185 of chapter ten of the *Tantrāloka* and chapter eleven of the *Tantrasāra* are dedicated to the teachings concerning the ascending series of seven perceivers and related matters taught in MV 2/1-46ab. MV 2/2 and 4- 7ab, quoted in TĀv ad 10/3-5, which is a condensed paraphrase of MV 2/2-7ab. TĀ 10/103-121 is a detailed reasoned explanation of MV 2/1-7ab, where the gradation from 15 to 1 units in relation to the seven perceivers is outlined.
[^15]: MV 2/2 is also quoted in TĀv ad 1/196.
