# ὀφειλέτης

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · noun

**Dodson:** a debtor, sinner — (a) a debtor, one who owes, one who is indebted, (b) one who has sinned against another (an Aramaism), a sinner. (Strong's 3781; headword ὀφειλέτης, ου, ὁ)
