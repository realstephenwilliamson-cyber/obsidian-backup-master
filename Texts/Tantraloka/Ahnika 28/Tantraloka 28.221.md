## Mūla

प्रेर्यमाणो विचरति भस्त्रायन्त्रगवायुवत् ।
अतः प्राग्गाढसंसुप्तोत्थितवत्स प्रबुद्ध्यते ॥ ^mula

preryamāṇo vicarati bhastrāyantragavāyuvat |
ataḥ prāggāḍhasaṃsuptotthitavatsa prabuddhyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 05 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 3#^pg116|p. 99]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=116>))
>
> This (assumes) the nature of the (vital) wind that circulates within the (now) solid machine of the body, impelled (preryamāṇa) by the Self, which is consciousness, as does air within bellows (impelled by the one who uses them). Thus that (foetus) awakens, like one who, initially in (very) deep sleep, rises from it. (with 28.220cd–221)
