## Mūla

वर्णशब्देन नीलादि यद्वा दीक्षोत्तरे यथा ।
संहारन्रग्निमरुतो रुद्रबिन्दुयुतान्स्मरेत् ॥ ^mula

varṇaśabdena nīlādi yadvā dīkṣottare yathā |
saṃhāranragnimaruto rudrabinduyutānsmaret || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 07 - Chapter Five, part 5#^pg216|p. 201]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=216>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 5.149.
>
> Or else, as in the Dīkṣottara, by the word ‘varṇa’ (is meant ‘a colour’) such as blue (it does not mean ‘a phoneme’). (with 5.148ab)
>
> (The yogi) should recollect in his heart (the letters of) Withdrawal (KṢ), Man (M), Fire (R) and Wind (Y) in union with Rudra (Ū) and the Point (Ṁ). In seven days he should see the object (of his meditation), awakened by the lamp of the seed-syllable, that burns intensely in the lotus of the Heart like a scintillating fire, variegated (with various colours) such as blue, yellow and red. The Point lit (by this flame) shines like a lamp or the (summer) sun unobscured by clouds. (with 5.148cd–150)
