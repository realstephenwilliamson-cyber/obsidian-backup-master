# LXX Proverbs 12

← [[LXX Proverbs 11]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Proverbs 13]] →

*Proverbs · [[LXX]]*

**12:1** He that loves instruction loves sense, but he that hates reproofs is a fool.  ^12-1

**12:2** He that has found favour with the Lord *is made* better; but a transgressor shall be passed over in silence.[^1]  ^12-2

**12:3** A man shall not prosper by wickedness; but the roots of the righteous shall not be taken up.  ^12-3

**12:4** A virtuous woman is a crown to her husband; but as a worm in wood, so a bad woman destroys her husband.  ^12-4

**12:5** The thoughts of the righteous *are true* judgments; but ungodly men devise deceits.  ^12-5

**12:6** The words of ungodly men are crafty; but the mouth of the upright shall deliver them.  ^12-6

**12:7** When the ungodly is overthrown, he vanishes away; but the houses of the just remain.  ^12-7

**12:8** The mouth of an understanding *man* is praised by a man; but he that is dull of heart is had in derision.  ^12-8

**12:9** Better is a man in dishonour serving himself, than one honouring himself and wanting bread.  ^12-9

**12:10** A righteous man has pity for the lives of his cattle; but the bowels of the ungodly are unmerciful.  ^12-10

**12:11** He that tills his own land shall be satisfied with bread; but they that pursue vanities are void of understanding. 11a He that enjoys himself in banquets of wine, shall leave dishonour in his own strong holds.  ^12-11

**12:12** The desires of the ungodly are evil; but the roots of the godly are firmly set.  ^12-12

**12:13** For the sin of *his* lips a sinner falls into snares; but a righteous man escapes from them. 13a He whose looks are gentle shall be pitied, but he that contends in the gates will afflict souls.  ^12-13

**12:14** The soul of a man shall be filled with good from the fruits of his mouth; and the recompence of his lips shall be given to him.  ^12-14

**12:15** The ways of fools are right in their own eyes; but a wise man hearkens to counsels.  ^12-15

**12:16** A fool declares his wrath the same day; but a prudent man hides his own disgrace.  ^12-16

**12:17** A righteous man declares the open truth; but an unjust witness is deceitful.  ^12-17

**12:18** Some wound as they speak, *like* swords; but the tongues of the wise heal.  ^12-18

**12:19** True lips establish testimony; but a hasty witness has an unjust tongue.  ^12-19

**12:20** *There is* deceit in the heart of him that imagines evil; but they that love peace shall rejoice.  ^12-20

**12:21** No injustice will please a just man; but the ungodly will be filled with mischief.  ^12-21

**12:22** Lying lips are an abomination to the Lord; but he that deals faithfully is accepted with him.  ^12-22

**12:23** An understanding man is a throne of wisdom; but the heart of fools shall meet with curses.  ^12-23

**12:24** The hand of chosen men shall easily obtain rule; but the deceitful shall be for a prey.  ^12-24

**12:25** A terrible word troubles the heart of a righteous man; but a good message rejoices him.  ^12-25

**12:26** A just arbitrator shall be his own friend; but mischief shall pursue sinners; and the way of ungodly men shall lead them astray.  ^12-26

**12:27** A deceitful man shall catch no game; but a blameless man is a precious possession.[^2]  ^12-27

**12:28** In the ways of righteousness is life; but the ways of those that remember injuries *lead* to death.  ^12-28

[^1]: 12:2 Or. better is he, etc.
[^2]: 12:27 *Gr.* pure.
