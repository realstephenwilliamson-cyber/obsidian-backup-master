# Psalm 92

← [[BCP Psalm 091]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 093]] →

*Bonum est confiteri* · Book Four · Eighteenth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 092]]

**92:1** It is a good thing to give thanks to the LORD, \*  
and to sing praises to your Name, O Most High; ^92-1

**92:2** To tell of your loving-kindness early in the morning \*  
and of your faithfulness in the night season; ^92-2

[p. 721] ^pg721

**92:3** On the psaltery, and on the lyre, \*  
and to the melody of the harp. ^92-3

**92:4** For you have made me glad by your acts, O LORD; \*  
and I shout for joy because of the works of your hands. ^92-4

**92:5** LORD, how great are your works! \*  
your thoughts are very deep. ^92-5

**92:6** The dullard does not know,  
nor does the fool understand, \*  
that though the wicked grow like weeds,  
and all the workers of iniquity flourish, ^92-6

**92:7** They flourish only to be destroyed for ever; \*  
but you, O LORD, are exalted for evermore. ^92-7

**92:8** For lo, your enemies, O LORD,  
lo, your enemies shall perish, \*  
and all the workers of iniquity shall be scattered. ^92-8

**92:9** But my horn you have exalted like the horns of wild bulls; \*  
I am anointed with fresh oil. ^92-9

**92:10** My eyes also gloat over my enemies, \*  
and my ears rejoice to hear the doom of the wicked who rise up against me. ^92-10

**92:11** The righteous shall flourish like a palm tree, \*  
and shall spread abroad like a cedar of Lebanon. ^92-11

**92:12** Those who are planted in the house of the LORD \*  
shall flourish in the courts of our God; ^92-12

**92:13** They shall still bear fruit in old age; \*  
they shall be green and succulent; ^92-13

**92:14** That they may show how upright the LORD is, \*  
my Rock, in whom there is no fault. ^92-14
