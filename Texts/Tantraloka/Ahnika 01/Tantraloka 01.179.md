## Mūla

शिवतादात्म्यमापन्ना समावेशोऽत्र शाम्भवः ।
तत्प्रसादात्पुनः पश्चाद्भाविनोऽत्र विनिश्चयाः ॥ ^mula

śivatādātmyamāpannā samāveśo'tra śāmbhavaḥ |
tatprasādātpunaḥ paścādbhāvino'tra viniścayāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. I
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 01 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v01 10 - Chapter One, part 9#^pg367|p. 353]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 01.pdf#page=367>))
>
> Thus, Śiva’s (śāmbhava) penetration is (a state of) consciousness (saṁvitti) free of thought constructs (avikalpā saṁvitti) that, independent of meditation (bhāvanā) or the like, has attained a state of oneness with Śiva. (with 1.178cd–179ab)
>
> The conceptions (viniścaya) that (arise) subsequently by the grace of that (nondiscursive awareness) attain a state of identity (with Śiva,) and (so) they serve no (further) purpose. (with 1.179cd–180ab)

*Lead-in:* ननु अत्र उत्पत्तौ विकल्पापेक्षित्वं मा भूत् तथात्वे हि शाक्तोपायादस्य भेदो न स्यात्, औत्तरकालिकाः पुनर्विकल्पाः किमत्र अपेक्ष्यन्ते न वा ? इत्याशङ्क्याह --
