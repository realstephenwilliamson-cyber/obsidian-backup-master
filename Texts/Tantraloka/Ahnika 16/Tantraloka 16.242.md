## Mūla

प्रदेशवृत्ति च ज्ञानमात्मनस्तत्र तत्र तत् ।
भोग्यज्ञानं नान्यदेहेष्वनुसन्धानमर्हति ॥ ^mula

pradeśavṛtti ca jñānamātmanastatra tatra tat |
bhogyajñānaṃ nānyadeheṣvanusandhānamarhati || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 07 - Chapter Sixteen, part 3#^pg126|p. 106]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=126>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 16.241cd–242ab.
>
> One’s own (sensory) perception (jñāna) is an activity that takes place in a particular location, and is the perception of the object (bhogyajñāna) here and there (in its own location). It cannot be the perception that conjoins (and ascertains sensory activity) (anusandhāna) within other bodies.

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* इन्द्रियाणां च नियतवृत्ति ज्ञानमिति कुतस्तदुल्लङ्घनेनापि देहान्तरादौ मनोऽनुसन्दध्यादत एवाह--

> प्रदेशवृत्ति च ज्ञानमात्मनस्तत्र तत्र तत् ।
> भोग्यज्ञानं नान्यदेहेष्वनुसन्धानमर्हति ॥

भोग्यज्ञानमिति--नीलसुखादिविषयं वेदनम्--इत्यर्थः ॥२४२ ॥
