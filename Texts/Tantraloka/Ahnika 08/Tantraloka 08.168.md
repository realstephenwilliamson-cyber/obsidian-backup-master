## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 06 - Chapter Eight, part 4#^pg212|p. 194]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=212>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 8.168cd–169ab.
>
> The hundred Rudras are located ten by ten in each of the ten directions outside the Egg of Brahmā, which they support everywhere by their own power. (with 8.167cd–168ab)
>
> The teacher (Sadyojyotis or Bṛhaspati?) has explained what the Egg of Brahmā is in (his commentary on) the venerable Raurava and elsewhere (ādi), (with the words): ‘The Egg is said to be an aggregate of entities (vastupiṇḍa) that has preserved its potency, has been sown (with fertilizing seed) (āvāpavat) and is undivided, which is in a state of propensity (abhimukhībhūta) towards manifestation. (The Egg) is threaded through with a trace of Tamas. Its upper shell (kapāla) consists of Sattva mixed with Rajas. The lower part of the shell consists of Tamas devoid of Sattva.’ (with 8.168cd–170)
