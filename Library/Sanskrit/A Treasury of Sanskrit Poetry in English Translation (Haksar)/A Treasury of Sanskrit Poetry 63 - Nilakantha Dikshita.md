#### 166. Satire on Physicians (V. Raghavan)

There is no use of people in health and people with incurable diseases; the timid as well as those with prolonged ailments are the good fortune of doctors. 

Neither too much courage nor too much fear should be insti lled in the patient; in the former case, being rid of anxiety, he wil l not pay; and in the latter too, because of loss of hope, he wi l l not pay. 

Let the medical prescription be whatever i t is; the diet restrictions should be severe; if he comes back to health, it is then due to the greatness of the doctors; if it is otherwise, it should be blamed on the violation of the diet-regulations. 

As ailments spread and people die, doctors slowly educate themselves in the nature of diseases .
