## Mūla

पिण्डो रक्तादिसारौघचालनाकर्षणादिषु ।
इत्थं विश्रान्तियोगेन घटिकार्धक्रमे सति ॥ ^mula

piṇḍo raktādisāraughacālanākarṣaṇādiṣu |
itthaṃ viśrāntiyogena ghaṭikārdhakrame sati || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 05 - Chapter Sixteen, part 1#^pg41|p. 21]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=41>))
>
> The great syllabic Mantra (piṇḍa) (that is used to) move and draw out (ākarṣaṇa) etc. the flux of the (vital) essences, such as blood and the like (in the animal’s body), consists of three letters – the Blooming Phoneme (PH) encapsulated by Fire (R), the Triangle (E) and Time (Ṁ) (i.e RPHREṀ). (with 16.48cd–49ab)
>
> By resting (in this practice) in this way, and by repeating (the Mantra) one hundred times, in the course of twelve minutes the animal is offered as a libation (nirvāpanam). (with 16.49cd–50ab)
