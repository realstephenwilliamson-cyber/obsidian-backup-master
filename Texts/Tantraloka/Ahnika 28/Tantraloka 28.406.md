## Mūla

वाच्यं वस्तु समाप्य प्रतर्पणं पूजनं भवेच्चक्रे ।
पुनरपरं वस्तु वदेत्पटलादूर्ध्वं तु नो जल्पेत् ॥ ^mula

vācyaṃ vastu samāpya pratarpaṇaṃ pūjanaṃ bhaveccakre |
punaraparaṃ vastu vadetpaṭalādūrdhvaṃ tu no jalpet || ^iast

## Translation

> 6) Once the matter (vastu) that is to be taught (vācya) has been concluded, lustration is offered to the assembly (cakra) (of disciples), and it is worshipped. Then he should again talk about another matter, (but) he should not talk about (a subject) that is (found further ahead,) after (that) chapter (patalād iirdhvam).).
