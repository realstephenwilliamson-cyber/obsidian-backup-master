## Mūla

या सा शक्तिर्जगद्धातुः कथिता समवायिनी ।
इच्छात्वं तस्य सा देवि सिसृक्षोः प्रतिपद्यते ॥ ^mula

yā sā śaktir jagaddhātuḥ kathitā samavāyinī |
icchātvaṁ tasya sā devi sisṛkṣoḥ pratipadyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2023 – March 2023
> p. 39 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=39>))
>
> **The Goddess of the Alphabet The Matrix and the Seeds**
>
> The power of the Sustainer of the Universe, said to be one with Him, (is the Goddess).[^4] It is She Who assumes the form of His will when He wishes to emit (the universe). Hear now how She, though one, becomes many. (with 3.5–6ab)

[^4]: MV 3/5ab quoted in TĀv ad 11/72-73ab. 3/5ab and 3/5cd are quoted in TĀv ad 1/2. MV 3/5-9ab is quoted by Rājānaka Rāma in SpKāvi. p.148 and Bhagavadutpala in SpPra ad 1/1. At the end of the latter citation, we find this extra line not found in the edition of the MV: *tatra mātṛtvam āpannā pañcāśadvarṇamālinī* / Thus, She Who is the fifty-lettered Mālinī, becomes the universal Mother (mātṛ).
