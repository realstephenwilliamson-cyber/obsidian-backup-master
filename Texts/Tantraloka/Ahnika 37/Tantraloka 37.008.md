## Mūla

यदुक्ताधिकसंवित्तिसिद्धवस्तुनिरूपणात् ।
अपूर्णसर्ववित्प्रोक्तिर्ज्ञायतेऽधरशासने ॥ ^mula

yaduktādhikasaṃvittisiddhavastunirūpaṇāt |
apūrṇasarvavitproktirjñāyate'dharaśāsane || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI

> One knows that the teachings of a lower school have been imparted by those who are not fully omniscient, by examining the reality that is proved (to exist) by the greater consciousness taught (here).

## Viveka

> यदुक्ताधिकसंवित्तिसिद्धवस्तुनिरूपणात् ।
> अपूर्णसर्ववित्प्रोक्तिर्ज्ञायतेऽधरशासने ॥
