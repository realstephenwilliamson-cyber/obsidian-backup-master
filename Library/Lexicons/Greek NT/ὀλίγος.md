# ὀλίγος

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · adjective

**Dodson:** small, brief, few, soon — (a) especially in plur: few, (b) in sing: small; hence, of time: short, of degree: light, slight, little. (Strong's 3641; headword ὀλίγος, η, ον)
