# Zohar 3.024a

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 7.03 - Parashat Va-Yiqra#^z3-24a|Parashat Va-Yiqra, at 3:24a]]

*Volume 3, folio 24a* · Vayikra

*Passage Vayikra 61:406 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 3.023b#^vayikra-61-406|Vayikra 61:406]].*

**Vayikra 61:407** R. Judah asked why David was punished at the hand of his son. R. Jose answered: ‘As has been explained, because another man would have shown him no mercy.’ ‘But’, said R. Judah, ‘Absalom sought to kill his father and devised worse evil against him than a stranger would have done?’ R. Jose said: ‘I have not heard any answer to this.’ ^vayikra-61-407

**Vayikra 61:408** Said R. Judah: ‘ I have heard an explanation, namely, that David sinned against Bath Sheba (lit. the daughter of seven), so God said, “Let the son of the daughter of a strange god come and avenge her.” And who was this? Absalom, who was the son of a “fair captive” (v. Deut. 21, 10 *et sqq*.).’ ^vayikra-61-408

**Vayikra 62:409** R. Jose then quoted the verse: “The Lord hath sworn by his right hand and by the arm of his strength” (Isa. 62, 8). ‘Whenever’, he said, ‘a man sins before God, there is a certain grade above which takes note of that sin to punish it; if the man repents, the sin is wiped out and judgement has no sway over him, but if not, that sin is inscribed with that grade. If he sins more, then another grade takes note of him and concurs with the previous grade, so that he requires a more fervent repentance. If he goes on sinning, further grades are added till the number of five is reached. ^vayikra-62-409

**Vayikra 62:410** When the Right Hand is thus completed, the Left Hand concurs with the Right and merges in it, and then there is no further room for repentance. When the final judgement is thus pronounced, ^vayikra-62-410

**Vayikra 62:411** five fingers are locked in five, the Right Hand in the Left, and therefore when God desires to pronounce the final judgement, then, “The Lord swears by his right hand and by the arm of his strength”.’[^1] ^vayikra-62-411

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Vayikra 63:412–63:416.*

[^1]: The last 27 lines of the Hebrew text are not translated.
