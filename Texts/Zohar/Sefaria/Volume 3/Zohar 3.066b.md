# Zohar 3.066b

← [[Zohar 3.066a]] · [[Zohar (Soncino)]] · [[Zohar 3.067a]] →

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 7.08 - Parashat Aharei Mot#^z3-66b|Parashat Aḥarei Mot, at 3:66b]]

*Volume 3, folio 66b* · Achrei Mot

*Passage Achrei Mot 29:182 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 3.066a#^achrei-mot-29-182|Achrei Mot 29:182]].*

**Achrei Mot 29:183** We have learnt that if Israel in exile show themselves deserving, God will have mercy on them and hasten to bring them forth from exile, and if not He keeps them there until the appointed time,[^1] and if when that comes they are still not worthy He has regard to the honour of His Name and does not forget them in exile, as it is written: “I shall remember my covenant with Jacob”, etc. (Lev. 26, 42).’ ^achrei-mot-29-183

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Achrei Mot 29:184–30:187.*

**Achrei Mot 30:188** R. Isaac said: ‘When the Holy King shall remember Israel for the sake of His Name and restore the Matrona to her place, then, as the Scripture says, “no man shall be in the tent of meeting when He comes to make atonement for the holy things”, just as it is written of the priest when he went in to unify the Holy Name and to join the King with the Matrona.’ ^achrei-mot-30-188

**Achrei Mot 30:189** R. Judah said: ‘It has been taught that the priest entered into one degree and bathed his flesh. Then he left that grade and entered into another and bathed, making peace between the two. Thus at every step he had to perform some fitting ceremony, and wear corresponding garments, until he had properly completed the service, bringing blessing above and below.’ ^achrei-mot-30-189

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Achrei Mot 31:190–31:192.*

**Achrei Mot 31:193** When all was linked together, all faces were illumined. Then all fell on their faces and trembled and said, “Blessed be the name of his glorious kingdom for ever and ever”. ^achrei-mot-31-193

[^1]: v. T. B. [[Sanhedrin 98#98a|Sanhedrin, 98a]].
