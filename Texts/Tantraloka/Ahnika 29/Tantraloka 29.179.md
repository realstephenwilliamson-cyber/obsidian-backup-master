## Mūla

प्राणाश्रितानां देवीनां ब्रह्मनासादिभेदिभिः ।
करन्ध्रैर्विशतापानचान्द्रचक्रेण तर्पणम् ॥ ^mula

prāṇāśritānāṃ devīnāṃ brahmanāsādibhedibhiḥ |
karandhrairviśatāpānacāndracakreṇa tarpaṇam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X

> Libation is offered to the goddesses present in the vital breath with the lunar Wheel of inhalation (apāna) by penetrating through the apertures (of the body), such as that of Brahmā and the nostrils.
