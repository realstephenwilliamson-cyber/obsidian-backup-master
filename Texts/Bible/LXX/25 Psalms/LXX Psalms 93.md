# LXX Psalms 93

← [[LXX Psalms 92]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 94]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 094]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 094]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**93:1** A Psalm of David for the fourth *day* of the week. The Lord is a God of vengeance; the God of vengeance has declared himself.[^1]  ^93-1

**93:2** Be thou exalted, thou that judgest the earth: render a reward to the proud.  ^93-2

**93:3** How long shall sinners, O Lord, how long shall sinners boast?  ^93-3

**93:4** They will utter and speak unrighteousness; all the workers of iniquity will speak *so*.  ^93-4

**93:5** They have afflicted thy people, O Lord, and hurt thine heritage.  ^93-5

**93:6** They have slain the widow and fatherless, and murdered the stranger.  ^93-6

**93:7** And they said, The Lord shall not see, neither shall the God of Jacob understand.  ^93-7

**93:8** Understand now, ye simple among the people; and ye fools, at length be wise.  ^93-8

**93:9** He that planted the ear, does he not hear? or he that formed the eye, does not he perceive?  ^93-9

**93:10** He that chastises the heathen, shall not he punish, *even* he that teaches man knowledge?  ^93-10

**93:11** The Lord knows the thoughts of men, that they are vain.  ^93-11

**93:12** Blessed is the man whomsoever thou shalt chasten, O Lord, and shalt teach him out of thy law;  ^93-12

**93:13** to give him rest from evil days, until a pit be digged for the sinful one.  ^93-13

**93:14** For the Lord will not cast off his people, neither will he forsake his inheritance;  ^93-14

**93:15** until righteousness return to judgment, and all the upright in heart shall follow it. Pause.  ^93-15

**93:16** Who will rise up for me against the transgressors? or who will stand up with me against the workers of iniquity?  ^93-16

**93:17** If the Lord had not helped me, my soul had almost sojourned in Hades.  ^93-17

**93:18** If I said, My foot has been moved;  ^93-18

**93:19** thy mercy, O Lord, helped me. O Lord, according to the multitude of my griefs within my heart, thy consolations have soothed my soul.[^2]  ^93-19

**93:20** Shall the throne of iniquity have fellowship with thee, which frames mischief by an ordinance?  ^93-20

**93:21** They will hunt for the soul of the righteous, and condemn innocent blood.  ^93-21

**93:22** But the Lord was my refuge; and my God the helper of my hope.  ^93-22

**93:23** And he will recompense to them their iniquity and their wickedness: the Lord our God shall utterly destroy them.  ^93-23

[^1]: 93:1 *Gr.* plural, *bis.*
[^2]: 93:19 *Lit.*'have loved'. *Alex.* ἠὺφπαναν, 'have gladdened'.
