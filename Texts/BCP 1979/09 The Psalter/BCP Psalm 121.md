# Psalm 121

← [[BCP Psalm 120]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 122]] →

*Levavi oculos* · Book Five · Twenty-seventh Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 121]]

**121:1** I lift up my eyes to the hills; \*  
from where is my help to come? ^121-1

**121:2** My help comes from the LORD, \*  
the maker of heaven and earth. ^121-2

**121:3** He will not let your foot be moved \*  
and he who watches over you will not fall asleep. ^121-3

**121:4** Behold, he who keeps watch over Israel \*  
shall neither slumber nor sleep; ^121-4

**121:5** The LORD himself watches over you; \*  
the LORD is your shade at your right hand, ^121-5

**121:6** So that the sun shall not strike you by day, \*  
nor the moon by night. ^121-6

**121:7** The LORD shall preserve you from all evil; \*  
it is he who shall keep you safe. ^121-7

**121:8** The LORD shall watch over your going out and your coming in, \*  
from this time forth for evermore. ^121-8
