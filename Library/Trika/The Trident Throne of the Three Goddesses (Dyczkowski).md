These Vidyās and Mantras are recited together in this sequence. Each recitation of all of them together is one japa. To activate them recite the entire sequence 100,000 times. Before beginning each session say: 

*oṁ sauḥ trividyāyai namaḥ* Then recite the following three verses taken from the *Tantrāloka* praising the triple Vidyā in her three forms. Recite the Sanskrit and then the translation. These are cognitive visualizations (*dhyāna*). 

*[p. 6]* ^pg7

*naumi citpratibhāṁ devīṁ parāṁ bhairavayoginīm |* *mātṛ-māna-prameyāṁśa-śūlāmbuja-kṛtāspadām ||* 

I bow to Parā (the Supreme One) who is the creative intuition of consciousness (*citpratibhā*). Bhairava’s (consort) united with Him (*bhairavayoginī*), She has made (her) place on (top of) the lotuses (of the Trika Trident, the) prongs of which are the aspects (of perception, namely) subject, object and means of knowledge. 

*dīptajyotiś-chaṭāpluṣṭa-bhedabandhatrayaṁ sphurat |* *stāj jñānaśūlaṁ satpakṣa-vipakṣotkartana-kṣamam ||* 

May (Parāparā), the pulsing radiant (*sphurat*) prong of knowledge, that has burnt up the triple fetter with its blazing rays of the light (of Śiva consciousness), have the power to uproot all that runs contrary (*vipakṣa*) to the truth (*satpakṣa*). 

*naumi devīṁ śarīrasthāṁ nṛtyato bhairavākṛteḥ |* *prāvṛṇ-megha-ghana-vyoma-vidyul-lekhāvilāsinīm ||* 

I bow to the Goddess (Aparā) who resides in the body of Bhairava’s dancing form and plays within it (like) a lightning flash in a sky (covered) with dense storm clouds. 

Then remembering your teachers say: 

*oṁ śrīgurubhyo namaḥ* 

Then begin *japa*.
