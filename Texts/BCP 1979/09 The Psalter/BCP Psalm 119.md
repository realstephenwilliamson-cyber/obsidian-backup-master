# Psalm 119

← [[BCP Psalm 118]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 120]] →

** · Book Five · Twenty-fourth Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 119]]

**Aleph** Beati immaculati

**119:1** Happy are they whose way is blameless, \*  
who walk in the law of the LORD! ^119-1

**119:2** Happy are they who observe his decrees \*  
and seek him with all their hearts! ^119-2

**119:3** Who never do any wrong, \*  
but always walk in his ways. ^119-3

**119:4** You laid down your commandments, \*  
that we should fully keep them. ^119-4

**119:5** Oh, that my ways were made so direct \*  
that I might keep your statutes! ^119-5

**119:6** Then I should not be put to shame, \*  
when I regard all your commandments. ^119-6

**119:7** I will thank you with an unfeigned heart, \*  
when I have learned your righteous judgments. ^119-7

**119:8** I will keep your statutes; \*  
do not utterly forsake me. ^119-8

[p. 764] ^pg764

**Beth** In quo corrigit?

**119:9** How shall a young man cleanse his way? \*  
By keeping to your words. ^119-9

**119:10** With my whole heart I seek you; \*  
let me not stray from your commandments. ^119-10

**119:11** I treasure your promise in my heart, \*  
that I may not sin against you. ^119-11

**119:12** Blessed are you, O LORD; \*  
instruct me in your statutes. ^119-12

**119:13** With my lips will I recite \*  
all the judgments of your mouth. ^119-13

**119:14** I have taken greater delight in the way of your decrees \*  
than in all manner of riches. ^119-14

**119:15** I will meditate on your commandments \*  
and give attention to your ways. ^119-15

**119:16** My delight is in your statutes; \*  
I will not forget your word. ^119-16

**Gimel** Retribue servo tuo

**119:17** Deal bountifully with your servant, \*  
that I may live and keep your word. ^119-17

**119:18** Open my eyes, that I may see \*  
the wonders of your law. ^119-18

**119:19** I am a stranger here on earth; \*  
do not hide your commandments from me. ^119-19

**119:20** My soul is consumed at all times \*  
with longing for your judgments. ^119-20

[p. 765] ^pg765

**119:21** You have rebuked the insolent; \*  
cursed are they who stray from your commandments! ^119-21

**119:22** Turn from me shame and rebuke, \*  
for I have kept your decrees. ^119-22

**119:23** Even though rulers sit and plot against me, \*  
I will meditate on your statutes. ^119-23

**119:24** For your decrees are my delight, \*  
and they are my counselors. ^119-24

**Daleth** Adhæsit pavimento

**119:25** My soul cleaves to the dust; \*  
give me life according to your word. ^119-25

**119:26** I have confessed my ways, and you answered me; \*  
instruct me in your statutes. ^119-26

**119:27** Make me understand the way of your commandments, \*  
that I may meditate on your marvelous works. ^119-27

**119:28** My soul melts away for sorrow; \*  
strengthen me according to your word. ^119-28

**119:29** Take from me the way of lying; \*  
let me find grace through your law. ^119-29

**119:30** I have chosen the way of faithfulness; \*  
I have set your judgments before me. ^119-30

**119:31** I hold fast to your decrees; \*  
O LORD, let me not be put to shame. ^119-31

**119:32** I will run the way of your commandments, \*  
for you have set my heart at liberty. ^119-32

[p. 766] ^pg766

**He** Legem pone

**119:33** Teach me, O LORD, the way of your statutes, \*  
and I shall keep it to the end. ^119-33

**119:34** Give me understanding, and I shall keep your law; \*  
I shall keep it with all my heart. ^119-34

**119:35** Make me go in the path of your commandments, \*  
for that is my desire. ^119-35

**119:36** Incline my heart to your decrees \*  
and not to unjust gain. ^119-36

**119:37** Turn my eyes from watching what is worthless; \*  
give me life in your ways. ^119-37

**119:38** Fulfill your promise to your servant, \*  
which you make to those who fear you. ^119-38

**119:39** Turn away the reproach which I dread, \*  
because your judgments are good. ^119-39

**119:40** Behold, I long for your commandments; \*  
in your righteousness preserve my life. ^119-40

**Waw** Et veniat super me

**119:41** Let your loving-kindness come to me, O LORD, \*  
and your salvation, according to your promise. ^119-41

**119:42** Then shall I have a word for those who taunt me, \*  
because I trust in your words. ^119-42

**119:43** Do not take the word of truth out of my mouth, \*  
for my hope is in your judgments. ^119-43

[p. 767] ^pg767

**119:44** I shall continue to keep your law; \*  
I shall keep it for ever and ever. ^119-44

**119:45** I will walk at liberty, \*  
because I study your commandments. ^119-45

**119:46** I will tell of your decrees before kings \*  
and will not be ashamed. ^119-46

**119:47** I delight in your commandments, \*  
which I have always loved. ^119-47

**119:48** I will lift up my hands to your commandments, \*  
and I will meditate on your statutes. ^119-48

**Zayin** Memor esto verbi tui

**119:49** Remember your word to your servant, \*  
because you have given me hope. ^119-49

**119:50** This is my comfort in my trouble, \*  
that your promise gives me life. ^119-50

**119:51** The proud have derided me cruelly, \*  
but I have not turned from your law. ^119-51

**119:52** When I remember your judgments of old, \*  
O LORD, I take great comfort. ^119-52

**119:53** I am filled with a burning rage, \*  
because of the wicked who forsake your law. ^119-53

**119:54** Your statutes have been like songs to me \*  
wherever I have lived as a stranger. ^119-54

**119:55** I remember your Name in the night, O LORD, \*  
and dwell upon your law. ^119-55

**119:56** This is how it has been with me, \*  
because I have kept your commandments. ^119-56

[p. 768] ^pg768

**Heth** Portio mea, Domine

**119:57** You only are my portion, O LORD; \*  
I have promised to keep your words. ^119-57

**119:58** I entreat you with all my heart, \*  
be merciful to me according to your promise. ^119-58

**119:59** I have considered my ways \*  
and turned my feet toward your decrees. ^119-59

**119:60** I hasten and do not tarry \*  
to keep your commandments. ^119-60

**119:61** Though the cords of the wicked entangle me, \*  
I do not forget your law. ^119-61

**119:62** At midnight I will rise to give you thanks, \*  
because of your righteous judgments. ^119-62

**119:63** I am a companion of all who fear you \*  
and of those who keep your commandments. ^119-63

**119:64** The earth, O LORD, is full of your love; \*  
instruct me in your statutes. ^119-64

**Teth** Bonitatem fecisti

**119:65** O LORD, you have dealt graciously with your servant, \*  
according to your word. ^119-65

**119:66** Teach me discernment and knowledge, \*  
for I have believed in your commandments. ^119-66

**119:67** Before I was afflicted I went astray, \*  
but now I keep your word. ^119-67

**119:68** You are good and you bring forth good; \*  
instruct me in your statutes. ^119-68

[p. 769] ^pg769

**119:69** The proud have smeared me with lies, \*  
but I will keep your commandments with my whole heart. ^119-69

**119:70** Their heart is gross and fat, \*  
but my delight is in your law. ^119-70

**119:71** It is good for me that I have been afflicted, \*  
that I might learn your statutes. ^119-71

**119:72** The law of your mouth is dearer to me \*  
than thousands in gold and silver. ^119-72

**Yodh** Manus tuæ fecerunt me

**119:73** Your hands have made me and fashioned me; \*  
give me understanding, that I may learn your commandments. ^119-73

**119:74** Those who fear you will be glad when they see me, \*  
because I trust in your word. ^119-74

**119:75** I know, O LORD, that your judgments are right \*  
and that in faithfulness you have afflicted me. ^119-75

**119:76** Let your loving-kindness be my comfort, \*  
as you have promised to your servant. ^119-76

**119:77** Let your compassion come to me, that I may live, \*  
for your law is my delight. ^119-77

**119:78** Let the arrogant be put to shame, for they wrong me with lies; \*  
but I will meditate on your commandments. ^119-78

**119:79** Let those who fear you turn to me, \*  
and also those who know your decrees. ^119-79

[p. 770] ^pg770

**119:80** Let my heart be sound in your statutes, \*  
that I may not be put to shame. ^119-80

**Kaph** Defecit in salutare

**119:81** My soul has longed for your salvation; \*  
I have put my hope in your word. ^119-81

**119:82** My eyes have failed from watching for your promise, \*  
and I say, "When will you comfort me?" ^119-82

**119:83** I have become like a leather flask in the smoke, \*  
but I have not forgotten your statutes. ^119-83

**119:84** How much longer must I wait? \*  
when will you give judgment against those who persecute me? ^119-84

**119:85** The proud have dug pits for me; \*  
they do not keep your law. ^119-85

**119:86** All your commandments are true; \*  
help me, for they persecute me with lies. ^119-86

**119:87** They had almost made an end of me on earth, \*  
but I have not forsaken your commandments. ^119-87

**119:88** In your loving-kindness, revive me, \*  
that I may keep the decrees of your mouth. ^119-88

**Lamedh** In æternum, Domine

**119:89** O LORD, your word is everlasting; \*  
it stands firm in the heavens. ^119-89

**119:90** Your faithfulness remains from one generation to another; \*  
you established the earth, and it abides. ^119-90

[p. 771] ^pg771

**119:91** By your decree these continue to this day, \*  
for all things are your servants. ^119-91

**119:92** If my delight had not been in your law, \*  
I should have perished in my affliction. ^119-92

**119:93** I will never forget your commandments, \*  
because by them you give me life. ^119-93

**119:94** I am yours; oh, that you would save me! \*  
for I study your commandments. ^119-94

**119:95** Though the wicked lie in wait for me to destroy me, \*  
I will apply my mind to your decrees. ^119-95

**119:96** I see that all things come to an end, \*  
but your commandment has no bounds. ^119-96

**Mem** Quomodo dilexi!

**119:97** Oh, how I love your law! \*  
all the day long it is in my mind. ^119-97

**119:98** Your commandment has made me wiser than my enemies, \*  
and it is always with me. ^119-98

**119:99** I have more understanding than all my teachers, \*  
for your decrees are my study. ^119-99

**119:100** I am wiser than the elders, \*  
because I observe your commandments. ^119-100

**119:101** I restrain my feet from every evil way, \*  
that I may keep your word. ^119-101

**119:102** I do not shrink from your judgments, \*  
because you yourself have taught me. ^119-102

**119:103** How sweet are your words to my taste! \*  
they are sweeter than honey to my mouth. ^119-103

[p. 772] ^pg772

**119:104** Through your commandments I gain understanding; \*  
therefore I hate every lying way. ^119-104

**Nun** Lucerna pedibus meis

**119:105** Your word is a lantern to my feet \*  
and a light upon my path. ^119-105

**119:106** I have sworn and am determined \*  
to keep your righteous judgments. ^119-106

**119:107** I am deeply troubled; \*  
preserve my life, O LORD, according to your word. ^119-107

**119:108** Accept, O LORD, the willing tribute of my lips, \*  
and teach me your judgments. ^119-108

**119:109** My life is always in my hand, \*  
yet I do not forget your law. ^119-109

**119:110** The wicked have set a trap for me, \*  
but I have not strayed from your commandments. ^119-110

**119:111** Your decrees are my inheritance for ever; \*  
truly, they are the joy of my heart. ^119-111

**119:112** I have applied my heart to fulfill your statutes \*  
for ever and to the end. ^119-112

**Samekh** Iniquos odio habui

**119:113** I hate those who have a divided heart, \*  
but your law do I love. ^119-113

[p. 773] ^pg773

**119:114** You are my refuge and shield; \*  
my hope is in your word. ^119-114

**119:115** Away from me, you wicked! \*  
I will keep the commandments of my God. ^119-115

**119:116** Sustain me according to your promise, that I may live, \*  
and let me not be disappointed in my hope. ^119-116

**119:117** Hold me up, and I shall be safe, \*  
and my delight shall be ever in your statutes. ^119-117

**119:118** You spurn all who stray from your statutes; \*  
their deceitfulness is in vain. ^119-118

**119:119** In your sight all the wicked of the earth are but dross; \*  
therefore I love your decrees. ^119-119

**119:120** My flesh trembles with dread of you; \*  
I am afraid of your judgments. ^119-120

**Ayin** Feci judicium

**119:121** I have done what is just and right; \*  
do not deliver me to my oppressors. ^119-121

**119:122** Be surety for your servant's good; \*  
let not the proud oppress me. ^119-122

**119:123** My eyes have failed from watching for your salvation \*  
and for your righteous promise. ^119-123

**119:124** Deal with your servant according to your loving-kindness \*  
and teach me your statutes. ^119-124

**119:125** I am your servant; grant me understanding, \*  
that I may know your decrees. ^119-125

**119:126** It is time for you to act, O LORD, \*  
for they have broken your law. ^119-126

[p. 774] ^pg774

**119:127** Truly, I love your commandments \*  
more than gold and precious stones. ^119-127

**119:128** I hold all your commandments to be right for me; \*  
all paths of falsehood I abhor. ^119-128

**Pe** Mirabilia

**119:129** Your decrees are wonderful; \*  
therefore I obey them with all my heart. ^119-129

**119:130** When your word goes forth it gives light; \*  
it gives understanding to the simple. ^119-130

**119:131** I open my mouth and pant; \*  
I long for your commandments. ^119-131

**119:132** Turn to me in mercy, \*  
as you always do to those who love your Name. ^119-132

**119:133** Steady my footsteps in your word; \*  
let no iniquity have dominion over me. ^119-133

**119:134** Rescue me from those who oppress me, \*  
and I will keep your commandments. ^119-134

**119:135** Let your countenance shine upon your servant \*  
and teach me your statutes. ^119-135

**119:136** My eyes shed streams of tears, \*  
because people do not keep your law. ^119-136

**Sadhe** Justus es, Domine

**119:137** You are righteous, O LORD, \*  
and upright are your judgments. ^119-137

[p. 775] ^pg775

**119:138** You have issued your decrees \*  
with justice and in perfect faithfulness. ^119-138

**119:139** My indignation has consumed me, \*  
because my enemies forget your words. ^119-139

**119:140** Your word has been tested to the uttermost, \*  
and your servant holds it dear. ^119-140

**119:141** I am small and of little account, \*  
yet I do not forget your commandments. ^119-141

**119:142** Your justice is an everlasting justice \*  
and your law is the truth. ^119-142

**119:143** Trouble and distress have come upon me, \*  
yet your commandments are my delight. ^119-143

**119:144** The righteousness of your decrees is everlasting; \*  
grant me understanding, that I may live. ^119-144

**Qoph** Clamavi in toto corde meo

**119:145** I call with my whole heart; \*  
answer me, O LORD, that I may keep your statutes. ^119-145

**119:146** I call to you;  
oh, that you would save me! \*  
I will keep your decrees. ^119-146

**119:147** Early in the morning I cry out to you, \*  
for in your word is my trust. ^119-147

**119:148** My eyes are open in the night watches, \*  
that I may meditate upon your promise. ^119-148

[p. 776] ^pg776

**119:149** Hear my voice, O LORD, according to your loving-kindness; \*  
according to your judgments, give me life. ^119-149

**119:150** They draw near who in malice persecute me; \*  
they are very far from your law. ^119-150

**119:151** You, O LORD, are near at hand, \*  
and all your commandments are true. ^119-151

**119:152** Long have I known from your decrees \*  
that you have established them for ever. ^119-152

**Resh** Vide humilitatem

**119:153** Behold my affliction and deliver me, \*  
for I do not forget your law. ^119-153

**119:154** Plead my cause and redeem me; \*  
according to your promise, give me life. ^119-154

**119:155** Deliverance is far from the wicked, \*  
for they do not study your statutes. ^119-155

**119:156** Great is your compassion, O LORD; \*  
preserve my life, according to your judgments. ^119-156

**119:157** There are many who persecute and oppress me, \*  
yet I have not swerved from your decrees. ^119-157

**119:158** I look with loathing at the faithless, \*  
for they have not kept your word. ^119-158

**119:159** See how I love your commandments! \*  
O LORD, in your mercy, preserve me. ^119-159

**119:160** The heart of your word is truth; \*  
all your righteous judgments endure for evermore. ^119-160

[p. 777] ^pg777

**Shin** Principes persecuti sunt

**119:161** Rulers have persecuted me without a cause, \*  
but my heart stands in awe of your word. ^119-161

**119:162** I am as glad because of your promise \*  
as one who finds great spoils. ^119-162

**119:163** As for lies, I hate and abhor them, \*  
but your law is my love. ^119-163

**119:164** Seven times a day do I praise you, \*  
because of your righteous judgments. ^119-164

**119:165** Great peace have they who love your law; \*  
for them there is no stumbling block. ^119-165

**119:166** I have hoped for your salvation, O LORD, \*  
and have fulfilled your commandments. ^119-166

**119:167** I have kept your decrees \*  
and I have loved them deeply. ^119-167

**119:168** I have kept your commandments and decrees, \*  
for all my ways are before you. ^119-168

**Taw** Appropinquet deprecatio

**119:169** Let my cry come before you, O LORD; \*  
give me understanding, according to your word. ^119-169

**119:170** Let my supplication come before you; \*  
deliver me, according to your promise. ^119-170

**119:171** My lips shall pour forth your praise, \*  
when you teach me your statutes. ^119-171

**119:172** My tongue shall sing of your promise, \*  
for all your commandments are righteous. ^119-172

[p. 778] ^pg778

**119:173** Let your hand be ready to help me, \*  
for I have chosen your commandments. ^119-173

**119:174** I long for your salvation, O LORD, \*  
and your law is my delight. ^119-174

**119:175** Let me live, and I will praise you, \*  
and let your judgments help me. ^119-175

**119:176** I have gone astray like a sheep that is lost; \*  
search for your servant,  
for I do not forget your commandments. ^119-176
