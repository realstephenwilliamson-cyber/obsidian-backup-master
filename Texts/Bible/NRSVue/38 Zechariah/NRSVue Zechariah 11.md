# Zechariah 11 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Zechariah 10|← Zechariah 10]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Zechariah 12|Zechariah 12 →]]

**11:1** Open your doors, O Lebanon, so that fire may devour your cedars! ^11-1

**11:2** Wail, O cypress, for the cedar has fallen, for the glorious trees are ruined! Wail, oaks of Bashan, for the thick forest has been felled! ^11-2

**11:3** Listen, the wail of the shepherds, for their glory is despoiled! Listen, the roar of the lions, for the thickets of the Jordan are destroyed! ^11-3

## Two kinds of shepherds

**11:4** “Thus says the LORD my God: Be a shepherd of the flock doomed to slaughter. ^11-4

**11:5** Those who buy them kill them and go unpunished, and those who sell them say, ‘Blessed be the LORD, for I have become rich,’ and their own shepherds have no pity on them. ^11-5

**11:6** For I will no longer have pity on the inhabitants of the earth, says the LORD. I will cause them, every one, to fall each into the hand of a neighbor and each into the hand of the king, and they shall devastate the earth, and I will deliver no one from their hand.” ^11-6

**11:7** So on behalf of the sheep merchants,^[Gk: Meaning of Heb uncertain] I became the shepherd of the flock doomed to slaughter. I took two staffs; one I named Favor, the other I named Unity, and I tended the sheep. ^11-7

**11:8** In one month I disposed of the three shepherds, for I had become impatient with them, and they also detested me. ^11-8

**11:9** So I said, “I will not be your shepherd. What is to die, let it die; what is to be destroyed, let it be destroyed; and let those that are left devour the flesh of one another!” ^11-9

**11:10** I took my staff Favor and broke it, annulling the covenant that I had made with all the peoples. ^11-10

**11:11** So it was annulled on that day, and the sheep merchants^[Gk: Meaning of Heb uncertain] who were watching me knew that it was the word of the LORD. ^11-11

**11:12** I then said to them, “If it seems right to you, give me my wages, but if not, keep them.” So they weighed out as my wages thirty shekels of silver. ^11-12

**11:13** Then the LORD said to me, “Throw it into the treasury”^[Syr: Heb *it to the potter*]—this lordly price at which I was valued by them. So I took the thirty shekels of silver and threw them into the treasury^[Syr: Heb *it to the potter*] in the house of the LORD. ^11-13

**11:14** Then I broke my second staff Unity, annulling the family ties between Judah and Israel. ^11-14

**11:15** Then the LORD said to me: “Take once more the implements of a worthless shepherd. ^11-15

**11:16** For I am now raising up in the land a shepherd who does not care for the perishing, or seek the wandering,^[Syr Compare Gk Vg: Heb *the youth*] or heal the maimed, or nourish the healthy,^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] but devours the flesh of the fat ones, tearing off even their hoofs. ^11-16

**11:17** Oh, my worthless shepherd, who deserts the flock! May the sword strike his arm and his right eye! Let his arm be completely withered, his right eye utterly blinded!” ^11-17
