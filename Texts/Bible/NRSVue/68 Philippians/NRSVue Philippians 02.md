## Imitating Christ’s humility

**2:1** If, then, there is any comfort in Christ, any consolation from love, any partnership in the Spirit, any tender affection and sympathy, ^2-1

**2:2** make my joy complete: be of the same mind, having the same love, being in full accord and of one mind. ^2-2

**2:3** Do nothing from selfish ambition or empty conceit, but in humility regard others as better than yourselves. ^2-3

**2:4** Let each of you look not to your own interests but to the interests of others. ^2-4

**2:5** Let the same mind be in you that was^[Or *that you have*] in Christ Jesus, ^2-5

**2:6** who, though he existed in the form of God, did not regard equality with God as something to be grasped, ^2-6

**2:7** but emptied himself, taking the form of a slave, assuming human likeness. And being found in appearance as a human, ^2-7

**2:8** he humbled himself and became obedient to the point of death—even death on a cross. ^2-8

**2:9** Therefore God exalted him even more highly and gave him the name that is above every other name, ^2-9

**2:10** so that at the name given to Jesus every knee should bend, in heaven and on earth and under the earth, ^2-10

**2:11** and every tongue should confess that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father. ^2-11

## Shining as lights in the world

**2:12** Therefore, my beloved, just as you have always obeyed me, not only in my presence but much more now in my absence, work on your own salvation with fear and trembling, ^2-12

**2:13** for it is God who is at work in you, enabling you both to will and to work for his good pleasure. ^2-13

**2:14** Do all things without murmuring and arguing, ^2-14

**2:15** so that you may be blameless and innocent, children of God without blemish in the midst of a crooked and perverse generation, in which you shine like stars in the world, ^2-15

**2:16** holding forth the word of life so that I can boast on the day of Christ that I did not run in vain or labor in vain. ^2-16

**2:17** But even if I am being poured out as a libation over the sacrifice and the service of your faith, I rejoice, and I rejoice together with all of you; ^2-17

**2:18** in the same way also you should rejoice and rejoice together with me. ^2-18

## Timothy and Epaphroditus

**2:19** I hope in the Lord Jesus to send Timothy to you soon, so that I, too, may be consoled by news of you. ^2-19

**2:20** I have no one so like myself who will be genuinely concerned for your welfare. ^2-20

**2:21** All of them are seeking their own interests, not those of Jesus Christ. ^2-21

**2:22** But Timothy’s^[Gk *his*] worth you know, how like a son with a father he has served with me in the work of the gospel. ^2-22

**2:23** I hope therefore to send him as soon as I see how things go with me, ^2-23

**2:24** and I trust in the Lord that I will also come soon. ^2-24

**2:25** Still, I think it necessary to send to you Epaphroditus—my brother and coworker and fellow soldier, your messenger^[Gk *apostle*] and minister to my need, ^2-25

**2:26** for he has been longing for^[Other ancient authorities read *longing to see*] all of you and has been distressed because you heard that he was ill. ^2-26

**2:27** He was indeed so ill that he nearly died. But God had mercy on him, and not only on him but on me also, so that I would not have one sorrow after another. ^2-27

**2:28** I am the more eager to send him, therefore, in order that you may rejoice at seeing him again and that I may be less anxious. ^2-28

**2:29** Welcome him, then, in the Lord with all joy, and honor such people, ^2-29

**2:30** because he came close to death for the work of Christ,^[Other ancient authorities read *of the Lord*] risking his life to make up for those services that you could not give me. ^2-30
