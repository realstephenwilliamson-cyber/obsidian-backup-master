## Mūla

एवं मुद्रागणं मन्त्री बध्नीयाद्धृदये बुधः ।
सर्वासां वाचकश्चासां ओं ह्रीं नाम ततो नमः ॥ ^mula

evaṃ mudrāgaṇaṃ mantrī badhnīyāddhṛdaye budhaḥ |
sarvāsāṃ vācakaścāsāṃ oṃ hrīṃ nāma tato namaḥ || ^iast

*Dyczkowski prints no Sanskrit for this chapter; the text is that of the KSTS edition (Muktabodha M00160).*

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, July 2024 – September 2024
> p. 31 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=10>))
>
> The wise reciter of mantra should make the group of gestures in this way in (front of his) heart. (The mantra that) denotes all of them (and is uttered when they are displayed) is OṂ HRĪṂ, the name (of the gesture) then (followed by) NAMAḤ.
