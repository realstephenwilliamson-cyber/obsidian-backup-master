## Mediator of a better covenant

**8:1** Now the main point in what we are saying is this: we have such a high priest, one who is seated at the right hand of the throne of the Majesty in the heavens, ^8-1

**8:2** a minister in the sanctuary and the true tent^[Or *tabernacle*] that the Lord, and not any mortal, has set up. ^8-2

**8:3** For every high priest is appointed to offer gifts and sacrifices; hence it is necessary for this priest also to have something to offer. ^8-3

**8:4** Now if he were on earth, he would not be a priest at all, since there are already those^[Other ancient authorities read *priests*] who offer gifts according to the law. ^8-4

**8:5** They offer worship in a sanctuary that is^[Gk lacks *a sanctuary that is*] a sketch and shadow of the heavenly one, just as Moses was warned when he was about to erect the tent.^[Or *tabernacle*] For, God^[Gk *he*] said, “See that you make everything according to the pattern that was shown you on the mountain.” ^8-5

**8:6** But Jesus^[Gk *he*] has now obtained a more excellent ministry, and to that degree he is the mediator of a better covenant, which has been enacted on the basis of better promises. ^8-6

**8:7** For if that first covenant had been faultless, there would have been no need to look for a second one. ^8-7

**8:8** God^[Gk *He*] finds fault with them when he says: “The days are surely coming, says the Lord, when I will establish a new covenant with the house of Israel and with the house of Judah, ^8-8

**8:9** not like the covenant that I made with their ancestors on the day when I took them by the hand to lead them out of the land of Egypt, for they did not continue in my covenant, and so I had no concern for them, says the Lord. ^8-9

**8:10** This is the covenant that I will make with the house of Israel after those days, says the Lord: I will put my laws in their minds and write them on their hearts, and I will be their God, and they shall be my people. ^8-10

**8:11** And they shall not teach one another or say to each other,^[Or *teach each one their fellow-citizen and each one their sibling, saying*] ‘Know the Lord,’ for they shall all know me, from the least of them to the greatest. ^8-11

**8:12** For I will be merciful toward their iniquities, and I will remember their sins^[Other ancient authorities add *and their lawless deeds*] no more.” ^8-12

**8:13** In speaking of a new covenant, he has made the first one obsolete, and what is obsolete and growing old will soon disappear. ^8-13
