## Chapter 17

**17:1** A prayer of David.  
Hear, O LORD, what is just;  
heed my cry, give ear to my prayer,  
uttered without guile. ^17-1

**17:2** My vindication will come from You;  
Your eyes will behold what is right. ^17-2

**17:3** You have visited me at night, probed my mind,  
You have tested me and found nothing amiss;  
[^1]I determined that my mouth should not transgress. ^17-3

**17:4** As for man’s dealings,  
in accord with the command of Your lips,  
I have kept in view the fate[^2] of the lawless. ^17-4

**17:5** My feet have held to Your paths;  
my legs have not given way. ^17-5

**17:6** I call on You;  
You will answer me, God;  
turn Your ear to me,  
hear what I say. ^17-6

**17:7** Display Your faithfulness in wondrous deeds,  
You who deliver with Your right hand  
those who seek refuge from assailants. ^17-7

**17:8** Guard me like the apple of Your eye;  
hide me in the shadow of Your wings ^17-8

**17:9** from the wicked who despoil me,  
my mortal enemies who[^3] encircle me. ^17-9

**17:10** Their hearts are closed to pity;[^4]  
they mouth arrogance; ^17-10

**17:11** now they hem in our feet on every side;  
they set their eyes roaming over the land. ^17-11

**17:12** He is like a lion eager for prey,  
a king of beasts lying in wait. ^17-12

**17:13** Rise, O LORD! Go forth to meet him.  
Bring him down;  
rescue me from the wicked with Your sword, ^17-13

**17:14** from men, O LORD, with Your hand,  
from men whose share in life is fleeting.  
But as to Your treasured ones,  
fill their bellies.[^5]  
Their sons too shall be satisfied,  
and have something to leave over for their young. ^17-14

**17:15** Then I, justified, will behold Your face;  
awake, I am filled with the vision of You. ^17-15

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Cf. Prov. 1.19; lit. “paths.”
[^3]: Or “from my enemies who avidly.”
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^5]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
