## Mūla

कुर्यादिति शिवेनोक्तं तत्र तत्र कृपालुना ।
शक्तिपातोदये जन्तोर्येनोपायेन दैशिकः ॥ ^mula

kuryāditi śivenoktaṃ tatra tatra kṛpālunā |
śaktipātodaye jantoryenopāyena daiśikaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 17 - Chapter Twenty-Five (continued) and Chapter Twenty-Six#^pg385|p. 365]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=385>))
>
> In this way, (the teacher) may even, by means of the offerings to the ancestors (śrāddha), generate the condition (for his deceased disciple) in which he enjoys worldly experience, liberation or both, as compassionate Śiva has declared in various places (in the scriptures). (with 25.21cd–22ab)
>
> Once a descent of the power (of grace) has arisen for someone, whatever be the means the teacher applies to extract (him from his fettered state) serves to extinguish it (nirvānāya) (and liberate him). (with 25.22cd–23ab)
