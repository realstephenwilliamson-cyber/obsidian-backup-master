## Mūla

मन्त्रास्तदनुसारेण तत्त्वेष्वेतद्द्वयं क्षिपेत् ।
पिण्डाक्षराणां सर्वेषां वर्णसंख्या विभेदतः ॥ ^mula

mantrāstadanusāreṇa tattveṣvetaddvayaṃ kṣipet |
piṇḍākṣarāṇāṃ sarveṣāṃ varṇasaṃkhyā vibhedataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 07 - Chapter Sixteen, part 3#^pg113|p. 93]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=113>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 16.220cd–221ab.
>
> The mantras of the two groups of eight beginning with Aghorī are the same as the Parts of Parāparā, and so both these should be placed within the principles, in accord with that. (with 16.220cd–221ab)
>
> (All the principles) up to Nature should be deposited? into the vowel of any of the phonemes of a Pinda (mantra formed from groups of phonemes). In accord with (their) differing numbers of phonemes, the remaining (set of principles from the individual soul onwards) should be conjoined within the remaining (phonemes). (with 16.221cd–222ab)
