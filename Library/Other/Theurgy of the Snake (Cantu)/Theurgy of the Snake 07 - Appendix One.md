## | Appendix One |

THE *YOGA KALANDAR* TEXT (ENGLISH)<sup>17</sup> 

1 

I salute first the venerable Nirañjan, After him I salute the feet of the Prophet. 

2 

Allah the Merciful, the Generous, the All-powerful Protector, He who has created the eighteen thousand worlds. 

3 

Know that the Prophet became anxious, and to the world The Messenger of Allah spoke many ideas. 

4 

Know also that Bibi Fatima is the daughter of the Messenger, The spouse of the venerable Ali, the Mother of the World. 

5 

After saluting the feet of all the Companions, Listen attentively! I will tell you all about unity-in-difference (*bhedābheda*). 

\[I pay my respect to all the angels living on the sky. 

By whose order, the whole system of the universe is maintained. 

Now, O people, hear the (great) tidings: 

I only tell you the mysteries of the four *Maqām*s.\]<sup>18</sup> 

Principles of the Stations | *Mokām Tattva* 

6 

Know that the station *nāsut* (Ar. *nāsūt*) is the Three Points (*tin tiharī*). 

The angel Azrael (Ar. Ajrāil) is the guardian there. 

7 

Know that the inside entirely is a place of fire. 

There a fire blazes constantly, but it is never to be heard. 

8 

Know that the *mūlādhār* \[*cakra*\] is the risen sun, and Understand that the existent-self (*jībattamā*) is its lord. 

9 

With the eyes closed and hearing withdrawn, there repeat the Name (*jikir*). 

Respecting the spiritual guide (*murśid*), follow his or her way.<sup>19</sup> There in the lotus dwells the lord of the house. 

10 

Kindle a fire in this land each day. 

11 

So that it can never be extinguished, Kindle this fire with the utmost care. 

12 

This fire renders the body immortal, Be careful that it never goes out. 

13 

Know that this fire is ever always in the Three Points. 

Thus for the tenth door you will make a lock. 

14 

Just as a beast squeezes to tighten \[the anus\] after it shits, In such a way apply pressure to the hidden root.

Always stay mindful of the breath within the body.

Principles of the Colors 

237 

Water, fire, earth, air — these are the four elements, Now I will tell you about their colors. 

238 

The wind is the color of green leaves, the fire is black, The color of the sun is red, and water is white. 

239
