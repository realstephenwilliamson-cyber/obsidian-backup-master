## Mūla

संवित्तिफलभेदोऽत्र न प्रकल्प्यो मनीषिभिः ।
भेदोऽपरोऽपि संक्षेपात्कथ्यमानोऽवधार्यताम् ॥ ^mula

samvittiphalabhedo 'tra na prakalpyo manīṣibhiḥ |
bhedo 'paro 'pi samkṣepāt kathyamāno 'vadhāryatām || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, July 2022 – September 2022
> p. 33 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Malini (journal), July-September 2022.pdf#page=35>))
>
> The wise should not imagine that there is any difference here in the fruit which is consciousness.[^43] (with 2.25ab)

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, October 2022 – December 2022
> p. 19 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=26>))
>
> **Chapter Two of the Mālinīvijayottara Tantra: [... continued from the Previous Issue] – Mark Dyczkowski – The Penetrations and the Five States of Consciousness of the Seven Perceivers**
>
> Another classification is now also taught in brief. Pay heed! The wise should know that the sequence of all the penetrations (āveśakrama) is (also) divided into another five types, each one distinct (from the other) in accord with the variety (bheda) of waking, dreaming and the rest (of the five states of consciousness).[^45] (with 2.25cd–26)

[^43]: MV 2/25ab is quoted in TĀv ad 1/226cd-227ab (227), 5/155cd-156ab (154cd-155ab), 34/3 and in the introductory remarks to 13/1.
[^45]: See TĀ 10/178b. I read with MS Kh: *bhedaiś cānyaiḥ pṛthag pṛthak* for *svavyāpārāt pṛthak pṛthak* – '(divided into five states of consciousness) because its own manner of functioning (of each state) is distinct (from the others).' MV 2/27-35ab is quoted in TĀv ad 10/302-308. TĀ 10/302 is a direct quote of MV 2/27 and TĀ 10/303-308 are an explanatory paraphrase of MV 2/28-33ab.
