## Chapter 147

**147:1** Hallelujah.  
It is good to chant hymns to our God;  
it is pleasant to sing glorious praise. ^147-1

**147:2** The LORD rebuilds Jerusalem;  
He gathers in the exiles of Israel. ^147-2

**147:3** He heals their broken hearts,  
and binds up their wounds. ^147-3

**147:4** He reckoned the number of the stars;  
to each He gave its name. ^147-4

**147:5** Great is our Lord and full of power;  
His wisdom is beyond reckoning. ^147-5

**147:6** The LORD gives courage to the lowly,  
and brings the wicked down to the dust. ^147-6

**147:7** Sing to the LORD a song of praise,  
chant a hymn with a lyre to our God, ^147-7

**147:8** who covers the heavens with clouds,  
provides rain for the earth,  
makes mountains put forth grass; ^147-8

**147:9** who gives the beasts their food,  
to the raven’s brood what they cry for. ^147-9

**147:10** He does not prize the strength of horses,  
nor value the fleetness[^1] of men; ^147-10

**147:11** but the LORD values those who fear Him,  
those who depend on His faithful care. ^147-11

**147:12** O Jerusalem, glorify the LORD;  
praise your God, O Zion! ^147-12

**147:13** For He made the bars of your gates strong,  
and blessed your children within you. ^147-13

**147:14** He endows your realm with well-being,  
and satisfies you with choice wheat. ^147-14

**147:15** He sends forth His word to the earth;  
His command runs swiftly. ^147-15

**147:16** He lays down snow like fleece,  
scatters frost like ashes. ^147-16

**147:17** He tosses down hail like crumbs—  
who can endure His icy cold? ^147-17

**147:18** He issues a command—it melts them;  
He breathes—the waters flow. ^147-18

**147:19** He issued His commands to Jacob,  
His statutes and rules to Israel. ^147-19

**147:20** He did not do so for any other nation;  
of such rules they know nothing.  
Hallelujah. ^147-20

[^1]: Lit. “thighs.”
