## Mūla

तदीशाः शक्तिशंभू च पञ्चावस्थाः प्रकीर्तिताः ।
पूर्ववत्सप्तभेदेऽपि स्वं मन्त्रेशेशशक्तयः ॥ ^mula

tadīśāḥ śaktiśambhū ca paṣcāvasthāḥ prakīrtitāḥ |
pūrvavat saptabhede 'pi svam mantreśeśaśaktayaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, October 2022 – December 2022
> p. 20 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=27>))
>
> (D) When (the Consciousness Deconditioned (perceivers) become objects and) the division is said to be nine-fold, the five states are said to be (1) their own (innate nature) (svam), (2) the Mantra (perceivers), (3) the Lords of Mantras, (4) their Lords (the Great Lords of Mantras), and (5) Śakti and Śiva. (with 2.31cd–32ab)

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, October 2022 – December 2022
> p. 21 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=28>))
>
> (E) O fair-hued Lady, again, when (the Mantra perceivers are the object and so) the division is into seven, the five (states) should be known to be (divided) as before as (1) (their) innate nature, (2) the Lords of Mantra, (3) (their) Lords (the Great Lords of Mantra), (4) Śakti and (5) Śiva. (with 2.32cd–33ab)
