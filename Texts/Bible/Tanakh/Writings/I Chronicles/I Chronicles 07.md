## Chapter 7

**7:1** The sons of Issachar: Tola, Puah, Jashub, and Shimron—four. ^7-1

**7:2** The sons of Tola: Uzzi, Rephaiah, Jeriel, Jahmai, Ibsam, Shemuel, chiefs of their clans, men of substance according to their lines; their number in the days of David was 22,600. ^7-2

**7:3** The sons of Uzzi: Izrahiah. And the sons of Izrahiah: Michael, Obadiah, Joel, and Isshiah—five. All of them were chiefs. ^7-3

**7:4** And together with them, by their lines, according to their clans, were units of the fighting force, 36,000, for they had many wives and sons. ^7-4

**7:5** Their kinsmen belonging to all the families of Issachar were in all 87,000 men of substance; they were all registered by genealogy. ^7-5

**7:6** [The sons of] Benjamin: Bela, Becher, and Jediael—three. ^7-6

**7:7** The sons of Bela: Ezbon, Uzzi, Uzziel, Jerimoth, and Iri—five, chiefs of clans, men of substance, registered by genealogy—22,034. ^7-7

**7:8** The sons of Becher: Zemirah, Joash, Eliezer, Elioenai, Omri, Jeremoth, Abijah, Anathoth, and Alemeth. All these were the sons of Becher; ^7-8

**7:9** and they were registered by genealogy according to their lines, as chiefs of their clans, men of substance—20,200. ^7-9

**7:10** The sons of Jediael: Bilhan. And the sons of Bilhan: Jeush, Benjamin, Ehud, Chenaanah, Zethan, Tarshish, and Ahishahar. ^7-10

**7:11** All these were the sons of Jediael, chiefs of the clans, men of substance—17,200, who made up the fighting force. ^7-11

**7:12** And Shuppim and Huppim were the sons of Ir; Hushim the sons of Aher. ^7-12

**7:13** The sons of Naphtali: Jahziel, Guni, Jezer, and Shallum, the descendants of Bilhah. ^7-13

**7:14** The sons of Manasseh: Asriel, whom his Aramean concubine bore; she bore Machir the father of Gilead. ^7-14

**7:15** And Machir took wives for Huppim and for Shuppim. The name of his sister was Maacah. And the name of the second was Zelophehad; and Zelophehad had daughters. ^7-15

**7:16** And Maacah the wife of Machir bore a son, and she named him Peresh; and the name of his brother was Sheresh; and his sons were Ulam and Rekem. ^7-16

**7:17** The sons of Ulam: Bedan. These were the sons of Gilead son of Machir son of Manasseh. ^7-17

**7:18** And his sister Hammolecheth bore Ishhod, Abiezer, and Mahlah. ^7-18

**7:19** The sons of Shemida were Ahian, Shechem, Likhi, and Aniam. ^7-19

**7:20** The sons of Ephraim: Shuthelah, his son Bered, his son Tahath, his son Eleadah, his son Tahath, ^7-20

**7:21** his son Zabad, his son Shuthelah, also Ezer and Elead. The men of Gath, born in the land, killed them because they had gone down to take their cattle. ^7-21

**7:22** And Ephraim their father mourned many days, and his brothers came to comfort him. ^7-22

**7:23** He cohabited with his wife, who conceived and bore a son; and she named him Beriah, because it occurred when there was misfortune[^1] in his house. ^7-23

**7:24** His daughter was Sheerah, who built both Lower and Upper Beth-horon, and Uzzen-sheerah. ^7-24

**7:25** His son Rephah, his son Resheph, his son Telah, his son Tahan, ^7-25

**7:26** his son Ladan, his son Ammihud, his son Elishama, ^7-26

**7:27** his son Non, his son Joshua. ^7-27

**7:28** Their possessions and settlements were Bethel and its dependencies, and on the east Naaran, and on the west Gezer and its dependencies, Shechem and its dependencies, and Aiah and its dependencies; ^7-28

**7:29** also along the borders of the Manassites, Beth-shean and its dependencies, Taanach and its dependencies, Megiddo and its dependencies, Dor and its dependencies. In these dwelt the sons of Joseph son of Israel. ^7-29

**7:30** The sons of Asher: Imnah, Ishvah, Ishvi, Beriah, and their sister Serah. ^7-30

**7:31** The sons of Beriah: Heber and Malchiel, who was the father of Birzaith. ^7-31

**7:32** Heber begot Japhlet, Shomer, Hotham, and their sister, Shua. ^7-32

**7:33** The sons of Japhlet: Pasach, Bimhal, and Ashvath. These were the sons of Japhlet. ^7-33

**7:34** The sons of Shemer: Ahi, Rohgah, Hubbah, and Aram. ^7-34

**7:35** The sons of Helem his brother: Zophah, Imna, Shelesh, and Amal. ^7-35

**7:36** The sons of Zophah: Suah, Harnepher, Shual, Beri, Imrah, ^7-36

**7:37** Bezer, Hod, Shamma, Shilshah, Ithran, and Beera. ^7-37

**7:38** The sons of Jether: Jephunneh, Pispa, and Ara. ^7-38

**7:39** The sons of Ulla: Arah, Hanniel, and Rizia. ^7-39

**7:40** All of these men of Asher, chiefs of the clans, select men, men of substance, heads of the chieftains. And they were registered by genealogy according to fighting force; the number of the men was 26,000 men. ^7-40

[^1]: Heb. bera‘ah.
