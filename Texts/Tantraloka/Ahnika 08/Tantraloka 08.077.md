## Mūla

तत्रैव दक्षिणे हेमहिमवद्द्वितयान्तरे ।
कैन्नरं नवसाहस्रं तत्सहस्राधिकायुषम् ॥ ^mula

tatraiva dakṣiṇe hemahimavaddvitayāntare |
kainnaraṃ navasāhasraṃ tatsahasrādhikāyuṣam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 04 - Chapter Eight, part 2#^pg143|p. 125]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=143>))
>
> There, to the south (of Meru), between the Hemakūṭa (Gold Peak) and Himavat (mountains), lies (the land of the) Kinnaras (that extends for) nine thousand (leagues), and the lifespan (of the creatures there) is one thousand (years) more than that.
