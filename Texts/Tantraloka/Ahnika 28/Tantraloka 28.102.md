## Mūla

जानन्ति प्रथमं गेहं ततस्तस्य समर्थताम् ।
बलाबलं ततः पश्चाद्विस्मयन्तेऽत्र मातरः ॥ ^mula

jānanti prathamaṃ gehaṃ tatastasya samarthatām |
balābalaṃ tataḥ paścādvismayante'tra mātaraḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 04 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 2#^pg68|p. 51]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=68>))
>
> (It is done seven times because) 1) the first (time), all the Mothers (come to) know (their devotee’s) house, 2) then (his) ability, and 3) after that, (his) strength and weakness. 4) Then they are amazed (with admiration) here, and they come in person, and 6) they (all) become joyful and 7) bestow (upon him) a boon. This, the Worship of the Embodiments is beloved of the Goddesses, and (so too) the Lord, who is accompanied by (his spiritual) family (parivāra) (that delights in it also). Thus, the wise (and learned) should do it. (with 28.102–104ab)
