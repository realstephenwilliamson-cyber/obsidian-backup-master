# Psalm 80

← [[BCP Psalm 079]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 081]] →

*Qui regis Israel* · Book Three · Sixteenth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 080]]

**80:1** Hear, O Shepherd of Israel, leading Joseph like a flock; \*  
shine forth, you that are enthroned upon the cherubim. ^80-1

**80:2** In the presence of Ephraim, Benjamin, and Manasseh, \*  
stir up your strength and come to help us. ^80-2

**80:3** Restore us, O God of hosts; \*  
show the light of your countenance, and we shall be saved. ^80-3

**80:4** O LORD God of hosts, \*  
how long will you be angered  
despite the prayers of your people? ^80-4

**80:5** You have fed them with the bread of tears; \*  
you have given them bowls of tears to drink. ^80-5

**80:6** You have made us the derision of our neighbors, \*  
and our enemies laugh us to scorn. ^80-6

[p. 703]

**80:7** Restore us, O God of hosts; \*  
show the light of your countenance, and we shall be saved. ^80-7

**80:8** You have brought a vine out of Egypt; \*  
you cast out the nations and planted it. ^80-8

**80:9** You prepared the ground for it; \*  
it took root and filled the land. ^80-9

**80:10** The mountains were covered by its shadow \*  
and the towering cedar trees by its boughs. ^80-10

**80:11** You stretched out its tendrils to the Sea \*  
and its branches to the River. ^80-11

**80:12** Why have you broken down its wall, \*  
so that all who pass by pluck off its grapes? ^80-12

**80:13** The wild boar of the forest has ravaged it, \*  
and the beasts of the field have grazed upon it. ^80-13

**80:14** Turn now, O God of hosts, look down from heaven;  
behold and tend this vine; \*  
preserve what your right hand has planted. ^80-14

**80:15** They burn it with fire like rubbish; \*  
at the rebuke of your countenance let them perish. ^80-15

**80:16** Let your hand be upon the man of your right hand, \*  
the son of man you have made so strong for yourself. ^80-16

**80:17** And so will we never turn away from you; \*  
give us life, that we may call upon your Name. ^80-17

**80:18** Restore us, O LORD God of hosts; \*  
show the light of your countenance, and we shall be saved. ^80-18
