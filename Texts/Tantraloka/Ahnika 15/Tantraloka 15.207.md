## Mūla

मुख्यत्वेन खमेवोर्ध्वं प्रकाशमयमुच्यते ।
तदेव मुख्यतोऽधस्तादप्रकाशं यतः स्फुटम् ॥ ^mula

mukhyatvena khamevordhvaṃ prakāśamayamucyate |
tadeva mukhyato'dhastādaprakāśaṃ yataḥ sphuṭam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 05 - Chapter Fifteen, part 4#^pg146|p. 128]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=146>))
>
> These, the faces (of Sadāśiva), are (respectively,) Space, Air, Fire, Water and Earth. The zenith is primarily the Space said to be the Light (of consciousness). The nadir is (also) primarily that same (Space), as it is clearly the absence of light. (with 15.206cd–207)
