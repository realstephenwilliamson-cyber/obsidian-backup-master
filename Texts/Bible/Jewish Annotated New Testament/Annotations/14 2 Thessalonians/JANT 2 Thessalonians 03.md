# 2 Thessalonians 3 (JANT notes)

Text: [[NRSVue 2 Thessalonians 03|NRSVue]] · [[2 Thessalonians 03|BSB and SBLGNT]] · Introduction: [[JANT 2 Thessalonians - Introduction]]

### 3.1–5: Prayer and encouragement

*[[NRSVue 2 Thessalonians 03#^3-1|Text]], p. 430* ^3-1

**[[NRSVue 2 Thessalonians 03#^3-1|1]]:** *Spread rapidly and be glorified*, successful preaching leads to glorification of God and perhaps conversion ([[Matthew 05#^5-16|Mt 5.16]]; [[Acts 13#^13-48|Acts 13.48]]; [[Romans 15#^15-9|Rom 15.9]]); many Jews likewise hoped Gentiles would recognize the God of Israel as the one true god, but attitudes toward proselytism varied and there were few serious efforts to convert Gentiles to Judaism (see [[Ezekiel 38#^38-23|Ezek 38.23]]; [[Ezekiel 39#^39-27|39.27]]; [[Zechariah 08#^8-23|Zech 8.23]]; *b. Shabb.* [[Shabbat 31#31a|31a]]). ^3-1-2

**[[NRSVue 2 Thessalonians 03#^3-3|3]]:** *Evil one* may be Satan (2.9); see the “Our Father” prayer in [[Matthew 06#^6-13|Mt 6.13]]. ^3-3

**[[NRSVue 2 Thessalonians 03#^3-5|5]]:** *Direct your hearts*, hinting at God’s control over the believers’ faith; [[Psalms 010#^10-17|Ps 10.17]]; [[I Chronicles 29#^29-18|1 Chr 29.18]]. Regardless of one’s knowledge of Torah, what is important is that “one directs one’s heart to heaven,” often with divine aid (*m. Men.* 13.11; *b. Ber.* [[Berakhot 16#16b|16b]]–17a). ^3-5

### 3.6–13: Denunciations of idleness

*[[NRSVue 2 Thessalonians 03#^3-6|Text]], p. 430* ^3-6

**[[NRSVue 2 Thessalonians 03#^3-6|6]]:** *Command*, stern language demonstrating the seriousness of the threat. ^3-6-2

**[[NRSVue 2 Thessalonians 03#^3-7|7]]:** *Imitate*, see [[1 Corinthians 04#^4-16|1 Cor 4.16]]; [[1 Corinthians 11#^11-1|11.1]]; [[Philippians 03#^3-17|Phil 3.17]]; *b. Ber.* [[Berakhot 62#62a|62a]], on imitating the actions of the rabbis in all areas of life. ^3-7

**[[NRSVue 2 Thessalonians 03#^3-8|8]]:** *Toil and labor*, [[Proverbs 10#^10-4|Prov 10.4]]; [[1 Thessalonians 02#^2-9|1 Thess 2.9]]; [[1 Thessalonians 05#^5-12|5.12–14]]; *Did.* 12.4–5; *m. Avot* 2.2 praises combining Torah study and labor, and rabbinic sources extol both manual labor (*ARN B* XXI, 23a) and commend fathers who teach their sons a craft (*m. Qidd*. 4.14; *t. Qidd*. 1.11; *b. Qidd*. [[Kiddushin 29#29a|29a]]; see also *m. Bik*. 3.3; *b. Qidd*. [[Kiddushin 33#33a|33a]]). Idleness may have been unseemly in the eyes of non-Christian neighbors, though some pagans disdained labor, at least by the learned. First Thess 4.12 requires that believers not depend on others. ^3-8

**[[NRSVue 2 Thessalonians 03#^3-9|9]]:** *Right*, see [[1 Corinthians 09#^9-4|1 Cor 9.4–15]]. ^3-9

**[[NRSVue 2 Thessalonians 03#^3-11|11]]:** *Living in idleness*, perhaps because they believed the end had arrived (see [[1 Thessalonians 04#^4-11|1 Thess 4.11]]). ^3-11

### 3.14–15: Warning the disobedient

*[[NRSVue 2 Thessalonians 03#^3-14|Text]], p. 431* · *Have nothing to do with them*, perhaps ejecting them from communal meals (3.10; [[Matthew 18#^18-17|Mt 18.17]]; [[1 Corinthians 05#^5-3|1 Cor 5.3]]; [[2 John 01#^1-10|2 Jn 1.10]]; 1QS 6–8). *Enemies* are former church members or those in disagreement with the Letter’s details. Similarly, Rabbi Eliezer was shunned by his colleagues after a legal dispute (*b. B. Metz*. [[Bava Metzia 59#59b|59b]]). ^3-14

### 3.16–18: Closing

*[[NRSVue 2 Thessalonians 03#^3-16|Text]], p. 431* · . The letter’s closing parallels [[1 Thessalonians 05#^5-23|1 Thess 5.23–28]] (cf. [[Torah 35 - Nasso#^6-24|Num 6.24–26]]). ^3-16

**[[NRSVue 2 Thessalonians 03#^3-17|17]]:** *Own hand*, [[1 Corinthians 16#^16-21|1 Cor 16.21]]; [[Colossians 04#^4-18|Col 4.18]]. ^3-17
