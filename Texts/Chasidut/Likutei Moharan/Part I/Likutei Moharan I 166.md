# Likutei Moharan I 166

← [[Likutei Moharan I 165]] · [[Likutei Moharan]] · [[Likutei Moharan I 167]] →

*Part I · Lesson 166* · [[Likutei Moharan]]

## 1

When people are by the tzaddik, he then has sovereignty.

Thus, Elisha is sometimes referred to as “a man of God” ([[II Kings 05#^5-8|2 Kings 5:8]]), and sometimes simply as “Elisha.” Our Sages teach ([[Zohar 4.06 - Parashat Be-Shallah#^z2-44b|Zohar II, 44b]]): When the student-prophets were with him, he was called “a man of God”; but when he was alone, he was simply called “Elisha.”
