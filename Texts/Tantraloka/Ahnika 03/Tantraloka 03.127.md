## Mūla

न तु सा ज्ञातृता यस्यां शुद्धज्ञेयाद्यपेक्षते ।
तस्यां दशायां ज्ञातृत्वमुच्यते योग्यतावशात् ॥ ^mula

na tu sā jñātṛtā yasyāṃ śuddhajñeyādyapekṣate |
tasyāṃ daśāyāṃ jñātṛtvamucyate yogyatāvaśāt || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. II
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 02 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v02 08 - Chapter Three, part 5#^pg250|p. 234]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 02.pdf#page=250>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 3.127cd–128ab.
>
> The knower (jñātṛ) who, by his very nature, is the direct experience (anubhava) (which is pure awareness free of thought constructs) is no ‘knower’ at all; the state of subjectivity (jñātṛtā) is absent in that which is dependent (even) on the pure (inner) object of knowledge (at one with consciousness, or on anything else). (with 3.126cd–127ab)
>
> It is called subjectivity on that leve (of Māyā on which consciousness is obscured by objectivity), because it is fit (to become such, and can moreover distinguish one object from another). This (level) corresponds to that of the means of knowledge (mentally) conceived (parikalpita) by the (supreme) subject, that precedes it (and on which it depends). (with 3.127cd–128ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* ननु यद्येवं तर्हि कथमस्य सर्वत्रैव ज्ञातृत्वमुच्यमानमपह्नूयताम् ? -- इत्याशङ्क्याह --

> तस्यां दशायां ज्ञातृत्वमुच्यते योग्यतावशात् ॥
> मानतैव तु सा प्राच्यप्रमातृपरिकल्पिता ।

तस्यामिति -- संकुचितप्रमातृरूपायाम् -- इत्यर्थः । योग्यतावशादिति - - ज्ञेयपरिच्छेदकारित्वलक्षणात् । सा पुनरियं दशा प्राच्येन आदिसिद्धेन परेण प्रमात्रा प्रमाणरूपत्वेन परिकल्पिता संकुचितज्ञानात्मकत्वात्तथात्वेनावभासिता -- इत्यर्थः । पर एव हि प्रमाता स्वस्वातन्त्र्यात् स्वं स्वरूपं गोपयित्वा संकुचितज्ञानात्मतामवभास्य ज्ञेयमपि पृथगुल्लासयेत् ॥ १२७ ॥
