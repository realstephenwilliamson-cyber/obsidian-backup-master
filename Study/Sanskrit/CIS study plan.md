# CIS study plan

A once-through plan for Antonia Ruppel's *The Cambridge Introduction to Sanskrit* ([[The Cambridge Introduction to Sanskrit (Ruppel)|the book in the vault]]). Your own notes on a chapter go in its companion note ([[Ruppel - Cambridge Introduction to Sanskrit|the list of companions]]: *Ruppel 1*, *Ruppel 2* …).

## How this plan works

- **Progress, not dates.** Each chapter has three or four steps with checkboxes. The current step is the first one not ticked; Home and the daily note show it with the day's session length. Tick a step when it is done (the Tasks plugin adds the date). Nothing is ever "behind".
- **The week.** Monday, Wednesday and Friday are the heavier days (reading, exercises, Readings); Tuesday and Thursday are review (the drill and the cards that are due). Saturday and Sunday are off for this track.
- **Session length** is in the two properties above: `cis_mwf` (minutes on Monday, Wednesday and Friday) and `cis_tth` (Tuesday and Thursday). They start at 15 and 5 and are raised by hand; the first plateau is 45/15, reached in days or weeks, with later plateaus if it holds and life allows. Nothing raises them automatically.
- **Each chapter:** 1 Read (M/W/F) → 2 Drill (T/Th; the unit of memory is a word, and forms are learned through exemplar words) → 3 Exercises, with the answer key after the drill (M/W/F) → 4 the Reading at the end of the chapter, from Chapter 5 on (M/W/F). Ruppel suggests doing a chapter's Readings when you are already working on the next chapter, since they often use what that chapter introduces ([[The Cambridge Introduction 04 - Chapter 0 Studying Sanskrit|Chapter 0]], p. 3); if you work that way, move the N.4 line below the next chapter's first step.
- **Opening block** (Chapters 1, 2 and 2a): a few weeks on the script, the sounds and reading in rhythm, with the daily letter card and the [[Conjunct Consonants - Reference List (Ruppel)|conjunct consonants]].
- **Course labels.** Each chapter shows its term and week in the Yogic Studies course (SKT 101: Chapters 1–12; 102: 13–24; 103: 25–36). They are an optional guide, not a track; whether 102 and 103 are used is decided at Chapter 13. Chapters 37–40 are outside the course.
- **Links** go to the chapter notes, not to pages, until the page labels in the chapter notes have been checked.

## Cards

- One review system, in the vault: the Spaced Repetition plugin. A chapter's cards are in its note `CIS Ch. N cards` (folder `Study/Sanskrit/CIS cards`), made as the plan reaches the chapter; the 108 letter cards are in `Study/Sanskrit/Flashcards`.
- The cards sample the paradigm: a verb across the persons and numbers introduced so far, a noun across its cases, in both directions; the citation form is one card among them.
- Sandhi is one long-lived set of cards that grows from Chapter 8 (then 9, 10, 11, 13 and 16) and is reviewed throughout.
- Handwriting: write first, then flip the card, in an Ink writing section — on the card note under *Practice*, or on the [[Sanskrit scratchpad]].
- Reference: [[CIS - Vocabulary (Ruppel)|vocabulary]] · [[CIS - Sandhi (Ruppel)|sandhi]] · [[CIS - Reference Grammar (Ruppel)|reference grammar]].
