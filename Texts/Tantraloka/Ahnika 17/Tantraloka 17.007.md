## Mūla

श्रुत्यन्ते केऽप्यतः शुक्लकृष्णरक्तं प्रपेदिरे ।
ततोऽग्नौ तर्पिताशेषमन्त्रे चिद्व्योममात्रके ॥ ^mula

śrutyante ke'pyataḥ śuklakṛṣṇaraktaṃ prapedire |
tato'gnau tarpitāśeṣamantre cidvyomamātrake || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 08 - Chapter Seventeen#^pg162|p. 142]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=162>))
>
> Thus, some have taught in the Vedas that each of the three, namely, fire, water and food, is threefold, that is, white, black and red. (with 17.6cd–7ab)
>
> Then one should purify the principles one after the other (kramāt) in the fire, within which libation has been offered to (all) the mantras, and (consisting of) the Void of Consciousness alone, is (their) universal (generic) nature. (with 17.7cd–8ab)

*Lead-in:* एवं सूत्रकॢप्तिमभिधाय तत्त्वशुद्धिमाह--

> ततोऽग्नौ तर्पिताशेषमन्त्रे चिद्व्योममात्रके ॥
> सामान्यरूपे तत्त्वानां क्रमाच्छुद्धिं समाचरेत् ।

सामान्यरूपे चिद्व्योममात्रके इति तत्सात्करणमेव हि नाम अस्मद्दर्शने शुद्धिः--इत्याशयः ॥
