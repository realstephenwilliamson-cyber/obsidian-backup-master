## Mūla

अथ प्राणस्य या वृत्तिः प्राणनाद्या निरूपिता ।
तदुपायतया ब्रूमोऽनुत्तरप्रविकासनम् ॥ ^mula

atha prāṇasya yā vṛttiḥ prāṇanādyā nirūpitā |
tadupāyatayā brūmo'nuttarapravikāsanam || ^iast

## Translation

> Now we will explain how the activity (vṛtti) of the vital breath (prāṇa), described as that of the vitality (prāṇanā) and the other (macrocosmic counterparts of the breaths of the microcosmic body), can be applied as a means to the unfolding (realization of) the Absolute (Anuttara).
