## Mūla

अप्रकाशा स्वप्रकाशाद्धर्मादेति प्रकाशताम् ।
प्रकाशे खलु विश्रान्तिं विश्वं श्रयति चेत्ततः ॥ ^mula

aprakāśā svaprakāśāddharmādeti prakāśatām |
prakāśe khalu viśrāntiṃ viśvaṃ śrayati cettataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 11 - Chapter Ten, part 2#^pg364|p. 345]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=364>))
>
> (This objection does not affect us at all. The truth is) that perceptibility (vedyatā), like other qualities, such as blue and the like, is unmanifest (aprakāśa) in itself (svayam), (but) manifests (‘shines’) by virtue of that quality of self-luminosity (that is the inherent nature of perceptibility, which is not perceptible in the manner of an object). (with 10.75cd–76ab)
>
> If objectivity thus abides at rest (in this way) in the light (of consciousness), it depends on nothing else, and has accomplished its task in every respect. (with 10.76cd–77ab)
