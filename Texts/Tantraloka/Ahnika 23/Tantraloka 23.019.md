## Mūla

विभवेन सुविस्तीर्णं ततस्तस्मै वदेत्स्वकम् ।
सर्वं कर्तव्यसारं यच्छास्त्राणां परमं रहः ॥ ^mula

vibhavena suvistīrṇaṃ tatastasmai vadetsvakam |
sarvaṃ kartavyasāraṃ yacchāstrāṇāṃ paramaṃ rahaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 14 - Chapter Twenty-Three#^pg323|p. 303]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=323>))
>
> After having worshipped and offered oblations as before and declared what he intends to do, the teacher should then sprinkle the disciple sixty-four (times) and then just once with water (infused with) the aesthetic essence (rasa) of the Mantras (used to consecrate it), observing the procedure (vidhi) taught above. (He should perform the rite) sumptuously and at great length. Then (the teacher) should tell (the disciple) all the essence of his duties and the supreme secret of the scriptures. (with 23.17cd–19)
>
> (The teacher should address Śiva saying): “(these are) the disciples who, impelled by Śiva’s power, are worthy of being graced by You.” (with 23.17cd–20ab)

## Viveka

> प्राग्वत्संपूज्य हुत्वा च श्रावयित्वा चिकीर्षितम् ॥
> ततोऽभिषिञ्चेत्तं शिष्यं चतुःषष्ट्या ततः सकृत् ।
> तन्मन्त्ररसतोयेन पूर्वोक्तविधिना गुरुः ॥
> विभवेन सुविस्तीर्णं ततस्तस्मै वदेत्स्वकम् ।
> सर्वं कर्तव्यसारं यच्छास्त्राणां परमं रहः ॥

चतुःषष्ट्येति--अर्थात्कलशैः । सकृदिति--एकेन । अनेन च ज्ञानस्यैव प्राधान्यात् क्रियायाः अनवकॢप्तिः प्रकाशिता, येन श्रीपूर्वशास्त्रे स्वकण्ठोक्तोऽपि अभिषेकविधिरिह वितत्य नोक्तः ॥
