## Mūla

स्थूलोपायः परोपायस्त्वेष मात्राकृतो लयः ।
अर्धमात्रा नव नव स्युश्चतुर्षु चतुर्षु यत् ॥ ^mula

sthūlopāyaḥ paropāyastveṣa mātrākṛto layaḥ |
ardhamātrā nava nava syuścaturṣu caturṣu yat || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 13 - Chapter Six, part 6#^pg452|p. 437]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=452>))
> Dyczkowski's numbering differs here; his markers: (231) (230ab-231ab), (232) (231cd-232ab).
>
> The gross means is nurtured by the exalted (udāra) progression (of the forms of) reflective awareness (that resonate through) the eighty-one parts (of the Mantra). The supreme means is this merger (laya) (that is, rest within one’s own nature) brought about by the measures (mātrā) (that are the energies of the Mantra). (with 6.230ab–231ab)
>
> The half measures are arranged, nine by nine, within every four (finger-breadths of the flow of the breath). (In this way,) the eighty-one parts arise (within a span of) thirty-six fingers’ (breadth). (with 6.231cd–232ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* एवमेतत्प्रसङ्गादभिधाय प्रकृतमेवानुसरति --

> अर्धमात्रा नव नव स्युश्चतुर्षु चतुर्षु यत् ॥
> अंगुलेष्विति षट्त्रिंशत्येकाशीतिपदोदयः ।

प्रत्यंगुलचतुष्टयं नवार्धमात्राः -- इत्यंगुलषट्त्रिंशदात्मनि प्राणचारेऽर्धमात्राणामेकाशीतिरुदियात् ॥ २३१ ॥
