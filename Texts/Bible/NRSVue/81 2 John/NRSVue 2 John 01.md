# 2 John 1 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue|index]]

Compare: [[2 John 01|BSB and SBLGNT]] · [[DRB 2 John 01|Douay-Rheims]] · [[JANT 2 John 01|JANT notes]]

## Salutation

**1:1** The elder to the elect lady and her children, whom I love in the truth, and not only I but also all who know the truth, ^1-1

**1:2** because of the truth that abides in us and will be with us forever: ^1-2

**1:3** Grace, mercy, and peace will be with us from God the Father and from^[Other ancient authorities add *the Lord*] Jesus Christ, the Father’s Son, in truth and love. ^1-3

## Truth and love

**1:4** I was overjoyed to find some of your children walking in the truth, just as we have been commanded by the Father. ^1-4

**1:5** But now, dear lady, I ask you, not as though I were writing you a new commandment but one we have had from the beginning: let us love one another. ^1-5

**1:6** And this is love, that we walk according to his commandments; this is the commandment just as you have heard it from the beginning—you must walk in it. ^1-6

**1:7** Many deceivers have gone out into the world, those who do not confess that Jesus Christ has come in the flesh; any such person is the deceiver and the antichrist! ^1-7

**1:8** Be on your guard, so that you do not lose what we^[Other ancient authorities read *you*] have worked for but may receive a full reward. ^1-8

**1:9** Everyone who does not abide in the teaching of Christ, but goes beyond it,^[Other ancient authorities read *turns aside*] does not have God; whoever abides in the teaching^[Other ancient authorities add *of Christ*] has both the Father and the Son. ^1-9

**1:10** If anyone comes to you and does not bring this teaching, do not receive and welcome this person into your house, ^1-10

**1:11** for to welcome is to participate in the evil deeds of such a person. ^1-11

## Final greetings

**1:12** Although I have much to write to you, I would rather not use paper and ink; instead, I hope to come to you and talk with you face to face, so that our^[Other ancient authorities read *your*] joy may be complete. ^1-12

**1:13** The children of your elect sister send you their greetings.^[Other ancient authorities add *Amen*] ^1-13
