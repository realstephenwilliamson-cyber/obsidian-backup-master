## Mūla

तत्त्वज्ञानार्कविध्वस्तध्वान्तस्य तु न कोऽप्ययम् ।
अन्त्येष्टिश्राद्धविध्यादिरुपयोगी कदाचन ॥ ^mula

tattvajñānārkavidhvastadhvāntasya tu na ko'pyayam |
antyeṣṭiśrāddhavidhyādirupayogī kadācana || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 17 - Chapter Twenty-Five (continued) and Chapter Twenty-Six#^pg379|p. 359]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=379>))
>
> None of these procedures, be it the last rites or the offerings to the ancestors etc., is ever of any use (and serves no function) for one whose darkness (of ignorance) has been dispelled by the sun of the knowledge of reality.
