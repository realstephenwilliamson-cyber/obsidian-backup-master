# 1 Peter 4 (JANT notes)

[[JANT 1 Peter 03|← 1 Peter 3]] · [[Jewish Annotated New Testament|JANT index]] · [[JANT 1 Peter 05|1 Peter 5 →]]

Text: [[NRSVue 1 Peter 04|NRSVue]] · [[1 Peter 04|BSB and SBLGNT]] · Introduction: [[JANT 1 Peter - Introduction]]

**[[NRSVue 1 Peter 04#^4-1|4.1–6]]:** **Rejection of former life**. ^4-1

**[[NRSVue 1 Peter 04#^4-1|1]]:** *Same intention*, which they share with Christ, namely *the will of God* (4.2). *Finished with sin*, in choosing to suffer in order to battle “earthy desires,” readers choose righteousness rather than sin. ^4-1-2

**[[NRSVue 1 Peter 04#^4-3|3]]:** *Gentiles* (Gk “ethnos”), or pagans. *Licentiousness . . . idolatry*, contrast between “you” and “the Gentiles,” see, e.g., [[Torah 29 - Achrei Mot#^18-24|Lev 18.24–30]], where the children of Israel are warned about the sinful behaviors of the Canaanites. ^4-3

**[[NRSVue 1 Peter 04#^4-4|4]]:** *Blaspheme*, they speak irreverently about God when they slander Christians who no longer join them in their ways. ^4-4

**[[NRSVue 1 Peter 04#^4-5|5]]:** *Give an accounting*, the reference here seems to be to the final judgment in heaven; in rabbinic literature the day of judgment is Rosh Ha-Shanah (New Year), *t. Rosh Ha-Shanah* 1.11–13. ^4-5

**[[NRSVue 1 Peter 04#^4-6|6]]:** *Gospel was proclaimed even to the dead*, possibly a second reference to the “harrowing of hell” (3.19), but more likely a reference to those who heard the gospel proclaimed but initially rejected it. [[Luke 16#^16-26|Lk 16.26]] indicates that repenting and gaining salvation after death is impossible, but see [[Daniel 12#^12-1|Dan 12.1–3]]; [[LXX 2 Maccabees 12#^12-43|2 Macc 12.43–45]]; [[John 05#^5-25|Jn 5.25–29]]; [[1 Corinthians 15#^15-9|1 Cor 15.9]]. *Live in the spirit*, believers who have suffered like Christ will ultimately be vindicated, like Christ. Another interpretation: Christians will live by the standard of God’s spirit, rather than the standards of the flesh, those who slander Christians. ^4-6

### 4.7–11: Ethical exhortations

*[[NRSVue 1 Peter 04#^4-7|Text]], p. 508* ^4-7

**[[NRSVue 1 Peter 04#^4-7|7]]:** *Be serious and discipline yourselves*, lit., “think wisely and be sober,” see the similar calls to be sober in 1.13; 5.18. *For the sake of your prayers*, wisdom and sobriety are precon-ditions for prayer (see 3.7,12; *Lev. Rab.* 12.1), or wisdom and sobriety will bring one to pray. ^4-7-2

**[[NRSVue 1 Peter 04#^4-8|8]]:** *Love for one another*, see 1.22. *Love covers a multitude of sins*, see [[Psalms 032#^32-1|Ps 32.1]]; [[Proverbs 10#^10-12|Prov 10.12]]; [[James 05#^5-20|Jas 5.20]]. ^4-8

**[[NRSVue 1 Peter 04#^4-10|10]]:** *Gift*, see 1 Cor 12–13 *you* c Gk *rest of the time in the flesh* d Or *they malign you* e Or *is at hand* where Paul insists that believers must use their individual gifts for the good of the whole community. ^4-10

**[[NRSVue 1 Peter 04#^4-11|11]]:** *Glory and the power*, a doxology (word of glory), often found at the end of songs, liturgies, or books of the Bible, [[Torah 16 - Beshalach#^15-18|Ex 15.18]]; [[Psalms 041#^41-14|Ps 41.13]]; 146.10; [[Romans 16#^16-27|Rom 16.27]]; [[1 Peter 05#^5-11|1 Pet 5.11]]. The Kaddish prayer at the end of Jewish prayer services, which developed in the later rabbinic period, similarly functions as a doxology. ^4-11

**[[NRSVue 1 Peter 04#^4-12|4.12–19]]:** **Enduring trials**. *Fiery ordeal* . . . *to test you*, possibly a reference to martyrdom, but more likely to a general suffering that tested the faith of believers, see [[Zechariah 13#^13-9|Zech 13.9]]; [[Psalms 066#^66-10|Ps 66.10]]; [[Proverbs 17#^17-3|Prov 17.3]]; [[Job 23#^23-10|Job 23.10]]; *Gen Rab.* 55.1; *b. Ber.* 5a. ^4-12

**[[NRSVue 1 Peter 04#^4-13|13]]:** *Sharing Christ’s sufferings*, in your own suffering you are imitating Christ, 2.21–25; 3.18. The Gk “koinoneo,” meaning sharing, is the same term that is used for mutuality and sharing in the Lord’s Supper, [[1 Corinthians 10#^10-16|1 Cor 10.16]]. ^4-13

**[[NRSVue 1 Peter 04#^4-14|14]]:** *Reviled for the name of Christ*, see [[Matthew 05#^5-11|Mt 5.11–12]]. ^4-14

**[[NRSVue 1 Peter 04#^4-16|16]]:** *Christian*, the term only appears two other times in the NT, both in Acts (11.26; 26.28). Accusations of illegal or immoral behavior based on being identified as “Christian” were known around 110 according to the letters between Pliny the Younger and the Emperor Trajan. The author of 1 Peter encourages Christians to feel pride in this designation, rather than shame. ^4-16

**[[NRSVue 1 Peter 04#^4-17|17]]:** *Household of God*, the church, extending the imagery from the household codes (2.18–3.12). *Gospel of God*, see [[Romans 01#^1-1|Rom 1.1]]. ^4-17

**[[NRSVue 1 Peter 04#^4-18|18]]:** See [[LXX Proverbs 11#^11-31|Prov 11.31]]\[LXX\]. ^4-18

**[[NRSVue 1 Peter 04#^4-19|19]]:** *Faithful Creator*, the only place where the NT refers to God as creator, meaning that God controls all of history from the beginning of time until the end of days. (See similar ideas in [[Isaiah 45#^45-6|Isa 45.6–7]]; *Ex. Rab.* 21.6.) Readers are told that they can and should be faithful and trust in this “faithful Creator.” ^4-19
