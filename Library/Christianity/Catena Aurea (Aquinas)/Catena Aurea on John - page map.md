# Catena Aurea — Commentary on the Gospel of John

> [!external] Source
> Thomas Aquinas, *Catena Aurea: Commentary on the Four Gospels, Collected out of the Works of the Fathers* — St John, tr. the Dominican Fathers under John Henry Newman (Oxford, new ed. 1874; orig. 1841). Public domain. Scan: <https://archive.org/details/catenaaurestjohn00thomuoft>

The *Catena Aurea* ("golden chain") strings together comments from more than sixty Church Fathers, verse by verse. This scan’s OCR text layer is noisy throughout (common misreadings like “the”→“tlic”, “He”→“Ile”), so rather than transcribe it as if it were clean, each chapter below links straight to its first page in the scan itself.

A cleaner, manually transcribed edition exists at <https://isidore.co/aquinas/english/CAJohn.htm> if you’d rather read it there, or want it pulled into the vault properly later.
