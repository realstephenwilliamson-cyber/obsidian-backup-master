# Psalm 122

← [[BCP Psalm 121]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 123]] →

*Lætatus sum* · Book Five · Twenty-seventh Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 122]]

**122:1** I was glad when they said to me, \*  
"Let us go to the house of the LORD." ^122-1

**122:2** Now our feet are standing \*  
within your gates, O Jerusalem. ^122-2

**122:3** Jerusalem is built as a city \*  
that is at unity with itself; ^122-3

[p. 780]

**122:4** To which the tribes go up,  
the tribes of the LORD, \*  
the assembly of Israel,  
to praise the Name of the LORD. ^122-4

**122:5** For there are the thrones of judgment, \*  
the thrones of the house of David. ^122-5

**122:6** Pray for the peace of Jerusalem: \*  
"May they prosper who love you. ^122-6

**122:7** Peace be within your walls \*  
and quietness within your towers. ^122-7

**122:8** For my brethren and companions' sake, \*  
I pray for your prosperity. ^122-8

**122:9** Because of the house of the LORD our God, \*  
I will seek to do you good." ^122-9
