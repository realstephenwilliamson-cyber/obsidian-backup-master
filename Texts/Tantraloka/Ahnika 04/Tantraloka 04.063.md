## Mūla

उक्तं मुख्यतयाचार्यो भवेद्यदि न सस्फुरः ।
तत्रैव च पुनः श्रीमद्रक्ताराधनकर्मणि ॥ ^mula

uktaṃ mukhyatayācāryo bhavedyadi na sasphuraḥ |
tatraiva ca punaḥ śrīmadraktārādhanakarmaṇi || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. III
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 03 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v03 04 - Chapter Four, part 3#^pg93|p. 80]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 03.pdf#page=93>))
>
> It is taught by this (statement) that the (the adept’s) own insight (svātmavijñāna) (into his own nature) is the cause of the effulgence (of Mantras etc). (Therefore,) if there is no teacher (available) who is effulgent (with consciousness to do this for him, he should cultivate this insight) as the principal (source of their empowerment). (with 4.62cd–63ab)
>
> Again, it is said there itself (in the Brahmayāāmala) that he who applies the aforementioned procedure (vidhi) regularly (without a break) to the worship of the venerable (goddess) Raktā is said to be a regular Śaiva initiate (samayin) within three and a half days, an apprentice (destined to become a teacher) (putraka) in a week, an adept (sādhaka) in fifteen days, and a teacher (ācārya) in a month. (with 4.63cd–64)
