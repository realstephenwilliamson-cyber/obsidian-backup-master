## A psalm of thanksgiving

**2:1** Then Jonah prayed to the LORD his God from the belly of the fish, ^2-1

**2:2** saying, “I called to the LORD out of my distress, and he answered me; out of the belly of Sheol I cried, and you heard my voice. ^2-2

**2:3** You cast me into the deep, into the heart of the seas, and the flood surrounded me; all your waves and your billows passed over me. ^2-3

**2:4** Then I said, ‘I am driven away from your sight; how^[Theodotion: Heb *surely*] shall I look again upon your holy temple?’ ^2-4

**2:5** The waters closed in over me; the deep surrounded me; weeds were wrapped around my head ^2-5

**2:6** at the roots of the mountains. I went down to the land whose bars closed upon me forever; yet you brought up my life from the Pit, O LORD my God. ^2-6

**2:7** As my life was ebbing away, I remembered the LORD, and my prayer came to you, into your holy temple. ^2-7

**2:8** Those who worship vain idols forsake their true loyalty. ^2-8

**2:9** But I with the voice of thanksgiving will sacrifice to you; what I have vowed I will pay. Deliverance belongs to the LORD!” ^2-9

**2:10** Then the LORD spoke to the fish, and it vomited Jonah out onto the dry land. ^2-10
