# Ezekiel 35

Compare: [[NRSVue Ezekiel 35|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 35

**35:1** The word of the LORD came to me: ^35-1

**35:2** O mortal, set your face against Mount Seir and prophesy against it. ^35-2

**35:3** Say to it: Thus said the Lord GOD: I am going to deal with you, Mount Seir: I will stretch out My hand against you and make you an utter waste. ^35-3

**35:4** I will turn your towns into ruins, and you shall be a desolation; then you shall know that I am the LORD. ^35-4

**35:5** Because you harbored an ancient hatred and handed the people of Israel over to the sword in their time of calamity, the time set for their punishment— ^35-5

**35:6** assuredly, as I live, declares the Lord GOD, I will doom you with blood; blood shall pursue you; I swear that, for your bloodthirsty hatred, blood shall pursue you.[^1] ^35-6

**35:7** I will make Mount Seir an utter waste, and I will keep all passersby away from it. ^35-7

**35:8** I will cover its mountains with the slain; men slain by the sword shall lie on your hills, in your valleys, and in all your watercourses. ^35-8

**35:9** I will make you a desolation for all time; your towns shall never be inhabited. And you shall know that I am the LORD. ^35-9

**35:10** Because you thought “The two nations and the two lands shall be mine and we shall possess them”—although the LORD was there[^2]— ^35-10

**35:11** assuredly, as I live, declares the Lord GOD, I will act with the same anger and passion that you acted with in your hatred of them. And I will make Myself known through them when I judge you. ^35-11

**35:12** You shall know that I the LORD have heard all the taunts you uttered against the hills of Israel: “They have been laid waste; they have been given to us as prey.” ^35-12

**35:13** And you spoke arrogantly against Me and multiplied your words[^3] against Me: I have heard it. ^35-13

**35:14** Thus said the LORD God: When the whole earth rejoices, I will make you a desolation. ^35-14

**35:15** As you rejoiced when the heritage of the House of Israel was laid waste, so will I treat you: the hill country of Seir and the whole of Edom, all of it, shall be laid waste. And they shall know that I am the LORD. ^35-15

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain; emendation yields “and the Lord heard it.”
[^3]: Emendation yields “and spoke arrogantly.”
