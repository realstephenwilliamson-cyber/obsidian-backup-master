# Zechariah 3 (Douay-Rheims)

*Zacharias 3 in the Douay-Rheims*

[[DRB Zechariah 02|← Zechariah 2]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Zechariah 04|Zechariah 4 →]]

**3:1** And the Lord shewed me Jesus the high priest standing before the angel of the Lord: and Satan stood on his right hand to be his adversary. ^3-1

**3:2** And the Lord said to Satan: The Lord rebuke thee, O Satan: and the Lord that chose Jerusalem rebuke thee: Is not this a brand plucked out of the fire? ^3-2

**3:3** And Jesus was clothed with filthy garments: and he stood before the face of the angel. ^3-3

**3:4** Who answered, and said to them that stood before him, saying: Take away the filthy garments from him. And he said to him: Behold I have taken away thy iniquity, and have clothed thee with change of garments. ^3-4

**3:5** And he said: Put a clean mitre upon his head: and they put a clean mitre upon his head, and clothed him with garments, and the angel of the Lord stood. ^3-5

**3:6** And the angel of the Lord protested to Jesus, saying: ^3-6

**3:7** Thus saith the Lord of hosts: If thou wilt walk in my ways, and Beep my charge, thou also shalt judge my house, and shalt keep my courts, and I will give thee some of them that are now present here to walk with thee. ^3-7

**3:8** Hear, O Jesus thou high priest, then and thy friends that dwell before thee, for they are portending men: for behold I WILL BRING MY SERVANT THE ORIENT. ^3-8

**3:9** For behold the stone that I have laid before Jesus: upon one stone there are seven eyes: behold I will grave the graving thereof, saith the Lord of hosts: and I will take away the iniquity of that land in one day. ^3-9

**3:10** In that day, saith the Lord of hosts, every man shell call his friend under the vine and under the fig tree. ^3-10
