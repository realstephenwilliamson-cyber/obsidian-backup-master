The 16-Petaled Lotus: The Flow of Time

aim hrīm śrīm aim namaḥ

aim hrīm śrīm klīm namaḥ

aim hrīm śrīm sauḥ namaḥ

aim hrīm śrīm mahā tripurasundarī ātmānam rakśa rakśa

Here we invoke the Sri Chakra’s 16-petaled lotus, which represents the flow of time through the lunar calendar and is identified with protection. The word *rakśa* also denotes protection; it means you must be protected every day, always. So you say, *aim klīm sauḥ*—through the creative, protective and dissolution phases, you need to be protected.

In the appellation Mahā Tripurasundarī, *mahā* means “the great,” belonging to the cosmos. If you take *mahā* and reverse the letters, it becomes *aham*, which means “I.” So *aham* relates to the inside and *mahā* relates to the outside of our bodies, mind and intellect. The next term, *tripura*, is the “three cities”—the waking, dreaming and sleeping states. Then *sundarī* means the most beautiful in all these states, relating to to the cosmos. Finally, we have *ātma*, which is the notion of the ego confined to this body, and *rakśa rakśa*, evoking protection. Thus we have, “May Sundarī protect me, protect me throughout all the 16 days.”
