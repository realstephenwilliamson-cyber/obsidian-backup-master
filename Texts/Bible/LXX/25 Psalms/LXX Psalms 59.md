# LXX Psalms 59

← [[LXX Psalms 58]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 60]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 060]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 060]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**59:1** For the end, for them that shall yet be changed; for an inscription by David for instruction,  ^59-1

**59:2** when he *had* burned Mesopotamia of Syria, and Syria Sobal, and Joab *had* returned and smitten *in* the valley of salt twelve thousand.  ^59-2

**59:3** O God, thou hast rejected and destroyed us; thou hast been angry, yet hast pitied us.  ^59-3

**59:4** Thou hast shaken the earth, and troubled it; heal its breaches, for it has been shaken.  ^59-4

**59:5** Thou hast shewn thy people hard things: thou hast made us drink the wine of astonishment.  ^59-5

**59:6** Thou hast given a token to them that fear thee, that they might flee from the bow. Pause.  ^59-6

**59:7** That thy beloved ones may be delivered; save with thy right hand, and hear me.  ^59-7

**59:8** God has spoken in his holiness; I will rejoice, and divide Sicima, and measure out the valley of tents.  ^59-8

**59:9** Galaad is mine, and Manasse is mine; and Ephraim is the strength of my head;[^1]  ^59-9

**59:10** Judas is my king; Moab is the caldron of my hope; over Idumea will I stretch out my shoe; the Philistines have been subjected to me.  ^59-10

**59:11** Who will lead me into the fortified city? who will guide me as far as Idumea?  ^59-11

**59:12** Wilt not thou, O God, who hast cast us off? and wilt not thou, O God, go forth with our forces?  ^59-12

**59:13** Give us help from trouble: for vain is the deliverance of man.  ^59-13

**59:14** In God will we do valiantly; and he shall bring to nought them that harass us.[^2]  ^59-14

[^1]: 59:9 *Or,* strengthening.
[^2]: 59:14 *Gr.* work power.
