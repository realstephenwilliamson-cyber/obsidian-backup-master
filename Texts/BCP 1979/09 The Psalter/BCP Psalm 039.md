# Psalm 39

← [[BCP Psalm 038]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 040]] →

*Dixi, Custodiam* · Book One · Eighth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 039]]

**39:1** I said, "I will keep watch upon my ways, \*  
so that I do not offend with my tongue. ^39-1

**39:2** I will put a muzzle on my mouth \*  
while the wicked are in my presence." ^39-2

**39:3** So I held my tongue and said nothing; \*  
I refrained from rash words;  
but my pain became unbearable. ^39-3

**39:4** My heart was hot within me;  
while I pondered, the fire burst into flame; \*  
I spoke out with my tongue: ^39-4

[p. 639] ^pg639

**39:5** LORD, let me know my end and the number of my days, \*  
so that I may know how short my life is. ^39-5

**39:6** You have given me a mere handful of days,  
and my lifetime is as nothing in your sight; \*  
truly, even those who stand erect are but a puff of wind. ^39-6

**39:7** We walk about like a shadow,  
and in vain we are in turmoil; \*  
we heap up riches and cannot tell who will gather them. ^39-7

**39:8** And now, what is my hope? \*  
O Lord, my hope is in you. ^39-8

**39:9** Deliver me from all my transgressions \*  
and do not make me the taunt of the fool. ^39-9

**39:10** I fell silent and did not open my mouth, \*  
for surely it was you that did it. ^39-10

**39:11** Take your affliction from me; \*  
I am worn down by the blows of your hand. ^39-11

**39:12** With rebukes for sin you punish us;  
like a moth you eat away all that is dear to us; \*  
truly, everyone is but a puff of wind. ^39-12

**39:13** Hear my prayer, O LORD,  
and give ear to my cry; \*  
hold not your peace at my tears. ^39-13

**39:14** For I am but a sojourner with you, \*  
a wayfarer, as all my forebears were. ^39-14

**39:15** Turn your gaze from me, that I may be glad again, \*  
before I go my way and am no more. ^39-15
