## Mūla

तेन तत्पर्व तद्वच्च स्वसन्तानादिमेलनम् ।
संवित्सर्वात्मिका देहभेदाद्या सङ्कुचेत्तु सा ॥ ^mula

tena tatparva tadvacca svasantānādimelanam |
saṃvitsarvātmikā dehabhedādyā saṅkucettu sā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 07 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 5#^pg184|p. 167]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=184>))
>
> He certainly attains (liberating spiritual) knowledge by union with a Yogini. Thus that (day on which it took place) is a time of fulfilment. And like that also is (the day one attained) union with one’s own (spiritual) lineage and the like. (with 28.372cd–373ab)
>
> The consciousness, which is all things, that contracts due to the differences (bheda) between the bodies (in which it operates, returns to its) state of (ongoing) expansion (vikasvarā) during a gathering (of Siddhas and Yoginīs) (melaka), because of the (mutual) reflection (of their consciousness, that takes place) by (their) union with one another, (with 28.373cd–374ab)

*Lead-in:* ननु योगिनीमेलकादवश्यमेष ज्ञानमाप्नोतीति अवश्यतायां किं प्रमाणम्?--इत्याशङ्क्य आह--

> संवित्सर्वात्मिका देहभेदाद्या सङ्कुचेत्तु सा ॥
> मेलकेऽन्योन्यसङ्घट्टप्रतिबिम्बाद्विकस्वरा ।

इह सर्वात्मकत्वेऽपि या संविद्देहभेदात् सङ्कोचप्राप्ता, सा मेलके सति अन्योन्यस्य सङ्घट्टेन प्रतिबिम्बात्परस्परं प्रतिसंक्रमणेन विकस्वरा सङ्कोचापहस्तनेन पूर्णा भवति--इत्यर्थः ॥
