# Psalm 98

← [[BCP Psalm 097]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 099]] →

*Cantate Domino* · Book Four · Nineteenth Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 098]]

**98:1** Sing to the LORD a new song, \*  
for he has done marvelous things. ^98-1

[p. 728]

**98:2** With his right hand and his holy arm \*  
has he won for himself the victory. ^98-2

**98:3** The LORD has made known his victory; \*  
his righteousness has he openly shown in the sight of the nations. ^98-3

**98:4** He remembers his mercy and faithfulness to the house of Israel, \*  
and all the ends of the earth have seen the victory of our God. ^98-4

**98:5** Shout with joy to the LORD, all you lands; \*  
lift up your voice, rejoice, and sing. ^98-5

**98:6** Sing to the LORD with the harp, \*  
with the harp and the voice of song. ^98-6

**98:7** With trumpets and the sound of the horn \*  
shout with joy before the King, the LORD. ^98-7

**98:8** Let the sea make a noise and all that is in it, \*  
the lands and those who dwell therein. ^98-8

**98:9** Let the rivers clap their hands, \*  
and let the hills ring out with joy before the LORD,  
when he comes to judge the earth. ^98-9

**98:10** In righteousness shall he judge the world \*  
and the peoples with equity. ^98-10
