## Mūla

तत्क्रमेणैव संक्रान्तिह्रासवृद्धी दिवानिशोः ।
इत्थं समानमरुतो वर्षद्वयविकल्पनम् ॥ ^mula

tatkrameṇaiva saṃkrāntihrāsavṛddhī divāniśoḥ |
itthaṃ samānamaruto varṣadvayavikalpanam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 13 - Chapter Six, part 6#^pg435|p. 420]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=435>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 6.208cd–209ab.
>
> The increase or decrease of (the time it takes for) the transits (to take place) during the day and at night (of the yearly cycle projected into the breath) is in accord with the sequence of the increase or decrease of the days and nights beginning with the equinox. (with 6.207cd–208ab)
>
> In this way, one imagines two years in (one) cycle (cāra) of the Equal Breath (samāna) together in one place. Here (in this case), there is no deliberation on inhalation and exhalation. (with 6.208cd–209ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* तदेवोपसंहरति -

> इत्थं समानमरुतो वर्षद्वयविकल्पनम् ॥
> चार एकत्र नह्यत्र श्वासप्रश्वासचर्चनम् ।

वर्षद्वय इति, संक्रान्तिचतुर्विंशतेरुदयात् । एकत्रेति, न तु प्राग्वत्प्राणापानात्मनि चारद्वये ॥ २०८ ॥
