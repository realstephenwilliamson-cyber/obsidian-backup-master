## Mūla

सा स्थूला वैखरी यस्याः कार्यं वाक्यादि भूयसा ।
अस्मिन्स्थूलत्रये यत्तदनुसन्धानमादिवत् ॥ ^mula

sā sthūlā vaikharī yasyāḥ kāryaṃ vākyādi bhūyasā |
asminsthūlatraye yattadanusandhānamādivat || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. II
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 02 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v02 11 - Chapter Three, part 8#^pg386|p. 370]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 02.pdf#page=386>))
>
> Gross Corporeal Speech (Vaikharī) is the cause that generates clearly (articulated) phonemes, and its products are the many words and the like. (with 3.244cd–245ab)
>
> Each of these three, in their gross aspect, is preceded by a specific (well determined) intention (anusandhāna) (to produce a particular sound). These three (forms of intention) are called the subtle (form of the three levels of Speech). (with 3.245cd–246ab)

*Lead-in:* एवं स्थूलं भेदत्रयमभिधाय सूक्ष्ममप्याह --

> अस्मिन्स्थूलत्रये यत्तदनुसन्धानमादिवत् ॥
> पृथक्पृथक्तत्त्रितयं सूक्ष्ममित्यभिशब्द्यते ।

अस्मिन् समनन्तरोक्ते स्थूले भेदत्रये यदाद्यम् जिगासाद्यात्मेच्छारूपमनुसन्धानं तदेव पृथक् पृथक् पश्न्तीमध्यमावैखरीगतं सूक्ष्मं भेदत्रय मुच्यते ॥ २४५ ॥
