## Mūla

नक्षत्राणां तदन्येषामुदयो मध्यतः क्रमात् ।
नागा लोकेशमूर्तीशा गणेशा जलतत्त्वतः ॥ ^mula

nakṣatrāṇāṃ tadanyeṣāmudayo madhyataḥ kramāt |
nāgā lokeśamūrtīśā gaṇeśā jalatattvataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 09 - Chapter Six, part 2#^pg303|p. 288]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=303>))
>
> The constellations and those (celestial bodies) other than them arise progressively in the intermediate (hours). (with 6.69ab)
>
> In (the course of) the eight praharas, (the following) arise progressively in groups of eight, from gross to subtle, pervading one another. (They are) 1) the Nāgas, 2) the Lords of the Directions, 3) the Lords of the Bodies (mūrti), 4) Gaṇeśas, 5) the Lords of the Principles from Water to Matter (pradhāna), 6) the Lords of the Principle of Knowledge (vidyātattva), 7) (then the eight) Bhairavas from Sakala to Kaṇṭhyoṣṭhya, (followed by) 8) the Supreme Lord’s energies, the Vāmeśās, who are the leaders of the heroes (vīranāyikā). (with 6.69cd–72ab)
