# Psalm 7

← [[BCP Psalm 006]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 008]] →

*Domine, Deus meus* · Book One · First Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 007]]

**7:1** O LORD my God, I take refuge in you; \*  
save and deliver me from all who pursue me; ^7-1

**7:2** Lest like a lion they tear me in pieces \*  
and snatch me away with none to deliver me. ^7-2

**7:3** O LORD my God, if I have done these things: \*  
if there is any wickedness in my hands, ^7-3

[p. 591] ^pg591

**7:4** If I have repaid my friend with evil, \*  
or plundered him who without cause is my enemy; ^7-4

**7:5** Then let my enemy pursue and overtake me, \*  
trample my life into the ground,  
and lay my honor in the dust. ^7-5

**7:6** Stand up, O LORD, in your wrath; \*  
rise up against the fury of my enemies. ^7-6

**7:7** Awake, O my God, decree justice; \*  
let the assembly of the peoples gather round you. ^7-7

**7:8** Be seated on your lofty throne, O Most High; \*  
O LORD, judge the nations. ^7-8

**7:9** Give judgment for me according to my righteousness, O LORD, \*  
and according to my innocence, O Most High. ^7-9

**7:10** Let the malice of the wicked come to an end,  
but establish the righteous; \*  
for you test the mind and heart, O righteous God. ^7-10

**7:11** God is my shield and defense; \*  
he is the savior of the true in heart. ^7-11

**7:12** God is a righteous judge; \*  
God sits in judgment every day. ^7-12

**7:13** If they will not repent, God will whet his sword; \*  
he will bend his bow and make it ready. ^7-13

**7:14** He has prepared his weapons of death; \*  
he makes his arrows shafts of fire. ^7-14

**7:15** Look at those who are in labor with wickedness, \*  
who conceive evil, and give birth to a lie. ^7-15

[p. 592] ^pg592

**7:16** They dig a pit and make it deep \*  
and fall into the hole that they have made. ^7-16

**7:17** Their malice turns back upon their own head; \*  
their violence falls on their own scalp. ^7-17

**7:18** I will bear witness that the LORD is righteous; \*  
I will praise the Name of the LORD Most High. ^7-18
