## Chapter 130

**130:1** A song of ascents.  
Out of the depths I call You, O LORD. ^130-1

**130:2** O Lord, listen to my cry;  
let Your ears be attentive  
to my plea for mercy. ^130-2

**130:3** If You keep account of sins, O LORD,  
Lord, who will survive? ^130-3

**130:4** Yours is the power to forgive  
so that You may be held in awe. ^130-4

**130:5** I look to the LORD;  
I look to Him;  
I await His word. ^130-5

**130:6** I am more eager for the Lord  
than watchmen for the morning,  
watchmen for the morning. ^130-6

**130:7** O Israel, wait for the LORD;  
for with the LORD is steadfast love  
and great power to redeem. ^130-7

**130:8** It is He who will redeem Israel from all their iniquities. ^130-8
