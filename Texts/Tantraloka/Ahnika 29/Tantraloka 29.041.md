## Mūla

भट्टेन्द्रवल्कलाहीन्द्रगजेन्द्राः समहीधराः ।
ऊर्ध्वरेतस एते षडधिकारपदोज्झिताः ॥ ^mula

bhaṭṭendravalkalāhīndragajendrāḥ samahīdharāḥ |
ūrdhvaretasa ete ṣaḍadhikārapadojjhitāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 09 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 1#^pg255|p. 238]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=255>))
>
> Bhaṭta, Indra, Valkala, Ahīndra, Gajendra and Mahīdhara —these six are celibate (ārdhvaretas), and (so) do not have the authority (to initiate and impart Mantras).
