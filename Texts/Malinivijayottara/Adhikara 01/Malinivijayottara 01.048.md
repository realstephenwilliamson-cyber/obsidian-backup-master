## Mūla

आत्मा चतुर्विधो ह्येष पुनरेष चतुर्विधः ।
आचार्यत्वादिभेदेन शुद्धात्मा परिपठ्यते ॥ ^mula

ātmā caturvidho hy eṣa punar eṣa caturvidhaḥ |
ācāryatvādibhedena śuddhātmā paripaṭhyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2022 – June 2022
> p. 48 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=24>))

> This Self is of four kinds.[^89] Again, (each of one) is of four kinds, according to the division of 'teacher' (*ācārya*) (adept *sādhaka,* apprentice *– putraka* and regular initiate *– samayin*). This is said to be the pure Self.

[^89]: See above, MV 1/22cd.
