# Psalm 108

← [[BCP Psalm 107]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 109]] →

*Paratum cor meum* · Book Five · Twenty-second Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 108]]

**108:1** My heart is firmly fixed, O God, my heart is fixed; \*  
I will sing and make melody. ^108-1

**108:2** Wake up, my spirit;  
awake, lute and harp; \*  
I myself will waken the dawn. ^108-2

**108:3** I will confess you among the peoples, O LORD; \*  
I will sing praises to you among the nations. ^108-3

[p. 750] ^pg750

**108:4** For your loving-kindness is greater than the heavens, \*  
and your faithfulness reaches to the clouds. ^108-4

**108:5** Exalt yourself above the heavens, O God, \*  
and your glory over all the earth. ^108-5

**108:6** So that those who are dear to you may be delivered, \*  
save with your right hand and answer me. ^108-6

**108:7** God spoke from his holy place and said, \*  
"I will exult and parcel out Shechem;  
I will divide the valley of Succoth. ^108-7

**108:8** Gilead is mine and Manasseh is mine; \*  
Ephraim is my helmet and Judah my scepter. ^108-8

**108:9** Moab is my washbasin,  
on Edom I throw down my sandal to claim it, \*  
and over Philistia will I shout in triumph." ^108-9

**108:10** Who will lead me into the strong city? \*  
who will bring me into Edom? ^108-10

**108:11** Have you not cast us off, O God? \*  
you no longer go out, O God, with our armies. ^108-11

**108:12** Grant us your help against the enemy, \*  
for vain is the help of man. ^108-12

**108:13** With God we will do valiant deeds, \*  
and he shall tread our enemies under foot. ^108-13
