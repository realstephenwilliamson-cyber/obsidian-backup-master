# 2 Corinthians 2 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue 2 Corinthians 01|← 2 Corinthians 1]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue 2 Corinthians 03|2 Corinthians 3 →]]

Compare: [[2 Corinthians 02|BSB and SBLGNT]] · [[DRB 2 Corinthians 02|Douay-Rheims]] · [[JANT 2 Corinthians 02|JANT notes]]

**2:1** So I made up my mind not to make another visit that causes you grief. ^2-1

**2:2** For if I cause you grief, who is there to make me glad but the one whom I have grieved? ^2-2

**2:3** And I wrote as I did, so that when I came, I might not suffer grief from those who should have made me rejoice, for I am confident about all of you, that my joy would be the joy of all of you. ^2-3

**2:4** For I wrote you out of much distress and anguish of heart and with many tears, not to cause you grief but to let you know the abundant love that I have for you. ^2-4

## Forgiveness for the offender

**2:5** But if anyone has caused grief, he has caused it not to me but to some extent—not to exaggerate it—to all of you. ^2-5

**2:6** This punishment by the majority is enough for such a person; ^2-6

**2:7** so now instead you should forgive and console him, so that he may not be overwhelmed by even worse grief. ^2-7

**2:8** So I urge you to reaffirm your love for him. ^2-8

**2:9** I wrote for this reason: to test you and to know whether you are obedient in everything. ^2-9

**2:10** Anyone whom you forgive, I also forgive. In fact, what I have forgiven, if I have forgiven anything, has been for your sake in the presence of Christ. ^2-10

**2:11** And we do this so that we may not be outwitted by Satan, for we are not ignorant of his designs. ^2-11

## Paul’s anxiety in Troas

**2:12** When I came to Troas to proclaim the good news^[Or *the gospel*] of Christ, a door was opened for me in the Lord, ^2-12

**2:13** but my mind could not rest because I did not find my brother Titus there. So I said farewell to them and went on to Macedonia. ^2-13

**2:14** But thanks be to God, who in Christ always leads us in triumphal procession and through us spreads in every place the fragrance that comes from knowing him.^[Gk *the fragrance of the knowledge of him*] ^2-14

**2:15** For we are the aroma of Christ to God among those who are being saved and among those who are perishing: ^2-15

**2:16** to the one group a fragrance from death^[Other ancient authorities read *fragrance of death that leads*] to death, to the other a fragrance from life^[Other ancient authorities read *fragrance of life that leads*] to life. Who is qualified for these things? ^2-16

**2:17** For we are not peddlers of God’s word like so many,^[Other ancient authorities read *like the rest*] but as persons of sincerity, as persons sent from God, we are speaking in Christ before God. ^2-17
