## Mūla

अधोवहा शिखाणुत्वं तेनेत्थं कल्पना कृता ।
नरशक्तिशिवाख्यस्य त्रयस्य बहुभेदताम् ॥ ^mula

adhovahā śikhāṇutvaṃ tenetthaṃ kalpanā kṛtā |
naraśaktiśivākhyasya trayasya bahubhedatām || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 08 - Chapter Seventeen#^pg161|p. 141]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=161>))
>
> The arms are the locus of action. Viṣṇu, whose nature is Māyā, is located in the throat. The topknot that flows downwards (represents the Impurity that engenders the state of limitation) of the individual soul. It is for this reason that (the knots are) imagined in this way. (with 17.4cd–5ab)
>
> The thread for the knot should be fashioned with three triple (tristrigunam) strands, in order to signify that the triad called Man, Śakti, and Śiva is of many kinds. (with 17.5cd–6ab)
