## Mūla

श्रीमदानन्दशास्त्रे च नाशुद्धिः स्याद्विपश्चितः ।
किन्तु स्नानं सुवस्त्रत्वं तुष्टिसञ्जननं भवेत् ॥ ^mula

śrīmadānandaśāstre ca nāśuddhiḥ syādvipaścitaḥ |
kintu snānaṃ suvastratvaṃ tuṣṭisañjananaṃ bhavet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 02 - Chapter Fifteen, part 1#^pg55|p. 37]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=55>))
>
> According to the venerable Ānandatantra, a wise man is not subject to impurity. However, (ritual) ablution and fine clothes, (although they do not remove it,) do give rise to (an inner) sense of satisfaction (tuṣṭi).
