## Mūla

एतेषामेव तत्त्वानाम्भुवनानाम्च शाङ्करि ।
य एकमपि जानाति सोऽपि योगफलम्लभेत् ॥ ^mula

eteṣām eva tattvānām bhuvanānām ca śāṅkari |
ya ekam api jānāti so 'pi yogaphalam labhet || ^iast

*Muktabodha's e-text of the KSTS edition (M00160) has:* eteṣāmeva tattvānāṃ bhuvanānāṃ ca śāṅkari | ya ekamagni jānāti so'pi yogaphalaṃ labhet ||

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, July 2022 – September 2022
> p. 30 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Malini (journal), July-September 2022.pdf#page=32>))
>
> Yogis worship it (by the practice of) yoga, and should know it in order to secure success in yoga. O Śāṅkarī, whoever knows even one of these principles or worlds will attain the fruits of yoga. (with 2.8cd–9)
