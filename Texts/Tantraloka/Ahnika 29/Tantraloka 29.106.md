## Mūla

तत्प्रधानं भवेच्चक्रमनुचक्रमतोऽपरम् ।
विकासात्तृप्तितः पाशोत्कर्तनात्कृतिशक्तितः ॥ ^mula

tatpradhānaṃ bhaveccakramanucakramato'param |
vikāsāttṛptitaḥ pāśotkartanātkṛtiśaktitaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 11 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 3#^pg318|p. 301]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=318>))
>
> Only that which streams with bliss (ānandasyandin) is the inner part of consciousness – that is the main (pradhāna) Wheel; (any) other than that is a subordinate Wheel (anucakra). (with 29.105cd–106ab)
>
> The word ‘cakra’ is said to be derive from the roots ‘ksī’ (expansion), ‘caka’ (satisfaction), ‘kṛtī’ (severing), and ‘kṛ’ (acting). Thus ‘cakra’ is that which expands, satisfies, severs (bondage), and acts. (with 29.106cd–107ab)

*Lead-in:* चक्रशब्दस्य च प्रवृत्तौ किं निमित्तम् ?--इत्याशङ्क्य आह--

> विकासात्तृप्तितः पाशोत्कर्तनात्कृतिशक्तितः ॥

> चक्रं कसेश्चकेः कृत्या करोतेश्च किलोदितम् ॥

कसी विकासे, चक तृप्तौ, कृती च्छेदने, डुकृञ् करणे,--इति धातुचतुष्टयार्थान्वयादत्र चक्रशब्दः । तेन विकसति, चकति, कृन्तति, करोतीति चक्रम् ॥
