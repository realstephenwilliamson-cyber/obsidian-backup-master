## Chapter 120

**120:1** A song of ascents.[^1]  
In my distress I called to the LORD  
and He answered me. ^120-1

**120:2** O LORD, save me from treacherous lips,  
from a deceitful tongue! ^120-2

**120:3** What can you profit,  
what can you gain,  
O deceitful tongue? ^120-3

**120:4** A warrior’s sharp arrows,  
with hot coals of broom-wood. ^120-4

**120:5** Woe is me, that I live with Meshech,  
that I dwell among the clans of Kedar. ^120-5

**120:6** Too long have I dwelt with those who hate peace. ^120-6

**120:7** I am all peace;  
but when I speak,  
they are for war. ^120-7

[^1]: A term of uncertain meaning.
