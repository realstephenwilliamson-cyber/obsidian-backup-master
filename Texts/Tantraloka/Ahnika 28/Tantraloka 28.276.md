## Mūla

यस्तु तावदयोग्योऽपि तथास्ते स शिवालये ।
पश्चादास्थानिबन्धेन तावदेव फलं भजेत् ॥ ^mula

yastu tāvadayogyo'pi tathāste sa śivālaye |
paścādāsthānibandhena tāvadeva phalaṃ bhajet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 05 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 3#^pg137|p. 120]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=137>))
>
> He who, even though he was not fit to this degree (fāvat) (to receive initiation when alive) takes refuge in that way (when about to die), in a place sacred to Śiva (Śivālaya), subsequently (receives) by virtue of his steadfast (faith) (2502) the fruit to the same degree (tāvat)) (as he would have done had he received initiation).
