## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. III
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 03 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v03 04 - Chapter Four, part 3#^pg97|p. 84]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 03.pdf#page=97>))
>
> If one were to ask what is the benefit of keeping, for example, the Mantras and the sacrificial substances secret, (we reply that) those who have learnt Mantras from books (but) have not been initiated, do not observe the prescribed code of conduct (samaya), and under the sway of the darkness (tāmasa) (of ignorance), are violent towards others and practice (magical rites) to control (them) and the like, and do not know the true nature (tattva) (of these Mantras), they thus clearly possess (many) defects. (with 4.67–68)
