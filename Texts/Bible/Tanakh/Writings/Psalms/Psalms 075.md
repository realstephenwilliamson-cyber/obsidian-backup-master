## Chapter 75

**75:1** For the leader; *al tashḥeth*.  
A psalm of Asaph, a song. ^75-1

**75:2** We praise You, O God;  
we praise You;  
Your presence is near;  
men tell of Your wondrous deeds. ^75-2

**75:3** “At the time I choose,  
I will give judgment equitably. ^75-3

**75:4** Earth and all its inhabitants dissolve;  
it is I who keep its pillars firm. *Selah*. ^75-4

**75:5** To wanton men I say, ‘Do not be wanton!’  
to the wicked, ‘Do not lift up your horns!’” ^75-5

**75:6** Do not lift your horns up high  
in vainglorious bluster.[^1] ^75-6

**75:7** For what lifts a man comes not from the east  
or the west or the wilderness;[^2] ^75-7

**75:8** for God it is who gives judgment;  
He brings down one man, He lifts up another. ^75-8

**75:9** There is a cup in the LORD’s hand  
with foaming wine fully mixed;  
from this He pours;  
all the wicked of the earth drink,  
draining it to the very dregs. ^75-9

**75:10** As for me, I will declare forever,  
I will sing a hymn to the God of Jacob. ^75-10

**75:11** “All the horns of the wicked I will cut;  
but the horns of the righteous shall be lifted up.” ^75-11

[^1]: Lit. “with arrogant neck you speak.”
[^2]: Reading midba¯r with many mss.
