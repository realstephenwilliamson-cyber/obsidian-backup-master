## Mūla

जडाभासेषु तत्त्वेषु संवित्स्थित्यै ततो गुरुः ।
आवाहनविभक्तिं प्राक् कृत्वा तुर्यविभक्तितः ॥ ^mula

jaḍābhāseṣu tattveṣu saṃvitsthityai tato guruḥ |
āvāhanavibhaktiṃ prāk kṛtvā turyavibhaktitaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 08 - Chapter Seventeen#^pg168|p. 148]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=168>))
>
> Then, in order to establish consciousness within the insentient principles (like Earth) (jadābhāsa), the teacher (should form and utter their Mantras). (In order to do this, he) should first apply the case ending of an invocation, (that is, the vocative, to the name of the principle,) and then the case ending of the dative, and then, by adding the salutation (namaskāra) at the end, he should imagine the full (unconditioned) being (sattā) (of consciousness within it). (with 17.16–17ab)
