# Psalm 5

← [[BCP Psalm 004]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 006]] →

*Verba mea auribus* · Book One · First Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 005]]

**5:1** Give ear to my words, O LORD; \*  
consider my meditation. ^5-1

**5:2** Hearken to my cry for help, my King and my God, \*  
for I make my prayer to you. ^5-2

**5:3** In the morning, LORD, you hear my voice; \*  
early in the morning I make my appeal and watch for you. ^5-3

**5:4** For you are not a God who takes pleasure in wickedness, \*  
and evil cannot dwell with you. ^5-4

**5:5** Braggarts cannot stand in your sight; \*  
you hate all those who work wickedness. ^5-5

[p. 589] ^pg589

**5:6** You destroy those who speak lies; \*  
the bloodthirsty and deceitful, O LORD, you abhor. ^5-6

**5:7** But as for me, through the greatness of your mercy I will go into your house; \*  
I will bow down toward your holy temple in awe of you. ^5-7

**5:8** Lead me, O LORD, in your righteousness,  
because of those who lie in wait for me; \*  
make your way straight before me. ^5-8

**5:9** For there is no truth in their mouth; \*  
there is destruction in their heart; ^5-9

**5:10** Their throat is an open grave; \*  
they flatter with their tongue. ^5-10

**5:11** Declare them guilty, O God; \*  
let them fall, because of their schemes. ^5-11

**5:12** Because of their many transgressions cast them out, \*  
for they have rebelled against you. ^5-12

**5:13** But all who take refuge in you will be glad; \*  
they will sing out their joy for ever. ^5-13

**5:14** You will shelter them, \*  
so that those who love your Name may exult in you. ^5-14

**5:15** For you, O LORD, will bless the righteous; \*  
you will defend them with your favor as with a shield. ^5-15
