## Mūla

तथा तेष्वपि तत्त्वेषु स्ववर्गेऽनुगमात्मकम् ।
व्यावृत्तं परवर्गाच्च कलेति शिवशासने ॥ ^mula

tathā teṣvapi tattveṣu svavarge'nugamātmakam |
vyāvṛttaṃ paravargācca kaleti śivaśāsane || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 02 - Chapter Eleven, part 1#^pg21|p. 1]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=21>))
>
> It was said before that a (metaphysical) principle (tattva) is said to be (the common universal) nature (rūpa) that extends through its own particular group of worlds, and is distinct from the other (principles). Analogously, according to Śaiva doctrine (śaivaśāsana), a Force (kalā) is said to be the (common) universal nature that extends through its own (given) group of these principles, as distinct from other groups. (with 11.2–3)

## Viveka

> यथा पूर्वोक्तभुवनमध्ये निजनिजं गणम् ।
> अनुयत्परतो भिन्नं तत्त्वं नामेति भण्यते ॥
> तथा तेष्वपि तत्त्वेषु स्ववर्गेऽनुगमात्मकम् ।
> व्यावृत्तं परवर्गाच्च कलेति शिवशासने ॥

तत्त्वेष्विति मध्ये, स्ववर्ग इति वक्ष्यमाणनीत्यैकतत्त्वादिरूपे, व्यावृत्तमिति अश्वादिभ्य ए(इ)व गोत्वम् ॥ ३ ॥
