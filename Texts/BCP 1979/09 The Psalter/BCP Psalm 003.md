# Psalm 3

← [[BCP Psalm 002]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 004]] →

*Domine, quid multiplicati* · Book One · First Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 003]]

**3:1** LORD, how many adversaries I have! \*  
how many there are who rise up against me! ^3-1

**3:2** How many there are who say of me, \*  
"There is no help for him in his God." ^3-2

**3:3** But you, O LORD, are a shield about me; \*  
you are my glory, the one who lifts up my head. ^3-3

**3:4** I call aloud upon the LORD, \*  
and he answers me from his holy hill; ^3-4

**3:5** I lie down and go to sleep; \*  
I wake again, because the LORD sustains me. ^3-5

**3:6** I do not fear the multitudes of people \*  
who set themselves against me all around. ^3-6

**3:7** Rise up, O LORD; set me free, O my God; \*  
surely, you will strike all my enemies across the face,  
you will break the teeth of the wicked. ^3-7

**3:8** Deliverance belongs to the LORD. \*  
Your blessing be upon your people! ^3-8
