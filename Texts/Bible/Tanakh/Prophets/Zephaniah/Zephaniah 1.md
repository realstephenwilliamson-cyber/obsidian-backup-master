## Chapter 1

**1:1** The word of the LORD that came to Zephaniah son of Cushi son of Gedaliah son of Amariah son of Hezekiah, during the reign of King Josiah son of Amon of Judah. ^1-1

**1:2** I will sweep everything away  
From the face of the earth  
—declares the LORD. ^1-2

**1:3** I will sweep away man and beast;  
I will sweep away the birds of the sky  
And the fish of the sea.  
I will make the wicked stumble,[^1]  
And I will destroy mankind  
From the face of the earth  
—declares the LORD. ^1-3

**1:4** I will stretch out My arm against Judah  
And against all who dwell in Jerusalem;  
And I will wipe out from this place  
Every vestige of Baal,  
And the name of the priestlings[^2] along with the priests; ^1-4

**1:5** And those who bow down on the roofs  
To the host of heaven;  
And those who bow down and swear to the LORD  
But also swear by Malcam;[^3] ^1-5

**1:6** And those who have forsaken the LORD,  
And those who have not sought the LORD  
And have not turned to Him. ^1-6

**1:7** Be silent before my Lord GOD,  
For the day of the LORD is approaching;  
For the LORD has prepared a sacrificial feast,[^4]  
Has bidden His guests purify themselves. ^1-7

**1:8** And on the day of the LORD’s sacrifice  
I will punish the officials  
And the king’s sons,[^5]  
[^6]And all who don a foreign vestment. ^1-8

**1:9** I will also punish on that day  
Everyone who steps over the threshold,  
Who fill their master’s[^7] palace  
With lawlessness and fraud. ^1-9

**1:10** In that day there shall be  
—declares the LORD—  
A loud outcry from the Fish Gate,  
And howling from the Mishneh,[^8]  
And a sound of great anguish from the hills. ^1-10

**1:11** The dwellers of the Machtesh[^9] howl;  
For all the tradesmen have perished,  
All who weigh silver are wiped out. ^1-11

**1:12** At that time,  
I will search Jerusalem with lamps;  
And I will punish the men  
Who rest untroubled on their lees,  
Who say to themselves,  
“The LORD will do nothing, good or bad.” ^1-12

**1:13** Their wealth shall be plundered  
And their homes laid waste.  
They shall build houses and not dwell in them,  
Plant vineyards and not drink their wine. ^1-13

**1:14** The great day of the LORD is approaching,  
Approaching most swiftly.  
Hark, the day of the LORD!  
It is bitter:  
There a warrior shrieks![^10] ^1-14

**1:15** That day shall be a day of wrath,  
A day of trouble and distress,  
A day of calamity and desolation,  
A day of darkness and deep gloom,  
A day of densest clouds, ^1-15

**1:16** A day of horn blasts and alarms—  
Against the fortified towns  
And the lofty corner towers. ^1-16

**1:17** I will bring distress on the people,  
And they shall walk like blind men,  
Because they sinned against the LORD;  
Their blood shall be spilled like dust,  
And their fat[^11] like dung. ^1-17

**1:18** Moreover, their silver and gold  
Shall not avail to save them.  
On the day of the LORD’s wrath,  
In the fire of His passion,  
The whole land shall be consumed;  
For He will make a terrible end  
Of all who dwell in the land. ^1-18

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Heb. kemarim, a term used only of priests of heathen gods.
[^3]: Apparently identical with “Milcom the abomination of the Ammonites”; cf. 1 Kings 11.5.
[^4]: I.e., a slaughter of sinners.
[^5]: Apparently brothers of King Amon, who exercised influence during the minority of King Josiah (2 Kings 22.1).
[^6]: Apparently references to two customs of heathen worship; cf. 2 Kings 10.22 and 1 Sam. 5.5.
[^7]: I.e., King Josiah’s.
[^8]: A quarter of Jerusalem; cf. 2 Kings 22.14.
[^9]: Another quarter of Jerusalem.
[^10]: Emendation yields: “The day of the Lord is faster than a runner, / Fleeter than a warrior”; cf. Ps. 19.6.
[^11]: Or “marrow”; meaning of Heb. uncertain.
