# Psalm 36 (NRSVue)

## Human wickedness and divine goodness

*To the leader. Of David, the servant of the LORD.*

**36:1** Transgression speaks to the wicked deep in their hearts; there is no fear of God before their eyes. ^36-1

**36:2** For they flatter themselves in their own eyes that their iniquity cannot be found out and hated. ^36-2

**36:3** The words of their mouths are mischief and deceit; they have ceased to act wisely and do good. ^36-3

**36:4** They plot mischief while on their beds; they are set on a way that is not good; they do not reject evil. ^36-4

**36:5** Your steadfast love, O LORD, extends to the heavens, your faithfulness to the clouds. ^36-5

**36:6** Your righteousness is like the mighty mountains; your judgments are like the great deep; you save humans and animals alike, O LORD. ^36-6

**36:7** How precious is your steadfast love, O God! All people may take refuge in the shadow of your wings. ^36-7

**36:8** They feast on the abundance of your house, and you give them drink from the river of your delights. ^36-8

**36:9** For with you is the fountain of life; in your light we see light. ^36-9

**36:10** O continue your steadfast love to those who know you and your salvation to the upright of heart! ^36-10

**36:11** Do not let the foot of the arrogant tread on me or the hand of the wicked drive me away. ^36-11

**36:12** There the evildoers lie prostrate; they are thrust down, unable to rise. ^36-12
