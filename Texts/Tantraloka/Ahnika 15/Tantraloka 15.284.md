## Mūla

यागेन देहं निष्पाद्य भावयेत शिवात्मकम् ।
गलिते विषयौन्मुख्ये पारिमित्ये विलापिते ॥ ^mula

yāgena dehaṃ niṣpādya bhāvayeta śivātmakam |
galite viṣayaunmukhye pārimitye vilāpite || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 06 - Chapter Fifteen, part 5#^pg187|p. 169]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=187>))
>
> After having in this way generated a body for the sacrifice (yāgena) which is completely full of bliss and devoid of all propensity (to move out of itself), (the adept) should meditate on it is as Śiva (śivātmakam). (with 15.283cd–284ab)
>
> When the limited (conditioned) state has ceased, and (all) propensity towards (outer sensory) objects fall away, what remains in the body, apart from the juice (of the aesthetic savour) (rasa) of Śiva’s bliss? (with 15.284cd–285ab)
