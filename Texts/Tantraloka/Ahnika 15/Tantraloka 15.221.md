## Mūla

अधितिष्ठत्यर्कमिव स विचित्रवपुर्यतः ।
स्वोत्था अपि दिशः स्वेशाः शक्राद्या ह्यधिशेरते ॥ ^mula

adhitiṣṭhatyarkamiva sa vicitravapuryataḥ |
svotthā api diśaḥ sveśāḥ śakrādyā hyadhiśerate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 05 - Chapter Fifteen, part 4#^pg152|p. 134]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=152>))
>
> This (other) division of the directions, formed in relation to oneself, is always (sadā) presided over by Śiva, for (Śiva’s) body is wonderfully varied, just as is the sun (and the directions in relation to it). (with 15.220cd–221ab)
>
> The directions that arise (in relation to) oneself are also presided over by their own lords, namely Indra and the rest. They are limbs (aṁśa) of the power of the Light, (and so) serve (and are in consonance with) the Light (of consciousness). (with 15.221cd–222ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* न केवलमेतत्परमेश्वर एव अधितिष्ठति, यावत् तच्छक्त्यंशरूपः शक्रादिर्लोकपालवर्गोऽपि--इत्याह--

> स्वोत्था अपि दिशः स्वेशाः शक्राद्या ह्यधिशेरते ॥
> ते हि प्रकाशशक्त्यंशाः प्रकाशानुविधायिनः ।

न केवलं स्वोत्था एव दिश एवं यावत् सौर्योऽपीति अपिना कटाक्षितम् ॥
