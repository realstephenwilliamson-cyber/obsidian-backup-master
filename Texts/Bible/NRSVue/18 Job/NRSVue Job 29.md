# Job 29 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Job 28|← Job 28]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Job 30|Job 30 →]]

## Job finishes his defense

**29:1** Job again took up his discourse and said: ^29-1

**29:2** “O that I were as in the months of old, as in the days when God watched over me, ^29-2

**29:3** when his lamp shone over my head, and by his light I walked through darkness, ^29-3

**29:4** when I was in my prime, when the friendship of God was upon my tent, ^29-4

**29:5** when the Almighty^[Traditional rendering of Heb *Shaddai*] was still with me, when my children were around me, ^29-5

**29:6** when my steps were washed with milk and the rock poured out for me streams of oil! ^29-6

**29:7** When I went out to the gate of the city, when I took my seat in the square, ^29-7

**29:8** the young men saw me and withdrew, and the aged rose up and stood; ^29-8

**29:9** the nobles refrained from talking and laid their hands on their mouths; ^29-9

**29:10** the voices of princes were hushed, and their tongues stuck to the roofs of their mouths. ^29-10

**29:11** When the ear heard, it commended me, and when the eye saw, it approved, ^29-11

**29:12** because I delivered the poor who cried and the orphan who had no helper. ^29-12

**29:13** The blessing of the wretched came upon me, and I caused the widow’s heart to sing for joy. ^29-13

**29:14** I put on righteousness, and it clothed me; my justice was like a robe and a turban. ^29-14

**29:15** I was eyes to the blind and feet to the lame. ^29-15

**29:16** I was a father to the needy, and I championed the cause of the stranger. ^29-16

**29:17** I broke the fangs of the unrighteous and made them drop their prey from their teeth. ^29-17

**29:18** Then I thought, ‘I shall die in my nest, and I shall multiply my days like the phoenix;^[Or *like sand*] ^29-18

**29:19** my roots spread out to the waters, with the dew all night on my branches; ^29-19

**29:20** my glory was fresh with me and my bow ever new in my hand.’ ^29-20

**29:21** “They listened to me and waited and kept silence for my counsel. ^29-21

**29:22** After I spoke they did not speak again, and my word dropped upon them like dew.^[Heb lacks *like dew*] ^29-22

**29:23** They waited for me as for the rain; they opened their mouths as for the spring rain. ^29-23

**29:24** I smiled on them when they had no confidence, and the light of my countenance they did not extinguish.^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^29-24

**29:25** I chose what they should do and sat as chief, and I lived like a king among his troops, like one who comforts mourners. ^29-25
