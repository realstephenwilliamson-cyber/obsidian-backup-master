## Mūla

मन्वाते नेह वै किञ्चित्तदपेक्षा त्वसौ कथम् ।
श्रूयतां संविदैकात्म्यतत्त्वेऽस्मिन्संव्यवस्थिते ॥ ^mula

manvāte neha vai kiñcittadapekṣā tvasau katham |
śrūyatāṃ saṃvidaikātmyatattve'sminsaṃvyavasthite || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 12 - Chapter Ten, part 3#^pg397|p. 378]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=397>))
>
> Surely (an opponent may object that), even if perceptibility is conceded to be a property of an entity, the Dissolution Deconditioned and Consciousness Deconditioned (perceivers) are not conscious of anything, so how can that (perceptibility exist) in relation to them? (with 10.132cd–133ab)
>
> Listen carefully! In this (our view), that maintains that reality is the oneness of consciousness, consciousness (cifti) is present in that which is established within this (oneness), even if insentient; so what to say of beings who, (asleep), will awake! When the time comes for his own awakening, a Dissolution Deconditioned (perceiver) will be divided' into two (types), as is (his) awakening, according to whether (it serves to make him) into a Mantra (perceiver) or (gives rise to) the life of transmigratory existence (bhava) (for him as a Conditioned perceiver). (with 10.133cd–135ab)
