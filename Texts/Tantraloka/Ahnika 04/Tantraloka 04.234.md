## Mūla

अनवच्छिन्नविज्ञानवैश्वरूप्यसुनिर्भरः ।
शास्त्रात्मना स्थितो देवो मिथ्यात्वं क्वापि नार्हति ॥ ^mula

anavacchinnavijñānavaiśvarūpyasunirbharaḥ |
śāstrātmanā sthito devo mithyātvaṃ kvāpi nārhati || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. III
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 03 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v03 10 - Chapter Four, part 9#^pg345|p. 332]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 03.pdf#page=345>))
>
> (But) in no case is it possible that (the Śaiva scriptures) are false, because it is the Lord (Himself) who, perfectly full with the omniformity of His unconditioned consciousness, abides as the essential nature of the scriptures (śāstrātmanā).
