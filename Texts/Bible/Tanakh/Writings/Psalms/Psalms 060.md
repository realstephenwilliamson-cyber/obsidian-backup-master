## Chapter 60

**60:1** For the leader; on *shushan eduth*.[^1] A *michtam* of David (to be taught), ^60-1

**60:2** when he fought with Aram-Naharaim and Aram-Zobah, and Joab returned and defeated Edom—[an army] of twelve thousand men—in the Valley of Salt.[^2] ^60-2

**60:3** O God, You have rejected us,  
You have made a breach in us;  
You have been angry;  
restore us! ^60-3

**60:4** You have made the land quake;  
You have torn it open.  
Mend its fissures,  
for it is collapsing. ^60-4

**60:5** You have made Your people suffer hardship;  
You have given us wine that makes us reel.[^3] ^60-5

**60:6** Give those who fear You because of Your truth  
a banner for rallying.[^4] *Selah*. ^60-6

**60:7** [^5]That those whom You love might be rescued,  
deliver with Your right hand and answer me. ^60-7

**60:8** God promised in His sanctuary[^6]  
that I would exultingly divide up Shechem,  
and measure the Valley of Sukkoth; ^60-8

**60:9** Gilead and Manasseh would be mine,  
Ephraim my chief stronghold,  
Judah my scepter; ^60-9

**60:10** Moab would be my washbasin;  
on Edom I would cast my shoe;  
acclaim me, O Philistia! ^60-10

**60:11** Would that I were brought to the bastion!  
Would that I were led to Edom! ^60-11

**60:12** But You have rejected us, O God;  
God, You do not march with our armies. ^60-12

**60:13** Grant us Your aid against the foe,  
for the help of man is worthless. ^60-13

**60:14** With God we shall triumph;  
He will trample our foes. ^60-14

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Cf. 2 Sam. 8; 1 Chron. 18.
[^3]: Or “You have sated Your people with a bitter draft.”
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^5]: Cf. Ps. 108.7–14.
[^6]: Or “by His holiness.”
