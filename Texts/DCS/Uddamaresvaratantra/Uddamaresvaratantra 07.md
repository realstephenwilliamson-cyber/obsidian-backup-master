# Uḍḍāmareśvaratantra — Chapter 7

[[Uddamaresvaratantra 06|← Chapter 6]] · [[Uddamaresvaratantra|← index]] · [[Uddamaresvaratantra 08|Chapter 8 →]]

athauṣadhīkaraṇaṃ nirūpyate

^s1

oṣadhī paramā śreṣṭhā gopitavyā prayatnataḥ

^s2

yasyāḥ prayogamātreṇa devatā yānti vaśyatām

^s3

oṣadhī sā budhaiḥ proktā cāṇḍālī lokaviśrutā

^s4

surāsuragaṇaiḥ pūjyā sarvakāryārthasādhinī

^s5

ekapattrī dvipattrī ca tripattrī turyapattrikā

^s6

anena vidhinā devi catuścaraṇagāminī

^s7

mānuṣāṇāṃ viśeṣeṇa vaśyakarmaṇi yojitā

^s8

ekapattrī tu svāṅgamalasaṃyuktā striyaṃ vaśam ānayati dvipattrī rājño rājapuruṣān

^s9

tripattrī śrīśākamaricasahitā duṣṭāṃ camūṃ vaśam ānayati catuṣpattrī ca kandusahitā mattaduṣṭagajaṃ vaśam ānayati

^s10

athotpāṭanavidhiḥ kathyate śanivāre śucir bhūtvā sāyaṃ saṃdhyādikaṃ vidhāya gandhapuṣpadhūpadīpanaivedyādibhiḥ pañcopacāraiḥ pūjādikaṃ vidhāya akṣataṃ phalaṃ haste gṛhītvā oṣadhisamīpe sthitvābhimantraṇaṃ kuryāt

^s11

tato 'nudite bhānau khadirakāṣṭhakalikena khanayet

^s12

tatra mantraḥ yena tvāṃ khanate brahmā hṛṣīkeśo maheśvaraḥ

^s13

śacīpatiḥ pitṛpatir jaleśaś ca dhanādhipaḥ

^s14

tena tvāṃ khanayiṣyāmi tiṣṭha tiṣṭha mahauṣadhi

^s15

iti paṭhitvā khanayet

^s16

śucir ārabhya ekānte prabhāte mantramuktitaḥ

^s17

saṃgrāhyam auṣadhaṃ siddhyai na bhavanti hi kāṣṭhavat

^s18

namo 'stv amṛtasambhūte balavīryavivardhini

^s19

balam āyuś ca me dehi pāpaṃ me naya dūrataḥ

^s20

yena cānena mantreṇa khanitvotpāṭyamānaṃ kṛtvā yaḥ pūrvam ānīto yo 'nyathā bhavet

^s21

atraiva tiṣṭha kalyāṇi mama kāryakarī bhava

^s22

mama kārye kṛte siddhe itas tvaṃ hi gamiṣyasi

^s23

uoṃ hrīṃ raktacāmuṇḍe hūṃ phaṭ svāhā

^s24

anena mantreṇa puṣyarkṣe hastarkṣe vā nakṣatre sarvāś cauṣadhya utpāṭanīyā yair naraiś ca udite bhānau oṣadhyaḥ khanyante utpāṭyante utpadyante vā tāsāṃ ravikiraṇapītaprabhāvenāvīryaprabhāvo bhavati

^s25

siddhikārikā na bhavanti

^s26

yady udite bhāskare utpāṭyante tadā tāsāṃ pūjā kartavyā

^s27

japāraktotpalaraktakaravīraraktacandanakuṅkumena gavyagomayena sapādahastabhūmiṃ saṃlipya tanmadhye caturasraṃ kārayet

^s28

raktacandanakuṅkumābhyāṃ tanmadhye vartulaṃ vitastimātraṃ bhānuṃ pūjayet

^s29

pārśve candrādigrahān pūjayet

^s30

tato raktabhaktapuṣparaktair vakṣyamāṇamantreṇa baliṃ dadyāt

^s31

uoṃ drāṃ drīṃ drūṃ draiṃ drauṃ draḥ saḥ svāhā

^s32

anena mantreṇa pūjāṃ kṛtvotpāṭayed vīryayuktā bhavati sarvakāryakṣamā bhavati

^s33
