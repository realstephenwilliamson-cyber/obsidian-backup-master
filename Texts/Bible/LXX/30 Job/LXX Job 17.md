# LXX Job 17

← [[LXX Job 16]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Job 18]] →

*Job · [[LXX]]*

**17:1** I perish, carried away by the wind, and I seek for burial, and obtain *it* not.  ^17-1

**17:2** Weary I intreat; and what have I done? and strangers have stolen my goods.  ^17-2

**17:3** Who is this? let him join hands with me.  ^17-3

**17:4** For thou hast hid their heart from wisdom; therefore thou shalt not exalt them.  ^17-4

**17:5** He shall promise mischief to *his* companions: but *their* eyes have failed for *their* children.  ^17-5

**17:6** But thou hast made me a byword among the nations, and I am become a scorn to them.  ^17-6

**17:7** For my eyes are dimmed through pain; I have been grievously beset by all.  ^17-7

**17:8** Wonder has seized true men upon this; and let the just rise up against the transgressor.  ^17-8

**17:9** But let the faithful hold on his own way, and let him that is pure of hands take courage.  ^17-9

**17:10** Howbeit, do ye all strengthen *yourselves* and come now, for I do not find truth in you.  ^17-10

**17:11** My days have passed in groaning, and my heart-strings are broken.  ^17-11

**17:12** I have turned the night into day: the light is short because of darkness.  ^17-12

**17:13** For if I remain, Hades is my habitation: and my bed has been made in darkness.  ^17-13

**17:14** I have called upon death to be my father, and corruption *to be* my mother and sister.  ^17-14

**17:15** Where then is yet my hope? or *where* shall I see my good?  ^17-15

**17:16** Will they go down with me to Hades, or shall we go down together to the tomb?  ^17-16
