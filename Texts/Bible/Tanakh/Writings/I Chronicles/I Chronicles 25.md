## Chapter 25

**25:1** David and the officers of the army set apart for service the sons of Asaph, of Heman, and of Jeduthun, who prophesied to the accompaniment of lyres, harps, and cymbals. The list of men who performed this work, according to their service, was: ^25-1

**25:2** Sons of Asaph: Zaccur, Joseph, Nethaniah, and Asarelah—sons of Asaph under the charge of Asaph, who prophesied by order of the king. ^25-2

**25:3** Jeduthun—the sons of Jeduthun: Gedaliah, Zeri, Jeshaiah, Hashabiah, Mattithiah—6, under the charge of their father Jeduthun, who, accompanied on the harp, prophesied, praising and extolling the LORD. ^25-3

**25:4** Heman—the sons of Heman: Bukkiah, Mattaniah, Uzziel, Shebuel, Jerimoth, Hananiah, Hanani, Eliathah, Giddalti, Romamti-ezer, Joshbekashah, Mallothi, Hothir, and Mahazioth; ^25-4

**25:5** all these were sons of Heman, the seer of the king, [who uttered] prophecies of God for His greater glory. God gave Heman fourteen sons and three daughters; ^25-5

**25:6** all these were under the charge of their father for the singing in the House of the LORD, to the accompaniment of cymbals, harps, and lyres, for the service of the House of God by order of the king. Asaph, Jeduthun, and Heman— ^25-6

**25:7** their total number with their kinsmen, trained singers of the LORD—all the masters, 288. ^25-7

**25:8** They cast lots for shifts on the principle of “small and great alike, like master like apprentice.” ^25-8

**25:9** The first lot fell to Asaph—to Joseph; the second, to Gedaliah, he and his brothers and his sons—12; ^25-9

**25:10** the third, to Zaccur: his sons and his brothers—12; ^25-10

**25:11** the fourth, to Izri: his sons and his brothers—12; ^25-11
