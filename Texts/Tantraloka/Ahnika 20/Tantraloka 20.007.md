## Mūla

बीजस्याप्यत्र कार्या च योजना कृपया गुरोः ।
यतो दीक्षा सुदीप्तत्वात्स्थावराण्यपि मोचयेत् ॥ ^mula

bījasyāpyatra kāryā ca yojanā kṛpayā guroḥ |
yato dīkṣā sudīptatvātsthāvarāṇyapi mocayet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 11 - Chapter Twenty#^pg255|p. 235]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=255>))
>
> (The seed) has been heated (in this way) so that it may not germinate. By this same validating sign (pratyaya), (the three Impurities, namely) Innate, the one called Māyā, and Karma, are burnt away by the power (bala) of mantra, visualization (dhyāna) and ritual action, so that they may not (be capable of producing) their own effect. This sign (pratyaya), namely, the sterilization of the seed, was described to me by the venerable Śambhunātha, who was well pleased. The teacher may, out of compassion, also conjoin the seed (to Śiva), for (this) initiation is so full of fervour (sudīpta) that it can liberate even plants. (with 20.5–7)

## Viveka

> [!viveka]- Viveka in English (Dyczkowski, vol. IX, p. 235)
> ![[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 11 - Chapter Twenty#Viveka on 20.5–7]]

> तप्तं नैतत्प्ररोहाय तेनैव प्रत्ययेन तु ।
> मलमायाख्यकर्माणि मन्त्रध्यानक्रियाबलात् ॥
> दग्धानि न स्वकार्याय निर्बीजप्रत्ययं त्विमम् ।
> स श्रीमान्सुप्रसन्नो मे शम्भुनाथो न्यरूपयत् ॥
> बीजस्याप्यत्र कार्या च योजना कृपया गुरोः ।
> यतो दीक्षा सुदीप्तत्वात्स्थावराण्यपि मोचयेत् ॥

स्वकार्यायेति--बध्यबन्धनाय । सुदीप्तत्वादिति--अदीप्तत्वे हि जङ्गमानामपि योजना असाध्या स्थावराणां का वार्त्ता-- इत्यभिप्रायः । तदुक्तम्--

"ऋक्षपक्षितरक्ष्वादीन् स्थावराण्यपि मोचयेत् ।"

इति ॥७ ॥
