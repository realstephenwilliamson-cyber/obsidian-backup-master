## Mūla

वह्निस्तच्छिवसङ्कल्पतादात्म्याच्छिवतात्मकः ।
इत्येतत्संस्क्रियातत्त्वं श्रीशंभुर्मे न्यरूपयत् ॥ ^mula

vahnistacchivasaṅkalpatādātmyācchivatātmakaḥ |
ityetatsaṃskriyātattvaṃ śrīśaṃbhurme nyarūpayat || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 08 - Chapter Fifteen, part 7#^pg271|p. 253]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=271>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 15.410cd–411ab.
>
> Fire, flaming in this way with the glorious power (of the teacher’s) true (and resolute) intention (satyasaṁkalpa), united with the Mantras and to the Lord, is one with Śiva’s intention, and so is Śiva Himself (śivātmaka). (with 15.407cd–408ab)
>
> The venerable Śambhunātha taught me this, the true (nature and procedure) of purifying the fire, and I have also explained it.’ May those whose intellects are pure reflect on it carefully. (with 15.408cd–409ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* एतच्च अस्माभिर्गुरुपरम्परोपनतमभिहितमिति विवेचनयोग्यम्--इत्याह--

> इत्येतत्संस्क्रियातत्त्वं श्रीशंभुर्मे न्यरूपयत् ॥
> मयापि दर्शितं शुद्धबुद्धयः प्रविविञ्चताम् ।
