# 1 Corinthians 3 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB 1 Corinthians 02|← 1 Corinthians 2]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB 1 Corinthians 04|1 Corinthians 4 →]]

Compare: [[1 Corinthians 03|BSB and SBLGNT]]

**3:1** And I, brethren, could not speak to you as unto spiritual, but as unto carnal. As unto little ones in Christ. ^3-1

**3:2** I gave you milk to drink, not meat; for you were not able as yet. But neither indeed are you now able; for you are yet carnal. ^3-2

**3:3** For, whereas there is among you envying and contention, are you not carnal, and walk according to man? ^3-3

**3:4** For while one saith, I indeed am of Paul; and another, I am of Apollo; are you not men? What then is Apollo, and what is Paul? ^3-4

**3:5** The ministers of him whom you have believed; and to every one as the Lord hath given. ^3-5

**3:6** I have planted, Apollo watered, but God gave the increase. ^3-6

**3:7** Therefore, neither he that planteth is any thing, nor he that watereth; but God that giveth the increase. ^3-7

**3:8** Now he that planteth, and he that watereth, ate one. And every man shall receive his own reward, according to his own labour. ^3-8

**3:9** For we are God’s coadjutors: you are God’s husbandry; you are God’s building. ^3-9

**3:10** According to the grace of God that is given to me, as a wise architect, I have laid the foundation; and another buildeth thereon. But let every man take heed how he buildeth thereupon. ^3-10

**3:11** For other foundation no man can lay, but that which is laid; which is Christ Jesus. ^3-11

**3:12** Now if any man build upon this foundation, gold, silver, precious stones, wood, hay, stubble: ^3-12

**3:13** Every man’s work shall be manifest; for the day of the Lord shall declare it, because it shall be revealed in fire; and the fire shall try every man’s work, of what sort it is. ^3-13

**3:14** If any man’s work abide, which he hath built thereupon, he shall receive a reward. ^3-14

**3:15** If any man’s work burn, he shall suffer loss; but he himself shall be saved, yet so as by fire. ^3-15

**3:16** Know you not, that you are the temple of God, and that the Spirit of God dwelleth in you? ^3-16

**3:17** But if any man violate the temple of God, him shall God destroy. For the temple of God is holy, which you are. ^3-17

**3:18** Let no man deceive himself: if any man among you seem to be wise in this world, let him become a fool, that he may be wise. ^3-18

**3:19** For the wisdom of this world is foolishness with God. For it is written: I will catch the wise in their own craftiness. ^3-19

**3:20** And again: The Lord knoweth the thoughts of the wise, that they are vain. ^3-20

**3:21** Let no man therefore glory in men. ^3-21

**3:22** For all things are yours, whether it be Paul, or Apollo, or Cephas, or the world, or life, or death, or things present, or things to some; for all are yours; ^3-22

**3:23** And you are Christ’s; and Christ is God’s. ^3-23
