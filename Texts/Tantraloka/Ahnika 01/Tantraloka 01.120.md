> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 1.119cd–120ab.
>
> 2) (The Mantra:) Those letters from which, by their (very) nature, (this) juice (rasa) flows as, for example, the labio-dental (V) and dental (S), once combined with some (other) letters, assume the state of seed-syllable (Mantras), and (so) make that (form of) consciousness manifest. Why then cannot an utterance (saṁjalpa) also, (although) essentially a thought construct, impart (a sense of) fulfilment to that same (consciousness) when (we say): “Bring about fulfilment! Nourish him with juice (rasa)!” 3) (The rite:) (Thus,) this rite (which nourishes) should be completed (by saying while offering oblations): “this nectar (amṛta), this milk, this clarified butter is a source of strength. May I nourish his seed with this!” (with 1.117–122)
