# LXX Psalms 42

← [[LXX Psalms 41]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 43]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 043]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 043]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**42:1** A Psalm of David. Judge me, O God, and plead my cause, against an ungodly nation: deliver me from the unjust and crafty man.  ^42-1

**42:2** For thou, O God, art my strength: wherefore hast thou cast me off? and why do I go sad of countenance, while the enemy oppresses *me*?  ^42-2

**42:3** Send forth thy light and thy truth: they have led me, and brought me to thy holy mountain, and to thy tabernacles.  ^42-3

**42:4** And I will go in to the altar of God, to God who gladdens my youth: I will give thanks to thee on the harp, O God, my God.  ^42-4

**42:5** Wherefore art thou very sad, O my soul? and wherefore dost thou trouble me? Hope in God; for I will give thanks to him, *who is* the health of my countenance, *and* my God.  ^42-5
