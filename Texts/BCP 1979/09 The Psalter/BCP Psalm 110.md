# Psalm 110

← [[BCP Psalm 109]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 111]] →

*Dixit Dominus* · Book Five · Twenty-third Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 110]]

**110:1** The LORD said to my Lord, "Sit at my right hand, \*  
until I make your enemies your footstool." ^110-1

**110:2** The LORD will send the scepter of your power out of Zion, \*  
saying, "Rule over your enemies round about you. ^110-2

**110:3** Princely state has been yours from the day of your birth; \*  
in the beauty of holiness have I begotten you,  
like dew from the womb of the morning." ^110-3

**110:4** The LORD has sworn and he will not recant: \*  
"You are a priest for ever after the order of Melchizedek." ^110-4

**110:5** The Lord who is at your right hand  
will smite kings in the day of his wrath; \*  
he will rule over the nations. ^110-5

[p. 754]

**110:6** He will heap high the corpses; \*  
he will smash heads over the wide earth. ^110-6

**110:7** He will drink from the brook beside the road; \*  
therefore he will lift high his head. ^110-7
