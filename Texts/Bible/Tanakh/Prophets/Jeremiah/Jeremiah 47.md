## Chapter 47

**47:1** The word of the LORD that came to the prophet Jeremiah concerning the Philistines, before Pharaoh conquered Gaza. ^47-1

**47:2** Thus said the LORD:  
See, waters are rising from the north,  
They shall become a raging torrent,  
They shall flood the land and its creatures,  
The towns and their inhabitants.  
Men shall cry out,  
All the inhabitants of the land shall howl, ^47-2

**47:3** At the clatter of the stamping hoofs of his stallions,  
At the noise of his chariots,  
The rumbling of their wheels,  
Fathers shall not look to their children  
Out of sheer helplessness[^1] ^47-3

**47:4** Because of the day that is coming  
For ravaging all the Philistines,  
For cutting off every last ally  
Of Tyre and Sidon.  
For the LORD will ravage the Philistines,  
The remnant from the island of Caphtor. ^47-4

**47:5** Baldness[^2] has come upon Gaza,  
Ashkelon is destroyed.  
O remnant of their valley,[^3]  
How long will you gash yourself?[^4] ^47-5

**47:6** “O sword of the LORD,  
When will you be quiet at last?  
Withdraw into your sheath,  
Rest and be still!” ^47-6

**47:7** How can it[^5] be quiet  
When the LORD has given it orders  
Against Ashkelon and the seacoast,  
Given it assignment there? ^47-7

[^1]: Lit. “weakness of hands.”
[^2]: Shaving the head and gashing the body were expressions of mourning; cf. Deut. 14.1.
[^3]: Septuagint reads “the Anakites”; cf. Josh. 11.22.
[^4]: Shaving the head and gashing the body were expressions of mourning; cf. Deut. 14.1.
[^5]: Heb. “you.”
