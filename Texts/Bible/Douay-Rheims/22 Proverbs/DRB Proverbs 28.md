# Proverbs 28 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Proverbs 27|← Proverbs 27]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Proverbs 29|Proverbs 29 →]]

**28:1** The wicked man fleeth, when no man pursueth: but the just, bold as a lion, shall be without dread. ^28-1

**28:2** For the sins of the land many are the princes thereof: and for the wisdom of a man, and the knowledge of those things that are said, the life of the prince shall be prolonged. ^28-2

**28:3** A poor man that oppresseth the poor, is like a violent shower, which bringeth a famine. ^28-3

**28:4** They that forsake the law, praise the wicked man: they that keep it, are incensed against him. ^28-4

**28:5** Evil men think not on judgment: but they that seek after the Lord, take notice of all things. ^28-5

**28:6** Better is the poor man walking in his simplicity, than the rich in crooked ways. ^28-6

**28:7** He that keepeth the law is a wise son: but he that feedeth gluttons, shameth his father. ^28-7

**28:8** He that heapeth together riches by usury and loan, gathereth them for him that will be bountiful to the poor. ^28-8

**28:9** He that turneth away his ears from hearing the law, his prayer shall be as abomination. ^28-9

**28:10** He that deceiveth the just in a wicked way, shall fall in his own destruction: and the upright shall possess his goods. ^28-10

**28:11** The rich man seemeth to himself wise: but the poor man that is prudent shall search him out. ^28-11

**28:12** In the joy of the just there is great glory: when the wicked reign, men are ruined. ^28-12

**28:13** He that hideth his sins, shall not prosper: but he that shall confess, and forsake them, shall obtain mercy. ^28-13

**28:14** Blessed is the man that is always fearful: but he that is hardened in mind, shall fall into evil. ^28-14

**28:15** As a roaring lion, and a hungry bear, so is a wicked prince over the poor people. ^28-15

**28:16** A prince void of prudence shall oppress many by calumny: but he that hateth covetousness, shall prolong his days. ^28-16

**28:17** A man that doth violence to the blood of a person, if he flee even to the pit, no man will stay him. ^28-17

**28:18** He that walketh uprightly, shall be saved: he that is perverse in his ways shall fall at once. ^28-18

**28:19** He that tilleth his ground, shall be filled with bread: but he that followeth idleness shall be filled with poverty. ^28-19

**28:20** A faithful man shall be much praised: but he that maketh haste to be rich, shall not be innocent. ^28-20

**28:21** He that hath respect to a person in judgment, doth not well: such a man even for a morsel of bread forsaketh the truth. ^28-21

**28:22** A man, that maketh haste to be rich, and envieth others, is ignorant that poverty shall come upon him. ^28-22

**28:23** He that rebuketh a man, shall afterward find favour with him, more than he that by a flattering tongue deceiveth him. ^28-23

**28:24** He that stealeth any thing from his father, or from his mother: and saith, This is no sin, is the partner of a murderer. ^28-24

**28:25** He that boasteth, and puffeth up himself, stirreth up quarrels: but he that trusteth in the Lord, shall be healed. ^28-25

**28:26** He that trusteth in his own heart, is a fool: but he that walketh wisely, he shall be saved. ^28-26

**28:27** He that giveth to the poor, shall not want: he that despiseth his entreaty, shall suffer indigence. ^28-27

**28:28** When the wicked rise up, men shall hide themselves: when they perish, the lust shall be multiplied. ^28-28
