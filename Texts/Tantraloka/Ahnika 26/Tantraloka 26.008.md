## Mūla

स्वसंविद्गुरुसंवित्त्योस्तुल्यप्रत्ययभागपि ।
शेषवृत्त्या समादेश्यस्तद्विघ्नादिप्रशान्तये ॥ ^mula

svasaṃvidgurusaṃvittyostulyapratyayabhāgapi |
śeṣavṛttyā samādeśyastadvighnādipraśāntaye || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 17 - Chapter Twenty-Five (continued) and Chapter Twenty-Six#^pg392|p. 372]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=392>))

> (Similarly), the way of life that remains (to be observed after having taken initiation) should also be taught to one who has the same insight (into the Śiva nature of) his own consciousness and that of the teacher, in order to quell obstacles etc. to that.

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* एवं गुरुप्रत्ययवत् स्वप्रत्ययोऽपि यस्यास्ति, तस्यापि एषैव वार्त्ता-- इत्याह--

> स्वसंविद्गुरुसंवित्त्योस्तुल्यप्रत्ययभागपि ।
> शेषवृत्त्या समादेश्यस्तद्विघ्नादिप्रशान्तये ॥
