# Psalm 106

← [[BCP Psalm 105]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 107]] →

** · Book Four · Twenty-first Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 106]]

**Part I** Confitemini Domino

**106:1** Hallelujah!  
Give thanks to the LORD, for he is good, \*  
for his mercy endures for ever. ^106-1

**106:2** Who can declare the mighty acts of the LORD \*  
or show forth all his praise? ^106-2

[p. 742]

**106:3** Happy are those who act with justice \*  
and always do what is right! ^106-3

**106:4** Remember me, O LORD, with the favor you have for your people, \*  
and visit me with your saving help; ^106-4

**106:5** That I may see the prosperity of your elect  
and be glad with the gladness of your people, \*  
that I may glory with your inheritance. ^106-5

**106:6** We have sinned as our forebears did; \*  
we have done wrong and dealt wickedly. ^106-6

**106:7** In Egypt they did not consider your marvelous works,  
nor remember the abundance of your love; \*  
they defied the Most High at the Red Sea. ^106-7

**106:8** But he saved them for his Name's sake, \*  
to make his power known. ^106-8

**106:9** He rebuked the Red Sea, and it dried up, \*  
and he led them through the deep as through a desert. ^106-9

**106:10** He saved them from the hand of those who hated them \*  
and redeemed them from the hand of the enemy. ^106-10

**106:11** The waters covered their oppressors; \*  
not one of them was left. ^106-11

**106:12** Then they believed his words \*  
and sang him songs of praise. ^106-12

**106:13** But they soon forgot his deeds \*  
and did not wait for his counsel. ^106-13

**106:14** A craving seized them in the wilderness, \*  
and they put God to the test in the desert. ^106-14

[p. 743]

**106:15** He gave them what they asked, \*  
but sent leanness into their soul. ^106-15

**106:16** They envied Moses in the camp, \*  
and Aaron, the holy one of the LORD. ^106-16

**106:17** The earth opened and swallowed Dathan \*  
and covered the company of Abiram. ^106-17

**106:18** Fire blazed up against their company, \*  
and flames devoured the wicked. ^106-18

**Psalm 106: Part II** Et fecerunt vitulum

**106:19** Israel made a bull-calf at Horeb \*  
and worshiped a molten image; ^106-19

**106:20** And so they exchanged their Glory \*  
for the image of an ox that feeds on grass. ^106-20

**106:21** They forgot God their Savior, \*  
who had done great things in Egypt, ^106-21

**106:22** Wonderful deeds in the land of Ham, \*  
and fearful things at the Red Sea. ^106-22

**106:23** So he would have destroyed them,  
had not Moses his chosen stood before him in the breach, \*  
to turn away his wrath from consuming them. ^106-23

**106:24** They refused the pleasant land \*  
and would not believe his promise. ^106-24

**106:25** They grumbled in their tents \*  
and would not listen to the voice of the LORD. ^106-25

**106:26** So he lifted his hand against them, \*  
to overthrow them in the wilderness, ^106-26

[p. 744]

**106:27** To cast out their seed among the nations, \*  
and to scatter them throughout the lands. ^106-27

**106:28** They joined themselves to Baal-Peor \*  
and ate sacrifices offered to the dead. ^106-28

**106:29** They provoked him to anger with their actions, \*  
and a plague broke out among them. ^106-29

**106:30** Then Phinehas stood up and interceded, \*  
and the plague came to an end. ^106-30

**106:31** This was reckoned to him as righteousness \*  
throughout all generations for ever. ^106-31

**106:32** Again they provoked his anger at the waters of Meribah, \*  
so that he punished Moses because of them; ^106-32

**106:33** For they so embittered his spirit \*  
that he spoke rash words with his lips. ^106-33

**106:34** They did not destroy the peoples \*  
as the LORD had commanded them. ^106-34

**106:35** They intermingled with the heathen \*  
and learned their pagan ways, ^106-35

**106:36** So that they worshiped their idols, \*  
which became a snare to them. ^106-36

**106:37** They sacrificed their sons \*  
and their daughters to evil spirits. ^106-37

**106:38** They shed innocent blood,  
the blood of their sons and daughters, \*  
which they offered to the idols of Canaan,  
and the land was defiled with blood. ^106-38

[p. 745]

**106:39** Thus they were polluted by their actions \*  
and went whoring in their evil deeds. ^106-39

**106:40** Therefore the wrath of the LORD was kindled against his people \*  
and he abhorred his inheritance. ^106-40

**106:41** He gave them over to the hand of the heathen, \*  
and those who hated them ruled over them. ^106-41

**106:42** Their enemies oppressed them, \*  
and they were humbled under their hand. ^106-42

**106:43** Many a time did he deliver them,  
but they rebelled through their own devices, \*  
and were brought down in their iniquity. ^106-43

**106:44** Nevertheless, he saw their distress, \*  
when he heard their lamentation. ^106-44

**106:45** He remembered his covenant with them \*  
and relented in accordance with his great mercy. ^106-45

**106:46** He caused them to be pitied \*  
by those who held them captive. ^106-46

**106:47** Save us, O LORD our God,  
and gather us from among the nations, \*  
that we may give thanks to your holy Name  
and glory in your praise. ^106-47

**106:48** Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel,  
from everlasting to everlasting; \*  
and let all the people say, "Amen!"  
Hallelujah! ^106-48
