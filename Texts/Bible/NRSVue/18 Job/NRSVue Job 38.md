# Job 38 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Job 37|← Job 37]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Job 39|Job 39 →]]

## The LORD answers Job

**38:1** Then the LORD answered Job out of the whirlwind: ^38-1

**38:2** “Who is this that darkens counsel by words without knowledge? ^38-2

**38:3** Gird up your loins like a man; I will question you, and you shall declare to me. ^38-3

**38:4** “Where were you when I laid the foundation of the earth? Tell me, if you have understanding. ^38-4

**38:5** Who determined its measurements—surely you know! Or who stretched the line upon it? ^38-5

**38:6** On what were its bases sunk, or who laid its cornerstone ^38-6

**38:7** when the morning stars sang together and all the heavenly beings^[Heb *sons of God*] shouted for joy? ^38-7

**38:8** “Or who shut in the sea with doors when it burst out from the womb, ^38-8

**38:9** when I made the clouds its garment and thick darkness its swaddling band, ^38-9

**38:10** and prescribed bounds for it, and set bars and doors, ^38-10

**38:11** and said, ‘Thus far shall you come and no farther, and here shall your proud waves be stopped’? ^38-11

**38:12** “Have you commanded the morning since your days began and caused the dawn to know its place, ^38-12

**38:13** so that it might take hold of the skirts of the earth, and the wicked be shaken out of it? ^38-13

**38:14** It is changed like clay under the seal, and it is dyed^[Cn: Heb *and they stand forth*] like a garment. ^38-14

**38:15** Light is withheld from the wicked, and their uplifted arm is broken. ^38-15

**38:16** “Have you entered into the springs of the sea or walked in the recesses of the deep? ^38-16

**38:17** Have the gates of death been revealed to you, or have you seen the gates of deep darkness? ^38-17

**38:18** Have you comprehended the expanse of the earth? Declare, if you know all this. ^38-18

**38:19** “Where is the way to the dwelling of light, and where is the place of darkness, ^38-19

**38:20** that you may take it to its territory and that you may discern the paths to its home? ^38-20

**38:21** Surely you know, for you were born then, and the number of your days is great! ^38-21

**38:22** “Have you entered the storehouses of the snow, or have you seen the storehouses of the hail, ^38-22

**38:23** which I have reserved for the time of trouble, for the day of battle and war? ^38-23

**38:24** What is the way to the place where the light is distributed or where the east wind is scattered upon the earth? ^38-24

**38:25** “Who has cut a channel for the torrents of rain and a way for the thunderbolt, ^38-25

**38:26** to bring rain on a land where no one lives, on the desert, which is empty of human life, ^38-26

**38:27** to satisfy the waste and desolate land, and to make the ground put forth grass? ^38-27

**38:28** “Has the rain a father, or who has fathered the drops of dew? ^38-28

**38:29** From whose womb did the ice come forth, and who has given birth to the hoarfrost of heaven? ^38-29

**38:30** The waters become hard like stone, and the face of the deep is frozen. ^38-30

**38:31** “Can you bind the chains of the Pleiades or loose the cords of Orion? ^38-31

**38:32** Can you lead forth the Mazzaroth in their season, or can you guide the Bear with its children? ^38-32

**38:33** Do you know the ordinances of the heavens? Can you establish their rule on the earth? ^38-33

**38:34** “Can you lift up your voice to the clouds, so that a flood of waters may cover you? ^38-34

**38:35** Can you send forth lightnings, so that they may go and say to you, ‘Here we are’? ^38-35

**38:36** Who has put wisdom in the inward parts^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] or given understanding to the mind?^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^38-36

**38:37** Who has the wisdom to number the clouds? Or who can tilt the waterskins of the heavens ^38-37

**38:38** when the dust runs into a mass and the clods cling together? ^38-38

**38:39** “Can you hunt the prey for the lion or satisfy the appetite of the young lions, ^38-39

**38:40** when they crouch in their dens or lie in wait in their covert? ^38-40

**38:41** Who provides for the raven its prey, when its young ones cry to God and wander about for lack of food? ^38-41
