## Chapter 15

**15:1** The spirit of God came upon Azariah son of Oded. ^15-1

**15:2** He came to Asa and said to him, “Listen to me, Asa and all Judah and Benjamin; the LORD is with you as long as you are with Him. If you turn to Him, He will respond to you, but if you forsake Him, He will forsake you. ^15-2

**15:3** Israel has gone many days without the true God, without a priest to give instruction and without Teaching. ^15-3

**15:4** But in distress it returned to the LORD God of Israel, and sought Him, and He responded to them. ^15-4

**15:5** At those times, no wayfarer[^1] was safe, for there was much tumult among all the inhabitants of the lands. ^15-5

**15:6** Nation was crushed by nation and city by city, for God threw them into panic with every kind of trouble. ^15-6

**15:7** As for you, be strong, do not be disheartened, for there is reward for your labor.” ^15-7

**15:8** When Asa heard these words, the prophecy of Oded the prophet, he took courage and removed the abominations from the entire land of Judah and Benjamin and from the cities that he had captured in the hill country of Ephraim. He restored the altar of the LORD in front of the porch of the LORD. ^15-8

**15:9** He assembled all the people of Judah and Benjamin and those people of Ephraim, Manasseh, and Simeon who sojourned among them, for many in Israel had thrown in their lot with him when they saw that the LORD his God was with him. ^15-9

**15:10** They were assembled in Jerusalem in the third month of the fifteenth year of the reign of Asa. ^15-10

**15:11** They brought sacrifices to the LORD on that day; they brought 700 oxen and 7,000 sheep of the spoil. ^15-11

**15:12** They entered into a covenant to worship the LORD God of their fathers with all their heart and with all their soul. ^15-12

**15:13** Whoever would not worship the LORD God of Israel would be put to death, whether small or great, whether man or woman. ^15-13

**15:14** So they took an oath to the LORD in a loud voice and with shouts, with trumpeting and blasts of the horn. ^15-14

**15:15** All Judah rejoiced over the oath, for they swore with all their heart and sought Him with all their will. He responded to them and gave them respite on every side. ^15-15

**15:16** [^2]He[^3] also deposed Maacah mother of King Asa from the rank of queen mother, because she had made an abominable thing for [the goddess] Asherah. Asa cut down her abominable thing, reduced it to dust, and burned it in the Wadi Kidron. ^15-16

**15:17** The shrines, indeed, were not abolished in Israel; however, Asa was wholehearted [with the LORD] all his life. ^15-17

**15:18** He brought into the House of God the things that he and his father had consecrated—silver, gold, and utensils. ^15-18

**15:19** There was no war until the thirty-fifth year of the reign of Asa. ^15-19

[^1]: Lit. “one who goes out and one who comes in.”
[^2]: With vv. 16–19, cf. 1 Kings 15.13–16.
[^3]: I.e., Asa.
