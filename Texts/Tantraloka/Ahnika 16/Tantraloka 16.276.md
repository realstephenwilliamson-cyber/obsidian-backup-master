## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 07 - Chapter Sixteen, part 3#^pg143|p. 123]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=143>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 16.275cd–276ab.
>
> A lover, who is beautiful with (the delight of) discourse and (joyful) union with (his) beloved, and so too etched (saṁskrta) with (the memories of) it, (whatever) else he (happens to be) doing, he is satisfied (and joyful) within himself. Likewise, one who is sanctified (bhāvita) by this mantric discourse, even when saying (anything) else, it is (spontaneously) identified with it even if he does not wish it, and it most certainly produces the same results. (with 16.276–277)
