## Mūla

आर्यावाक्यमिदं प्रोक्तमष्टमं नवभिः पदैः ।
हे ब्रह्मन्! हे विष्णो! हे रुद्र! शिवोऽसि वासुदेवस्त्वम् ॥ ^mula

āryāvākyamidaṃ proktamaṣṭamaṃ navabhiḥ padaiḥ |
he brahman! he viṣṇo! he rudra! śivo'si vāsudevastvam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 03 - Chapter Thirty, part 2#^pg66|p. 48]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=66>))
>
> This is said to be the eighth declaration, set in Ārya metre, (and it is) made with nine words. (with 30.79ab)
>
> 9) “O Brahmā! O Viṣṇu! O Rudra! You are Śiva! You are Vasudeva! O great sky! Abandon indeed the primeval body of earth (fashioned from) Fire and Soma.” (with 30.79cd–80ab)
