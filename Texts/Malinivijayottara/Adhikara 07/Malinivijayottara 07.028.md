## Mūla

मध्यमे द्वे युते कार्ये कनिष्ठे परुषावधि ।
तर्जन्यौ मध्यपार्श्वस्थे विरले परिकल्पिते ॥ ^mula

madhyame dve yute kārye kaniṣṭhe paruṣāvadhi |
tarjanyau madhyapārśvasthe virale parikalpite || ^iast

*Dyczkowski prints no Sanskrit for this chapter; the text is that of the KSTS edition (Muktabodha M00160).*

> The two middle fingers should be connected together and the two little fingers at the end of the *paruṣa (paruṣāvadhi);* the two index fingers are placed next to the middle fingers and splayed apart.
