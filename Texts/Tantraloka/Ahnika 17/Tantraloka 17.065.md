## Mūla

एवं क्रमात्कलातत्त्वे शुद्धे पाशं भुजाश्रितम् ।
छिन्द्यात्कला हि सा किञ्चित्कर्तृत्वोन्मीलनात्मिका ॥ ^mula

evaṃ kramātkalātattve śuddhe pāśaṃ bhujāśritam |
chindyātkalā hi sā kiñcitkartṛtvonmīlanātmikā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 08 - Chapter Seventeen#^pg188|p. 168]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=188>))
>
> (Continuing) in this way, (purifying one principle after the other) successively until the principle of Force (kalā) has been purified, (the teacher) should then sever the fetter (represented by the first knot in the thread) that hangs on (the disciple’s) arm. Indeed, Force is the opening up (of the soul’s) limited agency, and is the unfolding (jrmbha) of the Impurity called Karma. (Having placed) that knot on the tip of the sacrificial ladle, (the teacher) should burn it at the same time as (he offers) a full oblation into the radiant energy of the Mantra of Fire. (with 17.65–66)
