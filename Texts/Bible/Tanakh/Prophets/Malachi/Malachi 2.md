## Chapter 2

**2:1** And now, O priests, this charge is for you: ^2-1

**2:2** Unless you obey and unless you lay it to heart, and do honor to My name—said the LORD of Hosts—I will send a curse and turn your blessings into curses. (Indeed, I have turned them into curses, because you do not lay it to heart.) ^2-2

**2:3** I will put your seed under a ban,[^1] and I will strew dung upon your faces, the dung of your festal sacrifices, and you shall be carried out to its [heap]. ^2-3

**2:4** Know, then, that I have sent this charge to you that My covenant with Levi may endure—said the LORD of Hosts. ^2-4

**2:5** I had with him a covenant of life and well-being, which I gave to him, and of reverence, which he showed Me. For he stood in awe of My name. ^2-5

**2:6** [^2]Proper rulings were in his mouth,  
And nothing perverse was on his lips;  
He served Me with complete loyalty  
And held the many back from iniquity. ^2-6

**2:7** For the lips of a priest guard knowledge,  
And men seek rulings from his mouth;[^3]  
For he is a messenger of the LORD of Hosts. ^2-7

**2:8** But you have turned away from that course: You have made the many stumble through your rulings;[^4] you have corrupted the covenant of the Levites—said the LORD of Hosts. ^2-8

**2:9** And I, in turn, have made you despicable and vile in the eyes of all the people, because you disregard My ways and show partiality in your rulings. ^2-9

**2:10** Have we not all one Father? Did not one God create us? Why do we break faith with one another, profaning the covenant of our ancestors? ^2-10

**2:11** Judah has broken faith; abhorrent things have been done in Israel and in Jerusalem. For Judah has profaned what is holy to the LORD—what He desires—and espoused daughters of alien gods. ^2-11

**2:12** May the LORD leave to him who does this no descendants[^5] dwelling in the tents of Jacob and presenting offerings to the LORD of Hosts. ^2-12

**2:13** And this you do as well:[^6] You cover the altar of the LORD with tears, weeping, and moaning, so that He refuses to regard the oblation any more and to accept what you offer.[^7] ^2-13

**2:14** But you ask, “Because of what?” Because the LORD is a witness between you and the wife of your youth with whom you have broken faith, though she is your partner and covenanted spouse. ^2-14

**2:15** Did not the One make [all,] [^8]so that all remaining life-breath is His? And what does that One seek but godly folk? So be careful of your life-breath,[^9] and let no one break faith with the wife of his youth. ^2-15

**2:16** For I detest divorce—said the LORD, the God of Israel—and covering oneself with lawlessness as with a garment[^10]—said the LORD of Hosts. So be careful of your life-breath and do not act treacherously. ^2-16

**2:17** You have wearied the LORD with your talk. But you ask, “By what have we wearied [Him]?” By saying, “All who do evil are good in the sight of the LORD, and in them He delights,” or else, “Where is the God of justice?” ^2-17

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: See Hag. 2.10–13; cf. Lev. 10.8–11, Deut. 33.8, 10.
[^3]: Or: For the lips of a priest are observed; / Knowledge and ruling are sought from his mouth.
[^4]: By ruling falsely that an act was licit or an object ritually pure.
[^5]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^6]: Lit. “a second time”; Septuagint reads “which I detest”; cf. v. 16.
[^7]: Lit. “from your hand.”
[^8]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^9]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^10]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
