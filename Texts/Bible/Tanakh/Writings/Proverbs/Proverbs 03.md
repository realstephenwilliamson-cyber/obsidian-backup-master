# Proverbs 03

## Chapter 3

**3:1** My son, do not forget my teaching,  
But let your mind retain my commandments; ^3-1

**3:2** For they will bestow on you length of days,  
Years of life and well-being. ^3-2

**3:3** Let fidelity and steadfastness not leave you;  
Bind them about your throat,  
Write them on the tablet of your mind, ^3-3

**3:4** And you will find favor and approbation  
In the eyes of God and man. ^3-4

**3:5** Trust in the LORD with all your heart,  
And do not rely on your own understanding. ^3-5

**3:6** In all your ways acknowledge Him,  
And He will make your paths smooth. ^3-6

**3:7** Do not be wise in your own eyes;  
Fear the LORD and shun evil. ^3-7

**3:8** It will be a cure for your body,[^1]  
A tonic for your bones. ^3-8

**3:9** Honor the LORD with your wealth,  
With the best of all your income, ^3-9

**3:10** And your barns will be filled with grain,  
Your vats will burst with new wine. ^3-10

**3:11** Do not reject the discipline of the LORD, my son;  
Do not abhor His rebuke. ^3-11

**3:12** For whom the LORD loves, He rebukes,  
As a father the son whom he favors. ^3-12

**3:13** Happy is the man who finds wisdom,  
The man who attains understanding. ^3-13

**3:14** Her value in trade is better than silver,  
Her yield, greater than gold. ^3-14

**3:15** She is more precious than rubies;  
All of your goods cannot equal her. ^3-15

**3:16** In her right hand is length of days,  
In her left, riches and honor. ^3-16

**3:17** Her ways are pleasant ways,  
And all her paths, peaceful. ^3-17

**3:18** She is a tree of life to those who grasp her,  
And whoever holds on to her is happy. ^3-18

**3:19** The LORD founded the earth by wisdom;  
He established the heavens by understanding; ^3-19

**3:20** By His knowledge the depths burst apart,  
And the skies distilled dew. ^3-20

**3:21** My son, do not lose sight of them;  
Hold on to resourcefulness and foresight. ^3-21

**3:22** They will give life to your spirit  
And grace to your throat. ^3-22

**3:23** Then you will go your way safely  
And not injure your feet. ^3-23

**3:24** When you lie down you will be unafraid;  
You will lie down and your sleep will be sweet. ^3-24

**3:25** You will not fear sudden terror  
Or the disaster that comes upon the wicked, ^3-25

**3:26** For the LORD will be your trust;  
He will keep your feet from being caught. ^3-26

**3:27** Do not withhold good from one who  
deserves it  
When you have the power to do it [for him]. ^3-27

**3:28** Do not say to your fellow, “Come back again;  
I’ll give it to you tomorrow,” when you have it with you. ^3-28

**3:29** Do not devise harm against your fellow  
Who lives trustfully with you. ^3-29

**3:30** Do not quarrel with a man for no cause,  
When he has done you no harm. ^3-30

**3:31** Do not envy a lawless man,  
Or choose any of his ways; ^3-31

**3:32** For the devious man is an abomination to the LORD,  
But He is intimate with the straightforward. ^3-32

**3:33** The curse of the LORD is on the house of the wicked,  
But He blesses the abode of the righteous. ^3-33

**3:34** At scoffers He scoffs,  
But to the lowly He shows grace. ^3-34

**3:35** The wise shall obtain honor,  
But dullards get disgrace as their portion. ^3-35

[^1]: Lit. “navel.”
