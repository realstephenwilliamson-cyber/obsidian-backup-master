## Mūla

प्रोक्ता ह्यशुद्धिस्तत्रैव तस्य क्वापि विशुद्धता ।
पञ्चगव्ये पवित्रत्वं सोमचर्णनपात्रयोः ॥ ^mula

proktā hyaśuddhistatraiva tasya kvāpi viśuddhatā |
pañcagavye pavitratvaṃ somacarṇanapātrayoḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 04 - Chapter Fifteen, part 3#^pg124|p. 106]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=124>))
>
> Wine and the like are said to be impure in the scriptures of the fettered (i.e., the Vedas) (only) in order to safeguard the stability of society. (This is clear from the fact that) in certain cases there itself (in these same scriptures), that (same thing) is said to be pure. (with 15.170cd–171ab)
>
> The purity of the five products of a cow, the rites for the pressing (carṇana) of the soma and the jar where it is collected, the concluding ritual ablution (avabhṛthasnāna), the horns of a black antelope are held (in the sacrificer’s) hand, (the fact that) without (the presence of the sacrificer’s) wife, (there could be) no sacrifice, the equality of all the deities, the offering of rice beer (surā) to the fire in the Brahmasattra, the offerings to the sacrificial fire of the omentum (vapā), entrails, and the heart (of the sacrificial victim). (with 15.171cd–173ab)
