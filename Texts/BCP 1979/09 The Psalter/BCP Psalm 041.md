# Psalm 41

← [[BCP Psalm 040]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 042]] →

*Beatus qui intelligit* · Book One · Eighth Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 041]]

**41:1** Happy are they who consider the poor and needy! \*  
the LORD will deliver them in the time of trouble. ^41-1

[p. 642]

**41:2** The LORD preserves them and keeps them alive,  
so that they may be happy in the land; \*  
he does not hand them over to the will of their enemies. ^41-2

**41:3** The LORD sustains them on their sickbed \*  
and ministers to them in their illness. ^41-3

**41:4** I said, "LORD, be merciful to me; \*  
heal me, for I have sinned against you." ^41-4

**41:5** My enemies are saying wicked things about me: \*  
When will he die, and his name perish?" ^41-5

**41:6** Even if they come to see me, they speak empty words; \*  
their heart collects false rumors;  
they go outside and spread them. ^41-6

**41:7** All my enemies whisper together about me \*  
and devise evil against me. ^41-7

**41:8** "A deadly thing," they say, "has fastened on him; \*  
he has taken to his bed and will never get up again." ^41-8

**41:9** Even my best friend, whom I trusted,  
who broke bread with me, \*  
has lifted up his heel and turned against me. ^41-9

**41:10** But you, O LORD, be merciful to me and raise me up, \*  
and I shall repay them. ^41-10

**41:11** By this I know you are pleased with me, \*  
that my enemy does not triumph over me. ^41-11

**41:12** In my integrity you hold me fast, \*  
and shall set me before your face for ever. ^41-12

**41:13** Blessed be the LORD God of Israel, \*  
from age to age. Amen. Amen. ^41-13
