# Hebrews 3 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Hebrews 02|← Hebrews 2]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Hebrews 04|Hebrews 4 →]]

Compare: [[Hebrews 03|BSB and SBLGNT]]

**3:1** Wherefore, holy brethren, partakers of the heavenly vocation, consider the apostle and high priest of our confession, Jesus: ^3-1

**3:2** Who is faithful to him that made him, as was also Moses in all his house. ^3-2

**3:3** For this man was counted worthy of greater glory than Moses, by so much as he that hath built the house, hath greater honour than the house. ^3-3

**3:4** For every house is built by some man: but he that created all things, is God. ^3-4

**3:5** And Moses indeed was faithful in all his house as a servant, for a testimony of those things which were to be said: ^3-5

**3:6** But Christ as the Son in his own house: which house are we, if we hold fast the confidence and glory of hope unto the end. ^3-6

**3:7** Wherefore, as the Holy Ghost saith: Today if you shall hear his voice, ^3-7

**3:8** Harden not your hearts, as in the provocation; in the day of temptation in the desert, ^3-8

**3:9** Where your fathers tempted me, proved and saw my works, ^3-9

**3:10** Forty years: for which cause I was offended with this generation, and I said: They always err in heart. And they have not known my ways, ^3-10

**3:11** As I have sworn in my wrath: If they shall enter into my rest. ^3-11

**3:12** Take heed, brethren, lest perhaps there be in any of you an evil heart of unbelief, to depart from the living God. ^3-12

**3:13** But exhort one another every day, whilst it is called today, that none of you be hardened through the deceitfulness of sin. ^3-13

**3:14** For we are made partakers of Christ: yet so, if we hold the beginning of his substance firm unto the end. ^3-14

**3:15** While it is said, Today if you shall hear his voice, harden not your hearts, as in that provocation. ^3-15

**3:16** For some who heard did provoke: but not all that came out of Egypt by Moses. ^3-16

**3:17** And with whom was he offended forty years? Was it not with them that sinned, whose carcasses were overthrown in the desert? ^3-17

**3:18** And to whom did he swear, that they should not enter into his rest: but to them that were incredulous? ^3-18

**3:19** And we see that they could not enter in, because of unbelief. ^3-19
