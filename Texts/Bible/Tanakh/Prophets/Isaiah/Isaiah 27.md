# Isaiah 27

## Chapter 27

**27:1** In that day the LORD will punish,  
With His great, cruel, mighty sword  
Leviathan the Elusive[^1] Serpent—  
Leviathan the Twisting[^2] Serpent;  
He will slay the Dragon of the sea.[^3] ^27-1

**27:2** In that day,  
They shall sing of it:[^4]  
“Vineyard of Delight.”[^5] ^27-2

**27:3** I the LORD keep watch over it,  
I water it every moment;  
That no harm may befall it,[^6]  
I watch it night and day. ^27-3

**27:4** There is no anger in Me:  
If one offers Me thorns and thistles,  
I will march to battle against him,  
And set all of them on fire.[^7] ^27-4

**27:5** But if he holds fast to My refuge,  
He makes Me his friend;  
He makes Me his friend.[^8] ^27-5

**27:6** [In days] to come Jacob shall strike root,  
Israel shall sprout and blossom,  
And the face of the world  
Shall be covered with fruit. ^27-6

**27:7** Was he beaten as his beater has been?  
Did he suffer such slaughter as his slayers? ^27-7

**27:8** Assailing them[^9] with fury unchained,  
His pitiless blast bore them off  
On a day of gale. ^27-8

**27:9** [^10]Assuredly, by this alone  
Shall Jacob’s sin be purged away;  
This is the only price  
For removing his guilt:  
That he make all the altar-stones  
Like shattered blocks of chalk—  
With no sacred post left standing,  
Nor any incense altar. ^27-9

**27:10** Thus fortified cities lie desolate,  
Homesteads deserted, forsaken like a wilderness;  
There calves graze, there they lie down  
[^11]And consume its boughs. ^27-10

**27:11** When its crown is withered, they break;  
Women come and make fires with them.  
For they are a people without understanding;  
That is why  
Their Maker will show them no mercy,  
Their Creator will deny them grace. ^27-11

**27:12** And in that day, the LORD will beat out [the peoples like grain] from the channel of the Euphrates to the Wadi of Egypt; and you shall be picked up one by one, O children of Israel! ^27-12

**27:13** And in that day, a great ram’s horn shall be sounded; and the strayed who are in the land of Assyria and the expelled who are in the land of Egypt shall come and worship the LORD on the holy mount, in Jerusalem. ^27-13

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: The monster which the Lord vanquished of old (cf. 51.9; Ps. 74.13–14) was the embodiment of chaos; here it stands for the forces of evil in the present world.
[^4]: Apparently the earth; cf. 26.21.
[^5]: So some mss. (cf. Amos 5.11); other mss. and the editions have “Wine.”
[^6]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain; emendation yields “My eye is open upon it.”
[^7]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^8]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^9]: Lit. “Striving with her”; meaning of verse uncertain.
[^10]: This verse would read well before v. 6; the thought of vv. 7–8, dealing with the punishment of Israel’s enemies, is continued in vv. 10–11.
[^11]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain. Emendation yields “Or like a terebinth whose boughs / Break when its crown is withered.”
