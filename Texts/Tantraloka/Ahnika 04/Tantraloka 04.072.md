## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. III
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 03 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v03 04 - Chapter Four, part 3#^pg100|p. 87]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 03.pdf#page=100>))
>
> He who has worshipped (his) teacher with devotion by any (befitting) means, knows the purport of the scriptures through the series of initiations (dīkṣākrama) (the teacher gives him). Then, once he has obtained consecration (abhiṣeka), he becomes a ‘created’ (kalpita) teacher. However, even though he is such (and not one who is spontaneously enlightened), he is (equally) skilled in stemming the torrent (of his disciples’) fetters. (with 4.70cd–72ab)
>
> (The teacher) who attains spontaneous (āākasmika) awakened consciousness (bodha) of any matter taught in the scriptures (by practicing) in conformity with (the reality and) procedure (they teach) (yathākramayogena) is one who is ‘created-cum-uncreated’. (with 4.72cd–73ab)

> यो यथाक्रमयोगेन कस्ंमिश्चिच्छास्त्रवस्तुनि ॥
> आकस्मिकं व्रजेद्बोधं कल्पिताकल्पितो हि सः ।

यः कश्चित्कल्पितो गुरुः, कस्ंमिश्चित् -- लोकोत्तरे शास्त्रीये पारर्माथिकप्रमेयरूपे वस्तुनि, आकस्मिकं -- गुर्वाद्यनपेक्षमेव, यथाक्रमयोगेन -- यथावस्तु, बोधमासादयेत्, स कल्पितत्त्वेऽपि स्वयमेव बोधप्रवृत्तेरकल्पितः ॥ ७२ ॥
