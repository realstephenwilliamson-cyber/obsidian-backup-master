## Mūla

विषयप्रतिबिम्बं च तस्यामक्षकृतं बहिः ।
अतद्द्वारं समुत्प्रेक्षाप्रतिभादिषु तादृशी ॥ ^mula

viṣayapratibimbaṃ ca tasyāmakṣakṛtaṃ bahiḥ |
ataddvāraṃ samutprekṣāpratibhādiṣu tādṛśī || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 08 - Chapter Nine, part 6#^pg232|p. 215]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=232>))
>
> The reflection of the object (viṣaya) within it may be either external, that is, fashioned by the senses, or unmediated (by them), as happens when imagining, intuiting and so forth. The activity of the intellect is the cognitive consciousness (bodha) (it has of objects). And (so,) although (its cognitive) consciousness (bodha) is insentient, insofar as (the intellect) has as its firm support the Light of the consciousness of the Self, it is (nonetheless) capable (alam) (of perceiving its object). (with 9.228–229)
