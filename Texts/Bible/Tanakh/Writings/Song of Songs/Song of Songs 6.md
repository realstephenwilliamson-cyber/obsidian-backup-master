## Chapter 6

**6:1** “Whither has your beloved gone,  
O fairest of women?  
Whither has your beloved turned?  
Let us seek him with you.” ^6-1

**6:2** My beloved has gone down to his garden,  
To the beds of spices,  
To browse in the gardens  
And to pick lilies. ^6-2

**6:3** I am my beloved’s  
And my beloved is mine;  
He browses among the lilies. ^6-3

**6:4** You are beautiful, my darling, as Tirzah,  
Comely as Jerusalem,  
Awesome as bannered hosts.[^1] ^6-4

**6:5** Turn your eyes away from me,  
For they overwhelm me!  
Your hair is like a flock of goats  
Streaming down from Gilead. ^6-5

**6:6** Your teeth are like a flock of ewes  
Climbing up from the washing pool;  
All of them bear twins,  
And not one loses her young. ^6-6

**6:7** Your brow behind your veil  
[Gleams] like a pomegranate split open. ^6-7

**6:8** There are sixty queens,  
And eighty concubines,  
And damsels without number. ^6-8

**6:9** Only one is my dove,  
My perfect one,  
The only one of her mother,  
The delight of her who bore her.  
Maidens see and acclaim her;  
Queens and concubines, and praise her. ^6-9

**6:10** Who is she that shines through like the dawn,  
Beautiful as the moon,  
Radiant as the sun  
Awesome as bannered hosts?[^2] ^6-10

**6:11** I went down to the nut grove  
To see the budding of the vale;  
To see if the vines had blossomed,  
If the pomegranates were in bloom. ^6-11

**6:12** Before I knew it,  
My desire set me  
Mid the chariots of Ammi-nadib.[^3] ^6-12

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
