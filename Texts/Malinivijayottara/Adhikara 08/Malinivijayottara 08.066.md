## Mūla

वामा ज्येष्ठा च रौद्री च सर्वाः कालानलप्रभाः ।
ब्रह्मविष्णुहराः पूर्वं ये शाक्ताः प्रतिपादिताः ॥ ^mula

vāmā jyeṣṭhā ca raudrī ca sarvāḥ kālānalaprabhāḥ |
brahmaviṣṇuharāḥ pūrvaṁ ye śāktāḥ pratipāditāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2025 – April 2025
> p. 18 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=32>))
>
> One should then imagine (vikalpayet) another group of nine (goddesses) beginning with Vibhvī (arranged) anticlockwise. (They are) Vibhvī, Jñānī, Kriyā, Icchā, Vāgīśī, Jvālinī, Vāmā, Jyeṣṭhā, and Raudrī. All of them (shine) with the light of the Fire of Time.[^73] (with 8.65–66ab)

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2025 – April 2025
> p. 19 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=33>))
>
> In the centre, the stamens and the petals, one should meditate (respectively) on Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Hara, taught previously, empowered (with these energies). (They are) the lords of the circles of the Fire, Moon and Sun.[^74] (with 8.66cd–67)

[^73]: 60-66ab are paraphrased and explained in TĀ 15/302-306. 61-66ab are quoted in TĀv ad 15/303cd-307.
[^74]: The text here is partially corrupt. The sense however is clear on the basis of TĀ 15/307 and also SvT 2/72cd-73.
