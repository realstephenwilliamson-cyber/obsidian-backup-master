## Mūla

शूलयागाः षट् सहस्राण्येवं सार्धशतद्वयम् ।
या सा देवी परा शक्तिः प्राणवाहा व्यवस्थिता ॥ ^mula

śūlayāgāḥ ṣaṭ sahasrāṇyevaṃ sārdhaśatadvayam |
yā sā devī parā śaktiḥ prāṇavāhā vyavasthitā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 04 - Chapter Thirty-One, part 1#^pg103|p. 85]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=103>))
>
> Or what is the use of speaking much? The trident mandalas (Śūlayāga) with three tridents and seven spokes are six thousand. Such (is the number of) the two hundred and fifty (mandalas) mentioned previously (multiplied by twenty-four). (with 31.42cd–43ab)
>
> That Goddess who is the supreme (parā) power, established as the one who transports the vital breath, present within all things, her form (as Kundalini, circular like an) earring (kuṇdala), is fashioned (and made to abide) here (in this mandala) in a directly (apparent manner). (All) the reality levels (tattva) and their (presiding) goddesses, (indeed) everything, is installed (pratiṣthita) (and well established) within this. (with 31.43cd–44)
