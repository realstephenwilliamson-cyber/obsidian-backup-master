# V. Raghavan

Sanskrit scholar (22 August 1908–1979) of the University of Madras, where he rose to professor and head of the Department of Sanskrit and retired in 1968; his fields include Sanskrit poetics and aesthetics, manuscript cataloguing and music, and he was secretary of the Madras Music Academy from 1944 until his death ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V._Raghavan)). He is named a Kuppuswami Sastri Research Institute secretary and journal editor until his death on the same page; the page names no single final post.

Identification: Wikipedia lists no work on Abhinavagupta. An Exotic India listing for *Abhinavagupta and His Works* by "Dr. V. Raghavan" gives the same career (retired 1968 as Professor of Sanskrit and Head of the Department, University of Madras) ([listing](https://exoticindiaart.com/book/details/abhinavagupta-and-his-works-photo-copy-old-book-ide577)). The catalog note dates the book 1980, after his death; the same listing gives 1981 for its edition.
