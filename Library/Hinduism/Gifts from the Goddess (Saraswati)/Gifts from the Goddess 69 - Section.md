### Some Preliminaries

Before delving into the specifics of *Śrī Cakra Pūjā*, let’s talk a little about the foundational concepts that underlie the practice. Where does the puja come from? Why do different schools of Sri Vidya approach it differently? What are its elemental ideas and concepts—Shakti, Kundalini, Sri Chakra, mantra, ritual? We’ll begin by looking at a primary sourcebook of Sri Vidya instruction, the *Paraśurāma Kalpasūtra*, and then do some exploring to see what we can really learn from it.
