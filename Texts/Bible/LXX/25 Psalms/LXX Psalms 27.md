# LXX Psalms 27

← [[LXX Psalms 26]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 28]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 028]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 028]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**27:1** *A Psalm* of David. To thee, O Lord, have I cried; my God, be not silent toward me: lest thou be silent toward me, and so I should be likened to them that go down to the pit.  ^27-1

**27:2** Hearken to the voice of my supplication, when I pray to thee, when I lift up my hands toward thy holy temple.  ^27-2

**27:3** Draw not away my soul with sinners, and destroy me not with the workers of iniquity, who speak peace with their neighbours, but evils are in their hearts.  ^27-3

**27:4** Give them according to their works, and according to the wickedness of their devices: give them according to the works of their hands; render their recompence unto them.  ^27-4

**27:5** Because they have not attended to the works of the Lord, even to the works of his hands, thou shalt pull them down, and shalt not build them up.  ^27-5

**27:6** Blessed be the Lord, for he has hearkened to the voice of my petition.  ^27-6

**27:7** The Lord is my helper and my defender; my heart has hoped in him, and I am helped: my flesh has revived, and willingly will I give praise to him.[^1]  ^27-7

**27:8** The Lord is the strength of his people, and the saving defender of his anointed.[^2]  ^27-8

**27:9** Save thy people, and bless thine inheritance: and take care of them, and lift them up for ever.  ^27-9

[^1]: 27:7 *Or,* confess to him.
[^2]: 27:8 *Gr.* defender of the salvation of.
