## Mūla

प्रकाशः परमः शक्तिपातोऽवच्छेदवर्जितः ।
तथाविधोऽपि भोगांशावच्छेदेनोपलक्षितः ॥ ^mula

prakāśaḥ paramaḥ śaktipāto'vacchedavarjitaḥ |
tathāvidho'pi bhogāṃśāvacchedenopalakṣitaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 09 - Chapter Thirteen, part 5#^pg305|p. 283]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=305>))
>
> One should know that this is the essence here (of the teaching): the supreme (para) form of the descent of the power (of grace), free of (all) limitations, is (the direct experience of) the Light of the Self, that is completely full (and perfect) consciousness. Although the inferior (apara) form of the descent of the power (of grace) is also like this, it is characterised by the limitation imposed by the aspect (of consciousness) which is the worldly experience (that is the consequence of Karma). (Even 50,) ultimately, this (too) bestows identity with Śiva (śivatā). (with 13.254cd–256ab)

## Viveka

> [!viveka]- Viveka in English (Dyczkowski, vol. VII, p. 284)
> ![[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 09 - Chapter Thirteen, part 5#Viveka on 13.254–256]]

> इदं सारमिह ज्ञेयं परिपूर्णचिदात्मनः ॥
> प्रकाशः परमः शक्तिपातोऽवच्छेदवर्जितः ।
> तथाविधोऽपि भोगांशावच्छेदेनोपलक्षितः ॥
> अपरः शक्तिपातोऽसौ पर्यन्ते शिवताप्रदः ।

इदम् इहास्मिन् नवविधेऽपि शक्तिपाते सारं ज्ञेयं यत्परिमितेऽप्यात्मनि परिपूर्णचिदात्मतया यः परमः प्रकाशः, स परमः शक्तिपात उच्यते, यतोऽवच्छेदवर्जितस्तत्तदुपाधिविगलनादनवच्छिन्नसंविदेकस्वभावः - - इत्यर्थः । असौ पुनरपरः शक्तिपातो यः परिपूर्णचिदात्मतया प्रकाशमानत्वेऽपि भोगांशावच्छिन्नः । अत एवोक्तम् -- पर्यन्ते शिवताप्रद इति ॥
