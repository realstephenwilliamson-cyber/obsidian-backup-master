# Scriptural Anatomical Mapping (Aṅga-sthāna & Mātṛkā-nyāsa)

> [!abstract] The letters placed on the body
> In *mātṛkā-nyāsa* the fifty letters of the alphabet, from a to kṣa, are set one by one on the body, so that the body of the one who worships becomes the body of the goddess made of sound. Abhinavagupta gives the list in [[Tantraloka 15.116|Tantrāloka 15.116–120]], as part of the preparation for initiation; Jayaratha's commentary fills in the order and quotes a parallel verse. The meaning of each vowel as a phase of consciousness is the subject of [[Texts/Tantraloka/Ahnika 03/Tantraloka 03.001|Āhnika 3]].
>
> Everything below is checked against the Tantrāloka notes in this vault (root text and Viveka; translation in Dyczkowski vol. VIII, pp. 78–79). The crash-session version of this note had several places wrong; see the end.

[[Tantraloka 15.116|TĀ 15.116]]–117ab: the placing can be done with the mātṛkā, the mālinī, or both, one after the other; in the order of emanation (a to kṣa) or of withdrawal (kṣa to a), one letter at a time or as a group.

## 1. Head and face: the sixteen vowels

[[Tantraloka 15.117|TĀ 15.117]]cd–118ab. Pairs go right then left; for teeth and lips Jayaratha says lower, then upper.

> [!matrika]+ Vowels
> | Letter | IAST | Place | Phase of consciousness (TĀ 3) |
> | :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- |
> | अ | a | forehead (*lalāṭa*) | *anuttara*, the unsurpassed ([[Tantraloka 03.066\|3.66]]–67) |
> | आ | ā | mouth (*vaktra*) | *ānanda*, bliss ([[Tantraloka 03.068\|3.68]]) |
> | इ | i | right eye (*dṛk*) | *icchā*, will ([[Tantraloka 03.072\|3.72]]) |
> | ई | ī | left eye | *īśitrī*, will stirred into mastery (3.72) |
> | उ | u | right ear (*karṇa*) | *unmeṣa*, the opening of knowing ([[Tantraloka 03.074\|3.74]]–75) |
> | ऊ | ū | left ear | *ūnatā*, the appearance of lack ([[Tantraloka 03.076\|3.76]]) |
> | ऋ ॠ | ṛ ṝ | right / left nostril (*nāsā*) | the four nectar letters, ṛ ṝ ḷ ḹ ([[Tantraloka 03.081\|3.81]]–92) |
> | ऌ ॡ | ḷ ḹ | right / left cheek (*gaṇḍa*) | (as above) |
> | ए | e | lower teeth (*rada*) | the triangle, *trikoṇa* ([[Tantraloka 03.094\|3.94]]–95) |
> | ऐ | ai | upper teeth | the six-spoked, *ṣaḍara* ([[Tantraloka 03.096\|3.96]]) |
> | ओ | o | lower lip (*oṣṭha*) | a and ā joined with *unmeṣa*: the power of action becoming manifest (3.96–97) |
> | औ | au | upper lip | the fourteenth, where will, knowledge and action are all manifest: the trident, *triśūla* (3.104–105) |
> | अं | aṃ | crown lock (*śikhā*) | *bindu*, the undivided point of light ([[Tantraloka 03.110\|3.110]]–111) |
> | अः | aḥ | tongue (*jihvā*) | *visarga*, emission (from [[Tantraloka 03.137\|3.137]]) |

## 2. Arms, legs and trunk: the twenty-five stops

[[Tantraloka 15.118|TĀ 15.118]]cd–119. For the legs the root text names the hip and "the thigh and so on"; Jayaratha reads "and so on" as foot, toes and toenails.

> [!matrika]+ Stops
> | Group | Places, in order |
> | :--- | :--- |
> | क ka ख kha ग ga घ gha ङ ṅa | right shoulder (*skandha*), arm (*bāhu*), hand (*kara*), fingers (*aṅguli*), fingernails (*nakha*) |
> | च ca छ cha ज ja झ jha ञ ña | the same on the left |
> | ट ṭa ठ ṭha ड ḍa ढ ḍha ण ṇa | right hip (*kaṭi*), thigh (*ūru*), foot (*pāda*), toes (*aṅguli*), toenails (*nakha*) |
> | त ta थ tha द da ध dha न na | the same on the left |
> | प pa फ pha ब ba भ bha म ma | right side, left side (*pārśva*), back (*pṛṣṭha*), belly (*jaṭhara*), heart (*hṛd*) |

## 3. The tissues: the last nine letters

[[Tantraloka 15.119|TĀ 15.119]]d–120ab: "then the nine", on skin, blood, flesh, sinews, bone, fat, semen "and the rest". The root text names seven; the verse Jayaratha quotes supplies the last two.

> [!matrika]+ Semivowels, sibilants, ha and kṣa
> | Letter | IAST | Tissue |
> | :---: | :---: | :--- |
> | य | ya | skin (*tvak*) |
> | र | ra | blood (*rakta*) |
> | ल | la | flesh (*māṃsa*) |
> | व | va | sinews (*sūtra*; Jayaratha: *snāyu*) |
> | श | śa | bone (*asthi*) |
> | ष | ṣa | fat (*vasā*) |
> | स | sa | semen (*śukra*) |
> | ह | ha | breath (*prāṇa*), from Jayaratha's quoted verse |
> | क्ष | kṣa | sheath (*kośa*), from Jayaratha's quoted verse |

These run through the whole body rather than sitting at one point.

## 4. A comparison: Sefer Yetzirah

Sefer Yetzirah also maps letters onto the body: its seven double letters govern the seven "gates" of the face (two eyes, two ears, two nostrils, the mouth). In the Tantrāloka list the first fourteen vowels likewise take the openings and features of the face. The parallel is one of method; neither text refers to the other. (The crash-session version of this table matched the twelve simple letters to "twelve consonants on the limbs"; the Tantrāloka puts twenty consonants on the limbs, so that row has been removed.)

> [!question]- What changed from the crash-session draft
> The draft put a on the crown and ā on the forehead (TĀ: forehead and mouth); aṃ on the tongue and palate and aḥ on the throat (TĀ: crown lock and tongue); e/ai on the lips and o/au on the teeth (TĀ: the other way round); ba on the sacrum, bha on the navel, ma on the abdomen (TĀ: back, belly, heart); ra, la, va on the shoulder blades and nape (TĀ: blood, flesh, sinews, as tissues); kṣa on the third eye and crown (Jayaratha's verse: *kośa*).
