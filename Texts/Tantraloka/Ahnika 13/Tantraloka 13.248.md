## Mūla

इच्छन्यियासुर्भवति तदा नीयेत सद्गुरुम् ।
न सर्वः प्रतिभाविष्टः शक्त्या नीयेत सद्गुरुम् ॥ ^mula

icchanyiyāsurbhavati tadā nīyeta sadgurum |
na sarvaḥ pratibhāviṣṭaḥ śaktyā nīyeta sadgurum || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 09 - Chapter Thirteen, part 5#^pg302|p. 280]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=302>))
>
> If one who has been penetrated by (the power of) intuition (pratibhā) desires to go to a true teacher, wishing (to have his insight) confirmed, he is then led to him. (with 13.247cd–248ab)
>
> (However,) not everybody who has been penetrated by (this) intuition (pratibhā) is led by (its) power to a true teacher. Thus it is said that it is necessary to say that he desires to go (to a teacher), certainly not otherwise (for it would have been sufficient to say,) ‘penetrated by Rudra’s power, he is led to a true teacher. (with 13.248cd–249)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* नचैतदविशेषेणैव भवेत् -- इत्याह --

> न सर्वः प्रतिभाविष्टः शक्त्या नीयेत सद्गुरुम् ॥
> इति ब्रूते यियासुत्वं वक्तव्यं नान्यथा ध्रुवम् ।

यियासुत्वमिति प्रागेवोक्तं ब्रूत इति संबन्धः । अन्यथेति सर्वस्यैव शक्तिकर्तृके सद्गुरुप्रापणे । न वक्तव्यमिति यियासुत्वम् । यदि नाम हि सर्वोऽपि प्रातिभज्ञानभागाचार्यः सद्गुरुं प्रति अविशेषेण शक्त्या नीयेत, तद्यियासुशब्दस्य प्रयोगं विना

"रुद्रशक्तिसमाविष्टः(साधकः) स शिवेच्छया ।

भुक्तिमुक्तिप्रसिद्ध्यर्थं नीयते सद्गुरुं प्रति ॥"

इत्येतावदभिधानीयम् ॥
