# Mālinīvijayottara, chapter 5: The Path of the Worlds

36 verses, 1–36. Text and translation by Mark Dyczkowski, from *Mālinī*, July–September 2023: [PDF pp. 65–81](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=65>).

Dyczkowski's introduction to the chapter: [[Malinivijayottara 05 - Introduction (Dyczkowski)]].

## Verses


### 1) Kālāgni and the Netherworlds

- [[Malinivijayottara 05.001|5.1]] athātaḥ saṁpravakṣyāmi bhuvanādhvānam īśvari
- [[Malinivijayottara 05.002|5.2]] avīciḥ kumbhīpākaś ca rauravaś ca tṛtīyakaḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 05.003|5.3]] pātālāni tataḥ sapta teṣām ādau mahātalam
- [[Malinivijayottara 05.004|5.4]] sutalaṁ nitalaṁ ceti vitalaṁ talam eva ca

### 2) The Earth

- [[Malinivijayottara 05.005|5.5]] tadūrdhvaṁ pṛthivī jñeyā saptadvīpārṇavānvitā

### 3) The group of seven worlds

- [[Malinivijayottara 05.006|5.6]] bhuvolokas tad ūrdhve ca svarlokas tasya copari
- [[Malinivijayottara 05.007|5.7]] caturdaśavidho yatra bhūtagrāmaḥ pravartate
- [[Malinivijayottara 05.008|5.8]] mṛgasaṁjñaś ca paśvākhyaḥ pañcamo 'nyaś ca mānuṣaḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 05.009|5.9]] saumyaś ca prājāpatyaś ca brāhmaś cātra caturdaśa

### 4) and 5) Viṣṇu's World and Rudra's World

- [[Malinivijayottara 05.010|5.10]] bhuvanaṁ vaiṣṇavaṁ tasmān madīyaṁ tadanantaram
- [[Malinivijayottara 05.011|5.11]] kālāgnipūrvakair ebhir bhuvanaiḥ pañcabhiḥ priye
- [[Malinivijayottara 05.012|5.12]] tadbahiḥ śatarudrāṇāṁ bhuvanāni pṛthak pṛthak
- [[Malinivijayottara 05.013|5.13]] anantaḥ prathamas teṣāṁ kapālīśas tathāparaḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 05.014|5.14]] śīghro nidhīśvaraś caiva sarvavidyādhipo 'paraḥ

### Five Groups of Eight

- [[Malinivijayottara 05.015|5.15]] ebhir daśaikasaṁkhyātaiḥ śuddhaiḥ śuddhaṁ śataṁ matam
- [[Malinivijayottara 05.016|5.16]] lakulī bhārabhūtiś ca diṇḍhyāṣāḍhī sapuṣkarau
- [[Malinivijayottara 05.017|5.17]] etat patyaṣṭakaṁ proktam ato guhyātiguhyakam
- [[Malinivijayottara 05.018|5.18]] āmrātikeśajalpeśaśrīśailāḥ saharīndavaḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 05.019|5.19]] kanakhalaṁ nākhalaṁ ca kurukṣetraṁ gayā tathā
- [[Malinivijayottara 05.020|5.20]] sthāṇusvarṇākṣakāv ādyau bhadragokarṇakau parau
- [[Malinivijayottara 05.021|5.21]] sthūlaḥ sthūleśvaraḥ śaṅkukarṇakālañjarāv api
- [[Malinivijayottara 05.022|5.22]] sthāṇvaṣṭakam iti proktam ahaṁkārāvadhi sthitam

### The Eight Types of Divine Beings in the Intellect

- [[Malinivijayottara 05.023|5.23]] paiśācaṁ rākṣasaṁ yākṣaṁ gāndharvaṁ caindram eva ca

### The Group of Eight Yoga (Rudras) is within Fundamental Nature (pradhāna)

- [[Malinivijayottara 05.024|5.24]] yogāṣṭakaṁ pradhāne tu tatrādāv akṛtaṁ bhavet

### The Eleven Rudras who reside in the individual soul

- [[Malinivijayottara 05.025|5.25]] kaumāram aumaṁ śraikaṇṭham iti yogāṣṭakaṁ tathā

### The Rudras in the Kañcukas

- [[Malinivijayottara 05.026|5.26]] pracaṇḍo mādhavājāś ca anantaikaśivāv atha
- [[Malinivijayottara 05.027|5.27]] kalātattve parijñeyau surapañcāntakau pare

### Lords of the Maṇḍalas reside in the Principle of Māyā

- [[Malinivijayottara 05.028|5.28]] mahātejaḥprabhṛtayo maṇḍaleśānasaṁjñakāḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 05.029|5.29]] ekapiṅgekṣaṇeśānabhuvaneśapuraḥsarāḥ

### The Five world-orders in the principle of Knowledge

- [[Malinivijayottara 05.030|5.30]] vidyātattve 'pi pañcāhur bhuvanāni manīṣiṇaḥ

### The Worlds in the Remaining Pure Principles

- [[Malinivijayottara 05.031|5.31]] ambikā ca aghorā ca vāmadevī ca kīrtyate
- [[Malinivijayottara 05.032|5.32]] raudrī jyeṣṭhā ca vāmā ca tathā śaktisadāśivau
- [[Malinivijayottara 05.033|5.33]] evaṁ tu sarvatattveṣu śatam aṣṭādaśottaram
- [[Malinivijayottara 05.034|5.34]] śuddhenānena śuddhyanti sarvāṇy api na śaṁśayaḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 05.035|5.35]] sakalāvadhi saṁśodhya śive yogaṁ prakalpayet
- [[Malinivijayottara 05.036|5.36]] ity eṣa kīrtito mārgo bhuvanākhyasya me mataḥ

## Closing

This is the fifth chapter of the Mālinīvijayottara Tantra concerning the Path of the Worlds.

*iti śrīmālinīvijayottare tantre bhuvanādhvādhikāraḥ pañcamaḥ* ||

The print ends the chapter with two pages of tables of the worlds: [PDF pp. 82–83](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=82>).
