*Volume 3, folio 230a* · Pinchas

*Passage Pinchas 75:462 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 3.229b#^pinchas-75-462|Pinchas 75:462]].*

**Pinchas 75:463** And this was FROM THE CHILDREN OF ISRAEL, for he saw that Wrath coming down upon the children of Israel. ^pinchas-75-463

**Pinchas 76:464** He saw the letter *mim* descending upon them, which is the sign of the Angel of Death, so he snatched it and drew it to him and at once the Angel of Death drew back. ^pinchas-76-464

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Pinchas 76:465–76:467.*

**Pinchas 76:468** It may be asked, How can it be said that Phineas turned back the wrath of God from Israel seeing that so many died of the plague? ^pinchas-76-468

**Pinchas 76:469** The fact is, however, that not one of the children of Israel died save from the tribe of Simeon, for when the mixed multitude came they associated with the women of the tribe of Simeon after they had become proselytes and bore sons from them; and of these some died on account of the golden calf, some by plague, and some now. ^pinchas-76-469

**Pinchas 76:470** And because Israel and all the holy seed kept themselves clear, they were now numbered to show that not one of them was missing. ^pinchas-76-470
