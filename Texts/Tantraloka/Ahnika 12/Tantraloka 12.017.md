## Mūla

तदा तथा तेन तत्र तत्तद्भोग्यं विधिश्च सः ।
लौकिकालौकिकं सर्वं तेनात्र विनियोजयेत् ॥ ^mula

tadā tathā tena tatra tattadbhogyaṃ vidhiśca saḥ |
laukikālaukikaṃ sarvaṃ tenātra viniyojayet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 04 - Chapter Twelve#^pg129|p. 109]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=129>))
>
> Whenever, however, wherever, and by whatever (means) one’s own consciousness becomes propitious (prasīdati), then should, in that way, there and by that (means) be consumed (bhogya) (as the sacrificial substance), for this is the procedure (enjoined by Kaula scripture) (vidhi). (with 12.16cd–17ab)
>
> Thus, (a true Kaula) must apply all (kinds of sacrificial substances), both normally accepted (laukika) (by the Vedic norms) and those that are not (alaukika), if (he possesses) an unshaking (freedom from doubt) (niṣkampatva). (However,) if he trembles (with doubt) (sakampa), (his) fear (kampa) should be forcefully eliminated (with 12.17cd–18ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* एतच्च सर्वमेवात्र विहितम् -- इत्याह --

> लौकिकालौकिकं सर्वं तेनात्र विनियोजयेत् ॥
> निष्कम्पत्वे सकम्पस्तु कम्पं निर्ह्रासयेद् बलात् ।

निष्कम्पत्व इति निर्विकल्पकत्वे -- इत्यर्थः । कम्पं शङ्कादिरूपम् । बलान्निर्ह्रासयेत् तत्रैव यत्नपरो भवेत् -- इत्यर्थः ॥ १७ ॥
