# Psalm 73

← [[BCP Psalm 072]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 074]] →

*Quam bonus Israel!* · Book Three · Fourteenth Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 073]]

**73:1** Truly, God is good to Israel, \*  
to those who are pure in heart. ^73-1

**73:2** But as for me, my feet had nearly slipped; \*  
I had almost tripped and fallen; ^73-2

**73:3** Because I envied the proud \*  
and saw the prosperity of the wicked: ^73-3

**73:4** For they suffer no pain, \*  
and their bodies are sleek and sound; ^73-4

**73:5** In the misfortunes of others they have no share; \*  
they are not afflicted as others are; ^73-5

**73:6** Therefore they wear their pride like a necklace \*  
and wrap their violence about them like a cloak. ^73-6

**73:7** Their iniquity comes from gross minds, \*  
and their hearts overflow with wicked thoughts. ^73-7

**73:8** They scoff and speak maliciously; \*  
out of their haughtiness they plan oppression. ^73-8

**73:9** They set their mouths against the heavens, \*  
and their evil speech runs through the world. ^73-9

**73:10** And so the people turn to them \*  
and find in them no fault. ^73-10

**73:11** They say, "How should God know? \*  
is there knowledge in the Most High?" ^73-11

[p. 688]

**73:12** So then, these are the wicked; \*  
always at ease, they increase their wealth. ^73-12

**73:13** In vain have I kept my heart clean, \*  
and washed my hands in innocence. ^73-13

**73:14** I have been afflicted all day long, \*  
and punished every morning. ^73-14

**73:15** Had I gone on speaking this way, \*  
I should have betrayed the generation of your children. ^73-15

**73:16** When I tried to understand these things, \*  
it was too hard for me; ^73-16

**73:17** Until I entered the sanctuary of God \*  
and discerned the end of the wicked. ^73-17

**73:18** Surely, you set them in slippery places; \*  
you cast them down in ruin. ^73-18

**73:19** Oh, how suddenly do they come to destruction, \*  
come to an end, and perish from terror! ^73-19

**73:20** Like a dream when one awakens, O Lord, \*  
when you arise you will make their image vanish. ^73-20

**73:21** When my mind became embittered, \*  
I was sorely wounded in my heart. ^73-21

**73:22** I was stupid and had no understanding; \*  
I was like a brute beast in your presence. ^73-22

**73:23** Yet I am always with you; \*  
you hold me by my right hand. ^73-23

**73:24** You will guide me by your counsel, \*  
and afterwards receive me with glory. ^73-24

**73:25** Whom have I in heaven but you? \*  
and having you I desire nothing upon earth. ^73-25

[p. 689]

**73:26** Though my flesh and my heart should waste away, \*  
God is the strength of my heart and my portion for ever. ^73-26

**73:27** Truly, those who forsake you will perish; \*  
you destroy all who are unfaithful. ^73-27

**73:28** But it is good for me to be near God; \*  
I have made the Lord GOD my refuge. ^73-28

**73:29** I will speak of all your works \*  
in the gates of the city of Zion. ^73-29
