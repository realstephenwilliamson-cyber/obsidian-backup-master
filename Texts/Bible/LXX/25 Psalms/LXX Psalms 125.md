# LXX Psalms 125

← [[LXX Psalms 124]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 126]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 126]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 126]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**125:1** A Song of Degrees. When the Lord turned the captivity of Sion, we became as comforted ones.  ^125-1

**125:2** Then was our mouth filled with joy, and our tongue with exultation: then would they say among the Gentiles,  ^125-2

**125:3** The Lord has done great things among them. The Lord has done great things for us, we became joyful.  ^125-3

**125:4** Turn, O Lord, our captivity, as the streams in the south.  ^125-4

**125:5** They that sow in tears shall reap in joy.  ^125-5

**125:6** They went on and wept as they cast their seeds; but they shall surely come with exultation, bringing their sheaves *with them*.  ^125-6
