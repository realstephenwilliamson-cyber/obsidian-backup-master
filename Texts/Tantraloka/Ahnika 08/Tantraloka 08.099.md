## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 05 - Chapter Eight, part 3#^pg161|p. 143]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=161>))
>
> At a distance of twenty thousand leagues, to the south of the (salty) ocean, there is a mountain (called) Vidyutvat (Lightning Bearer). It (stands) three thousand leagues in height and breadth. (The people who live) here eat fruit. They are filthy, have long hair and beards. Naked, (their way of life) is like cattle; they eat grass and live for eighty years. There (on that mountain), the doors (to the netherworlds) are always without locks, so that the Accomplishment of the Crevice (bilasiddhi), (by virtue of which one finds subterranean treasure, may be easily achieved). The teacher (Bṛhaspati) has declared this in (his) venerable Rauravavārtike. (with 8.99–101)
