## Mūla

उक्तं च तस्य परतः स्थानमनन्ताधिपस्य देवस्य ।
स्थितिविलयसर्गकर्तुर्गुहाभगद्वारपालस्य ॥ ^mula

uktaṃ ca tasya parataḥ sthānamanantādhipasya devasya |
sthitivilayasargakarturguhābhagadvārapālasya || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 09 - Chapter Eight, part 7#^pg325|p. 307]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=325>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 8.303cd–304ab.
>
> It is said that beyond that (plane of Rudras) is the abode of Lord Ananta, who is the agent of persistence, destruction and creation. He is the guardian of the door of the Womb (bhaga) of the Cave (of Māyā).
