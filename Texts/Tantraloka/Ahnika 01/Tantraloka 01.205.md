## Mūla

तथोपचारस्यात्रैतन्निमित्तं सप्रयोजनम् ।
तन्मुखा स्फुटता धर्मिण्याशु तन्मयतास्थितिः ॥ ^mula

tathopacārasyātraitannimittaṃ saprayojanam |
tanmukhā sphuṭatā dharmiṇyāśu tanmayatāsthitiḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. I
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 01 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v01 11 - Chapter One, part 10#^pg397|p. 383]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 01.pdf#page=397>))
>
> Such (as explained in the following verses) is the reason here, along with purpose of the metaphor. (with 1.205ab)
>
> (This metaphor serves to indicate that) the clearly apparent state (of Śakti) within the bearer of (these) attributes, which is the rapid (realisation of) the state of oneness with (Him), has that as its entrance (tanmukhā). These same (divine) attributes, which are called (Śiva’s) powers, are venerated as each (of their many) forms, which are appropriate for the achievement of a state of oneness (tanmayībhāva) (with Śiva, who possesses them). (with 1.205cd–206)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* ननु उपचारे मुख्यार्थबाधादिना त्रितयेन अवश्यभाव्यं तच्चात्र किमस्ति न वा ? इत्याशङ्क्याह—

> तथोपचारस्यात्रैतन्निमित्तं सप्रयोजनम् ।

निमित्तम् इति कारणम्, तद्वशादेव हि उपचारो भवेत् -- इति भावः । तत्र शक्तेः शक्तिमद्रूपत्वाभिधाने बाधितस्तावत् मुख्योऽर्थः, संबन्धश्च तयोरुपायोपेयभावः ॥
