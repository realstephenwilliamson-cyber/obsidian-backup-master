## Mūla

तदर्थमेतदुदितं न तु मोक्षोपयोग्यदः ।
इत्यूचे परमेशः श्रीकुलगह्वरशासने ॥ ^mula

tadarthametaduditaṃ na tu mokṣopayogyadaḥ |
ityūce parameśaḥ śrīkulagahvaraśāsane || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 15 - Chapter Twenty-Four#^pg369|p. 349]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=369>))
>
> (The teacher) should 1) (first) block (rodhayet) the essence (tattva) (of the deceased person’s consciousness) by means of the Point (of the exhaled breath), and 2) pierce it with the Seed of Power (S). 3) He should (then) strike (ghaṭṭayet) (and catch hold of it) in the locus of Sound (of the inhaled breath), 4) beat it (and expand it out) with the Trident (AU). 5) (After that) he should beat (tāḍayeta) all the energies (kalā) repeatedly (one by one), with the emission (Ḥ) present within suṣumnā. Thus, that fettered soul (present in the corpse) trembles (kampate) or else raises the left hand (as a sign that the procedure has been effective). (The teacher) should then conjoin (the soul) to the supreme (reality). (The corpse trembles etc. because) those who are deluded would not believe that (the soul of the deceased) has been liberated without an assuring sign (pratyaya) (that it has taken place). It is (only) for that reason that this (sign) is mentioned (here), it does not aid (the achievement of) liberation. The Supreme Lord has said this in the venerable Kulagahvaraśāsana. (with 24.13–16)

## Viveka

> बिन्दुना रोधयेत्तत्त्वं शक्तिबीजेन वेधयेत् ।
> घट्टयेन्नाददेशे तु त्रिशूलेन तु ताडयेत् ॥
> सुषुम्नान्तर्गतेनैव विसर्गेण पुनः पुनः ।
> ताडयेत कलाः सर्वाः कम्पतेऽसौ ततः पशुः ॥
> उत्क्षिपेद्वामहस्तं वा ततस्तं योजयेत्परे ।
> प्रत्ययेन विना मोक्षो ह्यश्रद्धेयो विमोहितैः ॥
> तदर्थमेतदुदितं न तु मोक्षोपयोग्यदः ।
> इत्यूचे परमेशः श्रीकुलगह्वरशासने ॥
