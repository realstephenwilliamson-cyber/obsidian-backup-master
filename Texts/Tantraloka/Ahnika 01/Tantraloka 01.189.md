## Mūla

अव्यापकेभ्यस्तेनेदं भेदेन गणितं किल ।
अशुद्धिशुद्ध्यमानत्वशुद्धितस्तु मिथोऽपि तत् ॥ ^mula

avyāpakebhyastenedaṃ bhedena gaṇitaṃ kila |
aśuddhiśuddhyamānatvaśuddhitastu mitho'pi tat || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. I
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 01 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v01 10 - Chapter One, part 9#^pg381|p. 367]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 01.pdf#page=381>))
>
> The three called the Person, Knowledge and Power pervade the entire (series of thirty-six metaphysical principles). Thus, they have been reckoned apart from the other (principles), which are not pervasive. (with 1.188cd–189ab)
>
> They are also mutually (related and yet distinguished) as (the Person, which is the principle that spans) the impure (principles), (Knowledge, those) in the process of being purified (and so are pure-cum-impure), and (Power, the one that) is pure. (with 1.189cd)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* न केवलमेतत् तत्त्वान्तरेभ्यो भिद्यते, यावदन्योन्यमपि -- इत्याह --

> अशुद्धिशुद्ध्यमानत्वशुद्धितस्तु मिथोऽपि तत् ॥

पुमान् अशुद्धो -- भेदमयत्वात्, विद्या शुद्ध्यमाना भेदाभेदमयत्वात्, शुद्धा शक्तिः—अभेदमयत्वात् ॥ १८९ ॥
