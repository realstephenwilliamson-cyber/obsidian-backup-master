# Job 26 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Job 25|← Job 25]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Job 27|Job 27 →]]

**26:1** Then Job answered, and said: ^26-1

**26:2** Whose helper art thou? is it of him that is weak? and dost thou hold up the arm of him that has no strength? ^26-2

**26:3** To whom hast thou given counsel? perhaps to him that hath no wisdom, and thou hast shewn thy very great prudence. ^26-3

**26:4** Whom hast thou desired to teach? was it not him that made life? ^26-4

**26:5** Behold the giants groan under the waters, and they that dwell with them. ^26-5

**26:6** Hell is naked before him, and there is no covering for destruction. ^26-6

**26:7** He stretched out the north over the empty space, and hangeth the earth upon nothing. ^26-7

**26:8** He bindeth up the waters in his clouds, so that they break not out and fall down together. ^26-8

**26:9** He withholdeth the face of his throne, and spreadeth his cloud over it. ^26-9

**26:10** He hath set bounds about the waters, till light and darkness come to an end. ^26-10

**26:11** The pillars of heaven tremble, and dread at his beck. ^26-11

**26:12** By his power the seas are suddenly gathered together, and his wisdom has struck the proud one. ^26-12

**26:13** His spirit hath adorned the heavens, and his obstetric hand brought forth the winding serpent. ^26-13

**26:14** Lo, these things are said in part of his ways: and seeing we have heard scarce a little drop of his word, who shall be able to behold the thunder of his greatness? ^26-14
