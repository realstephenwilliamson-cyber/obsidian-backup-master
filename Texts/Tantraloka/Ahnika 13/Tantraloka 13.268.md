## Mūla

सा भोगमोक्षस्वातन्त्र्यमहालक्ष्मीरिहाक्षया ।
विष्ण्वादिरूपता देवे या काचित्सा निजात्मना ॥ ^mula

sā bhogamokṣasvātantryamahālakṣmīrihākṣayā |
viṣṇvādirūpatā deve yā kācitsā nijātmanā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 09 - Chapter Thirteen, part 5#^pg312|p. 290]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=312>))
>
> The perception of the unlimited nature of one’s own consciousness is said here to be accomplishment (siddhi). It is Mahālakṣmī (the Great Goddess of Prosperity) here (in this world) who, imperishable, is the freedom from which worldly benefits and liberation proceed. (with 13.267cd–268ab)
>
> Whatever form God (assumes) by Himself within (Himself), such as Viṣṇu, is subject to association with duality (bheda), and so is collocated on the plane of Māyā. Thus, even though (there is some) descent of the power (of grace), because (deity) is associated with those (limited) forms, they bestow (only) that much fruit (as befits their status), and certainly not ultimately Śiva’s state (śivatā). (with 13.268cd–270ab)
