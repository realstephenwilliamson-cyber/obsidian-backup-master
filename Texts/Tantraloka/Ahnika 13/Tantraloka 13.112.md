## Mūla

प्रति भिन्नेन भावेन स्पृहातो लोलिका मलः ।
विशुद्धस्वप्रकाशात्मशिवरूपतया विना ॥ ^mula

prati bhinnena bhāvena spṛhāto lolikā malaḥ |
viśuddhasvaprakāśātmaśivarūpatayā vinā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 06 - Chapter Thirteen, part 2#^pg211|p. 191]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=211>))
>
> The obscuration of a reality that is complete (and perfect) (pūrṇa) (essentially consists of) the absence (of the realization of its) plenitude, and that (sense of absence is) the intense hope (spṁā) of becoming full (and satisfied) by means of dualistic (bhinna) (conditioned) existence (bhāva). Thus, Impurity is (essentially the frustrating) craving (lolikā) (for worldly fulfilment).' (with 13.111cd–112ab)
>
> Without Śiva, who is pure, self-luminous light, nothing would be possible (and so nothing could exist). Thus, the cause here (of Impurity and the rest can only be) Maheśvara. (with 13.112cd–113ab)
