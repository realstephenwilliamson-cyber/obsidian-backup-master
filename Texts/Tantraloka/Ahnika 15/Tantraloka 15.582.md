## Mūla

तेनोक्तमनुतिष्ठेच्च निर्विकल्पं प्रयत्नतः ।
यतः शास्त्रादिसंबोधतन्मयीकृतमानसः ॥ ^mula

tenoktamanutiṣṭhecca nirvikalpaṃ prayatnataḥ |
yataḥ śāstrādisaṃbodhatanmayīkṛtamānasaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 10 - Chapter Fifteen, part 9#^pg356|p. 338]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=356>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 15.584cd–585ab.
>
> If (someone) transgresses the rules, he should ask the teacher (what should be done). Or, if (the teacher) is absent, (he should ask) one of his (same) kind (varga) who is a member of his own spiritual family, or if he too is not present, another (teacher). He should take care to follow what he says without thinking (about it) (nirvikalpa). This is because by virtue of (his) insight (sarzbodha) (into the meaning of) scripture etc., the mind of the teacher has been made one with it and he is Śiva himself, nor can a false word come from him. The purpose of bowing (prahvībhāva) before Śiva is to purify oneself. In the case of the teacher, it is to please (him), and so it serves both purposes. (It is the teacher’s) satisfaction that gives (good) fruits to the disciple. (with 15.581–584ab)
