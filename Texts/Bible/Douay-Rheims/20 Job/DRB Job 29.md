# Job 29 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Job 28|← Job 28]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Job 30|Job 30 →]]

**29:1** Job also added, taking up his parable, and said: ^29-1

**29:2** Who will grant me, that I might be according to the months past, according to the days in which God kept me? ^29-2

**29:3** When his lamp shined over my head, and I walked by his light in darkness? ^29-3

**29:4** As I was in the days of my youth, when God was secretly in my tabernacle? ^29-4

**29:5** When the Almighty was with me: and my servants round about me? ^29-5

**29:6** When I washed my feet with butter, and the rock poured me out rivers of oil? ^29-6

**29:7** When I went out to the gate of the city, and in the street they prepared me a chair? ^29-7

**29:8** The young men saw me, and hid themselves: and the old men rose up and stood. ^29-8

**29:9** The princes ceased to speak, and laid the finger on their mouth. ^29-9

**29:10** The rulers held their peace, and their tongue cleaved to their throat. ^29-10

**29:11** The ear that heard me blessed me, and the eye that saw me gave witness to me: ^29-11

**29:12** Because I had delivered the poor man that cried out; and the fatherless that had no helper. ^29-12

**29:13** The blessing of him that was ready to perish came upon me, and I comforted the heart of the widow. ^29-13

**29:14** I was clad with justice: and I clothed myself with my judgment, as with a robe and a diadem. ^29-14

**29:15** I was an eye to the blind, and a foot to the lame. ^29-15

**29:16** I was the father of the poor: and the cause which I knew not, I searched out most diligently. ^29-16

**29:17** I broke the jaws of the wicked man, and out of his teeth I took away the prey. ^29-17

**29:18** And I said: I shall die in my nest, and as a palm tree shall multiply my days. ^29-18

**29:19** My root is opened beside the waters, and dew shall continue in my harvest. ^29-19

**29:20** My glory shall always be renewed, and my bow in my hand shall be repaired. ^29-20

**29:21** They that heard me, waited for my sentence, and being attentive held their peace at my counsel. ^29-21

**29:22** To my words they durst add nothing, and my speech dropped upon them. ^29-22

**29:23** They waited for me as for rain, and they opened their mouth as for a latter shower. ^29-23

**29:24** If at any time I laughed on them, they believed not, and the light of my countenance fell not on earth. ^29-24

**29:25** If I had a mind to go to them, I sat first, and when I sat as a king, with his army standing about him, yet I was a comforter of them that mourned. ^29-25
