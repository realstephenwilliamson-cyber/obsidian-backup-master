# Uḍḍāmareśvaratantra — Chapter 5

[[Uddamaresvaratantra 04|← Chapter 4]] · [[Uddamaresvaratantra|← index]] · [[Uddamaresvaratantra 06|Chapter 6 →]]

śṛṇu putra pravakṣyāmi yathā trailokyamohanam

^s1

parapuruṣā vaśaṃ yānti yathāvat kathayāmi te

^s2

elā kuṣṭhaṃ ca lodhraṃ ca vacā ca raktacandanam

^s3

śmaśānabhasmasaṃyuktaṃ prayogaṃ māraṇāntakam

^s4

māraṇaṃ saṃkocakaraṇam

^s5

kulatthaṃ bilvapattraṃ ca rocanā ca manaḥśilā

^s6

etāni samabhāgāni sthāpayet tāmrabhājane

^s7

saptarātre sthite pātre tailam ebhiḥ paced budhaḥ

^s8

tailena bhagam ālipya bhartāram upagacchati

^s9

samprāpya maithunaṃ bhartā dāso bhavati nānyathā

^s10

nāgaraiḥ madhusaṃyuktair guṭikāṃ kārayed budhaḥ

^s11

aśvamūtreṇa saṃyujya puruṣāṇāṃ vaśaṃkaram

^s12

kuṅkumaṃ śatapuṣpaṃ ca priyaṅgu rocanaṃ tataḥ

^s13

ajākṣīreṇa dātavyaṃ yā bhāryā durbhagā bhavet

^s14

tataḥ sā subhagā nityaṃ patidāsatvam āpnuyāt

^s15

mātuluṅgasya bījāni paṅkajasya phalāni ca

^s16

samudrajaṃ raktacūrṇaṃ madhunā saha lepayet

^s17

tailayojitamātreṇa patir dāso bhaved dhruvam

^s18

adhunā sampravakṣyāmi yogānāṃ sāram uttamam

^s19

yena vijñātamātreṇa narī bhavati kiṃkarī

^s20

uśīraṃ candanaṃ caiva madhunā saha saṃyutam

^s21

gajahastaprayogo 'yaṃ sarvanarīprayojakaḥ

^s22

kevalaṃ śaśinā yuktaṃ kuṅkumaṃ lepayed bhage

^s23

nijavaj jāyate sāṅgaṃ jāyate yonisaṃcayam

^s24

lavaṃgaṃ saindhavaṃ kṣaudraṃ pippalī maricāni ca

^s25

narasya lepayed gātraṃ sa bhaved gajahastavat

^s26

varaṭāveśmasambānaṃ śītatoyena peṣayet

^s27

tenaiva liptamātreṇa bhage randhro na jāyate

^s28

tatra prakṣālitenātha prasavatvaṃ bhaviṣyati

^s29

yaḥ svaretaḥ samādāya ratyante savyapāṇinā

^s30

vāmapādaṃ striyo limpet sā tasya vaśagā bhavet

^s31

yā bhogaśeṣe kāntasya liṅgaṃ vāmāṅghriṇā spṛśet

^s32

yāvad āyur bhaved dāsaḥ sa tasyā nātra saṃśayaḥ

^s33

kapotaviṣṭhā sindhūtthamadhukaiḥ samabhāgikaiḥ

^s34

liptvā liṅgaṃ bhajed yāṃ tu sā vaśyā syād varāṅganā

^s35

ghanasāraṃ satallavyaṃ meḍhraṃ ca madhunā saha

^s36

piṣṭvā liptvā rajo yāṃ ca bhajet sā vaśyagā bhavet

^s37

rocanā kanakaṃ śambhubījaṃ karpūracandanam

^s38

ebhir viliptaliṅgo yāṃ bhajate sā vaśā bhavet

^s39
