## Mūla

उत्पत्त्यभावतस्तेन नित्यं न च विनश्यति ।
तत एवैकतायां चान्यात्मसाधारणत्वतः ॥ ^mula

utpattyabhāvatastena nityaṃ na ca vinaśyati |
tata evaikatāyāṃ cānyātmasādhāraṇatvataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 05 - Chapter Thirteen, part 1#^pg171|p. 151]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=171>))
>
> Again, from what could that (obscuring covering) be born? If it (were to be born) from Māyā, how could (Māyā) generate it in some place (for fettered) souls and not for those who are liberated? The previous objection would apply (here also). Again, if (you try and avoid this problem by saying that) there is no (instrumental) cause (nimitta) (of this obscuring covering), thus (the obscuration is just an illusion and so) it does not (really) come into being, then it would be permanent and so is not destroyed. (with 13.45cd–47ab)
>
> Thus, (for the same reason as before, namely) because it is one and the same, both for oneself and other souls (if it ceased for one soul, all of them should be liberated). (with 13.47cd)

> तत एवैकतायां चान्यात्मसाधारणत्वतः ॥

तत एव भेदे प्रमाणाभावलक्षणात् प्रागुक्ताद्धेतोरेकतायां सत्यां स्वपरसाधारणत्वाच्चैतन्न विनश्यतीति प्राच्येन सम्बन्धः । अस्य ह्येकत्वेन सर्वसाधारण्यादेकत्र नाशे सर्वत्रैवासौ स्यादिति युगपदेव सर्वे मुच्येरन् -- इत्यभिप्रायः ॥
