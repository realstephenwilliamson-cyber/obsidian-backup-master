# Nahum 2 (NRSVue)

## The destruction of the wicked city

**2:1** A scatterer has come up against you. Guard the ramparts; watch the road; gird your loins; collect all your strength. ^2-1

**2:2** (For the LORD is restoring the majesty of Jacob, as well as the majesty of Israel, though ravagers have ravaged them and ruined their branches.) ^2-2

**2:3** The shields of his warriors are red; his soldiers are clothed in crimson. The metal on the chariots flashes on the day when he musters them; the chargers^[Cn Compare Gk Syr: Heb *cypresses*] prance. ^2-3

**2:4** The chariots race madly through the streets; they rush to and fro through the squares; their appearance is like torches; they dart like lightning. ^2-4

**2:5** He calls his officers; they stumble as they come forward; they hasten to the wall, and the screen^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] is set up. ^2-5

**2:6** The river gates are opened; the palace trembles. ^2-6

**2:7** It is decreed^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] that the city^[Heb *it*] be exiled, its slave women led away, moaning like doves and beating their breasts. ^2-7

**2:8** Nineveh is like a pool whose waters^[Cn Compare Gk: Heb *a pool, from the days that she has become, and they*] run away. “Halt! Halt!”—but no one turns back. ^2-8

**2:9** “Plunder the silver; plunder the gold! There is no end of treasure! An abundance of every precious thing!” ^2-9

**2:10** Devastation, desolation, and destruction! Hearts faint and knees tremble; all loins quake; all faces grow pale! ^2-10

**2:11** What became of the lions’ den, the cave of the young lions, where the lion goes, and the lion’s cubs, with no one to disturb them? ^2-11

**2:12** The lion has torn enough for his whelps and strangled prey for his lionesses; he has filled his caves with prey and his dens with torn flesh. ^2-12

**2:13** See, I am against you, says the LORD of hosts, and I will burn your^[Heb *her*] chariots in smoke, and the sword shall devour your young lions; I will cut off your prey from the earth, and the voice of your messengers shall be heard no more. ^2-13
