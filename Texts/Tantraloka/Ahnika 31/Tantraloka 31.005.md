## Mūla

अङ्कयेदपरादङ्कात् पूर्वादपि तथैव ते ।
मत्स्यमध्ये क्षिपेत्सूत्रमायतं दक्षिणोत्तरे ॥ ^mula

aṅkayedaparādaṅkāt pūrvādapi tathaiva te |
matsyamadhye kṣipetsūtramāyataṃ dakṣiṇottare || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI

> Or else, if (by another method), the north and south are marked with a thread (the length of which) is equal (to the distance starting) from the mark in the west and from (that in) the east (at these two extremities), then in the middle (of the two points of intersection) of the oval (with the base line,) one should, in the same way (as before), cast the thread (so that it) extends through the south and the north. (with 31.4cd–5)
