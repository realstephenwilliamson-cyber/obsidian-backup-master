# Psalm 90 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 089|← Psalm 89]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 091|Psalm 91 →]]

## God’s eternity and human frailty

*A Prayer of Moses, the man of God.*

**90:1** Lord, you have been our dwelling place^[Or *our refuge*] in all generations. ^90-1

**90:2** Before the mountains were brought forth or ever you had formed the earth and the world, from everlasting to everlasting you are God. ^90-2

**90:3** You turn us^[Heb *humankind*] back to dust and say, “Turn back, you mortals.” ^90-3

**90:4** For a thousand years in your sight are like yesterday when it is past or like a watch in the night. ^90-4

**90:5** You sweep them away; they are like a dream, like grass that is renewed in the morning; ^90-5

**90:6** in the morning it flourishes and is renewed; in the evening it fades and withers. ^90-6

**90:7** For we are consumed by your anger; by your wrath we are overwhelmed. ^90-7

**90:8** You have set our iniquities before you, our secret sins in the light of your countenance. ^90-8

**90:9** For all our days pass away under your wrath; our years come to an end^[Syr: Heb *we bring our years to an end*] like a sigh. ^90-9

**90:10** The days of our life are seventy years or perhaps eighty, if we are strong; even then their span^[Cn Compare Gk Syr Jerome Tg: Heb *pride*] is only toil and trouble; they are soon gone, and we fly away. ^90-10

**90:11** Who considers the power of your anger? Your wrath is as great as the fear that is due you. ^90-11

**90:12** So teach us to count our days that we may gain a wise heart. ^90-12

**90:13** Turn, O LORD! How long? Have compassion on your servants! ^90-13

**90:14** Satisfy us in the morning with your steadfast love, so that we may rejoice and be glad all our days. ^90-14

**90:15** Make us glad as many days as you have afflicted us and as many years as we have seen evil. ^90-15

**90:16** Let your work be manifest to your servants and your glorious power to their children. ^90-16

**90:17** Let the favor of the Lord our God be upon us and prosper for us the work of our hands—O prosper the work of our hands! ^90-17
