# Job 32 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Job 31|← Job 31]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Job 33|Job 33 →]]

## Elihu rebukes Job’s friends

**32:1** So these three men ceased to answer Job, because he was righteous in his own eyes. ^32-1

**32:2** Then Elihu son of Barachel the Buzite, of the family of Ram, became angry. He was angry at Job because he justified himself rather than God; ^32-2

**32:3** he was angry also at Job’s three friends because they had found no answer, though they had declared Job to be in the wrong.^[Or *answer, and had put God in the wrong*] ^32-3

**32:4** Now Elihu had waited to speak to Job because they were older than he. ^32-4

**32:5** But when Elihu saw that there was no answer in the mouths of these three men, he became angry. ^32-5

**32:6** Elihu son of Barachel the Buzite answered: “I am young in years, and you are aged; therefore I was timid and afraid to declare my opinion to you. ^32-6

**32:7** I said, ‘Let days speak and many years teach wisdom.’ ^32-7

**32:8** But truly it is the spirit in a mortal, the breath of the Almighty^[Traditional rendering of Heb *Shaddai*] that makes for understanding. ^32-8

**32:9** It is not the old^[Gk Syr Vg: Heb *many*] who are wise nor the aged who understand what is right. ^32-9

**32:10** Therefore I say, ‘Listen to me; let me also declare my opinion.’ ^32-10

**32:11** “See, I waited for your words; I listened for your wise sayings while you searched out what to say. ^32-11

**32:12** I gave you my attention, but there was, in fact, no one who confuted Job, no one among you who answered his words. ^32-12

**32:13** Yet do not say, ‘We have found wisdom; God may vanquish him, not a human.’ ^32-13

**32:14** He has not directed his words against me, and I will not answer him with your speeches. ^32-14

**32:15** “They are dismayed; they answer no more; they have not a word to say. ^32-15

**32:16** And am I to wait because they do not speak, because they stand there and answer no more? ^32-16

**32:17** I also will give my answer; I also will declare my opinion. ^32-17

**32:18** For I am full of words; the spirit within me constrains me. ^32-18

**32:19** My belly is indeed like wine that has no vent; like new wineskins, it is ready to burst. ^32-19

**32:20** I must speak, so that I may find relief; I must open my lips and answer. ^32-20

**32:21** I will not show partiality to any person or use flattery toward anyone. ^32-21

**32:22** For I do not know how to flatter—or my Maker would soon put an end to me! ^32-22
