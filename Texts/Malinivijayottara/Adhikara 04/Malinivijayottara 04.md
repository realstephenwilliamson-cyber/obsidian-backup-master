# Mālinīvijayottara, chapter 4: Śaiva Yoga and the Distribution of the Tattvas

41 verses, 1–41. Text and translation by Mark Dyczkowski, from *Mālinī*, April–June 2023: [PDF pp. 54–64](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=54>).

Dyczkowski's introduction to the chapter: [[Malinivijayottara 04 - Introduction (Dyczkowski)]].

## Verses

- [[Malinivijayottara 04.001|4.1]] athaitad upasaṁśrutya munayo muditekṣaṇāḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 04.002|4.2]] yogamārgavidhiṁ devyā pṛṣṭena parameṣṭhinā
- [[Malinivijayottara 04.003|4.3]] evam uktaḥ sa taiḥ samyak kārtikeyo mahāmatiḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 04.004|4.4]] yogam ekatvam icchanti vastuno 'nyena vastunā
- [[Malinivijayottara 04.005|4.5]] dvirūpam api taj jñānaṁ vinā jñātuṁ na śakyate
- [[Malinivijayottara 04.006|4.6]] sabījayogasaṁsiddhyai mantralakṣaṇam apy alam
- [[Malinivijayottara 04.007|4.7]] kriyājñanavibhedena sā ca dvedhā nigadyate
- [[Malinivijayottara 04.008|4.8]] na ca yogādhikāritvam ekam evānayā bhavet

### The Tattvas in the Mālinī Sequence

- [[Malinivijayottara 04.009|4.9]] śrutvā caitat pater vākyaṁ romāṣcitaśarīriṇī
- [[Malinivijayottara 04.010|4.10]] abhinnamālinīkāye tattvāni bhuvanāni ca
- [[Malinivijayottara 04.011|4.11]] bhinnayonis tu yā deva tvayoktā mālinī mama
- [[Malinivijayottara 04.012|4.12]] evam ukto mahādevyā bhairavo bhūribhogadaḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 04.013|4.13]] surāsuraśiromaulīmālālālitaśāsanaḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 04.014|4.14]] yā mayā kathitā devi bhinnayonis tu mālinī
- [[Malinivijayottara 04.015|4.15]] phe dharātattvamuddiṣṭaṁ dāṁdijhānte 'nupūrvaśaḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 04.016|4.16]] ṭhādau ca saptake sapta puruṣādīni pūrvavat
- [[Malinivijayottara 04.017|4.17]] śivatattve gakārādināntān ṣoḍaśa lakṣayet

### The Tattvas and Worlds in the Three Vidyās

- [[Malinivijayottara 04.018|4.18]] pūrvavad veditavyāni tatsaṁkhyārṇavibhedataḥ

### Parāparā

- [[Malinivijayottara 04.019|4.19]] niṣkale padam ekārṇaṁ tryarṇaikārṇam iti  dvayam
- [[Malinivijayottara 04.020|4.20]] caturekākṣare dve ca māyāditritaye mate
- [[Malinivijayottara 04.021|4.21]] rañjake dvyarṇam uddiṣṭaṁ pradhāne tryarṇam iṣyate
- [[Malinivijayottara 04.022|4.22]] tataḥ pañcāṣṭakavyāptyā dvyekadvidvyakṣarāṇi tu
- [[Malinivijayottara 04.023|4.23]] ekaika  sārdhavarṇāni trīṇi tattve tu pārthive

### Aparā

- [[Malinivijayottara 04.024|4.24]] sārdhenāṇḍadvayaṁ vyāptam ekaikena pṛthag dvayam
- [[Malinivijayottara 04.025|4.25]] sārṇenāṇḍatrayaṁ vyāptaṁ triśūlena caturthakam
- [[Malinivijayottara 04.026|4.26]] etat sarvaṁ parijñeyaṁ yoginā hitam icchatā
- [[Malinivijayottara 04.027|4.27]] dvāv eva mokṣadau jñeyau jñānī yogī ca śāṅkari

### Three and Four Kinds of Knowledge

- [[Malinivijayottara 04.028|4.28]] jñānaṁ tat trividhaṁ proktaṁ tatrādyaṁ śrutam iṣyate
- [[Malinivijayottara 04.029|4.29]] śāstrārthasya parijñānaṁ vikṣiptasya śrutaṁ matam
- [[Malinivijayottara 04.030|4.30]] sarvam ālocya śāstrārtham ānupūrvyā vyavasthitam
- [[Malinivijayottara 04.031|4.31]] mandasvabhyastabhedena tatra svabhyastam ucyate
- [[Malinivijayottara 04.032|4.32]] ṁ labhet

### Types of Yoga and Yogis

- [[Malinivijayottara 04.033|4.33]] saṁprāpto ghaṭamānaś ca siddhaḥ siddhatamo 'nyathā
- [[Malinivijayottara 04.034|4.34]] samāveśoktivad yogas trividhaḥ samudāhṛtaḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 04.035|4.35]] prāptayogaḥ sa vijṣeyas trividho 'pi manīṣibhiḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 04.036|4.36]] yaḥ karoti tam icchanti ghaṭamānaṁ manīṣiṇaḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 04.037|4.37]] siddhayogas tadā jñeyo yogī yogaphalārthibhiḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 04.038|4.38]] bhuñjānas tatphalaṁ tena hīyate na kathaṁcana

### The Special Excellence of Yogis

- [[Malinivijayottara 04.039|4.39]] uttarottaravaiśiṣṭyam eteṣāṁ samudāhṛtam
- [[Malinivijayottara 04.040|4.40]] yato 'sya jñānam apy asti pūrvo yogaphalojjhitaḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 04.041|4.41]] ity etat kathitaṁ sarvaṁ vijñeyaṁ yogipūjite

## Closing

This is the fourth chapter of the *Mālinīvijayottara Tantra.*

*iti śrīmālinīvijayottare tantre caturtho 'dhikāraḥ* ||
