## Mūla

तथा विलोक्यमानोऽसौ विश्वान्तर्देवतामयः ।
ध्येयः पूज्यश्च तर्प्यश्च तदाविष्टो विमुच्यते ॥ ^mula

tathā vilokyamāno'sau viśvāntardevatāmayaḥ |
dhyeyaḥ pūjyaśca tarpyaśca tadāviṣṭo vimucyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 04 - Chapter Twelve#^pg123|p. 103]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=123>))

> Perceived in this way as consisting of (all) the deities in the universe, one should offer libations to it (tarpya), meditate on it and worship it. He who penetrates into it is liberated.
