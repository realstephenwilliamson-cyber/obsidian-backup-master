## Mūla

आयातिकथनं शास्त्रोपादेयत्वनिरूपणम् ।
इति सप्ताधिकामेनां त्रिंशतं यः सदा बुधः ॥ ^mula

āyātikathanaṃ śāstropādeyatvanirūpaṇam |
iti saptādhikāmenāṃ triṃśataṃ yaḥ sadā budhaḥ || ^iast

> There (in that context, now) will be stated the initial enunciation (of the content of the Tantrāloka and then) in a subsequent (more detailed one) than the one that follows, with (the corresponding) subsections. 1) The Types of Liberating Knowledge (vijñāna). 2) (Penetration into Ultimate Reality) Devoid of Means (Anupāya). 3) The Divine Means (śāmbhavopāya). 4) The Empowered Means (śāktopāya). 5) The Individual Means (āṇavopāya). 6) The Temporal Means (kālopāya) 7) The Arising of the Wheels (cakrodaya). 8) The Path of Space. (deśādhvan). 9) The Path of the Principles (tattvādhvan). 10) The Division of the Principles (tattvabhedanam). 11) The Path of the Forces and the Rest (kalādyadhvan). 12) The Application of the Path (ādhvopayoga). 13) The Descents of the Power (śaktipāta) and Obscuration (śaktipātatirohitī). 14) The Preamble to the Rite of Initiation (dīkṣopakramaṇa). 15) The Common Initiation into the Rule (samayadīkṣā). 16) The Initiation of the Apprentice (pautrika). 17) What needs be Done in the Procedure concerning the Apprentice (vidheyaprakriyā vidhau). 18) The Brief Initiation (sūkṣmā dīkṣā). 19) The Initiation of the Immediate Exit (from the Body) (sadyaḥsamutkrama). 20) The Initiation of the Scales (tulādīkṣā). 21) The Initiation of those Absent (pārokṣī). 22) The Extraction of Sectarian Signs (liṅgoddhara). 23) The Consecration (of a Teacher) (abhiṣecana). 24) The Funerary Rites (antyeṣṭi). 25) The Offering to the Dead (śrāddhaklṛpti). 26) A Description of the Remaining Observance (śeṣavṛttinirūpaṇa). 27) The Worship of the Liṅga (liṅgārcā). 28) The Many Kinds of Propitious Times (parvan), (the Offering of) the Sacred Thread and other Occasional Rites (bahubhitparvapavitrādi nimittaja). 29) The Secret Observance (rahasyacaryā). 30) The Current of Mantras. (mantraugha). 31) The (Trika) Maṇḍala (maṇḍala). 32) The Procedure concerning Mudrās (mudrikāvidhi). 33) The Gathering Together (of the Deities) (ekīkāra). 34) Entry into One’s Own Nature (svasvarūpapraveśa). 35) The Encounter of the Scriptures (śāstramelana). 36) An Account of the Transmission of the Scriptures (āyātikathana). 37) A Description of the Scriptures to be Adopted (śāstropādeyatvanirūpaṇ. (with 1.278–284ab)
>
> Thus, the wise man who constantly (and assiduously) practices these thirty-seven chapters becomes Bhairava Himself in person (directly visible) (sākṣāt). He whose awakened understanding of the thirty-seven (chapters) is perfectly complete (and His perfectly full consciousness (pervades) within the thirty-seven (principles)) is Bhairava, what wonder is it if by his (mere) glance, individual souls (also) attain Bhairava’s state? (with 1.284cd–286ab)
