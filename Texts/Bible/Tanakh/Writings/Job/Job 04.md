## Chapter 4

**4:1** Then Eliphaz the Temanite said in reply: ^4-1

**4:2** If one ventures a word with you, will it be too much?  
But who can hold back his words? ^4-2

**4:3** See, you have encouraged many;  
You have strengthened failing hands. ^4-3

**4:4** Your words have kept him who stumbled from falling;  
You have braced knees that gave way. ^4-4

**4:5** But now that it overtakes you, it is too much;  
It reaches you, and you are unnerved. ^4-5

**4:6** Is not your piety your confidence,  
Your integrity your hope? ^4-6

**4:7** Think now, what innocent man ever perished?  
Where have the upright been destroyed? ^4-7

**4:8** As I have seen, those who plow evil  
And sow mischief reap them. ^4-8

**4:9** They perish by a blast from God,  
Are gone at the breath of His nostrils. ^4-9

**4:10** The lion may roar, the cub may howl,  
But the teeth of the king of beasts are broken.[^1] ^4-10

**4:11** The lion perishes for lack of prey,  
And its whelps are scattered. ^4-11

**4:12** A word came to me in stealth;  
My ear caught a whisper of it. ^4-12

**4:13** In thought-filled visions of the night,  
When deep sleep falls on men, ^4-13

**4:14** Fear and trembling came upon me,  
Causing all my bones to quake with fright. ^4-14

**4:15** A wind passed by me,  
Making the hair of my flesh bristle. ^4-15

**4:16** It halted; its appearance was strange to me;  
A form loomed before my eyes;  
I heard a murmur, a voice, ^4-16

**4:17** “Can mortals be acquitted by God?  
Can man be cleared by his Maker? ^4-17

**4:18** If He cannot trust His own servants,  
And casts reproach[^2] on His angels, ^4-18

**4:19** How much less those who dwell in houses of clay,  
Whose origin is dust,  
Who are crushed like the moth, ^4-19

**4:20** Shattered between daybreak and evening,  
Perishing forever, unnoticed. ^4-20

**4:21** Their cord is pulled up  
And they die, and not with wisdom.” ^4-21

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
