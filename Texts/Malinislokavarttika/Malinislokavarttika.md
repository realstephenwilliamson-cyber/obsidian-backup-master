# Mālinīślokavārttika (GRETIL e-text)

Abhinavagupta's verse commentary on the first verse of the *Mālinīvijayottaratantra*, in roman transliteration.

- Verses I.1–399 are Jürgen Hanneder's critical text from *Abhinavagupta's Philosophy of Revelation* (Groningen: Egbert Forsten, 1998), which is also in your library: [[Hanneder - Abhinavagupta's Philosophy of Revelation]].
- The rest is from Madhusudan Kaul Shastri's first edition (Srinagar, 1921; KSTS 31).
