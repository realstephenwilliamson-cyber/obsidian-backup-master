# Psalm 91

← [[BCP Psalm 090]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 092]] →

*Qui habitat* · Book Four · Eighteenth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 091]]

**91:1** He who dwells in the shelter of the Most High, \*  
abides under the shadow of the Almighty. ^91-1

**91:2** He shall say to the LORD,  
"You are my refuge and my stronghold, \*  
my God in whom I put my trust." ^91-2

**91:3** He shall deliver you from the snare of the hunter \*  
and from the deadly pestilence. ^91-3

**91:4** He shall cover you with his pinions,  
and you shall find refuge under his wings; \*  
his faithfulness shall be a shield and buckler. ^91-4

**91:5** You shall not be afraid of any terror by night, \*  
nor of the arrow that flies by day; ^91-5

**91:6** Of the plague that stalks in the darkness, \*  
nor of the sickness that lays waste at mid-day. ^91-6

**91:7** A thousand shall fall at your side  
and ten thousand at your right hand, \*  
but it shall not come near you. ^91-7

[p. 720] ^pg720

**91:8** Your eyes have only to behold \*  
to see the reward of the wicked. ^91-8

**91:9** Because you have made the LORD your refuge, \*  
and the Most High your habitation, ^91-9

**91:10** There shall no evil happen to you, \*  
neither shall any plague come near your dwelling. ^91-10

**91:11** For he shall give his angels charge over you, \*  
to keep you in all your ways. ^91-11

**91:12** They shall bear you in their hands, \*  
lest you dash your foot against a stone. ^91-12

**91:13** You shall tread upon the lion and adder; \*  
you shall trample the young lion and the serpent under your feet. ^91-13

**91:14** Because he is bound to me in love,  
therefore will I deliver him; \*  
I will protect him, because he knows my Name. ^91-14

**91:15** He shall call upon me, and I will answer him; \*  
I am with him in trouble;  
I will rescue him and bring him to honor. ^91-15

**91:16** With long life will I satisfy him, \*  
and show him my salvation. ^91-16
