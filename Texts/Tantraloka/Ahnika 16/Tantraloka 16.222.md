## Mūla

अव्यक्तान्तं स्वरे न्यस्य शेषं शेषेषु योजयेत् ।
बीजानि सर्वतत्त्वेषु व्याप्तृत्वेन प्रकल्पयेत् ॥ ^mula

avyaktāntaṃ svare nyasya śeṣaṃ śeṣeṣu yojayet |
bījāni sarvatattveṣu vyāptṛtvena prakalpayet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 07 - Chapter Sixteen, part 3#^pg114|p. 94]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=114>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 16.221cd–222ab.
>
> (All the principles) up to Nature should be deposited? into the vowel of any of the phonemes of a Pinda (mantra formed from groups of phonemes). In accord with (their) differing numbers of phonemes, the remaining (set of principles from the individual soul onwards) should be conjoined within the remaining (phonemes). (with 16.221cd–222ab)
>
> Seed-syllable mantras should be conceived to be pervading all (their corresponding) principles. Others maintain that Pinda (mantras formed from groups of phonemes) should be deposited in the same way as seed-syllable (mantras formed from a single syllable) (bīja). (with 16.222cd–223ab)
