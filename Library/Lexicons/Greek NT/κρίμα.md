# κρίμα

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · noun

**Dodson:** a judgment, verdict, lawsuit — (a) a judgment, a verdict; sometimes implying an adverse verdict, a condemnation, (b) a case at law, a lawsuit. (Strong's 2917; headword κρίμα, ατος, τό)
