## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 13 - Chapter Six, part 6#^pg441|p. 426]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=441>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 6.220cd–221ab.
>
> The three short vowels (A I U) (occupy) twelve fingers’ breadth each (within exhalation), and the other (long ones – Ā Ī Ū) (occupy the same space) within (the flow of the) entry (of inhalation). They are on the path of the Sun and Moon, respectively. When the two vowels, I and U are conjoined with A and Ā, the two diphthongs E and O (become manifest, each occupying eighteen fingers’ breadth within the exhaled breath). (The wise) know that the two diphthongs AI and AU are within (the flow of the) entry (of inhalation). The neutral vowels are present in (the breath that) enters within the End of the Twelve (beyond the nose) (ṛ), on the forehead (Ṛ), in the throat (ḷ) and in the heart (Ḷ). The Point (bindu) and Emission (visarga) occupy the entire (inhaled and exhaled breath), respectively. (with 6.219–221)
