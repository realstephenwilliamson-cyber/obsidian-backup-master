# Exodus 6 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Exodus 05|← Exodus 5]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Exodus 07|Exodus 7 →]]

## Israel’s deliverance assured

**6:1** Then the LORD said to Moses, “Now you shall see what I will do to Pharaoh: indeed, by a mighty hand he will let them go; by a mighty hand he will drive them out of his land.” ^6-1

**6:2** God also spoke to Moses and said to him, “I am the LORD. ^6-2

**6:3** I appeared to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob as God Almighty,^[Traditional rendering of Heb *El Shaddai*] but by my name ‘The LORD’^[Heb *YHWH*; see note at 3.15] I did not make myself known to them. ^6-3

**6:4** I also established my covenant with them, to give them the land of Canaan, the land in which they resided as aliens. ^6-4

**6:5** I have also heard the groaning of the Israelites whom the Egyptians have enslaved, and I have remembered my covenant. ^6-5

**6:6** Say therefore to the Israelites: I am the LORD, and I will free you from the burdens of the Egyptians and deliver you from slavery to them. I will redeem you with an outstretched arm and with great acts of judgment. ^6-6

**6:7** I will take you as my people, and I will be your God. You shall know that I am the LORD your God, who has freed you from the burdens of the Egyptians. ^6-7

**6:8** I will bring you into the land that I swore to give to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob; I will give it to you for a possession. I am the LORD.” ^6-8

**6:9** Moses told this to the Israelites, but they would not listen to Moses, because of their broken spirit and their cruel slavery. ^6-9

**6:10** Then the LORD spoke to Moses, ^6-10

**6:11** “Go and tell Pharaoh king of Egypt to let the Israelites go out of his land.” ^6-11

**6:12** But Moses spoke to the LORD, “The Israelites have not listened to me; why should Pharaoh listen to me, poor speaker that I am?”^[Heb *me? I am uncircumcised of lips*] ^6-12

**6:13** Thus the LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron and gave them orders regarding the Israelites and Pharaoh king of Egypt, to free the Israelites from the land of Egypt. ^6-13

## The genealogy of Moses and Aaron

**6:14** The following are the heads of their ancestral houses: the sons of Reuben, the firstborn of Israel: Hanoch, Pallu, Hezron, and Carmi; these are the families of Reuben. ^6-14

**6:15** The sons of Simeon: Jemuel, Jamin, Ohad, Jachin, Zohar, and Shaul, the son of a Canaanite woman; these are the families of Simeon. ^6-15

**6:16** The following are the names of the sons of Levi according to their genealogies: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari, and the length of Levi’s life was one hundred thirty-seven years. ^6-16

**6:17** The sons of Gershon: Libni and Shimei, by their families. ^6-17

**6:18** The sons of Kohath: Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel, and the length of Kohath’s life was one hundred thirty-three years. ^6-18

**6:19** The sons of Merari: Mahli and Mushi. These are the families of the Levites according to their genealogies. ^6-19

**6:20** Amram married Jochebed his aunt, and she bore him Aaron and Moses, and the length of Amram’s life was one hundred thirty-seven years. ^6-20

**6:21** The sons of Izhar: Korah, Nepheg, and Zichri. ^6-21

**6:22** The sons of Uzziel: Mishael, Elzaphan, and Sithri. ^6-22

**6:23** Aaron married Elisheba, daughter of Amminadab and sister of Nahshon, and she bore him Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar. ^6-23

**6:24** The sons of Korah: Assir, Elkanah, and Abiasaph; these are the families of the Korahites. ^6-24

**6:25** Aaron’s son Eleazar married one of the daughters of Putiel, and she bore him Phinehas. These are the heads of the ancestral houses of the Levites by their families. ^6-25

**6:26** It was this same Aaron and Moses to whom the LORD said, “Bring the Israelites out of the land of Egypt, company by company.” ^6-26

**6:27** It was they who spoke to Pharaoh king of Egypt to bring the Israelites out of Egypt, the same Moses and Aaron. ^6-27

## Moses and Aaron obey God’s commands

**6:28** On the day when the LORD spoke to Moses in the land of Egypt, ^6-28

**6:29** the LORD said to Moses, “I am the LORD; tell Pharaoh king of Egypt all that I am speaking to you.” ^6-29

**6:30** But Moses said to the LORD, “Since I am a poor speaker,^[Heb *am uncircumcised of lips*] why should Pharaoh listen to me?” ^6-30
