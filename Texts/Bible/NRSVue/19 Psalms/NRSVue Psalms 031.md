# Psalm 31 (NRSVue)

## Prayer and praise for deliverance from enemies

*To the leader. A Psalm of David.*

**31:1** In you, O LORD, I seek refuge; do not let me ever be put to shame; in your righteousness deliver me. ^31-1

**31:2** Incline your ear to me; rescue me speedily. Be a rock of refuge for me, a strong fortress to save me. ^31-2

**31:3** You are indeed my rock and my fortress; for your name’s sake lead me and guide me; ^31-3

**31:4** take me out of the net that is hidden for me, for you are my refuge. ^31-4

**31:5** Into your hand I commit my spirit; you have redeemed me, O LORD, faithful God. ^31-5

**31:6** You hate^[Heb ms Gk Syr Jerome: MT *I hate*] those who pay regard to worthless idols, but I trust in the LORD. ^31-6

**31:7** I will exult and rejoice in your steadfast love, because you have seen my affliction; you have taken notice of my adversities ^31-7

**31:8** and have not delivered me into the hand of the enemy; you have set my feet in a broad place. ^31-8

**31:9** Be gracious to me, O LORD, for I am in distress; my eye wastes away from grief, my soul and body also. ^31-9

**31:10** For my life is spent with sorrow and my years with sighing; my strength fails because of my misery,^[Gk Syr: Heb *my iniquity*] and my bones waste away. ^31-10

**31:11** I am the scorn of all my adversaries, a horror^[Cn: Heb *exceedingly*] to my neighbors, an object of dread to my acquaintances; those who see me in the street flee from me. ^31-11

**31:12** I have passed out of mind like one who is dead; I have become like a broken vessel. ^31-12

**31:13** For I hear the whispering of many—terror all around!—as they scheme together against me, as they plot to take my life. ^31-13

**31:14** But I trust in you, O LORD; I say, “You are my God.” ^31-14

**31:15** My times are in your hand; deliver me from the hand of my enemies and persecutors. ^31-15

**31:16** Let your face shine upon your servant; save me in your steadfast love. ^31-16

**31:17** Do not let me be put to shame, O LORD, for I call on you; let the wicked be put to shame; let them go dumbfounded to Sheol. ^31-17

**31:18** Let the lying lips be stilled that speak insolently against the righteous with pride and contempt. ^31-18

**31:19** O how abundant is your goodness that you have laid up for those who fear you and accomplished for those who take refuge in you, in the sight of everyone! ^31-19

**31:20** In the shelter of your presence you hide them from human plots; you hold them safe under your shelter from contentious tongues. ^31-20

**31:21** Blessed be the LORD, for he has wondrously shown his steadfast love to me when I was beset as a city under siege. ^31-21

**31:22** I had said in my alarm, “I am driven far^[Heb mss: MT *cut off*] from your sight.” But you heard my supplications when I cried out to you for help. ^31-22

**31:23** Love the LORD, all you his saints. The LORD preserves the faithful but abundantly repays the one who acts haughtily. ^31-23

**31:24** Be strong, and let your heart take courage, all you who wait for the LORD. ^31-24
