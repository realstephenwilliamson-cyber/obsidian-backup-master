## Chapter 17

**17:1** The guilt of Judah is inscribed  
With a stylus of iron,  
Engraved with an adamant point  
On the tablet of their hearts,  
[^1]And on the horns of their altars, ^17-1

**17:2** While their children remember  
Their altars and sacred posts,  
By verdant trees,  
Upon lofty hills. ^17-2

**17:3** Because of the sin of your shrines  
Throughout your borders,  
I will make your rampart a heap in the field,  
And all your treasures a spoil.[^2] ^17-3

**17:4** You will forfeit,[^3] by your own act,  
The inheritance I have given you;  
I will make you a slave to your enemies  
In a land you have never known.  
For you have kindled the flame of My wrath  
Which shall burn for all time. ^17-4

**17:5** Thus said the LORD:  
Cursed is he who trusts in man,  
Who makes mere flesh his strength,  
And turns his thoughts from the LORD. ^17-5

**17:6** He shall be like a bush[^4] in the desert,  
Which does not sense the coming of good:  
It is set in the scorched places of the wilderness,  
In a barren land without inhabitant. ^17-6

**17:7** Blessed is he who trusts in the LORD,  
Whose trust is the LORD alone. ^17-7

**17:8** He shall be like a tree planted by waters,  
Sending forth its roots by a stream:  
It does not sense the coming of heat,  
Its leaves are ever fresh;  
It has no care in a year of drought,  
It does not cease to yield fruit. ^17-8

**17:9** Most devious is the heart;  
It is perverse—who can fathom it? ^17-9

**17:10** I the LORD probe the heart,  
Search the mind—  
To repay every man according to his ways,  
With the proper fruit of his deeds. ^17-10

**17:11** Like a partridge hatching what she did not lay,[^5]  
So is one who amasses wealth by unjust means;  
In the middle of his life it will leave him,  
And in the end he will be proved a fool. ^17-11

**17:12** O Throne of Glory exalted from of old,  
Our Sacred Shrine! ^17-12

**17:13** O Hope of Israel! O LORD!  
All who forsake You shall be put to shame,  
Those in the land who turn from You[^6]  
Shall be doomed[^7] men,  
For they have forsaken the LORD,  
The Fount of living waters. ^17-13

**17:14** Heal me, O LORD, and let me be healed;  
Save me, and let me be saved;  
For You are my glory. ^17-14

**17:15** See, they say to me:  
“Where is the prediction of the LORD?  
Let it come to pass!” ^17-15

**17:16** But I have not evaded  
Being a shepherd in your service,[^8]  
Nor have I longed for the fatal day.  
You know the utterances of my lips,  
They were ever before You. ^17-16

**17:17** Do not be a cause of dismay to me;  
You are my refuge in a day of calamity. ^17-17

**17:18** Let my persecutors be shamed,  
And let not me be shamed;  
Let them be dismayed,  
And let not me be dismayed.  
Bring on them the day of disaster,  
And shatter them with double destruction. ^17-18

**17:19** Thus said the LORD to me: Go and stand in the People’s Gate, by which the kings of Judah enter and by which they go forth, and in all the gates of Jerusalem, ^17-19

**17:20** and say to them: Hear the word of the LORD, O kings of Judah, and all Judah, and all the inhabitants of Jerusalem who enter by these gates! ^17-20

**17:21** Thus said the LORD: Guard yourselves for your own sake against carrying burdens[^9] on the sabbath day, and bringing them through the gates of Jerusalem. ^17-21

**17:22** Nor shall you carry out burdens from your houses on the sabbath day, or do any work, but you shall hallow the sabbath day, as I commanded your fathers. ^17-22

**17:23** (But they would not listen or turn their ear; they stiffened their necks and would not pay heed or accept discipline.) ^17-23

**17:24** If you obey Me—declares the LORD—and do not bring in burdens through the gates of this city on the sabbath day, but hallow the sabbath day and do no work on it, ^17-24

**17:25** then through the gates of this city shall enter kings who sit upon the throne of David, with their officers—riding on chariots and horses, they and their officers—and the men of Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem. And this city shall be inhabited for all time. ^17-25

**17:26** And people shall come from the towns of Judah and from the environs of Jerusalem, and from the land of Benjamin, and from the Shephelah, and from the hill country, and from the Negeb, bringing burnt offerings and sacrifices, meal offerings and frankincense, and bringing offerings of thanksgiving to the House of the LORD. ^17-26

**17:27** But if you do not obey My command to hallow the sabbath day and to carry in no burdens through the gates of Jerusalem on the sabbath day, then I will set fire to its gates; it shall consume the fortresses of Jerusalem and it shall not be extinguished. ^17-27

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain. Emendation yields “Surely the horns of their altars / Are as a memorial against them.”
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain. Emendation yields “Your hand must let go.”
[^4]: Or “tamarisk”; exact meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^5]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^6]: Lit. “Me.”
[^7]: Lit. “inscribed”; meaning of line uncertain.
[^8]: Exact force of Heb. uncertain. Emendation yields “urged you to [bring] misfortune.”
[^9]: Or “merchandise.”
