# Job 17 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Job 16|← Job 16]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Job 18|Job 18 →]]

**17:1** My spirit shall be wasted, my days shall be shortened, and only the grave remaineth for me. ^17-1

**17:2** I have not sinned, and my eye abideth in bitterness. ^17-2

**17:3** Deliver me O Lord, and set me beside thee, and let any man’s hand fight against me. ^17-3

**17:4** Thou hast set their heart far from understanding, therefore they shall not be exalted. ^17-4

**17:5** He promiseth a prey to his companions, and the eyes of his children shall fail. ^17-5

**17:6** He hath made me as it were a byword of the people, and I am an example before them. ^17-6

**17:7** My eye is dim through indignation, and my limbs are brought as it were to nothing. ^17-7

**17:8** The just shall be astonished at this, and the innocent shall be raised up against the hypocrite. ^17-8

**17:9** And the just man shall hold on his way, and he that hath clean hands shall be stronger and stronger. ^17-9

**17:10** Wherefore be you all converted, and come, and I shall not find among you any wise man. ^17-10

**17:11** My days have passed away, my thoughts are dissipated, tormenting my heart. ^17-11

**17:12** They have turned night into day, and after darkness I hope for light again. ^17-12

**17:13** If I wait hell is my house, and I have made my bed in darkness. ^17-13

**17:14** If I have said to rottenness: Thou art my father; to worms, my mother and my sister. ^17-14

**17:15** Where is now then my expectation, and who considereth my patience? ^17-15

**17:16** All that I have shall go down into the deepest pit: thinkest thou that there at least I shall have rest? ^17-16
