## Chapter 143

**143:1** A psalm of David.  
O LORD, hear my prayer;  
give ear to my plea, as You are faithful;  
answer me, as You are beneficent. ^143-1

**143:2** Do not enter into judgment with Your servant,  
for before You no creature is in the right. ^143-2

**143:3** My foe hounded me;  
he crushed me to the ground;  
he made me dwell in darkness  
like those long dead. ^143-3

**143:4** My spirit failed within me;  
my mind was numbed with horror. ^143-4

**143:5** Then I thought of the days of old;  
I rehearsed all Your deeds,  
recounted the work of Your hands. ^143-5

**143:6** I stretched out my hands to You,  
longing for You like thirsty earth. *Selah*. ^143-6

**143:7** Answer me quickly, O LORD;  
my spirit can endure no more.  
Do not hide Your face from me,  
or I shall become like those who descend into the Pit. ^143-7

**143:8** Let me learn of Your faithfulness by daybreak,  
for in You I trust;  
let me know the road I must take,  
for on You I have set my hope. ^143-8

**143:9** Save me from my foes, O LORD;  
to You I look for cover.[^1] ^143-9

**143:10** Teach me to do Your will,  
for You are my God.  
Let Your gracious spirit lead me  
on level ground. ^143-10

**143:11** For the sake of Your name, O LORD, preserve me;  
as You are beneficent, free me from distress. ^143-11

**143:12** As You are faithful, put an end to my foes;  
destroy all my mortal enemies,  
for I am Your servant. ^143-12

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
