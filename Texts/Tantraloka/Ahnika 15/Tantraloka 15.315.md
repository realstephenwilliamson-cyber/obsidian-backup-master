## Mūla

अत्र सर्वाणि तत्त्वानि भेदप्राणानि यत्ततः ।
आसनत्वेन भिन्नं हि संविदो विषयः स्मृतः ॥ ^mula

atra sarvāṇi tattvāni bhedaprāṇāni yattataḥ |
āsanatvena bhinnaṃ hi saṃvido viṣayaḥ smṛtaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 06 - Chapter Fifteen, part 5#^pg208|p. 190]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=208>))
>
> As (the existence of) all the principles here is based on duality (bhedaprāna), (they function together) as the Throne (of the deity), and so, different from consciousness, it is considered to be its field (of operation) (viṣaya). Now, these same principles are immersed in the supreme Bhairava, in a state of oneness (with him); (even so, they are also freely and willingly) emitted (by Him) as if different (from Him), in order to be applied as a (fitting) object of worship. Again, these same principles are endowed with the freedom (of consciousness) attained by (their) state of unity with the venerable Bhairava’s consciousness. Thus, they are capable of assuming the state of the one who worships (Supreme Śiva). (with 15.315–317)
