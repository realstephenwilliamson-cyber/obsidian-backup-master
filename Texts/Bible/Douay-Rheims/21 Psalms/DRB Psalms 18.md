# Psalms 18 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 17|← Psalms 17]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 19|Psalms 19 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 18|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**18:1** Unto the end. A psalm for David. ^18-1

**18:2** The heavens shew forth the glory of God, and the firmament declareth the work of his hands. ^18-2

**18:3** Day to day uttereth speech, and night to night sheweth knowledge. ^18-3

**18:4** There are no speeches nor languages, where their voices are not heard. ^18-4

**18:5** Their sound hath gone forth into all the earth: and their words unto the ends of the world. ^18-5

**18:6** He hath set his tabernacle in the sun: and he, as a bridegroom coming out of his bride chamber, Hath rejoiced as a giant to run the way: ^18-6

**18:7** His going out is from the end of heaven, And his circuit even to the end thereof: and there is no one that can hide himself from his heat. ^18-7

**18:8** The law of the Lord is unspotted, converting souls: the testimony of the Lord is faithful, giving wisdom to little ones. ^18-8

**18:9** The justices of the Lord are right, rejoicing hearts: the commandment of the Lord is lightsome, enlightening the eyes. ^18-9

**18:10** The fear of the Lord is holy, enduring for ever and ever: the judgments of the Lord are true, justified in themselves. ^18-10

**18:11** More to be desired than gold and many precious stones: and sweeter than honey and the honeycomb. ^18-11

**18:12** For thy servant keepeth them, and in keeping them there is a great reward. ^18-12

**18:13** Who can understand sins? from my secret ones cleanse me, O Lord: ^18-13

**18:14** And from those of others spare thy servant. If they shall have no dominion over me, then shall I be without spot: and I shall be cleansed from the greatest sin. ^18-14

**18:15** And the words of my mouth shall be such as may please: and the meditation of my heart always in thy sight. O Lord, my helper, and my redeemer. ^18-15
