## Mūla

इत्थं च मानसंप्लुत्यामपि नाधिगते गतिः ।
न व्यर्थता नानवस्था नान्योन्याश्रयतापि च ॥ ^mula

itthaṃ ca mānasaṃplutyāmapi nādhigate gatiḥ |
na vyarthatā nānavasthā nānyonyāśrayatāpi ca || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. III
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 03 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v03 04 - Chapter Four, part 3#^pg113|p. 100]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 03.pdf#page=113>))
>
> Now, although the means of knowledge concur to act together in this way, (this does not entail the fallacies) of apprehending something already known, or of (their application) being useless. (Nor does it entail) an infinite regress or vicious circle (anyonyāśrayatā).
