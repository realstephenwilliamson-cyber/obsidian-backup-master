# Psalm 77

← [[BCP Psalm 076]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 078]] →

*Voce mea ad Dominum* · Book Three · Fifteenth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 077]]

**77:1** I will cry aloud to God; \*  
I will cry aloud, and he will hear me. ^77-1

**77:2** In the day of my trouble I sought the Lord; \*  
my hands were stretched out by night and did not tire;  
I refused to be comforted. ^77-2

**77:3** I think of God, I am restless, \*  
I ponder, and my spirit faints. ^77-3

**77:4** You will not let my eyelids close; \*  
I am troubled and I cannot speak. ^77-4

**77:5** I consider the days of old; \*  
I remember the years long past; ^77-5

**77:6** I commune with my heart in the night; \*  
I ponder and search my mind. ^77-6

**77:7** Will the Lord cast me off for ever? \*  
will he no more show his favor? ^77-7

**77:8** Has his loving-kindness come to an end for ever? \*  
has his promise failed for evermore? ^77-8

**77:9** Has God forgotten to be gracious? \*  
has he, in his anger, withheld his compassion? ^77-9

**77:10** And I said, "My grief is this: \*  
the right hand of the Most High has lost its power." ^77-10

**77:11** I will remember the works of the LORD, \*  
and call to mind your wonders of old time. ^77-11

**77:12** I will meditate on all your acts \*  
and ponder your mighty deeds. ^77-12

**77:13** Your way, O God, is holy; \*  
who is so great a god as our God? ^77-13

[p. 694]

**77:14** You are the God who works wonders \*  
and have declared your power among the peoples. ^77-14

**77:15** By your strength you have redeemed your people, \*  
the children of Jacob and Joseph. ^77-15

**77:16** The waters saw you, O God;  
the waters saw you and trembled; \*  
the very depths were shaken. ^77-16

**77:17** The clouds poured out water;  
the skies thundered; \*  
your arrows flashed to and fro; ^77-17

**77:18** The sound of your thunder was in the whirlwind;  
your lightnings lit up the world; \*  
the earth trembled and shook. ^77-18

**77:19** Your way was in the sea,  
and your paths in the great waters, \*  
yet your footsteps were not seen. ^77-19

**77:20** You led your people like a flock \*  
by the hand of Moses and Aaron. ^77-20
