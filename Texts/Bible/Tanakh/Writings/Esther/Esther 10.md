## Chapter 10

**10:1** King Ahasuerus imposed tribute on the mainland and the islands. ^10-1

**10:2** All his mighty and powerful acts, and a full account of the greatness to which the king advanced Mordecai, are recorded in the Annals of the Kings of Media and Persia. ^10-2

**10:3** For Mordecai the Jew ranked next to King Ahasuerus and was highly regarded by the Jews and popular with the multitude of his brethren; he sought the good of his people and interceded for the welfare of all his kindred. ^10-3
