# Psalm 72 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 071|← Psalm 71]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 073|Psalm 73 →]]

## Prayer for guidance and support for the king

*Of Solomon.*

**72:1** Give the king your justice, O God, and your righteousness to a king’s son. ^72-1

**72:2** May he judge your people with righteousness and your poor with justice. ^72-2

**72:3** May the mountains yield prosperity for the people, and the hills, in righteousness. ^72-3

**72:4** May he defend the cause of the poor of the people, give deliverance to the needy, and crush the oppressor. ^72-4

**72:5** May he live^[Gk: Heb *may they fear you*] while the sun endures and as long as the moon, throughout all generations. ^72-5

**72:6** May he be like rain that falls on the mown grass, like showers that water the earth. ^72-6

**72:7** In his days may righteousness flourish and peace abound, until the moon is no more. ^72-7

**72:8** May he have dominion from sea to sea and from the River to the ends of the earth. ^72-8

**72:9** May his foes^[Cn: Heb *those who live in the wilderness*] bow down before him, and his enemies lick the dust. ^72-9

**72:10** May the kings of Tarshish and of the isles render him tribute; may the kings of Sheba and Seba bring gifts. ^72-10

**72:11** May all kings fall down before him, all nations give him service. ^72-11

**72:12** For he delivers the needy when they call, the poor and those who have no helper. ^72-12

**72:13** He has pity on the weak and the needy and saves the lives of the needy. ^72-13

**72:14** From oppression and violence he redeems their life, and precious is their blood in his sight. ^72-14

**72:15** Long may he live! May gold of Sheba be given to him. May prayer be made for him continually and blessings invoked for him all day long. ^72-15

**72:16** May there be abundance of grain in the land; may it wave on the tops of the mountains; may its fruit be like Lebanon; and may people blossom in the cities like the grass of the field. ^72-16

**72:17** May his name endure forever, his fame continue as long as the sun. May all nations be blessed in him;^[Or *bless themselves by him*] may they pronounce him happy. ^72-17

**72:18** Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, who alone does wondrous things. ^72-18

**72:19** Blessed be his glorious name forever; may his glory fill the whole earth. Amen and Amen. ^72-19

**72:20** The prayers of David son of Jesse are ended. *(Psalms 73–89)* ^72-20
