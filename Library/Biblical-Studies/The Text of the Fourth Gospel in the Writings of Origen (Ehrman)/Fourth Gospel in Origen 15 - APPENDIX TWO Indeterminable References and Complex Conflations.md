## APPENDIX TWO: Indeterminable References and Complex Conflations

The following list presents quotations and allusions whose biblical source cannot be determined, either because of the presence of close verbal parallels elsewhere in the tradition (e.g. 1:29, 36), or because of their highly conflate character (e.g. 6:33 ff). We have not differentiated among the citations, adaptations, and allusions because none of these materials can be used to reconstruct Origen's text under any circumstances. 

For each reference we have given the Greek materials before the Latin, listing those drawn from the John Commentary first and alphabetizing all the others.
