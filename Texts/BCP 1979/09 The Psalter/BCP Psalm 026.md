# Psalm 26

← [[BCP Psalm 025]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 027]] →

*Judica me, Domine* · Book One · Fifth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 026]]

**26:1** Give judgment for me, O LORD,  
for I have lived with integrity; \*  
I have trusted in the Lord and have not faltered. ^26-1

**26:2** Test me, O LORD, and try me; \*  
examine my heart and my mind. ^26-2

**26:3** For your love is before my eyes; \*  
I have walked faithfully with you. ^26-3

**26:4** I have not sat with the worthless, \*  
nor do I consort with the deceitful. ^26-4

**26:5** I have hated the company of evildoers; \*  
I will not sit down with the wicked. ^26-5

**26:6** I will wash my hands in innocence, O LORD, \*  
that I may go in procession round your altar, ^26-6

**26:7** Singing aloud a song of thanksgiving \*  
and recounting all your wonderful deeds. ^26-7

**26:8** LORD, I love the house in which you dwell \*  
and the place where your glory abides. ^26-8

**26:9** Do not sweep me away with sinners, \*  
nor my life with those who thirst for blood, ^26-9

[p. 617] ^pg617

**26:10** Whose hands are full of evil plots, \*  
and their right hand full of bribes. ^26-10

**26:11** As for me, I will live with integrity; \*  
redeem me, O LORD, and have pity on me. ^26-11

**26:12** My foot stands on level ground; \*  
in the full assembly I will bless the LORD. ^26-12
