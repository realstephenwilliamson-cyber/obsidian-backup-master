## Mūla

पुर्यष्टकस्याभावे च न स्वर्गनरकादयः ।
तथा कृत्वा न कर्तव्यं लौकिकं किञ्चनापि हि ॥ ^mula

puryaṣṭakasyābhāve ca na svarganarakādayaḥ |
tathā kṛtvā na kartavyaṃ laukikaṃ kiñcanāpi hi || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 15 - Chapter Twenty-Four#^pg373|p. 353]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=373>))
>
> And when there is no City of Eight, there is no heaven, hell or the like. Having acted (and performed the rite) in this way, no common worldly (i.e. Vedic smārta) practices (laukika) of any kind should be observed at all. It is said in the venerable Mādhavakula, that he who observes (its) teaching (śāsana) should avoid common (smārta) practices (laukika) such as giving alms, (offering) streams of water and the like, even though they relate to the dead. (with 24.21–22)
