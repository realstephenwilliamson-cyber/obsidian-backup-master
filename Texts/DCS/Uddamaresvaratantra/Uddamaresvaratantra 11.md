# Uḍḍāmareśvaratantra — Chapter 11

[[Uddamaresvaratantra 10|← Chapter 10]] · [[Uddamaresvaratantra|← index]] · [[Uddamaresvaratantra 12|Chapter 12 →]]

atha digbandhanamantraḥ

^s1

vajrakrodhāya mahādantāya daśadiśo bandha bandha haṃ phaṭ svāhā

^s2

atha yonisaṃkocanam

^s3

kumudaṃ haritālaṃ ca piṣṭvā yoniṃ pralepayet

^s4

trirātraṃ pañcarātraṃ ca yonir bhavati saṃyutā

^s5

māṃsī candanamustā ca tagaraṃ nāgakesaram

^s6

ebhir māsaprayogaṃ ca bhagalepanam uttamam

^s7

mālatīkusumais tailair valkair varāṅgalepanam

^s8

ṛtukāle 'thavā kuryāt tadā tattulyatā bhavet

^s9

ḍimbasyānīya pañcāṅgaṃ sarṣapatailapācitam

^s10

satatam abhilepena sā bhartāraṃ vaśaṃ nayet

^s11

mūlaṃ tu vānarīśṛṅgaṃ chāgīmūtreṇa lepayet

^s12

lepanāt tu tataḥ śiśnaṃ yathecchaṃ kāmayed balāt

^s13

saubhāgyapippalī lākṣā viṣaṃ ca kramavardhitam

^s14

lepaṃ prakṣālitaṃ liṅgaṃ nārī kāmayate cirāt

^s15

pārāvataṃ tathā guñjā śvetotpalaṃ samakṣikam

^s16

nābhilepanam ity uktaṃ vīryastambhakaraṃ param

^s17

dvādaśāraṃ likhec cakraṃ kuṅkumena samanvitam

^s18

bhūrjapattre 'thavā vastre netre baddhaphalādike

^s19

pattre pattre likhed bījaṃ hrīṃkāraṃ paramojjvalam

^s20

sādhyanāma tathā madhye karṇikāyāṃ viśeṣataḥ

^s21

vidarbhamantramukhyena tatkūṭaṃ parimaṇḍale

^s22

mṛttikābhiḥ samastābhiḥ pratimāṃ kārayed dṛḍham

^s23

karṣayet pramadāṃ nṝṇāṃ garvitāṃ tu na saṃśayaḥ

^s24

vairāgyaṃ na punar yāti dāsībhāvena tiṣṭhati

^s25

dāḍimaṃ pañcakolaṃ ca lohaṃ vajrorasaṃ ghṛtam

^s26

cūrṇaṃ bhūmandarā śākhā dantanakhaṃ karoti vai

^s27

manaḥśilā prayaṅguś ca rocanā nāgakesaram

^s28

netrāñjanasamāyuktaṃ sarvasattvavaśaṃkaram

^s29
