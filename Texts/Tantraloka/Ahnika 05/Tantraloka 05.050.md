## Mūla

नह्यत्र संस्थितिः कापि विभक्ता जडरूपिणः ।
यत्र कोऽपि व्यवच्छेदो नास्ति यद्विश्वतः स्फुरत् ॥ ^mula

nahyatra saṃsthitiḥ kāpi vibhaktā jaḍarūpiṇaḥ |
yatra ko'pi vyavacchedo nāsti yadviśvataḥ sphurat || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 04 - Chapter Five, part 2#^pg62|p. 47]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=62>))
>
> There is no (independent) state here divided off (from it of) any sort which belongs to (anything) that is insentient. (with 5.50ab)
>
> 6) Śambhunātha has taught us that where there is no limitation (vyavaccheda) at all, that which shines radiantly everywhere, its consciousness, from which nothing has been subtracted, fed by the Supreme Nectar (of freedom), wherein meditation (bhāvanā) or the like finds no direct application, is Cosmic Bliss (jagadānanda). (with 5.50cd–52ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* अत्रैव हेतूपन्यासः --

> नह्यत्र संस्थितिः कापि विभक्ता जडरूपिणः ।

श्वात्मेव स्वात्मना पूर्णा भावा भान्त्यमितस्य तु ।"(रि.प्र. २ ।१ ।७)

इति ॥
