## Mūla

आदायाधारशक्त्यादिशूलशृङ्गान्तमर्चयेत् ।
अग्निमारुतपृथ्व्यम्बुसषष्ठस्वरबिन्दुकम् ॥ ^mula

ādāyādhāraśaktyādiśūlaśṛṅgāntamarcayet |
agnimārutapṛthvyambusaṣaṣṭhasvarabindukam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 02 - Chapter Thirty, part 1#^pg25|p. 7]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=25>))
>
> In the abbreviated rite of worship, (the parts of the Trident) beginning with the power of the foundation and ending with the prongs of the Trident can be worshipped (all together,) assuming the first and the last seed syllable Mantra. (with 30.9cd–10ab)
>
> The Mantra of Ratiśekhara (consists of) Fire (R), Air (Y), Earth (L) and Water (V), along with the sixth vowel (Ū) and the Point. His faces and limbs (are formed) with the short vowels (A I U E O) and the long ones (Ā Ī Ū AI AU), respectively. (with 30.10cd–11ab)
