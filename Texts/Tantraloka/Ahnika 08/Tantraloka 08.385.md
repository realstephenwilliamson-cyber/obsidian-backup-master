## Mūla

अर्धमात्रः स्मृतो बिन्दुर्व्योमरूपी चतुष्कलः ।
तदर्धमर्धचन्द्रस्तदष्टांशेन निरोधिका ॥ ^mula

ardhamātraḥ smṛto bindurvyomarūpī catuṣkalaḥ |
tadardhamardhacandrastadaṣṭāṃśena nirodhikā || ^iast

## Translation

>
> The Point is said to (last) half a measure (ardhamātra) (of time). It has four parts and is vacuous. The Half-Moon is half that (length), while the Obstructress is one eighth.
