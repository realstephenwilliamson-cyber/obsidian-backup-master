# Job 35 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Job 34|← Job 34]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Job 36|Job 36 →]]

## Elihu condemns self-righteousness

**35:1** Elihu continued and said: ^35-1

**35:2** “Do you think this to be just? You say, ‘I am in the right before God.’ ^35-2

**35:3** If you ask, ‘What advantage have I? How am I better off than if I had sinned?’ ^35-3

**35:4** I will answer you and your friends with you. ^35-4

**35:5** Look at the heavens and see; observe the clouds, which are higher than you. ^35-5

**35:6** If you have sinned, what do you accomplish against him? And if your transgressions are multiplied, what do you do to him? ^35-6

**35:7** If you are righteous, what do you give to him, or what does he receive from your hand? ^35-7

**35:8** Your wickedness affects others like you, and your righteousness, other human beings. ^35-8

**35:9** “Because of the multitude of oppressions people cry out; they call for help because of the arm of the mighty. ^35-9

**35:10** But no one says, ‘Where is God my Maker, who gives strength in the night, ^35-10

**35:11** who teaches us more than the animals of the earth and makes us wiser than the birds of the air?’ ^35-11

**35:12** There they cry out, but he does not answer, because of the pride of evildoers. ^35-12

**35:13** Surely God does not hear an empty cry, nor does the Almighty^[Traditional rendering of Heb *Shaddai*] regard it. ^35-13

**35:14** How much less when you say that you do not see him, that the case is before him and you are waiting for him! ^35-14

**35:15** And now, because his anger does not punish and he does not greatly heed transgression,^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^35-15

**35:16** Job opens his mouth in empty talk; he multiplies words without knowledge.” ^35-16
