## Mūla

अवस्थात्रितयेऽप्यस्मिंस्तिरोभावनशीलया ।
शिवशक्त्योभयाक्रान्ताः प्रकुर्वन्ति विचेष्टितम् ॥ ^mula

avasthā tritaye 'py asmiṁs tirobhāvanaśīlay |
śivaśaktyobhayākrāntāḥ[^74] prakurvanti viceṣṭitam || ^iast

*Muktabodha's e-text of the KSTS edition (M00160) has:* avasthātritaye'pyasmiṃstirobhāvanaśīlayā | śivaśaktyobhayākrāntāḥ prakurvanti viceṣṭitam ||

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2022 – June 2022
> p. 46 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=22>))
>
> In these three states, attacked (as they are) by Śiva's power that conceals (their true nature), they behave (and act accordingly).[^75]

[^74]: kh: *śaktyubhayeti* /
[^75]: Quoted in TĀv ad 13/206cd-207ab, where the ‘three states’ are implicitly explained to be the past, present and future. Read, as does Vasudeva with Jayaratha, *śivaśaktyā samākrāntāḥ* for *śivaśaktyobhayākrāntāḥ* [kh: -*śaktyubhayā*-].
