# Psalm 35 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 034|← Psalm 34]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 036|Psalm 36 →]]

## Prayer for deliverance from enemies

*Of David.*

**35:1** Contend, O LORD, with those who contend with me; fight against those who fight against me! ^35-1

**35:2** Take hold of shield and buckler, and rise up to help me! ^35-2

**35:3** Draw the spear and javelin against my pursuers; say to my soul, “I am your salvation.” ^35-3

**35:4** Let them be put to shame and dishonor who seek after my life. Let them be turned back and confounded who devise evil against me. ^35-4

**35:5** Let them be like chaff before the wind, with the angel of the LORD driving them on. ^35-5

**35:6** Let their way be dark and slippery, with the angel of the LORD pursuing them. ^35-6

**35:7** For without cause they hid their net^[Heb *a pit, their net*] for me; without cause they dug a pit^[The word *pit* is transposed from the preceding line] for my life. ^35-7

**35:8** Let ruin come on them unawares, and let the net that they hid ensnare them; let them fall in it—to their ruin. ^35-8

**35:9** Then my soul shall rejoice in the LORD, exulting in his deliverance. ^35-9

**35:10** All my bones shall say, “O LORD, who is like you? You deliver the weak from those too strong for them, the weak and needy from those who despoil them.” ^35-10

**35:11** Malicious witnesses rise up; they ask me about things I do not know. ^35-11

**35:12** They repay me evil for good; my soul is forlorn. ^35-12

**35:13** But as for me, when they were sick, I wore sackcloth; I afflicted myself with fasting. I prayed with head bowed^[Or *My prayer turned back*] on my bosom, ^35-13

**35:14** as though I grieved for a friend or a brother; I went about as one who laments for a mother, bowed down and in mourning. ^35-14

**35:15** But at my stumbling they gathered in glee; they gathered together against me; ruffians whom I did not know tore at me without ceasing; ^35-15

**35:16** they impiously mocked more and more,^[Cn Compare Gk: Heb *like the profanest of mockers of a cake*] gnashing at me with their teeth. ^35-16

**35:17** How long, O LORD, will you look on? Rescue me from their ravages, my life from the lions! ^35-17

**35:18** Then I will thank you in the great congregation; in the mighty throng I will praise you. ^35-18

**35:19** Do not let my treacherous enemies rejoice over me or those who hate me without cause wink the eye. ^35-19

**35:20** For they do not speak peace, but they conceive deceitful words against those who are quiet in the land. ^35-20

**35:21** They open wide their mouths against me; they say, “Aha, Aha, our eyes have seen it.” ^35-21

**35:22** You have seen, O LORD; do not be silent! O Lord, do not be far from me! ^35-22

**35:23** Wake up! Rouse yourself for my defense, for my cause, my God and my Lord! ^35-23

**35:24** Vindicate me, O LORD, my God, according to your righteousness, and do not let them rejoice over me. ^35-24

**35:25** Do not let them say to themselves, “Aha, we have our heart’s desire.” Do not let them say, “We have swallowed you^[Heb *him*] up.” ^35-25

**35:26** Let all those who rejoice at my calamity be put to shame and confusion; let those who exalt themselves against me be clothed with shame and dishonor. ^35-26

**35:27** Let those who desire my vindication shout for joy and be glad and say evermore, “Great is the LORD, who delights in the welfare of his servant.” ^35-27

**35:28** Then my tongue shall tell of your righteousness and of your praise all day long. ^35-28
