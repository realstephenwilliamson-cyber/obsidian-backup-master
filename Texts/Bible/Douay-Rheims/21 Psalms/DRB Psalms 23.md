# Psalms 23 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 22|← Psalms 22]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 24|Psalms 24 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 23|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**23:1** On the first day of the week, a psalm for David. The earth is the Lord’s and the fulness thereof: the world, and all they that dwell therein. ^23-1

**23:2** For he hath founded it upon the seas; and hath prepared it upon the rivers. ^23-2

**23:3** Who shall ascend into the mountain of the Lord: or who shall stand in his holy place? ^23-3

**23:4** The innocent in hands, and clean of heart, who hath not taken his soul in vain, nor sworn deceitfully to his neighbour. ^23-4

**23:5** He shall receive a blessing from the Lord, and mercy from God his Saviour. ^23-5

**23:6** This is the generation of them that seek him, of them that seek the face of the God of Jacob. ^23-6

**23:7** Lift up your gates, O ye princes, and be ye lifted up, O eternal gates: and the King of Glory shall enter in. ^23-7

**23:8** Who is this King of Glory? the Lord who is strong and mighty: the Lord mighty in battle. ^23-8

**23:9** Lift up your gates, O ye princes, and be ye lifted up, O eternal gates: and the King of Glory shall enter in. ^23-9

**23:10** Who is this King of Glory? the Lord of hosts, he is the King of Glory. ^23-10
