## God’s glory in creation and the law

*To the leader. A Psalm of David.*

**19:1** The heavens are telling the glory of God, and the firmament^[Or *dome*] proclaims his handiwork. ^19-1

**19:2** Day to day pours forth speech, and night to night declares knowledge. ^19-2

**19:3** There is no speech, nor are there words; their voice is not heard; ^19-3

**19:4** yet their voice^[Gk Jerome Compare Syr: Heb *line*] goes out through all the earth and their words to the end of the world. In the heavens^[Heb *In them*] he has set a tent for the sun, ^19-4

**19:5** which comes out like a bridegroom from his wedding canopy, and like a strong man runs its course with joy. ^19-5

**19:6** Its rising is from the end of the heavens and its circuit to the end of them, and nothing is hid from its heat. ^19-6

**19:7** The law of the LORD is perfect, reviving the soul; the decrees of the LORD are sure, making wise the simple; ^19-7

**19:8** the precepts of the LORD are right, rejoicing the heart; the commandment of the LORD is clear, enlightening the eyes; ^19-8

**19:9** the fear of the LORD is pure, enduring forever; the ordinances of the LORD are true and righteous altogether. ^19-9

**19:10** More to be desired are they than gold, even much fine gold; sweeter also than honey and drippings of the honeycomb. ^19-10

**19:11** Moreover, by them is your servant warned; in keeping them there is great reward. ^19-11

**19:12** But who can detect one’s own errors? Clear me from hidden faults. ^19-12

**19:13** Keep back your servant also from the insolent;^[Or *from proud thoughts*] do not let them have dominion over me. Then I shall be blameless and innocent of great transgression. ^19-13

**19:14** Let the words of my mouth and the meditation of my heart be acceptable to you, O LORD, my rock and my redeemer. ^19-14
