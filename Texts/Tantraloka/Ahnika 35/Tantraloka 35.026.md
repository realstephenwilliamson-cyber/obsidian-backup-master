## Mūla

लौकिकं वैदिकं साङ्ख्यं योगादि पाञ्चरात्रकम् ।
बौद्धार्हतन्यायशास्त्रं पदार्थक्रमतन्त्रणम् ॥ ^mula

laukikaṃ vaidikaṃ sāṅkhyaṃ yogādi pāñcarātrakam |
bauddhārhatanyāyaśāstraṃ padārthakramatantraṇam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 09 - Chapter Thirty-Five#^pg222|p. 204]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=222>))
>
> All (the various learned and spiritual traditions, be they) worldly science (laukika), Vedic, the Sāmkhya, Yoga and the like, (the Vaiṣṇava) Pañcarātra, Buddhist, Jain, logic (nyāyaśāstra), grammar, (the dualist Śaiva) Siddhāntatantras and Śākta (Tantras) etc., are all born from the (five) Brahmās, distinguished from one another as Sadyojāta and the rest (of Sadāśiva’s five faces), as is taught in the venerable Svacchandatantra and other (scriptures). (with 35.26–27)
