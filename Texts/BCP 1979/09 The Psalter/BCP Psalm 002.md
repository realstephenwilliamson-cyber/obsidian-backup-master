# Psalm 2

← [[BCP Psalm 001]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 003]] →

*Quare fremuerunt gentes?* · Book One · First Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 002]]

**2:1** Why are the nations in an uproar? \*  
Why do the peoples mutter empty threats? ^2-1

**2:2** Why do the kings of the earth rise up in revolt,  
and the princes plot together, \*  
against the LORD and against his Anointed? ^2-2

**2:3** "Let us break their yoke," they say; \*  
"let us cast off their bonds from us." ^2-3

**2:4** He whose throne is in heaven is laughing; \*  
the Lord has them in derision. ^2-4

**2:5** Then he speaks to them in his wrath, \*  
and his rage fills them with terror. ^2-5

**2:6** "I myself have set my king \*  
upon my holy hill of Zion." ^2-6

**2:7** Let me announce the decree of the LORD: \*  
he said to me, "You are my Son;  
this day have I begotten you. ^2-7

**2:8** Ask of me, and I will give you the nations for your inheritance \*  
and the ends of the earth for your possession. ^2-8

**2:9** You shall crush them with an iron rod \*  
and shatter them like a piece of pottery." ^2-9

**2:10** And now, you kings, be wise; \*  
be warned, you rulers of the earth. ^2-10

**2:11** Submit to the LORD with fear, \*  
and with trembling bow before him; ^2-11

**2:12** Lest he be angry and you perish; \*  
for his wrath is quickly kindled. ^2-12

[p. 587] ^pg587

**2:13** Happy are they all \*  
who take refuge in him! ^2-13
