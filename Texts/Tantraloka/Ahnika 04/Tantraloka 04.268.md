## Mūla

मुद्रा छुम्मेति तेषां च विधानं स्वपरस्थितम् ।
तादात्म्यप्रतिपत्त्यै हि स्वं संतानं समाश्रयेत् ॥ ^mula

mudrā chummeti teṣāṃ ca vidhānaṃ svaparasthitam |
tādātmyapratipattyai hi svaṃ saṃtānaṃ samāśrayet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. III
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 03 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v03 11 - Chapter Four, part 10#^pg386|p. 373]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 03.pdf#page=386>))
>
> The expression ‘and the like’ alludes to the hermitage (ghara) (where the teachers resided), the village (which was their begging place) (pallī), the sacred seats, primary and secondary (pīṭha, upapīṭha) (of the goddess), the gestures (mudrā) and the secret signs (chummā). (These too are not taught in the Mālinīvijayottara because) the prescribed procedure for them (all) is (also) established (on the basis of the difference between) one’s own (school and that of) another. (with 4.267cd–268ab)
>
> (A Kaula initiate should know these things, because) in order to attain a state of oneness (with his deity, he) should remain faithful (samāśrayet) to his own lineage (santāna). He must not enjoy and worship the assembly (cakra) (of Kaula initiates and Yoginīs) with members of a different spiritual lineage. (with 4.268cd–269ab)
