## Mūla

भक्त्युल्लसत्पुलकतां स्फुटमङ्गभूषां
श्रीशंभुनाथनतिमेव ललाटिकां च ।
शैवश्रुतिं श्रवणभूषणमप्यवाप्य
सौभाग्यमभ्यधिकमुद्वहति स्म याऽन्तः ॥ ^mula

bhaktyullasatpulakatāṃ sphuṭamaṅgabhūṣāṃ
śrīśaṃbhunāthanatimeva lalāṭikāṃ ca |
śaivaśrutiṃ śravaṇabhūṣaṇamapyavāpya
saubhāgyamabhyadhikamudvahati sma yā'ntaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 11 - Chapter Thirty-Seven, part 1#^pg282|p. 264]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=282>))
>
> The clearly apparent ornament of her body was the horripilation due to (her) devotion; (the ornament of her) forehead was her respect for the venerable Śambhunātha; she also obtained as an ornament (for her) ears, her hearing of (the sacred) Śaiva (scriptures), (and so as a result), she bore within (herself) an extraordinary (inner) beauty (saubhāgya).
