## Mūla

नैव प्रमाणयेद् विद्वान् शैवमेवागमं श्रयेत् ।
यदार्षे पातहेतूक्तं तदस्मिन् वामशासने ॥ ^mula

naiva pramāṇayed vidvān śaivamevāgamaṃ śrayet |
yadārṣe pātahetūktaṃ tadasmin vāmaśāsane || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 11 - Chapter Thirty-Seven, part 1#^pg248|p. 230]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=248>))
>
> The Supreme Lord has said in the venerable Aanandaśāstra, for example, that a learned man should not select as (his) authority the Veda (rṣivākya), which (gives) much affliction (kleśa), yields little and unstable results, and is limited (mita) (in its teachings and application); (rather) he should take refuge only in Śaiva scripture (gama). (with 37.10–11ab)
>
> That which is said in the Veda to be the cause of a fall (into sin), in this doctrine of the left (vāmaśāsana), serves to lead rapidly to accomplishment. This is so because all (the Vedic teaching) of the ṛṣis (ārṣa) is in the very belly of Māyā. (with 37.11cd–12ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* ननु मन्वादिशास्त्रं यदि न ग्राह्यं तत् किं न अयं सर्व एव आचारो भ्रश्येत्?--इत्याशङ्क्य आह--

> यदार्षे पातहेतूक्तं तदस्मिन् वामशासने ॥
> आशुसिद्ध्यै यतः सर्वमार्षं मायोदरस्थितम् ।

पातहेतूक्तमिति--पातहेतोः सुरादेरुक्तं वचनम्--इत्यर्थः । पातकार्युक्तमिति तु स्पष्टः पाठः । मायोदरस्थितमिति-- लोकरक्षापरत्वात् ॥
