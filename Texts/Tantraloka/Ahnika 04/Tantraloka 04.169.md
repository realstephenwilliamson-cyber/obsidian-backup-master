## Mūla

एतस्यां स्वात्मसंवित्ताविदं सर्वमहं विभुः ।
इति प्रविकसद्रूपा संवित्तिरवभासते ॥ ^mula

etasyāṃ svātmasaṃvittāvidaṃ sarvamahaṃ vibhuḥ |
iti pravikasadrūpā saṃvittiravabhāsate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. III
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 03 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v03 08 - Chapter Four, part 7#^pg255|p. 242]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 03.pdf#page=255>))
>
> (Then) within this, its own self-consciousness (svātmasaṁvitti), an expanding form of consciousness (saṁvitti) manifests that “I, the All-pervading Lord, am all this.” Then consciousness, coloured by the enjoyment of the universal nature of everything (sarvātmabhāva) present within (it), is completely full (and all-encompassing). Even so, it dissolves away into the abode of (transcendent) Akula. (with 4.169–170)
