## Mūla

गलद्विजातीयतया प्राप्यं शीघ्रं हि लभ्यते ।
एवं चानादिसंसारोचितविज्ञानसन्ततेः ॥ ^mula

galadvijātīyatayā prāpyaṃ śīghraṃ hi labhyate |
evaṃ cānādisaṃsārocitavijñānasantateḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 05 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 3#^pg109|p. 92]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=109>))
>
> The goal is quicky attained by (means of) the stream of homogeneous (acts of) perception (jñānadārā) of this kind, (that develops) as (the true nature of the Self) becomes progressively more evident, in consonance with the falling away of the heterogeneous ones. (with 28.201cd–202ab)
>
> When the continuum of consciousness proper to the beginningless state of transmigratory existence is destroyed in this way, the (new manner of) knowing that transcends the worldly one (lokottaraṁ jñāna) assumes (the form of) another continuum (which is that of enlightened consciousness). (with 28.202cd–203ab)
