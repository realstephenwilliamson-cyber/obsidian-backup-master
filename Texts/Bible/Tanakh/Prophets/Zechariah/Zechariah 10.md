## Chapter 10

**10:1** Ask the LORD for rain  
In the season of late rain.[^1]  
It is the LORD who causes storms;[^2]  
And He will provide rainstorms for them,  
Grass in the fields for everyone.[^3] ^10-1

**10:2** For the teraphim[^4] spoke delusion,  
The augurs predicted falsely;  
And dreamers speak lies  
And console with illusions.  
That is why My people have strayed[^5] like a flock,  
They suffer[^6] for lack of a shepherd. ^10-2

**10:3** My anger is roused against the shepherds,  
And I will punish the he-goats.[^7]  
For the LORD of Hosts has taken thought  
In behalf of His flock, the House of Judah;  
He will make them like majestic chargers in battle. ^10-3

**10:4** From them shall come cornerstones,  
From them tent pegs,[^8]  
From them bows of combat,  
And every captain shall also arise from them. ^10-4

**10:5** And together they shall be like warriors in battle,  
Tramping in the dirt of the streets;  
They shall fight, for the LORD shall be with them,  
And they shall put horsemen to shame. ^10-5

**10:6** I will give victory to the House of Judah,  
And triumph to the House of Joseph.  
I will restore them, for I have pardoned them,  
And they shall be as though I had never disowned them;  
For I the LORD am their God,  
And I will answer their prayers. ^10-6

**10:7** Ephraim shall be like a warrior,  
And they[^9] shall exult as with wine;  
Their children shall see it and rejoice,  
They shall exult in the LORD. ^10-7

**10:8** I will whistle to them and gather them,  
For I will redeem them;  
They shall increase and continue increasing.[^10] ^10-8

**10:9** For though I sowed them among the nations,  
In the distant places they shall remember Me,  
They shall escape with their children and shall return. ^10-9

**10:10** I will bring them back from the land of Egypt  
And gather them from Assyria;  
And I will bring them to the lands of Gilead and Lebanon,  
And even they shall not suffice for them. ^10-10

**10:11** A hemmed-in force shall pass over the sea  
And shall stir up waves in the sea;[^11]  
And all the deeps of the Nile shall dry up.  
Down shall come the pride of Assyria,  
And the scepter of Egypt shall pass away. ^10-11

**10:12** But I will make them[^12] mighty through the LORD,  
And they shall march proudly[^13] in His name  
—declares the LORD. ^10-12

[^1]: Septuagint reads “in its season / The early rain and the late.” Cf. Deut. 11.14.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Emendation yields “[producing] food for men, / Grass in the fields for cattle.” Cf. Deut. 11.14–15.
[^4]: Idols consulted for oracles; cf. 1 Sam. 15.23; Ezek. 21.26.
[^5]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^6]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^7]: I.e., oppressive leaders; cf. Ezek. 34.17 ff.
[^8]: Emendation yields “shields and bucklers.”
[^9]: Emendation yields “And when Ephraim is victorious, / They…”
[^10]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^11]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^12]: I.e., Judah and Ephraim.
[^13]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain. Emendation yields “have glory”; cf. Isa. 45.25.
