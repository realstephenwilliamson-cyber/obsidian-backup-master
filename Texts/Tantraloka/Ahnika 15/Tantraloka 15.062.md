## Mūla

धृत्याप्यायमलप्लोषवीर्यव्याप्तिमृजिस्थितीः ।
अभेदं च क्रमादेति स्नानाष्टकपरो मुनिः ॥ ^mula

dhṛtyāpyāyamalaploṣavīryavyāptimṛjisthitīḥ |
abhedaṃ ca kramādeti snānāṣṭakaparo muniḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 03 - Chapter Fifteen, part 2#^pg66|p. 48]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=66>))
>
> The sage (muni) who is intent (on performing) the eight ablutions attain successively, (respectively,) stability (dhṛti) (Earth), nourishment (apyāya) (Water), the burning of impurity (malaploṣa) (Fire), vitality (vīrya) (Air), pervasion (vyāpti) (Space), the power to create (sṛji) (Moon) and maintain (sthiti) (Sun), and (then finally,) oneness (abheda) (i.e., the Self).
