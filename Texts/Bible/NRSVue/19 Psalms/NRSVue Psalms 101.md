## A sovereign’s pledge of integrity and justice

*Of David. A Psalm.*

**101:1** I will sing of loyalty and of justice; to you, O LORD, I will sing. ^101-1

**101:2** I will study the way that is blameless. When shall I attain it? I will walk with integrity of heart within my house; ^101-2

**101:3** I will not set before my eyes anything that is base. I hate the work of those who fall away; it shall not cling to me. ^101-3

**101:4** Perverseness of heart shall be far from me; I will know nothing of evil. ^101-4

**101:5** One who secretly slanders a neighbor I will destroy. A haughty look and an arrogant heart I will not tolerate. ^101-5

**101:6** I will look with favor on the faithful in the land, so that they may live with me; whoever walks in the way that is blameless shall minister to me. ^101-6

**101:7** No one who practices deceit shall remain in my house; no one who utters lies shall continue in my presence. ^101-7

**101:8** Morning by morning I will destroy all the wicked in the land, cutting off all evildoers from the city of the LORD. ^101-8
