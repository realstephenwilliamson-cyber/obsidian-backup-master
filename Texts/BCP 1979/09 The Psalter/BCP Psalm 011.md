# Psalm 11

← [[BCP Psalm 010]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 012]] →

*In Domino confido* · Book One · Second Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 011]]

**11:1** In the LORD have I taken refuge; \*  
how then can you say to me,  
"Fly away like a bird to the hilltop; ^11-1

**11:2** For see how the wicked bend the bow  
and fit their arrows to the string, \*  
to shoot from ambush at the true of heart. ^11-2

**11:3** When the foundations are being destroyed, \*  
what can the righteous do?" ^11-3

**11:4** The LORD is in his holy temple; \*  
the LORD'S throne is in heaven. ^11-4

**11:5** His eyes behold the inhabited world; \*  
his piercing eye weighs our worth. ^11-5

**11:6** The LORD weighs the righteous as well as the wicked, \*  
but those who delight in violence he abhors. ^11-6

**11:7** Upon the wicked he shall rain coals of fire and burning sulphur; \*  
a scorching wind shall be their lot. ^11-7

**11:8** For the LORD is righteous;  
he delights in righteous deeds; \*  
and the just shall see his face. ^11-8
