## Mūla

यच्चैतदुक्तमेतावत्कर्तव्यमिति तद्ध्रुवम् ।
तीव्रशक्तिगृहीतानां स्वयमेव हृदि स्फुरेत् ॥ ^mula

yaccaitaduktametāvatkartavyamiti taddhruvam |
tīvraśaktigṛhītānāṃ svayameva hṛdi sphuret || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 14 - Chapter Twenty-Three#^pg355|p. 335]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=355>))
>
> “What has been said (by the Lord) should be done in that (very) way” — that (intention) shines firm and radiantly, spontaneously (by itself) in the heart of those who are seized by an intense descent of the power (of grace).
