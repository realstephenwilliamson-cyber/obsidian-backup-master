# Nahum 3 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Nahum 02|← Nahum 2]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · —

## Ruin imminent and inevitable

**3:1** Woe, city of bloodshed, utterly deceitful, full of plunder—no end to the prey! ^3-1

**3:2** The crack of whip and rumble of wheel, galloping horse and bounding chariot! ^3-2

**3:3** Horsemen charging, flashing sword and glittering spear, piles of dead, heaps of corpses, dead bodies without end—they stumble over the bodies! ^3-3

**3:4** Because of the countless debaucheries of the prostitute, gracefully alluring, mistress of sorcery, who enslaves^[Heb *sells*] nations through her debaucheries and peoples through her sorcery, ^3-4

**3:5** I am against you, says the LORD of hosts, and will lift up your skirts over your face, and I will let nations look on your nakedness and kingdoms on your shame. ^3-5

**3:6** I will throw filth at you and treat you with contempt and make you a spectacle. ^3-6

**3:7** Then all who see you will shrink from you and say, “Nineveh is devastated; who will bemoan her?” Where shall I seek comforters for you? ^3-7

**3:8** Are you better than Thebes^[Or *No-amon*] that sat by the Nile, with water around her, her rampart a sea, water her wall? ^3-8

**3:9** Cush was her strength, Egypt, too, and that without limit; Put and the Libyans were her^[Gk Syr: Heb *your*] helpers. ^3-9

**3:10** Yet she became an exile; she went into captivity; even her infants were dashed in pieces at the head of every street; lots were cast for her nobles; all her dignitaries were bound in fetters. ^3-10

**3:11** You also will be drunken; you will go into hiding;^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] you will seek a refuge from the enemy. ^3-11

**3:12** All your fortresses are like fig trees with first-ripe figs—if shaken they fall into the mouth of the eater. ^3-12

**3:13** Look at your troops: they are women in your midst. The gates of your land are wide open to your foes; fire has devoured the bars of your gates. ^3-13

**3:14** Draw water for the siege; strengthen your forts; trample the clay; tread the mortar; take hold of the brick mold! ^3-14

**3:15** There the fire will devour you; the sword will cut you off. It will devour you like the locust. Multiply yourselves like the locust; multiply like the grasshopper! ^3-15

**3:16** You increased your merchants more than the stars of the heavens. The locust sheds its skin and flies away. ^3-16

**3:17** Your guards are like grasshoppers, your scribes like swarms^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] of locusts settling on the fences on a cold day—when the sun rises, they fly away; no one knows where they have gone. ^3-17

**3:18** Your shepherds are asleep, O king of Assyria; your nobles slumber. Your people are scattered on the mountains with no one to gather them. ^3-18

**3:19** There is no assuaging your hurt; your wound is mortal. All who hear the news about you clap their hands over you. For who has ever escaped your endless cruelty? ^3-19
