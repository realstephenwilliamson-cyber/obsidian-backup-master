## Chapter 24

**24:1** Of David. A psalm.  
The earth is the LORD’s and all that it holds,  
the world and its inhabitants. ^24-1

**24:2** For He founded it upon the ocean,  
set it on the nether-streams. ^24-2

**24:3** Who may ascend the mountain of the LORD?  
Who may stand in His holy place?— ^24-3

**24:4** He who has clean hands and a pure heart,  
who has not taken a false oath by My[^1] life  
or sworn deceitfully. ^24-4

**24:5** He shall carry away a blessing from the LORD,  
a just reward from God, his deliverer. ^24-5

**24:6** Such is the circle[^2] of those who turn to Him,  
Jacob, who seek Your presence. *Selah*. ^24-6

**24:7** O gates, lift up your heads!  
Up high, you everlasting doors,  
so the King of glory may come in! ^24-7

**24:8** Who is the King of glory?—  
the LORD, mighty and valiant,  
the LORD, valiant in battle. ^24-8

**24:9** O gates, lift up your heads!  
Lift them up, you everlasting doors,  
so the King of glory may come in! ^24-9

**24:10** Who is the King of glory?—  
the LORD of hosts,  
He is the King of glory! *Selah*. ^24-10

[^1]: Ancient versions and some mss. read “His.”
[^2]: Lit. “generation.”
