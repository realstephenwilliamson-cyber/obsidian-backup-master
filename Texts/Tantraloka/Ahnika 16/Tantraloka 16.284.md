## Mūla

विशेषतस्त्वमायीयशिवताभेदशालिनः ।
मोक्षेऽभ्युपायः सञ्जल्पो बन्धमोक्षौ ततः किल ॥ ^mula

viśeṣatastvamāyīyaśivatābhedaśālinaḥ |
mokṣe'bhyupāyaḥ sañjalpo bandhamokṣau tataḥ kila || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 07 - Chapter Sixteen, part 3#^pg147|p. 127]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=147>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 16.283cd–284ab.
>
> The means to liberation (used by a teacher) who is one with (his own essential) Śiva nature (śivatā) that is free of Māyā is especially (viśeṣatah) (his) discourse (saṁjalpa). Indeed, (both) liberation and bondage are due to this.
