## Mūla

ऊर्द्ध्वप्रसारितो मुष्टिर्दक्षिणोऽङ्गुष्ठगर्भगः ।
दण्डमुद्रेति विख्याता वैवस्वतकुलप्रिया ॥ ^mula

ūrddhvaprasārito muṣṭirdakṣiṇo'ṅguṣṭhagarbhagaḥ |
daṇḍamudreti vikhyātā vaivasvatakulapriyā || ^iast

> **6. Daṇḍa (the Stick)**
>
> The right fist is extended upwards and the thumb is placed within it. This called the gesture of the stick which is beloved of Vaivasvata's family.
