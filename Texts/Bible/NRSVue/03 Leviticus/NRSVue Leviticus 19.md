# Leviticus 19 (NRSVue)

## Ritual and moral holiness

**19:1** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ^19-1

**19:2** “Speak to all the congregation of the Israelites and say to them: You shall be holy, for I the LORD your God am holy. ^19-2

**19:3** You shall each revere your mother and father, and you shall keep my Sabbaths: I am the LORD your God. ^19-3

**19:4** Do not turn to idols or make cast images for yourselves: I am the LORD your God. ^19-4

**19:5** “When you offer a sacrifice of well-being to the LORD, offer it in such a way that it is acceptable on your behalf. ^19-5

**19:6** It shall be eaten on the same day you offer it or on the next day, and anything left over until the third day shall be consumed in fire. ^19-6

**19:7** If any of it is eaten on the third day, it is an abomination; it will not be acceptable. ^19-7

**19:8** All who eat it shall be subject to punishment, because they have profaned what is holy to the LORD, and any such person shall be cut off from the people. ^19-8

**19:9** “When you reap the harvest of your land, you shall not reap to the very edges of your field or gather the gleanings of your harvest. ^19-9

**19:10** You shall not strip your vineyard bare or gather the fallen grapes of your vineyard; you shall leave them for the poor and the alien: I am the LORD your God. ^19-10

**19:11** “You shall not steal; you shall not deal falsely; and you shall not lie to one another. ^19-11

**19:12** And you shall not swear falsely by my name, profaning the name of your God: I am the LORD. ^19-12

**19:13** “You shall not defraud your neighbor; you shall not steal; and you shall not keep for yourself the wages of a laborer until morning. ^19-13

**19:14** You shall not revile the deaf or put a stumbling block before the blind; you shall fear your God: I am the LORD. ^19-14

**19:15** “You shall not render an unjust judgment; you shall not be partial to the poor or defer to the great: with justice you shall judge your neighbor. ^19-15

**19:16** You shall not go around as a slanderer among your people, and you shall not stand idly by when the blood^[Heb *stand against the blood*] of your neighbor is at stake: I am the LORD. ^19-16

**19:17** “You shall not hate in your heart anyone of your kin; you shall reprove your neighbor, or you will incur guilt yourself. ^19-17

**19:18** You shall not take vengeance or bear a grudge against any of your people, but you shall love your neighbor as yourself: I am the LORD. ^19-18

**19:19** “You shall keep my statutes. You shall not let your animals breed with a different kind; you shall not sow your field with two kinds of seed; nor shall you put on a garment made of two different materials. ^19-19

**19:20** “If a man has sexual relations with a woman who is a slave, designated for another man but not ransomed or given her freedom, an inquiry shall be held. They shall not be put to death, since she has not been freed, ^19-20

**19:21** but he shall bring a guilt offering for himself to the LORD, at the entrance of the tent of meeting, a ram as guilt offering. ^19-21

**19:22** And the priest shall make atonement for him with the ram of guilt offering before the LORD for his sin that he committed, and the sin he committed shall be forgiven him. ^19-22

**19:23** “When you come into the land and plant all kinds of trees for food, then you shall regard their fruit as forbidden;^[Heb *as their uncircumcision*] three years it shall be forbidden^[Heb *uncircumcision*] to you; it must not be eaten. ^19-23

**19:24** In the fourth year all their fruit shall be set apart for rejoicing in the LORD. ^19-24

**19:25** But in the fifth year you may eat of their fruit, that their yield may be increased for you: I am the LORD your God. ^19-25

**19:26** “You shall not eat anything with its blood. You shall not practice augury or witchcraft. ^19-26

**19:27** You shall not round off the hair on your temples or mar the edges of your beard. ^19-27

**19:28** You shall not make any gashes in your flesh for the dead or tattoo any marks upon you: I am the LORD. ^19-28

**19:29** “Do not profane your daughter by making her a prostitute, that the land not become prostituted and full of depravity. ^19-29

**19:30** You shall keep my Sabbaths and revere my sanctuary: I am the LORD. ^19-30

**19:31** “Do not turn to mediums and spiritualists; do not seek them out, to be defiled by them: I am the LORD your God. ^19-31

**19:32** “You shall rise before the aged and defer to the old, and you shall fear your God: I am the LORD. ^19-32

**19:33** “When an alien resides with you in your land, you shall not oppress the alien. ^19-33

**19:34** The alien who resides with you shall be to you as the native-born among you; you shall love the alien as yourself, for you were aliens in the land of Egypt: I am the LORD your God. ^19-34

**19:35** “You shall not cheat in measuring length, weight, or quantity. ^19-35

**19:36** You shall have honest balances, honest weights, an honest ephah, and an honest hin: I am the LORD your God who brought you out of the land of Egypt. ^19-36

**19:37** You shall keep all my statutes and all my ordinances and observe them: I am the LORD.” ^19-37
