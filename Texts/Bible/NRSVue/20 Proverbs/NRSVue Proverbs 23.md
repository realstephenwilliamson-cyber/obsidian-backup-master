# Proverbs 23 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Proverbs 22|← Proverbs 22]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Proverbs 24|Proverbs 24 →]]

**23:1** When you sit down to eat with an official, observe carefully what^[Or *who*] is before you, ^23-1

**23:2** and put a knife to your throat if you have a big appetite. ^23-2

**23:3** Do not desire an official’s^[Heb *his*] delicacies, for they are deceptive food. ^23-3

**23:4** Do not wear yourself out to get rich; be wise enough to desist. ^23-4

**23:5** When your eyes light upon it, it is gone, for suddenly it takes wings to itself, flying like an eagle toward heaven. ^23-5

**23:6** Do not eat the bread of the stingy; do not desire their delicacies, ^23-6

**23:7** for like a hair in the throat, so are they.^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] “Eat and drink!” they say to you, but they do not mean it. ^23-7

**23:8** You will vomit up the little you have eaten, and you will waste your pleasant words. ^23-8

**23:9** Do not speak in the hearing of a fool, who will only despise the wisdom of your words. ^23-9

**23:10** Do not remove an ancient landmark or encroach on the fields of orphans, ^23-10

**23:11** for their vindicator^[Or *redeemer*] is strong; he will plead their cause against you. ^23-11

**23:12** Apply your mind to instruction and your ear to words of knowledge. ^23-12

**23:13** Do not withhold discipline from your children; if you beat them with a rod, they will not die. ^23-13

**23:14** If you beat them with the rod, you will save their lives from Sheol. ^23-14

**23:15** My child, if your heart is wise, my heart also will be glad. ^23-15

**23:16** My soul will rejoice when your lips speak what is right. ^23-16

**23:17** Do not let your heart envy sinners, but always continue in the fear of the LORD. ^23-17

**23:18** Surely there is a future, and your hope will not be cut off. ^23-18

**23:19** Hear, my child, and be wise, and direct your mind in the way. ^23-19

**23:20** Do not be among winebibbers or among gluttonous eaters of meat, ^23-20

**23:21** for the drunkard and the glutton will come to poverty, and drowsiness will clothe them with rags. ^23-21

**23:22** Listen to your father who begot you, and do not despise your mother when she is old. ^23-22

**23:23** Buy truth, and do not sell it; buy wisdom, instruction, and understanding. ^23-23

**23:24** The father of the righteous will greatly rejoice; he who fathers a wise son will be glad in him. ^23-24

**23:25** Let your father and mother be glad; let her who bore you rejoice. ^23-25

**23:26** My child, give me your heart, and let your eyes observe^[Or *delight in*] my ways. ^23-26

**23:27** For a prostitute is a deep pit; an adulteress^[Heb *alien woman*] is a narrow well. ^23-27

**23:28** She lies in wait like a robber and increases the number of the faithless. ^23-28

**23:29** Who has woe? Who has sorrow? Who has strife? Who has complaining? Who has wounds without cause? Who has redness of eyes? ^23-29

**23:30** Those who linger late over wine, those who keep trying mixed wines. ^23-30

**23:31** Do not look at wine when it is red, when it sparkles in the cup and goes down smoothly. ^23-31

**23:32** At the last it bites like a serpent and stings like an adder. ^23-32

**23:33** Your eyes will see strange things, and your mind utter perverse things. ^23-33

**23:34** You will be like one who lies down in the midst of the sea, like one who lies on the top of a mast.^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^23-34

**23:35** “They struck me,” you will say,^[Gk Syr Vg Tg: Heb lacks *you will say*] “but I was not hurt; they beat me, but I did not feel it. When shall I awake? I will seek another drink.” ^23-35
