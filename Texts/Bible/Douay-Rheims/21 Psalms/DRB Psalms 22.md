# Psalms 22 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 21|← Psalms 21]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 23|Psalms 23 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 22|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**22:1** A psalm for David. The Lord ruleth me: and I shall want nothing. ^22-1

**22:2** He hath set me in a place of pasture. He hath brought me up, on the water of refreshment: ^22-2

**22:3** He hath converted my soul. He hath led me on the paths of justice, for his own name’s sake. ^22-3

**22:4** For though I should walk in the midst of the shadow of death, I will fear no evils, for thou art with me. Thy rod and thy staff, they have comforted me. ^22-4

**22:5** Thou hast prepared a table before me against them that afflict me. Thou hast anointed my head with oil; and my chalice which inebriateth me, how goodly is it! ^22-5

**22:6** And thy mercy will follow me all the days of my life. And that I may dwell in the house of the Lord unto length of days. ^22-6
