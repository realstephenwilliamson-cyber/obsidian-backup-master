# Zohar 3.090a

← [[Zohar 3.089b]] · [[Zohar (Soncino)]] · [[Zohar 3.090b]] →

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 8.04 - Parashat Emor#^z3-90a|Parashat Emor, at 3:90a]]

*Volume 3, folio 90a* · Emor

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Emor 10:30–10:32.*

**Emor 11:33** [^1]HE SHALL NOT PROFANE HIS SEED AMONG HIS PEOPLE. Whoever discharges his semen without purpose will never be allowed to behold the divine Presence,[^2] and such a one is called wicked. This is not the case, however, if a man’s wife does not conceive; still, a man should pray that God should provide him a fitting vessel so that his seed should not be spoilt. ^emor-11-33

**Emor 11:34** For he who discharges his seed into a vessel that is not fitting spoils his seed; and if this is the case with ordinary men, how much more so with the priest who is the counterpart on earth of the supernal holiness. ^emor-11-34

**Emor 11:35** AMONG HIS PEOPLE: that is to say, this is a disgrace, a defect among his people. FOR I AM THE LORD WHICH SANCTIFY HIM: I am He that sanctifies him every day, and therefore he must not spoil his seed nor must any blemish be found in him. He must be holy throughout, so that the Holy One may be served by a holy one. ^emor-11-35

**Emor 11:36** And because God is served by the hand of the priest, who is holy, the priest is served by one who is sanctified by his purity, to wit, the Levite. The ordinary man, too, is served[^3] by one who has already sanctified himself, and thus Israel are set apart in holiness to serve the Holy One, blessed be He.’ ^emor-11-36

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Emor 12:39–12:40.*

[^1]: The first 23 lines of the Hebrew text are not found in our translation.
[^2]: v. T. B. Niddah, 13a.
[^3]: i.e. should have water poured over his hands, v. Shulcan Aruch.
