## Mūla

हस्तपादौ जलेनैव प्रक्षाल्याचमनादिकम् ।
तर्पणं जप इत्येवं भस्मस्नानं हि तैजसम् ॥ ^mula

hastapādau jalenaiva prakṣālyācamanādikam |
tarpaṇaṃ japa ityevaṃ bhasmasnānaṃ hi taijasam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 03 - Chapter Fifteen, part 2#^pg62|p. 44]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=62>))
>
> After he has repeated the (Mantra of the) Weapon (Phaṭ) (seven times) over the ash of a (sacrificial) fire, the impurity (mala) within the head, mouth, heart, genitals and feet is destroyed. The ash is consecrated (mantrita) with the Five Limbs. Then one should throw a handful of the ash, consecrated with the root Mantra and the limbs, onto the head. After one has sprinkled water on the hands and feet, one should sip it (ācamana) etc. This is followed by libation (tarpaṇa) and the repetition of Mantra (japa). Such is the radiantly powerful (taijasa) ablution with ashes. (with 15.53cd–55)
