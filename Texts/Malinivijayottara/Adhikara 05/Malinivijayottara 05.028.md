> **Lords of the Maṇḍalas reside in the Principle of Māyā**
>
> Those who are called the Lords of the Maṇḍalas reside in the Principle of Māyā. They are 1) Mahātejas and the rest (namely) 2) Vāmadeva, 3) Bhavodbhava (Born of Fettered Existence), 4) Ekapiṅgekṣaṇa (One Brown Eye), 5) Īśāna (Lord), 6) Bhuvaneśa (Lord of the Worlds), 7) Puraḥsara (Foremost) along with 8) Aṅguṣṭhamātra (Just (the measure of) a Thumb). They are as radiant as the Fire of Time.[^48] (with 5.28–29)

[^48]: MV 5/28-29 is quoted TĀv ad 8/299-300 (298cd-300ab).
