# Sirach 17 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Sirach 16|← Sirach 16]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Sirach 18|Sirach 18 →]]

**17:1** The Lord created humans out of earth and makes them return to it again. ^17-1

**17:2** He gave them a fixed number of days but granted them authority over everything on the earth.^[Lat: Gk *it*] ^17-2

**17:3** He endowed them with strength like his own^[Lat: Gk *proper to them*] and made them in his own image. ^17-3

**17:4** He put the fear of them^[Syr: Gk *him*] in all living beings and gave them dominion over beasts and birds.^[Other ancient authorities add as 17.5: *They obtained the use of the five faculties of the Lord; as sixth he distributed to them the gift of mind, and as seventh, reason, the interpreter of one’s faculties.*] ^17-4

**17:6** Discretion and tongue and eyes, ears and a mind for thinking he gave them. ^17-6

**17:7** He filled them with knowledge and understanding and showed them good and evil. ^17-7

**17:8** He put the fear of him into^[Other ancient authorities read *He set his eye upon*] their hearts to show them the majesty of his works.^[Other ancient authorities add *and he gave them to boast of his marvels forever*] ^17-8

**17:10** And they will praise his holy name, ^17-10

**17:9** to proclaim the grandeur of his works. ^17-9

**17:11** He bestowed knowledge upon them and allotted to them the law of life.^[Other ancient authorities add *so that they may know that they who are alive now are mortal*] ^17-11

**17:12** He established with them an eternal covenant and revealed to them his decrees. ^17-12

**17:13** Their eyes saw his glorious majesty, and their ears heard the glory of his voice. ^17-13

**17:14** He said to them, “Beware of all iniquity.” And he gave commandment to each of them concerning the neighbor. ^17-14

**17:15** Their ways are always before him; they will not be hidden from his eyes.^[Other ancient authorities add *16Their ways from youth tend toward evil, and they are unable to make for themselves hearts of flesh in place of their stony hearts. 17For in the division of the nations of the whole earth,*] ^17-15

**17:17** He appointed a ruler for every nation, but Israel is the Lord’s own portion.^[Other ancient authorities add as 17.18: *whom, being his firstborn, he brings up with discipline, and allotting to him the light of his love, he does not neglect him.*] ^17-17

**17:19** All their works are as the sun before him, and his eyes are ever upon their ways. ^17-19

**17:20** Their iniquities are not hidden from him, and all their sins are before the Lord.^[Other ancient authorities add as 17.21: *But the Lord, who is gracious and knows how they are formed, has neither left them nor abandoned them but has spared them.*] ^17-20

**17:22** One’s almsgiving is like a signet ring with the Lord,^[Gk *him*] and he will keep a person’s kindness like the apple of his eye.^[Other ancient authorities add *apportioning repentance to his sons and daughters*] ^17-22

**17:23** Afterward he will rise up and repay them, and he will bring their recompense on their heads. ^17-23

**17:24** Yet to those who repent he grants a return, and he encourages those who are losing hope. ^17-24

## A call to repentance

**17:25** Turn back to the Lord and forsake your sins; pray in his presence and lessen your offense. ^17-25

**17:26** Return to the Most High and turn away from iniquity^[Other ancient authorities add *for he will lead you out of darkness to the light of health.*] and hate intensely what is abhorrent. ^17-26

**17:27** Who will sing praises to the Most High in Hades in place of the living who give thanks? ^17-27

**17:28** From the dead, as from one who does not exist, thanksgiving has ceased; those who are alive and well sing the Lord’s praises. ^17-28

**17:29** How great is the mercy of the Lord and his forgiveness for those who return to him! ^17-29

**17:30** For not everything is within human capability, since human beings are not immortal. ^17-30

**17:31** What is brighter than the sun? Yet it can be eclipsed. So flesh and blood devise evil. ^17-31

**17:32** He marshals the host of the height of heaven, but all humans are dust and ashes. ^17-32
