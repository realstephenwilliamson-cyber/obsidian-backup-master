# Zohar 3.037b

← [[Zohar 3.037a]] · [[Zohar (Soncino)]] · [[Zohar 3.038a]] →

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 7.05 - Parashat Shemini#^z3-37b|Parashat Shemini, at 3:37b]]

*Volume 3, folio 37b* · Shmini

*Passage Shmini 4:31 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 3.037a#^shmini-4-31|Shmini 4:31]].*

**Shmini 4:32** Israel, who shared the sin of the priest, had to bring a similar offering, viz., “an ox and a ram for peace offerings, to sacrifice before the Lord”, an ox for their sin and a ram to complete this place with the perfection of Isaac. ^shmini-4-32

**Shmini 4:33** The term “sin-offering” is not used in connection with Israel as with the priest, because Israel had already suffered their punishment in many places, and therefore God did not wish to mention their sin; hence it is written here, “for a peace offering” and not “for a sin-offering”, as a sign of peace, to show that God had been reconciled with Israel in respect of this. ^shmini-4-33

**Shmini 4:34** Aaron, however, who had been saved from punishment through the prayer of Moses (v. Deut. 9, 20), and whose guilt still hung over him, had to bring a sin-offering, that his sin might be atoned for and that he should be purified and whole. ^shmini-4-34

**Shmini 4:35** On that day upper and lower beings reached their perfection, and there was peace everywhere in the joy of heaven and earth, and but for the tragedy of the sons of Aaron on that day there would have been no such joy above and below from the day that the Israelites came up from the Red Sea. On that day the sin of the Golden Calf was wiped out, and priests and people were cleansed of it. On that day all the accusers above were silenced, and they circled round Israel without being able to find any spot at which to attack them ^shmini-4-35

**Shmini 4:36** until the hour arrived when Nadab and Abihu rose up and spoilt the general joy, and wrath descended on the world. ^shmini-4-36

**Shmini 4:37** We have learnt that on that day it was the joy of the Community of Israel to be united [with her Spouse] by the bond of faith with all holy ties (for the incense binds all-hence its name),[^1] and they came and linked all the others together and left Her outside, and linked up something else.’ ^shmini-4-37

**Shmini 4:38** Said R. Phineas to him: ‘Do not say that they left her outside, but only that the Community of Israel was not united [to the rest] through them,[^2] for wherever male and female are not found together, the Community of Israel does not abide. Therefore it is written, “With this shall Aaron come into the sanctuary” (Lev. 16, 3), which was a warning to him that he should be male with female; and therefore a priest should not enter the sanctuary till he is married, in order that he may have a share in the union of the Community of Israel.’ R. Jose referred in this connection to the verse: “As a lily among thorns, so is my love among the daughters” (S.S. 2, 2). ^shmini-4-38

**Shmini 4:39** As has been explained,’ he said, ‘this verse is spoken by the Holy One, blessed be He, in praise of the Community of Israel in His love and desire for Her, and therefore it is a married man who should utter the praises of the Holy One, blessed be He, and of the Community of Israel. For, since he cleaves to his spouse and devotes his affection to her, when he comes to serve before the Holy God he awakens another love, that of the Holy One, blessed be He, for the Community of Israel. And for this the Holy One blesses him and the Community of Israel blesses him. ^shmini-4-39

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Shmini 4:40.*

[^1]: *Ketoreth*, connected with *katar*, Aramaic for “bind”.
[^2]: Because they were not married.
