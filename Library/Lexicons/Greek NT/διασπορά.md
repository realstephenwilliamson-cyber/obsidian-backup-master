# διασπορά

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · noun

**Dodson:** dispersion — lit: scattering abroad of seed by the sower, hence: dispersion, used especially of the Jews who had migrated and were scattered over the ancient world. (Strong's 1290; headword διασπορά, ᾶς, ἡ)
