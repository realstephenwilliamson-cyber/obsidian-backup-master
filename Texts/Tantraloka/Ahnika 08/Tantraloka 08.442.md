## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 11 - Chapter Eight, part 9#^pg408|p. 390]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=408>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 8.442cd–443ab.
>
> A) The Group of Eight Lords along with their entourage resides in the Water element, and is made up of 1) Lakulīśa (Lakulin), 2) Bhārabhūti, 3) Diṇḍin, 4) Āṣādhin, 5) Puṣkara (Lake), 6) Nimeṣa (Naimiṣa), 7) Prabhāsa (Shining One) and 8) Sureśa (Lord of the Gods, Amareśa, Immortal Lord). B) The group of eight Secrets (guhyāṣṭaka) resides in Fire. It consists of 1) Bhairava, 2) Kedāra, 3) Mahākāla, 4) Madhyama, 5) Āmra (Amrātakeśa), 6) Jalpa (Jalpeśa), 7) Śrīśaila and 8) Hariścandra (Harīndu). C) The group of eight Great Secrets (atiguhyāṣṭaka) resides in the Wind. It consists of 1) Bhīma (Bhīmeśvara), 2) Indra (Mahendra), 3) Aṭṭahāsa, 4) Vimala (Vimaleśvara), 5) Kanakhala, 6) Nākhala, 7) Kurusthiti (Kurukṣetra) and 8) Gaya. D) The group of Pure Ones who reside in the space of the subtle elements and senses are 1) Sthāṇu, 2) Svarṇākṣaka, 3) Bhadra, 4) Gokarṇaka, 5) Mahālaya, 6) Avimukta (Avimukteśa, the Lord of Avimukta, the Never Abandoned), 7) Rudrakoṭi and 8) Vastrāpada (Ambarāpada). E) The group of eight in the ego consists of 1) Sthūla, 2) Sthūleśvara, 3) Śaṅkuśruti (Śaṅkukarṇa, Conch Ears), 4) Kālañjara, 5) Maṇḍalabhṛt (Maṇḍaleśvara), 6) Mākoṭa, 7) Dviraṇḍa and 8) Chagalāṇḍa. (with 8.440cd–445ab)
