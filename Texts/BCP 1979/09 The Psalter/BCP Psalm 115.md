# Psalm 115

← [[BCP Psalm 114]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 116]] →

*Non nobis, Domine* · Book Five · Twenty-third Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 115]]

**115:1** Not to us, O LORD, not to us,  
but to your Name give glory; \*  
because of your love and because of your faithfulness. ^115-1

**115:2** Why should the heathen say, \*  
"Where then is their God?" ^115-2

**115:3** Our God is in heaven; \*  
whatever he wills to do he does. ^115-3

**115:4** Their idols are silver and gold, \*  
the work of human hands. ^115-4

**115:5** They have mouths, but they cannot speak; \*  
eyes have they, but they cannot see; ^115-5

**115:6** They have ears but they cannot hear; \*  
noses, but they cannot smell; ^115-6

[p. 758] ^pg758

**115:7** They have hands, but they cannot feel;  
feet, but they cannot walk; \*  
they make no sound with their throat. ^115-7

**115:8** Those who make them are like them, \*  
and so are all who put their trust in them. ^115-8

**115:9** O Israel, trust in the LORD; \*  
he is their help and their shield. ^115-9

**115:10** O house of Aaron, trust in the LORD; \*  
he is their help and their shield. ^115-10

**115:11** You who fear the LORD, trust in the LORD; \*  
he is their help and their shield. ^115-11

**115:12** The LORD has been mindful of us, and he will bless us; \*  
he will bless the house of Israel;  
he will bless the house of Aaron; ^115-12

**115:13** He will bless those who fear the LORD, \*  
both small and great together. ^115-13

**115:14** May the LORD increase you more and more, \*  
you and your children after you. ^115-14

**115:15** May you be blessed by the LORD, \*  
the maker of heaven and earth. ^115-15

**115:16** The heaven of heavens is the LORD'S, \*  
but he entrusted the earth to its peoples. ^115-16

**115:17** The dead do not praise the LORD, \*  
nor all those who go down into silence; ^115-17

**115:18** But we will bless the LORD, \*  
from this time forth for evermore.  
Hallelujah! ^115-18
