# Psalms 66 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 65|← Psalms 65]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 67|Psalms 67 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 66|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**66:1** Unto the end, in, hymns, a psalm of a canticle for David. ^66-1

**66:2** May God have mercy on us, and bless us: may he cause the light of his countenance to shine upon us, and may he have mercy on us. ^66-2

**66:3** That we may know thy way upon earth: thy salvation in all nations. ^66-3

**66:4** Let people confess to thee, O God: let all people give praise to thee. ^66-4

**66:5** Let the nations be glad and rejoice: for thou judgest the people with justice, and directest the nations upon earth. ^66-5

**66:6** Let the people, O God, confess to thee: let all the people give praise to thee: ^66-6

**66:7** The earth hath yielded her fruit. May God, our God bless us, ^66-7

**66:8** May God bless us: and all the ends of the earth fear him. ^66-8
