#### 50. On Being Poor (John Brough; V.N. Misra, L. Nathan and S. Vatsyayan)

Ah, Poverty, I mourn for your sad fate. 

So long ago you claimed me as your friend, And ever since you have been my constant guest. 

Where wil l you find a home when I am dead?
