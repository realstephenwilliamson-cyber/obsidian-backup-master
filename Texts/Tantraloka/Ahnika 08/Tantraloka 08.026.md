## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 03 - Chapter Eight, part 1#^pg99|p. 81]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=99>))
>
> The flaming fire of the All-pervasive Lord extends for one hundred million (daśakoṭi) leagues. The upper half of that is empty (and no living creature resides there). Above that reside, in due order, the Lords of the Hells, who experience nothing but suffering. Below, in the middle and above that, surrounded by other (minor) divisions, are (the hells) called Avīci, Kumbhīpāka' and Raurava. Within them are eleven, eleven and ten (hells), respectively. Thus, including them, they make thirty-five (hells) in all. Each one (of these hells) extends for nine million and nine hundred thousand (leagues); (the space) in between (each one extends for) one hundred thousand (leagues). Altogether (these hells) number one hundred and forty. Above (these hells) is Kūṣmāṇda, who lords over a world that extends for nine million and nine hundred thousand leagues. (with 8.24cd–28ab)
