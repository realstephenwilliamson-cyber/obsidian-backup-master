## Chapter 2

**2:1** These are the sons of Israel: Reuben, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, Zebulun, ^2-1

**2:2** Dan, Joseph, Benjamin, Naphtali, Gad, and Asher. ^2-2

**2:3** The sons of Judah: Er, Onan, and Shelah; these three, Bath-shua the Canaanite woman bore to him. But Er, Judah’s first-born, was displeasing to the LORD, and He took his life. ^2-3

**2:4** His daughter-in-law Tamar also bore him Perez and Zerah. Judah’s sons were five in all. ^2-4

**2:5** The sons of Perez: Hezron and Hamul. ^2-5

**2:6** The sons of Zerah: Zimri, Ethan, Heman, Calcol, and Dara, five in all. ^2-6

**2:7** The sons of Carmi: Achar, the troubler of Israel, who committed a trespass against the proscribed thing; ^2-7

**2:8** and Ethan’s son was Azariah. ^2-8

**2:9** The sons of Hezron that were born to him: Jerahmeel, Ram, and Chelubai. ^2-9

**2:10** Ram begot Amminadab, and Amminadab begot Nahshon, prince of the sons of Judah. ^2-10

**2:11** Nahshon was the father of Salma, Salma of Boaz, ^2-11

**2:12** Boaz of Obed, Obed of Jesse. ^2-12

**2:13** Jesse begot Eliab his first-born, Abinadab the second, Shimea the third, ^2-13

**2:14** Nethanel the fourth, Raddai the fifth, ^2-14

**2:15** Ozem the sixth, David the seventh; ^2-15

**2:16** their sisters were Zeruiah and Abigail. The sons of Zeruiah: Abishai, Joab, and Asahel, three. ^2-16

**2:17** Abigail bore Amasa, and the father of Amasa was Jether the Ishmaelite. ^2-17

**2:18** Caleb son of Hezron had children by his wife Azubah, and by Jerioth; these were her sons: Jesher, Shobab, and Ardon. ^2-18

**2:19** When Azubah died, Caleb married Ephrath, who bore him Hur. ^2-19

**2:20** Hur begot Uri, and Uri begot Bezalel. ^2-20

**2:21** Afterward Hezron had relations with the daughter of Machir father of Gilead—he had married her when he was sixty years old—and she bore him Segub; ^2-21

**2:22** and Segub begot Jair; he had twenty-three cities in the land of Gilead. ^2-22

**2:23** But Geshur and Aram took from them Havvoth-jair, Kenath and its dependencies, sixty towns. All these were the sons of Machir, the father of Gilead. ^2-23

**2:24** After the death of Hezron, in Caleb-ephrathah, Abijah, wife of Hezron, bore Ashhur, the father of Tekoa. ^2-24

**2:25** The sons of Jerahmeel the first-born of Hezron: Ram his first-born, Bunah, Oren, Ozem, and Ahijah. ^2-25

**2:26** Jerahmeel had another wife, whose name was Atarah; she was the mother of Onam. ^2-26

**2:27** The sons of Ram the first-born of Jerahmeel: Maaz, Jamin, and Eker. ^2-27

**2:28** The sons of Onam: Shammai and Jada. The sons of Shammai: Nadab and Abishur. ^2-28

**2:29** The name of Abishur’s wife was Abihail, and she bore him Ahban and Molid. ^2-29

**2:30** The sons of Nadab: Seled and Appaim; Seled died childless. ^2-30

**2:31** The sons of Appaim: Ishi. The sons of Ishi: Sheshan. The sons of Sheshan: Ahlai. ^2-31

**2:32** The sons of Jada, Shammai’s brother: Jether and Jonathan; Jether died childless. ^2-32

**2:33** The sons of Jonathan: Peleth and Zaza. These were the descendants of Jerahmeel. ^2-33

**2:34** Sheshan had no sons, only daughters; Sheshan had an Egyptian slave, whose name was Jarha. ^2-34

**2:35** So Sheshan gave his daughter in marriage to Jarha his slave; and she bore him Attai. ^2-35

**2:36** Attai begot Nathan, and Nathan begot Zabad. ^2-36

**2:37** Zabad begot Ephlal, and Ephlal begot Obed. ^2-37

**2:38** Obed begot Jehu, and Jehu begot Azariah. ^2-38

**2:39** Azariah begot Helez, and Helez begot Eleasah. ^2-39

**2:40** Eleasah begot Sisamai, and Sisamai begot Shallum. ^2-40

**2:41** Shallum begot Jekamiah, and Jekamiah begot Elishama. ^2-41

**2:42** The sons of Caleb brother of Jerahmeel: Meshah his first-born, who was the father of Ziph. The sons of Mareshah father of Hebron. ^2-42

**2:43** The sons of Hebron: Korah, Tappuah, Rekem, and Shema. ^2-43

**2:44** Shema begot Raham the father of Jorkeam, and Rekem begot Shammai. ^2-44

**2:45** The son of Shammai: Maon, and Maon begot Bethzur. ^2-45

**2:46** Ephah, Caleb’s concubine, bore Haran, Moza, and Gazez; Haran begot Gazez. ^2-46

**2:47** The sons of Jahdai: Regem, Jotham, Geshan, Pelet, Ephah, and Shaaph. ^2-47

**2:48** Maacah, Caleb’s concubine, bore Sheber and Tirhanah. ^2-48

**2:49** She also bore Shaaph father of Madmannah, Sheva father of Machbenah and father of Gibea; the daughter of Caleb was Achsah. ^2-49

**2:50** These were the descendants of Caleb.  
The sons of Hur the first-born of Ephrathah: Shobal father of Kiriath-jearim, ^2-50

**2:51** Salma father of Bethlehem, Hareph father of Beth-gader. ^2-51

**2:52** Shobal father of Kiriath-jearim had sons: Haroeh, half of the Menuhoth. ^2-52

**2:53** And the families of Kiriath-jearim: the Ithrites, the Puthites, the Shumathites, and the Mishraites; from these came the Zorathites and the Eshtaolites. ^2-53

**2:54** The sons of Salma: Bethlehem, the Netophathites, Atroth-beth-joab, and half of the Manahathites, the Zorites. ^2-54

**2:55** The families of the scribes that dwelt at Jabez: the Tirathites, the Shimeathites, the Sucathites; these are the Kenites who came from Hammath, father of the house of Rechab. ^2-55
