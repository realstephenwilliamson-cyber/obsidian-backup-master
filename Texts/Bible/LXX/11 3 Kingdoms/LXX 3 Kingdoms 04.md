# LXX 3 Kingdoms 04

← [[LXX 3 Kingdoms 03]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX 3 Kingdoms 05]] →

*3 Kingdoms · [[LXX]]*

**4:1** And king Solomon reigned over Israel.  ^4-1

**4:2** And these *are* the princes which he had; Azarias son of Sadoc.  ^4-2

**4:3** Eliaph, and Achia son of Seba, scribes; and Josaphat son of Achilud, recorder.  ^4-3

**4:4** And Banæas son of Jodae over the host; and Sadoc and Abiathar *were* priests.  ^4-4

**4:5** And Ornia the son of Nathan *was* over the officers; and Zabuth son of Nathan *was* the king's friend.[^1][^2][^3]  ^4-5

**4:6** And Achisar was steward, and Eliac the *chief* steward; and Eliab the son of Saph *was* over the family: and Adoniram the son of Audon over the tribute.  ^4-6

**4:7** And Solomon had twelve officers over all Israel, to provide for the king and his household; each one's turn came to supply for a month in the year.  ^4-7

**4:8** And these *were* their names: Been the son of Or in the mount of Ephraim, one.  ^4-8

**4:9** The son of Dacar, in Makes, and in Salabin, and Bæthsamys, and Elon as far as Bethanan, one.  ^4-9

**4:10** The son of Esdi in Araboth; his *was* Socho, and all the land of Opher.  ^4-10

**4:11** All Nephthador *belonged to* the son of Aminadab, Tephath daughter of Solomon was his wife, one.  ^4-11

**4:12** Bana son of Achiluth *had* Ithaanach, and Mageddo, and *his was* the whole house of San which was by Sesathan below Esrae, and from Bethsan as far as Sabelmaula, as far as Maëber Lucam, one.  ^4-12

**4:13** The son of Naber in Raboth Galaad, to him *fell* the lot of Ergab in Basan, sixty great cities with walls, and brazen bars, one.  ^4-13

**4:14** Achinadab son of Saddo, *had* Maanaim.  ^4-14

**4:15** Achimaas *was* in Nephthalim, and he took Basemmath daughter of Solomon to wife, one.  ^4-15

**4:16** Baana son of Chusi, in Aser and in Baaloth, one,  ^4-16

**4:17** Semei son of Ela, in Benjamin.  ^4-17

**4:18** Gaber son of Adai in the land of Gad, *the land* of Seon king of Esebon, and of Og king of Basan, and one officer in the land of Juda.  ^4-18

**4:19** Josaphat son of Phuasud *was* in Issachar.  ^4-19

[^1]: 4:5 *Gr.* appointed ones.
[^2]: 4:5 *Heb.* and *Alex.* insert 'priest.'
[^3]: 4:5 *Or,* companion.
