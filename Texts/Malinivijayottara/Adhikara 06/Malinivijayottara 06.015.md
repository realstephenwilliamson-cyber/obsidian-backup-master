## Mūla

उत्तरादिक्रमाद्द्व्येकभेदो विद्यादिके त्रये ।
काले प्रत्येकमुद्दिष्टमेकैकं तु यथाक्रमम् ॥ ^mula

uttarādikramād dvyekabhedo vidyādike traye |

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2024 – April 2024
> p. 28 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Malini (journal), January-April 2024.pdf#page=30>))
>
> In the three (principles) beginning with (limited) Knowledge (there are two worlds each which), from the upper one, successively take up, two and one fingers (space each. Thus, making nine altogether).[^19] (with 6.15ab)

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2024 – April 2024
> p. 28 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Malini (journal), January-April 2024.pdf#page=30>))
>
> The three worlds of Time occupy three fingers. The eight Lords of the Maṇḍala (up to Aṅguṣṭhamātra) occupy half a finger each. Beyond them there are three worlds, each one of which occupies two thirds of a finger, and two others that occupy a finger each. The same happens in the case of the eight worlds (of the Īśvara principle) and the five (of Sadāśiva). Thus, the former occupy half a finger each and the latter three within two fingers' space, and two in the other two fingers). One should think of Śiva (to be pervasive and *ni.skala*) as before, here in this procedure concerning the Path of the Worlds.[^20] (with 6.15cd–18ab)

[^19]: MV 6/15ab is quoted in TĀ 16/120cd. Attachment, Vidyā and Kalā each contain two worlds that extend 2 + 1 finger breadths within the body.
[^20]: TĀ 16/114-131 The Projection of the Path of the Worlds onto the Body (*bhuvanādhvan*). TĀ 16/114 to 119 is an extended explanation of MV 6/11cd-14. TĀ 16/1 21-127 is an expanded, filled out version of MV 6/15cd-18ab.
