## Mūla

तदेव जगदानन्दमस्मभ्यं शंभुरूचिवान् ।
तत्र विश्रान्तिराधेया हृदयोच्चारयोगतः ॥ ^mula

tadeva jagadānandamasmabhyaṃ śaṃbhurūcivān |
tatra viśrāntirādheyā hṛdayoccārayogataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 04 - Chapter Five, part 2#^pg63|p. 48]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=63>))

> 6) Śambhunātha has taught us that where there is no limitation (vyavaccheda) at all, that which shines radiantly everywhere, its consciousness, from which nothing has been subtracted, fed by the Supreme Nectar (of freedom), wherein meditation (bhāvanā) or the like finds no direct application, is Cosmic Bliss (jagadānanda). (with 5.50cd–52ab)
>
> Repose therein can (also) be achieved through the utterance from the Heart (hrdayoccāāra). (with 5.52cd)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* नचैतदुपदेशमात्रादेव विरन्तव्यम् -- इत्याह --

> तत्र विश्रान्तिराधेया हृदयोच्चारयोगतः ॥
