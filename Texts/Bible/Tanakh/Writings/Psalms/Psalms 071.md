## Chapter 71

**71:1** I seek refuge in You, O LORD;  
may I never be disappointed. ^71-1

**71:2** As You are beneficent, save me and rescue me;  
incline Your ear to me and deliver me. ^71-2

**71:3** Be a sheltering rock for me to which I may always repair;  
decree my deliverance,  
for You are my rock and my fortress. ^71-3

**71:4** My God, rescue me from the hand of the wicked,  
from the grasp of the unjust and the lawless. ^71-4

**71:5** For You are my hope,  
O Lord GOD,  
my trust from my youth. ^71-5

**71:6** While yet unborn, I depended on You;  
in the womb of my mother, You were my support;[^1]  
I sing Your praises always. ^71-6

**71:7** I have become an example for many,  
since You are my mighty refuge. ^71-7

**71:8** My mouth is full of praise to You,  
glorifying You all day long. ^71-8

**71:9** Do not cast me off in old age;  
when my strength fails, do not forsake me! ^71-9

**71:10** For my enemies talk against me;  
those who wait for me are of one mind, ^71-10

**71:11** saying, “God has forsaken him;  
chase him and catch him,  
for no one will save him!” ^71-11

**71:12** O God, be not far from me;  
my God, hasten to my aid! ^71-12

**71:13** Let my accusers perish in frustration;  
let those who seek my ruin be clothed in reproach and disgrace! ^71-13

**71:14** As for me, I will hope always,  
and add to the many praises of You. ^71-14

**71:15** My mouth tells of Your beneficence,  
of Your deliverance all day long,  
though I know not how to tell it. ^71-15

**71:16** I come with praise of Your mighty acts, O Lord GOD;  
I celebrate Your beneficence, Yours alone. ^71-16

**71:17** You have let me experience it, God, from my youth;  
until now I have proclaimed Your wondrous deeds, ^71-17

**71:18** and even in hoary old age do not forsake me, God,  
until I proclaim Your strength to the next generation,  
Your mighty acts, to all who are to come, ^71-18

**71:19** Your beneficence, high as the heavens, O God,  
You who have done great things;  
O God, who is Your peer! ^71-19

**71:20** You who have made me undergo many troubles and misfortunes  
will revive me again,  
and raise me up from the depths of the earth. ^71-20

**71:21** You will grant me much greatness,  
You will turn and comfort me. ^71-21

**71:22** Then I will acclaim You to the music of the lyre  
for Your faithfulness, O my God;  
I will sing a hymn to You with a harp,  
O Holy One of Israel. ^71-22

**71:23** My lips shall be jubilant, as I sing a hymn to You,  
my whole being, which You have redeemed. ^71-23

**71:24** All day long my tongue shall recite Your beneficent acts,  
how those who sought my ruin were frustrated and disgraced. ^71-24

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
