## Mūla

उक्तमेवेति शास्त्रेऽस्मिन् गुणांस्तत्त्वान्तरं विदुः ।
भुवनं पृथगेवात्र दर्शितं गुणभेदतः ॥ ^mula

uktameveti śāstre'smin guṇāṃstattvāntaraṃ viduḥ |
bhuvanaṃ pṛthagevātra darśitaṃ guṇabhedataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 08 - Chapter Nine, part 6#^pg228|p. 211]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=228>))
>
> When these qualities are disturbed, they generate their product. This is because a product of a differing type (from its cause) cannot originate from one which is stable in itself (lit. undisturbed). We said before that, in the scripture (we follow, the wise) know that the qualities are an independent principle (from Nature). (with 9.223–224ab)
>
> (Accordingly,) a separate world-order has been described here (in this book), associated with the Qualities. (with 9.224cd)
