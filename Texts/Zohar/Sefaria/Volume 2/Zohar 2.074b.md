# Zohar 2.074b

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 4.07 - Parashat Yitro#^z2-74b|Parashat Yitro, at 2:74b]]

*Volume 2, folio 74b* · Yitro

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Yitro 8:102.*

**Yitro 8:103** \2. When a man, not entirely bad, changes his ways and turns to the Lord, a good spirit begins to rest upon him, so that he is enabled to prevail against the evil that was in him, and for a time this new spirit thrusts itself forward into the expression of his face in the form of a *lion*. At a first casual examination his face would not inspire love, but gradually it becomes better comprehended and so better loved. ^yitro-8-103

**Yitro 8:104** When people look at him a sense of shame for his past misdeeds comes over him, for he feels that everyone knows his former evil ways, and the blood rushes to his face, and then again he turns pale. ^yitro-8-104

**Yitro 8:105** On his face are three veins, one on the right, one going up to the bridge of the nose, and a third which is joined thereto and branches downward from them. These veins form the shapes of the letters which are traced upon his face. They usually stand out prominently and quiver, but when he is penitent and gradually becomes accustomed to walking in the way of truth they subside. ^yitro-8-105

**Yitro 8:106** The mystery of these letters is contained in the word *Karib (Koph, Resh, Yod, Beth*, “near”), signifying that he had been *far* from holiness. Although there are also other veins in his face, these do not protrude, except when he treads the paths of crookedness and iniquity. ^yitro-8-106

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Yitro 12:226.*

**Yitro 12:227** When a man, not entirely bad, changes his ways and turns to the Lord, a good spirit begins to rest upon him, so that he is enabled to prevail against the evil that was in him, and for a time this new spirit thrusts itself forward into the expression of his face in the form of a *lion*. ^yitro-12-227

**Yitro 12:228** At a first casual examination his face would not inspire love, but gradually it becomes better comprehended and so better loved. When people look at him a sense of shame for his past misdeeds comes over him, for he feels that everyone knows his former evil ways, and the blood rushes to his face, and then again he turns pale. ^yitro-12-228

**Yitro 12:229** On his face are three veins, one on the right, one going up to the bridge of the nose, and a third which is joined thereto and branches downward from them. These veins form the shapes of the letters which are traced upon his face. They usually stand out prominently and quiver, but when he is penitent and gradually becomes accustomed to walking in the way of truth they subside. ^yitro-12-229

**Yitro 12:230** The mystery of these letters is contained in the word *Karib* (*Koph, Resh, Yod, Beth*, “near”), signifying that he had been *far* from holiness. ^yitro-12-230

**Yitro 12:231** Although there are also other veins in his face, these do not protrude, except when he treads the paths of crookedness and iniquity. Here again the seed of David are the reverse of other men, first appearing in the form of Man and then in that of Lion, finally separating and taking the form of the “other side”. ^yitro-12-231

**Yitro 12:232** \3. When a man deserts the ways of the Torah and follows ways of iniquity, the holy spirit, which formerly dwelt in his inner self, removes its influence from him and another spirit takes up its abode there, with another form which impresses itself on the outer lineaments and is there manifested to the vision of the wise in the form of an Ox. ^yitro-12-232
