# Job 42 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Job 41|← Job 41]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · —

## Job is humbled and satisfied

**42:1** Then Job answered the LORD: ^42-1

**42:2** “I know that you can do all things and that no purpose of yours can be thwarted. ^42-2

**42:3** ‘Who is this that hides counsel without knowledge?’ Therefore I have uttered what I did not understand, things too wonderful for me that I did not know. ^42-3

**42:4** ‘Hear, and I will speak; I will question you, and you declare to me.’ ^42-4

**42:5** I had heard of you by the hearing of the ear, but now my eye sees you; ^42-5

**42:6** therefore I despise myself and repent in dust and ashes.” ^42-6

## Job’s friends are humiliated

**42:7** After the LORD had spoken these words to Job, the LORD said to Eliphaz the Temanite: “My wrath is kindled against you and against your two friends, for you have not spoken of me what is right, as my servant Job has. ^42-7

**42:8** Now therefore take seven bulls and seven rams, and go to my servant Job, and offer up for yourselves a burnt offering, and my servant Job shall pray for you, for I will accept his prayer not to deal with you according to your folly, for you have not spoken of me what is right, as my servant Job has done.” ^42-8

**42:9** So Eliphaz the Temanite and Bildad the Shuhite and Zophar the Naamathite went and did what the LORD had told them, and the LORD accepted Job’s prayer. ^42-9

## Job’s fortunes are restored twofold

**42:10** And the LORD restored the fortunes of Job when he had prayed for his friends, and the LORD gave Job twice as much as he had before. ^42-10

**42:11** Then there came to him all his brothers and sisters and all who had known him before, and they ate bread with him in his house; they showed him sympathy and comforted him for all the evil that the LORD had brought upon him; and each of them gave him a piece of money^[Heb *a qesitah*] and a gold ring. ^42-11

**42:12** The LORD blessed the latter days of Job more than his beginning, and he had fourteen thousand sheep, six thousand camels, a thousand yoke of oxen, and a thousand donkeys. ^42-12

**42:13** He also had seven sons and three daughters. ^42-13

**42:14** He named the first Jemimah, the second Keziah, and the third Keren-happuch. ^42-14

**42:15** In all the land there were no women so beautiful as Job’s daughters, and their father gave them an inheritance along with their brothers. ^42-15

**42:16** After this Job lived one hundred and forty years and saw his children and his children’s children, four generations. ^42-16

**42:17** And Job died, old and full of days. *(Psalms 1–41)* ^42-17
