## Chapter 63

**63:1** Who is this coming from Edom,  
In crimsoned garments from Bozrah—  
Who is this, majestic in attire,  
Pressing forward[^1] in His great might?  
“It is I, who contend victoriously,  
Powerful to give triumph.”[^2] ^63-1

**63:2** Why is your clothing so red,  
Your garments like his who treads grapes?[^3] ^63-2

**63:3** “I trod out a vintage alone;  
Of the peoples[^4] no man was with Me.  
I trod them down in My anger,  
Trampled them in My rage;  
Their life-blood[^5] bespattered My garments,  
And all My clothing was stained. ^63-3

**63:4** For I had planned a day of vengeance,  
And My year of redemption arrived. ^63-4

**63:5** Then I looked, but there was none to help;  
I stared, but there was none to aid—  
So My own arm wrought the triumph,  
And My own rage[^6] was My aid. ^63-5

**63:6** I trampled peoples in My anger,  
I made them drunk with[^7] My rage,  
And I hurled their glory to the ground.” ^63-6

**63:7** I will recount the kind acts of the LORD,  
The praises of the LORD—  
For all that the LORD has wrought for us,  
The vast bounty to the House of Israel  
That He bestowed upon them  
According to His mercy and His great kindness. ^63-7

**63:8** He thought: Surely they are My people,  
Children who will not play false.  
[^8]So He was their Deliverer. ^63-8

**63:9** In all their troubles He was troubled,  
And the angel of His Presence delivered them.  
In His love and pity  
He Himself redeemed them,  
Raised them, and exalted them  
All the days of old. ^63-9

**63:10** But they rebelled, and grieved  
His holy spirit;  
Then He became their enemy,  
And Himself made war against them. ^63-10

**63:11** Then they[^9] remembered the ancient days,  
Him, who pulled His people[^10] out [of the water]:  
“Where is He who brought them up from the Sea  
Along with the shepherd[^11] of His flock?  
Where is He who put  
In their midst His holy spirit, ^63-11

**63:12** Who made His glorious arm  
March at the right hand of Moses,  
Who divided the waters before them  
To make Himself a name for all time, ^63-12

**63:13** Who led them through the deeps  
So that they did not stumble—  
As a horse in a desert, ^63-13

**63:14** Like a beast descending to the plain?”  
’Twas the spirit of the LORD gave them rest;[^12]  
Thus did You shepherd Your people  
To win for Yourself a glorious name. ^63-14

**63:15** Look down from heaven and see,  
From Your holy and glorious height!  
Where is Your zeal, Your power?  
Your yearning and Your love  
Are being withheld from us![^13] ^63-15

**63:16** Surely You are our Father:  
Though Abraham regard us not,  
And Israel recognize us not,  
You, O LORD, are our Father;  
From of old, Your name is “Our Redeemer.” ^63-16

**63:17** Why, LORD, do You make us stray from Your ways,  
And turn our hearts away from revering You?  
Relent for the sake of Your servants,  
The tribes that are Your very own! ^63-17

**63:18** Our foes have trampled Your Sanctuary,  
Which Your holy people possessed but a little while.[^14] ^63-18

**63:19** We have become as a people You never ruled,  
To which Your name was never attached.  
If You would but tear open the heavens and come down,  
So that mountains would quake before  
You— ^63-19

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain; emendation yields “striding.”
[^2]: Change of vocalization yields “Who contest triumphantly”; cf. 19.20.
[^3]: Lit. “in a press.”
[^4]: Emendation yields “Peoples, and…”
[^5]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^6]: Many mss. read weṣidqathi “My victorious [right hand]”; cf. 59.16.
[^7]: Many mss. and Targum read “I shattered them in”; cf. 14.25.
[^8]: Ancient versions read “So He was their Deliverer / 9In all their troubles. / No [so kethib] angel or messenger, / His own Presence delivered them.” Cf. Deut. 4.37 and note.
[^9]: Heb. “he.”
[^10]: Heb. moshe ‘ammo, a play on the name Moshe (Moses).
[^11]: So many mss. and ancient versions; other texts “shepherds.”
[^12]: Emendation yields “guided them.”
[^13]: Heb. “me.” Emendation yields “[Where are] Your yearning and Your love? / Let them not be restrained!”
[^14]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
