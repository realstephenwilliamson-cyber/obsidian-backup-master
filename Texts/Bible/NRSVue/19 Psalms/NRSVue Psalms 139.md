# Psalm 139 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 138|← Psalm 138]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 140|Psalm 140 →]]

## The inescapable God

*To the leader. Of David. A Psalm.*

**139:1** O LORD, you have searched me and known me. ^139-1

**139:2** You know when I sit down and when I rise up; you discern my thoughts from far away. ^139-2

**139:3** You search out my path and my lying down and are acquainted with all my ways. ^139-3

**139:4** Even before a word is on my tongue, O LORD, you know it completely. ^139-4

**139:5** You hem me in, behind and before, and lay your hand upon me. ^139-5

**139:6** Such knowledge is too wonderful for me; it is so high that I cannot attain it. ^139-6

**139:7** Where can I go from your spirit? Or where can I flee from your presence? ^139-7

**139:8** If I ascend to heaven, you are there; if I make my bed in Sheol, you are there. ^139-8

**139:9** If I take the wings of the morning and settle at the farthest limits of the sea, ^139-9

**139:10** even there your hand shall lead me, and your right hand shall hold me fast. ^139-10

**139:11** If I say, “Surely the darkness shall cover me, and night wraps itself around me,”^[Q ms: MT *and the light around me become night*] ^139-11

**139:12** even the darkness is not dark to you; the night is as bright as the day, for darkness is as light to you. ^139-12

**139:13** For it was you who formed my inward parts; you knit me together in my mother’s womb. ^139-13

**139:14** I praise you, for I am fearfully and wonderfully made. Wonderful are your works; that I know very well. ^139-14

**139:15** My frame was not hidden from you, when I was being made in secret, intricately woven in the depths of the earth. ^139-15

**139:16** Your eyes beheld my unformed substance. In your book were written all the days that were formed for me, when none of them as yet existed.^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^139-16

**139:17** How weighty to me are your thoughts, O God! How vast is the sum of them! ^139-17

**139:18** I try to count them—they are more than the sand; I come to the end^[Or *I awake*]—I am still with you. ^139-18

**139:19** O that you would kill the wicked, O God, and that the bloodthirsty would depart from me— ^139-19

**139:20** those who speak of you maliciously and lift themselves up against you for evil!^[Cn: Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^139-20

**139:21** Do I not hate those who hate you, O LORD? And do I not loathe those who rise up against you? ^139-21

**139:22** I hate them with perfect hatred; I count them my enemies. ^139-22

**139:23** Search me, O God, and know my heart; test me and know my thoughts. ^139-23

**139:24** See if there is any wicked^[Heb *hurtful*] way in me, and lead me in the way everlasting.^[Or *the ancient way*] ^139-24
