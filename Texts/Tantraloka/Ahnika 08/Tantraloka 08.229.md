## Mūla

व्यज्यन्ते तेन सर्गादौ नामरूपैरनेकधा ।
स्वांशेनैव महात्मानो न त्यजन्ति स्वकेतनम् ॥ ^mula

vyajyante tena sargādau nāmarūpairanekadhā |
svāṃśenaiva mahātmāno na tyajanti svaketanam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 07 - Chapter Eight, part 5#^pg267|p. 249]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=267>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 8.229cd–230ab.
>
> At the command of the Supreme Lord, impelled by Māyā and regulated by (the power of) Necessity (niyati), they are variously made manifest with many names and forms, by Brahmā in his manifest form at the beginning of (his new) creation. (with 8.228–229ab)
>
> (Even so,) they do not quit, even with a part of themselves (svāṁśena), their own abode. (with 8.229cd)
