# Jeremiah 6 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Jeremiah 05|← Jeremiah 5]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Jeremiah 07|Jeremiah 7 →]]

## The imminence and horror of the invasion

**6:1** Flee for safety, O children of Benjamin, from the midst of Jerusalem! Blow the trumpet in Tekoa, and raise a signal on Beth-haccherem, for evil looms out of the north and great destruction. ^6-1

**6:2** I have likened daughter Zion to the loveliest pasture.^[Or *I will destroy daughter Zion, the loveliest pasture*] ^6-2

**6:3** Shepherds with their flocks shall come against her. They shall pitch their tents around her; they shall pasture, all in their places. ^6-3

**6:4** “Prepare war against her; up, and let us attack at noon!” “Woe to us, for the day declines; the shadows of evening lengthen!” ^6-4

**6:5** “Up, and let us attack by night, and destroy her palaces!” ^6-5

**6:6** For thus says the LORD of hosts: Cut down her trees; cast up a siege ramp against Jerusalem. This is the city that must be punished;^[Or *the city of license*] there is nothing but oppression within her. ^6-6

**6:7** As a well keeps its water fresh, so she keeps fresh her wickedness; violence and destruction are heard within her; sickness and wounds are ever before me. ^6-7

**6:8** Take warning, O Jerusalem, or I shall turn from you in disgust and make you a desolation, an uninhabited land. ^6-8

**6:9** Thus says the LORD of hosts: Glean^[Cn Compare Gk: Heb *They shall glean*] thoroughly as a vine the remnant of Israel; like a grape gatherer, pass your hand again over its branches. ^6-9

**6:10** To whom shall I speak and give warning, that they may hear? See, their ears are closed;^[Heb *are uncircumcised*] they cannot listen. The word of the LORD is to them an object of scorn; they take no pleasure in it. ^6-10

**6:11** But I am full of the wrath of the LORD; I am weary of holding it in. Pour it out on the children in the street and on the gatherings of young men as well; both husband and wife shall be taken, the elderly and those full of days. ^6-11

**6:12** Their houses shall be turned over to others, their fields and wives together; for I will stretch out my hand against the inhabitants of the land, says the LORD. ^6-12

**6:13** For from the least to the greatest of them, everyone is greedy for unjust gain; and from prophet to priest, everyone deals falsely. ^6-13

**6:14** They have treated the wound of my people carelessly, saying, “Peace, peace,” when there is no peace. ^6-14

**6:15** They acted shamefully; they committed abomination, yet they were not ashamed; they did not know how to blush. Therefore they shall fall among those who fall; at the time that I punish them, they shall be overthrown, says the LORD. ^6-15

**6:16** Thus says the LORD: Stand at the crossroads and look, and ask for the ancient paths, where the good way lies; and walk in it, and find rest for your souls. But they said, “We will not walk in it.” ^6-16

**6:17** Also I raised up sentinels for you: “Give heed to the sound of the trumpet!” But they said, “We will not give heed.” ^6-17

**6:18** Therefore hear, O nations, and know, O congregation, what will happen to them. ^6-18

**6:19** Hear, O earth; I am going to bring disaster on this people, the fruit of their schemes, because they have not given heed to my words, and as for my teaching, they have rejected it. ^6-19

**6:20** Of what use to me is frankincense that comes from Sheba or sweet cane from a distant land? Your burnt offerings are not acceptable, nor are your sacrifices pleasing to me. ^6-20

**6:21** Therefore thus says the LORD: See, I am laying before this people stumbling blocks against which they shall stumble; parents and children together, neighbor and friend shall perish. ^6-21

**6:22** Thus says the LORD: See, a people is coming from the land of the north; a great nation is stirring from the farthest parts of the earth. ^6-22

**6:23** They grasp the bow and the javelin; they are cruel and have no mercy; their sound is like the roaring sea; they ride on horses, equipped like a warrior for battle, against you, O daughter Zion! ^6-23

**6:24** “We have heard news of them; our hands fall helpless; anguish has taken hold of us, pain as of a woman in labor. ^6-24

**6:25** Do not go out into the field or walk on the road, for the enemy has a sword; terror is on every side.” ^6-25

**6:26** O daughter of my people, put on sackcloth and roll in ashes; make mourning as for an only child, most bitter lamentation, for suddenly the destroyer will come upon us. ^6-26

**6:27** I have made you a tester and a refiner^[Or *a fortress*] among my people so that you may know and test their ways. ^6-27

**6:28** They are all stubbornly rebellious, going about with slanders; they are bronze and iron; all of them act corruptly. ^6-28

**6:29** The bellows blow fiercely; the lead is consumed by the fire;^[Cn: Heb *lead from their fire*] in vain the refining goes on, for the wicked are not removed. ^6-29

**6:30** They are called “rejected silver,” for the LORD has rejected them. ^6-30
