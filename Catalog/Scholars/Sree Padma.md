# Sree Padma

Research Affiliate in Asian Studies at Bowdoin College; her training is in the modern history and archaeology of India, and her current research is on indigenous medicine in Sri Lanka ([Bowdoin profile](https://www.bowdoin.edu/profiles/staff/spadma/index.html)). Bowdoin Books lists her *Vicissitudes of the Goddess* (Oxford University Press, 2013) in its series of books by Bowdoin faculty ([Bowdoin Books](https://books.bowdoin.edu/book/vicissitudes-of-the-goddess-reconstructions-of-the-gramadevata-in-indias-religious-traditions/)).

The profile gives the title "Research Affiliate in Asian Studies" at its head and "2018–: Research Associate Professor, Asian Studies Program" in its list of positions; it also lists "2010–: Executive Director, ISLE Program". Which is current is not settled.
