## Mūla

यदा यदा विनश्येत कर्म ध्वस्तं तदा तदा ।
अतो मोहपराधीनो यद्यप्यकृत किञ्चन ॥ ^mula

yadā yadā vinaśyeta karma dhvastaṃ tadā tadā |
ato mohaparādhīno yadyapyakṛta kiñcana || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 05 - Chapter Nine, part 3#^pg133|p. 116]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=133>))
>
> In this way, the firmness (drdhatā) and stability (dārdhya) of the latent impression (of past action) determines its fruit. To the degree to which (this intensity) decreases, Karma is destroyed. (with 9.132cd–133ab)
>
> Thus, whatever one who is under the sway of delusion has (done or) failed to do, is all burnt away, when insight (dawns) (jñānakāle). (with 9.133cd–134ab)
