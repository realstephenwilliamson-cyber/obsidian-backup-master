## Mūla

अकृत्वा गुरुयागं तु कृतमप्यकृतं यतः ।
तस्मात्प्रयत्नतः कार्यो गुरुयागो यथाबलम् ॥ ^mula

akṛtvā guruyāgaṃ tu kṛtamapyakṛtaṃ yataḥ |
tasmātprayatnataḥ kāryo guruyāgo yathābalam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 07 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 5#^pg215|p. 198]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=215>))
>
> Without performing a sacrificial rite for the teacher (guruyāga), even what has been done is (as if) it had not been done. Thus, the sacrificial rite for the teacher should be performed with effort and all one’s might (yathābala).
