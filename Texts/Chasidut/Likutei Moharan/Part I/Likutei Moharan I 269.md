# Likutei Moharan I 269

← [[Likutei Moharan I 268]] · [[Likutei Moharan]] · [[Likutei Moharan I 270]] →

*Part I · Lesson 269* · [[Likutei Moharan]]

## 1

The abode of the tzaddik is an indication of the deeds of the generation. A pointer to this is, “and she sat under a date palm” ([[Judges 04#^4-5|Judges 4:5]]). Our Sages, of blessed memory, said: Just as the date palm has only one heart, so too the Jewish people ([[Megillah 14#14a|Megillah 14a]]).
