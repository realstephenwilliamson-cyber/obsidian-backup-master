## Mūla

श्रीमत्क्रमरहस्ये च न्यरूपि परमेशिना ।
अर्घपात्रं यागधाम दीप इत्युच्यते त्रयम् ॥ ^mula

śrīmatkramarahasye ca nyarūpi parameśinā |
arghapātraṃ yāgadhāma dīpa ityucyate trayam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 09 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 1#^pg234|p. 217]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=234>))
>
> The Supreme Lord has described (this) in the venerable Kramarahasya. It is said that three things are secret (and essential) in a Kaula sacrifice. (They are) 1) the liquid offering (argha) in the sacrificial vessel, 2) the abode of the sacrifice (yāgadhāman), and 3) the lamp (dipa). 1) Of these, the liquid offering (argha) (contained in the sacrificial vessel is produced) by union with the female Kaula consort (śakti). 2) The abode (of the sacrifice) is said to be, in a progressively increasing order of excellence, the ground, the cloth, and the sacred seat of the body. 3) The lamps are fed with clarified butter, (for) cows are considered to be deities wandering on (the surface of) the earth. He who, once having known this, exerts (himself) in these three, is a (true) Kaula. (with 29.14–16)
