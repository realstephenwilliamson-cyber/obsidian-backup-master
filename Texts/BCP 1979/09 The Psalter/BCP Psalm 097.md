# Psalm 97

← [[BCP Psalm 096]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 098]] →

*Dominus regnavit* · Book Four · Nineteenth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 097]]

**97:1** The LORD is King;  
let the earth rejoice; \*  
let the multitude of the isles be glad. ^97-1

**97:2** Clouds and darkness are round about him, \*  
righteousness and justice are the foundations of his throne. ^97-2

[p. 727] ^pg727

**97:3** A fire goes before him \*  
and burns up his enemies on every side. ^97-3

**97:4** His lightnings light up the world; \*  
the earth sees it and is afraid. ^97-4

**97:5** The mountains melt like wax at the presence of the LORD, \*  
at the presence of the Lord of the whole earth. ^97-5

**97:6** The heavens declare his righteousness, \*  
and all the peoples see his glory. ^97-6

**97:7** Confounded be all who worship carved images  
and delight in false gods! \*  
Bow down before him, all you gods. ^97-7

**97:8** Zion hears and is glad, and the cities of Judah rejoice, \*  
because of your judgments, O LORD. ^97-8

**97:9** For you are the LORD,  
most high over all the earth; \*  
you are exalted far above all gods. ^97-9

**97:10** The LORD loves those who hate evil; \*  
he preserves the lives of his saints  
and delivers them from the hand of the wicked. ^97-10

**97:11** Light has sprung up for the righteous, \*  
and joyful gladness for those who are truehearted. ^97-11

**97:12** Rejoice in the LORD, you righteous, \*  
and give thanks to his holy Name. ^97-12
