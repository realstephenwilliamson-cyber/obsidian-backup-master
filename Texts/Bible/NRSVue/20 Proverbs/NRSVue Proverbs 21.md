# Proverbs 21 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Proverbs 20|← Proverbs 20]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Proverbs 22|Proverbs 22 →]]

**21:1** The king’s heart is a stream of water in the hand of the LORD; he turns it wherever he will. ^21-1

**21:2** All deeds are right in the sight of the doer, but the LORD weighs the heart. ^21-2

**21:3** To do righteousness and justice is more acceptable to the LORD than sacrifice. ^21-3

**21:4** Haughty eyes and a proud heart—the lamp of the wicked—are sin. ^21-4

**21:5** The plans of the diligent lead surely to abundance, but everyone who is hasty comes only to want. ^21-5

**21:6** The getting of treasures by a lying tongue is a fleeting vapor and a snare^[Gk: Heb *seekers*] of death. ^21-6

**21:7** The violence of the wicked will sweep them away because they refuse to do what is just. ^21-7

**21:8** The way of the guilty is crooked, but the conduct of the pure is right. ^21-8

**21:9** It is better to live in a corner of the housetop than in a house shared with a contentious wife. ^21-9

**21:10** The souls of the wicked desire evil; their neighbors find no mercy in their eyes. ^21-10

**21:11** When a scoffer is punished, the simple become wiser; when the wise are instructed, they increase in knowledge. ^21-11

**21:12** The Righteous One observes the house of the wicked; he casts the wicked down to ruin. ^21-12

**21:13** If you close your ear to the cry of the poor, you will cry out and not be heard. ^21-13

**21:14** A gift in secret averts anger, and a concealed bribe, strong wrath. ^21-14

**21:15** When justice is done, it is a joy to the righteous but dismay to evildoers. ^21-15

**21:16** Whoever wanders from the way of understanding will rest in the assembly of the dead. ^21-16

**21:17** Whoever loves pleasure will suffer want; whoever loves wine and oil will not be rich. ^21-17

**21:18** The wicked is a ransom for the righteous and the faithless for the upright. ^21-18

**21:19** It is better to live in a desert land than with a contentious and fretful wife. ^21-19

**21:20** Precious treasure remains^[Gk: Heb *and oil*] in the house of the wise, but the fool devours it. ^21-20

**21:21** Whoever pursues righteousness and kindness will find life^[Gk: Heb *life and righteousness*] and honor. ^21-21

**21:22** One wise person went up against a city of warriors and brought down the stronghold in which they trusted. ^21-22

**21:23** To watch over mouth and tongue is to keep out of trouble. ^21-23

**21:24** The proud, haughty person, named Scoffer, acts with arrogant pride. ^21-24

**21:25** The craving of the lazy person is fatal, for lazy hands refuse to labor. ^21-25

**21:26** All day long the wicked covet,^[Gk: Heb *all day long one covets covetously*] but the righteous give and do not hold back. ^21-26

**21:27** The sacrifice of the wicked is an abomination; how much more when brought with evil intent. ^21-27

**21:28** A false witness will perish, but a good listener will testify successfully. ^21-28

**21:29** The wicked put on a bold face, but the upright give thought to^[Or *establish*] their ways. ^21-29

**21:30** No wisdom, no understanding, no counsel can avail against the LORD. ^21-30

**21:31** The horse is made ready for the day of battle, but the victory belongs to the LORD. ^21-31
