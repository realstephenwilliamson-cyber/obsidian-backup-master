# dhyānaratnāvali

**Catalog number:** E0008  
**Manuscript:** IFP transcript T0232  
**Published:** Muktabodha Indological Research InstituteMuktabodha, United States, 2013

## Description

IFP transcript 232 copied from a manuscript belonging to Rajamani Gurukkal Perambar Kandikai, Madhurantakam

Compendium of descriptions of the visualized forms (dhyāna) of deities and their attributes along with
other other entities such as world-orders, metaphysical principles,
and channels of the vital breath.

 See Muktabodha digital library, IFP/EFEO collection at www.muktabodha.org for full bibliographic details.
