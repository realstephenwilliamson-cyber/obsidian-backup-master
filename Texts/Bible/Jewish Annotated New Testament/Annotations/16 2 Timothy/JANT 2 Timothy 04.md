# 2 Timothy 4 (JANT notes)

### 4.1–5: A final charge

*[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 04#^4-1|Text]], p. 446* ^4-1

**[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 04#^4-1|1]]:** *Judge the living and the dead*, [[Romans 02#^2-16|Rom 2.16]]; [[2 Corinthians 05#^5-10|2 Cor 5.10]]. *His appearing*, a reference to what is known as the “Second Coming.” ^4-1-2

**[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 04#^4-3|3]]:** *Sound doctrine*, lit., “healthy teaching.” ^4-3

**[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 04#^4-4|4]]:** *Myths*, as the opposite of true teaching or philosophy. Philo uses the term similarly (*On the Creation* 157; *Worse* 125; *Abr*. 243; *Spec. Laws* 4.178). See [[1 Timothy 01#^1-4|1 Tim 1.4]]; [[1 Timothy 04#^4-7|4.7]]; [[Titus 01#^1-14|Titus 1.14]]; [[2 Peter 01#^1-16|2 Pet 1.16]]. ^4-4

**[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 04#^4-5|5]]:** *Evangelist*, one who announces the gospel ([[Acts 02#^2-18|Acts 2.18]]; [[Acts 08#^8-4|8.4]],[[Acts 08#^8-12|12]],[[Acts 08#^8-35|35]],[[Acts 08#^8-40|40]]) mentioned together with apostles, prophets, pastors, and teachers as one of the gifts Christ gives to the church in [[Ephesians 04#^4-11|Eph 4.11]]. ^4-5

### 4.6–8: The Pauline model

*[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 04#^4-6|Text]], p. 446* ^4-6

**[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 04#^4-6|6]]:** *Libation*, Gk “spondas,” Heb “nesek,” a technical term for offering libations accompanying sacrifices ([[Torah 41 - Pinchas#^28-15|Num 28.15]]; [[Judges 06#^6-20|Judg 6.20]]; [[I Samuel 07#^7-6|1 Sam 7.6]]; [[Isaiah 57#^57-6|Isa 57.6]]; [[Jeremiah 07#^7-18|Jer 7.18]]; [[Jeremiah 19#^19-13|19.13]]; see also Philo, *Drunkenness* 152; Josephus, *Ant*. 6.22); the author derives the term here to signal Paul’s comment in [[Philippians 02#^2-17|Phil 2.17]], “I am being poured out as a libation over the sacrifice and the offering of your faith.” ^4-6-2

**[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 04#^4-7|7]]:** *Fought the good fight*, [[1 Timothy 01#^1-18|1 Tim 1.18]]; [[1 Timothy 06#^6-12|6.12]]. *Finished the race*, see [[Acts 20#^20-24|Acts 20.24]]; [[Hebrews 12#^12-1|Heb 12.1–2]]. *The faith*, for the Pastorals, *faith* is substantially a set of doctrines (see 1.5n.). ^4-7

**[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 04#^4-8|8]]:** *Crown*, an emblem of victory ([[1 Corinthians 09#^9-26|1 Cor 9.26]]) for his faithful life. *Lord, the righteous judge*, see [[Jeremiah 11#^11-20|Jer 11.20]]; [[Psalms 009#^9-5|Ps 9.4]]; [[LXX 2 Maccabees 12#^12-6|2 Macc 12.6]],[[LXX 2 Maccabees 12#^12-41|41]]; also part of Jewish burial prayers (“O true and righteous judge! Blessed be the true judge, all whose judgments are righteous and true”). *That day . . . his appearing*, the fulfillment of the hope for salvation; see LXX [[LXX Zephaniah 01#^1-14|Zeph 1.14]], “the great day of the Lord,” and 2.11, “The Lord will appear against them.” ^4-8

### 4.9–15: Personal instructions

*[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 04#^4-9|Text]], p. 447* ^4-9

**[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 04#^4-10|10]]:** *Demas*, see [[Colossians 04#^4-14|Col 4.14]]; Philem 24. *Crescens*, not mentioned elsewhere. *Titus*, see [[2 Corinthians 02#^2-13|2 Cor 2.13]]; [[2 Corinthians 07#^7-6|7.6]],[[2 Corinthians 07#^7-13|13–14]]; [[2 Corinthians 08#^8-6|8.6]],[[2 Corinthians 08#^8-16|16]],[[2 Corinthians 08#^8-23|23]]; [[2 Corinthians 12#^12-18|12.18]]; [[Galatians 02#^2-1|Gal 2.1]],[[Galatians 02#^2-3|3]]; [[Titus 01#^1-4|Titus 1.4]]. ^4-10

**[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 04#^4-11|11]]:** *Luke*, see [[Colossians 04#^4-14|Col 4.14]]; Philem 24. *Mark*, see [[Acts 12#^12-12|Acts 12.12]]; [[Acts 15#^15-27|15.27]]; [[Colossians 04#^4-11|Col 4.11]]; Philem 24. ^4-11

**[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 04#^4-12|12]]:** *Tychichus*, see [[Colossians 04#^4-7|Col. 4.7]]; [[Titus 03#^3-12|Titus 3.12]]. ^4-12

**[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 04#^4-13|13]]:** *Carpus*, not mentioned elsewhere. *Books*, sing. Gk “biblion” and Heb “sēper,” used for individual books of the Bible ([[LXX Tobit 01#^1-1|Tob 1.1]]); in the plural is used for groups of writings in the incipient Bible ([[Daniel 09#^9-2|Dan 9.2]]; [[LXX 1 Maccabees 12#^12-9|1 Macc 12.9]]). *Parchments* (Gk “membranas”), processed animal leather later used for Torah scrolls and other documents. ^4-13

**[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 04#^4-14|14]]:** *Alexander*, cannot be identified with certainty; see [[1 Timothy 01#^1-20|1 Tim 1.20]]; someone with this name is also mentioned in [[Acts 19#^19-33|Acts 19.33]]. *Pay him back for his deeds*, see [[LXX Psalms 61#^61-13|Ps 61.13]]\[LXX\] (“Because you will repay each according to his deeds”); [[LXX Psalms 27#^27-4|Ps 27.4]]\[LXX\]; [[Proverbs 24#^24-12|Prov 24.12]]; *Pss. Sol*. 95. ^4-14

### 4.16–22: Final encouragement, greetings, and benediction

*[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 04#^4-16|Text]], p. 447* ^4-16

**[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 04#^4-16|16]]:** *Defense*, reference to an early trial ([[Acts 23#^23-1|Acts 23.1–11]]) or to the first step of the legal procedure that brought Paul to prison. ^4-16-2

**[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 04#^4-17|17]]:** *All the Gentiles*, God’s salvation encompasses all humanity. See [[Romans 15#^15-11|Rom 15.11]] and [[Torah 04 - Vayera#^18-18|Gen 18.18]]; [[Torah 04 - Vayera#^22-18|22.18]]. *Lion’s mouth*, an echo of [[Psalms 022#^22-22|Ps 22.22]]\[LXX\] (“save me from the lion’s mouth”); see also [[Daniel 06#^6-21|Dan 6.20]],[[Daniel 06#^6-23|22]]\[LXX\]; [[LXX 4 Maccabees 18#^18-13|4 Macc 18.13]]. ^4-17

**[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 04#^4-18|18]]:** *The Lord will rescue me*, Gk “rhyomai,” expressing deliverance. Liberation from harm and oppression is a central component of biblical theology (see [[Torah 14 - Vaera#^6-6|Ex 6.6]]; [[Torah 16 - Beshalach#^14-30|14.30]]; [[Judges 06#^6-9|Judg 6.9]]; [[Psalms 007#^7-2|Ps 7.1]]; 17.13; [[Psalms 034#^34-19|34.18]]) and an essential component of prayers in both Judaism and Christianity ([[Matthew 06#^6-13|Mt 6.13]]; *Did.* 8.2; 10.5; [[Berakhot 16#16b|*b. Ber.* 16b]]). *Heavenly kingdom*, see [[Psalms 103#^103-9|Ps 103.9]]; [[Matthew 18#^18-3|Mt 18.3]]; [[Matthew 19#^19-23|19.23]]. In rabbinic literature, “malkut shamayim,” “the dominion of heaven,” becomes a common term; see [[Berakhot 13#13a|*b. Ber.* 13a]]. *Glory forever*, traditional doxology also used in [[Galatians 01#^1-5|Gal 1.5]] (cf. [[Psalms 072#^72-19|Ps 72.19]]; [[Psalms 104#^104-31|104.31]]; [[Psalms 111#^111-3|111.3]]; Pr Man 15; [[LXX 4 Maccabees 18#^18-24|4 Macc 18.24]]; [[Romans 11#^11-36|Rom 11.36]]; [[Romans 16#^16-27|16.27]]; [[Hebrews 13#^13-21|Heb 13.21]]; [[1 Peter 04#^4-11|1 Pet 4.11]]). ^4-18

**[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 04#^4-19|19]]:** *Prisca and Aquila*, Paul’s co-workers and travel companions (Prisca is a variant of Priscilla); see [[Acts 18#^18-2|Acts 18.2–3]],[[Acts 18#^18-18|18]],[[Acts 18#^18-26|26]]; [[Romans 16#^16-3|Rom 16.3–4]]; [[1 Corinthians 16#^16-19|1 Cor 16.19]]. *Onesiphorus*, cf. [[2 Timothy 01#^1-16|2 Tim 1.16–18]]. ^4-19

**[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 04#^4-20|20]]:** *Erastus*, see [[Acts 19#^19-22|Acts 19.22]]; [[Romans 16#^16-23|Rom 16.23]]; *Trophimus*, see [[Acts 20#^20-4|Acts 20.4–5]]; [[Acts 21#^21-29|21.29]]. ^4-20

**[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 04#^4-21|21]]:** *Before winter*, the unsafe season for sea travel began after Sukkot, the Feast of Booths, in the early fall. “The rabbis say, ‘Who makes a path in the Sea, from Shavuot (Pentecost) to Sukkot (Tabernacles), and in great waters a trail, from Sukkot (Tishrei, September–October) to Hanukkah (Kislev, December)’” (*y. Shabb.* 2.6). *Eubulus . . . Claudia*, not mentioned elsewhere. ^4-21

**[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 04#^4-22|22]]:** *Your spirit*, “your” is here singular and refers to Timothy. *Grace be with you*, is plural and refers to the community (see the earlier formula “the Lord be with you” in [[Judges 06#^6-12|Judg 6.12]]; [[Ruth 2#^2-4|Ruth 2.4]]). ancient authorities add *Amen* ^4-22
