# Zohar 3.178b

← [[Zohar 3.178a]] · [[Zohar (Soncino)]] · [[Zohar 3.179b]] →

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 9.06 - Parashat Qorah#^z3-178b|Parashat Qoraḥ, at 3:178b]]

*Volume 3, folio 178b* · Korach

*Passage Korach 8:37 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 3.178a#^korach-8-37|Korach 8:37]].*

**Korach 8:38** Hence it says “the Levite shall do”, i.e. “make” or “complete”. ^korach-8-38

**Korach 8:39** We may also translate, “And the Levite shall serve *hu* (him)”, the reference being to the Ancient One, and the Levite typifying Judgement, but for which men would not rise to the higher faith, nor study the Torah, nor carry out the precepts of the Torah for the service of the Holy King.’[^1] ^korach-8-39

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Korach 8:40–10:46.*

[^1]: The last 12 lines of the Hebrew text do not appear in our translation.
