## Mūla

पदानि पञ्च धीमूलपुंरागाख्ये त्रये त्रयम् ।
एकं त्वशुद्धवित्कालद्वये चैकं नियामके ॥ ^mula

padāni pañca dhīmūlapuṃrāgākhye traye trayam |

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 06 - Chapter Sixteen, part 2#^pg111|p. 91]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=111>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 16.213cd–214ab.
>
> (In this procedure, the deposition of the Vidyā of) Parāparā is in the reverse order (vailomya). 1) Thus, there are three Parts (pada) in the Earth (principle). 2) Then the Parts (in the group of principles ranging) Water to the ego are five, by taking into account the five groups of eight worlds (within them). 3) In the three (principles) called intellect, Root (Nature) and the individual soul, (in which is comprised also that of) Attachment, the Parts (pada) are three. 4) There is one Part in the two (principles) of Impure Knowledge and Time, and 5) one in that of Necessity. 6) It is said here that there is one Part, in the Force (of limited agency) (kalā) and Māyā (together), in due order. 7) There are five Parts in Pure Knowledge, Īśvara, Sadāśiva, Śiva and Śakti. This Parāparā (Vidyā) (thus consists of) nineteen Parts. (with 16.213–216ab)
