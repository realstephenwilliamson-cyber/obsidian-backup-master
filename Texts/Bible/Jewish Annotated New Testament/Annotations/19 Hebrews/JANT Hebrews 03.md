### 3.1–6: Jesus compared to Moses

*[[NRSVue Hebrews 03#^3-1|Text]], p. 467* · As with angels (1.5–14), so with Moses: Jesus is the superior interme-diary; cf. Matthew’s Gospel (esp. chs 2–7), wherein Jesus is a new Moses and the Sermon on the Mount evokes the giving of the Torah on Mt. Sinai. ^3-1

**[[NRSVue Hebrews 03#^3-1|1]]:** *Apostle,* “the one sent.” This is the only time the NT applies the term “apostle” to Jesus. *Confession*, most likely meaning the community’s commitment of fidelity to Jesus, but it is unclear if it refers to a formal ritual (see also 4.14; 10.23). Cf. [[2 Corinthians 09#^9-13|2 Cor 9.13]] where the expression is “confession of the gospel of Christ.” ^3-1-2

**[[NRSVue Hebrews 03#^3-2|2]]:** *Faithful*, persevering. The quotation is [[LXX Numbers 12#^12-7|Num 12.7–8]]\[LXX\]. ^3-2

**[[NRSVue Hebrews 03#^3-5|5]]:** *Servant* (Gk “therapon”) connotes a priest or a healer. *Testify to the things . . . spoken later*, Moses functions as an inferior prototype of Jesus. ^3-5

**[[NRSVue Hebrews 03#^3-6|6]]:** *We are his house*, both the Heb “bayit” and the Gk “oikos” can mean “house” in the sense of dwelling, but also “family, lineage, kin group,” which is most likely what is meant here. In the Tanakh the Temple is sometimes called “God’s house” (e.g., [[Jeremiah 07#^7-2|Jer 7.2]]; [[Psalms 023#^23-6|Ps 23.6]]). ^3-6

### 3.7–19: Leaving the wilderness and entering God’s rest

*[[NRSVue Hebrews 03#^3-7|Text]], p. 467* ^3-7

**[[NRSVue Hebrews 03#^3-7|7–11]]:** [[Psalms 095#^95-7|Ps 95.7–11]]. The first of several times the author quotes from Ps 95 between [[Hebrews 03#^3-7|Heb 3.7]] and 4.13. The Psalm evokes episodes that exemplify the defiance and lack of faith of the wilderness generation. For the role the Psalm plays in the argument of Hebrews, see 4.1–11n. ^3-7-2

**[[NRSVue Hebrews 03#^3-7|7]]:** *Holy Spirit*, Jewish tradition at this time, as reflected in an addition to the large Dead Sea Psalms Scroll, understood all of the Psalter to be divinely inspired (11QPsa 27.11). *His voice*, most likely referring to God’s voice. ^3-7-3

**[[NRSVue Hebrews 03#^3-8|8]]:** There are several instances of disobedience during the peoples’ sojourn in the wilderness narrated in Exodus and Numbers, including Moses striking a rock in response to the people’s demand for water, which God provides ([[Torah 16 - Beshalach#^17-1|Ex 17.1–7]]). *Testing*, the place where Moses struck the rock was named “Massah,” “testing,” and “Meribah,” “quarrel.” The same word is used in 2.18. ^3-8

**[[NRSVue Hebrews 03#^3-9|9]]:** *Me*, God. ^3-9

**[[NRSVue Hebrews 03#^3-10|10]]:** *Forty years*, Hebrews positions “forty years” differently from the original Psalm, a subtle alteration in language that effects a big change in the story—it reverses the order of events. In the Tanakh, God condemns the people to wander in the desert for forty years as punishment for their disobedience ([[Torah 37 - Sh'lach#^14-29|Num 14.29]]); in Hebrews their disobedience lasts for forty years, while the promise of rest goes unfulfilled; see 4.1–11n. ^3-10

**[[NRSVue Hebrews 03#^3-11|11]]:** *Rest*, for the Psalm—settlement in Canaan ([[Joshua 01#^1-13|Josh 1.13]]); for Hebrews—a future salvation (see 4.1–11n.). ^3-11

**[[NRSVue Hebrews 03#^3-12|12]]:** *Living God*, traditional epithet meant initially to contrast the God of Israel to idols; see e.g., 10.31; 12.22; [[Torah 45 - Vaetchanan#^5-23|Deut 5.26]]; [[Joshua 03#^3-10|Josh 3.10]]; [[1 Thessalonians 01#^1-9|1 Thess 1.9]]. ^3-12

**[[NRSVue Hebrews 03#^3-13|13]]:** *Today* (used in the Psalm quoted in v 7) makes the warnings immediate for the audience and functions as proof that the promise of rest was not fulfilled by settlement in the land. See 4.6–7n. ^3-13

**[[NRSVue Hebrews 03#^3-15|15]]:** [[Psalms 095#^95-7|Ps 95.7]], cited above. ^3-15

**[[NRSVue Hebrews 03#^3-17|17]]:** *Fell in the wilderness*, reflecting [[Torah 37 - Sh'lach#^14-29|Num 14.29]]. ^3-17

**[[NRSVue Hebrews 03#^3-19|19]]:** *Unbelief*, lack of persevering faith toward God on the part of Moses and the people. ^3-19
