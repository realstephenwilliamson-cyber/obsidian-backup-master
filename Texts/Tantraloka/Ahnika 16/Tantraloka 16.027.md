## Mūla

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 05 - Chapter Sixteen, part 1#^pg33|p. 13]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=33>))
>
> Whatever is sprinkled with the nectar of Śiva’s nonduality is indeed supreme. The purification, that is, the act of rendering supreme the liquid offering (argha), the flowers, the perfumes etc., as well as the wine and the (sacrificial) animal, is the perception (drṣti) (and gaze) of Śiva’s nonduality. (with 16.26cd–27)
