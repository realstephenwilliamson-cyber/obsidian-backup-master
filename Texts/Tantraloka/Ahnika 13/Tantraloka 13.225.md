## Mūla

इत्यादिभेदभिन्नो हि गुरोर्लाभ इहोदितः ।
तस्माद्दीक्षां स लभते सद्य एव शिवप्रदाम् ॥ ^mula

ityādibhedabhinno hi gurorlābha ihoditaḥ |
tasmāddīkṣāṃ sa labhate sadya eva śivapradām || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 08 - Chapter Thirteen, part 4#^pg286|p. 264]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=286>))
>
> The first type may have become (a self-accomplished teacher,) either all at once or gradually, completely or partially. These and others are said here to be the various types of teacher (the seeker) finds. The initiation he receives from (such a teacher) bestows Śiva immediately, and knowledge (of Śiva) is (its) nature. Thus, one with Śiva, at that very moment he knows all things as they truly are, and is said to be liberated in (this very) life. (with 13.224cd–226)
