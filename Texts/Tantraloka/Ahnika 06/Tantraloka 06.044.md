## Mūla

ईश्वरः प्राणमाता च विद्या देहप्रमातृता ।
अनाश्रयो हि शून्यत्वं ज्ञानमेव हि बुद्धिता ॥ ^mula

īśvaraḥ prāṇamātā ca vidyā dehapramātṛtā |
anāśrayo hi śūnyatvaṃ jñānameva hi buddhitā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 09 - Chapter Six, part 2#^pg272|p. 257]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=272>))
>
> Transcendental Śiva (Anāśrita) is the void perceiver, Sadāśiva is the subject of the intellect, Iśvara is the subject of the vital breath, and (Śuddha) Vidyā is the subjectivity of the body. (with 6.43cd–44ab)
>
> The absence of (objective) support is indeed voidness. Knowledge is the intellect. The possession of a universal nature (viśvātmatā) is the vital breath, (whereas) the state of union with objectivity (vedyaikatānatā) takes place within the body. (with 6.44cd–45ab)
