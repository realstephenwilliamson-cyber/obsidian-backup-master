# 1 Thessalonians 5 (JANT notes)

### 5.1–11: The timing of the Lord’s return

*[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 05#^5-1|Text]], p. 425* ^5-1

**[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 05#^5-1|1]]:** *Times and the seasons*: the end of days (see [[Daniel 02#^2-21|Dan 2.21]]). ^5-1-2

**[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 05#^5-2|2]]:** *Day of the Lord* (e.g., [[Isaiah 02#^2-12|Isa 2.12–17]]; [[Joel 2#^2-1|Joel 2.1]]; Am 5.18,20; [[Zephaniah 1#^1-7|Zeph 1.7]]), in the Tanakh, the eschatological time of divine justice, including, variously, punishment of evildoers, the redemption of Gentile nations or all creation (e.g., [[Isaiah 02#^2-2|Isa 2.2–4]]; [[Micah 4#^4-1|Mic 4.1–3]]), and the return of Israel to Zion. In the NT ([[1 Corinthians 01#^1-8|1 Cor 1.8]]; [[1 Corinthians 05#^5-5|5.5]]; [[2 Timothy 01#^1-18|2 Tim 1.18]]; [[Hebrews 09#^9-28|Heb 9.28]]; [[Hebrews 10#^10-5|10.5]]), it refers to Jesus’ return. *Thief in the night*, see [[Matthew 24#^24-43|Mt 24.43]]; [[Luke 12#^12-39|Lk 12.39]]. Whereas the Tanakh speaks of the sudden appearance of the end-time, the specific image of the thief appears to have originated with Jesus and his followers ([[Matthew 24#^24-43|Mt 24.43]]). ^5-2

**[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 05#^5-3|3]]:** *Peace and security*, the (false) promise of the Roman Empire; see Josephus, *Ant*. 14.160; 15.348; Tacitus, *Hist*. 2.12; 4.74. *Sudden destruction . . . labor pains*, see 1.6, also [[Isaiah 26#^26-17|Isa 26.17]]; [[Isaiah 42#^42-14|42.14]]; [[Isaiah 66#^66-8|66.8]]; [[Jeremiah 30#^30-6|Jer 30.6–7]]; [[Romans 08#^8-22|Rom 8.22]]. **4**: *Thief*, see 5.2n. ^5-3

**[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 05#^5-5|5]]:** *Children of light*, see [[Luke 16#^16-8|Lk 16.8]]; [[John 12#^12-36|Jn 12.36]]; [[Ephesians 05#^5-8|Eph 5.8]]; cf. [[1 John 02#^2-8|1 Jn 2.8–11]]. See also [[John 01#^1-4|Jn 1.4–5]],[[John 01#^1-7|7–9]]; [[John 03#^3-19|3.19–21]]; [[John 08#^8-12|8.12]]; [[John 12#^12-46|12.46]]. Light is associated with “Torah” ([[Proverbs 06#^6-23|Prov 6.23]]), darkness with evil ([[Proverbs 02#^2-13|Prov 2.13]]); God is pictured as light ([[Psalms 104#^104-2|Ps 104.2]]). The DSS, 1QM 15.9 (cf. 1QS 3.25–27), divides humankind into the “sons of light” and the “sons of darkness.” ^5-5

**[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 05#^5-6|6]]:** *Fall asleep*, i.e., die; see 4.13n., but here with the connotation of failing to be *awake and be sober* and in preparation for the end-time (see [[Matthew 25#^25-13|Mt 25.13–15]]; [[Matthew 24#^24-42|24.42]]; [[Mark 13#^13-33|Mk 13.33–37]]). ^5-6

**[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 05#^5-8|8]]:** *Breastplate . . . helmet*, see [[Romans 13#^13-11|Rom 13.11–13]]; [[Ephesians 06#^6-13|Eph 6.13–17]]; cf. [[Isaiah 59#^59-17|Isa 59.17]]; the Tanakh provides the antecedent idea of righteous armor; see [[Isaiah 59#^59-17|Isa 59.17]] and cf. [[LXX Wisdom of Solomon 05#^5-17|Wis 5.17–22]]. *Hope of salvation*, at the “parousia.” ^5-8

### 5.12–22: Exhortations and warnings

*[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 05#^5-12|Text]], p. 425* ^5-12

**[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 05#^5-12|12]]:** *Labor . . . have charge*, those leading the community, see 2.7. ^5-12-2

**[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 05#^5-14|14]]:** *Idlers*, lit., “disorderly ones” (Gk “ataktos”). *Patient*, in Jewish sources, usually an attribute of God (see [[Torah 21 - Ki Tisa#^34-6|Ex 34.6]]; [[Joel 2#^2-13|Joel 2.13]]; [[LXX Psalms 102#^102-8|Ps 102.8]]) but also of the sage ([[Proverbs 16#^16-32|Prov 16.32]]; [[Proverbs 17#^17-27|17.27]]; [[LXX Sirach 05#^5-11|Sir 5.11]]). ^5-14

**[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 05#^5-15|15]]:** *Evil for evil*, the Tanakh already warns against such retaliation (e.g., [[Proverbs 20#^20-22|Prov 20.22]]; [[Proverbs 24#^24-29|24.29]]; [[Proverbs 25#^25-21|25.21]]). ^5-15

**[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 05#^5-17|17]]:** *Pray without ceasing*, see 3.10. ^5-17

**[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 05#^5-19|19]]:** *Quench the Spirit*, i.e., be open to the Holy Spirit working though the individual. ^5-19

**[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 05#^5-20|20]]:** *Prophets*, or “prophecies,” a reference to ecstatic experiences; see [[1 Corinthians 14#^14-37|1 Cor 14.37–39]]. ^5-20

**[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 05#^5-21|21]]:** *Test*, examine closely to check genuineness; [[Torah 47 - Re'eh#^13-2|Deut 13.1–5]]. ^5-21

**[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 05#^5-22|22]]:** *Abstain from . . . evil*, see [[Job 01#^1-1|Job 1.1]],[[Job 01#^1-8|8]]; 1QS 1.4. ^5-22

### 5.23–28: Benediction

*[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 05#^5-23|Text]], p. 426* ^5-23

**[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 05#^5-23|23]]:** *God of peace*, Paul uses this phrase several times (cf. [[Romans 15#^15-33|Rom 15.33]]; [[Romans 16#^16-20|16.20]]; [[Philippians 04#^4-9|Phil 4.9]]; see [[Hebrews 13#^13-20|Heb 13.20]]); it is not found in the Tanakh but occurs in non-canonical literature, e.g., *T. Dan* 5.2. *Sanctify,* see 4.3. *Spirit and soul*, see [[LXX Wisdom of Solomon 15#^15-11|Wis 15.11]]. *B. Nidd.* 31a uses Heb “ruaḥ unʾshamah*.* ” It is not clear whether Paul views these as distinct aspects of the human being or as synonymous. ^5-23-2

**[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 05#^5-24|24]]:** *One who calls*, God. ^5-24

**[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 05#^5-25|25]]:** *Pray for us*, see [[Jeremiah 42#^42-2|Jer 42.2]]; [[2 Thessalonians 03#^3-1|2 Thess 3.1]]. ^5-25

**[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 05#^5-26|26]]:** *Holy kiss*, chaste expression of love (“agapē”) characterizing nascent communities of Jesus’ followers (cf. [[Romans 16#^16-16|Rom 16.16]]; [[1 Corinthians 16#^16-20|1 Cor 16.20]]; 2 Cor 16.12). ^5-26

**[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 05#^5-27|27]]:** *Read to all*, the assembly. ^5-27

**[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 05#^5-28|28]]:** A standard close to Paul’s letters (e.g., [[Romans 16#^16-20|Rom 16.20]]; [[1 Corinthians 16#^16-23|1 Cor 16.23]]). *Grace*, see 1.1. ^5-28
