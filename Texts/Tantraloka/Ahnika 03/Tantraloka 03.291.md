## Mūla

यदि तादृगनुग्राह्यो दैशिकस्योपसर्पति ।
अथासौ तादृशो न स्याद्भवभक्त्या च भावितः ॥ ^mula

yadi tādṛganugrāhyo daiśikasyopasarpati |
athāsau tādṛśo na syādbhavabhaktyā ca bhāvitaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. II
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 02 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v02 12 - Chapter Three, part 9#^pg424|p. 408]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 02.pdf#page=424>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 3.291cd–292ab.
>
> The teacher who is established herein evidently graces anyone who approaches him, if he is fit to be graced in this way. (with 3.290cd–291ab)
>
> Now if (the disciple) is not such, (but even then,) purified by devotion to Rudra (Bhava) and impelled by (the thought that) there will be such grace in the future, worships that (teacher), then the skilful (learned teacher) should impart initiation and (whatever other) wonderfully diverse (practice the disciple is fit to receive). (with 3.291cd–292)
