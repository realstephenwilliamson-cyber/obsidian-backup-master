# Ἀδρίας

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · noun

**Dodson:** the Adriatic Sea — the Hadria, a name given by sailors not merely to the Adriatic Sea, to which it properly belonged, but also to the open Mediterranean to the south-east of Italy, to the sea that lay between Malta, Italy, Greece, and Crete. (Strong's 99; headword Ἀδρίας, ου, ὁ)
