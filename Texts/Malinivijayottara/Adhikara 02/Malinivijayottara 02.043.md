## Mūla

चतुर्विधम्तु पिण्डस्थमषुद्धम्बुद्धमेव च ।
प्रबुद्धम्सुप्रबुद्धम्च पदस्थम्च चतुर्विधम् ॥ ^mula

caturvidham tu piṇḍastham aṣuddham buddham eva ca |
prabuddham suprabuddham ca padastham ca caturvidham || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, October 2022 – December 2022
> p. 23 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=30>))
>
> The Corporeal (piṇḍastha) is of four kinds, Unawakened (Abuddha), Awakened (Buddha), Well Awakened (Prabuddha) and Fully Awakened (Suprabuddha).[^61] (with 2.43ab)

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, October 2022 – December 2022
> p. 24 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=31>))
>
> *Abiding in Place (padastha) is of four kinds, namely,* Gone and Come (*gatāgata*), Well Dispersed (*suvikṣipta*), Well Conjoined (*samgata*) and Well Concentrated (*susamāhita*).[^62] (with 2.43d–44ab)

[^61]: Quoted in TĀv ad 10/239cd-240ab.
[^62]: Quoted in TĀv ad 10/252cd-253ab; TĀ 10/253ab is a direct quote of MV 2/44ab.
