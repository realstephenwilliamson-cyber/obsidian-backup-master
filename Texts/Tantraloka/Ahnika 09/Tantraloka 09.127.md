## Mūla

तस्यैव तत्फलं चित्रं कर्म यस्य पुरातनम् ।
क्षीवोऽपि राजा सूदं चेदादिशेत्प्रातरीदृशम् ॥ ^mula

tasyaiva tatphalaṃ citraṃ karma yasya purātanam |
kṣīvo'pi rājā sūdaṃ cedādiśetprātarīdṛśam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 05 - Chapter Nine, part 3#^pg127|p. 110]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=127>))
>
> Insofar as the subject who links together experience (anusaṁdhātr) is one and the same, it is he alone who accrues the varied fruit of that, his past action. (with 9.126cd–127ab)
>
> If a drunken king orders his cook in the morning to prepare a particular kind (of food) (īdṛśam) (for him at midday), he obtains that result without connecting (the two events) together (because he is drunk). In the same way, one also experiences the consequences (of the Karma) accrued in another life, (even) without consciously connecting it today (in the present to the previous experience). The reason for this is the previous unwavering (intention to act in a way as to give rise to a particular result). (with 9.127cd–129ab)
