# Nahum 3

← [[Nahum 2]] · [[Tanakh]]

Compare: [[NRSVue Nahum 03|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 3

**3:1** Ah, city of crime,  
Utterly treacherous,  
Full of violence,  
Where killing never stops! ^3-1

**3:2** Crack of whip  
And rattle of wheel,  
Galloping steed  
And bounding chariot! ^3-2

**3:3** Charging horsemen,  
Flashing swords,  
And glittering spears!  
Hosts of slain  
And heaps of corpses,  
Dead bodies without number—  
They stumble over bodies. ^3-3

**3:4** Because of the countless harlotries of the harlot,  
The winsome mistress of sorcery,  
Who ensnared[^1] nations with her harlotries  
And peoples with her sorcery, ^3-4

**3:5** I am going to deal with you  
—declares the LORD of Hosts.  
I will lift up your skirts over your face  
And display your nakedness to the nations  
And your shame to kingdoms. ^3-5

**3:6** I will throw loathsome things over you  
And disfigure you  
And make a spectacle of you. ^3-6

**3:7** All who see you will recoil from you  
And will say,  
“Nineveh has been ravaged!”  
Who will console her?  
Where shall I look for  
Anyone to comfort you? ^3-7

**3:8** Were you any better than No-amon,[^2]  
Which sat by the Nile,  
Surrounded by water—  
Its rampart a river,[^3]  
Its wall consisting of sea?[^4] ^3-8

**3:9** Populous Nubia  
And teeming Egypt,  
Put and the Libyans—  
They were her[^5] helpers. ^3-9

**3:10** Yet even she was exiled,  
She went into captivity.  
Her babes, too, were dashed in pieces  
At every street corner.  
Lots were cast for her honored men,  
And all her nobles were bound in chains. ^3-10

**3:11** You too shall be drunk  
And utterly overcome;[^6]  
You too shall seek  
A refuge from the enemy. ^3-11

**3:12** All your forts are fig trees  
With[^7] ripe fruit;  
If shaken they will fall  
Into the mouths of devourers. ^3-12

**3:13** Truly, the troops within you are women;  
The gates of your land have opened themselves  
To your enemies;  
Fire has consumed your gate bars. ^3-13

**3:14** Draw water for the siege,  
Strengthen your forts;  
Tread the clay,  
Trample the mud,  
Grasp the brick mold! ^3-14

**3:15** There fire will devour you,  
The sword will put an end to you;  
It will devour you like the grub.  
Multiply like grubs,  
Multiply like locusts![^8] ^3-15

**3:16** You had more traders  
Than the sky has stars—  
The grubs cast their skins and fly away. ^3-16

**3:17** Your guards were like locusts,  
Your marshals like piles of hoppers  
Which settle on the stone fences  
On a chilly day;  
When the sun comes out, they fly away,  
And where they are nobody knows. ^3-17

**3:18** Your shepherds are slumbering,  
O king of Assyria;  
Your sheepmasters are lying inert;[^9]  
Your people are scattered[^10] over the hills,  
And there is none to gather them. ^3-18

**3:19** There is no healing[^11] for your injury;  
Your wound is grievous.  
All who hear the news about you  
Clap their hands over you.  
For who has not suffered  
From your constant malice? ^3-19

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Amon was the tutelary deity of No (Thebes; cf. Jer. 46.25), which the Assyrians had sacked in 663 b.c.e.
[^3]: Heb. “sea.”
[^4]: Change of vocalization yields “water.”
[^5]: Heb. “your.”
[^6]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^7]: Emendation yields “Your troops are”; cf. next verse.
[^8]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^9]: Lit. “dwelling”; emendation yields “asleep.”
[^10]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^11]: Heb. kehah, a variant of gehah; see Prov. 17.22.
