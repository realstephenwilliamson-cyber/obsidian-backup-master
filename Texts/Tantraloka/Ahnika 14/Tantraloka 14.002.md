## Mūla

स्पृशन्प्रकाशते येन ततः स्वच्छन्द उच्यते ।
नियतिं कर्मफलयोराश्रित्यैष महेश्वरः ॥ ^mula

spṛśanprakāśate yena tataḥ svacchanda ucyate |
niyatiṃ karmaphalayorāśrityaiṣa maheśvaraḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 11 - Chapter Fourteen, opening page#^pg367|p. 345]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=367>))
>
> It is the Supreme Lord’s (Parameśvara) nature to manifest in two ways – in accord with (the constraints imposed by causal and Karmic) necessity (niyati), and independently of it. This is why he is said to be free (svacchanda). (with 14.1cd–2ab)
>
> On the basis of the (constraint of) Karmic necessity and its fruit, the Lord performs the functions of emanation, persistence and withdrawal in their intermediate form. (with 14.2cd–3ab)
