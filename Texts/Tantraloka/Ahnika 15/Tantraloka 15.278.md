## Mūla

धूपनैवेद्यतृप्त्याद्यैस्तथा व्याससमासतः ।
संसारवामाचारत्वात्सर्वं वामकरेण तु ॥ ^mula

dhūpanaivedyatṛptyādyaistathā vyāsasamāsataḥ |
saṃsāravāmācāratvātsarvaṃ vāmakareṇa tu || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 05 - Chapter Fifteen, part 4#^pg182|p. 164]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=182>))
>
> (The officiant) whose body is in this way the universe, and who, just by the deposition alone, has clearly become all things, should worship (his) body, which has become such, with flowers etc. and the (various) Mantras, separately, at length, or (just) briefly with (only) the main Mantra. So too, (he should worship it) with incense, food offerings, propitiatory offerings (tṛpti) and so on, all of these together, or (just) some (as desired). (with 15.276cd–278ab)
>
> (These rites) are contrary (vāma) to (the fettered condition of) transmigratory existence (saṁsāra), and so all the lustrations (tarpaṇa) should be applied by the teacher (deśika) with the left (vāma) hand, making use of the ring finger (and thumb). (with 15.278cd–279ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* ननु इदं न्यासादि सर्वं करकार्यं तत् करेण तावत् केन एतत् क्रियते ?--इति न जानीमः--इत्याशङ्क्य आह--

> संसारवामाचारत्वात्सर्वं वामकरेण तु ॥
> कुर्यात्तर्पणयोगं च दैशिकस्तदनामया ।

वामः संसारविपरीतो लोकबहिष्कृतो मुक्त्यनुगुणो रहस्य आचारः ॥
