# Likutei Moharan II 014

*Part II · Lesson 14* · [[Likutei Moharan]]

## 1

“For many are my adversaries, O Most High!” ([[Psalms 056#^56-3|Psalms 56:3]])—that is, he has enemies in the World Above, on high.3 This is as our Sages, of blessed memory, taught: Just as there are tormentors in the World Below, there are tormentors in the World Above ([[Sanhedrin 44#44b|Sanhedrin 44b]] and 103b).
