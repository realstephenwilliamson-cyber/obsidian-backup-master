# Proverbs 18 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Proverbs 17|← Proverbs 17]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Proverbs 19|Proverbs 19 →]]

**18:1** He that hath a mind to depart from a friend seeketh occasions: he shall ever be subject to reproach. ^18-1

**18:2** A fool receiveth not the words of prudence: unless thou say those things which are in his heart. ^18-2

**18:3** The wicked man when he is come into the depth of sins, contemneth: but ignominy and reproach follow him. ^18-3

**18:4** Words from the mouth of a men are as deep water: and the fountain of wisdom as an overflowing stream. ^18-4

**18:5** It is not good to accept the person of the wicked, to decline from the truth of judgment. ^18-5

**18:6** The lips of a fool intermeddle with strife: and his mouth provoketh quarrels. ^18-6

**18:7** The mouth of a fool is his destruction: and his lips are the ruin of his soul. ^18-7

**18:8** The words of the double tongued are as if they were harmless: and they reach even to the inner parts of the bowels. Fear casteth down the slothful: and the souls of the effeminate shall be hungry. ^18-8

**18:9** He that is loose and slack in his work, is the brother of him that wasteth his own works. ^18-9

**18:10** The name of the Lord is a strong tower: the just runneth to it, and shall be exalted. ^18-10

**18:11** The substance of the rich man is the city of his strength, and as a strong wall compassing him about. ^18-11

**18:12** Before destruction, the heart of a man is exalted: and before he be glorified, it is humbled. ^18-12

**18:13** He that answereth before he heareth sheweth himself to be a fool, and worthy of confusion. ^18-13

**18:14** The spirit of a man upholdeth his infirmity: but a spirit that is easily angered, who can bear? ^18-14

**18:15** A wise heart shall acquire knowledge: and the ear of the wise seeketh instruction. ^18-15

**18:16** A man’s gift enlargeth his may, and maketh him room before princes. ^18-16

**18:17** The just is first accuser of himself: his friend cometh, and shall search him. ^18-17

**18:18** The lot suppresseth contentions, and determineth even between the mighty. ^18-18

**18:19** A brother that is helped by his brother, is like a strong city: and judgments are like the bars of cities. ^18-19

**18:20** Of the fruit of a man’s mouth shall his belly be satisfied: and the offspring of his lips shall fill him. ^18-20

**18:21** Death and life are in the power of the tongue: they that love it, shall eat the fruits thereof. ^18-21

**18:22** He that hath found a good wife, hath found a good thing, and shall receive a pleasure from the Lord. He that driveth away a good wife, driveth away a good thing: but he that keepeth an adulteress, is foolish and wicked. ^18-22

**18:23** The poor will speak with supplications, and the rich will speak roughly. ^18-23

**18:24** A man amiable in society, shall be more friendly than a brother. ^18-24
