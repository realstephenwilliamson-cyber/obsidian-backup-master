## Chapter 32

**32:1** These three men ceased replying to Job, for he considered himself right. ^32-1

**32:2** Then Elihu son of Barachel the Buzite, of the family of Ram, was angry—angry at Job because he thought himself right against God. ^32-2

**32:3** He was angry as well at his three friends, because they found no reply, but merely condemned Job. ^32-3

**32:4** Elihu waited out Job’s speech, for they were all older than he. ^32-4

**32:5** But when Elihu saw that the three men had nothing to reply, he was angry. ^32-5

**32:6** Then Elihu son of Barachel the Buzite said in reply:  
I have but few years, while you are old;  
Therefore I was too awestruck and fearful  
To hold forth among you. ^32-6

**32:7** I thought, “Let age speak;  
Let advanced years declare wise things.” ^32-7

**32:8** But truly it is the spirit in men,  
The breath of Shaddai, that gives them understanding. ^32-8

**32:9** It is not the aged who are wise,  
The elders, who understand how to judge. ^32-9

**32:10** Therefore I say, “Listen to me;  
I too would hold forth.” ^32-10

**32:11** Here I have waited out your speeches,  
I have given ear to your insights,  
While you probed the issues; ^32-11

**32:12** But as I attended to you,  
I saw that none of you could argue with Job,  
Or offer replies to his statements. ^32-12

**32:13** I fear you will say, “We have found the wise course;  
God will defeat him, not man.” ^32-13

**32:14** He did not set out his case against me,  
Nor shall I use your reasons to reply to him. ^32-14

**32:15** They have been broken and can no longer reply;  
Words fail them. ^32-15

**32:16** I have waited till they stopped speaking,  
Till they ended and no longer replied. ^32-16

**32:17** Now I also would have my say;  
I too would like to hold forth, ^32-17

**32:18** For I am full of words;  
The wind in my belly presses me. ^32-18

**32:19** My belly is like wine not yet opened,  
Like jugs of new wine ready to burst. ^32-19

**32:20** Let me speak, then, and get relief;  
Let me open my lips and reply. ^32-20

**32:21** I would not show regard for any man,  
Or temper my speech for anyone’s sake; ^32-21

**32:22** For I do not know how to temper my speech—  
My Maker would soon carry me off! ^32-22
