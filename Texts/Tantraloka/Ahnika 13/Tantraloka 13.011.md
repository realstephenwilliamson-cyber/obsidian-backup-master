## Mūla

नहि कर्मास्ति तादृक्षं येन ज्ञानं प्रवर्तते ।
कर्मजत्वे च तज्ज्ञानं फलराशौ पतेद् ध्रुवम् ॥ ^mula

nahi karmāsti tādṛkṣaṃ yena jñānaṃ pravartate |
karmajatve ca tajjñānaṃ phalarāśau pated dhruvam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 05 - Chapter Thirteen, part 1#^pg149|p. 129]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=149>))
>
> There is no such kind of Karma that gives rise to knowledge. And (even if we assume) that that knowledge is a product of Karma, it would certainly fall amongst the mass of the fruits (of action).
