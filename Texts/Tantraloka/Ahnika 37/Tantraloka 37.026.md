## Mūla

अशेषतन्त्रसारं तु वामदक्षिणमाश्रितम् ।
एकत्र मिलितं कौलं श्रीषडर्धकशासने ॥ ^mula

aśeṣatantrasāraṃ tu vāmadakṣiṇamāśritam |
ekatra militaṃ kaulaṃ śrīṣaḍardhakaśāsane || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 11 - Chapter Thirty-Seven, part 1#^pg255|p. 237]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=255>))
>
> The Supreme Lord has declared this in the venerable Ratnamālā, (where we read): ‘the essence of all the Tantras, present in (those of) the Left (vāma) and Right (dakṣina), is united (milita) in one place as Kaula (doctrine, found) in the illustrious Trika system (ṣadardhaśāsana). (with 37.25cd–26)
