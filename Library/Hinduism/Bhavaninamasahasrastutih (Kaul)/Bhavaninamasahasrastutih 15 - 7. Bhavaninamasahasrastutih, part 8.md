Menā is the name of the wife of King Himalaya. She was so pious that even the great sages bore reverence towards her.? Parā-sakti assumed matured form? through her womb and was named Pārvatī, the ‘daughter of the king of mountains’. Menā stands for intelligence while Himalayas represent the ākāsa the ether, the first fundamental substance. Pārvati as the offspring is the conscious substance of the universe. She is also called Umā, the bright one and represents Brahmavidyā.

Hamsā is also the name of goddess ( Sarasvati) whose vehicle is a swan.
