## Mūla

एवं दैवस्त्वहोरात्र इति ह्यैक्योपसंहृतिः ।
तेन ये गुरवः श्रीमत्स्वच्छन्दोक्तिद्वयादितः ॥ ^mula

evaṃ daivastvahorātra iti hyaikyopasaṃhṛtiḥ |
tena ye guravaḥ śrīmatsvacchandoktidvayāditaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 11 - Chapter Six, part 4#^pg376|p. 361]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=376>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 6.137cd–138ab.
>
> The same teaching is seen in the venerable Svacchandaśāstra, (in a passage) beginning with the words, ‘that is a day and a night of the ancestors’, and ending with ‘such is the night and day of the gods’, which amounts to the same (thing). (with 6.136–137ab)
>
> Thus, those foolish teachers who, on the basis of the two statements made in the venerable Svacchanda and elsewhere, say that a year of the ancestors is a day of the gods, are mistaken. (with 6.137cd–138ab)

*Lead-in:* एवं पित्र्यं वर्षं देवानां दिनमिति यदन्यैरुक्तं तदयुक्तमेव -- इत्याह --

> तेन ये गुरवः श्रीमत्स्वच्छन्दोक्तिद्वयादितः ॥
> पित्र्यं वर्षं दिव्यदिनमूचुर्भ्रन्ता हि ते मुधा ।

"भ्रन्ता"इत्यत्र हेतुः "उक्तिद्वयादितः"इति । पितॄणामित्येकोक्तिः, एवं दैव इति द्वितीया, आदिशब्दाद्रुरुवृत्तिकारोक्तिः ॥ १३७ ॥
