# Psalms 123 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 122|← Psalms 122]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 124|Psalms 124 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 123|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**123:1** If it had not been that the Lord was with us, let Israel now say: ^123-1

**123:2** If it had not been that the Lord was with us, When men rose up against us, ^123-2

**123:3** Perhaps they had swallowed us up alive. When their fury was enkindled against us, ^123-3

**123:4** Perhaps the waters had swallowed us up. ^123-4

**123:5** Our soul hath passed through a torrent: perhaps our soul had passed through a water insupportable. ^123-5

**123:6** Blessed be the Lord, who hath not given us to be a prey to their teeth. ^123-6

**123:7** Our soul hath been delivered as a sparrow out of the snare of the followers. The snare is broken, and we are delivered. ^123-7

**123:8** Our help is in the name of the Lord, who made heaven and earth. ^123-8
