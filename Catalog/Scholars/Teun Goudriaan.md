# Teun Goudriaan

Last position: associate professor of Sanskrit and of Indian history, culture and religion at Utrecht University, 1965–1991; he retired in 1991 and died in Utrecht on 28 August 2016 ([Dutch Studies on South Asia](https://www.dutchstudies-satsea.nl/deelnemers/goudriaan-teun/); [INDOLOGY list, Sept. 2016](https://list.indology.info/pipermail/indology/2016-September/044179.html)).
