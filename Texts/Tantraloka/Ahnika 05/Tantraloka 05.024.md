## Mūla

मातृमानप्रमेयाख्यं धामाभेदेन भावयेत् ।
वह्न्यर्कसोमशक्तीनां तदेव त्रितयं भवेत् ॥ ^mula

mātṛmānaprameyākhyaṃ dhāmābhedena bhāvayet |
vahnyarkasomaśaktīnāṃ tadeva tritayaṃ bhavet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 03 - Chapter Five, part 1#^pg41|p. 26]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=41>))
>
> He should contemplate the (three) abodes of subject, means and object of knowledge of that possessor of power, at one (with each other and the Lord, the supreme subject), His abounding energy, Bhairava’s fiery effulgence (tejas). (with 5.23cd–24ab)
>
> That itself is the triad of the powers of Fire, Sun and Moon. (with 5.24cd)
