# Isaiah 1 (NRSVue)

— · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Isaiah 02|Isaiah 2 →]]

**1:1** The vision of Isaiah son of Amoz, which he saw concerning Judah and Jerusalem in the days of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah. ^1-1

## The wickedness of Judah

**1:2** Hear, O heavens, and listen, O earth, for the LORD has spoken: I reared children and brought them up, but they have rebelled against me. ^1-2

**1:3** The ox knows its owner and the donkey its master’s crib, but Israel does not know; my people do not understand. ^1-3

**1:4** Woe, sinful nation, people laden with iniquity, offspring who do evil, children who act corruptly, who have forsaken the LORD, who have despised the Holy One of Israel, \[\[who are utterly estranged!\]\]^[Gk OL lack: Heb adds *who are utterly estranged*] ^1-4

**1:5** Why do you seek further beatings? Why do you continue to rebel? The whole head is injured, and the whole heart faint. ^1-5

**1:6** From the sole of the foot to the head, there is no soundness in it, only bruises and sores and bleeding wounds; they have not been drained or bound up or softened with oil. ^1-6

**1:7** Your country lies desolate; your cities are burned with fire; in your very presence aliens devour your land; it is desolate, as overthrown by foreigners. ^1-7

**1:8** And daughter Zion is left like a booth in a vineyard, like a shelter in a cucumber field, like a besieged city. ^1-8

**1:9** If the LORD of hosts had not left us a few survivors, we would have been like Sodom and become like Gomorrah. ^1-9

**1:10** Hear the word of the LORD, you rulers of Sodom! Listen to the teaching of our God, you people of Gomorrah! ^1-10

**1:11** What to me is the multitude of your sacrifices? says the LORD; I have had enough of burnt offerings of rams and the fat of fed beasts; I do not delight in the blood of bulls or of lambs or of goats. ^1-11

**1:12** When you come to appear before me,^[Or *see my face*] who asked this from your hand? Trample my courts no more! ^1-12

**1:13** Bringing offerings is futile; incense is an abomination to me. New moon and Sabbath and calling of convocation—I cannot endure solemn assemblies with iniquity. ^1-13

**1:14** Your new moons and your appointed festivals my soul hates; they have become a burden to me; I am weary of bearing them. ^1-14

**1:15** When you stretch out your hands, I will hide my eyes from you; even though you make many prayers, I will not listen; your hands are full of blood. ^1-15

**1:16** Wash yourselves; make yourselves clean; remove your evil deeds from before my eyes; cease to do evil; ^1-16

**1:17** learn to do good; seek justice; rescue the oppressed; defend the orphan; plead for the widow. ^1-17

**1:18** Come now, let us argue it out, says the LORD: If your sins are like scarlet, will they become like snow? If they are red like crimson, will they become like wool? ^1-18

**1:19** If you are willing and obedient, you shall eat the good of the land, ^1-19

**1:20** but if you refuse and rebel, you shall be devoured by the sword, for the mouth of the LORD has spoken. ^1-20

## The degenerate city

**1:21** How the faithful city has become a prostitute! She that was full of justice, righteousness lodged in her—but now murderers! ^1-21

**1:22** Your silver has become dross; your wine is mixed with water. ^1-22

**1:23** Your princes are rebels and companions of thieves. Everyone loves a bribe and runs after gifts. They do not defend the orphan, and the widow’s cause does not come before them. ^1-23

**1:24** Therefore says the Sovereign, the LORD of hosts, the Mighty One of Israel: Surely I will pour out my wrath on my enemies and avenge myself on my foes! ^1-24

**1:25** I will turn my hand against you; I will smelt away your dross as with lye and remove all your alloy. ^1-25

**1:26** And I will restore your judges as at the first and your counselors as at the beginning. Afterward you shall be called the city of righteousness, the faithful city. ^1-26

**1:27** Zion shall be redeemed by justice, and those in her who repent, by righteousness. ^1-27

**1:28** But rebels and sinners shall be destroyed together, and those who forsake the LORD shall be consumed. ^1-28

**1:29** For you shall be ashamed of the oaks in which you delighted, and you shall blush for the gardens that you have chosen. ^1-29

**1:30** For you shall be like an oak whose leaf withers and like a garden without water. ^1-30

**1:31** The strong shall become like tinder and their work like a spark; they and their work shall burn together, with no one to quench them. ^1-31
