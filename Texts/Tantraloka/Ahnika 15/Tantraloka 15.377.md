## Mūla

षडुत्थे गन्धपुष्पाद्यैर्गणेशं ह्यैशगं यजेत् ।
उत्थितं विघ्नसंशान्त्यै पूजयित्वा विसर्जयेत् ॥ ^mula

ṣaḍutthe gandhapuṣpādyairgaṇeśaṃ hyaiśagaṃ yajet |
utthitaṃ vighnasaṃśāntyai pūjayitvā visarjayet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 07 - Chapter Fifteen, part 6#^pg248|p. 230]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=248>))
>
> Having done that, one should worship Ganeśa, who has arisen to quell obstacles and is located in the northeastern (quarter), with perfumes, flowers and the like. (He is on a throne) made of six (components), namely, Ananta and a lotus, (of which) the petals (are the four,) Dharma and the rest, and the calix made of true knowledge. Once worshipped, he should be dismissed. (with 15.376cd–377)
