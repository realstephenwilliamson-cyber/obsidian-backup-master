# LXX Proverbs 22

← [[LXX Proverbs 21]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Proverbs 23]] →

*Proverbs · [[LXX]]*

**22:1** A fair name is better than much wealth, and good favour is above silver and gold.  ^22-1

**22:2** The rich and the poor meet together; but the Lord made them both.  ^22-2

**22:3** An intelligent man seeing a bad man severely punished is himself instructed, but fools pass by and are punished.  ^22-3

**22:4** The fear of the Lord is the offspring of wisdom, and wealth, and glory, and life.  ^22-4

**22:5** Thistles and snares are in perverse ways; but he that keeps his soul will refrain from them.  ^22-5

**22:7** The rich will rule over the poor, and servants will lend to their own masters.  ^22-7

**22:8** He that sows wickedness shall reap troubles; and shall fully receive the punishment of his deeds. 8a God loves a cheerful and liberal man; but *a man* shall fully prove the folly of his works.[^1]  ^22-8

**22:9** He that has pity on the poor shall himself be maintained; for he has given of his own bread to the poor. 9a He that gives liberally secures victory and honour; but he takes away the life of them that possess *them*.  ^22-9

**22:10** Cast out a pestilent person from the council, and strife shall go out with him; for when he sits in the council he dishonours all.  ^22-10

**22:11** The Lord loves holy hearts, and all blameless persons are acceptable with him: a king rules with his lips.  ^22-11

**22:12** But the eyes of the Lord preserve discretion; but the transgressor despises *wise* words.  ^22-12

**22:13** The sluggard makes excuses, and says, *There is* a lion in the ways, and murderers in the streets.  ^22-13

**22:14** The mouth of a transgressor is a deep pit; and he that is hated of the Lord shall fall into it. 14a Evil ways are before a man, and he does not like to turn away from them; but it is needful to turn aside from a perverse and bad way.  ^22-14

**22:15** Folly is attached to the heart of a child, but the rod and instruction are *then* far from him.  ^22-15

**22:16** He that oppresses the poor, increases his own substance, yet gives to the rich so as to make it less.  ^22-16

**22:17** Incline thine ear to the words of wise men: hear also my word, and apply thine heart,  ^22-17

**22:18** that thou mayest know that they are good: and if thou lay them to heart, they shall also gladden thee on thy lips.  ^22-18

**22:19** That thy hope may be in the Lord, and he may make thy way known to thee.  ^22-19

**22:20** And do thou too repeatedly record them for thyself on the table of thine heart, for counsel and knowledge.  ^22-20

**22:21** I therefore teach thee truth, and knowledge good to hear; that thou mayest answer words of truth to them that question thee.[^2]  ^22-21

**22:22** Do no violence to the poor, for he is needy: neither dishonour the helpless *man* in the gates.  ^22-22

**22:23** For the Lord will plead his cause, and thou shalt deliver thy soul in safety.  ^22-23

**22:24** Be not companion to a furious man; neither lodge with a passionate man:  ^22-24

**22:25** lest thou learn of his ways, and get snares to thy soul.  ^22-25

**22:26** Become not surety from respect of a man's person.  ^22-26

**22:27** For if those have not whence to give compensation, they will take the bed *that is* under thee.  ^22-27

**22:28** Remove not the old landmarks, which thy fathers placed.[^3]  ^22-28

**22:29** It is fit that an observant man and *one* diligent in his business should attend on kings, and not attend on slothful men.  ^22-29

[^1]: 22:8a See 2 Cor. 9. 7. Compare *Heb.*
[^2]: 22:21 See 1 Pet. 3. 15.
[^3]: 22:28 *Gr.* eternal.
