# Sirach 37 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Sirach 36|← Sirach 36]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Sirach 38|Sirach 38 →]]

## False friends

**37:1** Every friend says, “I, too, am a friend,” but some friends are friends only in name. ^37-1

**37:2** Does not sorrow draw near to death when a companion and friend^[Heb reads *a friend like yourself*] turns into an enemy? ^37-2

**37:3** O inclination to evil, why were you formed to cover the land with deceit? ^37-3

**37:4** Some companions rejoice in the happiness of friends, but in time of trouble they are against them. ^37-4

**37:5** Some companions help a friend for their stomachs’ sake, yet in battle they will carry his shield. ^37-5

**37:6** Do not forget friends during the battle,^[Heb: Gk *in your heart*] and do not be unmindful of them when you distribute your spoils.^[Heb: Gk *him in your wealth*] ^37-6

## Caution in taking advice

**37:7** All counselors praise the counsel they give, but some give counsel in their own interest. ^37-7

**37:8** Be wary of counselors and learn first what is their interest, for they will take thought for themselves. They may cast the lot against you ^37-8

**37:9** and tell you, “Your way is good,” and then stand aside to see what happens to you. ^37-9

**37:10** Do not consult the one who regards you with suspicion; hide your intentions from those who are jealous of you. ^37-10

**37:11** Do not consult with a woman about her rival or with a coward about war, with a merchant about business or with a buyer about selling, with a miser about generosity^[Gk: Heb *with an evil person about generosity*] or with the merciless about kindness, with an idler about any work or with a seasonal laborer about finishing the job, with a lazy slave about a big task—pay no attention to any advice they give. ^37-11

**37:12** But associate with a godly person whom you know to be a keeper of the commandments, who is like-minded with yourself, and who will grieve with you if you fail. ^37-12

**37:13** And heed^[Heb: Gk *establish*] the counsel of your own heart, for no one is more faithful to you than it is. ^37-13

**37:14** For our own mind sometimes keeps us better informed than seven sentinels sitting high on a watchtower. ^37-14

**37:15** But above all pray to the Most High that he may direct your way in truth. ^37-15

## True and false wisdom

**37:16** Discussion is the beginning of every work, and counsel precedes every undertaking. ^37-16

**37:17** The mind is the root of all counsel;^[Heb: Gk *change*] ^37-17

**37:18** it sprouts four branches,^[Heb: Gk *four kinds of destiny appear*] good and evil, life and death, and it is the tongue that continually rules them. ^37-18

**37:19** Some people may be clever enough to teach many and yet be useless to themselves. ^37-19

**37:20** Skillful speakers may be hated; they will be destitute of all food, ^37-20

**37:21** for the Lord has withheld the gift of charm, since they are lacking in all wisdom. ^37-21

**37:22** If people are wise to their own advantage, the fruits of their good sense will be praiseworthy.^[Other ancient authorities read *trustworthy*] ^37-22

**37:23** The wise instruct their own people, and the fruits of their good sense are trustworthy. ^37-23

**37:24** The wise will have praise heaped upon them, and all who see them will call them happy. ^37-24

**37:25** The days of a person’s life are numbered, but the days of Israel are without number. ^37-25

**37:26** Those who are wise among their people will inherit honor,^[Other ancient authorities read *confidence*] and their names will live forever. ^37-26

## Concerning moderation

**37:27** My child, test yourself while you live; see what is bad for you, and do not give in to it. ^37-27

**37:28** For not everything is good for everyone, and no one enjoys everything. ^37-28

**37:29** Do not be greedy for every delicacy, and do not eat without restraint, ^37-29

**37:30** for overeating brings sickness, and gluttony leads to nausea. ^37-30

**37:31** Many have died of gluttony, but those who guard against it prolong their lives. ^37-31
