# Mnemotechnics vs Mnemo-technologies

> [!abstract] Definition
> Stiegler’s distinction between reflective, disciplined memory arts practiced by the subject (*mnemotechnics*) and automated, industrial technical apparatuses that expropriate and commodify memory (*mnemo-technologies*).

## The Contrast
- **Mnemotechnics:** Deliberate practices of human cultivation and memory retention developed in dialogue with tools (e.g., the art of memory, marginalia, Talmudic debate, monastic liturgy, musical scales). Here, the tool is subordinated to the discipline of spiritual and cognitive formation.
- **Mnemo-technologies:** Industrialized systems where the memory function is completely automated and internalized by the technical apparatus (e.g., algorithmic feeds, search indexing, predictive generative models).

## Consequences of Industrialization
When mnemotechnics are replaced by mnemo-technologies without corresponding social practices:
1. **Proletarianization of Mind:** Individuals lose their *savoir-faire* (know-how) and *savoir-vivre* (knowledge of how to live), becoming passive consumers of machine-curated memories.
2. **Temporal Disorientation:** Subjective retention is synchronized to the real-time demands of commercial media (*hyper-synchronization*).
