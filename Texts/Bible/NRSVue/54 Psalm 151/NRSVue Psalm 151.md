# Psalm 151 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue|index]]

*This psalm is ascribed to David as his own composition (though it is outside the number*^[Other ancient authorities add *of the one hundred fifty* (psalms)]), after he

*had fought in single combat with Goliath.*

**1:1** I was small among my brothers and the youngest in my father’s house; I tended my father’s sheep. ^1-1

**1:2** My hands made a harp; my fingers fashioned a lyre. ^1-2

**1:3** And who will tell my Lord? The Lord himself; it is he who hears.^[Other ancient authorities add *everything*; others add *me*; others read *who will hear me*] ^1-3

**1:4** It was he who sent his messenger^[Gk: Q ms *prophet*] and took me from my father’s sheep and anointed me with his anointing oil. ^1-4

**1:5** My brothers were handsome and tall, but the Lord was not pleased with them. ^1-5

**1:6** I went out to meet the foreigner,^[Gk: Q ms *Philistine*] and he cursed me by his idols. ^1-6

**1:7** But I drew his own sword; I beheaded him and removed disgrace from the people of Israel. ^1-7
