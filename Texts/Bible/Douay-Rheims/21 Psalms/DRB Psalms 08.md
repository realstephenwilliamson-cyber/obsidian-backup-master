# Psalms 8 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 07|← Psalms 7]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 09|Psalms 9 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 08|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**8:1** Unto the end, for the presses: a psalm of David. ^8-1

**8:2** O Lord our Lord, how admirable is thy name in the whole earth! For thy magnificence is elevated above the heavens. ^8-2

**8:3** Out of the mouth of infants and of sucklings thou hast perfected praise, because of thy enemies, that thou mayst destroy the enemy and the avenger. ^8-3

**8:4** For I will behold thy heavens, the works of thy fingers: the moon and the stars which thou hast founded. ^8-4

**8:5** What is man that thou art mindful of him? or the son of man that thou visitest hi? ^8-5

**8:6** Thou hast made him a little less than the angels, thou hast crowned him with glory and honour: ^8-6

**8:7** And hast set him over the works of thy hands. ^8-7

**8:8** Thou hast subjected all things under his feet, all sheep and oxen: moreover the beasts also of the fields. ^8-8

**8:9** The birds of the air, and the fishes of the sea, that pass through the paths of the sea. ^8-9

**8:10** O Lord our Lord, how admirable is thy name in all the earth! ^8-10
