# Haftarot

[[Torah]]

The haftarah (the reading from the Prophets) for each parashah, as Sefaria's calendar gives it for the diaspora. The Ashkenazi reading comes first; the Sephardi column is filled only where Sefaria's Sephardi reading differs, so an empty cell means the same reading, and "—" marks a row for which the table gives no reading (see the notes under it). Each link opens the first verse of the passage in the JPS Tanakh notes. Chapter and verse numbers are the Hebrew ones, as in Sefaria and the JPS notes; book names are Sefaria's (II Kings, not 2 Kings). Each parashah note repeats its readings in a *Haftarah* section; the readings for two portions read together follow the second of them.

- **Vayakhel and Metzora read alone.** In the study plan each is read alone once, and that Shabbat's haftarah is read: Vayakhel on Shabbat Shekalim (2027-03-06), Metzora on Shabbat HaGadol (2027-04-17). On those dates Sefaria gives the special Shabbat's haftarah, so neither portion's own haftarah is in this table.
- **Vayeilech read alone** falls between Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur, so always on Shabbat Shuvah, and Shuvah's haftarah is read (in the study plan, 2029-09-15). Sefaria's calendar gives none of Vayeilech's own.
- **Vayakhel-Pekudei** always falls on Shabbat Parah or Shabbat HaChodesh, and that Shabbat's haftarah is read (in the study plan, 2028-03-25 HaChodesh and 2029-03-10 Parah). Sefaria's calendar gives none of the joint portion's own.
- **Pinchas on or before 17 Tammuz.** Sefaria gives this reading on its Israel calendar for 2022-07-16, when 17 Tammuz fell on Shabbat; in the years it covers there is no diaspora date with Pinchas before 17 Tammuz to check. The study plan's Pinchas Shabbatot (2027-07-24, 2028-07-15, 2029-07-07) all fall after 17 Tammuz, and on them Sefaria gives Jeremiah 1:1–2:3.
- **Matot and Masei read separately.** In the years Sefaria's calendar covers, this happens only on the Israel calendar, from which these two rows come (2022-07-23 and 2022-07-30). In the study plan they are read together (2027-07-31, 2028-07-22, 2029-07-14).
- **V'Zot HaBerachah** is read on Simchat Torah, not on a Shabbat, so Sefaria's Shabbat calendar has no entry for it.

## Special Shabbatot

The haftarah of a special Shabbat is read in place of the week's own; the table gives what Sefaria's calendar shows on the dates read. It gives the Ashkenazi reading only, because Sefaria's Sephardi entry for Parah, HaChodesh, Rosh Chodesh and Machar Chodesh changes from date to date (on some dates it is the week's own Sephardi haftarah). For Shabbat Shekalim Sefaria gives the same Sephardi reading on both dates read: [[II Kings 11#^11-17|II Kings 11:17–12:17]].
