## Mūla

विज्ञानं तद्विकल्पात्मधर्मकोटीरपि स्पृशेत् ।
एकाशीतिपदोदारशक्त्यामर्शात्मकस्ततः ॥ ^mula

vijñānaṃ tadvikalpātmadharmakoṭīrapi spṛśet |

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 02 - Chapter Seven#^pg43|p. 25]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=43>))
>
> Therefore, as long as another pulsation (spanda) (of perception) does not arise, a single unitary (determinative) consciousness (vijñāna) persists, even though it may be in contact with a hundred thousand qualities (dharma) which are (all) determinate perceptions (vikalpa). (Thus, this single consciousness conjoins one determinate perception with another (anusandhi) in such a way as to make daily life possible). (with 7.36cd–37ab)
>
> Thus, it was explained previously that the (one unified) thought construct that reflects on the venerable energies of the Mantra with eighty-one parts (can grasp their unified whole, which is experienced within the one universal iva consciousness, and so) bestows Śivahood. (with 7.37cd–38ab)
