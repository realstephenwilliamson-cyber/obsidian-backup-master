# Psalm 108 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 107|← Psalm 107]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 109|Psalm 109 →]]

## Praise and prayer for victory

*A Song. A Psalm of David.*

**108:1** My heart is steadfast, O God, my heart is steadfast;^[Heb mss Gk Syr: MT lacks *my heart is steadfast*] I will sing and make melody. Awake, my soul!^[Cn Compare 57.8: Heb *also my soul*] ^108-1

**108:2** Awake, O harp and lyre! I will awake the dawn. ^108-2

**108:3** I will give thanks to you, O LORD, among the peoples, and I will sing praises to you among the nations. ^108-3

**108:4** For your steadfast love is higher than the heavens, and your faithfulness reaches to the clouds. ^108-4

**108:5** Be exalted, O God, above the heavens, and let your glory be over all the earth. ^108-5

**108:6** Give victory with your right hand, and answer me, so that those whom you love may be rescued. ^108-6

**108:7** God has promised in his sanctuary:^[Or *by his holiness*] “With exultation I will divide up Shechem and portion out the Vale of Succoth. ^108-7

**108:8** Gilead is mine; Manasseh is mine; Ephraim is my helmet; Judah is my scepter. ^108-8

**108:9** Moab is my washbasin; on Edom I hurl my shoe; over Philistia I shout in triumph.” ^108-9

**108:10** Who will bring me to the fortified city? Who will lead me to Edom? ^108-10

**108:11** Have you not rejected us, O God? You do not go out, O God, with our armies. ^108-11

**108:12** O grant us help against the foe, for human help is worthless. ^108-12

**108:13** With God we shall do valiantly; it is he who will tread down our foes. ^108-13
