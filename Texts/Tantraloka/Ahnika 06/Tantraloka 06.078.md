## Mūla

प्रकाशविश्रमवशात्तावेव हि दिनक्षपे ।
संवित्प्रतिक्षणं यस्मात्प्रकाशानन्दयोगिनी ॥ ^mula

prakāśaviśramavaśāttāveva hi dinakṣape |
saṃvitpratikṣaṇaṃ yasmātprakāśānandayoginī || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 10 - Chapter Six, part 3#^pg318|p. 303]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=318>))
>
> A Day and Night is divided by a lunar day, which extends for two and a quarter fingers’ space. (Thus) there are two – Day and Night – because (consciousness and the vital breath has two aspects. One is) Light (prakāśa) (corresponding to the Day), and (the other is) Rest (viśrama) (corresponding to the Night). (with 6.77cd–78ab)
>
> Every moment, consciousness is associated with Light and Bliss, and so those two, Day and Night, are such, fashioned as they are by that (same consciousness). (with 6.78cd–79ab)

## Viveka

> संवित्प्रतिक्षणं यस्मात्प्रकाशानन्दयोगिनी ॥
> तौ कॢप्तौ यावति तया तावत्येव दिनक्षपे ।

प्रतिक्षणमिति, सदैव प्रकाशानन्दमयीत्यर्थः । अतश्च तथा संविद उदयः प्रमातॄणां वेद्यग्रहणपरत्वादेस्तारतम्यात्कस्यचित्क्षणः कस्यचित्कल्पः कस्यचिन्निमेषोऽपि वा स्यात् ॥ ७८ ॥
