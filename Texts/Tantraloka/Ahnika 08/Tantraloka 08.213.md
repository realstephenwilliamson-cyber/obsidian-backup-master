## Mūla

श्रीकामिकायां कश्मीरवर्णने चोक्तवान्विभुः ।
सुरेश्वरीमहाधाम्नि ये म्रियन्ते च तत्पुरे ॥ ^mula

śrīkāmikāyāṃ kaśmīravarṇane coktavānvibhuḥ |
sureśvarīmahādhāmni ye mriyante ca tatpure || ^iast

> And the All-pervasive Lord has said in the venerable Kāmikā, in the course of describing Kashmir (kaśmīravarnana), that ‘the creatures who die in the great abode (sacred to) Sureśvarī and in that city, whether they belong to (highest castes) starting with Brahmins (or any other) down to (the lowest) mixed castes, animals, or (even) plants of the lowest order, (all) are ranked as Rudras (rudrajāti)’ (with 8.213–214)
