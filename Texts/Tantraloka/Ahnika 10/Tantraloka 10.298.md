## Mūla

लयाकले तु स्वं रूपं जाग्रत्तत्पूर्ववृत्ति तु ।
स्वप्नादीति क्रमं सर्वं सर्वत्रानुसरेद् बुधः ॥ ^mula

layākale tu svaṃ rūpaṃ jāgrattatpūrvavṛtti tu |
svapnādīti kramaṃ sarvaṃ sarvatrānusared budhaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 14 - Chapter Ten, part 5#^pg507|p. 488]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=507>))
>
> (Thus, in the division into thirteen in which the subjects) start from the Dissolution Deconditioned (perceiver – pralayākala), the waking state corresponds to his Own Nature, dreaming (to the Consciousness Deconditioned perceiver – vijñānākala) that precedes it, and so on (progressively). The wise man should thus follow the entire sequence everywhere (in all cases).
