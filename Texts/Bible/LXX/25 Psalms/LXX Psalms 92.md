# LXX Psalms 92

← [[LXX Psalms 91]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 93]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 093]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 093]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**92:1** For the day before the Sabbath, when the land was *first* inhabited, the praise of a Song by David. The Lord reigns; he has clothed himself with honour: the Lord has clothed and girded himself with strength; for he has established the world, which shall not be moved.[^1]  ^92-1

**92:2** Thy throne is prepared of old: thou art from everlasting.  ^92-2

**92:3** The rivers have lifted up, O Lord, the rivers have lifted up their voices,[^2]  ^92-3

**92:4** at the voices of many waters: the billows of the sea are wonderful: the Lord is wonderful in high places.[^3]  ^92-4

**92:5** Thy testimonies are made very sure: holiness becomes thine house, O Lord, for ever.[^4]  ^92-5

[^1]: 92:1 *Alex.* the sabbath-day.
[^2]: 92:3 *Alex.*+ the floods will lift up their waves. See *Heb.*
[^3]: 92:4 *q. d.* in answer to.
[^4]: 92:5 *Gr.* to length of days.
