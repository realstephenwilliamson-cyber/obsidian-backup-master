## Instructions concerning prayer

**2:1** First of all, then, I urge that supplications, prayers, intercessions, and thanksgivings be made for everyone, ^2-1

**2:2** for kings and all who are in high positions, so that we may lead a quiet and peaceable life in all godliness and dignity. ^2-2

**2:3** This is right and acceptable before God our Savior, ^2-3

**2:4** who desires everyone to be saved and to come to the knowledge of the truth. ^2-4

**2:5** For there is one God; there is also one mediator between God and humankind, Christ Jesus, himself human, ^2-5

**2:6** who gave himself a ransom for all—this was attested at the right time. ^2-6

**2:7** For this I was appointed a herald and an apostle (I am telling the truth;^[Other ancient authorities add *in Christ*] I am not lying), a teacher of the gentiles in faith and truth. ^2-7

**2:8** I desire, then, that in every place the men should pray, lifting up holy hands without anger or argument, ^2-8

**2:9** also that the women should dress themselves in moderate clothing with reverence and self-control, not with their hair braided or with gold, pearls, or expensive clothes, ^2-9

**2:10** but with good works, as is proper for women who profess reverence for God. ^2-10

**2:11** Let a woman^[Or *wife*] learn in silence with full submission. ^2-11

**2:12** I do not permit a woman^[Or *wife*] to teach or to have authority over a man;^[Or *her husband*] she is to keep silent. ^2-12

**2:13** For Adam was formed first, then Eve, ^2-13

**2:14** and Adam was not deceived, but the woman was deceived and became a transgressor. ^2-14

**2:15** Yet she will be saved through childbearing, provided they continue in faith and love and holiness, with self-control. ^2-15
