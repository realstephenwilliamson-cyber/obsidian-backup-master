## Mūla

अथ श्राद्धविधिः श्रीमत्षडर्धोक्तो निगद्यते ॥ ^mula

atha śrāddhavidhiḥ śrīmatṣaḍardhokto nigadyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 16 - Chapter Twenty-Five, opening page#^pg375|p. 355]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=375>))
>
> We will now explain the ritual procedure for the worship of the ancestors (Śrāddha) taught in the venerable Trika (scriptures).
>
> This is mentioned in the Siddhātantra, in the course of describing the Worship of the Icons (mā iga). (with 25.1–2ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* इदानीं द्वितीयार्धेन श्राद्धविधिमभिधातुमाह--

> अथ श्राद्धविधिः श्रीमत्षडर्धोक्तो निगद्यते ॥
