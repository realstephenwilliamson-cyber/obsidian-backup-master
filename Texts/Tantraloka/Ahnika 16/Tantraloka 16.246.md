## Mūla

यदा शोध्यं विना शोद्धृन्यासस्तत्रापि मन्त्रतः ।
जननादिक्रमं कुर्यात्तत्त्वसंश्लेषवर्जितम् ॥ ^mula

yadā śodhyaṃ vinā śoddhṛnyāsastatrāpi mantrataḥ |
jananādikramaṃ kuryāttattvasaṃśleṣavarjitam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 07 - Chapter Sixteen, part 3#^pg127|p. 107]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=127>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 16.245cd–246ab.
>
> If the purifying (mantras) are deposited without the object of purification, then there (in that case) also, (the teacher) should (anyway) perform the rite (krama) (in which the disciple’s soul is made) to take birth etc. (in the worlds) by means of (the appropriate) mantras, (but this should be done) without conjoining (him) with the principles.
