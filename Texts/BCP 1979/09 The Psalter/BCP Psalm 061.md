# Psalm 61

← [[BCP Psalm 060]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 062]] →

*Exaudi, Deus* · Book Two · Eleventh Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 061]]

**61:1** Hear my cry, O God, \*  
and listen to my prayer. ^61-1

**61:2** I call upon you from the ends of the earth  
with heaviness in my heart; \*  
set me upon the rock that is higher than I. ^61-2

**61:3** For you have been my refuge, \*  
a strong tower against the enemy. ^61-3

**61:4** I will dwell in your house for ever; \*  
I will take refuge under the cover of your wings. ^61-4

**61:5** For you, O God, have heard my vows; \*  
you have granted me the heritage of those who fear your Name. ^61-5

**61:6** Add length of days to the king's life; \*  
let his years extend over many generations. ^61-6

[p. 669] ^pg669

**61:7** Let him sit enthroned before God for ever; \*  
bid love and faithfulness watch over him. ^61-7

**61:8** So will I always sing the praise of your Name, \*  
and day by day I will fulfill my vows. ^61-8
