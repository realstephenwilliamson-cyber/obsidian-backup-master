## Mūla

इयतैव शिवं यायात् सद्यो भोगान् विभुज्य वा ।
श्रीमद्दीक्षोत्तरे चोक्तं चारे षट्त्रिंशदंगुले ॥ ^mula

iyataiva śivaṃ yāyāt sadyo bhogān vibhujya vā |
śrīmaddīkṣottare coktaṃ cāre ṣaṭtriṃśadaṃgule || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 08 - Chapter Seventeen#^pg199|p. 179]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=199>))
>
> In the course of an initiation without seed, (the teacher) should also purify the fetters, which (in that case) are the rules (initiates with seed must observe). He who has received an initiation without seed and has devotion to the God, to the fire, and to the teacher, may by virtue of this alone go to Śiva, either immediately or after fully enjoying the worldly benefits (he desires). (with 17.96–97ab)
>
> And it is said in the venerable Dikṣottara that ‘travelling (through the body of the disciple) for (spans of) of thirty-six fingers, (the teacher) should gradually (kramāt) abandon the reality levels (tattva) and the worlds from the feet up to the end of the head, (one after another). (Then,) thinking (dhyāyan) for just a moment (tuṭi) that — “the undifferentiated (niṣkala) disembodied (form of Śiva) am I, (who am) supreme. I cast him (my disciple) there by means of (Śiva’s) power” — he should initiate him.’ (with 17.97cd–99ab)
