## Mūla

नवच्छिद्रगतं चैकं नदन्तं व्यापकं ध्रुवम् ।
अनया हि खचारी श्रीयोगसञ्चार उच्यते ॥ ^mula

navacchidragataṃ caikaṃ nadantaṃ vyāpakaṃ dhruvam |
anayā hi khacārī śrīyogasañcāra ucyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 06 - Chapter Thirty-Two#^pg168|p. 150]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=168>))
>
> By (making the) sound (nāda) HĀ! (the yogi) sees (his own) Self wandering within the bodies of others and (that of) another (person) within his own body. Established on the plane of the going and coming (of the vital breath), by letting (it) go and taking (it) up, he is present in the nine cavities (of the body, moving through them back and forth). One (alone), within that, he is pervasive and well fixed (dhruva). It is said in the venerable Yogasamṁcāra that by virtue of this (mudrā), he is indeed a Skyfarer (khacāārin). (with 32.30–31)

## Viveka

> [!viveka]- Viveka in English (Dyczkowski, vol. XI, pp. 150–151)
> ![[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 06 - Chapter Thirty-Two#Viveka on 32.30–31]]

> परदेहेषु चात्मानं परं चात्मशरीरतः ।
> पश्येच्चरन्तं हानादाद्गमागमपदस्थितम् ॥
> नवच्छिद्रगतं चैकं नदन्तं व्यापकं ध्रुवम् ।
> अनया हि खचारी श्रीयोगसञ्चार उच्यते ॥

हानादेति--हाकारस्य नादेन उच्चारेण--इत्यर्थः । गमागमेति-- स्वदेहात् परदेहे, परदेहाद्वा स्वदेहे । खचारीत्यनेनापि खेचरीमुद्राबन्धानुवेधो दर्शितः ॥ ३१ ॥
