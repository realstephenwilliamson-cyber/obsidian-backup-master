# Toḍalatantra — Patala 9

[[Todalatantra 08|← Patala 8]] · [[Todalatantra|← index]] · [[Todalatantra 10|Patala 10 →]]

śrīdevī uvāca

^s1

tripurāyā mahāmantraṃ nityātantre śrutaṃ mayā

^s2

idānīṃ śrotum icchāmi navārṇasya ca vāsanām

^s3

śrīśiva uvāca

^s4

bhūmiś candraḥ śivo māyā śaktiḥ kṛśānusādanau

^s5

ardhacandraśca binduśca navārṇo merurucyate

^s6

bhūmibījajapādeva bhūpatir jāyate 'cirāt

^s7

candrabījajapādeva mahāsaundaryamāpnuyāt

^s8

śivabījajapād eva śivavadviharet kṣitau

^s9

ekatroccāraṇāt satyaṃ caturvargaphalapradam

^s10

śrīdevī uvāca

^s11

yattvayā kathitaṃ nātha yogajñānādikaṃ layam

^s12

yāvat kāmādi saṃdīpyo bhāvayogo na labhyate

^s13

ūrdhvaretā mahāyogī tadadhaḥ śaktiyogataḥ

^s14

brūhi me jagatāṃnātha kathaṃ dīrghāyuṣaṃ bhavet

^s15

śrīśiva uvāca

^s16

śṛṇu devi pravakṣyāmi yena dīrghāyuṣaṃ bhavet

^s17

śrutvā gopaya yatnena na prakāśyaṃ kadācana

^s18

pūjayet kālikāṃ devīṃ tāriṇīṃ vātha sundarīm

^s19

ṣoḍaśenopacāreṇa pañcatattvena pārvati

^s20

dinatrayaṃ pūjayitvā ṣaṭcakraṃ cintayedatha

^s21

tatastu parameśāni mālāmantraṃ samabhyaset

^s22

ṣoḍaśī vā mahāpūrvā pattre cābhyāsamācaret

^s23

catuṣpattre cāṣṭavāraṃ ṣaṭpattre dvādaśaṃ japet

^s24

daśapattre viṃśatiṃ ca dvādaśe vedanetrakam

^s25

ṣoḍaśe daśasaṃkhyaṃ tu caturvāraṃ śruvo'ṣṭakam

^s26

kumbhakena japenmantraṃ mālāṣaṭke sureśvari

^s27

evaṃ krameṇa deveśi sthiramāyuryadā bhavet

^s28

prajapedakṣamālāyāṃ yena mṛtyuṃjayo bhavet

^s29

śrīdevī uvāca

^s30

mūlacakrācchiro'ntā ca suṣumnā parikīrtitā

^s31

tadgarbhasthā ca yā śaktiḥ sā devī kuṇḍarūpikā

^s32

sadā kuṇḍalinī devī pañcāśadvarṇabhūṣitā

^s33

mālārūpā kathaṃ deva iti me saṃśayo hṛdi

^s34

śrīśiva uvāca

^s35

sahasrāre mahāpadme bījakośe śivālaye

^s36

haṃsena vāyuyogena pūrakeṇa samānayet

^s37

sahasrāraṃ tu samprāpya śivaṃ dṛṣṭvā tu kāminī

^s38

mālākāreṇa talliṅgaṃ saṃveṣṭya kuṇḍalī sadā

^s39

akārādilakārāntā kṣakāraṃ vaktrasaṃyutam

^s40

caramārṇaṃ sarandhraṃ ca nijapucchena kāminī

^s41

bhedayitvā svapucchena nāgapāśaṃ tadūrdhvake

^s42

etatkāraṇaṃ saṃvyāpya saṃsthitā kuṇḍalī yadā

^s43

tadaiva prajapenmantram amaratvaṃ sa vindati

^s44

recanāt kāminī devī praviśantī svaketanam

^s45

na tatra prajapenmantraṃ japānmṛtyumavāpnuyāt

^s46

recake chinnamālāyāṃ satyaṃ hi suravandite

^s47

chinne sūtre bhavenmṛtyuḥ puraiva kathitaṃ mayā

^s48

iti te kathitaṃ kānte yato mṛtyuṃjayo bhavet

^s49

athavā nijanāsāgre dṛṣṭimāropya yatnataḥ

^s50

vāyuṃ saṃdhārya yatnena ekoccāreṇa coccaret

^s51

mālāmantraṃ ṣoḍaśīṃ vā tathā cāṣṭādaśākṣarīm

^s52

sahasraṃ prajapenmantraṃ pratyahaṃ yadi pārvati

^s53

jitvā mṛtyuṃ jarāṃ rogaṃ dīrghakālaṃ sa jīvati

^s54

etadanyaṃ mahāyogaṃ bhogārthī nahi labhyate

^s55

athavā parameśāni ḍāmaroktavidhānataḥ

^s56

yajet kātyāyanīṃ devīṃ bhūtapūrvāṃ prayatnataḥ

^s57

tadā pañcasahasrābdaṃ niścitaṃ tu sa jīvati

^s58

iti te kathitaṃ sarvaṃ deharakṣaṇakāraṇam

^s59

na bhogī bhogamāpnoti yogī yogo na labhyate

^s60

etattattvena deveśi bhogo yogāyate dhruvam

^s61

śrīdevī uvāca

^s62

yanmālāyāṃ japenaiva kiṃ phalaṃ labhate naraḥ

^s63

pṛthak pṛthaṅ mahādeva pattramālāphalaṃ vada

^s64

śrīśiva uvāca

^s65

bhūmikāmī japen mantraṃ mūlādhāre catuṣṭaye

^s66

svādhiṣṭhāne japādeva mahendro jāyate'cirāt

^s67

maṇipūre japādeva bhavet svargasya bhājanam

^s68

anāhate mahāpadme japād brahmapuraṃ vrajet

^s69

viśuddhākhye japādeva viṣṇuloke vased dhruvam

^s70

ājñācakre ca japtavye śvetadvīpe vaset sadā

^s71

yanmālā parameśāni bāhyamālā prakīrtitā

^s72

antarmālā mahāmālā pañcāśadvarṇarūpiṇī

^s73

māhātmyaṃ tasya deveśi kiṃ vaktuṃ śakyate mayā

^s74

sahasrāre sthirībhūya yadi cāṣṭaśataṃ japet

^s75

tatphalāt koṭibhāgaikaṃ bhāgaṃ cānyena vidyate

^s76

pṛthivyāmavyayo deho bhavatyeva na saṃśayaḥ

^s77

yanmālāyāṃ japenmantramabhyāsārthaṃ hi pārvati

^s78

iti te kathitaṃ devi cirajīvī yathā bhavet

^s79

idānīṃ parameśāni bhūtakātyāyanīṃ śṛṇu

^s80

vedādyaṃ śabdabījaṃ ca mahāmāyāṃ tataḥ param

^s81

astrayugmaṃ vahnijāyā manuḥ saptākṣaraḥ paraḥ

^s82

śrīśivo'sya ṛṣiḥ prokto virāṭ chanda udāhṛtam

^s83

bhūtakātyāyanī devī dharmārthakāmadā sadā

^s84

praṇavena ṣaḍaṅgaṃ ca prāṇāyāmaṃ ca sundari

^s85

dhyānamasyāḥ pravakṣyāmi śṛṇu sundari sādaram

^s86

gauravarṇāṃ muktakeśīṃ sarvābharaṇabhūṣitām

^s87

paṭṭavastraparīdhānāṃ sadā ghūrṇitalocanām

^s88

vāmapāṇau raktapūrṇakharparaṃ dakṣiṇe kare

^s89

madyapūrṇasvarṇapātrāṃ grīvāyāṃ hārabhūṣitām

^s90

śaraccandrasamābhāsāṃ pādāṅgulivirājitām

^s91

evaṃ tāṃ varadāṃ nityāṃ bhāvayet siddhihetave

^s92

sāmānyārghyaṃ tato devi svavāme vinyasettataḥ

^s93

jīvanyāsādikaṃ kṛtvā pūjayet parameśvarīm

^s94

ṣoḍaśenopacāreṇa pañcatattvena sundari

^s95

yajettāṃ bahuyatnena gṛhamadhye dinatrayam

^s96

tato japenmahāmantraṃ gajāntakasahasrakam

^s97

tataśca pūjayed devīṃ śūnyāgāre dinatrayam

^s98

pratyahaṃ prajapenmantraṃ gajāntakasahasrakam

^s99

eva kṛte maheśāni yadi siddhirna jāyate

^s100

tataśca prajapenmantraṃ pitṛbhūmau dinatrayam

^s101

tataḥ siddhirbhaved devi satyaṃ satyaṃ hi supriye

^s102

tadvākyaṃ prārthanāvākyaṃ ḍāmarākhye mayoditam

^s103
