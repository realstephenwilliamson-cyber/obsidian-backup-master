## Mūla

यतः समस्तभावानां शिवात्सिद्धिमयादथो ।
पूर्णादव्यतिरेकित्वं कारकाणामिहार्चया ॥ ^mula

yataḥ samastabhāvānāṃ śivātsiddhimayādatho |
pūrṇādavyatirekitvaṃ kārakāṇāmihārcayā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 04 - Chapter Fifteen, part 3#^pg113|p. 95]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=113>))
>
> Since here (in our view), by ritual worship (arcā) all things (are realised to be) inseparable from Śiva, (who manifests, as the case may be,) as consisting of (all possible divine) attainments (for those who aspire to worldly benefits), or as (perfectly) full (and unlimited for those who seek liberation), (the same applies) to the factors that constitute action (kāraka), (each expressed by its own grammatical function, that work together to execute the action expressed by a verb). 2
