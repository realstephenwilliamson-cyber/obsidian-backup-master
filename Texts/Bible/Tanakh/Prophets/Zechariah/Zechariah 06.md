## Chapter 6

**6:1** I looked up again, and I saw: Four chariots were coming out from between the two mountains; the mountains were of copper. ^6-1

**6:2** The horses of the first chariot were bay, the horses of the second chariot were black; ^6-2

**6:3** the horses of the third chariot were white, and the horses of the fourth chariot were spotted—dappled. ^6-3

**6:4** And I spoke up and asked the angel who talked with me: “What are those, my lord?” ^6-4

**6:5** In reply, the angel said to me, “Those are the four winds of heaven coming out after presenting themselves to the Lord of all the earth. ^6-5

**6:6** The one with the black horses is going out to the region of the north; the white ones have gone out[^1] to what is to the west of them;[^2] the spotted ones have gone out[^3] to the region of the south; ^6-6

**6:7** and the dappled ones have gone out…”[^4] They were ready to start out and range the earth, and he gave them the order, “Start out and range the earth!” And they ranged the earth. ^6-7

**6:8** Then he alerted me, and said to me, “Take good note! Those that went out to the region of the north have done my pleasure[^5] in the region of the north.”[^6] ^6-8

**6:9** The word of the LORD came to me: ^6-9

**6:10** Receive from[^7] the exiled community—from Heldai, Tobijah, and Jedaiah, who have come from Babylon—and you, in turn, proceed the same day to the house of Josiah son of Zephaniah. ^6-10

**6:11** Take silver and gold and make crowns. Place [one] on the head of High Priest Joshua son of Jehozadak, ^6-11

**6:12** and say to him, “Thus said the LORD of Hosts: Behold, a man called the Branch[^8] shall branch out from the place where he is, and he shall build the Temple of the LORD. ^6-12

**6:13** He shall build the Temple of the LORD and shall assume majesty, and he shall sit on his throne and rule. And there shall also be a priest seated on his throne,[^9] and harmonious understanding shall prevail between them.” ^6-13

**6:14** The crowns shall remain in the Temple of the LORD as a memorial to Helem,[^10] Tobijah, Jedaiah, and Hen[^11] son of Zephaniah. ^6-14

**6:15** Men from far away shall come and take part in the building of the Temple of the LORD, and you shall know that I have been sent to you by the LORD of Hosts—if only you will obey the LORD your God! ^6-15

[^1]: Change of vocalization yields “will go out.”
[^2]: Cf. ’aḥor, “west,” Isa. 9.11. Emendation yields “the region of the west.”
[^3]: Change of vocalization yields “will go out.”
[^4]: Emendation yields “the bay ones will go out to the region of the east.”
[^5]: Cf. postbiblical naḥath ruaḥ, “gratification.” Emendation yields, “done the Lord’s pleasure.”
[^6]: I.e., Babylonia, whose communication with Judah was via North Mesopotamia and Syria; cf. 2.10–11.
[^7]: Emendation yields “the gift of.”
[^8]: See note at 3.8.
[^9]: Septuagint reads “on his right side.”
[^10]: The Syriac version reads “Heldai”; cf. v. 10.
[^11]: In v. 10, “Josiah.”
