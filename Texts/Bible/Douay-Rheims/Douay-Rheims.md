# Douay-Rheims

The Holy Bible in English, Douay-Rheims American edition of 1899, translated from the Latin Vulgate (eBible.org's text, `engDRA`, source files dated 3 November 2022; public domain). Added 13 October 2026 as the English of the Vulgate (decision D22). One note per chapter, a link target for each verse (`[[DRB Genesis 01#^1-3]]`), the books in the Douay order: 46 in the Old Testament, with Tobit, Judith, Wisdom, Sirach (Ecclesiasticus), Baruch and 1–2 Maccabees, and Esther (16 chapters) and Daniel (14 chapters) with the parts that come from the Greek; 27 in the New. 1,334 chapters, 35,811 verses.

- **Names.** The notes use eBible's book names (1 Samuel, 1 Kings, 1 Chronicles …); the table gives the Douay names (1 Kings, 3 Kings, 1 Paralipomenon …).
- **Numbering.** The chapters and verses are the Vulgate's. The Psalms are numbered as in the Vulgate and the Septuagint, so each Psalms note links its LXX note; for the Hebrew numbering see [[Psalms numbering]]. A psalm's title, where it has one, is part of its first verse.
- **Not in this text:** notes and commentary (the eBible text has none), and the Strong's numbers of the source files (left out).
