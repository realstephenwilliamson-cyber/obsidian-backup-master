# Psalms 37 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 36|← Psalms 36]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 38|Psalms 38 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 37|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**37:1** A psalm for David, for a remembrance of the sabbath. ^37-1

**37:2** Rebuke me not, O Lord, in thy indignation; nor chastise me in thy wrath. ^37-2

**37:3** For thy arrows are fastened in me: and thy hand hath been strong upon me. ^37-3

**37:4** There is no health in my flesh, because of thy wrath: there is no peace for my bones, because of my sins. ^37-4

**37:5** For my iniquities are gone over my head: and as a heavy burden are become heavy upon me. ^37-5

**37:6** My sores are putrified and corrupted, because of my foolishness. ^37-6

**37:7** I am become miserable, and am bowed down even to the end: I walked sorrowful all the day long. ^37-7

**37:8** For my loins are filled with illusions; and there is no health in my flesh. ^37-8

**37:9** I am afflicted and humbled exceedingly: I roared with the groaning of my heart. ^37-9

**37:10** Lord, all my desire is before thee, and my groaning is not hidden from thee. ^37-10

**37:11** My heart is troubled, my strength hath left me, and the light of my eyes itself is not with me. ^37-11

**37:12** My friends and my neighbours have drawn near, and stood against me. And they that were near me stood afar off: ^37-12

**37:13** And they that sought my soul used violence. And they that sought evils to me spoke vain things, and studied deceits all the day long. ^37-13

**37:14** But I, as a deaf man, heard not: and as a dumb man not opening his mouth. ^37-14

**37:15** And I became as a man that heareth not: and that hath no reproofs in his mouth. ^37-15

**37:16** For in thee, O Lord, have I hoped: thou wilt hear me, O Lord my God. ^37-16

**37:17** For I said: Lest at any time my enemies rejoice over me: and whilst my feet are moved, they speak great things against me. ^37-17

**37:18** For I am ready for scourges: and my sorrow is continually before me. ^37-18

**37:19** For I will declare my inequity: and I will think for my sin. ^37-19

**37:20** But my enemies live, and are stronger that I: and they hate me wrongfully are multiplied. ^37-20

**37:21** They that render evil for good, have detracted me, because I followed goodness. ^37-21

**37:22** Forsake me not, O Lord my God: do not thou depart from me. ^37-22

**37:23** Attend unto my help, O Lord, the God of my salvation. ^37-23
