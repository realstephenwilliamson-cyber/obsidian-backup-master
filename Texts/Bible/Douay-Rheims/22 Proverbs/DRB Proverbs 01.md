# Proverbs 1 (Douay-Rheims)

— · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Proverbs 02|Proverbs 2 →]]

**1:1** The parables of Solomon, the son of David, king of Israel. ^1-1

**1:2** To know wisdom, and instruction: ^1-2

**1:3** To understand the words of prudence: and to receive the instruction of doctrine, justice, and judgment, and equity: ^1-3

**1:4** To give subtilty to little ones, to the young man knowledge and understanding. ^1-4

**1:5** A wise man shall hear and shall be wiser: and he that understandeth, shall possess governments. ^1-5

**1:6** He shall understand a parable, and the interpretation, the words of the wise, and their mysterious sayings. ^1-6

**1:7** The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom. Fools despise wisdom and instruction. ^1-7

**1:8** My son, hear the instruction of thy father, and forsake not the law of thy mother: ^1-8

**1:9** That grace may be added to thy head, and a chain of gold to thy neck. ^1-9

**1:10** My son, if sinners shall entice thee, consent not to them. ^1-10

**1:11** If they shall say: Come with us, let us lie in wait for blood, let us hide snares for the innocent without cause: ^1-11

**1:12** Let us swallow him up alive like hell, and whole as one that goeth down into the pit. ^1-12

**1:13** We shall find all precious substance, we shall fill our houses with spoils. ^1-13

**1:14** Cast in thy lot with us, let us all have one purse. ^1-14

**1:15** My son, walk not thou with them, restrain thy foot from their paths. ^1-15

**1:16** For their feet run to evil, and make haste to shed blood. ^1-16

**1:17** But a net is spread in vain before the eyes of them that have wings. ^1-17

**1:18** And they themselves lie in wait for their own blood, and practise deceits against their own souls. ^1-18

**1:19** So the wage of every covetous man destroy the souls of the possessors. ^1-19

**1:20** Wisdom preacheth abroad, she uttereth her voice in the streets: ^1-20

**1:21** At the head of multitudes she crieth out, in the entrance of the gates of the city she uttereth her words, saying: ^1-21

**1:22** O children, how long will you love childishness, and fools covet those things which are hurtful to themselves, and the unwise hate knowledge? ^1-22

**1:23** Turn ye at my reproof: behold I will utter my spirit to you, and will shew you my words. ^1-23

**1:24** Because I called, and you refused: I stretched out my hand, and there was none that regarded. ^1-24

**1:25** You have despised all my counsel, and have neglected my reprehensions. ^1-25

**1:26** I also will laugh in your destruction, and will mock when that shall come to you which you feared. ^1-26

**1:27** When sudden calamity shall fall on you, and destruction, as a tempest, shall be at hand: when tribulation and distress shall come upon you: ^1-27

**1:28** Then shall they call upon me, and I will not hear: they shall rise in the morning and shall not find me: ^1-28

**1:29** Because they have hated instruction and received not the fear of the Lord, ^1-29

**1:30** Nor consented to my counsel, but despised all my reproof. ^1-30

**1:31** Therefore they shall eat the fruit of their own way, and shall be filled with their own devices. ^1-31

**1:32** The turning away of little ones shall kill them, and the prosperity of fools shall destroy them. ^1-32

**1:33** But he that shall hear me, shall rest without terror, and shall enjoy abundance, without fear of evils. ^1-33
