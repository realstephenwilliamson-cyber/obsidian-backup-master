# Mishneh Torah - Positive Mitzvot 01

← [[Mishneh Torah - Transmission of the Oral Law 01]] · [[Mishneh Torah - Positive Mitzvot]] · [[Mishneh Torah - Negative Mitzvot 01]] →

*Introduction · Positive Mitzvot · Chapter 1* · [[Mishneh Torah - Positive Mitzvot]] · [[Mishneh Torah]]

**1.** The first of the positive commandments is the mitzvah to know that there is a God, as \[[[Torah 17 - Yitro#^20-2|Exodus 20:2]]] states: "I am God, your Lord." ^1-1

**2.** To unify Him, as \[[[Torah 45 - Vaetchanan#^6-4|Deuteronomy 6:4]]] states: "God is our Lord, God is one." ^1-2

**3.** To love Him, as \[[[Torah 45 - Vaetchanan#^6-5|Deuteronomy 6:5]]] states: "And you shall love God, your Lord." ^1-3

**4.** To fear Him, as \[[[Torah 45 - Vaetchanan#^6-13|Deuteronomy 6:13]]] states: "Fear God, your Lord." ^1-4

**5.** To pray, as \[[[Torah 18 - Mishpatim#^23-25|Exodus 23:25]]] states: "And you shall serve God, your Lord." This service is prayer. ^1-5

**6.** To cling to Him, as \[[[Torah 46 - Eikev#^10-20|Deuteronomy 10:20]]] states: "And you shall cling to Him." ^1-6

**7.** To swear in His name, as \[[[Torah 46 - Eikev#^10-20|Deuteronomy 10:20]]] states: "And you shall swear in His name." ^1-7

**8.** To emulate His good and just ways, as \[[[Torah 50 - Ki Tavo#^28-9|Deuteronomy 28:9]]] states: "And you shall walk in His ways." ^1-8

**9.** To sanctify His name, as \[[[Torah 31 - Emor#^22-32|Leviticus 22:32]]] states: "And I shall be sanctified amidst the children of Israel." ^1-9

**10.** To recite the *Shema* twice daily, as \[[[Torah 45 - Vaetchanan#^6-7|Deuteronomy 6:7]]] states: "And you shall speak of them when you lie down and when you arise." ^1-10

**11.** To study Torah and to teach it [to others], as \[[[Torah 45 - Vaetchanan#^6-7|Deuteronomy 6:7]]] states: "And you shall teach them to your children." ^1-11

**12.** To tie *tefillin* upon our heads, as \[[[Torah 45 - Vaetchanan#^6-8|Deuteronomy 6:8]]] states: "And they shall be an emblem between your eyes." ^1-12

**13.** To tie *tefillin* upon our arms, as \[[[Torah 45 - Vaetchanan#^6-8|Deuteronomy 6:8]]] states: "And you shall tie them for a sign upon your arms." ^1-13

**14.** To make *tzitzit*, as \[[[Torah 37 - Sh'lach#^15-38|Numbers 15:38]]] states: "And you shall make *tzitzit* for them." ^1-14

**15.** To affix a *mezuzah*, as \[[[Torah 45 - Vaetchanan#^6-9|Deuteronomy 6:9]]] states: "And you shall write them on the doorposts of your home." ^1-15

**16.** To collect the people to hear the Torah [being read] in the year following the *shemitah* year, as \[[[Torah 52 - Vayeilech#^31-12|Deuteronomy 31:12]]] states: "Gather together the people." ^1-16

**17.** For each man to write a Torah scroll for himself, as \[[[Torah 52 - Vayeilech#^31-19|Deuteronomy 31:19]]] states: "Write down this song." ^1-17

**18.** For the king to write a second Torah scroll for himself in addition to the one which [he is required to write to fulfill the mitzvah incumbent upon] every man. Thus, he will have two Torah scrolls, as \[[[Torah 48 - Shoftim#^17-19|Deuteronomy 17:19]]] states: "And he shall write for himself a copy of this Torah." ^1-18

**19.** To bless [God] after eating, as \[[[Torah 46 - Eikev#^8-10|Deuteronomy 8:10]]] states: "[After] you eat and are satisfied, you shall bless God, your Lord." ^1-19

**20.** To build [God's] chosen house, [the Temple,] as \[[[Torah 19 - Terumah#^25-8|Exodus 25:8]]] states: "And you shall make a sanctuary for Me." ^1-20

**21.** To revere this house, as \[[[Torah 30 - Kedoshim#^19-30|Leviticus 19:30]]] states: "And revere My sanctuary." ^1-21

**22.** To keep watch over this house continuously, as \[[[Torah 38 - Korach#^18-2|Numbers 18:2]],4] states: "And you and- your descendants before the Tent of Testimony.... [And they shall keep the watch....]" ^1-22

**23.** For the Levites to serve in the sanctuary, as \[[[Torah 38 - Korach#^18-23|Numbers 18:23]]] states: "And the Levite shall serve...." ^1-23

**24.** For a priest to sanctify his hands and feet at the time of service [in the Temple], as \[[[Torah 21 - Ki Tisa#^30-19|Exodus 30:19]]] states: "And Aaron and his sons will wash...." ^1-24

**25.** To prepare the candles of the Sanctuary, as \[[[Torah 20 - Tetzaveh#^27-21|Exodus 27:21]]] states: "Aaron and his sons shall prepare it." ^1-25

**26.** For the priests to bless the Jews, as \[[[Torah 35 - Nasso#^6-23|Numbers 6:23]]] states: "In this manner, you shall bless the children of Israel." ^1-26

**27.** To arrange bread and frankincense before God [in the Temple] on every Sabbath, as \[[[Torah 19 - Terumah#^25-30|Exodus 25:30]]] states: "And you shall place the showbread on the table." ^1-27

**28.** To burn a spice offering twice [each] day, as \[[[Torah 20 - Tetzaveh#^30-7|Exodus 30:7]]] states: "And Aaron shall burn incense upon it...." ^1-28

**29.** To keep a fire burning on the altar for burnt offerings continually, as \[[[Torah 25 - Tzav#^6-6|Leviticus 6:6]]] states: "And you shall keep a fire burning continuously on the altar." ^1-29

**30.** To remove the ashes from the altar, as \[[[Torah 25 - Tzav#^6-3|Leviticus 6:3]]] states: "And he shall remove the ashes...." ^1-30

**31.** To send impure people out from the camp of the Divine Presence - i.e., the sanctuary - as \[[[Torah 35 - Nasso#^5-2|Numbers 5:2]]] states: "And you shall send from the camp all the leprous, any zav, and anyone who has contracted impurity because of a corpse." ^1-31

**32.** To show honor to Aaron's descendants and to give them priority regarding all holy matters, as \[[[Torah 31 - Emor#^21-8|Leviticus 21:8]]] states: "And you shall sanctify him." ^1-32

**33.** To clothe the priests with the priestly garments for service [in the Temple], as \[[[Torah 20 - Tetzaveh#^28-2|Exodus 28:2]]] states: "And you shall make holy garments...." ^1-33

**34.** To carry the ark upon our shoulders when it is to be carried, as \[[[Torah 35 - Nasso#^7-9|Numbers 7:9]]] states: "And they shall carry it on their shoulders." ^1-34

**35.** To anoint the High Priests and kings with the anointing oil, as \[[[Torah 21 - Ki Tisa#^30-30|Exodus 30:30-31]]] states: "[Anoint Aaron....] This shall be sacred anointing oil." ^1-35

**36.** For the priests to serve in the sanctuary in individual watches; and for them to all serve as one on the festivals, as \[[[Torah 48 - Shoftim#^18-6|Deuteronomy 18:6-8]]] states: "When the Levite shall come... [he can serve]...with the exception of that which is theirs by ancestral right." ^1-36

**37.** For the priests to become ritually impure and mourn for their relatives in the same manner as other Jews who are commanded to mourn, as \[[[Torah 31 - Emor#^21-3|Leviticus 21:3]]] states: "He shall become impure for her." ^1-37

**38.** For a High Priest to marry a virgin, as \[[[Torah 31 - Emor#^21-13|Leviticus 21:13]]] states: "And he shall marry a woman who is a virgin." ^1-38

**39.** To offer the *tamid* offerings each day, as \[[[Torah 41 - Pinchas#^28-3|Numbers 28:3]]] states: "[This is the fire offering...] two each day continuously." ^1-39

**40.** For the High Priest to offer a meal offering each day, as \[[[Torah 25 - Tzav#^6-13|Leviticus 6:13]]] states: "This is the sacrifice of Aaron and his descendants." ^1-40

**41.** To offer an additional sacrifice every Sabbath, as \[[[Torah 41 - Pinchas#^28-9|Numbers 28:9]]] states: "On the Sabbath day, two lambs...." ^1-41

**42.** To offer an additional sacrifice every Rosh Chodesh, as \[[[Torah 41 - Pinchas#^28-11|Numbers 28:11]]] states: "On your Rashei Chodashim...." ^1-42

**43.** To offer an additional sacrifice on Pesach, as \[[[Torah 31 - Emor#^23-36|Leviticus 23:36]]] states: "For seven days, you will bring a fire offering unto God...."[^1] ^1-43

**44.** To bring the meal offering of the *omer* on the day after the first day of Pesach together with a single lamb, as \[[[Torah 31 - Emor#^23-10|Leviticus 23:10]]] states: "And you shall bring the *omer...."* ^1-44

**45.** To offer an additional sacrifice on Shavuot, as \[[[Torah 41 - Pinchas#^28-26|Numbers 28:26]]] states: "On the day of the first fruits *[Bikkurim]...."* ^1-45

**46.** To bring two loaves and the sacrifices which accompany the loaves on Shavuot, as \[[[Torah 31 - Emor#^23-17|Leviticus 23:17]]] states: "From your dwellings, bring bread as a wave offering. And you shall offer upon the bread...." ^1-46

**47.** To offer an additional sacrifice on Rosh HaShanah, as \[[[Torah 41 - Pinchas#^29-1|Numbers 29:1]]] states: "And in the seventh month, on the first of the month...." ^1-47

**48.** To offer an additional sacrifice on the fast [of Yom Kippur], as \[[[Torah 41 - Pinchas#^29-7|Numbers 29:7]]] states: "On the tenth of the seventh month...." ^1-48

**49.** To carry out the service of the fast [of Yom Kippur], as \[[[Torah 29 - Achrei Mot#^16-3|Leviticus 16:3]]] states: "In this manner, Aaron will enter the [inner] sanctuary, with a young bull...." All the [particulars of] this service are stated in the *parashah* of *Acharei Mot.* ^1-49

**50.** To offer an additional sacrifice on the holiday of Sukkot, as \[[[Torah 41 - Pinchas#^29-13|Numbers 29:13]]] states: "And you shall present a burnt offering as a pleasing fragrance...." ^1-50

**51.** To offer an additional sacrifice on *Shemini Atzeret,* for it is a festival in its own right, as \[[[Torah 41 - Pinchas#^29-35|Numbers 29:35]]] states: "And on the eighth day.... ^1-51

**52.** To celebrate on the festivals, as \[[[Torah 18 - Mishpatim#^23-14|Exodus 23:14]]] states: "And you shall celebrate three festivals for Me." ^1-52

**53.** To appear [before God in the Temple] on the festivals, as \[[[Torah 47 - Re'eh#^16-16|Deuteronomy 16:16]]] states: "On three occasions during the year, all your males shall appear...." ^1-53

**54.** To rejoice on the festivals, as \[[[Torah 47 - Re'eh#^16-14|Deuteronomy 16:14]]] states: "And you shall rejoice on your festivals." ^1-54

**55.** To slaughter the Paschal lamb, as \[[[Torah 15 - Bo#^12-6|Exodus 12:6]]] states: "And the entire congregation shall slaughter it...." ^1-55

**56.** To eat the meat of the Paschal sacrifice roasted on the night of the fifteenth of Nisan, as \[[[Torah 15 - Bo#^12-8|Exodus 12:8]]] states: "And they shall eat the meat...." ^1-56

**57.** To offer the second Paschal sacrifice, as \[[[Torah 36 - Beha'alotcha#^9-11|Numbers 9:11]]] states: "In the second month, on the fourteenth of the month...." ^1-57

**58.** To eat the meat of the second Paschal offering together with matzot and bitter herbs, as \[[[Torah 36 - Beha'alotcha#^9-11|Numbers 9:11]]] states: "And you shall eat it with matzot and bitter herbs." ^1-58

**59.** To sound the trumpets when the sacrifices [are offered] and in times of difficulty, as \[[[Torah 36 - Beha'alotcha#^10-10|Numbers 10:10]]] states: "And you shall sound the trumpets...." ^1-59

**60.** For all animals to be sacrificed after their eighth day [of life], as \[[[Torah 31 - Emor#^22-27|Leviticus 22:27]]] states: "And on the eighth day and afterwards...." ^1-60

**61.** For all animal offerings to be unblemished, as \[[[Torah 31 - Emor#^22-21|Leviticus 22:21]]] states: "[When it is] unblemished, it will be desirable...." ^1-61

**62.** To salt all the sacrifices, as \[[[Torah 24 - Vayikra#^2-13|Leviticus 2:13]]] states: "Offer salt on all your sacrifices." ^1-62

**63.** The burnt offering, as \[[[Torah 24 - Vayikra#^1-3|Leviticus 1:3]]] states:"If his is a burnt offering...." ^1-63

**64.** The sin offering, as \[[[Torah 25 - Tzav#^6-18|Leviticus 6:18]]] states: "These are the laws of the sin offering...." ^1-64

**65.** The guilt offering, as \[[[Torah 25 - Tzav#^7-1|Leviticus 7:1]]] states: "These are the laws of the guilt offering...." ^1-65

**66.** The peace offering, as \[[[Torah 25 - Tzav#^7-11|Leviticus 7:11]]] states: "These are the laws of the peace offering...." ^1-66

**67.** The meal offering, as \[[[Torah 24 - Vayikra#^2-1|Leviticus 2:1]]] states: "When a person presents a meal offering...." ^1-67

**68.** For the [High] Court to offer a sacrifice if it renders an erroneous decision, as \[[[Torah 24 - Vayikra#^4-13|Leviticus 4:13]]] states: "If the entire congregation of Israel shall err...." ^1-68

**69.** For each individual to offer a sin offering if he unintentionally violates a negative commandment punishable by *karet*[^2], as \[[[Torah 24 - Vayikra#^5-1|Leviticus 5:1]]] states: "When a person sins...."[^3] ^1-69

**70.** For an individual to offer a sacrifice if he is in doubt whether or not he transgressed a prohibition for which he would be liable for a sin offering, as \[[[Torah 24 - Vayikra#^5-17|Leviticus 5:17-18]]] states: "If he does not know, he still bears responsibility...and he shall bring his guilt offering." This is referred to as "the conditional guilt offering." ^1-70

**71.** For [the following individuals:] a person who unknowingly used sacred property, a person who sinned by stealing, one [who had relations with] a maidservant designated for another person, or one who denied possession of an entrusted object and took a [false] oath, to bring a guilt offering. This is referred to as "the unconditional guilt offering."[^4] ^1-71

**72.** To offer "the adjustable guilt offering" [as atonement for the violation of certain transgressions], as \[[[Torah 24 - Vayikra#^5-1|Leviticus 5:1]],11] states: "If his means are not sufficient.... If his means do not suffice...." ^1-72

**73.** For a person to confess before God for any sin which he has committed. [This applies] when he brings a sacrifice [for atonement] and when he does not bring a sacrifice, as \[[[Torah 35 - Nasso#^5-6|Numbers 5:6]]] states: "And they shall confess the sins that they committed."[^5] ^1-73

**74.** For a *zav* to offer a sacrifice when he becomes purified [after his affliction], as \[[[Torah 28 - Metzora#^15-13|Leviticus 15:13]]] states: "When a *zav* will become pure...." ^1-74

**75.** For a *zavah* to offer a sacrifice when she be-comes purified [after her affliction], as \[[[Torah 28 - Metzora#^15-28|Leviticus 15:28]]] states: "When she becomes pure from [the condition] of *zavah...."* ^1-75

**76.** For a woman who gave birth to offer a sacrifice after she becomes purified, as \[[[Torah 27 - Tazria#^12-6|Leviticus 12:6]]] states: "And after the conclusion of her pure days...." ^1-76

**77.** For a person afflicted with *tzara’at* to offer a sacrifice after he becomes purified, as \[[[Torah 28 - Metzora#^14-1|Leviticus 14:1]]] states: "On the eighth day, he shall take...." ^1-77

**78.** To tithe one's herds, as \[[[Torah 33 - Bechukotai#^27-32|Leviticus 27:32]]] states: "And all the tithes of your cattle and sheep...." ^1-78

**79.** To sanctify the firstborn of a kosher animal and offer it as a sacrifice, as \[[[Torah 47 - Re'eh#^15-19|Deuteronomy 15:19]]] states: "Every firstling which shall be born...." ^1-79

**80.** To redeem firstborn sons, as \[[[Torah 38 - Korach#^18-15|Numbers 18:15]]] states: "However, you must surely redeem first-born humans." ^1-80

**81.** To redeem a firstling donkey, as \[[[Torah 21 - Ki Tisa#^34-20|Exodus 34:20]]] states: "Redeem a firstling donkey with a sheep." ^1-81

**82.** To decapitate a firstling donkey [which is not redeemed], as \[[[Torah 21 - Ki Tisa#^34-20|Exodus 34:20]]] states: "If you do not redeem it, you must decapitate it." ^1-82

**83.** For a person to bring all the sacrifices for which he is liable, be they obligatory or voluntary offerings, on the first pilgrimage festival that occurs, as \[[[Torah 47 - Re'eh#^12-5|Deuteronomy 12:5-6]]] states: "And you shall come there... and you shall bring there...." ^1-83

**84.** To sacrifice all the offerings in [God's] chosen house, [i.e., the Temple,], as \[[[Torah 47 - Re'eh#^12-14|Deuteronomy 12:14]]] states: "There, you will perform all that I command you." ^1-84

**85.** To tend to all the offerings from the Diaspora and bring them to *Eretz* [*Yisrael*], to [God's] chosen house, [i.e., the Temple,] as \[[[Torah 47 - Re'eh#^12-26|Deuteronomy 12:26]]] states: "Take the sacred offerings which you possess, and your pledges, and come [to the place that God will choose]." Based on the oral tradition, we have learned that this applies to sacred offerings from the Diaspora. ^1-85

**86.** To redeem sacred offerings that have become blemished, and thus have them to be permitted [for mundane use], as \[[[Torah 47 - Re'eh#^12-15|Deuteronomy 12:15]]] states: "But whenever you desire, you may slaughter...." Based on the oral tradition, we have learned that this refers only to sacred offerings which have become disqualified and have been redeemed. ^1-86

**87.** That an animal substituted for a sacred offering be regarded as sacred, as \[[[Torah 33 - Bechukotai#^27-33|Leviticus 27:33]]] states: "Both [the original animal] and the one substituted for it shall be consecrated." ^1-87

**88.** To eat the remains of the meal offerings, as \[[[Torah 25 - Tzav#^6-9|Leviticus 6:9]]] states: "Aaron and his sons will eat the remainder of it." ^1-88

**89.** To eat the flesh of the sin and guilt offerings,[^6] as \[[[Torah 20 - Tetzaveh#^29-33|Exodus 29:33]]] states: "And they shall eat [the sacrifices] with which atonement was made for them." ^1-89

**90.** To burn sacred meat which became impure, as \[[[Torah 25 - Tzav#^7-19|Leviticus 7:19]]] states: "And the flesh which touches anything impure... [shall be burnt]." ^1-90

**91.** To burn the leftovers [from the sacrifices], as \[[[Torah 25 - Tzav#^7-17|Leviticus 7:17]]] states: "The leftovers from the flesh of the sacrifice shall be burnt with fire on the third day." ^1-91

**92.** For a Nazirite to grow his hair long, as \[[[Torah 35 - Nasso#^6-5|Numbers 6:5]]] states: "He shall let the hair of his head grow without cutting it." ^1-92

**93.** For a Nazarite to shave his hair when bringing his sacrifices at the conclusion of his term as a Nazirite, or during his Nazirite term if he becomes impure, as \[[[Torah 35 - Nasso#^6-9|Numbers 6:9]]] states: "Should a person die in his presence,... [he must shave....]" ^1-93

**94.** For a person to fulfill any [promise] which he utters, be it a sacrifice, [a gift to] charity, or the like, as \[[[Torah 49 - Ki Teitzei#^23-24|Deuteronomy 23:24]]] states: "What you have spoken, take heed to fulfill...." ^1-94

**95.** To carry out the laws regarding the nullification of vows, as mentioned in the Torah. ^1-95

**96.** For everyone who comes in contact with the corpse of an animal to become impure, as \[[[Torah 26 - Shmini#^11-39|Leviticus 11:39]]] states: "Should an animal die...." ^1-96

**97.** For the [dead bodies of] the eight species of crawling animals [mentioned in the Torah] to impart ritual impurity, as \[[[Torah 26 - Shmini#^11-19|Leviticus 11:19]]] states: "These shall be impure for you...." ^1-97

**98.** For [certain] foods to impart impurity, as \[[[Torah 26 - Shmini#^11-34|Leviticus 11:34]]] states: "From all the food which you will eat...." ^1-98

**99.** For [a woman in the] *niddah* state to be impure and to impart impurity to others. ^1-99

**100.** For [a woman] who gives birth to be impure [like a woman] in the *niddah* state. ^1-100

**101.** For a person afflicted with *tzara’at* to be impure and to impart impurity. ^1-101

**102.** For a garment afflicted with *tzara’at* to be impure and to impart impurity. ^1-102

**103.** For a house afflicted with *tzara’at* to impart impurity. ^1-103

**104.** For *a zav* to impart impurity. ^1-104

**105.** For semen to impart impurity. ^1-105

**106.** For a *zavah* to impart impurity. ^1-106

**107.** For a corpse to impart impurity. ^1-107

**108.** For the sprinkling water [used for the purification process involving the red heifer] to impart impurity to a person who is ritually pure, and to impart ritual purity to a person who is ritually impure solely because of contact with a human corpse. All the laws dealing with these different types of impurity and the majority of the judgments regarding all types of ritual purity and impurity are explained explicitly in the Written Law.[^7] ^1-108

**109.** For the process of purification from all types of ritual impurity to involve immersion in the waters of a *mikveh*, as \[[[Torah 28 - Metzora#^15-16|Leviticus 15:16]]] states: "And he shall wash all his flesh in water." Based on the oral tradition, we have learned that this washing [involves immersion in a body of] water in which one's entire body can immerse at one time. ^1-109

**110.** For the process of purification from *tzara’at*, be it a person afflicted with *tzara’at* or a house afflicted with *tzara’at*, [to involve] a staff of cedar, a hyssop, the crimson wool, two birds, and spring water, as \[[[Torah 28 - Metzora#^14-2|Leviticus 14:2]]] states: "This shall be the purification process for the person afflicted with *tzara’at*...." ^1-110

**111.** For a person afflicted with *tzara’at* to shave all of his hair, as \[[[Torah 28 - Metzora#^14-9|Leviticus 14:9]]] states: "And it shall be on the seventh day, he shall shave all his hair." ^1-111

**112.** For a person afflicted with *tzara’at* to make known his condition to all others, according to the instructions mentioned in \[[[Torah 27 - Tazria#^13-45|Leviticus 13:45]]]: "His garments shall be torn, his hair shall grow uncut, he shall cover his face to the lip, and he shall cry out: `Impure! Impure!"' Similarly, all others who are ritually impure must make known their condition. ^1-112

**113.** To prepare the red heifer so that its ashes will be ready, as \[[[Torah 39 - Chukat#^19-9|Numbers 19:9]]] states: "And it will be a keepsake for the congregation of Israel." ^1-113

**114.** For a person who makes an endowment valuation to give the specific amount of money stated in the [Torah] portion, as \[[[Torah 33 - Bechukotai#^27-2|Leviticus 27:2]]] states: "When a person expresses a vow...." ^1-114

**115.** For a person who makes an endowment valuation concerning a non-kosher animal to give [the required amount of] money, as \[[[Torah 33 - Bechukotai#^27-11|Leviticus 27:11]]] states: "And he shall cause the animal to stand...." ^1-115

**116.** For a person who makes an endowment valuation concerning his home to give the value determined by the priest, as \[[[Torah 33 - Bechukotai#^27-14|Leviticus 27:14]]] states: "And the priest shall determine its value." ^1-116

**117.** For a person who consecrates his field to give the fixed amount determined by the [Torah], as \[[[Torah 33 - Bechukotai#^27-16|Leviticus 27:16]]] states: "And the value you attach to it shall be according to the amount of seed." ^1-117

**118.** For a person who unintentionally makes use of a sacred object to make restitution for what he misused [when] sinning against God, and for him to add one fifth of its value, as \[[[Torah 24 - Vayikra#^5-17|Leviticus 5:17]]] states: "For that which he sinned, [using something] sacred, he shall pay...." ^1-118

**119.** For the produce of the fourth year to be sacred, as \[[[Torah 30 - Kedoshim#^19-24|Leviticus 19:24]]] states: "Its produce shall be sacred, [an object of] praise to God...." ^1-119

**120.** To leave *pe'ah.* ^1-120

**121.** To leave *leket.* ^1-121

**122.** To leave a forgotten sheaf. ^1-122

**123.** To leave the incompletely formed grape clusters. ^1-123

**124.** To leave the individual fallen grapes. With regard to all these [five mitzvot], \[[[Torah 30 - Kedoshim#^19-10|Leviticus 19:10]]] states: "Leave them for the poor and the stranger." This [verse states] the positive commandment for all these.[^8] ^1-124

**125.** To bring the first fruits to God's chosen house, [the Temple,], as \[[[Torah 18 - Mishpatim#^23-19|Exodus 23:19]]] states: "The first fruits of your land...." ^1-125

**126.** To separate the greater *terumah* [and give it] to the priest, as \[[[Torah 48 - Shoftim#^18-4|Deuteronomy 18:4]]] states: "Give him the first of your grain." ^1-126

**127.** To separate a tithe of grain [and give it] to the Levites, as \[[[Torah 33 - Bechukotai#^27-30|Leviticus 27:30]]] states: "All the land's tithes...." ^1-127
