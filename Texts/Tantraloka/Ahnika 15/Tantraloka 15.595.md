## Mūla

अतत्त्वेऽभिनिवेशं च न कुर्यात्पक्षपाततः ।
जातिविद्याकुलाचारदेहदेशगुणार्थजान् ॥ ^mula

atattve'bhiniveśaṃ ca na kuryātpakṣapātataḥ |
jātividyākulācāradehadeśaguṇārthajān || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 10 - Chapter Fifteen, part 9#^pg360|p. 342]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=360>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 15.597cd–598ab.
>
> One should not be attached to what is not true (atattva) (moved) by (some foolish) prejudice (pakṣapāta). The eight tenacious errors (graha) explained in the Gahvara should be quickly abandoned as if they were demons (graha) (that take possession of people). (These are the attachments) induced by (pride of ones) caste, education (vidyā), (one’s) family, (one’s) behaviour, the body, country (of origin), (one’s) qualities and wealth. (with 15.595–596ab)
