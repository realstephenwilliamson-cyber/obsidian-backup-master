# LXX Psalms 132

← [[LXX Psalms 131]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 133]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 133]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 133]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**132:1** A Song of Degrees. See now! what is so good, or what so pleasant, as for brethren to dwell together?[^1]  ^132-1

**132:2** *It is* as ointment on the head, that ran down to the beard, *even* the beard of Aaron; that ran down to the fringe of his clothing.  ^132-2

**132:3** As the dew of Aermon, that comes down on the mountains of Sion: for there the Lord commanded the blessing, *even* life for ever.  ^132-3

[^1]: 132:1 *Alex.*+ 'for David.'
