# Psalms 147 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 146|← Psalms 146]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 148|Psalms 148 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 147|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**147:1** Praise the Lord, O Jerusalem: praise thy God, O Sion. ^147-1

**147:2** Because he hath strengthened the bolts of thy gates, he hath blessed thy children within thee. ^147-2

**147:3** Who hath placed peace in thy borders: and filleth thee with the fat of corn. ^147-3

**147:4** Who sendeth forth his speech to the earth: his word runneth swiftly. ^147-4

**147:5** Who giveth snow like wool: scattereth mists like ashes. ^147-5

**147:6** He sendeth his crystal like morsels: who shall stand before the face of his cold? ^147-6

**147:7** He shall send out his word, and shall melt them: his wind shall blow, and the waters shall run. ^147-7

**147:8** Who declareth his word to Jacob: his justices and his judgments to Israel. ^147-8

**147:9** He hath not done in like manner to every nation: and his judgments he hath not made manifest to them. Alleluia. ^147-9
