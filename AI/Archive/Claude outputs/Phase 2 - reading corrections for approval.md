# Phase 2: reading corrections for approval

6 October 2026. This is the second batch of phase 2: corrections of single words in the root text (Mūla). The structural repairs you approved earlier have already been applied.

## What this batch does

I compared the vault's Mūla, which comes from the Muktabodha e-text, with the second transcription (GRETIL / DCS, which are one transcription). There were 839 verses where they differ by more than nasal spelling. Twenty-eight checking agents read each disputed word on the KSTS page images. A second team of six screened every resulting change for grammar, metre and the commentary's quotations. A third check had two agents compare 80 random changed verses with the pages: in the first 40 they found one bad edit, now fixed (28.363), and the second 40 were all correct.

**Result: 467 verses, 488 words, in 34 āhnikas.** Only the Mūla block (Devanagari and IAST) changes; the `^mula` ids stay.

Most changes fix the e-text's typing slips: dropped or extra mātrās (*bhrataram* → *bhrātaram*), a misplaced *r* (*perpsoḥ* → *prepsoḥ*), lost visargas, garbled characters, and short words missing from the line (*nanu*, *tena*, *uktaṃ ca*, *taduktaṃ*).

**Bracketed readings.** KSTS itself prints 16 of the vault's 23 bracketed readings, in square brackets in 12 cases, and the vault now copies its brackets. The other 7 (9.151, 17.23, 26.33, 26.43, 28.363, 31.84, 32.50) were added by the e-text and are not in the print, so they go.

## Decision 1: evident misprints in KSTS (52 places in 49 verses)

At these places KSTS prints something that is not a Sanskrit word or breaks the grammar or metre: a dropped mātrā, a wrong letter, a misplaced repha. The commentary often quotes the right form. In 48 places the vault already has the correct form. In 4 (3.12, 5.107, 9.8, 15.570) it does not: it copies the misprint, or it has its own slip.

- **(a) Keep the correct form (my recommendation).** The Mūla follows KSTS everywhere else. The page-map note gets a list of these places with what KSTS prints, so anyone checking against the PDF sees why the text differs.
- **(b) Follow the print letter for letter**, misprints included, as you chose for *siṃtāṃśu* at 37.52. Then 49 verses carry non-words such as *vilpayate* (29.91) or *śyodhyasya* (16.191).

The six *piva-* / *pivet* / *pivatha* forms in āhnikas 17, 28 and 29 belong here as well. KSTS writes व for ब in that word throughout, so this may be the printer's spelling rather than a slip.

## Decision 2: which vol. IX copy goes into the vault

- **The DLI scan (2015.485523, 36.2 MB)** is clean, but it lacks printed pp. 217 and 255. Page 217 is commentary only. Page 255 holds the end of verse 15.514.
- **The archive.org copy with handwritten notes (36.9 MB)** is the same printing and complete. Its margins carry pencil and ink notes, like the only vol. VII copy.
- My suggestion is the complete annotated copy, with the page map pointing into it. Taking both adds 36 MB.

All twelve volumes together come to about 310 MB, which puts the vault near 9.5 of the 10 GB Sync allowance.
