# vijayottaratantra

**Catalog number:** E0010  
**Manuscript:** IFP transcript T0190  
**Published:** Muktabodha Indological Research InstituteMuktabodha, United States, 2013

## Description

IFP transcript 190 copied from a MS belonging to the GOML, Madras, No. R 13659.

The Vijayottara also called trisahasrasaṃhitā is an Upāgama of
the śaivasiddhānta. It deals with śaiva ritual.

 See Muktabodha digital library, IFP/EFEO collection at www.muktabodha.org for full bibliographic details.
