# LXX Psalms 43

← [[LXX Psalms 42]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 44]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 044]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 044]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**43:1** For the end, a Psalm for instruction, for the sons of Core.[^1]  ^43-1

**43:2** O God, we have heard with our ears, our fathers have told us, the work which thou wroughtest in their days, in the days of old.  ^43-2

**43:3** Thine hand utterly destroyed the heathen, and thou didst plant them: thou didst afflict the nations, and cast them out.  ^43-3

**43:4** For they inherited not the land by their *own* sword, and their *own* arm did not deliver them; but thy right hand, and thine arm, and the light of thy countenance, because thou wert well pleased in them.  ^43-4

**43:5** Thou art indeed my King and my God, who commandest deliverances for Jacob.  ^43-5

**43:6** In thee will we push down our enemies, and in thy name will we bring to nought them that rise up against us.  ^43-6

**43:7** For I will not trust in my bow, and my sword shall not save me.  ^43-7

**43:8** For thou hast saved us from them that afflicted us, and hast put to shame them that hated us.  ^43-8

**43:9** In God will we make our boast all the day, and to thy name will we give thanks for ever. Pause.[^2]  ^43-9

**43:10** But now thou hast cast off, and put us to shame; and thou wilt not go forth with our hosts.  ^43-10

**43:11** Thou hast turned us back before our enemies; and they that hated us spoiled for themselves.  ^43-11

**43:12** Thou madest us as sheep for meat; and thou scatteredst us among the nations.  ^43-12

**43:13** Thou hast sold thy people without price, and there was no profit by their exchange.  ^43-13

**43:14** Thou hast made us a reproach to our neighbours, a scorn and derision to them that are round about us.  ^43-14

**43:15** Thou hast made us a proverb among the Gentiles, a shaking of the head among the nations.  ^43-15

**43:16** All the day my shame is before me, and the confusion of my face has covered me,  ^43-16

**43:17** because of the voice of the slanderer and reviler; because of the enemy and avenger.  ^43-17

**43:18** All these things are come upon us: but we have not forgotten thee, neither have we dealt unrighteously in thy covenant.  ^43-18

**43:19** And our heart has not gone back; but thou hast turned aside our paths from thy way.  ^43-19

**43:20** For thou hast laid us low in a place of affliction, and the shadow of death has covered us.  ^43-20

**43:21** If we have forgotten the name of our God, and if we have spread out our hands to a strange god; shall not God search these things out?  ^43-21

**43:22** for he knows the secrets of the heart.  ^43-22

**43:23** For, for thy sake we are killed all the day long; we are counted as sheep for slaughter.  ^43-23

**43:24** Awake, wherefore sleepest thou, O Lord? arise, and do not cast *us* off for ever.  ^43-24

**43:25** Wherefore turnest thou thy face away, *and* forgettest our poverty and our affliction?  ^43-25

**43:26** For our soul has been brought down to the dust; our belly has cleaved to the earth.  ^43-26

**43:27** Arise, O Lord, help us, and redeem us for thy name's sake.  ^43-27

[^1]: 43:1 *Gr.* understanding.
[^2]: 43:9 *Gr.* will be praised etc. See Ps. 106.
