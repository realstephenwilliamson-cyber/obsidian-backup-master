## Chapter Four

The Blessed Lord continued:

\[1\] “First to Vivasvat, the Sun God,
I proclaimed this timeless *Yoga*.
Vivasvat to Manu taught it,
and Manu to King Ikshvaku.

\[2\] The royal *rishis* understood
what they received in succession,
but after a long time on earth
*Yoga* was lost, O Arjuna.

\[3\] It is just this ancient *Yoga*
that I am transmitting to you,
who are my friend and devotee.
This is the utmost secret teaching.”

Then Arjuna said:

\[4\] “But your birth, O Lord, was later;
Vivasvat was born before you.
How am I to understand that
you taught this in the beginning?”

Krishna answered:

\[5\] “Many of my births have passed now,
and many of yours, Arjuna.
I know all of my births, but you,
Great Warrior, do not know yours.

\[6\] Though I am birthless and undying
and am the Lord of all beings,
[p. 28] by controlling My own nature
I come to be, through My own power.

\[7\] Whenever *dharma*, or righteousness,
decreases, O Bharata,
and unrighteousness increases,
that is when I create Myself.

\[8\] For protection of the righteous
and destruction of evildoers,
for establishment of *dharma*
I come to be from age to age.

\[9\] And one who really knows the truth
of My divine birth and action,
when he abandons his body
he is not reborn but comes to Me.

\[10\] Gone is passion, fear, and anger
for those who are absorbed in Me.
Many, purified through wisdom,
have attained My state of being.

\[11\] Whoever comes to Me for refuge,
in whatever way, I reward them.
Human beings everywhere
follow My path, Son of Pritha.

\[12\] Desiring success through rituals
men sacrifice to the gods here.
Ritual acts do bring success
quickly in the human world.

\[13\] I created the four-caste system,
balancing *gunas* and their actions.
And although I created this,
know Me as the Eternal Non-Doer.

[p. 29] \[14\] I am untainted by action,
with no desire for action’s fruit.
One who understands Me as such
is himself not bound by action.

\[15\] The ancients, seekers of release,
knowing this engaged in action.
You too should engage in action
as in time past the ancients did.

\[16\] What is action and nonaction?
Even the sages had this question.
After I explain this to you,
you will be released from evil.

\[17\] First one must understand action,
and then understand wrong action,
and understand nonaction also.
Action’s ways are deep and subtle.

\[18\] One who sees nonaction in action
and sees action in nonaction,
such a one is accounted wise,
a *yogin* who performs all actions.

\[19\] Those who have excluded purpose
and desire from their ventures,
their *karma* burnt in insight’s fire—
the wise call such people sages.

\[20\] Unattached to fruits of action,
always content, not dependent,
even while engaged in action
one does nothing whatsoever.

\[21\] Mind indifferent, self restrained,
renouncing all acquisition,
[p. 30] acting only with the body,
one incurs no guilt in action.

\[22\] Satisfied with whatever comes,
non-dual and free from envy,
constant in success and failure,
although one acts, one is not bound.

\[23\] Attachment gone, liberated,
mind grounded deep in knowledge,
performing work as sacrifice,
one’s *karma* wholly melts away.

\[24\] *Brahman* is the offering, poured
by *Brahman* into *Brahman*’s fire.
*Brahman* will be attained by one
who sees in action only *Brahman*.

\[25\] Some *yogins* offer sacrifice
to a god, while others offer
sacrifice through the very act
of sacrifice in *Brahman*’s fire.

\[26\] Some offer senses such as hearing
in the fire of discipline;
others sensations such as sound
in the fire of the senses.

\[27\] Others offer all sensory action
and the action too of *prana
*in *yogic* fire of self-restraint
that is kindled by deep knowledge.

\[28\] Others offer their possessions
or their *Yogic* austerities;
ascetics offer Vedic study
and knowledge as their sacrifice.

[p. 31] \[29\] Others offer exhalation,
inhalation, exhalation,
controlling the path of breathing,
intent upon *pranayama*.

\[30\] Others, who restrain their diet,
offer prolonged inhalations.
All these understand sacrifice,
and sacrifice destroys their ills.

\[31\] Enjoy the sacrificial nectar
and go to primordial *Brahman*.
Non-sacrificers lose this world.
How then do they gain the other?

\[32\] There are many kinds of sacrifice
set before the face of *Brahman*.
Know that all are born of action
and you will be liberated.

\[33\] Better than material sacrifice
is the sacrifice of knowledge.
Every action, Son of Pritha,
is fully understood in wisdom.

\[34\] Understand that through prostrations,
humble inquiry, and service,
the wise will be moved to teach you
all that they know, O Arjuna.

\[35\] With that knowledge you shall never
fall again into delusion,
and then you will see all beings
in yourself and in Me also.

\[36\] Even if you were the most evil
of all evildoers and sinners,
[p. 32] you would, on the boat of wisdom,
cross beyond all iniquity.

\[37\] As a kindled fire reduces
firewood to ashes, Arjuna,
so does the fire of knowledge
turn all actions into ashes.

\[38\] Nothing in this world can be found
that purifies as knowledge does.
One who is perfected in *Yoga*
in time will find it in himself.

\[39\] Those with faith will attain knowledge.
When that knowledge has been attained
and one holds fast, restrains the senses,
he soon will come to utmost peace.

\[40\] Without knowledge and without faith
the one who doubts will be destroyed.
Neither this world, nor the one beyond,
nor happiness is for one who doubts.

\[41\] Renouncing action in *Yoga,*
all his doubt cut off by knowledge,
such a person, Self-possessed,
is not caught in bonds of action.

\[42\] Cut off with your sword of knowledge
the doubt that comes from ignorance
deep in your heart. O Bharata,
rise up now and practice *Yoga*!”
