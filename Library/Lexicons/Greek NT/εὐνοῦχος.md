# εὐνοῦχος

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · noun

**Dodson:** a eunuch, keeper of the bed-chamber — (a) a chamberlain, keeper of the bed-chamber of an eastern potentate, eunuch, (b) a eunuch, castrated person, or one who voluntarily abstains from marriage. (Strong's 2135; headword εὐνοῦχος, ου, ὁ)
