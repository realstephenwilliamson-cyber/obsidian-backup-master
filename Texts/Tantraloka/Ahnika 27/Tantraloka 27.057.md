## Mūla

अध्वा चेहासने प्रोक्तस्तत्सर्वत्रार्चयेदिदम् ।
आवाहनविसृष्टी तु तत्र प्राग्वत्समाचरेत् ॥ ^mula

adhvā cehāsane proktastatsarvatrārcayedidam |
āvāhanavisṛṣṭī tu tatra prāgvatsamācaret || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 18 - Chapter Twenty-Seven#^pg443|p. 423]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=443>))
>
> All Mantras are born from the pure and impure Paths. (Moreover,) it is said that the entire Path ending with Śiva is (present) here in (His) Throne. (Thus,) it should be worshiped (anyway,) everywhere (in all cases). The invocation and dismissal (of the deity) should be done (in the same way) as before. (with 27.56cd–57)
