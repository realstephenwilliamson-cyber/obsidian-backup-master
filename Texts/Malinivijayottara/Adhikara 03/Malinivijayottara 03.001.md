## Mūla

एवमुक्त्वा महादेवी जगदानन्दकारिणा ।
प्रणिपत्य पुनर्वाक्यमिदमाह जगत्पतिम् ॥ ^mula

evam uktvā mahādevī jagadānandakāriṇā |
praṇipatya punar vākyam idam āha jagatpatim || ^iast

*Muktabodha's e-text of the KSTS edition (M00160) has:* evamuktā mahādevī jagadānandakāriṇā | praṇipatya punarvākyamidamāha jagatpatim ||
