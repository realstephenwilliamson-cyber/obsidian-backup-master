# Psalm 68

← [[BCP Psalm 067]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 069]] →

*Exsurgat Deus* · Book Two · Thirteenth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 068]]

**68:1** Let God arise, and let his enemies be scattered; \*  
let those who hate him flee before him. ^68-1

**68:2** Let them vanish like smoke when the wind drives it away; \*  
as the wax melts at the fire, so let the wicked perish at the presence of God. ^68-2

**68:3** But let the righteous be glad and rejoice before God; \*  
let them also be merry and joyful. ^68-3

**68:4** Sing to God, sing praises to his Name;  
exalt him who rides upon the heavens; \*  
YAHWEH is his Name, rejoice before him! ^68-4

**68:5** Father of orphans, defender of widows, \*  
God in his holy habitation! ^68-5

**68:6** God gives the solitary a home and brings forth prisoners into freedom; \*  
but the rebels shall live in dry places. ^68-6

**68:7** O God, when you went forth before your people, \*  
when you marched through the wilderness, ^68-7

**68:8** The earth shook, and the skies poured down rain,  
at the presence of God, the God of Sinai, \*  
at the presence of God, the God of Israel. ^68-8

**68:9** You sent a gracious rain, O God, upon your inheritance; \*  
you refreshed the land when it was weary. ^68-9

**68:10** Your people found their home in it; \*  
in your goodness, O God, you have made provision for the poor. ^68-10

[p. 677]

**68:11** The Lord gave the word; \*  
great was the company of women who bore the tidings: ^68-11

**68:12** "Kings with their armies are fleeing away; \*  
the women at home are dividing the spoils." ^68-12

**68:13** Though you lingered among the sheepfolds, \*  
you shall be like a dove whose wings are covered with silver,  
whose feathers are like green gold. ^68-13

**68:14** When the Almighty scattered kings, \*  
it was like snow falling in Zalmon. ^68-14

**68:15** O mighty mountain, O hill of Bashan! \*  
O rugged mountain, O hill of Bashan! ^68-15

**68:16** Why do you look with envy, O rugged mountain,  
at the hill which God chose for his resting place? \*  
truly, the LORD will dwell there for ever. ^68-16

**68:17** The chariots of God are twenty thousand,  
even thousands of thousands; \*  
the Lord comes in holiness from Sinai. ^68-17

**68:18** You have gone up on high and led captivity captive;  
you have received gifts even from your enemies, \*  
that the LORD God might dwell among them. ^68-18

**68:19** Blessed be the Lord day by day, \*  
the God of our salvation, who bears our burdens. ^68-19

**68:20** He is our God, the God of our salvation; \*  
God is the LORD, by whom we escape death. ^68-20

**68:21** God shall crush the heads of his enemies, \*  
and the hairy scalp of those who go on still in their wickedness. ^68-21

[p. 678]

**68:22** The Lord has said, "I will bring them back from Bashan; \*  
I will bring them back from the depths of the sea; ^68-22

**68:23** That your foot may be dipped in blood, \*  
the tongues of your dogs in the blood of your enemies." ^68-23

**68:24** They see your procession, O God, \*  
your procession into the sanctuary, my God and my King. ^68-24

**68:25** The singers go before, musicians follow after, \*  
in the midst of maidens playing upon the hand-drums. ^68-25

**68:26** Bless God in the congregation; \*  
bless the LORD, you that are of the fountain of Israel. ^68-26

**68:27** There is Benjamin, least of the tribes, at the head;  
the princes of Judah in a company; \*  
and the princes of Zebulon and Naphtali. ^68-27

**68:28** Send forth your strength, O God; \*  
establish, O God, what you have wrought for us. ^68-28

**68:29** Kings shall bring gifts to you, \*  
for your temple's sake at Jerusalem. ^68-29

**68:30** Rebuke the wild beast of the reeds, \*  
and the peoples, a herd of wild bulls with its calves. ^68-30

**68:31** Trample down those who lust after silver; \*  
scatter the peoples that delight in war. ^68-31

**68:32** Let tribute be brought out of Egypt; \*  
let Ethiopia stretch out her hands to God. ^68-32

**68:33** Sing to God, O kingdoms of the earth; \*  
sing praises to the Lord. ^68-33

**68:34** He rides in the heavens, the ancient heavens; \*  
he sends forth his voice, his mighty voice. ^68-34

[p. 679]

**68:35** Ascribe power to God; \*  
his majesty is over Israel;  
his strength is in the skies. ^68-35

**68:36** How wonderful is God in his holy places! \*  
the God of Israel giving strength and power to his people!  
Blessed be God! ^68-36
