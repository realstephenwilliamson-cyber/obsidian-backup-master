## Mūla

स्थाणुस्वर्णाक्षकावाद्यौ भद्रगोकर्णकौ परौ ।
महाकालाविमुक्तेशरुद्रकोट्यम्बरापदाः ॥ ^mula

sthāṇusvarṇākṣakāv[^38] ādyau bhadragokarṇakau parau |
mahākālāvimukteśarudrakoṭyambarāpadāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, July 2023 – September 2023
> p. 73 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=78>))
>
> D) Now the pure (group of eight) (pavitra) will be declared. The first two are called SthāĀu and Svarṇa, the next two are Rudra and Gokarṇaka, (then come) Mahālaya, Avimukteśa, Rudrakoṭi and Ambarāpada.[^39] (with 5.19cd–20)

[^38]: Read -ākhyakāv for -arṇākṣakāv.
[^39]: MV 5/20 quoted in TĀv ad 8/209cd (209ab).
