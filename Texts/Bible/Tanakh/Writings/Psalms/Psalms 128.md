## Chapter 128

**128:1** A song of ascents.  
Happy are all who fear the LORD,  
who follow His ways. ^128-1

**128:2** You shall enjoy the fruit of your labors;  
you shall be happy and you shall prosper. ^128-2

**128:3** Your wife shall be like a fruitful vine within your house;  
your sons, like olive saplings around your table. ^128-3

**128:4** So shall the man who fears the LORD be blessed. ^128-4

**128:5** May the LORD bless you from Zion;  
may you share the prosperity of Jerusalem  
all the days of your life, ^128-5

**128:6** and live to see your children’s children.  
May all be well with Israel! ^128-6
