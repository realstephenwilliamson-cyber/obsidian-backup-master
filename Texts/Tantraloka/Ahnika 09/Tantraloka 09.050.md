## Mūla

स्वातन्त्र्यभासितभिदा पञ्चधा प्रविभज्यते ।
चिदानन्देषणाज्ञानक्रियाणां सुस्फुटत्वतः ॥ ^mula

svātantryabhāsitabhidā pañcadhā pravibhajyate |
cidānandeṣaṇājñānakriyāṇāṃ susphuṭatvataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 04 - Chapter Nine, part 2#^pg73|p. 56]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=73>))
>
> Śiva, whose nature is free discerning consciousness (svatantradrg) and is full (to overflowing) with five powers, manifesting (the domain) of duality by (the power of) His freedom, is divided into five. (with 9.49cd–50ab)
>
> As (the powers) of consciousness, bliss, will, knowledge and action become (progressively) clearly evident, (they manifest) as the group of five principles called Śiva, Śakti, Sadāśiva, Īśvara and Pure Knowledge (respectively). (with 9.50cd–51ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* पञ्चधा प्रविभागमेव दर्शयति --

> चिदानन्देषणाज्ञानक्रियाणां सुस्फुटत्वतः ॥
> शिवशक्तिसदेशानविद्याख्यं तत्त्वपञ्चकम् ।

सदेति -- सदाशिवः । तदुक्तम् --

"चिदानन्देषणाज्ञानक्रियापञ्चमहातनुः ।

विवर्तते महेशानस्तत्त्ववर्गेषु पञ्चधा ॥"

इति ॥

तथा,

"शिवशक्तिसदाशिवतामीश्वरविद्यामयीं च तत्त्वदशाम् ।

शक्तीनां पञ्चानां विभक्तभावेन भासयति ॥"

इति ॥ ५० ॥
