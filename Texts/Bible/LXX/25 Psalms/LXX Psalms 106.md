# LXX Psalms 106

← [[LXX Psalms 105]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 107]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 107]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 107]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**106:1** Alleluia. Give thanks to the Lord, for he is good; for his mercy *endures* for ever.  ^106-1

**106:2** Let them say *so* who have been redeemed by the Lord, whom he has redeemed from the hand of the enemy;  ^106-2

**106:3** and gathered them out of the countries, from the east, and west, and north, and south.[^1]  ^106-3

**106:4** They wandered in the wilderness in a dry land; they found no way to a city of habitation.  ^106-4

**106:5** Hungry and thirsty, their soul fainted in them.  ^106-5

**106:6** Then they cried to the Lord in their affliction, and he delivered them out of their distresses.  ^106-6

**106:7** And he guided them into a straight path, that they might go to a city of habitation.  ^106-7

**106:8** Let them acknowledge to the Lord his mercies, and his wonderful works to the children of men.  ^106-8

**106:9** For he satisfies the empty soul, and fills the hungry *soul* with good things,  ^106-9

**106:10** *even* them that sit in darkness and the shadow of death, fettered in poverty and iron;  ^106-10

**106:11** because they rebelled against the words of God, and provoked the counsel of the Most High.[^2]  ^106-11

**106:12** So their heart was brought low with troubles; they were weak, and there was no helper.  ^106-12

**106:13** Then they cried to the Lord in their affliction, and he saved them out of their distresses.  ^106-13

**106:14** And he brought them out of darkness and the shadow of death, and broke their bonds asunder.  ^106-14

**106:15** Let them acknowledge to the Lord his mercies, and his wonders to the children of men.  ^106-15

**106:16** For he broke to pieces the brazen gates, and crushed the iron bars.  ^106-16

**106:17** He helped them out of the way of their iniquity; for they were brought low because of their iniquities.[^3]  ^106-17

**106:18** Their soul abhorred all meat; and they drew near to the gates of death.  ^106-18

**106:19** Then they cried to the Lord in their affliction, and he saved them out of their distresses.  ^106-19

**106:20** He sent his word, and healed them, and delivered them out of their destructions.  ^106-20

**106:21** Let them acknowledge to the Lord his mercies, and his wonderful works to the children of men.  ^106-21

**106:22** And let them offer to him the sacrifice of praise, and proclaim his works with exultation.  ^106-22

**106:23** They that go down to the sea in ships, doing business in many waters;  ^106-23

**106:24** these *men* have seen the works of the Lord, and his wonders in the deep.  ^106-24

**106:25** He speaks, and the stormy wind arises, and its waves are lifted up.  ^106-25

**106:26** They go up to the heavens, and go down to the depths; their soul melts because of troubles.  ^106-26

**106:27** They are troubled, they stagger as a drunkard, and all their wisdom is swallowed up.  ^106-27

**106:28** Then they cry to the Lord in their affliction, and he brings them out of their distresses.  ^106-28

**106:29** And he commands the storm, and it is calmed into a gentle breeze, and its waves are still.  ^106-29

**106:30** And they are glad, because they are quiet; and he guides them to their desired haven.  ^106-30

**106:31** Let them acknowledge to the Lord his mercies, and his wonderful works to the children of men.  ^106-31

**106:32** Let them exalt him in the congregation of the people, and praise him in the seat of the elders.  ^106-32

**106:33** He turns rivers into a desert, and streams of water into a dry land;[^4][^5]  ^106-33

**106:34** a fruitful land into saltness, for the wickedness of them that dwell in it.  ^106-34

**106:35** He turns a wilderness into pools of water, and a dry land into streams of water.  ^106-35

**106:36** And there he causes the hungry to dwell, and they establish for themselves cities of habitation.  ^106-36

**106:37** And they sow fields, and plant vineyards, and they yield fruit of increase.  ^106-37

**106:38** And he blesses them, and they multiply exceedingly, and he diminishes not the number of their cattle.  ^106-38

**106:39** Again they become few, and are brought low, by the pressure of evils and pain.  ^106-39

**106:40** Contempt is poured upon their princes, and he causes them to wander in a desert and trackless land.  ^106-40

**106:41** But he helps the poor out of poverty, and makes *him* families as a flock.  ^106-41

**106:42** The upright shall see and rejoice; and all iniquity shall stop her mouth.  ^106-42

**106:43** Who is wise, and will observe these things, and understand the mercies of the Lord?  ^106-43

[^1]: 106:3 *Gr.* sea.
[^2]: 106:11 *Gr.* embittered the oracles.
[^3]: 106:17 see the Hebrew.
[^4]: 106:33 *Gr.* waters.
[^5]: 106:33 *Gr.* thirst.
