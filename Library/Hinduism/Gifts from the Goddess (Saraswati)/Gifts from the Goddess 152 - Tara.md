### Tārā

A star in the dark night, a sphere of light bursting forth from the vacuum: that is Tārā, the second of the great paths to self-realization. The unmanifest sound of *ōmkāra*<sup>442</sup> bursting forth into the manifest world: that is Tārā, too.

442  *Ōmkāra* is another term for primordial *bīja ōm* (or *aum)*. Its literal meaning is “the *ōm* syllable.”

Cut a sphere, with a central star in it, into two halves and place them side by side. 

Move the half-star into the surface centers of the two hemispheres. In this way does *ōmkāra* assume female form—where the center is moved to the periphery, and one appears as two. Out of this, the creation proceeds further—shown by the tail of the *ōmkāra*—and the third center of the world develops; that is jiva.<sup>443</sup> Jiva is located in 443  A manifest, living being; an embodied soul.
