# LXX Micah 03

← [[LXX Micah 02]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Micah 04]] →

*Micah · [[LXX]]*

**3:1** And he shall say, Hear now these words, ye heads of the house of Jacob, and ye remnant of the house of Israel; is it not for you to know judgment?  ^3-1

**3:2** *who* hate good, and seek evil; *who* tear their skins off them, and their flesh off their bones:  ^3-2

**3:3** even as they devoured the flesh of my people, and stripped their skins off them, and broke their bones, and divided *them* as flesh for the caldron, and as meat for the pot,  ^3-3

**3:4** thus they shall cry to the Lord, but he shall not hearken to them; and he shall turn away his face from them at that time, because they have done wickedly in their practices against themselves.  ^3-4

**3:5** Thus saith the Lord concerning the prophets that lead my people astray, that bite with their teeth, and proclaim peace to them; and *when* nothing was put into their mouth, they raised up war against them:  ^3-5

**3:6** therefore there shall be night to you instead of a vision, and there shall be to you darkness instead of prophecy; and the sun shall go down upon the prophets, and the day shall be dark upon them.  ^3-6

**3:7** And the seers of night-visions shall be ashamed, and the prophets shall be laughed to scorn: and all the people shall speak against them, because there shall be none to hearken to them.  ^3-7

**3:8** Surely I will strengthen myself with the Spirit of the Lord, and of judgment, and of power, to declare to Jacob his transgressions, and to Israel his sins.  ^3-8

**3:9** Hear now these words, ye chiefs of the house of Jacob, and the remnant of the house of Israel, who hate judgment, and pervert all righteousness;  ^3-9

**3:10** who build up Sion with blood, and Jerusalem with iniquity.  ^3-10

**3:11** The heads thereof have judged for gifts, and the priests thereof have answered for hire, and her prophets have divined for silver: and *yet* they have rested on the Lord, saying, Is not the Lord among us? no evil shall come upon us.  ^3-11

**3:12** Therefore on your account Sion shall be ploughed as a field, and Jerusalem shall be as a storehouse of fruits, and the mountain of the house as a grove of the forest.  ^3-12
