# The Cambridge Introduction to Sanskrit (Ruppel)

A. M. Ruppel's *The Cambridge Introduction to Sanskrit* (Cambridge University Press, 2017) — 41 numbered chapters (0–40, with an extra Chapter 2A on word stress), followed by appendices of reference material. The 42 chapter notes below are companions for study: each links to the exact page in the full textbook PDF, and — for the chapters that have a dedicated split-out PDF in the library folder — to that chapter PDF as well.

Companion volume: [[Ruppel - An Introductory Sanskrit Reader (2022).pdf|An Introductory Sanskrit Reader (Ruppel, BRILL 2021)]] — graded readings for building fluency once the grammar chapters are underway.

Full textbook: [[Library/Sanskrit/Ruppel/A. M. Ruppel - The Cambridge Introduction to Sanskrit-Cambridge University Press (2017).pdf|The Cambridge Introduction to Sanskrit (full PDF)]]
