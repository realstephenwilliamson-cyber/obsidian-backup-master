# Holy Days - my choices

One row for each date on which you chose between the Sunday's readings and collect and the feast's (the buttons on Home, and the homily template, write here; you can also edit a row by hand). *Use* is `sunday`, `feast` or `both` (both is for a homily). A date with no row follows the Prayer Book's rules ([[Holy Days (BCP 1979)]]). Home, the Daily Office and the homily template read this note.
