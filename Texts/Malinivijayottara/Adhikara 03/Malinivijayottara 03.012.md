## Mūla

बीजमत्र शिवः शक्तिर्योनिरित्यभिधीयते ।
वाचकत्वेन सर्वापि शंभोः शक्तिश्च सस्यते ॥ ^mula

bījam atra śivaḥ śaktir yonir ity abhidhīyate |
vācakatvena sarvāpi śaṁbhoḥ śaktiś ca sasyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2023 – March 2023
> p. 41 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=41>))
>
> Here (according to this tradition) the seed is Śiva and Śakti is termed the matrix (yoni). It is taught (in the Tantras) that Śambhu's power is the denotator although she is all things (that are the objects of denotation).[^13]

[^13]: MV 3/12a quoted in TĀv ad 15/125cd-126ab. MV 3/12cd. is quoted in TĀv ad 15/133cd-134ab.
