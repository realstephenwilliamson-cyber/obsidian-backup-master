10\. 21. Adityas: celestial gods in the *Veda.* Marici: the chief of the storm gods or Maruts. 10.22. Sama Veda: one of the collections (*samhita*) of hymns (much of which reproduces the *Rig Veda*) that contains instructions on chanting the verses. The text was recited during vedic sacrifice. Vasava: a name for Indra, the most important Vedic deity. 10.23. Rudras: storm gods. *Rudra* is an early name for Shiva, who became one of the three main gods of medieval Hinduism along with Vishnu and the Goddess (Devi). In Vishnu mythology Rudra is regarded as the destroyer of the universe, with Brahma the creator and Vishnu the preserver or maintainer. Rakshasas: a group of demons. Kubera: leader of the Rakshasas and the lord of wealth associated with the north. Agni: the fire god or flame itself. Meru: a mythical mountain residing at the center of the universe and supporting the heavens; the gods dwell there. 10.24. chief of house hold priests: or *hotri*, who would recite verses from the *Rig Veda* during vedic sacrifice. Skanda: god of war and son of Shiva; the chief general who leads the army of the gods against the demons. 10.25. Bhrigu: an ancient sage in the *Rig Veda.* 10.26. sacred fig: sacred in Hinduism, it is a symbol of the entire cosmos (see 15.1). Narada: a famous sage who frequently appears in religious literature. Citraratha: king of the Gandharavas or heavenly musicians; his name means “He Whose Chariot Is Bright.” Kapila: an ancient sage said to be the found er of the Samkhya school of philosophy. 10.27. the one Indra rides: this horse came out of the nectar that was produced in the mythical churning of the ocean. Airavata: the elephant that Indra rides. 10.28. Kandarpa: also called Kama (“Desire”). Vasuki*.* king of snakes. 10.29. Ananta: king of the Nagas, supernatural snake- beings. Ananta is also depicted in mythology as the snake whose coils encircle the earth and on whom Vishnu sleeps. Varuna: an ancient vedic water god, king of aquatic animals. Aryaman: king of the ancestors. Yama: god of death. 10.30. Prahlada: king of the demons, or Daityas, who became a devotee of Vishnu. Vainateya: Garuda, the son of Vinata, the great bird on whom Vishnu rides. 10.31. Rama: warrior who is the hero of the *Ramayana.* Makara: the greatest of sea monsters.
