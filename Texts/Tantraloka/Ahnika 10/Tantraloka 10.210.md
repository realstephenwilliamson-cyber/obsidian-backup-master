## Mūla

अतो भेदसमुल्लासकलां प्राथमिकीं बुधाः ।
चिन्वन्ति प्रतिभां देवीं सर्वज्ञत्वादिसिद्धये ॥ ^mula

ato bhedasamullāsakalāṃ prāthamikīṃ budhāḥ |
cinvanti pratibhāṃ devīṃ sarvajñatvādisiddhaye || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 13 - Chapter Ten, part 4#^pg445|p. 426]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=445>))
>
> Thus, the wise contemplate (and discern) (cinvanti) the first (energy of the initial) outpouring of duality (in the second moment) in order to attain omniscience and the rest. This is the Goddess Intuition (Pratibhā). She is said to be Śiva’s power. In the subsequent moments, from the third onwards, the Mahāmantreśas (mantrādhinātha) and their powers, followed by the Mantreśas and the rest, arise progressively. (with 10.210–211)
