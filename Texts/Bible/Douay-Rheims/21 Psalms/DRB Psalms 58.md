# Psalms 58 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 57|← Psalms 57]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 59|Psalms 59 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 58|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**58:1** Unto the end, destroy not, for David for an inscription of It title, when Saul sent and watched his house to kill him. ^58-1

**58:2** Deliver me from my enemies, O my God; and defend me from them that rise up against me. ^58-2

**58:3** Deliver me from them that work iniquity, and save me from bloody men. ^58-3

**58:4** For behold they have caught my soul: the mighty have rushed in upon me: ^58-4

**58:5** Neither is it my iniquity, nor my sin, O Lord: without iniquity have I run, and directed my steps. ^58-5

**58:6** Rise up thou to meet me, and behold: even thou, O Lord, the God of hosts, the God of Israel. Attend to visit all the nations: have no mercy on all them that work iniquity. ^58-6

**58:7** They shall return at evening, and shall suffer hunger like dogs: and shall go round about the city. ^58-7

**58:8** Behold they shall speak with their mouth, and a sword is in their lips: for who, say they, hath heard us? ^58-8

**58:9** But thou, O Lord, shalt laugh at them: thou shalt bring all the nations to nothing. ^58-9

**58:10** I will keep my strength to thee: for thou art my protector: ^58-10

**58:11** My God, his mercy shall prevent me. ^58-11

**58:12** God shall let me see over my enemies: slay them not, lest at any time my people forget. Scatter them by thy power; and bring them down, O Lord, my protector: ^58-12

**58:13** For the sin of their mouth, and the word of their lips: and let them be taken in their pride. And for their cursing and lying they shall be talked of, ^58-13

**58:14** When they are consumed: when they are consumed by thy wrath, and they shall be no more. And they shall know that God will rule Jacob, and all the ends of the earth. ^58-14

**58:15** They shall return at evening and shall suffer hunger like dogs: and shall go round about the city. ^58-15

**58:16** They shall be scattered abroad to eat, and shall murmur if they be not filled. ^58-16

**58:17** But I will sing thy strength: and will extol thy mercy in the morning. For thou art become my support, and my refuge, in the day of my trouble. ^58-17

**58:18** Unto thee, O my helper, will I sing, for thou art God my defence: my God my mercy. ^58-18
