## Mūla

सर्वं सर्वात्मकं यस्मात्तस्मात्सकलमातरि ।
लयाकलादिशक्तीनां संभवोऽस्त्येव तत्त्वतः ॥ ^mula

sarvaṃ sarvātmakaṃ yasmāttasmātsakalamātari |
layākalādiśaktīnāṃ saṃbhavo'styeva tattvataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 12 - Chapter Ten, part 3#^pg408|p. 389]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=408>))
>
> Everything is of the nature of all things; thus, it is possible for the powers of the Dissolution Deconditioned (pralayākala) and other (subjects) to be really present in (each) Conditioned (sakala) subject.
