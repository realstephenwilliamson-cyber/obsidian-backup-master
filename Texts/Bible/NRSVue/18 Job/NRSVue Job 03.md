# Job 3 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Job 02|← Job 2]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Job 04|Job 4 →]]

## Job curses the day he was born

**3:1** After this Job opened his mouth and cursed the day of his birth. ^3-1

**3:2** Job said: ^3-2

**3:3** “Let the day perish in which I was born, and the night that said, ‘A male is conceived.’ ^3-3

**3:4** Let that day be darkness! May God above not seek it or light shine on it. ^3-4

**3:5** Let gloom and deep darkness claim it. Let clouds settle upon it; let the blackness of the day terrify it. ^3-5

**3:6** That night—let thick darkness seize it! let it not rejoice among the days of the year; let it not come into the number of the months. ^3-6

**3:7** Yes, let that night be barren; let no joyful cry be heard^[Heb *come*] in it. ^3-7

**3:8** Let those curse it who curse the Sea,^[Cn: Heb *day*] those who are skilled to rouse up Leviathan. ^3-8

**3:9** Let the stars of its dawn be dark; let it hope for light but have none; may it not see the eyelids of the morning— ^3-9

**3:10** because it did not shut the doors of my mother’s womb and hide trouble from my eyes. ^3-10

**3:11** “Why did I not die at birth, come forth from the womb and expire? ^3-11

**3:12** Why were there knees to receive me or breasts for me to suck? ^3-12

**3:13** Now I would be lying down and quiet; I would be asleep; then I would be at rest ^3-13

**3:14** with kings and counselors of the earth who rebuild ruins for themselves, ^3-14

**3:15** or with princes who have gold, who fill their houses with silver. ^3-15

**3:16** Or why was I not buried like a stillborn child, like an infant that never sees the light? ^3-16

**3:17** There the wicked cease from troubling, and there the weary are at rest. ^3-17

**3:18** There the prisoners are at ease together; they do not hear the voice of the taskmaster. ^3-18

**3:19** The small and the great are there, and the slaves are free from their masters. ^3-19

**3:20** “Why is light given to one in misery and life to the bitter in soul, ^3-20

**3:21** who long for death, but it does not come, and dig for it more than for hidden treasures; ^3-21

**3:22** who rejoice exceedingly and are glad when they find the grave? ^3-22

**3:23** Why is light given to one who cannot see the way, whom God has fenced in? ^3-23

**3:24** For my sighing comes like^[Heb *before*] my bread, and my groanings are poured out like water. ^3-24

**3:25** Truly the thing that I fear comes upon me, and what I dread befalls me. ^3-25

**3:26** I am not at ease, nor am I quiet; I have no rest, but trouble comes.” ^3-26
