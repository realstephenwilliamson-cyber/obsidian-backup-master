### 17.1–4: Sin and forgiveness

*[[NRSVue Luke 17#^17-1|Text]], p. 150* · ([[Matthew 18#^18-6|Mt 18.6–7]]; [[Mark 09#^9-42|Mk 9.42]]). ^17-1

**[[NRSVue Luke 17#^17-1|1]]:** *Stumbling* (Gk “skandala”; cf. English “scandal”); offense or cause of sin (see, e.g., [[Torah 30 - Kedoshim#^19-14|Lev 19.14]]). ^17-1-2

**[[NRSVue Luke 17#^17-2|2]]:** *Millstone*, ca. 12–18 in (30–40 cm) in diameter and 2–4 in. (5–10 cm) thick, for grinding grain. *Little ones*, disciples. ^17-2

**[[NRSVue Luke 17#^17-3|3]]:** *Rebuke the offender*, see 3.19–20, 1QS 5.24–6.1 (“They shall rebuke one another in truth, humility, and charity . . .”); cf. CD 7.2–3; and cf. rabbinic injunctions to “tokheiḥah,” “rebuke” (e.g., [[Torah 30 - Kedoshim#^19-17|Lev 19.17]]; *Gen. Rab*. 54.3 \[R. Yose bar Hanina said: “Any love unaccompanied by criticism is no love at all”\]; *Sifra* 89a–89b). ^17-3

**[[NRSVue Luke 17#^17-3|3–4]]:** *Must forgive*, see [[Matthew 18#^18-15|Mt 18.15]],[[Matthew 18#^18-21|21–22]]. ^17-3-2

### 17.5–6: Saying concerning faith

*[[NRSVue Luke 17#^17-5|Text]], p. 150* · ([[Matthew 17#^17-19|Mt 17.19–21]]; [[Mark 09#^9-28|Mk 9.28–29]]). ^17-5

**[[NRSVue Luke 17#^17-5|5]]:** *Faith* (Gk “pistis”; Heb “ ʾemunah”). ^17-5-2

**[[NRSVue Luke 17#^17-6|6]]:** *Mustard seed*, see 13.19. ^17-6

### 17.7–10: Service without desire for reward

*[[NRSVue Luke 17#^17-7|Text]], p. 150* ^17-7

**[[NRSVue Luke 17#^17-7|7]]:** *Would say to your slave*, see 14.11n. ^17-7-2

**[[NRSVue Luke 17#^17-10|10]]:** *What we ought to have done*, obedience as a duty, but see 12.35–38. Compare *m. Avot* 1.3: “Be not like slaves that serve the master for the sake of a reward . . . .” ^17-10

### 17.11–19: Ten men with leprosy

*[[NRSVue Luke 17#^17-11|Text]], p. 151* · See 5.12–16. ^17-11

**[[NRSVue Luke 17#^17-12|12]]:** *Keeping their distance*, suggesting [[Torah 27 - Tazria#^13-45|Lev 13.45–46]]. ^17-12

**[[NRSVue Luke 17#^17-14|14]]:** *Priests*, see [[Torah 27 - Tazria#^13-2|Lev 13.2–3]]; [[Torah 28 - Metzora#^14-2|14.2–32]]. ^17-14

**[[NRSVue Luke 17#^17-16|16]]:** *Samaritan*, see 9.52–53n. The Samaritan would have gone to the priest on Mount Gerizim in Samaria; the *other nine* (v 17), presumably Jews, would go to Jerusalem. ^17-16

**[[NRSVue Luke 17#^17-18|18]]:** *Foreigner* (Gk “allogenēs”), see 7.9n. ^17-18

**[[NRSVue Luke 17#^17-19|19]]:** *Made you well*, lit., “saved you” (7.50; 8.48; 18.42). ^17-19

### 17.20–21: Kingdom of God

*[[NRSVue Luke 17#^17-20|Text]], p. 151* ^17-20

**[[NRSVue Luke 17#^17-20|20]]:** *When the kingdom . . . was coming*, pressing question also for Jesus’ followers (19.11; 21.7; [[Acts 01#^1-6|Acts 1.6]]). ^17-20-2

**[[NRSVue Luke 17#^17-21|21]]:** *Among you*, the Greek could also be translated “within you.” The messianic age, in Jewish thought characterized by a general resurrection, ingathering of the exiles, final judgment, and universal peace, is partially redefined to be something both internal to the individual and available in the present (11.20). ^17-21

### 17.22–37: Day of the Son of Man

*[[NRSVue Luke 17#^17-22|Text]], p. 151* · ([[Matthew 24#^24-17|Mt 24.17–18]],[[Matthew 24#^24-23|23]],[[Matthew 24#^24-26|26–28]],[[Matthew 24#^24-37|37–41]]; [[Mark 13#^13-14|Mk 13.14–16]],[[Mark 13#^13-19|19–23]]). ^17-22

**[[NRSVue Luke 17#^17-22|22]]:** *Days are coming*, referring to some unspecified time in the future; see e.g., [[II Kings 20#^20-17|2 Kings 20.17]]; [[Isaiah 36#^36-9|Isa 36.9]]; [[Jeremiah 51#^51-47|Jer 51.47]]*. Son of Man*, see 5.24n. ^17-22-2

**[[NRSVue Luke 17#^17-25|25]]:** *Endure much suffering*, see 9.22. ^17-25

**[[NRSVue Luke 17#^17-26|26–27]]:** *Noah*, see Gen 6–7. Rabbinic Judaism insists that Noah continually warned the people of the flood, from the time he planted the cedar trees for the wood with which to build the ark (see e.g., *b. Sanh*. 108a/b; *Pirqe R. El.* 22; *Gen. Rab*. 30.7; *Lev. Rab*. 27.5). ^17-26

**[[NRSVue Luke 17#^17-28|28–29]]:** *Lot*, see [[Torah 04 - Vayera#^18-16|Gen 18.16–19.28]]. ^17-28

**[[NRSVue Luke 17#^17-32|32]]:** *Lot’s wife*, who was turned into a pillar of salt ([[Torah 04 - Vayera#^19-26|Gen 19.26]]). ^17-32

**[[NRSVue Luke 17#^17-33|33]]:** See 9.24. ^17-33

**[[NRSVue Luke 17#^17-34|34]]:** Two in one bed (or on a dining couch), [[Matthew 24#^24-40|Mt 24.40]] offers “two \[men\] will be in the field,” in parallel to the two women grinding of v 35 ([[Matthew 24#^24-41|Mt 24.41]]). **\[36\]:** A scribal addition of [[Matthew 24#^24-20|Mt 24.20]]. ^17-34

**[[NRSVue Luke 17#^17-37|37]]:** [[Matthew 24#^24-28|Mt 24.28]]. On the unburied *corpse*, see “Parable of the Good Samaritan,” p. 136. *Vultures*, Gk “aetoi,” can be translated “eagles,” a symbol of Rome. diseases c Gk *his* d Or *within* e Other ancient authorities lack *in his day* ^17-37
