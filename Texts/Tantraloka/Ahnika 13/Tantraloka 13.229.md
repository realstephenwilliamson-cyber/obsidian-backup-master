## Mūla

क्रियया वान्तराकाररूपप्राणप्रवेशतः ।
तदा च देहसंस्थोऽपि स मुक्त इति भण्यते ॥ ^mula

kriyayā vāntarākārarūpaprāṇapraveśataḥ |
tadā ca dehasaṃstho'pi sa mukta iti bhaṇyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 08 - Chapter Thirteen, part 4#^pg287|p. 265]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=287>))
>
> There are various types of this (charismatic initiation), according to whether it is (imparted) individually or collectively, by (the teacher’s) talk (kathana), by an encounter (saṁgama), by a (compassionate) look (āvalocana), by (his explanation of) a sacred text (śāstra), by a transmission (saṁkramaṇa) (of power), by observing the conduct of equality (sāmyacaryā), by (eating) the sacrificial pap (caru), by the glorious power of Mantra, by ritual gesture (mudrā) and the like, by (ritual) action (kriyā), or by penetrating into the inner vital breath. (with 13.227cd–229ab)
>
> He is said to be liberated then, even though he is in the body. (with 13.229cd)
