## Mūla

अन्तरङ्गक्रमेणैव मुख्यचक्रस्य पूजनम् ।
यदेवानन्दसन्दोहि संविदो ह्यन्तरङ्गकम् ॥ ^mula

antaraṅgakrameṇaiva mukhyacakrasya pūjanam |
yadevānandasandohi saṃvido hyantaraṅgakam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 11 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 3#^pg318|p. 301]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=318>))
>
> After having drawn that (śakti) close by, worshipped each other mutually and offered libation to one another, the Main Wheel (mukhyacakra) is worshipped by the inner process (of consciousness). (with 29.104cd–105ab)
>
> Only that which streams with bliss (ānandasyandin) is the inner part of consciousness – that is the main (pradhāna) Wheel; (any) other than that is a subordinate Wheel (anucakra). (with 29.105cd–106ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* ननु कोऽसावान्तरः क्रमः, किञ्च तत् मुख्यं चक्रम्?--इत्याशङ्क्य आह--

> यदेवानन्दसन्दोहि संविदो ह्यन्तरङ्गकम् ॥
> तत्प्रधानं भवेच्चक्रमनुचक्रमतोऽपरम् ।

अतोऽपरमिति तथा न आनन्दसन्दोहि--इत्यर्थः ॥
