## Mūla

अनेन ज्ञातमात्रेण दीक्षानुग्रहकृद्भवेत् ।
समस्तकारणोल्लासपदे सुविदिते यतः ॥ ^mula

anena jñātamātreṇa dīkṣānugrahakṛdbhavet |
samastakāraṇollāsapade suvidite yataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 12 - Chapter Six, part 5#^pg414|p. 399]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=414>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 6.192cd–193ab.
>
> The Supreme Goddess (Parā Devī) is at the end of this (process,) where one who is conjoined (to Her) is not born (again). Just by knowing this alone, one can dispense grace and initiation. (with 6.191cd–192ab)
>
> When the plane of the outpouring of all the (six) causes (in their respective centres of manifestation) is well known, one thereby attains Śiva, the causeless cause of the universe. (with 6.192cd–193ab)
