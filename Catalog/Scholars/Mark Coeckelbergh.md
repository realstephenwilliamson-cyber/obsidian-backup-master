# Mark Coeckelbergh

Professor of Philosophy of Media and Technology at the University of Vienna since 2015, also Circle U Chair for Artificial Intelligence and ERA Chair at the Czech Academy of Sciences' Institute of Philosophy; he works on the ethics and politics of AI, robotics and digital technologies ([University of Vienna](https://philtech.univie.ac.at/mark-coeckelbergh); [team page](https://philtech.univie.ac.at/team/mark-coeckelbergh)).
