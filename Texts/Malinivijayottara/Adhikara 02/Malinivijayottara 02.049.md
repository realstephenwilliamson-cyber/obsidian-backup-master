## Mūla

पार्थिवम्प्राकृतम्चैव मायीयम्शाक्तमेव च ।
इति सम्क्षेपतः प्रोक्तमेतदण्डचतुष्टयम् ॥ ^mula

pārthivam prākṛtam caiva māyīyam śāktam eva ca |
iti samkṣepataḥ proktam etad aṇḍacatuṣṭayam || ^iast

## Translation

> **The Four Eggs and the Six Paths**
>
> It is said that this (sixfold Path is), in an abbreviated (form) (samkṣepataḥ), the group of four Eggs. (These are the Eggs) of Earth, Nature, Māyā and Power.[^68]

[^68]: MV 2/49 is quoted in TĀv ad 11/8, which is an explanation of it.
