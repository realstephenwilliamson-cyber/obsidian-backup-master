## Mūla

तच्च पूर्णेन्दुमेकं प्राग्वर्तितं प्राप्नुयाद्यथा ।
अन्योन्यग्रन्थियोगेन बद्धारत्वं प्रजायते ॥ ^mula

tacca pūrṇendumekaṃ prāgvartitaṃ prāpnuyādyathā |
anyonyagranthiyogena baddhāratvaṃ prajāyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 04 - Chapter Thirty-One, part 1#^pg93|p. 75]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=93>))
>
> That (pair) should reach (to make contact with) the one full moon drawn previously, in such a way that, by the union of (their) knots with one another, the spokes (of the Trident) are well connected. The same should be repeated on the other two sides. (with 31.20–21a)
