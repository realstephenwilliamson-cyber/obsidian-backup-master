## A plot against the king

**12:1** Now Mordecai took his rest in the courtyard with Gabatha and Tharra, the two eunuchs of the king who kept watch in the courtyard. ^12-1

**12:2** He overheard their conversation and inquired into their purposes and learned that they were preparing to lay hands on King Artaxerxes, and he informed the king concerning them. ^12-2

**12:3** Then the king examined the two eunuchs, and after they had confessed it, they were led away. ^12-3

**12:4** The king wrote these things down as a commemoration, and Mordecai wrote an account of them. ^12-4

**12:5** And the king ordered Mordecai to serve in the court and rewarded him for these things. ^12-5

**12:6** But Haman son of Hammedatha, a Bougean, who was in great honor with the king, determined to injure Mordecai and his people because of the two eunuchs of the king. ^12-6
