*Volume 3, folio 89b* · Emor

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Emor 7:23–7:25.*

**Emor 8:26** (AND HE THAT IS THE HIGH PRIEST AMONG HIS BRETHREN, ETC.) R. Abba here quoted the verse: To thee, O Lord, belongeth righteousness, but unto us confusion of face” (Dan. 9, 7). ‘ ^emor-8-26

**Emor 8:27** “Righteousness”’, he said, ‘refers refers to the place to which all the shining faces are attached, as it is attached to them; and “confusion of face” refers to the place from which the shining faces are removed. ^emor-8-27

**Emor 8:28** Therefore the High Priest should always present a face bright and shining and more joyful than others, seeing that he symbolizes the higher grade. ^emor-8-28

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Emor 9:29–12:38.*
