### XXXIV. XXXV. Enoch’s Journey to the North.

**34:1** And from thence I went towards the north to the ends of the earth, and there I saw a great and glorious device at the ends of the whole earth. ^34-1

**34:2** And here I saw three portals of heaven open in the heaven: through each of them proceed north winds: when they blow there is cold, hail, frost, snow, dew, and rain. ^34-2

**34:3** And out of one portal they blow for good: but when they blow through the other two portals, †it is with violence and affliction on the earth, and they blow with violence.† ^34-3
