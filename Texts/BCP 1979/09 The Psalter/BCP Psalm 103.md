# Psalm 103

← [[BCP Psalm 102]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 104]] →

*Benedic, anima mea* · Book Four · Twentieth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 103]]

**103:1** Bless the LORD, O my soul, \*  
and all that is within me, bless his holy Name. ^103-1

**103:2** Bless the LORD, O my soul, \*  
and forget not all his benefits. ^103-2

**103:3** He forgives all your sins \*  
and heals all your infirmities; ^103-3

**103:4** He redeems your life from the grave \*  
and crowns you with mercy and loving-kindness; ^103-4

**103:5** He satisfies you with good things, \*  
and your youth is renewed like an eagle's. ^103-5

**103:6** The LORD executes righteousness \*  
and judgment for all who are oppressed. ^103-6

**103:7** He made his ways known to Moses \*  
and his works to the children of Israel. ^103-7

**103:8** The LORD is full of compassion and mercy, \*  
slow to anger and of great kindness. ^103-8

**103:9** He will not always accuse us, \*  
nor will he keep his anger for ever. ^103-9

[p. 734] ^pg734

**103:10** He has not dealt with us according to our sins, \*  
nor rewarded us according to our wickedness. ^103-10

**103:11** For as the heavens are high above the earth, \*  
so is his mercy great upon those who fear him. ^103-11

**103:12** As far as the east is from the west, \*  
so far has he removed our sins from us. ^103-12

**103:13** As a father cares for his children, \*  
so does the LORD care for those who fear him. ^103-13

**103:14** For he himself knows whereof we are made; \*  
he remembers that we are but dust. ^103-14

**103:15** Our days are like the grass; \*  
we flourish like a flower of the field; ^103-15

**103:16** When the wind goes over it, it is gone, \*  
and its place shall know it no more. ^103-16

**103:17** But the merciful goodness of the LORD endures for ever on those who fear him, \*  
and his righteousness on children's children; ^103-17

**103:18** On those who keep his covenant \*  
and remember his commandments and do them. ^103-18

**103:19** The LORD has set his throne in heaven, \*  
and his kingship has dominion over all. ^103-19

**103:20** Bless the LORD, you angels of his,  
you mighty ones who do his bidding, \*  
and hearken to the voice of his word. ^103-20

**103:21** Bless the LORD, all you his hosts, \*  
you ministers of his who do his will. ^103-21

**103:22** Bless the LORD, all you works of his,  
in all places of his dominion; \*  
bless the LORD, O my soul. ^103-22
