## Chapter 123

**123:1** A song of ascents.  
To You, enthroned in heaven,  
I turn my eyes. ^123-1

**123:2** As the eyes of slaves follow their master’s hand,  
as the eyes of a slave-girl follow the hand of her mistress,  
so our eyes are toward the LORD our God,  
awaiting His favor. ^123-2

**123:3** Show us favor, O LORD,  
show us favor!  
We have had more than enough of contempt. ^123-3

**123:4** Long enough have we endured  
the scorn of the complacent,  
the contempt of the haughty. ^123-4
