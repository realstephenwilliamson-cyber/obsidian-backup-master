## Mūla

प्राच्यां विसर्गसत्तामनवच्छिदि ते पदे रूढाः ।
ये सिद्धिमाप्तुकामास्तेऽभ्युदितं रूपमाहरेयुरथो ॥ ^mula

prācyāṃ visargasattāmanavacchidi te pade rūḍhāḥ |
ye siddhimāptukāmāste'bhyuditaṃ rūpamāhareyuratho || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 11 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 3#^pg334|p. 317]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=334>))
>
> Now, those who, within this two-fold abode, tranquil and emergent, (thus) emitted (sṛṣṭa), attend to the essential being of emission (visargasattā) that precedes (them), become firmly fixed on the (supreme) unconditioned plane. (with 29.126cd–127ab)
>
> Now, those who wish to attain (worldly) accomplishment (siddhi) should eat the arisen (ejaculated) form (of that emission) (abhyudita). It is most pure, because of its proximity to consciousness, and (so it is) with that that they should worship. (with 29.127cd–128ab)

> ये सिद्धिमाप्तुकामास्तेऽभ्युदितं रूपमाहरेयुरथो ॥
> तेनैव पूजयेयुः संविन्नैकट्यशुद्धतमवपुषा ।

ये पिण्डस्थैर्यादिरूपां सिद्धिमाप्तुकामाः ते तदभ्युदितं रूपं कुण्डगोलकादिशब्दव्यपदेश्यमाहरेयुः । अथ तेनैव अभ्युदितेन रूपेण

"आनन्दो ब्रह्मणो रूपं...................... ।"

इति श्रुतेरानन्दमयतया संविन्नैकट्यात्

"तस्माद्यत्संविदो नातिदूरे तच्छुद्धमाहरेत् ।"

इति नीत्या शुद्धतमवपुषा पूजयेयुः--देवीचक्रं तर्पयेयुः-- इत्यर्थः ॥
