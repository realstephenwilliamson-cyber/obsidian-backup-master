## Mūla

वामदक्षिणसंस्थानचित्रत्वात्परिकल्पयेत् ।
वस्तुतो विश्वरूपास्ता देव्यो बोधात्मिका यतः ॥ ^mula

vāmadakṣiṇasaṃsthānacitratvātparikalpayet |
vastuto viśvarūpāstā devyo bodhātmikā yataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 06 - Chapter Fifteen, part 5#^pg213|p. 195]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=213>))
>
> Just as the double six-fold deposition was projected onto (one’s own) body previously, (so should it be done) here (on the deities). Then one may contemplate them, (each) endowed with their body, (variously) conceived according to one’s intention (sāṁkalpika), in accord with the various goals (kṛtya) (for which the rites are performed), as having two, four, six or more arms. One should imagine the various locations in the left and right (hands) (of their attributes,) such as a skull, trident, ascetic’s staff (khaṭvāṅga), (gestures bestowing) boons and fearlessness, a jar and so on. (with 15.326–328ab)
>
> In reality, these goddesses are consciousness, and so (do not have any particular form; rather, they) are all (and every) form (of all that exists). (Thus,) if they are to bestow liberation, they should essentially be (pure) unlimited consciousness alone. Then one should worship all the limbs and faces first, and at the end, the guardians of the directions and (their) weapons, together with the goddess (who is) in the middle, separately. (By worshipping the goddess in the centre,) all three (goddesses) are worshipped. (with 15.328cd–330ab)
