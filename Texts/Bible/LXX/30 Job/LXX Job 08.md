# LXX Job 08

← [[LXX Job 07]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Job 09]] →

*Job · [[LXX]]*

**8:1** Then Baldad the Sauchite answered, and said,  ^8-1

**8:2** How long wilt thou speak these things, *how long shall* the breath of thy mouth *be* abundant in words?  ^8-2

**8:3** Will the Lord be unjust when he judges; or will he that has made all things pervert justice?  ^8-3

**8:4** If thy sons have sinned before him, he has cast them away because of their transgression.  ^8-4

**8:5** But be thou early in prayer to the Lord Almighty.  ^8-5

**8:6** If thou art pure and true, he will hearken to thy supplication, and will restore to thee the habitation of righteousness.  ^8-6

**8:7** Though then thy beginning should be small, yet thy end should be unspeakably great.  ^8-7

**8:8** For ask of the former generation, and search diligently among the race of *our* fathers:  ^8-8

**8:9** (for we are of yesterday, and know nothing; for our life upon the earth is a shadow:)  ^8-9

**8:10** shall not these teach thee, and report *to thee*, and bring out words from *their* heart?  ^8-10

**8:11** Does the rush flourish without water, or shall the flag grow up without moisture?  ^8-11

**8:12** When it is yet on the root, and *though* it has not been cut down, does not any herb wither before it has received moisture?  ^8-12

**8:13** Thus then shall be the end of all that forget the Lord: for the hope of the ungodly shall perish.  ^8-13

**8:14** For his house shall be without inhabitants, and his tent shall prove a spider's web.  ^8-14

**8:15** If he should prop up his house, it shall not stand: and when he has taken hold of it, it shall not remain.  ^8-15

**8:16** For it is moist under the sun, and his branch shall come forth out of his dung-heap.  ^8-16

**8:17** He lies down upon a gathering of stones, and shall live in the midst of flints.  ^8-17

**8:18** If *God* should destroy *him*, his place shall deny him. Hast thou not seen such things,  ^8-18

**8:19** that such is the overthrow of the ungodly? and out of the earth another shall grow.  ^8-19

**8:20** For the Lord will by no means reject the harmless man; but he will not receive any gift of the ungodly.  ^8-20

**8:21** But he will fill with laughter the mouth of the sincere, and their lips with thanksgiving.  ^8-21

**8:22** But their adversaries shall clothe themselves with shame; and the habitation of the ungodly shall perish.  ^8-22
