## Mūla

जननादि ततः कर्म सर्वमेवंकृते कृतम् ।
परापरामनुस्मृत्य दद्यात्पूर्णाहुतिं पुनः ॥ ^mula

jananādi tataḥ karma sarvam evaṁkṛte kṛtam |
parāparām anusmṛtya dadyāt pūrṇāhutiṁ punaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2025 – April 2025
> p. 32 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=46>))
>
> Then, one should offer five oblations with the (five) limbs of the Vidyā. Thus, done in this way, all the (purificatory) rites beginning with (those performed at) birth are done.[^129] (with 8.113cd–114ab)

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2025 – April 2025
> p. 33 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=47>))
>
> Having recollected Parāparā, he should offer a full oblation again and (after) having worshipped the mother and the father of the fire, dismiss them. Then having taken fire (from the hearth, install it) again (separately) in order to prepare the sacrificial pap etc.[^130] (with 8.114cd–115)

[^129]: MV 8/113cd-114ab is quoted in TĀv ad 15/407cd-408ab (404cd-405ab). Read *evaṁkṛte* for *evaṁ kṛte.*
[^130]: MV 8/115 is paraphrased and explained in TĀ 15/414-415ab (411-412ab).
