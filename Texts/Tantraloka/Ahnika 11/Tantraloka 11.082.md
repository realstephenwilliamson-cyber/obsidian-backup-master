## Mūla

तेन गुप्तेन गुप्तास्ते शेषा वर्णास्त्विति स्फुटम् ।
एवं मामातृमानत्वमेयत्वैर्योऽवभासते ॥ ^mula

tena guptena guptāste śeṣā varṇāstviti sphuṭam |
evaṃ māmātṛmānatvameyatvairyo'vabhāsate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 03 - Chapter Eleven, part 2#^pg93|p. 73]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=93>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 11.82cd–83ab.
>
> (Śiva,) the Bestower of Boons, has clearly said that in the Siddhayogeśvarīmata, (where we read): ‘by keeping that (power inherent in the phonemes) hidden, (the Mantras) are kept hidden. (Apart from this power,) what remains are mere letters’. (with 11.81cd–82ab)
>
> This six-fold Path manifests in this way as noetic consciousness, subject, object and means of knowledge. It is (all) purified if (any one) of its own bodies is purified. (with 11.82cd–83ab)
