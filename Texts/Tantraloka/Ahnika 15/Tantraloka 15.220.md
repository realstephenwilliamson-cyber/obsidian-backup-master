## Mūla

दिशोऽपि प्रविभज्यन्ते प्राक्सव्योत्तरपश्चिमाः ।
स्वानुसारकृतं तं च दिग्विभागं सदा शिवः ॥ ^mula

diśo'pi pravibhajyante prāksavyottarapaścimāḥ |
svānusārakṛtaṃ taṃ ca digvibhāgaṃ sadā śivaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 05 - Chapter Fifteen, part 4#^pg152|p. 134]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=152>))
>
> What is the use of extensive (explanations)? The directions are divided into eastern, southern, northern and western in relation to the sacrificer (yaṣṭṛ), according to whether (a place is) in front of him (behind) and so on. (with 15.219cd–220ab)
>
> This (other) division of the directions, formed in relation to oneself, is always (sadā) presided over by Śiva, for (Śiva’s) body is wonderfully varied, just as is the sun (and the directions in relation to it). (with 15.220cd–221ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* अत्रापि परमेश्वर एव अधिष्ठाता--इत्याह--

> स्वानुसारकृतं तं च दिग्विभागं सदा शिवः ॥
> अधितिष्ठत्यर्कमिव स विचित्रवपुर्यतः ।
