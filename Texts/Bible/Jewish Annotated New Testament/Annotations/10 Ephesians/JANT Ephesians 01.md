# Ephesians 1 (JANT notes)

— · [[Jewish Annotated New Testament|JANT index]] · [[JANT Ephesians 02|Ephesians 2 →]]

Text: [[NRSVue Ephesians 01|NRSVue]] · [[Ephesians 01|BSB and SBLGNT]] · Introduction: [[JANT Ephesians - Introduction]]

### 1.1–2: Salutation

*[[NRSVue Ephesians 01#^1-1|Text]], p. 389* ^1-1

**[[NRSVue Ephesians 01#^1-1|1]]:** *Saints*, Gk “hagioi,” equivalent to Heb “qedoshim,” “holy ones,” here referring to those “who . . . are faithful.” The Torah describes Israel as a “holy nation” ([[Torah 17 - Yitro#^19-6|Ex 19.6]]) and commands that Israel “be holy” ([[Torah 30 - Kedoshim#^19-2|Lev 19.2]]). *In Ephesus*, absent from some early textual witnesses, while Marcion has “to the Laodiceans.” *Who . . . are faithful*, the emphasis on faith or belief (Heb “ʾemunah”) above performance of the obligations of the Law, repeated below (2.8**–**9), conforms to Paul’s program; see [[Romans 03#^3-21|Rom 3.21]]**–**4.25; [[Galatians 02#^2-15|Gal 2.15]]**–**3.25, and elsewhere. ^1-1-2

### 1.3–14: Blessing

*[[NRSVue Ephesians 01#^1-3|Text]], p. 389* ^1-3

**[[NRSVue Ephesians 01#^1-3|3]]:** *Blessed be*, reminiscent of the opening of blessing formulas used in the Tanakh (e.g., [[Torah 05 - Chayei Sara#^24-27|Gen 24.27]]); this formula, appearing more frequent in late Hebrew Bible texts and the Dead Sea Scrolls, became the standard opening formula in rabbinic blessings; see *m. Ber*. 6,9. *Spiritual blessing*, probably the blessing of “the seal of the promised Holy Spirit” (1.13). *Heavenly places*, Gk “ta epourania,” appears nowhere else in the NT. It refers to the unseen realm where God resides (1.20; 2.6). ^1-3-2

**[[NRSVue Ephesians 01#^1-4|4]]:** *Chose us . . . before the foundation of the world*, hints at predestination, the divine determination of the future, especially with regard to salvation; biblical and rabbinic texts highlight the importance of certain objects by claiming that they predate creation (see [[Proverbs 08#^8-22|Prov 8.22]]**–**31, *Gen. Rab*. 1.1). *Blameless* . . . *in love*, and not on account of our works. ^1-4

**[[NRSVue Ephesians 01#^1-5|5]]:** *Adoption as his children*, divinely chosen inheritance replaces biological descent. ^1-5

**[[NRSVue Ephesians 01#^1-6|6]]:** *Grace* (Gk “charitos”), “freely bestowed” and not earned; the rabbis (*Mek. Bo*, 5) explicitly reject the possibility of redemption through grace apart from works and insist upon the performance of “mitzvot,” divine commandments. *Beloved*, the meaning of the Heb name “David,” alluding to and affirming Jesus’ messianic status. ^1-6

**[[NRSVue Ephesians 01#^1-7|7]]:** *Redemption through his blood, the forgiveness of our trespasses*, referring to the crucifixion, understood as Jesus’ self-sacrifice; the blood of the sacrifice similarly was understood to effect atonement for sin ([[Torah 24 - Vayikra#^4-13|Lev 4.13]]**–**20; 16.14**–**20). ^1-7

**[[NRSVue Ephesians 01#^1-9|9]]:** *Mystery of his will*, or perhaps better, “secret,” referring to the disclosure of the truth that “Gentiles *saints who are also faithful* b Gk *in him* have become fellow heirs” of the covenantal promise, “members of the same body” as biological Israel; see 3.3**–**11. Jews were of different minds with respect to the prospect of the salvation of Gentiles. The rabbis asserted that Jews had a special claim on salvation (*m. Sanh*. 10.1), but some admitted that worthy Gentiles also merited salvation (*t. Sanh.* 13.2). ^1-9

**[[NRSVue Ephesians 01#^1-10|10]]:** *Fullness of time*, or “fulfillment of times,” that is, the completion of one era (see [[Daniel 12#^12-4|Dan 12.4]],[[Daniel 12#^12-9|9]]) and the birth of another, here that of Christ. ^1-10

**[[NRSVue Ephesians 01#^1-11|11]]:** *In Christ*, lit., “in him.” ^1-11

**[[NRSVue Ephesians 01#^1-11|11–13]]:** *In Christ we have also obtained an inheritance . . . In him you also . . . were marked with the seal*, Gk “Christ” = Heb “mašiaḥ,” hence: Messiah; biological Jews were the first to obtain an inheritance of salvation through the hoped-for Messiah, but Gentile believers in Christ now participate in the inheritance. ^1-11-2

**[[NRSVue Ephesians 01#^1-13|13]]:** *Marked with the seal of the promised Holy Spirit*, the reference to marking suggests an allusion to the seal of circumcision ([[Romans 04#^4-11|Rom 4.11]]; in [[Torah 03 - Lech Lecha#^17-11|Gen 17.11]], “sign”), now construed as spiritual and not in the flesh. ^1-13

### 1.15–23: Thanksgiving and prayer

*[[NRSVue Ephesians 01#^1-15|Text]], p. 390* ^1-15

**[[NRSVue Ephesians 01#^1-16|16]]:** *Give thanks*, rabbinic prayers also combine thanksgiving and supplication; see *b. Meg* 17b**–**18a. ^1-16

**[[NRSVue Ephesians 01#^1-17|17]]:** *Father of glory*, see [[Psalms 024#^24-7|Ps 24.7]]**–**10, with its repeated mention of “King of glory,” and, at [[Psalms 029#^29-3|Ps 29.3]], “God of glory.” *Spirit of wisdom and revelation*, reminiscent of the messianic ruler in [[Isaiah 11#^11-2|Isa 11.2]]. ^1-17

**[[NRSVue Ephesians 01#^1-18|18]]:** *Eyes of your heart*, the heart was understood as the seat of understanding ([[Torah 20 - Tetzaveh#^28-3|Ex 28.3]]; [[Torah 21 - Ki Tisa#^31-6|31.6]], and elsewhere); light, seen by the eyes, is associated with understanding; see Ecc 2.13**–**4 and *4 Ezra* \[2 Esd\] 14.25. ^1-18

**[[NRSVue Ephesians 01#^1-19|19]]:** *For us who believe*, advancing Paul’s agenda giving priority to belief. ^1-19

**[[NRSVue Ephesians 01#^1-20|20–22]]:** In tone and language like certain creation or enthronement psalms; see, e.g., [[Psalms 008#^8-2|Ps 8.1–3]],[[Psalms 008#^8-7|6]]; 110.1. Also reminiscent of Paul’s language in [[Philippians 02#^2-9|Phil 2.9]]**–**11. ^1-20

**[[NRSVue Ephesians 01#^1-20|20]]:** *This power . . . when he raised him from the dead*, like the second blessing of the rabbinic prayer, the Amidah, associating God’s power with resurrection; see *m. Ber*. 5.2. *Seated him at his right hand*, see [[Psalms 110#^110-1|Ps 110.1]]. ^1-20-2

**[[NRSVue Ephesians 01#^1-21|21]]:** *This age . . . age to come*, see [[Matthew 12#^12-32|Mt 12.32]] and especially [[Mark 10#^10-30|Mk 10.30]] (“the age to come eternal life”); the rabbinic Heb equivalent is “ʿolam ha-zeh” and “ʿolam ha-baʾ,” “this world” and “the world to come,” that is, the redeemed world following resurrection; see, e.g., [[Bava Batra 75#75b|*b. B. Bat*. 75b]]. ^1-21

**[[NRSVue Ephesians 01#^1-22|22–23]]:** *Over all things for the church, which is his body*, see 4.15**–**16 and [[1 Corinthians 12#^12-12|1 Cor 12.12]]**–**27. ^1-22

**[[NRSVue Ephesians 01#^1-22|22]]:** *Under his feet*, [[Psalms 008#^8-7|Ps 8.7]]. ^1-22-2
