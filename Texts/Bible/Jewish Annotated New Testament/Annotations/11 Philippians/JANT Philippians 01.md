# Philippians 1 (JANT notes)

— · [[Jewish Annotated New Testament|JANT index]] · [[JANT Philippians 02|Philippians 2 →]]

Text: [[NRSVue Philippians 01|NRSVue]] · [[Philippians 01|BSB and SBLGNT]] · Introduction: [[JANT Philippians - Introduction]]

### 1.1–2: Salutation

*[[NRSVue Philippians 01#^1-1|Text]], p. 399* ^1-1

**[[NRSVue Philippians 01#^1-1|1]]:** *Timothy*, a faithful Pauline courier and frequent missionary companion*,* had assisted in founding this church and remained popular among its members. *Servants*, lit., “slaves” (see translators’ note *a*: “douloi,” in LXX for Heb “ʿebed,” but rarely as a description of relation to God; see [[Jeremiah 02#^2-14|Jer 2.14]]). *Bishops and deacons,* overseers and assistants (see translators’ notes *b*, *c*). The exact functions of those officials with these titles have yet to be settled. ^1-1-2

**[[NRSVue Philippians 01#^1-2|2]]:** *Grace . . . peace*, standard opening for a letter (e.g., [[1 Corinthians 01#^1-3|1 Cor 1.3]]; [[Galatians 01#^1-3|Gal 1.3]]). *Lord* (Gk “kyrios,” used in LXX for “adonai/YHWH” but also as a simple title of respect), Paul’s usual title for Jesus within a standard opening; also as an occasional synonym for Christ (e.g., [[1 Corinthians 11#^11-27|1 Cor 11.27]]; [[2 Corinthians 02#^2-12|2 Cor 2.12]]; [[2 Corinthians 04#^4-5|4.5]]). ^1-2

### 1.3–11: Thanksgiving and prayer

*[[NRSVue Philippians 01#^1-3|Text]], p. 399* ^1-3

**[[NRSVue Philippians 01#^1-5|5]]:** *The first day*, of Paul’s preaching at Philippi. ^1-5

**[[NRSVue Philippians 01#^1-6|6]]:** *Day of Jesus Christ*, of his return and the close of this age (see 1.10; 2.16). ^1-6

**[[NRSVue Philippians 01#^1-9|9]]:** *Prayer* (for others), see [[Job 42#^42-8|Job 42.8]]. ^1-9

**[[NRSVue Philippians 01#^1-11|11]]:** *Harvest*, a standard image in Jesus’ teaching (e.g., [[Matthew 09#^9-37|Mt 9.37]]; see [[James 03#^3-18|Jas 3.18]]; see also the Hebrew prophets, [[Jeremiah 02#^2-3|Jer 2.3]]). ^1-11

### 1.12–26: Paul’s prison circumstances and outlook

*[[NRSVue Philippians 01#^1-12|Text]], p. 399* ^1-12

**[[NRSVue Philippians 01#^1-13|13]]:** *Imperial guard*, bodyguards to the emperor and family, a local contingent of this soldiery. *For Christ*, for preaching Christ. ^1-13

**[[NRSVue Philippians 01#^1-15|15–17]]:** *Envy . . . rivalry . . . goodwill*, as opposed to the ambitious, who are disposed to worsen his suffering, there stand others motivated by love, knowing the truth about Paul’s suffering; the former, Paul condemns less for their message than for their efforts to outdo him. ^1-15

**[[NRSVue Philippians 01#^1-20|20]]:** *Not be put to shame*: Egyptian, Greco-Roman, biblical, and rabbinic cultures held honor an ultimate asset, shame an ultimate disgrace (see [[Psalms 025#^25-3|Ps 25.3]]; [[Psalms 034#^34-4|34.3–5]] \[LXX 33.4–6\]; [[2 Corinthians 10#^10-8|2 Cor 10.8]]). ^1-20

**[[NRSVue Philippians 01#^1-21|21]]:** *Living . . . gain*, Paul’s approach is Stoic: focusing on the divine and ignoring vicissitudes of his situation. ^1-21

**[[NRSVue Philippians 01#^1-23|23]]:** *Be with Christ*: Not immediately upon execution but with the resurrection of all deceased Christian faithful upon Christ’s return (see [[1 Thessalonians 04#^4-13|1 Thess 4.13–17]]). ^1-23

**[[NRSVue Philippians 01#^1-25|25]]:** *Convinced*, Paul rationalizes that God will spare him execution so that he can assist the Philippians further. ^1-25

**[[NRSVue Philippians 01#^1-26|26]]:** *Boasting*, speaking exultantly of another, as of the Lord’s attributes and deeds (as in [[1 Corinthians 01#^1-26|1 Cor 1.26–31]]), likely drawing upon [[Jeremiah 09#^9-23|Jer 9.23–24]]. *Come to you again*, it is unknown whether this happened. ^1-26

### 1.27–2.18: Exhortations to unity and humility

*[[NRSVue Philippians 01#^1-27|Text]], p. 400* ^1-27

**[[NRSVue Philippians 01#^1-28|28]]:** *Your opponents*, most likely pagans opposing the gospel cause, i.e., Gentiles inside Philippi but outside its church. Of various opponents whom the Epistle notes (1.15–17,27–28; 3.2,18), only those designated in this verse may pose a current threat inside Philippi itself. ^1-28

**[[NRSVue Philippians 01#^1-29|29]]:** *Privilege . . . of suffering*, Tanakh passages that speak about suffering do not glorify it (e.g., Ps 13; 69; Job passim). ^1-29

**[[NRSVue Philippians 01#^1-30|30]]:** *Same struggle* that Paul underwent when founding this church (see [[1 Thessalonians 02#^2-2|1 Thess 2.2]]). *Still have*, Paul’s imprisonment. ^1-30
