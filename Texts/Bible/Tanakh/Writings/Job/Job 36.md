# Job 36

## Chapter 36

**36:1** Then Elihu spoke once more. ^36-1

**36:2** Wait a little and let me hold forth;  
There is still more to say for God. ^36-2

**36:3** I will make my opinions widely known;  
I will justify my Maker. ^36-3

**36:4** In truth, my words are not false;  
A man of sound opinions is before you. ^36-4

**36:5** See, God is mighty; He is not contemptuous;  
He is mighty in strength and mind. ^36-5

**36:6** He does not let the wicked live;  
He grants justice to the lowly. ^36-6

**36:7** He does not withdraw His eyes from the righteous;  
With kings on thrones  
He seats them forever, and they are exalted. ^36-7

**36:8** If they are bound in shackles  
And caught in trammels of affliction, ^36-8

**36:9** He declares to them what they have done,  
And that their transgressions are excessive; ^36-9

**36:10** He opens their understanding by discipline,  
And orders them back from mischief. ^36-10

**36:11** If they will serve obediently,  
They shall spend their days in happiness,  
Their years in delight. ^36-11

**36:12** But if they are not obedient,  
They shall perish by the sword,  
Die for lack of understanding. ^36-12

**36:13** But the impious in heart become enraged;  
They do not cry for help when He afflicts them. ^36-13

**36:14** They die in their youth;  
[Expire] among the depraved. ^36-14

**36:15** He rescues the lowly from their affliction,  
And opens their understanding through distress. ^36-15

**36:16** Indeed, He draws you away from the brink of distress  
To a broad place where there is no constraint;  
Your table is laid out with rich food. ^36-16

**36:17** You are obsessed with the case of the wicked man,  
But the justice of the case will be upheld. ^36-17

**36:18** Let anger at his affluence not mislead you;  
Let much bribery not turn you aside. ^36-18

**36:19** Will your limitless wealth avail you,[^1]  
All your powerful efforts? ^36-19

**36:20** Do not long for the night  
When peoples vanish where they are. ^36-20

**36:21** Beware! Do not turn to mischief;  
Because of that you have been tried by affliction. ^36-21

**36:22** See, God is beyond reach in His power;  
Who governs like Him? ^36-22

**36:23** Who ever reproached Him for His conduct?  
Who ever said, “You have done wrong”? ^36-23

**36:24** Remember, then, to magnify His work,  
Of which men have sung, ^36-24

**36:25** Which all men have beheld,  
Men have seen, from a distance. ^36-25

**36:26** See, God is greater than we can know;  
The number of His years cannot be counted. ^36-26

**36:27** He forms the droplets of water,  
Which cluster into rain, from His mist. ^36-27

**36:28** The skies rain;  
They pour down on all mankind. ^36-28

**36:29** Can one, indeed, contemplate the expanse of clouds,  
The thunderings from His pavilion? ^36-29

**36:30** See, He spreads His lightning over it;  
It fills the bed of the sea. ^36-30

**36:31** By these things He controls peoples;  
He gives food in abundance. ^36-31

**36:32** Lightning fills His hands;  
He orders it to hit the mark. ^36-32

**36:33** Its noise tells of Him.  
The kindling of anger against iniquity.[^2] ^36-33

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
