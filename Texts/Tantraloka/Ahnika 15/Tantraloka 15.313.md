## Mūla

तस्य नाभ्युत्थितं मूर्धरन्ध्रत्रयविनिर्गतम् ।
नादान्तात्म स्मरेच्छक्तिव्यापिनीसमनोज्ज्वलम् ॥ ^mula

tasya nābhyutthitaṃ mūrdharandhratrayavinirgatam |
nādāntātma smarecchaktivyāpinīsamanojjvalam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 06 - Chapter Fifteen, part 5#^pg203|p. 185]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=203>))
>
> One should recollect the three rays that have risen up from (Sadāśiva’s) navel. At the end of the End of Sound, they are Power (śakti), the Pervasive One (vyāpinī), and the Equal One (samanā). Resplendent, they have emerged from within the three cavities of the head, (and extend) up to the End of the Twelve. There also (on top of) them, are the three (pure) white lotuses of the Transmental. This is the Throne consisting of thirty-seven principles. (with 15.313–314)
