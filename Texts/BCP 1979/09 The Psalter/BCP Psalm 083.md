# Psalm 83

← [[BCP Psalm 082]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 084]] →

*Deus, quis similis?* · Book Three · Sixteenth Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 083]]

**83:1** O God, do not be silent; \*  
do not keep still nor hold your peace, O God; ^83-1

**83:2** For your enemies are in tumult, \*  
and those who hate you have lifted up their heads. ^83-2

**83:3** They take secret counsel against your people \*  
and plot against those whom you protect. ^83-3

**83:4** They have said, "Come, let us wipe them out from among the nations; \*  
let the name of Israel be remembered no more." ^83-4

**83:5** They have conspired together; \*  
they have made an alliance against you: ^83-5

**83:6** The tents of Edom and the Ishmaelites; \*  
the Moabites and the Hagarenes; ^83-6

**83:7** Gebal, and Ammon, and Amalek; \*  
the Philistines and those who dwell in Tyre. ^83-7

**83:8** The Assyrians also have joined them, \*  
and have come to help the people of Lot. ^83-8

**83:9** Do to them as you did to Midian, \*  
to Sisera, and to Jabin at the river of Kishon: ^83-9

**83:10** They were destroyed at Endor; \*  
they became like dung upon the ground. ^83-10

**83:11** Make their leaders like Oreb and Zeëb, \*  
and all their commanders like Zebah and Zalmunna, ^83-11

[p. 707] ^pg707

**83:12** Who said, "Let us take for ourselves \*  
the fields of God as our possession." ^83-12

**83:13** O my God, make them like whirling dust \*  
and like chaff before the wind; ^83-13

**83:14** Like fire that burns down a forest, \*  
like the flame that sets mountains ablaze. ^83-14

**83:15** Drive them with your tempest \*  
and terrify them with your storm; ^83-15

**83:16** Cover their faces with shame, O LORD, \*  
that they may seek your Name. ^83-16

**83:17** Let them be disgraced and terrified for ever; \*  
let them be put to confusion and perish. ^83-17

**83:18** Let them know that you, whose Name is YAHWEH, \*  
you alone are the Most High over all the earth. ^83-18
