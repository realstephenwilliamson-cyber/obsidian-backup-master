## Mūla

अपरं परापरं च परं च विधिमिच्छया ।
तद्योजनानुसारेण श्रित्वा न्यासः षडध्वनः ॥ ^mula

aparaṃ parāparaṃ ca paraṃ ca vidhimicchayā |
tadyojanānusāreṇa śritvā nyāsaḥ ṣaḍadhvanaḥ || ^iast

> The six Paths are deposited on the basis of the lower (apara), middling (parāpara) and supreme (para) procedure (relating to the three corresponding Vidyās), as (the teacher) wishes, in accord with their (appropriate) application.

## Viveka
