# Psalm 17 (NRSVue)

## Prayer for deliverance from persecutors

*A Prayer of David.*

**17:1** Hear a just cause, O LORD; attend to my cry; give ear to my prayer from lips free of deceit. ^17-1

**17:2** From you let my vindication come; let your eyes see the right. ^17-2

**17:3** If you try my heart, if you visit me by night, if you test me, you will find no wickedness in me; my mouth does not transgress. ^17-3

**17:4** As for what others do, by the word of your lips I have avoided the ways of the violent. ^17-4

**17:5** My steps have held fast to your paths; my feet have not slipped. ^17-5

**17:6** I call upon you, for you will answer me, O God; incline your ear to me; hear my words. ^17-6

**17:7** Wondrously show your steadfast love, O savior of those who seek refuge from their adversaries at your right hand. ^17-7

**17:8** Guard me as the apple of the eye; hide me in the shadow of your wings, ^17-8

**17:9** from the wicked who despoil me, my deadly enemies who surround me. ^17-9

**17:10** They close their hearts to pity; with their mouths they speak arrogantly. ^17-10

**17:11** They flush me out;^[Q ms Gk: MT *Our steps*] now they surround me; they set their eyes to cast me to the ground. ^17-11

**17:12** They are like a lion eager to tear, like a young lion lurking in ambush. ^17-12

**17:13** Rise up, O LORD, confront them, overthrow them! By your sword deliver my life from the wicked, ^17-13

**17:14** from mortals—by your hand, O LORD—from mortals whose portion in life is in this world. May their bellies be filled with what you have stored up for them; may their children have more than enough; may they leave something over to their little ones. ^17-14

**17:15** As for me, I shall behold your face in righteousness; when I awake I shall be satisfied, beholding your likeness. ^17-15
