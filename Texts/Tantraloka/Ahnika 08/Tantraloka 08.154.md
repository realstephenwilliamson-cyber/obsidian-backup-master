> 7) Satyaloka (the World of Truth) The abode of Brahmā beyond Tapoloka is Satyaloka (the World of Truth), which is one hundred and sixty million leagues in extent. Svayambhŭ (the Self-born One) resides there and reveals the universe. In Satya ((the World of) Truth) are the Vedas and other (related treatises, and the sages who live there) are immersed in bliss and purified by meditation and ritual action.
