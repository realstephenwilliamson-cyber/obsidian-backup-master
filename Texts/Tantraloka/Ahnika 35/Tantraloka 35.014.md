## Mūla

भोगापवर्गतद्धेतुप्रसिद्धिशतशोभितः ।
तद्विमर्शस्वभावोऽसौ भैरवः परमेश्वरः ॥ ^mula

bhogāpavargataddhetuprasiddhiśataśobhitaḥ |
tadvimarśasvabhāvo'sau bhairavaḥ parameśvaraḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 09 - Chapter Thirty-Five#^pg216|p. 198]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=216>))
>
> He who is adorned with (countless) hundreds of a priori convictions that are the cause of (both) worldly fruits (bhoga) and liberation, and whose nature is that reflective awareness, is Bhairava, the Supreme Lord (parameśvara).
