# Psalm 143

← [[BCP Psalm 142]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 144]] →

*Domine, exaudi* · Book Five · Twenty-ninth Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 143]]

**143:1** LORD, hear my prayer,  
and in your faithfulness heed my supplications; \*  
answer me in your righteousness. ^143-1

**143:2** Enter not into judgment with your servant, \*  
for in your sight shall no one living be justified. ^143-2

[p. 799]

**143:3** For my enemy has sought my life;  
he has crushed me to the ground; \*  
he has made me live in dark places like those who are long dead. ^143-3

**143:4** My spirit faints within me; \*  
my heart within me is desolate. ^143-4

**143:5** I remember the time past;  
I muse upon all your deeds; \*  
I consider the works of your hands. ^143-5

**143:6** I spread out my hands to you; \*  
my soul gasps to you like a thirsty land. ^143-6

**143:7** O LORD, make haste to answer me; my spirit fails me; \*  
do not hide your face from me  
or I shall be like those who go down to the Pit. ^143-7

**143:8** Let me hear of your loving-kindness in the morning,  
for I put my trust in you; \*  
show me the road that I must walk,  
for I lift up my soul to you. ^143-8

**143:9** Deliver me from my enemies, O LORD, \*  
for I flee to you for refuge. ^143-9

**143:10** Teach me to do what pleases you, for you are my God; \*  
let your good Spirit lead me on level ground. ^143-10

**143:11** Revive me, O LORD, for your Name's sake; \*  
for your righteousness' sake, bring me out of trouble. ^143-11

**143:12** Of your goodness, destroy my enemies  
and bring all my foes to naught, \*  
for truly I am your servant. ^143-12
