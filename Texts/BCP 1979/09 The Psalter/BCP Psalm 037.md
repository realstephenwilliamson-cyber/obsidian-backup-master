# Psalm 37

← [[BCP Psalm 036]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 038]] →

** · Book One · Seventh Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 037]]

**Part I** Noli æmulari

**37:1** Do not fret yourself because of evildoers; \*  
do not be jealous of those who do wrong. ^37-1

**37:2** For they shall soon wither like the grass, \*  
and like the green grass fade away. ^37-2

**37:3** Put your trust in the LORD and do good; \*  
dwell in the land and feed on its riches. ^37-3

**37:4** Take delight in the LORD, \*  
and he shall give you your heart's desire. ^37-4

**37:5** Commit your way to the LORD and put your trust in him, \*  
and he will bring it to pass. ^37-5

**37:6** He will make your righteousness as clear as the light \*  
and your just dealing as the noonday. ^37-6

**37:7** Be still before the LORD \*  
and wait patiently for him. ^37-7

**37:8** Do not fret yourself over the one who prospers, \*  
the one who succeeds in evil schemes. ^37-8

**37:9** Refrain from anger, leave rage alone; \*  
do not fret yourself; it leads only to evil. ^37-9

[p. 634]

**37:10** For evildoers shall be cut off, \*  
but those who wait upon the LORD shall possess the land. ^37-10

**37:11** In a little while the wicked shall be no more; \*  
you shall search out their place, but they will not be there. ^37-11

**37:12** But the lowly shall possess the land; \*  
they will delight in abundance of peace. ^37-12

**37:13** The wicked plot against the righteous \*  
and gnash at them with their teeth. ^37-13

**37:14** The Lord laughs at the wicked, \*  
because he sees that their day will come. ^37-14

**37:15** The wicked draw their sword and bend their bow  
to strike down the poor and needy, \*  
to slaughter those who are upright in their ways. ^37-15

**37:16** Their sword shall go through their own heart, \*  
and their bow shall be broken. ^37-16

**37:17** The little that the righteous has \*  
is better than great riches of the wicked. ^37-17

**37:18** For the power of the wicked shall be broken, \*  
but the LORD upholds the righteous. ^37-18

**Psalm 37: Part II** Novit Dominus

**37:19** The LORD cares for the lives of the godly, \*  
and their inheritance shall last for ever. ^37-19

**37:20** They shall not be ashamed in bad times, \*  
and in days of famine they shall have enough. ^37-20

**37:21** As for the wicked, they shall perish, \*  
and the enemies of the LORD, like the glory of the meadows, shall vanish;  
they shall vanish like smoke. ^37-21

[p. 635]

**37:22** The wicked borrow and do not repay, \*  
but the righteous are generous in giving. ^37-22

**37:23** Those who are blessed by God shall possess the land, \*  
but those who are cursed by him shall be destroyed. ^37-23

**37:24** Our steps are directed by the LORD; \*  
he strengthens those in whose way he delights. ^37-24

**37:25** If they stumble, they shall not fall headlong, \*  
for the LORD holds them by the hand. ^37-25

**37:26** I have been young and now I am old, \*  
but never have I seen the righteous forsaken,  
or their children begging bread. ^37-26

**37:27** The righteous are always generous in their lending, \*  
and their children shall be a blessing. ^37-27

**37:28** Turn from evil, and do good, \*  
and dwell in the land for ever. ^37-28

**37:29** For the LORD loves justice; \*  
he does not forsake his faithful ones. ^37-29

**37:30** They shall be kept safe for ever, \*  
but the offspring of the wicked shall be destroyed. ^37-30

**37:31** The righteous shall possess the land \*  
and dwell in it for ever. ^37-31

**37:32** The mouth of the righteous utters wisdom, \*  
and their tongue speaks what is right. ^37-32

**37:33** The law of their God is in their heart, \*  
and their footsteps shall not falter. ^37-33

**37:34** The wicked spy on the righteous \*  
and seek occasion to kill them. ^37-34

[p. 636]

**37:35** The LORD will not abandon them to their hand, \*  
nor let them be found guilty when brought to trial. ^37-35

**37:36** Wait upon the LORD and keep his way; \*  
he will raise you up to possess the land,  
and when the wicked are cut off, you will see it. ^37-36

**37:37** I have seen the wicked in their arrogance, \*  
flourishing like a tree in full leaf. ^37-37

**37:38** I went by, and behold, they were not there; \*  
I searched for them, but they could not be found. ^37-38

**37:39** Mark those who are honest;  
observe the upright; \*  
for there is a future for the peaceable. ^37-39

**37:40** Transgressors shall be destroyed, one and all; \*  
the future of the wicked is cut off. ^37-40

**37:41** But the deliverance of the righteous comes from the LORD; \*  
he is their stronghold in time of trouble. ^37-41

**37:42** The LORD will help them and rescue them; \*  
he will rescue them from the wicked and deliver them,  
because they seek refuge in him. ^37-42
