## Chapter 15

**15:1** He had houses made for himself in the City of David, and he prepared a place for the Ark of God, and pitched a tent for it. ^15-1

**15:2** Then David gave orders that none but the Levites were to carry the Ark of God, for the LORD had chosen them to carry the Ark of the LORD and to minister to Him forever. ^15-2

**15:3** David assembled all Israel in Jerusalem to bring up the Ark of the LORD to its place, which he had prepared for it. ^15-3

**15:4** Then David gathered together the Aaronides and the Levites: ^15-4

**15:5** the sons of Kohath: Uriel the officer and his kinsmen—120; ^15-5

**15:6** the sons of Merari: Asaiah the officer and his kinsmen—220; ^15-6

**15:7** the sons of Gershom: Joel the officer and his kinsmen—130; ^15-7

**15:8** the sons of Elizaphan: Shemaiah the officer and his kinsmen—200; ^15-8

**15:9** the sons of Hebron: Eliel the officer and his kinsmen—80; ^15-9

**15:10** the sons of Uzziel: Amminadab the officer and his kinsmen—112. ^15-10

**15:11** David sent for Zadok and Abiathar the priests, and for the Levites: Uriel, Asaiah, Joel, Shemaiah, Eliel, and Amminadab. ^15-11

**15:12** He said to them, “You are the heads of the clans of the Levites; sanctify yourselves, you and your kinsmen, and bring up the Ark of the LORD God of Israel to [the place] I have prepared for it. ^15-12

**15:13** Because you were not there the first time,[^1] the LORD our God burst out against us, for we did not show due regard for Him.” ^15-13

**15:14** The priests and Levites sanctified themselves in order to bring up the Ark of the LORD God of Israel. ^15-14

**15:15** The Levites carried the Ark of God by means of poles on their shoulders, as Moses had commanded in accordance with the word of the LORD. ^15-15

**15:16** David ordered the officers of the Levites to install their kinsmen, the singers, with musical instruments, harps, lyres, and cymbals, joyfully making their voices heard. ^15-16

**15:17** So the Levites installed Heman son of Joel and, of his kinsmen, Asaph son of Berechiah; and, of the sons of Merari their kinsmen, Ethan son of Kushaiah. ^15-17

**15:18** Together with them were their kinsmen of second rank, Zechariah, Ben, Jaaziel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Unni, Eliab, Benaiah, Maaseiah, Mattithiah, Eliphalehu, Mikneiah, Obed-edom and Jeiel the gatekeepers. ^15-18

**15:19** Also the singers Heman, Asaph, and Ethan to sound the bronze cymbals, ^15-19

**15:20** and Zechariah, Aziel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Unni, Eliab, Maaseiah, and Benaiah with harps on alamoth;[^2] ^15-20

**15:21** also Mattithiah, Eliphalehu, Mikneiah, Obed-edom, Jeiel, and Azaziah, with lyres to lead on the sheminith;[^3] ^15-21

**15:22** also Chenaniah, officer of the Levites in song;[^4] he was in charge of the song[^5] because he was a master. ^15-22

**15:23** Berechiah and Elkanah were gatekeepers for the Ark. ^15-23

**15:24** Shebaniah, Joshaphat, Nethanel, Amasai, Zechariah, Benaiah, and Eliezer the priests sounded the trumpets before the Ark of God, and Obededom and Jehiah were gatekeepers for the Ark. ^15-24

**15:25** [^6]Then David and the elders of Israel and the officers of the thousands who were going to bring up the Ark of the Covenant of the LORD from the house of Obed-edom were joyful. ^15-25

**15:26** Since God helped the Levites who were carrying the Ark of the Covenant of the LORD, they sacrificed seven bulls and seven rams. ^15-26

**15:27** Now David and all the Levites who were carrying the Ark, and the singers and Chenaniah, officer of song of the singers, were wrapped[^7] in robes of fine linen, and David wore a linen ephod. ^15-27

**15:28** All Israel brought up the Ark of the Covenant of the LORD with shouts and with blasts of the horn, with trumpets and cymbals, playing on harps and lyres. ^15-28

**15:29** As the Ark of the Covenant of the LORD arrived at the City of David, Michal daughter of Saul looked out of the window and saw King David leaping and dancing, and she despised him for it. ^15-29

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^5]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^6]: Vv. 25–29 are found also in 2 Sam. 6.12–16.
[^7]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
