## Mūla

उच्चारकरणध्यानवर्णस्थानप्रकल्पनैः ।
यो भवेत्स समावेशः सम्यगाणव उच्यते ॥ ^mula

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. I
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 01 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v01 10 - Chapter One, part 9#^pg355|p. 341]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 01.pdf#page=355>))
>
> The penetration (āveśa) that arises for one who does not think of anything, by an intense (guruṇā) awakening (prabodhataḥ), is said to be the one pertaining to Śiva (śāmbhava). The penetration one attains whilst thinking of (the nature of) reality (vastu) with the mind alone, without (having to practice) the utterance of Mantra (uccāra) (or the like), is here called Empowered (śākta). The penetration that takes place by (the practice) of the utterance (of Mantra and concentration on the movement of the breath) (uccāra), the instruments (of Yoga) (karaṇa), visualization (dhyāna), (the cycling of) phonemes (in the breath) (varṇa) (as the subtle practice of Mantra), and the formation of supports (sthānakalpanā) (for ritual action and concentration), is rightly said to be Individual (āṇava). (with 1.168–170)

## Viveka

> अकिञ्चिच्चिन्तकस्यैव गुरुणा प्रतिबोधतः ।
> उत्पद्यते य आवेशः शाम्भवोऽसावुदीरितः ॥
> उच्चाररहितं वस्तु चेतसैव विचिन्तयन् ।
> यं समावेशमाप्नोति शाक्तः सोऽत्राभिधीयते ॥
> उच्चारकरणध्यानवर्णस्थानप्रकल्पनैः ।
> यो भवेत्स समावेशः सम्यगाणव उच्यते ॥

व्यत्यासपाठे च अयमाशयः -- यद् उपायोपेयादिना द्वारद्वारिभावेन शाम्भवोपाये एव प्राधान्येन विश्रान्तिः इति ॥ १६७-१७० ॥
