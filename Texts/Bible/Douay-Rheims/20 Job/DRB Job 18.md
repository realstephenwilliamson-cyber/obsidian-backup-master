# Job 18 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Job 17|← Job 17]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Job 19|Job 19 →]]

**18:1** Then Baldad the Suhite answered, and said: ^18-1

**18:2** How long will you throw out words? understand first, and so let us speak. ^18-2

**18:3** Why are we reputed as beasts, and counted vile before you? ^18-3

**18:4** Thou that destroyest thy soul in thy fury, shall the earth be forsaken for thee, and shall rocks be removed out of their place? ^18-4

**18:5** Shall not the light of the wicked be extinguished, and the flame of his fire not shine? ^18-5

**18:6** The light shall be dark in his tabernacle, and the lamp that is over him, shall be put out. ^18-6

**18:7** The step of his strength shall be straitened, and his own counsel shall cast him down headlong. ^18-7

**18:8** For he hath thrust his feet into a net, and walketh in its meshes. ^18-8

**18:9** The sole of his foot shall be held in a snare, and thirst shall burn against him. ^18-9

**18:10** A gin is hidden for him in the earth, and his trap upon the path. ^18-10

**18:11** Fears shall terrify him on every side, and shall entangle his feet. ^18-11

**18:12** Let his strength be wasted with famine, and let hunger invade his ribs. ^18-12

**18:13** Let it devour the beauty of his skin, let the firstborn death consume his arms. ^18-13

**18:14** Let his confidence be rooted out of his tabernacle, and let destruction tread upon him like a king. ^18-14

**18:15** Let the companions of him that is not, dwell in his tabernacle, let brimstone be sprinkled in his tent. ^18-15

**18:16** Let his roots be dried up beneath, and his harvest destroyed above. ^18-16

**18:17** Let the memory of him perish from the earth and let not his name be renowned in the streets. ^18-17

**18:18** He shall drive him out of light into darkness, and shall remove him out of the world. ^18-18

**18:19** His seed shall not subsist, nor his offspring among his people, nor any remnants in his country. ^18-19

**18:20** They that come after him shall be astonished at his day, and horror shall fall upon them that went before. ^18-20

**18:21** These men are the tabernacles of the wicked, and this the place of him that knoweth not God. ^18-21
