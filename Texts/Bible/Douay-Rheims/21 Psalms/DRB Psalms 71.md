# Psalms 71 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 70|← Psalms 70]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 72|Psalms 72 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 71|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**71:1** A psalm on Solomon. ^71-1

**71:2** Give to the king thy judgment, O God: and to the king’s son thy justice: To judge thy people with justice, and thy poor with judgment. ^71-2

**71:3** Let the mountains receive peace for the people: and the hills justice. ^71-3

**71:4** He shall judge the poor of the people, and he shall save the children of the poor: and he shall humble the oppressor. ^71-4

**71:5** And he shall continue with the sun, and before the moon, throughout all generations. ^71-5

**71:6** He shall come down like rain upon the fleece; and as showers falling gently upon the earth. ^71-6

**71:7** In his days shall justice spring up, and abundance of peace, till the moon be taken sway. ^71-7

**71:8** And he shall rule from sea to sea, and from the river unto the ends of the earth. ^71-8

**71:9** Before him the Ethiopians shall fall down: and his enemies shall lick the ground. ^71-9

**71:10** The kings of Tharsis and the islands shall offer presents: the kings of the Arabians and of Saba shall bring gifts: ^71-10

**71:11** And all kings of the earth shall adore him: all nations shall serve him. ^71-11

**71:12** For he shall deliver the poor from the mighty: and the needy that had no helper. ^71-12

**71:13** He shall spare the poor and needy: and he shall save the souls of the poor. ^71-13

**71:14** He shall redeem their souls from usuries and iniquity: and their names shall be honourable in his sight. ^71-14

**71:15** And he shall live, and to him shall be given of the gold of Arabia, for him they shall always adore: they shall bless him all the day. ^71-15

**71:16** And there shall be a firmament on the earth on the tops of mountains, above Libanus shall the fruit thereof be exalted: and they of the city shall flourish like the grass of the earth. ^71-16

**71:17** Let his name be blessed for evermore: his name continueth before the sun. And in him shall all the tribes of the earth be blessed: all nations shall magnify him. ^71-17

**71:18** Blessed be the Lord, the God of Israel, who alone doth wonderful things. ^71-18

**71:19** And blessed be the name of his majesty for ever: and the whole earth shall be filled with his majesty. So be it. So be it. ^71-19

**71:20** The praises of David, the son of Jesse, are ended. ^71-20
