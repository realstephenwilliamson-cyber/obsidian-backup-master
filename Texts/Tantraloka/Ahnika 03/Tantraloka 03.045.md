## Mūla

तदाधारोपरागेण भान्ति खड्गे मुखादिवत् ॥ ^mula

yathā ca gandharūpaspṛgrasādyāḥ pratibimbitāḥ |
tadādhāroparāgeṇa bhānti khaḍge mukhādivat || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. II
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 02 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v02 05 - Chapter Three, part 2#^pg135|p. 119]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 02.pdf#page=135>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 3.43cd–44ab.
>
> (When) smell, form, touch, taste and the rest are reflected (in their corresponding medium of reflection), they manifest coloured with (the attributes) of their supporting medium, as is a face, for example, (that is reflected) in a sword. In the same way, this universe, reflected within consciousness, assumes all its properties, starting with (its) luminosity and freedom. (with 3.45–46)
