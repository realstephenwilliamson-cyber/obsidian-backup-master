## Chapter 138

**138:1** Of David.  
I praise You with all my heart,  
sing a hymn to You before the divine beings; ^138-1

**138:2** I bow toward Your holy temple  
and praise Your name for Your steadfast love and faithfulness,  
because You have exalted [^1]Your name, Your word, above all. ^138-2

**138:3** When I called, You answered me,  
You inspired me with courage.[^2] ^138-3

**138:4** All the kings of the earth shall praise You, O LORD,  
for they have heard the words You spoke. ^138-4

**138:5** They shall sing of the ways of the LORD,  
“Great is the majesty of the LORD!” ^138-5

**138:6** High though the LORD is, He sees the lowly;  
lofty, He perceives from afar. ^138-6

**138:7** Though I walk among enemies,  
You preserve me in the face of my foes;  
You extend Your hand;  
with Your right hand You deliver me. ^138-7

**138:8** The LORD will settle accounts for me.  
O LORD, Your steadfast love is eternal;  
do not forsake the work of Your hands. ^138-8

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
