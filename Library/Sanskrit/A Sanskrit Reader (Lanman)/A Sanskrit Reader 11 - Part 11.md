= The prose of the Brāhmaṇas 18 not difficult. In reading it, the chief thing is some familiarity with the style, i.e. ability to divide up the discourse aright into the little clauses and choppy sentences with which it proceeds. 

As a help 

to this it is important to observe that the particle átha marks the beginning of a new clause, and that the postpositive vā&č marks the foregoing word as the first of its clause.
