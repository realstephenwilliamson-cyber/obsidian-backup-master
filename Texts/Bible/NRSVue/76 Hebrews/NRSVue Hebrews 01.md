# Hebrews 1 (NRSVue)

Compare: [[Hebrews 01|BSB and SBLGNT]] · [[DRB Hebrews 01|Douay-Rheims]] · [[JANT Hebrews 01|JANT notes]]

## God has spoken by his son

**1:1** Long ago God spoke to our ancestors in many and various ways by the prophets, ^1-1

**1:2** but in these last days he has spoken to us by a Son,^[Or *the Son*] whom he appointed heir of all things, through whom he also created the worlds. ^1-2

**1:3** He is the reflection of God’s glory and the exact imprint of God’s very being, and he sustains^[Or *bears along*] all things by his powerful word. When he had made purification for^[Other ancient authorities add *our*] sins, he sat down at the right hand of the Majesty on high, ^1-3

**1:4** having become as much superior to angels as the name he has inherited is more excellent than theirs. ^1-4

## The son is superior to angels

**1:5** For to which of the angels did God^[Greek *he*] ever say, “You are my Son; today I have begotten you”? Or again, “I will be his Father, and he will be my Son”? ^1-5

**1:6** And again, when he brings the firstborn into the world, he says, “Let all God’s angels worship him.” ^1-6

**1:7** Of the angels he says, “He makes his angels winds and his servants flames of fire.” ^1-7

**1:8** But of the Son he says, “Your throne, O God, is^[Or *God is your throne*] forever and ever, and the scepter of righteousness is the scepter of your^[Other ancient authorities read *his*] kingdom. ^1-8

**1:9** You have loved righteousness and hated lawlessness;^[Other ancient authorities read *wickedness*] therefore God, your God, has anointed you with the oil of gladness beyond your companions.” ^1-9

**1:10** And, “In the beginning, Lord, you founded the earth, and the heavens are the work of your hands; ^1-10

**1:11** they will perish, but you remain; they will all wear out like clothing; ^1-11

**1:12** like a cloak you will roll them up, and like clothing^[Other ancient authorities lack *like clothing*] they will be changed. But you are the same, and your years will never end.” ^1-12

**1:13** And to which of the angels has he ever said, “Sit at my right hand until I make your enemies a footstool for your feet”? ^1-13

**1:14** Are not all angels^[Gk *all of them*] spirits in the divine service, sent to serve for the sake of those who are to inherit salvation? ^1-14
