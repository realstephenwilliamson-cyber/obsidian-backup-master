## Mūla

आकस्मिकं व्रजेद्बोधं कल्पिताकल्पितो हि सः ।
तस्य योऽकल्पितो भागः स तु श्रेष्ठतमः स्मृतः ॥ ^mula

ākasmikaṃ vrajedbodhaṃ kalpitākalpito hi saḥ |
tasya yo'kalpito bhāgaḥ sa tu śreṣṭhatamaḥ smṛtaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. III
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 03 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v03 04 - Chapter Four, part 3#^pg102|p. 89]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 03.pdf#page=102>))
>
> (The teacher) who attains spontaneous (āākasmika) awakened consciousness (bodha) of any matter taught in the scriptures (by practicing) in conformity with (the reality and) procedure (they teach) (yathākramayogena) is one who is ‘created-cum-uncreated’. (with 4.72cd–73ab)
>
> (OFf these two), his ‘uncreated’ aspect is considered to be the best (of all). This is so because the excellence (utkarṣa) (of his realisation) is determined by the progressive heightening of the (‘uncreated’) aspect, which is Pure Knowledge. (with 4.73cd–74ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* ननु कल्पितस्य गुरोः क्वचिदंशे यद्यकल्पितत्त्वं भवेत् तावता किम् ? इत्याशङ्क्याह --

> तस्य योऽकल्पितो भागः स तु श्रेष्ठतमः स्मृतः ॥
> उत्कर्षः शुद्धविद्यांशतारतम्यकृतो यतः ।

श्रेष्ठतमत्वे शुद्धविद्यातारतम्यकृत उत्कर्षो हेतुः -- शुद्धविद्याया एव तरतमभावो हि अकल्पितत्त्वादौ निमित्तमिति भावः ॥ ७३ ॥
