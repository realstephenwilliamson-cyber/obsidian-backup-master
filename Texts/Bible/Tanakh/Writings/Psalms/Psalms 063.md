## Chapter 63

**63:1** A psalm of David, when he was in the Wilderness of Judah. ^63-1

**63:2** God, You are my God;  
I search for You,  
my soul thirsts for You,  
my body yearns for You,  
as a parched and thirsty land that has no water. ^63-2

**63:3** I shall behold You in the sanctuary,  
and see Your might and glory, ^63-3

**63:4** Truly Your faithfulness is better than life;  
my lips declare Your praise. ^63-4

**63:5** I bless You all my life;  
I lift up my hands, invoking Your name. ^63-5

**63:6** I am sated as with a rich feast,[^1]  
I sing praises with joyful lips ^63-6

**63:7** when I call You to mind upon my bed,  
when I think of You in the watches of the night; ^63-7

**63:8** for You are my help,  
and in the shadow of Your wings  
I shout for joy. ^63-8

**63:9** My soul is attached to You;  
Your right hand supports me. ^63-9

**63:10** May those who seek to destroy my life  
enter the depths of the earth. ^63-10

**63:11** May they be gutted by the sword;  
may they be prey to jackals. ^63-11

**63:12** But the king shall rejoice in God;  
all who swear by Him shall exult,  
when the mouth of liars is stopped. ^63-12

[^1]: Lit. “suet and fat.”
