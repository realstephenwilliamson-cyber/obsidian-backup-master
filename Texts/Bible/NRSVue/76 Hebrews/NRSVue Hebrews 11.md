# Hebrews 11 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Hebrews 10|← Hebrews 10]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Hebrews 12|Hebrews 12 →]]

Compare: [[Hebrews 11|BSB and SBLGNT]] · [[DRB Hebrews 11|Douay-Rheims]] · [[JANT Hebrews 11|JANT notes]]

## The meaning of faith

**11:1** Now faith is the assurance^[Or *reality*] of things hoped for, the conviction^[Or *evidence*] of things not seen. ^11-1

**11:2** Indeed, by faith^[Gk *by this*] our ancestors received approval. ^11-2

**11:3** By faith we understand that the worlds were prepared by the word of God, so that what is seen was made from things that are not visible.^[Or *was not made out of visible things*] ^11-3

## The examples of Abel, Enoch, and Noah

**11:4** By faith Abel offered to God a more acceptable^[Gk *greater*] sacrifice than Cain’s. Through this he received approval as righteous, God himself giving approval to his gifts; he died, but through his faith^[Gk *through it*] he still speaks. ^11-4

**11:5** By faith Enoch was taken so that he did not experience death, and “he was not found, because God had taken him.” For it was attested before he was taken away that “he had pleased God.” ^11-5

**11:6** And without faith it is impossible to please him, for whoever would approach God must believe that he exists and that he rewards those who seek him. ^11-6

**11:7** By faith Noah, warned by God about events as yet unseen, respected the warning and built an ark to save his household; by this he condemned the world and became an heir to the righteousness that is in accordance with faith. ^11-7

## The faith of Abraham

**11:8** By faith Abraham obeyed when he was called to set out for a place that he was to receive as an inheritance, and he set out, not knowing where he was going. ^11-8

**11:9** By faith he stayed for a time in the land he had been promised, as in a foreign land, living in tents, as did Isaac and Jacob, who were heirs with him of the same promise. ^11-9

**11:10** For he looked forward to the city that has foundations, whose architect and builder is God. ^11-10

**11:11** By faith, with Sarah’s involvement, he received power of procreation, even though he was too old, because he considered^[Other ancient authorities read *By faith Sarah herself received power to conceive, even when she was past the age, since she considered*] him faithful who had promised. ^11-11

**11:12** Therefore from one person, and this one as good as dead, descendants were born, “as many as the stars of heaven and as the innumerable grains of sand by the seashore.” ^11-12

**11:13** All of these died in faith without having received the promises, but from a distance they saw and greeted them. They confessed that they were strangers and foreigners on the earth, ^11-13

**11:14** for people who speak in this way make it clear that they are seeking a homeland. ^11-14

**11:15** If they had been thinking of the land that they had left behind, they would have had opportunity to return. ^11-15

**11:16** But as it is, they desire a better homeland, that is, a heavenly one. Therefore God is not ashamed to be called their God; indeed, he has prepared a city for them. ^11-16

**11:17** By faith Abraham, when put to the test, offered up Isaac. He who had received the promises was ready to offer up his only son, ^11-17

**11:18** of whom he had been told, “It is through Isaac that descendants shall be named for you.” ^11-18

**11:19** He considered the fact that God is able even to raise someone from the dead—and, figuratively speaking, he did receive him back. ^11-19

**11:20** By faith Isaac invoked blessings for the future on Jacob and Esau. ^11-20

**11:21** By faith Jacob, when dying, blessed each of the sons of Joseph, “bowing in worship over the top of his staff.” ^11-21

**11:22** By faith Joseph, at the end of his life, made mention of the exodus of the Israelites and gave instructions about his burial.^[Gk *his bones*] ^11-22

## The faith of Moses

**11:23** By faith Moses was hidden by his parents for three months after his birth, because they saw that the child was beautiful, and they were not afraid of the king’s edict.^[Other ancient authorities add *By faith Moses, when he was grown up, killed the Egyptian, because he observed the humiliation of his brothers and sisters*] ^11-23

**11:24** By faith Moses, when he was grown up, refused to be called a son of Pharaoh’s daughter, ^11-24

**11:25** choosing rather to share ill-treatment with the people of God than to enjoy the fleeting pleasures of sin. ^11-25

**11:26** He considered abuse suffered for the Christ^[Or *the Messiah*] to be greater wealth than the treasures of Egypt, for he was looking ahead to the reward. ^11-26

**11:27** By faith he left Egypt, unafraid of the king’s anger, for he persevered as though^[Or *because*] he saw him who is invisible. ^11-27

**11:28** By faith he kept the Passover and the sprinkling of blood, so that the destroyer of the firstborn would not touch the firstborn of Israel.^[Gk *would not touch them*] ^11-28

## The faith of other Israelite heroes

**11:29** By faith the people passed through the Red Sea as if it were dry land, but when the Egyptians attempted to do so they were drowned. ^11-29

**11:30** By faith the walls of Jericho fell after they had been encircled for seven days. ^11-30

**11:31** By faith Rahab the prostitute did not perish with those who were disobedient,^[Or *unbelieving*] because she had received the spies in peace. ^11-31

**11:32** And what more should I say? For time would fail me to tell of Gideon, Barak, Samson, Jephthah, of David and Samuel and the prophets, ^11-32

**11:33** who through faith conquered kingdoms, administered justice, obtained promises, shut the mouths of lions, ^11-33

**11:34** quenched the power of fire, escaped the edge of the sword, were made strong out of weakness, became mighty in war, put foreign armies to flight. ^11-34

**11:35** Women received their dead by resurrection. Others were tortured, refusing to accept release, in order to obtain a better resurrection. ^11-35

**11:36** Others suffered mocking and flogging and even chains and imprisonment. ^11-36

**11:37** They were stoned to death; they were sawn in two;^[Other ancient authorities add *they were tempted*] they were killed by the sword; they went about in skins of sheep and goats, destitute, persecuted, tormented— ^11-37

**11:38** of whom the world was not worthy. They wandered in deserts and mountains and in caves and holes in the ground. ^11-38

**11:39** Yet all these, though they were commended for their faith, did not receive what was promised, ^11-39

**11:40** since God had provided something better so that they would not, apart from us, be made perfect. ^11-40
