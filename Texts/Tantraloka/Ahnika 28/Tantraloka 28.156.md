## Mūla

द्वादशग्रन्थिशक्तीनां ब्रह्मवक्त्रार्चिषामपि ।
विद्यापीठे चले लिङ्गे स्थण्डिले च गुरोर्गणे ॥ ^mula

dvādaśagranthiśaktīnāṃ brahmavaktrārciṣāmapi |
vidyāpīṭhe cale liṅge sthaṇḍile ca gurorgaṇe || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 04 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 2#^pg93|p. 76]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=93>))
>
> (Draped) on the manifest (vyakta) (iconic image of the deity), (the thread should extend) up to the side of (its) knees, (whereas) on a liṅga it (should extend) up to the pedestal (pīṭha).). During (all) the rites of worship, it is beautiful (adorning the officiant’s) head by (his) conceiving it to be the three principles, (and possessing the beauty) of the energies of the twelve knots, and so too of the flames (arci) of the (five faces of) Brahmā (i.e. Sadāśiva) (and his limbs). (with 28.155cd–156ab)
>
> The Seat of Knowledge (where the scriptures are placed and worshipped), the moveable Liñṅga, the sacrificial surface, the teacher and his group, the bell, the large and small ladle (sruk and sruva), the disciples, the bearers of sectarian signs, the door and the lintel above it (toraṇa), one’s own body, and the sacred seat (of the pit of the sacrificial) fire - it is right (that all of these be invested with the sacred thread,) as is beautiful (and befitting). Of nine colours, it should also be placed on the terrace and in the house where the sacrifice is celebrated. (with 28.156cd–158ab)
