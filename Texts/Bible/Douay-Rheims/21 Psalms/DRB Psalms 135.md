# Psalms 135 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 134|← Psalms 134]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 136|Psalms 136 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 135|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**135:1** Praise the Lord, for he is good: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^135-1

**135:2** Praise ye the God of gods: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^135-2

**135:3** Praise ye the Lord of lords: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^135-3

**135:4** Who alone doth great wonders: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^135-4

**135:5** Who made the heavens in understanding: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^135-5

**135:6** Who established the earth above the waters: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^135-6

**135:7** Who made the great lights: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^135-7

**135:8** The sun to rule over the day: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^135-8

**135:9** The moon and the stars to rule the night: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^135-9

**135:10** Who smote Egypt with their firstborn: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^135-10

**135:11** Who brought Israel from among them: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^135-11

**135:12** With a mighty hand and a stretched out arm: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^135-12

**135:13** Who divided the Red Sea into parts: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^135-13

**135:14** And brought out Israel through the midst thereof: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^135-14

**135:15** And overthrew Pharao and his host in the Red Sea: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^135-15

**135:16** Who led his people through the desert: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^135-16

**135:17** Who smote great kings: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^135-17

**135:18** And slew strong kings: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^135-18

**135:19** Sehon king of the Amorrhites: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^135-19

**135:20** And Og king of Basan: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^135-20

**135:21** And he gave their land for an inheritance: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^135-21

**135:22** For an inheritance to his servant Israel: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^135-22

**135:23** For he was mindful of us in our affliction: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^135-23

**135:24** And he redeemed us from our enemies: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^135-24

**135:25** Who giveth food to all flesh: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^135-25

**135:26** Give glory to the God of heaven: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^135-26

**135:27** Give glory to the Lord of lords: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^135-27
