# Psalms 74 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 73|← Psalms 73]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 75|Psalms 75 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 74|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**74:1** Unto the end, corrupt not, a psalm of a canticle for Asaph. ^74-1

**74:2** We will praise thee, O God: we will praise, and we will call upon thy name. We will relate thy wondrous works: ^74-2

**74:3** When I shall take a time, I will judge justices. ^74-3

**74:4** The earth is melted, and all that dwell therein: I have established the pillars thereof. ^74-4

**74:5** I said to the wicked: Do not act wickedly: and to the sinners: Lift not up the horn. ^74-5

**74:6** Lift not up your horn on high: speak not iniquity against God. ^74-6

**74:7** For neither from the east, nor from the west, nor from the desert hills: ^74-7

**74:8** For God is the judge. One he putteth down, and another he lifteth up: ^74-8

**74:9** For in the hand of the Lord there is a cup of strong wine full of mixture. And he hath poured it out from this to that: but the dregs thereof are not emptied: all the sinners of the earth shall drink. ^74-9

**74:10** But I will declare for ever: I will sing to the God of Jacob. ^74-10

**74:11** And I will break all the horns of sinners: but the horns of the just shall be exalted. ^74-11
