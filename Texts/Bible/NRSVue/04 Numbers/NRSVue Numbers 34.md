# Numbers 34 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Numbers 33|← Numbers 33]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Numbers 35|Numbers 35 →]]

## The boundaries of the land

**34:1** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, ^34-1

**34:2** “Command the Israelites, and say to them: When you enter the land of Canaan (this is the land that shall fall to you for an inheritance, the land of Canaan, defined by its boundaries), ^34-2

**34:3** your south sector shall extend from the wilderness of Zin along the side of Edom. Your southern boundary shall begin from the end of the Dead Sea^[Heb *Salt Sea*] on the east; ^34-3

**34:4** your boundary shall turn south of the ascent of Akrabbim and cross to Zin, and its outer limit shall be south of Kadesh-barnea; then it shall go on to Hazar-addar and cross to Azmon; ^34-4

**34:5** the boundary shall turn from Azmon to the Wadi of Egypt, and its termination shall be at the Sea. ^34-5

**34:6** “For the western boundary, you shall have the Great Sea and its^[Syr: Heb lacks *its*] coast; this shall be your western boundary. ^34-6

**34:7** “This shall be your northern boundary: from the Great Sea you shall mark out your line to Mount Hor; ^34-7

**34:8** from Mount Hor you shall mark it out to Lebo-hamath, and the outer limit of the boundary shall be at Zedad; ^34-8

**34:9** then the boundary shall extend to Ziphron, and its end shall be at Hazar-enan; this shall be your northern boundary. ^34-9

**34:10** “You shall mark out your eastern boundary from Hazar-enan to Shepham, ^34-10

**34:11** and the boundary shall continue down from Shepham to Riblah on the east side of Ain, and the boundary shall go down and reach the eastern slope of the sea of Chinnereth, ^34-11

**34:12** and the boundary shall go down to the Jordan, and its end shall be at the Dead Sea.^[Heb *Salt Sea*] This shall be your land with its boundaries all around.” ^34-12

**34:13** Moses commanded the Israelites, saying, “This is the land that you shall apportion by lot, which the LORD has commanded to give to the nine tribes and to the half-tribe, ^34-13

**34:14** for the tribe of the Reubenites by their ancestral houses and the tribe of the Gadites by their ancestral houses have taken their inheritance and also the half-tribe of Manasseh; ^34-14

**34:15** the two tribes and the half-tribe have taken their inheritance beyond the Jordan at Jericho eastward, toward the sunrise.” ^34-15

## Tribal leaders

**34:16** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, ^34-16

**34:17** “These are the names of the men who shall apportion the land to you for inheritance: the priest Eleazar and Joshua son of Nun. ^34-17

**34:18** You shall take one leader of every tribe to apportion the land for inheritance. ^34-18

**34:19** These are the names of the men: Of the tribe of Judah, Caleb son of Jephunneh. ^34-19

**34:20** Of the tribe of the Simeonites, Shemuel son of Ammihud. ^34-20

**34:21** Of the tribe of Benjamin, Elidad son of Chislon. ^34-21

**34:22** Of the tribe of the Danites a leader, Bukki son of Jogli. ^34-22

**34:23** Of the Josephites: of the tribe of the Manassites a leader, Hanniel son of Ephod, ^34-23

**34:24** and of the tribe of the Ephraimites a leader, Kemuel son of Shiphtan. ^34-24

**34:25** Of the tribe of the Zebulunites a leader, Eli-zaphan son of Parnach. ^34-25

**34:26** Of the tribe of the Issacharites a leader, Paltiel son of Azzan. ^34-26

**34:27** And of the tribe of the Asherites a leader, Ahihud son of Shelomi. ^34-27

**34:28** Of the tribe of the Naphtalites a leader, Pedahel son of Ammihud. ^34-28

**34:29** These were the ones whom the LORD commanded to apportion the inheritance for the Israelites in the land of Canaan.” ^34-29
