## Mūla

एवं कलाः पञ्चदश क्षीयन्ते शशिनः क्रमात् ।
आप्यायिन्यमृताब्रूपतादात्म्यात्षोडशी न तु ॥ ^mula

evaṃ kalāḥ pañcadaśa kṣīyante śaśinaḥ kramāt |
āpyāyinyamṛtābrūpatādātmyātṣoḍaśī na tu || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 10 - Chapter Six, part 3#^pg338|p. 323]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=338>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 6.97cd–98ab.
>
> The fifteen digits of the Moon decrease in this way successively (one after the other). But this is not the case with the sixteenth (digit), which nourishes (the universe) because it is one with nectar which is the water (of the inner divine libation).
