## Mūla

अन्यदाश्यानितमपि तदैव द्रावयेदियम् ।
प्रायश्चित्तादिकर्मभ्यो ब्रह्महत्यादिकर्मवत् ॥ ^mula

anyadāśyānitamapi tadaiva drāvayediyam |
prāyaścittādikarmabhyo brahmahatyādikarmavat || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. III
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 03 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v03 07 - Chapter Four, part 6#^pg240|p. 227]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 03.pdf#page=240>))

> Although other things (apart from doubt may) have also condensed (into thought constructs), they are dissolved away by that same (consciousness) the very moment (tadaiva) (they arise), just as, for example, (the great sin of) Brahminicide (is remedied) by the performance of the appropriate rites of expiation (prāyaścitta).
