## Mūla

पुरषोडशकम्ज्ञेयम्षड्विधोऽध्वा प्रकीर्तितः  ।
आप्यायिन्या द्वितीयम्च तत्र तत्त्वानि लक्षयेत् ॥ ^mula

puraṣoḍaśakam jñeyam ṣaḍvidho 'dhvā prakīrtitaḥ[^70] |
āpyāyinyā dvitīyam ca tatra tattvāni lakṣayet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, October 2022 – December 2022
> p. 25 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=32>))
>
> A) The first (Egg) is pervaded by (the power called) the Sustaining One (dhārikā). It is said that there is one principle there (i.e. Earth), and that one should recollect that the letter KṢ is present within Pada, Phoneme and Mantra, one for each one.
>
> One should know that there are sixteen worlds (there), beginning with (that of) Kālāgnirudra up to (that of) the excellent (world of) Vīrabhadra. (Such is) said to be the Path of six kinds (with regards to the first Egg). (with 2.50cd–52ab)

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, October 2022 – December 2022
> p. 26 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=33>))
>
> B) The second (Egg) is (pervaded by) the Nourishing One (āpyāyinī). One should perceive twenty-three principles there, beginning with Water (up to Nature), and as many phonemes beginning with H (and ending with Ṭ).[^71] The Padas are five as are the Mantras and the worlds fifty-six.[^72] (with 2.51cd–53)

[^70]: Abhinavagupta refers to the primary parameters of MV 2/50cd-57 concisely in TĀ 11/33-34ab, and makes an abridged paraphrases of the same in 11/51cd-54ab. 11/51cd-54ab (51-53) are a summary of MV 2/50cd-57. MV 2/50 is quoted and commented in the PTv Singh p. 102 ff.
[^71]: Read *tadvaddhādyakṣarāṇi* for *tadvaṭṭādyakṣarāṇi.*
[^72]: Two Mantras and two *Padas* consist of four syllables (*hasaṣaśa, valaraya*), three Mantras and three Padas are made of three classes of phonemes, that is, labials, dentals and cerebrals. Concerning the worlds, see below, chapter five.
