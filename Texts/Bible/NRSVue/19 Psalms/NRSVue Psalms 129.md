## Prayer for the downfall of Israel’s enemies

*A Song of Ascents.*

**129:1** Often have they attacked me from my youth—let Israel now say— ^129-1

**129:2** often have they attacked me from my youth, yet they have not prevailed against me. ^129-2

**129:3** The plowers plowed on my back; they made their furrows long. ^129-3

**129:4** The LORD is righteous; he has cut the cords of the wicked. ^129-4

**129:5** May all who hate Zion be put to shame and turned backward. ^129-5

**129:6** Let them be like the grass on the housetops that withers before it grows up, ^129-6

**129:7** with which reapers do not fill their hands or binders of sheaves their arms, ^129-7

**129:8** while those who pass by do not say, “The blessing of the LORD be upon you! We bless you in the name of the LORD!” ^129-8
