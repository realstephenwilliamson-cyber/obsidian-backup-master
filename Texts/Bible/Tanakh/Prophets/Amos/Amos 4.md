## Chapter 4

**4:1** Hear this word, you cows of Bashan  
On the hill of Samaria—  
Who defraud the poor,  
Who rob the needy;  
Who say to your[^1] husbands,  
“Bring, and let’s carouse!” ^4-1

**4:2** My Lord GOD swears by His holiness:  
Behold, days are coming upon you  
[^2]When you will be carried off in baskets,  
And, to the last one, in fish baskets, ^4-2

**4:3** And taken out [of the city]—  
Each one through a breach straight ahead—  
And flung on the refuse heap  
—declares the LORD. ^4-3

**4:4** Come to Bethel and transgress;  
To Gilgal, and transgress even more:  
Present your sacrifices the next morning  
And your tithes on the third day; ^4-4

**4:5** And burn a thank offering of leavened bread;[^3]  
And proclaim freewill offerings loudly.  
For you love that sort of thing, O Israelites  
—declares my Lord GOD. ^4-5

**4:6** I, on My part, have given you  
Cleanness of teeth in all your towns,  
And lack of food in all your settlements.  
Yet you did not turn back to Me  
—declares the LORD. ^4-6

**4:7** I therefore withheld the rain from you  
Three months before harvesttime:  
I would make it rain on one town  
And not on another;  
One field would be rained upon  
While another on which it did not rain  
Would wither. ^4-7

**4:8** So two or three towns would wander  
To a single town to drink water,  
But their thirst would not be slaked.  
Yet you did not turn back to Me  
—declares the LORD. ^4-8

**4:9** I scourged you with blight and mildew;  
Repeatedly[^4] your gardens and vineyards,  
Your fig trees and olive trees  
Were devoured by locusts.  
Yet you did not turn back to Me  
—declares the LORD. ^4-9

**4:10** I sent against you pestilence  
In the manner of Egypt;[^5]  
I slew your young men with the sword,  
Together with your captured horses,  
And I made the stench of your armies  
Rise in your very nostrils.  
Yet you did not turn back to Me  
—declares the LORD. ^4-10

**4:11** I have wrought destruction among you  
As when God destroyed Sodom and Gomorrah;  
You have become like a brand plucked from burning.  
Yet you have not turned back to Me  
—declares the LORD. ^4-11

**4:12** Assuredly,  
Because I am doing that to you,[^6]  
Even so will I act toward you, O Israel—  
Prepare to meet your God, O Israel! ^4-12

**4:13** Behold,  
He who formed the mountains,  
And created the wind,  
And has told man what His wish[^7] is,  
Who turns blackness[^8] into daybreak,  
And treads upon the high places of the earth—  
His name is the LORD, the God of Hosts. ^4-13

[^1]: Heb. “their.”
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Cf. Lev. 7.12–14; where, however, the bread is not to be burned.
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^5]: Alluding to the plagues at the time of the Exodus.
[^6]: Emendation yields “Because you are acting thus toward Me.”
[^7]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^8]: Cf. Joel 2.2. Emendation yields “darkness”; cf. 5.8.
