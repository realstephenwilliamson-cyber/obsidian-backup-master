## Chapter 3

**3:1** After that,  
I will pour out My spirit on all flesh;  
Your sons and daughters shall prophesy;  
Your old men shall dream dreams,  
And your young men shall see visions. ^3-1

**3:2** I will even pour out My spirit  
Upon male and female slaves in those days. ^3-2

**3:3** Before the great and terrible day of the LORD comes,[^1]  
I will set portents in the sky and on earth:  
Blood and fire and pillars of smoke; ^3-3

**3:4** The sun shall turn into darkness  
And the moon into blood. ^3-4

**3:5** But everyone who invokes the name of the LORD shall escape; for there shall be a remnant on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem, as the LORD promised. Anyone who invokes the LORD will be among the survivors.[^2] ^3-5

[^1]: Brought up from v. 4 for clarity.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
