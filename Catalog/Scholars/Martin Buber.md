# Martin Buber

A philosopher of dialogue who taught Jewish philosophy and ethics, and later religion, at the University of Frankfurt (1923–1933) and from 1938 held a post in sociology and social philosophy at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem ([Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/ENTRIES/buber/)).
