# Psalm 145 (NRSVue)

## The greatness and the goodness of God

*Praise. Of David.*

**145:1** I will extol you, my God and King, and bless your name forever and ever. ^145-1

**145:2** Every day I will bless you and praise your name forever and ever. ^145-2

**145:3** Great is the LORD and greatly to be praised; his greatness is unsearchable. ^145-3

**145:4** One generation shall extol your works to another and shall declare your mighty acts. ^145-4

**145:5** They will recount the glorious^[Q ms Gk Vg: MT *On the glorious*] splendor of your majesty, and on your wondrous works I will meditate. ^145-5

**145:6** They will proclaim the might of your awesome deeds, and I will declare your greatness. ^145-6

**145:7** They shall celebrate the fame of your abundant goodness and shall sing aloud of your righteousness. ^145-7

**145:8** The LORD is gracious and merciful, slow to anger and abounding in steadfast love. ^145-8

**145:9** The LORD is good to all, and his compassion is over all that he has made. ^145-9

**145:10** All your works shall give thanks to you, O LORD, and all your faithful shall bless you. ^145-10

**145:11** They shall speak of the glory of your kingdom and tell of your power, ^145-11

**145:12** to make known to all people your^[Gk Jerome Syr: Heb *his*] mighty deeds and the glorious splendor of your^[Heb *his*] kingdom. ^145-12

**145:13** Your kingdom is an everlasting kingdom, and your dominion endures throughout all generations. The LORD is faithful in all his words and gracious in all his deeds.^[Q ms Gk Syr: MT lacks *TheLORD. . . his deeds*] ^145-13

**145:14** The LORD upholds all who are falling and raises up all who are bowed down. ^145-14

**145:15** The eyes of all look to you, and you give them their food in due season. ^145-15

**145:16** You open your hand, satisfying the desire of every living thing. ^145-16

**145:17** The LORD is just in all his ways and kind in all his doings. ^145-17

**145:18** The LORD is near to all who call on him, to all who call on him in truth. ^145-18

**145:19** He fulfills the desire of all who fear him; he also hears their cry and saves them. ^145-19

**145:20** The LORD watches over all who love him, but all the wicked he will destroy. ^145-20

**145:21** My mouth will speak the praise of the LORD, and all flesh will bless his holy name forever and ever. ^145-21
