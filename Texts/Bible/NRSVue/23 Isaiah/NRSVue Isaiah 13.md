# Isaiah 13 (NRSVue)

## Proclamation against Babylon

**13:1** The oracle concerning Babylon that Isaiah son of Amoz saw. ^13-1

**13:2** On a bare hill raise a signal; cry aloud to them; wave the hand for them to enter the gates of the nobles. ^13-2

**13:3** I myself have commanded my consecrated ones, have summoned my warriors, my proudly exulting ones, to execute my anger.^[Gk: Heb *for my anger*] ^13-3

**13:4** Listen, a tumult on the mountains as of a great multitude! Listen, an uproar of kingdoms, of nations gathering together! The LORD of hosts is mustering an army for battle. ^13-4

**13:5** They come from a distant land, from the end of the heavens, the LORD and the weapons of his indignation, to destroy the whole earth. ^13-5

**13:6** Wail, for the day of the LORD is near; it will come like destruction from the Almighty!^[Traditional rendering of Heb *Shaddai*] ^13-6

**13:7** Therefore all hands will be feeble, and every human heart will melt, ^13-7

**13:8** and they will be terrified. Pangs and agony will seize them; they will be in anguish like a woman in labor. They will look aghast at one another; their faces will be aflame. ^13-8

**13:9** See, the day of the LORD is coming, cruel, with wrath and fierce anger, to make the earth a desolation and to destroy its sinners from it. ^13-9

**13:10** For the stars of the heavens and their constellations will not give their light; the sun will be dark at its rising, and the moon will not shed its light. ^13-10

**13:11** I will punish the world for its evil and the wicked for their iniquity; I will put an end to the pride of the arrogant and lay low the insolence of tyrants. ^13-11

**13:12** I will make mortals more rare than fine gold and humans than the gold of Ophir. ^13-12

**13:13** Therefore I will make the heavens tremble, and the earth will be shaken out of its place at the wrath of the LORD of hosts in the day of his fierce anger. ^13-13

**13:14** Like a gazelle on the run or like sheep with no one to gather them, all will turn back to their own people, and all will flee to their own lands. ^13-14

**13:15** Whoever is found will be thrust through, and whoever is caught will fall by the sword. ^13-15

**13:16** Their infants will be dashed to pieces before their eyes; their houses will be plundered and their wives raped. ^13-16

**13:17** See, I am stirring up the Medes against them, who have no regard for silver and do not delight in gold. ^13-17

**13:18** Their bows will slaughter the young men; they will have no mercy on the fruit of the womb; their eyes will not pity children. ^13-18

**13:19** And Babylon, the glory of kingdoms, the splendor and pride of the Chaldeans, will be like Sodom and Gomorrah when God overthrew them. ^13-19

**13:20** It will never be inhabited or lived in for all generations; Arabs will not pitch their tents there; shepherds will not make their flocks lie down there. ^13-20

**13:21** But wild animals will lie down there, and its houses will be full of howling creatures; there ostriches will live, and there goat-demons will dance. ^13-21

**13:22** Hyenas will cry in its towers and jackals in the pleasant palaces; its time is close at hand; and its days will not be prolonged. ^13-22
