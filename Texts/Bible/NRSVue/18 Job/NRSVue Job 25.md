## Bildad speaks: How can a mortal be righteous

## Before God?

**25:1** Then Bildad the Shuhite answered: ^25-1

**25:2** “Dominion and fear are with God;^[Heb *him*] he makes peace in his high heaven. ^25-2

**25:3** Is there any number to his armies? Upon whom does his light not arise? ^25-3

**25:4** How then can a mortal be righteous before God? How can one born of woman be pure? ^25-4

**25:5** If even the moon is not bright and the stars are not pure in his sight, ^25-5

**25:6** how much less a mortal, who is a maggot, and a human being, who is a worm!” ^25-6
