# Job 22 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Job 21|← Job 21]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Job 23|Job 23 →]]

## Eliphaz speaks: Job’s wickedness is great

**22:1** Then Eliphaz the Temanite answered: ^22-1

**22:2** “Can a mortal be of use to God? Can even the wisest be of service to him? ^22-2

**22:3** Is it any pleasure to the Almighty^[Traditional rendering of Heb *Shaddai*] if you are righteous, or is it gain to him if you make your ways blameless? ^22-3

**22:4** Is it for your piety that he reproves you and enters into judgment with you? ^22-4

**22:5** Is not your wickedness great? There is no end to your iniquities. ^22-5

**22:6** For you have exacted pledges from your family for no reason and stripped the naked of their clothing. ^22-6

**22:7** You have given no water to the weary to drink, and you have withheld bread from the hungry. ^22-7

**22:8** The powerful possess the land, and the favored live in it. ^22-8

**22:9** You have sent widows away empty-handed, and the arms of the orphans you have crushed.^[Gk Syr Tg Vg: Heb *were crushed*] ^22-9

**22:10** Therefore snares are around you, and sudden terror overwhelms you, ^22-10

**22:11** or darkness so that you cannot see; a flood of water covers you. ^22-11

**22:12** “Is not God high in the heavens? See the highest stars, how lofty they are! ^22-12

**22:13** Therefore you say, ‘What does God know? Can he judge through the deep darkness? ^22-13

**22:14** Thick clouds enwrap him, so that he does not see, and he walks on the dome of heaven.’ ^22-14

**22:15** Will you keep to the old way that the wicked have trod? ^22-15

**22:16** They were snatched away before their time; their foundation was washed away by a flood. ^22-16

**22:17** They said to God, ‘Leave us alone,’ and ‘What can the Almighty^[Traditional rendering of Heb *Shaddai*] do to us?’^[Gk Syr: Heb *them*] ^22-17

**22:18** Yet he filled their houses with good things—but the plans of the wicked are repugnant to me. ^22-18

**22:19** The righteous see it and are glad; the innocent laugh them to scorn, ^22-19

**22:20** saying, ‘Surely our adversaries are cut off, and what they left, the fire has consumed.’ ^22-20

**22:21** “Agree with God,^[Heb *him*] and be at peace; in this way good will come to you. ^22-21

**22:22** Receive instruction from his mouth, and lay up his words in your heart. ^22-22

**22:23** If you return to the Almighty,^[Traditional rendering of Heb *Shaddai*] you will be restored, if you remove unrighteousness from your tents, ^22-23

**22:24** if you treat gold like dust and gold of Ophir like the stones of the torrent bed, ^22-24

**22:25** and if the Almighty^[Traditional rendering of Heb *Shaddai*] is your gold and your precious silver, ^22-25

**22:26** then you will delight yourself in the Almighty^[Traditional rendering of Heb *Shaddai*] and lift up your face to God. ^22-26

**22:27** You will pray to him, and he will hear you, and you will pay your vows. ^22-27

**22:28** You will decide on a matter, and it will be established for you, and light will shine on your ways. ^22-28

**22:29** When others are humiliated, you say it is pride, for he saves the humble. ^22-29

**22:30** He will deliver even those who are guilty; they will escape because of the cleanness of your hands.”^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^22-30
