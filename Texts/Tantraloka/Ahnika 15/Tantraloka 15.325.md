## Mūla

परापरां रक्तवर्णां किञ्चिदुग्रां न भीषणाम् ।
अपरां वामशृङ्गे तु भीषणां कृष्णपिङ्गलाम् ॥ ^mula

parāparāṃ raktavarṇāṃ kiñcidugrāṃ na bhīṣaṇām |
aparāṃ vāmaśṛṅge tu bhīṣaṇāṃ kṛṣṇapiṅgalām || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 06 - Chapter Fifteen, part 5#^pg213|p. 195]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=213>))
>
> (One should mentally install and worship) Bhairavasadbhāva (JHKṢHÜM) on (the lotus of the prong) in the middle, Ratiśekhara (RYLVŪM) on the (lotus to his) right, and Navātman (RHRKṢMLVYŪM) on the (lotus to his) left. These three Bhairavas are accompanied by the (three) goddesses. (The goddess) Parā, (white) like the full moon, is (on the lotus) in the middle. Again, on (her) right is Parāparā, who is red and somewhat fierce, (but) not terrifying (bhīṣaṇā). Aparā is tawny-black (krṣṇapiṁṅgalā) and, terrifying, (is worshipped) on the left prong. (with 15.323cd–325)
