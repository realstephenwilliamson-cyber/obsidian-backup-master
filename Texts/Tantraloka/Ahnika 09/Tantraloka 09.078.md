## Mūla

मूर्तानां प्रतिघस्तेजोऽणूनां नामूर्त ईदृशम् ।
न च चेतनमात्मानमस्वतन्त्रो मलः क्षमः ॥ ^mula

mūrtānāṃ pratighastejo'ṇūnāṃ nāmūrta īdṛśam |
na ca cetanamātmānamasvatantro malaḥ kṣamaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 04 - Chapter Nine, part 2#^pg97|p. 80]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=97>))
>
> Again, (someone may point out that) light is covered by darkness (which is formless). (But we say) that this (obscuration) is brought about by the opposition between the atoms of fire, which have a form, and other atoms, which are contrary to them, as opposites. (Obscuration understood) in this way is not formless. (with 9.77cd–78ab)
>
> Impurity (is insentient); it is not free (that is, sentient), and (so) cannot obscure one’s own conscious nature (which is inherently free and sentient). (with 9.78cd–79a)
