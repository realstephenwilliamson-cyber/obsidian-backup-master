# LXX Psalms 136

← [[LXX Psalms 135]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 137]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 137]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 137]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**136:1** For David, *a Psalm* of Jeremias. By the rivers of Babylon, there we sat; and wept when we remembered Sion.[^1]  ^136-1

**136:2** We hung our harps on the willows in the midst of it.[^2]  ^136-2

**136:3** For there they that had taken us captive asked of us the words of a song; and they that had carried us away *asked* a hymn, *saying*, Sing us *one* of the songs of Sion.  ^136-3

**136:4** How should we sing the Lord's song in a strange land?[^3]  ^136-4

**136:5** If I forget thee, O Jerusalem, let my right hand forget *its skill*.  ^136-5

**136:6** May my tongue cleave to my throat, if I do not remember thee; if I do not prefer Jerusalem as the chief of my joy.  ^136-6

**136:7** Remember, O Lord, the children of Edom in the day of Jerusalem; who said, Rase *it*, rase *it*, even to its foundations.[^4]  ^136-7

**136:8** Wretched daughter of Babylon! blessed *shall he be* who shall reward thee as thou hast rewarded us.  ^136-8

**136:9** Blessed *shall he be* who shall seize and dash thine infants against the rock.  ^136-9

[^1]: 136:1 *Alex.* omits.
[^2]: 136:2 *Gr.* organs, *or,* instruments.
[^3]: 136:4 *Gr.* on.
[^4]: 136:7 *Gr.* empty, empty.
