# Proverbs 06

## Chapter 6

**6:1** My son, if you have stood surety for your fellow,  
Given your hand for another,[^1] ^6-1

**6:2** You have been trapped by the words of your mouth,  
Snared by the words of your mouth. ^6-2

**6:3** Do this, then, my son, to extricate yourself,  
For you have come into the power of your fellow:  
Go grovel—and badger your fellow; ^6-3

**6:4** Give your eyes no sleep,  
Your pupils no slumber. ^6-4

**6:5** Save yourself like a deer out of the hand [of a hunter],  
Like a bird out of the hand of a fowler. ^6-5

**6:6** Lazybones, go to the ant;  
Study its ways and learn. ^6-6

**6:7** Without leaders, officers, or rulers, ^6-7

**6:8** It lays up its stores during the summer,  
Gathers in its food at the harvest. ^6-8

**6:9** How long will you lie there, lazybones;  
When will you wake from your sleep? ^6-9

**6:10** A bit more sleep, a bit more slumber,  
A bit more hugging yourself in bed, ^6-10

**6:11** And poverty will come calling upon you,[^2]  
And want, like a man with a shield. ^6-11

**6:12** A scoundrel, an evil man  
Lives by crooked speech, ^6-12

**6:13** Winking his eyes,  
Shuffling his feet,  
Pointing his finger. ^6-13

**6:14** Duplicity is in his heart;  
He plots evil all the time;  
He incites quarrels. ^6-14

**6:15** Therefore calamity will come upon him without warning;  
Suddenly he will be broken beyond repair. ^6-15

**6:16** Six things the LORD hates;  
Seven are an abomination to Him: ^6-16

**6:17** A haughty bearing,  
A lying tongue,  
Hands that shed innocent blood, ^6-17

**6:18** A mind that hatches evil plots,  
Feet quick to run to evil, ^6-18

**6:19** A false witness testifying lies,  
And one who incites brothers to quarrel. ^6-19

**6:20** My son, keep your father’s commandment;  
Do not forsake your mother’s teaching. ^6-20

**6:21** Tie them over your heart always;  
Bind them around your throat. ^6-21

**6:22** When you walk it will lead you;  
When you lie down it will watch over you;  
And when you are awake it will talk with you. ^6-22

**6:23** For the commandment is a lamp,  
The teaching is a light,  
And the way to life is the rebuke that disciplines. ^6-23

**6:24** It will keep you from an evil woman,  
From the smooth tongue of a forbidden[^3] woman. ^6-24

**6:25** Do not lust for her beauty  
Or let her captivate you with her eyes. ^6-25

**6:26** The last loaf of bread will go for a harlot;  
A married woman will snare a person of honor. ^6-26

**6:27** Can a man rake embers into his bosom  
Without burning his clothes? ^6-27

**6:28** Can a man walk on live coals  
Without scorching his feet? ^6-28

**6:29** It is the same with one who sleeps with his fellow’s wife;  
None who touches her will go unpunished. ^6-29

**6:30** A thief is not held in contempt  
For stealing to appease his hunger; ^6-30

**6:31** Yet if caught he must pay sevenfold;  
He must give up all he owns. ^6-31

**6:32** He who commits adultery is devoid of sense;  
Only one who would destroy himself does such a thing. ^6-32

**6:33** He will meet with disease and disgrace;  
His reproach will never be expunged. ^6-33

**6:34** The fury of the husband will be passionate;  
He will show no pity on his day of vengeance. ^6-34

**6:35** He will not have regard for any ransom;  
He will refuse your bribe, however great. ^6-35

[^1]: Or “a stranger.”
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Lit. “alien.”
