## Mūla

अवेद्यो वेद्यतारूपाद्धर्माद्वेद्यत्वमागतः ।
वेद्यता भासमाना च स्वयं नीलादिधर्मवत् ॥ ^mula

avedyo vedyatārūpāddharmādvedyatvamāgataḥ |
vedyatā bhāsamānā ca svayaṃ nīlādidharmavat || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 11 - Chapter Ten, part 2#^pg360|p. 341]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=360>))
>
> (An opponent may object at this point that,) what you are saying amounts to this, namely, (that perceptibility) acts as does Being (sattā) that, when it is present, makes what does not exist existent. So too, inherence conjoined (to something) conjoins things that are not conjoined. The last (particular) quality (viśeṣa) (of an entity) is separation, (that makes things) that are not separate, separate. Brilliant white (makes) what is not white, white. In the same way, movement (makes something move). Similarly, the bearer of attributes (dharmin) endowed with all its qualities, such as blue and the like, (although) in itself imperceptible (avedya), becomes, by virtue of the quality of perceptibility, perceptible. (with 10.72cd–75ab)
>
> (This objection does not affect us at all. The truth is) that perceptibility (vedyatā), like other qualities, such as blue and the like, is unmanifest (aprakāśa) in itself (svayam), (but) manifests (‘shines’) by virtue of that quality of self-luminosity (that is the inherent nature of perceptibility, which is not perceptible in the manner of an object). (with 10.75cd–76ab)

*Lead-in:* ननु यथा (सत्ता)स्वरूपेण सती तथा वेद्यतापि स्वरूपेणैव वेद्येत्युक्ते स्वरूपस्यैव वेद्यत्वं वेदकत्वं च द्वितयं प्राप्नुयात्, तच्च न युक्तं, विरोधात् ? -- इत्याशङ्क्य स्वयंवेद्यतां स्वप्रकाशतायामेव विश्रमयति -

> वेद्यता भासमाना च स्वयं नीलादिधर्मवत् ॥
> अप्रकाशा स्वप्रकाशाद्धर्मादेति प्रकाशताम् ।

वेद्यता च यदा स्वयमवभासते तदासौ नीलादिवज्जडोचितेन रूपेणाप्रकाशमानापि स्वप्रकाशताख्यात्स्वरूपादेव प्रकाशतामेति स्वयमेव प्रकाशते, न तु स्वापेक्षया कर्मभावं लभते -- इत्यर्थः ॥ ७५ ॥
