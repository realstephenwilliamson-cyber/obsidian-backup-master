## Chapter 38

**38:1** A psalm of David. *Lehazkir*.[^1] ^38-1

**38:2** O LORD, do not punish me in wrath;  
do not chastise me in fury. ^38-2

**38:3** For Your arrows have struck me;  
Your blows have fallen upon me. ^38-3

**38:4** There is no soundness in my flesh because of Your rage,  
no wholeness in my bones because of my sin. ^38-4

**38:5** For my iniquities have overwhelmed me;[^2]  
they are like a heavy burden, more than I can bear. ^38-5

**38:6** My wounds stink and fester  
because of my folly. ^38-6

**38:7** I am all bent and bowed;  
I walk about in gloom all day long. ^38-7

**38:8** For my sinews are full of fever;  
there is no soundness in my flesh. ^38-8

**38:9** I am all benumbed and crushed;  
I roar because of the turmoil in my mind. ^38-9

**38:10** O Lord, You are aware of all my entreaties;  
my groaning is not hidden from You. ^38-10

**38:11** My mind reels;  
my strength fails me;  
my eyes too have lost their luster. ^38-11

**38:12** My friends and companions stand back from my affliction;  
my kinsmen stand far off. ^38-12

**38:13** Those who seek my life lay traps;  
those who wish me harm speak malice;  
they utter deceit all the time. ^38-13

**38:14** But I am like a deaf man, unhearing,  
like a dumb man who cannot speak up; ^38-14

**38:15** I am like one who does not hear,  
who has no retort on his lips. ^38-15

**38:16** But I wait for You, O LORD;  
You will answer, O Lord, my God. ^38-16

**38:17** For I fear they will rejoice over me;  
when my foot gives way they will vaunt themselves against me. ^38-17

**38:18** For I am on the verge of collapse;  
my pain is always with me. ^38-18

**38:19** I acknowledge my iniquity;  
I am fearful over my sin; ^38-19

**38:20** for my mortal enemies are numerous;  
my treacherous foes are many. ^38-20

**38:21** Those who repay evil for good  
harass me for pursuing good. ^38-21

**38:22** Do not abandon me, O LORD;  
my God, be not far from me; ^38-22

**38:23** hasten to my aid,  
O Lord, my deliverance. ^38-23

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Lit. “passed over my head.”
