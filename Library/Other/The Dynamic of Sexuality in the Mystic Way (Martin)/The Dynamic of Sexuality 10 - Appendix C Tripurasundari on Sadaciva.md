Appendix D 

Śrīcakra: In the popular Kaula form Śrīcakra: In the ‘inverted’ Samaya form proposed by Lakṣmīdhara 

The notable difference between these two diagrams lies in the orientation of the triangles. In the Kaula version, on the left, the four śrīkantha, or Śaiva triangles, have their apex upwards, and the śivayuvatis, or Śakti triangles, have their apex downwards. The model proposed by Lakṣmīdhara has the Śaiva triangles facing downwards, and the Śakti triangles facing upwards. The diagrams are taken from Brooks, *Secret of the Three Cities*, Appendix Fig. A and Fig. A1.
