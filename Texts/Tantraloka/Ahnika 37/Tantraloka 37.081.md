## Mūla

सोऽन्यश्च शांभवमरीचिचयप्रणश्य-
त्सङ्कोचहार्दनलिनीघटितोज्ज्वलश्रीः ।
तं लुम्पकः परिचचार समुद्यमेषु
साधुः समावहति हन्त करावलम्बम् ॥ ^mula

so'nyaśca śāṃbhavamarīcicayapraṇaśya-
tsaṅkocahārdanalinīghaṭitojjvalaśrīḥ |
taṃ lumpakaḥ paricacāra samudyameṣu
sādhuḥ samāvahati hanta karāvalambam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 11 - Chapter Thirty-Seven, part 1#^pg284|p. 266]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=284>))
>
> Lumpaka was another (devotee) whose blazing (spiritual) opulence (Śrī) (grew) by virtue of the lotus of (his) heart, as its contraction came to be (progressively) destroyed by the multitude of Śiva’s rays. He assisted (Abhinava in his) exertions. Indeed, a wise man is (always quick) to lend a hand!
