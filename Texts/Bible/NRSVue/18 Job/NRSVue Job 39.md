# Job 39 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Job 38|← Job 38]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Job 40|Job 40 →]]

**39:1** “Do you know when the mountain goats give birth? Do you observe the calving of the deer? ^39-1

**39:2** Can you number the months that they fulfill, and do you know the time when they give birth, ^39-2

**39:3** when they crouch to give birth to their offspring and are delivered of their young? ^39-3

**39:4** Their young ones become strong; they grow up in the open; they go forth and do not return to them. ^39-4

**39:5** “Who has let the wild ass go free? Who has loosed the bonds of the swift ass, ^39-5

**39:6** to which I have given the steppe for its home, the salt land for its dwelling place? ^39-6

**39:7** It scorns the tumult of the city; it does not hear the shouts of the driver. ^39-7

**39:8** It ranges the mountains as its pasture, and it searches after every green thing. ^39-8

**39:9** “Is the wild ox willing to serve you? Will it spend the night at your crib? ^39-9

**39:10** Can you tie it in the furrow with ropes, or will it harrow the valleys after you? ^39-10

**39:11** Will you depend on it because its strength is great, and will you hand over your labor to it? ^39-11

**39:12** Do you have faith in it that it will return and bring your grain to your threshing floor?^[Heb *your grain and your threshing floor*] ^39-12

**39:13** “The ostrich’s wings flap wildly, though its pinions lack plumage.^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^39-13

**39:14** For it leaves its eggs to the earth and lets them be warmed on the ground, ^39-14

**39:15** forgetting that a foot may crush them and that a wild animal may trample them. ^39-15

**39:16** It deals cruelly with its young, as if they were not its own; though its labor should be in vain, yet it has no fear; ^39-16

**39:17** because God has made it forget wisdom and given it no share in understanding. ^39-17

**39:18** When it spreads its plumes aloft,^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] it laughs at the horse and its rider. ^39-18

**39:19** “Do you give the horse its might? Do you clothe its neck with mane? ^39-19

**39:20** Do you make it leap like the locust? Its majestic snorting is terrible. ^39-20

**39:21** It paws^[Gk Syr Vg: Heb *they dig*] violently, exults mightily; it goes out to meet the weapons. ^39-21

**39:22** It laughs at fear and is not dismayed; it does not turn back from the sword. ^39-22

**39:23** Upon it rattle the quiver, the flashing spear, and the javelin. ^39-23

**39:24** With fierceness and rage it swallows the ground; it cannot stand still at the sound of the trumpet. ^39-24

**39:25** When the trumpet sounds, it says ‘Aha!’ From a distance it smells the battle, the thunder of the captains, and the shouting. ^39-25

**39:26** “Is it by your wisdom that the hawk soars and spreads its wings toward the south? ^39-26

**39:27** Is it at your command that the eagle mounts up and makes its nest on high? ^39-27

**39:28** It lives on the rock and makes its home in the fastness of the rocky crag. ^39-28

**39:29** From there it spies the prey; its eyes see it from far away. ^39-29

**39:30** Its young ones suck up blood, and where the slain are, there it is.” ^39-30
