# Psalm 135 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 134|← Psalm 134]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 136|Psalm 136 →]]

## Praise for God’s goodness and might

**135:1** Praise the LORD! Praise the name of the LORD; give praise, O servants of the LORD, ^135-1

**135:2** you who stand in the house of the LORD, in the courts of the house of our God. ^135-2

**135:3** Praise the LORD, for the LORD is good; sing to his name, for he is gracious. ^135-3

**135:4** For the LORD has chosen Jacob for himself, Israel as his own possession. ^135-4

**135:5** For I know that the LORD is great; our Lord is above all gods. ^135-5

**135:6** Whatever the LORD pleases he does, in heaven and on earth, in the seas and all deeps. ^135-6

**135:7** He it is who makes the clouds rise at the end of the earth; he makes lightnings for the rain and brings out the wind from his storehouses. ^135-7

**135:8** He it was who struck down the firstborn of Egypt, both humans and animals; ^135-8

**135:9** he sent signs and wonders into your midst, O Egypt, against Pharaoh and all his servants. ^135-9

**135:10** He struck down many nations and killed mighty kings— ^135-10

**135:11** Sihon, king of the Amorites, and Og, king of Bashan, and all the kingdoms of Canaan— ^135-11

**135:12** and gave their land as a heritage, a heritage to his people Israel. ^135-12

**135:13** Your name, O LORD, endures forever, your renown, O LORD, throughout all ages. ^135-13

**135:14** For the LORD will vindicate his people and have compassion on his servants. ^135-14

**135:15** The idols of the nations are silver and gold, the work of human hands. ^135-15

**135:16** They have mouths, but they do not speak; they have eyes, but they do not see; ^135-16

**135:17** they have ears, but they do not hear, a nose, but there is no breath in their mouths. ^135-17

**135:18** Those who make them and all who trust them shall become like them. ^135-18

**135:19** O house of Israel, bless the LORD! O house of Aaron, bless the LORD! ^135-19

**135:20** O house of Levi, bless the LORD! You who fear the LORD, bless the LORD! ^135-20

**135:21** Blessed be the LORD from Zion, he who resides in Jerusalem. Praise the LORD! ^135-21
