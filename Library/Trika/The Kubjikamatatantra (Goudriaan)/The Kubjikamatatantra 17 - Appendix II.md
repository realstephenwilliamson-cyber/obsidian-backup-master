## Appendix II

The Anugraha variant of the formulas to the six goddesses Dākinī etc. (pat. 23, after 148, in the mss. ABL; stanza 1 also in E)

1

ṣodhānyāsavidhānam tu, pūrveṇa kathitam mayā |

nirodhotkramaṇādīnām, kiṇ tv idam na prakāśayet ||

nigraham tu samākhyātam, ’nugraham śrṇu sāmpratam |

aim hrīm śrīṇm dmryūm qdām dīm dūm qaim daum dah glūṇm dam

qdākinīye

mām rakṣa 2 sarvasattvavaśaṁṁkarī ājṁñāsiddhikarī aghore hrīṇm paramaghore hrūm ghorarūpe hsryūm ehi hsphraim śrīkubjikāyai aghore amoghe varade hrīm mamājñām dehi dam qdākinīpādah ||
aim hrīṇm śrīṇm rmryūm rām rīṇm rūm raiṇm raum rah slūṇ ram

rākiṇīye

mām rakṣa 2 sarvasattvavaśaṁkarī ājñṁñāsiddhikarī aghore hrīṇm paramaghore hrūmṇm ghorarūpe hsryūm ehi hsphraim śrīkubjikāyai aghore amoghe varade hrīṇm mamājñām dehi ram rākiṇīpādah ||
aim hrīm śrīṇm lmryūm lāmṇ līṇ lūm laiṇm laum lah plūm lam lākinīye rren ehi hsphraim aghore amoghe varade hrīm mamājñām dehi

lam lākinīpādah ||

aim hrīm śrīṇm kmryūm kām kīmṇm kūm kaim kaum kah mlūmṇm kam

kākinīye

kerre ehi hsphraim aghore amoghe varade hrīm mamājñām dehi

kam kākinīpādah ||

aim hrīm śrīm smryūm sām sīṇm sūm saiṇm saum sah nlūm sam

śākinīye

keere ehi hsphraim aghore amoghe varade hrīm mamājñām dehi

sam śākinīpādah ||

aim hrīm śrīṇm hmryūm hām hīṇm hūm haim haum hah hlūm ham

hākinīye

ceee ehi hsphraim aghore amoghe varade hrīm mamājñām dehi

ham hākinīpādah ||

Variani readings: Ib pūrve ca E, sarveṇa B. 3 dmryūm readable only in L; glum B; dākiṇīpādah A, dākiṇīpāda B. 4 smyūm (for rmryūm) B; smem (for slūm) B, illeg. A; -kubjikāye A; rākinīpādah AL, rākiṇīpādam B. 5 hmyūm (for Imryūm) B, Imryām L; plum (for plūm) B. 6 kmryūm em., mss. almost illeg. (hmryūm ? B, hryām ? L); mlum (for mlūm) B; A omits the greater part of this mantra. 7 smryām (for smryūm) L; nlum (fr nlūm) AB. 8 hmraim (for hmryüm) L, illeg. A; Jor hlūm, B reads hlum, L hūm. 3-8 the datives on -ye (dākinīye etc.) are sometimes given in the mss. as -yai (rākiṇīyai B, lākinīyai AB, sākinīyai AB, hākinīyai AB); for paramaghore hrūm, AL read p. hūm; Jor ghorarūpe hsryūm, L usually reads gh. hsrāmṇm, while the bīja is usually corrupt in A; in B, it can also be read as hmryūm; for hsphraim, the mss. sometimes give hsphrem.
