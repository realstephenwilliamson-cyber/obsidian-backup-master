## Mūla

न बन्धमोक्षयोर्योगो भेदहेतोरसंभवात् ।
तस्मादज्ञानशब्देन ज्ञत्वकर्तृत्वधर्मणाम् ॥ ^mula

na bandhamokṣayoryogo bhedahetorasaṃbhavāt |
tasmādajñānaśabdena jñatvakartṛtvadharmaṇām || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 05 - Chapter Thirteen, part 1#^pg169|p. 149]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=169>))
>
> Even if (one admits) the existence of Māyā, Karma, the individual soul and the Lord’s will, (the soul’s) association with bondage and liberation is not thereby (reasonably explained,) because there is not the cause for the difference between them. (with 13.42cd–43ab)
>
> Thus, an intelligent person should say that the term ‘ignorance’ refers to some kind of (kiñcit) obscuring covering (that envelops) the individual loci of consciousness (cidāṇu) that are endowed with the attributes of subjectivity and agency. (with 13.43cd–44ab)

*Lead-in:* एवमणूनां ज्ञत्वकर्तृत्वप्रतिबन्धकमज्ञानशब्दवाच्यं किञ्चिदावरणं वक्तव्यं येनायं बद्धोऽयं मुक्त इति विभागो भवेत् - - इत्याह --

> तस्मादज्ञानशब्देन ज्ञत्वकर्तृत्वधर्मणाम् ॥
> चिदणूनामावरणं किञ्चिद्वाच्यं विपश्चिता ।

अज्ञानशब्देनेति, यदुक्तम् --

"मलोऽज्ञानं पशुत्वं च तिरस्काररसस्तमः ।

अविद्या ह्यावृतिर्मूर्च्छा पर्यायास्तत्र चोदिताः ॥"

इति ॥ ४३ ॥
