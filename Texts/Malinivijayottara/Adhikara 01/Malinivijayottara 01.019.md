## Mūla

विज्ञानकेवलानष्टौ बोधयामास पुद्गलान् ।
अघोरः परमो घोरो घोररूपस्तदाननः ॥ ^mula

vijñānakevalān aṣṭau bodhayāmāsa pudgalān |
aghoraḥ paramo ghoro ghorarūpas tadānanaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2022 – June 2022
> p. 41 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=17>))
>
> **The Great Creation**
>
> At the beginning of the creation of the universe, desiring to emit (it), he awakened by his own will the eight souls who are Isolated in Consciousness Alone (vijñānakevalin).[^44] (with 1.18cd–19ab)

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2022 – June 2022
> p. 42 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=18>))
>
> (Their names are) Aghora, Paramaghora (Supreme Ghora), Ghorarūpa, Ghoramukha, Bhīma, Bhīṣaṇa, Vamana, and Pibana. These eight (Aghoras, that constitute the group of eight Aghoras (*aghorāṣṭaka*), maintain, destroy, protect and (dispense) grace. (with 1.19cd–20)

[^44]: Quoted in TĀ ad 9/92cd-93ab and 10/139cd-140ab, paraphrased in TĀ 10/139cd-140ab.
