## Chapter 140

**140:1** For the leader. A psalm of David. ^140-1

**140:2** Rescue me, O LORD, from evil men;  
save me from the lawless, ^140-2

**140:3** whose minds are full of evil schemes,  
who plot war every day. ^140-3

**140:4** They sharpen their tongues like serpents;  
spiders’ poison is on their lips. *Selah*. ^140-4

**140:5** O LORD, keep me out of the clutches of the wicked;  
save me from lawless men  
who scheme to make me fall.[^1] ^140-5

**140:6** Arrogant men laid traps with ropes for me;  
they spread out a net along the way;  
they set snares for me. *Selah*. ^140-6

**140:7** I said to the LORD: You are my God;  
give ear, O LORD, to my pleas for mercy. ^140-7

**140:8** O GOD, my Lord, the strength of my deliverance,  
You protected my head on the day of battle.[^2] ^140-8

**140:9** O LORD, do not grant the desires of the wicked;  
do not let their plan succeed,  
[^3]else they be exalted. *Selah*. ^140-9

**140:10** May the heads of those who beset me  
be covered with the mischief of their lips. ^140-10

**140:11** may coals of fire drop down upon them,  
and they be cast into pits, never to rise again. ^140-11

**140:12** Let slanderers have no place in the land;  
let the evil of the lawless man drive him into corrals. ^140-12

**140:13** I know that the LORD will champion  
the cause of the poor, the right of the needy. ^140-13

**140:14** Righteous men shall surely praise Your name;  
the upright shall dwell in Your presence. ^140-14

[^1]: Lit. “push my feet.”
[^2]: Lit. “arms.”
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
