# Sirach 2 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Sirach 01|← Sirach 1]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Sirach 03|Sirach 3 →]]

## Duties toward God

**2:1** My child, when you come to serve the Lord, prepare yourself for testing.^[Or *trials*] ^2-1

**2:2** Set your heart right and be steadfast, and do not be impetuous in time of calamity. ^2-2

**2:3** Cling to him and do not depart, so that your last days may be prosperous. ^2-3

**2:4** Accept whatever befalls you, and in times of humiliation be patient. ^2-4

**2:5** For gold is tested in the fire, and those found acceptable, in the furnace of humiliation.^[Other ancient authorities add *in sickness and poverty put your trust in him*] ^2-5

**2:6** Trust in him, and he will help you; make your ways straight and hope in him. ^2-6

**2:7** You who fear the Lord, wait for his mercy; do not stray, or else you may fall. ^2-7

**2:8** You who fear the Lord, trust in him, and your reward will not be lost. ^2-8

**2:9** You who fear the Lord, hope for good things, for lasting joy and mercy.^[Other ancient authorities add *for his reward is an everlasting gift with joy*] ^2-9

**2:10** Consider the generations of old and see: Has anyone trusted in the Lord and been disappointed? Or has anyone persevered in the fear of the Lord^[Gk *of him*] and been forsaken? Or has anyone called upon him and been neglected? ^2-10

**2:11** For the Lord is compassionate and merciful; he forgives sins and saves in time of distress. ^2-11

**2:12** Woe to timid hearts and to slack hands and to the sinner who walks a double path! ^2-12

**2:13** Woe to the fainthearted who have no trust! Therefore they will have no shelter. ^2-13

**2:14** Woe to you who have lost patience! What will you do when the Lord’s reckoning comes? ^2-14

**2:15** Those who fear the Lord will not disobey his words, and those who love him will keep his ways. ^2-15

**2:16** Those who fear the Lord will seek his favor, and those who love him will be filled with his law. ^2-16

**2:17** Those who fear the Lord will prepare their hearts and will humble themselves before him. ^2-17

**2:18** Let us fall into the hands of the Lord but not into the hands of mortals, for equal to his majesty is his mercy.^[Syr adds *and equal to his name are his works*] ^2-18
