## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. III
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 03 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v03 09 - Chapter Four, part 8#^pg308|p. 295]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 03.pdf#page=308>))
>
> They say that the reflective awareness, uncreated and unsullied, which is this reality, is (Supreme) ‘P’ (aham) (consciousness). This, indeed, is the luminosity of the Light (of consciousness). This is the vitality (vīrya) and heart of all Mantras; without it, they would be insentient, like living beings without a heart. (with 4.192–193)
