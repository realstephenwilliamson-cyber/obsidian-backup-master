# Luke 18 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Luke 17|← Luke 17]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Luke 19|Luke 19 →]]

Compare: [[Luke 18|BSB and SBLGNT]]

**18:1** And he spoke also a parable to them, that we ought always to pray, and not to faint, ^18-1

**18:2** Saying: There was a judge in a certain city, who feared not God, nor regarded man. ^18-2

**18:3** And there was a certain widow in that city, and she came to him, saying: Avenge me of my adversary. ^18-3

**18:4** And he would not for a long time. But afterwards he said within himself: Although I fear not God, nor regard man, ^18-4

**18:5** Yet because this widow is troublesome to me, I will avenge her, lest continually coming she weary me. ^18-5

**18:6** And the Lord said: Hear what the unjust judge saith. ^18-6

**18:7** And will not God revenge his elect who cry to him day and night: and will he have patience in their regard? ^18-7

**18:8** I say to you, that he will quickly revenge them. But yet the Son of man, when he cometh, shall he find, think you, faith on earth? ^18-8

**18:9** And to some who trusted in themselves as just, and despised others, he spoke also this parable: ^18-9

**18:10** Two men went up into the temple to pray: the one a Pharisee, and the other a publican. ^18-10

**18:11** The Pharisee standing, prayed thus with himself: O God, I give thee thanks that I am not as the rest of men, extortioners, unjust, adulterers, as also is this publican. ^18-11

**18:12** I fast twice in a week: I give tithes of all that I possess. ^18-12

**18:13** And the publican, standing afar off, would not so much as lift up his eyes towards heaven; but struck his breast, saying: O god, be merciful to me a sinner. ^18-13

**18:14** I say to you, this man went down into his house justified rather that the other: because every one that exalteth himself, shall be humbled: and he that humbleth himself, shall be exalted. ^18-14

**18:15** And they brought unto him also infants, that he might touch them. Which when the disciples saw, they rebuked them. ^18-15

**18:16** But Jesus, calling them together, said: Suffer children to come to me, and forbid them not: for of such is the kingdom of God. ^18-16

**18:17** Amen, I say to you: Whosoever shall not receive the kingdom of God as a child, shall not enter into it. ^18-17

**18:18** And a certain ruler asked him, saying: Good master, what shall I do to possess everlasting life? ^18-18

**18:19** And Jesus said to him: Why dost thou call me good? None is good but God alone. ^18-19

**18:20** Thou knowest the commandments: Thou shalt not kill: Thou shalt not commit adultery: Thou shalt not steal: Thou shalt not bear false witness: Honour thy father and mother. ^18-20

**18:21** Who said: All these things have I kept from my youth. ^18-21

**18:22** Which when Jesus had heard, he said to him: Yet one thing is wanting to thee: sell all whatever thou hast, and give to the poor, and thou shalt have treasure in heaven: and come, follow me. ^18-22

**18:23** He having heard these things, became sorrowful; for he was very rich. ^18-23

**18:24** And Jesus seeing him become sorrowful, said: How hardly shall they that have riches enter into the kingdom of God. ^18-24

**18:25** For it is easier for a camel to pass through the eye of a needle, than for a rich man to enter into the kingdom of God. ^18-25

**18:26** And they that heard it, said: Who then can be saved? ^18-26

**18:27** He said to them: The things that are impossible with men, are possible with God. ^18-27

**18:28** Then Peter said: Behold, we have left all things, and have followed thee. ^18-28

**18:29** Who said to them: Amen, I say to you, there is no man that hath left house, or parents, or brethren, or wife, or children, for the kingdom of God’s sake, ^18-29

**18:30** Who shall not receive much more in this present time, and in the world to come life everlasting. ^18-30

**18:31** Then Jesus took unto him the twelve, and said to them: Behold, we go up to Jerusalem, and all things shall be accomplished which were written by the prophets concerning the Son of man. ^18-31

**18:32** For he shall be delivered to the Gentiles, and shall be mocked, and scourged, and spit upon: ^18-32

**18:33** And after they have scourged him, they will put him to death; and the third day he shall rise again. ^18-33

**18:34** And they understood none of these things, and this word was hid from them, and they understood not the things that were said. ^18-34

**18:35** Now it came to pass, when he drew nigh to Jericho, that a certain blind man sat by the way side, begging. ^18-35

**18:36** And when he heard the multitude passing by, he asked what this meant. ^18-36

**18:37** And they told him, that Jesus of Nazareth was passing by. ^18-37

**18:38** And he cried out, saying: Jesus, son of David, have mercy on me. ^18-38

**18:39** And they that went before, rebuked him, that he should hold his peace: but he cried out much more: Son of David, have mercy on me. ^18-39

**18:40** And Jesus standing, commanded him to be brought unto him. And when he was come near, he asked him, ^18-40

**18:41** Saying: What wilt thou that I do to thee? But he said: Lord, that I may see. ^18-41

**18:42** And Jesus said to him: Receive thy sight: thy faith hath made thee whole. ^18-42

**18:43** And immediately he saw, and followed him, glorifying God. And all the people, when they saw it, gave praise to God. ^18-43
