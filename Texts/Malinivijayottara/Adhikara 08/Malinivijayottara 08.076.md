## Mūla

अग्नीशरक्षोवायूनां दक्षिणे च यथाक्रमम् ।
शक्त्यङ्गानि शिवाङ्गानि तथैव विधिना स्मरेत् ॥ ^mula

agnīśarakṣovāyūnāṁ dakṣiṇe ca yathākramam |
śaktyaṅgāni śivāṅgāni tathaiva vidhinā smaret || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2025 – April 2025
> p. 21 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=35>))
>
> After that one should deposit the five limbs of the Vidyā in the south-east etc., that is, in the south-east, north-east, south-west, north-west and south, in due order.[^83] (75cd-76ab). (with 8.75cd–76ab)

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2025 – April 2025
> p. 21 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=35>))
>
> One should recollect with the same procedure the limbs of Śakti and those of Śiva. However, (one should also) meditate on the Weapon mantra in (all) the directions, beginning with (east governed by) Indra, the Eye in the middle and (on the petals of the lotus), the group of eight beginning with Aghora along with the eight (corresponding goddesses) beginning with Aghorī. (with 8.76cd–77)

[^83]: TĀ 15/353cd-365 is a paraphrase of MV 8/75cd-84ab interspersed with direct quotations. MV 8/75cd-76ab is quoted in TĀv ad 15/357cd-358 (357-358ab).
