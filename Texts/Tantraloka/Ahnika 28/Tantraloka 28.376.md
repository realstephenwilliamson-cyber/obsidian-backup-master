## Mūla

यः सर्वतन्मयीभावे ह्लादो नत्वेककस्य सः ।
आनन्दनिर्भरा संवित्प्रत्येकं सा तथैकताम् ॥ ^mula

yaḥ sarvatanmayībhāve hlādo natvekakasya saḥ |
ānandanirbharā saṃvitpratyekaṃ sā tathaikatām || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 07 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 5#^pg186|p. 169]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=186>))
>
> Thus, in a gathering of many people (for a performance) of song, dance and the like, the enjoyment (they experience is collective) when they are all one (equally immersed in the show together), (that joy) is not (experienced by) one (who is alone). (with 28.375cd–376ab)
>
> The consciousness, full to overflowing with bliss, (of each one) individually, attaining in this way oneness when dance and the like is its object, enjoys a state of complete (unobstructed) bliss.! (with 28.376cd–377ab)
