## Mūla

तस्मात्सा परमेशेच्छा ययायं मोहितस्तथा ।
अनन्तकालसंवेद्यदुःखपात्रत्वमीहते ॥ ^mula

tasmātsā parameśecchā yayāyaṃ mohitastathā |
anantakālasaṃvedyaduḥkhapātratvamīhate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 12 - Chapter Fourteen, and Appendices to Chapters Eleven and Twelve#^pg375|p. 353]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=375>))

> Thus, such is the Supreme Lord’s will due to which one is deluded (mohita) in this way and chooses to be a victim of suffering for endless time.
