## Mūla

बिम्बात्समुदयो यस्या इत्युक्ता प्रतिबिम्बता ।
विम्बस्य यस्या उदय इत्युक्ता तदुपायता ॥ ^mula

bimbātsamudayo yasyā ityuktā pratibimbatā |
vimbasya yasyā udaya ityuktā tadupāyatā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 06 - Chapter Thirty-Two#^pg153|p. 135]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=153>))
>
> (In the sense of) the arising (of Mudrā) from an original image (bimba) (as does a reflection of an image in a mirror or an impression of a seal in wax), the state of (Mudrā) is said to be that of a reflection (pratibimba). (In the sense that) the arising is that of its original image, (Mudrā) is said to be the means to (the realization of) that (original image).

## Viveka

> [!viveka]- Viveka in English (Dyczkowski, vol. XI, pp. 135–136)
> ![[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 06 - Chapter Thirty-Two#Viveka on 32.2]]

*Lead-in:* तदेव तात्पर्यद्वारेण आह--

> बिम्बात्समुदयो यस्या इत्युक्ता प्रतिबिम्बता ।
> बिम्बस्य यस्या उदय इत्युक्ता तदुपायता ॥

समुदय इति उत्पत्तिः । यस्या इति--प्रतिबिम्बरूपाया मुद्रायाः--इति षष्ठ्यर्थः, यस्याश्च सकाशात्--इति पञ्चम्यर्थः । उदय इति ज्ञप्तिस्तदुपायतेति ज्ञप्तिद्वारिका बिम्बोपायतेत्यर्थः ॥ २ ॥
