## Mūla

भ्राता तदीयोऽभिनवश्च नाम्ना
न केवलं सच्चरितैरपि स्वैः ।
पीतेन विज्ञानरसेन यस्य
तत्रैव तृष्णा ववृधे निकामम् ॥ ^mula

bhrātā tadīyo'bhinavaśca nāmnā
na kevalaṃ saccaritairapi svaiḥ |
pītena vijñānarasena yasya
tatraiva tṛṣṇā vavṛdhe nikāmam || ^iast

> Her brother was Abhinava by name, not only because of his own righteous deeds. One who has drunk the aesthetic juice (rasa) of consciousness, his craving there itself (for it) grows exceedingly (nikāmam), by virtue of that.
