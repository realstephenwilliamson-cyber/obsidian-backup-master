## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 06 - Chapter Sixteen, part 2#^pg83|p. 63]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=83>))
>
> Now (we go on to expound) the deposition of the Path of the Phonemes here in (all) three procedures. The first (phoneme) is within four (fingers? space). (The following) twenty-three phonemes (each take up a space of) two fingers. Each (of the following) six phonemes (extend) within three (fingers’ space), and each of (the last) four are within (a space of) four (fingers). The phonemes, (deposited) in the reverse order, (are those) of Mātrkā and Mālinī. Śiva, who pervades (all of them), has sixteen (phonemes). The other two procedures (are derived from this one), like (the deposition of) the principles, adding a twelve(-finger space for the intermediate one) and (another) twelve (for the supreme one), to the space alloted to the twenty-three phonemes that come after the first one. (with 16.139cd–142)
