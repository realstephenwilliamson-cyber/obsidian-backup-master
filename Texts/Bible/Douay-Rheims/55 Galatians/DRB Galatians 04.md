# Galatians 4 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Galatians 03|← Galatians 3]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Galatians 05|Galatians 5 →]]

Compare: [[Galatians 04|BSB and SBLGNT]]

**4:1** Now I say, as long as the heir is a child, he differeth nothing from a servant, though he be lord of all; ^4-1

**4:2** But is under tutors and governors until the time appointed by the father: ^4-2

**4:3** So we also, when we were children, were serving under the elements of the world. ^4-3

**4:4** But when the fulness of the time was come, God sent his Son, made of a woman, made under the law: ^4-4

**4:5** That he might redeem them who were under the law: that we might receive the adoption of sons. ^4-5

**4:6** And because you are sons, God hath sent the Spirit of his Son into your hearts, crying: Abba, Father. ^4-6

**4:7** Therefore now he is not a servant, but a son. And if a son, an heir also through God. ^4-7

**4:8** But then indeed, not knowing God, you served them, who, by nature, are not gods. ^4-8

**4:9** But now, after that you have known God, or rather are known by God: how turn you again to the weak and needy elements, which you desire to serve again? ^4-9

**4:10** You observe days, and months, and times, and years. ^4-10

**4:11** I am afraid of you, lest perhaps I have laboured in vain among you. ^4-11

**4:12** Be ye as I, because I also am as you: brethren, I beseech you: you have not injured me at all. ^4-12

**4:13** And you know, how through infirmity of the flesh, I preached the gospel to you heretofore: and your temptation in my flesh, ^4-13

**4:14** You despised not, nor rejected: but received me as an angel of God, even as Christ Jesus. ^4-14

**4:15** Where is then your blessedness? For I bear you witness, that, if it could be done, you would have plucked out your own eyes, and would have given them to me. ^4-15

**4:16** Am I then become your enemy, because I tell you the truth? ^4-16

**4:17** They are zealous in your regard not well: but they would exclude you, that you might be zealous for them. ^4-17

**4:18** But be zealous for that which is good in a good thing always: and not only when I am present with you. ^4-18

**4:19** My little children, of whom I am in labour again, until Christ be formed in you. ^4-19

**4:20** And I would willingly be present with you now, and change my voice: because I am ashamed for you. ^4-20

**4:21** Tell me, you that desire to be under the law, have you not read the law? ^4-21

**4:22** For it is written that Abraham had two sons: the one by a bondwoman, and the other by a free woman. ^4-22

**4:23** But he who was of the bondwoman, was born according to the flesh: but he of the free woman, was by promise. ^4-23

**4:24** Which things are said by an allegory. For these are the two testaments. The one from mount Sina, engendering unto bondage; which is Agar: ^4-24

**4:25** For Sina is a mountain in Arabia, which hath affinity to that Jerusalem which now is, and is in bondage with her children. ^4-25

**4:26** But that Jerusalem, which is above, is free: which is our mother. ^4-26

**4:27** For it is written: Rejoice, thou barren, that bearest not: break forth and cry, thou that travailest not: for many are the children of the desolate, more than of her that hath a husband. ^4-27

**4:28** Now we, brethren, as Isaac was, are the children of promise. ^4-28

**4:29** But as then he, that was born according to the flesh, persecuted him that was after the spirit; so also it is now. ^4-29

**4:30** But what saith the scripture? Cast out the bondwoman and her son; for the son of the bondwoman shall not be heir with the son of the free woman. ^4-30

**4:31** So then, brethren, we are not the children of the bondwoman, but of the free: by the freedom wherewith Christ has made us free. ^4-31
