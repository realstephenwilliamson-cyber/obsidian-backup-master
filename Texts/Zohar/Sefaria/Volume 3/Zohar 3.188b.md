# Zohar 3.188b

← [[Zohar 3.188a]] · [[Zohar (Soncino)]] · [[Zohar 3.189a]] →

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 9.09 - Yanuqa#^z3-188b|Yanuqa, at 3:188b]]

*Volume 3, folio 188b* · Balak

*Passage Balak 6:67 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 3.188a#^balak-6-67|Balak 6:67]].*

**Balak 6:68** and the boy said: ‘Holy sages, do you desire the bread of ease without warfare, or the bread of warfare?’ R. Eleazar replied: ‘Beloved son, dear and holy, so we desire. We are practised in all weapons of war, and we know how to use the sword and the bow and the lance and the sling, but thou art young and hast not yet seen how mighty warriors contend in battle.’ ^balak-6-68

**Balak 6:69** The boy rejoiced and said: ‘In truth I have not seen, but it is written: “Let not him that girdeth on boast like him that putteth off.’ They then laid the table with all requirements. Said R. Eleazar: ‘How I rejoice over this youngster, and how many new points will be brought out at this table! Therefore I said that I knew that his heart was being agitated by the holy spirit like a bell.’ ^balak-6-69

**Balak 6:70** Said the boy: ‘He that desires bread at the point of the sword, let him eat.’ R. Eleazar drew him near to him and said: ‘Because you boasted, you must commence the fight.’ ‘But’, said the boy, ‘I said at first that the fighting would be after the meal. Now, however, whoever wants fine flour must bring his weapons in his hands.’ R. Eleazar replied: ‘It is most fitting for you to give us a taste of your weapons.’ ^balak-6-70

**Balak 6:71** The boy then took the text: “It shall be that when ye eat of the bread of the land, ye shall offer up an heave offering unto the Lord” (Num. 15, 19). ‘This verse refers to the Omer of waving (*tenufah)*. ^balak-6-71

**Balak 6:72** What is *tenufah?* ^balak-6-72

**Balak 6:73** We may read it *Tenu feh*, “Give a mouth”, the mouth being symbolic of the honour which we have to give to the Holy One, blessed be He. Hence the Omer had to be lifted up to show that we give to God this “mouth”, since the chief praise of the Supreme King is when Israel prepare for him this honour and give glory to the King. ^balak-6-73

**Balak 6:74** Why was the Omer from barley and not from wheat? Because barley ripens first, whereas wheat is the more perfect food, being symbolical of the elimination of sin by the substitution of *hittah* (wheat) for *het* (sin). ^balak-6-74

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Balak 6:75–6:76.*

**Balak 6:77** You, Companions, who have not attended on R. Shemaya the Saint, say that in the five kinds of com there is no share for the “other side”, but in truth in whatever rots in the ground there is a share for the other side, namely the chaff and the straw. ^balak-6-77

**Balak 6:78** Said R. Eleazar: ‘Assuredly, it is so.’ ^balak-6-78
