## Mūla

मायातत्त्वलये त्वेते प्रयान्ति परमं पदम् ।
मायोर्ध्वे ये सिताध्वस्थास्तेषां परशिवे लयः ॥ ^mula

māyātattvalaye tvete prayānti paramaṃ padam |
māyordhve ye sitādhvasthāsteṣāṃ paraśive layaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 12 - Chapter Six, part 5#^pg404|p. 389]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=404>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 6.175cd–176ab.
>
> When reabsorption occurs, Brahmā, Viṣṇu, the Hundred Rudras and the five groups of eight dissolve progressively into one another on the path up to Māyā. When Māyā (also) dissolves away, they attain the Supreme State. (with 6.174–175ab)
>
> (Then,) those who reside beyond Māyā on the Pure Path dissolve into the Supreme Śiva. But according to others, there is duality (bheda) (even in this form of) reabsorption also, because (some) secondary, adjunct duality (aupādhikād bhedād) (persists) there. (with 6.175cd–176ab)
