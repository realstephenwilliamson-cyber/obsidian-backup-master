## Mūla

यदधिष्ठेयमेवेह नाधिष्ठातृ कदाचन ।
संवेदनगतं वेद्यं तज्जाग्रत्समुदाहृतम् ॥ ^mula

yadadhiṣṭheyameveha nādhiṣṭhātṛ kadācana |
saṃvedanagataṃ vedyaṃ tajjāgratsamudāhṛtam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 13 - Chapter Ten, part 4#^pg460|p. 441]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=460>))
>
> The waking state is said to be that in which objectivity (vedya) is in the purview of awareness (saṁvedanagata). Indeed, it is (invariably) that which is sustained (adhiṣṭheya) (by consciousness), never the sustaining subject (adhiṣṭhātṛ).
