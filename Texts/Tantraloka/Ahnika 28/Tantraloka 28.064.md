## Mūla

तेन सर्वं हुतं चेष्टं त्रैलोक्यं सचराचरम् ।
ज्ञानिने योगिने वापि यो ददाति करोति वा ॥ ^mula

tena sarvaṃ hutaṃ ceṣṭaṃ trailokyaṃ sacarācaram |
jñānine yogine vāpi yo dadāti karoti vā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 03 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 1#^pg54|p. 37]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=54>))
>
> ‘Thus, he who gives or does (something of benefit) to a man of knowledge or to a yogi, (acquires the merit of) having offered as oblation all that (he) desires, the triple world, (all that lives and) moves and is immobile.’
