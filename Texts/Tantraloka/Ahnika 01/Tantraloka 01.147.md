## Mūla

यथा विस्फुरितदृशामनुसन्धिं विनाप्यलम् ।
भाति भावः स्फुटस्तद्वत्केषामपि शिवात्मता ॥ ^mula

yathā visphuritadṛśāmanusandhiṃ vināpyalam |
bhāti bhāvaḥ sphuṭastadvatkeṣāmapi śivātmatā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. I
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 01 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v01 09 - Chapter One, part 8#^pg329|p. 315]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 01.pdf#page=329>))
>
> Just as an entity (bhāāva) manifests clearly (and directly) for those whose eyes (drk) are wide open, without having to (apply their mind to) determine its nature (anusaṁdhi), in the same way Śiva’s nature (manifests in an instant) to some (elevated souls) also. 2) (Whereas, if the clear and certain) reflective awareness (of one’s own identity as all things) comes by repeated deliberation (carcana) on the (developing) process of (increasing) conviction (niścayakrama) that is part of the notion (vikalpa) (that ‘I am Śiva and all this’), (the wise) know that to be indeed the means based on knowledge. (with 1.147–148)
