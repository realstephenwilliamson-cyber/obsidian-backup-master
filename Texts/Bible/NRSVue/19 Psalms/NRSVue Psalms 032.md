## The joy of forgiveness

*Of David. A Maskil.*

**32:1** Happy are those whose transgression is forgiven, whose sin is covered. ^32-1

**32:2** Happy are those to whom the LORD imputes no iniquity and in whose spirit there is no deceit. ^32-2

**32:3** While I kept silent, my body wasted away through my groaning all day long. ^32-3

**32:4** For day and night your hand was heavy upon me; my strength was dried up^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] as by the heat of summer. *Selah* ^32-4

**32:5** Then I acknowledged my sin to you, and I did not hide my iniquity; I said, “I will confess my transgressions to the LORD,” and you forgave the guilt of my sin. *Selah* ^32-5

**32:6** Therefore let all who are faithful offer prayer to you; at a time of distress,^[Cn: Heb *at a time of finding only*] the rush of mighty waters shall not reach them. ^32-6

**32:7** You are a hiding place for me; you preserve me from trouble; you surround me with glad cries of deliverance. *Selah* ^32-7

**32:8** I will instruct you and teach you the way you should go; I will counsel you with my eye upon you. ^32-8

**32:9** Do not be like a horse or a mule, without understanding, whose temper must be curbed with bit and bridle, else it will not stay near you. ^32-9

**32:10** Many are the torments of the wicked, but steadfast love surrounds those who trust in the LORD. ^32-10

**32:11** Be glad in the LORD and rejoice, O righteous, and shout for joy, all you upright in heart. ^32-11
