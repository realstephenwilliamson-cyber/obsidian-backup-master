# Philemon 1 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue|index]]

Compare: [[Philemon 01|BSB and SBLGNT]] · [[DRB Philemon 01|Douay-Rheims]] · [[JANT Philemon 01|JANT notes]]

## Salutation

**1:1** Paul, a prisoner of Christ Jesus, and Timothy our brother, To our beloved coworker Philemon, ^1-1

**1:2** to our^[Other ancient authorities add *beloved*] sister Apphia, to our fellow soldier Archippus, and to the church in your^[Gk *your* is singular] house: ^1-2

**1:3** Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ. ^1-3

## Philemon’s love and faith

**1:4** I thank my God always when I mention you^[In verses 4–21, *you* is singular] in my prayers, ^1-4

**1:5** because I hear of your love for all the saints and your faith toward the Lord Jesus. ^1-5

**1:6** I pray that the partnership of your faith may become effective as you comprehend all the good that we^[Other ancient authorities read *you* (plural)] share in Christ.^[Other ancient authorities add *Jesus*] ^1-6

**1:7** I have^[Other *ancient authorities read we have*] indeed received much joy and encouragement from your love, because the hearts of the saints have been refreshed through you, my brother. ^1-7

## Paul’s plea for Onesimus

**1:8** For this reason, though I am more than bold enough in Christ to command you to do the right thing, ^1-8

**1:9** yet I would rather appeal to you on the basis of love—and I, Paul, do this as an old man and now also as a prisoner of Christ Jesus.^[Or *as an ambassador of Christ Jesus, and now also his prisoner*] ^1-9

**1:10** I am appealing to you for my child, Onesimus, whose father I have become during my imprisonment. ^1-10

**1:11** Formerly he was useless to you, but now he is indeed useful^[The name Onesimus means *useful* or *beneficial*] to^[Other ancient authorities read *both to*] you and to me. ^1-11

**1:12** I am sending him, that is, my own heart, back to you.^[Other ancient authorities read *you; receive him*] ^1-12

**1:13** I wanted to keep him with me so that he might minister to me in your place during my imprisonment for the gospel, ^1-13

**1:14** but I preferred to do nothing without your consent in order that your good deed might be voluntary and not something forced. ^1-14

**1:15** Perhaps this is the reason he was separated from you for a while, so that you might have him back for the long term, ^1-15

**1:16** no longer as a slave but more than a slave, a beloved brother—especially to me but how much more to you, both in the flesh and in the Lord. ^1-16

**1:17** So if you consider me your partner, welcome him as you would welcome me. ^1-17

**1:18** If he has wronged you in any way or owes you anything, charge that to me. ^1-18

**1:19** I, Paul, am writing this with my own hand: I will repay it. I say nothing about your owing me even your own self. ^1-19

**1:20** Yes, brother, let me have this benefit from you in the Lord! Refresh my heart in Christ.^[Other ancient authorities read *in the Lord*] ^1-20

**1:21** Confident of your obedience, I am writing to you, knowing that you will do even more than I ask. ^1-21

**1:22** One thing more: prepare a guest room for me, for I am hoping through your prayers to be restored to you. ^1-22

## Final greetings and benediction

**1:23** Epaphras, my fellow prisoner in Christ Jesus, sends greetings to you,^[Here *you* is singular] ^1-23

**1:24** and so do Mark, Aristarchus, Demas, and Luke, my coworkers. ^1-24

**1:25** The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit.^[Other ancient authorities add *Amen*] ^1-25
