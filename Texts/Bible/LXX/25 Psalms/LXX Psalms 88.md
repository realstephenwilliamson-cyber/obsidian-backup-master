# LXX Psalms 88

← [[LXX Psalms 87]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 89]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 089]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 089]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**88:1** *A Psalm* of instruction for Ætham the Israelite.  ^88-1

**88:2** I will sing of thy mercies, O Lord, for ever: I will declare thy truth with my mouth to all generations.  ^88-2

**88:3** For thou hast said, Mercy shall be built up for ever: thy truth shall be established in the heavens.  ^88-3

**88:4** I made a covenant with my chosen ones, I sware unto David my servant.  ^88-4

**88:5** I will establish thy seed for ever, and build up thy throne to all generations. Pause.[^1]  ^88-5

**88:6** The heavens shall declare thy wonders, O Lord; and thy truth in the assembly of the saints.  ^88-6

**88:7** For who in the heavens shall be compared to the Lord? and who shall be likened to the Lord among the sons of God?[^2]  ^88-7

**88:8** God is glorified in the council of the saints; great and terrible toward all that are round about him.  ^88-8

**88:9** O Lord God of hosts, who is like to thee? thou art mighty, O Lord, and thy truth is round about thee.  ^88-9

**88:10** Thou rulest the power of the sea; and thou calmest the tumult of its waves.  ^88-10

**88:11** Thou hast brought down the proud as one that is slain; and with the arm of thy power thou hast scattered thine enemies.  ^88-11

**88:12** The heavens are thine, and the earth is thine: thou hast founded the world, and the fulness of it.  ^88-12

**88:13** Thou hast created the north and the west: Thabor and Hermon shall rejoice in thy name.[^3]  ^88-13

**88:14** Thine is the mighty arm: let thy hand be strengthened, let thy right hand be exalted.  ^88-14

**88:15** Justice and judgment are the establishment of thy throne: mercy and truth shall go before thy face.  ^88-15

**88:16** Blessed is the people that knows the joyful sound: they shall walk, O Lord, in the light of thy countenance.  ^88-16

**88:17** And in thy name shall they rejoice all the day: and in thy righteousness shall they be exalted.  ^88-17

**88:18** For thou art the boast of their strength; and in thy good pleasure shall our horn be exalted,  ^88-18

**88:19** for *our* help is of the Lord; and of the Holy One of Israel, our king.  ^88-19

**88:20** Then thou spokest in vision to thy children, and saidst, I have laid help on a mighty one; I have exalted one chosen out of my people.  ^88-20

**88:21** I have found David my servant; I have anointed him by *my* holy mercy.[^4]  ^88-21

**88:22** For my hand shall support him; and mine arm shall strengthen him.  ^88-22

**88:23** The enemy shall have no advantage against him; and the son of transgression shall not hurt him again.  ^88-23

**88:24** And I will hew down his foes before him, and put to flight those that hate him.  ^88-24

**88:25** But my truth and my mercy shall be with him; and in my name shall his horn be exalted.  ^88-25

**88:26** And I will set his hand in the sea, and his right hand in the rivers.  ^88-26

**88:27** He shall call upon me, *saying*, Thou art my Father, my God, and the helper of my salvation.  ^88-27

**88:28** And I will make him *my* first-born, higher than the kings of the earth.  ^88-28

**88:29** I will keep my mercy for him for ever, and my covenant *shall be* firm with him.  ^88-29

**88:30** And I will establish his seed for ever and ever, and his throne as the days of heaven.  ^88-30

**88:31** If his children should forsake my law, and walk not in my judgments;  ^88-31

**88:32** if they should profane my ordinances, and not keep my commandments;  ^88-32

**88:33** I will visit their transgressions with a rod, and their sins with scourges.  ^88-33

**88:34** But my mercy I will not utterly remove from him, nor wrong my truth.  ^88-34

**88:35** Neither will I by any means profane my covenant; and I will not make void the things that proceed out of my lips.  ^88-35

**88:36** Once have I sworn by my holiness, that I will not lie to David.  ^88-36

**88:37** His seed shall endure for ever, and his throne as the sun before me;  ^88-37

**88:38** and as the moon *that is* established for ever, and as the faithful witness in heaven. Pause.  ^88-38

**88:39** But thou hast cast off and set at nought, thou hast rejected thine anointed.  ^88-39

**88:40** Thou hast overthrown the covenant of thy servant; thou hast profaned his sanctuary, *casting it* to the ground.  ^88-40

**88:41** Thou hast broken down all his hedges; thou hast made his strong holds a terror.  ^88-41

**88:42** All that go by the way have spoiled him: he is become a reproach to his neighbours.  ^88-42

**88:43** Thou hast exalted the right hand of his enemies; thou hast made all his enemies to rejoice.  ^88-43

**88:44** Thou hast turned back the help of his sword, and hast not helped him in the battle.  ^88-44

**88:45** Thou hast deprived him of glory: thou hast broken down his throne to the ground.[^5]  ^88-45

**88:46** Thou hast shortened the days of his throne: thou hast poured shame upon him. Pause.[^6]  ^88-46

**88:47** How long, O Lord, wilt thou turn away, for ever? shall thine anger flame out as fire?  ^88-47

**88:48** Remember what my being is: for hast thou created all the sons of men in vain?  ^88-48

**88:49** What man is there who shall live, and not see death? shall *any one* deliver his soul from the hand of Hades? Pause.  ^88-49

**88:50** Where are thine ancient mercies, O Lord, which thou swarest to David in thy truth?  ^88-50

**88:51** Remember, O Lord, the reproach of thy servants, which I have borne in my bosom, *even the reproach* of many nations;  ^88-51

**88:52** wherewith thine enemies have reviled, O Lord: wherewith they have reviled the recompence of thine anointed.  ^88-52

**88:53** Blessed be the Lord for ever. So be it, so be it.  ^88-53

[^1]: 88:5 *Gr.* prepare.
[^2]: 88:7 *Gr.* clouds.
[^3]: 88:13 *Gr.* sea.
[^4]: 88:21 *Heb.* and *Alex.* oil, see Ps. 91. 10.
[^5]: 88:45 *Gr.* purification.
[^6]: 88:46 *Alex.* time.
