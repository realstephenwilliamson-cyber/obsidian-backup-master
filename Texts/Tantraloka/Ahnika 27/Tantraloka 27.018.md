## Mūla

सूत्रे पात्रे ध्वजे वस्त्रे स्वयम्भूबाणपूजिते ।
नदीप्रस्रवणोत्थे च नाह्वानं नापि कल्पना ॥ ^mula

sūtre pātre dhvaje vastre svayambhūbāṇapūjite |
nadīprasravaṇotthe ca nāhvānaṃ nāpi kalpanā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 18 - Chapter Twenty-Seven#^pg425|p. 405]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=425>))
>
> Of all the aniconic (avyakta) signs (of the presence of deity) (liṅga), the main one is (natural), not fashioned (by man) (akalpita). It is said in various places in the Supreme Lord’s (scripture), in which the defining characteristic (of Liṅgas is stated,) that there is neither invocation nor artifact (kalpanā) with regard to a thread (that is used for a rosary), a (sacrificial) jar, a flag, a cloth, a spontaneously born precious stone (bāṇa) venerated (as a liṅga), and one that has arisen in the current of a river. Nor is there any restriction (in this case) as to the (location) of a sacred seat (pīṭha) or building (where they are enshrined), (the suitable) limbs of mantras (by which they are worshipped), time (when it should be done), or the like. (with 27.17–19ab)
