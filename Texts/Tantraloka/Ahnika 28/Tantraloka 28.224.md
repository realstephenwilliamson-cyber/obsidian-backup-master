## Mūla

भोगान्कर्मकृतान्भुङ्क्ते योन्ययोनिजदेहगः ।
उक्तं च गह्वराभिख्ये शास्त्रे शीतांशुमौलिना ॥ ^mula

bhogānkarmakṛtānbhuṅkte yonyayonijadehagaḥ |
uktaṃ ca gahvarābhikhye śāstre śītāṃśumaulinā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 05 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 3#^pg116|p. 99]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=116>))
>
> There also, the vital force, along with the body, gradually becomes stronger, and because of the strength of karmic necessity, the vital force assumes the state of the organs of sense, as the contact with consciousness (saṁvitsparśa) there in the channels (nādī) of the voids (of the senses) becomes more intense. The strength of the vital breath nourished gradually in this way, (the soul) in the body, whether it is born from a womb or not, experiences intensely the (many) worldly experiences (bhoga) brought about by (its) karma. (with 28.222–224ab)
>
> (Śiva) also (ca), who bears the (crescent) moon as a jewel in his hair, has said in the scripture called Gahvara that, ‘just as after having constructed a house, the householder occupies it, in the same way, (the Lord who is) the embodied soul (occupies) the body after he has made it. Prior (to that embodied condition,) he is just a subtle radiant pulse (of consciousness) (sphuraṇamātra), devoid of (all) qualities such as action and the like, and is called ‘undifferentiated’ (niṣkala). (When, on the contrary,) the principles of the senses etc. are (operating and so) fully evident (sphuṭa), it is called ‘the differentiated soul’ (sakalātman).’ This and more is said in the venerable Gahvara (tantra). One can read much (more about this) from that. (with 28.224cd–227ab)
