## Mūla

वेधदीक्षा च बहुधा तत्र तत्र निरूपिता ।
सा चाभ्यासवता कार्या येनोर्ध्वोर्ध्वप्रवेशतः ॥ ^mula

vedhadīkṣā ca bahudhā tatra tatra nirūpitā |
sā cābhyāsavatā kāryā yenordhvordhvapraveśataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 13 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 5#^pg395|p. 378]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=395>))
>
> The initiation by piercing (vedha) has been described in various places (in the scriptures) as being of many kinds. It must be performed by (a teacher) who is well-practiced, by whom the disciple is made to enter (progressively) ever higher (levels) and an (outer) validatory sign (pratyaya) certainly (dhruvam) arises, (testifying to) the piercing of (the disciple’s) cakras (cakrasabheda). (with 29.237–238ab)
