# Psalm 75

← [[BCP Psalm 074]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 076]] →

*Confitebimur tibi* · Book Three · Fifteenth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 075]]

**75:1** We give you thanks, O God, we give you thanks, \*  
calling upon your Name and declaring all your wonderful deeds. ^75-1

**75:2** "I will appoint a time," says God; \*  
"I will judge with equity. ^75-2

**75:3** Though the earth and all its inhabitants are quaking, \*  
I will make its pillars fast. ^75-3

**75:4** I will say to the boasters, 'Boast no more,' \*  
and to the wicked, 'Do not toss your horns; ^75-4

**75:5** Do not toss your horns so high, \*  
nor speak with a proud neck.'" ^75-5

**75:6** For judgment is neither from the east nor from the west, \*  
nor yet from the wilderness or the mountains. ^75-6

**75:7** It is God who judges; \*  
he puts down one and lifts up another. ^75-7

**75:8** For in the LORD'S hand there is a cup,  
full of spiced and foaming wine, which he pours out, \*  
and all the wicked of the earth shall drink and drain the dregs. ^75-8

**75:9** But I will rejoice for ever; \*  
I will sing praises to the God of Jacob. ^75-9

**75:10** He shall break off all the horns of the wicked; \*  
but the horns of the righteous shall be exalted. ^75-10
