# 1 Corinthians 2 (NRSVue)

Compare: [[1 Corinthians 02|BSB and SBLGNT]] · [[DRB 1 Corinthians 02|Douay-Rheims]] · [[JANT 1 Corinthians 02|JANT notes]]

## Proclaiming Christ crucified

**2:1** When I came to you, brothers and sisters, I did not come proclaiming the testimony^[Other ancient authorities read *mystery*] of God to you with superior speech or wisdom. ^2-1

**2:2** For I decided to know nothing among you except Jesus Christ and him crucified. ^2-2

**2:3** And I came to you in weakness and in fear and in much trembling. ^2-3

**2:4** My speech and my proclamation were made not with persuasive words of wisdom^[Other ancient authorities read *the persuasiveness of wisdom*] but with a demonstration of the Spirit and of power, ^2-4

**2:5** so that your faith might rest not on human wisdom but on the power of God. ^2-5

## The true wisdom of God

**2:6** Yet among the mature we do speak wisdom, though it is not a wisdom of this age or of the rulers of this age, who are being destroyed. ^2-6

**2:7** But we speak God’s wisdom, a hidden mystery, which God decreed before the ages for our glory ^2-7

**2:8** and which none of the rulers of this age understood, for if they had, they would not have crucified the Lord of glory. ^2-8

**2:9** But, as it is written, “What no eye has seen, nor ear heard, nor the human heart conceived, what God has prepared for those who love him”— ^2-9

**2:10** God has revealed to us through the Spirit, for the Spirit searches everything, even the depths of God. ^2-10

**2:11** For what human knows what is truly human except the human spirit that is within? So also no one comprehends what is truly God’s except the Spirit of God. ^2-11

**2:12** Now we have received not the spirit of the world but the Spirit that is from God, so that we may understand the gifts bestowed on us by God. ^2-12

**2:13** And we speak of these things in words not taught by human wisdom but taught by the Spirit, interpreting spiritual things to those who are spiritual.^[Or *interpreting spiritual things spiritually* or *comparing spiritual things with spiritual*] ^2-13

**2:14** Those who are unspiritual^[Or *natural*] do not receive the gifts of God’s Spirit, for they are foolishness to them, and they are unable to understand them because they are spiritually discerned. ^2-14

**2:15** Those who are spiritual discern all things, and they are themselves subject to no one else’s scrutiny. ^2-15

**2:16** “For who has known the mind of the Lord so as to instruct him?” But we have the mind of Christ. ^2-16
