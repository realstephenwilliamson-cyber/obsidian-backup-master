## Mūla

उक्तं श्रीपूर्वशास्त्रे च स्रुचमापूर्य सर्पिषा ।
कृत्वा शिष्यं तथात्मस्थं मूलमन्त्रमनुस्मरन् ॥ ^mula

uktaṃ śrīpūrvaśāstre ca srucamāpūrya sarpiṣā |
kṛtvā śiṣyaṃ tathātmasthaṃ mūlamantramanusmaran || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 08 - Chapter Seventeen#^pg197|p. 177]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=197>))
>
> Moreover, it is said in Mālinīvijayottara: ‘Once (the teacher) has filled the (sacrificial) ladle with clarified butter and placed the disciple in that way within (his own) Self, he should recollect the root Mantra. Unifying Śiva, (his) Power, (his own) Self, the disciple, the clarified butter, and the fire, undistracted, he should slowly go to the End of the Twelve (and enter it). There, having suspended the breath, meditating on (Śiva), with and without (His) energies (sakalaniṣkala), he should abide there unperturbed, until (all) the clarified butter is consumed’. (with 17.88–90)
