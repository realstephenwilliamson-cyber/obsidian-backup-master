# Matthew 28 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Matthew 27|← Matthew 27]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · —

Compare: [[Matthew 28|BSB and SBLGNT]] · [[DRB Matthew 28|Douay-Rheims]] · [[JANT Matthew 28|JANT notes]]

## The resurrection of Jesus

**28:1** After the Sabbath, as the first day of the week was dawning, Mary Magdalene and the other Mary went to see the tomb. ^28-1

**28:2** And suddenly there was a great earthquake, for an angel of the Lord, descending from heaven, came and rolled back the stone and sat on it. ^28-2

**28:3** His appearance was like lightning and his clothing white as snow. ^28-3

**28:4** For fear of him the guards shook and became like dead men. ^28-4

**28:5** But the angel said to the women, “Do not be afraid, for I know that you are looking for Jesus who was crucified. ^28-5

**28:6** He is not here, for he has been raised, as he said. Come, see the place where he^[Other ancient authorities read *the Lord*] lay. ^28-6

**28:7** Then go quickly and tell his disciples, ‘He has been raised from the dead,^[Other ancient authorities lack *from the dead*] and indeed he is going ahead of you to Galilee; there you will see him.’ This is my message for you.” ^28-7

**28:8** So they left the tomb quickly with fear and great joy and ran to tell his disciples. ^28-8

**28:9** Suddenly Jesus met them and said, “Greetings!” And they came to him, took hold of his feet, and worshiped him. ^28-9

**28:10** Then Jesus said to them, “Do not be afraid; go and tell my brothers and sisters to go to Galilee; there they will see me.” ^28-10

## The report of the guard

**28:11** While they were going, some of the guard went into the city and told the chief priests everything that had happened. ^28-11

**28:12** After the priests^[Gk *they*] had assembled with the elders, they devised a plan to give a large sum of money to the soldiers, ^28-12

**28:13** telling them, “You must say, ‘His disciples came by night and stole him away while we were asleep.’ ^28-13

**28:14** If this comes to the governor’s ears, we will satisfy him and keep you out of trouble.” ^28-14

**28:15** So they took the money and did as they were directed. And this story is still told among the Judeans to this day. ^28-15

## The commissioning of the disciples

**28:16** Now the eleven disciples went to Galilee, to the mountain to which Jesus had directed them. ^28-16

**28:17** When they saw him, they worshiped him, but they doubted. ^28-17

**28:18** And Jesus came and said to them, “All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to me. ^28-18

**28:19** Go therefore and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit ^28-19

**28:20** and teaching them to obey everything that I have commanded you. And remember, I am with you always, to the end of the age.”^[Other ancient authorities add *Amen*] ^28-20
