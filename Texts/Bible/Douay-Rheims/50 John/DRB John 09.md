# John 9 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB John 08|← John 8]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB John 10|John 10 →]]

Compare: [[John 09|BSB and SBLGNT]]

**9:1** And Jesus passing by, saw a man, who was blind from his birth: ^9-1

**9:2** And his disciples asked him: Rabbi, who hath sinned, this man, or his parents, that he should be born blind? ^9-2

**9:3** Jesus answered: Neither hath this man sinned, nor his parents; but that the works of God should be made manifest in him. ^9-3

**9:4** I must work the works of him that sent me, whilst it is day: the night cometh, when no man can work. ^9-4

**9:5** As long as I am in the world, I am the light of the world. ^9-5

**9:6** When he had said these things, he spat on the ground, and made clay of the spittle, and spread the clay on his eyes, ^9-6

**9:7** And said to him: Go, wash in the pool of Siloe, which is interpreted, Sent. He went therefore, and washed, and he came seeing. ^9-7

**9:8** The neighbours therefore, and they who had seen him before that he was a beggar, said: Is not this he that sat and begged? Some said: This is he. ^9-8

**9:9** But others said: No, but he is like him. But he said: I am he. ^9-9

**9:10** They said therefore to him: How were thy eyes opened? ^9-10

**9:11** He answered: That man that is called Jesus made clay, and anointed my eyes, and said to me: Go to the pool of Siloe, and wash. And I went, I washed, and I see. ^9-11

**9:12** And they said to him: Where is he? He saith: I know not. ^9-12

**9:13** They bring him that had been blind to the Pharisees. ^9-13

**9:14** Now it was the sabbath, when Jesus made the clay, and opened his eyes. ^9-14

**9:15** Again therefore the Pharisees asked him, how he had received his sight. But he said to them: He put clay upon my eyes, and I washed, and I see. ^9-15

**9:16** Some therefore of the Pharisees said: This man is not of God, who keepeth not the sabbath. But others said: How can a man that is a sinner do such miracles? And there was a division among them. ^9-16

**9:17** They say therefore to the blind man again: What sayest thou of him that hath opened they eyes? And he said: He is a prophet. ^9-17

**9:18** The Jews then did not believe concerning him, that he had been blind, and had received his sight, until they called the parents of him that had received his sight, ^9-18

**9:19** And asked them, saying: Is this your son, who you say was born blind? How then doth he now see? ^9-19

**9:20** His parents answered them, and said: We know that this is our son, and that he was born blind: ^9-20

**9:21** But how he now seeth, we know not; or who hath opened his eyes, we know not: ask himself: he is of age, let him speak for himself. ^9-21

**9:22** These things his parents said, because they feared the Jews: for the Jews had already agreed among themselves, that if any man should confess him to be Christ, he should be put out of the synagogue. ^9-22

**9:23** Therefore did his parents say: He is of age, ask himself. ^9-23

**9:24** They therefore called the man again that had been blind, and said to him: Give glory to God. We know that this man is a sinner. ^9-24

**9:25** He said therefore to them: If he be a sinner, I know not: one thing I know, that whereas I was blind, now I see. ^9-25

**9:26** They said then to him: What did he to thee? How did he open thy eyes? ^9-26

**9:27** He answered them: I have told you already, and you have heard: why would you hear it again? will you also become his disciples? ^9-27

**9:28** They reviled him therefore, and said: Be thou his disciple; but we are the disciples of Moses. ^9-28

**9:29** We know that God spoke to Moses: but as to this man, we know not from whence he is. ^9-29

**9:30** The man answered, and said to them: Why, herein is a wonderful thing, that you know not from whence he is, and he hath opened my eyes. ^9-30

**9:31** Now we know that God doth not hear sinners: but if a man be a server of God, and doth his will, him he heareth. ^9-31

**9:32** From the beginning of the world it hath not been heard, that any man hath opened the eyes of one born blind. ^9-32

**9:33** Unless this man were of God, he could not do any thing. ^9-33

**9:34** They answered, and said to him: Thou wast wholly born in sins, and dost thou teach us? And they cast him out. ^9-34

**9:35** Jesus heard that they had cast him out: and when he had found him, he said to him: Dost thou believe in the Son of God? ^9-35

**9:36** He answered, and said: Who is he, Lord, that I may believe in him? ^9-36

**9:37** And Jesus said to him: Thou hast both seen him; and it is he that talketh with thee. ^9-37

**9:38** And he said: I believe, Lord. And falling down, he adored him. ^9-38

**9:39** And Jesus said: For judgment I am come into this world; that they who see not, may see; and they who see, may become blind. ^9-39

**9:40** And some of the Pharisees, who were with him, heard: and they said unto him: Are we also blind? ^9-40

**9:41** Jesus said to them: If you were blind, you should not have sin: but now you say: We see. Your sin remaineth. ^9-41
