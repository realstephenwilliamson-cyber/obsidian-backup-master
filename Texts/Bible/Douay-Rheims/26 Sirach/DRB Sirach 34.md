# Sirach 34 (Douay-Rheims)

*Ecclesiasticus 34 in the Douay-Rheims*

[[DRB Sirach 33|← Sirach 33]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Sirach 35|Sirach 35 →]]

**34:1** The hopes of a man that is void of understanding are vain and deceitful: and dreams lift up fools. ^34-1

**34:2** The man that giveth heed to lying visions, is like to him that catcheth at a shadow, and followeth after the wind. ^34-2

**34:3** The vision of dreams is the resemblance of one thing to another: as when a man’s likeness is before the face of a man. ^34-3

**34:4** What can be made clean by the unclean? and what truth can come from that which is false? ^34-4

**34:5** Deceitful divinations and lying omens and the dreams of evildoers, are vanity: ^34-5

**34:6** And the heart fancieth as that of a woman in travail: except it be a vision sent forth from the most High, set no thy heart upon them. ^34-6

**34:7** For dreams have deceived many, and they have failed that put their trust in them. ^34-7

**34:8** The word of the law shall be fulfilled without a lie, and wisdom shall be made plain in the mouth of the faithful. ^34-8

**34:9** What doth he know, that hath not been tried? A man that hath much experience, shall think of many things: and he that hath learned many things, shall shew forth understanding. ^34-9

**34:10** He that hath no experience, knoweth little: and he that hath been experienced in many things, multiplieth prudence. ^34-10

**34:11** He that hath not been tried, what manner of things doth he know? he that hath been surprised, shall abound with subtlety. ^34-11

**34:12** I have seen many things by travelling, and many customs of things. ^34-12

**34:13** Sometimes I have been in danger of death for these things, and I have been delivered by the grace of God. ^34-13

**34:14** The spirit of those that fear God; is sought after, and by his regard shall be blessed. ^34-14

**34:15** For their hope is on him that saveth them, and the eyes of God are upon them that love him. ^34-15

**34:16** He that feareth the Lord shall tremble at nothing, and shall not be afraid for he is his hope. ^34-16

**34:17** The soul of him that feareth the Lord is blessed. ^34-17

**34:18** To whom doth he look, and who in his strength? ^34-18

**34:19** The eyes of the Lord are upon them that fear him, he is their powerful protector, and strong stay, a defence from the heat, and a cover from the sun at noon, ^34-19

**34:20** A preservation from stumbling, and a help from falling; he raiseth up the soul, and enlighteneth the eyes, and giveth health, and life, and blessing. ^34-20

**34:21** The offering of him that sacrificeth of a thing wrongfully gotten, is stained, and the mockeries of the unjust are not acceptable. ^34-21

**34:22** The Lord is only for them that wait upon him in the way of truth and justice. ^34-22

**34:23** The most High approveth not the gifts of the wicked: neither hath he respect to the oblations of the unjust, nor will he be pacified for sins by the multitude of their sacrifices. ^34-23

**34:24** He that offereth sacrifice of the goods of the poor, is as one that sacrificeth the son in the presence of his father. ^34-24

**34:25** The bread of the needy, is the life of the poor: he that defraudeth them thereof, is a man of blood. ^34-25

**34:26** He that taketh away the bread gotten by sweat, is like him that killeth his neighbour. ^34-26

**34:27** He that sheddeth blood, and he that defraudeth the labourer of his hire, are brothers. ^34-27

**34:28** When one buildeth up, and another pulleth down: what profit have they but the labour? ^34-28

**34:29** When one prayeth, and another curseth: whose voice will God hear? ^34-29

**34:30** He that washeth himself after touching the dead, if he toucheth him again, what doth his washing avail? ^34-30

**34:31** So a man that fasteth for his sins, and doth the same again, what doth his humbling himself profit him? who will hear his prayer? ^34-31
