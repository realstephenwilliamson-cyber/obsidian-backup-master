## Chapter 9

**9:1** Job said in reply: ^9-1

**9:2** Indeed I know that it is so:  
Man cannot win a suit against God. ^9-2

**9:3** If he insisted on a trial with Him,  
He would not answer one charge in a thousand. ^9-3

**9:4** Wise of heart and mighty in power—  
Who ever challenged Him and came out whole?— ^9-4

**9:5** Him who moves mountains without their knowing it,  
Who overturns them in His anger; ^9-5

**9:6** Who shakes the earth from its place,  
Till its pillars quake; ^9-6

**9:7** Who commands the sun not to shine;  
Who seals up the stars; ^9-7

**9:8** Who by Himself spread out the heavens,  
And trod on the back of the sea; ^9-8

**9:9** Who made the Bear[^1] and Orion,  
Pleiades, and the chambers of the south wind; ^9-9

**9:10** Who performs great deeds which cannot be fathomed,  
And wondrous things without number. ^9-10

**9:11** He passes me by—I do not see Him;  
He goes by me, but I do not perceive Him. ^9-11

**9:12** He snatches away—who can stop Him?  
Who can say to Him, “What are You doing?” ^9-12

**9:13** God does not restrain His anger;  
Under Him Rahab’s[^2] helpers sink down. ^9-13

**9:14** How then can I answer Him,  
Or choose my arguments against Him? ^9-14

**9:15** Though I were in the right, I could not speak out,  
But I would plead for mercy with my judge. ^9-15

**9:16** If I summoned Him and He responded,  
I do not believe He would lend me His ear. ^9-16

**9:17** For He crushes me for a hair;[^3]  
He wounds me much for no cause. ^9-17

**9:18** He does not let me catch my breath,  
But sates me with bitterness. ^9-18

**9:19** If a trial of strength—He is the strong one;  
If a trial in court—who will summon Him for me? ^9-19

**9:20** Though I were innocent,  
My mouth would condemn me;  
Though I were blameless, He would prove me crooked. ^9-20

**9:21** I am blameless—I am distraught;  
I am sick of life. ^9-21

**9:22** It is all one; therefore I say,  
“He destroys the blameless and the guilty.” ^9-22

**9:23** When suddenly a scourge brings death,  
He mocks as the innocent fail. ^9-23

**9:24** The earth is handed over to the wicked one;  
He covers the eyes of its judges.  
If it is not He, then who? ^9-24

**9:25** My days fly swifter than a runner;  
They flee without seeing happiness; ^9-25

**9:26** They pass like reed-boats,  
Like an eagle swooping onto its prey. ^9-26

**9:27** If I say, “I will forget my complaint;  
Abandon my sorrow[^4] and be diverted,” ^9-27

**9:28** I remain in dread of all my suffering;  
I know that You will not acquit me. ^9-28

**9:29** It will be I who am in the wrong;  
Why then should I waste effort? ^9-29

**9:30** If I washed with soap,  
Cleansed my hands with lye, ^9-30

**9:31** You would dip me in muck  
Till my clothes would abhor me. ^9-31

**9:32** He is not a man, like me, that I can answer Him,  
That we can go to law together. ^9-32

**9:33** No arbiter is between us  
To lay his hand on us both. ^9-33

**9:34** If He would only take His rod away from me  
And not let His terror frighten me, ^9-34

**9:35** Then I would speak out without fear of Him;  
For I know myself not to be so. ^9-35

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: A primeval monster.
[^3]: With Targum and Peshitta; or “with a storm.”
[^4]: Lit. “face.”
