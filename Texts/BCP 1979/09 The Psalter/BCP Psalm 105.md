# Psalm 105

← [[BCP Psalm 104]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 106]] →

** · Book Four · Twenty-first Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 105]]

**Part I** Confitemini Domino

**105:1** Give thanks to the LORD and call upon his Name; \*  
make known his deeds among the peoples. ^105-1

**105:2** Sing to him, sing praises to him, \*  
and speak of all his marvelous works. ^105-2

**105:3** Glory in his holy Name; \*  
let the hearts of those who seek the LORD rejoice. ^105-3

**105:4** Search for the LORD and his strength; \*  
continually seek his face. ^105-4

**105:5** Remember the marvels he has done, \*  
his wonders and the judgments of his mouth, ^105-5

**105:6** O offspring of Abraham his servant, \*  
O children of Jacob his chosen. ^105-6

**105:7** He is the LORD our God; \*  
his judgments prevail in all the world. ^105-7

**105:8** He has always been mindful of his covenant, \*  
the promise he made for a thousand generations: ^105-8

**105:9** The covenant he made with Abraham, \*  
the oath that he swore to Isaac, ^105-9

**105:10** Which he established as a statute for Jacob, \*  
an everlasting covenant for Israel, ^105-10

**105:11** Saying, "To you will I give the land of Canaan \*  
to be your allotted inheritance." ^105-11

[p. 739] ^pg739

**105:12** When they were few in number, \*  
of little account, and sojourners in the land, ^105-12

**105:13** Wandering from nation to nation \*  
and from one kingdom to another, ^105-13

**105:14** He let no one oppress them \*  
and rebuked kings for their sake, ^105-14

**105:15** Saying, "Do not touch my anointed \*  
and do my prophets no harm." ^105-15

**105:16** Then he called for a famine in the land \*  
and destroyed the supply of bread. ^105-16

**105:17** He sent a man before them, \*  
Joseph, who was sold as a slave. ^105-17

**105:18** They bruised his feet in fetters; \*  
his neck they put in an iron collar. ^105-18

**105:19** Until his prediction came to pass, \*  
the word of the LORD tested him. ^105-19

**105:20** The king sent and released him; \*  
the ruler of the peoples set him free. ^105-20

**105:21** He set him as a master over his household, \*  
as a ruler over all his possessions, ^105-21

**105:22** To instruct his princes according to his will \*  
and to teach his elders wisdom. ^105-22

**Psalm 105: Part II** Et intravit Israel

**105:23** Israel came into Egypt, \*  
and Jacob became a sojourner in the land of Ham. ^105-23

[p. 740] ^pg740

**105:24** The LORD made his people exceedingly fruitful; \*  
he made them stronger than their enemies; ^105-24

**105:25** Whose heart he turned, so that they hated his people, \*  
and dealt unjustly with his servants. ^105-25

**105:26** He sent Moses his servant, \*  
and Aaron whom he had chosen. ^105-26

**105:27** They worked his signs among them, \*  
and portents in the land of Ham. ^105-27

**105:28** He sent darkness, and it grew dark; \*  
but the Egyptians rebelled against his words. ^105-28

**105:29** He turned their waters into blood \*  
and caused their fish to die. ^105-29

**105:30** Their land was overrun by frogs, \*  
in the very chambers of their kings. ^105-30

**105:31** He spoke, and there came swarms of insects \*  
and gnats within all their borders. ^105-31

**105:32** He gave them hailstones instead of rain, \*  
and flames of fire throughout their land. ^105-32

**105:33** He blasted their vines and their fig trees \*  
and shattered every tree in their country. ^105-33

**105:34** He spoke, and the locust came, \*  
and young locusts without number, ^105-34

**105:35** Which ate up all the green plants in their land \*  
and devoured the fruit of their soil. ^105-35

**105:36** He struck down the firstborn of their land, \*  
the firstfruits of all their strength. ^105-36

**105:37** He led out his people with silver and gold; \*  
in all their tribes there was not one that stumbled. ^105-37

[p. 741] ^pg741

**105:38** Egypt was glad of their going, \*  
because they were afraid of them. ^105-38

**105:39** He spread out a cloud for a covering \*  
and a fire to give light in the night season. ^105-39

**105:40** They asked, and quails appeared, \*  
and he satisfied them with bread from heaven. ^105-40

**105:41** He opened the rock, and water flowed, \*  
so the river ran in the dry places. ^105-41

**105:42** For God remembered his holy word \*  
and Abraham his servant. ^105-42

**105:43** So he led forth his people with gladness, \*  
his chosen with shouts of joy. ^105-43

**105:44** He gave his people the lands of the nations, \*  
and they took the fruit of others' toil, ^105-44

**105:45** That they might keep his statutes \*  
and observe his laws.  
Hallelujah! ^105-45
