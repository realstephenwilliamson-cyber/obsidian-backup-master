## Mūla

क्रमान्मुख्यातिमात्रेण विधिनैत्यन्ततः शिवम् ।
अन्ये यियासुरित्यादिग्रन्थं प्राग्ग्रन्थसङ्गतम् ॥ ^mula

kramānmukhyātimātreṇa vidhinaityantataḥ śivam |
anye yiyāsurityādigranthaṃ prāggranthasaṅgatam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 09 - Chapter Thirteen, part 5#^pg300|p. 278]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=300>))
>
> (The disciple’s) desire for worldly benefits grows progressively more primary, according to whether (the descent of the power of grace is) intensely mild, moderately mild, or mildly mild. (However, he too) ultimately attains Śiva by virtue of the rite (of initiation). (with 13.245cd–246ab)
>
> According to others, the line, ‘he who desires (to go to a true teacher)’ etc. is linked to the previous ones, and is considered to refer to the moderately intense descent of the power (of grace). (with 13.246cd–247ab)

*Lead-in:* श्रीपूर्वशास्त्रीयमेव ग्रन्थं मतान्तरेण व्याचष्टे --

> अन्ये यियासुरित्यादिग्रन्थं प्राग्ग्रन्थसङ्गतम् ॥
> कुर्वन्ति मध्यतीव्राख्यशक्तिसंपातगोचरम् ।

प्राग्ग्रन्थसङ्गतमिति प्राग्ग्रन्थेन "अज्ञानेन सह"इत्यादिना सङ्गतं संबद्धम् -- इत्यर्थः । अत्रैव विशेषणद्वारेण हेतुर्मध्यतीव्राख्यशक्तिसंपातगोचरमिति ॥
