## Mūla

अहं शिवो मन्त्रमयः सङ्कल्पा मे तदात्मकाः ।
तज्जं च कुण्डवह्न्यादि शिवात्मेति स्फुटं स्मरेत् ॥ ^mula

ahaṃ śivo mantramayaḥ saṅkalpā me tadātmakāḥ |
tajjaṃ ca kuṇḍavahnyādi śivātmeti sphuṭaṃ smaret || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 07 - Chapter Fifteen, part 6#^pg260|p. 242]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=260>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 15.397cd–398ab.
>
> One should recollect clearly that “I am Śiva, made of Mantras; my intentions (saṁkalpa) are of that (same) nature; (so too,) the pit, the fire and the rest that born from them are Śiva by nature.” Thus, even in that case, (one should perform the rite of) purification to a greater or lesser extent, whether it is being done or has been done, by observing whether (and to what degree this thought) is firm (and stable) or not. (with 15.395–396)
