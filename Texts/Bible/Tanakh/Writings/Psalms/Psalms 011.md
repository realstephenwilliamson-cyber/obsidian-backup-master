## Chapter 11

**11:1** For the leader. Of David.  
In the LORD I take refuge;  
how can you say to me,  
“Take to the hills like a bird![^1] ^11-1

**11:2** For see, the wicked bend the bow,  
they set their arrow on the string  
to shoot from the shadows at the upright. ^11-2

**11:3** When the foundations are destroyed,  
what can the righteous man do?”[^2] ^11-3

**11:4** The LORD is in His holy palace;  
the LORD—His throne is in heaven;  
His eyes behold, His gaze searches mankind. ^11-4

**11:5** The LORD seeks out the righteous man,  
but loathes the wicked one who loves injustice. ^11-5

**11:6** He will rain down upon the wicked blazing coals and sulfur;  
a scorching wind shall be their lot.[^3] ^11-6

**11:7** For the LORD is righteous;  
He loves righteous deeds;  
the upright shall behold His face. ^11-7

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain; lit. “your hill, bird!”
[^2]: Or “For the foundations are destroyed; what has the Righteous One done?” Or “If the foundations are destroyed, what has the righteous man accomplished?”
[^3]: Lit. “the portion of their cup.”
