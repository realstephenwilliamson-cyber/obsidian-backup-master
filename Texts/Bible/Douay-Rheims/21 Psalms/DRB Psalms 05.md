# Psalms 5 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 04|← Psalms 4]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 06|Psalms 6 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 05|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**5:1** Unto the end, for her that obtaineth the inheritance. A psalm of David. ^5-1

**5:2** Give ear, O Lord, to my words, understand my cry. ^5-2

**5:3** Hearken to the voice of my prayer, O my King and my God. ^5-3

**5:4** For to thee will I pray: O Lord, in the morning thou shalt hear my voice. ^5-4

**5:5** In the morning I will stand before thee, and will see: because thou art not a God that willest iniquity. ^5-5

**5:6** Neither shall the wicked dwell near thee: nor shall the unjust abide before thy eyes. ^5-6

**5:7** Thou hatest all the workers of iniquity: Thou wilt destroy all that speak a lie. The bloody and the deceitful man the Lord will abhor. ^5-7

**5:8** But as for me in the multitude of thy mercy, I will come into thy house; I will worship towards thy holy temple, in thy fear. ^5-8

**5:9** Conduct me, O Lord, in thy justice: because of my enemies, direct my way in thy sight. ^5-9

**5:10** For there is no truth in their mouth; their heart is vain. ^5-10

**5:11** Their throat is an open sepulchre: they dealt deceitfully with their tongues: judge them, O God. Let them fall from their devices: according to the multitude of their wickedness cast them out: for they have provoked thee, O Lord. ^5-11

**5:12** But let all them be glad that hope in thee: they shall rejoice for ever, and thou shalt dwell in them. And all they that love thy name shall glory in thee: ^5-12

**5:13** For thou wilt bless the just. O Lord, thou hast crowned us, as with a shield of thy good will. ^5-13
