# LXX Psalms 23

← [[LXX Psalms 22]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 24]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 024]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 024]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**23:1** A Psalm for David on the first day of the week. The earth is the Lord's and the fulness thereof; the world, and all that dwell in it.  ^23-1

**23:2** He has founded it upon the seas, and prepared it upon the rivers.  ^23-2

**23:3** Who shall go up to the mountain of the Lord, and who shall stand in his holy place?  ^23-3

**23:4** He that is innocent in his hands and pure in his heart; who has not lifted up his soul to vanity, nor sworn deceitfully to his neighbour.[^1]  ^23-4

**23:5** He shall receive a blessing from the Lord, and mercy from God his Saviour.  ^23-5

**23:6** This is the generation of them that seek him, that seek the face of the God of Jacob. Pause.  ^23-6

**23:7** Lift up your gates, ye princes, and be ye lifted up, ye everlasting doors; and the king of glory shall come in.  ^23-7

**23:8** Who is this king of glory? the Lord strong and mighty, the Lord mighty in battle.  ^23-8

**23:9** Lift up your gates, ye princes; and be ye lift up, ye everlasting doors; and the king of glory shall come in.  ^23-9

**23:10** Who is this king of glory? The Lord of hosts, he is this king of glory.  ^23-10

[^1]: 23:4 *Or,* against.
