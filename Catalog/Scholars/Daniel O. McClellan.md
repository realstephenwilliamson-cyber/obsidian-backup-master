# Daniel O. McClellan

A public-facing scholar of the Bible and religion with a PhD from the University of Exeter, Honorary Fellow of the Edward Cadbury Centre at the University of Birmingham; his research is on conceptualizations of deity, scripture and religious identity through cognitive linguistics and the cognitive science of religion ([University of Birmingham](https://www.birmingham.ac.uk/staff/profiles/tr/mcclellan-daniel); [his site](https://danielomcclellan.com/about)).
