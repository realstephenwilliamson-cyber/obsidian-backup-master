# ἀσιτία

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · noun

**Dodson:** abstinence, a fast — either: lack or wheat, lack of food (the literal meaning), or: abstinence from food, a fast, loss of appetite, sea-sickness (the extended meaning). (Strong's 776; headword ἀσιτία, ας, ἡ)
