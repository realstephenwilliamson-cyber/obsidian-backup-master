## Mūla

महाप्रकाशस्तत्तेन मयि सर्वमिदं जगत् ।
न च तत्केनचिद्बाह्यप्रतिबिम्बवदर्पितम् ॥ ^mula

mahāprakāśastattena mayi sarvamidaṃ jagat |
na ca tatkenacidbāhyapratibimbavadarpitam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 05 - Chapter Sixteen, part 1#^pg62|p. 42]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=62>))
>
> (The insight) that “I am the supreme reality” is in no circumstances like (the perception of) a jar. “This is the Great Light (of consciousness), and so all this (universe) is within me, and that is not deposited (within me) by some external (reality) as is a reflection (in mirror). I am the author of this (creation), I preside over it and it is not dependent on (anything) else. Purified thus by the awareness (saṁvedana) of the great pervasion (mahāvyāpti) (of consciousness), which is such, a creature who has become my equal is said to be liberated.” (with 16.90cd–93ab)
