### 3.1–26: God is just, and the one who justifies

*[[NRSVue Romans 03#^3-1|Text]], p. 292* ^3-1

**[[NRSVue Romans 03#^3-1|1]]:** The diatribe continues. The interlocutor does not appear to be a Jew, since v 2 refers to the Jews as “they.” ^3-1-2

**[[NRSVue Romans 03#^3-2|2]]:** *The Jews,* lit., “they.” *The oracles*, or words/prophecies of God; see discussions in 9.30–10.21. ^3-2

**[[NRSVue Romans 03#^3-4|4]]:** *Liar . . . true*, even if we all are shown to be lying, God will be shown to be true. *As it is written,* [[Psalms 051#^51-6|Ps 51.6]]\[50.6 LXX\]. See also [[Psalms 116#^116-11|Ps 116.11]]\[LXX 115.2\]; 1QS 11.9–14. ^3-4

**[[NRSVue Romans 03#^3-5|5]]:** *Injustice* . . . *justice,* that our actions are shown to be unjust validates the idea of justice, since we accept *the justice of God*, the standard by which we are judged. *I speak in a human way*, Paul makes clear that he is not questioning divine justice or encouraging evil so that this justice might be manifested. ^3-5

**[[NRSVue Romans 03#^3-7|7–8]]:** *Condemned* . . . *Let us do evil . . .* Paul condemns a misrepresentation of his teaching. ^3-7

**[[NRSVue Romans 03#^3-9|9]]:** *Under the power of sin*, lit., “are under sin”; see 6.23. ^3-9

**[[NRSVue Romans 03#^3-10|10–18]]:** Paul cites several verses from various books to illustrate his point; he must be aware of contrary passages (from among the Psalms esp.) that indicate the interlocutor’s devotion to righteousness and claim to live accordingly. Paul’s argument should not be universalized but understood as a polemical diatribe against hypothetical accusers; see, e.g., [[Psalms 040#^40-17|Ps 40.16]]; 97.10–12; 119; and Paul’s own self-confession of blamelessness in [[Philippians 03#^3-6|Phil 3.6]]. ^3-10

**[[NRSVue Romans 03#^3-10|10]]:** See [[Ecclesiastes 07#^7-20|Eccl 7.20]]; see also [[Psalms 014#^14-3|Ps 14.3]]; [[Psalms 053#^53-4|53.4]]; 1QH 9.14. ^3-10-2

**[[NRSVue Romans 03#^3-11|11]]:** See [[Psalms 014#^14-2|Ps 14.2]]; cf. 53.3. ^3-11

**[[NRSVue Romans 03#^3-12|12]]:** See [[Psalms 014#^14-3|Ps 14.3]]. ^3-12

**[[NRSVue Romans 03#^3-13|13–14]]:** See [[Psalms 005#^5-10|Ps 5.9]]; 10.7; [[Psalms 140#^140-4|140.3]]. ^3-13

**[[NRSVue Romans 03#^3-15|15–17]]:** See [[Isaiah 59#^59-7|Isa 59.7–8]]; cf. [[Proverbs 01#^1-16|Prov 1.16]]. ^3-15

**[[NRSVue Romans 03#^3-18|18]]:** See [[Psalms 036#^36-2|Ps 36.1]]. ^3-18

**[[NRSVue Romans 03#^3-19|19]]:** *Those who are under the law*, i.e., to Israel, entrusted to announce God’s words to the nations. *Every mouth may be silenced*, no one has the right to speak against God’s righteousness/justice. ^3-19

**[[NRSVue Romans 03#^3-20|20]]:** Paul develops the psalmist’s humility, not his own, in [[Psalms 143#^143-2|Ps 143.2]], and God’s faithfulness in [[Psalms 143#^143-1|Ps 143.1]]; cf. *1 En.* 81.5–6; 1QS 11. *By deeds prescribed by the law*, lit., “from works/rites of (a) law/convention/tradition”: Paul appears to be referring to cir- cumcision for marking the people of God, based on the argument leading up to ch 4. An alternative translation is: “Because as a result (of completing transformation that results) from rites of (related to the) convention (culminating in circumcision) not all flesh will be made righteous before him, for through (completing) circumcision comes knowledge of sin (i.e., after becoming dedicated to God’s righteousness by the rite of circumcision, a former Gentile, now as a Jew, becomes culpable to live according to the conventions of righteousness under Torah).” ^3-20

**[[NRSVue Romans 03#^3-21|21]]:** *Apart from law*, or independent of \[undertaking\] a convention \[of circumcised, Jewish identity\]; *attested by the law and the prophets* (i.e., by the “oracles/words of God” which the Jews are to declare to the nations, see 3.2; 10.14–15). ^3-21

**[[NRSVue Romans 03#^3-22|22]]:** Or the righteousness of God through Jesus Christ’s faithfulness for all who are faithful (to it). *Distinction*, Gk “diastolē,” better translated “utterance” and so “outcry”; the verb can mean both “distinguish” and “command” and can carry the sense of ““discrimination”“ rather than merely difference; cf. Heb 12.20. “Outcry” brings out the point of v 19, that everyone is “silenced,” i.e., everyone is culpable; see 10.12. ^3-22

**[[NRSVue Romans 03#^3-24|24]]:** *They are now justified by his grace as a gift*, lit., “they are being justified freely by his benefaction,” see 1.5; *redemption*, in the sense of ransoming/freeing of slaves; here they are ransomed from sin and death. ^3-24

**[[NRSVue Romans 03#^3-25|25]]:** *Sacrifice of atonement*, lit., “conciliation” (i.e., \[his noble death or voluntary martyrdom, through faith, in conformity with divine will\] gaining favorable standing; cf. [[LXX 4 Maccabees 17#^17-21|4 Macc 17.21–22]]), or (less likely) as a place of expiation (his sacrifice of substitutionary atonement, i.e., that the Christ, in dying, pays the penalty for human sin), drawing upon the language of Yom Kippur ([[Torah 29 - Achrei Mot#^16-13|Lev 16.13–15]]; Heb “mercy seat” \[‘kappōret’\]). *By his blood*, lit., “in his blood” (i.e., by his death); *effective* not in Gk; *through faith*, or through faithfulness. ^3-25

**[[NRSVue Romans 03#^3-26|26]]:** *And that he justifies the one who has faith in Jesus*, or “and that he \[God\] because of the faithfulness of Jesus justifies \[every\]one,” whether Jew or Greek. ^3-26

### 3.27–31: God’s faithfulness is for all

*[[NRSVue Romans 03#^3-27|Text]], p. 293* · The issue throughout Romans is that the faithfulness of God includes non-Jews (foreskinned) as well as Jews (circumcised) as equal recipients of God’s benefaction in Christ, see 1.5. ^3-27

**[[NRSVue Romans 03#^3-27|27]]:** *Then what becomes of boasting?* i.e., for being identified as one of the righteous ones. *By that of works?* i.e., by being circumcised. *Law of faith*, or by faithfulness. ^3-27-2

**[[NRSVue Romans 03#^3-28|28]]:** *By faith*, or by faithfulness; *apart from works prescribed by the law*, without circumcision (in this case proselyte conversion); *prescribed by the*, not in Gk. ^3-28

**[[NRSVue Romans 03#^3-29|29–30]]:** *Gentiles* are justified by faithfulness to God’s revelation in Christ apart from becoming Jews. See “God Is One for All Humanity,” p. 294. ^3-29

**[[NRSVue Romans 03#^3-31|31]]:** Better: *Do we then overthrow* (or make obsolete) *the law* (i.e., of circumcision, by which Israel has been marked and entrusted to announce God’s word to the nations) *by this faith*? W*e uphold* (establish) *the law*, that is, the ideal that the circumcision of Jews is an advantage for Jews. For the Jew, Christ is the goal of Torah righteousness directly; for the non-Jew, Christ is the goal of Torah righteousness indirectly, for Israel announces Torah righteousness for Jew and non-Jew; see 10.4. ^3-31
