# Proverbs 19

← [[Proverbs 18]] · [[Tanakh]] · [[Proverbs 20]] →

Compare: [[NRSVue Proverbs 19|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 19

**19:1** Better a poor man who lives blamelessly  
Than one who speaks perversely and is a dullard. ^19-1

**19:2** A person without knowledge is surely not good;  
He who moves hurriedly blunders. ^19-2

**19:3** A man’s folly subverts his way,  
And his heart rages against the LORD. ^19-3

**19:4** Wealth makes many friends,  
But a poor man loses his last friend. ^19-4

**19:5** A false witness will not go unpunished;  
He who testifies lies will not escape. ^19-5

**19:6** Many court the favor of a great man,  
And all are the friends of a dispenser of gifts. ^19-6

**19:7** All the brothers of a poor man despise him;  
How much more is he shunned by his friends!  
He who pursues words—they are of no avail.[^1] ^19-7

**19:8** He who acquires wisdom is his own best friend;  
He preserves understanding and attains happiness. ^19-8

**19:9** A false witness will not go unpunished;  
He who testifies falsely is doomed. ^19-9

**19:10** Luxury is not fitting for a dullard,  
Much less that a servant rule over princes. ^19-10

**19:11** A man shows intelligence by his forebearance;  
It is his glory when he overlooks an offense. ^19-11

**19:12** The rage of a king is like the roar of a lion;  
His favor is like dew upon the grass. ^19-12

**19:13** A stupid son is a calamity to his father;  
The nagging of a wife is like the endless dripping of water. ^19-13

**19:14** Property and riches are bequeathed by fathers,  
But an efficient wife comes from the LORD. ^19-14

**19:15** Laziness induces sleep,  
And a negligent person will go hungry. ^19-15

**19:16** He who has regard for his life pays regard to commandments;  
He who is heedless of his ways will die. ^19-16

**19:17** He who is generous to the poor makes a loan to the LORD;  
He will repay him his due. ^19-17

**19:18** Discipline your son while there is still hope,  
And[^2] do not set your heart on his destruction.[^3] ^19-18

**19:19** A hot-tempered man incurs punishment;  
If you try to save him you will only make it worse.[^4] ^19-19

**19:20** Listen to advice and accept discipline  
In order that you may be wise in the end. ^19-20

**19:21** Many designs are in a man’s mind,  
But it is the LORD’s plan that is accomplished. ^19-21

**19:22** Greed is a reproach to a man;[^5]  
Better be poor than a liar. ^19-22

**19:23** He who fears the LORD earns life;  
He shall abide in contentment,[^6]  
Free from misfortune. ^19-23

**19:24** The lazy man buries his hand in the bowl;  
He will not even bring it to his mouth. ^19-24

**19:25** Beat the scoffer and the simple will become clever;  
Reprove an intelligent man and he gains knowledge. ^19-25

**19:26** A son who causes shame and disgrace  
Plunders his father, puts his mother to flight. ^19-26

**19:27** My son, cease to stray from words of knowledge  
And receive discipline. ^19-27

**19:28** A malicious witness scoffs at justice,  
And the speech of the wicked conceals mischief. ^19-28

**19:29** Punishments are in store for scoffers  
And blows for the backs of dullards. ^19-29

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Or “But.”
[^3]: Or “pay attention to his moaning.”
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^5]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^6]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
