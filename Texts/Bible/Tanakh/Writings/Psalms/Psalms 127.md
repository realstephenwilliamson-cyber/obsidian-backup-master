## Chapter 127

**127:1** A song of ascents. Of Solomon.  
Unless the LORD builds the house,  
its builders labor in vain on it;  
unless the LORD watches over the city,  
the watchman keeps vigil in vain. ^127-1

**127:2** In vain do you rise early  
and stay up late,  
you who toil for the bread you eat;  
He provides as much for His loved ones while they sleep.[^1] ^127-2

**127:3** Sons are the provision[^2] of the LORD;  
the fruit of the womb, His reward. ^127-3

**127:4** Like arrows in the hand of a warrior  
are sons born to a man in his youth. ^127-4

**127:5** Happy is the man who fills his quiver with them;  
they shall not be put to shame  
when they contend with the enemy in the gate. ^127-5

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Lit. “heritage.”
