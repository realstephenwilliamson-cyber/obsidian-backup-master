## Mūla

रूपकत्वाच्च रूपं तत्तादात्म्यं योगिनः पुनः ।
रूपस्थं तत्समापत्त्यौदासीन्यं रूपिणां विदुः ॥ ^mula

rūpakatvācca rūpaṃ tattādātmyaṃ yoginaḥ punaḥ |
rūpasthaṃ tatsamāpattyaudāsīnyaṃ rūpiṇāṃ viduḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 14 - Chapter Ten, part 5#^pg481|p. 462]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=481>))
>
> Insofar as (the perceiver) metaphorically (rūpakatvāt) (identifies with the object of cognition, he is himself called) ‘Form’ (rūpa). Yogis (call) the state of identity with that (subject) ‘Established in Form’ (rūpastha). (The wise) know that those who, by means of the corresponding contemplative attainment (samāpatti), are endowed with the subject’s form (rūpin), are in a state of transcendent detachment (audasīnya) (from all phenomena with form). The contraction (to which consciousness is subject due to) objectivity does not occur here. Thus those who possess intuitive insight (prasaṁkhyāna) (call it), in accord with that, the ‘Great Pervasion’ (mahāvyāpti). (with 10.261–262)
