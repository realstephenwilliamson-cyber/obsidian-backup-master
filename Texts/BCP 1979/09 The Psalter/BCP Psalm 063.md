# Psalm 63

← [[BCP Psalm 062]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 064]] →

*Deus, Deus meus* · Book Two · Twelfth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 063]]

**63:1** O God, you are my God; eagerly I seek you; \*  
my soul thirsts for you, my flesh faints for you,  
as in a barren and dry land where there is no water. ^63-1

**63:2** Therefore I have gazed upon you in your holy place, \*  
that I might behold your power and your glory. ^63-2

**63:3** For your loving-kindness is better than life itself; \*  
my lips shall give you praise. ^63-3

**63:4** So will I bless you as long as I live \*  
and lift up my hands in your Name. ^63-4

**63:5** My soul is content, as with marrow and fatness, \*  
and my mouth praises you with joyful lips, ^63-5

**63:6** When I remember you upon my bed, \*  
and meditate on you in the night watches. ^63-6

**63:7** For you have been my helper, \*  
and under the shadow of your wings I will rejoice. ^63-7

[p. 671] ^pg671

**63:8** My soul clings to you; \*  
your right hand holds me fast. ^63-8

**63:9** May those who seek my life to destroy it \*  
go down into the depths of the earth; ^63-9

**63:10** Let them fall upon the edge of the sword, \*  
and let them be food for jackals. ^63-10

**63:11** But the king will rejoice in God;  
all those who swear by him will be glad; \*  
for the mouth of those who speak lies shall be stopped. ^63-11
