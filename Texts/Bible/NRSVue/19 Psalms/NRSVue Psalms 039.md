# Psalm 39 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 038|← Psalm 38]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 040|Psalm 40 →]]

## Prayer for wisdom and forgiveness

*To the leader: to Jeduthun. A Psalm of David.*

**39:1** I said, “I will guard my ways that I may not sin with my tongue; I will keep a muzzle on my mouth as long as the wicked are in my presence.” ^39-1

**39:2** I was silent and still; I held my peace to no avail; my distress grew worse; ^39-2

**39:3** my heart became hot within me. While I mused, the fire burned; then I spoke with my tongue: ^39-3

**39:4** “LORD, let me know my end and what is the measure of my days; let me know how fleeting my life is. ^39-4

**39:5** You have made my days a few handbreadths, and my lifetime is as nothing in your sight. Surely everyone stands as a mere breath. *Selah* ^39-5

**39:6** Surely everyone goes about like a shadow. Surely for nothing they are in turmoil; they heap up and do not know who will gather. ^39-6

**39:7** “And now, O Lord, what do I wait for? My hope is in you. ^39-7

**39:8** Deliver me from all my transgressions. Do not make me the scorn of the fool. ^39-8

**39:9** I am silent; I do not open my mouth, for it is you who have done it. ^39-9

**39:10** Remove your stroke from me; I am worn down by the blows^[Heb *hostility*] of your hand. ^39-10

**39:11** “You chastise mortals in punishment for sin, consuming like a moth what is dear to them; surely everyone is a mere breath. *Selah* ^39-11

**39:12** “Hear my prayer, O LORD, and give ear to my cry; do not hold your peace at my tears. For I am your passing guest, an alien, like all my forebears. ^39-12

**39:13** Turn your gaze away from me, that I may smile again, before I depart and am no more.” ^39-13
