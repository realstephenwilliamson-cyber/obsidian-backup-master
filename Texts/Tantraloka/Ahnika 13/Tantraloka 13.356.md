## Mūla

यस्तूर्ध्वोर्ध्वपथप्रेप्सुरधरं गुरुमागमम् ।
जिहासेच्छक्तिपातेन स धन्यः प्रोन्मुखीकृतः ॥ ^mula

yastūrdhvordhvapathaprepsuradharaṃ gurumāgamam |
jihāsecchaktipātena sa dhanyaḥ pronmukhīkṛtaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 10 - Chapter Thirteen, part 6#^pg364|p. 342]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=364>))
>
> Lucky is the one who, wishing to enter a higher path, has been rendered propense (towards it) (pronmukhīkrta) by a descent of the power (of grace), abandons an inferior teacher and scripture.
