## Mūla

भोगापवर्गपरिपूरणकल्पवल्ली
भोगैकदानरसिकां सुरसिद्धसिन्धुम् ।
न्यक्कुर्वती हरपिनाककलावतीर्णा
यद्भूषयत्यविरतं तटिनी वितस्ता ॥ ^mula

bhogāpavargaparipūraṇakalpavallī
bhogaikadānarasikāṃ surasiddhasindhum |
nyakkurvatī harapinākakalāvatīrṇā
yadbhūṣayatyavirataṃ taṭinī vitastā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 11 - Chapter Thirty-Seven, part 1#^pg267|p. 249]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=267>))
>
> The river Vitastā, brought down (into the world) by a blow from Śiva’s trident (pināka), adorns without a break (by its constant flow, the city). (A veritable) wish-granting creeper that fulfils (those who bathe in it with) both worldly fruits and liberation, demeaning (the Ganges), the river of the gods and Siddhas, which gives only worldly fruits.
