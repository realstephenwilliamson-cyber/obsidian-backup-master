## Mūla

सर्वंसहस्ततोऽधःस्थ ऊर्ध्वस्थोऽधिकृतो गुरुः ।
स्वात्मीयाधरसंस्पर्शात्प्राणयन्नधराः क्रियाः ॥ ^mula

sarvaṃsahastato'dhaḥstha ūrdhvastho'dhikṛto guruḥ |
svātmīyādharasaṃsparśātprāṇayannadharāḥ kriyāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 10 - Chapter Thirteen, part 6#^pg346|p. 324]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=346>))
>
> A limited (teacher) who is immersed in an infinite (number of limitations) does not know that which is unlimited. A teacher who is established above (in a higher tradition) is all-enduring and so has (also) been authorized (to be a teacher in) lower (traditions). (with 13.321cd–322ab)
>
> The most excellent (teacher of all) is one who, belonging to a more elevated (tradition) (žrdhvastha), vitalizes the rites of inferior (traditions) by the contact with his own, and so renders them fruitful. (with 13.322cd–323ab)

*Lead-in:* नन्वेवमूर्ध्वस्थोऽपि पूर्णज्ञानो गुरुरधःस्थे वैष्णवादौ कथङ्कारमधिकारं कुर्यात्? -- इत्याशङ्क्याह --

> स्वात्मीयाधरसंस्पर्शात्प्राणयन्नधराः क्रियाः ॥
> सफलीकुरुते यत्तदूर्ध्वस्थो गुरुरुत्तमः ।

यदूर्ध्वस्थो गुरुः स्वात्मसाक्षात्कारात् संपूर्णाद्वयज्ञानपरामृतसंपातादधराः क्रियाः प्राणयंस्तदनुप्राणिताः संपादयन् परिपूर्णभुक्तिमुक्तिप्रदतया सफलयति, तदसावुत्तमः सर्वातिशायी -- इत्यर्थः ॥
