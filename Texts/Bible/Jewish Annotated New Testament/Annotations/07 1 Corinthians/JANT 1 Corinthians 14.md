### 14.1–40: Prophecy and tongues

*[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 14#^14-1|Text]], p. 346* ^14-1

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 14#^14-1|1]]:** *Strive for . . . spiritual gifts* “pneumatika,” by following Paul’s instructions; see 12.1–11nn., and “Spiritual Gifts,” p. 343. ^14-1-2

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 14#^14-2|2]]:** *Speaking mysteries*, talking in incomprehensible language about heavenly secrets. ^14-2

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 14#^14-3|3]]:** Prophecy enlightens more than tongues because it is immediately comprehensible; see 12.8; [[LXX Wisdom of Solomon 14#^14-28|Wis 14.28]]; *Sib. Or.* 3.163,298,491; Plutarch, *Pyth. orac.* , 24–25. ^14-3

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 14#^14-8|8]]:** *Battle*, Roman army musicians signaled maneuvers with instruments; see also [[Torah 36 - Beha'alotcha#^10-2|Num 10.2–9]]; [[Joshua 06#^6-4|Josh 6.4–20]]; 4Q491 frag. 11, col 2.1–7; frag. 13.1–6. ^14-8

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 14#^14-15|15]]:** *Sing praise*, chanting of psalms or hymns acclaiming God’s accomplishments. ^14-15

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 14#^14-16|16]]:** *Blessing*, thanksgiving, assented by other believers proclaiming *“*Amen*”* (Heb “truly”; see [[Psalms 106#^106-48|Ps 106.48]]; [[Nehemiah 05#^5-13|Neh 5.13]]; [[I Chronicles 16#^16-36|1 Chr 16.36]]; *m. Ber.* 8.8; *m. Taʾan.* 2.5; *t. Rosh Ha-Shanah* 2.14). *Outsider*, or unlearned (Gk “idiotēs,” whence “idiot”) in the gospel. ^14-16

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 14#^14-18|18]]:** *More than all of you*, not boasting, since the gift of tongues is inferior to the gift of interpretation and prophecy (14.4). ^14-18

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 14#^14-20|20]]:** *Children*, see 2.6n.; 3.1n.; 13.11. ^14-20

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 14#^14-21|21]]:** [[Isaiah 28#^28-11|Isa 28.11–12]], in a version different from both the Masoretic and extant Greek texts. *Law*, Jewish Scriptures (see 9.8–11nn.; [[Romans 03#^3-19|Rom 3.19]]). Paul considers prophetic writing as well as the Torah to be authoritative, as does rabbinic tradition ([[Gittin 36#36a|*b. Git.* 36a]]). ^14-21

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 14#^14-22|22]]:** *Not for unbelievers*, because it is alienating. The idea that the same instrument can have opposite effects appears in [[LXX Wisdom of Solomon 11#^11-5|Wis 11.5]]. ^14-22

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 14#^14-23|23]]:** *Out of your mind*, as with some Greek ecstatic utterances; a negative response by *outsiders* to glossolalia ([[LXX Wisdom of Solomon 14#^14-28|Wis 14.28]]; [[Acts 02#^2-4|Acts 2.4–13]]). ^14-23

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 14#^14-25|25]]:** [[Isaiah 45#^45-14|Isa 45.14]]; see also 3.16; [[Ezekiel 39#^39-21|Ezek 39.21]]; [[Zechariah 08#^8-23|Zech 8.23]]. ^14-25

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 14#^14-26|26]]:** *Lesson*, instruction (see LXX [[LXX Psalms 59#^59-1|Ps 59.1]]; [[LXX Sirach 09#^9-1|Sir 9.1]]). *Revelation*, wisdom utterance or prophecy (see 14.6; also 12.8–10). *Tongue*, see 12.10; 13.1. *Interpretation*, see 12.10; 14.13. ^14-26

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 14#^14-27|27]]:** *Two or at most three*, the number of a legal quorum, perhaps traditional (see [[Matthew 18#^18-20|Mt 18.20]]), to keep worship decent and orderly (v 40). ^14-27

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 14#^14-29|29]]:** *Weigh what is said*, see [[Torah 48 - Shoftim#^18-22|Deut 18.22]]. ^14-29

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 14#^14-32|32]]:** *Subject*, unlike Greek oracles, which overtook and controlled their agents. ^14-32

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 14#^14-33|33b–36]]:** Some scholars consider this passage an interpolation: it appears to contradict 11.5; its position varies among manuscripts; it is easily removed without disrupting the discourse; thus the NRSV puts it in parentheses. ^14-33

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 14#^14-34|34]]:** *Women* (Gk “gynaikes”), or *“*wives” (see 12.10,28,30; 14.2,4–6,13,27–28). *The law*, perhaps *the law* of creation, that a “husband is the head of his wife” (11.3; see also LXX [[LXX Genesis 03#^3-16|Gen 3.16]]**).** Interpretations that attribute Paul’s injunction to an imagined misogynist “rabbinic background” are unfounded; see “Gender,” p. 611. Jewish women spoke publicly in their capacity as civil leaders (e.g., Josephus, *Ant.* 13.405) and leaders of synagogues (e.g., *CIJ* 741). ^14-34

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 14#^14-39|39]]:** *Friends* (Gk “adelphoi”), lit., “brothers.” ^14-39

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 14#^14-40|40]]:** *Decently*, deco-rously. *In order*, according to the divine, cosmic order delineated in the previous two chapters. ^14-40
