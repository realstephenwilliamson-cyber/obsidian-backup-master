## Mūla

इत्थं सृष्टिस्थितिध्वंसतिरोभावमनुग्रहः ।
इति पञ्चसु कर्तृत्वं शिवत्वं संविदात्मनः ॥ ^mula

itthaṃ sṛṣṭisthitidhvaṃsatirobhāvamanugrahaḥ |
iti pañcasu kartṛtvaṃ śivatvaṃ saṃvidātmanaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII

> In this way, the agency of consciousness (that performs) the five (tasks) of emanation, persistence, withdrawal, obscuration and grace is its Śiva nature (śivatva).
