## Chapter 122

**122:1** A song of ascents. Of David. I rejoiced when they said to me,  
“We are going to the House of the LORD.” ^122-1

**122:2** Our feet stood inside your gates, O Jerusalem, ^122-2

**122:3** Jerusalem built up, a city knit together, ^122-3

**122:4** to which tribes would make pilgrimage,  
the tribes of the LORD,  
—as was enjoined upon Israel—  
to praise the name of the LORD. ^122-4

**122:5** There the thrones of judgment stood,  
thrones of the house of David. ^122-5

**122:6** Pray for the well-being of Jerusalem;  
“May those who love you be at peace. ^122-6

**122:7** May there be well-being within your ramparts,  
peace in your citadels.” ^122-7

**122:8** For the sake of my kin and friends,  
I pray for your well-being; ^122-8

**122:9** for the sake of the house of the LORD our God,  
I seek your good. ^122-9
