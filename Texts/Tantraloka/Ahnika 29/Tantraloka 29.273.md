## Mūla

तद्विभागक्रमे सिद्धः स गुरुर्मोचयेत् पशून् ।
गुरोरग्रे विशेच्छिष्यो वक्त्रं वक्त्रे तु वेधयेत् ॥ ^mula

tadvibhāgakrame siddhaḥ sa gururmocayet paśūn |
guroragre viśecchiṣyo vaktraṃ vaktre tu vedhayet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 13 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 5#^pg419|p. 402]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=419>))
>
> Having known the centre (between exhalation and inhalation) which, indivisibly (one), should be known in all respects, he is (never) stained. (Fully) accomplished (siddha) in (the practice of) the sequence of its types, he is a (true) teacher who can liberate the fettered. (with 29.272cd–273ab)
>
> The disciple should sit before the teacher. (The teacher) should pierce (him) (as he inserts) the mouth (of his central vital energy) into the mouth (of the disciple’s vital energy), and the form (of his object of sight) (rūpa) into the form (the disciple sees), until he fuses with (the senses, that are) the domains (of this procedure). (with 29.273cd–274ab)

## Viveka

> रूपं रूपे तु विषयैर्यावत्समरसीभवेत् ।
> स्वाग्रोपविष्टस्य हि शिष्यस्य गुरुर्वक्त्रे

".........................शैवी मुखमिहोच्यते ।"(वि.भै.श्लो. २०)

इत्याद्युक्त्या तन्मध्यशक्तौ स्वां मध्यशक्तिं तदीयरूपे तद्ग्रहके चक्षुरिन्द्रिये स्वं चक्षुरिन्द्रियरूपमेतदुपलक्षितेषु तत्तदिन्द्रियान्तरेष्वपि स्वेन्द्रियान्तराणि वेधयेत् तन्निमित्तं प्रयुञ्जीत, यावद्विषयीक्रियमाणैरेभिः समरसीभवेत्-- तदैकात्म्यमासादयेत्--इत्यर्थः ॥
