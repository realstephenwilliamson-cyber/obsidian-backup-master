### Text

**1** [and] they [ob]eyed your father Patireza […] ^t-1

**2** [and] among the attendants of the royal wardrobe […] performing ^t-2

**3** the service of the King according to all that they (?) rece[ived …] … In the same year ^t-3

**4** the King’s spirit was suffering [… the bo]oks of his father [we]re read in front of him; and among ^t-4

**5** the books was found a scroll, [sea]led with the seven seals of the ring of Darius, his father. Its title: ^t-5

**6** [«… of Dar]ius the King to the attendants of the Empire /of the who[le e]arth,/ peace». It was opened, it was read, it was found written in it: «Darius the King ^t-6

**7** [to the … who will] rule after me and to the attendants of the Empire, p[ea]ce. Let it be known to you that every violent and deceitful person ^t-7
