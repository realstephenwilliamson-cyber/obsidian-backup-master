## Chapter 137

**137:1** By the rivers of Babylon,  
there we sat,  
sat and wept,  
as we thought of Zion. ^137-1

**137:2** There on the poplars  
we hung up our lyres, ^137-2

**137:3** for our captors asked us there for songs,  
our tormentors,[^1] for amusement:  
“Sing us one of the songs of Zion.” ^137-3

**137:4** How can we sing a song of the LORD  
on alien soil? ^137-4

**137:5** If I forget you, O Jerusalem,  
let my right hand wither;[^2] ^137-5

**137:6** let my tongue stick to my palate  
if I cease to think of you,  
if I do not keep Jerusalem in memory  
even at my happiest hour. ^137-6

**137:7** Remember, O LORD, against the Edomites  
the day of Jerusalem’s fall;  
how they cried, “Strip her, strip her  
to her very foundations!” ^137-7

**137:8** Fair Babylon, you predator,[^3]  
a blessing on him who repays you in kind  
what you have inflicted on us; ^137-8

**137:9** a blessing on him who seizes your babies  
and dashes them against the rocks! ^137-9

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Others “forget its cunning.”
[^3]: With Targum; others “who are to be destroyed.”
