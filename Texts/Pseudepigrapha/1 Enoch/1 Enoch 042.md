### XLII. The Dwelling-places of Wisdom and of Unrighteousness.

**42:1** Wisdom found no place where she might dwell;
Then a dwelling-place was assigned her in the heavens. ^42-1

**42:2** Wisdom went forth to make her dwelling among the children of men,
And found no dwelling-place:
Wisdom returned to her place
And took her seat among the angels. ^42-2

**42:3** And unrighteousness went forth from her chambers:
Whom she sought not she found,
And dwelt with them,
As rain in a desert,
And dew on a thirsty land. ^42-3
