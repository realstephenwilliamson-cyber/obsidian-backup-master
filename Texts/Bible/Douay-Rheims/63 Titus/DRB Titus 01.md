# Titus 1 (Douay-Rheims)

— · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Titus 02|Titus 2 →]]

Compare: [[Titus 01|BSB and SBLGNT]]

**1:1** Paul, a servant of God, and an apostle of Jesus Christ, according to the faith of the elect of God and the acknowledging of the truth, which is according to godliness: ^1-1

**1:2** Unto the hope of life everlasting, which God, who lieth not, hath promised before the times of the world: ^1-2

**1:3** But hath in due times manifested his word in preaching, which is committed to me according to the commandment of God our Saviour: ^1-3

**1:4** To Titus my beloved son, according to the common faith, grace and peace from God the Father, and from Christ Jesus our Saviour. ^1-4

**1:5** For this cause I left thee in Crete, that thou shouldest set in order the things that are wanting, and shouldest ordain priests in every city, as I also appointed thee: ^1-5

**1:6** If any be without crime, the husband of one wife, having faithful children, not accused of riot, or unruly. ^1-6

**1:7** For a bishop must be without crime, as the steward of God: not proud, not subject to anger, not given to wine, no striker, not greedy of filthy lucre: ^1-7

**1:8** But given to hospitality, gentle, sober, just, holy, continent: ^1-8

**1:9** Embracing that faithful word which is according to doctrine, that he may be able to exhort in sound doctrine, and to convince the gainsayers. ^1-9

**1:10** For there are also many disobedient, vain talkers, and seducers: especially they who are of the circumcision: ^1-10

**1:11** Who must be reproved, who subvert whole houses, teaching things which they ought not, for filthy lucre’s sake. ^1-11

**1:12** One of them a prophet of their own, said, The Cretians are always liars, evil beasts, slothful bellies. ^1-12

**1:13** This testimony is true. Wherefore rebuke them sharply, that they may be sound in the faith; ^1-13

**1:14** Not giving heed to Jewish fables and commandments of men, who turn themselves away from the truth. ^1-14

**1:15** All things are clean to the clean: but to them that are defiled, and to unbelievers, nothing is clean: but both their mind and their conscience are defiled. ^1-15

**1:16** They profess that they know God: but in their works they deny him; being abominable, and incredulous, and to every good work reprobate. ^1-16
