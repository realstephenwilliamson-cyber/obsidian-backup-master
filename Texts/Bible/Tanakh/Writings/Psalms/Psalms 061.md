## Chapter 61

**61:1** For the leader; with instrumental music. Of David. ^61-1

**61:2** Hear my cry, O God,  
heed my prayer. ^61-2

**61:3** From the end of the earth I call to You;  
when my heart is faint,  
You lead me to a rock that is high above me. ^61-3

**61:4** For You have been my refuge,  
a tower of strength against the enemy. ^61-4

**61:5** O that I might dwell in Your tent forever,  
take refuge under Your protecting wings. *Selah*. ^61-5

**61:6** O God, You have heard my vows;  
grant the request[^1] of those who fear Your name. ^61-6

**61:7** Add days to the days of the king;  
may his years extend through generations; ^61-7

**61:8** may he dwell in God’s presence forever;  
appoint[^2] steadfast love to guard him. ^61-8

**61:9** So I will sing hymns to Your name forever,  
as I fulfill my vows day after day. ^61-9

[^1]: Taking the noun yr¦t as an alternate form of ’r¦t; cf. Ps. 21.3.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
