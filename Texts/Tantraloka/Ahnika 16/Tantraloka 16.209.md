## Mūla

वर्णाध्वा यद्यपि प्रोक्तः शोध्यः पाशात्मकस्तु सः ।
मायीयः शोधकस्त्वन्यः शिवात्मा परवाङ्मयः ॥ ^mula

varṇādhvā yadyapi proktaḥ śodhyaḥ pāśātmakastu saḥ |
māyīyaḥ śodhakastvanyaḥ śivātmā paravāṅmayaḥ || ^iast

> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 16.208cd–209ab.
>
> Although it is said (in the scriptures) that the Path of the Phonemes is an object of purification and that it is a fetter (pāśa) and (belongs to the domain of) Māyā (māyīya), (there is) another (aspect that) is purifying, which is Śiva by nature and Supreme Speech.
