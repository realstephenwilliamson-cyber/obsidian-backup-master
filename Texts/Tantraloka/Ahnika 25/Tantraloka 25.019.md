## Mūla

अथ वच्मः स्फुटं श्रीमत्सिद्धये नाडिचारणम् ।
या वाहयितुमिष्येत नाडी तामेव भावयेत् ॥ ^mula

atha vacmaḥ sphuṭaṃ śrīmatsiddhaye nāḍicāraṇam |
yā vāhayitumiṣyeta nāḍī tāmeva bhāvayet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 17 - Chapter Twenty-Five (continued) and Chapter Twenty-Six#^pg384|p. 364]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=384>))
>
> We will now talk clearly about the movement through the channels (of the breath) (nādicāraṇa), (as it is taught) in the venerable Siddhā. (with 25.19ab)
>
> (The officiant) should meditate (bhāvayet) on the channel he wishes to cause to flow (with the flux of the breath). When the channel is in a state of oneness (fanmayībhāva) with the meditation (bhāvanā), it clearly (begins to) breathe. Or else, in order to make (the breath) flow (within) that (channel) he chooses (to do so), having pressed that part (of the body) with the (corresponding) hand, he should incline the abdomen (of the corpse). (Then that) channel immediately (begins to) transport (the flow of the breath). (with 25.19cd–21ab)
