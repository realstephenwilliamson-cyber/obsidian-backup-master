## Mūla

वैष्णवादिः पशुः प्रोक्तो न योग्यः पतिशासने ।

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 07 - Chapter Sixteen, part 3#^pg131|p. 111]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=131>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 16.255cd–256ab.
>
> It is said in the venerable Pauṣkara that ‘(the mantras of) Brahmā and Viṣṇu etc. are not the same (as ours). Located in the Egg (of Brahmā), they belong to the domain of Nature (prādhānika) and, impure (sāñjana), they are sāttvika, rājasa or tīāīmasa. A Vaiṣṇava teacher and the like is said to be (just) a fettered soul. This is because, due to (his) impure judgement (parāmarśa), he has been consecrated by identification with them (tanmayībhāvīta). He is not qualified (to receive and impart) the Śaiva teachings (patiśāstra). According to the view of the venerable Matañga and others, ‘the mantras that are on the pure path are those of Lord Śiva and the other (Śaiva deities).’ The teacher who is identified with them is indeed Śiva. (with 16.254cd–257ab)

## Viveka

> [!viveka]- Viveka in English (Dyczkowski, vol. IX, p. 112)
> ![[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 07 - Chapter Sixteen, part 3#Viveka on 16.254–257]]

> उक्तं श्रीपौष्करेऽन्ये च ब्रह्मविष्ण्वादयोऽण्डगाः ॥
> प्राधानिकाः साञ्जनास्ते सात्त्वराजसतामसाः ।
> तैरशुद्धपरामर्शात्तन्मयीभावितो गुरुः ॥
> वैष्णवादिः पशुः प्रोक्तो न योग्यः पतिशासने ।
> ये मन्त्राः शुद्धमार्गस्थाः शिवभट्टारकादयः ॥
> श्रीमन्मतङ्गादिदृशा तन्मयो हि गुरुः शिवः ।

तन्मय इति-- शुद्धपरामर्शात्मशिवभट्टारकादिमन्त्रैकात्म्यमापन्नः-- इत्यर्थः ॥
