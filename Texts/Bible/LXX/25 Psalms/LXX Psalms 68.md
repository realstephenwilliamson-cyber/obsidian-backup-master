# LXX Psalms 68

← [[LXX Psalms 67]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 69]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 069]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 069]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**68:1** For the end, *a Psalm* of David, for alternate *strains*.[^1]  ^68-1

**68:2** Save me, O God; for the waters have come in to my soul.  ^68-2

**68:3** I am stuck fast in deep mire, and there is no standing: I am come in to the depths of the sea, and a storm has overwhelmed me.  ^68-3

**68:4** I am weary *of* crying, my throat has become hoarse; mine eyes have failed by my waiting on my God.  ^68-4

**68:5** They that hate me without a cause are more than the hairs of my head: my enemies that persecute me unrighteously are strengthened: then I restored that which I took not away.[^2]  ^68-5

**68:6** O God, thou knowest my foolishness; and my transgressions are not hidden from thee.  ^68-6

**68:7** Let not them that wait on thee, O Lord of hosts, be ashamed on my account: let not them that seek thee, be ashamed on my account, O God of Israel.  ^68-7

**68:8** For I have suffered reproach for thy sake; shame has covered my face.  ^68-8

**68:9** I became strange to my brethren, and a stranger to my mother's children.  ^68-9

**68:10** For the zeal of thine house has eaten me up; and the reproaches of them that reproached thee are fallen upon me.  ^68-10

**68:11** And I bowed down my soul with fasting, and that was made my reproach.  ^68-11

**68:12** And I put on sackcloth for my covering; and I became a proverb to them.  ^68-12

**68:13** They that sit in the gate talked against me, and they that drank wine sang against me.  ^68-13

**68:14** But I *will cry* to thee, O Lord, in my prayer; O God, it is a propitious time: in the multitude of thy mercy hear me, in the truth of thy salvation.  ^68-14

**68:15** Save me from the mire, that I stick not *in it*: let me be delivered from them that hate me, and from the deep waters.  ^68-15

**68:16** Let not the waterflood drown me, nor let the deep swallow me up; neither let the well shut its mouth upon me.  ^68-16

**68:17** Hear me, O Lord; for thy mercy is good: according to the multitude of thy compassions look upon me.  ^68-17

**68:18** And turn not away thy face from thy servant; for I am afflicted: hear me speedily.[^3]  ^68-18

**68:19** Draw nigh to my soul and redeem it: deliver me because of mine enemies.  ^68-19

**68:20** For thou knowest my reproach, and my shame, and my confusion; all that afflict me are before thee.  ^68-20

**68:21** My soul has waited for reproach and misery; and I waited for one to grieve with me, but there was none; and for one to comfort me, but I found none.  ^68-21

**68:22** They gave *me* also gall for my food, and made me drink vinegar for my thirst.  ^68-22

**68:23** Let their table before them be for a snare, and for a recompence, and for a stumbling-block.  ^68-23

**68:24** Let their eyes be darkened that they should not see; and bow down their back continually.  ^68-24

**68:25** Pour out thy wrath upon them, and let the fury of thine anger take hold on them.  ^68-25

**68:26** Let their habitation be made desolate; and let there be no inhabitant in their tents:  ^68-26

**68:27** because they persecuted him whom thou hast smitten; and they have added to the grief of my wounds.  ^68-27

**68:28** Add iniquity to their iniquity; and let them not come into thy righteousness.  ^68-28

**68:29** Let them be blotted out of the book of the living, and let them not be written with the righteous.  ^68-29

**68:30** I am poor and sorrowful; but the salvation of thy countenance has helped me.  ^68-30

**68:31** I will praise the name of my God with a song, I will magnify him with praise;  ^68-31

**68:32** and *this* shall please God more than a young calf having horns and hoofs.  ^68-32

**68:33** Let the poor see and rejoice; seek the Lord diligently, and ye shall live.  ^68-33

**68:34** For the Lord hears the poor, and does not set at nought his fettered ones.  ^68-34

**68:35** Let the heavens and the earth praise him, the sea, and all things moving in them.  ^68-35

**68:36** For God will save Sion, and the cities of Judea shall be built; and *men* shall dwell there, and inherit it.  ^68-36

**68:37** And the seed of his servants shall possess it, and they that love his name shall dwell therein.  ^68-37

[^1]: 68:1 See Ps. 44. title.
[^2]: 68:5 *Or,* paid for, *or,* made up for.
[^3]: 68:18 *Or,* son.
