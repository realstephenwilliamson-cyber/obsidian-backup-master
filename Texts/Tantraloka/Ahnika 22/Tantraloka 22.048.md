## Mūla

तमेवाराधयेद्धीमांस्तत्तज्जिज्ञासनोन्मुखः ।
इति दीक्षाविधिः प्रोक्तो लिङ्गोद्धरणपश्चिमः ॥ ^mula

tamevārādhayeddhīmāṃstattajjijñāsanonmukhaḥ |
iti dīkṣāvidhiḥ prokto liṅgoddharaṇapaścimaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 13 - Chapter Twenty-Two#^pg314|p. 294]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=314>))
>
> The wise and fortunate one (susthita) who, having attained a teacher who is a treasure of all knowledge, should worship him (and render him propitious), intent as he is with the desire of knowing each and everything. (with 22.47cd–48ab)
>
> Thus, the rite (vidhi) of initiation has been taught, ending with the extraction of sectarian signs. (with 22.48cd)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* अथ प्रथमार्धेन प्रकृतार्थगर्भीकारेण प्रकरणार्थमुपसंहरति--

> इति दीक्षाविधिः प्रोक्तो लिङ्गोद्धरणपश्चिमः ॥

इति श्रीमदाचार्याभिनवगुप्तपादविरचिते श्रीतन्त्रालोके लिङ्गोद्धारप्रकाशनं नाम द्वाविंशमाह्निकम् ॥ २२ ॥

इति शिवम् ॥ ४८ ॥
