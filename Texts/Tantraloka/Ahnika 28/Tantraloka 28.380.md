## Mūla

नैव प्रवेशयेत्संवित्सङ्कोचननिबन्धनम् ।
यावन्त्येव शरीराणि स्वाङ्गवत्स्युः सुनिर्भराम् ॥ ^mula

naiva praveśayetsaṃvitsaṅkocananibandhanam |
yāvantyeva śarīrāṇi svāṅgavatsyuḥ sunirbharām || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 07 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 5#^pg189|p. 172]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=189>))
>
> Therefore, one should (take care) not allow (anyone) to enter a gathering (cakra) (of initiates thus) engaged in worship and the like, (whose consciousness) is not one (with the participants), differs in kind (from that of the others), and fetters (them) with the contraction (of their) consciousness (by his presence). (with 28.379cd–380ab)
>
> In a gathering (cakra) (of initiates), one should worship as many bodies (as are present, because) having (all) penetrated into the one, well-filled consciousness, they would (each individually and collectively) be like one’s own body. (with 28.380cd–381ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* एवं मेलकादावतन्मयस्य प्रवेशनिषेधात् तत्प्रवेशाभ्यनुज्ञानेऽपि विशेषावद्योतनाय तन्मया एव अत्र प्रवेशनीयाः--इत्याह--

> यावन्त्येव शरीराणि स्वाङ्गवत्स्युः सुनिर्भराम् ॥
> एकां संविदमाविश्य चक्रे तावन्ति पूजयेत् ।

शरीराणीत्यनेन शरीरिणामत्र वस्तुतः कश्चिद्भेदो नास्तीति सूचितम् । अत एव उक्तम्--एकां सुनिर्भरां संविदमाविश्येति स्वाङ्गवदिति च ॥
