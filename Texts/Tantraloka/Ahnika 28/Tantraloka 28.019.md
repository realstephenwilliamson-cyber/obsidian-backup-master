## Mūla

तन्मेलकसमायुक्तास्ते तत्पूजापराः सदा ।
योऽप्यतन्मय एषोऽपि तत्काले स्वक्रमार्चनात् ॥ ^mula

tanmelakasamāyuktāste tatpūjāparāḥ sadā |
yo'pyatanmaya eṣo'pi tatkāle svakramārcanāt || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 03 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 1#^pg30|p. 13]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=30>))
>
> Those (exceptional few) who are well versed in (attending to) the movement of those cycles (of the breath homologized to time and the deities of the energies of the senses) (cakracāra), (their) consciousness full (because of that), and are fully engaged in that meeting (of Siddhas and Yoginīs) (melaka), are all always intent on the worship (that befits such occasions). Even (one whose consciousness) is not co-extensive (atanmaya) with (their collective consciousness), it becomes one with it (tanmaya) at that time (tatkāle), by virtue of the worship (that is taking place in accord with) the liturgy of one’s own (Kula) (svakramārcana), (or simply) by (just) joining (melaka) that assembly (saṅgha) of Siddhas and Yoginīs. (with 28.18cd–20ab)
