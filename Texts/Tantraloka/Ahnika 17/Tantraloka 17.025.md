## Mūla

आप्यायकं च प्रोच्छालं वौषडादि प्रदीपयेत् ।
तत्र बाह्येऽपि तादात्म्यप्रसिद्धं कर्म चोद्यते ॥ ^mula

āpyāyakaṃ ca procchālaṃ vauṣaḍādi pradīpayet |
tatra bāhye'pi tādātmyaprasiddhaṃ karma codyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 08 - Chapter Seventeen#^pg172|p. 152]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=172>))
>
> The act of reverent salutation (i.e. NAMAH) is plenitude. The union of Fire and Moon, which is the next one (i.e. SVĀHĀ), is nourishment. The nourishing VAUṢAT and the like energizes (a further) outpouring (of nourishment). (with 17.24cd–25ab)
>
> There (in that case), if a rite is prescribed that is well-known to have (a particular) nature in (the everyday) outer (world) also, the teacher should not in any circumstance modify the part (of the Mantra that denotes the deity who) is the object (of the rite) (karmapada). (with 17.25cd–26ab)
