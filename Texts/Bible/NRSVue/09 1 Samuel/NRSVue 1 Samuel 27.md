# 1 Samuel 27 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue 1 Samuel 26|← 1 Samuel 26]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue 1 Samuel 28|1 Samuel 28 →]]

## David serves king Achish of Gath

**27:1** David said in his heart, “I shall certainly perish one day by the hand of Saul; there is nothing better for me than to escape to the land of the Philistines; then Saul will despair of seeking me any longer within the borders of Israel, and I shall escape out of his hand.” ^27-1

**27:2** So David set out and went over, he and the six hundred men who were with him, to King Achish son of Maoch of Gath. ^27-2

**27:3** David stayed with Achish at Gath, he and his troops, every man with his household, and David with his two wives, Ahinoam of Jezreel and Abigail of Carmel, Nabal’s widow. ^27-3

**27:4** When Saul was told that David had fled to Gath, he no longer sought for him. ^27-4

**27:5** Then David said to Achish, “If I have found favor in your sight, let a place be given me in one of the country towns so that I may live there, for why should your servant live in the royal city with you?” ^27-5

**27:6** So that day Achish gave him Ziklag; therefore Ziklag has belonged to the kings of Judah to this day. ^27-6

**27:7** The length of time that David lived in the country of the Philistines was one year and four months. ^27-7

**27:8** Now David and his men went up and made raids on the Geshurites, the Girzites, and the Amalekites, for these were the landed settlements from Telam^[Compare Gk 15.4: Heb *from of old*] on the way to Shur and on to the land of Egypt. ^27-8

**27:9** David struck the land, leaving neither man nor woman alive, but took away the sheep, the oxen, the donkeys, the camels, and the clothing and came back to Achish. ^27-9

**27:10** When Achish asked, “Against whom^[Q ms Gk Vg: MT lacks *whom*] have you made a raid today?” David would say, “Against the Negeb of Judah,” or “Against the Negeb of the Jerahmeelites,” or “Against the Negeb of the Kenites.” ^27-10

**27:11** David left neither man nor woman alive to be brought back to Gath, thinking, “They might tell about us and say, ‘David has done so and so.’ ” Such was his practice all the time he lived in the country of the Philistines. ^27-11

**27:12** Achish trusted David, thinking, “He has made himself utterly abhorrent to his people Israel; therefore he shall always be my servant.” ^27-12
