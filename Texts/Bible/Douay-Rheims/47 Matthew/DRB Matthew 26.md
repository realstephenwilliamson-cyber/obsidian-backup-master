# Matthew 26 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Matthew 25|← Matthew 25]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Matthew 27|Matthew 27 →]]

Compare: [[Matthew 26|BSB and SBLGNT]]

**26:1** And it came to pass, when Jesus had ended all these words, he said to his disciples: ^26-1

**26:2** You know that after two days shall be the pasch, and the son of man shall be delivered up to be crucified: ^26-2

**26:3** Then were gathered together the chief priests and ancients of the people into the court of the high priest, who was called Caiphas: ^26-3

**26:4** And they consulted together, that by subtilty they might apprehend Jesus, and put him to death. ^26-4

**26:5** But they said: Not on the festival day, lest perhaps there should be a tumult among the people. ^26-5

**26:6** And when Jesus was in Bethania, in the house of Simon the leper, ^26-6

**26:7** There came to him a woman having an alabaster box of precious ointment, and poured it on his head as he was at table. ^26-7

**26:8** And the disciples seeing it, had indignation, saying: To what purpose is this waste? ^26-8

**26:9** For this might have been sold for much, and given to the poor. ^26-9

**26:10** And Jesus knowing it, said to them: Why do you trouble this woman? for she hath wrought a good work upon me. ^26-10

**26:11** For the poor you have always with you: but me you have not always. ^26-11

**26:12** For she in pouring this ointment upon my body, hath done it for my burial. ^26-12

**26:13** Amen I say to you, wheresoever this gospel shall be preached in the whole world, that also which she hath done, shall be told for a memory of her. ^26-13

**26:14** Then went one of the twelve, who was called Judas Iscariot, to the chief priests, ^26-14

**26:15** And said to them: What will you give me, and I will deliver him unto you? But they appointed him thirty pieces of silver. ^26-15

**26:16** And from thenceforth he sought opportunity to betray him. ^26-16

**26:17** And on the first day of the Azymes, the disciples came to Jesus, saying: Where wilt thou that we prepare for thee to eat the pasch? ^26-17

**26:18** But Jesus said: Go ye into the city to a certain man, and say to him: the master saith, My time is near at hand, with thee I make the pasch with my disciples. ^26-18

**26:19** And the disciples did as Jesus appointed to them, and they prepared the pasch. ^26-19

**26:20** But when it was evening, he sat down with his twelve disciples. ^26-20

**26:21** And whilst they were eating, he said: Amen I say to you, that one of you is about to betray me. ^26-21

**26:22** And they being very much troubled, began every one to say: Is it I, Lord? ^26-22

**26:23** But he answering, said: He that dippeth his hand with me in the dish, he shall betray me. ^26-23

**26:24** The Son of man indeed goeth, as it is written of him: but woe to that man by whom the Son of man shall be betrayed: it were better for him, if that man had not been born. ^26-24

**26:25** And Judas that betrayed him, answering, said: Is it I, Rabbi? He saith to him: Thou hast said it. ^26-25

**26:26** And whilst they were at supper, Jesus took bread, and blessed, and broke: and gave to his disciples, and said: Take ye, and eat. This is my body. ^26-26

**26:27** And taking the chalice, he gave thanks, and gave to them, saying: Drink ye all of this. ^26-27

**26:28** For this is my blood of the new testament, which shall be shed for many unto remission of sins. ^26-28

**26:29** And I say to you, I will not drink from henceforth of this fruit of the vine, until that day when I shall drink it with you new in the kingdom of my Father. ^26-29

**26:30** And a hymn being said, they went out unto mount Olivet. ^26-30

**26:31** Then Jesus said to them: All you shall be scandalized in me this night. For it is written: I will strike the shepherd, and the sheep of the flock shall be dispersed. ^26-31

**26:32** But after I shall be risen again, I will go before you into Galilee. ^26-32

**26:33** And Peter answering, said to him: Although all shall be scandalized in thee, I will never be scandalized. ^26-33

**26:34** Jesus said to him: Amen I say to thee, that in this night before the cock crow, thou wilt deny me thrice. ^26-34

**26:35** Peter saith to him: Yea, though I should die with thee, I will not deny thee. And in like manner said all the disciples. ^26-35

**26:36** Then Jesus came with them into a country place which is called Gethsemani; and he said to his disciples: Sit you here, till I go yonder and pray. ^26-36

**26:37** And taking with him Peter and the two sons of Zebedee, he began to grow sorrowful and to be sad. ^26-37

**26:38** Then he saith to them: My soul is sorrowful even unto death: stay you here, and watch with me. ^26-38

**26:39** And going a little further, he fell upon his face, praying, and saying: My Father, if it be possible, let this chalice pass from me. Nevertheless not as I will, but as thou wilt. ^26-39

**26:40** And he cometh to his disciples, and findeth them asleep, and he saith to Peter: What? Could you not watch one hour with me? ^26-40

**26:41** Watch ye, and pray that ye enter not into temptation. The spirit indeed is willing, but the flesh weak. ^26-41

**26:42** Again the second time, he went and prayed, saying: My Father, if this chalice may not pass away, but I must drink it, thy will be done. ^26-42

**26:43** And he cometh again and findeth them sleeping: for their eyes were heavy. ^26-43

**26:44** And leaving them, he went again: and he prayed the third time, saying the selfsame word. ^26-44

**26:45** Then he cometh to his disciples, and saith to them: Sleep ye now and take your rest; behold the hour is at hand, and the Son of man shall be betrayed into the hands of sinners. ^26-45

**26:46** Rise, let us go: behold he is at hand that will betray me. ^26-46

**26:47** As he yet spoke, behold Judas, one of the twelve, came, and with him a great multitude with swords and clubs, sent from the chief priests and the ancients of the people. ^26-47

**26:48** And he that betrayed him, gave them a sign, saying: Whomsoever I shall kiss, that is he, hold him fast. ^26-48

**26:49** And forthwith coming to Jesus, he said: Hail, Rabbi. And he kissed him. ^26-49

**26:50** And Jesus said to him: Friend, whereto art thou come? Then they came up, and laid hands on Jesus, and held him. ^26-50

**26:51** And behold one of them that were with Jesus, stretching forth his hand, drew out his sword: and striking the servant of the high priest, cut off his ear. ^26-51

**26:52** Then Jesus saith to him: Put up again thy sword into its place: for all that take the sword shall perish with the sword. ^26-52

**26:53** Thinkest thou that I cannot ask my Father, and he will give me presently more than twelve legions of angels? ^26-53

**26:54** How then shall the scriptures be fulfilled, that so it must be done? ^26-54

**26:55** In that same hour Jesus said to the multitudes: You are come out as it were to a robber with swords and clubs to apprehend me. I sat daily with you, teaching in the temple, and you laid not hands on me. ^26-55

**26:56** Now all this was done, that the scriptures of the prophets might be fulfilled. Then the disciples all leaving him, fled. ^26-56

**26:57** But they holding Jesus led him to Caiphas the high priest, where the scribes and the ancients were assembled. ^26-57

**26:58** And Peter followed him afar off, even to the court of the high priest. And going in, he sat with the servants, that he might see the end. ^26-58

**26:59** And the chief priests and the whole council sought false witness against Jesus, that they might put him to death: ^26-59

**26:60** And they found not, whereas many false witnesses had come in. And last of all there came two false witnesses: ^26-60

**26:61** And they said: This man said, I am able to destroy the temple of God, and after three days to rebuild it. ^26-61

**26:62** And the high priest rising up, said to him: Answerest thou nothing to the things which these witness against thee? ^26-62

**26:63** But Jesus held his peace. And the high priest said to him: I adjure thee by the living God, that thou tell us if thou be the Christ the Son of God. ^26-63

**26:64** Jesus saith to him: Thou hast said it. Nevertheless I say to you, hereafter you shall see the Son of man sitting on the right hand of the power of God, and coming in the clouds of heaven. ^26-64

**26:65** Then the high priests rent his garments, saying: He hath blasphemed; what further need have we of witnesses? Behold, now you have heard the blasphemy: ^26-65

**26:66** What think you? But they answering, said: He is guilty of death. ^26-66

**26:67** Then did they spit in his face, and buffeted him: and others struck his face with the palms of their hands, ^26-67

**26:68** Saying: Prophesy unto us, O Christ, who is he that struck thee? ^26-68

**26:69** But Peter sat without in the court: and there came to him a servant maid, saying: Thou also wast with Jesus the Galilean. ^26-69

**26:70** But he denied before them all, saying: I know not what thou sayest. ^26-70

**26:71** And as he went out of the gate, another maid saw him, and she saith to them that were there: This man also was with Jesus of Nazareth. ^26-71

**26:72** And again he denied with an oath, I know not the man. ^26-72

**26:73** And after a little while they came that stood by, and said to Peter: Surely thou also art one of them; for even thy speech doth discover thee. ^26-73

**26:74** Then he began to curse and to swear that he knew not the man. And immediately the cock crew. ^26-74

**26:75** And Peter remembered the word of Jesus which he had said: Before the cock crow, thou wilt deny me thrice. And going forth, he wept bitterly. ^26-75
