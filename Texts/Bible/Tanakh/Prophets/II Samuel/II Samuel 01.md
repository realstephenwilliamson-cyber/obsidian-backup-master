## Chapter 1

**1:1** After the death of Saul—David had already returned from defeating the Amalekites—David stayed two days in Ziklag. ^1-1

**1:2** On the third day, a man came from Saul’s camp, with his clothes rent and earth on his head; and as he approached David, he flung himself to the ground and bowed low. ^1-2

**1:3** David said to him, “Where are you coming from?” He answered, “I have just escaped from the camp of Israel.” ^1-3

**1:4** “What happened?” asked David. “Tell me!” And he told him how the troops had fled the battlefield, and that, moreover, many of the troops had fallen and died; also that Saul and his son Jonathan were dead. ^1-4

**1:5** “How do you know,” David asked the young man who brought him the news, “that Saul and his son Jonathan are dead?” ^1-5

**1:6** The young man who brought him the news answered, “I happened to be at Mount Gilboa, and I saw Saul leaning on his spear, and the chariots and horsemen closing in on him. ^1-6

**1:7** He looked around and saw me, and he called to me. When I responded, ‘At your service,’ ^1-7

**1:8** he asked me, ‘Who are you?’ And I told him that I was an Amalekite. ^1-8

**1:9** Then he said to me, ‘Stand over me, and finish me off, for I am in agony and am barely alive.’[^1] ^1-9

**1:10** So I stood over him and finished him off, for I knew that he would never rise from where he was lying.[^2] Then I took the crown from his head and the armlet from his arm, and I have brought them here to my lord.” ^1-10

**1:11** David took hold of his clothes and rent them, and so did all the men with him. ^1-11

**1:12** They lamented and wept, and they fasted until evening for Saul and his son Jonathan, and for the soldiers of the LORD[^3] and the House of Israel who had fallen by the sword. ^1-12

**1:13** David said to the young man who had brought him the news, “Where are you from?” He replied, “I am the son of a resident alien, an Amalekite.” ^1-13

**1:14** “How did you dare,” David said to him, “to lift your hand and kill the LORD’s anointed?” ^1-14

**1:15** Thereupon David called one of the attendants and said to him, “Come over and strike him!” He struck him down and he died. ^1-15

**1:16** And David said to him, “Your blood be on your own head! Your own mouth testified against you when you said, ‘I put the LORD’s anointed to death.’” ^1-16

**1:17** And David intoned this dirge over Saul and his son Jonathan— ^1-17

**1:18** He ordered the Judites to be taught [The Song of the] Bow.[^4] It is recorded in the Book of Jashar.[^5] ^1-18

**1:19** Your glory, O Israel,  
Lies slain on your heights;  
How have the mighty fallen! ^1-19

**1:20** Tell it not in Gath,  
Do not proclaim it in the streets of Ashkelon,  
Lest the daughters of the Philistine rejoice,  
Lest the daughters of the uncircumcised exult. ^1-20

**1:21** O hills of Gilboa—  
Let there be no dew or rain on you,  
Or bountiful fields,[^6]  
For there the shield of warriors lay rejected,  
The shield of Saul,  
Polished with oil no more. ^1-21

**1:22** From the blood of slain,  
From the fat of warriors—  
The bow of Jonathan  
Never turned back;  
The sword of Saul  
Never withdrew empty. ^1-22

**1:23** Saul and Jonathan,  
Beloved and cherished,  
Never parted  
In life or in death!  
They were swifter than eagles,  
They were stronger than lions! ^1-23

**1:24** Daughters of Israel,  
Weep over Saul,  
Who clothed you in crimson and finery,  
Who decked your robes with jewels of gold. ^1-24

**1:25** How have the mighty fallen  
In the thick of battle—  
Jonathan, slain on your heights! ^1-25

**1:26** I grieve for you,  
My brother Jonathan,  
You were most dear to me.  
Your love was wonderful to me  
More than the love of women. ^1-26

**1:27** How have the mighty fallen,  
The weapons of war[^7] perished! ^1-27

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Septuagint reads “Judah.”
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^5]: See note at Josh. 10.13.
[^6]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain. Emendation yields “springs from the deep” (cf. Ugaritic shr‘thmtm, and Gen. 7.11; 8.2).
[^7]: I.e., Saul and Jonathan.
