## Mūla

उपदेशस्त्वयं मन्दमध्यशक्तेर्निजां क्रमात् ।
शक्तिं ज्वलयितुं प्रोक्तः सा ह्येवं जाज्वलीत्यलम् ॥ ^mula

upadeśastvayaṃ mandamadhyaśakternijāṃ kramāt |
śaktiṃ jvalayituṃ proktaḥ sā hyevaṃ jājvalītyalam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 14 - Chapter Twenty-Three#^pg355|p. 335]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=355>))
>
> This teaching has been imparted to one who has received a weak or medium power (of grace), in order to gradually inflame his own (spiritual) power, which (as a result) can (finally) burn intensely, fully (in all its splendour).
