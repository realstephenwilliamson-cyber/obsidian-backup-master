## Chapter Eleven

Arjuna then said:

\[1\] “In kindness to me you have spoken
of the transcendent mystery
called the highest Self, the *Atman*.
This has cleared up my delusion.

\[2\] Of beings’ origin and passing
I have heard in great detail
from you, O Krishna, and have heard
of your eternal majesty.

\[3\] Just as you have described your Self,
I would like, O highest Lord,
to look upon your godly form,
O Spirit unsurpassable.

\[4\] If you think it is possible
for me to see you like this, Lord,
then make your everlasting Self
visible to me, God of *Yoga*.”

The Blessed Lord said:

\[5\] “Now look upon, son of Pritha,
my hundred, no, my thousand forms,
all my various shapes and colors,
all my divine appearances.

\[6\] Look upon Indra’s gods of storm,
roaring deities, gods of Agni,
celestial horsemen, many wonders
unseen before, O Arjuna!

[p. 65] \[7\] Behold the entire universe
moving and unmoving at once
here in my body, Arjuna,
whatever you desire to see.

\[8\] But you aren’t able to see Me
with these natural eyes of yours.
I now give you an eye divine.
Behold My majestic power!”

Then Sanjaya said:

\[9\] Having spoken in this way, O King,
Krishna, the great lord of *Yoga*
revealed to the son of Pritha
his supreme majestic form,

\[10\] A form of many mouths and eyes,
aspects many and marvelous,
multiple divine ornaments,
many divine brandished weapons,

\[11\] Wearing godly clothes and garlands,
heavenly perfumes and ointments,
composed of marvels, a divinity
infinite in all directions.

\[12\] If a thousand suns were to rise
all at once into the heavens,
such a brightness would resemble
the brilliant light of that Great Being.

\[13\] What Arjuna was seeing there
was the entire universe
divided into many parts
in the body of the God of Gods.

[p. 66] \[14\] Then Arjuna, who was so amazed
that his hair was standing on end,
bowed his head to the Deity,
and with palms joined said to Him:

\[15\] “I see the gods in your body, O Divine One,
   the gods and all kinds of beings assembled,
Lord Brahma seated on his lotus throne,
   all the seers, all the serpent divinities.

\[16\] I see your many arms, many bellies, faces, eyes,
   in every direction, an infinite form;
I see no end, no middle, no beginning,
   O Lord of All, whose form is all that is!

\[17\] Crowned, holding a mace, bearing a discus,
   a mass of brilliance, radiant on all sides,
I see You, impossible to see completely,
   a luminous burning beyond all measure.

\[18\] You are the fixed, supreme object of knowledge,
   You are the ultimate resting place of all,
You are the eternal protector of *dharma*,
   the primordial Spirit, I now understand.

\[19\] Infinite power without beginning or end,
   with countless arms, the moon and sun Your eyes,
I see You, sacrificial fire in Your mouth,
   burning this universe with Your own splendor.

\[20\] This entire space between heaven and earth
   is filled with You alone in all directions.
Seeing Your wondrous and terrifying form,
   the three worlds tremble, O magnificent Self!

\[21\] Over there throngs of deities enter You.
   Some, terrified, bow reverently, praise You,
[p. 67] saying “Hail,”—throngs of great, perfected *rishis*
   singing Your glory with abundant praise.

\[22\] The Rudras, Adityas, Vasus, the Sadhyas,
   the Vishves, the two Ashvins, Maruts, Ushmapas,
Gandharvas, Yakshas, Asuras, the perfected,
   all behold You with astonished wonder.

\[23\] Seeing your great form, your many mouths and eyes,
   O Mighty One with many arms, legs, and feet,
many bellies, and mouths with terrible tusks,
   the worlds tremble at you, and I tremble too.

\[24\] Reaching the sky, blazing with many colors,
   Your mouth gaping wide, Your huge fiery eyes—
having seen all this, my heart trembles and pounds.
   I’ve lost my nerve, I can find no peace, Vishnu!

\[25\] Having seen all your mouths with their many tusks,
   blazing like the fires that destroy time and space,
I don’t know which way is which, I find no comfort.
   Have mercy, Lord of Gods, Abode of the World!

\[26\] And going into You, Dhritarashtra’s sons
   crowded in with all the rulers of the earth,
Bhishma, Drona, Karna, the charioteer’s son,
   along with all of our foremost warriors.

\[27\] They disappear quickly into Your mouths,
   Your terrible mouths, wide open and tusked.
Some of them are stuck between Your jagged teeth.
   I can see them there with their heads crushed in.

\[28\] As all of the many rivers of the world
   rush down in torrents into the ocean,
so too all those heroes of the world of men
   hurry into the furnaces of Your mouths.

[p. 68] \[29\] As moths or other insects fly directly
   into a flame and are swiftly destroyed,
so too entire worlds fly into Your mouths
   and to their instantaneous destruction.

\[30\] Licking them up into your fiery mouths
   You devour worlds from every direction.
Your terrible radiance fills the universe,
   burns and consumes it entire, O Vishnu!

\[31\] Who are you, Lord of awesome form?
Homage to You! Be merciful!
I want to know You, Primal One;
I do not understand Your actions.”

And the Holy One said:

\[32\] “I am Time, mighty world-destroyer,
come to annihilate every world.
Even without you all these warriors
facing each other will cease to exist.

\[33\] And so stand up, Arjuna, seize the glory!
   Conquer the enemy and enjoy kingship.
These men have already been destroyed by Me.
   Merely be My instrument, O great Archer.

\[34\] Drona, Bhishma, Jayadratha, and Karna,
   and all the other heroic warriors
have been killed by Me. So do not falter. Kill!
   Fight! You will be victorious in battle!”

Then Sanjaya said:

\[35\] After hearing this from the Handsome-haired One,
   Arjuna, who was now trembling in terror,
[p. 69] joined his palms, and bowing low to the ground
   stammered out in fright these words to Krishna:

\[36\] “Rightly, Krishna, the universe rejoices
   and is delighted in rendering You praise.
Terrified demons flee in all directions;
   perfected beings in throngs bow down to You.

\[37\] And why should they not bow to you, Great Being,
   greater even than Brahma the Creator?
Infinite Lord, you are the universe’s home,
   eternal being and nonbeing and beyond both.

\[38\] You are God primordial, ancient Spirit.
   You are the universe’s supreme abode.
You are the knower, the known, the state beyond,
   pervading the universe, the infinite form.

\[39\] You are Wind, Death, Fire, Water, and Moon,
   Lord of all creatures, the first great-grandfather.
All reverence to You a thousand times over,
   again and again all reverence to You!

\[40\] Reverence to You from in front and behind,
   Reverence to You from all sides also, O All!
You are infinite valor, immeasurable might.
   Permeating all, You therefore are All.

\[41\] For anything I’ve said thinking You just a friend,
   ‘Ho, Krishna,’ ‘Descendant of Yadu,’ ‘Comrade,’
anything not knowing Your majestic power,
   through negligence or even affection,

\[42\] Making a joke, or disrespectfully,
   playing around, in bed, seated, or eating,
alone or even before others, Krishna,
   I ask for your forgiveness, O Boundless One.

[p. 70] \[43\] Father of the moving and the unmoving,
   venerated guru of the universe,
there is nothing like you in the three worlds,
   nothing greater, Incomparable Being.

\[44\] Bowing down, therefore, prostrating my body,
   I beg forgiveness of You, Lord to be praised.
As a father to a son, as friend to friend,
   lover to beloved, please have mercy, God.

\[45\] Seeing what has never been seen before
   I am excited, but my mind trembles in fear.
Show me, O God, the form You were in before.
   Be merciful, Lord, the universe’s abode!

\[46\] I want to see You as you were before, crowned
   with a diadem, armed with club and discus,
a figure with four limbs. Become that again,
   O Thousand-armed One possessing every form!”

The Blessed One spoke:

\[47\] “This supreme form has been shown to you, Arjuna,
   by My grace and through My *Yogic* power,
a form of pure, infinite, primal splendor,
   never before seen by anyone but you.

\[48\] Not by Vedic sacrifice or recitation,
   not by rituals, gifts, or austerities,
can I be seen like this in the world of men
   by any other man than you, Arjuna.

\[49\] Do not tremble or be confused
seeing this sublime form of Mine.
Be free from fear, of cheerful mind.
See the form I am in again.”

[p. 71] \[50\] Having spoken in this way to Arjuna,
   Krishna showed him his familiar form again,
and the Great Soul’s gentle, wondrous appearance
   brought peace and calm to his frightened spirit.

And Arjuna said:

\[51\] “Seeing You in this pleasant
human form, O Lord Krishna,
my mind is now composed again
and my thoughts are back to normal.”

The Blessed Lord said:

\[52\] “This form of Mine that you’ve beheld
is difficult for one to see.
Even the gods long constantly
to catch sight of this form of Mine.

\[53\] Not through study of the *Vedas*,
not through austere practices,
not by gifts or sacrifices
can I be seen as you have seen Me.

\[54\] Only by unwavering devotion
can I be seen, O Arjuna,
can I be known, can be attained
in this way, O great warrior.

\[55\] Doing My work, trusting in Me,
devoted to Me, without desire,
free from enmity toward all beings—
one comes to Me, Son of Pandu.”
