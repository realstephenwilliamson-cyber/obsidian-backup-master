A SUMMARY OF BERNARD STIEGLER, 

*TECHNICS AND TIME, 4* 

*Faculties and Functions of Noesis in the Post-Truth Age* 

*by Daniel Ross* 

New Introduction. Opening and Closing 

Stiegler begins by invoking 9/11, as indicating the end of a (or the) world and the necessity of a new one, as a sign that beckons, after an end of possibilities brought about by total economic war. 

Human worlds and human being can always be inhuman. The meaning of being ‘human’ is actually nothing more than to have the possibility of not being inhuman. Cultures are these non-inhuman worlds, now befouled by globalization. Exosomatic life requires noodiversity capable of taking care (today, of the biosphere), and the possibility for care to become hubris is the horizon of the *Antigone* chorus – echoed in the two ‘Warnings to Humanity’ issued by scientists (in 1992 and 2017), which, concerning the possibility that everything worthy of care will be destroyed, are in this way *more-than-tragic*. 

This need for care means that exosomatic life must cultivate the ordeal of the local, precarious différance of microcosm and macrocosm. The sense of this ordeal has been lost. There will be no third warning.

PART ONE. ON THE ‘NOTION OF INFORMATION’ 

Chapter 1. Faculties and Functions in Exosomatization 

*Moore’s law* produces disruption, but is not a law, not scientific: it is prophecy and precept – a technological performativity exceeding Austin, a regime not of truth but of untruth. We must analyse: (1) *miniaturization and speed*; (2) *exosomatization*, metastabilized through a conflict of the *psychic faculties* and a conflict of the *academic faculties*, resulting from a conflict of noetic functions. This exceeds the oppositions between the ideal and the material: it is a question of hypermatter. This conflict now produces, not just intergenerational conflict, but intergenerational disintegration – implying not just a change of epoch, but of era. There is an *absence of epoch*, requiring a new ‘history of truth’ from an exosomatic viewpoint. 

*General organology*: to survive, human beings must (1) *produce* artificial organs; (2) *learn to practise* these organs; (3) *institute* social organizations articulating the generations in relation to these productions and practices (education). It is not just a case of the necessity of functional learning. *Dysfunctions* are what produce phase shifts that in turn produce a history of truth, where the *future* is produced as the *truth* of (overall entropic) becoming – as a *possible future* and a *promise*, but arising from the accumulated past that *contains* a concealed truth remaining always improbably to come. *Truth*: the challenge to continue exosomatization. 

*What are the conditions of education?* They are the conditions of what Kant called the faculties of knowing, desiring and judging (which are all faculties of deciding), but also of the academic faculties. These are the faculties of noesis but they are also historical: exosomatic. But in the Anthropocene, a new conflict arises, destroying the conditions of temporalization, that is, of the exercise of these noetic faculties. This produces the post-truth absence of epoch. 

*Data science*: a new academic faculty tending to bring about the disappearance of the others. But just as bad is for the other faculties to refuse to think and critique data science: hence we require digital studies. *Noesis is the question of the relationship between these psychic faculties and these academic faculties*, implying it is located neither in the subject 

*[p. 3]* ^pg3

nor in the institution (of a discipline): it is the circulation of a process of transindividuation in and by simple and complex exorganisms. 

*Entropy, anti-entropy*: Wiener does not think biological/spiritual and machine processes are the same, but he thinks that they are similarly locally anti-entropic. For Bailly and Longo, anti-entropy occurs only in the *extended critical situation* of life, whose parameters cannot be reduced to points. Hence where Simondon speaks of internal resonance, Longo refers similarly to bio-resonance. This critical organic locality is a *whole constantly in crisis*: more than just feedback, it is self-healing; its complexity is not that of algorithms or physical systems. 

*Living matter cannot be separated in terms of hardware and software*: it is strongly-correlated organogenesis, functioning anti-entropically. Its ‘associated milieu’ is not just the exterior milieu: it is the *genetic* milieu connecting the members of the species and producing and reproducing organogenesis. But simple and complex exorganisms create a milieu that is *not just genetic*, but composed of *artifacts*, first thermodynamic, then cybernetic, leading to confusion about entropy, information and its probabilistic calculability. 

Chapter 2. Negentropy and Anti-Entropy: The Post-Truth of Information 

Attempts to transfer entropy to other fields have mostly been disastrous, always ignoring the exosomatic viewpoint – *the fact that artifacts are tertiary retentions* constituting a new form of life, noetic life. But however erroneously applied, *conjunctions between information and entropy have performatively structured industries and markets*, and transhumanist storytelling has transformed the notions of knowledge and truth: this performativity is thus also a *materialized ideology*. If the new conflict of the faculties and functions is to be resolved, as it must be, then this involves the noetic faculties regaining their ability to produce neganthropic prescriptions. 

*Shannon’s goal was to optimize signal transmission*: he understood informational content in terms of its *improbability, understood as uncertainty*. Understanding this in terms of *yes/no possibilities* makes this uncertainty measurable and quantifiable: the more improbable, the more it matters to me. It is via this understanding that the link 

*[p. 4]* ^pg4

between information and entropy appears (which can be seen in another way in Brillouin, for whom *Maxwell’s demon* constitutes this link). 

Three sources of confusion about *what entropy is* in human, informational, machinic and scientific phenomena: (1) neglect of the question of exosomatization; (2) ignorance of the consequences of the exosomatic viewpoint for scientific activity itself; (3) repression of the question of knowledge of the probable – if it is even possible to quantify the possible, or to reduce the possible to the calculable or the analytical (neither is possible). 

Entropy cannot be thought with the vocabulary of ontology. *Entropy is not*: it *becomes* in a relation whose stakes are the future (bifurcation). So we need Simondon, yet he wrongly thinks information as non-substantial, instead of as a dimension of the technological schematism produced *by* its hypomnesic supports. He wants to think information as ‘relational’, but in fact *it is individuation that is relational*, while *information is hypermaterial*: information therefore both supports and undermines individuation. In psychosocial individuation, the improbable always involves the creation of a law, a care that is not biological but technological, and *performatively* economic, social and juridical. Law as obligation. 

Derrida sees that the anamnesic dialectic is always constituted by the hypomnesic situation, but he fails to conclude from this that mortifying exosomatization is constitutive of *survie* in a way that is irreducible to vital différance. In relation to Heidegger: only by thinking in terms of hypermatter can we uproot the fantasy of the *pharmakon*’s uprooting from some pre-exosomatic soil. 

Exosomatic organs are capable of functioning well with respect to entropy while still destroying biological functioning. In the Entropocene, this becomes the question of *refunctionalizing knowledge that has been defunctionalized qua pure production function*, especially in the age of post-truth, given that it is *dysfunctions that enable individuation to* *‘function’*, to bifurcate. Bifurcations occur through shocks. *How to not be afraid of dysfunctions* bearing bifurcations whose outcomes, which are never calculably controllable, can therefore never be guaranteed? It is *necessary* to worry, but without fearing or denying: the issue of tragedy; this amounts to the question of a *quasi-causal morality* capable of *performatively becoming the cause of its accident*. 

*[p. 5]* ^pg5

So we must distinguish between two kinds of fear, rethink the courage of truth, by: (1) studying physics *with* exosomatic organs; (2) inscribing exosomatic organs into a *point of view*, that of a *locality*, a *singularity*, not just physical or biological but *neganthropological*; (3) seeing this viewpoint as a *process* composed of anamnesic loops and noetic spirals, inscribed in other spirals, calling for a *new cosmology* (not a ‘philosophy of nature’, because it is not a question of ‘universal structures’). Denial and cowardice that do not want to fear *generate fear*…and post-truth. 

*Shannon, Brillouin, Wiener*: all conceive information as calculable. Knowledge is dissolved into information. Hegel’s absolute knowledge becomes *absolute non-knowledge* – which becomes, step-by-step, *totalization*. With planetary reticulation eliminating singularities in the service of speculation and disinvestment, any bifurcations that pose a threat to profit are prohibited. Speculative capital brings the risk of collapse, because it disinvests and denoetizes: the accomplishment of nihilism as total computation, inscribing irresponsibility into algorithms. 

Chapter 3. Informational Tension and Totalization 

*Measurement relates to information* because it is always relative to an *instrumental matrix* providing a system of measurement, which is always *finitely* precise. *This finitude is also that of a locality*, itself composed of artificial organs, and relative to an observer’s ‘situation’: *hence the principle that knowledge is incomplete*. All forms of knowledge, which are always a form of the struggle against anthropic disindividuation, are an individuation qua différance. The implications of Brillouin’s question of measurement go far: to the epistemological question of the *originarily exosomatized functions of science*, in thoroughly exosomatic noetic life. And Montévil adds: anti-entropy is not reducible to the *synchronic* differentiation of constitutive elements; anti-entropy *produces* this negentropy, as an *in-dividuation*, as a *diachronic function producing metastable negentropy*. 

*What then of information?* A necessary question because: (1) information lies at the *heart* of Simondon’s thought; (2) information *technologies* are implemented as *functions*; (3) this implementation functions in a *cretinizing* way, as a result of a *new conflict of the* 

*[p. 6]* ^pg6

*psychic noetic faculties, leading to the disappearance of the institutional noetic faculties*; (4) this conflict itself results from a *defunctionalization and refunctionalization* of noetic functions; (5) this conflict *raises the question* of *two intermittent dimensions* of the noetic soul: *topoi*, constituting the micro-macro-cosmic dimension of noesis, and the *theory of four causes*; (6) the theory of *topoi* returns as that of *endosomatic and exosomatic localities in entropic becoming*; (7) but *efficiency short-circuits this return*, because this *efficiency erases the other three causes* of micro-macro-cosmic dimensionality, that is, of the neganthropological place; (8) but without the other three causes, *efficient causality is bound to ruin itself*, and so computational metaphysics ends up positing the *triviality of the formal cause*, dissolved into probabilistic correlations; (9) *the formal cause can be reconstituted only by exosomatically rediscovering the final cause*, excluded by mechanics then biology. 

*Yuk Hui* recalls that for Simondon *information is disparation – relational*, which, as transductive, *effects a transformation between* these terms and *of* these terms, itself stemming from a *primordial and irreducible phase shift* in individuation, constituting its *dynamism*. Metaphysical *oppositions* can arise thanks to the primordial fact that the psychic individual cannot individuate without participating in collective individuation. It is as the *link between these two levels of individuation* – these *differentiated orders of magnitude*, these *scales* that are *not spontaneously commensurable but between which metastable commensurabilities can be established* – that individuation is participation. This leads to *cosmology*: disparation is always divided and polarized by two microcosmic and macrocosmic points of view: *speculative and sur-realist* – *neganthropic*, and exosomatically *performative*, defining noetic locality as a *step beyond biological negentropy*. 

*Symbolic Misery* argues: (1) Simondon’s account posits *three kinds of individuals* (psychic, collective, technical); (2) yet *there is no technical individuation as such*, contradicting his assertion that there is no individual outside (processes of) individuation. For Simondon, only *living* individuals can *converge* in the true sense of individuation, becoming *teleologically indivisible* (what Longo describes as anti-entropy). Yet he also describes as individuation: (1) the *concretization of machines* resulting from *functional integration*; (2) *technogeographical associated milieus*, transforming natural forces and turning them into *technical functions* – *Gestell*. 

*Simondon neglects two points*: (1) the *pharmacological* dimension of technics qua *process of exosomatization requiring psychosocial individuation*, where *machinism* is a mutation that 

*[p. 7]* ^pg7

*generalizes proletarianization*; (2) the *politics and economics* of this pharmacological question, expressed as *relations of force between individuating and disindividuating tendencies*, between *capital* (calculation) and *work* (the realization of calculation, but reducible to calculation only at the cost of reducing work to labour). *Work-as-individuation* and *proletarianized-labour-as-disindividuation* are two faces of the *pharmakon*, leading to increases of entropy and anthropy, but also opening new negentropic possibilities concretized as neganthropic works. 

Stiegler’s *(re)organization of fields*. *Mechanology*: a branch of neganthropology studying the relation between endosomatic organs, social organizations and *machinic* exosomatic organs (but requiring a pharmacological exorganology). *Exorganology*: mediating between endosomatic organology and mechanology. 

*Why does Simondon not want to talk about technical individuation?* Because it cannot occur without psychosocial individuation, which, like any individuation, is *dis-automatizing*. And this is indeed equally true of technical individuation, which forms a maieutic, *provided that it is interpreted by a psychosocial operator*. 

*Industrial technical individuation* does functionally integrate psychic and collective individuals, *yet this proves to be disintegrating*: destructive and unsustainable. Even if such a disintegrating individuation is impossible *de droit*, it is possible *de fait*. This disintegration means that the *production of human-originated value can no longer be renewed*, eliminating opportunities for bifurcation, eventually disintegrating the technical system itself. But we must recognize this *fact* of technical individuation: what is indivisible is what cannot be divided without *eventually* being destroyed. *Anthropic* technical individuation emerges from exosomatization (as has always been the case), but *it now presents itself as the very object of the conflict of the faculties*. 

The penultimate paragraph of Simondon’s introduction to *On the Mode of Existence of Technical Objects* sees *the machine as a way of opposing the death of the universe*: strikingly non-pharmacological. Simondon’s naïve *belief that mechanology can contain hubris* leads to his blindness about what the twenty-first century will bring. He repeats Plato’s gesture that assigns to dialectics the function of controlling the *pharmakon*. But as Derrida points out, this dialectic has *always already* been affected by the *pharmakon*. 

*[p. 8]* ^pg8

Nevertheless, Simondon is crucial to any attempt to overcome contemporary denial, because he does put technics at the heart of individuation. What we therefore require is a critique of his notion of individuation that starts from the lack of a pharmacological understanding of the way in which, in complex exorganisms, information both requires exosomatization and is required by it. 

Hui shows how Simondon rejects Shannon’s quantification of information as insufficient, but that he does so without critiquing the computational definition as such. Simondon argues for a non-probabilistic *quality* or *tension* of information, but does not refer to negentropy in Schrödinger’s sense. *In a way, Schrödinger introduces protention as characteristic of organic matter*, so it is surprising that Simondon doesn’t go in this direction, given that his ‘tension’ presupposes a *protention* of the receiver: the receiver must be *tensed*, in at-tention, in *pro-tension*. *Protention*: a phenomenological concept, taken up by Derrida to characterize life. Protention constitutes the *possibility of tension*, which, *fed by ‘information’*, is *amplified* by the latter and amplifies it in return: this is more than just ‘feedback’. 

*Hui on Simondon*: a signification is produced by the *resolution of a tension between signal and scheme*. As relation rather than form, information is therefore the process through which the transindividual is constituted *locally*, within a system charged with potentials – *tensed*. But this ‘scheme’ must be understood as composed of tertiary retentions, according to the *hypermaterial specificities* of its support, which means that the schema is inseparable from the information itself. 

*Bavelas* asserted that an experimental informational process involved a change of probabilities that certain hypotheses were correct. *Shannon* denied the subjective element, but *MacKay* retorted that ‘information’ must be defined in a way that does indeed relate to the change in mentality of the receiver produced by signification. So: in-formation becomes trans-formation, defined *not by what it is but by what it does*. This resonates with Simondon: *an operation in a system, producing a transformation*. A difference that creates differences. Here, information can be replaced with *writing* (in Derrida’s sense) – trace, retention, protention – *provided that we distinguish regimes of différant traces*, constituting a *noetic* différance that we can begin to think as individuation – with and beyond Simondon and Derrida.

PART TWO. PHARMACOLOGY OF LOCALITY 

Chapter 4. The Exosomatization of Noesis 

Conceiving machines and living things as organisms, *Wiener’s cybernetics* is the science of what they have in common. For *Simondon*, even if machines ‘tend towards concretization’, they remain divisible; his *mechanology* is the science of the *process of concretization of technical objects*, but also of their *orchestration* – of this concretization and of the technogeographical milieus within which it occurs. The association of a milieu with a technical object produces a third milieu of which this object becomes the origin via ‘adaptation-concretization’, involving a geographical and technical *techno-logical performativity*, which Simondon oddly describes as meaning that the technical object becomes ‘the condition of itself’, and which leads to the generalization of technogeographical associated milieus – the Anthropocene. 

In this biospheric macrocosm, *massively urbanized human geography becomes a new natural resource*, like water for the Guimbal turbine but at the biospheric scale, via data centres and so on, *accomplishing nihilism* as thoroughly computational capitalism and dissolving public things – law. What leads from the artisanal stage to the industrial stage? Well-known answer: the reconvergence of science and technology, turning formal knowledge into a weapon of economic war. 

For Simondon, if man can set up technogeographical associated milieus, it is because he is himself the bearer of an associated milieu, and if we can create technical beings, it is because we have within us a play of matter-form relations. *But is it not that, from the outset, the noetic soul has exosomatically produced its non-living but vital organs* that transductively constitute the matter/form relation by making artifices, as the *primordial schema* of an imagination that is *neither transcendental nor empirical*, and that no doubt emerges from a *pre-noetic* stage of exosomatization (prior to the noesis of intuition, understanding, imagination and reason)? 

If, as Simondon thinks, it is a question of the ability to imagine what we produce, *then it is a question of the conditioning of the present by the future, a horizon of finalities, formed by protentions*. But such a conditioning is possible only because a future is projected 

*[p. 10]* ^pg10

on the basis of tertiary retentions, constituting an associated milieu as both *transindividual* (collective) and *preindividual* (bequeathed by deceased individuals, bearing potentials and tensions that are the conditions of the tension of information). So it is a question *neither* of *equating* machines and living things (Wiener) *nor* of *ranking* them (Simondon), but of their *transductive co-individuation* through the exosomatic co-genesis of metastabilized functions. 

It is indeed a matter of establishing an *anti-entropic industrial age*. This requires: (1) a *new critique of noesis*; (2) a *new theory of information, calculation and the improbable*; (3) a *new critique of economy*, based on the techno-logical performativity of the sciences, subjected to efficient causality. We must learn to live quasi-causally with indeterminacy: within anthropy (*das Man*) and neganthropy (noetic différance between being and beings). Wiener raises this question of indeterminacy to the cosmic scale – via Augustine, the devil and incompleteness. 

In cosmic terms, the question of being-for-death then becomes the question of *being-for-life beyond my death*, and its relationship to hypomnesic and *orthothetic* tertiary retention. Kant did not see the pharmacological tenor of *orthotēs*. We should see Heidegger’s ‘resoluteness’ as the quasi-cause of the undetermined, performatively and locally transindividuated on the basis of potentials introduced into the *there* by tertiary retentions, particularly hypomnesic ones insofar as they require interpretation. 

Simondon wants to liberate information from its technological tenor, so he posits the independence of information from its supports. Yet he himself says that *the technical means by which human beings communicate forms the medium of the possibility of invention by supporting what he names the transindividual*. *Shannon*: information is entropic when, being unexpected, it disorganizes a waiting psychic organization. *Wiener*: information is anti-entropic because it increases organization. 

But contra Shannon, *what is not expected cannot affect the one who was not expecting it*. *Only one who is tensed, expectant, is affectable*. For Simondon, the increase in organization is *amplification*: the very definition of individuation, and precisely *not* a probabilistic calculation: it is the fruit of an individuation that is transindividually metastabilized via technical supports that are tertiary retentions. 

*[p. 11]* ^pg11

But my retentions and protentions may also be *unconscious*: repressed and denied protentions appearing only as the unexpected, but which, affecting me, become quasi-causally what I am – my future insofar as it bifurcates against all odds. *Being-for-life* (which always composes with being-for-death *and is always noetically sexual*, both *thanatological* and *erotological*), which is not ‘authentic’ but *courageous*, *transforms entropic fate into neganthropic spiritual energy* – libidinal energy concretizable by the faculty of dreaming in exosomatization. 

The receiver is always multidimensional: inscribed in a process, associated with a milieu, tensed because out of phase, unfinished, in différance. What he expects, he does not know, meaning both: (1) things he does not know whether they will ever happen; (2) the *irreducibly indeterminate*, not inscribable in any horizon because unconscious, improbable, other – not just a psychoanalytic but a *cosmic* other. This is something beyond Heidegger’s ‘undetermined’: inscribed in the fate of the cosmos and announcing itself as the *pharmacology of Gestell*. 

*If information arrives to me, it is because it affects me*. This involves: (1) a relationship between *primary, secondary and tertiary retentions generating primary, secondary and tertiary protentions*; (2) in the Anthropocene, this occurs *immediately* as a tertiary retention, capable of being disseminated very far, decontextualizing it and thus affecting my *there*; (3) this produces a *confusion and telescoping* of *primary and tertiary* retentions, the former presenting themselves in advance *as* tertiary; (4) all these arrangements of tertiary retentions are techno-logical schemas trans-forming the *there*; *tele-technologies*, where techno-logical performativity *always begins here*, with this *there*. 

*Recapitulation*: (1) exosomatic organs are *pharmaka*, both increasing entropy and reducing it negentropically, requiring knowledge; (2) we must introduce the terms *anthropy and neganthropy*; (3) Lévi-Strauss does not see the *function* of his own knowledge in terms of exosomatization, and therefore rejects Leroi-Gourhan; (4) today’s informational organs produce epochlessness and post-truth, hence we require a pharmacology of information and knowledge technologies redefining (*transvaluing*) material, formal, and final causes in the context of computational efficient causality; (5) we therefore require a new architectonics of informational instruments involved in *impure because pharmacological reason*. 

*[p. 12]* ^pg12

*Nicholas Georgescu-Roegen* introduces the term *exosomatization* into economics, referring to bio-economy because it is indeed a question of keeping entropy low. But this cannot be kept within the framework of entropy/negentropy. As a result, Georgescu-Roegen cannot account for the *performative and quasi-causal process entailed by the pharmacological character of tertiary retentions*. 

The condition of noesis is pharmacological, and therefore *atranscendental*: its conditions of possibility are its conditions of impossibility. This atranscendental philosophy is a transvaluation of the values of metaphysics annihilated by the latter’s accomplishment as *Gestell*. The Overman gives way to *Neganthropos*. *Transhumanism*, on the other hand, is *archi-nihilistic*, positing that machines will become more intelligent than us, and yet that we will still somehow be transformed into immortals. We will be perfect but we will be cretinized – such would be the fate of the Overman in absolute non-knowledge. 

Chapter 5. Seven Theses on the Conflict of the Faculties and Functions 

1\. Kant’s *schematism of the imagination is exosomatic*, and artificial retention is the condition of all noetic retention and protention, and therefore all rationality. 

2\. The faculty of knowing evolves via the progressive exteriorization of the *noetic functions* that are intuition, imagination, understanding, reason. This begins with *hypomnesic* tertiary retentions, which are both *analytic and synthetic* (discretizing and unifying): acts of synthesis are always *interpretations* (*selecting* retentions in order to *project* protentions). 

3\. Between the Renaissance and today: (a) the *intuition* operates through constantly improving observational instruments (affecting the iGen); (b) the *understanding* evolves through computing; (c) the *imagination* becomes an *industrial function* when observational instruments become instruments for the production of behavioural programs (the culture industry). Heidegger did not see that noesis is exosomatic différance, and that what is prepared in Plato’s time (via alphabetical tertiary retention) is what Kant will describe as the *division of the faculty of knowledge into functions*: 

*[p. 13]* ^pg13

the understanding functions *analytically* and reason functions *synthetically*, producing bifurcations, *ideas* functioning to constitute the kingdom of ends, itself constituting the neganthropological horizon of *consistences*. 

4\. Today, data can present itself only as *formatted and calculable*, whereas in Kant’s time, data meant the *given*, which was characterized by its *diversity, not yet being ‘formatted’*, such ‘formatting’ being for Kant the function of the concepts of the understanding. 

5\. The data economy and ‘big data’ are based on probabilistic models operating *faster* than the endosomatic nervous system. The noetic body is noetic *only insofar as it is exosomatized and divided by the functions and faculties of knowing, desiring and judging* – but this speed differential undermines this possibility. 

6\. Current computational fixed capital leads to collapse, demanding new forms of knowledge, ways of taking care of the *pharmakon*, a new general and therefore libidinal economy. Such a new economy requires a *transvaluation of all values*, which *can be established only as a new economy of the drives* – *which alone produce value*. Today we face a new cretinism, which is a *deficiency*: a deprivation of social interactions, replaced by calculations, turning the psyche into a desert that grows, rotting noesis. Capitalism might be an episteme, but, by generalizing proletarianization, *it becomes an anti-episteme* – thereby becoming *suicidal*, given that *we need knowledge in order to live*, that this anti-epistemic capitalism *accelerates exosomatization in the service of shameless speculation*, and that it *fails to renew libidinal energies*. 

7\. *Neganthropology* posits that it *is* possible to renew knowledge, provided we *critique the limits of exosomatization* as pharmacological. The *there* of neganthropology is *Gestell*, being without Dasein, who has become proletarianized and indifferent. Neganthropology is *neither science nor philosophy*: it is a *limit situation requiring a leap capable of mobilizing a reinvention of libidinal economy*. The future *does not lie in degrowth or deindustrialization*, but in a pharmacology of hyper-industrial society. The intensification of the tension of anthropy and neganthropy: *hubris*, the *pharmakon*, *Gewalt*. The task of neganthropology is to complete deconstruction from the exosomatic viewpoint, and it must revisit Deleuze and Guattari on quasi-causality and ecosophy. It will need to redefine the four causes from the standpoint of quasi-causality, techno-logical performativity and exosomatization. 

*[p. 14]* ^pg14

Chapter 6. Market of Information, Calculability, Knowledge 

In its modern form, *information is a commodity that loses value over time as it is disseminated across a space that is also a market*. Producing a world in constant transformation, it becomes imperative for individuals to orient themselves (e.g., the history of Left and Right), leading to Hegel’s everyday informational practice, the ‘*realistic prayer*’. What informs me is what *gives me an advantage insofar as others are not aware of it*: a *rivalrous good*. This gives rise to *philistinism* (Hölderlin) and the submission of *otium* to *negotium*, as the bourgeoisie seize hold of the aristocratic heritage hitherto practised as *otium*, cultivating incalculables as freedom. 

1844: *telegraph*. 1877: *telephone*. 1881: *mechanography*. Information becomes a consumable good with a calculable value. What had been circulated by ‘clerics’ becomes a commodity on the market of ‘news’: new product, new process, new social change as a result, new conflict brought by such changes – *progress*. Information becomes the opposite of knowledge, which is *non-rivalrous*. If informational value evaporates, it is an entropic tendency. Information is then: (1) a *signal in time*, vectorizing a space homogenized by a distribution network; (2) an *economy*, but one constituted by calculability, to which anything can be reduced. 

Information is calculable *only up to the point where it is yet to produce knowledge*. Calculability depends on homogeneous space, but if the space is a *heterogeneous locality*, then orientation involves transforming the locality according to criteria that amount to knowledge: such localities are *theres*, places that are also *viewpoints*. But information *can* turn back into knowledge, as can happen when we read the paper ‘late’, involving *not just comprehension but surprehension*, requiring the synthetic power of reason *to judge what we had hitherto tended just to consume*. 

Defining information as *negentropic* relied on relating it to biology: as what leads a system to change its state. Simondon adds: I can be affected only by what individuates me because I’m already tensed – *information is a tension*. Hence it is an *open system* (von Bertalanffy); closed systems are entropic. In biology, the open system is the *organism*. The *exosomatic* organism qua open system we call the *idiotext*. 

*[p. 15]* ^pg15

Information industries tend to reduce the idiotext to a computational subsystem, which is possible for two reasons: (1) the performativity of lightning-quick information supports (speeds made possible partly by Moore’s law); (2) idiotexts oscillate between neganthropic (singularizing) and anthropic (desingularizing) tendencies, hence the noetic soul is always *intermittent*. 

*Sense data* furnishes the understanding with the ‘manifold of intuition’, but such data is always the object of an interpretation, via the *subjective principle of differentiation* that arranges internal and external sense. The idiotext is *above all* an interpretive system, *which interprets the infidelity of its milieu and what it means for the promise of fidelity*. *Fidelity*: the condition for maintaining the openness of the open system. *Truth*: the bifurcatory power of a system that, *without this knowledge*, would be bound to close. 

On the *ordinary* register, transindividual significations may indeed present themselves as finite, discrete, calculable – *grammatization as analysis*. But shifting to the *extra-ordinary*, significations generate meaning that is not soluble into analysis, being instead the *fruit of a synthesis* – hermeneutic, but not a circle or a feedback loop; rather, a *spiral*, one that is performatively individuating, intensifying the singularity of the idiomatic locality that is the idiotext. 

Data of intuition: a punch, a slap, a shock, the death of one’s father – *events*. 

*Herder* saw in man’s *verticalization* the principle of his noetic development. Kant accuses him of therefore conceiving reason as nothing more than an acquisition, whereas for Kant himself reason is transcendental, without genesis. So Herder sees what Kant fails to see, yet he ignores the fact that artificial organs defunctionalize and refunctionalize endosomatic organs. He does not see that artificial organs are exosomatic, *secreting a form of life that needs reason*. Noesis turns the *Umwelt* into the *Welt*, a *there* formed by exosomatic organs, arrangements of which constitute a world or cosmos, presenting itself in complex exorganisms but tending to return to disorder, hence requiring *care*. Care of the *there* includes education: recapitulations of world-comings (of past worlds), such that there-being ceaselessly recapitulates anew its birth and rebirth into the world. Dasein must always re-dis-cover the world that never ceases to absent itself, sometimes to the point of engendering madness. 

*[p. 16]* ^pg16

Today, madness is everywhere and the going-ill of the world has crossed a threshold. Neoliberalism prevailed because it embraced the destruction of collective individuation, replacing it with markets based on information. But the origins of this evolution are older, lying in the dynamic of grammatization, and this is why a new critique of political economy must be specific to the age of information and digital grammatization. 

Neoliberalism denies that *noetic différance is irreducible to vital différance*, *positing that survival forms the only valid selection criteria*. This vision is both reinforced and undermined by the Anthropocene: denial has never been so painful and difficult, yet this makes it even stronger – *madness*. The *there* has been overwhelmed by information (as Nietzsche foresaw), but this raises anew the question of locality. *For without locality,* *there can be no negentropy, no anti-entropy and no neganthropy*. 

Chapter 7. Neganthropic Locality 

In *Being and Time*, locality is the *there* of there-being. The idiotext conceives locality as a process and dynamic *always already de-localized by exosomatization*. Dasein sees only that it is ‘in the world’, sees only that it ‘has to be’, but remains as unconscious of its technicity as it is of being-towards-death that exorganizes its protentions, these being interpretations of retentions – those it has lived and those it has inherited. 

In the *there*, the present is constituted psychically and collectively (and more than both) through what happens to me/us, establishing a di-mensionality without which no individuation can occur. *Stereotype/traumatype*: anthropic/neganthropic potentials expressed by automatisms and involuntary recurrences in the encounter with whatever is there (slogan, madeleine). Traumatypes intensify the *singularity* of the occurrence; stereotypes

Psychic unconsciouses *weave their motifs on the textuality of preindividual backgrounds*, constituting a locality: the idiomatic noodiversity of a neganthropy. It is the *différance of fact and law* that opens knowledge to what exceeds the understanding – as *neganthropic promise*, negotiated as a *com-promise* between stereotypes and traumatypes, as a play of tendencies through which différance is localized as the transindividuation of an always greater *there*. 

The *drives* stem from entropy/negentropy, and neganthropology *economizes* them as desire, *disautomatizing* the drives and installing anthropy/neganthropy as *survie*. On one hand, the improbable is unexpected by the idiotext; on the other hand, *the idiotext leaps upon it*, individuates itself in it, constituting and destituting its sur-vival – testifying to the fact that the idiotext was waiting (*only*) for it, but without knowing it, *because its stereotypes were mobilized in order to not know it*. It awaited *only this* in the non-knowledge of the promise – and as *anamnesis*.

Quasi-causally, à la *Whitehead’s attack* on the milieu, *Canguilhem’s infidelity* and *normativity* of the milieu, and *Leroi-Gourhan’s technicity* of the milieu.

PART THREE. ORGANOLOGY OF INTERMITTENCE 

Chapter 8. Idiomatic Différance 

*General organology* is neither a science nor a philosophy. It is: (1) an *agreement* between disciplines concerning the need to consider the exosomatic viewpoint; (2) a *method* of dealing in a *transdisciplinary* way with the *pharmakon in the current state of emergency*. General organology is a *general heuristic* whose function is to constitute *agreements* between disciplines – and where a *discipline* is a *rule of life* allowing exosomatic life to maintain its open and promising neganthropic future. 

Scientific disciplines need disciplines of *care in general*: a *mutually deproletarianizing exchange between domestic and scientific disciplines* through a *new practice of intermittence*. *Noesis*: the performative (sur-realistic) power to develop the *quasi-cause of imperfection*, of the default, as the performative affirmation of *right* beyond the *fact* of imperfection, transindividuated as what is necessary – as *obligation*. 

Singularity nourishes a *symbolic order* by disturbing it through what exceeds it, which always initially appears as disorder. Singularity: *idiocy* – hence this idiocy is also *dia-bolical*, in that it can become the *opposite* of the singular, such as a new cliché. Singularity constitutes *diabols*, which are the *condition* of symbols. 

*Technics and Time, 5*: (1) *primordial tension* is what, stemming from the *there* of the *polis*, constitutes *hubris*, excess as counterpart of the default, as diabolical operator of individuation and disindividuation, as *pharmakon*; (2) *metaphysics*, inaugurated in *Phaedrus*, denies this dia-bolical dia-mension of noesis. Yet *denial is not just rejection*: Plato posits that anamnesis and therefore the dialectic *precede* hypomnesis and the *pharmakon*, so that the task of the dialectic would be to *control* the *pharmakon* (a program prescribed in *Republic*), understanding *alētheia* as *orthotēs*, feeding into the Kantian differentiation of the functions of analysis and synthesis. Heidegger’s ‘*history of truth*’ should be understood as the history of this denial, and as a history of denial *that itself has a function* in the différantiation of noesis – through the *distinction between analysis and synthesis that was made possible and necessary by literal grammatization*. 

*[p. 20]* ^pg20

*Courage* means: to do with imperfection what is necessary *to remake the noetic faculties and functions*, where: (1) *locality* (idiomaticity of the idiotext) is *in principle* insoluble and *in fact* soluble; (2) the *idiomatization of the dia-bolical* constitutes the metastable equilibrium of the *there* that must cultivate its neganthropic potentials, *law negotiating with fact across technical tendencies that affect it as its dream* – requiring the arrangement of the Great and the Small. This is an *a-transcendental situation* that involves: (1) the irreducibly constitutive and constitutively destitutive *pharmakon*; (2) an interminable struggle to take care of locality. It is never a matter of *demonizing* the *diabolon*, but rather of cultivating it, transindividuating it, as the fortune of living worthily. 

*The task of the remaining three volumes of Technics and Time*: to learn the conditions under which locality can be re-formed in biospheric reticulation. *Symbols and Diabols*: on the dia-bolical as constituting the *idios* as the unthought of philosophy. *War of Spirits*: on the war of *revenant* idiomatic localities in the globalized grammatization process leading to a libidinal diseconomy via global childishness. *Idiocy*: offering an atranscendental approach to the idiotext, and interpreting the eschatological Anthropocenic situation. 

To deny the constitutive and destitutive scope of the *pharmakon* is to hypostasize dynamic tendencies into mythical, religious, ideological, Hollywoodian and post-veridical figures. *Judea*: the *pharmakon* presents itself as the serpent and engenders the *scapegoat and guilt, replacing shame*. *The Book* presents idiomatic différance as babelization. But idiomatic différance is the *condition* of neganthropic différantiation, requiring the localization of a taking place. 

Can *algorithmic* tertiary retention engender *bipolar intensification*? How to localize a *reticulated, deterritorialized idiomatic taking-place?* The planes of *territorialization and urbanity are not reducible*: a *locality without a neighbourhood, without shared responsibility, is irrevocably anthropic*. Value produced by transvaluation must be reproducible, and this begins with *local care taken of the conditions of subsistence*. *Inhabited territory is the first surface on which tertiary retentions are inscribed*: it can be an object of care only as *idiomatized* space. Faced with nationalist regression, *we should not deny the territorial dimension of the there*, or the *insolubility of ghosts*: we should guarantee the *there*’s opening and its inscription. Until now, *calendarity* has organized *shared intermittence* within a *cardinality* itself common to all, *defining the confines of a cosmology*.

Chapter 9. Intermittence in the National There 

*Noetic intermittence*: there is always an oscillation between the ordinary and extra-ordinary, between descents and ascents. These oscillations are metastabilized by the calendarity and cardinality of the *there*, through the cultivation of this intermittence: some (the *ordained*) are assigned to the *ministry of intermittence* and others (the congregation) only to attend to *ritual* intermittence. In Western monotheism, *otium* is not just worship and liturgy: it is the practice of intermittent truth in the arts, letters, law, science, etc. A privilege of the nobility, *otium* then comes to be claimed by the bourgeoisie (after Diderot, the *Académie royale de peinture et de sculpture*, etc.). *Otium* becomes a dimension of bourgeois life at the moment when the industrial economy begins to rearrange science and technics *so as to subject otium to negotium*. 

*Philistinism* then appears, purchasing *otium* at a nice price, followed by the culture industry, and eventually the ‘*wage earners of the ideal*’ (Milner), who want to save their social status as a *secular public service*, which neoliberalism turns into a service *industry*. We must consider the constitution of this group: (1) as a dimension of the integration of *otium* into *negotium*, on its way to economic war; (2) in the context of new forms of hypomnesic tertiary retention, starting with analogue, *disintegrating this culture claimed by the bourgeoisie* and making it possible for the analogue *program industries* to work against the *program institutions* based on literal tertiary retention (schools, universities, etc.). 

*Otium* and consistences are thereby diluted into this philistinism, *as the feeling for the extra-ordinary comes to be replaced by the sensational*. The culture industry may not initially have been informational, yet it does, from the outset, subject calendarity to the calculability of *ratings*, at the same time reducing noetic souls to their *purchasing* actions. Whereas, prior to the dawn of the culture industry, noetic souls would go in search of the extra-ordinary, beginning with the ‘desire for stories’. 

*Intermittence has thus been dissolved*. But without it, there is *no desire*, and therefore *no love*, and therefore *no philia* – only *disintegration*. Drive-based society no longer has any *there*, which requires *love of the there* – *attachment* to it. Reading Wiener with Lotka, we 

*[p. 22]* ^pg22

must once again become capable of facing entropy *worthily*, that is, *noetically*: noesis is the dignity of life, its *grandeur* as the ability to intermittently change order of magnitude. This dignity is what makes life worth living *up until the last moment*. 

It is what is *possible-and-impossible* insofar as one *cannot* say ‘after me, the deluge’, and insofar as one *can* say, *after having lived a lot*, that it was worth the effort because *there will be other lives transmitting what makes it worth this effort* – a *living on*, continuing the *incompletion of life, and, beyond life, of noesis*. The fading of intermittence and the evaporation of *alētheia* generate *generalized cowardice*. As collective individuation unravels, psychic individuation disaggregates, the drives come unbound, and the result is a proliferation of *unpredictable and uncontrollable behaviour* – unleashing the death drive, the *entropic drive* contained and retained by neganthropic locality. 

This dissolution by efficiency makes it necessary to *revisit the theory of four causes* to discover the conditions of a *new sustainable efficiency*, in an age where hypercomplex exorganisms throw law into question in new ways. *New local intermittences* are required, associated with the *caretakers of diversality*, *not by ignoring that form of care that is universalism*, but by *subjecting it to neganthropological critique*. Furthermore, we should *compensate caregivers* for the care they take of themselves by taking care of others, entailing a new libidinal economy that *remunerates desire* as the condition of contribution – a necessity in order to move towards the Neganthropocene. 

What organizes the *solidarity* of complex exorganisms is what the Greeks called *philia*. Politics is what generates the rule of law, making *jurisprudential bifurcation* possible – transforming tribes into *demes* (*Cleisthenes*), into a *demos*. There is no *there* without attachment, without wanting to leave and return: the experience of enlargement, the haunting of the extra-ordinary. *Enlargement*: an individuation that enriches, stemming from the aesthetic of the *Divers* and passing through *exoticism* (*Victor Segalen*) – a struggle in the tension between the entropic Universe and the negentropic Diverse. If it is true that this enlargement can reach the point of the ‘world citizen’, then it becomes necessary to read Kant’s cosmopolitanism with Mauss, Vernadsky, Lotka and Schmitt. 

*[p. 23]* ^pg23

The *there* is always more or less exotic, *haunted by what remains absent*. *It must be desired and loved*: intermittently present in extra-ordinary ways. In globalization, intermittences disappear, engendering *cynical lovelessness*, stupefied by efficiencies. 

The *psyche* is a succession of individuations *resolving problematic states by communicating with what is larger and smaller than it*. This ‘communication’ operates through tertiary retentions. We must *reconstitute orders of magnitude and relations of scale* so as to make it possible to *cultivate* the intermittent extra-ordinariness of exotic différance – as the *fourfold causality of the Diverse*. 

What’s needed is a *macroeconomy that organizes the relations between all scales of locality* – between the many scales of idiotextuality. This *new culture of intermittences* would no longer be cultural or religious, but *economico-political*: differentiating the calculable and the incalculable will become an *institutional* function. The primordial finality required by the archi-protention brought to the cosmic scale is *to locally preserve noesis in the biosphere*, which must be preserved in its *exosomatic* becoming. 

After our hypomnesic withdrawal, it seems impossible to *recuperate*. In the past, technological nativities made possible *fertile revenances* (not just ‘old demons’) by mixing past and future, accomplishing idiotextuality. After a few generations of withdrawal, this no longer takes place. 

When traumatypes fail to give intermittent access to the extra-ordinary, because the rubbish of institutional waste fails to decompose into *humus*, *monsters return* – this is therefore a question of *recycling*. *In philosophy, recycling is called critique* – which analyses, decomposes. For the *there* to be there, it must come, and come *back, born and reborn through knowledge*: pursuing individuation qua incompleteness. *Noetic différance is an always recommenced birth* – a (re)conversion, a *change of viewpoint*. 

With industrialization, access to intermittences becomes secular, disciplinary, democratic, egalitarian, institutional. This access then comes to be related to the *history* of knowledge and truth – including in law. *School* becomes a way *to introduce new generations to the history of truth*, and into the history of the *national there*. Nevertheless, this was done as if the *national there* was the *repository of the universal*. 

*[p. 24]* ^pg24

The *academic system* is set up as a condition for the formation of industrial complex exorganisms, replacing religious forms of the intermittence of truth with the intermittence of *historical* truth unfolding as progress, controversies, disputes. School, however, *tends to eliminate the experience of the intermittence of knowledge*, promising to *resolve* contradictions, turning knowledge into information. Nevertheless, it *starts* with the idea of *converting* illiterate psychic individuals into ‘enlightened’ citizen-producers. 

Religious or secular, the *culture of intermittency* is *always* that of *attention to the extra-ordinary*. *Attention* is constituted by arranging psychic retentions and protentions, underpinned by collective ones and supported by tertiary ones. Given that the *intermittent noetic being needs the extra-ordinary in order to pursue exosomatization that begins with the dream*, then when the extra-ordinary ceases to be cultivated, what results is an age of *wild conversions* exploding with the *energy of despair*. 

Chapter 10. Detachment of the Id 

*We need programs*: exosomatization requires the *formation of attention*, attention being what takes care of the *pharmaka* that it also produces. When this formation is forced to compete with the *attention economy*, it becomes a *deformation*, which is possible *only because hypomnesic tertiary retentions can produce both long circuits and short-circuits*. Long circuits *go back over the spiral turns of idiotextuality*, connecting the largest and the smallest from out of the default of origin and towards the common infinity of the end. But the control now taken over behavioural programs also amounts to a *liquidation of the super-ego*, which had hitherto carried out the binding of exosomatic solidarity called *philia*, based on *psychic and collective identificatory attachment*. 

If long circuits of transindividuation are *infinitely* long, then science and knowledge are connected to *desire*: as with love, their objects *present themselves only as infinite*. With secular public education, the consciousness that is formed is also a *historical configuration of the organization of the id*. The critical consciousness of the mature individual, forming *historical consciousness*, articulates in a very long circuit the universal history of humanity qua *we* with the individual history of an *I*. 

*[p. 25]* ^pg25

The institution of *industrial democracy* entails this formation of attention as *political organization*. But consciousness, whose seat is the ego, belongs to the id that *overflows* this ego. It is the *id* that accomplishes the ‘subterranean’ (and mystagogical) connections between the largest and smallest. Because this situation is irreducible, the neganthropic experience of truth remains intermittent and local, *ça* and *là*. Long circuits go back over the intricacies of the turns in the idiotextual arrangements between largest and smallest, *infinitely* large and *infinitely* small in a way that can be projected only by a cosmology that we are here calling surrealist. 

With *Ferry’s national education*, ascending generations were *technologically native* on the basis of the *same* kinds of tertiary retentions as the descending generations. *Before* Ferry, it is true that the generations *did not interiorize the letter so as to constitute a generation of peers with access to knowledge as fellow citizens*, but they did still grow up *frequenting the Book*. What the Enlightenment challenged is the notion that the Book constitutes the first and last criterion of the interpretation and judgment of the worth of books. 

When the *national there* is deterritorialized by the culture industries, the id is disintegrated, techno-logical nativities are disarticulated, and infinitely long circuits of transindividuation are left inaccessible (since they require the id). Intermittence and knowledge come to be replaced by information. But what also made this replacement possible is the fact that modernity understood truth as *certainty in the service of exosomatization*, repressing and denying uncertainty and the undetermined. The potential to enrich knowledge thus found itself exhausted when higher education turned into a global market. 

The ability to *enrich and renew knowledge* is *neganthropy*. That this ability could crumble away is possible only because grammatization has led to the liquidation of idiomaticities. *Formal idioms* are constituted by formal causality, *which can never be isolated from other causalities*. When, in the name of fighting against anthropocentrism and its teleologies, *final causality is neutralized*, the door is opened to *turning formal idioms into informational formalisms that eliminate all idiocy*, all neganthropy, all capacity to bifurcate. *Formalism is then reduced to calculation*. Simondon did not see this. 

Compulsory education, then, is the way in which *the nation-state is constituted as an industrial democracy of designers and producers of exosomatization*. But in so doing, it also sets 

*[p. 26]* ^pg26

up the Anthropocene: (1) the academic system is based on Newtonian epistemology, *The Conflict of the Faculties* stemming from modern experimental science and its determinism; (2) this education does indeed *adapt* to industrial production, adapting consumers to market changes, teaching ‘skills’, shifting the role of books from study to ‘use’; (3) the acceleration of computing leaves education and law lagging behind technology and the market, causing them to lose *credit*. 

*Disciplines* are what, through moments of exception and banalization, emerge as knowledge. Traumatypes are transindividuated only by dissolving into the ‘well-known’, through which *their traumatizing capacity is forgotten*. In this way, traumatypes slip back into the preindividual, *perhaps* to return. 

The *possibility of bifurcating* via the intermittency of truth always supposes a *conversion of the gaze*. We *access the extra-ordinary* by crossing an order of magnitude irreducible to quantification – a ‘qualitative leap’ (even if it increasingly *passes through* quantification). Heidegger fails to see that *Weltgeschichtlichkeit* involves *not just recounting but counting*: as a result, he too ends up perpetuating metaphysical denial. 

When the noetic soul accesses the extra-ordinary, *what had seemed impossible* becomes the *only possibility* – the extra-ordinarity of truth coming to appear as necessary and evident. To feel this necessity, *a conversion is required*. For centuries, such a conversion involved *vows*, differentiating the officiants from the officiated. Then a change occurs in the relationship between *otium* and *negotium*. But *the transcendental conception of the schematism dooms the Enlightenment to having its reason become rationalization*, which will in turn become *post-veridical denoetization*, diluting all intermittences into the homogeneous phase spaces of the disembedded market (Polanyi). 

The *dimensionality* of noesis qua experience of improbability is initially regularized by setting up forms of order, rules of consecration of times and places, etc. After being secularized, *otium* was set on the path to submitting to a rule, no longer intermittent but permanent: *production*, becoming calculable, with the *incalculable hitherto known as God shifting to become merely what guarantees the market as homogeneous phase space (money)*. In the Anthropocene, the dimensionality between existence and consistence is *nolonger-there*. Striving to eliminate imperfection, it leads to barbarism, madness and the 

*[p. 27]* ^pg27

sacrifice of the *pharmakos*. Unless the *desire to know* is reopened, this madness will become ever more blind, murderous and suicidal. 

Every society has an *experience of truth*: an experience of intermittence as the experience of relations of scale between orders of magnitude, making it possible to create pharmacological orders conferring complex exorganisms with coherence, solidity and law (as a constitution of violence). A *history of truth* should be a *history of selection and its criteria*: this is *every* question. The privileged being questions only because it is *put into question*. Selection is *the* question as what is *no longer* questioned, having been automated: the metastable and regulated sharing of selection criteria, composed of psychic and collective retentions and protentions. 

*Shared criteria are delineated on scales*. Rules are selection criteria, themselves requiring local criteria of interpretation and application: this is why an idiotext is irreducibly local. *Interpretation is individuation*: of the *rule*; of the *interpreter*. What plays out between the rule and the viewpoint is the dia-bolical, which exceeds the rule, the rule being symbolic, synchronized with other rules. The play of noetic différance, beneath and beyond rules: this is called *meaning*. 

Traumatypes are arrangements of psychic and collective retentions and protentions, woven by the id and by the *there*, producing new selection criteria, selecting otherwise. Such bifurcations occur ‘*step by step*’, by ‘internal resonance’, as a quantum leap, both *collectively* (rearranging *circuits of transindividuation*) and *psychically* (transforming the *idiolect*). The idiolect is the ‘grammar’ common to idiotexts *smaller than it*, hidden within it, of which it is the site as a unity of *diverse characters* and an entanglement of spiral turns. 

*A society is what secretes its selection criteria and transforms them in order to produce specific technosocial metabolisms*. Post-truth arises when techno-logical performativity is brought to the biospheric scale. A new critique is required because *criteria always stem from grammatization*. For Kant, it was a question of a critique of *reason* in relation to the *lawfulness* of the understanding; today, it is the understanding itself, as calculation, that requires critique. 

*[p. 28]* ^pg28

What must be understood is not just what the *spirit of capitalism was, but why it fades*, along with everything *that it makes fade*: intermittence, locality, questioning, critique. *Boltanski and Chiapello* point out capitalism’s capacity for survival *by absorbing its critiques*, something that ‘Marx and other “detractors”’ did not anticipate. But is it right to call Marx a *detractor*? In offering a *critique*, Marx sets out the *limits* of capitalism, but at the same time presents himself as an *admirer of the bourgeoisie*. 

In philosophy, *critique is never denunciation*, but analysis, establishing the *necessity* of the object and its limits. But today we need a critique *of* Marx, because: (1) the *critique of German idealism remains unfinished*, materialism having to become *hyper-materialism* in the epoch of techno-logical performativity; (2) *idealization* is not soluble into German idealism; (3) *calculation* is not examined with regard to *information theory oriented by the market as phase space*. 

*We posit* that: (1) capitalism does contain a *critical function*; (2) but it is *not truly a critique*; (3) a *new critique* must therefore be constituted. *Above all*, it must be a critique of the *destruction of processes of final causation and theories of final causality*, brought about by an *instrumentation of noesis* that generates a conflict, not just between the faculties, but of the functions (for example, between the understanding and reason). 

*No-longer-being-there is there from the beginning*, because the beginning is a default of origin, *an already-there that is still not yet there* and will never be there other than as différance. Today, this is so *in an exceptional way*, because *post-truth disintegrates the elements essential to the articulation of the generations*. The question of revenance and return is that of *rebirth as knowledge*. Knowledge is rebirth because it is always *an experience of truth re-emerging from forgetting as a trans-figured return of the extra-ordinary* (this is what made it possible for theology to be conjoined with ontology, and what makes it possible to open up a new dialogue with the faithful). In fact, this structure is *pharmacological, and in this way tragic*. 

In short, there is no capitalism without the functionalization of critique, but this has been destroyed by a new dogmatics. *We must negotiate, with a sustainable alternative capitalism, for the refunctionalization of a new critique*.

In 1794, Kant is the recipient of a letter from Frederick William II, censoring him. In 1798, he publishes his account of the conflict of the *higher faculties* (theology, law, medicine) and the *lower faculty* (philosophy). The ‘higher’ are so because they are organized with the Authority. The ‘lower’ is the locus of reason because it is in this respect *free* (philosophy not yet being clearly distinguished from science) – it bears the freedom to unfold separately from efficient causality. *Today*, we confront a *new conflict*, which is still that of finalities – but theology is more or less gone, and law and philosophy are in conflict with *new faculties aiming to submit them to efficient causality*, conditioning them by depriving them of freedom. 

*Exorganology* attempts to deal with this new conflict, which stems fundamentally from thermodynamic theory and its consequences in biology, information theory, cybernetics…and neganthropology. *It aims to negotiate, with the new power (the economy),* *the conditions of its and our survival*. For this, it is necessary to *reconsider the whole history of truth from the viewpoint of the post-veridical situation*, so as to free it and ourselves *from* this situation, not by denying or dialecticizing it, but by *reconfiguring a quasi-causality that could only be a new understanding of the theory of four causes*. This is the task of the remaining volumes.
