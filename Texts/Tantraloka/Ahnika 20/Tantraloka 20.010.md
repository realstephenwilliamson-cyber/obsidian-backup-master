## Mūla

निराचारेण दीक्षायां प्रत्ययस्तु न गद्यते ।
ज्ञानं स्वप्रत्ययं यस्मान्न फलान्तरमर्हति ॥ ^mula

nirācāreṇa dīkṣāyāṃ pratyayastu na gadyate |
jñānaṃ svapratyayaṃ yasmānna phalāntaramarhati || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 11 - Chapter Twenty#^pg255|p. 235]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=255>))
>
> The initiation a teacher imparts who has known within himself that (his) repetition of Mantra, oblation and worship have been accomplished successfully is said to be one that bears an (outer) validating sign of (its) accomplishment (pratyaya). This procedure is not for the renouncer (avadhūta), who has transcended practice (nirācāra) and knows the true nature (of things) (tattvajñā). The initiation that bears a visible sign (of its accomplishment) should be imparted by (teachers) who practice outer ritual (sācāra). It is said that there is no (such outer) sign when an initiation is performed by (a teacher) who has transcended practice (nirācāra). (with 20.8–10ab)
>
> Knowledge is self-validating and so does not require another (ulterior) result (to establish its validity). It is said in the Siddhāmata (that, on the contrary, the efficacity of) visualization (dhyāna) and the like can (only) be established by its fruit. (with 20.10cd–11ab)
