# Sirach 7 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Sirach 06|← Sirach 6]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Sirach 08|Sirach 8 →]]

## Miscellaneous advice

**7:1** Do no evil, and evil will never overtake you. ^7-1

**7:2** Stay away from wrong, and it will turn away from you. ^7-2

**7:3** Do^[Gk *My child, do*] not sow in the furrows of injustice, and you will not reap a sevenfold crop. ^7-3

**7:4** Do not seek from the Lord high office or the seat of honor from the king. ^7-4

**7:5** Do not assert your righteousness before the Lord or display your wisdom before the king. ^7-5

**7:6** Do not seek to become a judge, or you may be unable to root out injustice; perhaps you will fear the powerful and so mar your integrity. ^7-6

**7:7** Commit no offense against the public, and do not disgrace yourself among the people. ^7-7

**7:8** Do not commit a sin twice; even for one you will not go unpunished. ^7-8

**7:9** Do not say, “He will consider the great number of my gifts, and when I make an offering to the Most High God, he will accept it.” ^7-9

**7:10** Do not grow weary when you pray; do not neglect to give alms. ^7-10

**7:11** Do not ridicule a person who is embittered in spirit, for there is one who humbles and exalts. ^7-11

**7:12** Do not devise^[Heb: Gk *plow*] a lie against your brother or do the same to a friend. ^7-12

**7:13** Refuse to utter any lie, for it is a habit that results in no good. ^7-13

**7:14** Do not babble in the company of the elders, and do not repeat yourself when you pray. ^7-14

**7:15** Do not hate hard labor or farm work, which was created by the Most High. ^7-15

**7:16** Do not enroll in the ranks of sinners; remember that retribution will not delay. ^7-16

**7:17** Humble yourself to the utmost, for the punishment of the ungodly is fire and worms.^[Heb *for the expectation of mortals is worms*] ^7-17

## Relations with others

**7:18** Do not exchange a friend for money or a real brother for the gold of Ophir. ^7-18

**7:19** Do not dismiss^[Heb: Gk *deprive yourself of*] a wise and good wife, for her charm is worth more than gold. ^7-19

**7:20** Do not abuse slaves who work faithfully or hired laborers who give of themselves. ^7-20

**7:21** Let your soul love intelligent slaves;^[Heb *Love a wise slave as yourself*] do not withhold from them their freedom. ^7-21

**7:22** Do you have cattle? Look after them; if they are profitable to you, keep them. ^7-22

**7:23** Do you have children? Discipline them, and make them obedient^[Gk *bend their necks*] from their youth. ^7-23

**7:24** Do you have daughters? Be concerned for their chastity,^[Gk *body*] and do not show yourself too indulgent with them.^[Gk *do not brighten your face toward them*] ^7-24

**7:25** Give a daughter in marriage, and you complete a great task, but give her to a sensible man. ^7-25

**7:26** Do you have a wife who pleases you?^[Heb Syr lack *who pleases you*] Do not divorce her, but do not trust yourself to one whom you detest. ^7-26

**7:27** With all your heart honor your father, and do not forget the birth pangs of your mother. ^7-27

**7:28** Remember that it was of your parents^[Gk *them*] you were born; how will you repay what they have given to you? ^7-28

**7:29** With all your soul fear the Lord and revere his priests. ^7-29

**7:30** With all your might love your Maker, and do not neglect his ministers. ^7-30

**7:31** Fear the Lord and honor the priest, and give him his portion, as you have been commanded: the first fruits, the purification offering, the gift of the shoulders, the sacrifice of sanctification, and the first fruits of the holy things. ^7-31

**7:32** Stretch out your hand to the poor, so that your blessing may be complete. ^7-32

**7:33** Give graciously to all the living; do not withhold kindness even from the dead. ^7-33

**7:34** Do not lag behind those who weep, but mourn with those who mourn. ^7-34

**7:35** Do not hesitate to visit the sick, because for such deeds you will be loved. ^7-35

**7:36** In all you do, remember the end of your life, and then you will never sin. ^7-36
