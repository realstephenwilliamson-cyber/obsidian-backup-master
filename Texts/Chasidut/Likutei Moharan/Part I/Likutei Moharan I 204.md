# Likutei Moharan I 204

← [[Likutei Moharan I 203]] · [[Likutei Moharan]] · [[Likutei Moharan I 205]] →

*Part I · Lesson 204* · [[Likutei Moharan]]

## 1

The value of charity is known, especially when [the charity] is given to a Torah scholar. As it is brought: The money that is given to a Torah scholar is the aspect of “support for Torah” ([[Zohar 7.07 - Parashat Metsora#^z3-53b|Zohar III, 53b]]).

Indeed, this money that we give to a Torah scholar is an aspect of Torah, literally. No transgression can cancel this money given to a Torah scholar. As our Sages, of blessed memory, said: Transgression cannot extinguish Torah ([[Sotah 21#21a|Sotah 21a]]). This money is thus literally an aspect of Torah, as explained above.

Thus, *MAOT* (money) is an acronym of “*V ’ein Aveirah M’khabeh* Torah (but transgression cannot extinguish Torah).”

There is much more to this matter. For when [Rebbe Nachman] revealed this, he told some story connected with it, but we merited to record only these things.
