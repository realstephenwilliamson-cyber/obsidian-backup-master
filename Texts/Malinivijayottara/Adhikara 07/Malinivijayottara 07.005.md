## Mūla

तर्जनीमध्यमानामा दक्षिणस्य प्रसारिताः ।
कनिष्ठाङ्गुष्ठकाक्रान्तास्त्रिशूलं परिकीर्तितम् ॥ ^mula

tarjanīmadhyamānāmā dakṣiṇasya prasāritāḥ |
kaniṣṭhāṅguṣṭhakākrāntāstriśūlaṃ parikīrtitam || ^iast

> **1. Triśūla – the Trident Gesture**
>
> The index, middle and ring fingers of the right (hand) are extended (while) the thumb covers (and holds down) the little finger – this is said to be the Trident.
