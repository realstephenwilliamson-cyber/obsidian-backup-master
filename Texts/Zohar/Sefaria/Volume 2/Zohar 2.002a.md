# Zohar 2.002a

← [[Zohar 1.251a]] · [[Zohar (Soncino)]] · [[Zohar 2.002b]] →

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 4.03 - Parashat Shemot#^z2-2a|Parashat Shemot, at 2:2a]]

*Volume 2, folio 2a* · Shemot

**Shemot 1:1** NOW THESE ARE THE NAMES OF THE CHILDREN OF ISRAEL WHICH CAME INTO EGYPT EVERY MAN AND HIS HOUSEHOLD CAME WITH JACOB. It is written: *And the wise shall be resplendent as the splendour (*zohar*) of the firmament, and they that turn many to righteousness shall be like the stars for ever and ever* (Dan. 12, 3). “The wise” are those who penetrate to the real essence of wisdom; “they shall be resplendent”, i.e. illumined with the radiance of the supernal Wisdom; “as the splendour”, this is the flashing of the Stream that goes forth from Eden (Gen. 11, 10), this being alluded to as “the firmament”. There are suspended the stars, the planets, the sun and the moon, and all the radiant lights. ^shemot-1-1

**Shemot 1:2** The brightness of this firmament shines upon the Garden of Eden, and in the midst of the Garden stands the Tree of Life, whose branches spread over all forms and trees and spices in fitting vessels. All the beasts of the field and all the fowls of the air shelter beneath the branches of this Tree. ^shemot-1-2

**Shemot 1:3** The fruit of the Tree gives life to all. It is everlasting. The “other side” has no abode therein, but only the side of holiness. Blessed are they who taste thereof; they will live for ever and ever, and it is they who are called “the wise”, and they are vouchsafed life in this world as well as in the world to come. ^shemot-1-3

**Shemot 1:4** The Tree rises to a height of five hundred parasangs, and its circumference is six myriads of parasangs. Within this Tree is a light[^1] out of which radiate certain colours: they come and go, never being at rest save in the Tree. ^shemot-1-4

**Shemot 1:5** Should they issue from it to show themselves in the brightness which does not shine of itself,[^2] they are not at rest but flit about. From this Tree went forth twelve tribes,[^3] who had long been warmed by it, and they went down with this light that does not shine of itself into the exile of Egypt, accompanied by multitudes of heavenly hosts. ^shemot-1-5

**Shemot 2:6** R. Simeon compared the Egyptian with the Babylonian exile, basing his remarks on the text: “The word of the Lord came (lit. coming came) to Ezekiel” (Ezek. 1, 3). ‘Why’, he said, ‘the double expression “coming came”? Moreover, if Ezekiel was indeed a faithful prophet, why did he disclose the whole of his vision? Is it right and meet for one whom the king has invited to his palace to reveal all the secrets which he has seen there? Now Ezekiel was indeed a faithful prophet, and whatever he saw he faithfully kept secret, and whatever he revealed he revealed by permission of the Holy One, blessed be He, and for proper reasons. ^shemot-2-6

[^1]: *Tifereth*.
[^2]: *Malkuth*.
[^3]: The twelve ‘obliquities’ of *Tifereth*.
