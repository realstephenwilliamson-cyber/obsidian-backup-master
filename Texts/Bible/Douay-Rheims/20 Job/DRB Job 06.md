# Job 6 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Job 05|← Job 5]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Job 07|Job 7 →]]

**6:1** But Job answered, and said: ^6-1

**6:2** O that my sins, whereby I have deserved wrath, and the calamity that I suffer, were weighed in a balance. ^6-2

**6:3** As the sand of the sea this would appear heavier: therefore my words are full of sorrow: ^6-3

**6:4** For the arrows of the Lord are in me, the rage whereof drinketh up my spirit, and the terrors of the Lord war against me. ^6-4

**6:5** Will the wild ass bray when he hath grass? or will the ox low when he standeth before a full manger? ^6-5

**6:6** Or can an unsavoury thing be eaten, that is not seasoned with salt? or can a man taste that which when tasted bringeth death? ^6-6

**6:7** The things which before my soul would not touch, now, through anguish are my meats. ^6-7

**6:8** Who will grant that my request may come: and that God may give me what I look for? ^6-8

**6:9** And that he that hath begun may destroy me, that he may let loose his hand, and cut me off? ^6-9

**6:10** And that this may be my comfort, that afflicting me with sorrow, he spare not, nor I contradict the words of the Holy One. ^6-10

**6:11** For what is my strength, that I can hold out? or what is my end that I should keep patience? ^6-11

**6:12** My strength is not the strength of stones, nor is my flesh of brass. ^6-12

**6:13** Behold there is no help for me in myself, and my familiar friends also are departed from me. ^6-13

**6:14** He that taketh away mercy from his friend, forsaketh the fear of the Lord. ^6-14

**6:15** My brethren have passed by me, as the torrent that passeth swiftly in the valleys. ^6-15

**6:16** They that fear the hoary frost, the snow shall fall upon them. ^6-16

**6:17** At the time when they shall be scattered they shall perish: and after it groweth hot they shall be melted out of their place. ^6-17

**6:18** The paths of their steps are entangled: they shall walk in vain, and shall perish. ^6-18

**6:19** Consider the paths of Thema, the ways of Saba, and wait a little while. ^6-19

**6:20** They are confounded, because I have hoped: they are come also even unto me, and are covered with shame. ^6-20

**6:21** Now you are come: and now seeing my affliction you are afraid. ^6-21

**6:22** Did I say: Bring to me, and give me of your substance? ^6-22

**6:23** Or deliver me from the hand of the enemy, and rescue me out of the hand of the mighty? ^6-23

**6:24** Teach me, and I will hold my peace: and if I have been ignorant in any thing, instruct me. ^6-24

**6:25** Why have you detracted the words of truth, whereas there is none of you that can reprove me? ^6-25

**6:26** You dress up speeches only to rebuke, and you utter words to the wind. ^6-26

**6:27** You rush in upon the fatherless, and you endeavour to overthrow your friend. ^6-27

**6:28** However finish what you have begun, give ear, and see whether I lie. ^6-28

**6:29** Answer, I beseech you, without contention: and speaking that which is just, judge ye. ^6-29

**6:30** And you shall not And iniquity in my tongue, neither shall folly sound in my mouth. ^6-30
