# Sirach 39 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Sirach 38|← Sirach 38]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Sirach 40|Sirach 40 →]]

**39:1** They seek out the wisdom of all the ancients and are concerned with prophecies; ^39-1

**39:2** they preserve the sayings of the famous and penetrate the subtleties of parables; ^39-2

**39:3** they seek out the hidden meanings of proverbs and are at home with the obscurities of parables. ^39-3

**39:4** They serve among the great and appear before rulers; they travel in foreign nations and learn what is good and evil in the human lot. ^39-4

**39:5** They set their hearts to rise early to seek the Lord who made them and to petition the Most High; they open their mouths in prayer and ask pardon for their sins. ^39-5

**39:6** If the great Lord is willing, they will be filled with the spirit of understanding; they will pour forth words of wisdom of their own and give thanks to the Lord in prayer. ^39-6

**39:7** The Lord^[Gk *He himself*] will direct their counsel and knowledge as they meditate on his mysteries. ^39-7

**39:8** They will show the discipline of their training and will glory in the law of the Lord’s covenant. ^39-8

**39:9** Many will praise their understanding; it will never be blotted out. Their memory will not disappear, and their names will live through all generations. ^39-9

**39:10** Nations will speak of their wisdom, and the congregation will proclaim their praise. ^39-10

**39:11** If they live long, they will leave a name greater than a thousand, and if they go to rest, it is enough for them. ^39-11

## A hymn of praise to God

**39:12** I have more on my mind to express; I am full like the full moon. ^39-12

**39:13** Listen to me, my faithful children, and blossom like a rose growing by a stream of water. ^39-13

**39:14** Send out fragrance like incense and put forth blossoms like a lily. Raise your voice^[Syr: Gk *fragrance*] and sing a hymn of praise; bless the Lord for all his works. ^39-14

**39:15** Ascribe majesty to his name and give thanks to him with praise, with songs on your lips and with harps; this is what you shall say in thanksgiving: ^39-15

**39:16** “All the works of the Lord are very good, and whatever he commands will be done at the appointed time. ^39-16

**39:17** No one can say, ‘What is this?’ or ‘Why is that?’—for at the appointed time everything will be made known. At his word the waters stood in a heap and the reservoirs of water at the word of his mouth. ^39-17

**39:18** At his command, his purpose is fulfilled, and none can limit his saving power. ^39-18

**39:19** The works of all are before him, and nothing can be hidden from his eyes. ^39-19

**39:20** From age to age he observes all, and nothing is too marvelous for him. ^39-20

**39:21** No one can say, ‘What is this?’ or ‘Why is that?’—for everything has been created for its own purpose. ^39-21

**39:22** “His blessing covers the dry land like a river and drenches it like a flood. ^39-22

**39:23** The nations will inherit his wrath, as when he turned a watered land into salt. ^39-23

**39:24** To the faithful his ways are straight but full of pitfalls for the lawless. ^39-24

**39:25** From the beginning good things were created for the good but for sinners good things and bad.^[Heb Lat: Gk *sinners bad things*] ^39-25

**39:26** The basic necessities of human life are water and fire and iron and salt and wheat flour and milk and honey, the blood of the grape and oil and clothing. ^39-26

**39:27** All these are good for the godly, but for sinners they turn into evils. ^39-27

**39:28** “There are winds created for vengeance, and in their anger they can dislodge mountains;^[Heb Syr: Gk *can scourge mightily*] on the day of reckoning they will pour out their strength and calm the anger of their Maker. ^39-28

**39:29** Fire and hail and famine and pestilence—all these have been created for vengeance; ^39-29

**39:30** the fangs of wild animals and scorpions and vipers and the sword that punishes the ungodly with destruction. ^39-30

**39:31** They take delight in doing his bidding, always ready for his service on earth, and when their time comes they never disobey his command.” ^39-31

**39:32** So from the beginning I have been steadfast and have thought it out and left it in writing: ^39-32

**39:33** All the works of the Lord are good, and he will supply every need in its time. ^39-33

**39:34** No one can say, “This is not as good as that,” for everything proves good in its appointed time. ^39-34

**39:35** So now sing praise with all your heart and voice, and bless the name of the Lord. ^39-35
