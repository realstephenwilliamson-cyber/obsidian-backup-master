## Mūla

विभज्यते विभागश्च पुनरेव त्रिशस्त्रिशः ।
पूर्वोदये तु विश्रम्य द्वितीयेनोल्लसेद्यदा ॥ ^mula

vibhajyate vibhāgaśca punareva triśastriśaḥ |
pūrvodaye tu viśramya dvitīyenollasedyadā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 02 - Chapter Seven#^pg58|p. 40]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=58>))
>
> The Day and Night (of exhalation and inhalation) are therefore each divided by three, and each of these parts is again divided by three within this emergence of the reality (tattvodaya) (of Mantra). (with 7.59cd–60ab)
>
> If once (the yogi) has rested in the first emergence (of the breath), (he) pours out (exhaling) by the second, and enters (inhaling in the third,) by (thus) uniting the halves, this is (the equivalent of) half the emergence (of a breathing cycle) described previously. If time, as the full emergence (udaya) (of the breathing cycle), manifests equally in the triad (trika) of entry, rest and emergence (of the breath), then (each of these) is a third of its own arising. (with 7.60cd–62ab)
