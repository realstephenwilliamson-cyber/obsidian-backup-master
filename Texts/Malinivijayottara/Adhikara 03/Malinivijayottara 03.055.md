## Mūla

परेयमनया सिद्धिः सर्वकामफलप्रदा ।
नाशिष्याय प्रदेयेयं नाभक्ताय कदाचन ॥ ^mula

pareyam anayā siddhiḥ sarvakāmaphalapradā |
nāśiṣyāya pradeyeyaṁ nābhaktāya kadācana || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2023 – March 2023
> p. 50 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=50>))
>
> This is Parā (Vidyā). It is by this that (one achieves) the accomplishment that bestows the fruit of every desire. O fair-faced lady, this should never be given[^44] to one who is not a (true) disciple and not a devotee. It should be given to one who sees devoutly that the three – Rudra, Rudra's power and the teacher – are equal. (with 3.55–56)

[^44]: Read *pradeyeyaṁ* for *padeyeyaṁ*.
