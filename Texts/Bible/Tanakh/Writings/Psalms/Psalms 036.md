# Psalms 036

## Chapter 36

**36:1** For the leader. Of the servant of the LORD, of David. ^36-1

**36:2** I know[^1] what Transgression says to the wicked;  
he has no sense of the dread of God, ^36-2

**36:3** because its speech is seductive to him  
till his iniquity be found out and he be hated.[^2] ^36-3

**36:4** His words are evil and deceitful;  
he will not consider doing good. ^36-4

**36:5** In bed he plots mischief;  
he is set on a path of no good,  
he does not reject evil. ^36-5

**36:6** O LORD, Your faithfulness reaches to heaven;  
Your steadfastness to the sky; ^36-6

**36:7** Your beneficence is like the high mountains;  
Your justice like the great deep;  
man and beast You deliver, O LORD. ^36-7

**36:8** How precious is Your faithful care, O God!  
Mankind shelters in the shadow of Your wings. ^36-8

**36:9** They feast on the rich fare of Your house;  
You let them drink at Your refreshing stream. ^36-9

**36:10** With You is the fountain of life;  
by Your light do we see light. ^36-10

**36:11** Bestow Your faithful care on those devoted to You,  
and Your beneficence on upright men. ^36-11

**36:12** Let not the foot of the arrogant tread on me,  
or the hand of the wicked drive me away. ^36-12

**36:13** There lie the evildoers, fallen,  
thrust down, unable to rise. ^36-13

[^1]: Lit. “In my heart is.”
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
