## Mūla

ततो द्विगुणमाने तु द्विरूपं न्यासमाचरेत् ।
ततोऽपि द्विगुणे सृष्टिसंहृतिद्वितयेन तम् ॥ ^mula

tato dviguṇamāne tu dvirūpaṃ nyāsamācaret |
tato'pi dviguṇe sṛṣṭisaṃhṛtidvitayena tam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 18 - Chapter Twenty-Seven#^pg434|p. 414]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=434>))
>
> By the association (of each one) with Power and its possessor, (the number of phases and beads) is there said to be double (the previous one, that is, fifty-four). Thus, when it is double the measure, one should perform the deposition in two forms, (one for iva and the other for Śakti). (33cd. 34ab) Then again, when that is double, by (depositing) that (in the forward and reverse order, that is,) in the two sequences of emanation and withdrawal, (they become one hundred and eight). Either (the fifty letters of) Mātrkā or (those of) Mālinī may be deposited (onto the rosary that) measures fifty (beads). (with 27.34cd–35ab)
