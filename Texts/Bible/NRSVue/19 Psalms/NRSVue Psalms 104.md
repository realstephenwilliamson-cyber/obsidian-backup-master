# Psalm 104 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 103|← Psalm 103]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 105|Psalm 105 →]]

## God the Creator and provider

**104:1** Bless the LORD, O my soul. O LORD my God, you are very great. You are clothed with honor and majesty, ^104-1

**104:2** wrapped in light as with a garment. You stretch out the heavens like a tent; ^104-2

**104:3** you set the beams of your^[Heb *his*] chambers on the waters; you make the clouds your^[Heb *his*] chariot; you ride on the wings of the wind; ^104-3

**104:4** you make the winds your^[Heb *his*] messengers, fire and flame your^[Heb *his*] ministers. ^104-4

**104:5** You set the earth on its foundations, so that it shall never be shaken. ^104-5

**104:6** You cover it with the deep as with a garment; the waters stood above the mountains. ^104-6

**104:7** At your rebuke they flee; at the sound of your thunder they take to flight. ^104-7

**104:8** They rose up to the mountains, ran down to the valleys, to the place that you appointed for them. ^104-8

**104:9** You set a boundary that they may not pass, so that they might not again cover the earth. ^104-9

**104:10** You make springs gush forth in the valleys; they flow between the hills, ^104-10

**104:11** giving drink to every wild animal; the wild asses quench their thirst. ^104-11

**104:12** By the streams^[Heb *By them*] the birds of the air have their habitation; they sing among the branches. ^104-12

**104:13** From your lofty abode you water the mountains; the earth is satisfied with the fruit of your work. ^104-13

**104:14** You cause the grass to grow for the cattle and plants for people to cultivate, to bring forth food from the earth ^104-14

**104:15** and wine to gladden the human heart, oil to make the face shine and bread to strengthen the human heart. ^104-15

**104:16** The trees of the field^[Gk: Heb *trees of theLORD*] are watered abundantly, the cedars of Lebanon that he planted. ^104-16

**104:17** In them the birds build their nests; the stork has its home in the fir trees. ^104-17

**104:18** The high mountains are for the wild goats; the rocks are a refuge for the coneys. ^104-18

**104:19** You have made the moon to mark the seasons; the sun knows its time for setting. ^104-19

**104:20** You make darkness, and it is night, when all the animals of the forest come creeping out. ^104-20

**104:21** The young lions roar for their prey, seeking their food from God. ^104-21

**104:22** When the sun rises, they withdraw and lie down in their dens. ^104-22

**104:23** People go out to their work and to their labor until the evening. ^104-23

**104:24** O LORD, how manifold are your works! In wisdom you have made them all; the earth is full of your creatures. ^104-24

**104:25** There is the sea, great and wide; creeping things innumerable are there, living things both small and great. ^104-25

**104:26** There go the ships and Leviathan that you formed to sport in it. ^104-26

**104:27** These all look to you to give them their food in due season; ^104-27

**104:28** when you give to them, they gather it up; when you open your hand, they are filled with good things. ^104-28

**104:29** When you hide your face, they are dismayed; when you take away their breath, they die and return to their dust. ^104-29

**104:30** When you send forth your spirit,^[Or *your breath*] they are created, and you renew the face of the ground. ^104-30

**104:31** May the glory of the LORD endure forever; may the LORD rejoice in his works— ^104-31

**104:32** who looks on the earth and it trembles, who touches the mountains and they smoke. ^104-32

**104:33** I will sing to the LORD as long as I live; I will sing praise to my God while I have being. ^104-33

**104:34** May my meditation be pleasing to him, for I rejoice in the LORD. ^104-34

**104:35** Let sinners be consumed from the earth, and let the wicked be no more. Bless the LORD, O my soul. Praise the LORD! ^104-35
