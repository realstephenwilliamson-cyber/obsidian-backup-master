# Zohar 2.056b

← [[Zohar 2.056a]] · [[Zohar (Soncino)]] · [[Zohar 2.057a]] →

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 4.06 - Parashat Be-Shallah#^z2-56b|Parashat Be-Shallaḥ, at 2:56b]]

*Volume 2, folio 56b* · Beshalach

*Passage Beshalach 18:262 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 2.056a#^beshalach-18-262|Beshalach 18:262]].*

**Beshalach 18:263** From the left side the chariots of the unholy principalities rise up. They also are linked one with the other, grade to grade, the greatest of them being, as we have already pointed out, “the firstborn of Pharaoh”, whom the Holy One killed. ^beshalach-18-263

**Beshalach 18:264** All of these unholy powers are delivered unto the judgement of the Kingdom, the which is called “the great sea”, in order that they may be uprooted each in his own grade, and be utterly cast down, and when they are broken above, all their counterparts below are also broken and lost in the “lower sea”. ^beshalach-18-264

**Beshalach 18:265** As to the “captains” (*shalishim*) who were drowned in the Red Sea, it has already been made clear that all these grades consist of three (*shalosh*) attributes each (two and one, the triad, corresponding to the holy triad above). They were all delivered unto Her (the Shekinah’s) hand, that their power might be broken. ^beshalach-18-265

**Beshalach 18:266** All the ten punishments which the Holy One brought on Egypt were achieved by the power of one “hand”, for the “left hand” is included in the right, the ten fingers forming one entity in correspondence to the Ten expressions by which the Holy One is designated. Then came a punishment which was equal to all the rest, that of the sea: “The last one was the hardest” (Isa. 8, 23). And in the future the Holy One will deal similarly with all the hosts, princes and chieftains of Edom (Rome), as it is written: “Who is he who cometh from Edom, with dyed garments from Bozrah? I that speak in righteousness, mighty to save” (Isa. 63, 1).’ ^beshalach-18-266

**Beshalach 18:267** PHARAOH’S CHARIOTS AND MIGHT HE CAST INTO THE SEA. R. Isaac referred to the verse: “When he uttereth his voice, there is a multitude of waters in the heavens” (Jer. 10, 13), and said: ‘According to tradition, the Holy One created seven heavens, and in each heaven stars and planets are fixed. Arabot is above them all. ^beshalach-18-267

**Beshalach 18:268** The length of each heaven is such that it would take two hundred years to traverse, and the distance between each heaven and the next would take five hundred years to traverse. As for Arabot, one would need one thousand five hundred years to cover its whole length, and the selfsame number for traversing its breadth. And all the heavens are lighted from the radiance of Arabot. ^beshalach-18-268

**Beshalach 18:269** Above Arabot is the heaven of the *Hayoth*, ^beshalach-18-269

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Beshalach 18:270.*

**Beshalach 18:271** and above this latter sphere another heaven, brighter than all, as it is written: “And the likeness of the firmament upon the heads of the Hayoth” (Ezek. 1, 22). And below there are many chariots at the right hand and at the left, of many grades, each with its own name. ^beshalach-18-271

**Beshalach 18:272** And beneath them are others, smaller and yet more varied, which are the smallest ranks of this celestial but unholy order; as it is written: “The sea is great… small beasts and great are there” (Ps. 104, 25), ^beshalach-18-272

**Beshalach 18:273** as we have affirmed, that on the left side below there is a ruler, the “other side”, attached to those above, but they are crushed by the great holy power, according to our interpretation of the words, “Pharaoh’s chariots and his might he cast into the sea”.’ ^beshalach-18-273

**Beshalach 19:274** THY RIGHT HAND, O LORD, GLORIFIED IN POWER. R. Simeon said: ‘In the hour when the morning breaks, the Hind (Shekinah) rises and starts from her place in order to enter the two hundred palaces of the King. When a man studies the Torah in solitude at midnight, at the hour when the north wind springs up and the Hind desires to be astir, he is taken with her into supernal realms, to appear before the King. When dawn brightens and he recites his prayers, and unifies the Holy Name in manner due, he is encircled with a thread of grace; ^beshalach-19-274
