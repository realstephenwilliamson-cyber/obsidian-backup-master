# Psalm 139

← [[BCP Psalm 138]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 140]] →

*Domine, probasti* · Book Five · Twenty-ninth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 139]]

**139:1** LORD, you have searched me out and known me; \*  
you know my sitting down and my rising up;  
you discern my thoughts from afar. ^139-1

**139:2** You trace my journeys and my resting-places \*  
and are acquainted with all my ways. ^139-2

**139:3** Indeed, there is not a word on my lips, \*  
but you, O LORD, know it altogether. ^139-3

**139:4** You press upon me behind and before \*  
and lay your hand upon me. ^139-4

**139:5** Such knowledge is too wonderful for me; \*  
it is so high that I cannot attain to it. ^139-5

**139:6** Where can I go then from your Spirit? \*  
where can I flee from your presence? ^139-6

**139:7** If I climb up to heaven, you are there; \*  
if I make the grave my bed, you are there also. ^139-7

**139:8** If I take the wings of the morning \*  
and dwell in the uttermost parts of the sea, ^139-8

**139:9** Even there your hand will lead me \*  
and your right hand hold me fast. ^139-9

**139:10** If I say, "Surely the darkness will cover me, \*  
and the light around me turn to night," ^139-10

**139:11** Darkness is not dark to you;  
the night is as bright as the day; \*  
darkness and light to you are both alike. ^139-11

**139:12** For you yourself created my inmost parts; \*  
you knit me together in my mother's womb. ^139-12

[p. 795]

**139:13** I will thank you because I am marvelously made; \*  
your works are wonderful, and I know it well. ^139-13

**139:14** My body was not hidden from you, \*  
while I was being made in secret  
and woven in the depths of the earth. ^139-14

**139:15** Your eyes beheld my limbs, yet unfinished in the womb;  
all of them were written in your book; \*  
they were fashioned day by day,  
when as yet there was none of them. ^139-15

**139:16** How deep I find your thoughts, O God! \*  
how great is the sum of them! ^139-16

**139:17** If I were to count them, they would be more in number than the sand; \*  
to count them all, my life span would need to be like yours. ^139-17

**139:18** Oh, that you would slay the wicked, O God! \*  
You that thirst for blood, depart from me. ^139-18

**139:19** They speak despitefully against you; \*  
your enemies take your Name in vain. ^139-19

**139:20** Do I not hate those, O LORD, who hate you? \*  
and do I not loathe those who rise up against you? ^139-20

**139:21** I hate them with a perfect hatred; \*  
they have become my own enemies. ^139-21

**139:22** Search me out, O God, and know my heart; \*  
try me and know my restless thoughts. ^139-22

**139:23** Look well whether there be any wickedness in me \*  
and lead me in the way that is everlasting. ^139-23
