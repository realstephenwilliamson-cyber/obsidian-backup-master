## Mūla

मेरोर्दक्षिणतो हेमनिषधौ यौ तदन्तरे ।
हर्याख्यं नवसाहस्रं तत्सहस्राधिकायुषम् ॥ ^mula

merordakṣiṇato hemaniṣadhau yau tadantare |
haryākhyaṃ navasāhasraṃ tatsahasrādhikāyuṣam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 04 - Chapter Eight, part 2#^pg143|p. 125]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=143>))

> To the south of Meru, between the Niṣadha and Hemakūtṭa, lies (the land) called Hari. It extends for nine thousand leagues, and the lifespan (of its inhabitants extends for) three more thousand years (than that).
