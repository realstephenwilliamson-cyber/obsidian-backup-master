## Mūla

रुद्रशक्तिसमावेशः पञ्चधा ननु चर्च्यते ।

rudraśaktisamāveśaḥ pañcadhā nanu carcyate |

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. I
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 01 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v01 11 - Chapter One, part 10#^pg387|p. 373]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 01.pdf#page=387>))
>
> Surely, (one may ask: the Mālinīvijayottara) is expounding the five-fold penetration of Rudra’s power, so what occasion is there to describe the penetration of the Elements and the rest there (in that context)? If you were to justify this by saying that this (topic is discussed there only) incidentally (in the course of the main discussion, our response is that) people without (sensible) views and with tastes other than (ours) are not our own (people). (with 1.192–193)
