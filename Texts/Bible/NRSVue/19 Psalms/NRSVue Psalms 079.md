# Psalm 79 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 078|← Psalm 78]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 080|Psalm 80 →]]

## Plea for mercy for Jerusalem

*A Psalm of Asaph.*

**79:1** O God, the nations have come into your inheritance; they have defiled your holy temple; they have laid Jerusalem in ruins. ^79-1

**79:2** They have given the bodies of your servants to the birds of the air for food, the flesh of your faithful to the wild animals of the earth. ^79-2

**79:3** They have poured out their blood like water all around Jerusalem, and there was no one to bury them. ^79-3

**79:4** We have become a taunt to our neighbors, mocked and derided by those around us. ^79-4

**79:5** How long, O LORD? Will you be angry forever? Will your jealous wrath burn like fire? ^79-5

**79:6** Pour out your anger on the nations that do not know you and on the kingdoms that do not call on your name. ^79-6

**79:7** For they have devoured Jacob and laid waste his habitation. ^79-7

**79:8** Do not remember against us the iniquities of our ancestors; let your compassion come speedily to meet us, for we are brought very low. ^79-8

**79:9** Help us, O God of our salvation, for the glory of your name; deliver us and forgive our sins, for your name’s sake. ^79-9

**79:10** Why should the nations say, “Where is their God?” Let the avenging of the outpoured blood of your servants be known among the nations before our eyes. ^79-10

**79:11** Let the groans of the prisoners come before you; according to your great power, preserve those doomed to die. ^79-11

**79:12** Return sevenfold into the bosom of our neighbors the taunts with which they taunted you, O Lord! ^79-12

**79:13** Then we your people, the flock of your pasture, will give thanks to you forever; from generation to generation we will recount your praise. ^79-13
