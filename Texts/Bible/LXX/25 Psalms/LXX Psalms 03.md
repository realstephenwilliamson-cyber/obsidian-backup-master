# LXX Psalms 03

← [[LXX Psalms 02]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 04]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 003]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 003]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**3:1** A Psalm of David, when he fled from the presence of his son Abessalom.  ^3-1

**3:2** O Lord, why are they that afflict me multiplied? many rise up against me.  ^3-2

**3:3** Many say concerning my soul, There is no deliverance for him in his God. Pause.[^1]  ^3-3

**3:4** But thou, O Lord, art my helper: my glory, and the one that lifts up my head.  ^3-4

**3:5** I cried to the Lord with my voice, and he heard me out of his holy mountain. Pause.  ^3-5

**3:6** I lay down and slept; I awaked; for the Lord will help me.  ^3-6

**3:7** I will not be afraid of ten thousands of people, who beset me round about.  ^3-7

**3:8** Arise, Lord; deliver me, my God: for thou hast smitten all who were without cause mine enemies; thou hast broken the teeth of sinners.  ^3-8

**3:9** Deliverance is the Lord's, and thy blessing is upon thy people.  ^3-9

[^1]: 3:3 The word Διάψαλμα (Selah) has been rendered Pause, as most intelligible to the English reader.
