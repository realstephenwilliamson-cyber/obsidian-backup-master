## Chapter 2

**2:1** The word that Isaiah son of Amoz prophesied concerning Judah and Jerusalem. ^2-1

**2:2** In the days to come,  
The Mount of the LORD’s House  
Shall stand firm above the mountains  
And tower above the hills;  
And all the nations  
Shall gaze on it with joy. ^2-2

**2:3** And the many peoples shall go and say:  
“Come,  
Let us go up to the Mount of the LORD,  
To the House of the God of Jacob;  
That He may instruct us in His ways,  
And that we may walk in His paths.”  
For instruction shall come forth[^1] from Zion,  
The word of the LORD from Jerusalem. ^2-3

**2:4** Thus He will judge among the nations  
And arbitrate for the many peoples,  
And they shall beat their swords into plowshares[^2]  
And their spears into pruning hooks:  
Nation shall not take up  
Sword against nation;  
They shall never again know[^3] war. ^2-4

**2:5** O House of Jacob!  
Come, let us walk  
By the light of the LORD. ^2-5

**2:6** For you have forsaken [the ways of] your people,  
O House of Jacob!  
For they are full [of practices] from the East,  
And of soothsaying like the Philistines;  
They abound in customs[^4] of the aliens.[^5] ^2-6

**2:7** Their land is full of silver and gold,  
There is no limit to their treasures;  
Their land is full of horses,  
There is no limit to their chariots. ^2-7

**2:8** And their land is full of idols;  
They bow down to the work of their hands,  
To what their own fingers have wrought. ^2-8

**2:9** But man shall be humbled,  
And mortal brought low—  
Oh, do not forgive them![^6] ^2-9

**2:10** Go deep into the rock,  
Bury yourselves in the ground,  
Before the terror of the LORD  
And His dread majesty! ^2-10

**2:11** Man’s haughty look shall be brought low,  
And the pride of mortals shall be humbled.  
None but the LORD shall be  
Exalted in that day. ^2-11

**2:12** For the LORD of Hosts has ready a day  
Against all that is proud and arrogant,  
Against all that is lofty—so that it is brought low: ^2-12

**2:13** Against all the cedars of Lebanon,  
Tall and stately,  
And all the oaks of Bashan; ^2-13

**2:14** Against all the high mountains  
And all the lofty hills; ^2-14

**2:15** Against every soaring tower  
And every mighty wall; ^2-15

**2:16** Against all the ships of Tarshish[^7]  
And all the gallant barks. ^2-16

**2:17** Then man’s haughtiness shall be humbled  
And the pride of man brought low.  
None but the LORD shall be  
Exalted in that day. ^2-17

**2:18** As for idols, they shall vanish completely. ^2-18

**2:19** And men shall enter caverns in the rock  
And hollows in the ground—  
Before the terror of the LORD  
And His dread majesty,  
When He comes forth to overawe the earth. ^2-19

**2:20** On that day, men shall fling away,  
To the flying foxes[^8] and the bats,  
The idols of silver  
And the idols of gold  
Which they made for worshiping. ^2-20

**2:21** And they shall enter the clefts in the rocks  
And the crevices in the cliffs,  
Before the terror of the LORD  
And His dread majesty,  
When He comes forth to overawe the earth. ^2-21

**2:22** Oh, cease to glorify man,  
Who has only a breath in his nostrils!  
For by what does he merit esteem? ^2-22

[^1]: I.e., oracles will be obtainable.
[^2]: More exactly, the iron points with which wooden plows were tipped.
[^3]: Cf. Judg. 3.2.
[^4]: Cf. Targum; lit. “children.”
[^5]: Emendation yields “For they are full of divination / and have abundance of soothsaying, / Like Philistines / And like alien folk.”
[^6]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain. Emendation yields “And their idols with them”; cf. vv. 17–21.
[^7]: Probably a type of large ship.
[^8]: Exact meaning of Heb. uncertain.
