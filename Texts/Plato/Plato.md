# Plato

The dialogues in the Loeb translations (Fowler, Lamb, Bury, Shorey) with Burnet's Greek, from the Perseus Digital Library: one note per Stephanus page, English above the Greek, with a link target for each section (`^227a`). The Loeb notes are footnotes. Added 12 October 2026, from the cloud build of October 2026; the *Laws* and the *Letters* were left out, and of the *Republic* only Books I–V, the part with Shorey's English; Books VI–X were added on 22 October 2026 in Jowett's translation from Project Gutenberg (one note per book, no Stephanus numbers).
