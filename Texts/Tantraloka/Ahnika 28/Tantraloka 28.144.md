## Mūla

सति वित्ते पुनः शाठ्यं व्याधये नरकाय च ।
नित्यपूजासु पूर्णत्वं पर्वपूजाप्रपूरणात् ॥ ^mula

sati vitte punaḥ śāṭhyaṃ vyādhaye narakāya ca |
nityapūjāsu pūrṇatvaṃ parvapūjāprapūraṇāt || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 04 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 2#^pg89|p. 72]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=89>))
>
> The thread may even be made of (just) vegetable fibres or braided with kusa grass if there is no money (for an expensive one). But if there is the money (and the patron says he has none), (his miserly) deceitfulness (will be) the cause of illness (here in this world) and (condemn him) to hell (in the next). (with 28.143cd–144ab)
>
> The regular rites of worship are completed by the worship (performed) during the parvans, which serves to complete (them). There also, (that worship) is completed by worshipping (the deity) with the sacred thread. (with 28.144cd–145ab)
