# Proverbs 14 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Proverbs 13|← Proverbs 13]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Proverbs 15|Proverbs 15 →]]

**14:1** A wise woman buildeth her house: but the foolish will pull down with her hands that also which is built. ^14-1

**14:2** He that walketh in the right way, and feareth God, Cis despised by him that goeth by an infamous way. ^14-2

**14:3** In the mouth of a fool is the rod of pride: but the lips of the wise preserve them. ^14-3

**14:4** Where there are no oxen, the crib is empty: but where there is much corn, there the strength of the ox is manifest. ^14-4

**14:5** A faithful witness will not lie: but a deceitful witness uttereth a lie. ^14-5

**14:6** A scorner seeketh wisdom, and findeth it not: the learning of the wise is easy. ^14-6

**14:7** Go against a foolish man, and he knoweth not the lips of prudence. ^14-7

**14:8** The wisdom of a. discreet man is to understand his way: and the imprudence of fools erreth. ^14-8

**14:9** A fool will laugh at sin, but among the just grace shall abide. ^14-9

**14:10** The heart that knoweth the bitterness of his own soul, in his joy the stranger shall not intermeddle. ^14-10

**14:11** The house of the wicked shall be destroyed: but the tabernacles of the just shall flourish. ^14-11

**14:12** There is a way which seemeth just to a man: but the ends thereof lead to death. ^14-12

**14:13** Laughter shall be mingled with sorrow, and mourning taketh hold of the end of joy. ^14-13

**14:14** A fool shall be filled with his own ways, and the good man shall be above him. ^14-14

**14:15** The innocent believeth every word: the discreet man considereth his steps. No good shall come to the deceitful son: but the wise servant shall prosper in his dealings, and his way shall be made straight. ^14-15

**14:16** A wise man feareth and declineth from evil: the fool leapeth over and is confident. ^14-16

**14:17** The impatient man shall work folly: and the crafty man is hateful. ^14-17

**14:18** The childish shall possess folly, and the prudent, shall look for knowledge. ^14-18

**14:19** The evil shall fall down before the good: and the wicked before the gates of the just. ^14-19

**14:20** The poor man shall be hateful even to his own neighbour: but the friends of the rich are many. ^14-20

**14:21** He that despiseth his neighbour, sinneth: but he that sheweth mercy to the poor, shall be blessed. He that believeth in the Lord, loveth mercy. ^14-21

**14:22** They err that work evil: but mercy and truth prepare good things. ^14-22

**14:23** In much work there shall be abundance: but where there are many words, there is oftentimes want. ^14-23

**14:24** The crown of the wise is their riches: the folly of fools, imprudence. ^14-24

**14:25** A faithful witness delivereth souls: and the double dealer uttereth lies. ^14-25

**14:26** In the fear of the Lord is confidence of strength, and there shall be hope for his children. ^14-26

**14:27** The fear of the Lord is a fountain of life, to decline from the ruin of death. ^14-27

**14:28** In the multitude of people is the dignity of the king: and in the small number of people the dishonour of the prince. ^14-28

**14:29** He that is patient, is governed with much wisdom: but he that is impatient, exalteth his folly. ^14-29

**14:30** Soundness of heart is the life of the flesh: but envy is the rottenness of the bones. ^14-30

**14:31** He that oppresseth the poor, upbraideth his Maker: but he that hath pity on the poor, honoureth him. ^14-31

**14:32** The wicked man shall be driven out in his wickedness: but the just hath hope in his death. ^14-32

**14:33** In the heart of the prudent resteth wisdom, and it shall instruct all the ignorant. ^14-33

**14:34** Justice exalteth a nation: but sin maketh nations miserable. ^14-34

**14:35** A wise servant is acceptable to the king: he that is good for nothing shall feel his anger. ^14-35
