## Chapter 11

**11:1** When Athaliah, the mother of Ahaziah, learned that her son was dead, she promptly killed off all who were of royal stock. ^11-1

**11:2** But Jehosheba, daughter of King Joram and sister of Ahaziah, secretly took Ahaziah’s son Joash away from among the princes who were being slain, and [put][^1] him and his nurse in a bedroom. And they[^2] kept him hidden from Athaliah so that he was not put to death. ^11-2

**11:3** He stayed with her for six years, hidden in the House of the LORD,[^3] while Athaliah reigned over the land. ^11-3

**11:4** In the seventh year, Jehoiada sent for the chiefs of the hundreds of the Carites[^4] and of the guards, and had them come to him in the House of the LORD. He made a pact with them, exacting an oath from them in the House of the LORD, and he showed them the king’s son. ^11-4

**11:5** He instructed them: “This is what you must do: One-third of those who are on duty for the week shall maintain guard[^5] over the royal palace; ^11-5

**11:6** another third shall be [stationed] at the Sur Gate;[^6] and the other third shall be at the gate behind the guards; you shall keep guard over the House on every side.[^7] ^11-6

**11:7** The two divisions of yours who are off duty this week shall keep guard over the House of the LORD for the protection of the king. ^11-7

**11:8** You shall surround the king on every side, every man with his weapons at the ready; and whoever breaks through the ranks shall be killed. Stay close to the king in his comings and goings.” ^11-8

**11:9** The chiefs of hundreds did just as Jehoiada ordered: Each took his men—those who were on duty that week and those who were off duty that week—and they presented themselves to Jehoiada the priest. ^11-9

**11:10** The priest gave the chiefs of hundreds King David’s spears[^8] and quivers that were kept in the House of the LORD. ^11-10

**11:11** The guards, each with his weapons at the ready, stationed themselves—from the south end of the House to the north end of the House, at the altar and the House—to guard the king on every side. ^11-11

**11:12** [Jehoiada] then brought out the king’s son, and placed upon him the crown and the insignia.[^9] They anointed him and proclaimed him king; they clapped their hands and shouted, “Long live the king!” ^11-12

**11:13** When Athaliah heard the shouting of the guards [and] the people, she came out to the people in the House of the LORD. ^11-13

**11:14** She looked about and saw the king standing by the pillar, as was the custom, the chiefs with their trumpets beside the king, and all the people of the land rejoicing and blowing trumpets. Athaliah rent her garments and cried out, “Treason, treason!” ^11-14

**11:15** Then the priest Jehoiada gave the command to the army officers, the chiefs of hundreds, and said to them, “Take her out between the ranks[^10] and, if anyone follows her, put him to the sword.” For the priest thought: “Let her not be put to death in the House of the LORD.” ^11-15

**11:16** They cleared a passageway for her and she entered the royal palace through the horses’ entrance: there she was put to death. ^11-16

**11:17** And Jehoiada solemnized the covenant between the LORD, on the one hand, and the king and the people, on the other—as well as between the king and the people—that they should be the people of the LORD. ^11-17

**11:18** Thereupon all the people of the land went to the temple of Baal. They tore it down and smashed its altars and images to bits, and they slew Mattan, the priest of Baal, in front of the altars. [Jehoiada] the priest then placed guards over the House of the LORD. ^11-18

**11:19** He took the chiefs of hundreds, the Carites,[^11] the guards, and all the people of the land, and they escorted the king from the House of the LORD into the royal palace by the gate of the guards. And he ascended the royal throne. ^11-19

**11:20** All the people of the land rejoiced, and the city was quiet. As for Athaliah, she had been put to the sword in the royal palace. ^11-20

[^1]: Cf. 2 Chron. 22.11.
[^2]: 2 Chron. 22.11 reads “she.”
[^3]: Jehosheba was the wife of the high priest Jehoiada; cf. 2 Chron. 22.11.
[^4]: Perhaps the Cherethites (cf. 2 Sam. 20.23) or the Carians. They were members of the king’s bodyguard.
[^5]: Heb. “and who keep guard.”
[^6]: 2 Chron. 23.5 reads “Foundation Gate.”
[^7]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^8]: 2 Chron. 23.9 adds “and shields.”
[^9]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^10]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^11]: Perhaps the Cherethites (cf. 2 Sam. 20.23) or the Carians. They were members of the king’s bodyguard.
