# LXX Psalms 110

← [[LXX Psalms 109]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 111]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 111]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 111]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**110:1** Alleluia. I will give thee thanks, O Lord, with my whole heart, in the council of the upright, and *in* the congregation.  ^110-1

**110:2** The works of the Lord are great, sought out according to all his will.[^1]  ^110-2

**110:3** His work is *worthy of* thanksgiving and honour: and his righteousness endures for ever and ever.  ^110-3

**110:4** He has caused his wonderful works to be remembered: the Lord is merciful and compassionate.  ^110-4

**110:5** He has given food to them that fear him: he will remember his covenant for ever.  ^110-5

**110:6** He has declared to his people the power of his works, to give them the inheritance of the heathen.  ^110-6

**110:7** The works of his hands are truth and judgment: all his commandments are sure:  ^110-7

**110:8** established for ever and ever, done in truth and uprightness.  ^110-8

**110:9** He sent redemption to his people: he commanded his covenant for ever: holy and fearful is his name.  ^110-9

**110:10** The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom, and all that act accordingly have a good understanding; his praise endures for ever and ever.[^2]  ^110-10

[^1]: 110:2 Comp. *Gr.* with *Heb.*
[^2]: 110:10 *Or,* sum.
