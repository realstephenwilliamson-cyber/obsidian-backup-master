## Mūla

क्षयरवलबीजैस्तु दीप्तैर्बिन्दुविभूषितैः ।
झकारसंहृतिप्राणाः सषष्ठस्वरबिन्दुकाः ॥ ^mula

kṣayaravalabījaistu dīptairbinduvibhūṣitaiḥ |
jhakārasaṃhṛtiprāṇāḥ saṣaṣṭhasvarabindukāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 02 - Chapter Thirty, part 1#^pg30|p. 12]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=30>))
>
> Omitting the last three (letters of Navātmā), one should fashion (his) six limbs, that is, the Heart and the rest, with the six (letters that follow), conjoined with the six long vowels. (His) faces should be fashioned with the energized seeds KṢ, Y, R, V, and L and decorated with the Point. (with 30.15–16ab)
>
> The letter JH, Withdrawal (KṢ) and the Vital Breath (H), along with the sixth vowel (Ū) and the Point – (JHKṢHŪṀ); this is (the mantra) Bhairavasadbhāva, adorned with the Half-Moon and the rest. The Mantras of Mātṛkā and Mālinī were taught previously. (with 30.16cd–17)
