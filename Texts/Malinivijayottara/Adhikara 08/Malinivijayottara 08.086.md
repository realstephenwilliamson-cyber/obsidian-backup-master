## Mūla

पञ्चगव्यं ततः कुर्याद्वदनैः पञ्चभिर्बुधः ।
गोमूत्रं गोमयं चैव क्षीरं दधि घृतं तथा ॥ ^mula

pañcagavyaṁ tataḥ kuryād vadanaiḥ pañcabhir budhaḥ |
gomūtraṁ gomayaṁ caiva kṣīraṁ dadhi ghṛtaṁ tathā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2025 – April 2025
> p. 25 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=39>))
>
> The wise man should then (consecrate) the five products of a cow, that is, cow urine, dung, milk, curd and clarified butter, (with the mantras of the) five faces (of Sadāśiva) and with the six limbs. He should consecrate the water (in which sacred) kuśa (grass is immersed) with the six limbs, ending with the upper ones (?)...[^92] One should sprinkle the ground with a little of this (water) and set aside the rest.[^93] (with 8.86–88ab)

[^92]: I cannot understand the following line (8/87cd): *mudre dravāmṛtaṁ baddhvā tattvaṁ tasya vicintayet |* Abhinavagupta does not refer to it.
[^93]: See TĀ 15/371-372.
