# Psalms 61 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 60|← Psalms 60]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 62|Psalms 62 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 61|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**61:1** Unto the end, for Idithun, a psalm of David. ^61-1

**61:2** Shall not my soul be subject to God? for from him is my salvation. ^61-2

**61:3** For he is my God and my saviour: he is my protector, I shall be moved no more. ^61-3

**61:4** How long do you rush in upon a man? you all kill, as if you were thrusting down a leaning wall, and a tottering fence. ^61-4

**61:5** But they have thought to cast away my price; I ran in thirst: they blessed with their mouth, but cursed with their heart. ^61-5

**61:6** But be thou, O my soul, subject to God: for from him is my patience. ^61-6

**61:7** For he is my God and my saviour: he is my helper, I shall not be moved. ^61-7

**61:8** In God is my salvation and my glory: he is the God of my help, and my hope is in God. ^61-8

**61:9** Trust in him, all ye congregation of people: pour out your hearts before him. God is our helper for ever. ^61-9

**61:10** But vain are the sons of men, the sons of men are liars in the balances: that by vanity they may together deceive. ^61-10

**61:11** Trust not in iniquity, and cover not robberies: if riches abound, set not your heart upon them. ^61-11

**61:12** God hath spoken once, these two things have I heard, that power belongeth to God, ^61-12

**61:13** And mercy to thee, O Lord; for thou wilt render to every man according to his works. ^61-13
