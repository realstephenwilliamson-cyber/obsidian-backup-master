# Job 17 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Job 16|← Job 16]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Job 18|Job 18 →]]

## Job prays for relief

**17:1** “My spirit is broken; my days are extinct; the grave is ready for me. ^17-1

**17:2** Surely there are mockers around me, and my eye dwells on their provocation. ^17-2

**17:3** “Lay down a pledge for me with yourself; who is there who will give surety for me? ^17-3

**17:4** Since you have closed their minds to understanding, therefore you will not let them triumph. ^17-4

**17:5** Those who denounce friends for reward—the eyes of their children will fail. ^17-5

**17:6** “He has made me a byword of the peoples, and I am one before whom people spit. ^17-6

**17:7** My eye has grown dim from grief, and all my members are like a shadow. ^17-7

**17:8** The upright are appalled at this, and the innocent stir themselves up against the godless. ^17-8

**17:9** Yet the righteous hold to their way, and they who have clean hands grow stronger and stronger. ^17-9

**17:10** But you, come back now, all of you, and I shall not find a sensible person among you. ^17-10

**17:11** My days are past; my plans are broken off, the desires of my heart. ^17-11

**17:12** They make night into day; ‘The light,’ they say, ‘is near to the darkness.’^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^17-12

**17:13** If I look for Sheol as my house, if I spread my couch in darkness, ^17-13

**17:14** if I say to the Pit, ‘You are my father,’ and to the worm, ‘My mother’ or ‘My sister,’ ^17-14

**17:15** where then is my hope? Who will see my hope? ^17-15

**17:16** Will it go down to the bars of Sheol? Shall we descend together into the dust?” ^17-16
