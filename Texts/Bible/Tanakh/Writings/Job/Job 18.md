# Job 18

← [[Job 17]] · [[Tanakh]] · [[Job 19]] →

Compare: [[NRSVue Job 18|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 18

**18:1** Then Bildad the Shuhite said in reply: ^18-1

**18:2** How long? Put an end to talk!  
Consider, and then we shall speak. ^18-2

**18:3** Why are we thought of as brutes,  
Regarded by you as stupid? ^18-3

**18:4** You who tear yourself to pieces in anger—  
Will earth’s order be disrupted[^1] for your sake?  
Will rocks be dislodged from their place? ^18-4

**18:5** Indeed, the light of the wicked fails;  
The flame of his fire does not shine. ^18-5

**18:6** The light in his tent darkens;  
His lamp fails him. ^18-6

**18:7** His iniquitous strides are hobbled;  
His schemes overthrow him. ^18-7

**18:8** He is led by his feet into the net;  
He walks onto the toils. ^18-8

**18:9** The trap seizes his heel;  
The noose tightens on him. ^18-9

**18:10** The rope for him lies hidden on the ground;  
His snare, on the path. ^18-10

**18:11** Terrors assault him on all sides  
And send his feet flying. ^18-11

**18:12** His progeny hunger;  
Disaster awaits his wife.[^2] ^18-12

**18:13** The tendons under his skin are consumed;  
Death’s first-born consumes his tendons. ^18-13

**18:14** He is torn from the safety of his tent;  
Terror marches him to the king.[^3] ^18-14

**18:15** It lodges in his desolate tent;  
Sulfur is strewn upon his home. ^18-15

**18:16** His roots below dry up,  
And above, his branches wither. ^18-16

**18:17** All mention of him vanishes from the earth;  
He has no name abroad. ^18-17

**18:18** He is thrust from light to darkness,  
Driven from the world. ^18-18

**18:19** He has no seed or breed among his people,  
No survivor where he once lived. ^18-19

**18:20** Generations to come will be appalled at his fate,  
As the previous ones are seized with horror. ^18-20

**18:21** “These were the haunts of the wicked;  
Here was the place of him who knew not God.” ^18-21

[^1]: Lit. “the earth be abandoned.”
[^2]: Lit. “rib” (cf. Gen. 2.22); or “stumbling.”
[^3]: Viz., of the netherworld.
