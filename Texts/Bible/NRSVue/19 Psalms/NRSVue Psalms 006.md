# Psalm 6 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 005|← Psalm 5]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 007|Psalm 7 →]]

## Prayer for recovery from grave illness

*To the leader: with stringed instruments; according to The Sheminith. A Psalm of David.*

**6:1** O LORD, do not rebuke me in your anger or discipline me in your wrath. ^6-1

**6:2** Be gracious to me, O LORD, for I am languishing; O LORD, heal me, for my bones are shaking with terror. ^6-2

**6:3** My soul also is struck with terror, while you, O LORD—how long? ^6-3

**6:4** Turn, O LORD, save my life; deliver me for the sake of your steadfast love. ^6-4

**6:5** For in death there is no remembrance of you; in Sheol who can give you praise? ^6-5

**6:6** I am weary with my moaning; every night I flood my bed with tears; I drench my couch with my weeping. ^6-6

**6:7** My eyes waste away because of grief; they grow weak because of all my foes. ^6-7

**6:8** Depart from me, all you workers of evil, for the LORD has heard the sound of my weeping. ^6-8

**6:9** The LORD has heard my supplication; the LORD accepts my prayer. ^6-9

**6:10** All my enemies shall be ashamed and struck with terror; they shall turn back and in a moment be put to shame. ^6-10
