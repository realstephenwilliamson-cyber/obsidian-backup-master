# svātantrayadīpikā

**Catalog number:** M00533  
**Author:** mānasarāma  
**Manuscript:** Manuscript  
**Published:** Muktabodha Indological Research Institute, India

## Description

Summary of the main tenents of Kashmiri śaivism by Mānasarāma the grand teacher of Swami Lakṣmanjoo. It is written in the form of short aphorisms on which the author himself comments.
