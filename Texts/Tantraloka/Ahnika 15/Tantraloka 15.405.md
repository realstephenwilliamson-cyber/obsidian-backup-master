## Mūla

जन्माद्यखिलसंस्कारशुद्धोऽग्निस्तावता भवेत् ।
पञ्चाङ्गमेव पृथ्व्यादिरूपं कठिनतादिकाः ॥ ^mula

janmādyakhilasaṃskāraśuddho'gnistāvatā bhavet |
pañcāṅgameva pṛthvyādirūpaṃ kaṭhinatādikāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 08 - Chapter Fifteen, part 7#^pg269|p. 251]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=269>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 15.407cd–408ab.
>
> Thus, one should offer, one by one, (five) oblations with the Five Limbs. In this way, the fire (receives) all the (purifying) rites of passage (saṁskāra), starting from (the one imparted at) birth, and so is pure. (with 15.404cd–405ab)
>
> The Five Limbs are Earth and the rest (of the gross elements).“” They sustain (their corresponding) powers of solidity and the rest and perform the (empowering) rites (of passage) of the fire, starting with that of (the impregnation of) the womb. (with 15.405cd–406ab)
