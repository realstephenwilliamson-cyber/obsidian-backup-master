## Mūla

किं तु तूष्णीं-स्थितिर्यद्वा कृत्यं तदनुवर्तनम् ।
यस्त्वकल्पितरूपोऽपि संवाददृढताकृते ॥ ^mula

kiṃ tu tūṣṇīṃ-sthitiryadvā kṛtyaṃ tadanuvartanam |
yastvakalpitarūpo'pi saṃvādadṛḍhatākṛte || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. III
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 03 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v03 04 - Chapter Four, part 3#^pg103|p. 90]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 03.pdf#page=103>))
>
> Just as according to the dualist (Śaivite schools) (the fettered souls who achieve liberation at some point (ādisiddha) and are as they say) ‘liberated Śivas’ (muktaśiva) are inferior to (Śiva Himself) Who is such originally (anādisiddha)} in the same way, (teachers) who have attained knowledge (in the manner just described) are inferior to (the teacher) who attains this knowledge spontaneously. In (the latter’s) presence, (the former) have no authority, just as the ‘liberated Śiva’ souls (have none in Śiva’s presence). They keep quiet, or if they are to do anything, they behave according to his example. (with 4.74cd–76ab)
>
> That teacher who, even though uncreated (akalpita), has by the confirmation (obtained) from other (teachers or scriptures) strengthened (his conviction of the genuineness of his enlightenment) and is thus further purified, is Bhairava in person (sākṣșāāt). (with 4.76cd–77ab)

*Lead-in:* ननु इह स्वतः प्रवृत्ततर्कस्यापि अकल्पितस्य यन्नाम शास्त्रादिसापेक्षत्वं प्रागुक्तं, तत् किमस्य, दूषणमुत भूषणम् ? इत्याशङ्क्याह --

> यस्त्वकल्पितरूपोऽपि संवाददृढताकृते ॥
> अन्यतो लब्धसंस्कारः स साक्षाद्भैरवो गुरुः ।

यः पुनरकल्पितरूपोऽपि गुरुः स्वानुभवमात्रगोचरस्य स्वयंप्रवृत्तस्य ज्ञानस्य परत्रापि तथोपलभ्यमानत्वात्मना संवादनेन "एवमेतत् नान्यथा"इत्येवं रूपं दार्ढ्यं कर्तुम्"अन्यतो -- गुरुशास्त्रादेः समस्तात्, गुरुतः शास्त्रतो वा व्यस्तात्, प्राप्तातिशयः स स्वात्मनि नैराकाङ्क्ष्येण शाक्षाद्भैरवः"पूर्णपरसंविदाविष्ट इत्यर्थः ॥ ७६ ॥
