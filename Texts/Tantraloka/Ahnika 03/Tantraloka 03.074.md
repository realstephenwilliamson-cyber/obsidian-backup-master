## Mūla

ज्ञातव्यविश्वोन्मेषात्मा ज्ञानशक्तितया स्थितः ।
इयं परापरा देवी घोरां या मातृमण्डलीम् ॥ ^mula

jñātavyaviśvonmeṣātmā jñānaśaktitayā sthitaḥ |
iyaṃ parāparā devī ghorāṃ yā mātṛmaṇḍalīm || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. II
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 02 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v02 06 - Chapter Three, part 3#^pg186|p. 170]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 02.pdf#page=186>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 3.73cd–74ab.
>
> The reflective awareness of one’s own nature that, prior (to its aroused state), dwelt alone without companion (ekavīraka), and is (at the same time) the expansion (unmeṣa) (U) of the (objective) universe which is (yet) to be known (jñātavya), abides as the power of knowledge. (with 3.73cd–74ab)
>
> This is the Middling (Parāparā) Goddess, who emits without ceasing the Ghorā circle of Mothers, which is the sole illuminator of the pure-cum-impure Path. (with 3.74cd–75ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* एतदेव स्वदर्शनभङ्ग्या योजयति --

> इयं परापरा देवी घोरां या मातृमण्डलीम् ॥
> सृजत्यविरतं शुद्धाशुद्धमार्गैकदीपिकाम् ।

घोरामिति । यदुक्तम् --

"मिश्रकर्मफलासक्तिं पूर्ववज्जनयन्ति याः ।

मुक्तिमार्गनिरोधन्यस्ताः स्युर्घोराः परापराः ॥"

इति । शुद्धाशुद्धेति -- न पुनर्घोरतर्यादिवदधोऽधःपातिनीम् -- इति भावः ॥ ७४ ॥
