## Chapter 17

**17:1** My spirit is crushed, my days run out;  
The graveyard waits for me. ^17-1

**17:2** Surely mocking men keep me company,  
And with their provocations I close my eyes. ^17-2

**17:3** Come now, stand surety for me!  
Who will give his hand on my behalf? ^17-3

**17:4** You have hidden understanding from their minds;  
Therefore You must not exalt [them]. ^17-4

**17:5** He informs on his friends for a share [of their property],  
And his children’s eyes pine away. ^17-5

**17:6** He made me a byword among people;  
I have become like Tophet[^1] of old. ^17-6

**17:7** My eyes fail from vexation;  
All shapes seem to me like shadows. ^17-7

**17:8** The upright are amazed at this;  
The pure are aroused against the impious. ^17-8

**17:9** The righteous man holds to his way;  
He whose hands are clean grows stronger. ^17-9

**17:10** But all of you, come back now;  
I shall not find a wise man among you. ^17-10

**17:11** My days are done, my tendons severed,  
The strings of my heart. ^17-11

**17:12** They say that night is day,  
That light is here—in the face of darkness. ^17-12

**17:13** If I must look forward to Sheol as my home,  
And make my bed in the dark place, ^17-13

**17:14** Say to the Pit, “You are my father,”  
To the maggots, “Mother,” “Sister”— ^17-14

**17:15** Where, then, is my hope?  
Who can see hope for me? ^17-15

**17:16** Will it descend to Sheol?  
Shall we go down together to the dust? ^17-16

[^1]: That consumed children; cf. Jer. 7.31.
