# Psalm 95

← [[BCP Psalm 094]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 096]] →

*Venite, exultemus* · Book Four · Nineteenth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 095]]

**95:1** Come, let us sing to the LORD; \*  
let us shout for joy to the Rock of our salvation. ^95-1

**95:2** Let us come before his presence with thanksgiving \*  
and raise a loud shout to him with psalms. ^95-2

**95:3** For the LORD is a great God, \*  
and a great King above all gods. ^95-3

**95:4** In his hand are the caverns of the earth, \*  
and the heights of the hills are his also. ^95-4

**95:5** The sea is his, for he made it, \*  
and his hands have molded the dry land. ^95-5

[p. 725]

**95:6** Come, let us bow down, and bend the knee, \*  
and kneel before the LORD our Maker. ^95-6

**95:7** For he is our God,  
and we are the people of his pasture and the sheep of his hand. \*  
Oh, that today you would hearken to his voice! ^95-7

**95:8** Harden not your hearts,  
as your forebears did in the wilderness, \*  
at Meribah, and on that day at Massah,  
when they tempted me. ^95-8

**95:9** They put me to the test, \*  
though they had seen my works. ^95-9

**95:10** Forty years long I detested that generation and said, \*  
"This people are wayward in their hearts;  
they do not know my ways." ^95-10

**95:11** So I swore in my wrath, \*  
"They shall not enter into my rest." ^95-11
