## Mūla

ततोऽर्घपात्रमादाय भावाभावाविकल्पितम् ।
ततश्चास्त्राग्निसंदग्धं शक्त्यम्बुप्लावितं शुवि ॥ ^mula

tato 'rghapātram ādāya bhāvābhāvāvikalpitam |
tataś cāstrāgnisaṁdagdhaṁ śaktyambuplāvitaṁ śuvi || ^iast

## Translation

> Then taking up the sacrificial vessel (*arghapātra*) which, devoid of notions of existence or non-existence, burnt by the fire of the Weapon[^53] and flooded by the water of Śakti, is pure.

[^53]: see TĀ 30/41cd.
