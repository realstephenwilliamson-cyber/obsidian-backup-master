## Mūla

ब्रह्माद्यनाश्रितान्तानां चिनुते सृष्टिसंहृती ।
शश्वद्यद्यप्यपानोऽयमित्थं वहति किंत्वसौ ॥ ^mula

brahmādyanāśritāntānāṃ cinute sṛṣṭisaṃhṛtī |
śaśvadyadyapyapāno'yamitthaṃ vahati kiṃtvasau || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 12 - Chapter Six, part 5#^pg410|p. 395]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=410>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 6.187cd–188ab.
>
> The creation and absorption of (the various causal powers) from Brahmā to Anāśritaśiva is discerned (here) as the contraction and expansion of the great sacred seat (mahāpītha) known as the Root. (with 6.186cd–187ab)
>
> Although the (descending) inhaled breath (apāna) flows continuously in this way, however, the exertion (that impels it) is unconscious. Yogis (however) cultivate it with effort. (with 6.187cd–188ab)
