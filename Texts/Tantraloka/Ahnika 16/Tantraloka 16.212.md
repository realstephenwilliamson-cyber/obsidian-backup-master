## Mūla

सद्योजातादिवक्त्राणि हृदाद्यङ्गानि पञ्च च ।
षट्कृत्वो न्यस्य षट्त्रिंशन्न्यासं कुर्याद्धरादितः ॥ ^mula

sadyojātādivaktrāṇi hṛdādyaṅgāni pañca ca |
ṣaṭkṛtvo nyasya ṣaṭtriṃśannyāsaṃ kuryāddharāditaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 06 - Chapter Sixteen, part 2#^pg111|p. 91]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=111>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 16.211cd–212ab.
>
> And it is also said in the Mālinīvijayottara: ‘(To) these (adepts who are) embraced by them’ (and so on). After having deposited the (fives) faces, namely, Sadyojāta and the rest, and the five limbs, that is, the heart and so on, six times, one should perform the deposition of the thirty-six (principles) from Earth onwards. (with 16.211cd–212)
