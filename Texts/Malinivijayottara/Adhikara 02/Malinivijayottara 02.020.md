## Mūla

आणवोऽयं समाख्यातः शाक्तोऽप्येवंविधः स्मृतः ।
एवं शाम्भवमप्यैभिर्भेदैर्भिन्नं विलक्षयेत् ॥ ^mula

āṇavo 'yam samākhyātaḥ śākto 'py evamvidhaḥ smṛtaḥ |
evam śāmbhavam apy aibhir bhedair bhinnam vilakṣayet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, July 2022 – September 2022
> p. 32 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Malini (journal), July-September 2022.pdf#page=34>))
>
> This (penetration) can (also) be characterized as being of these (three) kinds. (One) is called Individual (*āṇava*). (Another) considered to be such is the Empowered one, as is the Śāmbhava one.
