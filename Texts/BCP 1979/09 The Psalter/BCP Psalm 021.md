# Psalm 21

← [[BCP Psalm 020]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 022]] →

*Domine, in virtute tua* · Book One · Fourth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 021]]

**21:1** The king rejoices in your strength, O LORD; \*  
how greatly he exults in your victory! ^21-1

**21:2** You have given him his heart's desire; \*  
you have not denied him the request of his lips. ^21-2

[p. 609] ^pg609

**21:3** For you meet him with blessings of prosperity, \*  
and set a crown of fine gold upon his head. ^21-3

**21:4** He asked you for life, and you gave it to him: \*  
length of days, for ever and ever. ^21-4

**21:5** His honor is great, because of your victory; \*  
splendor and majesty have you bestowed upon him. ^21-5

**21:6** For you will give him everlasting felicity \*  
and will make him glad with the joy of your presence. ^21-6

**21:7** For the king puts his trust in the LORD; \*  
because of the loving-kindness of the Most High, he will not fall. ^21-7

**21:8** Your hand will lay hold upon all your enemies; \*  
your right hand will seize all those who hate you. ^21-8

**21:9** You will make them like a fiery furnace \*  
at the time of your appearing, O LORD; ^21-9

**21:10** You will swallow them up in your wrath, \*  
and fire shall consume them. ^21-10

**21:11** You will destroy their offspring from the land \*  
and their descendants from among the peoples of the earth. ^21-11

**21:12** Though they intend evil against you  
and devise wicked schemes, \*  
yet they shall not prevail. ^21-12

**21:13** For you will put them to flight \*  
and aim your arrows at them. ^21-13

**21:14** Be exalted, O LORD, in your might; \*  
we will sing and praise your power. ^21-14
