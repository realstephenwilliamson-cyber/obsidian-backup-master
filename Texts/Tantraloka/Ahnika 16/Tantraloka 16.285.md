## Mūla

विकल्पेऽपि गुरोः सम्यगभिन्नशिवताजुषः ।
अविकल्पकपर्यन्तप्रतीक्षा नोपयुज्यते ॥ ^mula

> Even if a teacher who is an authentic state of identity with Śiva is engaged in thought, it is not necessary (nor proper) to examine whether he is ultimately free of thought constructs.
