# Psalms 3 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 02|← Psalms 2]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 04|Psalms 4 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 03|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**3:1** The psalm of David when he fled from the face of his son Absalom. ^3-1

**3:2** Why, O Lord, are they multiplied that afflict me? many are they who rise up against me. ^3-2

**3:3** Many say to my soul: There is no salvation for him in his God. ^3-3

**3:4** But thou, O Lord art my protector, my glory, and the lifter up of my head. ^3-4

**3:5** I have cried to the Lord with my voice: and he hath heard me from his holy hill. ^3-5

**3:6** I have slept and taken my rest: and I have risen up, because the Lord hath protected me. ^3-6

**3:7** I will not fear thousands of the people, surrounding me: arise, O Lord; save me, O my God. ^3-7

**3:8** For thou hast struck all them who are my adversaries without cause: thou hast broken the teeth of sinners. ^3-8

**3:9** Salvation is of the Lord: and thy blessing is upon thy people. ^3-9
