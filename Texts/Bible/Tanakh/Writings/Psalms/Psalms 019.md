## Chapter 19

**19:1** For the leader. A psalm of David. ^19-1

**19:2** The heavens declare the glory of God,  
the sky proclaims His handiwork. ^19-2

**19:3** Day to day makes utterance,  
night to night speaks out. ^19-3

**19:4** There is no utterance,  
there are no words,  
whose sound goes unheard.[^1] ^19-4

**19:5** Their voice[^2] carries throughout the earth,  
their words to the end of the world.  
He placed in them[^3] a tent for the sun, ^19-5

**19:6** who is like a groom coming forth from the chamber,  
like a hero, eager to run his course. ^19-6

**19:7** His rising-place is at one end of heaven,  
and his circuit reaches the other;  
nothing escapes his heat. ^19-7

**19:8** The teaching of the LORD is perfect,  
renewing life;  
the decrees of the LORD are enduring,  
making the simple wise; ^19-8

**19:9** The precepts of the LORD are just,  
rejoicing the heart;  
the instruction of the LORD is lucid,  
making the eyes light up. ^19-9

**19:10** The fear of the LORD is pure,  
abiding forever;  
the judgments of the LORD are true,  
righteous altogether, ^19-10

**19:11** more desirable than gold,  
than much fine gold;  
sweeter than honey,  
than drippings of the comb. ^19-11

**19:12** Your servant pays them heed;  
in obeying them there is much reward. ^19-12

**19:13** Who can be aware of errors?  
Clear me of unperceived guilt, ^19-13

**19:14** and from willful sins[^4] keep Your servant;  
let them not dominate me;  
then shall I be blameless  
and clear of grave offense. ^19-14

**19:15** May the words of my mouth  
and the prayer of my heart[^5]  
be acceptable to You,  
O LORD, my rock and my redeemer. ^19-15

[^1]: With Septuagint, Symmachus, and Vulgate; or “their sound is not heard.”
[^2]: Cf. Septuagint, Symmachus, and Vulgate; Arabic qawwah, “to shout.”
[^3]: Viz., the heavens.
[^4]: Or “arrogant men”; cf. Ps. 119.51.
[^5]: For leb as a source of speech, see note to Eccl. 5.1.
