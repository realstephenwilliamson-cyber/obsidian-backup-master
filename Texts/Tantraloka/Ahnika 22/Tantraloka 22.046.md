## Mūla

विज्ञानार्थी तथा शिष्यो गुरोर्गुर्वन्तरं त्विति ।
गुरूणां भूयसां मध्ये यतो विज्ञानमुत्तमम् ॥ ^mula

vijñānārthī tathā śiṣyo gurorgurvantaraṃ tviti |
gurūṇāṃ bhūyasāṃ madhye yato vijñānamuttamam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 13 - Chapter Twenty-Two#^pg313|p. 293]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=313>))
>
> It is said in the venerable Mataśāstra and in many (other) places that ‘just as a bee desiring fine scent goes from flower to flower, so too a (good) student who desires knowledge should go from teacher to teacher.’ (with 22.45–46ab)
>
> Amongst the many teachers, one’s teacher (in the fullest sense is the one) from whom one has obtained the highest knowledge, and his is the chief initiation here within consciousness. (with 22.46cd–47ab)
