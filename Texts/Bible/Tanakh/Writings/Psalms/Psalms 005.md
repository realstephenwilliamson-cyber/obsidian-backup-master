## Chapter 5

**5:1** For the leader; on *neḥiloth*.[^1] A psalm of David. ^5-1

**5:2** Give ear to my speech, O LORD;  
consider my utterance. ^5-2

**5:3** Heed the sound of my cry,  
my king and God,  
for I pray to You. ^5-3

**5:4** Hear my voice, O LORD, at daybreak;  
at daybreak I plead before You, and wait. ^5-4

**5:5** For You are not a God who desires wickedness;  
evil cannot abide with You; ^5-5

**5:6** wanton men cannot endure in Your sight.  
You detest all evildoers; ^5-6

**5:7** You doom those who speak lies;  
murderous, deceitful men the LORD abhors. ^5-7

**5:8** But I, through Your abundant love, enter Your house;  
I bow down in awe at Your holy temple. ^5-8

**5:9** O LORD, lead me along Your righteous [path][^2]  
because of my watchful foes;  
make Your way straight before me. ^5-9

**5:10** For there is no sincerity on their lips;[^3]  
their heart is [filled with] malice;  
their throat is an open grave;  
their tongue slippery. ^5-10

**5:11** Condemn them, O God;  
let them fall by their own devices;  
cast them out for their many crimes,  
for they defy You. ^5-11

**5:12** But let all who take refuge in You rejoice,  
ever jubilant as You shelter them;  
and let those who love Your name exult in You. ^5-12

**5:13** For You surely bless the righteous man, O LORD,  
encompassing him with favor like a shield. ^5-13

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Or “as You are righteous, lead me.”
[^3]: Lit. “mouth.”
