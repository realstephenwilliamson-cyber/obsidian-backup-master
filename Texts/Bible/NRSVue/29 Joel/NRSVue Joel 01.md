# Joel 1 (NRSVue)

— · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Joel 02|Joel 2 →]]

**1:1** The word of the LORD that came to Joel son of Pethuel: ^1-1

## Lament over the ruin of the country

**1:2** Hear this, O elders; give ear, all inhabitants of the land! Has such a thing happened in your days or in the days of your ancestors? ^1-2

**1:3** Tell your children of it, and let your children tell their children, and their children another generation. ^1-3

**1:4** What the cutting locust left, the swarming locust has eaten; what the swarming locust left, the hopping locust has eaten; and what the hopping locust left, the destroying locust has eaten. ^1-4

**1:5** Wake up, you drunkards, and weep, and wail, all you wine drinkers, over the sweet wine, for it is cut off from your mouth. ^1-5

**1:6** For a nation has invaded my land, powerful and innumerable; its teeth are lions’ teeth, and it has the fangs of a lioness. ^1-6

**1:7** It has laid waste my vines and splintered my fig trees; it has stripped off their bark and thrown it down; their branches have turned white. ^1-7

**1:8** Lament like a virgin dressed in sackcloth for the husband of her youth. ^1-8

**1:9** The grain offering and the drink offering are cut off from the house of the LORD. The priests mourn, the ministers of the LORD. ^1-9

**1:10** The fields are devastated, the ground mourns, for the grain is destroyed, the wine dries up, the oil fails. ^1-10

**1:11** Be dismayed, you farmers; wail, you vinedressers, over the wheat and the barley, for the crops of the field are ruined. ^1-11

**1:12** The vine withers; the fig tree droops. Pomegranate, palm, and apple—all the trees of the field are dried up; surely, joy withers away among the people. ^1-12

## A call to repentance and prayer

**1:13** Put on sackcloth and lament, you priests; wail, you ministers of the altar. Come, pass the night in sackcloth, you ministers of my God! Grain offering and drink offering are withheld from the house of your God. ^1-13

**1:14** Consecrate a fast; call a solemn assembly. Gather the elders and all the inhabitants of the land to the house of the LORD your God, and cry out to the LORD. ^1-14

**1:15** Alas for the day! For the day of the LORD is near, and as destruction from the Almighty^[Traditional rendering of Heb *Shaddai*] it comes. ^1-15

**1:16** Is not the food cut off before our eyes, joy and gladness from the house of our God? ^1-16

**1:17** The seed shrivels under the clods;^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] the storehouses are desolate; the granaries are ruined because the grain has withered. ^1-17

**1:18** How the animals groan! The herds of cattle wander about because there is no pasture for them; even the flocks of sheep are perishing. ^1-18

**1:19** To you, O LORD, I cry, for fire has devoured the pastures of the wilderness, and flames have burned all the trees of the field. ^1-19

**1:20** Even the wild animals cry to you because the watercourses are dried up, and fire has devoured the pastures of the wilderness. ^1-20
