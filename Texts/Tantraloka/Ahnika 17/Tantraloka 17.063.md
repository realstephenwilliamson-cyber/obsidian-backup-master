## Mūla

पृथक्त्वं च मलो मायाभिधानस्तस्य संभवे ।
कर्मक्षयेऽपि नो मुक्तिर्भवेद्विद्येश्वरादिवत् ॥ ^mula

pṛthaktvaṃ ca malo māyābhidhānastasya saṃbhave |
karmakṣaye'pi no muktirbhavedvidyeśvarādivat || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 08 - Chapter Seventeen#^pg187|p. 167]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=187>))
>
> (This) state of separation is the Impurity (mala) called Māyā. As long as it continues to arise, even if karma is exhausted, there is no liberation, as happens, for example, with the Vidyeśvaras.
