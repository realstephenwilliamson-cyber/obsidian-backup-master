## Mūla

तत्सर्वं प्राकृतं प्रोक्तं विनाशोत्पत्तिसंयुतम् ।
अथ सकलभुवनमानं यन्मह्यं निगदितं निजैर्गुरुभिः ॥ ^mula

tatsarvaṃ prākṛtaṃ proktaṃ vināśotpattisaṃyutam |
atha sakalabhuvanamānaṃ yanmahyaṃ nigaditaṃ nijairgurubhiḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 10 - Chapter Eight, part 8#^pg393|p. 375]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=393>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 8.406cd–407ab.
>
> According to (this) teaching, everything from Earth up to Śadāśiva is said to be Nature (prākrta) and is subject to creation and destruction. (with 8.405cd–406ab)
>
> I will now declare in brief the extent of all the worlds as my venerable teacher has taught me, so that it may be quickly transmitted to the intellect. (with 8.406cd–407ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* एवं पुरमानतत्त्वयोजनात्म प्रमेयद्वयमभिधाय पुरसंग्रहाख्यं प्रमेयमासूत्रयितुमुपक्रमते -

> अथ सकलभुवनमानं यन्मह्यं निगदितं निजैर्गुरुभिः ॥
> तद्वक्ष्यते समासाद् बुद्धौ येनाशु सङ्क्रामेत् ।

येनेति, समासाभिधानेन, अत एवैतन्निष्प्रयोजनं न -- इत्याशयः ॥ ४०६ ॥
