## Mūla

वामामृतादिभिर्मुख्यैः पवित्रैः सुमनोरमैः ।
भूमिं रजांसि करणीं खटिकां मूलतोऽर्चयेत् ॥ ^mula

vāmāmṛtādibhirmukhyaiḥ pavitraiḥ sumanoramaiḥ |
bhūmiṃ rajāṃsi karaṇīṃ khaṭikāṃ mūlato'rcayet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 05 - Chapter Thirty-One, part 2#^pg127|p. 109]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=127>))

> (First of all,) one must worship the ground (on which the mandala is to be drawn), and the coloured powders, chalk and thread (by which that is done), with the root (mantra) and with holy and beautiful (offerings), foremost of which is (wine), the nectar of the left.
