# LXX Sirach 05

← [[LXX Sirach 04]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Sirach 06]] →

*Sirach · [[LXX]]*

**5:1** Set not thy heart upon thy goods; and say not, I have enough for my life.  ^5-1

**5:2** Follow not thine own mind and thy strength, to walk in the ways of thy heart:  ^5-2

**5:3** and say not, Who shall control me for my works? for the Lord will surely revenge thy pride.  ^5-3

**5:4** Say not, I have sinned, and what harm hath happened unto me? for the Lord is longsuffering, he will in no wise let thee go.  ^5-4

**5:5** Concerning propitiation, be not without fear to add sin unto sin:  ^5-5

**5:6** and say not, His mercy is great; he will be pacified for the multitude of my sins: for mercy and wrath come from him, and his indignation resteth upon sinners.  ^5-6

**5:7** Make no tarrying to turn to the Lord, and put not off from day to day: for suddenly shall the wrath of the Lord come forth, and in thy security thou shalt be destroyed, and perish in the day of vengeance.  ^5-7

**5:8** Set not thine heart upon goods unjustly gotten; for they shall not profit thee in the day of calamity.  ^5-8

**5:9** Winnow not with every wind, and go not into every way: for so doth the sinner that hath a double tongue.  ^5-9

**5:10** Be stedfast in thy understanding; and let thy word be the same.  ^5-10

**5:11** Be swift to hear; and let thy life be sincere; and with patience give answer.  ^5-11

**5:12** If thou hast understanding, answer thy neighbour; if not, lay thy hand upon thy mouth.  ^5-12

**5:13** Honour and shame is in talk: and the tongue of man is his fall.  ^5-13

**5:14** Be not called a whisperer, and lie not in wait with thy tongue: for a foul shame is upon the thief, and an evil condemnation upon the double tongue.  ^5-14

**5:15** Be not ignorant of any thing in a great matter or a small.  ^5-15
