# Lamentations 4

## Chapter 4

**4:1** Alas!  
The gold is dulled,[^1]  
Debased the finest gold!  
The sacred[^2] gems are spilled  
At every street corner. ^4-1

**4:2** The precious children of Zion;  
Once valued as gold—  
Alas, they are accounted as earthen pots,  
Work of a potter’s hands! ^4-2

**4:3** Even jackals offer the breast  
And suckle their young;  
But my poor people has turned cruel,  
Like ostriches of the desert. ^4-3

**4:4** The tongue of the suckling cleaves  
To its palate for thirst.  
Little children beg for bread;  
None gives them a morsel. ^4-4

**4:5** Those who feasted on dainties  
Lie famished in the streets;  
Those who were reared in purple  
Have embraced refuse heaps. ^4-5

**4:6** The guilt of my poor[^3] people  
Exceeded the iniquity[^4] of Sodom,  
Which was overthrown in a moment,  
Without a hand striking it. ^4-6

**4:7** Her elect were purer than snow,  
Whiter than milk;  
Their limbs were ruddier than coral,  
Their bodies[^5] were like sapphire. ^4-7

**4:8** Now their faces are blacker than soot,  
They are not recognized in the streets;  
Their skin has shriveled on their bones,  
It has become dry as wood. ^4-8

**4:9** Better off were the slain of the sword  
Than those slain by famine,  
Who pined away, [as though] wounded,  
For lack of[^6] the fruits of the field. ^4-9

**4:10** With their own hands, tenderhearted women  
Have cooked their children;  
Such became their fare,  
In the disaster of my poor[^7] people. ^4-10

**4:11** The LORD vented all His fury,  
Poured out His blazing wrath;  
He kindled a fire in Zion  
Which consumed its foundations. ^4-11

**4:12** The kings of the earth did not believe,  
Nor any of the inhabitants of the world,  
That foe or adversary could enter  
The gates of Jerusalem. ^4-12

**4:13** It was for the sins of her prophets,  
The iniquities of her priests,  
Who had shed in her midst  
The blood of the just. ^4-13

**4:14** They wandered blindly through the streets,  
Defiled with blood,  
So that no one was able  
To touch their garments. ^4-14

**4:15** “Away! Unclean!” people shouted at them,  
“Away! Away! Touch not!”  
So they wandered and wandered again;  
For the nations had resolved:  
“They shall stay here no longer.” ^4-15

**4:16** [^8]The LORD’s countenance has turned away from them,  
He will look on them no more.  
They showed no regard for priests,  
No favor to elders. ^4-16

**4:17** Even now our eyes pine away  
In vain for deliverance.  
As we waited, still we wait  
For a nation that cannot help. ^4-17

**4:18** Our steps were checked,  
We could not walk in our squares.[^9]  
Our doom is near, our days are done—  
Alas, our doom has come! ^4-18

**4:19** Our pursuers were swifter  
Than the eagles in the sky;  
They chased us in the mountains,  
Lay in wait for us in the wilderness. ^4-19

**4:20** The breath of our life, the LORD’s anointed,  
Was captured in their traps—  
He in whose shade we had thought  
To live among the nations. ^4-20

**4:21** Rejoice and exult, Fair Edom,  
Who dwell in the land of Uz!  
To you, too, the cup shall pass,  
You shall get drunk and expose your nakedness. ^4-21

**4:22** Your iniquity, Fair Zion, is expiated;  
He will exile you no longer.  
Your iniquity, Fair Edom, He will note;  
He will uncover your sins. ^4-22

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Emendation yields “precious.”
[^3]: See note at 3.48.
[^4]: I.e., punishment.
[^5]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^6]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^7]: See note at 3.48.
[^8]: Meaning of line uncertain.
[^9]: Or “With long strides.”
