## Mūla

निःशेषशास्त्रसदनं किल मध्यदेश-
स्तस्मिन्नजायत गुणाभ्यधिको द्विजन्मा ।
कोऽप्यत्रिगुप्त इति नामनिरुक्तगोत्रः
शास्त्राब्धिचर्वणकलोद्यदगस्त्यगोत्रः ॥ ^mula

niḥśeṣaśāstrasadanaṃ kila madhyadeśa-
stasminnajāyata guṇābhyadhiko dvijanmā |
ko'pyatrigupta iti nāmaniruktagotraḥ
śāstrābdhicarvaṇakalodyadagastyagotraḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 11 - Chapter Thirty-Seven, part 1#^pg261|p. 243]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=261>))
>
> Madhyadeśa (the Land in the Middle)“ is indeed the abode of all the scriptures. Here was born a brahmin who possessed extraordinary qualities, a certain Atrigupta, whose family lineage (gotra) is declared by the etymology of his name, and who, intent on the art of relishing the ocean of the scriptures, (rightly) belongs to Agastya’s clan (gotra).”
