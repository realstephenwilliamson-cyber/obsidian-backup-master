## Précis Appendix

The précis at the head of each chapter are collected here.

They are expanded to include the entire set of précis from those chapters in *Leviathan* that appear in excerpted form in the text. The added précis attach to material in the chapters that is unique to *Leviathan*; they are identified with italics. For entirely novel chapters and the new Part iv, titles are provided. This supplementary material is included in order to provide a comprehensive overview of the relative extent of both continuity and novelty in *Leviathan*.
