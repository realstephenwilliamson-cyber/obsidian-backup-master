## Mūla

अजरामरपददानप्रवणं कुलसंज्ञितं परमम् ।
येऽप्यप्राप्तविबोधास्तेऽभ्युदितोत्फुल्लयागसंरूढाः ॥ ^mula

ajarāmarapadadānapravaṇaṃ kulasaṃjñitaṃ paramam |
ye'pyaprāptavibodhāste'bhyuditotphullayāgasaṃrūḍhāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 11 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 3#^pg335|p. 318]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=335>))
>
> That (ejaculate is) also (taken) from the main mouth and passes mutually (from mouth to) mouth. For it is said that it is the supreme (sacrificial substance) called Kula, which bestows the plane (of being that is) free of old age and death. (with 29.128cd–129ab)
>
> Those who have not attained (complete) enlightenment (aprāptavibodha), (but) are well-established in the sacrifice that has blossomed by the ejaculate, the deities located in that Wheel, fashioned by that (consciousness) (parikalpita), attain discerning knowledge (of consciousness, and bestow its fruits to the less enlightened). (with 29.129cd–130ab)
