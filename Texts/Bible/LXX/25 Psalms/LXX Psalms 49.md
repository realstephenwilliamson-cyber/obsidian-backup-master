# LXX Psalms 49

← [[LXX Psalms 48]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 50]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 050]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 050]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**49:1** A Psalm for Asaph. The God of gods, the Lord, has spoken, and called the earth from the rising of the sun to the going down *thereof*.[^1]  ^49-1

**49:2** Out of Sion *comes* the excellence of his beauty.  ^49-2

**49:3** God, our God, shall come manifestly, and shall not keep silence: a fire shall be kindled before him, and round about him there shall be a very great tempest.  ^49-3

**49:4** He shall summon the heaven above, and the earth, that he may judge his people.  ^49-4

**49:5** Assemble ye his saints to him, those that have engaged in a covenant with him upon sacrifices.  ^49-5

**49:6** And the heavens shall declare his righteousness: for God is judge. Pause.  ^49-6

**49:7** Hear, my people, and I will speak to thee, O Israel: and I will testify to thee: I am God, thy God.  ^49-7

**49:8** I will not reprove thee on account of thy sacrifices; for thy whole-burnt-offerings are before me continually.  ^49-8

**49:9** I will take no bullocks out of thine house, nor he-goats out of thy flocks.  ^49-9

**49:10** For all the wild beasts of the thicket are mine, the cattle on the mountains, and oxen.  ^49-10

**49:11** I know all the birds of the sky; and the beauty of the field is mine.  ^49-11

**49:12** If I should be hungry, I will not tell thee: for the world is mine, and the fulness of it.  ^49-12

**49:13** Will I eat the flesh of bulls, or drink the blood of goats?  ^49-13

**49:14** Offer to God the sacrifice of praise; and pay thy vows to the Most High.  ^49-14

**49:15** And call upon me in the day of affliction; and I will deliver thee, and thou shalt glorify me. Pause.  ^49-15

**49:16** But to the sinner God has said, Why dost thou declare my ordinances, and take up my covenant in thy mouth?  ^49-16

**49:17** Whereas thou hast hated instruction, and hast cast my words behind *thee*.  ^49-17

**49:18** If thou sawest a thief, thou rannest along with him, and hast cast in thy lot with adulterers.[^2]  ^49-18

**49:19** Thy mouth has multiplied wickedness, and thy tongue has framed deceit.[^3]  ^49-19

**49:20** Thou didst sit and speak against thy brother, and didst scandalise thy mother's son.  ^49-20

**49:21** These things thou didst, and I kept silence: thou thoughtest wickedly that I should be like thee; *but* I will reprove thee, and set *thine offences* before thee.  ^49-21

**49:22** Now consider these things, ye that forget God, lest he rend *you*, and there be no deliverer.  ^49-22

**49:23** The sacrifice of praise will glorify me: and that is the way wherein I will shew to him the salvation of God.[^4]  ^49-23

[^1]: 49:1 *Or,* of.
[^2]: 49:18 See 1 Pe 4. 4.
[^3]: 49:19 From these words in *Alex.* to Ps. 79. 11, thirty psalms are wanting.
[^4]: 49:23 *Gr.* there.
