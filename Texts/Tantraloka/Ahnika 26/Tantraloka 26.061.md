## Mūla

क्रिया स्यात्तन्मयीभूत्यै हृदयाह्लाददायिभिः ।
शिवाभेदभराद्भाववर्गः श्च्योतति यं रसम् ॥ ^mula

kriyā syāttanmayībhūtyai hṛdayāhlādadāyibhiḥ |
śivābhedabharādbhāvavargaḥ ścyotati yaṃ rasam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 17 - Chapter Twenty-Five (continued) and Chapter Twenty-Six#^pg412|p. 392]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=412>))
>
> The essence of consciousness is freedom, and that is its dense (uninterrupted) bliss. Thus, (it is for this reason that ritual) action (kriyā) aimed at (attaining) a state of oneness (with consciousness) (must be performed with the aid of factors that) bring joy to the heart. (with 26.60cd–61ab)
>
> In order to worship (in the highest sense), the wise (awakened) man should offer (as an act of worship) into the Supreme Abode the juice (of aesthetic delight) (rasa) that exudes from (the multitude of) phenomena, (pressed down) because of the weight of (their) oneness with Śiva. (with 26.61cd–62ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* अत एव एषां चिदानन्दघनमेव रूपं पूजायोग्यम्--इत्याह--

> शिवाभेदभराद्भाववर्गः श्च्योतति यं रसम् ॥
> तमेव परमे धाम्नि पूजनायार्पयेद् बुधः ।
