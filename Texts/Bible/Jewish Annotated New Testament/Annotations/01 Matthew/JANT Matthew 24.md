### 24.1–14: The Temple’s destruction

*[[NRSVue Matthew 24#^24-1|Text]], p. 54* · ([[Mark 13#^13-1|Mk 13.1–13]]; [[Luke 21#^21-5|Lk 21.5–19]]). ^24-1

**[[NRSVue Matthew 24#^24-1|1–2]]:** *All will be thrown down*, evoking prophetic threats against the Temple (e.g., [[Jeremiah 26#^26-4|Jer 26.4–6]]; [[Micah 3#^3-12|Mic 3.12]]; see also [[Matthew 26#^26-67|Matt 26.67]]; [[Matthew 27#^27-40|27.40]]; [[Acts 06#^6-14|Acts 6.14]]; [[LXX 2 Maccabees 02#^2-22|2 Macc 2.22]]; Josephus, *J.W*. 6.267; 1QpHab 9.6–7; [[Sukkah 51#51b|*b. Sukk.* 51b]]). Temple renovations begun by Herod the Great ca. 19 BCE were not completed in Jesus’ time. ^24-1-2

**[[NRSVue Matthew 24#^24-3|3]]:** *Mount of Olives*, see 21.1n.; the Temple could be seen from there. *Sign*, like the Pharisees, the disciples seek a sign; Jesus offers a prediction. *Your coming*, Gk “parousia,” a term associated with military and imperial parades. Among Jesus’ followers, it came to mean Jesus’ return to inaugurate the final judgment and the full messianic age (e.g., [[1 Thessalonians 02#^2-19|1 Thess 2.19]]). ^24-3

**[[NRSVue Matthew 24#^24-5|5]]:** *I am the Messiah*, see 7.15n. on false prophets. Josephus lists several first-century messianic claimants (*J.W.* 6.259–63; 288). See “Messianic Movements,” p. 622. ^24-5

**[[NRSVue Matthew 24#^24-6|6]]:** *Rumors of wars*, see [[Isaiah 19#^19-1|Isa 19.1–4]]; [[Jeremiah 51#^51-46|Jer 51.46]]; [[Zechariah 14#^14-1|Zech 14.1–37]]; [[Daniel 07#^7-21|Dan 7.21]]; [[Daniel 09#^9-26|9.26]]; 11; see also [[Revelation 06#^6-4|Rev 6.4]],[[Revelation 06#^6-12|12–17]]; *Pss. Sol*. 15.7–15; *2 Bar*. 27.3–5; *4 Ezra* \[2 Esd\] 13.31–32; *Sib. Or*. 3.635–51. ^24-6

**[[NRSVue Matthew 24#^24-8|8]]:** *Birth pangs*, [[Isaiah 26#^26-17|Isa 26.17]]; [[Daniel 02#^2-28|Dan 2.28]]; *4 Ezra* \[2 Esd\] 13.31–32; see also [[Joel 2#^2-10|Joel 2.10–11]]; Am 4.6–13; [[Zechariah 14#^14-5|Zech 14.5]]; [[Revelation 06#^6-8|Rev 6.8]]; [[Revelation 18#^18-8|18.8]]; *2 Bar*. 27.6; 70.8; *m. Ber*. 9.2; [[Berakhot 55#55a|*b. Ber*. 55a]]. ^24-8

**[[NRSVue Matthew 24#^24-9|9]]:** *Hand you over*, 10.16–25n.; 16.24n.; [[John 15#^15-18|Jn 15.18–21]]. ^24-9

**[[NRSVue Matthew 24#^24-10|10–12]]:** *Will betray one another*, see 18.6n. ^24-10

**[[NRSVue Matthew 24#^24-14|14]]:** *All the nations*, Both Jews and Gentiles (see 8.10n.; 9.17; 15.28; 18.6; 21.21; 28.19). Matthew defers the eschaton until the world is evangelized (see 28.19–20n.). Jewish sources link the eschaton to the obedience and repentance of Israel; see [[Ezekiel 36#^36-24|Ezek 36.24–29]]; [[Daniel 12#^12-12|Dan 12.12–13]]; [[LXX Tobit 13#^13-6|Tob 13.6]]; *b. Bat*. 10a; [[Nedarim 13#13b|*b. Ned*. 13b]]. ^24-14

### 24.15–35: The coming of the Son of Man

*[[NRSVue Matthew 24#^24-15|Text]], p. 55* · ([[Mark 13#^13-14|Mk 13.14–32]]; [[Luke 21#^21-20|Lk 21.20–33]]). ^24-15

**[[NRSVue Matthew 24#^24-15|15]]:** *Desolating sacrilege*, [[Daniel 09#^9-27|Dan 9.27]]; [[Daniel 11#^11-31|11.31]]; [[Daniel 12#^12-11|12.11]]. This referred originally to the action of Antiochus IV (called “Epiphanes”) profaning the altar of the Jerusalem Temple in 168 or 167 BCE ([[LXX 1 Maccabees 01#^1-54|1 Macc 1.54]]). ^24-15-2

**[[NRSVue Matthew 24#^24-16|16]]:** *Flee*, Matthew may be referencing the revolt of 66–70 CE when Jews fled Jerusalem (Eusebius, *Hist. eccl*. 3.5.3); see also [[Zechariah 14#^14-5|Zech 14.5]]. ^24-16

**[[NRSVue Matthew 24#^24-20|20]]:** *On a sabbath*, in Jewish law, breaking Sabbath laws is permissible to preserve life (see 12.1n.; [[LXX 1 Maccabees 02#^2-41|1 Macc 2.41]] [[Shabbat 128#128b|*b. Shabb.* 128b]]; [[Yoma 85#85a|*b. Yoma* 85a]]-b). ^24-20

**[[NRSVue Matthew 24#^24-21|21–22]]:** See [[Joel 2#^2-2|Joel 2.2]]; [[Daniel 12#^12-1|Dan 12.1]]. *Elect*, for Matthew, faithful church members ([[Torah 17 - Yitro#^19-16|Ex 19.16]]; [[Torah 51 - Nitzavim#^30-3|Deut 30.3–4]]; [[Isaiah 11#^11-11|Isa 11.11]],[[Isaiah 11#^11-16|16]]; [[Isaiah 18#^18-3|18.3]]; [[Isaiah 27#^27-13|27.13]]; [[Ezekiel 39#^39-27|Ezek 39.27]]). *Days will be cut short*, [[LXX Sirach 36#^36-8|Sir 36.8]]; *2 Bar*. 20.1-2; 54.1; 83.1; *4 Ezra* \[2 Esd\] 4.26. ^24-21

**[[NRSVue Matthew 24#^24-23|23–24]]:** See 24.5n. ^24-23

**[[NRSVue Matthew 24#^24-28|28]]:** *Vultures*, Gk “aetoi,” lit., “eagles”; perhaps referring to Roman troops. ^24-28

**[[NRSVue Matthew 24#^24-29|29]]:** [[Isaiah 19#^19-10|Isa 19.10]]; [[Ezekiel 32#^32-7|Ezek 32.7]]; [[Joel 2#^2-10|Joel 2.10–11]]; Am 8.9; [[Zephaniah 1#^1-15|Zeph 1.15]]. ^24-29

**[[NRSVue Matthew 24#^24-30|30]]:** *Sign*, i.e., the coming of the Son of Man, who is his own sign. *Son of Man coming on the clouds*, see 8.20n.; 10.23n.; 26.64; [[Daniel 07#^7-13|Dan 7.13–14]]; [[1 Thessalonians 04#^4-16|1 Thess 4.16]]. ^24-30

**[[NRSVue Matthew 24#^24-31|31]]:** *Trumpet*, see [[Isaiah 27#^27-13|Isa 27.13]]; [[Zechariah 09#^9-14|Zech 9.14]]. ^24-31

**[[NRSVue Matthew 24#^24-34|34]]:** See 10.23; 16.28n.; 23.36n. *His elect*, see 24.22n. ^24-34

### 24.36–44: Watchfulness

*[[NRSVue Matthew 24#^24-36|Text]], p. 56* · ([[Mark 13#^13-33|Mk 13.33–37]]; [[Luke 21#^21-34|Lk 21.34–36]]; [[Luke 17#^17-26|17.26–35]]). ^24-36

**[[NRSVue Matthew 24#^24-36|36]]:** 11.27. The Son appears subordinate to the Father (see [[John 14#^14-28|Jn 14.28]]; [[1 Corinthians 15#^15-28|1 Cor 15.28]]). Only God knows when the time will arrive ([[Zechariah 14#^14-7|Zech 14.7]]; *2 Bar*. 21.8). ^24-36-2

**[[NRSVue Matthew 24#^24-37|37]]:** *Days of Noah*, time of sinfulness ([[Torah 02 - Noach#^6-11|Gen 6.11–12]]; see [[Isaiah 54#^54-9|Isa 54.9]]; see also [[Sanhedrin 108#108a|*b. Sanh*. 108a]], “The generation of the flood have no portion in the world to come (Heb “olam haba”). ^24-37

**[[NRSVue Matthew 24#^24-39|39]]:** *Coming of the Son of Man*, without warning. ^24-39

**[[NRSVue Matthew 24#^24-40|40–41]]:** *In the field . . . grinding meal*, a gendered pair. ^24-40

**[[NRSVue Matthew 24#^24-42|42]]:** *Keep awake*, 25.13 ([[Mark 13#^13-35|Mk 13.35]],[[Mark 13#^13-37|37]]; [[Mark 14#^14-34|14.34]]; [[1 Thessalonians 05#^5-6|1 Thess 5.6]]). ^24-42

**[[NRSVue Matthew 24#^24-43|43]]:** Like a *thief*, see [[1 Thessalonians 05#^5-2|1 Thess 5.2]]; [[Revelation 03#^3-3|Rev 3.3]]; [[Revelation 16#^16-15|16.15]]. ^24-43

### 24.45–51: Parable of the faithful servant

*[[NRSVue Matthew 24#^24-45|Text]], p. 56* · ([[Luke 12#^12-42|Lk 12.42–46]]). ^24-45

**[[NRSVue Matthew 24#^24-45|45]]:** *Faithful and wise slave,* community leaders responsible for keeping followers faithful until Jesus’ (the “master’s”) return. *Food*, spiritual nourishment ([[Torah 36 - Beha'alotcha#^11-12|Num 11.12–13]]; [[1 Corinthians 03#^3-1|1 Cor 3.1–3]]; [[2 Timothy 02#^2-15|2 Tim 2.15]]; [[Hebrews 05#^5-12|Heb 5.12]],[[Hebrews 05#^5-14|14]]). ^24-45-2

**[[NRSVue Matthew 24#^24-51|51]]:** See 8.12n.; 13.42,50; 21.41; 22.13; 25.30,41,46. ^24-51
