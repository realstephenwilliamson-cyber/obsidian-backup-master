# Psalm 16

← [[BCP Psalm 015]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 017]] →

*Conserva me, Domine* · Book One · Third Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 016]]

**16:1** Protect me, O God, for I take refuge in you; \*  
I have said to the LORD, "You are my Lord,  
my good above all other." ^16-1

[p. 600] ^pg600

**16:2** All my delight is upon the godly that are in the land, \*  
upon those who are noble among the people. ^16-2

**16:3** But those who run after other gods \*  
shall have their troubles multiplied. ^16-3

**16:4** Their libations of blood I will not offer, \*  
nor take the names of their gods upon my lips. ^16-4

**16:5** O LORD, you are my portion and my cup; \*  
it is you who uphold my lot. ^16-5

**16:6** My boundaries enclose a pleasant land; \*  
indeed, I have a goodly heritage. ^16-6

**16:7** I will bless the LORD who gives me counsel; \*  
my heart teaches me, night after night. ^16-7

**16:8** I have set the LORD always before me; \*  
because he is at my right hand I shall not fall. ^16-8

**16:9** My heart, therefore, is glad, and my spirit rejoices; \*  
my body also shall rest in hope. ^16-9

**16:10** For you will not abandon me to the grave, \*  
nor let your holy one see the Pit. ^16-10

**16:11** You will show me the path of life; \*  
in your presence there is fullness of joy,  
and in your right hand are pleasures for evermore. ^16-11
