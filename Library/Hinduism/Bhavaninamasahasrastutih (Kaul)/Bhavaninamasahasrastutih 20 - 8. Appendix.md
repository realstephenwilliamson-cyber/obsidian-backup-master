ABOUT THE BOOK

Bhavāni-nāma-sahasra-stutih is an eulogy of a thousand divine names of Bhavāni Parā Śakti. It celebrates the qualities and characteristics \`of the primordial mover. Basically a treatise on non-duality in the \$ākta tradition dealing with the secrets of Kundalini Yoga, its philosophy is quite akin to the Krama doctrine of Kashmir Śaivism. It also provides hints on the Kula, the Spaṁùda and the Pratyabhijñā systems of aiva

philosophy.

This Śākta text is based on the voluminous Rudrayāmala Tañṁtra which figures in the list of Bhairava Agamas. The famous Vijñānabhairava and Parātriśikā held in the highest esteem by Kashmiri aiva scholars, constitute two chapters of the same Tantra. This essentially reveals close affinity between the cuïts of Śākta and aiva thought.

The mistakes and misprints detected in the text have been removed and corrections made in consultation with Pandit Raghunath Kokiloo, a learned Pandit, who is a follower of the Sākta tradition of Sahib Kaul (17th century) of Kashmir. The present work is a maiden translation into English of the Sanskrit text, also offering a commentary with foot-notes and relevant quotations from other sacred works. These features render the book useful to the general reader and the Sakta scholar alike. “It is a profound experience”, observes Sri M.P. Pandit, “to read this

exposition”.

ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Born in 1914 at Srinagar (Kashmir), Jankinath Kaul ‘Kamal’ took keen interest in scholarly pursuits right from his boyhood days. He remained a devoted teacher for over three decades until he retired as Senior Lecturer from the D.A.V. Institute, Srinagar in 1979. With his good educational background, which includes a Master's degree in Sanskrit from the University of Jammu & Kashmir, and with his flair for writing in several languages, Shri Kaul has accomplished fine literary translations of Sanskrit verse into English, Hindi and Kashmiri. Having authored several books and monographs, he has also composed devotional poem in Sanskrit, Hindi and Kashmiri, and has published a good number of learned papers in English and Sanskrit in the fields of Kashmir Shaivism and Vedanta philosophy.
