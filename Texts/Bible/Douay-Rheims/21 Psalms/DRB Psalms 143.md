# Psalms 143 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 142|← Psalms 142]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 144|Psalms 144 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 143|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**143:1** Blessed be the Lord my God, who teacheth my hands to fight, and my fingers to war. ^143-1

**143:2** My mercy, and my refuge: my support, and my deliverer: My protector, and I have hoped in him: who subdueth my people under me. ^143-2

**143:3** Lord, what is man, that thou art made known to him? or the son of man, that thou makest account of him? ^143-3

**143:4** Man is like to vanity: his days pass away like a shadow. ^143-4

**143:5** Lord, bow down thy heavens and descend: touch the mountains and they shall smoke. ^143-5

**143:6** Send forth lightning, and thou shalt scatter them: shoot out thy arrows, and thou shalt trouble them. ^143-6

**143:7** Put forth thy hand from on high, take me out, and deliver me from many waters: from the hand of strange children: ^143-7

**143:8** Whose mouth hath spoken vanity: and their right hand is the right hand of iniquity. ^143-8

**143:9** To thee, O God, I will sing a new canticle: on the psaltery and an instrument of ten strings I will sing praises to thee. ^143-9

**143:10** Who givest salvation to kings: who hast redeemed thy servant David from the malicious sword: ^143-10

**143:11** Deliver me, And rescue me out of the hand of strange children; whose mouth hath spoken vanity: and their right hand is the right hand of iniquity: ^143-11

**143:12** Whose sons are as new plants in their youth: Their daughters decked out, adorned round about after the similitude of a temple: ^143-12

**143:13** Their storehouses full, flowing out of this into that. Their sheep fruitful in young, abounding in their goings forth: ^143-13

**143:14** Their oxen fat. There is no breach of wall, nor passage, nor crying out in their streets. ^143-14

**143:15** They have called the people happy, that hath these things: but happy is that people whose God is the Lord. ^143-15
