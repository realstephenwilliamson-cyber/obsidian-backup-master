# Job 41

← [[Job 40]] · [[Tanakh]] · [[Job 42]] →

Compare: [[NRSVue Job 41|NRSVue]] (Heb 41:1–26 = NRSVue 41:9–34; NRSVue 41:1–8 = Heb 40:25–32)

## Chapter 41

**41:1** See, any hope [of capturing] him must be disappointed;  
One is prostrated by the very sight of him. ^41-1

**41:2** There is no one so fierce as to rouse him;  
Who then can stand up to Me? ^41-2

**41:3** Whoever confronts Me I will requite,  
For everything under the heavens is Mine. ^41-3

**41:4** I will not be silent concerning him  
Or the praise of his martial exploits.[^1] ^41-4

**41:5** Who can uncover his outer garment?  
Who can penetrate the folds of his jowls? ^41-5

**41:6** Who can pry open the doors of his face?  
His bared teeth strike terror. ^41-6

**41:7** His protective scales are his pride,  
Locked with a binding seal. ^41-7

**41:8** One scale touches the other;  
Not even a breath can enter between them. ^41-8

**41:9** Each clings to each;  
They are interlocked so they cannot be parted. ^41-9

**41:10** His sneezings flash lightning,  
And his eyes are like the glimmerings of dawn. ^41-10

**41:11** Firebrands stream from his mouth;  
Fiery sparks escape. ^41-11

**41:12** Out of his nostrils comes smoke  
As from a steaming, boiling cauldron. ^41-12

**41:13** His breath ignites coals;  
Flames blaze from his mouth. ^41-13

**41:14** Strength resides in his neck;  
Power leaps before him. ^41-14

**41:15** The layers of his flesh stick together;  
He is as though cast hard; he does not totter. ^41-15

**41:16** His heart is cast hard as a stone,  
Hard as the nether millstone. ^41-16

**41:17** Divine beings are in dread as he rears up;  
As he crashes down, they cringe. ^41-17

**41:18** No sword that overtakes him can prevail,  
Nor spear, nor missile, nor lance. ^41-18

**41:19** He regards iron as straw,  
Bronze, as rotted wood. ^41-19

**41:20** No arrow can put him to flight;  
Slingstones turn into stubble for him. ^41-20

**41:21** Clubs[^2] are regarded as stubble;  
He scoffs at the quivering javelin. ^41-21

**41:22** His underpart is jagged shards;  
It spreads a threshing-sledge on the mud. ^41-22

**41:23** He makes the depths seethe like a cauldron;  
He makes the sea [boil] like an ointment-pot. ^41-23

**41:24** His wake is a luminous path;  
He makes the deep seem white-haired. ^41-24

**41:25** There is no one on land who can dominate him,  
Made as he is without fear. ^41-25

**41:26** He sees all that is haughty;  
He is king over all proud beasts. ^41-26

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
