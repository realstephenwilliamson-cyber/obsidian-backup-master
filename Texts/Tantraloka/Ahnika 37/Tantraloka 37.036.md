## Mūla

प्राक्कर्मभोगिपशुतोचितभोगभाजा
किं जन्मना ननु सुखैकपदेऽपि धाम्नि ।
सर्वो हि भाविनि परं परितोषमेति
संभाविते न तु निमेषिणि वर्तमाने ॥ ^mula

prākkarmabhogipaśutocitabhogabhājā
kiṃ janmanā nanu sukhaikapade'pi dhāmni |
sarvo hi bhāvini paraṃ paritoṣameti
saṃbhāvite na tu nimeṣiṇi vartamāne || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 11 - Chapter Thirty-Seven, part 1#^pg260|p. 242]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=260>))
>
> What use is birth, be it even in a place where there is only happiness, (if it be one) in which one (only) experiences the consequences that befit the fettered state, that suffers the consequences of previous actions (karman)? The greatest (possible) delight for all (who are here is only) in what they hope for in the future, not indeed in the present, that lasts (just) an instant.

## Viveka
