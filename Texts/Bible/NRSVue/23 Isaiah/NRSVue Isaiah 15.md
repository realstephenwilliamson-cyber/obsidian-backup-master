# Isaiah 15 (NRSVue)

## An oracle concerning Moab

**15:1** An oracle concerning Moab. Because Ar is laid waste in a night, Moab is undone; because Kir is laid waste in a night, Moab is undone. ^15-1

**15:2** Daughter Dibon^[Cn: Heb *the house and Dibon*] has gone up to the high places to weep; over Nebo and over Medeba Moab wails. Every head is shaved; every beard is shorn; ^15-2

**15:3** in the streets they bind on sackcloth; on the housetops and in the squares everyone wails and melts in tears. ^15-3

**15:4** Heshbon and Elealeh cry out; their voices are heard as far as Jahaz; therefore the loins of Moab quiver;^[Or *the armed men of Moab cry aloud*] his soul trembles. ^15-4

**15:5** My heart cries out for Moab; his fugitives flee to Zoar, to Eglath-shelishiyah. For at the ascent of Luhith they go up weeping; on the road to Horonaim they raise a cry of destruction; ^15-5

**15:6** the waters of Nimrim are a desolation; the grass is withered; the new growth fails; vegetation is no more. ^15-6

**15:7** Therefore the abundance they have gained and what they have laid up they carry away over the Wadi of the Willows. ^15-7

**15:8** For a cry has gone around the land of Moab; the wailing reaches to Eglaim; the wailing reaches to Beer-elim. ^15-8

**15:9** For the waters of Dibon^[Q ms Vg: MT *Dimon*] are full of blood, yet I will bring upon Dibon^[Q ms Vg: MT *Dimon*] even more—a lion for those of Moab who escape, for the remnant of the land. ^15-9
