# Sirach 15 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Sirach 14|← Sirach 14]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Sirach 16|Sirach 16 →]]

**15:1** Whoever fears the Lord will do this, and whoever holds to the law will obtain wisdom.^[Gk *her*] ^15-1

**15:2** She will come to meet him like a mother, and like a young bride she will welcome him. ^15-2

**15:3** She will feed him with the bread of understanding and give him the water of wisdom to drink. ^15-3

**15:4** He will lean on her and not fall, and he will rely on her and not be put to shame. ^15-4

**15:5** She will exalt him above his neighbors and will open his mouth in the midst of the assembly. ^15-5

**15:6** Gladness^[Other ancient authorities read *He will find gladness*] and a crown of rejoicing and an everlasting name he will inherit. ^15-6

**15:7** The foolish will not obtain her, and sinners will not see her. ^15-7

**15:8** She is far from arrogance, and liars will never think of her. ^15-8

**15:9** Praise is unseemly on the lips of a sinner, for it has not been sent from the Lord. ^15-9

**15:10** For in wisdom must praise be uttered, and the Lord will make it prosper. ^15-10

## Freedom of choice

**15:11** Do not say, “It was the Lord’s doing that I fell away,” for he does not do^[Heb: Gk *you ought not to do*] what he hates. ^15-11

**15:12** Do not say, “It was he who led me astray,” for he has no need of the sinful. ^15-12

**15:13** The Lord hates all abominations; such things are not loved by those who fear him. ^15-13

**15:14** It was he who created humans in the beginning, and he left them in the power of their own inclinations.^[Heb: Gk *deliberation*] ^15-14

**15:15** If you choose, you can keep the commandments, and to act faithfully is a matter of your choice. ^15-15

**15:16** He has placed before you fire and water; stretch out your hand for whichever you choose. ^15-16

**15:17** Before each person are life and death, and whichever one chooses will be given. ^15-17

**15:18** For great is the wisdom of the Lord; he is mighty in power and sees everything; ^15-18

**15:19** his eyes are on those who fear him, and he knows every human action. ^15-19

**15:20** He has not commanded anyone to be ungodly, and he has not given anyone permission to sin. ^15-20
