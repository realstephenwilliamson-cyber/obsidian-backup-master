## Mūla

शंभुनाथेनोपदिष्टां दृष्टां सद्भावशासने ।
सुधाग्निमरुतो मन्दपरकालाग्निवायवः ॥ ^mula

śaṃbhunāthenopadiṣṭāṃ dṛṣṭāṃ sadbhāvaśāsane |
sudhāgnimaruto mandaparakālāgnivāyavaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 12 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 4#^pg382|p. 365]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=382>))
>
> Now I will talk about an initiation that possesses (signs) that instil conviction (of its effectiveness) (pratyaya), taught (to me) by the wise and satisfied Śambhunātha, who saw it in the Sadbhāvaśāsana. (with 29.211cd–212ab)
>
> Each one of (the following) three syllabic groups (piṇḍa) is said to bring about paralysis, namely: (the one) composed of nectar (S), fire (R) and wind (Y); (the one consisting of) the slow one (D), the next one (DH), time (M), fire (R) and wind (Y); (the one made of) fire (R), moon (S), breath (H), the peak (K), fire (R) and wind (Y). AIl (three) have the sixth (vowel U).). (with 29.212cd–213)
