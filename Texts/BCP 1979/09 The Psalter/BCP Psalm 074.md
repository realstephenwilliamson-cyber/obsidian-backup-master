# Psalm 74

← [[BCP Psalm 073]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 075]] →

*Ut quid, Deus?* · Book Three · Fourteenth Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 074]]

**74:1** O God, why have you utterly cast us off? \*  
why is your wrath so hot against the sheep of your pasture? ^74-1

**74:2** Remember your congregation that you purchased long ago, \*  
the tribe you redeemed to be your inheritance,  
and Mount Zion where you dwell. ^74-2

**74:3** Turn your steps toward the endless ruins; \*  
the enemy has laid waste everything in your sanctuary. ^74-3

**74:4** Your adversaries roared in your holy place; \*  
they set up their banners as tokens of victory. ^74-4

**74:5** They were like men coming up with axes to a grove of trees; \*  
they broke down all your carved work with hatchets and hammers. ^74-5

**74:6** They set fire to your holy place; \*  
they defiled the dwelling-place of your Name  
and razed it to the ground. ^74-6

**74:7** They said to themselves, "Let us destroy them altogether." \*  
They burned down all the meeting-places of God in the land. ^74-7

[p. 690] ^pg690

**74:8** There are no signs for us to see;  
there is no prophet left; \*  
there is not one among us who knows how long. ^74-8

**74:9** How long, O God, will the adversary scoff? \*  
will the enemy blaspheme your Name for ever? ^74-9

**74:10** Why do you draw back your hand? \*  
why is your right hand hidden in your bosom? ^74-10

**74:11** Yet God is my King from ancient times, \*  
victorious in the midst of the earth. ^74-11

**74:12** You divided the sea by your might \*  
and shattered the heads of the dragons upon the waters; ^74-12

**74:13** You crushed the heads of Leviathan \*  
and gave him to the people of the desert for food. ^74-13

**74:14** You split open spring and torrent; \*  
you dried up ever-flowing rivers. ^74-14

**74:15** Yours is the day, yours also the night; \*  
you established the moon and the sun. ^74-15

**74:16** You fixed all the boundaries of the earth; \*  
you made both summer and winter. ^74-16

**74:17** Remember, O LORD, how the enemy scoffed, \*  
how a foolish people despised your Name. ^74-17

**74:18** Do not hand over the life of your dove to wild beasts; \*  
never forget the lives of your poor. ^74-18

**74:19** Look upon your covenant; \*  
the dark places of the earth are haunts of violence. ^74-19

**74:20** Let not the oppressed turn away ashamed; \*  
let the poor and needy praise your Name. ^74-20

**74:21** Arise, O God, maintain your cause; \*  
remember how fools revile you all day long. ^74-21

[p. 691] ^pg691

**74:22** Forget not the clamor of your adversaries, \*  
the unending tumult of those who rise up against you. ^74-22
