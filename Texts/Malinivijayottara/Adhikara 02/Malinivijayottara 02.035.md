## Mūla

इच्छानिवृत्तेः स्वस्थत्वादभिन्नमपि पञ्चधा ।
इति पञ्चात्मके भेदे विज्ञेयं वस्तु कीर्तितम् ॥ ^mula

icchānivṛtteḥ svasthatvād abhinnam api[^49] pañcadhā |
iti paṣcātmake[^50] bhede vijñeyam vastu kīrtitam || ^iast

*Muktabodha's e-text of the KSTS edition (M00160) has:* icchānivṛtteḥ svasthatvādabhinnamapi pañcadhā | iti pañcātmake bhede vijñeyaṃ vastu kīrtitam ||

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, October 2022 – December 2022
> p. 21 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=28>))
>
> Although (Śiva is) undivided, even so (his nature is) fivefold because of (1) (His (purposeful) activity and (2) sovereignty (savyāpārādhipatvena) and because (3) he impels consciousness (independently of and) in the absence of (both) these (two powers of knowledge and action in pure volition), and (also) because of (4) the cessation of volition (in his power of bliss), and because (5) (as consciousness itself) he is established within himself.[^51] Thus is explained what needs to be understood concerning the five-fold division.[^52] (with 2.34cd–35)
