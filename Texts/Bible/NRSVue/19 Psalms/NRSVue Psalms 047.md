## God’s rule over the nations

*To the leader. Of the Korahites. A Psalm.*

**47:1** Clap your hands, all you peoples; shout to God with loud songs of joy. ^47-1

**47:2** For the LORD, the Most High, is awesome, a great king over all the earth. ^47-2

**47:3** He subdued peoples under us and nations under our feet. ^47-3

**47:4** He chose our heritage for us, the pride of Jacob whom he loves. *Selah* ^47-4

**47:5** God has gone up with a shout, the LORD with the sound of a trumpet. ^47-5

**47:6** Sing praises to God, sing praises; sing praises to our King, sing praises. ^47-6

**47:7** For God is the king of all the earth; sing praises with a psalm.^[Heb *Maskil*] ^47-7

**47:8** God is king over the nations; God sits on his holy throne. ^47-8

**47:9** The princes of the peoples gather as the people of the God of Abraham. For the shields of the earth belong to God; he is highly exalted. ^47-9
