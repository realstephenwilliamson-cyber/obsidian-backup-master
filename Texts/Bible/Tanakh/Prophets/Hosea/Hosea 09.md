## Chapter 9

**9:1** Rejoice not, O Israel,  
As other peoples exult;  
For you have strayed  
Away from your God:  
[^1]You have loved a harlot’s fee  
By every threshing floor of new grain. ^9-1

**9:2** Threshing floor and winepress  
Shall not join them,  
And the new wine shall betray her. ^9-2

**9:3** They shall not be able to remain  
In the land of the LORD.  
But Ephraim shall return to Egypt  
And shall eat unclean food in Assyria.[^2] ^9-3

**9:4** It shall be for them like the food of mourners,  
All who partake of which are defiled.  
They will offer no libations of wine to the LORD,  
And no sacrifices of theirs will be pleasing to Him;  
But their food will be only for their hunger,  
It shall not come into the House of the LORD. ^9-4

**9:5** What will you do about feast days,  
About the festivals of the LORD? ^9-5

**9:6** Behold, they have gone from destruction[^3]  
[With] the silver they treasure.  
Egypt shall hold them fast,[^4]  
Moph[^5] shall receive them in burial.  
Weeds are their heirs;  
Prickly shrubs occupy their [old] homes. ^9-6

**9:7** The days of punishment have come  
For your heavy guilt;  
The days of requital have come—  
Let Israel know it!  
The prophet was distraught,  
The inspired man driven mad  
By constant harassment. ^9-7

**9:8** Ephraim watches for my God.  
As for the prophet,[^6]  
Fowlers’ snares are on all his paths,  
Harassment in the House of his God. ^9-8

**9:9** They have been as grievously corrupt  
As in the days of Gibeah;[^7]  
He will remember their iniquity,  
He will punish their sins. ^9-9

**9:10** I found Israel [as pleasing]  
As grapes in the wilderness;  
Your fathers seemed to Me  
Like the first fig to ripen on a fig tree.[^8]  
But when they came to Baal-peor,  
They turned aside to shamefulness;[^9]  
Then they became as detested  
As they had been loved.[^10] ^9-10

**9:11** From birth, from the womb, from conception  
Ephraim’s glory shall be  
Like birds that fly away.[^11] ^9-11

**9:12** Even if they rear their infants,  
I will bereave them of men.  
Woe to them indeed  
When I turn away from them![^12] ^9-12

**9:13** It shall go with Ephraim  
As I have seen it go with Tyre,  
Which was planted in a meadow;[^13]  
Ephraim too must bring out  
His children to slayers. ^9-13

**9:14** Give them, O LORD—give them what?  
Give them a womb that miscarries,  
And shriveled breasts! ^9-14

**9:15** All their misfortune [began] at Gilgal,  
For there I disowned them.[^14]  
For their evil deeds  
I will drive them out of My House.  
I will accept them no more;  
All their officials are[^15] disloyal. ^9-15

**9:16** [^16]Ephraim[^17] is stricken,  
Their stock is withered;  
They can produce no fruit.  
Even if they do bear children,  
I will slay their cherished offspring. ^9-16

**9:17** My God rejects them  
Because they have not obeyed Him,  
And they shall go wandering  
Among the nations. ^9-17

[^1]: Emendation and rearrangement yield: “You have loved fornication / By every threshing floor and press; / The new grain shall not join them, / And the new wine shall fail them.”
[^2]: The lands of the heathen and the food there are unclean; cf. Ezek. 4.13; Amos 7.17.
[^3]: Emendation yields “to Assyria.”
[^4]: Cf. 8.10.
[^5]: Believed to be Memphis, elsewhere called Noph.
[^6]: Emendation yields “the prophet of my God.”
[^7]: See Judg. 19–20.
[^8]: Emendation yields “like a ripe fig in a waterless waste”; cf. 13.5.
[^9]: Cf. Num. 25.1–3.
[^10]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^11]: V. 16 would read well after v. 11.
[^12]: Emendation yields: “Even if they wean their babes, / They shall be dismayed because of them.”
[^13]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^14]: The specific allusion is uncertain.
[^15]: Emendation yields “They are all.”
[^16]: V. 16 would read well after v. 11.
[^17]: Targum reads “Their crown,” i.e., of a tree.
