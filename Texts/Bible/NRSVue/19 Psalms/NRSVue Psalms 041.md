# Psalm 41 (NRSVue)

## Assurance of God’s help and a plea for healing

*To the leader. A Psalm of David.*

**41:1** Happy are those who consider the poor;^[Or *weak*] the LORD delivers them in the day of trouble. ^41-1

**41:2** The LORD protects them and keeps them alive; they are called happy in the land. You do not give them up to the will of their enemies. ^41-2

**41:3** The LORD sustains them on their sickbed; in their illness you heal all their infirmities.^[Heb *you change all his bed*] ^41-3

**41:4** As for me, I said, “O LORD, be gracious to me; heal me, for I have sinned against you.” ^41-4

**41:5** My enemies wonder in malice when I will die and my name perish. ^41-5

**41:6** And when they come to see me, they utter empty words while their hearts gather mischief; when they go out, they tell it abroad. ^41-6

**41:7** All who hate me whisper together about me; they imagine the worst for me. ^41-7

**41:8** They think that a deadly thing has fastened on me, that I will not rise again from where I lie. ^41-8

**41:9** Even my close friend in whom I trusted, who ate of my bread, has lifted the heel against me. ^41-9

**41:10** But you, O LORD, be gracious to me, and raise me up, that I may repay them. ^41-10

**41:11** By this I know that you are pleased with me: because my enemy has not triumphed over me. ^41-11

**41:12** But you have upheld me because of my integrity and set me in your presence forever. ^41-12

**41:13** Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, from everlasting to everlasting. Amen and Amen. *(Psalms 42–72)* ^41-13
