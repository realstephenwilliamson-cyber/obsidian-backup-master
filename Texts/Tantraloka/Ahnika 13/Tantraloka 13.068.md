## Mūla

भोगपर्यायमाहात्म्यात्काले क्वापि फलं प्रति ।
विरोधात्कर्मणी रुद्धे तिष्ठतः साम्यमीदृशम् ॥ ^mula

bhogaparyāyamāhātmyātkāle kvāpi phalaṃ prati |
virodhātkarmaṇī ruddhe tiṣṭhataḥ sāmyamīdṛśam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 06 - Chapter Thirteen, part 2#^pg186|p. 166]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=186>))
>
> Now, (if you say that) his will (to act in this way) operates depending on the balancing out of (opposite) Karmas (that cancel each other out) (karmasāmya), then (you) should explain what that (state of balance) is also. What (do you mean by the) balancing out of Karmas? (You may reply that,) the worldly experience (which is the consequence of Karma) (bhoga) arises in succession, and so the fruit (of Karma) arises at (some) time and place. (So, it may happen that) two (opposite) Karmas (one good and the other bad) block (the effects of one another) and so are immobilized. The balance (of Karmas) is like this. (with 13.67–68)

## Viveka

> [!viveka]- Viveka in English (Dyczkowski, vol. VII, pp. 167–168)
> ![[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 06 - Chapter Thirteen, part 2#Viveka on 13.67–68]]

*Lead-in:* तदेवाह -

> भोगपर्यायमाहात्म्यात्काले क्वापि फलं प्रति ।
> विरोधात्कर्मणी रुद्धे तिष्ठतः साम्यमीदृशम् ॥

इह भोगक्रममहिम्ना बहुषु कर्मसु क्षीणेषु क्वाप्यनियते काले यत्परिपक्वेऽपि तुल्यफलविरुद्धे कर्मणी विरोधात्फलं प्रति रुद्धे तिष्ठतः प्रातिस्विकं प्रतिनियतं भोगं दातुमुदासाते, तदनन्तरभावीनि कर्माणि चापरिपक्वत्वाद् अर्थाद्भोगाय नोन्मुखीभवन्ति, तदीदृशं विरुद्धकर्मद्वयविरोधलक्षणं कर्मणां साम्यमुच्यते -- इति वाक्यार्थः ॥ ६८ ॥
