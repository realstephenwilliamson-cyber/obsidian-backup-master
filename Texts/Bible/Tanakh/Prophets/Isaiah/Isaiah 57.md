## Chapter 57

**57:1** The righteous man perishes,  
And no one considers;  
Pious men are taken away,  
And no one gives thought  
That because of evil  
The righteous was taken away. ^57-1

**57:2** Yet he shall come to peace,  
He shall have rest on his couch[^1]  
Who walked straightforward. ^57-2

**57:3** But as for you, come closer,  
You sons of a sorceress,  
You offspring of an adulterer and a harlot![^2] ^57-3

**57:4** With whom do you act so familiarly?  
At whom do you open your mouth  
And stick out your tongue?  
Why, you are children of iniquity,  
Offspring of treachery— ^57-4

**57:5** You who inflame[^3] yourselves  
Among the terebinths,  
Under every verdant tree;  
Who slaughter children in the wadis,  
Among[^4] the clefts of the rocks. ^57-5

**57:6** With such[^5] are your share and portion,[^6]  
They, they are your allotment;  
To them you have poured out libations,  
Presented offerings.  
Should I relent in the face of this? ^57-6

**57:7** On a high and lofty hill  
You have set your couch;  
There, too, you have gone up  
To perform sacrifices. ^57-7

**57:8** Behind the door and doorpost  
You have directed your thoughts;  
[^7]Abandoning Me, you have gone up  
On the couch you made so wide.  
You have made a covenant with them,[^8]  
You have loved bedding with them;[^9]  
You have chosen lust.[^10] ^57-8

**57:9** You have approached the king[^11] with oil,  
You have provided many perfumes.  
And you have sent your envoys afar,  
Even down to the netherworld.[^12] ^57-9

**57:10** Though wearied by much travel,  
You never said, “I give up!”  
You found gratification for your lust,  
And so you never cared. ^57-10

**57:11** Whom do you dread and fear,  
That you tell lies?[^13]  
But you gave no thought to Me,  
You paid no heed.  
It is because I have stood idly by so long[^14]  
That you have no fear of Me. ^57-11

**57:12** I hereby pronounce judgment upon your deeds:[^15]  
Your assorted [idols][^16] shall not avail you, ^57-12

**57:13** Shall not save you when you cry out.  
They shall all be borne off by the wind,  
Snatched away by a breeze.  
But those who trust in Me shall inherit the land  
And possess My sacred mount. ^57-13

**57:14** [The LORD] says:  
Build up, build up a highway!  
Clear a road!  
Remove all obstacles  
From the road of My people! ^57-14

**57:15** For thus said He who high aloft  
Forever dwells, whose name is holy:  
I dwell on high, in holiness;  
Yet with the contrite and the lowly in spirit—  
Reviving the spirits of the lowly,  
Reviving the hearts of the contrite. ^57-15

**57:16** For I will not always contend,  
I will not be angry forever:  
Nay, I who make spirits flag,[^17]  
Also create the breath of life. ^57-16

**57:17** For their[^18] sinful greed I was angry;  
I struck them and turned away in My wrath.  
Though stubborn, they follow the way of their hearts,[^19] ^57-17

**57:18** I note how they fare and will heal them:  
I will guide them and mete out solace to them,  
And to the mourners among them ^57-18

**57:19** heartening,[^20] comforting[^21] words:  
It shall be well,  
Well with the far and the near  
—said the LORD—  
And I will heal them. ^57-19

**57:20** But the wicked are like the troubled sea  
Which cannot rest,  
Whose waters toss up mire and mud. ^57-20

**57:21** There is no safety  
—said my God—  
For the wicked. ^57-21

[^1]: Heb. “They shall have rest on their couches.”
[^2]: Lit. “she acts the harlot.”
[^3]: I.e., in some frenzied idolatrous rite.
[^4]: Heb. “under.”
[^5]: The cult-trees referred to above in v. 5.
[^6]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^7]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^8]: The cult-trees referred to above in v. 5.
[^9]: I.e., with the objects behind door and doorpost.
[^10]: Like Ugaritic yd, from root ydd, “to love.”
[^11]: Or “Molech.”
[^12]: I.e., you have brought tribute to alien cults as to a king.
[^13]: Emendation yields “Them you dreaded and feared, / And so you gave them thought.”
[^14]: Emendation yields “and shut My eyes.”
[^15]: Lit. “your retribution and your deeds.”
[^16]: Brought up from v. 13 for clarity.
[^17]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^18]: I.e., Israel’s. Cf. “My people,” v. 14.
[^19]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain. Emendation yields “When they have walked broken in the contrition of their hearts.”
[^20]: Lit. “the vigor of”; cf. Eccl. 12.1 and postbiblical bori.
[^21]: The Heb. nib is otherwise unknown; its meaning is inferred from that of nid (cf. the verb nad “to condole”) in the parallel expres-sion in Job 16.5.
