# Exodus 40 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Exodus 39|← Exodus 39]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · —

**40:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying: ^40-1

**40:2** The first month, the first day of the month, thou shalt set up the tabernacle of the testimony, ^40-2

**40:3** And shalt put the ark in it, and shalt let down the veil before it: ^40-3

**40:4** And thou shalt bring in the table, and set upon it the things that are commanded according to the rite. The candlestick shall stand with its lamps, ^40-4

**40:5** And the altar of gold whereon the incense is burnt, before the ark of the testimony. Thou shalt put the hanging in the entry of the tabernacle, ^40-5

**40:6** And before it the altar of holocaust: ^40-6

**40:7** The laver between the altar and the tabernacle, and thou shalt fill it with water. ^40-7

**40:8** And thou shalt encompass the court with hangings, and the entry thereof. ^40-8

**40:9** And thou shalt take the oil of unction and anoint the tabernacle with its vessels, that they may be sanctified: ^40-9

**40:10** The altar of holocaust and all its vessels: ^40-10

**40:11** The laver with its foot: thou shalt consecrate all with the oil of unction, that they may be most holy. ^40-11

**40:12** And thou shalt bring Aaron and his sons to the door of the tabernacle of the testimony, and having washed them with water, ^40-12

**40:13** Thou shalt put on them the holy vestments, that they may minister to me, and that the unction of them may prosper to an everlasting priesthood. ^40-13

**40:14** And Moses did all that the Lord had commanded. ^40-14

**40:15** So in the first month of the second year, the first day of the month, the tabernacle was set up. ^40-15

**40:16** And Moses reared it up, and placed the boards and the sockets and the bars, and set up the pillars, ^40-16

**40:17** And spread the roof over the tabernacle, putting over it a cover, as the Lord had commanded. ^40-17

**40:18** And he put the testimony in the ark, thrusting bars underneath, and the oracle above. ^40-18

**40:19** And when he had brought the ark into the tabernacle, he drew the veil before it to fulfill the commandment of the Lord. ^40-19

**40:20** And he set the table in the tabernacle of the testimony at the north side without the veil, ^40-20

**40:21** Setting there in order the loaves of proposition, as the Lord had commanded Moses. ^40-21

**40:22** He set the candlestick also in the tabernacle of the testimony over against the table on the south side, ^40-22

**40:23** Placing the lamps in order, according to the precept of the Lord. ^40-23

**40:24** He set also the altar of gold under the roof of the testimony over against the veil, ^40-24

**40:25** And burnt upon it the incense of spices, as the Lord had commanded Moses. ^40-25

**40:26** And he put also the hanging in the entry of the tabernacle of the testimony, ^40-26

**40:27** And the altar of holocaust of the entry of the testimony, offering the holocaust, and the sacrifices upon it, as the Lord had commanded. ^40-27

**40:28** And he set the laver between the tabernacle of the testimony and the altar, filling it with water. ^40-28

**40:29** And Moses and Aaron, and his sons washed their hands and feet, ^40-29

**40:30** When they went into the tabernacle of the covenant, and went to the altar, as the Lord had commanded Moses. ^40-30

**40:31** He set up also the court round about the tabernacle and the altar, drawing the hanging in the entry thereof. After all things were perfected, ^40-31

**40:32** The cloud covered the tabernacle of the testimony, and the glory of the Lord filled it. ^40-32

**40:33** Neither could Moses go into the tabernacle of the covenant, the cloud covering all things and the majesty of the Lord shining, for the cloud had covered all. ^40-33

**40:34** If at any time the cloud removed from the tabernacle, the children of Israel went forward by their troops: ^40-34

**40:35** If it hung over, they remained in the same place. ^40-35

**40:36** For the cloud of the Lord hung over the tabernacle by day, and a fire by night, in the sight of all the children of Israel throughout all their mansions. ^40-36
