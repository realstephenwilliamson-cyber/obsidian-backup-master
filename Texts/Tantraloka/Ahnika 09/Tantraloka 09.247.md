## Mūla

ननु श्रीमन्मतङ्गादौ कलायाः कर्तृतोदिता ।
तस्यां सत्यां हि विद्याद्याः करणत्वार्हताजुषः ॥ ^mula

nanu śrīmanmataṅgādau kalāyāḥ kartṛtoditā |
tasyāṃ satyāṃ hi vidyādyāḥ karaṇatvārhatājuṣaḥ || ^iast

> But surely (someone may object at this point) that according to the venerable Mataṁgatantra etc., (limited) agency arises from the Force (of limited agency), and it is only when that exists that (impure) Knowledge and the rest become fit to be instruments of action.
