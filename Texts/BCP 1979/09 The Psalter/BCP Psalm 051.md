# Psalm 51

← [[BCP Psalm 050]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 052]] →

*Miserere mei, Deus* · Book Two · Tenth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 051]]

**51:1** Have mercy on me, O God, according to your loving-kindness; \*  
in your great compassion blot out my offenses. ^51-1

**51:2** Wash me through and through from my wickedness \*  
and cleanse me from my sin. ^51-2

**51:3** For I know my transgressions, \*  
and my sin is ever before me. ^51-3

**51:4** Against you only have I sinned \*  
and done what is evil in your sight. ^51-4

**51:5** And so you are justified when you speak \*  
and upright in your judgment. ^51-5

**51:6** Indeed, I have been wicked from my birth, \*  
a sinner from my mother's womb. ^51-6

**51:7** For behold, you look for truth deep within me, \*  
and will make me understand wisdom secretly. ^51-7

**51:8** Purge me from my sin, and I shall be pure; \*  
wash me, and I shall be clean indeed. ^51-8

**51:9** Make me hear of joy and gladness, \*  
that the body you have broken may rejoice. ^51-9

**51:10** Hide your face from my sins \*  
and blot out all my iniquities. ^51-10

[p. 657] ^pg657

**51:11** Create in me a clean heart, O God, \*  
and renew a right spirit within me. ^51-11

**51:12** Cast me not away from your presence \*  
and take not your holy Spirit from me. ^51-12

**51:13** Give me the joy of your saving help again \*  
and sustain me with your bountiful Spirit. ^51-13

**51:14** I shall teach your ways to the wicked, \*  
and sinners shall return to you. ^51-14

**51:15** Deliver me from death, O God, \*  
and my tongue shall sing of your righteousness,  
O God of my salvation. ^51-15

**51:16** Open my lips, O Lord, \*  
and my mouth shall proclaim your praise. ^51-16

**51:17** Had you desired it, I would have offered sacrifice, \*  
but you take no delight in burnt-offerings. ^51-17

**51:18** The sacrifice of God is a troubled spirit; \*  
a broken and contrite heart, O God, you will not despise. ^51-18

**51:19** Be favorable and gracious to Zion, \*  
and rebuild the walls of Jerusalem. ^51-19

**51:20** Then you will be pleased with the appointed sacrifices,  
with burnt-offerings and oblations; \*  
then shall they offer young bullocks upon your altar. ^51-20
