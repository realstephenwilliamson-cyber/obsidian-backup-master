# Isaiah 42 (Douay-Rheims)

*Isaias 42 in the Douay-Rheims*

[[DRB Isaiah 41|← Isaiah 41]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Isaiah 43|Isaiah 43 →]]

**42:1** Behold my servant, I will uphold him: my elect, my soul delighteth in him: I have given my spirit upon him, he shall bring forth judgment to the Gentiles. ^42-1

**42:2** He shall not cry, nor have respect to person, neither shall his voice be heard abroad. ^42-2

**42:3** The bruised reed he shall not break, and smoking flax he shall not quench: he shall bring forth judgment unto truth. ^42-3

**42:4** He shall not be sad, nor troublesome, till he set judgment in the earth: and the islands shall wait for his law. ^42-4

**42:5** Thus saith the Lord God that created the heavens, and stretched them out: that established the earth, and the things that spring out of it: that giveth breath to the people upon it, and spirit to them that tread thereon. ^42-5

**42:6** I the Lord have called thee in justice, and taken thee by the hand, and preserved thee. And I have given thee for a covenant of the people, for a light of the Gentiles: ^42-6

**42:7** That thou mightest open the eyes of the blind, and bring forth the prisoner out of prison, and them that sit in darkness out of the prison house. ^42-7

**42:8** I the Lord, this is my name: I will not give my glory to another, nor my praise to graven things. ^42-8

**42:9** The things that were first, behold they are come: and new things do I declare: before they spring forth, I will make you head them. ^42-9

**42:10** Sing ye to the Lora a new song, his praise is from the ends of the earth: you that go down to the sea, and all that are therein: ye islands, and ye inhabitants of them. ^42-10

**42:11** Let the desert and the cities thereof be exalted: Cedar shall dwell in houses: ye inhabitants of Petra, give praise, they shall cry from the top of the mountains. ^42-11

**42:12** They shall give glory to the Lord, and shall declare his praise in the islands. ^42-12

**42:13** The Lord shall go forth as a mighty man, as a man of war shall he stir up zeal: he shall shout and cry: he shall prevail against his enemies. ^42-13

**42:14** I have always held my peace, I have I kept silence, I have been patient, I will speak now as a woman in labour: I will destroy, and swallow up at once. ^42-14

**42:15** I will lay waste the mountains and hills, and will make all their grass to wither: and I will turn rivers into islands, and will dry up the standing pools. ^42-15

**42:16** And I will lead the blind into the way which they know not: and in the paths which they were ignorant of I will make them walk: I will make darkness light before them, and crooked things straight: these things have I done to them, and have not forsaken them. ^42-16

**42:17** They are turned back: let them be greatly confounded, that trust in a graven thing, that say to a molten thing: You are our god. ^42-17

**42:18** Hear, ye deaf, and, ye blind, behold that you may see. ^42-18

**42:19** Who is blind, but my servant? or deaf, but he to whom I have sent my messengers? Who is blind, but he that is sold? or who is blind, but the servant of the Lord? ^42-19

**42:20** Thou that seest many things, wilt thou not observe them? thou that hast ears open, wilt thou not hear? ^42-20

**42:21** And the Lord was willing to sanctify him, and to magnify the law, and exalt it. ^42-21

**42:22** But this is a people that is robbed and wasted: they are all the snare of young men, and they are hid in the houses of prisons: they are made a prey, and there is none to deliver them: a spoil, and there is none that saith: Restore. ^42-22

**42:23** Who is there among you that will give ear to this, that will attend and hearken for times to come? ^42-23

**42:24** Who hath given Jacob for a spoil, and Israel to robbers? hath not the Lord himself, against whom we have sinned? And they would not walk in his ways, and they have not hearkened to his law. ^42-24

**42:25** And he hath poured out upon him the indignation of his fury, and a strong battle, and hath burnt him round about, and he knew not: and set him on fire, and he understood not. ^42-25
