## Mūla

शतमष्टोत्तरं तत्र रौद्रं शाक्तमथोत्तरम् ।
यामलस्थितियोगे तु रुद्रशक्त्यविभागिता ॥ ^mula

śatamaṣṭottaraṃ tatra raudraṃ śāktamathottaram |
yāmalasthitiyoge tu rudraśaktyavibhāgitā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 13 - Chapter Six, part 6#^pg462|p. 447]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=462>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 6.247cd–248ab.
>
> (The first half of each of these periods of time, which consists) of one hundred and eight breaths, belongs to Rudra, and the second to (His) power. In (this) union (yoga), which is the abiding state of the couple (yāmalasthiti), Rudra’s power is undivided (from him).
