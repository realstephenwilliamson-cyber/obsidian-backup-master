# LXX Psalms 139

← [[LXX Psalms 138]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 140]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 140]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 140]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**139:1** For the end, a Psalm of David.  ^139-1

**139:2** Rescue me, O Lord, from the evil man; deliver me from the unjust man.  ^139-2

**139:3** Who have devised injustice in their hearts; all the day they prepared war.  ^139-3

**139:4** They have sharpened their tongue as *the tongue* of a serpent; the poison of asps is under their lips. Pause.  ^139-4

**139:5** Keep me, O Lord, from the hand of the sinner; rescue me from unjust men; who have purposed to overthrow my goings.  ^139-5

**139:6** The proud have hid a snare for me, and have stretched out ropes *for* snares for my feet; they set a stumbling-block for me near the path. Pause.  ^139-6

**139:7** I said to the Lord, Thou art my God; hearken, O Lord, to the voice of my supplication.  ^139-7

**139:8** O Lord God, the strength of my salvation; thou hast screened my head in the day of battle.  ^139-8

**139:9** Deliver me not, O Lord, to the sinner, according to my desire: they have devised *mischief* against me; forsake me not, lest they should be exalted. Pause.  ^139-9

**139:10** *As for* the head of them that compass me, the mischief of their lips shall cover them.  ^139-10

**139:11** Coals of fire shall fall upon them on the earth; and thou shalt cast them down in afflictions: they shall not bear up *under them*.  ^139-11

**139:12** A talkative man shall not prosper on the earth: evils shall hunt the unrighteous man to destruction.  ^139-12

**139:13** I know that the Lord will maintain the cause of the poor, and the right of the needy ones.[^1]  ^139-13

**139:14** Surely the righteous shall give thanks to thy name: the upright shall dwell in thy presence.  ^139-14

[^1]: 139:13 *Gr.* singular.
