## Mūla

मध्ये देव्यभिधा पूज्या त्रयं भवति पूजितम् ।
ततो मध्यगतात्तस्माद् बोधराशेः सदैवतात् ॥ ^mula

madhye devyabhidhā pūjyā trayaṃ bhavati pūjitam |
tato madhyagatāttasmād bodharāśeḥ sadaivatāt || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 06 - Chapter Fifteen, part 5#^pg217|p. 199]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=217>))
>
> In reality, these goddesses are consciousness, and so (do not have any particular form; rather, they) are all (and every) form (of all that exists). (Thus,) if they are to bestow liberation, they should essentially be (pure) unlimited consciousness alone. Then one should worship all the limbs and faces first, and at the end, the guardians of the directions and (their) weapons, together with the goddess (who is) in the middle, separately. (By worshipping the goddess in the centre,) all three (goddesses) are worshipped. (with 15.328cd–330ab)
>
> Thus, the limbs (of the deity) and the rest has come forth from that mass of consciousness endowed with the divinity (of the Goddess) present in the centre; sparks (of its light) should be worshipped separately. (with 15.330cd–331ab)
