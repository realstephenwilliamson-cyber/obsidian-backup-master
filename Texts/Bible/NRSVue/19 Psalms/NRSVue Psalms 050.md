## The acceptable sacrifice

*A Psalm of Asaph.*

**50:1** The mighty one, God the LORD, speaks and summons the earth from the rising of the sun to its setting. ^50-1

**50:2** Out of Zion, the perfection of beauty, God shines forth. ^50-2

**50:3** Our God comes and does not keep silent; before him is a devouring fire and a mighty tempest all around him. ^50-3

**50:4** He calls to the heavens above and to the earth, that he may judge his people: ^50-4

**50:5** “Gather to me my faithful ones, who made a covenant with me by sacrifice!” ^50-5

**50:6** The heavens declare his righteousness, for God himself is judge. *Selah* ^50-6

**50:7** “Hear, O my people, and I will speak, O Israel, I will testify against you. I am God, your God. ^50-7

**50:8** Not for your sacrifices do I rebuke you; your burnt offerings are continually before me. ^50-8

**50:9** I will not accept a bull from your house or goats from your folds. ^50-9

**50:10** For every wild animal of the forest is mine, the cattle on a thousand hills. ^50-10

**50:11** I know all the birds of the air,^[Gk Syr Tg: Heb *mountains*] and all that moves in the field is mine. ^50-11

**50:12** “If I were hungry, I would not tell you, for the world and all that is in it is mine. ^50-12

**50:13** Do I eat the flesh of bulls or drink the blood of goats? ^50-13

**50:14** Offer to God a sacrifice of thanksgiving,^[Or *make thanksgiving your sacrifice to God*] and pay your vows to the Most High. ^50-14

**50:15** Call on me in the day of trouble; I will deliver you, and you shall glorify me.” ^50-15

**50:16** But to the wicked God says, “What right have you to recite my statutes or take my covenant on your lips? ^50-16

**50:17** For you hate discipline, and you cast my words behind you. ^50-17

**50:18** You make friends with a thief when you see one, and you keep company with adulterers. ^50-18

**50:19** You give your mouth free rein for evil, and your tongue frames deceit. ^50-19

**50:20** You sit and speak against your kin; you slander your own mother’s child. ^50-20

**50:21** These things you have done, and I have been silent; you thought that I was one just like yourself. But now I rebuke you and lay the charge before you. ^50-21

**50:22** Mark this, then, you who forget God, or I will tear you apart, and there will be no one to deliver. ^50-22

**50:23** Those who bring thanksgiving as their sacrifice honor me; to those who go the right way,^[Heb *who set a way*] I will show the salvation of God.” ^50-23
