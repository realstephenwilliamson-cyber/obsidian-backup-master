## Mūla

श्रीदेव्यायामले तूक्तमष्टारान्तस्त्रिशूलके ।
चक्रे भैरवसन्नाभावघोराद्यष्टकारके ॥ ^mula

śrīdevyāyāmale tūktamaṣṭārāntastriśūlake |
cakre bhairavasannābhāvaghorādyaṣṭakārake || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 08 - Chapter Fifteen, part 7#^pg302|p. 284]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=302>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 15.462cd–463ab.
>
> (In order to impart the initiation that bestows immediate release, the teacher,) having (first) worshipped externally that (hand) which severs the vital breaths (from the body), should place it on the head (of the disciple) who wishes to ascend upwards immediately, (his) fetters paralysed and more. By this, (the rite of) Śiva’s Hand, the disciple clearly becomes a regular initiate.’ (with 15.460–463)
