# Psalms 45 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 44|← Psalms 44]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 46|Psalms 46 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 45|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**45:1** Unto the end, for the sons of Core, for the hidden. ^45-1

**45:2** Our God is our refuge and strength: a helper in troubles, which have found us exceedingly. ^45-2

**45:3** Therefore we will not fear, when the earth shall be troubled; and the mountains shall be removed into the heart of the sea. ^45-3

**45:4** Their waters roared and were troubled: the mountains were troubled with his strength. ^45-4

**45:5** The stream of the river maketh the city of God joyful: the most High hath sanctified his own tabernacle. ^45-5

**45:6** God is in the midst thereof, it shall not be moved: God will help it in the morning early. ^45-6

**45:7** Nations were troubled, and kingdoms were bowed down: he uttered his voice, the earth trembled. ^45-7

**45:8** The Lord of armies is with us: the God of Jacob is our protector. ^45-8

**45:9** Come and behold ye the works of the Lord: what wonders he hath done upon earth, ^45-9

**45:10** Making wars to cease even to the end of the earth. He shall destroy the bow, and break the weapons: and the shield he shall burn in the fire. ^45-10

**45:11** Be still and see that I am God; I will be exalted among the nations, and I will be exalted in the earth. ^45-11

**45:12** The Lord of armies is with us: the God of Jacob is our protector. ^45-12
