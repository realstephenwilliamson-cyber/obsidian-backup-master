## Mūla

श्रीपूर्वशास्त्रे कथितां वच्मः कारणकल्पनाम् ।
शिवः स्वतन्त्रदृग्रूपः पञ्चशक्तिसुनिर्भरः ॥ ^mula

śrīpūrvaśāstre kathitāṃ vacmaḥ kāraṇakalpanām |
śivaḥ svatantradṛgrūpaḥ pañcaśaktisunirbharaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 04 - Chapter Nine, part 2#^pg73|p. 56]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=73>))
>
> Now that the true nature of the relationship between cause and effect has thus been established, we will explain the (progressive) formation (kalpanā) of the causes (in the form of the series of metaphysical principles), as described in the Śrīpūrvaśāstra (i.e. the Mālinīvijayottara). (with 9.48cd–49ab)
>
> Śiva, whose nature is free discerning consciousness (svatantradrg) and is full (to overflowing) with five powers, manifesting (the domain) of duality by (the power of) His freedom, is divided into five. (with 9.49cd–50ab)
