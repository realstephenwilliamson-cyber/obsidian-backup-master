## Mūla

तेनोक्तं मालिनीतन्त्रे विचार्य ज्ञानयोगिते ।
यतश्च मोक्षदः प्रोक्तः स्वभ्यस्तज्ञानवान्बुधैः ॥ ^mula

tenoktaṃ mālinītantre vicārya jñānayogite |
yataśca mokṣadaḥ proktaḥ svabhyastajñānavānbudhaiḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 10 - Chapter Thirteen, part 6#^pg353|p. 331]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=353>))
>
> Thus, after having examined (the nature of) one who possesses knowledge (jñānayogita), (the Lord) says in the Mālinītantra:.. and because, the wise say, (only) one who possesses well exercised knowledge bestows liberation.
