## Mūla

उपादेयं च हेयं च तदेतत्परिकीर्तितम् ।
ज्ञात्वैतज्ज्ञेयसर्वस्वं सर्वसिद्ध्यरहो भवेत् ॥ ^mula

upādeyaṁ ca heyaṁ ca tadetat parikīrtitam |
jñātvaitajjñeyasarvasvaṁ sarvasiddhyaraho bhavet[^90] || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2022 – June 2022
> p. 49 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=25>))
>
> This is said to be what is to be adopted and what is to be abandoned. Once known this, which is the essence of all that is to be known, one is worthy (of attaining) all accomplishments (*siddhi*).

[^90]: k, kh: *siddhiphalaṁ labhet*
