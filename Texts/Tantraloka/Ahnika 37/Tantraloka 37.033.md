## Mūla

षडर्धसारं सच्छास्त्रमुपादेयमिदं स्फुटम् ।
षट्त्रंशता तत्त्वबलेन सूता
यद्यप्यनन्ता भुवनावलीयम् ।
ब्रह्माण्डमत्यन्तमनोहरं तु
वैचित्र्यवर्जं नहि रम्यभावः ॥ ^mula

ṣaḍardhasāraṃ sacchāstramupādeyamidaṃ sphuṭam |
ṣaṭtraṃśatā tattvabalena sūtā
yadyapyanantā bhuvanāvalīyam |
brahmāṇḍamatyantamanoharaṃ tu
vaicitryavarjaṃ nahi ramyabhāvaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 11 - Chapter Thirty-Seven, part 1#^pg257|p. 239]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=257>))
>
> Bestowing in this way without effort the great fruit which is liberation in this life, and arranged in such a way so as to bestow the great enjoyment (of all worldly benefits) as one desires, clearly, it is this, the essence of Trika (ṣadardha), that is the true scripture (śāstra) that one should adopt (upādeya). (with 37.32–33ab)
>
> Although all this endless series of worlds has been generated by the might of the thirty-six principles, it is the Egg of Brahmā that is the most delightful. (Indeed,) without variety there is no beauty.

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* ग्रन्थकृतां साङ्गोपाङ्गः परिचयः इदानीमेतद्ग्रन्थाभिधाने स्वात्मनि योग्यतां प्रकाशयितुं सातिशयत्वप्रयोजकीकारेण देशवंशदैशिकादिक्रममुट्टङ्क्य स्वेतिवृत्तमभिधत्ते--

> षट्त्रंशता तत्त्वबलेन सूता
> यद्यप्यनन्ता भुवनावलीयम् ।
> ब्रह्माण्डमत्यन्तमनोहरं तु
> वैचित्र्यवर्जं नहि रम्यभावः ॥
> भूरादिसप्तपुरपूर्णतमेऽपि तस्मिन्
> मन्ये द्वितीयभुवनं भवनं सुखस्य ।
