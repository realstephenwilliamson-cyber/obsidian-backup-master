# LXX Song of Songs 07

← [[LXX Song of Songs 06]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Song of Songs 08]] →

*Song of Songs · [[LXX]]*

**7:1** Return, return, O Sunamite; return, return, and we will look at thee. What will ye see in the Sunamite? She comes as bands of armies.  ^7-1

**7:2** Thy steps are beautiful in shoes, O daughter of the prince: the joints of *thy* thighs are like chains, the work of the craftsman.  ^7-2

**7:3** Thy navel is *as* a turned bowl, not wanting liquor; thy belly is *as* a heap of wheat set about with lilies.  ^7-3

**7:4** Thy two breasts are as two twin fawns.  ^7-4

**7:5** Thy neck is as an ivory tower; thine eyes are as pools in Esebon, by the gates of the daughter of many: thy nose is as the tower of Libanus, looking toward Damascus.  ^7-5

**7:6** Thy head upon thee is as Carmel, and the curls of thy hair like scarlet; the king is bound in the galleries.  ^7-6

**7:7** How beautiful art thou, and how sweet art thou, *my* love!  ^7-7

**7:8** This is thy greatness in thy delights: thou wast made like a palm tree, and thy breasts to clusters.  ^7-8

**7:9** I said, I will go up to the palm tree, I will take hold of its high boughs: and now shall thy breasts be as clusters of the vine, and the smell of thy nose as apples;  ^7-9

**7:10** and thy throat as good wine, going well with my kinsman, suiting my lips and teeth.  ^7-10

**7:11** I am my kinsman's, and his desire is toward me.  ^7-11

**7:12** Come, my kinsman, let us go forth into the field; let us lodge in the villages.  ^7-12

**7:13** Let us go early into the vineyards; let us see if the vine has flowered, *if* the blossoms have appeared, if the pomegranates have blossomed; there will I give thee my breasts.  ^7-13

**7:14** The mandrakes have given a smell, and at our doors *are* all kinds of choice fruits, new and old. O my kinsman, I have kept *them* for thee.  ^7-14
