# Psalm 34 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 033|← Psalm 33]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 035|Psalm 35 →]]

## Praise for deliverance from trouble

*Of David, when he feigned madness before Abimelech, so that he drove him out, and he went away.*

**34:1** I will bless the LORD at all times; his praise shall continually be in my mouth. ^34-1

**34:2** My soul makes its boast in the LORD; let the humble hear and be glad. ^34-2

**34:3** O magnify the LORD with me, and let us exalt his name together. ^34-3

**34:4** I sought the LORD, and he answered me and delivered me from all my fears. ^34-4

**34:5** Look to him, and be radiant, so your^[Gk Syr Jerome: Heb *their*] faces shall never be ashamed. ^34-5

**34:6** This poor soul cried and was heard by the LORD and was saved from every trouble. ^34-6

**34:7** The angel of the LORD encamps around those who fear him and delivers them. ^34-7

**34:8** O taste and see that the LORD is good; happy are those who take refuge in him. ^34-8

**34:9** O fear the LORD, you his holy ones, for those who fear him have no want. ^34-9

**34:10** The young lions suffer want and hunger, but those who seek the LORD lack no good thing. ^34-10

**34:11** Come, O children, listen to me; I will teach you the fear of the LORD. ^34-11

**34:12** Which of you desires life and covets many days to enjoy good? ^34-12

**34:13** Keep your tongue from evil and your lips from speaking deceit. ^34-13

**34:14** Depart from evil, and do good; seek peace, and pursue it. ^34-14

**34:15** The eyes of the LORD are on the righteous, and his ears are open to their cry. ^34-15

**34:16** The face of the LORD is against evildoers, to cut off the remembrance of them from the earth. ^34-16

**34:17** When the righteous cry for help, the LORD hears and rescues them from all their troubles. ^34-17

**34:18** The LORD is near to the brokenhearted and saves the crushed in spirit. ^34-18

**34:19** Many are the afflictions of the righteous, but the LORD rescues them from them all. ^34-19

**34:20** He keeps all their bones; not one of them will be broken. ^34-20

**34:21** Evil brings death to the wicked, and those who hate the righteous will be condemned. ^34-21

**34:22** The LORD redeems the life of his servants; none of those who take refuge in him will be condemned. ^34-22
