# Psalm 57 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 056|← Psalm 56]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 058|Psalm 58 →]]

## Praise and assurance under persecution

*To the leader: Do Not Destroy. Of David. A Miktam, when he fled from Saul, in the cave.*

**57:1** Be merciful to me, O God; be merciful to me, for in you my soul takes refuge; in the shadow of your wings I will take refuge, until the destroying storms pass by. ^57-1

**57:2** I cry to God Most High, to God who fulfills his purpose for me. ^57-2

**57:3** He will send from heaven and save me; he will put to shame those who trample on me. *Selah* God will send forth his steadfast love and his faithfulness. ^57-3

**57:4** I lie down among lions that greedily devour^[Cn: Heb *are aflame for*] human prey; their teeth are spears and arrows, their tongues sharp swords. ^57-4

**57:5** Be exalted, O God, above the heavens. Let your glory be over all the earth. ^57-5

**57:6** They set a net for my steps; my soul was bowed down. They dug a pit in my path, but they have fallen into it themselves. *Selah* ^57-6

**57:7** My heart is steadfast, O God; my heart is steadfast. I will sing and make melody. ^57-7

**57:8** Awake, my soul! Awake, O harp and lyre! I will awake the dawn. ^57-8

**57:9** I will give thanks to you, O Lord, among the peoples; I will sing praises to you among the nations. ^57-9

**57:10** For your steadfast love is as high as the heavens; your faithfulness extends to the clouds. ^57-10

**57:11** Be exalted, O God, above the heavens. Let your glory be over all the earth. ^57-11
