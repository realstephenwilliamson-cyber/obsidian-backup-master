## Mūla

यथा यथा परापेक्षातानवं प्रातिभे भवेत् ।
तथा तथा गुरुरसौ श्रेष्ठो विज्ञानपारगः ॥ ^mula

yathā yathā parāpekṣātānavaṃ prātibhe bhavet |
tathā tathā gururasau śreṣṭho vijñānapāragaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 07 - Chapter Thirteen, part 3#^pg231|p. 209]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=231>))
>
> The most excellent teacher is one who knows everything (spontaneously by himself); to the degree in which his intuition is independent of other (outer factors, the better he is).
