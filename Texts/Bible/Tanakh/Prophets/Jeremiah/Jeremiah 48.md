## Chapter 48

**48:1** Concerning Moab.[^1]  
Thus said the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel:  
Alas, that Nebo should be ravaged,  
Kiriathaim captured and shamed,  
The stronghold shamed and dismayed! ^48-1

**48:2** Moab’s glory is no more;  
In Heshbon they have planned[^2] evil against her:  
“Come, let us make an end of her as a nation!”  
You too, O Madmen, shall be silenced;[^3]  
The sword is following you. ^48-2

**48:3** Hark! an outcry from Horonaim,  
Destruction and utter ruin! ^48-3

**48:4** Moab is broken;  
Her young ones cry aloud;[^4] ^48-4

**48:5** They climb to Luhith  
Weeping continually;  
On the descent to Horonaim  
A distressing cry of anguish is heard: ^48-5

**48:6** Flee, save your lives!  
And be like Aroer in the desert.[^5] ^48-6

**48:7** Surely, because of your trust  
In your wealth and in your treasures,  
You too shall be captured.  
And Chemosh shall go forth to exile,  
Together with his priests and attendants. ^48-7

**48:8** The ravager shall come to every town;  
No town shall escape.  
The valley shall be devastated  
And the tableland laid waste  
—because the LORD has spoken. ^48-8

**48:9** Give wings to Moab,  
For she must go hence.[^6]  
Her towns shall become desolate,  
With no one living in them. ^48-9

**48:10** Cursed be he who is slack in doing the LORD’s work! Cursed be he who withholds his sword from blood! ^48-10

**48:11** Moab has been secure from his youth on—  
He is settled on his lees  
And has not been poured from vessel to vessel—  
He has never gone into exile.  
Therefore his fine flavor has remained  
And his bouquet is unspoiled. ^48-11

**48:12** But days are coming—declares the LORD—when I will send men against him to tip him over; they shall empty his vessels and smash his jars. ^48-12

**48:13** And Moab shall be shamed because of Chemosh, as the House of Israel were shamed because of Bethel, on whom they relied. ^48-13

**48:14** How can you say: We are warriors,  
Valiant men for war? ^48-14

**48:15** Moab is ravaged,  
His towns have been entered,  
His choice young men  
Have gone down to the slaughter  
—declares the King whose name is LORD of Hosts. ^48-15

**48:16** The doom of Moab is coming close,  
His downfall is approaching swiftly. ^48-16

**48:17** Condole with him, all who live near him,  
All you who know him by name!  
Say: “Alas, the strong rod is broken,  
The lordly staff!” ^48-17

**48:18** Descend from glory  
And sit in thirst,[^7]  
O inhabitant of Fair Dibon;  
For the ravager of Moab has entered your town,  
He has destroyed your fortresses. ^48-18

**48:19** Stand by the road and look out,  
O inhabitant of Aroer.  
Ask of him who is fleeing  
And of her who is escaping:  
Say, “What has happened?” ^48-19

**48:20** Moab is shamed and dismayed;  
Howl and cry aloud!  
Tell at the Arnon  
That Moab is ravaged! ^48-20

**48:21** Judgment has come upon the tableland—upon Holon, Jahzah, and Mephaath; ^48-21

**48:22** upon Dibon, Nebo, and Beth-diblathaim; ^48-22

**48:23** upon Kiriathaim, Beth-gamul, and Beth-meon; ^48-23

**48:24** upon Kerioth and Bozrah—upon all the towns of the land of Moab, far and near. ^48-24

**48:25** The might of Moab has been cut down,  
His strength is broken  
—declares the LORD. ^48-25

**48:26** Get him drunk  
For he vaunted himself against the LORD.  
Moab shall vomit till he is drained,  
And he too shall be a laughingstock. ^48-26

**48:27** Wasn’t Israel a laughingstock to you?  
Was he ever caught among thieves,  
That you should shake your head[^8]  
Whenever you speak of him? ^48-27

**48:28** Desert the cities  
And dwell in the crags,  
O inhabitants of Moab!  
Be like a dove that nests  
In the sides of a pit. ^48-28

**48:29** We have heard of Moab’s pride—  
Most haughty is he—  
Of his arrogance and pride,  
His haughtiness and self-exaltation. ^48-29

**48:30** I know his insolence—declares the LORD—the wickedness that is in him,[^9] the wickedness he has[^10] committed. ^48-30

**48:31** Therefore I will howl for Moab,  
I will cry out for all Moab,  
I[^11] will moan for the men of Kir-heres. ^48-31

**48:32** With greater weeping than for Jazer  
I weep for you, O vine of Sibmah,  
Whose tendrils crossed the sea,  
Reached to the sea,[^12] to Jazer.  
A ravager has come down  
Upon your fig and grape harvests. ^48-32

**48:33** Rejoicing and gladness  
Are gone from the farm land,  
From the country of Moab;  
I have put an end to wine in the presses,  
No one treads [the grapes] with shouting—  
The shout is a shout no more.[^13] ^48-33

**48:34** There is an outcry from Heshbon to Elealeh,  
They raise their voices as far as Jahaz,  
From Zoar to Horonaim and Eglath-shelishiah.  
The waters of Nimrim  
Shall also become desolation. ^48-34

**48:35** And I will make an end in Moab  
—declares the LORD—  
Of those who offer at a shrine  
And burn incense to their god. ^48-35

**48:36** Therefore,  
My heart moans for Moab like a flute;  
Like a flute my heart moans  
For the men of Kir-heres—  
Therefore,  
The gains they have made shall vanish[^14]— ^48-36

**48:37** For every head is bald  
And every beard is shorn;  
On all hands there are gashes,  
And on the loins sackcloth. ^48-37

**48:38** On all the roofs of Moab,  
And in its squares  
There is naught but lamentation;  
For I have broken Moab  
Like a vessel no one wants  
—declares the LORD. ^48-38

**48:39** How he is dismayed! Wail!  
How Moab has turned his back in shame!  
Moab shall be a laughingstock  
And a shock to all those near him. ^48-39

**48:40** For thus said the LORD:  
See, he soars like an eagle  
And spreads out his wings against Moab! ^48-40

**48:41** Kerioth shall be captured  
And the strongholds shall be seized.  
In that day, the heart of Moab’s warriors  
Shall be like the heart of a woman in travail. ^48-41

**48:42** And Moab shall be destroyed as a people,  
For he vaunted himself against the LORD. ^48-42

**48:43** Terror, and pit, and trap[^15]  
Upon you who dwell in Moab!  
—declares the LORD. ^48-43

**48:44** He who flees from the terror  
Shall fall into the pit;  
And he who climbs out of the pit  
Shall be caught in the trap.  
For I will bring upon Moab  
The year of their doom  
—declares the LORD. ^48-44

**48:45** In the shelter of Heshbon  
Fugitives halt exhausted;  
For fire went forth from Heshbon,  
Flame from the midst[^16] of Sihon,  
Consuming the brow of Moab,  
The pate of the people of Shaon.[^17] ^48-45

**48:46** Woe to you, O Moab!  
The people of Chemosh are undone,  
For your sons are carried off into captivity,  
Your daughters into exile. ^48-46

**48:47** But I will restore the fortunes of Moab in the days to come—declares the LORD.  
Thus far is the judgment on Moab. ^48-47

[^6]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
