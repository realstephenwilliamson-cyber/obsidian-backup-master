# Isaiah 21 (Douay-Rheims)

*Isaias 21 in the Douay-Rheims*

[[DRB Isaiah 20|← Isaiah 20]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Isaiah 22|Isaiah 22 →]]

**21:1** The burden of the desert of the sea. As whirlwinds come from the south, it cometh from the desert from a terrible land. ^21-1

**21:2** A grievous vision is told me: he that is unfaithful dealeth unfaithfully: and he that is a spoiler, spoileth. Go up, O Elam, besiege, O Mede: I have made all the mourning thereof to cease. ^21-2

**21:3** Therefore are my loins filled with pain, anguish hath taken hold of me, as the anguish of a woman in labour: I fell down at the hearing of it, I was troubled at the seeing of it. ^21-3

**21:4** My heart failed, darkness amazed me: Babylon my beloved is become a wonder to me. ^21-4

**21:5** Prepare the table, behold in the watchtower them that eat and drink: arise, ye princes, take up the shield. ^21-5

**21:6** For thus hath the Lord said to me: Go, and set a watchman: and whatsoever he shall see, let him tell. ^21-6

**21:7** And he saw a chariot with two horsemen, a rider upon an ass, and a rider upon a camel: and he beheld them diligently with much heed. ^21-7

**21:8** And a lion cried out: I am upon the watchtower of the Lord, standing continually by day: and I am upon my ward, standing whole nights. ^21-8

**21:9** Behold this man cometh, the rider upon the chariot with two horsemen, and he answered, and said: Babylon is fallen, she is fallen, and all the graven gods thereof are broken unto the ground. ^21-9

**21:10** O my thrashing and the children of my door, that which I have heard of the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, I have declared unto you. ^21-10

**21:11** The burden of Duma calleth to me out of Seir: Watchman, what of the eight? watchman, what of the night? ^21-11

**21:12** The watchman said: The morning cometh, also the night: if you seek, seek: return, come. ^21-12

**21:13** The burden in Arabia. In the forest at evening you shall sleep, in the paths of Dedanim. ^21-13

**21:14** Meeting the thirsty bring him water, you that inhabit the land of the south, meet with bread him that fleeth. ^21-14

**21:15** For they are fled from before the swords, from the sword that hung over them, from the bent bow, from the face of a grievous battle. ^21-15

**21:16** For thus saith the Lord to me: Within a year, according to the years of a hireling, all the glory of Cedar shall be taken away. ^21-16

**21:17** And the residue of the number of strong archers of the children of Cedar shall be diminished: for the Lord the God of Israel hath spoken it. ^21-17
