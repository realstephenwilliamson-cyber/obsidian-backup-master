# Cybernetic Anamnesis

> [!abstract] Definition
> A programmatic framework for critical remembering (*anamnesis*) that works through and within digital algorithmic architectures rather than seeking an impossible return to pre-digital nostalgia. It integrates curatorial literacy, archival verification, and democratic governance of data infrastructures to restore epistemic trust.

## Core Principles
From the conclusion of [[Cybernetic Anamnesis text 2025-11-05|Cybernetic Anamnesis]]:
1. **Rejection of Techno-Luddism:** Since [[Tertiary Retention|tertiary retention]] constitutes human consciousness from the start (see [[Epimetheus and the Default of Origin]]), rejecting technical media is impossible.
2. **Reconstitution of the Archive:** Actively combating "commanded amnesia" by constructing resilient, redundant, cryptographic archives of documentary proof.
3. **Curatorial Literacy:** Developing educational and liturgical habits that restore cognitive friction and deliberative latency to digital encounters.
4. **Hermeneutical Justice:** Recognizing that fair access to meaning-making mechanisms is foundational to global and democratic justice.
