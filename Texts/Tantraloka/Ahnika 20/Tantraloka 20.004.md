## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 11 - Chapter Twenty#^pg254|p. 234]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=254>))
>
> (The teacher who is) one-pointedly engaged in repeating Mantra, (should imagine that on the palm of his) right hand is an intensely flaming triangle, which is the abode of Fire (R), to be burning all around with the phonemes of Fire (R), fanned by the abode of the Wind (Y). Taking hold of some seed, he should imagine that it is burning (there in his) hand and within (his) heart. The seed, burning with the fire of an uninterrupted stream (dhoranī) of PHATṬs and energized by the Fire (of the letter R), becomes sterile and incapable of germination. (with 20.2–4)

## Viveka

> [!viveka]- Viveka in English (Dyczkowski, vol. IX, p. 234)
> ![[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 11 - Chapter Twenty#Viveka on 20.2–4]]

> त्रिकोणे वह्निसदने वह्निवर्णोज्ज्वलेऽभितः ।
> वायव्यपुरनिर्धूते करे सव्ये सुजाज्वले ॥
> बीजं किञ्चिद् गृहीत्वैतत्तथैव हृदयान्तरे ।
> करे च दह्यमानं सच्चिन्तयेत्तज्जपैकयुक् ॥
> वह्निदीपितफट्कारधोरणीदाहपीडितम् ।
> बीजं निर्बीजतामेति स्वसूतिकरणाक्षमम् ॥

वह्निसदन इति--अर्थात् ऊर्ध्वमुखे । वह्निवर्णेति--रेफः । वायव्यपुरम्--षडश्रम्, अर्थात् यकारैर्लाञ्छितम् । सव्य इति-- दक्षिणे । बीजं किञ्चिदिति--धान्यादि । तज्जपैकयुगिति फट्कारोद्दीपितरेफावर्तनपरः--इत्यर्थः ॥ ४ ॥
