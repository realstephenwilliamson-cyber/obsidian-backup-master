## Mūla

शिवादिसकलात्मान्ताः शक्तिमन्तः प्रकीर्तिताः ।
तच्छक्तयश्च विज्ञेयास्तद्वदेव विचक्षणैः ॥ ^mula

śivādisakalātmāntāḥ śaktimantaḥ prakīrtitāḥ |
tacchaktayaś ca vijñeyās tadvad eva vicakṣaṇaiḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, July 2022 – September 2022
> p. 29 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Malini (journal), July-September 2022.pdf#page=31>))
>
> (The subjects who are) the possessors of power (are seven); they are said (to range) from Śiva to the Conditioned One (Sakala). The wise should know that, in the same way, their (corresponding) powers (are seven).[^16]

[^16]: MV 2/2 and 4-7ab are quoted in TĀv ad 10/3-5. MV 2/3 is not quoted there or elsewhere in the TĀ or the TĀv.
