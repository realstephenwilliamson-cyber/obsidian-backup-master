## Mūla

तद्द्वयालम्बना मातृव्यापारात्मक्रिया इति ।
तान्मात्रस्तु गणो ध्वान्तप्रधानाया अहंकृतेः ॥ ^mula

taddvayālambanā mātṛvyāpārātmakriyā iti |
tānmātrastu gaṇo dhvāntapradhānāyā ahaṃkṛteḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 09 - Chapter Nine, part 7#^pg278|p. 259]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=278>))
>
> And the teacher (Utpaladeva) has said that the mind, reacting (to what it receives through the external senses), determines (what it is, generating thereby the thought processes (kalpana) associated with action and the like), that are based on the two of them (unity and diversity), and consists of the activity of the subject. (with 9.278cd–279ab)
>
> The group of subtle elements (arises) from the predominantly tāmasika ego. (with 9.279cd)
