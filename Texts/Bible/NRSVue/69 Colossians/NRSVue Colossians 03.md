## The new life in Christ

**3:1** So if you have been raised with Christ, seek the things that are above, where Christ is, seated at the right hand of God. ^3-1

**3:2** Set your minds on the things that are above, not on the things that are on earth, ^3-2

**3:3** for you have died, and your life is hidden with Christ in God. ^3-3

**3:4** When Christ who is your^[Other authorities read *our*] life is revealed, then you also will be revealed with him in glory. ^3-4

**3:5** Put to death, therefore, whatever in you is earthly: sexual immorality, impurity, passion, evil desire, and greed (which is idolatry). ^3-5

**3:6** On account of these the wrath of God is coming on those who are disobedient.^[Gk *the sons of disobedience*; other ancient authorities lack *on those who are disobedient*] ^3-6

**3:7** These are the ways you also once followed, when you were living that life.^[Or *living among such people*] ^3-7

**3:8** But now you must get rid of all such things: anger, wrath, malice, slander, and abusive^[Or *filthy*] language from your mouth. ^3-8

**3:9** Do not lie to one another, seeing that you have stripped off the old self with its practices ^3-9

**3:10** and have clothed yourselves with the new self, which is being renewed in knowledge according to the image of its creator. ^3-10

**3:11** In that renewal^[Gk *its creator,11where*] there is no longer Greek and Jew, circumcised and uncircumcised, barbarian, Scythian, enslaved and free, but Christ is all and in all! ^3-11

**3:12** Therefore, as God’s chosen ones, holy and beloved, clothe yourselves with compassion, kindness, humility, meekness, and patience. ^3-12

**3:13** Bear with one another and, if anyone has a complaint against another, forgive each other; just as the Lord^[Other ancient authorities read *just as Christ*] has forgiven you, so you also must forgive. ^3-13

**3:14** Above all, clothe yourselves with love, which binds everything together in perfect harmony. ^3-14

**3:15** And let the peace of Christ^[Other ancient authorities read *of God*] rule in your hearts, to which indeed you were called in one body. And be thankful. ^3-15

**3:16** Let the word of Christ^[Other ancient authorities read *of God* or *of the Lord*] dwell in you richly; teach and admonish one another in all wisdom; and with gratitude in your hearts sing psalms, hymns, and spiritual songs to God.^[Other ancient authorities read *to the Lord*] ^3-16

**3:17** And whatever you do, in word or deed, do everything in the name of the Lord Jesus, giving thanks to God the Father through him. ^3-17

## Rules for Christian households

**3:18** Wives, be subject to your husbands, as is fitting in the Lord. ^3-18

**3:19** Husbands, love your wives and never treat them harshly. ^3-19

**3:20** Children, obey your parents in everything, for this is your acceptable duty in the Lord. ^3-20

**3:21** Fathers, do not provoke your children, or they may lose heart. ^3-21

**3:22** Slaves, obey your earthly masters^[In Gk the same word is used for *master* and *Lord*] in everything, not with a slavery performed merely for looks, to please people, but wholeheartedly, fearing the Lord.^[In Gk the same word is used for *master* and *Lord*] ^3-22

**3:23** Whatever task you must do, work as if your soul depends on it, as for the Lord and not for humans, ^3-23

**3:24** since you know that from the Lord you will receive the inheritance as your reward; you serve the Lord Christ. ^3-24

**3:25** For the wrongdoer will be paid back for whatever wrong has been done, and there is no partiality. ^3-25
