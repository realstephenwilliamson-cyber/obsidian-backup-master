# Psalms 19 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 18|← Psalms 18]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 20|Psalms 20 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 19|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**19:1** Unto the end. A psalm for David. ^19-1

**19:2** May the Lord hear thee in the day of tribulation: may the name of the God of Jacob protect thee. ^19-2

**19:3** May he send thee help from the sanctuary: and defend thee out of Sion. ^19-3

**19:4** May he be mindful of all thy sacrifices: and may thy whole burnt offering be made fat. ^19-4

**19:5** May he give thee according to thy own heart; and confirm all thy counsels. ^19-5

**19:6** We will rejoice in thy salvation; and in the name of our God we shall be exalted. ^19-6

**19:7** The Lord fulfill all thy petitions: now have I known that the Lord hath saved his anointed. He will hear him from his holy heaven: the salvation of his right hand is in powers. ^19-7

**19:8** Some trust in chariots, and some in horses: but we will call upon the name of the Lord our God. ^19-8

**19:9** They are bound, and have fallen; but we are risen, and are set upright. O Lord, save the king: and hear us in the day that we shall call upon thee. ^19-9
