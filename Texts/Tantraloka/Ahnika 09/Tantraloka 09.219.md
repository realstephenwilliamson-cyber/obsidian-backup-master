## Mūla

तथापि वस्तुसत्तेयमिहास्माभिर्निरूपिता ।
तस्यां च न क्रमः कोऽपि स्याद्वा सोऽपि विपर्ययात् ॥ ^mula

tathāpi vastusatteyamihāsmābhirnirūpitā |
tasyāṃ ca na kramaḥ ko'pi syādvā so'pi viparyayāt || ^iast

> Even so, we have described the (very) being of reality (vastusattā) here, and there is no succession in it at all (because everything is interdependent). Even if there were to be, it could also be reversed.
