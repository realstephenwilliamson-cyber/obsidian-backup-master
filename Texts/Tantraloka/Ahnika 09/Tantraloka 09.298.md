## Mūla

काणादैस्तत्स्वप्रतीतिविरुद्धं केन गृह्यताम् ।
पटहे ध्वनिरित्येव भात्यबाधितमेव यत् ॥ ^mula

kāṇādaistatsvapratītiviruddhaṃ kena gṛhyatām |
paṭahe dhvanirityeva bhātyabādhitameva yat || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 09 - Chapter Nine, part 7#^pg293|p. 274]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=293>))
>
> The followers of Kanāda say that sound, unlike touch, is a quality (only of space). Who could accept (such a view, that) runs contrary to one’s own experience? (with 9.297cd–298ab)
>
> What appears, without being contradicted (by experience), is that the sound is in the drum (not in the ear). (with 9.298cd)
