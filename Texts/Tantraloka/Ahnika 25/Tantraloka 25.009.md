## Mūla

बुभुक्षोस्तु क्रियाभ्यासभूमानौ फलभूमनि ।
हेतू ततो मृतोद्धारश्राद्धाद्यस्मै समाचरेत् ॥ ^mula

bubhukṣostu kriyābhyāsabhūmānau phalabhūmani |
hetū tato mṛtoddhāraśrāddhādyasmai samācaret || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 17 - Chapter Twenty-Five (continued) and Chapter Twenty-Six#^pg379|p. 359]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=379>))
>
> In the case of (a disciple) who desires worldly benefits, (the teacher) should perform (the rites) to rescue the dead, and make offerings to the ancestors (Śrāddha) etc. for (his benefit, on the basis of the principle that) an abundance of ritual and practice are the causes of (a correspondingly great) abundance of fruits.

## Viveka

> [!viveka]- Viveka in English (Dyczkowski, vol. IX, p. 359)
> ![[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 17 - Chapter Twenty-Five (continued) and Chapter Twenty-Six#Viveka on 25.9]]

> बुभुक्षोस्तु क्रियाभ्यासभूमानौ फलभूमनि ।
> हेतू ततो मृतोद्धारश्राद्धाद्यस्मै समाचरेत् ॥

उक्तं च--

"दीक्षाज्ञानविशुद्धानामन्त्येष्ट्याप्यमलात्मनाम् ।

तथापि कार्यमीशोक्तं श्राद्धं वै विधिपूरणम् ॥"

इति । अनेन च श्राद्धादेः प्रयोजनमुक्तम् ॥ ९ ॥
