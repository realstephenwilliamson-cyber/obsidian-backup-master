# Job 24 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Job 23|← Job 23]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Job 25|Job 25 →]]

## Job complains of violence on the earth

**24:1** “Why are times not kept by the Almighty,^[Traditional rendering of Heb *Shaddai*] and why do those who know him never see his days? ^24-1

**24:2** The wicked^[Gk: Heb *they*] remove landmarks; they seize flocks and pasture them. ^24-2

**24:3** They drive away the donkey of the orphan; they take the widow’s ox for a pledge. ^24-3

**24:4** They thrust the needy off the road; the poor of the earth all hide themselves. ^24-4

**24:5** Like wild asses in the desert they go out to their toil, scavenging in the wasteland food for their young. ^24-5

**24:6** They reap in a field not their own, and they glean in the vineyard of the wicked. ^24-6

**24:7** They lie all night naked, without clothing, and have no covering in the cold. ^24-7

**24:8** They are wet with the rain of the mountains and cling to the rock for want of shelter. ^24-8

**24:9** “There are those who snatch the orphan child from the breast and take as a pledge the infant of the poor. ^24-9

**24:10** They go about naked, without clothing; though hungry, they carry the sheaves; ^24-10

**24:11** between their terraces^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] they press out oil; they tread the winepresses but suffer thirst. ^24-11

**24:12** From the city the dying groan, and the throat of the wounded cries for help; yet God pays no attention to their prayer. ^24-12

**24:13** “There are those who rebel against the light, who are not acquainted with its ways and do not stay in its paths. ^24-13

**24:14** The murderer rises at dusk to kill the poor and needy and in the night is like a thief. ^24-14

**24:15** The eye of the adulterer also waits for the twilight, saying, ‘No eye will see me,’ and he disguises his face. ^24-15

**24:16** In the dark they dig through houses; by day they shut themselves up; they do not know the light. ^24-16

**24:17** For deep darkness is morning to all of them; for they are friends with the terrors of deep darkness. ^24-17

**24:18** “Swift are they on the face of the waters; their portion in the land is cursed; no treader turns toward their vineyards. ^24-18

**24:19** Drought and heat snatch away the snow waters; so does Sheol those who have sinned. ^24-19

**24:20** The womb forgets them; the worm finds them sweet; they are no longer remembered, so wickedness is broken like a tree. ^24-20

**24:21** “They harm^[Gk Tg: Heb *feed on* or *associate with*] the childless woman and do no good to the widow. ^24-21

**24:22** Yet God^[Heb *he*] prolongs the life of the mighty by his power; they rise up when they despair of life. ^24-22

**24:23** He gives them security, and they are supported; his eyes are upon their ways. ^24-23

**24:24** They are exalted a little while and then are gone; they wither and fade like the mallow;^[Gk: Heb *like all others*] they are cut off like the heads of grain. ^24-24

**24:25** If it is not so, who will prove me a liar and show that there is nothing in what I say?” ^24-25
