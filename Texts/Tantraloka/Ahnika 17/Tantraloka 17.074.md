## Mūla

मायान्ते शुद्धिमायाते वागीशी या पुराभवत् ।
माया शक्तिमयी सैव विद्याशक्तित्वमश्नुते ॥ ^mula

māyānte śuddhimāyāte vāgīśī yā purābhavat |
māyā śaktimayī saiva vidyāśaktitvamaśnute || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 08 - Chapter Seventeen#^pg191|p. 171]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=191>))
>
> When (everything) up to the end of Māyā has been purified, (the Goddess) Vāgīśīi, who was previously the power of Māyā, is (now) the power of Pure Knowledge (śuddhavidyā). Having invoked this Pure Knowledge, (the teacher) should offer it a libation. Once the plane of Sadāśiva has been purified sufficiently in this way by (the same) process, having severed the topknot along with the knot (on the thread), he should burn the fundamental Impurity of the individual soul (ā&navamala). (with 17.74–76ab)
