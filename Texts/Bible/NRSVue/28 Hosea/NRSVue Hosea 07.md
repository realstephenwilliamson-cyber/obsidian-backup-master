# Hosea 7 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Hosea 06|← Hosea 6]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Hosea 08|Hosea 8 →]]

**7:1** when I would heal Israel, the corruption of Ephraim is revealed, and the wicked deeds of Samaria, for they deal falsely; the thief breaks in, and the bandits raid outside. ^7-1

**7:2** But they do not consider that I remember all their wickedness. Now their deeds surround them; they are before my face. ^7-2

**7:3** By their wickedness they make the king glad, and the officials by their treachery. ^7-3

**7:4** They are all adulterers; they are like a heated oven whose baker does not need to stir the fire from the kneading of the dough until it is leavened. ^7-4

**7:5** On the day of our king the officials became sick with the heat of wine; he stretched out his hand with mockers. ^7-5

**7:6** For they are kindled^[Gk Syr: Heb *brought near*] like an oven; their heart burns within them; all night their anger smolders; in the morning it blazes like a flaming fire. ^7-6

**7:7** All of them are hot as an oven, and they devour their rulers. All their kings have fallen; none of them calls upon me. ^7-7

**7:8** Ephraim mixes himself with the peoples; Ephraim is a cake not turned. ^7-8

**7:9** Foreigners devour his strength, but he does not know it; gray hairs are sprinkled upon him, but he does not know it. ^7-9

**7:10** Israel’s pride testifies against^[Or *humbles*] him; yet they do not return to the LORD their God or seek him, for all this. ^7-10

## Futile reliance on the nations

**7:11** Ephraim has become like a dove, silly and without sense; they call upon Egypt, they go to Assyria. ^7-11

**7:12** As they go, I will cast my net over them; I will bring them down like birds of the air; I will discipline them according to the report made to their assembly.^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^7-12

**7:13** Woe to them, for they have strayed from me! Destruction to them, for they have rebelled against me! I would redeem them, but they speak lies against me. ^7-13

**7:14** They do not cry to me from the heart, but they wail upon their beds; they gash themselves for grain and wine; they rebel against me. ^7-14

**7:15** It was I who trained and strengthened their arms, yet they plot evil against me. ^7-15

**7:16** They turn to that which does not profit;^[Cn: Meaning of Heb uncertain] they have become like a defective bow; their officials shall fall by the sword because of the rage of their tongue. So much for their babbling in the land of Egypt. ^7-16
