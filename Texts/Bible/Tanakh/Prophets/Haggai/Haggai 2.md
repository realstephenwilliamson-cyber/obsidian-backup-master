## Chapter 2

**2:1** on the twenty-first day of the seventh month, the word of the LORD came through the prophet Haggai: ^2-1

**2:2** Speak to Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, the governor of Judah, and to the high priest Joshua son of Jehozadak, and to the rest of the people: ^2-2

**2:3** Who is there left among you who saw this House in its former splendor? How does it look to you now? It must seem like nothing to you. ^2-3

**2:4** But be strong, O Zerubbabel—says the LORD—be strong, O high priest Joshua son of Jehozadak; be strong, all you people of the land—says the LORD—and act! For I am with you—says the LORD of Hosts. ^2-4

**2:5** So I promised you when you came out of Egypt, and My spirit is still in your midst. Fear not! ^2-5

**2:6** For thus said the LORD of Hosts: In just a little while longer I will shake the heavens and the earth, the sea and the dry land; ^2-6

**2:7** I will shake all the nations. And the precious things of all the nations shall come [here], and I will fill this House with glory, said the LORD of Hosts. ^2-7

**2:8** Silver is Mine and gold is Mine—says the LORD of Hosts. ^2-8

**2:9** The glory of this latter House shall be greater than that of the former one, said the LORD of Hosts; and in this place I will grant prosperity—declares the LORD of Hosts. ^2-9

**2:10** On the twenty-fourth day of the ninth [month], in the second year of Darius, the word of the LORD came to the prophet Haggai: ^2-10

**2:11** Thus said the LORD of Hosts: Seek a ruling from the priests, as follows: ^2-11

**2:12** If a man is carrying sacrificial flesh in a fold of his garment, and with that fold touches bread, stew, wine, oil, or any other food, will the latter become holy? In reply, the priests said, “No.” ^2-12

**2:13** Haggai went on, “If someone defiled by a corpse touches any of these, will it be defiled?” And the priests responded, “Yes.” ^2-13

**2:14** Thereupon Haggai said: That is how this people and that is how this nation looks to Me—declares the LORD—and so, too, the work of their hands: Whatever they offer there is defiled. ^2-14

**2:15** And now take thought, from this day backward:[^1] As long as no stone had been laid on another in the House of the LORD, ^2-15

**2:16** if one came to a heap of twenty measures,[^2] it would yield only ten; and if one came to a wine vat to skim off fifty measures, the press would yield only twenty. ^2-16

**2:17** I struck you—all the works of your hands—with blight and mildew and hail, but you did not return[^3] to Me—declares the LORD. ^2-17

**2:18** Take note, from this day forward—from the twenty-fourth day of the ninth month, from the day when the foundation was laid for the LORD’s Temple—take note ^2-18

**2:19** while the seed is still in the granary, and the vine, fig tree, pomegranate, and olive tree have not yet borne fruit. For from this day on I will send blessings. ^2-19

**2:20** And the word of the LORD came to Haggai a second time on the twenty-fourth day of the month: ^2-20

**2:21** Speak to Zerubbabel the governor of Judah: I am going to shake the heavens and the earth. ^2-21

**2:22** And I will overturn the thrones of kingdoms and destroy the might of the kingdoms of the nations. I will overturn chariots and their drivers. Horses and their riders shall fall, each by the sword of his fellow. ^2-22

**2:23** On that day—declares the LORD of Hosts—I will take you, O My servant Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel—declares the LORD—and make you as a signet;[^4] for I have chosen you—declares the LORD of Hosts. ^2-23

[^1]: Or “forward.”
[^2]: I.e., of grain.
[^3]: Lit. “there was not with you to Me”; cf. Amos 4.9.
[^4]: I.e., bring you close to Me; contrast Jer. 22.24–30.
