## Mūla

अभिचारे च मन्त्राणां नमस्कारादिजातयः ।
अक्षिषण्मुनिवर्गेभ्यो द्वितीयाः सह बिन्दुना ॥ ^mula

abhicāre ca mantrāṇāṃ namaskārādijātayaḥ |
akṣiṣaṇmunivargebhyo dvitīyāḥ saha bindunā || ^iast

## Translation

> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 03 - Chapter Thirty, part 2#^pg51|p. 33]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=51>))

> PHAT). (with 30.43cd–45ab)
>
> Kālakarṣiṇī, the Essence of the Mothers (mātrsadbhāva), consists of the second letters of the second (KH), sixth (PH) and seventh class (of consonants) (R), along with the letter of the Womb (E) and the Point (M). Or else it is also agreed (that it can also) be without the first (letter - KH i.e., PAREM) or the final one (R i.e., KHPHREM). (with 30.45cd–46)
