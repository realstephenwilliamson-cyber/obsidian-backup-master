## Mūla

संवेद्यजीवनाभिख्यप्रयत्नस्पन्दसुन्दरः ।
प्राणः कन्दात्प्रभृत्येव तथाप्यत्र न सुस्फुटः ॥ ^mula

saṃvedyajīvanābhikhyaprayatnaspandasundaraḥ |
prāṇaḥ kandātprabhṛtyeva tathāpyatra na susphuṭaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 09 - Chapter Six, part 2#^pg276|p. 261]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=276>))

> The vital breath, beautiful with the pulse (spanda) of the exertion called perceptible vitality (sarnvedyajīvana), (originates) from the Bulb (in the genitals) (kanda); even so is not clearly evident here.
