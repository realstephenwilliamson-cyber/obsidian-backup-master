## The folly of trust in riches

*To the leader. Of the Korahites. A Psalm.*

**49:1** Hear this, all you peoples; give ear, all inhabitants of the world, ^49-1

**49:2** both low and high, rich and poor together. ^49-2

**49:3** My mouth shall speak wisdom; the meditation of my heart shall be understanding. ^49-3

**49:4** I will incline my ear to a proverb; I will solve my riddle to the music of the harp. ^49-4

**49:5** Why should I fear in times of trouble, when the iniquity of my persecutors surrounds me, ^49-5

**49:6** those who trust in their wealth and boast of the abundance of their riches? ^49-6

**49:7** Truly, no ransom avails for one’s life;^[Or *no one can ransom a brother*] there is no price one can give to God for it. ^49-7

**49:8** For the ransom of life is costly and can never suffice, ^49-8

**49:9** that one should live on forever and never see the Pit. ^49-9

**49:10** When we look at the wise, they die; fool and dolt perish together and leave their wealth to others. ^49-10

**49:11** Their graves^[Gk Syr Compare Tg: Heb *their inward thought*] are their homes forever, their dwelling places to all generations, though they named lands their own. ^49-11

**49:12** Mortals cannot abide in their pomp; they are like the animals that perish. ^49-12

**49:13** Such is the fate of the foolhardy, the end of those^[Tg: Heb *after them*] who are pleased with their lot. *Selah* ^49-13

**49:14** Like sheep they are appointed for Sheol; Death shall be their shepherd; straight to the grave they descend,^[Cn: Heb *the upright shall have dominion over them in the morning*] and their form shall waste away; Sheol shall be their home.^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^49-14

**49:15** But God will ransom my soul from the power of Sheol, for he will receive me. *Selah* ^49-15

**49:16** Do not be afraid when some become rich, when the wealth of their houses increases. ^49-16

**49:17** For when they die they will carry nothing away; their wealth will not go down after them. ^49-17

**49:18** Though in their lifetime they count themselves happy—for you are praised when you do well for yourself— ^49-18

**49:19** they^[Cn: Heb *you*] will go to the company of their ancestors, who will never again see the light. ^49-19

**49:20** Mortals cannot abide in their pomp; they are like the animals that perish. ^49-20
