## Mūla

न तथात्वाय योगीच्छाविष्टशावशरीरवत् ।
योगिनः परदेहादिजीवत्तापादने निजम् ॥ ^mula

na tathātvāya yogīcchāviṣṭaśāvaśarīravat |
yoginaḥ paradehādijīvattāpādane nijam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 05 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 3#^pg110|p. 93]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=110>))
>
> The unity and diversity present in the corporeal continuum does not serve to bring about the same (unity and diversity) of the cognitive continuum. It is as happens with the body of a corpse penetrated by a yogi’s will (that comes to life but does not alter the yogi’s consciousness, because he does not identify with it). (with 28.205cd–206ab)
>
> When, for example, a yogi gives life to another body without abandoning his own body, do not (his) various perceptions serve as material causes (of the operation of the senses in the other body)? (with 28.206cd–207ab)
