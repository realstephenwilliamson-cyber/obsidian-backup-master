## Mūla

दद्याद्येन विशुद्धं तद्यागयोग्यत्वमश्नुते ।
तत्रादौ शिवतापत्तिस्वातन्त्र्यावेश एव यः ॥ ^mula

dadyādyena viśuddhaṃ tadyāgayogyatvamaśnute |
tatrādau śivatāpattisvātantryāveśa eva yaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 02 - Chapter Fifteen, part 1#^pg51|p. 33]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=51>))
>
> The disciple should give the money he has collected by begging or his own to the teacher in advance, so that it is pure and becomes fit (to offer for) the sacrifice. (with 15.37cd–38ab)
>
> There (in that case), first of all, one should choose (as one’s) teacher only one who is (profoundly) penetrated by the freedom that arises by (his) attainment of Śiva’s state (which is oneness with it). (Only) then is he capable of initiating (others). (with 15.38cd–39ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* एवमादौ शिष्याणां तत्तद्भेदभिन्नतया योग्यतालक्षणमौचित्यं परीक्ष्य, स्नानभेदादि प्रतिपादयितुं पीठिकाबन्धमारचयति--

> तत्रादौ शिवतापत्तिस्वातन्त्र्यावेश एव यः ॥
> स एव हि गुरुः कार्यस्ततोऽसौ दीक्षणे क्षमः ।
