# Psalms 112 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 111|← Psalms 111]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 113|Psalms 113 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 112|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**112:1** Praise the Lord, ye children: praise ye the name of the Lord. ^112-1

**112:2** Blessed be the name of the Lord, from henceforth now and for ever. ^112-2

**112:3** From the rising of the sun unto the going down of the same, the name of the Lord is worthy of praise. ^112-3

**112:4** The Lord is high above all nations; and his glory above the heavens. ^112-4

**112:5** Who is as the Lord our God, who dwelleth on high: ^112-5

**112:6** And looketh down on the low things in heaven and in earth? ^112-6

**112:7** Raising up the needy from the earth, and lifting up the poor out of the dunghill: ^112-7

**112:8** That he may place him with princes, with the princes of his people. ^112-8

**112:9** Who maketh a barren woman to dwell in a house, the joyful mother of children. ^112-9
