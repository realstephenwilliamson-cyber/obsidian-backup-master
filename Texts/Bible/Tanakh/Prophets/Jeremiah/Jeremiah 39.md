# Jeremiah 39

← [[Jeremiah 38]] · [[Tanakh]] · [[Jeremiah 40]] →

Compare: [[NRSVue Jeremiah 39|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 39

**39:1** In the ninth year of King Zedekiah of Judah, in the tenth month, King Nebuchadrezzar of Babylon moved against Jerusalem with his whole army, and they laid siege to it. ^39-1

**39:2** And in the eleventh year of Zedekiah, on the ninth day of the fourth month, the [walls of] the city were breached. ^39-2

**39:3** All the officers of the king of Babylon entered, and took up quarters at the middle gate—Nergal-sarezer, Samgar-nebo, Sarsechim the Rab-saris, Nergal-sarezer the Rab-mag,[^1] and all the rest of the officers of the king of Babylon. ^39-3

**39:4** When King Zedekiah of Judah saw them, he and all the soldiers fled. They left the city at night, by way of the king’s garden, through the gate between the double walls; and he set out toward the Arabah.[^2] ^39-4

**39:5** But the Chaldean troops pursued them, and they overtook Zedekiah in the steppes of Jericho. They captured him and brought him before King Nebuchadrezzar of Babylon at Riblah in the region of Hamath; and he put him on trial. ^39-5

**39:6** The king of Babylon had Zedekiah’s children slaughtered at Riblah before his eyes; the king of Babylon had all the nobles of Judah slaughtered. ^39-6

**39:7** Then the eyes of Zedekiah were put out and he was chained in bronze fetters, that he might be brought to Babylon. ^39-7

**39:8** The Chaldeans burned down the king’s palace and the houses[^3] of the people by fire, and they tore down the walls of Jerusalem. ^39-8

**39:9** The remnant of the people that was left in the city, and the defectors who had gone over to him—the remnant of the people that was left—were exiled by Nebuzaradan, the chief of the guards, to Babylon. ^39-9

**39:10** But some of the poorest people who owned nothing were left in the land of Judah by Nebuzaradan, the chief of the guards, and he gave them vineyards and fields at that time. ^39-10

**39:11** King Nebuchadrezzar of Babylon had given orders to Nebuzaradan, the chief of the guards, concerning Jeremiah: ^39-11

**39:12** “Take him and look after him; do him no harm, but grant whatever he asks of you.” ^39-12

**39:13** So Nebuzaradan, the chief of the guards, and Nebushazban the Rab-saris, and Nergal-sarezer the Rab-mag, and all the commanders of the king of Babylon sent ^39-13

**39:14** and had Jeremiah brought from the prison compound. They committed him to the care of Gedaliah son of Ahikam son of Shaphan, that he might be left at liberty in a house.[^4] So he dwelt among the people. ^39-14

**39:15** The word of the LORD had come to Jeremiah while he was still confined in the prison compound: ^39-15

**39:16** Go and say to Ebed-melech the Cushite: “Thus said the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel: I am going to fulfill My words concerning this city—for disaster, not for good—and they shall come true on that day in your presence. ^39-16

**39:17** But I will save you on that day—declares the LORD; you shall not be delivered into the hands of the men you dread. ^39-17

**39:18** I will rescue you, and you shall not fall by the sword. You shall escape with your life,[^5] because you trusted Me—declares the LORD.” ^39-18

[^1]: Titles of officers.
[^2]: Hoping to escape across the Jordan.
[^3]: Taking Heb. singular as collective, with Kimhi.
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^5]: See note at 38.2.
