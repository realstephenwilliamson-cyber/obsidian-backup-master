# Judges 5 (NRSVue)

## The song of Deborah

**5:1** Then Deborah and Barak son of Abinoam sang on that day, ^5-1

**5:2** “When locks are long in Israel, when the people offer themselves willingly^[Meaning of Heb uncertain]—bless the LORD! ^5-2

**5:3** Hear, O kings; give ear, O princes; to the LORD I will sing; I will make melody to the LORD, the God of Israel. ^5-3

**5:4** LORD, when you went out from Seir, when you marched from the region of Edom, the earth trembled, and the heavens poured; the clouds indeed poured water. ^5-4

**5:5** The mountains quaked before the LORD, the One of Sinai, before the LORD, the God of Israel. ^5-5

**5:6** In the days of Shamgar son of Anath, in the days of Jael, caravans ceased, and travelers kept to the byways. ^5-6

**5:7** The peasantry prospered in Israel; they grew fat on plunder, because^[Or *ceased in Israel, ceased until*] you arose, Deborah, arose as a mother in Israel. ^5-7

**5:8** When new gods were chosen, then war was in the gates. Was shield or spear to be seen among forty thousand in Israel? ^5-8

**5:9** My heart goes out to the commanders of Israel who offered themselves willingly among the people. Bless the LORD. ^5-9

**5:10** Sing of it, you who ride on white donkeys, you who sit on rich carpets,^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] and you who walk by the way. ^5-10

**5:11** To the sound of musicians^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] at the watering places, there they repeat the triumphs of the LORD, the triumphs of his peasantry in Israel. Then down to the gates marched the people of the LORD. ^5-11

**5:12** Awake, awake, Deborah! Awake, awake, utter a song! Arise, Barak, lead away your captives, O son of Abinoam. ^5-12

**5:13** Then down marched the remnant of the nobles; the people of the LORD marched down for him^[Gk mss: Heb *me*] against the mighty. ^5-13

**5:14** From Ephraim they set out^[Cn: Heb *From Ephraim their root*] into the valley,^[Gk: Heb *in Amalek*] following you, Benjamin, with your kin; from Machir marched down the commanders, and from Zebulun those who bear the marshal’s staff; ^5-14

**5:15** the chiefs of Issachar came with Deborah, and Issachar faithful to Barak; into the valley they rushed out at his heels. Among the clans of Reuben there were great searchings of heart. ^5-15

**5:16** Why did you tarry among the sheepfolds, to hear the piping for the flocks? Among the clans of Reuben there were great searchings of heart. ^5-16

**5:17** Gilead stayed beyond the Jordan, and Dan, why did he abide with the ships? Asher sat still at the coast of the sea, settling down by his landings. ^5-17

**5:18** Zebulun is a people that scorned death; Naphtali, too, on the heights of the field. ^5-18

**5:19** The kings came; they fought; then fought the kings of Canaan, at Taanach, by the waters of Megiddo; they got no spoils of silver. ^5-19

**5:20** The stars fought from heaven; from their courses they fought against Sisera. ^5-20

**5:21** The torrent Kishon swept them away, the onrushing torrent, the torrent Kishon. March on, my soul, with might! ^5-21

**5:22** Then loud beat the horses’ hoofs with the galloping, galloping of his steeds. ^5-22

**5:23** Curse Meroz, says the angel of the LORD; curse bitterly its inhabitants, because they did not come to the help of the LORD, to the help of the LORD against the mighty. ^5-23

**5:24** Most blessed of women be Jael, the wife of Heber the Kenite, of tent-dwelling women most blessed. ^5-24

**5:25** Water he asked, milk she gave; she brought him curds in a lordly bowl. ^5-25

**5:26** She put her hand to the tent peg and her right hand to the workers’ mallet; she struck Sisera a blow; she crushed his head; she shattered and pierced his temple. ^5-26

**5:27** Between her feet he sank, he fell, he lay still; between her feet he sank, he fell; where he sank, there he fell dead. ^5-27

**5:28** Out of the window she peered; the mother of Sisera gazed^[Gk Compare Tg: Heb *exclaimed*] through the lattice: ‘Why is his chariot so long in coming? Why tarry the hoofbeats of his chariots?’ ^5-28

**5:29** Her wisest ladies make answer; indeed, she answers the question herself: ^5-29

**5:30** ‘Are they not finding and dividing the spoil? A woman or two for every man; spoil of dyed stuffs for Sisera, spoil of dyed stuffs embroidered, two pieces of dyed work embroidered for my neck as spoil?’ ^5-30

**5:31** So perish all your enemies, O LORD! But may your friends be like the sun as it rises in its might.” And the land had rest forty years. ^5-31
