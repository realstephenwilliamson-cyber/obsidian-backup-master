# 2 Timothy 4 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB 2 Timothy 03|← 2 Timothy 3]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · —

Compare: [[2 Timothy 04|BSB and SBLGNT]]

**4:1** I charge thee, before God and Jesus Christ, who shall judge the living and the dead, by his coming, and his kingdom: ^4-1

**4:2** Preach the word: be instant in season, out of season: reprove, entreat, rebuke in all patience and doctrine. ^4-2

**4:3** For there shall be a time, when they will not endure sound doctrine; but, according to their own desires, they will heap to themselves teachers, having itching ears: ^4-3

**4:4** And will indeed turn away their hearing from the truth, but will be turned unto fables. ^4-4

**4:5** But be thou vigilant, labour in all things, do the work of an evangelist, fulfill thy ministry. Be sober. ^4-5

**4:6** For I am even now ready to be sacrificed: and the time of my dissolution is at hand. ^4-6

**4:7** I have fought a good fight, I have finished my course, I have kept the faith. ^4-7

**4:8** As to the rest, there is laid up for me a crown of justice, which the Lord the just judge will render to me in that day: and not only to me, but to them also that love his coming. Make haste to come to me quickly. ^4-8

**4:9** For Demas hath left me, loving this world, and is gone to Thessalonica: ^4-9

**4:10** Crescens into Galatia, Titus into Dalmatia. ^4-10

**4:11** Only Luke is with me. Take Mark, and bring him with thee: for he is profitable to me for the ministry. ^4-11

**4:12** But Tychicus I have sent to Ephesus. ^4-12

**4:13** The cloak that I left at Troas, with Carpus, when thou comest, bring with thee, and the books, especially the parchments. ^4-13

**4:14** Alexander the coppersmith hath done me much evil: the Lord will reward him according to his works: ^4-14

**4:15** Whom do thou also avoid, for he hath greatly withstood our words. ^4-15

**4:16** At my first answer no man stood with me, but all forsook me: may it not be laid to their charge. ^4-16

**4:17** But the Lord stood by me, and strengthened me, that by me the preaching may be accomplished, and that all the Gentiles may hear: and I was delivered out of the mouth of the lion. ^4-17

**4:18** The Lord hath delivered me from every evil work: and will preserve me unto his heavenly kingdom, to whom be glory for ever and ever. Amen. ^4-18

**4:19** Salute Prisca and Aquila, and the household of Onesiphorus. ^4-19

**4:20** Erastus remained at Corinth. And Trophimus I left sick at Miletus. ^4-20

**4:21** Make haste to come before winter Eubulus and Pudens, and Linus and Claudia, and all the brethren, salute thee. ^4-21

**4:22** The Lord Jesus Christ be with thy spirit. Grace be with you. Amen. ^4-22
