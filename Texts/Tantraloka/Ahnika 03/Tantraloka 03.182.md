## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. II
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 02 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v02 09 - Chapter Three, part 6#^pg319|p. 303]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 02.pdf#page=319>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 3.182cd–183ab.
>
> These two tetrads (Y, R, L, V and Ś, 5, S, K), (which come after) the previously well-established plane of the group of twenty-five (consonants from K to M), correspond successively to duality (bheda) and nonduality (abheda). The first tetrad (represents) the capacity of consciousness (saṁvitti) to contemplate (and conjoin) (sarndhāna) duality. The other tetrad (represents) the one caused by virtue of which duality (bheda) is firmly established in nonduality (abheda). (with 3.181–183ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* एतदेव प्रपञ्चयति --

> आद्यं चतुष्कं संवित्तेर्भेदसंधानकोविदम् ॥
> भेदस्याभेदरूढ्येकहेतुरन्यच्चतुष्टयम् ।

भेदेति -- संविदः सकाशाद्विच्छिन्नम् -- इत्यर्थः । अत एवास्य स्थूल वायवादिभूतरूपत्वमुक्तम् । अभेदेति -- इच्छाशक्तिसमुत्थत्वेन शाक्तेजोमात्र रूपत्वात् ॥ १८२ ॥
