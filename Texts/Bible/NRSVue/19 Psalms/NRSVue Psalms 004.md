# Psalm 4 (NRSVue)

## Confident plea for deliverance from enemies

*To the leader: with stringed instruments. A Psalm of David.*

**4:1** Answer me when I call, O God of my right! You gave me room when I was in distress. Be gracious to me, and hear my prayer. ^4-1

**4:2** How long, you people, shall my honor suffer shame? How long will you love vain words and seek after lies? *Selah* ^4-2

**4:3** But know that the LORD has set apart the faithful for himself; the LORD hears when I call to him. ^4-3

**4:4** When you are disturbed,^[Or *are angry*] do not sin; ponder it on your beds, and be silent. *Selah* ^4-4

**4:5** Offer right sacrifices, and put your trust in the LORD. ^4-5

**4:6** There are many who say, “O that we might see some good! Let the light of your face shine on us, O LORD!” ^4-6

**4:7** You have put gladness in my heart more than when their grain and wine abound. ^4-7

**4:8** I will both lie down and sleep in peace, for you alone, O LORD, make me lie down in safety. ^4-8
