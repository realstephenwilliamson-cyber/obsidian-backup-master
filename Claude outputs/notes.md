# Glossed homily page: mock-up notes (Proper 28A, 15 Nov 2026)

Read-only on the vault. Readings: Track 2 (Zephaniah 1:7, 12-18; Psalm 90), as the homily note's `readings` give them.

## Where each gloss was found
- **JANT:** `Texts/Bible/Jewish Annotated New Testament/Annotations/`, parsed as nt-page does. 1 Thess 5:1-11 has 7 notes; Matt 25:14-30 has 5.
- **Fathers:** `Texts/Fathers/` notes that link a verse, clipped to 260 characters. Found: Chrysostom on John, Hom. 34 and 45, both on 1 Thess 5:3.
- **Parallels:** `Texts/Crosswalks/Synoptic parallels.md`, row 48 (Lk 19:12-27). Cited texts are the links inside the JANT notes.

## Missing or sparse
- **Gospel, no Father.** The vault's Fathers are three works on John. Augustine, Tractate 10, alludes to the hidden talent without a link, so the view cannot find it; the mock-up shows it, flagged.
- **Zephaniah and Psalm 90:** no JANT, Father or parallel. One reverse hit: JANT on 1 Thess 5.2 cites Zeph 1.7.
- **Not checked:** the Summa (611 notes), `Library/`, your own glosses.
- **nt-page quirks carried over:** Qumran links dropped; labels lose their book ("13.28", "40a"); a clip can open on a stray reference.

## Cost and risk (estimates)
- **A, side columns:** about 250 lines JS, 60 CSS. Medium-high risk: widens the pane in a note holding your writing; three widths; print needs its own layout.
- **B, under the verses:** about 200 JS, 30 CSS. Medium-low: interrupts the text even when closed.
- **C, drawer:** about 150 JS, 25 CSS. Low; the sticky phone drawer needs testing in Obsidian.

All share a lookup lifted from nt-page. Speed is unmeasured: perhaps 0.2-0.5 s added if drawn with the page, near nothing if filled on first open.

## Recommendation
**C.** Smallest and safest; it keeps the text clean for reading aloud and does not look empty when a reading has no glosses, as two of four do this Sunday. A is handsomest on the Gospel, mostly empty elsewhere.
