## Mūla

आर्यावाक्यं सप्तमं स्यात्तच्चतुर्दशभिः पदैः ।
प्रभञ्जनस्त्वमित्येवं पाठो निःश्वासशासने ॥ ^mula

āryāvākyaṃ saptamaṃ syāttaccaturdaśabhiḥ padaiḥ |
prabhañjanastvamityevaṃ pāṭho niḥśvāsaśāsane || ^iast

> This is the seventh declaration, set in Ārya metre, (consisting of) fourteen words. The reading found in the Nihśvāsaśāīsana is ‘you are breath’.
