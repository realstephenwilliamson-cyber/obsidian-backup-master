## Mūla

लयाकलादौ नोवाच त्रायोदश्यादिकं विभुः ।
नन्वस्तु वेद्यता भावधर्मः किन्तु लयाकलौ ॥ ^mula

layākalādau novāca trāyodaśyādikaṃ vibhuḥ |
nanvastu vedyatā bhāvadharmaḥ kintu layākalau || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 12 - Chapter Ten, part 3#^pg397|p. 378]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=397>))
>
> Observing that they differ from Conditioned (perceivers), it is with this intention that the Omnipresent Lord has not said that the Dissolution Deconditioned (perceivers) etc. have thirteen etc. aspects. (with 10.131cd–132ab)
>
> Surely (an opponent may object that), even if perceptibility is conceded to be a property of an entity, the Dissolution Deconditioned and Consciousness Deconditioned (perceivers) are not conscious of anything, so how can that (perceptibility exist) in relation to them? (with 10.132cd–133ab)

*Lead-in:* अधुना तु तदेव घटयितुं प्रलयाकलविज्ञानाकलापेक्षया तन्मूलभूता वेद्यतैव नास्ति? -- इत्याशङ्कते -

> नन्वस्तु वेद्यता भावधर्मः किन्तु लयाकलौ ॥
> मन्वाते नेह वै किञ्चित्तदपेक्षा त्वसौ कथम् ।

प्रलयाकलविज्ञानाकलौ हि प्रसुप्तभुजगशून्यसमाधिस्थयोगिप्रायत्वान्न किञ्चिज्जानीतः, -- इति तयोर्वेदितृत्वमेव नास्तीत्याश्चर्यं तदपेक्षयापि कथङ्कारं वेद्यता भावधर्मः स्यात् ? ॥ १३२ ॥
