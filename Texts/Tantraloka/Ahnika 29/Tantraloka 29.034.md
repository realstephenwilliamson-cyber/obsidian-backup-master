## Mūla

सिल्लाई एरुणया तथा कुमारी च बोधाई ।
समहालच्छी चापरमेखलया शक्तयः षडिमाः ॥ ^mula

sillāī eruṇayā tathā kumārī ca bodhāī |
samahālacchī cāparamekhalayā śaktayaḥ ṣaḍimāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 09 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 1#^pg251|p. 234]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=251>))
>
> These six (princes) are, in due order, Amaranātha, Varadevanātha, Citranātha, Alinātha, Vindhyanātha and Guḍikanātha. These are (their) six consorts (śakti): Sillāī along with Eruṇā, Kumārī, Bhodāī and Mahālacchī, along with Aparamekhalā (respectively). (with 29.33cd–34)
