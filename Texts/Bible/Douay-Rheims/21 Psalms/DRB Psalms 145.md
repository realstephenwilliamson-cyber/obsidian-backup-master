# Psalms 145 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 144|← Psalms 144]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 146|Psalms 146 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 145|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**145:1** Alleluia, of Aggeus and Zacharias. ^145-1

**145:2** Praise the Lord, O my soul, in my life I will praise the Lord: I will sing to my God as long as I shall be. Put not your trust in princes: ^145-2

**145:3** In the children of men, in whom there is no salvation. ^145-3

**145:4** His spirit shall go forth, and he shall return into his earth: in that day all their thoughts shall perish. ^145-4

**145:5** Blessed is he who hath the God of Jacob for his helper, whose hope is in the Lord his God: ^145-5

**145:6** Who made heaven and earth, the sea, and all things that are in them. ^145-6

**145:7** Who keepeth truth for ever: who executeth judgment for them that suffer wrong: who giveth food to the hungry. The Lord looseth them that are fettered: ^145-7

**145:8** The Lord enlighteneth the blind. The Lord lifteth up them that are cast down: the Lord loveth the just. ^145-8

**145:9** The Lord keepeth the strangers, he will support the fatherless and the widow: and the ways of sinners he will destroy. ^145-9

**145:10** The Lord shall reign for ever: thy God, O Sion, unto generation and generation. ^145-10
