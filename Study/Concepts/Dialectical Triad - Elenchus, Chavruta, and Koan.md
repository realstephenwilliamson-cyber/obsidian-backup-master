# Dialectical Triad — Elenchus, Chavruta, and Koan

> [!abstract] Definition
> The convergent cross-cultural emergence in the Axial Age of paired, adversarial, and dissonant dialogical practices as the primary engine of spiritual and intellectual formation.

## Three Archetypes of Formative Friction
In [[Artificial Interlocutor text 2026-03-25|Artificial Interlocutor]] and [[Second Domestication outline 2026-07-07|The Second Domestication]]:
1. **Socratic *Elenchus* (Greece):** Rigorous interlocution that peels away unexamined premises, leaving the seeker in productive paralysis (*aporia*).
2. **Rabbinic *Chavruta* (Judaism):** Paired study in the *Bet Midrash* where peers duel across the open folio of the Talmud, operating on the premise that genuine understanding only emerges through sharpened contention (*bar-palgata*).
3. **Zen *Kōan* (Buddhism):** The master confronts the student with an insoluble paradoxical statement (e.g., the sound of one hand clapping), breaking the conceptual grip of the ego.
