## Mūla

अज्ञानमिति न ज्ञानाभावश्चातिप्रसङ्गतः ।
स हि लोष्टादिकेऽप्यस्ति न च तस्यास्ति संसृतिः ॥ ^mula

ajñānamiti na jñānābhāvaścātiprasaṅgataḥ |
sa hi loṣṭādike'pyasti na ca tasyāsti saṃsṛtiḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. I
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 01 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v01 05 - Chapter One, part 4#^pg138|p. 124]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 01.pdf#page=138>))
>
> (According to us,) ignorance is not a (total) lack of knowledge, for (if that were so, the use of the term) would be too extensive. That (ignorance) is also (present) in a stone and the like, and that is not subject to transmigration.
