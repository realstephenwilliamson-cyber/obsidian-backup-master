## Mūla

फलदानाक्षमे योगिन्युपायैकोपदेशिनि ।
वरं ज्ञानी योऽभ्युपायं दिशेदपि च मोचयेत् ॥ ^mula

phaladānākṣame yoginyupāyaikopadeśini |
varaṃ jñānī yo'bhyupāyaṃ diśedapi ca mocayet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 10 - Chapter Thirteen, part 6#^pg357|p. 335]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=357>))
>
> (A teacher) who is a man of knowledge (jñānin), who can teach the means (to liberation) and can liberate (others), is better than (a teacher who is) a yogi who cannot give the (desired worldly) fruits and can only teach the means (to attain them).

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* यः पुनः संप्राप्तो घटमानो वा योगी भुक्तिमात्रमपि दातुं न समर्थः परं तदुपायोपदेशकुशलः, तमनादृत्यापि ज्ञानी गुरुः कार्यो यस्तदुपायमुपदिशन् क्रमेण मोचयेदपि -- इत्याह --

> फलदानाक्षमे योगिन्युपायैकोपदेशिनि ।
> वरं ज्ञानी योऽभ्युपायं दिशेदपि च मोचयेत् ॥
