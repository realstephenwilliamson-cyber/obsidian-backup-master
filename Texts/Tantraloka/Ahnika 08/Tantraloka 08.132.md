## Mūla

वैद्युताद्रैवतस्तावांस्तत्र पुष्टिवहाम्बुदाः ।
ऊर्ध्वं च रोगाम्बुमुचः संवर्तास्तदनन्तरे ॥ ^mula

vaidyutādraivatastāvāṃstatra puṣṭivahāmbudāḥ |
ūrdhvaṃ ca rogāmbumucaḥ saṃvartāstadanantare || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 05 - Chapter Eight, part 3#^pg188|p. 170]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=188>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 8.132cd–133ab.
>
> At the same (distance above) Vidyūta is the wind Raivata. There (within it) are clouds that rain nourishment (puști). Just above that is the wind Saṁvarta, in which the clouds rain disease. There in (the wind) Raivata reside those who have attained (the magical powers of) yellow pigment (rocana), collyrium, ashes and the rest. (with 8.132–133ab)
