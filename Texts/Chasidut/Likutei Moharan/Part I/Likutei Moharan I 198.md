*Part I · Lesson 198* · [[Likutei Moharan]]

## 1

When a person screams out to God, they tell him to move. As it is written ([[Torah 16 - Beshalach#^14-15|Exodus 14:15]]), “Why are you crying out to Me? Speak to the Children of Israel and let them start moving.”
