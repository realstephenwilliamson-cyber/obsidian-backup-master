## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. I
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 01 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v01 13 - Chapter One, part 12#^pg485|p. 471]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 01.pdf#page=485>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 1.325cd–326ab.
>
> (The matters discussed) in the twenty-ninth chapter are: a) those who are fit (to perform Kaula rites), b) the Kula rite (kulakrama) dedicated to the Siddhas and (their) consorts, c) the procedure for the rite of worship (according to Mādhvakula), d) the procedure concerning the Tantric consort, e) the liturgy of the secret doctrine, f) initiation, g) consecration (of the adept and teacher), and h) the (initiation by) piercing (the inner centres). (with 1.323cd–324)
