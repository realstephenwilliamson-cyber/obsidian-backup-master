## Mūla

सर्वभावपरिक्षीणः परवेध उदाहृतः ।
मनुशक्तिभुवनरूपज्ञापिण्डस्थाननाडिपरभेदात् ॥ ^mula

sarvabhāvaparikṣīṇaḥ paravedha udāhṛtaḥ |
manuśaktibhuvanarūpajñāpiṇḍasthānanāḍiparabhedāt || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 13 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 5#^pg410|p. 393]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=410>))
>
> Other teachers who know the secret (teachings) reckon that there are nine types of (initiation by) piercing, classified as 1) Mantra, 2) Śakti, 3) (of the piercing of) the Worlds, 4) Form (rūpa),), 5) Knowledge (jñā), 6) the body (piṇḍa), 7) Location (sthāna), 8) the Channels (of the vital breath) (nādī), and 9) Supreme (para). (with 29.254cd–255ab)
