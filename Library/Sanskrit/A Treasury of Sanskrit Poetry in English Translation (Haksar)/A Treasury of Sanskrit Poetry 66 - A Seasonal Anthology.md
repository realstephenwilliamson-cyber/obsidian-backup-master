Early Winter. A bhinanda 

The round villages are charming now at day's end with threshing circles scattered on the common for treading of the heaped up rice; the dung tires cast a ring of smoke that hangs low overhead from weight of frost.

Later Winter: Anon. 

The heavy snow is falling, not easy to distinguish among the smoke-grey damanaka trees, but for the ti re of dung it forms a tent of beauty.
