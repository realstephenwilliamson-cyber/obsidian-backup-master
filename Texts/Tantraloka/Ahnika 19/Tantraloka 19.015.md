## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 10 - Chapter Nineteen#^pg227|p. 207]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=227>))
>
> Now here (according to others, this can be done) as is the animal sacrifice (paśukarman) described previously. Once entered the root somewhere, by means of one or other (of the groups of) the sixteen supports, the six wheels, the three targets, (or) the five voids, or elsewhere, whilst severing (the root), beginning with the bulb, etc., he should conjoin (the disciple to his) preferred abode by contemplating oneness and applying a full oblation. (with 19.15–16)
