## Chapter 97

**97:1** The LORD is king!  
Let the earth exult,  
the many islands rejoice! ^97-1

**97:2** Dense clouds are around Him,  
righteousness and justice are the base of His throne. ^97-2

**97:3** Fire is His vanguard,  
burning His foes on every side. ^97-3

**97:4** His lightnings light up the world;  
the earth is convulsed at the sight; ^97-4

**97:5** mountains melt like wax at the LORD’s presence,  
at the presence of the Lord of all the earth. ^97-5

**97:6** The heavens proclaim His righteousness  
and all peoples see His glory. ^97-6

**97:7** All who worship images,  
who vaunt their idols,  
are dismayed;  
all divine beings bow down to Him. ^97-7

**97:8** Zion, hearing it, rejoices,  
the towns[^1] of Judah exult,  
because of Your judgments, O LORD. ^97-8

**97:9** For You, LORD, are supreme over all the earth;  
You are exalted high above all divine beings. ^97-9

**97:10** O you who love the LORD, hate evil!  
He guards the lives of His loyal ones,  
saving them from the hand of the wicked. ^97-10

**97:11** Light is sown for the righteous,  
radiance[^2] for the upright. ^97-11

**97:12** O you righteous, rejoice in the LORD  
and acclaim His holy name! ^97-12

[^1]: Or “women.”
[^2]: Others “joy.”
