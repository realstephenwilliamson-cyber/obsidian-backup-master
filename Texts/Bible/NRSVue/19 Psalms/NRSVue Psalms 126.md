## A harvest of joy

*A Song of Ascents.*

**126:1** When the LORD restored the fortunes of Zion,^[Or *brought back those who returned to Zion*] we were like those who dream. ^126-1

**126:2** Then our mouth was filled with laughter and our tongue with shouts of joy; then it was said among the nations, “The LORD has done great things for them.” ^126-2

**126:3** The LORD has done great things for us, and we rejoiced. ^126-3

**126:4** Restore our fortunes, O LORD, like the watercourses in the Negeb. ^126-4

**126:5** May those who sow in tears reap with shouts of joy. ^126-5

**126:6** Those who go out weeping, bearing the seed for sowing, shall come home with shouts of joy, carrying their sheaves. ^126-6
