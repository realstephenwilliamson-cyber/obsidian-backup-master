## Mūla

व्यापिन्या तद्दिवारात्रं लीयते साप्यनाश्रिते ।
परार्धकोट्या हत्वापि शक्तिकालमनाश्रिते ॥ ^mula

vyāpinyā taddivārātraṃ līyate sāpyanāśrite |
parārdhakoṭyā hatvāpi śaktikālamanāśrite || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 12 - Chapter Six, part 5#^pg393|p. 378]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=393>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 6.164cd–165ab.
>
> When Energy (Śakti) reaches the end of its period (of existence), it dissolves into the Pervasive One (Vyāpinī), whose Day and Night is that (Energy’s lifespan). (The Pervasive One dissolves) into Śiva Without Support (anāśrita), whose Day and Night equals a day of Energy multiplied by ten million trillion (parārdhakoṭi). At the end of this period, multiplied by a trillion (parārdha), he too dissolves into the plane With Mind (sāmanasa), termed Equality (Sāmya). (with 6.163cd–165)
