## Mūla

उभयतो भ्रामयेत्तत्र यथाग्रे हाकृतिर्भवेत् ।
कोट्यां तत्र कृतं सूत्रं नयेद्रेखां तु पूर्विकाम् ॥ ^mula

ubhayato bhrāmayettatra yathāgre hākṛtirbhavet |
koṭyāṃ tatra kṛtaṃ sūtraṃ nayedrekhāṃ tu pūrvikām || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI

> One should rotate (a thread) there on both sides in such a way as (as to produce) the shape of (the letter) H in front (of the middle thread). Having a placed a thread there on (its) extremity, one should extend the line (produced) previously.
