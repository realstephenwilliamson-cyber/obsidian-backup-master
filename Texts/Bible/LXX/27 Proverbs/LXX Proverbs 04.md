# LXX Proverbs 04

← [[LXX Proverbs 03]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Proverbs 05]] →

*Proverbs · [[LXX]]*

**4:1** Hear, ye children, the instruction of a father, and attend to know understanding.  ^4-1

**4:2** For I give you a good gift; forsake ye not my law.  ^4-2

**4:3** For I also was a son obedient to *my* father, and loved in the sight of *my* mother:  ^4-3

**4:4** who spoke and instructed me, *saying*, Let our speech be fixed in thine heart, keep *our* commandments, forget them not:  ^4-4

**4:5** and do not neglect the speech of my mouth.[^1]  ^4-5

**4:6** And forsake it not, and it shall cleave to thee: love it, and it shall keep thee.  ^4-6

**4:8** Secure it, and it shall exalt thee: honour it, that it may embrace thee;[^2]  ^4-8

**4:9** that it may give unto thy head a crown of graces, and may cover thee with a crown of delight.  ^4-9

**4:10** Hear, *my* son, and receive my words; and the years of thy life shall be increased, that the resources of thy life may be many.  ^4-10

**4:11** For I teach thee the ways of wisdom; and I cause thee to go in right paths.[^3]  ^4-11

**4:12** For when thou goest, thy steps shall not be straitened; and when thou runnest, thou shalt not be distressed.  ^4-12

**4:13** Take hold of my instruction; let it not go,—but keep it for thyself for thy life.  ^4-13

**4:14** Go not in the ways of the ungodly, neither covet the ways of transgressors.  ^4-14

**4:15** In whatever place they shall pitch their camp, go not thither; but turn from them, and pass away.  ^4-15

**4:16** For they cannot sleep, unless they have done evil: their sleep is taken away, and they rest not.  ^4-16

**4:17** For these live upon the bread of ungodliness, and are drunken with wine of transgression.  ^4-17

**4:18** But the ways of the righteous shine like light; they go on and shine, until the day be fully come.[^4]  ^4-18

**4:19** But the ways of the ungodly are dark; they know not how they stumble.  ^4-19

**4:20** *My* son, attend to my speech; and apply thine ear to my words:  ^4-20

**4:21** that thy fountains may not fail thee; keep them in *thine* heart.  ^4-21

**4:22** For they are life to those that find them, and health to all *their* flesh.[^5]  ^4-22

**4:23** Keep thine heart with the utmost care; for out of these are the issues of life.  ^4-23

**4:24** Remove from thee a froward mouth, and put far away from thee unjust lips.  ^4-24

**4:25** Let thine eyes look right on, and let thine eyelids assent *to* just *things*.  ^4-25

**4:26** Make straight paths for thy feet, and order thy ways aright.  ^4-26

**4:27** Turn not aside to the right hand nor to the left, but turn away thy foot from an evil way: 27a [for God knows the ways on the right hand, but those on the left are crooked:] 27b and he will make thy ways straight, and will guide thy steps in peace.[^6]  ^4-27

[^1]: 4:5 See Appendix - Alexandrian codex has: “Get wisdom, get understanding, forget not, nor decline from the words of my mouth.”
[^2]: 4:8 *Lit.* dig a trench about her, see *Heb.*
[^3]: 4:11 *Gr.* wheel-tracks, see chap 2. 18; Heb. 12. 13.
[^4]: 4:18 *Gr.* order itself aright.
[^5]: 4:22 *Or,* healing.
[^6]: 4:27a *Heb.* omits.
