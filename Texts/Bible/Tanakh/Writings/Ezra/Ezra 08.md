## Chapter 8

**8:1** These are the chiefs of the clans and the register of the genealogy of those who came up with me from Babylon in the reign of King Artaxerxes: ^8-1

**8:2** Of the sons of Phinehas, Gershom; of the sons of Ithamar, Daniel; of the sons of David, Hattush. ^8-2

**8:3** Of the sons of Shecaniah: of the sons of Parosh, Zechariah; through him the genealogy of 150 males was registered. ^8-3

**8:4** Eliehoenai son of Zerahiah, of the sons of Pahath-moab, and with him 200 males. ^8-4

**8:5** Of the sons of Shecaniah son of Jahaziel; and with him 300 males. ^8-5

**8:6** And of the sons of Adin, Ebed son of Jonathan; and with him 50 males. ^8-6

**8:7** And of the sons of Elam, Jeshaiah son of Athaliah; and with him 70 males. ^8-7

**8:8** And of the sons of Shephatiah, Zebadiah son of Michael; and with him 80 males. ^8-8

**8:9** Of the sons of Joab, Obadiah son of Jehiel; and with him 218 males. ^8-9

**8:10** And of the sons of Shelomith, the son of Josiphiah; and with him 160 males. ^8-10

**8:11** And of the sons of Bebai, Zechariah son of Bebai; and with him 28 males. ^8-11

**8:12** And of the sons of Azgad, Johanan son of Hakkatan; and with him 110 males. ^8-12

**8:13** And of the sons of Adonikam, who were the last; and these are their names: Eliphelet, Jeiel, and Shemaiah; and with them 60 males. ^8-13

**8:14** And of the sons of Bigvai, Uthai and Zaccur; and with them 70 males. ^8-14

**8:15** These I assembled by the river that enters Ahava, and we encamped there for three days. I reviewed the people and the priests, but I did not find any Levites there. ^8-15

**8:16** I sent for Eliezer, Ariel, Shemaiah, Elnathan, Jarib, Elnathan, Nathan, Zechariah, and Meshullam, the leading men, and also for Joiarib and Elnathan, the instructors, ^8-16

**8:17** and I gave them an order for Iddo, the leader at the place [called] Casiphia. I gave them a message to convey to Iddo [and] his brother, temple-servants at the place [called] Casiphia, that they should bring us attendants for the House of our God. ^8-17

**8:18** Thanks to the benevolent care of our God for us, they brought us a capable man of the family of Mahli son of Levi son of Israel, and Sherebiah and his sons and brothers, 18 in all, ^8-18

**8:19** and Hashabiah, and with him Jeshaiah of the family of Merari, his brothers and their sons, 20 in all; ^8-19

**8:20** and of the temple servants whom David and the officers had appointed for the service of the Levites—220 temple servants, all of them listed by name. ^8-20

**8:21** I proclaimed a fast there by the Ahava River to afflict ourselves before our God to beseech Him for a smooth journey for us and for our children and for all our possessions; ^8-21

**8:22** for I was ashamed to ask the king for soldiers and horsemen to protect us against any enemy on the way, since we had told the king, “The benevolent care of our God is for all who seek Him, while His fierce anger is against all who forsake Him.” ^8-22

**8:23** So we fasted and besought our God for this, and He responded to our plea. ^8-23

**8:24** Then I selected twelve of the chiefs of the priests, namely Sherebiah and Hashabiah with ten of their brothers, ^8-24

**8:25** and I weighed out to them the silver, the gold, and the vessels, the contribution to the House of our God which the king, his counselors and officers, and all Israel who were present had made. ^8-25

**8:26** I entrusted to their safekeeping the weight of six hundred and fifty talents of silver, one hundred silver vessels of one talent each, one hundred talents of gold; ^8-26

**8:27** also, twenty gold bowls worth one thousand darics and two vessels of good, shining bronze, as precious as gold. ^8-27

**8:28** I said to them, “You are consecrated to the LORD, and the vessels are consecrated, and the silver and gold are a freewill offering to the LORD God of your fathers. ^8-28

**8:29** Guard them diligently until such time as you weigh them out in the presence of the officers of the priests and the Levites and the officers of the clans of Israel in Jerusalem in the chambers of the House of the LORD.” ^8-29

**8:30** So the priests and the Levites received the cargo of silver and gold and vessels by weight, to bring them to Jerusalem to the House of our God. ^8-30

**8:31** We set out for Jerusalem from the Ahava River on the twelfth of the first month. We enjoyed the care of our God, who saved us from enemy ambush on the journey. ^8-31

**8:32** We arrived in Jerusalem and stayed there three days. ^8-32

**8:33** On the fourth day the silver, gold, and vessels were weighed out in the House of our God into the keeping of Meremoth son of Uriah the priest, with whom was Eleazar son of Phinehas. Jozabad son of Jeshua, and Noadiah son of Binnui, the Levites, were with them. ^8-33

**8:34** Everything accorded as to number and weight, the entire cargo being recorded at that time. ^8-34

**8:35** The returning exiles who arrived from captivity made burnt offerings to the God of Israel: twelve bulls for all Israel, ninety-six rams, seventy-seven lambs and twelve he-goats as a purification offering, all this a burnt offering to the LORD. ^8-35

**8:36** They handed the royal orders to the king’s satraps and the governors of the province of Beyond the River who gave support to the people and the House of God. ^8-36
