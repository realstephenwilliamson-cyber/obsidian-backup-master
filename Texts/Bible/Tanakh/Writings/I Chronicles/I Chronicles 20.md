## Chapter 20

**20:1** [^1]At the turn of the year, the season when kings go out [to battle], Joab led out the army force and devastated the land of Ammon, and then besieged Rabbah, while David remained in Jerusalem; Joab reduced Rabbah and left it in ruins. ^20-1

**20:2** David took the crown from the head of their king; he found that it weighed a talent of gold, and in it were precious stones. It was placed on David’s head. He also carried off a vast amount of booty from the city. ^20-2

**20:3** He led out the people who lived there and he hacked them[^2] with saws and iron threshing boards and axes;[^3] David did thus to all the towns of Ammon. Then David and all the troops returned to Jerusalem. ^20-3

**20:4** After this, fighting broke out with the Philistines at Gezer; that was when Sibbecai the Hushathite killed Sippai, a descendant of the Rephaim, and they were humbled. ^20-4

**20:5** Again there was fighting with the Philistines, and Elhanan son of Jair killed Lahmi, the brother of Goliath the Gittite; his spear had a shaft like a weaver’s beam. ^20-5

**20:6** Once again there was fighting at Gath. There was a giant of a man who had twenty-four fingers [and toes], six [on each hand] and six [on each foot]; he too was descended from the Raphah. ^20-6

**20:7** When he taunted Israel, Jonathan son of David’s brother Shimea killed him. ^20-7

**20:8** These were descended from the Raphah in Gath, and they fell by the hands of David and his men. ^20-8

[^1]: With vv. 1–3, cf. 2 Sam. 11.1; 12.30–31.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain. 2 Sam. 12.31 has “set them to work.”
[^3]: Heb. megeroth; cf. 2 Sam. 12.31 magzeroth, “axes.”
