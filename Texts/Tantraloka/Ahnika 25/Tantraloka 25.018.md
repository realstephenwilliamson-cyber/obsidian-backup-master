## Mūla

तथोक्तदेवपूजादिचक्रयागान्तकर्मणा ।
रुद्रत्वमेत्यसौ जन्तुर्भोगान्दिव्यान्समश्नुते ॥ ^mula

tathoktadevapūjādicakrayāgāntakarmaṇā |
rudratvametyasau janturbhogāndivyānsamaśnute || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 17 - Chapter Twenty-Five (continued) and Chapter Twenty-Six#^pg383|p. 363]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=383>))
>
> And the same is said in the venerable Mukuṭa (where we read): ‘he who has been a follower of Śiva’s scriptures and returns to those of the Veda is, like a broken bell, that is, nothing at all. In the same way, thanks to the worship of the deity (in the manner) described in the beginning, and the sacrifice of the assembly (cakrayāga) at the end, the (deceased) person assumes Rudra’s state, and attains divine benefits (divyabhoga) (thereby).’ (with 25.16–18)

## Viveka

> [!viveka]- Viveka in English (Dyczkowski, vol. IX, pp. 363–364)
> ![[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 17 - Chapter Twenty-Five (continued) and Chapter Twenty-Six#Viveka on 25.16–18]]

> श्रीमौकुटे तथा चोक्तं शिवशास्त्रे स्थितोऽपि यः ।
> प्रत्येति वैदिके भग्नघण्टावन्न स किञ्चन ॥
> तथोक्तदेवपूजादिचक्रयागान्तकर्मणा ।
> रुद्रत्वमेत्यसौ जन्तुर्भोगान्दिव्यान्समश्नुते ॥

भग्नघण्टावदिति--भग्ना हि घण्टा न स्वं कार्यं कुर्यात्, नापि लौहमित्युभयभ्रष्टतामेव आसादयेत्--इत्यर्थः । अत एवोक्तम्--न स किञ्चनेति ॥ १८ ॥
