# Psalms 129 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 128|← Psalms 128]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 130|Psalms 130 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 129|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**129:1** Out of the depths I have cried to thee, O Lord: ^129-1

**129:2** Lord, hear my voice. Let thy ears be attentive to the voice of my supplication. ^129-2

**129:3** If thou, O Lord, wilt mark iniquities: Lord, who shall stand it. ^129-3

**129:4** For with thee there is merciful forgiveness: and by reason of thy law, I have waited for thee, O Lord. My soul hath relied on his word: ^129-4

**129:5** My soul hath hoped in the Lord. ^129-5

**129:6** From the morning watch even until night, let Israel hope in the Lord. ^129-6

**129:7** Because with the Lord there is mercy: and with him plentiful redemption. ^129-7

**129:8** And he shall redeem Israel from all his iniquities. ^129-8
