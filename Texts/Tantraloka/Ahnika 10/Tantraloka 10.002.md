## Mūla

तेषाममीषां तत्त्वानां स्ववर्गेष्वनुगामिनाम् ।
भेदान्तरमपि प्रोक्तं शास्त्रेऽत्र श्रीत्रिकाभिधे ॥ ^mula

teṣāmamīṣāṃ tattvānāṃ svavargeṣvanugāminām |
bhedāntaramapi proktaṃ śāstre'tra śrītrikābhidhe || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 10 - Chapter Ten, part 1#^pg305|p. 286]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=305>))
>
> Here in the scripture (Śāstra) called Śrītrika (i.e. the Maālinīvijayottara), another (way) also has been taught of dividing up (and classifying) these? principles, which inhere in (and pervade) (anugāmin)? their respective groups (of particulars, of which they are the universals).
