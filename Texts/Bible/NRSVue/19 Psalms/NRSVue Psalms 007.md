# Psalm 7 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 006|← Psalm 6]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 008|Psalm 8 →]]

## Plea for help against persecutors

*A Shiggaion of David, which he sang to the LORD concerning Cush, a Benjaminite.*

**7:1** O LORD my God, in you I take refuge; save me from all my pursuers, and deliver me, ^7-1

**7:2** or like a lion they will tear me apart; they will drag me away, with no one to rescue. ^7-2

**7:3** O LORD my God, if I have done this, if there is wrong in my hands, ^7-3

**7:4** if I have repaid my ally with harm or plundered my foe without cause, ^7-4

**7:5** then let the enemy pursue and overtake me, trample my life to the ground, and lay my soul in the dust. *Selah* ^7-5

**7:6** Rise up, O LORD, in your anger; lift yourself up against the fury of my enemies; awake, O my God;^[Or *awake for me*] you have appointed a judgment. ^7-6

**7:7** Let the assembly of the peoples be gathered around you, and over it take your seat^[Cn: Heb *return*] on high. ^7-7

**7:8** The LORD judges the peoples; judge me, O LORD, according to my righteousness and according to the integrity that is in me. ^7-8

**7:9** O let the evil of the wicked come to an end, but establish the righteous, you who test the minds and hearts, O righteous God. ^7-9

**7:10** God is my shield, who saves the upright in heart. ^7-10

**7:11** God is a righteous judge and a God who has indignation every day. ^7-11

**7:12** If one does not repent, God^[Heb *he*] will whet his sword; he has bent and strung his bow; ^7-12

**7:13** he has prepared his deadly weapons, making his arrows fiery shafts. ^7-13

**7:14** See how they conceive evil and are pregnant with mischief and bring forth lies. ^7-14

**7:15** They make a pit, digging it out, and fall into the hole that they have made. ^7-15

**7:16** Their mischief returns upon their own heads, and on their own heads their violence descends. ^7-16

**7:17** I will give to the LORD the thanks due to his righteousness and sing praise to the name of the LORD, the Most High. ^7-17
