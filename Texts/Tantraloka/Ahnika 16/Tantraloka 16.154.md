## Mūla

षडङ्गी सकलान्यत्वाद् द्विविधा वक्त्रवत्पुनः ।
द्वादशत्वेन गुणिता चतुर्विंशतिभेदिका ॥ ^mula

ṣaḍaṅgī sakalānyatvād dvividhā vaktravatpunaḥ |
dvādaśatvena guṇitā caturviṃśatibhedikā || ^iast

> 4) The energy (śakti) with five faces becomes sixfold by being divided in the same way, and then again twofold, according to whether she is alone (ekāki) or coupled (yāmalatva), and so she is in this way twelve. a2) 5) Possessing six limbs, she is differentiated (sakala) or otherwise, and so is of two kinds. Again, like the (six) faces, she is multiplied by twelve, and so she is divided into twenty-four (varieties). (with 16.151cd–154)
>
> 8) (By adding its aspect) as a single deity (ekavīra), it abides as fourteen. (with 16.151cd–156)
