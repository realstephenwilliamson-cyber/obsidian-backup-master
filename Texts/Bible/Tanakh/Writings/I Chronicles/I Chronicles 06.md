## Chapter 6

**6:1** The sons of Levi: Gershom, Kohath, and Merari. ^6-1

**6:2** And these are the names of the sons of Gershom: Libni and Shimei. ^6-2

**6:3** The sons of Kohath: Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel. ^6-3

**6:4** The sons of Merari: Mahli and Mushi. These were the families of the Levites according to their clans. ^6-4

**6:5** Of Gershom: his son Libni, his son Jahath, his son Zimmah, ^6-5

**6:6** his son Joah, his son Iddo, his son Zerah, his son Jeatherai. ^6-6

**6:7** The sons of Kohath: his son Amminadab, his son Korah, his son Assir, ^6-7

**6:8** his son Elkanah, his son Ebiasaph, his son Assir, ^6-8

**6:9** his son Tahath, his son Uriel, his son Uzziah, and his son Shaul. ^6-9

**6:10** The sons of Elkanah: Amasai and Ahimoth, ^6-10

**6:11** his son Elkanah, his son Zophai, his son Nahath, ^6-11

**6:12** his son Eliab, his son Jeroham, his son Elkanah. ^6-12

**6:13** The sons of Samuel: his first-born Vashni, and[^1] Abijah. ^6-13

**6:14** The sons of Merari: Mahli, his son Libni, his son Shimei, his son Uzzah, ^6-14

**6:15** his son Shimea, his son Haggiah, and his son Asaiah. ^6-15

**6:16** These were appointed by David to be in charge of song in the House of the LORD, from the time the Ark came to rest. ^6-16

**6:17** They served at the Tabernacle of the Tent of Meeting with song until Solomon built the House of the LORD in Jerusalem; and they carried out their duties as prescribed for them. ^6-17

**6:18** Those were the appointed men; and their sons were: the Kohathites: Heman the singer, son of Joel son of Samuel ^6-18

**6:19** son of Elkanah son of Jeroham son of Eliel son of Toah ^6-19

**6:20** son of Zuph son of Elkanah son of Mahath son of Amasai ^6-20

**6:21** son of Elkanah son of Joel son of Azariah son of Zephaniah ^6-21

**6:22** son of Tahath son of Assir son of Ebiasaph son of Korah ^6-22

**6:23** son of Izhar son of Kohath son of Levi son of Israel; ^6-23

**6:24** and his kinsman Asaph, who stood on his right, namely, Asaph son of Berechiah son of Shimea ^6-24

**6:25** son of Michael son of Baaseiah son of Malchijah ^6-25

**6:26** son of Ethni son of Zerah son of Adaiah ^6-26

**6:27** son of Ethan son of Zimmah son of Shimei ^6-27

**6:28** son of Jahath son of Gershom son of Levi. ^6-28

**6:29** On the left were their kinsmen: the sons of Merari: Ethan son of Kishi son of Abdi son of Malluch ^6-29

**6:30** son of Hashabiah son of Amaziah son of Hilkiah ^6-30

**6:31** son of Amzi son of Bani son of Shemer ^6-31

**6:32** son of Mahli son of Mushi son of Merari son of Levi; ^6-32

**6:33** and their kinsmen the Levites were appointed for all the service of the Tabernacle of the House of God. ^6-33

**6:34** But Aaron and his sons made offerings upon the altar of burnt offering and upon the altar of incense, performing all the tasks of the most holy place, to make atonement for Israel, according to all that Moses the servant of God had commanded. ^6-34

**6:35** These are the sons of Aaron: his son Eleazar, his son Phinehas, his son Abishua, ^6-35

**6:36** his son Bukki, his son Uzzi, his son Zerahiah, ^6-36

**6:37** his son Meraioth, his son Amariah, his son Ahitub, ^6-37

**6:38** his son Zadok, his son Ahimaaz. ^6-38

**6:39** [^2]These are their dwelling-places according to their settlements within their borders: to the sons of Aaron of the families of Kohathites, for theirs was the [first] lot; ^6-39

**6:40** they gave them Hebron in the land of Judah and its surrounding pasturelands, ^6-40

**6:41** but the fields of the city and its villages they gave to Caleb son of Jephunneh. ^6-41

**6:42** To the sons of Aaron they gave the cities[^3] of refuge: Hebron and Libnah with its pasturelands, Jattir and Eshtemoa with its pasturelands, ^6-42

**6:43** Hilen with its pasturelands, Debir with its pasturelands, ^6-43

**6:44** Ashan with its pasturelands, and Beth-shemesh with its pasturelands. ^6-44

**6:45** From the tribe of Benjamin, Geba with its pasturelands, Alemeth with its pasturelands, and Anathoth with its pasturelands. All their cities throughout their families were thirteen. ^6-45

**6:46** To the remaining Kohathites were given by lot out of the family of the tribe, out of the half-tribe, the half of Manasseh, ten cities. ^6-46

**6:47** To the Gershomites according to their families were allotted thirteen cities out of the tribes of Issachar, Asher, Naphtali, and Manasseh in Bashan. ^6-47

**6:48** To the Merarites according to their families were allotted twelve cities out of the tribes of Reuben, Gad, and Zebulun. ^6-48

**6:49** So the people of Israel gave the Levites the cities with their pasturelands. ^6-49

**6:50** They gave them by lot out of the tribe of the Judahites these cities that are mentioned by name, and out of the tribe of the Simeonites, and out of the tribe of the Benjaminites. ^6-50

**6:51** And some of the families of the sons of Kohath had cities of their territory out of the tribe of Ephraim. ^6-51

**6:52** They gave them the cities of refuge: Shechem with its pasturelands in the hill country of Ephraim, Gezer with its pasturelands, ^6-52

**6:53** Jokmeam with its pasturelands, Beth-horon with its pasturelands, ^6-53

**6:54** Aijalon with its pasturelands, Gath-rimmon with its pasturelands; ^6-54

**6:55** and out of the half-tribe of Manasseh: Aner with its pasturelands, and Bileam with its pasturelands, for the rest of the families of the Kohathites. ^6-55

**6:56** To the Gershomites; out of the half-tribe of Manasseh: Golan in Bashan with its pasturelands and Ashtaroth with its pasturelands; ^6-56

**6:57** and out of the tribe of Issachar: Kedesh with its pasturelands, Dobrath with its pasturelands, ^6-57

**6:58** Ramoth with its pasturelands, and Anem with its pasturelands; ^6-58

**6:59** out of the tribe of Asher: Mashal with its pasturelands, Abdon with its pasturelands, ^6-59

**6:60** Hukok with its pasturelands, and Rehob with its pasturelands; ^6-60

**6:61** and out of the tribe of Naphtali: Kedesh in Galilee with its pasturelands; Hammon with its pasturelands, and Kiriathaim with its pasturelands. ^6-61

**6:62** To the rest of the Merarites, out of the tribe of Zebulun: Rimmono with its pasturelands, Tabor with its pasturelands; ^6-62

**6:63** and beyond the Jordan at Jericho, on the east side of the Jordan, out of the tribe of Reuben: Bezer in the wilderness with its pasturelands, Jahaz with its pasturelands, ^6-63

**6:64** Kedemoth with its pasturelands, and Mephaath with its pasturelands; ^6-64

**6:65** and out of the tribe of Gad: Ramoth in Gilead with its pasturelands, Mahanaim with its pasturelands, ^6-65

**6:66** Heshbon with its pasturelands, and Jazer with its pasturelands. ^6-66

[^1]: Some ancient vv. read “Joel, and the second”; cf. 1 Sam. 8.2.
[^2]: With vv. 24–51, cf. Josh. 21.3–42.
[^3]: Josh. 21.13, “city.”
