## Mūla

भेदः परः कलादीनां भुवनत्वेन यः स्थितः ।
असृजत्तमसावेव भोगिनां भोगसिद्धये ॥ ^mula

bhedaḥ paraḥ kalādīnāṁ bhuvanatvena yaḥ sthitaḥ |
asṛjat tam asāv eva bhogināṁ bhogasiddhaye || ^iast

## Translation

> p. 46 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=22>))

> He emitted the other division (*bheda*) of the Force (of limited agency) and the rest (of the reality levels) that exist as the world orders so that the worldly experience (*bhoga*) of those given to worldly experience may be attained (by them).
