# LXX Psalms 142

← [[LXX Psalms 141]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 143]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 143]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 143]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**142:1** A Psalm of David, when his son pursued him. O Lord, attend to my prayer: hearken to my supplication in thy truth; hear me in thy righteousness.  ^142-1

**142:2** And enter not into judgment with thy servant, for in thy sight shall no *man* living be justified.  ^142-2

**142:3** For the enemy has persecuted my soul; he has brought my life down to the ground; he has made me to dwell in a dark *place*, as those that have been long dead.  ^142-3

**142:4** Therefore my spirit was grieved in me; my heart was troubled within me.  ^142-4

**142:5** I remembered the days of old; and I meditated on all thy doings: *yea*, I meditated on the works of thine hands.  ^142-5

**142:6** I spread forth my hands to thee; my soul *thirsts* for thee, as a dry land. Pause.  ^142-6

**142:7** Hear me speedily, O Lord; my spirit has failed; turn not away thy face from me, else I shall be like to them that go down to the pit.  ^142-7

**142:8** Cause me to hear thy mercy in the morning; for I have hoped in thee; make known to me, O Lord, the way wherein I should walk; for I have lifted up my soul to thee.  ^142-8

**142:9** Deliver me from mine enemies, O Lord; for I have fled to thee for refuge.  ^142-9

**142:10** Teach me to do thy will; for thou art my God; thy good Spirit shall guide me in the straight *way*.[^1]  ^142-10

**142:11** Thou shalt quicken me, O Lord, for thy name's sake; in thy righteousness thou shalt bring my soul out of affliction.  ^142-11

**142:12** And in thy mercy thou wilt destroy mine enemies, and wilt destroy all those that afflict my soul; for I am thy servant.  ^142-12

[^1]: 142:10 *Alex.* as *Heb.*'land of uprightness.'
