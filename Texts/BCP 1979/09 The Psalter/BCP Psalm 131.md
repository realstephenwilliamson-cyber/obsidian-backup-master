# Psalm 131

← [[BCP Psalm 130]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 132]] →

*Domine, non est* · Book Five · Twenty-seventh Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 131]]

**131:1** O LORD, I am not proud; \*  
I have no haughty looks. ^131-1

**131:2** I do not occupy myself with great matters, \*  
or with things that are too hard for me. ^131-2

**131:3** But I still my soul and make it quiet,  
like a child upon its mother's breast; \*  
my soul is quieted within me. ^131-3

**131:4** O Israel, wait upon the LORD, \*  
from this time forth for evermore. ^131-4
