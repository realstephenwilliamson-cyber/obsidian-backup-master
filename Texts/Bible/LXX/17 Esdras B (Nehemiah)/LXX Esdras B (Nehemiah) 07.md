# LXX Esdras B (Nehemiah) 07

← [[LXX Esdras B (Nehemiah) 06]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Esdras B (Nehemiah) 08]] →

*Esdras B (Nehemiah) · [[LXX]]*

**7:1** And it came to pass, when the wall was built, and I had set up the doors, and the porters and the singers and the Levites were appointed,  ^7-1

**7:2** that I gave charge to Ananias my brother, and Ananias the ruler of the palace, over Jerusalem: for he was a true man, and one that feared God beyond many.  ^7-2

**7:3** And I said to them, The gates of Jerusalem shall not be opened till sunrise; and while they are still watching, let the doors be shut, and bolted; and set watches of them that dwell in Jerusalem, *every* man at his post, and *every* man over against his house.  ^7-3

**7:4** Now the city *was* wide and large; and the people *were* few in it, and the houses were not built.  ^7-4

**7:5** And God put *it* into my heart, and I gathered the nobles, and the rulers, and the people, into companies: and I found a register of the company that came up first, and I found written in it as follows:  ^7-5

**7:6** Now these *are* the children of the country, that came up from captivity, of the number which Nabuchodonosor king of Babylon carried away, and they returned to Jerusalem and to Juda, *every* man to his city;  ^7-6

**7:7** with Zorobabel, and Jesus, and Neemia, Azaria, and Reelma, Naemani, Mardochaeus, Balsan, Maspharath, Esdra, Boguia, Inaum, Baana, Masphar, men of the people of Israel.  ^7-7

**7:8** The children of Phoros, two thousand one hundred and seventy-two.  ^7-8

**7:9** The children of Saphatia, three hundred and seventy-two.  ^7-9

**7:10** The children of Era, six hundred and fifty-two.  ^7-10

**7:11** The children of Phaath Moab, with the children of Jesus and Joab, two thousand six hundred and eighteen.  ^7-11

**7:12** The children of Aelam, a thousand two hundred and fifty-four.  ^7-12

**7:13** The children of Zathuia, eight hundred and forty-five.  ^7-13

**7:14** The children of Zacchu, seven hundred and sixty.  ^7-14

**7:15** The children of Banui, six hundred and forty-eight.  ^7-15

**7:16** The children of Bebi, six hundred and twenty-eight.  ^7-16

**7:17** The children of Asgad, two thousand three hundred and twenty-two.  ^7-17

**7:18** The children of Adonicam, six hundred and sixty-seven.  ^7-18

**7:19** The children of Bagoi, two thousand and sixty-seven.  ^7-19

**7:20** The children of Edin, six hundred and fifty-five.  ^7-20

**7:21** The children of Ater, *the son* of Ezekias, ninety-eight.  ^7-21

**7:22** The children of Esam, three hundred and twenty-eight.  ^7-22

**7:23** The children of Besei, three hundred and twenty-four.  ^7-23

**7:24** The children of Ariph, a hundred and twelve: the children of Asen, two hundred and twenty-three.  ^7-24

**7:25** The children of Gabaon, ninety-five.  ^7-25

**7:26** The children of Baethalem, a hundred and twenty-three: the children of Atopha, fifty-six.  ^7-26

**7:27** The children of Anathoth, a hundred and twenty-eight.  ^7-27

**7:28** The men of Bethasmoth, forty-two.  ^7-28

**7:29** The men of Cariatharim, Caphira, and Beroth, seven hundred and forty-three.  ^7-29

**7:30** The men of Arama and Gabaa, six hundred and twenty.  ^7-30

**7:31** The men of Machemas, a hundred and twenty-two.  ^7-31

**7:32** The men of Baethel and Ai, a hundred and twenty-three.  ^7-32

**7:33** The men of Nabia, a hundred an fifty-two.  ^7-33

**7:34** The men of Elamaar, one thousand two hundred and fifty-two.  ^7-34

**7:35** The children of Eram, three hundred and twenty.  ^7-35

**7:36** The children of Jericho, three hundred and forty-five.  ^7-36

**7:37** The children of Lodadid and Ono, seven hundred and twenty-one.  ^7-37

**7:38** The children of Sanana, three thousand nine hundred and thirty.  ^7-38

**7:39** The priests; the sons of Jodae, *pertaining* to the house of Jesus, nine hundred and seventy-three.  ^7-39

**7:40** The children of Emmer, one thousand and fifty-two.  ^7-40

**7:41** The children of Phaseur, one thousand two hundred and forty-seven.  ^7-41

**7:42** The children of Eram, a thousand and seventeen.  ^7-42

**7:43** The Levites; the children of Jesus the son of Cadmiel, with the children of Uduia, seventy-four.  ^7-43

**7:44** The singers; the children of Asaph, a hundred and forty-eight.  ^7-44

**7:45** The porters; the children of Salum, the children of Ater, the children of Telmon, the children of Acub, the children of Atita, the children of Sabi, a hundred and thirty-eight.  ^7-45

**7:46** The Nathinim; the children of Sea, the children of Aspha, the children of Tabaoth,  ^7-46

**7:47** the children of Kiras, the children of Asuia, the children of Phadon,  ^7-47

**7:48** the children of Labana, the children of Agaba, the children of Selmei,  ^7-48

**7:49** the children of Anan, the children of Gadel, the children of Gaar,  ^7-49

**7:50** the children of Raaia, the children of Rasson, the children of Necoda,  ^7-50

**7:51** the children of Gezam, the children of Ozi, the children of Phese,  ^7-51

**7:52** the children of Besi, the children of Meinon, the children of Nephosasi,  ^7-52

**7:53** the children of Bacbuc, the children of Achipha, the children of Arur,  ^7-53

**7:54** the children of Basaloth, the children of Mida, the children of Adasan,  ^7-54

**7:55** the children of Barcue, the children of Sisarath, the children of Thema,  ^7-55

**7:56** the children of Nisia, the children of Atipha.  ^7-56

**7:57** The children of the servants of Solomon; the children of Sutei, the children of Sapharat, the children of Pherida,  ^7-57

**7:58** the children of Jelel, the children of Dorcon, the children of Gadael,  ^7-58

**7:59** the children of Saphatia, the children of Ettel, the children of Phacarath, the children of Sabaim, the children of Emim.  ^7-59

**7:60** All the Nathinim, and children of the servants of Solomon, *were* three hundred and ninety-two.  ^7-60

**7:61** And these went up from Thelmeleth, Thelaresa, Charub, Eron, Jemer: but they could not declare the houses of their families, or their seed, whether they were of Israel.  ^7-61

**7:62** The children of Dalaia, the children of Tobia, the children of Necoda, six hundred and forty-two.  ^7-62

**7:63** And of the priests; the children of Ebia, the children of Acos, the children of Berzelli, for they took wives of the daughters of Berzelli the Galaadite, and they were called by their name.  ^7-63

**7:64** These sought the pedigree of their company, and it was not found, and they were removed *as polluted* from the priesthood.  ^7-64

**7:65** And the Athersastha said, that they should not eat of the most holy things, until a priest should stand up to give light.  ^7-65

**7:66** And all the congregation was about forty-two thousand three hundred and sixty,  ^7-66

**7:67** besides their men-servants and their maid-servants: these were seven thousand three hundred and thirty seven: and the singing-men and singing-women, two hundred and forty-five.  ^7-67

**7:69** Two thousand seven hundred asses.  ^7-69

**7:70** And part of the heads of families gave into the treasury to Neemias for the work a thousand pieces of gold, fifty bowls, and thirty priests' *garments*.  ^7-70

**7:71** And *some* of the heads of families gave into the treasuries of the work, twenty thousand pieces of gold, and two thousand three hundred pounds of silver.  ^7-71

**7:72** And the rest of the people gave twenty thousand pieces of gold, and two thousand two hundred pounds of silver, and sixty-seven priests' *garments*.  ^7-72

**7:73** And the priests, and Levites, and porters, and singers, and *some* of the people, and the Nathinim, and all Israel, dwelt in their cities.  ^7-73
