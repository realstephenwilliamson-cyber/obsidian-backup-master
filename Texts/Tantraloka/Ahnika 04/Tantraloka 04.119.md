## Mūla

सैवाशुद्धिः पराख्याता शुद्धिस्तद्धीविमर्दनम् ।
एवं स्वदेहं बोधैकपात्रं गलितभेदकम् ॥ ^mula

saivāśuddhiḥ parākhyātā śuddhistaddhīvimardanam |
evaṃ svadehaṃ bodhaikapātraṃ galitabhedakam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. III
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 03 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v03 05 - Chapter Four, part 4#^pg151|p. 138]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 03.pdf#page=151>))
>
> E) The supreme form of impurity (aśuddhi) is said to be the (persistent) notion (buddhi) of separation (from Śiva that prevails) within (the experience of) these (metaphysical principles deployed in the body, although they are in fact) Śiva by nature. Purity is the obliteration of that notion. (with 4.118cd–119ab)
>
> F) Beholding his own body in this way, as the unique vessel (pātra) of (awakened) consciousness, free of duality, the (yogi) master (of all) (prabhu) abides in a state of (pure) free consciousness alone. (with 4.119cd–120ab)
