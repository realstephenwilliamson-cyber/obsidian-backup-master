# LXX Psalms 121

← [[LXX Psalms 120]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 122]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 122]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 122]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**121:1** A Song of Degrees. I was glad when they said to me, Let us go into the house of the Lord.  ^121-1

**121:2** Our feet stood in thy courts, O Jerusalem.  ^121-2

**121:3** Jerusalem is built as a city whose fellowship is complete.  ^121-3

**121:4** For thither the tribes went up, the tribes of the Lord, as a testimony for Israel, to give thanks unto the name of the Lord.  ^121-4

**121:5** For there are set thrones for judgment, *even* thrones for the house of David.  ^121-5

**121:6** Pray now for the peace of Jerusalem: and *let there be* prosperity to them that love thee.[^1]  ^121-6

**121:7** Let peace, I pray, be within thine host, and prosperity in thy palaces.  ^121-7

**121:8** For the sake of my brethren and my neighbours, I have indeed spoken peace concerning thee.  ^121-8

**121:9** Because of the house of the Lord our God, I have diligently sought thy good.  ^121-9

[^1]: 121:6 *Or,* ask Jerusalem how it is with her.
