## Mūla

अन्योन्यं गुरुसन्तानो यः शिवज्ञाननिष्ठितः ।
इत्थं स्थिते त्रयं मुख्यं कारणं सहकारि च ॥ ^mula

anyonyaṃ gurusantāno yaḥ śivajñānaniṣṭhitaḥ |
itthaṃ sthite trayaṃ mukhyaṃ kāraṇaṃ sahakāri ca || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 05 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 3#^pg111|p. 94]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=111>))
>
> Thus, as the cognitive continuum is primary (and fundamental in this case), this lineage of teachers, which is grounded in the knowledge of Śiva, differs from? a lineage (based on common) parentage. This being so, three (factors are) primary (in this case), namely, the (basic) cause (which is the teacher), the attendant cause (that aids it) (sahākārin), (which is the teacher’s wife,) and the product of the one cause, (that is, the disciples). Such is the teacher’s entourage. (with 28.207cd–209ab)

## Viveka

> तेन विज्ञानसन्तानप्राधान्याद्यौनसन्ततेः ॥
> अन्योन्यं गुरुसन्तानो यः शिवज्ञाननिष्ठितः ।
> इत्थं स्थिते त्रयं मुख्यं कारणं सहकारि च ॥
> एककारणकार्यं च वस्त्वित्येष गुरोर्गणः ।

इत्थं स्थित इति--यौनसन्ततेर्गुणभावेन गुरुसन्ततेरेव प्राधान्ये न्यायये--इत्यर्थः ॥
