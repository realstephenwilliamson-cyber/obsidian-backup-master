# LXX Psalms 111

← [[LXX Psalms 110]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 112]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 112]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 112]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**111:1** Alleluia. Blessed is the man that fears the Lord: he will delight greatly in his commandments.  ^111-1

**111:2** His seed shall be mighty in the earth: the generation of the upright shall be blessed.  ^111-2

**111:3** Glory and riches shall be in his house; and his righteousness endures for evermore.  ^111-3

**111:4** To the upright light has sprung up in darkness: he is pitiful, and merciful, and righteous.  ^111-4

**111:5** The good man is he that pities and lends: he will direct his affairs with judgment.  ^111-5

**111:6** For he shall not be moved for ever; the righteous shall be in everlasting remembrance.  ^111-6

**111:7** He shall not be afraid of *any* evil report: his heart is ready to trust in the Lord.  ^111-7

**111:8** His heart is established, he shall not fear, till he shall see *his desire* upon his enemies.  ^111-8

**111:9** He has dispersed abroad; he has given to the poor; his righteousness endures for evermore: his horn shall be exalted with honour.  ^111-9

**111:10** The sinner shall see and be angry, he shall gnash his teeth, and consume away: the desire of the sinner shall perish.  ^111-10
