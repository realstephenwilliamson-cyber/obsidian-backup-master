## Chapter 88

**88:1** A song. A psalm of the Korahites. For the leader; on maḥalath leannoth.[^1] A maskil of Heman the Ezrahite. ^88-1

**88:2** O LORD, God of my deliverance,  
when I cry out in the night[^2] before You, ^88-2

**88:3** let my prayer reach You;  
incline Your ear to my cry. ^88-3

**88:4** For I am sated with misfortune;  
I am at the brink of Sheol. ^88-4

**88:5** I am numbered with those who go down to the Pit;  
I am a helpless man ^88-5

**88:6** abandoned[^3] among the dead,  
like bodies lying in the grave  
of whom You are mindful no more,  
and who are cut off from Your care. ^88-6

**88:7** You have put me at the bottom of the Pit,  
in the darkest places, in the depths. ^88-7

**88:8** Your fury lies heavy upon me;  
You afflict me with all Your breakers. *Selah*. ^88-8

**88:9** You make my companions shun me;  
You make me abhorrent to them;  
I am shut in and do not go out. ^88-9

**88:10** My eyes pine away from affliction;  
I call to You, O LORD, each day;  
I stretch out my hands to You. ^88-10

**88:11** Do You work wonders for the dead?  
Do the shades rise to praise You? *Selah*. ^88-11

**88:12** Is Your faithful care recounted in the grave,  
Your constancy in the place of perdition? ^88-12

**88:13** Are Your wonders made known in the netherworld,[^4]  
Your beneficent deeds in the land of oblivion? ^88-13

**88:14** As for me, I cry out to You, O LORD;  
each morning my prayer greets You. ^88-14

**88:15** Why, O LORD, do You reject me,  
do You hide Your face from me? ^88-15

**88:16** From my youth I have been afflicted  
and near death;  
I suffer Your terrors wherever I turn.[^5] ^88-16

**88:17** Your fury overwhelms me;  
Your terrors destroy me. ^88-17

**88:18** They swirl about me like water all day long;  
they encircle me on every side. ^88-18

**88:19** You have put friend and neighbor far from me  
and my companions out of my sight.[^6] ^88-19

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Or “by day I cry out [and] by night.”
[^3]: Lit. “released.”
[^4]: Lit. “darkness.”
[^5]: Following Saadia; meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^6]: Lit. “into darkness.”
