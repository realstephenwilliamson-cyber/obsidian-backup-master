## Mūla

ते यान्त्यण्डान्तरे रौद्रं पुरं नाधः कदाचन ।
तत्स्थाः सर्वे शिवं यान्ति रुद्राः श्रीकण्ठदीक्षिताः ॥ ^mula

te yāntyaṇḍāntare raudraṃ puraṃ nādhaḥ kadācana |
tatsthāḥ sarve śivaṃ yānti rudrāḥ śrīkaṇṭhadīkṣitāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 06 - Chapter Eight, part 4#^pg205|p. 187]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=205>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 8.158cd–159ab.
>
> Those who are devoid of knowledge and have not been initiated, (but are) devoted to the worship of the Liṅga, go to Rudra’s abode within the Egg of Brahmā, and never go to (worlds) below 1.5 (with 8.157cd–158ab)
>
> All the Rudras who reside there, initiated by Śrīkanṇṭha, reach Śiva when (their period of) office comes to an end, together with the host of Rudra’s daughters. (with 8.158cd–159ab)
