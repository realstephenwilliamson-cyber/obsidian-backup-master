## Mūla

प्रत्यवायं यतोऽभ्येति चरेत्तन्नेदृशं क्रमम् ।
उक्तं श्रीमद्गह्वरे च परमेशेन तादृशम् ॥ ^mula

pratyavāyaṃ yato'bhyeti carettannedṛśaṃ kramam |
uktaṃ śrīmadgahvare ca parameśena tādṛśam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 10 - Chapter Thirteen, part 6#^pg344|p. 322]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=344>))
>
> (By doing so,) he transgresses the rules in his own teaching (dṛṣṭi) and in that of the others (he has accepted); (thus adulterated,) the rite (krama) becomes an obstacle (pratyavāya) (for his further development), and so he should not practice like this (or adhere to such a tradition). (with 13.318cd–319ab)
>
> The Supreme Lord has said something similar in the venerable Gahvara: ‘That kind of knowledge of Śiva does not shine in those who are devoted to other scriptures.’ (with 13.319cd–320ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* न केवलमेतत्स्वच्छन्दशास्त्रे एवोक्तं यावदन्यत्रापि -- इत्याह --

> उक्तं श्रीमद्गह्वरे च परमेशेन तादृशम् ॥

तदेव पठति -

> नान्यशास्त्राभियुक्तेषु शिवज्ञानं प्रकाशते ।

न प्रकाशते इति तत्राधिकारिणस्ते न भवन्ति -- इत्यर्थः ॥
