# Isaiah 56 (Douay-Rheims)

*Isaias 56 in the Douay-Rheims*

[[DRB Isaiah 55|← Isaiah 55]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Isaiah 57|Isaiah 57 →]]

**56:1** Thus saith the Lord: Keep ye judgment, and do justice: for my salvation is near to come, and my justice to be revealed. ^56-1

**56:2** Blessed is the man that doth this, and the son of man that shall lay hold on this: that keepeth the sabbath from profaning it, that keepeth his hands from doing any evil. ^56-2

**56:3** And let not the son of the stranger, that adhereth to the Lord, speak, saying: The Lord will divide and separate me from his people. And let not the eunuch say: Behold I am a dry tree. ^56-3

**56:4** For thus saith the Lord to the eunuchs, They that shall keep my sabbaths, and shall choose the things that please me, and shall hold fast my covenant: ^56-4

**56:5** I will give to them in my house, and within my walls, a place, and a name better than sons and daughters: I will give them an everlasting name which shall never perish. ^56-5

**56:6** And the children of the stranger that adhere to the Lord, to worship him, and to love his name, to be his servants: every one that keepeth the sabbath from profaning it, and that holdeth fast my covenant: ^56-6

**56:7** I will bring them into my holy mount, and will make them joyful in my house of prayer: their holocausts, and their victims shall please me upon my altar: for my house shall be called the house of prayer, for all nations. ^56-7

**56:8** The Lord God, who gathereth the scattered of Israel, saith: I will still gather unto him his congregation. ^56-8

**56:9** All ye beasts of the field come to devour, all ye beasts of the forest. ^56-9

**56:10** His watchmen are all blind, they are all ignorant: dumb dogs not able to bark, seeing vain things, sleeping and loving dreams. ^56-10

**56:11** And meet impudent dogs, they never had enough: the shepherds themselves knew no understanding: all have turned aside into their own way, every one after his own gain, from the first even to the last. ^56-11

**56:12** Come, let us take wine, and be filled with drunkenness: and it shall be as today, so also tomorrow, and much more. ^56-12
