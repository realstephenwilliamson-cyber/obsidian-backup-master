# Σαδδουκαῖος

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · noun

**Dodson:** a Sadducee — a Sadducee, a member of the aristocratic party among the Jews, from whom the high-priests were almost invariably chosen. (Strong's 4523; headword Σαδδουκαῖος, ου, ὁ)
