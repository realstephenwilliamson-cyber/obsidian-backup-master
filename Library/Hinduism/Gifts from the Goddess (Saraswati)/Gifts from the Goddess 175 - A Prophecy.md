### A Prophecy

From an essay written in the early ’90s.

There are some things I am sure of:

We are going to see beauty, harmony, peace, 

abundance and prosperity ascendant in this world, like never before.

You are going to witness it in your lifetimes. Love is going to open up in mighty rivers. Crime will not pay.

Pettiness will vanish.

You will love all as you love your own children. I love you.

I will give you peace.

That is a promise.

A
