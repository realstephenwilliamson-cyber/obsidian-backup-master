# Zohar 3.184a

← [[Zohar 3.183b]] · [[Zohar (Soncino)]] · [[Zohar 3.184b]] →

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 9.07 - Parashat Huqqat#^z3-184a|Parashat Ḥuqqat, at 3:184a]]

*Volume 3, folio 184a* · Chukat

*Passage Chukat 11:79 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 3.183b#^chukat-11-79|Chukat 11:79]].*

**Chukat 11:80** So it says, “They called on the name of Baal”, etc. (I Kings 18, 26), yet they availed nothing, for one thing because it was not permitted to them to bring fire down from heaven, and for another because they did not use the right invocation, since God confused them, as it says, “Thou didst turn their heart backwards” (*Ibid.* 37).’ ^chukat-11-80

**Chukat 11:81** R. Simeon said: ‘Here I must tell you something. Whoever knows how to perform the correct ceremony and recite the proper words can certainly influence the Holy One, blessed be He. If so, it may be asked, what is the superiority of the righteous who know the root of the matter and can concentrate their minds and thoughts more than the others who do not know this? ^chukat-11-81

**Chukat 11:82** The truth is that those who do not know the basis of the ceremony, and perform it only as a mat|ter of rote, draw down to themselves an influence from behind the shoulders of the Holy One, blessed be He, which is only called “Providence”, ^chukat-11-82

**Chukat 11:83** but those who do know draw forth blessings from the place which is called “Thought” until upper and lower beings are blessed and the Holy Name is blessed through them. Happy are they in that God is near them and ready to answer them when they call. ^chukat-11-83

**Chukat 12:84** AND THE LORD SAID TO MOSES, DO NOT FEAR HIM. ^chukat-12-84

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Chukat 12:85.*

**Chukat 12:86** Og was one of those who clave to Abraham and were circumcised with him. ^chukat-12-86

**Chukat 12:87** Hence Moses was afraid that he would not be able to overcome the sign which Abraham had impressed upon him. ^chukat-12-87

**Chukat 12:88** Therefore God said to him, “Do not fear him (*otho*),” as much as to say, Do not fear that sign (*oth*) which is upon him, because he has impaired that sign of his, and whoever impairs his sign deserves to be annihilated. ^chukat-12-88
