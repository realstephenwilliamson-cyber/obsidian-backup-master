## Mūla

तत्स्यादस्यान्यतत्त्वेऽपि युक्तस्य गुरुणा शिशोः ।
दीक्षा ह्यस्योपयुज्येत संस्क्रियायां स संस्कृतः ॥ ^mula

tatsyādasyānyatattve'pi yuktasya guruṇā śiśoḥ |
dīkṣā hyasyopayujyeta saṃskriyāyāṃ sa saṃskṛtaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 07 - Chapter Sixteen, part 3#^pg152|p. 132]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=152>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 16.295cd–296ab.
>
> Thus, even though the disciple may have been applied (yukta) by the teacher to (some) other principle (tattva), he can (nevertheless) accomplish every single thing (tat tat) there may be (in any of the principles) by the might (bala) of Mantra, knowledge and Yoga. (with 16.295cd–296ab)
>
> (The disciple’s) initiation should rightly serve to purify (him). (Once he is) purified (and empowered thereby), he, wise (as he is), obtains worldly benefits or (attains) liberation (as the case may be) by his own (inherent) strength (svabala) alone. (with 16.296cd–297ab)
