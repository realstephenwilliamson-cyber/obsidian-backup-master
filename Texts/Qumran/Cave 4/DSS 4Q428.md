### Frag. 7 (= 1QHa xv — xvi; 1Q35 1 i)

**1** my regulation, but you have called me [to your kindness, and to your forgiveness you have brought me,] ^f7-1

**2** and in the abundance of your compassion, to a[ll the judgments of …] ^f7-2

**3** I have been cast in guilt of unfaithfulness [… injustice, and in the lap …] ^f7-3

**4** with abundant impurity, and since my youth in [bloodshed, and up to old age …] ^f7-4

**5** My God, you have steadied my feet in the path [of your heart and to hear …] ^f7-5

**6** my ear and my heart to understand yo[ur] truth [… I had closed] ^f7-6

**7** (my) ear at your *[p. 901]* teaching until […] ^f7-7

**8** you have established knowledge in my vitals and [you] have glori[ﬁed me …] ^f7-8

**9** to him more for the stumbling-block of sin for … [… your justice is constant] ^f7-9

**10** for ever, because not belongs [to …] ways of […] ^f7-10

**11** Blank I give you tha[nks,] Lord, because [you have set me at the source of streams] ^f7-11

**12** in a dry land, in the spring of water in a [parched] land, […] ^f7-12

**1** yo[ur] name, for ever and ever they bless you, to the extent of their knowledge, day after day] ^f8c1-1

**3** be found any m[ore. But] your [truth] will be displayed for /endless/ glory and et[ernal] peace. ^f8c1-3

**4** [Blank Blessed are you, L]ord, for you have given [your servant] ^f8c1-4

**5** [the insight of knowledge to understand your wonders …] … […] ^f8c1-5
