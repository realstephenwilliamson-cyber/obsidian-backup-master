# ἐξίστημι

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · verb

**Dodson:** I astonish, am astonished — (lit: I remove from a standing position), (a) in trans. tenses: I astonish, amaze, (b) in intrans. tenses: I am astonished, amazed; I am out of my mind, am mad. (Strong's 1839; headword ἐξίστημι)
