# Psalm 136 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 135|← Psalm 135]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 137|Psalm 137 →]]

## God’s work in creation and in history

**136:1** O give thanks to the LORD, for he is good, for his steadfast love endures forever. ^136-1

**136:2** O give thanks to the God of gods, for his steadfast love endures forever. ^136-2

**136:3** O give thanks to the Lord of lords, for his steadfast love endures forever; ^136-3

**136:4** who alone does great wonders, for his steadfast love endures forever; ^136-4

**136:5** who by understanding made the heavens, for his steadfast love endures forever; ^136-5

**136:6** who spread out the earth on the waters, for his steadfast love endures forever; ^136-6

**136:7** who made the great lights, for his steadfast love endures forever; ^136-7

**136:8** the sun to rule over the day, for his steadfast love endures forever; ^136-8

**136:9** the moon and stars to rule over the night, for his steadfast love endures forever; ^136-9

**136:10** who struck Egypt through their firstborn, for his steadfast love endures forever; ^136-10

**136:11** and brought Israel out from among them, for his steadfast love endures forever; ^136-11

**136:12** with a strong hand and an outstretched arm, for his steadfast love endures forever; ^136-12

**136:13** who divided the Red Sea^[Or *Sea of Reeds*] in two, for his steadfast love endures forever; ^136-13

**136:14** and made Israel pass through the midst of it, for his steadfast love endures forever; ^136-14

**136:15** but overthrew Pharaoh and his army in the Red Sea,^[Or *Sea of Reeds*] for his steadfast love endures forever; ^136-15

**136:16** who led his people through the wilderness, for his steadfast love endures forever; who made water flow from the rock, for his steadfast love endures forever;^[Gk: Heb lacks *who made water . . . forever*] ^136-16

**136:17** who struck down great kings, for his steadfast love endures forever; ^136-17

**136:18** and killed famous kings, for his steadfast love endures forever; ^136-18

**136:19** Sihon, king of the Amorites, for his steadfast love endures forever; ^136-19

**136:20** and Og, king of Bashan, for his steadfast love endures forever; ^136-20

**136:21** and gave their land as a heritage, for his steadfast love endures forever; ^136-21

**136:22** a heritage to his servant Israel, for his steadfast love endures forever. ^136-22

**136:23** It is he who remembered us in our low estate, for his steadfast love endures forever; ^136-23

**136:24** and rescued us from our foes, for his steadfast love endures forever; ^136-24

**136:25** who gives food to all flesh, for his steadfast love endures forever. ^136-25

**136:26** O give thanks to the God of heaven, for his steadfast love endures forever. ^136-26
