## Mūla

विज्ञानाकलभेदेऽपि स्वं मन्त्रा मन्त्रनायकाः ।
तदीशाः शक्तिशंभ्वित्थं पञ्च स्युर्जाग्रदादयः ॥ ^mula

vijñānākalabhede'pi svaṃ mantrā mantranāyakāḥ |
tadīśāḥ śaktiśaṃbhvitthaṃ pañca syurjāgradādayaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 14 - Chapter Ten, part 5#^pg510|p. 491]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=510>))
>
> (4) When the Consciousness Deconditioned (subjects become objects, the divisions are nine). The five states, waking and the rest, correspond respectively to (one’s) Own Nature, the Mantra (subjects), the Lords of Mantra, the Great Lords of Mantra, and Śiva, along with His power.
