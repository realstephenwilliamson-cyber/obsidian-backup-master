## Mūla

कनिष्ठया विदार्यास्यं तर्जनीभ्यां भ्रुवौ तथा ।
अनामे मध्यमे वक्त्रे जिह्वया तालुकं स्पृशेत् ॥ ^mula

kaniṣṭhayā vidāryāsyaṃ tarjanībhyāṃ bhruvau tathā |
anāme madhyame vaktre jihvayā tālukaṃ spṛśet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 06 - Chapter Thirty-Two#^pg167|p. 149]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=167>))
>
> Holding the mouth wide open with the little fingers, and, in the same way, the eyebrows with the index fingers, (placing) the ring and middle (fingers) on the mouth, (the yogi) should touch the palate with (his) tongue. This is the Goddess Karañkinī (the Skeleton). (with 32.26–27a)
