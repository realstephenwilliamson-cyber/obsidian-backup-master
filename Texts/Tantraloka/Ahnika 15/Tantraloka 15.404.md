## Mūla

रूढं ज्ञत्वादिपञ्चाङ्गविस्पष्टं जाज्वलीत्यलम् ।
तेनाङ्गपञ्चकैरेव हुतिं दद्यात्सकृत्सकृत् ॥ ^mula

rūḍhaṃ jñatvādipañcāṅgavispaṣṭaṃ jājvalītyalam |
tenāṅgapañcakaireva hutiṃ dadyātsakṛtsakṛt || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 08 - Chapter Fifteen, part 7#^pg268|p. 250]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=268>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 15.406cd–407ab.
>
> Noetic consciousness (jñāna), aroused by the will and mounted on the plane of (that) action which is reflective awareness (vimarśa), burns intensely and clearly with the Five Limbs, namely, omniscience and the rest. (with 15.403cd–404ab)
>
> Thus, one should offer, one by one, (five) oblations with the Five Limbs. In this way, the fire (receives) all the (purifying) rites of passage (saṁskāra), starting from (the one imparted at) birth, and so is pure. (with 15.404cd–405ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* अत एव आह--

> तेनाङ्गपञ्चकैरेव हुतिं दद्यात्सकृत्सकृत् ॥
> जन्माद्यखिलसंस्कारशुद्धोऽग्निस्तावता भवेत् ।

जन्मादीति जन्मन आदिर्गर्भाधानादिरखिलसंस्कारस्तच्च आदिर्यस्य नामकरणादेरिति एकशेषः, तेन गर्भाधानपुंसवनसीमन्तोन्नयन जातकर्मनामकरणाख्यस्यसंस्कारपञ्चकस्य च अनेन सिद्धिरिति । तदुक्तम्--

"ततस्त्वाहुतयः पञ्चविद्याङ्गैरेव होमयेत् ।

जननादि ततः कर्म सर्वमेवं कृते कृतम् ॥"(८ ।११४)

इति ॥
