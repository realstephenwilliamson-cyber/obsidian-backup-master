# Psalms 031

Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 031]] · Septuagint (Brenton): [[LXX Psalms 30]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

Compare: [[NRSVue Psalms 031|NRSVue]] (Heb 31:1 = superscription; Heb 31:2–25 = NRSVue 31:1–24)

## Chapter 31

**31:1** For the leader. A psalm of David. ^31-1

**31:2** I seek refuge in You, O LORD;  
may I never be disappointed;  
as You are righteous, rescue me. ^31-2

**31:3** Incline Your ear to me;  
be quick to save me;  
be a rock, a stronghold for me,  
a citadel, for my deliverance. ^31-3

**31:4** For You are my rock and my fortress;  
You lead me and guide me as befits Your name. ^31-4

**31:5** You free me from the net laid for me,  
for You are my stronghold. ^31-5

**31:6** Into Your hand I entrust my spirit;  
You redeem me, O LORD, faithful God. ^31-6

**31:7** I detest those who rely on empty folly,  
but I trust in the LORD. ^31-7

**31:8** Let me exult and rejoice in Your faithfulness  
when You notice my affliction,  
are mindful of my deep distress, ^31-8

**31:9** and do not hand me over to my enemy,  
but grant me relief.[^1] ^31-9

**31:10** Have mercy on me, O LORD,  
for I am in distress;  
my eyes are wasted by vexation,  
my substance and body too.[^2] ^31-10

**31:11** My life is spent in sorrow,  
my years in groaning;  
my strength fails because of my iniquity,  
my limbs waste away. ^31-11

**31:12** Because of all my foes  
I am the particular butt of my neighbors,  
a horror to my friends;  
those who see me on the street avoid me. ^31-12

**31:13** I am put out of mind like the dead;  
I am like an object given up for lost. ^31-13

**31:14** I hear the whisperings of many,  
intrigue[^3] on every side,  
as they scheme together against me,  
plotting to take my life. ^31-14

**31:15** But I trust in You, O LORD;  
I say, “You are my God!” ^31-15

**31:16** My fate is in Your hand;  
save me from the hand of my enemies and pursuers. ^31-16

**31:17** Show favor to Your servant;  
as You are faithful, deliver me. ^31-17

**31:18** O LORD, let me not be disappointed when I call You;  
let the wicked be disappointed;  
let them be silenced in Sheol; ^31-18

**31:19** let lying lips be stilled  
that speak haughtily against the righteous  
with arrogance and contempt. ^31-19

**31:20** How abundant is the good  
that You have in store for those who fear You,  
that You do in the full view of men  
for those who take refuge in You. ^31-20

**31:21** You grant them the protection of Your presence  
against scheming men;[^4]  
You shelter them in Your pavilion  
from contentious tongues. ^31-21

**31:22** Blessed is the LORD,  
for He has been wondrously faithful to me,  
a veritable bastion. ^31-22

**31:23** Alarmed, I had thought,  
“I am thrust out of Your sight”;  
yet You listened to my plea for mercy  
when I cried out to You. ^31-23

**31:24** So love the LORD, all you faithful;  
the LORD guards the loyal,  
and more than requites  
him who acts arrogantly. ^31-24

**31:25** Be strong and of good courage,  
all you who wait for the LORD. ^31-25

[^1]: Lit. “make my feet stand in a broad place.”
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Others “terror.”
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
