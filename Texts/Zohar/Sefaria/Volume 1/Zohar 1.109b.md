*Volume 1, folio 109b* · Vayera

*Passage Vayera 21:297 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 1.109a#^vayera-21-297|Vayera 21:297]].*

**Vayera 21:298** R. Eleazar interpreted the words “and they are turned round about by His devices”, in the following manner. ‘The Holy One guides the course of events so as to bring to pass a seemingly stable state of things; but when the sons of men imagine that all before them is fixed and firmly established, then the Holy One turns His works into something altogether different from their former state. ^vayera-21-298

**Vayera 21:299** Further,’ he said, ‘we may translate not “devices”, but “device”, i.e. “instrument”, and compare God to a potter who, in turning his wheel, constantly fashions new vessels according to his fancy. So is the Holy One constantly reshaping His works, ^vayera-21-299

**Vayera 21:300** the instrument which constitutes His potter’s wheel, so to speak, being the lower world Judgement Court. ^vayera-21-300

**Vayera 21:301** And all is done in accordance with man’s works. If they are good, the wheel revolves to the right, making the course of events highly favourable to them; and however long the wheel revolves, punishment never settles on that side. ^vayera-21-301

**Vayera 21:302** Should men, however, turn to evil ways the Holy One imparts to His device a spin to the left, and all things now take a direction to the left, and the wheel gives to events a course unfavourable to the sons of men. ^vayera-21-302

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Vayera 27:391–27:393.*
