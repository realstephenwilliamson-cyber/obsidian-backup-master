>
> 23) In (Chapter Twenty-Three, which) deals with the consecration (of the teacher), is found a) the examination and creation of a teacher, b) his Mantra observance, c) the removal of knowledge (from a deceitful disciple), d) the categories of those (deceitful teachers) and e) the (consecration into) the state of an adept. 24) Now, in Chapter Twenty-Four, called ‘the last sacrificial rite’ (antyayāga), is discussed what should be taught there, namely: a) the recipient (adhikārin), b) the rite of passage (saṁskāraḥ) and c) its purpose. (with 1.316–317)
