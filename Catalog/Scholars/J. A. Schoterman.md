# J. A. Schoterman

Jan Anthony Schoterman (27 March 1948–29 June 1989), a Dutch Indologist whose fields were Hindu Tantrism, especially the Kubjikā school, and Indian influence in Indonesia; he took his PhD at Utrecht in 1982, taught Indology there, and was a research scholar at the KITLV in Leiden; he died before securing a permanent position ([Who Was Who in Indology](https://whowaswho-indology.info/6037/schoterman-jan-anthony/)).
