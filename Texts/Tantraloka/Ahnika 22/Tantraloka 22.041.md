## Mūla

भैरवीये चतुःषष्टौ तान्पशून्दीक्षयेत् त्रिके ।
सिद्धवीरावलीसारे भैरवीये कुलेऽपि च ॥ ^mula

bhairavīye catuḥṣaṣṭau tānpaśūndīkṣayet trike |
siddhavīrāvalīsāre bhairavīye kule'pi ca || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 13 - Chapter Twenty-Two#^pg311|p. 291]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=311>))
>
> (The teacher) should initiate into Trika those fettered souls who have been initiated into the Siddhānta, that is, Tantra divided into ten and eighteen (Tantras), and into the sixty-four Bhairava (Tantras). It is said in the Siddhavīrāvalisāra as well as in Bhairava’s Kula < that the initiation that is called Anuttara is preceded by a series of five initiations. (with 22.40cd–42ab)

## Viveka

> सिद्धान्ते दीक्षितास्तन्त्रे दशाष्टादशभेदिनि ॥
> भैरवीये चतुःषष्टौ तान्पशून्दीक्षयेत् त्रिके ।
> सिद्धवीरावलीसारे भैरवीये कुलेऽपि च ॥
> पञ्चदीक्षाक्रमोपात्ता दीक्षानुत्तरसंज्ञिता ।

एतच्च त्रयोदशाह्निक एव विचारितमिति तत एव अवधार्यम् ॥
