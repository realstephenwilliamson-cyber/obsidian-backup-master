# Psalms 87 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 86|← Psalms 86]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 88|Psalms 88 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 87|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**87:1** A canticle of a psalm for the sons of Core: unto the end, for Maheleth, to answer understanding of Eman the Ezrahite. ^87-1

**87:2** O Lord, the God of my salvation: I have cried in the day, and in the night before thee. ^87-2

**87:3** Let my prayer come in before thee: incline thy ear to my petition. ^87-3

**87:4** For my soul is filled with evils: and my life hath drawn nigh to hell. ^87-4

**87:5** I am counted among them that go down to the pit: I am become as a man without help, ^87-5

**87:6** Free among the dead. Like the slain sleeping in the sepulchres, whom thou rememberest no more: and they are cast off from thy hand. ^87-6

**87:7** They have laid me in the lower pit: in the dark places, and in the shadow of death. ^87-7

**87:8** Thy wrath is strong over me: and all thy waves thou hast brought in upon me. ^87-8

**87:9** Thou hast put away my acquaintance far from me: they have set me an abomination to themselves. I was delivered up, and came not forth: ^87-9

**87:10** My eyes languished through poverty. All the day I cried to thee, O Lord: I stretched out my hands to thee. ^87-10

**87:11** Wilt thou shew wonders to the dead? or shall physicians raise to life, and give praise to thee? ^87-11

**87:12** Shall any one in the sepulchre declare thy mercy: and thy truth in destruction? ^87-12

**87:13** Shall thy wonders be known in the dark; and thy justice in the land of forgetfulness? ^87-13

**87:14** But I, O Lord, have cried to thee: and in the morning my prayer shall prevent thee. ^87-14

**87:15** Lord, why castest thou off my prayer: why turnest thou away thy face from me? ^87-15

**87:16** I am poor, and in labours from my youth: and being exalted have been humbled and troubled. ^87-16

**87:17** Thy wrath hath come upon me: and thy terrors have troubled me. ^87-17

**87:18** They have come round about me like water all the day: they have compassed me about together. ^87-18

**87:19** Friend and neighbour thou hast put far from me: and my acquaintance, because of misery. ^87-19
