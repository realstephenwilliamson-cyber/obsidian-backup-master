# Zohar 3.090b

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 8.04 - Parashat Emor#^z3-90b|Parashat Emor, at 3:90b]]

*Volume 3, folio 90b* · Emor

*Passage Emor 11:36 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 3.090a#^emor-11-36|Emor 11:36]].*

**Emor 11:37** He further expounded the verse: “Salvation is the Lord’s; thy blessing be upon thy people” (Ps. 3, 9). ‘We have learnt’, he said, ‘that wherever Israel go into exile the Shekinah goes with them.[^1] When, therefore, they will come forth from captivity, who will be delivered? Israel or God? The answer is given in the verse, “Salvation is the Lord’s”. And when will this be? When “thy blessing is upon thy people, selah”, to take them out of captivity and deal well with them.’ ^emor-11-37

**Emor 13:41** WHOEVER HE BE OF THY SEED THROUGHOUT THEIR GENERATIONS THAT HATH A BLEMISH. R. Isaac said: ‘Whoever has a blemish is not fitted to serve before the Holy One, as we have laid down, that whoever has a blemish has no true faith, and his blemish bears witness against him.’ ^emor-13-41

**Emor 13:42** R. Eleazar was once sitting in the room of his father-in-law, and he complained that his eyes were watering. A man happened to pass by who had lost one eye. ‘Let us ask him,’ said the father-in-law. He replied: ‘He is blemished, and therefore not to be trusted.’ ‘Still let us ask him,’ said the other. So they said to him: ‘Who is mightiest in the world?’ He replied: ‘A rich man; and I shall remain with him through all.’ Said R. Eleazar: ‘His words show that he has no religious faith in him.’ He then discoursed on the verse: “For the law and for the testimony; if they speak not according to this word” (Isa. 8, 20). ^emor-13-42

**Emor 13:43** “‘Law” here’, he said, ‘signifies the Written Law, and “testimony” the Oral Law. The Oral Law does not rest in a blemished place, because it is fashioned from the Written Law. Hence the text says “Bind up the testimony” (*Ibid.* 16), because there in the Oral Law is the bundle of life, and the knot of faith is tied up with the testimony above, ^emor-13-43

**Emor 13:44** and thence diverse paths through all the worlds. ^emor-13-44

**Emor 13:45** “Seal the law among my disciples” (*Ibid.*) Were is the seal of the Written Law? In “my disciples”, that is, the prophets. The whole is established only in its completeness, and holiness rests on it only when it is complete, when part is joined with part and there is no empty place. Hence no man with a blemish must draw near to serve, nor must a sacrifice with a blemish be offered. ^emor-13-45

**Emor 13:46** But surely it may be said, God abides only in a broken place, a broken vessel, as it is written, “With him that is of a contrite and humble spirit” (Isa. 57, 15)? That is so, for this place is more perfect than all, when a man humbles himself so that the grandeur of all, the heavenly grandeur may rest upon him. But it is not written “with the blind and crippled”. If a man humbles himself, God raises him. ^emor-13-46

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Emor 20:84–20:85.*
