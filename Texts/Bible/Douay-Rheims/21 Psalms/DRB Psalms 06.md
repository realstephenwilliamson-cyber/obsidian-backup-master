# Psalms 6 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 05|← Psalms 5]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 07|Psalms 7 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 06|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**6:1** Unto the end, in verses, a psalm for David, for the octave. ^6-1

**6:2** O Lord, rebuke me not in thy indignation, nor chastise me in thy wrath. ^6-2

**6:3** Have mercy on me, O Lord, for I am weak: heal me, O Lord, for my bones are troubled. ^6-3

**6:4** And my soul is troubled exceedingly: but thou, O Lord, how long? ^6-4

**6:5** Turn to me, O Lord, and deliver my soul: O save me for thy mercy’s sake. ^6-5

**6:6** For there is no one in death, that is mindful of thee: and who shall confess to thee in hell? ^6-6

**6:7** I have laboured in my groanings, every night I will wash my bed: I will water my couch with my tears. ^6-7

**6:8** My eye is troubled through indignation: I have grown old amongst all my enemies. ^6-8

**6:9** Depart from me, all ye workers of iniquity: for the Lord hath heard the voice of my weeping. ^6-9

**6:10** The Lord hath heard my supplication: the Lord hath received my prayer. ^6-10

**6:11** Let all my enemies be ashamed, and be very much troubled: let them be turned back, and be ashamed very speedily. ^6-11
