### LXIV. Vision of the fallen Angels in the Place of Punishment.

**64:1** And other forms I saw hidden in that place. ^64-1

**64:2** I heard the voice of the angel saying: ‘These are the angels who descended to the earth, and revealed what was hidden to the children of men, and seduced the children of men into committing sin.’ ^64-2
