# Psalms 81 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 80|← Psalms 80]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 82|Psalms 82 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 81|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**81:1** A psalm for Asaph. God hath stood in the congregation of gods: and being in the midst of them he judgeth gods. ^81-1

**81:2** How long will you judge unjustly: and accept the persons of the wicked? ^81-2

**81:3** Judge for the needy and fatherless: do justice to the humble and the poor. ^81-3

**81:4** Rescue the poor; and deliver the needy out of the hand of the sinner. ^81-4

**81:5** They have not known nor understood: they walk on in darkness: all the foundations of the earth shall be moved. ^81-5

**81:6** I have said: You are gods and all of you the sons of the most High. ^81-6

**81:7** But you like men shall die: and shall fall like one of the princes. ^81-7

**81:8** Arise, O God, judge thou the earth: for thou shalt inherit among all the nations. ^81-8
