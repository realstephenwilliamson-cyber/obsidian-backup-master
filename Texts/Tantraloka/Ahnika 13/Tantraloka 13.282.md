## Mūla

द्विगुणा संस्क्रियास्त्येषां लिङ्गोद्धृत्याथ दीक्षया ।
दुष्टाधिवासविगमे पुष्पैः कुम्भोऽधिवास्यते ॥ ^mula

dviguṇā saṃskriyāstyeṣāṃ liṅgoddhṛtyātha dīkṣayā |
duṣṭādhivāsavigame puṣpaiḥ kumbho'dhivāsyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 09 - Chapter Thirteen, part 5#^pg320|p. 298]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=320>))
>
> However, if by (the power of) Śiva’s freedom they too (become) propense (to attaining) Śiva’s state, the purification (they have to undergo) is double, (as it takes place first) by the ‘extraction of (their) sectarian marks’ (liñgoddhrti) (which signals their conversion, and then) by initiation. ahead), (with 13.281cd–282ab)
>
> A jar (that smells bad) is perfumed with flowers in order to eliminate the bad smell. The purification that it requires is double. Such is not the procedure when a jar is clean. (with 13.282cd–283ab)
