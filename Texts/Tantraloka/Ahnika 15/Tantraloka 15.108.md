## Mūla

तत्रैव धाम्नि बाह्येऽन्तर्यागश्रीः प्रतितिष्ठति ।
नान्यत्रगत्या मोक्षोऽस्ति सोऽज्ञानग्रन्थिकर्तनात् ॥ ^mula

tatraiva dhāmni bāhye'ntaryāgaśrīḥ pratitiṣṭhati |
nānyatragatyā mokṣo'sti so'jñānagranthikartanāt || ^iast

> The inner (spiritual) wealth of the sacrifice (yāgaśrī) becomes well-established there in whatever external place the lotus of the heart blossoms (46254). ": (with 15.107cd–108ab)
>
> According to the venerable Vīrāvalī, liberation is not (attained) by going elsewhere (to some other place). It is (attained) by severing the knots of ignorance, and that (takes place) by the expansion (vikāsa) of consciousness. (with 15.108cd–109ab)
