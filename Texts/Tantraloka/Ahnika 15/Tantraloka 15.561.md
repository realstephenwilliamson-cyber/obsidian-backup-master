## Mūla

गुरोः शास्त्रस्य देवीनां नाम मन्त्रे यतस्ततः ।
अर्चातोऽन्यत्र नोच्चार्यमाहूतं तर्पयेत्ततः ॥ ^mula

guroḥ śāstrasya devīnāṃ nāma mantre yatastataḥ |
arcāto'nyatra noccāryamāhūtaṃ tarpayettataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 10 - Chapter Fifteen, part 9#^pg349|p. 331]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=349>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 15.563cd–564ab.
>
> The name of the teacher, the scripture, and the goddesses is a Mantra, and so should not be uttered (at any time) elsewhere apart from when worshipping, and once invoked, libation should be offered to it. For the Lord has said that if libation were not to be offered to a Mantra that has come (before the worshipper in the place of worship), it steals away half of (his) body. (with 15.561–562)
