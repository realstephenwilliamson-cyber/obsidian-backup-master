## Mūla

विशेषस्त्वयमेतस्यां यावज्जीवं शिशोर्गुरुः ।
शेषवृत्त्यै शुद्धतत्त्वसृष्टिं कुर्वीत पूर्णया ॥ ^mula

viśeṣastvayametasyāṃ yāvajjīvaṃ śiśorguruḥ |
śeṣavṛttyai śuddhatattvasṛṣṭiṃ kurvīta pūrṇayā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 08 - Chapter Seventeen#^pg204|p. 184]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=204>))
>
> In the course of the initiation with seed, (the fetter of the observance of) the rules need not be purified (and so removed). The special feature of this (initiation) is that, in order that the disciple may fulfil (his) remaining obligations (Śeṣavrtti) for as long as he lives, the teacher should emit the pure principles with a full (oblation). (with 17.99cd–100)
