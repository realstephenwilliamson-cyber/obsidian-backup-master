# Psalms numbering

Psalms are numbered differently in the Hebrew (Masoretic Text) tradition and the Greek Septuagint (LXX). This table shows the correspondence.

**Hebrew** = MT = JPS 1985 = BSB = BCP 1979 Psalter (psalm numbers)  
**LXX** = Rahlfs = Brenton English Septuagint

**Verse numbers** differ too. The Hebrew (and the LXX) count most psalm titles as verse 1, sometimes as verses 1–2 (e.g. Psalm 51); English Bibles and the BCP don't, and the BCP 1979 also divides some psalms into verses its own way. So there's no single offset per psalm: compare the notes side by side.
