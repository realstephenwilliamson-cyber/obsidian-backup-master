## Mūla

आ शिवात्सकलान्तं ये मातारः सप्त ते द्विधा ।
न्यग्भूतोद्रिक्तशक्तित्वात्तद्भेदो वेद्यभेदकः ॥ ^mula

ā śivātsakalāntaṃ ye mātāraḥ sapta te dvidhā |
nyagbhūtodriktaśaktitvāttadbhedo vedyabhedakaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 10 - Chapter Ten, part 1#^pg321|p. 302]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=321>))
>
> (Thus,) the seven perceivers from Śiva to the Conditioned one (sakala) are of two kinds, according to whether their power is subordinated (to them and dormant) (nyagbhūta) or predominates (udrikta) (over them and is stimulated to act). This same distinction (also applies to) the object of knowledge (and so) divides (it) up.

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* एवं प्रसङ्गात्सकलादिशक्तीनां स्वरूपमभिधाय प्रकृतमेवाह --

> आ शिवात्सकलान्तं ये मातारः सप्त ते द्विधा ।
> न्यग्भूतोद्रिक्तशक्तित्वात्तद्भेदो वेद्यभेदकः ॥
