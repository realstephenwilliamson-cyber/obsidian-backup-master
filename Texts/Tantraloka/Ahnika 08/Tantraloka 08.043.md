## Mūla

एतानि यातनास्थानं गुरुमन्त्रादिदूषिणाम् ।
ततो भूम्यूर्ध्व [मध्य] तो मेरुः सहस्राणि स षोडश ॥ ^mula

etāni yātanāsthānaṃ gurumantrādidūṣiṇām |
tato bhūmyūrdhva [madhya] to meruḥ sahasrāṇi sa ṣoḍaśa || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 04 - Chapter Eight, part 2#^pg115|p. 97]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=115>))
>
> 1) After that (above it) is (a zone of) darkness where the ground is heated (like burning coal). 2) Then comes (a waste land) devoid (of any living being). 3) Then (finally comes the abode of the) snakes. These (three zones) are places of torment, (where) those who speak ill of (their) teacher, the Mantras and the like (go)." (with 8.42cd–43ab)
>
> Then, in the middle of the earth, to a height of eighty-four thousand (leagues), rises mount Meru, the root of which is merged into it to the extent of sixteen thousand (leagues). The width at its base is sixteen thousand (leagues), and its upper extent (to the summit) is double that. Made of gold, it is the abode of all the immortals. (with 8.43cd–44)
