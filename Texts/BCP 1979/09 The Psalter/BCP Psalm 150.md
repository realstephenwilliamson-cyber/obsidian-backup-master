# Psalm 150

← [[BCP Psalm 149]] · [[BCP 1979]]

*Laudate Dominum* · Book Five · Thirtieth Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 150]]

**150:1** Hallelujah!  
Praise God in his holy temple; \*  
praise him in the firmament of his power. ^150-1

[p. 808] ^pg808

**150:2** Praise him for his mighty acts; \*  
praise him for his excellent greatness. ^150-2

**150:3** Praise him with the blast of the ram's-horn; \*  
praise him with lyre and harp. ^150-3

**150:4** Praise him with timbrel and dance; \*  
praise him with strings and pipe. ^150-4

**150:5** Praise him with resounding cymbals; \*  
praise him with loud-clanging cymbals. ^150-5

**150:6** Let everything that has breath \*  
praise the Lord.  
Hallelujah! ^150-6
