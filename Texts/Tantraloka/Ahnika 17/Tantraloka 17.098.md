## Mūla

तत्त्वान्यापादमूर्धान्तं भुवनानि त्यजेत्क्रमात् ।
तुटिमात्रं निष्कलं तददेहं तदहंपरम् ॥ ^mula

tattvānyāpādamūrdhāntaṃ bhuvanāni tyajetkramāt |
tuṭimātraṃ niṣkalaṃ tadadehaṃ tadahaṃparam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 08 - Chapter Seventeen#^pg200|p. 180]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=200>))
>
> And it is said in the venerable Dikṣottara that ‘travelling (through the body of the disciple) for (spans of) of thirty-six fingers, (the teacher) should gradually (kramāt) abandon the reality levels (tattva) and the worlds from the feet up to the end of the head, (one after another). (Then,) thinking (dhyāyan) for just a moment (tuṭi) that — “the undifferentiated (niṣkala) disembodied (form of Śiva) am I, (who am) supreme. I cast him (my disciple) there by means of (Śiva’s) power” — he should initiate him.’ (with 17.97cd–99ab)

## Viveka

> [!viveka]- Viveka in English (Dyczkowski, vol. IX, pp. 180–184)
> ![[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 08 - Chapter Seventeen#Viveka on 17.97–99]]

> श्रीमद्दीक्षोत्तरे चोक्तं चारे षट्त्रशदंगुले ॥
> तत्त्वान्यापादमूर्धान्तं भुवनानि त्यजेत्क्रमात् ।
> तुटिमात्रं निष्कलं तददेहं तदहंपरम् ॥
> शक्त्या तत्र क्षिपाम्येनमिति ध्यायंस्तु दीक्षयेत् ।

तदिति--परब्रह्मस्वरूपमित्यर्थः, अत एव निष्कलमिति अदेहमिति चोक्तम् । अहंपरमिति--अहंपरामर्शस्वभावम्--इत्यर्थः ॥
