# Arthur Anthony Macdonell

Boden Professor of Sanskrit at Oxford from 1899 until his retirement about four years before his death in 1930, and earlier Deputy Professor of Sanskrit; he worked on Vedic language and literature and wrote a *History of Sanskrit Literature* (1900) ([British Academy memoir](https://www.thebritishacademy.ac.uk/documents/5226/17p415.pdf)).
