## Mūla

ततो भावास्तत्र भावाः शक्तिराधारिका ततः ॥ ^mula

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 03 - Chapter Eleven, part 2#^pg113|p. 93]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=113>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 11.107cd–108ab.
>
> Thus, intuitive awareness (pratīti) alone is in this way the sustaining and creative (energy). And that is (one with) Śiva. Existing things (bhāva) (come forth) from that and (subsist) there. Thus Śakti is the sustaining (ground) (ādhārikā) (of all things).

*Lead-in:* तदाह --

> तस्मात्प्रतीतिरेवेत्थं कर्त्री धर्त्री च सा शिवः ।
