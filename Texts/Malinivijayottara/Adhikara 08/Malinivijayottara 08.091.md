> **Worship of Gaṇeśa**
>
> After having offered perfume and incense etc., one should worship Gaṇeśa. After having placed a seat (for him) generated from six (components, as will be explained), one should worship the Lord of Obstacles with (the syllable) GŪAUṀ and with perfumes and flowers etc.[^98] His limbs (are formed) with the letter G in conjunction with the six long (vowels). Having meditated on him as having three eyes and joyful, with the face of an elephant and form of a dwarf,[^99] then he who desires accomplishments should, after he has been dismissed, worship the great Weapon (Mantra).[^100] (with 8.90–92; the print has (91–92))

[^97]: Variant reading: *tasyāṅgānīti*

[^99]: MV 8/91-92ab is quoted in TĀv ad 15/377cd-378ab (376cd-377) The KSTS edition of the MV reads gām ity anena for gūauṁ ity anena.
[^100]: In TĀ 15/(378cd-381ab) (377 + 2, 3 and 4), Abhinavagupta summarizes MV 8/92cd-100. MV 8/92cd is quoted in the TĀv ad 15/378cd-381ab (377 + 2, 3 and 4).h, g: ceti
