# Song of Songs 8 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Song of Songs 07|← Song of Songs 7]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · —

**8:1** O that you were like a brother to me, who nursed at my mother’s breast! If I met you outside, I would kiss you, and no one would despise me. ^8-1

**8:2** I would lead you and bring you into my mother’s house and into the chamber of the one who bore me.^[Gk Syr: Heb *my mother; she* (or *you*) *will teach me*] I would give you spiced wine to drink, from the juice of my pomegranates. ^8-2

**8:3** O that his left hand were under my head and that his right hand embraced me! ^8-3

**8:4** I charge you, O daughters of Jerusalem, do not stir up or awaken love until it is ready! ^8-4

## Homecoming

**8:5** Who is that coming up from the wilderness, leaning upon her beloved? Under the apple tree I awakened you. There your mother was in labor with you; there she who bore you was in labor. ^8-5

**8:6** Set me as a seal upon your heart, as a seal upon your arm, for love is strong as death, passion fierce as the grave. Its flashes are flashes of fire, a raging flame. ^8-6

**8:7** Many waters cannot quench love, neither can floods drown it. If one offered for love all the wealth of one’s house, it^[Or *he*] would be utterly scorned. ^8-7

**8:8** We have a little sister, and she has no breasts. What shall we do for our sister, on the day when she is spoken for? ^8-8

**8:9** If she is a wall, we will build upon her a battlement of silver, but if she is a door, we will enclose her with boards of cedar. ^8-9

**8:10** I was a wall, and my breasts were like towers; then I was in his eyes as one who brings^[Or *finds*] peace. ^8-10

**8:11** Solomon had a vineyard at Baal-hamon; he entrusted the vineyard to keepers; each one was to bring for its fruit a thousand pieces of silver. ^8-11

**8:12** My vineyard, my very own, is for myself; you, O Solomon, may have the thousand and the keepers of the fruit two hundred! ^8-12

**8:13** O you who dwell in the gardens, my companions are listening for your voice; let me hear it. ^8-13

**8:14** Make haste, my beloved, and be like a gazelle or a young stag upon the mountains of spices! ^8-14
