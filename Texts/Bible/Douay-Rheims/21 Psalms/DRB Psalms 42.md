# Psalms 42 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 41|← Psalms 41]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 43|Psalms 43 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 42|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**42:1** A psalm for David. Judge me, O God, and distinguish my cause from the nation that is not holy: deliver me from the unjust and deceitful man. ^42-1

**42:2** For thou art God my strength: why hast thou cast me off? and why do I go sorrowful whilst the enemy afflicteth me? ^42-2

**42:3** Send forth thy light and thy truth: they have conducted me, and brought me unto thy holy hill, and into thy tabernacles. ^42-3

**42:4** And I will go in to the altar of God: to God who giveth joy to my youth. ^42-4

**42:5** To thee, O God my God, I will give praise upon the harp: why art thou sad, O my soul? and why dost thou disquiet me? ^42-5

**42:6** Hope in God, for I will still give praise to him: the salvation of my countenance, and my God. ^42-6
