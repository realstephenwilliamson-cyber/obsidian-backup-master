## Chapter 109

**109:1** For the leader. Of David. A psalm.  
O God of my praise,  
do not keep aloof, ^109-1

**109:2** for the wicked and the deceitful  
open their mouth against me;  
they speak to me with lying tongue. ^109-2

**109:3** They encircle me with words of hate;  
they attack me without cause. ^109-3

**109:4** They answer my love with accusation  
[^1]and I must stand judgment.[^2] ^109-4

**109:5** They repay me with evil for good,  
with hatred for my love. ^109-5

**109:6** Appoint a wicked man over him;  
may an accuser stand at his right side; ^109-6

**109:7** may he be tried and convicted;  
may he be judged and found guilty. ^109-7

**109:8** May his days be few;  
may another take over his position.[^3] ^109-8

**109:9** May his children be orphans,  
his wife a widow. ^109-9

**109:10** May his children wander from their hovels,  
begging in search of [bread]. ^109-10

**109:11** May his creditor seize all his possessions;  
may strangers plunder his wealth. ^109-11

**109:12** May no one show him mercy;  
may none pity his orphans; ^109-12

**109:13** may his posterity be cut off;  
may their names be blotted out in the next generation. ^109-13

**109:14** May God be ever mindful of his father’s iniquity,  
and may the sin of his mother not be blotted out. ^109-14

**109:15** May the LORD be aware of them always  
and cause their names to be cut off from the earth, ^109-15

**109:16** because he was not minded to act kindly,  
and hounded to death the poor and needy man,  
one crushed in spirit. ^109-16

**109:17** He loved to curse—may a curse come upon him!  
He would not bless—may blessing be far from him! ^109-17

**109:18** May he be clothed in a curse like a garment,  
may it enter his body like water,  
his bones like oil. ^109-18

**109:19** Let it be like the cloak he wraps around him,  
like the belt he always wears. ^109-19

**109:20** May the LORD thus repay my accusers,  
all those who speak evil against me. ^109-20

**109:21** Now You, O God, my Lord,  
act on my behalf as befits Your name.  
Good and faithful as You are, save me. ^109-21

**109:22** For I am poor and needy,  
and my heart is pierced within me. ^109-22

**109:23** I fade away like a lengthening shadow;  
I am shaken off like locusts. ^109-23

**109:24** My knees give way from fasting;  
my flesh is lean, has lost its fat. ^109-24

**109:25** I am the object of their scorn;  
when they see me, they shake their head. ^109-25

**109:26** Help me, O LORD, my God;  
save me in accord with Your faithfulness, ^109-26

**109:27** that men may know that it is Your hand,  
that You, O LORD, have done it. ^109-27

**109:28** Let them curse, but You bless;  
let them rise up, but come to grief,  
while Your servant rejoices. ^109-28

**109:29** My accusers shall be clothed in shame,  
wrapped in their disgrace as in a robe. ^109-29

**109:30** My mouth shall sing much praise to the LORD;  
I will acclaim Him in the midst of a throng, ^109-30

**109:31** because He stands at the right hand of the needy,  
to save him from those who would condemn him. ^109-31

[^1]: Or “but I am all prayer”; meaning of Heb. uncertain, but see v. 7.
[^2]: Or “but I am all prayer”; meaning of Heb. uncertain, but see v. 7.
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
