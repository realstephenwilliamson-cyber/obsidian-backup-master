# Revelation 12 (Douay-Rheims)

*Apocalypse 12 in the Douay-Rheims*

[[DRB Revelation 11|← Revelation 11]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Revelation 13|Revelation 13 →]]

Compare: [[Revelation 12|BSB and SBLGNT]]

**12:1** And a great sign appeared in heaven: A woman clothed with the sun, and the moon under her feet, and on her head a crown of twelve stars: ^12-1

**12:2** And being with child, she cried travailing in birth, and was in pain to be delivered. ^12-2

**12:3** And there was seen another sign in heaven: and behold a great red dragon, having seven heads, and ten horns: and on his head seven diadems: ^12-3

**12:4** And his tail drew the third part of the stars of heaven, and cast them to the earth: and the dragon stood before the woman who was ready to be delivered; that, when she should be delivered, he might devour her son. ^12-4

**12:5** And she brought forth a man child, who was to rule all nations with an iron rod: and her son was taken up to God, and to his throne. ^12-5

**12:6** And the woman fled into the wilderness, where she had a place prepared by God, that there they should feed her a thousand two hundred sixty days. ^12-6

**12:7** And there was a great battle in heaven, Michael and his angels fought with the dragon, and the dragon fought and his angels: ^12-7

**12:8** And they prevailed not, neither was their place found any more in heaven. ^12-8

**12:9** And that great dragon was cast out, that old serpent, who is called the devil and Satan, who seduceth the whole world; and he was cast unto the earth, and his angels were thrown down with him. ^12-9

**12:10** And I heard a loud voice in heaven, saying: Now is come salvation, and strength, and the kingdom of our God, and the power of his Christ: because the accuser of our brethren is cast forth, who accused them before our God day and night. ^12-10

**12:11** And they overcame him by the blood of the Lamb, and by the word of the testimony, and they loved not their lives unto death. ^12-11

**12:12** Therefore rejoice, O heavens, and you that dwell therein. Woe to the earth, and to the sea, because the devil is come down unto you, having great wrath, knowing that he hath but a short time. ^12-12

**12:13** And when the dragon saw that he was cast unto the earth, he persecuted the woman, who brought forth the man child: ^12-13

**12:14** And there were given to the woman two wings of a great eagle, that she might fly into the desert unto her place, where she is nourished for a time and times, and half a time, from the face of the serpent. ^12-14

**12:15** And the serpent cast out of his mouth after the woman, water as it were a river; that he might cause her to be carried away by the river. ^12-15

**12:16** And the earth helped the woman, and the earth opened her mouth, and swallowed up the river, which the dragon cast out of his mouth. ^12-16

**12:17** And the dragon was angry against the woman: and went to make war with the rest of her seed, who keep the commandments of God, and have the testimony of Jesus Christ. ^12-17

**12:18** And he stood upon the sand of the sea. ^12-18
