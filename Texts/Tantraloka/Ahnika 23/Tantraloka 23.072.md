## Mūla

राज्ञे द्रुह्यन्नमात्याङ्गभूतोऽपि हि विहन्यते ।
विपर्ययस्तु नेत्येवमूर्ध्वां दृष्टिं समाश्रयेत् ॥ ^mula

rājñe druhyannamātyāṅgabhūto'pi hi vihanyate |
viparyayastu netyevamūrdhvāṃ dṛṣṭiṃ samāśrayet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 14 - Chapter Twenty-Three#^pg348|p. 328]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=348>))
>
> He who attacks a king, even if he is one of (his) ministers, is executed, but that is not so the other way around (that is, if a king attacks his minister). In the same way, (it is better to) follow a more elevated doctrine (and so be like the king, than follow a lower one and be like the minister).
