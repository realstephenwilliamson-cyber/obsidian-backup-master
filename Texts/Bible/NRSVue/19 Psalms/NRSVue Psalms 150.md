## Praise for God’s surpassing greatness

**150:1** Praise the LORD! Praise God in his sanctuary; praise him in his mighty firmament!^[Or *dome*] ^150-1

**150:2** Praise him for his mighty deeds; praise him according to his surpassing greatness! ^150-2

**150:3** Praise him with trumpet sound; praise him with lute and harp! ^150-3

**150:4** Praise him with tambourine and dance; praise him with strings and pipe! ^150-4

**150:5** Praise him with clanging cymbals; praise him with loud clashing cymbals! ^150-5

**150:6** Let everything that breathes praise the LORD! Praise the LORD! ^150-6
