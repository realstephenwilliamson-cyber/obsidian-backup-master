## Mūla

मायोत्तीर्णं हि यद्रूपं ब्रह्मादीनां पुरोदितम् ।
आसनं त्वेतदेव स्यान्नतु मायाञ्जनाञ्जितम् ॥ ^mula

māyottīrṇaṃ hi yadrūpaṃ brahmādīnāṃ puroditam |
āsanaṃ tvetadeva syānnatu māyāñjanāñjitam || ^iast

> The form of Brahmā and the rest, that was said before to transcend Māyā, is this throne (sana), which is certainly not stained by the stain of Māyā.
