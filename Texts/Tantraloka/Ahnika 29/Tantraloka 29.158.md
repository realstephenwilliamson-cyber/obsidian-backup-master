## Mūla

तत्संमेलनयोगे देहान्ताख्ये च यामले चक्रे ।
कुचमध्यहृदयदेशादोष्ठान्तं कण्ठगं यदव्यक्तम् ॥ ^mula

tatsaṃmelanayoge dehāntākhye ca yāmale cakre |
kucamadhyahṛdayadeśādoṣṭhāntaṃ kaṇṭhagaṃ yadavyaktam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 12 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 4#^pg354|p. 337]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=354>))
>
> (These eight Wheels are experienced by the couple together), (1) in the going and coming (of the breath), (2) in the faculty of ascertainment, (3) in the (sense of) hearing, (4) in (the sense of) sight, (5) in the contact of the two sex organs, (6) in the conjunction of their coupling (saṁmelayoga), (7) in the one said to be at the end of the body, and 8) in the Wheel of the Couple (in union). (with 29.157cd–158ab)
>
> Having heard the inarticulate (ayvakta) (sound) (that rises up) from the location of (Śakti’s) heart between the breasts and ends at the lips (1) (in her) throat, (2) in the middle between these two Wheels (during sex), (3) when (after orgasm,) arousal has subsided, (they attain tranquillity, and all the energies of Sound) come to rest. And the supreme Bhairava of Sound, eight-fold in this way, (who manifests) there made of light, resonance and wind, is said to be the supreme pervasion of the one who recites Mantra. (with 29.158cd–160ab)
