## Mūla

कर्णिका पीतला रक्तपीतशुक्लं च केसरम् ।
दलानि पद्मबाह्यस्था शुक्ला च प्रतिवारणी ॥ ^mula

karṇikā pītalā raktapītaśuklaṃ ca kesaram |
dalāni padmabāhyasthā śuklā ca prativāraṇī || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 05 - Chapter Thirty-One, part 2#^pg125|p. 107]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=125>))
>
> The calix (of each lotus) is yellow, the stamens are red, yellow and white. The petals (are white) and the protective border (prativāranī) (around) outside the lotus is (also) white. The trident (is coloured) with black powder, whereas the line of Brahmā is white. The tip of the trident has a flame, and the staff (of the Trident) is yellow. A wise man should always worship 59 there (on) the lotus in the middle of the trident, Parā above him, the other (goddess i.e. Parāparā) on the right and Aparā on the left. (with 31.93cd–96)
