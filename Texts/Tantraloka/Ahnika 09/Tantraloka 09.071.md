## Mūla

कीदृशं प्रत्यणुमिति प्रश्ने नास्त्युत्तरं वचः ।
मलश्चावरणं तच्च नावार्यस्य विशेषकम् ॥ ^mula

kīdṛśaṃ pratyaṇumiti praśne nāstyuttaraṃ vacaḥ |
malaścāvaraṇaṃ tacca nāvāryasya viśeṣakam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 04 - Chapter Nine, part 2#^pg90|p. 73]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=90>))
>
> Again, if you say that the Lord (directs and) applies this paralyzing power of Impurity, there would be no reply to the question, “to what kind of individual soul (would He choose to do so)?” (with 9.70cd–71ab)
>
> (Some maintain) that impurity is an obscuring covering (āvaraṇa) that does not alter the nature of what it covers, (but merely) prevents one from perceiving it, like the cloth that covers a jar. (In that case, it would be the perceiver who is affected by it and not the object). It is Śiva who would not (be able to) see the nature of individual souls obscured by Impurity, and so this Impurity would (effectively) be His. (with 9.71cd–73ab)
