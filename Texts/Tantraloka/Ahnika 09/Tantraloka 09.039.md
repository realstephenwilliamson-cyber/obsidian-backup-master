## Mūla

कर्तेति पुंसः कर्तृत्वाभिमानोऽपि विभोः कृतिः ।
अत एव तथाभानपरमार्थतया स्थितेः ॥ ^mula

karteti puṃsaḥ kartṛtvābhimāno'pi vibhoḥ kṛtiḥ |
ata eva tathābhānaparamārthatayā sthiteḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 04 - Chapter Nine, part 2#^pg60|p. 43]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=60>))
>
> Therefore, Śiva, Whose one body is all things, is the (sole) agent of each and every creation (nirmāna). Even the individual soul’s presumption that he is the agent is the All-pervasive Lord’s doing. (with 9.38cd–39ab)
>
> Thus, as the true nature of the relationship between cause and effect is the manifestation (of the Lord) in this way, (his manifestation, both) in the scriptures and in the world, is wonderfully diverse. (with 9.39cd–40ab)
