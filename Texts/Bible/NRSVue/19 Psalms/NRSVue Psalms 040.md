## Thanksgiving for deliverance and prayer for help

*To the leader. Of David. A Psalm.*

**40:1** I waited patiently for the LORD; he inclined to me and heard my cry. ^40-1

**40:2** He drew me up from the desolate pit,^[Cn: Heb *pit of tumult*] out of the miry bog, and set my feet upon a rock, making my steps secure. ^40-2

**40:3** He put a new song in my mouth, a song of praise to our God. Many will see and fear and put their trust in the LORD. ^40-3

**40:4** Happy are those who make the LORD their trust, who do not turn to the proud, to those who go astray after false gods. ^40-4

**40:5** You have multiplied, O LORD my God, your wondrous deeds and your thoughts toward us; none can compare with you. Were I to proclaim and tell of them, they would be more than can be counted. ^40-5

**40:6** Sacrifice and offering you do not desire, but you have given me an open ear.^[Heb *ears you have dug for me*] Burnt offering and sin offering you have not required. ^40-6

**40:7** Then I said, “Here I am; in the scroll of the book it is written of me.^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^40-7

**40:8** I delight to do your will, O my God; your law is within my heart.” ^40-8

**40:9** I have told the glad news of deliverance in the great congregation; see, I have not restrained my lips, as you know, O LORD. ^40-9

**40:10** I have not hidden your saving help within my heart; I have spoken of your faithfulness and your salvation; I have not concealed your steadfast love and your faithfulness from the great congregation. ^40-10

**40:11** Do not, O LORD, withhold your mercy from me; let your steadfast love and your faithfulness keep me safe forever. ^40-11

**40:12** For evils have encompassed me without number; my iniquities have overtaken me until I cannot see; they are more than the hairs of my head, and my heart fails me. ^40-12

**40:13** Be pleased, O LORD, to deliver me; O LORD, make haste to help me. ^40-13

**40:14** Let all those be put to shame and confusion who seek to snatch away my life; let those be turned back and brought to dishonor who desire my hurt. ^40-14

**40:15** Let those be appalled because of their shame who say to me, “Aha, Aha!” ^40-15

**40:16** But may all who seek you rejoice and be glad in you; may those who love your salvation say continually, “Great is the LORD!” ^40-16

**40:17** As for me, I am poor and needy, but the Lord takes thought for me. You are my help and my deliverer; do not delay, O my God. ^40-17
