## Mūla

कखलम्बिकयोर्मध्ये तत्तत्त्वमनुचिन्तयेत् ।
पद्माकृति कखतत्त्वमैश्वरं चिन्तयेद्बुधः ॥ ^mula

kakhalambikayor madhye tat tattvam anucintayet[^65] |
padmākṛti[^68] kakhatattvam aiśvaraṁ cintayed budhaḥ[^69] || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2025 – April 2025
> p. 17 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=31>))
>
> **The Square at Śuddhavidyā between the Uvula and Cavity of Brahmā**
>
> One should meditate (in this way) on the square base (*catuṣkikā*) which is above the knot and below the three prongs. They say that it is the principle of (Pure) Knowledge and that it has two coverings.[^66] *One should meditate (anucintayet) on that principle in the middle (between) the Cavity of Brahmā (kakha) and the uvula.*[^67] (with 8.60–61ab)

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2025 – April 2025
> p. 18 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=32>))
>
> **The Lotus of the Iśvara Principle in the Cavity of Brahmā**
>
> The wise man should meditate (cintayet) on the Īśvara principle within the Cavity of Brahma (kakha).[^71] Its form a lotus flower, blooming and white, it has a calix, stamens and seeds. (with 8.61cd–62ab)
