## Mūla

विचित्रे फलसंपत्तिः प्रकाशाधीनिका यतः ।
इत्थं सूर्याश्रया दिक्स्यात्सा विचित्रापि तादृशी ॥ ^mula

vicitre phalasaṃpattiḥ prakāśādhīnikā yataḥ |
itthaṃ sūryāśrayā diksyātsā vicitrāpi tādṛśī || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 05 - Chapter Fifteen, part 4#^pg151|p. 133]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=151>))
>
> Each direction bestows (it own specific) fruit to that (person, whatever be the direction in which he resides,) even if (they) interchange (between them), and is various (in relation to his location). This is because the wealth of the fruit (of ritual action) depends (solely) on the Light (of which the directions are expressions). (with 15.217cd–218ab)
>
> In this way, (each) direction is relative to the sun, (and so,) even though it is relative (vicitrā), it is such (in relation to the sun). (Each direction is) presided over by Śiva (maheśa), who bears their (diverse and mutually) relative form (citratadrūpa). (with 15.218cd–219ab)

## Viveka

> इत्थं सूर्याश्रया दिक्स्यात्सा विचित्रापि तादृशी ॥
> अधिष्ठिता महेशेन चित्रतद्रूपधारिणा ।

इत्थमुक्तेन प्रकारेण सूर्याश्रयापि दिक् संभाव्यते यतः प्रतिनियतरूपत्वाभावात् चित्रा तादृशी सूर्याश्रयापि परसूक्ष्मस्थूलात्मतया चित्रं तासां दिशां रूपं धारयता महेश्वरेण अधिष्ठिता परप्रकाशभित्तिलग्नैव--इत्यर्थः ॥
