# Psalms 52 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 51|← Psalms 51]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 53|Psalms 53 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 52|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**52:1** Unto the end, for Maeleth, understandings to David. The fool said in his heart: There is no God. ^52-1

**52:2** They are corrupted, and become abominable in iniquities: there is none that doth good. ^52-2

**52:3** God looked down from heaven on the children of men: to see if there were any that did understand, or did seek God. ^52-3

**52:4** All have gone aside, they are become unprofitable together, there is none that doth good, no not one. ^52-4

**52:5** Shall not all the workers of iniquity know, who eat up my people as they eat bread? ^52-5

**52:6** They have not called upon God: there have they trembled for fear, where there was no fear. For God hath scattered the bones of them that please men: they have been confounded, because God hath despised them. ^52-6

**52:7** Who will give out of Sion the salvation of Israel? when God shall bring back the captivity of his people, Jacob shall rejoice, and Israel shall be glad. ^52-7
