# Proverbs 08

## Chapter 8

**8:1** It is Wisdom calling,  
Understanding raising her voice. ^8-1

**8:2** She takes her stand at the topmost heights,  
By the wayside, at the crossroads, ^8-2

**8:3** Near the gates at the city entrance;  
At the entryways, she shouts, ^8-3

**8:4** “O men, I call to you;  
My cry is to all mankind. ^8-4

**8:5** O simple ones, learn shrewdness;  
O dullards, instruct your minds. ^8-5

**8:6** Listen, for I speak noble things;  
Uprightness comes from my lips; ^8-6

**8:7** My mouth utters truth;  
Wickedness is abhorrent to my lips. ^8-7

**8:8** All my words are just,  
None of them perverse or crooked; ^8-8

**8:9** All are straightforward to the intelligent man,  
And right to those who have attained knowledge. ^8-9

**8:10** Accept my discipline rather than silver,  
Knowledge rather than choice gold. ^8-10

**8:11** For wisdom is better than rubies;  
No goods can equal her. ^8-11

**8:12** “I, Wisdom, live with Prudence;  
I attain knowledge and foresight. ^8-12

**8:13** To fear the LORD is to hate evil;  
I hate pride, arrogance, the evil way,  
And duplicity in speech. ^8-13

**8:14** Mine are counsel and resourcefulness;  
I am understanding; courage is mine. ^8-14

**8:15** Through me kings reign  
And rulers decree just laws; ^8-15

**8:16** Through me princes rule,  
Great men and all the righteous judges.[^1] ^8-16

**8:17** Those who love me I love,  
And those who seek me will find me. ^8-17

**8:18** Riches and honor belong to me,  
Enduring wealth and success. ^8-18

**8:19** My fruit is better than gold, fine gold,  
And my produce better than choice silver. ^8-19

**8:20** I walk on the way of righteousness,  
On the paths of justice. ^8-20

**8:21** I endow those who love me with substance;  
I will fill their treasuries. ^8-21

**8:22** “The LORD created me at the beginning of His course  
As the first of His works of old. ^8-22

**8:23** In the distant past I was fashioned,  
At the beginning, at the origin of earth. ^8-23

**8:24** There was still no deep when I was brought forth,  
No springs rich in water; ^8-24

**8:25** Before [the foundations of] the mountains were sunk,  
Before the hills I was born. ^8-25

**8:26** He had not yet made earth and fields,  
Or the world’s first clumps of clay. ^8-26

**8:27** I was there when He set the heavens into place;  
When He fixed the horizon upon the deep; ^8-27

**8:28** When He made the heavens above firm,  
And the fountains of the deep gushed forth; ^8-28

**8:29** When He assigned the sea its limits,  
So that its waters never transgress His command;  
When He fixed the foundations of the earth, ^8-29

**8:30** I was with Him as a confidant,  
A source of delight every day,  
Rejoicing before Him at all times, ^8-30

**8:31** Rejoicing in His inhabited world,  
Finding delight with mankind. ^8-31

**8:32** Now, sons, listen to me;  
Happy are they who keep my ways. ^8-32

**8:33** Heed discipline and become wise;  
Do not spurn it. ^8-33

**8:34** Happy is the man who listens to me,  
Coming early to my gates each day,  
Waiting outside my doors. ^8-34

**8:35** For he who finds me finds life  
And obtains favor from the LORD. ^8-35

**8:36** But he who misses me destroys himself;  
All who hate me love death.” ^8-36

[^1]: According to some Heb. mss. and printed editions, “judges of the earth.”
