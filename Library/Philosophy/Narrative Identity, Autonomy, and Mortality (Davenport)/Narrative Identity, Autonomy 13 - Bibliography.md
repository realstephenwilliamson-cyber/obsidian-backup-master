## Bibliography

1\. Almost all sources listed here also have an endnote reference the first time they are cited (though I include most book subtitles only in this bibliography). A few sources used as general background but not explicitly cited in any endnote are also included here for the reader’s convenience.

2\. Every scholarly source referenced in the endnotes should have a full entry here, excluding a few websites, famous novels and plays, films, songs or music recordings, newspapers, popular periodicals, dissertations, or references to another work within a primary citation.

3\. Generally, if more than one chapter in an edited book or one essay in a collection of essays is cited, then the edited volume or collection has its own full entry here, and each of the chapters or essays cited has its own entry with abbreviated information on the collection in which it originated or is reprinted.

4\. When only one chapter or essay from an edited volume is referenced, the entry here for the chapter or essay includes full information on the volume in which it is found.

5\. The entries for most articles reprinted in such collections include only the information on the reprinted version; when helpful, a few include information on the original publication of the article, followed by the book in which the article is reprinted.

6\. Unless otherwise noted, chapter endnotes always refer to the latest version or reprint of an article in the entry given here.

7\. When multiple editions of a work are in use, I usually refer to sections or chapters as well as specific pages of the edition that I am citing.

8\. I generally refer to the primary name of the publisher for each work listed here, rather than to different imprints (Clarendon, Belknap, etc.) except where the imprint is crucial for identifying the edition cited. Unless the publisher is obscure or European and American versions differ significantly, I also generally omit the place of publication, since most academic publishers now publish in many cities around the world.
