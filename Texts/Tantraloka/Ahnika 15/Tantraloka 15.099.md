## Mūla

तत्र देशे नियत्येत्थं ज्ञानयोगौ स्थितौ क्वचित् ।
यथा चातन्मयोऽप्येति पापितां तैः समागमात् ॥ ^mula

tatra deśe niyatyetthaṃ jñānayogau sthitau kvacit |
yathā cātanmayo'pyeti pāpitāṃ taiḥ samāgamāt || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 03 - Chapter Fifteen, part 2#^pg86|p. 68]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=86>))
>
> Just as barbarians, who by their very nature tread the path of unrighteousness (adharma), and such is necessarily the case there (in their) country, (conversely,) knowledge and Yoga are present in some (other) places. (with 15.98cd–99ab)
>
> Again, by keeping the company of these (impious people), even one who is not of (their) same nature becomes a sinner. Similarly, he who is in a sacred seat (114), (keeping the company of those who are there,) becomes the receptacle of knowledge, Yoga and the rest. (with 15.99cd–100ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* ननु एवं तत्र चेत् ज्ञानयोगादि स्थितं तावता अन्येषां किं भवेत्?- -इत्याशङ्कामुपशमयितुं दृष्टान्तयति--

> यथा चातन्मयोऽप्येति पापितां तैः समागमात् ॥
> तथा पीठस्थितोऽप्येति ज्ञानयोगादिपात्रताम् ।

अतन्मयो निष्पापः, ज्ञानयोगादिबहिष्कृतश्च । तैः--पापिभिः, पीठस्थितैश्च ॥
