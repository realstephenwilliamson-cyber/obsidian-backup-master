## Mūla

प्रतिहन्तीह मायीयं विकल्पं भेदभावकम् ।
शुद्धविद्यापरामर्शो यः स एव त्वनेकधा ॥ ^mula

pratihantīha māyīyaṃ vikalpaṃ bhedabhāvakam |
śuddhavidyāparāmarśo yaḥ sa eva tvanekadhā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. III
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 03 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v03 05 - Chapter Four, part 4#^pg146|p. 133]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 03.pdf#page=146>))
>
> This cogitation (vikalpana) is Pure Knowledge (free of thought constructs that,) clearly evident, eliminates the Māyic thought (vikalpa) that generates (and ponders on) the relative distinctions (bhedabhāvaka) (between things) here (in everyday life). (with 4.113cd–114ab)
>
> % (In the context of the ritual worship of the deity,) this same reflective awareness, which is Pure Knowledge (śuddhavidyāparāmarśa), is of many kinds, according to whether it (arises) in association with the ablution (snāna) (that precedes the rite), (the preliminary process of) purification, worship (of the deity), the offering of oblations (to it), the visualization (of it), or the repeated recitation of Mantra and the rest. (with 4.114cd–115ab)
