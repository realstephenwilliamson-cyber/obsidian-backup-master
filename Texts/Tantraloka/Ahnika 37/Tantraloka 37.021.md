## Mūla

मण्डलं सारमुक्तं हि मण्डश्रुत्या शिवाह्वयम् ।
एवमन्योन्यसंभेदवृत्ति पीठचतुष्टयम् ॥ ^mula

maṇḍalaṃ sāramuktaṃ hi maṇḍaśrutyā śivāhvayam |
evamanyonyasaṃbhedavṛtti pīṭhacatuṣṭayam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 11 - Chapter Thirty-Seven, part 1#^pg253|p. 235]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=253>))
>
> The one that bestows (deep) reflection (manana) and salvation (trāṇa) is called ‘Mantra’. The reinforcement there (in that respect) by Vidyā is fulfilment (āpyāya) (of Mantra). It is that which illumines what should be known. Mudrā is the counterpart (pratikṛti) of Mantra, and Maṇḍala fulfils it. It is said to be the essence and, in accord with (the sense of) the word ‘maṇḍa’, it is called Śiva. The four pīṭhas penetrate one another in this way and so, in actual fact, everything is present in each single pīṭha. (with 37.19cd–22ab)
