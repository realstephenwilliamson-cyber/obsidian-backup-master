## Mūla

तच्च यत्सर्वसर्वज्ञदृष्टं तच्चापि किं भवेत् ।
यदशेषोपदेशेन सूयतेऽनुत्तरं फलम् ॥ ^mula

tacca yatsarvasarvajñadṛṣṭaṃ taccāpi kiṃ bhavet |
yadaśeṣopadeśena sūyate'nuttaraṃ phalam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 11 - Chapter Thirty-Seven, part 1#^pg246|p. 228]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=246>))
>
> And that (scripture is the one) that is perceived by all who are omniscient. (with 37.6a)
>
> (And which one is that?) (It is) the one that, because its teaching is complete, (and by teaching it completely) (aśeṣopadeśena) gives rise to the fruit which is Anuttara (beyond which there is nothing higher) (anuttara). (with 37.6bcd)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* एवंविधं च एतत् किम् ?--इत्याशङ्क्य आह--

> तच्च यत्सर्वसर्वज्ञदृष्टं तच्चापि किं भवेत् ।

सर्वसर्वज्ञदृष्टमपि किं भवेत्?--इत्याशङ्कापुरःसरीकारेण तत्स्वरूपं दर्शयति--

> यदशेषोपदेशेन सूयतेऽनुत्तरं फलम् ॥

अत्र च अन्तरा श्लोकद्वयमन्यथा लिखितमधरे व्यत्ययेन न्याययमिति तत्रैव व्याख्यास्यामः ॥ ६ ॥
