# LXX Psalms 108

← [[LXX Psalms 107]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 109]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 109]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 109]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**108:1** For the end, a Psalm of David. O God, pass not over my praise in silence;  ^108-1

**108:2** for the mouth of the sinner and the mouth of the crafty *man* have been opened against me: they have spoken against me with a crafty tongue.  ^108-2

**108:3** And they have compassed me with words of hatred; and fought against me without a cause.  ^108-3

**108:4** Instead of loving me, they falsely accused me: but I continued to pray.[^1]  ^108-4

**108:5** And they rewarded me evil for good, and hatred for my love.  ^108-5

**108:6** Set thou a sinner against him; and let the devil stand at his right hand.[^2]  ^108-6

**108:7** When he is judged, let him go forth condemned: and let his prayer become sin.  ^108-7

**108:8** Let his days be few: and let another take his office of overseer.  ^108-8

**108:9** Let his children be orphans, and his wife a widow.  ^108-9

**108:10** Let his children wander without a dwelling-place, and beg: let them be cast out of their habitations.  ^108-10

**108:11** Let *his* creditor exact all that belongs to him: and let strangers spoil his labours.  ^108-11

**108:12** Let him have no helper; neither let there be any one to have compassion on his fatherless children.  ^108-12

**108:13** Let his children be *given up* to utter destruction: in one generation let his name be blotted out.  ^108-13

**108:14** Let the iniquity of his fathers be remembered before the Lord; and let not the sin of his mother be blotted out.  ^108-14

**108:15** Let them be before the Lord continually; and let their memorial be blotted out from the earth.  ^108-15

**108:16** Because he remembered not to shew mercy, but persecuted the needy and poor man, and *that* to slay him that was pricked in the heart.  ^108-16

**108:17** He loved cursing also, and it shall come upon him; and he took not pleasure in blessing, so it shall be removed far from him.  ^108-17

**108:18** Yea, he put on cursing as a garment, and it is come as water into his bowels, and as oil into his bones.  ^108-18

**108:19** Let it be to him as a garment which he puts on, and as a girdle with which he girds himself continually.  ^108-19

**108:20** This is the dealing of the Lord with those who falsely accuse me, and of them that speak evil against my soul.  ^108-20

**108:21** But thou, O Lord, Lord, deal *mercifully* with me, for thy name's sake: for thy mercy is good.  ^108-21

**108:22** Deliver me, for I am poor and needy; and my heart is troubled within me.  ^108-22

**108:23** I am removed as a shadow in its going down: I am tossed up and down like locusts.  ^108-23

**108:24** My knees are weakened through fasting, and my flesh is changed by reason of *the want of* oil.  ^108-24

**108:25** I became also a reproach to them: *when* they saw me they shook their heads.  ^108-25

**108:26** Help me, O Lord my God; and save me according to thy mercy.  ^108-26

**108:27** And let them know that this is thy hand; and *that* thou, Lord, hast wrought it.  ^108-27

**108:28** Let them curse, but thou shalt bless: let them that rise up against me be ashamed, but let thy servant rejoice.  ^108-28

**108:29** Let those that falsely accuse me be clothed with shame, and let them cover themselves with their shame as with a mantle.  ^108-29

**108:30** I will give thanks to the Lord abundantly with my mouth; and in the midst of many I will praise him.  ^108-30

**108:31** For he stood on the right hand of the poor, to save *me* from them that persecute my soul.  ^108-31

[^1]: 108:4 *Or,* in return for my love.
[^2]: 108:6 *Or,* the accuser.
