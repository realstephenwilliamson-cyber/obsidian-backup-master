Most men grant, that a government ought not to be divided; but they would have it moderated and bounded by some limits. Truly it is very reasonable it should be so . . . for my part, I  wish that not only kings, but all other persons, endued with supreme authority, would so temper themselves . . . within the limits of natural and divine law. 

 Thomas Hobbes, *De Cive* , EW II 96 note
