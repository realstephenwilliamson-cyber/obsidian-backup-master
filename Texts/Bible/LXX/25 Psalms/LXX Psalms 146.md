# LXX Psalms 146

← [[LXX Psalms 145]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 147]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 147]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 147]] (Heb 147:1–11 = LXX 146) · [[Psalms numbering]]

**146:1** Alleluia, *a Psalm* of Aggæus and Zacharias. Praise ye the Lord: for psalmody is a good thing; let praise be sweetly sung to our God.  ^146-1

**146:2** The Lord builds up Jerusalem; and he will gather together the dispersed of Israel.  ^146-2

**146:3** He heals the broken in heart, and binds up their wounds.  ^146-3

**146:4** He numbers the multitudes of stars; and calls them all by names.  ^146-4

**146:5** Great is our Lord, and great is his strength; and his understanding is infinite.  ^146-5

**146:6** The Lord lifts up the meek; but brings sinners down to the ground.  ^146-6

**146:7** Begin *the song* with thanksgiving to the Lord; sing praises on the harp to our God:  ^146-7

**146:8** who covers the heaven with clouds, who prepares rain for the earth, who causes grass to spring up on the mountains, [and green herb for the service of men;][^1]  ^146-8

**146:9** and gives to cattle their food, and to the young ravens that call upon him.  ^146-9

**146:10** He will not take pleasure in the strength of a horse; neither is he well-pleased with the legs of a man.  ^146-10

**146:11** The Lord takes pleasure in them that fear him, and in all that hope in his mercy.  ^146-11

[^1]: 146:8 See Ps 103. 14.
