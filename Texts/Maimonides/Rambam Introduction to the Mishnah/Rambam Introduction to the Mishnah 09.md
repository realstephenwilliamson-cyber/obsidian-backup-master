# Rambam Introduction to the Mishnah 09

*Section 9* · [[Rambam Introduction to the Mishnah]]

**The first division** are explanations transmitted from the mouth of Moshe and they have a hint in Scripture and it it possible to extrapolate them by way of reasoning. And about this there is no disagreement; if one says, "So has it been transmitted to me," there is nothing to say about it. ^9-1
