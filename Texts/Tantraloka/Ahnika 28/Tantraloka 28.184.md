## Mūla

ततो विसर्जनं कार्यं गुप्तमाभरणादिकम् ।
नैवेद्यं गुरुरादाय यागार्थे तन्नियोजयेत् ॥ ^mula

tato visarjanaṃ kāryaṃ guptamābharaṇādikam |
naivedyaṃ gururādāya yāgārthe tanniyojayet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 04 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 2#^pg97|p. 80]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=97>))
>
> After that, it is necessary to worship the teacher (again) and give him a lactating young cow adorned with clothes, silver, gold, jewels and the like. (Then) the teacher should say: “may your rite be complete”, (to which the assembled disciples) respond: “so be it”. After this, the rite of the dismissal of the God of the gods needs to be performed (well), so that he may come again. (with 28.182–184a)
>
> When the teacher has accepted the offering of the ornaments and the rest, (all of) which should be kept (well) hidden, he should keep it in reserve (niyojayet) for the sacrifice. (with 28.184bcd)
