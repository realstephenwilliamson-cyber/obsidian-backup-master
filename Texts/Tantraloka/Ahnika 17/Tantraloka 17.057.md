## Mūla

नात्र बाह्याहुतिर्देया दैशिकस्य पृथक् पुनः ।
दद्याद्वा यदि नो दोषः स्यादुपायः स भावने ॥ ^mula

nātra bāhyāhutirdeyā daiśikasya pṛthak punaḥ |
dadyādvā yadi no doṣaḥ syādupāyaḥ sa bhāvane || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 08 - Chapter Seventeen#^pg185|p. 165]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=185>))
>
> (There are yet) other teachers who say that this kind (of ritual action) is (just inwardly) imagined (bhāvanāmaya). The teacher need not offer an outer oblation here (in this case). Or else, if he were to offer it separately, that does not (entail) a defect; (indeed,) it is a means (upāya) to aid meditation (bhāvana). (with 17.56cd–57)
