## Mūla

धर्माधर्मात्मकं कर्म सुखदुःखादिलक्षणम् ।
लक्षयेत्सुखदुःखादि स्वं कार्यं हेतुभावतः ॥ ^mula

dharmādharmātmakaṃ karma sukhaduḥkhādilakṣaṇam |
lakṣayetsukhaduḥkhādi svaṃ kāryaṃ hetubhāvataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 05 - Chapter Nine, part 3#^pg124|p. 107]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=124>))
>
> The Supreme Lord has said as much in the Śrīpūrvaśāstra. ‘(The wise) prefer (the view that) Impurity is ignorance, (not a material substance, as the dualists maintain). It is the cause of (Karma, which is) the sprout of transmigratory existence (saṁsāra). Karma is (both) virtue (dharma) and vice (adharma), and the characteristic sign of (Karma is that we experience) pleasure, pain, and the like, (that are the consequences of Karma).’ (with 9.120–121ab)
>
> The result of one’s own (Karma) is experienced as pleasure, pain, and the like, in accord with (their Karmic) causes. (with 9.121cd)
