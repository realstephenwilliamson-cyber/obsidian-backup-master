## Mūla

कुलकालीविधौ चोक्तं वैष्णवानां विशेषतः ।
भस्मनिष्ठाप्रपन्नानामित्यादौ नैव योग्यता ॥ ^mula

kulakālīvidhau coktaṃ vaiṣṇavānāṃ viśeṣataḥ |
bhasmaniṣṭhāprapannānāmityādau naiva yogyatā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII

> And (so) it is said in the Kālīkulavidhi: ‘(Śaivas who) steadfastly cover (themselves with) ashes (bhasmaniṣthā) and Vaiṣṇavas, in particular, are not qualified (to belong to this tradition).’
