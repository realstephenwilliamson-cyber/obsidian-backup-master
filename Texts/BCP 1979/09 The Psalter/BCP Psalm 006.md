# Psalm 6

← [[BCP Psalm 005]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 007]] →

*Domine, ne in furore* · Book One · First Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 006]]

**6:1** LORD, do not rebuke me in your anger; \*  
do not punish me in your wrath. ^6-1

[p. 590]

**6:2** Have pity on me, LORD, for I am weak; \*  
heal me, LORD, for my bones are racked. ^6-2

**6:3** My spirit shakes with terror; \*  
how long, O LORD, how long? ^6-3

**6:4** Turn, O LORD, and deliver me; \*  
save me for your mercy's sake. ^6-4

**6:5** For in death no one remembers you; \*  
and who will give you thanks in the grave? ^6-5

**6:6** I grow weary because of my groaning; \*  
every night I drench my bed  
and flood my couch with tears. ^6-6

**6:7** My eyes are wasted with grief \*  
and worn away because of all my enemies. ^6-7

**6:8** Depart from me, all evildoers, \*  
for the LORD has heard the sound of my weeping. ^6-8

**6:9** The LORD has heard my supplication; \*  
the LORD accepts my prayer. ^6-9

**6:10** All my enemies shall be confounded and quake with fear; \*  
they shall turn back and suddenly be put to shame. ^6-10
