### Frags. 1 - 3

**1** Words of the pr[ay]er which Nabonidus, king of [the] la[nd of Baby]lon, the [great] king, prayed [when he was afﬂicted] ^f1-1

**2** by a malignant inﬂammation, by decree of the G[od Most Hi]gh, in Teiman. [I, Nabonidus,] was afﬂicted [by a malignant inﬂammation] ^f1-2
