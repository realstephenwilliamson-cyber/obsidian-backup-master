## Chapter 65

**65:1** For the leader. A psalm of David. A song. ^65-1

**65:2** Praise befits You in Zion, O God;  
vows are paid to You; ^65-2

**65:3** all mankind[^1] comes to You,  
You who hear prayer. ^65-3

**65:4** When all manner of sins overwhelm me,  
it is You who forgive our iniquities. ^65-4

**65:5** Happy is the man You choose and bring near  
to dwell in Your courts;  
may we be sated with the blessings of Your house,  
Your holy temple. ^65-5

**65:6** Answer us with victory through awesome deeds,  
O God, our deliverer,  
in whom all the ends of the earth  
and the distant seas  
put their trust; ^65-6

**65:7** who by His power fixed the mountains firmly,  
who is girded with might, ^65-7

**65:8** who stills the raging seas,  
the raging waves,  
and tumultuous peoples. ^65-8

**65:9** Those who live at the ends of the earth are awed by Your signs;  
You make the lands of sunrise and sunset shout for joy. ^65-9

**65:10** You take care of the earth and irrigate it;  
You enrich it greatly,  
with the channel of God full of water;  
You provide grain for men;  
for so do You prepare it. ^65-10

**65:11** Saturating its furrows,  
leveling its ridges,  
You soften it with showers,  
You bless its growth. ^65-11

**65:12** You crown the year with Your bounty;  
fatness is distilled in Your paths; ^65-12

**65:13** the pasturelands distill it;  
the hills are girded with joy. ^65-13

**65:14** The meadows are clothed with flocks,  
the valleys mantled with grain;  
they raise a shout, they break into song. ^65-14

[^1]: Lit. “flesh.”
