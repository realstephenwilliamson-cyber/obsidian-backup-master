## Chapter 3

**3:1** Upon my couch at night[^1]  
I sought the one I love—  
I sought, but found him not. ^3-1

**3:2** “I must rise and roam the town,  
Through the streets and through the squares;  
I must seek the one I love.”  
I sought but found him not. ^3-2

**3:3** I met the watchmen[^2]  
Who patrol the town.  
“Have you seen the one I love?” ^3-3

**3:4** Scarcely had I passed them  
When I found the one I love.  
I held him fast, I would not let him go  
Till I brought him to my mother’s house,  
To the chamber of her who conceived me ^3-4

**3:5** I adjure you, O maidens of Jerusalem,  
By gazelles or by hinds of the field:  
Do not wake or rouse  
Love until it please! ^3-5

**3:6** Who is she that comes up from the desert  
Like columns of smoke,  
In clouds of myrrh and frankincense,  
Of all the powders of the merchant? ^3-6

**3:7** There is Solomon’s couch,  
Encircled by sixty warriors  
Of the warriors of Israel, ^3-7

**3:8** All of them trained[^3] in warfare,  
Skilled in battle,  
Each with sword on thigh  
Because of terror by night. ^3-8

**3:9** King Solomon made him a palanquin  
Of wood from Lebanon. ^3-9

**3:10** He made its posts of silver,  
Its back[^4] of gold,  
Its seat of purple wool.  
Within, it was decked with love  
By the maidens of Jerusalem.[^5] ^3-10

**3:11** O maidens of Zion, go forth  
And gaze upon King Solomon  
Wearing the crown that his mother  
Gave him on his wedding day,  
On his day of bliss. ^3-11

[^1]: I.e., in a dream.
[^2]: Lit. “The watchmen met me.”
[^3]: Cf. Akkadian ah©a†zu, “to learn.”
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^5]: Emendation yields “ebony, / O maidens of Jerusalem!”
