# Philippians 3 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Philippians 02|← Philippians 2]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Philippians 04|Philippians 4 →]]

Compare: [[Philippians 03|BSB and SBLGNT]] · [[DRB Philippians 03|Douay-Rheims]] · [[JANT Philippians 03|JANT notes]]

**3:1** Finally, my brothers and sisters, rejoice in the Lord. To write the same things to you is not troublesome to me, and for you it is a source of steadfastness. ^3-1

## Breaking with the past

**3:2** Beware of the dogs, beware of the evil workers, beware of those who mutilate the flesh!^[Gk *the mutilation*] ^3-2

**3:3** For it is we who are the circumcision, who worship in the Spirit of God^[Other ancient authorities read *worship God in spirit*] and boast in Christ Jesus and have no confidence in the flesh— ^3-3

**3:4** even though I, too, have reason for confidence in the flesh. If anyone else has reason to be confident in the flesh, I have more: ^3-4

**3:5** circumcised on the eighth day, a member of the people of Israel, of the tribe of Benjamin, a Hebrew born of Hebrews; as to the law, a Pharisee; ^3-5

**3:6** as to zeal, a persecutor of the church; as to righteousness under the law, blameless. ^3-6

**3:7** Yet whatever gains I had, these I have come to regard as loss because of Christ. ^3-7

**3:8** More than that, I regard everything as loss because of the surpassing value of knowing Christ Jesus my Lord. For his sake I have suffered the loss of all things, and I regard them as rubbish, in order that I may gain Christ ^3-8

**3:9** and be found in him, not having a righteousness of my own that comes from the law but one that comes through faith in Christ,^[Or *through the faith of Christ*] the righteousness from God based on faith. ^3-9

**3:10** I want to know Christ^[Gk *him*] and the power of his resurrection and the sharing of his sufferings by becoming like him in his death, ^3-10

**3:11** if somehow I may attain the resurrection from the dead. ^3-11

## Pressing toward the goal

**3:12** Not that I have already obtained this or have already reached the goal,^[Or *have already been made perfect*] but I press on to lay hold of that for which Christ^[Other ancient authorities read *Christ Jesus*] has laid hold of me. ^3-12

**3:13** Brothers and sisters, I do not consider that I have laid hold^[Other ancient authorities read *yet laid hold*] of it, but one thing I have laid hold of: forgetting what lies behind and straining forward to what lies ahead, ^3-13

**3:14** I press on toward the goal, toward the prize of the heavenly^[Gk *upward*] call of God in Christ Jesus. ^3-14

**3:15** Let those of us, then, who are mature^[Or *perfect*] think this way, and if you think differently about anything, this, too, God will reveal to you. ^3-15

**3:16** Only let us hold fast to what we have attained. ^3-16

**3:17** Brothers and sisters, join in imitating me, and observe those who live according to the example you have in us. ^3-17

**3:18** For many live as enemies of the cross of Christ; I have often told you of them, and now I tell you even with tears. ^3-18

**3:19** Their end is destruction, their god is the belly, and their glory is in their shame; their minds are set on earthly things. ^3-19

**3:20** But our citizenship^[Or *commonwealth*] is in heaven, and it is from there that we are expecting a Savior, the Lord Jesus Christ. ^3-20

**3:21** He will transform the body of our humiliation^[Or *our humble bodies*] that it may be conformed to the body of his glory,^[Or *his glorious body*] by the power that also enables him to make all things subject to himself. ^3-21
