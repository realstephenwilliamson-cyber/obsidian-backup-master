# agnikāryapaddhati

**Catalog number:** M00246  
**Manuscript:** Manuscript no.:  781 Research Library J. and K. Govt. Shrinigar  
**Published:** Muktabodha Indological Research Institute, India

## Description

A short liturgical manual from Kashmir on fire sacrifice.  Contains very interesting incidental information on the deities worshipped in Kashmir. Some are main stream Kaula Tantra of the early period, such as Siddhalakṣmī who was the tutelary goddess of the Malla kings in Nepal.
