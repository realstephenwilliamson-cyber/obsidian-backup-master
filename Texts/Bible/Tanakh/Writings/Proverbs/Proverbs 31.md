# Proverbs 31

← [[Proverbs 30]] · [[Tanakh]]

Compare: [[NRSVue Proverbs 31|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 31

**31:1** The words of Lemuel, king of Massa, with which his mother admonished him: ^31-1

**31:2** No, my son!  
No, O son of my womb!  
No, O son of my vows! ^31-2

**31:3** Do not give your strength to women,  
Your vigor,[^1] to those who destroy kings.[^2] ^31-3

**31:4** Wine is not for kings, O Lemuel;  
Not for kings to drink,  
Nor any strong drink for princes, ^31-4

**31:5** Lest they drink and forget what has been ordained,  
And infringe on the rights of the poor. ^31-5

**31:6** Give strong drink to the hapless  
And wine to the embittered. ^31-6

**31:7** Let them drink and forget their poverty,  
And put their troubles out of mind. ^31-7

**31:8** Speak up for the dumb,  
For the rights of all the unfortunate. ^31-8

**31:9** Speak up, judge righteously,  
Champion the poor and the needy. ^31-9

**31:10** What a rare find is a capable wife!  
Her worth is far beyond that of rubies. ^31-10

**31:11** Her husband puts his confidence in her,  
And lacks no good thing. ^31-11

**31:12** She is good to him, never bad,  
All the days of her life. ^31-12

**31:13** She looks for wool and flax,  
And sets her hand to them with a will. ^31-13

**31:14** She is like a merchant fleet,  
Bringing her food from afar. ^31-14

**31:15** She rises while it is still night,  
And supplies provisions for her household,  
The daily fare of her maids. ^31-15

**31:16** She sets her mind on an estate and acquires it;  
She plants a vineyard by her own labors. ^31-16

**31:17** She girds herself with strength,  
And performs her tasks with vigor.[^3] ^31-17

**31:18** She sees[^4] that her business thrives;  
Her lamp never goes out at night. ^31-18

**31:19** She sets her hand to the distaff;  
Her fingers work the spindle. ^31-19

**31:20** She gives generously to the poor;  
Her hands are stretched out to the needy. ^31-20

**31:21** She is not worried for her household because of snow,  
For her whole household is dressed in crimson. ^31-21

**31:22** She makes covers for herself;  
Her clothing is linen and purple. ^31-22

**31:23** Her husband is prominent in the gates,  
As he sits among the elders of the land. ^31-23

**31:24** She makes cloth and sells it,  
And offers a girdle to the merchant. ^31-24

**31:25** She is clothed with strength and splendor;  
She looks to the future cheerfully. ^31-25

**31:26** Her mouth is full of wisdom,  
Her tongue with kindly teaching. ^31-26

**31:27** She oversees the activities of her household  
And never eats the bread of idleness. ^31-27

**31:28** Her children declare her happy;  
Her husband praises her, ^31-28

**31:29** “Many women have done well,  
But you surpass them all.” ^31-29

**31:30** Grace is deceptive,  
Beauty is illusory;  
It is for her fear of the LORD  
That a woman is to be praised. ^31-30

**31:31** Extol her for the fruit of her hand,  
And let her works praise her in the gates. ^31-31

[^1]: Lit. “ways.”
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Lit. “And exerts her arms.”
[^4]: Lit. “tastes.”
