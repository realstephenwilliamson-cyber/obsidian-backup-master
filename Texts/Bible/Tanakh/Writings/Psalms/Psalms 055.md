# Psalms 055

Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 055]] · Septuagint (Brenton): [[LXX Psalms 54]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

Compare: [[NRSVue Psalms 055|NRSVue]] (Heb 55:1 = superscription; Heb 55:2–24 = NRSVue 55:1–23)

## Chapter 55

**55:1** For the leader; with instrumental music. A *maskil* of David. ^55-1

**55:2** Give ear, O God, to my prayer;  
do not ignore my plea; ^55-2

**55:3** pay heed to me and answer me.  
I am tossed about, complaining and moaning ^55-3

**55:4** at the clamor of the enemy,  
because of the oppression of the wicked;  
for they bring evil upon me  
and furiously harass me. ^55-4

**55:5** My heart is convulsed within me;  
terrors of death assail me. ^55-5

**55:6** Fear and trembling invade me;  
I am clothed with horror. ^55-6

**55:7** I said,  
“O that I had the wings of a dove!  
I would fly away and find rest; ^55-7

**55:8** surely, I would flee far off;  
I would lodge in the wilderness; *selah* ^55-8

**55:9** I would soon find me a refuge  
from the sweeping wind,  
from the tempest.” ^55-9

**55:10** O Lord, confound their speech, confuse it!  
For I see lawlessness and strife in the city; ^55-10

**55:11** day and night they make their rounds on its walls;  
evil and mischief are inside it. ^55-11

**55:12** Malice is within it;  
fraud and deceit never leave its square. ^55-12

**55:13** It is not an enemy who reviles me  
—I could bear that;  
it is not my foe who vaunts himself against me  
—I could hide from him; ^55-13

**55:14** but it is you, my equal,  
my companion, my friend; ^55-14

**55:15** sweet was our fellowship;  
we walked together in God’s house. ^55-15

**55:16** Let Him incite death against them;  
may they go down alive into Sheol!  
For where they dwell,  
there evil is. ^55-16

**55:17** As for me, I call to God;  
the LORD will deliver me. ^55-17

**55:18** Evening, morning, and noon,  
I complain and moan,  
and He hears my voice. ^55-18

**55:19** He redeems me unharmed  
from the battle against me;  
it is as though many are on my side.[^1] ^55-19

**55:20** God who has reigned from the first,  
who will have no successor,  
hears and humbles those who have no fear of God. *Selah*. ^55-20

**55:21** He[^2] harmed his ally,  
he broke his pact; ^55-21

**55:22** his talk was smoother than butter,  
yet his mind was on war;  
his words were more soothing than oil,  
yet they were drawn swords. ^55-22

**55:23** Cast your burden on the LORD and He will sustain you;  
He will never let the righteous man collapse. ^55-23

**55:24** For You, O God, will bring them down to the nethermost Pit—  
those murderous, treacherous men;  
they shall not live out half their days;  
but I trust in You. ^55-24

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: I.e., the friend of v. 14.
