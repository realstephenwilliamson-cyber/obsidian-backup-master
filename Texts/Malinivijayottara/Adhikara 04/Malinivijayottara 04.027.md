## Mūla

द्वावेव मोक्षदौ ज्ञेयौ ज्ञानी योगी च शाङ्करि ।
पृथक्त्वात्तत्र * * * * बोद्धव्यं फलकाङ्क्षिभिः ॥ ^mula

dvāv eva mokṣadau jñeyau jñānī yogī ca śāṅkari |
pṛthaktvāt tatra * * * * boddhavyaṁ phalakāṅkṣibhiḥ || ^iast

*Muktabodha's e-text of the KSTS edition (M00160) has:* dvāveva mokṣadau jñeyau jñānī yogī ca śāṅkari | pṛthakttvāttatra * * * * boddhavyaṃ phalakāṅkṣibhiḥ ||

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, April 2023 – June 2023
> p. 21 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=62>))
>
> *O Śāṅkarī, only two should be known*[^19] *to bestow liberation – the man of knowledge (jñānin) and the yogi.* There (in that case) because they are distinct... those who desire to obtain (good) fruits should know....[^20]

[^19]: MV 4/27ab is quoted in the TĀv intro to 13/329. There the reading *proktau* – 'are said' is found instead of *jñeyau* – 'should be known'.
[^20]: *pṛthakttvāt tatra* * * * * *boddhavyaṁ phalakāṅkṣibhiḥ* || 27cd. The text here is lacunose and corrupt.
