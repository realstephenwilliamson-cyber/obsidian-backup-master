## Mūla

आकर्षादर्शौ चेत्यष्टकमेतत्प्रमाणानाम् ।
अलुप्तविभवाः सर्वे मायातत्त्वाधिकारिणः ॥ ^mula

ākarṣādarśau cetyaṣṭakametatpramāṇānām |
aluptavibhavāḥ sarve māyātattvādhikāriṇaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 09 - Chapter Eight, part 7#^pg339|p. 321]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=339>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 8.329cd–330ab.
>
> The five Bindus are: Sādhya (Goal), Dhātr (Benefactor), Damaneśa (Lord of the Garland), Dhyāna (Meditation) and Bhasma (Ash). The group of eight (Pāśupata) scriptures (pramāna) (consists of): Pañcārtha, Guhya, Rudrāṇkuśa, Hrdaya, Lakṣaṇa, Vyūha, Ākarṣa and Ādarśa. (with 8.328–329ab)
>
> The beings that are competent (adhikārin) for the Principle of Māyā all possess unobstructed power. Their bodies are made of Māyā and enjoy the experience (bhoga) proper to them. (with 8.329cd–330ab)
