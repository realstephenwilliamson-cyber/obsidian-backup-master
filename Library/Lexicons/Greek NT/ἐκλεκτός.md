# ἐκλεκτός

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · adjective

**Dodson:** chosen, elect, choice, select — chosen out, elect, choice, select, sometimes as subst: of those chosen out by God for the rendering of special service to Him (of the Hebrew race, particular Hebrews, the Messiah, and the Christians). (Strong's 1588; headword ἐκλεκτός, ή, όν)
