## Mūla

जातेऽग्नौ संस्कृते शैवे शब्दराशिं च मालिनीम् ।
पितरौ पूजयित्वा स्वं शुद्धं धाम विसर्जयेत् ॥ ^mula

jāte'gnau saṃskṛte śaive śabdarāśiṃ ca mālinīm |
pitarau pūjayitvā svaṃ śuddhaṃ dhāma visarjayet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 08 - Chapter Fifteen, part 7#^pg273|p. 255]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=273>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 15.413cd–414ab.
>
> After Śiva’s fire has been purified in this way and (our) parents Śabdarāśi and Mātrkā have been worshipped, one should send their own pure (glorious) power (dhāman) away. Taking a part of (this) pure fire, install it separately in order to (prepare) the sacrificial pap (caru). (with 15.411–412ab)
