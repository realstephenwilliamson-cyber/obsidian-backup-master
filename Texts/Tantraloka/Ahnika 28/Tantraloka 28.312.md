## Mūla

तीर्थे श्वपचगृहे वा नष्टस्मृतिरपि परित्यजेद्देहम् ।
ज्ञानसमकालमुक्तः कैवल्यं याति हतशोकः ॥ ^mula

tīrthe śvapacagṛhe vā naṣṭasmṛtirapi parityajeddeham |
jñānasamakālamuktaḥ kaivalyaṃ yāti hataśokaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 06 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 4#^pg151|p. 134]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=151>))
>
> ‘Liberated at the (very) same time (he attained supreme spiritual) knowledge, (wherever) he may abandon the body, even if he is unconscious, be it in a sacred bathing place (tīrtha) or in the house of (the lowest) outcaste (śvapaca), free of grief, he attains (complete) emancipation (kaivalya)’
