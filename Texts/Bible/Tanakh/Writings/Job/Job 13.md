# Job 13

Compare: [[NRSVue Job 13|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 13

**13:1** My eye has seen all this;  
My ear has heard and understood it. ^13-1

**13:2** What you know, I know also;  
I am not less than you. ^13-2

**13:3** Indeed, I would speak to the Almighty;  
I insist on arguing with God. ^13-3

**13:4** But you invent lies;  
All of you are quacks. ^13-4

**13:5** If you would only keep quiet  
It would be considered wisdom on your part. ^13-5

**13:6** Hear now my arguments,  
Listen to my pleading. ^13-6

**13:7** Will you speak unjustly on God’s behalf?  
Will you speak deceitfully for Him? ^13-7

**13:8** Will you be partial toward Him?  
Will you plead God’s cause? ^13-8

**13:9** Will it go well when He examines you?  
Will you fool Him as one fools men? ^13-9

**13:10** He will surely reprove you  
If in your heart[^1] you are partial toward Him. ^13-10

**13:11** His threat will terrify you,  
And His fear will seize you. ^13-11

**13:12** Your briefs are empty[^2] platitudes;  
Your responses are unsubstantial.[^3] ^13-12

**13:13** Keep quiet; I will have my say,  
Come what may upon me. ^13-13

**13:14** How long! I will take my flesh in my teeth;  
I will take my life in my hands. ^13-14

**13:15** He may well slay me; I may have no hope;[^4]  
Yet I will argue my case before Him. ^13-15

**13:16** In this too is my salvation:  
That no impious man can come into His presence. ^13-16

**13:17** Listen closely to my words;  
Give ear to my discourse. ^13-17

**13:18** See now, I have prepared a case;  
I know that I will win it. ^13-18

**13:19** For who is it that would challenge me?  
I should then keep silent and expire. ^13-19

**13:20** But two things do not do to me,  
So that I need not hide from You: ^13-20

**13:21** Remove Your hand from me,  
And let not Your terror frighten me. ^13-21

**13:22** Then summon me and I will respond,  
Or I will speak and You reply to me. ^13-22

**13:23** How many are my iniquities and sins?  
Advise me of my transgression and sin. ^13-23

**13:24** Why do You hide Your face,  
And treat me like an enemy? ^13-24

**13:25** Will You harass a driven leaf,  
Will You pursue dried-up straw, ^13-25

**13:26** That You decree for me bitter things  
And make me answer for[^5] the iniquities of my youth, ^13-26

**13:27** That You put my feet in the stocks  
And watch all my ways,  
Hemming in my footsteps?[^6] ^13-27

**13:28** Man wastes away like a rotten thing,  
Like a garment eaten by moths. ^13-28

[^1]: Lit. “secret.”
[^2]: Lit. “ashen.”
[^3]: Lit. “clayey.”
[^4]: So with kethib; others with qere “Though He slay me, yet will I trust in Him.”
[^5]: Lit. “inherit.”
[^6]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
