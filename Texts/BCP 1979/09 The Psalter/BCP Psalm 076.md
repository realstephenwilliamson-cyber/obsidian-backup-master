# Psalm 76

← [[BCP Psalm 075]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 077]] →

*Notus in Judæa* · Book Three · Fifteenth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 076]]

**76:1** In Judah is God known; \*  
his Name is great in Israel. ^76-1

**76:2** At Salem is his tabernacle, \*  
and his dwelling is in Zion. ^76-2

**76:3** There he broke the flashing arrows, \*  
the shield, the sword, and the weapons of battle. ^76-3

**76:4** How glorious you are! \*  
more splendid than the everlasting mountains! ^76-4

**76:5** The strong of heart have been despoiled;  
they sink into sleep; \*  
none of the warriors can lift a hand. ^76-5

**76:6** At your rebuke, O God of Jacob, \*  
both horse and rider lie stunned. ^76-6

**76:7** What terror you inspire! \*  
who can stand before you when you are angry? ^76-7

**76:8** From heaven you pronounced judgment; \*  
the earth was afraid and was still; ^76-8

**76:9** When God rose up to judgment \*  
and to save all the oppressed of the earth. ^76-9

**76:10** Truly, wrathful Edom will give you thanks, \*  
and the remnant of Hamath will keep your feasts. ^76-10

**76:11** Make a vow to the LORD your God and keep it; \*  
let all around him bring gifts to him who is worthy to be feared. ^76-11

**76:12** He breaks the spirit of princes, \*  
and strikes terror in the kings of the earth. ^76-12
