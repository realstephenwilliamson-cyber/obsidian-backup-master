### 2.1–27: Exhortation to endurance

*[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 02#^2-1|Text]], p. 443* ^2-1

**[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 02#^2-1|1]]:** *Child*, see 1.2n. ^2-1-2

**[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 02#^2-2|2]]:** *Through many witnesses*, the Gk preposition “dia” is ambiguous; rendering the phrase as “in the presence of many witnesses” better reinforces the connection between Paul and Timothy. ^2-2

**[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 02#^2-3|3]]:** *Soldier*, the soldier, athlete, and the farmer exemplify commitment, discipline, and preparedness (see [[1 Corinthians 09#^9-7|1 Cor 9.7]],[[1 Corinthians 09#^9-24|24]]; [[Ephesians 06#^6-11|Eph 6.11–17]]). ^2-3

**[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 02#^2-4|4]]:** *Everyday affairs*, here could also mean “do business, trade.” See Philo, *Spec. Laws* 2.65 and *Sifre Deut.* 357; [[Rosh Hashanah 31#31b|*b. Rosh Ha-Shanah* 31b]]; [[Sanhedrin 41#41a|*b. Sanh.* 41a]], “For 40 years he (Johanan b. Zakkai) was in business, 40 years he studied, and 40 years he taught.” ^2-4

**[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 02#^2-5|5]]:** *Athlete*, Eleazar the martyr in [[LXX 4 Maccabees 06#^6-10|4 Macc 6.10]] is also portrayed as an athlete. ^2-5

**[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 02#^2-6|6]]:** *Farmer* . . . *first share of the crops*, see [[Torah 48 - Shoftim#^20-6|Deut 20.6]]; [[1 Corinthians 09#^9-7|1 Cor 9.7]]. *M. B. Metz.* 7.2 notes, “And these may eat, according to the Torah: The one who works with what is attached to the ground when the labor finished; and with what is detached from the ground \[even\] before its work is completed—if it is something that grows from the ground.” ^2-6

**[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 02#^2-7|7]]:** *Give you understanding*, see [[Proverbs 02#^2-6|Prov 2.6]]. ^2-7

### 2.8–13: Jesus as model to follow

*[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 02#^2-8|Text]], p. 444* ^2-8

**[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 02#^2-8|8]]:** *Raised from the dead*, see [[Romans 01#^1-3|Rom 1.3–4]]; [[Romans 02#^2-17|2.17–18]]. *David,* the Messiah was expected to be a descendant of King David ([[Isaiah 11#^11-1|Isa 11.1]]; [[Jeremiah 23#^23-5|Jer 23.5–6]]; [[John 07#^7-42|Jn 7.42]]). The fifteenth blessing of the *Amidah* expresses the Jewish wish in redemption through a Davidic messiah: “Make the Branch of David to sprout quickly, and let his horn be exalted by your salvation, for we await your salvation all the day. Blessed art you, O Lord, who makes the horn of salvation to sprout.” ^2-8-2

**[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 02#^2-9|9]]:** *Suffer hardship*, see 1.8n. *Chained*, see 1.16. *Criminal*, Gk “kakourgos,” see [[Luke 23#^23-32|Lk 23.32]],[[Luke 23#^23-33|33]],[[Luke 23#^23-39|39]], where the term describes the men crucified with Jesus. ^2-9

**[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 02#^2-10|10]]:** *Elect*, Gk “chosen,” see [[Isaiah 65#^65-9|Isa 65.9]],[[Isaiah 65#^65-15|15]]; [[LXX Psalms 88#^88-4|Ps 88.3]] [as printed; the verse is LXX 88:4]; 104.6; [[I Chronicles 16#^16-13|1 Chr 16.13]]; [[Romans 09#^9-11|Rom 9.11]]; [[Romans 11#^11-5|11.5]],[[Romans 11#^11-7|7]],[[Romans 11#^11-28|28]] (referring to Israel, here in Romans meaning the Jewish people); [[1 Thessalonians 01#^1-4|1 Thess 1.4]]; [[1 Peter 02#^2-9|1 Pet 2.9]]; [[2 Peter 01#^1-10|2 Pet 1.10]] (referring to the church). ^2-10

**[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 02#^2-11|11]]:** *Saying is sure*, Gk “pistos ho logos,” “a trustworthy saying,” a distinctive phrase characteristic of the Pastoral Epistles ([[1 Timothy 01#^1-15|1 Tim 1.15]]; [[1 Timothy 03#^3-1|3.1]]; [[1 Timothy 04#^4-9|4.9]]; [[Titus 03#^3-8|Titus 3.8]]); see 1.13n. *Have died . . . will also live*, see [[Romans 06#^6-5|Rom 6.5]]. ^2-11

**[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 02#^2-12|12]]:** *Endure*, Gk “hypomenomen” the LXX’s most common translation of Heb “qāwāh,” “to hope,” used for those who remain faithful and will receive their reward ([[Psalms 025#^25-3|Ps 25.3]],[[Psalms 025#^25-5|5]]; [[Psalms 027#^27-14|27.14]]; [[Psalms 033#^33-20|33.20]]; [[Psalms 037#^37-9|37.9]]). *Reign*, see [[Revelation 03#^3-21|Rev 3.21]]. *Deny us*, see [[Matthew 10#^10-33|Mt 10.33]]; [[Luke 12#^12-8|Lk 12.8–9]]. ^2-12

**[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 02#^2-13|13]]:** See [[Romans 03#^3-3|Rom 3.3–4]]; [[1 Corinthians 10#^10-13|1 Cor 10.13]]; [[1 Thessalonians 05#^5-24|1 Thess 5.24]]; [[Revelation 19#^19-11|Rev 19.11]]. ^2-13

### 2.14–26: Timothy’s charge and the household of faith

*[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 02#^2-14|Text]], p. 444* ^2-14

**[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 02#^2-17|17]]:** *Hymenaeus*, condemned also in [[1 Timothy 01#^1-20|1 Tim 1.20]]. *Philetus* (“beloved”), not attested elsewhere in the NT. ^2-17

**[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 02#^2-18|18]]:** *Resurrection has already taken place*, the resurrection of the believers, which they argued, had already taken place probably at the moment of baptism. One logical result of claiming present salvation was the view that resurrection was also present, and thus that the bodies of believers were somehow either transformed or now free from earthly constraints (including moral behavior); the technical term for this view is “realized eschatology.” See [[1 Corinthians 04#^4-8|1 Cor 4.8]]; [[1 Corinthians 15#^15-12|15.12]]. ^2-18

**[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 02#^2-19|19]]:** *The Lord knows those who are his*, see [[LXX Numbers 16#^16-5|Num 16.5]]\[LXX\]. *Calls on the name of the Lord*, see [[LXX Isaiah 26#^26-13|Isa 26.13]]\[LXX\]; [[LXX Joel 03#^3-5|Joel 3.5]]\[LXX\], and for more general references [[Torah 03 - Lech Lecha#^13-4|Gen 13.4]]; [[Torah 04 - Vayera#^21-33|21.33]]; [[I Kings 18#^18-24|1 Kings 18.24]] (i.e., to pray). *Turn away from wickedness*, see [[Isaiah 52#^52-11|Isa 52.11]]; [[Proverbs 03#^3-7|Prov 3.7]]; [[LXX Sirach 17#^17-26|Sir 17.26]]. ^2-19

**[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 02#^2-20|20–21]]:** *Cleanse themselves*, the author is speaking of a moral status, not ritual purity. *Special* . . . *ordinary*, Gk “timēn . . . atimian,” lit., “honor” and “dishonor.” The difference between “honorable” and “dishonorable” offspring lies in the difference between those who fear the Lord and those who break the commandments ([[LXX Sirach 10#^10-19|Sir 10.19]]). ^2-20

**[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 02#^2-22|22]]:** *Pure heart* (Gk “katharas kardias”; Heb “lē āhôr”), see [[Psalms 051#^51-12|Ps 51.10]]; [[Proverbs 22#^22-11|Prov 22.11]]; “clean and without sin” ([[Proverbs 20#^20-9|Prov 20.9]]). The psalmist applies the distinction between “clean” and “unclean” metaphorically to the heart, the seat of thought and will in the Tanakh. ^2-22

**[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 02#^2-24|24]]:** *Lord’s servant*, [[Titus 01#^1-1|Titus 1.1]] (Gk “doulon de kyriou”; Heb “ʿee yhwh”) denotes the patriarchs Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob ([[Torah 05 - Chayei Sara#^24-14|Gen 24.14]]; [[Torah 06 - Toldot#^26-24|26.24]]; [[Ezekiel 37#^37-25|Ezek 37.25]]), priests ([[Psalms 134#^134-1|Ps 134.1]]; [[Psalms 135#^135-1|135.1–2]]), and prophets (Am 3.7). Moses “my servant” ([[Joshua 01#^1-2|Josh 1.2]]) and King David hold a special place in this category ([[I Samuel 19#^19-4|1 Sam 19.4]]; [[I Kings 03#^3-6|1 Kings 3.6]]), and God also calls Israel “my servant” (see [[Isaiah 41#^41-8|Isa 41.8]]). Common in Paul is “servant of Christ” ([[Romans 01#^1-1|Rom 1.1]]; [[Galatians 01#^1-10|Gal 1.10]]; [[Philippians 01#^1-1|Phil 1.1]]). *Apt teacher*, see Philo, *Rewards* 27. ^2-24

**[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 02#^2-25|25]]:** *Correcting opponents with gentleness*, [[Titus 01#^1-9|Titus 1.9]],[[Titus 01#^1-13|13]]. See 1QS 5.24–25, “Each man is to reprove his fellow in truth, humility and loving-kindness,” based on [[Torah 30 - Kedoshim#^19-17|Lev 19.17]]. *Know the truth* (Gk “epignōsin alētheias”), idiomatic expression characteristic of the Pastoral Epistles (3.7; [[1 Timothy 02#^2-4|1 Tim 2.4]]; [[Titus 01#^1-1|Titus 1.1]]). ^2-25

**[[NRSVue 2 Timothy 02#^2-26|26]]:** *Devil*, Gk “diabolou” is used in [[Ephesians 04#^4-27|Eph 4.27]]; [[Ephesians 06#^6-11|6.11]]; [[1 Timothy 03#^3-6|1 Tim 3.6–7]],[[1 Timothy 03#^3-11|11]]; [[2 Timothy 03#^3-3|2 Tim 3.3]]; [[Titus 02#^2-3|Titus 2.3]] and elsewhere. “Satan” is also used ([[1 Timothy 01#^1-20|1 Tim 1.20]]). Believers who abandoned the faith are said to have fallen back into the discipleship of Satan ([[1 Corinthians 05#^5-5|1 Cor 5.5]] and [[1 Timothy 05#^5-15|1 Tim 5.15]]). ^2-26
