## Mūla

स्वच्छसंवेदनोदारविकलाप्रबलीकृतम् ।
इह कर्मानुसन्धानभेदादेकं विभिद्यते ॥ ^mula

svacchasaṃvedanodāravikalāprabalīkṛtam |
iha karmānusandhānabhedādekaṃ vibhidyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 09 - Chapter Nine, part 7#^pg266|p. 247]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=266>))
>
> (Now) will be stated my reply here (to this objection), taught to me by the venerable Śambhunātha, and (powerfully) strengthened by the expansion (of my) noble and pure (translucent) consciousness. (with 9.264cd–265ab)
>
> Here (according to this teaching), action is one, (but) it is divided (and diversified) by the difference in the intention (to act) (anusaṁdhāna). There (in that case, this) intention (is basically) of five kinds, and so the organs of action are five. (with 9.265cd–266ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* तदाह -

> इह कर्मानुसन्धानभेदादेकं विभिद्यते ॥
> तत्रानुसंधिः पञ्चात्मा पञ्च कर्मेन्द्रियाण्यतः ।

तत्रेति कर्मभेदनिमित्तानुसन्धानभेदे सति -- इत्यर्थः । पञ्चात्मेति पञ्चप्रकारः । अत इत्यनुसंधानस्य पञ्चप्रकारत्वात् ॥ २६५ ॥
