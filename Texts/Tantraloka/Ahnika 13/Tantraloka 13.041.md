## Mūla

तस्मात्सांख्यदृशापीदमज्ञानं नैव युज्यते ।
अज्ञानेन विना बन्धमोक्षौ नैव व्यवस्थया ॥ ^mula

tasmātsāṃkhyadṛśāpīdamajñānaṃ naiva yujyate |
ajñānena vinā bandhamokṣau naiva vyavasthayā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 05 - Chapter Thirteen, part 1#^pg168|p. 148]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=168>))
>
> Thus (as no sound reply is possible to such questions, the existence of) this ignorance cannot be logically justified from the Sārikhyā point of view also. (with 13.41ab)
>
> Neither the liberated state nor that of bondage would be possible without (admitting the existence of some form of) ignorance (ajñāna). But this (ignorance) cannot be explained by means of the aforesaid arguments. (with 13.41cd–42ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* अत आह --

> तस्मात्सांख्यदृशापीदमज्ञानं नैव युज्यते ।

सांख्यदृशापीत्यपिशब्देन न केवलं प्रागुक्तक्रमेणैतन्न युज्यते यावदनेनापि मतेन -- इत्युक्तम् ॥

नन्विह भोगापवर्गमात्रमेवोपादेयमिति तदेव विविच्यतां किमज्ञानयुक्तायुक्तत्वकथनेन? -- इत्याशङ्क्याह --

> अज्ञानेन विना बन्धमोक्षौ नैव व्यवस्थया ॥
> युज्येते तच्च कथितयुक्तिभिर्नोपपद्यते ।

व्यवस्थयेति -

"अयं बद्धोऽयं मुक्तः"

इति ॥ ४१ ॥
