## Mūla

विद्या विवेचयत्यस्य कर्म तत्कार्यकारणे ।
रागोऽपि रञ्जयत्येनं स्वभोगेष्वशुचिष्वपि ॥ ^mula

vidyā vivecayaty asya karma tatkāryakāraṇe |
rāgo 'pi[^59] rañjayaty enaṁ svabhogeṣv aśuciṣv api || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2022 – June 2022
> p. 44 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=20>))
>
> (This) knowledge discerns the action (karman) of that (individual soul), its effect and the cause (of its activity).[^58] (with 1.28ab)

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2022 – June 2022
> p. 44 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=20>))
>
> Attachment (rāga) also makes him take pleasure (and be attached) to his own worldly pleasures, although they are impure.[^60] (Next,) Necessity (niyati) conjoins the individual soul to (the fruits of) his own action (karman).[^61] (Finally) time, which is determined (by units) beginning with a moment (tuṭi)[^62], also measures out (and conditions) (kalayati) him.[^63] (with 1.28cd–29)

[^59]: K: *rāgo ‘nurañjayatīti* /
[^58]: MV 1/28ab is quoted in TĀv ad 9/192cd-193. The translation of this line follows Abhinavagupta’s implicit explanation of it in TĀ 9/192cd-193. Another possible translation could be: ‘(This limited) knowledge discerns the action (*karman*) of that (individual soul) so that (it may act) as the cause (that accomplishes) the effect (which is the aim of) that (individual soul) (*kārya*).’
[^60]: Quoted in TĀv ad 9/199cd-200ab, which explains this.
[^61]: Quoted in TĀv ad 9/202cd, which explains this.
[^62]: A ‘tu˜i’ is an eighth of a second. As in this case, the word may also denote generically a very small span of time.
[^63]: MV 1/29cd is praphrased as TĀ 9/201cd and quoted in TĀv ad 9/201cd-202ab. MV 1/27-29 is quoted in TĀv ad 1/37-38ab.
