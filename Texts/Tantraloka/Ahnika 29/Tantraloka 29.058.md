## Mūla

आत्मनो वाऽथवा शक्तेश्चक्रस्याथ स्मरेदिमम् ।
न्यस्यत्वेन विधिं देहे पीठाख्ये पारमेश्वरम् ॥ ^mula

ātmano vā'thavā śakteścakrasyātha smaredimam |
nyasyatvena vidhiṃ dehe pīṭhākhye pārameśvaram || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 10 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 2#^pg271|p. 254]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=271>))
>
> This procedure is called that of the sacred seats, (and is taught by) the Supreme Lord (parameśvara). One should recollect it as that of the (outer) circle (cakra) (of sacred seats), that should be deposited onto the body, whether one’s own or that of the (Kaula) consort (śakti).
