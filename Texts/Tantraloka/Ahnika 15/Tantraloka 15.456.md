## Mūla

त्रस्यन्तीवेति तत्तच्चिदक्षैस्तत्सहकारिभिः ।
ततः स दक्षिणे हस्ते दीप्तं सर्वाध्वपूरितम् ॥ ^mula

trasyantīveti tattaccidakṣaistatsahakāribhiḥ |
tataḥ sa dakṣiṇe haste dīptaṃ sarvādhvapūritam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 08 - Chapter Fifteen, part 7#^pg297|p. 279]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=297>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 15.458cd–459ab.
>
> (But) those who are afflicted (mudrita) by some ghosts or the like are gripped, as it were, by fear, because (the teacher) is beautiful, (shining radiantly) with the rays of the Mantras deposited (on his body). Thus, one can perceive their presence or absence very well by means of the senses assisted (by a descent of the power of grace). (with 15.455cd–456ab)
>
> Then (the teacher) should worship on his right hand, by means of the left, the wheel of Mantras, energized (dīpta) and full of all the Paths, that burn up all the fetters. (He should) place that hand on the head of (his) disciple, onto whose body the series of paths have been deposited, and he should deposit in due order (the Mantra) on each limb (of his body), and touch it with that same (hand). (with 15.456cd–458ab)
