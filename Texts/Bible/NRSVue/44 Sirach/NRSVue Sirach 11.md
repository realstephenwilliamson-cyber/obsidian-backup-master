# Sirach 11 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Sirach 10|← Sirach 10]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Sirach 12|Sirach 12 →]]

## The deceptiveness of appearances

**11:1** The wisdom of the humble lifts their heads high and seats them among the great. ^11-1

**11:2** Do not praise individuals for their good looks or loathe anyone because of appearance alone. ^11-2

**11:3** The bee is small among flying creatures, but what it produces is the origin of sweet things. ^11-3

**11:4** Do not mock those wearing only a loincloth or make fun of those whose day is bitter,^[Heb: Gk *Do not boast about wearing fine clothes, and do not exalt yourself when you are honored*] for the works of the Lord are wonderful and his works are concealed from humans. ^11-4

**11:5** Many oppressed have sat upon a throne,^[Heb: Gk *Many sovereigns have sat on the ground*] and one whom no one expected has worn a crown. ^11-5

**11:6** Many rulers have been utterly disgraced, and the honored have been handed over to others. ^11-6

## Deliberation and caution

**11:7** Do not find fault before you investigate; examine first and then criticize. ^11-7

**11:8** Do not answer before you listen, and do not interrupt when another is speaking. ^11-8

**11:9** Do not argue about a matter that does not concern you, and do not sit with sinners when they judge a case. ^11-9

**11:10** My child, do not busy yourself with many matters; if you multiply activities, you will not be held blameless. If you pursue, you will not overtake, and by fleeing you will not escape. ^11-10

**11:11** There are those who work and struggle and hurry but are so much the more in want. ^11-11

**11:12** There are others who are slow and need help, who lack strength and abound in poverty, but the eyes of the Lord look kindly upon them; he lifts them out of their lowly condition ^11-12

**11:13** and raises up their heads to the amazement of the many. ^11-13

**11:14** Good things and bad, life and death, poverty and wealth, come from the Lord.^[Other ancient authorities add as 11.15–16: *15Wisdom, understanding, and knowledge of the law come from the Lord; affection and the ways of good works come from him. 16Error and darkness were created with sinners; evil grows old with those who take pride in malice.*] ^11-14

**11:17** The Lord’s gift remains with the devout, and his favor brings lasting success. ^11-17

**11:18** A person becomes rich through diligence and self-denial, and the reward allotted to him is this: ^11-18

**11:19** when he says, “I have found rest, and now I shall feast on my goods!” he does not realize how time passes by; he will leave them to others and will die. ^11-19

**11:20** Stand by your agreement and attend to it, and grow old in your work. ^11-20

**11:21** Do not wonder at the works of a sinner, but trust in the Lord and keep at your job, for it is easy in the sight of the Lord to make the poor person suddenly rich, in an instant. ^11-21

**11:22** The blessing of the Lord is^[Heb: Gk *is in*] the reward of the pious, and quickly his blessing flourishes. ^11-22

**11:23** Do not say, “What do I need, and what further benefit can be mine?” ^11-23

**11:24** Do not say, “I have enough, and what harm can come to me now?” ^11-24

**11:25** In the day of prosperity, adversity is forgotten, and in the day of adversity, prosperity is not remembered. ^11-25

**11:26** For it is easy for the Lord on the day of death to reward individuals according to their conduct. ^11-26

**11:27** An hour’s misery makes one forget delights, and at the close of one’s life one’s deeds are revealed. ^11-27

**11:28** Call no one happy before his death; by how he ends, a person becomes known.^[Heb: Gk *and through his children a person becomes known*] ^11-28

## Care in choosing friends

**11:29** Do not invite everyone into your home, for many are the tricks of the crafty. ^11-29

**11:30** Like a decoy partridge in a cage, so is the mind of the proud, and like spies they observe your weakness,^[Heb: Gk *downfall*] ^11-30

**11:31** for they lie in wait, turning good into evil, and to worthy actions they attach blame. ^11-31

**11:32** From a spark many coals are kindled, and a sinner lies in wait to shed blood. ^11-32

**11:33** Beware of scoundrels, for they devise evil, and they may ruin your reputation forever. ^11-33

**11:34** Receive strangers into your home, and they will stir up trouble for you and will make you a stranger to your own family. ^11-34
