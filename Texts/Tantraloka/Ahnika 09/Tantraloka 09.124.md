## Mūla

चित्रैर्हेत्वन्तरं किञ्चित्तच्च कर्मेह दर्शनात् ।
स्वाङ्गे प्रसादरौक्ष्यादि जायमानं स्वकर्मणा ॥ ^mula

citrairhetvantaraṃ kiñcittacca karmeha darśanāt |
svāṅge prasādaraukṣyādi jāyamānaṃ svakarmaṇā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 05 - Chapter Nine, part 3#^pg126|p. 109]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=126>))
>
> Moreover, (the variety) one experiences of pleasure, pain, and the rest, even when (their) perceived cause is the same, indicates that there is some other cause (apart from that one). That is Karma, because it is seen (to be operating in this way) here (in this world). (with 9.123bcd–124ab)
>
> Observing in one’s own (present) body that, for example, (tranquil,) well-disposed (prasāda), or troubled (rakṣa) (states of being) arise due to one’s own action, one conceives that the Karma (accumulated) in another (previous) body is the cause (of one’s present condition). (with 9.124cd–125ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* एतदेव उपपादयति --

> स्वाङ्गे प्रसादरौक्ष्यादि जायमानं स्वकर्मणा ॥
> दृष्टमित्यन्यदेहस्थं कारणं कर्म कल्प्यते ।

यतः स्वाङ्गे विचित्रं प्रसादरौक्ष्यादि स्वेन पांसुलेपाद्यात्मना कर्मणा जायमानं दृष्टम् अतो "दृष्टवददृष्टकल्पना"इति न्यायात् अत्रापि कर्मैव न तु पिशाचादि कारणं कल्प्यते, किं तु तदन्यदेहस्थं -- देहान्तरोपचितम् -- इत्यर्थः ॥ १२४ ॥
