## Chapter 16

**16:1** [^1]Dispatch as messenger  
The ruler of the land,  
From Sela in the wilderness  
To the mount of Fair Zion: ^16-1

**16:2** “Like fugitive birds,  
Like nestlings driven away,  
Moab’s villagers linger  
By the fords of the Arnon. ^16-2

**16:3** Give advice,  
Offer counsel.[^2]  
At high noon make  
Your shadow like night:  
Conceal the outcasts,  
Betray not the fugitives. ^16-3

**16:4** Let Moab’s outcasts[^3]  
Find asylum in you;  
Be a shelter for them  
Against the despoiler.”  
For violence has vanished,  
Rapine is ended,  
And marauders have perished from this land. ^16-4

**16:5** And a throne shall be established in goodness  
In the tent of David,  
And on it shall sit in faithfulness  
A ruler devoted to justice  
And zealous for equity.[^4] ^16-5

**16:6** “We have heard of Moab’s pride—  
Most haughty is he—  
Of his pride and haughtiness and arrogance,  
And of the iniquity in him.”[^5] ^16-6

**16:7** Ah, let Moab howl;  
Let all in Moab howl!  
For the raisin-cakes[^6] of Kir-hareseth  
You shall moan most pitifully. ^16-7

**16:8** The vineyards of Heshbon are withered,  
And the vines of Sibmah;  
Their tendrils spread  
To Baale-goiim,[^7]  
And reached to Jazer,  
And strayed to the desert;  
Their shoots spread out  
And crossed the sea. ^16-8

**16:9** Therefore,  
As I weep for Jazer,  
So I weep for Sibmah’s vines;  
O Heshbon and Elealeh,  
I drench you with my tears.  
Ended are the shouts  
Over your fig and grain harvests.[^8] ^16-9

**16:10** Rejoicing and gladness  
Are gone from the farm land;  
In the vineyards no shouting  
Or cheering is heard.  
No more does the treader  
Tread wine in the presses—  
The shouts have been silenced.[^9] ^16-10

**16:11** Therefore,  
Like a lyre my heart moans for Moab,  
And my very soul for Kir-heres. ^16-11

**16:12** And when it has become apparent that Moab has gained nothing in the outdoor shrine, he shall come to pray in his temple—but to no avail. ^16-12

**16:13** That is the word that the LORD spoke concerning Moab long ago. ^16-13

**16:14** And now the LORD has spoken: In three years, fixed like the years of a hired laborer, Moab’s population, with all its huge multitude, shall shrink. Only a remnant shall be left, of no consequence. ^16-14

[^1]: Meaning of vv. 1 and 2 uncertain.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Heb. “my outcasts, Moab.”
[^4]: 14.32, above, would read well here.
[^5]: Baddaw is a suffixed form of the preposition bede: Nah. 2.13; Hab. 2.13; Job 39.25; with suffixes, Job 11.3, 41.4.
[^6]: Jer. 48.36 has “men.”
[^7]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^8]: Jer. 48.32 reads “A ravager has come down / Upon your fig and grape harvests.”
[^9]: Lit. “I have silenced.”
