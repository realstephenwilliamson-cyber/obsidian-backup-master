# LXX Psalms 122

← [[LXX Psalms 121]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 123]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 123]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 123]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**122:1** A Song of Degrees. Unto thee who dwellest in heaven have I lifted up mine eyes.  ^122-1

**122:2** Behold, as the eyes of servants *are directed* to the hands of their masters, *and* as the eyes of a maid-servant to the hands of her mistress; so our eyes *are directed* to the Lord our God, until he have mercy upon us.  ^122-2

**122:3** Have pity upon us, O Lord, have pity upon us: for we are exceedingly filled with contempt.  ^122-3

**122:4** *Yea*, our soul has been exceedingly filled *with it*: *let* the reproach *be* to them that are at ease, and contempt to the proud.[^1]  ^122-4

[^1]: 122:4 *Or,* we are the reproach of them that are at ease.
