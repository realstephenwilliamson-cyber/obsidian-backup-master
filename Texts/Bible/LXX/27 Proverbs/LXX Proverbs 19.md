# LXX Proverbs 19

← [[LXX Proverbs 18]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Proverbs 20]] →

*Proverbs · [[LXX]]*

**19:3** The folly of a man spoils his ways: and he blames God in his heart.  ^19-3

**19:4** Wealth acquires many friends; but the poor is deserted even of the friend he has.  ^19-4

**19:5** A false witness shall not be unpunished, and he that accuses unjustly shall not escape.  ^19-5

**19:6** Many court the favour of kings; but every bad man becomes a reproach to *another* man.  ^19-6

**19:7** Every one who hates *his* poor brother shall also be far from friendship. Good understanding will draw near to them that know it, and a sensible man will find it. He that does much harm perfects mischief; and he that uses provoking words shall not escape.  ^19-7

**19:8** He that procures wisdom loves himself; and he that keeps wisdom shall find good.  ^19-8

**19:9** A false witness shall not be unpunished; and whosoever shall kindle mischief shall perish by it.  ^19-9

**19:10** Delight does not suit a fool, nor *is it seemly* if a servant should begin to rule with haughtiness.  ^19-10

**19:11** A merciful man is long-suffering; and his triumph overtakes transgressors.[^1]  ^19-11

**19:12** The threatening of a king is like the roaring of a lion; but as dew on the grass, so is his favour.  ^19-12

**19:13** A foolish son is a disgrace to his father: vows *paid out* of the hire of a harlot are not pure.  ^19-13

**19:14** Fathers divide house and substance to *their* children: but a wife is suited to a man by the Lord.  ^19-14

**19:15** Cowardice possesses the effeminate *man*; and the soul of the sluggard shall hunger.[^2]  ^19-15

**19:16** He that keeps the commandment keeps his own soul; but he that despises his ways shall perish.  ^19-16

**19:17** He that has pity on the poor lends to the Lord; and he will recompense to him according to his gift.  ^19-17

**19:18** Chasten thy son, for so he shall be hopeful; and be not exalted in thy soul to haughtiness.  ^19-18

**19:19** A malicious man shall be severely punished, and if he commit injury, he shall also lose his life.  ^19-19

**19:20** Hear, son, the instruction of thy father, that thou mayest be wise at thy latter end.  ^19-20

**19:21** *There are* many thoughts in a man's heart; but the counsel of the Lord abides for ever.  ^19-21

**19:22** Mercy is a fruit to a man: and a poor man is better than a rich liar.  ^19-22

**19:23** The fear of the Lord is life to a man: and he shall lodge without fear in places where knowledge is not seen.[^3]  ^19-23

**19:24** He that unjustly hides his hands in his bosom, will not even *bring* them up to his mouth.  ^19-24

**19:25** When a pestilent character is scourged, a simple man is made wiser: and if thou reprove a wise man, he will understand discretion.  ^19-25

**19:26** He that dishonours his father, and drives away his mother, shall be disgraced and shall be exposed to reproach.  ^19-26

**19:27** A son who ceases to attend to the instruction of a father will cherish evil designs.  ^19-27

**19:28** He that becomes surety for a foolish child will despise the ordinance: and the mouth of ungodly men shall drink down judgments.  ^19-28

**19:29** Scourges are preparing for the intemperate, and punishments likewise for fools.  ^19-29

[^1]: 19:11 *Gr.* boasting comes upon.
[^2]: 19:15 *Or,* keeps down.
[^3]: 19:23 *Or,*'but he that is without fear (sc. of the Lord) shall dwell' etc.
