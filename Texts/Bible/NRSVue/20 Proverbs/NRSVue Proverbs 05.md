# Proverbs 5 (NRSVue)

## Warning against impurity and infidelity

**5:1** My child, be attentive to my wisdom; incline your ear to my understanding, ^5-1

**5:2** so that you may hold on to prudence, and your lips may guard knowledge. ^5-2

**5:3** For the lips of a loose woman^[Heb *strange woman*] drip honey, and her speech is smoother than oil, ^5-3

**5:4** but in the end she is bitter as wormwood, sharp as a two-edged sword. ^5-4

**5:5** Her feet go down to death; her steps follow the path to Sheol. ^5-5

**5:6** She does not keep straight to the path of life; her ways wander, and she does not know it. ^5-6

**5:7** And now, my child,^[Gk Vg: Heb *children*] listen to me, and do not depart from the words of my mouth. ^5-7

**5:8** Keep your way far from her, and do not go near the door of her house, ^5-8

**5:9** lest you give your honor to others and your years to the merciless, ^5-9

**5:10** and strangers take their fill of your wealth, and your labors go to the house of an alien, ^5-10

**5:11** and at the end of your life you groan, when your flesh and body are consumed, ^5-11

**5:12** and you say, “Oh, how I hated discipline, and my heart despised reproof! ^5-12

**5:13** I did not listen to the voice of my teachers or incline my ear to my instructors. ^5-13

**5:14** Now I am at the point of utter ruin in the public assembly.” ^5-14

**5:15** Drink water from your own cistern, flowing water from your own well. ^5-15

**5:16** Should your springs be scattered abroad, streams of water in the streets? ^5-16

**5:17** Let them be for yourself alone and not for sharing with strangers. ^5-17

**5:18** Let your fountain be blessed, and rejoice in the wife of your youth, ^5-18

**5:19** a lovely deer, a graceful doe. May her breasts satisfy you at all times; may you be intoxicated always by her love. ^5-19

**5:20** Why should you be intoxicated, my son, by another woman and embrace the bosom of an adulteress?^[Heb *alien woman*] ^5-20

**5:21** For human ways are under the eyes of the LORD, and he examines all their paths. ^5-21

**5:22** The iniquities of the wicked ensnare them, and they are caught in the coils of their sin. ^5-22

**5:23** They die for lack of discipline, and because of their great folly they are lost. ^5-23
