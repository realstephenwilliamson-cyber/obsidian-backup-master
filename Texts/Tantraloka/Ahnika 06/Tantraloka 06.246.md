## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 13 - Chapter Six, part 6#^pg461|p. 446]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=461>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 6.246cd–247ab.
>
> Such is the arising of the classes (of phonemes) in gross form. Now we will talk about the arising of the (individual) phonemes. Each letter, once a day and night, is equivalent to two hundred and sixteen breaths, that takes up thirty-six caṣakas of external time. (with 6.245cd–246)

*Lead-in:* तदाह --

> एकैकवर्णे प्राणानां द्विशतं षोडशाधिकम् ।
> बहिश्चषकषट्त्रिंशद्दिन इत्थं तथा निशि ॥

चषकषट्त्रिंशदिति, प्रतिचषकं षण्णां प्राणचाराणामुदयात् । तेन पञ्चाशतो वर्णानां साष्टशतं सहस्रदशकं प्राणचाराणां स्यात् ॥ २४६ ॥
