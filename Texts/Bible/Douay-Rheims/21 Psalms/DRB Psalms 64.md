# Psalms 64 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 63|← Psalms 63]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 65|Psalms 65 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 64|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**64:1** To the end, a psalm of David. The canticle of Jeremias and Ezechiel to the people of the captivity, when they began to go out. ^64-1

**64:2** A Hymn, O God, becometh thee in Sion: and a vow shall be paid to thee in Jerusalem. ^64-2

**64:3** O hear my prayer: all flesh shall come to thee. ^64-3

**64:4** The words of the wicked have prevailed over us: and thou wilt pardon our transgressions. ^64-4

**64:5** Blessed is he whom thou hast chosen and taken to thee: he shall dwell in thy courts. We shall be filled with the good things of thy house; holy is thy temple, ^64-5

**64:6** Wonderful in justice. Hear us, O God our saviour, who art the hope of all the ends of the earth, and in the sea afar off. ^64-6

**64:7** Thou who preparest the mountains by thy strength, being girded with power: ^64-7

**64:8** Who troublest the depth of the sea, the noise of its waves. The Gentiles shall be troubled, ^64-8

**64:9** And they that dwell in the uttermost borders shall be afraid at thy signs: thou shalt make the outgoings of the morning and of the evening to be joyful. ^64-9

**64:10** Thou hast visited the earth, and hast plentifully watered it; thou hast many ways enriched it. The river of God is filled with water, thou hast prepared their food: for so is its preparation. ^64-10

**64:11** Fill up plentifully the streams thereof, multiply its fruits; it shall spring up and rejoice in its showers. ^64-11

**64:12** Thou shalt bless the crown of the year of thy goodness: and thy fields shall be filled with plenty. ^64-12

**64:13** The beautiful places of the wilderness shall grow fat: and the hills shall be girded about with joy, ^64-13

**64:14** The rams of the flock are clothed, and the vales shall abound with corn: they shall shout, yea they shall sing a hymn. ^64-14
