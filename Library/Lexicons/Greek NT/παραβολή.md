# παραβολή

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · noun

**Dodson:** a parable, comparison — (a) a comparison, (b) a parable, often of those uttered by our Lord, (c) a proverb, an adage. (Strong's 3850; headword παραβολή, ῆς, ἡ)
