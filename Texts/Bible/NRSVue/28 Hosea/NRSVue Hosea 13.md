# Hosea 13 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Hosea 12|← Hosea 12]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Hosea 14|Hosea 14 →]]

## Relentless judgment on Israel

**13:1** When Ephraim spoke, there was trembling; he was exalted in Israel, but he incurred guilt through Baal and died. ^13-1

**13:2** And now they keep on sinning and make a cast image for themselves, idols of silver made according to their understanding, all of them the work of artisans. “Sacrifice to these,” they say.^[Cn Compare Gk: Heb *To these they say sacrifices of people*] People are kissing calves! ^13-2

**13:3** Therefore they shall be like the morning mist or like the dew that goes away early, like chaff that swirls from the threshing floor or like smoke from a window. ^13-3

**13:4** Yet I have been the LORD your God ever since the land of Egypt; you know no God but me, and besides me there is no savior. ^13-4

**13:5** It was I who fed^[Gk Syr: Heb *knew*] you in the wilderness, in the land of drought. ^13-5

**13:6** When I fed^[Cn: Heb *according to their pasture*] them, they were satisfied; they were satisfied, and their heart was proud; therefore they forgot me. ^13-6

**13:7** So I will become like a lion to them; like a leopard I will lurk beside the way. ^13-7

**13:8** I will fall upon them like a bear robbed of her cubs and will tear open the covering of their heart; there I will devour them like a lion, as a wild animal would mangle them. ^13-8

**13:9** I will destroy you, O Israel; who can help you?^[Gk Syr: Heb *for in me is your help*] ^13-9

**13:10** Where now is^[Gk Syr Vg: Heb *I will be*] your king, that he may save you? Where in all your cities are your rulers, of whom you said, “Give me a king and rulers”? ^13-10

**13:11** I gave you a king in my anger, and I took him away in my wrath. ^13-11

**13:12** Ephraim’s iniquity is bound up; his sin is kept in store. ^13-12

**13:13** The pangs of childbirth come for him, but he is an unwise son, for at the proper time he does not present himself at the mouth of the womb. ^13-13

**13:14** Shall I ransom them from the power of Sheol? Shall I redeem them from Death? O Death, where are^[Gk Syr: Heb *I will be*] your plagues? O Sheol, where is^[Gk Syr: Heb *I will be*] your destruction? Compassion is hidden from my eyes. ^13-14

**13:15** Although he may flourish among rushes,^[Or *among brothers*] the east wind shall come, a blast from the LORD, rising from the wilderness, and his fountain shall dry up; his spring shall be parched. It shall strip his treasury of every precious thing. ^13-15

**13:16** ^[14.1 in Heb]Samaria shall bear her guilt because she has rebelled against her God; they shall fall by the sword; their little ones shall be dashed in pieces, and their pregnant women ripped open. ^13-16
