# Psalm 141 (NRSVue)

## Prayer for preservation from evil

*A Psalm of David.*

**141:1** I call upon you, O LORD; come quickly to me; give ear to my voice when I call to you. ^141-1

**141:2** Let my prayer be counted as incense before you and the lifting up of my hands as an evening sacrifice. ^141-2

**141:3** Set a guard over my mouth, O LORD; keep watch over the door of my lips. ^141-3

**141:4** Do not turn my heart to any evil, to busy myself with wicked deeds in company with those who work iniquity; do not let me eat of their delicacies. ^141-4

**141:5** Let the righteous strike me; let the faithful correct me. Never let the oil of the wicked anoint my head,^[Gk: Meaning of Heb uncertain] for my prayer is continually^[Cn: Heb *for continually and my prayer*] against their wicked deeds. ^141-5

**141:6** When they are given over to those who shall condemn them, then they shall learn that my words were pleasant. ^141-6

**141:7** Like a rock that one breaks apart and shatters on the land, so shall their bones be strewn at the mouth of Sheol.^[Meaning of Heb of 141.5–7 is uncertain] ^141-7

**141:8** But my eyes are turned toward you, O GOD, my Lord; in you I seek refuge; do not leave me defenseless. ^141-8

**141:9** Keep me from the trap that they have laid for me and from the snares of evildoers. ^141-9

**141:10** Let the wicked fall into their own nets, while I alone escape. ^141-10
