As far as we can see, his most impressive work consisted of two books On Truth, hich seem to have presented a complete cosmology along with an analysis of human condition. Commentary

There is a recent edition of Antiphon, with translation and detailed commenta Pendrick 2002, which I have consulted and drawn on heavily.

1-10. Ancient sources are divided about the identity of Antiphon. Antiphon Rhamnous was a notable figure of Athenian politics and a speech-writer, w was put to death in 411 Bc. There was a later poet put to death by Dionysiu of Syracuse. There may have been a third Antiphon, a sophist and soothsay roughly contemporary with the politician but not identical with him, Sor authorities identify the sophist with the politician (recently, Hourcade 200 Gagarin 2002), while others distinguish them (Pendrick). The evidence consi of historical references and also questions of style and content.

The earliest reference to Antiphon “the Sophist” is that of Xenophon in which portrays him as a philosophical rival to Socrates. He mocks Socrates |
his poverty and provides for the latter an occasion for homilies on what is mi important in life.

In 9 Hermogenes wavers between the two views on stylistic and histori grounds. He identifies three works which are securely by the same author, t On Truth, On Concord, and Statesman. While we cannot say for certain whet the rwo Antiphons are identical, we can with Hermogenes note that even if this only one Antiphon, his two alter egos can for practical purposes be treat separately. And in the present work we are concerned with the sophist, not!
