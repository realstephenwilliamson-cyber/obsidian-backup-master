# LXX Jonah 02

← [[LXX Jonah 01]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Jonah 03]] →

*Jonah · [[LXX]]*

**2:1** Now the Lord had commanded a great whale to swallow up Jonas: and Jonas was in the belly of the whale three days and three nights.  ^2-1

**2:2** And Jonas prayed to the Lord his God out of the belly of the whale,  ^2-2

**2:3** and said, I cried in my affliction to the Lord my God, and he hearkened to me, *even* to my cry out of the belly of hell: thou heardest my voice.  ^2-3

**2:4** Thou didst cast me into the depths of the heart of the sea, and the floods compassed me: all thy billows and thy waves have passed upon me.  ^2-4

**2:5** And I said, I am cast out of thy presence: shall I indeed look again toward thy holy temple?  ^2-5

**2:6** Water was poured around me to the soul: the lowest deep compassed me, my head went down[^1]  ^2-6

**2:7** to the clefts of the mountains; I went down into the earth, whose bars are the everlasting barriers: yet, O Lord my God, let my ruined life be restored.  ^2-7

**2:8** When my soul was failing me, I remembered the Lord; and may my prayer come to thee into thy holy temple.[^2]  ^2-8

**2:9** They that observe vanities and lies have forsaken their own mercy.[^3]  ^2-9

**2:10** But I will sacrifice to thee with the voice of praise and thanksgiving: all that I have vowed I will pay to thee, the Lord of *my* salvation.[^4]  ^2-10

**2:11** And the whale was commanded by the Lord, and it cast up Jonas on the dry *land*.  ^2-11

[^1]: 2:6 *Gr.* last.
[^2]: 2:8 *Gr.* from me.
[^3]: 2:9 *Gr.* vain and false *things.*
[^4]: 2:10 *Or,* for a thank-offering to the Lord.
