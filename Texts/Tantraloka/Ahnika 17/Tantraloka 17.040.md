## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 08 - Chapter Seventeen#^pg180|p. 160]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=180>))
>
> The fifteen mantras (that are used) for the purification (that takes place) by merger are, in due order, the group of eight beginning with Pibanī, the group of six (limbs) beginning with the Weapon, and Parā. (with 17.39cd–40ab)
>
> “Having uttered the mantra of Aparā (HRĪḤ HŪM PHAT), the teacher should first of all say that: “I perform for the soul of so-and-so (who is within the Earth principle, the rite of) impregnation”. Then, while he utters that same mantra (of Aparā) and ending with SVĀHĀ, he should offer three oblations. After that, having uttered the mantra of Parāparā, (the teacher should say): “I perform for the soul of so-and-so, who has (just been) born, (the rite by which he is given the capacity) to be the one who experiences what is to be experienced (as the consequence of past Karma) (bhogabhoktrtva).” (Then,) having uttered (the mantra) of Parāparā and SVĀHĀ at the end, he should offer three oblations.” (After that) once uttered the mantra of Pibanī (PIBA HE RURU RARA PHAT HUM HAH PHAT), (he should say): “I perform for the soul of so-and-so (the rite of) merger into (his) worldly experience (bhoga)”, and (whilst uttering) again the mantra (of Pibanī), ending with SVĀHĀ, he should offer three oblations, with clarified butter and sesame seeds etc. The procedure is the same for Vamanī and the rest, up to the end of the fifteen mantras mentioned (above). (with 17.40cd–45ab)
