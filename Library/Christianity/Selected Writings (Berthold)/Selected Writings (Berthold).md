# Maximus Confessor: Selected Writings

Translation and notes by George C. Berthold, introduction by Jaroslav Pelikan, preface by Irénée-Henri Dalmais. Paulist Press, New York and Mahwah, 1985. The Classics of Western Spirituality.

Converted from the PDF on 12 Oct 2026 (the PDF's Adobe Paper Capture OCR layer, and a fresh OCR (Tesseract 5, eng) on the 66 pages where the layer has many misread words; each PDF page shows two book pages). Each PDF page starts with a line `*[p. N]*` giving the printed page number (or the PDF page where none is printed) and ending in the block id `^pgK`, K being the PDF page, so a link to PDF page K can be written `[[Chapter note#^pgK]]`. > [!note] Scan of two book pages per PDF page; OCR text
> Each PDF page shows two book pages, so each page marker gives both page numbers. The text is the PDF's own OCR layer, except on 66 of the 130 PDF pages, where the layer had many misread words and a fresh OCR of the page images replaces it. On those pages the small raised note numbers are often lost or misread ("®" for 9), italics are lost, and on a few of them specks in the gutter come out as stray letters ("i", "Disa s").
