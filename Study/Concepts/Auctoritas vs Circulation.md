# Auctoritas vs Circulation

> [!abstract] Definition
> The breakdown of the classical Hobbesian maxim *auctoritas, non veritas, facit legem* ("authority, not truth, makes law") in the algorithmic epoch, wherein neither truth nor sovereign authority binds human action, leaving **circulation** as the sole governing metric of reality.

## The Hobbesian Inversion
Hobbes argued in *Leviathan* that to avert civil war, sovereign authority must settle disputes by fixing the public meaning of words: words bind because the sovereign backs them with the sword.

In [[Sea Peoples flowchart 2026-07-24|Let's Just Blame the Sea Peoples]] (Movement One, Piece 2):
- Contemporary political speech reveals that even the most powerful head of state can declare an event or war concluded thirty-eight times, and the declaration binds nothing—not markets, missiles, nor bureaucracy.
- A performative utterance that functioned would need saying only once.
- In informational saturation, words cost nothing to produce and nothing to circulate. Authority does not bind law; velocity and volume of transmission replace authority.

## Diagnostic Core
The crisis of speech in digital culture is not simply "truth's weakness" (which reduces to cynicism), but the **unboundness of words**: the severing of language from institutional, physical, and performative accountability.
