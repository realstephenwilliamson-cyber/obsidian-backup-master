## Mūla

तत्रोक्तलक्षणः कर्मयोगज्ञानविशारदः ।
उत्तरोत्तरताभूम्युत्कृष्टो गुरुरुदीरितः ॥ ^mula

tatroktalakṣaṇaḥ karmayogajñānaviśāradaḥ |
uttarottaratābhūmyutkṛṣṭo gururudīritaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 02 - Chapter Fifteen, part 1#^pg34|p. 16]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=34>))
>
> The teacher, whose characteristics have been described, can be an expert in the rites, Yoga or knowledge (of the scriptures). The second is better than the first and the last is more excellent than the previous two.
