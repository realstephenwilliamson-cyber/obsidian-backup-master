### 3.1–6: Sardis

*[[NRSVue Revelation 03#^3-1|Text]], p. 545* ^3-1

**[[NRSVue Revelation 03#^3-1|1]]:** *Sardis*, a wealthy inland city; Josephus, *Ant* 14.235,259–61, describes the success of its Jewish community later reflected in its enormous synagogue, built and expanded over the third through seventh centuries ce. From its Hellenistic design, sheer size, and proximity to the city gymnasium, the synagogue points to a comfortable relationship between Jews and others in Sardis. *Seven spirits*, see 1.4n. *Seven stars*, see 1.16n.; 1.20. ^3-1-2

**[[NRSVue Revelation 03#^3-2|2]]:** *Perfect in the sight of my God*, the congregations are supposed to be seeking perfection in purity and comportment to stand in the presence of God and serve in the heavenly liturgy (7.15; 8.2). The use of the possessive pronoun (*my God*) may refer to the Gospels’ tradition of Jesus’ last words ([[Mark 15#^15-34|Mk 15.34]], after [[Psalms 022#^22-2|Ps 22.2]]). ^3-2

**[[NRSVue Revelation 03#^3-3|3]]:** *Thief*, likely reference to Jesus’ aphorism about the surprise of the eschaton’s arrival, [[Matthew 24#^24-42|Mt 24.42–44]]. ^3-3

**[[NRSVue Revelation 03#^3-4|4]]:** *Soiled their clothes* (see 22.11), ritually impure garments, which might be replaced with clean ones (see 3.18; 7.14; 19.14); John regards the congregation as impure in a moral, not necessarily cultic, sense. ^3-4

**[[NRSVue Revelation 03#^3-5|5]]:** *White robes*, see 1.14n. *Book of life*, [[Psalms 069#^69-29|Ps 69.28]]; [[Daniel 12#^12-1|Dan 12.1]], based on Mesopotamian models; the list of those who are redeemed. See also 13.8; 17.8; 20.12,15. ^3-5

### 3.7–13: Philadelphia

*[[NRSVue Revelation 03#^3-7|Text]], p. 546* ^3-7

**[[NRSVue Revelation 03#^3-7|7]]:** *Philadelphia*, hill-town in western Asia Minor, known for its trade-guilds; it suffered a series of terrible earthquakes from 17 CE on (Pliny, *Nat.* 2.86.200; Strabo, *Geogr*. 12.8.18; 13.4.10). There is no evidence predating the third century ce of Jews there, though their presence earlier cannot be ruled out. *Key of David . . . shut*, [[Isaiah 22#^22-22|Isa 22.22]]. As in [[Matthew 17#^17-9|Mt 17.9]]; [[Matthew 18#^18-18|18.18]], the key symbolizes authority. ^3-7-2

**[[NRSVue Revelation 03#^3-8|8]]:** *Open door . . . shut*, an opportunity that no one can prevent them from acting on. ^3-8

**[[NRSVue Revelation 03#^3-9|9]]:** *Synagogue of Satan,* see “So-Called Jews and Their Synagogue of Satan,” p. 543. ^3-9

**[[NRSVue Revelation 03#^3-11|11]]:** *Crown*, see 2.10n. ^3-11

**[[NRSVue Revelation 03#^3-12|12]]:** *Pillar in the temple*, symbol of permanence. Revelation refers often to the symbolic value of the heavenly Temple’s furniture and infrastructure (4.5; 11.4). *New Jerusalem*, see chs 21–22. Some early Jewish apocalyptic writers and prophets imagined God’s Temple in heaven, a utopian Jerusalem, that would appear or descend at the end of time (e.g., in various formulations, Ezek 40–48; *1 En.* 90.28–29; *2 Bar.* 4). The New Prophecy, an early second-century Christian movement that started in Asia Minor, expected the new Jerusalem in the town of Pepouza. See “The Heavenly Temple Cult,” p. 553 and “A Holy City Without a Holy Temple,” p. 573. *New name*, see 2.17n. ^3-12

### 3.14–22: Laodicea

*[[NRSVue Revelation 03#^3-14|Text]], p. 546* ^3-14

**[[NRSVue Revelation 03#^3-14|14]]:** *Laodicea*, important regional city that suffered earthquakes in 60 CE; it had a considerable Jewish community (Josephus, *Ant.* 12.147–53). *Amen*, Heb assent to prayer; used as a title for Jesus in [[2 Corinthians 01#^1-20|2 Cor 1.20]], meaning the definitive acceptance of God’s will. *Origin of God’s creation*, or “beginning” (see translators’ note *a*); see also [[John 01#^1-1|Jn 1.1–3]]. ^3-14-2

**[[NRSVue Revelation 03#^3-18|18]]:** *Gold refined by fire*, metaphor for purification from sin; see [[Proverbs 27#^27-21|Prov 27.21]]. *White robes*, see 1.14n. ^3-18

**[[NRSVue Revelation 03#^3-19|19]]:** God is the one who reproves those he loves; see [[Proverbs 03#^3-12|Prov 3.12]]. ^3-19
