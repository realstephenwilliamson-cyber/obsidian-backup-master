## Mūla

निष्फला पुनरुक्तिस्तु नास्मभ्यं जातु रोचते ।
इत्येवं मन्त्रविद्यादिस्वरूपमुपवर्णितम् ॥ ^mula

niṣphalā punaruktistu nāsmabhyaṃ jātu rocate |
ityevaṃ mantravidyādisvarūpamupavarṇitam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 03 - Chapter Thirty, part 2#^pg82|p. 64]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=82>))
>
> (The nature of) the virility (of Mantras) has (already) been taught here previously. The teacher should proceed accordingly everywhere (in all instances) in accord with (their) purpose, (the form of their) seed syllables, the (manner in which they induce) penetration into (the supreme reality), and the (manner of their) utterance, etc. There is nothing about (all) this that has not already been explained. Repetition is fruitless and we do not like it at all. (with 30.121cd–123ab)
>
> The nature of the Mantras and Vidyā etc. has thus been described. (with 30.123cd)
