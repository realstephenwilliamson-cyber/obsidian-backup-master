## Mūla

अपरेयं समाख्याता रुद्रशक्तिं परां शृणु ।
मन्त्राः संमुखतां यान्ति ययोच्चारितमात्रया ॥ ^mula

apareyaṁ samākhyātā rudraśaktiṁ parāṁ śṛṇu |
mantrāḥ saṁmukhatāṁ yānti yayoccāritamātrayā || ^iast

## Translation

> **Parā Vidyā**
>
> This Aparā (Vidyā) has been taught. (Now) listen to Parā, Rudra's (supreme) power. By just uttering it (all) Mantras (come before one who utters it and) become propense (to act) (*sanmukha*).
