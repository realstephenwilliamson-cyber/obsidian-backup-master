## Mūla

हृच्छूलग्रन्थिभेदैश्चिद्रुद्रशक्तिं प्रबोधयेत् ।
वायुचक्रान्तनिलयं बिन्द्वाख्यं नाभिमण्डलम् ॥ ^mula

hṛcchūlagranthibhedaiścidrudraśaktiṃ prabodhayet |
vāyucakrāntanilayaṃ bindvākhyaṃ nābhimaṇḍalam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 06 - Chapter Thirty-Two#^pg174|p. 156]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=174>))
>
> (There,) consciousness should awaken Rudra’s power by moving through the five seeds, by progressively breaking through the Trident, along with the various knots of the trident of the heart. (with 32.43–44ab)
>
> (In this way, the yogi) comes to the sphere of the navel (nābhimandala), located near the Wheel of the Wind, (then) to the location of the uvula (lambikāsthāna) (at the back of the throat), and (above that, to) the one called Bindu, that has come forth from the twelve knots. Then, (descending) again in reverse from the Wheel of the Moon, he should enter into the body made of the gross elements, and again play the game that takes place within the cage of Māyā. (Then,) by means of the Skyfaring (Goddess), the wise should again operate emission and re-absorption.’ This is the procedure concerning the Skyfaring Goddess, found in (the section concerning) Yoga in the venerable Virāāvalī. (with 32.44cd–47ab)
