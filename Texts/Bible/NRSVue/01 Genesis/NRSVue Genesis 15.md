# Genesis 15 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Genesis 14|← Genesis 14]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Genesis 16|Genesis 16 →]]

## God’s covenant with Abram

**15:1** After these things the word of the LORD came to Abram in a vision, “Do not be afraid, Abram, I am your shield; your reward shall be very great.” ^15-1

**15:2** But Abram said, “O Lord GOD, what will you give me, for I continue childless, and the heir of my house is Eliezer of Damascus?”^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^15-2

**15:3** And Abram said, “You have given me no offspring, so a slave born in my house is to be my heir.” ^15-3

**15:4** But the word of the LORD came to him, “This man shall not be your heir; no one but your very own issue shall be your heir.” ^15-4

**15:5** He brought him outside and said, “Look toward heaven and count the stars, if you are able to count them.” Then he said to him, “So shall your descendants be.” ^15-5

**15:6** And he believed the LORD, and the LORD^[Heb *he*] reckoned it to him as righteousness. ^15-6

**15:7** Then he said to him, “I am the LORD who brought you from Ur of the Chaldeans, to give you this land to possess.” ^15-7

**15:8** But he said, “O Lord GOD, how am I to know that I shall possess it?” ^15-8

**15:9** He said to him, “Bring me a heifer three years old, a female goat three years old, a ram three years old, a turtledove, and a young pigeon.” ^15-9

**15:10** He brought him all these and cut them in two, laying each half over against the other, but he did not cut the birds in two. ^15-10

**15:11** And when birds of prey came down on the carcasses, Abram drove them away. ^15-11

**15:12** As the sun was going down, a deep sleep fell upon Abram, and a deep and terrifying darkness descended upon him. ^15-12

**15:13** Then the LORD^[Heb *he*] said to Abram, “Know this for certain, that your offspring shall be aliens in a land that is not theirs and shall be slaves there, and they shall be oppressed for four hundred years, ^15-13

**15:14** but I will bring judgment on the nation that they serve, and afterward they shall come out with great possessions. ^15-14

**15:15** As for yourself, you shall go to your ancestors in peace; you shall be buried in a good old age. ^15-15

**15:16** And they shall come back here in the fourth generation, for the iniquity of the Amorites is not yet complete.” ^15-16

**15:17** When the sun had gone down and it was dark, a smoking fire pot and a flaming torch passed between these pieces. ^15-17

**15:18** On that day the LORD made a covenant with Abram, saying, “To your descendants I give this land, from the river of Egypt to the great river, the River Euphrates, ^15-18

**15:19** the land of the Kenites, the Kenizzites, the Kadmonites, ^15-19

**15:20** the Hittites, the Perizzites, the Rephaim, ^15-20

**15:21** the Amorites, the Canaanites, the Girgashites, and the Jebusites.” ^15-21
