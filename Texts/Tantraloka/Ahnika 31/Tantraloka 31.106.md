## Mūla

अष्टांगुलप्रमाणैः स्याद्धस्तमात्रं समन्ततः ।
शूलाग्रं शूलमध्यं तच्छूलमूलं तु तद्भवेत् ॥ ^mula

aṣṭāṃgulapramāṇaiḥ syāddhastamātraṃ samantataḥ |
śūlāgraṃ śūlamadhyaṃ tacchūlamūlaṃ tu tadbhavet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 05 - Chapter Thirty-One, part 2#^pg128|p. 110]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=128>))
>
> ‘Having fashioned the staff, (one should draw) the trident with three squares measuring eight finger-breadths all around, (that is to say) just one handspan on all sides.’ (with 31.105cd–106ab)
>
> ‘That (group of three squares) consists of the tip of the trident, the middle of the trident, and the base of the trident. In the middle of it, one should fashion an enclosure (vedī), both sides of which (extend for) six finger-breadths, and is twelve finger-breadths long on both sides (that is, above and below, respectively). In the base (of the trident), one should fashion (another) enclosure, the shape of a half-moon, four finger-breadths high, and the same on both sides. (with 31.106cd–108)
