## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 09 - Chapter Thirteen, part 5#^pg313|p. 291]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=313>))
>
> Just as a king bestows his favour on somebody because he has the freedom (to do so), similarly, Viṣṇu and the other (deities) are capable (of gracing their devotees) because they are penetrated by the Lord’s power. (with 13.270cd–271ab)
>
> Thus some (wise yogi), due to (a weak) descent of the power (of grace), which is fit to function (only) within the womb of Māyā, discriminates the soul from Nature and has gone (beyond) Nature. Someone (else), by virtue of a more elevated (descent of power) than that, having quickly understood the distinction (vivecitā) between the soul and the Force (of limited agency) (kalā), knows in a moment the difference between the soul and Māyā. (with 13.271cd–273ab)
