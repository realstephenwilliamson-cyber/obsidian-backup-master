# Sirach 43 (Douay-Rheims)

*Ecclesiasticus 43 in the Douay-Rheims*

[[DRB Sirach 42|← Sirach 42]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Sirach 44|Sirach 44 →]]

**43:1** The firmament on high is his beauty, the beauty of heaven with its glorious shew. ^43-1

**43:2** The sun when he appeareth shewing forth at his rising, an admirable instrument, the work of the most High. ^43-2

**43:3** At noon he burneth the earth, and who can abide his burning heat? As one keeping a furnace in the works of heat: ^43-3

**43:4** The sun three times as much, burneth the mountains, breathing out fiery vapours, and shining with his beams, he blindeth the eyes. ^43-4

**43:5** Great is the Lord that made him, and at his words he hath hastened his course. ^43-5

**43:6** And the moon in all in her season, is for a declaration of times and a sign of the world. ^43-6

**43:7** From the moon is the sign of the festival day, a light that decreaseth in her perfection. ^43-7

**43:8** The month is called after her name, increasing wonderfully in her perfection. ^43-8

**43:9** Being an instrument of the armies on high, shining gloriously in the Armament of heaven. ^43-9

**43:10** The glory of the stars is the beauty of heaven; the Lord enlighteneth the world on high. ^43-10

**43:11** By the words of the holy one they shall stand in judgment, and shall never fail in their watches. ^43-11

**43:12** Look upon the rainbow, and bless him that made it: it is very beautiful in its brightness. ^43-12

**43:13** It encompasseth the heaven about with the circle of its glory, the hands of the most High have displayed it. ^43-13

**43:14** By his commandment he maketh the snow to fall apace, and sendeth forth swiftly the lightnings of his judgment. ^43-14

**43:15** Through this are the treasures opened, and the clouds fly out like birds. ^43-15

**43:16** By his greatness he hath fixed the clouds, and the hailstones are broken. ^43-16

**43:17** At his sight shall the mountains be shaken, and at his will the south wind shall blow. ^43-17

**43:18** The noise of his thunder shall strike the earth, so doth the northern storm, and the whirlwind: ^43-18

**43:19** And as the birds lighting upon the earth, he scattereth snow, and the falling thereof, is as the coming down of locusts. ^43-19

**43:20** The eye admireth at the beauty of the whiteness thereof, and the heart is astonished at the shower thereof. ^43-20

**43:21** He shall pour frost as salt upon the earth: and when it freezeth, it shall become like the tops of thistles. ^43-21

**43:22** The cold north wind bloweth, and the water is congealed into crystal; upon every gathering together of waters it shall rest, and shall clothe the waters as a breastplate. ^43-22

**43:23** And it shall devour the mountains, and burn the wilderness, and consume all that is green as with fire. ^43-23

**43:24** A present remedy of all is the speedy coming of a cloud, and a dew that meeteth it, by the heat that cometh, shall overpower it. ^43-24

**43:25** At his word the wind is still, and with his thought he appeaseth the deep, and the Lord hath planted islands therein. ^43-25

**43:26** Let them that sail on the sea, tell the dangers thereof: and when we hear with our ears, we shall admire. ^43-26

**43:27** There are great and wonderful works: a variety of beasts, and of all living things, and the monstrous creatures of whales. ^43-27

**43:28** Through him is established the end of their journey, and by his word all things are regulated. ^43-28

**43:29** We shall say much, and yet shall want words: but the sum of our words is, He is all. ^43-29

**43:30** What shall we be able to do to glorify him? for the Almighty himself is above all his works. ^43-30

**43:31** The Lord is terrible, and exceeding great, and his power is admirable. ^43-31

**43:32** Glorify the Lord as much as ever you can, for he will yet far exceed, and his magnificence is wonderful. ^43-32

**43:33** Blessing the Lord, exalt him as much as you can: for he is above all praise. ^43-33

**43:34** When you exalt him put forth all your strength, and be not weary: for you can never go far enough. ^43-34

**43:35** Who shall see him, and declare him? and who shall magnify him as he is from the beginning? ^43-35

**43:36** There are many things hidden from us that are greater than these: for we have seen but a few of his works. ^43-36

**43:37** But the Lord hath made all things, and to the godly he hath given wisdom. ^43-37
