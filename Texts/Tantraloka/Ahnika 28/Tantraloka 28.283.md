## Mūla

धातुदोषाच्च संसारसंस्कारास्ते प्रबोधिताः ।
छिद्रगा अपि भूयिष्ठज्ञानदग्धा न रोहिणः ॥ ^mula

dhātudoṣācca saṃsārasaṃskārāste prabodhitāḥ |
chidragā api bhūyiṣṭhajñānadagdhā na rohiṇaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 06 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 4#^pg140|p. 123]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=140>))
>
> (In the case of those who possess spiritual knowledge,) the latent traces of (the life of) their transmigratory existence that are awakened by a defect (in the balance of the body’s) constitutive elements and are also present in (their) weak points (chidra) (that mark discontinuities in their practice), are burnt up by (the fire of their) abundant knowledge, and do not develop (again). (Whereas,) those (spiritual) latent impressions (saṁskāra) pertaining to liberation (kaivalyabhāgīya), that have never unfolded when in good health (in the case of those whose spiritual knowledge is less developed, may) also do so when sick, by virtue of a descent of (the Lord’s) power (of grace). (with 28.283–284)
