# Psalms 059

## Chapter 59

**59:1** For the leader; *al tashḥeth*. Of David. A *michtam*; when Saul sent men to watch his house in order to put him to death.[^1] ^59-1

**59:2** Save me from my enemies, O my God;  
secure me against my assailants. ^59-2

**59:3** Save me from evildoers;  
deliver me from murderers. ^59-3

**59:4** For see, they lie in wait for me;  
fierce men plot against me  
for no offense of mine,  
for no transgression, O LORD; ^59-4

**59:5** for no guilt of mine  
do they rush to array themselves against me.  
Look, rouse Yourself on my behalf! ^59-5

**59:6** You, O LORD God of hosts,  
God of Israel,  
bestir Yourself to bring all nations to account;  
have no mercy on any treacherous villain. *Selah*. ^59-6

**59:7** They come each evening growling like dogs,  
roaming the city. ^59-7

**59:8** They rave with their mouths,  
sharp words[^2] are on their lips;  
[they think,] “Who hears?” ^59-8

**59:9** But You, O LORD, laugh at them;  
You mock all the nations. ^59-9

**59:10** O my[^3] strength, I wait for You;  
for God is my haven. ^59-10

**59:11** My faithful God will come to aid me;  
God will let me gloat over my watchful foes. ^59-11

**59:12** Do not kill them lest my people be unmindful;  
with Your power make wanderers of them;  
bring them low, O our shield, the Lord, ^59-12

**59:13** because of their sinful mouths,  
the words on their lips.  
Let them be trapped by their pride,  
and by the imprecations and lies they utter. ^59-13

**59:14** In Your fury put an end to them;  
put an end to them that they be no more;  
that it may be known to the ends of the earth  
that God does rule over Jacob. *Selah*. ^59-14

**59:15** They come each evening growling like dogs,  
roaming the city. ^59-15

**59:16** They wander in search of food;  
and whine if they are not satisfied. ^59-16

**59:17** But I will sing of Your strength,  
extol each morning Your faithfulness;  
for You have been my haven,  
a refuge in time of trouble. ^59-17

**59:18** O my strength, to You I sing hymns;  
for God is my haven, my faithful God. ^59-18

[^1]: Cf. 1 Sam. 19.11.
[^2]: Lit. “swords.”
[^3]: With several mss.; cf. v. 18; lit. “His.”
