## Mūla

अनया विद्वस्य पशोरुपभोगसमर्थता भवति ।
विद्या चास्य कलातः शरणान्तर्दीपकप्रभेवाभूत् ॥ ^mula

anayā vidvasya paśorupabhogasamarthatā bhavati |
vidyā cāsya kalātaḥ śaraṇāntardīpakaprabhevābhūt || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 07 - Chapter Nine, part 5#^pg216|p. 199]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=216>))
>
> A weak force (kalā ślakṣṇā) (which is the limited power to act) is, like a second consciousness (citi), conjoined to that pure (soul). Penetrated by this, the fettered soul is capable of experiencing (the consequences of Karma) (upabhoga). The (limited capacity for) knowledge (vidyā) (the soul) possesses is (derived) from (this) Force (which is its limited capacity to act). Like the light of a lamp set in an alcove (śaraṇa), it discerns the fettered soul’s sensations of pleasure and pain (each) separately (from one another). From (this same) principle, the Force (of limited agency), arises (that of) Attachment. (Colouring the impassioned soul) like red dye (kaṣāyavat) a clean cloth. It is due to this that (the soul) does not wish to give up the joy of the experience (saṁvid) of the pleasures of transmigratory existence. Thus sullied by ignorance and rendered capable (to know and act) by the strength of (this) threefold covering, the fettered soul falls helplessly down headlong into the womb of the worldly experience (upabhoga) of the Abyss (gahana) (of Māyā). (with 9.208cd–212ab)
