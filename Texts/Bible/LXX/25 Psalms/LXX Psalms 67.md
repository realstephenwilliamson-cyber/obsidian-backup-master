# LXX Psalms 67

← [[LXX Psalms 66]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 68]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 068]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 068]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**67:1** For the end, a Psalm of a Song by David.  ^67-1

**67:2** Let God arise, and let his enemies be scattered; and let them that hate him flee from before him.  ^67-2

**67:3** As smoke vanishes, let them vanish: as wax melts before the fire, so let the sinners perish from before God.  ^67-3

**67:4** But let the righteous rejoice; let them exult before God: let them be delighted with joy.  ^67-4

**67:5** Sing to God, sing praises to his name: make a way for him that rides upon the west (the Lord is his name) and exult before him. They shall be troubled before the face of him,  ^67-5

**67:6** *who is* the father of the orphans, and judge of the widows: *such is* God in his holy place.  ^67-6

**67:7** God settles the solitary in a house; leading forth prisoners mightily, also them that act provokingly, *even* them that dwell in tombs.  ^67-7

**67:8** O God, when thou wentest forth before thy people, when thou wentest through the wilderness; Pause:  ^67-8

**67:9** the earth quaked, yea, the heavens dropped *water* at the presence of the God of Sina, at the presence of the God of Israel.  ^67-9

**67:10** O God, thou wilt grant to thine inheritance a gracious rain; for it was weary, but thou didst refresh it.  ^67-10

**67:11** Thy creatures dwell in it: thou hast in thy goodness prepared for the poor.[^1]  ^67-11

**67:12** The Lord God will give a word to them that preach *it* in a great company.  ^67-12

**67:13** The king of the forces of the beloved, of the beloved, *will* even *grant them* for the beauty of the house to divide the spoils.  ^67-13

**67:14** Even if ye should lie among the lots, *ye shall have* the wings of a dove covered with silver, and her breast with yellow gold.[^2]  ^67-14

**67:15** When the heavenly One scatters kings upon it, they shall be made snow-white in Selmon.  ^67-15

**67:16** The mountain of God is a rich mountain; a swelling mountain, a rich mountain.[^3]  ^67-16

**67:17** Wherefore do ye conceive *evil*, ye swelling mountains? *this is* the mountain which God has delighted to dwell in; yea, the Lord will dwell *in it* for ever.  ^67-17

**67:18** The chariots of God are ten thousand fold, thousands of rejoicing ones: the Lord is among them, in Sina, in the holy place.  ^67-18

**67:19** Thou art gone up on high, thou hast led captivity captive, thou hast received gifts for man, yea, for *they were* rebellious, that thou mightest dwell among them.[^4]  ^67-19

**67:20** Blessed be the Lord God, blessed be the Lord daily; and the God of our salvation shall prosper us. Pause.  ^67-20

**67:21** Our God is the God of salvation; and to the Lord belong the issues from death.  ^67-21

**67:22** But God shall crush the heads of his enemies; the hairy crown of them that go on in their trespasses.  ^67-22

**67:23** The Lord said, I will bring again from Basan, I will bring *my people* again through the depths of the sea.  ^67-23

**67:24** That thy foot may be dipped in blood, *and* the tongue of thy dogs *be stained* with that of *thine* enemies.  ^67-24

**67:25** Thy goings, O God, have been seen; the goings of my God, the king, in the sanctuary.  ^67-25

**67:26** The princes went first, next before the players on instruments, in the midst of damsels playing on timbrels.  ^67-26

**67:27** Praise God in the congregations, the Lord from the fountains of Israel.  ^67-27

**67:28** There is Benjamin the younger *one* in ecstasy, the princes of Juda their rulers, the princes of Zabulon, the princes of Nephthali.  ^67-28

**67:29** O God, command thou thy strength: strengthen, O God, this which thou hast wrought in us.  ^67-29

**67:30** Because of thy temple at Jerusalem shall kings bring presents to thee.  ^67-30

**67:31** Rebuke the wild beasts of the reed: let the crowd of bulls with the heifers of the nations *be rebuked*, so that they who have been proved with silver may not be shut out: scatter thou the nations that wish for wars.  ^67-31

**67:32** Ambassadors shall arrive out of Egypt; Ethiopia shall hasten *to stretch out* her hand readily to God.  ^67-32

**67:33** Sing to God, ye kingdoms of the earth; sing psalms to the Lord. Pause.  ^67-33

**67:34** Sing to God that rides on the heaven of heaven, eastward: lo, he will utter a mighty sound with his voice.[^5]  ^67-34

**67:35** Give ye glory to God: his excellency is over Israel, and his power is in the clouds.  ^67-35

**67:36** God is wonderful in his holy *places*, the God of Israel: he will give power and strength to his people: blessed be God.[^6]  ^67-36

[^1]: 67:11 See the *Hebrew.*
[^2]: 67:14 *Lit.* greenness of gold.
[^3]: 67:16 *Gr.* curdled like cheese.
[^4]: 67:19 *Gr.* having gone up.
[^5]: 67:34 *Gr.* mounts, *or,* has mounted.
[^6]: 67:36 *Or,* among his holy ones.
