# Psalm 20

← [[BCP Psalm 019]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 021]] →

*Exaudiat te Dominus* · Book One · Fourth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 020]]

**20:1** May the LORD answer you in the day of trouble, \*  
the Name of the God of Jacob defend you; ^20-1

**20:2** Send you help from his holy place \*  
and strengthen you out of Zion; ^20-2

**20:3** Remember all your offerings \*  
and accept your burnt sacrifice; ^20-3

**20:4** Grant you your heart's desire \*  
and prosper all your plans. ^20-4

**20:5** We will shout for joy at your victory  
and triumph in the Name of our God; \*  
may the LORD grant all your requests. ^20-5

**20:6** Now I know that the LORD gives victory to his anointed; \*  
he will answer him out of his holy heaven,  
with the victorious strength of his right hand. ^20-6

**20:7** Some put their trust in chariots and some in horses, \*  
but we will call upon the Name of the LORD our God. ^20-7

**20:8** They collapse and fall down, \*  
but we will arise and stand upright. ^20-8

**20:9** O LORD, give victory to the king \*  
and answer us when we call. ^20-9
