## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 09 - Chapter Eight, part 7#^pg346|p. 328]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=346>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 8.340cd–341ab.
>
> They are Vāmā (Woman), Jyeṣṭhā (Best), Raudrī (Fierce One), Kālī, Kalavikaraṇī (Remover of Energy), Balavikaraṇī (Remover of Strength), Balapramathanī (Aggressor of Strength), Damanī (Controller) and Manonmanī (Mind Beyond Mind). All of those (goddess) are yellow and have three eyes. The principal seventy million mantras are located here in the Principle of Knowledge. Each of these (mantras) has a million million (arbudalakṣa) ancillary (aṁśa) (mantras). The worlds here have the form of a lotus. The lords of the seventy million (arbuda) (mantras) are the seven Vidyārājñīs, namely, Triguṇī and the rest. (with 8.338cd–340)
