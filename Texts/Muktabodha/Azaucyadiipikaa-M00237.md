# āśaucyadiipikaa

**Catalog number:** M00237  
**Author:** vedajñāna  
**Published:** India

## Description

E-text created from IFP/EFEO transcript T00370 pages 326 to 400

Copied from a manuscript belonging to Sundaradiksitar Tirunelveli

This is another of the works by vedajñāna who lived in Vyaaghrapura in
Tamil Nadu. As its name suggests it deals with matters of purity and
impurity as prescribed by the Siddhaantaagamas.
