# Psalm 48

← [[BCP Psalm 047]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 049]] →

*Magnus Dominus* · Book Two · Ninth Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 048]]

**48:1** Great is the LORD, and highly to be praised; \*  
in the city of our God is his holy hill. ^48-1

**48:2** Beautiful and lofty, the joy of all the earth, is the  
hill of Zion, \*  
the very center of the world and the city of the great King. ^48-2

**48:3** God is in her citadels; \*  
he is known to be her sure refuge. ^48-3

**48:4** Behold, the kings of the earth assembled \*  
and marched forward together. ^48-4

**48:5** They looked and were astounded; \*  
they retreated and fled in terror. ^48-5

**48:6** Trembling seized them there; \*  
they writhed like a woman in childbirth,  
like ships of the sea when the east wind shatters them. ^48-6

**48:7** As we have heard, so have we seen,  
in the city of the LORD of hosts, in the city of our God; \*  
God has established her for ever. ^48-7

**48:8** We have waited in silence on your loving-kindness, O God, \*  
in the midst of your temple. ^48-8

**48:9** Your praise, like your Name, O God, reaches to the world's end; \*  
your right hand is full of justice. ^48-9

**48:10** Let Mount Zion be glad  
and the cities of Judah rejoice, \*  
because of your judgments. ^48-10

**48:11** Make the circuit of Zion;  
walk round about her; \*  
count the number of her towers. ^48-11

[p. 652] ^pg652

**48:12** Consider well her bulwarks;  
examine her strongholds; \*  
that you may tell those who come after. ^48-12

**48:13** This God is our God for ever and ever; \*  
he shall be our guide for evermore. ^48-13
