acknowledgments

much more hands-on role in all aspects of the project this time around, to impressive effect; to Andrea Zanin and Heather van der Hoop, who brought fresh eyes and editorial viewpoints to the work when they were most needed; and to Jeff Werner, whose elegantly subdued design work is beautifully suited to this content.

During the preparation of *Gifts from the Goddess*, I lost my mother, Mary Teresa Howell Bowden, to a long and difficult battle with Alzheimer’s disease. An old-school Irish girl, she was sweetly open-minded toward and genuinely interested in this project (she even added an image of Guruji and a Lakshmi yantra to her precious collection of Roman Catholic devotional items). I sorely miss her warmth, conversation and laughter.

Last but certainly not least, love and gratitude go out to my wife, friend and longtime co-conspirator, Dr. Anna Pavlotsky, who once again gave me the time and space I needed to complete work on this very big, very complex book—despite a persistent barrage of often urgently competing demands and crises, from the mundane to the life-threatening (and all amid her own elemental exile’s journey of return). Love and thanks also to our sons, Alex and Dylan, who have—each in his own way—supported this work across many years. I am proud of your accomplishments, and even prouder of the fine men you have become and the worthwhile things you will accomplish in life. 

*И наконец для Софии, мой ангел дождя, еще раз спасибо за заботу о папе*.

Michael M. Bowden Boston October 2018
