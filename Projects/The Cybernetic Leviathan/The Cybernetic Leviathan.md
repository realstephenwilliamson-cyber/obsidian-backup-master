# The Cybernetic Leviathan

Hobbes's Leviathan read against cybernetics and digital society. The 2019 working title was *The {Post-Modern, Cybernetic, Capitalist} Leviathan*.

Source of the phrase: [[Grant - Post-Modernism, Lyotard and Baudrillard]].

**There's no current draft.** The plan listed `Cybernetic Leviathan Research Plan_.docx` (25 Apr 2025) as the current version, but it is an AI research report written to you: it refers to "the user's larger scholarly project" and ends with 91 web sources accessed 25 Apr 2025. It's in AI below. Your own writing on the project runs from June 2018 to September 2024 and is gathered in [[Leviathan fragments 2018-2024]].
