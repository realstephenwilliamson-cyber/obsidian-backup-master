## Mūla

समस्तज्ञातृवेद्यत्वे नैकविज्ञातृवेद्यता ।
तस्मान्न वेद्यता नाम भावधर्मोऽस्ति कश्चन ॥ ^mula

samastajñātṛvedyatve naikavijñātṛvedyatā |
tasmānna vedyatā nāma bhāvadharmo'sti kaścana || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 10 - Chapter Ten, part 1#^pg330|p. 311]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=330>))
>
> (According to this theory, if an entity) is perceptible to one subject, it is imperceptible to another one. But if it were to be perceptible to all subjects, it would not be perceptible to only one. (with 10.25cd–26ab)
>
> Thus, perceptibility is not some property of an entity. (with 10.26cd)
