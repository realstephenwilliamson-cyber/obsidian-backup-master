# makuṭottararahasya

**Catalog number:** M00525  
**Main title:** mantrayogoddhāra  
**Manuscript:** IFP transcript T00164  
**Published:** Muktabodha Indological Research Institute, India

## Description

A description of how to draw a yantra called śrīsabhā and the placement of the deities around the central couple, that is, the dancing śiva as Cidambara and his consort.
