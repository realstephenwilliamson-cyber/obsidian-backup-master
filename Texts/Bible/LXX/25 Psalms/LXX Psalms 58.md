# LXX Psalms 58

← [[LXX Psalms 57]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 59]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 059]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 059]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**58:1** For the end. Destroy not: by David for a memorial, when Saul sent, and watched his house to kill him.  ^58-1

**58:2** Deliver me from mine enemies, O God; and ransom me from those that rise up against me.  ^58-2

**58:3** Deliver me from the workers of iniquity, and save me from bloody men.  ^58-3

**58:4** For, behold, they have hunted after my soul; violent men have set upon me: neither *is it* my iniquity, nor my sin, O Lord.  ^58-4

**58:5** Without iniquity I ran and directed *my course aright*: awake to help me, and behold.  ^58-5

**58:6** And thou, Lord God of hosts, the God of Israel, draw nigh to visit all the heathen; pity not any that work iniquity. Pause.  ^58-6

**58:7** They shall return at evening, and hunger like a dog, and go round about the city.  ^58-7

**58:8** Behold, they shall utter a voice with their mouth, and a sword is in their lips; for who, *say they*, has heard?  ^58-8

**58:9** But thou, Lord, wilt laugh them to scorn; thou wilt utterly set at nought all the heathen.  ^58-9

**58:10** I will keep my strength, *looking* to thee; for thou, O God, art my helper.  ^58-10

**58:11** *As for* my God, his mercy shall go before me: my God will shew me *vengeance* on mine enemies.  ^58-11

**58:12** Slay them not, lest they forget thy law; scatter them by thy power; and bring them down, O Lord, my defender.[^1]  ^58-12

**58:13** *For* the sin of their mouth, *and* the word of their lips, let them be even taken in their pride.  ^58-13

**58:14** And for *their* cursing and falsehood shall utter destruction be denounced: *they shall fall* by the wrath of utter destruction, and shall not be; so shall they know that the God of Jacob is Lord of the ends of the earth. Pause.  ^58-14

**58:15** They shall return at evening, and be hungry as a dog, and go round about the city.  ^58-15

**58:16** They shall be scattered hither and thither for meat; and if they be not satisfied, they shall even murmur.  ^58-16

**58:17** But I will sing to thy strength, and in the morning will I exult *in* thy mercy; for thou hast been my supporter, and my refuge in the day of mine affliction.  ^58-17

**58:18** *Thou art* my helper; to thee, my God, will I sing; thou art my supporter, O my God, *and* my mercy.  ^58-18

[^1]: 58:12 some read λαοῦ 'people'.
