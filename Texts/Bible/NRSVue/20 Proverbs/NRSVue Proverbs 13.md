# Proverbs 13 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Proverbs 12|← Proverbs 12]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Proverbs 14|Proverbs 14 →]]

**13:1** A wise child loves discipline,^[Cn: Heb *A wise child the discipline of his father*] but a scoffer does not listen to rebuke. ^13-1

**13:2** From the fruit of their words good persons eat good things, but the desire of the treacherous is for wrongdoing. ^13-2

**13:3** Those who guard their mouths preserve their lives; those who open wide their lips come to ruin. ^13-3

**13:4** The appetite of the lazy craves and gets nothing, while the appetite of the diligent is richly supplied. ^13-4

**13:5** The righteous hate falsehood, but the wicked act shamefully and disgracefully. ^13-5

**13:6** Righteousness guards one whose way is upright, but sin overthrows the wicked. ^13-6

**13:7** Some pretend to be rich yet have nothing; others pretend to be poor yet have great wealth. ^13-7

**13:8** Wealth is a ransom for a person’s life, but a poor person pays no attention to a rebuke. ^13-8

**13:9** The light of the righteous rejoices,^[Or *shines*] but the lamp of the wicked goes out. ^13-9

**13:10** By insolence the empty-headed person makes strife, but wisdom is with those who take advice. ^13-10

**13:11** Wealth hastily gotten^[Gk Vg: Heb *from vanity*] will dwindle, but those who gather little by little will increase it. ^13-11

**13:12** Hope deferred makes the heart sick, but a desire fulfilled is a tree of life. ^13-12

**13:13** Those who despise a word bring destruction on themselves, but those who respect a command will be rewarded. ^13-13

**13:14** The teaching of the wise is a fountain of life, so that one may avoid the snares of death. ^13-14

**13:15** Good sense wins favor, but the way of the faithless is their ruin.^[Cn Compare Gk Syr Vg Tg: Heb *is enduring*] ^13-15

**13:16** The clever do all things intelligently, but the fool displays folly. ^13-16

**13:17** A bad messenger brings trouble, but a faithful envoy, healing. ^13-17

**13:18** Poverty and disgrace are for the one who ignores instruction, but one who heeds reproof is honored. ^13-18

**13:19** A desire realized is sweet to the soul, but to turn away from evil is an abomination to fools. ^13-19

**13:20** Whoever walks with the wise becomes wise, but the companion of fools suffers harm. ^13-20

**13:21** Misfortune pursues sinners, but prosperity rewards the righteous. ^13-21

**13:22** The good leave an inheritance to their children’s children, but the sinner’s wealth is laid up for the righteous. ^13-22

**13:23** The field of the poor may yield much food, but it is swept away through injustice. ^13-23

**13:24** Those who spare the rod hate their children, but those who love them are diligent to discipline them. ^13-24

**13:25** The righteous have enough to satisfy their appetite, but the belly of the wicked is empty. ^13-25
