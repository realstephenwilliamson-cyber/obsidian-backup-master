## Mūla

प्राप्तं सोऽस्य गुरुर्दीक्षा नात्र मुख्या हि संविदि ।
सर्वज्ञाननिधानं तु गुरुं संप्राप्य सुस्थितः ॥ ^mula

prāptaṃ so'sya gururdīkṣā nātra mukhyā hi saṃvidi |
sarvajñānanidhānaṃ tu guruṃ saṃprāpya susthitaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 13 - Chapter Twenty-Two#^pg314|p. 294]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=314>))
>
> Amongst the many teachers, one’s teacher (in the fullest sense is the one) from whom one has obtained the highest knowledge, and his is the chief initiation here within consciousness. (with 22.46cd–47ab)
>
> The wise and fortunate one (susthita) who, having attained a teacher who is a treasure of all knowledge, should worship him (and render him propitious), intent as he is with the desire of knowing each and everything. (with 22.47cd–48ab)
