## Mūla

यो ह्यखण्डितसद्भावमात्मतत्त्वं प्रपद्यते ।
केतकीकुसुमसौरभे भृशं भृङ्ग एव रसिको न मक्षिका ।
भैरवीयपरमाद्वयार्चने कोऽपि रज्यति महेशचोदितः ॥ ^mula

yo hyakhaṇḍitasadbhāvamātmatattvaṃ prapadyate |
ketakīkusumasaurabhe bhṛśaṃ bhṛṅga eva rasiko na makṣikā |
bhairavīyaparamādvayārcane ko'pi rajyati maheśacoditaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. III
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 03 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v03 11 - Chapter Four, part 10#^pg393|p. 380]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 03.pdf#page=393>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 4.276cd–277ab.
>
> It is only the bee who delights intensely in the sweet fragrance of Ketaka flowers, not the fly. It is only a rare person who, inspired by Śiva, is attracted to Bhairava’s supreme nondual worship.
