## Mūla

प्रकाशस्यैव शक्त्यंशा लोकपास्तेन कीर्तिताः ।
इत्थं स्वाधीनरूपापि दिक्सौरी तूपदिश्यते ॥ ^mula

prakāśasyaiva śaktyaṃśā lokapāstena kīrtitāḥ |
itthaṃ svādhīnarūpāpi diksaurī tūpadiśyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 05 - Chapter Fifteen, part 4#^pg153|p. 135]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=153>))
>
> The sovereignty (aiśvarya) of the Light is Indra (E).Its majestic splendour (mahas) is Agni (SE). The control (yantṛtva) (the light exerts) and (its) dreadfulness is Yama (S). The demon (Nirṛti, SW) is its decreased state (ūnimā) (in the sphere of objectivity). (Its condition as) the object of illumination (prakāśya) is Varuṇa (W). It is called Vāyu (NW) because of its (random) mobility (cāñcalya). As it is associated with the accumulation of phenomenal things (bhāvasaṁcaya), it is (Kubera) the god of wealth (N). When that ceases, (Īśāna is) the pervasive Lord (vibhu) (NE). Ananta’s form is invisible (below in the nadir), and Brahmā, who nourishes (bṛṁhaka), is the pervasive lord above (at the zenith). Thus, (in this way,) the limbs of the power of the Light (of consciousness) are (deities of the directions,) called the ‘guardians of the world’ (lokapāla). (with 15.222cd–225ab)
>
> Although a direction is independent in this way (that is, as relative to one thing or another), it is (normally) indicated in relation to the sun. (This is because) all becomes certainly visible there (in its light). (with 15.225cd–226ab)

*Lead-in:* ननु परमशिवात्प्रभृति सदाशिवादिक्रमेण तत्तदाश्रयावलम्बनादनियतरूपा दिगिति उक्तम्, तत् वयं पुनः कतरां तामाश्रयामहे ?--इति न जानीमः--इत्याशङ्क्य आह--

> इत्थं स्वाधीनरूपापि दिक्सौरी तूपदिश्यते ॥
> तत्र सर्वो हि निष्कम्पं प्रकाशत्वं प्रपद्यते ।

इत्थमुक्तेन प्रकारेण प्रतिनियतत्वाभावात् स्वतन्त्ररूपापि दिक् सौरी एव उपदिश्यते--ग्राह्यतया उच्यते यतः सर्वोऽयं लोकस्तत्र निष्कम्पं प्रकाशत्वं प्रपद्यते दृढं प्रतिपत्तिभाग्भवेत्--इत्यर्थः ॥
