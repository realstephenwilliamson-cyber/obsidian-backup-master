*Volume 3, folio 92b* · Emor

*Passage Emor 18:74 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 3.092a#^emor-18-74|Emor 18:74]].*

**Emor 18:75** So, too, if he performs a cruel action, he has a corresponding effect on that day and impairs it, so that subsequently it becomes cruel to him and tries to destroy him, giving him measure for measure. ^emor-18-75

**Emor 18:76** Israel are withheld from cruelty more than all other peoples, and must not manifest any deed of the kind, since many watchful eyes are upon them.’ ^emor-18-76

**Emor 19:77** R. Simeon said: ^emor-19-77

**Emor 19:78** When God resolves to punish the world with famine, He makes the proclamation Himself, and not by the hand of a herald, as in the case of all other punishments.[^1] ^emor-19-78

**Emor 19:79** From that moment it is forbidden to a man who still has plenty to make a show of it, since in so doing he defies the word of the King and, as it were, drives the emissaries of the King from their place. Therefore it was that Jacob said to his sons when the famine came, “Do not show yourselves off’, as much as to say: Why do you cause blemish both above and below, and belie the word of the king and all his emissaries? ^emor-19-79

**Emor 19:80** For Jacob still had much corn, and yet he only desired to “buy among those that came”, so that no blame should attach to his actions. ^emor-19-80

**Emor 20:81** So, too, when Aaron blessed the people he raised his right hand above his left, so that the action below should stimulate a corresponding action above.’ ^emor-20-81

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Emor 20:82–37:186.*

[^1]: v. T. B. Taanith, 2a.
