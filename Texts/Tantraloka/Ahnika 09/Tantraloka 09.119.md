## Mūla

अपध्वस्तमलस्त्वन्तःशिवावेशवशीकृतः ।
अहंभावपरोऽप्येति न कर्माधीनवृत्तिताम् ॥ ^mula

apadhvastamalastvantaḥśivāveśavaśīkṛtaḥ |
ahaṃbhāvaparo'pyeti na karmādhīnavṛttitām || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 05 - Chapter Nine, part 3#^pg123|p. 106]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=123>))
>
> He in whom Impurity has been destroyed, dominated (vaśīkrta) by his inward absorption (āveśa) in Śiva, being (entirely) intent on the egoity (of pure consciousness) (ahamhbhāva), does not become subject to Karma.
