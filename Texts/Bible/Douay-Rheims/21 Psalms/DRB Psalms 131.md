# Psalms 131 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 130|← Psalms 130]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 132|Psalms 132 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 131|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**131:1** O Lord, remember David, and all his meekness. ^131-1

**131:2** How he swore to the Lord, he vowed a vow to the God of Jacob: ^131-2

**131:3** If I shall enter into the tabernacle of my house: if I shall go up into the bed wherein I lie: ^131-3

**131:4** If I shall give sleep to my eyes, or slumber to my eyelids, ^131-4

**131:5** Or rest to my temples: until I find out a place for the Lord, a tabernacle for the God of Jacob. ^131-5

**131:6** Behold we have heard of it in Ephrata: we have found it in the fields of the wood. ^131-6

**131:7** We will go into his tabernacle: We will adore in the place where his feet stood. ^131-7

**131:8** Arise, O Lord, into thy resting place: thou and the ark, which thou hast sanctified. ^131-8

**131:9** Let thy priests be clothed with justice: and let thy saints rejoice. ^131-9

**131:10** For thy servant David’s sake, turn not away the face of thy anointed. ^131-10

**131:11** The Lord hath sworn truth to David, and he will not make it void: of the fruit of thy womb I will set upon thy throne. ^131-11

**131:12** If thy children will keep thy covenant, and these my testimonies which I shall teach them: Their children also for evermore shall sit upon thy throne. ^131-12

**131:13** For the Lord hath chosen Sion: he hath chosen it for his dwelling. ^131-13

**131:14** This is my rest for ever and ever: here will I dwell, for I have chosen it. ^131-14

**131:15** Blessing, I will bless her widow: I will satisfy her poor with bread. ^131-15

**131:16** I will clothe her priests with salvation: and her saints shall rejoice with exceeding great joy. ^131-16

**131:17** There will I bring forth a horn to David: I have prepared a lamp for my anointed. ^131-17

**131:18** His enemies I will clothe with confusion: but upon him will my sanctification flourish. ^131-18
