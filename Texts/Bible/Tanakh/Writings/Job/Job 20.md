# Job 20

Compare: [[NRSVue Job 20|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 20

**20:1** Zophar the Naamathite said in reply: ^20-1

**20:2** In truth, my thoughts urge me to answer  
(It is because of my feelings ^20-2

**20:3** When I hear reproof that insults me);  
A spirit out of my understanding makes me reply: ^20-3

**20:4** Do you not know this, that from time immemorial,  
Since man was set on earth, ^20-4

**20:5** The joy of the wicked has been brief,  
The happiness of the impious, fleeting? ^20-5

**20:6** Though he grows as high as the sky,  
His head reaching the clouds, ^20-6

**20:7** He perishes forever, like his dung;  
Those who saw him will say, “Where is he?” ^20-7

**20:8** He flies away like a dream and cannot be found;  
He is banished like a night vision. ^20-8

**20:9** Eyes that glimpsed him do so no more;  
They cannot see him in his place any longer. ^20-9

**20:10** His sons ingratiate themselves with the poor;  
His own hands must give back his wealth. ^20-10

**20:11** His bones, still full of vigor,  
Lie down in the dust with him. ^20-11

**20:12** Though evil is sweet to his taste,  
And he conceals it under his tongue; ^20-12

**20:13** Though he saves it, does not let it go,  
Holds it inside his mouth, ^20-13

**20:14** His food in his bowels turns  
Into asps’ venom within him. ^20-14

**20:15** The riches he swallows he vomits;  
God empties it out of his stomach. ^20-15

**20:16** He sucks the poison of asps;  
The tongue of the viper kills him. ^20-16

**20:17** Let him not enjoy the streams,  
The rivers of honey, the brooks of cream. ^20-17

**20:18** He will give back the goods unswallowed;  
The value of the riches, undigested. ^20-18

**20:19** Because he crushed and tortured the poor,  
He will not build up the house he took by force. ^20-19

**20:20** He will not see his children tranquil;  
He will not preserve one of his dear ones.[^1] ^20-20

**20:21** With no survivor to enjoy it,  
His fortune will not prosper. ^20-21

**20:22** When he has all he wants, trouble will come;  
Misfortunes of all kinds will batter him. ^20-22

**20:23** Let that fill his belly;  
Let Him loose His burning anger at him,  
And rain down His weapons upon him. ^20-23

**20:24** Fleeing from iron arrows,  
He is shot through from a bow of bronze. ^20-24

**20:25** Brandished and run through his body,  
The blade, through his gall,  
Strikes terror into him. ^20-25

**20:26** Utter darkness waits for his treasured ones;  
A fire fanned by no man will consume him;  
Who survives in his tent will be crushed. ^20-26

**20:27** Heaven will expose his iniquity;  
Earth will rise up against him. ^20-27

**20:28** His household will be cast forth by a flood,  
Spilled out on the day of His wrath. ^20-28

**20:29** This is the wicked man’s portion from God,  
The lot God has ordained for him. ^20-29

[^1]: For this meaning of bet·en and ḥamud, cf. Hos. 9.16.
