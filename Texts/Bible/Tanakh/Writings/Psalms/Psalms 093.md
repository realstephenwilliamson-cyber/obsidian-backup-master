## Chapter 93

**93:1** The LORD is king,  
He is robed in grandeur;  
the LORD is robed,  
He is girded with strength.  
The world stands firm;  
it cannot be shaken. ^93-1

**93:2** Your throne stands firm from of old;  
from eternity You have existed. ^93-2

**93:3** The ocean sounds, O LORD,  
the ocean sounds its thunder,  
the ocean sounds its pounding. ^93-3

**93:4** Above the thunder of the mighty waters,  
more majestic than the breakers of the sea  
is the LORD, majestic on high. ^93-4

**93:5** Your decrees are indeed enduring;  
holiness befits Your house,  
O LORD, for all times. ^93-5
