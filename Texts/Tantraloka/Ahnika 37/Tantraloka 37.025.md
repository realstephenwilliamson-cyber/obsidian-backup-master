## Mūla

तस्यापि परमं सारं मालिनीविजयोत्तरम् ।
उक्तं श्रीरत्नमालायामेतच्च परमेशिना ॥ ^mula

tasyāpi paramaṃ sāraṃ mālinīvijayottaram |
uktaṃ śrīratnamālāyāmetacca parameśinā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 11 - Chapter Thirty-Seven, part 1#^pg254|p. 236]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=254>))
>
> The Siddhayogeśvarīmata is the main (most important Tantra) of the Vidyāpītha. (Beyond even that is) its supreme essence, namely, the Mālinīvijayottara. (with 37.24cd–25ab)
>
> The Supreme Lord has declared this in the venerable Ratnamālā, (where we read): ‘the essence of all the Tantras, present in (those of) the Left (vāma) and Right (dakṣina), is united (milita) in one place as Kaula (doctrine, found) in the illustrious Trika system (ṣadardhaśāsana). (with 37.25cd–26)
