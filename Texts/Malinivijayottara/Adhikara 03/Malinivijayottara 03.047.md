## Mūla

पुनस्तथैव शिखरं जठरं केवलं ततः ।
दक्षजानुयुतं कर्णं कण्ठं केवलमेव च ॥ ^mula

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2023 – March 2023
> p. 48 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=48>))
>
> Having deposited the tooth (GH) as before (i.e., GHO), and the staff alone (R), the hip (M) and the right earring (U); the second tooth (KH) with the tongue (I), the right back (śikhara) (BH) along with the nose (Ī), (GHORAMUKHI is formed). (Next extract) the hip alone (M), again in the same way the back (BH) (along with Ī), then the stomach (Ṣa) alone, the ear (Ṇ) together with the right knee (E) (makes BHĪṢAṆE). (Then) having deposited the throat (Va) alone and hip (Ma) alone (form VAMA), the heart (P) along with the tongue (I) and extracted the face alone (B) (makes PIBA), and (then) the vital breath (Ha) with the first knee (E) (i.e., HE). (with 3.46–48)
