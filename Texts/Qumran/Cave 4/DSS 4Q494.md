### Text

**1** [the chiefs of the] tri[be]s […] ^t-1

**2** And the priests and the levites and the chiefs of the […] ^t-2

**3** the priests; and the same for the levites. And the divisions [of the fathers of the congregation, ﬁfty-two. They shall arrange the chiefs of the priests behind the] ^t-3

**4** High Priest and his second (in rank), [twelve] chiefs [to serve in perpetuity before God. And the twenty-six chiefs of the divisions] ^t-4

**5** shall serve in their divisions, [and after them the chiefs of the levites, to serve always, twelve, one per tribe.] ^t-5

**6** [And after] them, the chiefs […] ^t-6
