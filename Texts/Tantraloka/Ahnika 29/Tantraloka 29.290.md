## Mūla

लोभमोहमदक्रोधरागमायाजुषश्च ये ।
तैःसाकं न च कर्तव्यमेतच्छ्रेयोर्थिनात्मनि ॥ ^mula

lobhamohamadakrodharāgamāyājuṣaśca ye |
taiḥsākaṃ na ca kartavyametacchreyorthinātmani || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 13 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 5#^pg427|p. 410]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=427>))
>
> One who desires what is best for himself should not perform (this rite) with those who do not know the supreme Brahman, those devoid (of the knowledge of) the going and coming (of the breath), those who are given over to greed, infatuation (for women), conceit, anger, attachment, and delusion (māyā). (with 29.288cd–290)
