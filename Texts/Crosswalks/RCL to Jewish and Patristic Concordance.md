# RCL to Jewish & Patristic Polyphonic Concordance

> [!abstract] Architectural Purpose
> This concordance integrates the Sunday Revised Common Lectionary (RCL) with the concurrent Jewish liturgical cycle, aligning the weekly Shabbat Torah Parashah, Rashi’s medieval commentary, the Zohar’s mystical homilies, the Ishbitzer Rebbe’s *Mei HaShiloach*, and Patristic Johannine commentaries (Augustine’s *Tractates on John* and Aquinas’s *Catena Aurea*).
>
> Use this reference during homily preparation and daily study to identify cross-traditional resonances between the Sunday Eucharistic readings and the Torah portion.

---

## 3. Psalter Tri-Fold Crosswalk (Hebrew / Greek / BCP 1979)

When preaching or reflecting on the psalm in the RCL, the psalm numbers diverge between the Masoretic Text (Tanakh) and the Septuagint/Vulgate (see [[Texts/Crosswalks/Psalms numbering|Psalms numbering]]):

* **Psalms 1–8:** MT = LXX
* **Psalms 9–10:** MT 9–10 = LXX Psalm 9
* **Psalms 11–113:** MT = LXX + 1 (e.g., MT Psalm 23 = LXX Psalm 22)
* **Psalms 114–115:** MT 114–115 = LXX Psalm 113
* **Psalms 116:** MT 116 = LXX Psalms 114 & 115
* **Psalms 117–146:** MT = LXX + 1
* **Psalms 147:** MT 147 = LXX Psalms 146 & 147
* **Psalms 148–150:** MT = LXX

The RCL notes embed the JPS psalm only. The three versions of each psalm are:
- Hebrew Tanakh text: `Texts/Bible/Tanakh/Writings/Psalms/Psalms <NNN>.md`
- Greek Septuagint text: `Texts/Bible/LXX/25 Psalms/LXX Psalms <NNN>.md`
- Liturgical translation: `Texts/BCP 1979/09 The Psalter/BCP Psalm <NNN>.md`
