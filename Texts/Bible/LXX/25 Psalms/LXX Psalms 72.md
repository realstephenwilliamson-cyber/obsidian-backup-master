# LXX Psalms 72

← [[LXX Psalms 71]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 73]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 073]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 073]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**72:1** A Psalm for Asaph. How good is God to Israel, to the upright in heart!  ^72-1

**72:2** But my feet were almost overthrown; my goings very nearly slipped.  ^72-2

**72:3** For I was jealous of the transgressors, beholding the tranquillity of sinners.  ^72-3

**72:4** For there is no sign of reluctance in their death: and *they have* firmness under their affliction.  ^72-4

**72:5** They are not in the troubles of *other* men; and they shall not be scourged with *other* men.  ^72-5

**72:6** Therefore pride has possessed them; they have clothed themselves with their injustice and ungodliness.  ^72-6

**72:7** Their injustice shall go forth as out of fatness: they have fulfilled their intention.  ^72-7

**72:8** They have taken counsel and spoken in wickedness: they have uttered unrighteousness loftily.  ^72-8

**72:9** They have set their mouth against heaven, and their tongue has gone through upon the earth.  ^72-9

**72:10** Therefore shall my people return hither: and full days shall be found with them.[^1]  ^72-10

**72:11** And they said, How does God know? and is there knowledge in the Most High?[^2]  ^72-11

**72:12** Behold, these *are* the sinners, and they that prosper always: they have possessed wealth.  ^72-12

**72:13** And I said, Verily in vain have I justified my heart, and washed my hands in innocency.  ^72-13

**72:14** For I was plagued all the day, and my reproof *was* every morning.  ^72-14

**72:15** If I said, I will speak thus; behold, I *should* have broken covenant with the generation of thy children.  ^72-15

**72:16** And I undertook to understand this, *but* it is too hard for me,  ^72-16

**72:17** until I go into the sanctuary of God; *and so* understand the latter end.  ^72-17

**72:18** Surely thou hast appointed *judgments* to them because of their crafty dealings: thou hast cast them down when they were lifted up.  ^72-18

**72:19** How have they become desolate! suddenly they have failed: they have perished because of their iniquity.  ^72-19

**72:20** As the dream of one awakening, O Lord, in thy city thou wilt despise their image.  ^72-20

**72:21** For my heart has rejoiced, and my reins have been gladdened.[^3]  ^72-21

**72:22** But I *was* vile and knew not: I became brutish before thee.  ^72-22

**72:23** Yet I am continually with thee: thou hast holden my right hand.  ^72-23

**72:24** Thou hast guided me by thy counsel, and thou hast taken me to thyself with glory.  ^72-24

**72:25** For what have I in heaven *but thee*? and what have I desired upon the earth beside thee?  ^72-25

**72:26** My heart and my flesh have failed: *but* God *is the strength* of my heart, and God is my portion for ever.  ^72-26

**72:27** For, behold, they that remove themselves far from thee shall perish: thou hast destroyed every one that goes a whoring from thee.  ^72-27

**72:28** But it is good for me to cleave close to God, to put my trust in the Lord; that I may proclaim all thy praises in the gates of the daughter of Sion.  ^72-28

[^1]: 72:10 *Gr.* in.
[^2]: 72:11 *Gr.* if there is.
[^3]: 72:21 *Gr.* changed.
