## Mūla

तादृक्शक्तिनिपातेद्धो यो द्रागूर्ध्वमिमं नयेत् ।
तत्तद्गिरिनदीप्रायावच्छिन्ने क्षेत्रपीठके ॥ ^mula

tādṛkśaktinipāteddho yo drāgūrdhvamimaṃ nayet |
tattadgirinadīprāyāvacchinne kṣetrapīṭhake || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 10 - Chapter Thirteen, part 6#^pg347|p. 325]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=347>))
>
> Even a follower of inferior teachings who, inspired by that kind of descent of the power (of grace), serves such a teacher, is quickly elevated by him. (with 13.323cd–324ab)
>
> (Just as a weak person who is on low ground is not capable of negotiating) a sacred site (on high ground) that is covered with mountains, rivers and the like, similarly (a teacher) who belongs to inferior traditions has no authority (to teach and practice) the knowledge of those that are correspondingly superior. (with 13.324cd–325ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* ननु यथा पूर्वः पूर्वः परत्र परत्राधिकारी, तथा परः परः पूर्वत्रापि कस्मान्न? -- इत्याशङ्कां दृष्टान्तोपदर्शनेन प्रशमयति --

> तत्तद्गिरिनदीप्रायावच्छिन्ने क्षेत्रपीठके ॥
> उत्तरोत्तरविज्ञाने नाधिकार्यधरोऽधरः ।

इह यथा तैस्तैर्गिरिनदीप्रायैर्निम्नोन्नतैर्भूमिभागैरवच्छेदमीयुष्यूर्ध्वोर्ध्वे कोट्टप्राये क्षेत्रसंस्थानादावधरोऽधरो निम्ननिम्नदेशस्थो नाधिकारी, तथाधोऽधःस्थितो वैष्णवादिरुत्तरोत्तरे शैवादौ विज्ञानेऽपि -- इति वाक्यार्थः ॥
