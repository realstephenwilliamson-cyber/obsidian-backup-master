# Psalms 089

Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 089]] · Septuagint (Brenton): [[LXX Psalms 88]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

Compare: [[NRSVue Psalms 089|NRSVue]] (Heb 89:1 = superscription; Heb 89:2–53 = NRSVue 89:1–52)

## Chapter 89

**89:1** A *maskil* of Ethan the Ezrahite. ^89-1

**89:2** I will sing of the LORD’s steadfast love forever;  
to all generations I will proclaim Your faithfulness with my mouth. ^89-2

**89:3** I declare, “Your steadfast love is confirmed forever;  
there in the heavens You establish Your faithfulness.” ^89-3

**89:4** “I have made a covenant with My chosen one;  
I have sworn to My servant David: ^89-4

**89:5** I will establish your offspring forever,  
I will confirm your throne for all generations.” *Selah*. ^89-5

**89:6** Your wonders, O LORD, are praised by the heavens,  
Your faithfulness, too, in the assembly of holy beings. ^89-6

**89:7** For who in the skies can equal the LORD,  
can compare with the LORD among the divine beings, ^89-7

**89:8** a God greatly dreaded in the council of holy beings,  
held in awe by all around Him? ^89-8

**89:9** O LORD, God of hosts,  
who is mighty like You, O LORD?  
Your faithfulness surrounds You; ^89-9

**89:10** You rule the swelling of the sea;  
when its waves surge, You still them. ^89-10

**89:11** You crushed Rahab; he was like a corpse;  
with Your powerful arm You scattered Your enemies. ^89-11

**89:12** The heaven is Yours,  
the earth too;  
the world and all it holds—  
You established them. ^89-12

**89:13** North and south—  
You created them;  
Tabor and Hermon sing forth Your name. ^89-13

**89:14** Yours is an arm endowed with might;  
Your hand is strong;  
Your right hand, exalted. ^89-14

**89:15** Righteousness and justice are the base of Your throne;  
steadfast love and faithfulness stand before You. ^89-15

**89:16** Happy is the people who know the joyful shout;  
O LORD, they walk in the light of Your presence. ^89-16

**89:17** They rejoice in Your name all day long;  
they are exalted through Your righteousness. ^89-17

**89:18** For You are their strength in which they glory;  
our horn is exalted through Your favor. ^89-18

**89:19** Truly our shield is of the LORD,  
our king, of the Holy One of Israel. ^89-19

**89:20** Then[^1] You spoke to Your faithful ones in a vision  
and said, “I have conferred power upon a warrior;  
I have exalted one chosen out of the people. ^89-20

**89:21** I have found David, My servant;  
anointed him with My sacred oil. ^89-21

**89:22** My hand shall be constantly with him,  
and My arm shall strengthen him. ^89-22

**89:23** No enemy shall oppress him,[^2]  
no vile man afflict him. ^89-23

**89:24** I will crush his adversaries before him;  
I will strike down those who hate him. ^89-24

**89:25** My faithfulness and steadfast love shall be with him;  
his horn shall be exalted through My name. ^89-25

**89:26** I will set his hand upon the sea,  
his right hand upon the rivers. ^89-26

**89:27** He shall say to Me,  
‘You are my father, my God, the rock of my deliverance.’ ^89-27

**89:28** I will appoint him first-born,  
highest of the kings of the earth. ^89-28

**89:29** I will maintain My steadfast love for him always;  
My covenant with him shall endure. ^89-29

**89:30** I will establish his line forever,  
his throne, as long as the heavens last. ^89-30

**89:31** If his sons forsake My Teaching  
and do not live by My rules; ^89-31

**89:32** if they violate My laws,  
and do not observe My commands, ^89-32

**89:33** I will punish their transgression with the rod,  
their iniquity with plagues. ^89-33

**89:34** But I will not take away My steadfast love from him;  
I will not betray My faithfulness. ^89-34

**89:35** I will not violate My covenant,  
or change what I have uttered. ^89-35

**89:36** I have sworn by My holiness, once and for all;  
I will not be false to David. ^89-36

**89:37** His line shall continue forever,  
his throne, as the sun before Me, ^89-37

**89:38** as the moon, established forever,  
an enduring witness in the sky.” *Selah*. ^89-38

**89:39** Yet You have rejected, spurned,  
and become enraged at Your anointed. ^89-39

**89:40** You have repudiated the covenant with Your servant;  
You have dragged his dignity in the dust. ^89-40

**89:41** You have breached all his defenses,  
shattered his strongholds. ^89-41

**89:42** All who pass by plunder him;  
he has become the butt of his neighbors. ^89-42

**89:43** You have exalted the right hand of his adversaries,  
and made all his enemies rejoice. ^89-43

**89:44** You have turned back the blade of his sword,  
and have not sustained him in battle. ^89-44

**89:45** You have brought his splendor[^3] to an end  
and have hurled his throne to the ground. ^89-45

**89:46** You have cut short the days of his youth;  
You have covered him with shame. *Selah*. ^89-46

**89:47** How long, O LORD; will You forever hide Your face,  
will Your fury blaze like fire? ^89-47

**89:48** O remember how short my life is;[^4]  
why should You have created every man in vain? ^89-48

**89:49** What man can live and not see death,  
can save himself from the clutches of Sheol? *Selah*. ^89-49

**89:50** O Lord, where is Your steadfast love of old  
which You swore to David in Your faithfulness? ^89-50

**89:51** Remember, O Lord, the abuse flung at Your servants  
that I have borne in my bosom [from] many peoples,[^5] ^89-51

**89:52** how Your enemies, O LORD, have flung abuse,  
abuse at Your anointed at every step. ^89-52

**89:53** Blessed is the LORD forever;  
Amen and Amen. ^89-53

[^1]: Referring to vv. 4–5; cf. 2 Sam. 7.1–17.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^5]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
