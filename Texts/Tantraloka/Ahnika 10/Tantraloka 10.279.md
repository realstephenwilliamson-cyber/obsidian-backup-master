## Mūla

नात्र योगस्य सद्भावो भावनादेरभावतः ।
अप्रमेयेऽपरिच्छिन्ने स्वतन्त्रे भाव्यता कुतः ॥ ^mula

nātra yogasya sadbhāvo bhāvanāderabhāvataḥ |
aprameye'paricchinne svatantre bhāvyatā kutaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 14 - Chapter Ten, part 5#^pg492|p. 473]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=492>))
>
> There is no meditation (bhāāvanā) or anything of the sort (in this state), and so there is no yoga here. Where is the object of meditation within (a reality) that is not objectively perceivable (aprameya) and is unlimited and free? Thus, the Lord has not taught any yogic name for it. (with 10.279–280ab)
