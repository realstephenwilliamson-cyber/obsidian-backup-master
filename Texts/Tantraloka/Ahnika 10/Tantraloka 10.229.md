## Mūla

इति पञ्च पदान्याहुरेकस्मिन्वेदके सति ।
तत्र यैषा धरातत्त्वाच्छिवान्ता तत्त्वपद्धतिः ॥ ^mula

iti pañca padānyāhurekasminvedake sati |
tatra yaiṣā dharātattvācchivāntā tattvapaddhatiḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 13 - Chapter Ten, part 4#^pg458|p. 439]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=458>))
>
> (The teachers) say that the levels are five, when there is one subject. They are waking, dreaming, deep sleep, the Fourth, and the one beyond the Fourth. (with 10.228cd–229ab)
>
> If there is (only) one subject throughout this series of principles ranging from Earth to Śiva, the waking and other states must necessarily (be his states). (I will now describe them as) I have learnt them by the grace of Śambhunātha. (with 10.229cd–230)
