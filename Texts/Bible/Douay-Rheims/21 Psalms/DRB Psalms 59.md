# Psalms 59 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 58|← Psalms 58]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 60|Psalms 60 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 59|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**59:1** Unto the end, for them that shall be changed, for the inscription of a title, to David himself, for doctrine, ^59-1

**59:2** When he set fire to Mesopotamia of Syria and Sobal and Joab returned and slew of Edom, in the vale of the saltpits, twelve thousand men. ^59-2

**59:3** O God, thou hast cast us off, and hast destroyed us; thou hast been angry, and hast had mercy on us. ^59-3

**59:4** Thou hast moved the earth, and hast troubled it: heal thou the breaches thereof, for it has been moved. ^59-4

**59:5** Thou hast shewn thy people hard things; thou hast made us drink wine of sorrow. ^59-5

**59:6** Thou hast given a warning to them that fear thee: that they may flee from before the bow: That thy beloved may be delivered. ^59-6

**59:7** Save me with thy right hand, and hear me. ^59-7

**59:8** God hath spoken in his holy place: I will rejoice, and I will divide Sichem; and will mete out the vale of tabernacles. ^59-8

**59:9** Galaad is mine, and Manasses is mine: and Ephraim is the strength of my head. Juda is my king: ^59-9

**59:10** Moab is the pot of my hope. Into Edom will I stretch out my shoe: to me the foreigners are made subject. ^59-10

**59:11** Who will bring me into the strong city? who will lead me into Edom? ^59-11

**59:12** Wilt not thou, O God, who hast cast us off? and wilt not thou, O God, go out with our armies? ^59-12

**59:13** Give us help from trouble: for vain is the salvation of man. ^59-13

**59:14** Through God we shall do mightily: and he shall bring to nothing them that afflict us. ^59-14
