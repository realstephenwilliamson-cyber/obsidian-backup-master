## Mūla

क्रोष्टुकी भीममुद्रा च वायुवेगा हयानना ।
गम्भीरा घोषणी चेति चतुर्विंशत्यरे विधिः ॥ ^mula

kroṣṭukī bhīmamudrā ca vāyuvegā hayānanā |
gambhīrā ghoṣaṇī ceti caturviṃśatyare vidhiḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 07 - Chapter Thirty-Three#^pg189|p. 171]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=189>))
>
> 2) The prescription for the (wheel) with twenty-four spokes (12 + 12) is that (the following goddess should be worshipped there. 2A) These are) the eight (Mothers) Māheśī, Brāhmaṇī, Skāndī, Vaiṣṇavī, Aindrī, Yamātmikā, Cāmuṇḍā and Yogīśī; or else (one may worship) Aghorā and the rest along with the four Mothers of the southeast, southwest, northwest and northeast. 2B) (They are) accompanied by the twelve (goddesses) Nandā, Bhadrā (Auspicious), Jayā (Victory), Kālī, Karālī (Fierce Teeth), Vikṛtānanā (Deformed Face), Kroṣṭukī (Jackel), Bhīmamudrā (Terrible Look), Vāyuvegā (Swift as the Wind), Hayānanā (Horse Face), Gambhirā (Profound), and Ghoṣaṇī (Shouting One). (with 33.3–5)
