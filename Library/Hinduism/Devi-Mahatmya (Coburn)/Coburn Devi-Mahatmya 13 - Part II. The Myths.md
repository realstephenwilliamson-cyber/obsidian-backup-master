The Myths

The basic point of this second portion of our study can be put quite simply: in the DM we have the first discursive account in Sanskrit of the Goddess’s role in the demise of the Asuras (1) Madhu and Kaitṭabha, (2) Mahiṣa, and (3) Śumbha and Niśumbha.? Mythological accounts of the encounter betwecn these Asuras and various deities are known, with varying degrees of richness and detail, prior to the time of our text, and therc is some evidence to suggest an earlier association of the Goddess with one or two of the myths. But, to our knowlcdge, there is no antecedent occasion on which (1) thc three myths have been recounted together as a group, or (2) the Goddess has been portrayed as instrumental in effecting the divine triumph in all three encounters.,

Documentation of this point, in comparison with our investigation of epithets, is more limited, for the liturgical nature of the Vedic corpus is suchas to disincline it from thc recounting of myths.?
