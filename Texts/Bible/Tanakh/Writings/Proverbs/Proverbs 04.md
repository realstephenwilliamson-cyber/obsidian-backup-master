# Proverbs 04

Compare: [[NRSVue Proverbs 04|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 4

**4:1** Sons, heed the discipline of a father;  
Listen and learn discernment, ^4-1

**4:2** For I give you good instruction;  
Do not forsake my teaching. ^4-2

**4:3** Once I was a son to my father,  
The tender darling of my mother. ^4-3

**4:4** He instructed me and said to me,  
“Let your mind hold on to my words;  
Keep my commandments and you will live. ^4-4

**4:5** Acquire wisdom, acquire discernment;  
Do not forget and do not swerve from my words. ^4-5

**4:6** Do not forsake her and she will guard you;  
Love her and she will protect you. ^4-6

**4:7** The beginning[^1] of wisdom is—acquire wisdom;  
With all your acquisitions, acquire discernment. ^4-7

**4:8** Hug her to you and she will exalt you;  
She will bring you honor if you embrace her. ^4-8

**4:9** She will adorn your head with a graceful wreath;  
Crown you with a glorious diadem.” ^4-9

**4:10** My son, heed and take in my words,  
And you will have many years of life. ^4-10

**4:11** I instruct you in the way of wisdom;  
I guide you in straight courses. ^4-11

**4:12** You will walk without breaking stride;  
When you run, you will not stumble. ^4-12

**4:13** Hold fast to discipline; do not let go;  
Keep it; it is your life. ^4-13

**4:14** Do not enter on the path of the wicked;  
Do not walk on the way of evil men. ^4-14

**4:15** Avoid it; do not pass through it;  
Turn away from it; pass it by. ^4-15

**4:16** For they cannot sleep unless they have done evil;  
Unless they make someone fall they are robbed of sleep. ^4-16

**4:17** They eat the bread of wickedness  
And drink the wine of lawlessness. ^4-17

**4:18** The path of the righteous is like radiant sunlight,  
Ever brightening until noon. ^4-18

**4:19** The way of the wicked is all darkness;  
They do not know what will make them stumble. ^4-19

**4:20** My son, listen to my speech;  
Incline your ear to my words. ^4-20

**4:21** Do not lose sight of them;  
Keep them in your mind. ^4-21

**4:22** They are life to him who finds them,  
Healing for his whole body. ^4-22

**4:23** More than all that you guard, guard your mind,  
For it is the source of life. ^4-23

**4:24** Put crooked speech away from you;  
Keep devious talk far from you. ^4-24

**4:25** Let your eyes look forward,  
Your gaze be straight ahead. ^4-25

**4:26** Survey the course you take,  
And all your ways will prosper. ^4-26

**4:27** Do not swerve to the right or the left;  
Keep your feet from evil. ^4-27

[^1]: Or “best part.”
