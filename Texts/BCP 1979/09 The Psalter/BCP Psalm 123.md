# Psalm 123

← [[BCP Psalm 122]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 124]] →

*Ad te levavi oculos meos* · Book Five · Twenty-seventh Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 123]]

**123:1** To you I lift up my eyes, \*  
to you enthroned in the heavens. ^123-1

**123:2** As the eyes of servants look to the hand of their masters, \*  
and the eyes of a maid to the hand of her mistress, ^123-2

**123:3** So our eyes look to the LORD our God, \*  
until he show us his mercy. ^123-3

**123:4** Have mercy upon us, O LORD, have mercy, \*  
for we have had more than enough of contempt, ^123-4

**123:5** Too much of the scorn of the indolent rich, \*  
and of the derision of the proud. ^123-5
