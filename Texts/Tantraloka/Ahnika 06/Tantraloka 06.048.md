## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 09 - Chapter Six, part 2#^pg275|p. 260]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=275>))
>
> Exertion (yatna) (that is, the movement of the vital breath) is considered to be of two kinds. A1) (The first is) just vitality itself (jīvanamātra). A2) (The second kind) is intent on (impelling) that (fatpara). Again, it is divided into two (other) kinds, according to B1) whether (the vital breath and its movement) is perceptible (sarivedya) or B2) imperceptible (asarivedya). (Moreover,) each one (of these four) is discerned to be of two kinds, according to whether it is clearly evident (sphuṭa) or not (sphuṭa). (with 6.47cd–48)

## Viveka

> यत्नो जीवनमात्रात्मा तत्परश्च द्विधा मतः ॥
> संवेद्यश्चाप्यसंवेद्यो द्विधेत्थं भिद्यते पुनः ।
> स्फुटास्फुटत्वाद् द्वैविध्यं प्रत्येकं परिभावयेत् ॥

"यत्न"इति प्राणीयः स्पन्दः । जीवनमात्रात्मेति, स्वारसिको येनावयवानां स्तब्धतैव न स्यात् । तत्पर इच्छापूर्वकः । प्रत्येकमिति, चतुर्णां द्वैविध्येऽष्टधा प्राणीयो यत्न इति सिद्धम् ॥ ४८ ॥
