# Psalm 72

← [[BCP Psalm 071]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 073]] →

*Deus, judicium* · Book Two · Fourteenth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 072]]

**72:1** Give the King your justice, O God, \*  
and your righteousness to the King's son; ^72-1

**72:2** That he may rule your people righteously \*  
and the poor with justice; ^72-2

**72:3** That the mountains may bring prosperity to the people, \*  
and the little hills bring righteousness. ^72-3

**72:4** He shall defend the needy among the people; \*  
he shall rescue the poor and crush the oppressor. ^72-4

**72:5** He shall live as long as the sun and moon endure, \*  
from one generation to another. ^72-5

**72:6** He shall come down like rain upon the mown field, \*  
like showers that water the earth. ^72-6

**72:7** In his time shall the righteous flourish; \*  
there shall be abundance of peace till the moon shall be no more. ^72-7

**72:8** He shall rule from sea to sea, \*  
and from the River to the ends of the earth. ^72-8

[p. 686]

**72:9** His foes shall bow down before him, \*  
and his enemies lick the dust. ^72-9

**72:10** The kings of Tarshish and of the isles shall pay tribute, \*  
and the kings of Arabia and Saba offer gifts. ^72-10

**72:11** All kings shall bow down before him, \*  
and all the nations do him service. ^72-11

**72:12** For he shall deliver the poor who cries out in distress, \*  
and the oppressed who has no helper. ^72-12

**72:13** He shall have pity on the lowly and poor; \*  
he shall preserve the lives of the needy. ^72-13

**72:14** He shall redeem their lives from oppression and violence, \*  
and dear shall their blood be in his sight. ^72-14

**72:15** Long may he live!  
and may there be given to him gold from Arabia; \*  
may prayer be made for him always,  
and may they bless him all the day long. ^72-15

**72:16** May there be abundance of grain on the earth,  
growing thick even on the hilltops; \*  
may its fruit flourish like Lebanon,  
and its grain like grass upon the earth. ^72-16

**72:17** May his Name remain for ever  
and be established as long as the sun endures; \*  
may all the nations bless themselves in him and call him blessed. ^72-17

**72:18** Blessed be the Lord GOD, the God of Israel, \*  
who alone does wondrous deeds! ^72-18

**72:19** And blessed be his glorious Name for ever! \*  
and may all the earth be filled with his glory.  
Amen. Amen. ^72-19
