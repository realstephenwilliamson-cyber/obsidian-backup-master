# καταρτίζω

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · verb

**Dodson:** I fit together, prepare — (a) I fit (join) together; met: I compact together, (b) act. and mid: I prepare, perfect, for his (its) full destination or use, bring into its proper condition (whether for the first time, or after a lapse). (Strong's 2675; headword καταρτίζω)
