## Mūla

मध्यशृङ्गावसाने तु तृतीयं विलिखेत्ततः ।
सव्यासव्ये तथैवेह कटिस्थाब्जे समालिखेत् ॥ ^mula

madhyaśṛṅgāvasāne tu tṛtīyaṃ vilikhettataḥ |
savyāsavye tathaiveha kaṭisthābje samālikhet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 05 - Chapter Thirty-One, part 2#^pg124|p. 106]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=124>))
>
> One should draw a lotus above that (staff) in the middle part (of the mandala). A third one should then be drawn at the end of the middle prong. In the same way, one should draw two other lotuses (of the same size) on the wall (kaṭi), to the right and the left. (with 31.92cd–93)
>
> The calix (of each lotus) is yellow, the stamens are red, yellow and white. The petals (are white) and the protective border (prativāranī) (around) outside the lotus is (also) white. The trident (is coloured) with black powder, whereas the line of Brahmā is white. The tip of the trident has a flame, and the staff (of the Trident) is yellow. A wise man should always worship 59 there (on) the lotus in the middle of the trident, Parā above him, the other (goddess i.e. Parāparā) on the right and Aparā on the left. (with 31.93cd–96)
