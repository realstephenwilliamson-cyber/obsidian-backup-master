“I offer worship to the secret *yōginī* (*gupta yōginyaḥ*), whose chakra fulfills all directions and all desires (*sarvāśāparipūraka cakrē*); and to Tripurēśī, controller of the waking, dreaming and sleeping states, who governs this chakra.”

Perform the *drīm mudrā* of Sarva Vidrāviṇī.
