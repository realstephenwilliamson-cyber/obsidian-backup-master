### 9.1–33: Israel’s place in God’s purpose

*[[NRSVue Romans 09#^9-1|Text]], p. 303* · Paul explains the state of Jews who do not share his convictions about Christ or the Gentile mission. Noting that some Jews will stumble over any announcement of salvation to the nations, Paul calls this refusal “seeking their own righteousness,” that is, they see justice as served only for those who are Israelites or who join Israel through proselyte conversion. Paul claims not to fault them (although his viewpoint expresses judgment). ^9-1

**[[NRSVue Romans 09#^9-1|1–3]]:** Change of focus from non-Jews and Christ-followers (who nevertheless remain the epistle’s audience) to explaining the present predicament of non-Christ-following Jews. If, as Paul has argued, Scripture foretold the gospel, why have not all Jews embraced it? ^9-1-2

**[[NRSVue Romans 09#^9-3|3]]:** *I myself were accursed*, parallel with Moses’ vicarious offer in [[Torah 21 - Ki Tisa#^32-32|Ex 32.32]]. *Cut off*, perhaps a pun on the issue of circumcision. *My own people*, lit., “brethren”; i.e., fellow Israelites. *Flesh*, here and v 5, shared ethnic identification, including by proselyte conversion; see vv 7–8; 1.3. ^9-3

**[[NRSVue Romans 09#^9-4|4]]:** *Israelites*, Jews. *To them belong*, lit., “of whom are”; *adoption*, lit., “sonship”; the Israelites precede the Gentiles in being members of God’s family. *Glory*, God’s presence (Heb “kavod”) related to the Temple and Zion. *Covenants*, or *things work together for good*, or *in all things God works for good* d Gk *among many brothers* e Or *Is it Christ Jesus . . . for us?* f Gk *my brothers* g Or *the Christ* h Or *Messiah, who is God over all, blessed forever*; or *Messiah. May he who is God over all be blessed forever* covenant; *giving of the law,* lit., “the legislation,” probably implying the Mosaic Torah; *worship*, Temple sacrificial system \[Gk “latreia” for Heb “avodah”\]. ^9-4

**[[NRSVue Romans 09#^9-5|5]]:** *To them belong*, lit., “of whom \[are\]”; *the patriarchs,* or fathers. *According to the flesh,* by ethnic descent. *Messiah who is over all*, both Jew and Gentile. ^9-5

**[[NRSVue Romans 09#^9-6|6]]:** Gk begins “But”; *had failed* (to come true), lit., “has fallen”; *truly*, not in Gk. *Belong to Israel*, lit. “For not all are the ones from Israel; these ones are Israel.” Paul apparently means: “For not all \[the Israelites of vv 4–5\] are the ones from Israel \[who are presently bringing the message to the nations (although some, like Paul, are doing so successfully, so the word of God has not fallen even if some Israelites are presently stumbling rather than completing the task)\]; \[nevertheless\] these ones \[these Israelites who are stumbling, v 4\] are \[legitimate members of\] Israel \[who should carry out this task, but failure to do so (yet) does not alter their legitimate standing as Israel, because they come through the line of the promised seed\].” ^9-6

**[[NRSVue Romans 09#^9-7|7–9]]:** Ethnic descent is not denied, but the right to the inheritance is qualified. ^9-7

**[[NRSVue Romans 09#^9-7|7]]:** [[Torah 04 - Vayera#^21-12|Gen 21.12]]. *True* is not in Gk. ^9-7-2

**[[NRSVue Romans 09#^9-8|8]]:** *Flesh*, ethnic descent; *descendants*, lit., “seed.” ^9-8

**[[NRSVue Romans 09#^9-9|9]]:** See [[Torah 04 - Vayera#^18-14|Gen 18.14]]. ^9-9

**[[NRSVue Romans 09#^9-10|10–13]]:** Paul’s response continues with the discrimination between Isaac’s seed (i.e., the twins Esau and Jacob): only Jacob, who becomes Israel, is the elect one through whom the divine promise to Abraham is kept. The discrimination occurred in Rebecca’s womb, so that it cannot demonstrate anything except God’s sovereign choice. The election is thus neither a punishment nor a reward. Consequently, implicitly, Jews not “yet” faithful to Christ are not at fault. By God’s design, they are not yet persuaded of the gospel. Contrast Philo, *Virtues* 207–9; *Praem.* 58–60; *Leg*. *all*. 3.75–88, who attributes election and rejection as God’s response to behavior. ^9-10

**[[NRSVue Romans 09#^9-10|10]]:** [[Torah 06 - Toldot#^25-21|Gen 25.21]]. ^9-10-2

**[[NRSVue Romans 09#^9-11|11]]:** *Bad*, Gk “phaulos” can also be translated “petty,” in keeping with the Genesis narrative, wherein Esau values a meal over his birthright. *Election*, choice. ^9-11

**[[NRSVue Romans 09#^9-12|12]]:** See [[Torah 06 - Toldot#^25-23|Gen 25.23]]. ^9-12

**[[NRSVue Romans 09#^9-13|13]]:** See [[Malachi 1#^1-2|Mal 1.2–3]]. *Hated*, hyperbole to sharpen comparison with the one chosen. The choice of Jacob over Esau does not mean that the “younger” Jesus community is preferable to the “elder,” Jewish community; instead, God does the unexpected. ^9-13

**[[NRSVue Romans 09#^9-15|15]]:** See [[Torah 21 - Ki Tisa#^33-19|Ex 33.19]], in its original context, the verse concerns the revelation of the divine to Moses; Paul rein-terprets it to show divine sovereignty regarding election. ^9-15

**[[NRSVue Romans 09#^9-16|16]]:** *It*, God’s choice among Israelites. *Exertion*, lit., “running.” The term reinforces the theme of Israel’s role as herald of God’s good news to the nations, and Paul seeks to explain that even though some Israelites are not joining him in the task, they remain those chosen by God. Note that God’s choice here is among family members: not Israelites versus non- Israelites, but among Israelites, as in 9.1–6. See 9.30–33. ^9-16

**[[NRSVue Romans 09#^9-17|17]]:** See [[Torah 14 - Vaera#^9-16|Ex 9.16]]. *Pharaoh*, the plagues he faced were for the proclamation of God’s name among the nations. ^9-17

**[[NRSVue Romans 09#^9-18|18]]:** *Chooses*, or wills/wants among Israelites as well as among the Gentiles; *hardens the heart*, makes resistant to change, as with Pharaoh (v 19). ^9-18

**[[NRSVue Romans 09#^9-20|20–24]]:** See [[Isaiah 29#^29-16|Isa 29.16]]; 45.9; [[Isaiah 64#^64-7|64.8]]; [[Jeremiah 18#^18-1|Jer 18.1–11]]; [[LXX Wisdom of Solomon 15#^15-7|Wis 15.7]]; also [[Jeremiah 50#^50-25|Jer 50.25]], for vessels of wrath. ^9-20

**[[NRSVue Romans 09#^9-20|20]]:** *Argue with God*, for contrasting views of this, see Abraham, [[Torah 04 - Vayera#^18-22|Gen 18.22–33]], and [[Job 42#^42-1|Job 42.1–6]]. ^9-20-2

**[[NRSVue Romans 09#^9-21|21]]:** *Same ump*, i.e., same descent group; *object*, or vessel; *special us*e, lit., “honor”; *ordinary use*, lit., “dishonor.” ^9-21

**[[NRSVue Romans 09#^9-22|22–24]]:** *What if*, lit., “But if ”; *his power*, or his capability. *Endured*, or brought forth; i.e., threw, as in pottery; *objects*, or vessels; or “fashioned with much patience the vessels of wrath fit for destruction.” The example is presented hypotheti-cally; Paul does not indicate that this has occurred, but is emphasizing God’s sovereignty over what might seem puzzling. ^9-22

**[[NRSVue Romans 09#^9-23|23]]:** *And what*, not in Gk. ^9-23

**[[NRSVue Romans 09#^9-24|24]]:** *Jews*, or Judeans, in this case, in contrast to the *Gentiles*, lit., “nations,” probably signifying the rest of the Israelite tribes scattered among the nations, based upon the next statement from Hosea. ^9-24

**[[NRSVue Romans 09#^9-25|25–26]]:** [[Hosea 02#^2-25|Hos 2.23]]; the first part of this verse in LXX, which Paul does not cite, is about “sowing,” i.e., “seed”; see vv 27–29. ^9-25

**[[NRSVue Romans 09#^9-26|26]]:** [[Hosea 02#^2-1|Hos 1.10]]. Hosea refers to the dispersed northern tribes of Israel in contrast to the Judeans. Paul speaks of the division between the remnant such as himself and those Israelites who are stumbling (metaphorically, the northern tribes now lost but to be reunited with the Judeans). He anticipates 11.26, the declaration that all Israel will be preserved. ^9-26

**[[NRSVue Romans 09#^9-27|27–29]]:** The remnant will be saved. ^9-27

**[[NRSVue Romans 09#^9-27|27]]:** [[Isaiah 10#^10-22|Isa 10.22]] conflated with [[Hosea 02#^2-1|Hos 1.10]], following from v 26. *Children of Israel were*, “are” or “will be”; no verb in Gk; *only*, not in Gk. *Saved*, protected/kept safe from destruction, restored to the task of declaring the good news. ^9-27-2

**[[NRSVue Romans 09#^9-28|28]]:** [[Isaiah 10#^10-23|Isa 10.23]] conflated with [[Isaiah 28#^28-22|Isa 28.22]]. ^9-28

**[[NRSVue Romans 09#^9-29|29]]:** [[LXX Isaiah 01#^1-9|Isa 1.9]]\[LXX\], *survivors*, lit., “seed.” NRSV translates according to the meaning of Isaiah in Heb, where the reference is to the living remnant, not to their descendants (“seed”), as in Paul’s text. *Sodom . . . Gomorrah*, see Gen 19. ^9-29

**[[NRSVue Romans 09#^9-30|30–33]]:** A diatribe in elliptical language, omitting critical verbs and nouns: NRSV supplies *have attained it, that is . . . that is based . . . succeed in . . . strive for it on the basis . . . as if it were based.* Some Jews have not carried out their task of proclaiming the good news to the nations, while some Gentiles have come to know the news from those (like Paul) who have; this development is unexpected ( 3.1–2; 11.17–26). ^9-30

**[[NRSVue Romans 09#^9-30|30]]:** *Gentiles*, lit., “(those from the) nations.” *Who did not strive for*, lit., “who are not pursuing”; *faith*, or faithfulness. ^9-30-2

**[[NRSVue Romans 09#^9-31|31]]:** *Did not succeed in fulfilling that law*, lit., “did not arrive sooner”; Israel, by delineating the family of righteousness by circumcision, missed the point of such delineation, which some Gentiles nevertheless grasped, that the God of Israel is the only God also of the nations. ^9-31

**[[NRSVue Romans 09#^9-32|32]]:** *Faith*, better: faithfulness. *Works*, the action of circumcision for Gentiles completing proselyte conversion. Those Jews confuse the sign of inclusion (circumcision) with the reason for inclusion, not recognizing the claims of Gentiles based on faithfulness to Christ. ^9-32

**[[NRSVue Romans 09#^9-33|33]]:** [[Isaiah 28#^28-16|Isa 28.16]] (also in 10.11), that God has put an obstruction in the path (see also Isa 8, esp. v 14). The stone aimed not to cause failure but, ironically, to help them by slowing them down so that they did not miss the righteousness *work and cut it short in righteousness, because the Lord will make the sentence shortened on the earth* b Or *descendants*; Gk *seed* they were to declare. The stone could be Christ, or the proclamation of the gospel, or the inclusion of Gentiles apart from circumcision. Implicitly, these Jews will be kept safe until they regain their step and properly achieve the aim of the message, righteousness, by way of the gospel. *Rock that will make them fall*, lit., “rock of offense”; “fall” is not in the Gk, and goes against the argument, cf. 11.11. *And whoever believes*, whoever is faithful; *in him*, or to it; i.e., the stone, or the righteousness of God’s revealing of Christ/Messiah. ^9-33
