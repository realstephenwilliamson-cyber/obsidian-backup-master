## Mūla

सिद्ध्यङ्गमिति मोक्षाय प्रत्यूह इति कोविदाः ।
शिवशासनमाहात्म्यं विदन्नप्यत एव हि ॥ ^mula

siddhyaṅgamiti mokṣāya pratyūha iti kovidāḥ |
śivaśāsanamāhātmyaṃ vidannapyata eva hi || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. III
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 03 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v03 02 - Chapter Four, part 1#^pg39|p. 26]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 03.pdf#page=39>))
>
> This (form of attachment) is said (in the scriptures) to be (devotion to some lower deity that accords with one’s own natural disposition. This deity is technically termed) ‘the limb (arnśaka) called one’s own nature (svabhāva)’. It is evident (that, at best, it is the source of) accomplishment (siddhi) (of a lower level of attainment), which is (just) (an ancillary) limb (aṅga) (of the path). Thus, according to the wise, it is an obstacle to liberation. (with 4.19cd–20ab)
>
> Thus, although the fool knows the greatness of Śaiva doctrine, affected by (this) attachment, he would prefer (the teachings) of the Vaiṣṇavas or others. (with 4.20cd–21ab)
