## Mūla

भवदोषोऽनुप्लवश्च ग्लानिः शोषो विमूढता ।
अहंममात्मतातङ्को मायाशक्तिरथावृतिः ॥ ^mula

bhavadoṣo'nuplavaśca glāniḥ śoṣo vimūḍhatā |
ahaṃmamātmatātaṅko māyāśaktirathāvṛtiḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 05 - Chapter Nine, part 3#^pg101|p. 84]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=101>))
>
> Impurity is given a number of names in various places (in the scriptures) in accord with its nature. Thus, for example, (it is called) desire (abhilāṣa), ignorance, nescience, craving (lolikā), absence of insight (aprathā), the defect of phenomenal existence (bhavadoṣa), the boat of the soul (anuplava), lassitude (glāni), aridity (śoṣa), delusion (vimūḍhatā), the state of ‘I’ and ‘mine’, fear, the power of Māyā, the veil, the seed of defects, the fettered state (paśutva), and the cause of the sprout of transmigratory existence (saṁsāra). (with 9.84cd–86)
