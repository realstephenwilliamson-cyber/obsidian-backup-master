# Psalm 81

← [[BCP Psalm 080]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 082]] →

*Exultate Deo* · Book Three · Sixteenth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 081]]

**81:1** Sing with joy to God our strength \*  
and raise a loud shout to the God of Jacob. ^81-1

**81:2** Raise a song and sound the timbrel, \*  
the merry harp, and the lyre. ^81-2

**81:3** Blow the ram's-horn at the new moon, \*  
and at the full moon, the day of our feast. ^81-3

**81:4** For this is a statute for Israel, \*  
a law of the God of Jacob. ^81-4

**81:5** He laid it as a solemn charge upon Joseph, \*  
when he came out of the land of Egypt. ^81-5

**81:6** I heard an unfamiliar voice saying, \*  
"I eased his shoulder from the burden;  
his hands were set free from bearing the load." ^81-6

**81:7** You called on me in trouble, and I saved you; \*  
I answered you from the secret place of thunder  
and tested you at the waters of Meribah. ^81-7

**81:8** Hear, O my people, and I will admonish you: \*  
O Israel, if you would but listen to me! ^81-8

**81:9** There shall be no strange god among you; \*  
you shall not worship a foreign god. ^81-9

**81:10** I am the LORD your God,  
who brought you out of the land of Egypt and said, \*  
"Open your mouth wide, and I will fill it." ^81-10

**81:11** And yet my people did not hear my voice, \*  
and Israel would not obey me. ^81-11

**81:12** So I gave them over to the stubbornness of their hearts, \*  
to follow their own devices. ^81-12

[p. 705] ^pg705

**81:13** Oh, that my people would listen to me! \*  
that Israel would walk in my ways! ^81-13

**81:14** I should soon subdue their enemies \*  
and turn my hand against their foes. ^81-14

**81:15** Those who hate the LORD would cringe before him, \*  
and their punishment would last for ever. ^81-15

**81:16** But Israel would I feed with the finest wheat \*  
and satisfy him with honey from the rock. ^81-16
