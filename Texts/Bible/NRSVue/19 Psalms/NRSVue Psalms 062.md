## Song of trust in God alone

*To the leader: according to Jeduthun. A Psalm of David.*

**62:1** For God alone my soul waits in silence; from him comes my salvation. ^62-1

**62:2** He alone is my rock and my salvation, my fortress; I shall never be shaken. ^62-2

**62:3** How long will you assail a person, will you batter your victim, all of you, as you would a leaning wall, a tottering fence? ^62-3

**62:4** Their only plan is to bring down a person of prominence. They take pleasure in falsehood; they bless with their mouths, but inwardly they curse. *Selah* ^62-4

**62:5** For God alone my soul waits in silence, for my hope is from him. ^62-5

**62:6** He alone is my rock and my salvation, my fortress; I shall not be shaken. ^62-6

**62:7** On God rests my deliverance and my honor; my mighty rock, my refuge is in God. ^62-7

**62:8** Trust in him at all times, O people; pour out your heart before him; God is a refuge for us. *Selah* ^62-8

**62:9** Those of low estate are but a breath; those of high estate are a delusion; in the balances they go up; they are together lighter than a breath. ^62-9

**62:10** Put no confidence in extortion, and set no vain hopes on robbery; if riches increase, do not set your heart on them. ^62-10

**62:11** Once God has spoken; twice have I heard this: that power belongs to God, ^62-11

**62:12** and steadfast love belongs to you, O Lord. For you repay to all according to their work. ^62-12
