# Genesis 25 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Genesis 24|← Genesis 24]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Genesis 26|Genesis 26 →]]

## Abraham marries Keturah

**25:1** Abraham took another wife, whose name was Keturah. ^25-1

**25:2** She bore him Zimran, Jokshan, Medan, Midian, Ishbak, and Shuah. ^25-2

**25:3** Jokshan was the father of Sheba and Dedan. The sons of Dedan were Asshurim, Letushim, and Leummim. ^25-3

**25:4** The sons of Midian were Ephah, Epher, Hanoch, Abida, and Eldaah. All these were the children of Keturah. ^25-4

**25:5** Abraham gave all he had to Isaac. ^25-5

**25:6** But to the sons of his concubines Abraham gave gifts, while he was still living, and he sent them away from his son Isaac, eastward to the east country. ^25-6

## The death of Abraham

**25:7** This is the length of Abraham’s life, one hundred seventy-five years. ^25-7

**25:8** Abraham breathed his last and died in a good old age, old and full of years, and was gathered to his people. ^25-8

**25:9** His sons Isaac and Ishmael buried him in the cave of Machpelah, in the field of Ephron son of Zohar the Hittite, east of Mamre, ^25-9

**25:10** the field that Abraham purchased from the Hittites. There Abraham was buried with his wife Sarah. ^25-10

**25:11** After the death of Abraham, God blessed his son Isaac. And Isaac settled at Beer-lahai-roi. ^25-11

## Ishmael’s descendants

**25:12** These are the descendants of Ishmael, Abraham’s son, whom Hagar the Egyptian, Sarah’s slave, bore to Abraham. ^25-12

**25:13** These are the names of the sons of Ishmael, named in the order of their birth: Nebaioth, the firstborn of Ishmael; and Kedar, Adbeel, Mibsam, ^25-13

**25:14** Mishma, Dumah, Massa, ^25-14

**25:15** Hadad, Tema, Jetur, Naphish, and Kedemah. ^25-15

**25:16** These are the sons of Ishmael, and these are their names, by their villages and by their encampments, twelve princes according to their tribes. ^25-16

**25:17** (This is the length of the life of Ishmael, one hundred thirty-seven years; he breathed his last and died and was gathered to his people.) ^25-17

**25:18** They settled from Havilah to Shur, which is opposite Egypt in the direction of Assyria; he settled down^[Heb *he fell*] alongside^[Or *down in opposition to*] all his people. ^25-18

## The birth and youth of Esau and Jacob

**25:19** These are the descendants of Isaac, Abraham’s son: Abraham was the father of Isaac, ^25-19

**25:20** and Isaac was forty years old when he married Rebekah, daughter of Bethuel the Aramean of Paddan-aram, sister of Laban the Aramean. ^25-20

**25:21** Isaac prayed to the LORD for his wife because she was barren, and the LORD granted his prayer, and his wife Rebekah conceived. ^25-21

**25:22** The children struggled together within her, and she said, “If it is to be this way, why do I live?”^[Syr: Meaning of Heb uncertain] So she went to inquire of the LORD. ^25-22

**25:23** And the LORD said to her, “Two nations are in your womb, and two peoples born of you shall be divided; the one shall be stronger than the other; the elder shall serve the younger.” ^25-23

**25:24** When her time to give birth was at hand, there were twins in her womb. ^25-24

**25:25** The first came out red, all his body like a hairy mantle, so they named him Esau. ^25-25

**25:26** Afterward his brother came out, with his hand gripping Esau’s heel, so he was named Jacob.^[That is, *he takes by the heel* or *he supplants*] Isaac was sixty years old when she bore them. ^25-26

**25:27** When the boys grew up, Esau was a skillful hunter, a man of the field, while Jacob was a quiet man, living in tents. ^25-27

**25:28** Isaac loved Esau because he was fond of game, but Rebekah loved Jacob. ^25-28

## Esau sells his birthright

**25:29** Once when Jacob was cooking a stew, Esau came in from the field, and he was famished. ^25-29

**25:30** Esau said to Jacob, “Let me eat some of that red stuff, for I am famished!” (Therefore he was called Edom.^[That is, *red*]) ^25-30

**25:31** Jacob said, “First sell me your birthright.” ^25-31

**25:32** Esau said, “I am about to die; of what use is a birthright to me?” ^25-32

**25:33** Jacob said, “Swear to me first.” So he swore to him and sold his birthright to Jacob. ^25-33

**25:34** Then Jacob gave Esau bread and lentil stew, and he ate and drank and rose and went his way. Thus Esau despised his birthright. ^25-34
