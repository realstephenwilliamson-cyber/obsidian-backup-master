# Zohar 3.189b

← [[Zohar 3.189a]] · [[Zohar (Soncino)]] · [[Zohar 3.190a]] →

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 9.09 - Yanuqa#^z3-189b|Yanuqa, at 3:189b]]

*Volume 3, folio 189b* · Balak

*Passage Balak 6:86 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 3.189a#^balak-6-86|Balak 6:86]].*

**Balak 6:87** This Vine is the one that is recognized as holy, in contrast to another which is called the “strange vine”, the grapes of which are hard and biter. But this is the vine from which all the holy ones drew the taste of old and good wine, the wine in which Jacob poured water so that all who knew the taste of wine could drink it with relish. ^balak-6-87

**Balak 6:88** When this vine came near the Shekinah it put forth three branches. Then it “was as though it budded”, like a bride bedecking herself, and it entered in the love and the joy of that wine to the place where joy abides. Then “its blossoms shot forth”: its love went out to its beloved and it began to sing in love. Then were those tender grapes ripened and filled with the good old wine into which Jacob had poured water. ^balak-6-88

**Balak 6:89** Therefore one who blesses over wine should pour some water into it, since the blessing, “Have mercy, O Lord, upon thy people Israel”, should not be said save with water in the wine.’ ^balak-6-89

**Balak 6:90** R. Eleazar and R. Abba were amazed. ^balak-6-90

**Balak 6:91** They said: ‘Holy angel, messenger from above, thy wine has conquered in the mystery of the holy spirit.’ They all came and kissed him, and R. Eleazar said: ‘Blessed be God for sending me here.’ ^balak-6-91

**Balak 6:92** The boy then said: ‘Bread and wine are the essence of the meal, all the rest being subsidiary. The Torah is begging of you, saying: “Come, eat ye of my bread and drink of the wine I have mingled” (Prov. 9, 5). Since the Torah calls you, you must obey her behest.’ They said: ‘That is assuredly so.’ So they sat and ate and rejoiced with him. After they had finished eating they still lingered at the table, ^balak-6-92

**Balak 6:93** and the boy discoursed further, on the verse: “And Moab said to the elders of Midian”. ‘It does not say, “the elders of Moab”,’ he said, ‘but simply “Moab”: the younger took counsel of the elders. What counsel did they give them? They gave a counsel which redounded to their own hurt. They said to Moab: We have reared a curse among us, namely Moses their chief, through a certain priest who was among us, who brought him up in his house and gave him his daughter to wife. Nay more, he gave him money and sent him to Egypt to destroy all the land, and he and all his household followed him. If we can uproot that chief of theirs, all his people will be immediately uprooted. All the nefarious plan of Peor was from Midian. ^balak-6-93

**Balak 6:94** On their advice they hired Balaam, and when they saw that Balaam could not prevail, they adopted another plan and prostituted their daughters and wives more than Moab. They planned with their prince that he should prostitute his daughter, thinking to catch Moses in their net. They invested her with all kinds of magic in order to catch their chief, but God “turns the wise backwards”. ^balak-6-94
