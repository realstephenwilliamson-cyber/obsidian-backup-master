# Psalms 102

Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 102]] · Septuagint (Brenton): [[LXX Psalms 101]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

Compare: [[NRSVue Psalms 102|NRSVue]] (Heb 102:1 = superscription; Heb 102:2–29 = NRSVue 102:1–28)

## Chapter 102

**102:1** A prayer of the lowly man when he is faint and pours forth his plea before the LORD. ^102-1

**102:2** O LORD, hear my prayer;  
let my cry come before You. ^102-2

**102:3** Do not hide Your face from me  
in my time of trouble;  
turn Your ear to me;  
when I cry, answer me speedily. ^102-3

**102:4** For my days have vanished like smoke  
and my bones are charred like a hearth. ^102-4

**102:5** My body is stricken and withered like grass;  
too wasted[^1] to eat my food; ^102-5

**102:6** on account of my vehement groaning  
my bones show through my skin.[^2] ^102-6

**102:7** I am like a great owl in the wilderness,  
an owl among the ruins. ^102-7

**102:8** I lie awake; I am like  
a lone bird upon a roof. ^102-8

**102:9** All day long my enemies revile me;  
my deriders use my name to curse. ^102-9

**102:10** For I have eaten ashes like bread  
and mixed my drink with tears, ^102-10

**102:11** because of Your wrath and Your fury;  
for You have cast me far away. ^102-11

**102:12** My days are like a lengthening shadow;  
I wither like grass. ^102-12

**102:13** But You, O LORD, are enthroned forever;  
Your fame endures throughout the ages. ^102-13

**102:14** You will surely arise and take pity on Zion,  
for it is time to be gracious to her;  
the appointed time has come. ^102-14

**102:15** Your servants take delight in its stones,  
and cherish its dust. ^102-15

**102:16** The nations will fear the name of the LORD,  
all the kings of the earth, Your glory. ^102-16

**102:17** For the LORD has built Zion;  
He has appeared in all His glory. ^102-17

**102:18** He has turned to the prayer of the destitute[^3]  
and has not spurned their prayer. ^102-18

**102:19** May this be written down for a coming generation,  
that people yet to be created may praise the LORD. ^102-19

**102:20** For He looks down from His holy height;  
the LORD beholds the earth from heaven ^102-20

**102:21** to hear the groans of the prisoner,  
to release those condemned to death; ^102-21

**102:22** that the fame of the LORD may be recounted in Zion,  
His praises in Jerusalem, ^102-22

**102:23** when the nations gather together,  
the kingdoms, to serve the LORD. ^102-23

**102:24** He drained my strength in mid-course,  
He shortened my days. ^102-24

**102:25** I say, “O my God, do not take me away  
in the midst of my days,  
You whose years go on for generations on end. ^102-25

**102:26** Of old You established the earth;  
the heavens are the work of Your hands. ^102-26

**102:27** They shall perish, but You shall endure;  
they shall all wear out like a garment;  
You change them like clothing and they pass away. ^102-27

**102:28** But You are the same, and Your years never end. ^102-28

**102:29** May the children of Your servants dwell securely  
and their offspring endure in Your presence.” ^102-29

[^1]: Others “I forget.”
[^2]: Lit. “cling to my flesh.”
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
