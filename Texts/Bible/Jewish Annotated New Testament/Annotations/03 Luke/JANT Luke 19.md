# Luke 19 (JANT notes)

### 19.1–10: Zacchaeus

*[[NRSVue Luke 19#^19-1|Text]], p. 154* ^19-1

**[[NRSVue Luke 19#^19-1|1]]:** *Jericho*, a customs center (see 10.30n.; 19.1). ^19-1-2

**[[NRSVue Luke 19#^19-2|2]]:** *Zacchaeus*, a Gk form of a name from the Heb for “righteous” or “upright,” as in R. Yoḥanan ben Zakkai. *Chief tax collector*, see 3.12n. *Rich*, see 6.24n.; 18.18–23. ^19-2

**[[NRSVue Luke 19#^19-7|7]]:** *Sinner*, see 5.8n.,30; 15.1–2. ^19-7

**[[NRSVue Luke 19#^19-8|8]]:** *I will give . . . will pay back*, lit., “give . . . do pay back” (present tense): Zacchaeus is less repenting than he is attesting his righteousness. *Four times*, see [[Torah 18 - Mishpatim#^21-37|Ex 22.1]] on making restitution for a theft. ^19-8

**[[NRSVue Luke 19#^19-9|9]]:** *Son of Abraham*, see 13.16n. ^19-9

**[[NRSVue Luke 19#^19-10|10]]:** *Son of Man*, see 5.24. ^19-10

### 19.11–27: Parable of the pounds

*[[NRSVue Luke 19#^19-11|Text]], p. 154* · ([[Matthew 25#^25-14|Mt 25.14–30]]; [[Mark 13#^13-34|Mk 13.34]]). ^19-11

**[[NRSVue Luke 19#^19-11|11]]:** *Kingdom of God was to appear*, messianic age would dawn; see 17.21n. ^19-11-2

**[[NRSVue Luke 19#^19-12|12]]:** *To get royal power*, reminiscent of Archelaus’s visit to Rome in 4 BCE to gain his father Herod’s kingdom (Josephus, *Ant*. 17.9.4; 17.10). ^19-12

**[[NRSVue Luke 19#^19-13|13]]:** *Pounds* (Gk “minas”), gold coins worth 100 drachmas (see 15.8n.), a substantial amount. ^19-13

**[[NRSVue Luke 19#^19-14|14]]:** *Sent a delegation*, the response of the months’ wages for a laborer Judeans to Archelaus; in 6 CE Rome replaced Archelaus with direct rule in Judea. ^19-14

**[[NRSVue Luke 19#^19-23|23]]:** *Bank* (Gk “trape-za”; lit., “table”), to invest. ^19-23

**[[NRSVue Luke 19#^19-26|26]]:** See 8.18. ^19-26

**[[NRSVue Luke 19#^19-27|27]]:** *Slaughter them*, a reference is political abuse as well as a warning regarding the final judgment. ^19-27

### 19.28–40: Triumphal procession

*[[NRSVue Luke 19#^19-28|Text]], p. 155* · ([[Matthew 21#^21-1|Mt 21.1–9]]; [[Mark 11#^11-1|Mk 11.1–10]]; [[John 12#^12-12|Jn 12.12–18]]). ^19-28

**[[NRSVue Luke 19#^19-29|29]]:** *Bethphage*, otherwise unknown; *Bethany*, east of Jerusalem (see 10.38n.). *Mount of Olives*, hill opposite Jerusalem, on the eastern side of Kidron Valley; place for the Messiah’s appearance (see [[Zechariah 14#^14-4|Zech 14.4]]); Josephus, *Ant*. 20.169 associates the location with a messianic figure: “There came out of Egypt about this time to Jerusalem, one that said he was a prophet, and advised the multitude of the common people to go along with him to the Mount of Olives . . . ” (see also *J.W.* 2.262). ^19-29

**[[NRSVue Luke 19#^19-30|30]]:** *You will find*, Jesus has local supporters. *Colt*, see [[Zechariah 09#^9-9|Zech 9.9]]. Riding into Jerusalem on a donkey indicates royalty (see [[II Samuel 18#^18-9|2 Sam 18.9]]; [[II Samuel 19#^19-27|19.26]]; [[I Kings 01#^1-33|1 Kings 1.33–40]]). ^19-30

**[[NRSVue Luke 19#^19-32|32]]:** *Found it as he had told them*, see 22.13. ^19-32

**[[NRSVue Luke 19#^19-36|36]]:** *Spreading their cloaks*, to greet a king (see [[II Kings 09#^9-13|2 Kings 9.13]]). ^19-36

**[[NRSVue Luke 19#^19-37|37]]:** *Disciples*, distinct from the crowd (contrast other versions of this event). *Deeds of power*, healings, resuscitations, exorcisms, etc. ^19-37

**[[NRSVue Luke 19#^19-38|38]]:** Combination of [[Psalms 118#^118-26|Ps 118.26]], part of the Hallel prayer (see 13.35n.) and [[Zechariah 09#^9-9|Zech 9.9]] (see also [[Luke 02#^2-14|Lk 2.14]]; [[Luke 13#^13-35|13.35]]). *King*, the charge against Jesus (23.2–3,37–38). *Highest heaven*, by the Hellenistic period, heaven was understood as having multiple levels (see e.g., [[2 Corinthians 12#^12-2|2 Cor 12.2]]). ^19-38

**[[NRSVue Luke 19#^19-39|39]]:** *Pharisees*, their last appearance in this Gospel. *Order your disciples to stop*, either they fear the proclamation would invite Roman attention (and so they are protecting him; see 13.31n.), or they seek to prevent Jesus from fulfilling his mission (and so their last appearance is malevolent). ^19-39

**[[NRSVue Luke 19#^19-40|40]]:** *Stones would shout*, see [[Habakkuk 2#^2-11|Hab 2.11]]; ironic in anticipation of 21.5–6. ^19-40

### 19.41–44: Second lament over Jerusalem

*[[NRSVue Luke 19#^19-41|Text]], p. 156* · See 13.33–34; cf. 23.27–31. ^19-41

**[[NRSVue Luke 19#^19-42|42]]:** *Things that make for peace*, the verse likely reflects knowledge of Jerusalem’s destruction in 70 CE (19.44; 21.6,20–24). The name “Jerusalem” suggests a city of peace (Heb “shalom”). **43–44**: *You did not recognize*, suggesting the city’s destruction is caused by its failure to follow Jesus. Earlier prophets predicted Babylon’s destruction of Jerusalem in 586 BCE ([[Isaiah 29#^29-3|Isa 29.3]]; [[Jeremiah 06#^6-6|Jer 6.6]]; [[Ezekiel 04#^4-2|Ezek 4.2]]), and Jesus is here in continuity with them. *Hem you in*, Roman troops surrounded the city to starve the population. *One stone upon another*, only the Kotel (Western Wall) of the retaining mound remains standing. ^19-42

### 19.45–46: Temple disruption

*[[NRSVue Luke 19#^19-45|Text]], p. 156* · ([[Matthew 21#^21-12|Mt 21.12–13]]; [[Mark 11#^11-15|Mk 11.15–17]]; [[John 02#^2-13|Jn 2.13–17]]). ^19-45

**[[NRSVue Luke 19#^19-45|45]]:** *Selling*, for sacrificial offerings (2.24); because Luke does not specify the reason for the vendors, the text can suggest venal activity. ^19-45-2

**[[NRSVue Luke 19#^19-46|46]]:** *House of prayer*, see [[Isaiah 56#^56-7|Isa 56.7]]. *Den of robbers*, see [[Jeremiah 07#^7-11|Jer 7.11]]; a place where thieves go with their ill-gotten gains. The point is not corruption or overcharging, but ritual without accompanying repentance and good deeds. The den or “cave” is where robbers take their ill-gotten gains. ^19-46

### 19.47–48: Teaching in the Temple

*[[NRSVue Luke 19#^19-47|Text]], p. 156* · ([[Mark 11#^11-18|Mk 11.18–19]]). ^19-47

**[[NRSVue Luke 19#^19-47|47]]:** *Chief priests, the scribes, and the leaders*, Jesus’ adversaries in Jerusalem (20.19). ^19-47-2
