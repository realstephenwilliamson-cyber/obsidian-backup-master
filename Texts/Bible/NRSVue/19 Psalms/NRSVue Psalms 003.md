## Trust in God under adversity

*A Psalm of David, when he fled from his son Absalom.*

**3:1** O LORD, how many are my foes! Many are rising against me; ^3-1

**3:2** many are saying to me, “There is no help for you^[Syr: Heb *him*] in God.” *Selah* ^3-2

**3:3** But you, O LORD, are a shield around me, my glory, and the one who lifts up my head. ^3-3

**3:4** I cry aloud to the LORD, and he answers me from his holy hill. *Selah* ^3-4

**3:5** I lie down and sleep; I wake again, for the LORD sustains me. ^3-5

**3:6** I am not afraid of ten thousands of people who have set themselves against me all around. ^3-6

**3:7** Rise up, O LORD! Deliver me, O my God! For you strike all my enemies on the cheek; you break the teeth of the wicked. ^3-7

**3:8** Deliverance belongs to the LORD; may your blessing be on your people! *Selah* ^3-8
