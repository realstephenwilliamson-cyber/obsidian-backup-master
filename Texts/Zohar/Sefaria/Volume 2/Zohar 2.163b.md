*Volume 2, folio 163b* · Terumah

*Passage Terumah 70:694 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 2.163a#^terumah-70-694|Terumah 70:694]].*

**Terumah 70:695** but when Israel does not assemble to worship the Lord, they lose this dignity, since they do not ascend and cannot praise their Master in fitting manner. Even if ten alone are present, the supernal hosts join them in their worship. ^terumah-70-695

**Terumah 70:696** The reason is that all the supports of that King are in the number ten, and therefore ten worshippers are sufficient if there are no more. ^terumah-70-696

**Terumah 70:697** Even if ten alone are present, the supernal hosts join them in their worship. The reason is that all the supports of that King are in the number ten, and therefore ten worshippers are sufficient if there are no more. ^terumah-70-697

**Terumah 70:698** Therefore, concerning the Tabernacle it says: “And thou shalt make the Tabernacle with ten (‘*eser*) pieces of tapestry”, ten being the number required for the full perfection of the Tabernacle. ^terumah-70-698

**Terumah 71:699** The shortened form of “ten” (‘*eser*) is used here in order to show that the Shekinah herself is not included in the ten, since she broods above the congregation. So it is in all places where a shortened form is used for “ten”, as, for instance, “It stood upon twelve (*shne ‘asar)* oxen” (I Kings 12, 25), where the Shekinah was not included in the number, for it says: “and the sea (i.e. the Shekinah) was set above them” (*Ibid.*). ^terumah-71-699

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Terumah 71:700.*

**Terumah 72:701** R. Hiya discoursed on the words: *Who coverest thyself with light as with a garment, who stretchest out the heavens like a curtain (yeri’ah*) (Ps. 104, 2). Said he: ‘These words have been interpreted as follows: When the Holy One was about to create the world He robed Himself in the primordial light and created the heavens. ^terumah-72-701

**Terumah 72:702** At first the light was at the right and the darkness at the left. What, then, did the Holy One do? He merged the one into the other and from them formed the heavens: *shamaim* (heavens) is composed of *esh* and *mayim* (fire and water, i.e. right and left). He brought them together and harmonized them, ^terumah-72-702

**Terumah 72:703** and when they were united as one, He stretched them out like a curtain, and formed them into the letter *vau*. From this letter the light spread, so that “curtain” became “curtains”, as it is written: “And thou shalt make the tabernacle with ten curtains.” ^terumah-72-703

**Terumah 72:704** Seven firmaments are stretched out and stored in the supernal treasure-house, as has been explained, and over them is one firmament which has no colour and no place in the world of cognition, and is outside the range of contemplation; but, though hidden, it diffuses light to all and speeds them each on its fitting orbit. ^terumah-72-704

**Terumah 72:705** Beyond that firmament knowledge cannot penetrate, and man must close his mouth and not seek to reflect upon it. He who does so reflect is turned backwards, for it passes our knowledge- ^terumah-72-705

**Terumah 72:706** The ten curtains of the Tabernacle symbolized the ten firmaments, and their mystery can be comprehended only by the wise of heart’ He who grasps this attains great wisdom and penetrates into the mysteries of the universe, for he contemplates in this that which is above in the region to which every one of them is attached, except those two that are at the right and at the left, and which are hidden with the Shekinah.’ ^terumah-72-706
