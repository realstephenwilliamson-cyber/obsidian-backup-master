## Mūla

अष्टादश विजानीयाद्भुवनानि समासतः ।
शिवतत्त्वं परं शान्तं कला तत्रावकाशदा ॥ ^mula

aṣṭādaśa vijānīyād bhuvanāni samāsataḥ |
śivatattvam param śāntam kalā tatrāvakāśadā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, October 2022 – December 2022
> p. 26 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=33>))
>
> D) The fourth (Egg) is (pervaded by) the Sanctifying One (utpūyanī). (The wise) know that there are three principles there (i.e., Śuddhavidyā, Īśvara and Sadāśiva). One should perceive that there are three phonemes (each for) one Mantra and one Pada.[^74] In brief, one should know that there are eighteen (worlds). (with 2.55–56ab)

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, October 2022 – December 2022
> p. 27 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=34>))
>
> E) The Śiva principle is supreme and tranquil. The Force there is the Bestower of Space (avakāśadā). One should perceive that the group of sixteen vowels is there as well as one Mantra and one Pada (consisting of the same sixteen vowels). The six-fold Path is said in brief to be thus.[^75] (with 2.56cd–57)

[^74]: *The three phonemes are G, KH, K. The one Mantra and one Pada are GAKHAKA.*
[^75]: MV 2/50cd-57 quoted in TĀv ad 11/51cd-54ab (51-53), which summarizes these.
