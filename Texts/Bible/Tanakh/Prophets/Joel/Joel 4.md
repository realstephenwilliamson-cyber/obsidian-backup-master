# Joel 4

← [[Joel 3]] · [[Tanakh]]

Compare: [[NRSVue Joel 03|NRSVue]] (Heb 4:1–21 = NRSVue 3:1–21)

## Chapter 4

**4:1** For lo! in those days  
And in that time,  
When I restore the fortunes  
Of Judah and Jerusalem, ^4-1

**4:2** I will gather all the nations  
And bring them down to the Valley of Jehoshaphat.[^1]  
There I will contend with them  
Over My very own people, Israel,  
Which they scattered among the nations.  
For they divided My land among themselves ^4-2

**4:3** And cast lots over My people;  
And they bartered a boy for a whore,  
And sold a girl for wine, which they drank. ^4-3

**4:4** What is this you are doing to Me, O Tyre, Sidon, and all the districts of Philistia? Are you requiting Me for something I have done, or are you doing something for My benefit? Quick as a flash, I will pay you back; ^4-4

**4:5** for you have taken My gold and My silver, and have carried off My precious treasures to your palaces; ^4-5

**4:6** and you have sold the people of Judah and the people of Jerusalem to the Ionians, so that you have removed them far away from their homeland. ^4-6

**4:7** Behold, I will rouse them to leave the place you have sold them to, and I will pay you back: ^4-7

**4:8** I will deliver your sons and daughters into the hands of the people of Judah, and they will sell them into captivity to a distant nation—for the LORD has spoken. ^4-8

**4:9** Proclaim this among the nations:  
Prepare for battle!  
Arouse the warriors,  
Let all the fighters come and draw near! ^4-9

**4:10** Beat your plowshares[^2] into swords,  
And your pruning hooks into spears.  
Let even the weakling say, “I am strong.” ^4-10

**4:11** Rouse yourselves[^3] and come,  
All you nations;  
Come together  
From round about.  
There bring down[^4]  
Your warriors, O LORD! ^4-11

**4:12** Let the nations rouse themselves and march up  
To the Valley of Jehoshaphat;[^5]  
For there I will sit in judgment  
Over all the nations round about. ^4-12

**4:13** Swing the sickle,  
For the crop is ripe;  
Come and tread,  
For the winepress is full,  
The vats are overflowing!  
For great is their wickedness. ^4-13

**4:14** Multitudes upon multitudes  
In the Valley of Decision!  
For the day of the LORD is at hand  
In the Valley of Decision. ^4-14

**4:15** Sun and moon are darkened,  
And stars withdraw their brightness. ^4-15

**4:16** And the LORD will roar from Zion,  
And shout aloud from Jerusalem,  
So that heaven and earth tremble.  
But the LORD will be a shelter to His people,  
A refuge to the children of Israel. ^4-16

**4:17** And you shall know that I the LORD your God  
Dwell in Zion, My holy mount.  
And Jerusalem shall be holy;  
Nevermore shall strangers pass through it. ^4-17

**4:18** And in that day,  
The mountains shall drip with wine,  
The hills shall flow with milk,  
And all the watercourses of Judah shall flow with water;  
A spring shall issue from the House of the LORD  
And shall water the Wadi of the Acacias. ^4-18

**4:19** Egypt shall be a desolation,  
And Edom a desolate waste,  
Because of the outrage to the people of Judah,  
In whose land they shed the blood of the innocent. ^4-19

**4:20** But Judah shall be inhabited forever,  
And Jerusalem throughout the ages. ^4-20

**4:21** Thus I will treat as innocent their blood  
Which I have not treated as innocent;[^6]  
And the LORD shall dwell in Zion. ^4-21

[^1]: Here understood as “The Lord contends”; contrast v. 12.
[^2]: See note at Isa. 2.4.
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^5]: Here understood as “The Lord judges”; contrast v. 2.
[^6]: Emendation yields “their unavenged blood shall be avenged.”
