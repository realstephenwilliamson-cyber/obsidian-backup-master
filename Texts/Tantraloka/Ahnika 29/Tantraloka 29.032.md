## Mūla

विन्ध्योऽजितोऽप्यजरया सह मेखलया परे ।
मच्छन्दः कुंकुणाम्बा च षड्युग्मं साधिकारकम् ॥ ^mula

vindhyo'jito'pyajarayā saha mekhalayā pare |
macchandaḥ kuṃkuṇāmbā ca ṣaḍyugmaṃ sādhikārakam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 09 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 1#^pg250|p. 233]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=250>))
>
> Khagendranātha together with Vijjambā, Vaktaṣti with Ilāiambā, Vimala with Anantamekhalāmbā, are in the east. Kāūrmanātha with (his) consort (śakti) Maṅgalā, Jaitra with IHlāīambā, Avijita with Ānandamekhalā, are in the south. Meṣanātha with Kāmamaṁṁgalā, Vindhya and Ajita with Kullāīambā and Ajaramekhalā, respectively, are in the west. Macchandanātha and Kuṁñkuṇāmbā and the six pairs (of princes and their consorts) with authority (to give initiation) are in the north (of the Mandala). Such is the second line that extends from the northwest to the northeast. (with 29.29cd–33ab)
