## Mūla

पृथक्तत्त्वविधौ दीक्षां योग्यतावशवर्तिनः । तत्त्वाभ्यासविधानेन सिद्धयोगी समाचरेत् ॥ ^mula

pṛthaktattvavidhau dīkṣāṃ yogyatāvaśavartinaḥ | tattvābhyāsavidhānena siddhayogī samācaret || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 13 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 5#^pg387|p. 370]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=387>))
>
> The (fully) accomplished (siddha) yogi should initiate (the disciple,) according to (his) aptitude, into the procedure prescribed for separate reality levels, by means of repeated practice of (each) reality level. (The disciple) who aspires to liberation and has been initiated (in this way as a spiritual son), should be taught the sacrifice (performed according to the) Kula liturgy (kulakrama), along with the five states (of consciousness), when abiding (in the practice that) remains (to be observed for the rest of his life). (with 29.219–220)
