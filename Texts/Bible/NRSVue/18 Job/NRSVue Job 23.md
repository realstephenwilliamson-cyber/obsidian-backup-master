# Job 23 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Job 22|← Job 22]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Job 24|Job 24 →]]

## Job replies: My complaint is bitter

**23:1** Then Job answered: ^23-1

**23:2** “Today also my complaint is bitter;^[Syr Vg Tg: Heb *rebellious*] his^[Gk Syr: Heb *my*] hand is heavy despite my groaning. ^23-2

**23:3** Oh, that I knew where I might find him, that I might come even to his dwelling! ^23-3

**23:4** I would lay my case before him and fill my mouth with arguments. ^23-4

**23:5** I would learn what he would answer me and understand what he would say to me. ^23-5

**23:6** Would he contend with me in the greatness of his power? No, but he would give heed to me. ^23-6

**23:7** There the upright could reason with him, and I should be acquitted forever by my judge. ^23-7

**23:8** “If I go forward, he is not there; or backward, I cannot perceive him; ^23-8

**23:9** on the left he hides, and I cannot behold him; I turn^[Syr Vg: Heb *he turns*] to the right, but I cannot see him. ^23-9

**23:10** But he knows the way that I take; when he has tested me, I shall come out like gold. ^23-10

**23:11** My foot has held fast to his steps; I have kept his way and have not turned aside. ^23-11

**23:12** I have not departed from the commandment of his lips; I have treasured his words in my bosom.^[Gk: Heb *words more than my daily bread*] ^23-12

**23:13** But he stands alone, and who can dissuade him? What he desires, that he does. ^23-13

**23:14** For he will complete what he appoints for me, and many such things are in his mind. ^23-14

**23:15** Therefore I am terrified at his presence; when I consider, I am in dread of him. ^23-15

**23:16** God has made my heart faint; the Almighty^[Traditional rendering of Heb *Shaddai*] has terrified me. ^23-16

**23:17** If only I could vanish in darkness, and thick darkness would cover my face!^[Or *But I am not destroyed by the darkness; he has concealed the thick darkness from me*] ^23-17
