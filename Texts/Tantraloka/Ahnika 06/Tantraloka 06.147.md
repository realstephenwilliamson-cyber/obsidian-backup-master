## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 11 - Chapter Six, part 4#^pg383|p. 368]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=383>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 6.147cd–148ab.
>
> The life of the Rudras who reside in Water is equal to a day and a night of the Rudras who reside in Fire, and so on up to the principle of unmanifest Nature (avyakta). In this way, it proceeds for a hundred years (of each Rudra), progressively (upwards). (Such is this) division of Day and Night, that accords with the length of each one’s lifespan of a hundred (divine years).
