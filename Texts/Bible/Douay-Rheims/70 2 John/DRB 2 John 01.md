# 2 John 1 (Douay-Rheims)

— · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · —

Compare: [[2 John 01|BSB and SBLGNT]]

**1:1** The ancient to the lady Elect, and her children, whom I love in the truth, and not I only, but also all they that have known the truth, ^1-1

**1:2** For the sake of the truth which dwelleth in us, and shall be with us for ever. ^1-2

**1:3** Grace be with you, mercy, and peace from God the Father, and from Christ Jesus the Son of the Father; in truth and charity. ^1-3

**1:4** I was exceeding glad, that I found of thy children walking in truth, as we have received a commandment from the Father. ^1-4

**1:5** And now I beseech thee, lady, not as writing a new commandment to thee, but that which we have had from the beginning, that we love one another. ^1-5

**1:6** And this is charity, that we walk according to his commandments. For this is the commandment, that, as you have heard from the beginning, you should walk in the same: ^1-6

**1:7** For many seducers are gone out into the world, who confess not that Jesus Christ is come in the flesh: this is a seducer and an antichrist. ^1-7

**1:8** Look to yourselves, that you lose not the things which you have wrought: but that you may receive a full reward. ^1-8

**1:9** Whosoever revolteth, and continueth not in the doctrine of Christ, hath not God. He that continueth in the doctrine, the same hath both the Father and the Son. ^1-9

**1:10** If any man come to you, and bring not this doctrine, receive him not into the house nor say to him, God speed you. ^1-10

**1:11** For he that saith unto him, God speed you, communicateth with his wicked works. ^1-11

**1:12** Having more things to write unto you, I would not by paper and ink: for I hope that I shall be with you, and speak face to face: that your joy may be full. ^1-12

**1:13** The children of thy sister Elect salute thee. ^1-13
