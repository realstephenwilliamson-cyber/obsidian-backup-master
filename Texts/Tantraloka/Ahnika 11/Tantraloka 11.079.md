## Mūla

ध्रुवं कवित्ववक्तृत्वशालितां यान्ति सर्वतः ।
यावद्धामनि सङ्केतनिकारकलनोज्झिते ॥ ^mula

dhruvaṃ kavitvavaktṛtvaśālitāṃ yānti sarvataḥ |
yāvaddhāmani saṅketanikārakalanojjhite || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 03 - Chapter Eleven, part 2#^pg90|p. 70]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=90>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 11.78cd–80ab.
>
> Those who are well-established in this creative intuition (pratibhā) which, abounding with (this) primal (phonemic consciousness), is great, certainly attain eloquence and poetic genius in all respects. (with 11.78cd–79ab)
>
> Indeed, what does one who thus rests within the abode which is pure consciousness and is free of the (conditioning) activity (kalanā) of all conventions, not know or do? (with 11.79cd–80ab)
