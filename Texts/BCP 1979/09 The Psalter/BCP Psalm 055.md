# Psalm 55

← [[BCP Psalm 054]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 056]] →

*Exaudi, Deus* · Book Two · Tenth Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 055]]

**55:1** Hear my prayer, O God; \*  
do not hide yourself from my petition. ^55-1

**55:2** Listen to me and answer me; \*  
I have no peace, because of my cares. ^55-2

**55:3** I am shaken by the noise of the enemy \*  
and by the pressure of the wicked; ^55-3

**55:4** For they have cast an evil spell upon me \*  
and are set against me in fury. ^55-4

**55:5** My heart quakes within me, \*  
and the terrors of death have fallen upon me. ^55-5

**55:6** Fear and trembling have come over me, \*  
and horror overwhelms me. ^55-6

**55:7** And I said, "Oh, that I had wings like a dove! \*  
I would fly away and be at rest. ^55-7

**55:8** I would flee to a far-off place \*  
and make my lodging in the wilderness. ^55-8

**55:9** I would hasten to escape \*  
from the stormy wind and tempest." ^55-9

**55:10** Swallow them up, O Lord;  
confound their speech; \*  
for I have seen violence and strife in the city. ^55-10

[p. 661]

**55:11** Day and night the watchmen make their rounds upon her walls, \*  
but trouble and misery are in the midst of her. ^55-11

**55:12** There is corruption at her heart; \*  
her streets are never free of oppression and deceit. ^55-12

**55:13** For had it been an adversary who taunted me,  
then I could have borne it; \*  
or had it been an enemy who vaunted himself against me,  
then I could have hidden from him. ^55-13

**55:14** But it was you, a man after my own heart, \*  
my companion, my own familiar friend. ^55-14

**55:15** We took sweet counsel together, \*  
and walked with the throng in the house of God. ^55-15

**55:16** Let death come upon them suddenly;  
let them go down alive to the grave; \*  
for wickedness is in their dwellings, in their very midst. ^55-16

**55:17** But I will call upon God, \*  
and the LORD will deliver me. ^55-17

**55:18** In the evening, in the morning, and at noonday,  
I will complain and lament, \*  
and he will hear my voice. ^55-18

**55:19** He will bring me safely back from the battle waged against me; \*  
for there are many who fight me. ^55-19

**55:20** God, who is enthroned of old, will hear me and bring them down; \*  
they never change; they do not fear God. ^55-20

**55:21** My companion stretched forth his hand against his comrade; \*  
he has broken his covenant. ^55-21

[p. 662]

**55:22** His speech is softer than butter, \*  
but war is in his heart. ^55-22

**55:23** His words are smoother than oil, \*  
but they are drawn swords. ^55-23

**55:24** Cast your burden upon the LORD,  
and he will sustain you; \*  
he will never let the righteous stumble. ^55-24

**55:25** For you will bring the bloodthirsty and deceitful \*  
down to the pit of destruction, O God. ^55-25

**55:26** They shall not live out half their days, \*  
but I will put my trust in you. ^55-26
