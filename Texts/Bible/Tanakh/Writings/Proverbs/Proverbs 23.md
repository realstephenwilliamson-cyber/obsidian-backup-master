# Proverbs 23

Compare: [[NRSVue Proverbs 23|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 23

**23:1** When you sit down to dine with a ruler,  
Consider well who is before you. ^23-1

**23:2** Thrust a knife into your gullet  
If you have a large appetite. ^23-2

**23:3** Do not crave for his dainties,  
For they are counterfeit food. ^23-3

**23:4** Do not toil to gain wealth;  
Have the sense to desist. ^23-4

**23:5** You see it, then it is gone;  
It grows wings and flies away,  
Like an eagle, heavenward. ^23-5

**23:6** Do not eat of a stingy man’s food;  
Do not crave for his dainties; ^23-6

**23:7** He is like one keeping accounts;  
“Eat and drink,” he says to you,  
But he does not really mean it. ^23-7

**23:8** The morsel you eat you will vomit;  
You will waste your courteous words. ^23-8

**23:9** Do not speak to a dullard,  
For he will disdain your sensible words. ^23-9

**23:10** Do not remove ancient boundary stones;  
Do not encroach upon the field of orphans, ^23-10

**23:11** For they have a mighty Kinsman,  
And He will surely take up their cause with you. ^23-11

**23:12** Apply your mind to discipline  
And your ears to wise sayings. ^23-12

**23:13** Do not withhold discipline from a child;  
If you beat him with a rod he will not die. ^23-13

**23:14** Beat him with a rod  
And you will save him from the grave. ^23-14

**23:15** My son, if your mind gets wisdom,  
My mind, too, will be gladdened. ^23-15

**23:16** I shall rejoice with all my heart[^1]  
When your lips speak right things. ^23-16

**23:17** Do not envy sinners in your heart,  
But only God-fearing men, at all times, ^23-17

**23:18** For then you will have a future,  
And your hope will never fail. ^23-18

**23:19** Listen, my son, and get wisdom;  
Lead your mind in a [proper] path. ^23-19

**23:20** Do not be of those who guzzle wine,  
Or glut themselves on meat; ^23-20

**23:21** For guzzlers and gluttons will be impoverished,  
And drowsing will clothe you in tatters. ^23-21

**23:22** Listen to your father who begot you;  
Do not disdain your mother when she is old. ^23-22

**23:23** Buy truth and never sell it,  
And wisdom, discipline, and understanding. ^23-23

**23:24** The father of a righteous man will exult;  
He who begets a wise son will rejoice in him. ^23-24

**23:25** Your father and mother will rejoice;  
She who bore you will exult. ^23-25

**23:26** Give your mind to me, my son;  
Let your eyes watch my ways. ^23-26

**23:27** A harlot is a deep pit;  
A forbidden[^2] woman is a narrow well. ^23-27

**23:28** She too lies in wait as if for prey,  
And destroys the unfaithful among men. ^23-28

**23:29** Who cries, “Woe!” who, “Alas!”;  
Who has quarrels, who complaints;  
Who has wounds without cause;  
Who has bleary eyes? ^23-29

**23:30** Those whom wine keeps till the small hours,  
Those who gather to drain the cups. ^23-30

**23:31** Do not ogle that red wine  
As it lends its color to the cup,  
As it flows on smoothly; ^23-31

**23:32** In the end, it bites like a snake;  
It spits like a basilisk. ^23-32

**23:33** Your eyes will see strange sights;  
Your heart[^3] will speak distorted things. ^23-33

**23:34** You will be like one lying in bed on high seas,  
Like one lying on top of the rigging.[^4] ^23-34

**23:35** “They struck me, but I felt no hurt;  
They beat me, but I was unaware;  
As often as I wake,  
I go after it again.” ^23-35

[^1]: Lit. “kidneys.”
[^2]: Lit. “alien.”
[^3]: See note to 15.28.
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
