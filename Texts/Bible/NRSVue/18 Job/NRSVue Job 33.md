# Job 33 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Job 32|← Job 32]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Job 34|Job 34 →]]

## Elihu rebukes Job

**33:1** “But now, hear my speech, O Job, and listen to all my words. ^33-1

**33:2** See, I open my mouth; the tongue in my mouth speaks. ^33-2

**33:3** My words declare the uprightness of my heart, and what my lips know they speak sincerely. ^33-3

**33:4** The spirit of God has made me, and the breath of the Almighty^[Traditional rendering of Heb *Shaddai*] gives me life. ^33-4

**33:5** Answer me, if you can; set your words in order before me; take your stand. ^33-5

**33:6** See, before God I am as you are; I, too, was formed from a piece of clay. ^33-6

**33:7** No fear of me need terrify you; my pressure will not be heavy on you. ^33-7

**33:8** “Surely, you have spoken in my hearing, and I have heard the sound of your words. ^33-8

**33:9** You say, ‘I am clean, without transgression; I am pure, and there is no iniquity in me. ^33-9

**33:10** Look, he finds occasions against me; he counts me as his enemy; ^33-10

**33:11** he puts my feet in the stocks and watches all my paths.’ ^33-11

**33:12** “But in this you are not right. I will answer you: God is greater than any mortal. ^33-12

**33:13** Why do you contend against him, saying, ‘He will answer none of my^[Compare Gk: Heb *his*] words’? ^33-13

**33:14** For God speaks in one way and in two, though people do not perceive it. ^33-14

**33:15** In a dream, in a vision of the night, when deep sleep falls on mortals, while they slumber on their beds, ^33-15

**33:16** then he opens their ears and terrifies them with warnings, ^33-16

**33:17** that he may turn them aside from their deeds and keep them from pride, ^33-17

**33:18** to spare their souls from the Pit, their lives from traversing the River. ^33-18

**33:19** They are also chastened with pain upon their beds and with continual strife in their bones, ^33-19

**33:20** so that their lives loathe bread and their appetites dainty food. ^33-20

**33:21** Their flesh is so wasted away that it cannot be seen, and their bones, once invisible, now stick out. ^33-21

**33:22** Their souls draw near the Pit and their lives to those who bring death. ^33-22

**33:23** Then, if there should be for one of them an angel, a mediator, one of a thousand, one who declares a person upright, ^33-23

**33:24** and he is gracious to that person and says, ‘Deliver him from going down into the Pit; I have found a ransom; ^33-24

**33:25** let his flesh become fresh with youth; let him return to the days of his youthful vigor,’ ^33-25

**33:26** then he prays to God and is accepted by him; he comes into his presence with joy, and God^[Heb *he*] repays him for his righteousness. ^33-26

**33:27** That person sings to others and says, ‘I sinned and perverted what was right, and it was not paid back to me. ^33-27

**33:28** He has redeemed my soul from going down to the Pit, and my life shall see the light.’ ^33-28

**33:29** “God indeed does all these things, twice, three times, with mortals, ^33-29

**33:30** to bring back their souls from the Pit, so that they may see the light of life.^[Syr: Heb *to be lighted with the light of life*] ^33-30

**33:31** Pay heed, Job, listen to me; be silent, and I will speak. ^33-31

**33:32** If you have anything to say, answer me; speak, for I desire to justify you. ^33-32

**33:33** If not, listen to me; be silent, and I will teach you wisdom.” ^33-33
