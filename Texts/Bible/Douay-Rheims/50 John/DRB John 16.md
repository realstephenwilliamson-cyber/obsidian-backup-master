# John 16 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB John 15|← John 15]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB John 17|John 17 →]]

Compare: [[John 16|BSB and SBLGNT]]

**16:1** These things have I spoken to you, that you may not be scandalized. ^16-1

**16:2** They will put you out of the synagogues: yea, the hour cometh, that whosoever killeth you, will think that he doth a service to God. ^16-2

**16:3** And these things will they do to you; because they have not known the Father, nor me. ^16-3

**16:4** But these things I have told you, that when the hour shall come, you may remember that I told you of them. ^16-4

**16:5** But I told you not these things from the beginning, because I was with you. And now I go to him that sent me, and none of you asketh me: Whither goest thou? ^16-5

**16:6** But because I have spoken these things to you, sorrow hath filled your heart. ^16-6

**16:7** But I tell you the truth: it is expedient to you that I go: for if I go not, the Paraclete will not come to you; but if I go, I will send him to you. ^16-7

**16:8** And when he is come, he will convince the world of sin, and of justice, and of judgment. ^16-8

**16:9** Of sin: because they believed not in me. ^16-9

**16:10** And of justice: because I go to the Father; and you shall see me no longer. ^16-10

**16:11** And of judgment: because the prince of this world is already judged. ^16-11

**16:12** I have yet many things to say to you: but you cannot bear them now. ^16-12

**16:13** But when he, the Spirit of truth, is come, he will teach you all truth. For he shall not speak of himself; but what things soever he shall hear, he shall speak; and the things that are to come, he shall shew you. ^16-13

**16:14** He shall glorify me; because he shall receive of mine, and shall shew it to you. ^16-14

**16:15** All things whatsoever the Father hath, are mine. Therefore I said, that he shall receive of mine, and shew it to you. ^16-15

**16:16** A little while, and now you shall not see me; and again a little while, and you shall see me: because I go to the Father. ^16-16

**16:17** Then some of the disciples said one to another: What is this that he saith to us: A little while, and you shall not see me; and again a little while, and you shall see me, and, because I go to the Father? ^16-17

**16:18** They said therefore: What is this that he saith, A little while? we know not what he speaketh. ^16-18

**16:19** And Jesus knew that they had a mind to ask him; and he said to them: Of this do you inquire among yourselves, because I said: A little while, and you shall not see me; and again a little while, and you shall see me? ^16-19

**16:20** Amen, amen I say to you, that you shall lament and weep, but the world shall rejoice; and you shall be made sorrowful, but your sorrow shall be turned into joy. ^16-20

**16:21** A woman, when she is in labour, hath sorrow, because her hour is come; but when she hath brought forth the child, she remembereth no more the anguish, for joy that a man is born into the world. ^16-21

**16:22** So also you now indeed have sorrow; but I will see you again, and your heart shall rejoice; and your joy no man shall take from you. ^16-22

**16:23** And in that day you shall not ask me any thing. Amen, amen I say to you: if you ask the Father any thing in my name, he will give it you. ^16-23

**16:24** Hitherto you have not asked any thing in my name. Ask, and you shall receive; that your joy may be full. ^16-24

**16:25** These things I have spoken to you in proverbs. The hour cometh, when I will no more speak to you in proverbs, but will shew you plainly of the Father. ^16-25

**16:26** In that day you shall ask in my name; and I say not to you, that I will ask the Father for you: ^16-26

**16:27** For the Father himself loveth you, because you have loved me, and have believed that I came out from God. ^16-27

**16:28** I came forth from the Father, and am come into the world: again I leave the world, and I go to the Father. ^16-28

**16:29** His disciples say to him: Behold, now thou speakest plainly, and speakest no proverb. ^16-29

**16:30** Now we know that thou knowest all things, and thou needest not that any man should ask thee. By this we believe that thou camest forth from God. ^16-30

**16:31** Jesus answered them: Do you now believe? ^16-31

**16:32** Behold, the hour cometh, and it is now come, that you shall be scattered every man to his own, and shall leave me alone; and yet I am not alone, because the Father is with me. ^16-32

**16:33** These things I have spoken to you, that in me you may have peace. In the world you shall have distress: but have confidence, I have overcome the world. ^16-33
