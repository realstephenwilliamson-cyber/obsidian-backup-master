§ 307

Chapter of R. Nehuniah Ben HaQanah, Which He Taught R. Ishmael[^a] *Who will proclaim the mighty acts of YWY* (Ps 106:2) and who is able to recount the praise of the King of Kings of Kings? These are the attending angels. You are declared majestic, You are praised, etc., YWY, God of Israel, blessed be He forever, You are exalted forever.

§ 308[^a]

R. Ishmael said:

At once R.
