## Mūla

पञ्चाशीतिशती या चत्वारिंशत्समुत्तरा कथिता ।
तां सप्तत्या भित्त्वा दीक्षाभेदान्स्वयं कलयेत् ॥ ^mula

pañcāśītiśatī yā catvāriṃśatsamuttarā kathitā |

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 06 - Chapter Sixteen, part 2#^pg100|p. 80]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=100>))

> 2(The teacher) may calculate for himself the varieties of initiations, having multiplied by seventy the aforementioned eight thousand five hundred and forty varieties. According to the Mālinītantra, the types of initiations here (in this case) number five hundred and ninety-seven thousand eight hundred. (with 16.185–186)
