# Ephesians 5 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Ephesians 04|← Ephesians 4]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Ephesians 06|Ephesians 6 →]]

Compare: [[Ephesians 05|BSB and SBLGNT]] · [[DRB Ephesians 05|Douay-Rheims]] · [[JANT Ephesians 05|JANT notes]]

**5:1** Therefore be imitators of God, as beloved children, ^5-1

**5:2** and walk in love, as Christ loved us^[Other ancient authorities read *you*] and gave himself up for us, a fragrant offering and sacrifice to God. ^5-2

## Renounce pagan ways

**5:3** But sexual immorality and impurity of any kind or greed must not even be mentioned among you, as is proper among saints. ^5-3

**5:4** Entirely out of place is obscene, silly, and vulgar talk; but instead, let there be thanksgiving. ^5-4

**5:5** Be sure of this, that no sexually immoral or impure person or one who is greedy (that is, an idolater) has any inheritance in the kingdom of Christ and of God. ^5-5

**5:6** Let no one deceive you with empty words, for because of these things the wrath of God comes on those who are disobedient.^[Gk *sons of disobedience*] ^5-6

**5:7** Therefore do not be associated with them, ^5-7

**5:8** for once you were darkness, but now in the Lord you are light. Walk as children of light, ^5-8

**5:9** for the fruit of the light^[Other ancient authorities read *of the Spirit*] is found in all that is good and right and true. ^5-9

**5:10** Try to find out what is pleasing to the Lord. ^5-10

**5:11** Take no part in the unfruitful works of darkness; rather, expose them. ^5-11

**5:12** For it is shameful even to mention what such people do secretly, ^5-12

**5:13** but everything exposed by the light becomes visible, ^5-13

**5:14** for everything that becomes visible is light. Therefore it says, “Sleeper, awake! Rise from the dead, and Christ will shine on you.” ^5-14

**5:15** Be careful, then, how you live,^[Gk *walk*] not as unwise people but as wise, ^5-15

**5:16** making the most of the time, because the days are evil. ^5-16

**5:17** So do not be foolish, but understand what the will of the Lord is. ^5-17

**5:18** Do not get drunk with wine, for that is debauchery, but be filled with the Spirit, ^5-18

**5:19** as you sing psalms and hymns and spiritual songs to one another, singing and making melody to the Lord in your hearts, ^5-19

**5:20** giving thanks to God the Father at all times and for everything in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, ^5-20

**5:21** being subject to one another out of reverence for Christ. ^5-21

## The Christian household

**5:22** Wives, be subject^[Gk lacks *be subject*] to your husbands as to the Lord, ^5-22

**5:23** for the husband is the head of the wife just as Christ is the head of the church, his body, and is himself its Savior. ^5-23

**5:24** Just as the church is subject to Christ, so also wives ought to be, in everything, to their husbands. ^5-24

**5:25** Husbands, love your wives, just as Christ loved the church and gave himself up for her ^5-25

**5:26** in order to make her holy by cleansing her with the washing of water by the word, ^5-26

**5:27** so as to present the church to himself in splendor, without a spot or wrinkle or anything of the kind, so that she may be holy and without blemish. ^5-27

**5:28** In the same way, husbands should love their wives as their own bodies. He who loves his wife loves himself. ^5-28

**5:29** For no one ever hates his own flesh, but he nourishes and tenderly cares for it, just as Christ does for the church, ^5-29

**5:30** because we are members of his body.^[Other ancient authorities add *of his flesh and of his bones*] ^5-30

**5:31** “For this reason a man will leave his father and mother and be joined to his wife, and the two will become one flesh.” ^5-31

**5:32** This is a great mystery, but I am speaking about Christ and the church. ^5-32

**5:33** Each of you, however, should love his wife as himself, and a wife should respect^[Or *fear*] her husband. ^5-33
