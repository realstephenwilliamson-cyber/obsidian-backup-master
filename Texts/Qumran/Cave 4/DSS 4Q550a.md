### Text

**1** a man; but the King kno[ws] whether there is […] ^t-1

**2** and his good name will not pass away, [and his] loyal[ty …] ^t-2

**3** (of) the King will be for Patireza, son of … […] ^t-3

**4** there fell upon him the dread of the house of Safra […] ^t-4

**5** Ushay the King of Tama[r,] and it will be given […] ^t-5

**6** my house and my belongings to all, that which … […] ^t-6

**7** will be measured; and you shall receive your father’s service […] ^t-7
