## Mūla

ध्यायन् प्राग्वत्प्रयोगेण शिवं सकलनिष्कलम् ।
द्व्यात्मकं वा क्षिपेत्पूर्णां प्रशान्तकरणेन तु ॥ ^mula

dhyāyan prāgvatprayogeṇa śivaṃ sakalaniṣkalam |
dvyātmakaṃ vā kṣipetpūrṇāṃ praśāntakaraṇena tu || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 08 - Chapter Seventeen#^pg193|p. 173]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=193>))
>
> Then, (the teacher), thinking of all that needs to be known (prameya) taught previously, having conjoined the disciple’s body etc. with his own body and vital breath etc., should recollect that everything (viśva) is present within his own body and vital breath etc. (Then, his) intellect firm and mind undistracted, meditating by means of the method (prakriyā) taught (previously) on the two paths of time and space (as) present within the vital breath, the breath (as) present within (Śiva’s) power, and that that (power rests) within consciousness, (he should then meditate on how) that pure consciousness, which is iva, is not separate from the disciple’s consciousness, and is undivided from the fire of Mantra and the rest. Meditating (thus,) with (the same) application (prayoga) (of mantra etc.) as before on Ñiva, with differentiated energies and without them (sakalaniṣkala) or as both together, he should cast a full (oblation into the fire) with a completely tranquil mind (praśāntakaranṇa). (with 17.78cd–82)

## Viveka

> [!viveka]- Viveka in English (Dyczkowski, vol. IX, p. 174)
> ![[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 08 - Chapter Seventeen#Viveka on 17.78–82]]

> ततः प्रागुक्तसकलप्रमेयं परिचिन्तयन् ॥
> शिष्यदेहादिमात्मीयदेहप्राणादियोजितम् ।
> कृत्वात्मदेहप्राणादेर्विश्वमन्तरनुस्मरेत् ॥
> उक्तप्रक्रियया चैवं दृढबुद्धिरनन्यधीः ।
> प्राणस्थं देशकालाध्वयुगं प्राणं च शक्तिगम् ॥
> तां च संविद्गतां शुद्धां संविदं शिवरूपिणीम् ।
> शिष्यसंविदभिन्नां च मन्त्रवह्न्याद्यभेदिनीम् ॥
> ध्यायन् प्राग्वत्प्रयोगेण शिवं सकलनिष्कलम् ।
> द्व्यात्मकं वा क्षिपेत्पूर्णां प्रशान्तकरणेन तु ॥

शक्तिगमिति--कालशक्तिगतम्--इत्यर्थः । द्व्यात्मकमिति-- सकलनिष्कलोभयस्वभावम्--इत्यर्थः । प्रागुक्तेति उक्तप्रक्रिययेति प्राग्वदिति च--अनेन षोडशपञ्चदशाह्निकादौ एतद्विस्तरेणोक्तमिति स्मारितम्, अत एव एतदस्माभिरपि ग्रन्थविस्तरभयान्नेह वितानितम् ॥८२ ॥
