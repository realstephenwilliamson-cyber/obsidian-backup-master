## Mūla

हृदये संमुखौ हस्तौ संलग्नौ प्रसृताङ्गुली ।
नमस्कृतिरियं मुद्रा मन्त्रवन्दनकर्मणि ॥ ^mula

hṛdaye saṃmukhau hastau saṃlagnau prasṛtāṅgulī |
namaskṛtiriyaṃ mudrā mantravandanakarmaṇi || ^iast

*Dyczkowski prints no Sanskrit for this chapter; the text is that of the KSTS edition (Muktabodha M00160).*

## Translation

>
> **23. Nati (Salutation)**
>
> Joining the hands together in front of the heart with the fingers extended – this is the gesture of Salutation (*namaskṛti*) (which is used for) the act of praising the mantra (deity).
