*Volume 3, folio 49a* · Tazria

*Passage Tazria 24:123 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 3.048b#^tazria-24-123|Tazria 24:123]].*

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Tazria 24:124–24:128.*

**Tazria 24:129** AND IF HIS HAIR BE FALLEN OFF FROM THE FRONT PART OF HIS HEAD. This front part is what is called ‘ the face of wrath”, and all who depend from that “face” are bold, stem and cruel. But when the “hair” is removed from the side of that “face”, all these are removed and rendered powerless. ^tazria-24-129

**Tazria 24:130** For, as we have learnt, all those who depend from the “hair of the head” are superior to the others and not bold-faced like them. Hence the faces of the latter are red like fire from the flashing of the Lamp of Darkness. ^tazria-24-130

**Tazria 25:131** Rabbi Isaac said: ‘This plague is called “reddish-white” (Lev. 13, 42); it is still a plague if the white is visible and the red has not disappeared. When, however, the whole has become white, mercy is present and judgement departs.’ ^tazria-25-131

**Tazria 25:132** Rabbi Abba said: ‘Sometimes the Female is sullied by the sins of men and sometimes the Male, and the priest knew from which side the punishment came, ^tazria-25-132

**Tazria 25:133** and he also knew the sacrifices which ought to be brought, as it is written: “The sacrifices of *Elohim* (i.e. sacrifices to avert punishment) are a contrite spirit” (Ps. 51, 18).’ ^tazria-25-133

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Tazria 25:134.*
