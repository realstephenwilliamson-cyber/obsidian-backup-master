# Sirach 3 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Sirach 02|← Sirach 2]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Sirach 04|Sirach 4 →]]

## Duties toward parents

**3:1** Listen to me your father, O children; act accordingly, that you may be safe. ^3-1

**3:2** For the Lord honors a father above his children, and he confirms a mother’s judgment over her sons. ^3-2

**3:3** Those who honor their father atone for sins, ^3-3

**3:4** and those who respect their mother are like those who lay up treasure. ^3-4

**3:5** Those who honor their father will have joy in their children, and when they pray they will be heard. ^3-5

**3:6** Those who respect their father will have long life, and those who honor^[Heb: Other ancient authorities read *comfort*] their mother obey the Lord;^[Heb: Gk *and those who obey the Lord honor their mother*] ^3-6

**3:7** they will serve their parents as their masters.^[In other ancient authorities this line is preceded by *Those who fear the Lord honor their father,*] ^3-7

**3:8** Honor your father by word and deed, that his blessing may come upon you. ^3-8

**3:9** For a father’s blessing strengthens the houses of the children, but a mother’s curse uproots their foundations. ^3-9

**3:10** Do not glorify yourself by your father’s dishonor, for your father’s dishonor is no glory to you. ^3-10

**3:11** The honor of one’s father is one’s own glory, and a mother dishonored is a disgrace to her children. ^3-11

**3:12** My child, help your father in his old age, and do not grieve him as long as he lives; ^3-12

**3:13** even if his mind fails, be patient with him; do not despise him because you have all your faculties. ^3-13

**3:14** For kindness to a father will not be forgotten and will be credited to you against your sins; ^3-14

**3:15** in the day of your distress it will be remembered in your favor; like frost in fair weather, your sins will melt away. ^3-15

**3:16** Whoever forsakes a father is like a blasphemer, and whoever angers a mother is cursed by the Lord. ^3-16

## Humility

**3:17** My child, perform your tasks with humility; then you will be loved more than a giver of gifts.^[Heb Syr: Gk *more than an acceptable person*] ^3-17

**3:18** The greater you are, the more you must humble yourself; so you will find favor in the sight of the Lord.^[Other ancient authorities add as 3.19, *Many are lofty and renowned, but to the humble he reveals his secrets.*] ^3-18

**3:20** For great is the might of the Lord, but by the humble he is glorified. ^3-20

**3:21** Neither seek what is too difficult for you nor investigate what is beyond your power. ^3-21

**3:22** Reflect upon what you have been commanded, for what is hidden is not your concern. ^3-22

**3:23** In matters greater than your own affairs, do not meddle, for things beyond human understanding have been shown you. ^3-23

**3:24** For their conceit has led many astray, and wrong opinion has impaired their judgment. ^3-24

**3:25** Without eyes there is no light; without knowledge there is no wisdom.^[Heb: Other ancient authorities lack 3.25] ^3-25

**3:26** A stubborn mind will fare badly at the end, and whoever loves danger will perish in it. ^3-26

**3:27** A stubborn mind will be burdened by troubles, and the sinner adds sin to sins. ^3-27

**3:28** When calamity befalls the proud, there is no healing, for an evil plant has taken root in him. ^3-28

**3:29** The mind of the intelligent appreciates proverbs, and an attentive ear is the desire of the wise. ^3-29

## Alms for the poor

**3:30** As water extinguishes a blazing fire, so almsgiving atones for sin. ^3-30

**3:31** Those who repay favors give thought to the future; when they fall they will find support. ^3-31
