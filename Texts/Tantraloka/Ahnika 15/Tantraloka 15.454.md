## Mūla

सत्यत्यन्तमदृष्टे प्रागपि जायेत योग्यता ।
कृतप्रज्ञा हि विन्यस्तमन्त्रं देहं जलं स्थलम् ॥ ^mula

satyatyantamadṛṣṭe prāgapi jāyeta yogyatā |
kṛtaprajñā hi vinyastamantraṃ dehaṃ jalaṃ sthalam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 08 - Chapter Fifteen, part 7#^pg296|p. 278]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=296>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 15.456cd–457ab.
>
> When such an aid to sight and the other senses is present, they become fit (to perceive) even what was previously totally invisible. (with 15.453cd–454ab)
>
> Those whose insight is developed (krtaprajñā), observing a body, water, a place, an icon and the like onto which Mantras have been deposited, know (immediately) whether (the Mantra deity is) present or not. (with 15.454cd–455ab)
