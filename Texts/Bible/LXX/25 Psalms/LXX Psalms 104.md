# LXX Psalms 104

← [[LXX Psalms 103]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 105]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 105]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 105]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**104:1** Alleluia. Give thanks to the Lord, and call upon his name; declare his works among the heathen.  ^104-1

**104:2** Sing to him, yea, sing praises to him: tell forth all his wonderful works.  ^104-2

**104:3** Glory in his holy name: let the heart of them that seek the Lord rejoice.  ^104-3

**104:4** Seek ye the Lord, and be strengthened; seek his face continually.  ^104-4

**104:5** Remember his wonderful works that he has done; his wonders, and the judgments of his mouth;  ^104-5

**104:6** *ye* seed of Abraam, his servants, *ye* children of Jacob, his chosen ones.  ^104-6

**104:7** He is the Lord our God; his judgments are in all the earth.  ^104-7

**104:8** He has remembered his covenant for ever, the word which he commanded for a thousand generations:  ^104-8

**104:9** which he established as a covenant to Abraam, and *he remembered* his oath to Isaac.  ^104-9

**104:10** And he established it to Jacob for an ordinance, and to Israel for an everlasting covenant;  ^104-10

**104:11** saying, To thee will I give the land of Chanaan, the line of your inheritance:  ^104-11

**104:12** when they were few in number, very few, and sojourners in it.  ^104-12

**104:13** And they went from nation to nation, and from *one* kingdom to another people.  ^104-13

**104:14** He suffered no man to wrong them; and he rebuked kings for their sakes:  ^104-14

**104:15** *saying*, Touch not my anointed ones; and do my prophets no harm.  ^104-15

**104:16** Moreover he called for a famine upon the land; he broke the whole support of bread.  ^104-16

**104:17** He sent a man before them; Joseph was sold for a slave.  ^104-17

**104:18** They hurt his feet with fetters; his soul passed into iron,[^1][^2]  ^104-18

**104:19** until the time that his cause came on; the word of the Lord tried him as fire.  ^104-19

**104:20** The king sent and loosed him; *even* the prince of the people, and let him go free.  ^104-20

**104:21** He made him lord over his house, and ruler of all his substance;  ^104-21

**104:22** to chastise his rulers at his pleasure, and to teach his elders wisdom.  ^104-22

**104:23** Israel also came into Egypt, and Jacob sojourned in the land of Cham.  ^104-23

**104:24** And he increased his people greatly, and made them stronger than their enemies.  ^104-24

**104:25** And he turned their heart to hate his people, to deal craftily with his servants.  ^104-25

**104:26** He sent forth Moses his servant, *and* Aaron whom he had chosen.  ^104-26

**104:27** He established among them his signs, and *his* wonders in the land of Cham.  ^104-27

**104:28** He sent forth darkness, and made it dark; yet they rebelled against his words.[^3]  ^104-28

**104:29** He turned their waters into blood, and slew their fish.  ^104-29

**104:30** Their land produced frogs abundantly, in the chambers of their kings.  ^104-30

**104:31** He spoke, and the dog-fly came, and lice in all their coasts.  ^104-31

**104:32** He turned their rain into hail, *and sent* flaming fire in their land.  ^104-32

**104:33** And he smote their vines and their fig-trees; and broke every tree of their coast.  ^104-33

**104:34** He spoke, and the locust came, and caterpillars innumerable,  ^104-34

**104:35** and devoured all the grass in their land, and devoured the fruit of their ground.  ^104-35

**104:36** He smote also every first-born of their land, the first-fruits of all their labour.  ^104-36

**104:37** And he brought them out with silver and gold; and there was not a feeble one among their tribes.  ^104-37

**104:38** Egypt rejoiced at their departing; for the fear of them fell upon them.  ^104-38

**104:39** He spread out a cloud for a covering to them, and fire to give them light by night.  ^104-39

**104:40** They asked, and the quail came, and he satisfied them with the bread of heaven.  ^104-40

**104:41** He clave the rock, and the waters flowed; rivers ran in dry places.  ^104-41

**104:42** For he remembered his holy word, which *he promised* to Abraam his servant.  ^104-42

**104:43** And he brought out his people with exultation, and his chosen with joy;  ^104-43

**104:44** and gave them the lands of the heathen; and they inherited the labours of the people;  ^104-44

**104:45** that they might keep his ordinances, and diligently seek his law.  ^104-45

[^1]: 104:18 *Gr.* humbled.
[^2]: 104:18 *Or,* his body, see *Heb.*
[^3]: 104:28 *Gr.* embittered.
