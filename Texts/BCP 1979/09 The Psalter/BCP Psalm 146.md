# Psalm 146

← [[BCP Psalm 145]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 147]] →

*Lauda, anima mea* · Book Five · Thirtieth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 146]]

**146:1** Hallelujah!  
Praise the LORD, O my soul! \*  
I will praise the LORD as long as I live;  
I will sing praises to my God while I have my being. ^146-1

**146:2** Put not your trust in rulers, nor in any child of earth, \*  
for there is no help in them. ^146-2

**146:3** When they breathe their last, they return to earth, \*  
and in that day their thoughts perish. ^146-3

**146:4** Happy are they who have the God of Jacob for their help! \*  
whose hope is in the LORD their God; ^146-4

**146:5** Who made heaven and earth, the seas, and all that is in them; \*  
who keeps his promise for ever; ^146-5

**146:6** Who gives justice to those who are oppressed, \*  
and food to those who hunger. ^146-6

**146:7** The LORD sets the prisoners free;  
the LORD opens the eyes of the blind; \*  
the LORD lifts up those who are bowed down; ^146-7

**146:8** The LORD loves the righteous;  
the LORD cares for the stranger; \*  
he sustains the orphan and widow,  
but frustrates the way of the wicked. ^146-8

**146:9** The LORD shall reign for ever, \*  
your God, O Zion, throughout all generations.  
Hallelujah! ^146-9
