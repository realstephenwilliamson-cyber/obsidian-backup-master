# Sirach 48 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Sirach 47|← Sirach 47]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Sirach 49|Sirach 49 →]]

## Elijah

**48:1** Then Elijah arose, a prophet like fire, and his word burned like a torch. ^48-1

**48:2** He brought a famine upon them, and by his zeal he made them few in number. ^48-2

**48:3** By the word of the Lord he shut up the heavens and also three times brought down fire. ^48-3

**48:4** How glorious you were, Elijah, in your wondrous deeds! Who can boast^[Or *be glorified*] as you can? ^48-4

**48:5** You who raised a corpse from death and from Hades, by the word of the Most High, ^48-5

**48:6** who sent kings down to destruction and famous men from their sickbeds, ^48-6

**48:7** who heard rebuke at Sinai and judgments of vengeance at Horeb, ^48-7

**48:8** who anointed kings to inflict retribution and prophets to succeed you,^[Heb: Gk *him*] ^48-8

**48:9** who were taken up by a whirlwind of fire in a chariot with horses of fire, ^48-9

**48:10** who were prepared at the appointed time^[Heb: Gk *are for reproofs*] to calm wrath before it breaks out in fury, to turn the hearts of parents to their children and to restore the tribes of Jacob. ^48-10

**48:11** Happy are those who saw you and were adorned^[Other ancient authorities read *and have died*] in love! For we also shall surely live.^[Meaning of Gk uncertain] ^48-11

## Elisha

**48:12** When Elijah was enveloped in the whirlwind, Elisha was filled with his spirit. He performed twice as many signs and marvels with every utterance of his mouth.^[Heb: Gk lacks *He performed . . . mouth*] Never in his lifetime did he tremble before any ruler, nor could anyone intimidate him at all. ^48-12

**48:13** Nothing was too hard for him,^[Heb: Gk *no word surpassed him*] and when he was dead his body prophesied. ^48-13

**48:14** In his life he did wonders, and in death his deeds were marvelous. ^48-14

**48:15** Despite all this the people did not repent, nor did they forsake their sins, until they were carried off as plunder from their land and were scattered over all the earth. The people were left very few in number but with a ruler from the house of David. ^48-15

**48:16** Some of them did what was right, but others sinned more and more. ^48-16

## Hezekiah

**48:17** Hezekiah fortified his city and brought water into its midst; he tunneled the rock with iron tools and built cisterns for the water. ^48-17

**48:18** In his days Sennacherib invaded the country; he sent his commander^[Other ancient authorities add *from Lachish*] and departed; he shook his fist against Zion and made great boasts in his arrogance. ^48-18

**48:19** Then their hearts and hands were shaken, and they were in anguish, like women in labor. ^48-19

**48:20** But they called upon the Lord who is merciful, spreading out their hands toward him. The Holy One quickly heard them from heaven and delivered them through Isaiah. ^48-20

**48:21** The Lord^[Gk *He*] struck down the camp of the Assyrians, and his angel wiped them out. ^48-21

**48:22** For Hezekiah did what was pleasing to the Lord, and he kept firmly to the ways of his ancestor David, as he was commanded by the prophet Isaiah, who was great and trustworthy in his visions. ^48-22

## Isaiah

**48:23** In Isaiah’s^[Gk *his*] days the sun went backward, and he prolonged the life of the king. ^48-23

**48:24** By his dauntless spirit he saw the future and comforted the mourners in Zion. ^48-24

**48:25** He revealed what was to occur to the end of time and the hidden things before they happened. ^48-25
