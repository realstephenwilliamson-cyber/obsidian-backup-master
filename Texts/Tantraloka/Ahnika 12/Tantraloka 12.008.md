## Mūla

इत्थं घटं पटं लिङ्गं स्थण्डिलं पुस्तकं जलम् ।
यद्वा किञ्चित् क्वचित्पश्येत्तत्र तन्मयतां व्रजेत् ॥ ^mula

itthaṃ ghaṭaṃ paṭaṃ liṅgaṃ sthaṇḍilaṃ pustakaṃ jalam |
yadvā kiñcit kvacitpaśyettatra tanmayatāṃ vrajet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 04 - Chapter Twelve#^pg123|p. 103]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=123>))
>
> (He should regard every ritual object) in this way, be it the (sacrificial) jar, cloth, Liṁñga, sacrificial ground, book, (consecrated) water, or anything at all. Or else, wherever he may see (things in this way), he there becomes of that same (conscious) nature.
