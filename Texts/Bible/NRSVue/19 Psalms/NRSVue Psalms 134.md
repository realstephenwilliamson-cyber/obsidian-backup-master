## Praise in the night

*A Song of Ascents.*

**134:1** Come, bless the LORD, all you servants of the LORD, who stand by night in the house of the LORD! ^134-1

**134:2** Lift up your hands to the holy place, and bless the LORD. ^134-2

**134:3** May the LORD, maker of heaven and earth, bless you from Zion. ^134-3
