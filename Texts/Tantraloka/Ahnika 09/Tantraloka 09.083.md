## Mūla

तमीशं प्रति युक्तं यद् भूयसां स्यात्सधर्मता ।
तेन स्वरूपस्वातन्त्र्यमात्रं मलविजृम्भितम् ॥ ^mula

tamīśaṃ prati yuktaṃ yad bhūyasāṃ syātsadharmatā |
tena svarūpasvātantryamātraṃ malavijṛmbhitam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 05 - Chapter Nine, part 3#^pg100|p. 83]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=100>))
>
> Again, if both are equally pure, why should (the souls) not be the ones who impel (Impurity) towards the Lord? For those who possess the (prevailing) quality are the majority. (with 9.82cd–83ab)
>
> Therefore, (the varied) expansion (vijrmbhita) of Impurity is only (an expression of) the freedom (inherent in the Lord’s) own nature. I have explained (all) this extensively elsewhere, and so let this suffice. (with 9.83cd–84ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* इदानीमेतदुपसंहरन् प्रकृतमेवानुसरति --

> तेन स्वरूपस्वातन्त्र्यमात्रं मलविजृम्भितम् ॥
> निर्णीतं विततं चैतन्मयान्यत्रेत्यलं पुनः ।

अन्यत्रेति प्रथमाह्निकादौ अत एव पुनरित्युक्तम् ॥

न चास्य क्वचिदपि विगीततास्ति -- इत्याह ।
