## Mūla

इति नित्यविधिः प्रोक्तो नैमित्तिकमथोच्यते ॥ ^mula

iti nityavidhiḥ prokto naimittikamathocyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 02 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, opening page#^pg18|p. 1]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=18>))
>
> Thus (everything) has been said concerning the regular (obligatory) ritual procedure (nityavidhi). Now (we will) talk about the occasional (naimittika) one.
