# Psalm 11 (NRSVue)

## Song of trust in God

*To the leader. Of David.*

**11:1** In the LORD I take refuge; how can you say to me, “Flee like a bird to the mountains,^[Gk Syr Jerome Tg: Heb *flee to your mountain, O bird*] ^11-1

**11:2** for look, the wicked bend the bow, they have fitted their arrow to the string, to shoot in the dark at the upright in heart. ^11-2

**11:3** If the foundations are destroyed, what can the righteous do?” ^11-3

**11:4** The LORD is in his holy temple; the LORD’s throne is in heaven. His eyes behold; his gaze examines humankind. ^11-4

**11:5** The LORD tests the righteous and the wicked, and his soul hates the lover of violence. ^11-5

**11:6** On the wicked he will rain coals of fire and sulfur; a scorching wind shall be the portion of their cup. ^11-6

**11:7** For the LORD is righteous; he loves righteous deeds; the upright shall behold his face. ^11-7
