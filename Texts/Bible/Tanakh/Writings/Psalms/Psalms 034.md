## Chapter 34

**34:1** Of David, when he feigned madness in the presence of Abimelech, who turned him out, and he left.[^1] ^34-1

**34:2** I bless the LORD at all times;  
praise of Him is ever in my mouth. ^34-2

**34:3** I glory in the LORD;  
let the lowly hear it and rejoice. ^34-3

**34:4** Exalt the LORD with me;  
let us extol His name together. ^34-4

**34:5** I turned to the LORD, and He answered me;  
He saved me from all my terrors. ^34-5

**34:6** Men look to Him and are radiant;  
let their faces not be downcast. ^34-6

**34:7** Here was a lowly man who called,  
and the LORD listened,  
and delivered him from all his troubles. ^34-7

**34:8** The angel of the LORD camps around those who fear Him  
and rescues them. ^34-8

**34:9** Taste and see how good the LORD is;  
happy the man who takes refuge in Him! ^34-9

**34:10** Fear the LORD, you His consecrated ones,  
for those who fear Him lack nothing. ^34-10

**34:11** Lions have been reduced to starvation,  
but those who turn to the LORD shall not lack any good. ^34-11

**34:12** Come, my sons, listen to me;  
I will teach you what it is to fear the LORD. ^34-12

**34:13** Who is the man who is eager for life,  
who desires years of good fortune? ^34-13

**34:14** Guard your tongue from evil,  
your lips from deceitful speech. ^34-14

**34:15** Shun evil and do good,  
seek amity[^2] and pursue it. ^34-15

**34:16** The eyes of the LORD are on the righteous,  
His ears attentive to their cry. ^34-16

**34:17** The face of the LORD is set against evildoers,  
to erase their names from the earth. ^34-17

**34:18** They[^3] cry out, and the LORD hears,  
and saves them from all their troubles. ^34-18

**34:19** The LORD is close to the brokenhearted;  
those crushed in spirit He delivers. ^34-19

**34:20** Though the misfortunes of the righteous be many,  
the LORD will save him from them all, ^34-20

**34:21** Keeping all his bones intact,  
not one of them being broken. ^34-21

**34:22** One misfortune is the deathblow of the wicked;  
the foes of the righteous shall be ruined. ^34-22

**34:23** The LORD redeems the life of His servants;  
all who take refuge in Him shall not be ruined. ^34-23

[^1]: Cf. 1 Sam. 21.14 ff.
[^2]: Or “integrity.”
[^3]: Viz., the righteous of v. 16.
