## Mūla

तां च चिद्रूपतोन्मेषं बाह्यत्वं तन्निमेषताम् ।
भविनां त्वन्तिकोऽप्येवं न भातीत्यतिदूरता ॥ ^mula

tāṃ ca cidrūpatonmeṣaṃ bāhyatvaṃ tannimeṣatām |
bhavināṃ tvantiko'pyevaṃ na bhātītyatidūratā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 13 - Chapter Ten, part 4#^pg449|p. 430]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=449>))
>
> (The master Utpaladeva) says that interiority (anantratvam) is a state of close proximity to consciousness. It is the expansion (unmeṣa) of the conscious nature, whereas externality is (its) withdrawal (nimeṣa). (with 10.218cd–219ab)
>
> Even though (Śiva) is close to those subject to transmigration (bhavin) in this way, he does not manifest (as such, and so the idea arises that He is) very far away. (with 10.219cd)
