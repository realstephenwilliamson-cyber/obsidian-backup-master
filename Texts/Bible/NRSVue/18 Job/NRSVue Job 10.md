## Job: I loathe my life

**10:1** “I loathe my life; I will give free utterance to my complaint; I will speak in the bitterness of my soul. ^10-1

**10:2** I will say to God, ‘Do not condemn me; let me know why you contend against me. ^10-2

**10:3** Does it seem good to you to oppress, to despise the work of your hands and favor the schemes of the wicked? ^10-3

**10:4** Do you have eyes of flesh? Do you see as humans see? ^10-4

**10:5** Are your days like the days of mortals or your years like human years, ^10-5

**10:6** that you seek out my iniquity and search for my sin, ^10-6

**10:7** although you know that I am not guilty, and there is no one to deliver out of your hand? ^10-7

**10:8** Your hands fashioned and made me, and now you turn and destroy me.^[Cn Compare Gk Syr: Heb *made me together all around, and you destroy me*] ^10-8

**10:9** Remember that you fashioned me like clay, and will you turn me to dust again? ^10-9

**10:10** Did you not pour me out like milk and curdle me like cheese? ^10-10

**10:11** You clothed me with skin and flesh and knit me together with bones and sinews. ^10-11

**10:12** You have granted me life and steadfast love, and your care has preserved my spirit. ^10-12

**10:13** Yet these things you hid in your heart; I know that this was your purpose. ^10-13

**10:14** If I sin, you watch me and do not acquit me of my iniquity. ^10-14

**10:15** If I am wicked, woe to me! If I am righteous, I cannot lift up my head, for I am filled with disgrace and look upon my affliction. ^10-15

**10:16** Bold as a lion you hunt me; you repeat your exploits against me. ^10-16

**10:17** You renew your witnesses against me and increase your vexation toward me; you bring fresh troops against me.^[Cn Compare Gk: Heb *toward me; changes and a troop are with me*] ^10-17

**10:18** “ ‘Why did you bring me forth from the womb? Would that I had died before any eye had seen me ^10-18

**10:19** and were as though I had not been, carried from the womb to the grave. ^10-19

**10:20** Are not the days of my life few?^[Cn Compare Gk Syr: Heb *Are not my days few? Let him cease!*] Let me alone, that I may find a little comfort^[Heb *that I may brighten up a little*] ^10-20

**10:21** before I go, never to return, to the land of gloom and deep darkness, ^10-21

**10:22** the land of gloom^[Heb *gloom as darkness, deep darkness*] and chaos, where light is like darkness.’ ” ^10-22
