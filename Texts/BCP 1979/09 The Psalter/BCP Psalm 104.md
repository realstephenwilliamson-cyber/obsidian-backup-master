# Psalm 104

← [[BCP Psalm 103]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 105]] →

*Benedic, anima mea* · Book Four · Twentieth Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 104]]

**104:1** Bless the LORD, O my soul; \*  
O LORD my God, how excellent is your greatness!  
you are clothed with majesty and splendor. ^104-1

**104:2** You wrap yourself with light as with a cloak \*  
and spread out the heavens like a curtain. ^104-2

**104:3** You lay the beams of your chambers in the waters above; \*  
you make the clouds your chariot;  
you ride on the wings of the wind. ^104-3

**104:4** You make the winds your messengers \*  
and flames of fire your servants. ^104-4

**104:5** You have set the earth upon its foundations, \*  
so that it never shall move at any time. ^104-5

**104:6** You covered it with the Deep as with a mantle; \*  
the waters stood higher than the mountains. ^104-6

**104:7** At your rebuke they fled; \*  
at the voice of your thunder they hastened away. ^104-7

**104:8** They went up into the hills and down to the valleys beneath, \*  
to the places you had appointed for them. ^104-8

**104:9** You set the limits that they should not pass; \*  
they shall not again cover the earth. ^104-9

**104:10** You send the springs into the valleys; \*  
they flow between the mountains. ^104-10

**104:11** All the beasts of the field drink their fill from them, \*  
and the wild asses quench their thirst. ^104-11

**104:12** Beside them the birds of the air make their nests \*  
and sing among the branches. ^104-12

[p. 736]

**104:13** You water the mountains from your dwelling on high; \*  
the earth is fully satisfied by the fruit of your works. ^104-13

**104:14** You make grass grow for flocks and herds \*  
and plants to serve mankind; ^104-14

**104:15** That they may bring forth food from the earth, \*  
and wine to gladden our hearts, ^104-15

**104:16** Oil to make a cheerful countenance, \*  
and bread to strengthen the heart. ^104-16

**104:17** The trees of the LORD are full of sap, \*  
the cedars of Lebanon which he planted, ^104-17

**104:18** In which the birds build their nests, \*  
and in whose tops the stork makes his dwelling. ^104-18

**104:19** The high hills are a refuge for the mountain goats, \*  
and the stony cliffs for the rock badgers. ^104-19

**104:20** You appointed the moon to mark the seasons, \*  
and the sun knows the time of its setting. ^104-20

**104:21** You make darkness that it may be night, \*  
in which all the beasts of the forest prowl. ^104-21

**104:22** The lions roar after their prey \*  
and seek their food from God. ^104-22

**104:23** The sun rises, and they slip away \*  
and lay themselves down in their dens. ^104-23

**104:24** Man goes forth to his work \*  
and to his labor until the evening. ^104-24

**104:25** O LORD, how manifold are your works! \*  
in wisdom you have made them all;  
the earth is full of your creatures. ^104-25

[p. 737]

**104:26** Yonder is the great and wide sea  
with its living things too many to number, \*  
creatures both small and great. ^104-26

**104:27** There move the ships,  
and there is that Leviathan, \*  
which you have made for the sport of it. ^104-27

**104:28** All of them look to you \*  
to give them their food in due season. ^104-28

**104:29** You give it to them; they gather it; \*  
you open your hand, and they are filled with good things. ^104-29

**104:30** You hide your face, and they are terrified; \*  
you take away their breath,  
and they die and return to their dust. ^104-30

**104:31** You send forth your Spirit, and they are created; \*  
and so you renew the face of the earth. ^104-31

**104:32** May the glory of the LORD endure for ever; \*  
may the LORD rejoice in all his works. ^104-32

**104:33** He looks at the earth and it trembles; \*  
he touches the mountains and they smoke. ^104-33

**104:34** I will sing to the LORD as long as I live; \*  
I will praise my God while I have my being. ^104-34

**104:35** May these words of mine please him; \*  
I will rejoice in the LORD. ^104-35

**104:36** Let sinners be consumed out of the earth, \*  
and the wicked be no more. ^104-36

**104:37** Bless the LORD, O my soul. \*  
Hallelujah! ^104-37
