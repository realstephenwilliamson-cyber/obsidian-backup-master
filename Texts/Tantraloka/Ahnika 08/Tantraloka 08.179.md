## Mūla

मा भूदण्डत्वमित्याहुरन्ये भेदकयोजनम् ।
इत्थमुक्तविरिञ्चाण्डभृतो रुद्राः शतं हि यत् ॥ ^mula

mā bhūdaṇḍatvamityāhuranye bhedakayojanam |
itthamuktaviriñcāṇḍabhṛto rudrāḥ śataṃ hi yat || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 06 - Chapter Eight, part 4#^pg221|p. 203]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=221>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 8.179cd–180ab.
>
> Others say that these distinguishing features are added in order to counter the objection that the state of the Egg (as an aggregate of entities) would not only be that of these states (i.e. the body etc.), but also that of (others) in the domain of the Path subordinate to Māyā. (with 8.178cd–179ab)
>
> The one hundred Rudras, who, as described, sustain the Egg of Brahmā, have as their Lords eleven Rudras of great splendour. (with 8.179cd–180ab)

*Lead-in:* एवं प्रकरणादण्डस्वरूपं व्याख्याय प्रकृतमेवानुसरति --
