# LXX Isaiah 12

← [[LXX Isaiah 11]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Isaiah 13]] →

*Isaiah · [[LXX]]*

**12:1** And in that day thou shalt say, I *will* bless thee, O Lord; for thou wast angry with me, but thou hast turned aside thy wrath, and hast pitied me.  ^12-1

**12:2** Behold, my God is my Saviour; I will trust in him, and not be afraid: for the Lord is my glory and my praise, and is become my salvation.  ^12-2

**12:3** Draw ye therefore water with joy out of the wells of salvation.  ^12-3

**12:4** And in that day thou shalt say, Sing to the Lord, call aloud upon his name, proclaim his glorious *deeds* among the Gentiles; make mention that his name is exalted.  ^12-4

**12:5** Sing praise to the name of the Lord; for he has done great *things*: declare this in all the earth.  ^12-5

**12:6** Exalt and rejoice, ye that dwell in Sion: for the Holy One of Israel is exalted in the midst of her.[^1]  ^12-6

[^1]: 12:6 *Heb.* and *Alex.*‘of thee’.
