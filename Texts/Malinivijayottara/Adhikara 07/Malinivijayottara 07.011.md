## Mūla

वामतो वक्त्रगां कुर्याद्वाममुष्टेः कनिष्ठिकाम् ।
दंष्ट्रेयं कीर्तिता देवि चामुण्डाकुलनन्दिनी ॥ ^mula

vāmato vaktragāṃ kuryādvāmamuṣṭeḥ kaniṣṭhikām |
daṃṣṭreyaṃ kīrtitā devi cāmuṇḍākulanandinī || ^iast

*Dyczkowski prints no Sanskrit for this chapter; the text is that of the KSTS edition (Muktabodha M00160).*

> **7. Daṃṣṭra (the Fang)**
>
> Place the little finger of the left fist on the mouth to the left. This, O goddess, is praised as (the gesture of) the Fang that is the delight of Cāmuṇḍā's Kula.
