# Job 27

## Chapter 27

**27:1** Job again took up his theme and said: ^27-1

**27:2** By God who has deprived me of justice!  
By Shaddai who has embittered my life! ^27-2

**27:3** As long as there is life in me,  
And God’s breath is in my nostrils, ^27-3

**27:4** My lips will speak no wrong,  
Nor my tongue utter deceit. ^27-4

**27:5** Far be it from me to say you are right;  
Until I die I will maintain my integrity. ^27-5

**27:6** I persist in my righteousness and will not yield;  
I shall be free of reproach[^1] as long as I live. ^27-6

**27:7** May my enemy be as the wicked;  
My assailant, as the wrongdoer. ^27-7

**27:8** For what hope has the impious man when he is cut down,  
When God takes away his life? ^27-8

**27:9** Will God hear his cry  
When trouble comes upon him, ^27-9

**27:10** When he seeks the favor of Shaddai,  
Calls upon God at all times? ^27-10

**27:11** I will teach you what is in God’s power,  
And what is with Shaddai I will not conceal. ^27-11

**27:12** All of you have seen it,  
So why talk nonsense? ^27-12

**27:13** This is the evil man’s portion from God,  
The lot that the ruthless receive from Shaddai: ^27-13

**27:14** Should he have many sons—they are marked for the sword;  
His descendants will never have their fill of bread; ^27-14

**27:15** Those who survive him will be buried in a plague,  
And their widows will not weep; ^27-15

**27:16** Should he pile up silver like dust,  
Lay up clothing like dirt— ^27-16

**27:17** He may lay it up, but the righteous will wear it,  
And the innocent will share the silver. ^27-17

**27:18** The house he built is like a bird’s nest,  
Like the booth a watchman makes. ^27-18

**27:19** He lies down, a rich man, with [his wealth] intact;  
When he opens his eyes it is gone. ^27-19

**27:20** Terror overtakes him like a flood;  
A storm wind makes off with him by night. ^27-20

**27:21** The east wind carries him far away, and he is gone;  
It sweeps him from his place. ^27-21

**27:22** Then it hurls itself at him without mercy;  
He tries to escape from its force. ^27-22

**27:23** It claps its hands at him,  
And whistles at him from its place. ^27-23

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
