# Psalm 40

← [[BCP Psalm 039]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 041]] →

*Expectans, expectavi* · Book One · Eighth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 040]]

**40:1** I waited patiently upon the LORD; \*  
he stooped to me and heard my cry. ^40-1

**40:2** He lifted me out of the desolate pit, out of the mire and clay; \*  
he set my feet upon a high cliff and made my footing sure. ^40-2

**40:3** He put a new song in my mouth,  
a song of praise to our God; \*  
many shall see, and stand in awe,  
and put their trust in the LORD. ^40-3

**40:4** Happy are they who trust in the LORD! \*  
they do not resort to evil spirits or turn to false gods. ^40-4

**40:5** Great things are they that you have done, O LORD my God!  
how great your wonders and your plans for us! \*  
there is none who can be compared with you. ^40-5

**40:6** Oh, that I could make them known and tell them! \*  
but they are more than I can count. ^40-6

**40:7** In sacrifice and offering you take no pleasure \*  
(you have given me ears to hear you); ^40-7

**40:8** Burnt-offering and sin-offering you have not required, \*  
and so I said, "Behold, I come. ^40-8

**40:9** In the roll of the book it is written concerning me: \*  
'I love to do your will, O my God;  
your law is deep in my heart.'" ^40-9

**40:10** I proclaimed righteousness in the great congregation; \*  
behold, I did not restrain my lips;  
and that, O LORD, you know. ^40-10

**40:11** Your righteousness have I not hidden in my heart;  
I have spoken of your faithfulness and your deliverance; \*  
I have not concealed your love and faithfulness from the great congregation. ^40-11

[p. 641] ^pg641

**40:12** You are the LORD;  
do not withhold your compassion from me; \*  
let your love and your faithfulness keep me safe for ever, ^40-12

**40:13** For innumerable troubles have crowded upon me;  
my sins have overtaken me, and I cannot see; \*  
they are more in number than the hairs of my head,  
and my heart fails me. ^40-13

**40:14** Be pleased, O LORD, to deliver me; \*  
O LORD, make haste to help me. ^40-14

**40:15** Let them be ashamed and altogether dismayed  
who seek after my life to destroy it; \*  
let them draw back and be disgraced  
who take pleasure in my misfortune. ^40-15

**40:16** Let those who say "Aha!" and gloat over me be confounded, \*  
because they are ashamed. ^40-16

**40:17** Let all who seek you rejoice in you and be glad; \*  
let those who love your salvation continually say,  
Great is the LORD!" ^40-17

**40:18** Though I am poor and afflicted, \*  
the Lord will have regard for me. ^40-18

**40:19** You are my helper and my deliverer; \*  
do not tarry, O my God. ^40-19
