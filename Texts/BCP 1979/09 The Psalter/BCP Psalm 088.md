# Psalm 88

← [[BCP Psalm 087]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 089]] →

*Domine, Deus* · Book Three · Seventeenth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 088]]

**88:1** O LORD, my God, my Savior, \*  
by day and night I cry to you. ^88-1

**88:2** Let my prayer enter into your presence; \*  
incline your ear to my lamentation. ^88-2

**88:3** For I am full of trouble; \*  
my life is at the brink of the grave. ^88-3

**88:4** I am counted among those who go down to the Pit; \*  
I have become like one who has no strength; ^88-4

**88:5** Lost among the dead, \*  
like the slain who lie in the grave, ^88-5

**88:6** Whom you remember no more, \*  
for they are cut off from your hand. ^88-6

**88:7** You have laid me in the depths of the Pit, \*  
in dark places, and in the abyss. ^88-7

**88:8** Your anger weighs upon me heavily, \*  
and all your great waves overwhelm me. ^88-8

**88:9** You have put my friends far from me;  
you have made me to be abhorred by them; \*  
I am in prison and cannot get free. ^88-9

**88:10** My sight has failed me because of trouble; \*  
LORD, I have called upon you daily;  
I have stretched out my hands to you. ^88-10

**88:11** Do you work wonders for the dead? \*  
will those who have died stand up and give you thanks? ^88-11

**88:12** Will your loving-kindness be declared in the grave? \*  
your faithfulness in the land of destruction? ^88-12

[p. 713]

**88:13** Will your wonders be known in the dark? \*  
or your righteousness in the country where all is forgotten? ^88-13

**88:14** But as for me, O LORD, I cry to you for help; \*  
in the morning my prayer comes before you. ^88-14

**88:15** LORD, why have you rejected me? \*  
why have you hidden your face from me? ^88-15

**88:16** Ever since my youth, I have been wretched and at the point of death; \*  
I have borne your terrors with a troubled mind. ^88-16

**88:17** Your blazing anger has swept over me; \*  
your terrors have destroyed me; ^88-17

**88:18** They surround me all day long like a flood; \*  
they encompass me on every side. ^88-18

**88:19** My friend and my neighbor you have put away from me, \*  
and darkness is my only companion. ^88-19
