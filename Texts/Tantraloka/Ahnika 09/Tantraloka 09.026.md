## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 03 - Chapter Nine, part 1#^pg52|p. 35]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=52>))
>
> A sprout, just like the one (born from a seed), is generated after a yogi has willed it, because (a causal) necessity manifests with regards to that kind of form (that the yogi) wants it to be. (If you do not accept this example as valid, everybody knows) that a causal relationship between a jar and a cloth, for example, (may) manifest in a dream, in accord with (the same causal) necessity, without it being invalidated (bādhāśūnyena) as long as (the dream 12815). (with 9.26–27)
