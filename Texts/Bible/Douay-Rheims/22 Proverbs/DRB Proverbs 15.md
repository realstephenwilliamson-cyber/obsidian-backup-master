# Proverbs 15 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Proverbs 14|← Proverbs 14]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Proverbs 16|Proverbs 16 →]]

**15:1** A mild answer breaketh wrath: but a harsh word stirreth up fury. ^15-1

**15:2** The tongue of the wise adorneth knowledge: but the mouth of fools bubbleth out folly. ^15-2

**15:3** The eyes of the Lord in every place behold the good and the evil. ^15-3

**15:4** A peaceable tongue is a tree of life: but that which is immoderate, shall crush the spirit. ^15-4

**15:5** A fool laugheth at the instruction of his father: but he that regardeth reproofs shall become prudent. In abundant justice there is the greatest strength: but the devices of the wicked shall be rooted out. ^15-5

**15:6** The house of the just is very much strength: and in the fruits of the wicked is trouble. ^15-6

**15:7** The lips of the wise shall disperse knowledge: the heart of fools shall be unlike. ^15-7

**15:8** The victims of the wicked are abominable to the Lord: the vows of the just are acceptable. ^15-8

**15:9** The way of the wicked is an abomination to the Lord: he that followeth justice is beloved by him. ^15-9

**15:10** Instruction is grievous to him that forsaketh the way of life: he that hateth reproof shall die. ^15-10

**15:11** Hell and destruction are before the Lord: how much more the hearts of the children of men? ^15-11

**15:12** A corrupt man loveth not one that reproveth him: nor will he go to the wise. ^15-12

**15:13** A glad heart maketh a cheerful countenance: but by grief of mind the spirit is cast down. ^15-13

**15:14** The heart of the wise seeketh instruction: and the mouth of fools feedeth on foolishness. ^15-14

**15:15** All the days of the poor are evil: a secure mind is like a continual feast. ^15-15

**15:16** Better is a little with the fear of the Lord, than great treasures without content, ^15-16

**15:17** It is better to be invited to herbs with love, than to a fatted calf with hatred. ^15-17

**15:18** A passionate man stirreth up strifes: he that is patient appeaseth those that are stirred up. ^15-18

**15:19** The way of the slothful is as a hedge of thorns; the way of the just is without offence. ^15-19

**15:20** A wise son maketh a father joyful: but the foolish man despiseth his mother. ^15-20

**15:21** Folly is joy to the fool: and the wise man maketh straight his steps. ^15-21

**15:22** Designs are brought to nothing where there is no counsel: but where there are many counsellors, they are established. ^15-22

**15:23** A man rejoiceth in the sentence of his mouth: and a word in due time is best. ^15-23

**15:24** The path of life is above for the wise, that he may decline from the lowest hell. ^15-24

**15:25** The Lord will destroy the house of the proud: and will strengthen the borders of the widow. ^15-25

**15:26** Evil thoughts are an abomination to the Lord: and pure words most beautiful shall be confirmed by him. ^15-26

**15:27** He that is greedy of gain troubleth his own house: but he that hateth bribes shall live. By mercy and faith sins are purged away: and by the fear of the Lord every one declineth from evil. ^15-27

**15:28** The mind of the just studieth obedience: the mouth of the wicked over floweth with evils. ^15-28

**15:29** The Lord is far from the wicked: and he will hear the prayers of the just. ^15-29

**15:30** The light of the eyes rejoiceth the soul: a good name maketh the bones fat. ^15-30

**15:31** The ear that heareth the reproofs of life, shall abide in the midst of the wise. ^15-31

**15:32** He that rejecteth instruction, despiseth his own soul: but he that yieldeth to reproof possesseth understanding. ^15-32

**15:33** The fear of the Lord is the lesson of wisdom: and humility goeth before glory. ^15-33
