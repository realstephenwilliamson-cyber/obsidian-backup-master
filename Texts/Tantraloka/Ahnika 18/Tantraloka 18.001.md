## Mūla

अथ संक्षिप्तदीक्षेयं शिवतापत्तिदोच्यते ।
न रजो नाधिवासोऽत्र न भूक्षेत्रपरिग्रहः ।
यत्र तत्र प्रदेशे तु पूजयित्वा गुरुः शिवम् ॥ ^mula

atha saṃkṣiptadīkṣeyaṃ śivatāpattidocyate |
na rajo nādhivāso'tra na bhūkṣetraparigrahaḥ |
yatra tatra pradeśe tu pūjayitvā guruḥ śivam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 09 - Chapter Eighteen#^pg213|p. 193]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=213>))
>
> Coloured powder is not (required) here (to draw a mandala), nor the preliminary purification (adhivāsa) (prior to initiation) or the appropriation of the ground (on which to perform it). A teacher who has crossed over the reality levels (fattvapāraga) can initiate (his disciple) anywhere once he has worshipped Śiva, and meditated on the Path mentally (within himself). By whatever Path the teacher who is steeped (bhāvita) solely with a sense of identity with Śiva (śivabhāva) performs (this initiation, whatever be CHAPTER EIGHTEEN the number of) reality levels (it involves, starting with many) up to (just) one (ekatattvānta); it is devoid of such (purifying processes whereby the disciple is induced) to take birth (successively in higher world orders). (with 18.1–3ab)
