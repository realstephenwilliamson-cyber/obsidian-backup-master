# Proverbs 14

← [[Proverbs 13]] · [[Tanakh]] · [[Proverbs 15]] →

Compare: [[NRSVue Proverbs 14|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 14

**14:1** The wisest of women builds her house,  
But folly tears it down with its own hands. ^14-1

**14:2** He who maintains his integrity fears the LORD;  
A man of devious ways scorns Him. ^14-2

**14:3** In the mouth of a fool is a rod of haughtiness,  
But the lips of the wise protect them. ^14-3

**14:4** If there are no oxen the crib is clean,  
But a rich harvest comes through the strength of the ox. ^14-4

**14:5** An honest witness will not lie;  
A false witness testifies lies. ^14-5

**14:6** A scoffer seeks wisdom in vain,  
But knowledge comes easily to the intelligent man. ^14-6

**14:7** Keep your distance from a dullard,  
For you will not learn wise speech. ^14-7

**14:8** It is the wisdom of a clever man to understand his course;  
But the stupidity of the dullard is delusion. ^14-8

**14:9** Reparations mediate between fools,  
Between the upright, good will. ^14-9

**14:10** The heart alone knows its bitterness,  
And no outsider can share in its joy. ^14-10

**14:11** The house of the wicked will be demolished,  
But the tent of the upright will flourish. ^14-11

**14:12** A road may seem right to a man,  
But in the end it is a road to death. ^14-12

**14:13** The heart may ache even in laughter,  
And joy may end in grief. ^14-13

**14:14** An unprincipled man reaps the fruits of his ways;  
A good man, of his deeds.[^1] ^14-14

**14:15** A simple person believes anything;  
A clever man ponders his course. ^14-15

**14:16** A wise man is diffident and shuns evil,  
But a dullard rushes in confidently. ^14-16

**14:17** An impatient man commits folly;  
A man of intrigues will be hated. ^14-17

**14:18** Folly is the lot of the simple,  
But clever men glory in knowledge.[^2] ^14-18

**14:19** Evil men are brought low before the good,  
So are the wicked at the gates of the righteous. ^14-19

**14:20** A pauper is despised even by his peers,  
But a rich man has many friends. ^14-20

**14:21** He who despises his fellow is wrong;  
He who shows pity for the lowly is happy. ^14-21

**14:22** Surely those who plan evil go astray,  
While those who plan good earn steadfast love. ^14-22

**14:23** From all toil there is some gain,  
But idle chatter is pure loss. ^14-23

**14:24** The ornament of the wise is their wealth;  
The stupidity of dullards is stupidity. ^14-24

**14:25** A truthful witness saves lives;  
He who testifies lies [spreads] deceit. ^14-25

**14:26** Fear of the LORD is a stronghold,  
A refuge for a man’s children. ^14-26

**14:27** Fear of the LORD is a fountain of life,  
Enabling one to avoid deadly snares. ^14-27

**14:28** A numerous people is the glory of a king;  
Without a nation a ruler is ruined. ^14-28

**14:29** Patience results in much understanding;  
Impatience gets folly as its portion. ^14-29

**14:30** A calm disposition gives bodily health;  
Passion is rot to the bones. ^14-30

**14:31** He who withholds what is due to the poor affronts his Maker;  
He who shows pity for the needy honors Him. ^14-31

**14:32** The wicked man is felled by his own evil;  
The righteous man finds security in his death. ^14-32

**14:33** Wisdom rests quietly in the mind of a prudent man,  
But among dullards it makes itself known. ^14-33

**14:34** Righteousness exalts a nation;  
Sin is a reproach to any people. ^14-34

**14:35** The king favors a capable servant;  
He rages at an incompetent one. ^14-35

[^1]: Taking ‘al as from ‘ll; cf. Hos. 12.3.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
