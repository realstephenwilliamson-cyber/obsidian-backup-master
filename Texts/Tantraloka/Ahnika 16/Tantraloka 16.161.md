## Mūla

येन येन हि मन्त्रेण तन्त्रेऽस्मिन्नुद्भवः कृतः ।
तेनैव दीक्षयेन्मन्त्री इत्याज्ञा पारमेश्वरी ॥ ^mula

yena yena hi mantreṇa tantre'sminnudbhavaḥ kṛtaḥ |
tenaiva dīkṣayenmantrī ityājñā pārameśvarī || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 06 - Chapter Sixteen, part 2#^pg91|p. 71]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=91>))
>
> And, amongst the Trika scriptures (ṣaḍardhaśāstra), this is taught in the venerable Sāraśāstra (where we read): ‘the learned (teacher) should (bring about this) purification here by means of the eight, Aghora and the rest, or else with the Mantras (derived from) the monosyllabic (Vidyā of Parā) (ekākṣarā) (namely, those of Parāparā and Aparā), or by the sequence of (the letters of) the Mātṛkā (alphabet), or by Bhairava’s heart, or by the Heart of the Khecarīs (KHPHREṀ), or by Bhairava, O Great Goddess, (or) with the five faces and limbs. The Supreme Goddess (Parameśvarī) commands that (the teacher who) recites Mantra should initiate (his disciple) with whichever Mantra (taught) in this Tantra has made (the disciple) leap (upwards) (udbhava) (pierced by its energy).’ (with 16.158cd–161)
