# LXX Sirach 02

← [[LXX Sirach 01]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Sirach 03]] →

*Sirach · [[LXX]]*

**2:1** My son, if thou come to serve the Lord God, prepare thy soul for temptation.  ^2-1

**2:2** Set thy heart aright, and constantly endure, and make not haste in time of trouble.  ^2-2

**2:3** Cleave unto him, and depart not away, that thou mayest be increased at thy last end.  ^2-3

**2:4** Whatsoever is brought upon thee take cheerfully, and be patient when thou art changed to a low estate.  ^2-4

**2:5** For gold is tried in the fire, and acceptable men in the furnace of adversity.  ^2-5

**2:6** Believe in him, and he will help thee; order thy way aright, and trust in him.  ^2-6

**2:7** Ye that fear the Lord, wait for his mercy; and go not aside, lest ye fall.  ^2-7

**2:8** Ye that fear the Lord, believe him; and your reward shall not fail.  ^2-8

**2:9** Ye that fear the Lord, hope for good, and for everlasting joy and mercy.  ^2-9

**2:10** Look at the generations of old, and see; did ever any trust in the Lord, and was confounded? or did any abide in his fear, and was forsaken? or whom did he ever despise, that called upon him?  ^2-10

**2:11** For the Lord is full of compassion and mercy, longsuffering, and very pitiful, and forgiveth sins, and saveth in time of affliction.  ^2-11

**2:12** Woe be to fearful hearts, and faint hands, and the sinner that goeth two ways!  ^2-12

**2:13** Woe unto him that is fainthearted! for he believeth not; therefore shall he not be defended.  ^2-13

**2:14** Woe unto you that have lost patience! and what will ye do when the Lord shall visit you?  ^2-14

**2:15** They that fear the Lord will not disobey his word; and they that love him will keep his ways.  ^2-15

**2:16** They that fear the Lord will seek that which is wellpleasing unto him; and they that love him shall be filled with the law.  ^2-16

**2:17** They that fear the Lord will prepare their hearts, and humble their souls in his sight,  ^2-17

**2:18** *saying*, We will fall into the hands of the Lord, and not into the hands of men: for as his majesty is, so is his mercy.  ^2-18
