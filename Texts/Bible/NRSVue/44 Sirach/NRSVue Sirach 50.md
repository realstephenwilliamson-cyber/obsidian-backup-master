# Sirach 50 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Sirach 49|← Sirach 49]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Sirach 51|Sirach 51 →]]

## Simon son of Onias

**50:1** The leader of his brothers and the pride of his people^[Heb Syr: Gk lacks this line.] was the high priest, Simon son of Onias, who in his life repaired the house and in his time fortified the temple. ^50-1

**50:2** He laid the foundations for the high double walls, the high retaining walls for the temple enclosure. ^50-2

**50:3** In his days a water cistern was dug,^[Heb: Meaning of Gk uncertain] a reservoir like the sea in circumference. ^50-3

**50:4** He considered how to save his people from ruin and fortified the city against siege. ^50-4

**50:5** How glorious he was as he gazed from the tent,^[Heb: Gk *in his courses in the temple*] as he came out of the house of the curtain, ^50-5

**50:6** like the morning star among the clouds, like the full moon at the festal season,^[Heb: Meaning of Gk uncertain] ^50-6

**50:7** like the sun shining on the temple of the Most High, like the rainbow gleaming in splendid clouds, ^50-7

**50:8** like roses in the days of the spring harvest, like lilies by springs of water, like a green shoot on Lebanon on a summer day, ^50-8

**50:9** like fire and incense in the censer, like a vessel of hammered gold studded with all kinds of precious stones, ^50-9

**50:10** like an olive tree laden with fruit, and like a cypress towering in the clouds. ^50-10

**50:11** When he put on his glorious robe and clothed himself in perfect splendor, when he went up to the holy altar, he made the court of the sanctuary glorious. ^50-11

**50:12** When he received the portions from the hands of the priests, as he stood by the hearth of the altar with a garland of brothers around him, he was like a young cedar on Lebanon surrounded by the trunks of palm trees. ^50-12

**50:13** All the sons of Aaron in their splendor held the Lord’s offering in their hands before the whole congregation of Israel. ^50-13

**50:14** Finishing the service at the altars^[Other ancient authorities read *altar*] and arranging the offering to the Most High, the Almighty, ^50-14

**50:15** he held out his hand for the cup and poured a drink offering of the blood of the grape; he poured it out at the foot of the altar, a pleasing odor to the Most High, the king of all. ^50-15

**50:16** Then the sons of Aaron shouted; they blew their trumpets of hammered metal; they sounded a mighty fanfare as a reminder before the Most High. ^50-16

**50:17** Then all the people together quickly fell to the ground on their faces to worship their Lord, the Almighty, God Most High. ^50-17

**50:18** Then the singers praised him with their voices in sweet and full-toned melody.^[Other ancient authorities read *in sweet melody throughout the house*] ^50-18

**50:19** And the people of the Lord Most High offered their prayer before the Merciful One, until the order of worship of the Lord was ended and they completed his ritual. ^50-19

**50:20** Then Simon^[Gk *he*] came down and raised his hands over the whole congregation of Israelites, to pronounce the blessing of the Lord with his lips and to glory in his name, ^50-20

**50:21** and they bowed down in worship a second time to receive the blessing from the Most High. ^50-21

## A benediction

**50:22** And now bless the God of all, who everywhere works great wonders, who exalts our days from birth^[Heb *who nurtures humankind from the womb*] and deals with us according to his mercy. ^50-22

**50:23** May he give us^[Other ancient authorities read *you*] gladness of heart, and may there be peace in our^[Other ancient authorities read *your*] days in Israel, as in the days of old. ^50-23

**50:24** May he entrust to us his mercy, and may he deliver us in our^[Other ancient authorities read *his*] days! ^50-24

## Epilogue

**50:25** Two nations my soul detests, and the third is not even a nation: ^50-25

**50:26** those who live in Seir^[Heb Compare Lat: Gk *on the mountain of Samaria*] and the Philistines and the foolish people who live in Shechem. ^50-26

**50:27** Instruction in understanding and knowledge I have written in this book, Jesus son of Eleazar son of Sirach^[Heb: Meaning of Gk uncertain] of Jerusalem, whose mind poured forth wisdom. ^50-27

**50:28** Happy are those who concern themselves with these things, and those who lay them to heart will become wise. ^50-28

**50:29** For if they put them into practice, they will be equal to anything, for the fear^[Heb: Other ancient authorities read *light*] of the Lord is their path.^[Other ancient authorities add *And to the pious he gave wisdom. Blessed be the Lord forever. May it be; may it be.*] ^50-29
