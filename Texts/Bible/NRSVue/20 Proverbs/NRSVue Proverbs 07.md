## The false attractions of adultery

**7:1** My child, keep my words and store up my commandments with you; ^7-1

**7:2** keep my commandments and live; keep my teachings as the apple^[Heb *little man*] of your eye; ^7-2

**7:3** bind them on your fingers; write them on the tablet of your heart. ^7-3

**7:4** Say to wisdom, “You are my sister,” and call insight your intimate friend, ^7-4

**7:5** that they may keep you from the loose woman,^[Heb *strange woman*] from the adulteress^[Heb *alien woman*] with her smooth words. ^7-5

**7:6** For at the window of my house I looked out through my lattice, ^7-6

**7:7** and I saw among the simple ones, I observed among the youths, a young man without sense, ^7-7

**7:8** passing along the street near her corner, taking the road to her house ^7-8

**7:9** in the twilight, in the evening, at the time of night and darkness. ^7-9

**7:10** Then a woman comes toward him decked out like a prostitute, with hidden intent. ^7-10

**7:11** She is loud and wayward; her feet do not stay at home; ^7-11

**7:12** now in the street, now in the squares, and at every corner she lies in wait. ^7-12

**7:13** She seizes him and kisses him, and with impudent face she says to him: ^7-13

**7:14** “I had to offer sacrifices, and today I have paid my vows; ^7-14

**7:15** so now I have come out to meet you, to seek you eagerly, and I have found you! ^7-15

**7:16** I have decked my couch with coverings, colored spreads of Egyptian linen; ^7-16

**7:17** I have perfumed my bed with myrrh, aloes, and cinnamon. ^7-17

**7:18** Come, let us take our fill of love until morning; let us delight ourselves with love. ^7-18

**7:19** For my husband is not at home; he has gone on a long journey. ^7-19

**7:20** He took a bag of money with him; he will not come home until full moon.” ^7-20

**7:21** With much seductive speech she persuades him; with her smooth talk she compels him. ^7-21

**7:22** Right away he follows her and goes like an ox to the slaughter or bounds like a stag toward the trap^[Cn Compare Gk: Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^7-22

**7:23** until an arrow pierces its entrails. He is like a bird rushing into a snare, not knowing that it will cost him his life. ^7-23

**7:24** And now, my children, listen to me, and be attentive to the words of my mouth. ^7-24

**7:25** Do not let your hearts turn aside to her ways; do not stray into her paths. ^7-25

**7:26** For many are those she has laid low, and numerous are her victims. ^7-26

**7:27** Her house is the way to Sheol, going down to the chambers of death. ^7-27
