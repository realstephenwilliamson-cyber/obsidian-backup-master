# LXX Psalms 85

← [[LXX Psalms 84]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 86]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 086]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 086]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**85:1** A Prayer of David. O Lord, incline thine ear, and hearken to me; for I am poor and needy.  ^85-1

**85:2** Preserve my soul, for I am holy; save thy servant, O God, who hopes in thee.  ^85-2

**85:3** Pity me, O Lord: for to thee will I cry all the day.  ^85-3

**85:4** Rejoice the soul of thy servant: for to thee, O Lord, have I lifted up my soul.  ^85-4

**85:5** For thou, O Lord, art kind, and gentle; and plenteous in mercy to all that call upon thee.  ^85-5

**85:6** Give ear to my prayer, O Lord; and attend to the voice of my supplication.  ^85-6

**85:7** In the day of my trouble I cried to thee: for thou didst hear me.  ^85-7

**85:8** There is none like to thee, O Lord, among the gods; and there are no *works* like to thy works.  ^85-8

**85:9** All nations whom thou hast made shall come, and shall worship before thee, O Lord; and shall glorify thy name.  ^85-9

**85:10** For thou art great, and doest wonders: thou art the only *and* the great God.  ^85-10

**85:11** Guide me, O Lord, in thy way, and I will walk in thy truth: let my heart rejoice, that I may fear thy name.  ^85-11

**85:12** I will give thee thanks, O Lord my God, with all my heart; and I will glorify thy name for ever.  ^85-12

**85:13** For thy mercy is great toward me; and thou hast delivered my soul from the lowest hell.  ^85-13

**85:14** O God, transgressors have risen up against me, and an assembly of violent *men* have sought my life; and have not set thee before them.  ^85-14

**85:15** But thou, O Lord God, art compassionate and merciful, long-suffering, and abundant in mercy and true.  ^85-15

**85:16** Look thou upon me, and have mercy upon me: give thy strength to thy servant, and save the son of thine handmaid.  ^85-16

**85:17** Establish with me a token for good; and let them that hate me see *it* and be ashamed; because thou, O Lord, hast helped me, and comforted me.  ^85-17
