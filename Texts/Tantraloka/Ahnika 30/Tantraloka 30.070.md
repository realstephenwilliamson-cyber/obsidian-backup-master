## Mūla

रविसोमवह्निसङ्घट्टबिन्दुदेहो हहह समुत्क्राम ।
तृतीयमार्यावाक्यं प्राक्संख्यैरेकाधिकैः पदैः ॥ ^mula

ravisomavahnisaṅghaṭṭabindudeho hahaha samutkrāma |
tṛtīyamāryāvākyaṃ prāksaṃkhyairekādhikaiḥ padaiḥ || ^iast

> 3) “O Haṁsa! Hayagrīva! Pervasive Lord! You are Sadāśiva! You are the Supreme, called the individual soul (jīva). Your body is the Point, which is the union of Sun, Moon, and Fire. Ha! Ha! Ha! Come forth (from the body)!” (with 30.69cd–70ab)
>
> This is the third declaration, set in Ārya metre, made of one more word than the number of the previous one. (with 30.70cd)
