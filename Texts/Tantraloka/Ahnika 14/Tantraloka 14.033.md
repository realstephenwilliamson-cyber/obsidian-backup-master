## Mūla

प्रोक्ता सा सारशास्त्रेषु भोगोपायतयोदिता ।
यदि सर्वगतो देवो वदोत्क्रम्य क्व यास्यति ॥ ^mula

proktā sā sāraśāstreṣu bhogopāyatayoditā |
yadi sarvagato devo vadotkramya kva yāsyati || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 12 - Chapter Fourteen, and Appendices to Chapters Eleven and Twelve#^pg382|p. 360]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=382>))
>
> Quitting the body voluntarily (utkrānti), which is said with great respect everywhere (by all) to serve as a means to liberation is, according to the Sāra scriptures (we follow), (merely) a means to obtaining worldly benefits (in the next life). (with 14.32cd–33ab)
>
> (The Ūrmikaulārnavatantra declares): ‘If God is omnipresent, (pray) tell (me) — where will He go once (he has) quit (the body)? And if He is not omnipresent, He would then be like (any common object such as) a jar. This yogic practice of (voluntarily) leaving (the body) is said to take place part by part (ekadeśena). How can (any such) exiting reasonably apply to Śiva, who is devoid of parts?’ (with 14.33cd–35ab)
