## Mūla

भवेत्कोऽपि तिरोभूतः पुनरुन्मुखितोऽपि सन् ।
विनापि दैशिकात्प्राग्वत्स्वयमेव विमुच्यते ॥ ^mula

bhavetko'pi tirobhūtaḥ punarunmukhito'pi san |
vināpi daiśikātprāgvatsvayameva vimucyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 14 - Chapter Twenty-Three#^pg356|p. 336]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=356>))
>
> As (was said) before, it is possible that someone who was (previously) obscured may, once (he is) propense again (to seek realisation), liberate (himself) by himself alone, without a teacher.
