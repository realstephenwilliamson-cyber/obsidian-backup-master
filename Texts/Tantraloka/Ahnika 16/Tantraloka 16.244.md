## Mūla

यथामलं मनो दूरस्थितमप्याशु पश्यति ।
तथा प्रत्ययदीक्षायां तत्तद्भुवनदर्शनम् ॥ ^mula

yathāmalaṃ mano dūrasthitamapyāśu paśyati |
tathā pratyayadīkṣāyāṃ tattadbhuvanadarśanam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 07 - Chapter Sixteen, part 3#^pg127|p. 107]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=127>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 16.243cd–244ab.
>
> Just as a (completely) pure (clear) mind quickly (and effortlessly) sees a distant (object), in the same way (the initiand has) a vision of each world in the course of the initiation of firm conviction (pratyayadīkṣā).
