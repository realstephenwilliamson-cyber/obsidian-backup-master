## Mūla

तारुण्यसागरतरङ्गभरानपोह्य
वैराग्यपोतमधिरुह्य दृढं हठेन ।
यो भक्तिरोहणमवाप्य महेशचिन्ता-
रत्नैरलं दलयति स्म भवापदस्ताः ॥ ^mula

tāruṇyasāgarataraṅgabharānapohya
vairāgyapotamadhiruhya dṛḍhaṃ haṭhena |
yo bhaktirohaṇamavāpya maheśacintā-
ratnairalaṃ dalayati sma bhavāpadastāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 11 - Chapter Thirty-Seven, part 1#^pg270|p. 252]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=270>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 37.56.
>
> Once he had set aside the burdens of the oceanic waves (of his stormy) youth, he mounted with vigour (haṭhena) on the firm boat of detachment, and then, having attained the (full) development of (his) devotion, thanks to the (precious) gems of (his) meditation on Maheśa, totally destroyed the calamities of (worldly) existence.
