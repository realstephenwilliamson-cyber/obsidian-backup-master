## Mūla

ततोऽपि देहारम्भीणि तत्त्वानि परिशोधयेत् ।
शिवात्मकेष्वप्येतेषु बुद्धिर्या व्यतिरेकिणी ॥ ^mula

tato'pi dehārambhīṇi tattvāni pariśodhayet |
śivātmakeṣvapyeteṣu buddhiryā vyatirekiṇī || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. III
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 03 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v03 05 - Chapter Four, part 4#^pg150|p. 137]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 03.pdf#page=150>))
>
> D) He who has performed the prescribed ablution in this way and offered libations (tarpita) to the deities of bliss (that govern the senses) should then proceed to also purify the metaphysical principles (tattva) that constitute the body. (with 4.117cd–118ab)
>
> E) The supreme form of impurity (aśuddhi) is said to be the (persistent) notion (buddhi) of separation (from Śiva that prevails) within (the experience of) these (metaphysical principles deployed in the body, although they are in fact) Śiva by nature. Purity is the obliteration of that notion. (with 4.118cd–119ab)
