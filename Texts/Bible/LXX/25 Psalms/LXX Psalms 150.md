# LXX Psalms 150

← [[LXX Psalms 149]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 151]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 150]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 150]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**150:1** Alleluia. Praise God in his holy places: praise him in the firmament of his power.  ^150-1

**150:2** Praise him on *account of* his mighty acts: praise him according to his abundant greatness.  ^150-2

**150:3** Praise him with the sound of a trumpet: praise him with psaltery and harp.  ^150-3

**150:4** Praise him with timbrel and dance: praise him with stringed instruments and the organ.  ^150-4

**150:5** Praise him with melodious cymbals: praise him with loud cymbals.  ^150-5

**150:6** Let every thing that has breath praise the Lord.  ^150-6
