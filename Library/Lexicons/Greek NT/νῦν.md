# νῦν

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · adverb

**Dodson:** now, already, at present — adv. (a) of time: just now, even now; just at hand, immediately, (b) of logical connection: now then, (c) in commands and appeals: at this instant. (Strong's 3568; headword νῦν)
