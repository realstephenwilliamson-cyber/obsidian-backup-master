# Hosea 6 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Hosea 05|← Hosea 5]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Hosea 07|Hosea 7 →]]

## A call to repentance

**6:1** “Come, let us return to the LORD, for it is he who has torn, and he will heal us; he has struck down, and he will bind us up. ^6-1

**6:2** After two days he will revive us; on the third day he will raise us up, that we may live before him. ^6-2

**6:3** Let us know, let us press on to know the LORD; his appearing is as sure as the dawn; he will come to us like the showers, like the spring rains that water the earth.” ^6-3

## Impenitence of Israel and Judah

**6:4** What shall I do with you, O Ephraim? What shall I do with you, O Judah? Your love is like a morning cloud, like the dew that goes away early. ^6-4

**6:5** Therefore I have hewn them by the prophets; I have killed them by the words of my mouth, and my^[Gk Syr: Heb *your*] judgment goes forth as the light. ^6-5

**6:6** For I desire steadfast love and not sacrifice, the knowledge of God rather than burnt offerings. ^6-6

**6:7** But at^[Cn: Heb *like*] Adam they transgressed the covenant; there they dealt faithlessly with me. ^6-7

**6:8** Gilead is a city of evildoers, tracked with blood. ^6-8

**6:9** As robbers lie in wait^[Cn: Meaning of Heb uncertain] for someone, so the priests are banded together;^[Syr: Heb *are a company*] they murder on the road to Shechem; they commit a monstrous crime. ^6-9

**6:10** In the house of Israel I have seen a horrible thing; Ephraim’s prostitution is there; Israel is defiled. ^6-10

**6:11** For you also, O Judah, a harvest is appointed. When I would restore the fortunes of my people, ^6-11
