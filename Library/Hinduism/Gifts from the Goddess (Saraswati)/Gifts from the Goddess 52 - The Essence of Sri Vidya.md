### The Essence of Sri Vidya

A fragment from the early 1990s.

The essence of Sri Vidya lies in:

What you may call *ascension*—going up;

1.	

*Transcendence*—looking beyond what is apparent; and

2.	

Most importantly, *transformation*.

3.	

These are, in fact, the three primal characteristics of spirituality: you try to ascend from the lower to the higher.

B Ego breaks down when we:

Understand the nature of the senses;

1.	

Understand the need to let go of our attachment to the senses;

2.	

Train ourselves to work free of our filters and limitations; and

3.	

Finally, let go of our sensory limits by suspending judgments.

4.	

All these are the objects of Goddess worship, and the Tantra of Lalitā.
