# LXX Sirach 33

← [[LXX Sirach 32]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Sirach 34]] →

*Sirach · [[LXX]]*

**33:1** Have mercy upon us, O Lord God of all, and behold us:  ^33-1

**33:2** and send thy fear upon all the nations that seek not after thee.  ^33-2

**33:3** Lift up thy hand against the strange nations, and let them see thy power.  ^33-3

**33:4** As thou wast sanctified in us before them: so be thou magnified among them before us.  ^33-4

**33:5** And let them know thee, as we have known thee, that there is no God but only thou, O God.  ^33-5

**33:6** Shew new signs, and make other strange wonders: glorify thy hand and thy right arm, that they may set forth thy wondrous works.  ^33-6

**33:7** Raise up indignation, and pour out wrath: take away the adversary, and destroy the enemy.  ^33-7

**33:8** Make the time short, remember the covenant, and let them declare thy wonderful works.  ^33-8

**33:9** Let him that escapeth be consumed by the rage of the fire; and let them perish that oppress the people.  ^33-9

**33:10** Smite in sunder the heads of the rulers of the heathen, that say, There is none other but we.  ^33-10

**33:11** Gather all the tribes of Jacob together.  ^33-11

**33:12** A cheerful and good heart will have a care of his meat and diet.  ^33-12
