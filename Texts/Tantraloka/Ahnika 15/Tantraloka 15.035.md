## Mūla

उचितां संविधित्सुस्तां वासनां तादृशीं श्रयेत् ।
आयातशक्तिपातस्य दीक्षां प्रति न दैशिकः ॥ ^mula

ucitāṃ saṃvidhitsustāṃ vāsanāṃ tādṛśīṃ śrayet |
āyātaśaktipātasya dīkṣāṃ prati na daiśikaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 02 - Chapter Fifteen, part 1#^pg48|p. 30]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=48>))
>
> In this way, after having personally questioned (the disciple), known (his desires) and deliberated (accordingly), the teacher, wishing to impart the appropriate rite (of initiation), should, (first of all), assume the mental disposition (vāsanā) which is such (as accords with it). (with 15.34cd–35ab)
>
> The teacher should not disdain (the disciple) who has received a descent of the power (of grace and be averse to) initiating him. This is how Śambhu(nātha) has explained (Śiva’s) command. (with 15.35cd–36ab)
