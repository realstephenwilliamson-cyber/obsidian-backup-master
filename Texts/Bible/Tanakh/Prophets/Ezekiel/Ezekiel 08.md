## Chapter 8

**8:1** In the sixth year, on the fifth day of the sixth month, I was sitting at home, and the elders of Judah were sitting before me, and there the hand of the Lord GOD fell upon me. ^8-1

**8:2** As I looked, there was a figure that had the appearance of fire:[^1] from what appeared as his loins down, [he was] fire; and from his loins up, his appearance was resplendent and had the color of amber. ^8-2

**8:3** He stretched out the form of a hand, and took me by the hair of my head. A spirit lifted me up between heaven and earth and brought me in visions of God to Jerusalem, to the entrance of the Penimith[^2] Gate that faces north; that was the site of the infuriating image that provokes fury. ^8-3

**8:4** And the Presence of the God of Israel appeared there, like the vision that I had seen in the valley.[^3] ^8-4

**8:5** And He said to me, “O mortal, turn your eyes northward.” I turned my eyes northward, and there, north of the gate of the altar, was[^4] that infuriating image on the approach.[^5] ^8-5

**8:6** And He said to me, “Mortal, do you see what they are doing, the terrible abominations that the House of Israel is practicing here, to drive Me far[^6] from My Sanctuary? You shall yet see even greater abominations!” ^8-6

**8:7** Then He brought me to the entrance of the court;[^7] and I looked, and there was a hole in the wall. ^8-7

**8:8** He said to me, “Mortal, break through the wall”; so I broke through the wall and found an entrance. ^8-8

**8:9** And He said to me, “Enter and see the vile abominations that they are practicing here.” ^8-9

**8:10** I entered and looked, and there all detestable forms of creeping things and beasts and all the fetishes of the House of Israel were depicted over the entire wall. ^8-10

**8:11** Before them stood seventy men, elders of the House of Israel, with Jaazaniah son of Shaphan standing in their midst. Everyone had a censer in his hand, and a thick cloud of incense smoke ascended. ^8-11

**8:12** Again He spoke to me, “O mortal, have you seen what the elders of the House of Israel are doing in the darkness, everyone in his image-covered chamber? For they say, ‘The LORD does not see us; the LORD has abandoned the country.’” ^8-12

**8:13** And He said to me, “You shall see even more terrible abominations which they practice.” ^8-13

**8:14** Next He brought me to the entrance of the north gate of the House of the LORD;[^8] and there sat the women bewailing Tammuz.[^9] ^8-14

**8:15** He said to me, “Have you seen, O mortal? You shall see even more terrible abominations than these.” ^8-15

**8:16** Then He brought me into the inner court of the House of the LORD, and there, at the entrance to the Temple of the LORD, between the portico and the altar, were about twenty-five men, their backs to the Temple of the LORD and their faces to the east; they were bowing low to the sun in the east. ^8-16

**8:17** And He said to me, “Do you see, O mortal? Is it not enough for the House of Judah to practice the abominations that they have committed here, that they must fill the country with lawlessness and provoke Me still further and thrust the branch to their nostrils?[^10] ^8-17

**8:18** I in turn will act with fury, I will show no pity or compassion; though they cry aloud to Me, I will not listen to them.” ^8-18

[^1]: Septuagint “a man.”
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: See chap. 1 and 3.22–23.
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain; emendation yields “north of the gate was the altar of.”
[^5]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^6]: Or “at a distance.”
[^7]: I.e., the outer court of the Temple.
[^8]: I.e., the gate of the inner court.
[^9]: A Babylonian god.
[^10]: Apparently meaning “goad Me to fury”; “their” is a euphemism for “My.”
