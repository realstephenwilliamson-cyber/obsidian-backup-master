## Mūla

स इत्यन्तो ग्रन्थ एष द्वितीयविषयः स्फुटः ।
अन्यस्तु मन्दतीव्राख्यशक्तिपातविधिं प्रति ॥ ^mula

sa ityanto grantha eṣa dvitīyaviṣayaḥ sphuṭaḥ |
anyastu mandatīvrākhyaśaktipātavidhiṃ prati || ^iast

## Translation

> This is the final verse (of the section) concerning the second subject (namely, the descent of the power (of grace) that is moderately intense), which is (now) clear. The other (verses that follow) concern the teaching regarding the descent of the power (of grace) that is mildly intense.
