## Mūla

प्रत्यात्मभिन्नमेवैतत् सुखदुःखादिभेदतः ।
एकस्यामेव जगति भोगसाधनसंहतौ ॥ ^mula

pratyātmabhinnamevaitat sukhaduḥkhādibhedataḥ |
ekasyāmeva jagati bhogasādhanasaṃhatau || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 06 - Chapter Nine, part 4#^pg162|p. 145]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=162>))
>
> (The group of principles ranging) from the Force (of limited agency) up to Earth, that is within Māyā and will be described (further ahead), differs for each individual soul. (This is clear from the fact) that, for example, the joy and suffering (each one experiences are) different. (with 9.167cd–168ab)
>
> If the universe were to be a single aggregate of means to worldly experience (bhoga), (the experience of) pleasure and the like would be the same for all (souls). How then could one account for the differences (between each individual’s) experiences? (with 9.168cd–169ab)

*Lead-in:* ननु कलादीनां स्वरूपाभेदेऽपि सुखदुःखादिभेदे किं हीयेत ? -- इत्याशङ्क्याह --

> एकस्यामेव जगति भोगसाधनसंहतौ ॥
> सुखादीनां समं व्यक्तेर्भोगभेदः कुतो भवेत् ।

जगतीति -- जनिमरणधर्मिणि अनपवर्गे -- इत्यर्थः । भोगसाधनसंहतावितिकलादिक्षित्यन्तायाम् । सममिति -- युगपत्साधारणतयेति यावत् । इह कारणभेदात् कार्यभेदः इति तावदविवादः । तच्च प्रत्यात्मं कलादिवसुधान्तं भोगसाधनम् चेदभिन्नं तत् कुतस्त्योऽयं सुखादिभोगस्य भेदः इति ॥ १६८ ॥
