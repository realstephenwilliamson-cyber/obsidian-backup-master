# Psalm 78

← [[BCP Psalm 077]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 079]] →

** · Book Three · Fifteenth Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 078]]

**Part I** Attendite, popule

**78:1** Hear my teaching, O my people; \*  
incline your ears to the words of my mouth. ^78-1

**78:2** I will open my mouth in a parable; \*  
I will declare the mysteries of ancient times. ^78-2

[p. 695] ^pg695

**78:3** That which we have heard and known,  
and what our forefathers have told us, \*  
we will not hide from their children. ^78-3

**78:4** We will recount to generations to come  
the praiseworthy deeds and the power of the LORD, \*  
and the wonderful works he has done. ^78-4

**78:5** He gave his decrees to Jacob  
and established a law for Israel, \*  
which he commanded them to teach their children; ^78-5

**78:6** That the generations to come might know,  
and the children yet unborn; \*  
that they in their turn might tell it to their children; ^78-6

**78:7** So that they might put their trust in God, \*  
and not forget the deeds of God,  
but keep his commandments; ^78-7

**78:8** And not be like their forefathers,  
a stubborn and rebellious generation, \*  
a generation whose heart was not steadfast,  
and whose spirit was not faithful to God. ^78-8

**78:9** The people of Ephraim, armed with the bow, \*  
turned back in the day of battle; ^78-9

**78:10** They did not keep the covenant of God, \*  
and refused to walk in his law; ^78-10

**78:11** They forgot what he had done, \*  
and the wonders he had shown them. ^78-11

**78:12** He worked marvels in the sight of their forefathers, \*  
in the land of Egypt, in the field of Zoan. ^78-12

**78:13** He split open the sea and let them pass through; \*  
he made the waters stand up like walls. ^78-13

[p. 696] ^pg696

**78:14** He led them with a cloud by day, \*  
and all the night through with a glow of fire. ^78-14

**78:15** He split the hard rocks in the wilderness \*  
and gave them drink as from the great deep. ^78-15

**78:16** He brought streams out of the cliff, \*  
and the waters gushed out like rivers. ^78-16

**78:17** But they went on sinning against him, \*  
rebelling in the desert against the Most High. ^78-17

**78:18** They tested God in their hearts, \*  
demanding food for their craving. ^78-18

**78:19** They railed against God and said, \*  
"Can God set a table in the wilderness? ^78-19

**78:20** True, he struck the rock, the waters gushed out, and the gullies overflowed; \*  
but is he able to give bread  
or to provide meat for his people?" ^78-20

**78:21** When the LORD heard this, he was full of wrath; \*  
a fire was kindled against Jacob,  
and his anger mounted against Israel; ^78-21

**78:22** For they had no faith in God, \*  
nor did they put their trust in his saving power. ^78-22

**78:23** So he commanded the clouds above \*  
and opened the doors of heaven. ^78-23

**78:24** He rained down manna upon them to eat \*  
and gave them grain from heaven. ^78-24

**78:25** So mortals ate the bread of angels; \*  
he provided for them food enough. ^78-25

[p. 697] ^pg697

**78:26** He caused the east wind to blow in the heavens \*  
and led out the south wind by his might. ^78-26

**78:27** He rained down flesh upon them like dust \*  
and wingèd birds like the sand of the sea. ^78-27

**78:28** He let it fall in the midst of their camp \*  
and round about their dwellings. ^78-28

**78:29** So they ate and were well filled, \*  
for he gave them what they craved. ^78-29

**78:30** But they did not stop their craving, \*  
though the food was still in their mouths. ^78-30

**78:31** So God's anger mounted against them; \*  
he slew their strongest men  
and laid low the youth of Israel. ^78-31

**78:32** In spite of all this, they went on sinning \*  
and had no faith in his wonderful works. ^78-32

**78:33** So he brought their days to an end like a breath \*  
and their years in sudden terror. ^78-33

**78:34** Whenever he slew them, they would seek him, \*  
and repent, and diligently search for God. ^78-34

**78:35** They would remember that God was their rock, \*  
and the Most High God their redeemer. ^78-35

**78:36** But they flattered him with their mouths \*  
and lied to him with their tongues. ^78-36

**78:37** Their heart was not steadfast toward him, \*  
and they were not faithful to his covenant. ^78-37

[p. 698] ^pg698

**78:38** But he was so merciful that he forgave their sins  
and did not destroy them; \*  
many times he held back his anger  
and did not permit his wrath to be roused. ^78-38

**78:39** For he remembered that they were but flesh, \*  
a breath that goes forth and does not return. ^78-39

**Psalm 78: Part II** Quoties exacerbaverunt

**78:40** How often the people disobeyed him in the wilderness \*  
and offended him in the desert! ^78-40

**78:41** Again and again they tempted God \*  
and provoked the Holy One of Israel. ^78-41

**78:42** They did not remember his power \*  
in the day when he ransomed them from the enemy; ^78-42

**78:43** How he wrought his signs in Egypt \*  
and his omens in the field of Zoan. ^78-43

**78:44** He turned their rivers into blood, \*  
so that they could not drink of their streams. ^78-44

**78:45** He sent swarms of flies among them, which ate them up, \*  
and frogs, which destroyed them. ^78-45

**78:46** He gave their crops to the caterpillar, \*  
the fruit of their toil to the locust. ^78-46

**78:47** He killed their vines with hail \*  
and their sycamores with frost. ^78-47

**78:48** He delivered their cattle to hailstones \*  
and their livestock to hot thunderbolts. ^78-48

[p. 699] ^pg699

**78:49** He poured out upon them his blazing anger: \*  
fury, indignation, and distress,  
a troop of destroying angels. ^78-49

**78:50** He gave full rein to his anger;  
he did not spare their souls from death; \*  
but delivered their lives to the plague. ^78-50

**78:51** He struck down all the firstborn of Egypt, \*  
the flower of manhood in the dwellings of Ham. ^78-51

**78:52** He led out his people like sheep \*  
and guided them in the wilderness like a flock. ^78-52

**78:53** He led them to safety, and they were not afraid; \*  
but the sea overwhelmed their enemies. ^78-53

**78:54** He brought them to his holy land, \*  
the mountain his right hand had won. ^78-54

**78:55** He drove out the Canaanites before them  
and apportioned an inheritance to them by lot; \*  
he made the tribes of Israel to dwell in their tents. ^78-55

**78:56** But they tested the Most High God, and defied him, \*  
and did not keep his commandments. ^78-56

**78:57** They turned away and were disloyal like their fathers; \*  
they were undependable like a warped bow. ^78-57

**78:58** They grieved him with their hill-altars \*  
they provoked his displeasure with their idols. ^78-58

**78:59** When God heard this, he was angry \*  
and utterly rejected Israel. ^78-59

**78:60** He forsook the shrine at Shiloh, \*  
the tabernacle where he had lived among his people. ^78-60

[p. 700] ^pg700

**78:61** He delivered the ark into captivity, \*  
his glory into the adversary's hand. ^78-61

**78:62** He gave his people to the sword \*  
and was angered against his inheritance. ^78-62

**78:63** The fire consumed their young men; \*  
there were no wedding songs for their maidens. ^78-63

**78:64** Their priests fell by the sword, \*  
and their widows made no lamentation. ^78-64

**78:65** Then the LORD woke as though from sleep, \*  
like a warrior refreshed with wine. ^78-65

**78:66** He struck his enemies on the backside \*  
and put them to perpetual shame. ^78-66

**78:67** He rejected the tent of Joseph \*  
and did not choose the tribe of Ephraim; ^78-67

**78:68** He chose instead the tribe of Judah \*  
and Mount Zion, which he loved. ^78-68

**78:69** He built his sanctuary like the heights of heaven, \*  
like the earth which he founded for ever. ^78-69

**78:70** He chose David his servant, \*  
and took him away from the sheepfolds. ^78-70

**78:71** He brought him from following the ewes, \*  
to be a shepherd over Jacob his people  
and over Israel his inheritance. ^78-71

**78:72** So he shepherded them with a faithful and true heart \*  
and guided them with the skillfulness of his hands. ^78-72
