## Mūla

अदृष्टमण्डलोऽप्येवं यः कश्चिद्वेत्ति तत्त्वतः ।
स सिद्धिभाग्भवेन्नित्यं स योगी स च दीक्षितः ॥ ^mula

adṛṣṭamaṇḍalo'pyevaṃ yaḥ kaścidvetti tattvataḥ |
sa siddhibhāgbhavennityaṃ sa yogī sa ca dīkṣitaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. III
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 03 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v03 03 - Chapter Four, part 2#^pg78|p. 65]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 03.pdf#page=78>))
>
> It is said in the (Parā)triṁśikā that: ‘Whoever knows (this reality) as it truly is (tattvataḥ) is always successful (siddhibhāk), even though he has not (been initiated and so has not) seen the maṇḍala. He indeed is a (true) yogi and initiate.’ ‘He who knows (reality) thus as it truly is has the initiation that leads to Nirvāṇa!’ (with 4.49–50)
