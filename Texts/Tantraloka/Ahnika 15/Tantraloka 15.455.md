## Mūla

प्रतिमादि च पश्यन्तो विदुः संनिध्यसंनिधी ।
न्यस्तमन्त्रांशुसुभगात्किञ्चिद्भूतादिमुद्रिताः ॥ ^mula

pratimādi ca paśyanto viduḥ saṃnidhyasaṃnidhī |
nyastamantrāṃśusubhagātkiñcidbhūtādimudritāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 08 - Chapter Fifteen, part 7#^pg297|p. 279]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=297>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 15.457cd–458ab.
>
> Those whose insight is developed (krtaprajñā), observing a body, water, a place, an icon and the like onto which Mantras have been deposited, know (immediately) whether (the Mantra deity is) present or not. (with 15.454cd–455ab)
>
> (But) those who are afflicted (mudrita) by some ghosts or the like are gripped, as it were, by fear, because (the teacher) is beautiful, (shining radiantly) with the rays of the Mantras deposited (on his body). Thus, one can perceive their presence or absence very well by means of the senses assisted (by a descent of the power of grace). (with 15.455cd–456ab)

*Lead-in:* एवं स्वसंवेदनमपि अपह्नुवानान्प्रति आह--

> न्यस्तमन्त्रांशुसुभगात्किञ्चिद्भूतादिमुद्रिताः ॥
> त्रस्यन्तीवेति तत्तच्चिदक्षैस्तत्सहकारिभिः ।

लोके हि मन्त्रन्यासाविष्कृततेजः सौभाग्यादाचार्यादेर्भूतादिमुद्रिता जन्तवस्त्रस्यन्त इव दृश्यन्ते इति । एवं तेन शक्तिपातादिना सहकृतैरिन्द्रियैस्तस्य तस्य संनिधानासंनिधानादेश्चित् चेतनवदेवं भवेत्--इत्यर्थः । इयता च मन्त्रावेशलक्षणः पार्यन्तिकसंस्कार उक्तः ॥
