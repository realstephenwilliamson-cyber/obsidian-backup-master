## Mūla

विचित्रफलकर्मौघवशात्तत्तच्छरीरभाक् ।
शरीरभाक्त्वं चैतावद्यत्तद्गर्भस्थदेहगः ॥ ^mula

vicitraphalakarmaughavaśāttattaccharīrabhāk |
śarīrabhāktvaṃ caitāvadyattadgarbhasthadehagaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 05 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 3#^pg114|p. 97]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=114>))
>
> Although Śiva is all-pervading, by sealing (Himself into a) contracted (state,) fashioned out of his own free will, he assumes this or that body, under the sway of the (massive) flux of Karma that bears extraordinarily varied consequences. (with 28.217cd–218ab)
>
> Such is (the assumption of an) embodied state, namely, the initial emergence of the life force (prāṇnana) from consciousness, blocked in the void (of deep sleep) that takes place in the body within the womb. (with 28.218cd–219ab)

*Lead-in:* किं नाम च अस्य शरीरभाक्त्वमुच्येत ?--इत्याशङ्क्य आह--

> शरीरभाक्त्वं चैतावद्यत्तद्गर्भस्थदेहगः ॥
> संवित्तेः शून्यरूढायाः प्रथमः प्राणनोदयः ।

तच्च शरीरभाक्त्वं संवित्तेरेतावत् यदस्याः शून्यदशामधिशयानाया बहिरुच्छलनादुदराकाशगर्भे वर्तमानं देहं गतः प्रथमः प्राणनोदयः

"प्राक् संवित् प्राणे परिणता ।"

इति नयेन आद्यस्पन्दसंज्ञितया प्राणनामात्ररूपतया संवित् आश्यानतामाश्रिता--इत्यर्थः ॥
