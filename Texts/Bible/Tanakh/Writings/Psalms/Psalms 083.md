## Chapter 83

**83:1** A song, a psalm of Asaph. ^83-1

**83:2** O God, do not be silent;  
do not hold aloof;  
do not be quiet, O God! ^83-2

**83:3** For Your enemies rage,  
Your foes assert themselves.[^1] ^83-3

**83:4** They plot craftily against Your people,  
take counsel against Your treasured ones. ^83-4

**83:5** They say, “Let us wipe them out as a nation;  
Israel’s name will be mentioned no more.” ^83-5

**83:6** Unanimous in their counsel  
they have made an alliance against You— ^83-6

**83:7** the clans of Edom and the Ishmaelites,  
Moab and the Hagrites, ^83-7

**83:8** Gebal, Ammon, and Amalek,  
Philistia with the inhabitants of Tyre; ^83-8

**83:9** Assyria too joins forces with them;  
they give support to the sons of Lot. *Selah*. ^83-9

**83:10** Deal with them as You did with Midian,  
with Sisera, with Jabin,  
at the brook Kishon— ^83-10

**83:11** who were destroyed at En-dor,  
who became dung for the field. ^83-11

**83:12** Treat their great men like Oreb and Zeeb,  
all their princes like Zebah and Zalmunna, ^83-12

**83:13** who said, “Let us take the meadows of God as our possession.” ^83-13

**83:14** O my God, make them like thistledown,  
like stubble driven by the wind. ^83-14

**83:15** As a fire burns a forest,  
as flames scorch the hills, ^83-15

**83:16** pursue them with Your tempest,  
terrify them with Your storm. ^83-16

**83:17** Cover[^2] their faces with shame  
so that they seek Your name, O LORD. ^83-17

**83:18** May they be frustrated and terrified,  
disgraced and doomed forever. ^83-18

**83:19** May they know  
that Your name, Yours alone, is the LORD,  
supreme over all the earth. ^83-19

[^1]: Lit. “lift up the head.”
[^2]: Lit. “Fill.”
