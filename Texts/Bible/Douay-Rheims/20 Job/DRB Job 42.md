# Job 42 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Job 41|← Job 41]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · —

**42:1** Then Job answered the Lord, and said: ^42-1

**42:2** I know that thou canst do all things, and no thought is hid from thee. ^42-2

**42:3** Who is this that hideth counsel without knowledge? Therefore I have spoken unwisely, and things that above measure exceeded my knowledge. ^42-3

**42:4** Hear, and I will speak: I will ask thee, and do thou tell me. ^42-4

**42:5** With the hearing of the ear, I have heard thee, but now my eye seeth thee. ^42-5

**42:6** Therefore I reprehend myself, and do penance in dust and ashes. ^42-6

**42:7** And after the Lord had spoken these words to Job, he said to Eliphaz the Themanite: My wrath is kindled against thee, and against thy two friends, because you have not spoken the thing that is right before my, as my servant Job hath. ^42-7

**42:8** Take unto you therefore seven oxen, and seven rams, and go to my servant Job, and offer for yourselves a holocaust: and my servant Job shall pray for you: his face I will accept, that folly be not imputed to you: for you have not spoken right things before me, as my servant Job hath. ^42-8

**42:9** So Eliphaz the Themanite, and Baldad the Suhite, and Sophar the Naamathite went, and did as the Lord had spoken to them, and the Lord accepted the face of Job. ^42-9

**42:10** The Lord also was turned at the penance of Job, when he prayed for his friends. And the Lord gave Job twice as much as he had before. ^42-10

**42:11** And all his brethren came to him, and all his sisters, and all that knew him before, and they ate bread with him in his house: and bemoaned him, and comforted him upon all the evil that God had brought upon him. And every man gave him one ewe, and one earring of fold. ^42-11

**42:12** And the Lord blessed the latter end of Job more than his beginning. And he had fourteen thousand sheep, and six thousand camels, and a thousand yoke of oxen, and a thousand she asses. ^42-12

**42:13** And he had seven sons, and three daughters. ^42-13

**42:14** And he called the names of one Dies, and the name of the second Cassia, and the name of the third Cornustibil. ^42-14

**42:15** And there were not found in all the earth women so beautiful as the daughters of Job: and their father gave them inheritance among their brethren. ^42-15

**42:16** And Job lived after these things, a hundred and forty years, and he saw his children, and his children’s children, unto the fourth generation, and he died an old man, and full of days. ^42-16
