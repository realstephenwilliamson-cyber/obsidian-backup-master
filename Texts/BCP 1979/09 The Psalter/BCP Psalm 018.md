# Psalm 18

← [[BCP Psalm 017]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 019]] →

** · Book One · Third Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 018]]

**Part I** Diligam te, Domine.

**18:1** I love you, O LORD my strength, \*  
O LORD my stronghold, my crag, and my haven. ^18-1

**18:2** My God, my rock in whom I put my trust, \*  
my shield, the horn of my salvation, and my refuge;  
you are worthy of praise. ^18-2

**18:3** I will call upon the LORD, \*  
and so shall I be saved from my enemies. ^18-3

**18:4** The breakers of death rolled over me, \*  
and the torrents of oblivion made me afraid. ^18-4

**18:5** The cords of hell entangled me, \*  
and the snares of death were set for me. ^18-5

**18:6** I called upon the LORD in my distress \*  
and cried out to my God for help. ^18-6

**18:7** He heard my voice from his heavenly dwelling; \*  
my cry of anguish came to his ears. ^18-7

[p. 603]

**18:8** The earth reeled and rocked; \*  
the roots of the mountains shook;  
they reeled because of his anger. ^18-8

**18:9** Smoke rose from his nostrils  
and a consuming fire out of his mouth; \*  
hot burning coals blazed forth from him. ^18-9

**18:10** He parted the heavens and came down \*  
with a storm cloud under his feet. ^18-10

**18:11** He mounted on cherubim and flew; \*  
he swooped on the wings of the wind. ^18-11

**18:12** He wrapped darkness about him; \*  
he made dark waters and thick clouds his pavilion. ^18-12

**18:13** From the brightness of his presence, through the clouds, \*  
burst hailstones and coals of fire. ^18-13

**18:14** The LORD thundered out of heaven; \*  
the Most High uttered his voice. ^18-14

**18:15** He loosed his arrows and scattered them; \*  
he hurled thunderbolts and routed them. ^18-15

**18:16** The beds of the seas were uncovered,  
and the foundations of the world laid bare, \*  
at your battle cry, O LORD,  
at the blast of the breath of your nostrils. ^18-16

**18:17** He reached down from on high and grasped me; \*  
he drew me out of great waters. ^18-17

**18:18** He delivered me from my strong enemies  
and from those who hated me; \*  
for they were too mighty for me. ^18-18

**18:19** They confronted me in the day of my disaster; \*  
but the LORD was my support. ^18-19

[p. 604]

**18:20** He brought me out into an open place; \*  
he rescued me because he delighted in me. ^18-20

**Psalm 18: Part II Et retribuet mihi**

**18:21** The LORD rewarded me because of my righteous dealing; \*  
because my hands were clean he rewarded me; ^18-21

**18:22** For I have kept the ways of the LORD \*  
and have not offended against my God; ^18-22

**18:23** For all his judgments are before my eyes, \*  
and his decrees I have not put away from me; ^18-23

**18:24** For I have been blameless with him \*  
and have kept myself from iniquity; ^18-24

**18:25** Therefore the LORD rewarded me according to my righteous dealing, \*  
because of the cleanness of my hands in his sight. ^18-25

**18:26** With the faithful you show yourself faithful, O God; \*  
with the forthright you show yourself forthright. ^18-26

**18:27** With the pure you show yourself pure, \*  
but with the crooked you are wily. ^18-27

**18:28** You will save a lowly people, \*  
but you will humble the haughty eyes. ^18-28

**18:29** You, O LORD, are my lamp; \*  
my God, you make my darkness bright. ^18-29

**18:30** With you I will break down an enclosure; \*  
with the help of my God I will scale any wall. ^18-30

**18:31** As for God, his ways are perfect;  
the words of the LORD are tried in the fire; \*  
he is a shield to all who trust in him. ^18-31

[p. 605]

**18:32** For who is God, but the LORD? \*  
who is the Rock, except our God? ^18-32

**18:33** It is God who girds me about with strength \*  
and makes my way secure. ^18-33

**18:34** He makes me sure-footed like a deer \*  
and lets me stand firm on the heights. ^18-34

**18:35** He trains my hands for battle \*  
and my arms for bending even a bow of bronze. ^18-35

**18:36** You have given me your shield of victory; \*  
your right hand also sustains me;  
your loving care makes me great. ^18-36

**18:37** You lengthen my stride beneath me, \*  
and my ankles do not give way. ^18-37

**18:38** I pursue my enemies and overtake them; \*  
I will not turn back till I have destroyed them. ^18-38

**18:39** I strike them down, and they cannot rise; \*  
they fall defeated at my feet. ^18-39

**18:40** You have girded me with strength for the battle; \*  
you have cast down my adversaries beneath me;  
you have put my enemies to flight. ^18-40

**18:41** I destroy those who hate me;  
they cry out, but there is none to help them; \*  
they cry to the LORD, but he does not answer. ^18-41

**18:42** I beat them small like dust before the wind; \*  
I trample them like mud in the streets. ^18-42

**18:43** You deliver me from the strife of the peoples; \*  
you put me at the head of the nations. ^18-43

**18:44** A people I have not known shall serve me;  
no sooner shall they hear than they shall obey me; \*  
strangers will cringe before me. ^18-44

[p. 606]

**18:45** The foreign peoples will lose heart; \*  
they shall come trembling out of their strongholds. ^18-45

**18:46** The LORD lives! Blessed is my Rock! \*  
Exalted is the God of my salvation! ^18-46

**18:47** He is the God who gave me victory \*  
and cast down the peoples beneath me. ^18-47

**18:48** You rescued me from the fury of my enemies;  
you exalted me above those who rose against me; \*  
you saved me from my deadly foe. ^18-48

**18:49** Therefore will I extol you among the nations, O LORD, \*  
and sing praises to your Name. ^18-49

**18:50** He multiplies the victories of his king; \*  
he shows loving-kindness to his anointed,  
to David and his descendants for ever. ^18-50
