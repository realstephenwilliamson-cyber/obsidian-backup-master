# Śāktopāya — The Cognitive Path (Āhnika 4)

> [!abstract] Definition
> The contemplative cognitive pathway in *Tantrāloka* (Āhnika 4), where dualistic discursive thoughts (*aśuddha-vikalpa*) are systematically purified and dissolved through pure philosophical insight and reflective meditation (*śuddha-vikalpa* / *jñāna-śakti*).

## Core Practices
- **Purification of Thought (*Vikalpa-Saṃskāra*):** Rather than forcibly suppressing thought, the practitioner cultivates thoughts that affirm the non-dual identity of self and cosmos.
- **Integration of Krama:** Houses the esoteric doctrine of the Twelve Kālīs, tracing the purification of sensory perception.
