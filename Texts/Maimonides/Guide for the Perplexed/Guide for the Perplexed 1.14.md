# Guide for the Perplexed 1.14

← [[Guide for the Perplexed 1.13]] · [[Guide for the Perplexed]] · [[Guide for the Perplexed 1.15]] →

*Part 1, Chapter 14* · [[Guide for the Perplexed]]

THE homonymous term adam is in the first place the name of the first man, being, as Scripture indicates, derived from adamah, “earth.” Next, it means “mankind,” as “My spirit shall not strive with man (adam)” ([[Torah 01 - Bereshit#^6-3|Gen. 6:3]]). Again “Who knoweth the spirit of the children of man (adam)” ([[Ecclesiastes 03#^3-21|Eccles. 3:21]]); “so that a man (adam) has no pre-eminence above a beast” ([[Ecclesiastes 03#^3-19|Eccles. 3:19]]). Adam. signifies also “the multitude,” “the lower classes” as opposed to those distinguished from the rest, as “Both low (bene adam) and high (bene ish)” ([[Psalms 049#^49-3|Ps. 49:3]]).

It is in this third signification that it occurs in the verses, “The sons of the higher order (Elohim) saw the daughters of the lower order (adam)” ([[Torah 01 - Bereshit#^6-2|Gen. 6:2]]); and “Forsooth! as the humble man (adam) you shall die” ([[Psalms 082#^82-7|Ps. 82:7]]).
