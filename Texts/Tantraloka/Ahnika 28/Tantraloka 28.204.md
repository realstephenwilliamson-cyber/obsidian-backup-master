## Mūla

कारणं मुख्यमाद्यं तद्गुरुविज्ञानमात्मगम् ।
अत्यन्तं स्वविशेषाणां तत्रार्पणवशात् स्फुटम् ॥ ^mula

kāraṇaṃ mukhyamādyaṃ tadguruvijñānamātmagam |
atyantaṃ svaviśeṣāṇāṃ tatrārpaṇavaśāt sphuṭam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 05 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 3#^pg109|p. 92]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=109>))
>
> The primary and first cause of such a venerable (udāra) continuum of (enlightened) noetic consciousness (vijñāna) proper (to a condition) which is not that of transmigratory existence, is the (enlightened) knowledge the teacher possesses (guruvijñāna) (that has been transmitted to the disciple, and) is present within him (ātmaga). (with 28.203cd–204ab)
>
> It is reasonable (to consider the teacher’s knowledge) to be (the true and proper) material cause (upādāna) (of the stream of the disciple’s enlightened consciousness) because, extremely clear, it is placed there (within him) with (all) its own particular characteristics (svaviśeṣa), even though (their) bodies are separate. (with 28.204cd–205ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* तदेव अस्य उपपादयति--

> अत्यन्तं स्वविशेषाणां तत्रार्पणवशात् स्फुटम् ॥
> उपादानं हि तद्युक्तं देहभेदे हि सत्यपि ।

तत्रार्पणवशादिति--उपादानकारणं हि अनुगामि भवेत्--इति भावः ॥
