# 1 Corinthians 5 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB 1 Corinthians 04|← 1 Corinthians 4]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB 1 Corinthians 06|1 Corinthians 6 →]]

Compare: [[1 Corinthians 05|BSB and SBLGNT]]

**5:1** It is absolutely heard, that there is fornication among you, and such fornication as the like is not among the heathens; that one should have his father’s wife. ^5-1

**5:2** And you are puffed up; and have not rather mourned, that he might be taken away from among you, that hath done this deed. ^5-2

**5:3** I indeed, absent in body, but present in spirit, have already judged, as though I were present, him that hath so done, ^5-3

**5:4** In the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, you being gathered together, and my spirit, with the power of our Lord Jesus; ^5-4

**5:5** To deliver such a one to Satan for the destruction of the flesh, that the spirit may be saved in the day of our Lord Jesus Christ. ^5-5

**5:6** Your glorying is not good. Know you not that a little leaven corrupteth the whole lump? ^5-6

**5:7** Purge out the old leaven, that you may be a new paste, as you are unleavened. For Christ our pasch is sacrificed. ^5-7

**5:8** Therefore let us feast, not with the old leaven, nor with the leaven of malice and wickedness; but with the unleavened bread of sincerity and truth. ^5-8

**5:9** I wrote to you in an epistle, not to keep company with fornicators. ^5-9

**5:10** I mean not with the fornicators of this world, or with the covetous, or the extortioners, or the servers of idols; otherwise you must needs go out of this world. ^5-10

**5:11** But now I have written to you, not to keep company, if any man that is named a brother, be a fornicator, or covetous, or a server of idols, or a railer, or a drunkard, or an extortioner: with such a one, not so much as to eat. ^5-11

**5:12** For what have I to do to judge them that are without? Do not you judge them that are within? ^5-12

**5:13** For them that are without, God will judge. Put away the evil one from among yourselves. ^5-13
