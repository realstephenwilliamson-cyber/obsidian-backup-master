# Psalm 38 (NRSVue)

## A penitent sufferer’s plea for healing

*A Psalm of David, for the memorial offering.*

**38:1** O LORD, do not rebuke me in your anger or discipline me in your wrath. ^38-1

**38:2** For your arrows have sunk into me, and your hand has come down on me. ^38-2

**38:3** There is no soundness in my flesh because of your indignation; there is no health in my bones because of my sin. ^38-3

**38:4** For my iniquities have gone over my head; they weigh like a burden too heavy for me. ^38-4

**38:5** My wounds grow foul and fester because of my foolishness; ^38-5

**38:6** I am utterly bowed down and prostrate; all day long I go around mourning. ^38-6

**38:7** For my loins are filled with burning, and there is no soundness in my flesh. ^38-7

**38:8** I am utterly spent and crushed; I groan because of the tumult of my heart. ^38-8

**38:9** O Lord, all my longing is known to you; my sighing is not hidden from you. ^38-9

**38:10** My heart throbs; my strength fails me; as for the light of my eyes—it also has gone from me. ^38-10

**38:11** My friends and companions stand aloof from my affliction, and my neighbors stand far off. ^38-11

**38:12** Those who seek my life lay their snares; those who seek to hurt me speak of ruin and meditate on treachery all day long. ^38-12

**38:13** But I am like the deaf; I do not hear; like the mute, who cannot speak. ^38-13

**38:14** Truly, I am like one who does not hear and in whose mouth is no retort. ^38-14

**38:15** But it is for you, O LORD, that I wait; it is you, O Lord my God, who will answer. ^38-15

**38:16** For I pray, “Only do not let them rejoice over me, those who boast against me when my foot slips.” ^38-16

**38:17** For I am ready to fall, and my pain is ever with me. ^38-17

**38:18** I confess my iniquity; I am sorry for my sin. ^38-18

**38:19** Those who are my foes without cause^[Q ms: MT *my living foes*] are mighty, and many are those who hate me wrongfully. ^38-19

**38:20** Those who render me evil for good are my adversaries because I follow after good. ^38-20

**38:21** Do not forsake me, O LORD; O my God, do not be far from me; ^38-21

**38:22** make haste to help me, O Lord, my salvation. ^38-22
