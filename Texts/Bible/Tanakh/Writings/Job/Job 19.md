# Job 19

Compare: [[NRSVue Job 19|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 19

**19:1** Job said in reply: ^19-1

**19:2** How long will you grieve my spirit,  
And crush me with words? ^19-2

**19:3** Time and again[^1] you humiliate me,  
And are not ashamed to abuse me. ^19-3

**19:4** If indeed I have erred,  
My error remains with me. ^19-4

**19:5** Though you are overbearing toward me,  
Reproaching me with my disgrace, ^19-5

**19:6** Yet know that God has wronged me;  
He has thrown up siege works around me. ^19-6

**19:7** I cry, “Violence!” but am not answered;  
I shout, but can get no justice. ^19-7

**19:8** He has barred my way; I cannot pass;  
He has laid darkness upon my path. ^19-8

**19:9** He has stripped me of my glory,  
Removed the crown from my head. ^19-9

**19:10** He tears down every part of me; I perish;  
He uproots my hope like a tree. ^19-10

**19:11** He kindles His anger against me;  
He regards me as one of His foes. ^19-11

**19:12** His troops advance together;  
They build their road toward me  
And encamp around my tent. ^19-12

**19:13** He alienated my kin from me;  
My acquaintances disown me. ^19-13

**19:14** My relatives are gone;  
My friends have forgotten me. ^19-14

**19:15** My dependents and maidservants regard me as a stranger;  
I am an outsider to them. ^19-15

**19:16** I summon my servant but he does not respond;  
I must myself entreat him. ^19-16

**19:17** My odor is repulsive to my wife;  
I am loathsome to my children. ^19-17

**19:18** Even youngsters disdain me;  
When I rise, they speak against me. ^19-18

**19:19** All my bosom friends detest me;  
Those I love have turned against me. ^19-19

**19:20** My bones stick to my skin and flesh;  
I escape with the skin of my teeth. ^19-20

**19:21** Pity me, pity me! You are my friends;  
For the hand of God has struck me! ^19-21

**19:22** Why do you pursue me like God,  
Maligning me insatiably?[^2] ^19-22

**19:23** O that my words were written down;  
Would they were inscribed in a record, ^19-23

**19:24** Incised on a rock forever  
With iron stylus and lead! ^19-24

**19:25** But I know that my Vindicator lives;  
In the end He will testify on earth— ^19-25

**19:26** This, after my skin will have been peeled off.  
But I would behold God while still in my flesh, ^19-26

**19:27** I myself, not another, would behold Him;  
Would see with my own eyes:  
My heart[^3] pines within me. ^19-27

**19:28** You say, “How do we persecute him?  
The root of the matter is in him.”[^4] ^19-28

**19:29** Be in fear of the sword,  
For [your] fury is iniquity worthy of the sword;  
Know there is a judgment! ^19-29

[^1]: Lit. “Ten times.”
[^2]: Lit. “You are not satisfied with my flesh.”
[^3]: Lit. “kidneys.”
[^4]: With many mss. and versions; printed editions, “me.”
