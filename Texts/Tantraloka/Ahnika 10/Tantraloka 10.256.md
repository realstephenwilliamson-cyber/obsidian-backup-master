## Mūla

वेद्ये स्वातन्त्र्यभाग् ज्ञानं स्वप्नं व्याप्तितया भजेत् ।
मानभूमिरियं मुख्या स्वप्नो ह्यामर्शनात्मकः ॥ ^mula

vedye svātantryabhāg jñānaṃ svapnaṃ vyāptitayā bhajet |
mānabhūmiriyaṃ mukhyā svapno hyāmarśanātmakaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 14 - Chapter Ten, part 5#^pg478|p. 459]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=478>))
>
> Dreaming is (a type of conceptual) cognitive consciousness (jñāna) which, with respect to the object of knowledge, is creatively free. Independent in every way of external objectivity (vedyasattā), (for the men of knowledge,) it abides as the state (termed) ‘Pervasion’ (vyāpti). (with 10.255cd–256ab)
>
> The dream (state) primarily corresponds to the sphere of the means of knowledge and its nature is (mental) awareness (āmarśana). (with 10.256cd)
