### 4.1–9: Instructions on morality

*[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 04#^4-1|Text]], p. 423* ^4-1-2

**[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 04#^4-1|1]]:** *Live*, lit., “walk,” an echo of a biblical expression (e.g., [[Psalms 001#^1-1|Ps 1.1]]), as well the rabbinic concept of “halakhah,” understood as “law,” but lit., “walking.” ^4-1-3

**[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 04#^4-3|3]]:** *Will of God*, [[LXX Tobit 12#^12-18|Tob 12.18]]; [[LXX 2 Maccabees 01#^1-3|2 Macc 1.3–4]]; [[LXX 2 Maccabees 12#^12-16|12.16]]. *Sanctification*, or “holiness” (same word in vv 4,7); believers are called to be “set apart” (Heb “qadosh”) by their conduct (see, e.g., [[Torah 30 - Kedoshim#^19-2|Lev 19.2]]), which leads to eternal life (see [[Romans 06#^6-22|Rom 6.22]]). *Fornication*, sexual relations outside marriage, seen by some Jews as a particularly pagan sin (e.g., *Jub*. 25.1). ^4-3

**[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 04#^4-4|4]]:** *Control your own body*, lit., “control your own vessel,” which could mean either one’s sexual organs or one’s wife; see translators note *a*, [[I Samuel 21#^21-6|1 Sam 21.5]], and *b. Meg.* 12b, where “vessel” (Heb “kli”) has a sexual connotation. ^4-4

**[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 04#^4-5|5]]:** *Lustful passion . . . Gentiles*, Paul identified Gentiles’ idolatry with immorality, especially sexual licentiousness (see [[Romans 01#^1-24|Rom 1.24–27]]; [[Galatians 05#^5-19|Gal 5.19–21]]; [[1 Timothy 01#^1-9|1 Tim 1.9–10]]; see also *m. Avod. Zar.* 2.1). The vice list reminds the Thessalonians what they have left behind (see [[1 Corinthians 06#^6-9|1 Cor 6.9–11]]) in turning to the God of Israel. *Who do not know God*, [[Jeremiah 10#^10-25|Jer 10.25]]. ^4-5

**[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 04#^4-6|6]]:** *Wrong or exploit*, take advantage of because of greed. *Avenger*, for God as avenger, [[Torah 53 - Ha'Azinu#^32-25|Deut 32.25]]; [[Isaiah 43#^43-1|Isa 43.1]]; [[Micah 5#^5-14|Mic 5.14]]; [[Nahum 1#^1-2|Nah 1.2]]; [[Psalms 099#^99-8|Ps 99.8]]; [[Romans 12#^12-19|Rom 12.19]]; [[Romans 13#^13-4|13.4]]; [[Revelation 06#^6-10|Rev 6.10]]; *1 En.* 47.2.7. *Impurity,* i.e., immorality. *Holiness*, see 3.13. ^4-6

**[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 04#^4-8|8]]:** *Holy Spirit*, see 1.5n. as well as [[Isaiah 03#^3-10|Isa 3.10–11]]; [[Psalms 051#^51-13|Ps 51.11]]; [[Ezekiel 36#^36-27|Ezek 36.27]]; [[Ezekiel 37#^37-14|37.14]]. ^4-8

### 4.9–12: Communal ethos

*[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 04#^4-9|Text]], p. 424* · See 2.8–9. ^4-9

**[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 04#^4-9|9]]:** Love of the brothers \[and sisters\], Gk “philadelphia,” see [[LXX 2 Maccabees 15#^15-14|2 Macc 15.14]]; [[LXX 4 Maccabees 13#^13-21|4 Macc 13.21]],[[LXX 4 Maccabees 13#^13-23|23]],[[LXX 4 Maccabees 13#^13-26|26]]; [[LXX 4 Maccabees 14#^14-1|14.1]]; [[LXX 4 Maccabees 15#^15-10|15.10]]; Philo, *Gaius* 87,92. *Love one another*, see [[Matthew 22#^22-36|Mt 22.36–40]]; [[John 13#^13-34|Jn 13.34–35]]; [[John 15#^15-12|15.12]],[[John 15#^15-17|17]]; see also [[Torah 30 - Kedoshim#^19-18|Lev 19.18]]; CD 6.20–21; 1QS 1.3–4. ^4-9-2

**[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 04#^4-10|10]]:** *Beloved*, Gk “brothers,” 3.2n. Paul uses the term thirteen times in this letter to suggest the new relationships formed among the believers; see Philo, *Spec. Laws* 1.52; *Virtues* 103–104. ^4-10

**[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 04#^4-11|11]]:** *Aspire to live quietly* (Gk “philotimesthai hēsychazein,” lit., “be eager \[or ambitious\] to rest, to live in quiet”), Paul’s phrasing distinguishes the community from Epicureans who withdrew from society to pursue their ideal of “ataraxia,” “freedom from disturbance.” *Work with your hands*, emulating Paul (2.9). ^4-11

**[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 04#^4-12|12]]:** *Outsiders*, non-believers in the wider community, thus avoiding the antagonism reported in 1.6. ^4-12

### 4.13–17: The salvation of those who have died

*[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 04#^4-13|Text]], p. 424* ^4-13

**[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 04#^4-13|13]]:** *Uninformed*, perhaps a response to a question sent by the Thessalonians. *Those who have died*, lit., “who have fallen asleep”; see for the idiom [[LXX Genesis 47#^47-30|Gen 47.30]]\[LXX\]; [[LXX 3 Kingdoms 02#^2-10|1 Kings 2.10]]\[LXX\]; [[LXX Isaiah 43#^43-17|Isa 43.17]]\[LXX\] and, in relation to resurrection, [[Daniel 12#^12-2|Dan 12.2]]; [[LXX 2 Maccabees 12#^12-44|2 Macc 12.44–45]]; *1 En*. 92.3. *Who have no hope*, those who do not believe. ^4-13-2

**[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 04#^4-14|14]]:** *Through Jesus*, those who had faith in Jesus will be resurrected. ^4-14

**[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 04#^4-15|15]]:** *Word of the Lord*, Paul may be referring to a revelation he received from God; the Gospels record no such teaching. These verses are the primary source (see also [[Matthew 24#^24-29|Mt 24.29–31]]) of the “Rapture” (from Gk “rapto,” “to take up” “catch up” \[cf. 4.17\]), the view, developed in the late nineteenth century, that the righteous will be taken to heaven and so escape the eschatological tribulation (see 1.10n., 3.4n.; see also *4 Ezra* \[2 Esd\] 6.26). *Precede those who have died*, they will not miss out on salvation and, in fact, will meet Jesus first. ^4-15

**[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 04#^4-16|16]]:** *Lord*, Jesus. *Archangel*, an angel of high rank (see Jude 9, the Bible’s only other use of the term), or the “chief princes” ([[Daniel 10#^10-13|Dan 10.13]] in reference to Michael) in God’s heavenly court, see [[Job 01#^1-6|Job 1.6]]; *Apoc. Mos.* 40.1; Philo, *Heir.* 205–206. Jewish tradition developed the idea of seven archangels (see [[LXX Tobit 12#^12-15|Tob 12.15]]; *1 En*. 20,81,90; *Serekh Shirot Olot le-Shabbat* \[a Qumran Angelic Liturgy\]) including Michael, Gabriel, and Raphael. *Trumpet*, the sounding of the trumpet or ram’s horn (Heb “shofar”) associated with the proclamation of redemption, e.g., [[Isaiah 27#^27-13|Isa 27.13]]; [[Joel 2#^2-1|Joel 2.1]],[[Joel 2#^2-15|15]]; [[Zechariah 09#^9-14|Zech 9.14]]; 1QM 2.16–3.12; *L. A. E.* 22. *Gen. Rab*. 56.9 “But in the end they will be redeemed by a ram’s horn.” ^4-16
