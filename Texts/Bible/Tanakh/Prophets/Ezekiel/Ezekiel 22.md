## Chapter 22

**22:1** The word of the LORD came to me: ^22-1

**22:2** Further, O mortal, arraign, arraign[^1] the city of bloodshed; declare to her all her abhorrent deeds! ^22-2

**22:3** Say: Thus said the Lord GOD: O city in whose midst blood is shed, so that your hour is approaching; within which fetishes are made, so that you have become unclean! ^22-3

**22:4** You stand guilty of the blood you have shed, defiled by the fetishes you have made. You have brought on your day; you have reached your year.[^2] Therefore I will make you the mockery of the nations and the scorn of all the lands. ^22-4

**22:5** Both the near and the far shall scorn you, O besmirched of name, O laden with iniquity! ^22-5

**22:6** Every one of the princes of Israel in your midst used his strength for the shedding of blood. ^22-6

**22:7** Fathers and mothers have been humiliated within you; strangers have been cheated in your midst; orphans and widows have been wronged within you. ^22-7

**22:8** You have despised My holy things and profaned My sabbaths. ^22-8

**22:9** Base[^3] men in your midst were intent on shedding blood; in you they have eaten upon the mountains;[^4] and they have practiced depravity in your midst. ^22-9

**22:10** In you they have uncovered their fathers’ nakedness;[^5] in you they have ravished women during their impurity. ^22-10

**22:11** They have committed abhorrent acts with other men’s wives; in their depravity they have defiled their own daughters-in-law; in you they have ravished their own sisters, daughters of their fathers. ^22-11

**22:12** They have taken bribes within you to shed blood. You have taken advance and accrued interest;[^6] you have defrauded your countrymen to your profit. You have forgotten Me—declares the Lord GOD. ^22-12

**22:13** Lo, I will strike My hands over the ill-gotten gains that you have amassed, and over the bloodshed that has been committed in your midst. ^22-13

**22:14** Will your courage endure, will your hands remain firm in the days when I deal with you? I the LORD have spoken and I will act. ^22-14

**22:15** I will scatter you among the nations and disperse you through the lands; I will consume the uncleanness out of you. ^22-15

**22:16** You shall be dishonored in the sight of nations, and you shall know that I am the LORD. ^22-16

**22:17** The word of the LORD came to me: ^22-17

**22:18** O mortal, the House of Israel has become dross to Me; they are all copper, tin, iron, and lead. But in a crucible, the dross shall turn into silver.[^7] ^22-18

**22:19** Assuredly, thus said the Lord GOD: Because you have all become dross, I will gather you into Jerusalem. ^22-19

**22:20** As silver, copper, iron, lead, and tin are gathered into a crucible to blow the fire upon them, so as to melt them, so will I gather you in My fierce anger and cast you [into the fire] and melt you. ^22-20

**22:21** I will gather you and I will blow upon you the fire of My fury, and you shall be melted in it. ^22-21

**22:22** As silver is melted in a crucible, so shall you be melted in it. And you shall know that I the LORD have poured out My fury upon you. ^22-22

**22:23** The word of the LORD came to me: ^22-23

**22:24** O mortal, say to her: You are an uncleansed land, not to be washed with rain[^8] on the day of indignation. ^22-24

**22:25** Her gang of prophets[^9] are like roaring lions in her midst, rending prey. They devour human beings; they seize treasure and wealth; they have widowed many women in her midst. ^22-25

**22:26** Her priests have violated My Teaching: they have profaned what is sacred to Me, they have not distinguished between the sacred and the profane, they have not taught the difference between the unclean and the clean, and they have closed their eyes to My sabbaths. I am profaned in their midst. ^22-26

**22:27** Her officials are like wolves rending prey in her midst; they shed blood and destroy lives to win ill-gotten gain. ^22-27

**22:28** Her prophets, too, daub the wall for them with plaster:[^10] They prophesy falsely and divine deceitfully for them; they say, “Thus said the Lord GOD,” when the LORD has not spoken. ^22-28

**22:29** And the people of the land have practiced fraud and committed robbery; they have wronged the poor and needy, have defrauded the stranger without redress. ^22-29

**22:30** And I sought a man among them to repair the wall or to stand in the breach before Me in behalf of this land, that I might not destroy it; but I found none. ^22-30

**22:31** I have therefore poured out My indignation upon them; I will consume them with the fire of My fury. I will repay them for their conduct—declares the Lord GOD. ^22-31

[^1]: Lit. “will you arraign, arraign.”
[^2]: Some Babylonian mss. and ancient versions read “the time of your years has come.”
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^4]: I.e., in idolatry. Emendation yields “with the blood”; cf. Lev. 19.26.
[^5]: I.e., have cohabited with a former wife of the father; cf. Lev. 18.7–8.
[^6]: Cf. note at 18.8.
[^7]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^8]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^9]: Septuagint reads “Whose chieftains.”
[^10]: Cf. 13.10 ff.
