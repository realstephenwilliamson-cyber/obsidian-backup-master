# δικαίωμα

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · noun

**Dodson:** a thing pronounced to be just — a thing pronounced (by God) to be righteous (just, the right); or the restoration of a criminal, a fresh chance given him; a righteous deed, an instance of perfect righteousness. (Strong's 1345; headword δικαίωμα, ατος, τό)
