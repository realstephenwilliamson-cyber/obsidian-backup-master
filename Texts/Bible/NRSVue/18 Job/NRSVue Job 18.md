# Job 18 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Job 17|← Job 17]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Job 19|Job 19 →]]

## Bildad speaks: God punishes the wicked

**18:1** Then Bildad the Shuhite answered: ^18-1

**18:2** “How long will you hunt for words? Consider, and then we shall speak. ^18-2

**18:3** Why are we counted as cattle? Why are we stupid in your sight? ^18-3

**18:4** You who tear yourself in your anger—shall the earth be forsaken because of you or the rock be removed out of its place? ^18-4

**18:5** “Surely the light of the wicked is put out, and the flame of their fire does not shine. ^18-5

**18:6** The light is dark in their tent, and the lamp above them is put out. ^18-6

**18:7** Their strong steps are shortened, and their own schemes throw them down. ^18-7

**18:8** For they are thrust into a net by their own feet, and they walk into a pitfall. ^18-8

**18:9** A trap seizes them by the heel; a snare lays hold of them. ^18-9

**18:10** A rope is hid for them in the ground, a trap for them in the path. ^18-10

**18:11** Terrors frighten them on every side and chase them at their heels. ^18-11

**18:12** Their strength is consumed by hunger,^[Or *Disaster is hungry for them*] and calamity is ready for their stumbling. ^18-12

**18:13** By disease their skin is consumed;^[Cn: Heb *It consumes the limbs of his skin*] the firstborn of Death consumes their limbs. ^18-13

**18:14** They are torn from the tent in which they trusted and are brought to the king of terrors. ^18-14

**18:15** In their tents nothing remains; sulfur is scattered upon their habitations. ^18-15

**18:16** Their roots dry up beneath, and their branches wither above. ^18-16

**18:17** Their memory perishes from the earth, and they have no name in the street. ^18-17

**18:18** They are thrust from light into darkness and driven out of the world. ^18-18

**18:19** They have no offspring or descendant among their people and no survivor where they used to live. ^18-19

**18:20** They of the west are appalled at their fate, and horror seizes those of the east. ^18-20

**18:21** Surely such are the dwellings of the ungodly; such is the place of those who do not know God.” ^18-21
