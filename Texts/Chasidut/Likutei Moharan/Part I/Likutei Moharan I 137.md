*Part I · Lesson 137* · [[Likutei Moharan]]

## 1

“*Chelki Hashem* (The Lord is my portion), I said to keep Your words.” ([[Psalms 119#^119-57|Psalms 119:57]])

That is, my ‘Godly portion from above’ says to me and teaches me “to keep Your words.”
