# Psalm 30 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 029|← Psalm 29]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 031|Psalm 31 →]]

## Thanksgiving for recovery from grave illness

*A Psalm. A Song at the dedication of the temple. Of David.*

**30:1** I will extol you, O LORD, for you have drawn me up and did not let my foes rejoice over me. ^30-1

**30:2** O LORD my God, I cried to you for help, and you have healed me. ^30-2

**30:3** O LORD, you brought up my soul from Sheol, restored me to life from among those gone down to the Pit.^[Or *that I should not go down to the Pit*] ^30-3

**30:4** Sing praises to the LORD, O you his faithful ones, and give thanks to his holy name. ^30-4

**30:5** For his anger is but for a moment; his favor is for a lifetime. Weeping may linger for the night, but joy comes with the morning. ^30-5

**30:6** As for me, I said in my prosperity, “I shall never be moved.” ^30-6

**30:7** By your favor, O LORD, you had established me as a strong mountain; you hid your face; I was dismayed. ^30-7

**30:8** To you, O LORD, I cried, and to the LORD I made supplication: ^30-8

**30:9** “What profit is there in my death, if I go down to the Pit? Will the dust praise you? Will it tell of your faithfulness? ^30-9

**30:10** Hear, O LORD, and be gracious to me! O LORD, be my helper!”^[Or *TheLORDheard and was gracious to me; theLORDbecame my helper*] ^30-10

**30:11** You have turned my mourning into dancing; you have taken off my sackcloth and clothed me with joy, ^30-11

**30:12** so that my soul^[Heb *that glory*] may praise you and not be silent. O LORD my God, I will give thanks to you forever. ^30-12
