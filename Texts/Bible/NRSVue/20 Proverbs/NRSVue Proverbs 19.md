# Proverbs 19 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Proverbs 18|← Proverbs 18]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Proverbs 20|Proverbs 20 →]]

**19:1** Better the poor walking in integrity than one perverse of speech who is a fool. ^19-1

**19:2** Desire without knowledge is not good, and one who moves too hurriedly misses the way. ^19-2

**19:3** One’s own folly leads to ruin, yet the heart rages against the LORD. ^19-3

**19:4** Wealth brings many friends, but the poor are left friendless. ^19-4

**19:5** A false witness will not go unpunished, and a liar will not escape. ^19-5

**19:6** Many seek the favor of the generous, and everyone is a friend to a giver of gifts. ^19-6

**19:7** If the poor are hated even by their kin, how much more are they shunned by their friends! When they call after them, they are not there.^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^19-7

**19:8** To get wisdom is to love oneself; to keep understanding is to prosper. ^19-8

**19:9** A false witness will not go unpunished, and the liar will perish. ^19-9

**19:10** It is not fitting for a fool to live in luxury, much less for a slave to rule over princes. ^19-10

**19:11** Those with good sense are slow to anger, and it is their glory to overlook an offense. ^19-11

**19:12** A king’s anger is like the growling of a lion, but his favor is like dew on the grass. ^19-12

**19:13** A stupid child is ruin to a father, and a wife’s quarreling is a continual dripping of rain. ^19-13

**19:14** House and wealth are inherited from parents, but a prudent wife is from the LORD. ^19-14

**19:15** Laziness brings on deep sleep; an idle person will suffer hunger. ^19-15

**19:16** Those who keep the commandment will live; those who are heedless of their ways will die. ^19-16

**19:17** Whoever is kind to the poor lends to the LORD and will be repaid in full. ^19-17

**19:18** Discipline your children while there is hope; do not set your heart on their destruction. ^19-18

**19:19** A violent-tempered person will pay the penalty; if you effect a rescue, you will only have to do it again.^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^19-19

**19:20** Listen to advice and accept instruction, that you may gain wisdom for the future. ^19-20

**19:21** The human mind may devise many plans, but it is the purpose of the LORD that will be established. ^19-21

**19:22** What is desirable in a person is loyalty, and it is better to be poor than a liar. ^19-22

**19:23** The fear of the LORD is life indeed; filled with it one rests secure and suffers no harm. ^19-23

**19:24** The lazy person buries a hand in the dish and will not even bring it back to the mouth. ^19-24

**19:25** Strike a scoffer, and the simple will learn prudence; reprove the intelligent, and they will gain knowledge. ^19-25

**19:26** Those who do violence to their father and chase away their mother are children who cause shame and bring reproach. ^19-26

**19:27** My child, stop ignoring instruction, straying^[Cn: Meaning of Heb uncertain] from words of knowledge. ^19-27

**19:28** A worthless witness mocks at justice, and the mouth of the wicked devours iniquity. ^19-28

**19:29** Punishments are prepared for scoffers and flogging for the backs of fools. ^19-29
