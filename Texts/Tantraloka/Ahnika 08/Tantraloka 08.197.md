## Mūla

विजयाख्यं पुरं चास्य ये स्मरन्तो महेश्वरम् ।
जलेषु मरुषु चाग्नौ शिरश्छेदेन वा मृताः ॥ ^mula

vijayākhyaṃ puraṃ cāsya ye smaranto maheśvaram |
jaleṣu maruṣu cāgnau śiraśchedena vā mṛtāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 07 - Chapter Eight, part 5#^pg237|p. 219]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=237>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 8.197cd–198ab.
>
> Beyond that, for ten million (leagues, lives) Vīrabhadra, whose light is like that of the fire at the end of an aeon (yuga). His world is called Vijaya. Those who have died in water, in deserts, or in a fire, or have been decapitated, while recalling Maheśvara, attain Vīrabhadra, who is the Lord’s consciousness of great splendour. (with 8.196cd–198ab)
