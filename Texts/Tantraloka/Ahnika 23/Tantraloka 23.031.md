## Mūla

प्राप्ताभिषेकः स गुरुः षण्मासान्मन्त्रपद्धतिम् ।
सर्वां तन्त्रोदितां ध्यायेज्जपेच्चातन्मयत्वतः ॥ ^mula

prāptābhiṣekaḥ sa guruḥ ṣaṇmāsānmantrapaddhatim |
sarvāṃ tantroditāṃ dhyāyejjapeccātanmayatvataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 14 - Chapter Twenty-Three#^pg331|p. 311]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=331>))
>
> The (new) teacher who has (just) been consecrated should recite for six months the entire liturgy of Mantras taught in the scriptures, and meditate on (them) until he becomes one with them. If he has become one with them, he then attains (their) vitality (vīrya), and (so) may sever the fetters (of his disciples). Thus, he should exert himself (to attain and) abide in (that) state of unity. (with 23.31–32)
