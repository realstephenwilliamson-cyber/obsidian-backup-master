## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 12 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 4#^pg368|p. 351]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=368>))
>
> (The body) is the support of all the gods, the cremation ground, frightening with the funeral pyre (citi) (of consciousness — citi), which destroys all things). Attended by Siddhas and Yoginīs, it is their very terrible playground, wherein all embodied forms come to an end. Full of the countless pyres (of the senses), and pervaded by the halos of their rays, the flux of the darkness (of duality) is destroyed and, free of all thought-constructs, it is the sole abode of bliss. Entering this (body) — the cremation ground of emptiness – who does not achieve perfection? (with 29.183–185)
