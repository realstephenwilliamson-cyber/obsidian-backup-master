# Jonah 2 (Douay-Rheims)

*Jonas 2 in the Douay-Rheims*

[[DRB Jonah 01|← Jonah 1]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Jonah 03|Jonah 3 →]]

**2:1** Now the Lord prepared a great fish to swallow up Jonas: and Jonas was in the belly of the fish three days and three nights. ^2-1

**2:2** And Jonas prayed to the Lord his God out of the belly of the fish. ^2-2

**2:3** And he said: I cried out of my affliction to the Lord, and he heard me: I cried out of the belly of hell, and thou hast heard my voice. ^2-3

**2:4** And thou hast cast me forth into the deep in the heart of the sea, and a flood hath compassed me: all thy billows, and thy waves have passed over me. ^2-4

**2:5** And I said: I am cast away out of the sight of thy eyes: but yet I shall see thy holy temple again. ^2-5

**2:6** The waters compassed me about even to the soul: the deep hath closed me round about, the sea hath covered my head. ^2-6

**2:7** I went down to the lowest parts of the mountains: the bars of the earth have shut me up for ever: and thou wilt bring up my life from corruption, O Lord my God. ^2-7

**2:8** When my soul was in distress within me, I remembered the Lord: that my prayer may come to thee, unto thy holy temple. ^2-8

**2:9** They that are vain observe vanities, forsake their own mercy. ^2-9

**2:10** But I with the voice of praise will sacrifice to thee: I will pay whatsoever I have vowed for my salvation to the Lord. ^2-10

**2:11** And the Lord spoke to the fish: and it vomited out Jonas upon the dry land. ^2-11
