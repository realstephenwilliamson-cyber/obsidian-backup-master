# Psalms 7 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 06|← Psalms 6]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 08|Psalms 8 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 07|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**7:1** The psalm of David which he sung to the Lord for the words of Chusi the son of Jemini. [2 Kings 16] ^7-1

**7:2** O Lord my God, in thee have I put my trust: save me from all them that persecute me, and deliver me. ^7-2

**7:3** Lest at any time he seize upon my soul like a lion, while there is no one to redeem me, nor to save. ^7-3

**7:4** O Lord my God, if I have done this thing, if there be iniquity in my hands: ^7-4

**7:5** If I have rendered to them that repaid me evils, let me deservedly fall empty before my enemies. ^7-5

**7:6** Let the enemy pursue my soul, and take it, and tread down my life on the earth, and bring down my glory to the dust. ^7-6

**7:7** Rise up, O Lord, in thy anger: and be thou exalted in the borders of my enemies. And arise, O Lord my God, in the precept which thou hast commanded: ^7-7

**7:8** And a congregation of people shall surround thee. And for their sakes return thou on high. ^7-8

**7:9** The Lord judgeth the people. Judge me, O Lord, according to my justice, and according to my innocence in me. ^7-9

**7:10** The wickedness of sinners shall be brought to nought: and thou shalt direct the just: the searcher of hearts and reins is God. ^7-10

**7:11** Just is my help from the Lord: who saveth the upright of heart. ^7-11

**7:12** God is a just judge, strong and patient: is he angry every day? ^7-12

**7:13** Except you will be converted, he will brandish his sword: he hath bent his bow and made it ready. ^7-13

**7:14** And in it he hath prepared the instruments of death, he hath made ready his arrows for them that burn. ^7-14

**7:15** Behold he hath been in labour with injustice; he hath conceived sorrow, and brought forth iniquity. ^7-15

**7:16** He hath opened a pit and dug it; and he is fallen into the hole he made. ^7-16

**7:17** His sorrow shall be turned on his own head: and his iniquity shall comedown upon his crown. ^7-17

**7:18** I will give glory to the Lord according to his justice: and will sing to the name of the Lord the most high. ^7-18
