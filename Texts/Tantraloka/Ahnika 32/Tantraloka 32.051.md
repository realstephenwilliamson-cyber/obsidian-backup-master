## Mūla

वृषणद्वयलिङ्गं च प्राप्य कायं गता त्वियम् ।
भवस्थानाभवस्थानमुच्चारेणावधारयेत् ॥ ^mula

vṛṣaṇadvayaliṅgaṃ ca prāpya kāyaṃ gatā tviyam |
bhavasthānābhavasthānamuccāreṇāvadhārayet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 06 - Chapter Thirty-Two#^pg177|p. 159]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=177>))
>
> Having reached the nose, the two eyes, the heart, the two breasts, the two testicles and the penis, this (mudrā) is present (in all) the body. One should ascertain the abode of transmigration and the abode of its absence by the upward movement (uccārena) (of Mudrā). This is the mental (form of the Seal of the Skyfarer). (with 32.50cd–52a)
