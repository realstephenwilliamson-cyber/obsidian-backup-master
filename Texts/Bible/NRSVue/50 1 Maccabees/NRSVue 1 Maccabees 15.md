# 1 Maccabees 15 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue 1 Maccabees 14|← 1 Maccabees 14]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue 1 Maccabees 16|1 Maccabees 16 →]]

## Letter of Antiochus vii

**15:1** Antiochus, son of King Demetrius, sent a letter from the islands of the sea to Simon, the priest and ethnarch of the Jews, and to all the nation; ^15-1

**15:2** its contents were as follows: “King Antiochus to Simon the high priest and ethnarch and to the nation of the Jews, greetings. ^15-2

**15:3** Whereas certain scoundrels have gained control of the kingdom of our ancestors, and I intend to lay claim to the kingdom so that I may restore it as it formerly was, and have recruited a host of mercenary troops and have equipped warships, ^15-3

**15:4** and intend to make a landing in the country so that I may proceed against those who have destroyed our country and those who have devastated many cities in my kingdom, ^15-4

**15:5** now therefore I confirm to you all the tax remissions that the kings before me have granted you and a release from all the other payments from which they have released you. ^15-5

**15:6** I permit you to mint your own coinage as money for your country, ^15-6

**15:7** and I grant freedom to Jerusalem and the sanctuary. All the weapons that you have prepared and the strongholds that you have built and now hold shall remain yours. ^15-7

**15:8** Every debt you owe to the royal treasury and any such future debts shall be canceled for you from henceforth and for all time. ^15-8

**15:9** When we gain control of our kingdom, we will bestow great honor on you and your nation and the temple, so that your glory will become manifest in all the earth.” ^15-9

**15:10** In the one hundred seventy-fourth year Antiochus set out and invaded the land of his ancestors. All the troops rallied to him, so that there were only a few with Trypho. ^15-10

**15:11** Antiochus pursued him, and Trypho^[Gk *he*] came in his flight to Dor, which is by the sea, ^15-11

**15:12** for he knew that troubles had converged on him, and his troops had deserted him. ^15-12

**15:13** So Antiochus encamped against Dor, and with him were one hundred twenty thousand warriors and eight thousand cavalry. ^15-13

**15:14** He surrounded the town, and the ships joined battle from the sea; he pressed the town hard from land and sea and permitted no one to leave or enter it. ^15-14

## Rome supports the Jews

**15:15** Then Numenius and his companions arrived from Rome, with letters to the kings and countries, in which the following was written: ^15-15

**15:16** “Lucius, consul of the Romans, to King Ptolemy, greetings. ^15-16

**15:17** The envoys of the Jews have come to us as our friends and allies to renew our ancient friendship and alliance. They had been sent by the high priest Simon and by the Jewish people ^15-17

**15:18** and have brought a gold shield weighing one thousand minas. ^15-18

**15:19** We therefore have decided to write to the kings and countries that they should not seek their harm or make war against them and their cities and their country or make alliance with those who war against them. ^15-19

**15:20** And it has seemed good to us to accept the shield from them. ^15-20

**15:21** Therefore if any scoundrels have fled to you from their country, hand them over to the high priest Simon, so that he may punish them according to their law.” ^15-21

**15:22** The consul^[Gk *He*] wrote the same thing to King Demetrius and to Attalus and Ariarathes and Arsaces ^15-22

**15:23** and to all the countries, and to Sampsames,^[The name is uncertain] and to the Spartans, and to Delos, and to Myndos, and to Sicyon, and to Caria, and to Samos, and to Pamphylia, and to Lycia, and to Halicarnassus, and to Rhodes, and to Phaselis, and to Cos, and to Side, and to Aradus and Gortyna and Cnidus and Cyprus and Cyrene. ^15-23

**15:24** They also sent a copy of these things to the high priest Simon. ^15-24

## Antiochus vii threatens Simon

**15:25** King Antiochus besieged Dor for the second time, continually throwing his forces against it and making engines of war, and he shut Trypho up and kept him from going out or in. ^15-25

**15:26** And Simon sent to Antiochus^[Gk *him*] two thousand picked troops, to fight for him, and silver and gold and a large amount of military equipment. ^15-26

**15:27** But he refused to receive them and broke all the agreements he formerly had made with Simon and became estranged from him. ^15-27

**15:28** He sent to him Athenobius, one of his Friends, to confer with him, saying, “You hold control of Joppa and Gazara and the citadel in Jerusalem; they are cities of my kingdom. ^15-28

**15:29** You have devastated their territory, you have done great damage in the land, and you have taken possession of many places in my kingdom. ^15-29

**15:30** Now, then, hand over the cities that you have seized and the tribute money of the places that you have conquered outside the borders of Judea, ^15-30

**15:31** or else pay me five hundred talents of silver for the destruction that you have caused and five hundred talents more for the tribute money of the cities. Otherwise we will come and make war on you.” ^15-31

**15:32** So Athenobius, the king’s Friend, came to Jerusalem, and when he saw the splendor of Simon, and the sideboard with its gold and silver plate, and his great magnificence, he was amazed. When he reported to him the king’s message, ^15-32

**15:33** Simon said to him in reply: “We have neither taken foreign land nor seized foreign property, but only the inheritance of our ancestors, which at one time had been unjustly taken by our enemies. ^15-33

**15:34** Now that we have the opportunity, we are firmly holding the inheritance of our ancestors. ^15-34

**15:35** As for Joppa and Gazara, which you demand, they were causing great damage among the people and to our land, for them we will give you one hundred talents.” Athenobius^[Gk *He*] did not answer him a word ^15-35

**15:36** but returned in wrath to the king and reported to him these words and also the splendor of Simon and all that he had seen. And the king was very angry. ^15-36

## Victory over Cendebeus

**15:37** Meanwhile Trypho embarked on a ship and escaped to Orthosia. ^15-37

**15:38** Then the king made Cendebeus commander-in-chief of the coastal country and gave him troops of infantry and cavalry. ^15-38

**15:39** He commanded him to encamp against Judea, to build up Kedron and fortify its gates, and to make war on the people, but the king pursued Trypho. ^15-39

**15:40** So Cendebeus came to Jamnia and began to provoke the people and invade Judea and take the people captive and kill them. ^15-40

**15:41** He built up Kedron and stationed horsemen and troops there, so that they might go out and make raids along the highways of Judea, as the king had ordered him. ^15-41
