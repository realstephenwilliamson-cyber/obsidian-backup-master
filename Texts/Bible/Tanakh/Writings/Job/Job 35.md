# Job 35

Compare: [[NRSVue Job 35|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 35

**35:1** Elihu said in reply: ^35-1

**35:2** Do you think it just  
To say, “I am right against God”? ^35-2

**35:3** If you ask how it benefits you,  
“What have I gained from not sinning?” ^35-3

**35:4** I shall give you a reply,  
You, along with your friends. ^35-4

**35:5** Behold the heavens and see;  
Look at the skies high above you. ^35-5

**35:6** If you sin, what do you do to Him?  
If your transgressions are many,  
How do you affect Him? ^35-6

**35:7** If you are righteous,  
What do you give Him;  
What does He receive from your hand? ^35-7

**35:8** Your wickedness affects men like yourself;  
Your righteousness, mortals. ^35-8

**35:9** Because of contention the oppressed cry out;  
They shout because of the power of the great. ^35-9

**35:10** But none says, “Where is my God, my Maker,  
Who gives strength in the night; ^35-10

**35:11** Who gives us more knowledge than the beasts of the earth,  
Makes us wiser than the birds of the sky?” ^35-11

**35:12** Then they cry out, but He does not respond  
Because of the arrogance of evil men. ^35-12

**35:13** Surely it is false that God does not listen,  
That Shaddai does not take note of it. ^35-13

**35:14** Though you say, “You do not take note of it,”  
The case is before Him;  
So wait for Him. ^35-14

**35:15** But since now it does not seem so,  
He vents his anger;  
He does not realize that it may be long drawn out.[^1] ^35-15

**35:16** Hence Job mouths empty words,  
And piles up words without knowledge. ^35-16

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
