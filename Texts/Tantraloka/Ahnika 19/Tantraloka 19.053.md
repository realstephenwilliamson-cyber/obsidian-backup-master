## Mūla

अकृताधिकृतिर्वापि गुरुः समयशुद्धये ।
अधस्तनपदावस्थो न तु ज्ञानेद्धचेतनः ॥ ^mula

akṛtādhikṛtirvāpi guruḥ samayaśuddhaye |
adhastanapadāvastho na tu jñāneddhacetanaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 10 - Chapter Nineteen#^pg248|p. 228]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=248>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 19.52cd–53ab.
>
> In this way, a regular or other (initiate) may, with the consent of (his) teacher, perform the aforementioned (initiation that brings about) the immediate exit (from the body) by himself, at the aforementioned time, in order to accomplish the aforementioned purpose. The teacher who is well practiced in the coming and going of the movement of the vital breath (but), in order to purify (and eliminate the constraints imposed by) the Rule, has not done what he is authorized to do (and is his duty), is on an inferior level. Not so one whose consciousness is burning with knowledge. (with 19.51cd–53)

## Viveka

> इत्थं सद्यःसमुत्क्रान्तिर्योक्ता तामाज्ञया गुरोः ॥
> समययादिरपि प्रोक्तकाले प्रोक्तार्थसिद्धये ।
> स्वयं कुर्यात्समभ्यस्तप्राणचारगमागमः ॥
> अकृताधिकृतिर्वापि गुरुः समयशुद्धये ।
> अधस्तनपदावस्थो न तु ज्ञानेद्धचेतनः ॥

अकृतेति--यदुक्तम्-- "अधिकारं न चेत्कुर्याद्विद्येशः स्यात्तनुक्षये ।"इति । ज्ञानेद्ध इति--परमाद्वयनिष्ठस्य हि

"मा किञ्चित्त्यज मा गृहाण.................... ।"

इति नयेन विधिनिषेधाविषयत्वात् को नाम समयलोपस्य अवकाश एव-- इत्याशयः ॥
