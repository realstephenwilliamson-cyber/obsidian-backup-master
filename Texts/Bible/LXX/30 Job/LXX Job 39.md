# LXX Job 39

← [[LXX Job 38]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Job 40]] →

*Job · [[LXX]]*

**39:1** *Say* if thou knowest the time of the bringing forth of the wild goats of the rock, and *if* thou hast marked the calving of the hinds:  ^39-1

**39:2** and *if* thou hast numbered the full months of their being with young, and *if* thou hast relieved their pangs:  ^39-2

**39:3** and hast reared their young without fear; and wilt thou loosen their pangs?  ^39-3

**39:4** Their young will break forth; they will be multiplied with offspring: *their young* will go forth, and will not return to them.  ^39-4

**39:5** And who is he that sent forth the wild ass free? and who loosed his bands?  ^39-5

**39:6** whereas I made his habitation the wilderness, and the salt land his coverts.  ^39-6

**39:7** He laughs to scorn the multitude of the city, and hears not the chiding of the tax-gatherer.  ^39-7

**39:8** He will survey the mountains *as* his pasture, and he seeks after every green thing.  ^39-8

**39:9** And will the unicorn be willing to serve thee, or to lie down at thy manger?  ^39-9

**39:10** And wilt thou bind his yoke with thongs, or will he plough furrows for thee in the plain?  ^39-10

**39:11** And dost thou trust him, because his strength is great? and wilt thou commit thy works to him?  ^39-11

**39:12** And wilt thou believe that he will return to thee thy seed, and bring *it* in *to* thy threshing-floor?  ^39-12

**39:13** The peacock has a beautiful wing: if the stork and the ostrich conceive, *it is worthy of notice*,  ^39-13

**39:14** for *the ostrich* will leave her eggs in the ground, and warm them on the dust,  ^39-14

**39:15** and has forgotten that the foot will scatter them, and the wild beasts of the field trample them.  ^39-15

**39:16** She has hardened *herself* against her young ones, as though *she bereaved* not herself: she labours in vain without fear.  ^39-16

**39:17** For God has withholden wisdom from her, and not given her a portion in understanding.  ^39-17

**39:18** In her season she will lift herself on high; she will scorn the horse and his rider.  ^39-18

**39:19** Hast thou invested the horse with strength, and clothed his neck with terror?  ^39-19

**39:20** And hast thou clad him in perfect armour, and made his breast glorious with courage?  ^39-20

**39:21** He paws exulting in the plain, and goes forth in strength into the plain.  ^39-21

**39:22** He laughs to scorn a king as he meets him, and will by no means turn back from the sword.  ^39-22

**39:23** The bow and sword resound against him; and *his* rage will swallow up the ground:  ^39-23

**39:24** and he will not believe until the trumpet sounds.  ^39-24

**39:25** And when the trumpet sounds, he says, Aha! and afar off he smells the war with prancing and neighing.  ^39-25

**39:26** And does the hawk remain steady by thy wisdom, having spread out her wings unmoved, looking toward the region of the south?  ^39-26

**39:27** And does the eagle rise at thy command, and the vulture remain sitting over his nest,  ^39-27

**39:28** on a crag of a rock, and in a secret *place*?  ^39-28

**39:29** Thence he seeks food, his eyes observe from far.  ^39-29

**39:30** And his young ones roll themselves in blood, and wherever the carcases may be, immediately they are found.  ^39-30
