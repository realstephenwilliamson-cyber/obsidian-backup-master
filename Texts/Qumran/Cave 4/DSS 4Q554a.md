### Text

**1** […] And the staircase which [goes up at its side] is four cubits wide, and goes round ^t-1

**2** [and up to a height of two rods, up to … Blank] Blank ^t-2

**3** [And he brought me into the block and showed me the houses there, from one door] to another, ﬁfteen; eight from the side up to the corner, ^t-3

**4** [and seven from the corner up to the other porch.] Length of the houses: three rods, 21 (cubits) long; and their width: ^t-4

**5** [two rods, 14 cubits. And all the rooms the same.] Their height is two rods, 14 cubits. Their door is in the middle; ^t-5

**6** [it is two rods, 14 cubits, wide. And he measured the width of the middle of the houses and of the interior of …,] four [cubits]; length and height, one rod, seven cubits ^t-6

**7** [… The site is 19 cubits] in length and 12 cubits in width. The house ^t-7

**8** [has 22 beds, and there are 11 lattice windows above the beds.] At their side is the outer gutter. ^t-8

**9** [And he measured … from the window: height, two cubits; width, … cubits, and its thickness is the width of the wall. Heig]ht of its inner part: cubits ^t-9

**10** [… and of the other … cubits … and he measured the edges of the platforms: ninete]en [cubits long] and [twelve] cubits wide. ^t-10

**11** [… and their height … they open …] two rods; cubits, four- ^t-11

**12** [teen; their *[p. 1113]* width is three cubits and they are ten (cubits) long …] one and a half [cu]bits, and its height within ^t-12

**13** […] … the roof which is over them ^t-13
