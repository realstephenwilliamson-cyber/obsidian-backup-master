## Chapter 8

**8:1** Bildad the Shuhite said in reply: ^8-1

**8:2** How long will you speak such things?  
Your utterances are a mighty wind! ^8-2

**8:3** Will God pervert the right?  
Will the Almighty pervert justice? ^8-3

**8:4** If your sons sinned against Him,  
He dispatched them for their transgression. ^8-4

**8:5** But if you seek God  
And supplicate the Almighty, ^8-5

**8:6** If you are blameless and upright,  
He will protect you,  
And grant well-being to your righteous home. ^8-6

**8:7** Though your beginning be small,  
In the end you will grow very great. ^8-7

**8:8** Ask the generation past,  
Study what their fathers have searched out— ^8-8

**8:9** For we are of yesterday and know nothing;  
Our days on earth are a shadow— ^8-9

**8:10** Surely they will teach you and tell you,  
Speaking out of their understanding. ^8-10

**8:11** Can papyrus thrive without marsh?  
Can rushes grow without water? ^8-11

**8:12** While still tender, not yet plucked,  
They would wither before any other grass. ^8-12

**8:13** Such is the fate of all who forget God;  
The hope of the impious man comes to naught— ^8-13

**8:14** Whose confidence is a thread of gossamer,[^1]  
Whose trust is a spider’s web. ^8-14

**8:15** He leans on his house—it will not stand;  
He seizes hold of it, but it will not hold. ^8-15

**8:16** He stays fresh even in the sun;  
His shoots spring up in his garden; ^8-16

**8:17** His roots are twined around a heap,  
They take hold of a house of stones.[^2] ^8-17

**8:18** When he is uprooted from his place,  
It denies him, [saying,]  
“I never saw you.” ^8-18

**8:19** Such is his happy lot;  
And from the earth others will grow. ^8-19

**8:20** Surely God does not despise the blameless;  
He gives no support to evildoers. ^8-20

**8:21** He will yet fill your mouth with laughter,  
And your lips with shouts of joy. ^8-21

**8:22** Your enemies will be clothed in disgrace;  
The tent of the wicked will vanish. ^8-22

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
