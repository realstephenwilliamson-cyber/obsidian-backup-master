# Numbers 23 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Numbers 22|← Numbers 22]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Numbers 24|Numbers 24 →]]

**23:1** Then Balaam said to Balak, “Build me seven altars here, and prepare seven bulls and seven rams for me.” ^23-1

**23:2** Balak did as Balaam had said and offered^[Heb mss Gk: MT reads *Balak and Balaam offered*] a bull and a ram on each altar. ^23-2

**23:3** Then Balaam said to Balak, “Stay here beside your burnt offerings while I go aside. Perhaps the LORD will come to meet me. Whatever he shows me I will tell you.” And he went to a bare height. ^23-3

**23:4** Then God met Balaam, and Balaam^[Heb *he*] said to him, “I have arranged the seven altars and have offered a bull and a ram on each altar.” ^23-4

**23:5** The LORD put a word in Balaam’s mouth and said, “Return to Balak, and this is what you must say.” ^23-5

**23:6** So he returned to Balak,^[Heb *him*] who was standing beside his burnt offerings with all the officials of Moab. ^23-6

**23:7** Then Balaam^[Heb *he*] uttered his oracle, saying, “Balak has brought me from Aram, the king of Moab from the eastern mountains: ‘Come, curse Jacob for me. Come, denounce Israel!’ ^23-7

**23:8** How can I curse whom God has not cursed? How can I denounce those whom the LORD has not denounced? ^23-8

**23:9** For from the top of the crags I see him; from the hills I behold him. Here is a people living alone and not reckoning itself among the nations! ^23-9

**23:10** Who can count the dust of Jacob or number the dust cloud^[Or *fourth part*] of Israel? Let me die the death of the upright, and let my end be like his!” ^23-10

**23:11** Then Balak said to Balaam, “What have you done to me? I brought you to curse my enemies, but now you have done nothing but bless them.” ^23-11

**23:12** He answered, “Must I not take care to say what the LORD puts into my mouth?” ^23-12

## Balaam’s second oracle

**23:13** So Balak said to him, “Come with me to another place from which you may see them; you shall see only part of them and shall not see them all; then curse them for me from there.” ^23-13

**23:14** So he took him to the field of Zophim, to the top of Pisgah. He built seven altars and offered a bull and a ram on each altar. ^23-14

**23:15** Balaam^[Heb *he*] said to Balak, “Stand here beside your burnt offerings, while I meet the LORD over there.” ^23-15

**23:16** The LORD met Balaam, put a word into his mouth, and said, “Return to Balak, and this is what you shall say.” ^23-16

**23:17** When he came to him, he was standing beside his burnt offerings with the officials of Moab. Balak said to him, “What has the LORD said?” ^23-17

**23:18** Then Balaam^[Heb *he*] uttered his oracle, saying, “Rise, Balak, and hear; listen to me, O son of Zippor: ^23-18

**23:19** God is not a human being, that he should lie, or a mortal, that he should change his mind. Has he promised, and will he not do it? Has he spoken, and will he not fulfill it? ^23-19

**23:20** See, I received a command to bless; he has blessed, and I cannot revoke it. ^23-20

**23:21** He has not beheld misfortune in Jacob, nor has he seen trouble in Israel. The LORD their God is with them, acclaimed as a king among them. ^23-21

**23:22** God, who brings them out of Egypt, is like the horns of a wild ox for them. ^23-22

**23:23** Surely there is no enchantment against Jacob, no divination against Israel; now it shall be said of Jacob and Israel, ‘See what God has done!’ ^23-23

**23:24** Look, a people rising up like a lioness and rousing itself like a lion! It does not lie down until it has eaten the prey and drunk the blood of the slain.” ^23-24

**23:25** Then Balak said to Balaam, “Do not curse them at all, and do not bless them at all.” ^23-25

**23:26** But Balaam answered Balak, “Did I not speak to you, saying, ‘Whatever the LORD says, that is what I must do’?” ^23-26

**23:27** So Balak said to Balaam, “Come now, I will take you to another place; perhaps it will please God that you may curse them for me from there.” ^23-27

**23:28** So Balak took Balaam to the top of Peor, which overlooks the wasteland.^[Or *overlooks Jeshimon*] ^23-28

**23:29** Balaam said to Balak, “Build me seven altars here, and prepare seven bulls and seven rams for me.” ^23-29

**23:30** So Balak did as Balaam had said and offered a bull and a ram on each altar. ^23-30
