# Hebrews 5 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Hebrews 04|← Hebrews 4]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Hebrews 06|Hebrews 6 →]]

Compare: [[Hebrews 05|BSB and SBLGNT]]

**5:1** For every high priest taken from among men, is ordained for men in the things that appertain to God, that he may offer up gifts and sacrifices for sins: ^5-1

**5:2** Who can have compassion on them that are ignorant and that err: because he himself also is compassed with infirmity. ^5-2

**5:3** And therefore he ought, as for the people, so also for himself, to offer for sins. ^5-3

**5:4** Neither doth any man take the honour to himself, but he that is called by God, as Aaron was. ^5-4

**5:5** So Christ also did not glorify himself, that he might be made a high priest: but he that said unto him: Thou art my Son, this day have I begotten thee. ^5-5

**5:6** As he saith also in another place: Thou art a priest for ever, according to the order of Melchisedech. ^5-6

**5:7** Who in the days of his flesh, with a strong cry and tears, offering up prayers and supplications to him that was able to save him from death, was heard for his reverence. ^5-7

**5:8** And whereas indeed he was the Son of God, he learned obedience by the things which he suffered: ^5-8

**5:9** And being consummated, he became, to all that obey him, the cause of eternal salvation. ^5-9

**5:10** Called by God a high priest according to the order of Melchisedech. ^5-10

**5:11** Of whom we have much to say, and hard to be intelligibly uttered: because you are become weak to hear. ^5-11

**5:12** For whereas for the time you ought to be masters, you have need to be taught again what are the first elements of the words of God: and you are become such as have need of milk, and not of strong meat. ^5-12

**5:13** For every one that is a partaker of milk, is unskillful in the word of justice: for he is a little child. ^5-13

**5:14** But strong meat is for the perfect; for them who by custom have their senses exercised to the discerning of good and evil. ^5-14
