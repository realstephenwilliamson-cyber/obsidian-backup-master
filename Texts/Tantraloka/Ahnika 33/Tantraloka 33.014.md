## Mūla

देवीकान्ततदर्धौ दारुकहलिसोमनाथशर्माणः ।

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 07 - Chapter Thirty-Three#^pg193|p. 175]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=193>))
>
> (The powerholders worshipped in the wheel of twenty-four spokes are) Saṁvarta, Lakulin, Bhrgu, Sita, Baka, Khadgin, Pinākin, Bhujaga, Bali, Kāla, Dvi, Chagalāṇda, Śikhin, Śoṇa, Meṣa, Mīna, Tridandin, along with Āṣādhin, Devīkānta, Tadardha, Dāruka, Halin, Somanātha, and Śarman. (with 33.13–14ab)
>
> Jaya, Vijaya, Jayānta, Ajita, Sujaya, Jaya, Rudra, Kīrtana, Āvaha, and its Mūrti, Utsāhada, and Vardhana, Bala, Subala, Bhadrada, Āvahaka, Tadvān, Dātā, Īśa, Nandana, Samabhadra, Tanmūrti, Śivada, Sumanaḥ, Spṛhaṇaka, Durga, Kāla, called Bhadra, Ceto’nuga, Kauśika, Kāla, Viśva, Suśiva, and after them, Kopa. These are (Bhairavas) on (the wheel) with thirty-four spokes. By reading (their names) in the feminine, these are (their) powers. (with 33.14cd–17ab)
