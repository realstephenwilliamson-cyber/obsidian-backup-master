The Fourth *Āvaraṇa*: The 14 Worlds

The fourth *āvaraṇa* (*caturdhāvaraṇam*) is the 14 triangles (*caturdasara*). It is the *sarvasaubhāgyadāyaka cakra*, the “wheel that bestows all auspiciousness.”

The explosions that result in creation are best understood in three stages: (1) the explosion of the interface between the inner self and the outer worlds, as 

*[p. 389]* ^pg418

manifested by the *aṣṭakōṇa* (the seventh enclosure); (2) the explosion of the outer universe, manifested by the *antardasara* (the sixth enclosure); and (3) the explosion of the inner self—the ego, as manifested by the *bahirdasara* (or fifth enclosure). The mantra *blūm* means a taste of wonder. Looking at all the 14 worlds that have emerged from the Mother’s womb is what creates this sense of wonder and awe.
