# The Jewish Annotated New Testament (2nd ed.)

Amy-Jill Levine and Marc Zvi Brettler (eds.), *The Jewish Annotated New Testament*, second edition (Oxford University Press, 2017), converted on 13 October 2026 from Stephen's copy ([[Levine, Brettler (eds.) - The Jewish Annotated New Testament|catalog note]]). Everything but the Bible text: the introductions, the annotations, the shaded boxes, the essays, the tables and the glossary. The book prints the NRSV of 1989 with the NRSV's own lettered notes; those are left out here, because the [[NRSVue]] is in the vault. Read the annotations beside it, remembering that the update changed some wording (the annotations quote the 1989 text).

- **Annotations:** one note per chapter (`JANT Matthew 05`), 260 in all. Each section heading of the book (`5.1–7.29: The Sermon on the Mount`) is a heading here; each note starts with its key, linked to the verse in the NRSVue (`[[NRSVue Matthew 05#^5-3|3]]`). Every annotation has a block id (`[[JANT Matthew 05#^5-3]]`). The page of the book is given at each section heading.
- **Boxes** (the shaded essays inside the books) are callouts in the chapter where they stand, with their page.
- **Introductions, essays, tables, glossary:** one note each; each page starts with *[p. N]* (the printed page, which is what the book's cross-references cite: "see 'Genealogy,' p. 11") and a block id `^pgK` (K the page of the PDF, [[Levine, Brettler (eds.) - The Jewish Annotated New Testament (2017).pdf|PDF]]).
- **Keys as printed.** A few keys in the book are misprinted; they link where they point: Matthew 27 has a note "48" after 51; Romans 8 a note "9" after 28; 2 Corinthians 8 a note "5" that is about 8.15; Jude a note "7–18" for 17–18. Two keys printed with a colon (John "21:1–25", Revelation "11:1–14") were read as chapter and verse, and "7.14–21" in Matthew 17 as 17.14–21.
