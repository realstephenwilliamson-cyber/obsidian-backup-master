# τύπος

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · noun

**Dodson:** a figure, model, type — (originally: the mark of a blow, then a stamp struck by a die), (a) a figure; a copy, image, (b) a pattern, model, (c) a type, prefiguring something or somebody. (Strong's 5179; headword τύπος, ου, ὁ)
