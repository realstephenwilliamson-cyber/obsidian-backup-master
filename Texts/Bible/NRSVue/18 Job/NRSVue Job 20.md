# Job 20 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Job 19|← Job 19]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Job 21|Job 21 →]]

## Zophar speaks: Wickedness receives just

## Retribution

**20:1** Then Zophar the Naamathite answered: ^20-1

**20:2** “Listen! My thoughts urge me to answer because of the agitation within me. ^20-2

**20:3** I hear censure that insults me, and a spirit beyond my understanding answers me. ^20-3

**20:4** Do you not know this from of old, ever since mortals were placed on earth, ^20-4

**20:5** that the exulting of the wicked is short and the joy of the godless is but for a moment? ^20-5

**20:6** Even though they mount up high as the heavens and their head reaches to the clouds, ^20-6

**20:7** they will perish forever like their own dung; those who have seen them will say, ‘Where are they?’ ^20-7

**20:8** They will fly away like a dream and not be found; they will be chased away like a vision of the night. ^20-8

**20:9** The eye that saw them will see them no more, nor will their place behold them any longer. ^20-9

**20:10** Their children will seek the favor of the poor, and their hands will give back their wealth. ^20-10

**20:11** Their bodies, once full of youth, will lie down in the dust with them. ^20-11

**20:12** “Though wickedness is sweet in their mouth, though they hide it under their tongues, ^20-12

**20:13** though they are loath to let it go and hold it in their mouths, ^20-13

**20:14** yet their food is turned in their stomachs; it is the venom of asps within them. ^20-14

**20:15** They swallow down riches and vomit them up again; God casts them out of their bellies. ^20-15

**20:16** They will suck the poison of asps; the tongue of a viper will kill them. ^20-16

**20:17** They will not look on the rivers, the streams flowing with honey and curds. ^20-17

**20:18** They will give back the fruit of their toil and will not swallow it down; from the profit of their trading they will get no enjoyment. ^20-18

**20:19** For they have crushed and abandoned the poor; they have seized a house that they did not build. ^20-19

**20:20** “For they knew no quiet in their bellies; in their greed they let nothing escape. ^20-20

**20:21** There was nothing left after they had eaten; therefore their prosperity will not endure. ^20-21

**20:22** In full sufficiency they will be in distress; all the force of misery will come upon them. ^20-22

**20:23** To fill their belly to the full, God^[Heb *he*] will send his fierce anger into them and rain it upon them as their food. ^20-23

**20:24** They will flee from an iron weapon; a bronze arrow will strike them through. ^20-24

**20:25** It is drawn forth and comes out of their body, and the glittering point comes out of their gall; terrors come upon them. ^20-25

**20:26** Utter darkness is laid up for their treasures; a fire fanned by no one will devour them; what is left in their tent will be consumed. ^20-26

**20:27** The heavens will reveal their iniquity, and the earth will rise up against them. ^20-27

**20:28** The possessions of their house will be carried away, dragged off in the day of God’s^[Heb *his*] wrath. ^20-28

**20:29** This is the portion of the wicked from God, the heritage decreed for them by God.” ^20-29
