# 1 Enoch 28 (Charles)

[[1 Enoch 027|← 1 Enoch 27]] · [[1 Enoch|index]] · [[1 Enoch 029|1 Enoch 29 →]]

### XXVIII-XXXIII. Further Journey to the East.

**28:1** And thence I went 〚towards the east〛, into the midst 〚of the mountain range〛 of the desert, and I saw a wilderness and it was solitary, full of trees **and plants**. ^28-1

**28:2** 〚And〛 water gushed forth from above. ^28-2

**28:3** Rushing like a copious watercourse [which flowed] towards the north-west it caused **clouds** and dew to ascend on every side. ^28-3
