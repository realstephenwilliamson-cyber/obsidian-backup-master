## Mūla

मन्त्रास्तत्पतयः सेशा रूपस्थमिति कीर्त्यते ।
रूपातीतम्परा शक्तिः सव्यापाराप्यनामया ॥ ^mula

mantrās tatpatayaḥ seśā rūpastham iti kīrtyate |
rūpātītam parā śaktiḥ savyāpārāpy anāmayā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, October 2022 – December 2022
> p. 23 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=30>))
>
> (The wise) know that the other state (in which the creatures etc. are instruments of objectivization of the substratum consciousness) is (called) 'Abiding in Place' (*padastha*). The Mantras, their Lords (i.e the Lords of Mantra), along with (their) Lords (i.e., the Great Lords of Mantra), are said to be 'Abiding in Form' (*rūpastha*). The Supreme Power, which is stainless and active, is 'Beyond Form' (*rūpātīta*). Śiva should be known to be transcendent (*niṣprapañca*), unmanifest (*nirbhāsa*), pure, established in His own nature and beyond all (states and things). One is liberated when He is known.[^60] (with 2.40cd–42)

[^60]: MV 2/40d-42 is quoted with no variants in TĀ 10/284cd-286.
