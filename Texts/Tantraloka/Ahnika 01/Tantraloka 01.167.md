## Mūla

तदेतत्त्रिविधत्वं हि शास्त्रे श्रीपूर्वनामनि ।
आदेशि परमेशित्रा समावेशविनिर्णये ॥ ^mula

tadetattrividhatvaṃ hi śāstre śrīpūrvanāmani |
ādeśi parameśitrā samāveśavinirṇaye || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. I
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 01 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v01 10 - Chapter One, part 9#^pg354|p. 340]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 01.pdf#page=354>))

> This three-fold manner (of means to realisation) was taught by the Supreme Lord in the scripture called Śrīpūrva (i.e. the Maālinīvijayottara) in the course of explaining (the forms) of penetration (samāveśa) (into and by the supreme state).
