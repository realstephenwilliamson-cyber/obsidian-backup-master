# 2 Thessalonians 1 (JANT notes)

— · [[Jewish Annotated New Testament|JANT index]] · [[JANT 2 Thessalonians 02|2 Thessalonians 2 →]]

Text: [[NRSVue 2 Thessalonians 01|NRSVue]] · [[2 Thessalonians 01|BSB and SBLGNT]] · Introduction: [[JANT 2 Thessalonians - Introduction]]

### 1.1–3: Greeting and thanksgiving

*[[NRSVue 2 Thessalonians 01#^1-1|Text]], p. 428* · The conventional salutation and thanksgiving parallel, or copy, 1 Thess. ^1-1

**[[NRSVue 2 Thessalonians 01#^1-1|1]]:** *Silvanus*, sometimes regarded as the Silas of [[Acts 15#^15-22|Acts 15.22]],[[Acts 15#^15-40|40]]; [[Acts 17#^17-1|17.1]]. *Timothy*, Paul’s co-worker and emissary ([[Acts 16#^16-1|Acts 16.1–3]] and elsewhere; [[1 Corinthians 16#^16-10|1 Cor 16.10–11]]; [[Philippians 02#^2-19|Phil 2.19–24]]). ^1-1-2

**[[NRSVue 2 Thessalonians 01#^1-3|3]]:** *Love . . . for one another*, believers’ solidarity, dividing them from outsiders ([[John 13#^13-34|Jn 13.34]]; [[Philippians 01#^1-9|Phil 1.9]]). Qumran scrolls (1QS 5.1–20) and some rabbinic texts (*b. Pesah.* [[Pesachim 49#49b|49b]]) encourage tight bonds among the learned and separation from the unlearned. ^1-3

### 1.4–10: God’s righteous judgment

*[[NRSVue 2 Thessalonians 01#^1-4|Text]], p. 428* ^1-4

**[[NRSVue 2 Thessalonians 01#^1-4|4]]:** *Among the churches* suggests a network of Pauline congregations (1.7; [[Romans 16#^16-4|Rom 16.4]],[[Romans 16#^16-16|16]]; [[2 Corinthians 08#^8-1|2 Cor 8.1]]; [[1 Thessalonians 01#^1-7|1 Thess 1.7]],[[1 Thessalonians 01#^1-9|9]]). *Steadfastness and faith* emphasize not the content of faith but devotion (see [[Job 02#^2-9|Job 2.9–10]]; also see *b. Ber*. [[Berakhot 61#61b|61b]] on commitment to God and Torah despite Roman persecution). *Persecutions . . . afflictions*, state persecution of Jesus’ followers was sporadic until the empire-wide Decian persecution in 250. Whether the Thessalonians faced state persecution, local ostracism, economic hardship, etc., or whether the reference is conventional or drawing upon [[1 Thessalonians 02#^2-14|1 Thess 2.14b–16]] cannot be determined. ^1-4-2

**[[NRSVue 2 Thessalonians 01#^1-5|5]]:** *This is evidence*, this ambiguous phrase might refer to steadfastness and faith. Their afflictions may be seen as God’s punishment for sins, or perhaps God’s testing of the righteous who will be rewarded in the “kingdom of God.” Similar ideas appear in biblical ([[Proverbs 03#^3-12|Prov 3.12]]) and rabbinic (*b. Ber.* [[Berakhot 5#5a|5a]]: “sufferings of \[divine\] love”) sources. ^1-5

**[[NRSVue 2 Thessalonians 01#^1-6|6]]:** *Repay with affliction* when the faithful will be rewarded and their persecutors punished. Notions of eternal destruction emerge in late Second Temple texts ([[Daniel 12#^12-2|Dan 12.2]]; [[LXX 4 Maccabees 10#^10-15|4 Macc 10.15]]; 1QM 1.5; 1QS 2.14–15; 5.10–13; see also [[LXX 2 Maccabees 06#^6-12|2 Macc 6.12–16]]; *Pss. Sol.* 13.9–10; *2 Bar.* 13.3–10; cf. *Gen. Rab.* 33.1). ^1-6

**[[NRSVue 2 Thessalonians 01#^1-7|7]]:** *The Lord Jesus is revealed* as the agent of justice ([[Romans 02#^2-5|Rom 2.5]]). Similar expectations of a divinely sent redeemer who punishes Israel’s oppressors appear in *1 En.* 48.1–49.4; *4 Ezra* \[2 Esd\] 7.28; 13.32. *Mighty angels*, see [[Zechariah 14#^14-5|Zech 14.5]]; [[Psalms 078#^78-49|Ps 78.49]]; *1 En.* 54.6; 61.10; 1QM 1.10–11. ^1-7

**[[NRSVue 2 Thessalonians 01#^1-8|8]]:** *Flaming fire*, [[Isaiah 66#^66-15|Isa 66.15]]; [[Daniel 07#^7-9|Dan 7.9–10]]. *Vengeance*, some Jewish texts forecast God’s destruction of opponents (*Jub.* 21.21–24; 1QS 10.20; 4Q511 1–8; CD 8.1–19; *2 Bar.* 54.14-22; *Mek. Shirata* 6). *Who do not know God . . . who do not obey the gospel*, perhaps two groups of opponents: unevangelized pagans ([[Jeremiah 10#^10-25|Jer 10.25]]; [[Galatians 04#^4-8|Gal 4.8]]; [[1 Thessalonians 04#^4-5|1 Thess 4.5]]) and Jews who know but do not obey Christian teaching ([[Romans 10#^10-16|Rom 10.16–21]]). ^1-8

**[[NRSVue 2 Thessalonians 01#^1-9|9]]:** *Eternal destruction*, belief in eternal (sometimes post-mortem) punishment began to appear in late biblical ([[Isaiah 26#^26-19|Isa 26.19]]; [[Daniel 12#^12-2|Dan 12.2]]) and then Second Temple and rabbinic texts (*Pss. Sol.* 3.16; *1 En.* 53.2; *b. Shabb*. [[Shabbat 152#152b|152b]]). ^1-9

**[[NRSVue 2 Thessalonians 01#^1-10|10]]:** *Saints*, community members who *have believed*. ^1-10

### 1.11-12: Prayer

*[[NRSVue 2 Thessalonians 01#^1-11|Text]], p. 428* ^1-11

**[[NRSVue 2 Thessalonians 01#^1-11|11]]:** *Worthy of his call*, election language ([[Torah 45 - Vaetchanan#^7-6|Deut 7.6]]; [[Torah 50 - Ki Tavo#^26-17|26.17]]; [[Isaiah 41#^41-9|Isa 41.9]]; [[Romans 09#^9-6|Rom 9.6–7]]; [[Romans 11#^11-29|11.29]]; [[Ephesians 01#^1-18|Eph 1.18]]; *Jub.* 2.9; 15.30–31; [[1 Peter 02#^2-9|1 Pet 2.9]]; cf. [[2 Thessalonians 02#^2-13|2 Thess 2.13]]). The author recalls the believers’ chosenness in order to demand good works and faith. Jewish texts link election with demands for Torah-obedience ([[Joshua 24#^24-22|Josh 24.22]]; [[Psalms 105#^105-45|Ps 105.45]]; *Pss. Sol.* 9–11; *Jub.* 2.17–22; *m. Avot* 3.17 on the demand that Israel, God’s beloved, not forsake the Torah). ^1-11-2

**[[NRSVue 2 Thessalonians 01#^1-12|12]]:** *Name*, possibly referring to [[LXX Isaiah 66#^66-5|Isa 66.5]]\[LXX\] (“so that the name of the Lord may be glorified”), with “Lord” referring to Jesus rather than to God. ^1-12
