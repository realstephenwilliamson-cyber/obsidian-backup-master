# Guide for the Perplexed (Friedländer)

Maimonides' *Guide for the Perplexed* in Michael Friedländer's translation (1903, public domain), one note per chapter (183 notes). Sefaria describes it as "Maimonides’ 12th-century philosophical masterwork, written as a letter to his student demonstrating the compatibility of Judaism and philosophy." Verse references are linked where Sefaria links the same verse to that chapter.
