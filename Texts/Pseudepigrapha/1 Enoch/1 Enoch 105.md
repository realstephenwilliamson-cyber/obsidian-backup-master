# 1 Enoch 105 (Charles)

[[1 Enoch 104|← 1 Enoch 104]] · [[1 Enoch|index]] · [[1 Enoch 106|1 Enoch 106 →]]

## CV. God and the Messiah to dwell with Man.

**105:1** In those days the Lord bade (them) to summon and testify to the children of earth concerning their wisdom: Show (it) unto them; for ye are their guides, and a recompense over the whole earth. ^105-1

**105:2** For I and My Son will be united with them for ever in the paths of uprightness in their lives; and ye shall have peace: rejoice, ye children of uprightness. Amen. ^105-2
