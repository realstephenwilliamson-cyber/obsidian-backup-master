## Mūla

ततो ब्रह्माण्डमध्येऽपि ज्ञानशक्तिर्विभो रविः ।
दिशां विभागं कुरुते प्रकाशघनवृत्तिमान् ॥ ^mula

tato brahmāṇḍamadhye'pi jñānaśaktirvibho raviḥ |
diśāṃ vibhāgaṃ kurute prakāśaghanavṛttimān || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 05 - Chapter Fifteen, part 4#^pg148|p. 130]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=148>))

> Thus, the pervasive Lord’s power of knowledge is the Sun, that shines intensely in the centre of the Egg of Brahmā, dividing up the directions.
