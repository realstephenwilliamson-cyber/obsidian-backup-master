## Mūla

विज्ञानाकल एवात्र ततो मातापकञ्चुकः ।
मायानिविष्टेऽप्यकले तथेत्येकादशात्मता ॥ ^mula

vijñānākala evātra tato mātāpakañcukaḥ |
māyāniviṣṭe'pyakale tathetyekādaśātmatā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 11 - Chapter Ten, part 2#^pg384|p. 365]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=384>))
>
> When the principle of Māyā is the object of knowledge, (the subject in whom) the coverings are in abeyance also becomes an object of knowledge. As Māyā (at this level) is the unity of the coverings (fused together within it), extremely subtle. Thus, the subject here is ijñā 13 who is free of coverings. (The object of knowledge for him) is the Dissolution Deconditioned (pralayākala) subject, who is immersed in Māyā. In this way, it consists of eleven (aspects). (with 10.108–109)
