# ἔρημος

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · adjective

**Dodson:** deserted, desolate, a desert — as an adj: deserted, desolate, waste; hence: the desert, to the east and south of Palestine; of a person: deserted, abandoned, desolate. (Strong's 2048; headword ἔρημος, ον)
