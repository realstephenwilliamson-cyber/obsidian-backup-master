# παρακαλέω

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · verb

**Dodson:** I summon, entreat, admonish, comfort — (a) I send for, summon, invite, (b) I beseech, entreat, beg, (c) I exhort, admonish, (d) I comfort, encourage, console. (Strong's 3870; headword παρακαλέω)
