## Chapter 9

**9:1** For the leader; *‘almuth labben*.[^1] A psalm of David. ^9-1

**9:2** I will praise You, LORD, with all my heart;  
I will tell all Your wonders. ^9-2

**9:3** I will rejoice and exult in You,  
singing a hymn to Your name, O Most High. ^9-3

**9:4** When my enemies retreat,  
they stumble to their doom at Your presence. ^9-4

**9:5** For You uphold my right and claim,  
enthroned as righteous judge. ^9-5

**9:6** You blast the nations;  
You destroy the wicked;  
You blot out their name forever. ^9-6

**9:7** The enemy is no more—  
ruins everlasting;  
You have torn down their cities;  
their very names are lost.[^2] ^9-7

**9:8** But the LORD abides forever;  
He has set up His throne for judgment; ^9-8

**9:9** it is He who judges the world with righteousness,  
rules the peoples with equity. ^9-9

**9:10** The LORD is a haven for the oppressed,  
a haven in times of trouble. ^9-10

**9:11** Those who know Your name trust You,  
for You do not abandon those who turn to You, O LORD. ^9-11

**9:12** Sing a hymn to the LORD, who reigns in Zion;[^3]  
declare His deeds among the peoples. ^9-12

**9:13** For He does not ignore the cry of the afflicted;  
He who requites bloodshed is mindful of them.[^4] ^9-13

**9:14** Have mercy on me, O LORD;  
see my affliction at the hands of my foes,  
You who lift me from the gates of death, ^9-14

**9:15** so that in the gates of Fair Zion[^5]  
I might tell all Your praise,  
I might exult in Your deliverance. ^9-15

**9:16** The nations sink in the pit they have made;  
their own foot is caught in the net they have hidden. ^9-16

**9:17** The LORD has made Himself known:  
He works judgment;  
the wicked man is snared by his own devices. *Higgaion*.[^6] *Selah*. ^9-17

**9:18** Let the wicked be[^7] in Sheol,  
all the nations who ignore God! ^9-18

**9:19** Not always shall the needy be ignored,  
nor the hope of the afflicted forever lost. ^9-19

**9:20** Rise, O LORD!  
Let not men have power;  
let the nations be judged in Your presence. ^9-20

**9:21** Strike fear into them,[^8] O LORD;  
let the nations know they are only men. *Selah*. ^9-21

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain; some mss. and ancient versions, ‘al muth labben, as though “over the death of the son.”
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Or “O You who dwell in Zion.”
[^4]: Order of Hebrew clauses inverted for clarity.
[^5]: Lit. “the Daughter of Zion.”
[^6]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^7]: Others “return to.”
[^8]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
