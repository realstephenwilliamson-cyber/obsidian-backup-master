## Chapter Sixteen

The Blessed Lord spoke:

\[1\] “Being fearless and pure of heart,
persevering in Jnana *Yoga*,
charity, restraint, and sacrifice,
study, ardor, and uprightness,

\[2\] Nonviolence, non-calumny,
calmness, truth, renunciation,
compassion, freedom from desire,
kindness, modesty, steadiness,

\[3\] Power, patience, strength, purity,
freedom from malice and from pride—
these are the traits of those destined
to be divine, O Arjuna.

\[4\] Hypocrisy, arrogance, deceit,
anger, harshness, and ignorance—
these are the traits of those destined
to be demonic, O Arjuna.

\[5\] Divine destiny liberates,
demonic destiny imprisons.
Do not worry! You are destined
to be divine, O Arjuna.

\[6\] So in this world there are two kinds
of created beings. The divine
has just been thoroughly explained.
Now hear from me the demonic.

[p. 88] \[7\] Demonic beings do not know
when to act and when not to act.
Purity is not found in them,
nor is truth or good behavior.

\[8\] They say that the world is not real,
has no foundation, has no God,
that God has not produced the world.
What then? The world is caused by lust.

\[9\] Holding this view, these lost souls,
men of little intelligence,
evil actors, come forth, enemies
intent on this world’s destruction.

\[10\] Insatiable in their desires,
hypocritical and arrogant,
holding false ideas, deluded,
they proceed with impure purpose.

\[11\] Clinging to immense anxiety
that ends when they dissolve in death;
fulfilling desire their highest goal,
convinced that this is all there is;

\[12\] Bound by a hundred snares of hope,
devoted to desire and anger,
they strive to gratify desire
with hoards of wealth unjustly gained.

\[13\] ‘This is what I obtained today;
I will gratify this desire;
this is now mine, and that also
will be added to my riches;

\[14\] That enemy has been killed by me,
and I will kill others as well;
[p. 89] I am a lord, and I enjoy
success, power, and happiness;

\[15\] I am wealthy and highborn.
Who else is my equal? I will
offer sacrifice, donate, rejoice.’
This is how they are deluded.

\[16\] Carried away by many dreams,
trapped in a net of delusion,
living to fulfill their desires,
they fall into a hell most foul.

\[17\] Self-centered, stubborn, arrogant
with pride of wealth, they sacrifice
in name only, hypocritically,
not according to precedent.

\[18\] Egotistic and insolent,
attached to force, desire, anger,
out of envy they despise Me
in their own and others’ bodies.

\[19\] They hate, they are cruel, they are vile
and vicious men. I am always
hurling them into demons’ wombs
in cycles of transmigrations.

\[20\] Entering the wombs of demons,
deluded in birth after birth,
and never, Arjuna, reaching Me,
they go to the worst place of all.

\[21\] There is a triple gate to hell
that is destructive of the self:
these are desire, greed, and anger.
Therefore one should renounce all three.

[p. 90] \[22\] If when released, Son of Kunti,
from this triple *tamasic* gate
one does what is best for the self,
then he reaches the highest goal.

\[23\] One who ignores scriptural rules
and acts according to desire
never will attain perfection,
nor happiness, nor the highest goal.

\[24\] So let scripture set your standard
for what may and may not be done.
Knowing what the scriptures prescribe
should guide your actions in this world.”
