# Likutei Moharan (Mykoff)

*Likutei Moharan* in Moshe Mykoff's translation (Breslov Research Institute), following Sefaria's structure: one note per lesson, Part I (lessons 1–286) and Part II (lessons 1–125), with the lesson's numbered sections as headings. Sefaria describes it as "Rebbe Nachman of Breslov’s magnum opus, containing interpretations of talmudic stories and of parts of the Zohar, among other sermons." Scripture references in the text are linked where Sefaria links the same verse to that lesson. Sefaria's English has the translation only, not the Breslov Research Institute's notes; *A Pleasant Song* and *Additions from Manuscript* have no English on Sefaria.
