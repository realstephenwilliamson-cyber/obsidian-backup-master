## Mūla

ते मेरुगाः सकृच्छम्भुं ये वार्चन्ति यथोचितम् ।
मेरोः प्रदक्षिणाप्योदग्दिक्षु विष्कम्भपर्वताः ॥ ^mula

te merugāḥ sakṛcchambhuṃ ye vārcanti yathocitam |
meroḥ pradakṣiṇāpyodagdikṣu viṣkambhaparvatāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 04 - Chapter Eight, part 2#^pg126|p. 108]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=126>))
>
> The natives of the sacred land of India (bhāratavarṣa) who are devoted to cherished meritorious works (isfapūrta),), and those who worship Śiva (even) once in the proper manner, go to mount Meru. (with 8.57cd–58ab)
>
> (Around mount) Meru, (moving in a clockwise direction) from east to north, are the mountains that support it (viṣkambha). (These are,) mount Mandara, Gandhamāda, Vipula and Supārśvaka. White, yellow, blue and red, (respectively), these mountains are at the feet (of Mount Meru). Having fixed (itself into) the earth by means of them, it stands (erect and) immobile. (with 8.58cd–60ab)
