# ἀγαπητός

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · adjective

**Dodson:** loved, beloved — loved, beloved, with two special applications: the Beloved, a title of the Messiah (Christ), as beloved beyond all others by the God who sent Him; of Christians, as beloved by God, Christ, and one another. (Strong's 27; headword ἀγαπητός, ή, όν)
