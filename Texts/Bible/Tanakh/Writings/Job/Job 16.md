# Job 16

Compare: [[NRSVue Job 16|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 16

**16:1** Job said in reply: ^16-1

**16:2** I have often heard such things;  
You are all mischievous comforters. ^16-2

**16:3** Have windy words no limit?  
What afflicts you that you speak on? ^16-3

**16:4** I would also talk like you  
If you were in my place;  
I would barrage you with words,  
I would wag my head over you. ^16-4

**16:5** I would encourage you with words,[^1]  
My moving lips would bring relief. ^16-5

**16:6** If I speak, my pain will not be relieved,  
And if I do not—what have I lost? ^16-6

**16:7** Now He has truly worn me out;  
You have destroyed my whole community. ^16-7

**16:8** You have shriveled me;  
My gauntness serves as a witness,  
And testifies against me. ^16-8

**16:9** In His anger He tears and persecutes me;  
He gnashes His teeth at me;  
My foe stabs me with his eyes. ^16-9

**16:10** They open wide their mouths at me;  
Reviling me, they strike my cheeks;  
They inflame themselves against me. ^16-10

**16:11** God hands me over to an evil man,  
Thrusts me into the clutches of the wicked. ^16-11

**16:12** I had been untroubled, and He broke me in pieces;  
He took me by the scruff and shattered me;  
He set me up as His target; ^16-12

**16:13** His bowmen surrounded me;  
He pierced my kidneys; He showed no mercy;  
He spilled my bile onto the ground. ^16-13

**16:14** He breached me, breach after breach;  
He rushed at me like a warrior. ^16-14

**16:15** I sewed sackcloth over my skin;  
I buried my glory[^2] in the dust. ^16-15

**16:16** My face is red with weeping;  
Darkness covers my eyes ^16-16

**16:17** For no injustice on my part  
And for the purity of my prayer![^3] ^16-17

**16:18** Earth, do not cover my blood;  
Let there be no resting place for my outcry! ^16-18

**16:19** Surely now my witness is in heaven;  
He who can testify for me is on high. ^16-19

**16:20** O my advocates, my fellows,  
Before God my eyes shed tears; ^16-20

**16:21** Let Him arbitrate between a man and God  
As between a man and his fellow. ^16-21

**16:22** For a few more years will pass,  
And I shall go the way of no return. ^16-22

[^1]: Lit. “my mouth.”
[^2]: Lit. “made my horn enter into.”
[^3]: Or “Though I did no injustice, / And my prayer was pure.”
