## Mūla

सिद्धयो भाववैमल्यं तथापि निखिलोत्तमम् ।
विमलीभूतहृदयो यत्तत्र प्रतिबिम्बयेत् ॥ ^mula

siddhayo bhāvavaimalyaṃ tathāpi nikhilottamam |
vimalībhūtahṛdayo yattatra pratibimbayet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 03 - Chapter Fifteen, part 2#^pg93|p. 75]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=93>))
>
> Even though (some) accomplishments (may be the result) of a combination (of factors, spatial and temporal,) in accord with the power of Necessity (niyati), the purity of one’s disposition (bhāvavaimalya) is still the most excellent of all (of them). (with 15.110cd–111ab)
>
> A person whose heart is pure should cause (the Deity who is) the goal to be achieved (sādhya) to be reflected there (within it). Its stability (dārḍhya) (there) serves to make (his practice) fruitful. (with 15.111cd–112ab)
