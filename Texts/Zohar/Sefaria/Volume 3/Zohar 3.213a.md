# Zohar 3.213a

← [[Zohar 3.212b]] · [[Zohar (Soncino)]] · [[Zohar 3.213b]] →

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 9.11 - Parashat Pinehas#^z3-213a|Parashat Pineḥas, at 3:213a]]

*Volume 3, folio 213a* · Pinchas

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Pinchas 1:1–2:5.*

**Pinchas 2:6** [^1]PHINEAS THE SON OF ELEAZAR THE SON OF AARON THE PRIEST. R. Simeon said: ‘Israel deserved to be destroyed at that time, and were only saved by that deed of Phineas.’ ... ^pinchas-2-6

**Pinchas 2:7** Said R. Simeon: ‘If a man receives a soul in the course of its transmigration and it does not properly fit into him, he as it were belies his trust to the King, and I apply to him the verse: “If he find something lost and deny it and swear falsely” (Lev. 6, 3). ^pinchas-2-7

**Pinchas 3:8** We have learnt that a completely righteous man is not thrust aside, but one who is not completely righteous may be thrust aside. A completely righteous man is one who does not receive migratory souls out of their course, who builds on his own inheritance, and digs his wells and plants his trees there. ^pinchas-3-8

**Pinchas 3:9** The righteous one who is not completely so builds on another’s inheritance: he labours but does not know if what he produces will remain his. In respect of himself he is called good and righteous, but not in respect of that inheritance. ^pinchas-3-9

**Pinchas 3:10** He is like a man who builds a fair building, but when he examines the foundation he finds that it is sloping and crooked. His building in itself is good, but in respect of its foundation it is bad, and therefore is not called a perfect building. ^pinchas-3-10

[^1]: From the beginning of the section *Pinhas* to the end of the Zohar, a large portion of the text in the original is taken up by the *Ray’a Mehemna*; hence the frequency of the omissions in the translation. On the section *Mattoth* there is only a short piece of Zohar of too allusive a nature to be made intelligible in a translation. There is no Zohar at all on the sections *Mas’e, Debarim, Ekeb, Re’eh, Shofetim, Ki Teze, Ki Tabo, Nizabim*, and *Vezoth Haberakah*.
