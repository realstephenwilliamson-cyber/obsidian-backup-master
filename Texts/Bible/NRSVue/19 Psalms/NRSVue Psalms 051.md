# Psalm 51 (NRSVue)

## Prayer for cleansing and pardon

*To the leader. A Psalm of David, when the prophet Nathan came to him, after he had gone in to*

*Bathsheba.*

**51:1** Have mercy on me, O God, according to your steadfast love; according to your abundant mercy, blot out my transgressions. ^51-1

**51:2** Wash me thoroughly from my iniquity, and cleanse me from my sin. ^51-2

**51:3** For I know my transgressions, and my sin is ever before me. ^51-3

**51:4** Against you, you alone, have I sinned and done what is evil in your sight, so that you are justified in your sentence and blameless when you pass judgment. ^51-4

**51:5** Indeed, I was born guilty, a sinner when my mother conceived me. ^51-5

**51:6** You desire truth in the inward being;^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] therefore teach me wisdom in my secret heart. ^51-6

**51:7** Purge me with hyssop, and I shall be clean; wash me, and I shall be whiter than snow. ^51-7

**51:8** Let me hear joy and gladness; let the bones that you have crushed rejoice. ^51-8

**51:9** Hide your face from my sins, and blot out all my iniquities. ^51-9

**51:10** Create in me a clean heart, O God, and put a new and right^[Or *steadfast*] spirit within me. ^51-10

**51:11** Do not cast me away from your presence, and do not take your holy spirit from me. ^51-11

**51:12** Restore to me the joy of your salvation, and sustain in me a willing^[Or *generous*] spirit. ^51-12

**51:13** Then I will teach transgressors your ways, and sinners will return to you. ^51-13

**51:14** Deliver me from bloodshed, O God, O God of my salvation, and my tongue will sing aloud of your deliverance. ^51-14

**51:15** O Lord, open my lips, and my mouth will declare your praise. ^51-15

**51:16** For you have no delight in sacrifice; if I were to give a burnt offering, you would not be pleased. ^51-16

**51:17** The sacrifice acceptable to God^[Or *My sacrifice, O God,*] is a broken spirit; a broken and contrite heart, O God, you will not despise. ^51-17

**51:18** Do good to Zion in your good pleasure; rebuild the walls of Jerusalem; ^51-18

**51:19** then you will delight in right sacrifices, in burnt offerings and whole burnt offerings; then bulls will be offered on your altar. ^51-19
