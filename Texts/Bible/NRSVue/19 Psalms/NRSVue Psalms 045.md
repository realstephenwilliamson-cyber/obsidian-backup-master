# Psalm 45 (NRSVue)

## Ode for a royal wedding

*To the leader: according to Lilies. Of the Korahites. A Maskil. A love song.*

**45:1** My heart overflows with a goodly theme; I address my verses to the king; my tongue is like the pen of a ready scribe. ^45-1

**45:2** You are the most handsome of men; grace is poured upon your lips; therefore God has blessed you forever. ^45-2

**45:3** Gird your sword on your thigh, O mighty one, in your glory and majesty. ^45-3

**45:4** In your majesty ride on victoriously for the cause of truth and to defend^[Cn: Heb *and the meekness of*] the right; let your right hand teach you dread deeds. ^45-4

**45:5** Your arrows are sharp in the heart of the king’s enemies; the peoples fall under you. ^45-5

**45:6** Your throne, O God,^[Or *Your throne is a throne of God, it*] endures forever and ever. Your royal scepter is a scepter of equity; ^45-6

**45:7** you love righteousness and hate wickedness. Therefore God, your God, has anointed you with the oil of gladness beyond your companions; ^45-7

**45:8** your robes are all fragrant with myrrh and aloes and cassia. From ivory palaces stringed instruments make you glad; ^45-8

**45:9** daughters of kings are among your ladies of honor; at your right hand stands the queen in gold of Ophir. ^45-9

**45:10** Hear, O daughter, consider and incline your ear; forget your people and your father’s house, ^45-10

**45:11** and the king will desire your beauty. Since he is your lord, bow to him; ^45-11

**45:12** Daughter Tyre will seek your favor with gifts, the richest of the people ^45-12

**45:13** with all kinds of wealth. The princess is decked in her chamber with gold-woven robes;^[Or *people.13All glorious is the princess within, gold embroidery is her clothing*] ^45-13

**45:14** in many-colored robes she is led to the king; behind her the virgins, her companions, follow. ^45-14

**45:15** With joy and gladness they are led along as they enter the palace of the king. ^45-15

**45:16** In the place of ancestors you, O king,^[Heb lacks *O king*] shall have sons; you will make them princes in all the earth. ^45-16

**45:17** I will cause your name to be celebrated in all generations; therefore the peoples will praise you forever and ever. ^45-17
