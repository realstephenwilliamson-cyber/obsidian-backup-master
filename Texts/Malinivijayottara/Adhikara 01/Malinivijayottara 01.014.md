## Mūla

मयाप्येतत्पुरा प्राप्तमघोरात्परमात्मनः ।
उपादेयं च हेयं च विज्ञेयं परमार्थतः ॥ ^mula

mayāpy etat purā prāptam aghorāt paramātmanaḥ |
upādeyaṁ ca heyaṁ ca vijñeyaṁ paramārthataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2022 – June 2022
> p. 41 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=17>))
>
> I too obtained that in the past from Aghora, the supreme Self. One should (first of all) know thoroughly, as it truly is (*paramārthataḥ*), what should be adopted and what should be abandoned. It is said that this group of six should be adopted by those who desire (good) fruits, namely, Śiva and Śakti, along with the Vidyeśas, the Mantra (perceivers) and the Mantreśvara souls. (with 1.14–15)
