## Israel’s god—judge of all the earth

*To the leader: with stringed instruments. A Psalm of Asaph. A Song.*

**76:1** In Judah God is known; his name is great in Israel. ^76-1

**76:2** His abode has been established in Salem, his dwelling place in Zion. ^76-2

**76:3** There he broke the flashing arrows, the shield, the sword, and the weapons of war. *Selah* ^76-3

**76:4** Glorious are you, more majestic than the everlasting mountains.^[Gk: Heb *the mountains of prey*] ^76-4

**76:5** The courageous were stripped of their spoil; they sank into sleep; none of the troops was able to lift a hand. ^76-5

**76:6** At your rebuke, O God of Jacob, both rider and horse lay stunned. ^76-6

**76:7** But you indeed are awesome! Who can stand before you when your anger is roused? ^76-7

**76:8** From the heavens you uttered judgment; the earth feared and was still ^76-8

**76:9** when God rose up to establish judgment, to save all the oppressed of the earth. *Selah* ^76-9

**76:10** Human wrath serves only to praise you, when you bind the last bit of your^[Heb lacks *your*] wrath around you. ^76-10

**76:11** Make vows to the LORD your God and perform them; let all who are around him bring gifts to the one who is awesome, ^76-11

**76:12** who cuts off the spirit of princes, who inspires fear in the kings of the earth. ^76-12
