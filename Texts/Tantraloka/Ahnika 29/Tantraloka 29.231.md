## Mūla

तात्पर्यमस्य पादस्य स सिद्धीः संप्रयच्छति ।
गुरुर्यः साधकः प्राक्स्यादन्यो मोक्षं ददात्यलम् ॥ ^mula

tātparyamasya pādasya sa siddhīḥ saṃprayacchati |
gururyaḥ sādhakaḥ prāksyādanyo mokṣaṃ dadātyalam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 13 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 5#^pg392|p. 375]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=392>))
>
> The implicit sense of this quarter verse (pāda) is that the teacher who was previously an adept bestows (yogic) accomplishments, whereas the other (who is a teacher from the start) is capable of giving liberation. (Even so, their teacher) should impart all the knowledge of three kinds to both of them, and give his own consent djfid@)) to the performance of their own (respective) rite (of initiation). (with 29.231–232)
