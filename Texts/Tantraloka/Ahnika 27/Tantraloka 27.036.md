## Mūla

दीक्षायां मुख्यतो मन्त्रांस्तान्पञ्चदश दैशिकः ।
यदि वा तत्त्वभुवनकलामन्त्रपदार्णजैः ॥ ^mula

dīkṣāyāṃ mukhyato mantrāṃstānpañcadaśa daiśikaḥ |
yadi vā tattvabhuvanakalāmantrapadārṇajaiḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 18 - Chapter Twenty-Seven#^pg434|p. 414]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=434>))
>
> The teacher should deposit both the two (that is, Mātrkā as well as Mālinī) on the most excellent (rosary which is made of one hundred and fifteen beads,) after having deposited the fifteen Mantras taught previously as the main ones for the rite of initiation. (with 27.35cd–36ab)
>
> Or if the rosary is made (of beads) numbering the principles, worlds, Forces, Mantras, parts of Mantras (pada) or phonemes, the teacher should perform the corresponding deposition (onto it). (with 27.36cd–37ab)
