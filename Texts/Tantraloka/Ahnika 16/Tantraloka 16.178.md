## Mūla

भोगश्च सद्य उत्क्रान्त्या देहेनैवाथ सङ्गतः ।
तदैवाभ्यासतो वापि देहान्ते वेत्यसौ चतुः ॥ ^mula

bhogaśca sadya utkrāntyā dehenaivātha saṅgataḥ |
tadaivābhyāsato vāpi dehānte vetyasau catuḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 06 - Chapter Sixteen, part 2#^pg98|p. 78]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=98>))
>
> And (so, the consequent) experience (bhoga) is of four kinds, according to whether (it comes about) by 1) the immediate exit (from the body brought about by the initiation) (sadya utkrānti), or 2) in association with the body just then (immediately after the initiation), or 3) by as a result of practice, or else 4) when the body comes to an end.
