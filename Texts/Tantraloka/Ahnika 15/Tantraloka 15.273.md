## Mūla

भानपि प्राणबुद्ध्यादिः स्वं तथा न विकल्पयेत् ।
प्रत्युतातिस्वतन्त्रात्मविपरीतस्वधर्मताम् ॥ ^mula

bhānapi prāṇabuddhyādiḥ svaṃ tathā na vikalpayet |
pratyutātisvatantrātmaviparītasvadharmatām || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 05 - Chapter Fifteen, part 4#^pg180|p. 162]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=180>))
>
> Under the influence (of the Lord’s) free will, that accomplishes what is extremely difficult to do (atidurghaṭā), this (limited subject who identifies with) the vital breath and the intellect etc., even though he thus shines (as one with Śiva), cannot conceive himself to be such. (with 15.272cd–273ab)
>
> (Indeed,) on the contrary, convinced that his own nature is the very opposite of the total freedom of the Lord, (believing that he is) mortal, powerless and dependent, he sinks (to increasingly lower levels). (with 15.273cd–274ab)
