# Psalm 111 (NRSVue)

## Praise for God’s wonderful works

**111:1** Praise the LORD! I will give thanks to the LORD with my whole heart, in the company of the upright, in the congregation. ^111-1

**111:2** Great are the works of the LORD, studied by all who delight in them. ^111-2

**111:3** Full of honor and majesty is his work, and his righteousness endures forever. ^111-3

**111:4** He has gained renown by his wonderful deeds; the LORD is gracious and merciful. ^111-4

**111:5** He provides food for those who fear him; he is ever mindful of his covenant. ^111-5

**111:6** He has shown his people the power of his works, in giving them the heritage of the nations. ^111-6

**111:7** The works of his hands are faithful and just; all his precepts are trustworthy. ^111-7

**111:8** They are established forever and ever, to be performed with faithfulness and uprightness. ^111-8

**111:9** He sent redemption to his people; he has commanded his covenant forever. Holy and awesome is his name. ^111-9

**111:10** The fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom; all those who practice it^[Gk Syr: Heb *them*] have a good understanding. His praise endures forever. ^111-10
