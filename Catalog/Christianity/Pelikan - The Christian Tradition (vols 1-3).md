**Checked against the file** on 7 Oct 2026: the title pages of the three volumes confirm Jaroslav Pelikan's The Christian Tradition: A History of the Development of Doctrine, vol. 1 The Emergence of the Catholic Tradition (100-600), vol. 2 The Spirit of Eastern Christendom (600-1700) and vol. 3 The Growth of Medieval Theology (600-1300), University of Chicago Press (vol. 1 apparently 1971, the scan being too coarse to be sure; vol. 2 1974; vol. 3 1978), so the city (Chicago) was added and the subtitle property, which listed the volume titles rather than a printed subtitle, was emptied, while year and pages stay empty because the note has three files.
