## Mūla

सिद्धसाधनि तत्पूर्वं शब्दब्रह्मस्वरूपिणि ।
समस्तबन्धशब्देन सहितं च निकृन्तनि ॥ ^mula

siddhasādhani tatpūrvaṃ śabdabrahmasvarūpiṇi |
samastabandhaśabdena sahitaṃ ca nikṛntani || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 03 - Chapter Thirty, part 2#^pg78|p. 60]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=78>))
>
> SIDDHASĀDHANI comes at the beginning of that (Vidyā), (then comes) ŚABDABRAHMASVARŪPIṆI along with the word SAMASTABANDHA and NIKṚNTANI. (Then comes) BODHANI invoked (in the vocative with) ŚIVASADBHĀVAJANANI. (These) five words consist of five, eight, nine, three, and eight syllables, respectively. This is the (first) Vidyā, which is that of the supreme Brahman, consisting of fifty syllables that, benevolent (śivā), bestows liberation. (with 30.108–110ab)
