## Mūla

पादांगुष्ठादि विभो निबन्धनं बन्धनं ह्युग्रम् ।
आर्यावाक्यमिदं पूर्वं भुवनाख्यैः पदैर्भवेत् ॥ ^mula

pādāṃguṣṭhādi vibho nibandhanaṃ bandhanaṃ hyugram |
āryāvākyamidaṃ pūrvaṃ bhuvanākhyaiḥ padairbhavet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 03 - Chapter Thirty, part 2#^pg63|p. 45]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=63>))
>
> The Brahmavidyā 1) 1.40 pervasive Lord, come here from the supreme plane; "You are eternal. Abandon the extremity of the body (i.e. Your presence within the body and death) (dehānta).' The tethering (of the soul) from the toes of the feet (up through the rest of the body) is indeed terrible bondage! (with 30.66cd–67ab)
>
> This, the first declaration (vākya), set in Ārya metre, is made of (as many) words (pada) as are said to be the worlds. (with 30.67cd)
