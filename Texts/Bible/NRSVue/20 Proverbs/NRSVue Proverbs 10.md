# Proverbs 10 (NRSVue)

## Wise sayings of Solomon

**10:1** The proverbs of Solomon. A wise child makes a glad father, but a foolish child is a mother’s grief. ^10-1

**10:2** Treasures gained by wickedness do not profit, but righteousness delivers from death. ^10-2

**10:3** The LORD does not let the righteous go hungry, but he thwarts the craving of the wicked. ^10-3

**10:4** A slack hand causes poverty, but the hand of the diligent makes rich. ^10-4

**10:5** A child who gathers in summer is prudent, but a child who sleeps in harvest brings shame. ^10-5

**10:6** Blessings are on the head of the righteous, but the mouth of the wicked conceals violence. ^10-6

**10:7** The memory of the righteous is a blessing, but the name of the wicked will rot. ^10-7

**10:8** The wise of heart will heed commandments, but one with foolish lips will come to ruin. ^10-8

**10:9** Whoever walks in integrity walks securely, but whoever follows perverse ways will be found out. ^10-9

**10:10** Whoever winks the eye causes trouble, but one who rebukes boldly makes peace.^[Gk: Heb *but one with foolish lips will come to ruin*] ^10-10

**10:11** The mouth of the righteous is a fountain of life, but the mouth of the wicked conceals violence. ^10-11

**10:12** Hatred stirs up strife, but love covers all offenses. ^10-12

**10:13** On the lips of one who has understanding wisdom is found, but a rod is for the back of one who lacks sense. ^10-13

**10:14** The wise lay up knowledge, but the mouth of a fool brings ruin near. ^10-14

**10:15** The wealth of the rich is their fortress; the poverty of the poor is their ruin. ^10-15

**10:16** The wage of the righteous leads to life, the gain of the wicked to sin. ^10-16

**10:17** Whoever heeds instruction is on the path to life, but one who rejects a rebuke goes astray. ^10-17

**10:18** Lying lips conceal hatred, and whoever utters slander is a fool. ^10-18

**10:19** When words are many, transgression is not lacking, but the prudent are restrained in speech. ^10-19

**10:20** The tongue of the righteous is choice silver; the mind of the wicked is of little worth. ^10-20

**10:21** The lips of the righteous feed many, but fools die for lack of sense. ^10-21

**10:22** The blessing of the LORD makes rich, and toil adds nothing to it. ^10-22

**10:23** Doing wrong is like sport to a fool, but wise conduct is pleasure to a person of understanding. ^10-23

**10:24** What the wicked dread will come upon them, but the desire of the righteous will be granted. ^10-24

**10:25** When the tempest passes, the wicked are no more, but the righteous are established forever. ^10-25

**10:26** Like vinegar to the teeth and smoke to the eyes, so are the lazy to their employers. ^10-26

**10:27** The fear of the LORD prolongs life, but the years of the wicked will be short. ^10-27

**10:28** The hope of the righteous ends in gladness, but the expectation of the wicked comes to nothing. ^10-28

**10:29** The way of the LORD is a stronghold for the upright but destruction for evildoers. ^10-29

**10:30** The righteous will never totter, but the wicked will not remain on the earth. ^10-30

**10:31** The mouth of the righteous brings forth wisdom, but the perverse tongue will be cut off. ^10-31

**10:32** The lips of the righteous know what is acceptable, but the mouth of the wicked what is perverse. ^10-32
