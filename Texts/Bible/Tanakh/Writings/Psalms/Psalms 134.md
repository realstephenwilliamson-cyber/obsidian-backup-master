## Chapter 134

**134:1** A song of ascents.  
Now bless the LORD,  
all you servants of the LORD  
who stand nightly  
in the house of the LORD. ^134-1

**134:2** Lift your hands toward the sanctuary  
and bless the LORD. ^134-2

**134:3** May the LORD,  
maker of heaven and earth,  
bless you from Zion. ^134-3
