# B.N. Pandit

Baljinath Pandit (1916–7 September 2007), a Sanskrit scholar described as an authority on Kashmir Shaivism; he was Professor of Sanskrit and Philosophy at Himachal Pradesh University and a member of the Academic Council of the Muktabodha Indological Research Institute ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baljinath_Pandit)). The page gives no dates for the professorship, so no later post is known.

Identification: Wikipedia credits *History of Kashmir Shaivism* (Utpal Publications) to "B.N. Pandit", the form the catalog uses; it gives 1989 for the book, the catalog note 1990.
