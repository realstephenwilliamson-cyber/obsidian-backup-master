# Psalm 52

← [[BCP Psalm 051]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 053]] →

*Quid gloriaris?* · Book Two · Tenth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 052]]

**52:1** You tyrant, why do you boast of wickedness \*  
against the godly all day long? ^52-1

[p. 658]

**52:2** You plot ruin;  
your tongue is like a sharpened razor, \*  
O worker of deception. ^52-2

**52:3** You love evil more than good \*  
and lying more than speaking the truth. ^52-3

**52:4** You love all words that hurt, \*  
O you deceitful tongue. ^52-4

**52:5** Oh, that God would demolish you utterly, \*  
topple you, and snatch you from your dwelling,  
and root you out of the land of the living! ^52-5

**52:6** The righteous shall see and tremble, \*  
and they shall laugh at him, saying, ^52-6

**52:7** "This is the one who did not take God for a refuge, \*  
but trusted in great wealth  
and relied upon wickedness." ^52-7

**52:8** But I am like a green olive tree in the house of God; \*  
I trust in the mercy of God for ever and ever. ^52-8

**52:9** I will give you thanks for what you have done \*  
and declare the goodness of your Name in the presence of the godly. ^52-9
