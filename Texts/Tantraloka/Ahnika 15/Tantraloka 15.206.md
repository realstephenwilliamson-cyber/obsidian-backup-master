## Mūla

पातालवक्त्रमधरमप्रकाशतया स्थितेः ।
खमरुद्वह्निजलभूखानि वक्त्राण्यमुष्य हि ॥ ^mula

pātālavaktramadharamaprakāśatayā sthiteḥ |
khamarudvahnijalabhūkhāni vaktrāṇyamuṣya hi || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 05 - Chapter Fifteen, part 4#^pg145|p. 127]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=145>))
>
> Īśāna is the zenith, as (He is) the Light (of consciousness). Tatpuruṣa is the face that spreads forth in front (pūrva, and so is the east – pūrva). Aghora (acaṇḍa) is the southern (direction). Vāmadeva, who is (cool and) pleasing (saumyaka), is the northern (vāmā) (quarter). As Sadyojāta faces away (from the Light or is on the other side of it), he is said to be the western (quarter) (paścimā) (which is behind – paścimā). As the face of the netherworlds (pātālavaktra) abides without light, it is the nadir. (with 15.204cd–206ab)
>
> These, the faces (of Sadāśiva), are (respectively,) Space, Air, Fire, Water and Earth. The zenith is primarily the Space said to be the Light (of consciousness). The nadir is (also) primarily that same (Space), as it is clearly the absence of light. (with 15.206cd–207)
