## Mūla

तस्मात्तस्यैव वर्णस्य युक्ता शोधकशोध्यता ।
श्रीपूर्वशास्त्रे चाप्युक्तं ते तैरालिङ्गिता इति ॥ ^mula

tasmāttasyaiva varṇasya yuktā śodhakaśodhyatā |
śrīpūrvaśāstre cāpyuktaṃ te tairāliṅgitā iti || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 06 - Chapter Sixteen, part 2#^pg110|p. 90]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=110>))
> Dyczkowski numbers 16.211cd as 16.211ab.
>
> And (so too) Sadyojyotis has said in his commentary on the Svāyambhuva: ‘Yes indeed! the Assemblage of Sounds (śabda) (has two natures,) one is that it is the bondage (that binds the soul) (pāśa) and the other that is Śiva.’ Therefore it is reasonable (to maintain) that (one and the) same phoneme (can be both) purifying (śodhaka) and purified (śodhya). (with 16.210–211)
>
> And it is also said in the Mālinīvijayottara: ‘(To) these (adepts who are) embraced by them’ (and so on). After having deposited the (fives) faces, namely, Sadyojāta and the rest, and the five limbs, that is, the heart and so on, six times, one should perform the deposition of the thirty-six (principles) from Earth onwards. (with 16.211cd–212)
