# Psalm 119 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 118|← Psalm 118]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 120|Psalm 120 →]]

## The glories of God’s law

**119:1** Happy are those whose way is blameless, who walk in the law of the LORD. ^119-1

**119:2** Happy are those who keep his decrees, who seek him with their whole heart, ^119-2

**119:3** who also do no wrong but walk in his ways. ^119-3

**119:4** You have commanded your precepts to be kept diligently. ^119-4

**119:5** O that my ways may be steadfast in keeping your statutes! ^119-5

**119:6** Then I shall not be put to shame, having my eyes fixed on all your commandments. ^119-6

**119:7** I will praise you with an upright heart, when I learn your righteous ordinances. ^119-7

**119:8** I will observe your statutes; do not utterly forsake me. ^119-8

**119:9** How can young people keep their way pure? By guarding it according to your word. ^119-9

**119:10** With my whole heart I seek you; do not let me stray from your commandments. ^119-10

**119:11** I treasure your word in my heart, so that I may not sin against you. ^119-11

**119:12** Blessed are you, O LORD; teach me your statutes. ^119-12

**119:13** With my lips I declare all the ordinances of your mouth. ^119-13

**119:14** I delight in the way of your decrees as much as in all riches. ^119-14

**119:15** I will meditate on your precepts and fix my eyes on your ways. ^119-15

**119:16** I will delight in your statutes; I will not forget your word. ^119-16

**119:17** Deal bountifully with your servant, so that I may live and observe your word. ^119-17

**119:18** Open my eyes, so that I may behold wondrous things out of your law. ^119-18

**119:19** I live as an alien in the land; do not hide your commandments from me. ^119-19

**119:20** My soul is consumed with longing for your ordinances at all times. ^119-20

**119:21** You rebuke the insolent, accursed ones, who wander from your commandments; ^119-21

**119:22** take away from me their scorn and contempt, for I have kept your decrees. ^119-22

**119:23** Even though princes sit plotting against me, your servant will meditate on your statutes. ^119-23

**119:24** Your decrees are my delight; they are my counselors. ^119-24

**119:25** My soul clings to the dust; revive me according to your word. ^119-25

**119:26** When I told of my ways, you answered me; teach me your statutes. ^119-26

**119:27** Make me understand the way of your precepts, and I will meditate on your wondrous works. ^119-27

**119:28** My soul melts away for sorrow; strengthen me according to your word. ^119-28

**119:29** Put false ways far from me, and graciously teach me your law. ^119-29

**119:30** I have chosen the way of faithfulness; I set your ordinances before me. ^119-30

**119:31** I cling to your decrees, O LORD; let me not be put to shame. ^119-31

**119:32** I run the way of your commandments, for you enlarge my understanding. ^119-32

**119:33** Teach me, O LORD, the way of your statutes, and I will observe it to the end. ^119-33

**119:34** Give me understanding, that I may keep your law and observe it with my whole heart. ^119-34

**119:35** Lead me in the path of your commandments, for I delight in it. ^119-35

**119:36** Turn my heart to your decrees and not to selfish gain. ^119-36

**119:37** Turn my eyes from looking at vanities; be gracious to me^[Q ms: MT *give me life*] according to your word. ^119-37

**119:38** Confirm to your servant your promise, which is for those who fear you. ^119-38

**119:39** Turn away the disgrace that I dread, for your ordinances are good. ^119-39

**119:40** See, I have longed for your precepts; in your righteousness be gracious to me.^[Q ms: MT *give me life*] ^119-40

**119:41** Let your steadfast love come to me, O LORD, your salvation according to your promise. ^119-41

**119:42** Then I shall have an answer for those who taunt me, for I trust in your word. ^119-42

**119:43** Do not take the word of truth utterly out of my mouth, for my hope is in your ordinances. ^119-43

**119:44** I will keep your law continually, forever and ever. ^119-44

**119:45** I shall walk at liberty, for I have sought your precepts. ^119-45

**119:46** I will also speak of your decrees before kings and shall not be put to shame; ^119-46

**119:47** I find my delight in your commandments because I love them. ^119-47

**119:48** I revere your commandments, which I love, and I will meditate on your statutes. ^119-48

**119:49** Remember your word to your servant, in which you have made me hope. ^119-49

**119:50** This is my comfort in my distress, that your promise gives me life. ^119-50

**119:51** The arrogant utterly deride me, but I do not turn away from your law. ^119-51

**119:52** When I think of your ordinances from of old, I take comfort, O LORD. ^119-52

**119:53** Hot indignation seizes me because of the wicked, those who forsake your law. ^119-53

**119:54** Your statutes have been my songs wherever I make my home. ^119-54

**119:55** I remember your name in the night, O LORD, and keep your law. ^119-55

**119:56** This blessing has fallen to me, for I have kept your precepts. ^119-56

**119:57** The LORD is my portion; I promise to keep your words. ^119-57

**119:58** I implore your favor with all my heart; be gracious to me according to your promise. ^119-58

**119:59** When I think of your ways, I turn my feet to your decrees; ^119-59

**119:60** I hurry and do not delay to keep your commandments. ^119-60

**119:61** Though the cords of the wicked ensnare me, I do not forget your law. ^119-61

**119:62** At midnight I rise to praise you, because of your righteous ordinances. ^119-62

**119:63** I am a companion of all who fear you, of those who keep your precepts. ^119-63

**119:64** The earth, O LORD, is full of your steadfast love; teach me your statutes. ^119-64

**119:65** You have dealt well with your servant, O LORD, according to your word. ^119-65

**119:66** Teach me good judgment and knowledge, for I believe in your commandments. ^119-66

**119:67** Before I was humbled I went astray, but now I keep your word. ^119-67

**119:68** You are good and do good; teach me your statutes. ^119-68

**119:69** The arrogant smear me with lies, but with my whole heart I keep your precepts. ^119-69

**119:70** Their hearts are thick like fat,^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] but I delight in your law. ^119-70

**119:71** It is good for me that I was humbled, so that I might learn your statutes. ^119-71

**119:72** The law of your mouth is better to me than thousands of gold and silver pieces. ^119-72

**119:73** Your hands have made and fashioned me; give me understanding that I may learn your commandments. ^119-73

**119:74** Those who fear you shall see me and rejoice, because I have hoped in your word. ^119-74

**119:75** I know, O LORD, that your judgments are right and that in faithfulness you have humbled me. ^119-75

**119:76** Let your steadfast love become my comfort according to your promise to your servant. ^119-76

**119:77** Let your mercy come to me, that I may live, for your law is my delight. ^119-77

**119:78** Let the arrogant be put to shame, for they have subverted me with guile; as for me, I will meditate on your precepts. ^119-78

**119:79** Let those who fear you turn to me, so that they may know your decrees. ^119-79

**119:80** May my heart be blameless in your statutes, so that I may not be put to shame. ^119-80

**119:81** My soul languishes for your salvation; I hope in your word. ^119-81

**119:82** My eyes fail with watching for your promise; I ask, “When will you comfort me?” ^119-82

**119:83** For I have become like a wineskin in the smoke, yet I have not forgotten your statutes. ^119-83

**119:84** How long must your servant endure? When will you judge those who persecute me? ^119-84

**119:85** The arrogant have dug pitfalls for me; they flout your law. ^119-85

**119:86** All your commandments are enduring; I am persecuted without cause; help me! ^119-86

**119:87** They have almost made an end of me on earth, but I have not forsaken your precepts. ^119-87

**119:88** In your steadfast love spare my life, so that I may keep the decrees of your mouth. ^119-88

**119:89** The LORD exists forever; your word is firmly fixed in heaven. ^119-89

**119:90** Your faithfulness endures to all generations; you have established the earth, and it stands fast. ^119-90

**119:91** By your appointment they stand today, for all things are your servants. ^119-91

**119:92** If your law had not been my delight, I would have perished in my misery. ^119-92

**119:93** I will never forget your precepts, for by them you have given me life. ^119-93

**119:94** I am yours; save me, for I have sought your precepts. ^119-94

**119:95** The wicked lie in wait to destroy me, but I consider your decrees. ^119-95

**119:96** I have seen a limit to all perfection, but your commandment is exceedingly broad. ^119-96

**119:97** Oh, how I love your law! It is my meditation all day long. ^119-97

**119:98** Your commandment makes me wiser than my enemies, for it is always with me. ^119-98

**119:99** I have more understanding than all my teachers, for your decrees are my meditation. ^119-99

**119:100** I understand more than the aged, for I keep your precepts. ^119-100

**119:101** I hold back my feet from every evil way, in order to keep your word. ^119-101

**119:102** I do not turn away from your ordinances, for you have taught me. ^119-102

**119:103** How sweet are your words to my taste, sweeter than honey to my mouth! ^119-103

**119:104** Through your precepts I get understanding; therefore I hate every false way. ^119-104

**119:105** Your word is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path. ^119-105

**119:106** I have sworn an oath and confirmed it, to observe your righteous ordinances. ^119-106

**119:107** I am severely afflicted; give me life, O LORD, according to your word. ^119-107

**119:108** Accept my offerings of praise, O LORD, and teach me your ordinances. ^119-108

**119:109** I hold my life in my hand continually, but I do not forget your law. ^119-109

**119:110** The wicked have laid a snare for me, but I do not stray from your precepts. ^119-110

**119:111** Your decrees are my heritage forever; they are the joy of my heart. ^119-111

**119:112** I incline my heart to perform your statutes forever, to the end. ^119-112

**119:113** I hate the double-minded, but I love your law. ^119-113

**119:114** You are my hiding place and my shield; I hope in your word. ^119-114

**119:115** Go away from me, you evildoers, that I may keep the commandments of my God. ^119-115

**119:116** Uphold me according to your promise, that I may live, and let me not be put to shame in my hope. ^119-116

**119:117** Hold me up, that I may be safe and have regard for your statutes continually. ^119-117

**119:118** You spurn all who go astray from your statutes, for their cunning is in vain. ^119-118

**119:119** All the wicked of the earth I count^[Q ms Gk Vg: MT *you bring to an end*] as dross; therefore I love your decrees. ^119-119

**119:120** My flesh trembles for fear of you, and I am afraid of your judgments. ^119-120

**119:121** I have done what is just and right; do not leave me to my oppressors. ^119-121

**119:122** Guarantee your servant’s well-being; do not let the godless oppress me. ^119-122

**119:123** My eyes fail from watching for your salvation and for the fulfillment of your righteous promise. ^119-123

**119:124** Deal with your servant according to your steadfast love, and teach me your statutes. ^119-124

**119:125** I am your servant; give me understanding, so that I may know your decrees. ^119-125

**119:126** It is time for the LORD to act, for your law has been broken. ^119-126

**119:127** Truly I love your commandments more than gold, more than fine gold. ^119-127

**119:128** Truly I direct my steps by all your precepts;^[Gk Jerome: Meaning of Heb uncertain] I hate every false way. ^119-128

**119:129** Your decrees are wonderful; therefore my soul keeps them. ^119-129

**119:130** The unfolding of your words gives light; it imparts understanding to the simple. ^119-130

**119:131** With open mouth I pant, because I long for your commandments. ^119-131

**119:132** Turn to me and be gracious to me, as is your custom toward those who love your name. ^119-132

**119:133** Keep my steps steady according to your promise, and never let iniquity have dominion over me. ^119-133

**119:134** Redeem me from human oppression, that I may keep your precepts. ^119-134

**119:135** Make your face shine upon your servant, and teach me your statutes. ^119-135

**119:136** My eyes shed streams of tears because your law is not kept. ^119-136

**119:137** You are righteous, O LORD, and your judgments are right. ^119-137

**119:138** You have appointed your decrees in righteousness and in all faithfulness. ^119-138

**119:139** My zeal consumes me because my foes forget your words. ^119-139

**119:140** Your promise is well tried, and your servant loves it. ^119-140

**119:141** I am small and despised, yet I do not forget your precepts. ^119-141

**119:142** Your righteousness is an everlasting righteousness, and your law is the truth. ^119-142

**119:143** Trouble and anguish have come upon me, but your commandments are my delight. ^119-143

**119:144** Your decrees are righteous forever; give me understanding that I may live. ^119-144

**119:145** With my whole heart I cry; answer me, O LORD. I will keep your statutes. ^119-145

**119:146** I cry to you; save me, that I may observe your decrees. ^119-146

**119:147** I rise before dawn and cry for help; I put my hope in your words. ^119-147

**119:148** My eyes are awake before each watch of the night, that I may meditate on your promise. ^119-148

**119:149** In your steadfast love hear my voice; O LORD, in your justice preserve my life. ^119-149

**119:150** Those who persecute me with evil purpose draw near; they are far from your law. ^119-150

**119:151** Yet you are near, O LORD, and all your commandments are true. ^119-151

**119:152** Long ago I learned from your decrees that you have established them forever. ^119-152

**119:153** Look on my misery and rescue me, for I do not forget your law. ^119-153

**119:154** Plead my cause and redeem me; give me life according to your promise. ^119-154

**119:155** Salvation is far from the wicked, for they do not seek your statutes. ^119-155

**119:156** Great is your mercy, O LORD; be gracious to me^[Q ms: MT *give me life*] according to your justice. ^119-156

**119:157** Many are my persecutors and my adversaries, yet I do not swerve from your decrees. ^119-157

**119:158** I look at the faithless with disgust because they do not keep your commands. ^119-158

**119:159** Consider how I love your precepts; be gracious to me^[Q ms: MT *give me life*] according to your steadfast love. ^119-159

**119:160** The sum of your word is truth, and every one of your righteous ordinances endures forever. ^119-160

**119:161** Princes persecute me without cause, but my heart stands in awe of your words. ^119-161

**119:162** I rejoice at your word like one who finds great spoil. ^119-162

**119:163** I hate and abhor falsehood, but I love your law. ^119-163

**119:164** Seven times a day I praise you for your righteous ordinances. ^119-164

**119:165** Great peace have those who love your law; nothing can make them stumble. ^119-165

**119:166** I hope for your salvation, O LORD, and I fulfill your commandments. ^119-166

**119:167** My soul keeps your decrees; I love them exceedingly. ^119-167

**119:168** I keep your precepts and decrees, for all my ways are before you. ^119-168

**119:169** Let my cry come before you, O LORD; give me understanding according to your word. ^119-169

**119:170** Let my supplication come before you; deliver me according to your promise. ^119-170

**119:171** My lips will pour forth praise, because you teach me your statutes. ^119-171

**119:172** My tongue will sing of your promise, for all your commandments are right. ^119-172

**119:173** Let your hand be ready to help me, for I have chosen your precepts. ^119-173

**119:174** I long for your salvation, O LORD, and your law is my delight. ^119-174

**119:175** Let me live that I may praise you, and let your ordinances help me. ^119-175

**119:176** I have gone astray like a lost sheep; seek out your servant, for I do not forget your commandments. ^119-176
