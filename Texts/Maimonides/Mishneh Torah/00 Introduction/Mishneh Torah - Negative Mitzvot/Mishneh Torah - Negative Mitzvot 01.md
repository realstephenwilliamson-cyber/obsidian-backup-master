# Mishneh Torah - Negative Mitzvot 01

← [[Mishneh Torah - Positive Mitzvot 01]] · [[Mishneh Torah - Negative Mitzvot]] · [[Mishneh Torah - Foundations of the Torah 01]] →

*Introduction · Negative Mitzvot · Chapter 1* · [[Mishneh Torah - Negative Mitzvot]] · [[Mishneh Torah]]

**1.** The first mitzvah of the negative commandments is not to consider the thought that there is another divinity aside from God, as \[[[Torah 17 - Yitro#^20-3|Exodus 20:3]]] states: "You shall have no other gods before Me." ^1-1

**2.** Not to make an idol - not to make one oneself or have one made for oneself by others - as \[[[Torah 17 - Yitro#^20-4|Exodus 20:4]]] states: "Do not make an idol for yourselves." ^1-2

**3.** Not to make false gods even for others, as \[[[Torah 30 - Kedoshim#^19-4|Leviticus 19:4]]] states: "Do not make molten gods for yourselves." ^1-3

**4.** Not to make images for decoration, even when one does not worship them, as \[[[Torah 17 - Yitro#^20-20|Exodus 20:20]]] states: "Do not make a representation of anything that is with Me." ^1-4

**5.** Not to bow down to any false gods, even though they are not generally worshiped by bowing down before them, as \[[[Torah 17 - Yitro#^20-5|Exodus 20:5]]] states: "Do not bow down to them." ^1-5

**6.** Not to worship false gods with the types of service with which it is customary to worship them, as \[[[Torah 17 - Yitro#^20-4|Exodus 20:4]]] states: "Do not serve them." ^1-6

**7.** Not to offer one's son to *Molech,* as \[[[Torah 29 - Achrei Mot#^18-21|Leviticus 18:21]]] states: "Do not give [any] of your children to offer to *Molech*." ^1-7

**8.** Not to perform the deeds associated with an *ov*, as \[[[Torah 30 - Kedoshim#^19-31|Leviticus 19:31]]] states: "Do not turn to the *ovot*." ^1-8

**9.** Not to perform the deeds associated with a *yid'oni,* as \[[[Torah 30 - Kedoshim#^19-31|Leviticus 19:31]]] states: "Do not turn to... the *yid'onim*." ^1-9

**10.** Not to take interest in the worship of false gods, as \[[[Torah 30 - Kedoshim#^19-4|Leviticus 19:4]]] states: "Do not turn to false gods." ^1-10

**11.** Not to erect a pillar [for purposes of worship], as \[[[Torah 48 - Shoftim#^16-22|Deuteronomy 16:22]]] states: "Do not erect a pillar for yourselves." ^1-11

**12.** Not to make hewn stones [upon which to prostrate oneself], as \[[[Torah 32 - Behar#^26-1|Leviticus 26:1]]] states: "You shall not place hewn stones..." ^1-12

**13.** Not to plant a tree on the Temple [premises], as \[[[Torah 48 - Shoftim#^16-21|Deuteronomy 16:21]]] states: "Do not plant an *asherah* or any other tree...." ^1-13

**14.** Not to take an oath on a false god [as requested by] one of its worshipers, nor to have one of them take an oath on [their false god], as \[[[Torah 18 - Mishpatim#^23-13|Exodus 23:13]]] states: "And the name of other gods you shall not mention, nor should it be heard from your mouth." ^1-14

**15.** Not to act as a missionary *[madiach]* to persuade the Jews to worship false gods], as \[[[Torah 18 - Mishpatim#^23-13|Exodus 23:13]]] states: "And the name of other gods... shall not be heard from your mouth." [Our Sages taught that] this is a prohibition against acting as a missionary for the worship of false gods. ^1-15

**16.** Not to act as a missionary *[mesit]* to persuade an individual Jew to worship false gods, as \[[[Torah 47 - Re'eh#^13-12|Deuteronomy 13:12]]] states with regard to such missionaries: "And they shall not continue to do so." ^1-16

**17.** Not to show affection for a *mesit,* as \[[[Torah 47 - Re'eh#^13-9|Deuteronomy 13:9]]] states: "Do not show appreciation for him." ^1-17

**18.** Not to reduce one's hatred for a *mesit,* as \[[[Torah 47 - Re'eh#^13-9|Deuteronomy 13:9]]] states: "Do not listen to him." ^1-18

**19.** Not to [try to] save a *mesit,* but rather to see to it that he is executed, as \[[[Torah 47 - Re'eh#^13-9|Deuteronomy 13:9]]] states: "Do not have mercy upon him." ^1-19

**20.** For the person whom a *mesit* tried to convince not to advance any arguments on behalf of the *mesit*, as \[[[Torah 47 - Re'eh#^13-9|Deuteronomy 13:9]]] states: "Do not have pity... [upon him]." ^1-20

**21.** For the person whom a *mesit* tried to convince not to withhold any evidence he is aware of that would incriminate the *mesit,* as \[[[Torah 47 - Re'eh#^13-9|Deuteronomy 13:9]]] states: "And do not try to cover up for him." ^1-21

**22.** Not to benefit from ornaments that have adorned false gods, as \[[[Torah 46 - Eikev#^7-25|Deuteronomy 7:25]]] states: "Do not covet the gold or silver which is upon them...." ^1-22

**23.** Not to rebuild an apostate city to its former stature, as \[[[Torah 47 - Re'eh#^13-17|Deuteronomy 13:17]]] states: "It shall never be rebuilt." ^1-23

**24.** Not to benefit from the property of an apostate city, as \[[[Torah 47 - Re'eh#^13-18|Deuteronomy 13:18]]] states: "Let nothing that has been condemned cling to your hand." ^1-24

**25.** Not to benefit from false gods, from any of their accessories, anything offered to them, or any wine brought as a libation for them, as \[[[Torah 46 - Eikev#^7-26|Deuteronomy 7:26]]] states: "Do not bring an abomination into your house." ^1-25

**26.** Not to prophesy in the name of false gods, as \[[[Torah 48 - Shoftim#^18-20|Deuteronomy 18:20]]] states: "[The prophet]... who speaks in the name of other gods [shall die]." ^1-26

**27.** Not to relate false prophecies, as \[[[Torah 48 - Shoftim#^18-20|Deuteronomy 18:20]]] states: "When a prophet presumptuously makes a declaration in My name which I have not commanded him...." ^1-27

**28.** Not to listen to someone who prophesies in the name of false gods, as \[[[Torah 47 - Re'eh#^13-4|Deuteronomy 13:4]]] states: "Do not listen to the words of that prophet." ^1-28

**29.** Not to refrain from executing a false prophet, nor to fear him, as \[[[Torah 48 - Shoftim#^18-22|Deuteronomy 18:22]]] states: "Do not fear him." ^1-29

**30.** Not to follow the laws or customs of the worshipers of false gods, as \[[[Torah 30 - Kedoshim#^20-23|Leviticus 20:23]]] states: "Do not follow the practices of the nation [that I am driving out before you]...." ^1-30

**31.** Not to practice black magic, as \[[[Torah 48 - Shoftim#^18-10|Deuteronomy 18:10]]] states: "There shall not be found among you... a magician." ^1-31

**32.** Not to practice divination, as \[[[Torah 30 - Kedoshim#^19-26|Leviticus 19:26]]] states: "Do not practice divination." ^1-32

**33.** Not to act as a soothsayer, as [Leviticus, *ibid.*] states: "Do not act as a soothsayer." ^1-33

**34.** Not to practice sorcery, as \[[[Torah 48 - Shoftim#^18-10|Deuteronomy 18:10]]] states: "There shall not be found among you... a sorcerer." ^1-34

**35.** Not to cast spells, as \[[[Torah 48 - Shoftim#^18-11|Deuteronomy 18:11]]] states: "[There shall not be found among you...] one who casts spells." ^1-35

**36.** Not to consult an *ov,* as [Deuteronomy, *ibid.*] states: "[There shall not be found among you...] one who consults an *ov.*" ^1-36

**37.** Not to consult *a yid'oni,* as [Deuteronomy, *ibid.*] states: "[There shall not be found among you...] one who consults an *ov* or a *yid'oni*." ^1-37

**38.** Not to seek information from the dead in dreams, as [Deuteronomy, *ibid.*] states: "[There shall not be found among you...] one who attempts to communicate with the dead." ^1-38

**39.** For a woman not to wear articles appropriate for men, as \[[[Torah 49 - Ki Teitzei#^22-5|Deuteronomy 22:5]]] states: "A woman should not wear a man's clothing." ^1-39

**40.** For a man not to wear articles appropriate for women, as \[[[Torah 49 - Ki Teitzei#^22-5|Deuteronomy 22:5]]] states: "A man should not wear a woman's clothing." [This prohibition was instituted] because this is an idolatrous custom. Explicit statements to this effect are found in the texts describing their worship. ^1-40

**41.** Not to tattoo our bodies like the worshipers of false gods, as \[[[Torah 30 - Kedoshim#^19-28|Leviticus 19:28]]] states: "Do not tattoo your flesh." ^1-41

**42.** Not to wear *sha'atnez,* as do the priests of false gods, as \[[[Torah 49 - Ki Teitzei#^22-11|Deuteronomy 22:11]]] states: "Do not wear *sha'atnez*." ^1-42

**43.** Not to shave the temples of our heads, as do the worshipers of false gods, as \[[[Torah 30 - Kedoshim#^19-27|Leviticus 19:27]]] states: "Do not shave your temples." ^1-43

**44.** Not to shave off our beards entirely, as do the priests of false gods, as \[[[Torah 30 - Kedoshim#^19-27|Leviticus 19:27]]] states: "Do not shave the corners of your beard." ^1-44

**45.** Not to make cuts in our flesh, as the worshipers of false gods do, as \[[[Torah 47 - Re'eh#^14-1|Deuteronomy 14:1]]] states: "Do not cut yourselves." *Gedidah is* another term for cutting. ^1-45

**46.** Not to ever dwell in the land of Egypt, as \[[[Torah 48 - Shoftim#^17-16|Deuteronomy 17:16]]] states: "Do not ever return on this path again." ^1-46

**47.** Not to stray after the thoughts of one's heart or the sights one's eyes behold, as \[[[Torah 37 - Sh'lach#^15-39|Numbers 15:39]]] states: "Do not stray after your heart and eyes." ^1-47

**48.** Not to establish a covenant with the seven [Canaanite] nations, as \[[[Torah 45 - Vaetchanan#^7-2|Deuteronomy 7:2]]] states: "Do not establish a covenant with them." ^1-48

**49.** Not to allow a single member of the seven [Canaanite] nations to live, as \[[[Torah 48 - Shoftim#^20-16|Deuteronomy 20:16]]] states: "Do not allow a soul to live." ^1-49

**50.** Not to show favor to the worshipers of false gods, as \[[[Torah 45 - Vaetchanan#^7-2|Deuteronomy 7:2]]] states: "Do not show them favor." ^1-50

**51.** Not to allow the worshipers of false gods to settle in our land, as \[[[Torah 18 - Mishpatim#^23-33|Exodus 23:33]]] states: "They shall not settle in your land." ^1-51

**52.** Not to marry gentiles, as \[[[Torah 45 - Vaetchanan#^7-3|Deuteronomy 7:3]]] states: "Do not marry among them." ^1-52

**53.** For a Jewish woman never to marry an Ammonite or Moabite [even after conversion], as \[[[Torah 49 - Ki Teitzei#^23-4|Deuteronomy 23:4]]] states: "An Ammonite or a Moabite shall never enter the congregation of God." ^1-53

**54.** Not to prevent the third generation of [converts from] the descendants of Esau from marrying among [the Jewish people], as \[[[Torah 49 - Ki Teitzei#^23-8|Deuteronomy 23:8]]] states: "Do not [utterly] despise an Edomite." ^1-54

**55.** Not to prevent the third generation of Egyptian [converts] from marrying among [the Jewish people], as \[[[Torah 49 - Ki Teitzei#^23-8|Deuteronomy 23:8]]] states: "Do not [utterly] despise an Egyptian." ^1-55

**56.** Not to make an offer of peace to Ammon and Moav at the outbreak of war, as is done for other nations, as \[[[Torah 49 - Ki Teitzei#^23-7|Deuteronomy 23:7]]] states: "Do not seek their peace and welfare...." ^1-56

**57.** Not to destroy fruit trees nor to destroy anything else of value, as \[[[Torah 48 - Shoftim#^20-19|Deuteronomy 20:19]]] states: "Do not destroy its trees." ^1-57

**58.** For soldiers not to fear or become frightened of the enemy during war, as \[[[Torah 46 - Eikev#^7-21|Deuteronomy 7:21]]] states: "Do not panic before them," and \[[[Torah 44 - Devarim#^3-22|Deuteronomy 3:22]]] states: "Do not fear them." ^1-58

**59.** Not to forget the wicked deeds which Amalek perpetrated against us, as \[[[Torah 49 - Ki Teitzei#^25-19|Deuteronomy 25:19]]] states: "Do not forget." ^1-59

**60.** The prohibition against blessing [i.e., cursing] God's name, as \[[[Torah 18 - Mishpatim#^22-27|Exodus 22:27]]] states: "Do not curse God." \[[[Torah 31 - Emor#^24-16|Leviticus 24:16]]] mentions the punishment: "One who curses the name of God shall surely die." This is a general principle: A negative commandment is involved whenever the Torah mentions the punishments of *karet* or execution - with the exception of circumcision and the Paschal sacrifice which are positive commandments punishable by *karet.* ^1-60

**61.** Not to violate an oath, as \[[[Torah 30 - Kedoshim#^19-12|Leviticus 19:12]]] states: "Do not swear falsely in My name." ^1-61

**62.** Not to take an oath in vain, as \[[[Torah 17 - Yitro#^20-7|Exodus 20:7]]] states: "Do not take the name of God, your Lord, in vain." ^1-62

**63.** Not to profane the name of the Holy One, blessed be He, as \[[[Torah 31 - Emor#^22-32|Leviticus 22:32]]] states: "Do not profane My holy name." ^1-63

**64.** Not to test the promises of God, as \[[[Torah 45 - Vaetchanan#^6-16|Deuteronomy 6:16]]] states: "Do not test God, your Lord." ^1-64

**65.** Not to destroy the Temple, synagogues, or houses of study; and similarly, not to erase any of [God’s] sacred names, nor to destroy any sacred texts, as \[[[Torah 47 - Re'eh#^12-3|Deuteronomy 12:3-4]]] states: "You shall surely destroy them. Do not do this to God, your Lord."[^1] ^1-65

**66.** Not to leave a corpse that was hung on the gallows, as \[[[Torah 49 - Ki Teitzei#^21-23|Deuteronomy 21:23]]] states: "Do not leave his body on the gallows." ^1-66

**67.** Not to interrupt the watch held around the Temple, as \[[[Torah 29 - Achrei Mot#^18-30|Leviticus 18:30]]] states: "And you shall keep My watch."[^2]." ^1-67

**68.** For a priest not to enter the Temple building at all times, as \[[[Torah 29 - Achrei Mot#^16-2|Leviticus 16:2]]] states: "And he shall not enter the sanctuary at all times." ^1-68

**69.** For a priest with a disqualifying physical deformity not to proceed beyond [the beginning of] the altar, as \[[[Torah 31 - Emor#^21-23|Leviticus 21:23]]] states: "However, to the *parochet[^3]* he shall not approach." ^1-69

**70.** For a priest with a disqualifying physical deformity not to serve [in the Temple], as \[[[Torah 31 - Emor#^21-17|Leviticus 21:17]]] states: "[Anyone...] who has a blemish [may not approach to present]...." ^1-70

**71.** For a priest with a disqualifying physical deformity of a temporary nature not to serve [in the Temple], as \[[[Torah 31 - Emor#^21-18|Leviticus 21:18]]] states: "Anyone who has a blemish may not approach [to offer a sacrifice]. ^1-71

**72.** For the Levites not to perform the services of the priests, and for the priests not to perform the services of the Levites, as \[[[Torah 38 - Korach#^18-3|Numbers 18:3]]] states with regard to the Levites: "Neither to the sacred vessels nor to the altar shall they approach, so that they do not die, neither they nor you." ^1-72

**73.** For a person who is intoxicated not to enter the Temple nor to render a halachic decision, as \[[[Torah 26 - Shmini#^10-9|Leviticus 10:9]]] states: "Do not drink wine or strong drink when you enter the Tent of Testimony," and [ibid., 10:11] states: "or when you render a decision for the children of Israel." ^1-73

**74.** For a non-priest not to serve in the Temple, as \[[[Torah 38 - Korach#^18-4|Numbers 18:4]]] states: "An unauthorized person shall not approach them." ^1-74

**75.** For a priest who is ritually impure not to serve [in the Temple], as \[[[Torah 31 - Emor#^22-2|Leviticus 22:2]]] states: "And they shall separate themselves from the sacred offerings of the children of Israel." ^1-75

**76.** For a priest who has immersed himself in a *mikveh* in order to purify himself from ritual impurity not to serve [in the Temple] until the conclusion, of that day, as \[[[Torah 31 - Emor#^21-6|Leviticus 21:6]]] states: "And they shall not profane...." ^1-76

**77.** For a priest who is ritually impure not to enter the Temple courtyard, as \[[[Torah 35 - Nasso#^5-3|Numbers 5:3]]] states: "And they shall not make your camp impure." This refers to the camp of the Divine Presence. ^1-77

**78.** For a person who is impure not to enter the camp of the Levites, the parallel for all time being the Temple Mount, as \[[[Torah 49 - Ki Teitzei#^23-11|Deuteronomy 23:11]]] states: "Do not enter the camp." This refers to the camp of the Levites. ^1-78

**79.** Not to build the altar with hewn stones, as \[[[Torah 17 - Yitro#^20-22|Exodus 20:22]]] states: "Do not make them hewn." ^1-79

**80.** Not to ascend to the altar using steps, as \[[[Torah 17 - Yitro#^20-23|Exodus 20:23]]] states: "Do not ascend My altar with steps." ^1-80

**81.** Not to extinguish the fire of the altar, as \[[[Torah 25 - Tzav#^6-6|Leviticus 6:6]]] states: "Burn a continuous fire on the altar. Do not extinguish it." ^1-81

**82.** Not to offer any [undesired] incense offering or any sacrifices on the golden altar, as \[[[Torah 20 - Tetzaveh#^30-9|Exodus 30:9]]] states: "Do not offer any unauthorized incense upon it." ^1-82

**83.** Not to duplicate the composition of the anointing oil, as \[[[Torah 21 - Ki Tisa#^30-32|Exodus 30:32]]] states: "And do not duplicate its formula." ^1-83

**84.** Not to anoint an unauthorized person with the anointing oil, as \[[[Torah 21 - Ki Tisa#^30-32|Exodus 30:32]]] states: "Do not anoint the flesh of a person with it." ^1-84

**85.** Not to duplicate the composition of the incense offering, as \[[[Torah 21 - Ki Tisa#^30-37|Exodus 30:37]]] states: "Do not duplicate its formula." ^1-85

**86.** Not to remove the staves of the ark, as \[[[Torah 19 - Terumah#^25-16|Exodus 25:16]]] states: "They shall not be removed from it." ^1-86

**87.** For the [High Priest's] breastplate not to come loose from the ephod, as \[[[Torah 20 - Tetzaveh#^28-28|Exodus 28:28]]] states: "The breastplate shall not come loose from the ephod." ^1-87

**88.** For the [High Priest's] cloak not to tear, as \[[[Torah 20 - Tetzaveh#^28-32|Exodus 28:32]]] states: "It shall have a woven border around it. It shall not tear." ^1-88

**89.** Not to offer sacred offerings outside [the Temple], as \[[[Torah 47 - Re'eh#^12-13|Deuteronomy 12:13]]] states: "Be careful, lest you offer your burnt offerings...." ^1-89

**90.** Not to slaughter sacred offerings outside [the Temple], as \[[[Torah 29 - Achrei Mot#^17-3|Leviticus 17:3-4]]] states: "A person who slaughters an ox or a sheep without bringing it to the Tent of Testimony. He shall be punished by *karet*." ^1-90

**91.** Not to consecrate animals with disqualifying physical blemishes [as sacrifices to be offered on] the altar, as \[[[Torah 31 - Emor#^22-20|Leviticus 22:20]]] states: "Any [animal] which has a blemish shall not be sacrificed." This prohibition involves [the animal's] consecration. ^1-91

**92.** Not to slaughter animals with disqualifying physical blemishes as sacrifices, as \[[[Torah 31 - Emor#^22-22|Leviticus 22:22]]] states: "Do not sacrifice these to God." ^1-92

**93.** Not to sprinkle the blood of animals with disqualifying physical blemishes on the altar, as \[[[Torah 31 - Emor#^22-24|Leviticus 22:24]]] states: "Do not sacrifice to God...." This prohibition involves sprinkling [the animal's] blood." ^1-93

**94.** Not to burn the sacrificial portions of animals with disqualifying physical blemishes [as sacrifices on the altar], as \[[[Torah 31 - Emor#^22-22|Leviticus 22:22]]] states: "Do not make a fire offering of them." ^1-94

**95.** Not to offer animals with disqualifying physical blemishes of a temporary nature as sacrifices, as \[[[Torah 48 - Shoftim#^17-1|Deuteronomy 17:1]]] states: "Do not sacrifice to God, your Lord, an ox or a sheep that has a blemish." This refers to a blemish of a temporary nature. ^1-95

**96.** Not to offer an animal with a disqualifying physical blemish as a sacrifice when it was brought by gentiles, as \[[[Torah 31 - Emor#^22-25|Leviticus 22:25]]] states: "Do not offer sacrifices [from these animals] when they are given by gentiles." ^1-96

**97.** Not to inflict a disqualifying physical blemish upon a consecrated animal, as \[[[Torah 31 - Emor#^22-21|Leviticus 22:21]]] states: "It shall not have a blemish." This can be interpreted to mean: Do not inflict a blemish upon it. ^1-97

**98.** Not to burn as an offering anything which is sweetened or leavened, as \[[[Torah 24 - Vayikra#^2-11|Leviticus 2:11]]] states: "You may not burn anything leavened or sweet [as a fire offering]." ^1-98

**99.** Not to offer a sacrifice which is unsalted, as \[[[Torah 24 - Vayikra#^2-13|Leviticus 2:13]]] states: "Do not omit the salt of God's covenant." ^1-99

**100.** Not to offer as a sacrifice an animal received by a prostitute as her fee, or an animal received in exchange for a dog, as \[[[Torah 49 - Ki Teitzei#^23-19|Deuteronomy 23:19]]] states: "Do not bring a prostitute's fee or the price of a dog...." ^1-100

**101.** Not to slaughter an animal and its offspring on the same day, as \[[[Torah 31 - Emor#^22-28|Leviticus 22:28]]] states: "Do not slaughter it and its offspring on the same day." ^1-101

**102.** Not to place olive oil on the meal offering brought by a sinner, as \[[[Torah 24 - Vayikra#^5-11|Leviticus 5:11]]] states: "Do not place oil upon it." ^1-102

**103.** Not to place frankincense on such a sacrifice, as [Leviticus, *ibid.*] continues: "...and do not place frankincense upon it." ^1-103

**104.** Not to place oil on the meal offering brought by a *sotah,* as \[[[Torah 35 - Nasso#^5-15|Numbers 5:15]]] states: "Do not pour oil upon it." ^1-104

**105.** Not to place frankincense on such a sacrifice, as [Numbers, *ibid.*] continues: "...and do not place frankincense upon it." ^1-105

**106.** Not to substitute [another animal] for [one selected as] a sacred offering, as \[[[Torah 33 - Bechukotai#^27-10|Leviticus 27:10]]] states: "Do not exchange it or substitute another for it." ^1-106

**107.** Not to change the designation of a consecrated animal from one sacrifice to another, as \[[[Torah 33 - Bechukotai#^27-26|Leviticus 27:26]]] states regarding a firstling animal: "A person should not consecrate it" - i.e., he should not consecrate it as another sacrifice. ^1-107

**108.** Not to redeem a firstling kosher animal, as \[[[Torah 38 - Korach#^18-17|Numbers 18:17]]] states: "Nevertheless, a firstling ox... do not redeem." ^1-108

**109.** Not to sell the tithe of cattle, as \[[[Torah 33 - Bechukotai#^27-33|Leviticus 27:33]]] states: "It shall not be redeemed." ^1-109

**110.** Not to sell a field that has been dedicated, as \[[[Torah 33 - Bechukotai#^27-28|Leviticus 27:28]]] states: "It shall not be sold." ^1-110

**111.** Not to redeem a field that has been dedicated, as [Leviticus, *ibid.*] states: "It shall not be redeemed." ^1-111

**112.** Not to sever the head of a bird [brought as] a sin offering, as \[[[Torah 24 - Vayikra#^5-8|Leviticus 5:8]]] states: "He shall pinch off its head...[without separating it]." ^1-112

**113.** Not to work with consecrated animals, as \[[[Torah 47 - Re'eh#^15-19|Deuteronomy 15:19]]] states: "Do not work with your firstling ox." ^1-113

**114.** Not to shear consecrated animals, as \[[[Torah 47 - Re'eh#^15-19|Deuteronomy 15:19]]] states: "Do not shear your firstling sheep." ^1-114

**115.** Not to slaughter the Paschal sacrifice while one possesses *chametz*, as \[[[Torah 18 - Mishpatim#^23-18|Exodus 23:18]]] states: "Do not sacrifice the blood of My offering in the presence of *chametz*." ^1-115

**116.** Not to allow the sacrificial portions of the Paschal sacrifice to become disqualified by remaining overnight, as \[[[Torah 18 - Mishpatim#^23-18|Exodus 23:18]]] states: "Do not allow the fat of My festive offering to remain until the morning." ^1-116

**117.** Not to allow the meat of the Paschal sacrifice to remain [overnight], as \[[[Torah 15 - Bo#^12-10|Exodus 12:10]]] states: "Do not leave over any of it until the morning." ^1-117

**118.** Not to allow the meat of the *Chaggigah* sacrifice to remain until the third day, as \[[[Torah 47 - Re'eh#^16-4|Deuteronomy 16:4]]] states: "Do not leave over any of the meat...." According to the oral tradition, we have learned that the verse refers to the *Chaggigah* sacrifice offered on the fourteenth of Nisan. The phrase "until the morning" [in that verse] refers to the morning of the second day of Pesach, the third day after [the sacrifice] was offered. ^1-118

**119.** Not to allow the meat of the second Paschal sacrifice to remain until the morning, as \[[[Torah 36 - Beha'alotcha#^9-12|Numbers 9:12]]] states: "Do not leave over any of it until the morning." ^1-119

**120.** Not to allow the meat of the thanksgiving offering to remain until the morning, as \[[[Torah 31 - Emor#^22-30|Leviticus 22:30]]] states: "Do not leave over any of it until the morning." The same law applies to all other sacrifices. They should not be left over beyond the time allotted for their consumption. ^1-120

That a prophet is not permitted to introduce a new measure and say that the Holy One, blessed be He, commanded this mitzvah to us and that it should be added to the Torah's mitzvot, or [say that He commanded that we] eliminate one of the 613 mitzvot mentioned above. ^1-368

**369.** However, if a court, together with the prophet of that age, adds a commandment as an ordinance, a lesson, or as a decree, this is not considered as an addition. He is not saying that the Holy One, blessed be He, commanded us to make an *eruv* or read the *Megillah* at its [appointed] time. Were he to say so, he would be adding to the Torah. ^1-369

**370.** Instead, we are saying that the prophets and the courts ordained and commanded that the *Megillah* be read at its [appointed] time in order to recall the praise of the Holy One, blessed be He, the salvation He wrought for us, and His response to our cries, so that we will bless Him, extol Him, and inform the future generations of the truth of the Torah's promise ([[Torah 45 - Vaetchanan#^4-7|Deuteronomy 4:7]]): "What nation is so great that it has God [close to it....]". Similar principles apply with regard to all the other Rabbinic commandments, be they positive commandments or negative commandments. ^1-370
