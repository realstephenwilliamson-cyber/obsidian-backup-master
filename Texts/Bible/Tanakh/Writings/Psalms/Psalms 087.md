## Chapter 87

**87:1** [^1]Of the Korahites. A psalm. A song. ^87-1

**87:2** The LORD loves the gates of Zion,  
His foundation on the holy mountains,[^2]  
more than all the dwellings of Jacob. ^87-2

**87:3** Glorious things are spoken of you,  
O city of God. *Selah*. ^87-3

**87:4** I mention Rahab[^3] and Babylon among those who acknowledge Me;  
Philistia, and Tyre, and Cush—each was born there. ^87-4

**87:5** Indeed, it shall be said of Zion,  
“Every man was born there.”  
He, the Most High, will preserve it.[^4] ^87-5

**87:6** The LORD will inscribe in the register of peoples  
that each was born there. *Selah*. ^87-6

**87:7** Singers and dancers alike [will say]:  
“All my roots[^5] are in You.” ^87-7

[^1]: The meaning of many passages in this psalm is uncertain.
[^2]: Order of lines inverted for clarity.
[^3]: A primeval monster; here, a poetic term for Egypt; cf. Isa. 30.7.
[^4]: Or “He will preserve it supreme.”
[^5]: Lit. “sources.”
