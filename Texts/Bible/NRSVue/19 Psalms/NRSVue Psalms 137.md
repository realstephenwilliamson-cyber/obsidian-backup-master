# Psalm 137 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 136|← Psalm 136]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 138|Psalm 138 →]]

## Lament over the destruction of Jerusalem

**137:1** By the rivers of Babylon—there we sat down, and there we wept when we remembered Zion. ^137-1

**137:2** On the willows^[Or *poplars*] there we hung up our harps. ^137-2

**137:3** For there our captors asked us for songs, and our tormentors asked for mirth, saying, “Sing us one of the songs of Zion!” ^137-3

**137:4** How could we sing the LORD’s song in a foreign land? ^137-4

**137:5** If I forget you, O Jerusalem, let my right hand wither! ^137-5

**137:6** Let my tongue cling to the roof of my mouth, if I do not remember you, if I do not set Jerusalem above my highest joy. ^137-6

**137:7** Remember, O LORD, against the Edomites the day of Jerusalem’s fall, how they said, “Tear it down! Tear it down! Down to its foundations!” ^137-7

**137:8** O daughter Babylon, you devastator!^[Or *you who are devastated*] Happy shall they be who pay you back what you have done to us! ^137-8

**137:9** Happy shall they be who take your little ones and dash them against the rock! ^137-9
