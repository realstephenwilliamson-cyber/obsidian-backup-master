## Mūla

ततस्तु तैजसं तत्त्वं शिवाग्नेरत्र संस्थितिः ।
ते चैनं वह्निमायान्ति वाह्नीं ये धारणां श्रिताः ॥ ^mula

tatastu taijasaṃ tattvaṃ śivāgneratra saṃsthitiḥ |
te cainaṃ vahnimāyānti vāhnīṃ ye dhāraṇāṃ śritāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 07 - Chapter Eight, part 5#^pg253|p. 235]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=253>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 8.205cd–206ab.
>
> Next comes the Fire Principle. Śiva’s fire is located here. Those who concentrate on the (subtle element of) fire reach this fire. Eight are the Lords (nāyakāṣtaka) of the (worlds of the) Fire Principle, beginning with Bhairava and ending with Harīndu. (with 8.205–206ab)
