## Mūla

तत्रादौ यागसदनं शुभक्षेत्रे मनोरमम् ।
कारयेदग्निकुण्डेन वर्तुलेन समन्वितम् ॥ ^mula

tatrādau yāgasadanaṁ śubhakṣetre manoramam |
kārayed agnikuṇḍena vartulena samanvitam || ^iast

## Translation

> **Selection of Place (sthānakalpana)**
>
> There (in that case), one must first of all select an abode for the sacrifice which is pleasing and situated in an auspicious place. It should have a round sacrificial hearth for the fire (*agnikuṇḍa*) (measuring) twenty-five fingers all around, a navel half (that size), a surrounding girdle a quarter (of that), and a beautiful lip.[^1] (with 8.2–3)

[^1]: See TĀ 15/388 ff and also ŚSP vol. 1 pp. 230 ff.
