## Law or faith

**3:1** You foolish Galatians! Who has bewitched you? It was before your eyes that Jesus Christ was publicly exhibited as crucified! ^3-1

**3:2** The only thing I want to learn from you is this: Did you receive the Spirit by doing the works of the law or by believing what you heard? ^3-2

**3:3** Are you so foolish? Having started with the Spirit, are you now ending with the flesh? ^3-3

**3:4** Did you experience so much for nothing?—if it really was for nothing. ^3-4

**3:5** Well then, does God^[Gk *he*] supply you with the Spirit and work miracles among you by your doing the works of the law or by your believing what you heard? ^3-5

**3:6** Just as Abraham “believed God, and it was reckoned to him as righteousness,” ^3-6

**3:7** so, you see, those who believe are the descendants of Abraham. ^3-7

**3:8** And the scripture, foreseeing that God would reckon as righteous the gentiles by faith, declared the gospel beforehand to Abraham, saying, “All the gentiles shall be blessed in you.” ^3-8

**3:9** For this reason, those who believe are blessed with Abraham who believed. ^3-9

**3:10** For all who rely on the works of the law are under a curse, for it is written, “Cursed is everyone who does not observe and obey all the things written in the book of the law.” ^3-10

**3:11** Now it is evident that no one is reckoned as righteous before God by the law, for “the one who is righteous will live by faith.”^[Or *The one who is righteous through faith will live*] ^3-11

**3:12** But the law does not rest on faith; on the contrary, “Whoever does the works of the law^[Gk *does them*] will live by them.” ^3-12

**3:13** Christ redeemed us from the curse of the law by becoming a curse for us—for it is written, “Cursed is everyone who hangs on a tree”— ^3-13

**3:14** in order that in Christ Jesus the blessing of Abraham might come to the gentiles, so that we might receive the promise of the Spirit through faith. ^3-14

## The promise to Abraham

**3:15** Brothers and sisters, I give an example from daily life: once a person’s will^[Or *covenant*] has been ratified, no one adds to it or annuls it. ^3-15

**3:16** Now the promises were made to Abraham and to his offspring;^[Gk *seed*] it does not say, “And to offsprings,”^[Gk *seeds*] as of many, but it says, “And to your offspring,”^[Gk *seed*] that is, to one person, who is Christ. ^3-16

**3:17** My point is this: the law, which came four hundred thirty years later, does not annul a covenant previously ratified by God, so as to nullify the promise. ^3-17

**3:18** For if the inheritance comes from the law, it no longer comes from the promise, but God granted it to Abraham through the promise. ^3-18

## The purpose of the law

**3:19** Why then the law? It was added because of transgressions, until the offspring^[Gk *seed*] would come to whom the promise had been made, and it was ordained through angels by a mediator. ^3-19

**3:20** Now a mediator involves more than one party, but God is one. ^3-20

**3:21** Is the law then opposed to the promises of God? Certainly not! For if a law had been given that could make alive, then righteousness would indeed come through the law. ^3-21

**3:22** But the scripture has imprisoned all things under the power of sin, so that what was promised through the faith of Jesus Christ^[Or *through faith in Jesus Christ*] might be given to those who believe. ^3-22

**3:23** Now before faith came, we were imprisoned and guarded under the law until faith would be revealed. ^3-23

**3:24** Therefore the law was our disciplinarian until Christ came, so that we might be reckoned as righteous^[Or *be justified*] by faith. ^3-24

**3:25** But now that faith has come, we are no longer subject to a disciplinarian, ^3-25

**3:26** for in Christ Jesus you are all children of God through faith. ^3-26

**3:27** As many of you as were baptized into Christ have clothed yourselves with Christ. ^3-27

**3:28** There is no longer Jew or Greek; there is no longer slave or free; there is no longer male and female, for all of you are one in Christ Jesus. ^3-28

**3:29** And if you belong to Christ, then you are Abraham’s offspring,^[Gk *seed*] heirs according to the promise. ^3-29
