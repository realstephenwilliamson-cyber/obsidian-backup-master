## Mūla

तत्र द्वारपतीन्पूज्य महास्त्रेणाभिमन्त्रितम् ।
पुष्पं विनिक्षिपेद्ध्यात्वा ज्वलद्विघ्नप्रशान्तये ॥ ^mula

tatra dvārapatīn pūjya mahāstreṇābhimantritam |
puṣpaṁ vinikṣiped dhyātvā jvaladvighnapraśāntaye || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, October 2024 – December 2024
> p. 17 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=16>))
>
> (After bathing) Unperturbed and pure, he should enter into the abode of the sacrifice that has been purified with (the mantra of) the Weapon. *Having worshipped the Lords of the Door there, he should throw a burning flower consecrated with (the mantra of) the Great Weapon, having meditated (on it) in order to pacify the obstacles.*[^12] (with 8.15cd–16)

[^12]: MV 8/16 is quoted in TĀv ad 4/251cd. The printed edition of the MV reads *dvārapatīn pūjya for dvārapatīn iṣṭvā.*
