# Psalms 144 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 143|← Psalms 143]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 145|Psalms 145 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 144|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**144:1** I will extol thee, O God my king: and I will bless thy name for ever; yea, for ever and ever. ^144-1

**144:2** Every day I will bless thee: and I will praise thy name for ever; yea, for ever and ever. ^144-2

**144:3** Great is the Lord, and greatly to be praised: and of his greatness there is no end. ^144-3

**144:4** Generation and generation shall praise thy works: and they shall declare thy power. ^144-4

**144:5** They shall speak of the magnificence of the glory of thy holiness: and shall tell thy wondrous works. ^144-5

**144:6** And they shall speak of the might of thy terrible acts: and shall declare thy greatness. ^144-6

**144:7** They shall publish the memory of the abundance of thy sweetness: and shall rejoice in thy justice. ^144-7

**144:8** The Lord is gracious and merciful: patient and plenteous in mercy. ^144-8

**144:9** The Lord is sweet to all: and his tender mercies are over all his works. ^144-9

**144:10** Let all thy works, O lord, praise thee: and let thy saints bless thee. ^144-10

**144:11** They shall speak of the glory of thy kingdom: and shall tell of thy power: ^144-11

**144:12** To make thy might known to the sons of men: and the glory of the magnificence of thy kingdom. ^144-12

**144:13** Thy kingdom is a kingdom of all ages: and thy dominion endureth throughout all generations. The Lord is faithful in all his words: and holy in all his works. ^144-13

**144:14** The Lord lifteth up all that fall: and setteth up all that are cast down. ^144-14

**144:15** The eyes of all hope in thee, O Lord: and thou givest them meat in due season. ^144-15

**144:16** Thou openest thy hand, and fillest with blessing every living creature. ^144-16

**144:17** The Lord is just in all his ways: and holy in all his works. ^144-17

**144:18** The Lord is nigh unto all them that call upon him: to all that call upon him in truth. ^144-18

**144:19** He will do the will of them that fear him: and he will hear their prayer, and save them. ^144-19

**144:20** The Lord keepeth all them that love him; but all the wicked he will destroy. ^144-20

**144:21** My mouth shall speak the praise of the Lord: and let all flesh bless thy holy name for ever; yea, for ever and ever. ^144-21
