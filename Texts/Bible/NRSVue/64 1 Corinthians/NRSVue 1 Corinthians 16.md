# 1 Corinthians 16 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 15|← 1 Corinthians 15]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · —

Compare: [[1 Corinthians 16|BSB and SBLGNT]] · [[DRB 1 Corinthians 16|Douay-Rheims]] · [[JANT 1 Corinthians 16|JANT notes]]

## The collection for the saints

**16:1** Now concerning the collection for the saints: you should follow the directions I gave to the churches of Galatia. ^16-1

**16:2** On the first day of every week, each of you is to put aside and save whatever extra you earn, so that collections need not be taken when I come. ^16-2

**16:3** And when I arrive, I will send any whom you approve with letters to take your gift to Jerusalem. ^16-3

**16:4** If it seems advisable that I should go also, they will accompany me. ^16-4

## Plans for travel

**16:5** I will visit you after passing through Macedonia—for I intend to pass through Macedonia— ^16-5

**16:6** and perhaps I will stay with you or even spend the winter, so that you may send me on my way, wherever I go. ^16-6

**16:7** I do not want to see you now just in passing, for I hope to spend some time with you, if the Lord permits. ^16-7

**16:8** But I will stay in Ephesus until Pentecost, ^16-8

**16:9** for a wide door for effective work has opened to me, and there are many adversaries. ^16-9

**16:10** If Timothy comes, see that he has nothing to fear among you, for he is doing the work of the Lord just as I am; ^16-10

**16:11** therefore let no one despise him. Send him on his way in peace, so that he may come to me, for I am expecting him with the brothers and sisters. ^16-11

**16:12** Now concerning our brother Apollos, I strongly urged him to visit you with the other brothers and sisters, but he was not at all willing to come now. He will come when he has the opportunity. ^16-12

## Final messages and greetings

**16:13** Keep alert; stand firm in the faith; be courageous; be strong. ^16-13

**16:14** Let all that you do be done in love. ^16-14

**16:15** Now, brothers and sisters, you know that members of the household of Stephanas were the first fruits in Achaia, and they have devoted themselves to the service of the saints; ^16-15

**16:16** I urge you to put yourselves at the service of such people and of everyone who works and toils with them. ^16-16

**16:17** I rejoice at the coming of Stephanas and Fortunatus and Achaicus, because they have made up for your absence, ^16-17

**16:18** for they refreshed my spirit as well as yours. So give recognition to such persons. ^16-18

**16:19** The churches of Asia send greetings. Aquila and Prisca, together with the church in their house, greet you warmly in the Lord. ^16-19

**16:20** All the brothers and sisters send greetings. Greet one another with a holy kiss. ^16-20

**16:21** I, Paul, write this greeting with my own hand. ^16-21

**16:22** Let anyone be accursed who has no love for the Lord. Our Lord, come!^[Or *Our Lord has come*] ^16-22

**16:23** The grace of the Lord Jesus be with you. ^16-23

**16:24** My love be with all of you in Christ Jesus.^[Other ancient authorities add *Amen*] ^16-24
