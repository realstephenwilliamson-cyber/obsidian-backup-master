## Mūla

अधरेषु च तत्त्वेषु या सिद्धिर्योगजास्य सा ।
विमोचनायां नोपायः स्थितापि धनदारवत् ॥ ^mula

adhareṣu ca tattveṣu yā siddhiryogajāsya sā |
vimocanāyāṃ nopāyaḥ sthitāpi dhanadāravat || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 10 - Chapter Thirteen, part 6#^pg351|p. 329]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=351>))
>
> The accomplishment (siddhi) (a yogi) has (attained) in the lower principles, that is born from (the practice of) Yoga, even if stable (sthitāpi), is not a means to liberation, just as (the acquisition of) a wife or wealth (are not).
