## Chapter 30

**30:1** But now those younger than I deride me,  
[Men] whose fathers I would have disdained to put among my sheep dogs. ^30-1

**30:2** Of what use to me is the strength of their hands?  
All their vigor[^1] is gone. ^30-2

**30:3** Wasted from want and starvation,  
They flee to a parched land,  
To the gloom of desolate wasteland. ^30-3

**30:4** They pluck saltwort and wormwood;  
The roots of broom are their food. ^30-4

**30:5** Driven out from society,[^2]  
They are cried at like a thief. ^30-5

**30:6** They live in the gullies of wadis,  
In holes in the ground, and in rocks, ^30-6

**30:7** Braying among the bushes,  
Huddling among the nettles, ^30-7

**30:8** Scoundrels, nobodies,  
Stricken from the earth. ^30-8

**30:9** Now I am the butt of their gibes;  
I have become a byword to them. ^30-9

**30:10** They abhor me; they keep their distance from me;  
They do not withhold spittle from my face. ^30-10

**30:11** Because God[^3] has disarmed[^4] and humbled me,  
They have thrown off restraint in my presence. ^30-11

**30:12** Mere striplings assail me at my right hand:  
They put me to flight;  
They build their roads for my ruin. ^30-12

**30:13** They tear up my path;  
They promote my fall,  
Although it does them no good. ^30-13

**30:14** They come as through a wide breach;  
They roll in like raging billows.[^5] ^30-14

**30:15** Terror tumbles upon me;  
It sweeps away my honor like the wind;  
My dignity[^6] vanishes like a cloud. ^30-15

**30:16** So now my life runs out;  
Days of misery have taken hold of me. ^30-16

**30:17** By night my bones feel gnawed;  
My sinews never rest. ^30-17

**30:18** With great effort I change clothing;  
The neck of my tunic fits my waist.[^7] ^30-18

**30:19** He regarded me as clay,  
I have become like dust and ashes. ^30-19

**30:20** I cry out to You, but You do not answer me;  
I wait, but You do [not] consider me. ^30-20

**30:21** You have become cruel to me;  
With Your powerful hand You harass me. ^30-21

**30:22** You lift me up and mount me on the wind;  
You make my courage melt. ^30-22

**30:23** I know You will bring me to death,  
The house assigned for all the living. ^30-23

**30:24** Surely He would not strike at a ruin  
If, in calamity, one cried out to Him.[^8] ^30-24

**30:25** Did I not weep for the unfortunate?  
Did I not grieve for the needy? ^30-25

**30:26** I looked forward to good fortune, but evil came;  
I hoped for light, but darkness came. ^30-26

**30:27** My bowels are in turmoil without respite;  
Days of misery confront me. ^30-27

**30:28** I walk about in sunless gloom;  
I rise in the assembly and cry out. ^30-28

**30:29** I have become a brother to jackals,  
A companion to ostriches. ^30-29

**30:30** My skin, blackened, is peeling off me;  
My bones are charred by the heat. ^30-30

**30:31** So my lyre is given over to mourning,  
My pipe, to accompany weepers. ^30-31

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Lit. “He.”
[^4]: Lit. “loosened my [bow] string.”
[^5]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^6]: Heb. yeshu‘athi taken as related to shoa‘, “noble.”
[^7]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^8]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
