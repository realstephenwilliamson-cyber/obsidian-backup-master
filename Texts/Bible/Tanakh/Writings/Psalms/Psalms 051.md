## Chapter 51

**51:1** For the leader. A psalm of David, ^51-1

**51:2** when Nathan the prophet came to him after he had come to Bathsheba.[^1] ^51-2

**51:3** Have mercy upon me, O God,  
as befits Your faithfulness;  
in keeping with Your abundant compassion,  
blot out my transgressions. ^51-3

**51:4** Wash me thoroughly of my iniquity,  
and purify me of my sin; ^51-4

**51:5** for I recognize my transgressions,  
and am ever conscious of my sin. ^51-5

**51:6** Against You alone have I sinned,  
and done what is evil in Your sight;  
so You are just in Your sentence,  
and right in Your judgment. ^51-6

**51:7** Indeed I was born with iniquity;  
with sin my mother conceived me. ^51-7

**51:8** Indeed You desire truth about that which is hidden;  
teach me wisdom about secret things.[^2] ^51-8

**51:9** Purge me with hyssop till I am pure;  
wash me till I am whiter than snow. ^51-9

**51:10** Let me hear tidings of joy and gladness;  
let the bones You have crushed exult. ^51-10

**51:11** Hide Your face from my sins;  
blot out all my iniquities. ^51-11

**51:12** Fashion a pure heart for me, O God;  
create in me a steadfast spirit. ^51-12

**51:13** Do not cast me out of Your presence,  
or take Your holy spirit away from me. ^51-13

**51:14** Let me again rejoice in Your help;  
let a vigorous spirit sustain me. ^51-14

**51:15** I will teach transgressors Your ways,  
that sinners may return to You. ^51-15

**51:16** Save me from bloodguilt,  
O God, God, my deliverer,  
that I may sing forth Your beneficence. ^51-16

**51:17** O Lord, open my lips,  
and let my mouth declare Your praise. ^51-17

**51:18** You do not want me to bring sacrifices;  
You do not desire burnt offerings; ^51-18

**51:19** True sacrifice to God is a contrite spirit;  
God, You will not despise  
a contrite and crushed heart. ^51-19

**51:20** May it please You to make Zion prosper;  
rebuild the walls of Jerusalem. ^51-20

**51:21** Then You will want sacrifices offered in righteousness,  
burnt and whole offerings;  
then bulls will be offered on Your altar. ^51-21

[^1]: Cf. 2 Sam. 12.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
