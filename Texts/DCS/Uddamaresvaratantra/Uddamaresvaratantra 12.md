# Uḍḍāmareśvaratantra — Chapter 12

[[Uddamaresvaratantra 11|← Chapter 11]] · [[Uddamaresvaratantra|← index]] · [[Uddamaresvaratantra 13|Chapter 13 →]]

oṃ hrīṃ śrīṃ klīṃ draṃ caṇḍogre trinetre cāmuṇḍe ariṣṭe hūṃ phaṭ svāhā

^s1

hrīṃ namāmy ahaṃ mahādevaṃ nṛsiṃhaṃ bhīmarūpiṇam oṃ namas tasmai

^s2

śrīpārvaty uvāca

^s3

uḍḍīśena samākīrṇe yogivṛndasamākule

^s4

praṇamya śirasā gaurī paripṛcchati śaṃkaram

^s5

śrīśvara uvāca

^s6

śṛṇu tvaṃ hi varārohe siddhyarthaṃ prativāsase

^s7

taṃ vadiṣyāmi te devi sarvaṃ tat samupāhṛtam

^s8

devi yo dvijo mantrais tu viprahanyān na saṃśayaḥ

^s9

pūrvoditaṃ mayoḍḍīśaṃ kathyate tava bhaktitaḥ

^s10

uḍḍīśaṃ ca namaskṛtya rudraṃ caiva sudurjayam

^s11

kapardinaṃ virūpākṣaṃ sarvabhūtabhayāvaham

^s12

prathamaṃ bhūtakaraṇaṃ dvitīyonmādanaṃ tathā

^s13

tṛtīyaṃ dveṣaṇaṃ caiva turyam uccāṭanaṃ tathā

^s14

grāmoccāṭaṃ pañcamaṃ ca ṣaṣṭhaṃ ca jalastambhanam

^s15

saptamam agnistambhaṃ ca vaśīkaraṇam aṣṭamam

^s16

anyān api prayogāṃś ca śṛṇuṣva vai varānane

^s17

śivena kathitā yogā uḍḍīśe śāstraniścaye

^s18

andhī ca vandhīkaraṇaṃ mūkīkāras tathaiva ca

^s19

gātrasaṃkocanaṃ caiva bhūtajvarakaras tathā

^s20

astraśastrasya truṭitaṃ pānīyasya vināśanam

^s21

dadhimadhunāśanaṃ ca nakhakaraṇaṃ tathaiva ca

^s22

gajānāṃ vājināṃ caiva prakopanaṃ parasparam

^s23

ākarṣaṇaṃ bhujaṃgānāṃ mānavānāṃ tathā dhruvam

^s24

sasyavināśanaṃ caiva garbhasyāntardhikāraṇam

^s25

vetālāñjanasiddhiś ca ulūkasiddhir api hi

^s26

anyān api mahāraudrān prayogān śṛṇu sāmpratam

^s27

vidyāmantraprayogāṃś ca auṣadhaṃ cābhicārikam

^s28

guptā guptatarāḥ kāryā rakṣitavyāḥ prayatnataḥ

^s29

akulīnādhamabuddher bhaktihīnasya vai tathā

^s30

hiṃsakasya ca kṣudrasya nindakasya viśeṣataḥ

^s31

svārthaṃ phalādilubdhasya upadeśam amanyataḥ

^s32

asmin śāpe pūruṣās te varjanīyāḥ prayatnataḥ

^s33

etaiś ca saha saṃyogo na kāryaḥ sarvadā budhaiḥ

^s34

ya uḍḍīśakriyāśaktibhedaṃ kurvanti mohitāḥ

^s35

te duṣṭā durjayāś caiva kim atra bahubhāṣitaiḥ

^s36

yadīcchāsiddhim ātmānam ātmārthaṃ hi tathaiva ca

^s37

satpūruṣāya dātavyaṃ devagururatāya ca

^s38

prayogās tu prayoktavyāḥ sādhakaiḥ śatrukāraṇe

^s39

animittā nivartante svātmagrāhe na saṃśayaḥ

^s40

asaṃtuṣṭo hy ayuktaś ca prayogān iti nācaret

^s41

śāstrasiddhavicārās tu sāmantakoṣakāḥ śubhāḥ

^s42

oṃ glauṃ gaṃ gaṇeśāya namaḥ

^s43

mantreṇānena mantrajñaḥ kumbhakāramṛdā tathā

^s44

lambodaraṃ prakurvīta pūjayed upacārakaiḥ

^s45

sāptāhikaṃ trisaṃdhyāyāṃ japtavyaṃ sāvadhānataḥ

^s46

sahasraikapramāṇena japācchāntir bhaviṣyati

^s47

prātar aṣṭottaraṃ japtvā labhed buddhiṃ śubhāṃ naraḥ

^s48

māsenaikena deveśi śrīlābhaś ca bhaved dhruvam

^s49

ṣaṇmāsena varārohe mahādhanapatir bhavet

^s50

trikālajñānavettā ca varṣaikena na saṃśayaḥ

^s51

aiṃ namaḥ svāhā

^s52

prātaḥ sahasravāraṃ tu prajaptena prapūjayet

^s53

varadāṃ tu mahādevīṃ śvetagandhānulepanaiḥ

^s54

puṣpair jāpair dakṣiṇādisopacārais tu pratyaham

^s55

saptame divase hy evaṃ vāgaiśvaryaṃ prajāyate

^s56

bhavet sadyaḥ pravaktā ca śrutismṛtidharo 'pi ca

^s57

bāndhavaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ cirāyuḥ sukham edhate

^s58

oṃ klīṃ mantreṇānena deveśi sādhakaḥ japam ārabhet

^s59

raktavastrāvṛto nityaṃ tathā kuṅkumajāṅgale

^s60

saptāhajapamātreṇa hy ānayet tridaśāṅganām

^s61

oṃ drīṃ drīṃ drīṃ drīṃ svāhā

^s62

pūrvavidhāno hi japed ekāntasaṃsthitaḥ

^s63

ākarṣeti striyaṃ śastāṃ sālaṃkārāṃ suvāsasam

^s64

oṃ haiṃ haḥ huṃ

^s65

ūrdhvadṛṣṭiprayogeṇa japel lakṣatrayaṃ priye

^s66

sarvapāpavinirmukto jāyate khecare pade

^s67

oṃ drīṃ kārīṇḍaḥ kṣaḥ kṣīṃ phaṭ svāhā

^s68

ekapādasthito bhadre japed aṣṭottaraṃ śatam

^s69

yad yat prārthayate vastu tad dadāti dine dine

^s70

yajen naraviśeṣaṃ ca devāgnigurubrāhmaṇaiḥ

^s71

oṃ śrīṃ kṣīṃ lohaṃ muñca kili kili amukaṃ kāṭaya kāṭaya mātaṃgini svāhā

^s72

mantreṇānena pūrvāhṇe pūjayann upacārakaiḥ

^s73

śarāvaṃ pūrayitvā tu catuṣpathe baliṃ haret

^s74

samantraṃ kṣipyati pumān puruṣaṃ yadi paśyati

^s75

ātmacintitakāryāṇi sādhayaty eva nānyathā

^s76

oṃ stambhini svāhā kapālini svāhā drīṃ drīṃ vaiṣādārthini svāhā chaḥ chaḥ

^s77

mantreṇa mṛttikāṃ japtvā pratārya saptadhā jale

^s78

saṃmukhībhūya kṣiptvā ca japtvā cāyutaṃ vāsare

^s79

tena siddho bhaven mantraḥ sādhakasya na saṃśayaḥ

^s80

oṃ śrīṃ kṣāṃ kṣīṃ kṣūṃ kṣaiṃ kṣauṃ kṣaḥ

^s81

madhyāhnasamaye sūryasammukhe japam ācaret

^s82

ayutaṃ japtamātreṇa svasāmarthyaṃ prapaśyati

^s83

tasya dṛṣṭinipātena dvipadaś ca catuṣpadaḥ

^s84

jvarābhibhūtā jāyante apūrvā mantrasampadaḥ

^s85

oṃ haṃsaḥ haṃsaṃ so 'haṃ svāhā

^s86

ekaviṃśatijaptena jalena jvarapīḍitaḥ

^s87

vimucyate pānamātrāt sadyaḥ svasthaś ca jāyate

^s88

oṃ drīṃ namo ghoreśvari ghoramukhi cāmuṇḍe ūrdhvakeśi vikṛtānane drīṃ drīṃ huṃ phaṭ huṃ svāhā

^s89

ayaṃ sarvadalanamantraḥ

^s90

oṃ drīṃ drīṃ drīṃ phaṭ phaṭ phaṭ svāhā sarvagrahāṇāṃ trāsanaṃ kuru kuru aṅguliprahāreṇa

^s91

oṃ sraṃ srāṃ sriṃ srīṃ sruṃ srūṃ sreṃ sraiṃ sroṃ srauṃ sraṃ sraḥ ha raṃ rauṃ rīṃ rūṃ raiṃ reviḥ chuṃ chuṃ haṃsaḥ amṛtavarcase svāhā

^s92

anena mantreṇodakaṃ śarāvaṃ saṃkṣipyāṣṭottaraśatenābhimantritaṃ kṛtvā pibet prātar utthāya saṃvatsareṇa vallīpalitavarjito bhavati

^s93

vṛkṣasthāvarajaṅgamākṛtiṃ samāṅgīkārāc ca vyāghralomādikaṃ pūrvodaryāṃ bhasmīkaroti sarvajanapriyo bhavati cirāyur bhavati

^s94

oṃ namo bhagavate rudrāya caṇḍeśvarāya huṃ huṃ huṃ phaṭ svāhā

^s95

anena mantreṇa japaṃ kṛtvā śīghram īpsitaṃ labhet

^s96

oṃ hūṃ drīṃ kṣaṃ kṣāṃ kṣiṃ kṣīṃ kṣuṃ kṣūṃ kṣeṃ kṣaiṃ kṣoṃ kṣauṃ kṣaṃ kṣaḥ hūṃ phaṭ svāhā

^s97

imaṃ mantraṃ pūrvaṃ lakṣam ekaṃ japet taddaśāṃśam ayutaṃ havanaṃ kuryāt

^s98

ekaikaṃ samidhaṃ ghṛtāktāṃ juhuyāt siddho bhavati gaṅgāgoloke na te meghāḥ praṇaśyanti na ca varṣanti vāsavo nadasamudraṃ śoṣayati meghastambho bhavati

^s99

udakamadhye sthitvā japaṃ karoty anāvṛṣṭikāle 'tivṛṣṭiṃ karoti

^s100

oṃ drīṃ pracalite kubere hūṃ hūṃ kili kili svāhā

^s101

pūrvavelāyām ādarśadīpasamīpe ṣaḍaṅgulena bhājane sūryamaṇḍale kumāraṃ vāme veśayati pūrvam ayutajapaḥ kartavyaḥ pañcopacāreṇa pūjā ca kartavyā pūrvābhiś ca svarājye

^s102

oṃ śrīṃ himajāte prayaccha me dhanaṃ svāhā

^s103

anena mantreṇa siddhārthakaṃ ghṛtamiśritaṃ hunet aṣṭottaraśate na annapānavimiśritaṃ sahasreṇa hunet manasaḥ prārthitaṃ labhet ayutaṃ hunecchrīsumanā bhavet lakṣaṃ huned grāmaśataṃ labhet

^s104

oṃ namo namaḥ

^s105

imaṃ mantraṃ śataṃ japet sarvakāmaprado 'yaṃ mantraḥ

^s106

oṃ drīṃ śrīṃ sārase siddhikari krīṃ namaḥ svāhā

^s107

imaṃ mantraṃ lakṣam ekaṃ japed raktakaravīraiś ca pūjayet satataṃ sarvakāmado 'yaṃ mantraḥ

^s108

oṃ druṃ kṣeṃ kṣeṃ huṃ kṣaḥ amukaṃ kṣaḥ svāhā

^s109

anena mantreṇa rājikālavaṇatuṣakaṇṭakaśivanirmālyaṃ tailena yutaṃ hunet samastaśrībhājanaṃ bhavati

^s110

oṃ huṃ huṃ huṃ lūṃ laṃ lauṃ huṃ laḥ amukaṃ chaḥ chaḥ svāhā

^s111

anena mantreṇa siddhārthaṃ bhasmanā saha mantritaṃ kartavyaṃ yasya gṛhe prakṣipya mantrabalipāṃśvair ākṣipet tasya bāhustambho bhavati

^s112

ripusainyāgre kṣipet śatrusainyastambho bhavati aśvagajanarā niśceṣṭā bhavanti vikalā bhavanti samantādevākulā bhavanti

^s113

oṃ ruṃ ruṃ mukhe svāhā

^s114

anena mantreṇa japtatailena mukhaṃ prakṣālya tilatailena gātrābhyaṅgaṃ vā vidhāya vātādikaṃ dinasaptakena naśyati

^s115

aiṃ mātaṃgi vimalāvati vikarāle drīṃ chaḥ chaḥ svāhā

^s116

anena mantreṇa japāpuṣpaṃ parijapya vārīṇi nadyādau homayet saptāhena īpsitaṃ phalaṃ labhet

^s117

kārtavīryārjuno nāma rājā bāhusahasrabhṛt

^s118

tannāmakīrtanād eva hṛtaṃ naṣṭaṃ ca labhyate

^s119

nityaṃ nityaṃ japet kiṃcid vidyāṃ vittasya prāptaye

^s120

oṃ maṃ kiṇi svāhā

^s121

iti vṛścikamantraḥ

^s122

oṃ huṃ huṃ haṃsaḥ haṃsaṃ so 'haṃ so 'haṃ svāhā

^s123

iti sarvaviṣāpaharaṇamantraḥ

^s124

mayūrapicchena kuśena śareṇa śaradaṇḍena vā taddehe sammārjanaṃ kuryāt

^s125

haṃ hāṃ hiṃ hīṃ huṃ hūṃ heṃ haiṃ hoṃ hauṃ haṃ haḥ chaḥ chaḥ svāhā

^s126

imaṃ gokṣīrasadṛśaṃ vāraṃ vāraṃ vicintayed vā varānanamukhe śirasi śarīre tataḥ kaṇṭhe tato hṛdi nābhimaṇḍale guhye tathā sarvāṅge cintayet tathā pūrakeṇa varārohe kaṇṭhadaṣṭo 'pi jīvati

^s127

oṃ gaṃ gaṇapataye mahāgaṇapataye vighnaharāya mataṃgasambhavāya lambodarāya gaurīpriyaputrāya hrīṃ gāṃ namaḥ raṃ haṃ kṣaḥ svāhā

^s128

gorocanāviṣarājikāpippalīnīcayavair mahātailena saha devadattaiś ca lakṣitān ālikhet nimbakāṣṭhena pratikṛtiṃ hutvā pṛṣṭhato likhet sadyo jvaravilopo bhavati śāntir bhavati

^s129
