## Mūla

अस्त्रमन्त्रितमृद्धूतमलः पञ्चाङ्गमन्त्रितैः ।
जलैर्मूर्धादिपादान्तं क्रमादाक्षालयेत्ततः ॥ ^mula

astramantritamṛddhūtamalaḥ pañcāṅgamantritaiḥ |
jalairmūrdhādipādāntaṃ kramādākṣālayettataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 02 - Chapter Fifteen, part 1#^pg58|p. 40]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=58>))
>
> Once the officiant has cleansed (himself of the impurity of) Earth with the Weapon (Phaṭ), he should gradually spray (his body,) beginning with the head to the feet, with water consecrated (mantrita) with the Five Limbs, and so immerse it whilst repeating the root Mantra along with (its) limbs, until he becomes one (with it). Having got up (and gone outside), he should offer libation (and propitiate,) with water and Mālinī, the sun, who satiates (and delights) things (viśvatarpaka) and, in the sky, contains all the holy (sat) deities of light. (with 15.49–51ab)
