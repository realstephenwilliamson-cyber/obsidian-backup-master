## Chapter 50

**50:1** A psalm of Asaph.  
God, the LORD God[^1] spoke  
and summoned the world from east to west. ^50-1

**50:2** From Zion, perfect in beauty,  
God appeared ^50-2

**50:3** —let our God come and not fail to act!  
Devouring fire preceded Him;  
it stormed around Him fiercely. ^50-3

**50:4** He summoned the heavens above,  
and the earth, for the trial of His people. ^50-4

**50:5** “Bring in My devotees,  
who made a covenant with Me over sacrifice!” ^50-5

**50:6** Then the heavens proclaimed His righteousness,  
for He is a God who judges. *Selah*. ^50-6

**50:7** “Pay heed, My people, and I will speak,  
O Israel, and I will arraign you.  
I am God, your God. ^50-7

**50:8** I censure you not for your sacrifices,  
and your burnt offerings, made to Me daily; ^50-8

**50:9** I claim no bull from your estate,  
no he-goats from your pens. ^50-9

**50:10** For Mine is every animal of the forest,  
the beasts on a thousand mountains.[^2] ^50-10

**50:11** I know every bird of the mountains,  
the creatures of the field are subject to Me. ^50-11

**50:12** Were I hungry, I would not tell you,  
for Mine is the world and all it holds. ^50-12

**50:13** Do I eat the flesh of bulls,  
or drink the blood of he-goats? ^50-13

**50:14** Sacrifice a thank offering to God,  
and pay your vows to the Most High. ^50-14

**50:15** Call upon Me in time of trouble;  
I will rescue you, and you shall honor Me.” ^50-15

**50:16** And to the wicked, God said:  
“Who are you to recite My laws,  
and mouth the terms of My covenant, ^50-16

**50:17** seeing that you spurn My discipline,  
and brush My words aside? ^50-17

**50:18** When you see a thief, you fall in with him,  
and throw in your lot with adulterers; ^50-18

**50:19** you devote your mouth to evil,  
and yoke your tongue to deceit; ^50-19

**50:20** you are busy maligning your brother,  
defaming the son of your mother. ^50-20

**50:21** If I failed to act when you did these things,  
you would fancy that I was like you;  
so I censure you and confront you with charges. ^50-21

**50:22** Mark this, you who are unmindful of God,  
lest I tear you apart and no one save you. ^50-22

**50:23** He who sacrifices a thank offering honors Me,  
and to him who improves his way[^3]  
I will show the salvation of God.” ^50-23

[^1]: Heb. ’El ’Elohim YHWH.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
