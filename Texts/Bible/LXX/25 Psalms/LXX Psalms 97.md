# LXX Psalms 97

← [[LXX Psalms 96]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 98]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 098]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 098]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**97:1** A Psalm of David. Sing to the Lord a new song; for the Lord has wrought wonderful works, his right hand, and his holy arm, have wrought salvation for him.  ^97-1

**97:2** The Lord has made known his salvation, he has revealed his righteousness in the sight of the nations.  ^97-2

**97:3** He has remembered his mercy to Jacob, and his truth to the house of Israel; all the ends of the earth have seen the salvation of our God.  ^97-3

**97:4** Shout to God, all the earth; sing, and exult, and sing psalms.  ^97-4

**97:5** Sing to the Lord with a harp, with a harp, and the voice of a psalm.  ^97-5

**97:6** With trumpets of metal, and the sound of a trumpet of horn make a joyful noise to the Lord before the king.  ^97-6

**97:7** Let the sea be moved, and the fulness of it; the world, and they that dwell in it.  ^97-7

**97:8** The rivers shall clap their hands together; the mountains shall exult.[^1]  ^97-8

**97:9** For he is come to judge the earth; he shall judge the world in righteousness, and the nations in uprightness.  ^97-9

[^1]: 97:8 *Alex.*+ before the Lord, for he comes.
