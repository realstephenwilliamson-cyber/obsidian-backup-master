## Chapter 6

**6:1** [^1]“Come, let us turn back to the LORD:  
He attacked, and He can heal us;  
He wounded, and He can bind us up. ^6-1

**6:2** In two days He will make us whole again;  
On the third day He will raise us up,  
And we shall be whole by His favor. ^6-2

**6:3** Let us pursue obedience to the LORD,  
And we shall become obedient.  
His appearance is as sure as daybreak,  
And He will come to us like rain,  
Like latter rain that refreshes[^2] the earth.” ^6-3

**6:4** What can I do for you, Ephraim,  
What can I do for you, Judah,[^3]  
When your goodness is like morning clouds,  
Like dew so early gone? ^6-4

**6:5** That is why I have hewn down the prophets,[^4]  
Have slain them with the words of My mouth:  
And the day that dawned [brought on] your punishment.[^5] ^6-5

**6:6** For I desire goodness, not sacrifice;  
Obedience to God, rather than burnt offerings. ^6-6

**6:7** [^6]But they, to a man, have transgressed the Covenant.  
This is where they have been false to Me: ^6-7

**6:8** Gilead is a city of evildoers,  
Tracked up with blood. ^6-8

**6:9** The gang of priests is  
Like the ambuscade of bandits  
Who murder on the road to Shechem,  
For they have encouraged[^7] depravity. ^6-9

**6:10** In the House of Israel[^8] I have seen  
A horrible thing;  
Ephraim has fornicated there,  
Israel has defiled himself. ^6-10

**6:11** (Even Judah has reaped a harvest of you!)[^9]  
When I would restore My people’s fortunes, ^6-11

[^1]: As anticipated at the end of chapter 5, Israel seeks the Lord’s favor; His answer begins with v. 4.
[^2]: Taking yoreh as equivalent of yarweh.
[^3]: Emendation yields “Israel”; cf. “Ephraim…Israel” in v. 10.
[^4]: Emendation yields “your children”; cf. 9.13.
[^5]: Cf. v. 3; but meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^6]: Meaning of vv. 7–11 unclear in part.
[^7]: Heb. “done”; cf. 5.1–3.
[^8]: Emendation yields “Beth-shean.”
[^9]: Cf. 5.9–10; but meaning of clause uncertain.
