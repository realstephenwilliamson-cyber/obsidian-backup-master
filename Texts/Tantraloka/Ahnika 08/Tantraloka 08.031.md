## Mūla

पातालाष्टकमेकैकमष्टमे हाटकः प्रभुः ।
प्रतिलोकं नियुक्तात्मा श्रीकण्ठो हठतो बहूः ॥ ^mula

pātālāṣṭakamekaikamaṣṭame hāṭakaḥ prabhuḥ |
pratilokaṃ niyuktātmā śrīkaṇṭho haṭhato bahūḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 03 - Chapter Eight, part 1#^pg105|p. 87]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=105>))
>
> The eight netherworlds (pātāla) are each nine thousand (leagues) in height. There is a space between them of one thousand (leagues). Hāṭaka is the Lord in (Sauvarṇa), the eighth one. (with 8.30cd–31ab)
>
> According to the teaching of the venerable Raurava, Śrīkantha, who has been appointed (as Hāthakeśvara to reign over) each of the (lower) worlds, forcefully (hathatah) (removing obstacles,) bestows many accomplishments (siddhi). (with 8.31cd–32ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* अस्य चैवमभिधाने किं निमित्तम् ? -- इत्याशङ्क्याह --

> प्रतिलोकं नियुक्तात्मा श्रीकण्ठो हठतो बहूः ॥
> सिद्धीर्ददात्यसावेवं श्रीमद्रौरवशासने ।

शिद्धीः"इति सालोक्यादिरूपाः । यदुक्तं तत्र --

"प्रतिलोकेनियुक्तात्मा श्रीकण्ठो भगवानसौ ।

करोति हाटको भूत्वा पातालद्वारपालनम् ॥

हठेन भङ्क्त्वा यन्त्राणि पातालेषु महोदयाः ।

सिद्धीरभ्यस्तसन्मन्त्रान् साधकांल्लम्भयत्यसौ ॥"

इति ॥ ३१ ॥
