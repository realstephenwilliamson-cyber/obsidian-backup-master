# Toḍalatantra — Patala 5

[[Todalatantra 04|← Patala 4]] · [[Todalatantra|← index]] · [[Todalatantra 06|Patala 6 →]]

śrīdevī uvāca

^s1

tvatprasādānmahādeva pavitrāhaṃ na cānyathā

^s2

śambhunāthārcanaṃ deva śrotum icchāmi sāmpratam

^s3

śiva uvāca

^s4

śṛṇu pārvati vakṣyāmi yanmāṃ tvaṃ paripṛcchasi

^s5

nakulīśaṃ samuddhṛtya manusvaravibhūṣitam

^s6

bindunādakalāyuktaṃ prāsādākhyaṃ mahāmanum

^s7

asya mantrasya māhātmyam ūrdhvāmnāye mayoditam

^s8

namaskāraṃ samuddhṛtya vāntaṃ netravibhūṣitam

^s9

vāruṇaṃ mukhavṛttaṃ ca vāyuṃ lalāṭasaṃyutam

^s10

ayaṃ pañcākṣaro mantraḥ pañcāmnāyaphalapradaḥ

^s11

praṇavādi yadā devi tadā mantraṃ ṣaḍakṣaram

^s12

prāsādākhyaṃ samuddhṛtya ardhanārīśvarāya ca

^s13

punaḥ prāsādamuddhṛtya mantraṃ paramagopanam

^s14

evaṃ bahuvidhākāraṃ vigrahaṃ me nagātmaje

^s15

kaṇṭhe tu garalaṃ devi na kalau bhāvayet kvacit

^s16

yadīcchedātmano mṛtyuṃ yadi unmattam icchati

^s17

tadaiva sahasā devi nīlakaṇṭham upāsate

^s18

duradṛṣṭavaśād devi nīlakaṇṭhastavādikam

^s19

karoti kārayedvāpi mama hatyāṃ karoti saḥ

^s20

ata eva maheśāni sa pāpiṣṭho na cānyathā

^s21

niṣiddhācaraṇaṃ pāpaṃ karoti yadi pāmaraḥ

^s22

putradārādhanaṃ tasya nāśameti na saṃśayaḥ

^s23

kaṇṭhe tu garalaṃ devi yadi pūjāparo bhavet

^s24

ihaloke daridraḥ syānmṛte śūkaratāṃ vrajet

^s25

nīlakaṇṭhasya yanmantraṃ yadi kuryāt puraskriyām

^s26

pakṣāntare maheśāni tasya mṛtyurna cānyathā

^s27

śṛṇu devi pravakṣyāmi pārthivaṃ śivapūjanam

^s28

tatrādau parameśāni gurudevaṃ namet sudhīḥ

^s29

oṃ harāya namaskāraṃ mṛttikāmāharet sudhīḥ

^s30

maheśvarāya namaskāraṃ liṅgaṃ nirmāya yatnataḥ

^s31

śūlapāṇe ihoccārya susaṃpratiṣṭhito bhava

^s32

anena manunā devi jīvanyāso vidhīyate

^s33

śakāraṃ bindusaṃyuktaṃ dīrghamuktaṃ ṣaḍaṅgakam

^s34

tasya dhyānaṃ pravakṣyāmi śṛṇuṣva susamāhitā

^s35

oṃ dhyāyennityaṃ maheśaṃ rajatagirinibhaṃ cārucandrāvataṃsaṃ ratnākalpojjvalāṅgaṃ paraśumṛgavarābhītihastaṃ prasannam

^s36

padmāsīnaṃ samantāt stutam amaragaṇair vyāghrakṛttiṃ vasānaṃ viśvādyaṃ viśvabījaṃ nikhilabhayaharaṃ pañcavaktraṃ trinetram

^s37

puṣpaṃ śirasi saṃdhārya mānasaiḥ pūjanaṃ caret

^s38

punardhyātvā maheśāni śive puṣpaṃ nidhāya ca

^s39

pinākadhṛgiti coccārya ihāgaccha dvayaṃ vadet

^s40

iha tiṣṭha tato dvaṃdvaṃ saṃnidhehi dvayam iha

^s41

iha sanni tato ruddhasvaśabdaṃ ca tato vadet

^s42

yāvat pūjāṃ samuccārya tataścaivaṃ karomyaham

^s43

snānīyaṃ ca paśupatiṃ ṅeyuktaṃ ca namaścaret

^s44

vedādyaṃ yojayeddevi brāhmaṇaḥ sādhakottamaḥ

^s45

etat pādyaṃ maheśāni ṣaḍakṣaramanuṃ tataḥ

^s46

namaskāraṃ samuccārya sarvaṃ dadyād vicakṣaṇaḥ

^s47

pūjayitvā maheśāni cāṣṭamūrtiṃ prapūjayet

^s48

śarvo bhavastathā rudra ugro bhīmaḥ paśoḥ patiḥ

^s49

mahādevaṃ ca īśānaṃ ṅeyutaṃ kuru yatnataḥ

^s50

kṣitiṃ jalaṃ tathā cāgniṃ vāyuṃ cākāśameva ca

^s51

yajamānaṃ tathā somaṃ sūryaṃ ca mūrtinā saha

^s52

sarvatra ṅeyutaṃ kṛtvā pūjayet sādhakottamaḥ

^s53

praṇavādinamo'ntena vāmāvartena pūjayet

^s54

mūrtayo'ṣṭau śivasyaitāḥ pūrvādikramayogataḥ

^s55

āgneyyāntāḥ prapūjyātha vidyāṃ liṅgiśivaṃ yajet

^s56

aṣṭottarasahasraṃ vā śataṃ vā prajapettataḥ

^s57

guhyātiguhyagoptā tvaṃ gṛhāṇāsmatkṛtaṃ japam

^s58

siddhirbhavatu me deva tvatprasādānmaheśvara

^s59

tatastoyaṃ samādāya japaṃ caiva samarpayet

^s60

mukhavādyaṃ tataḥ kṛtvā cāṣṭāṅgaṃ praṇamet sudhīḥ

^s61

saṃhāreṇa mahādeva kṣamasveti visarjayet

^s62

evaṃ pūjā prakartavyā śaktimantrān yajed yadi

^s63

prāsādādīn mahāmantrān yadi dīkṣāparo bhavet

^s64

śaktidīkṣā na kartavyā kadācidapi mohataḥ

^s65

sa śaiva iti vikhyātaḥ sarvatantreśvaro bhavet

^s66

athātaḥ sampravakṣyāmi sūtraṃ paramagopanam

^s67

haro maheśvaraścaiva śūlapāṇiḥ pinākadhṛk

^s68

paśupatiḥ śivaścaiva mahādeva iti kramāt

^s69

aṣṭamūrtis tato devi pūjayet sādhakottamaḥ

^s70

tato japenmaheśāni mukhavādyaṃ tataḥ param

^s71

etadanyanna kartavyaṃ śaktidīkṣāparo yadi

^s72

niṣiddhācaraṇāddevi pāpabhāg jāyate naraḥ

^s73

nyūnādhikaṃ mahādevi yadi pūjādikaṃ caret

^s74

sa guruṃ cāpi śiṣyaṃ ca śivahatyāṃ prayacchati

^s75

nyūnādhikaṃ maheśāni yadi caikākṣaraṃ bhavet

^s76

varṇasaṃkhyā maheśāni brahmahatyā bhaviṣyati

^s77

ata eva sa pāpiṣṭhaḥ satyaṃ satyaṃ sureśvari

^s78

evaṃ pūjāṃ vidhāyādau tataścānyaṃ prapūjayet

^s79

ādau śivaṃ pūjayitvā śaktipūjā tataḥ param

^s80

yat kiṃcid upacāraṃ hi tasya kiṃcinnivedayet

^s81

anyathā mūtravat sarvaṃ gaṅgātoyaṃ bhaved yadi

^s82

ata eva maheśāni ādau liṅgaṃ prapūjayet

^s83

śivasnānodakaṃ devi mūrdhni saṃdhārayed yadi

^s84

satyaṃ satyaṃ maheśāni śivatulyo na saṃśayaḥ

^s85

śivarūpī svayaṃ bhūtvā devīpūjāṃ samācaret

^s86

śaivavaiṣṇavadaurgārkagāṇapatyaindradīkṣitaḥ

^s87

ādau liṅgaṃ pūjayitvā yadi cānyat prapūjayet

^s88

tatphalaṃ koṭiguṇitaṃ satyaṃ satyaṃ na saṃśayaḥ

^s89

anyadevaṃ pūjayitvā śivaṃ paścād yajed yadi

^s90

tasya pūjāphalaṃ caiva bhujyate yakṣarākṣasaiḥ

^s91

iti te kathitaṃ kānte tantrāṇāṃ sāramuttamam

^s92

bahu kiṃ kathyate devi bhūyaḥ kiṃ śrotumicchasi

^s93
