## Mūla

विद्यामूर्तिमथात्माख्यां द्वितीयां परिकल्पयेत् ।
मध्ये भैरवसद्भावं दक्षिणे रतिशेखरम् ॥ ^mula

vidyāmūrtimathātmākhyāṃ dvitīyāṃ parikalpayet |
madhye bhairavasadbhāvaṃ dakṣiṇe ratiśekharam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 06 - Chapter Fifteen, part 5#^pg211|p. 193]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=211>))
>
> (It is said that) one should worship Sadāśiva, the Great Ghost, and the icon (of the Mantra deity consisting of two and) a half syllables (first), and (then) the (goddess) Parā above (it) as supreme (paratvena), with scent and flowers etc. (Thus,) one should (mentally) install (parikalpayet) the icon of the Vidyā (deity) (vidyāmūrti) (on the Throne), called the Self as the second. (with 15.322–323ab)
>
> (One should mentally install and worship) Bhairavasadbhāva (JHKṢHÜM) on (the lotus of the prong) in the middle, Ratiśekhara (RYLVŪM) on the (lotus to his) right, and Navātman (RHRKṢMLVYŪM) on the (lotus to his) left. These three Bhairavas are accompanied by the (three) goddesses. (The goddess) Parā, (white) like the full moon, is (on the lotus) in the middle. Again, on (her) right is Parāparā, who is red and somewhat fierce, (but) not terrifying (bhīṣaṇā). Aparā is tawny-black (krṣṇapiṁṅgalā) and, terrifying, (is worshipped) on the left prong. (with 15.323cd–325)
