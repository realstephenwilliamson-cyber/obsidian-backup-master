# τελειόω

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · verb

**Dodson:** I complete, accomplish, make perfect — (a) as a course, a race, or the like: I complete, finish (b) as of time or prediction: I accomplish, (c) I make perfect; pass: I am perfected. (Strong's 5048; headword τελειόω)
