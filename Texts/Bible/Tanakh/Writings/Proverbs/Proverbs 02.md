# Proverbs 02

## Chapter 2

**2:1** My son, if you accept my words  
And treasure up my commandments; ^2-1

**2:2** If you make your ear attentive to wisdom  
And your mind open to discernment; ^2-2

**2:3** If you call to understanding  
And cry aloud to discernment, ^2-3

**2:4** If you seek it as you do silver  
And search for it as for treasures, ^2-4

**2:5** Then you will understand the fear of the LORD  
And attain knowledge of God. ^2-5

**2:6** For the LORD grants wisdom;  
Knowledge and discernment are by His decree. ^2-6

**2:7** He reserves ability for the upright  
And is a shield for those who live blamelessly, ^2-7

**2:8** Guarding the paths of justice,  
Protecting the way of those loyal to Him. ^2-8

**2:9** You will then understand what is right, just,  
And equitable—every good course. ^2-9

**2:10** For wisdom will enter your mind  
And knowledge will delight you. ^2-10

**2:11** Foresight will protect you,  
And discernment will guard you. ^2-11

**2:12** It will save you from the way of evil men,  
From men who speak duplicity, ^2-12

**2:13** Who leave the paths of rectitude  
To follow the ways of darkness, ^2-13

**2:14** Who rejoice in doing evil  
And exult in the duplicity of evil men, ^2-14

**2:15** Men whose paths are crooked  
And who are devious in their course. ^2-15

**2:16** It will save you from the forbidden[^1] woman,  
From the alien woman whose talk is smooth, ^2-16

**2:17** Who forsakes the companion of her youth  
And disregards the covenant of her God. ^2-17

**2:18** Her house sinks down to Death,  
And her course leads to the shades. ^2-18

**2:19** All who go to her cannot return  
And find again the paths of life. ^2-19

**2:20** So follow the way of the good  
And keep to the paths of the just. ^2-20

**2:21** For the upright will inhabit the earth,  
The blameless will remain in it. ^2-21

**2:22** While the wicked will vanish from the land  
And the treacherous will be rooted out of it. ^2-22

[^1]: Lit. “strange.”
