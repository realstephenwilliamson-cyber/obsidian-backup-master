## Mūla

एकां संविदमाविश्य चक्रे तावन्ति पूजयेत् ।
प्रविष्टश्चेत्प्रमादेन सङ्कोचं न व्रजेत्ततः ॥ ^mula

ekāṃ saṃvidamāviśya cakre tāvanti pūjayet |
praviṣṭaścetpramādena saṅkocaṃ na vrajettataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 07 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 5#^pg189|p. 172]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=189>))
>
> In a gathering (cakra) (of initiates), one should worship as many bodies (as are present, because) having (all) penetrated into the one, well-filled consciousness, they would (each individually and collectively) be like one’s own body. (with 28.380cd–381ab)
>
> If someone has entered (the gathering) by mistake, (but the collective consciousness) does not for that reason contract, (each member of the gathering) should (continue) to engage in his own common practice (samācāra) (that) commenced (before he entered), together with him. (with 28.381cd–382ab)
