Book: `Library/Trika/Rastogi - The Krama Tantricism of Kashmir, Volume I (1979).pdf` (326 pages; ABBYY FineReader 8 re-typeset text, no scan; body 9 pt, notes 8 pt, calls 5.8 pt raised; the Devanagari quotations in the notes are image fragments). Notes: `Library/Trika/The Krama Tantricism of Kashmir, Volume I (Rastogi)/`; the conversion of 7 Oct already gave the calls as `<sup>N</sup>` and made 47 footnotes. Stephen chose option 3 (18 Oct): pair calls only with notes that have text. Done with the Agent SDK on the laptop.

## Result

108 new footnotes on 62 pages in notes 01 and 03–07 (155 with the earlier 47). The other notes stay as they were.

## Method (`transform.py`)

Per page (pages that already had footnotes left alone): the notes zone from the layer (8 pt lines below the body) and the image fragments in it; a note is taken only if its label "N." is text, nothing between it and the next text label (or the end of the zone) is an image, and the next page's zone does not begin with unlabelled text or an image (a run-on); the body must have exactly one `<sup>N</sup>`; the Markdown must have exactly one paragraph run starting `N\.` whose words match the layer's note (difflib ratio ≥ 0.85). Then `<sup>N</sup>` → `[^N]` (or `[^N-p<page>]` where N is already used in the file) and the note's paragraphs → `[^N]: text`.
