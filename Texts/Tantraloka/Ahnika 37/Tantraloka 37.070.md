## Mūla

आचार्यमभ्यर्थयते स्म गाढं
संपूर्णतन्त्राधिगमाय सम्यक् ।
जायेत दैवानुगृहीतबुद्धेः
संपत्प्रबन्धैकरसैव संपत् ॥ ^mula

ācāryamabhyarthayate sma gāḍhaṃ
saṃpūrṇatantrādhigamāya samyak |
jāyeta daivānugṛhītabuddheḥ
saṃpatprabandhaikarasaiva saṃpat || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 11 - Chapter Thirty-Seven, part 1#^pg279|p. 261]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=279>))
>
> This was the group (of disciples) who requested the teacher (to teach them,) in order to learn (all the) Tantras fully, in depth and properly. May the wealth (saṁpat) of the intellect of one graced by (good) fate be dedicated solely to (this) literary work, (which is itself an unbroken stream of) prosperity (saṁpatprabandha).
