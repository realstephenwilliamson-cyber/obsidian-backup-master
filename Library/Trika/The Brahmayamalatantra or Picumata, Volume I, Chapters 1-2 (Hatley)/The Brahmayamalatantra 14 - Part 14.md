Citations of the Brahmayāmala in the Tantrāloka of Abhinavagupta

Abhinavagupta (f. 975—1025) refers to or cites the BraYa fifteen times in nine chapters of the Tantrāloka: 4.54b, 4.60b, 5.97c, 13.145ab, 15.44c, 18.9a, 23.43d, 27.21d, 27.24b, 27.29a, 28.383a, 28.409c, 28.419b, 28.423b, and 29.11a. The following represents something between a manuscript collation and edition of chapter four of the Dīksottara section of the Nisvāsakārikā, a poorly transmitted supplement to the Nisvāsatattvasamhitā. This is entitled Tantrā-vatārapatala, “Chapter of the Descent of Revelation.” As discussed in §1.9.1, this chapter of the Dzksottara has a close relationship to chapter 39 of the Bra Ya, on the subject of the streams of revelation. The edition is based upon electronic texts of manuscript transcript numbers 17, 127, and 150 of the French Institute of Pondicherry, reported as T,, T127; and T,;0; respectively. These etexts were input by R. SATHYANARAYANaN, S. A. S. Sarma, and Nibedita RourT, with contributions by Dominic GoopazL and Nirajan Karre. Outline of the Tantrāvatārapatala vv. 1—5: The Devṛs questions concerning who was initiated by whom into which tantra, and the divisions of the tantras.
