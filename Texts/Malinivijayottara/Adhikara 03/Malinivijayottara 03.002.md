## Mūla

एवमेतन्महादेव नान्यथा समुदाहृतम् ।
यथाख्यातं तथा ज्ञातमादितः समनुक्रमात् ॥ ^mula

evam etan mahādeva nānyathā samudāhṛtam |
yathākhyātaṁ tathā jñātam āditaḥ samanukramāt || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2023 – March 2023
> p. 39 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=39>))
>
> “O Great God, things are just as you have said (they are), not otherwise. As you have spoken, so have I understood, right from the beginning in good order. Now I desire to hear (from you), O supreme Lord, (the Mantras) that denote (*vācaka*) the (range of) realities (*vasturūpa*) from Śiva onwards. By your grace, please tell them (to me).” (with 3.2–3)
