## Mūla

कादिभान्ताः केसरेषु प्राणोऽष्टस्वरसंयुतः ।
सबिन्दुको दलेष्वष्टस्वथ स्वं नाम दीपितम् ॥ ^mula

kādibhāntāḥ kesareṣu prāṇo'ṣṭasvarasaṃyutaḥ |
sabinduko daleṣvaṣṭasvatha svaṃ nāma dīpitam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 02 - Chapter Thirty, part 1#^pg23|p. 5]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=23>))
>
> (The twenty-four phonemes) from K to BH are (each in one of the twenty-four) stamens (of the lotus). (with 30.7ab)
>
> The vital breath (H) conjoined (successively) with the eight vowels along with bindu is in the eight petals. The names of the nine powers are energized (dīpita) (by the mantra OM). Ś, 5, S, together with the Point (bindu), is in the three circles. (with 30.7cd–8ab)
