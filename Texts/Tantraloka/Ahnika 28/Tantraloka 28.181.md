## Mūla

चतुस्त्रिद्व्येकमासादिदिनैकान्तं महोत्सवम् ।
कुर्यात्ततो न व्रजेयुरन्यस्थानं कदाचन ॥ ^mula

catustridvyekamāsādidinaikāntaṃ mahotsavam |
kuryāttato na vrajeyuranyasthānaṃ kadācana || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 04 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 2#^pg97|p. 80]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=97>))
>
> (Then) Śiva’s devotees should be fed and worshipped (with offerings), as much as (the penitent) is (financially) capable of doing. He should (then) organise a great festival, that can go on for four, three, two, one month (or less), up to (only) one day. (Those who attend it) should not go anywhere else at any time (for its entire duration). (with 28.180cd–181)
