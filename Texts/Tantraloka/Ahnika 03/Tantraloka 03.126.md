## Mūla

स्थितिर्माताहमस्मीति ज्ञाता शास्त्रज्ञवद्यतः ।
अज्ञ एव यतो ज्ञातानुभवात्मा न रूपतः ॥ ^mula

sthitirmātāhamasmīti jñātā śāstrajñavadyataḥ |
ajña eva yato jñātānubhavātmā na rūpataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. II
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 02 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v02 08 - Chapter Three, part 5#^pg247|p. 231]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 02.pdf#page=247>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 3.126cd–127ab.
>
> The perceiver (mātṛ) is a state of consciousness independent of the consonance of the (instrumental) means (upāya) (of perception), namely, the object of knowledge and the rest, (and is self-established in the immediacy of the awareness that:) “I am” (aham asmīti); (and so, latently containing within itself all knowledge,) is the knower (jñātṛ), like one who knows the scriptures (but does not wish to explain them and so remains silent). (with 3.125cd–126ab)
>
> The knower (jñātṛ) who, by his very nature, is the direct experience (anubhava) (which is pure awareness free of thought constructs) is no ‘knower’ at all; the state of subjectivity (jñātṛtā) is absent in that which is dependent (even) on the pure (inner) object of knowledge (at one with consciousness, or on anything else). (with 3.126cd–127ab)

*Lead-in:* ननु प्रमासंबन्धात् प्रमाता उच्यते, प्रमा च व्यतिरिक्तज्ञेयविषयात्प्रमाणाद्भवति इति व्यतिरिक्तं ज्ञेयादि परिहृत्य कथमस्य ज्ञातृत्वं स्यात् ? -- इत्याशङ्क्याह --

> अज्ञ एव यतो ज्ञातानुभवात्मा न रूपतः ॥
> न तु सा ज्ञातृता यस्यां शुद्धज्ञेयाद्यपेक्षते ।

यो हि प्रतिनियतज्ञानात्म प्रमाणादिकमपेक्ष्य "ज्ञाता"इत्युच्यते सोऽज्ञ एव न प्रमातैव भवति -- इत्यर्थः । प्रमातुः खलु अनन्योन्मुखत्वादन्यनिरपेक्षमेव रूपं यथोक्तं न पुनरस्य सापेक्षं रूपं भवितुमर्हति -- परस्य परनिष्ठत्वानुपपत्तेः । तदाह -- ङ तु"इत्यादि ॥ १२६ ॥
