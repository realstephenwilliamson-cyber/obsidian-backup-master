# Job 13 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Job 12|← Job 12]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Job 14|Job 14 →]]

**13:1** “Look, my eye has seen all this; my ear has heard and understood it. ^13-1

**13:2** What you know, I also know; I am not inferior to you. ^13-2

**13:3** But I would speak to the Almighty,^[Traditional rendering of Heb *Shaddai*] and I desire to argue my case with God. ^13-3

**13:4** As for you, you whitewash with lies; all of you are worthless physicians. ^13-4

**13:5** If you would only keep silent, that would be your wisdom! ^13-5

**13:6** Hear now my reasoning, and listen to the pleadings of my lips. ^13-6

**13:7** Will you speak falsely for God and speak deceitfully for him? ^13-7

**13:8** Will you show partiality toward him; will you plead the case for God? ^13-8

**13:9** Will it be well with you when he searches you out? Or can you deceive him as one person deceives another? ^13-9

**13:10** He will surely rebuke you if in secret you show partiality. ^13-10

**13:11** Will not his majesty terrify you and the dread of him fall upon you? ^13-11

**13:12** Your maxims are proverbs of ashes; your defenses are defenses of clay. ^13-12

**13:13** “Let me have silence, and I will speak, and let come on me what may. ^13-13

**13:14** I will take my flesh in my teeth and put my life in my hand.^[Gk: Heb *Why should I take . . . in my hand?*] ^13-14

**13:15** See, he will kill me; I have no hope;^[Or *Though he kill me, yet I will trust in him*] but I will defend my ways to his face. ^13-15

**13:16** This will be my salvation, that the godless shall not come before him. ^13-16

**13:17** Listen carefully to my words, and let my declaration be in your ears. ^13-17

**13:18** I have indeed prepared my case; I know that I shall be vindicated. ^13-18

**13:19** Who is there who will contend with me? For then I would be silent and die. ^13-19

## Job’s despondent prayer

**13:20** “Only grant two things to me; then I will not hide myself from your face: ^13-20

**13:21** withdraw your hand far from me, and do not let dread of you terrify me. ^13-21

**13:22** Then call, and I will answer; or let me speak, and you reply to me. ^13-22

**13:23** How many are my iniquities and my sins? Make me know my transgression and my sin. ^13-23

**13:24** Why do you hide your face and count me as your enemy? ^13-24

**13:25** Will you frighten a windblown leaf and pursue dry chaff? ^13-25

**13:26** For you write bitter things against me and make me reap^[Heb *inherit*] the iniquities of my youth. ^13-26

**13:27** You put my feet in the stocks and watch all my paths; you set a bound to the soles of my feet. ^13-27

**13:28** One wastes away like a rotten thing, like a garment that is moth-eaten. ^13-28
