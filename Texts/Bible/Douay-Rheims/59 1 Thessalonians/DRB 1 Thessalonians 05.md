# 1 Thessalonians 5 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB 1 Thessalonians 04|← 1 Thessalonians 4]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · —

Compare: [[1 Thessalonians 05|BSB and SBLGNT]]

**5:1** But of the times and moments, brethren, you need not, that we should write to you; ^5-1

**5:2** For yourselves know perfectly, that the day of the Lord shall so come, as a thief in the night. ^5-2

**5:3** For when they shall say, peace and security; then shall sudden destruction come upon them, as the pains upon her that is with child, and they shall not escape. ^5-3

**5:4** But you, brethren, are not in darkness, that that day should overtake you as a thief. ^5-4

**5:5** For all you are the children of light, and children of the day: we are not of the night, nor of darkness. ^5-5

**5:6** Therefore, let us not sleep, as others do; but let us watch, and be sober. ^5-6

**5:7** For they that sleep, sleep in the night; and they that are drunk, are drunk in the night. ^5-7

**5:8** But let us, who are of the day, be sober, having on the breastplate of faith and charity, and for a helmet the hope of salvation. ^5-8

**5:9** For God hath not appointed us unto wrath, but unto the purchasing of salvation by our Lord Jesus Christ, ^5-9

**5:10** Who died for us; that, whether we watch or sleep, we may live together with him. ^5-10

**5:11** For which cause comfort one another; and edify one another, as you also do. ^5-11

**5:12** And we beseech you, brethren, to know them who labour among you, and are over you in the Lord, and admonish you: ^5-12

**5:13** That you esteem them more abundantly in charity, for their work’s sake. Have peace with them. ^5-13

**5:14** And we beseech you, brethren, rebuke the unquiet, comfort the feeble minded, support the weak, be patient towards all men. ^5-14

**5:15** See that none render evil for evil to any man; but ever follow that which is good towards each other, and towards all men. ^5-15

**5:16** Always rejoice. ^5-16

**5:17** Pray without ceasing. ^5-17

**5:18** In all things give thanks; for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus concerning you all. ^5-18

**5:19** Extinguish not the spirit. ^5-19

**5:20** Despise not prophecies. ^5-20

**5:21** But prove all things; hold fast that which is good. ^5-21

**5:22** From all appearance of evil refrain yourselves. ^5-22

**5:23** And may the God of peace himself sanctify you in all things; that your whole spirit, and soul, and body, may be preserved blameless in the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ. ^5-23

**5:24** He is faithful who hath called you, who also will do it. ^5-24

**5:25** Brethren, pray for us. ^5-25

**5:26** Salute all the brethren with a holy kiss. ^5-26

**5:27** I charge you by the Lord, that this epistle be read to all the holy brethren. ^5-27

**5:28** The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you. Amen. ^5-28
