## Chapter 41

**41:1** For the leader. A psalm of David. ^41-1

**41:2** Happy is he who is thoughtful of the wretched;  
in bad times may the LORD keep him from harm. ^41-2

**41:3** May the LORD guard him and preserve him;  
and may he be thought happy in the land.  
Do not subject him to the will of his enemies. ^41-3

**41:4** The LORD will sustain him on his sickbed;  
You shall wholly transform his bed of suffering.[^1] ^41-4

**41:5** I said, “O LORD, have mercy on me,  
heal me, for I have sinned against You.” ^41-5

**41:6** My enemies speak evilly of me,  
“When will he die and his name perish?” ^41-6

**41:7** If one comes to visit, he speaks falsely;  
his mind stores up evil thoughts;  
once outside, he speaks them. ^41-7

**41:8** All my enemies whisper together against me,  
imagining the worst for me. ^41-8

**41:9** “Something baneful has settled in him;  
he’ll not rise from his bed again.” ^41-9

**41:10** My ally in whom I trusted,  
even he who shares my bread,  
has been utterly false to me.[^2] ^41-10

**41:11** But You, O LORD, have mercy on me;  
let me rise again and repay them. ^41-11

**41:12** Then shall I know that You are pleased with me:  
when my enemy cannot shout in triumph over me. ^41-12

**41:13** You will support me because of my integrity,  
and let me abide in Your presence forever. ^41-13

**41:14** Blessed is the LORD, God of Israel,  
from eternity to eternity.  
Amen and Amen. ^41-14

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
