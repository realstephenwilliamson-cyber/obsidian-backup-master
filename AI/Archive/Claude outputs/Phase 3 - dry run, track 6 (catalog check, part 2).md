How to read this list:
- *old → new* shows the values as the Properties panel shows them; lists are separated by "; ", and "(empty)" means no value.

- ² after the id means the note was in the second pass's 30-note sample. ✎ means the body line was reworded at the merge, following the second pass or a check of the evidence.
- Body lines go inside the description, above any **Text layer** line and above the `---` before *Also in your library*. That is where the vault's earlier check lines sit (0 of 527 are below the `---`). In 59 notes the line is appended at the end, because the description is the end of the note.
