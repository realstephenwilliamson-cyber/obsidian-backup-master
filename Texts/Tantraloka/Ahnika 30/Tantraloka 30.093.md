## Mūla

लघुत्वेन तुलाशुद्धिः सद्यः प्रत्ययकारिणी ।
तारः शमरयैः पिण्डो नतिश्च चतुरर्णकम् ॥ ^mula

laghutvena tulāśuddhiḥ sadyaḥ pratyayakāriṇī |
tāraḥ śamarayaiḥ piṇḍo natiśca caturarṇakam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 03 - Chapter Thirty, part 2#^pg71|p. 53]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=71>))
>
> The Saviour (OṀ), the group (piṇḍa) (formed) with (the letters) Ś, M, R, and Y, (along with vowel Ū) and salutation (NAMAḤ) (constitute) the four syllabled (Mantra). The purification (brought about by the Initiation of the) Scales (tulāśuddhi) instils immediate conviction (that it has been effective), by (perceiving that he who has received it loses weight and so) is light. This (Mantra) paralyzes the Śākinīs; it is the weak spot (marman) (of the body), the heart, and life (itself). (with 30.93–94ab)
