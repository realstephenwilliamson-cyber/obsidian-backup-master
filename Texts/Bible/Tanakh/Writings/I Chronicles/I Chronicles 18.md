## Chapter 18

**18:1** [^1]Sometime afterward, David attacked the Philistines and subdued them; and David took Gath and its dependencies from the Philistines. ^18-1

**18:2** He also defeated the Moabites; the Moabites became tributary vassals of David. ^18-2

**18:3** David defeated Hadadezer, king of Zobah-hamath, who was on his way to set up his monument at the Euphrates River. ^18-3

**18:4** David captured 1,000 chariots and 7,000 horsemen and 20,000 foot soldiers of his force; and David hamstrung all the chariot horses except for 100, which he retained. ^18-4

**18:5** And when the Arameans of Damascus came to the aid of King Hadadezer of Zobah-hamath, David struck down 22,000 of the Arameans. ^18-5

**18:6** David stationed [garrisons] in Aram of Damascus, and the Arameans became tributary vassals of David. The LORD gave David victory wherever he went. ^18-6

**18:7** David took the gold shields[^2] carried by Hadadezer’s retinue and brought them to Jerusalem; ^18-7

**18:8** and from Tibbath and Cun, towns of Hadadezer, David took a vast amount of copper, from which Solomon made the bronze tank, the columns, and the bronze vessels. ^18-8

**18:9** When King Tou of Hamath heard that David had defeated the entire army of King Hadadezer of Zobah, ^18-9

**18:10** he sent his son Hadoram to King David to greet him and to congratulate him on his military victory over Hadadezer—for Hadadezer had been at war with Tou; [he brought with him] all manner of gold, silver, and copper objects. ^18-10

**18:11** King David dedicated these to the LORD, along with the other silver and gold that he had taken from all the nations: from Edom, Moab, and Ammon; from the Philistines and the Amalekites. ^18-11

**18:12** Abshai son of Zeruiah struck down Edom in the Valley of Salt, 18,000 in all. ^18-12

**18:13** He stationed garrisons in Edom, and all the Edomites became vassals of David. The LORD gave David victory wherever he went. ^18-13

**18:14** David reigned over all Israel, and David executed true justice among all his people. ^18-14

**18:15** Joab son of Zeruiah was commander of the army; Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud was recorder; ^18-15

**18:16** Zadok son of Ahitub and Abimelech son of Abiathar were priests; Shavsha was scribe; ^18-16

**18:17** Benaiah son of Jehoiada was commander of the Cherethites and the Pelethites; and David’s sons were first ministers of the king. ^18-17

[^1]: With this chapter, cf. 2 Sam. 8.
[^2]: Or “quivers.”
