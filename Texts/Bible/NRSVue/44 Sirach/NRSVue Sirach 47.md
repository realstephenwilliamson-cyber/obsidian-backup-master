# Sirach 47 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Sirach 46|← Sirach 46]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Sirach 48|Sirach 48 →]]

## Nathan

**47:1** After him Nathan rose up to prophesy in the days of David. ^47-1

## David

**47:2** As the fat is set apart from the offering of well-being, so David was set apart from the Israelites. ^47-2

**47:3** He played with lions as though they were young goats and with bears as though they were lambs of the flock. ^47-3

**47:4** In his youth did he not kill a giant and take away the people’s disgrace, when he whirled the stone in the sling and struck down the boasting Goliath? ^47-4

**47:5** For he called on the Lord, the Most High, and he gave strength to his right arm to strike down a mighty warrior and to exalt the power^[Gk *horn*] of his people. ^47-5

**47:6** So they glorified him for the tens of thousands and praised him for the blessings bestowed by the Lord, when the glorious diadem was given to him. ^47-6

**47:7** For he wiped out his enemies on every side and scorned his adversaries the Philistines; he crushed their power^[Gk *horn*] to our own day. ^47-7

**47:8** In all that he did he gave thanks to the Holy One, the Most High, proclaiming his glory; he sang praise with all his heart, and he loved his Maker. ^47-8

**47:9** He placed singers before the altar, to make sweet melody with their voices.^[Other ancient authorities add *and daily they sing their praises*] ^47-9

**47:10** He gave beauty to the festivals and arranged their times throughout the year,^[Gk *to completion*] while they praised God’s^[Gk *his*] holy name and the sanctuary resounded from early morning. ^47-10

**47:11** The Lord took away his sins and exalted his power^[Gk *horn*] forever; he gave him a covenant of kingship and a glorious throne in Israel. ^47-11

## Solomon

**47:12** After him a wise son rose up who because of him lived in security:^[Heb: Gk *in a broad place*] ^47-12

**47:13** Solomon reigned in an age of peace, because God gave him rest all around, so that he might build a house in his name and provide a sanctuary to stand forever. ^47-13

**47:14** How wise you were when you were young! You overflowed like the Nile^[Heb: Gk *a river*] with understanding. ^47-14

**47:15** Your influence spread throughout the earth, and you filled it with proverbs having deep meaning. ^47-15

**47:16** Your fame reached to far-off islands, and you were loved for your peaceful reign. ^47-16

**47:17** For your songs, proverbs, parables, and understanding the nations marveled at you. ^47-17

**47:18** In the name of the Lord God, who is called the God of Israel, you gathered gold like tin and amassed silver like lead. ^47-18

**47:19** But you brought in women to lie at your side, and through your body you were brought into subjection. ^47-19

**47:20** You stained your honor and defiled your family line, so that you brought wrath upon your children, and they were grieved^[Other ancient authorities read *I was grieved*] at your folly, ^47-20

**47:21** because the sovereignty was divided and a rebel kingdom arose out of Ephraim. ^47-21

**47:22** But the Lord will never give up his mercy or cause any of his words^[Heb Syr: Gk *works*] to perish; he will never blot out the descendants of his chosen one or destroy the family line of him who loved him. So he gave a remnant to Jacob and to David a root from his own family. ^47-22

## Rehoboam and Jeroboam

**47:23** Solomon rested with his ancestors and left behind him one of his sons broad in^[Heb Syr: Gk *the people’s*] folly and lacking in sense, Rehoboam, whose policy drove the people to revolt. Then Jeroboam son of Nebat led Israel into sin and started Ephraim on its sinful ways. ^47-23

**47:24** Their sins increased more and more until they were exiled from their land. ^47-24

**47:25** For they sought out every kind of wickedness until vengeance came upon them. ^47-25
