## Mūla

तथापि नादिफान्तोऽयं क्रमो मुख्यः प्रकीर्तितः ।
फकारादिसमुच्चारान्नकारान्तेऽध्वमण्डलम् ॥ ^mula

tathāpi nādiphānto'yaṃ kramo mukhyaḥ prakīrtitaḥ |
phakārādisamuccārānnakārānte'dhvamaṇḍalam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 04 - Chapter Fifteen, part 3#^pg103|p. 85]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=103>))
>
> Although there are many kinds of (alphabets mentioned in the scriptures) in which the seeds and matrices are mixed, even so, this one which begins with N and ends with PH is considered to be the main one. (with 15.127cd–128ab)
>
> How can this full (and perfect) consciousness, that by uttering the letters from PH to N reabsorbs into itself all the cosmic order, be expressed in words? Thus, although she is praised in the scriptures in many ways as the various types (of alphabets in which the order of the letters is mixed up), such as (the one called) Kulaputtilikā, this one, which begins with N and ends with PH, that is, Mālinī, is the main one. (with 15.128cd–130ab)
