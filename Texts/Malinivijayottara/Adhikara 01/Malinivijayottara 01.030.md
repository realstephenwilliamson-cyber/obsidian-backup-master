## Mūla

तत एव कलातत्त्वादव्यक्तमसृजत्ततः  ।
गुणानष्टगुणां तेभ्यो धियं धीतोऽप्यहङ्कृतम्  ॥ ^mula

tata eva kalātattvād avyaktam asṛjat tataḥ[^64] |
guṇān aṣṭaguṇāṁ tebhyo dhiyaṁ dhīto 'py ahaṅkṛtam[^65] || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2022 – June 2022
> p. 44 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=20>))
>
> **Nature and the Lower Principles**
>
> Then (Māyā) generated the Unmanifest (Nature) (avyakta) from the principle of the Force (of limited agency). Then (from that) the qualities (were produced), and from them the intellect with its eight qualities. Similarly, the ego (originated) from the intellect.[^66]

[^64]: k, kh: *tattvamavyaktam*/
[^65]: k, kh, g: *ahaṁkṛtim*
