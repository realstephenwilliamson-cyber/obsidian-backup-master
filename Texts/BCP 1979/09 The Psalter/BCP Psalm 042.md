# Psalm 42

← [[BCP Psalm 041]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 043]] →

*Quemadmodum* · Book Two · Eighth Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 042]]

**42:1** As the deer longs for the water-brooks, \*  
so longs my soul for you, O God. ^42-1

**42:2** My soul is athirst for God, athirst for the living God; \*  
when shall I come to appear before the presence of God? ^42-2

**42:3** My tears have been my food day and night, \*  
while all day long they say to me,  
"Where now is your God?" ^42-3

**42:4** I pour out my soul when I think on these things; \*  
how I went with the multitude and led them into the house of God, ^42-4

**42:5** With the voice of praise and thanksgiving, \*  
among those who keep holy-day. ^42-5

**42:6** Why are you so full of heaviness, O my soul? \*  
and why are you so disquieted within me? ^42-6

**42:7** Put your trust in God; \*  
for I will yet give thanks to him,  
who is the help of my countenance, and my God. ^42-7

**42:8** My soul is heavy within me; \*  
therefore I will remember you from the land of Jordan,  
and from the peak of Mizar among the heights of Hermon. ^42-8

**42:9** One deep calls to another in the noise of your cataracts; \*  
all your rapids and floods have gone over me. ^42-9

**42:10** The LORD grants his loving-kindness in the daytime; \*  
in the night season his song is with me,  
a prayer to the God of my life. ^42-10

[p. 644]

**42:11** I will say to the God of my strength,  
"Why have you forgotten me? \*  
and why do I go so heavily while the enemy oppresses me?" ^42-11

**42:12** While my bones are being broken, \*  
my enemies mock me to my face; ^42-12

**42:13** All day long they mock me \*  
and say to me, "Where now is your God?" ^42-13

**42:14** Why are you so full of heaviness, O my soul? \*  
and why are you so disquieted within me? ^42-14

**42:15** Put your trust in God; \*  
for I will yet give thanks to him,  
who is the help of my countenance, and my God. ^42-15
