# LXX Psalms 114

← [[LXX Psalms 113]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 115]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 116]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 116]] (Heb 116:1–9 = LXX 114) · [[Psalms numbering]]

**114:1** Alleluia. I am well pleased, because the Lord will hearken to the voice of my supplication.  ^114-1

**114:2** Because he has inclined his ear to me, therefore will I call upon him while I live.  ^114-2

**114:3** The pangs of death compassed me; the dangers of hell found me: I found affliction and sorrow.  ^114-3

**114:4** Then I called on the name of the Lord: O Lord, deliver my soul.  ^114-4

**114:5** The Lord is merciful and righteous; yea, our God has pity.  ^114-5

**114:6** The Lord preserves the simple: I was brought low, and he delivered me.[^1]  ^114-6

**114:7** Return to thy rest, O my soul; for the Lord has dealt bountifully with thee.  ^114-7

**114:8** For he has delivered my soul from death, mine eyes from tears, and my feet from falling.  ^114-8

**114:9** I shall be well-pleasing before the Lord in the land of the living.  ^114-9

[^1]: 114:6 *Gr.* infants.
