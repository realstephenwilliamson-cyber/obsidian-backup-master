# Wisdom of Solomon 4 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Wisdom of Solomon 03|← Wisdom of Solomon 3]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Wisdom of Solomon 05|Wisdom of Solomon 5 →]]

**4:1** Better than this is childlessness with virtue, for in the memory of virtue^[Gk *it*] is immortality, because it is known both by God and by mortals. ^4-1

**4:2** When it is present, people imitate^[Other ancient authorities read *honor*] it, and they long for it when it has gone; throughout all time it marches, crowned in triumph, victor in the contest for prizes that are undefiled. ^4-2

**4:3** But the prolific brood of the ungodly will be of no use, and none of their illegitimate seedlings will strike a deep root or take a firm hold. ^4-3

**4:4** For even if they put forth boughs for a while, standing insecurely they will be shaken by the wind, and by the violence of the winds they will be uprooted. ^4-4

**4:5** The branches will be broken off before they come to maturity, and their fruit will be useless, not ripe enough to eat and good for nothing. ^4-5

**4:6** For children born of unlawful unions are witnesses of evil against their parents when God examines them.^[Gk *at their examination*] ^4-6

**4:7** But the righteous, even if they die early, will be at rest. ^4-7

**4:8** For old age is not honored for length of time or measured by number of years, ^4-8

**4:9** but understanding is gray hair for anyone, and a blameless life is ripe old age. ^4-9

**4:10** There was one who pleased God and was loved by him and while living among sinners was taken up. ^4-10

**4:11** He was caught up so that evil might not change his understanding or guile deceive his soul. ^4-11

**4:12** For the fascination of wickedness obscures what is good, and roving desire perverts the innocent mind. ^4-12

**4:13** Being perfected in a short time, he fulfilled long years, ^4-13

**4:14** for his soul was pleasing to the Lord; therefore he hastened him from the midst of wickedness. Yet the peoples saw and did not understand or take such a thing to heart, ^4-14

**4:15** that God’s grace and mercy are with his elect and that he watches over his holy ones. ^4-15

## The triumph of the righteous

**4:16** The righteous who have died will condemn the ungodly who are living, and youth that is quickly perfected^[Or *ended*] will condemn the prolonged old age of the unrighteous. ^4-16

**4:17** For they will see the end of the wise and will not understand what the Lord purposed for them and for what he kept them safe. ^4-17

**4:18** The unrighteous^[Gk *They*] will see and will have contempt for them, but the Lord will laugh them to scorn. ^4-18

**4:19** After this they will become dishonored corpses and an outrage among the dead forever, because he will dash them speechless to the ground and shake them from the foundations; they will be left utterly dry and barren, and they will suffer anguish, and the memory of them will perish. ^4-19

## The final judgment

**4:20** They will come with dread when their sins are reckoned up, and their lawless deeds will convict them to their face. ^4-20
