# Psalms 146 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 145|← Psalms 145]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 147|Psalms 147 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 146|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**146:1** Praise ye the Lord, because psalm is good: to our God be joyful and comely praise. ^146-1

**146:2** The Lord buildeth up Jerusalem: he will gather together the dispersed of Israel. ^146-2

**146:3** Who healeth the broken of heart, and bindeth up their bruises. ^146-3

**146:4** Who telleth the number of the stars: and calleth them all by their names. ^146-4

**146:5** Great is our Lord, and great is his power: and of his wisdom there is no number. ^146-5

**146:6** The Lord lifteth up the meek, and bringeth the wicked down even to the ground. ^146-6

**146:7** Sing ye to the Lord with praise: sing to our God upon the harp. ^146-7

**146:8** Who covereth the heaven with clouds, and prepareth rain for the earth. Who maketh grass to grow on the mountains, and herbs for the service of men. ^146-8

**146:9** Who giveth to beasts their food: and to the young ravens that call upon him. ^146-9

**146:10** He shall not delight in the strength of the horse: nor take pleasure in the legs of a man. ^146-10

**146:11** The Lord taketh pleasure in them that fear him: and in them that hope in his mercy. ^146-11
