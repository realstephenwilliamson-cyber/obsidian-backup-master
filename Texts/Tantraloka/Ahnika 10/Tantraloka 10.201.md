## Mūla

यथा हि चिरदुःखार्तः पश्चादात्तसुखस्थितिः ।
विस्मरत्येव तद्दुःखं सुखविश्रान्तिवर्त्मना ॥ ^mula

yathā hi ciraduḥkhārtaḥ paścādāttasukhasthitiḥ |
vismaratyeva tadduḥkhaṃ sukhaviśrāntivartmanā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 13 - Chapter Ten, part 4#^pg438|p. 419]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=438>))
>
> Just as a person long afflicted by suffering, who subsequently regains (his normal) state of health (sukha), forgets that pain because he rests in (his present) happiness, so is it with those people who are established in (pure) consciousness (saṁvedana), in which ideation (vikalpa) has ceased, have no notion of (even its) existence (sattā), because it has (all) come to rest. (with 10.201–202)
