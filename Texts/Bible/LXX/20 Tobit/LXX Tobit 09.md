# LXX Tobit 09

← [[LXX Tobit 08]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Tobit 10]] →

*Tobit · [[LXX]]*

**9:1** Then Tobias called Raphael, and said unto him,  ^9-1

**9:2** Brother Azarias, take with thee a servant, and two camels, and go to Rages of Media to Gabael, and bring me the money, and bring him to the wedding.  ^9-2

**9:3** For Raguel hath sworn that I shall not depart.  ^9-3

**9:4** But my father counteth the days; and if I tarry long, he will be very sorry.  ^9-4

**9:5** So Raphael went out, and lodged with Gabael, and gave him the handwriting: who brought forth bags which were sealed up, and gave them to him.  ^9-5

**9:6** And early in the morning they went forth both together, and came to the wedding: and Tobias blessed his wife.  ^9-6
