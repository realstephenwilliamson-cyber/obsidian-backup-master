# mantrakaumudī

**Catalog number:** M00229  
**Author:** devanātha thakkur  
**Published:** Dharbanga, India, 1960

## Description

The Mantrakaumudii is by Devanaatha Thakkur who was was born c. 1490 in
Mithilaa where he lived and worked. He is the author numerous works, on
various subjects, not only Tantra. He wrote the Mantrakaumudii at the
age of 75 in 1564. It mentions 99 works on Tantra. It deals with aspects
of the practice of Mantra. Thakkur founded through the general
acceptance of this work a new school of Tantra in Mithilaa which is free
of Vaama practices.
