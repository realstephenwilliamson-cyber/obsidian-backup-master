## Mūla

तद्दृष्टदोषात्क्रोधादेः सम्यक्ज्ञातर्यसौ कुतः ।
गुरवस्तु स्वयंभ्वादि वर्ज्यं यद्यामलादिषु ॥ ^mula

taddṛṣṭadoṣātkrodhādeḥ samyakjñātaryasau kutaḥ |
guravastu svayaṃbhvādi varjyaṃ yadyāmalādiṣu || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 14 - Chapter Twenty-Three#^pg321|p. 301]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=321>))
>
> (The injunction) in the Devyāyāmala to avoid (teachers) from Kāñcī etc. is because those (who come from these regions) are seen to have defects such as anger and the like. How (can that be in the case of teacher) who possesses right knowledge? (with 23.14cd–15ab)
>
> The teachers have declared that (statements) in the Devyāyāmala and elsewhere, (such as) ‘a ‘self-generated’ (teacher who has no teacher) should be avoided’, all refer to (a teacher) who specializes in the performance of rituals (karmin) (not one who focuses on knowledge of the scriptures). (with 23.15cd–16ab)
