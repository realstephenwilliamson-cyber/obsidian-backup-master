## Mūla

शिवतत्त्वं ततः पश्चात्तेजोरूपमनाकुलम् ।
सर्वेषां व्यापकत्वेन सबाह्याभ्यन्तरं स्मरेत् ॥ ^mula

śivatattvaṁ tataḥ paścāt tejorūpam anākulam |
sarveṣāṁ vyāpakatvena sabāhyābhyantaraṁ smaret || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, October 2023 – December 2023
> p. 18 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapter 6 (2023).pdf#page=6>))
>
> Beyond that (one should recollect) the Śiva principle, which is undisturbed (*anākulam*) radiant energy (*tejas*), as pervading all (the principles), along with (everything that is) external and internal.[^9]

[^9]: MV 6/5 is quoted in TĀ 16/105. All the depositions end with tranquil formless (niṣkala) Śiva. In other words, the tranquilility of an infinite, omnipresent Void of Śiva consciousness is the ultimate goal. This is the ultimate condition of the initiand at the climax of his initiation.
