# Anupāya — The Direct Path (Āhnika 2)

> [!abstract] Definition
> The "methodless method" or "pathless path" expounded in *Tantrāloka* (Āhnika 2), where realization occurs instantly through the sovereign grace of the Guru or spontaneous illumination without the intermediary of ritual, contemplation, or yogic discipline.

## Core Characteristics
- In *Anupāya*, there is no journey from ignorance to enlightenment: reality is recognized as already completely manifest (*svaprakāśa*).
- As Abhinavagupta declares: *"Because consciousness is ever-present, what means could possibly reveal it? The sun cannot be illuminated by a firefly."*
- Reserved for those whose awareness is so purified that a single word from the teacher sparks irreversible awakening.
