## Mūla

आ तन्मयत्वसंसिद्धेरा चाभीष्टफलोदयात् ।
पुत्रकः साधको व्यक्तमव्यक्तं वा समाश्रयेत् ॥ ^mula

ā tanmayatvasaṃsiddherā cābhīṣṭaphalodayāt |
putrakaḥ sādhako vyaktamavyaktaṃ vā samāśrayet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 18 - Chapter Twenty-Seven#^pg424|p. 404]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=424>))
>
> As long as (he has not) attained a state of identification (with the deity, or) until the desired fruit has not arisen, a spiritual son (putraka) and adept (sāādhaka) may resort (samāśrayet) to a manifest (outwardly visible iconic form) (vyakta) or (to a mental representation or an aniconic form in which the deity) is not (externally visibly) manifest (avyakta).
