## Mūla

इति मातृगणः प्रोक्तः सर्वकामफलप्रदः ।
योगिनां योगसिद्ध्यर्थं किमन्यत्परिपृच्छसि ॥ ^mula

iti mātṛgaśaḥ[^59] proktaḥ sarvakāmaphalapradaḥ |
yogināṁ yogasiddhyarthaṁ kim anyat paripṛcchasi || ^iast

*Muktabodha's e-text of the KSTS edition (M00160) has:* iti mātṛgaṇaḥ proktaḥ sarvakāmaphalapradaḥ | yogināṃ yogasiddhyarthaṃ kimanyatparipṛcchasi ||

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2023 – March 2023
> p. 53 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=53>))

> The host of Mantras[^60] that bestows the fruits of all desires has thus been taught in order that yogis may attain the accomplishment of Yoga. What else are you asking (me to tell you)?
