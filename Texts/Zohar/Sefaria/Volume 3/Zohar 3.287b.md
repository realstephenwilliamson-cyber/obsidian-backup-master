# Zohar 3.287b

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 9.14 - Parashat Ha'azinu#^z3-287b|Parashat Ha’azinu, at 3:287b]]

*Volume 3, folio 287b* · Ha'Azinu

*Passage Ha'Azinu 5:20 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 3.287a#^ha-azinu-5-20|Ha'Azinu 5:20]].*

**Ha'Azinu 5:21** Observe that Moses made a long exordium saying, “Give ear, ye heavens, and hear, O earth, let my discourse drop as the dew”, etc. Why all this? “Because I will call on the name of the Lord”. ^ha-azinu-5-21

**Ha'Azinu 5:22** We have learnt that when Moses said “Give ear, ye heavens, and I will speak”, all worlds were shaken. A voice came forth and said: Moses, why dost thou shake the world, being but a mortal? He replied: Because I will call on the name of the Lord. Forthwith they were silent and listened to his words.’[^1] ^ha-azinu-5-22

[^1]: Here in the original follows the *Idra Zuta*, v. Appendix.
