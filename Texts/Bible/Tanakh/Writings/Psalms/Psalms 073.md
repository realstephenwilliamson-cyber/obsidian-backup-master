## Chapter 73

**73:1** A psalm of Asaph.  
God is truly good to Israel,  
to those whose heart is pure. ^73-1

**73:2** As for me, my feet had almost strayed,  
my steps were nearly led off course, ^73-2

**73:3** for I envied the wanton;  
I saw the wicked at ease. ^73-3

**73:4** Death has no pangs for them;  
their body is healthy. ^73-4

**73:5** They have no part in the travail of men;  
they are not afflicted like the rest of mankind. ^73-5

**73:6** So pride adorns their necks,  
lawlessness enwraps them as a mantle. ^73-6

**73:7** Fat shuts out their eyes;  
their fancies are extravagant.[^1] ^73-7

**73:8** They scoff and plan evil;  
from their eminence they plan wrongdoing. ^73-8

**73:9** They set their mouths against heaven,  
and their tongues range over the earth. ^73-9

**73:10** So they pound His people again and again,  
until they are drained of their very last tear.[^2] ^73-10

**73:11** Then they say, “How could God know?  
Is there knowledge with the Most High?” ^73-11

**73:12** Such are the wicked;  
ever tranquil, they amass wealth. ^73-12

**73:13** It was for nothing that I kept my heart pure  
and washed my hands in innocence, ^73-13

**73:14** seeing that I have been constantly afflicted,  
that each morning brings new punishments. ^73-14

**73:15** Had I decided to say these things,  
I should have been false to the circle of Your disciples. ^73-15

**73:16** So I applied myself to understand this,  
but it seemed a hopeless task ^73-16

**73:17** till I entered God’s sanctuary  
and reflected on their fate. ^73-17

**73:18** You surround them with flattery;  
You make them fall through blandishments. ^73-18

**73:19** How suddenly are they ruined,  
wholly swept away by terrors. ^73-19

**73:20** When You are aroused You despise their image,  
as one does a dream after waking, O Lord.[^3] ^73-20

**73:21** My mind was stripped of its reason,  
my feelings were numbed.[^4] ^73-21

**73:22** I was a dolt, without knowledge;  
I was brutish toward You. ^73-22

**73:23** Yet I was always with You,  
You held my right hand; ^73-23

**73:24** You guided me by Your counsel  
and led me toward honor.[^5] ^73-24

**73:25** Whom else have I in heaven?  
And having You, I want no one on earth. ^73-25

**73:26** My body and mind fail;  
but God is the stay[^6] of my mind, my portion forever. ^73-26

**73:27** Those who keep far from You perish;  
You annihilate all who are untrue to You. ^73-27

**73:28** As for me, nearness to God is good;  
I have made the Lord GOD my refuge,  
that I may recount all Your works. ^73-28

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^4]: Lit. “I was pierced through in my kidneys.”
[^5]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain; others “And afterward receive me with glory.”
[^6]: Lit. “rock.”
