## Chapter 28

**28:1** Of David.  
O LORD, I call to You;  
my rock, do not disregard me,  
for if You hold aloof from me,  
I shall be like those gone down into the Pit. ^28-1

**28:2** Listen to my plea for mercy  
when I cry out to You,  
when I lift my hands  
toward Your inner sanctuary. ^28-2

**28:3** Do not count me[^1] with the wicked and evildoers  
who profess goodwill toward their fellows  
while malice is in their heart. ^28-3

**28:4** Pay them according to their deeds,  
their malicious acts;  
according to their handiwork pay them,  
give them their deserts. ^28-4

**28:5** For they do not consider the LORD’s deeds,  
the work of His hands.  
May He tear them down,  
never to rebuild them! ^28-5

**28:6** Blessed is the LORD,  
for He listens to my plea for mercy. ^28-6

**28:7** The LORD is my strength and my shield;  
my heart trusts in Him.  
I was helped,[^2] and my heart exulted,  
so I will glorify Him with my song. ^28-7

**28:8** The LORD is their strength;[^3]  
He is a stronghold for the deliverance of His anointed. ^28-8

**28:9** Deliver and bless Your very own people;  
tend them and sustain them forever. ^28-9

[^1]: Or “drag me off”; Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Or “strengthened.”
[^3]: Septuagint, Saadia, and others render, and some mss. read, ‘oz le‘ammo, “the strength of His people.”
