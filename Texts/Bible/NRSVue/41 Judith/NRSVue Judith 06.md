# Judith 6 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Judith 05|← Judith 5]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Judith 07|Judith 7 →]]

## Achior handed over to the Israelites

**6:1** When the disturbance made by the people outside the council had died down, Holofernes, the commander of the Assyrian army, said to Achior^[Other ancient authorities add *and to all the Moabites*] in the presence of all the foreign contingents: ^6-1

**6:2** “Who are you, Achior and you mercenaries of Ephraim, to prophesy among us as you have done today and tell us not to make war against the people of Israel because their God will defend them? What god is there except Nebuchadnezzar? He will send his forces and destroy them from the face of the earth. Their God will not save them; ^6-2

**6:3** we the king’s^[Gk *his*] servants will destroy them as one person. They cannot resist the might of our cavalry. ^6-3

**6:4** We will overwhelm them;^[Other ancient authorities add *with it*] their mountains will be drunk with their blood, and their fields will be full of their dead. Not even their footprints will survive our attack; they will utterly perish. So says King Nebuchadnezzar, lord of the whole earth. For he has spoken; none of his words shall be in vain. ^6-4

**6:5** “As for you, Achior, you Ammonite mercenary, you have said these words in a moment of perversity; you shall not see my face again from this day until I take revenge on this people that came out of Egypt. ^6-5

**6:6** Then at my return the sword of my army and the spear^[OL Syr: Gk *people*] of my servants shall pierce your sides, and you shall fall among their wounded. ^6-6

**6:7** Now my slaves are going to take you back into the hill country and put you in one of the towns beside the passes. ^6-7

**6:8** You will not die until you perish along with them. ^6-8

**6:9** If you really hope in your heart that they will not be taken, then do not look downcast! I have spoken, and none of my words shall fail to come true.” ^6-9

**6:10** Then Holofernes ordered his slaves who waited on him in his tent to seize Achior and take him away to Bethulia and hand him over to the Israelites. ^6-10

**6:11** So the slaves took him and led him out of the camp into the plain, and from the plain they went up into the hill country and came to the springs below Bethulia. ^6-11

**6:12** When the men of the town saw them,^[Other ancient authorities add *on the top of the hill*] they seized their weapons and ran out of the town to the top of the hill, and all the slingers kept them from coming up by throwing stones at them. ^6-12

**6:13** So having taken shelter below the hill, they bound Achior and left him lying at the foot of the hill and returned to their master. ^6-13

**6:14** Then the Israelites came down from their town and found him; they untied him and brought him into Bethulia and placed him before the magistrates of their town, ^6-14

**6:15** who in those days were Uzziah son of Micah, of the tribe of Simeon, and Chabris son of Gothoniel, and Charmis son of Melchiel. ^6-15

**6:16** They called together all the elders of the town, and all their young men and women ran to the assembly. They set Achior in the midst of all their people, and Uzziah questioned him about what had happened. ^6-16

**6:17** He answered and told them what had taken place at the council of Holofernes, and all that he had said in the presence of the Assyrian leaders, and all that Holofernes had boasted he would do against the house of Israel. ^6-17

**6:18** Then the people fell down and worshiped God and cried out: ^6-18

**6:19** “O Lord God of heaven, see their arrogance, and have pity on our people in their humiliation, and look kindly today on the faces of those who are consecrated to you.” ^6-19

**6:20** Then they reassured Achior and praised him highly. ^6-20

**6:21** Uzziah took him from the assembly to his own house and gave a banquet for the elders, and all that night they called on the God of Israel for help. ^6-21
