## Supplication for mercy

*A Song of Ascents.*

**123:1** To you I lift up my eyes, O you who are enthroned in the heavens! ^123-1

**123:2** As the eyes of servants look to the hand of their master, as the eyes of a maid to the hand of her mistress, so our eyes look to the LORD our God, until he has mercy upon us. ^123-2

**123:3** Have mercy upon us, O LORD, have mercy upon us, for we have had more than enough of contempt. ^123-3

**123:4** Our soul has had more than its fill of the scorn of those who are at ease, of the contempt of the proud. ^123-4
