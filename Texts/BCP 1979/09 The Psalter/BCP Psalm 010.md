# Psalm 10

← [[BCP Psalm 009]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 011]] →

*Ut quid, Domine?* · Book One · Second Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 010]]

**10:1** Why do you stand so far off, O LORD, \*  
and hide yourself in time of trouble? ^10-1

**10:2** The wicked arrogantly persecute the poor, \*  
but they are trapped in the schemes they have devised. ^10-2

**10:3** The wicked boast of their heart's desire; \*  
the covetous curse and revile the LORD. ^10-3

**10:4** The wicked are so proud that they care not for God; \*  
their only thought is, "God does not matter." ^10-4

[p. 595] ^pg595

**10:5** Their ways are devious at all times;  
your judgments are far above out of their sight; \*  
they defy all their enemies. ^10-5

**10:6** They say in their heart, "I shall not be shaken; \*  
no harm shall happen to me ever." ^10-6

**10:7** Their mouth is full of cursing, deceit, and oppression; \*  
under their tongue are mischief and wrong. ^10-7

**10:8** They lurk in ambush in public squares  
and in secret places they murder the innocent; \*  
they spy out the helpless. ^10-8

**10:9** They lie in wait, like a lion in a covert;  
they lie in wait to seize upon the lowly; \*  
they seize the lowly and drag them away in their net. ^10-9

**10:10** The innocent are broken and humbled before them; \*  
the helpless fall before their power. ^10-10

**10:11** They say in their heart, "God has forgotten; \*  
he hides his face; he will never notice." ^10-11

**10:12** Rise up, O LORD;  
lift up your hand, O God; \*  
do not forget the afflicted. ^10-12

**10:13** Why should the wicked revile God? \*  
why should they say in their heart, "You do not care"? ^10-13

**10:14** Surely, you behold trouble and misery; \*  
you see it and take it into your own hand. ^10-14

**10:15** The helpless commit themselves to you, \*  
for you are the helper of orphans. ^10-15

**10:16** Break the power of the wicked and evil; \*  
search out their wickedness until you find none. ^10-16

[p. 596] ^pg596

**10:17** The LORD is King for ever and ever; \*  
the ungodly shall perish from his land. ^10-17

**10:18** The LORD will hear the desire of the humble; \*  
you will strengthen their heart and your ears shall hear; ^10-18

**10:19** To give justice to the orphan and oppressed, \*  
so that mere mortals may strike terror no more. ^10-19
