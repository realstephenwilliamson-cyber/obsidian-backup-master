# Job 31 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Job 30|← Job 30]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Job 32|Job 32 →]]

**31:1** “I have made a covenant with my eyes; how, then, could I look upon a virgin? ^31-1

**31:2** What would be my portion from God above and my heritage from the Almighty^[Traditional rendering of Heb *Shaddai*] on high? ^31-2

**31:3** Does not calamity befall the unrighteous and disaster the workers of iniquity? ^31-3

**31:4** Does he not see my ways and number all my steps? ^31-4

**31:5** “If I have walked with falsehood, and my foot has hurried to deceit— ^31-5

**31:6** let me be weighed in a just balance, and let God know my integrity!— ^31-6

**31:7** if my step has turned aside from the way, and my heart has followed my eyes, and if any spot has clung to my hands, ^31-7

**31:8** then let me sow and another eat, and let what grows for me be rooted out. ^31-8

**31:9** “If my heart has been enticed by a woman and I have lain in wait at my neighbor’s door, ^31-9

**31:10** then let my wife grind for another, and let other men kneel over her. ^31-10

**31:11** For that would be a heinous crime; that would be a criminal offense; ^31-11

**31:12** for that would be a fire consuming down to Abaddon, and it would burn to the root all my harvest. ^31-12

**31:13** “If I have rejected the cause of my male or female slaves, when they brought a complaint against me, ^31-13

**31:14** what then shall I do when God rises up? When he makes inquiry, what shall I answer him? ^31-14

**31:15** Did not he who made me in the womb make them? And did not one fashion us in the womb? ^31-15

**31:16** “If I have withheld anything that the poor desired or have caused the eyes of the widow to fail ^31-16

**31:17** or have eaten my morsel alone and the orphan has not eaten from it— ^31-17

**31:18** for from my youth I reared the orphan^[Heb *him*] like a father, and from my mother’s womb I guided the widow^[Heb *her*]— ^31-18

**31:19** if I have seen anyone perish for lack of clothing or a poor person without covering, ^31-19

**31:20** whose loins have not blessed me, and who was not warmed with the fleece of my sheep; ^31-20

**31:21** if I have raised my hand against the orphan because I saw I had supporters at the gate; ^31-21

**31:22** then let my shoulder blade fall from my shoulder, and let my arm be broken from its socket. ^31-22

**31:23** For I was in terror of calamity from God, and I could not have faced his majesty. ^31-23

**31:24** “If I have made gold my trust or called fine gold my confidence, ^31-24

**31:25** if I have rejoiced because my wealth was great or because my hand had gotten much, ^31-25

**31:26** if I have looked at the sun^[Heb *the light*] when it shone or the moon moving in splendor, ^31-26

**31:27** and my heart has been secretly enticed, and my mouth has kissed my hand, ^31-27

**31:28** this also would be an iniquity to be punished by the judges, for I should have been false to God above. ^31-28

**31:29** “If I have rejoiced at the ruin of those who hated me or exulted when evil overtook them— ^31-29

**31:30** I have not let my mouth sin by asking for their lives with a curse— ^31-30

**31:31** if those of my tent ever said, ‘O that we might be sated with his flesh!’^[Meaning of Heb uncertain]— ^31-31

**31:32** the stranger has not lodged in the street; I have opened my doors to the traveler— ^31-32

**31:33** if I have concealed my transgressions as others do,^[Or *as Adam did*] by hiding my iniquity in my bosom, ^31-33

**31:34** because I stood in great fear of the multitude, and the contempt of families terrified me, so that I kept silence and did not go out of doors— ^31-34

**31:35** O that I had one to hear me! (Here is my signature! Let the Almighty^[Traditional rendering of Heb *Shaddai*] answer me!) O that I had the indictment written by my adversary! ^31-35

**31:36** Surely I would carry it on my shoulder; I would bind it on me like a crown; ^31-36

**31:37** I would give him an account of all my steps; like a prince I would approach him. ^31-37

**31:38** “If my land has cried out against me and its furrows have wept together, ^31-38

**31:39** if I have eaten its yield without payment and caused the death of its owners, ^31-39

**31:40** let thorns grow instead of wheat and foul weeds instead of barley.” The words of Job are ended. ^31-40
