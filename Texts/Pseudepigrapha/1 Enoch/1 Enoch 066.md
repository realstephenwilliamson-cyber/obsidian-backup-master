# 1 Enoch 66 (Charles)

[[1 Enoch 065|← 1 Enoch 65]] · [[1 Enoch|index]] · [[1 Enoch 067|1 Enoch 67 →]]

### LXVI. The Angels of the Waters bidden to hold them in Check.

**66:1** And after that he showed me the angels of punishment who are prepared to come and let loose all the powers of the waters which are beneath in the earth in order to bring judgement and destruction on all who [abide and] dwell on the earth. ^66-1

**66:2** And the Lord of Spirits gave commandment to the angels who were going forth, that they should not cause **the waters** to rise but should hold them in check; for those angels were over the powers of the waters. ^66-2

**66:3** And I went away from the presence of Enoch. ^66-3
