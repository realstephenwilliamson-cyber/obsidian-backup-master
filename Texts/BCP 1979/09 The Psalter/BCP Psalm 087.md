# Psalm 87

← [[BCP Psalm 086]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 088]] →

*Fundamenta ejus* · Book Three · Seventeenth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 087]]

**87:1** On the holy mountain stands the city he has founded; \*  
the LORD loves the gates of Zion  
more than all the dwellings of Jacob. ^87-1

**87:2** Glorious things are spoken of you, \*  
O city of our God. ^87-2

**87:3** I count Egypt and Babylon among those who know me; \*  
behold Philistia, Tyre, and Ethiopia:  
in Zion were they born. ^87-3

**87:4** Of Zion it shall be said, "Everyone was born in her, \*  
and the Most High himself shall sustain her." ^87-4

**87:5** The LORD will record as he enrolls the peoples, \*  
"These also were born there." ^87-5

**87:6** The singers and the dancers will say, \*  
"All my fresh springs are in you." ^87-6
