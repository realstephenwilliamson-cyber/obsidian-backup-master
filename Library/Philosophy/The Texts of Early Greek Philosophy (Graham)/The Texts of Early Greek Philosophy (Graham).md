Translated by Daniel W. Graham. Cambridge University Press, Cambridge, 2010.

Converted from the PDF on 22 Oct 2026 (fresh OCR with Tesseract 5 (grc+eng) of the page images, each half of the two-page spreads read on its own (the earlier OCR layer broke up the Greek)). > [!note] Fresh OCR of the page images (Greek and English)
> Each PDF page holds two printed pages: the Greek text and its apparatus on the left, Graham's translation on the right, given one after the other. Both were read by OCR (Tesseract, Ancient Greek and English models). The English is reliable apart from a few words at the inner margin. The Greek is not reliable for exact reading: accents and letters are often misread, short words are sometimes dropped, and some Greek words come out in Latin letters (γὰρ as *yap*). For the Greek, read the PDF (the *Open the PDF* button on the catalog note).
