## Mūla

तावती चैश्वरी रात्रिर्यत्र प्राणः प्रशाम्यति ।
प्राणगर्भस्थमप्यत्र विश्वं सौषुम्नवर्त्मना ॥ ^mula

tāvatī caiśvarī rātriryatra prāṇaḥ praśāmyati |
prāṇagarbhasthamapyatra viśvaṃ sauṣumnavartmanā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 12 - Chapter Six, part 5#^pg390|p. 375]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=390>))
> Dyczkowski's numbering differs here; his markers: (157cd-159a) (157-158ab), (159b-160ab) (158d-159).
>
> The duration of Māyā multiplied by a hundred trillion (parāārdha) is equivalent to a day of Īśvara. (During this time,) the Sound (nāda) which is the vital breath (prāna) emits the universe. Such is also (the length of) the Night of Īśvara, in which the vital breath comes to rest, and with it the universe that resides within it. (with 6.157–158ab)
>
> When the vital breath is stilled in the Cavity of Brahmā (brahmabila) (on top of the head, having arrived there) by the path of Suṣumnā, consciousness, pervading (all things), remains. That too is dissolved away (progressively), one aspect at a time (from level to level) into ever subtler states. (with 6.158d–159)
