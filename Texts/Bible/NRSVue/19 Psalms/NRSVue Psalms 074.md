# Psalm 74 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 073|← Psalm 73]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 075|Psalm 75 →]]

## Plea for help in time of national humiliation

*A Maskil of Asaph.*

**74:1** O God, why do you cast us off forever? Why does your anger smoke against the sheep of your pasture? ^74-1

**74:2** Remember your congregation, which you acquired long ago, which you redeemed to be the tribe of your heritage. Remember Mount Zion, where you came to dwell. ^74-2

**74:3** Direct your steps to the perpetual ruins; the enemy has destroyed everything in the sanctuary. ^74-3

**74:4** Your foes have roared within your holy place; they set up their emblems there. ^74-4

**74:5** At the upper entrance they hacked the wooden trellis with axes.^[Cn Compare Gk Syr: Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^74-5

**74:6** And then, with hatchets and hammers, they smashed all its carved work. ^74-6

**74:7** They set your sanctuary on fire; they desecrated the dwelling place of your name, bringing it to the ground. ^74-7

**74:8** They said to themselves, “We will utterly subdue them”; they burned all the meeting places of God in the land. ^74-8

**74:9** We do not see our emblems; there is no longer any prophet, and there is no one among us who knows how long. ^74-9

**74:10** How long, O God, is the foe to scoff? Is the enemy to revile your name forever? ^74-10

**74:11** Why do you hold back your hand; why do you keep your hand in^[Cn: Heb *do you consume your right hand from*] your bosom? ^74-11

**74:12** Yet God my King is from of old, working salvation in the earth. ^74-12

**74:13** You divided the sea by your might; you broke the heads of the dragons in the waters. ^74-13

**74:14** You crushed the heads of Leviathan; you gave him as food^[Heb *food for the people*] for the creatures of the wilderness. ^74-14

**74:15** You cut openings for springs and torrents; you dried up ever-flowing streams. ^74-15

**74:16** Yours is the day, yours also the night; you established the luminaries^[Or *moon*; Heb *light*] and the sun. ^74-16

**74:17** You have fixed all the bounds of the earth; you made summer and winter. ^74-17

**74:18** Remember this, O LORD, how the enemy scoffs, and an impious people reviles your name. ^74-18

**74:19** Do not deliver the soul of your dove to the wild animals; do not forget the life of your poor forever. ^74-19

**74:20** Have regard for your^[Gk Syr: Heb *the*] covenant, for the dark places of the land are full of the haunts of violence. ^74-20

**74:21** Do not let the downtrodden be put to shame; let the poor and needy praise your name. ^74-21

**74:22** Rise up, O God, plead your cause; remember how the impious scoff at you all day long. ^74-22

**74:23** Do not forget the clamor of your foes, the uproar of your adversaries that goes up continually. ^74-23
