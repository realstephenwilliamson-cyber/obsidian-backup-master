## Mūla

गुरोर्भवेत्तदा सर्वसाम्ये को भेद उच्यताम् ।
अंशेनाप्यथ वैषम्ये न ततोऽर्थक्रिया हि सा ॥ ^mula

gurorbhavettadā sarvasāmye ko bheda ucyatām |
aṃśenāpyatha vaiṣamye na tato'rthakriyā hi sā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 07 - Chapter Sixteen, part 3#^pg140|p. 120]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=140>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 16.270cd–271ab.
>
> If by (deep) contemplation (bhāvanā) of the meaning (purpose, goal and ultimate nature of) Mantras (mantrārtha), the teacher’s discourse becomes like that (of Mantra), then if it is the same in all respects, then pray tell, what is the difference (between them)? (with 16.270cd–271ab)
>
> (But) if there is even (just a small) partial difference, the purposeful action (arthakriyā) (derived) from it is not (as is that of Mantra). (with 16.271cd)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* ननु असिद्धं सर्वसाम्यं, मन्त्रेतरस्य हि अभिजल्पस्य भिन्नैवानुपूर्वी ?--इत्याशङ्क्याह--

> अंशेनाप्यथ वैषम्ये न ततोऽर्थक्रिया हि सा ॥

वैषम्य इति--आनुपूर्व्यादिना मन्त्राद्भेदे । तत इति--अभिसञ्जल्पात् । सेति--मन्त्रकार्या ॥ २७१ ॥
