## Mūla

एतावद्भिः पदैरेतदार्यावाक्यं द्वितीयकम् ।
हंस हयग्रीव विभो सदाशिवस्त्वं परोऽसि जीवाख्यः ॥ ^mula

etāvadbhiḥ padairetadāryāvākyaṃ dvitīyakam |
haṃsa hayagrīva vibho sadāśivastvaṃ paro'si jīvākhyaḥ || ^iast

> This is the second declaration, set in Ārya metre; it is made of as many words (as the preceding one). (with 30.69ab)
>
> 3) “O Haṁsa! Hayagrīva! Pervasive Lord! You are Sadāśiva! You are the Supreme, called the individual soul (jīva). Your body is the Point, which is the union of Sun, Moon, and Fire. Ha! Ha! Ha! Come forth (from the body)!” (with 30.69cd–70ab)
