## Chapter 141

**141:1** A psalm of David.  
I call You, O LORD, hasten to me;  
give ear to my cry when I call You. ^141-1

**141:2** Take my prayer as an offering of incense,  
my upraised hands as an evening sacrifice. ^141-2

**141:3** O LORD, set a guard over my mouth,  
a watch at the door of my lips; ^141-3

**141:4** let my mind not turn to an evil thing,  
to practice deeds of wickedness  
with men who are evildoers;  
let me not feast on their dainties. ^141-4

**141:5** [^1]Let the righteous man strike me in loyalty,  
let him reprove me;  
let my head not refuse such choice oil.  
My prayers are still against their[^2] evil deeds. ^141-5

**141:6** May their judges slip on the rock,  
but let my words be heard, for they are sweet. ^141-6

**141:7** As when the earth is cleft and broken up  
our bones are scattered at the mouth of Sheol. ^141-7

**141:8** My eyes are fixed upon You, O GOD my Lord;  
I seek refuge in You, do not put me in jeopardy. ^141-8

**141:9** Keep me from the trap laid for me,  
and from the snares of evildoers. ^141-9

**141:10** Let the wicked fall into their nets  
while I alone come through. ^141-10

[^1]: Meaning of vv. 5–7 uncertain.
[^2]: I.e., the evildoers of v. 4.
