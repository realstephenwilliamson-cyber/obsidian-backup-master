## Mūla

मन्त्रमन्त्रेश्वरेशानाः शक्तिशंभू च किर्तितौ ।
प्रलयाकलभेदेऽपि स्वं विज्ञानकलावुभौ ॥ ^mula

mantramantreśvareśānāḥ śaktiśambhū ca kirtitau |
pralayākalabhede 'pi svam vijñānakalāv ubhau || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, October 2022 – December 2022
> p. 20 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=27>))
>
> (B) When the division is thirteen-fold (1) (the waking state corresponds to) the innate nature. (2-5) Both the two Deconditioned (subjects),[^47] Mantras, Lords of Mantras, (their) Lords (the Great Lords Mantra) as well as Śakti and Śiva are as before. (with 2.29cd–31ab)

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, October 2022 – December 2022
> p. 20 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=27>))
>
> (C) In the (eleven-fold) division when the Dissolution Deconditioned (perceivers are the object, the five states correspond to) (1) (their) own (innate nature) (svam), (that is, the first two types of perceivers abiding as wakeful awareness), (2) both the Consciousness Deconditioned (perceiver) and (3) its power (in dreaming and deep sleep, respectively); (4) the Mantras, Lords of Mantras and (their) Lords (the Great Lords of Mantra) (in the Fourth state), (5) Śakti and Śiva, as before (in the state Beyond the Fourth). (with 2.30cd–31ab)

[^47]: Read with MS G *svarūpam akalau* for *svarūpasakalau.*
