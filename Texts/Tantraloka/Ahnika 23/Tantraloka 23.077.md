## Mūla

अत एव पुराभूतगुर्वभावो यदा तदा ।
तदन्यं लक्षणोपेतमाश्रयेत्पुनरुन्मुखः ॥ ^mula

ata eva purābhūtagurvabhāvo yadā tadā |
tadanyaṃ lakṣaṇopetamāśrayetpunarunmukhaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 14 - Chapter Twenty-Three#^pg350|p. 330]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=350>))

> Thus, if the preceding teacher is no longer alive, (his disciple) again intent (on liberation) should take refuge with another one who is endowed with (his same) mark (of excellence).
