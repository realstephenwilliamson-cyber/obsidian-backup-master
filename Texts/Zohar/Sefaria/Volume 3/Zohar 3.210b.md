# Zohar 3.210b

← [[Zohar 3.210a]] · [[Zohar (Soncino)]] · [[Zohar 3.211a]] →

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 9.10 - Commentary on Balaq, continued#^z3-210b|Commentary on Balaq, continued, at 3:210b]]

*Volume 3, folio 210b* · Balak

**Balak 45:459** R. Simeon said: ‘The loathsomeness of Balaam may be seen from the fact that in all the section it never says, “The Lord said” or “spoke” to Balaam. What it says is, “The Lord placed a word in the mouth of Balaam”, like one who places a bit in the mouth of an ass. ^balak-45-459

**Balak 45:460** God said to him: Sinner, think not that through you the blessing of my sons will be confirmed or otherwise. They do not require you; but return to Balak, and when you open your mouth, it will not be to say your own words, but *KOh*[^1] (thus) will speak out of your mouth.’ ^balak-45-460

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Balak 45:461–45:463.*

**Balak 45:464** COME CURSE ME JACOB. R. Jose said: This means: Cast them down from the grade in which they stand, for then they will be uprooted from the world. COME, PROVOKE ISRAEL; that is, the supernal Israel, so that wrath may be aroused. ^balak-45-464

**Balak 45:465** FOR FROM THE TOP OF THE ROCKS I SEE HIM. R. Isaac said: These are the patriarchs. AND FROM THE HILLS I BEHOLD HIM: these are the matriarchs. From neither side can they be cursed. ^balak-45-465

**Balak 45:466** R. Jose said: ‘It means: Who can prevail against Israel, since he is attached to the source whence all rocks, that is, all forceful deeds, issue. LO IT IS A PEOPLE THAT DWELL ALONE: as it is written, “The Lord did lead him alone” (Deut. 32, 12).’ ^balak-45-466

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Balak 45:467.*

**Balak 45:468** WHO CAN COUNT THE DUST OF JACOB: this dust is the place whence the first man was created, and from that dust issue many hosts and camps, many flames and arrows and catapults and lances and swords. ^balak-45-468

**Balak 45:469** OR NUMBER THE FOURTH PART OF ISRAEL: this refers to David, who was the fourth foot of the Throne.[^2] ^balak-45-469

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Balak 45:470.*

[^1]: The Shekinah.
[^2]: The last 3 lines of the Hebrew text are not found in our translation.
