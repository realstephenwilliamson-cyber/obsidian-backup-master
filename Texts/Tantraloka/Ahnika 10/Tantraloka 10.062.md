## Mūla

स्यादित्येतत्स्वपक्षघ्नं दुष्प्रयोगास्त्रवत्तव ।
अस्माकं तु स्वप्रकाशशिवतामात्रवादिनाम् ॥ ^mula

syādityetatsvapakṣaghnaṃ duṣprayogāstravattava |
asmākaṃ tu svaprakāśaśivatāmātravādinām || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 11 - Chapter Ten, part 2#^pg353|p. 334]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=353>))
>
> (The objection) that has been voiced, namely, that perceptibility is an attribute of the (perceivable) entity; (as such,) it would be (perceptible) to all – (our response to that reply is that,) like a weapon badly handled, this (response itself) confutes your own view, (not ours). (with 10.61cd–62ab)
>
> We who are the votaries of the doctrine that only Śiva exists and is self-luminous never say that He manifests to another. (with 10.62cd–63ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* अस्मत्पक्षे पुनः परप्रकाशात्मा स्वतन्त्रः शिव एवास्तीति तदपेक्षया नान्यः कश्चिदिति सर्वार्थस्यैवाभावात् कं प्रति चकास्यात् -- इत्याह - -

> अस्माकं तु स्वप्रकाशशिवतामात्रवादिनाम् ॥
> अन्यं प्रति चकास्तीति वच एव न विद्यते ।

मायापदे तु प्रमातृभेदेऽपि तत्तन्मातृमयत्वादर्थः सुखादिवत्प्रत्येकं नियत एवेत्युक्तं प्राक् ॥
