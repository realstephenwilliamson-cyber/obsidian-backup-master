## Mūla

शिवाभिन्नोऽपि हि गुरुरनुग्रहमयीं विभोः ।
मुख्यां शक्तिमुपासीनोऽनुगृह्णीयात्स सर्वथा ॥ ^mula

śivābhinno'pi hi gururanugrahamayīṃ vibhoḥ |
mukhyāṃ śaktimupāsīno'nugṛhṇīyātsa sarvathā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 14 - Chapter Twenty-Three#^pg346|p. 326]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=346>))
>
> Although the teacher is one with Śiva (and so has the power to obscure as well as grace), devoted (as he is) to the pervasive Lord’s principal power, which is grace, may he (always) grace (everybody) in every way!

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* ननु यद्येवं तच्छिवाभेदिनोऽस्य पञ्चविधकृत्यकारित्वं किं न खण्ड्येत?--इत्याशङ्क्याह--
