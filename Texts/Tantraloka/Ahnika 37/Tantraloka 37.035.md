## Mūla

तत्रापि च त्रिदिवभोगमहार्घवर्ष-
द्वीपान्तरादधिकमेव कुमारिकाह्वम् ।
यत्राधराधरपदात्परमं शिवान्त-
मारोढुमप्यधिकृतिः कृतिनामनर्घा ॥ ^mula

tatrāpi ca tridivabhogamahārghavarṣa-
dvīpāntarādadhikameva kumārikāhvam |
yatrādharādharapadātparamaṃ śivānta-
māroḍhumapyadhikṛtiḥ kṛtināmanarghā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 11 - Chapter Thirty-Seven, part 1#^pg259|p. 241]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=259>))
>
> Although completely full of the seven worlds beginning from earth (bhūr), I think that within (the Egg), it is the second world that is the abode of joy. Where else is there (such a world,) adorned with the (two) kinds of enjoyment of the unfolding of day and night, embellished by the wonderful (and various) courses of the Sun and Moon? And there also, of the other continents and islands (in this world), greatly precious enjoyment of the gods, the greatest is the one called ‘“Kumārikā’.” (with 37.34–35ab)
>
> It is where the priceless faculty (adhikrti) called creative action (krti) exists, to ascend gradually from lower levels up to the Śiva, the supreme summit. (with 37.35cd)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* तत्रापि च त्रिदिवभोगमहार्घवर्ष-

> द्वीपान्तरादधिकमेव कुमारिकाह्वम् ।

द्वितीयभुवनमिति--भुवर्लोकः । तत्रेति--द्वितीयभुवने । वर्षाणीलावृतादीनि । द्वीपाः--शाकादयः ॥ अधिकत्वमेव दर्शयति—

यत्राधराधरपदात्परमं शिवान्त-

> मारोढुमप्यधिकृतिः कृतिनामनर्घा ॥
