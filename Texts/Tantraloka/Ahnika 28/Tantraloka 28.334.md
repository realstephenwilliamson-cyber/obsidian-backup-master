## Mūla

सैवात्र लीनता प्रोक्ता सत्त्वे रजसि तामसे ।
नीलपीतादिके ज्ञेये यतः प्राक्कल्पितां तनुम् ॥ ^mula

saivātra līnatā proktā sattve rajasi tāmase |
nīlapītādike jñeye yataḥ prākkalpitāṃ tanum || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 06 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 4#^pg161|p. 144]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=161>))
>
> This state of merger within (the qualities of Nature, namely,) sattva, rajas, and tamas, is said here to be this initial sustaining support (adhiṣṭhāna) (of the body) by consciousness, that takes place by its state of identity (with it) (tādātmyavrtti). (with 28.333cd–334ab)
>
> Consciousness can sustain (the existence) of an object, such as (the colours) blue and yellow, after having sustained the body fashioned previously. (with 28.334cd–335ab)
