# Psalms 12 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 11|← Psalms 11]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 13|Psalms 13 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 12|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**12:1** Unto the end, a psalm for David. How long, O Lord, wilt thou forget me unto the end? how long dost thou turn away thy face from me? ^12-1

**12:2** How long shall I take counsels in my soul, sorrow in my heart all the day? ^12-2

**12:3** How long shall my enemy be exalted over me? ^12-3

**12:4** Consider, and hear me, O Lord my God. Enlighten my eyes that I never sleep in death: ^12-4

**12:5** Lest at any time my enemy say: I have prevailed against him. They that trouble me will rejoice when I am moved: ^12-5

**12:6** But I have trusted in thy mercy. My heart shall rejoice in thy salvation: I will sing to the Lord, who giveth me good things: yea I will sing to the name of the Lord the most high. ^12-6
