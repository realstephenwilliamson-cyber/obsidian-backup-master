## About

The *Devi Mahatmya*, also known as the ‘*Durga Saptashati*’, is a Divine Book of the Soul – helping to understand Consciousness of the Universe. The Divine Mother, through Divine Allegory, explains the workings of Her Play (Leela) as the World and Life, and describes clearly how human Consciousness and the Consciousness of the Universe, works and how 

to live properly life. 

“*The Devi Mahatmya (Durga Saptashati) helps to bring self-*

*realization towards achieving Enlightenment.”* 

The *Devi Mahatmya* teaches to better human beings. With thoughtful presentation of the Glory of the Devi, and insightful interpretation of the significance of Her Divine Activities, the *Devi Mahatmya* depicts an allegorical journey of the Devi, the Divine Mother, with a view to setting examples for human beings. 

Human beings can often be arrogant feeling confident about knowing lot about nature, but a reading the *Devi Mahatmya* changes the viewpoint of the humans by making humans aware of their subjective consciousness about the Universe which is nothing but a delusional concept of reality. The book helps humans to be aware of the ‘Objective Reality’. The *Devi Mahatmya* fills the gap between the divine and humane and helps to make the lives of human beings more responsible and meaningful. With reference to the Acts of the Devi, the Divine Mother, the *Devi Mahatmya* inspires everyone to be able to fight against the inner demon and finally become a better version of themselves.
