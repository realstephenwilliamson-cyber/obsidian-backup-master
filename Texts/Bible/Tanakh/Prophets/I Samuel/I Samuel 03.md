## Chapter 3

**3:1** Young Samuel was in the service of the LORD under Eli. In those days the word of the LORD was rare; prophecy was not widespread. ^3-1

**3:2** One day, Eli was asleep in his usual place; his eyes had begun to fail and he could barely see. ^3-2

**3:3** The lamp of God had not yet gone out, and Samuel was sleeping in the temple of the LORD where the Ark of God was. ^3-3

**3:4** The LORD called out to Samuel, and he answered, “I’m coming.” ^3-4

**3:5** He ran to Eli and said, “Here I am; you called me.” But he replied, “I didn’t call you; go back to sleep.” So he went back and lay down. ^3-5

**3:6** Again the LORD called, “Samuel!” Samuel rose and went to Eli and said, “Here I am; you called me.” But he replied, “I didn’t call, my son; go back to sleep.”— ^3-6

**3:7** Now Samuel had not yet experienced the LORD; the word of the LORD had not yet been revealed to him.— ^3-7

**3:8** The LORD called Samuel again, a third time, and he rose and went to Eli and said, “Here I am; you called me.” Then Eli understood that the LORD was calling the boy. ^3-8

**3:9** And Eli said to Samuel, “Go lie down. If you are called again, say, ‘Speak, LORD, for Your servant is listening.’” And Samuel went to his place and lay down. ^3-9

**3:10** The LORD came, and stood there, and He called as before: “Samuel! Samuel!” And Samuel answered, “Speak, for Your servant is listening.” ^3-10

**3:11** The LORD said to Samuel: “I am going to do in Israel such a thing that both ears of anyone who hears about it will tingle. ^3-11

**3:12** In that day I will fulfill against Eli all that I spoke concerning his house, from beginning to end. ^3-12

**3:13** And I declare to him that I sentence his house to endless punishment for the iniquity he knew about—how his sons committed sacrilege at will[^1]—and he did not rebuke them. ^3-13

**3:14** Assuredly, I swear concerning the house of Eli that the iniquity of the house of Eli will never be expiated by sacrifice or offering.” ^3-14

**3:15** Samuel lay there until morning; and then he opened the doors of the House of the LORD. Samuel was afraid to report the vision to Eli, ^3-15

**3:16** but Eli summoned Samuel and said, “Samuel, my son”; and he answered, “Here.” ^3-16

**3:17** And [Eli] asked, “What did He say to you? Keep nothing from me. Thus and more may God do to you[^2] if you keep from me a single word of all that He said to you!” ^3-17

**3:18** Samuel then told him everything, withholding nothing from him. And [Eli] said, “He is the LORD; He will do what He deems right.” ^3-18

**3:19** Samuel grew up and the LORD was with him: He did not leave any of Samuel’s[^3] predictions unfulfilled. ^3-19

**3:20** All Israel, from Dan to Beer-sheba, knew that Samuel was trustworthy as a prophet of the LORD. ^3-20

**3:21** And the LORD continued to appear at Shiloh: the LORD revealed Himself to Samuel at Shiloh with the word of the LORD; ^3-21

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain. Septuagint reads “against God.”
[^2]: A formula of adjuration.
[^3]: Heb. “his.”
