## Chapter 25

**25:1** O LORD, You are my God;  
I will extol You, I will praise Your name.  
For You planned graciousness[^1] of old,  
Counsels of steadfast faithfulness. ^25-1

**25:2** For You have turned a city into a stone heap,  
A walled town into a ruin,  
The citadel of strangers[^2] into rubble,[^3]  
Never to be rebuilt. ^25-2

**25:3** Therefore a fierce people must honor You,  
A city of cruel nations must fear You. ^25-3

**25:4** For You have been a refuge for the poor man,  
A shelter for the needy man in his distress—  
Shelter from rainstorm, shade from heat.  
When the fury of tyrants was like a winter[^4] rainstorm, ^25-4

**25:5** The rage of strangers[^5] like heat in the desert,  
You subdued the heat with the shade of clouds,  
The singing[^6] of the tyrants was vanquished. ^25-5

**25:6** The LORD of Hosts will make on this mount[^7]  
For all the peoples  
A banquet of rich viands,  
A banquet of choice wines—  
Of rich viands seasoned with marrow,  
Of choice wines[^8] well refined. ^25-6

**25:7** And He will destroy on this mount[^9] the shroud  
That is drawn over the faces of all the peoples  
And the covering that is spread  
Over all the nations: ^25-7

**25:8** He will destroy death[^10] forever.  
My Lord GOD will wipe the tears away  
From all faces  
And will put an end to the reproach of His people[^11]  
Over all the earth—  
For it is the LORD who has spoken. ^25-8

**25:9** In that day they shall say:  
This is our God;  
We trusted in Him, and He delivered us.  
This is the LORD, in whom we trusted;  
Let us rejoice and exult in His deliverance! ^25-9

**25:10** For the hand of the LORD shall descend  
Upon this mount,[^12]  
And Moab[^13] shall be trampled under Him  
As straw is threshed to bits at Madmenah.[^14] ^25-10

**25:11** Then He will spread out His hands in their homeland,[^15]  
As a swimmer spreads his hands out to swim,  
And He will humble their pride  
Along with the emblems of their power.[^16] ^25-11

**25:12** Yea, the secure fortification of their[^17] walls  
He will lay low and humble,  
Will raze to the ground, to the very dust. ^25-12
