## Mūla

प्राणिनो जलजाः पूर्वदीक्षिताः शम्भुना स्वयम् ।
विधिना भाविना श्रीमन्मीननाथावतारिणा ॥ ^mula

prāṇino jalajāḥ pūrvadīkṣitāḥ śambhunā svayam |
vidhinā bhāvinā śrīmanmīnanāthāvatāriṇā || ^iast

## Translation

> [[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 17 - Chapter Twenty-Five (continued) and Chapter Twenty-Six#^pg415|p. 395]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=415>))

> Aquatic creatures have (in fact already) been initiated previously by Śiva Himself, by means of the procedure that will be (taught later,) that has been brought down (into the world) by the venerable Mīnanātha.
