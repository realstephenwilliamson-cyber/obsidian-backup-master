## Mūla

निष्कले सकले वैति लयं योजनिकाबलात् ।
इति प्रमेयं कथितं दीक्षाकाले गुरोर्यथा ॥ ^mula

niṣkale sakale vaiti layaṃ yojanikābalāt |
iti prameyaṃ kathitaṃ dīkṣākāle guroryathā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 07 - Chapter Sixteen, part 3#^pg157|p. 137]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=157>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 16.310cd–311ab.
>
> Then, when Māyā has dissolved away, and he is (an individual) experiencing subject who has experienced all the joy (of his good Karma), he merges into transcendental (niṣkala) or immanent (sakala) (Śiva,) by virtue of the power (bala) of the (initiation he has received, that) conjoins (to higher reality levels). (with 16.310cd–311ab)
>
> Thus, that which needs to be known has been declared, namely the manner of the initiation (the teacher imparts) at (the appropriate) time. (with 16.311cd)

*Lead-in:* आह्निकार्थमेव श्लोकस्य प्रथमार्धेनोपसंहरति--

> इति प्रमेयं कथितं दीक्षाकाले गुरोर्यथा ॥

इति श्रीमदाचार्याभिनवगुप्तपादविरचिते श्रीतन्त्रालोके प्रमेयप्रकाशनं नाम षोडशमाह्निकम् ॥ १६ ॥

इतीत्थमेतत्प्रमेयमिहोक्तं यथा येन प्रकारेण काले शक्तिपातावसरे गुरोर्दीक्षा कार्या भवेदिति शुभम् ॥ ३११ ॥
