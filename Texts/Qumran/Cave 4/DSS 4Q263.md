### Text

**1** [… And in addition,] no-one should raise [a matter against his fellow in front of the Many] ^t-1

**2** [unless it is with repro]of in the *[p. 545]* presence of witnesses. And in this way shall they beha[ve in all their places of residence, whenever one fellow meets] ^t-2

**3** [another,] the junior [shall obe]y the senior in \<work> and in wea[lth. They shall eat together, together they shall bless] ^t-3

**4** [and together they shall take counsel. In every] p[la]ce where there are [ten men of the Community council, there should not be missing] ^t-4

**5** [amongst them a priest. And every]one [shall sit before him] according to [his] r[ank …] ^t-5
