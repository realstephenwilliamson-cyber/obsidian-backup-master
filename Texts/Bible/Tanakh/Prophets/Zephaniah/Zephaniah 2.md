## Chapter 2

**2:1** Gather together, gather,  
[^1]O nation without shame, ^2-1

**2:2** Before the day the decree is born—  
The day flies by like chaff—  
Before the fierce anger  
Of the LORD overtakes you,  
Before the day of anger  
Of the LORD overtakes you. ^2-2

**2:3** Seek the LORD,  
All you humble of the land  
Who have fulfilled His law;  
Seek righteousness,  
Seek humility.  
Perhaps you will find shelter  
On the day of the LORD’s anger. ^2-3

**2:4** Indeed, Gaza shall be deserted  
And Ashkelon desolate;  
Ashdod’s people shall be expelled in broad daylight,  
And Ekron shall be uprooted. ^2-4

**2:5** Ah, nation of Cherethites  
Who inhabit the seacoast!  
There is a word of the LORD against you,  
O Canaan,[^2] land of the Philistines:  
I will lay you waste  
Without inhabitants. ^2-5

**2:6** The seacoast Cheroth[^3] shall become  
An abode for shepherds and folds for flocks, ^2-6

**2:7** And shall be a portion for the remnant of the House of Judah;  
On these [pastures] they shall graze [their flocks],  
They shall lie down[^4] at eventide  
In the houses of Ashkelon.  
For the LORD their God will take note of them  
And restore their fortunes. ^2-7

**2:8** I have heard the insults of Moab  
And the jeers of the Ammonites,  
Who have insulted My people  
And gloated over their country. ^2-8

**2:9** Assuredly, as I live  
—declares the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel—  
Moab shall become like Sodom  
And the Ammonites like Gomorrah:  
Clumps[^5] of weeds and patches[^6] of salt,  
And desolation evermore.  
The remnant of My people shall plunder them,  
The remainder of My nation shall possess them. ^2-9

**2:10** That is what they’ll get for their haughtiness,  
For insulting and jeering  
At the people of the LORD of Hosts. ^2-10

**2:11** The LORD will show Himself terrible against them,  
Causing all the gods on earth to shrivel;[^7]  
And all the coastlands of the nations  
Shall bow down to Him—  
Every man in his own home. ^2-11

**2:12** You Cushites too—  
They shall be slain by My sword.[^8] ^2-12

**2:13** And He will stretch out His arm against the north  
And destroy Assyria;  
He will make Nineveh a desolation,  
Arid as the desert. ^2-13

**2:14** In it flocks shall lie down,  
Every species of beast,  
While jackdaws and owls roost on its capitals,  
The great owl hoots in the window,  
And the raven [croaks] on the threshold.  
For he has stripped its cedarwork bare.[^9] ^2-14

**2:15** Is this the gay city  
That dwelt secure,  
That thought in her heart,  
“I am, and there is none but me”?  
Alas, she is become a waste,  
A lair of wild beasts!  
Everyone who passes by her  
Hisses and gestures with his hand.[^10] ^2-15

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain. Emendation yields: “O straw [Aramaic gel] not gathered in, / Before you are driven like flying chaff”; cf. Ps. 35.5.
[^2]: Or “Phoenicia,” of which Philistia is regarded as an extension southward.
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^4]: Change of vocalization yields “rest [them]”; cf. Song of Songs 1.7.
[^5]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^6]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^7]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^8]: Emendation yields “shall be slain by the sword of the Lord.”
[^9]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^10]: To ward off a like fate from himself; cf. Jer. 18.16 and note.
