## Chapter 30

**30:1** Oh, disloyal sons!  
—declares the LORD—  
Making plans  
Against My wishes,  
Weaving schemes  
Against My will,  
Thereby piling  
Guilt on guilt— ^30-1

**30:2** Who set out to go down to Egypt  
Without asking Me,  
To seek refuge with Pharaoh,  
To seek shelter under the protection of Egypt. ^30-2

**30:3** The refuge with Pharaoh shall result in your shame;  
The shelter under Egypt’s protection, in your chagrin. ^30-3

**30:4** Though his officers are present in Zoan,[^1]  
And his messengers[^2] reach as far as Hanes, ^30-4

**30:5** They all shall come to shame  
Because of a people that does not avail them,  
That is of no help or avail,  
But [brings] only chagrin and disgrace. ^30-5

**30:6** The “Beasts of the Negeb” Pronouncement.  
Through a[^3] land of distress and hardship,  
Of lion and roaring[^4] king-beast,  
Of viper and flying seraph,[^5]  
They convey their wealth on the backs of asses,  
Their treasures on camels’ humps,  
To a people of no avail. ^30-6

**30:7** For the help of Egypt  
Shall be vain and empty.  
Truly, I call this,  
“They are a threat that has ceased.”[^6] ^30-7

**30:8** Now,  
Go, write it down on a tablet  
And inscribe it in a record,  
That it may be with them for future days,  
A witness[^7] forever. ^30-8

**30:9** For it is a rebellious people,  
Faithless children,  
Children who refused to heed  
The instruction of the LORD; ^30-9

**30:10** Who said to the seers,  
“Do not see,”  
To the prophets, “Do not prophesy truth to us;  
Speak to us falsehoods,  
Prophesy delusions. ^30-10

**30:11** Leave the way!  
Get off the path!  
Let us hear no more  
About the Holy One of Israel!” ^30-11

**30:12** Assuredly,  
Thus said the Holy One of Israel:  
Because you have rejected this word,  
And have put your trust and reliance  
In that which is fraudulent and tortuous— ^30-12

**30:13** Of a surety,  
This iniquity shall work on you  
Like a spreading breach that occurs in a lofty wall,  
Whose crash comes sudden and swift. ^30-13

**30:14** It is smashed as one smashes an earthen jug,  
Ruthlessly shattered  
So that no shard is left in its breakage  
To scoop coals from a brazier,  
Or ladle water from a puddle. ^30-14

**30:15** For thus said my Lord GOD,  
The Holy One of Israel,  
“You shall triumph by stillness and quiet;  
Your victory shall come about  
Through calm and confidence.”  
But you refused. ^30-15

**30:16** “No,” you declared.  
“We shall flee on steeds”—  
Therefore you shall flee!  
“We shall ride on swift mounts”—  
Therefore your pursuers shall prove swift! ^30-16

**30:17** One thousand before the shout of one—  
You shall flee at the shout of five;  
Till what is left of you  
Is like a mast on a hilltop,  
Like a pole upon a mountain. ^30-17

**30:18** Truly, the LORD is waiting to show you grace,  
Truly, He will arise to pardon you.  
For the LORD is a God of justice;  
Happy are all who wait for Him. ^30-18

**30:19** Indeed, O people in Zion, dwellers of Jerusalem, you shall not have cause to weep. He will grant you His favor at the sound of your cry; He will respond as soon as He hears it. ^30-19

**30:20** My Lord will provide for you meager bread and scant water. Then your Guide will no more be ignored,[^8] but your eyes will watch your Guide; ^30-20

**30:21** and, whenever you deviate to the right or to the left, your ears will heed the command from behind you: “This is the road; follow it!” ^30-21

**30:22** And you will treat as unclean the silver overlay of your images and the golden plating of your idols. You will cast[^9] them away like a menstruous woman. “Out!” you will call to them. ^30-22

**30:23** So rain shall be provided for the seed with which you sow the ground, and the bread that the ground brings forth shall be rich and fat. Your livestock, in that day, shall graze in broad pastures; ^30-23

**30:24** as for the cattle and the asses that till the soil, they shall partake of salted fodder that has been winnowed with shovel and fan. ^30-24

**30:25** And on every high mountain and on every lofty hill, there shall appear brooks and watercourses—on a day of heavy slaughter, when towers topple. ^30-25

**30:26** And the light of the moon shall become like the light of the sun, and the light of the sun shall become sevenfold, like the light of the seven days, when the LORD binds up His people’s wounds and heals the injuries it has suffered. ^30-26

**30:27** Behold the LORD Himself[^10]  
Comes from afar  
In blazing wrath,  
With a heavy burden[^11]—  
His lips full of fury,  
His tongue like devouring fire, ^30-27

**30:28** And his breath like a raging torrent  
Reaching halfway up the neck—  
To set a misguiding yoke[^12] upon nations  
And a misleading bridle upon the jaws of peoples, ^30-28

**30:29** For you, there shall be singing  
As on a night when a festival is hallowed;  
There shall be rejoicing as when they march  
With flute, with timbrels, and with lyres[^13]  
To the Rock of Israel on the Mount of the LORD. ^30-29

**30:30** For the LORD will make His majestic voice heard  
And display the sweep of His arm  
In raging wrath,  
In a devouring blaze of fire,  
In tempest, and rainstorm, and hailstones. ^30-30

**30:31** Truly, Assyria, who beats with the rod,  
Shall be cowed by the voice of the LORD; ^30-31

**30:32** And each time the appointed staff passes by,  
The LORD will bring down [His arm] upon him  
And will do battle with him as he waves it.[^14] ^30-32

**30:33** The Topheth[^15] has long been ready for him;  
He too is destined for Melech[^16]—  
His firepit has been made both wide and deep,  
With plenty of fire and firewood,  
And with the breath of the LORD  
Burning in it like a stream of sulfur. ^30-33
