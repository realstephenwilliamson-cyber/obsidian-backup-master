## The Union of Shiva and Shakti

### The Union of Shiva and Shakti216

216  This work has circulated among Guruji’s followers for years under the title *Lalitā* A commentary from the late 1980s on the 999th name of *Sahasranāma: Amrita Bhāṣyam*, which simply means that it the Goddess as set out in the Lalitā Sahasranāma. <sup>217</sup> is a learned commentary or exposition given by Guruji on the *Sahasranāma*. Shiva is the knower. Shakti is the known. They appear to be separated in space and/
or in time. An interval measures the distance in space and time together. The scientific formulation (where *c* is the speed of light; that is, 186,000 miles per second) 

217  *Śivaśaktyaikyarūpin̄ī*, which means, “Her form is the union of would be:

(Interval) <sup>2</sup> = (Distance) <sup>2</sup> *−* (Time difference × c) <sup>2</sup>

*Aikya* means union. They were not united, but now we are uniting them; that is, now we’re reducing the interval between them to zero. *Aikya rūpiṇī* means “having the form of union.” The form of what? Of the zero interval.

There are two ways for this interval to be zero. The first is to make the distance and the time difference both equal to zero. That is, to place the seer and seen at the same place at the same time, so they are one and the same. This is the state of just being. There is no energy in it, not even the desire or power to move.

The second way is for space and time differences to exist, but for both to be equal. 

When the spatial interval is equal to *time interval × c* , the interval vanishes. This is an interesting case of dynamic power: Shiva is the power of being, Shakti is the power of awareness, desire, knowledge and manifestation. When the two merge, they become total power—they can be anywhere at any time, have any knowledge and manifest any desire.

And that is the secret of this great mantra. It teaches us how to become total power. The power to be, to know, to heal, even across great distances or time intervals. For example, we can travel to any place we desire in zero time, via teleportation. Not physically, of course, but mentally. We don’t even have to know the path, because we’re not following any path—there *is* no path. And we don’t need to travel through telephone lines either, the sky will do.
