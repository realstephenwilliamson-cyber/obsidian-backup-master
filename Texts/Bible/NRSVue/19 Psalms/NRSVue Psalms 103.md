# Psalm 103 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 102|← Psalm 102]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 104|Psalm 104 →]]

## Thanksgiving for God’s goodness

*Of David.*

**103:1** Bless the LORD, O my soul, and all that is within me, bless his holy name. ^103-1

**103:2** Bless the LORD, O my soul, and do not forget all his benefits— ^103-2

**103:3** who forgives all your iniquity, who heals all your diseases, ^103-3

**103:4** who redeems your life from the Pit, who crowns you with steadfast love and mercy, ^103-4

**103:5** who satisfies you with good as long as you live^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] so that your youth is renewed like the eagle’s. ^103-5

**103:6** The LORD works vindication and justice for all who are oppressed. ^103-6

**103:7** He made known his ways to Moses, his acts to the people of Israel. ^103-7

**103:8** The LORD is merciful and gracious, slow to anger and abounding in steadfast love. ^103-8

**103:9** He will not always accuse, nor will he keep his anger forever. ^103-9

**103:10** He does not deal with us according to our sins nor repay us according to our iniquities. ^103-10

**103:11** For as the heavens are high above the earth, so great is his steadfast love toward those who fear him; ^103-11

**103:12** as far as the east is from the west, so far he removes our transgressions from us. ^103-12

**103:13** As a father has compassion for his children, so the LORD has compassion for those who fear him. ^103-13

**103:14** For he knows how we were made; he remembers that we are dust. ^103-14

**103:15** As for mortals, their days are like grass; they flourish like a flower of the field; ^103-15

**103:16** for the wind passes over it, and it is gone, and its place knows it no more. ^103-16

**103:17** But the steadfast love of the LORD is from everlasting to everlasting on those who fear him, and his righteousness to children’s children, ^103-17

**103:18** to those who keep his covenant and remember to do his commandments. ^103-18

**103:19** The LORD has established his throne in the heavens, and his kingdom rules over all. ^103-19

**103:20** Bless the LORD, O you his angels, you mighty ones who do his bidding, obedient to his spoken word. ^103-20

**103:21** Bless the LORD, all his hosts, his ministers who do his will. ^103-21

**103:22** Bless the LORD, all his works, in all places of his dominion. Bless the LORD, O my soul. ^103-22
