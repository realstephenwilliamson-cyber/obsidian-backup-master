### Text

**1** […] … I have preferred you above all ﬂesh ^t-1

**2** [… and those seven departed from me, and] I awoke from my dream. Then ^t-2

**3** [I thought: «This vision is like the other one. I am amazed that the whole vision is to come». And] I [hid] (this vision) too, in my heart and to no-one ^t-3

**4** [did I reveal it. And we went to my father Isaac, and he also blessed me likewise. Then, w]hen my father Jacob tithed ^t-4

**5** [everything he had, according to his *[p. 453]* vow, then I served for the ﬁrst time at the head of] the [priesthood,] and to me, among his sons, he gave ^t-5

**6** [the offering … to God, and he clothed me with the clothing of the priesthood and ﬁlled my hand, and I became a priest for Go]d of eternity […] ^t-6
