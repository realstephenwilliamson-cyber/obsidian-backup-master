## Mūla

श्लींपदं पशुशब्दं च हूंफडन्तं भवादिकम् ।
एतत्पाशुपतं प्रोक्तमर्धसप्ताक्षरं परम् ॥ ^mula

ślīṁpadaṁ[^54] paśuśabdaṁ ca hūṁ phaḍantaṁ bhavādikam |
etat pāśupataṁ proktam ardhasaptākṣaraṁ param || ^iast

*Muktabodha's e-text of the KSTS edition (M00160) has:* ślīpadaṃ paśuśabdaṃ ca hūṃphaḍantaṃ bhavādikam | etatpāśupataṃ proktamardhasaptākṣaraṃ param ||

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2023 – March 2023
> p. 52 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=52>))
>
> **The Pāśupata Weapon**
>
> The syllable ŚLĪṀ, the word PAŚU, HŪṀ PHAṬ at the end and BHAVA at the beginning. This is said to be the Pāśupata (Weapon). Supreme, it consists of seven and a half syllables.[^55]

[^54]: g: *ślaśabdam*. Printed edition reads *ślīpadaṁ*. The accepted reading is that of MS Jh.
[^55]: TĀ 30/41cd is an abridged version of MV 3/65. The form of the mantra of the Weapon taught here is BHAVA ŚLĪṀ, PAŚU HŪṀ PHAṬ. Abhinavagupta has substituted BHAVA with OṀ.
