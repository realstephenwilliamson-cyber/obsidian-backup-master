## Mūla

नार्घपात्रेऽत्र कुसुमं कुर्याद्देवार्चनाकृते ।
अर्घपात्रे तदमृतीभूतमम्ब्वेव पूजितम् ॥ ^mula

nārghapātre'tra kusumaṃ kuryāddevārcanākṛte |
arghapātre tadamṛtībhūtamambveva pūjitam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 06 - Chapter Fifteen, part 5#^pg191|p. 173]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=191>))
>
> For fear of mixing (these) flowers with the ones already taken for the worship of the sacrificial vessel, one should not place (any) flower here in the sacrificial vessel to worship the deities. Only that liquid (ambu) in the sacrificial vessel, transformed into nectar, is worshipped. And that is only done to propitiate the Mantras and purify the substances required for the sacrifice. (with 15.293cd–295ab)
