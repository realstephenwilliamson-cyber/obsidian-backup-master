## Mūla

स्वच्छन्दमृत्योरपि यद् भीष्मादेः श्रूयते किल ।
भोगवैरस्यसंप्राप्तौ जीवितान्तोपसर्पणम् ॥ ^mula

svacchandamṛtyorapi yad bhīṣmādeḥ śrūyate kila |
bhogavairasyasaṃprāptau jīvitāntopasarpaṇam || ^iast

## Translation

> (Indeed,) one hears that those who, like Bhīṣma, were free to die when they wished, gave up their life when they were totally averse to (further) worldly experience.
