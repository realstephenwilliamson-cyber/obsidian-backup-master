# Psalms 122 (Douay-Rheims)

**122:1** To thee have I lifted up my eyes, who dwellest in heaven. ^122-1

**122:2** Behold as the eyes of the servants are on the hands of their masters, As the eyes of the handmaid are on the hands of her mistress: so are our eyes unto the Lord our God, until he have mercy on us. ^122-2

**122:3** Have mercy on us, O Lord, have mercy on us: for we are greatly filled with contempt. ^122-3

**122:4** For our soul is greatly filled: we are a reproach to the rich, and contempt to the proud. ^122-4
