## Mūla

चत्वारोऽथान्यशास्त्रस्थे शिष्ये पञ्चकमुच्यते ।
लिङ्गिनां केवलो ग्रन्थिस्तोरणे दश कल्पयेत् ॥ ^mula

catvāro'thānyaśāstrasthe śiṣye pañcakamucyate |
liṅgināṃ kevalo granthistoraṇe daśa kalpayet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 04 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 2#^pg96|p. 79]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=96>))
>
> The knots (of the thread placed on) on the Seat of Knowledge (vidyāpīṭha) should be fifty; the one on the idol and the pedestal of the Liṅga, forty-eight, the one on the bell, twenty-five; the one on the little ladle, eighteen; and the one on the large ladle, twenty-four. (The thread) in the terrace has thirty-six (knots), the one in the pavilion, twelve. (The one) in the house (reserved for) the bathing (of the deities), sixteen. and (the one) in the shrine for meditation, twenty-four. (The one placed) on the teacher, seven. (The one) on the adepts, five. There are seven (knots in the thread placed) on the spiritual son, four in that for the regular initiate. It is said that (the thread placed) on the disciple who follows a different scripture has five (knots). (The thread) for those who bear sectarian signs has only one (knot). Fashion one with ten (knots for the one) on the lintel (above the door) (toraṇa). There are eight knots (in the threads placed) on the doors. (with 28.158cd–162ab)
