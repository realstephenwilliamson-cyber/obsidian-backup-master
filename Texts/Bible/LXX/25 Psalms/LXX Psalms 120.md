# LXX Psalms 120

← [[LXX Psalms 119]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 121]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 121]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 121]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**120:1** A Song of Degrees. I lifted up mine eyes to the mountains, whence my help shall come.  ^120-1

**120:2** My help *shall come* from the Lord, who made the heaven and the earth.  ^120-2

**120:3** Let not thy foot be moved; and let not thy keeper slumber.  ^120-3

**120:4** Behold, he that keeps Israel shall not slumber nor sleep.  ^120-4

**120:5** The Lord shall keep thee: the Lord is thy shelter upon thy right hand.  ^120-5

**120:6** The sun shall not burn thee by day, neither the moon by night.  ^120-6

**120:7** May the Lord preserve thee from all evil: the Lord shall keep thy soul.  ^120-7

**120:8** The Lord shall keep thy coming in, and thy going out, from henceforth and even for ever.  ^120-8
