## Mūla

आचाराच्छक्तिमप्येव नान्यथेत्यूर्मिशासने ।
नित्याद्यल्पाल्पकं कुर्याद्यदुक्तं ब्रह्मयामले ॥ ^mula

ācārācchaktimapyeva nānyathetyūrmiśāsane |
nityādyalpālpakaṃ kuryādyaduktaṃ brahmayāmale || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 14 - Chapter Twenty-Three#^pg336|p. 316]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=336>))
>
> According to the Ūrmiśāsana ‘a Brahmin should be introduced (yojayet) to the secret (teaching), and so too (his) partner (śakti), (only) after having examined whether their conduct is contrary (to the norms) - not otherwise!’ (with 23.42cd–43ab)
>
> (The teacher) may perform (his own) regular daily rites etc very briefly. As is said in the Brahmayāmala: ‘O beloved! he who has observed the Vow of the (Observance of the) Vidyā may, alternatively, recollect all (the rite) mentally.’ (with 23.43cd–44ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* एवं परानुग्रहव्यग्रतया नित्याद्यपि अयं संक्षेपेण कुर्यात्-- इत्याह--

> नित्याद्यल्पाल्पकं कुर्याद्यदुक्तं ब्रह्मयामले ॥
> चीर्णविद्याव्रतः सर्वं मनसा वा स्मरेत् प्रिये ।
