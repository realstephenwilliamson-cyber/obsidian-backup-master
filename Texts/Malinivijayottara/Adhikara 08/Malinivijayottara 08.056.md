## Mūla

एकैकाङ्गुलमेतत्स्याच्छूलस्यामलसारकम्  ।
ततो नालमनन्ताख्यं दण्डमस्य प्रकल्पयेत् ॥ ^mula

ekaikāṅgulam etat syāc chūlasyāmalasārakam[^59] |
tato nālam anantākhyaṁ daṇḍam asya prakalpayet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2025 – April 2025
> p. 15 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=29>))
>
> **The Base of the Trident**
>
> [^60]Initially, the Energy of the Foundation (should be projected into the base of the Trident) four fingers below the naval. (Then) the four, that is, Earth (dharā), Water (suroda), the Boat (pota) and the Bulb (kanda), each (taking up) a finger breadth, constitute the swelling at the base (āmalasāraka) of the Trident.[^61] (with 8.55–56ab)

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2025 – April 2025
> p. 16 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=30>))
>
> **The Shaft of the Trident**
>
> Then (beyond that) one should imagine the channel which is the staff of that (Trident) called Ananta that extends up to the uvula. There is said to be a knot above the staff.[^62] O great Goddess, without piercing through (that) great ocean of nets and fetters, a man does not attain union (*yoga*) with Śiva. (with 8.56cd–58ab)

[^59]: k: *caturaṅgulametad*
[^60]: TĀ 15/297cd-365 is a commentary on MV 8/55-84ab, most of which is quoted or paraphrased as it goes along. These deal with the projection of the Trident of the Trika maṇḍala into the vital breath. Abhinavagupta describes the Trika Trident Throne in 15/297cd-312, following quite closely the account in MV 8/55-68.
[^61]: MV 8/55 quoted in TĀv ad 15/297cd-303ab.
[^62]: Read *nālordhvaṁ* for *śūlordhvaṁ*.
