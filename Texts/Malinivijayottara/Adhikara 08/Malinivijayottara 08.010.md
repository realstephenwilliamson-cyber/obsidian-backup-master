## Mūla

विधिस्नानादिकं चात्र पूर्ववत्किंतु वारिणा ।
साधारणविधिस्नातो विद्यात्रितयमन्त्रितम् ॥ ^mula

vidhisnānādikaṁ cātra pūrvavat kiṁtu vāriṇā |
sādhāraṇavidhisnāto vidyātritayamantritam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, October 2024 – December 2024
> p. 16 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=15>))

> 2) *Also, when bathing with water, the earth (used) is consecrated seven times with the Weapon (astra).*[^8] Then, having touched the body with it as before, he should then perform the ablution (that removes) impurity (*malasnāna*). The procedure, bathing and the rest is as before, but (done) with water. (with 8.9–10ab)

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, October 2024 – December 2024
> p. 16 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=15>))
>
> 3) In order to (perform) the ablution with mantra, the knower of mantra should (first) have bathed in accordance with the common procedure, and should (then) throw on (his head) water that has been consecrated with the three Vidyās (of Parā, Parāparā and Aparā). (with 8.10cd–11ab)

[^8]: MV 8/9ab is quoted in TĀv ad 15/49-51ab.
