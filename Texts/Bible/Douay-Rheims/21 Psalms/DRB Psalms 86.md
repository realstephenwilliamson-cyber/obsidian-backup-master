# Psalms 86 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 85|← Psalms 85]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 87|Psalms 87 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 86|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**86:1** For the sons of Core, a psalm of a canticle. The foundations thereof are in the holy mountains: ^86-1

**86:2** The Lord loveth the gates of Sion above all the tabernacles of Jacob. ^86-2

**86:3** Glorious things are said of thee, O city of God. ^86-3

**86:4** I will be mindful of Rahab and of Babylon knowing me. Behold the foreigners, and Tyre, and the people of the Ethiopians, these were there. ^86-4

**86:5** Shall not Sion say: This man and that man is born in her? and the Highest himself hath founded her. ^86-5

**86:6** The Lord shall tell in his writings of peoples and of princes, of them that have been in her. ^86-6

**86:7** The dwelling in thee is as it were of all rejoicing. ^86-7
