# 1 Corinthians 10 (JANT notes)

Text: [[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 10|NRSVue]] · [[1 Corinthians 10|BSB and SBLGNT]] · Introduction: [[JANT 1 Corinthians - Introduction]]

### 10.1–13: Israel’s negative examples

*[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 10#^10-1|Text]], p. 338* · Paul admonishes the Corinthians using biblical negative exemplars. ^10-1

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 10#^10-1|1]]:** *Ancestors*, Israelites, the Corinthians’ spiritual forbears ([[Galatians 03#^3-7|Gal 3.7]]). *Cloud*, divine presence protecting Israelites in the desert ([[Torah 16 - Beshalach#^13-21|Ex 13.21]]; [[Torah 16 - Beshalach#^14-19|14.19–22]]), seen as God’s Wisdom ([[LXX Wisdom of Solomon 10#^10-17|Wis 10.17]]; Philo, *Heir.* \[42\] 203–04; *Mek. Beshallah* 1 on [[Torah 16 - Beshalach#^13-17|Ex 13.17–22]]), which Paul identifies as Christ (1.20–21). ^10-1-2

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 10#^10-2|2]]:** *Baptized*, infused with the *cloud*’s Spirit and immersed in the *sea* (at the Exodus). Jewish tradition does not see this event as “baptism,” but regarded it as both present and proleptic salvation (*Mek. Beshallah* on [[Torah 16 - Beshalach#^14-13|Ex 14.13]]; [[Torah 16 - Beshalach#^15-2|15.2]]). ^10-2

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 10#^10-3|3]]:** *Spiritual food*, manna, “bread from heaven” ([[Torah 16 - Beshalach#^16-4|Ex 16.4-36]]; [[Torah 36 - Beha'alotcha#^11-6|Num 11.6–9]]; see [[John 06#^6-31|Jn 6.31–32]]), the “bread of angels” ([[Psalms 078#^78-24|Ps 78.24–25]]). ^10-3

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 10#^10-4|4]]:** *Spiritual drink*, water ([[Torah 16 - Beshalach#^17-1|Ex 17.1–7]]; [[Torah 39 - Chukat#^20-1|Num 20.1–13]]) that Moses drew from the *rock* at Kadesh (for water as a divine gift, particularly in relation to Miriam’s well, see *t. Sukk.* 3.3,11; *b. Taʾan.* 9a). Paul identifies the *rock* with *Christ*, since it *followed them* and sustained them, similar to Jewish interpretations of the rock and its supply of water as wisdom or Torah (Am 8.11; [[LXX Wisdom of Solomon 11#^11-4|Wis 11.4]]; Philo, *Leg. all* 2.86; *Dreams* 2.221–22; *L. A. B.* 10.7; *b. B. Kamma* 82a; *Song Rab.* 1.19). ^10-4

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 10#^10-5|5]]:** Paul, following some biblical texts, characterizes Israel’s period of wandering as faithless (e.g., Ps 106), while others idealize it (esp. [[Jeremiah 02#^2-2|Jer 2.2]]). ^10-5

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 10#^10-6|6]]:** *Examples*, counterexamples, since the pattern of faithlessness and condemnation jeopardizes their final judgment ([[Nehemiah 09#^9-11|Neh 9.11–21]]). *Desire evil*, see Num 11. ^10-6

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 10#^10-7|7]]:** LXX [[LXX Exodus 32#^32-6|Ex 32.6]]. *Idolaters*, golden-calf worshippers punished by death ([[Torah 21 - Ki Tisa#^32-27|Ex 32.27–28]]), just as the unfaithful will be condemned in the final judgment. *Play*, revel, likely including dancing ([[II Samuel 06#^6-5|2 Sam 6.5]]), which may have erotic connotations ([[Torah 06 - Toldot#^26-8|Gen 26.8]]). ^10-7

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 10#^10-8|8]]:** *Sexual immorality*, [[Torah 40 - Balak#^25-1|Num 25.1–2]] portrays idolatry as a consequence of intercourse with Moabites. *Twenty-three thousand,* likely is a variant of the 24,000 of [[Torah 40 - Balak#^25-9|Num 25.9]] (see also [[Torah 41 - Pinchas#^26-62|Num 26.62]]). ^10-8

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 10#^10-9|9]]:** *Christ*, in some manuscripts “the Lord,” paraphrasing [[Torah 45 - Vaetchanan#^6-16|Deut 6.16]] (see [[Torah 16 - Beshalach#^17-7|Ex 17.7]]; [[Matthew 04#^4-7|Mt 4.7]]). *Test* (Gk “ekpeirazō”), the limits of Christ’s forgiveness; the same word names the place where the Israelites demanded Moses extract water from the rock (LXX [[LXX Exodus 17#^17-7|Ex 17.7]]; see also LXX [[Psalms 077#^77-19|Ps 77.18]],[[LXX Psalms 77#^77-41|41]],[[LXX Psalms 77#^77-56|56]]; Heb “masah”). *Serpents*, [[Torah 39 - Chukat#^21-6|Num 21.6–7]], where Israel is punished for “speaking against the Lord.” ^10-9

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 10#^10-10|10]]:** *Destroyer*, see 5.5n., likely an angel who slew some Israelites for complaining ([[Torah 36 - Beha'alotcha#^11-1|Num 11.1]] and 16.32,35; see also [[Torah 15 - Bo#^12-23|Ex 12.23]]; [[II Samuel 24#^24-16|2 Sam 24.16]]; [[LXX Wisdom of Solomon 18#^18-25|Wis 18.25]]; *T. Abr.* A 8.12; 16.1–16; *3 Bar.* 4.8; 9.7; *Deut. Rab.* 11.10; *Midr. Tanh.* on [[Torah 09 - Vayeshev#^39-1|Gen 39.1]]; *b. Avod. Zar.* 20b). ^10-10

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 10#^10-12|12]]:** *Standing*, the posture of the righteous and redeemed. *Fall\[*ing*\]*, the position of the condemned ([[Torah 16 - Beshalach#^14-13|Ex 14.13]]; [[Torah 21 - Ki Tisa#^32-28|32.28]]). ^10-12

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 10#^10-13|13]]:** *Faithful*, see 1.9n. *Beyond your strength*, the motif of testing appears in biblical and rabbinic literature regarding the suffering of the righteous (Gen 22; [[Proverbs 03#^3-12|Prov 3.12]],[[Proverbs 03#^3-19|19]]; *Gen. Rab.* 40.3; *b. Ber.* 5a-b \[Heb “yisurin shel ʾahavah,” “chastisements of love”\]). *Way out*, believers survive *testing* by remaining faithful to God (see [[Zechariah 13#^13-9|Zech 13.9]]). ^10-13

### 10.14–22: Idolatry versus fidelity

*[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 10#^10-14|Text]], p. 339* · The remainder of ch 10 through ch 14 instructs believers on appropriate communal behavior. ^10-14

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 10#^10-14|14]]:** *Friends* (Gk “agapētos”), lit., “beloved” (4.14; 15.58). ^10-14-2

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 10#^10-16|16]]:** *Cup*, wine shared in the Lord’s Supper (see Introduction), representing Christ’s *blood*. *Sharing* (Gk “koinonia”), lit., “fellowship, communion”; see 1.9n. *Bread*, representing *the body of Christ*. See “Baptism and Eucharist,” p. 674. ^10-16

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 10#^10-17|17]]:** *One*, uniting believers with each other and with Christ. Blessing wine and bread was fundamental to Jewish festival meals (see *m. Ber.* 6.1), as reflected in the blessing recited over the cup at the end of a meal, “birkat ha-mazon”, “benediction on nourishment” (Philo, *Cont.* 10 \[79–80\]; Josephus, *J.W*. 2.131; *Jub.* 22.6–9; *m. Ber.* 7.1–5; 8.8; *t. Ber.* 5.7; *y. Ber.* 7.2–3;*b. Ber*. 51a–52a). *Body*, see 12.12–26. ^10-17

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 10#^10-18|18]]:** *People of Israel*, lit., “Israel according to the flesh.” *Eat the sacrifices*, communal events like the Passover created fellowship, making Israelites *partners in the altar* (see 1.9n.; [[Torah 25 - Tzav#^7-5|Lev 7.5–8]]; [[Torah 48 - Shoftim#^18-1|Deut 18.1–5]]). ^10-18

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 10#^10-20|20]]:** *Pagans*, not in the Greek. *They sacrifice . . . to God*, see LXX [[LXX Deuteronomy 32#^32-17|Deut 32.17]]. *Demons* (Gk “daimones”), pagan deities, a general, neutral term for spirits or divine powers that later was reserved for malevolent ones. Jewish folk-belief in such powers lasted beyond the Middle Ages (*b. Ber.* 6a; *b. Pesah.* 109b–112a; *b. Sukk.* 28a; *Gen. Rab.* 20; *Eccl. Rab.* 2.6; *Pirqe R. El.* 34; Nachmanides on [[Torah 33 - Bechukotai#^27-7|Lev. 27.7]]; *Zohar* 3.229b; *Shulchan Arukh*, *Orech Chaim*, 4.2; 90.6; 181.2; *Yoreh Deʾah*, 116.5; 179.16,19 ). ^10-20

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 10#^10-21|21]]:** *Lord*, Christ. ^10-21

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 10#^10-22|22]]:** *Jealousy*, see [[Torah 53 - Ha'Azinu#^32-21|Deut 32.21]]. ^10-22

### 10.23–11.1: More dietary concerns

*[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 10#^10-23|Text]], p. 339* · Paul advocates a position similar to some rabbinic authorities, who consider meat slaughtered by idolatrous apostates permissible (*b. Hul.* 4b), or presume that an object found in public is ritually pure (*b. Hul.* 9b). ^10-23

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 10#^10-23|23]]:** See 6.12n. ^10-23-2

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 10#^10-25|25]]:** *Market*, see 8.13n. *Conscience*, see 8.10n. ^10-25

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 10#^10-26|26]]:** [[Psalms 024#^24-1|Ps 24.1]]\[LXX 23.1\]. ^10-26

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 10#^10-27|27]]:** *Without raising any question*, assume the food has not been sacrificed to idols. ^10-27

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 10#^10-32|32]]:** *Offense to Jews*, by flagrantly consuming food offered to idols. ^10-32

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 10#^10-33|33]]:** *Saved*, see 5.5n.; 15.2n. ^10-33
