## The blessedness of unity

*A Song of Ascents.*

**133:1** How very good and pleasant it is when kindred live together in unity! ^133-1

**133:2** It is like the precious oil on the head, running down upon the beard, on the beard of Aaron, running down over the collar of his robes. ^133-2

**133:3** It is like the dew of Hermon, which falls on the mountains of Zion. For there the LORD ordained his blessing, life forevermore. ^133-3
