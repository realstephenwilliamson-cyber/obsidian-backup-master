## Mūla

तावतीं गतिमायान्ति भुवनेऽत्र निवेशिताः ।
ततः कोट्या वीरभद्रो युगान्ताग्निसमप्रभः ॥ ^mula

tāvatīṃ gatimāyānti bhuvane'tra niveśitāḥ |
tataḥ koṭyā vīrabhadro yugāntāgnisamaprabhaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 07 - Chapter Eight, part 5#^pg236|p. 218]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=236>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 8.196cd–197ab.
>
> As is the initiation (the teacher) imparts in the sphere of Vidyeśa, such is the path by which they are placed here, in (this) world (of Bhadrakālī). (with 8.195cd–196ab)
>
> Beyond that, for ten million (leagues, lives) Vīrabhadra, whose light is like that of the fire at the end of an aeon (yuga). His world is called Vijaya. Those who have died in water, in deserts, or in a fire, or have been decapitated, while recalling Maheśvara, attain Vīrabhadra, who is the Lord’s consciousness of great splendour. (with 8.196cd–198ab)
