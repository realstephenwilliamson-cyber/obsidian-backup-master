## Mūla

द्विधा च नवधा चैव पञ्चाशद्धा च मालिनी ।
बीजयोन्यात्मकाद्भेदाद्द्विधा बीजं स्वरा मताः ॥ ^mula

dvidhā ca navadhā caiva pañcāśaddhā ca mālinī |
bījayonyātmakād bhedād dvidhā bījaṁ svarā matāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2023 – March 2023
> p. 40 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=40>))
>
> *She has arisen there such that She is differentiated into the state of the energies of the phonemes (mātṛbhāva)*[^10] and is divided into two, nine and fifty (aspects) and (also manifests as) Mālinī.[^11] (with 3.9cd–10ab)

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2023 – March 2023
> p. 41 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=41>))
>
> (She is) of two kinds because (her) nature is (both) seed and matrix. The vowels are considered to be the seed, and the matrix (is formed) with the consonants, beginning with K. (She is of) nine kinds according to the variety of classes (of phonemes) and, due to the difference between (each) phoneme, she burns resplendent with fifty rays. (with 3.10cd–11)

[^10]: MV 3/9cd quoted in TĀv ad 11/72-73ab.
[^11]: Concerning Mātṛkā see TĀ 15/117cd-120. The two aspects are the seeds (i.e., the vowels) and the matrices (*yoni*) (i.e., the consonants). The nine are the eight classes of phonemes and the letter KṢ. The fifty aspects are the fifty phonemes. Cf. 3/65 ff. Concerning Mālinī see TĀ 15/121-135ab ff.
