## A plea for justice

*A Psalm of Asaph.*

**82:1** God has taken his place in the divine council; in the midst of the gods he holds judgment: ^82-1

**82:2** “How long will you judge unjustly and show partiality to the wicked? *Selah* ^82-2

**82:3** Give justice to the weak and the orphan; maintain the right of the lowly and the destitute. ^82-3

**82:4** Rescue the weak and the needy; deliver them from the hand of the wicked.” ^82-4

**82:5** They have neither knowledge nor understanding; they walk around in darkness; all the foundations of the earth are shaken. ^82-5

**82:6** I say, “You are gods, children of the Most High, all of you; ^82-6

**82:7** nevertheless, you shall die like mortals and fall like any prince.”^[Or *fall as one man, O princes*] ^82-7

**82:8** Rise up, O God, judge the earth, for all the nations belong to you! ^82-8
