## Mūla

तासु संदधतश्चित्तमवधानैकधर्मकम् । तत्तत्सिद्धिसमावेशः स्वयमेवोपजायते ॥ ^mula

tāsu saṃdadhataścittamavadhānaikadharmakam | tattatsiddhisamāveśaḥ svayamevopajāyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 13 - Chapter Ten, part 4#^pg446|p. 427]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=446>))
>
> Placing the mind (citta), solely intent on concentrated attention, in these (moments of perception), penetration (samāveśa) into their respective accomplishments (siddhi) takes place spontaneously.
