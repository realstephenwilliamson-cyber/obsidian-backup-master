# Psalm 89 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 088|← Psalm 88]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 090|Psalm 90 →]]

## God’s covenant with David

*A Maskil of Ethan the Ezrahite.*

**89:1** I will sing of your steadfast love, O LORD,^[Gk: Heb *the steadfast love of theLORD*] forever; with my mouth I will proclaim your faithfulness to all generations. ^89-1

**89:2** I declare that your steadfast love is established forever; your faithfulness is as firm as the heavens. ^89-2

**89:3** You said, “I have made a covenant with my chosen one; I have sworn to my servant David: ^89-3

**89:4** ‘I will establish your descendants forever and build your throne for all generations.’ ” *Selah* ^89-4

**89:5** Let the heavens praise your wonders, O LORD, your faithfulness in the assembly of the holy ones. ^89-5

**89:6** For who in the skies can be compared to the LORD? Who among the heavenly beings is like the LORD, ^89-6

**89:7** a God feared in the council of the holy ones, great and awesome^[Gk Syr: Heb *greatly awesome*] above all who are around him? ^89-7

**89:8** O LORD God of hosts, who is as mighty as you, O LORD? Your faithfulness surrounds you. ^89-8

**89:9** You rule the raging of the sea; when its waves rise, you still them. ^89-9

**89:10** You crushed Rahab like a carcass; you scattered your enemies with your mighty arm. ^89-10

**89:11** The heavens are yours; the earth also is yours; the world and all that is in it—you founded them. ^89-11

**89:12** The north and the south^[Or *Zaphon and Yamin*]—you created them; Tabor and Hermon joyously praise your name. ^89-12

**89:13** You have a mighty arm; strong is your hand, high your right hand. ^89-13

**89:14** Righteousness and justice are the foundation of your throne; steadfast love and faithfulness go before you. ^89-14

**89:15** Happy are the people who know the festal shout, who walk, O LORD, in the light of your countenance; ^89-15

**89:16** they exult in your name all day long and extol^[Cn: Heb *are exalted in*] your righteousness. ^89-16

**89:17** For you are the glory of their strength; by your favor our horn is exalted. ^89-17

**89:18** For our shield belongs to the LORD, our king to the Holy One of Israel. ^89-18

**89:19** Then you spoke in a vision to your faithful one and said, “I have set the crown^[Cn: Heb *help*] on one who is mighty; I have exalted one chosen from the people. ^89-19

**89:20** I have found my servant David; with my holy oil I have anointed him; ^89-20

**89:21** my hand shall always remain with him; my arm also shall strengthen him. ^89-21

**89:22** The enemy shall not outwit him; the wicked shall not humble him. ^89-22

**89:23** I will crush his foes before him and strike down those who hate him. ^89-23

**89:24** My faithfulness and steadfast love shall be with him, and in my name his horn shall be exalted. ^89-24

**89:25** I will set his hand on the sea and his right hand on the rivers. ^89-25

**89:26** He shall cry to me, ‘You are my Father, my God, and the Rock of my salvation!’ ^89-26

**89:27** I will make him the firstborn, the highest of the kings of the earth. ^89-27

**89:28** Forever I will keep my steadfast love for him, and my covenant with him will stand firm. ^89-28

**89:29** I will establish his line forever and his throne as long as the heavens endure. ^89-29

**89:30** If his children forsake my law and do not walk according to my ordinances, ^89-30

**89:31** if they violate my statutes and do not keep my commandments, ^89-31

**89:32** then I will punish their transgression with the rod and their iniquity with scourges, ^89-32

**89:33** but I will not remove from him my steadfast love or be false to my faithfulness. ^89-33

**89:34** I will not violate my covenant or alter the word that went forth from my lips. ^89-34

**89:35** Once and for all I have sworn by my holiness; I will not lie to David. ^89-35

**89:36** His line shall continue forever, and his throne endure before me like the sun. ^89-36

**89:37** It shall be established forever like the moon, an enduring witness in the skies.” *Selah* ^89-37

**89:38** But now you have spurned and rejected him; you are full of wrath against your anointed. ^89-38

**89:39** You have renounced the covenant with your servant; you have defiled his crown in the dust. ^89-39

**89:40** You have broken through all his walls; you have laid his strongholds in ruins. ^89-40

**89:41** All who pass by plunder him; he has become the scorn of his neighbors. ^89-41

**89:42** You have exalted the right hand of his foes; you have made all his enemies rejoice. ^89-42

**89:43** Moreover, you have turned back the edge of his sword, and you have not supported him in battle. ^89-43

**89:44** You have removed the scepter from his hand^[Cn: Heb *removed his cleanness*] and hurled his throne to the ground. ^89-44

**89:45** You have cut short the days of his youth; you have covered him with shame. *Selah* ^89-45

**89:46** How long, O LORD? Will you hide yourself forever? How long will your wrath burn like fire? ^89-46

**89:47** Remember how short my time is—^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] for what vanity you have created all mortals! ^89-47

**89:48** Who can live and never see death? Who can escape the power of Sheol? *Selah* ^89-48

**89:49** Lord, where is your steadfast love of old, which by your faithfulness you swore to David? ^89-49

**89:50** Remember, O Lord, how your servant is taunted, how I bear in my bosom the insults of the peoples,^[Cn: Heb *bosom all of many peoples*] ^89-50

**89:51** with which your enemies taunt, O LORD, with which they taunted the footsteps of your anointed. ^89-51

**89:52** Blessed be the LORD forever. Amen and Amen. *(Psalms 90–106)* ^89-52
