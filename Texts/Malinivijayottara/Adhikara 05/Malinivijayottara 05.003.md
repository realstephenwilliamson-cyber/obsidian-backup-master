## Mūla

पातालानि ततः सप्त तेषामादौ महातलम् ।
रसातलं ततश्चान्यत्तलातलमतः परम् ॥ ^mula

pātālāni tataḥ sapta teṣām ādau mahātalam |
rasātalaṁ tataś cānyat talātalam ataḥ param || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, July 2023 – September 2023
> p. 70 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=75>))
>
> Then come the seven nether-worlds worlds (*pātāla*). The first of them is Mahātala, then Rasātala and after that is another (called) Talātala, (then) Sutala and Nitala, Vitala and Tala. By purifying the Hāṭaka (worlds), all of them are considered to have been purified. (with 5.3–4)
