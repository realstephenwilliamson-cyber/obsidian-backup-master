## The sons of Israel and the descendants of Judah

**2:1** These are the sons of Israel: Reuben, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, Zebulun, ^2-1

**2:2** Dan, Joseph, Benjamin, Naphtali, Gad, and Asher. ^2-2

**2:3** The sons of Judah: Er, Onan, and Shelah; these three the Canaanite woman Bath-shua bore to him. Now Er, Judah’s firstborn, was wicked in the sight of the LORD, and he put him to death. ^2-3

**2:4** His daughter-in-law Tamar also bore him Perez and Zerah. Judah had five sons in all. ^2-4

**2:5** The sons of Perez: Hezron and Hamul. ^2-5

**2:6** The sons of Zerah: Zimri, Ethan, Heman, Calcol, and Darda,^[Heb mss Gk Vg Syr: MT *Dara*] five in all. ^2-6

**2:7** The son of Carmi: Achar, the troubler of Israel, who transgressed in the matter of the devoted thing; ^2-7

**2:8** and Ethan’s son was Azariah. ^2-8

**2:9** The sons of Hezron who were born to him: Jerahmeel, Ram, and Chelubai.^[Or *Caleb*] ^2-9

**2:10** Ram became the father of Amminadab, and Amminadab became the father of Nahshon, prince of the sons of Judah. ^2-10

**2:11** Nahshon became the father of Salma, Salma of Boaz, ^2-11

**2:12** Boaz of Obed, Obed of Jesse. ^2-12

**2:13** Jesse became the father of Eliab his firstborn, Abinadab the second, Shimea the third, ^2-13

**2:14** Nethanel the fourth, Raddai the fifth, ^2-14

**2:15** Ozem the sixth, David the seventh; ^2-15

**2:16** and their sisters were Zeruiah and Abigail. The sons of Zeruiah: Abishai, Joab, and Asahel, three. ^2-16

**2:17** Abigail bore Amasa, and the father of Amasa was Jether the Ishmaelite. ^2-17

**2:18** Caleb son of Hezron had children by his wife Azubah and by Jerioth;^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] these were her sons: Jesher, Shobab, and Ardon. ^2-18

**2:19** When Azubah died, Caleb married Ephrath, who bore him Hur. ^2-19

**2:20** Hur became the father of Uri, and Uri became the father of Bezalel. ^2-20

**2:21** Afterward Hezron went in to the daughter of Machir father of Gilead, whom he married when he was sixty years old, and she bore him Segub, ^2-21

**2:22** and Segub became the father of Jair, who had twenty-three towns in the land of Gilead. ^2-22

**2:23** But Geshur and Aram took from them Havvoth-jair, Kenath and its villages, sixty towns. All these were descendants of Machir, father of Gilead. ^2-23

**2:24** After the death of Hezron in Caleb-ephrathah, Abijah wife of Hezron bore him Ashhur, father of Tekoa. ^2-24

**2:25** The sons of Jerahmeel, the firstborn of Hezron: Ram his firstborn, Bunah, Oren, Ozem, and Ahijah. ^2-25

**2:26** Jerahmeel also had another wife, whose name was Atarah; she was the mother of Onam. ^2-26

**2:27** The sons of Ram, the firstborn of Jerahmeel: Maaz, Jamin, and Eker. ^2-27

**2:28** The sons of Onam: Shammai and Jada. The sons of Shammai: Nadab and Abishur. ^2-28

**2:29** The name of Abishur’s wife was Abihail, and she bore him Ahban and Molid. ^2-29

**2:30** The sons of Nadab: Seled and Appaim; and Seled died childless. ^2-30

**2:31** The son of Appaim: Ishi. The son of Ishi: Sheshan. The son of Sheshan: Ahlai. ^2-31

**2:32** The sons of Jada, Shammai’s brother: Jether and Jonathan; and Jether died childless. ^2-32

**2:33** The sons of Jonathan: Peleth and Zaza. These were the descendants of Jerahmeel. ^2-33

**2:34** Now Sheshan had no sons, only daughters, but Sheshan had an Egyptian slave whose name was Jarha. ^2-34

**2:35** So Sheshan gave his daughter in marriage to his slave Jarha, and she bore him Attai. ^2-35

**2:36** Attai became the father of Nathan, and Nathan of Zabad. ^2-36

**2:37** Zabad became the father of Ephlal, and Ephlal of Obed. ^2-37

**2:38** Obed became the father of Jehu, and Jehu of Azariah. ^2-38

**2:39** Azariah became the father of Helez, and Helez of Eleasah. ^2-39

**2:40** Eleasah became the father of Sismai, and Sismai of Shallum. ^2-40

**2:41** Shallum became the father of Jekamiah, and Jekamiah of Elishama. ^2-41

**2:42** The sons of Caleb brother of Jerahmeel: Mesha his firstborn, who was father of Ziph. The sons of Mareshah father of Hebron. ^2-42

**2:43** The sons of Hebron: Korah, Tappuah, Rekem, and Shema. ^2-43

**2:44** Shema became father of Raham, father of Jorkeam; and Rekem became the father of Shammai. ^2-44

**2:45** The son of Shammai: Maon; and Maon was the father of Beth-zur. ^2-45

**2:46** Ephah also, Caleb’s concubine, bore Haran, Moza, and Gazez; and Haran became the father of Gazez. ^2-46

**2:47** The sons of Jahdai: Regem, Jotham, Geshan, Pelet, Ephah, and Shaaph. ^2-47

**2:48** Maacah, Caleb’s concubine, bore Sheber and Tirhanah. ^2-48

**2:49** She also bore Shaaph father of Madmannah, Sheva father of Machbenah and father of Gibea; and the daughter of Caleb was Achsah. ^2-49

**2:50** These were the descendants of Caleb. The sons of Hur the firstborn of Ephrathah: Shobal father of Kiriath-jearim, ^2-50

**2:51** Salma father of Bethlehem, and Hareph father of Beth-gader. ^2-51

**2:52** Shobal father of Kiriath-jearim had other sons: Haroeh, half of the Menuhoth. ^2-52

**2:53** And the families of Kiriath-jearim: the Ithrites, the Puthites, the Shumathites, and the Mishraites; from these came the Zorathites and the Eshtaolites. ^2-53

**2:54** The sons of Salma: Bethlehem, the Netophathites, Atroth-beth-joab, and half of the Manahathites, the Zorites. ^2-54

**2:55** The families also of the scribes that lived at Jabez: the Tirathites, the Shimeathites, and the Sucathites. These are the Kenites who came from Hammath, father of the house of Rechab. ^2-55
