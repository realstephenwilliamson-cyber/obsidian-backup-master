## Mūla

मण्डूकप्लवसिंहावलोकनाद्यैर्यथायथं न्यायैः ।
अविहतपूर्वापरकं शास्त्रार्थं योजयेदसङ्कीर्णम् ॥ ^mula

maṇḍūkaplavasiṃhāvalokanādyairyathāyathaṃ nyāyaiḥ |
avihatapūrvāparakaṃ śāstrārthaṃ yojayedasaṅkīrṇam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 07 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 5#^pg200|p. 183]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=200>))
>
> He should apply (his disciple’s mind) to the meaning of the scripture which is not adulterated (with any other scripture) and is not self-contradictory, by applying as appropriate, (the exegetical methods denoted by) the maxims of ‘the leap of a frog’ (when one moves abruptly between passages without intermediate steps) and ‘the gaze of the lion’ (analysing passages in their context, as does a lion who, walking, always keeps an eye on what is going on behind him and to the sides) etc.
