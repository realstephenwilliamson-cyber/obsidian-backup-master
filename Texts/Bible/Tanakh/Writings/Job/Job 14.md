# Job 14

Compare: [[NRSVue Job 14|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 14

**14:1** Man born of woman is short-lived and sated with trouble. ^14-1

**14:2** He blossoms like a flower and withers;  
He vanishes like a shadow and does not endure. ^14-2

**14:3** Do You fix Your gaze on such a one?  
Will You go to law with me? ^14-3

**14:4** Who can produce a clean thing out of an unclean one? No one![^1] ^14-4

**14:5** His days are determined;  
You know the number of his months;  
You have set him limits that he cannot pass. ^14-5

**14:6** Turn away from him, that he may be at ease  
Until, like a hireling, he finishes out his day. ^14-6

**14:7** There is hope for a tree;  
If it is cut down it will renew itself;  
Its shoots will not cease. ^14-7

**14:8** If its roots are old in the earth,  
And its stump dies in the ground, ^14-8

**14:9** At the scent of water it will bud  
And produce branches like a sapling. ^14-9

**14:10** But mortals languish and die;  
Man expires; where is he? ^14-10

**14:11** The waters of the sea fail,  
And the river dries up and is parched. ^14-11

**14:12** So man lies down never to rise;  
He will awake only when the heavens are no more,  
Only then be aroused from his sleep. ^14-12

**14:13** O that You would hide me in Sheol,  
Conceal me until Your anger passes,  
Set me a fixed time to attend to me. ^14-13

**14:14** If a man dies, can he live again?  
All the time of my service I wait  
Until my replacement comes. ^14-14

**14:15** You would call and I would answer You;  
You would set Your heart on Your handiwork. ^14-15

**14:16** Then You would not count my steps,  
Or keep watch over my sin. ^14-16

**14:17** My transgression would be sealed up in a pouch;  
You would coat over my iniquity. ^14-17

**14:18** Mountains collapse and crumble;  
Rocks are dislodged from their place. ^14-18

**14:19** Water wears away stone;  
Torrents wash away earth;  
So you destroy man’s hope, ^14-19

**14:20** You overpower him forever and he perishes;  
You alter his visage and dispatch him. ^14-20

**14:21** His sons attain honor and he does not know it;  
They are humbled and he is not aware of it. ^14-21

**14:22** He feels only the pain of his flesh,  
And his spirit mourns in him. ^14-22

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
