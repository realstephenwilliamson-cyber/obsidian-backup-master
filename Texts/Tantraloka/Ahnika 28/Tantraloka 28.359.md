## Mūla

प्रमात्रन्तरसद्भावः संविन्निष्ठो न तद्गतः ।
घटादेरस्तिता संविन्निष्ठिता न तु तद्गता ॥ ^mula

pramātrantarasadbhāvaḥ saṃvinniṣṭho na tadgataḥ |
ghaṭāderastitā saṃvinniṣṭhitā na tu tadgatā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 06 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 4#^pg176|p. 159]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=176>))
>
> As all inferences are (ultimately) based on one’s own cognitive awareness (saṁvedana), (even) the existence of (these) other subjects is based on consciousness and not on that (object itself). (with 28.358cd–359ab)
>
> (The knowledge of the) existence of (an object) such as a jar is grounded in consciousness, and not on that (jar itself as an object of knowledge). In the same way, (the knowledge of the existence) of (these) other perceivers is grounded in consciousness, and not on them (themselves as objects of knowledge). (with 28.359cd–360ab)
