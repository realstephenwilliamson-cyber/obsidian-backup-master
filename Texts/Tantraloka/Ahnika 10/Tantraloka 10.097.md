## Mūla

अवेद्यमेव भानं हि तथा कमनुयुञ्ज्महे ।
एवं पञ्चदशात्मेयं धरा तद्वज्जलादयः ॥ ^mula

avedyameva bhānaṃ hi tathā kamanuyuñjmahe |
evaṃ pañcadaśātmeyaṃ dharā tadvajjalādayaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 11 - Chapter Ten, part 2#^pg376|p. 357]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=376>))
>
> (An entity is) perceivable (vedya) because it manifests, and it is not perceivable (avedya) because it does not manifest as perceptible. Manifestation is like that – whom should we blame? (with 10.96cd–97ab)
>
> In this way, the Earth (principle) has fifteen aspects, as do those (principles) from Water to Unmanifest Nature, insofar as they are perceptible (vedyatā) to the Fully Conditioned (sakala) (subjects). (with 10.97cd–98ab)
