[^1]: Eriugena’s translation of this heading does not include mention of a “first book” (CCSG 7:2) and consequently does not include the second heading, below, concerning a “second book” (cf. Later Greek scribes copied the work as a single item, often in a large codex containing other works by Maximos, but retained the headings regarding the original division of the *Responses* into two books; cf.
