# χώρα

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · noun

**Dodson:** region, land, fields — (a) a country or region, (b) the land, as opposed to the sea, (c) the country, distinct from town, (d) plur: fields. (Strong's 5561; headword χώρα, ας, ἡ)
