## Mūla

उपास्यता तत्तत्स्थाने प्राङ्न्यास्यो रतिशेखरः ।
इत्थं श्रीपूर्वशास्त्रे मे सम्प्रदायं न्यरूपयत् ॥ ^mula

upāsyatā tattatsthāne prāṅnyāsyo ratiśekharaḥ |
itthaṃ śrīpūrvaśāstre me sampradāyaṃ nyarūpayat || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 05 - Chapter Fifteen, part 4#^pg165|p. 147]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=165>))
>
> In the same way, if the main (deity) to be worshipped is the Lord Bhairavasadbhāva, Ratiśkhara should be deposited first in each corresponding place. (with 15.244cd–245ab)
>
> The traditional teaching (sarnpradāāya) concerning the procedure for the depositions (described) in this way in the Śrīpūrvaśāstra was explained to me by Śambhunātha. (And this explanation is correct;) otherwise, how else can we explain the fact that the deposition (of Ratiśekhara) is omitted? He too possesses limbs (as do the others). (with 15.245cd–246)
