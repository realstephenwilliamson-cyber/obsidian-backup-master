## Mūla

अनुत्तरपदाप्तये तदिदमाणवं दर्शिताभ्युपायमतिविस्तरान्ननु विदांकुरुध्वं बुधाः ॥ ^mula

anuttarapadāptaye tadidamāṇavaṃ darśitābhyupāyamativistarānnanu vidāṃkurudhvaṃ budhāḥ || ^iast

> This, the Individual Means (ānavābhyupāya), has been explained at great length so that one may attain the plane of Anuttara (the Absolute). May the wise take care to understand it clearly. (with 12.26ab)
