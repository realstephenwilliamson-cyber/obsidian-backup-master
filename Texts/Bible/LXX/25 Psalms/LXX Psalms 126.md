# LXX Psalms 126

← [[LXX Psalms 125]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 127]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 127]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 127]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**126:1** A Song of Degrees. Except the Lord build the house, they that build labour in vain: except the Lord keep the city, the watchman watches in vain.  ^126-1

**126:2** It is vain for you to rise early: ye rise up after resting, ye that eat the bread of grief; while he gives sleep to his beloved.  ^126-2

**126:3** Behold, the inheritance of the Lord, children, the reward of the fruit of the womb.  ^126-3

**126:4** As arrows in the hand of a mighty man; so are the children of those who were outcasts.  ^126-4

**126:5** Blessed is the man who shall satisfy his desire with them: they shall not be ashamed when they shall speak to their enemies in the gates.  ^126-5
