# Psalm 140

← [[BCP Psalm 139]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 141]] →

*Eripe me, Domine* · Book Five · Twenty-ninth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 140]]

**140:1** Deliver me, O LORD, from evildoers; \*  
protect me from the violent, ^140-1

**140:2** Who devise evil in their hearts \*  
and stir up strife all day long. ^140-2

**140:3** They have sharpened their tongues like a serpent; \*  
adder's poison is under their lips. ^140-3

**140:4** Keep me, O LORD, from the hands of the wicked; \*  
protect me from the violent,  
who are determined to trip me up. ^140-4

**140:5** The proud have hidden a snare for me  
and stretched out a net of cords; \*  
they have set traps for me along the path. ^140-5

**140:6** I have said to the LORD, "You are my God; \*  
listen, O LORD, to my supplication. ^140-6

**140:7** O Lord GOD, the strength of my salvation, \*  
you have covered my head in the day of battle. ^140-7

**140:8** Do not grant the desires of the wicked, O LORD, \*  
nor let their evil plans prosper. ^140-8

**140:9** Let not those who surround me lift up their heads; \*  
let the evil of their lips overwhelm them. ^140-9

**140:10** Let hot burning coals fall upon them; \*  
let them be cast into the mire, never to rise up again." ^140-10

**140:11** A slanderer shall not be established on the earth, \*  
and evil shall hunt down the lawless. ^140-11

**140:12** I know that the LORD will maintain the cause of the poor \*  
and render justice to the needy. ^140-12

[p. 797] ^pg797

**140:13** Surely, the righteous will give thanks to your Name, \*  
and the upright shall continue in your sight. ^140-13
