# 1 Thessalonians 5 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 04|← 1 Thessalonians 4]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · —

Compare: [[1 Thessalonians 05|BSB and SBLGNT]] · [[DRB 1 Thessalonians 05|Douay-Rheims]] · [[JANT 1 Thessalonians 05|JANT notes]]

**5:1** Now concerning the times and the seasons, brothers and sisters, you do not need to have anything written to you. ^5-1

**5:2** For you yourselves know very well that the day of the Lord will come like a thief in the night. ^5-2

**5:3** When they say, “There is peace and security,” then sudden destruction will come upon them, as labor pains come upon a pregnant woman, and there will be no escape! ^5-3

**5:4** But you, brothers and sisters, are not in darkness, for that day to surprise you like a thief; ^5-4

**5:5** for you are all children of light and children of the day; we are not of the night or of darkness. ^5-5

**5:6** So, then, let us not fall asleep as others do, but let us keep awake and be sober, ^5-6

**5:7** for those who sleep sleep at night, and those who are drunk get drunk at night. ^5-7

**5:8** But since we belong to the day, let us be sober and put on the breastplate of faith and love and for a helmet the hope of salvation. ^5-8

**5:9** For God has destined us not for wrath but for obtaining salvation through our Lord Jesus Christ, ^5-9

**5:10** who died for us, so that whether we are awake or asleep we may live with him. ^5-10

**5:11** Therefore encourage one another and build up each other, as indeed you are doing. ^5-11

## Final exhortations, greetings, and benediction

**5:12** But we appeal to you, brothers and sisters, to respect those who labor among you and have charge of you in the Lord and admonish you; ^5-12

**5:13** esteem them very highly in love because of their work. Be at peace among yourselves. ^5-13

**5:14** And we urge you, brothers and sisters, to admonish the idlers, encourage the fainthearted, help the weak, be patient with all of them. ^5-14

**5:15** See that none of you repays evil for evil, but always seek to do good to one another and to all. ^5-15

**5:16** Rejoice always, ^5-16

**5:17** pray without ceasing, ^5-17

**5:18** give thanks in all circumstances, for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus for you. ^5-18

**5:19** Do not quench the Spirit. ^5-19

**5:20** Do not despise prophecies, ^5-20

**5:21** but test everything; hold fast to what is good; ^5-21

**5:22** abstain from every form of evil. ^5-22

**5:23** May the God of peace himself sanctify you entirely, and may your spirit and soul and body be kept sound^[Or *complete*] and blameless at the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ. ^5-23

**5:24** The one who calls you is faithful, and he will do this. ^5-24

**5:25** Brothers and sisters, pray for us. ^5-25

**5:26** Greet all the brothers and sisters with a holy kiss. ^5-26

**5:27** I solemnly command you by the Lord that this letter be read to all the brothers and sisters. ^5-27

**5:28** The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you.^[Other ancient authorities add *Amen*] ^5-28
