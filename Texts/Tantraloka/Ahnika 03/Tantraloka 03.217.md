## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. II
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 02 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v02 10 - Chapter Three, part 7#^pg352|p. 336]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 02.pdf#page=352>))
>
> ‘It is said that the union (saṁghaṭṭa) of the two (namely, mind and thought,) takes place in the centre of the calix of the lotus of the Heart. That is said to be the inner emission, and its sign (lakṣaṇa) is the repose of the mind. The sign of the second emission is the awakening of the mind. This entire universe, mobile (living) and immobile (insentient), shines here as one (ekībhūta). (This emission is of two types). When there is a subtle division here between subject and object, that is said to be (immanent and so is) ‘with aspects’ (sakala). (The second type) is united with (transcendental) Śiva, and so is ‘without aspects’ (niṣkala). The sign of the third emission is the dissolving away of the mind. It is full (and perfect) cognitive consciousness (saṁpūrṇagrahaṇa) that corresponds to the cessation (vicchitti) (of the distinction between) subject (grāhaka) and object (grāhya). In a state of oneness (ekībhāva), it is subtle, complete knowledge (vijñāna), satisfied in its own nature.’ (with 3.215cd–219)
