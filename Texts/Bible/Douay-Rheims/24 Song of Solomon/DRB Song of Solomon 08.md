# Song of Solomon 8 (Douay-Rheims)

*Canticle of Canticles 8 in the Douay-Rheims*

[[DRB Song of Solomon 07|← Song of Solomon 7]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · —

**8:1** Who shall give thee to me for my brother, sucking the breasts of my mother, that I may find thee without, and kiss thee, and now no man may despise me? ^8-1

**8:2** I will take hold of thee, and bring thee Into my mother’s house: there thou shalt teach me, and I will give thee a cup of spiced wine and new wine of my pomegranates. ^8-2

**8:3** His left hand under my head, and his right hand shall embrace me. ^8-3

**8:4** I adjure you, O daughters of Jerusalem, that you stir not up, nor awake my love till she please. ^8-4

**8:5** Who is this that cometh up from the desert, flowing with delights, leaning upon her beloved? Under the apple tree I raised thee up: there thy mother was corrupted, there she was defloured that bore thee. ^8-5

**8:6** Put me as a seal upon thy heart, as a seal upon thy arm, for love is strong as death, jealousy as hard as hell, the lamps thereof are fire and flames. ^8-6

**8:7** Many waters cannot quench charity, neither can the floods drown it: if a man should give all the substance of his house for love, he shall despise it as nothing. ^8-7

**8:8** Our sister is little, and hath no breasts. What shall we do to our sister in the day when she is to be spoken to? ^8-8

**8:9** If she be a wall: let us build upon it bulwarks of silver: if she be a door, let us join it together with boards or cedar. ^8-9

**8:10** I am a wall: and my breasts are as a tower since I am become in his presence as one finding peace. ^8-10

**8:11** The peaceable had a vineyard, in that which hath people: he let out the same to keepers, every man bringeth for the fruit thereof a thousand pieces of silver. ^8-11

**8:12** My vineyard is before me. A thousand are for thee, the peaceable, and two hundred for them that keep the fruit thereof. ^8-12

**8:13** Thou that dwellest in the gardens, the friends hearken: make me hear thy voice. ^8-13

**8:14** Flee away, O my beloved, and be like to the roe, and to the young hart upon the mountains of aromatical spices. ^8-14
