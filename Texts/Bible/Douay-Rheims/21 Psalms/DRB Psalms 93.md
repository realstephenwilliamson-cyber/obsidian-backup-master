# Psalms 93 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 92|← Psalms 92]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 94|Psalms 94 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 93|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**93:1** The Lord is the God to whom revenge belongeth: the God of revenge hath acted freely. ^93-1

**93:2** Lift up thyself, thou that judgest the earth: render a reward to the proud. ^93-2

**93:3** How long shall sinners, O Lord: how long shall sinners glory? ^93-3

**93:4** Shall they utter, and speak iniquity: shall all speak who work injustice? ^93-4

**93:5** Thy people, O Lord, they have brought low: and they have afflicted thy inheritance. ^93-5

**93:6** They have slain the widow and the stranger: and they have murdered the fatherless. ^93-6

**93:7** And they have said: The Lord shall not see: neither shall the God of Jacob understand. ^93-7

**93:8** Understand, ye senseless among the people: and, you fools, be wise at last. ^93-8

**93:9** He that planted the ear, shall he not hear? or he that formed the eye, doth he not consider? ^93-9

**93:10** He that chastiseth nations, shall he not rebuke: he that teacheth man knowledge? ^93-10

**93:11** The Lord knoweth the thoughts of men, that they are vain. ^93-11

**93:12** Blessed is the man whom thou shalt instruct, O Lord: and shalt teach him out of thy law. ^93-12

**93:13** That thou mayst give him rest from the evil days: till a pit be dug for the wicked. ^93-13

**93:14** For the Lord will not cast off his people: neither will he forsake his own inheritance. ^93-14

**93:15** Until justice be turned into judgment: and they that are near it are all the upright in heart. ^93-15

**93:16** Who shall rise up for me against the evildoers? or who shall stand with me against the workers of iniquity? ^93-16

**93:17** Unless the Lord had been my helper, my soul had almost dwelt in hell. ^93-17

**93:18** If I said: My foot is moved: thy mercy, O Lord, assisted me. ^93-18

**93:19** According to the multitude of my sorrows in my heart, thy comforts have given joy to my soul. ^93-19

**93:20** Doth the seat of iniquity stick to thee, who framest labour in commandment? ^93-20

**93:21** They will hunt after the soul of the just, and will condemn innocent blood. ^93-21

**93:22** But the Lord is my refuge: and my God the help of my hope. ^93-22

**93:23** And he will render them their iniquity: and in their malice he will destroy them: the Lord our God will destroy them. ^93-23
