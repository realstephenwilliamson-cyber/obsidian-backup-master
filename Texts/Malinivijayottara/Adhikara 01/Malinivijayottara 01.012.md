## Mūla

सर्वसिद्धिकरं ब्रूहि प्रसादात्परमेश्वर ।
एवमुक्तस्तदा देव्या प्रहस्योवाच विश्वराट् ॥ ^mula

sarvasiddhikaraṁ brūhi prasādāt parameśvara |
evam uktas tadā[^41] devyā prahasyovāca viśvarāṭ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2022 – June 2022
> p. 40 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=16>))
>
> “The Siddhayogīśvarītantra you have uttered extending for ninety million came forth originally in three divisions (bheda). O Maheśvara, you have explained the very extensive Path of Yoga in the Mālinīvijayatantra consisting of thirty million (verses). Similarly, you once again uttered its summary in twelve thousand (verses). (But) that too is (so) extensive that it cannot be grasped by those with little intellect. Thus, O Supreme Lord, please, summarizing that, (also) utter (a version) in brief that is beneficial to those of little intellect and gives rise to every accomplishment (siddhi).”
>
> When the Goddess had said this, the lord of the universe, smiling, said: “O Goddess, listen, I will utter the Mālinīvijayottara, that belongs to the teachings (mata) of the Siddhayogīśvarī (Tantra).[^42] (with 1.8cd–13)

[^41]: Printed edition: *uktas tata*
[^42]: MV 1/8cd-13 is quoted in TĀv ad 9/7.
