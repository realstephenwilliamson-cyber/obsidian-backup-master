# Psalm 130

← [[BCP Psalm 129]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 131]] →

*De profundis* · Book Five · Twenty-seventh Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 130]]

**130:1** Out of the depths have I called to you, O LORD;  
LORD, hear my voice; \*  
let your ears consider well the voice of my supplication. ^130-1

**130:2** If you, LORD, were to note what is done amiss, \*  
O LORD, who could stand? ^130-2

**130:3** For there is forgiveness with you; \*  
therefore you shall be feared. ^130-3

[p. 785]

**130:4** I wait for the LORD; my soul waits for him; \*  
in his word is my hope. ^130-4

**130:5** My soul waits for the LORD,  
more than watchmen for the morning, \*  
more than watchmen for the morning. ^130-5

**130:6** O Israel, wait for the LORD, \*  
for with the LORD there is mercy; ^130-6

**130:7** With him there is plenteous redemption, \*  
and he shall redeem Israel from all their sins. ^130-7
