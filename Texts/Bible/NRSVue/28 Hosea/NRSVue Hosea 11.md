# Hosea 11 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Hosea 10|← Hosea 10]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Hosea 12|Hosea 12 →]]

## God’s compassion despite Israel’s ingratitude

**11:1** When Israel was a child, I loved him, and out of Egypt I called my son. ^11-1

**11:2** The more I^[Gk: Heb *they*] called them, the more they went from me;^[Gk: Heb *them*] they kept sacrificing to the Baals and offering incense to idols. ^11-2

**11:3** Yet it was I who taught Ephraim to walk; I took them up in my^[Gk Syr Vg: Heb *his*] arms, but they did not know that I healed them. ^11-3

**11:4** I led them with cords of human kindness, with bands of love. I was to them like those who lift infants to their cheeks.^[Or *who ease the yoke on their jaws*] I bent down to them and fed them. ^11-4

**11:5** They shall return to the land of Egypt, and Assyria shall be their king, because they have refused to return to me. ^11-5

**11:6** The sword rages in their cities; it consumes their oracle priests and devours because of their schemes. ^11-6

**11:7** My people are bent on turning away from me. To the Most High they call, but he does not raise them up at all.^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^11-7

**11:8** How can I give you up, Ephraim? How can I hand you over, O Israel? How can I make you like Admah? How can I treat you like Zeboiim? My heart recoils within me; my compassion grows warm and tender. ^11-8

**11:9** I will not execute my fierce anger; I will not again destroy Ephraim, for I am God and no mortal, the Holy One in your midst, and I will not come in wrath.^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^11-9

**11:10** They shall go after the LORD, who roars like a lion; when he roars, his children shall come trembling from the west. ^11-10

**11:11** They shall come trembling like birds from Egypt and like doves from the land of Assyria, and I will return them to their homes, says the LORD. ^11-11

**11:12** ^[12.1 in Heb]Ephraim has surrounded me with lies and the house of Israel with deceit, but Judah still walks^[Heb *roams* or *rules*] with God and is faithful to the Holy One. ^11-12
