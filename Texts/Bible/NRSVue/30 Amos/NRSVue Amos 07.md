# Amos 7 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Amos 06|← Amos 6]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Amos 08|Amos 8 →]]

## Locusts, fire, and a plumb line

**7:1** This is what the Lord GOD showed me: he was forming locusts at the time the latter growth began to sprout (it was the latter growth after the king’s mowings). ^7-1

**7:2** When they had finished eating the grass of the land, I said, “O Lord GOD, forgive, I beg you! How can Jacob stand? He is so small!” ^7-2

**7:3** The LORD relented concerning this; “It shall not be,” said the LORD. ^7-3

**7:4** This is what the Lord GOD showed me: the Lord GOD was calling for judgment by fire, and it devoured the great deep and was eating up the land. ^7-4

**7:5** Then I said, “O Lord GOD, cease, I beg you! How can Jacob stand? He is so small!” ^7-5

**7:6** The LORD relented concerning this; “This also shall not be,” said the Lord GOD. ^7-6

**7:7** This is what he showed me: the Lord was standing beside a wall built with a plumb line, with a plumb line in his hand. ^7-7

**7:8** And the LORD said to me, “Amos, what do you see?” And I said, “A plumb line.” Then the Lord said, “See, I am setting a plumb line in the midst of my people Israel; I will spare them no longer; ^7-8

**7:9** the high places of Isaac shall be made desolate, and the sanctuaries of Israel shall be laid waste, and I will rise against the house of Jeroboam with the sword.” ^7-9

## Amaziah complains to the king

**7:10** Then Amaziah, the priest of Bethel, sent to King Jeroboam of Israel, saying, “Amos has conspired against you in the very center of the house of Israel; the land is not able to bear all his words. ^7-10

**7:11** For thus Amos has said, ‘Jeroboam shall die by the sword, and Israel must go into exile away from his land.’ ” ^7-11

**7:12** And Amaziah said to Amos, “O seer, go, flee away to the land of Judah, earn your bread there, and prophesy there, ^7-12

**7:13** but never again prophesy at Bethel, for it is the king’s sanctuary, and it is a temple of the kingdom.” ^7-13

**7:14** Then Amos answered Amaziah, “I am^[Or *was*] no prophet nor a prophet’s son, but I am^[Or *was*] a herdsman and a dresser of sycamore trees, ^7-14

**7:15** and the LORD took me from following the flock, and the LORD said to me, ‘Go, prophesy to my people Israel.’ ^7-15

**7:16** “Now therefore hear the word of the LORD. You say, ‘Do not prophesy against Israel, and do not preach against the house of Isaac.’ ^7-16

**7:17** Therefore thus says the LORD: Your wife shall become a prostitute in the city, and your sons and your daughters shall fall by the sword, and your land shall be parceled out by line; you yourself shall die in an unclean land, and Israel shall surely go into exile away from its land.” ^7-17
