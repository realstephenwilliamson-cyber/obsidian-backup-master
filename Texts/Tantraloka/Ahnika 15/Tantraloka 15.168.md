## Mūla

अर्चायै योग्यमानन्दो यस्मादुन्मग्नता चितः ।
तेनाचिद्रूपदेहादिप्राधान्यविनिमज्जकम् ॥ ^mula

arcāyai yogyamānando yasmādunmagnatā citaḥ |
tenācidrūpadehādiprādhānyavinimajjakam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 04 - Chapter Fifteen, part 3#^pg123|p. 105]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=123>))
>
> Whatever causes (one’s own) immersed consciousness to emerge is suitable (as a substance to be offered) for worship, because the bliss (experienced in this way) is the emergence of consciousness. (with 15.167cd–168ab)
>
> Thus, whatever captivates the heart (hrdayahārin), suppressing the predominance of (the mistaken identification with) the body etc. which is not consciousness, and (so) generates bliss, is a suitable (offering for) worship (pūjā). (with 15.168cd–169ab)

*Lead-in:* अतश्च इदं सिद्धम्--इत्याह--

> तेनाचिद्रूपदेहादिप्राधान्यविनिमज्जकम् ॥
> आनन्दजननं पूजायोग्यं हृदयहारि यत् ।

यदभिप्रायेणैव

"या या संविदुदारा यो योऽप्यानन्दसुन्दरो भावः ।

जगति यदद्भुतरूपं तत्तद्देव्यास्तवाकारः ॥"

इत्यादि अन्यैरुक्तम् ॥
