## Mūla

विद्यामूर्तिं ततो दध्यान्मन्त्रेणानेन शाङ्करि ।
दण्डाक्रान्तं महाप्राणं दण्डारूढं सनाभिकम् ॥ ^mula

vidyāmūrtiṁ tato dadhyān mantreṇānena śāṅkari |
daṇḍākrāntaṁ mahāprāṇaṁ daṇḍārūḍhaṁ sanābhikam || ^iast

> **Navātman, Limbs and Faces**
>
> He should then add the *Vidyāmūrti*[^19] by means of the (following) Mantra. The great vital breath (H) preceded and followed (*ākrānta*) by the staff ®, the navel (KṢ), the hip (M), the left breast (L), the neck (V), the left part of the back (Y) adorned with ornament of the left (ear) (Ū) and (then) the Void of the Point and the Half Moon adorned with Sound (*nāda*) and Śakti's Point (*śaktibindu*). O goddess, this most excellent *piṇḍa* (mantra) is taught (in the scriptures) (*śruta*) as the Ninefold One (i.e., Navātman). This, the producer of all accomplishments, has been mentioned (here) secretly (*sarahasyam*). (with 8.21–24ab)

[^19]: Literally 'the icon of the Vidyā'. This is formed from Navātman – RHRKṢMLVYŪṀ. Concerning this seed syllable Mantra, see TĀ 30/11cd-16ab.
