## Mūla

स तन्निबन्धं विदधे महार्थं
युक्त्यागमोदीरिततन्त्रतत्त्वम् ।
आलोकमासाद्य यदीयमेष
लोकः सुखं सञ्चरिता क्रियासु ॥ ^mula

sa tannibandhaṃ vidadhe mahārthaṃ
yuktyāgamodīritatantratattvam |
ālokamāsādya yadīyameṣa
lokaḥ sukhaṃ sañcaritā kriyāsu || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 11 - Chapter Thirty-Seven, part 1#^pg284|p. 266]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=284>))
>
> (Thus), he composed this greatly precious (mahārtha) work, in which is expounded the essence of the Tantras (tantratattva), in accord with reason (yukti) and the tradition (āgama). Easy is the practice of (their) rites once (the people of) this world has acquired (its) light (āloka).
