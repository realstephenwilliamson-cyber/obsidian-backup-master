### 7.1–52: Festival of Tabernacles

*[[NRSVue John 07#^7-1|Text]], p. 191* ^7-1

**[[NRSVue John 07#^7-2|2]]:** Tabernacles, or *Booths* (Heb “Sukkot”), a week-long fall pilgrimage festival celebrating the harvest and commemorating Israel’s sojourn in the desert after the Exodus. All males were required to attend this festival ([[Torah 31 - Emor#^23-42|Lev 23.42]]; [[Torah 47 - Re'eh#^16-16|Deut 16.16]], which specifies Passover, Weeks, and Booths as required). Jesus’ refusal (v 8), followed by his covert attendance (v 10), shows that he obeys the requirement but not in the company of his brothers. ^7-2

**[[NRSVue John 07#^7-3|3]]:** *His brothers*, likely biological relations rather than disciples (for “brothers” as disciples, see 20.17). ^7-3

**[[NRSVue John 07#^7-6|6–7]]:** Jesus’ brothers (vv 3,5,10), like “the Jews,” represent the hostile world. ^7-6

**[[NRSVue John 07#^7-8|8]]:** Jesus tries to hide his intentions. Despite this statement, he secretly goes to Jerusalem (v 10). ^7-8

**[[NRSVue John 07#^7-11|11]]:** *The Jews*, here the Jewish crowds in Jerusalem. ^7-11

**[[NRSVue John 07#^7-13|13]]:** *The Jews*, here the authorities. ^7-13

**[[NRSVue John 07#^7-14|14–18]]:** Jesus teaches openly in the Temple (see 8.20n.), which he mentions when interrogated by Annas (18.20). ^7-14

**[[NRSVue John 07#^7-15|15]]:** *The Jews*, here apparently the crowd listening to Jesus. ^7-15

**[[NRSVue John 07#^7-18|18]]:** An implicit criticism of Jewish authorities as self-glorifying (see [[Matthew 23#^23-5|Mt 23.5–7]]). ^7-18

**[[NRSVue John 07#^7-22|22–24]]:** Jesus refers to the healing of the lame man (ch 5). *Moses*, the Torah ([[Torah 03 - Lech Lecha#^17-10|Gen 17.10]]; [[Torah 27 - Tazria#^12-3|Lev 12.3]]), including *the patriarchs*, traditions predating the time of Moses. *of the living God* b Other ancient authorities read *Judas Iscariot son of Simon;* others, *Judas son of Simon from Karyot* (Kerioth) c Other ancient authorities read *was not at liberty* d Or *Tabernacles* e Other ancient authorities read *wants it* f Other ancient authorities add *yet* g Other ancient authorities lack *as it were* h Or *this man know his letters* ^7-22

**[[NRSVue John 07#^7-25|25]]:** The *people of Jerusalem* know the sentiment against Jesus (contrast the crowd of v 20). John distinguishes here between the people and the authorities; “hoi Ioudaioi” (“the Jews”) is not used of either group in vv 25–26. ^7-25

**[[NRSVue John 07#^7-27|27–28]]:** *No one will know* . . . , possible allusion to the “hidden Messiah,” one of several first-century strands of messianic expectation that is directly related to the idea of a preexistent Messiah whose existence will be revealed at a specific time (see *1 En*. 52.2–5). Rabbinic sources sometimes list the name of the Messiah as one of the items created on the sixth day of creation, which implies the pre-existence and therefore eventual revelation of a hidden Messiah (e.g., *b. Pesah*. 54a). Although the crowd claims to know where Jesus came from — perhaps an allusion to Nazareth (see 1.45; 6.42) — Jesus asserts that they do not know where he really comes from — God. He therefore fulfills the “hidden Messiah” tradition. ^7-27

**[[NRSVue John 07#^7-30|30]]:** *They*, perhaps the people of Jerusalem (v 25), but their attempt to arrest him suggests a reference to the authorities. ^7-30

**[[NRSVue John 07#^7-31|31]]:** The *crowd* continues to be divided, with some believing that Jesus fulfills messianic criteria such as performing *signs*. [[Torah 47 - Re'eh#^13-2|Deut 13.1–5]]; 18.15–19 as well as Josephus, *Ant*. 20.168, express reservations about the belief that the prophet-like-Moses (Deut) or the Messiah (Josephus) will perform signs and wonders, but John implies that this was a well-entrenched belief. ^7-31

**[[NRSVue John 07#^7-32|32]]:** *Pharisees* and *chief priests*, John often groups these two groups together, suggesting that, unlike the other evangelists, he was not aware of the differences between the Pharisees and the Sadducees (the priestly class; see, e.g., [[Matthew 22#^22-23|Mt 22.23]]; [[Mark 12#^12-18|Mk 12.18-27]]; [[Acts 23#^23-8|Acts 23.8]]). See “Jewish Movements of the NT Period,” p.614. *Temple police*, guards who kept order in the Temple (see [[Acts 05#^5-22|Acts 5.22]],[[Acts 05#^5-26|26]]; Josephus, *J.W.* 4.293). ^7-32

**[[NRSVue John 07#^7-34|34–35]]:** Another misunderstanding. The Jews take Jesus to refer to the Dispersion (the Diaspora), yet readers know that Jesus is alluding to his death. *Greeks*, Gentiles rather than Diaspora Jews. ^7-34

**[[NRSVue John 07#^7-37|37–39]]:** According to rabbinic sources (e.g., *m. Sukk*. 4.9–10), at Sukkoth people brought water in a golden pitcher from the Pool of Siloam to the Temple. The rabbis emphasized the joy that accompanied this ceremony: “Anyone who has never seen the rejoicing at the place of \[water\] drawing, has never seen rejoicing in all his days” (*m. Sukk*. 5.1). *Last day*, “Hoshana Rabbah,” the “great” hosanna, the culmination of these prayers (*b. Sukk.* 53a). ^7-37

**[[NRSVue John 07#^7-38|38]]:** *Scripture has said* . . . , this does not correspond to a specific biblical verse but contains allusions to [[Proverbs 18#^18-4|Prov 18.4]]; [[Isaiah 44#^44-3|Isa 44.3]]; [[Isaiah 58#^58-11|58.11]]. This type of scriptural usage may indicate that the author was working from memory rather than with a written text. ^7-38

**[[NRSVue John 07#^7-39|39]]:** *Spirit*, NRSV capitalizes this word to identify this as the Holy Spirit predicted in 14.26 and 20.22; see also 15.26. *Glorified*, Jesus’ death and resurrection (13.31). ^7-39

**[[NRSVue John 07#^7-40|40–42]]:** Speculations that the Messiah would be born in Bethlehem of Judea, not in Galilee ([[Micah 5#^5-1|Mic 5.2]]; [[Matthew 02#^2-1|Mt 2.1]]; [[Luke 02#^2-1|Lk 2.1–7]]) and descended from King David (see [[II Samuel 07#^7-16|2 Sam 7.16]]). *Bethlehem*, David’s home town ([[I Samuel 17#^17-12|1 Sam 17.12]]). ^7-40

**[[NRSVue John 07#^7-49|49]]:** *Crowd . . . does not know the law*, ordinary Jews are not experts in legal matters. ^7-49

**[[NRSVue John 07#^7-50|50]]:** *Nicodemus*, see 3.1. ^7-50

**[[NRSVue John 07#^7-51|51]]:** *Hearing*, [[Torah 48 - Shoftim#^19-15|Deut 19.15–21]] mandates taking testimony from accusers and witnesses. **\[7.53–8.11: The adulterous woman\].** Not part of the original Gospel, this episode appears in mss of other Gospels, often after [[Luke 21#^21-38|Lk 21.38]], where it better fits the narrative. Here the conclusion (v 11) does not lead to v 12. ^7-51
