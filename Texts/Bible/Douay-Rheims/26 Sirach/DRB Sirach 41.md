# Sirach 41 (Douay-Rheims)

*Ecclesiasticus 41 in the Douay-Rheims*

[[DRB Sirach 40|← Sirach 40]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Sirach 42|Sirach 42 →]]

**41:1** O death, how bitter is the remembrance of thee to a man that hath peace in his possessions! ^41-1

**41:2** To a man that is at rest, and whose ways are prosperous in all things, and that is yet able to take meat! ^41-2

**41:3** O death, thy sentence is welcome to the man that is in need, and to him whose strength faileth: ^41-3

**41:4** Who is in a decrepit age, and that is in care about all things, and to the distrustful that loseth patience! ^41-4

**41:5** Fear not the sentence of death. Remember what things have been before thee, and what shall come after thee: this sentence is from the Lord upon all flesh. ^41-5

**41:6** And what shall come upon thee by the good pleasure of the most High? Whether ten, or a hundred, or a thousand years. ^41-6

**41:7** For among the dead there is no accusing of life. ^41-7

**41:8** The children of sinners become children of abominations, and they that converse near the houses of the ungodly. ^41-8

**41:9** The inheritance of the children of sinners shall perish, and with their posterity shall be a perpetual reproach. ^41-9

**41:10** The children will complain of an ungodly father, because for his sake they are in reproach. ^41-10

**41:11** Woe to you, ungodly men, who have forsaken the law of the most high Lord. ^41-11

**41:12** And if you be born, you shall be born in malediction: and if you die, in malediction shall be your portion. ^41-12

**41:13** All things that are of the earth, shall return into the earth: so the ungodly shall from malediction to destruction. ^41-13

**41:14** The mourning of men is about their body, but the name of the ungodly shall be blotted out. ^41-14

**41:15** Take care of a good name: for this shall continue with thee, more than a thousand treasures precious and great. ^41-15

**41:16** A good life hath its number of days: but a good name shall continue for ever. ^41-16

**41:17** My children, keep discipline in peace: for wisdom that is hid, and a treasure that is not seen, what profit is there in them both? ^41-17

**41:18** Better is the man that hideth his folly, then the man that hideth his wisdom. ^41-18

**41:19** Wherefore have a shame of these things I am now going to speak of. ^41-19

**41:20** For it is not good to keep all shamefacedness: and all things do not please all men in opinion. ^41-20

**41:21** Be ashamed of fornication before father and mother: and of a lie before a governor and a man in power: ^41-21

**41:22** Of an offence before a prince, and a judge: of iniquity before a congregation and a people: ^41-22

**41:23** Of injustice before a companion and friend: and in regard to the place where thou dwellest, ^41-23

**41:24** Of theft, and of the truth of God, and the covenant: of leaning with thy elbow over meat, and of deceit in giving and taking: ^41-24

**41:25** Of silence before them that salute thee: of looking upon a harlot: and of turning away thy face from thy kinsman. ^41-25

**41:26** Turn not sway thy face from thy neighbour, and of taking away a portion and not restoring. ^41-26

**41:27** Gaze not upon another man’s wife, and be not inquisitive after his handmaid, and approach not her bed. ^41-27

**41:28** Be ashamed of upbraiding speeches before friends: and after thou hast given, upbraid not. ^41-28
