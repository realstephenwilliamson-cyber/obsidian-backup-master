## Mūla

तदाधिवासं कृत्वाह्नि द्वितीये मण्डलं लिखेत् ।
सामुदायिकयागेऽथ तथान्यत्र यथोदितम् ॥ ^mula

tadādhivāsaṃ kṛtvāhni dvitīye maṇḍalaṃ likhet |
sāmudāyikayāge'tha tathānyatra yathoditam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 05 - Chapter Sixteen, part 1#^pg21|p. 1]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=21>))
>
> Now, in order to attain the status of a spiritual son (putraka), (we will proceed) to expound the ritual procedure (vidhi) here (for this, as) explained by Śiva (Himself). If the teacher wishes to promote a regular initiate (samayin) to the status of a spiritual son, adept (sādhaka) or teacher (deśika), then once the preliminary rite of purification (adhivāsa) has been performed, he should draw the maṇḍala on the following day, in the manner previously described for the composite sacrifice (sāmudāyikayāga) and elsewhere. (with 16.1–2)
>
> A group of five wheels numbering six, eight, twelve, sixteen and twenty-four (spokes, respectively, is taught in the scripture called Śrīpūrva. (Wheels) with thirty-two and sixty-four (spokes) are (described) in the venerable Traisirasamata? The relationship between innumerable wheels is described in the venerable Siddhā and elsewhere. (with 16.2cd–4)
