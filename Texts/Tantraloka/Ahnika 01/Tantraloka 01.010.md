## Mūla

श्रीसोमानन्दबोधश्रीमदुत्पलविनिःसृताः ।
जयन्ति संविदामोदसन्दर्भा दिक्प्रसर्पिणः ॥ ^mula

śrīsomānandabodhaśrīmadutpalaviniḥsṛtāḥ |
jayanti saṃvidāmodasandarbhā dikprasarpiṇaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. I
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 01 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v01 03 - Chapter One, part 2#^pg84|p. 70]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 01.pdf#page=84>))
>
> (The collections of fragrances (delighting) consciousness that come forth from a beautiful blue lotus (utpala) by (its) blossoming with the joy of the (shining) moon which is beauty (itself), and spread in (all) directions, excel (all). In the same way,) the compositions (inspired by) the bliss of consciousness, that have come forth from the venerable (śrīmat) Utpala(deva) by virtue of (his) awakening by the illustrious Somānanda, and have spread in (all) directions, excel (all).

## Viveka
