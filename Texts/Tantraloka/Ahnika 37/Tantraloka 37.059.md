## Mūla

स तन्मयीभूय न लोकवर्तनी-
मजीगणत् कामपि केवलं पुनः ।
तदीयसंभोगविवृद्धये पुरा
करोति दास्यं गुरुवेश्मसु स्वयम् ॥ ^mula

sa tanmayībhūya na lokavartanī-
majīgaṇat kāmapi kevalaṃ punaḥ |
tadīyasaṃbhogavivṛddhaye purā
karoti dāsyaṃ guruveśmasu svayam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI

> Totally immersed in deity, he had no concern for anything in the world at all. AIl he did was to serve in the homes of (his numerous) teachers himself, in order to develop his (spiritual) experience.
