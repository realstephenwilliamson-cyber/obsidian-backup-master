# LXX Psalms 81

← [[LXX Psalms 80]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 82]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 082]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 082]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**81:1** A Psalm for Asaph. God stands in the assembly of gods; and in the midst *of them* will judge gods.  ^81-1

**81:2** How long will ye judge unrighteously, and accept the persons of sinners? Pause.  ^81-2

**81:3** Judge the orphan and poor: do justice to the low and needy.[^1]  ^81-3

**81:4** Rescue the needy, and deliver the poor out of the hand of the sinner.  ^81-4

**81:5** They know not, nor understand; they walk on in darkness: all the foundations of the earth shall be shaken.  ^81-5

**81:6** I have said, Ye are gods; and all *of you* children of the Most High.  ^81-6

**81:7** But ye die as men, and fall as one of the princes.  ^81-7

**81:8** Arise, O God, judge the earth: for thou shalt inherit all nations.  ^81-8

[^1]: 81:3 *Gr.* justify.
