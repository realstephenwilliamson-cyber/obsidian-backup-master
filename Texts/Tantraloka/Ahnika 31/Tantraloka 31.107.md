## Mūla

वेदी मध्ये प्रकर्तव्या उभयोश्च षडंगुलम् ।
द्वादशांगुलदीर्घा तु उभयोः पार्श्वयोस्तथा ॥ ^mula

vedī madhye prakartavyā ubhayośca ṣaḍaṃgulam |
dvādaśāṃguladīrghā tu ubhayoḥ pārśvayostathā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 05 - Chapter Thirty-One, part 2#^pg129|p. 111]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=129>))
>
> ‘That (group of three squares) consists of the tip of the trident, the middle of the trident, and the base of the trident. In the middle of it, one should fashion an enclosure (vedī), both sides of which (extend for) six finger-breadths, and is twelve finger-breadths long on both sides (that is, above and below, respectively). In the base (of the trident), one should fashion (another) enclosure, the shape of a half-moon, four finger-breadths high, and the same on both sides. (with 31.106cd–108)
