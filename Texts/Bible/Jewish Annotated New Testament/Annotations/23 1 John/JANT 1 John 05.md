# 1 John 5 (JANT notes)

[[JANT 1 John 04|← 1 John 4]] · [[Jewish Annotated New Testament|JANT index]] · —

Text: [[NRSVue 1 John 05|NRSVue]] · [[1 John 05|BSB and SBLGNT]] · Introduction: [[JANT 1 John - Introduction]]

**[[NRSVue 1 John 05#^5-1|5.1]]:** *Born of God*, remade in the image of God ([[Torah 01 - Bereshit#^1-26|Gen 1.26–27]]; [[John 01#^1-12|Jn 1.12–13]]: “born, not of blood or of the will of the flesh or of the will of man, but of God”). ^5-1

**[[NRSVue 1 John 05#^5-2|2–3]]:** *We . . . obey his commandments*, see [[John 14#^14-15|Jn 14.15–17]]. [[Torah 45 - Vaetchanan#^6-4|Deut. 6.4–9]] likewise combines the exhortation to love God by obeying his commandments. ^5-2

**[[NRSVue 1 John 05#^5-4|4–5]]:** *Conquers*, overcomes, is no longer subject to the power of (the world). The rabbis understood that human beings engaged in a constant internal struggle between following their “evil inclination” (Heb “yezer ha-ra”) or their “good inclination” (Heb “yezer ha-tov”). Righteous behavior and following the Law were keys to “conquering” (Heb “kovesh”) the evil inclination (e.g., “Raba said: ‘Though God created the *yezer ha-ra,* he created the Law as an antidote against it’” \[*b. B. Bat.* 16a; also [[Kiddushin 30#30b|*b. Qidd.* 30b]] and [[Avodah Zarah 17#17a|*b. Avod. Zar.* 17a]]\]) ^5-4

**[[NRSVue 1 John 05#^5-6|6–8]]:** *Water . . . blood . . . Spirit*, probably another connection to John’s Gospel, which refers (19.34) to a spear being thrust into Jesus’ side, from which blood and water emerge. *Water* is baptism, in the sense of cleansing from impurity (see, e.g., [[Torah 28 - Metzora#^14-9|Lev 14.9]]); *blood* is sacrifice (e.g., Lev 4); the *Spirit* is the true witness ([[John 16#^16-13|Jn 16.13]]). Both water (e.g., [[Torah 28 - Metzora#^14-8|Lev 14. 8–9]]) and blood (e.g., [[Torah 24 - Vayikra#^4-5|Lev 4.5–7]]) act as agents of purification in the Tanakh. ^5-6

### 5.13–21: Sin and eternal life

*[[NRSVue 1 John 05#^5-13|Text]], p. 526* ^5-13

**[[NRSVue 1 John 05#^5-16|16–17]]:** *Sin that is mortal*, the precise meaning is not clear, but possibly *mortal* refers to sin that prevents one from attaining eternal life (see 2.25n.). Not loving brothers and sisters seems to be the sin referred to here (see 3.14–15). ^5-16

**[[NRSVue 1 John 05#^5-18|18]]:** *Those who are born of God do not sin*, perhaps a reference to [[John 03#^3-2|Jn 3.2–10]] and 3.31–26; see 1.8. ^5-18

**[[NRSVue 1 John 05#^5-19|19]]:** That moral corruption would increase in the time before God’s intervention was an expectation reflected in [[Daniel 08#^8-9|Dan 8.9–11]]; the author of 1 John understands human lawlessness to be the manifestation of the devil’s power. ^5-19

**[[NRSVue 1 John 05#^5-20|20–21]]:** *Him who is true*, the “true” and only God, in contrast to *keep yourselves from idols*. This abrupt conclusion suggests that the author understands readers as having Gentile backgrounds. 7*There are three that testify in heaven, the Father, the Word, and the Holy Spirit, and these three are one. 8And there are three that testify on earth:* b Other ancient authorities read *in the Son* c Gk *your brother* d Gk *he* e Other ancient authorities read *know the true God* f Other ancient authorities add *Amen* ^5-20
