## Qualifications of bishops

**3:1** The saying is sure:^[Some interpreters place these words at the end of the previous paragraph. Other ancient authorities read *The saying is commonly accepted*] whoever aspires to the office of bishop^[Or *overseer*] desires a noble task. ^3-1

**3:2** Now a bishop^[Or *an overseer*] must be above reproach, married only once,^[Gk *the husband of one wife*] temperate, self-controlled, respectable, hospitable, an apt teacher, ^3-2

**3:3** not a drunkard, not violent but gentle, not quarrelsome, and not a lover of money. ^3-3

**3:4** He must manage his own household well, keeping his children submissive and respectful in every way, ^3-4

**3:5** for if someone does not know how to manage his own household, how can he take care of God’s church? ^3-5

**3:6** He must not be a recent convert,^[Gk *neophyte*] or he may be puffed up with conceit and fall into the condemnation of the devil. ^3-6

**3:7** Moreover, he must be well thought of by outsiders, so that he may not fall into disgrace and the snare of the devil. ^3-7

## Qualifications of deacons

**3:8** Deacons likewise must be serious, not double-tongued, not indulging in much wine, not greedy for money; ^3-8

**3:9** they must hold fast to the mystery of the faith with a clear conscience. ^3-9

**3:10** And let them first be tested; then, if they prove themselves blameless, let them serve as deacons. ^3-10

**3:11** Women^[Or *Women deacons* or *Wives*] likewise must be serious, not slanderers, but temperate, faithful in all things. ^3-11

**3:12** Let deacons be married only once,^[Gk *be husbands of one wife*] and let them manage their children and their households well; ^3-12

**3:13** for those who serve well as deacons gain a good standing for themselves and great boldness in the faith that is in Christ Jesus. ^3-13

## The mystery of our religion

**3:14** I hope to come to you soon, but I am writing these instructions to you so that, ^3-14

**3:15** if I am delayed, you may know how one ought to behave in the household of God, which is the church of the living God, the pillar and support of the truth. ^3-15

**3:16** Without any doubt, the mystery of godliness is great: He^[Gk *Who*; other ancient authorities read *God* or *Which*] was revealed in flesh, vindicated^[Or *justified*] in spirit,^[Or *by the Spirit*] seen by angels, proclaimed among gentiles, believed in throughout the world, taken up in glory. ^3-16
