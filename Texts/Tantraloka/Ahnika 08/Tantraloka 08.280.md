## Mūla

आध्यात्मिकाश्चतस्रः प्रकृत्युपादानकालभाग्याख्याः ।
पञ्च विषयोपरमतोऽर्जनरक्षासङ्गसंक्षयविघातैः ॥ ^mula

ādhyātmikāścatasraḥ prakṛtyupādānakālabhāgyākhyāḥ |
pañca viṣayoparamato'rjanarakṣāsaṅgasaṃkṣayavighātaiḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 08 - Chapter Eight, part 6#^pg301|p. 283]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=301>))
> Dyczkowski's numbering differs here; his markers: (280b), (280cd-281ab) (279d-280).
>
> ‘(Within the sphere of objectivity), four (realities) are internal (ādhyātmika), namely, 1A) Nature (prakṛti),), 2A) the material cause (upādāna), 3A) time, and 4A) fate (bhāgya). Five (are the forms of external false contentment) brought about by abstaining from sense objects (which cause suffering by the activity of the senses engaged in) 1B) the acquisition (of sense objects and the pleasure they give) (arjana), 2B) by protecting (them) (rakṣā), 3B) attachment (to them) (saṁga), 4B) (their) destruction (sarikṣaya) and 5B) by the violence (vighāta) (to others they entail). (with 8.279d–280)
>
> The followers of Kapila have said as much (in the following words). (with 8.280b)
