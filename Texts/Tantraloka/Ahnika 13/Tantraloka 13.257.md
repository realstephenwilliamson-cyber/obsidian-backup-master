## Mūla

नास्ति व्यापार इत्येवं निरपेक्षः स सर्वतः ।
तेन मायान्तराले ये रुद्रा ये च तदूर्ध्वतः ॥ ^mula

nāsti vyāpāra ityevaṃ nirapekṣaḥ sa sarvataḥ |
tena māyāntarāle ye rudrā ye ca tadūrdhvataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 09 - Chapter Thirteen, part 5#^pg306|p. 284]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=306>))
>
> In neither case (of these two types) is (their) operation (vyāpāra) that of Karma and the like, (that operate) within Māyā. Thus, (the descent of the power of grace) is independent in every way (of any factors apart from Śiva’s will). (with 13.256cd–257ab)
>
> Thus, the Rudras who reside within Māyā and those who are above them identify vigorously (haṭhāt) with Bhairava when the post assigned to them comes to an end. Indeed, those who are not overcome by Māyā (and so) are free of Karma and the rest, attain this or that accomplishment (siddhi) by the descent of (Śiva’s) power (of grace). (with 13.257cd–259ab)
