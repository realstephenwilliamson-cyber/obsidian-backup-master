## Chapter 1

**1:1** There was a man in the land of Uz named Job. That man was blameless and upright; he feared God and shunned evil. ^1-1

**1:2** Seven sons and three daughters were born to him; ^1-2

**1:3** his possessions were seven thousand sheep, three thousand camels, five hundred yoke of oxen and five hundred she-asses, and a very large household. That man was wealthier than anyone in the East. ^1-3

**1:4** It was the custom of his sons to hold feasts, each on his set day in his own home. They would invite their three sisters to eat and drink with them. ^1-4

**1:5** When a round of feast days was over, Job would send word to them to sanctify themselves, and, rising early in the morning, he would make burnt offerings, one for each of them; for Job thought, “Perhaps my children have sinned and blasphemed God in their thoughts.” This is what Job always used to do. ^1-5

**1:6** One day the divine beings presented themselves before the LORD, and the Adversary[^1] came along with them. ^1-6

**1:7** The LORD said to the Adversary, “Where have you been?” The Adversary answered the LORD, “I have been roaming all over the earth.” ^1-7

**1:8** The LORD said to the Adversary, “Have you noticed My servant Job? There is no one like him on earth, a blameless and upright man who fears God and shuns evil!” ^1-8

**1:9** The Adversary answered the LORD, “Does Job not have good reason to fear God? ^1-9

**1:10** Why, it is You who have fenced him round, him and his household and all that he has. You have blessed his efforts so that his possessions spread out in the land. ^1-10

**1:11** But lay Your hand upon all that he has and he will surely blaspheme You to Your face.” ^1-11

**1:12** The LORD replied to the Adversary, “See, all that he has is in your power; only do not lay a hand on him.” The Adversary departed from the presence of the LORD. ^1-12

**1:13** One day, as his sons and daughters were eating and drinking wine in the house of their eldest brother, ^1-13

**1:14** a messenger came to Job and said, “The oxen were plowing and the she-asses were grazing alongside them ^1-14

**1:15** when Sabeans attacked them and carried them off, and put the boys to the sword; I alone have escaped to tell you.” ^1-15

**1:16** This one was still speaking when another came and said, “God’s fire fell from heaven, took hold of the sheep and the boys, and burned them up; I alone have escaped to tell you.” ^1-16

**1:17** This one was still speaking when another came and said, “A Chaldean formation of three columns made a raid on the camels and carried them off and put the boys to the sword; I alone have escaped to tell you.” ^1-17

**1:18** This one was still speaking when another came and said, “Your sons and daughters were eating and drinking wine in the house of their eldest brother ^1-18

**1:19** when suddenly a mighty wind came from the wilderness. It struck the four corners of the house so that it collapsed upon the young people and they died; I alone have escaped to tell you.” ^1-19

**1:20** Then Job arose, tore his robe, cut off his hair, and threw himself on the ground and worshiped. ^1-20

**1:21** He said, “Naked came I out of my mother’s womb, and naked shall I return there; the LORD has given, and the LORD has taken away; blessed be the name of the LORD.” ^1-21

**1:22** For all that, Job did not sin nor did he cast reproach on God. ^1-22

[^1]: Heb. ha-satan.
