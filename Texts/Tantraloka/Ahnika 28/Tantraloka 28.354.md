## Mūla

भासतेऽपि परे लोके स्वप्नवद्वासनाक्रमात् ।
ननु मात्रन्तरैर्बन्धुपुत्राद्यैस्तत्तथा न किम् ॥ ^mula

bhāsate'pi pare loke svapnavadvāsanākramāt |
nanu mātrantarairbandhuputrādyaistattathā na kim || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 06 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 4#^pg173|p. 156]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=173>))
>
> That kind (of body) manifests in another world, together with (others who are) similar to his relatives, sons, friends and so on, as (happens) in a dream, due to the sequence of latent impressions (of his past Karma). (with 28.353cd–354ab)
>
> Surely (one may ask), why is that not known in that way (as it is to the dreamer) by other perceivers, such as (his) relatives and sons (themselves)? (with 28.354cd–355a)
