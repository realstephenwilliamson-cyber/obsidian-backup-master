## Mūla

एषां न मन्त्रो न ध्यानं न पूजा नापि कल्पना ।
न समय्यादिकाचार्य पर्यन्तः कोऽपि विभ्रमः ॥ ^mula

eṣāṃ na mantro na dhyānaṃ na pūjā nāpi kalpanā |
na samayyādikācārya paryantaḥ ko'pi vibhramaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. II
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 02 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v02 03 - Chapter Two, part 2#^pg61|p. 45]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 02.pdf#page=61>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 2.36cd–37ab.
>
> All that remains of their thoughts of (the various) kinds (aṁśa) of pleasure and pain, doubt and fear, is just (their) supreme absorption (parāveśa) (in the pure Light of consciousness) free of thought. For them there is no Mantra, no meditation, no worship, imagination (kalpanā) nor any wandering (vibhrama) (through the grades of initiation,) starting from that of a common initiate (samayin) to that of a teacher. (with 2.36–37)
