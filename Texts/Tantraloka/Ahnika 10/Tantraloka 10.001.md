## Mūla

उच्यते त्रिकशास्त्रैकरहस्यं तत्त्वभेदनम् ॥ ^mula

ucyate trikaśāstraikarahasyaṃ tattvabhedanam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 09 - Chapter Nine, part 7#^pg303|pp. 284–286]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=303>))
>
> The Division of the Principles, which is a (sacred) secret found only in the (Mālinīvijaąyottara, which is the acme of) Trika scriptures, will (now be) taught. (with 10.1cd)

## Viveka

> इदानीमेषामेव तत्त्वानां द्वितीयार्धेन पाञ्चदश्यादिभेदमभिधातुं प्रतिजानीते उच्यते त्रिकशास्त्रैकरहस्यं तत्त्वभेदनम् ॥
