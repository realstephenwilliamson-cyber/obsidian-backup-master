## Mūla

तथा पीठस्थितोऽप्येति ज्ञानयोगादिपात्रताम् ।
मुख्यत्वेन शरीरेऽन्तः प्राणे संविदि पश्यतः ॥ ^mula

tathā pīṭhasthito'pyeti jñānayogādipātratām |
mukhyatvena śarīre'ntaḥ prāṇe saṃvidi paśyataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 03 - Chapter Fifteen, part 2#^pg86|p. 68]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=86>))
>
> Again, by keeping the company of these (impious people), even one who is not of (their) same nature becomes a sinner. Similarly, he who is in a sacred seat (114), (keeping the company of those who are there,) becomes the receptacle of knowledge, Yoga and the rest. (with 15.99cd–100ab)
>
> (For one who,) seeing that all these things are primarily within the body, the vital breath and consciousness, of what use are these many other outer pilgrimages? (with 15.100cd–101ab)
