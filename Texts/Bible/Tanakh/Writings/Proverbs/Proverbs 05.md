# Proverbs 05

## Chapter 5

**5:1** My son, listen to my wisdom;  
Incline your ear to my insight, ^5-1

**5:2** That you may have foresight,  
While your lips hold fast to knowledge. ^5-2

**5:3** For the lips of a forbidden[^1] woman drip honey;  
Her mouth is smoother than oil; ^5-3

**5:4** But in the end she is as bitter as wormwood,  
Sharp as a two-edged sword. ^5-4

**5:5** Her feet go down to Death;  
Her steps take hold of Sheol. ^5-5

**5:6** She does not chart a path of life;  
Her course meanders for lack of knowledge. ^5-6

**5:7** So now, sons, pay heed to me,  
And do not swerve from the words of my mouth. ^5-7

**5:8** Keep yourself far away from her;  
Do not come near the doorway of her house ^5-8

**5:9** Lest you give up your vigor to others,  
Your years to a ruthless one; ^5-9

**5:10** Lest strangers eat their fill of your strength,  
And your toil be for the house of another; ^5-10

**5:11** And in the end you roar,  
When your flesh and body are consumed, ^5-11

**5:12** And say,  
“O how I hated discipline,  
And heartily spurned rebuke. ^5-12

**5:13** I did not pay heed to my teachers,  
Or incline my ear to my instructors. ^5-13

**5:14** Soon I was in dire trouble  
Amidst the assembled congregation.” ^5-14

**5:15** Drink water from your own cistern,  
Running water from your own well. ^5-15

**5:16** Your springs will gush forth  
In streams in the public squares. ^5-16

**5:17** They will be yours alone,  
Others having no part with you. ^5-17

**5:18** Let your fountain be blessed;  
Find joy in the wife of your youth— ^5-18

**5:19** A loving doe, a graceful mountain goat.  
Let her breasts satisfy you at all times;  
Be infatuated with love of her always. ^5-19

**5:20** Why be infatuated, my son, with a forbidden[^2] woman?  
Why clasp the bosom of an alien woman? ^5-20

**5:21** For a man’s ways are before the eyes of God;  
He surveys his entire course. ^5-21

**5:22** The wicked man will be trapped in his iniquities;  
He will be caught up in the ropes of his sin. ^5-22

**5:23** He will die for lack of discipline,  
Infatuated by his great folly. ^5-23

[^1]: Lit. “strange.”
[^2]: Lit. “strange.”
