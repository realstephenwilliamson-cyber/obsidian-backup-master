## Mūla

मूलं सहस्रं साष्टोक्तं त्रिशक्तौ ब्रह्मवक्त्रकम् ।
अर्चिषां तु शतं साष्टं ततः पूर्णाहुतिं क्षिपेत् ॥ ^mula

mūlaṃ sahasraṃ sāṣṭoktaṃ triśaktau brahmavaktrakam |
arciṣāṃ tu śataṃ sāṣṭaṃ tataḥ pūrṇāhutiṃ kṣipet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 04 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 2#^pg97|p. 80]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=97>))
>
> The root mantra is recited one thousand and eight times. Brahmā’s face (is placed) in the three energies, and the flames are one hundred and eight. After that, one should cast a full oblation (into the fire).
