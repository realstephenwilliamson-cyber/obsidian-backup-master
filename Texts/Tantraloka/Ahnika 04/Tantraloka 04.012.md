## Mūla

तथाभासनमेवास्य द्वैतमुक्तं महेशितुः ।
तद्द्वयापासनेनायं परामर्शोऽभिधीयते ॥ ^mula

tathābhāsanamevāsya dvaitamuktaṃ maheśituḥ |
taddvayāpāsanenāyaṃ parāmarśo'bhidhīyate || ^iast

## Translation

> Duality is said to be the manifestation of this Great Lord in this way. The removal of that duality is said to be this reflective awareness (that this is so).
