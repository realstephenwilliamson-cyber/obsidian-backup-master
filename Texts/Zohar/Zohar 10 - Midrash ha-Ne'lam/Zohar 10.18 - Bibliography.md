# [p. 599] *Bibliography*

This bibliography includes works cited and utilized by the translator for this volume, except for standard rabbinic texts and most reference works. Readers seeking further resources on the *Zohar* can consult the bibliography in volume 1 of the *Zohar: Pritzker Edition*; *The Library of Gershom Scholem on Jewish Mysticism: Catalogue*, edited by Joseph Dan, Esther Liebes, and Shmuel Reem; and Don Karr, “Notes on the *Zohar* in English.”
