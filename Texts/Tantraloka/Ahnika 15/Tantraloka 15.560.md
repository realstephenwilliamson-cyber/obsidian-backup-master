## Mūla

उपदेशाय न दोषा हृदयं चेन्न विद्विषेत् ।
विजातीयविकल्पांशोत्पुंसनाय यतेत च ॥ ^mula

upadeśāya na doṣā hṛdayaṃ cenna vidviṣet |
vijātīyavikalpāṃśotpuṃsanāya yateta ca || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 10 - Chapter Fifteen, part 9#^pg348|p. 330]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=348>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 15.562cd–563ab.
>
> One should never be revolted by the hero’s sacrificial substances, such as wine. Nor should one criticise those who delight in them; (rather,) one should always praise them. There are no defects in teaching (others about them) if one is not (secretly) averse to them in (his) heart. One should exert oneself to abandon (all) contrary notions (vijātīyavikalpa) (about them). (with 15.559–560)
