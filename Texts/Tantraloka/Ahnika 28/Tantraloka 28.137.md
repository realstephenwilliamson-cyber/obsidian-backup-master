## Mūla

ग्रन्थयस्तत्त्वसंख्याताः षडध्वकलनावशात् ।
यद्वा व्याससमासाभ्यां चित्राः सद्गन्धपूरिताः ॥ ^mula

granthayastattvasaṃkhyātāḥ ṣaḍadhvakalanāvaśāt |
yadvā vyāsasamāsābhyāṃ citrāḥ sadgandhapūritāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 04 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 2#^pg87|p. 70]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=87>))
>
> Thus, the thread made of eighteen strands is inferior; that of one hundred and eight is medium, and (the one made by multiplying this) by three (again) is considered to be the best. The number of knots, variously coloured (citra) and saturated with good perfume, may correspond to that of the principles (tattva), or else (the number may be arrived at by calculating the number) in accord with (constituent elements of) the six paths, in an abbreviated or detailed way. (with 28.136–137)
