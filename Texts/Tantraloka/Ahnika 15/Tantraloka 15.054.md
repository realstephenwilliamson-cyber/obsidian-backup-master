## Mūla

कवक्त्रहृद्गुह्यपदे पञ्चाङ्गैर्भस्म मन्त्रितम् ।
भस्ममुष्टिं साङ्गमूलजप्तां मूर्ध्नि क्षिपेत्ततः ॥ ^mula

kavaktrahṛdguhyapade pañcāṅgairbhasma mantritam |
bhasmamuṣṭiṃ sāṅgamūlajaptāṃ mūrdhni kṣipettataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 03 - Chapter Fifteen, part 2#^pg62|p. 44]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=62>))
>
> After he has repeated the (Mantra of the) Weapon (Phat) (seven times) over the ash of a (sacrificial) fire, the impurity (mala) within the head, mouth, heart, genitals and feet is destroyed. The ash is consecrated (mantrita) with the Five Limbs. Then one should throw a handful of the ash, consecrated with the root Mantra’ and the limbs, onto the head. After one has sprinkled water on the hands and feet, one should sip it (ācamana) etc. This is followed by libation (tarpaṇa) and the repetition of Mantra (japa). Such is the radiantly powerful (faijasa) ablution with ashes. (with 15.53cd–55)
