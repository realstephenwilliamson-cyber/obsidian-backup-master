## Mūla

पित्र्यं वर्षं दिव्यदिनमूचुर्भ्रान्ता हि ते मुधा ।
दिव्यार्काब्दसहस्राणि युगेषु चतुरादितः ॥ ^mula

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 11 - Chapter Six, part 4#^pg377|p. 362]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=377>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 6.138cd–139ab.
>
> Thus, those foolish teachers who, on the basis of the two statements made in the venerable Svacchanda and elsewhere, say that a year of the ancestors is a day of the gods, are mistaken. (with 6.137cd–138ab)
>
> The (four) Ages (yuga) (last) twelve thousand divine (years,) divided into four periods (of time) that decrease progressively by one (thousand divine years,) starting from four (to three, two, and one). There are (also) eight junctions in (between) them of as many hundreds (of years). (Multiplying) the four Ages by seventy-one, (we arrive at the length of) a ‘reign of a Manu (manvantara)’, fourteen of which make a day of Brahmā and the Indras that, coming successively (in this period of time), are fourteen. (with 6.138cd–140ab)
