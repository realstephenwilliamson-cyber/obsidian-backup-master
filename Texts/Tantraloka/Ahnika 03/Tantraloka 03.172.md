## Mūla

क्रियाशक्त्यात्मकं विश्वमयं तस्मात्स्फुरेद्यतः ।
इच्छा कामो विषं ज्ञानं क्रिया देवी निरञ्जनम् ॥ ^mula

kriyāśaktyātmakaṃ viśvamayaṃ tasmātsphuredyataḥ |
icchā kāmo viṣaṃ jñānaṃ kriyā devī nirañjanam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. II
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 02 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v02 09 - Chapter Three, part 6#^pg311|p. 295]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 02.pdf#page=311>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 3.172cd–173ab.
>
> The Stainless (nirañjana) is the Supreme Abode, while its principle (is the powerholder) who is ‘stained’ (sāñjana) (that is, made manifest). This is because it is from that that the cosmic nature (viśvamaya), which is the power of action, manifests. (with 3.171cd–172ab)
>
> Passion is the will, Poison is knowledge, and the goddess of action is the Stainless. Penetration (samāveśa) into this triad is said to be Śiva (the transcendent) and Bhairava (the immanent). (with 3.172cd–173ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* ननु कामादेस्तत्त्वत्रयस्य इच्छादिशक्तित्रयरूपत्वमस्ति, तत्कथमेकस्यैव अमृतबीजस्य एतद्रूपत्वमुक्तम् ? -- इत्याशङ्क्य विभागावेदनपूर्वमेषामविभागमेव द्रढयति --

> इच्छा कामो विषं ज्ञानं क्रिया देवी निरञ्जनम् ॥
> एतत्त्रयसमावेशः शिवो भैरव उच्यते ।

भैरव इति -- विश्वमयत्वेन पूर्णत्वात् । अत एव "तदेव ब्रह्म परमम्"इत्याद्युक्तम् ॥ १७२ ॥
