# Genesis 35 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Genesis 34|← Genesis 34]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Genesis 36|Genesis 36 →]]

## Jacob returns to Bethel

**35:1** God said to Jacob, “Arise, go up to Bethel, and settle there. Make an altar there to the God who appeared to you when you fled from your brother Esau.” ^35-1

**35:2** So Jacob said to his household and to all who were with him, “Put away the foreign gods that are among you, and purify yourselves, and change your clothes; ^35-2

**35:3** then come, let us go up to Bethel, that I may make an altar there to the God who answered me in the day of my distress and has been with me wherever I have gone.” ^35-3

**35:4** So they gave to Jacob all the foreign gods that they had and the rings that were in their ears, and Jacob hid them under the oak that was near Shechem. ^35-4

**35:5** As they journeyed, a terror from God fell upon the cities all around them, so that no one pursued them. ^35-5

**35:6** Jacob came to Luz, that is, Bethel, which is in the land of Canaan, he and all the people who were with him, ^35-6

**35:7** and there he built an altar and called the place El-bethel,^[That is, *God of Bethel*] because it was there that God had revealed himself to him when he fled from his brother. ^35-7

**35:8** And Deborah, Rebekah’s nurse, died, and she was buried under an oak below Bethel. So it was called Allon-bacuth.^[That is, *oak of weeping*] ^35-8

**35:9** God appeared to Jacob again when he came from Paddan-aram, and he blessed him. ^35-9

**35:10** God said to him, “Your name is Jacob; no longer shall you be called Jacob, but Israel shall be your name.” So he was called Israel. ^35-10

**35:11** God said to him, “I am God Almighty:^[Traditional rendering of Heb *El Shaddai*] be fruitful and multiply; a nation and a company of nations shall come from you, and kings shall spring from you. ^35-11

**35:12** The land that I gave to Abraham and Isaac I will give to you, and I will give the land to your offspring after you.” ^35-12

**35:13** Then God went up from him at the place where he had spoken with him. ^35-13

**35:14** Jacob set up a pillar in the place where he had spoken with him, a pillar of stone, and he poured out a drink offering on it and poured oil on it. ^35-14

**35:15** So Jacob called the place where God had spoken with him Bethel. ^35-15

## The birth of Benjamin and the death of Rachel

**35:16** Then they journeyed from Bethel, and when they were still some distance from Ephrath, Rachel was in childbirth, and she had hard labor. ^35-16

**35:17** When she was in her hard labor, the midwife said to her, “Do not be afraid, for now you have another son.” ^35-17

**35:18** As her soul was departing, for she was dying, she named him Ben-oni,^[That is, *son of my sorrow*] but his father called him Benjamin.^[That is, *son of the right hand* or *son of the south*] ^35-18

**35:19** And Rachel died, and she was buried on the way to Ephrath, that is, Bethlehem, ^35-19

**35:20** and Jacob set up a pillar at her grave; it is the pillar of Rachel’s tomb, which is there to this day. ^35-20

**35:21** Israel journeyed on and pitched his tent beyond the tower of Eder. ^35-21

**35:22** While Israel lived in that land, Reuben went and lay with Bilhah his father’s concubine, and Israel heard of it. Now the sons of Jacob were twelve. ^35-22

**35:23** The sons of Leah: Reuben (Jacob’s firstborn), Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, and Zebulun. ^35-23

**35:24** The sons of Rachel: Joseph and Benjamin. ^35-24

**35:25** The sons of Bilhah, Rachel’s maid: Dan and Naphtali. ^35-25

**35:26** The sons of Zilpah, Leah’s maid: Gad and Asher. These were the sons of Jacob who were born to him in Paddan-aram. ^35-26

## The death of Isaac

**35:27** Jacob came to his father Isaac at Mamre, or Kiriath-arba, that is, Hebron, where Abraham and Isaac had resided as aliens. ^35-27

**35:28** Now the days of Isaac were one hundred eighty years. ^35-28

**35:29** And Isaac breathed his last; he died and was gathered to his people, old and full of days, and his sons Esau and Jacob buried him. ^35-29
