# Job 6 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Job 05|← Job 5]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Job 07|Job 7 →]]

## Job replies: My complaint is just

**6:1** Then Job answered: ^6-1

**6:2** “O that my vexation were weighed and all my calamity laid in the balances! ^6-2

**6:3** For then it would be heavier than the sand of the sea; therefore my words have been rash. ^6-3

**6:4** For the arrows of the Almighty^[Traditional rendering of Heb *Shaddai*] are in me; my spirit drinks their poison; the terrors of God are arrayed against me. ^6-4

**6:5** Does the wild ass bray over its grass or the ox low over its fodder? ^6-5

**6:6** Can that which is tasteless be eaten without salt, or is there any flavor in the juice of mallows?^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^6-6

**6:7** My appetite refuses to touch them; they are like food that is loathsome to me.^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^6-7

**6:8** “O that I might have my request and that God would grant my desire, ^6-8

**6:9** that it would please God to crush me, that he would let loose his hand and cut me off! ^6-9

**6:10** This would be my consolation; I would even exult^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] in unrelenting pain, for I have not denied the words of the Holy One. ^6-10

**6:11** What is my strength, that I should wait? And what is my end, that I should be patient? ^6-11

**6:12** Is my strength the strength of stones, or is my flesh bronze? ^6-12

**6:13** In truth I have no help in me, and any resource is driven from me. ^6-13

**6:14** “Those who withhold^[Syr Vg Compare Tg: Meaning of Heb uncertain] kindness from a friend forsake the fear of the Almighty.^[Traditional rendering of Heb *Shaddai*] ^6-14

**6:15** My companions are treacherous like a torrent bed, like swollen streams that pass away, ^6-15

**6:16** that run dark with ice, turbid with melting snow. ^6-16

**6:17** In time of heat they disappear; when it is hot, they vanish from their place. ^6-17

**6:18** The caravans turn aside from their course; they go up into the waste and perish. ^6-18

**6:19** The caravans of Tema look; the travelers of Sheba hope. ^6-19

**6:20** They are disappointed because they were confident; they come there and are confounded. ^6-20

**6:21** Such you have now become to me;^[Cn Compare Gk Syr: Meaning of Heb uncertain] you see my calamity and are afraid. ^6-21

**6:22** Have I said, ‘Make me a gift’? Or, ‘From your wealth offer a bribe for me’? ^6-22

**6:23** Or, ‘Save me from an opponent’s hand’? Or, ‘Ransom me from the hand of oppressors’? ^6-23

**6:24** “Teach me, and I will be silent; make me understand how I have gone wrong. ^6-24

**6:25** How forceful are honest words! But your reproof, what does it reprove? ^6-25

**6:26** Do you think that you can reprove words, as if the speech of the desperate were wind? ^6-26

**6:27** You would even cast lots over the orphan and bargain over your friend. ^6-27

**6:28** “But now, be pleased to look at me, for I will not lie to your face. ^6-28

**6:29** Turn, I pray; let no wrong be done. Turn now; my vindication is at stake. ^6-29

**6:30** Is there any wrong on my tongue? Cannot my taste discern calamity? ^6-30
