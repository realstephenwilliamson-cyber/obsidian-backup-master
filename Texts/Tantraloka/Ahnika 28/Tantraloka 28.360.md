## Mūla

तद्वन्मात्रन्तरेऽप्येषा संविन्निष्ठा न तद्गता ।
तेन स्थितमिदं यद्यद्भाव्यते तत्तदेव हि ॥ ^mula

tadvanmātrantare'pyeṣā saṃvinniṣṭhā na tadgatā |
tena sthitamidaṃ yadyadbhāvyate tattadeva hi || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 06 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 4#^pg177|p. 160]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=177>))
>
> (The knowledge of the) existence of (an object) such as a jar is grounded in consciousness, and not on that (jar itself as an object of knowledge). In the same way, (the knowledge of the existence) of (these) other perceivers is grounded in consciousness, and not on them (themselves as objects of knowledge). (with 28.359cd–360ab)
>
> Thus, this has been established, namely, that whatever (tendency) awakens (at the moment) when the body comes to an end, it corresponds to what one has pondered on (bhāvyate) (most deeply in life). If that were not so, (any latent impression of) any other kind would be awakened (randomly). (with 28.360cd–361ab)
