# Joel 2

← [[Joel 1]] · [[Tanakh]] · [[Joel 3]] →

Compare: [[NRSVue Joel 02|NRSVue]] (NRSVue 2:28–32 = Heb 3:1–5)

## Chapter 2

**2:1** Blow a horn in Zion,  
Sound an alarm on My holy mount!  
Let all dwellers on earth tremble,  
For the day of the LORD has come!  
It is close— ^2-1

**2:2** A day of darkness and gloom,  
A day of densest cloud  
Spread like soot over the hills.  
A vast, enormous horde—  
Nothing like it has ever happened,  
And it shall never happen again  
Through the years and ages. ^2-2

**2:3** Their vanguard is a consuming fire,  
Their rear guard a devouring flame.  
Before them the land was like the Garden of Eden,  
Behind them, a desolate waste:  
Nothing has escaped them. ^2-3

**2:4** They have the appearance of horses,  
They gallop just like steeds. ^2-4

**2:5** With a clatter as of chariots  
They bound on the hilltops,  
With a noise like a blazing fire  
Consuming straw;  
Like an enormous horde  
Arrayed for battle. ^2-5

**2:6** Peoples tremble before them,  
All faces turn ashen.[^1] ^2-6

**2:7** They rush like warriors,  
They scale a wall like fighters.  
And each keeps to his own track.  
Their paths never cross;[^2] ^2-7

**2:8** No one jostles another,  
Each keeps to his own course.  
And should they fall through a loophole,  
They do not get hurt.[^3] ^2-8

**2:9** They rush up the wall,  
They dash about in the city;  
They climb into the houses,  
They enter like thieves  
By way of the windows. ^2-9

**2:10** Before them earth trembles,  
Heaven shakes,  
Sun and moon are darkened,  
And stars withdraw their brightness. ^2-10

**2:11** And the LORD roars aloud  
At the head of His army;  
For vast indeed is His host,  
Numberless are those that do His bidding.  
For great is the day of the LORD,  
Most terrible—who can endure it? ^2-11

**2:12** “Yet even now”—says the LORD—  
“Turn back to Me with all your hearts,  
And with fasting, weeping, and lamenting.” ^2-12

**2:13** Rend your hearts  
Rather than your garments,  
And turn back to the LORD your God.  
For He is gracious and compassionate,  
Slow to anger, abounding in kindness,  
And renouncing punishment. ^2-13

**2:14** Who knows but He may turn and relent,  
And leave a blessing behind  
For meal offering and drink offering  
To the LORD your God?[^4] ^2-14

**2:15** Blow a horn in Zion,  
Solemnize a fast,  
Proclaim an assembly! ^2-15

**2:16** Gather the people,  
Bid the congregation purify themselves.[^5]  
Bring together the old,  
Gather the babes  
And the sucklings at the breast;  
Let the bridegroom come out of his chamber,  
The bride from her canopied couch. ^2-16

**2:17** Between the portico and the altar,  
Let the priests, the LORD’s ministers, weep  
And say:  
“Oh, spare Your people, LORD!  
Let not Your possession become a mockery,  
To be taunted by nations!  
Let not the peoples say,  
‘Where is their God?’” ^2-17

**2:18** Then the LORD was roused  
On behalf of His land  
And had compassion  
Upon His people. ^2-18

**2:19** In response to His people  
The LORD declared:  
“I will grant you the new grain,  
The new wine, and the new oil,  
And you shall have them in abundance.  
Nevermore will I let you be  
A mockery among the nations. ^2-19

**2:20** I will drive the northerner[^6] far from you,  
I will thrust it into a parched and desolate land—  
Its van to the Eastern Sea[^7]  
And its rear to the Western Sea;[^8]  
And the stench of it shall go up,  
And the foul smell rise.”  
For [the LORD] shall work great deeds. ^2-20

**2:21** Fear not, O soil, rejoice and be glad;  
For the LORD has wrought great deeds. ^2-21

**2:22** Fear not, O beasts of the field,  
For the pastures in the wilderness  
Are clothed with grass.  
The trees have borne their fruit;  
Fig tree and vine  
Have yielded their strength. ^2-22

**2:23** O children of Zion, be glad,  
Rejoice in the LORD your God.  
For He has given you the early rain in [His] kindness,  
Now He makes the rain fall [as] formerly—  
The early rain and the late— ^2-23

**2:24** And threshing floors shall be piled with grain,  
And vats shall overflow with new wine and oil. ^2-24

**2:25** “I will repay you for the years[^9]  
Consumed by swarms and hoppers,  
By grubs and locusts,  
The great army I let loose against you. ^2-25

**2:26** And you shall eat your fill  
And praise the name of the LORD your God  
Who dealt so wondrously with you—  
My people shall be shamed no more. ^2-26

**2:27** And you shall know  
That I am in the midst of Israel:  
That I the LORD am your God  
And there is no other.  
And My people shall be shamed no more.” ^2-27

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain; cf. Nah. 2.11.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^4]: When the locusts depart, there will again be yield enough for offerings; see 1.9.
[^5]: Cf. Exod. 19.10; Zeph. 1.7.
[^6]: I.e., the locusts. Emendation yields “My multitude”; cf. “nation” (1.6), “horde,” “army,” and “host” (2.2, 5, 11, and 25).
[^7]: The Dead Sea.
[^8]: The Mediterranean Sea.
[^9]: Emendation yields “double what was.”
