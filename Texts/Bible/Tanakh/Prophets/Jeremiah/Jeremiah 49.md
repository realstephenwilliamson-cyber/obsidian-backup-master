## Chapter 49

**49:1** Concerning the Ammonites.  
Thus said the LORD:  
Has Israel no sons,  
Has he no heir?  
Then why has Milcom[^1] dispossessed Gad,  
And why have his people settled in Gad’s[^2] towns? ^49-1

**49:2** Assuredly, days are coming  
—declares the LORD—  
When I will sound the alarm of war  
Against Rabbah of the Ammonites;  
It shall become a desolate mound,  
And its villages shall be set on fire.  
And Israel shall dispossess  
Those who dispossessed him  
—said the LORD. ^49-2

**49:3** Howl, O Heshbon, for Ai is ravaged!  
Cry out, O daughters of Rabbah!  
Gird on sackcloth, lament,  
And run to and fro in the sheepfolds.[^3]  
For Milcom shall go into exile,  
Together with his priests and attendants. ^49-3

**49:4** Why do you glory in strength,  
Your strength is drained,[^4]  
O rebellious daughter,  
You who relied on your treasures,  
[Who said:] Who dares attack me? ^49-4

**49:5** I am bringing terror upon you  
—declares the Lord GOD of Hosts—  
From all those around you.  
Every one of you shall be driven in every direction,[^5]  
And none shall gather in the fugitives. ^49-5

**49:6** But afterward I will restore the fortunes of the Ammonites—declares the LORD. ^49-6

**49:7** Concerning Edom.  
Thus said the LORD of Hosts:  
Is there no more wisdom in Teman?  
Has counsel vanished from the prudent?  
Has their wisdom gone stale? ^49-7

**49:8** Flee, turn away, sit down low,  
O inhabitants of Dedan,  
For I am bringing Esau’s doom upon him,  
The time when I deal with him. ^49-8

**49:9** If vintagers were to come upon you,  
Would they leave no gleanings?  
Even thieves in the night  
Would destroy only for their needs![^6] ^49-9

**49:10** But it is I who have bared Esau,  
Have exposed his place of concealment;  
He cannot hide.  
His offspring is ravaged,  
His kin and his neighbors—  
He is no more.[^7] ^49-10

**49:11** “Leave your orphans with me,  
I will rear them;  
Let your widows rely on me!” ^49-11

**49:12** For thus said the LORD: If they who rightly should not drink of the cup must drink it, are you the one to go unpunished? You shall not go unpunished: you will have to drink! ^49-12

**49:13** For by Myself I swear—declares the LORD—Bozrah shall become a desolation, a mockery, a ruin, and a curse;[^8] and all its towns shall be ruins for all time. ^49-13

**49:14** I have received tidings from the LORD,  
And an envoy is sent out among the nations:  
Assemble, and move against her,  
And rise up for war! ^49-14

**49:15** For I will make you least among nations,  
Most despised among men. ^49-15

**49:16** Your horrible nature,[^9]  
Your arrogant heart has seduced you,  
You who dwell in clefts of the rock,  
Who occupy the height of the hill!  
Should you nest as high as the eagle,  
From there I will pull you down  
—declares the LORD. ^49-16

**49:17** And Edom shall be a cause of appallment; whoever passes by will be appalled and will hiss[^10] at all its wounds. ^49-17

**49:18** It shall be like the overthrow of Sodom and Gomorrah and their neighbors—said the LORD: no man shall live there, no human shall sojourn there. ^49-18

**49:19** It shall be as when a lion comes up out of the jungle of the Jordan against a secure pasture: in a moment I can harry him out of it and appoint over it anyone I choose.[^11] Then who is like Me? Who can summon Me? Who is the shepherd that can stand up against Me? ^49-19

**49:20** Hear, then, the plan which the LORD has devised against Edom, and what He has purposed against the inhabitants of Teman:  
Surely the shepherd boys  
Shall drag them away;  
Surely the pasture shall be  
Aghast because of them. ^49-20

**49:21** At the sound of their downfall  
The earth shall shake;  
The sound of screaming  
Shall be heard at the Sea of Reeds. ^49-21

**49:22** See, like an eagle he flies up,  
He soars and spreads his wings against Bozrah;  
And the heart of Edom’s warriors in that day  
Shall be like the heart of a woman in travail. ^49-22

**49:23** Concerning Damascus.  
Hamath and Arpad are shamed,  
For they have heard bad news.  
They shake with anxiety,  
Like[^12] the sea which cannot rest. ^49-23

**49:24** Damascus has grown weak,  
She has turned around to flee;  
Trembling has seized her,  
Pain and anguish have taken hold of her,  
Like a woman in childbirth. ^49-24

**49:25** How has the glorious city not been deserted,[^13]  
The citadel of my joy! ^49-25

**49:26** Assuredly, her young men shall lie fallen in her squares.  
And all her warriors shall be stilled in that day  
—declares the LORD of Hosts. ^49-26

**49:27** I will set fire to the wall of Damascus,  
And it shall consume the fortresses of Ben-hadad. ^49-27

**49:28** Concerning Kedar and the kingdoms of Hazor, which King Nebuchadrezzar of Babylon conquered.  
Thus said the LORD:  
Arise, march against Kedar,  
And ravage the Kedemites! ^49-28

**49:29** They will take away their tents and their flocks,  
Their tent cloths and all their gear;  
They shall carry off their camels,  
And shall proclaim against them:  
Terror all around! ^49-29

**49:30** Flee, wander far,  
Sit down low, O inhabitants of Hazor  
—says the LORD.  
For King Nebuchadrezzar of Babylon  
Has devised a plan against you  
And formed a purpose against you: ^49-30

**49:31** Rise up, attack a tranquil nation  
That dwells secure  
—says the LORD—  
That has no barred gates,  
That dwells alone. ^49-31

**49:32** Their camels shall become booty,  
And their abundant flocks a spoil;  
And I will scatter to every quarter  
Those who have their hair clipped;  
And from every direction I will bring  
Disaster upon them  
—says the LORD. ^49-32

**49:33** Hazor shall become a lair of jackals,  
A desolation for all time.  
No man shall live there,  
No human shall sojourn there. ^49-33

**49:34** The word of the LORD that came to the prophet Jeremiah concerning Elam, at the beginning of the reign of King Zedekiah of Judah: ^49-34

**49:35** Thus said the LORD of Hosts: I am going to break the bow of Elam, the mainstay of their strength. ^49-35

**49:36** And I shall bring four winds against Elam from the four quarters of heaven, and scatter them to all those winds. There shall not be a nation to which the fugitives from Elam do not come. ^49-36

**49:37** And I will break Elam before their enemies, before those who seek their lives; and I will bring disaster upon them, My flaming wrath—declares the LORD. And I will dispatch the sword after them until I have consumed them. ^49-37

**49:38** And I will set My throne in Elam,  
And wipe out from there king and officials  
—says the LORD. ^49-38

**49:39** But in the days to come I will restore the fortunes of Elam—declares the LORD. ^49-39

[^1]: The name of the Ammonite deity; vocalized Malcam here and in v. 3.
[^2]: Heb. “his.”
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain; for “strength” cf. Akkadian emuqu.
[^5]: Lit. “each man straight ahead.”
[^6]: Obad. 1.5 reads: “If thieves were to come to you, / Marauders by night, / They would steal no more than they needed. / If vintagers came to you, / They would surely leave some gleanings.”
[^7]: Some Septuagint mss. read “And there is none to say.”
[^8]: Cf. note at 24.9 and 42.18.
[^9]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^10]: Cf. note at 18.16.
[^11]: Emendation yields “he can harry them [i.e., the sheep] out of it; and what champion could one place in charge of them?”
[^12]: So a few mss. Most mss. and editions read “In.”
[^13]: Emendation yields “How has the glorious city been deserted”; so Vulgate.
