# Leviticus 24 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Leviticus 23|← Leviticus 23]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Leviticus 25|Leviticus 25 →]]

**24:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying: ^24-1

**24:2** Command the children of Israel, that they bring unto thee the finest and dearest oil of olives, to furnish the lamps continually, ^24-2

**24:3** Without the veil of the testimony in the tabernacle of the covenant. And Aaron shall set them from evening until morning before the Lord, by a perpetual service and rite in your generations. ^24-3

**24:4** They shall be set upon the most pure candlestick before the Lord continually. ^24-4

**24:5** Thou shalt take also fine hour, and shalt bake twelve leaves thereof, two tenths shall be in every loaf: ^24-5

**24:6** And thou shalt set them six and six one against another upon the most clean table before the Lord: ^24-6

**24:7** And thou shalt put upon them the dearest frankincense, that the bread may be for a memorial of the oblation of the Lord. ^24-7

**24:8** Every sabbath they shall be changed before the Lord, being received of the children of Israel by an everlasting covenant: ^24-8

**24:9** And they shall be Aaron’s and his sons’, that they may eat them in the holy place: because it is most holy of the sacrifices of the Lord by a perpetual right. ^24-9

**24:10** And behold there went out the son of a woman of Israel, whom she had of an Egyptian, among the children of Israel, and fell at words in the camp with a man of Israel. ^24-10

**24:11** And when he had blasphemed the name, and had cursed it, he was brought to Moses: (now his mother was called Salumith, the daughter of Dabri, of the tribe of Dan:) ^24-11

**24:12** And they put him into prison, till they might know what the Lord would command. ^24-12

**24:13** And the Lord spoke to Moses, ^24-13

**24:14** Saying: Bring forth the blasphemer without the camp, and let them that heard him, put their hands upon his head, and let all the people stone him. ^24-14

**24:15** And thou shalt speak to the children of Israel: the man that curseth his God, shall bear his sin: ^24-15

**24:16** And he that blasphemeth the name of the Lord, dying let him die: all the multitude shall stone him, whether he be a native or a stranger. He that blasphemeth the name of the Lord, dying let him die. ^24-16

**24:17** He that striketh and killeth a man, dying let him die. ^24-17

**24:18** He that killeth a beast, shall make it good, that is to say, shall give beast for beast. ^24-18

**24:19** He that giveth a blemish to any of his neighbours: as he hath done, so shall it be done to him: ^24-19

**24:20** Breach for breach, eye for eye, tooth for tooth, shall he restore. What blemish he gave, the like shall he be compelled to suffer. ^24-20

**24:21** He that striketh a beast, shall render another. He that striketh a man shall be punished. ^24-21

**24:22** Let there be equal judgment among you, whether he be a stranger, or a native that offends: because I am the Lord your God. ^24-22

**24:23** And Moses spoke to the children of Israel: and they brought forth him that had blasphemed, without the camp, and they stoned him. And the children of Israel did as the Lord had commanded Moses. ^24-23
