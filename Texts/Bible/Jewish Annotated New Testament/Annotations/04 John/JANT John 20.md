# John 20 (JANT notes)

Text: [[NRSVue John 20|NRSVue]] · [[John 20|BSB and SBLGNT]] · Introduction: [[JANT John - Introduction]]

### 20.1–31: Resurrection appearances

*[[NRSVue John 20#^20-1|Text]], p. 216* · (Mt 28; Mk 16; Lk 24). ^20-1

**[[NRSVue John 20#^20-1|1–18]]:** The Empty Tomb.  ^20-1-2

**[[NRSVue John 20#^20-1|1]]:** *First day*, Sunday. Mary waited until the Sabbath was over. *Mary Magdalene*, is named in Mt and Mk; Lk does not name the women at the tomb. In Jn, Mary Magdalene is alone. ^20-1-3

**[[NRSVue John 20#^20-2|2]]:** *We do not know*, the use of the first person plural implies that others were present, though the narrative indicates that Mary was alone. ^20-2

**[[NRSVue John 20#^20-3|3–8]]:** A race between the contenders for Jesus’ closest disciple. This passage may intimate the Johannine effort to elevate itself over churches that viewed Peter as the principal disciple. ^20-3

**[[NRSVue John 20#^20-8|8]]:** *Believed*, either the prophecy of Jesus’ resurrection or simply the testimony of Mary Magdalene. ^20-8

**[[NRSVue John 20#^20-9|9]]:** *Scripture . . . rise from the dead*, the Tanakh has no such specific quotation. This may be a general comment similar to [[1 Corinthians 15#^15-4|1 Cor 15.4]]: “He was raised on the third day in accordance with the scriptures.” ^20-9

**[[NRSVue John 20#^20-11|11]]:** The language echoes [[Song of Songs 2#^2-9|Song 2.9]]. The reference to searching for a loved one but failing to find him is parallel to [[Song of Songs 3#^3-1|Song 3.1–4]] and 5.2–8; Mary’s turning twice to face Jesus in 20.14,16 links the story to [[Song of Songs 2#^2-17|Song 2.17]]; [[Song of Songs 6#^6-5|6.5]] and especially, 6.14\[LXX 7.1\]. ^20-11

**[[NRSVue John 20#^20-14|14–16]]:** Mary, possibly through tears, recognizes Jesus neither by sight nor by his voice, but she does know him when he speaks her name; see 10.3. This may be a hint at his transformed appearance, or simply reflect the commonplace experience that it can be difficult to recognize a person whom we are not expecting to see. See also 6.20,21, and 21.5. ^20-14

**[[NRSVue John 20#^20-15|15]]:** *Woman*, signaling that Jesus is about to impart a revelation. *Whom are you looking for*, echoes 1.38. ^20-15

**[[NRSVue John 20#^20-17|17]]:** *Do not hold*, it is not clear whether Jesus is asking Mary to let go of him, or warning her not to touch him; see [[Song of Songs 3#^3-4|Song 3.4]]. *Ascended*, see 14.3; 16.10. Jesus leaves both to return to the Father and to prepare to receive his followers. ^20-17

### 20.18–29: Appearances to the disciples

*[[NRSVue John 20#^20-18|Text]], p. 217* · John emphasizes Jesus’ post-crucifixion corporeality. On the one hand, he can walk through walls, which suggests that his body does not have the substance that it had before his death, or that the process of resurrection is not yet complete. On the other hand, he invites Thomas to touch his wounds, implying that he has a physical body with the same properties as mortal humans do. ^20-18

**[[NRSVue John 20#^20-19|19]]:** *Fear of the Jews*, a recurrent theme (7.13; 19.38) relating to Jesus’ prediction that his followers will be persecuted just as he was (16.2–3). *Peace be with you*, traditional Jewish greeting (see Tob 12.47). ^20-19

**[[NRSVue John 20#^20-22|22]]:** *He breathed on them,* giving them new life (see 3.5; [[Torah 01 - Bereshit#^2-7|Gen 2.7]]). *Spirit*, alluding to the Paraclete (14.16,26; 15.26; 16.7; cf. Acts 2). ^20-22

**[[NRSVue John 20#^20-23|23]]:** *Forgive . . . retain*, Jesus delegates authority to the disciples; see also [[Matthew 16#^16-19|Mt 16.19]]; [[Matthew 18#^18-18|18.18]]. ^20-23

**[[NRSVue John 20#^20-24|24]]:** *Thomas*, also known as Doubting Thomas. Thomas’s other statements perhaps express resignation (11.16) or puzzlement (14.5). ^20-24

**[[NRSVue John 20#^20-28|28]]:** *Lord . . . God*, a full recognition of Jesus’ identity, as given at the beginning (1.1–14). A possible allusion to [[Psalms 035#^35-23|Ps 35.23]], “My God and my Lord.” ^20-28

### 20.30–31: Statement of purpose

*[[NRSVue John 20#^20-30|Text]], p. 217* ^20-30

**[[NRSVue John 20#^20-30|30]]:** *Other signs*, implying that John offers only a selection. *This book*, Gospel of John. ^20-30-2

**[[NRSVue John 20#^20-31|31]]:** *Come to believe*, or, continue to believe. ^20-31
