# Leviticus 22 (NRSVue)

## The use of holy offerings

**22:1** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ^22-1

**22:2** “Direct Aaron and his sons to deal carefully with the sacred donations of the Israelites, which they dedicate to me, so that they may not profane my holy name: I am the LORD. ^22-2

**22:3** Say to them, ‘If anyone among all your offspring throughout your generations comes near the sacred donations, which the Israelites dedicate to the LORD, while he is in a state of uncleanness, that person shall be cut off from my presence: I am the LORD.’ ^22-3

**22:4** No one of Aaron’s offspring who has a defiling skin disease or suffers a discharge may eat of the sacred donations until he is clean. Whoever touches anything made unclean by a corpse or a man who has had an emission of semen, ^22-4

**22:5** and whoever touches any swarming thing by which he may be made unclean or any human being by whom he may be made unclean—whatever his uncleanness may be— ^22-5

**22:6** the person who touches any such shall be unclean until evening and shall not eat of the sacred donations unless he has washed his body in water. ^22-6

**22:7** When the sun sets he shall be clean, and afterward he may eat of the sacred donations, for they are his food. ^22-7

**22:8** That which died or was torn by wild animals he shall not eat, becoming unclean by it: I am the LORD. ^22-8

**22:9** They shall keep my charge, so that they may not incur guilt and die because of it for having profaned them: I am the LORD; I sanctify them. ^22-9

**22:10** “No layperson shall eat of the sacred donations. No bound or hired servant of the priest shall eat of the sacred donations, ^22-10

**22:11** but if a priest acquires anyone by purchase, the person may eat of them, and those who are born in his house may eat of his food. ^22-11

**22:12** If a priest’s daughter marries a layman, she shall not eat of the offering of the sacred donations, ^22-12

**22:13** but if a priest’s daughter is widowed or divorced, without offspring, and returns to her father’s house, as in her youth, she may eat of her father’s food. No layperson shall eat of it. ^22-13

**22:14** If a man eats of the sacred donation unintentionally, he shall add one-fifth of its value to it and give the sacred donation to the priest. ^22-14

**22:15** No one shall profane the sacred donations of the Israelites, which they offer to the LORD, ^22-15

**22:16** causing them to bear guilt requiring a guilt offering, by eating their sacred donations, for I am the LORD; I sanctify them.” ^22-16

## Acceptable offerings

**22:17** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, ^22-17

**22:18** “Speak to Aaron and his sons and all the Israelites and say to them: When anyone of the house of Israel or of the aliens residing in Israel presents an offering, whether in payment of a vow or as a freewill offering that is offered to the LORD as a burnt offering, ^22-18

**22:19** to be acceptable on your behalf it shall be a male without blemish, of the cattle or the sheep or the goats. ^22-19

**22:20** You shall not offer anything that has a blemish, for it will not be acceptable on your behalf. ^22-20

**22:21** “When anyone offers a sacrifice of well-being to the LORD, in fulfillment of a vow or as a freewill offering, from the herd or from the flock, to be acceptable it must be perfect; there shall be no blemish in it. ^22-21

**22:22** Anything blind or injured or maimed or having a discharge or an itch or scabs—these you shall not offer to the LORD or put any of them on the altar as offerings by fire^[Or *as gifts*] to the LORD. ^22-22

**22:23** An ox or a lamb that is deformed or stunted you may present for a freewill offering, but it will not be accepted for a vow. ^22-23

**22:24** Any animal that has its testicles bruised or crushed or torn or cut, you shall not offer to the LORD; such you shall not do within your land, ^22-24

**22:25** nor shall you accept any such animals from a foreigner to offer as food to your God; since they are mutilated, with a blemish in them, they shall not be accepted on your behalf.” ^22-25

**22:26** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, ^22-26

**22:27** “When an ox or a sheep or a goat is born, it shall remain seven days with its mother, and from the eighth day on it shall be acceptable as the LORD’s offering by fire.^[Or *theLORD’s gift*] ^22-27

**22:28** But you shall not slaughter, from the herd or the flock, an animal with its young on the same day. ^22-28

**22:29** When you sacrifice a thanksgiving offering to the LORD, you shall sacrifice it so that it may be acceptable on your behalf. ^22-29

**22:30** It shall be eaten on the same day; you shall not leave any of it until morning: I am the LORD. ^22-30

**22:31** “Thus you shall keep my commandments and observe them: I am the LORD. ^22-31

**22:32** You shall not profane my holy name, that I may be sanctified among the Israelites: I am the LORD; I sanctify you, ^22-32

**22:33** I who brought you out of the land of Egypt to be your God: I am the LORD.” ^22-33
