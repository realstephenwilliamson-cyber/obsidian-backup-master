There are often said to be three types of aspirants, each of whom reflects a different *bhava* (mentality): *divya* (said to be superior and associated with the *sattva guna), vira* (said to be the appropriate type of spirituality for engaging in left-handed tantric rites and sometimes associated with the *rajas guna),* and *pasu* (the lowest, "beastly," type of person or spirituality). There are appropriate paths for each type of aspirant, and perhaps appropriate types of goddesses, although I have not yet found clear examples of the Mahavidyas being related to the three *bhavas.* See Sures Chandra Banerji, *Tantra in Bengal: A Study of Its Origin, Development and Influence* (Calcutta: Naya Prokash, 1 9 7 7 ) , pp. 1 5 5 - 5 6 .
