## Mūla

यागं कुर्वीत मतिमांस्तत्रायं क्रम उच्यते ।
दिश्युदीच्यां रुद्रकोणाद्वायव्यन्तं गणेश्वरम् ॥ ^mula

yāgaṃ kurvīta matimāṃstatrāyaṃ krama ucyate |
diśyudīcyāṃ rudrakoṇādvāyavyantaṃ gaṇeśvaram || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 09 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 1#^pg245|p. 228]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=245>))
>
> Now, the wise man should perform the sacrifice on an auspicious red cloth measuring twelve (arka) fingers, (or) two or three times the size; in a circle (vyoman) (drawn and) made beautiful with vermillion (sindūra), or else fashioned with indigo (rājavarta); in a vessel full of wine, be it a skull (or) coconut shell, or else on (their) collective form. (He should perform the sacrifice) where the Mandala is located on such (an arrangement). The pantheon (krama) (celebrated) there is said to be this one (which follows). (with 29.25–27ab)
>
> In the north, commencing with the northeast corner and ending in the northwest, he should worship Ganṇeśa, Vaṭuka, the three teachers, the Siddhas, Yoginīs, and the sacred seat (pīṭha). In the eastern quarter, starting from below Gaṇeśa, he should worship the assembly of Siddhas (siddhacakra) in the four quarters, ending (where he began,) in the place below Gaṇeśa. (with 29.27cd–29ab)
