## Longing for God and his help in distress

*To the leader. A Maskil of the Korahites.*

**42:1** As a deer longs for flowing streams, so my soul longs for you, O God. ^42-1

**42:2** My soul thirsts for God, for the living God. When shall I come and behold the face of God? ^42-2

**42:3** My tears have been my food day and night, while people say to me continually, “Where is your God?” ^42-3

**42:4** These things I remember, as I pour out my soul: how I went with the throng^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] and led them in procession to the house of God, with glad shouts and songs of thanksgiving, a multitude keeping festival. ^42-4

**42:5** Why are you cast down, O my soul, and why are you disquieted within me? Hope in God, for I shall again praise him, my help ^42-5

**42:6** and my God. My soul is cast down within me; therefore I remember you from the land of Jordan and of Hermon, from Mount Mizar. ^42-6

**42:7** Deep calls to deep at the thunder of your torrents; all your waves and your billows have gone over me. ^42-7

**42:8** By day the LORD commands his steadfast love, and at night his song is with me, a prayer to the God of my life. ^42-8

**42:9** I say to God, my rock, “Why have you forgotten me? Why must I walk about mournfully because the enemy oppresses me?” ^42-9

**42:10** As with a deadly wound in my body, my adversaries taunt me, while they say to me continually, “Where is your God?” ^42-10

**42:11** Why are you cast down, O my soul, and why are you disquieted within me? Hope in God, for I shall again praise him, my help and my God. ^42-11
