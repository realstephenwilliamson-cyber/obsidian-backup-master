## Mūla

अरामध्यं प्रकर्तव्यमराधस्तु षडंगुलम् ।
चतुरंगुलनिम्नं तु मध्यं तु परिकल्पयेत् ॥ ^mula

arāmadhyaṃ prakartavyamarādhastu ṣaḍaṃgulam |
caturaṃgulanimnaṃ tu madhyaṃ tu parikalpayet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 05 - Chapter Thirty-One, part 2#^pg130|p. 112]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=130>))
>
> Above the pedestal base, which is, by virtue of its own form (and nature), the root of the trident, that needs to be well made. The tip of the (middle) trident should be made very sharp and be one finger-breadth (thick). The middle of the prongs should be made six finger-breadths (long), and the lower part of the prongs (should also be) six finger-breadths.’ (with 31.112–113ab)
>
> ‘(The upper) eastern and (lower) western (part of the prong in the middle) should be fashioned (in such a way that) the middle (part of it) is depressed by four finger-breadths. Such is the prong here in the middle. Outside that (on its two sides), one should fashion with three threads each (three circles), and draw (within them) three lotuses on the tip of the trident. (These lotuses have a radius of) twelve finger-breadths (set at a distance of) four finger-breadths (from one other). Those on the right and left (both) have three and eight petals (respectively), and the one in the middle, twelve. (Thus making) three wheels (cakratraya). (Then) a circle (vātapura) consisting of a lotus eight finger-breadths (wide) with (eight) petals, called (the Lotus of) Wisdom, is (drawn) at the base of the trident. (with 31.113cd–116abc)
