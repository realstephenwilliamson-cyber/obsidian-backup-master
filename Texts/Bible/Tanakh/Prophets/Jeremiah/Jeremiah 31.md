# Jeremiah 31

## Chapter 31

**31:1** [^1]At that time—declares the LORD—I will be God to all the clans of Israel, and they shall be My people. ^31-1

**31:2** Thus said the LORD:  
The people escaped from the sword,  
Found favor in the wilderness;  
When Israel was marching homeward ^31-2

**31:3** The LORD revealed Himself to me[^2] of old.  
Eternal love I conceived for you then;  
Therefore I continue My grace to you. ^31-3

**31:4** I will build you firmly again,  
O Maiden Israel!  
Again you shall take up your timbrels  
And go forth to the rhythm of the dancers. ^31-4

**31:5** Again you shall plant vineyards  
On the hills of Samaria;  
Men shall plant and live to enjoy them. ^31-5

**31:6** For the day is coming when watchmen  
Shall proclaim on the heights of Ephraim:  
Come, let us go up to Zion,  
To the LORD our God! ^31-6

**31:7** For thus said the LORD:  
Cry out in joy for Jacob,  
Shout at the crossroads[^3] of the nations!  
Sing aloud in praise, and say:  
Save, O LORD, Your people,[^4]  
The remnant of Israel. ^31-7

**31:8** I will bring them in from the northland,  
Gather them from the ends of the earth—  
The blind and the lame among them,  
Those with child and those in labor—  
In a vast throng they shall return here. ^31-8

**31:9** They shall come with weeping,  
And with compassion[^5] will I guide them.  
I will lead them to streams of water,  
By a level road where they will not stumble.  
For I am ever a Father to Israel,  
Ephraim is My first-born. ^31-9

**31:10** Hear the word of the LORD, O nations,  
And tell it in the isles afar.  
Say:  
He who scattered Israel will gather them,  
And will guard them as a shepherd his flock. ^31-10

**31:11** For the LORD will ransom Jacob,  
Redeem him from one too strong for him. ^31-11

**31:12** They shall come and shout on the heights of Zion,  
Radiant over the bounty of the LORD—  
Over new grain and wine and oil,  
And over sheep and cattle.  
They shall fare like a watered garden,  
They shall never languish again. ^31-12

**31:13** Then shall maidens dance gaily,  
Young men and old alike.  
I will turn their mourning to joy,  
I will comfort them and cheer them in their grief. ^31-13

**31:14** I will give the priests their fill of fatness,  
And My people shall enjoy My full bounty  
—declares the LORD. ^31-14

**31:15** Thus said the LORD:  
A cry is heard in Ramah[^6]—  
Wailing, bitter weeping—  
Rachel weeping for her children.  
She refuses to be comforted  
For her children, who are gone. ^31-15

**31:16** Thus said the LORD:  
Restrain your voice from weeping,  
Your eyes from shedding tears;  
For there is a reward for your labor  
—declares the LORD:  
They shall return from the enemy’s land. ^31-16

**31:17** And there is hope for your future  
—declares the LORD:  
Your children shall return to their country. ^31-17

**31:18** I can hear Ephraim lamenting:  
You have chastised me, and I am chastised  
Like a calf that has not been broken.  
Receive me back, let me return,  
For You, O LORD, are my God. ^31-18

**31:19** Now that I have turned back, I am filled with remorse;  
Now that I am made aware, I strike my thigh.[^7]  
I am ashamed and humiliated,  
For I bear the disgrace of my youth. ^31-19

**31:20** Truly, Ephraim is a dear son to Me,  
A child that is dandled!  
Whenever I have turned[^8] against him,  
My thoughts would dwell on him still.  
That is why My heart yearns for him;  
I will receive him back in love  
—declares the LORD. ^31-20

**31:21** Erect markers,  
Set up signposts;[^9]  
Keep in mind the highway,  
The road that you traveled.  
Return, Maiden Israel!  
Return to these towns of yours! ^31-21

**31:22** How long will you waver,  
O rebellious daughter?  
(For the LORD has created something new on earth:  
A woman courts[^10] a man.) ^31-22

**31:23** Thus said the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel: They shall again say this in the land of Judah and in its towns, when I restore their fortunes:  
“The LORD bless you,  
Abode of righteousness,  
O holy mountain!” ^31-23

**31:24** Judah and all its towns alike shall be inhabited by the farmers and such as move about[^11] with the flocks. ^31-24

**31:25** For I will give the thirsty abundant drink, and satisfy all who languish. ^31-25

**31:26** At this I awoke and looked about, and my sleep[^12] had been pleasant to me. ^31-26

**31:27** See, a time is coming—declares the LORD—when I will sow the House of Israel and the House of Judah with seed of men and seed of cattle; ^31-27

**31:28** and just as I was watchful over them to uproot and to pull down, to overthrow and to destroy and to bring disaster, so I will be watchful over them to build and to plant—declares the LORD. ^31-28

**31:29** In those days, they shall no longer say, “Parents have eaten sour grapes and children’s teeth are blunted.”[^13] ^31-29

**31:30** But every one shall die for his own sins: whosoever eats sour grapes, his teeth shall be blunted. ^31-30

**31:31** See, a time is coming—declares the LORD—when I will make a new covenant with the House of Israel and the House of Judah. ^31-31

**31:32** It will not be like the covenant I made with their fathers, when I took them by the hand to lead them out of the land of Egypt, a covenant which they broke, though I espoused[^14] them—declares the LORD. ^31-32

**31:33** But such is the covenant I will make with the House of Israel after these days—declares the LORD: I will put My Teaching into their inmost being and inscribe it upon their hearts. Then I will be their God, and they shall be My people. ^31-33

**31:34** No longer will they need to teach one another and say to one another, “Heed the LORD”; for all of them, from the least of them to the greatest, shall heed Me—declares the LORD.  
For I will forgive their iniquities,  
And remember their sins no more. ^31-34

**31:35** Thus said the LORD,  
Who established the sun for light by day,  
The laws of moon and stars for light by night,  
Who stirs up the sea into roaring waves,  
Whose name is LORD of Hosts: ^31-35

**31:36** If these laws should ever be annulled by Me  
—declares the LORD—  
Only then would the offspring of Israel cease  
To be a nation before Me for all time. ^31-36

**31:37** Thus said the LORD: If the heavens above could be measured, and the foundations of the earth below could be fathomed, only then would I reject all the offspring of Israel for all that they have done—declares the LORD. ^31-37

**31:38** See, a time is coming—declares the LORD—when the city shall be rebuilt for the LORD from the Tower of Hananel to the Corner Gate; ^31-38

**31:39** and the measuring line shall go straight out to the Gareb Hill, and then turn toward Goah. ^31-39

**31:40** And the entire Valley of the Corpses and Ashes, and all the fields as far as the Wadi Kidron, and the corner of the Horse Gate on the east, shall be holy to the LORD. They shall never again be uprooted or overthrown. ^31-40
