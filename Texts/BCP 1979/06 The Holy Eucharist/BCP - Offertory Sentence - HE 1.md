# Offertory Sentence - HE 1

[[BCP 1979]] · *The Holy Eucharist* · pages 343–344

[p. 343]

## Offertory Sentences

*One of the following, or some other appropriate sentence of Scripture, may be used*

Offer to God a sacrifice of thanksgiving, and make good thy vows unto the Most High. *[[BCP Psalm 050#^50-14|Psalm 50:14]]*

Ascribe to the Lord the honor due his Name; bring offerings and come into his courts. *[[BCP Psalm 096#^96-8|Psalm 96:8]]*

Walk in love, as Christ loved us and gave himself for us, an offering and sacrifice to God. *[[Ephesians 05#^5-2|Ephesians 5:2]]*

I beseech you, brethren, by the mercies of God, to present yourselves as a living sacrifice, holy and acceptable to God, which is your spiritual worship. *[[Romans 12#^12-1|Romans 12:1]]*

If thou bring thy gift to the altar, and there rememberest that thy brother hath aught against thee, leave there thy gift before the altar, and go thy way; first be reconciled to thy brother, and then come and offer thy gift. *[[Matthew 05#^5-23|Matthew 5:23]],24*

[p. 344]

Through Christ let us continually offer to God the sacrifice of praise, that is, the fruit of lips that acknowledge his Name. But to do good and to distribute, forget not; for with such sacrifices God is well pleased. *[[Hebrews 13#^13-15|Hebrews 13:15]],16*

Worthy art thou, O Lord our God, to receive glory and honor and power; for thou hast created all things, and by thy will they were created and have their being. *[[Revelation 04#^4-11|Revelation 4:11]]*

Thine, O Lord, is the greatness, and the power, and the glory, and the victory, and the majesty. For all that is in the heaven and in the earth is thine. Thine is the kingdom, O Lord, and thou art exalted as head above all. *[[I Chronicles 29#^29-11|1 Chronicles 29:11]]*

*or this bidding*

Let us with gladness present the offerings and oblations of our life and labor to the Lord.
