# Genesis 5 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Genesis 04|← Genesis 4]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Genesis 06|Genesis 6 →]]

## Adam’s descendants to Noah and his sons

**5:1** This is the list of the descendants of Adam. When God created humans,^[Heb *adam*] he made them^[Heb *him*] in the likeness of God. ^5-1

**5:2** Male and female he created them, and he blessed them and called them humans^[Heb *adam*] when they were created. ^5-2

**5:3** When Adam had lived one hundred thirty years, he became the father of a son in his likeness, according to his image, and named him Seth. ^5-3

**5:4** The days of Adam after he became the father of Seth were eight hundred years, and he had other sons and daughters. ^5-4

**5:5** Thus all the days that Adam lived were nine hundred thirty years, and he died. ^5-5

**5:6** When Seth had lived one hundred five years, he became the father of Enosh. ^5-6

**5:7** Seth lived after the birth of Enosh eight hundred seven years and had other sons and daughters. ^5-7

**5:8** Thus all the days of Seth were nine hundred twelve years, and he died. ^5-8

**5:9** When Enosh had lived ninety years, he became the father of Kenan. ^5-9

**5:10** Enosh lived after the birth of Kenan eight hundred fifteen years and had other sons and daughters. ^5-10

**5:11** Thus all the days of Enosh were nine hundred five years, and he died. ^5-11

**5:12** When Kenan had lived seventy years, he became the father of Mahalalel. ^5-12

**5:13** Kenan lived after the birth of Mahalalel eight hundred and forty years and had other sons and daughters. ^5-13

**5:14** Thus all the days of Kenan were nine hundred and ten years, and he died. ^5-14

**5:15** When Mahalalel had lived sixty-five years, he became the father of Jared. ^5-15

**5:16** Mahalalel lived after the birth of Jared eight hundred thirty years and had other sons and daughters. ^5-16

**5:17** Thus all the days of Mahalalel were eight hundred ninety-five years, and he died. ^5-17

**5:18** When Jared had lived one hundred sixty-two years he became the father of Enoch. ^5-18

**5:19** Jared lived after the birth of Enoch eight hundred years and had other sons and daughters. ^5-19

**5:20** Thus all the days of Jared were nine hundred sixty-two years, and he died. ^5-20

**5:21** When Enoch had lived sixty-five years, he became the father of Methuselah. ^5-21

**5:22** Enoch walked with God after the birth of Methuselah three hundred years and had other sons and daughters. ^5-22

**5:23** Thus all the days of Enoch were three hundred sixty-five years. ^5-23

**5:24** Enoch walked with God; then he was no more, because God took him. ^5-24

**5:25** When Methuselah had lived one hundred eighty-seven years, he became the father of Lamech. ^5-25

**5:26** Methuselah lived after the birth of Lamech seven hundred eighty-two years and had other sons and daughters. ^5-26

**5:27** Thus all the days of Methuselah were nine hundred sixty-nine years, and he died. ^5-27

**5:28** When Lamech had lived one hundred eighty-two years, he became the father of a son; ^5-28

**5:29** he named him Noah, saying, “Out of the ground that the LORD has cursed this one shall bring us relief from our work and from the toil of our hands.” ^5-29

**5:30** Lamech lived after the birth of Noah five hundred ninety-five years and had other sons and daughters. ^5-30

**5:31** Thus all the days of Lamech were seven hundred seventy-seven years, and he died. ^5-31

**5:32** After Noah was five hundred years old, Noah became the father of Shem, Ham, and Japheth. ^5-32
