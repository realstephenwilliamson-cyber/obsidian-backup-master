# Psalms 118 (Douay-Rheims)

**118:1** ALEPH. Blessed are the undefiled in the way, who walk in the law of the Lord. ^118-1

**118:2** Blessed are they who search his testimonies: that seek him with their whole heart. ^118-2

**118:3** For they that work iniquity, have not walked in his ways. ^118-3

**118:4** Thou hast commanded thy commandments to be kept most diligently. ^118-4

**118:5** O! that my ways may be directed to keep thy justifications. ^118-5

**118:6** Then shall I not be confounded, when I shall look into all thy commandments. ^118-6

**118:7** I will praise thee with uprightness of heart, when I shall have learned the judgments of thy justice. ^118-7

**118:8** I will keep thy justifications: O! do not thou utterly forsake me. ^118-8

**118:9** BETH. By what doth a young man correct his way? by observing thy words. ^118-9

**118:10** With my whole heart have I sought after thee: let me not stray from thy commandments. ^118-10

**118:11** Thy words have I hidden in my heart, that I may not sin against thee. ^118-11

**118:12** Blessed art thou, O Lord: teach me thy justifications. ^118-12

**118:13** With my lips I have pronounced all the judgments of thy mouth. ^118-13

**118:14** I have been delighted in the way of thy testimonies, as in all riches. ^118-14

**118:15** I will meditate on thy commandments: and I will consider thy ways. ^118-15

**118:16** I will think of thy justifications: I will not forget thy words. ^118-16

**118:17** GIMEL. Give bountifully to thy servant, enliven me: and I shall keep thy words. ^118-17

**118:18** Open thou my eyes: and I will consider the wondrous things of thy law. ^118-18

**118:19** I am a sojourner on the earth: hide not thy commandments from me. ^118-19

**118:20** My soul hath coveted to long for thy justifications, at all times. ^118-20

**118:21** Thou hast rebuked the proud: they are cursed who decline from thy commandments. ^118-21

**118:22** Remove from reproach and contempt: because I have sought after thy testimonies. ^118-22

**118:23** For princes sat, and spoke against me: but thy servant was employed in thy justifications. ^118-23

**118:24** For thy testimonies are my meditation: and thy justifications my counsel. ^118-24

**118:25** DALETH. My soul hath cleaved to the pavement: quicken thou me according to thy word. ^118-25

**118:26** I have declared my ways, and thou hast heard me: teach me thy justifications. ^118-26

**118:27** Make me to understand the way of thy justifications: and I shall be exercised in thy wondrous works. ^118-27

**118:28** My soul hath slumbered through heaviness: strengthen thou me in thy words. ^118-28

**118:29** Remove from me the way of iniquity: and out of thy law have mercy on me. ^118-29

**118:30** I have chosen the way of truth: thy judgments I have not forgotten. ^118-30

**118:31** I have stuck to thy testimonies, O Lord: put me not to shame. ^118-31

**118:32** I have run the way of thy commandments, when thou didst enlarge my heart. ^118-32

**118:33** HE. Set before me for a law the way of thy justifications, O Lord: and I will always seek after it. ^118-33

**118:34** Give me understanding, and I will search thy law; and I will keep it with my whole heart. ^118-34

**118:35** Lead me into the path of thy commandments; for this same I have desired. ^118-35

**118:36** Incline my heart into thy testimonies and not to covetousness. ^118-36

**118:37** Turn away my eyes that they may not behold vanity: quicken me in thy way. ^118-37

**118:38** Establish thy word to thy servant, in thy fear. ^118-38

**118:39** Turn away my reproach, which I have apprehended: for thy judgments are delightful. ^118-39

**118:40** Behold I have longed after thy precepts: quicken me in thy justice. ^118-40

**118:41** VAU. Let thy mercy also come upon me, O Lord: thy salvation according to thy word. ^118-41

**118:42** So shall I answer them that reproach me in any thing; that I have trusted in thy words. ^118-42

**118:43** And take not thou the word of truth utterly out of my mouth: for in thy words have I hoped exceedingly. ^118-43

**118:44** So shall I always keep thy law, for ever and ever. ^118-44

**118:45** And I walked at large: because I have sought after thy commandments. ^118-45

**118:46** And I spoke of thy testimonies before kings: and I was not ashamed. ^118-46

**118:47** I meditated also on thy commandments, which I loved. ^118-47

**118:48** And I lifted up my hands to thy commandments, which I loved: and I was exercised in thy justifications. ^118-48

**118:49** ZAIN. Be thou mindful of thy word to thy servant, in which thou hast given me hope. ^118-49

**118:50** This hath comforted me in my humiliation: because thy word hath enlivened me. ^118-50

**118:51** The proud did iniquitously altogether: but I declined not from thy law. ^118-51

**118:52** I remembered, O Lord, thy judgments of old: and I was comforted. ^118-52

**118:53** A fainting hath taken hold of me, because of the wicked that forsake thy law. ^118-53

**118:54** Thy justifications were the subject of my song, in the place of my pilgrimage. ^118-54

**118:55** In the night I have remembered thy name, O Lord: and have kept thy law. ^118-55

**118:56** This happened to me: because I sought after thy justifications. ^118-56

**118:57** HETH. O Lord, my portion, I have said, I would keep the law. ^118-57

**118:58** I entreated thy face with all my heart: have mercy on me according to thy word. ^118-58

**118:59** I have thought on my ways: and turned my feet unto thy testimonies. ^118-59

**118:60** I am ready, and am not troubled: that I may keep thy commandments. ^118-60

**118:61** The cords of the wicked have encompassed me: but I have not forgotten thy law. ^118-61

**118:62** I rose at midnight to give praise to thee; for the judgments of thy justification. ^118-62

**118:63** I am a partaker with all them that fear thee, and that keep thy commandments. ^118-63

**118:64** The earth, O Lord, is full of thy mercy: teach me thy justifications. ^118-64

**118:65** TETH. Thou hast done well with thy servant, O Lord, according to thy word. ^118-65

**118:66** Teach me goodness and discipline and knowledge; for I have believed thy commandments. ^118-66

**118:67** Before I was humbled I offended; therefore have I kept thy word. ^118-67

**118:68** Thou art good; and in thy goodness teach me thy justifications. ^118-68

**118:69** The iniquity of the proud hath been multiplied over me: but I will seek thy commandments with my whole heart. ^118-69

**118:70** Their heart is curdled like milk: but I have meditated on thy law. ^118-70

**118:71** It is good for me that thou hast humbled me, that I may learn thy justifications. ^118-71

**118:72** The law of thy mouth is good to me, above thousands of gold and silver. ^118-72

**118:73** JOD. Thy hands have made me and formed me: give me understanding, and I will learn thy commandments. ^118-73

**118:74** They that fear thee shall see me, and shall be glad: because I have greatly hoped in thy words. ^118-74

**118:75** I know, O Lord, that thy judgments are equity: and in thy truth thou hast humbled me. ^118-75

**118:76** O! let thy mercy be for my comfort, according to thy word unto thy servant. ^118-76

**118:77** Let thy tender mercies come unto me, and I shall live: for thy law is my meditation. ^118-77

**118:78** Let the proud be ashamed, because they have done unjustly towards me: but I will be employed in thy commandments. ^118-78

**118:79** Let them that fear thee turn to me and they that know thy testimonies. ^118-79

**118:80** Let my heart be undefiled in thy justifications, that I may not be confounded. ^118-80

**118:81** CAPH. My soul hath fainted after thy salvation: and in thy word I have very much hoped. ^118-81

**118:82** My eyes have failed for thy word, saying: When wilt thou comfort me? ^118-82

**118:83** For I am become like a bottle in the frost: I have not forgotten thy justifications. ^118-83

**118:84** How many are the days of thy servant: when wilt thou execute judgment on them that persecute me? ^118-84

**118:85** The wicked have told me fables: but not as thy law. ^118-85

**118:86** All thy statutes are truth: they have persecuted me unjustly, do thou help me. ^118-86

**118:87** They had almost made an end of me upon earth: but I have not forsaken thy commandments. ^118-87

**118:88** Quicken thou me according to thy mercy: and I shall keep the testimonies of thy mouth. ^118-88

**118:89** LAMED. For ever, O Lord, thy word standeth firm in heaven. ^118-89

**118:90** Thy truth unto all generations: thou hast founded the earth, and it continueth. ^118-90

**118:91** By thy ordinance the day goeth on: for all things serve thee. ^118-91

**118:92** Unless thy law had been my meditation, I had then perhaps perished in my abjection. ^118-92

**118:93** Thy justifications I will never forget: for by them thou hast given me life. ^118-93

**118:94** I am thine, save thou me: for I have sought thy justifications. ^118-94

**118:95** The wicked have waited for me to destroy me: but I have understood thy testimonies. ^118-95

**118:96** I have seen an end to all persecution: thy commandment is exceeding broad. ^118-96

**118:97** MEM. O how have I loved thy law, O Lord! it is my meditation all the day. ^118-97

**118:98** Through thy commandment, thou hast made me wiser than my enemies: for it is ever with me. ^118-98

**118:99** I have understood more than all my teachers: because thy testimonies are my meditation. ^118-99

**118:100** I have had understanding above ancients: because I have sought thy commandments. ^118-100

**118:101** I have restrained my feet from every evil way: that I may keep thy words. ^118-101

**118:102** I have not declined from thy judgments, because thou hast set me a law. ^118-102

**118:103** How sweet are thy words to my palate! more than honey to my mouth. ^118-103

**118:104** By thy commandments I have had understanding: therefore have I hated every way of iniquity. ^118-104

**118:105** NUN. Thy word is a lamp to my feet, and a light to my paths. ^118-105

**118:106** I have sworn and am determined to keep the judgments of thy justice. ^118-106

**118:107** I have been humbled, O Lord, exceedingly: quicken thou me according to thy word. ^118-107

**118:108** The free offerings of my mouth make acceptable, O Lord: and teach me thy judgments. ^118-108

**118:109** My soul is continually in my hands: and I have not forgotten thy law. ^118-109

**118:111** I have purchased thy testimonies for an inheritance for ever: because they are a joy to my heart. ^118-111

**118:112** I have inclined my heart to do thy justifications for ever, for the reward. ^118-112

**118:114** Thou art my helper and my protector: and in thy word I have greatly hoped. ^118-114
