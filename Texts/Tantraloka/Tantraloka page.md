# Tantrāloka page

One verse of the [[Tantraloka|Tantrāloka]] laid out like a page of the printed śāstra: the mūla in Devanagari and IAST, Dyczkowski's translation, and Jayaratha's Viveka in Dyczkowski's English (his notes folded, the page numbers kept) or in Sanskrit, or both; beside it the word analysis and your `word::` lines, and on the other side the secondary literature, every other note that cites the verse, the concepts, the day of the study plan that reads it and your own `question::`, `claim::` and `connects::` lines. Type a verse in the box (*1.1*) or use the arrows; the verse and the Viveka choice are kept in this note's properties, and when *verse* is empty the page shows the first verse of today's portion. On a narrow screen the columns stack under the text.
