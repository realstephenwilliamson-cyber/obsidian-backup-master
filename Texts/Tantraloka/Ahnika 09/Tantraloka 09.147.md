## Mūla

तेषामणूनां स मल ईश्वरेच्छावशाद्भृशम् ।
प्रबुध्यते तथा चोक्तं शास्त्रे श्रीपूर्वनामनि ॥ ^mula

teṣāmaṇūnāṃ sa mala īśvarecchāvaśādbhṛśam |
prabudhyate tathā coktaṃ śāstre śrīpūrvanāmani || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 06 - Chapter Nine, part 4#^pg143|p. 126]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=143>))
>
> The Impurity of these souls is forcefully awakened by the power (vaśa) of the Lord’s will. (with 9.147abc)
>
> The same is said in the scripture (śāstra) called Śrīpūrva (i.e. the Mālinīvijayottara): ‘The desire for worldly pleasure (bhoga) arises in him by the power of the Lord’s will. Thus, (Aghoreśa, alias Ananta,) the first Great Lord of Mantra, disturbs Māyā, and goes on to fashion the diverse world of transmigration (saṁsāra) in order to assist (the soul) who desires worldly (experience).’ (with 9.147–149ab)
