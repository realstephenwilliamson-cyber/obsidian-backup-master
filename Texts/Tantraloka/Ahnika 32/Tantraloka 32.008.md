## Mūla

देवीसंनिधये तत्स्यादलं किं डम्बरैर्वृथा ।
काम्ये कर्मणि ताश्च स्युर्मुख्याः कस्यापि जातुचित् ॥ ^mula

devīsaṃnidhaye tatsyādalaṃ kiṃ ḍambarairvṛthā |
kāmye karmaṇi tāśca syurmukhyāḥ kasyāpi jātucit || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 06 - Chapter Thirty-Two#^pg158|p. 140]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=158>))
>
> Whatever place one who is penetrated by the venerable (goddess) Khecarī (the Skyfarer) (may choose) to take up as his dwelling (samāśrayet), that (act by itself) is enough to make the goddesses draw close. So why (indulge) in useless chatter (about other mudrās)? (with 32.7cd–8ab)
>
> However, in the case of optional (magical) rites (performed for some specific purpose) (kāmyakarman), they may sometimes be primary for someone. (with 32.8cd)
