## Chapter 2

**2:1** I looked up, and I saw four horns.[^1] ^2-1

**2:2** I asked the angel who talked with me, “What are those?” “Those,” he replied, “are the horns that tossed Judah, Israel, and Jerusalem.”[^2] ^2-2

**2:3** Then the LORD showed me four smiths. ^2-3

**2:4** “What are they coming to do?” I asked. He replied: “Those are the horns that tossed Judah, so that no man could raise his head; and these men have come to throw them into a panic,[^3] to hew down[^4] the horns of the nations that raise a horn against the land of Judah, to toss it.” ^2-4

**2:5** I looked up, and I saw a man holding a measuring line. ^2-5

**2:6** “Where are you going?” I asked. “To measure Jerusalem,” he replied, “to see how long and wide it is to be.” ^2-6

**2:7** But the angel who talked with me came forward, and another angel came forward to meet him. ^2-7

**2:8** The former said to him, “Run to that young man and tell him:  
“Jerusalem shall be peopled as a city without walls, so many shall be the men and cattle it contains. ^2-8

**2:9** And I Myself—declares the LORD—will be a wall of fire all around it, and I will be a glory inside it. ^2-9

**2:10** “Away, away! Flee from the land of the north—says the LORD—though I swept you [there] like the four winds of heaven—declares the LORD.” ^2-10

**2:11** Away, escape, O Zion, you who dwell in Fair Babylon! ^2-11

**2:12** For thus said the LORD of Hosts—He who sent me after glory[^5]—concerning the nations that have taken you as spoil: “Whoever touches you touches the pupil of his own[^6] eye. ^2-12

**2:13** For I will lift My hand against them, and they shall be spoil for those they enslaved.”—Then you shall know that I was sent by the LORD of Hosts. ^2-13

**2:14** Shout for joy, Fair Zion! For lo, I come; and I will dwell in your midst—declares the LORD. ^2-14

**2:15** In that day many nations will attach themselves to the LORD and become His[^7] people, and He[^8] will dwell in your midst. Then you will know that I was sent to you by the LORD of Hosts. ^2-15

**2:16** The LORD will take Judah to Himself as His portion[^9] in the Holy Land, and He will choose Jerusalem once more. ^2-16

**2:17** Be silent, all flesh, before the LORD!  
For He is roused from His holy habitation. ^2-17

[^1]: The four horns correspond to the four winds of v. 10.
[^2]: The four horns correspond to the four winds of v. 10.
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain; emendation yields “to sharpen ax heads.”
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^5]: Emendation yields “whose Presence sent me.”
[^6]: According to ancient Jewish tradition, a scribal change for “My.”
[^7]: Heb. “My.”
[^8]: Heb. “I.”
[^9]: Emendation yields “allot to Judah its portion”; cf. Num. 34.17.
