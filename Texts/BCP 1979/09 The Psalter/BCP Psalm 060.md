# Psalm 60

← [[BCP Psalm 059]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 061]] →

*Deus, repulisti nos* · Book Two · Eleventh Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 060]]

**60:1** O God, you have cast us off and broken us; \*  
you have been angry;  
oh, take us back to you again. ^60-1

**60:2** You have shaken the earth and split it open; \*  
repair the cracks in it, for it totters. ^60-2

**60:3** You have made your people know hardship; \*  
you have given us wine that makes us stagger. ^60-3

**60:4** You have set up a banner for those who fear you, \*  
to be a refuge from the power of the bow. ^60-4

**60:5** Save us by your right hand and answer us, \*  
that those who are dear to you may be delivered. ^60-5

**60:6** God spoke from his holy place and said: \*  
"I will exult and parcel out Shechem;  
I will divide the valley of Succoth. ^60-6

**60:7** Gilead is mine and Manasseh is mine; \*  
Ephraim is my helmet and Judah my scepter. ^60-7

[p. 668] ^pg668

**60:8** Moab is my wash-basin,  
on Edom I throw down my sandal to claim it, \*  
and over Philistia will I shout in triumph." ^60-8

**60:9** Who will lead me into the strong city? \*  
who will bring me to Edom? ^60-9

**60:10** Have you not cast us off, O God? \*  
you no longer go out, O God, with our armies. ^60-10

**60:11** Grant us your help against the enemy, \*  
for vain is the help of man. ^60-11

**60:12** With God we will do valiant deeds, \*  
and he shall tread our enemies under foot. ^60-12
