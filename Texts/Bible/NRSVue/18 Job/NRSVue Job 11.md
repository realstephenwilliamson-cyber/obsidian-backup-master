# Job 11 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Job 10|← Job 10]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Job 12|Job 12 →]]

## Zophar speaks: Job’s guilt deserves punishment

**11:1** Then Zophar the Naamathite answered: ^11-1

**11:2** “Should a multitude of words go unanswered, and should one full of talk be vindicated? ^11-2

**11:3** Should your babble put others to silence, and when you mock, shall no one shame you? ^11-3

**11:4** For you say, ‘My conduct^[Gk: Heb *teaching*] is pure, and I am clean in God’s^[Heb *your*] sight.’ ^11-4

**11:5** But O that God would speak and open his lips to you ^11-5

**11:6** and that he would tell you the secrets of wisdom! For wisdom is many-sided.^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] Know then that God exacts of you less than your guilt deserves. ^11-6

**11:7** “Can you find out the deep things of God? Can you find out the limit of the Almighty?^[Traditional rendering of Heb *Shaddai*] ^11-7

**11:8** It is higher than heaven^[Heb *The heights of heaven*]—what can you do? Deeper than Sheol—what can you know? ^11-8

**11:9** Its measure is longer than the earth and broader than the sea. ^11-9

**11:10** If he passes through and imprisons and assembles for judgment, who can hinder him? ^11-10

**11:11** For he knows those who are worthless; when he sees iniquity, will he not consider it? ^11-11

**11:12** But the stupid will get understanding when a wild ass is born human.^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^11-12

**11:13** “If you direct your heart rightly, you will stretch out your hands toward him. ^11-13

**11:14** If iniquity is in your hand, put it far away, and do not let wickedness reside in your tents. ^11-14

**11:15** Surely then you will lift up your face without blemish; you will be secure and will not fear. ^11-15

**11:16** You will forget your misery; you will remember it as waters that have passed away. ^11-16

**11:17** And your life will be brighter than the noonday; its darkness will be like the morning. ^11-17

**11:18** And you will have confidence because there is hope; you will be protected^[Or *you will look around*] and take your rest in safety. ^11-18

**11:19** You will lie down, and no one will make you afraid; many will entreat your favor. ^11-19

**11:20** But the eyes of the wicked will fail; all way of escape will be lost to them, and their hope is to breathe their last.” ^11-20
