## Mūla

अपरा सा परा काली भीषणा चण्डयोगिनी ।
दृष्ट्वैतन्मण्डलं देव्यः सर्वा नृत्यन्ति सर्वदा ॥ ^mula

aparā sā parā kālī bhīṣaṇā caṇḍayoginī |
dṛṣṭvaitanmaṇḍalaṃ devyaḥ sarvā nṛtyanti sarvadā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 04 - Chapter Thirty-One, part 1#^pg102|p. 84]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=102>))
>
> The best colours, each for one of the goddesses, is red lead (sindūra), (dark blue or black) lapis lazuli (rājavarta); and the whitest chalk. The Goddess Parā is (white) like the moon. The Goddess Parāparā is red. The other one is Aparā, who is the black (kālī), terrific, and fierce Yogini (candayoginī). (with 31.39cd–41ab)
>
> Seeing this mandala all the Goddesses always dance (with joy). Even if it is not worshiped or it is seen by one who is not initiated, he would be initiated by the Mothers! (with 31.41cd–42ab)

## Viveka

> अनर्चितेऽप्यदीक्षेण दृष्टे दीक्ष्येत मातृभिः ।
