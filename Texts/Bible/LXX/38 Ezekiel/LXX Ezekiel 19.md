# LXX Ezekiel 19

← [[LXX Ezekiel 18]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Ezekiel 20]] →

*Ezekiel · [[LXX]]*

**19:1** Moreover do thou take up a lamentation for the prince of Israel,  ^19-1

**19:2** and say, Why is thy mother become a whelp in the midst of lions? in the midst of lions she has multiplied her whelps.  ^19-2

**19:3** And one of her whelps sprang forth; he became a lion, and learned to take prey, he devoured men.  ^19-3

**19:4** And the nations heard a report of him; he was caught in their pit, and they brought him into the land of Egypt in chains.  ^19-4

**19:5** And she saw that he was driven away from her, *and* her hope *of him* perished, and she took another of her whelps; she made him a lion.  ^19-5

**19:6** And he went up and down in the midst of lions, he became a lion, and learned to take prey, he devoured men.  ^19-6

**19:7** And he prowled in his boldness and laid waste their cities, and made the land desolate, and the fulness of it, by the voice of his roaring.  ^19-7

**19:8** Then the nations set upon him from the countries round about, and they spread their nets upon him: he was taken in their pit.  ^19-8

**19:9** And they put him in chains and in a cage, *and* he came to the king of Babylon; and he cast him into prison, that his voice should not be heard on the mountains of Israel.  ^19-9

**19:10** Thy mother was as a vine and as a blossom on a pomegranate tree, planted by water: her fruit and her shoots abounded by reason of much water.  ^19-10

**19:11** And she became a rod for a tribe of princes, and was elevated in her bulk in the midst of *other* trees, and she saw her bulk in the multitude of her branches.  ^19-11

**19:12** But she was broken down in wrath, she was cast upon the ground, and the east wind dried up her choice *branches*: vengeance came upon them, and the rod of her strength was withered; fire consumed it.  ^19-12

**19:13** And now they have planted her in the wilderness, in a dry land.  ^19-13

**19:14** And fire is gone out of a rod of her choice *boughs*, and has devoured her; and there was no rod of strength in her. Her race is become a parable of lamentation, and it shall be for a lamentation.  ^19-14
