## Mūla

एवं जातो मृतोऽस्मीति जन्ममृत्युविचित्रताः ।
अजन्मन्यमृतौ भान्ति चित्तभित्तौ स्वनिर्मिताः ॥ ^mula

evaṃ jāto mṛto'smīti janmamṛtyuvicitratāḥ |
ajanmanyamṛtau bhānti cittabhittau svanirmitāḥ || ^iast

> Similarly, the various forms of birth and death (are just the notions that) “I am born” and “I am dead”, that are one’s own creations appearing on the screen of the Mind, that is devoid of birth and death.
