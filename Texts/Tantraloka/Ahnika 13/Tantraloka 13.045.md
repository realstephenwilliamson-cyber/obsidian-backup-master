## Mūla

भेदे प्रमाणाभावाच्च तदेकं निखिलात्मसु ।
तच्च कस्मात्प्रसूतं स्यान्मायातश्चेत्कथं नु सा ॥ ^mula

bhede pramāṇābhāvācca tadekaṃ nikhilātmasu |
tacca kasmātprasūtaṃ syānmāyātaścetkathaṃ nu sā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 05 - Chapter Thirteen, part 1#^pg170|p. 150]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=170>))
>
> (If that ignorance) is established (siddha) to be an obscuring covering (in this way), it (must be) undifferentiated by its very nature. (Indeed, it must be) one and the same for all individual souls, for there is no valid means of knowledge (that could establish its) multiplicity. (with 13.44cd–45ab)
>
> Again, from what could that (obscuring covering) be born? If it (were to be born) from Māyā, how could (Māyā) generate it in some place (for fettered) souls and not for those who are liberated? The previous objection would apply (here also). Again, if (you try and avoid this problem by saying that) there is no (instrumental) cause (nimitta) (of this obscuring covering), thus (the obscuration is just an illusion and so) it does not (really) come into being, then it would be permanent and so is not destroyed. (with 13.45cd–47ab)
