## Mūla

बहिःकर्म ततः कुर्याद्दिक्षु सर्वासु दैशिकः ।
ओं क्षः क्षः सर्वभूतेभ्यः स्वाहेति मनुनामुना ॥ ^mula

bahiḥkarma[^141] tataḥ kuryād dikṣu sarvāsu daiśikaḥ |
oṁ kṣaḥ kṣaḥ sarvabhūtebhyaḥ svāheti manunāmunā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2025 – April 2025
> p. 35 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=49>))
>
> The teacher should then perform the rite of offering bali in all the quarters with this mantra: 'OṀ KṢAḤ KṢAḤ SARVABHŪTEBHYO SVĀHĀ'.[^142]

[^141]: *bahiḥkarma* emended to *balikarma*
[^142]: MV 8/124 is with reference to TĀ 15/476-478ab (472-474ab) (not quoted by Jayaratha).
