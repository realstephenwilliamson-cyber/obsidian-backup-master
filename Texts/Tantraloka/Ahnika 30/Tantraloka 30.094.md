## Mūla

शाकिनीस्तोभनं मर्म हृदयं जीवितं त्विदम् ।
षष्ठप्राणत्रिकूटोर्ध्वबाहुशूलाख्यबिन्दुभिः ॥ ^mula

śākinīstobhanaṃ marma hṛdayaṃ jīvitaṃ tvidam |
ṣaṣṭhaprāṇatrikūṭordhvabāhuśūlākhyabindubhiḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 03 - Chapter Thirty, part 2#^pg72|p. 54]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=72>))
>
> The Saviour (OṀ), the group (piṇḍa) (formed) with (the letters) Ś, M, R, and Y, (along with vowel Ū) and salutation (NAMAḤ) (constitute) the four syllabled (Mantra). The purification (brought about by the Initiation of the) Scales (tulāśuddhi) instils immediate conviction (that it has been effective), by (perceiving that he who has received it loses weight and so) is light. This (Mantra) paralyzes the Śākinīs; it is the weak spot (marman) (of the body), the heart, and life (itself). (with 30.93–94ab)
>
> (The Mantra consisting) of the sixth (vowel Ū), the Vital Breath (H), the Triple Peak (K), the Raised Arm (JH), the Trident (AU), and the Point adorned with Unstruck Sound (anacka) (i.e. Nāda), the Nose (i.e. Śakti), and the Fragment of the Moon (candrakhanda) that faces downwards (i.e. Ardhacandra), is the Heart, called ‘Bhairava’, that destroys all things. (with 30.94cd–95)
