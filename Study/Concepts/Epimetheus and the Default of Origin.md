# Epimetheus and the Default of Origin

> [!abstract] Definition
> Bernard Stiegler's reading of the Promethean/Epimethean myth in Plato's *Protagoras*, positing that humanity has no natural, innate biological essence (*the fault of Epimetheus*); humanity is constituted from its very inception by artificial, prosthetic supplementarity (*the gift of Prometheus*).

## The Mythological Reading
In *Protagoras* [[Protagoras 320#^320d|320d–322a]], Epimetheus (the one who thinks after the fact) distributes powers and protections to all mortal creatures (claws, fur, speed, wings). Forgetting humanity, he leaves mankind naked, unarmed, and defenseless. To save humankind from oblivion, Prometheus steals technical fire and arts from Hephaestus and Athena.

## Philosophical Significance
In [[Stiegler - Technics and Time (vols 1-3)|Technics and Time, Vol. 1]]:
- Human beings have no original, biological nature that was later "corrupted" by technology.
- The human *origin is a default*—a lack that must always be made up by technics.
- Because humanity is originally prosthetic, questions of human liberation, ethics, or politics cannot take place outside of technics, but must occur as a struggle over technical governance.
