## Mūla

नीत्वा तत्रासने पूर्वं मूर्तिभूतं घटं न्यसेत् ।
तस्य दक्षिणदिग्भागे वार्धानीं विनिवेशयेत्  ॥ ^mula

nītvā tatrāsane pūrvaṁ mūrtibhūtaṁ[^115] ghaṭaṁ nyaset |
tasya dakṣiṇadigbhāge vārdhānīṁ viniveśayet[^116] || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2025 – April 2025
> p. 30 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=44>))
>
> Having brought the jar which serves as the icon (of the deity), one should place it there on (its) seat in the east.[^117] One should place the (small) jar (vārdhānī) in the quarter to its right (dakṣiṇa).[^118]

[^115]: k: *mūrtibhūtāṁ parām*
[^116]: Variant reading: *vinivedayed*
[^117]: Read *pūrve* for *pūrvaṁ.*
[^118]: MV 8/107 is quoted in TĀv ad 15/381cd-388ab (378-385ab). TĀ 15/387cd-388ab (384cd-385ab) is an explanatory paraphrase of MV 8/107.
