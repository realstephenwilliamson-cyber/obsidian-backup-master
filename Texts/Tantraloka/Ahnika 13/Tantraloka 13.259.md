## Mūla

शक्तिपातवशादेव तां तां सिद्धिमुपाश्रिताः ।
ननु पूजाजपध्यानशङ्करासेवनादिभिः ॥ ^mula

śaktipātavaśādeva tāṃ tāṃ siddhimupāśritāḥ |
nanu pūjājapadhyānaśaṅkarāsevanādibhiḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 09 - Chapter Thirteen, part 5#^pg307|p. 285]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=307>))
>
> Thus, the Rudras who reside within Māyā and those who are above them identify vigorously (haṭhāt) with Bhairava when the post assigned to them comes to an end. Indeed, those who are not overcome by Māyā (and so) are free of Karma and the rest, attain this or that accomplishment (siddhi) by the descent of (Śiva’s) power (of grace). (with 13.257cd–259ab)
>
> Surely, (someone may object that) they have attained the state of Mantra and the other (higher level subjects) by worship, the repetition (of Mantras), meditation, attending on iva and the like. So how are they independent of Karma? (with 13.259cd–260ab)
