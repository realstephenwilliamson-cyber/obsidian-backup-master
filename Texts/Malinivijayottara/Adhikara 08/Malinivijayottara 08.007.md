## Mūla

ततोऽवासाः सुवासा वा हस्तौ पादौ च धावयेत् ।
आचम्य मार्जनं कुर्याद्विद्यया भूरिवर्णया ॥ ^mula

tato 'vāsāḥ suvāsā vā hastau pādau ca dhāvayet |
ācamya mārjanaṁ kuryād vidyayā bhūrivarṇayā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, October 2024 – December 2024
> p. 15 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=14>))
>
> Without clothes or (dressed) in fine clothes, (the officiant) should then wash his hands and feet and, after having sipped water (ācamya), he should wipe (the body clean) (mārjana) with a Vidyā of many letters. Having performed the common deposition, prayed for purification (aghamarṣana) with the second (Vidyā) and attended reverently (upasthāna) with Mālinī, he should repeat (japet) the monosyllabic (Vidyā of) Parā.[^7] (with 8.7–8)

[^7]: MV 8/7cd-8 is quoted in the TĀv ad 15/53cd-55.
