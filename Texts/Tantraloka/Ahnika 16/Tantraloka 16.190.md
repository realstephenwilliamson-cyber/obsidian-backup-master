## Mūla

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 06 - Chapter Sixteen, part 2#^pg102|p. 82]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=102>))
>
> Due to the diverse (forms of) purifying (Mantras) and (their) objects of purification, as well as the (consequent) diversity of (the rites) that need to be performed (itikartavya), this initiation is of many kinds. (However) there are (only) seventy kinds (of initiation that can be performed) without (depositing) the object of purification.
