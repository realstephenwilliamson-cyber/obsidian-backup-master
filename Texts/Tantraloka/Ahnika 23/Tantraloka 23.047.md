## Mūla

तत्र तत्र नियुञ्जीत न तु जातु विपर्ययात् ।
ननु तद्वस्त्वयोग्यस्य तत्रेच्छा जायते कुतः ॥ ^mula

tatra tatra niyuñjīta na tu jātu viparyayāt |
nanu tadvastvayogyasya tatrecchā jāyate kutaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 14 - Chapter Twenty-Three#^pg338|p. 318]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=338>))
>
> When (dispensing) knowledge, be it that of the scriptures, (the manner of acquisition of magical and yogic) accomplishments (siddhi), or the status of a teacher etc., (the teacher) should examine (the initiate) repeatedly. (The teacher) should apply him to whatever he is competent (to practice), certainly not the other way around (that is, to what he is not). (with 23.46–47ab)
>
> Surely (one may ask), how can a desire arise there (in the case) of one who is not qualified for these things? It is that desire itself, sustained by the Lord, that indicates his competence. (with 23.47cd–48ab)
