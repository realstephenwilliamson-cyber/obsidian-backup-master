## Mūla

रौद्री ज्येष्ठा च वामा च तथा शक्तिसदाशिवौ ।
एतानि सकले पञ्च भुवनानि विदुर्बुधाः ॥ ^mula

raudrī jyeṣṭhā ca vāmā ca tathā śaktisadāśivau |
etāni sakale pañca bhuvanāni vidur budhāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, July 2023 – September 2023
> p. 76 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=81>))
>
> The wise know that these five world-orders are within (immanent) Sakala (Śiva), namely, Raudrī, Jyeṣṭhā and Vāmā as well as the two, Śakti and Sadāśiva.
