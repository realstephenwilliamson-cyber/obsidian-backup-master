## Plea for justice and declaration of righteousness

*Of David.*

**26:1** Vindicate me, O LORD, for I have walked in my integrity, and I have trusted in the LORD without wavering. ^26-1

**26:2** Prove me, O LORD, and try me; test my heart and mind. ^26-2

**26:3** For your steadfast love is before my eyes, and I walk in faithfulness to you.^[Or *in your faithfulness*] ^26-3

**26:4** I do not sit with the worthless, nor do I consort with hypocrites; ^26-4

**26:5** I hate the company of evildoers and will not sit with the wicked. ^26-5

**26:6** I wash my hands in innocence and go around your altar, O LORD, ^26-6

**26:7** singing aloud a song of thanksgiving and telling all your wondrous deeds. ^26-7

**26:8** O LORD, I love the house in which you dwell and the place where your glory abides. ^26-8

**26:9** Do not sweep me away with sinners nor my life with the bloodthirsty, ^26-9

**26:10** those in whose hands are evil devices and whose right hands are full of bribes. ^26-10

**26:11** But as for me, I walk in my integrity; redeem me and be gracious to me. ^26-11

**26:12** My foot stands on level ground; in the great congregation I will bless the LORD. ^26-12
