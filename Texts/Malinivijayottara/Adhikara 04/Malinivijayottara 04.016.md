## Mūla

ठादौ च सप्तके सप्त पुरुषादीनि पूर्ववत् ।
इङ्घेषु त्रयं विद्याद्विद्यातः सकलावधि ॥ ^mula

ṭhādau ca saptake sapta puruṣādīni pūrvavat |
iṅgheṣu trayaṁ vidyād vidyātaḥ sakalāvadhi || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, April 2023 – June 2023
> p. 18 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=59>))
>
> [^10]It is taught that the Earth principle is (located) within the phoneme PH. One should observe the twenty-three principles beginning with water and ending with Nature to be within the corresponding phonemes beginning with D and ending with JH, respectively. Within the group of seven phonemes beginning with ṬH (up to A) are, as before, the seven (principles) beginning with the individual soul. (with 4.15–16ab)

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, April 2023 – June 2023
> p. 18 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=59>))
>
> The three (principles) from Pure Knowledge ending with immanent Śiva (*sakala*) are in the (phonemes) I, Ṅ and GH. One should discern (how) the remaining sixteen phonemes from G to N are within the Śiva principle. The forces, the parts (of mantra) (*pada*), the mantras and the world-orders, O fair lady, should be known to be as before distributed in that (same) numerical order as they are (in relation to) the phonemes (of Mātṛkā). (with 4.16cd–18ab)

[^10]: Abhinavagupta comments on MV 4/15-25 in PTv Singh p. 104 ff.
