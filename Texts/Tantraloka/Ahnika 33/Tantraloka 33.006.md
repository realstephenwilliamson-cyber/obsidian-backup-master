## Mūla

सिद्धिर्वृद्धिर्द्युतिर्लक्ष्मीर्मेधा कान्तिः सुधा धृतिः ।
दीप्तिः पुष्टिर्मतिः कीर्तिः सुस्थितिः सुगतिः स्मृतिः ॥ ^mula

siddhirvṛddhirdyutirlakṣmīrmedhā kāntiḥ sudhā dhṛtiḥ |
dīptiḥ puṣṭirmatiḥ kīrtiḥ susthitiḥ sugatiḥ smṛtiḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 07 - Chapter Thirty-Three#^pg190|p. 172]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=190>))
>
> (The goddesses of the wheel of sixteen spokes are) Siddhi (Accomplishment), Vṛddhi (Increase), Dyuti (Lusture), Lakṣmī, Medhā (Intellegence), Kānti (Beauty), Svadhā, Dhṛti (Fortitude), Dīpti (Radiance), Puṣṭi (Satisfaction), Mati (Intellegence), Kīrti (Fame), Susthiti (Good State), Sugati (Good Fortune), Smṛti (Memory) and Suprabhā (Beautiful Light), who is the sixteenth. (They are) the powers of Śrīkaṇṭha and the rest. (with 33.6–7ab)
