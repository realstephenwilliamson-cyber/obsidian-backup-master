# Proverbs 10

## Chapter 10

**10:1** The proverbs of Solomon:  
A wise son brings joy to his father;  
A dull son is his mother’s sorrow. ^10-1

**10:2** Ill-gotten wealth is of no avail,  
But righteousness saves from death. ^10-2

**10:3** The LORD will not let the righteous go hungry,  
But He denies the wicked what they crave. ^10-3

**10:4** Negligent hands cause poverty,  
But diligent hands enrich. ^10-4

**10:5** He who lays in stores during the summer is a capable son,  
But he who sleeps during the harvest is an incompetent. ^10-5

**10:6** Blessings light upon the head of the righteous,  
But lawlessness covers the mouth of the wicked. ^10-6

**10:7** The name of the righteous is invoked in blessing,  
But the fame of the wicked rots. ^10-7

**10:8** He whose heart is wise accepts commands,  
But he whose speech is foolish comes to grief. ^10-8

**10:9** He who lives blamelessly lives safely,  
But he who walks a crooked path will be found out. ^10-9

**10:10** He who winks his eye causes sorrow;  
He whose speech is foolish comes to grief. ^10-10

**10:11** The mouth of the righteous is a fountain of life,  
But lawlessness covers the mouth of the wicked. ^10-11

**10:12** Hatred stirs up strife,  
But love covers up all faults. ^10-12

**10:13** Wisdom is to be found on the lips of the intelligent,  
But a rod is ready for the back of the senseless. ^10-13

**10:14** The wise store up knowledge;  
The mouth of the fool is an imminent ruin. ^10-14

**10:15** The wealth of a rich man is his fortress;  
The poverty of the poor is his ruin. ^10-15

**10:16** The labor of the righteous man makes for life;  
The produce of the wicked man makes for want. ^10-16

**10:17** He who follows discipline shows the way to life,  
But he who ignores reproof leads astray. ^10-17

**10:18** He who conceals hatred has lying lips,  
While he who speaks forth slander is a dullard. ^10-18

**10:19** Where there is much talking, there is no lack of transgressing,  
But he who curbs his tongue[^1] shows sense. ^10-19

**10:20** The tongue of a righteous man is choice silver,  
But the mind of the wicked is of little worth. ^10-20

**10:21** The lips of the righteous sustain many,  
But fools die for lack of sense. ^10-21

**10:22** It is the blessing of the LORD that enriches,  
And no toil can increase it. ^10-22

**10:23** As mischief is sport for the dullard,  
So is wisdom for the man of understanding. ^10-23

**10:24** What the wicked man plots overtakes him;  
What the righteous desire is granted. ^10-24

**10:25** When the storm passes the wicked man is gone,  
But the righteous is an everlasting foundation. ^10-25

**10:26** Like vinegar to the teeth,  
Like smoke to the eyes,  
Is a lazy man to those who send him on a mission. ^10-26

**10:27** The fear of the LORD prolongs life,  
While the years of the wicked will be shortened. ^10-27

**10:28** The righteous can look forward to joy,  
But the hope of the wicked is doomed. ^10-28

**10:29** The way of the LORD is a stronghold for the blameless,  
But a ruin for evildoers. ^10-29

**10:30** The righteous will never be shaken;  
The wicked will not inhabit the earth. ^10-30

**10:31** The mouth of the righteous produces wisdom,  
But the treacherous tongue shall be cut off. ^10-31

**10:32** The lips of the righteous know what is pleasing;  
The mouth of the wicked [knows] duplicity. ^10-32

[^1]: Lit. “lips.”
