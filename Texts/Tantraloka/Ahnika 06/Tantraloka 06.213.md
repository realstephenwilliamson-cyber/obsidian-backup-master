## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 13 - Chapter Six, part 6#^pg437|p. 422]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=437>))
>
> The Time within the Equalizing Breath (samāna) has been taught. Now (the flow of Time) within the Ascending Breath (udāna) will be described. What has been said with regards to the (temporal) pervasion of the exhaled breath (prāṇa) applies here to the Ascending Breath. The only (difference) is that the place within (which prāṇa flows is the End of the Twelve of) the nose, (whereas) the Upper Abode (of the Ascending Breath) is above the Cavity of Brahmā (in the location of the End of the Twelve) in the extremity of Śakti (śaktyanta). Thus, the entire (span of) Time (ranging from) a period of one tuṭi to sixty years should also be observed within the Ascending Breath, (as it travels) from the Heart to the End of the Twelve of the head. (212-214ab) (21160-213) (with 6.212–213)

## Viveka

> [!viveka]- Viveka in English (Dyczkowski, vol. IV, pp. 422–423)

> प्राणव्याप्तौ यदुक्तं तदुदानेऽप्यत्र केवलम् ।
> नासाशक्त्यन्तयोः स्थाने ब्रह्मरन्ध्रेर्ध्वधामनी ॥
> तेनोदानेऽत्र हृदयान्मूर्धन्यद्वादशान्तगम् ।
> तुट्यादिषष्ठिवर्षान्तं विश्वं काले विचारयेत् ॥

यदि परमत्रानयोः प्राणोदानयोरियान्विशेषो यत्प्राणस्य ब्रह्मरन्ध्रवर्तिनासिक्यद्वादशान्तमुदयस्थानमस्य तु ऊर्ध्वधामवर्तिशक्तिद्वादशान्तमिति । तेनेति, शक्तिद्वादशान्तं यावदुदानव्याप्तेः ॥ २१३ ॥
