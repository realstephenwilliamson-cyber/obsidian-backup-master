## Mūla

एतज्ज्ञात्वा परित्यज्यं सर्वसिद्धिफलं लभेत् ।
तत्रेशः सर्वकृच्छान्तः सर्वज्ञः सर्वकृत्प्रभुः ॥ ^mula

etaj jñātvā parityajyaṁ sarvasiddhiphalaṁ labhet |
tatreśaḥ sarvakṛc chāntaḥ sarvajñaḥ sarvakṛtprabhuḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2022 – June 2022
> p. 41 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=17>))
>
> *It is said that Impurity and Karma, Māyīya and Āṇava (Impurity) and all the universe should all be abandoned*.[^43] It has been determined that this is what needs to be known. Having known that and given it up, one obtains the fruit which is all the accomplishments (*siddhi*). (with 1.16–17ab)

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2022 – June 2022
> p. 41 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=17>))
>
> **The Characteristics of the Lord**
>
> There (in that context), the (sovereign) Lord (*īśvara*), who does everything, is tranquil; the (prevailing) Lord (*prabhu*), who does everything, is omniscient. Infinite (*ananta*), he is (both) (immanent) 'with parts' (*sakala*) and (transcendent) 'without parts' (*niṣkala*), and his power is also such. (with 1.17cd–18ab)

[^43]: 1/16abc in the edition of the MV reads: malaḥ karma ca māyā ca māyīyam akhilaṁ jagat / *sarvaṁ heyam iti proktaṁ* A variant version of 16abc is quoted in TĀv ad 1/28-30. It reads malaṁ karma ca māyīyamāṇavam akhilaṁ ca yat / sarvaṁ heyam iti proktaṁ ‘It is said that every Impurity, (whether it be) Karma, Māyīya or Āṇava, should all be abandoned’
