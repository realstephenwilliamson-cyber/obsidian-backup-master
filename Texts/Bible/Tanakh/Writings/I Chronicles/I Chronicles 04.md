## Chapter 4

**4:1** The sons of Judah: Perez, Hezron, Carmi, Hur, and Shobal. ^4-1

**4:2** Reaiah son of Shobal begot Jahath, and Jahath begot Ahumai and Lahad. These were the families of the Zorathites. ^4-2

**4:3** These were [the sons of] the father of Etam: Jezreel, Ishma, and Idbash; and the name of their sister was Hazlelponi, ^4-3

**4:4** and Penuel was the father of Gedor, and Ezer the father of Hushah. These were the sons of Hur, the first-born of Ephrathah, the father of Bethlehem. ^4-4

**4:5** Ashhur the father of Tekoa had two wives, Helah and Naarah; ^4-5

**4:6** Naarah bore him Ahuzam, Hepher, Temeni, and Ahashtari. These were the sons of Naarah. ^4-6

**4:7** The sons of Helah: Zereth, Zohar, and Ethnan. ^4-7

**4:8** Koz was the father of Anub, Zobebah, and the families of Aharhel son of Harum. ^4-8

**4:9** Jabez was more esteemed than his brothers; and his mother named him Jabez, “Because,” she said, “I bore him in pain.”[^1] ^4-9

**4:10** Jabez invoked the God of Israel, saying, “Oh, bless me, enlarge my territory, stand by me, and make me not suffer pain from misfortune!” And God granted what he asked. ^4-10

**4:11** Chelub the brother of Shuhah begot Mehir, who was the father of Eshton. ^4-11

**4:12** Eshton begot Bethrapha, Paseah, and Tehinnah father of Ir-nahash. These were the men of Recah. ^4-12

**4:13** The sons of Kenaz: Othniel and Seraiah; and the sons of Othniel: Hathath ^4-13

**4:14** and Meonothai. He begot Ophrah. Seraiah begot Joab father of Ge-harashim,[^2] so-called because they were craftsmen. ^4-14

**4:15** The sons of Caleb son of Jephunneh: Iru, Elah, and Naam; and the sons of Elah: Kenaz. ^4-15

**4:16** The sons of Jehallelel: Ziph, Ziphah, Tiria, and Asarel. ^4-16

**4:17** The sons of Ezrah: Jether, Mered, Epher, and Jalon. She[^3] conceived and bore Miriam, Shammai, and Ishbah father of Eshtemoa. ^4-17

**4:18** And his Judahite wife bore Jered father of Gedor, Heber father of Soco, and Jekuthiel father of Zanoah. These were the sons of Bithiah daughter of Pharaoh, whom Mered married. ^4-18

**4:19** The sons of the wife of Hodiah sister of Naham were the fathers of Keilah the Garmite and Eshtemoa the Maacathite. ^4-19

**4:20** The sons of Shimon: Amnon, Rinnah, Ben-hanan, and Tilon. The sons of Ishi: Zoheth and Ben-zoheth. ^4-20

**4:21** The sons of Shelah son of Judah: Er father of Lecah, Laadah father of Mareshah, and the families of the linen factory at Beth-ashbea; ^4-21

**4:22** and Jokim, and the men of Cozeba and Joash, and Saraph, who married into Moab and Jashubi Lehem (the records are ancient). ^4-22

**4:23** These were the potters who dwelt at Netaim and Gederah; they dwelt there in the king’s service. ^4-23

**4:24** The sons of Simeon: Nemuel, Jamin, Jarib, Zerah, Shaul; ^4-24

**4:25** his son Shallum, his son Mibsam, his son Mishma. ^4-25

**4:26** The sons of Mishma: his son Hammuel, his son Zaccur, his son Shimei. ^4-26

**4:27** Shimei had sixteen sons and six daughters; but his brothers had not many children; in all, their families were not as prolific as the Judahites. ^4-27

**4:28** They dwelt in Beersheba, Moladah, Hazar-shual, ^4-28

**4:29** Bilhah, Ezem, Tolad, ^4-29

**4:30** Bethuel, Hormah, Ziklag, ^4-30

**4:31** Beth-marcaboth, Hazar-susim, Beth-biri, and Shaaraim. These were their towns until David became king, ^4-31

**4:32** together with their villages, Etam, Ain, Rimmon, Tochen, and Ashan—five towns, ^4-32

**4:33** along with all their villages that were around these towns as far as Baal; such were their settlements.  
Registered in their genealogy were: ^4-33

**4:34** Meshobab, Jamlech, Joshah son of Amaziah, ^4-34

**4:35** Joel, Jehu son of Joshibiah son of Seraiah son of Asiel. ^4-35

**4:36** Elioenai, Jaakobah, Jeshohaiah, Asaiah, Adiel, Jesimiel, Benaiah, ^4-36

**4:37** Ziza son of Shiphi son of Allon son of Jedaiah son of Shimri son of Shemaiah— ^4-37

**4:38** these mentioned by name were chiefs in their families, and their clans increased greatly. ^4-38

**4:39** They went to the approaches to Gedor, to the eastern side of the valley, in search of pasture for their flocks. ^4-39

**4:40** They found rich, good pasture, and the land was ample, quiet, and peaceful. The former inhabitants were of Ham; ^4-40

**4:41** those recorded by name came in the days of King Hezekiah of Judah, and attacked their encampments and the Meunim who were found there, and wiped them out forever, and settled in their place, because there was pasture there for their flocks. ^4-41

**4:42** And some of them, five hundred of the Simeonites, went to Mount Seir, with Pelatiah, Neariah, Rephaiah, and Uzziel, sons of Ishi, at their head, ^4-42

**4:43** and they destroyed the last surviving Amalekites, and they live there to this day. ^4-43

[^1]: Heb. ‘oṣeb, connected with “Jabez.”
[^2]: Lit. “the valley of the craftsmen.”
[^3]: Apparently Bithiah; cf. v. 18.
