# Galatians 1 (NRSVue)

Compare: [[Galatians 01|BSB and SBLGNT]] · [[DRB Galatians 01|Douay-Rheims]] · [[JANT Galatians 01|JANT notes]]

## Salutation

**1:1** Paul an apostle—sent neither by human commission nor from human authorities but through Jesus Christ and God the Father, who raised him from the dead— ^1-1

**1:2** and all the brothers and sisters with me, To the churches of Galatia: ^1-2

**1:3** Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ, ^1-3

**1:4** who gave himself for our sins to set us free from the present evil age, according to the will of our God and Father, ^1-4

**1:5** to whom be the glory forever and ever. Amen. ^1-5

## There is no other gospel

**1:6** I am astonished that you are so quickly deserting the one who called you in the grace of Christ and are turning to a different gospel— ^1-6

**1:7** not that there is another gospel, but there are some who are confusing you and want to pervert the gospel of Christ. ^1-7

**1:8** But even if we or an angel^[Or *a messenger*] from heaven should proclaim to you a gospel contrary to what we proclaimed to you, let that one be accursed! ^1-8

**1:9** As we have said before, so now I repeat, if anyone proclaims to you a gospel contrary to what you received, let that one be accursed! ^1-9

**1:10** Am I now seeking human approval or God’s approval? Or am I trying to please people? If I were still pleasing people, I would not be a servant of Christ. ^1-10

## Paul’s vindication of his apostleship

**1:11** For I want you to know, brothers and sisters, that the gospel that was proclaimed by me is not of human origin, ^1-11

**1:12** for I did not receive it from a human source, nor was I taught it, but I received it through a revelation of Jesus Christ. ^1-12

**1:13** You have heard, no doubt, of my earlier life in Judaism. I was violently persecuting the church of God and was trying to destroy it. ^1-13

**1:14** I advanced in Judaism beyond many among my people of the same age, for I was far more zealous for the traditions of my ancestors. ^1-14

**1:15** But when the one^[Other ancient authorities read *God*] who had set me apart before I was born and called me through his grace was pleased ^1-15

**1:16** to reveal his Son to me,^[Gk *in me*] so that I might proclaim him among the gentiles, I did not confer with any human, ^1-16

**1:17** nor did I go up to Jerusalem to those who were already apostles before me, but I went away at once into Arabia, and afterward I returned to Damascus. ^1-17

**1:18** Then after three years I did go up to Jerusalem to visit Cephas and stayed with him fifteen days, ^1-18

**1:19** but I did not see any other apostle except James the Lord’s brother. ^1-19

**1:20** In what I am writing to you, before God, I do not lie! ^1-20

**1:21** Then I went into the regions of Syria and Cilicia, ^1-21

**1:22** and I was still unknown by sight to the churches of Judea that are in Christ; ^1-22

**1:23** they only heard it said, “The one who formerly was persecuting us is now proclaiming the faith he once tried to destroy.” ^1-23

**1:24** And they glorified God because of me. ^1-24
