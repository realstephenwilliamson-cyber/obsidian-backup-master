# Psalms 10 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 09|← Psalms 9]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 11|Psalms 11 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 10|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**10:1** Unto the end. A psalm for David. ^10-1

**10:2** In the Lord I put my trust: how then do you say to my soul: Get thee away from hence to the mountain like a sparrow? ^10-2

**10:3** For, lo, the wicked have bent their bow; they have prepared their arrows in the quiver; to shoot in the dark the upright of heart. ^10-3

**10:4** For they have destroyed the things which thou hast made: but what has the just man done? ^10-4

**10:5** The Lord is in his holy temple, the Lord’s throne is in heaven. His eyes look on the poor man: his eyelids examine the sons of men. ^10-5

**10:6** The Lord trieth the just and the wicked: but he that loveth iniquity hateth his own soul. ^10-6

**10:7** He shall rain snares upon sinners: fire and brimstone and storms of winds shall be the portion of their cup. ^10-7

**10:8** For the Lord is just, and hath loved justice: his countenance hath beheld righteousness. ^10-8
