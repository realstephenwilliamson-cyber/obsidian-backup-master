## Mūla

अथोच्यते शिवेनोक्तः पवित्रकविधिः स्फुटः ।
श्रीरत्नमालात्रिशिरःशास्त्रयोः सूचितः पुनः ॥ ^mula

athocyate śivenoktaḥ pavitrakavidhiḥ sphuṭaḥ |
śrīratnamālātriśiraḥśāstrayoḥ sūcitaḥ punaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 04 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 2#^pg77|p. 60]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=77>))
>
> We will now talk about the procedure (used to perform the rite of the offering) of the sacred thread (pavitra). It is clearly taught by Śiva in the venerable Ratnamālā and in the Triśsirahśāstra, whereas it is (only) mentioned briefly in the teachings of the venerable Siddhā, Aṭana (i.e. Niśātaṇa), Sadbhāva (i.e. Tantrasadbhāva)' and Mālinīsāra. There (out of these), we will primarily (expound) the procedure taught in the venerable Mālā. (with 28.112–113)
