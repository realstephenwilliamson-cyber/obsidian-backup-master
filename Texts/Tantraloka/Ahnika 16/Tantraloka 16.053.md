## Mūla

चक्रजुष्टश्च तत्रैव स वीरपशुरुच्यते ।
यस्त्वन्यत्रापि निहतः सामस्त्येनांशतोऽपि वा ॥ ^mula

cakrajuṣṭaśca tatraiva sa vīrapaśurucyate |
yastvanyatrāpi nihataḥ sāmastyenāṃśato'pi vā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 05 - Chapter Sixteen, part 1#^pg43|p. 23]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=43>))
>
> 8) The sacrificial animal (vīrapaśu) that has been introduced into the sacrificial surface, killed, (its union with iva) accomplished (sādhita) (with yoga and Mantras) there itself, and is pleasing to the circle (of goddesses) (cakrajuṣta) (to whom it is offered as their sacrificial meal), is said to be ‘heroic’. (with 16.52cd–53ab)
>
> (The sacrificial animal that is killed) ‘there itself’, on the sacrificial area, ‘is pleasing’, that is, consumed (by the circle of deities and initiates). The (animal) that is killed elsewhere should also be offered to the deity, whether completely or (just) in part. That one is considered an external sacrificial animal (which is outside the eight kinds) (bāhyapaśu). (with 16.53cd–54ab)
