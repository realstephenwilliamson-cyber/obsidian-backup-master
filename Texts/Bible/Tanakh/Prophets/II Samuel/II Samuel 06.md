## Chapter 6

**6:1** David again assembled all the picked men of Israel, thirty thousand strong. ^6-1

**6:2** [^1]Then David and all the troops that were with him set out from Baalim[^2] of Judah to bring up from there the Ark of God to which the Name was attached, the name LORD of Hosts Enthroned on the Cherubim. ^6-2

**6:3** They loaded the Ark of God onto a new cart and conveyed it from the house of Abinadab, which was on the hill; and Abinadab’s sons, Uzza and Ahio, guided the [^3]new cart. ^6-3

**6:4** They conveyed it from Abinadab’s house on the hill, [Uzza walking][^4] alongside the Ark of God and Ahio walking in front of the Ark. ^6-4

**6:5** Meanwhile, David and all the House of Israel danced before the LORD to [the sound of] all kinds of cypress wood [instruments],[^5] with lyres, harps, timbrels, sistrums, and cymbals. ^6-5

**6:6** But when they came to the threshing floor of Nacon, Uzzah reached out for the Ark of God and grasped it, for the oxen had stumbled.[^6] ^6-6

**6:7** The LORD was incensed at Uzzah. And God struck him down on the spot for his indiscretion,[^7] and he died there beside the Ark of God. ^6-7

**6:8** David was distressed because the LORD had inflicted a breach upon Uzzah; and that place was named Perez-uzzah,[^8] as it is still called. ^6-8

**6:9** David was afraid of the LORD that day; he said, “How can I let the Ark of the LORD come to me?” ^6-9

**6:10** So David would not bring the Ark of the LORD to his place in the City of David; instead, David diverted it to the house of Obed-edom the Gittite. ^6-10

**6:11** The Ark of the LORD remained in the house of Obed-edom the Gittite three months, and the LORD blessed Obed-edom and his whole household. ^6-11

**6:12** It was reported to King David: “The LORD has blessed Obed-edom’s house and all that belongs to him because of the Ark of God.” [^9]Thereupon David went and brought up the Ark of God from the house of Obed-edom to the City of David, amid rejoicing. ^6-12

**6:13** When the bearers of the Ark of the LORD had moved forward six paces, he sacrificed an ox and a fatling.[^10] ^6-13

**6:14** David whirled with all his might before the LORD; David was girt with a linen ephod. ^6-14

**6:15** Thus David and all the House of Israel brought up the Ark of the LORD with shouts and with blasts of the horn. ^6-15

**6:16** As the Ark of the LORD entered the City of David, Michal daughter of Saul looked out of the window and saw King David leaping and whirling before the LORD; and she despised him for it. ^6-16

**6:17** They brought in the Ark of the LORD and set it up in its place inside the tent which David had pitched for it, and David sacrificed burnt offerings and offerings of well-being before the LORD. ^6-17

**6:18** When David finished sacrificing the burnt offerings and the offerings of well-being, he blessed the people in the name of the LORD of Hosts. ^6-18

**6:19** And he distributed among all the people—the entire multitude of Israel, man and woman alike—to each a loaf of bread, a cake made in a pan, and a raisin cake.[^11] Then all the people left for their homes. ^6-19

**6:20** David went home to greet his household. And Michal daughter of Saul came out to meet David and said, “Didn’t the king of Israel do himself honor today—exposing himself today in the sight of the slavegirls of his subjects, as one of the riffraff might expose himself!” ^6-20

**6:21** David answered Michal, “It was before the LORD who chose me instead of your father and all his family and appointed me ruler over the LORD’s people Israel! I will dance before the LORD ^6-21

**6:22** and dishonor myself even more, and be low in my own[^12] esteem; but among the slavegirls that you speak of I will be honored.” ^6-22

**6:23** So to her dying day Michal daughter of Saul had no children. ^6-23

[^1]: Vv. 2–12 are found also in 1 Chron. 13.5–14, with variations.
[^2]: Identical with Baalah, another name for Kiriath-jearim, where the Ark had been kept (cf. 1 Sam. 6.21; 1 Chron. 13.6; Josh. 15.9).
[^3]: Septuagint and 4QSama read “cart alongside.”
[^4]: Cf. vv. 6–7.
[^5]: Cf. Kimhi; the parallel passage 1 Chron. 13.8 reads “with all their might and with songs.”
[^6]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^7]: So Targum; 1 Chron. 13.10 reads “because he had laid a hand on the Ark.”
[^8]: I.e., “the Breach of Uzzah”; cf. 5.20 and note.
[^9]: Vv. 12b–14 are found, with variations, in 1 Chron. 15.25–27; vv. 15–19a, with variations, in 1 Chron. 15.28–16.3; vv. 19b–20a, with variations, in 1 Chron. 16.43.
[^10]: 4QSama reads “seven oxen and seven [rams]”; cf. 1 Chron. 15.26.
[^11]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^12]: Septuagint reads “your.”
