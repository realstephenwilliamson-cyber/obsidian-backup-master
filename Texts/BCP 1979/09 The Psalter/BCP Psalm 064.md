# Psalm 64

← [[BCP Psalm 063]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 065]] →

*Exaudi, Deus* · Book Two · Twelfth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 064]]

**64:1** Hear my voice, O God, when I complain; \*  
protect my life from fear of the enemy. ^64-1

**64:2** Hide me from the conspiracy of the wicked, \*  
from the mob of evildoers. ^64-2

**64:3** They sharpen their tongue like a sword, \*  
and aim their bitter words like arrows, ^64-3

**64:4** That they may shoot down the blameless from ambush; \*  
they shoot without warning and are not afraid. ^64-4

**64:5** They hold fast to their evil course; \*  
they plan how they may hide their snares. ^64-5

**64:6** They say, "Who will see us?  
who will find out our crimes? \*  
we have thought out a perfect plot." ^64-6

**64:7** The human mind and heart are a mystery; \*  
but God will loose an arrow at them,  
and suddenly they will be wounded. ^64-7

[p. 672]

**64:8** He will make them trip over their tongues, \*  
and all who see them will shake their heads. ^64-8

**64:9** Everyone will stand in awe and declare God's deeds; \*  
they will recognize his works. ^64-9

**64:10** The righteous will rejoice in the LORD and put their trust in him, \*  
and all who are true of heart will glory. ^64-10
