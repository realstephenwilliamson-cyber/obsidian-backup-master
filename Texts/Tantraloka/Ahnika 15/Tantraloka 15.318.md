## Mūla

पूजकः परतत्त्वात्मा पूज्यं तत्त्वं परापरम् ।
सृष्टत्वादपरं तत्त्वजालमासनतास्पदम् ॥ ^mula

pūjakaḥ paratattvātmā pūjyaṃ tattvaṃ parāparam |
sṛṣṭatvādaparaṃ tattvajālamāsanatāspadam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 06 - Chapter Fifteen, part 5#^pg209|p. 191]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=209>))
>
> The worshipper is the supreme reality (paratattva). The object of worship is emitted, and so is the intermediate (parāpara) reality. The lower (apara) (aspect of the one reality) is the aggregate of principles, which is the location of the Throne.
