## Judgment on the deceitful

*To the leader. A Maskil of David, when Doeg the Edomite came to Saul and said to him, “David has*

*come to the house of Ahimelech.”*

**52:1** Why do you boast, O mighty one, of mischief done against the godly?^[Cn Compare Syr: Heb *the kindness of God*] All day long ^52-1

**52:2** you are plotting destruction. Your tongue is like a sharp razor, you worker of treachery. ^52-2

**52:3** You love evil more than good and lying more than speaking the truth. *Selah* ^52-3

**52:4** You love all words that devour, O deceitful tongue. ^52-4

**52:5** But God will break you down forever; he will snatch and tear you from your tent; he will uproot you from the land of the living. *Selah* ^52-5

**52:6** The righteous will see and fear and will laugh at the evildoer,^[Heb *him*] saying, ^52-6

**52:7** “See the one who would not take refuge in God but trusted in abundant riches and sought refuge in wealth!”^[Syr Tg: Heb *in his destruction*] ^52-7

**52:8** But I am like a green olive tree in the house of God. I trust in the steadfast love of God forever and ever. ^52-8

**52:9** I will thank you forever because of what you have done. In the presence of the faithful I will proclaim^[Cn: Heb *wait for*] your name, for it is good. ^52-9
