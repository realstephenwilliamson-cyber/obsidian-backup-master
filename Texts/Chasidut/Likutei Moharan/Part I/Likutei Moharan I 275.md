# Likutei Moharan I 275

← [[Likutei Moharan I 274]] · [[Likutei Moharan]] · [[Likutei Moharan I 276]] →

*Part I · Lesson 275* · [[Likutei Moharan]]

## 1

Know! a candle is made from each and every mitzvah. When a person passes away, if [his] is a great soul that is very precious in God’s eyes, then they allow her to search in the King’s treasury. He is allowed to search and to take whatever he wants from the treasuries of the Blessed King. This is the ultimate pleasure of the World to Come.

In order to search one needs candles, as our Sages, of blessed memory, said: Searching is with candles ([[Pesachim 7#7b|Pesachim 7b]]). They learned this from the verse, “The soul of man is the candle of God, [with which] He searches [man’s] innermost depths” ([[Proverbs 20#^20-27|Proverbs 20:27]]). And the candles are made from the mitzvot, as mentioned above, in the aspect of “A mitzvah is a candle” ([[Proverbs 06#^6-23|Proverbs 6:23]]). It is with these candles that, after passing away, the soul searches in the King’s treasury.

This is the aspect of “Among the dead, *chaphshi* (who are exempt)” ([[Psalms 088#^88-6|Psalms 88:6]]) ; because once a person dies he is exempted from the mitzvot ([[Shabbat 151#151b|Shabbat 151b]]). *ChaPhShi* is the aspect of the *ChiPooS* (search) that is made from the mitzvot, for the mitzvot are candles by which the search is made, as explained above.

Nevertheless, there is a tzaddik who sacrifices himself [to God] during his lifetime, so that even while still alive he searches in his Father’s treasuries.
