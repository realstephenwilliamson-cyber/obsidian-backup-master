## Mūla

समय्यधिकृतोऽन्यत्र गुरुणा विभुमर्चयेत् ।
तमापादितरुद्रांशं समयान् श्रावयेद् गुरुः ॥ ^mula

samayyadhikṛto'nyatra guruṇā vibhumarcayet |
tamāpāditarudrāṃśaṃ samayān śrāvayed guruḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 09 - Chapter Fifteen, part 8#^pg334|p. 316]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=334>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 15.523cd–524ab.
>
> A regular initiate who has been rendered fit to regularly study, hear the scriptures, (to perform regular daily) rite (pūjā) and serve the teacher, should, on other occasions, worship the All-pervasive Lord with (his) teacher." (with 15.520cd–521ab)
>
> (Now) the teacher should tell (the disciple, who has thus by his devotion) become Rudra’s limb (rudrāṁśa), the sixty-four pledges (samaya) described in the Devyāyāmala and other (scriptures). There are eight groups of pledges (samayāṣṭaka) (each of which consists of) eight (rules). (They are:) 1) what is not to said (avāda), 2) what is not to be done (akaraṇa), 3) (what is to be) kept hidden (gūḍhi), 4) (what should be) worshipped (pūjā), 5) (to whom is offered) libation (tarpaṇa), 6) (whom one should) respect (bhāvana), 7) (what one should) strike down (hanana), and 8) (who is to be) driven mad (mohana). (with 15.521cd–523ab)
