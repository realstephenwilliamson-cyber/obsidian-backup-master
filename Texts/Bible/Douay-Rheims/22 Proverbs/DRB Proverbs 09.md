# Proverbs 9 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Proverbs 08|← Proverbs 8]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Proverbs 10|Proverbs 10 →]]

**9:1** Wisdom hath built herself a house, she hath hewn her out seven pillars. ^9-1

**9:2** She hath slain her victims, mingled her wine, and set forth her table. ^9-2

**9:3** She hath sent her maids to invite to the tower, and to the walls of the city: ^9-3

**9:4** Whosoever is a little one, let him come to me. And to the unwise she said: ^9-4

**9:5** Come, eat my bread, and drink the wine which I have mingled for you. ^9-5

**9:6** Forsake childishness, and live, and walk by the ways of prudence. ^9-6

**9:7** He that teacheth a scorner, doth an injury to himself: and he that rebuketh a wicked man, getteth himself a blot. ^9-7

**9:8** Rebuke not a scorner lest he hate thee. Rebuke a wise man, and he will love thee. ^9-8

**9:9** Give an occasion to a wise man, and wisdom shall be added to him. Teach a just man, and he shall make haste to receive it. ^9-9

**9:10** The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom: and the knowledge of the holy is prudence. ^9-10

**9:11** For by me shall thy days be multiplied, and years of life shall be added to thee. ^9-11

**9:12** If thou be wise, thou shalt be so to thyself: and if a scorner, thou alone shalt bear the evil. ^9-12

**9:13** A foolish woman and clamorous, and full of allurements, and knowing nothing at all, ^9-13

**9:14** Sat at the door of her house, upon a seat, in a high place of the city, ^9-14

**9:15** To call them that pass by the way, and go on their journey: ^9-15

**9:16** He that is a little one, let him turn to me. And to the fool she said: ^9-16

**9:17** Stolen waters are sweeter, and hid den bread is more pleasant. ^9-17

**9:18** And he did not know that giants are there, and that her guests are in the depths of hell. ^9-18
