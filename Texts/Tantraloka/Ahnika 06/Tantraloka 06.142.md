## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 11 - Chapter Six, part 4#^pg379|p. 364]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=379>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 6.142cd–143ab.
>
> Kālāgni (the Fire of Time) initially manifests from the hells. Thus, (although) the Lord is all-pervasive (vibhu), he is also situated below (at the bottom of the hell worlds). This is the teaching of the venerable Raurava. (with 6.141cd–142ab)
>
> Once the ashes (of the universe consumed by the Fire of Time) have been swept away by Brahmā’s yawn, the universe is inundated by the water of his sweat. Such is (its) condition when the night (of universal destruction) comes. (with 6.142cd–143ab)
