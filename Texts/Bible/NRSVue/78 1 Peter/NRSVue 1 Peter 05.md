## Tending the flock of God

**5:1** Now as an elder myself and a witness of the sufferings of Christ, as well as one who shares in the glory to be revealed, I exhort the elders among you ^5-1

**5:2** to tend the flock of God that is in your charge, exercising the oversight,^[Other ancient authorities lack *exercising the oversight*] not under compulsion but willingly, as God would have you do it,^[Or *in a godly manner*; other ancient authorities lack *as God would have you do it*] not for sordid gain but eagerly. ^5-2

**5:3** Do not lord it over those in your charge, but be examples to the flock. ^5-3

**5:4** And when the chief shepherd appears, you will win the crown of glory that never fades away. ^5-4

**5:5** In the same way, you who are younger must be subject to the elders.^[Or *of those who are older*] And all of you must clothe yourselves with humility in your dealings with one another, for “God opposes the proud but gives grace to the humble.” ^5-5

**5:6** Humble yourselves, therefore, under the mighty hand of God, so that he may exalt you in due time. ^5-6

**5:7** Cast all your anxiety on him, because he cares for you. ^5-7

**5:8** Discipline yourselves; keep alert. Like a roaring lion your adversary the devil prowls around, looking for someone to devour. ^5-8

**5:9** Resist him, steadfast in your faith, for you know that your brothers and sisters in all the world are undergoing the same kinds of suffering. ^5-9

**5:10** And after you have suffered for a little while, the God of all grace, who has called you to his eternal glory in Christ,^[Other ancient authorities read *Christ Jesus*] will himself restore, support, strengthen, and establish you. ^5-10

**5:11** To him be the power^[Other ancient authorities read *glory and power*] forever and ever. Amen. ^5-11

## Final greetings and benediction

**5:12** Through Silvanus, whom I consider a faithful brother, I have written this short letter to encourage you and to testify that this is the true grace of God. Stand fast in it. ^5-12

**5:13** Your sister church^[Gk *She who is*] in Babylon, chosen together with you, sends you greetings, and so does my son Mark. ^5-13

**5:14** Greet one another with a kiss of love. Peace to all of you who are in Christ.^[Other ancient authorities add *Jesus* or *Amen* or *Jesus. Amen*] ^5-14
