## Mūla

तद्वन्नासापयोभ्यां तु कल्प्यौ विष्णुप्रजापती ।
स्वरावाद्यतृतीयौ तु वाचकौ पद्मचक्रयोः ॥ ^mula

tadvan nāsāpayobhyāṁ tu kalpyau viṣṇuprajāpatī |
svarāv ādyatṛtīyau tu vācakau padmacakrayoḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2023 – March 2023
> p. 53 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=53>))
>
> Viṣṇu and Prajāpati should be conceived in the same way with the Nose (Ī) and the Milk (Ā). The first and and third vowel (A, I) denote (their weapons) the discus (IṀ) and the lotus (AṀ).[^58]

[^58]: Quoted in TĀv ad 30/60-64ab. MV 3/66-67 is paraphrased as TĀ 30/42-43ab. The text reads in both the edition of the MV and Jayaratha’s citation of it: *svarāv ādyatṛtīyau tu vācakau padmacakrayoḥ* (MV 3/67cd). Read as in the KSTS edition This line literally says: ‘The first and third vowel (A, I) denote (their weapons), the lotus and the discus.’ Viṣṇu is represented by ĪṀ and Brahmā by ĀṀ. If we accept this reading as it stands that would mean that their respective would be denotes by AṀ and IṀ, respectively. However, the weapons of all the other guardians are represented by their corresponding short vowels. Thus, for example, Indra is LĀṀ and his weapon is LAṀ. There seems to be no reason of this anomoly. Accordingly, we may reverse the order of the words in the text, which we can easily do without compromising the metre, to read *cakrapadmayoḥ* instead of *padmacakrayoḥ*. Or else assume that the stated order does not correspond and assign AṀ to the weapon of Prajāpati who is ĀṀ and IṀ to that of Viṣṇu who is ĪṀ.
