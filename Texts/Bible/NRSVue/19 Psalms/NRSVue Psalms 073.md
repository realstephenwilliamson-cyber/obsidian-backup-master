## Plea for relief from oppressors

*A Psalm of Asaph.*

**73:1** Truly God is good to Israel, to those who are pure in heart. ^73-1

**73:2** But as for me, my feet had almost stumbled; my steps had nearly slipped. ^73-2

**73:3** For I was envious of the arrogant; I saw the prosperity of the wicked. ^73-3

**73:4** For they have no pain; their bodies are sound and sleek. ^73-4

**73:5** They are not in trouble as others are; they are not plagued like other people. ^73-5

**73:6** Therefore pride is their necklace; violence covers them like a garment. ^73-6

**73:7** Their eyes swell out with fatness; their hearts overflow with follies. ^73-7

**73:8** They scoff and speak with malice; loftily they threaten oppression. ^73-8

**73:9** They set their mouths against heaven, and their tongues range over the earth. ^73-9

**73:10** Therefore the people turn and praise them^[Cn: Heb *his people return here*] and find no fault in them.^[Cn: Heb *abundant waters are drained by them*] ^73-10

**73:11** And they say, “How can God know? Is there knowledge in the Most High?” ^73-11

**73:12** Such are the wicked; always at ease, they increase in riches. ^73-12

**73:13** All in vain I have kept my heart clean and washed my hands in innocence. ^73-13

**73:14** For all day long I have been plagued and am punished every morning. ^73-14

**73:15** If I had said, “I will talk on in this way,” I would have been untrue to the circle of your children. ^73-15

**73:16** But when I thought how to understand this, it seemed to me a wearisome task, ^73-16

**73:17** until I went into the sanctuary of God; then I perceived their end. ^73-17

**73:18** Truly you set them in slippery places; you make them fall to ruin. ^73-18

**73:19** How they are destroyed in a moment, swept away utterly by terrors! ^73-19

**73:20** They are^[Cn: Heb *Lord*] like a dream when one awakes; on awaking you despise their phantoms. ^73-20

**73:21** When my soul was embittered, when I was pricked in heart, ^73-21

**73:22** I was stupid and ignorant; I was like a brute beast toward you. ^73-22

**73:23** Nevertheless, I am continually with you; you hold my right hand. ^73-23

**73:24** You guide me with your counsel, and afterward you will receive me with honor.^[Or *to glory*] ^73-24

**73:25** Whom have I in heaven but you? And there is nothing on earth that I desire other than you. ^73-25

**73:26** My flesh and my heart may fail, but God is the strength^[Heb *rock*] of my heart and my portion forever. ^73-26

**73:27** Indeed, those who are far from you will perish; you put an end to those who are false to you. ^73-27

**73:28** But for me it is good to be near God; I have made the Lord GOD my refuge, to tell of all your works. ^73-28
