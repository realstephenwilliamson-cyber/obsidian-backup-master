# 2 Corinthians 2 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB 2 Corinthians 01|← 2 Corinthians 1]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB 2 Corinthians 03|2 Corinthians 3 →]]

Compare: [[2 Corinthians 02|BSB and SBLGNT]]

**2:1** But I determined this with myself, not to come to you again in sorrow. ^2-1

**2:2** For if I make you sorrowful, who is he then that can make me glad, but the same who is made sorrowful by me? ^2-2

**2:3** And I wrote this same to you; that I may not, when I come, have sorrow upon sorrow, from them of whom I ought to rejoice: having confidence in you all, that my joy is the joy of you all. ^2-3

**2:4** For out of much affliction and anguish of heart, I wrote to you with many tears: not that you should be made sorrowful: but that you might know the charity I have more abundantly towards you. ^2-4

**2:5** And if any one have caused grief, he hath not grieved me; but in part, that I may not burden you all. ^2-5

**2:6** To him who is such a one, this rebuke is sufficient, which is given by many: ^2-6

**2:7** So that on the contrary, you should rather forgive him and comfort him, lest perhaps such a one be swallowed up with overmuch sorrow. ^2-7

**2:8** Wherefore, I beseech you, that you would confirm your charity towards him. ^2-8

**2:9** For to this end also did I write, that I may know the experiment of you, whether you be obedient in all things. ^2-9

**2:10** And to whom you have pardoned any thing, I also. For, what I have pardoned, if I have pardoned any thing, for your sakes have I done it in the person of Christ. ^2-10

**2:11** That we be not overreached by Satan. For we are not ignorant of his devices. ^2-11

**2:12** And when I was come to Troas for the gospel of Christ, and a door was opened unto me in the Lord, ^2-12

**2:13** I had no rest in my spirit, because I found not Titus my brother; but bidding them farewell, I went into Macedonia. ^2-13

**2:14** Now thanks be to God, who always maketh us to triumph in Christ Jesus, and manifesteth the odour of his knowledge by us in every place. ^2-14

**2:15** For we are the good odour of Christ unto God, in them that are saved, and in them that perish. ^2-15

**2:16** To the one indeed the odour of death unto death: but to the others the odour of life unto life. And for these things who is so sufficient? ^2-16

**2:17** For we are not as many, adulterating the word of God; but with sincerity, but as from God, before God, in Christ we speak. ^2-17
