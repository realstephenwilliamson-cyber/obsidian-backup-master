## Chapter 47

**47:1** For the leader. Of the Korahites. A psalm. ^47-1

**47:2** All you peoples, clap your hands,  
raise a joyous shout for God. ^47-2

**47:3** For the LORD Most High is awesome,  
great king over all the earth; ^47-3

**47:4** He subjects peoples to us,  
sets nations at our feet. ^47-4

**47:5** He chose our heritage for us,  
the pride of Jacob whom He loved. *Selah*. ^47-5

**47:6** God ascends midst acclamation;  
the LORD, to the blasts of the horn. ^47-6

**47:7** Sing, O sing to God;  
sing, O sing to our king; ^47-7

**47:8** for God is king over all the earth;  
sing a hymn.[^1] ^47-8

**47:9** God reigns over the nations;  
God is seated on His holy throne. ^47-9

**47:10** The great of the peoples are gathered together,  
the retinue of Abraham’s God;  
for the guardians of the earth belong to God;  
He is greatly exalted. ^47-10

[^1]: Heb. maskil, a musical term of uncertain meaning.
