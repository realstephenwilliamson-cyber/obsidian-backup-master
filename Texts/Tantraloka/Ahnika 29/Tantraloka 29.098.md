## Mūla

उपकारि द्वयं तत्र फलमन्यत्तदात्मकम् ।
ओष्ठ्यान्त्यत्रितयासेवी ब्रह्मचारी स उच्यते ॥ ^mula

upakāri dvayaṃ tatra phalamanyattadātmakam |
oṣṭhyāntyatritayāsevī brahmacārī sa ucyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 11 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 3#^pg303|p. 286]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=303>))
>
> Bliss is the supreme Brahman, and that is present in the body in three forms. There, two (namely, meat and wine,) are aids, and the other one (i.e. sexual union) is the fruit, which is of that (blissful) nature. (with 29.97cd–98ab)
>
> A Brahmacāārin is said to be one who makes use of the three that come at the end of the series of the labials. (with 29.98cd)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* एवमेषां ब्रह्ममयत्वादेतदनुष्ठाता ब्रह्मचारीत्युच्यते--इत्याह--

> ओष्ठ्यान्त्यत्रितयासेवी ब्रह्मचारी स उच्यते ॥

ओष्ठ्यः पवर्गः, तस्य अन्त्यो मकारस्तत्त्रितयम्--मद्यमांसमैथुन लक्षणम् ॥ ९८ ॥

ननु

ङ मांसभक्षणे दोषो न मद्ये न च मैथुने ।

प्रवृत्तिरेषा भूतानां निवृत्तिस्तु महाफला ॥"(मनु. ५ ।५६)
