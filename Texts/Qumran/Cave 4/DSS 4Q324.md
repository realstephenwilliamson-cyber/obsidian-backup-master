### Frag. 1

**1** [On the twenty-third of it (the ﬁfth month of the ﬁfth year) is] the entrance of [Eliashib. On the thirtieth of it is the entrance of Jakim.] ^f1-1

**2** [(The day) after the sabbath of Jakim is the ﬁr]st of the si[xth (month). On the seventh of it is the entrance of Huppah.] ^f1-2

**3** [On the four]teenth of it [is the entrance of Jeshebeab.] … On the tw[enty-ﬁrst] ^f1-3

**4** [of it is the entrance of Bilga]h. On the twenty-[eighth of] it is the entrance of Immer. [Day] ^f1-4

**5** [four of Immer is the ﬁ]rst of the seventh. On the fo[u]rth of it is the entrance of He[zir]. ^f1-5

**6** [Day six of] Hezir, which is the tenth of the seventh, is the day [of atonement] ^f1-6

**7** […] for the covenant. On the eleventh of the seventh is the entrance of [Aphses …] ^f1-7
