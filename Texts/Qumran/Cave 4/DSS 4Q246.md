### Col. i

**1** […] settled [up]on him and he fell before the throne ^1-1

**2** [… k]ing for ever. You are angry, and have changed you ^1-2

**3** […] … your vision, and everything that shall come for ever. ^1-3

**4** [… mi]ghty ones, oppression will come upon the earth ^1-4

**5** […] and great slaughter in the provinces ^1-5

**6** […] king of Assyria [and E]gypt ^1-6

**7** […] and he will be great over the earth ^1-7

**8** […] they [will d]o, and all will serve ^1-8

**9** [… gr]eat will he be called and he will be designated by his name. ^1-9

### Col. ii

**1** He will be called son of God, and they will call him son of the Most High. Like the sparks ^2-1

**2** that you saw, so will their kingdom be; they will rule several year[s] over ^2-2

**3** the earth and crush everything; a people will crush another people, and a province another provi[n]ce. ^2-3

**4** Blank Until the people of God arises and makes everyone rest from the sword. Blank ^2-4

**5** His kingdom will be an eternal kingdom, and all his paths in truth. He will jud[ge] ^2-5

**6** the earth in truth and all will make peace. The sword will cease from the earth, ^2-6

**7** and all the provinces will pay him homage. The great God is his strength, ^2-7

**8** he will wage war for him; he will place the peoples in his hand and ^2-8

**9** cast them all away before him. His rule will be an eternal rule, and all the abysses ^2-9
