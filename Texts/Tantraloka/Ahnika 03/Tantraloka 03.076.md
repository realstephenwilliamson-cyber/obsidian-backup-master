## Mūla

ऊनताभासनं संविन्मात्रत्वे जायते तदा ।
रूढं तज्ज्ञेयवर्गस्य स्थितिप्रारम्भ उच्यते ॥ ^mula

ūnatābhāsanaṃ saṃvinmātratve jāyate tadā |
rūḍhaṃ tajjñeyavargasya sthitiprārambha ucyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. II
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 02 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v02 06 - Chapter Three, part 3#^pg188|p. 172]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 02.pdf#page=188>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 3.75cd–76ab.
>
> When the objective aspect, in the process of expansion, is aroused (out of its state of rest within itself), an appearance of a lessening (ūnatā) (of subjectivity) (Ū) takes places within pure consciousness due to the powerful (influence of its arousal). (with 3.75cd–76ab)
>
> When that has developed (rūḍha), it is said to be the beginning of the persistence (sthiti) of objectivity. This state of development (rūḍhi) amounts to the assumption of diverse forms by the ocean of consciousness. Yogis discern this (stage) to be the seed of the weave of duality (bhedasaṁdarbha). (with 3.76cd–77)
