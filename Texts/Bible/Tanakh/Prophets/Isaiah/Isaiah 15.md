## Chapter 15

**15:1** The “Moab” Pronouncement.  
Ah, in the night Ar was sacked,  
Moab was ruined;  
Ah, in the night Kir was sacked,  
Moab was ruined. ^15-1

**15:2** He went up to the temple to weep,  
Dibon[^1] [went] to the outdoor shrines.  
Over Nebo and Medeba  
Moab is wailing;  
On every head is baldness,  
Every beard is shorn. ^15-2

**15:3** In its streets, they are girt with sackcloth;  
On its roofs, in its squares,  
Everyone is wailing,  
Streaming with tears. ^15-3

**15:4** Heshbon and Elealeh cry out,  
Their voice carries to Jahaz.  
Therefore,  
The shock troops of Moab shout,[^2]  
His body is convulsed. ^15-4

**15:5** My heart cries out for Moab—  
His fugitives flee down to Zoar,  
To Eglath-shelishiyah.  
For the ascent of Luhith  
They ascend with weeping;  
On the road to Horonaim  
They raise a cry of anguish. ^15-5

**15:6** Ah, the waters of Nimrim  
Are become a desolation;  
The grass is sear,  
The herbage is gone,  
Vegetation is vanished. ^15-6

**15:7** Therefore,  
The gains they have made, and their stores,  
They carry to the Wadi of Willows. ^15-7

**15:8** Ah, the cry has compassed  
The country of Moab:  
All the way to Eglaim her wailing,  
Even at Beer-elim her wailing! ^15-8

**15:9** Ah, the waters of Dimon are full of blood[^3]  
For I pour added [water] on Dimon;  
I drench[^4] it—for Moab’s refugees—  
With soil[^5] for its remnant. ^15-9

[^1]: Regarded as the principal city of Moab.
[^2]: Change of vocalization yields “The loins of Moab are trembling.”
[^3]: Emendation yields “tears.”
[^4]: Cf. 16.9.
[^5]: Emendation yields “tears”; cf. Ugaritic ’dm‘t.
