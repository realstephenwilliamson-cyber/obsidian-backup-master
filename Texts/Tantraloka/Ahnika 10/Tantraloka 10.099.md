## Mūla

सकला इति तत्कोशषट्कोद्रेकोपलक्षणम् ।
उद्भूताशुद्धचिद्रागकलादिरसकञ्चुकाः ॥ ^mula

sakalā iti tatkośaṣaṭkodrekopalakṣaṇam |
udbhūtāśuddhacidrāgakalādirasakañcukāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 11 - Chapter Ten, part 2#^pg378|p. 359]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=378>))
>
> It is said (in the Mālinīvijayottara) that the Fully Conditioned (sakala) (subjects) possess (the principles) ranging from the Force (of limited agency) up to Earth. (But this is only) a secondary characterization (of these perceivers, which serves to) indicate that, (in their case, all) six coverings (kośa, i.e. kañcuka) have emerged (and are functioning). (with 10.98cd–99ab)
>
> The Fully Conditioned (sakala) (perceivers) are those in which the six coverings, namely, impure Knowledge, Attachment (rāga), the Force (of limited agency) (kalā) and the rest, have emerged (and function) (udbhūta). In the case of those called (Deconditioned by) Dissolution (Pralayākala) (perceivers), all the coverings are in abeyance (nyagbhūta), whereas (in the case of the) Deconditioned by Consciousness ((Vi)jñānākala) (perceivers), these coverings have been destroyed. This is the teaching. (with 10.98cd–100)
