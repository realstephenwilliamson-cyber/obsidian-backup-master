# Psalm 140 (NRSVue)

## Prayer for deliverance from enemies

*To the leader. A Psalm of David.*

**140:1** Deliver me, O LORD, from evildoers; protect me from those who are violent, ^140-1

**140:2** who plan evil things in their minds and stir up wars continually. ^140-2

**140:3** They make their tongue sharp as a snake’s, and under their lips is the venom of vipers. *Selah* ^140-3

**140:4** Guard me, O LORD, from the hands of the wicked; protect me from the violent who have planned my downfall. ^140-4

**140:5** The arrogant have hidden a trap for me, and with cords they have spread a net,^[Or *they have spread cords as a net*] along the road they have set snares for me. *Selah* ^140-5

**140:6** I say to the LORD, “You are my God; give ear, O LORD, to the voice of my supplications.” ^140-6

**140:7** O LORD, my Lord, my strong deliverer, you have covered my head in the day of battle. ^140-7

**140:8** Do not grant, O LORD, the desires of the wicked; do not further their evil plot.^[Heb adds *they are exalted*] *Selah* ^140-8

**140:9** Those who surround me lift up their heads;^[Cn: Heb *those who surround me are uplifted in head*] let the mischief of their lips overwhelm them! ^140-9

**140:10** Let burning coals fall on them! Let them be flung into pits, no more to rise! ^140-10

**140:11** Do not let the slanderer be established in the land; let evil speedily hunt down the violent! ^140-11

**140:12** I know that the LORD maintains the cause of the needy and executes justice for the poor. ^140-12

**140:13** Surely the righteous shall give thanks to your name; the upright shall live in your presence. ^140-13
