## Mūla

यथा यथा हि गगनमुत्पतेत्कलहंसकः ।
जले बिम्बं ब्रुडत्यस्य तथेत्यत्राप्ययं विधिः ॥ ^mula

yathā yathā hi gaganamutpatetkalahaṃsakaḥ |
jale bimbaṃ bruḍatyasya tathetyatrāpyayaṃ vidhiḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 08 - Chapter Fifteen, part 7#^pg284|p. 266]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=284>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 15.433cd–434ab.
>
> According to how a goose (kalahamhsa) flies up into the sky, so does its image (bimba) move (reflected) in water. The same happens here.
