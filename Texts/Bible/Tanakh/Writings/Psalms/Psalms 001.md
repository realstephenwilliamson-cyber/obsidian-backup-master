## Chapter 1

**1:1** Happy is the man who has not followed the counsel of the wicked,  
or taken the path of sinners,  
or joined the company of the insolent; ^1-1

**1:2** rather, the teaching of the LORD is his delight,  
and he studies[^1] that teaching day and night. ^1-2

**1:3** He is like a tree planted beside streams of water,  
which yields its fruit in season,  
whose foliage never fades,  
and whatever it produces thrives.[^2] ^1-3

**1:4** Not so the wicked;  
rather, they are like chaff that wind blows away. ^1-4

**1:5** Therefore the wicked will not survive judgment,  
nor will sinners, in the assembly of the righteous. ^1-5

**1:6** For the LORD cherishes the way of the righteous,  
but the way of the wicked is doomed. ^1-6

[^1]: Or “recites”; lit. “utters.”
[^2]: Or “he does prospers.”
