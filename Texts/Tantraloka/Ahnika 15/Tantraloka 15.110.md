## Mūla

हेतुरित्युभयत्रापि यागौको यन्मनोरमम् ।
नियतिप्राणतायोगात्सामग्रीतस्तु यद्यपि ॥ ^mula

heturityubhayatrāpi yāgauko yanmanoramam |
niyatiprāṇatāyogātsāmagrītastu yadyapi || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 03 - Chapter Fifteen, part 2#^pg92|p. 74]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=92>))
>
> According to (our) teachers however, the cause of both liberation and accomplishment (siddhi) is a pure mind. Thus, in both cases, (the right) place (to perform) the sacrifice is one that is (beautiful and) pleasing to the mind (manorama). (with 15.109cd–110ab)
>
> Even though (some) accomplishments (may be the result) of a combination (of factors, spatial and temporal,) in accord with the power of Necessity (niyati), the purity of one’s disposition (bhāvavaimalya) is still the most excellent of all (of them). (with 15.110cd–111ab)
