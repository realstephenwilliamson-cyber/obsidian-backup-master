## The parable of the lost sheep

**15:1** Now all the tax collectors and sinners were coming near to listen to him. ^15-1

**15:2** And the Pharisees and the scribes were grumbling and saying, “This fellow welcomes sinners and eats with them.” ^15-2

**15:3** So he told them this parable: ^15-3

**15:4** “Which one of you, having a hundred sheep and losing one of them, does not leave the ninety-nine in the wilderness and go after the one that is lost until he finds it? ^15-4

**15:5** And when he has found it, he lays it on his shoulders and rejoices. ^15-5

**15:6** And when he comes home, he calls together his friends and neighbors, saying to them, ‘Rejoice with me, for I have found my lost sheep.’ ^15-6

**15:7** Just so, I tell you, there will be more joy in heaven over one sinner who repents than over ninety-nine righteous persons who need no repentance. ^15-7

## The parable of the lost coin

**15:8** “Or what woman having ten silver coins, if she loses one of them, does not light a lamp, sweep the house, and search carefully until she finds it? ^15-8

**15:9** And when she has found it, she calls together her friends and neighbors, saying, ‘Rejoice with me, for I have found the coin that I had lost.’ ^15-9

**15:10** Just so, I tell you, there is joy in the presence of the angels of God over one sinner who repents.” ^15-10

## The parable of the prodigal and his brother

**15:11** Then Jesus^[Gk *he*] said, “There was a man who had two sons. ^15-11

**15:12** The younger of them said to his father, ‘Father, give me the share of the wealth that will belong to me.’ So he divided his assets between them. ^15-12

**15:13** A few days later the younger son gathered all he had and traveled to a distant region, and there he squandered his wealth in dissolute living. ^15-13

**15:14** When he had spent everything, a severe famine took place throughout that region, and he began to be in need. ^15-14

**15:15** So he went and hired himself out to one of the citizens of that region, who sent him to his fields to feed the pigs. ^15-15

**15:16** He would gladly have filled his stomach^[Other ancient authorities read *filled himself*] with the pods that the pigs were eating, and no one gave him anything. ^15-16

**15:17** But when he came to his senses he said, ‘How many of my father’s hired hands have bread enough and to spare, but here I am dying of hunger! ^15-17

**15:18** I will get up and go to my father, and I will say to him, “Father, I have sinned against heaven and before you; ^15-18

**15:19** I am no longer worthy to be called your son; treat me like one of your hired hands.” ’ ^15-19

**15:20** So he set off and went to his father. But while he was still far off, his father saw him and was filled with compassion; he ran and put his arms around him and kissed him. ^15-20

**15:21** Then the son said to him, ‘Father, I have sinned against heaven and before you; I am no longer worthy to be called your son.’^[Other ancient authorities add *Treat me like one of your hired servants*] ^15-21

**15:22** But the father said to his slaves, ‘Quickly, bring out a robe—the best one—and put it on him; put a ring on his finger and sandals on his feet. ^15-22

**15:23** And get the fatted calf and kill it, and let us eat and celebrate, ^15-23

**15:24** for this son of mine was dead and is alive again; he was lost and is found!’ And they began to celebrate. ^15-24

**15:25** “Now his elder son was in the field, and as he came and approached the house, he heard music and dancing. ^15-25

**15:26** He called one of the slaves and asked what was going on. ^15-26

**15:27** He replied, ‘Your brother has come, and your father has killed the fatted calf because he has got him back safe and sound.’ ^15-27

**15:28** Then he became angry and refused to go in. His father came out and began to plead with him. ^15-28

**15:29** But he answered his father, ‘Listen! For all these years I have been working like a slave for you, and I have never disobeyed your command, yet you have never given me even a young goat so that I might celebrate with my friends. ^15-29

**15:30** But when this son of yours came back, who has devoured your assets with prostitutes, you killed the fatted calf for him!’ ^15-30

**15:31** Then the father^[Gk *he*] said to him, ‘Son, you are always with me, and all that is mine is yours. ^15-31

**15:32** But we had to celebrate and rejoice, because this brother of yours was dead and has come to life; he was lost and has been found.’ ” ^15-32
