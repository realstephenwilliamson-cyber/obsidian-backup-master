# Psalm 97 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 096|← Psalm 96]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 098|Psalm 98 →]]

## The glory of God’s reign

**97:1** The LORD is king! Let the earth rejoice; let the many coastlands be glad! ^97-1

**97:2** Clouds and thick darkness are all around him; righteousness and justice are the foundation of his throne. ^97-2

**97:3** Fire goes before him and consumes his adversaries on every side. ^97-3

**97:4** His lightnings light up the world; the earth sees and trembles. ^97-4

**97:5** The mountains melt like wax before the LORD, before the Lord of all the earth. ^97-5

**97:6** The heavens proclaim his righteousness, and all the peoples behold his glory. ^97-6

**97:7** All servants of images are put to shame, those who make their boast in worthless idols; all gods bow down before him. ^97-7

**97:8** Zion hears and is glad, and the towns^[Heb *daughters*] of Judah rejoice because of your judgments, O God. ^97-8

**97:9** For you, O LORD, are most high over all the earth; you are exalted far above all gods. ^97-9

**97:10** You who love the LORD, hate evil; he guards the lives of his faithful; he rescues them from the hand of the wicked. ^97-10

**97:11** Light dawns^[Gk Syr Jerome: Heb *is sown*] for the righteous and joy for the upright in heart. ^97-11

**97:12** Rejoice in the LORD, O you righteous, and give thanks to his holy name! ^97-12
