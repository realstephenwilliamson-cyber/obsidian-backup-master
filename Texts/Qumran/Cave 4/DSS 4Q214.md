### Frag. 2 (= CTL Bodleian col. d; 4Q214b 2 - 6 i)

**1** [… to sprin]kle [the blood on the sides of the altar, and again wash] ^f2-1

**2** [your hands and] your [fe]et fr[om the blood, and begin to offer] ^f2-2

**3** [the sal]ted [portions. Offer] the head [ﬁrst, and] ^f2-3

**4** [cover it with] the [fat,] and not should be seen [on it blood of the slaughtered cow. And after it] ^f2-4

**5** the [neck (?),] and after them, the forelegs, [and after them the breast with the ribs.] ^f2-5

**6** [And afte]r them the thighs and the spine of [the loin. And after the thighs, the hind-legs] ^f2-6

**7** [was]hed, with the entrails. And all of th[em salted with salt, in the way which is ﬁtting] ^f2-7

**8** [for the]m, according to their need. Blank And af[ter this, ﬂour mixed with oil.] ^f2-8

**9** [And] after everything, [pour] wine, [and burn incense over them] ^f2-9

**10** [so that] your service may be in or[der and all your offerings …] ^f2-10
