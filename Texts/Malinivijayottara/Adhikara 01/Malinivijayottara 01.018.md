## Mūla

सकलो निष्कलोऽनन्तः शक्तिरप्यस्य तद्विधा ।
स सिसृक्षुर्जगत्सृष्टेरादावेव निजेच्छया ॥ ^mula

sakalo niṣkalo 'nantaḥ śaktir apy asya tadvidhā |
sa sisṛkṣur jagatsṛṣṭer ādāv eva nijecchayā || ^iast

## Translation

> **The Characteristics of the Lord**
>
> There (in that context), the (sovereign) Lord (*īśvara*), who does everything, is tranquil; the (prevailing) Lord (*prabhu*), who does everything, is omniscient. Infinite (*ananta*), he is (both) (immanent) 'with parts' (*sakala*) and (transcendent) 'without parts' (*niṣkala*), and his power is also such. (with 1.17cd–18ab)

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2022 – June 2022
> p. 41 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=17>))

> **The Great Creation**
>
> At the beginning of the creation of the universe, desiring to emit (it), he awakened by his own will the eight souls who are Isolated in Consciousness Alone (vijñānakevalin).[^44] (with 1.18cd–19ab)

[^44]: Quoted in TĀ ad 9/92cd-93ab and 10/139cd-140ab, paraphrased in TĀ 10/139cd-140ab.
