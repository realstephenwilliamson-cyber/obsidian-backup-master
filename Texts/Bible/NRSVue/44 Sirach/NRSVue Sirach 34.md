# Sirach 34 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Sirach 33|← Sirach 33]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Sirach 35|Sirach 35 →]]

## Dreams mean nothing

**34:1** The senseless have vain and false hopes, and dreams give wings to fools. ^34-1

**34:2** As one who catches at a shadow and pursues the wind, so is anyone who pays attention to dreams. ^34-2

**34:3** What is seen in dreams is but a reflection, the likeness of a face looking at itself. ^34-3

**34:4** From an unclean thing, what can be clean? And from something false, what can be true? ^34-4

**34:5** Divinations and omens and dreams are unreal, and like a woman in labor the mind has fantasies. ^34-5

**34:6** Unless they are sent by intervention from the Most High, pay no attention to them. ^34-6

**34:7** For dreams have deceived many, and those who put their hope in them have perished. ^34-7

**34:8** Without such deceptions the law will be fulfilled, and wisdom is complete in a trustworthy mouth. ^34-8

## Experience as a Teacher

**34:9** People who have traveled^[Other ancient authorities read *are educated*] know many things, and those with much experience know what they are talking about. ^34-9

**34:10** The inexperienced know few things, ^34-10

**34:11** but those who have traveled acquire much cleverness. ^34-11

**34:12** I have seen many things in my travels, and I understand more than I can express. ^34-12

**34:13** I have often been in danger of death but have escaped because of these experiences. ^34-13

## Fear the Lord

**34:14** The spirit of those who fear the Lord will live, ^34-14

**34:15** for their hope is in him who saves them. ^34-15

**34:16** Those who fear the Lord will not be timid or play the coward, for he is their hope. ^34-16

**34:17** Happy are souls that fear the Lord! ^34-17

**34:18** To whom do they look? And who is their support? ^34-18

**34:19** The eyes of the Lord are on those who love him, a mighty shield and strong support, a shelter from scorching heat and a shade from noonday sun, a guard against stumbling and a help against falling. ^34-19

**34:20** He lifts up the soul and makes the eyes sparkle; he gives health and life and blessing. ^34-20

## Offering sacrifices

**34:21** If one sacrifices ill-gotten goods, the offering is blemished;^[Other ancient authorities read *is made in mockery*] ^34-21

**34:22** the gifts^[Other ancient authorities read *mockeries*] of the lawless are not acceptable. ^34-22

**34:23** The Most High is not pleased with the offerings of the ungodly, nor for a multitude of sacrifices does he forgive sins. ^34-23

**34:24** Like one who kills a son before his father’s eyes is the person who offers a sacrifice from the property of the poor. ^34-24

**34:25** The bread of the needy is the life of the poor; whoever deprives them of it is a murderer. ^34-25

**34:26** To take away a neighbor’s living is to commit murder; ^34-26

**34:27** to deprive an employee of wages is to shed blood. ^34-27

**34:28** When one builds and another tears down, what do they gain but hard work? ^34-28

**34:29** When one prays and another curses, to whose voice will the Lord listen? ^34-29

**34:30** If one washes after touching a corpse and touches it again, what has been gained by washing? ^34-30

**34:31** So if one fasts for his sins and goes again and does the same things, who will listen to his prayer? And what has he gained by humbling himself? ^34-31
