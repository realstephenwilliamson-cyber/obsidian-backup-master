## Mūla

एवम्जलादिमूलान्तम्तत्त्वव्रातमिदम्महत् ।
पृथग्भेदैरिमैर्भिन्नम्विज्ञेयम्तत्फलेप्सुभिः ॥ ^mula

evam jalādimūlāntam tattvavrātam idam mahat |
pṛthagbhedair imair bhinnam vijñeyam tatphalepsubhiḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, July 2022 – September 2022
> p. 29 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Malini (journal), July-September 2022.pdf#page=31>))
>
> Those who aspire to (their) respective fruits should know that in the same way (as the Earth principle, each) separate (principle in) this great manifold of principles from Water to Nature is divided into these (fifteen) divisions.
