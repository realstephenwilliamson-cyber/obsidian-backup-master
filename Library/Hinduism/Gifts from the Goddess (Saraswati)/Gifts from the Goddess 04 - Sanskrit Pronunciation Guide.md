## Sanskrit Pronunciation Guide

This chart follows the order of the Sanskrit syllabary. In general, all diacritical marks are pronounced. A dash over a vowel makes it longer. The *c* is always soft, as in *church*. When an *s* has diacritics (as with *ś* or *ṣ*), pronounce it as *sh*. When an *n* has diacritics (as with *ṅ*, *ñ* and *ṇ*), pronounce it as *n*. More specifically:
