# Tantrāloka study plan

[[Tantraloka study plan|Overview]] · [[Study plan - Practice run 2026|Practice run]] · [[Study plan - Year 1 (2026-27)|Year 1]] · [[Study plan - Year 2 (2027-28)|Year 2]] · [[Study plan - Year 3 (2028-29)|Year 3]]

The whole Tantrāloka with Jayaratha's *Viveka* in three years, read alongside Dyczkowski's translation. The cycle runs with the Revised Common Lectionary: it starts on Advent Sunday, 29 November 2026 (Year B), and ends the day before Advent 2029, so each three-year cycle that follows starts again with Year B.

## How the portions were set

- **All 5,845 verses in 1,099 days.** A typical day is 5 verses and about 4 pages of Dyczkowski. Portions are balanced by pages of Dyczkowski (which carries Jayaratha's commentary) more than by verse count, so a day with a long commentary has fewer verses.
- **Jayaratha's units are never split.** When he comments on several verses together, they fall on the same day. That's why a few days are long lists (1.285–326 is 42 verses under one comment) and a few are a single verse with a long commentary (1.1 is about 30 pages).
- **Each āhnika starts on a new day**, so finishing one is a clean milestone.
- **Missed days don't move the calendar.** The plan keeps its dates, as daf yomi does; a missed day is one click away in the tables below, to catch up or let go. (Home's list of days without a note was removed on 19 Oct 2026.)
- **Parashah** is the portion read on the coming Shabbat (diaspora reckoning). On a festival Shabbat with no weekly portion, the festival is shown in brackets.
- **Lectionary** shows the coming Sunday (bold on the Sunday itself), with any feast that falls on the day in italics (a feast that falls in Holy Week is marked on the Monday after the Second Sunday of Easter, where the Prayer Book's Calendar moves it).

## Practice run

From 3 October to 26 November 2026 you read the last 55 portions of the plan (Āhnikas 31–37), exactly as they will fall in October–November 2029. 27 and 28 November are open for catching up. Notes you write now will come back to you on the same verses in 2029.

## Daily notes

Each day's note lives in `Journal/<year>/<date>`. Open it from Home (**Open today's note**) or from the Calendar. The template fills in the day's verses, the Dyczkowski pages, the parashah and the Sunday as links. Under **Earlier on these verses** it lists any note you wrote on the same verses before, including in the practice run, so in the 2029 cycle you'll see what you wrote in 2026.

The plan tables are plain text: if a plugin ever stops working, the dates and portions are still readable here.
