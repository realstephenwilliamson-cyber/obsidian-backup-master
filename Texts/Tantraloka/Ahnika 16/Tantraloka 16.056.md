## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 05 - Chapter Sixteen, part 1#^pg44|p. 24]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=44>))
>
> (The sacrificer attains) a kingdom (rājya), the attainment (of his desires), the consolidation of that (attainment), devotion to Śiva, oneness with Him, the knowledge of Śiva, the attainment of the world of Mantras, the condition (of Śiva’s) attendant, and conjunction (sāyujya) with Him. As (the benefits of the offering) is the same as that of the sacrificial animal, commencing with the external one and ending with the heroic, the flowers and the rest (that are offered), also have (and give) these benefits (lābha) by worshipping Śiva (śivapūjā). (with 16.54cd–56ab)
>
> What indeed does the Lord of the Gods, whose nature it is to grace all, not grace, (both) mobile and immobile, by (this) one means — the sacrifice? (with 16.56cd–57ab)
