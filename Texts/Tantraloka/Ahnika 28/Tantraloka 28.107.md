## Mūla

हस्ते च पञ्चमी षष्ठी पूर्वास्वथ पुनर्वसौ ।
सप्तमी तत्परा पित्र्ये रोहिण्यां नवमी तथा ॥ ^mula

haste ca pañcamī ṣaṣṭhī pūrvāsvatha punarvasau |
saptamī tatparā pitrye rohiṇyāṃ navamī tathā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 04 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 2#^pg68|p. 51]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=68>))
>
> 1) The the first lunar day when (the sun is) in the (constellation) called Śruti (i.e. Śravaṇa), 2) the fourth day in the three Uttarās, 3) the fifth day in Hasta, 4) the sixth day in (the three) Pūrvās, and, again, 5) the seventh day in Vasu, 6) the one after that in Pitrī, 7) the ninth in Rohiṇī, 8) the twelfth in Mūla, 9) the fourteenth (bhūtā) in Brāhma (i.e. Tiṣya), 10) the full moon in Aśvinī, 11) and the New Moon (amāvasyā) in Dhaniṣṭhā, which is the eleventh. The days of the week amongst which any one (may be in) conjunction, are Sunday, the following three (i.e. Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday) and Friday. This is said to be a Yogaparvan (a Sacred Time of Union). During a Sacred Time of Union, whether at night or during the day, one should perform the Worship of the Embodiments in every way. The wise man who honours all these days, called ‘the Sacred Times of Union’ (yogaparvan), with the Worship of the Embodiments, also becomes a regular initiate (samayin), (even) without the maṇḍala. Such is this Worship of the Embodiments according to the Siddhayogīśvarīmata. (with 28.106cd–111)
