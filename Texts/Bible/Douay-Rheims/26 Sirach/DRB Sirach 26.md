# Sirach 26 (Douay-Rheims)

*Ecclesiasticus 26 in the Douay-Rheims*

[[DRB Sirach 25|← Sirach 25]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Sirach 27|Sirach 27 →]]

**26:1** Happy is the husband of a good wife: for the number of his years is double. ^26-1

**26:2** A virtuous woman rejoiceth her husband: and shall fulfill the years of his life in peace. ^26-2

**26:3** A good wife is a good portion, she shall be given in the portion of them that fear God, to a man for his good deeds. ^26-3

**26:4** Rich or poor, if his heart is good, his countenance shall be cheerful at all times. ^26-4

**26:5** Of three things my heart hath been afraid, and at the fourth my face hath trembled: ^26-5

**26:6** The accusation of a city, and the gathering together of the people: ^26-6

**26:7** And a false calumny, all are more grievous than death. ^26-7

**26:8** A jealous woman is the grief and mourning of the heart. ^26-8

**26:9** With a jealous woman is a scourge of the tongue which communicateth with all. ^26-9

**26:10** As a yoke of oxen that is moved to and fro, so also is a wicked woman: he that hath hold of her, is as he that taketh hold of a scorpion. ^26-10

**26:11** A drunken woman is a great wrath: and her reproach and shame shall not be hid. ^26-11

**26:12** The fornication of a woman shall be known by the haughtiness of her eyes, and by her eyelids. ^26-12

**26:13** On a daughter that turneth not away herself, set a strict watch: lest finding an opportunity she abuse herself. ^26-13

**26:14** Take heed of the impudence of her eyes, and wonder not if she slight thee. ^26-14

**26:15** She will open her mouth as a thirsty traveller to the fountain, and will drink of every water near her, and will sit down by every hedge, and open her quiver against every arrow, until she fail. ^26-15

**26:16** The grace of a diligent woman shall delight her husband, and shall fat his bones. ^26-16

**26:17** Her discipline is the gift of God. ^26-17

**26:18** Such is a wise and silent woman, and there is nothing so much worth as a well instructed soul. ^26-18

**26:19** A holy and shamefaced woman is grace upon grace. ^26-19

**26:20** And no price is worthy of a continent soul. ^26-20

**26:21** As the sun when it riseth to the world in the high places of God, so is the beauty of a good wife for the ornament of her house. ^26-21

**26:22** As the lamp shining upon the holy candlestick, so is the beauty of the face in a ripe age. ^26-22

**26:23** As golden pillars upon bases of silver, so are the firm feet upon the soles of a steady woman. ^26-23

**26:24** As everlasting foundations upon a solid rock, so the commandments of God In the heart of a holy woman. ^26-24

**26:25** At two things my heart is grieved, and the third bringeth anger upon me: ^26-25

**26:26** A man of was fainting through poverty: and a man of sense despised: ^26-26

**26:27** And he that passeth over from justice to sin, God hath prepared such an one for the sword. ^26-27

**26:28** Two sorts of callings have appeared to me hard and dangerous: a merchant is hardly free from negligence: and a huckster shall not be justified from the sins of the lips. ^26-28
