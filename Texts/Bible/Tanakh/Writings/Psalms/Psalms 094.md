## Chapter 94

**94:1** God of retribution, LORD,  
God of retribution, appear! ^94-1

**94:2** Rise up, judge of the earth,  
give the arrogant their deserts! ^94-2

**94:3** How long shall the wicked, O LORD,  
how long shall the wicked exult, ^94-3

**94:4** shall they utter insolent speech,  
shall all evildoers vaunt themselves? ^94-4

**94:5** They crush Your people, O LORD,  
they afflict Your very own; ^94-5

**94:6** they kill the widow and the stranger;  
they murder the fatherless, ^94-6

**94:7** thinking, “The LORD does not see it,  
the God of Jacob does not pay heed.” ^94-7

**94:8** Take heed, you most brutish people;  
fools, when will you get wisdom? ^94-8

**94:9** Shall He who implants the ear not hear,  
He who forms the eye not see? ^94-9

**94:10** Shall He who disciplines nations not punish,  
He who instructs men in knowledge? ^94-10

**94:11** The LORD knows the designs of men to be futile. ^94-11

**94:12** Happy is the man whom You discipline, O LORD,  
the man You instruct in Your teaching, ^94-12

**94:13** to give him tranquillity in times of misfortune,  
until a pit be dug for the wicked. ^94-13

**94:14** For the LORD will not forsake His people;  
He will not abandon His very own. ^94-14

**94:15** Judgment shall again accord with justice  
and all the upright shall rally to it. ^94-15

**94:16** Who will take my part against evil men?  
Who will stand up for me against wrongdoers? ^94-16

**94:17** Were not the LORD my help,  
I should soon dwell in silence. ^94-17

**94:18** When I think my foot has given way,  
Your faithfulness, O LORD, supports me. ^94-18

**94:19** When I am filled with cares,  
Your assurance soothes my soul. ^94-19

**94:20** Shall the seat of injustice be Your partner,  
that frames mischief by statute? ^94-20

**94:21** They band together to do away with the righteous;  
they condemn the innocent to death. ^94-21

**94:22** But the LORD is my haven;  
my God is my sheltering rock. ^94-22

**94:23** He will make their evil recoil upon them,  
annihilate them through their own wickedness;  
the LORD our God will annihilate them. ^94-23
