## Mūla

सकलावधि संशोध्य शिवे योगं प्रकल्पयेत् ।
बुभुक्षोः सकलं ध्यात्वा योगं कुर्वीत योगवित् ॥ ^mula

sakalāvadhi saṁśodhya śive yogaṁ prakalpayet |
bubhukṣoḥ sakalaṁ dhyātvā yogaṁ kurvīta yogavit || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, July 2023 – September 2023
> p. 76 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=81>))
>
> There is no doubt that when this (series of worlds) is purified, all (the other paths) are also purified. The great-minded one who is to purify all the Path should conceive union (yoga) within Śiva (for his disciple), (only after) having purified (them all right) up to (immanent) Śiva 'with parts' (*sakala*). (The teacher) who knows yoga should bring about union (yoga) for (the disciple) who desires worldly benefits, having (thus) meditated on (Śiva) 'with parts' (*sakala*). (with 5.34–35)
