## Mūla

उक्तं श्रीपूर्वशास्त्रे च धराव्यक्तात्मकं द्वयम् ।
असंख्यातं निशाशक्तिसंज्ञं त्वेकस्वरूपकम् ॥ ^mula

uktaṃ śrīpūrvaśāstre ca dharāvyaktātmakaṃ dvayam |
asaṃkhyātaṃ niśāśaktisaṃjñaṃ tvekasvarūpakam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 09 - Chapter Eight, part 7#^pg337|p. 319]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=337>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 8.325cd–326ab.
>
> It is said in the Śrīpūrvaśāstra (Mālinīvijayottara) that two (Eggs), that is, Earth and Unmanifest Nature, are innumerable, whereas (those) of Māyā (niga)) and Power (are only) one.
