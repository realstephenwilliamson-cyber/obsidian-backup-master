## Chapter 103

**103:1** Of David.  
Bless the LORD, O my soul,  
all my being, His holy name. ^103-1

**103:2** Bless the LORD, O my soul  
and do not forget all His bounties. ^103-2

**103:3** He forgives all your sins,  
heals all your diseases. ^103-3

**103:4** He redeems your life from the Pit,  
surrounds you with steadfast love and mercy. ^103-4

**103:5** He satisfies you with good things in the prime of life,[^1]  
so that your youth is renewed like the eagle’s. ^103-5

**103:6** The LORD executes righteous acts  
and judgments for all who are wronged. ^103-6

**103:7** He made known His ways to Moses,  
His deeds to the children of Israel. ^103-7

**103:8** The LORD is compassionate and gracious,  
slow to anger, abounding in steadfast love. ^103-8

**103:9** He will not contend forever,  
or nurse His anger for all time. ^103-9

**103:10** He has not dealt with us according to our sins,  
nor has He requited us according to our iniquities. ^103-10

**103:11** For as the heavens are high above the earth,  
so great is His steadfast love toward those who fear Him. ^103-11

**103:12** As east is far from west,  
so far has He removed our sins from us. ^103-12

**103:13** As a father has compassion for his children,  
so the LORD has compassion for those who fear Him. ^103-13

**103:14** For He knows how we are formed;  
He is mindful that we are dust. ^103-14

**103:15** Man, his days are like those of grass;  
he blooms like a flower of the field; ^103-15

**103:16** a wind passes by and it is no more,  
its own place no longer knows it. ^103-16

**103:17** But the LORD’s steadfast love is for all eternity  
toward those who fear Him,  
and His beneficence is for the children’s children ^103-17

**103:18** of those who keep His covenant  
and remember to observe His precepts. ^103-18

**103:19** The LORD has established His throne in heaven,  
and His sovereign rule is over all. ^103-19

**103:20** Bless the LORD, O His angels,  
mighty creatures who do His bidding,  
ever obedient to His bidding; ^103-20

**103:21** bless the LORD, all His hosts,  
His servants who do His will; ^103-21

**103:22** bless the LORD, all His works,  
through the length and breadth of His realm;  
bless the LORD, O my soul. ^103-22

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
