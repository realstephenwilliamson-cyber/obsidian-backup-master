# Psalm 14

← [[BCP Psalm 013]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 015]] →

*Dixit insipiens* · Book One · Second Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 014]]

**14:1** The fool has said in his heart, "There is no God." \*  
All are corrupt and commit abominable acts;  
there is none who does any good. ^14-1

**14:2** The LORD looks down from heaven upon us all, \*  
to see if there is any who is wise,  
if there is one who seeks after God. ^14-2

**14:3** Every one has proved faithless;  
all alike have turned bad; \*  
there is none who does good; no, not one. ^14-3

**14:4** Have they no knowledge, all those evildoers \*  
who eat up my people like bread  
and do not call upon the LORD? ^14-4

**14:5** See how they tremble with fear, \*  
because God is in the company of the righteous. ^14-5

**14:6** Their aim is to confound the plans of the afflicted, \*  
but the LORD is their refuge. ^14-6

[p. 599]

**14:7** Oh, that Israel's deliverance would come out of Zion! \*  
when the LORD restores the fortunes of his people,  
Jacob will rejoice and Israel be glad. ^14-7
