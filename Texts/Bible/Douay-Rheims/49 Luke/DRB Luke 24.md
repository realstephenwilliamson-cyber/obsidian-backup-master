# Luke 24 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Luke 23|← Luke 23]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · —

Compare: [[Luke 24|BSB and SBLGNT]]

**24:1** And on the first day of the week, very early in the morning, they came to the sepulchre, bringing the spices which they had prepared. ^24-1

**24:2** And they found the stone rolled back from the sepulchre. ^24-2

**24:3** And going in, they found not the body of the Lord Jesus. ^24-3

**24:4** And it came to pass, as they were astonished in their mind at this, behold, two men stood by them, in shining apparel. ^24-4

**24:5** And as they were afraid, and bowed down their countenance towards the ground, they said unto them: Why seek you the living with the dead? ^24-5

**24:6** He is not here, but is risen. Remember how he spoke unto you, when he was in Galilee, ^24-6

**24:7** Saying: The Son of man must be delivered into the hands of sinful men, and be crucified, and the third day rise again. ^24-7

**24:8** And they remembered his words. ^24-8

**24:9** And going back from the sepulchre, they told all these things to the eleven, and to all the rest. ^24-9

**24:10** And it was Mary Magdalen, and Joanna, and Mary of James, and the other women that were with them, who told these things to the apostles. ^24-10

**24:11** And these words seemed to them as idle tales; and they did not believe them. ^24-11

**24:12** But Peter rising up, ran to the sepulchre, and stooping down, he saw the linen cloths laid by themselves; and went away wondering in himself at that which was come to pass. ^24-12

**24:13** And behold, two of them went, the same day, to a town which was sixty furlongs from Jerusalem, named Emmaus. ^24-13

**24:14** And they talked together of all these things which had happened. ^24-14

**24:15** And it came to pass, that while they talked and reasoned with themselves, Jesus himself also drawing near, went with them. ^24-15

**24:16** But their eyes were held, that they should not know him. ^24-16

**24:17** And he said to them: What are these discourses that you hold one with another as you walk, and are sad? ^24-17

**24:18** And the one of them, whose name was Cleophas, answering, said to him: Art thou only a stranger to Jerusalem, and hast not known the things that have been done there in these days? ^24-18

**24:19** To whom he said: What things? And they said: Concerning Jesus of Nazareth, who was a prophet, mighty in work and word before God and all the people; ^24-19

**24:20** And how our chief priests and princes delivered him to be condemned to death, and crucified him. ^24-20

**24:21** But we hoped, that it was he that should have redeemed Israel: and now besides all this, today is the third day since these things were done. ^24-21

**24:22** Yea and certain women also of our company affrighted us, who before it was light, were at the sepulchre, ^24-22

**24:23** And not finding his body, came, saying, that they had also seen a vision of angels, who say that he is alive. ^24-23

**24:24** And some of our people went to the sepulchre, and found it so as the women had said, but him they found not. ^24-24

**24:25** Then he said to them: O foolish, and slow of heart to believe in all things which the prophets have spoken. ^24-25

**24:26** Ought not Christ to have suffered these things, and so to enter into his glory? ^24-26

**24:27** And beginning at Moses and all the prophets, he expounded to them in all the scriptures, the things that were concerning him. ^24-27

**24:28** And they drew night to the town, whither they were going: and he made as though he would go farther. ^24-28

**24:29** But they constrained him; saying: Stay with us, because it is towards evening, and the day is now far spent. And he went in with them. ^24-29

**24:30** And it came to pass, whilst he was at table with them, he took bread, and blessed, and brake, and gave to them. ^24-30

**24:31** And their eyes were opened, and they knew him: and he vanished out of their sight. ^24-31

**24:32** And they said one to the other: Was not our heart burning within us, whilst he spoke in this way, and opened to us the scriptures? ^24-32

**24:33** And rising up, the same hour, they went back to Jerusalem: and they found the eleven gathered together, and those that were staying with them, ^24-33

**24:34** Saying: The Lord is risen indeed, and hath appeared to Simon. ^24-34

**24:35** And they told what things were done in the way; and how they knew him in the breaking of the bread. ^24-35

**24:36** Now whilst they were speaking these things, Jesus stood in the midst of them, and saith to them: Peace be to you; it is I, fear not. ^24-36

**24:37** But they being troubled and frightened, supposed that they saw a spirit. ^24-37

**24:38** And he said to them: Why are you troubled, and why do thoughts arise in your hearts? ^24-38

**24:39** See my hands and feet, that it is I myself; handle, and see: for a spirit hath not flesh and bones, as you see me to have. ^24-39

**24:40** And when he had said this, he shewed them his hands and feet. ^24-40

**24:41** But while they yet believed not, and wondered for joy, he said: Have you any thing to eat? ^24-41

**24:42** And they offered him a piece of a broiled fish, and a honeycomb. ^24-42

**24:43** And when he had eaten before them, taking the remains, he gave to them. ^24-43

**24:44** And he said to them: These are the words which I spoke to you, while I was yet with you, that all things must needs be fulfilled, which are written in the law of Moses, and in the prophets, and in the psalms, concerning me. ^24-44

**24:45** Then he opened their understanding, that they might understand the scriptures. ^24-45

**24:46** And he said to them: Thus it is written, and thus it behoved Christ to suffer, and to rise again from the dead, the third day: ^24-46

**24:47** And that penance and remission of sins should be preached in his name, unto all nations, beginning at Jerusalem. ^24-47

**24:48** And you are witnesses of these things. ^24-48

**24:49** And I send the promise of my Father upon you: but stay you in the city till you be endued with power from on high. ^24-49

**24:50** And he led them out as far as Bethania: and lifting up his hands, he blessed them. ^24-50

**24:51** And it came to pass, whilst he blessed them, he departed from them, and was carried up to heaven. ^24-51

**24:52** And they adoring went back into Jerusalem with great joy. ^24-52

**24:53** And they were always in the temple, praising and blessing God. Amen. ^24-53
