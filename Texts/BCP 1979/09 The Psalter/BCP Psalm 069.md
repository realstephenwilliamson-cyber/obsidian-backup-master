# Psalm 69

← [[BCP Psalm 068]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 070]] →

*Salvum me fac* · Book Two · Thirteenth Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 069]]

**69:1** Save me, O God, \*  
for the waters have risen up to my neck. ^69-1

**69:2** I am sinking in deep mire, \*  
and there is no firm ground for my feet. ^69-2

**69:3** I have come into deep waters, \*  
and the torrent washes over me. ^69-3

**69:4** I have grown weary with my crying;  
my throat is inflamed; \*  
my eyes have failed from looking for my God. ^69-4

**69:5** Those who hate me without a cause are more than the hairs of my head;  
my lying foes who would destroy me are mighty. \*  
Must I then give back what I never stole? ^69-5

**69:6** O God, you know my foolishness, \*  
and my faults are not hidden from you. ^69-6

**69:7** Let not those who hope in you be put to shame through me, Lord GOD of hosts; \*  
let not those who seek you be disgraced because of me, O God of Israel. ^69-7

[p. 680] ^pg680

**69:8** Surely, for your sake have I suffered reproach, \*  
and shame has covered my face. ^69-8

**69:9** I have become a stranger to my own kindred, \*  
an alien to my mother's children. ^69-9

**69:10** Zeal for your house has eaten me up; \*  
the scorn of those who scorn you has fallen upon me. ^69-10

**69:11** I humbled myself with fasting, \*  
but that was turned to my reproach. ^69-11

**69:12** I put on sack-cloth also, \*  
and became a byword among them. ^69-12

**69:13** Those who sit at the gate murmur against me, \*  
and the drunkards make songs about me. ^69-13

**69:14** But as for me, this is my prayer to you, \*  
at the time you have set, O LORD: ^69-14

**69:15** "In your great mercy, O God, \*  
answer me with your unfailing help. ^69-15

**69:16** Save me from the mire; do not let me sink; \*  
let me be rescued from those who hate me  
and out of the deep waters. ^69-16

**69:17** Let not the torrent of waters wash over me,  
neither let the deep swallow me up; \*  
do not let the Pit shut its mouth upon me. ^69-17

**69:18** Answer me, O LORD, for your love is kind; \*  
in your great compassion, turn to me." ^69-18

**69:19** "Hide not your face from your servant; \*  
be swift and answer me, for I am in distress. ^69-19

**69:20** Draw near to me and redeem me; \*  
because of my enemies deliver me. ^69-20

[p. 681] ^pg681

**69:21** You know my reproach, my shame, and my dishonor; \*  
my adversaries are all in your sight." ^69-21

**69:22** Reproach has broken my heart, and it cannot be healed; \*  
I looked for sympathy, but there was none,  
for comforters, but I could find no one. ^69-22

**69:23** They gave me gall to eat, \*  
and when I was thirsty, they gave me vinegar to drink. ^69-23

**69:24** Let the table before them be a trap \*  
and their sacred feasts a snare. ^69-24

**69:25** Let their eyes be darkened, that they may not see, \*  
and give them continual trembling in their loins. ^69-25

**69:26** Pour out your indignation upon them, \*  
and let the fierceness of your anger overtake them. ^69-26

**69:27** Let their camp be desolate, \*  
and let there be none to dwell in their tents. ^69-27

**69:28** For they persecute him whom you have stricken \*  
and add to the pain of those whom you have pierced. ^69-28

**69:29** Lay to their charge guilt upon guilt, \*  
and let them not receive your vindication. ^69-29

**69:30** Let them be wiped out of the book of the living \*  
and not be written among the righteous. ^69-30

**69:31** As for me, I am afflicted and in pain; \*  
your help, O God, will lift me up on high. ^69-31

**69:32** I will praise the Name of God in song; \*  
I will proclaim his greatness with thanksgiving. ^69-32

**69:33** This will please the LORD more than an offering of oxen, \*  
more than bullocks with horns and hoofs. ^69-33

[p. 682] ^pg682

**69:34** The afflicted shall see and be glad; \*  
you who seek God, your heart shall live. ^69-34

**69:35** For the LORD listens to the needy, \*  
and his prisoners he does not despise. ^69-35

**69:36** Let the heavens and the earth praise him, \*  
the seas and all that moves in them; ^69-36

**69:37** For God will save Zion and rebuild the cities of Judah; \*  
they shall live there and have it in possession. ^69-37

**69:38** The children of his servants will inherit it, \*  
and those who love his Name will dwell therein. ^69-38
