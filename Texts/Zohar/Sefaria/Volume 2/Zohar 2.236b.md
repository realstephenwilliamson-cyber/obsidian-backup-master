# Zohar 2.236b

← [[Zohar 2.236a]] · [[Zohar (Soncino)]] · [[Zohar 2.237a]] →

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 6.06 - Parashat Pequdei#^z2-236b|Parashat Pequdei, at 2:236b]]

*Volume 2, folio 236b* · Pekudei

*Passage Pekudei 35:306 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 2.236a#^pekudei-35-306|Pekudei 35:306]].*

**Pekudei 35:307** But the Calf was the appropriate form. For from the side of gold there emerged the dross, out of which spread in all directions all the forces of the “left side”. All these forces have the red colour of gold, and are under the influence of the sun. For when the sun reaches his full strength he generates gold in the earth; and the Chieftain ruling under the force of the sun has the appearance of a calf, and is described as “the destruction (*keteb*) that wasteth at noonday” (Ps. 91, 6). ^pekudei-35-307

**Pekudei 35:308** The red side, the defiled spirit, is the same as the Evil Serpent. On him there rides a male-female being, called *eleh* (these), so called for the reason that they appear everywhere in various guises. On the opposite side is the Holy Spirit, called *zoth* (this)[^1] , pointing to the emblem of the holy covenant which is always found on man. The others, however, are called “these”, wherefore it is written “yea, *these* [^2] may forget, yet will not I forget thee” (Isa. 49, 15). ^pekudei-35-308

**Pekudei 35:309** The same is alluded to in “For *these* things I weep” (Lam. 1, 16), inasmuch as that sin was the cause of many weepings for Israel. Alternately, “For *these* I weep”, to wit, for the dark forces of that region to whom was given the licence to dominate over Israel and to destroy the Sanctuary. “*These*” (*eleh*) points thus to the powers of the *unclean-side*, and “I weep” points to the Holy Spirit, who is named “I”. ^pekudei-35-309

**Pekudei 35:310** One might indeed urge against this, citing the words: “*These (eleh*) are the words of the covenant” (Deut. 28, 69).[^3] But in truth, even here the word *eleh* (these) points to the curses awaiting him who will transgress “the words of the covenant”. Similarly, in the passage, “*These* are the commandments, which the Lord commanded Moses” (Lev. 27, 34), the word “*these*” is a warning to man to purify himself by the observance of the precepts of the Torah and not to stray from the right path, but to keep afar from the evil powers. ^pekudei-35-310

**Pekudei 35:311** As regards “*These* are the generations of Noah” (Gen. 6, 9), the word eleh (these) is assuredly appropriate there, inasmuch as Noah begat Ham, the father of Canaan, in regard to whom it is written, “Cursed be Canaan” (*Ibid.* 9, 25). This, then, is the esoteric implication of *eleh* (these), ^pekudei-35-311

**Pekudei 35:312** pointing in our text to the dross and refuse of the gold. Now Aaron offered up the gold because he was of the side of fire, and gold is under the influence of the force of fire, the two being, as it were, one. ^pekudei-35-312

**Pekudei 35:313** The Unclean Spirit, whose haunting place is the desert, thus found at that moment an occasion to seize hold of him; and so, whereas Israel standing at Mount Sinai were purged of the primitive venom that the evil spirit injected into the world, thereby bringing death to all mankind, now the same evil spirit defiled them anew, took hold of them and brought again death to them and to all mankind and for all their generations to follow. Scripture so says: “I said: Ye are godlike beings…. But ye shall die like men”, etc. (Ps. 82, 6-7). Aaron therefore had to purge himself afterwards during the seven sacred days,[^4] and after that by means of a calf.[^5] ^pekudei-35-313

**Pekudei 36:314** Observe that Aaron had to purge himself, for but for him the calf would not have emerged. For Aaron belonged to the “right side”, he symbolized the strength of the sun, the source whence gold originates; and so the unclean spirit came down and insinuated itself, with the result that the Israelites were defiled, and Aaron also. ^pekudei-36-314

**Pekudei 36:315** Aaron was defiled through the emergence of the calf that belongs to the left side, as it says: “and the face of the ox on the left side” (Ezek 1, 10). Thus, though Aaron was of the “right side”, the “left side” was there in absorption, the side that gave occasion for the calf. ^pekudei-36-315

[^1]: Allusion to Isa. 59, 21: “this is My covenant with them”.
[^2]: Allusion to the Golden Calf, v. Midrash Eikhoh, *in loco.*
[^3]: This is cited to prove that *eleh* (these) is associated with the good as well as evil.
[^4]: v. Lev. 8, 33.
[^5]: v. *Ibid.* 9, 2.
