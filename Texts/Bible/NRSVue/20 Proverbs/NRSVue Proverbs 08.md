# Proverbs 8 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Proverbs 07|← Proverbs 7]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Proverbs 09|Proverbs 9 →]]

## The gifts of wisdom

**8:1** Does not wisdom call and understanding raise her voice? ^8-1

**8:2** On the heights, beside the way, at the crossroads she takes her stand; ^8-2

**8:3** beside the gates in front of the town, at the entrance of the portals she cries out: ^8-3

**8:4** “To you, O people, I call, and my cry is to all who live. ^8-4

**8:5** O simple ones, learn prudence; acquire intelligence, you who lack it. ^8-5

**8:6** Hear, for I will speak noble things, and from my lips will come what is right, ^8-6

**8:7** for my mouth will utter truth; wickedness is an abomination to my lips. ^8-7

**8:8** All the words of my mouth are righteous; there is nothing twisted or crooked in them. ^8-8

**8:9** They are all straight to one who understands and right to those who find knowledge. ^8-9

**8:10** Take my instruction instead of silver and knowledge rather than choice gold, ^8-10

**8:11** for wisdom is better than jewels, and all that you may desire cannot compare with her. ^8-11

**8:12** I, wisdom, live with prudence, and I attain knowledge and discretion. ^8-12

**8:13** The fear of the LORD is hatred of evil. Pride and arrogance and the way of evil and perverted speech I hate. ^8-13

**8:14** I have good advice and sound wisdom; I have insight; I have strength. ^8-14

**8:15** By me kings reign, and rulers decree what is just; ^8-15

**8:16** by me rulers rule, and nobles, all who govern rightly. ^8-16

**8:17** I love those who love me, and those who seek me diligently find me. ^8-17

**8:18** Riches and honor are with me, enduring wealth and prosperity. ^8-18

**8:19** My fruit is better than gold, even fine gold, and my yield than choice silver. ^8-19

**8:20** I walk in the way of righteousness, along the paths of justice, ^8-20

**8:21** endowing with wealth those who love me and filling their treasuries. ^8-21

## Wisdom’s part in creation

**8:22** “The LORD created me at the beginning^[Or *me as the beginning*] of his work,^[Heb *way*] the first of his acts of long ago. ^8-22

**8:23** Ages ago I was set up, at the first, before the beginning of the earth. ^8-23

**8:24** When there were no depths I was brought forth, when there were no springs abounding with water. ^8-24

**8:25** Before the mountains had been shaped, before the hills, I was brought forth, ^8-25

**8:26** when he had not yet made earth and fields^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] or the world’s first bits of soil. ^8-26

**8:27** When he established the heavens, I was there; when he drew a circle on the face of the deep, ^8-27

**8:28** when he made firm the skies above, when he established the fountains of the deep, ^8-28

**8:29** when he assigned to the sea its limit, so that the waters might not transgress his command, when he marked out the foundations of the earth, ^8-29

**8:30** then I was beside him, like a master worker,^[Or *little child*] and I was daily his^[Gk: Heb lacks *his*] delight, playing before him always, ^8-30

**8:31** playing in his inhabited world and delighting in the human race. ^8-31

**8:32** “And now, my children, listen to me: happy are those who keep my ways. ^8-32

**8:33** Hear instruction and be wise, and do not neglect it. ^8-33

**8:34** Happy is the one who listens to me, watching daily at my gates, waiting beside my doors. ^8-34

**8:35** For whoever finds me finds life and obtains favor from the LORD, ^8-35

**8:36** but those who miss me injure themselves; all who hate me love death.” ^8-36
