## Mūla

अर्घपात्रमपादानं तस्मादादीयते यतः ।
यच्च तत्स्थं जलाद्येतत्करणं शोधनेऽर्चने ॥ ^mula

arghapātramapādānaṃ tasmādādīyate yataḥ |
yacca tatsthaṃ jalādyetatkaraṇaṃ śodhane'rcane || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 04 - Chapter Fifteen, part 3#^pg119|p. 101]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=119>))
>
> The ablative is (represented by) the sacrificial vessel (arghapātra), since (the sacrificial liquid) is taken from it. The water etc. contained in it is the instrument of purification and worship (and so represents the instrumental case).

## Viveka

> अर्घपात्रमपादानं तस्मादादीयते यतः ।
> यच्च तत्स्थं जलाद्येतत्करणं शोधनेऽर्चने ॥

जलादीति आदिशब्दात् सुरादि, अनेन च द्रव्याणां योग्यत्वं निरूपयितुं प्रक्रान्तम् ॥ १६० ॥
