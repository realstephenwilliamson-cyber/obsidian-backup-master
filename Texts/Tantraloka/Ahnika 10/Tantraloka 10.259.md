## Mūla

न भावग्रहणं तेन सुष्ठु सुप्तत्वमुच्यते ।
तत्साम्याल्लौकिकीं निद्रां सुषुप्तं मन्वते बुधाः ॥ ^mula

na bhāvagrahaṇaṃ tena suṣṭhu suptatvamucyate |
tatsāmyāllaukikīṃ nidrāṃ suṣuptaṃ manvate budhāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 14 - Chapter Ten, part 5#^pg479|p. 460]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=479>))
>
> The state of deep sleep is profoundly silent. It is said to prevail when the body of the creatures and the rest mentioned above (i.e. the principles and denotation) is (one with) the sustaining consciousness (adhiṣtṭhātr) which is the fixed (eternal) (dhruva) seed of all things. The same cognizing subject who is present in the direct experience (proper to the waking state) and in the thought (proper to dreaming) is present (in deep sleep also). There is no perception of phenomenal existence (bhāva), and so (the state in which he reposes in himself alone) is called deep sleep. (with 10.257cd–259ab)
>
> The wise consider common sleep (laukikī nidrā) to be deep sleep because they are the same (in three essential respects). (They are both) the seed state (of other forms of consciousness), (they are both equally) devoid of perception, and are silent. (with 10.259cd–260ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* ननु यद्येवं तर्हि कथमन्तःकरणधर्मो गाढनिद्रापि लोकेषु सुषुप्तमित्युच्यते? इत्याशङ्क्याह -

> तत्साम्याल्लौकिकीं निद्रां सुषुप्तं मन्वते बुधाः ॥
> बीजभावोऽथाग्रहणं साम्यं तूष्णींस्वभावता ।

किं नाम च तत्साम्यम् ? -- इत्याशंक्योक्तम् तूष्णींस्वभावतेति । तूष्णीं -स्वभावेऽपि किं निमित्तम्? -- इत्याशङ्क्याह -- "बीजभावः"इति संभाव्यमानभाविकार्यसंबन्धात्, अग्रहणमिति च बाह्यविषयासंवेदनात् ॥ २५९ ॥
