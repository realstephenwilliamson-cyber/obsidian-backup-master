## Mūla

देहं दिवानिशं पश्यन्नर्चयन्स्याच्छिवात्मकः ।
विश्वात्मदेहविश्रान्तितृप्तस्तल्लिङ्गनिष्ठितः ॥ ^mula

dehaṃ divāniśaṃ paśyannarcayansyācchivātmakaḥ |
viśvātmadehaviśrāntitṛptastalliṅganiṣṭhitaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 06 - Chapter Fifteen, part 5#^pg188|p. 170]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=188>))
>
> Viewing and worshipping the body night and day as full of the juice (of the aesthetic savour of) Śiva’s bliss (śivānandarasa) and the thirty-six principles, (the adept) becomes Śiva. (with 15.285cd–286ab)
>
> Satisfied by (his) repose in (his) cosmic body, he is grounded in (and devoted to) that (as the) Liṅga, and (so no longer) desires (any) external Liṅga, vow, sacred place, discipline (caryā) or the like. (with 15.286cd–287ab)
