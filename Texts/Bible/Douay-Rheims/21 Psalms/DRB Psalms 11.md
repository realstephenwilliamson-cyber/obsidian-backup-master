# Psalms 11 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 10|← Psalms 10]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 12|Psalms 12 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 11|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**11:1** Unto the end; for the octave, a psalm for David. ^11-1

**11:2** Save me, O Lord, for there is now no saint: truths are decayed from among the children of men. ^11-2

**11:3** They have spoken vain things every one to his neighbour: with deceitful lips, and with a double heart have they spoken. ^11-3

**11:4** May the Lord destroy all deceitful lips, and the tongue that speaketh proud things. ^11-4

**11:5** Who have said: We will magnify our tongue; our lips are our own; who is Lord over us? ^11-5

**11:6** By reason of the misery of the needy, and the groans of the poor, now will I arise, saith the Lord. I win set him in safety; I will deal confidently in his regard. ^11-6

**11:7** The words of the Lord are pure words: as silver tried by the fire, purged from the earth refined seven times. ^11-7

**11:8** Thou, O Lord, wilt preserve us: and keep us from this generation for ever. ^11-8

**11:9** The wicked walk round about: according to thy highness, thou best multiplied the children of men. ^11-9
