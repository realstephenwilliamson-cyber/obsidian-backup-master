## Mūla

भोत्स्यते यत्ततः प्रोक्तं तत्साम्यात्मकमादितः ।
सुखं सत्त्वं प्रकाशत्वात् प्रकाशो ह्लाद उच्यते ॥ ^mula

bhotsyate yattataḥ proktaṃ tatsāmyātmakamāditaḥ |
sukhaṃ sattvaṃ prakāśatvāt prakāśo hlāda ucyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 08 - Chapter Nine, part 6#^pg226|p. 209]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=226>))
>
> In this way, (the existence of) this pure (generic) objectivity (is inferred from) what one experiences from pleasure, pain and delusion. Thus, it is said that (that exists) originally as the state of (balanced) equality (sāmya) (between these three). (with 9.220cd–221ab)
>
> Pleasure is saftva because it is light, and light is said to be joy (hlāda). Suffering is rajas because it is action, and (that) action oscillates between that and its absence (tadatatkrama). Delusion is tamas, which covers over (and obscures the mind), because it is associated with an absence of light. (with 9.221cd–222)
