## Mūla

उवाच सद्योज्योतिश्च वृत्तौ स्वायम्भुवस्य तत् ।
बाढमेको हि पाशात्मा शब्दोऽन्यश्च शिवात्मकः ॥ ^mula

uvāca sadyojyotiśca vṛttau svāyambhuvasya tat |
bāḍhameko hi pāśātmā śabdo'nyaśca śivātmakaḥ || ^iast

> And (so too) Sadyojyotis has said in his commentary on the Svāyambhuva: ‘Yes indeed! the Assemblage of Sounds (śabda) (has two natures,) one is that it is the bondage (that binds the soul) (pāśa) and the other that is Śiva.’ Therefore it is reasonable (to maintain) that (one and the) same phoneme (can be both) purifying (śodhaka) and purified (śodhya).
