## Homiletical Proclamation (*Praedicatio*)

> [!reflection] The Door of Humility
> We encounter Christ today not as an imperial checkpoint, but as the Way itself. When Jesus tells Thomas, *"No one comes to the Father except through me,"* he is answering a disciple who is demanding a geographical road map (*"Lord, we do not know where you are going; how can we know the way?"*).
> 
> As Augustine observed in Tractate 69, Christ does not point down a road external to Himself; He dissolves the distance between the traveler and the destination. In the parallel language of Parashat Emor, holiness is not a private possession guarded by a caste; it is the burning presence of the Divine entering mortal flesh.
