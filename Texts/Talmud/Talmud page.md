# Talmud page

One side of a daf of the [[Talmud]] laid out like the printed page: the text down the middle (the translation in bold, Rabbi Steinsaltz's explanation around it), beside it the halakhot of the *Mishneh Torah* that its Ein Mishpat names and the vault's own passages on these lines, Ein Mishpat, Torah Or and Mesoret HaShas in the margins, and at the edges the notes that cite this side and your own `question::`, `claim::` and `connects::` lines on it. Type a daf in the box (*Berakhot 2a*) or use the arrows; the side is kept in this note's *amud* property, and when that is empty the page shows today's daf from the [[Daf Yomi 2026-2029|Daf Yomi table]]. *Translation only* folds the explanation away. On a narrow screen the columns stack under the text.
