# Proverbs 27 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Proverbs 26|← Proverbs 26]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Proverbs 28|Proverbs 28 →]]

**27:1** Boast not for tomorrow, for thou knowest not what the day to come may bring forth. ^27-1

**27:2** Let another praise thee, and not thy own mouth: a stranger, and not thy own lips. ^27-2

**27:3** A stone is heavy, and sand weighty: but the anger of a fool is heavier than them both. ^27-3

**27:4** Anger hath no mercy, nor fury when it breaketh forth: and who can bear the violence of one provoked? ^27-4

**27:5** Open rebuke is better than hidden love. ^27-5

**27:6** Better are the wounds of a friend, than the deceitful kisses of an enemy. ^27-6

**27:7** A soul that is full shall tread upon the honeycomb: and a soul that is hungry shall take even bitter for sweet. ^27-7

**27:8** As a bird that wandereth from her nest, so is a man that leaveth his place. ^27-8

**27:9** Ointment and perfumes rejoice the heart: and the good counsels of a friend are sweet to the soul. ^27-9

**27:10** Thy own friend, and thy father’s friend forsake not: and go not into thy brother’s house in the day of thy affliction. Better is a neighbour that is near, than a brother afar off. ^27-10

**27:11** Study wisdom, my son, and make my heart joyful, that thou mayst give an answer to him that reproacheth. ^27-11

**27:12** The prudent man seeing evil hideth himself: little ones passing on have suffered losses. ^27-12

**27:13** Take away his garment that hath been surety for a stranger: and take from him a pledge for strangers. ^27-13

**27:14** He that blesseth his neighbour with a loud voice, rising in the night, shall be like to him that curseth. ^27-14

**27:15** Roofs dropping through in a cold day, and a contentious woman are alike. ^27-15

**27:16** He that retaineth her, is as he that would hold the wind, and shall call in the oil of his right hand. ^27-16

**27:17** Iron sharpeneth iron, so a man sharpeneth the countenance of his friend. ^27-17

**27:18** He that keepeth the fig tree, shall eat the fruit thereof: and he that is the keeper of his master, shall be glorified. ^27-18

**27:19** As the faces of them that look therein, shine in the water, so-the hearts of men are laid open to the wise. ^27-19

**27:20** Hell and destruction are never filled: so the eyes of men are never satisfied. ^27-20

**27:21** As silver is tried in the fining-pot and gold in the furnace: so a man is tried by the mouth of him that praiseth. The heart of the wicked seeketh after evils, but the righteous heart seeketh after knowledge. ^27-21

**27:22** Though thou shouldst bray a fool in the mortar, as when a pestle striketh upon sodden barley, his folly would not be taken from him. ^27-22

**27:23** Be diligent to know the countenance of thy cattle, and consider thy own flocks: ^27-23

**27:24** For thou shalt not always have power: but a crown shall be given to generation and generation. ^27-24

**27:25** The meadows are open, and the green herbs have appeared, and the hay is gathered out of the mountains. ^27-25

**27:26** Lambs are for thy clothing: and kids for the price of the field. ^27-26

**27:27** Let the milk of the goats be enough for thy food, and for the necessities of thy house, and for maintenance for thy handmaids. ^27-27
