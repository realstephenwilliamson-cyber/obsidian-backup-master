## Chapter 85

**85:1** For the leader. Of the Korahites. A psalm. ^85-1

**85:2** O LORD, You will favor[^1] Your land,  
restore[^2] Jacob’s fortune; ^85-2

**85:3** You will forgive[^3] Your people’s iniquity,  
pardon[^4] all their sins; *selah* ^85-3

**85:4** You will withdraw[^5] all Your anger,  
turn[^6] away from Your rage. ^85-4

**85:5** Turn again, O God, our helper,  
revoke Your displeasure with us. ^85-5

**85:6** Will You be angry with us forever,  
prolong Your wrath for all generations? ^85-6

**85:7** Surely You will revive us again,  
so that Your people may rejoice in You. ^85-7

**85:8** Show us, O LORD, Your faithfulness;  
grant us Your deliverance. ^85-8

**85:9** Let me hear what God, the LORD, will speak;  
He will promise well-being to His people, His faithful ones;  
may they not turn to folly. ^85-9

**85:10** His help is very near those who fear Him,  
to make His glory dwell in our land. ^85-10

**85:11** Faithfulness and truth meet;  
justice and well-being kiss. ^85-11

**85:12** Truth springs up from the earth;  
justice looks down from heaven. ^85-12

**85:13** The LORD also bestows His bounty;  
our land yields its produce. ^85-13

**85:14** Justice goes before Him  
as He sets out on His way. ^85-14

[^1]: Or “have favored.”
[^2]: Or “have restored.”
[^3]: Or “have forgiven.”
[^4]: Or “have pardoned.”
[^5]: Or “have withdrawn.”
[^6]: Or “have turned.”
