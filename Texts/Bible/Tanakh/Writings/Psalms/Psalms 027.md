## Chapter 27

**27:1** Of David.  
The LORD is my light and my help;  
whom should I fear?  
The LORD is the stronghold of my life,  
whom should I dread? ^27-1

**27:2** When evil men assail me  
to devour my flesh[^1]—  
it is they, my foes and my enemies,  
who stumble and fall. ^27-2

**27:3** Should an army besiege me,  
my heart would have no fear;  
should war beset me,  
still would I be confident. ^27-3

**27:4** One thing I ask of the LORD,  
only that do I seek:  
to live in the house of the LORD  
all the days of my life,  
to gaze upon the beauty of the LORD,  
to frequent[^2] His temple. ^27-4

**27:5** He will shelter me in His pavilion  
on an evil day,  
grant me the protection of His tent,  
raise me high upon a rock. ^27-5

**27:6** Now is my head high  
over my enemies round about;  
I sacrifice in His tent with shouts of joy,  
singing and chanting a hymn to the LORD. ^27-6

**27:7** Hear, O LORD, when I cry aloud;  
have mercy on me, answer me. ^27-7

**27:8** In Your behalf[^3] my heart says:  
“Seek My face!”  
O LORD, I seek Your face. ^27-8

**27:9** Do not hide Your face from me;  
do not thrust aside Your servant in anger;  
You have ever been my help.  
Do not forsake me, do not abandon me,  
O God, my deliverer. ^27-9

**27:10** Though my father and mother abandon me,  
the LORD will take me in. ^27-10

**27:11** Show me Your way, O LORD,  
and lead me on a level path  
because of my watchful foes. ^27-11

**27:12** Do not subject me to the will of my foes,  
for false witnesses and unjust accusers  
have appeared against me. ^27-12

**27:13** Had I not the assurance  
that I would enjoy the goodness of the LORD  
in the land of the living… ^27-13

**27:14** Look to the LORD;  
be strong and of good courage!  
O look to the LORD! ^27-14

[^1]: Or “to slander me”; cf. Dan. 3.8; 6.25.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
