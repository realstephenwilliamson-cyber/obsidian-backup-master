## Chapter 133

**133:1** A song of ascents. Of David.  
How good and how pleasant it is  
that brothers dwell together. ^133-1

**133:2** It is like fine oil on the head  
running down onto the beard,  
the beard of Aaron,  
that comes down over the collar of his robe; ^133-2

**133:3** like the dew of Hermon  
that falls upon the mountains of Zion.  
There the LORD ordained blessing,  
everlasting life. ^133-3
