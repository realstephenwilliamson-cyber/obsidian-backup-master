## Chapter 22

**22:1** For the leader; on *ayyeleth ha-shaḥar*.[^1] A psalm of David. ^22-1

**22:2** My God, my God,  
why have You abandoned me;  
why so far from delivering me  
and from my anguished roaring? ^22-2

**22:3** My God,  
I cry by day—You answer not;  
by night, and have no respite. ^22-3

**22:4** But You are the Holy One,  
enthroned,  
the Praise of Israel.[^2] ^22-4

**22:5** In You our fathers trusted;  
they trusted, and You rescued them. ^22-5

**22:6** To You they cried out  
and they escaped;  
in You they trusted  
and were not disappointed. ^22-6

**22:7** But I am a worm, less than human;  
scorned by men, despised by people. ^22-7

**22:8** All who see me mock me;  
they curl their lips,[^3]  
they shake their heads. ^22-8

**22:9** “Let him commit himself to the LORD;  
let Him rescue him,  
let Him save him,  
for He is pleased with him.” ^22-9

**22:10** You drew me[^4] from the womb,  
made me secure at my mother’s breast. ^22-10

**22:11** I became Your charge at birth;  
from my mother’s womb You have been my God. ^22-11

**22:12** Do not be far from me,  
for trouble is near,  
and there is none to help. ^22-12

**22:13** Many bulls surround me,  
mighty ones of Bashan encircle me. ^22-13

**22:14** They open their mouths at me  
like tearing, roaring lions. ^22-14

**22:15** My life ebbs away:[^5]  
all my bones are disjointed;  
my heart is like wax,  
melting within me; ^22-15

**22:16** my vigor dries up like a shard;  
my tongue cleaves to my palate;  
You commit me to the dust of death. ^22-16

**22:17** Dogs surround me;  
a pack of evil ones closes in on me,  
like lions [they maul] my hands and feet.[^6] ^22-17

**22:18** I take the count of all my bones  
while they look on and gloat. ^22-18

**22:19** They divide my clothes among themselves,  
casting lots for my garments. ^22-19

**22:20** But You, O LORD, be not far off;  
my strength, hasten to my aid. ^22-20

**22:21** Save my life from the sword,  
my precious life[^7] from the clutches of a dog. ^22-21

**22:22** Deliver me from a lion’s mouth;  
from the horns of wild oxen rescue[^8] me. ^22-22

**22:23** Then will I proclaim Your fame to my brethren,  
praise You in the congregation. ^22-23

**22:24** You who fear the LORD, praise Him!  
All you offspring of Jacob, honor Him!  
Be in dread of Him, all you offspring of Israel! ^22-24

**22:25** For He did not scorn, He did not spurn  
the plea[^9] of the lowly;  
He did not hide His face from him;  
when he cried out to Him, He listened. ^22-25

**22:26** Because of You I offer praise[^10] in the great congregation;  
I pay my vows in the presence of His worshipers. ^22-26

**22:27** Let the lowly eat and be satisfied;  
let all who seek the LORD praise Him.  
Always be of good cheer! ^22-27

**22:28** Let all the ends of the earth pay heed and turn to the LORD,  
and the peoples of all nations prostrate themselves before You; ^22-28

**22:29** for kingship is the LORD’s  
and He rules the nations. ^22-29

**22:30** All those in full vigor shall eat and prostrate themselves;  
all those at death’s door, whose spirits flag,  
shall bend the knee before Him.[^11] ^22-30

**22:31** Offspring shall serve Him;  
the Lord’s fame shall be proclaimed to the generation ^22-31

**22:32** to come;  
they shall tell of His beneficence to people yet to be born,  
for He has acted. ^22-32

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Or “But You are holy, enthroned upon the praises of Israel.”
[^3]: Lit. “they open wide with a lip.”
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^5]: Lit. “I am poured out like water.”
[^6]: With Rashi; cf. Isa. 38.13.
[^7]: Lit. “only one.”
[^8]: Lit. “answer.”
[^9]: Or “plight.”
[^10]: Lit. “From You is my praise.”
[^11]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain; others “All the fat ones of the earth shall eat and worship; / All they that go down to the dust shall kneel before Him, / Even he that cannot keep his soul alive.”
