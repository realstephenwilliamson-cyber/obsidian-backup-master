## Chapter 23

**23:1** When David reached a ripe old age, he made his son Solomon king over Israel. ^23-1

**23:2** Then David assembled all the officers of Israel and the priests and the Levites. ^23-2

**23:3** The Levites, from the age of thirty and upward, were counted; the head-count of their males was 38,000: ^23-3

**23:4** of these there were 24,000 in charge of the work of the House of the LORD, 6,000 officers and magistrates, ^23-4

**23:5** 4,000 gatekeepers, and 4,000 for praising the LORD “with instruments I devised for singing praises.” ^23-5

**23:6** David formed them into divisions:  
The sons of Levi: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari. ^23-6

**23:7** The Gershonites: Ladan and Shimei. ^23-7

**23:8** The sons of Ladan: Jehiel the chief, Zetham, and Joel—3. ^23-8

**23:9** The sons of Shimei: Shelomith, Haziel, and Haran—3. These were the chiefs of the clans of the Ladanites. ^23-9

**23:10** And the sons of Shimei: Jahath, Zina, Jeush, and Beriah; these were the sons of Shimei—4. ^23-10

**23:11** Jahath was the chief and Zizah the second, but Jeush and Beriah did not have many children, so they were enrolled together as a single clan. ^23-11

**23:12** The sons of Kohath: Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel—4. ^23-12

**23:13** The sons of Amram: Aaron and Moses. Aaron was set apart, he and his sons, forever, to be consecrated as most holy, to make burnt offerings to the LORD and serve Him and pronounce blessings in His name forever. ^23-13

**23:14** As for Moses, the man of God, his sons were named after the tribe of Levi. ^23-14

**23:15** The sons of Moses: Gershom and Eliezer. ^23-15

**23:16** The sons of Gershom: Shebuel the chief. ^23-16

**23:17** And the sons of Eliezer were: Rehabiah the chief. Eliezer had no other sons, but the sons of Rehabiah were very numerous. ^23-17

**23:18** The sons of Izhar: Shelomith the chief. ^23-18

**23:19** The sons of Hebron: Jeriah the chief, Amariah the second, Jahaziel the third, and Jekameam the fourth. ^23-19

**23:20** The sons of Uzziel: Micah the chief and Isshiah the second. ^23-20

**23:21** The sons of Merari: Mahli and Mushi. The sons of Mahli: Eleazar and Kish. ^23-21

**23:22** Eleazar died having no sons but only daughters; the sons of Kish, their kinsmen, married them. ^23-22

**23:23** The sons of Mushi: Mahli, Eder, and Jeremoth—3. ^23-23

**23:24** These are the sons of Levi by clans, with their clan chiefs as they were enrolled, with a list of their names by heads, who did the work of the service of the House of the LORD from the age of twenty and upward. ^23-24

**23:25** For David said, “The LORD God of Israel has given rest to His people and made His dwelling in Jerusalem forever. ^23-25

**23:26** Therefore the Levites need not carry the Tabernacle and all its various service vessels.” ^23-26

**23:27** Among the last acts of David was the counting of the Levites from the age of twenty and upward. ^23-27

**23:28** For their appointment was alongside the Aaronites for the service of the House of the LORD, to look after the courts and the chambers, and the purity of all the holy things, and the performance of the service of the House of God, ^23-28

**23:29** and the rows of bread, and the fine flour for the meal offering, and the unleavened wafers, and the cakes made on the griddle and soaked, and every measure of capacity and length; ^23-29

**23:30** and to be present every morning to praise and extol the LORD, and at evening too, ^23-30

**23:31** and whenever offerings were made to the LORD, according to the quantities prescribed for them, on sabbaths, new moons and holidays, regularly, before the LORD; ^23-31

**23:32** and so to keep watch over the Tent of Meeting, over the holy things, and over the Aaronites their kinsmen, for the service of the House of the LORD. ^23-32
