# Mālinīvijayottara, chapter 6: The Path of the Body

30 verses, 1–30. Text and translation by Mark Dyczkowski, from *Mālinī*, October–December 2023 and January–April 2024: [PDF pp. 1–7](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapter 6 (2023).pdf#page=1>) · [PDF pp. 28–35](<Library/Trika/Malini (journal), January-April 2024.pdf#page=28>).

Dyczkowski's introduction to the chapter: [[Malinivijayottara 06 - Introduction (Dyczkowski)]].

## Verses


### The Path in the Body and the Proce- dures for its Deposition 1. The Deposition of the Thirty-six Principles onto the Body

- [[Malinivijayottara 06.001|6.1]] athāsya vastujātasya yathā dehe vyavasthitiḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 06.002|6.2]] pādādhaḥ pañcabhūtānī vyāptyā dvyaṅgulayā nyaset
- [[Malinivijayottara 06.003|6.3]] tadvat tadupariṣṭāt tu parvaṣaṭkāvasānakam
- [[Malinivijayottara 06.004|6.4]] tato māyāditattvāni catvāri susamāhitaḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 06.005|6.5]] śivatattvaṁ tataḥ paścāt tejorūpam anākulam

### 2) The Deposition of the Five Ele- ments onto the Body

- [[Malinivijayottara 06.006|6.6]] ṣaṭtriṁśattattvabhedena nyāso 'yaṁ samudāhṛtaḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 06.007|6.7]] nābher ūrdhvaṁ tu yāvat syāt parvaṣaṭkam anukramāt
- [[Malinivijayottara 06.008|6.8]] dvāviṁśatiś ca parvāṇi tadūrdhvaṁ tejasāvṛtam
- [[Malinivijayottara 06.009|6.9]] ākāśāntaṁ paraṁ śāntaṁ sarveṣāṁ vyāpakaṁ smaret

### Chapter Six of the Mālinīvijayottara Tantra [... continued from Previous Issue] – Rajanaka Mark Dyczkowski – 3) The Deposition of the Three Principles

- [[Malinivijayottara 06.010|6.10]] trikhaṇḍe kaṇṭhaparyantam ātmatattvam udāhṛtam

### 4) The Deposition of the Path of the Worlds on the Body

- [[Malinivijayottara 06.011|6.11]] evaṁ tattvavidhiḥ prokto bhuvanādhvā tathocyate
- [[Malinivijayottara 06.012|6.12]] gulphāntaṁ vinyased dhyātvā yathāvad anupūrvaśaḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 06.013|6.13]] vinyaset tu dviraṇḍāntaṁ tryaṅgulaṁ chagalāṇḍakam
- [[Malinivijayottara 06.014|6.14]] tato 'py ardhāṅgulavyāptyā puraṣaṭkam anukramāt
- [[Malinivijayottara 06.015|6.15]] uttarādikramād dvyekabhedo vidyādike traye
- [[Malinivijayottara 06.016|6.16]] maṇḍalādhipatīnāṁ tu vyāptir ardhāṅgulā matā
- [[Malinivijayottara 06.017|6.17]] dvitīyasya tu saṁpūrṇā pañcakaṁ samudāhṛtam

### 5) The Lower (Apara) Procedure Deposition of the Parts of Mantra in relation to the Categories of the Six- fold Path

- [[Malinivijayottara 06.018|6.18]] bhuvanādhvavidhāv atra pūrvavac cintayec chivam
- [[Malinivijayottara 06.019|6.19]] teṣāṁ tanmantravad vyāptir yathedānīṁ tathā śṛṇu
- [[Malinivijayottara 06.020|6.20]] daśāṅgulāni trīṇy asmād ekaṁ pañcadaśāṅgulam

### The Deposition of the Parāparā Vidyā

- [[Malinivijayottara 06.021|6.21]] ūnaviṁśatike bhede padānāṁ vyāptir ucyate
- [[Malinivijayottara 06.022|6.22]] saptāṅgulāni catvāri daśāṅgulamataḥ param
- [[Malinivijayottara 06.023|6.23]] dvādaśāṅgulamanyacca dve 'nye pañcāṅgule pṛthak
- [[Malinivijayottara 06.024|6.24]] vyāpakaṁ padam anyac ca *** tat parikīrtitam

### 6) The Intermediate (Parāpara) Procedure

- [[Malinivijayottara 06.025|6.25]] pūrvavat pṛthivītattvaṁ vijñeyaṁ caturaṅgulam

### 7) The Supreme (Para) Procedure

- [[Malinivijayottara 06.026|6.26]] pradhānaṁ tryaṅgulaṁ jñeyaṁ śeṣaṁ pūrvavad ādiśet

### Conclusion

- [[Malinivijayottara 06.027|6.27]] catuṣparva pradhānaṁ ca śeṣaṁ pūrvavadāśrayet
- [[Malinivijayottara 06.028|6.28]] tattvamārgavidhānena jñātavyaḥ paramārthataḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 06.029|6.29]] tritayatvaṁ prakurvīta tattvavarṇoktavartmanā
- [[Malinivijayottara 06.030|6.30]] prakartavyā vidhānena dīkṣā sarvaphalapradā

## Closing

This is the sixth chapter of the *Mālinīvijayottara Tantra* concerning the Path of the Body.

*iti śrīmālinīvijayottare tantre dehamārgādhikāraḥ ṣaṣṭhaḥ* ||
