# Job 7 (NRSVue)

## Job: My suffering is without end

**7:1** “Do not human beings have a hard service on earth, and are not their days like the days of a laborer? ^7-1

**7:2** Like a slave who longs for the shadow, and like laborers who look for their wages, ^7-2

**7:3** so I am allotted months of emptiness, and nights of misery are apportioned to me. ^7-3

**7:4** When I lie down I say, ‘When shall I rise?’ But the night is long, and I am full of tossing until dawn. ^7-4

**7:5** My flesh is clothed with worms and dirt; my skin hardens, then breaks out again. ^7-5

**7:6** My days are swifter than a weaver’s shuttle and come to their end without hope.^[Or *as the thread runs out*] ^7-6

**7:7** “Remember that my life is a breath; my eye will never again see good. ^7-7

**7:8** The eye that beholds me will see me no more; while your eyes are upon me, I shall be gone. ^7-8

**7:9** As the cloud fades and vanishes, so those who go down to Sheol do not come up; ^7-9

**7:10** they return no more to their houses, nor do their places know them any more. ^7-10

**7:11** “Therefore I will not restrain my mouth; I will speak in the anguish of my spirit; I will complain in the bitterness of my soul. ^7-11

**7:12** Am I the Sea or the Dragon that you set a guard over me? ^7-12

**7:13** When I say, ‘My bed will comfort me, my couch will ease my complaint,’ ^7-13

**7:14** then you scare me with dreams and terrify me with visions, ^7-14

**7:15** so that I would choose strangling and death rather than this body. ^7-15

**7:16** I loathe my life; I would not live forever. Let me alone, for my days are a breath. ^7-16

**7:17** What are humans, that you make so much of them, that you set your mind on them, ^7-17

**7:18** visit them every morning, test them every moment? ^7-18

**7:19** Will you not look away from me for a while, let me alone until I swallow my spittle? ^7-19

**7:20** If I sin, what do I do to you, you watcher of humanity? Why have you made me your target? Why have I become a burden to you? ^7-20

**7:21** Why do you not pardon my transgression and take away my iniquity? For now I shall lie in the earth; you will seek me, but I shall not be.” ^7-21
