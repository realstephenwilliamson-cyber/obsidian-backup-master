## Mūla

अभिन्नं संविदश्चैतच्चक्राणां चक्रवालकम् ।
स्वाम्यावरणभेदेन बहुधा तत्प्रयोजयेत् ॥ ^mula

abhinnaṃ saṃvidaścaitaccakrāṇāṃ cakravālakam |
svāmyāvaraṇabhedena bahudhā tatprayojayet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 07 - Chapter Thirty-Three#^pg200|p. 182]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=200>))
>
> In this way, this consciousness is the reflective awareness of the essential nature of the object of worship. And that surrounding circle of wheels (around the centre) is one with consciousness. Many are the ways in which that may be applied (by the devotee), in accord with the various surrounding enclosures (of the attendant deities) of the Lord. (with 33.28cd–29)
