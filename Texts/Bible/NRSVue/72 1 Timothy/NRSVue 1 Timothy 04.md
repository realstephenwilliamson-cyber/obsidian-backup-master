# 1 Timothy 4 (NRSVue)

Compare: [[1 Timothy 04|BSB and SBLGNT]] · [[DRB 1 Timothy 04|Douay-Rheims]] · [[JANT 1 Timothy 04|JANT notes]]

## False asceticism

**4:1** Now the Spirit expressly says that in later^[Or *the last*] times some will renounce^[Or *move away from*] the faith by paying attention to deceitful spirits and teachings of demons, ^4-1

**4:2** through the hypocrisy of liars whose consciences are seared with a hot iron. ^4-2

**4:3** They forbid marriage and abstain from certain foods, which God created to be received with thanksgiving by those who believe and know the truth. ^4-3

**4:4** For everything created by God is good, and nothing is to be rejected, provided it is received with thanksgiving, ^4-4

**4:5** for it is sanctified by God’s word and by prayer. ^4-5

## A good minister of Jesus Christ

**4:6** If you put these instructions before the brothers and sisters, you will be a good servant^[Or *deacon*] of Christ Jesus, nourished on the words of the faith and of the sound teaching that you have followed. ^4-6

**4:7** Have nothing to do with profane and foolish tales. Train yourself in godliness, ^4-7

**4:8** for, while physical training is of some value, godliness is valuable in every way, holding promise for both the present life and the life to come. ^4-8

**4:9** The saying is sure and worthy of full acceptance. ^4-9

**4:10** For to this end we toil and suffer reproach,^[Other ancient authorities read *struggle*] because we have our hope set on the living God, who is the Savior of all people, especially of those who believe. ^4-10

**4:11** Command and teach these things. ^4-11

**4:12** Let no one despise your youth, but set the believers an example in speech and conduct, in love, in faith, in purity. ^4-12

**4:13** Until I arrive, give attention to the public reading of scripture,^[Gk *to the reading*] to exhorting, to teaching. ^4-13

**4:14** Do not neglect the gift that is in you, which was given to you through prophecy with the laying on of hands by the council of elders. ^4-14

**4:15** Put these things into practice, devote yourself to them, so that all may see your progress. ^4-15

**4:16** Pay close attention to yourself and to your teaching; continue in these things, for in doing this you will save both yourself and your hearers. ^4-16
