# Zohar 1.231a

← [[Zohar 1.230b]] · [[Zohar (Soncino)]] · [[Zohar 1.231b]] →

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 3.07 - Parashat Va-Yhi#^z1-231a|Parashat Va-Yḥi, at 1:231a]]

*Volume 1, folio 231a* · Vayechi

*Passage Vayechi 43:439 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 1.230b#^vayechi-43-439|Vayechi 43:439]].*

**Vayechi 43:440** “I have sworn unto David my servant”, to wit, that he will always be established in this *Zaddik*, the foundation of the world, save in the time of *galuth*, when the flow of blessing is cut off, and faith is defective, and all joy is banished. During this period, at nightfall, joy no longer enters before the King. ^vayechi-43-440

**Vayechi 43:441** Yet, though rejoicings do not enter, angels stand outside and chant hymns, and at midnight when the impulse from below arrives on high, God arouses all the hosts of the heaven for lamentation and strikes the firmament, causing upper and lower worlds to quake; ^vayechi-43-441

**Vayechi 43:442** nor is there any respite save when those below commence to study the Torah. Then God and all those with Him listen with joy to that voice, and relief is felt. For on the day on which the Sanctuary below was destroyed, God swore that He would not enter the celestial Jerusalem until Israel should enter the earthly Jerusalem. ^vayechi-43-442

**Vayechi 43:443** Now all those singers stand outside and chant hymns in the three watches of the night and intone praises, and all the hosts of the heavens sing at night and Israel by day, nor is the sanctification recited above until it is recited by Israel below, and only then do all the hosts of heaven sanctify the holy name together. Hence, Israel are holy and are sanctified by upper and lower angels, since the sanctification of the holy name is complete only when uttered above and below together.’ ^vayechi-43-443

**Vayechi 43:444** R. Jose discoursed on the verse: *Whereupon were the foundations thereof fastened* ? (Job 38, 6). He said: ‘When God created the world, He established it on seven pillars, but upon what those pillars rest no one may know, since it is a recondite and inscrutable mystery. ^vayechi-43-444

**Vayechi 43:445** The world did not come into being until God took a certain stone, which is called the “foundation stone”, and cast it into the abyss so that it held fast there, and from it the world was planted. This is the central point of the universe, and on this point stands the holy of holies. This is the stone referred to in the verses, “Who laid the corner-stone thereof” (*Ibid.* 6), “the stone of testing, the precious corner-stone” (Is. 28, 16), and “the stone that the builders despise became the head of the corner” (Ps. 118, 22). ^vayechi-43-445

**Vayechi 43:446** This stone is compounded of fire, water, and air, and rests on the abyss. Sometimes water flows from it and fills the deep. This stone is set as a sign in the centre of the world. It is referred to in the words, “And Jacob took a stone and set it as a pillar” (Gen. 31, 45). ^vayechi-43-446

**Vayechi 43:447** Not that he took this stone, which was created from the beginning, but he established it above and below, by making there a “house of God”. ^vayechi-43-447

**Vayechi 43:448** This stone has on it seven eyes, as it is written, “On one stone seven eyes” (Zech. 3, 9), and it is called “foundation stone”, for one thing because the world was planted from it, and for another because God set it as a source of blessing to the world. ^vayechi-43-448

**Vayechi 43:449** Now at sunset, the Cherubim which stood in that place used to strike their wings together and spread them out, and when the sound of the beating of their wings was heard above, those angels who chanted hymns in the night began to sing, in order that the glory of God might ascend from below on high. The striking of the Cherubim’s wings itself intoned the psalm, “Behold, bless ye the Lord, all ye servants of the Lord… Iift up your hands to the sanctuary, etc.” (Ps. 133). This was the signal for the heavenly angels to commence. ^vayechi-43-449

**Vayechi 43:450** At the second watch the Cherubim again beat their wings, giving the signal to the angels of that watch. The psalm of the Cherubim this time was “They that trust in the Lord are like Mount Zion, etc.” (Ps. 125). ^vayechi-43-450
