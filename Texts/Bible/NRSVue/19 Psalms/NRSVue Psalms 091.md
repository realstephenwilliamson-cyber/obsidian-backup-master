# Psalm 91 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 090|← Psalm 90]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 092|Psalm 92 →]]

## Assurance of God’s protection

**91:1** You who live in the shelter of the Most High, who abide in the shadow of the Almighty,^[Traditional rendering of Heb *Shaddai*] ^91-1

**91:2** will say to the LORD, “My refuge and my fortress; my God, in whom I trust.” ^91-2

**91:3** For he will deliver you from the snare of the hunter and from the deadly pestilence; ^91-3

**91:4** he will cover you with his pinions, and under his wings you will find refuge; his faithfulness is a shield and defense. ^91-4

**91:5** You will not fear the terror of the night or the arrow that flies by day ^91-5

**91:6** or the pestilence that stalks in darkness or the destruction that wastes at noonday. ^91-6

**91:7** A thousand may fall at your side, ten thousand at your right hand, but it will not come near you. ^91-7

**91:8** You will only look with your eyes and see the punishment of the wicked. ^91-8

**91:9** Because you have made the LORD your refuge,^[Cn: Heb *Because you,LORD, are my refuge; you have made*] the Most High your dwelling place, ^91-9

**91:10** no evil shall befall you, no scourge come near your tent. ^91-10

**91:11** For he will command his angels concerning you to guard you in all your ways. ^91-11

**91:12** On their hands they will bear you up, so that you will not dash your foot against a stone. ^91-12

**91:13** You will tread on the lion and the adder; the young lion and the serpent you will trample under foot. ^91-13

**91:14** Those who love me, I will deliver; I will protect those who know my name. ^91-14

**91:15** When they call to me, I will answer them; I will be with them in trouble; I will rescue them and honor them. ^91-15

**91:16** With long life I will satisfy them and show them my salvation. ^91-16
