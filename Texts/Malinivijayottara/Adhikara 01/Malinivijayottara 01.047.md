## Mūla

अनेन क्रमयोगेन संप्राप्तः परमं पदम् ।
न भूयः पशुतामेति शुद्धे स्वात्मनि तिष्ठति ॥ ^mula

anena kramayogena saṁprāptaḥ paramaṁ padam |
na bhūyaḥ paśutām eti śuddhe svātmani tiṣṭhati || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2022 – June 2022
> p. 48 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=24>))
>
> Once the supreme plane has been attained[^87] by means of this gradual union (kramayoga), one never returns to the fettered state, (but always) abides within one's own pure Self.[^88]

[^87]: Quoted in TĀ 22/6cd-7ab. Read saṁprāptaṁ for saṁprāptaḥ.
[^88]: TĀ 22/6cd-7ab, literal quote of MV 1/47. See there for Abhinavagupta’s interpretation of this verse.
