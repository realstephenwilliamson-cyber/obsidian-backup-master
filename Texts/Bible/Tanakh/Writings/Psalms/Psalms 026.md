## Chapter 26

**26:1** Of David.  
Vindicate me, O LORD,  
for I have walked without blame;  
I have trusted in the LORD;  
I have not faltered. ^26-1

**26:2** Probe me, O LORD, and try me,  
test my heart and mind;[^1] ^26-2

**26:3** for my eyes are on Your steadfast love;  
I have set my course by it.[^2] ^26-3

**26:4** I do not consort with scoundrels,  
or mix with hypocrites; ^26-4

**26:5** I detest the company of evil men,  
and do not consort with the wicked; ^26-5

**26:6** I wash my hands in innocence,  
and walk around Your altar, O LORD, ^26-6

**26:7** raising my voice in thanksgiving,  
and telling all Your wonders. ^26-7

**26:8** O LORD, I love Your temple abode,  
the dwelling-place of Your glory. ^26-8

**26:9** Do not sweep me away with sinners,  
or [snuff out] my life with murderers, ^26-9

**26:10** who have schemes at their fingertips,  
and hands full of bribes. ^26-10

**26:11** But I walk without blame;  
redeem me, have mercy on me! ^26-11

**26:12** My feet are on level ground.  
In assemblies I will bless the LORD. ^26-12

[^1]: Lit. “kidneys and heart.”
[^2]: Or “I am aware of Your faithfulness, and always walk in Your true [path].”
