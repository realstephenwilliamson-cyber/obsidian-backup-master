# Isaiah 12 (Douay-Rheims)

*Isaias 12 in the Douay-Rheims*

[[DRB Isaiah 11|← Isaiah 11]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Isaiah 13|Isaiah 13 →]]

**12:1** And thou shalt say in that day: I will give thanks to thee, O Lord, for thou wast angry with me: thy wrath is turned away, and thou hast comforted me. ^12-1

**12:2** Behold, God is my saviour, I will deal confidently, and will not fear: O because the Lord is my strength, and my praise, and he is become my salvation. ^12-2

**12:3** You shall draw waters with joy out of the saviour’s fountains: ^12-3

**12:4** And you shall say in that day: Praise ye the Lord, and call upon his name: make his works known among the people: remember that his name is high. ^12-4

**12:5** Sing ye to the Lord, for he hath done great things: shew this forth in all the earth. ^12-5

**12:6** Rejoice, and praise, O thou habitation of Sion: for great is he that is in the midst of thee, the Holy One of Israel. ^12-6
