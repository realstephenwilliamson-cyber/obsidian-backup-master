## Mūla

तद्बाह्यमिह तत्सिद्धिविशेषाय न मुक्तये ।
आभ्यन्तरं नगाग्रादि देहान्तःप्राणयोजनम् ॥ ^mula

tadbāhyamiha tatsiddhiviśeṣāya na muktaye |
ābhyantaraṃ nagāgrādi dehāntaḥprāṇayojanam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 03 - Chapter Fifteen, part 2#^pg76|p. 58]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=76>))
>
> The places (commonly) mentioned (in the scriptures), namely, mountain peaks, riverbanks, solitary Liṅgas and the like, are outer places that serve here (only as means to attain) particular (desired) accomplishments (siddhi), not liberation. (with 15.80cd–81ab)
>
> The projection (yojana) of inner mountain peaks and the like into the vital breath within the body may serve as a means for adepts (sādhaka) (who desire worldly benefits) to attain accomplishment (siddhi), but not liberation. The place where a sacred seat (pīṭha) is located is said in the scriptures to be always fit for the performance of the sacrifice (yāga). And as that (place) has (both) an outer and inner form, it is clearly apparent (both) externally and within the body. (with 15.81cd–83ab)
