## Song of trust and security in God

*A Miktam of David.*

**16:1** Protect me, O God, for in you I take refuge. ^16-1

**16:2** I say to the LORD, “You are my Lord; I have no good apart from you.”^[Jerome Tg: Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^16-2

**16:3** As for the holy ones in the land, they are the noble ones in whom is all my delight. ^16-3

**16:4** Those who choose another god multiply their sorrows;^[Cn: Meaning of Heb uncertain] their drink offerings of blood I will not pour out or take their names upon my lips. ^16-4

**16:5** The LORD is my chosen portion and my cup; you hold my lot. ^16-5

**16:6** The boundary lines have fallen for me in pleasant places; I have a goodly heritage. ^16-6

**16:7** I bless the LORD, who gives me counsel; in the night also my heart instructs me. ^16-7

**16:8** I keep the LORD always before me; because he is at my right hand, I shall not be moved. ^16-8

**16:9** Therefore my heart is glad, and my soul rejoices; my body also rests secure. ^16-9

**16:10** For you do not give me up to Sheol or let your faithful one see the Pit. ^16-10

**16:11** You show me the path of life. In your presence there is fullness of joy; in your right hand are pleasures forevermore. ^16-11
