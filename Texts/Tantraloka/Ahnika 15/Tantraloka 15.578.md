## Mūla

मेलकार्धनिशाचर्या जनवर्जं च तन्नहि ।
मांसादिदाहगन्धं च जिघ्रेद्देवीप्रियो ह्यसौ ॥ ^mula

melakārdhaniśācaryā janavarjaṃ ca tannahi |
māṃsādidāhagandhaṃ ca jighreddevīpriyo hyasau || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 10 - Chapter Fifteen, part 9#^pg355|p. 337]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=355>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 15.580cd–581ab.
>
> One should keep the company (āsīta) of those who have true knowledge, and exert oneself with those who are enthused by knowledge. One should not approach (a woman) who has not been purified (asaṁskṛtā). Moreover, (if one does,) one should not render fruitless what is born from that (encounter). There, where the practice at midnight (ardhaniśācaryā) of the assembly (of male and female initiates) (melaka) takes place, should be (a secluded place where) there are no people (janavarja). One should smell the aroma of burning meat etc. (One who does so) is pleasing to the goddess. (with 15.577–578)
