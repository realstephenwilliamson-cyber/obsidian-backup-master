# Proverbs 11 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Proverbs 10|← Proverbs 10]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Proverbs 12|Proverbs 12 →]]

**11:1** A false balance is an abomination to the LORD, but an accurate weight is his delight. ^11-1

**11:2** When pride comes, then comes disgrace, but wisdom is with the humble. ^11-2

**11:3** The integrity of the upright guides them, but the crookedness of the treacherous destroys them. ^11-3

**11:4** Riches do not profit in the day of wrath, but righteousness delivers from death. ^11-4

**11:5** The righteousness of the blameless keeps their ways straight, but the wicked fall by their own wickedness. ^11-5

**11:6** The righteousness of the upright saves them, but the treacherous are taken captive by their schemes. ^11-6

**11:7** When the wicked die, hope perishes, and the expectation of strength comes to nothing. ^11-7

**11:8** The righteous are delivered from trouble, and the wicked come into it instead. ^11-8

**11:9** With their mouths the godless would destroy their neighbors, but by knowledge the righteous are delivered. ^11-9

**11:10** When it goes well with the righteous, the city rejoices, and when the wicked perish, there is jubilation. ^11-10

**11:11** By the blessing of the upright a city is exalted, but it is overthrown by the mouth of the wicked. ^11-11

**11:12** Whoever belittles another lacks sense, but an intelligent person remains silent. ^11-12

**11:13** A gossip goes about telling secrets, but one who is trustworthy in spirit keeps a confidence. ^11-13

**11:14** Where there is no guidance, a nation^[Or *an army*] falls, but in an abundance of counselors there is safety. ^11-14

**11:15** To guarantee loans for a stranger brings trouble, but there is safety in refusing to do so. ^11-15

**11:16** A gracious woman gets honor, but she who hates virtue is covered with shame.^[Compare Gk Syr: Heb lacks *but she . . . shame*] The timid become destitute,^[Gk: Heb lacks *The timid . . . destitute*] but the aggressive gain riches. ^11-16

**11:17** Those who are kind reward themselves, but the cruel do themselves harm. ^11-17

**11:18** The wicked earn no real gain, but those who sow righteousness get a true reward. ^11-18

**11:19** Whoever is steadfast in righteousness will live, but whoever pursues evil will die. ^11-19

**11:20** Crooked minds are an abomination to the LORD, but those of blameless ways are his delight. ^11-20

**11:21** Be assured, the wicked will not go unpunished, but those who are righteous will escape. ^11-21

**11:22** Like a gold ring in a pig’s snout is a beautiful woman without good sense. ^11-22

**11:23** The desire of the righteous ends only in good, the expectation of the wicked in wrath. ^11-23

**11:24** Some give freely yet grow all the richer; others withhold what is due and only suffer want. ^11-24

**11:25** A generous person will be enriched, and one who gives water will get water. ^11-25

**11:26** The people curse those who hold back grain, but a blessing is on the head of those who sell it. ^11-26

**11:27** Whoever diligently seeks good seeks favor, but evil comes to the one who searches for it. ^11-27

**11:28** Those who trust in their riches will wither,^[Cn: Heb *fall*] but the righteous will flourish like green leaves. ^11-28

**11:29** Those who trouble their households will inherit wind, and the fool will be servant to the wise. ^11-29

**11:30** The fruit of the righteous is a tree of life, and the wise capture souls. ^11-30

**11:31** If the righteous are repaid on earth, how much more the wicked and the sinner! ^11-31
