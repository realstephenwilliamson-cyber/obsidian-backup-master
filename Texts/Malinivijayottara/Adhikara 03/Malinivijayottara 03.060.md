## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2023 – March 2023
> p. 50 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=50>))
>
> **The Yoginīs, Limbs and Faces of the Vidyās**
>
> Eight very powerful Yoginīs are born from the limbs of Parāparā, seven of which should be known to consist of five, six, five, four, two, three and two letters in due order[^46] and one of eleven letters amongst which are two half letters (i.e., consonants without vowels).[^47] (with 3.59–60ab)

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2023 – March 2023
> p. 51 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=51>))
>
> The limbs of the three Vidyās (vidyāṅga) consist of the Living Being (S) along with the six long vowels together with (their) separate (respective) classes (of invocations) (jāti); the faces (are formed from the same Living Being (S)) with the five short vowels.[^49] (with 3.60cd–61ab)

[^48]: k, kh: *jātivibhedata*
[^46]: These are the seven parts of Parāparā Vidyā up to VAMA. See TĀ 30/20-26ab.
[^47]: These are the two phonemes Ṭ of the two PHAṬs. MV 3/59-60ab is quoted in the TĀv ad 30/15-28ab.
[^49]: MV 3/60cd-61ab is quoted in TĀv ad 30/62cd-64ab. Abhinavagupta provides an explanatory paraphrase of this verse: ‘The limbs (of Parā) are the letter S conjoined with the six long vowels, whereas the faces are also S, but conjoined with the five short vowels and the Void (Ṁ).’ TĀ 30/36cd-37ab. Thus, the six limbs are: SĀṀ, SĪṀ, SŪṀ, SAIṀ, SAUṀ and SA.H. The five faces are: SAṀ, SIṀ, SUṀ, SEṀ, and SoṀ. The MV does expressly state that these are the limbs and faces of the three Vidyās. Abhinavagupta omits this detail. Moreover, he cites this verse just after an exposition of Parā Vidyā. This suggests that he related these limbs and face just to Parā, probably considering her to be emblematic of all three Vidyās. The permutations based on the letter S with which Parā Vidyā begins further support this view. Concerning the limbs, see TĀ 30/36cd-37ab and the classes of invocations — *jāti* — ibid. 43cd-45ab.
