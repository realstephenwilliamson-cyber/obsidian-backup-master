## Mūla

किलाव्यक्तध्वनौ तस्मिन्वादने परितुष्यति ।
या तु स्फुटानां वर्णानामुत्पत्तौ कारणं भवेत् ॥ ^mula

kilāvyaktadhvanau tasminvādane parituṣyati |
yā tu sphuṭānāṃ varṇānāmutpattau kāraṇaṃ bhavet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. II
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 02 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v02 11 - Chapter Three, part 8#^pg385|p. 369]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 02.pdf#page=385>))
>
> The absence of division (in this sense) is pleasing; witness (for example,) the effect of playing a regularly recurrent rhythm (tālapātha). Indeed, (everybody) is completely satisfied by (a musical) sound (dhvani) (which is just one note, and hence is, one could say,) unmanifest (music). (with 3.243cd–244ab)
>
> Gross Corporeal Speech (Vaikharī) is the cause that generates clearly (articulated) phonemes, and its products are the many words and the like. (with 3.244cd–245ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* एवं मध्यमायाः स्थूलं रूपमुक्त्वा वैखर्या अप्यभिधत्ते --

> या तु स्फुटानां वर्णानामुत्पत्तौ कारणं भवेत् ॥
> सा स्थूला वैखरी यस्याः कार्यं वाक्यादि भूयसा ।

स्फुटानामिति परस्परवैलक्षण्यावस्थानेन श्रोत्राकर्ण्यमानानाम् । अत एव पारुष्यादत्र लोकस्य नासक्तिः ॥ २४४ ॥
