## Chapter 3

**3:1** For lo!  
The Sovereign LORD of Hosts  
Will remove from Jerusalem and from Judah  
Prop and stay,  
Every prop of food  
And every prop of water:[^1] ^3-1

**3:2** Soldier and warrior,  
Magistrate and prophet,  
Augur and elder; ^3-2

**3:3** Captain of fifty,  
Magnate and counselor,  
Skilled artisan and expert enchanter;[^2] ^3-3

**3:4** And He[^3] will make boys their rulers,  
And babes shall govern them. ^3-4

**3:5** So the people shall oppress one another—  
Each oppressing his fellow:  
The young shall bully the old;  
And the despised [shall bully] the honored. ^3-5

**3:6** For should a man seize his brother,  
In whose father’s house there is clothing:[^4]  
“Come, be a chief over us,  
And let this ruin[^5] be under your care,” ^3-6

**3:7** The other will thereupon protest,  
“I will not be a dresser of wounds,  
With no food or clothing in my own house.  
You shall not make me chief of a people!” ^3-7

**3:8** Ah, Jerusalem has stumbled,  
And Judah has fallen,  
Because by word and deed  
They insult the LORD,  
Defying His majestic glance. ^3-8

**3:9** Their partiality in judgment[^6] accuses them;  
They avow their sins like Sodom,  
They do not conceal them.  
Woe to them! For ill  
Have they served themselves. ^3-9

**3:10** (Hail[^7] the just man, for he shall fare well;  
He shall eat the fruit of his works. ^3-10

**3:11** Woe to the wicked man, for he shall fare ill;  
As his hands have dealt, so shall it be done to him.) ^3-11

**3:12** My people’s rulers are babes,  
It is governed by women.[^8]  
O my people!  
Your leaders are misleaders;  
They have confused the course of your paths. ^3-12

**3:13** The LORD stands up to plead a cause,  
He rises to champion peoples.[^9] ^3-13

**3:14** The LORD will bring this charge  
Against the elders and officers of His people:  
“It is you who have ravaged the vineyard;  
That which was robbed from the poor is in your houses. ^3-14

**3:15** How dare you crush My people  
And grind the faces of the poor?”  
—says my Lord GOD of Hosts. ^3-15

**3:16** The LORD said:  
“Because the daughters of Zion  
Are so vain  
And walk with heads thrown back,[^10]  
With roving eyes,  
And with mincing gait,  
Making a tinkling with their feet”— ^3-16

**3:17** My Lord will bare[^11] the pates  
Of the daughters of Zion,  
The LORD will uncover their heads. ^3-17

**3:18** In that day, my LORD will strip off the finery[^12] of the anklets, the fillets, and the crescents; ^3-18

**3:19** of the eardrops, the bracelets, and the veils; ^3-19

**3:20** the turbans, the armlets, and the sashes; of the talismans and the amulets; ^3-20

**3:21** the signet rings and the nose rings; ^3-21

**3:22** of the festive robes, the mantles, and the shawls; the purses, ^3-22

**3:23** the lace gowns, and the linen vests; and the kerchiefs and the capes. ^3-23

**3:24** And then—  
Instead of perfume, there shall be rot;  
And instead of an apron, a rope;  
Instead of a diadem of beaten-work,  
A shorn head;  
Instead of a rich robe,  
A girding of sackcloth;  
A burn instead of beauty.[^13] ^3-24

**3:25** Her[^14] men shall fall by the sword,  
Her fighting manhood in battle; ^3-25

**3:26** And her gates shall lament and mourn,  
And she shall be emptied,[^15]  
Shall sit on the ground. ^3-26

[^1]: Emendation yields “clothing”; cf. v. 7; 4.1.
[^2]: Emendation yields “craftsman.”
[^3]: Heb. “I.”
[^4]: Emendation yields “His father’s son, saying…”
[^5]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain. Emendation yields “wound.”
[^6]: So Targum; cf. Deut. 1.17; 16.19.
[^7]: Emendation yields “Happy is.”
[^8]: Emendation yields “boys”; cf. v. 4 (and v. 5).
[^9]: Septuagint “His people”; cf. vv. 14, 15.
[^10]: Lit. “throats bent back.”
[^11]: So Saadia. To bare a woman’s head in public was an intolerable humiliation; cf. Mishnah Baba Kamma 8.6.
[^12]: Many of the articles named in vv. 18–24 cannot be identified with certainty.
[^13]: The complete Isaiah scroll from Qumran, hereafter 1QIsa, reads “For shame shall take the place of beauty”; cf. note k.
[^14]: I.e., Zion’s; cf. vv. 16, 17; Heb. “your.”
[^15]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain. Emendation yields “her wall”; cf. Lam. 2.8.
