## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 07 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 5#^pg195|p. 178]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=195>))
>
> Although they differed from one another in the activity (of their) consciousness, they were (all equally) completely full of (spiritual, liberating) knowledge. They were all dedicated to engaging in (the study and explanation of) the scriptures, (ritual) worship, initiation, sexual union (nidhuvana), (and consuming) meat and alcohol (offered to the deity). There is, (moreover, a further) subdivision (of their types), according to (whether they are characterized as having) quelled (their) pride, forgiven (others’) anger, and the like. (with 28.391–393)
