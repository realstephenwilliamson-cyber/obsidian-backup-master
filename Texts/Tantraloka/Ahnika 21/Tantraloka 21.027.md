## Mūla

चिरविघटिते सेनायुग्मे यथामिलिते पुनर्हयगजनरं स्वां स्वां जातिं रसादभिधावति ।
करणपवनैर्नाडीचक्रैस्तथैव समागतैर्निजनिजरसादेकीभाव्यं स्वजालवशीकृतैः ॥ ^mula

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 12 - Chapter Twenty-One#^pg277|pp. 257–264]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=277>))
>
> It is just as when two armies separated for a long time are again joined together, the horses, elephants and men each naturally (tend) to go to their same kind. The same happens with the organs of sense, vital breaths, and the configurations of the vital channels (nādīcakra) (of the teacher and the disciple) that have come together, brought under control by their own net, that are unified by themselves spontaneously.

## Viveka

> करणपवनैर्नाडीचक्रैस्तथैव समागतैर्निजनिजरसादेकीभाव्यं स्वजालवशीकृतैः ॥

यथाहि चिरं विश्लिष्टेऽपि कटकद्वये पुनः सङ्घटिते हयादयो हयादिभिरेव निजनिजानुगुण्येन सङ्घटन्ते, तथैव जालप्रयोगमहिम्ना गौरवाः प्राणाद्याः पाशवैः प्रणाद्यैरेव-- इति पिण्डार्थः ॥ २७ ॥
