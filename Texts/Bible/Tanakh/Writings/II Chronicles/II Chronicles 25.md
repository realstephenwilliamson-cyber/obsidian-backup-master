## Chapter 25

**25:1** [^1]Amaziah was twenty-five years old when he became king, and he reigned twenty-nine years in Jerusalem; his mother’s name was Jehoaddan of Jerusalem. ^25-1

**25:2** He did what was pleasing to the LORD, but not with a whole heart. ^25-2

**25:3** Once he had the kingdom firmly under control, he executed the courtiers who had assassinated his father the king. ^25-3

**25:4** But he did not put their children to death for [he acted] in accordance with what is written in the Teaching, in the Book of Moses, where the LORD commanded, “Parents shall not die for children, nor shall children die for parents, but every person shall die only for his own crime.”[^2] ^25-4

**25:5** Amaziah assembled the men of Judah, and he put all the men of Judah and Benjamin under officers of thousands and officers of hundreds, by clans. He mustered them from the age of twenty upward, and found them to be 300,000 picked men fit for service, able to bear spear and shield. ^25-5

**25:6** He hired 100,000 warriors from Israel for 100 talents of silver. ^25-6

**25:7** Then a man of God came to him and said, “O king! Do not let the army of Israel go with you, for the LORD is not with Israel—all these Ephraimites. ^25-7

**25:8** But go by yourself and do it; take courage for battle, [else] God will make you fall before the enemy. For in God there is power to help one or make one fall!” ^25-8

**25:9** Amaziah said to the man of God, “And what am I to do about the 100 talents I gave for the Israelite force?” The man of God replied, “The LORD has the means to give you much more than that.” ^25-9

**25:10** So Amaziah detached the force that came to him from Ephraim, [ordering them] to go back to their place. They were greatly enraged against Judah and returned to their place in a rage. ^25-10

**25:11** Amaziah took courage and, leading his army, he marched to the Valley of Salt. He slew 10,000 men of Seir; ^25-11

**25:12** another 10,000 the men of Judah captured alive and brought to the top of Sela. They threw them down from the top of Sela and every one of them was burst open. ^25-12

**25:13** The men of the force that Amaziah had sent back so they would not go with him into battle made forays against the towns of Judah from Samaria to Beth-horon. They slew 3,000 of them, and took much booty. ^25-13

**25:14** After Amaziah returned from defeating the Edomites, he had the gods of the men of Seir brought, and installed them as his gods; he prostrated himself before them, and to them he made sacrifice. ^25-14

**25:15** The LORD was enraged at Amaziah, and sent a prophet to him who said to him, “Why are you worshiping the gods of a people who could not save their people from you?” ^25-15

**25:16** As he spoke to him, [Amaziah] said to him, “Have we appointed you a counselor to the king? Stop, else you will be killed!” The prophet stopped, saying, “I see God has counseled that you be destroyed, since you act this way and disregard my counsel.” ^25-16

**25:17** Then King Amaziah of Judah took counsel and sent this message to Joash son of Jehoahaz son of Jehu, king of Israel, “Come, let us confront each other!” ^25-17

**25:18** King Joash of Israel sent back this message to King Amaziah of Judah, “The thistle in Lebanon sent this message to the cedar in Lebanon, ‘Give your daughter to my son in marriage.’ But a wild beast in Lebanon passed by and trampled the thistle. ^25-18

**25:19** You boast that you have defeated the Edomites and you are ambitious to get more glory. Now stay at home, lest, provoking disaster you fall, dragging Judah down with you.” ^25-19

**25:20** But Amaziah paid no heed—it was God’s doing, in order to deliver them up because they worshiped the gods of Edom. ^25-20

**25:21** King Joash of Israel marched up, and he and King Amaziah of Judah confronted each other at Beth-shemesh in Judah. ^25-21

**25:22** The men of Judah were routed by Israel, and they all fled to their homes. ^25-22

**25:23** King Joash of Israel captured Amaziah son of Joash son of Jehoahaz, king of Judah, in Beth-shemesh. He brought him to Jerusalem and made a breach of 400 cubits in the wall of Jerusalem, from the Ephraim Gate to the Corner Gate. ^25-23

**25:24** Then, with all the gold and silver and all the utensils that were to be found in the House of God in the custody of Obed-edom, and with the treasuries of the royal palace, and with the hostages, he returned to Samaria. ^25-24

**25:25** King Amaziah son of Joash of Judah lived fifteen years after the death of King Joash son of Jehoahaz of Israel. ^25-25

**25:26** The other events of Amaziah’s reign, early and late, are recorded in the book of the kings of Judah and Israel. ^25-26

**25:27** From the time that Amaziah turned from following the LORD, a conspiracy was formed against him in Jerusalem, and he fled to Lachish; but they sent men after him to Lachish and they put him to death there. ^25-27

**25:28** They brought his body back on horses and buried him with his fathers in the city of Judah. ^25-28

[^1]: Cf. 2 Kings 14.
[^2]: Cf. Deut. 24.16.
