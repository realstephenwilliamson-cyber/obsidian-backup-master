## Mūla

एवं सोमार्कतेजःसु शिवभावेन भावनात् ।
निमज्जन्धौतमालिन्यः क्व वा योग्यो न जायते ॥ ^mula

evaṃ somārkatejaḥsu śivabhāvena bhāvanāt |
nimajjandhautamālinyaḥ kva vā yogyo na jāyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 03 - Chapter Fifteen, part 2#^pg65|p. 47]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=65>))

> He who, immersing (himself) in the same way into the radiant energies of the Moon and the Sun,' by contemplating (their state and his own as) Śiva, is cleansed of impurity. What does such a one not become fit for?
