### 3.1–13: The certainty of the Christ’s return

*[[NRSVue 2 Peter 03#^3-1|Text]], p. 516* · [[2 Peter 03#^3-1|2 Pet 3.1–7]] follows Jude 17–18. ^3-1

**[[NRSVue 2 Peter 03#^3-1|1]]:** *Beloved* (Gk “agapetoi”), common Christian epistolary address (see vv 8,14,17; [[1 Corinthians 04#^4-14|1 Cor 4.14]]; [[2 Corinthians 07#^7-1|2 Cor 7.1]]). *Second letter*, in addition to 1 Peter. ^3-1-2

**[[NRSVue 2 Peter 03#^3-2|2]]:** *Apostles*, the Twelve, although perhaps including Paul (and others), see [[Romans 01#^1-1|Rom 1.1]]; [[Romans 11#^11-13|11.13]]; [[Romans 16#^16-7|16.7]], etc. The author compares the apostles to the biblical prophets (see 1.16–21). *Lord and Savior*, cf. 1.11; 2.20; 3.18. ^3-2

**[[NRSVue 2 Peter 03#^3-3|3]]:** *Last days*, a Hebraism (“aḥarit ha-yamim,” e.g., [[Torah 12 - Vayechi#^49-1|Gen 49.1]]; [[Torah 40 - Balak#^24-14|Num 24.14]]), for the time of distress that precedes the end. *Scoffers will come*, referring to those who deny Jesus’ return. ^3-3

**[[NRSVue 2 Peter 03#^3-4|4]]:** *Where is the promise of his coming*, a similar claim about the delay of messianic times appears in *b. Sanh.* 97b; *Midr. Ps.* 77. The form of denial by a question is common in Tanakh, see, e.g., [[Jeremiah 17#^17-15|Jer 17.15]], [[Malachi 2#^2-17|Mal 2.17]], [[Psalms 079#^79-10|Ps 79.10]]. ^3-4

**[[NRSVue 2 Peter 03#^3-5|5]]:** *By the word of God heavens existed*, see Gen 1; [[Psalms 033#^33-6|Ps 33.6]]. ^3-5

**[[NRSVue 2 Peter 03#^3-6|6]]:** *Formed out of water*, [[Torah 01 - Bereshit#^1-1|Gen 1.1–2]]. *Deluged with water*, Noah’s flood (Gen 6–9). ^3-6

**[[NRSVue 2 Peter 03#^3-7|7]]:** *Reserved for fire*, see “Destruction by Fire,” p. 516. God promised never again to destroy the world by flood ([[Torah 02 - Noach#^9-11|Gen 9.11]]), leading some rabbinic sources to posit a possible destruction by means other than water (*Mekhilta de-Rabbi Ishmael*, *Amalek* 1; *t. Ta*ʾ*an.* 2.13). ^3-7

> [!note] Destruction by Fire *(p. 516)*
> Several biblical prophecies describe God’s future wrath with fiery images: “The day is coming, burning like an oven ([[Malachi 3#^3-19|Mal 4.1]]); “For the Lord has a day of vengeance . . . the streams of Edom shall be turned into pitch . . . her land shall become burning pitch. Night and day it shall not be guenched” ([[Isaiah 34#^34-8|Isa 34.8–10]]); “their fire shall not be quenched” ([[Isaiah 66#^66-24|Isa 66.24]]); “His wrath is poured out like fire” ([[Nahum 1#^1-6|Nah 1.6]]); “For in the fire of my passion all the earth shall be consumed” ([[Zephaniah 3#^3-8|Zeph 3.8]]). It is often unclear if the fire is literal or metaphorical. These prophecies do not focus on global destruction but on punishing God’s and Israel’s enemies as well as their land. The proximity of flood and fire is found in rabbinic sources as well (e.g., *Mekhilta de-Rabbi Ishmael, Amalek* 1*; t. Ta*ʾ*an.* 2.13). Although eschatological fire is not mentioned in rabbinic literature, it does appear in the Hellenistic-Jewish texts (e.g., *1 En.* 1; 6; *Sib. Or.* 3.71–76; 4.130–135,162–178).

**[[NRSVue 2 Peter 03#^3-8|8]]:** *Like a thousand years*, see [[Psalms 090#^90-4|Ps 90.4]]. The author seeks to dispel doubt about the delay of the final judgment. The Psalm is also cited in relation to the eschaton in *b. Rosh Ha-Shanah* 3a; *Gen. Rab*. 8.2; *Lev. Rab*. 20.1; *Song Rab*. 5.11 and elsewhere. See also *Jub*. 4.29–30 on Adam’s lifespan. ^3-8

**[[NRSVue 2 Peter 03#^3-10|10]]:** *Like a thief*, see [[1 Thessalonians 05#^5-2|1 Thess 5.2]]. ^3-10

**[[NRSVue 2 Peter 03#^3-13|13]]:** *New heavens . . . new earth*, see [[Isaiah 65#^65-17|Isa 65.17]]; [[Isaiah 66#^66-22|66.22]]; [[Matthew 24#^24-35|Mt 24.35]]; [[Hebrews 01#^1-10|Heb 1.10–12]]; [[Hebrews 12#^12-25|12.25–29]]; [[Revelation 21#^21-1|Rev 21.1]]; *1 En*. 45.4–5; *Sifre Deut.* 306; *Pesiq. Rav Kah*. 22.5). ^3-13

### 3.14–18: Final exhortation and doxology

*[[NRSVue 2 Peter 03#^3-14|Text]], p. 517* ^3-14

**[[NRSVue 2 Peter 03#^3-14|14]]:** *Peace*, evoking Heb “shalom,” with connotations of “complete”; see e.g., [[Isaiah 32#^32-17|Isa 32.17]]. ^3-14-2

**[[NRSVue 2 Peter 03#^3-15|15–16]]:** *Patience*, a reiteration of the idea in 3.9. See [[Romans 02#^2-4|Rom 2.4]]; [[Romans 09#^9-22|9.22]]. *Our beloved brother Paul wrote to you \[ . . . \] the other scriptures*, referring to Paul’s letters as authoritative *scripture*, this verse is one of the earliest indications of general Christian acceptance of the Pauline literature. *Hard to understand*, Peter and Paul were often at odds (see Gal 2), yet both Acts 15 and 2 Peter show them as working in harmony. *Twist to their own destruction*, perhaps by understanding Paul as promoting antinomianism. ^3-15

**[[NRSVue 2 Peter 03#^3-17|17]]:** *Beloved*, see v 1n. ^3-17

**[[NRSVue 2 Peter 03#^3-18|18]]:** *Grace and knowledge*, see 1.2; the author creates a circular closure. *Day of eternity*, the term is found only here in NT, but see [[LXX Sirach 18#^18-10|Sir 18.10]], “As a drop of water \[ . . . \] so are a few years among the days of eternity.” ^3-18
