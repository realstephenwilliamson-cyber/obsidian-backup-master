## Mūla

एषैव शक्तिमुद्रा चेदधोधावितपाणिका ।
दशानामंगुलीनां तु मुष्टिबन्धादनन्तरम् ॥ ^mula

eṣaiva śaktimudrā cedadhodhāvitapāṇikā |
daśānāmaṃgulīnāṃ tu muṣṭibandhādanantaram || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 06 - Chapter Thirty-Two#^pg182|p. 164]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=182>))
>
> 8) Looking downwards, endowed with sound (inner) vision (samyagdrṣti), the two hands contracted (and bent), as if throwing himself suddenly into a deep abyss by penetrating with audacity (and forcefully into consciousness) (sāhasa) - this is the Skyfarer who, called Vīrabhairava, develops consciousness. (KhecarT) has thus been described in eight ways in the venerable Bhargāṣtakaśikhākula. (with 32.54–62)
>
> 5) This same (mudrā) is the Mudrā of Power (śaktimudrā) if the lower hand is shifted downwards. (with 32.58ab)
>
> 6) By suddenly spreading out the ten fingers after (the hands) have been clenched into fists, the goddess Khecarī is considered to be (the Seal of) the Five Kundalinīs. (with 32.58cd–59ab)
