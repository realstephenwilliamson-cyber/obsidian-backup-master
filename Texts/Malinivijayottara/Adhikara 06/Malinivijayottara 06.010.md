## Mūla

त्रिखण्डे कण्ठपर्यन्तमात्मतत्त्वमुदाहृतम् ।
विद्यातत्त्वमतोर्ध्वं तु शिवतत्त्वं तु पूर्ववत् ॥ ^mula

trikhaṇḍe kaṇṭhaparyantam ātmatattvam udāhṛtam |
vidyātattvam atordhvaṁ tu śivatattvaṁ tu pūrvavat || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2024 – April 2024
> p. 26 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Malini (journal), January-April 2024.pdf#page=28>))
>
> **Chapter Six of the Mālinīvijayottara Tantra [... continued from Previous Issue] – Rajanaka Mark Dyczkowski – 3) The Deposition of the Three Principles**
>
> In (the case of the deposition) in three parts, it is said that the principle of the Self (ātmatattva) extends (for seventy-two fingers) up to the throat. The principle of Knowledge (vidyātattva) is above that (by twelve fingers) and, as before, the Śiva principle (śivatattva) is (pervasive).[^17]

[^17]: MV 6/6cd-9ab and 6/10 are quoted in TĀv ad 16/109-110ab. The deposition of the three principles: the Self (Ātmatattva) occupies seventy-two fingers, Knowledge (Vidyātattva) twelve and Śivatattva, as before, is not counted. Thus, 72 + 12 = 84.
