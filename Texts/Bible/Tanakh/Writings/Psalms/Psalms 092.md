## Chapter 92

**92:1** A psalm. A song; for the sabbath day. ^92-1

**92:2** It is good to praise the LORD,  
to sing hymns to Your name, O Most High, ^92-2

**92:3** To proclaim Your steadfast love at daybreak,  
Your faithfulness each night ^92-3

**92:4** With a ten-stringed harp,  
with voice and lyre together. ^92-4

**92:5** You have gladdened me by Your deeds, O LORD;  
I shout for joy at Your handiwork. ^92-5

**92:6** How great are Your works, O LORD,  
how very subtle[^1] Your designs! ^92-6

**92:7** A brutish man cannot know,  
a fool cannot understand this: ^92-7

**92:8** though the wicked sprout like grass,  
though all evildoers blossom,  
it is only that they may be destroyed forever. ^92-8

**92:9** But You are exalted, O LORD, for all time. ^92-9

**92:10** Surely, Your enemies, O LORD,  
surely, Your enemies perish;  
all evildoers are scattered. ^92-10

**92:11** You raise my horn high like that of a wild ox;  
I am soaked in freshening oil. ^92-11

**92:12** I shall see the defeat of my watchful foes,  
hear of the downfall of the wicked who beset me. ^92-12

**92:13** The righteous bloom like a date-palm;  
they thrive like a cedar in Lebanon; ^92-13

**92:14** planted in the house of the LORD,  
they flourish in the courts of our God. ^92-14

**92:15** In old age they still produce fruit;  
they are full of sap and freshness, ^92-15

**92:16** attesting that the LORD is upright,  
my rock, in whom there is no wrong. ^92-16

[^1]: Or “profound.”
