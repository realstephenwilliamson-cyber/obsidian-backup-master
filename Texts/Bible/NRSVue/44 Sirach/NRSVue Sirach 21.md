# Sirach 21 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Sirach 20|← Sirach 20]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Sirach 22|Sirach 22 →]]

## Various sins

**21:1** Have you sinned, my child? Do so no more, but ask forgiveness for your past sins. ^21-1

**21:2** Flee from sin as from a snake, for if you approach sin it will bite you. Its teeth are lion’s teeth and can destroy human lives. ^21-2

**21:3** All lawlessness is like a two-edged sword; there is no healing for the wound it inflicts. ^21-3

**21:4** Panic and insolence will waste away riches; thus the house of the proud will be uprooted.^[Other ancient authorities read *laid waste*] ^21-4

**21:5** The prayer of the poor goes from their lips to the ears of God,^[Gk *his ears*] and his judgment comes speedily. ^21-5

**21:6** Those who hate reproof walk in the sinner’s steps, but those who fear the Lord repent in their heart. ^21-6

**21:7** The mighty in speech are widely known; when they slip, the sensible person knows it. ^21-7

**21:8** Those who build their houses with other people’s money are like those who gather stones for their own burial mounds. ^21-8

**21:9** An assembly of the lawless is like a bundle of tinder, and their end is a blazing fire. ^21-9

**21:10** The way of sinners is paved with smooth stones, but at its end is the pit of Hades. ^21-10

## Wisdom and foolishness

**21:11** Those who keep the law control their thoughts, and the fulfillment of the fear of the Lord is wisdom. ^21-11

**21:12** Those who are not clever cannot be taught, but there is a cleverness that increases bitterness. ^21-12

**21:13** The knowledge of the wise will increase like a flood and their counsel like a life-giving spring. ^21-13

**21:14** The mind^[Syr Lat: Gk *entrails*] of a fool is like a broken jar; it can hold no knowledge. ^21-14

**21:15** When intelligent people hear a wise saying, they praise it and add to it; when fools^[Syr: Gk *revelers*] hear it, they laugh at^[Syr: Gk *dislike*] it and throw it behind their backs. ^21-15

**21:16** The chatter^[Syr: Gk *explanation*] of fools is like a burden on a journey, but delight is found in the speech of the intelligent. ^21-16

**21:17** The utterance of sensible people is sought in the assembly, and they ponder their words in their minds. ^21-17

**21:18** Like a house in ruins is wisdom to a fool, and the knowledge of the ignorant is meaningless talk. ^21-18

**21:19** To the senseless, education is fetters on their feet and like manacles on their right hands. ^21-19

**21:20** Fools raise their voices when they laugh, but the wise^[Syr Lat: Gk *clever*] smile quietly. ^21-20

**21:21** To the sensible, education is like a golden ornament and like a bracelet on the right arm. ^21-21

**21:22** The foot of a fool rushes into a house, but an experienced person waits respectfully outside. ^21-22

**21:23** A boor peers into the house from the door, but a cultivated person remains outside. ^21-23

**21:24** It is ill-mannered for a person to listen at a door; the discreet would be grieved by the disgrace. ^21-24

**21:25** The lips of babblers speak of what is not their concern,^[Other ancient authorities read *The lips of strangers speak of these things*] but the words of the prudent are weighed in the balance. ^21-25

**21:26** The mind of fools is in their mouth, but the mouth of the wise is in^[Other ancient authorities omit *in*] their mind. ^21-26

**21:27** When ungodly people curse an adversary, they curse themselves. ^21-27

**21:28** Whisperers degrade themselves and are hated in their neighborhoods. ^21-28
