# Psalms 102 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 101|← Psalms 101]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 103|Psalms 103 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 102|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**102:1** For David himself. Bless the Lord, O my soul: and let all that is within me bless his holy name. ^102-1

**102:2** Bless the Lord, O my soul, and never forget all he hath done for thee. ^102-2

**102:3** Who forgiveth all thy iniquities: who healeth all thy diseases. ^102-3

**102:4** Who redeemeth thy life from destruction: who crowneth thee with mercy and compassion. ^102-4

**102:5** Who satisfieth thy desire with good things: thy youth shall be renewed like the eagle’s. ^102-5

**102:6** The Lord doth mercies, and judgment for all that suffer wrong. ^102-6

**102:7** He hath made his ways known to Moses: his wills to the children of Israel. ^102-7

**102:8** The ford is compassionate and merciful: longsuffering and plenteous in mercy. ^102-8

**102:9** He will not always be angry: nor will he threaten for ever. ^102-9

**102:10** He hath not dealt with us according to our sins: nor rewarded us according to our iniquities. ^102-10

**102:11** For according to the height of the heaven above the earth: he hath strengthened his mercy towards them that fear him. ^102-11

**102:12** As far as the east is from the west, so far hath he removed our iniquities from us. ^102-12

**102:13** As a father hath compassion on his children, so hath the Lord compassion on them that fear him: ^102-13

**102:14** For he knoweth our frame. He remembereth that we are dust: ^102-14

**102:15** Man’s days are as grass, as the flower of the field so shall he flourish. ^102-15

**102:16** For the spirit shall pass in him, and he shall not be: and he shall know his place no more. ^102-16

**102:17** But the mercy of the Lord is from eternity and unto eternity upon them that fear him: And his justice unto children’s children, ^102-17

**102:18** To such as keep his covenant, And are mindful of his commandments to do them. ^102-18

**102:19** The Lord hath prepared his throne in heaven: and his kingdom shall rule over all. ^102-19

**102:20** Bless the Lord, all ye his angels: you that are mighty in strength, and execute his word, hearkening to the voice of his orders. ^102-20

**102:21** Bless the Lord, all ye his hosts: you ministers of his that do his will. ^102-21

**102:22** Bless the Lord, all his works: in every place of his dominion, O my soul, bless thou the Lord. ^102-22
