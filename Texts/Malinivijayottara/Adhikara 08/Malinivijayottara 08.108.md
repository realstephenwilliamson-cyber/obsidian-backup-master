## Mūla

आत्ममूर्त्यादिपूज्यान्तं कुम्भे विन्यस्य मन्त्रवित् ।
गन्धपुष्पादिभिः पूज्य वार्धान्यां पूजयेदिमम्  ॥ ^mula

ātmamūrtyādipūjyāntaṁ kumbhe vinyasya mantravit |
gandhapuṣpādibhiḥ pūjya vārdhānyāṁ pūjayed imam[^119] || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2025 – April 2025
> p. 31 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=45>))
>
> Once the knower of mantra has deposited onto the (big) jar (*kumbha*) (the mantras) beginning with that of the icon of the Self (*ātmamūrti*) up to that of the object of worship and worshipped with scent and flowers etc., he should worship the Weapon within the (small) jar.[^120]

[^119]: kh: *pūjayedasim*
[^120]: MS Kh reads *asim –* 'sword' or 'weapon' for *imam –* 'this'. See commentary on SvT 3/70cd-75ab.
