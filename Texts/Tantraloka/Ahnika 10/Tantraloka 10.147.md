## Mūla

संभोक्ष्यमाणां दृष्ट्वैव रभसाद्याति संमदम् ।
तामेव दृष्ट्वा च तदा समानाशयभागपि ॥ ^mula

saṃbhokṣyamāṇāṃ dṛṣṭvaiva rabhasādyāti saṃmadam |
tāmeva dṛṣṭvā ca tadā samānāśayabhāgapi || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 12 - Chapter Ten, part 3#^pg404|p. 385]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=404>))
>
> A man who sees a beautiful woman standing before him, even though he is absorbed in just imagining that he will enjoy her in the future, becomes suddenly full of passion. (with 10.146cd–147ab)
>
> (If) another (man), once having seen that same (woman) is not gripped then by the same sentiments, even though he has the same disposition (āśaya), whom do we blame? (with 10.147cd–148ab)

*Lead-in:* ननु संभुज्यमानापि कुलवधूर्यदि योग्यतामात्रेण कञ्चित्प्रति भोग्या तदविशेषात्सर्वान्प्रत्येवास्तु? -- इत्याशङ्क्याह --

तदेत्येकतरसंमदावसरे -- इत्यर्थः । समानाशयभागपीति तेनैव भोक्ता सदृशरागादिवासनोऽपि -- इत्यर्थः ॥ १४७ ॥
