## Mūla

विद्याकलान्तं सिद्धान्ते वामदक्षिणशास्त्रयोः ।
सदाशिवान्तं समनापर्यन्तं मतयामले ॥ ^mula

vidyākalāntaṃ siddhānte vāmadakṣiṇaśāstrayoḥ |
sadāśivāntaṃ samanāparyantaṃ matayāmale || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 06 - Chapter Fifteen, part 5#^pg209|p. 191]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=209>))
>
> (The Throne) reaches up to the (nine) powers of Knowledge in the Siddhānta. In the scriptures of the Left (vāma) and Right (dakṣiṇa), (it extends) up to Sadāśiva, and up to the Equal One (samanā) in the Matayāmala. (Its final) culmination in the Transmental (unmanā) is taught (only) here (in the Trika). Thus it is (said in the Mālinīvijayottara to be) the supreme throne (with respect to the other traditions). After worshipping the Throne, one must worship the series of teachers, as will be explained further ahead. (with 15.319–320)
