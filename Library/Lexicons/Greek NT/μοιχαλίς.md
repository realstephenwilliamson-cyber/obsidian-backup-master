# μοιχαλίς

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · adjective

**Dodson:** an adulteress — (a) an adulteress (that is, a married woman who commits adultery), (b) Hebraistically: extended to those who worship any other than the true God. (Strong's 3428; headword μοιχαλίς, ίδος, ἡ)
