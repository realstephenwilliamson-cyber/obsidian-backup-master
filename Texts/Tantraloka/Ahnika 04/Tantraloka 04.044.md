## Mūla

तत्संनिधानेनान्येषु कल्पितेष्वधिकारिता ।
स समस्तं च शास्त्रार्थं सत्तर्कादेव मन्यते ॥ ^mula

tatsaṃnidhānenānyeṣu kalpiteṣvadhikāritā |
sa samastaṃ ca śāstrārthaṃ sattarkādeva manyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. III
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 03 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v03 03 - Chapter Four, part 2#^pg71|p. 58]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 03.pdf#page=71>))
>
> He, amongst all teachers, is said to be the chief one (mukhya). In his presence, all other (teachers) who have been formed (kalpita) (by other teachers) are devoid of authority. (with 4.43cd–44ab)
>
> Moreover, by virtue of (his) sound discriminative reasoning alone he grasps the entire purport of the scripture. (with 4.44cd)
