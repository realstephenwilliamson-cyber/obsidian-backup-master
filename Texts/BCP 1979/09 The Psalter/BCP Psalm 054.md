# Psalm 54

← [[BCP Psalm 053]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 055]] →

*Deus, in nomine* · Book Two · Tenth Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 054]]

**54:1** Save me, O God, by your Name; \*  
in your might, defend my cause. ^54-1

**54:2** Hear my prayer, O God; \*  
give ear to the words of my mouth. ^54-2

**54:3** For the arrogant have risen up against me,  
and the ruthless have sought my life, \*  
those who have no regard for God. ^54-3

**54:4** Behold, God is my helper; \*  
it is the Lord who sustains my life. ^54-4

**54:5** Render evil to those who spy on me; \*  
in your faithfulness, destroy them. ^54-5

[p. 660]

**54:6** I will offer you a freewill sacrifice \*  
and praise your Name, O LORD, for it is good. ^54-6

**54:7** For you have rescued me from every trouble, \*  
and my eye has seen the ruin of my foes. ^54-7
