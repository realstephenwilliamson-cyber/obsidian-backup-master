## Mūla

सार्णेनाण्डत्रयं व्याप्तं त्रिशूलेन चतुर्थकम् ।
सर्वातीतं विसर्गेण पराया व्याप्तिरिष्यते ॥ ^mula

sārṇenāṇḍatrayaṁ vyāptaṁ triśūlena caturthakam |
sarvātītaṁ visargeṇa parāyā vyāptir iṣyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, April 2023 – June 2023
> p. 20 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=61>))
>
> The three Eggs (that extend up to the end of Māyā) are pervaded by the letter S and the fourth one by the Trident (AU) and that which is beyond all by emission (Ḥ). (This) is considered to be the pervasion of Parā.[^17]

[^17]: MV 4/24-25, quoted in TĀv ad 16/216-222 (216cd-223ab). The first quarter of verse 25 is quoted in TĀv ad 3/166 (165cd-166ab), the second quarter is in TĀv ad 3/104 (3/104cd-105ab). All of verse 25 is quoted in TĀv ad 4/186cd-188ab and 11/89.
