# Isaiah 35 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Isaiah 34|← Isaiah 34]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Isaiah 36|Isaiah 36 →]]

## The return of the redeemed to Zion

**35:1** The wilderness and the dry land shall be glad; the desert shall rejoice and blossom; like the crocus ^35-1

**35:2** it shall blossom abundantly and rejoice with joy and shouting. The glory of Lebanon shall be given to it, the majesty of Carmel and Sharon. They shall see the glory of the LORD, the majesty of our God.\]\]^[Q ms lacks 34.17–35.2] ^35-2

**35:3** Strengthen the weak hands and make firm the feeble knees. ^35-3

**35:4** Say to those who are of a fearful heart, “Be strong, do not fear! Here is your God. He will come with vengeance, with terrible recompense. He will come and save you.” ^35-4

**35:5** Then the eyes of the blind shall be opened, and the ears of the deaf shall be opened; ^35-5

**35:6** then the lame shall leap like a deer, and the tongue of the speechless sing for joy. For waters shall break forth in the wilderness and streams in the desert; ^35-6

**35:7** the burning sand shall become a pool and the thirsty ground springs of water; the haunt of jackals shall become a swamp;^[Cn: Heb *in the haunt of jackals is her resting place*] the grass shall become reeds and rushes. ^35-7

**35:8** A highway shall be there,^[Q ms Gk Syr: MT *A highway and a way shall be there*] and it shall be called the Holy Way; the unclean shall not travel on it,^[Or *pass it by*] but it shall be for God’s people;^[Cn: Heb *for them*] no traveler, not even fools, shall go astray. ^35-8

**35:9** No lion shall be there, nor shall any ravenous beast come up on it; they shall not be found there, but the redeemed shall walk there. ^35-9

**35:10** And the ransomed of the LORD shall return and come to Zion with singing; everlasting joy shall be upon their heads; they shall obtain joy and gladness, and sorrow and sighing shall flee away. ^35-10
