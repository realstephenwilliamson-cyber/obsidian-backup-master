# Psalm 13

← [[BCP Psalm 012]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 014]] →

*Usquequo, Domine?* · Book One · Second Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 013]]

**13:1** How long, O LORD?  
will you forget me for ever? \*  
how long will you hide your face from me? ^13-1

[p. 598]

**13:2** How long shall I have perplexity in my mind,  
and grief in my heart, day after day? \*  
how long shall my enemy triumph over me? ^13-2

**13:3** Look upon me and answer me, O LORD my God; \*  
give light to my eyes, lest I sleep in death; ^13-3

**13:4** Lest my enemy say, "I have prevailed over him," \*  
and my foes rejoice that I have fallen. ^13-4

**13:5** But I put my trust in your mercy; \*  
my heart is joyful because of your saving help. ^13-5

**13:6** I will sing to the LORD, for he has dealt with me richly; \*  
I will praise the Name of the Lord Most High. ^13-6
