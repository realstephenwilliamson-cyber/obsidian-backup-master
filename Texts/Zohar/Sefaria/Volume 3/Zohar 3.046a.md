# Zohar 3.046a

← [[Zohar 3.045b]] · [[Zohar (Soncino)]] · [[Zohar 3.046b]] →

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 7.06 - Parashat Tazri'a#^z3-46a|Parashat Tazri’a, at 3:46a]]

*Volume 3, folio 46a* · Tazria

*Passage Tazria 17:70 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 3.045b#^tazria-17-70|Tazria 17:70]].*

**Tazria 17:71** So with the’plague of leprosy, of those marks which are visible to all, it is written, “And the priest shall see and declare him unclean”, for assuredly those come from the side of uncleanness and are not chastisements of love. ^tazria-17-71

**Tazria 17:72** In the same way, he who reproves his neighbour in love should not let other men hear in order that he may not be ashamed; and if he reproves him publicly he does not show true friendship. ^tazria-17-72

**Tazria 17:73** So God, in reproving a man, acts with him lovingly. At first He smites him inwardly. If he repents, well and good, but if not, he smites him under his garments, and this is called “chastisement of love”. If he still does not repent, God smites him on his face where all can see, so that they may know that he is a sinner and not beloved of his Master.’ ^tazria-17-73

**Tazria 17:74** The man thereupon said to them: ‘You arc in a conspiracy against me: of a surety you are of those who frequent the house of R. Simeon ben Yohai, who have no fear of anyone. If my sons were to come up, they would punish you for speaking thus loudly.’ They said: ‘The Law teaches us to do so, as it says, “Wisdom crieth in the chief places of concourse”, etc. (Prov. 1, 21). If we were to be afraid to speak words of the Torah before thee, we should be ashamed before God.’ The man thereupon lifted up his hands and wept. Meanwhile his sons came up. The youngest one said: ‘The support of heaven is here. ^tazria-17-74

**Tazria 18:75** King Solomon said: “All have I seen in the days of my vanity” (Eccl. 7, 15). In the days of King Solomon the moon was full, and Solomon was the wisest of men and he saw all: ^tazria-18-75

**Tazria 18:76** he saw “All” attached to the Moon and illuminating her like the sun. ^tazria-18-76

**Tazria 18:77** Now, when the righteous are numerous in the world, this “All” does not depart from the Sun, and receives all anointing and joy from above to unite with the Moon, for whose sake it is thus enriched. ^tazria-18-77

**Tazria 18:78** But when sinners are numerous in the world, and the Moon is darkened, the “Righteous One perisheth in his righteousness”, that is, he loses all this as he cannot unite with the Moon. Then, too, all the side of the left is aroused, and the prosperity of sinners is prolonged; hence it is written, “and there is a wicked man who prolongeth his life in his evildoing”. ^tazria-18-78
