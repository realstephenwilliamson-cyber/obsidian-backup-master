## Mūla

अस्मद्गुर्वागमस्त्वेष तिरोभूते स्वयं शिशौ ।
न कुप्येन्न शपेद्धीमान् स ह्यनुग्राहकः सदा ॥ ^mula

asmadgurvāgamastveṣa tirobhūte svayaṃ śiśau |
na kupyenna śapeddhīmān sa hyanugrāhakaḥ sadā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 14 - Chapter Twenty-Three#^pg345|p. 325]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=345>))
>
> Our teacher’s tradition (gurvāgama) (maintains) that the disciple is obscured by his own doing (svayam). A wise man should not be angry nor curse; he is always gracious (and helpful to everybody).
