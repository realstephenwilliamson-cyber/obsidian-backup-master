## Chapter 23

**23:1** The word of the LORD came to me: ^23-1

**23:2** O mortal, once there were two women, daughters of one mother. ^23-2

**23:3** They played the whore in Egypt; they played the whore while still young. There their breasts were squeezed, and there their virgin nipples were handled. ^23-3

**23:4** Their names were: the elder one, Oholah;[^1] and her sister, Oholibah.[^2] They became Mine, and they bore sons and daughters. As for their names, Oholah is Samaria, and Oholibah is Jerusalem. ^23-4

**23:5** Oholah whored while she was Mine, and she lusted after her lovers, after the Assyrians, warriors[^3] ^23-5

**23:6** clothed in blue, governors and prefects, horsemen mounted on steeds—all of them handsome young fellows. ^23-6

**23:7** She bestowed her favors upon them—upon all the pick of the Assyrians—and defiled herself with all their fetishes after which she lusted. ^23-7

**23:8** She did not give up the whoring she had begun with the Egyptians; for they had lain with her in her youth, and they had handled her virgin nipples and had poured out their lust upon her. ^23-8

**23:9** Therefore I delivered her into the hands of her lovers, into the hands of the Assyrians after whom she lusted. ^23-9

**23:10** They exposed her nakedness; they seized her sons and daughters, and she herself was put to the sword. And because of the punishment inflicted upon her, she became a byword among women. ^23-10

**23:11** Her sister Oholibah saw this; yet her lusting was more depraved than her sister’s, and her whoring more debased. ^23-11

**23:12** She lusted after the Assyrians, governors and prefects, warriors[^4] gorgeously clad, horsemen mounted on steeds—all of them handsome young fellows. ^23-12

**23:13** And I saw how she had defiled herself. Both of them followed the same course, ^23-13

**23:14** but she carried her harlotries further. For she saw men sculptured upon the walls, figures of Chaldeans drawn in vermilion, ^23-14

**23:15** girded with belts round their waists, and with flowing turbans on their heads, all of them looking like officers—a picture of Babylonians whose native land was Chaldea. ^23-15

**23:16** At the very sight of them she lusted after them, and she sent messengers for them to Chaldea. ^23-16

**23:17** So the Babylonians came to her for lovemaking and defiled her with their whoring; and she defiled herself with them until she turned from them in disgust. ^23-17

**23:18** She flaunted her harlotries and exposed her nakedness, and I turned from her in disgust, as I had turned disgusted from her sister. ^23-18

**23:19** But she whored still more, remembering how in her youth she had played the whore in the land of Egypt; ^23-19

**23:20** she lusted for concubinage with them, whose members were like those of asses and whose organs[^5] were like those of stallions. ^23-20

**23:21** Thus you reverted to the wantonness of your youth, remembering[^6] your youthful breasts, when the men of Egypt handled your nipples. ^23-21

**23:22** Assuredly, Oholibah, thus said the Lord GOD: I am going to rouse against you the lovers from whom you turned in disgust, and I will bring them upon you from all around— ^23-22

**23:23** the Babylonians and all the Chaldeans, [the people of] Pekod, Shoa, and Koa, and all the Assyrians with them, all of them handsome young fellows, governors and prefects, officers and warriors,[^7] all of them riding on horseback. ^23-23

**23:24** They shall attack you with fleets[^8] of wheeled chariots and a host of troops; they shall set themselves against you on all sides with bucklers, shields, and helmets. And I will entrust your punishment to them, and they shall inflict their punishments on you. ^23-24

**23:25** I will direct My passion against you, and they shall deal with you in fury: they shall cut off your nose and ears. The last of you shall fall by the sword; they[^9] shall take away your sons and daughters, and your remnant shall be devoured by fire. ^23-25

**23:26** They shall strip you of your clothing and take away your dazzling jewels. ^23-26

**23:27** I will put an end to your wantonness and to your whoring in the land of Egypt, and you shall not long for them or remember Egypt any more. ^23-27

**23:28** For thus said the Lord GOD: I am going to deliver you into the hands of those you hate, into the hands of those from whom you turned in disgust. ^23-28

**23:29** They shall treat you with hate, and they shall take away all you have toiled for, and leave you naked and bare; your naked whoredom, wantonness, and harlotry will be exposed. ^23-29

**23:30** These things shall be done to you for your harlotries with the nations, for defiling yourself with their fetishes. ^23-30

**23:31** You walked in your sister’s path; therefore I will put her cup into your hand. ^23-31

**23:32** Thus said the Lord GOD:  
You shall drink of your sister’s cup,  
So deep and wide;  
It shall cause derision and scorn,  
It holds so much. ^23-32

**23:33** You shall be filled with drunkenness and woe.  
The cup of desolation and horror,  
The cup of your sister Samaria— ^23-33

**23:34** You shall drink it and drain it,  
And gnaw its shards;[^10]  
And you shall tear your breasts.  
For I have spoken—declares the Lord GOD. ^23-34

**23:35** Assuredly, thus said the Lord GOD: Because you have forgotten Me and cast Me behind your back, you in turn must suffer for your wanton whoring. ^23-35

**23:36** Then the LORD said to me: O mortal, arraign[^11] Oholah and Oholibah, and charge them with their abominations. ^23-36

**23:37** For they have committed adultery, and blood is on their hands; truly they have committed adultery with their fetishes, and have even offered to them as food the children they bore to Me. ^23-37

**23:38** At the same time they also did this to Me: they defiled My Sanctuary and profaned My sabbaths. ^23-38

**23:39** On the very day that they slaughtered their children to their fetishes, they entered My Sanctuary to desecrate it. That is what they did in My House. ^23-39

**23:40** Moreover, they sent for men to come from afar, [men] to whom a messenger was sent; and they came. For them, [Oholibah,] you bathed, painted your eyes, and donned your finery; ^23-40

**23:41** and you sat on a grand couch with a set table in front of it—and it was My incense and My oil you laid upon it. ^23-41

**23:42** And the noise of a carefree multitude was there, of numerous men brought drunk from the desert;[^12] and they put bracelets on their arms and splendid crowns upon their heads. ^23-42

**23:43** Then I said, “To destruction with adultery! Look, they are still going on with those same fornications of hers.”[^13] ^23-43

**23:44** And they would go to her as one goes to a prostitute; that is how they went to Oholah and Oholibah, wanton women. ^23-44

**23:45** But righteous men shall punish them with the punishments for adultery and for bloodshed, for they are adulteresses and have blood on their hands. ^23-45

**23:46** For thus said the Lord GOD: Summon an assembly against them, and make them an object of horror and plunder. ^23-46

**23:47** Let the assembly pelt them with stones and cut them down with their swords; let them kill their sons and daughters, and burn down their homes. ^23-47

**23:48** I will put an end to wantonness in the land; and all the women shall take warning not to imitate your wantonness. ^23-48

**23:49** They shall punish you for your wantonness, and you shall suffer the penalty for your sinful idolatry. And you shall know that I am the Lord GOD. ^23-49
