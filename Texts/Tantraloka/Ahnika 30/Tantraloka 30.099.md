## Mūla

तुलामेलकयोगः श्रीतन्त्रसद्भावशासने ।
य उक्तः शम्भुनाथेन स मया दर्शितः क्रमात् ॥ ^mula

tulāmelakayogaḥ śrītantrasadbhāvaśāsane |
ya uktaḥ śambhunāthena sa mayā darśitaḥ kramāt || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 03 - Chapter Thirty, part 2#^pg74|p. 56]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=74>))
>
> I have (thus) revealed in due order (the mantras required for the) sacrifice of the balance (that eliminates weight) and (for) the union (with the Śākinīs, given) in the Tantrasadbhāva, that was revealed (to me) by Śambhunātha.
