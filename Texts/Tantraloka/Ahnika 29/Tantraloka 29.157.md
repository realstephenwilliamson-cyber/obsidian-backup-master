## Mūla

यद्भैरवाष्टकपदं तल्लभतेऽष्टककलाभिन्नम् ।
गमनागमनेऽवसितौ कर्णे नयने द्विलिङ्गसंपर्के ॥ ^mula

yadbhairavāṣṭakapadaṃ tallabhate'ṣṭakakalābhinnam |
gamanāgamane'vasitau karṇe nayane dviliṅgasaṃparke || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 12 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 4#^pg353|p. 336]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=353>))
>
> Thus, he who has mounted onto (this) group of eight Wheels (and is focused on them), practicing (the innate) spontaneous (sahaja) repetition of Mantra (japa) within the Supreme Abode (of consciousness in the centre), attains the plane of the eight Bhairavas, which is differentiated by the energies (kalā) of the group of eight (stages of the development of Sound). (with 29.156cd–157ab)
>
> (These eight Wheels are experienced by the couple together), (1) in the going and coming (of the breath), (2) in the faculty of ascertainment, (3) in the (sense of) hearing, (4) in (the sense of) sight, (5) in the contact of the two sex organs, (6) in the conjunction of their coupling (saṁmelayoga), (7) in the one said to be at the end of the body, and 8) in the Wheel of the Couple (in union). (with 29.157cd–158ab)

*Lead-in:* ननु किं नाम चक्राष्टकं यदारूढोऽपि जपमाचरेत्?--इत्याशङ्क्य आह--

> गमनागमनेऽवसितौ कर्णे नयने द्विलिङ्गसंपर्के ॥
> तत्संमेलयोगे देहान्ताख्ये च यामले चक्रे ।

गमनागमने प्राणसहिते अपाने--इत्यर्थः । अवसितावध्यवसाये बुद्धाविति यावत् । संपर्कः--स्पर्शमात्रम् । तत्संमेलनयोगे इति तयोर्द्वयोर्लिङ्गयोः संमेलनयोगे सङ्घट्टावसरे--इत्यर्थः । देहान्ताख्ये इति--द्वादशान्ते । यामले चक्रे इति--सर्वशेषः ॥
