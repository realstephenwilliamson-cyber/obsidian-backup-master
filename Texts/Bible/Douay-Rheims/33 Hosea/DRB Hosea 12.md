# Hosea 12 (Douay-Rheims)

*Osee 12 in the Douay-Rheims*

[[DRB Hosea 11|← Hosea 11]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Hosea 13|Hosea 13 →]]

**12:1** Ephraim feedeth on the wind, and followeth the burning heat: all the day long he multiplied lies and desolation: and he hath made a covenant with the Assyrians, and carried oil into Egypt. ^12-1

**12:2** Therefore there is a judgment of the Lord with Juda, and a visitation for Jacob: he will render to him according to his ways, and according to his devices. ^12-2

**12:3** In the womb he supplanted his brother: and by his strength he had success with an angel. ^12-3

**12:4** And he prevailed over the angel, and was strengthened: he wept, and made supplication to him: he found him in Bethel, and there he spoke with us. ^12-4

**12:5** Even the Lord the God of hosts, the Lord is his memorial. ^12-5

**12:6** Therefore turn thou to thy God: keep mercy and judgment, and hope in thy God always. ^12-6

**12:7** He is like Chanaan, there is a deceitful balance in his hand, he hath loved oppression. ^12-7

**12:8** And Ephraim said: But yet I am become rich, I have found me an idol: all my labours shall not find me the iniquity that I have committed. ^12-8

**12:9** And I that am the Lord thy God from the land of Egypt, will yet cause thee to dwell in tabernacles, as in the days of the feast. ^12-9

**12:10** And I have spoken by the prophets, and I have multiplied visions, and I have used similitudes by the ministry of the prophets. ^12-10

**12:11** If Galaad be an idol, then in vain were they in Galgal offering sacrifices with bullocks: for their altars also are as heaps in the furrows of the field. ^12-11

**12:12** Jacob fled into the country of Syria, and Israel served for a wife, and was a keeper for a wife. ^12-12

**12:13** But the Lord by a prophet brought Israel out of Egypt: and he was preserved by a prophet. ^12-13

**12:14** Ephraim hath provoked me to wrath with his bitterness, and his blood shall come upon him, and his Lord will render his reproach unto him. ^12-14
