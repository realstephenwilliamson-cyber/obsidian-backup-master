# Psalm 109 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 108|← Psalm 108]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 110|Psalm 110 →]]

## Prayer for vindication and vengeance

*To the leader. Of David. A Psalm.*

**109:1** Do not be silent, O God of my praise. ^109-1

**109:2** For wicked and deceitful mouths are opened against me, speaking against me with lying tongues. ^109-2

**109:3** They surround me with words of hate and attack me without cause. ^109-3

**109:4** In return for my love they accuse me, even while I make prayer for them.^[Syr: Heb *I prayer*] ^109-4

**109:5** So they reward me evil for good and hatred for my love. ^109-5

**109:6** They say,^[Heb lacks *They say*] “Appoint a wicked man against him; let an accuser stand on his right. ^109-6

**109:7** When he is tried, let him be found guilty; let his prayer be counted as sin. ^109-7

**109:8** May his days be few; may another seize his position. ^109-8

**109:9** May his children be orphans and his wife a widow. ^109-9

**109:10** May his children wander about and beg; may they be driven out of^[Gk: Heb *may they seek*] the ruins they inhabit. ^109-10

**109:11** May the creditor seize all that he has; may strangers plunder the fruits of his toil. ^109-11

**109:12** May there be no one to do him a kindness nor anyone to pity his orphaned children. ^109-12

**109:13** May his posterity be cut off; may his name be blotted out in the second generation. ^109-13

**109:14** May the iniquity of his father^[Cn: Heb *fathers*] be remembered before the LORD, and do not let the sin of his mother be blotted out. ^109-14

**109:15** Let them be before the LORD continually, and may his^[Gk: Heb *their*] memory be cut off from the earth. ^109-15

**109:16** For he did not remember to show kindness but pursued the poor and needy and the brokenhearted to their death. ^109-16

**109:17** He loved to curse; let curses come on him. He did not like blessing; may it be far from him. ^109-17

**109:18** He clothed himself with cursing as his coat; may it soak into his body like water, like oil into his bones. ^109-18

**109:19** May it be like a garment that he wraps around himself, like a belt that he wears every day.” ^109-19

**109:20** May that be the reward of my accusers from the LORD, of those who speak evil against my life. ^109-20

**109:21** But you, O LORD my Lord, act on my behalf for your name’s sake; because your steadfast love is good, deliver me. ^109-21

**109:22** For I am poor and needy, and my heart is pierced within me. ^109-22

**109:23** I am gone like a shadow at evening; I am shaken off like a locust. ^109-23

**109:24** My knees are weak through fasting; my body has become gaunt. ^109-24

**109:25** I am an object of scorn to my accusers; when they see me, they shake their heads. ^109-25

**109:26** Help me, O LORD my God! Save me according to your steadfast love. ^109-26

**109:27** Let them know that this is your hand; you, O LORD, have done it. ^109-27

**109:28** Let them curse, but you will bless. Let my assailants be put to shame;^[Gk: Heb *They have risen up and have been put to shame*] may your servant be glad. ^109-28

**109:29** May my accusers be clothed with dishonor; may they be wrapped in their own shame as in a mantle. ^109-29

**109:30** With my mouth I will give great thanks to the LORD; I will praise him in the midst of the throng. ^109-30

**109:31** For he stands at the right hand of the needy, to save them from those who would condemn them to death. ^109-31
