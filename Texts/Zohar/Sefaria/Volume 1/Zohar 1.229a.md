# Zohar 1.229a

← [[Zohar 1.228b]] · [[Zohar (Soncino)]] · [[Zohar 1.229b]] →

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 3.07 - Parashat Va-Yhi#^z1-229a|Parashat Va-Yḥi, at 1:229a]]

*Volume 1, folio 229a* · Vayechi

*Passage Vayechi 38:398 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 1.228b#^vayechi-38-398|Vayechi 38:398]].*

**Vayechi 38:399** R. Hiya discoursed on the verse: *House and wealth are an inheritance from parents, but from the Lord is a prudent wife*. (Prov. 19, 14). ‘When God gives a house and money to a man,’ he said, ‘sometimes he bequeaths the whole to his son, and therefore these things, although they are ultimately from God, may be called “inheritance of parents”. But the possession of a good wife comes to man only from God. For God mates couples before they are born, ^vayechi-38-399

**Vayechi 38:400** and when a man is worthy he obtains a wife according to his deserts. ^vayechi-38-400

**Vayechi 38:401** Sometimes it happens that after the lot has been cast, that man perverts his ways, and then his mate is transferred to another until he rectifies his ways, or else until his time comes, and then the other is removed to make way for him and he comes into his own; and this is grievous in the sight of God, to remove one man to make way for another. Nor is it only a prudent wife who is from God. ^vayechi-38-401

**Vayechi 38:402** For if God has purposed to bestow benefits on a man, but he goes astray to the “other side”, then from that other side to which he cleaves there shall come to him one who shall bring upon him all accusations and all ills. ^vayechi-38-402

**Vayechi 38:403** Hence of the wife who is not prudent Solomon said: “And I find that woman more bitter than death” (Eccl. 7, 26), because it is the man’s sins which have drawn her on him. Hence, when God is pleased with a man, he provides for him a wife who is prudent, and redeems him from the other side. ^vayechi-38-403

**Vayechi 38:404** Hence Jacob said, “the angel who hath redeemed me from all wrong”, meaning that a wife had not been assigned to him from the “other side”, and that there was no defect in his seed, all of them being righteous and perfect.’ ^vayechi-38-404

**Vayechi 38:405** SHALL BLESS THE CHILDREN. They were deserving of blessing because Joseph had kept the sign of the holy covenant. When Joseph said, “they are my sons whom God has given me here”, he showed his father the sign of the covenant which he had kept, and therefore they were meet for blessing, and he also was deserving of blessing in abundance. Hence Jacob gave to the others only one blessing, but to Joseph many blessings, as it says, “the blessings of thy father… shall be upon the head of Joseph” (Gen. 49, 26). ^vayechi-38-405

**Vayechi 38:406** R. Judah discoursed on the verse: *Unto thee do I lift up mine eyes, O thou that sittest in the heavens* (Ps. 123, 1). He said: ‘Prayer offered with true devotion is directed on high to the supernal recess, from whence issue all blessings and all freedom, to support the universe. ^vayechi-38-406

**Vayechi 38:407** It is attached above to the mystery of the supreme Wisdom, and it is attached below to him who sits on the throne of the patriarchs which is called heaven. Hence it is written here: “Who sits in the heavens.” ^vayechi-38-407

**Vayechi 38:408** When the blessings issue from the supernal recess, they are all received by this place called heaven, and from thence they flow down till they come to the place called the “Righteous One the foundation of the world”, from whence are blessed all the (heavenly) hosts and camps after their kind. ^vayechi-38-408

**Vayechi 38:409** All these heavenly legions are crowned by seventy-two lights, of which seventy[^1] form a circle about the world, while in the[^2][^3] from which the whole of the circumference is fed. The house of the holy of holies is the place for that spirit of all spirits, where lies hid the mystery of all mysteries, and when this removes, all move after it.’ ^vayechi-38-409

[^1]: The seventy Chieftains.
[^2]: The Shekinah.
[^3]: The Shekinah.
