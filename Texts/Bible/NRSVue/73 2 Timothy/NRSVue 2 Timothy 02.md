## A good soldier of Christ Jesus

**2:1** You then, my child, be strong in the grace that is in Christ Jesus, ^2-1

**2:2** and what you have heard from me through many witnesses entrust to faithful people who will be able to teach others as well. ^2-2

**2:3** Share in suffering like a good soldier of Christ Jesus. ^2-3

**2:4** No one serving in the army gets entangled in everyday affairs; the soldier’s aim is to please the enlisting officer. ^2-4

**2:5** And in the case of an athlete, no one is crowned without competing according to the rules. ^2-5

**2:6** It is the farmer who does the work who ought to have the first share of the crops. ^2-6

**2:7** Think over what I say, for the Lord will give you understanding in all things. ^2-7

**2:8** Remember Jesus Christ, raised from the dead, a descendant of David—that is my gospel, ^2-8

**2:9** for which I suffer hardship, even to the point of being chained like a criminal. But the word of God is not chained. ^2-9

**2:10** Therefore I endure everything for the sake of the elect, so that they may also obtain the salvation that is in Christ Jesus, with eternal glory. ^2-10

**2:11** The saying is sure: If we have died with him, we will also live with him; ^2-11

**2:12** if we endure, we will also reign with him; if we deny him, he will also deny us; ^2-12

**2:13** if we are faithless, he remains faithful—he^[Other ancient authorities read *for he*] cannot deny himself. ^2-13

## A worker approved by God

**2:14** Remind them of this, and warn them before the Lord^[Other ancient authorities read *God*] that they are to avoid wrangling over words, which does no good but only ruins those who are listening. ^2-14

**2:15** Do your best to present yourself to God as one approved by him, a worker who has no need to be ashamed, rightly explaining the word of truth. ^2-15

**2:16** Avoid profane chatter, for it will lead people^[Gk *for they will advance*] into more and more impiety, ^2-16

**2:17** and their talk will spread like gangrene. Among them are Hymenaeus and Philetus, ^2-17

**2:18** who have swerved from the truth, saying resurrection^[Other ancient authorities read *the resurrection*] has already occurred. They are upsetting the faith of some. ^2-18

**2:19** But God’s firm foundation stands, bearing this inscription: “The Lord knows those who are his,” and, “Let everyone who calls on the name of the Lord turn away from wickedness.” ^2-19

**2:20** In a large house there are utensils not only of gold and silver but also of wood and clay, some for special^[Gk *honorable*] use, some for ordinary.^[Gk *dishonorable*] ^2-20

**2:21** All who cleanse themselves of the things I have mentioned^[Gk *of these things*] will become special^[Gk *honorable*] utensils, dedicated and useful to the owner of the house, ready for every good work. ^2-21

**2:22** Shun youthful passions and pursue righteousness, faith, love, and peace, along with those who call on the Lord from a pure heart. ^2-22

**2:23** Have nothing to do with stupid and senseless controversies; you know that they breed quarrels. ^2-23

**2:24** And the Lord’s servant must not be quarrelsome but kindly to everyone, an apt teacher, patient, ^2-24

**2:25** correcting opponents with gentleness. God may perhaps grant that they will repent and come to know the truth ^2-25

**2:26** and that they may escape from the snare of the devil, having been held captive by him to do his will.^[Or *by him, to do his* (that is, God’s) *will*] ^2-26
