## Mūla

न किञ्चित्तामसस्तस्य सुखदुःखाच्छुभाशुभम् ।
नन्वत्र तामसो नाम कथं योग्यो विधौ भवेत् ॥ ^mula

na kiñcittāmasastasya sukhaduḥkhācchubhāśubham |
nanvatra tāmaso nāma kathaṃ yogyo vidhau bhavet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 09 - Chapter Fifteen, part 8#^pg316|p. 298]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=316>))
> Dyczkowski's numbering differs here; his markers: (492cd-493) (490b-491ab), (494) (491cd-492ab).
>
> (The dream experiences of a disciple vary) in this way, according to the variety (of the qualities (guṇa) to which he is subject). The sattvic (disciple) sees (his) dream very clearly, the rājasic by means of signs and (other) indications, and finally, the āĀmasic one does not see anything at all. The auspicious and inauspicious character of the latter’s (dreams) is determined solely by the pleasure or suffering (he experiences when asleep). (with 15.490b–491ab)
>
> Surely, (one may ask) here, how can the tāāmasic (disciple) be fit for the rite (of initiation)? (I reply that) there is no need to argue. Any (particular) quality (may be possessed) by anybody anywhere (according to what the case may be or the person). (with 15.491cd–492ab)

*Lead-in:* ननु इह तामसस्य दीक्षाकर्मणि अधिकार एव कुतस्त्यः?-- इत्याशङ्कां प्रदर्श्य निराकरोति--

> मैव मा विग्रहं कश्चित् क्वचित्कस्यापि वै गुणः ।

वैशब्दोऽवधारणे, स च सर्वत्र संबन्धनीयः । गुण इति-- अर्थादुद्रिक्तः ॥
