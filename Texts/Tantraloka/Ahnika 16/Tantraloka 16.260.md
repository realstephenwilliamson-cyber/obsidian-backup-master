## Mūla

सञ्जल्पो ह्यभिसंक्रान्तः सोऽद्याप्यस्तीति गृह्यताम् ।
यस्तथाविधसञ्जल्पबलात्कोऽपि स्वतन्त्रकः ॥ ^mula

sañjalpo hyabhisaṃkrāntaḥ so'dyāpyastīti gṛhyatām |
yastathāvidhasañjalpabalātko'pi svatantrakaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 07 - Chapter Sixteen, part 3#^pg133|p. 113]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=133>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 16.259cd–260ab.
>
> The initial discourse (sañjalpa) (that constitutes the teaching originated) from the first teacher. By being successively transmitted in this way in all respects from each preceding (teacher to the next, it has come down to us and) exists today also (as it did in the past). Thus, you should accept (this view to be the correct one). (with 16.259cd–260ab)
>
> It is well determined (and there can be no doubt,) that whatever independent reflective awareness (of meaning) (vimarśa) one may conceive by the power (bala) of that (same) kind of (inner) discourse, it is also of that same nature (as is involved in the transmission of the teachings). (with 16.260cd–261ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* एवं च तन्माहात्म्योपनतो यः कश्चन वैकल्पिको व्यवहारः, सोऽपि ध्रुवं तदात्मैव--इत्याह--

> यस्तथाविधसञ्जल्पबलात्कोऽपि स्वतन्त्रकः ॥
> विमर्शः कल्प्यते सोऽपि तदात्मैव सुनिश्चतः ।

एवमादिगुरोः प्रभृति प्रवृत्त एक एवेयत्कालपर्यन्तं प्रतिसङ्कान्तवृत्तितयानुवर्तमानस्तत्तदभिजल्पवपुषा स्फुरतीति सिद्धम् ॥
