Another contemplation of the same passage

And, again, to speak concisely, the one who by practice and contemplation \[1377B} wondrously transmits to his listeners the principles concerning the cross, has become another Joseph, augmenting his spiritual knowledge by the addition of the good things acquired from contemplation. And he will be “of Arimathea” when, through genuine familiarity with the virtues actualized in practice, he cuts away from himself every attachment to material things.

Another contemplation of the same passage

On the other hand, those who blasphemously teach the principles concerning God’s embodiment crucify Christ, but whosoever proclaims the principles of God’s incarnation with boldness and piety to all, places Christ in the

tomb. {1377C\]
