## Translation

> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 04 - Chapter Thirty-One, part 1#^pg101|p. 83]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=101>))

> The extent of the variety of maṇḍalas is innumerable because of these differences. (Moreover) the maṇḍalas are endless (because of the possible differences in their) pedestal (pīṭha), platform (vīthi), the outer ground (for the doors etc.), throat, ears, and cheek, and so (also) because of the differences (in their) main and secondary decorations (śobha) and the (various) options (to make) the parallel lines (of the borders) (guṇarekhā); (so too) because of the various (number of) svastikas, that may be two (or more) up to eight, and because (any of these additions) may optionally be present or absent. Then, (after the doors have been drawn), one should add coloured powders as required to make it beautiful. (36cd-39ab) (with 31.36–38)
