## Chapter 52

**52:1** For the leader. A *maskil* of David, ^52-1

**52:2** when Doeg the Edomite came and informed Saul, telling him, “David came to Ahimelech’s house.”[^1] ^52-2

**52:3** Why do you boast of your evil, brave fellow?  
God’s faithfulness never ceases.[^2] ^52-3

**52:4** Your tongue devises mischief,  
like a sharpened razor that works treacherously. ^52-4

**52:5** You prefer evil to good,  
the lie, to speaking truthfully. *Selah*. ^52-5

**52:6** You love all pernicious words,  
treacherous speech. ^52-6

**52:7** So God will tear you down for good,  
will break you and pluck you from your tent,  
and root you out of the land of the living. *Selah*. ^52-7

**52:8** The righteous, seeing it, will be awestruck;  
they will jibe at him, saying, ^52-8

**52:9** “Here was a fellow who did not make God his refuge,  
but trusted in his great wealth,  
relied upon his mischief.” ^52-9

**52:10** But I am like a thriving olive tree in God’s house;  
I trust in the faithfulness of God forever and ever. ^52-10

**52:11** I praise You forever, for You have acted;  
I declare that Your name is good[^3]  
in the presence of Your faithful ones. ^52-11

[^1]: Cf. 1 Sam. 22.9 ff.
[^2]: Lit. “is all the day.”
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain; others “I will wait for Your name for it is good.”
