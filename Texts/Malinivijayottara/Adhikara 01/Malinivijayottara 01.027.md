## Mūla

असूत सा कलातत्त्वं यद्योगादभवत्पुमान् ।
जातकर्तृत्वसामर्थ्यो विद्यारागौ ततोऽसृजत् ॥ ^mula

asūta sā kalātattvaṁ yad yogād abhavat pumān |
jātakartṛtvasāmarthyo vidyārāgau tato 'sṛjat || ^iast

## Translation

> **The Kañcukas**
>
> That (power of Māyā) generated the principle which is the Force (of limited agency) (kalātattva), due to union with which the individual soul comes to possess the capacity (to be an) agent.[^57] Then (that Force) emitted (the principles of the capacity for) knowledge (vidyā) and attachment (rāga).

[^57]: MV 1/25d and 27a are quoted in TĀv ad 9/40cd-41ab; MV 1/27d is quoted in TĀv ad 9/203abc; MV 1/27abc is quoted in TĀv ad 9/174 and 189cd-190ab. Abhinavagupta refers to MV 1/27abc in TĀ 9/190cd-191ab.
