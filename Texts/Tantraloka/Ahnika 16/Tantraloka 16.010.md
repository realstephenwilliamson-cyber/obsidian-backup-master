## Mūla

गणपतिगुरुपरमाख्याः परमेष्ठी पूर्वसिद्धवाक्क्षेत्रपतिः ।
इति सप्तकमाख्यातं गुरुपङ्क्तिविधौ प्रपूज्यमस्मद्गुरुभिः ॥ ^mula

gaṇapatiguruparamākhyāḥ parameṣṭhī pūrvasiddhavākkṣetrapatiḥ |
iti saptakamākhyātaṃ gurupaṅktividhau prapūjyamasmadgurubhiḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 05 - Chapter Sixteen, part 1#^pg24|p. 4]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=24>))
>
> According to my teacher (Śambhunātha), the group of seven that should be worshiped in the procedure (for worshipping) the lineage of teachers consists of 1) Gaṇeśa, 2) (one’s own) teacher, 3) the teacher of the teacher, 4) the teacher of the latter (parameṣthin), 5) the first realized (pūrvasiddha) (teacher), 6) (the Goddess) of Speech, and 7) the guardian of the place (kṣetrapati).
