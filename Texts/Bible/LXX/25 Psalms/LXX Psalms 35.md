# LXX Psalms 35

← [[LXX Psalms 34]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 36]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 036]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 036]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**35:1** For the end, by David the servant of the Lord.  ^35-1

**35:2** The transgressor, that he may sin, says within himself, *that* there is no fear of God before his eyes.  ^35-2

**35:3** For he has dealt craftily before him, to discover his iniquity and hate it.[^1]  ^35-3

**35:4** The words of his mouth are transgression and deceit: he is not inclined to understand *how* to do good.  ^35-4

**35:5** He devises iniquity on his bed; he gives himself to every evil way; and does not abhor evil.  ^35-5

**35:6** O Lord, thy mercy is in the heaven; and thy truth *reaches* to the clouds.  ^35-6

**35:7** Thy righteousness is as the mountains of God, thy judgments are as a great deep: O Lord, thou wilt preserve men and beasts.[^2]  ^35-7

**35:8** How hast thou multiplied thy mercy, O God! so the children of men shall trust in the shelter of thy wings.  ^35-8

**35:9** They shall be fully satisfied with the fatness of thine house; and thou shalt cause them to drink of the full stream of thy delights.[^3]  ^35-9

**35:10** For with thee is the fountain of life: in thy light we shall see light.  ^35-10

**35:11** Extend thy mercy to them that know thee; and thy righteousness to the upright in heart.  ^35-11

**35:12** Let not the foot of pride come against me, and let not the hand of sinners move me.  ^35-12

**35:13** There have all the workers of iniquity fallen: they are cast out, and shall not be able to stand.  ^35-13

[^1]: 35:3 *q. d.* with regard to the discovering.
[^2]: 35:7 *Or,* vast mountains.
[^3]: 35:9 *Gr.* intoxicated.
