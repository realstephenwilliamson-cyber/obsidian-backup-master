Interestingly, although the Mālinivijayottara defines more subdivisions of prānā-yāma than other Tantras it makes no mention of the common five-fold teleological classification of the vital energy itself.47 Also absent in the Mālinīvijayottara is the commonly encountered astronomical terminology used to describe the movements of the vital energy in the body and through the channels.48 Perhaps these have been left out because they are of no practical use in the immediate practice of Ṣadañgayoga.
