# Song of Songs 1 (NRSVue)

— · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Song of Songs 02|Song of Songs 2 →]]

**1:1** The Song of Songs, which is Solomon’s. ^1-1

## Colloquy of the young woman and friends

**1:2** Let him kiss me with the kisses of his mouth! For your love is better than wine; ^1-2

**1:3** your anointing oils are fragrant; your name is perfume poured out; therefore the maidens love you. ^1-3

**1:4** Draw me after you; let us make haste. The king has brought me into his chambers. We will exult and rejoice in you; we will extol your love more than wine; rightly do they love you. ^1-4

**1:5** I am black and beautiful, O daughters of Jerusalem, like the tents of Kedar, like the curtains of Solomon. ^1-5

**1:6** Do not gaze at me because I am dark, because the sun has gazed on me. My mother’s sons were angry with me; they made me keeper of the vineyards, but my own vineyard I have not kept! ^1-6

**1:7** Tell me, you whom my soul loves, where you pasture your flock, where you make it lie down at noon, for why should I be like one who is veiled beside the flocks of your companions? ^1-7

**1:8** If you do not know, O fairest among women, follow the tracks of the flock and pasture your kids beside the shepherds’ tents. ^1-8

## Colloquy of the young man, friends, and the young

## Woman

**1:9** I compare you, my love, to a mare among Pharaoh’s chariots. ^1-9

**1:10** Your cheeks are comely with ornaments, your neck with strings of jewels. ^1-10

**1:11** We will make you ornaments of gold, studded with silver. ^1-11

**1:12** While the king was on his couch, my nard gave forth its fragrance. ^1-12

**1:13** My beloved is to me a bag of myrrh that lies between my breasts. ^1-13

**1:14** My beloved is to me a cluster of henna blossoms in the vineyards of En-gedi. ^1-14

**1:15** Ah, you are beautiful, my love; ah, you are beautiful; your eyes are doves. ^1-15

**1:16** Ah, you are beautiful, my beloved, truly lovely. Our couch is green; ^1-16

**1:17** the beams of our house are cedar; our rafters^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] are pine. ^1-17
