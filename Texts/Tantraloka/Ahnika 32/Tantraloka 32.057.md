## Mūla

शान्ताख्या सा हस्तयुग्ममूर्ध्वाधः स्थितमुद्गतम् ।
समदृष्ट्यावलोक्यं च बहिर्योजितपाणिकम् ॥ ^mula

śāntākhyā sā hastayugmamūrdhvādhaḥ sthitamudgatam |
samadṛṣṭyāvalokyaṃ ca bahiryojitapāṇikam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 06 - Chapter Thirty-Two#^pg181|p. 163]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=181>))
>
> 8) Looking downwards, endowed with sound (inner) vision (samyagdrṣti), the two hands contracted (and bent), as if throwing himself suddenly into a deep abyss by penetrating with audacity (and forcefully into consciousness) (sāhasa) - this is the Skyfarer who, called Vīrabhairava, develops consciousness. (KhecarT) has thus been described in eight ways in the venerable Bhargāṣtakaśikhākula. (with 32.54–62)
>
> 4) (The next form of Khecarī Mudrā) is called ‘Tranquil’ (Śāntā). The two hands (i.e. arms) are one above the other, and the hands are united externally. (The yogi) should gaze at the upraised one with balanced (concentrated) vision.
