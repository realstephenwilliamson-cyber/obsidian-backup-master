## Mūla

वर्षातपसमायोगाद्दिव्योऽप्येवंविधो मतः ।
किंतु तत्र परां मन्त्री स्रवन्तीममृतं स्मरेत् ॥ ^mula

varṣātapasamāyogād divyo 'py evaṁvidho mataḥ |
kiṁtu tatra parāṁ mantrī sravantīm amṛtaṁ smaret || ^iast

> 5) The divine ablution, which is performed in conjunction with the rain and the sun, is considered to be like this, only that there (in that case), the reciter of mantra should (mentally) recollect (the Vidyā of) Parā as flowing forth nectar.
