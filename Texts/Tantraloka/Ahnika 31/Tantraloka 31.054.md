## Mūla

इत्येतत्कथितं गुप्ते षडर्धहृदये परे ।
षट्के प्रोक्तं सूचितं श्रीसिद्धयोगीश्वरीमते ॥ ^mula

ityetatkathitaṃ gupte ṣaḍardhahṛdaye pare |
ṣaṭke proktaṃ sūcitaṃ śrīsiddhayogīśvarīmate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 04 - Chapter Thirty-One, part 1#^pg108|p. 90]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=108>))
>
> This is stated in the hidden, supreme Ṣaḍardhahṛdaya of six thousand verses (ṣaṭka), and (just implicitly) mentioned in the Siddhayogīśvarīmata. ‘After having first of all traced the maṇḍala that bestows all (one’s) desires, equipped with a large trident adorned with lotuses and wheels etc., one should draw a trident in each door except the western one. (One may) also fashion in (each of) the corners a large trident that has (its own) tree. One should fashion (the lotuses) of the spokes born from the ocean of nectar in groups of three on the tip of the trident, or else eight times (that,) or just three times only. The divine abode of the skyfaring (goddesses) (khecarī) has in this way been well outlined.’ (with 31.54–58ab)
