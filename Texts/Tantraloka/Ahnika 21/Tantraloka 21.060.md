## Mūla

शाठ्यं तत्र न कार्यं च तत्कृत्वाधो व्रजेच्छिशुः ।
न पुनः कीर्तयेत्तस्य पापं कीर्तयिता व्रजेत् ॥ ^mula

śāṭhyaṃ tatra na kāryaṃ ca tatkṛtvādho vrajecchiśuḥ |
na punaḥ kīrtayettasya pāpaṃ kīrtayitā vrajet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 12 - Chapter Twenty-One#^pg295|p. 275]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=295>))
>
> (A teacher) who knows reality (tattvavid) may also, even if he is not present in every way, liberate fettered souls as (he) wishes. He is Śiva in person (sākṣāt), and so he should always be venerated. The disciple must not be stingy there (about this). If he is, he goes down (falling to lower realms). Again, (the teacher) should not divulge his (disciple’s) sin. One who divulges (the sin of others) goes to hell. Thus, he should seek to avoid (this). Such is the prescription (vidhi) (taught) in the Dīkṣottara. (with 21.59–61ab)

## Viveka

> सर्वथा वर्तमानोऽपि तत्त्वविन्मोचयेत्पशून् ।
> इच्छयैव शिवः साक्षात्तस्मात्तं पूजयेत्सदा ॥
> शाठ्यं तत्र न कार्यं च तत्कृत्वाधो व्रजेच्छिशुः ।
> न पुनः कीर्तयेत्तस्य पापं कीर्तयिता व्रजेत् ॥
> निरयं वर्जयेत्तस्मादिति दीक्षोत्तरे विधिः ।

सर्वथेति--येन केनचित्प्रकारेण । शाठ्यम् विचिकित्सा । वर्जयेदिति-- पापकीर्तनम् ॥
