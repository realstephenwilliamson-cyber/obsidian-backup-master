# Uḍḍāmareśvaratantra — Chapter 3

[[Uddamaresvaratantra 02|← Chapter 2]] · [[Uddamaresvaratantra|← index]] · [[Uddamaresvaratantra 04|Chapter 4 →]]

kākasya pakṣau saṃgṛhya sarpakañcukam eva ca

^s1

śmaśānabhasmasaṃyuktaṃ śatror uccāṭanaṃ bhavet

^s2

ity uccāṭanādhikāraḥ

^s3

bhūtavādaṃ pravakṣyāmi yathā garuḍabhāṣitam

^s4

yenaiva jñātamātreṇa śatravo yānti vaśyatām

^s5

śatruniryāsaṃ saṃgṛhya cūrṇaṃ sattvaṃ ca tasya vai

^s6

bhāvayet saptarātraṃ tu bhojanaiḥ saha dāpayet

^s7

dattamātreṇa tenaiva piśācair gṛhyate tu saḥ

^s8

svasthīkāre pradātavyā jayā tu madhuyaṣṭikā

^s9

niryāsaiḥ śālasambhūtair bījāni katakasya ca

^s10

bhāvayet saptarātraṃ tu bhojanaiḥ saha dīyate

^s11

tasya bhakṣaṇamātreṇa grahaiḥ saṃgṛhyate naraḥ

^s12

śarkarādugdhapānena svastho bhavati nānyathā

^s13

vāstukasya tu niryāsaṃ bījāni katakasya ca

^s14

marditvā yātayāmābhyāṃ gṛhyate brahmarākṣasaiḥ

^s15

śarkarādugdhapānena svastho bhavati nānyathā

^s16

kapikacchukaniryāsair mātuluṅgasya bījakam

^s17

bhāvayet saptarātraṃ tu bhakṣye pāne pradāpayet

^s18

uoṃ namo bhagavate uḍḍāmareśvarāya maheśvaro nāma svāhā

^s19

āmalakyāś ca cūrṇena strīdugdhasahitena ca

^s20

pāyayitvā viṣkṛtaṃ tu svāsthyaṃ saṃjāyate dhruvam

^s21

śarkarāpadmakiñjalkapadmakesarakalkakaiḥ

^s22

cūrṇīkṛtais tac cūrṇaṃ tu dugdhena saha dāpayet

^s23

tadā duṣṭagrahair muktaḥ svasthaś ca jāyate naraḥ

^s24
