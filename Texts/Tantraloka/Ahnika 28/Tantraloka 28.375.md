## Mūla

बहुदर्पणवद्दीप्तः सर्वायेताप्ययत्नतः ।
अत एव गीतगीतप्रभृतौ बहुपर्षदि ॥ ^mula

bahudarpaṇavaddīptaḥ sarvāyetāpyayatnataḥ |
ata eva gītagītaprabhṛtau bahuparṣadi || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 07 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 5#^pg186|p. 169]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=186>))
>
> The flux of the rays of their own (senses), pouring forth, reflected in the (individual) consciousness (of each of those present,) as if within many mirrors, (thus) intensified (dīpta), spontaneously becomes them all (sarvāyeta). (with 28.374cd–375ab)
>
> Thus, in a gathering of many people (for a performance) of song, dance and the like, the enjoyment (they experience is collective) when they are all one (equally immersed in the show together), (that joy) is not (experienced by) one (who is alone). (with 28.375cd–376ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* सर्वाकारत्वमेव च अस्याः परानन्दनिर्भरं पूर्णं रूपम्--इत्याह--

> अत एव गीतगीतप्रभृतौ बहुपर्षदि ॥
> यः सर्वतन्मयीभावे ह्लादो नत्वेककस्य सः ।

अत इति--सर्वाकारत्वादेव अस्याः । सर्वतन्मयीभाव इति--तावत्यंशे सर्वेषां भेदविगलनात् ॥
