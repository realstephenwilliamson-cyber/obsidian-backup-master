Compare: [[NRSVue Nahum 02|NRSVue]] (Heb 2:1 = NRSVue 1:15; Heb 2:2–14 = NRSVue 2:1–13) · [[NRSVue Nahum 01#^1-15|NRSVue 1:15]]

## Chapter 2

**2:1** Behold on the hills  
The footsteps of a herald  
Announcing good fortune!  
“Celebrate your festivals, O Judah,  
Fulfill your vows.  
Never again shall scoundrels invade you,  
They have totally vanished.” ^2-1

**2:2** [^1]A shatterer has come up against you.  
Man the guard posts,  
Watch the road;  
Steady your loins,  
Brace all your strength! ^2-2

**2:3** For the LORD has restored the Pride[^2] of Jacob  
As well as the Pride[^3] of Israel,[^4]  
Though marauders have laid them waste  
And ravaged their branches. ^2-3

**2:4** His warriors’ shields are painted red,  
And the soldiers are clothed in crimson;  
The chariots are like flaming torches,[^5]  
On the day they are made ready.  
The [arrows of] cypress wood are poisoned,[^6] ^2-4

**2:5** The chariots dash about frenzied in the fields,  
They rush through the meadows.  
They appear like torches,  
They race like streaks of lightning. ^2-5

**2:6** [^7]He commands his burly men;  
They stumble as they advance,  
They hasten up to her wall,  
Where wheeled shelters are set up.[^8] ^2-6

**2:7** The floodgates are opened,  
And the palace is deluged.[^9] ^2-7

**2:8** And Huzzab is exiled and carried away,[^10]  
While her handmaidens escort [her][^11]  
As with the voices of doves,  
Beating their breasts. ^2-8

**2:9** Nineveh has been like a [placid] pool of water  
From earliest times;[^12]  
Now they flee.  
“Stop! Stop!”—  
But none can turn them back. ^2-9

**2:10** “Plunder silver! Plunder gold!”  
There is no limit to the treasure;  
It is a hoard of all precious objects. ^2-10

**2:11** Desolation, devastation, and destruction!  
Spirits sink,  
Knees buckle,  
All loins tremble,  
All faces turn ashen.[^13] ^2-11

**2:12** What has become of that lions’ den,  
That pasture[^14] of great beasts,  
Where lion and lion’s breed walked,  
And lion’s cub—with none to disturb them? ^2-12

**2:13** [Where is] the lion that tore victims for his cubs  
And strangled for his lionesses,  
And filled his lairs with prey  
And his dens with mangled flesh? ^2-13

**2:14** I am going to deal with you  
—declares the LORD of Hosts:  
I will burn down her chariots in smoke,[^15]  
And the sword shall devour your great beasts;  
I will stamp out your killings from the earth,  
And the sound of your messengers[^16]  
Shall be heard no more. ^2-14

[^1]: This verse would read well after v. 3.
[^2]: Emendation yields “vine.”
[^3]: Emendation yields “vine.”
[^4]: “Jacob” refers to the northern kingdom (cf. Amos 6.8; 8.7); Israel refers to the southern kingdom, regarded as the remnant of Israel after the fall of the northern kingdom (cf. Mic. 1.13–15).
[^5]: Understanding peladoth as equivalent to lappidoth.
[^6]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain. Emendation yields “The horsemen charge”; cf. 3.3.
[^7]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^8]: To protect the crews that swung the battering rams.
[^9]: I.e., the walls are breached and the palace is overrun.
[^10]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain. Emendation yields “And its mistress is led out and exiled.”
[^11]: Emendation yields “moan.”
[^12]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^13]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain; cf. note at Joel 2.6.
[^14]: Emendation yields “cave.”
[^15]: Emendation yields “your thicket in fire.”
[^16]: Emendation yields “devouring.”
