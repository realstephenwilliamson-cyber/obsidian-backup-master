# The Zohar: Pritzker Edition

Translation and commentary by Daniel C. Matt (volumes 1–9, 2004–2016), Nathan Wolski (volume 10, 2016), Joel Hecker (volume 11, 2016) and Wolski with Hecker (volume 12, 2017), Stanford University Press. Volume 11 was added on 14 Oct 2026.

Converted from the EPUBs on 1 Oct 2026; the Index of Sources was added for volumes 1–2 and 5–9 on 4 Oct 2026. The original e-book files are no longer in the vault. One note per parashah or section. Matt's commentary notes are footnotes in the note that cites them. Two kinds of marker are in the text:

- **Folios** like **[1:15a]** are the standard Zohar references. The block ID sits with the bold folio label at the head of the printed page on which that folio begins; the folio turn itself is marked in brackets in the text, like [15a], usually one to three paragraphs further down. So `[[Zohar 1.07 - Parashat Be-Reshit#^z1-15a]]` opens the page where [[Zohar 1.07 - Parashat Be-Reshit#^z1-15a|Zohar 1:15a]] begins.
- **Print pages** like [p. 106] match the printed volume.

Each parashah note also answers to "Zohar" plus the parashah name, e.g. [[Zohar 1.07 - Parashat Be-Reshit|Zohar Be-Reshit]].
