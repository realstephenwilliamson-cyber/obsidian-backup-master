## Mūla

दाहाप्यायमयीं शुद्धिं दीप्तसौम्यविभेदतः ।
क्रमेण कुर्यादथवा मातृसद्भावमन्त्रतः ॥ ^mula

dāhāpyāyamayīṃ śuddhiṃ dīptasaumyavibhedataḥ |
krameṇa kuryādathavā mātṛsadbhāvamantrataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 09 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 1#^pg238|p. 221]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=238>))
>
> Having entered the abode of the sacrifice (yāgaukas), rich with scent and incense, facing east (or) north, (the officiant should purify himself) in due order (krameṇa) with the Parā or Mālinī (sequence of phonemes, deposited) both in the reverse and in the forward order. This purification, according to whether it is (done in the reverse order, is) inflamed, or (done in the forward order,) tranquil, consists of (the process of) combustion and nourishment. Or else (this may be done with just) the mantra Mātṛsadbhāva (alone). (with 29.18–19)

## Viveka

> [!viveka]- Viveka in English (Dyczkowski, vol. X, pp. 222–223)
> ![[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 09 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 1#Viveka on 29.18–19]]

> यागौको गन्धधूपाढ्यं प्रविश्य प्रागुदङ्मुखः ।
> परया वाऽथ मालिन्या विलोमाच्चानुलोमतः ॥
> दाहाप्यायमयीं शुद्धिं दीप्तसौम्यविभेदतः ।
> क्रमेण कुर्यादथवा मातृसद्भावमन्त्रतः ॥

प्रविश्येति--देहलीमात्रपूजनपूर्वम् । विलोमादिति--संहारक्रमेण पादाभ्यां शिरोन्तम् । अनुलोमत इति--सृष्टिक्रमेण शिरस्तः पादान्तम् । दाहे दीप्ता आप्यायने सौम्या--इत्युक्तम्--क्रमेणेति ॥ १८-१९ ॥
