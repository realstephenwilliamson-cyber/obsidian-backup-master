## Mūla

आनन्द उद्भवः कम्पो निद्रा घूर्णिश्च पञ्चमी ।
इत्येवं वदता शक्तितारतम्याभिधायिना ॥ ^mula

ānanda udbhavaḥ kampo nidrā ghūrṇiśca pañcamī |
ityevaṃ vadatā śaktitāratamyābhidhāyinā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 11 - Chapter Twenty#^pg259|p. 239]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=259>))
>
> This same (kind of initiation) has been mentioned by the Supreme Lord in the Śrīpūrvaśāstra (Mālinīvijayottara) also, where he speaks of the gradation of the (descent of the) power (of grace), as indicated by (the signs of its attainment). They are joy, an upward leap, trembling, sleep, and the rolling of the eyes (ghūrni), as the fifth. (with 20.11cd–13)
