# Job 39

Compare: [[NRSVue Job 39|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 39

**39:1** Do you know the season when the mountain goats give birth?  
Can you mark the time when the hinds calve? ^39-1

**39:2** Can you count the months they must complete?  
Do you know the season they give birth, ^39-2

**39:3** When they couch to bring forth their offspring,  
To deliver their young? ^39-3

**39:4** Their young are healthy; they grow up in the open;  
They leave and return no more. ^39-4

**39:5** Who sets the wild ass free?  
Who loosens the bonds of the onager, ^39-5

**39:6** Whose home I have made the wilderness,  
The salt land his dwelling-place? ^39-6

**39:7** He scoffs at the tumult of the city,  
Does not hear the shouts of the driver. ^39-7

**39:8** He roams the hills for his pasture;  
He searches for any green thing. ^39-8

**39:9** Would the wild ox agree to serve you?  
Would he spend the night at your crib? ^39-9

**39:10** Can you hold the wild ox by ropes to the furrow?  
Would he plow up the valleys behind you? ^39-10

**39:11** Would you rely on his great strength  
And leave your toil to him? ^39-11

**39:12** Would you trust him to bring in the seed  
And gather it in from your threshing floor? ^39-12

**39:13** The wing of the ostrich beats joyously;  
Are her pinions and plumage like the stork’s? ^39-13

**39:14** She leaves her eggs on the ground,  
Letting them warm in the dirt, ^39-14

**39:15** Forgetting they may be crushed underfoot,  
Or trampled by a wild beast. ^39-15

**39:16** Her young are cruelly abandoned as if they were not hers;  
Her labor is in vain for lack of concern. ^39-16

**39:17** For God deprived her of wisdom,  
Gave her no share of understanding, ^39-17

**39:18** Else she would soar on high,  
Scoffing at the horse and its rider. ^39-18

**39:19** Do you give the horse his strength?  
Do you clothe his neck with a mane? ^39-19

**39:20** Do you make him quiver like locusts,  
His majestic snorting [spreading] terror? ^39-20

**39:21** He[^1] paws with force, he runs with vigor,  
Charging into battle. ^39-21

**39:22** He scoffs at fear; he cannot be frightened;  
He does not recoil from the sword. ^39-22

**39:23** A quiverful of arrows whizzes by him,  
And the flashing spear and the javelin. ^39-23

**39:24** Trembling with excitement, he swallows[^2] the land;  
He does not turn aside at the blast of the trumpet. ^39-24

**39:25** As the trumpet sounds, he says, “Aha!”  
From afar he smells the battle,  
The roaring and shouting of the officers. ^39-25

**39:26** Is it by your wisdom that the hawk grows pinions,  
Spreads his wings to the south? ^39-26

**39:27** Does the eagle soar at your command,  
Building his nest high, ^39-27

**39:28** Dwelling in the rock,  
Lodging upon the fastness of a jutting rock? ^39-28

**39:29** From there he spies out his food;  
From afar his eyes see it. ^39-29

**39:30** His young gulp blood;  
Where the slain are, there is he. ^39-30

[^1]: Lit. “They …”
[^2]: Or “digs up.”
