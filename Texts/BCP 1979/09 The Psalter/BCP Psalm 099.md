# Psalm 99

← [[BCP Psalm 098]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 100]] →

*Dominus regnavit* · Book Four · Nineteenth Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 099]]

**99:1** The LORD is King;  
let the people tremble; \*  
he is enthroned upon the cherubim;  
let the earth shake. ^99-1

[p. 729] ^pg729

**99:2** The LORD is great in Zion; \*  
he is high above all peoples. ^99-2

**99:3** Let them confess his Name, which is great and awesome; \*  
he is the Holy One. ^99-3

**99:4** "O mighty King, lover of justice,  
you have established equity; \*  
you have executed justice and righteousness in Jacob." ^99-4

**99:5** Proclaim the greatness of the LORD our God  
and fall down before his footstool; \*  
he is the Holy One. ^99-5

**99:6** Moses and Aaron among his priests,  
and Samuel among those who call upon his Name, \*  
they called upon the LORD, and he answered them. ^99-6

**99:7** He spoke to them out of the pillar of cloud; \*  
they kept his testimonies and the decree that he gave them. ^99-7

**99:8** "O LORD our God, you answered them indeed; \*  
you were a God who forgave them,  
yet punished them for their evil deeds." ^99-8

**99:9** Proclaim the greatness of the LORD our God  
and worship him upon his holy hill; \*  
for the LORD our God is the Holy One. ^99-9
