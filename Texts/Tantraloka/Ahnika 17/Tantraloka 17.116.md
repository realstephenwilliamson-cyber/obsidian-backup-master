## Mūla

मायान्तशुद्धौ सर्वाः स्युः क्रिया ह्यपरया सदा ।
द्व्यात्मया सकलान्ते तु निष्कले परयैव तु ॥ ^mula

māyāntaśuddhau sarvāḥ syuḥ kriyā hyaparayā sadā |
dvyātmayā sakalānte tu niṣkale parayaiva tu || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 08 - Chapter Seventeen#^pg209|p. 189]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=209>))
>
> The rule (niyama) regarding all the Mantras in general (applied) in the course of (this) initiation will (now be) stated. All the rites (kriyā) (that serve) to purify (the principles) up to the end of Māyā should always be (done) with (the mantra of) Aparā. (Those from Pure Knowledge) up to the end of immanent Śiva (sakala) with Parāparā (dvyātmā), and those up to transcendental Śiva (niṣkala) with Parā (Mantra). (The Mantras of) Pibanī and the rest (of the group of eight goddesses are applied) up to the end of Īśa, and the Five Limbs (aṅgapañcaka) at the end of immanent Śiva (sakala). The best teacher, should perform the rite with an eye on the prescription (vidhi) (executed) in this way. (with 17.115cd–117)
