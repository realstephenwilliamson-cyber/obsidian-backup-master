# Zechariah 2 (Douay-Rheims)

*Zacharias 2 in the Douay-Rheims*

[[DRB Zechariah 01|← Zechariah 1]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Zechariah 03|Zechariah 3 →]]

**2:1** And I lifted up my eyes, and saw, and behold a man, with a measuring line in his hand. ^2-1

**2:2** And I said: Whither goest thou? and he said to me: To measure Jerusalem, and to see how great is the breadth thereof, and how great the length thereof. ^2-2

**2:3** And behold the angel that spoke in me went forth, and another angel went out to meet him. ^2-3

**2:4** And he said to him: Run, speak to this young man, saying: Jerusalem shall be inhabited without walls, by reason of the multitude of men, and of the beasts in the midst thereof. ^2-4

**2:5** And I will be to it, saith the Lord, a wall of fire round about: and I will be in glory in the midst thereof. ^2-5

**2:6** O, O flee ye out of the land of the north, saith the Lord, for I have scattered you into the four winds of heaven, saith the Lord. ^2-6

**2:7** O Sion, flee, thou that dwellest with the daughter of Babylon: ^2-7

**2:8** For thus saith the Lord of hosts: After the glory he hath sent me to the nations that have robbed you: for he that toucheth you, toucheth the apple of my eye: ^2-8

**2:9** For behold I lift up my hand upon them, and they shall be a prey to those that served them: and you shall know that the Lord of hosts sent me. ^2-9

**2:10** Sing praise, and rejoice, O daughter of Sion: for behold I come, and I will dwell in the midst of thee: saith the Lord. ^2-10

**2:11** And many nations shall be joined to the Lord in that day, and they shall be my people, and I will dwell in the midst of thee: and thou shalt know that the Lord of hosts hath sent me to thee. ^2-11

**2:12** And the Lord shall possess Juda his portion in the sanctified land: and he shall yet choose Jerusalem. ^2-12

**2:13** Let all flesh be silent at the presence of the Lord: for he is risen up out of his. holy habitation. ^2-13
