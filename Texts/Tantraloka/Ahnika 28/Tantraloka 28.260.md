## Mūla

तावत्सिद्धिजुषोऽप्युक्ता मुक्त्यै क्षेत्रोपयोगिता ।
सम्यग्ज्ञानिनि वृत्तान्तः पुरस्तात्तूपदेक्ष्यते ॥ ^mula

tāvatsiddhijuṣo'pyuktā muktyai kṣetropayogitā |
samyagjñānini vṛttāntaḥ purastāttūpadekṣyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 05 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 3#^pg129|p. 112]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=129>))
>
> Moreover, it is said by that (scripture) that, by engaging in practice (centred) on the principles of Earth and the rest, (those who do so come to) possess only the accomplishments (siddhi) (corresponding to them), (whereas attending) sacred places serves as an aid to (attaining) liberation. (with 28.259cd–260ab)
>
> The course of life (vrttānta) of one who possesses proper knowledge (of reality) will be taught further ahead. (with 28.260cd)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* नन्वेवं क्षेत्रोपयोगः किमज्ञानामेव किं स्वित् ज्ञानिनामपि?-- इत्याशङ्क्य आह--

> सम्यग्ज्ञानिनि वृत्तान्तः पुरस्तात्तूपदेक्ष्यते ॥
