## Mūla

दीक्षायां च प्रतिष्ठायां समयानां विशोधने ।
कामनार्थं च कर्तव्यो मूर्तियागः स पञ्चधा ॥ ^mula

dīkṣāyāṃ ca pratiṣṭhāyāṃ samayānāṃ viśodhane |
kāmanārthaṃ ca kartavyo mūrtiyāgaḥ sa pañcadhā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 04 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 2#^pg60|p. 43]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=60>))
>
> The Worship of the Embodiments (mūrtiyāga) should be performed when the sacred thread is offered (to the deity), when rites for the ancestors (śrāddha) (are performed), on the days when a parvan (occurs), when a solar or a lunar eclipse and the like occur, so too during the common (laukika) parvans (that are not the Kaula ones), during a festival (utsava), a marriage and the like, when a brahminical sacrificial rite (yajñakarman) (takes place), during an initiation and the installation (of a Liñga etc), when the pledges are cleansed (viśodhana) (of the sins of transgression), and (finally,) in order to (get what one) desires. (The Worship of the Embodiments) is of five kinds. (with 28.76cd–78)
