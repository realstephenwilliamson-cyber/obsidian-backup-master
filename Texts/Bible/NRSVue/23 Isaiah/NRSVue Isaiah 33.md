# Isaiah 33 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Isaiah 32|← Isaiah 32]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Isaiah 34|Isaiah 34 →]]

## A prophecy of deliverance from foes

**33:1** Woe to the destroyer, who yourself have not been destroyed; you treacherous one, with whom no one has dealt treacherously! When you have ceased to destroy, you will be destroyed; and when you have stopped dealing treacherously, you will be dealt with treacherously. ^33-1

**33:2** O LORD, be gracious to us; we wait for you. Be our arm every morning, our salvation in the time of trouble. ^33-2

**33:3** At the sound of tumult, peoples fled; before your majesty, nations scattered. ^33-3

**33:4** Spoil was gathered as the caterpillar gathers; as locusts leap, they leaped^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] upon it. ^33-4

**33:5** The LORD is exalted; he dwells on high; he filled Zion with justice and righteousness; ^33-5

**33:6** he will be the stability of your times, abundance of salvation, wisdom, and knowledge; the fear of the LORD is Zion’s treasure.^[Heb *his treasure*] ^33-6

**33:7** Listen! The people of Ariel^[Heb mss: Meaning of MT uncertain] cry out in the streets; the envoys of peace weep bitterly. ^33-7

**33:8** The highways are deserted; travelers have quit the road. The treaty is broken; its oaths^[Q ms: MT *cities*] are despised, the people are disregarded. ^33-8

**33:9** The land mourns and languishes; Lebanon is confounded and withers away; Sharon is like a desert, and Bashan and Carmel shake off their leaves. ^33-9

**33:10** “Now I will arise,” says the LORD, “now I will lift myself up; now I will be exalted. ^33-10

**33:11** You conceive chaff; you bring forth stubble; wind like a fire^[Cn: Heb *your breath*] will consume you. ^33-11

**33:12** And the peoples will be as if burned to lime, like thorns cut down that are burned in the fire.” ^33-12

**33:13** Hear, you who are far away, what I have done, and you who are near, acknowledge my might. ^33-13

**33:14** The sinners in Zion are afraid; trembling has seized the godless: “Who among us can live with the devouring fire? Who among us can live with everlasting flames?” ^33-14

**33:15** Those who walk righteously and speak uprightly, who despise the gain of oppression, who wave away a bribe instead of accepting it, who stop their ears from hearing of bloodshed and shut their eyes from looking on evil, ^33-15

**33:16** they will live on the heights; their refuge will be the fortresses of rocks; their food will be supplied, their water assured. ^33-16

## The land of the majestic king

**33:17** Your eyes will see the king in his beauty; they will behold a land that stretches far away. ^33-17

**33:18** Your mind will muse on the terror: “Where is the one who counted? Where is the one who weighed the tribute? Where is the one who counted the towers?” ^33-18

**33:19** No longer will you see the insolent people, the people of an obscure speech that you cannot comprehend, stammering in a language that you cannot understand. ^33-19

**33:20** Look on Zion, the city of our appointed festivals! Your eyes will see Jerusalem, a quiet habitation, an immovable tent whose stakes will never be pulled up and none of whose ropes will be broken. ^33-20

**33:21** But there the LORD in majesty will be for us a place of broad rivers and streams where no galley with oars can go nor stately ship can pass. ^33-21

**33:22** For the LORD is our judge; the LORD is our ruler; the LORD is our king; he will save us. ^33-22

**33:23** Your rigging hangs loose; it cannot hold the mast firm in its place or keep the sail spread out. Then the blind will divide abundant spoil,^[Cn: Heb *Then prey and spoil in abundance will be divided*] and the lame will take plunder. ^33-23

**33:24** And no inhabitant will say, “I am sick”; the people who live there will be forgiven their iniquity. ^33-24
