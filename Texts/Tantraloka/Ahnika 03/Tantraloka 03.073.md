## Mūla

तदा घोराः परा देव्यो जाताःशैवाध्वदैशिकाः ।
स्वात्मप्रत्यवमर्शो यः प्रागभूदेकवीरकः ॥ ^mula

tadā ghorāḥ parā devyo jātāḥśaivādhvadaiśikāḥ |
svātmapratyavamarśo yaḥ prāgabhūdekavīrakaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. II
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 02 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v02 06 - Chapter Three, part 3#^pg186|p. 170]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 02.pdf#page=186>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 3.72cd–73ab.
>
> If this same (power of the will) is in its aroused (prakṣubdha) form, it becomes Īsitrī (the Sovereign One) (Ī). Then the supreme goddesses Aghorā, who are the teachers of the Śaiva path, come into being. (with 3.72cd–73ab)
>
> The reflective awareness of one’s own nature that, prior (to its aroused state), dwelt alone without companion (ekavīraka), and is (at the same time) the expansion (unmeṣa) (U) of the (objective) universe which is (yet) to be known (jñātavya), abides as the power of knowledge. (with 3.73cd–74ab)

*Lead-in:* एवमिच्छाशक्तिं द्विप्रकारामभिधाय ज्ञानशक्तिमप्याह --

> स्वात्मप्रत्यवमर्शो यः प्रागभूदेकवीरकः ॥
> ज्ञातव्यविश्वोन्मेषात्मा ज्ञानशक्तितया स्थितः ।

इह खलु प्राक् प्रक्षुब्धत्वरूपत्वात्पूर्वं व्यतिरिक्तविमृश्याभावात् स्वात्ममात्रनिष्ठः, अत एव "एकवीरको"यः परामर्शः आसीत्, स एव ज्ञानशक्तित्वेन अन्तर्विजिज्ञास्यतया इष्टस्य विश्वस्य योऽसौ उन्मेषः आद्यः परिस्पन्दः, तद्रूपः सन् अवस्थितः इति पञ्चमबीजनिर्णय इति ॥ ७३ ॥
