# LXX Psalms 151

← [[LXX Psalms 150]] · [[LXX]]

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Not in the Hebrew canon (in the LXX only) · [[Psalms numbering]]

**151:1** This Psalm is a genuine one of David, though supernumerary, *composed* when he fought in single combat with Goliad. I was small among my brethren, and youngest in my father's house: I tended my father's sheep.[^1]  ^151-1

**151:2** My hands formed a musical instrument, and my fingers tuned a psaltery.  ^151-2

**151:3** And who shall tell my Lord? the Lord himself, he himself hears.  ^151-3

**151:4** He sent forth his angel, and took me from my father's sheep, and he anointed me with the oil of his anointing.  ^151-4

**151:5** My brothers were handsome and tall; but the Lord did not take pleasure in them.  ^151-5

**151:6** I went forth to meet the Philistine; and he cursed me by his idols.  ^151-6

**151:7** But I drew his own sword, and beheaded him, and removed reproach from the children of Israel.  ^151-7

[^1]: 151:1 *Alex.* Goliath.
