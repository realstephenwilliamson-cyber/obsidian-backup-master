# LXX Psalms 25

← [[LXX Psalms 24]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 26]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 026]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 026]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**25:1** *A Psalm* of David. Judge me, O Lord; for I have walked in my innocence: and hoping in the Lord I shall not be moved.  ^25-1

**25:2** Prove me, O Lord, and try me; purify as with fire my reins and my heart.  ^25-2

**25:3** For thy mercy is before mine eyes: and I am well pleased with thy truth.  ^25-3

**25:4** I have not sat with the council of vanity, and will in nowise enter in with transgressors.  ^25-4

**25:5** I have hated the assembly of wicked doers; and will not sit with ungodly *men*.  ^25-5

**25:6** I will wash my hands in innocency, and compass thine altar, O Lord:[^1]  ^25-6

**25:7** to hear the voice of praise, and to declare all thy wonderful works.  ^25-7

**25:8** O Lord, I have loved the beauty of thy house, and the place of the tabernacle of thy glory.  ^25-8

**25:9** Destroy not my soul together with the ungodly, nor my life with bloody men:  ^25-9

**25:10** in whose hands *are* iniquities, *and* their right hand is filled with bribes.  ^25-10

**25:11** But I have walked in my innocence: redeem me, and have mercy upon me.  ^25-11

**25:12** My foot stands in an even place: in the congregations will I bless thee, O Lord.[^2]  ^25-12

[^1]: 25:6 *Gr.* innocent *things.*
[^2]: 25:12 *Gr.* in evenness or uprightness.
