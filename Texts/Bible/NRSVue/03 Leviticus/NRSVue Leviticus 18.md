# Leviticus 18 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Leviticus 17|← Leviticus 17]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Leviticus 19|Leviticus 19 →]]

## Sexual relations

**18:1** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ^18-1

**18:2** “Speak to the Israelites and say to them: I am the LORD your God. ^18-2

**18:3** You shall not do as they do in the land of Egypt, where you lived, and you shall not do as they do in the land of Canaan, to which I am bringing you. You shall not follow their statutes. ^18-3

**18:4** My ordinances you shall observe, and my statutes you shall keep, following them: I am the LORD your God. ^18-4

**18:5** You shall keep my statutes and my ordinances; by doing so one shall live: I am the LORD. ^18-5

**18:6** “None of you shall approach anyone near of kin to uncover nakedness: I am the LORD. ^18-6

**18:7** You shall not uncover the nakedness of your father, which is the nakedness of your mother; she is your mother; you shall not uncover her nakedness. ^18-7

**18:8** You shall not uncover the nakedness of your father’s wife; it is the nakedness of your father. ^18-8

**18:9** You shall not uncover the nakedness of your sister, your father’s daughter or your mother’s daughter, whether born at home or born abroad. ^18-9

**18:10** You shall not uncover the nakedness of your son’s daughter or of your daughter’s daughter, for their nakedness is your own nakedness. ^18-10

**18:11** You shall not uncover the nakedness of your father’s wife’s daughter, born in your father’s house, since she is your sister. ^18-11

**18:12** You shall not uncover the nakedness of your father’s sister; she is your father’s flesh. ^18-12

**18:13** You shall not uncover the nakedness of your mother’s sister, for she is your mother’s flesh. ^18-13

**18:14** You shall not uncover the nakedness of your father’s brother; that is, you shall not approach his wife; she is your aunt. ^18-14

**18:15** You shall not uncover the nakedness of your daughter-in-law: she is your son’s wife; you shall not uncover her nakedness. ^18-15

**18:16** You shall not uncover the nakedness of your brother’s wife; it is your brother’s nakedness. ^18-16

**18:17** You shall not uncover the nakedness of a woman and her daughter, and you shall not take^[Or *marry*] her son’s daughter or her daughter’s daughter to uncover her nakedness; they are your^[Gk: Heb lacks *your*] flesh; it is depravity. ^18-17

**18:18** And you shall not take^[Or *marry*] a woman as a rival to her sister, uncovering her nakedness while her sister is still alive. ^18-18

**18:19** “You shall not approach a woman to uncover her nakedness while she is in her menstrual uncleanness. ^18-19

**18:20** You shall not have sexual relations with your neighbor’s wife and defile yourself with her. ^18-20

**18:21** You shall not give any of your offspring to sacrifice them^[Heb *to pass them over*] to Molech and so profane the name of your God: I am the LORD. ^18-21

**18:22** You shall not lie with a male as with a woman; it is an abomination. ^18-22

**18:23** You shall not have sexual relations with any animal and defile yourself with it, nor shall any woman give herself to an animal to have sexual relations with it; it is perversion. ^18-23

**18:24** “Do not defile yourselves in any of these ways, for by all these practices the nations I am casting out before you have defiled themselves. ^18-24

**18:25** Thus the land became defiled, and I punished it for its iniquity, and the land vomited out its inhabitants. ^18-25

**18:26** But you shall keep my statutes and my ordinances and commit none of these abominations, either the native-born or the alien who resides among you ^18-26

**18:27** (for the inhabitants of the land, who were before you, committed all of these abominations, and the land became defiled); ^18-27

**18:28** otherwise the land will vomit you out for defiling it, as it vomited out the nation that was before you. ^18-28

**18:29** For whoever commits any of these abominations shall be cut off from their people. ^18-29

**18:30** So keep my charge not to commit any of these abominations that were done before you and not to defile yourselves by them: I am the LORD your God.” ^18-30
