## Mūla

अद्वैतमेव न द्वैतमित्याज्ञा परमेशितुः ।
सिद्धान्तवैष्णवाद्युक्ता मन्त्रा मलयुतास्ततः ॥ ^mula

advaitameva na dvaitamityājñā parameśituḥ |
siddhāntavaiṣṇavādyuktā mantrā malayutāstataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 10 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 2#^pg288|p. 271]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=288>))
>
> IOr else one may worship in the centre according to whatever is one’s own (preferred) procedure (svānuṣṭhāna). The Supreme Lord’s command is that (there should) only be nonduality (between pure and impure offerings,) not duality. It is because (of this duality) that Mantras taught (in the dualist scriptures of) the Siddhānta, Vaiṣṇavas and the like are (sullied) with impurity (mala). As they cannot bear this much (tāvat) energy (fejas), they are without life here in (these rites grounded in) nonduality. (with 29.73cd–75ab)
