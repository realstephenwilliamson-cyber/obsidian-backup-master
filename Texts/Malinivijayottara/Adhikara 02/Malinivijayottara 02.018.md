## Mūla

पञ्चधा भूतसंज्ञस्तु तथा त्रिंशतिधा परः ।
आत्माख्यस्त्रिविधः प्रोक्तो दशधा मन्त्रसंज्ञकः ॥ ^mula

pañcadhā bhūtasamjñas tu tathā trimśatidhā paraḥ |
ātmākhyas trividhaḥ prokto daśadhā mantrasamjñakaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, July 2022 – September 2022
> p. 31 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Malini (journal), July-September 2022.pdf#page=33>))
>
> **The Penetrations of Rudra's Power**
>
> O fair faced lady, penetration by Rudra's power is described (paripaṭhyate)[^34] as being of five kinds. (These are the penetrations) of 1) the gross elements, 2) the metaphysical principles, 3) the Self, 4) the Lords of Mantra and 5) power.[^35] The one called elemental is here of five kinds and the next one thirty. The one called the Self is said to be of three kinds and the one called Mantra, ten kinds. Again, one should know that in reality the one called Power is also of two kinds. (Thus) this penetration is said to be divided into fifty kinds.[^36] (with 2.17–19)

[^34]: Jayaratha reads *nanu caryate* for *paripaṭhyate.* See TĀ 3/293ab (3/292), 13/117cd-120ab, 214-216.
[^35]: One could understand, as Jayaratha apparently does –*mantreśaśakti*- to means 'Mantra and the power of the Lord'. See note to the citation of these in TĀv 1/186cd-187ab.
[^36]: MV 2/17-19 is quoted in TĀv ad 1/186cd-187ab.
