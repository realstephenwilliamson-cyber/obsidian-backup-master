# Psalm 70

← [[BCP Psalm 069]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 071]] →

*Deus, in adjutorium* · Book Two · Thirteenth Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 070]]

**70:1** Be pleased, O God, to deliver me; \*  
O LORD, make haste to help me. ^70-1

**70:2** Let those who seek my life be ashamed  
and altogether dismayed; \*  
let those who take pleasure in my misfortune  
draw back and be disgraced. ^70-2

**70:3** Let those who say to me "Aha!" and gloat over me turn back, \*  
because they are ashamed. ^70-3

**70:4** Let all who seek you rejoice and be glad in you; \*  
let those who love your salvation say for ever,  
"Great is the LORD!" ^70-4

**70:5** But as for me, I am poor and needy; \*  
come to me speedily, O God. ^70-5

**70:6** You are my helper and my deliverer; \*  
O LORD, do not tarry. ^70-6
