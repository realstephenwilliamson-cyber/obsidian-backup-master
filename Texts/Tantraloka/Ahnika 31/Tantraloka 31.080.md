## Mūla

दलानि शुक्लवर्णानि प्रतिवारणया सह ।
पीठं तद्वच्चतुष्कोणं कर्णिकार्धसमं बहिः ॥ ^mula

dalāni śuklavarṇāni prativāraṇayā saha |
pīṭhaṃ tadvaccatuṣkoṇaṃ karṇikārdhasamaṃ bahiḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 05 - Chapter Thirty-One, part 2#^pg120|p. 102]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=120>))
>
> The calix should be coloured yellow and the stamens white, red and yellow, according to whether they are at the root, middle or end (i.e. on the left, middle or right). The petals, along with the protective border (prativāraṇā), are white. Such also is the square pedestal (pīṭha) (on which the lotus is situated). (It extends) outwards (beyond the lotus) for a space equal to half (the width) of the calix. (It is supported) by its (four) legs, that are of four (different) colours, namely, (moving clockwise) beginning with the one in the southeast, white, red, yellow and black. The prongs are (coloured with the same) three (colours, omitting red). This is how the maṇḍala should be. The shaft (of the trident) is purple (nīlarakta). The bulb (at the base of the shaft) (āmalasāraka) is yellow and the root should be coloured red. It should be fashioned in the western (lower part of the maṇḍala), leaving a space of four finger-breadths to the east (i.e. in front) of the door. (with 31.79–83ab)
