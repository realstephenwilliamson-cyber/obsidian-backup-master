# Φίλιππος

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · noun

**Dodson:** Philip — Philip, (a) one of the twelve apostles of Jesus, (b) tetrarch of the Ituraean and Trachonitic region, half-brother of Herod Antipas, tetrarch of Galilee, (c) one of the seven original deacons at Jerusalem and a missionary. (Strong's 5376; headword Φίλιππος, ου, ὁ)
