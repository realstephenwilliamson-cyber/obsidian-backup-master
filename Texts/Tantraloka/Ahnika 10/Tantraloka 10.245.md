## Mūla

सर्वसत्तासमापूर्णं विश्वं पश्येद्यतो यतः ।
ज्ञानी ततस्ततः संवित्तत्त्वमस्य प्रकाशते ॥ ^mula

sarvasattāsamāpūrṇaṃ viśvaṃ paśyedyato yataḥ |
jñānī tatastataḥ saṃvittattvamasya prakāśate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 13 - Chapter Ten, part 4#^pg471|p. 452]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=471>))

> The principle of consciousness manifests to the man of knowledge (jñānin) to the degree in which he perceives the universe to be completely filled with the Being (sattā) of all things.
