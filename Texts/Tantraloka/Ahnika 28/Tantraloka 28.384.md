## Mūla

प्रवेशं संप्रविष्टस्य न विचारं तु कारयेत् ।
लोकाचारस्थितो यस्तु प्रविष्टे तादृशे तु सः ॥ ^mula

praveśaṃ saṃpraviṣṭasya na vicāraṃ tu kārayet |
lokācārasthito yastu praviṣṭe tādṛśe tu saḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 07 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 5#^pg191|p. 174]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=191>))
>
> It is also said in the venerable Picumata that ‘first of all, (take care) to protect the entrance (to the rite) with effort, (to prevent those who are not qualified from coming in). One should not deliberate concerning one who has entered.’ (with 28.383cd–384ab)
>
> If such a one who is engaged in the common observance (of Vedic norms) (lokācāra) (happens to have) entered (the gathering, the officiant should interrupt the rite). Without completing that common (basic) practice (samācāra) (at that time), he should worship the assembly (cakra) again (later). (with 28.384cd–385ab)

*Lead-in:* एतच्च अतन्मयत्वेऽपि अधिकृतविषयं ज्ञेयम्, न अन्यथा--इत्याह--

तादृशे इति--लोकाचारस्थिते । स इति--चक्राद्यर्चयिता । तमिति-- मेलकादावाम्नातम् । पुनरिति--तस्मिन्निर्गते, परेऽहनि वा ॥
