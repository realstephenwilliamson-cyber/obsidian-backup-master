# Isaiah 41

Compare: [[NRSVue Isaiah 41|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 41

**41:1** Stand silent before Me, coastlands,  
And let nations renew their strength.[^1]  
Let them approach to state their case;  
Let us come forward together for argument. ^41-1

**41:2** Who has roused a victor[^2] from the East,  
Summoned him to His service?  
Has delivered up nations to him,  
And trodden sovereigns down?  
Has rendered their[^3] swords like dust,  
Their[^4] bows like wind-blown straw? ^41-2

**41:3** He pursues them, he goes on unscathed;  
No shackle[^5] is placed on his feet. ^41-3

**41:4** Who has wrought and achieved this?  
He who announced the generations from the start—  
I, the LORD, who was first  
And will be with the last as well. ^41-4

**41:5** The coastlands look on in fear,  
The ends of earth tremble.  
They draw near and come; ^41-5

**41:6** Each one helps the other,  
Saying to his fellow, “Take courage!” ^41-6

**41:7** The woodworker encourages the smith;  
He who flattens with the hammer  
[Encourages] him who pounds the anvil.  
He says of the riveting, “It is good!”  
And he fixes it with nails,  
That it may not topple. ^41-7

**41:8** But you, Israel, My servant,  
Jacob, whom I have chosen,  
Seed of Abraham My friend— ^41-8

**41:9** You whom I drew from the ends of the earth  
And called from its far corners,  
To whom I said: You are My servant;  
I chose you, I have not rejected you— ^41-9

**41:10** Fear not, for I am with you,  
Be not frightened, for I am your God;  
I strengthen you and I help you,  
I uphold you with My victorious right hand. ^41-10

**41:11** Shamed and chagrined shall be  
All who contend with you;  
They who strive with you  
Shall become as naught and shall perish. ^41-11

**41:12** You may seek, but shall not find  
Those who struggle with you;  
Less than nothing shall be  
The men who battle against you. ^41-12

**41:13** For I the LORD am your God,  
Who grasped your right hand,  
Who say to you: Have no fear;  
I will be your help. ^41-13

**41:14** Fear not, O worm Jacob,  
O men of[^6] Israel:  
*I* will help you  
—declares the LORD—  
*I* your Redeemer, the Holy One of Israel. ^41-14

**41:15** I will make of you a threshing board,  
A new thresher, with many spikes;  
You shall thresh mountains to dust,  
And make hills like chaff. ^41-15

**41:16** You shall winnow them  
And the wind shall carry them off;  
The whirlwind shall scatter them.  
But you shall rejoice in the LORD,  
And glory in the Holy One of Israel. ^41-16

**41:17** The poor and the needy  
Seek water,[^7] and there is none;  
Their tongue is parched with thirst.  
I the LORD will respond to them.  
I, the God of Israel, will not forsake them. ^41-17

**41:18** I will open up streams on the bare hills  
And fountains amid the valleys;  
I will turn the desert into ponds,  
The arid land into springs of water. ^41-18

**41:19** I will plant cedars in the wilderness,  
Acacias and myrtles and oleasters;  
I will set cypresses in the desert,  
Box trees and elms as well— ^41-19

**41:20** That men may see and know,  
Consider and comprehend  
That the LORD’s hand has done this,  
That the Holy One of Israel has wrought it. ^41-20

**41:21** Submit your case, says the LORD;  
Offer your pleas, says the King of Jacob. ^41-21

**41:22** Let them approach[^8] and tell us what will happen.  
Tell us what has occurred,[^9]  
And we will take note of it;  
Or announce to us what will occur,  
That we may know the outcome. ^41-22

**41:23** Foretell what is yet to happen,  
That we may know that you are gods!  
Do anything, good or bad,  
That we may be awed and see.[^10] ^41-23

**41:24** Why, you are less than nothing,  
Your effect is less than nullity;  
One who chooses you is an abomination. ^41-24

**41:25** I have roused him from the north, and he has come,  
From the sunrise, one who invokes My name;  
And he has trampled rulers like mud,  
Like a potter treading clay. ^41-25

**41:26** Who foretold this from the start, that we may note it;  
From aforetime, that we might say, “He is right”?  
Not one foretold, not one announced;  
No one has heard your utterance! ^41-26

**41:27** The things once predicted to Zion—  
Behold, here they are![^11]  
And again I send a herald to Jerusalem. ^41-27

**41:28** But I look and there is not a man;  
Not one of them can predict  
Or can respond when I question him. ^41-28

**41:29** See, they are all nothingness,  
Their works are nullity,  
Their statues are naught and nil. ^41-29

[^1]: Connection of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Lit. “victory.”
[^3]: Heb. “his.”
[^4]: Heb. “his.”
[^5]: ’rḥ has this meaning in Old Aramaic.
[^6]: Emendation yields “maggot.”
[^7]: I.e., on the homeward march through the desert.
[^8]: Taking yaggishu intransitively; cf. hiqriv. Exod. 14.10.
[^9]: I.e., former prophecies by your gods which have been fulfilled.
[^10]: Change of vocalization yields “fear”; cf. v. 10.
[^11]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
