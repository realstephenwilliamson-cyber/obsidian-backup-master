## Mūla

वर्तनां च विजानाति स गुरुस्त्रिकशासने ।
तस्य पादरजो मूर्ध्नि धार्यं शिवसमीहिना ॥ ^mula

vartanāṃ ca vijānāti sa gurustrikaśāsane |
tasya pādarajo mūrdhni dhāryaṃ śivasamīhinā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 04 - Chapter Thirty-One, part 1#^pg106|p. 88]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=106>))
>
> He who knows the pervasive extent (vyāpti) of this maṇḍala, the deposition of the deities (within it) and (how to) draw it, is a (true) teacher in the Trika teachings (trikaśāsana). It behoves one who aspires to (realise) Śiva to bear the dust of his feet on (his) head (by bowing devoutly to him). (with 31.50cd–51)
