## Prayer for deliverance from enemies

*To the leader. A Psalm of David.*

**13:1** How long, O LORD? Will you forget me forever? How long will you hide your face from me? ^13-1

**13:2** How long must I bear pain^[Syr: Heb *hold counsels*] in my soul and have sorrow in my heart all day long? How long shall my enemy be exalted over me? ^13-2

**13:3** Consider and answer me, O LORD my God! Give light to my eyes, or I will sleep the sleep of death, ^13-3

**13:4** and my enemy will say, “I have prevailed”; my foes will rejoice because I am shaken. ^13-4

**13:5** But I trusted in your steadfast love; my heart shall rejoice in your salvation. ^13-5

**13:6** I will sing to the LORD because he has dealt bountifully with me. ^13-6
