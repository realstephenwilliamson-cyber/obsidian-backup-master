## Mūla

अनुसन्धाय सदा चेदास्ते मन्त्रोदयं स वै वेत्ति ।
अत्रैव जपं कुर्यादनुचक्रैकत्वसंविदागमने ॥ ^mula

anusandhāya sadā cedāste mantrodayaṃ sa vai vetti |
atraiva japaṃ kuryādanucakraikatvasaṃvidāgamane || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 12 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 4#^pg347|p. 330]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=347>))
>
> The Resonance (dhvani), which is the reflective awareness of consciousness, that arises in this way (by practicing the consciousness of Being) in the abode of the contemplative attainment which is penetration (āveśasamāpatti) into the three-fold reflective awareness (of emission), is here (according to us,) the vitality of Mantra. He indeed knows the arising of Mantra (mantrodaya) if, eager to obtain such fruit that arises there, constantly contemplates his own mantra. (with 29.147cd–149ab)
>
> Here itself, (immersed in the Resonance of awareness,) he should practice the repetition of Mantra (japa) explained (previously) in units of one hundred thousand (lakṣa); as a dynamic state of the Sound (of awareness) (nādavrtti), so that consciousness, in a state of oneness with the subordinate Wheels, may come about (altogether) all at once. (with 29.149cd–150ab)

*Lead-in:* भूयो भूयश्च अत्रैव भावनापरेण भाव्यं येन मध्यचक्रे एव एकाग्र्यं सिद्ध्येत्--इत्याह--

> अत्रैव जपं कुर्यादनुचक्रैकत्वसंविदागमने ॥
> युगपल्लक्षविभेदप्रपञ्चितं नादवृत्त्यैव ।

"क्रमशोऽनुचक्रदेव्यः संविच्चक्रं हि मध्यमं यान्ति ।"(११२)

इत्यादिदृशा युगपदनुचक्रदेवीनामेकत्वेन मुख्यचक्ररूपायां संविदि यदागमनं विश्रान्तिस्तन्निमित्तमत्रैव संवित्परामर्शात्मनि उदीयमाने मन्त्रे नादामर्शमात्रमयतया

"उदये सङ्गमे शान्तौ त्रिलक्षो जप उच्यते ।"

इत्याद्युक्तेन लक्षविभेदेन प्रपञ्चितं जपं कुर्यात्--भूयो भूयोऽनुसन्धानं विदध्यात्--इत्यर्थः ॥
