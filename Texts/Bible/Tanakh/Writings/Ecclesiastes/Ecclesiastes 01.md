## Chapter 1

**1:1** The words of Koheleth[^1] son of David, king in Jerusalem. ^1-1

**1:2** Utter futility!—said Koheleth—  
Utter futility! All is futile! ^1-2

**1:3** What real value is there for a man  
In all the gains[^2] he makes beneath the sun? ^1-3

**1:4** One generation goes, another comes,  
But the earth remains the same forever. ^1-4

**1:5** The sun rises, and the sun sets—  
And glides[^3] back to where it rises. ^1-5

**1:6** Southward blowing,  
Turning northward,  
Ever turning blows the wind;  
On its rounds the wind returns. ^1-6

**1:7** All streams flow into the sea,  
Yet the sea is never full;  
To the place [from] which they flow  
The streams flow back again.[^4] ^1-7

**1:8** All such things are wearisome:  
No man can ever state them;  
The eye never has enough of seeing,  
Nor the ear enough of hearing. ^1-8

**1:9** Only that shall happen  
Which has happened,  
Only that occur  
Which has occurred;  
There is nothing new  
Beneath the sun! ^1-9

**1:10** Sometimes there is a phenomenon of which they say, “Look, this one is new!”—it occurred long since, in ages that went by before us. ^1-10

**1:11** The earlier ones are not remembered; so too those that will occur later will no more be remembered than[^5] those that will occur at the very end. ^1-11

**1:12** I, Koheleth, was king in Jerusalem over Israel. ^1-12

**1:13** I set my mind to study and to probe with wisdom all that happens under the sun.—An unhappy business, that, which God gave men to be concerned with! ^1-13

**1:14** I observed all the happenings beneath the sun, and I found that all is futile and pursuit[^6] of wind: ^1-14

**1:15** A twisted thing that cannot be made straight,  
A lack that cannot be made good. ^1-15

**1:16** I said to myself: “Here I have grown richer and wiser than any that ruled before me over Jerusalem, and my mind has zealously absorbed wisdom and learning.” ^1-16

**1:17** And so I set my mind to appraise wisdom and to appraise madness and folly. And I learned—that this too was pursuit of wind: ^1-17

**1:18** For as wisdom grows, vexation grows;  
To increase learning is to increase heartache. ^1-18

[^1]: Probably “the Assembler,” i.e., of hearers or of sayings; cf. 12.9–11.
[^2]: So Rashbam. Heb. ‘amal usually has this sense in Ecclesiastes; cf. Ps. 105.44.
[^3]: So Targum; cf. Bereshith Rabbah on Gen. 1.17.
[^4]: According to popular belief, through tunnels; so Targum and Rashi.
[^5]: Lit. “will not be remembered like…” For ‘im meaning “like,” cf. 2.16; 7.11; Job 9.26.
[^6]: Lit. “tending,” from root ra‘ah, “to shepherd.”
