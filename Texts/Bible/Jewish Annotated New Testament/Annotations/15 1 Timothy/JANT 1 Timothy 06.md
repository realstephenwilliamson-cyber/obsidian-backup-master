# 1 Timothy 6 (JANT notes)

[[JANT 1 Timothy 05|← 1 Timothy 5]] · [[Jewish Annotated New Testament|JANT index]] · —

Text: [[NRSVue 1 Timothy 06|NRSVue]] · [[1 Timothy 06|BSB and SBLGNT]] · Introduction: [[JANT 1 Timothy - Introduction]]

**[[NRSVue 1 Timothy 06#^6-1|6.1–10]]:** **The yoke of slavery.** ^6-1

**[[NRSVue 1 Timothy 06#^6-1|1]]:** *Slavery*, unlike the Household Codes (Haustafeln) in [[Ephesians 06#^6-5|Eph 6.5–9]]; [[Colossians 03#^3-22|Col 3.22–4.1]], this passage lacks a corresponding stricture for masters. For the Tanakh’s view of the relations between slaves and masters, see [[Torah 47 - Re'eh#^15-12|Deut 15.12–17]]. ^6-1-2

**[[NRSVue 1 Timothy 06#^6-2|2]]:** *Believing masters*, consistent with [[Colossians 03#^3-22|Col 3.22–23]] but with the explicit notice that the slave owner is also a church member. ^6-2

**[[NRSVue 1 Timothy 06#^6-3|3]]:** *Sound words of our Lord Jesus*, the Gospels record no explicit critique of the institution of slavery. *Godliness*, lit., “good (or proper) reverence” (Gk “eusebeia”); the NRSV translates the same word as “religion” in 3.16; see 2.2; 4.7–8; 6.5,6,11). ^6-3

**[[NRSVue 1 Timothy 06#^6-6|6]]:** *Contentment*, lit., “self-sufficiency” (Gk “autarkeia”). ^6-6

**[[NRSVue 1 Timothy 06#^6-7|7]]:** Possible allusion to [[Job 01#^1-21|Job 1.21]] or [[Ecclesiastes 05#^5-14|Eccl 5.15]]. *themselves to good deeds* e Other ancient authorities add *Withdraw yourself from such people* f Other ancient authorities read *world—it is certain that* ^6-7

**[[NRSVue 1 Timothy 06#^6-9|9–10]]:** *Rich . . . love of money*, exhortation to avoid personal peril (see [[LXX Sirach 14#^14-3|Sir 14.3–10]]; [[LXX Sirach 31#^31-5|31.5–11]]). On the dangers of wealth and the concern with contentment, see *m. Avot* 4.1: “Ben Zoma would say, ‘Who is rich? One who is satisfied with his lot. As is stated ([[Psalms 128#^128-2|Ps 128.2]]): If you eat of the toil of your hands, fortunate are you, and good is to you; fortunate are you in this world, and good is to you in the World to Come.’” ^6-9

### 6.11–21: Moral exhortations

*[[NRSVue 1 Timothy 06#^6-11|Text]], p. 440* ^6-11

**[[NRSVue 1 Timothy 06#^6-11|11]]:** *Pursue righteousness*, [[Isaiah 51#^51-1|Isa 51.1]]; [[Proverbs 15#^15-9|Prov. 15.9]]; [[Proverbs 21#^21-21|21.21]]. ^6-11-2

**[[NRSVue 1 Timothy 06#^6-12|12]]:** *Fight the good fight*, 1.18n. *Eternal life,* or “life of the coming age” (Gk “aiōn”). *Good confession*, or “good acknowledgment,” “good assent” to the “sound teaching” (4.6). ^6-12

**[[NRSVue 1 Timothy 06#^6-13|13]]:** *Pontius Pilate*, only NT reference to him outside the Gospels. *Good confession*, the acknowledgment ([[John 18#^18-33|Jn 18.33–38]]) or the refusal to deny ([[Matthew 27#^27-11|Mt 27.11–13]]) that Jesus is the Messiah. ^6-13

**[[NRSVue 1 Timothy 06#^6-14|14]]:** *Commandment* (Gk “entolē”), this may refer to Timothy’s profession of faith (v 12). *Manifestation* (Gk “epiphania,” whence English “epiphany”), Jesus’ return. *King of kings Lord of lords*, see [[Daniel 02#^2-37|Dan 2.37]]; [[LXX 2 Maccabees 13#^13-4|2 Macc 13.4]]; [[LXX 3 Maccabees 05#^5-35|3 Macc 5.35]]; 2 Esd 7.12; [[Revelation 17#^17-14|Rev 17.14]]; [[Revelation 19#^19-16|19.16]]; Philo, *Spec. Law* 1.18. ^6-14

**[[NRSVue 1 Timothy 06#^6-16|16]]:** *Dwells in unapproachable light*, associations of the divine with light are common ([[Psalms 036#^36-10|Ps 36.9]]; [[1 John 01#^1-5|1 Jn 1.5]]; Philo, *Life of Moses* 2.70). *No one has ever seen*, see [[1 John 04#^4-12|1 Jn 4.12]] but contrast [[Torah 18 - Mishpatim#^24-10|Ex 24.10]], where the divine is both approachable and visible; see also Isa 6; Ezek 1–3; *Mekh. de Rabbi Ishmael* states “A maidservant saw at the Red Sea what Isaiah and Ezekiel did not see.” See also *b. Ber.* [[Berakhot 6#6a|6a]]. *Amen*, 1.17n. ^6-16

**[[NRSVue 1 Timothy 06#^6-19|19]]:** *Storing up . . . treasure*, see [[Matthew 06#^6-19|Mt 6.19–20]]; *m. Peʾah* 1.1 (“capital in the world to come” as the fruit of righteous acts now); *T. Levi* 13.5. ^6-19

**[[NRSVue 1 Timothy 06#^6-20|20]]:** *Falsely called knowledge*, perhaps a reference to a Gnostic form of teaching in which secret knowledge is the key to salvation. ^6-20

**[[NRSVue 1 Timothy 06#^6-21|21]]:** *Grace*, see 1.1–2nn. ancient authorities it is singular. Other ancient authorities add *Amen* ^6-21
