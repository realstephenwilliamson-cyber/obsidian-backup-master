## Descendants of David and Solomon

**3:1** These are the sons of David who were born to him in Hebron: the firstborn Amnon, by Ahinoam the Jezreelite; the second Daniel, by Abigail the Carmelite; ^3-1

**3:2** the third Absalom, son of Maacah daughter of King Talmai of Geshur; the fourth Adonijah, son of Haggith; ^3-2

**3:3** the fifth Shephatiah, by Abital; the sixth Ithream, by his wife Eglah; ^3-3

**3:4** six were born to him in Hebron, where he reigned for seven years and six months. And he reigned thirty-three years in Jerusalem. ^3-4

**3:5** These were born to him in Jerusalem: Shimea, Shobab, Nathan, and Solomon, four by Bath-shua daughter of Ammiel; ^3-5

**3:6** then Ibhar, Elishama, Eliphelet, ^3-6

**3:7** Nogah, Nepheg, Japhia, ^3-7

**3:8** Elishama, Eliada, and Eliphelet, nine. ^3-8

**3:9** All these were David’s sons, besides the sons of the concubines, and Tamar was their sister. ^3-9

**3:10** The descendants of Solomon: Rehoboam, Abijah his son, Asa his son, Jehoshaphat his son, ^3-10

**3:11** Joram his son, Ahaziah his son, Joash his son, ^3-11

**3:12** Amaziah his son, Azariah his son, Jotham his son, ^3-12

**3:13** Ahaz his son, Hezekiah his son, Manasseh his son, ^3-13

**3:14** Amon his son, Josiah his son. ^3-14

**3:15** The sons of Josiah: Johanan the firstborn, the second Jehoiakim, the third Zedekiah, the fourth Shallum. ^3-15

**3:16** The descendants of Jehoiakim: Jeconiah his son, Zedekiah his son; ^3-16

**3:17** and the sons of Jeconiah the captive: Shealtiel his son, ^3-17

**3:18** Malchiram, Pedaiah, Shenazzar, Jekamiah, Hoshama, and Nedabiah. ^3-18

**3:19** The sons of Pedaiah: Zerubbabel and Shimei; and the sons of Zerubbabel: Meshullam and Hananiah, and Shelomith was their sister; ^3-19

**3:20** and Hashubah, Ohel, Berechiah, Hasadiah, and Jushab-hesed, five. ^3-20

**3:21** The sons of Hananiah: Pelatiah and Jeshaiah, his son Rephaiah, his son Arnan, his son Obadiah, his son Shecaniah. ^3-21

**3:22** The son of Shecaniah: Shemaiah, and the sons of Shemaiah: Hattush, Igal, Bariah, Neariah, and Shaphat—six. ^3-22

**3:23** The sons of Neariah: Elioenai, Hizkiah, and Azrikam, three. ^3-23

**3:24** The sons of Elioenai: Hodaviah, Eliashib, Pelaiah, Akkub, Johanan, Delaiah, and Anani, seven. ^3-24
