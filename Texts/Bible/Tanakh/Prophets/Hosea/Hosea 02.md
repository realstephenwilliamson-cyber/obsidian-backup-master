## Chapter 2

**2:1** [^1]The number of the people of Israel shall be like that of the sands of the sea, which cannot be measured or counted; and instead of being told, “You are Not-My-People,”[^2] they shall be called Children-of-the-Living-God. ^2-1

**2:2** The people of Judah and the people of Israel shall assemble together and appoint one head over them; and they shall rise from the ground[^3]—for marvelous shall be the day of Jezreel![^4] ^2-2

**2:3** Oh, call[^5] your brothers “My People,”  
And your sisters “Lovingly Accepted!” ^2-3

**2:4** Rebuke[^6] your mother, rebuke her—  
For she is not My wife  
And I am not her husband—  
And let her put away her harlotry from her face  
And her adultery from between her breasts. ^2-4

**2:5** Else will I strip her naked  
And leave her as on the day she was born:  
And I will make her like a wilderness,  
Render her like desert land,  
And let her die of thirst. ^2-5

**2:6** I will also disown her children;  
For they are now a harlot’s brood, ^2-6

**2:7** In that their mother has played the harlot,  
She that conceived them has acted shamelessly—  
Because she thought,  
“I will go after my lovers,  
Who supply my bread and my water,  
My wool and my linen,  
My oil and my drink.” ^2-7

**2:8** Assuredly,  
I will hedge up her[^7] roads with thorns  
And raise walls against her,  
And she shall not find her paths. ^2-8

**2:9** Pursue her lovers as she will,  
She shall not overtake them;  
And seek them as she may,  
She shall never find them.  
Then she will say,  
“I will go and return  
To my first husband,  
For then I fared better than now.” ^2-9

**2:10** And she did not consider this:  
It was I who bestowed on her  
The new grain and wine and oil;  
I who lavished silver on her  
And gold—which they used for Baal. ^2-10

**2:11** Assuredly,  
I will take back My new grain in its time  
And My new wine in its season,  
And I will snatch away My wool and My linen  
That serve to cover her nakedness. ^2-11

**2:12** Now will I uncover her shame  
In the very sight of her lovers,  
And none shall save her from Me. ^2-12

**2:13** And I will end all her rejoicing:  
Her festivals, new moons, and sabbaths—  
All her festive seasons. ^2-13

**2:14** I will lay waste her vines and her fig trees,  
Which she thinks are a fee  
She received from her lovers;  
I will turn them into brushwood,  
And beasts of the field shall devour them. ^2-14

**2:15** Thus will I punish her  
For the days of the Baalim,  
On which she brought them offerings;  
When, decked with earrings and jewels,  
She would go after her lovers,  
Forgetting Me  
—declares the LORD. ^2-15

**2:16** Assuredly,  
I will speak coaxingly to her  
And lead her through the wilderness[^8]  
And speak to her tenderly. ^2-16

**2:17** I will give her her vineyards from there,  
And the Valley of Achor[^9] as a plowland of hope.[^10]  
There she shall respond as in the days of her youth,  
When she came up from the land of Egypt. ^2-17

**2:18** And in that day  
—declares the LORD—  
You will call [Me] Ishi,[^11]  
And no more will you call Me Baali.[^12] ^2-18

**2:19** For I will remove the names of the Baalim from her mouth,  
And they shall nevermore be mentioned by name. ^2-19

**2:20** In that day, I will make a covenant for them with the beasts of the field, the birds of the air, and the creeping things of the ground; I will also banish[^13] bow, sword, and war from the land. Thus I will let them lie down in safety. ^2-20

**2:21** And I will espouse you forever:  
I will espouse you [^14]with righteousness and justice,  
And with goodness and mercy, ^2-21

**2:22** And I will espouse you with faithfulness;  
Then you shall be devoted to the LORD. ^2-22

**2:23** In that day,  
I will respond  
—declares the LORD—  
I will respond to the sky,  
And it shall respond to the earth; ^2-23

**2:24** And the earth shall respond  
With new grain and wine and oil,  
And they shall respond to Jezreel.[^15] ^2-24

**2:25** I will sow her in the land as My own;  
And take Lo-ruhamah back in favor;  
And I will say to Lo-ammi, “You are My people,”  
And he will respond, “[You are] my God.” ^2-25

[^1]: Vv. 1–3 anticipate the conclusion of the chapter.
[^2]: See 1.9.
[^3]: Meaning, perhaps, “from their wretched condition,” or “to ascendancy over the land.”
[^4]: I.e., the day when the name Jezreel will convey a promise (2.23–25) instead of a threat (1.4–5).
[^5]: The Lord addresses Hosea and his fellow North Israelites; see 1.9. The mother is the nation; her children the individual North Israelites.
[^6]: The Lord addresses Hosea and his fellow North Israelites; see 1.9. The mother is the nation; her children the individual North Israelites.
[^7]: Heb. “your.” Vv. 8–9 would read well after v. 15.
[^8]: I.e., her ravaged land (see vv. 5, 10–11, 14); so Ibn Ezra.
[^9]: A desolate region; cf. Isa. 65.10; see further Josh. 7.25–26.
[^10]: Connecting pethaḥ with pittaḥ “to plow” (see Isa. 28.24). Meaning of Heb. uncertain; others “door of hope.”
[^11]: Both Ishi and Baali mean “my husband,” but the latter also means “my Baal.”
[^12]: Both Ishi and Baali mean “my husband,” but the latter also means “my Baal.”
[^13]: Lit. “break.”
[^14]: As the bride-price which the bridegroom will pay, He will confer these qualities on her, so that she will never offend again.
[^15]: I.e., “God sows.” The names of Hosea’s children (1.3–8) are applied here to Israel.
