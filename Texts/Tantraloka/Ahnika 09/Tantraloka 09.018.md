## Mūla

तथोपलम्भमात्रं तौ उपलम्भश्च किं तथा ।
उपलब्धापि विज्ञानस्वभावो योऽस्य सोऽपि हि ॥ ^mula

tathopalambhamātraṃ tau upalambhaśca kiṃ tathā |
upalabdhāpi vijñānasvabhāvo yo'sya so'pi hi || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 03 - Chapter Nine, part 1#^pg47|p. 30]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=47>))
>
> (Our reply is) that, succession or its absence is not (something) more that is added to the inherent nature (of an entity). They are only the manner in which it is perceived. (with 9.17cd–18a)
>
> (If you ask): “why should perception (upalambha) be such (if it has no objective basis)?” (with 9.18b)
>
> If (you were to say) that the perceiver also, whose nature is consciousness, should perceive successively, because its nature is the perception of succession, (with 9.18cd–19ab)

*Lead-in:* ननु उपलम्भस्यापि एवंभावे किं निमित्तम् ? -- इत्याह --

> .........................उपलम्भश्च किं तथा ।

इह खलु उपलब्धा क्रमाक्रमाभ्यामेव तत्तदर्थजातमुपलभते इत्युपलम्भस्यापि क्रमाक्रमायोगः, उपलब्धुश्च संविन्मात्ररूपत्वेऽपि क्रमाक्रमोपलम्भस्वभावत्वादेवंभावः ॥

तदेतदाशङ्कते -

> उपलब्धापि विज्ञानस्वभावो योऽस्य सोऽपि हि ॥
> क्रमोपलम्भरूपत्वात् क्रमेणोपलभेत चेत् ।
