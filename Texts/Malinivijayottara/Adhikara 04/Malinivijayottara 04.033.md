## Mūla

संप्राप्तो घटमानश्च सिद्धः सिद्धतमोऽन्यथा ।
योगी चतुर्विधो देवि यथावत्प्रतिपद्यते ॥ ^mula

saṁprāpto ghaṭamānaś ca siddhaḥ siddhatamo 'nyathā |
yogī caturvidho devi yathāvat pratipadyate || ^iast

> **Types of Yoga and Yogis**
>
> O goddess, one rightly understands a yogi to be of four kinds (namely as) a) one who has acquired (the teachings) (*saṁprāpta*); b) one who is being formed (*ghaṭamāna*); c) one who has accomplished (yoga) (*siddha*) and d) one who is most accomplished (*siddhatama*).
