## Mūla

शिवाभेदेन तत्कुर्यात्तद्वत्पञ्चममप्ययम् ।
तिरोभावाभिधं कृत्यं तथासौ शिवतात्मकः ॥ ^mula

śivābhedena tatkuryāttadvatpañcamamapyayam |
tirobhāvābhidhaṃ kṛtyaṃ tathāsau śivatātmakaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 14 - Chapter Twenty-Three#^pg344|p. 324]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=344>))
>
> Again, if the teacher, being one with Śiva, can perform His five-fold task (krti), his authority is then, for this reason, full (and complete). Thus, just as the teacher is authorized to perform (the following) four tasks, that is, the creation and maintenance of the pure principles, the destruction of Impurity and the grace (of bestowing) union (yojanā) (with Śiva); in the same way, because he is not separate from Śiva, he may perform the fifth task, called obscuration. It is in this way that Śiva’s state is his (very) nature. (with 23.59–61)

## Viveka

> [!viveka]- Viveka in English (Dyczkowski, vol. IX, p. 324)
> ![[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 14 - Chapter Twenty-Three#Viveka on 23.59–61]]

> गुरुः पुनः शिवाभिन्नः सन्यः पञ्चविधां कृतिम् ।
> कुर्याद्यदि ततः पूर्णमधिकारित्वमस्य तत् ॥
> अतो यथा शुद्धतत्त्वसृष्टिस्थित्योर्मलात्यये ।
> योजनानुग्रहे कार्यचतुष्केऽधिकृतो गुरुः ॥
> शिवाभेदेन तत्कुर्यात्तद्वत्पञ्चममप्ययम् ।
> तिरोभावाभिधं कृत्यं तथासौ शिवतात्मकः ॥

अत इति--पञ्चकृत्यकारित्वेन पूर्णाधिकारित्वात् । तदिति-- कार्यचतुष्कम् । एवं कृत्यपञ्चककारित्वेन अस्य किं स्यात्?-- इत्याशङ्क्योक्तम्--तथासौ शिवतात्मकः इति ॥ ६१ ॥
