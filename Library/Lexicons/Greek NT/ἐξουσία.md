# ἐξουσία

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · noun

**Dodson:** power, authority, weight — (a) power, authority, weight, especially: moral authority, influence, (b) in a quasi-personal sense, derived from later Judaism, of a spiritual power, and hence of an earthly power. (Strong's 1849; headword ἐξουσία, ας, ἡ)
