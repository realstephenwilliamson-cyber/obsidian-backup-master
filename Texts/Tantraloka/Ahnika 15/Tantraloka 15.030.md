## Mūla

प्रारब्धदेहभेदे तु भुङ्क्तेऽसावणिमादिकम् ।
भुक्त्वोर्ध्वं याति यत्रैष युक्तोऽथ सकलेऽकले ॥ ^mula

prārabdhadehabhede tu bhuṅkte'sāvaṇimādikam |
bhuktvordhvaṃ yāti yatraiṣa yukto'tha sakale'kale || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 02 - Chapter Fifteen, part 1#^pg45|p. 27]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=45>))
>
> (The disciple) enjoys the (yogic powers) to become small at will and the rest, in the type of body he has assumed (prārabdhadeha), and then once he has enjoyed that, he travels upwards to where (the teacher) conjoins (him), namely, to (a form of Śiva) with parts (sakala), or (to Śiva Himself) without parts (akala).
