## Mūla

श्रीवीरावल्यमर्यादप्रभृतौ शास्त्रसञ्चये ।
स एष परमो यागः स्तुतः शीतांशुमौलिना ॥ ^mula

śrīvīrāvalyamaryādaprabhṛtau śāstrasañcaye |
sa eṣa paramo yāgaḥ stutaḥ śītāṃśumaulinā || ^iast

## Translation

> The Lord Whose Crest Jewel is the Moon has praised this (inner worship) as the supreme sacrifice in a multitude (sañcaya) of scriptures, such as the venerable Vīrāvalī, Amaryāda and others.
