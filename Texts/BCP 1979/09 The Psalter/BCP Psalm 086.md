# Psalm 86

← [[BCP Psalm 085]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 087]] →

*Inclina, Domine* · Book Three · Seventeenth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 086]]

**86:1** Bow down your ear, O LORD, and answer me, \*  
for I am poor and in misery. ^86-1

[p. 710] ^pg710

**86:2** Keep watch over my life, for I am faithful; \*  
save your servant who puts his trust in you. ^86-2

**86:3** Be merciful to me, O LORD, for you are my God; \*  
I call upon you all the day long. ^86-3

**86:4** Gladden the soul of your servant, \*  
for to you, O LORD, I lift up my soul. ^86-4

**86:5** For you, O LORD, are good and forgiving, \*  
and great is your love toward all who call upon you. ^86-5

**86:6** Give ear, O LORD, to my prayer, \*  
and attend to the voice of my supplications. ^86-6

**86:7** In the time of my trouble I will call upon you, \*  
for you will answer me. ^86-7

**86:8** Among the gods there is none like you, O LORD, \*  
nor anything like your works. ^86-8

**86:9** All the nations you have made will come and worship you, O LORD, \*  
and glorify your Name. ^86-9

**86:10** For you are great;  
you do wondrous things; \*  
and you alone are God. ^86-10

**86:11** Teach me your way, O LORD,  
and I will walk in your truth; \*  
knit my heart to you that I may fear your Name. ^86-11

**86:12** I will thank you, O LORD my God, with all my heart, \*  
and glorify your Name for evermore. ^86-12

**86:13** For great is your love toward me; \*  
you have delivered me from the nethermost Pit. ^86-13

[p. 711] ^pg711

**86:14** The arrogant rise up against me, O God,  
and a band of violent men seeks my life; \*  
they have not set you before their eyes. ^86-14

**86:15** But you, O LORD, are gracious and full of compassion, \*  
slow to anger, and full of kindness and truth. ^86-15

**86:16** Turn to me and have mercy upon me; \*  
give your strength to your servant;  
and save the child of your handmaid. ^86-16

**86:17** Show me a sign of your favor,  
so that those who hate me may see it and be ashamed; \*  
because you, O LORD, have helped me and comforted me. ^86-17
