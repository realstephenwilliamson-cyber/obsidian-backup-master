## Mūla

श्रीपूर्वशास्त्रे तेनोक्तं यावत्तेनैव नोद्धृतः ।
अत्र ह्यर्थोऽयमेतावत्पूर्वोक्तज्ञानवृंहितः ॥ ^mula

śrīpūrvaśāstre tenoktaṃ yāvattenaiva noddhṛtaḥ |
atra hyartho'yametāvatpūrvoktajñānavṛṃhitaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 14 - Chapter Twenty-Three#^pg348|p. 328]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=348>))
>
> Thus, it is said in the Mālinīvijayottara (Śrīpūrvaśāstra) (with regard to this, that one who has been made to fall by his teacher cannot be elevated again) until he has been rescued (from his fallen state) by that same (teacher). (with 23.73ab)
>
> The sense here is as follows. The word ‘that’ signifies that the teacher is just the same (tāvat) one (as the first), who is nurtured by the knowledge previously imparted. (with 23.73cd–74ab)
