# LXX Psalms 145

← [[LXX Psalms 144]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 146]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 146]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 146]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**145:1** Alleluia, *a Psalm* of Aggæus and Zacharias. My soul, praise the Lord.  ^145-1

**145:2** While I live will I praise the Lord: I will sing praises to my God as long as I exist.  ^145-2

**145:3** Trust not in princes, nor in the children of men, in whom there is no safety.  ^145-3

**145:4** His breath shall go forth, and he shall return to his earth; in that day all his thoughts shall perish.  ^145-4

**145:5** Blessed is he whose helper is the God of Jacob, whose hope is in the Lord his God:  ^145-5

**145:6** who made heaven, and earth, the sea, and all things in them: who keeps truth for ever:  ^145-6

**145:7** who executes judgment for the wronged: who gives food to the hungry. The Lord looses the fettered ones:  ^145-7

**145:8** the Lord gives wisdom to the blind: The Lord sets up the broken down: the Lord loves the righteous: the Lord preserves the strangers;  ^145-8

**145:9** he will relieve the orphan and widow: but will utterly remove the way of sinners.  ^145-9

**145:10** The Lord shall reign for ever, *even* thy God, O Sion, to all generations.  ^145-10
