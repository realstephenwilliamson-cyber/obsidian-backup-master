# ἱλαστήριον

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · adjective

**Dodson:** a sin offering, covering — (a) a sin offering, by which the wrath of the deity shall be appeased; a means of propitiation, (b) the covering of the ark, which was sprinkled with the atoning blood on the Day of Atonement. (Strong's 2435; headword ἱλαστήριον, ου, τό)
