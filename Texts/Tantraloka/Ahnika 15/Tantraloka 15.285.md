## Mūla

देहे किमवशिष्येत शिवानन्दरसादृते ।
शिवानन्दरसापूर्णं षट्त्रिंशत्तत्त्वनिर्भरम् ॥ ^mula

dehe kimavaśiṣyeta śivānandarasādṛte |
śivānandarasāpūrṇaṃ ṣaṭtriṃśattattvanirbharam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 06 - Chapter Fifteen, part 5#^pg188|p. 170]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=188>))
>
> When the limited (conditioned) state has ceased, and (all) propensity towards (outer sensory) objects fall away, what remains in the body, apart from the juice (of the aesthetic savour) (rasa) of Śiva’s bliss? (with 15.284cd–285ab)
>
> Viewing and worshipping the body night and day as full of the juice (of the aesthetic savour of) Śiva’s bliss (śivānandarasa) and the thirty-six principles, (the adept) becomes Śiva. (with 15.285cd–286ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* अतश्च एवमभ्याससहितः साधकः शिव एव भवेत्--इत्याह--

> देहं दिवानिशं पश्यन्नर्चयन्स्याच्छिवात्मकः ।
