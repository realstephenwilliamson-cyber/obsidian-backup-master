# Psalm 66 (NRSVue)

## Praise for God’s goodness to Israel

*To the leader. A Song. A Psalm.*

**66:1** Make a joyful noise to God, all the earth; ^66-1

**66:2** sing the glory of his name; give to him glorious praise. ^66-2

**66:3** Say to God, “How awesome are your deeds! Because of your great power, your enemies cringe before you. ^66-3

**66:4** All the earth worships you; they sing praises to you, sing praises to your name.” *Selah* ^66-4

**66:5** Come and see what God has done: he is awesome in his deeds among mortals. ^66-5

**66:6** He turned the sea into dry land; they passed through the river on foot. There we rejoiced in him, ^66-6

**66:7** who rules by his might forever, whose eyes keep watch on the nations—let the rebellious not exalt themselves. *Selah* ^66-7

**66:8** Bless our God, O peoples; let the sound of his praise be heard, ^66-8

**66:9** who has kept us among the living and has not let our feet slip. ^66-9

**66:10** For you, O God, have tested us; you have tried us as silver is tried. ^66-10

**66:11** You brought us into the net; you laid burdens on our backs; ^66-11

**66:12** you let people ride over our heads; we went through fire and through water; yet you have brought us out to a spacious place.^[Cn Compare Gk Syr Jerome Tg: Heb *to a saturation*] ^66-12

**66:13** I will come into your house with burnt offerings; I will pay you my vows, ^66-13

**66:14** those that my lips uttered and my mouth promised when I was in trouble. ^66-14

**66:15** I will offer to you burnt offerings of fatted calves, with the smoke of the sacrifice of rams; I will make an offering of bulls and goats. *Selah* ^66-15

**66:16** Come and hear, all you who fear God, and I will tell what he has done for me. ^66-16

**66:17** I cried aloud to him, and he was extolled with my tongue. ^66-17

**66:18** If I had cherished iniquity in my heart, the Lord would not have listened. ^66-18

**66:19** But truly God has listened; he has heard the words of my prayer. ^66-19

**66:20** Blessed be God, who has not rejected my prayer or removed his steadfast love from me. ^66-20
