## Chapter 3

**3:1** A psalm of David when he fled from his son Absalom. ^3-1

**3:2** O LORD, my foes are so many!  
Many are those who attack me; ^3-2

**3:3** many say of me,  
“There is no deliverance for him through God.” *Selah*.[^1] ^3-3

**3:4** But You, O LORD, are a shield about me,  
my glory, He who holds my head high. ^3-4

**3:5** I cry aloud to the LORD,  
and He answers me from His holy mountain. *Selah*. ^3-5

**3:6** I lie down and sleep and wake again,  
for the LORD sustains me. ^3-6

**3:7** I have no fear of the myriad forces  
arrayed against me on every side. ^3-7

**3:8** Rise, O LORD!  
Deliver me, O my God!  
For You slap all my enemies in the face;[^2]  
You break the teeth of the wicked. ^3-8

**3:9** Deliverance is the LORD’s;  
Your blessing be upon Your people! *Selah*. ^3-9

[^1]: A liturgical direction of uncertain meaning.
[^2]: Lit. “cheek.”
