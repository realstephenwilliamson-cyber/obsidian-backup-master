# Psalm 93

← [[BCP Psalm 092]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 094]] →

*Dominus regnavit* · Book Four · Eighteenth Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 093]]

**93:1** The LORD is King;  
he has put on splendid apparel; \*  
the LORD has put on his apparel  
and girded himself with strength. ^93-1

**93:2** He has made the whole world so sure \*  
that it cannot be moved; ^93-2

**93:3** Ever since the world began, your throne has been established; \*  
you are from everlasting. ^93-3

**93:4** The waters have lifted up, O LORD,  
the waters have lifted up their voice; \*  
the waters have lifted up their pounding waves. ^93-4

**93:5** Mightier than the sound of many waters,  
mightier than the breakers of the sea, \*  
mightier is the LORD who dwells on high. ^93-5

**93:6** Your testimonies are very sure, \*  
and holiness adorns your house, O LORD,  
for ever and for evermore. ^93-6
