# Psalm 143 (NRSVue)

## Prayer for deliverance from enemies

*A Psalm of David.*

**143:1** Hear my prayer, O LORD; give ear to my supplications in your faithfulness; answer me in your righteousness. ^143-1

**143:2** Do not enter into judgment with your servant, for no one living is righteous before you. ^143-2

**143:3** For the enemy has pursued me, crushing my life to the ground, making me sit in darkness like those long dead. ^143-3

**143:4** Therefore my spirit faints within me; my heart within me is appalled. ^143-4

**143:5** I remember the days of old; I think about all your deeds; I meditate on the works of your hands. ^143-5

**143:6** I stretch out my hands to you; my soul thirsts for you like a parched land. *Selah* ^143-6

**143:7** Answer me quickly, O LORD; my spirit fails. Do not hide your face from me, or I shall be like those who go down to the Pit. ^143-7

**143:8** Let me hear of your steadfast love in the morning, for in you I put my trust. Teach me the way I should go, for to you I lift up my soul. ^143-8

**143:9** Save me, O LORD, from my enemies; I have fled to you for refuge.^[Heb ms Gk: MT *to you I have hidden*] ^143-9

**143:10** Teach me to do your will, for you are my God. Let your good spirit lead me on a level path. ^143-10

**143:11** For your name’s sake, O LORD, preserve my life. In your righteousness bring me out of trouble. ^143-11

**143:12** In your steadfast love cut off my enemies, and destroy all my adversaries, for I am your servant. ^143-12
