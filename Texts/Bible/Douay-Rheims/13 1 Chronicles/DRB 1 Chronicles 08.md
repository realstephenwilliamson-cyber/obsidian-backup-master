# 1 Chronicles 8 (Douay-Rheims)

*1 Paralipomenon 8 in the Douay-Rheims*

[[DRB 1 Chronicles 07|← 1 Chronicles 7]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB 1 Chronicles 09|1 Chronicles 9 →]]

**8:1** Now Benjamin beget Bale his firstborn, Asbel the second, Ahara the third, ^8-1

**8:2** Nohaa the fourth, and Rapha the fifth. ^8-2

**8:3** And the sons of Bale were Addar, and Gera, and Abiud, ^8-3

**8:4** And Abisue, and Naamar, and Ahoe, ^8-4

**8:5** And Gera, and Sephuphan, and Huram. ^8-5

**8:6** These are the sons of Ahod, heads of families that dwelt in Gabaa, who were removed into Mrtnahsth. ^8-6

**8:7** And Naaman, and Achia, and Gera he removed them, and beget Oza, and Ahiud. ^8-7

**8:8** And Saharim begot in the land of Moab, after he sent away Husim and Bara his wives. ^8-8

**8:9** And he beget of Hodes his wife Jobab, and Sebia, and Mesa, and Molchom, ^8-9

**8:10** And Jehus and Sechia, and Marma. These were his sons heads of their families. ^8-10

**8:11** And Mehusim beget Abitob, and Elphaal. ^8-11

**8:12** And the sons of Elphaal were Heber, and Misaam, and Samad: who built One, and Led, and its daughters. ^8-12

**8:13** And Baria, and Sama were heads of their kindreds that dwelt in Aialon: these drove away the inhabitants of Geth. ^8-13

**8:14** And Ahio, and Sesac, and Jerimoth, ^8-14

**8:15** And Zabadia, and Arod, and Heder, ^8-15

**8:16** And Michael, and Jespha, and Joha, the sons of Baria. ^8-16

**8:17** And Zabadia, and Mosollam, and Hezeci, and Heber, ^8-17

**8:18** And Jesamari, and Jezlia, and Jobab, sons of Elphaal, ^8-18

**8:19** And Jacim, and Zechri, and Zabdi, ^8-19

**8:20** And Elioenai, and Selethai, and Elial, ^8-20

**8:21** And Adaia, and Baraia, and Samareth, the sons of Semei. ^8-21

**8:22** And Jespham, and Heber, and Eliel, ^8-22

**8:23** And Abdon, and Zechri, and Hanan, ^8-23

**8:24** And Hanania, and Elam, and Anathothia. ^8-24

**8:25** And Jephdaia, and Phanuel the sons of Sesac. ^8-25

**8:26** And Samsari, and Sohoria and Otholia, ^8-26

**8:27** And Jersia, and Elia, and Zechri, the sons of Jeroham. ^8-27

**8:28** These were the chief fathers, and heads of their families who dwelt in Jerusalem. ^8-28

**8:29** And at Gabaon dwelt Abigabaon, and the name of his wife was Maacha: ^8-29

**8:30** And his firstborn son Abdon, and Sur, and Cia, and Baal, and Nadab, ^8-30

**8:31** And Gedor, and Ahio, and Zacher, and Macelloth: ^8-31

**8:32** And Macelloth beget Samaa: and they dwelt over against their brethren in Jerusalem with their brethren. ^8-32

**8:33** And Ner beget Cia, and Cia beget Saul. And Saul begot Jonathan and Melchisua, and Abinadab, and Esbaal. ^8-33

**8:34** And the son of Jonathan was Meribbaal: and Meribbaal begot Micha. ^8-34

**8:35** And the sons of Micha were Phithon, and Melech, and Tharaa, and Ahaz. ^8-35

**8:36** And Ahaz beget Joada: and Joada beget Alamath, and Azmoth, and Zamri: and Zamri beget Mesa, ^8-36

**8:37** And Mesa beget Banaa, whose son was Rapha, of whom was born Elasa, who beget Asel. ^8-37

**8:38** And Asel had six sons whose names were Ezricam, Bochru, Ismahel, Saria, Obdia, and Hanan. All these were the sons of Asel. ^8-38

**8:39** And the sons of Esec, his brother, were Ulam the firstborn, and Jehus the second, and Eliphelet the third. ^8-39

**8:40** And the sons of Ulam were most valiant men, and archers of great strength: and they had many sons and grandsons, even to a hundred and fifty. All these were children of Benjamin. ^8-40
