## Mūla

शिवान्तं सितपद्मान्ते त्रिशूलानां त्रये क्रमात् ।
मध्यशूले मध्यगः स्यात्सद्भावः परया सह ॥ ^mula

śivāntaṃ sitapadmānte triśūlānāṃ traye kramāt |
madhyaśūle madhyagaḥ syātsadbhāvaḥ parayā saha || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 05 - Chapter Sixteen, part 1#^pg24|p. 4]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=24>))
>
> Then, having received the authorization (ājñā) to do so, the object of worship, that is, the power of the base (ādhāraśakti) and so forth, is worshipped appropriately (alam) with (offerings of) flowers, incense and the rest, beginning from the root of the Trident and ending with Śiva, at the extremity of the white lotuses. (This is repeated) in due order for (all) three tridents. (with 16.11–12ab)
>
> Bhairavasadbhāva, together with Parā, is in middle of the middle trident. Navātmā, together with Aparā, is on the left (prong), and the other (couple) is on the right one. Ratiśekhara, along with Parāparā, is on the middle prong of the right trident. Bhairavasadbhāva, along with Parā, is on the right prong, (and Navātmā with Aparā on the left prong). Navātmā, along with Aparā, is on the middle prong of the left trident, and Sadbhairava with Parā is on the other left prong (and Ratiśekhara together with Parāparā is on the right prong). (with 16.12cd–15ab)
