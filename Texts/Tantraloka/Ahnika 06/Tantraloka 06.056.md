## Mūla

वामा संसारिणामीशा प्रभुशक्तिर्विधायिनी ।
ज्येष्ठा तु सुप्रबुद्धानां बुभुत्सूनां च रौद्रिका ॥ ^mula

vāmā saṃsāriṇāmīśā prabhuśaktirvidhāyinī |
jyeṣṭhā tu suprabuddhānāṃ bubhutsūnāṃ ca raudrikā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 09 - Chapter Six, part 2#^pg282|p. 267]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=282>))
>
> Vāmā is the (aspect of the) Lord’s power (prabhuśakti) who is the (sovereign) Goddess (īśā) of (fettered souls) subject to transmigration. Jyeṣṭhā is the ruler (vidhāyinī) of the well-awakened, and Raudrikā (is the goddess) of those who aspire to worldly benefits.
