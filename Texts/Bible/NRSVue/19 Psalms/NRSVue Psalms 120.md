## Prayer for deliverance from slanderers

*A Song of Ascents.*

**120:1** In my distress I cry to the LORD, that he may answer me: ^120-1

**120:2** “Deliver me, O LORD, from lying lips, from a deceitful tongue.” ^120-2

**120:3** What shall be given to you? And what more shall be done to you, you deceitful tongue? ^120-3

**120:4** A warrior’s sharp arrows, with glowing coals of the broom tree! ^120-4

**120:5** Woe is me, that I am an alien in Meshech, that I must live among the tents of Kedar. ^120-5

**120:6** Too long have I had my dwelling among those who hate peace. ^120-6

**120:7** I am for peace, but when I speak, they are for war. ^120-7
