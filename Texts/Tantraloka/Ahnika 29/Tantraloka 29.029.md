## Mūla

सिद्धचक्रं दिक्चतुष्के गणेशाधस्तनान्तकम् ।
खगेन्द्रः सहविज्जाम्ब इल्लाई अम्बया सह ॥ ^mula

siddhacakraṃ dikcatuṣke gaṇeśādhastanāntakam |
khagendraḥ sahavijjāmba illāī ambayā saha || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 09 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 1#^pg247|p. 230]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=247>))
>
> In the north, commencing with the northeast corner and ending in the northwest, he should worship Ganṇeśa, Vaṭuka, the three teachers, the Siddhas, Yoginīs, and the sacred seat (pīṭha). In the eastern quarter, starting from below Gaṇeśa, he should worship the assembly of Siddhas (siddhacakra) in the four quarters, ending (where he began,) in the place below Gaṇeśa. (with 29.27cd–29ab)
>
> Khagendranātha together with Vijjambā, Vaktaṣti with Ilāiambā, Vimala with Anantamekhalāmbā, are in the east. Kāūrmanātha with (his) consort (śakti) Maṅgalā, Jaitra with IHlāīambā, Avijita with Ānandamekhalā, are in the south. Meṣanātha with Kāmamaṁṁgalā, Vindhya and Ajita with Kullāīambā and Ajaramekhalā, respectively, are in the west. Macchandanātha and Kuṁñkuṇāmbā and the six pairs (of princes and their consorts) with authority (to give initiation) are in the north (of the Mandala). Such is the second line that extends from the northwest to the northeast. (with 29.29cd–33ab)
