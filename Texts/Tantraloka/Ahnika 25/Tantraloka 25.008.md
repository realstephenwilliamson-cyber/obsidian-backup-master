## Mūla

एकेनैव विधानेन यद्यपि स्यात्कृतार्थता ।
तथापि तन्मयीभावसिद्ध्यै सर्वं विधिं चरेत् ॥ ^mula

ekenaiva vidhānena yadyapi syātkṛtārthatā |
tathāpi tanmayībhāvasiddhyai sarvaṃ vidhiṃ caret || ^iast

## Translation

> Although the purpose is achieved by just one of (these) procedures, even so, in order to achieve a state of oneness, (the teacher) should apply all the procedures.
