# Isaiah 52

Compare: [[NRSVue Isaiah 52|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 52

**52:1** Awake, awake, O Zion!  
Clothe yourself in splendor;  
Put on your robes of majesty,  
Jerusalem, holy city!  
For the uncircumcised and the unclean  
Shall never enter you again. ^52-1

**52:2** Arise, shake off the dust,  
Sit [on your throne], Jerusalem!  
Loose the bonds from your neck,  
O captive one, Fair Zion! ^52-2

**52:3** For thus said the LORD:  
You were sold for no price,  
And shall be redeemed without money. ^52-3

**52:4** For thus said the Lord GOD:  
Of old, My people went down  
To Egypt to sojourn there;  
But Assyria has robbed them,  
Giving nothing in return.[^1] ^52-4

**52:5** What therefore do I gain here?  
—declares the LORD—  
For My people has been carried off for nothing,  
Their mockers howl  
—declares the LORD—  
And constantly, unceasingly,  
My name is reviled. ^52-5

**52:6** Assuredly, My people shall learn My name,  
Assuredly [they shall learn] on that day  
That I, the One who promised,  
Am now at hand. ^52-6

**52:7** How welcome on the mountain  
Are the footsteps of the herald  
Announcing happiness,  
Heralding good fortune,  
Announcing victory,  
Telling Zion, “Your God is King!” ^52-7

**52:8** Hark!  
Your watchmen raise their voices,  
As one they shout for joy;  
For every eye shall behold  
The LORD’s return to Zion. ^52-8

**52:9** Raise a shout together,  
O ruins of Jerusalem!  
For the LORD will comfort His people,  
Will redeem Jerusalem. ^52-9

**52:10** The LORD will bare His holy arm  
In the sight of all the nations,  
And the very ends of earth shall see  
The victory of our God. ^52-10

**52:11** Turn, turn away, touch naught unclean  
As you depart from there;  
Keep pure, as you go forth from there,  
You who bear the vessels of the LORD![^2] ^52-11

**52:12** For you will not depart in haste,  
Nor will you leave in flight;  
For the LORD is marching before you,  
The God of Israel is your rear guard. ^52-12

**52:13** “Indeed, My servant shall prosper,  
Be exalted and raised to great heights. ^52-13

**52:14** Just as the many were appalled at him[^3]—  
So marred was his appearance, unlike that of man,  
His form, beyond human semblance— ^52-14

**52:15** Just so he shall startle[^4] many nations.  
Kings shall be silenced because of him,  
For they shall see what has not been told them,  
Shall behold what they never have heard.” ^52-15

[^1]: Whereas the Israelites themselves sought hospitality in Egypt, Assyria (i.e., the Chaldean Empire) has exiled them by force.
[^2]: Cf. Ezra 1.7–8; 5.14–15.
[^3]: Heb. “you.”
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
