## Mūla

श्रीशौरिसंज्ञतनयः किल कर्णनामा
यो यौवने विदितशांभवतत्त्वसारः ।
देहं त्यजन् प्रथयति स्म जनस्य सत्यं
योगच्युतं प्रति महामुनिकृष्णवाक्यम् ॥ ^mula

śrīśaurisaṃjñatanayaḥ kila karṇanāmā
yo yauvane viditaśāṃbhavatattvasāraḥ |
dehaṃ tyajan prathayati sma janasya satyaṃ
yogacyutaṃ prati mahāmunikṛṣṇavākyam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 11 - Chapter Thirty-Seven, part 1#^pg275|p. 257]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=275>))
>
> (Amongst them) was a (young) man called Karna, the son of the venerable Śauri, who in his youth had (already) come to know the essence of Śiva’s true nature (śāmbhavatattva). Leaving the body (when he was still young), he demonstrated the truth of the words of Krṣna the great ascetic, about those fallen from (the practice) of Yoga.
