# Proverbs 22 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Proverbs 21|← Proverbs 21]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Proverbs 23|Proverbs 23 →]]

**22:1** A good name is to be chosen rather than great riches, and favor is better than silver or gold. ^22-1

**22:2** The rich and the poor have this in common: the LORD is the maker of them all. ^22-2

**22:3** The clever see danger and hide, but the simple go on and suffer for it. ^22-3

**22:4** The reward for humility and fear of the LORD is riches and honor and life. ^22-4

**22:5** Thorns and snares are in the way of the perverse; the cautious will keep far from them. ^22-5

**22:6** Train children in the right way, and when old, they will not stray. ^22-6

**22:7** The rich rule over the poor, and the borrower is the slave of the lender. ^22-7

**22:8** Whoever sows injustice will reap calamity, and the rod of anger will fail. ^22-8

**22:9** Those who are generous are blessed, for they share their bread with the poor. ^22-9

**22:10** Drive out a scoffer, and strife goes out; quarreling and abuse will cease. ^22-10

**22:11** Those who love a pure heart and are gracious in speech will have the king as a friend. ^22-11

**22:12** The eyes of the LORD keep watch over knowledge, but he overthrows the words of the faithless. ^22-12

**22:13** The lazy person says, “There is a lion outside! I shall be killed in the streets!” ^22-13

**22:14** The mouth of a loose woman^[Heb *strange woman*] is a deep pit; he with whom the LORD is angry falls into it. ^22-14

**22:15** Folly is bound up in the heart of a child, but the rod of discipline drives it far away. ^22-15

**22:16** Oppressing the poor in order to enrich oneself, and giving to the rich, will lead only to loss. ^22-16

## Sayings of the wise

**22:17** The words of the wise: Incline your ear and hear my words^[Cn Compare Gk: Heb *Incline your ear, and hear the words of the wise*] and apply your mind to my teaching, ^22-17

**22:18** for it will be pleasant if you keep them within you, if all of them are ready on your lips. ^22-18

**22:19** So that your trust may be in the LORD, I have made them known to you today, yes, to you. ^22-19

**22:20** Have I not written for you thirty^[Cn: Heb *in the past* or *noble*] sayings of admonition and knowledge, ^22-20

**22:21** to show you what is right and true, so that you may give a true answer to those who sent you? ^22-21

**22:22** Do not rob the poor because they are poor or crush the afflicted at the gate, ^22-22

**22:23** for the LORD pleads their cause and despoils of life those who despoil them. ^22-23

**22:24** Make no friends with those given to anger, and do not associate with hotheads, ^22-24

**22:25** lest you learn their ways and entangle yourself in a snare. ^22-25

**22:26** Do not be one of those who give pledges, who become surety for debts. ^22-26

**22:27** If you have nothing with which to pay, why should your bed be taken from under you? ^22-27

**22:28** Do not remove the ancient landmark that your ancestors set up. ^22-28

**22:29** Do you see those who are skillful in their work? They will serve kings; they will not serve common people. ^22-29
