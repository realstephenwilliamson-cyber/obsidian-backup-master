# Job 12 (NRSVue)

## Job replies: I am a laughingstock

**12:1** Then Job answered: ^12-1

**12:2** “No doubt you are the people, and wisdom will die with you. ^12-2

**12:3** But I have understanding as well as you; I am not inferior to you. Who does not know such things as these? ^12-3

**12:4** I am a laughingstock to my friends; I, who called upon God and he answered me, a just and blameless man, I am a laughingstock. ^12-4

**12:5** Those at ease have contempt for misfortune,^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] but it is ready for those whose feet are unstable. ^12-5

**12:6** The tents of robbers are at peace, and those who provoke God are secure, who bring their god in their hands.^[Or *whom God brought forth by his hand*; meaning of Heb uncertain] ^12-6

**12:7** “But ask the animals, and they will teach you, the birds of the air, and they will tell you; ^12-7

**12:8** ask the plants of the earth,^[Or *speak to the earth*] and they will teach you, and the fish of the sea will declare to you. ^12-8

**12:9** Who among all these does not know that the hand of the LORD has done this? ^12-9

**12:10** In his hand is the life of every living thing and the breath of every human being. ^12-10

**12:11** Does not the ear test words as the palate tastes food? ^12-11

**12:12** Is wisdom with the aged and understanding in length of days? ^12-12

**12:13** “With God^[Heb *him*] are wisdom and strength; he has counsel and understanding. ^12-13

**12:14** If he tears down, no one can rebuild; if he shuts someone in, no one can open up. ^12-14

**12:15** If he withholds the waters, they dry up; if he sends them out, they overwhelm the land. ^12-15

**12:16** With him are strength and wisdom; the deceived and the deceiver are his. ^12-16

**12:17** He leads counselors away stripped and makes fools of judges. ^12-17

**12:18** He looses the sash of kings and binds a waistcloth on their loins. ^12-18

**12:19** He leads priests away stripped and overthrows the mighty. ^12-19

**12:20** He deprives of speech those who are trusted and takes away the discernment of the elders. ^12-20

**12:21** He pours contempt on princes and looses the belt of the strong. ^12-21

**12:22** He uncovers deep things from the darkness and brings deep darkness to light. ^12-22

**12:23** He makes nations great, then destroys them; he enlarges nations, then leads them away. ^12-23

**12:24** He strips understanding from the leaders^[Heb adds *of the people*] of the earth and makes them wander in a pathless waste. ^12-24

**12:25** They grope in the dark without light; he makes them stagger like a drunkard. ^12-25
