## Mūla

पद्माकारौ करौ कृत्वा पद्ममुद्रां प्रदर्शयेत् ।
संमुखौ प्रसृतौ कृत्वा करानन्तरिताङ्गुली ॥ ^mula

padmākārau karau kṛtvā padmamudrāṃ pradarśayet |
saṃmukhau prasṛtau kṛtvā karānantaritāṅgulī || ^iast

*Dyczkowski prints no Sanskrit for this chapter; the text is that of the KSTS edition (Muktabodha M00160).*

> **2. Padma – the Lotus Gesture**
>
> Having made the two hands into the form of a (blooming) lotus, display the gesture of the lotus. (with 7.6ab–6ab)

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, July 2024 – September 2024

>
> **3. Śakti (Power)**
>
> Extending the hands first in front of each other and withdrawing the fingers inwardly, extending the middle fingers touching (each other) is considered to be (the gesture of) Power. (with 7.6cd–7ab)
