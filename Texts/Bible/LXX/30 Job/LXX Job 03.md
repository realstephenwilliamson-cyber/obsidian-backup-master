# LXX Job 03

← [[LXX Job 02]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Job 04]] →

*Job · [[LXX]]*

**3:1** After this Job opened his mouth, and cursed his day,  ^3-1

**3:2** saying,  ^3-2

**3:3** Let the day perish in which I was born, and that night in which they said, Behold a man-child!  ^3-3

**3:4** Let that night be darkness, and let not the Lord regard it from above, neither let light come upon it.  ^3-4

**3:5** But let darkness and the shadow of death seize it; let blackness come upon it;  ^3-5

**3:6** let that day and night be cursed, let darkness carry them away; let it not come into the days of the year, neither let it be numbered with the days of the months.  ^3-6

**3:7** But let that night be pain, and let not mirth come upon it, nor joy.  ^3-7

**3:8** But let him that curses that day curse it, *even* he that is ready to attack the great whale.  ^3-8

**3:9** Let the stars of that night be darkened; let it remain *dark*, and not come into light; and let it not see the morning star arise:  ^3-9

**3:10** because it shut not up the gates of my mother's womb, for *so* it would have removed sorrow from my eyes.  ^3-10

**3:11** For why died I not in the belly? and *why* did I not come forth from the womb and die immediately?  ^3-11

**3:12** And why did the knees support me? and why did I suck the breasts?  ^3-12

**3:13** Now I should have lain down and been quiet, I should have slept and been at rest,  ^3-13

**3:14** with kings *and* councillors of the earth, who gloried in *their* swords;  ^3-14

**3:15** or with rulers, whose gold was abundant, who filled their houses with silver:  ^3-15

**3:16** or *I should have been* as an untimely birth proceeding from his mother's womb, or as infants who never saw light.  ^3-16

**3:17** There the ungodly have burnt out the fury of rage; there the wearied in body rest.  ^3-17

**3:18** And the men of old time have together ceased to hear the exactor's voice.  ^3-18

**3:19** The small and great are there, and the servant that feared his lord.  ^3-19

**3:20** For why is light given to those who are in bitterness, and life to the souls which are in griefs?  ^3-20

**3:21** who desire death, and obtain it not, digging *for it* as *for* treasures;  ^3-21

**3:22** and would be very joyful if they should gain it?  ^3-22

**3:23** Death *is* rest to *such* a man, for God has hedged him in.  ^3-23

**3:24** For my groaning comes before my food, and I weep being beset with terror.  ^3-24

**3:25** For the terror of which I meditated has come upon me, and that which I had feared has befallen me.  ^3-25

**3:26** I was not at peace, nor quiet, nor had I rest; yet wrath came upon me.  ^3-26
