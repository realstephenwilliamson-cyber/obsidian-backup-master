### XXIII. The Fire that deals with the Luminaries of Heaven.

**23:1** From thence I went to another place to the west of the ends of the earth. ^23-1

**23:2** And I saw a 〚burning〛 fire which ran without resting, and paused not from its course day or night but (ran) regularly. ^23-2

**23:3** And I asked saying: ‘What is this which rests not?’ ^23-3

**23:4** Then Raguel, one of the holy angels who was with me, answered me 〚and said unto me〛: ‘This course ⌜of fire⌝ 〚which thou hast seen〛 is the fire in the west which †persecutes† all the luminaries of heaven.’ ^23-4
