## Mūla

अर्थे ज्ञाता यदा यो यस्तद्वेद्यं वपुरुच्यताम् ।
तत्तद्विज्ञातृवेद्यत्वं सर्वान्प्रत्येव भासताम् ॥ ^mula

arthe jñātā yadā yo yastadvedyaṃ vapurucyatām |
tattadvijñātṛvedyatvaṃ sarvānpratyeva bhāsatām || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 11 - Chapter Ten, part 2#^pg357|p. 338]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=357>))
>
> Unlike you, (we do not maintain) here (in our view) that the subject is fixed (and determined) (niyata) in the course of cognition. You should say that the perceptible objective nature of an entity (vedyaṁ vapus) (is always the same,) whoever the perceiver may be or whenever (he may be present). (with 10.67cd–68ab)
>
> He who insists that the perceptibility (of a particular object apprehended) by the particular subject (who perceives it) should be manifest to all has, I believe, reached the limit of deafness. That perceptibility (as we understand it to be) (vedyatā) does not manifest in this way to any other (subject). (with 10.68cd–69)
