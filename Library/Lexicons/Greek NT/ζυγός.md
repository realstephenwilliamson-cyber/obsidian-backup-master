# ζυγός

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · noun

**Dodson:** a yoke, heavy burden, pair of scales — a yoke; hence met: (a Jewish idea) of a heavy burden, comparable to the heavy yokes resting on the bullocks' necks; a balance, pair of scales. (Strong's 2218; headword ζυγός, οῦ, ὁ)
