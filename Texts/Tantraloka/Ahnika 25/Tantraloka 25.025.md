## Mūla

मुक्तिर्विवेकात्तत्त्वानां दीक्षातो योगतो यदि ।
चर्यामात्रात्कथं सा स्यादित्यतः सममुत्तरम् ॥ ^mula

muktirvivekāttattvānāṃ dīkṣāto yogato yadi |
caryāmātrātkathaṃ sā syādityataḥ samamuttaram || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 17 - Chapter Twenty-Five (continued) and Chapter Twenty-Six#^pg386|p. 366]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=386>))
>
> “If liberation (is attained) by discriminating (viveka) between the metaphysical principles (tattva), initiation or Yoga, how can it (be attained) by (observance and right) conduct (caryā) alone?” Then, the all-pervasive Lord, smiling, uttered the corresponding answer: “How has (this) your delusion (arisen)? It is well established (and recognised) that the Supreme Lord graces everybody. See (for yourself)!” (with 25.25–26)
