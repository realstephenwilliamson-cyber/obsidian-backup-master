## Mūla

तदेषा धारणाध्यानसमाधित्रितयी पराम् ।
संविदं प्रति नो कञ्चिदुपयोगं समश्नुते ॥ ^mula

tadeṣā dhāraṇādhyānasamādhitritayī parām |
saṃvidaṃ prati no kañcidupayogaṃ samaśnute || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. III
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 03 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v03 05 - Chapter Four, part 4#^pg129|p. 116]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 03.pdf#page=129>))

> (Thus,) these three (ancillaries of Yoga), namely, fixation, meditation and contemplative absorption, are of no use at all in attaining supreme consciousness.
