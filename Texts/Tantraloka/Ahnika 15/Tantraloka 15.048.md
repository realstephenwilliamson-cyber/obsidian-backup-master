## Mūla

तत्रेष्टमन्त्रहृदयो गोरजोऽन्तः पदत्रयम् ।
गत्वागत्य भजेत्स्नानं पार्थिवं धृतिदायकम् ॥ ^mula

tatreṣṭamantrahṛdayo gorajo'ntaḥ padatrayam |
gatvāgatya bhajetsnānaṃ pārthivaṃ dhṛtidāyakam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 02 - Chapter Fifteen, part 1#^pg58|p. 40]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=58>))
>
> There (in this context), one who has taken to heart (and is fully absorbed) in (his) chosen Mantra, and takes three steps in and out of (a heap of) cow dung, (performs) the ablution of Earth, that bestows stability (dhṛti).
