# Psalms 110 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 109|← Psalms 109]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 111|Psalms 111 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 110|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**110:1** I will praise thee, O Lord, with my whole heart; in the council of the just: and in the congregation. ^110-1

**110:2** Great are the works of the Lord: sought out according to all his wills. ^110-2

**110:3** His work is praise and magnificence: and his justice continueth for ever and ever. ^110-3

**110:4** He hath made a remembrance of his wonderful works, being a merciful and gracious Lord: ^110-4

**110:5** He hath given food to them that fear him. He will be mindful for ever of his covenant: ^110-5

**110:6** He will shew forth to his people the power of his works. ^110-6

**110:7** That he may give them the inheritance of the Gentiles: the works of his hands are truth and judgment. ^110-7

**110:8** All his commandments are faithful: confirmed for ever and ever, made in truth and equity. ^110-8

**110:9** He hath sent redemption to his people: he hath commanded his covenant for ever. Holy and terrible is his name: ^110-9

**110:10** The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom. A good understanding to all that do it: his praise continueth for ever and ever. ^110-10
