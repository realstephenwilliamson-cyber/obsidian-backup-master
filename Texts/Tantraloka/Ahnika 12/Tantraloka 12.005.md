## Mūla

सर्वं सर्वत्र रूपं च तस्यापि न न भासते ।
नह्यवच्छेदितां क्वापि स्वप्नेऽपि विषहामहे ॥ ^mula

sarvaṃ sarvatra rūpaṃ ca tasyāpi na na bhāsate |
nahyavaccheditāṃ kvāpi svapne'pi viṣahāmahe || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 04 - Chapter Twelve#^pg122|p. 102]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=122>))
>
> (The nature of the Path is such that) its entire form (sarvan rūpam) manifests everywhere (in every part of it, so that each particular aspect contains within itself everything else).ć We do not tolerate the existence of limitation even when (we) dream.
