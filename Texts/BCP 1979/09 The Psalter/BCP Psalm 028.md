# Psalm 28

← [[BCP Psalm 027]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 029]] →

*Ad te, Domine* · Book One · Fifth Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 028]]

**28:1** O LORD, I call to you;  
my Rock, do not be deaf to my cry; \*  
lest, if you do not hear me,  
I become like those who go down to the Pit. ^28-1

**28:2** Hear the voice of my prayer when I cry out to you, \*  
when I lift up my hands to your holy of holies. ^28-2

**28:3** Do not snatch me away with the wicked or with the evildoers, \*  
who speak peaceably with their neighbors,  
while strife is in their hearts. ^28-3

**28:4** Repay them according to their deeds, \*  
and according to the wickedness of their actions. ^28-4

**28:5** According to the work of their hands repay them, \*  
and give them their just deserts. ^28-5

**28:6** They have no understanding of the LORD'S doings,  
nor of the works of his hands; \*  
therefore he will break them down and not build them up. ^28-6

**28:7** Blessed is the LORD! \*  
for he has heard the voice of my prayer. ^28-7

**28:8** The LORD is my strength and my shield; \*  
my heart trusts in him, and I have been helped; ^28-8

**28:9** Therefore my heart dances for joy, \*  
and in my song will I praise him. ^28-9

[p. 620] ^pg620

**28:10** The LORD is the strength of his people, \*  
a safe refuge for his anointed. ^28-10

**28:11** Save your people and bless your inheritance; \*  
shepherd them and carry them for ever. ^28-11
