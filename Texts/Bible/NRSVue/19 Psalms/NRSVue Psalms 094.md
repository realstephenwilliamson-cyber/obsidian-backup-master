# Psalm 94 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 093|← Psalm 93]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 095|Psalm 95 →]]

## God the avenger of the righteous

**94:1** O LORD, you God of vengeance, you God of vengeance, shine forth! ^94-1

**94:2** Rise up, O judge of the earth; give to the proud what they deserve! ^94-2

**94:3** O LORD, how long shall the wicked, how long shall the wicked exult? ^94-3

**94:4** They pour out their arrogant words; all the evildoers boast. ^94-4

**94:5** They crush your people, O LORD, and afflict your heritage. ^94-5

**94:6** They kill the widow and the stranger; they murder the orphan, ^94-6

**94:7** and they say, “The LORD does not see; the God of Jacob does not perceive.” ^94-7

**94:8** Understand, O dullest of the people; fools, when will you be wise? ^94-8

**94:9** He who planted the ear, does he not hear? He who formed the eye, does he not see? ^94-9

**94:10** He who disciplines the nations, he who teaches knowledge to humankind, does he not chastise? ^94-10

**94:11** The LORD knows our thoughts,^[Heb *the thoughts of humankind*] that they are but an empty breath. ^94-11

**94:12** Happy are those whom you discipline, O LORD, and whom you teach out of your law, ^94-12

**94:13** giving them respite from days of trouble, until a pit is dug for the wicked. ^94-13

**94:14** For the LORD will not forsake his people; he will not abandon his heritage; ^94-14

**94:15** for justice will return to the righteous, and all the upright in heart will follow it. ^94-15

**94:16** Who rises up for me against the wicked? Who stands up for me against evildoers? ^94-16

**94:17** If the LORD had not been my help, my soul would soon have lived in the land of silence. ^94-17

**94:18** When I thought, “My foot is slipping,” your steadfast love, O LORD, held me up. ^94-18

**94:19** When the cares of my heart are many, your consolations cheer my soul. ^94-19

**94:20** Can wicked rulers be allied with you, those who contrive mischief by statute? ^94-20

**94:21** They band together against the life of the righteous and condemn the innocent to death. ^94-21

**94:22** But the LORD has become my stronghold and my God the rock of my refuge. ^94-22

**94:23** He will repay them for their iniquity and wipe them out for their wickedness; the LORD our God will wipe them out. ^94-23
