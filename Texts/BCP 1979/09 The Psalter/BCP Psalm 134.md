# Psalm 134

← [[BCP Psalm 133]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 135]] →

*Ecce nunc* · Book Five · Twenty-eighth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 134]]

**134:1** Behold now, bless the LORD, all you servants of the LORD, \*  
you that stand by night in the house of the LORD. ^134-1

**134:2** Lift up your hands in the holy place and bless the LORD; \*  
the LORD who made heaven and earth bless you out of Zion. ^134-2
