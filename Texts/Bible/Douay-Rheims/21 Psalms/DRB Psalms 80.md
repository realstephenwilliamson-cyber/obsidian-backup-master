# Psalms 80 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 79|← Psalms 79]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 81|Psalms 81 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 80|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**80:1** Unto the end, for the winepresses, a psalm for Asaph himself. ^80-1

**80:2** Rejoice to God our helper: sing aloud to the God of Jacob. ^80-2

**80:3** Take a psalm, and bring hither the timbrel: the pleasant psaltery with the harp. ^80-3

**80:4** Blow up the trumpet on the new moon, on the noted day of your solemnity. ^80-4

**80:5** For it is a commandment in Israel, and a judgment to the God of Jacob. ^80-5

**80:6** He ordained it for a testimony in Joseph, when he came out of the land of Egypt: he heard a tongue which he knew not. ^80-6

**80:7** He removed his back from the burdens: his hands had served in baskets. ^80-7

**80:8** Thou calledst upon me in affliction, and I delivered thee: I heard thee in the secret place of tempest: I proved thee at the waters of contradiction. ^80-8

**80:9** Hear, O my people, and I will testify to thee: O Israel, if thou wilt hearken to me, ^80-9

**80:10** There shall be no new god in thee: neither shalt thou adore a strange god. ^80-10

**80:11** For I am the Lord thy God, who brought thee out of the land of Egypt: open thy mouth wide, and I will fill it. ^80-11

**80:12** But my people heard not my voice: and Israel hearkened not to me. ^80-12

**80:13** So I let them go according to the desires of their heart: they shall walk in their own inventions. ^80-13

**80:14** If my people had heard me: if Israel had walked in my ways: ^80-14

**80:15** I should soon have humbled their enemies, and laid my hand on them that troubled them. ^80-15

**80:16** The enemies of the Lord have lied to him: and their time shall be for ever. ^80-16

**80:17** And he fed them with the fat of wheat, and filled them with honey out of the rock. ^80-17
