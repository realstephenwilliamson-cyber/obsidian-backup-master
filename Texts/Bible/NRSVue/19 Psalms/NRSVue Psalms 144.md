# Psalm 144 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 143|← Psalm 143]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 145|Psalm 145 →]]

## Prayer for national deliverance and security

*Of David.*

**144:1** Blessed be the LORD, my rock, who trains my hands for war and my fingers for battle, ^144-1

**144:2** my rock^[Cn: Heb *my steadfast love*] and my fortress, my stronghold and my deliverer, my shield, in whom I take refuge, who subdues the peoples^[Q ms Heb mss Syr Aquila Jerome: MT *my people*] under me. ^144-2

**144:3** O LORD, what are humans that you regard them, or mortals that you think of them? ^144-3

**144:4** They are like a breath; their days are like a passing shadow. ^144-4

**144:5** Bow your heavens, O LORD, and come down; touch the mountains so that they smoke. ^144-5

**144:6** Make the lightning flash and scatter them; send out your arrows and rout them. ^144-6

**144:7** Stretch out your hand from on high; set me free and rescue me from the mighty waters, from the hand of foreigners, ^144-7

**144:8** whose mouths speak lies and whose right hands are false. ^144-8

**144:9** I will sing a new song to you, O God; upon a ten-stringed harp I will play to you, ^144-9

**144:10** the one who gives victory to kings, who rescues his servant David. ^144-10

**144:11** Rescue me from the cruel sword, and deliver me from the hand of aliens, whose mouths speak lies, and whose right hands are false. ^144-11

**144:12** May our sons in their youth be like plants full grown, our daughters like corner pillars, cut for the building of a palace. ^144-12

**144:13** May our barns be filled with produce of every kind; may our sheep increase by thousands, by tens of thousands in our fields, ^144-13

**144:14** and may our cattle be heavy with young. May there be no breach in the walls,^[Heb lacks *in the walls*] no exile, and no cry of distress in our streets. ^144-14

**144:15** Happy are the people to whom such blessings fall; happy are the people whose God is the LORD. ^144-15
