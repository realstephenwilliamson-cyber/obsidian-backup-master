# Ephesians 6 (JANT notes)

[[JANT Ephesians 05|← Ephesians 5]] · [[Jewish Annotated New Testament|JANT index]] · —

Text: [[NRSVue Ephesians 06|NRSVue]] · [[Ephesians 06|BSB and SBLGNT]] · Introduction: [[JANT Ephesians - Introduction]]

**[[NRSVue Ephesians 06#^6-1|6.1–3]]:** *Obey your parents*, followed by a near quotation of [[Torah 45 - Vaetchanan#^5-16|Deut 5.16]], suggesting that honoring parents means obeying them. According to [[Kiddushin 31#31b|*b. Qidd*. 31b]], obeying is subsumed under the commandment to fear parents ([[Torah 30 - Kedoshim#^19-3|Lev 19.3]]), while honoring means substantively to support them when they are in *bones* b Other ancient authorities lack *in the Lord* need. ^6-1

**[[NRSVue Ephesians 06#^6-3|3]]:** *Be well with you . . . live long*, order reversed from [[Torah 45 - Vaetchanan#^5-16|Deut 5.16]], which refers to all Israel living long in the land of Israel, not personal longevity. ^6-3

**[[NRSVue Ephesians 06#^6-4|4]]:** *Fathers, do not provoke your children*, perhaps because it may cause them to dishonor you. *b. Moʾed Qat.* 17a derives this lesson from [[Torah 30 - Kedoshim#^19-14|Lev 19.14]], “You shall not . . . put a stumbling block before the blind.” *Discipline and instruction*, discipline = Gk “paideia”; Heb “musar” ([[Proverbs 01#^1-8|Prov 1.8]]) has a similar range of meanings. ^6-4

**[[NRSVue Ephesians 06#^6-5|5–9]]:** Regulating relationships between slaves and masters. ^6-5

**[[NRSVue Ephesians 06#^6-5|5–6]]:** *As slaves of Christ*, submission to one’s master, whether human or divine, is God’s will. ^6-5-2

**[[NRSVue Ephesians 06#^6-5|5]]:** *Slaves*, the Tanakh knows of two types of “slaves”: foreign slaves ([[Torah 32 - Behar#^25-44|Lev 25.44–46]]; know as “Canaanite slaves” in rabbinic tradition) and Hebrew slaves ([[Torah 18 - Mishpatim#^21-2|Ex 21.2]]**–**6; [[Torah 32 - Behar#^25-39|Lev 25.39]]**–**43; [[Torah 47 - Re'eh#^15-12|Deut 15.12]]**–**18). The law demands that the latter be treated with great respect and kindness, while the former are, by implication, subject to harsher discipline and are treated as property that can be passed in inheritance. In light of the erasure of the boundary between Jews and Gentiles, it is likely that the earlier distinction between categories of slaves is no longer operative. ^6-5-3

**[[NRSVue Ephesians 06#^6-8|8]]:** *Whether we are slaves or free*, divine reward comes to those who do good, whatever their station in life; perhaps a reinterpretation of [[Galatians 03#^3-28|Gal 3.28]]. ^6-8

**[[NRSVue Ephesians 06#^6-9|9]]:** *Stop threatening them*, [[Torah 32 - Behar#^25-43|Lev 25.43]] commands essentially the same, though only with respect to the Hebrew servant. ^6-9

### 6.10–17: The armor of God

*[[NRSVue Ephesians 06#^6-10|Text]], p. 397* ^6-10

**[[NRSVue Ephesians 06#^6-11|11]]:** *Whole armor of God*, needed to triumph in the cosmic battle against the forces of evil (v 12). God’s armor is spiritual— “belt of truth,” “breastplate of righteousness” (v 14), “shield of faith” (v 16), “helmet of salvation,” and “sword of the Spirit” (v 17), all of which are necessary to defeat the “flaming arrows of the evil one”; cf. [[Isaiah 59#^59-17|Isa 59.17]]. ^6-11

**[[NRSVue Ephesians 06#^6-12|12]]:** *Spiritual forces . . . in the heavenly places*, see [[Revelation 12#^12-7|Rev 12.7]]**–**9. ^6-12

**[[NRSVue Ephesians 06#^6-14|14]]:** *Belt of truth*, see [[Isaiah 11#^11-5|Isa 11.5]]. ^6-14

**[[NRSVue Ephesians 06#^6-17|17]]:** *Sword*, armor could be dignified ornaments, but *m. Shabb*. 6.4, citing [[Isaiah 02#^2-4|Isa 2.4]], advises “A man should not go out with a sword, bow, shield, club, or spear,” because, in the view of the sages, “they are nothing but ugly.” ^6-17

### 6.18–24: Final exhortations and blessing

*[[NRSVue Ephesians 06#^6-18|Text]], p. 397* ^6-18

**[[NRSVue Ephesians 06#^6-19|19]]:** *Pray also for me*, the author expresses reliance on the spiritual support of the saints. *With boldness*, Gk “parrēsia”; the related common rabbinic Heb term “parhesia” means “in public.” ^6-19

**[[NRSVue Ephesians 06#^6-20|20]]:** *In chains*, see 3.1. ^6-20

**[[NRSVue Ephesians 06#^6-21|21]]:** *Tychicus*, see [[Colossians 04#^4-7|Col 4.7]]**–**9. ^6-21
