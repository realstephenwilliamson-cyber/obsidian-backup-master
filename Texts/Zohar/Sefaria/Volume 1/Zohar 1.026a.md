# Zohar 1.026a

← [[Zohar 1.025b]] · [[Zohar (Soncino)]] · [[Zohar 1.026b]] →

*Volume 1, folio 26a* · Bereshit

*Passage Bereshit 21:238 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 1.025b#^bereshit-21-238|Bereshit 21:238]].*

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Bereshit 21:239.*

**Bereshit 21:240** AND THE LORD GOD FORMED MAN. “Man” here refers to Israel, whom God shaped at that time both for this world and for the future world. Further, the word *vayizer* (and he formed) implies that God brought them under the aegis of His own name by shaping the two eyes like the letter *Yod* and the nose between like the letter *Vau…*. ^bereshit-21-240

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Bereshit 21:241–21:242.*

**Bereshit 21:243** Forthwith at that time He planted Israel in the holy Garden of Eden, as it is said: “and the Lord God planted” (Gen. 2, 8). The two names here refer to the Father and the Mother; the “Garden” is the Shekinah on earth, and “Eden” is the supernal Mother; “the man” is the Central Column; the Shekinah was to be his plantation, his spouse who was never to depart from him and was to be his perpetual delight. Thus God at that time planted Israel as a holy shoot, as it is written, “the branch of my planting, the work of my hands, in which I glory”. ^bereshit-21-243

**Bereshit 21:244** AND THE LORD GOD CAUSED TO GROW. The two names may be referred to the Father and the Mother; “every pleasant tree” refers to the *Zaddik*; “good to eat” refers to the Central Column, through which He provided food for all, and from which alone the *Zaddik* is nourished, as the Shekinah from him. These have no need of the lower world, but, on the contrary, all below are nourished from him. For in this period of captivity the Shekinah and “the Life of the universe” are only nourished by the eighteen blessings of Israel’s prayer, but at the time he will be food for all. ^bereshit-21-244

**Bereshit 21:245** AND THE TREE OF LIFE. This means that at that time the Tree of Life will be planted in the Garden, so that “he shall take also of the Tree of Life and eat and live for ever” (Gen. 3, 22). The Shekinah will no longer be in the power of the “evil influence”, i.e. the mixed multitude who are “the tree of the knowledge of good and evil”, and shall no longer receive into itself anyone unclean, to fulfil what is written, “the Lord alone shall lead him and there shall be no strange god with him” (Deut. 32, 12). For this reason proselytes will no longer be admitted in the days of the Messiah. The Shekinah will be like a vine on which there cannot be grafted any shoot from another species, ^bereshit-21-245

**Bereshit 21:246** and Israel shall be “every tree pleasant to see”, and their former beauty shall be restored to them, of which we are told: “He cast from heaven to earth the beauty of Israel” (Lam. 2, 1). “The tree of the knowledge of good and evil” shall be thrust from them and shall not cleave to them or mingle with them, for of Israel it is said: “and of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil ye shall not eat”. This tree is the “mixed multitude”, and God pointed out to them that through mixing with them they suffered two losses, of the first and of the second Temple, as it is said: “and on the day that thou eatest of it thou shalt surely die”. They caused the *Zaddik* to be left parched and desolate by the loss of the first Temple, which is the Shekinah in heaven, and by the loss of the second Temple, which is the Shekinah on earth. Hence it is written, “and the river shall be drained dry”; i.e. the river *vau* shall dry in the lower *he*, so as to deprive it of the flow of *yod* issuing from *En-Sof*. ^bereshit-21-246

**Bereshit 21:247** But as soon as Israel shall go forth from captivity, that is, the holy people alone, then that river which was dried up shall become “the river that goes forth from Eden to water the garden”. This river is the Central Column; “goes forth from Eden” is the supernal Mother; “to water the garden” is the Shekinah on earth. ^bereshit-21-247

**Bereshit 21:248** In reference to that time it is said of Moses and Israel, “Then thou shalt delight in the Lord”, and the words shall be fulfilled, “then Moses shall sing” (Ex. 15, 1).[^1] ^bereshit-21-248

[^1]: The Hebrew text of this page does not correspond entirely to the English translation.
