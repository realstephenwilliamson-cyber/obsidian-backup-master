## Chapter 144

**144:1** Of David.  
Blessed is the LORD, my rock,  
who trains my hands for battle,  
my fingers for warfare; ^144-1

**144:2** my faithful one, my fortress,  
my haven and my deliverer,  
my shield, in whom I take shelter,  
who makes peoples[^1] subject to me. ^144-2

**144:3** O LORD, what is man that You should care about him,  
mortal man, that You should think of him? ^144-3

**144:4** Man is like a breath;  
his days are like a passing shadow. ^144-4

**144:5** O LORD, bend Your sky and come down;  
touch the mountains and they will smoke. ^144-5

**144:6** Make lightning flash and scatter them;  
shoot Your arrows and rout them. ^144-6

**144:7** Reach Your hand down from on high;  
rescue me, save me from the mighty waters,  
from the hands of foreigners, ^144-7

**144:8** whose mouths speak lies,  
and whose oaths[^2] are false. ^144-8

**144:9** O God, I will sing You a new song,  
sing a hymn to You with a ten-stringed harp, ^144-9

**144:10** to You who give victory to kings,  
who rescue His servant David from the deadly sword. ^144-10

**144:11** Rescue me, save me from the hands of foreigners,  
whose mouths speak lies,  
and whose oaths[^3] are false. ^144-11

**144:12** [^4]For our sons are like saplings,  
well-tended in their youth;  
our daughters are like cornerstones  
trimmed to give shape to a palace. ^144-12

**144:13** Our storehouses are full,  
supplying produce of all kinds;  
our flocks number thousands,  
even myriads, in our fields; ^144-13

**144:14** our cattle are well cared for.  
There is no breaching and no sortie,  
and no wailing in our streets. ^144-14

**144:15** Happy the people who have it so;  
happy the people whose God is the LORD. ^144-15

[^1]: So Targum, Saadia; others “my people.”
[^2]: With Rashi; lit. “right hand.”
[^3]: With Rashi; lit. “right hand.”
[^4]: The meaning of several phrases in vv. 12–14 is uncertain.
