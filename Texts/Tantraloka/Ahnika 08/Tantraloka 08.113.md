## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 05 - Chapter Eight, part 3#^pg176|p. 158]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=176>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 8.113cd–114ab.
>
> Beyond mount Lokāloka is (an area of) darkness (that extends for) three hundred and fifty-one million, nine hundred and forty thousand (leagues) outside mount Loka. (That) is the size of the seven oceans, that (together), around Lokāloka, is the emperor of the oceans called Garbhoda, within which is the entire earth. (with 8.113–114)
