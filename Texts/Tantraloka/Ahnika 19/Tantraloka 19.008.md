## Mūla

दृष्ट्वा शिष्यं जराग्रस्तं व्याधिना परिपीडितम् ।
उत्क्रमय्य ततस्त्वेनं परतत्त्वे नियोजयेत् ॥ ^mula

dṛṣṭvā śiṣyaṃ jarāgrastaṃ vyādhinā paripīḍitam |
utkramayya tatastvenaṃ paratattve niyojayet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 10 - Chapter Nineteen#^pg222|pp. 202–203]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=222>))
>
> (The teacher) should not (impart the initiation that) causes one to exit (from the body) if (his) Impurity has not matured (and so is not ready to be removed), or to one who (still) has a residue of Karma left. It is said in the venerable Gahvara that ‘having observed that the disciple is consumed by old age and is severely afflicted by illness, (the teacher) should cause him to quit (the body) and then conjoin him to the Supreme Reality. (with 19.7–8)

## Viveka

> [!viveka]- Viveka in English (Dyczkowski, vol. IX, pp. 202–203)
> ![[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 10 - Chapter Nineteen#Viveka on 19.7–8]]

> नत्वपक्वमले नापि शेषकार्मिकविग्रहे ।
> कुर्यादुत्क्रमणं श्रीमद्गह्वरे च निरूपितम् ॥
> दृष्ट्वा शिष्यं जराग्रस्तं व्याधिना परिपीडितम् ।
> उत्क्रमयय ततस्त्वेनं परतत्त्वे नियोजयेत् ॥

अपक्वमल इति--अनायातशक्तिपातः--इत्यर्थः । शेषकार्मिकविग्रह इति--अनासन्नमरण इति यावत् । नच एतद्युक्तिमात्रसिद्धमेव--इत्याह-- श्रीमद्गह्वर इति ॥ ८ ॥
