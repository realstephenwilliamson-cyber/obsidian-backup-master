## Mūla

स्वात्मन्येव चिदाकाशे विश्वमस्म्यवभासयन् ।
स्रष्टा विश्वात्मक इति प्रथया भैरवात्मता ॥ ^mula

svātmanyeva cidākāśe viśvamasmyavabhāsayan |
sraṣṭā viśvātmaka iti prathayā bhairavātmatā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. II
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 02 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v02 12 - Chapter Three, part 9#^pg420|p. 404]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 02.pdf#page=420>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 3.283cd–284ab.
>
> “I, manifesting the universe in my own Self, the Sky of Consciousness, am the creator who is all things (viśvātmaka)!” — by this insight one becomes Bhairava. “The entire cosmic order (ṣadadhvajāta) is reflected within me alone, I am the agent of persistence!” — (with this insight) this (one’s own) cosmic nature becomes evidently apparent. “The universe melts away into me, whose nature is the scorching fire of the great, perpetually active (satatodita) consciousness” — seeing thus, one becomes peaceful. (with 3.283–285)
