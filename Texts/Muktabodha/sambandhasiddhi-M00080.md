# sambandhasiddhi

**Catalog number:** M00080  
**Author:** utpaladeva  
**Published:** Research Department, Jammu and Kashmir State, Srinagar, India, 1921

## Description

E-text transcribed from volume 34 of the Kashmir Series of Texts and Studies, where it is bundled together with other texts of Utpaladeva: ājaḍapramātṛsiddhi iśvarasiddhi īśvarapratyabhijñākārikāvṛtti  The sambandhasiddhi,ājaḍapramātṛsiddhī and iśvarasiddhi are collectively known as the siddhitrayī .
