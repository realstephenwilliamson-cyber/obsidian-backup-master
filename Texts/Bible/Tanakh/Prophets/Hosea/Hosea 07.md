## Chapter 7

**7:1** When I would heal Israel,  
The guilt of Ephraim reveals itself  
And the wickedness of Samaria.  
For they have acted treacherously,  
With thieves breaking in  
And bands raiding outside. ^7-1

**7:2** And they do not consider  
That I remembered all their wickedness.  
Why, their misdeeds have been all around them,[^1]  
They have been ever before Me. ^7-2

**7:3** [^2]In malice they make a king merry,  
And officials in treachery. ^7-3

**7:4** They commit adultery,[^3] all of them,  
Like an oven fired by a baker,  
Who desists from stoking only  
From the kneading of the dough to its leavening. ^7-4

**7:5** The day they made our king sick  
[And] officials with the poison of wine,  
He gave his hand to traitors.[^4] ^7-5

**7:6** For they approach their ambush  
With their hearts like an oven:[^5]  
Through the night  
Their baker[^6] has slept;  
In the morning, it flares up  
Like a blazing fire. ^7-6

**7:7** They all get heated like an oven  
And devour their rulers—  
None of them calls to Me.  
All their kings have fallen [by their hand]. ^7-7

**7:8** Ephraim is among the peoples;  
He is rotting away.  
Ephraim is like a cake—  
Incapable of turning.[^7] ^7-8

**7:9** Strangers have consumed his strength,  
But he has taken no notice;  
Also, mold[^8] is scattered over him,  
But he has taken no notice. ^7-9

**7:10** Though Israel’s pride has been humbled  
Before his very eyes,  
They have not turned back  
To their God the LORD;  
They have not sought Him  
In spite of everything. ^7-10

**7:11** Instead, Ephraim has acted  
Like a silly dove with no mind:  
They have appealed to Egypt!  
They have gone to Assyria! ^7-11

**7:12** When they go, I will spread  
My net over them,  
I will bring them down  
Like birds of the sky;  
I will chastise them  
When I hear their bargaining.[^9] ^7-12

**7:13** Woe to them  
For straying from Me;  
Destruction to them  
For rebelling against Me!  
For I was their Redeemer;  
Yet they have plotted treason against Me. ^7-13

**7:14** [^10]But they did not cry out to Me sincerely  
As they lay wailing.[^11]  
They debauch[^12] over new grain and new wine,  
They are faithless[^13] to Me. ^7-14

**7:15** *I* braced, *I* strengthened their arms,  
And they plot evil against *Me*! ^7-15

**7:16** They come back;  
They have been of no use,[^14]  
Like a slack bow.  
Their officers shall fall by the sword,  
Because of the stammering[^15] of their tongues.  
Such shall be [the results of] their jabbering[^16]  
In the land of Egypt. ^7-16

[^1]: Emendation yields “Me.”
[^2]: Vv. 3–6 would read well in the order 4, 6, 3, 5.
[^3]: Emendation yields “rage.”
[^4]: I.e., he trusted traitors; but meaning of verse uncertain.
[^5]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^6]: Emendation yields “rage.”
[^7]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^8]: Like Akkadian sh™†bu; others “gray hairs.”
[^9]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^10]: This verse would read well after 8.2.
[^11]: I.e., in penitence; cf. Isa. 58.5.
[^12]: Cf. Aramaic gar/yegur “to commit adultery”; for the thought, cf. 4.11.
[^13]: Taking yasuru as equivalent to yasoru, from sarar; cf. 9.15 end.
[^14]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^15]: Cf. Arabic zaghu†m and zughmu†m “a stammerer.”
[^16]: I.e., the negotiations conducted in the Egyptian language.
