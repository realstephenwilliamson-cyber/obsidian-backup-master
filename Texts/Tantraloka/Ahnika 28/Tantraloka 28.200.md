## Mūla

पुनश्च प्रकटीभूय भैरवीभावभाजनम् ।
तत्रास्य प्रकटीभावे भुक्तिमुक्त्यात्मके भृशम् ॥ ^mula

punaśca prakaṭībhūya bhairavībhāvabhājanam |
tatrāsya prakaṭībhāve bhuktimuktyātmake bhṛśam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 05 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 3#^pg108|p. 91]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=108>))
>
> Again, by virtue of its own (inherent) freedom, this (Self) abides (in the body) as if hidden, and then, once (its true nature) again becomes clearly apparent, it is endowed with Bhairava’s state of being (bhairavībhāva). (with 28.199cd–200ab)
>
> There (in that case), the (most) appropriate and intense (bhrśam) means of rendering (the true nature of the Self) clearly apparent, which is both (the attainment of all) worldly benefits and liberation, is this lineage of (transmission of liberating) knowledge (jñānasantāna) (which is the continuum of cognitive consciousness). (with 28.200cd–201ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* ननु अस्य पुनः स्वत एव चेद्भैरवीभावो भवेत् तत्कृतमनया प्रक्रान्तया दर्शनव्यवस्थया; मलपरिपाकादिश्च हेतुर्न न्यायय इत्युक्तम्, तत्कतरस्तावदत्र समुचित उपाय इति न जानीमः ?-- इत्याशङ्क्य आह--

> तत्रास्य प्रकटीभावे भुक्तिमुक्त्यात्मके भृशम् ॥
> य उपायः समुचितो ज्ञानसन्तान एष सः ।
