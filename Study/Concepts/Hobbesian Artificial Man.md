# Hobbesian Artificial Man

> [!abstract] Definition
> Thomas Hobbes's foundational metaphor in the Introduction to *Leviathan* (1651), defining the Commonwealth not as a natural community or divine ordinance, but as an *automaton* or "Artificial Man" created by human covenant, in which sovereignty is an artificial soul giving motion to mechanical joints and organs.

## Anatomical Schema of Leviathan
Hobbes systematically maps the anatomical body onto the political apparatus:
- **Artificial Soul:** The Sovereign (monarch or assembly) giving life, will, and motion.
- **Artificial Joints:** Public magistrates and judicial officers.
- **Artificial Nerves:** Rewards and punishments.
- **Artificial Strength:** Wealth and riches of citizens.
- **Artificial Memory:** Counselors and administrative archives.
- **Artificial Health & Sickness:** Concord is health; sedition and faction are disease; civil war is death.

## Cybernetic Leviathan Synthesis
In [[The Cybernetic Leviathan|The Cybernetic Leviathan]], this seventeenth-century mechanical metaphor is recognized as an early cybernetic model:
- The state is an information-processing feedback machine designed to optimize security and prevent systemic collapse.
- In contemporary society, the "Artificial Man" is instantiated directly into algorithmic infrastructure, cloud platforms, and data flows.
