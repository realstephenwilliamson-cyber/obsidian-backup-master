# καταλαμβάνω

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · verb

**Dodson:** I seize tight hold of, overtake, comprehend — (a) I seize tight hold of, arrest, catch, capture, appropriate, (b) I overtake, (c) mid. aor: I perceived, comprehended. (Strong's 2638; headword καταλαμβάνω)
