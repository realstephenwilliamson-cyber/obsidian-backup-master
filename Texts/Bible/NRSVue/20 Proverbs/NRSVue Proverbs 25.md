## Further wise sayings of Solomon

**25:1** These are other proverbs of Solomon that the officials of King Hezekiah of Judah copied. ^25-1

**25:2** It is the glory of God to conceal things, but the glory of kings is to search things out. ^25-2

**25:3** Like the heavens for height, like the earth for depth, so the mind of kings is unsearchable. ^25-3

**25:4** Take away the dross from the silver, and the smith has material for a vessel; ^25-4

**25:5** take away the wicked from the presence of the king, and his throne will be established in righteousness. ^25-5

**25:6** Do not put yourself forward in the king’s presence or stand in the place of the great, ^25-6

**25:7** for it is better to be told, “Come up here,” than to be put lower in the presence of a noble. What your eyes have seen ^25-7

**25:8** do not hastily bring into court, for^[Cn: Heb *or else*] what will you do in the end, when your neighbor puts you to shame? ^25-8

**25:9** Argue your case with your neighbor directly, and do not disclose another’s secret, ^25-9

**25:10** or else someone who hears you will bring shame upon you, and your ill repute will have no end. ^25-10

**25:11** A word fitly spoken is like apples of gold in a setting of silver. ^25-11

**25:12** Like a gold ring or an ornament of gold is a wise rebuke to a listening ear. ^25-12

**25:13** Like the cold of snow in the time of harvest are faithful messengers to those who send them; they refresh the spirit of their masters. ^25-13

**25:14** Like clouds and wind without rain is one who boasts of a gift never given. ^25-14

**25:15** With patience a ruler may be persuaded, and a soft tongue can break bones. ^25-15

**25:16** If you have found honey, eat only enough for you, lest, having too much, you vomit it up. ^25-16

**25:17** Let your foot be seldom in your neighbor’s house, lest the neighbor become weary of you and hate you. ^25-17

**25:18** Like a war club, a sword, or a sharp arrow is one who bears false witness against a neighbor. ^25-18

**25:19** Like a bad tooth or a lame foot is trust in a faithless person in time of trouble. ^25-19

**25:20** Like vinegar on a wound^[Gk: Heb *Like one who takes off a garment on a cold day, like vinegar on lye*] is one who sings songs to a heavy heart. Like a moth in clothing or a worm in wood, sorrow gnaws at the human heart.^[Gk Syr Tg: Heb lacks *Like a moth . . . human heart*] ^25-20

**25:21** If your enemies are hungry, give them bread to eat, and if they are thirsty, give them water to drink, ^25-21

**25:22** for you will heap coals of fire on their heads, and the LORD will reward you. ^25-22

**25:23** The north wind produces rain, and a backbiting tongue, angry looks. ^25-23

**25:24** It is better to live in a corner of the housetop than in a house shared with a contentious wife. ^25-24

**25:25** Like cold water to a thirsty soul, so is good news from a far country. ^25-25

**25:26** Like a muddied spring or a polluted fountain are the righteous who give way before the wicked. ^25-26

**25:27** It is not good to eat much honey or to seek honor on top of honor. ^25-27

**25:28** Like a city breached, without walls, is one who lacks self-control. ^25-28
