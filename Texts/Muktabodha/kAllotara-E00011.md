# kāllotara

**Catalog number:** E00011  
**Main title:** kālottaratantra  
**Manuscript:** NGMPP Reel No. - B 118/7 manuscript no. 5-4632  
**Published:** Muktabodha Indological Research Institute, India

## Description

This manuscript contains various versions of kāllotaratantra they are:
1) jñānapañcāsikā
 2) śatikam also called kālajñāna-  One Hundred Verse Kālottara
3) kālottaram sārddhaśatikam - One Hundred and Fifty Verse Kālottara
4) dviśatikam - Two Hundred Verse Kāalottara
 5) adhyuṣṭaśatikam Three Hundred and Fifty Verse Kālottara
6) saptaśatikam - Seven Hundred Verse Kālottara
7) trayodaśaśatikam - Thirteen Hundred Verse Kālottara
