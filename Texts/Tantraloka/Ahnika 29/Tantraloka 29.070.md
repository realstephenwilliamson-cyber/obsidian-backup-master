## Mūla

एकवीरा चक्रयुक्ता चक्रयामलगापि वा ।
ईशेन्द्राग्नियमक्रव्यात्कवायूदक्षु हासतः ॥ ^mula

ekavīrā cakrayuktā cakrayāmalagāpi vā |
īśendrāgniyamakravyātkavāyūdakṣu hāsataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 10 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 2#^pg279|p. 262]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=279>))
>
> Once having worshipped the arising of the Wheel (cakrodaya) (that is, the cyclic repetition of mantra in consonance with the breath) in this way, one should worship the mistress of Kula, that is, (Kāla-)Saṁkarṣiṇī, who nourishes the withdrawal (of all things into her infinite being). (This should be done) in the centre (in Cakriṇ?s place), at the (inner) extremity of (the spokes) within that (Wheel). (She may be) alone (ekavīrāā), absorbed in contemplation (yuktā) within the Wheel, or also participating in the union (with her partner) within the Wheel. (with 29.69–70ab)
>
> (In each direction, that is,) the northeast, east, southeast, south, southwest (kravyād), west (ka), northwest and north, one should worship (the sacred seats) three (at a time,) commencing with Aṭṭahāsa, along with their own (corresponding) triad, as will be (taught). (with 29.70cd–71ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* अत्र कथं पीठानि साहित्येन पूज्यानि?--इत्याशङ्क्य आह--

> ईशेन्द्राग्नियमक्रव्यात्कवायूदक्षु हासतः ॥
> त्रिकं त्रिकं यजेदेतद्भाविस्वत्रिकसंयुतम् ।

एवमीशानकोणे अट्टहासश्चरित्रं कुलगिरिश्चेति त्रयं यजेद्यावदुदीच्यामाम्रतकेश्वरे राजगृहं श्रीपर्वतश्चेति त्रयमिति । न च एतदेकैकं पीठं केवलमेव यजेत्--इत्याह-- भाविस्वत्रिकसंयुतमिति, भावीति--वक्ष्यमाणम् ॥
