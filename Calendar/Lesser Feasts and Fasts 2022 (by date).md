# Lesser Feasts and Fasts 2022 (by date)

The commemorations of *Lesser Feasts and Fasts 2022*, with the changes made by the 2024 General Convention, as listed by date on The Lectionary Page (saved 8 Oct 2026). Square brackets mark commemorations, and alternative dates, approved for trial use only. Holy Days (feasts of our Lord, apostles, evangelists and the other Major Feasts) are not in this list; Home takes them from the Prayer Book.

Home shows today's commemoration in the date box. Under the Prayer Book's rules (BCP pp. 17–18), a commemoration gives way to a Sunday, a Principal Feast or a Holy Day, and no fixed-date feast is kept in Holy Week, Easter Week or on Ash Wednesday; Home says so on those days.

The last column links the biography and collects in [[Great Cloud of Witnesses|A Great Cloud of Witnesses]] (2016). Matches were made by name and checked by hand; 56 commemorations added since 2016 have no entry there.
