# Wisdom of Solomon 9 (NRSVue)

## Prayer for wisdom

**9:1** “O God of my ancestors and Lord of mercy, who have made all things by your word ^9-1

**9:2** and by your wisdom have formed humankind to have dominion over the creatures you have made ^9-2

**9:3** and rule the world in holiness and righteousness and pronounce judgment in uprightness of soul, ^9-3

**9:4** give me the wisdom that sits by your throne, and do not reject me from among your children. ^9-4

**9:5** For I am your servant, the son of your female servant, a man who is weak and short-lived, with little understanding of judgment and laws, ^9-5

**9:6** for even one who is perfect among humans will be regarded as nothing without the wisdom that comes from you. ^9-6

**9:7** You have chosen me to be king of your people and to be judge over your sons and daughters. ^9-7

**9:8** You have given command to build a temple on your holy mountain and an altar in the city of your habitation, a copy of the holy tent that you prepared from the beginning. ^9-8

**9:9** With you is wisdom, she who knows your works and was present when you made the world; she understands what is pleasing in your sight and what is right according to your commandments. ^9-9

**9:10** Send her forth from the holy heavens, and from the throne of your glory send her, that she may labor at my side and that I may learn what is pleasing to you. ^9-10

**9:11** For she knows and understands all things, and she will guide me wisely in my actions and guard me with her glory. ^9-11

**9:12** Then my works will be acceptable, and I shall judge your people justly and shall be worthy of the throne^[Gk *thrones*] of my father. ^9-12

**9:13** For who can learn the counsel of God? Or who can discern what the Lord wills? ^9-13

**9:14** For the reasoning of mortals is worthless, and our designs are likely to fail, ^9-14

**9:15** for a perishable body weighs down the soul, and this earthy tent burdens the thoughtful^[Or *anxious*] mind. ^9-15

**9:16** We can hardly guess at what is on earth, and what is at hand we find with labor, but who has traced out what is in the heavens? ^9-16

**9:17** Who has learned your counsel unless you have given wisdom and sent your holy spirit from on high? ^9-17

**9:18** And thus the paths of those on earth were set right, and people were taught what pleases you and were saved by wisdom.” ^9-18
