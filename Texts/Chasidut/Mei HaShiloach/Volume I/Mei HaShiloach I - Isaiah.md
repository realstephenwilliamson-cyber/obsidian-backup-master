## Teaching 1 (Isaiah 52:11)

*“… be pure of heart, you who carry the vessels of God.”* ([[Isaiah 52#^52-11|Yeshayahu, 52:11]])  
All the positive mitzvot are called “the vessels of God.” Therefore the prophet is warning man, “be pure of heart, you who carry the vessels of God,” meaning you yourself, when you perform the positive mitzvot, see to it that you refine yourself from all the negative mitzvot.[^1] For example, when your hands take hold of the *etrog* (citron) to fulfill the positive commandment during the Succot festival, be sure that your hands are clean of any sins that could be done with the hands. In this way you will be performing the *mitzvot* in a state of completeness.

[^1]: The Hebrew word for “be pure of heart”—*hibaru*, is closely related to the Hebrew word for “refinement”—*birrur*.
