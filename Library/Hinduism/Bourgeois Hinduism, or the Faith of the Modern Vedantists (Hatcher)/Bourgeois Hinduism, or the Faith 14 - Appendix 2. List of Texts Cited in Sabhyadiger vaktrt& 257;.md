## Appendix 2. List of Texts Cited in Sabhyadiger vaktrt&#257;

Sabhyadiger vaktrtā

\_

The authors of the discourses collected in Sabhyadiger vaktrtā frequently cite \_

Sanskrit passages from revealed texts (śruti) or other traditional texts (e.g., smrti, itihāsa, and tantra) to amplify and lend authority to their presentations.

\_ Sabhyadiger vaktrtā provides no detailed citations, but every effort has been \_ made to identify these Sanskrit passages so that interested readers may consider the range and type of sources drawn on by members of the Tattvabodhinī Sabhā to articulate their vision of religion and morality. In some cases—for instance, a piece of gnomic verse in Discourse Eighteen and a verse purportedly from the Yoga-vāśistha Rāmāyan a in Discourse Two—it has \_ \_ \_ proven impossible to find a direct textual reference. Citations from Sanskrit sources are given by book, chapter, and verse as appropriate.
