# LXX Sirach 03

← [[LXX Sirach 02]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Sirach 04]] →

*Sirach · [[LXX]]*

**3:1** Hear me your father, O children, and do thereafter, that ye may be safe.  ^3-1

**3:2** For the Lord hath given the father honour over the children, and hath confirmed the authority of the mother over the sons.  ^3-2

**3:3** Whoso honoureth his father maketh an atonement for his sins:  ^3-3

**3:4** and he that honoureth his mother is as one that layeth up treasure.  ^3-4

**3:5** Whoso honoureth his father shall have joy of *his own* children; and when he maketh his prayer, he shall be heard.  ^3-5

**3:6** He that honoureth his father shall have a long life; and he that is obedient unto the Lord shall be a comfort to his mother,  ^3-6

**3:7** and will do service unto his parents, as to his masters.  ^3-7

**3:8** Honour thy father and mother both in word and deed, that a blessing may come upon thee from them.  ^3-8

**3:9** For the blessing of the father establisheth the houses of children; but the curse of the mother rooteth out foundations.  ^3-9

**3:10** Glory not in the dishonour of thy father; for thy father's dishonour is no glory unto thee.  ^3-10

**3:11** For the glory of a man is from the honour of his father; and a mother in dishonour is a reproach to the children.  ^3-11

**3:12** My son, help thy father in his age, and grieve him not as long as he liveth.  ^3-12

**3:13** And if his understanding fail, have patience with him; and despise him not when thou art in thy full strength.  ^3-13

**3:14** For the relieving of thy father shall not be forgotten: and instead of sins it shall be added to build thee up.  ^3-14

**3:15** In the day of thine affliction it shall be remembered; thy sins also shall melt away, as the ice in the fair warm weather.  ^3-15

**3:16** He that forsaketh his father is as a blasphemer; and he that angereth his mother is cursed of God.  ^3-16

**3:17** My son, go on with thy business in meekness; so shalt thou be beloved of him that is approved.  ^3-17

**3:18** The greater thou art, the more humble thyself, and thou shalt find favour before the Lord.  ^3-18

**3:20** For the power of the Lord is great, and he is honoured of the lowly.  ^3-20

**3:21** Seek not out the things that are too hard for thee, neither search the things that are above thy strength.  ^3-21

**3:22** But what is commanded thee, think thereupon *with reverence*; for it is not needful for thee *to see with thine eyes* the things that are in secret.  ^3-22

**3:23** Be not curious in unnecessary matters; for more things are shewed unto thee than men understand.  ^3-23

**3:24** For many are deceived by their own vain opinion; and an evil suspicion hath overthrown their judgment.  ^3-24

**3:26** A stubborn heart shall fare evil at the last; and he that loveth danger shall perish therein.  ^3-26

**3:27** An obstinate heart shall be laden with sorrows; and the wicked man shall heap sin upon sin.  ^3-27

**3:28** In the punishment of the proud there is no remedy; for the plant of wickedness hath taken root in him.  ^3-28

**3:29** The heart of the prudent will understand a parable; and an attentive ear is the desire of a wise man.  ^3-29

**3:30** Water will quench a flaming fire; and alms maketh an atonement for sins.  ^3-30

**3:31** And he that requiteth good turns is mindful of that which may come hereafter; and when he falleth, he shall find a stay.  ^3-31
