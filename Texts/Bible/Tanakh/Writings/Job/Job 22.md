# Job 22

Compare: [[NRSVue Job 22|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 22

**22:1** Eliphaz the Temanite said in reply: ^22-1

**22:2** Can a man be of use to God,  
A wise man benefit Him? ^22-2

**22:3** Does Shaddai gain if you are righteous?  
Does He profit if your conduct is blameless? ^22-3

**22:4** Is it because of your piety that He arraigns you,  
And enters into judgment with you? ^22-4

**22:5** You know that your wickedness is great,  
And that your iniquities have no limit. ^22-5

**22:6** You exact pledges from your fellows without reason,  
And leave them naked, stripped of their clothes; ^22-6

**22:7** You do not give the thirsty water to drink;  
You deny bread to the hungry. ^22-7

**22:8** The land belongs to the strong;  
The privileged occupy it. ^22-8

**22:9** You have sent away widows empty-handed;  
The strength of the fatherless is broken. ^22-9

**22:10** Therefore snares are all around you,  
And sudden terrors frighten you, ^22-10

**22:11** Or darkness, so you cannot see;  
A flood of waters covers you. ^22-11

**22:12** God is in the heavenly heights;  
See the highest stars, how lofty! ^22-12

**22:13** You say, “What can God know?  
Can He govern through the dense cloud? ^22-13

**22:14** The clouds screen Him so He cannot see  
As He moves about the circuit of heaven.” ^22-14

**22:15** Have you observed the immemorial path  
That evil men have trodden; ^22-15

**22:16** How they were shriveled up before their time  
And their foundation poured out like a river? ^22-16

**22:17** They said to God, “Leave us alone;  
What can Shaddai do about it?” ^22-17

**22:18** But it was He who filled their houses with good things.  
(The thoughts of the wicked are beyond me!) ^22-18

**22:19** The righteous, seeing it,[^1] rejoiced;  
The innocent laughed with scorn. ^22-19

**22:20** Surely their substance was destroyed,  
And their remnant consumed by fire. ^22-20

**22:21** Be close to Him and wholehearted;  
Good things will come to you thereby. ^22-21

**22:22** Accept instruction from His mouth;  
Lay up His words in your heart. ^22-22

**22:23** If you return to Shaddai you will be restored,  
If you banish iniquity from your tent; ^22-23

**22:24** If you regard treasure as dirt,  
Ophir-gold as stones of the wadi, ^22-24

**22:25** And Shaddai be your treasure  
And precious silver for you, ^22-25

**22:26** When you seek the favor of Shaddai,  
And lift up your face to God, ^22-26

**22:27** You will pray to Him, and He will listen to you,  
And you will pay your vows. ^22-27

**22:28** You will decree and it will be fulfilled,  
And light will shine upon your affairs. ^22-28

**22:29** When others sink low, you will say it is pride;  
For He saves the humble. ^22-29

**22:30** He will deliver the guilty;  
He will be delivered through the cleanness of your hands. ^22-30

[^1]: Referring to v. 16.
