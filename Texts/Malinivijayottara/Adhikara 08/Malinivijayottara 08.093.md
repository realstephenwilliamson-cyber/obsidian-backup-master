## Mūla

दत्त्वानन्तं तथा धर्मं ज्ञानं वैराग्यमेव च ।
ऐश्वर्यं कर्णिकायां च षडुत्थमिदमासनम् ॥ ^mula

dattvānantaṁ tathā dharmaṁ jñānaṁ vairāgyam eva ca |
aiśvaryaṁ karṇikāyāṁ ca[^101] ṣaḍuttham idam āsanam || ^iast

## Translation

> (This is done) once Ananta, Dharma, Knowledge, Dispassion, Mastery and the calix (of a lotus) have been put in place. This is the throne made of six (components).[^102]
