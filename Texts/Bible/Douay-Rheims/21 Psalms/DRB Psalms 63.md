# Psalms 63 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 62|← Psalms 62]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 64|Psalms 64 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 63|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**63:1** Unto the end, a psalm for David. ^63-1

**63:2** Hear, O God, my prayer, when I make supplication to thee: deliver my soul from the fear of the enemy. ^63-2

**63:3** Thou hast protected me from the assembly of the malignant; from the multitude of the workers of iniquity. ^63-3

**63:4** For they have whetted their tongues like a sword; they have bent their bow a bitter thing, ^63-4

**63:5** To shoot in secret the undefiled. ^63-5

**63:6** They will shoot at him on a sudden, and will not fear: they are resolute in wickedness. They have talked of hiding snares; they have said: Who shall see them? ^63-6

**63:7** They have searched after iniquities: they have failed in their search. Man shall come to a deep heart: ^63-7

**63:8** And God shall be exalted. The arrows of children are their wounds: ^63-8

**63:9** And their tongues against them are made weak. All that saw them were troubled; ^63-9

**63:10** And every man was afraid. And they declared the works of God: and understood his doings. ^63-10

**63:11** The just shall rejoice in the Lord, and shall hope in him: and all the upright in heart shall be praised. ^63-11
