## Mūla

लिप्तायां भुवि पीठे चतुरस्रे पङ्कजत्रयं कजगे ।
कुर्याद्विद्यापीठं स्याद्रसवह्न्यंगुलं त्वेतत् ॥ ^mula

liptāyāṃ bhuvi pīṭhe caturasre paṅkajatrayaṃ kajage |
kuryādvidyāpīṭhaṃ syādrasavahnyaṃgulaṃ tvetat || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 07 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 5#^pg199|p. 182]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=199>))
>
> When the ground has been smeared (with cow dung), one should draw three lotuses on a square seat that is placed within a lotus, This is the Seat of Wisdom (vidyāpītṭha) and measures thirty-six digits. Vāgīśvarī is on (the lotus) in the centre, the teachers and Ganeśa, on those on the right and the left, respectively, and (the deity who is) the Lord of the Rite (Kalpeśvara) on the lotus below (the seat). Having offered libations (to them) with water and flowers and offered libation in the right way (samyak) to the assembly (cakra) (of all of them collectively), along with the sacrificial jar used (there), in accord with the common (basic) procedure, the wise (teacher) may begin to explain (the scripture), beginning with (an explanation of the overall) interconnection (saṁbandha) (between the various parts of the scripture). (with 28.397–399)
