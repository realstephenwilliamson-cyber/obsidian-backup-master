# Psalm 95 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 094|← Psalm 94]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 096|Psalm 96 →]]

## A call to worship and obedience

**95:1** O come, let us sing to the LORD; let us make a joyful noise to the rock of our salvation! ^95-1

**95:2** Let us come into his presence with thanksgiving; let us make a joyful noise to him with songs of praise! ^95-2

**95:3** For the LORD is a great God and a great King above all gods. ^95-3

**95:4** In his hand are the depths of the earth; the heights of the mountains are his also. ^95-4

**95:5** The sea is his, for he made it, and the dry land, which his hands have formed. ^95-5

**95:6** O come, let us worship and bow down; let us kneel before the LORD, our Maker! ^95-6

**95:7** For he is our God, and we are the people of his pasture and the sheep of his hand. O that today you would listen to his voice! ^95-7

**95:8** Do not harden your hearts, as at Meribah, as on the day at Massah in the wilderness, ^95-8

**95:9** when your ancestors tested me and put me to the proof, though they had seen my work. ^95-9

**95:10** For forty years I loathed that generation and said, “They are a people whose hearts go astray, and they do not regard my ways.” ^95-10

**95:11** Therefore in my anger I swore, “They shall not enter my rest.” ^95-11
