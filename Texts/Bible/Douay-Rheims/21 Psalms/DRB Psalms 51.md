# Psalms 51 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 50|← Psalms 50]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 52|Psalms 52 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 51|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**51:1** Unto the end, understanding for David, ^51-1

**51:2** When Doeg the Edomite came and told Saul David went to the house of Achimelech. [1 Kings 22] ^51-2

**51:3** Why dost thou glory in malice, thou that art mighty in iniquity? ^51-3

**51:4** All the day long thy tongue hath devised injustice: as a sharp razor, thou hast wrought deceit. ^51-4

**51:5** Thou hast loved malice more than goodness: and iniquity rather than to speak righteousness. ^51-5

**51:6** Thou hast loved all the words of ruin, O deceitful tongue. ^51-6

**51:7** Therefore will God destroy thee for ever: he will pluck thee out, and remove thee from thy dwelling place: and thy root out of the land of the living. ^51-7

**51:8** The just shall see and fear, and shall laugh at him, and say: ^51-8

**51:9** Behold the man that made not God his helper: But trusted in the abundance of his riches: and prevailed in his vanity. ^51-9

**51:10** But I, as a fruitful olive tree in the house of God, have hoped in the mercy of God for ever, yea for ever and ever. ^51-10

**51:11** I will praise thee for ever, because thou hast done it: and I will wait on thy name, for it is good in the sight of thy saints. ^51-11
