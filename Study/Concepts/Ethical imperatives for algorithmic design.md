# Ethical imperatives for algorithmic design

> [!abstract] Definition
> The obligations Stephen's writing places on those who build algorithmic systems. They follow from his diagnosis: if "the program that is running the machine" holds the authority Hobbes gave the sovereign, design is a political act, and the harm to prevent is the capture of what people can want and expect. Only imperatives his texts state appear here.

## Why the imperative falls on design

Track 3 (12 Jun 2018) of [[Leviathan fragments 2018-2024|Leviathan fragments 2018-2024]]: once "computerised society as a totality constitutes a machine", "the political authority is not the sovereign but the program that is running the machine is"; Track 5 adds "the further abstraction of Programmer". The Thoughts fragment (31 Dec 2020) shows the issue is design, not content: the platform a father named his daughter after became "a major catalyst of ongoing persecutions of the Rohingya". The same fragment targets "surveillance capitalism" and quotes *The Age of Disruption* on users "dispossessed of their own protentions — that is, their own desires, expectations, volitions, will."

## Imperatives stated in his texts

The governing rule is Part I of [[Second Domestication outline 2026-07-07|The Second Domestication]]: every memory technology is poison and cure at once, "and *the prescription is written by the surrounding practice*. The instrument alone decides nothing; the practices built around it decide everything."

1. **Build the mechanisms in time.** The 2020 abstract: "We have a relatively short time to put in place the social and cultural mechanisms that will be required to maintain even the semblance of humanity as the info-technological reticulation becomes increasingly dense."
2. **Do not optimise for an end that is not the user's.** Part II: the feed is "optimized for an end (engagement) that is not yours", and "for the first time, the instrument's owners profit from its wildness."
3. **Treat curation as the infrastructure of inheritance.** Part IV: "what commentary and canon did for the scroll, transparency and public accountability must do for the feed — curation treated as the infrastructure of inheritance, not the private property of whoever profits from its wildness."
4. **Engineer deliberately what the analog world gave by accident.** Part IV: friction, slowness and intergenerational transmission were "free byproducts of material limitation"; the repair is "the first *deliberate* engineering of what the analog gave us for free."
5. **Keep a person answerable.** Part III: "diffuse accountability is not attestation. A thousand hands on the loom and no one answerable for the cloth." See [[Crisis of Attestation]].
6. **Count the cost where the node sits.** cyberLevi2 (14 Jun 2018): fear of violent death is "sublimated to varying degrees usually simply depending on where your node on the network is located (i.e. was the land you are standing on colonized or did it produce colonizers?)".

## Open questions

- cyberleviNotes (4 Jul 2018) makes language the *techne* "presupposed by the social contract and therefore governed by Hobbes's *Natural Laws*". If code is language (cyberLevi2: the terms "boil down to language, be it binary, or human speech"), the seventeenth law — no man "a fit Arbitrator in his own cause" — would bind the designers of ranking and moderation. The fragments stop before this step.
- Is the 2020 abstract's "saved only by our inefficiency" a design principle (preserve friction) or a lament?
- Part IV's confessed tension: "a counter-practice that the platform absorbs and sells back is domestication's counterfeit." How does an imperative avoid becoming a feature?
- The Hub files this under Synthesis & Theology; Track 5 ties the Programmer to the Kabbalistic "source code of Creation". Theological or philosophical register?
