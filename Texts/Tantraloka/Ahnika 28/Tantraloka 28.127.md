## Mūla

पवित्रके प्रकाशत्वसिद्ध्यै कृष्णस्य वर्त्मनः ।
तदेतद्बहुशास्त्रोक्तं रूपं देवो न्यरूपयत् ॥ ^mula

pavitrake prakāśatvasiddhyai kṛṣṇasya vartmanaḥ |
tadetadbahuśāstroktaṃ rūpaṃ devo nyarūpayat || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 04 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 2#^pg84|p. 67]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=84>))
>
> Thus, the rite of the sacred thread should be performed during the six months the sun moves southwards, so as to illumine (its) dark path. (with 28.126cd–127ab)
>
> The form (and nature of this sacred thread) ruipa),), taught in many scriptures, the Lord has described in just one word in the venerable Kulāgama (called) Ratnamālā. The procedure concerning the sacred thread, which completes all (ritual) procedures, should be done (sometime) during the moment in time (mentioned) here, and always% during the bright lunar fortnight. (with 28.127cd–129ab)
