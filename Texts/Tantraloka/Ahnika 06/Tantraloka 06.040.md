## Mūla

उद्रिक्ताभोगकार्यात्मविश्वैकात्म्यमिदं यतः ।
एतदीश्वररूपत्वं परमात्मनि यत्किल ॥ ^mula

udriktābhogakāryātmaviśvaikātmyamidaṃ yataḥ |
etadīśvararūpatvaṃ paramātmani yatkila || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 09 - Chapter Six, part 2#^pg270|p. 255]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=270>))
>
> This is the Īśvara principle. That is said to be Śiva’s body, because (Īśvara, the Lord of the Universe,) is one with the universe of phenomena (kārya) in a state of abounding plenitude (which he experiences as ‘all this am I’). (with 6.39cd–40ab)
>
> This same principle which in the supreme subject is Īśvara, is in the Māyic subject (the obscuring covering) called the principle of time (kālatattva). (with 6.40cd–41ab)
