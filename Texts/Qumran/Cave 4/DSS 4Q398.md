### Frag. 14 - 17 col. ii (= 4Q399 i-ii; 4QMMT C 25 - 32)

**1** [forgiv]en (their) sins. Remember David, who was a man of the pious ones, [and] he, too, ^f14c2-1

**2** [was] freed from many afﬂictions and was forgiven. And also we have written to you ^f14c2-2

**3** some of the works of the Torah which we think are good for you and for your people, for we s[a]w ^f14c2-3

**6** so that at the end of time, you may rejoice in ﬁnding that some of our words are true. ^f14c2-6
