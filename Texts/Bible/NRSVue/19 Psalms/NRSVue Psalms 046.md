# Psalm 46 (NRSVue)

## God’s defense of his city and people

*To the leader. Of the Korahites. According to Alamoth. A Song.*

**46:1** God is our refuge and strength, a very present^[Or *well proved*] help in trouble. ^46-1

**46:2** Therefore we will not fear, though the earth should change, though the mountains shake in the heart of the sea, ^46-2

**46:3** though its waters roar and foam, though the mountains tremble with its tumult. *Selah* ^46-3

**46:4** There is a river whose streams make glad the city of God, the holy habitation of the Most High. ^46-4

**46:5** God is in the midst of the city;^[Heb *of it*] it shall not be moved; God will help it when the morning dawns. ^46-5

**46:6** The nations are in an uproar; the kingdoms totter; he utters his voice; the earth melts. ^46-6

**46:7** The LORD of hosts is with us; the God of Jacob is our refuge.^[Or *fortress*] *Selah* ^46-7

**46:8** Come, behold the works of the LORD; see what desolations he has brought on the earth. ^46-8

**46:9** He makes wars cease to the end of the earth; he breaks the bow and shatters the spear; he burns the shields with fire. ^46-9

**46:10** “Be still, and know that I am God! I am exalted among the nations; I am exalted in the earth.” ^46-10

**46:11** The LORD of hosts is with us; the God of Jacob is our refuge.^[Or *fortress*] *Selah* ^46-11
