## Mūla

तत्त्वेश्वर त्वया नास्य पुत्रकस्य शिवाज्ञया ।
प्रतिबन्धः प्रकर्तव्यो यातुः पदमनामयम् ॥ ^mula

tattveśvara tvayā nāsya putrakasya śivājñayā |
pratibandhaḥ prakartavyo yātuḥ padamanāmayam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 08 - Chapter Seventeen#^pg181|p. 161]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=181>))

> “O Lord of (this) principle (fattveśvara)! By Śiva’s command you should not create (any) obstacle for this spiritual son (putraka), who is on his way to the plane free of (all) disease.”
