# Jaroslav Pelikan

Sterling Professor Emeritus of History at Yale, on its faculty from 1962 and once dean of its Graduate School; a historian of Christian doctrine, author of the five-volume *The Christian Tradition* ([Yale Bulletin, 2006](http://archives.news.yale.edu/v34.n30/story26.html)).
