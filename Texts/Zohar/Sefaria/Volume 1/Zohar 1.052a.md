*Volume 1, folio 52a* · Bereshit

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Bereshit 87:277–87:286.*

**Bereshit 87:287** AND THEY HEARD THE VOICE OF THE LORD GOD WALKING IN THE GARDEN. (Note the form *mithalech* (walking) instead of the usual *mehalech*.) ^bereshit-87-287

**Bereshit 88:288** Until he sinned, man was gifted with the wisdom of celestial illumination, and he did not for an instant quit the Tree of Life. But when he was seduced by his desire to know what was below, he weakly followed it until he became separated from the Tree of Life, and knew evil and forsook good: hence the Scripture says ‘for thou art not a God that hath pleasure in wickedness, evil shall not sojourn with thee’ (Ps. 5, 5). He who is drawn after evil may not abide with the Tree of Life. ^bereshit-88-288
