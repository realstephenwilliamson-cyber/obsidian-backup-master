# LXX Psalms 128

← [[LXX Psalms 127]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 129]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 129]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 129]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**128:1** A Song of Degrees. Many a time have they warred against me from my youth, let Israel now say:  ^128-1

**128:2** Many a time have they warred against me from my youth: and yet they prevailed not against me.  ^128-2

**128:3** The sinners wrought upon my back: they prolonged their iniquity.[^1]  ^128-3

**128:4** The righteous Lord has cut asunder the necks of sinners.  ^128-4

**128:5** Let all that hate Sion be put to shame and turned back.  ^128-5

**128:6** Let them be as the grass of the house-tops, which withers before it is plucked up.  ^128-6

**128:7** Wherewith the reaper fills not his hand, nor he that makes up the sheaves, his bosom.  ^128-7

**128:8** Neither do they that go by say, The blessing of the Lord be upon you: we have blessed you in the name of the Lord.  ^128-8

[^1]: 128:3 שיך iniquity, easily read for שוכה a furrow.
