## Mūla

एतावद्भिरसंख्यातैः स्वभावैर्यत्प्रकाशते ।
केऽप्यंशांशिकया तेन विशन्त्यन्ये निरंशतः ॥ ^mula

etāvadbhirasaṃkhyātaiḥ svabhāvairyatprakāśate |
ke'pyaṃśāṃśikayā tena viśantyanye niraṃśataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. II
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 02 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v02 02 - Chapter Two, part 1#^pg30|p. 14]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 02.pdf#page=30>))
>
> He shines as these (four forms) and the countless natures (svabhāva) (of all the means included in them), and so some penetrate (into Him) part by part while others do so (directly fully), not part by part (niraṁśataḥ).
