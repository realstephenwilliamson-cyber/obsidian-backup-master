## God’s blessings in the home

*A Song of Ascents. Of Solomon.*

**127:1** Unless the LORD builds the house, those who build it labor in vain. Unless the LORD guards the city, the guard keeps watch in vain. ^127-1

**127:2** It is in vain that you rise up early and go late to rest, eating the bread of anxious toil, for he gives sleep to his beloved.^[Or *for he provides for his beloved during sleep*] ^127-2

**127:3** Sons are indeed a heritage from the LORD, the fruit of the womb a reward. ^127-3

**127:4** Like arrows in the hand of a warrior are the sons of one’s youth. ^127-4

**127:5** Happy is the man who has his quiver full of them. He shall not be put to shame when he speaks with his enemies in the gate. ^127-5
