## Mūla

फलं सर्वमपूर्णत्वे तत्र तत्र प्रकल्पितम् ।
अकल्पिते हि पूर्णत्वे फलमन्यत्किमुच्यताम् ॥ ^mula

phalaṃ sarvamapūrṇatve tatra tatra prakalpitam |
akalpite hi pūrṇatve phalamanyatkimucyatām || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. III
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 03 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v03 10 - Chapter Four, part 9#^pg324|p. 311]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 03.pdf#page=324>))
>
> When (one’s own consciousness) is not in a state of perfect plenitude (apūrṇatva), all the fruits (of the many spiritual disciplines) are conceived (prakalpita) (to exist in various forms), according to the circumstances (tatra tatra). Once having attained (that true uncreated and) unconceived (akalpita) fullness, pray tell what other fruit is there (beyond that)?
