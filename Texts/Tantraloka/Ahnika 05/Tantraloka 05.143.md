## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 07 - Chapter Five, part 5#^pg209|p. 194]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=209>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 5.144.
>
> 1) In the first awareness (saṁvedana) of the initial sensation of ‘pleasure’ (sukha), 2) the moans of passion (sītkāra), 3) (a pleasing external) entity (sat) (like a woman), propriety (samyak) and equanimity (sāmya), is a touch (sparśa) of the consciousness of the absolute. The fourteenth letter (AU) united in a single nature (with the previous letter S) (represents the act of) entering into the (perpetually) expanding (Central Channel), which runs through and within the three abodes of the heart, throat and lips. (The yogi) intent on the repetition (of the seed-syllable SAUH) unites the two paths of the End of the Twelve with the Heart, while the emission (H) (of the last) part (of SAUH) is uttered. (with 5.142–144)
