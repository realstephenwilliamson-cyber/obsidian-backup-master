## A word of comfort to Baruch

**45:1** The word that the prophet Jeremiah spoke to Baruch son of Neriah, when he wrote these words in a scroll at the dictation of Jeremiah, in the fourth year of King Jehoiakim son of Josiah of Judah: ^45-1

**45:2** Thus says the LORD, the God of Israel, to you, O Baruch: ^45-2

**45:3** You said, “Woe is me! The LORD has added sorrow to my pain; I am weary with my groaning, and I find no rest.” ^45-3

**45:4** Thus you shall say to him, “Thus says the LORD: I am going to break down what I have built and pluck up what I have planted—that is, the whole land. ^45-4

**45:5** And you, do you seek great things for yourself? Do not seek them, for I am going to bring disaster upon all flesh, says the LORD, but I will give you your life as a prize of war in every place to which you may go.” ^45-5
