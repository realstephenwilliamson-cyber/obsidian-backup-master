## Mūla

एतावच्छक्तितत्त्वे तु विज्ञेयं खल्वहर्निशम् ।
शक्तिः स्वकालविलये व्यापिन्यां लीयते पुनः ॥ ^mula

etāvacchaktitattve tu vijñeyaṃ khalvaharniśam |

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 12 - Chapter Six, part 5#^pg392|p. 377]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=392>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 6.163cd–164ab.
>
> (When) Sound (Nāda) (comes to the end of its period of existence, it dissolves into the End of Sound – Nādānta). (Then,) having forcefully pierced through the Aperture of Brahmā by the dynamism of the End of Sound (nādāntavrttyā), (it dissolves) into the Principle of Energy (Śaktitattva) when its time comes to an end. One should know that a Day and Night in the Principle of Energy is this much (i.e. Sadāśiva’s lifespan). (with 6.162–163ab)
>
> When Energy (Śakti) reaches the end of its period (of existence), it dissolves into the Pervasive One (Vyāpinī), whose Day and Night is that (Energy’s lifespan). (The Pervasive One dissolves) into Śiva Without Support (anāśrita), whose Day and Night equals a day of Energy multiplied by ten million trillion (parārdhakoṭi). At the end of this period, multiplied by a trillion (parārdha), he too dissolves into the plane With Mind (sāmanasa), termed Equality (Sāmya). (with 6.163cd–165)
