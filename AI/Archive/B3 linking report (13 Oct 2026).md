## How the links are made

- New Testament → `Texts/Bible/New Testament` (`[[John 03#^3-16|John 3:16]]`); Torah verses → the parashah notes; the rest of the Tanakh → the JPS notes (Hebrew numbering); deuterocanonical books and references marked LXX → `Texts/Bible/LXX`; Zohar folios → the note holding `^z<part>-<folio>` (Pritzker first; the sub-work named in the sentence, e.g. Tosefta, when there is one); Tantrāloka → `Tantraloka NN.NNN`; Avot → `Pirkei Avot`.
- The text keeps its wording: only the reference becomes a link with the printed form as its label. Inside tables the pipe is written `\|`.
- **Numbering.** Books in Judaica are read as Hebrew-numbered; for every other book the linker looks for evidence (a verse that exists only in one numbering, or a printed "[English 23:3]"). English-numbered references are moved to the Hebrew verse with the Copenhagen Alliance English→Hebrew table (CC BY-SA 4.0). The vault's Torah notes divide the Ten Commandments as Sefaria does (Exod 20:13 and Deut 5:17 hold four commandments), so English and BHS Exod 20:14–26 and Deut 5:18–33 are moved too. A reference that would move is linked only when the book is shown to be English-numbered (or Hebrew/BHS for the Decalogue); otherwise it is left for you.
- Church Father translations (Maximus, Constas, Berthold, Sherwood, the Catena): Psalms, Samuel/Kings and Greek Daniel are left for you (LXX book names and numbering). Psalm references near Augustine, Jerome, the Vulgate or the Septuagint are left too.
