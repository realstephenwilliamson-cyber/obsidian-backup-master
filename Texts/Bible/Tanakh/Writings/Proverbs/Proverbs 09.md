# Proverbs 09

## Chapter 9

**9:1** Wisdom has built her house,  
She has hewn her seven pillars. ^9-1

**9:2** She has prepared the feast,  
Mixed the wine,  
And also set the table. ^9-2

**9:3** She has sent out her maids to announce  
On the heights of the town, ^9-3

**9:4** “Let the simple enter here”;  
To those devoid of sense she says, ^9-4

**9:5** “Come, eat my food  
And drink the wine that I have mixed; ^9-5

**9:6** Give up simpleness and live,  
Walk in the way of understanding.” ^9-6

**9:7** To correct a scoffer,  
Or rebuke a wicked man for his blemish,  
Is to call down abuse on oneself.[^1] ^9-7

**9:8** Do not rebuke a scoffer, for he will hate you;  
Reprove a wise man, and he will love you. ^9-8

**9:9** Instruct a wise man, and he will grow wiser;  
Teach a righteous man, and he will gain in learning. ^9-9

**9:10** The beginning of wisdom is fear of the LORD,  
And knowledge of the Holy One is understanding. ^9-10

**9:11** For through me your days will increase,  
And years be added to your life. ^9-11

**9:12** If you are wise, you are wise for yourself;  
If you are a scoffer, you bear it alone. ^9-12

**9:13** The stupid woman bustles about;  
She is simple and knows nothing. ^9-13

**9:14** She sits in the doorway of her house,  
Or on a chair at the heights of the town, ^9-14

**9:15** Calling to all the wayfarers  
Who go about their own affairs, ^9-15

**9:16** “Let the simple enter here”;  
And to those devoid of sense she says, ^9-16

**9:17** “Stolen waters are sweet,  
And bread eaten furtively is tasty.” ^9-17

**9:18** He does not know that the shades are there,  
That her guests are in the depths of Sheol. ^9-18

[^1]: Clauses transposed for clarity.
