# Psalm 81 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 080|← Psalm 80]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 082|Psalm 82 →]]

## God’s appeal to stubborn Israel

*To the leader: according to The Gittith. Of Asaph.*

**81:1** Sing aloud to God our strength; shout for joy to the God of Jacob. ^81-1

**81:2** Raise a song; sound the tambourine, the sweet lyre with the harp. ^81-2

**81:3** Blow the trumpet at the new moon, at the full moon, on our festal day. ^81-3

**81:4** For it is a statute for Israel, an ordinance of the God of Jacob. ^81-4

**81:5** He made it a decree in Joseph, when he went out over^[Or *against*] the land of Egypt. I hear a voice I had not known: ^81-5

**81:6** “I relieved your^[Heb *his*] shoulder of the burden; your^[Heb *his*] hands were freed from the basket. ^81-6

**81:7** In distress you called, and I rescued you; I answered you in the secret place of thunder; I tested you at the waters of Meribah. *Selah* ^81-7

**81:8** Hear, O my people, while I admonish you; O Israel, if you would but listen to me! ^81-8

**81:9** There shall be no strange god among you; you shall not bow down to a foreign god. ^81-9

**81:10** I am the LORD your God, who brought you up out of the land of Egypt. Open your mouth wide, and I will fill it. ^81-10

**81:11** “But my people did not listen to my voice; Israel would not submit to me. ^81-11

**81:12** So I gave them over to their stubborn hearts, to follow their own counsels. ^81-12

**81:13** O that my people would listen to me, that Israel would walk in my ways! ^81-13

**81:14** Then I would quickly subdue their enemies and turn my hand against their foes. ^81-14

**81:15** Those who hate the LORD would cringe before him, and their doom would last forever. ^81-15

**81:16** I would feed you^[Cn: Heb *he would feed him*] with the finest of the wheat, and with honey from the rock I would satisfy you.” ^81-16
