## Chapter 46

**46:1** For the leader. Of the Korahites;  
on *alamoth*.[^1] A song. ^46-1

**46:2** God is our refuge and stronghold,  
a help in trouble, very near. ^46-2

**46:3** Therefore we are not afraid  
though the earth reels,  
though mountains topple into the sea— ^46-3

**46:4** its waters rage and foam;  
in its swell mountains quake. *Selah*. ^46-4

**46:5** There is a river whose streams gladden God’s city,  
the holy dwelling-place of the Most High. ^46-5

**46:6** God is in its midst, it will not be toppled;  
by daybreak God will come to its aid. ^46-6

**46:7** Nations rage, kingdoms topple;  
at the sound of His thunder the earth dissolves. ^46-7

**46:8** The LORD of hosts is with us;  
the God of Jacob is our haven. *Selah*. ^46-8

**46:9** Come and see what the LORD has done,  
how He has wrought desolation on the earth. ^46-9

**46:10** He puts a stop to wars throughout the earth,  
breaking the bow, snapping the spear,  
consigning wagons to the flames. ^46-10

**46:11** “Desist! Realize that I am God!  
I dominate the nations;  
I dominate the earth.” ^46-11

**46:12** The LORD of hosts is with us;  
the God of Jacob is our haven. *Selah*. ^46-12

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
