Four-part PDF set by Navjivan Rastogi on the Krama tradition.

Not to be confused with Rastogi's other, separate books also in the library: *Introduction to the Tantrāloka: A Study in Structure* and *The Krama Tantricism of Kashmir, Vol. I* — both single-volume works, catalogued on their own.

**Checked against the file** on 7 Oct 2026: still in review, because the four files start at Part Two of the work, on p. 352, so pp. 1–351 and any title page are not in them (the part-title, read from the OCR text since the page images render blank, is 'Metaphysics and Tantric Esotericism … Krama: An Analytical Exposition', with the word before Krama unreadable), and the opening pages of the files give no year, publisher or this note's subtitle.

**Text layer of all 4 files** (5 Oct 2026): OCR text added (Latin-script and Devanagari models, so diacritics and Devanagari come through); the page images are unchanged. The scans without text went with the trash when it was emptied on 6 Oct 2026.

**Settled** (25 Oct 2026, from library catalogues and the web; Open items): there is no year or publisher to find: the work is unpublished. The Internet Archive item that made it public (uploaded by *indologicalbooks*, 24 Aug 2012, in four parts) and eGangotri's post describe it as an unpublished work of Rastogi's, written as a sequel to his *The Krama Tantricism of Kashmir*, under the title *Metaphysics and Tantric Esotericism of Krama: An Analytical Exposition* ([archive.org](https://archive.org/details/MetaphysicsAndTantricEsotericismOfKramaAnAnalyticalExposition-); [eGangotri](https://egangotri.org/2012/08/24/metaphysics-and-tantric-esotericism-of-krama-an-analytical-exposition-navjivan-rastogi/)). The subtitle now follows that title and the part-title in the files; the earlier subtitle is in neither.
