## Mūla

प्राहुरावरणं तच्च शक्त्यन्तं यावदस्ति हि ।
यद्यपि प्राक् शिवाख्येऽपि तत्त्वे भुवनपद्धतिः ॥ ^mula

prāhurāvaraṇaṃ tacca śaktyantaṃ yāvadasti hi |
yadyapi prāk śivākhye'pi tattve bhuvanapaddhatiḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 02 - Chapter Eleven, part 1#^pg27|p. 7]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=27>))
>
> (The wise) say that the (Cosmic) Egg is an enveloping covering (āvaraṇa) that (extends) up to (the Principle of) Power, and is responsible for the division of the world orders into groups (up to that Principle). (with 11.12cd–13ab)
>
> (The Egg of Power does not extend further) because even though a series of world-orders was previously said to be present in the principle called Śiva, it offers no resistance to penetration (apratigha), and so no enveloping covering is possible within it. (with 11.13cd–14ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* नन्वत्र भुवनानां विभागेनावस्थानमस्ति तत्कारणमण्डरूपमावरणं च नास्ति, -- इत्यपूर्वमिदमभिधानम् -- इत्याह --

> यद्यपि प्राक् शिवाख्येऽपि तत्त्वे भुवनपद्धतिः ॥
> उक्ता तथाप्यप्रतिघे नास्मिन्नावृतिसंभवः ।

"शिवाख्येऽपि तत्त्वे"इति तात्स्थ्यात् व्यापिन्यादावपि -- इत्यर्थः । "अप्रतिघे"इति विश्वोत्तीर्णतया शून्यरूपत्वात्, आवृत्यभावे चायमेव विशेषणद्वारेण हेतुः ॥ १३ ॥
