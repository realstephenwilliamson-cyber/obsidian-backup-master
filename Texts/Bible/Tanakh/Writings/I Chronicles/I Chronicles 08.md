## Chapter 8

**8:1** Benjamin begot Bela his first-born, Ashbel the second, Aharah the third, ^8-1

**8:2** Nohah the fourth, and Rapha the fifth. ^8-2

**8:3** And Bela had sons: Addar, Gera, Abihud, ^8-3

**8:4** Abishua, Naaman, Ahoah, ^8-4

**8:5** Gera, Shephuphan, and Huram. ^8-5

**8:6** These were the sons of Ehud—they were chiefs of clans of the inhabitants of Geba, and they were exiled to Manahath: ^8-6

**8:7** Naaman, Ahijah, and Gera—he exiled them and begot Uzza and Ahihud. ^8-7

**8:8** And Shaharaim had sons in the country of Moab after he had sent away Hushim and Baara his wives. ^8-8

**8:9** He had sons by Hodesh his wife: Jobab, Zibia, Mesha, Malcam, ^8-9

**8:10** Jeuz, Sachiah, and Mirmah. These were his sons, chiefs of clans. ^8-10

**8:11** He also begot by Hushim: Abitub and Elpaal. ^8-11

**8:12** The sons of Elpaal: Eber, Misham, and Shemed, who built Ono and Lod with its dependencies, ^8-12

**8:13** and Beriah and Shema—they were chiefs of clans of the inhabitants of Aijalon, who put to flight the inhabitants of Gath; ^8-13

**8:14** and Ahio, Shashak, and Jeremoth. ^8-14

**8:15** Zebadiah, Arad, Eder, ^8-15

**8:16** Michael, Ishpah, and Joha were sons of Beriah. ^8-16

**8:17** Zebadiah, Meshullam, Hizki, Heber, ^8-17

**8:18** Ishmerai, Izliah, and Jobab were the sons of Elpaal. ^8-18

**8:19** Jakim, Zichri, Zabdi, ^8-19

**8:20** Elienai, Zillethai, Eliel, ^8-20

**8:21** Adaiah, Beraiah, and Shimrath were the sons of Shimei. ^8-21

**8:22** Ishpan, Eber, Eliel, ^8-22

**8:23** Abdon, Zichri, Hanan, ^8-23

**8:24** Hananiah, Elam, Anthothiah, ^8-24

**8:25** Iphdeiah, and Penuel were the sons of Shashak. ^8-25

**8:26** Shamsherai, Shehariah, Athaliah, ^8-26

**8:27** Jaareshiah, Elijah, and Zichri were the sons of Jeroham. ^8-27

**8:28** These were the chiefs of the clans, according to their lines. These chiefs dwelt in Jerusalem. ^8-28

**8:29** The father of Gibeon dwelt in Gibeon, and the name of his wife was Maacah. ^8-29

**8:30** His first-born son: Abdon; then Zur, Kish, Baal, Nadab, ^8-30

**8:31** Gedor, Ahio, Zecher. ^8-31

**8:32** Mikloth begot Shimeah. And they dwelt in Jerusalem opposite their kinsmen, with their kinsmen. ^8-32

**8:33** Ner begot Kish, Kish begot Saul, Saul begot Jonathan, Malchi-shua, Abinadab, and Eshbaal; ^8-33

**8:34** and the son of Jonathan was Merib-baal; and Merib-baal begot Micah. ^8-34

**8:35** The sons of Micah: Pithon, Melech, Taarea, and Ahaz. ^8-35

**8:36** Ahaz begot Jehoaddah; and Jehoaddah begot Alemeth, Azmaveth, and Zimri; Zimri begot Moza. ^8-36

**8:37** Moza begot Binea; his son Raphah; his son Eleasah, his son Azel. ^8-37

**8:38** Azel had six sons, and these are their names: Azrikam, Bocheru, Ishmael, Sheariah, Obadiah, and Hanan. All these were the sons of Azel. ^8-38

**8:39** The sons of Eshek his brother: Ulam his first-born, Jeush the second, and Eliphelet the third. ^8-39

**8:40** The descendants of Ulam—men of substance, who drew the bow, had many children and grandchildren—one hundred and fifty; all these were Benjaminites. ^8-40
