## Mūla

स चैष परमेशानशुद्धविद्याविजृम्भितम् ।
अस्य भेदाश्च बहवो निर्भित्तिः सहभित्तिकः ॥ ^mula

sa caiṣa parameśānaśuddhavidyāvijṛmbhitam |
asya bhedāśca bahavo nirbhittiḥ sahabhittikaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. III
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 03 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v03 03 - Chapter Four, part 2#^pg72|p. 59]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 03.pdf#page=72>))
>
> A cause that is not commonly known to everyone is what is meant by the expression ‘without cause’. (In actual fact, the cause in this case is just) the unfolding of the Supreme Lord’s Pure Knowledge. (with 4.46cd–47ab)
>
> There are many types of (teacher, who belong to two principal categories, namely, those) who need no support (of a teacher or scripture) (nirbhitti) and (those) who do (sahabhittika). (The latter is of two types, according as to whether the support is applied) everywhere (sarvaga) or (partially) to an aspect (aṛhśagata). Again, (the latter is of two kinds, according to whether) the aspect is established in primary or secondary (mukhyāmukhya) (sources). The support is essentially dependence on something else, and that other thing is (one’s own spiritual) insight (prajñā) or its product (that is, these teachings). (with 4.47cd–48)
