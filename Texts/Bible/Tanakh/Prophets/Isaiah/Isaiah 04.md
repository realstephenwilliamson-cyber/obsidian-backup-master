## Chapter 4

**4:1** In that day, seven women shall take hold of one man, saying,  
“We will eat our own food  
And wear our own clothes;  
Only let us be called by your name—  
Take away our disgrace!” ^4-1

**4:2** [^1]In that day,  
The radiance of the LORD  
Will lend beauty and glory,  
And the splendor of the land[^2]  
[Will give] dignity and majesty,  
To the survivors of Israel. ^4-2

**4:3** And those who remain in Zion  
And are left in Jerusalem—  
All who are inscribed for life in Jerusalem—  
Shall be called holy. ^4-3

**4:4** When my Lord has washed away  
The filth of the daughters of Zion,[^3]  
And from Jerusalem’s midst  
Has rinsed out her infamy—  
In a spirit of judgment  
And in a spirit of purging— ^4-4

**4:5** the LORD will create[^4] over the whole shrine and meeting place of Mount Zion cloud by day and smoke with a glow of flaming fire by night. Indeed, over all the glory[^5] shall hang a canopy, ^4-5

**4:6** which shall serve as a pavilion for shade from heat by day and as a shelter for protection against drenching rain. ^4-6

[^1]: For the interpretation of this verse, cf. 28.5. For “radiance,” cf. Septuagint and the Syriac ṣemḥa, and for “splendor,” cf. the meaning of peri in 10.12.
[^2]: Emendation yields “my Lord”; cf. the parallelism (in reverse order) in 3.17.
[^3]: Emendation yields “Daughter Zion,” i.e., Zion personified; cf. 1.8 and note.
[^4]: Emendation yields “spread”; cf. Ps. 105.39.
[^5]: Emendation yields “His whole shrine.”
