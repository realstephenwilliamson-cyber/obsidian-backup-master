## Mūla

अकुलीनेषु संपर्कात्तत्कुलात्पतनाद्भयम् ।
एकपात्रे कुलाम्नाये तस्मात्तान्परिवर्जयेत् ॥ ^mula

akulīneṣu saṃparkāttatkulātpatanādbhayam |
ekapātre kulāmnāye tasmāttānparivarjayet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 10 - Chapter Fifteen, part 9#^pg352|p. 334]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=352>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 15.574cd–575ab.
>
> By contact with those who do not belong to (the same) Kula, there is fear of a fall from that (which is one’s) Kula. A Kula tradition is exclusive (ekapātra), and so one must avoid those (who belong to other ones). However, if by mistake one has made friends (with a member of another tradition, and takes) part in (his) gathering (goṣṭhi), one should worship the assembly (cakra) (of deities and initiates there along with them). (with 15.572–573ab)
