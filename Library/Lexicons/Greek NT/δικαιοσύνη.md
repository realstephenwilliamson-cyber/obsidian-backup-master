# δικαιοσύνη

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · noun

**Dodson:** justice, justness, righteousness — (usually if not always in a Jewish atmosphere), justice, justness, righteousness, righteousness of which God is the source or author, but practically: a divine righteousness. (Strong's 1343; headword δικαιοσύνη, ης, ἡ)
