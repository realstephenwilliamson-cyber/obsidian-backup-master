## Chapter 6

**6:1** Flee for refuge, O people of Benjamin,  
Out of the midst of Jerusalem!  
Blow the horn in Tekoa,  
Set up a signal at Beth-haccerem!  
For evil is appearing from the north,  
And great disaster. ^6-1

**6:2** Fair Zion, the lovely and delicate,  
I will destroy.[^1] ^6-2

**6:3** Against her come shepherds with their flocks,  
They pitch tents all around her;  
Each grazes the sheep under his care.[^2] ^6-3

**6:4** Prepare for[^3] battle against her:  
“Up! we will attack at noon.”  
“Alas for us! for day is declining,  
The shadows of evening grow long.” ^6-4

**6:5** “Up! let us attack by night,  
And wreck her fortresses.” ^6-5

**6:6** For thus said the LORD of Hosts:  
Hew down her trees,  
And raise a siegemound against Jerusalem.  
She is the city destined for punishment;[^4]  
Only fraud is found in her midst. ^6-6

**6:7** As a well flows with water,  
So she flows with wickedness.  
Lawlessness and rapine are heard in her;  
Before Me constantly are sickness and wounds. ^6-7

**6:8** Accept rebuke, O Jerusalem,  
Lest I come to loathe you,  
Lest I make you a desolation,  
An uninhabited land. ^6-8

**6:9** Thus said the LORD of Hosts:  
Let them glean[^5] over and over, as a vine,  
The remnant of Israel.  
Pass your hand again,  
Like a vintager,  
Over its branches. ^6-9

**6:10** [^6]To whom shall I speak,  
Give warning that they may hear?  
Their ears are blocked  
And they cannot listen.  
See, the word of the LORD has become for them  
An object of scorn; they will have none of it. ^6-10

**6:11** But I am filled with the wrath of the LORD,  
I cannot hold it in.  
Pour it on the infant in the street,  
And on the company of youths gathered together!  
Yes, men and women alike shall be captured,  
Elders and those of advanced years. ^6-11

**6:12** Their houses shall pass to others,  
Fields and wives as well,  
For I will stretch out My arm  
Against the inhabitants of the country  
—declares the LORD. ^6-12

**6:13** For from the smallest to the greatest,  
They are all greedy for gain;  
Priest and prophet alike,  
They all act falsely. ^6-13

**6:14** They offer healing offhand  
For the wounds of My people,  
Saying, “All is well, all is well,”  
When nothing is well. ^6-14

**6:15** They have acted shamefully;  
They have done abhorrent things—  
Yet they do not feel shame,  
And they cannot be made to blush.  
Assuredly, they shall fall among the falling,  
They shall stumble at the time when I punish them  
—said the LORD. ^6-15

**6:16** Thus said the LORD:  
Stand by the roads and consider,  
Inquire about ancient paths:  
Which is the road to happiness?  
Travel it, and find tranquillity for yourselves.  
But they said, “We will not.” ^6-16

**6:17** And I raised up watchmen[^7] for you:  
“Hearken to the sound of the horn!”  
But they said, “We will not.” ^6-17

**6:18** Hear well, O nations,  
And know, O community, what is in store for them.[^8] ^6-18

**6:19** Hear, O earth!  
I am going to bring disaster upon this people,  
The outcome of their own schemes;  
For they would not hearken to My words,  
And they rejected My Instruction. ^6-19

**6:20** What need have I of frankincense  
That comes from Sheba,  
Or fragrant cane from a distant land?  
Your burnt offerings are not acceptable  
And your sacrifices are not pleasing to Me. ^6-20

**6:21** Assuredly, thus said the LORD:  
I shall put before this people stumbling blocks  
Over which they shall stumble—  
Fathers and children alike,  
Neighbor and friend shall perish. ^6-21

**6:22** Thus said the LORD:  
See, a people comes from the northland,  
A great nation is roused  
From the remotest parts of the earth. ^6-22

**6:23** They grasp the bow and javelin;  
They are cruel, they show no mercy;  
The sound of them is like the roaring sea.  
They ride upon horses,  
Accoutered like a man for battle,  
Against you, O Fair Zion! ^6-23

**6:24** “We have heard the report of them,  
Our hands fail;  
Pain seizes us,  
Agony like a woman in childbirth. ^6-24

**6:25** Do not go out into the country,  
Do not walk the roads!  
For the sword of the enemy is there,  
Terror on every side.” ^6-25

**6:26** My poor people,[^9]  
Put on sackcloth  
And strew dust on yourselves!  
Mourn, as for an only child;  
Wail bitterly,  
For suddenly the destroyer  
Is coming upon us. ^6-26

**6:27** I have made you an assayer of My people  
—A refiner[^10]—  
You are to note and assay their ways. ^6-27

**6:28** They are copper and iron:  
They are all stubbornly defiant;  
They deal basely[^11]  
All of them act corruptly. ^6-28

**6:29** The bellows puff;  
The lead is consumed by fire.  
Yet the smelter smelts to no purpose—  
The dross[^12] is not separated out. ^6-29

**6:30** They are called “rejected silver,”  
For the LORD has rejected them. ^6-30

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Understanding yado as in Ps. 95.7.
[^3]: Lit. “Consecrate.”
[^4]: Emendation yields “She is the city of falseness.”
[^5]: Emendation yields “Glean” (singular).
[^6]: The prophet speaks.
[^7]: I.e., prophets.
[^8]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^9]: Lit. “Daughter that is My people”; so, frequently, in poetry. See 4.11 and note.
[^10]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^11]: See note at Lev. 19.16.
[^12]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
