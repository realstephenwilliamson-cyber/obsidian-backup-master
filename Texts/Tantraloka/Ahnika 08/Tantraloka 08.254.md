## Mūla

तत्त्वं तत्र तु संक्षुब्धा गुणाः प्रसुवते धियम् ।
न वैषम्यमनापन्नं कारणं कार्यसूतये ॥ ^mula

tattvaṃ tatra tu saṃkṣubdhā guṇāḥ prasuvate dhiyam |
na vaiṣamyamanāpannaṃ kāraṇaṃ kāryasūtaye || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 08 - Chapter Eight, part 6#^pg282|p. 264]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=282>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 8.254cd–255ab.
>
> The principle called the qualities (guṇa) is located above the intellect, (but is) below Nature (prakṛti).). There indeed the disturbed qualities generate the intellect. (with 8.253cd–254ab)
>
> A cause that is not out of (its basic) state of balance (is not ready) to generate its effect. (with 8.254cd)
