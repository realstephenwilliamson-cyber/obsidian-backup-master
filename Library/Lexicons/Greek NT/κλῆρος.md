# κλῆρος

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · noun

**Dodson:** a lot, portion — (a) a lot, (b) a portion assigned; hence: a portion of the people of God assigned to one's care, a congregation. (Strong's 2819; headword κλῆρος, ου, ὁ)
