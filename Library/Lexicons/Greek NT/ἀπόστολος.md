# ἀπόστολος

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · noun

**Dodson:** an apostle, a messenger, an envoy, a delegate — a messenger, envoy, delegate, one commissioned by another to represent him in some way, especially a man sent out by Jesus Christ Himself to preach the Gospel; an apostle. (Strong's 652; headword ἀπόστολος, ου, ὁ)
