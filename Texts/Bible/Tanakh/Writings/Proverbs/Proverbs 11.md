# Proverbs 11

Compare: [[NRSVue Proverbs 11|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 11

**11:1** False scales are an abomination to the LORD;  
An honest[^1] weight pleases Him. ^11-1

**11:2** When arrogance appears, disgrace follows,  
But wisdom is with those who are unassuming. ^11-2

**11:3** The integrity of the upright guides them;  
The deviousness of the treacherous leads them to ruin. ^11-3

**11:4** Wealth is of no avail on the day of wrath,  
But righteousness saves from death. ^11-4

**11:5** The righteousness of the blameless man smooths his way,  
But the wicked man is felled by his wickedness. ^11-5

**11:6** The righteousness of the upright saves them,  
But the treacherous are trapped by their malice. ^11-6

**11:7** At death the hopes of a wicked man are doomed,  
And the ambition of evil men comes to nothing. ^11-7

**11:8** The righteous man is rescued from trouble  
And the wicked man takes his place. ^11-8

**11:9** The impious man destroys his neighbor through speech,  
But through their knowledge the righteous are rescued. ^11-9

**11:10** When the righteous prosper the city exults;  
When the wicked perish there are shouts of joy. ^11-10

**11:11** A city is built up by the blessing of the upright,  
But it is torn down by the speech of the wicked. ^11-11

**11:12** He who speaks contemptuously of his fellowman is devoid of sense;  
A prudent man keeps his peace. ^11-12

**11:13** A base fellow gives away secrets,  
But a trustworthy soul keeps a confidence. ^11-13

**11:14** For want of strategy an army falls,  
But victory comes with much planning. ^11-14

**11:15** Harm awaits him who stands surety for another;[^2]  
He who spurns pledging shall be secure. ^11-15

**11:16** A graceful woman obtains honor;  
Ruthless men obtain wealth. ^11-16

**11:17** A kindly man benefits himself;  
A cruel man makes trouble for himself. ^11-17

**11:18** The wicked man earns illusory wages,  
But he who sows righteousness has a true reward. ^11-18

**11:19** Righteousness is a prop of life,  
But to pursue evil leads to death. ^11-19

**11:20** Men of crooked mind are an abomination to the LORD,  
But those whose way is blameless please Him. ^11-20

**11:21** Assuredly,[^3] the evil man will not escape,  
But the offspring of the righteous will be safe. ^11-21

**11:22** Like a gold ring in the snout of a pig  
Is a beautiful woman bereft of sense. ^11-22

**11:23** What the righteous desire can only be good;  
What the wicked hope for [stirs] wrath. ^11-23

**11:24** One man gives generously and ends with more;  
Another stints on doing the right thing and incurs a loss. ^11-24

**11:25** A generous person enjoys prosperity;  
He who satisfies others shall himself be sated. ^11-25

**11:26** He who withholds grain earns the curses of the people,  
But blessings are on the head of the one who dispenses it. ^11-26

**11:27** He who earnestly seeks what is good pursues what is pleasing;  
He who is bent on evil, upon him it shall come. ^11-27

**11:28** He who trusts in his wealth shall fall,  
But the righteous shall flourish like foliage. ^11-28

**11:29** He who makes trouble for his household shall inherit the wind;  
A fool is a slave to the wise-hearted. ^11-29

**11:30** The fruit of the righteous is a tree of life;  
A wise man captivates people. ^11-30

**11:31** If the righteous on earth get their deserts,  
How much more the wicked man and the sinner. ^11-31

[^1]: Lit. “whole.”
[^2]: Or “a stranger.”
[^3]: Lit. “Hand to hand”; meaning of Heb. uncertain.
