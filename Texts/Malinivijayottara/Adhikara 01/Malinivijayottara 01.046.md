## Mūla

योगदीक्षां समासाद्य ज्ञात्वा योगं समभ्यसेत् ।
योगसिद्धिमवाप्नोति तदन्ते शाश्वतं पदम् ॥ ^mula

yogadīkṣāṁ samāsādya jñātvā yogaṁ samabhyaset |
yogasiddhim avāpnoti tadante śāśvataṁ padam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2022 – June 2022
> p. 48 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=24>))
>
> (Another type of aspirant,) having obtained initiation into yoga and understood (it), should practice yoga. (Thus, he first) attains the accomplishment (*siddhi*) (that comes from) yoga, and at the end of that, the eternal plane (of existence).
