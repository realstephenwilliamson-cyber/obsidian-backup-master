## Mūla

ते मन्त्रादित्वमापन्नाः कथं कर्मानपेक्षिणः ।
मैवं तथाविधोत्तीर्णशिवध्यानजपादिषु ॥ ^mula

te mantrāditvamāpannāḥ kathaṃ karmānapekṣiṇaḥ |
maivaṃ tathāvidhottīrṇaśivadhyānajapādiṣu || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 09 - Chapter Thirteen, part 5#^pg308|p. 286]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=308>))
>
> Surely, (someone may object that) they have attained the state of Mantra and the other (higher level subjects) by worship, the repetition (of Mantras), meditation, attending on iva and the like. So how are they independent of Karma? (with 13.259cd–260ab)
>
> (I reply that) that is not so. Let us begin by examining what moves them initially (to practice) the repetition (of Mantras) and this kind of meditation on Śiva, Who transcends (Karma) and the like. (with 13.260cd–261ab)

*Lead-in:* एतदेव परिहरति -

> मैवं तथाविधोत्तीर्णशिवध्यानजपादिषु ॥
> प्रवृत्तिरेव प्रथममेषां कस्माद्विविच्यताम् ।

इह तावदिदमेव चिन्त्यतां यदेषां शुद्धात्मनां प्रथममेव तथाविधान्मायान्तश्चारिणः कर्मादेरुत्तीर्णस्तदपरामृष्टो योऽयं शिवस्तद्विषयेषु ध्यानजपादिषु कस्मात्प्रवृत्तिः किं तत्र निमित्तम् -- इत्यर्थः ॥
