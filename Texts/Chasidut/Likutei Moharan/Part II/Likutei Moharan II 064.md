# Likutei Moharan II 064

← [[Likutei Moharan II 063]] · [[Likutei Moharan]] · [[Likutei Moharan II 065]] →

*Part II · Lesson 64* · [[Likutei Moharan]]

## 1

We see that the super-wealthy, almost without exception, are real eccentrics. As we observe empirically, anyone who is [super-]wealthy has a great *mishegaas* .

Know! the money makes him crazy. This is because the money stems from the fallen wealth of the prophets. For all the prophets were wealthy ([[Nedarim 38#38a|Nedarim 38a]]). When the prophetic spirit came over the prophet, it was as if he was mad. As Rashi explains: “and he prophesied” ([[I Samuel 18#^18-10|1 Samuel 18:10]])—he spoke deliriously.

In the case of the aforementioned super-wealthy, the money creates genuine insanity. Consequently, their wealth makes them eccentric.
