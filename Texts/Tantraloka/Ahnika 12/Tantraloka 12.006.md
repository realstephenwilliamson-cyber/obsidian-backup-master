## Mūla

एवं विश्वाध्वसंपूर्णं कालव्यापारचित्रितम् ।
देशकालमयस्पन्दसद्म देहं विलोकयेत् ॥ ^mula

evaṃ viśvādhvasaṃpūrṇaṃ kālavyāpāracitritam |
deśakālamayaspandasadma dehaṃ vilokayet || ^iast

## Translation

> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 04 - Chapter Twelve#^pg122|p. 102]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=122>))

> One should view the body in this way as full of all the paths (of manifestation), wonderfully diversified by the activity of time, as the abode (sadman) of the pulse (spanda) which is time and space.
