## Mūla

देहान्ते शिव एवेति नास्य देहान्तरस्थितिः ।
येऽपि तत्त्वावतीर्णानां शङ्कराज्ञानुवर्तिनाम् ॥ ^mula

dehānte śiva eveti nāsya dehāntarasthitiḥ |
ye'pi tattvāvatīrṇānāṃ śaṅkarājñānuvartinām || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 05 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 3#^pg122|p. 105]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=122>))
>
> Thus (by virtue of this grace), the one whose contraction has been burnt away by initiation (dīkṣā) and the knowledge of Śiva, when (he dies and his) body comes to an end, he becomes Śiva Himself, and does not (incarnate anymore) in other bodies. (with 28.236cd–237ab)
>
> There are those who die in the abodes (gṛha) of (Liṅgas) that have descended, in accord with Śiva’s command, from (higher) principles. These include, amongst others, (Liṅgas) that are spontaneously born of themselves (svayambhū), (as well as those installed) by ascetics (muni), Vedic seers (devarṣi) or by (pious) people. (Those who die there) attain their (corresponding) world, and are initiated by the Lord of (that) world, in due order. Whether they descend or not into the mortal (world), they are without further rebirth, and attain Śiva. (with 28.237cd–239ab)
