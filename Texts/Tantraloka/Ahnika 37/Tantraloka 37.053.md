## Mūla

तस्यान्वये महति कोऽपि वराहगुप्त-
नामा बभूव भगवान् स्वयमन्तकाले ।
गीर्वाणसिन्धुलहरीकलिताग्रमूर्धा
यस्याकरोत् परमनुग्रहमाग्रहेण ॥ ^mula

tasyānvaye mahati ko'pi varāhagupta-
nāmā babhūva bhagavān svayamantakāle |
gīrvāṇasindhulaharīkalitāgramūrdhā
yasyākarot paramanugrahamāgraheṇa || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 11 - Chapter Thirty-Seven, part 1#^pg268|p. 250]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=268>))
>
> Within his great lineage, there was a certain (descendent) called Varāhagupta. The Lord, the top of whose head is adorned by the waves of (the Ganges,) the river of the gods, by his affection (for him,) spontaneously bestowed upon him, at the point of death, his supreme grace.
