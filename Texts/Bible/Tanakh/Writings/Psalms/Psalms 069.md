# Psalms 069

Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 069]] · Septuagint (Brenton): [[LXX Psalms 68]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

Compare: [[NRSVue Psalms 069|NRSVue]] (Heb 69:1 = superscription; Heb 69:2–37 = NRSVue 69:1–36)

## Chapter 69

**69:1** For the leader. On *shoshannim*.[^1] Of David. ^69-1

**69:2** Deliver me, O God,  
for the waters have reached my neck; ^69-2

**69:3** I am sinking into the slimy deep  
and find no foothold;  
I have come into the watery depths;  
the flood sweeps me away. ^69-3

**69:4** I am weary with calling;  
my throat is dry;  
my eyes fail  
while I wait for God. ^69-4

**69:5** More numerous than the hairs of my head  
are those who hate me without reason;  
many are those who would destroy me,  
my treacherous enemies.  
Must I restore what I have not stolen? ^69-5

**69:6** God, You know my folly;  
my guilty deeds are not hidden from You. ^69-6

**69:7** Let those who look to You,  
O Lord, GOD of hosts,  
not be disappointed on my account;  
let those who seek You,  
O God of Israel,  
not be shamed because of me. ^69-7

**69:8** It is for Your sake that I have been reviled,  
that shame covers my face; ^69-8

**69:9** I am a stranger to my brothers,  
an alien to my kin. ^69-9

**69:10** My zeal for Your house has been my undoing;  
the reproaches of those who revile You have fallen upon me. ^69-10

**69:11** When I wept and fasted,  
I was reviled for it. ^69-11

**69:12** I made sackcloth my garment;  
I became a byword among them. ^69-12

**69:13** Those who sit in the gate talk about me;  
I am the taunt of drunkards. ^69-13

**69:14** As for me, may my prayer come to You, O LORD,  
at a favorable moment;  
O God, in Your abundant faithfulness,  
answer me with Your sure deliverance. ^69-14

**69:15** Rescue me from the mire;  
let me not sink;  
let me be rescued from my enemies,  
and from the watery depths. ^69-15

**69:16** Let the floodwaters not sweep me away;  
let the deep not swallow me;  
let the mouth of the Pit not close over me. ^69-16

**69:17** Answer me, O LORD,  
according to Your great steadfastness;  
in accordance with Your abundant mercy  
turn to me; ^69-17

**69:18** do not hide Your face from Your servant,  
for I am in distress;  
answer me quickly. ^69-18

**69:19** Come near to me and redeem me;  
free me from my enemies. ^69-19

**69:20** You know my reproach,  
my shame, my disgrace;  
You are aware of all my foes. ^69-20

**69:21** Reproach breaks my heart,  
I am in despair;[^2]  
I hope for consolation, but there is none,  
for comforters, but find none. ^69-21

**69:22** They give me gall for food,  
vinegar to quench my thirst. ^69-22

**69:23** May their table be a trap for them,  
a snare for their allies. ^69-23

**69:24** May their eyes grow dim so that they cannot see;  
may their loins collapse continually. ^69-24

**69:25** Pour out Your wrath on them;  
may Your blazing anger overtake them; ^69-25

**69:26** may their encampments be desolate;  
may their tents stand empty. ^69-26

**69:27** For they persecute those You have struck;  
they talk about the pain of those You have felled. ^69-27

**69:28** Add that to their guilt;  
let them have no share of Your beneficence; ^69-28

**69:29** may they be erased from the book of life,  
and not be inscribed with the righteous. ^69-29

**69:30** But I am lowly and in pain;  
Your help, O God, keeps me safe. ^69-30

**69:31** I will extol God’s name with song,  
and exalt Him with praise. ^69-31

**69:32** That will please the LORD more than oxen,  
than bulls with horns and hooves. ^69-32

**69:33** The lowly will see and rejoice;  
you who are mindful of God, take heart! ^69-33

**69:34** For the LORD listens to the needy,  
and does not spurn His captives. ^69-34

**69:35** Heaven and earth shall extol Him,  
the seas, and all that moves in them. ^69-35

**69:36** For God will deliver Zion  
and rebuild the cities of Judah;  
they shall live there and inherit it; ^69-36

**69:37** the offspring of His servants shall possess it;  
those who cherish His name shall dwell there. ^69-37

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
