# Job 5 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Job 04|← Job 4]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Job 06|Job 6 →]]

## Job is corrected by God

**5:1** “Call now; is there anyone who will answer you? To which of the holy ones will you turn? ^5-1

**5:2** Surely vexation kills the fool, and jealousy slays the simple. ^5-2

**5:3** I have seen fools taking root, but suddenly I cursed their dwelling. ^5-3

**5:4** Their children are far from safety, they are crushed in the gate, and there is no one to deliver them. ^5-4

**5:5** The hungry eat their harvest, and they take it even out of the thorns,^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] and the thirsty^[Aquila Symmachus Syr Vg: Heb *snare*] pant after their wealth. ^5-5

**5:6** For misery does not come from the earth, nor does trouble sprout from the ground, ^5-6

**5:7** but humans are born to trouble just as sparks^[Or *birds*; Heb *sons of Resheph*] fly upward. ^5-7

**5:8** “As for me, I would seek God, and to God I would commit my cause. ^5-8

**5:9** He does great things and unsearchable, marvelous things without number. ^5-9

**5:10** He gives rain on the earth and sends waters on the fields; ^5-10

**5:11** he sets on high those who are lowly, and those who mourn are lifted to safety. ^5-11

**5:12** He frustrates the devices of the crafty, so that their hands achieve no success. ^5-12

**5:13** He takes the wise in their own craftiness, and the schemes of the wily are brought to a quick end. ^5-13

**5:14** They meet with darkness in the daytime and grope at noonday as in the night. ^5-14

**5:15** But he saves the needy from the sword of their mouth, from the hand of the mighty. ^5-15

**5:16** So the poor have hope, and injustice shuts its mouth. ^5-16

**5:17** “How happy is the one whom God reproves; therefore do not despise the discipline of the Almighty.^[Traditional rendering of Heb *Shaddai*] ^5-17

**5:18** For he wounds, but he binds up; he strikes, but his hands heal. ^5-18

**5:19** He will deliver you from six troubles; in seven no harm shall touch you. ^5-19

**5:20** In famine he will redeem you from death and in war from the power of the sword. ^5-20

**5:21** You shall be hidden from the scourge of the tongue and shall not fear destruction when it comes. ^5-21

**5:22** At destruction and famine you shall laugh and shall not fear the wild animals of the earth. ^5-22

**5:23** For you shall be in league with the stones of the field, and the wild animals shall be at peace with you. ^5-23

**5:24** You shall know that your tent is safe; you shall inspect your fold and miss nothing. ^5-24

**5:25** You shall know that your descendants will be many and your offspring like the grass of the earth. ^5-25

**5:26** You shall come to your grave in ripe old age, as a shock of grain comes up to the threshing floor in its season. ^5-26

**5:27** See, we have searched this out; it is true. Hear, and know it for yourself.” ^5-27
