# Exodus 31 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Exodus 30|← Exodus 30]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Exodus 32|Exodus 32 →]]

## Bezalel and Oholiab

**31:1** The LORD spoke to Moses, ^31-1

**31:2** “See, I have called by name Bezalel son of Uri son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah, ^31-2

**31:3** and I have filled him with a divine spirit,^[Or *with the spirit of God*] with ability, intelligence, and knowledge, and every kind of skill, ^31-3

**31:4** to devise artistic designs, to work in gold, silver, and bronze, ^31-4

**31:5** in cutting stones for setting, and in carving wood, to work in every kind of craft. ^31-5

**31:6** Moreover, I have appointed with him Oholiab son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan, and I have given skill to all the skillful, so that they may make all that I have commanded you: ^31-6

**31:7** the tent of meeting, and the ark of the covenant, and the cover that is on it, and all the furnishings of the tent, ^31-7

**31:8** the table and its utensils, and the pure lampstand with all its utensils, and the altar of incense, ^31-8

**31:9** and the altar of burnt offering with all its utensils, and the basin with its stand, ^31-9

**31:10** and the finely worked vestments, the holy vestments for the priest Aaron and the vestments of his sons, for their service as priests, ^31-10

**31:11** and the anointing oil and the fragrant incense for the holy place. They shall do just as I have commanded you.” ^31-11

## The Sabbath law

**31:12** The LORD said to Moses, ^31-12

**31:13** “You yourself are to speak to the Israelites, ‘You shall surely keep my Sabbaths, for this is a sign between me and you throughout your generations, given in order that you may know that I, the LORD, sanctify you. ^31-13

**31:14** You shall keep the Sabbath, because it is holy for you; everyone who profanes it shall be put to death; whoever does any work on it shall be cut off from among the people. ^31-14

**31:15** Six days shall work be done, but the seventh day is a Sabbath of solemn rest, holy to the LORD; whoever does any work on the Sabbath day shall be put to death. ^31-15

**31:16** Therefore the Israelites shall keep the Sabbath, observing the Sabbath throughout their generations, as a perpetual covenant. ^31-16

**31:17** It is a sign forever between me and the Israelites that in six days the LORD made heaven and earth, and on the seventh day he rested and was refreshed.’ ” ^31-17

## The two tablets of the covenant

**31:18** When God^[Heb *he*] finished speaking with Moses on Mount Sinai, he gave him the two tablets of the covenant, tablets of stone, written with the finger of God. ^31-18
