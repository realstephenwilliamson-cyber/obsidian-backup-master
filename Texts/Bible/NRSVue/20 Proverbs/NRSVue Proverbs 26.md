# Proverbs 26 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Proverbs 25|← Proverbs 25]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Proverbs 27|Proverbs 27 →]]

**26:1** Like snow in summer or rain in harvest, so honor is not fitting for a fool. ^26-1

**26:2** Like a sparrow in its flitting, like a swallow in its flying, an undeserved curse goes nowhere. ^26-2

**26:3** A whip for the horse, a bridle for the donkey, and a rod for the back of fools. ^26-3

**26:4** Do not answer fools according to their folly, lest you be a fool yourself. ^26-4

**26:5** Answer fools according to their folly, lest they be wise in their own eyes. ^26-5

**26:6** It is like cutting off one’s foot and drinking down violence, to send a message by a fool. ^26-6

**26:7** The legs of a lame person hang limp; so does a proverb in the mouth of a fool. ^26-7

**26:8** It is like binding a stone in a sling to give honor to a fool. ^26-8

**26:9** Like a thornbush brandished by the hand of a drunkard is a proverb in the mouth of a fool. ^26-9

**26:10** Like an archer who wounds everybody is one who hires a passing fool or drunkard.^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^26-10

**26:11** Like a dog that returns to its vomit is a fool who reverts to his folly. ^26-11

**26:12** Do you see people wise in their own eyes? There is more hope for fools than for them. ^26-12

**26:13** The lazy person says, “There is a lion in the road! There is a lion in the streets!” ^26-13

**26:14** As a door turns on its hinges, so does a lazy person in bed. ^26-14

**26:15** The lazy person buries a hand in the dish and is too tired to bring it back to the mouth. ^26-15

**26:16** The lazy person is wiser in self-esteem than seven who can answer discreetly. ^26-16

**26:17** Like someone who takes a passing dog by the ears is one who meddles in the quarrel of another. ^26-17

**26:18** Like a maniac who shoots deadly firebrands and arrows, ^26-18

**26:19** so is one who deceives a neighbor and says, “I am only joking!” ^26-19

**26:20** For lack of wood the fire goes out, and where there is no whisperer, quarreling ceases. ^26-20

**26:21** As charcoal is to hot embers and wood to fire, so is a quarrelsome person for kindling strife. ^26-21

**26:22** The words of a whisperer are like delicious morsels; they go down into the inner parts of the body. ^26-22

**26:23** Like the glaze^[Cn: Heb *silver of dross*] covering an earthen vessel are smooth^[Gk: Heb *burning*] lips with an evil heart. ^26-23

**26:24** An enemy dissembles in speaking while harboring deceit within; ^26-24

**26:25** when an enemy speaks graciously, do not believe it, for there are seven abominations concealed within; ^26-25

**26:26** though hatred is covered with guile, the enemy’s wickedness will be exposed in the assembly. ^26-26

**26:27** Whoever digs a pit will fall into it, and a stone will come back on the one who starts it rolling. ^26-27

**26:28** A lying tongue hates its victims, and a flattering mouth works ruin. ^26-28
