# Psalm 102 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 101|← Psalm 101]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 103|Psalm 103 →]]

## Prayer to the eternal king for help

*A prayer of one afflicted, when faint and pleading before the LORD.*

**102:1** Hear my prayer, O LORD; let my cry come to you. ^102-1

**102:2** Do not hide your face from me in the day of my distress. Incline your ear to me; answer me speedily in the day when I call. ^102-2

**102:3** For my days pass away like smoke, and my bones burn like a furnace. ^102-3

**102:4** My heart is stricken and withered like grass; I am too wasted to eat my bread. ^102-4

**102:5** Because of my loud groaning, my bones cling to my skin. ^102-5

**102:6** I am like a desert owl^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] of the wilderness, like a little owl of the waste places. ^102-6

**102:7** I lie awake; I am like a lonely bird on the housetop. ^102-7

**102:8** All day long my enemies taunt me; those who deride me use my name for a curse. ^102-8

**102:9** Indeed, I eat ashes like bread and mingle tears with my drink, ^102-9

**102:10** because of your indignation and anger, for you have lifted me up and thrown me aside. ^102-10

**102:11** My days are like a lengthening shadow; I wither away like grass. ^102-11

**102:12** But you, O LORD, are enthroned forever; your name endures to all generations. ^102-12

**102:13** You will rise up and have compassion on Zion, for it is time to favor it; the appointed time has come. ^102-13

**102:14** For your servants hold its stones dear and have pity on its dust. ^102-14

**102:15** The nations will fear the name of the LORD and all the kings of the earth your glory. ^102-15

**102:16** For the LORD will build up Zion; he will appear in his glory. ^102-16

**102:17** He will regard the prayer of the destitute and will not despise their prayer. ^102-17

**102:18** Let this be recorded for a generation to come, so that a people yet unborn may praise the LORD: ^102-18

**102:19** that he looked down from his holy height, from heaven the LORD looked at the earth, ^102-19

**102:20** to hear the groans of the prisoners, to set free those who were doomed to die, ^102-20

**102:21** so that the name of the LORD may be declared in Zion and his praise in Jerusalem, ^102-21

**102:22** when peoples gather together, and kingdoms, to serve the LORD. ^102-22

**102:23** He has broken my strength in midcourse; he has shortened my days. ^102-23

**102:24** “O my God,” I say, “do not take me away at the midpoint of my life, you whose years endure throughout all generations.” ^102-24

**102:25** Long ago you laid the foundation of the earth, and the heavens are the work of your hands. ^102-25

**102:26** They will perish, but you endure; they will all wear out like a garment. You change them like clothing, and they pass away, ^102-26

**102:27** but you are the same, and your years have no end. ^102-27

**102:28** The children of your servants shall live secure; their offspring shall be established in your presence. ^102-28
