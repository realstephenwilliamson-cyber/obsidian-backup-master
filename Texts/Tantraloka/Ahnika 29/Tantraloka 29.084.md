## Mūla

पञ्चलक्षा इमे प्रोक्ता दशांशं होममाचरेत् ।
नेत्रे गमागमे वक्त्रे हंसे चैवाक्षसूत्रके ॥ ^mula

pañcalakṣā ime proktā daśāṃśaṃ homamācaret |
netre gamāgame vaktre haṃse caivākṣasūtrake || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 10 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 2#^pg295|p. 278]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=295>))
>
> Wt is said that the repetition (japa) of three hundred thousand (with three objects of attention) takes place 1) in the (locus of) emergence (of the vital breath), 2) in the union (of the channels of the vital breath,) and 3) in the tranquillity (of the repose of the breath). These are said to be the five objects of attention (corresponding to the repetition of five hundred thousand times), they are 1) within the mouth (G@sya),), 2) in the going and coming (of the breath), 3) in the thread, 4) in the one called Haṁsa, and 5) in the Śaiva couple. (Having done that,) one should offer oblations (into the fire numbering) a tenth (of the number of repetitions)? (with 29.83–84ab)
>
> It is said that repetition of six hundred thousand times (with six objects takes place) 1) within (the sense of sight in) the eye, 2) in the going and coming (of the breath), 3) in the mouth, 4) within Haṁsa, 5) in the thread of the rosary, and 6) in the union of Śiva and Śakti. (with 29.84cd–85ab)
