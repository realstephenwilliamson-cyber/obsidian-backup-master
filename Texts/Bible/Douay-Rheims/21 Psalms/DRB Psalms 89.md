# Psalms 89 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 88|← Psalms 88]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 90|Psalms 90 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 89|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**89:1** A prayer of Moses the man of God. Lord, thou hast been our refuge from generation to generation. ^89-1

**89:2** Before the mountains were made, or the earth and the world was formed; from eternity and to eternity thou art God. ^89-2

**89:3** Turn not man away to be brought low: and thou hast said: Be converted, O ye sons of men. ^89-3

**89:4** For a thousand years in thy sight are as yesterday, which is past. And as a watch in the night, ^89-4

**89:5** Things that are counted nothing, shall their years be. ^89-5

**89:6** In the morning man shall grow up like grass; in the morning he shall flourish and pass away: in the evening he shall fall, grow dry, and wither. ^89-6

**89:7** For in thy wrath we have fainted away: and are troubled in thy indignation. ^89-7

**89:8** Thou hast set our iniquities before thy eyes: our life in the light of thy countenance. ^89-8

**89:9** For all our days are spent; and in thy wrath we have fainted away. Our years shall be considered as a spider: ^89-9

**89:10** The days of our years in them are threescore and ten years. But if in the strong they be fourscore years: and what is more of them is labour and sorrow. For mildness is come upon us: and we shall be corrected. ^89-10

**89:11** Who knoweth the power of thy anger, and for thy fear ^89-11

**89:12** Can number thy wrath? So make thy right hand known: and men learned in heart, in wisdom. ^89-12

**89:13** Return, O Lord, how long? and be entreated in favour of thy servants. ^89-13

**89:14** We are filled in the morning with thy mercy: and we have rejoiced, and are delighted all our days. ^89-14

**89:15** We have rejoiced for the days in which thou hast humbled us: for the years in which we have seen evils. ^89-15

**89:16** Look upon thy servants and upon their works: and direct their children. ^89-16

**89:17** And let the brightness of the Lord our God be upon us: and direct thou the works of our hands over us; yea, the work of our hands do thou direct. ^89-17
