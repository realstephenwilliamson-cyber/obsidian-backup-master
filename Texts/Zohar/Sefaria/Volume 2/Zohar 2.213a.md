# Zohar 2.213a

← [[Zohar 2.212b]] · [[Zohar (Soncino)]] · [[Zohar 2.213b]] →

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 6.05 - Parashat Va-Yaqhel#^z2-213a|Parashat Va-Yaqhel, at 2:213a]]

*Volume 2, folio 213a* · Vayakhel

*Passage Vayakhel 25:353 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 2.212b#^vayakhel-25-353|Vayakhel 25:353]].*

**Vayakhel 25:354** As the firmament revolves, the volume of melodious sound, with the same refrain, goes up from all the numerous legions, at each of the four sides of the firmament. The firmament then becomes illumined with an exceedingly bright light, brighter than before, displaying, in a different mode, a colour composed of the whole range of colours; ^vayakhel-25-354

**Vayakhel 25:355** and the aforementioned Divine Name of four letters becomes augmented by an additional letter from on high, to wit, the letter *vau*, resulting in V- TETRAGRAMMATON (lit. and TETRAGRAMMATON), expressive of both the male and female principles. ^vayakhel-25-355

**Vayakhel 25:356** Yet the Divine Name is not perfect except when it is composed of the nine letters, to wit, TETRAGRAMMATON ELHYM (Elohim). When these letters are thus joined, ^vayakhel-25-356

**Vayakhel 25:357** that firmament shines with thirty-two lights, and all is joy, all is joined in one recondite union, both the upper and the lower world. ^vayakhel-25-357

**Vayakhel 25:358** In that same firmament, again, there is on the north side a flaming light that is never extinguished, to the right of which there are impressions of other letters, forming ten Sacred Names, in which are implicit seventy Names. ^vayakhel-25-358

**Vayakhel 25:359** By that firmament are borne along all the lower firmaments within the region of holiness as far as the boundary of the “other” firmaments belonging to the “other side”. These latter are called “curtains of goats’ hair”, allusion to which is made in the verse, “And he made curtains of goats’ hair for a tent over the tabernacle.” ^vayakhel-25-359

**Vayakhel 25:360** For there are curtains and curtains. The “curtains of the tabernacle” are the counterpart of the firmaments spread over the Holy Beasts in the Holy Tabernacle; whereas the “curtains of goats hair” represent altogether different firmaments, those of the “other side”. The former contain the mystery of the legions of the holy spirits; the latter represent the substance of mundane matter, of bodily appetites and actions, and hence form the outer covering of the inner firmaments similar to the shell that surrounds the brain. The latter are called “heavens of the Lord”. ^vayakhel-25-360

**Vayakhel 25:361** Opposite to these lower heavens are the supernal heavens, heavens within heavens, called the “heavens of the Holy Beasts”, in allusion to the supernal mighty Holy Beasts. They contain the mystery of the Torah, and on the highest heaven of all, namely, the eighth,[^1] there are engraved the twenty-two letters of the alphabet. That heaven is supported by the supernal Holy Beasts, and itself is of an essence entirely undisclosed, beyond any attribute of colour, ^vayakhel-25-361

**Vayakhel 25:362** whilst being itself the source and origin of all colours and all light. There is neither light nor darkness in it, but the souls of the righteous, as from behind a wall, discern the light which it sends forth and which illumines the supreme heaven, a light never ceasing, a light not to be known or grasped. ^vayakhel-25-362

**Vayakhel 25:363** There are lower heavens and upper heavens, both referred to in the passage, saying, “The heavens, heavens of the Lord” (Ps. 115, 16), but this is the supremest heaven, raised over them all. Up to this point some hint is given by the holy names by which the Holy One, blessed be He, is called, but beyond this point the discernment even of the wisest cannot pierce, and it is altogether outside the range of our faculties, excepting for one gleam of light, too minute to be dwelt on. ^vayakhel-25-363

[^1]: Binah.
