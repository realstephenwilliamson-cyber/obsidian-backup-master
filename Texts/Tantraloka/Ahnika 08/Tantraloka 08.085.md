## Mūla

कन्याद्वीपे च नवमे दक्षिणेनाब्धिमध्यगाः ।
उपद्वीपाः षट् कुलाद्रिसप्तकेन विभूषिते ॥ ^mula

kanyādvīpe ca navame dakṣiṇenābdhimadhyagāḥ |

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 05 - Chapter Eight, part 3#^pg151|p. 133]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=151>))
>
> The island of Kumārikā, which is the ninth (region of Bhārata), is adorned with seven principal mountains (kulādri). There are six minor islands within it, situated to the south, in the middle of the sea (of Vāruṇa). These islands are called 1) Añga, 2) Yava, 3) Malaya, 4) Śaṁku, 5) Kumuda and 6) Varāha. (One of the peaks on mount) Malaya is Agastya. There (at the foot of this mountain is mount) Trikūta, on which is (the city of) Lañkā. These are the six minor islands. (with 8.85–86)
