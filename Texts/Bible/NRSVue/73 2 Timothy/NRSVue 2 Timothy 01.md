# 2 Timothy 1 (NRSVue)

Compare: [[2 Timothy 01|BSB and SBLGNT]] · [[DRB 2 Timothy 01|Douay-Rheims]] · [[JANT 2 Timothy 01|JANT notes]]

## Salutation

**1:1** Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus by the will of God, for the sake of the promise of life that is in Christ Jesus, ^1-1

**1:2** To Timothy, my beloved child: Grace, mercy, and peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord. ^1-2

## Thanksgiving and encouragement

**1:3** I am grateful to God—whom I worship with a clear conscience, as my ancestors did—when I remember you constantly in my prayers night and day. ^1-3

**1:4** Recalling your tears, I long to see you so that I may be filled with joy. ^1-4

**1:5** I am reminded of your sincere faith, a faith that lived first in your grandmother Lois and your mother Eunice and now, I am sure, lives in you. ^1-5

**1:6** For this reason I remind you to rekindle the gift of God that is within you through the laying on of my hands, ^1-6

**1:7** for God did not give us a spirit of cowardice but rather a spirit of power and of love and of self-discipline. ^1-7

**1:8** Do not be ashamed, then, of the testimony about our Lord or of me his prisoner, but join with me in suffering for the gospel, in the power of God, ^1-8

**1:9** who saved us and called us with a holy calling, not according to our works but according to his own purpose and grace, and this grace was given to us in Christ Jesus before the ages began,^[Gk lacks *began*] ^1-9

**1:10** but it has now been revealed through the appearing of our Savior Jesus Christ,^[Other ancient authorities read *Christ Jesus*] who abolished death and brought life and immortality to light through the gospel. ^1-10

**1:11** For this gospel I was appointed a herald and an apostle and a teacher,^[Other ancient authorities add *of the gentiles*] ^1-11

**1:12** and for this reason I suffer as I do. But I am not ashamed, for I know the one in whom I have put my trust, and I am sure that he is able to guard the deposit I have entrusted to him.^[Or *the deposit he has entrusted to me*] ^1-12

**1:13** Hold to the standard of sound teaching that you have heard from me, in the faith and love that are in Christ Jesus. ^1-13

**1:14** Guard the good deposit entrusted to you, with the help of the Holy Spirit living in us. ^1-14

**1:15** You are aware that all who are in Asia have turned away from me, including Phygelus and Hermogenes. ^1-15

**1:16** May the Lord grant mercy to the household of Onesiphorus, because he often refreshed me and was not ashamed of my chain; ^1-16

**1:17** when he arrived in Rome, he eagerly^[Or *promptly*] searched for me and found me ^1-17

**1:18** —may the Lord grant that he will find mercy from the Lord on that day! And you know very well how much service he rendered in Ephesus. ^1-18
