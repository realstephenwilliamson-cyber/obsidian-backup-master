## Mūla

माता परं बन्धुरिति प्रवादः
स्नेहोऽतिगाढीकुरुते हि पाशान् ।
तन्मूलबन्धे गलिते किलास्य
मन्ये स्थिता जीवत एव मुक्तिः ॥ ^mula

mātā paraṃ bandhuriti pravādaḥ
sneho'tigāḍhīkurute hi pāśān |
tanmūlabandhe galite kilāsya
manye sthitā jīvata eva muktiḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 11 - Chapter Thirty-Seven, part 1#^pg270|p. 252]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=270>))
>
> As the saying goes, a mother is the greatest friend. (However,) love makes the fetters (that bind to worldly life) very firm. When this, the root fetter, had fallen away, I consider that for him liberation in this life itself was accomplished (sthitā).
