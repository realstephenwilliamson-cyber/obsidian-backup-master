## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 07 - Chapter Eight, part 5#^pg255|p. 237]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=255>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 8.210cd–211ab.
>
> Those who are not initiated (in the worlds of) the gross elements, thinking haughtily (that they have attained) the Śiva principle; those whose concentration is mature (and firm) (praudhadhāraṇa), even though they have no knowledge, are outside the Egg (of Brahmā of the Earth principle). The same applies to those who have been initiated (but) reside in the worlds of the Earth, Water, Fire, Wind and Sky (to attain worldly accomplishments), to the extent that their purpose is to purify just that much (of some lower principle) to unite (with it), and not to strive for the supreme plane. (It is like that also for) those who have died in the sacred places (āyatana), where (the gods) have descended (onto the earth) in that (appropriate) manner. Once having attained their abode (pada), in due course (kramāt) they attain a state of identity with Śiva. (with 8.209cd–212ab)
