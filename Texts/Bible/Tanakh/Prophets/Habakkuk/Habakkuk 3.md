# Habakkuk 3

← [[Habakkuk 2]] · [[Tanakh]]

Compare: [[NRSVue Habakkuk 03|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 3

**3:1** A prayer of the prophet Habakkuk. In the mode of *Shigionoth*.[^1] ^3-1

**3:2** O LORD! I have learned of Your renown;  
I am awed, O LORD, by Your deeds.  
Renew them in these years,  
Oh, make them known in these years!  
Though angry, may You remember compassion. ^3-2

**3:3** God is coming from Teman,  
The Holy One from Mount Paran. *Selah*.[^2]  
His majesty covers the skies,  
His splendor fills the earth: ^3-3

**3:4** It is a brilliant light  
Which gives off rays on every side—  
And therein His glory is enveloped.[^3] ^3-4

**3:5** Pestilence marches before Him,  
And plague comes forth at His heels. ^3-5

**3:6** When He stands, He makes the earth shake;[^4]  
When He glances, He makes nations tremble.  
The age-old mountains are shattered,  
The primeval hills sink low.  
[^5]His are the ancient routes: ^3-6

**3:7** As a scene of havoc I behold  
The tents of Cushan;  
Shaken are the pavilions  
Of the land of Midian! ^3-7

**3:8** Are You wroth, O LORD, with Neharim?  
Is Your anger against Neharim,  
Your rage against Yam[^6]—  
That You are driving Your steeds,  
Your victorious chariot? ^3-8

**3:9** All bared and ready is Your bow.  
Sworn are the rods of the word.[^7] *Selah*.  
You make the earth burst into streams, ^3-9

**3:10** The mountains rock at the sight of You,  
A torrent of rain comes down;  
Loud roars the deep,  
The sky returns the echo.[^8] ^3-10

**3:11** Sun [and] moon stand still on high  
As Your arrows fly in brightness,  
Your flashing spear in brilliance. ^3-11

**3:12** You tread the earth in rage,  
You trample nations in fury. ^3-12

**3:13** You have come forth to deliver Your people,  
To deliver Your anointed.[^9]  
[^10]You will smash the roof of the villain’s house,  
Raze it from foundation to top. *Selah*. ^3-13

**3:14** You will crack [his] skull with Your[^11] bludgeon;  
Blown away shall be his warriors,  
Whose delight is to crush me suddenly,  
To devour a poor man in an ambush. ^3-14

**3:15** [^12]You will make Your steeds tread the sea,  
Stirring the mighty waters. ^3-15

**3:16** I heard and my bowels quaked,  
My lips quivered at the sound;  
Rot entered into my bone,  
I trembled where I stood.  
Yet I wait calmly for the day of distress,  
For a people to come to attack us. ^3-16

**3:17** Though the fig tree does not bud  
And no yield is on the vine,  
Though the olive crop has failed  
And the fields produce no grain,  
Though sheep have vanished from the fold  
And no cattle are in the pen, ^3-17

**3:18** Yet will I rejoice in the LORD,  
Exult in the God who delivers me. ^3-18

**3:19** My Lord GOD is my strength:  
He makes my feet like the deer’s  
And lets me stride upon the heights.  
For the leader; with instrumental music.[^13] ^3-19

[^1]: Meaning uncertain; perhaps “psalms of supplication”; cf. Ps. 7.1.
[^2]: A musical direction of uncertain meaning.
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^4]: Cf. Targum and Septuagint.
[^5]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^6]: Neharim (lit. “Floods”) and Yam (lit. “Sea”) were marine monsters vanquished by the Lord in hoary antiquity. On Yam see Ps. 74.13; Job 7.12. A being called both Yam and Nahar figures in early Canaanite literature.
[^7]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^8]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^9]: I.e., the king of Judah.
[^10]: Emendation yields: You will strike the heads of men of evil, / Smash the pates of Your adversaries. Selah. / You will crack their skulls with Your bludgeon; / Dispersed, blown like chaff shall be they / Who lie in wait to swallow the innocent, / To devour the poor in an ambush.
[^11]: Heb. “His.”
[^12]: Or: 15You will make Your steeds tread the sea, / Stirring the mighty waters, / 16That I may have rest on a day of distress, / When a people come up to attack us. / But this report made my bowels quake, / These tidings made my lips quiver; / Rot entered into my bone, / I trembled where I stood: / 17That the fig tree does not bud, / And no yield is on the vine; / The olive crop has failed, / And the fields produce no grain; / The sheep have vanished from the fold, / And no cattle are in the pen.
[^13]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
