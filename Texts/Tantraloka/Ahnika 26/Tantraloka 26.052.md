## Mūla

धूपैश्च तर्पणं कार्यं श्रद्धाभक्तिबलोचितैः ।
दीप्तानां शक्तिनादादिमन्त्राणामासवैः पलैः ॥ ^mula

dhūpaiśca tarpaṇaṃ kāryaṃ śraddhābhaktibalocitaiḥ |
dīptānāṃ śaktinādādimantrāṇāmāsavaiḥ palaiḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 17 - Chapter Twenty-Five (continued) and Chapter Twenty-Six#^pg408|p. 388]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=408>))
>
> Once the host of Mantras has been invoked, one must offer (them) libation (tarpaṇa), along with offerings of flowers, wine, and incense (of various kinds), as befits the strength of (the officiant’s) faith and devotion. Libation should first be offered to the energized Mantras of Śakti and (Her) Lord etc. with wine, meat and blood, and then with flowers, incense and the rest of the full extent (of the offerings). (with 26.51cd–53ab)

## Viveka

> आवाहिते मन्त्रगणे पुष्पासवनिवेदनैः ॥
> धूपैश्च तर्पणं कार्यं श्रद्धाभक्तिबलोचितैः ।
> दीप्तानां शक्तिनादादिमन्त्राणामासवैः पलैः ॥
> रक्तैः प्राक् तर्पणं पश्चात् पुष्पधूपादिविस्तरैः ।
