## Mūla

न ध्वस्तव्याधिकः को हि भिषजं बहु मन्यते ।
असूयुर्नूनमध्वस्तव्याधिः स्वस्थायते बलात् ॥ ^mula

na dhvastavyādhikaḥ ko hi bhiṣajaṃ bahu manyate |
asūyurnūnamadhvastavyādhiḥ svasthāyate balāt || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 14 - Chapter Twenty-Three#^pg352|p. 332]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=352>))
>
> Who is it that, having been cured of a disease, does not have a great deal of respect (for his) doctor? (However,) a detractor (who does not like the doctor), will (nevertheless) certainly stubbornly (balāt) (insist) that he is curing (himself,) (although) he continues to be sick. In the same way, what will one who is satisfied (and has faith in the) knowledge (he has received) not offer the teacher? But if not, although he is not satisfied (and has no faith), he will certainly (act) as if he is satisfied. (with 23.81–82)
