# I Chronicles 01

[[Tanakh]] · [[I Chronicles 02]] →

Compare: [[NRSVue 1 Chronicles 01|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 1

**1:1** Adam, Seth, Enosh; ^1-1

**1:2** Kenan, Mahalalel, Jared; ^1-2

**1:3** Enoch, Methuselah, Lamech; ^1-3

**1:4** Noah, Shem, Ham, and Japheth. ^1-4

**1:5** [^1]The sons of Japheth: Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Tubal, Meshech, and Tiras. ^1-5

**1:6** The sons of Gomer: Ashkenaz, Diphath, and Togarmah. ^1-6

**1:7** The sons of Javan: Elishah, Tarshish, Kittim, and Rodanim. ^1-7

**1:8** The sons of Ham: Cush, Mizraim, Put, and Canaan. ^1-8

**1:9** The sons of Cush: Seba, Havilah, Sabta, Raama, and Sabteca. The sons of Raama: Sheba and Dedan. ^1-9

**1:10** Cush begot Nimrod; he was the first mighty one on earth. ^1-10

**1:11** Mizraim begot the Ludim, the Anamim, the Lehabim, the Naphtuhim, ^1-11

**1:12** the Pathrusim, the Casluhim (whence the Philistines came forth), and the Caphtorim. ^1-12

**1:13** Canaan begot Sidon his first-born, and Heth, ^1-13

**1:14** and the Jebusites, the Amorites, the Girgashites, ^1-14

**1:15** the Hivites, the Arkites, the Sinites, ^1-15

**1:16** the Arvadites, the Zemarites, and the Hamathites. ^1-16

**1:17** The sons of Shem: Elam, Asshur, Arpachshad, Lud, Aram, Uz, Hul, Gether, and Meshech. ^1-17

**1:18** Arpachshad begot Shelah; and Shelah begot Eber. ^1-18

**1:19** Two sons were born to Eber: the name of the one was Peleg (for in his days the earth was divided), and the name of his brother Joktan. ^1-19

**1:20** Joktan begot Almodad, Sheleph, Hazarmaveth, Jerah, ^1-20

**1:21** Hadoram, Uzal, Diklah, ^1-21

**1:22** Ebal, Abimael, Sheba, ^1-22

**1:23** Ophir, Havilah, and Jobab; all these were the sons of Joktan. ^1-23

**1:24** Shem, Arpachshad, Shelah; ^1-24

**1:25** Eber, Peleg, Reu; ^1-25

**1:26** Serug, Nahor, Terah; ^1-26

**1:27** Abram, that is, Abraham. ^1-27

**1:28** [^2]The sons of Abraham: Isaac and Ishmael. ^1-28

**1:29** This is their line: The first-born of Ishmael, Nebaioth; and Kedar, Adbeel, Mibsam, ^1-29

**1:30** Mishma, Dumah, Massa, Hadad, Tema, ^1-30

**1:31** Jetur, Naphish, and Kedmah. These are the sons of Ishmael. ^1-31

**1:32** The sons of Keturah, Abraham’s concubine: she bore Zimran, Jokshan, Medan, Midian, Ishbak, and Shuah. The sons of Jokshan: Sheba and Dedan. ^1-32

**1:33** The sons of Midian: Ephah, Epher, Enoch, Abida, and Eldaah. All these were the descendants of Keturah. ^1-33

**1:34** Abraham begot Isaac. The sons of Isaac: Esau and Israel. ^1-34

**1:35** The sons of Esau: Eliphaz, Reuel, Jeush, Jalam, and Korah. ^1-35

**1:36** The sons of Eliphaz: Teman, Omar, Zephi, Gatam, Kenaz, Timna, and Amalek. ^1-36

**1:37** The sons of Reuel: Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah. ^1-37

**1:38** The sons of Seir: Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah, Dishon, Ezer, and Dishan. ^1-38

**1:39** The sons of Lotan: Hori and Homam; and Lotan’s sister was Timna. ^1-39

**1:40** The sons of Shobal: Alian, Manahath, Ebal, Shephi, and Onam. The sons of Zibeon: Aiah and Anah. ^1-40

**1:41** The sons of Anah: Dishon. The sons of Dishon: Hamran, Eshban, Ithran, and Cheran. ^1-41

**1:42** The sons of Ezer: Bilhan, Zaavan, and Jaakan. The sons of Dishan: Uz and Aran. ^1-42

**1:43** [^3]These are the kings who reigned in the land of Edom before any king reigned over the Israelites: Bela son of Beor, and the name of his city was Dinhabah. ^1-43

**1:44** When Bela died, Jobab son of Zerah from Bozrah succeeded him as king. ^1-44

**1:45** When Jobab died, Husham of the land of the Temanites succeeded him as king. ^1-45

**1:46** When Husham died, Hadad son of Bedad, who defeated the Midianites in the country of Moab, succeeded him as king, and the name of his city was Avith. ^1-46

**1:47** When Hadad died, Samlah of Masrekah succeeded him as king. ^1-47

**1:48** When Samlah died, Saul of Rehoboth-on-the-River succeeded him as king. ^1-48

**1:49** When Saul died, Baal-hanan son of Achbor succeeded him as king. ^1-49

**1:50** When Baal-hanan died, Hadad succeeded him as king; and the name of his city was Pai, and his wife’s name Mehetabel daughter of Matred daughter of Me-zahab. ^1-50

**1:51** And Hadad died.  
The clans of Edom were the clans of Timna, Alvah, Jetheth, ^1-51

**1:52** Oholibamah, Elah, Pinon, ^1-52

**1:53** Kenaz, Teman, Mibzar, ^1-53

**1:54** Magdiel, and Iram; these are the clans of Edom. ^1-54

[^1]: With vv. 5–23, cf. Gen. 10.1–30.
[^2]: With vv. 28–33, cf. Gen. 25.1–16.
[^3]: With vv. 43–50, cf. Gen. 36.31–43.
