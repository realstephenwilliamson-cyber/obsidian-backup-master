## Chapter 25

Compare: [[NRSVue Numbers 25#^25-10|NRSVue]]

**25:10** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, ^25-10

**25:11** “Phinehas, son of Eleazar son of Aaron the priest, has turned back My wrath from the Israelites by displaying among them his passion for Me, so that I did not wipe out the Israelite people in My passion. ^25-11

**25:12** Say, therefore, ‘I grant him My pact of friendship. ^25-12

**25:13** It shall be for him and his descendants after him a pact of priesthood for all time, because he took impassioned action for his God, thus making expiation for the Israelites.’” ^25-13

**25:14** The name of the Israelite who was killed, the one who was killed with the Midianite woman, was Zimri son of Salu, chieftain of a Simeonite ancestral house. ^25-14

**25:15** The name of the Midianite woman who was killed was Cozbi daughter of Zur; he was the tribal head of an ancestral house in Midian. ^25-15

**25:16** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, ^25-16

**25:17** “Assail the Midianites and defeat them— ^25-17

**25:18** for they assailed you by the trickery they practiced against you—because of the affair of Peor and because of the affair of their kinswoman Cozbi, daughter of the Midianite chieftain, who was killed at the time of the plague on account of Peor.” ^25-18

## Chapter 26

Compare: [[NRSVue Numbers 26|NRSVue]]

**26:1** When the plague was over, the LORD said to Moses and to Eleazar son of Aaron the priest, ^26-1

**26:2** “Take a census of the whole Israelite community from the age of twenty years up, by their ancestral houses, all Israelites able to bear arms.” ^26-2

**26:3** [^1]So Moses and Eleazar the priest, on the steppes of Moab, at the Jordan near Jericho, gave instructions about them, namely, ^26-3

**26:4** those from twenty years up, as the LORD had commanded Moses.  
The descendants of the Israelites who came out of the land of Egypt were: ^26-4

**26:5** Reuben, Israel’s first-born. Descendants of Reuben: [Of] Enoch,[^2] the clan of the Enochites; of Pallu, the clan of the Palluites; ^26-5

**26:6** of Hezron, the clan of the Hezronites; of Carmi, the clan of the Carmites. ^26-6

**26:7** Those are the clans of the Reubenites. The persons enrolled came to 43,730. ^26-7

**26:8** Born to[^3] Pallu: Eliab. ^26-8

**26:9** The sons of Eliab were Nemuel, and Dathan and Abiram. These are the same Dathan and Abiram, chosen in the assembly, who agitated against Moses and Aaron as part of Korah’s band when they agitated against the LORD. ^26-9

**26:10** Whereupon the earth opened its mouth and swallowed them up with Korah—when that band died, when the fire consumed the two hundred and fifty men—and they became an example. ^26-10

**26:11** The sons of Korah, however, did not die. ^26-11

**26:12** Descendants of Simeon by their clans: Of Nemuel, the clan of the Nemuelites; of Jamin, the clan of the Jaminites; of Jachin, the clan of the Jachinites; ^26-12

**26:13** of Zerah, the clan of the Zerahites; of Saul,[^4] the clan of the Saulites. ^26-13

**26:14** Those are the clans of the Simeonites; [persons enrolled:] 22,200. ^26-14

**26:15** Descendants of Gad by their clans: Of Zephon, the clan of the Zephonites; of Haggi, the clan of the Haggites; of Shuni, the clan of the Shunites; ^26-15

**26:16** of Ozni, the clan of the Oznites; of Eri, the clan of the Erites; ^26-16

**26:17** of Arod, the clan of the Arodites; of Areli, the clan of the Arelites. ^26-17

**26:18** Those are the clans of Gad’s descendants; persons enrolled: 40,500. ^26-18

**26:19** Born to[^5] Judah: Er and Onan. Er and Onan died in the land of Canaan. ^26-19

**26:20** Descendants of Judah by their clans: Of Shelah, the clan of the Shelanites; of Perez, the clan of the Perezites; of Zerah, the clan of the Zerahites. ^26-20

**26:21** Descendants of Perez: of Hezron, the clan of the Hezronites; of Hamul, the clan of the Hamulites. ^26-21

**26:22** Those are the clans of Judah; persons enrolled: 76,500. ^26-22

**26:23** Descendants of Issachar by their clans: [Of] Tola, the clan of the Tolaites; of Puvah, the clan of the Punites; ^26-23

**26:24** of Jashub, the clan of the Jashubites; of Shimron, the clan of the Shimronites. ^26-24

**26:25** Those are the clans of Issachar; persons enrolled: 64,300. ^26-25

**26:26** Descendants of Zebulun by their clans: Of Sered, the clan of the Seredites; of Elon, the clan of the Elonites; of Jahleel, the clan of the Jahleelites. ^26-26

**26:27** Those are the clans of the Zebulunites; persons enrolled: 60,500. ^26-27

**26:28** The sons of Joseph were Manasseh and Ephraim—by their clans. ^26-28

**26:29** Descendants of Manasseh: Of Machir, the clan of the Machirites.—Machir begot Gilead.—Of Gilead, the clan of the Gileadites. ^26-29

**26:30** These were the descendants of Gilead: [Of] Iezer, the clan of the Iezerites; of Helek, the clan of the Helekites; ^26-30

**26:31** [of] Asriel, the clan of the Asrielites; [of] Shechem, the clan of the Shechemites; ^26-31

**26:32** [of] Shemida, the clan of the Shemidaites; [of] Hepher, the clan of the Hepherites.— ^26-32

**26:33** Now Zelophehad son of Hepher had no sons, only daughters. The names of Zelophehad’s daughters were Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah.— ^26-33

**26:34** Those are the clans of Manasseh; persons enrolled: 52,700. ^26-34

**26:35** These are the descendants of Ephraim by their clans: Of Shuthelah, the clan of the Shuthelahites; of Becher, the clan of the Becherites; of Tahan, the clan of the Tahanites. ^26-35

**26:36** These are the descendants of Shuthelah: Of Eran, the clan of the Eranites. ^26-36

**26:37** Those are the clans of Ephraim’s descendants; persons enrolled: 32,500.  
Those are the descendants of Joseph by their clans. ^26-37

**26:38** The descendants of Benjamin by their clans: Of Bela, the clan of the Belaites; of Ashbel, the clan of the Ashbelites; of Ahiram, the clan of the Ahiramites; ^26-38

**26:39** of Shephupham, the clan of the Shuphamites; of Hupham, the clan of the Huphamites. ^26-39

**26:40** The sons of Bela were Ard and Naaman: [Of Ard,] the clan of the Ardites; of Naaman, the clan of the Naamanites. ^26-40

**26:41** Those are the descendants of Benjamin by their clans; persons enrolled: 45,600. ^26-41

**26:42** These are the descendants of Dan by their clans: Of Shuham, the clan of the Shuhamites. Those are the clans of Dan,[^6] by their clans. ^26-42

**26:43** All the clans of the Shuhamites; persons enrolled: 64,400. ^26-43

**26:44** Descendants of Asher by their clans: Of Imnah, the clan of the Imnites; of Ishvi, the clan of the Ishvites; of Beriah, the clan of the Beriites. ^26-44

**26:45** Of the descendants of Beriah: Of Heber, the clan of the Heberites; of Malchiel, the clan of the Malchielites.— ^26-45

**26:46** The name of Asher’s daughter was Serah.— ^26-46

**26:47** These are the clans of Asher’s descendants; persons enrolled: 53,400. ^26-47

**26:48** Descendants of Naphtali by their clans: Of Jahzeel, the clan of the Jahzeelites; of Guni, the clan of the Gunites; ^26-48

**26:49** of Jezer, the clan of the Jezerites; of Shillem, the clan of the Shillemites. ^26-49

**26:50** Those are the clans of the Naphtalites, clan by clan; persons enrolled: 45,400. ^26-50

**26:51** This is the enrollment of the Israelites: 601,730. ^26-51

**26:52** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, ^26-52

**26:53** “Among these shall the land be apportioned as shares, according to the listed names: ^26-53

**26:54** with larger groups increase the share, with smaller groups reduce the share. Each is to be assigned its share according to its enrollment. ^26-54

**26:55** The land, moreover, is to be apportioned by lot; and the allotment shall be made according to the listings of their ancestral tribes. ^26-55

**26:56** Each portion shall be assigned by lot, whether for larger or smaller groups.” ^26-56

**26:57** This is the enrollment of the Levites by their clans: Of Gershon, the clan of the Gershonites; of Kohath, the clan of the Kohathites; of Merari, the clan of the Merarites. ^26-57

**26:58** These are the clans of Levi: The clan of the Libnites, the clan of the Hebronites, the clan of the Mahlites, the clan of the Mushites, the clan of the Korahites.—Kohath begot Amram. ^26-58

**26:59** The name of Amram’s wife was Jochebed daughter of Levi, who was born to Levi in Egypt; she bore to Amram Aaron and Moses and their sister Miriam. ^26-59

**26:60** To Aaron were born Nadab and Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar. ^26-60

**26:61** Nadab and Abihu died when they offered alien fire before the LORD.— ^26-61

**26:62** Their enrollment of 23,000 comprised all males from a month up. They were not part of the regular enrollment of the Israelites, since no share was assigned to them among the Israelites. ^26-62

**26:63** These are the persons enrolled by Moses and Eleazar the priest who registered the Israelites on the steppes of Moab, at the Jordan near Jericho. ^26-63

**26:64** Among these there was not one of those enrolled by Moses and Aaron the priest when they recorded the Israelites in the wilderness of Sinai. ^26-64

**26:65** For the LORD had said of them, “They shall die in the wilderness.” Not one of them survived, except Caleb son of Jephunneh and Joshua son of Nun. ^26-65

## Chapter 27

Compare: [[NRSVue Numbers 27|NRSVue]]

**27:1** The daughters of Zelophehad, of Manassite family—son of Hepher son of Gilead son of Machir son of Manasseh son of Joseph—came forward. The names of the daughters were Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah. ^27-1

**27:2** They stood before Moses, Eleazar the priest, the chieftains, and the whole assembly, at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting, and they said, ^27-2

**27:3** “Our father died in the wilderness. He was not one of the faction, Korah’s faction, which banded together against the LORD, but died for his own sin; and he has left no sons. ^27-3

**27:4** Let not our father’s name be lost to his clan just because he had no son! Give us a holding among our father’s kinsmen!” ^27-4

**27:5** Moses brought their case before the LORD. ^27-5

**27:6** And the LORD said to Moses, ^27-6

**27:7** “The plea of Zelophehad’s daughters is just: you should give them a hereditary holding among their father’s kinsmen; transfer their father’s share to them. ^27-7

**27:8** “Further, speak to the Israelite people as follows: ‘If a man dies without leaving a son, you shall transfer his property to his daughter. ^27-8

**27:9** If he has no daughter, you shall assign his property to his brothers. ^27-9

**27:10** If he has no brothers, you shall assign his property to his father’s brothers. ^27-10

**27:11** If his father had no brothers, you shall assign his property to his nearest relative in his own clan, and he shall inherit it.’ This shall be the law of procedure for the Israelites, in accordance with the LORD’s command to Moses.” ^27-11

**27:12** The LORD said to Moses, “Ascend these heights of Abarim and view the land that I have given to the Israelite people. ^27-12

**27:13** When you have seen it, you too shall be gathered to your kin, just as your brother Aaron was. ^27-13

**27:14** For, in the wilderness of Zin, when the community was contentious, you disobeyed My command to uphold My sanctity in their sight by means of the water.” Those are the Waters of Meribath-kadesh,[^7] in the wilderness of Zin. ^27-14

**27:15** Moses spoke to the LORD, saying, ^27-15

**27:16** “Let the LORD, Source of the breath of all flesh, appoint someone over the community ^27-16

**27:17** who shall go out before them and come in before them, and who shall take them out and bring them in,[^8] so that the LORD’s community may not be like sheep that have no shepherd.” ^27-17

**27:18** And the LORD answered Moses, “Single out Joshua son of Nun, an inspired man, and lay your hand upon him. ^27-18

**27:19** Have him stand before Eleazar the priest and before the whole community, and commission him in their sight. ^27-19

**27:20** Invest him with some of your authority, so that the whole Israelite community may obey. ^27-20

**27:21** But he shall present himself to Eleazar the priest, who shall on his behalf seek the decision of the Urim before the LORD. By such instruction they shall go out and by such instruction they shall come in, he and all the Israelites, the whole community.” ^27-21

**27:22** Moses did as the LORD commanded him. He took Joshua and had him stand before Eleazar the priest and before the whole community. ^27-22

**27:23** He laid his hands upon him and commissioned him—as the LORD had spoken through Moses. ^27-23

## Chapter 28

Compare: [[NRSVue Numbers 28|NRSVue]]

**28:1** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying: ^28-1

**28:2** Command the Israelite people and say to them: Be punctilious in presenting to Me at stated times the offerings of food due Me,[^9] as offerings by fire of pleasing odor to Me. ^28-2

**28:3** Say to them: These are the offerings by fire that you are to present to the LORD:  
As a regular burnt offering every day, two yearling lambs without blemish. ^28-3

**28:4** You shall offer one lamb in the morning, and the other lamb you shall offer at twilight. ^28-4

**28:5** And as a meal offering, there shall be a tenth of an *ephah* of choice flour with a quarter of a *hin* of beaten oil mixed in— ^28-5

**28:6** the regular burnt offering instituted at Mount Sinai[^10]—an offering by fire of pleasing odor to the LORD. ^28-6

**28:7** The libation with it shall be a quarter of a *hin* for each lamb, to be poured in the sacred precinct as an offering of fermented drink[^11] to the LORD. ^28-7

**28:8** The other lamb you shall offer at twilight, preparing the same meal offering and libation as in the morning—an offering by fire of pleasing odor to the LORD. ^28-8

**28:9** On the sabbath day: two yearling lambs without blemish, together with two-tenths of a measure[^12] of choice flour with oil mixed in as a meal offering, and with the proper libation— ^28-9

**28:10** a burnt offering for every sabbath, in addition to the regular burnt offering and its libation. ^28-10

**28:11** On your new moons you shall present a burnt offering to the LORD: two bulls of the herd, one ram, and seven yearling lambs, without blemish. ^28-11

**28:12** As meal offering for each bull: three-tenths of a measure of choice flour with oil mixed in. As meal offering for each ram: two-tenths of a measure of choice flour with oil mixed in. ^28-12

**28:13** As meal offering for each lamb: a tenth of a measure of fine flour with oil mixed in. Such shall be the burnt offering of pleasing odor, an offering by fire to the LORD. ^28-13

**28:14** Their libations shall be: half a *hin* of wine for a bull, a third of a *hin* for a ram, and a quarter of a *hin* for a lamb. That shall be the monthly burnt offering for each new moon of the year. ^28-14

**28:15** And there shall be one goat as a sin offering to the LORD, to be offered in addition to the regular burnt offering and its libation. ^28-15

**28:16** In the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month, there shall be a passover sacrifice to the LORD, ^28-16

**28:17** and on the fifteenth day of that month a festival. Unleavened bread shall be eaten for seven days. ^28-17

**28:18** The first day shall be a sacred occasion: you shall not work at your occupations. ^28-18

**28:19** You shall present an offering by fire, a burnt offering, to the LORD: two bulls of the herd, one ram, and seven yearling lambs—see that they are[^13] without blemish. ^28-19

**28:20** The meal offering with them shall be of choice flour with oil mixed in: prepare three-tenths of a measure for a bull, two-tenths for a ram; ^28-20

**28:21** and for each of the seven lambs prepare one-tenth of a measure. ^28-21

**28:22** And there shall be one goat for a sin offering, to make expiation in your behalf. ^28-22

**28:23** You shall present these in addition to the morning portion of the regular burnt offering. ^28-23

**28:24** You shall offer the like daily for seven days as food, an offering by fire of pleasing odor to the LORD; they shall be offered, with their libations, in addition to the regular burnt offering. ^28-24

**28:25** And the seventh day shall be a sacred occasion for you: you shall not work at your occupations. ^28-25

**28:26** On the day of the first fruits, your Feast of Weeks, when you bring an offering of new grain to the LORD, you shall observe a sacred occasion: you shall not work at your occupations. ^28-26

**28:27** You shall present a burnt offering of pleasing odor to the LORD: two bulls of the herd, one ram, seven yearling lambs. ^28-27

**28:28** The meal offering with them shall be of choice flour with oil mixed in, three-tenths of a measure for a bull, two-tenths for a ram, ^28-28

**28:29** and one-tenth for each of the seven lambs. ^28-29

**28:30** And there shall be one goat for expiation in your behalf. ^28-30

**28:31** You shall present them—see that they are[^14] without blemish—with their libations, in addition to the regular burnt offering and its meal offering. ^28-31
