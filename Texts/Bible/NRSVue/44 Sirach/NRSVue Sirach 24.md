# Sirach 24 (NRSVue)

## THE PRAISE OF WISDOM^[This heading is included in the Gk text.]

**24:1** Wisdom praises herself and tells of her glory in the midst of her people. ^24-1

**24:2** In the assembly of the Most High she opens her mouth, and in the presence of his hosts she tells of her glory: ^24-2

**24:3** “I came forth from the mouth of the Most High and covered the earth like a mist. ^24-3

**24:4** I encamped in the heights, and my throne was in a pillar of cloud. ^24-4

**24:5** Alone I compassed the vault of heaven and traversed the depths of the abyss. ^24-5

**24:6** Over waves of the sea, over all the earth, and over every people and nation I have held sway.^[Other ancient authorities read *I have acquired a possession*] ^24-6

**24:7** Among all these I sought a resting place; in whose inheritance should I abide? ^24-7

**24:8** “Then the Creator of all things gave me a command, and my Creator pitched my tent. He said, ‘Encamp in Jacob, and in Israel receive your inheritance.’ ^24-8

**24:9** Before the ages, in the beginning, he created me, and for all the ages I shall not cease to be. ^24-9

**24:10** In the holy tent I ministered before him, and so I was established in Zion. ^24-10

**24:11** Thus in the beloved city he gave me a resting place, and in Jerusalem was my domain. ^24-11

**24:12** I took root in an honored people; in the portion of the Lord is my inheritance. ^24-12

**24:13** “I grew tall like a cedar in Lebanon and like a cypress on the heights of Hermon. ^24-13

**24:14** I grew tall like a palm tree in En-gedi^[Other ancient authorities read *on the beaches*] and like rosebushes in Jericho; like a fair olive tree in the field and like a plane tree beside water^[Other ancient authorities omit *beside water*] I grew tall. ^24-14

**24:15** Like cassia and camel’s thorn I gave forth perfume, and like choice myrrh I spread my fragrance, like galbanum, onycha, and stacte, and like the odor of incense in the tent. ^24-15

**24:16** Like a terebinth I spread out my branches, and my branches are glorious and graceful. ^24-16

**24:17** Like the vine I bud forth delights, and my blossoms become glorious and abundant fruit.^[Other ancient authorities add as 24.18: *I am the mother of beautiful love, of fear, of knowledge, and of holy hope; I give to all my children; these things are eternal for those who are named by him.*] ^24-17

**24:19** “Come to me, you who desire me, and eat your fill of my fruits. ^24-19

**24:20** For the memory of me is sweeter than honey and the possession of me sweeter than the honeycomb. ^24-20

**24:21** Those who eat of me will hunger for more, and those who drink of me will thirst for more. ^24-21

**24:22** Whoever obeys me will not be put to shame, and those who work with me will not sin.” ^24-22

## Wisdom and the law

**24:23** All this is the book of the covenant of the Most High God, the law that Moses commanded us as an inheritance for the congregations of Jacob.^[Other ancient authorities add as 24.24: *Do not cease to be strong in the Lord, cling to him so that he may strengthen you; the Lord Almighty alone is God, and besides him there is no savior.*] ^24-23

**24:25** It becomes full, like the Pishon, with wisdom and like the Tigris at the time of the spring harvest. ^24-25

**24:26** It fills up, like the Euphrates, with understanding, and like the Jordan at harvest time. ^24-26

**24:27** It pours forth instruction like the Nile,^[Syr: Gk *It makes instruction shine forth like light*] like the Gihon at the time of vintage. ^24-27

**24:28** The first man did not know wisdom^[Gk *her*] fully, nor will the last one fathom her. ^24-28

**24:29** For her thoughts are more abundant than the sea and her counsel deeper than the great abyss. ^24-29

**24:30** I came forth like a canal from a river, like a water channel into a garden. ^24-30

**24:31** I said, “I will water my garden and drench my flower beds.” And look, my canal became a river and my river a sea. ^24-31

**24:32** I will again make instruction shine forth like the dawn, and I will make it clear from far away. ^24-32

**24:33** I will again pour out teaching like prophecy and leave it to all future generations. ^24-33

**24:34** Observe that I have not labored for myself alone but for all who seek wisdom.^[Gk *her*] ^24-34
