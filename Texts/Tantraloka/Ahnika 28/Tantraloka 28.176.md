## Mūla

तदच्छिद्रं ममास्त्वीश पवित्रेण तवाज्ञया ।
मूलमन्त्रः पूरयेति क्रियानियममित्यथ ॥ ^mula

tadacchidraṃ mamāstvīśa pavitreṇa tavājñayā |
mūlamantraḥ pūrayeti kriyāniyamamityatha || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 04 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 2#^pg97|p. 80]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=97>))
>
> Then, holding the thread in the hands joined (together), one should recite this (prayer): “O pervasive Lord, if willingly or involuntarily, I have omitted to do something (I should have done), by virtue of the sacred thread and by Your command, O Lord, may that not be a weakening defect (tadachidra) for me.” (This prayer should be followed by) the root Mantra (and the words) “complete the ritual requirement (kriyāniyama)”. (with 28.175–176)
