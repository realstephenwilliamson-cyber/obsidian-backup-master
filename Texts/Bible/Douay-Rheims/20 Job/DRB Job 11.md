# Job 11 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Job 10|← Job 10]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Job 12|Job 12 →]]

**11:1** Then Sophar the Naamathite answered, and said: ^11-1

**11:2** Shall not he that speaketh much, hear also? or shall a man full of talk be justified? ^11-2

**11:3** Shall men hold their peace to thee only? and when thou hast mocked others, shall no man confute thee? ^11-3

**11:4** For thou hast said: My word is pure, and I am clean in thy sight. ^11-4

**11:5** And I wish that God would speak with thee, and would open his lips to thee, ^11-5

**11:6** That he might shew thee the secrets of wisdom, and that his law is manifold, and thou mightest understand that he exacteth much less of thee, than thy iniquity deserveth. ^11-6

**11:7** Peradventure thou wilt comprehend the steps of God, and wilt find out the Almighty perfectly? ^11-7

**11:8** He is higher than heaven, and what wilt thou do? he is deeper than hell, and how wilt thou know? ^11-8

**11:9** The measure of him is longer than the earth, and broader than the sea. ^11-9

**11:10** If he shall overturn all things, or shall press them together, who shall contradict him? ^11-10

**11:11** For he knoweth the vanity of men, and when he seeth iniquity, doth he not consider it? ^11-11

**11:12** A vain man is lifted up into pride, and thinketh himself born free like a wild ass’s colt. ^11-12

**11:13** Rut thou hast hardened thy heart, and hast spread thy hands to him. ^11-13

**11:14** If thou wilt put away from thee the iniquity that is in thy hand, and lot not injustice remain in thy tabernacle: ^11-14

**11:15** Then mayst thou lift up thy face without spot, and thou shalt be steadfast, and shalt not fear. ^11-15

**11:16** Thou shalt also forget misery, and remember it only as waters that are passed away. ^11-16

**11:17** And brightness like that of the noonday, shall arise to thee at evening: and when thou shalt think thyself consumed, thou shalt rise as the day star. ^11-17

**11:18** And thou shalt have confidence, hope being set before thee, and being buried thou shalt sleep secure. ^11-18

**11:19** Thou shalt rest, and there shall be none to make thee afraid: and many shall entreat thy face. ^11-19

**11:20** But the eyes of the wicked shall decay, and the way to escape shall fail them, and their hope the abomination of the soul. ^11-20
