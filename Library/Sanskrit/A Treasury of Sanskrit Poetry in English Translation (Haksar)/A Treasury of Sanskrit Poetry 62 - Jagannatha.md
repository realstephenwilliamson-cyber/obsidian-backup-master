#### 164. The Retort (J.M. Masson)

"Small-waisted girl why are you so thin?" 

"Why do you concern yourself with other people's affairs?" 

"Tel l me nonetheless, and give me joy." 

"Go away, traveller, your wife will tell you;"

1 65 

#### 165. A Word of Warning (V.N. Misra, L. Nathan and S. Vatsyayan)

My soul , I tel l you, watch out. Don't take up with 

that cowherd. 

Whose skin is the hue of fresh rain clouds, the one who 

pastures H i s herd in Vrindavana. He's shrewd. He' ll charm you 

first with his smile, Then his looks. Your senses will fail, and then oblivion.
