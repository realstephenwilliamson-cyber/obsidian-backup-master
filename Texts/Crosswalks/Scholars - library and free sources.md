## Free text collections

- **Muktabodha Digital Library**: [Muktabodha Indological Research Institute - Digital Library](https://muktabodha.org/digital-library/): Entry page for the library: 570+ searchable Sanskrit e-texts (Saiva, Virasaiva, Pancaratra and other traditions, including the KSTS Kashmir Saiva corpus Dyczkowski and others keyed in), 2,000+ French Institute of Pondicherry paper transcripts, and Vedic manuscript images from Gokarna; 3,000+ texts in all. Free, no registration mentioned. The page points to the new e-text interface (muktabodha-digital-library.org) and the legacy catalogue (muktalib7.com/DL_CATALOG_ROOT/digital_library.htm, seen in search results) which is the only route to the IFP and Vedic collections.
- **GRETIL**: [GRETIL - Göttingen Register of Electronic Texts in Indian Languages](https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil.html): Free, standardized machine-readable Sanskrit (also Pali, Prakrit, Dravidian, Tibetan) e-texts contributed by scholars and institutions, in TEI XML, HTML and plain text, with cumulative downloads by language; organised by Vedic, epic, religious literature, philosophy, grammar and sastra. Not a Saiva-specialist archive, but a standard first stop for e-texts.
- **SARIT**: [SARIT - Search and Retrieval of Indic Texts](https://sarit.indology.info/): Columbia/Heidelberg project: citable TEI-encoded electronic editions of Sanskrit and other Indian-language texts with documented edit history; Creative Commons licensed, free to all; master files maintained on GitHub. Strong in philosophical and sastric texts; Saiva coverage not itemised on the landing page.
- **Digital Corpus of Sanskrit (DCS)**: [Digital Corpus of Sanskrit (Oliver Hellwig)](http://www.sanskrit-linguistics.org/dcs/index.php?contents=impressum): Lemmatized, part-of-speech-tagged Sanskrit corpus 'from all layers and genres' maintained by Oliver Hellwig (Berlin); free web interface with word queries, KWIC search, parallel-text and Vedic-concordance tools and per-text reading view; CC BY 3.0; full data dump on GitHub (github.com/OliverHellwig/sanskrit, seen in search results).
- **Kashmir Series of Texts and Studies (KSTS) on the Internet Archive**: [Internet Archive - KSTS vol. XXXI, Svacchandatantra with Ksemaraja's Uddyota (Digital Library of India scan)](https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.282917): Example KSTS volume: public-domain scan from the Digital Library of India collection (uploaded by Public Resource, 2017), 337 pp., downloadable as PDF and PDF-with-OCR-text. Other DLI scans of KSTS volumes seen in search results: vol. I (https://archive.org/details/dli.ernet.407482), vol. II (https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.281055), vols. VI-VII (https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.282872).
- **Kashmir Series of Texts and Studies (KSTS) on the Internet Archive**: [Internet Archive - KSTS catalogue (Research and Publications Department, J&K)](https://archive.org/details/ugNZ_kashmir-series-of-texts-and-studies-ksts-catalog-research-and-publications-department-jk): PDF catalogue of the series (uploaded 2021 to the Digital Library of India / JaiGyan collections) - useful for identifying volume numbers to search for; it does not link to the volumes themselves.
