In gāndharva singing only one or a maximum of two notes could 225. 

be dropped - i.e., a jāti could be rendered only hexatonic or pentatonic. In dhruvā singing, however, even three notes could be dropped. The avakrsiā dhruvā was one such, which employed only four notes (NS. 28, 77). Abhinava specifies there that such a four note formation can only be used in dhruvā singing. He also says, that, the avakrst{ā dhruvā is used in karuna rasa and has prolific long syllables.
