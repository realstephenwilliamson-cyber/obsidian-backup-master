# Zohar 3.155a

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 8.11 - Parashat Be-Ha'alotekha#^z3-155a|Parashat Be-Ha’alotekha, at 3:155a]]

*Volume 3, folio 155a* · Beha'alotcha

*Passage Beha'alotcha 20:122 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 3.154b#^beha-alotcha-20-122|Beha'alotcha 20:122]].*

**Beha'alotcha 20:123** Now the third standard also had under it three divisions. One contained sixty-two thousand and seven hundred; the second forty-one thousand and five hundred; whilst the third contained fifty-three thousand and four hundred. All this besides all the other grades scattered all around them, grades upon grades innumerable, as well as lower grades, executioners, who have the impudence of a dog and bite like an ass. Woe to whoever finds himself near them and under their judgement! ^beha-alotcha-20-123

**Beha'alotcha 21:124** On the fourth side the fourth standard, on the west, had for its symbol Man, the angel Raphael,[^1] with whom there is healing. ^beha-alotcha-21-124

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Beha'alotcha 21:125.*

**Beha'alotcha 21:126** Blessed are Israel in whose glory the Holy One, blessed be He, delights, and to whom He assigned a portion above all other nations and in whose praise He glorifies Himself, as we read: “And he said unto me, Thou art my servant”, etc. (Isa. 49, 3).’ ^beha-alotcha-21-126

**Beha'alotcha 22:127** AND IT CAME TO PASS WHEN THE ARK SET FORWARD…. R. Eleazar said: ‘What is the meaning of the inverted letter *Nun* introduced here twice?[^2] ^beha-alotcha-22-127

**Beha'alotcha 22:128** We explain it thus. We read a little before: “And the ark of the covenant of the Lord went before them three days’ journey, to seek a resting-place for them,’. Now, as soon as the ark set off the Nun[^3] accompanied it, with its face turned towards Israel. The Shekinah ever hovered over the ark, but the love of the Holy One, blessed be He, towards Israel was such that even though they strayed from the straight path He would not forsake them, but always turned His countenance towards them, for otherwise they could not endure in the world. ^beha-alotcha-22-128

**Beha'alotcha 22:129** So that whilst “the ark… went before them three days’ journey”, the *Nun* (symbol of the Shekinah) remained inseparable from it, and accompanied it, yet turned her face away from the ark and towards Israel, like a young hart that, whilst going, turns its face towards its starting-point. ^beha-alotcha-22-129

**Beha'alotcha 22:130** Thus when Moses said, “Rise, O Lord…”, implying “do not forsake us, turn Thy face towards us”, the *Nun* turned round facing Israel in the manner of one turning his face towards his beloved friend. “And when it rested” the *Nun* turned its face again towards the ark.’ ^beha-alotcha-22-130

**Beha'alotcha 22:131** Said R. Simeon: ‘O Eleazar, assuredly it is as you said, saving that when the ark rested the Shekinah did *not* turn her countenance away from Israel. This is clearly shown by the second *Nun*, which is also of an inverted shape. ^beha-alotcha-22-131

**Beha'alotcha 22:132** The truth is that when Moses said “Return, O Lord”, and the ark rested, the Shekinah turned back and stood on the other side of the ark,[^4] but her countenance turned both towards Israel and towards the ark. Israel, however, caused afterwards the turning away of the Shekinah from them. So we read: *And the people were as murmurers (K’mithonnim).[^5]* ^beha-alotcha-22-132

[^1]: Whose chief function, as his name (RaFA=to cure) denotes, was the healing of men.
[^2]: In the Massoretic text there are inserted here two isolated *Nuns*, turned upside down and sideways, one preceding and the other following the two verses beginning with our text.
[^3]: i.e. the Shekinah symbolized by this letter.
[^4]: i.e. instead of between the ark and Israel, as during the journey.
[^5]: A play upon the Hebrew word, which contains two *Nuns* of ordinary shape, their faces, as it were, being away from Israel.
