## The eternal dwelling of God in Zion

*A Song of Ascents.*

**132:1** O LORD, remember in David’s favor all the hardships he endured; ^132-1

**132:2** how he swore to the LORD and vowed to the Mighty One of Jacob, ^132-2

**132:3** “I will not enter my house or get into my bed; ^132-3

**132:4** I will not give sleep to my eyes or slumber to my eyelids, ^132-4

**132:5** until I find a place for the LORD, a dwelling place for the Mighty One of Jacob.” ^132-5

**132:6** We heard of it in Ephrathah; we found it in the fields of Jaar. ^132-6

**132:7** “Let us go to his dwelling place; let us worship at his footstool.” ^132-7

**132:8** Rise up, O LORD, and go to your resting place, you and the ark of your might. ^132-8

**132:9** Let your priests be clothed with righteousness, and let your faithful shout for joy. ^132-9

**132:10** For your servant David’s sake do not turn away the face of your anointed one. ^132-10

**132:11** The LORD swore to David a sure oath from which he will not turn back: “One of the sons of your body I will set on your throne. ^132-11

**132:12** If your sons keep my covenant and my decrees that I shall teach them, their sons also, forevermore, shall sit on your throne.” ^132-12

**132:13** For the LORD has chosen Zion; he has desired it for his habitation: ^132-13

**132:14** “This is my resting place forever; here I will reside, for I have desired it. ^132-14

**132:15** I will abundantly bless its provisions; I will satisfy its poor with bread. ^132-15

**132:16** Its priests I will clothe with salvation, and its faithful will shout for joy. ^132-16

**132:17** There I will cause a horn to sprout up for David; I have prepared a lamp for my anointed one. ^132-17

**132:18** His enemies I will clothe with disgrace, but on him, his crown will gleam.” ^132-18
