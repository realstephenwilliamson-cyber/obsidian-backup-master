# θυγάτηρ

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · noun

**Dodson:** a daughter — a daughter; hence (Hebraistic?), of any female descendent, however far removed; even of one unrelated: my young lady. (Strong's 2364; headword θυγάτηρ, τρός, ἡ)
