## Mūla

उक्तं श्रीपूर्वशास्त्रे च न द्वैतं नापि चाद्वयम् ।
लिङ्गपूजादिकं सर्वमित्युपक्रम्य शम्भुना ॥ ^mula

uktaṃ śrīpūrvaśāstre ca na dvaitaṃ nāpi cādvayam |
liṅgapūjādikaṃ sarvamityupakramya śambhunā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. III
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 03 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v03 05 - Chapter Four, part 4#^pg139|p. 126]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 03.pdf#page=139>))
>
> Moreover, Lord Śiva has said in the Śrīpūrvaśāstra (the Mālinīvijayottara), in the passage that begins: ‘there is neither duality nor nonduality and no worship of the Liṅga or the like . . . .’ (He goes on to say that) ‘everything is prescribed here, or else (one could say that everything is) prohibited.’ (The point is that) ‘the Yogas (of other systems), along with the various ancillaries such as breath control, are artificial (mental constructs) (kṛtrima). Thus, they are not worth (even) a sixteenth part of this one, which is uncreated (and authentic) (akṛtrima).’ (with 4.106–108ab)
