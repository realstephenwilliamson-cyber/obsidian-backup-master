## Mūla

कुर्यादिति गुरुः प्राह स्वरूपाप्यायनद्वयात् ।
तारो वर्णोऽथ संबुद्धिपदं त्वामित्यतः परम् ॥ ^mula

kuryāditi guruḥ prāha svarūpāpyāyanadvayāt |
tāro varṇo'tha saṃbuddhipadaṃ tvāmityataḥ param || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 08 - Chapter Seventeen#^pg164|p. 144]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=164>))
>
> The teacher has said that, in the course of invocation (of each principle), one should (utter) a phoneme from the Mātṛkā (order of the alphabet), and in the course of (its) worship (pūjā), from the Mālinī (order), because the two, respectively, (differentiate) the nature (of each one) and nourish (them). (with 17.10cd–11ab)
>
> (First comes) the Saviour (NAMAH), now the (appropriate) phoneme (54 PHA), the name (of the principle) in the vocative (DHARE), and after that (the second person pronoun) ‘you’ (TVĀM), the verb (to invoke) in the first person singular (ĀVĀHAYĀMD, then the Energizer (dīpaka) (NAMA), (the pronoun) ‘to you’ (TUBHYAM), the name (of the principle) in the dative (e.g. DHARĀYAI) and then after that (finally), the appropriate Energizer (NAMAH or SVĀHĀ). The various rites should be performed in association with the Mantra, modified (according to the principle being purified) (āhamantra). (with 17.11cd–13ab)
