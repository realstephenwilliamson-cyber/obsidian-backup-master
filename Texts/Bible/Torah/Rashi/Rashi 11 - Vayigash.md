# Rashi 11 - Vayigash

← [[Rashi 10 - Miketz]] · [[Rashi (Metsudah)]] · [[Rashi 12 - Vayechi]] →

Rashi on *[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^44-18|Genesis 44:18–47:27]]* · Text: [[Torah 11 - Vayigash]]

## Chapter 44

### 44:18

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^44-18|Genesis 44:18]]

**He approached … in my master’s ears.** [Meaning:] may my words penetrate your ears.

**And do not be angry.** From this you may infer that he spoke harshly to him.

**For you are equal to Pharaoh.** [Meaning:] you are as esteemed in my eyes as a king. This is the plain meaning. But, its Midrashic explanation is: “Ultimately you will be stricken with leprosy on his account as the Pharaoh was stricken on account of my grandmother Sarah for detaining her for only one night.” Another explanation [for: “You are equal to Pharaoh.”] Just as Pharaoh issues decrees without carrying them out, and makes promises without fulfilling them, you, too, do the same. Is this, then, what you meant by “setting your eyes” when you said that you would “set your eyes on him?” Another explanation for “You are equal to Pharaoh;” If you will provoke me I will kill you and your master.

### 44:19

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^44-19|Genesis 44:19]]

**My master asked his servants.** From the start you encountered us with subterfuge. Why did you need to ask all these questions? Were we seeking [to marry] your daughter, or were you seeking [to marry] our sister? Nevertheless, “We said to my master”—we withheld nothing from you.

### 44:20

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^44-20|Genesis 44:20]]

**His brother is dead.** It was because of fear that he uttered an untruth. He thought, “If I tell him that he is alive, he will say, ‘Bring him to me.’”

**Alone of his mother.** [I.e.,] from that mother he has no other brother.

### 44:22

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^44-22|Genesis 44:22]]

**For if he left his father he would die.** If he will leave his father then we are concerned that he may die en route for his mother, too, died while en route.

### 44:29

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^44-29|Genesis 44:29]]

**And misfortune befall him.** For the Satan attempts to do harm during a time of danger.

**You will bring my white head down, etc.** As long as he is beside me I find comfort through him [for the loss] of his mother and his brother. And if he dies, it will seem to me as if all three died the same day.

### 44:31

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^44-31|Genesis 44:31]]

**When he sees that the lad is not [with us], he will die.** [I.e.,] his father [will die] from grief.

### 44:32

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^44-32|Genesis 44:32]]

**For your servant became surety for the lad.** If you should ask why I involve myself in this fray more than my other brothers? [the reason is that] that they are all out [of this matter] whereas, I have tied myself [to it] with a strong bond by accepting to be an outcast in both worlds.

### 44:33

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^44-33|Genesis 44:33]]

**Let your servant remain, etc.** I am superior to him for all purposes—in strength, in waging war and for service.

## Chapter 45

### 45:1

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^45-1|Genesis 45:1]]

**Yoseif could not contain his emotions in the presence of all who stood.** He could not bear that the Egyptians standing by him would hear his brothers’ humiliation when he would make himself known to them.

### 45:2

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^45-2|Genesis 45:2]]

**And the house of Pharaoh heard.** [בֵּית פַּרְעֹה is the same as] בֵּיתוֹ שֶׁל פַּרְעֹה i.e., his servants and his household. Here, the reference is not to a literal house but, rather, is similar to בֵּית יִשְׂרָאֵל —the household of Israel, or בֵּית יְהוּדָה —the household of Yehudah, *meisnede* in O.F.

### 45:3

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^45-3|Genesis 45:3]]

**They were shocked at his presence.** Out of shame.

### 45:4

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^45-4|Genesis 45:4]]

**Please come close to me.** He saw them recoiling backwards so he thought: “Now, my brothers feel ashamed,” so he called to them tenderly and pleadingly and showed them that he was circumcised.

### 45:5

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^45-5|Genesis 45:5]]

**To preserve life.** [Meaning:] so that I may be a preserver of life for you.

### 45:6

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^45-6|Genesis 45:6]]

**For it is now two years that there has been famine.** [Two years], of the [seven] hunger years, have passed.

### 45:8

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^45-8|Genesis 45:8]]

**As a father.** [Meaning:] as a colleague and protector of the king.

### 45:9

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^45-9|Genesis 45:9]]

**Go up to my father.** Eretz Yisrael is higher than all the countries.

### 45:11

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^45-11|Genesis 45:11]]

**Lest you become impoverished.** דִּלְמָא תִּתְמַסְכֵּן —lest you become impoverished [Onkelos]. From the same root as מוֹרִישׁ וּמַעֲשִׁיר .

### 45:12

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^45-12|Genesis 45:12]]

**Behold, your eyes see—** my glory, and that I am your brother for I am circumcised as you are and, furthermore, “That I speaks to you” in the Holy Language [Hebrew].

**Along with my brother Binyomin’s eyes.** He mentions them all alike to imply: “Just as I have no animosity toward my brother Binyomin, for he was not there when I was sold, so, too, I have no animosity in my heart toward you.”

### 45:14

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^45-14|Genesis 45:14]]

**He fell upon his brother Binyomin and wept.** [He wept] for the two Temples which were destined to be in Binyomin’s territory and, in the end, will be destroyed.

**And Binyomin wept upon his neck.** For the Tabernacle of Shiloh which was destined to be in Yoseif’s territory and in the end will be destroyed.

### 45:15

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^45-15|Genesis 45:15]]

**After that.** After they saw him weeping and his intentions toward them were peaceful—**[After that] his brothers spoke with him.** Because at first they felt shame before him.

### 45:16

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^45-16|Genesis 45:16]]

**The news was heard in Pharaoh’s house.** [בֵּית פַּרְעֹה is] the same as בְּבֵית פַּרְעֹה —in Pharaoh’s house, and here it [בַּיִת —house] is meant literally.

### 45:17

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^45-17|Genesis 45:17]]

**Load up your beasts.** With grain.

### 45:18

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^45-18|Genesis 45:18]]

**The best of the Land of Egypt.** [Referring to] the Land of Goshen. [Here] he prophesied without realizing it because it would ultimately become like the depths of the sea which contains no fish.

**The fat of the land.** חֵלֶב always means the best part of something.

### 45:19

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^45-19|Genesis 45:19]]

**Now you are commanded—** Through me to tell them—

**To do the following.** Tell them that it is with my permission.

### 45:23

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^45-23|Genesis 45:23]]

**He sent the following.** [Meaning:] according to this amount. And what was the amount? “Ten male donkeys, etc.”

**The best of Egypt.** We find in the Talmud that he sent him aged wine from which old people derive pleasure. [However, in the] Midrash Aggadah [we find that it was] pounded beans.

**Grain, bread.** As Onkelos translates it.

**And food.** Things that are eaten with bread.

### 45:24

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^45-24|Genesis 45:24]]

**Do not be troubled along the way.** Do not become involved in halachic discussion so that the road not become a menace to you. An alternate explanation: Do not take very large steps and arrive [at your evening stop] while there is still daylight. As for the plain meaning of the verse, it can be said that, because they were embarrassed, he was worried lest they quarrel on the way concerning his being sold, to dispute with one another by saying, “It was because of you that he was sold” [or,] “You are the one who told slanderous things about him, and caused us to hate him.”

### 45:26

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^45-26|Genesis 45:26]]

**He is ruler.** [וְכִי is used as וַאֲשֶׁר]—*that* he is ruler.

**His heart stood still.** [וַיָפָג לִבּוֹ means:] his heart changed and he ceased believing. His heart was not swayed by their words. In Mishnaic Hebrew we find it as מְפִיגִין טַעְמָן. This is similar to “Without ceasing” or [for example] וְרֵיחוֹ לֹא נָמָר is translated וְרֵיחֵיהּ לָא פָג —“His scent has not ceased.”

### 45:27

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^45-27|Genesis 45:27]]

**All the words of Yoseif.** He [Yoseif] gave them as a sign—the subject that he was studying at the time that he left him [Yaakov]— the topic of עֶגְלָה עֲרוּפָה —the heifer whose neck is broken. This is [alluded to by] what is said: “And he saw the wagons [עֲגָלוֹת] that Yoseif had sent.” and it does not state, “Which Pharaoh had sent.”

**The spirit of Yaakov was revived.** The Divine Presence, which had left him, rested on him.

### 45:28

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^45-28|Genesis 45:28]]

**It is too much.** Much joy and pleasure is still in store for me since my son Yoseif, is alive.

## Chapter 46

### 46:1

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^46-1|Genesis 46:1]]

**To Beer Sheva.** [בְּאֵרָה שָּׁבַע] is the same as לִבְאֵר שָׁבַע. The suffix ה' replaces the prefix ל' .

**To the God of his father Yitzchok.** A person must honor his father more than he must honor his grandfather. Therefore, he associated [the sacrifices] with Yitzchok and not with Avraham.

### 46:2

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^46-2|Genesis 46:2]]

**Yaakov, Yaakov.** [The repetition of Yaakov] is a term of affection.

### 46:3

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^46-3|Genesis 46:3]]

**Do not be afraid to go down to Egypt.** [He was told this] because he was distressed that he was required to leave Eretz Yisrael.

### 46:4

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^46-4|Genesis 46:4]]

**And I will bring you up.** [With these words] He promised him that he will be buried in Eretz Yisrael.

### 46:6

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^46-6|Genesis 46:6]]

**That they had acquired in the Land of Canaan.** But, that which he acquired in Padan Arom he gave entirely to Eisov for his share in the Cave of Machpeilah. He said, “Possessions acquired outside of Eretz Yisrael hold no value for me.” This is what he meant by: [“The grave] that I acquired for myself.” He put piles of gold and silver for him [Eisov] similar to a heap [כְּרִי] and said to him, “Take these.”

### 46:7

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^46-7|Genesis 46:7]]

**And his granddaughters.** [Referring to:] Asher’s daughter, Serach and Leivi’s daughter, Yocheved.

### 46:8

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^46-8|Genesis 46:8]]

**Who were coming.** Concerning that particular time Scripture refers to them as “those who are coming.” There is [therefore] no need to wonder why it does not write, “who came.”

### 46:10

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^46-10|Genesis 46:10]]

**The son of the Canaanite woman.** The son of Deenah who was violated by a Canaanite. When they killed Shechem, Deenah refused to leave [the city] until Shimon swore to her that he would marry her.

### 46:15

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^46-15|Genesis 46:15]]

**These are the sons of Leah … along with his daughter Deenah.** The males are attributed to Leah while the females are attributed to Yaakov, to teach you that if the woman emits seed first then she will give birth to a male and if the man emits seed first then she will give birth to a female.

**Thirty-three.** However, if you count them individually you will find only thirty-two! But this, [the missing one] is Yocheved who was born “between the walls” as they entered the city, as it is said: “That was *born* to Leivi in Egypt.” Her birth was in Egypt but she was not conceived in Egypt.

### 46:19

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^46-19|Genesis 46:19]]

**The sons of Rochel, Yaakov’s wife.** Whereas concerning all [the other wives], the term אֵשֶׁת [wife] is not used for them. But, this is because she was the mainstay of the house.

### 46:26

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^46-26|Genesis 46:26]]

**All the souls coming with Yaakov.** [These] who left the Land of Canaan to come to Egypt. The word הַבָּאָה, here, is not in the past tense but, rather, in the present tense, as in: “In the evening she would come,” and as in: “And behold, his daughter Rochel was coming with the sheep.” Therefore, the accent is on the latter syllable on the א' because when they left, coming from the Land of Canaan they were only sixty-six [souls]. [But,] the second time [הַבָּאָה is mentioned]: “All the souls of the House of Yaakov that came to Egypt, were seventy,” it is in the past tense, therefore, the accent is on the first syllable, the ב'. This is because once they came there, they were seventy for they found Yoseif and his two sons there and Yocheved was added to them “between the walls.” According to the one who says that a twin sister was born with each tribe we must assume that they died before going down to Egypt, for they are not counted here. The following I found in Vayikra Rabbah: Eisov had [only] six souls and Scripture refers to them as “The *souls* of his household”—plural, because they worshiped many gods, whereas, Yaakov, had seventy [souls] and Scripture refers to them as “a *soul*” because they all worshiped one God.

### 46:28

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^46-28|Genesis 46:28]]

**So that he might direct him.** As Onkelos explains it: to prepare a place for him and to show how to settle there.

**Ahead of him.** [I.e.,] before he [Yaakov] will arrive there. The Midrashic explanation; לְהוֹרוֹת לְפָנָיו: to prepare a house of study from which would emanate [Torah] teaching.

### 46:29

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^46-29|Genesis 46:29]]

**Yoseif harnessed his chariot.** He himself harnessed the horses to the chariot to be diligent in honoring his father.

**He appeared before him.** [Meaning:] Yoseif appeared to his father.

**And he wept on his neck for a long time.** [וַיֵבְךְּ עוֹד refers to] profuse weeping. Similarly: “For He does not place upon a person עוֹד” [where עוֹד] is a term for profusion, meaning: He [God] does not place upon him accusations which are in excess of his [actual] sins. Here, too, he wept a great deal—more than is usual. But, Yaakov did not fall on Yoseif’s neck nor did he kiss him. Our Sages explained that this was because he was reading the “Shema.”

### 46:30

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^46-30|Genesis 46:30]]

**Now I can die.** The plain meaning is as Onkelos explains it. But, the Midrashic explanation is: “I had thought that I would die twice—in this world and in the World-to-Come—because the Divine Presence had departed from me and I thought that God would hold me responsible for your death. But, now that you are still alive I will die but once.

### 46:31

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^46-31|Genesis 46:31]]

**I will say to him, my brothers, etc.** And I will also tell him: “The men are shepherds, etc.”

### 46:34

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^46-34|Genesis 46:34]]

**So that you will be able to settle in the Land of Goshen.** And it [the land] is what you require because it is a land of pasture. And when you tell him that you have no expertise in other work he will distance you from himself and he will settle you there.

**Since every shepherd is abhorrent to Egypt.** Because they [the sheep] are deities to them.

## Chapter 47

### 47:2

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^47-2|Genesis 47:2]]

**From among his brothers.** From the least strong among them, who do not [even] appear strong, for if he [Pharaoh] would find them strong he might induct them into his military. These [weaker ones] were: Reuvain, Shimon, Leivi, Yissachar and Binyomin, those whose names Moshe did not repeat when he blessed them. But, the names of the strong ones he repeated: “And this is for Yehudah—hear, God, the voice of Yehudah.” “And about Gad he said, ‘Blessed be He that extends Gad.’” “And about Naphtali he said, ‘Naphtali.’” “And about Dan he said, ‘Dan.’” And so, too, concerning Zevulun. And so, too, concerning Asher. The above is quoted from Bereishis Rabbah which is the Aggadah of the Land of Israel. But, in our Babylonian Talmud we find that those whose names Moshe repeated were the weak ones and those were the ones he [Yoseif] brought before Pharaoh. And the fact that Yehudah’s name is repeated± - it is not repeated due to his being weak—but there is a reason for this as is mentioned in Bava Kamma. In the Berayso of the Sifrei we have the version—in [the portion of] *V’zos Haberachah*—that is the same as our Talmud [in Bava Kamma.]

### 47:6

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^47-6|Genesis 47:6]]

**Capable men.** That are experts in their occupation of tending sheep.

**Over my [herds].** Over my sheep.

### 47:7

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^47-7|Genesis 47:7]]

**And Yaakov blessed.** This was a greeting, as is customary for all who periodically appear before kings, *saluder* [to greet] in O.F.

### 47:9

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^47-9|Genesis 47:9]]

**The years of the temporary residence.** [שְׁנֵי מְגוּרַי means:] the years of my being a stranger. [I.e.,] all my life I have been a stranger in the land.

**I have not attained.** Insofar as happiness is concerned.

### 47:10

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^47-10|Genesis 47:10]]

**Yaakov blessed.** As is customary for those who take leave of royalty, they bless them and take their leave. What blessing did he give him? That the Nile rise as he approached it. [This blessing was welcome to Pharaoh] because the Land of Egypt is not watered by rainfall, but, rather, by the Nile rising and irrigating. From the time that Yaakov blessed him, Pharaoh would come to the Nile and it would rise toward him and, thereby, irrigate the land.

### 47:11

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^47-11|Genesis 47:11]]

**Ramseis.** It was part of the Land of Goshen.

### 47:12

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^47-12|Genesis 47:12]]

**According to the children.** [Meaning:] according to the needs of all the members of the household.

### 47:13

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^47-13|Genesis 47:13]]

**There was no bread in all the land.** [Scripture now] returns to the beginning of the account, to the beginning of the famine years.

**They were worn out.** [וַתֵּלַהּ is] similar to וַתִּלְאֶה —meaning exhaustion as Onkelos translates it. Similar to this is: “As an exhausted person [כְּמִתְלַהְלֵהַּ] who shoots firebrands.”

### 47:14

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^47-14|Genesis 47:14]]

**Through the purchases they made.** [Meaning:] they gave him the money.

### 47:15

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^47-15|Genesis 47:15]]

**Is no.** As Onkelos translates it: שְׁלִים “is ended.”

### 47:17

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^47-17|Genesis 47:17]]

**He tended them.** [וַיְנַהֲלֵם is] similar to וַיְנַהֲגֵם —He led them. Similar to this is: “There is no one to lead her,” [or:] “He leads me beside tranquil waters.”

### 47:18

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^47-18|Genesis 47:18]]

**The second year—** Of the famine years.

**For the money is used up, etc.** [Meaning:] that the money and livestock are used up and everything has come into my master’s hand.

**Except our bodies.** The same as: if not for our bodies.

### 47:19

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^47-19|Genesis 47:19]]

**Give us seed-grain.** To sow in the ground. Although Yoseif had said, “And for another five years there will be no plowing nor harvest,” [nevertheless,] as soon as Yaakov came to Egypt blessing followed in his wake, and they began to sow and the famine ended. We learned this in the Tosefta of Sotah.

**Not become desolate.** לֹא תֵשָׁם means let it not become desolate. [Onkelos translates it] לָא תְבוּר from the term שְׂדֵה בּוּר —a barren field—one which is not plowed.

### 47:20

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^47-20|Genesis 47:20]]

**The land became Pharaoh’s [property].** [Meaning:] it had been bought for him.

### 47:21

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^47-21|Genesis 47:21]]

**He transferred the people.** Yoseif [transferred them] from city to city as a reminder that they no longer had a share in the land, and settled those of one city in another. Scripture had no need to write this, but only to let you know Yoseif’s praiseworthiness. For his intent was to remove shame from his brothers, so that the people not call them exiles.

**From one end of the border of Egypt, etc.** He did this to all the cities that were in the kingdom of Egypt, from one end of the border to the other.

### 47:22

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^47-22|Genesis 47:22]]

**The priests.** [הַכֹּהֲנִים means] the priests. Whenever the word כֹּהֵן is used it refers to a servant of a deity, except those that refer to high rank as in כֹּהֵן מִדְיָן —“The chief of Midian,” or כֹּהֵן אוֹן —“The chief of On.”

**The priests had an allotment.** [Meaning:] an allotted portion of bread per day.

### 47:23

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^47-23|Genesis 47:23]]

**Here.** [הֵא is] the same as הִנֵּה —behold, as in: “I, too, behold, [הֵא] will avenge your way upon your head.”

### 47:24

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^47-24|Genesis 47:24]]

**For seed for your fields.** For each year [not only the first year].

**And for those of your households.** I.e., as food for your man servants and maid servants that are in your homes.

**For your little ones.** [טַפְּכֶם means] young children.

### 47:25

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^47-25|Genesis 47:25]]

**May we find favor.** That you may do for us as you have said.

**And we will be slaves to Pharaoh.** As regards to paying him this tax annually as a law which will not be repealed.

### 47:27

[[Torah 11 - Vayigash#^47-27|Genesis 47:27]]

**Yisrael lived in the Land of Egypt.** And where [within the Land of Egypt]? In the Land of Goshen, which is part of the Land of Egypt.

**They acquired property there.** [וַיֵּאָחֲזוּ means] taking possession [by settling there].
