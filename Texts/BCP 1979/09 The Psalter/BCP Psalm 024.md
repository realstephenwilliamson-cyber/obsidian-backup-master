# Psalm 24

← [[BCP Psalm 023]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 025]] →

*Domini est terra* · Book One · Fifth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 024]]

**24:1** The earth is the LORD'S and all that is in it, \*  
the world and all who dwell therein. ^24-1

**24:2** For it is he who founded it upon the seas \*  
and made it firm upon the rivers of the deep. ^24-2

**24:3** "Who can ascend the hill of the LORD? \*  
and who can stand in his holy place?" ^24-3

**24:4** "Those who have clean hands and a pure heart, \*  
who have not pledged themselves to falsehood,  
nor sworn by what is a fraud. ^24-4

**24:5** They shall receive a blessing from the LORD \*  
and a just reward from the God of their salvation." ^24-5

**24:6** Such is the generation of those who seek him, \*  
of those who seek your face, O God of Jacob. ^24-6

[p. 614] ^pg614

**24:7** Lift up your heads, O gates;  
lift them high, O everlasting doors; \*  
and the King of glory shall come in. ^24-7

**24:8** "Who is this King of glory?" \*  
"The LORD, strong and mighty,  
the LORD, mighty in battle." ^24-8

**24:9** Lift up your heads, O gates;  
lift them high, O everlasting doors; \*  
and the King of glory shall come in. ^24-9

**24:10** "Who is he, this King of glory?" \*  
"The LORD of hosts,  
he is the King of glory." ^24-10
