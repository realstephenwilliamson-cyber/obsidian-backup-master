# Ezekiel 11 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Ezekiel 10|← Ezekiel 10]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Ezekiel 12|Ezekiel 12 →]]

## Judgment on wicked counselors

**11:1** The spirit lifted me up and brought me to the east gate of the house of the LORD, which faces east. There, at the entrance of the gateway, were twenty-five men; among them I saw Jaazaniah son of Azzur and Pelatiah son of Benaiah, officials of the people. ^11-1

**11:2** He said to me, “Mortal, these are the men who devise iniquity and who give wicked counsel in this city; ^11-2

**11:3** they say, ‘The time is not near to build houses; this city is the pot, and we are the meat.’ ^11-3

**11:4** Therefore prophesy against them; prophesy, O mortal.” ^11-4

**11:5** Then the spirit of the LORD fell upon me, and he said to me, “Say, Thus says the LORD: This is what you think, O house of Israel; I know the things that come into your mind. ^11-5

**11:6** You have killed many in this city and have filled its streets with the slain. ^11-6

**11:7** Therefore thus says the Lord GOD: The slain whom you have placed within it are the meat, and this city is the pot, but you shall be taken out of it. ^11-7

**11:8** You have feared the sword, and I will bring the sword upon you, says the Lord GOD. ^11-8

**11:9** I will take you out of it and give you over to the hands of foreigners and execute judgments upon you. ^11-9

**11:10** You shall fall by the sword; I will judge you at the border of Israel. And you shall know that I am the LORD. ^11-10

**11:11** This city shall not be your pot, and you shall not be the meat inside it; I will judge you at the border of Israel. ^11-11

**11:12** Then you shall know that I am the LORD, whose statutes you have not followed and whose ordinances you have not kept, but you have acted according to the ordinances of the nations that are around you.” ^11-12

**11:13** Now, while I was prophesying, Pelatiah son of Benaiah died. Then I fell down on my face, cried with a loud voice, and said, “Ah Lord GOD! You are finishing off the remnant of Israel!” ^11-13

## God will restore Israel

**11:14** Then the word of the LORD came to me: ^11-14

**11:15** Mortal, your kinsfolk, your own kin, your fellow exiles,^[Gk Syr: Heb *people of your kindred*] the whole house of Israel, all of them, are those of whom the inhabitants of Jerusalem have said, “Stay far from the LORD; to us this land is given for a possession.” ^11-15

**11:16** Therefore say: Thus says the Lord GOD: Though I removed them far away among the nations and though I scattered them among the countries, yet I have been a sanctuary to them for a little while^[Or *a little sanctuary*] in the countries where they have gone. ^11-16

**11:17** Therefore say: Thus says the Lord GOD: I will gather you from the peoples and assemble you out of the countries where you have been scattered, and I will give you the land of Israel. ^11-17

**11:18** When they come there, they will remove from it all its detestable things and all its abominations. ^11-18

**11:19** I will give them one heart and put a new spirit within them;^[Heb mss Gk Syr Vg: MT *you*] I will remove the heart of stone from their flesh and give them a heart of flesh, ^11-19

**11:20** so that they may follow my statutes and keep my ordinances and obey them. Then they shall be my people, and I will be their God. ^11-20

**11:21** But as for those whose heart goes after their detestable things and their abominations,^[Cn: Heb *And to the heart of their detestable things and their abominations their heart goes*] I will bring their deeds upon their own heads, says the Lord GOD. ^11-21

**11:22** Then the cherubim lifted up their wings, with the wheels beside them, and the glory of the God of Israel was above them. ^11-22

**11:23** And the glory of the LORD ascended from the middle of the city and stopped on the mountain east of the city. ^11-23

**11:24** The spirit lifted me up and brought me in a vision by the spirit of God into Chaldea, to the exiles. Then the vision that I had seen left me. ^11-24

**11:25** And I told the exiles all the things that the LORD had shown me. ^11-25
