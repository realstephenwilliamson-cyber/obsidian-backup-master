# ὥρα

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · noun

**Dodson:** an hour, season — (a) a definite space of time, a season, (b) an hour, (c) the particular time for anything. (Strong's 5610; headword ὥρα, ας, ἡ)
