# LXX Psalms 82

← [[LXX Psalms 81]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 83]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 083]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 083]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**82:1** A Song of a Psalm for Asaph.  ^82-1

**82:2** O God, who shall be compared to thee? be not silent, neither be still, O God.  ^82-2

**82:3** For behold, thine enemies have made a noise; and they that hate thee have lifted up the head.  ^82-3

**82:4** Against thy people they have craftily imagined a device, and have taken counsel against thy saints.  ^82-4

**82:5** They have said, Come, and let us utterly destroy them out of the nation; and let the name of Israel be remembered no more at all.  ^82-5

**82:6** For they have taken counsel together with one consent: they have made a confederacy against thee;  ^82-6

**82:7** even the tents of the Idumeans, and the Ismaelites; Moab, and the Agarenes;  ^82-7

**82:8** Gebal, and Ammon, and Amalec; the Philistines also, with them that dwell at Tyre.  ^82-8

**82:9** Yea, Assur too is come with them: they have become a help to the children of Lot. Pause.  ^82-9

**82:10** Do thou to them as to Madiam, and to Sisera; as to Jabin at the brook of Kison.  ^82-10

**82:11** They were utterly destroyed at Aendor: they became as dung for the earth.  ^82-11

**82:12** Make their princes as Oreb and Zeb, and Zebee and Salmana; *even* all their princes:  ^82-12

**82:13** who said, Let us take to ourselves the altar of God as an inheritance.[^1]  ^82-13

**82:14** O my God, make them as a wheel; as stubble before the face of the wind.  ^82-14

**82:15** As fire which shall burn up a wood, as the flame may consume the mountains;  ^82-15

**82:16** so shalt thou persecute them with thy tempest, and trouble them in thine anger.  ^82-16

**82:17** Fill their faces with dishonour; so shall they seek thy name, O Lord.  ^82-17

**82:18** Let them be ashamed and troubled for evermore; yea, let them be confounded and destroyed.  ^82-18

**82:19** And let them know that thy name is Lord; that thou alone art Most High over all the earth.  ^82-19

[^1]: 82:13 *Alex.* sanctuary.
