# κομίζω

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · verb

**Dodson:** I bring, receive, recover — (a) act: I convey, bring, carry, (b) mid: I receive back, receive what has belonged to myself but has been lost, or else promised but kept back, or: I get what has come to be my own by earning, recover. (Strong's 2865; headword κομίζω)
