# Hosea 01

Compare: [[NRSVue Hosea 01|NRSVue]] (NRSVue 1:10–11 = Heb 2:1–2)

## Chapter 1

**1:1** The word of the LORD that came to Hosea son of Beeri, in the reigns of Kings Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah of Judah, and in the reign of King Jeroboam son of Joash of Israel. ^1-1

**1:2** When the LORD first spoke to Hosea, the LORD said to Hosea, “Go, get yourself a wife of whoredom and children of whoredom; for the land will stray[^1] from following the LORD.”[^2] ^1-2

**1:3** So he went and married Gomer daughter of Diblaim. She conceived and bore him a son, ^1-3

**1:4** and the LORD instructed him, “Name him Jezreel; for, I will soon punish the House of Jehu[^3] for the bloody deeds at Jezreel[^4] and put an end to the monarchy of the House of Israel. ^1-4

**1:5** In that day, I will break the bow of Israel in the Valley of Jezreel.” ^1-5

**1:6** She conceived again and bore a daughter; and He said to him, “Name her Lo-ruhamah;[^5] for I will no longer accept the House of Israel or pardon them.[^6] ^1-6

**1:7** (But I will accept the House of Judah. And I will give them victory through the LORD their God; I will not give them victory with bow and sword and battle, by horses and riders.)” ^1-7

**1:8** After weaning Lo-ruhamah, she conceived and bore a son. ^1-8

**1:9** Then He said, “Name him Lo-ammi;[^7] for you[^8] are not My people, and I will not be your [God].”[^9] ^1-9

[^1]: Lit. “whore away.”
[^2]: Force of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Emendation yields “Israel”; cf. next note.
[^4]: See 1 Kings 21.1–24; 2 Kings 9.21–35. Emendation yields “the Baal days”; cf. 2.15.
[^5]: I.e., “Not-accepted”; cf. 2.3, 6, and 25.
[^6]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain; emendation yields “but will disown them”; cf. 9.15 and elsewhere.
[^7]: I.e., “Not-My-People.”
[^8]: I.e., you and your fellow countrymen.
[^9]: Cf. 2.25.
