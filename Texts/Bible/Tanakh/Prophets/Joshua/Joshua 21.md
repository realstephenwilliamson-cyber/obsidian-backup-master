## Chapter 21

**21:1** The heads of the ancestral houses of the Levites approached the priest Eleazar, Joshua son of Nun, and the heads of the ancestral houses of the Israelite tribes, ^21-1

**21:2** and spoke to them at Shiloh in the land of Canaan, as follows: “The LORD commanded through Moses that we be given towns to live in, along with their pastures for our livestock.” ^21-2

**21:3** So the Israelites, in accordance with the LORD’s command, assigned to the Levites, out of their own portions, the following towns with their pastures: ^21-3

**21:4** The [first] lot among the Levites fell to the Kohathite clans. To the descendants of the priest Aaron, there fell by lot 13 towns from the tribe of Judah, the tribe of Simeon, and the tribe of Benjamin; ^21-4

**21:5** and to the remaining Kohathites [there fell] by lot 10 towns from the clans of the tribe of Ephraim, the tribe of Dan, and the half-tribe of Manasseh. ^21-5

**21:6** To the Gershonites [there fell] by lot 13 towns from the clans of the tribe of Issachar, the tribe of Asher, the tribe of Naphtali, and the half-tribe of Manasseh in Bashan. ^21-6

**21:7** [And] to the Merarites, by their clans—12 towns from the tribe of Reuben, the tribe of Gad, and the tribe of Zebulun. ^21-7

**21:8** The Israelites assigned those towns with their pastures by lot to the Levites—as the LORD had commanded through Moses. ^21-8

**21:9** From the tribe of the Judites and the tribe of the Simeonites were assigned the following towns, which will be listed by name; ^21-9

**21:10** they went to the descendants of Aaron among the Kohathite clans of the Levites, for the first lot had fallen to them. ^21-10

**21:11** To them were assigned in the hill country of Judah Kiriath-arba—that is, Hebron—together with the pastures around it. [Arba was] the father of the Anokites.[^1] ^21-11

**21:12** They gave the fields and the villages of the town to Caleb son of Jephunneh as his holding. ^21-12

**21:13** But to the descendants of Aaron the priest they assigned Hebron—the city of refuge for manslayers—together with its pastures, Libnah with its pastures, ^21-13

**21:14** Jattir with its pastures, Eshtemoa with its pastures, ^21-14

**21:15** Holon with its pastures, Debir with its pastures, ^21-15

**21:16** Ain with its pastures, Juttah with its pastures, and Beth-shemesh with its pastures—9 towns from those two tribes. ^21-16

**21:17** And from the tribe of Benjamin: Gibeon with its pastures, Geba with its pastures, ^21-17

**21:18** Anathoth with its pastures, and Almon with its pastures—4 towns. ^21-18

**21:19** All the towns of the descendants of the priest Aaron, 13 towns with their pastures. ^21-19

**21:20** [^2]As for the other clans of the Kohathites, the remaining Levites descended from Kohath, the towns in their lot were: From the tribe of Ephraim ^21-20

**21:21** they were given, in the hill country of Ephraim, Shechem—the city of refuge for manslayers—with its pastures, Gezer with its pastures, ^21-21

**21:22** Kibzaim with its pastures, and Beth-horon with its pastures—4 towns. ^21-22

**21:23** From the tribe of Dan, Elteke with its pastures, Gibbethon with its pastures, ^21-23

**21:24** Aijalon with its pastures, and Gath-rimmon with its pastures—4 towns. ^21-24

**21:25** And from the half-tribe of Manasseh, Taanach with its pastures, and Gath-rimmon with its pastures—2 towns. ^21-25

**21:26** All the towns for the remaining clans of the Kohathites came to 10, with their pastures. ^21-26

**21:27** To the Gershonites of the levitical clans: From the half-tribe of Manasseh, Golan in Bashan—the city of refuge for manslayers—with its pastures, and Beeshterah with its pastures—2 towns. ^21-27

**21:28** From the tribe of Issachar: Kishion with its pastures, Dobrath with its pastures, ^21-28

**21:29** Jarmuth with its pastures, and En-gannim with its pastures—4 towns. ^21-29

**21:30** From the tribe of Asher: Mishal with its pastures, Abdon with its pastures, ^21-30

**21:31** Helkath with its pastures, and Rehob with its pastures—4 towns. ^21-31

**21:32** From the tribe of Naphtali, Kedesh in Galilee—the city of refuge for manslayers—with its pastures, Hammoth-dor with its pastures, and Kartan with its pastures—3 towns. ^21-32

**21:33** All the towns of the Gershonites, by their clans, came to 13 towns, with their pastures. ^21-33

**21:34** To the remaining Levites, the clans of the Merarites: From the tribe of Zebulun, Jokneam with its pastures, Kartah with its pastures, ^21-34

**21:35** Dimnah with its pastures, and Nahalal with its pastures—4 towns.[^3] ^21-35

**21:36** *(no text for this verse in this translation)* ^21-36

**21:37** *(no text for this verse in this translation)* ^21-37

**21:38** From the tribe of Gad, Ramoth in Gilead—the city of refuge for manslayers—with its pastures, Mahanaim with its pastures, ^21-38

**21:39** Heshbon with its pastures, and Jazer with its pastures—4 towns in all. ^21-39

**21:40** All the towns which went by lot to the Merarites, by their clans—the rest of the levitical clans—came to 12 towns. ^21-40

**21:41** All the towns of the Levites within the holdings of the Israelites came to 48 towns, with their pastures. ^21-41

**21:42** [^4]Thus those towns were assigned, every town with its surrounding pasture; and so it was with all those towns. ^21-42

**21:43** The LORD gave to Israel the whole country which He had sworn to their fathers that He would assign to them; they took possession of it and settled in it. ^21-43

**21:44** The LORD gave them rest on all sides, just as He had promised to their fathers on oath. Not one man of all their enemies withstood them; the LORD delivered all their enemies into their hands. ^21-44

**21:45** Not one of the good things which the LORD had promised to the House of Israel was lacking. Everything was fulfilled. ^21-45

[^1]: Elsewhere Anakites; cf. Num. 13.22; Deut. 9.2.
[^2]: Explicating v. 5.
[^3]: Some mss. and editions add the following (cf. 1 Chron. 6.63–64): “And from the tribe of Reuben: Bezer with its pastures, Jahaz with its pastures, Kedemoth with its pastures, and Mephaath with its pastures—4 towns.”
[^4]: Meaning of verse uncertain.
