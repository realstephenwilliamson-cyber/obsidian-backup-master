# Ephesians 5 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Ephesians 04|← Ephesians 4]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Ephesians 06|Ephesians 6 →]]

Compare: [[Ephesians 05|BSB and SBLGNT]]

**5:1** Be ye therefore followers of God, as most dear children; ^5-1

**5:2** And walk in love, as Christ also hath loved us, and hath delivered himself for us, an oblation and a sacrifice to God for an odour of sweetness. ^5-2

**5:3** But fornication, and all uncleanness, or covetousness, let it not so much as be named among you, as becometh saints: ^5-3

**5:4** Or obscenity, or foolish talking, or scurrility, which is to no purpose; but rather giving of thanks. ^5-4

**5:5** For know you this and understand, that no fornicator, or unclean, or covetous person (which is a serving of idols), hath inheritance in the kingdom of Christ and of God. ^5-5

**5:6** Let no man deceive you with vain words. For because of these things cometh the anger of God upon the children of unbelief. ^5-6

**5:7** Be ye not therefore partakers with them. ^5-7

**5:8** For you were heretofore darkness, but now light in the Lord. Walk then as children of the light. ^5-8

**5:9** For the fruit of the light is in all goodness, and justice, and truth; ^5-9

**5:10** Proving what is well pleasing to God: ^5-10

**5:11** And have no fellowship with the unfruitful works of darkness, but rather reprove them. ^5-11

**5:12** For the things that are done by them in secret, it is a shame even to speak of. ^5-12

**5:13** But all things that are reproved, are made manifest by the light; for all that is made manifest is light. ^5-13

**5:14** Wherefore he saith: Rise thou that sleepest, and arise from the dead: and Christ shall enlighten thee. ^5-14

**5:15** See therefore, brethren, how you walk circumspectly: not as unwise, ^5-15

**5:16** But as wise: redeeming the time, because the days are evil. ^5-16

**5:17** Wherefore become not unwise, but understanding what is the will of God. ^5-17

**5:18** And be not drunk with wine, wherein is luxury; but be ye filled with the holy Spirit, ^5-18

**5:19** Speaking to yourselves in psalms, and hymns, and spiritual canticles, singing and making melody in your hearts to the Lord; ^5-19

**5:20** Giving thanks always for all things, in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, to God and the Father: ^5-20

**5:21** Being subject one to another, in the fear of Christ. ^5-21

**5:22** Let women be subject to their husbands, as to the Lord: ^5-22

**5:23** Because the husband is the head of the wife, as Christ is the head of the church. He is the saviour of his body. ^5-23

**5:24** Therefore as the church is subject to Christ, so also let the wives be to their husbands in all things. ^5-24

**5:25** Husbands, love your wives, as Christ also loved the church, and delivered himself up for it: ^5-25

**5:26** That he might sanctify it, cleansing it by the laver of water in the word of life: ^5-26

**5:27** That he might present it to himself a glorious church, not having spot or wrinkle, or any; such thing; but that it should be holy, and without blemish. ^5-27

**5:28** So also ought men to love their wives as their own bodies. He that loveth his wife, loveth himself. ^5-28

**5:29** For no man ever hated his own flesh; but nourisheth and cherisheth it, as also Christ doth the church: ^5-29

**5:30** Because we are members of his body, of his flesh, and of his bones. ^5-30

**5:31** For this cause shall a man leave his father and mother, and shall cleave to his wife, and they shall be two in one flesh. ^5-31

**5:32** This is a great sacrament; but I speak in Christ and in the church. ^5-32

**5:33** Nevertheless let every one of you in particular love his wife as himself: and let the wife fear her husband. ^5-33
