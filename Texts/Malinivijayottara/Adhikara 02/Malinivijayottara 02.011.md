## Mūla

स्पृष्टाः सम्भाषितास्तेन दृष्टाश्च प्रीतचेतसा ।
नराः पापैः प्रमुच्यन्ते सप्तजन्मकृतैरपि ॥ ^mula

spṛṣṭāḥ sambhāṣitāstena[^25] dṛṣṭāś ca prītacetasā |
narāḥ pāpaiḥ pramucyante saptajanmakṛtair api || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, July 2022 – September 2022
> p. 30 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Malini (journal), July-September 2022.pdf#page=32>))
>
> Whereas he who knows all these reality levels (tattva) (each) as they truly are is a (true) teacher,[^27] who, said to be equal to me, reveals the vitality of Mantras.[^28] People who are touched by (such a) one, converse with him[^29] and are seen (by him) with a loving mind are freed from (their) sins, even those committed during seven lifetimes. Again, those living beings who, impelled by Śiva, have been initiated by him, having attained the fruit they desire, go to the plane devoid of disease (and death).[^30] (with 2.10–12)

[^25]: MS Kh: *sambhāvitā*
[^27]: MV 2/10ab quoted in TĀv ad 23/77.
[^28]: MV 2/10 is quoted in TĀv ad 4/228ab.
[^29]: The reading in the citation of MV 2/10-12 in TĀv ad 23/11-13ab and the edition of the MV is *sambhāṣitās* ('converse with (him)'), whereas TĀ 13/220c reads *sambhāvitās* ('are recollected (by him)' and MS Kh of the MV. Like Vasudeva, but unlike Gnoli, I take the former to be the better reading, as *kathana* – 'speaking' is one of the standard ways of transmitting higher states of consciousness.
[^30]: MV 2/10-12 is quoted as TĀ 13/219cd-222ab and also in TĀv ad 23/11-12ab. The reading in MS Kh of MV 2/12d (which is the one accepted by Vasudeva) is *gacchanti paramam padam* – 'go to the supreme plane' instead of *padam gacchanty anāmayam* – 'go to the plane devoid of disease (and death)'. The former reading is also found in the quote of these in TĀv ad 23/11-12ab.
