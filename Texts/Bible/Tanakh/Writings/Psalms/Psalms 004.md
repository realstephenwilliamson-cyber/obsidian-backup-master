## Chapter 4

**4:1** For the leader; with instrumental music.[^1] A psalm of David. ^4-1

**4:2** Answer me when I call,  
O God, my vindicator!  
You freed me from distress;  
have mercy on me and hear my prayer. ^4-2

**4:3** You men, how long will my glory be mocked,  
will you love illusions,  
have recourse to frauds? *Selah*. ^4-3

**4:4** Know that the LORD singles out the faithful for Himself;  
the LORD hears when I call to Him. ^4-4

**4:5** So tremble, and sin no more;  
ponder it on your bed, and sigh.[^2] ^4-5

**4:6** Offer sacrifices in righteousness  
and trust in the LORD. ^4-6

**4:7** Many say, “O for good days!”  
Bestow Your favor on us,[^3] O LORD. ^4-7

**4:8** You put joy into my heart  
when their grain and wine show increase. ^4-8

**4:9** Safe and sound, I lie down and sleep,  
for You alone, O LORD, keep me secure.[^4] ^4-9

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Others “be still.”
[^3]: Lit. “Lift up the light of Your countenance upon us”; cf. Num. 6.25 f.
[^4]: Or “for You, O Lord, keep me alone and secure.”
