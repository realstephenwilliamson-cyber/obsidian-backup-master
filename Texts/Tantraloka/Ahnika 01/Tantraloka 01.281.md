(deśādhvan). 9) The Path of the Principles (tattvādhvan). 10) The Division of the Principles (tattvabhedanam). 11) The Path of the Forces and the Rest (kalādyadhvan). 12) The Application of the Path (ādhvopayoga). 13) The Descents of the Power (śaktipāta) and Obscuration (śaktipātatirohitī). 14) The Preamble to the Rite of Initiation (dīkṣopakramaṇa). 15) The Common Initiation into the Rule (samayadīkṣā). 16) The Initiation of the Apprentice (pautrika). 17) What needs be Done in the Procedure concerning the Apprentice (vidheyaprakriyā vidhau). 18) The Brief Initiation (sūkṣmā dīkṣā). 19) The Initiation of the Immediate Exit (from the Body) (sadyaḥsamutkrama). 20) The Initiation of the Scales (tulādīkṣā). 21) The Initiation of those Absent (pārokṣī). 22) The Extraction of Sectarian Signs (liṅgoddhara). 23) The Consecration (of a Teacher) (abhiṣecana). 24) The Funerary Rites (antyeṣṭi). 25) The Offering to the Dead (śrāddhaklṛpti). 26) A Description of the Remaining Observance (śeṣavṛttinirūpaṇa). 27) The Worship of the Liṅga (liṅgārcā). 28) The Many Kinds of Propitious Times (parvan), (the Offering of) the Sacred Thread and other Occasional Rites (bahubhitparvapavitrādi nimittaja). 29) The Secret Observance (rahasyacaryā). 30) The Current of Mantras. (mantraugha). 31) The (Trika) Maṇḍala (maṇḍala). 32) The Procedure concerning Mudrās (mudrikāvidhi). 33) The Gathering Together (of the Deities) (ekīkāra). 34) Entry into One’s Own Nature (svasvarūpapraveśa). 35) The Encounter of the Scriptures (śāstramelana). 36) An Account of the Transmission of the Scriptures (āyātikathana). 37) A Description of the Scriptures to be Adopted (śāstropādeyatvanirūpaṇ.
