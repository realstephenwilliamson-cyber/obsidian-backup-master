# Menachot

*Seder Kodashim* · [[Talmud]]

Tractate Menachot of the Babylonian Talmud in the William Davidson edition (Koren–Steinsaltz English), one note per daf, dapim 2–110 (109 notes). In each note the bold words translate the Talmud's text and the rest is Rabbi Steinsaltz's explanation, as on Sefaria.
