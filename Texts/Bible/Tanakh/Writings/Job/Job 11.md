## Chapter 11

**11:1** Then Zophar the Naamathite said in reply: ^11-1

**11:2** Is a multitude of words unanswerable?  
Must a loquacious person be right? ^11-2

**11:3** Your prattle may silence men;  
You may mock without being rebuked, ^11-3

**11:4** And say, “My doctrine is pure,  
And I have been innocent in Your sight.” ^11-4

**11:5** But would that God might speak,  
And talk to you Himself. ^11-5

**11:6** He would tell you the secrets of wisdom,  
For there are many sides to sagacity;  
And know that God has overlooked for you some of your iniquity.[^1] ^11-6

**11:7** Would you discover the mystery of God?  
Would you discover the limit of the Almighty? ^11-7

**11:8** Higher than heaven—what can you do?  
Deeper than Sheol—what can you know? ^11-8

**11:9** Its measure is longer than the earth  
And broader than the sea. ^11-9

**11:10** Should He pass by, or confine,  
Or call an assembly, who can stop Him?[^2] ^11-10

**11:11** For He knows deceitful men;  
When He sees iniquity, does He not discern it? ^11-11

**11:12** A hollow man will get understanding,  
When a wild ass is born a man.[^3] ^11-12

**11:13** But if you direct your mind,  
And spread forth your hands toward Him— ^11-13

**11:14** If there is iniquity with you, remove it,  
And do not let injustice reside in your tent— ^11-14

**11:15** Then, free of blemish, you will hold your head high,  
And, when in straits,[^4] be unafraid. ^11-15

**11:16** You will then put your misery out of mind,  
Consider it as water that has flowed past. ^11-16

**11:17** Life will be brighter than noon;[^5]  
You will shine, you will be like the morning. ^11-17

**11:18** You will be secure, for there is hope,  
And, entrenched,[^6] you will rest secure; ^11-18

**11:19** You will lie down undisturbed;  
The great will court your favor. ^11-19

**11:20** But the eyes of the wicked pine away;  
Escape is cut off from them;  
They have only their last breath to look forward to. ^11-20

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^4]: Heb. muṣaq; other Heb. editions muṣṣaq, “you will be firm.”
[^5]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^6]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
