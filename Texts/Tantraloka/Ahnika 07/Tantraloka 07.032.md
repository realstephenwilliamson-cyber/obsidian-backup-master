## Mūla

तावानेको विकल्पः स्याद्विविधं वस्तु कल्पयन् ।
ये त्वित्थं न विदुस्तेषां विकल्पो नोपपद्यते ॥ ^mula

tāvāneko vikalpaḥ syādvividhaṃ vastu kalpayan |
ye tvitthaṃ na vidusteṣāṃ vikalpo nopapadyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 02 - Chapter Seven#^pg39|p. 21]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=39>))
>
> Until another pulse (spanda) (of cognitive consciousness) arises, that representational ideation (vikalpa) abides alone in this way as a single (unified whole), (eka) conceiving (kalpayan) the diversity (of manifest) reality (vastu). (with 7.31cd–32ab)
>
> (The existence) of representational ideation (vikalpa) of those (Buddhists) who do not know (things) in this way could not be logically (explained). No single (unified representational ideation) would ever exist in (all) the Three Worlds. (with 7.32cd–33ab)

## Viveka

> ये त्वित्थं च विदुस्तेषां विकल्पो नोपपद्यते ॥
> स ह्येको न भवेत्कश्चित् त्रिजगत्यपि जातुचित् ।

ये इति बौद्धाः । इत्थमिति स्पन्दान्तरोदय एव विकल्पान्तराणामुदयो नत्वेकस्मिन्नेव स्पन्दे इत्यर्थः । एक इति, येन स्मृत्यपलापाद्यनेकाश्रयनिबन्धनो बाह्यो व्यवहारः सिद्ध्येत् ।
