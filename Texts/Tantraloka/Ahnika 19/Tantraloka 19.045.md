## Mūla

जवी तथात्मा संसुप्तामर्शोऽप्येवं प्रबुध्यते ।
प्रबुद्धः स च सञ्जातो न चादीक्षित उच्यते ॥ ^mula

javī tathātmā saṃsuptāmarśo'pyevaṃ prabudhyate |
prabuddhaḥ sa ca sañjāto na cādīkṣita ucyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 10 - Chapter Nineteen#^pg245|p. 225]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=245>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 19.44cd–45ab.
>
> Just as a light breeze becomes (a strong) fast (wind) when combined with (other wind) which is of the same kind as itself, so is it with the Self (of the dying man). Even though its reflective awareness is in deep sleep (saṁsuptāmarśah), it awakens in this way (when combined with the reflective awareness of Mantra). (with 19.44cd–45ab)
>
> Once he has awakened, he is not said to be uninitiated. Initiation is (essentially) nothing but (an empowering and purifying) sacrament (saṁskāra), and he does indeed possess it. (with 19.45cd–46ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* एवं तर्ह्यस्य अदीक्षिताग्रे मन्त्रपाठात् स्फुटमेवापतितः समयलोपः?--इत्याशङ्क्याह--

> प्रबुद्धः स च सञ्जातो न चादीक्षित उच्यते ॥
> दीक्षा हि नाम संस्कारो न त्वन्यत्सोऽस्ति चास्य हि ।

दीक्षा हि नाम मान्त्रः संस्कारः, स च अस्य प्रबोधान्यथानुपपत्त्या स्वरसत एव मन्त्रामर्शादिना सिद्ध इति को नामास्य समयलोपार्थः ॥
