# Ezekiel 15 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Ezekiel 14|← Ezekiel 14]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Ezekiel 16|Ezekiel 16 →]]

## The useless vine

**15:1** The word of the LORD came to me: ^15-1

**15:2** O mortal, how does the wood of the vine surpass all other wood, the vine branch that is among the trees of the forest? ^15-2

**15:3** Is wood taken from it to make anything? Does one take a peg from it on which to hang any object? ^15-3

**15:4** It is put in the fire for fuel; when the fire has consumed both ends of it and the middle of it is charred, is it useful for anything? ^15-4

**15:5** When it was whole it was used for nothing; how much less—when the fire has consumed it, and it is charred—can it ever be used for anything! ^15-5

**15:6** Therefore thus says the Lord GOD: Like the wood of the vine among the trees of the forest, which I have given to the fire for fuel, so I will give up the inhabitants of Jerusalem. ^15-6

**15:7** I will set my face against them; although they escape from the fire, the fire shall still consume them, and you shall know that I am the LORD when I set my face against them. ^15-7

**15:8** And I will make the land desolate because they have acted faithlessly, says the Lord GOD. ^15-8
