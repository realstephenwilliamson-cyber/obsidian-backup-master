# Logos and Microcosm in Maximus the Confessor

> [!abstract] Definition
> St. Maximus the Confessor’s Christocentric cosmology (*Ambigua*, *Quaestiones ad Thalassium*), positing that the uncreated Logos holds within Himself the inner principles (*logoi*) of all created beings, making creation an ongoing manifestation and incarnation of the Word.

## The Architecture of the *Logoi*
- Every created entity possesses a unique *logos* (divine intention, essence, and teleology) originating in the eternal divine Logos.
- Because all *logoi* are unified in Christ, the universe is fundamentally sacramental and non-dual: God is totally transcendent in essence yet entirely present in the *logoi* of His creatures.
- The human person is called to function as a *microcosm* (*mikrokosmos*) and mediator, gathering up the divisions of nature (uncreated/created, intelligible/sensory, heaven/earth) and uniting them in God through love.
