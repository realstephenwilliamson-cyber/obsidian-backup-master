## Mūla

विकल्प्यं शून्यरूपे न प्रमातरि विकल्पनम् ।
बुद्धिर्ध्यानमयी तत्र प्राण उच्चारणात्मकः ॥ ^mula

vikalpyaṃ śūnyarūpe na pramātari vikalpanam |
buddhirdhyānamayī tatra prāṇa uccāraṇātmakaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 03 - Chapter Five, part 1#^pg32|p. 17]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=32>))
>
> While it is possible to truly form a conceptual representation of the Supreme Lordship in the intellect, vital breath and body, no (such) conception can be formed in the subject who is void. (with 5.16cd–17ab)
>
> There (in that context), the intellect is meditation (dhyāna), and the vital breath ‘utterance’ (uccāra). (In its secondary form,) the utterance (uccāraṇa) (of the vital breath) consists of the five modalities (vṛtti), starting with the exhaled breath (prāṇa) up to the pervasive breath (vyāna). (with 5.17cd–18ab)
