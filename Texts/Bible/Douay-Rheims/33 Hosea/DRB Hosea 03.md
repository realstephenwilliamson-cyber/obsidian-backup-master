# Hosea 3 (Douay-Rheims)

*Osee 3 in the Douay-Rheims*

[[DRB Hosea 02|← Hosea 2]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Hosea 04|Hosea 4 →]]

**3:1** And the Lord said to me: Go yet again, and love a woman beloved of her friend, and an adulteress: as the Lord loveth the children of Israel, and they look to strange gods, and love the husks of the grapes. ^3-1

**3:2** And I bought her to me for fifteen pieces of silver, and for a core of barley, and for half a core of barley. ^3-2

**3:3** And I said to her: Thou shalt wait for me many days: thou shalt not play the harlot, and thou shalt be no man’s, and I also will wait for thee. ^3-3

**3:4** For the children of Israel shall sit many days without king, and without prince, and without sacrifice, and without altar, and without ephod, and without theraphim. ^3-4

**3:5** And after this the children of Israel shall return, and shall seek the Lord their God, and David their king: and they shall fear the Lord, and his goodness in the last days. ^3-5
