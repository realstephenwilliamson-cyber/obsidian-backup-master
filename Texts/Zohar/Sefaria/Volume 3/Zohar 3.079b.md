# Zohar 3.079b

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 7.08 - Parashat Aharei Mot#^z3-79b|Parashat Aḥarei Mot, at 3:79b]]

*Volume 3, folio 79b* · Achrei Mot

*Passage Achrei Mot 72:405 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 3.079a#^achrei-mot-72-405|Achrei Mot 72:405]].*

**Achrei Mot 73:406** R. Simeon said: ‘It is written: “And it shall come to pass that from one new moon to another, and from one Sabbath to another”, etc. (Isa. 66, 23). Why is “new moon” put side by side with “Sabbath”? Because both are of one grade, being the time when one is joined to the other. On Sabbath there is joy and an additional soul, because the Ancient One reveals Himself and the wedlock is prepared. ^achrei-mot-73-406

**Achrei Mot 73:407** Happy the generation in which R. Simeon lived! To it the words apply, “Happy art thou O land, when thy king is a free man”. This is R. Simeon, who holds his head erect to expound doctrine and fears not, like a free man who says what he wants to say without fear.’ ^achrei-mot-73-407

**Achrei Mot 74:408** So, too, at the renewal of the moon, because the sun illumines her with the joyful light of the Ancient One above. ^achrei-mot-74-408

**Achrei Mot 74:409** Therefore the offering of new moon is an atonement above. ^achrei-mot-74-409

**Achrei Mot 74:410** It is written, “The burnt offering of the Sabbath beside (*‘al*, lit. upon) the continual burnt offering” (Num. 28, 10). The word *‘al* here signifies that the thought should be directed to the very highest more than on other days. Similarly it is written, “And Hannah prayed to (‘al, lit. upon) the Lord” (I Sam. 1, 19), because children depend upon the holy *mazzal*, as we have pointed out.’ ^achrei-mot-74-410

**Achrei Mot 75:411** R. Jose found R. Abba similarly interpreting the words, “Cast thy burden upon the Lord” (Ps. 55, 23), because food also depends on *mazzal*. R. Judah expounded similarly the verse, “For this (‘al *zoth*) let every one that is godly pray to thee” (*Ibid.* 32, 6): verily, to that which is above *zoth*. ^achrei-mot-75-411

**Achrei Mot 75:412** R. Isaac said: ‘Happy are the righteous in that many precious treasures are stored up for them in the other world, where God will have joyous converse with them, as we have laid down. Happy their portion in this world and the next, as it is written: “But let all those that put their trust in thee rejoice, let them ever shout for joy because thou defendest them, let them also that love thy name be joyful in thee” (Ps. 5, 12).’ ^achrei-mot-75-412
