## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. II
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 02 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v02 09 - Chapter Three, part 6#^pg303|p. 287]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 02.pdf#page=303>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 3.168cd–169ab.
>
> The Lord Himself has said that in the venerable Kulagahvara, which is so called in agreement with its meaning, derived from the oneness of power (kula) and its possessor (gahvara). “Out of love for You (O goddess), the Principle of Poison (viṣatattva), (which is Unstruck Sound, otherwise) called Devoid of Vowels (anacka), has been revealed. Its form is that of the Cavity of the Beak of a Crow, and it is devoid of visualization (dhyāna) and meditative support (dhāraṇa).” (with 3.168–169)
