## Mūla

कार्या तेषामिहान्त्येष्टिर्गुरुणातिकृपालुना ।
न मण्डलादिकं त्वत्र भवेत् श्माशानिके विधौ ॥ ^mula

kāryā teṣāmihāntyeṣṭirguruṇātikṛpālunā |
na maṇḍalādikaṃ tvatra bhavet śmāśānike vidhau || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 15 - Chapter Twenty-Four#^pg367|p. 347]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=367>))
>
> ‘A very compassionate teacher should also perform the last rites here for those who have been killed by the application of mantras (mantrayoga) and the like and are in the hells.’ (with 24.9cd–10ab)
>
> (It is generally taught that) mandalas and the like are not required here in this ritual procedure, (performed) in the cremation ground. (However,) there are some (authorities) who say that that should also be done (there in that) abode of ghosts. (with 24.10cd–11ab)
