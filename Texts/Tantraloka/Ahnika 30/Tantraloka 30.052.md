## Mūla

दण्डो जीवस्त्रिशूलं च दक्षांगुल्यपरस्तनौ ।
नाभिकण्ठौ मरुद्रुद्रौ विसर्गः सत्रिशूलकः ॥ ^mula

daṇḍo jīvastriśūlaṃ ca dakṣāṃgulyaparastanau |
nābhikaṇṭhau marudrudrau visargaḥ satriśūlakaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 03 - Chapter Thirty, part 2#^pg55|p. 37]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=55>))
>
> The Staff (of the Trident) (R), the Living Being (S), the Trident (J), the Right Finger (BH), the other (i.e. Left) Breast (L), the Navel (KṢ) and Throat (V), Wind (Y) and Rudra (Ū), and emission (Ḥ) is along with the Trident (AU). (This mantra) is said to be the Lord of all the assemblies (cakra) of Yoginīs. Just by uttering this, (one attains the state of) consciousness that arises from Parā. (with 30.52–53)
