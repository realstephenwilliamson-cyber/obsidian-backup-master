## Praise the judge of the world

*A Psalm.*

**98:1** O sing to the LORD a new song, for he has done marvelous things. His right hand and his holy arm have gotten him victory. ^98-1

**98:2** The LORD has made known his victory; he has revealed his vindication in the sight of the nations. ^98-2

**98:3** He has remembered his steadfast love and faithfulness to the house of Israel. All the ends of the earth have seen the victory of our God. ^98-3

**98:4** Make a joyful noise to the LORD, all the earth; break forth into joyous song and sing praises. ^98-4

**98:5** Sing praises to the LORD with the lyre, with the lyre and the sound of melody. ^98-5

**98:6** With trumpets and the sound of the horn make a joyful noise before the King, the LORD. ^98-6

**98:7** Let the sea roar and all that fills it, the world and those who live in it. ^98-7

**98:8** Let the floods clap their hands; let the hills sing together for joy ^98-8

**98:9** at the presence of the LORD, for he is coming to judge the earth. He will judge the world with righteousness and the peoples with equity. ^98-9
