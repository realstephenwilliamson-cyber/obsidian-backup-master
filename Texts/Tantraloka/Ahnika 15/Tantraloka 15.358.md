## Mūla

ततो जपं प्रकुर्वीत प्रतिमन्त्रं द्विपञ्चधा ।
एकैकस्य त्र्यात्मकत्वादभेदाच्चापि सर्वशः ॥ ^mula

tato japaṃ prakurvīta pratimantraṃ dvipañcadhā |
ekaikasya tryātmakatvādabhedāccāpi sarvaśaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 07 - Chapter Fifteen, part 6#^pg237|p. 219]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=237>))
>
> Abiding here in this way in a state of identity with the Goddess (Mātrsadbhāva), (who resides) at the extremity of the Trident and, presenting her the universe (as an offering), one should offer lustration (tarpayet) to the host of deities, and then recite each of the Mantras twice five times. (with 15.357–358ab)
>
> (It is done this way) because the triadic nature is present in each one (of the Mantras), and also because (they are) undivided (from one another) in all respects (and so form an undifferentiated unity). (with 15.358cd)

*Lead-in:* एतदेव व्याप्तिमुखेन अपि घटयति--

> एकैकस्य त्र्यात्मकत्वादभेदाच्चापि सर्वशः ॥

एकैकस्मिन्मन्त्रे त्रितयं प्रत्येकमन्तः कृतत्रितयमस्तीति प्रतिमन्त्रं सार्वात्म्यात् नवत्वं स्वरूपस्थित्यविभागात्मना च एकत्वमिति दशधात्वम् ॥ ३५८ ॥
