## Mūla

इति पञ्चविधामेनां कलनां कुर्वती परा ।
देवी काली तथा कालकर्षिणी चेति कथ्यते ॥ ^mula

iti pañcavidhāmenāṃ kalanāṃ kurvatī parā |
devī kālī tathā kālakarṣiṇī ceti kathyate || ^iast

## Translation

> Thus, the Goddess Parā, Who performs these five operations (kalanā), is called Kālī and Kālakarṣiṇī (the Destroyer of Time).
