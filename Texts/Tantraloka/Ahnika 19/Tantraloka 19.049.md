## Mūla

कुर्वंस्तस्मिंश्चलत्येति न लोपं तद्वदत्र हि ।
यतोऽस्य प्रत्ययप्राप्तिप्रेप्सोः समयिनस्तथा ॥ ^mula

kurvaṃstasmiṃścalatyeti na lopaṃ tadvadatra hi |
yato'sya pratyayaprāptiprepsoḥ samayinastathā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 10 - Chapter Nineteen#^pg247|p. 227]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=247>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 19.48cd–49ab.
>
> Just as a regular initiate applying Mantras causes (a piece of) wood or (clod of) earth to move, and does not thereby transgress (any rule), such is the case here also. (with 19.48cd–49ab)
>
> (The dying man) is liberated (spontaneously) by his own nature (svabhāvena).“' The initiate acts (in this way) because he wishes to get a sign confirming (the power of Mantra) (pratyaya), there is no harm in that. (with 19.49cd–50ab)
