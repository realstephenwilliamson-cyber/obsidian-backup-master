## Mūla

शिवाभिन्नमथात्मानं पञ्चकृत्यकरं स्मरेत् ।
स्वात्मनः करणं मन्त्रान्मूर्तिं चानुजिघृक्षया ॥ ^mula

śivābhinnamathātmānaṃ pañcakṛtyakaraṃ smaret |
svātmanaḥ karaṇaṃ mantrānmūrtiṃ cānujighṛkṣayā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 05 - Chapter Sixteen, part 1#^pg56|p. 36]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=56>))
>
> Now (atha) (the teacher) should recollect (his own) Self, at one with Śiva, who performs the five tasks (of creation, persistence, withdrawal, grace and obscuration) (pañcakṛtya), and that his own instrument of action (svātmanaḥ karaṇam) (i.e. the senses) are the mantras, and that, by virtue of his desire to grace (others), (he is Śiva's) iconic body (of Mantra) (mūrti).
