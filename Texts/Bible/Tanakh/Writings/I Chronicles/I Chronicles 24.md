## Chapter 24

**24:1** The divisions of the Aaronites were:  
The sons of Aaron: Nadab and Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar. ^24-1

**24:2** Nadab and Abihu died in the lifetime of their father, and they had no children, so Eleazar and Ithamar served as priests. ^24-2

**24:3** David, Zadok of the sons of Eleazar, and Ahimelech of the sons of Ithamar divided them into offices by their tasks. ^24-3

**24:4** The sons of Eleazar turned out to be more numerous by male heads than the sons of Ithamar, so they divided the sons of Eleazar into sixteen chiefs of clans and the sons of Ithamar into eight clans. ^24-4

**24:5** They divided them by lot, both on an equal footing, since they were all sanctuary officers and officers of God—the sons of Eleazar and the sons of Ithamar. ^24-5

**24:6** Shemaiah son of Nathanel, the scribe, who was of the Levites, registered them under the eye of the king, the officers, and Zadok the priest, and Ahimelech son of Abiathar, and the chiefs of clans of the priests and Levites—one clan more taken for Eleazar for each one taken of Ithamar.[^1] ^24-6

**24:7** The first lot fell on Jehoiarib; the second on Jedaiah; ^24-7

**24:8** the third on Harim; the fourth on Seorim; ^24-8

**24:9** the fifth on Malchijah; the sixth on Mijamin; ^24-9

**24:10** the seventh on Hakkoz; the eighth on Abijah; ^24-10

**24:11** the ninth on Jeshua; the tenth on Shecaniah; ^24-11

**24:12** the eleventh on Eliashib; the twelfth on Jakim; ^24-12

**24:13** the thirteenth on Huppah; the fourteenth on Jeshebeab; ^24-13

**24:14** the fifteenth on Bilgah; the sixteenth on Immer; ^24-14

**24:15** the seventeenth on Hezir; the eighteenth on Happizzez; ^24-15

**24:16** the nineteenth on Pethahiah; the twentieth on Jehezkel; ^24-16

**24:17** the twenty-first on Jachin; the twenty-second on Gamul; ^24-17

**24:18** the twenty-third on Delaiah; the twenty-fourth on Maaziah. ^24-18

**24:19** According to this allocation of offices by tasks, they were to enter the House of the LORD as was laid down for them by Aaron their father, as the LORD God of Israel had commanded him. ^24-19

**24:20** The remaining Levites: the sons of Amram: Shubael; the sons of Shubael: Jehdeiah; ^24-20

**24:21** Rehabiah. The sons of Rehabiah: Isshiah, the chief. ^24-21

**24:22** Izharites: Shelomoth. The sons of Shelomoth: Jahath ^24-22

**24:23** and Benai, Jeriah; the second, Amariah; the third, Jahaziel; the fourth, Jekameam. ^24-23

**24:24** The sons of Uzziel: Micah. The sons of Micah: Shamir. ^24-24

**24:25** The brother of Micah: Isshiah. The sons of Isshiah: Zechariah. ^24-25

**24:26** The sons of Merari: Mahli and Mushi. The sons of Jaazaiah, his son— ^24-26

**24:27** the sons of Merari by Jaazaiah his son: Shoham, Zakkur, and Ibri. ^24-27

**24:28** Mahli: Eleazar; he had no sons. ^24-28

**24:29** Kish: the sons of Kish: Jerahmeel. ^24-29

**24:30** The sons of Mushi: Mahli, Eder, and Jerimoth. These were the sons of the Levites by their clans. ^24-30

**24:31** These too cast lots corresponding to their kinsmen, the sons of Aaron, under the eye of King David and Zadok and Ahimelech and the chiefs of the clans of the priests and Levites, on the principle of “chief and youngest brother alike.” ^24-31

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
