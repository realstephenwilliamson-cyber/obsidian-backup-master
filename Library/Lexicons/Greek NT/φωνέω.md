# φωνέω

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · verb

**Dodson:** I crow, shout, summon — I give forth a sound, hence: (a) of a cock: I crow, (b) of men: I shout, (c) trans: I call (to myself), summon; I invite, address. (Strong's 5455; headword φωνέω)
