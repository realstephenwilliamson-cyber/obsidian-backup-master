# Job 15 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Job 14|← Job 14]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Job 16|Job 16 →]]

## Eliphaz speaks: Job undermines religion

**15:1** Then Eliphaz the Temanite answered: ^15-1

**15:2** “Should the wise answer with windy knowledge and fill themselves with the east wind? ^15-2

**15:3** Should they argue in unprofitable talk or in words that can do no good? ^15-3

**15:4** But you are doing away with the fear of God and hindering meditation before God. ^15-4

**15:5** For your iniquity teaches your mouth, and you choose the tongue of the crafty. ^15-5

**15:6** Your own mouth condemns you, not I; your own lips testify against you. ^15-6

**15:7** “Are you the firstborn of the human race? Were you brought forth before the hills? ^15-7

**15:8** Have you listened in the council of God? And do you limit wisdom to yourself? ^15-8

**15:9** What do you know that we do not know? What do you understand that is not clear to us? ^15-9

**15:10** The gray-haired and the aged are on our side, those older than your father. ^15-10

**15:11** Are the consolations of God too small for you or the word that deals gently with you? ^15-11

**15:12** Why does your heart carry you away, and why do your eyes flash,^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^15-12

**15:13** so that you turn your spirit against God, and let such words go out of your mouth? ^15-13

**15:14** What are mortals, that they can be clean? Or those born of woman, that they can be righteous? ^15-14

**15:15** God puts no trust even in his holy ones, and the heavens are not clean in his sight; ^15-15

**15:16** how much less one who is abominable and corrupt, one who drinks iniquity like water! ^15-16

**15:17** “I will show you; listen to me; what I have seen I will declare— ^15-17

**15:18** what sages have told and their ancestors have not hidden, ^15-18

**15:19** to whom alone the land was given, and no stranger passed among them. ^15-19

**15:20** The wicked writhe in pain all their days, through all the years that are laid up for the ruthless. ^15-20

**15:21** Terrifying sounds are in their ears; in prosperity the destroyer will come upon them. ^15-21

**15:22** They despair of returning from darkness, and they are destined for the sword. ^15-22

**15:23** They wander abroad for bread, saying, ‘Where is it?’ They know that a day of darkness is ready at hand; ^15-23

**15:24** distress and anguish terrify them; they prevail against them like a king prepared for battle. ^15-24

**15:25** Because they stretched out their hands against God and bid defiance to the Almighty,^[Traditional rendering of Heb *Shaddai*] ^15-25

**15:26** running stubbornly against him with a thickly bossed shield; ^15-26

**15:27** because they have covered their faces with their fat and gathered fat upon their loins, ^15-27

**15:28** they will live in desolate cities, in houses that no one should inhabit, houses destined to become heaps of ruins; ^15-28

**15:29** they will not be rich, and their wealth will not endure, nor will they strike root in the earth;^[Vg: Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^15-29

**15:30** they will not escape from darkness; the flame will dry up their shoots, and their blossom^[Gk: Heb *mouth*] will be swept away by the wind. ^15-30

**15:31** Let them not trust in emptiness, deceiving themselves, for emptiness will be their recompense. ^15-31

**15:32** It will be paid in full before their time, and their branch will not be green. ^15-32

**15:33** They will shake off their unripe grape, like the vine, and cast off their blossoms, like the olive tree. ^15-33

**15:34** For the company of the godless is barren, and fire consumes the tents of bribery. ^15-34

**15:35** They conceive mischief and bring forth evil, and their belly prepares deceit.” ^15-35
