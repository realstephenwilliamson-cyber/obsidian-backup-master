## Mūla

तस्माद्रहस्यशास्त्रेषु ये मन्त्रास्तान्बुधो बहिः ।
न प्रतिष्ठापयेज्जातु विशेषाद् व्यक्तरूपिणः ॥ ^mula

tasmādrahasyaśāstreṣu ye mantrāstānbudho bahiḥ |
na pratiṣṭhāpayejjātu viśeṣād vyaktarūpiṇaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 18 - Chapter Twenty-Seven#^pg422|p. 402]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=422>))
>
> Moreover, the Supreme Lord has said this in the Jñānottara. (There we read): ‘Śiva loves sacrifices (yāga), especially (when He is) in the midst of the (Circle of the) Mothers.’ Thus, a wise man should most certainly (jātu) not install the Mantras of the secret scriptures externally, especially not those that are (externally) manifest (and spoken out aloud). That is why the installation procedure taught elsewhere (in other scriptures) for the benefit of the deceased, (according to our) scriptures, should not be performed here (in this case). Rather, on the contrary, a common (sādhāraṇī) (rite of installation) should be performed (with common mantras). (with 27.6–8)
