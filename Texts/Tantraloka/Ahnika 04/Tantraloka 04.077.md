## Mūla

अन्यतो लब्धसंस्कारः स साक्षाद्भैरवो गुरुः ।
यतः शास्त्रक्रमात्तज्ज्ञगुरुप्रज्ञानुशीलनात् ॥ ^mula

anyato labdhasaṃskāraḥ sa sākṣādbhairavo guruḥ |
yataḥ śāstrakramāttajjñaguruprajñānuśīlanāt || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. III
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 03 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v03 04 - Chapter Four, part 3#^pg105|p. 92]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 03.pdf#page=105>))
>
> That teacher who, even though uncreated (akalpita), has by the confirmation (obtained) from other (teachers or scriptures) strengthened (his conviction of the genuineness of his enlightenment) and is thus further purified, is Bhairava in person (sākṣșāāt). (with 4.76cd–77ab)
>
> This is so because the knowledge proved trustworthy by oneself, (attained) by putting into practice the procedures laid down in the scriptures (śāstrakrama) and attending to the wisdom (prajñā) of a teacher who knows them, is full (and perfect) (pūrṇa), and so leads to the realisation of one’s authentic identity as Bhairava (bhairavāyate). (with 4.77cd–78ab)

*Lead-in:* ननु कथं संवादमात्रादेव एतत्स्यात् ? -- इत्याशङ्क्याह --

> यतः शास्त्रक्रमात्तज्ज्ञगुरुप्रज्ञानुशीलनात् ॥
> आत्मप्रत्ययितं ज्ञानं पूर्णत्वाद्भैरवायते ।

यतः शास्त्राधिगमक्रमेण शास्त्रज्ञगुरुप्रज्ञाया अनुशीलनाच्च "इत्थमिदं, नानित्थम्"इत्येवं रूपात् पर्यालोचनात् ससंवादं सत् ज्ञानम् आत्मनि सञ्जातप्रत्ययम् "एवमेवैतत्, नान्यथा"इत्येवंनिश्चयोत्पादात् दार्ढ्यं प्राप्तम्, अत एव नैराकाङ्क्ष्यात् पूर्णं सत् भैरवायते -- निराशंसानुत्तरपरज्ञानरूपतया प्रस्फुरति, इत्यर्थः ॥ ७७ ॥
