## Divine majesty and human dignity

*To the leader: according to The Gittith. A Psalm of David.*

**8:1** O LORD, our Sovereign, how majestic is your name in all the earth! You have set your glory above the heavens. ^8-1

**8:2** Out of the mouths of babes and infants you have founded a bulwark because of your foes, to silence the enemy and the avenger. ^8-2

**8:3** When I look at your heavens, the work of your fingers, the moon and the stars that you have established; ^8-3

**8:4** what are humans that you are mindful of them, mortals^[Heb *son of man*] that you care for them? ^8-4

**8:5** Yet you have made them a little lower than God^[Or *than the divine beings* or *angels*] and crowned them with glory and honor. ^8-5

**8:6** You have given them dominion over the works of your hands; you have put all things under their feet, ^8-6

**8:7** all sheep and oxen, and also the beasts of the field, ^8-7

**8:8** the birds of the air, and the fish of the sea, whatever passes along the paths of the seas. ^8-8

**8:9** O LORD, our Sovereign, how majestic is your name in all the earth! ^8-9
