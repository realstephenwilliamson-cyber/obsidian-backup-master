# Zechariah 9 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Zechariah 08|← Zechariah 8]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Zechariah 10|Zechariah 10 →]]

## Judgment on Israel’s enemies

**9:1** An Oracle. The word of the LORD is against the land of Hadrach and will rest upon Damascus. For to the LORD belongs the capital^[Heb *eye*] of Aram,^[Cn: Heb *of Adam* (or *of humankind*)] as do all the tribes of Israel; ^9-1

**9:2** Hamath also, which borders on it, Tyre and Sidon, though they are very wise. ^9-2

**9:3** Tyre has built itself a rampart and heaped up silver like dust and gold like the dirt of the streets. ^9-3

**9:4** But now, the Lord will strip it of its possessions and hurl its wealth into the sea, and it shall be devoured by fire. ^9-4

**9:5** Ashkelon shall see it and be afraid; Gaza, too, and shall writhe in anguish; Ekron also, because its hopes are withered. The king shall perish from Gaza; Ashkelon shall be uninhabited; ^9-5

**9:6** a mongrel people shall settle in Ashdod, and I will make an end of the pride of Philistia. ^9-6

**9:7** I will take away its blood from its mouth and its abominations from between its teeth; it, too, shall be a remnant for our God; it shall be like a clan in Judah, and Ekron shall be like the Jebusites. ^9-7

**9:8** Then I will encamp at my house as a guard, so that no one shall march to and fro; no oppressor shall again overrun them, for now I have seen with my own eyes. ^9-8

## The coming ruler of God’s people

**9:9** Rejoice greatly, O daughter Zion! Shout aloud, O daughter Jerusalem! See, your king comes to you; triumphant and victorious is he, humble and riding on a donkey, on a colt, the foal of a donkey. ^9-9

**9:10** He^[Gk: Heb *I*] will cut off the chariot from Ephraim and the war horse from Jerusalem; and the battle bow shall be cut off, and he shall command peace to the nations; his dominion shall be from sea to sea and from the River to the ends of the earth. ^9-10

**9:11** As for you also, because of the blood of my covenant with you, I will set your prisoners free from the waterless pit. ^9-11

**9:12** Return to your stronghold, O prisoners of hope; today I declare that I will restore to you double. ^9-12

**9:13** For I have bent Judah as my bow; I have made Ephraim its arrow.^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] I will arouse your sons, O Zion, against your sons, O Greece, and wield you like a warrior’s sword. ^9-13

**9:14** Then the LORD will appear over them, and his arrow go forth like lightning; the Lord GOD will sound the trumpet and march forth in the whirlwinds of the south. ^9-14

**9:15** The LORD of hosts will protect them, and they shall consume and conquer the slingers;^[Cn: Heb *the slingstones*] they shall drink their blood^[Gk: Heb *they roared*] like wine and be full like a bowl, drenched like the corners of the altar. ^9-15

**9:16** On that day the LORD their God will save them, for they are the flock of his people, for like the jewels of a crown they shall shine on his land. ^9-16

**9:17** For what goodness and beauty are his! Grain shall make the young men flourish, and new wine the young women. ^9-17
