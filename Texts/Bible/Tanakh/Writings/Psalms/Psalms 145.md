## Chapter 145

**145:1** A song of praise. Of David.  
I will extol You, my God and king,  
and bless Your name forever and ever. ^145-1

**145:2** Every day will I bless You  
and praise Your name forever and ever. ^145-2

**145:3** Great is the LORD and much acclaimed;  
His greatness cannot be fathomed. ^145-3

**145:4** One generation shall laud Your works to another  
and declare Your mighty acts. ^145-4

**145:5** The glorious majesty of Your splendor  
and Your wondrous acts[^1] will I recite. ^145-5

**145:6** Men shall talk of the might of Your awesome deeds,  
and I will recount Your greatness. ^145-6

**145:7** They shall celebrate Your abundant goodness,  
and sing joyously of Your beneficence. ^145-7

**145:8** The LORD is gracious and compassionate,  
slow to anger and abounding in kindness. ^145-8

**145:9** The LORD is good to all,  
and His mercy is upon all His works. ^145-9

**145:10** All Your works shall praise You, O LORD,  
and Your faithful ones shall bless You. ^145-10

**145:11** They shall talk of the majesty of Your kingship,  
and speak of Your might, ^145-11

**145:12** to make His mighty acts known among men  
and the majestic glory of His kingship. ^145-12

**145:13** Your kingship is an eternal kingship;  
Your dominion is for all generations. ^145-13

**145:14** The LORD supports all who stumble,  
and makes all who are bent stand straight. ^145-14

**145:15** The eyes of all look to You expectantly,  
and You give them their food when it is due. ^145-15

**145:16** You give it openhandedly,  
feeding every creature to its heart’s content. ^145-16

**145:17** The LORD is beneficent in all His ways  
and faithful in all His works. ^145-17

**145:18** The LORD is near to all who call Him,  
to all who call Him with sincerity. ^145-18

**145:19** He fulfills the wishes of those who fear Him;  
He hears their cry and delivers them. ^145-19

**145:20** The LORD watches over all who love Him,  
but all the wicked He will destroy. ^145-20

**145:21** My mouth shall utter the praise of the LORD,  
and all creatures[^2] shall bless His holy name forever and ever. ^145-21

[^1]: A Qumran Pss. scroll reads: “they will speak of, and Your wonders.”
[^2]: Lit. “flesh.”
