## Mūla

नान्यशास्त्राभियुक्तेषु शिवज्ञानं प्रकाशते ।
तन्न सैद्धान्तिको वामे नासौ दक्षे स नो मते ॥ ^mula

nānyaśāstrābhiyukteṣu śivajñānaṃ prakāśate |
tanna saiddhāntiko vāme nāsau dakṣe sa no mate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 10 - Chapter Thirteen, part 6#^pg345|p. 323]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=345>))
>
> The Supreme Lord has said something similar in the venerable Gahvara: ‘That kind of knowledge of Śiva does not shine in those who are devoted to other scriptures.’ (with 13.319cd–320ab)
>
> A follower of (dualist Śaiva) Siddhānta is not (qualified for the teachings) of the Left (current of scripture), the follower of the latter for the Right, (this one for) the Mata, (that one for) Kulakaula Trika —rather, it is the other way around. (with 13.320cd–321ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* तदेव विभज्य दर्शयति --

> तन्न सैद्धान्तिको वामे नासौ दक्षे स नो मते ॥
> कुलकौले त्रिके नासौ पूर्वः पूर्वः परत्र तु ।

अधिकारी न भवेत् -- इति संबन्धः । अयं पुनरत्र विशेषो यत्पूर्वः पूर्वः परत्र सर्वत्राधिकारी भवेदित्युक्तं पूर्वः पूर्वः परत्र त्विति ॥
