## Mūla

योगी विशेत्तदा तत्तच्चक्रेशत्वं हठाद् व्रजेत् ।
यथा सर्वेशिना बोधेनाक्रान्तापि तनुः क्वचित् ॥ ^mula

yogī viśettadā tattaccakreśatvaṃ haṭhād vrajet |
yathā sarveśinā bodhenākrāntāpi tanuḥ kvacit || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 06 - Chapter Five, part 4#^pg160|p. 145]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=160>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 5.109cd–110ab.
>
> When the yogi penetrates into these five, starting with ‘joy’, he (spontaneously and) forcefully (haṭhāt) becomes the master of their respective Wheels. (with 5.108cd–109ab)
>
> The body, although pervaded by consciousness, which is the master of all things, is capable of acting (only) to a certain degree in certain places, (like) the sense of sight, (that can only) perceive form. In the same way, only a certain (type of experience) can arise by penetrating somewhere into a (particular) Wheel. (with 5.109cd–110)
