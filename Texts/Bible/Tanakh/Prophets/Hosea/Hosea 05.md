## Chapter 5

**5:1** Hear this, O priests,  
Attend, O House[^1] of Israel,  
And give ear, O royal house;  
For right conduct is your responsibility!  
But you have been a snare to Mizpah  
And a net spread out over Tabor; ^5-1

**5:2** For when trappers dug deep pitfalls,  
I was the only reprover of them all.[^2] ^5-2

**5:3** Yes, I have watched Ephraim,  
Israel has not escaped my notice:  
Behold, you have fornicated, O Ephraim;  
Israel has defiled himself! ^5-3

**5:4** Their habits do not let them  
Turn back to their God;  
Because of the lecherous impulse within them,  
They pay no heed to the LORD. ^5-4

**5:5** [^3]Israel’s pride shall be humbled before his very eyes,  
As Israel and Ephraim fall because of their sin  
(And Judah falls with them). ^5-5

**5:6** Then they will go with their sheep and cattle  
To seek the LORD, but they will not find Him.  
He has cast them off:[^4] ^5-6

**5:7** [Because] they have broken faith with the LORD,  
Because they have[^5] begotten  
Alien children.  
Therefore, the new moon  
Shall devour their portion.[^6] ^5-7

**5:8** Sound a ram’s horn in Gibeah,  
A trumpet in Ramah;  
Give the alarm in Beth-aven;[^7]  
After you,[^8] Benjamin! ^5-8

**5:9** Ephraim is stricken with horror  
On a day of chastisement.  
Against the tribes[^9] of Israel  
I proclaim certainties: ^5-9

**5:10** The officers of Judah have acted  
Like shifters of field boundaries;  
On them I will pour out  
My wrath like water. ^5-10

**5:11** Ephraim is defrauded,  
Robbed of redress,  
Because he has witlessly  
Gone after futility.[^10] ^5-11

**5:12** For it is I who am like rot to Ephraim,  
Like decay to the House of Judah;[^11] ^5-12

**5:13** Yet when Ephraim became aware of his sickness,  
Judah[^12] of his sores,  
Ephraim repaired to Assyria—  
He sent envoys to a patron[^13] king!  
He will never be able to cure you,  
Will not heal you of your sores. ^5-13

**5:14** No, I will be like a lion to Ephraim,  
Like a great beast to the House of Judah;[^14]  
I, I will attack and stride away,  
Carrying the prey that no one can rescue; ^5-14

**5:15** And I will return to My abode—  
Till they realize their guilt.  
In their distress, they will seek Me  
And beg for My favor. ^5-15

[^1]: Emendation yields “prophets.”
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: This passage would read well after 5.15; cf. 5.6 with 6.6.
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^5]: Emendation yields “He has.”
[^6]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^7]: The three towns named, in the territory of Benjamin, are now being wrested from Israel by Judah; see v. 10.
[^8]: Emendation yields “Stir up.”
[^9]: I.e., the kingdoms of Judah and Israel (Ephraim).
[^10]: Cf. Targum and Septuagint; but meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^11]: Emendation yields “Israel.”
[^12]: Emendation yields “Israel.”
[^13]: Compare the verb ryb in the sense of “to champion, uphold the cause of,” in Isa. 1.17; 3.13; 19.20 end; 51.22.
[^14]: Emendation yields “Israel.”
