## Mūla

श्रीमद्रात्रिकुले चोक्तं मोक्षः शङ्कापहानितः ।
अशुद्धवासनस्यैषा मोक्षवार्तापि दुर्लभा ॥ ^mula

śrīmadrātrikule coktaṃ mokṣaḥ śaṅkāpahānitaḥ |
aśuddhavāsanasyaiṣā mokṣavārtāpi durlabhā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 10 - Chapter Fifteen, part 9#^pg359|p. 341]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=359>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 15.595cd–596ab.
>
> It is said in the venerable Rātrikula: ‘(The chance of achieving) liberation is destroyed by doubts; for one whose latent tendencies are impure, even (just) this talk of liberation is hard to obtain (durlabhā).
