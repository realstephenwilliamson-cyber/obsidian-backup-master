# Sirach 27 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Sirach 26|← Sirach 26]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Sirach 28|Sirach 28 →]]

**27:1** Many have committed sin for gain,^[Other ancient authorities read *a trifle*] and those who seek to get rich will avert their eyes. ^27-1

**27:2** As a stake is driven firmly into a fissure between stones, so sin is wedged in between selling and buying. ^27-2

**27:3** If a person is not steadfast in the fear of the Lord, his house will be quickly overthrown. ^27-3

## Tests in life

**27:4** When a sieve is shaken, the refuse appears; so does a person’s waste when he speaks. ^27-4

**27:5** The kiln tests the potter’s vessels, and the test of a person is in his conversation. ^27-5

**27:6** Its fruit discloses the cultivation of a tree; so does speech^[Other ancient authorities read *reasoning*] the thoughts of the human mind. ^27-6

**27:7** Do not praise anyone before he speaks, for this is the way people are tested. ^27-7

## Reward and retribution

**27:8** If you pursue justice, you will attain it and wear it like a glorious robe. ^27-8

**27:9** Birds roost with their own kind, so honesty comes home to those who practice it. ^27-9

**27:10** A lion lies in wait for prey; so does sin for evildoers. ^27-10

## Varieties of speech

**27:11** The conversation of the godly is always wise, but the fool changes like the moon. ^27-11

**27:12** Among stupid people limit your time, but among thoughtful people linger on. ^27-12

**27:13** The talk of fools is offensive, and their laughter is wantonly sinful. ^27-13

**27:14** Their cursing and swearing make one’s hair stand on end, and their quarrels make others stop their ears. ^27-14

**27:15** The strife of the proud leads to bloodshed, and their abuse is grievous to hear. ^27-15

## Betraying secrets

**27:16** Whoever betrays secrets destroys confidence and will never find a congenial friend. ^27-16

**27:17** Love your friends and keep faith with them, but if you betray their secrets, do not follow after them. ^27-17

**27:18** For as a person destroys an enemy,^[Other ancient authorities read *corpse*] so you have destroyed the friendship of your neighbor. ^27-18

**27:19** And as you allow a bird to escape from your hand, so you have let your neighbor go and will not catch him again. ^27-19

**27:20** Do not go after him, for he is too far off and has escaped like a gazelle from a snare. ^27-20

**27:21** For a wound may be bandaged, and there is reconciliation after abuse, but whoever has betrayed secrets is without hope. ^27-21

## Hypocrisy and retribution

**27:22** Whoever winks the eye plots harm, and those who know such a one will keep their distance. ^27-22

**27:23** In your presence their mouths are all sweetness, and they admire your words, but later they will twist their speech and with your own words they will cause a scandal. ^27-23

**27:24** I have hated many things but them above all; even the Lord hates them. ^27-24

**27:25** Those who throw a stone straight up throw it on their own heads, and a treacherous blow opens up many wounds. ^27-25

**27:26** Whoever digs a pit will fall into it, and whoever sets a snare will be caught in it. ^27-26

**27:27** If people do evil, it will roll back upon them, and they will not know where it came from. ^27-27

**27:28** Mockery and abuse issue from the proud, but vengeance lies in wait for them like a lion. ^27-28

**27:29** Those who rejoice in the fall of the godly will be caught in a snare, and pain will consume them before their death. ^27-29

## Anger and vengeance

**27:30** Anger and wrath, these also are abominations, yet a sinner holds on to them. ^27-30
