## Mūla

प्राग्वैष्णवाः सौगताश्च सिद्धान्तादिविदस्ततः ।
क्रमात् त्रिकार्थविज्ञानचन्द्रोत्सुकितदृष्टयः ॥ ^mula

prāgvaiṣṇavāḥ saugatāśca siddhāntādividastataḥ |
kramāt trikārthavijñānacandrotsukitadṛṣṭayaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 10 - Chapter Thirteen, part 6#^pg360|p. 338]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=360>))
>
> The ascetics (muni) such as Nārada who ask questions in the Mālinīvijayottara were previously Vaiṣṇavas, Buddhists, knowers of (dualist Śaiva) Siddhāntas, and so on progressively, before (their) views (developed) into a desire for (the true) knowledge, (resplendent like) the moon of the Trika teachings (trikārtha). (with 13.347cd–348)

## Viveka

> क्रमात् त्रिकार्थविज्ञानचन्द्रोत्सुकितदृष्टयः ॥
