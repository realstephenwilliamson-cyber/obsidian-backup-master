# Zohar 2.236a

← [[Zohar 2.235b]] · [[Zohar (Soncino)]] · [[Zohar 2.236b]] →

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 6.06 - Parashat Pequdei#^z2-236a|Parashat Pequdei, at 2:236a]]

*Volume 2, folio 236a* · Pekudei

*Passage Pekudei 32:297 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 2.235b#^pekudei-32-297|Pekudei 32:297]].*

**Pekudei 32:298** Likewise, the lower Tabernacle was wrought by Bezalel and Oholiab, the one of the right, the other of the left, followed by “every wisehearted man”, all after the supernal pattern. ^pekudei-32-298

**Pekudei 32:299** On the day the Tabernacle was reared up death was removed from the world, that is, it was deprived of its dominion over the world. For, indeed, the entire extinction of the evil impulse will not come to pass until the coming of King Messiah, when the Holy One, blessed be He, will rejoice in His works and “he will swallow up death for ever” (Isa. 25, 8). Yet when the Tabernacle was reared up by the hand of Moses the power of the evil impulse was subdued so that it could not exercise dominion. At that time the power of Samael, the wielder of the fury of the “left side”, was removed from the evil serpent, so that the latter was not able to dominate the world or attach himself to man and lead him astray. ^pekudei-32-299

**Pekudei 32:300** R. Judah said: ‘It is written: “And Moses used to take the tent and pitch it without the camp” (Ex. 33, 7). The reason of this was that Moses did not wish that the “holy side” should rest in the midst of the side of defilement.’ Said R. Eleazar: ‘So long as the “holy side” rules, the side of defilement is powerless and bows before it. So we have learnt that so long as Jerusalem is in its fulness wicked, Tyre remains devastated.’[^1] ^pekudei-32-300

**Pekudei 33:301** R. Eleazar said: ‘It is written: “And Rebekah lifted up her eyes, and when she saw Isaac, she alighted from the camel” (Gen. 24, 64). ^pekudei-33-301

**Pekudei 33:302** What is the significance of this verse that it should be written in the Torah? Is it merely to tell us that the sight of Isaac’s good looks made her alight from the camel? The truth is that this verse contains a recondite meaning. Observe that when Rebekah encountered Isaac it was the time of *Minha* (afternoon prayer), a time when Rigour is at large in the world, and she discerned him as one who was of the region of fierce Rigour, a region symbolized by the camel (*gamal*),[^2] and hence she leaned and slipped off that camel. ^pekudei-33-302

**Pekudei 34:303** This camel is all-devouring and all-exterminating, is always ready to inflict death on man. Hence, whenever a man sees a camel in his dream it signifies that death was decreed upon him, but he was delivered. ^pekudei-34-303

**Pekudei 34:304** Observe that the side of defilement is so designated by reason of it having brought death into the world. The same it was that seduced Adam and his wife, and Samael is the one that rides on it and leads the world astray, and brings about the death of every one. It was Adam who first drew him to himself, so that he became their seducer. Of this Solomon said, “and come not nigh the door of her house” (Prov. 5, 8), for whoever comes nigh her house, she emerges, attaches herself, and is drawn unto him. ^pekudei-34-304

**Pekudei 34:305** Thus when Rebekah discerned Isaac as being of the side of Rigour, the side of the dross of gold, she straightway alighted from the camel so as to break herself loose from the Rigour and the dross. ^pekudei-34-305

**Pekudei 35:306** Observe that when the Israelites committed the sin of the Golden Calf there was no reason why they should have made a calf rather than anything else of the evil side. But, indeed, they did not choose the Calf, but merely said: “Up, make us a god who shall go before us” (Ex. 32, 1); and Aaron intended to delay them. ^pekudei-35-306

[^1]: v. T. B. [[Pesachim 42#42b|Pesahim 42b]].
[^2]: The Zohar plays upon the Hebrew root GML, which as G *a* M *a* L =camel, and as G *e* M *u* L=reward, requitement, for good or evil deed.
