## Mūla

श्रीमन्निशाकुलेऽप्युक्तं मिथ्याभावितचेतसः ।
मलमायाविचारेण क्लिश्यन्ते स्वल्पबुद्धयः ॥ ^mula

śrīmanniśākule'pyuktaṃ mithyābhāvitacetasaḥ |
malamāyāvicāreṇa kliśyante svalpabuddhayaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 07 - Chapter Thirteen, part 3#^pg220|p. 200]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=220>))
>
> It is also said in the venerable Niśākula: ‘O dear one, stupid people whose minds are overcome with delusion are afflicted by (their mistaken) notion (vicāra) of Māyā and Impurity. O beloved, what can a speck of dust on a crystal do (to it)? (So-called) Impurity (mala) is like the blue (colour) of the sky. One should therefore abandon (all) doubt that Impurity (mala) (has any absolute existence and can sully the Self).’ (with 13.126–127)
