## Acknowledgments

I wish to thank each of the contributors for their hard work and, most especially, their patience, for this volume took shape rather slowly over the course of five years. I extend heartfelt thanks to the staff at the State University of New York Press as well, especially Nancy Ellgate and Judith Block, who have been cheerful and supportive editors. Anonymous readers made several helpful suggestions and comments, and I am grateful for their insights. My gratitude extends also to Judy Davis, who prepared the index.

Portions of C. Mackenzie Brown’s chapter are reproduced from his book The Devī-Gītā; The Song of the Goddess: A Translation, Annotation, and Commentary (Albany: State University of New York Press, 1998).

All photographs in chapter 6 were taken by Sree Padma.
