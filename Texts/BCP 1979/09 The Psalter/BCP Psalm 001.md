# Psalm 1

[[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 002]] →

*Beatus vir qui non abiit* · Book One · First Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 001]]

**1:1** Happy are they who have not walked in the counsel of the wicked, \*  
nor lingered in the way of sinners,  
nor sat in the seats of the scornful! ^1-1

**1:2** Their delight is in the law of the LORD, \*  
and they meditate on his law day and night. ^1-2

**1:3** They are like trees planted by streams of water,  
bearing fruit in due season, with leaves that do not wither; \*  
everything they do shall prosper. ^1-3

**1:4** It is not so with the wicked; \*  
they are like chaff which the wind blows away. ^1-4

**1:5** Therefore the wicked shall not stand upright when judgment comes, \*  
nor the sinner in the council of the righteous. ^1-5

**1:6** For the LORD knows the way of the righteous, \*  
but the way of the wicked is doomed. ^1-6
