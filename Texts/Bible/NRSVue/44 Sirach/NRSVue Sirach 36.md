# Sirach 36 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Sirach 35|← Sirach 35]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Sirach 37|Sirach 37 →]]

## A prayer for God’s people

**36:1** Have mercy upon us, O God^[Heb: Gk *O Master, the God*] of all, ^36-1

**36:2** and put all the nations in fear of you. ^36-2

**36:3** Lift up your hand against foreign nations and let them see your might. ^36-3

**36:4** As you have used us to show your holiness to them, so use them to show your glory to us. ^36-4

**36:5** Then they will know,^[Heb: Gk *And let them know you*] as we have known, that there is no God but you, O Lord. ^36-5

**36:6** Give new signs and work other wonders; ^36-6

**36:7** make your hand and right arm glorious. ^36-7

**36:8** Rouse your anger and pour out your wrath; ^36-8

**36:9** destroy the adversary and wipe out the enemy. ^36-9

**36:10** Hasten the day, and remember the appointed time,^[Other ancient authorities read *remember your oath*] and let people recount your mighty deeds. ^36-10

**36:11** Let survivors be consumed in the fiery wrath, and may those who harm your people meet destruction. ^36-11

**36:12** Crush the heads of hostile rulers who say, “There is no one but ourselves.” ^36-12

**36:13** Gather all the tribes of Jacob,^[Verse numbers 14 and 15 are not used in chapter 36] ^36-13

**36:16** and give them their inheritance, as at the beginning. ^36-16

**36:17** Have mercy, O Lord, on the people called by your name, on Israel, whom you have named^[Other ancient authorities read *you have likened to*] your firstborn. ^36-17

**36:18** Have pity on the city of your sanctuary,^[Or *on your holy city*] Jerusalem, the place of your dwelling.^[Heb: Gk *your rest*] ^36-18

**36:19** Fill Zion with your majesty^[Heb Syr: Gk *the celebration of your wondrous deeds*] and your temple^[Heb Syr: Gk Lat *people*] with your glory. ^36-19

**36:20** Bear witness to those whom you created in the beginning, and fulfill the prophecies spoken in your name. ^36-20

**36:21** Reward those who wait for you, and let your prophets be found trustworthy. ^36-21

**36:22** Hear, O Lord, the prayer of your servants, according to your goodwill toward^[Heb and two Gk mss: Lat and most Gk mss read *according to the blessing of Aaron for*] your people, and all who are on the earth will know that you are the Lord, the God of the ages. ^36-22

## Concerning discrimination

**36:23** The stomach will take any food, yet one food is better than another. ^36-23

**36:24** As the palate tastes the kinds of game, so an intelligent mind detects false words. ^36-24

**36:25** A perverse mind will cause grief, but a person with experience will pay him back. ^36-25

**36:26** A woman will accept any man as a husband, but one daughter is preferable to another. ^36-26

**36:27** A woman’s beauty lights up a man’s face, and there is nothing he desires more. ^36-27

**36:28** If kindness and humility mark her speech, her husband is more fortunate than other men. ^36-28

**36:29** He who acquires a wife gets his best possession,^[Heb: Gk *enters upon a possession*] a helper fit for him and a pillar of support.^[Heb: Gk *rest*] ^36-29

**36:30** Where there is no fence, the property will be plundered, and where there is no wife, a man will become a fugitive and a wanderer.^[Heb: Gk *wander about and sigh*] ^36-30

**36:31** For who will trust a nimble robber who skips from city to city? So who will trust a man who has no nest but lodges wherever nighttime finds him? ^36-31
