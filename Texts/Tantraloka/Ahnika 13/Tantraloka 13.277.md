## Mūla

अज्ञानाख्याद्वियोक्तेति शिवभावप्रकाशकः ।
नान्येन शिवभावो हि केनचित्संप्रकाशते ॥ ^mula

ajñānākhyādviyokteti śivabhāvaprakāśakaḥ |
nānyena śivabhāvo hi kenacitsaṃprakāśate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 09 - Chapter Thirteen, part 5#^pg317|p. 295]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=317>))
>
> The descent of the power (of grace) that comes from the supreme Lord separates (the soul) from the Impurity called ignorance, and so (He is said to be) one who illumines Śiva’s being (Śivabhāva). (with 13.276cd–277ab)
>
> Śiva’s being is not illumined by any other (deity). (with 13.277cd)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* ननु स एवैवं कस्मात्? -- इत्याशङ्क्याह --

> नान्येन शिवभावो हि केनचित्संप्रकाशते ॥

अन्येनेति विष्ण्वादिसंबन्धिना, ते ह्यत्र भ्रन्ता एव -- इत्यभिप्रायः ॥ २७७ ॥
