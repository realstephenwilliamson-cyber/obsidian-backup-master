## Mūla

मुक्तस्तदैव काले तु यन्त्रं तिष्ठति केवलम् ।
सुरापः स्तेयहारी च ब्रह्महा गुरुतल्पगः ॥ ^mula

muktastadaiva kāle tu yantraṃ tiṣṭhati kevalam |
surāpaḥ steyahārī ca brahmahā gurutalpagaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 03 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 1#^pg58|p. 41]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=58>))
>
> It is also said in the venerable Niśāṭtana: ‘when (a word) has come forth from the teacher’s mouth and has reached within (the disciple’s) ears, (the disciple) is liberated at that very moment by the penetration (of consciousness) brought about by the teacher’s words (kathanāveśana); (merged into it,) all that remains (of the disciple) is the machine (of his body)." (This may happen to anybody, be it) a drunkard, a thief, one who has murdered a brahmin (or) defiled (his) teacher’s bed, an outcaste or brahmin, a child, old man or a youth. A man of knowledge living nearby purifies even the country (in which he lives). Present there is the God along with the Goddess and (their devoted) servants.’ (with 28.72–75ab)
