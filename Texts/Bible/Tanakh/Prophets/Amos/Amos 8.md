## Chapter 8

**8:1** This is what my Lord GOD showed me: There was a basket of figs.[^1] ^8-1

**8:2** He said, “What do you see, Amos?” “A basket of figs,” I replied. And the LORD said to me: “The hour of doom[^2] has come for My people Israel; I will not pardon them again. ^8-2

**8:3** And the singing women of the palace shall howl on that day—declares my Lord GOD:  
So many corpses  
Left lying everywhere!  
Hush!” ^8-3

**8:4** Listen to this, you who devour the needy, annihilating the poor of the land,[^3] ^8-4

**8:5** saying, “If only the new moon were over, so that we could sell grain; the sabbath, so that we could offer wheat for sale, using an *ephah* that is too small, and a shekel that is too big,[^4] tilting a dishonest scale, ^8-5

**8:6** and selling grain refuse as grain! We will buy the poor for silver, the needy for a pair of sandals.” ^8-6

**8:7** The LORD swears by[^5] the Pride of Jacob: “I will never forget any of their doings.” ^8-7

**8:8** Shall not the earth shake for this  
And all that dwell on it mourn?  
Shall it not all rise like the Nile  
And surge and subside like the Nile of Egypt? ^8-8

**8:9** And in that day  
—declares my Lord GOD—  
I will make the sun set at noon,  
I will darken the earth on a sunny day. ^8-9

**8:10** I will turn your festivals into mourning  
And all your songs into dirges;  
I will put sackcloth on all loins  
And tonsures on every head.  
I will make it[^6] mourn as for an only child,  
All[^7] of it as on a bitter day. ^8-10

**8:11** A time is coming—declares my Lord GOD—when I will send a famine upon the land: not a hunger for bread or a thirst for water, but for hearing the words of the LORD. ^8-11

**8:12** Men shall wander from sea to sea[^8] and from north to east to seek the word of the LORD, but they shall not find it. ^8-12

**8:13** In that day, the beautiful maidens and the young men shall faint with thirst— ^8-13

**8:14** Those who swear by the guilt of Samaria,  
Saying, “As your god lives, Dan,”[^9]  
And “As the way to Beer-sheba lives”[^10]—  
They shall fall to rise no more. ^8-14

[^1]: Heb. qayiṣ, lit. “summer fruit.”
[^2]: Heb. qeṣ.
[^3]: Emendation yields “who on every new moon devour the needy, and on every sabbath the humble of the land”; cf. v. 5.
[^4]: Giving short measures of grain, but using oversize weights for the silver received in payment.
[^5]: Or “concerning”; cf. 6.8 with note.
[^6]: I.e., the earth; cf. vv. 8 and 9d.
[^7]: Lit. “the end.”
[^8]: Emendation yields “south to west.”
[^9]: See 1 Kings 12.28–29.
[^10]: See 5.5 with note.
