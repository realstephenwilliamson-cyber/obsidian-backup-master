## Mūla

परतद्रूपता शम्भोराद्याच्छक्त्यविभागिनः ।
तेनायमत्र वाक्यार्थो विज्ञेयं प्रोन्मिषत्स्वयम् ॥ ^mula

paratadrūpatā śambhorādyācchaktyavibhāginaḥ |
tenāyamatra vākyārtho vijñeyaṃ pronmiṣatsvayam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. I
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 01 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v01 10 - Chapter One, part 9#^pg361|p. 347]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 01.pdf#page=361>))
>
> And (the term) ‘penetration’ (āveśa) is that state (of identity) with supreme (awakened consciousness) (paratadrūpatā) (that emerges) from Śambhu, Who is undivided from (His own) power and precedes (all other means to realization). (It takes place) by virtue of the submergence of that (contracted) nature of the one who is not free (asvatantra), which is one’s own (svatadrūpatā). (with 1.173cd–174ab)
>
> Thus, the meaning of this statement here is this: the object of realization (vijñeya), unfolding by itself spontaneously, even without (any intervening) intellection (niścaya), suddenly subordinating the (limited) subject reflected in the mirror of the (supreme) subject (mātr), continuously revealing its own glory (vibhūti), abides at increasingly higher levels (tāratamyatah) within the excellence of the purity (nairmalya) of the Heart (of reflective awareness). (with 1.174cd–176ab)
