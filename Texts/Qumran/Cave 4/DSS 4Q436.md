### Frag. 1 col. i (= 4Q435 1 i)

**1** knowledge to strengthen the downcast heart and to triumph in it over the spirit, to console those oppressed in the epoch of their anguish, to lift the hands of the fallen ones, ^f1c1-1

**2** to make receptacles of knowledge, to give knowledge to the wise. And the upright will increase insight to understand ^f1c1-2

**3** your deeds which you did in the years of old, in the years of generation after generation, eternal knowledge which ^f1c1-3

**4** [yo]u [have set] in front of me. You have preserved your law before me, and your covenant has been conﬁrmed for me, and you have strengthened upon my heart ^f1c1-4

**5** […] to walk in your paths. You called my heart to attention and you sharpened my kidneys so that they do not forget your laws. ^f1c1-5

**6** […] you […] your law, and you opened my kidneys and you strengthened me so that I would follow the paths of ^f1c1-6

**7** […] your […]. You have made my mouth like a sharpened sword and have opened my tongue to the words of holiness; and you have placed ^f1c1-7

**8** […] instruction, so that they do not meditate on the actions of the man whose lips are in the Pit. You have strengthened my feet ^f1c1-8

**9** […] and with your hand you have taken hold of my right hand and you have sent me. Blank In […] ^f1c1-9

**10** […] you have [re]moved from me, and in stead of it you have given a pure heart; the evil inclination [you have] remo[ved …] ^f1c1-10
