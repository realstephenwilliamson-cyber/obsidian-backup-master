## Mūla

तत्त्वभावं तथान्योऽपि कलादिस्तत्त्वविस्तरः ।
निरुद्धशक्तेर्या किंचित्कर्तृतोद्वलनात्मिका ॥ ^mula

tattvabhāvaṃ tathānyo'pi kalādistattvavistaraḥ |
niruddhaśakteryā kiṃcitkartṛtodvalanātmikā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 06 - Chapter Nine, part 4#^pg153|p. 136]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=153>))
>
> Just as Māyā, which is the Lord’s power, assumes the condition of a differentiated metaphysical principle, the other extensive (manifestation) of the principles from the Force (of limited agency) (kalā) onwards occurs in the same way. (with 9.154cd–155ab)
>
> The Force (of limited agency) (kalā) is said to be the power of the Lord, which strengthens the limited capacity to act (kiṁcitkartṛtā) of the soul, whose powers have been blocked, and casts it down (kṣeptrī) (onto the lower levels of embodied existence). (with 9.155cd–156ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* एतदेवोपपादयति -

> निरुद्धशक्तेर्या किंचित्कर्तृतोद्वलनात्मिका ॥

> नाथस्य शक्तिः साधस्तात्पुंसः क्षेप्त्री कलोच्यते ।

निरुद्धशक्तेरिति -- स्वरूपस्य गोपितत्वात् । अधस्तादिति -- प्राणादिप्रमातृतायाम् । कलाशब्दस्य च अत्र प्रवृत्तावधःप्रक्षेप एव निमित्तम् ॥ १५५ ॥
