## Mūla

ततः परमघोरान्तं पाद्यकाद्ये च पूर्ववत् ।
परापरा समाख्याता अपरा च प्रकथ्यते ॥ ^mula

tataḥ paramaghorāntaṁ pādyakādye ca pūrvavat |
parāparā samākhyātā aparā ca prakathyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2023 – March 2023
> p. 48 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=48>))
>
> (Extract) four staffs (R) of the trident, the first pair of which is united with the right ornament (U)[^36] (whereas the second pair is not) (RU RU RA RA). (Then,) at the end of it, deposit the left foot (PH) (onto which) the skull (Ṭ) has fallen (PHAṬ). Then (finally the syllable) that comes after PARAMAGHORE (i.e., HUṀ),[^37] and as before, the foot (PH) with the skull (Ṭ) (PHAṬ). (with 3.49–50ab)

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2023 – March 2023
> p. 49 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=49>))
>
> Parāparā has been taught,[^38] (now) Aparā is going to be taught.[^39] (with 3.50cd)

[^36]: Read *dvayam usthitam* for *dvayasaṁsthi(mutthi)tam*.
[^37]: The following HAḤ seems to have been omitted.
[^38]: See TĀ 30/20-36ab. The Parāparā Vidyā is OṀ AGHORE HRĪḤ PARAMAGHORE HUṀ GHORARŪPE HAḤ GHORAMUKHĪ BHĪMA BHĪṢAṆE VAMA PIBA HE RURU RARA PHAṬ HUṀ HAḤ PHAṬ. Notice, by the way, that whereas Abhinavagupta presents Aparā and Parā Vidyās, he does not present Parāparā Vidyā, although he does of course refer to it and its structure.
[^39]: Concerning Aparā Vidyā, see TĀ 30/26cd.
