# Psalms 136 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 135|← Psalms 135]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 137|Psalms 137 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 136|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**136:1** Upon the rivers of Babylon, there we sat and wept: when we remembered Sion: ^136-1

**136:2** On the willows in the midst thereof we hung up our instruments. ^136-2

**136:3** For there they that led us into captivity required of us the words of songs. And they that carried us away, said: Sing ye to us a hymn of the songs of Sion. ^136-3

**136:4** How shall we sing the song of the Lord in a strange land? ^136-4

**136:5** If I forget thee, O Jerusalem, let my right hand be forgotten. ^136-5

**136:6** Let my tongue cleave to my jaws, if I do not remember thee: If I make not Jerusalem the beginning of my joy. ^136-6

**136:7** Remember, O Lord, the children of Edom, in the day of Jerusalem: Who say: Rase it, rase it, even to the foundation thereof. ^136-7

**136:8** O daughter of Babylon, miserable: blessed shall he be who shall repay thee thy payment which thou hast paid us. ^136-8

**136:9** Blessed be he that shall take and dash thy little ones against the rock. ^136-9
