# Proverbs 13

Compare: [[NRSVue Proverbs 13|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 13

**13:1** A wise son—it is through the discipline of his father;  
A scoffer—he never heard reproof. ^13-1

**13:2** A man enjoys good from the fruit of his speech;  
But out of the throat of the treacherous comes lawlessness. ^13-2

**13:3** He who guards his tongue[^1] preserves his life;  
He who opens wide his lips, it is his ruin. ^13-3

**13:4** A lazy man craves, but has nothing;  
The diligent shall feast on rich fare. ^13-4

**13:5** A righteous man hates lies;  
The wicked man is vile and disgraceful. ^13-5

**13:6** Righteousness protects him whose way is blameless;  
Wickedness subverts the sinner. ^13-6

**13:7** One man pretends to be rich and has nothing;  
Another professes to be poor and has much wealth. ^13-7

**13:8** Riches are ransom for a man’s life,  
The poor never heard a reproof. ^13-8

**13:9** The light of the righteous is radiant;  
The lamp of the wicked is extinguished. ^13-9

**13:10** Arrogance yields nothing but strife;[^2]  
Wisdom belongs to those who seek advice. ^13-10

**13:11** Wealth may dwindle to less than nothing,  
But he who gathers little by little increases it. ^13-11

**13:12** Hope deferred sickens the heart,  
But desire realized is a tree of life. ^13-12

**13:13** He who disdains a precept will be injured thereby;  
He who respects a command will be rewarded. ^13-13

**13:14** The instruction of a wise man is a fountain of life,  
Enabling one to avoid deadly snares. ^13-14

**13:15** Good sense wins favor;  
The way of treacherous men is unchanging.[^3] ^13-15

**13:16** Every clever man acts knowledgeably,  
But a dullard exposes his stupidity. ^13-16

**13:17** Harm befalls a wicked messenger;  
A faithful courier brings healing. ^13-17

**13:18** Poverty and humiliation are for him who spurns discipline;  
But he who takes reproof to heart gets honor. ^13-18

**13:19** Desire realized is sweet to the soul;  
To turn away from evil is abhorrent to the stupid. ^13-19

**13:20** He who keeps company with the wise becomes wise,  
But he who consorts with dullards comes to grief. ^13-20

**13:21** Misfortune pursues sinners,  
But the righteous are well rewarded. ^13-21

**13:22** A good man has what to bequeath to his grandchildren,  
For the wealth of sinners is stored up for the righteous. ^13-22

**13:23** The tillage of the poor yields much food;  
But substance is swept away for lack of moderation. ^13-23

**13:24** He who spares the rod hates his son,  
But he who loves him disciplines him early. ^13-24

**13:25** The righteous man eats to his heart’s content,  
But the belly of the wicked is empty. ^13-25

[^1]: Lit. “mouth.”
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Or “harsh.”
