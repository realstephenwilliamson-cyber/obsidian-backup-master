## Chapter 2

**2:1** Alas!  
The Lord in His wrath  
Has shamed[^1] Fair Zion,  
Has cast down from heaven to earth  
The majesty of Israel.  
He did not remember His Footstool[^2]  
On His day of wrath. ^2-1

**2:2** The Lord has laid waste without pity  
All the habitations of Jacob;  
He has razed in His anger  
Fair Judah’s strongholds.  
He has brought low in dishonor  
The kingdom and its leaders. ^2-2

**2:3** In blazing anger He has cut down  
All the might of Israel;  
He has withdrawn His right hand  
In the presence of the foe;  
He has ravaged Jacob like flaming fire,  
Consuming on all sides. ^2-3

**2:4** He bent His bow like an enemy,  
Poised His right hand like a foe;  
He slew all who delighted the eye.  
He poured out His wrath like fire  
In the Tent of Fair Zion. ^2-4

**2:5** The Lord has acted like a foe,  
He has laid waste Israel,  
Laid waste all her citadels,  
Destroyed her strongholds.  
He has increased within Fair Judah  
Mourning and moaning. ^2-5

**2:6** He has stripped His Booth[^3] like a garden,  
He has destroyed His Tabernacle;[^4]  
The LORD has ended in Zion  
Festival and sabbath;  
In His raging anger He has spurned  
King and priest. ^2-6

**2:7** The Lord has rejected His altar,  
Disdained His Sanctuary.  
He has handed over to the foe  
The walls of its citadels;  
They raised a shout in the House of the LORD  
As on a festival day. ^2-7

**2:8** The LORD resolved to destroy  
The wall of Fair Zion;  
He measured with a line,[^5] refrained not  
From bringing destruction.  
He has made wall and rampart to mourn,  
Together they languish. ^2-8

**2:9** Her gates have sunk into the ground,  
He has smashed her bars to bits;  
Her king and her leaders are in exile,[^6]  
Instruction[^7] is no more;  
Her prophets, too, receive  
No vision from the LORD. ^2-9

**2:10** Silent sit on the ground  
The elders of Fair Zion;  
They have strewn dust on their heads  
And girded themselves with sackcloth;  
The maidens of Jerusalem have bowed  
Their heads to the ground. ^2-10

**2:11** My eyes are spent with tears,  
My heart is in tumult,  
My being melts away[^8]  
Over the ruin of my poor people,[^9]  
As babes and sucklings languish  
In the squares of the city. ^2-11

**2:12** They keep asking their mothers,  
“Where is bread and wine?”  
As they languish like battle-wounded  
In the squares of the town,  
As their life runs out  
In their mothers’ bosoms. ^2-12

**2:13** What can I take as witness[^10] or liken  
To you, O Fair Jerusalem?  
What can I match with you to console you,  
O Fair Maiden Zion?  
For your ruin is vast as the sea:  
Who can heal you? ^2-13

**2:14** Your seers prophesied to you  
Delusion and folly.  
They did not expose your iniquity  
So as to restore your fortunes,  
But prophesied to you oracles  
Of delusion and deception. ^2-14

**2:15** All who pass your way  
Clap their hands at you;  
They hiss and wag their head  
At Fair Jerusalem:[^11]  
“Is this the city that was called  
Perfect in Beauty,  
Joy of All the Earth?” ^2-15

**2:16** All your enemies  
Jeer at you;  
They hiss and gnash their teeth,  
And cry: “We’ve ruined her!  
Ah, this is the day we hoped for;  
We have lived to see it!”[^12] ^2-16

**2:17** The LORD has done what He purposed,  
Has carried out the decree  
That He ordained long ago;  
He has torn down without pity.  
He has let the foe rejoice over you,  
Has exalted the might of your enemies. ^2-17

**2:18** Their heart cried out[^13] to the Lord.  
O wall of Fair Zion,  
Shed tears like a torrent  
Day and night!  
Give yourself no respite,  
Your eyes no rest. ^2-18

**2:19** Arise, cry out in the night  
At the beginning of the watches,  
Pour out your heart like water  
In the presence of the Lord!  
Lift up your hands to Him  
For the life of your infants,  
Who faint for hunger  
At every street corner. ^2-19

**2:20** See, O LORD, and behold,  
To whom You have done this!  
Alas, women eat their own fruit,  
Their new-born[^14] babes!  
Alas, priest and prophet are slain  
In the Sanctuary of the Lord! ^2-20

**2:21** Prostrate in the streets lie  
Both young and old.  
My maidens and youths  
Are fallen by the sword;  
You slew them on Your day of wrath,  
You slaughtered without pity. ^2-21

**2:22** You summoned, as on a festival,  
My neighbors from round about.  
On the day of the wrath of the LORD,  
None survived or escaped;  
Those whom I bore[^15] and reared  
My foe has consumed. ^2-22

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: I.e., the Temple.
[^3]: I.e., the Temple.
[^4]: Lit. “(Tent of) Meeting.”
[^5]: I.e., He made His plans.
[^6]: Lit. “among the nations.”
[^7]: Heb. torah, here priestly instruction; cf. Jer. 18.18; Hag. 2.11; Mal. 2.6.
[^8]: Lit. “My liver spills on the ground.”
[^9]: Lit. “the daughter of my people”; so elsewhere in poetry.
[^10]: Emendation yields “compare.”
[^11]: These gestures were intended to ward off the calamity from the viewer; cf., e.g., Jer. 18.16 and note; Job 27.23.
[^12]: Lit. “We have attained, we have seen.”
[^13]: Emendation yields “Cry aloud.”
[^14]: The root has this meaning in Arabic; others “dandled.”
[^15]: The root has this meaning in Arabic; others “dandled.”
