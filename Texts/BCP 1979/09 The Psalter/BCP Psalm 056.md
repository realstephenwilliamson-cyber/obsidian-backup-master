# Psalm 56

← [[BCP Psalm 055]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 057]] →

*Miserere mei, Deus* · Book Two · Eleventh Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 056]]

**56:1** Have mercy on me, O God,  
for my enemies are hounding me; \*  
all day long they assault and oppress me. ^56-1

**56:2** They hound me all the day long; \*  
truly there are many who fight against me, O Most High. ^56-2

**56:3** Whenever I am afraid, \*  
I will put my trust in you. ^56-3

**56:4** In God, whose word I praise,  
in God I trust and will not be afraid, \*  
for what can flesh do to me? ^56-4

**56:5** All day long they damage my cause; \*  
their only thought is to do me evil. ^56-5

[p. 663] ^pg663

**56:6** They band together; they lie in wait; \*  
they spy upon my footsteps;  
because they seek my life. ^56-6

**56:7** Shall they escape despite their wickedness? \*  
O God, in your anger, cast down the peoples. ^56-7

**56:8** You have noted my lamentation;  
put my tears into your bottle; \*  
are they not recorded in your book? ^56-8

**56:9** Whenever I call upon you, my enemies will be put to flight; \*  
this I know, for God is on my side. ^56-9

**56:10** In God the LORD, whose word I praise,  
in God I trust and will not be afraid, \*  
for what can mortals do to me? ^56-10

**56:11** I am bound by the vow I made to you, O God; \*  
I will present to you thank-offerings; ^56-11

**56:12** For you have rescued my soul from death and my feet  
from stumbling, \*  
that I may walk before God in the light of the living. ^56-12
