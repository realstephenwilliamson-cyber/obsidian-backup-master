## Mūla

जपाकुसुमसङ्काशं चैतन्यं तस्य मध्यतः ।
वायुना प्रेरितं चक्रं वह्निना परिदीपितम् ॥ ^mula

japākusumasaṅkāśaṃ caitanyaṃ tasya madhyataḥ |
vāyunā preritaṃ cakraṃ vahninā paridīpitam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 12 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 4#^pg384|p. 367]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=384>))
>
> (S(The teacher) should (now) deposit the Seed of Śakti (the letter E) and the one called (the seed of Māyā) with all its limbs (HRĪM). (Then,) Mantra (H) is deposited in the Wheel of the Heart, adorned with the twelve vowels, and in the middle of that is consciousness, which is like a China rose (japākusuma). (The teacher) should meditate on how the Wheel is impelled by the wind (Y) and inflamed by fire (R), and should repeat the Mantra in association with (the disciple’s) name (uttered) in between (each repetition). (In this way,) the disciple is, without doubt, paralysed in half the twinkling of an eye. (with 29.214–216)
