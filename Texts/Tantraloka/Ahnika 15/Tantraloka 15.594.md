## Mūla

न लिखेन्मन्त्रहृदयं श्रीमन्मालोदितं किल ।
तदङ्गादुद्धरेन्मन्त्रं न तु लेखे विलेखयेत् ॥ ^mula

na likhenmantrahṛdayaṃ śrīmanmāloditaṃ kila |
tadaṅgāduddharenmantraṃ na tu lekhe vilekhayet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 10 - Chapter Fifteen, part 9#^pg360|p. 342]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=360>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 15.596cd–597ab.
>
> One should not write the (seed syllable) taught in the venerable Mālā (i.e., Kularatnamālā), which is the Heart of (all) Mantras. (It is said there that) ‘the Mantra should be extracted from its body. It should not be written in a manuscript (lekha).’
