## Mūla

कर्मपाशेऽत्र होतव्ये पूर्णयास्य शुभाशुभम् ।
अशुभं वा भवद्भूतं भावि वाथ समस्तकम् ॥ ^mula

karmapāśe'tra hotavye pūrṇayāsya śubhāśubham |
aśubhaṃ vā bhavadbhūtaṃ bhāvi vātha samastakam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 08 - Chapter Seventeen#^pg189|p. 169]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=189>))
>
> When the fetter of Karma is to be offered to the fire here by means of a full (oblation, the teacher should say): “of this (person) I burn away the good and bad (Karma)”, or (as the case may be), “(only) the bad Karma”, or “the present, past, and the future (Karma)” or “all (of it) together.” (He should then utter) PHAT three times (followed by) VAUṢAT, and cast a full (oblation into the fire). (with 17.69–70ab)
