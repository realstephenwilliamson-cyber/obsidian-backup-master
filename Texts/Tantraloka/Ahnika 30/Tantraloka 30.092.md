## Mūla

एतेन शक्त्युच्चारस्थबीजेनालभ्यते पशुः ।
कृतदीक्षाविधिः पूर्वं ब्रह्मघ्नोऽपि विशुद्ध्यति ॥ ^mula

etena śaktyuccārasthabījenālabhyate paśuḥ |
kṛtadīkṣāvidhiḥ pūrvaṃ brahmaghno'pi viśuddhyati || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 03 - Chapter Thirty, part 2#^pg71|p. 53]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=71>))
>
> The Point (M), the Vital Breath (H), Nectar (S), Water (V), Wind (Y), along with the sixth vowel (Ū) – with this seed syllable, present within (the upward flow of) the utterance Power (śaktyuccāārastha), (the sacrificial) animal is fetched (for the sacrifice). One (for whom) the rite of initiation has been performed, even if he has murdered a Brahmin, is purified (in this way). (with 30.91cd–92)
