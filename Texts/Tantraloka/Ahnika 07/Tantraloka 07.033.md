## Mūla

स ह्येको न भवेत्कश्चित् त्रिजगत्यपि जातुचित् ।
शब्दारूषणया ज्ञानं विकल्पः किल कथ्यते ॥ ^mula

sa hyeko na bhavetkaścit trijagatyapi jātucit |
śabdārūṣaṇayā jñānaṃ vikalpaḥ kila kathyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 02 - Chapter Seven#^pg40|p. 22]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=40>))
>
> (The existence) of representational ideation (vikalpa) of those (Buddhists) who do not know (things) in this way could not be logically (explained). No single (unified representational ideation) would ever exist in (all) the Three Worlds. (with 7.32cd–33ab)
>
> A thought construct (vikalpa) is, according to (the Buddhists, as it is according to us,) knowledge (that has become such because it is) pervaded“' with words. Now if this (process of pervasion) were to be successive, who could conceive of anything, and how could it be conceived in this way? (If one accepted their thesis,) not even (the word) ‘jar’ could be an (object of) thought (vikalpa). What then to say (of the word) ‘standing’ (in the phrase ‘the jar is standing (ghaṭtas tiṣthati)’). In short, no differentiated ideation (vikalpa) could exist (in their view of things), even if it were to subsist for just a single measure (mdtra)”) (of time) or a part of one (amātra). (with 7.33cd–35ab)
