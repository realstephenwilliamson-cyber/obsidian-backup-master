## Mūla

तदावेशवशाच्छिष्यस्तन्मयत्वं प्रपद्यते ।
यथा हि रक्तहृदयस्तांस्तान्कान्तागुणान्स्वयम् ॥ ^mula

tadāveśavaśācchiṣyastanmayatvaṃ prapadyate |
yathā hi raktahṛdayastāṃstānkāntāguṇānsvayam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 08 - Chapter Fifteen, part 7#^pg296|p. 278]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=296>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 15.454cd–455ab.
>
> At the sudden sight of the sacrificial ground (i.e., the Mandala) springing forth radiantly (ullāsita) with the power of the Mantras (projected onto it), by virtue of the penetration (āveśa) of their (power), the disciple attains a state of oneness (tanmaya) (with it). (with 15.451cd–452ab)
>
> Just as one who is in love (raktahrdaya) sees by himself (spontaneously each of the many) virtues of the beloved, so does one purified by a descent of the power (of grace) see in this way the presence of Mantras (in the Mandala). (with 15.452cd–453ab)

> यथा हि रक्तहृदयस्तांस्तान्कान्तागुणान्स्वयम् ॥
> पश्यत्येवं शक्तिपातसंस्कृतो मन्त्रसन्निधिम् ।
