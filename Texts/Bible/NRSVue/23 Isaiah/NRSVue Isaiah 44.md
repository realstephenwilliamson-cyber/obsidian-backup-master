# Isaiah 44 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Isaiah 43|← Isaiah 43]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Isaiah 45|Isaiah 45 →]]

## God’s blessing on Israel

**44:1** But now hear, O Jacob my servant, Israel whom I have chosen! ^44-1

**44:2** Thus says the LORD who made you, who formed you in the womb and will help you: Do not fear, O Jacob my servant, Jeshurun whom I have chosen. ^44-2

**44:3** For I will pour water on the thirsty land and streams on the dry ground; I will pour my spirit upon your descendants and my blessing on your offspring. ^44-3

**44:4** They shall spring up like a green tamarisk,^[Q ms: MT *in the midst of grass*] like willows by flowing streams. ^44-4

**44:5** This one will say, “I am the LORD’s”; another will be called by the name of Jacob; yet another will write on the hand, “The LORD’s,” and adopt the name of Israel. ^44-5

**44:6** Thus says the LORD, the King of Israel, and his Redeemer, the LORD of hosts: I am the first, and I am the last; besides me there is no god. ^44-6

**44:7** Who is like me? Let them proclaim it; let them declare and set it forth before me. Who has announced from of old the things to come?^[Cn: Heb *from my placing an eternal people and things to come*] Let them tell us^[Tg: Heb *them*] what is yet to be. ^44-7

**44:8** Do not fear or be afraid; have I not told you from of old and declared it? You are my witnesses! Is there any god besides me? There is no other rock; I know not one. ^44-8

## The absurdity of idol worship

**44:9** All who make idols are nothing, and the things they delight in do not profit; their witnesses neither see nor know, and so they will be put to shame. ^44-9

**44:10** Who would fashion a god or cast an image that can do no good? ^44-10

**44:11** All its devotees shall be put to shame; the artisans, too, are merely human. Let them all assemble; let them stand up; they shall be terrified; they shall all be put to shame. ^44-11

**44:12** The blacksmith works it with a tool over the coals, shaping it with hammers and forging it with his strong arm; he becomes hungry, and his strength fails; he drinks no water and is faint. ^44-12

**44:13** The carpenter stretches a line, marks it out with a stylus, fashions it with planes, and marks it with a compass; he makes it in human form, with human beauty, to be set up in a shrine. ^44-13

**44:14** He cuts down cedars or chooses a holm tree or an oak and lets it grow strong among the trees of the forest. He plants a cedar and the rain nourishes it. ^44-14

**44:15** Then it can be used as fuel. Part of it he takes and warms himself; he kindles a fire and bakes bread. Then he makes a god and worships it, makes it a carved image and bows down before it. ^44-15

**44:16** Half of it he burns in the fire; over this half he roasts meat, eats it,^[Cn Compare Gk Syr: Heb *he eats, he roasts a roast*] and is satisfied. He also warms himself and says, “Ah, I am warm^[Q ms: MT *I see*] by the fire!” ^44-16

**44:17** The rest of it he makes into a god, his idol, bows down to it and worships it; he prays to it and says, “Save me, for you are my god!” ^44-17

**44:18** They do not know, nor do they comprehend, for their eyes are shut, so that they cannot see, and their minds as well, so that they cannot understand. ^44-18

**44:19** No one considers, nor is there knowledge or discernment to say, “Half of it I burned in the fire; I also baked bread on its coals; I roasted meat and have eaten. Now shall I make the rest of it an abomination? Shall I fall down before a block of wood?” ^44-19

**44:20** He feeds on ashes; a deluded mind has led him astray, and he cannot save himself or say, “Is not this thing in my right hand a fraud?” ^44-20

## Israel is not forgotten

**44:21** Remember these things, O Jacob, and Israel, for you are my servant; I formed you, you are my servant; O Israel, do not forget me.^[Q ms Compare Gk Syr Tg: MT *you will not be forgotten by me*] ^44-21

**44:22** I have swept away your transgressions like a cloud and your sins like mist; return to me, for I have redeemed you. ^44-22

**44:23** Sing, O heavens, for the LORD has done it; shout, O depths of the earth; break forth into singing, O mountains, O forest and every tree in it! For the LORD has redeemed Jacob and will be glorified in Israel. ^44-23

**44:24** Thus says the LORD, your Redeemer, who formed you in the womb: I am the LORD, who made all things, who alone stretched out the heavens, who by myself spread out the earth; ^44-24

**44:25** who frustrates the omens of soothsayers and makes fools of diviners; who turns back the wise and makes their knowledge foolish; ^44-25

**44:26** who confirms the word of his servant and fulfills the prediction of his messengers; who says of Jerusalem, “It shall be inhabited,” and of the cities of Judah, “They shall be rebuilt, and I will raise up their ruins”; ^44-26

**44:27** who says to the deep, “Be dry—I will dry up your rivers”; ^44-27

**44:28** who says of Cyrus, “He is my shepherd, and he shall carry out all my purpose”; and who says of Jerusalem, “It shall be rebuilt,” and of the temple, “Your foundation shall be laid.” ^44-28
