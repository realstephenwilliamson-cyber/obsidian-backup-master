## Mūla

शिवं व्रजेदित्यर्थोऽत्र पूर्वापरविवेचनात् ।
व्याख्यातः श्रीमतास्माकं गुरुणा शम्भुमूर्तिना ॥ ^mula

śivaṃ vrajedityartho'tra pūrvāparavivecanāt |
vyākhyātaḥ śrīmatāsmākaṃ guruṇā śambhumūrtinā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 10 - Chapter Nineteen#^pg220|p. 200]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=220>))
>
> When death (dehapāta) is immanent, having attained Śiva’s initiation (śāṇkarī dīkṣā), by the flashing forth of a descent of the power (of race), (immediately after) the moment (he receives) initiation, he attains Śiva. The matter (artha), discerned from beginning to end, and so (taught) here, has been explained to us by (our) teacher, who is a veritable incarnation of Śambhu. (with 19.2–3)

## Viveka

> शाङ्करीं दीक्षां तस्माद्दीक्षाक्षणात्परम् ॥

> व्याख्यातः श्रीमतास्माकं गुरुणा शम्भुमूर्तिना ॥

समीपस्थ इति द्वित्र्यादिक्षणभाविनि ॥ ३ ॥
