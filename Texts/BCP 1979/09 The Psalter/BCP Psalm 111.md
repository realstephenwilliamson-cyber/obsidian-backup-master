# Psalm 111

← [[BCP Psalm 110]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 112]] →

*Confitebor tibi* · Book Five · Twenty-third Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 111]]

**111:1** Hallelujah!  
I will give thanks to the LORD with my whole heart, \*  
in the assembly of the upright, in the congregation. ^111-1

**111:2** Great are the deeds of the LORD! \*  
they are studied by all who delight in them. ^111-2

**111:3** His work is full of majesty and splendor, \*  
and his righteousness endures for ever. ^111-3

**111:4** He makes his marvelous works to be remembered; \*  
the LORD is gracious and full of compassion. ^111-4

**111:5** He gives food to those who fear him; \*  
he is ever mindful of his covenant. ^111-5

**111:6** He has shown his people the power of his works \*  
in giving them the lands of the nations. ^111-6

**111:7** The works of his hands are faithfulness and justice; \*  
all his commandments are sure. ^111-7

**111:8** They stand fast for ever and ever, \*  
because they are done in truth and equity. ^111-8

**111:9** He sent redemption to his people;  
he commanded his covenant for ever; \*  
holy and awesome is his Name. ^111-9

[p. 755]

**111:10** The fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom; \*  
those who act accordingly have a good understanding;  
his praise endures for ever. ^111-10
