## Mūla

पृथगासनपूजायां क्रमान्मन्त्रा इमे स्मृताः ।
संक्षेपपूजने तु प्रागाद्यमन्त्यं च बीजकम् ॥ ^mula

pṛthagāsanapūjāyāṃ kramānmantrā ime smṛtāḥ |
saṃkṣepapūjane tu prāgādyamantyaṃ ca bījakam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 02 - Chapter Thirty, part 1#^pg24|p. 6]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=24>))
>
> KṢMAM is in the Ghost (i.e., Sadāśiva). One should imagine that JRAM is on the prongs of the Trident. These are said to be, one by one in due order, the Mantras (required) for the worship of the Throne. (with 30.8cd–9ab)
>
> In the abbreviated rite of worship, (the parts of the Trident) beginning with the power of the foundation and ending with the prongs of the Trident can be worshipped (all together,) assuming the first and the last seed syllable Mantra. (with 30.9cd–10ab)
