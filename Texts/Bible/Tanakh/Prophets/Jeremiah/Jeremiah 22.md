# Jeremiah 22

Compare: [[NRSVue Jeremiah 22|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 22

**22:1** Thus said the LORD: Go down to the palace of the king of Judah, where you shall utter this word. ^22-1

**22:2** Say: “Hear the word of the LORD: O king of Judah, you who sit on the throne of David, and your courtiers and your subjects who enter these gates! ^22-2

**22:3** Thus said the LORD: Do what is just and right; rescue from the defrauder him who is robbed; do not wrong the stranger, the fatherless, and the widow; commit no lawless act, and do not shed the blood of the innocent in this place. ^22-3

**22:4** For if you fulfill this command, then through the gates of this palace shall enter kings of David’s line who sit upon his throne, riding horse-drawn chariots, with their courtiers and their subjects. ^22-4

**22:5** But if you do not heed these commands, I swear by Myself—declares the LORD—that this palace shall become a ruin.” ^22-5

**22:6** For thus said the LORD concerning the royal palace of Judah:  
You are as Gilead to Me,  
As the summit of Lebanon;  
But I will make you a desert,  
Uninhabited towns. ^22-6

**22:7** I will appoint destroyers against you,  
Each with his tools;  
They shall cut down your choicest cedars  
And make them fall into the fire. ^22-7

**22:8** And when many nations pass by this city and one man asks another, “Why did the LORD do thus to that great city?” ^22-8

**22:9** the reply will be, “Because they forsook the covenant with the LORD their God and bowed down to other gods and served them.” ^22-9

**22:10** Do not weep for the dead[^1]  
And do not lament for him;  
Weep rather for him who is leaving,[^2]  
For he shall never come back  
To see the land of his birth! ^22-10

**22:11** For thus said the LORD concerning Shallum[^3] son of King Josiah of Judah, who succeeded his father Josiah as king, but who has gone forth from this place: He shall never come back. ^22-11

**22:12** He shall die in the place to which he was exiled, and he shall not see this land again. ^22-12

**22:13** Ha! he who builds his house with unfairness  
And his upper chambers with injustice,  
Who makes his fellow man work without pay  
And does not give him his wages, ^22-13

**22:14** Who thinks: I will build me a vast palace  
With spacious upper chambers,  
Provided with windows,  
Paneled in cedar,  
Painted with vermilion! ^22-14

**22:15** Do you think you are more a king  
Because you compete in cedar?  
Your father ate and drank[^4]  
And dispensed justice and equity—  
Then all went well with him. ^22-15

**22:16** He upheld the rights of the poor and needy—  
Then all was well.  
That is truly heeding Me[^5]  
—declares the LORD. ^22-16

**22:17** But your eyes and your mind are only  
On ill-gotten gains,  
On shedding the blood of the innocent,  
On committing fraud and violence. ^22-17

**22:18** Assuredly, thus said the LORD concerning Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah:  
They shall not mourn for him,  
“Ah, brother! Ah, sister!”  
They shall not mourn for him,  
“Ah, lord! Ah, his majesty!”[^6] ^22-18

**22:19** He shall have the burial of an ass,  
Dragged out and left lying  
Outside the gates of Jerusalem. ^22-19

**22:20** [^7]Climb Lebanon and cry out,  
Raise your voice in Bashan,  
Cry out from Abarim,  
For all your lovers are crushed. ^22-20

**22:21** I spoke to you when you were prosperous;  
You said, “I will not listen.”  
That was your way ever since your youth,  
You would not heed Me. ^22-21

**22:22** All your shepherds[^8] shall be devoured by the wind,  
And your lovers shall go into captivity.  
Then you shall be shamed and humiliated  
Because of all your depravity. ^22-22

**22:23** You who dwell in Lebanon,  
Nestled among the cedars,  
How much grace will you have[^9]  
When pains come upon you,  
Travail as in childbirth! ^22-23

**22:24** As I live—declares the LORD—if you, O King Coniah, son of Jehoiakim, of Judah, were[^10] a signet on my right hand, I would tear you off even from there. ^22-24

**22:25** I will deliver you into the hands of those who seek your life, into the hands of those you dread, into the hands of King Nebuchadrezzar of Babylon and into the hands of the Chaldeans. ^22-25

**22:26** I will hurl you and the mother who bore you into another land, where you were not born; there you shall both die. ^22-26

**22:27** They shall not return to the land that they yearn to come back to. ^22-27

**22:28** Is this man Coniah  
A wretched broken pot,  
A vessel no one wants?  
Why are he and his offspring hurled out,  
And cast away in a land they knew not? ^22-28

**22:29** O land, land, land,  
Hear the word of the LORD! ^22-29

**22:30** Thus said the LORD:  
Record this man as without succession,  
One who shall never be found acceptable;  
For no man of his offspring shall be accepted  
To sit on the throne of David  
And to rule again in Judah. ^22-30

[^1]: I.e., Josiah; see 2 Kings 23.29–30.
[^2]: I.e., the king called by his throne name Jehoahaz in 2 Kings 23.31 ff., and by his private name Shallum here in v. 11 and in 1 Chron. 3.15.
[^3]: I.e., the king called by his throne name Jehoahaz in 2 Kings 23.31 ff., and by his private name Shallum here in v. 11 and in 1 Chron. 3.15.
[^4]: I.e., he was content with the simple necessities of life.
[^5]: Or “That is the reward for heeding Me.”
[^6]: They shall express neither sorrow at the loss of a relative nor grief at the death of a ruler.
[^7]: Israel is addressed.
[^8]: Change of vocalization yields “paramours.”
[^9]: Septuagint reads “How you will groan.”
[^10]: Heb. “If Coniah…were…”; Coniah (Jeconiah in 24.1) is identical with Jehoiachin, 2 Kings 24.8 ff.
