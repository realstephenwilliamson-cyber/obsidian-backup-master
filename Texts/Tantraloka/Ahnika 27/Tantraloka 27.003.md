## Mūla

स्ववीर्यानन्दमाहात्म्यप्रवेशवशशालिनीम् ।
ये सिद्धिं ददते तेषां बाह्यत्वं रूपविच्युतिः ॥ ^mula

svavīryānandamāhātmyapraveśavaśaśālinīm |
ye siddhiṃ dadate teṣāṃ bāhyatvaṃ rūpavicyutiḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 18 - Chapter Twenty-Seven#^pg420|p. 400]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=420>))
>
> (Such Mantras) bestow the accomplishment (siddhi) endowed with the penetration (āveśa) which is entry into the glorious power (māhātmya) of the bliss of their own (innate) vitality. Their external state (i.e. uttered aloud as public Mantras that are not kept secret) amounts to a fall from (their essential) nature.
