## Mūla

तथा हि भासते यत्तन्नीलमन्तः प्रवेदने ।
सङ्कल्परूपे बाह्यस्य तदधिष्ठातृ बोधकम् ॥ ^mula

tathā hi bhāsate yattannīlamantaḥ pravedane |
saṅkalparūpe bāhyasya tadadhiṣṭhātṛ bodhakam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 13 - Chapter Ten, part 4#^pg462|p. 443]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=462>))
>
> That (imagined) colour blue that manifests in that way within one’s (own) awareness, which is the conscious intent (saṁkalparūpa) (to imagine it), is the sustaining noetic consciousness (adhiṣṭhātṛ bodhaka) of the (supposed) outer (blue). However, (the state in which) the blue that manifests externally (outside the perceiver) has no sustaining subjectivity of any kind is said be the waking state. (with 10.234–235)
