# Proverbs 29

← [[Proverbs 28]] · [[Tanakh]] · [[Proverbs 30]] →

Compare: [[NRSVue Proverbs 29|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 29

**29:1** One oft reproved may become stiffnecked,  
But he will be suddenly broken beyond repair. ^29-1

**29:2** When the righteous become great the people rejoice,  
But when the wicked dominate the people groan. ^29-2

**29:3** A man who loves wisdom brings joy to his father,  
But he who keeps company with harlots will lose his wealth. ^29-3

**29:4** By justice a king sustains the land,  
But a fraudulent man tears it down. ^29-4

**29:5** A man who flatters his fellow  
Spreads a net for his feet. ^29-5

**29:6** An evil man’s offenses are a trap for himself,  
But the righteous sing out joyously. ^29-6

**29:7** A righteous man is concerned with the cause of the wretched;  
A wicked man cannot understand such concern. ^29-7

**29:8** Scoffers inflame a city,  
But the wise allay anger. ^29-8

**29:9** When a wise man enters into litigation with a fool  
There is ranting and ridicule, but no satisfaction. ^29-9

**29:10** Bloodthirsty men detest the blameless,  
But the upright seek them out. ^29-10

**29:11** A dullard vents all his rage,  
But a wise man calms it down. ^29-11

**29:12** A ruler who listens to lies,  
All his ministers will be wicked. ^29-12

**29:13** A poor man and a fraudulent man meet;  
The LORD gives luster to the eyes of both. ^29-13

**29:14** A king who judges the wretched honestly,  
His throne will be established forever. ^29-14

**29:15** Rod and reproof produce wisdom,  
But a lad out of control is a disgrace to his mother. ^29-15

**29:16** When the wicked increase, offenses increase,  
But the righteous will see their downfall. ^29-16

**29:17** Discipline your son and he will give you peace;  
He will gratify you with dainties. ^29-17

**29:18** For lack of vision a people lose restraint,  
But happy is he who heeds instruction. ^29-18

**29:19** A slave cannot be disciplined by words;  
Though he may comprehend, he does not respond. ^29-19

**29:20** If you see a man hasty in speech,  
There is more hope for a fool than for him. ^29-20

**29:21** A slave pampered from youth  
Will come to a bad end.[^1] ^29-21

**29:22** An angry man provokes a quarrel;  
A hot-tempered man commits many offenses. ^29-22

**29:23** A man’s pride will humiliate him,  
But a humble man will obtain honor. ^29-23

**29:24** He who shares with a thief is his own enemy;  
He hears the imprecation and does not tell.[^2] ^29-24

**29:25** A man’s fears become a trap for him,  
But he who trusts in the LORD shall be safeguarded. ^29-25

**29:26** Many seek audience with a ruler,  
But it is from the LORD that a man gets justice. ^29-26

**29:27** The unjust man is an abomination to the righteous,  
And he whose way is straight is an abomination to the wicked. ^29-27

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Cf. Lev. 5.1.
