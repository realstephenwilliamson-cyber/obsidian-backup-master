## Mūla

युज्यते सर्वतोदिक्कं स्वातन्त्र्योल्लासधामनि ।
स्वातन्त्र्यात्तु निजं रूपम् बोद्धृधर्मादविच्युतम् ॥ ^mula

yujyate sarvatodikkaṃ svātantryollāsadhāmani |
svātantryāttu nijaṃ rūpam boddhṛdharmādavicyutam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 02 - Chapter Eleven, part 1#^pg26|p. 6]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=26>))
>
> (Supreme Śiva) is the omnipresent abode of the exuberance of the creative freedom (of consciousness), and so no classification, whether a convention or a conception (kalanā), is applicable here (to Him). (with 11.9cd–10ab)
>
> We can clearly observe that even though (Śiva’s) own (essential) nature (rūpa) does not fall from (His) inherent nature as the (supreme and universal) subject (boddhrdharma), He freely (chooses) to become an object of our understanding. (He does this) so that instruction may be possible, in order to achieve (true and) ultimate absorption (in Him). It is because of this that it is said that the Śiva Principle (śivatattva) also is associated with a Force. (with 11.10cd–12ab)
