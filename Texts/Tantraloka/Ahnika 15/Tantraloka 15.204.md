## Mūla

ईशन्रघोरवामाख्यसद्योऽधोभेदतो दिशः ।
ईश ऊर्ध्वं प्रकाशत्वात्पूर्वं वक्त्रं प्रसारि यत् ॥ ^mula

īśanraghoravāmākhyasadyo'dhobhedato diśaḥ |
īśa ūrdhvaṃ prakāśatvātpūrvaṃ vaktraṃ prasāri yat || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 05 - Chapter Fifteen, part 4#^pg144|p. 126]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=144>))
>
> In due order (after the principles, Śiva and Śakti), Sadāśiva is the lord. His body consists of the Mantras of the five (Brahmās). This is because the directions are derived from the division (of these Five Brahmās), called Īśāna, Tatpuruṣa, Aghora, Vāmadeva, and Sadyojāta, (along with the) lower (face). (with 15.203cd–204ab)
>
> Īśāna is the zenith, as (He is) the Light (of consciousness). Tatpuruṣa is the face that spreads forth in front (pūrva, and so is the east – pūrva). Aghora (acaṇḍa) is the southern (direction). Vāmadeva, who is (cool and) pleasing (saumyaka), is the northern (vāmā) (quarter). As Sadyojāta faces away (from the Light or is on the other side of it), he is said to be the western (quarter) (paścimā) (which is behind – paścimā). As the face of the netherworlds (pātālavaktra) abides without light, it is the nadir. (with 15.204cd–206ab)
