Guruji in 2010. It gives a good sense of what it was like to 

chat with him when he was in full “guru mode.”

Guru: None, really. You were given to me by Shiva and Shakti. The Mother and I 

happen to be your trustees.

D: What is your role, then?

G: To help you mature from beast to human to divine being.

D: What is the purpose of that?

G: As far as I know, it is just a game. We are all playing roles in a drama.

D: So what if I decide to be a bad guy?

G: That is your choice. Who am I to say no?

D: You won’t correct me?

G: No.

D: If I am the creator, then the world should obey me. But it does not. I feel powerless to control it.

G: This is going to be a little long. Do you have patience to listen now? D: Yes, I’m willing.

G: Good. Then let’s begin with your three states of being—waking, dreaming and sleeping. When you’re awake, you can know and act; time flows continuously. When you’re dreaming, you can know but can’t act; time moves in jumps. When you’re asleep, you can’t know and you can’t act; time is also asleep. So you see, time is like space. Life is its property, like energy-matter. Time arranges things.
