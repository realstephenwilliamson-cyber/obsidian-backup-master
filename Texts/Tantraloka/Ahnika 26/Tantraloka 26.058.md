## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 17 - Chapter Twenty-Five (continued) and Chapter Twenty-Six#^pg411|p. 391]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=411>))
>
> The rite (kriyā), be it brief or extensive, is fashioned (parikalpitā) by the (attendant) instrumental factors (kāraka) that (should) bring joy to the heart. These may be external or imagined (sarrkalpaja), (and comprise), for example, the mental conjunction (anusandhāna) of locations (in the cosmic order) and time, qualities, the (sacrificial) substances, and (ritual) actions. (None of them serve) to specify the field (viṣaya) (in which, whatever it may be,) one who aspires to liberation (practices and accomplishes) the most excellent procedure (naihśreyasa-vidhi). (with 26.58–59)
