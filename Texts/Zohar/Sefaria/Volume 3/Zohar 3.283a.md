*Volume 3, folio 283a* · Ki Teitzei, Vayeilech

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Ki Teitzei 28:139–30:146.*

**Vayeilech 1:1** AND MOSES WENT AND SPAKE THESE WORDS UNTO ALL ISRAEL. R. Hizkiah cited here the verse: “That caused his glorious arm to go at the right hand of Moses, that divided the water before them” (Isa. 63, 12). ‘Happy’, he said, ‘are Israel in that God chose them, and because He chose them He called them sons, firstborn, holy, brothers, and came down to dwell among them and sought to establish them after the supernal pattern, and spread over them seven clouds of glory. ^vayeilech-1-1
