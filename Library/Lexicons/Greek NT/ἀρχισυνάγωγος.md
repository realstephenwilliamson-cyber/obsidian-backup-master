# ἀρχισυνάγωγος

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · noun

**Dodson:** a leader of the synagogue — a leader of the synagogue, a leader connected with the synagogue: sometimes there was only one, and the name was in some cases merely honorary. (Strong's 752; headword ἀρχισυνάγωγος, ου, ὁ)
