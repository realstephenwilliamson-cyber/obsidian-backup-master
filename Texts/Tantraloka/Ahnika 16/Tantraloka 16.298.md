## Mūla

पुत्रको वा न तावान्स्यादपि तु स्वबलोचितः ।
यस्तु विज्ञानयोगादिवन्ध्यः सोऽन्धो यथा पथि ॥ ^mula

putrako vā na tāvānsyādapi tu svabalocitaḥ |
yastu vijñānayogādivandhyaḥ so'ndho yathā pathi || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 07 - Chapter Sixteen, part 3#^pg153|p. 133]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=153>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 16.297cd–298ab.
>
> Thus, he who has the strength (in himself to sustain) insight (vijñāna), Yoga and the like, whether he is initially a regular (initiate) or a spiritual son, he should not (limit himself) to being this only, but rather to what is consonant with (his) own (inherent spiritual) strength. (with 16.297cd–298ab)
>
> However, he who (on the contrary) is devoid of insight, Yoga and the like, is like a blind man (walking) on the road; he depends completely on the teacher, for (both) worldly benefits and liberation. (with 16.298cd–299ab)
