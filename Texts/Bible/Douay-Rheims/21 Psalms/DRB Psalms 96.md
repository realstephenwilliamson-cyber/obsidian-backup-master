# Psalms 96 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 95|← Psalms 95]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 97|Psalms 97 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 96|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**96:1** For the same David, when his land was restored again to him. The Lord hath reigned, let the earth rejoice: let many islands be glad. ^96-1

**96:2** Clouds and darkness are round about him: justice and judgment are the establishment of his throne. ^96-2

**96:3** A fire shall go before him, and shall burn his enemies round about. ^96-3

**96:4** His lightnings have shone forth to the world: the earth saw and trembled. ^96-4

**96:5** The mountains melted like wax, at the presence of the Lord: at the presence of the Lord of all the earth. ^96-5

**96:6** The heavens declared his justice: and all people saw his glory. ^96-6

**96:7** Let them be all confounded that adore graven things, and that glory in their idols. Adore him, all you his angels: ^96-7

**96:8** Sion heard, and was glad. And the daughters of Juda rejoiced, because of thy judgments, O Lord. ^96-8

**96:9** For thou art the most high Lord over all the earth: thou art exalted exceedingly above all gods. ^96-9

**96:10** You that love the Lord, hate evil: the Lord preserveth the souls of his saints, he will deliver them out of the hand of the sinner. ^96-10

**96:11** Light is risen to the just, and joy to the right of heart. ^96-11

**96:12** Rejoice, ye just, in the Lord: and give praise to the remembrance of his holiness. ^96-12
