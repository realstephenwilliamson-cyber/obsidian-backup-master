# Torah (JPS)

The Five Books of Moses in the JPS 1985 translation, one note per weekly parasha (Sefaria's 54 divisions). Each verse carries a block ID: `[[Torah 01 - Bereshit#^1-1]]` opens Genesis 1:1. Rashi's commentary: [[Rashi (Metsudah)]]. The [[Torah View]] lays out a passage like a page of a Mikraot Gedolot, with Rashi and the texts that cite it.
