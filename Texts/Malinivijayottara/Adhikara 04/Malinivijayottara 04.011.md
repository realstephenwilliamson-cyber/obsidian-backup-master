## Mūla

भिन्नयोनिस्तु या देव त्वयोक्ता मालिनी मम ।
तस्या अङ्गे यथैतानि संस्थितानि तथा वद ॥ ^mula

bhinnayonis tu yā deva tvayoktā mālinī mama |
tasyā aṅge yathaitāni saṁsthitāni tathā vada || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, April 2023 – June 2023
> p. 17 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=58>))
>
> “(I have) properly understood (how) the principles (*tattva*), world-orders, forces, parts (of mantras) and mantras are within (Mātṛkā, that is) the body of Mālinī, in which the vowels and consonants are not mixed (*abhinna*). O god, (now) tell (me) how they are present within the body of Mālinī you have taught me whose vowels and consonants are mixed (*bhinnayoni*).” (with 4.10–11)
