# Psalm 147 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 146|← Psalm 146]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 148|Psalm 148 →]]

## Praise for God’s care for Jerusalem

**147:1** Praise the LORD! How good it is to sing praises to our God, for he is gracious, and a song of praise is fitting. ^147-1

**147:2** The LORD builds up Jerusalem; he gathers the outcasts of Israel. ^147-2

**147:3** He heals the brokenhearted and binds up their wounds. ^147-3

**147:4** He determines the number of the stars; he gives to all of them their names. ^147-4

**147:5** Great is our Lord and abundant in power; his understanding is beyond measure. ^147-5

**147:6** The LORD lifts up the downtrodden; he casts the wicked to the ground. ^147-6

**147:7** Sing to the LORD with thanksgiving; make melody to our God on the lyre. ^147-7

**147:8** He covers the heavens with clouds, prepares rain for the earth, makes grass grow on the hills. ^147-8

**147:9** He gives to the animals their food and to the young ravens when they cry. ^147-9

**147:10** His delight is not in the strength of the horse nor his pleasure in the speed of a runner,^[Heb *legs of a person*] ^147-10

**147:11** but the LORD takes pleasure in those who fear him, in those who hope in his steadfast love. ^147-11

**147:12** Extol the LORD, O Jerusalem! Praise your God, O Zion! ^147-12

**147:13** For he strengthens the bars of your gates; he blesses your children within you. ^147-13

**147:14** He grants peace^[Or *prosperity*] within your borders; he fills you with the finest of wheat. ^147-14

**147:15** He sends out his command to the earth; his word runs swiftly. ^147-15

**147:16** He gives snow like wool; he scatters frost like ashes. ^147-16

**147:17** He hurls down hail like crumbs—who can stand before his cold? ^147-17

**147:18** He sends out his word and melts them; he makes his wind blow, and the waters flow. ^147-18

**147:19** He declares his word to Jacob, his statutes and ordinances to Israel. ^147-19

**147:20** He has not dealt thus with any other nation; they do not know his ordinances. Praise the LORD! ^147-20
