# Job 12

## Chapter 12

**12:1** Then Job said in reply: ^12-1

**12:2** Indeed, you are the [voice of] the people,  
And wisdom will die with you. ^12-2

**12:3** But I, like you, have a mind,  
And am not less than you.  
Who does not know such things? ^12-3

**12:4** I have become a laughingstock to my friend—  
“One who calls to God and is answered,  
Blamelessly innocent”—a laughingstock. ^12-4

**12:5** [^1]In the thought of the complacent there is contempt for calamity;  
It is ready for those whose foot slips.*-*[^2] ^12-5

**12:6** Robbers live untroubled in their tents,  
And those who provoke God are secure,  
Those whom God’s hands have produced.[^3] ^12-6

**12:7** But ask the beasts, and they will teach you;  
The birds of the sky, they will tell you, ^12-7

**12:8** Or speak to the earth, it will teach you;  
The fish of the sea, they will inform you. ^12-8

**12:9** Who among all these does not know  
That the hand of the LORD has done this? ^12-9

**12:10** In His hand is every living soul  
And the breath of all mankind. ^12-10

**12:11** Truly, the ear tests arguments  
As the palate tastes foods. ^12-11

**12:12** Is wisdom in the aged  
And understanding in the long-lived? ^12-12

**12:13** With Him are wisdom and courage;  
His are counsel and understanding. ^12-13

**12:14** Whatever He tears down cannot be rebuilt;  
Whomever He imprisons cannot be set free. ^12-14

**12:15** When He holds back the waters, they dry up;  
When He lets them loose, they tear up the land. ^12-15

**12:16** With Him are strength and resourcefulness;  
Erring and causing to err are from Him. ^12-16

**12:17** He makes counselors go about naked[^4]  
And causes judges to go mad. ^12-17

**12:18** He undoes the belts of kings,  
And fastens loincloths on them. ^12-18

**12:19** He makes priests go about naked,[^5]  
And leads temple-servants[^6] astray. ^12-19

**12:20** He deprives trusty men of speech,  
And takes away the reason of elders. ^12-20

**12:21** He pours disgrace upon great men,  
And loosens the belt of the mighty. ^12-21

**12:22** He draws mysteries out of the darkness,  
And brings obscurities to light. ^12-22

**12:23** He exalts nations, then destroys them;  
He expands nations, then leads them away. ^12-23

**12:24** He deranges the leaders of the people,  
And makes them wander in a trackless waste. ^12-24

**12:25** They grope without light in the darkness;  
He makes them wander as if drunk. ^12-25

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^4]: A sign of madness.
[^5]: A sign of madness.
[^6]: Cf. Ugaritic ytnm, a class of temple servants; others “the mighty.”
