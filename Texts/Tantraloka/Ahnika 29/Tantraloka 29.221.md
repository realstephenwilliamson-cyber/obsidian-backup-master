## Mūla

जाग्रदादिषु संवित्तिर्यथा स्यादनपायिनी ।
कुलयागस्तथादेश्यो योगिनीमुखसंस्थितः ॥ ^mula

jāgradādiṣu saṃvittiryathā syādanapāyinī |
kulayāgastathādeśyo yoginīmukhasaṃsthitaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X

>
> The Kula sacrifice (is taught orally). It is in the mouth of the Yogini. It should be taught (to the initiate) in such a way that consciousness may (be realised to abide,) without waning away, in (all) the states of waking and the rest.
