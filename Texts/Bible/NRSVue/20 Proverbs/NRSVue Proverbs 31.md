# Proverbs 31 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Proverbs 30|← Proverbs 30]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · —

## The teaching of king Lemuel’s mother

**31:1** The words of King Lemuel. An oracle that his mother taught him: ^31-1

**31:2** No, my son! No, son of my womb! No, son of my vows! ^31-2

**31:3** Do not give your strength to women, your ways to those who destroy kings. ^31-3

**31:4** It is not for kings, O Lemuel, it is not for kings to drink wine or for rulers to desire^[Cn: Heb *where*] strong drink, ^31-4

**31:5** lest they drink and forget what has been decreed and pervert the rights of all the afflicted. ^31-5

**31:6** Give strong drink to one who is perishing and wine to those in bitter distress; ^31-6

**31:7** let them drink and forget their poverty and remember their misery no more. ^31-7

**31:8** Speak out for those who cannot speak, for the rights of all the destitute.^[Heb *all children of passing away*] ^31-8

**31:9** Speak out; judge righteously; defend the rights of the poor and needy. ^31-9

## Ode to a woman of strength

**31:10** A woman of strength who can find? She is far more precious than jewels. ^31-10

**31:11** The heart of her husband trusts in her, and he will have no lack of gain. ^31-11

**31:12** She does him good and not harm all the days of her life. ^31-12

**31:13** She seeks wool and flax and works with willing hands. ^31-13

**31:14** She is like the ships of the merchant; she brings her food from far away. ^31-14

**31:15** She rises while it is still night and provides food for her household and tasks for her female servants. ^31-15

**31:16** She considers a field and buys it; with the fruit of her hands she plants a vineyard. ^31-16

**31:17** She girds herself with strength and makes her arms strong. ^31-17

**31:18** She perceives that her merchandise is profitable. Her lamp does not go out at night. ^31-18

**31:19** She puts her hands to the distaff, and her hands hold the spindle. ^31-19

**31:20** She opens her hand to the poor and reaches out her hands to the needy. ^31-20

**31:21** She is not afraid for her household when it snows, for all her household are clothed in crimson. ^31-21

**31:22** She makes herself coverings; her clothing is fine linen and purple. ^31-22

**31:23** Her husband is known in the city gates, taking his seat among the elders of the land. ^31-23

**31:24** She makes linen garments and sells them; she supplies the merchant with sashes. ^31-24

**31:25** Strength and dignity are her clothing, and she laughs at the time to come. ^31-25

**31:26** She opens her mouth with wisdom, and the teaching of kindness is on her tongue. ^31-26

**31:27** She looks well to the ways of her household and does not eat the bread of idleness. ^31-27

**31:28** Her children rise up and call her happy; her husband, too, and he praises her: ^31-28

**31:29** “Many women have done excellently, but you surpass them all.” ^31-29

**31:30** Charm is deceitful and beauty is vain, but a woman who fears the LORD is to be praised. ^31-30

**31:31** Give her a share in the fruit of her hands, and let her works praise her in the city gates. ^31-31
