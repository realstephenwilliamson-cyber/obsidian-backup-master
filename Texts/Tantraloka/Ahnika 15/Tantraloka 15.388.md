## Mūla

त्रिकं यामलतैक्याभ्यामेकं वा मन्त्रदैवतम् ।
अग्निकार्यविधानाय ततः कुण्डं प्रकल्पयेत् ॥ ^mula

trikaṃ yāmalataikyābhyāmekaṃ vā mantradaivatam |
agnikāryavidhānāya tataḥ kuṇḍaṃ prakalpayet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 07 - Chapter Fifteen, part 6#^pg257|p. 239]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=257>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 15.390cd–391ab.
>
> The group of Mantras, strengthened by union (samāpatti) with the liquid in the (big) jar, and contemplated as being essentially just fire alone, nourishes (and fulfils) all the ritual procedure. Thus, the teacher should worship the entire host of Mantras in the (big) jar by means of the procedure (of the six-fold deposition described) previously, and the Weapon that removes (all) obstacles in the (small) jar. Then in the middle of the building, in a mandala made of scent (or unguent) (gandhamandala), the teacher should worship the triad (of deities) (trika), united as couples or single, or else just a single Mantra deity. (with 15.385cd–388ab)
>
> Next, in order to perform the fire sacrifice (agnikārya), (the sacrificer) should prepare the pit (kuṇḍa). (This is) not the usual (hearth for daily use) (apūrva); (rather,) it is generated from the intention (to perform the rite and attain its fruit,) and the (inner) discourse (saṁjalpa) of pure Mantras etc. (with 15.388cd–389ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* इदानीं वह्निकार्यमभिधातुमुपक्रमते--

> अग्निकार्यविधानाय ततः कुण्डं प्रकल्पयेत् ॥
> शुद्धमन्त्रादिसञ्जल्पसङ्कल्पोत्थमपूर्वकम् ।

एवं मण्डलपूजानन्तरमग्निकर्म विधातुमद्वयमयतया शुद्धस्य अत एव मन्त्रादिरूपस्य

"क्रिया कुण्डलिनी कुण्डं...................... ।"

इत्याद्यनुसंध्यात्मनः सञ्जल्पस्य प्ररोहात्मकात् सङ्कल्पादुत्थितम् । अत एव अलौकिकत्वादपूर्वं कुण्डं प्रकल्पयेत् बहिरेवमनुसन्दध्यात्-- इत्यर्थः ॥
