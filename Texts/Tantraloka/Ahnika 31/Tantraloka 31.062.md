## Mūla

वेदाश्रिते त्रिहस्ते प्राक् पूर्वमर्धं विभाजयेत् ।
हस्तार्धं सर्वतस्त्यक्त्वा पूर्वोदग्याम्यदिग्गतम् ॥ ^mula

vedāśrite trihaste prāk pūrvamardhaṃ vibhājayet |
hastārdhaṃ sarvatastyaktvā pūrvodagyāmyadiggatam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 04 - Chapter Thirty-One, part 1#^pg113|p. 95]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=113>))
>
> “One should first of all divide the front half within a square (enclosure) (measuring) three handspans. Having left half a handspan on all the sides” to the east, north and south, (the remaining space is divided) by sixteen squares of three finger-breadths above (i.e horizontally) and laterally (i.e. vertically), having omitted two squares each on the southern and northern sides. (with 31.62–63)
