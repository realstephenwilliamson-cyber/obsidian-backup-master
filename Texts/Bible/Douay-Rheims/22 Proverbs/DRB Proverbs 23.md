# Proverbs 23 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Proverbs 22|← Proverbs 22]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Proverbs 24|Proverbs 24 →]]

**23:1** When thou shalt sit to eat with a prince, consider diligently what is set before thy face. ^23-1

**23:2** And put a knife to thy throat, if it be so that thou have thy soul in thy own power. ^23-2

**23:3** Be not desirous of his meats, in which is the bread of deceit. ^23-3

**23:4** Labour not to be rich: but set bounds to thy prudence. ^23-4

**23:5** Lift not up thy eyes to riches which thou canst not have: because they shall make themselves wings like those of an eagle, and shall fly towards heaven. ^23-5

**23:6** Eat not with an envious man, and desire not his meats: ^23-6

**23:7** Because like a soothsayer, and diviner, he thinketh that which he knoweth not. Eat and drink, will he say to thee: and his mind is not with thee. ^23-7

**23:8** The meats which thou hadst eaten, thou shalt vomit up: and shalt loose thy beautiful words. ^23-8

**23:9** Speak not in the ears of fools: because they will despise the instruction of thy speech. ^23-9

**23:10** Touch not the bounds of little ones: and enter not into the field of the fatherless: ^23-10

**23:11** For their near kinsman is strong: and he will judge their cause against thee. ^23-11

**23:12** Let thy heart apply itself to instruction: and thy ears to words of knowledge. ^23-12

**23:13** Withhold not correction from a child: for if thou strike him with the rod, he shall not die. ^23-13

**23:14** Thou shalt beat him with the rod, and deliver his soul from hell. ^23-14

**23:15** My son, if thy mind be wise, my heart shall rejoice with thee: ^23-15

**23:16** And my reins shall rejoice, when thy lips shall speak what is right. ^23-16

**23:17** Let not thy heart envy sinners: but be thou in the fear of the Lord all the day long: ^23-17

**23:18** Because thou shalt have hope in the latter end, and thy expectation shall not be taken away. ^23-18

**23:19** Hear thou, my son, and be wise: and guide thy mind in the way. ^23-19

**23:20** Be not in the feasts of great drinkers, nor in their revellings, who contribute flesh to eat: ^23-20

**23:21** Because they that give themselves to drinking, and that club together shall be consumed; and drowsiness shall be clothed with rags. ^23-21

**23:22** Hearken to thy father, that beget thee: and despise not thy mother when she is old. ^23-22

**23:23** Buy truth, and do not sell wisdom, and instruction, and understanding. ^23-23

**23:24** The father of the just rejoiceth greatly: he that hath begotten a wise son, shall have joy in him. ^23-24

**23:25** Let thy father, and thy mother be joyful, and let her rejoice that bore thee. ^23-25

**23:26** My son, give me thy heart: and let thy eyes keep my ways. ^23-26

**23:27** For a harlot is a deep ditch: and a strange woman is a narrow pit. ^23-27

**23:28** She lieth in wait in the way as a robber, and him whom she shall see unwary, she will kill. ^23-28

**23:29** Who hath woe? whose father hath woe? who hath contentions? who falls into pits? who hath wounds without cause? who hath redness of eyes? ^23-29

**23:30** Surely they that pass their time in wine, and study to drink of their cups. ^23-30

**23:31** Look not upon the wine when it is yellow, when the colour thereof shineth in the glass: it goeth in pleasantly, ^23-31

**23:32** But in the end, it will bite like a snake, and will spread abroad poison like a basilisk. ^23-32

**23:33** Thy eyes shall behold strange women, and thy heart shall utter perverse things. ^23-33

**23:34** And thou shalt be as one sleeping in the midst of the sea, and as a pilot fast asleep, when the stern is lost. ^23-34

**23:35** And thou shalt say: They have beaten me, but I was not sensible of pain: they drew me, and I felt not: when shall I awake, and find wine again? ^23-35
