## Mūla

तेनात्महृदयानीतं प्राक्कृत्वा पुद्गलं ततः ।
मायायां तद्धरातत्त्वशरीराण्यस्य संसृजेत् ॥ ^mula

tenātmahṛdayānītaṃ prākkṛtvā pudgalaṃ tataḥ |
māyāyāṃ taddharātattvaśarīrāṇyasya saṃsṛjet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 08 - Chapter Seventeen#^pg178|p. 158]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=178>))
>
> (Now the disciple has reached the stage in which) he is fit to be born everywhere (in all the wombs). (This is so for three reasons. One is) because he has moved away from the place where his fetters (are located); (secondly,) because he has attained an independent place (where he is free) (svatantrasthāna), and (thirdly,) because he is not subject (anymore) to his own karma. Thus, (because these conditions obtain,) having initially brought the (disciple’s) Self into his own heart, (the teacher) should (now) generate within Māyā the bodies (of the disciple) that pertain to the Earth principle. (with 17.34–35)
