## Mūla

शिशुरपि तदभेददृशा भक्तिबलाच्चाभ्युपैति शिवभावम् ।
यद्यपि विकल्पवृत्तेरपि मोक्षं दीक्षयैव देहान्ते ॥ ^mula

śiśurapi tadabhedadṛśā bhaktibalāccābhyupaiti śivabhāvam |
yadyapi vikalpavṛtterapi mokṣaṃ dīkṣayaiva dehānte || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 06 - Chapter Sixteen, part 2#^pg105|p. 85]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=105>))
>
> The disciple also, seeing that the teacher possesses (the capacity to bestow) the grace of such progressively (developing) Śivahood, attains Śiva’s state by (his) perception of oneness (with his teacher) and the strength of (his) devotion. (with 16.198cd–199ab)
>
> Even if (dualistic) thought (vikalpavrtti) (persists after the disciple has been initiated, he attains) liberation by that same initiation at the time of death. Although the pervasive Lord (Himself) has said this in the scriptures, (he should not be complacent); it behoves (yuktā) (him to cultivate and maintain) a firm intention (drdhavāsanā) (to realize his identity with Śiva). (with 16.199cd–200ab)

> चिद्वृत्तेर्वैचित्र्याच्चाञ्चल्येऽपि क्रमेण सन्धानात् ॥
> तस्मिंस्तस्मिन्वस्तुनि रूढिरवश्यं शिवात्मिका भवति ।
