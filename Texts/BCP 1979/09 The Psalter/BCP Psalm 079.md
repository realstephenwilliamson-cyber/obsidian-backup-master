# Psalm 79

← [[BCP Psalm 078]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 080]] →

*Deus, venerunt* · Book Three · Sixteenth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 079]]

**79:1** O God, the heathen have come into your inheritance;  
they have profaned your holy temple; \*  
they have made Jerusalem a heap of rubble. ^79-1

**79:2** They have given the bodies of your servants as food for the birds of the air, \*  
and the flesh of your faithful ones to the beasts of the field. ^79-2

**79:3** They have shed their blood like water on every side of Jerusalem, \*  
and there was no one to bury them. ^79-3

**79:4** We have become a reproach to our neighbors, \*  
an object of scorn and derision to those around us. ^79-4

**79:5** How long will you be angry, O LORD? \*  
will your fury blaze like fire for ever? ^79-5

**79:6** Pour out your wrath upon the heathen who have not known you \*  
and upon the kingdoms that have not called upon your Name. ^79-6

**79:7** For they have devoured Jacob \*  
and made his dwelling a ruin. ^79-7

**79:8** Remember not our past sins;  
let your compassion be swift to meet us; \*  
for we have been brought very low. ^79-8

**79:9** Help us, O God our Savior, for the glory of your Name; \*  
deliver us and forgive us our sins, for your Name's sake. ^79-9

[p. 702]

**79:10** Why should the heathen say, "Where is their God?" \*  
Let it be known among the heathen and in our sight  
that you avenge the shedding of your servants' blood. ^79-10

**79:11** Let the sorrowful sighing of the prisoners come before you, \*  
and by your great might spare those who are condemned to die. ^79-11

**79:12** May the revilings with which they reviled you, O Lord, \*  
return seven-fold into their bosoms. ^79-12

**79:13** For we are your people and the sheep of your pasture; \*  
we will give you thanks for ever  
and show forth your praise from age to age. ^79-13
