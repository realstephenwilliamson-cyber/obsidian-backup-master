# Psalms 53 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 52|← Psalms 52]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 54|Psalms 54 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 53|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**53:1** Unto the end, In verses, understanding for David. ^53-1

**53:2** When the men of Ziph had come and said to Saul: Is not David hidden with us? ^53-2

**53:3** Save me, O God, by thy name, and judge me in thy strength. ^53-3

**53:4** O God, hear my prayer: give ear to the words of my mouth. ^53-4

**53:5** For strangers have risen up against me; and the mighty have sought after my soul: and they have not set God before their eyes. ^53-5

**53:6** For behold God is my helper: and the Lord is the protector of my soul. ^53-6

**53:7** Turn back the evils upon my enemies; and cut them off in thy truth. ^53-7

**53:8** I will freely sacrifice to thee, and will give praise, O God, to thy name: because it is good: ^53-8

**53:9** For thou hast delivered me out of all trouble: and my eye hath looked down upon my enemies. ^53-9
