# D31d — endnote calls linked to their endnotes

Date: 15 Oct 2026. Local only. Output: `/home/claude/d31c2/endnotes/<book folder>/<exact note name>.md` for every note that changes (Ricoeur: 17 notes; Stiegler: 10 notes). Script: `/home/claude/d31c2/endnotes.py`; full log: `/home/claude/d31c2/endnotes_log.json`.

## What was done

- Every `<sup>N</sup>` call in the body becomes `[[<notes note>#^<id>|<superscript digits>]]` (Unicode superscripts, no `<sup>` around the link). Nothing else in the body changes.
- Every endnote paragraph gets a block id on its last line: Ricoeur `^n-C07-12` (chapter 7, note 12; `^n-ed-5` for the Editor's Introduction, whose notes form their own section), Stiegler `^n-83`. No endnote paragraph already carried a block id (page markers are lines of their own), so no second id was ever needed.
- A call is linked only when its number matches exactly one endnote of its chapter (Ricoeur) or of the book-wide sequence (Stiegler) **and** it is the next number in sequence; the rest are left as `<sup>` and listed below.
- `text_source` in every changed note gets `; endnote calls linked 15 Oct 2026`. Frontmatter is otherwise untouched.
- **One layout change, please review:** in both Notes sections the converter had run several endnotes together in one paragraph (`… p. 126. 49 Stiegler, *States of Shock*, p. 126.`). A block id can only mark a whole paragraph, so such paragraphs were split at the next note number — only where the number is exactly the next in sequence, is preceded by sentence punctuation and followed by a capital, an asterisk or a quotation mark. No words change. Ricoeur: 7 paragraphs split; Stiegler: 23. All listed at the end.
