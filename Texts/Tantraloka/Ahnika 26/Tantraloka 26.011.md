## Mūla

तत्रैषां शेषवृत्त्यर्थं नित्यनैमित्तिके ध्रुवे ।
काम्यवर्जं यतः कामाश्चित्राश्चित्राभ्युपायकाः ॥ ^mula

tatraiṣāṃ śeṣavṛttyarthaṃ nityanaimittike dhruve |
kāmyavarjaṃ yataḥ kāmāścitrāścitrābhyupāyakāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 17 - Chapter Twenty-Five (continued) and Chapter Twenty-Six#^pg394|p. 374]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=394>))
>
> There (in that context), for the purpose of the remaining (post-initiatory) observance (śeṣavṛtti), I will teach the regular (nitya) and occasional (naimittika) (rites,) omitting the optional (magical) (kāmya) (ones), for desires are (very) varied, and so too (equally) diverse are the means (to their attainment).
