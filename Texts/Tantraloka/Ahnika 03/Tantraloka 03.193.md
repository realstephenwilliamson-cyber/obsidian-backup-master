## Mūla

तत्त्रिकं परमेशस्य पूर्णा शक्तिः प्रगीयते ।

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. II
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 02 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v02 10 - Chapter Three, part 7#^pg327|p. 311]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 02.pdf#page=327>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 3.193cd–194ab.
>
> (The wise) say that that same group of three is Bhairava’s supreme glory. That Triad (Trika) is proclaimed to be the Supreme Lord’s power in all its fullness. (with 3.192cd–193ab)
>
> As everything (viśva) is encompassed by that (triad), when this is properly worshipped, He who is the possessor of all powers and free of limitation is (truly) worshipped. (with 3.193cd–194ab)

*Lead-in:* तदाह—

> तेनाक्षिप्तं यतो विश्वमतोऽस्मिन्समुपासिते ॥
> विश्वशक्ताववच्छेदवन्ध्ये जातमुपासनम् ।

यतस्तेन त्रिकेण सर्वमिदमाक्षिप्तम् अतोऽस्मिन्नेव -- स्वातन्त्र्यशक्तिमात्रपरमार्थे त्रिके समावेशशालिनः

"..........................शैवी मुखमिहोच्यते ।"

इत्याद्युक्त्या तद्द्वारेणैव अनवच्छिन्नस्वभावत्वात्पूर्णे शक्तिमद्रूपेऽपि अयत्नेनैव समावेशो जायते -- इति वाक्यार्थः ॥ १९३ ॥
