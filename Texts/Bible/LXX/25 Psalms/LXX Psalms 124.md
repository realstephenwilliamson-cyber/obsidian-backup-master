# LXX Psalms 124

← [[LXX Psalms 123]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 125]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 125]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 125]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**124:1** A Song of Degrees. They that trust in the Lord *shall be* as mount Sion: he that dwells in Jerusalem shall never be moved.  ^124-1

**124:2** The mountains are round about her, and *so* the Lord is round about his people, from henceforth and even for ever.  ^124-2

**124:3** For the Lord will not allow the rod of sinners to be upon the lot of the righteous; lest the righteous should stretch forth their hands to iniquity.  ^124-3

**124:4** Do good, O Lord, to them *that are* good, and to them *that are* upright in heart.  ^124-4

**124:5** But them that turn aside to crooked ways the Lord will lead away with the workers of iniquity: *but* peace *shall be* upon Israel.[^1]  ^124-5

[^1]: 124:5 *Or,* let peace be.
