## Chapter 8

**8:1** [Put] a ram’s horn to your mouth—  
Like an eagle[^1] over the House of the LORD;[^2]  
Because they have transgressed My covenant  
And been faithless to My teaching. ^8-1

**8:2** Israel cries out to Me,  
“O my God, we are devoted to You.”[^3] ^8-2

**8:3** Israel rejects what is good;  
An enemy shall pursue him.[^4] ^8-3

**8:4** They have made kings,  
But not with My sanction;  
They have made officers,  
But not of My choice.  
Of their silver and gold  
They have made themselves images,  
To their own undoing. ^8-4

**8:5** He rejects[^5] your calf, Samaria!  
I am furious with them!  
Will they never be capable of [^6]purity? ^8-5

**8:6** For it was Israel’s doing;  
It was only made by a joiner,  
It is not a god.  
No, the calf of Samaria shall be  
Reduced to splinters! ^8-6

**8:7** They sow wind,  
And they shall reap whirlwind—  
Standing stalks devoid of ears  
And yielding no flour.  
If they do yield any,  
Strangers shall devour it. ^8-7

**8:8** Israel is bewildered;[^7]  
They have now become among the nations  
Like an unwanted vessel, ^8-8

**8:9** [Like] a lonely wild ass.  
For they have gone up to Assyria,  
Ephraim has[^8] courted friendship. ^8-9

**8:10** And while they are courting among the nations,  
There I will hold them fast;[^9]  
And they shall begin to diminish in number  
From the burden of king [and] officers.[^10] ^8-10

**8:11** For Ephraim has multiplied altars—for guilt;  
His altars have redounded to his guilt: ^8-11

**8:12** The many teachings I wrote for him  
Have been treated as something alien. ^8-12

**8:13** When they present sacrifices to Me,[^11]  
It is but flesh for them to eat:  
The LORD has not accepted them.  
Behold, He remembers their iniquity,  
He will punish their sins:  
Back to Egypt with them! ^8-13

**8:14** Israel has ignored his Maker  
And built temples  
(And Judah has fortified many cities).  
So I will set fire to his cities,  
And it shall consume their fortresses. ^8-14

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Emendation yields “Israel.”
[^3]: See note g at 7.14.
[^4]: Emendation yields “They pursue delusion.”
[^5]: Emendation yields “I reject.”
[^6]: Emendation yields “understanding, / That House of Israel?”
[^7]: A play on words: The Heb. root bala‘, which means “bewildered” here (cf. Isa. 28.7), means “devour” in the preceding verse.
[^8]: Emendation yields “In Egypt they have.”
[^9]: Cf. 9.6; but meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^10]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^11]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
