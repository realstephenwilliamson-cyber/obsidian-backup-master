## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. II
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 02 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v02 09 - Chapter Three, part 6#^pg313|p. 297]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 02.pdf#page=313>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 3.173cd–174ab.
>
> Passion is the will, Poison is knowledge, and the goddess of action is the Stainless. Penetration (samāveśa) into this triad is said to be Śiva (the transcendent) and Bhairava (the immanent). (with 3.172cd–173ab)
>
> Our teachers have said that one should always establish (oneself) here (in the pervasive Principle of Poison). Once having entered into the Principle of Poison, no ghost, poison or (malevolent) planet (can harm one for whom) all that manifests by virtue of (his) meditation (bhāvanā) is ‘I alone’ (exist). (with 3.173cd–174)
