# LXX Proverbs 31

← [[LXX Proverbs 30]] · [[LXX]]

*Proverbs · [[LXX]]*

**31:10** Who shall find a virtuous woman? for such a one is more valuable than precious stones.  ^31-10

**31:11** The heart of her husband trusts in her: such a one shall stand in no need of fine spoils.  ^31-11

**31:12** For she employs all her living for her husband's good.  ^31-12

**31:13** Gathering wool and flax, she makes it serviceable with her hands.  ^31-13

**31:14** She is like a ship trading from a distance: so she procures her livelihood.  ^31-14

**31:15** And she rises by night, and gives food to her household, and *appointed* tasks to her maidens.  ^31-15

**31:16** She views a farm, and buys it: and with the fruit of her hands she plants a possession.  ^31-16

**31:17** She strongly girds her loins, and strengthens her arms for work.  ^31-17

**31:18** And she finds by experience that working is good; and her candle goes not out all night.  ^31-18

**31:19** She reaches forth her arms to needful *works*, and applies her hands to the spindle.  ^31-19

**31:20** And she opens her hands to the needy, and reaches out fruit to the poor.  ^31-20

**31:21** Her husband is not anxious about those at home when he tarries anywhere abroad: for all her household are clothed.[^1]  ^31-21

**31:22** She makes for her husband clothes of double texture, and garments for herself of fine linen and scarlet.[^2]  ^31-22

**31:23** And her husband becomes a distinguished *person* in the gates, when he sits in council with the old inhabitants of the land.  ^31-23

**31:24** She makes fine linens, and sells girdles to the Chananites: she opens her mouth heedfully and with propriety, and controls her tongue.  ^31-24

**31:25** She puts on strength and honour; and rejoices in the last days.  ^31-25

**31:26** The ways of her household are careful, and she eats not the bread of idleness.  ^31-26

**31:27** But she opens her mouth wisely, and according to law.  ^31-27

**31:28** And *her* kindness to them sets up her children for them, and they grow rich, and her husband praises her.  ^31-28

**31:29** Many daughters have obtained wealth, many have wrought valiantly; but thou hast exceeded, thou hast surpassed all.  ^31-29

**31:30** Charms are false, and woman's beauty is vain: for it is a wise woman that is blessed, and let her praise the fear of the Lord.  ^31-30

**31:31** Give her of the fruit of her lips; and let her husband be praised in the gates.  ^31-31

[^1]: 31:21 *Gr.* those with her.
[^2]: 31:22 Comp. *Heb.* and *A. V.*
