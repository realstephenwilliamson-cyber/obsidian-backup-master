## Mūla

सदोदितः स एवोक्तः परमं हृदयं महत् ।
हृदये स्वविमर्शोऽसौ द्राविताशेषविश्वकः ॥ ^mula

sadoditaḥ sa evoktaḥ paramaṃ hṛdayaṃ mahat |
hṛdaye svavimarśo'sau drāvitāśeṣaviśvakaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. III
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 03 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v03 09 - Chapter Four, part 8#^pg293|p. 280]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 03.pdf#page=293>))
>
> As (consciousness) is reflective awareness, its spontaneous and perpetually emergent (sadodita) resonance (dhvani) (of pure self-awareness, which is the supreme level of Speech,) is called the supreme, Great Heart (SAUHṆ). (with 4.181cd–182ab)
>
> The self-awareness (svavimarśa) (from which) all things have flowed forth (drāvita) (and dissolved away) within the Heart (of consciousness), present in the beginning and end of perception (bhāvagraha), is called, according to the (Spanda) teachings, the universal vibration of consciousness (sāmānyaspanda), which is the outpouring (uccalana) (of consciousness) within one’s own nature. (with 4.182cd–183)
