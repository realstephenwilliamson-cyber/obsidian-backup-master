## Chapter 17

**17:1** Elijah the Tishbite, an inhabitant of Gilead, said to Ahab, “As the LORD lives, the God of Israel whom I serve, there will be no dew or rain except at my bidding.” ^17-1

**17:2** The word of the LORD came to him: ^17-2

**17:3** “Leave this place; turn eastward and go into hiding by the Wadi Cherith, which is east of the Jordan. ^17-3

**17:4** You will drink from the wadi, and I have commanded the ravens to feed you there.” ^17-4

**17:5** He proceeded to do as the LORD had bidden: he went, and he stayed by the Wadi Cherith, which is east of the Jordan. ^17-5

**17:6** The ravens brought him bread and meat every morning and every evening, and he drank from the wadi. ^17-6

**17:7** After some time the wadi dried up, because there was no rain in the land. ^17-7

**17:8** And the word of the LORD came to him: ^17-8

**17:9** “Go at once to Zarephath of Sidon, and stay there; I have designated a widow there to feed you.” ^17-9

**17:10** So he went at once to Zarephath. When he came to the entrance of the town, a widow was there gathering wood. He called out to her, “Please bring me a little water in your pitcher, and let me drink.” ^17-10

**17:11** As she went to fetch it, he called out to her, “Please bring along a piece of bread for me.” ^17-11

**17:12** “As the LORD your God lives,” she replied, “I have nothing baked, nothing but a handful of flour in a jar and a little oil in a jug. I am just gathering a couple of sticks, so that I can go home and prepare it for me and my son; we shall eat it and then we shall die.” ^17-12

**17:13** “Don’t be afraid,” said Elijah to her. “Go and do as you have said; but first make me a small cake from what you have there, and bring it out to me; then make some for yourself and your son. ^17-13

**17:14** For thus said the LORD, the God of Israel: The jar of flour shall not give out and the jug of oil shall not fail until the day that the LORD sends rain upon the ground.” ^17-14

**17:15** She went and did as Elijah had spoken, and she and he and her household had food for a long time. ^17-15

**17:16** The jar of flour did not give out, nor did the jug of oil fail, just as the LORD had spoken through Elijah. ^17-16

**17:17** After a while, the son of the mistress of the house fell sick, and his illness grew worse, until he had no breath left in him. ^17-17

**17:18** She said to Elijah, “What harm have I done you, O man of God, that you should come here to recall my sin and cause the death of my son?” ^17-18

**17:19** “Give me the boy,” he said to her; and taking him from her arms, he carried him to the upper chamber where he was staying, and laid him down on his own bed. ^17-19

**17:20** He cried out to the LORD and said, “O LORD my God, will You bring calamity upon this widow whose guest I am, and let her son die?” ^17-20

**17:21** Then he stretched out over the child three times, and cried out to the LORD, saying, “O LORD my God, let this child’s life return to his body!” ^17-21

**17:22** The LORD heard Elijah’s plea; the child’s life returned to his body, and he revived. ^17-22

**17:23** Elijah picked up the child and brought him down from the upper room into the main room, and gave him to his mother. “See,” said Elijah, “your son is alive.” ^17-23

**17:24** And the woman answered Elijah, “Now I know that you are a man of God and that the word of the LORD is truly in your mouth.” ^17-24
