## Thanksgiving for vindication

*A Psalm. A Song for the Sabbath Day.*

**92:1** It is good to give thanks to the LORD, to sing praises to your name, O Most High, ^92-1

**92:2** to declare your steadfast love in the morning and your faithfulness by night, ^92-2

**92:3** to the music of the lute and the harp, to the melody of the lyre. ^92-3

**92:4** For you, O LORD, have made me glad by your work; at the works of your hands I sing for joy. ^92-4

**92:5** How great are your works, O LORD! Your thoughts are very deep! ^92-5

**92:6** The dullard cannot know; the stupid cannot understand this: ^92-6

**92:7** though the wicked sprout like grass and all evildoers flourish, they are doomed to destruction forever, ^92-7

**92:8** but you, O LORD, are on high forever. ^92-8

**92:9** For your enemies, O LORD, for your enemies shall perish; all evildoers shall be scattered. ^92-9

**92:10** But you have exalted my horn like that of the wild ox; you have anointed me with fresh oil. ^92-10

**92:11** My eyes have seen the downfall of my enemies; my ears have heard the doom of my evil assailants. ^92-11

**92:12** The righteous flourish like the palm tree and grow like a cedar in Lebanon. ^92-12

**92:13** They are planted in the house of the LORD; they flourish in the courts of our God. ^92-13

**92:14** In old age they still produce fruit; they are always green and full of sap, ^92-14

**92:15** showing that the LORD is upright; he is my rock, and there is no unrighteousness in him. ^92-15
