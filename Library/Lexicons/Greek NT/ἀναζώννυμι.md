# ἀναζώννυμι

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · verb

**Dodson:** I gird up, brace up — I gird up, brace up (with a view to active exertion); a metaphor from the girding of the flowing tunic, to prevent its hampering one in active work. (Strong's 328; headword ἀναζώννυμι)
