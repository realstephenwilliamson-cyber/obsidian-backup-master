# Psalm 70 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 069|← Psalm 69]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 071|Psalm 71 →]]

## Prayer for deliverance from enemies

*To the leader. Of David, for the memorial offering.*

**70:1** Be pleased, O God, to deliver me. O LORD, make haste to help me! ^70-1

**70:2** Let those be put to shame and confusion who seek my life. Let those be turned back and brought to dishonor who desire to hurt me. ^70-2

**70:3** Let those who say, “Aha, Aha!” turn back because of their shame. ^70-3

**70:4** Let all who seek you rejoice and be glad in you. Let those who love your salvation say evermore, “God is great!” ^70-4

**70:5** But I am poor and needy; hasten to me, O God! You are my help and my deliverer; O LORD, do not delay! ^70-5
