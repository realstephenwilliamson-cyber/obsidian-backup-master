## Mūla

विधिं पूर्वोदितं सर्वं कृत्वा समयशुद्धितः ।
क्षुरिकामस्य विन्यस्येज्ज्वलन्तीं मर्मकर्तरीम् ॥ ^mula

vidhiṃ pūrvoditaṃ sarvaṃ kṛtvā samayaśuddhitaḥ |
kṣurikāmasya vinyasyejjvalantīṃ marmakartarīm || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 10 - Chapter Nineteen#^pg223|p. 203]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=223>))
>
> Once having completed all the procedure explained above and purified (the disciple) from the rules (thus exempting him from their observance), (the teacher) should deposit onto him (the Vidyā called) the Razor (kṣurikā) that, blazing (with energy), severs the vital points (marman).
