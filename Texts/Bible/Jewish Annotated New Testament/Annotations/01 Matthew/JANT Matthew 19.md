### 19.1–12: Divorce and marriage

*[[NRSVue Matthew 19#^19-1|Text]], p. 45* · ([[Mark 10#^10-2|Mk 10.2–12]]). ^19-1

**[[NRSVue Matthew 19#^19-1|1–2]]:** *Beyond the Jordan*, east of the Jordan. Jesus avoids Samaria (10.5). ^19-1-2

**[[NRSVue Matthew 19#^19-3|3–9]]:** See [[Luke 16#^16-18|Lk 16.18]]. ^19-3

**[[NRSVue Matthew 19#^19-3|3]]:** *Test him*, see 16.1n. *For any cause*, Jewish teachers permitted divorce of a laborer d The denarius was the usual day’s wage for a laborer e Gk *brother* (following [[Torah 49 - Ki Teitzei#^24-1|Deut 24.1–4]]) but debated reasons to permit it (5.31–32nn.; *m. Git*. 9.10; *m. Ketub*. 7.6; *m. Ned*. 11.12; *b. Sanh*. 22a). ^19-3-2

**[[NRSVue Matthew 19#^19-4|4–6]]:** See 6.26n. *Have you not read*, a rabbinic formula, “binyan ʾav mishne ketuvim” (“construction of a father from two writings”) entails utilizing two passages (*male and female*, [[Torah 01 - Bereshit#^1-27|Gen 1.27]]; *A man leaves . . . flesh*, [[Torah 01 - Bereshit#^2-24|Gen 2.24]]) to issue a ruling concerning a third ([[Torah 49 - Ki Teitzei#^24-1|Deut 24.1–4]]; e.g., *b. B. Metz.* 87b; cf. [[Malachi 2#^2-16|Mal 2.16]]). ^19-4

**[[NRSVue Matthew 19#^19-7|7]]:** *Certificate of dismissal*, [[Torah 49 - Ki Teitzei#^24-1|Deut 24.1–4]]; see 5.31–32n. ^19-7

**[[NRSVue Matthew 19#^19-9|9]]:** *Unchastity*, see 5.32n.; 19.4–6n. Jesus’ opinion on divorce resonates closely with the rabbinic House of Shammai, which permitted divorce only for serious transgressions. The House of Hillel was more lenient (see *b. Git.* 90a). ^19-9

**[[NRSVue Matthew 19#^19-12|12]]:** *Made themselves eunuchs*, could be taken literally, but could also refer to those who have forsworn marriage and procreation. Jewish sources strongly encourage both marriage and procreation ([[Torah 01 - Bereshit#^1-28|Gen 1.28]]; cf. [[Torah 04 - Vayera#^19-5|Gen 19.5]]; CD 4.20–5.2; *b. Yebam*. 63a), although some Jews in the Second Temple period valued celibacy (CD 6.11–7.9, which seems to indicate that at least some of the Dead Sea Scrolls group members at Qumran were celibate, although scholars debate this conclusion; Philo, *Cont*. *Life* 8.68). ^19-12

### 19.13–15: Blessing children

*[[NRSVue Matthew 19#^19-13|Text]], p. 46* · ([[Mark 10#^10-13|Mk 10.13–16]]; [[Luke 18#^18-15|Lk 18.15–17]]). See 18.3n. ^19-13

**[[NRSVue Matthew 19#^19-13|13]]:** *Were being brought*, testifying to the Jewish love and concern for children. *Lay his hands on them*, presumably to bless them; see [[Torah 12 - Vayechi#^48-14|Gen 48.14]]; [[Torah 54 - V'Zot HaBerachah#^34-9|Deut 34.9]]. ^19-13-2

**[[NRSVue Matthew 19#^19-14|14]]:** *To such as these*, see 18.1–5. ^19-14

### 19.16–30: The rich young man

*[[NRSVue Matthew 19#^19-16|Text]], p. 46* · ([[Mark 10#^10-17|Mk 10.17–30]]; [[Luke 18#^18-18|Lk 18.18–30]]; [[Luke 22#^22-28|22.28–30]]). ^19-16

**[[NRSVue Matthew 19#^19-16|16]]:** *What good deed must I do*, the question is illegitimate; eternal life was regarded as part of the covenant with Israel; living within the covenant is a response to this gift ([[Torah 29 - Achrei Mot#^18-5|Lev 18.5]]; see *m. Sanh*. 10.1, “All Israel has a share in the world to come”). ^19-16-2

**[[NRSVue Matthew 19#^19-18|18–19]]:** From the Decalogue in [[Torah 17 - Yitro#^20-12|Ex 20.12–16]]; [[Torah 45 - Vaetchanan#^5-16|Deut 5.16–20]], combined with *Love your neighbor* ([[Torah 30 - Kedoshim#^19-18|Lev 19.18]]), perceived as a cornerstone commandment (*b. Shabb*. 31a; cf. [[Romans 13#^13-9|Rom 13.9–10]]; [[James 02#^2-11|Jas 2.11]]; see also “The Concept of Neighbor” p. 645). ^19-18

**[[NRSVue Matthew 19#^19-21|21]]:** See 26.6–13; [[Luke 12#^12-33|Lk 12.33]]; [[Acts 02#^2-43|Acts 2.43–47]]; [[Acts 04#^4-34|4.34–37]]; [[Galatians 02#^2-10|Gal 2.10]]. *Give . . . to the poor*, mandated by Rabbinic law (*b. B. Bat.* 10a; cf. [[LXX Tobit 04#^4-5|Tob 4.5–11]]), which discourages people from impover-ishing themselves and so becoming dependent on others. *Treasure in heaven*, see 6.19–20. ^19-21

**[[NRSVue Matthew 19#^19-24|24]]:** *Camel to of unchastity, causes her to commit adultery*; others add at the end of the verse *and he who marries a divorced woman commits adultery* b Other ancient authorities add *from my youth* c Gk lacks *the money go through the eye of a needle*, something impossible; cf. “an elephant passing through a needle’s eye” (*b. Ber*. 55b; *b. B. Metz.* 38b). ^19-24

**[[NRSVue Matthew 19#^19-26|26]]:** *All things are possible*, [[Torah 04 - Vayera#^18-14|Gen 18.14]]; [[Jeremiah 32#^32-17|Jer 32.17]]. ^19-26

**[[NRSVue Matthew 19#^19-28|28]]:** *Renewal of all things*, messianic age; see 8.20n.; 10.23n.; 25.30–31; *1 En*. 62.5. *Twelve tribes of Israel*, Jesus’ disciples will judge fellow Jews. ^19-28

**[[NRSVue Matthew 19#^19-29|29]]:** *Inherit eternal life*, see 19.16n. ^19-29

**[[NRSVue Matthew 19#^19-30|30]]:** *First will be last*, eschatological reversal ([[Mark 10#^10-31|Mk 10.31]]; [[Luke 13#^13-30|Lk 13.30]]). ^19-30
