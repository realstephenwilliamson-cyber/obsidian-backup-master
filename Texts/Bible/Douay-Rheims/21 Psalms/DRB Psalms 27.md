# Psalms 27 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 26|← Psalms 26]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 28|Psalms 28 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 27|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**27:1** A psalm for David himself. Unto thee will I cry, O Lord: O my God, be not thou silent to me: lest thou be silent to me, I become like them that go down into the pit. ^27-1

**27:2** Hear, O Lord, the voice of my supplication, when I pray to thee; when I lift up my hands to thy holy temple. ^27-2

**27:3** Draw me not away together with the wicked; and with the workers of iniquity destroy me not: Who speak peace with their neighbour, but evils are in their hearts. ^27-3

**27:4** Give them according to their works, and according to the wickedness of their inventions. According to the works of their hands give thou to them: render to them their reward. ^27-4

**27:5** Because they have not understood the works of the Lord, and the operations of his hands: thou shalt destroy them, and shalt not build them up. ^27-5

**27:6** Blessed be the Lord, for he hath heard the voice of my supplication. ^27-6

**27:7** The Lord is my helper and my protector: in him hath my heart confided, and I have been helped. And my flesh hath flourished again, and with my will I will give praise to him. ^27-7

**27:8** The Lord is the strength of his people, and the protector of the salvation of his anointed. ^27-8

**27:9** Save, O Lord, thy people, and bless thy inheritance: and rule them and exalt them for ever. ^27-9
