Compare: [[NRSVue Job 25|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 25

**25:1** Bildad the Shuhite said in reply: ^25-1

**25:2** Dominion and dread are His;  
He imposes peace in His heights. ^25-2

**25:3** Can His troops be numbered?  
On whom does His light not shine? ^25-3

**25:4** How can man be in the right before God?  
How can one born of woman be cleared of guilt? ^25-4

**25:5** Even the moon is not bright,  
And the stars are not pure in His sight. ^25-5

**25:6** How much less man, a worm,  
The son-of-man, a maggot. ^25-6
