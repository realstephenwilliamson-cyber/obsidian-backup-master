# Psalm 109

← [[BCP Psalm 108]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 110]] →

*Deua, laudem* · Book Five · Twenty-second Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 109]]

**109:1** Hold not your tongue, O God of my praise; \*  
for the mouth of the wicked,  
the mouth of the deceitful, is opened against me. ^109-1

[p. 751] ^pg751

**109:2** They speak to me with a lying tongue; \*  
they encompass me with hateful words  
and fight against me without a cause. ^109-2

**109:3** Despite my love, they accuse me; \*  
but as for me, I pray for them. ^109-3

**109:4** They repay evil for good, \*  
and hatred for my love. ^109-4

**109:5** Set a wicked man against him, \*  
and let an accuser stand at his right hand. ^109-5

**109:6** When he is judged, let him be found guilty, \*  
and let his appeal be in vain. ^109-6

**109:7** Let his days be few, \*  
and let another take his office. ^109-7

**109:8** Let his children be fatherless, \*  
and his wife become a widow. ^109-8

**109:9** Let his children be waifs and beggars; \*  
let them be driven from the ruins of their homes. ^109-9

**109:10** Let the creditor seize everything he has; \*  
let strangers plunder his gains. ^109-10

**109:11** Let there be no one to show him kindness, \*  
and none to pity his fatherless children. ^109-11

**109:12** Let his descendants be destroyed, \*  
and his name be blotted out in the next generation. ^109-12

**109:13** Let the wickedness of his fathers be remembered before the LORD, \*  
and his mother's sin not be blotted out; ^109-13

**109:14** Let their sin be always before the LORD; \*  
but let him root out their names from the earth; ^109-14

[p. 752] ^pg752

**109:15** Because he did not remember to show mercy, \*  
but persecuted the poor and needy  
and sought to kill the brokenhearted. ^109-15

**109:16** He loved cursing,  
let it come upon him; \*  
he took no delight in blessing,  
let it depart from him. ^109-16

**109:17** He put on cursing like a garment, \*  
let it soak into his body like water  
and into his bones like oil; ^109-17

**109:18** Let it be to him like the cloak which he wraps around himself, \*  
and like the belt that he wears continually. ^109-18

**109:19** Let this be the recompense from the LORD to my accusers, \*  
and to those who speak evil against me. ^109-19

**109:20** But you, O Lord my God,  
oh, deal with me according to your Name; \*  
for your tender mercy's sake, deliver me. ^109-20

**109:21** For I am poor and needy, \*  
and my heart is wounded within me. ^109-21

**109:22** I have faded away like a shadow when it lengthens; \*  
I am shaken off like a locust. ^109-22

**109:23** My knees are weak through fasting, \*  
and my flesh is wasted and gaunt. ^109-23

**109:24** I have become a reproach to them; \*  
they see and shake their heads. ^109-24

**109:25** Help me, O LORD my God; \*  
save me for your mercy's sake. ^109-25

[p. 753] ^pg753

**109:26** Let them know that this is your hand, \*  
that you, O LORD, have done it. ^109-26

**109:27** They may curse, but you will bless; \*  
let those who rise up against me be put to shame,  
and your servant will rejoice. ^109-27

**109:28** Let my accusers be clothed with disgrace \*  
and wrap themselves in their shame as in a cloak. ^109-28

**109:29** I will give great thanks to the LORD with my mouth; \*  
in the midst of the multitude will I praise him; ^109-29

**109:30** Because he stands at the right hand of the needy, \*  
to save his life from those who would condemn him. ^109-30
