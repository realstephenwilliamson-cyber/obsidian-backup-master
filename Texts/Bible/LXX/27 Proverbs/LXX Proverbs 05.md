# LXX Proverbs 05

← [[LXX Proverbs 04]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Proverbs 06]] →

*Proverbs · [[LXX]]*

**5:1** *My* son, attend to my wisdom, and apply thine ear to my words;  ^5-1

**5:2** that thou mayest keep good understanding, and the discretion of my lips gives thee a charge. Give no heed to a worthless woman;  ^5-2

**5:3** for honey drops from the lips of a harlot, who for a season pleases thy palate:  ^5-3

**5:4** but afterwards thou wilt find her more bitter than gall, and sharper than a two-edged sword.[^1]  ^5-4

**5:5** For the feet of folly lead those who deal with her down to the grave with death; and her steps are not established.  ^5-5

**5:6** For she goes not upon the paths of life; but her ways are slippery, and not easily known.  ^5-6

**5:7** Now then, *my* son, hear me, and make not my words of none effect.  ^5-7

**5:8** Remove thy way far from her; draw not near to the doors of her house:  ^5-8

**5:9** lest thou give away thy life to others, and thy substance to the merciless:  ^5-9

**5:10** lest strangers be filled with thy strength, and thy labours come into the houses of strangers;  ^5-10

**5:11** and thou repent at last, when the flesh of thy body is consumed,  ^5-11

**5:12** and thou shalt say, How have I hated instruction, and my heart avoided reproofs!  ^5-12

**5:13** I heard not the voice of him that instructed me, and taught me, neither did I apply mine ear.[^2]  ^5-13

**5:14** I was almost in all evil in the midst of the congregation and assembly.  ^5-14

**5:15** Drink waters out of thine own vessels, and out of thine own springing wells.  ^5-15

**5:16** Let not waters out of thy fountain be spilt by thee, but let thy waters go into thy streets.  ^5-16

**5:17** Let them be only thine own, and let no stranger partake with thee.  ^5-17

**5:18** Let thy fountain of water be *truly* thine own; and rejoice with the wife of thy youth.  ^5-18

**5:19** Let *thy* loving hart and thy graceful colt company with thee, and let her be considered thine own, and be with thee at all times; for ravished with her love thou shalt be greatly increased.  ^5-19

**5:20** Be not intimate with a strange woman, neither fold thyself in the arms of a woman not thine own.  ^5-20

**5:21** For the ways of a man are before the eyes of God, and he looks on all his paths.  ^5-21

**5:22** Iniquities ensnare a man, and every one is bound in the chains of his own sins.  ^5-22

**5:23** Such a man dies with the uninstructed; and he is cast forth from the abundance of his own substance, and has perished through folly.  ^5-23

[^1]: 5:4 *Gr.* a more bitter thing.
[^2]: 5:13 *Alex.* omits 'not.'
