## Mūla

सप्तभेदे तु मन्त्राख्ये स्वं मन्त्रेशा महेश्वराः ।
शक्तिः शंभुश्च पञ्चोक्ता अवस्था जाग्रदादयः ॥ ^mula

saptabhede tu mantrākhye svaṃ mantreśā maheśvarāḥ |
śaktiḥ śaṃbhuśca pañcoktā avasthā jāgradādayaḥ || ^iast

> (5) When the Mantra (subjects become objects of knowledge) and the divisions are seven, the five states of waking etc. correspond to (one’s) Own Nature, the Lords of Mantra, the Great Lords of Mantra, Power and Śiva.
