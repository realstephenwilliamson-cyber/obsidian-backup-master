## Godlessness in the last days

**3:1** You must understand this, that in the last days distressing times will come. ^3-1

**3:2** For people will be lovers of themselves, lovers of money, boasters, arrogant, abusive, disobedient to their parents, ungrateful, unholy, ^3-2

**3:3** unfeeling, implacable, slanderers, profligates, brutes, haters of good, ^3-3

**3:4** treacherous, reckless, swollen with conceit, lovers of pleasure rather than lovers of God, ^3-4

**3:5** holding to the outward form of godliness but denying its power. Avoid them! ^3-5

**3:6** For among them are those who make their way into households and captivate immature women, overwhelmed by their sins and swayed by all kinds of desires, ^3-6

**3:7** who are always studying yet never able to recognize truth. ^3-7

**3:8** As Jannes and Jambres opposed Moses, so these people, of corrupt mind and counterfeit faith, also oppose the truth. ^3-8

**3:9** But they will not make much progress because, as in the case of those two men,^[Gk lacks *two men*] their folly will become plain to everyone. ^3-9

## Paul’s charge to Timothy

**3:10** Now you have observed my teaching, my conduct, my aim in life, my faith, my patience, my love, my steadfastness, ^3-10

**3:11** my persecutions, and my sufferings, the things that happened to me in Antioch, Iconium, and Lystra. What persecutions I endured! Yet the Lord rescued me from all of them. ^3-11

**3:12** Indeed, all who want to live a godly life in Christ Jesus will be persecuted. ^3-12

**3:13** But wicked people and impostors will go from bad to worse, deceiving others and being deceived. ^3-13

**3:14** But as for you, continue in what you have learned and firmly believed, knowing from whom you learned it ^3-14

**3:15** and how from childhood you have known sacred writings that are able to instruct you for salvation through faith in Christ Jesus. ^3-15

**3:16** All scripture is inspired by God and is^[Or *Every scripture inspired by God is also*] useful for teaching, for reproof, for correction, and for training in righteousness, ^3-16

**3:17** so that the person of God may be proficient, equipped for every good work. ^3-17
