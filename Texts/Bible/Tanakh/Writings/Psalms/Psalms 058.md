## Chapter 58

**58:1** For the leader; *al tashḥeth*. Of David. A *michtam*. ^58-1

**58:2** O mighty ones,[^1] do you really decree what is just?  
Do you judge mankind with equity? ^58-2

**58:3** In your minds you devise wrongdoing in the land;  
with your hands you deal out lawlessness.[^2] ^58-3

**58:4** The wicked are defiant from birth;  
the liars go astray from the womb. ^58-4

**58:5** Their venom is like that of a snake,  
a deaf viper that stops its ears ^58-5

**58:6** so as not to hear the voice of charmers  
or the expert mutterer of spells. ^58-6

**58:7** O God, smash their teeth in their mouth;  
shatter the fangs of lions, O LORD; ^58-7

**58:8** let them melt, let them vanish like water;  
let Him aim His arrows that they be cut down; ^58-8

**58:9** like a snail that melts away as it moves;[^3]  
like a woman’s stillbirth, may they never see the sun! ^58-9

**58:10** Before the thorns grow into a bramble,  
may He whirl them away alive in fury.[^4] ^58-10

**58:11** The righteous man will rejoice when he sees revenge;  
he will bathe his feet in the blood of the wicked. ^58-11

**58:12** Men will say,  
“There is, then, a reward for the righteous;  
there is, indeed, divine justice on earth.” ^58-12

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
