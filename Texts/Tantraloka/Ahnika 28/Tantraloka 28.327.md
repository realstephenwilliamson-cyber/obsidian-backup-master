## Mūla

क्रमाद्रजस्तमोलीनः कर्मयोनिविमूढगः ।
तत्रेन्द्रियाणां संमोहश्वासायासपरीतता ॥ ^mula

kramādrajastamolīnaḥ karmayonivimūḍhagaḥ |
tatrendriyāṇāṃ saṃmohaśvāsāyāsaparītatā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 06 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 4#^pg157|p. 140]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=157>))
>
> If (he dies) when sattva is well developed, merged (therein), he then travels upwards. Merged in rajas and tamas (when they are prevalent, he is born amongst those intent on) action (karman), (or) in the wombs of unconscious (creatures), respectively. (with 28.326cd–327ab)
>
> There (in that case), the obscuration of the senses, the difficulty in breathing, that runs counter (to their operation) and the like, is the experience of death (that takes place) in the body; it is not the (actual) quitting of the body. (with 28.327cd–328ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* ननु अन्तकाले सत्त्वादिमयत्वेऽपि श्वासायासहिक्कागद्गदादिवैवश्येन सर्वेषां मूढतयैव भाव्यम्, तथात्वे च एषां कथमूर्ध्वगत्यादिसमुचिता देहान्तरसङ्गतिः सङ्गच्छताम् ?-- इत्याशङ्क्य आह--

> तत्रेन्द्रियाणां संमोहश्वासायासपरीतता ॥
> इत्यादिमृतिभोगोऽयं देहे न त्यजनं तनोः ।

इह यन्नाम सत्यामपि तनौ इन्द्रियसंमोहादिः, सोऽयं गौण्या वृत्त्या देहत्यजनशब्दवाच्यो मृतिभोग उच्यते, न तु साक्षादेव देहत्यागः, तदानीमपि अस्य स्फुटत्वेनैव अवस्थानात् ॥
