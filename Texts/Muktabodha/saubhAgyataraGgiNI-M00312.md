# saubhāgyataraṅgiṇī

**Catalog number:** M00312  
**Author:** mukunda  
**Manuscript:** Part of NGMCP Manuscript No. : 5-4656 Reel No. : A 175/11  
**Published:** Muktabodha Indological Research Institute, India

## Description

Description by Mark S.G. Dyczkowski
This is a liturgy (paddhati) for the worship of the goddess
Tripurāsundarī written by a certain Mukunda. He refers to Veda and Vedic  yajñā
with great regard as a source of the teachings concerning the goddess Tripurā whom
he identifies at the beginning of his work with the goddess Durgā of the
Devīmāhātmya.  Thus he quotes here and there short passages from the Vedas and
extensively from the Purāṇas. Notoriously, the Purāṇas draw freely from other
sources, especially Tantras.  He quotes from the Kūrmapurāṇa, Kālikā,
which are his favorites, especially the latter, as well as the Mārkaṇḍeyapurāṇa, Varāha, Viṣṇu, Bhaviṣya, Devī, Matysa,
Skanda, and Vāmana.  The Tantras from which he draws include Vāmakeśvara  which he quotes most, the Jñānārṇava, Tantrarāja,  Uttara
(probably the late redaction of the Rudrayāmala),  and Durgātantra,
which suggests a relatively recent date. Other sources include the
Dakṣiṇāmūrtisaṃhitā and śrīkramasaṃhitā. The texts breaks off in the fifth  chapter (taraṅga).
