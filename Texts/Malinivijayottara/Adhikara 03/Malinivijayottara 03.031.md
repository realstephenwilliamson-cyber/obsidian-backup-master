## Mūla

विषयेष्वेव संलीनानधोऽधः पातयन्त्यणून् ।
रुद्राणून्याः समालिङ्ग्य घोरतर्योऽपराः स्मृताः ॥ ^mula

viṣayeṣv eva saṁlīnān adho 'dhaḥ pātayanty aṇūn |
rudrāṇūn yāḥ samāliṅgya ghorataryo 'parāḥ smṛtāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2023 – March 2023
> p. 44 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=44>))
>
> The Extremely Terrible (Ghoratarā powers) are said to be the lower (aparā) ones; embracing the individual souls (rudrāṇu) attached to sense objects, they cast them down to ever lower levels.[^23]

[^23]: MV 3/31 quoted in TĀv ad 3/103cd-104ab, 8/35cd-42ab and 13/279cd-280ab. MV 3/31ab quoted in TĀv ad 4/23cd- 24ab.
