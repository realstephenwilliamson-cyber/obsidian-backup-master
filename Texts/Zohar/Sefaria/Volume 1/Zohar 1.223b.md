# Zohar 1.223b

← [[Zohar 1.223a]] · [[Zohar (Soncino)]] · [[Zohar 1.224a]] →

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 3.07 - Parashat Va-Yhi#^z1-223b|Parashat Va-Yḥi, at 1:223b]]

*Volume 1, folio 223b* · Vayechi

*Passage Vayechi 31:283 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 1.223a#^vayechi-31-283|Vayechi 31:283]].*

**Vayechi 31:284** From between her feet went forth a youth[^1] who stretched from one end of the world to the other with sixty clubs of fire, and who is also called “Enoch son of Jered.” ^vayechi-31-284

**Vayechi 31:285** He was called “son of Jered” (lit. descent) in reference to the ten stages by which the Shekinah descended to the earth. Under him are stationed many *Hayyoth*, ^vayechi-31-285

**Vayechi 31:286** under which again is fastened the hair of the moon, which is called “the knobs of the sceptre”. Her hands and feet take hold of it like a strong lion holding his prey. ^vayechi-31-286

**Vayechi 31:287** Her nails are those who call to mind the sins of men and inscribe them with all rigour and exactness. ^vayechi-31-287

**Vayechi 31:288** The offscourings of her nails are all those who do not cleave to the Body of the King and suck from the side of uncleanness, when the moon begins to diminish. ^vayechi-31-288

**Vayechi 31:289** Now, after Solomon had inherited the moon in its fullness, he also desired to inherit it in its defective state, and therefore he sought to acquire the knowledge of spirits and demons, so as to inherit the moon on every side. ^vayechi-31-289

**Vayechi 31:290** And in the days of King Solomon, the moon shone in its fullness. ^vayechi-31-290

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Vayechi 31:291.*

**Vayechi 31:292** As for the wisdom of Egypt, this is the lower wisdom which is called “the handmaid behind the millstones”, and which was also included in the wisdom of Solomon.’ Said R. Abba: ‘How thankful am I that I asked you this question, since I have received so illuminating an answer.’ ^vayechi-31-292

**Vayechi 31:293** R. Simeon said further: ‘With regard to Solomon’s words, “What profit hath man in all his labour?” (Eccl. 1, 3), these do not apply to labour in the study of the Torah, since the statement is qualified by the words, “wherein he laboureth under the sun”, and the study of the Torah is above the sun.’ Said R. Hiya: ‘Study of the Torah which is prosecuted for worldly ends is also accounted “under the sun”, as it does not ascend aloft ‘. R. Eleazar said: ‘Though a man should live a thousand years, yet at the time of his departure from the world it seems to him as if he had only lived a single day.’ ^vayechi-31-293

**Vayechi 32:294** WHEN I SLEEP WITH MY FATHERS. Happy is the lot of the patriarchs that the Almighty has made them a holy chariot above and has taken delight in them to be crowned with them; hence it is written, “Only in thy fathers the Lord took delight” (Deut. 10, 15). R. Eleazar said: ‘Jacob knew that he was to be crowned in his fathers and his fathers with him. Hence we have learnt regarding the graven letters that in the letter *shin* there are three strokes, one on one side and one on the other side and one combining them, and this is the allusion in the verse: “And the middle bar in the midst of the boards shall pass through from end to end” (Ex. 26, 28). Hence Jacob said: “I shall lie with my fathers”.’ ^vayechi-32-294

[^1]: Metatron.
