# Psalm 47

← [[BCP Psalm 046]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 048]] →

*Omnes gentes, plaudite* · Book Two · Ninth Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 047]]

**47:1** Clap your hands, all you peoples; \*  
shout to God with a cry of joy. ^47-1

**47:2** For the LORD Most High is to be feared; \*  
he is the great King over all the earth. ^47-2

**47:3** He subdues the peoples under us, \*  
and the nations under our feet. ^47-3

**47:4** He chooses our inheritance for us, \*  
the pride of Jacob whom he loves. ^47-4

**47:5** God has gone up with a shout, \*  
the LORD with the sound of the ram's-horn. ^47-5

**47:6** Sing praises to God, sing praises; \*  
sing praises to our King, sing praises. ^47-6

**47:7** For God is King of all the earth; \*  
sing praises with all your skill. ^47-7

**47:8** God reigns over the nations; \*  
God sits upon his holy throne. ^47-8

**47:9** The nobles of the peoples have gathered together \*  
with the people of the God of Abraham. ^47-9

**47:10** The rulers of the earth belong to God, \*  
and he is highly exalted. ^47-10
