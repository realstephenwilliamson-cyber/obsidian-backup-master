# Psalm 71

← [[BCP Psalm 070]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 072]] →

*In te, Domine, speravi* · Book Two · Fourteenth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 071]]

**71:1** In you, O LORD, have I taken refuge; \*  
let me never be ashamed. ^71-1

**71:2** In your righteousness, deliver me and set me free; \*  
incline your ear to me and save me. ^71-2

**71:3** Be my strong rock, a castle to keep me safe; \*  
you are my crag and my stronghold. ^71-3

**71:4** Deliver me, my God, from the hand of the wicked, \*  
from the clutches of the evildoer and the oppressor. ^71-4

**71:5** For you are my hope, O LORD God, \*  
my confidence since I was young. ^71-5

**71:6** I have been sustained by you ever since I was born;  
from my mother's womb you have been my strength; \*  
my praise shall be always of you. ^71-6

**71:7** I have become a portent to many; \*  
but you are my refuge and my strength. ^71-7

**71:8** Let my mouth be full of your praise \*  
and your glory all the day long. ^71-8

**71:9** Do not cast me off in my old age; \*  
forsake me not when my strength fails. ^71-9

**71:10** For my enemies are talking against me, \*  
and those who lie in wait for my life take counsel together. ^71-10

**71:11** They say, "God has forsaken him;  
go after him and seize him; \*  
because there is none who will save." ^71-11

[p. 684] ^pg684

**71:12** O God, be not far from me; \*  
come quickly to help me, O my God. ^71-12

**71:13** Let those who set themselves against me be put to shame and be disgraced; \*  
let those who seek to do me evil be covered with scorn and reproach. ^71-13

**71:14** But I shall always wait in patience, \*  
and shall praise you more and more. ^71-14

**71:15** My mouth shall recount your mighty acts  
and saving deeds all day long; \*  
though I cannot know the number of them. ^71-15

**71:16** I will begin with the mighty works of the Lord GOD; \*  
I will recall your righteousness, yours alone. ^71-16

**71:17** O God, you have taught me since I was young, \*  
and to this day I tell of your wonderful works. ^71-17

**71:18** And now that I am old and gray-headed, O God, do not forsake me, \*  
till I make known your strength to this generation  
and your power to all who are to come. ^71-18

**71:19** Your righteousness, O God, reaches to the heavens; \*  
you have done great things;  
who is like you, O God? ^71-19

**71:20** You have showed me great troubles and adversities, \*  
but you will restore my life  
and bring me up again from the deep places of the earth. ^71-20

**71:21** You strengthen me more and more; \*  
you enfold and comfort me, ^71-21

[p. 685] ^pg685

**71:22** Therefore I will praise you upon the lyre for your faithfulness, O my God; \*  
I will sing to you with the harp, O Holy One of Israel. ^71-22

**71:23** My lips will sing with joy when I play to you, \*  
and so will my soul, which you have redeemed. ^71-23

**71:24** My tongue will proclaim your righteousness all day long, \*  
for they are ashamed and disgraced who sought to do me harm. ^71-24
