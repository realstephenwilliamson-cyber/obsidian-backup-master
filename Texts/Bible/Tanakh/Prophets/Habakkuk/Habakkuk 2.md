# Habakkuk 2

Compare: [[NRSVue Habakkuk 02|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 2

**2:1** I will stand on my watch,  
Take up my station at the[^1] post,  
And wait to see what He will say to me,  
What He[^2] will reply to my complaint. ^2-1

**2:2** The LORD answered me and said:  
Write the prophecy down,  
Inscribe it clearly on tablets,  
So that it can be read easily. ^2-2

**2:3** For there is yet a prophecy[^3] for a set term,  
A truthful witness for a time that will come.  
Even if it tarries, wait for it still;  
For it will surely come, without delay: ^2-3

**2:4** Lo, his spirit within him is puffed up, not upright,  
But[^4] the righteous man is rewarded with life  
For his fidelity. ^2-4

**2:5** How much less then shall the defiant[^5] go unpunished,  
The treacherous, arrogant man  
Who has made his maw as wide as Sheol,  
Who is as insatiable as Death,  
Who has harvested all the nations  
And gathered in all the peoples! ^2-5

**2:6** Surely all these shall pronounce a satire against him,  
A pointed epigram concerning him.  
They shall say:  
Ah, you who pile up what is not yours—  
How much longer?—  
And make ever heavier your load of indebtedness! ^2-6

**2:7** Right suddenly will your creditors[^6] arise,  
And those who remind[^7] you will awake,  
And you will be despoiled by them. ^2-7

**2:8** Because you plundered many nations,  
All surviving peoples shall plunder you—  
For crimes against men and wrongs against lands,  
Against cities and all their inhabitants. ^2-8

**2:9** Ah, you who have acquired gains  
To the detriment of your own house,  
Who have destroyed many peoples[^8]  
In order to set your nest on high  
To escape disaster! ^2-9

**2:10** You have plotted shame for your own house,  
And guilt for yourself; ^2-10

**2:11** For a stone shall cry out from the wall,  
And a rafter shall answer it from the woodwork. ^2-11

**2:12** Ah, you who have built a town with crime,  
And established a city with infamy, ^2-12

**2:13** So that peoples have had to toil for the fire,[^9]  
And nations to weary themselves for naught!  
[^10]Behold, it is from the LORD of Hosts: ^2-13

**2:14** For the earth shall be filled  
With awe for the glory of the LORD  
As water covers the sea.[^11] ^2-14

**2:15** Ah, you who make others drink to intoxication  
As you pour out[^12] your wrath,  
In order to gaze upon their nakedness![^13] ^2-15

**2:16** You shall be sated with shame  
Rather than glory:  
Drink in your turn and stagger![^14]  
The cup in the right hand of the LORD  
Shall come around to you,  
And disgrace to[^15] your glory. ^2-16

**2:17** For the lawlessness against Lebanon shall cover you,  
The destruction of beasts shall overwhelm you[^16]—  
For crimes against men and wrongs against lands,  
Against cities and all their inhabitants. ^2-17

**2:18** [^17]What has the carved image availed,  
That he who fashioned it has carved it  
For an image and a false oracle—  
That he who fashioned his product has trusted in it,  
Making dumb idols? ^2-18

**2:19** Ah, you who say, “Wake up” to wood,  
“Awaken,” to inert stone!  
Can that give an oracle?  
Why, it is encased in gold and silver,  
But there is no breath inside it. ^2-19

**2:20** But the LORD in His holy Abode—  
Be silent before Him all the earth! ^2-20

[^1]: 1QpHab reads “my.”
[^2]: Taking ’ashib as equivalent to yashib.
[^3]: Emendation yields “the prophecy is a witness.”
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain. Emendation yields “Lo, there is a reward for the upright—/ the life breath within him—/ And…”
[^5]: Connecting hyyn (1 QpHab hwn) with the root hwn, Deut. 1.41; for the thought cf. Prov. 11.31. Meaning of rest of line uncertain.
[^6]: Lit. “usurers.”
[^7]: Lit. “shake”; the same verb means “to call to mind” in Samaritan Aramaic.
[^8]: Brought up from v. 10 for clarity.
[^9]: I.e., without profit.
[^10]: Connection with the next four lines uncertain; they might read better after v. 20.
[^11]: Cf. Isa. 11.9.
[^12]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain. Emendation yields “from the bowl of.”
[^13]: Cf. Gen. 9.21–22.
[^14]: Emendation yields “uncover yourself”; cf. Lam. 4.21.
[^15]: Or “vomit of disgrace upon.”
[^16]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^17]: This verse would read well after v. 19.
