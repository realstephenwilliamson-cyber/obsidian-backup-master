# Psalm 148

← [[BCP Psalm 147]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 149]] →

*Laudate Dominum* · Book Five · Thirtieth Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 148]]

**148:1** Hallelujah!  
Praise the LORD from the heavens; \*  
praise him in the heights. ^148-1

**148:2** Praise him, all you angels of his; \*  
praise him, all his host. ^148-2

**148:3** Praise him, sun and moon; \*  
praise him, all you shining stars. ^148-3

[p. 806] ^pg806

**148:4** Praise him, heaven of heavens, \*  
and you waters above the heavens. ^148-4

**148:5** Let them praise the Name of the LORD; \*  
for he commanded, and they were created. ^148-5

**148:6** He made them stand fast for ever and ever; \*  
he gave them a law which shall not pass away. ^148-6

**148:7** Praise the LORD from the earth, \*  
you sea-monsters and all deeps; ^148-7

**148:8** Fire and hail, snow and fog, \*  
tempestuous wind, doing his will; ^148-8

**148:9** Mountains and all hills, \*  
fruit trees and all cedars; ^148-9

**148:10** Wild beasts and all cattle, \*  
creeping things and wingèd birds; ^148-10

**148:11** Kings of the earth and all peoples, \*  
princes and all rulers of the world; ^148-11

**148:12** Young men and maidens, \*  
old and young together. ^148-12

**148:13** Let them praise the Name of the LORD, \*  
for his Name only is exalted,  
his splendor is over earth and heaven. ^148-13

**148:14** He has raised up strength for his people  
and praise for all his loyal servants, \*  
the children of Israel, a people who are near him.  
Hallelujah! ^148-14
