## Mūla

आह्निकानां समभ्यस्येत् स साक्षाद्भैरवो भवेत् ।
सप्तत्रिंशत्सु सम्पूर्णबोधो यद्भैरवो भवेत् ॥ ^mula

āhnikānāṃ samabhyasyet sa sākṣādbhairavo bhavet |
saptatriṃśatsu sampūrṇabodho yadbhairavo bhavet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. I
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 01 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v01 13 - Chapter One, part 12#^pg475|p. 461]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 01.pdf#page=475>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 1.286cd–287ab.
>
> Thus, the wise man who constantly (and assiduously) practices these thirty-seven chapters becomes Bhairava Himself in person (directly visible) (sākṣāt). He whose awakened understanding of the thirty-seven (chapters) is perfectly complete (and His perfectly full consciousness (pervades) within the thirty-seven (principles)) is Bhairava, what wonder is it if by his (mere) glance, individual souls (also) attain Bhairava’s state? (with 1.284cd–286ab)
