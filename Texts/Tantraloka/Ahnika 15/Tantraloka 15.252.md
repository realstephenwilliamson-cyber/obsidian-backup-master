## Mūla

साध्यानुष्ठानभेदेन न्यासकाले स्मरेद् गुरुः ।
परैव देवीत्रितयमध्ये याऽभेदिनी स्थिता ॥ ^mula

sādhyānuṣṭhānabhedena nyāsakāle smared guruḥ |
paraiva devītritayamadhye yā'bhedinī sthitā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 05 - Chapter Fifteen, part 4#^pg169|p. 151]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=169>))
>
> The teacher should recollect (Kālasaṁkarṣi when (he is) depositing (her) as (possessing) limbs, faces, attendants, the twelve (other) powers and more, according to the type (of his ritual) observance (of the deity which is his) goal (sādhyānuṣṭhāna). (with 15.251cd–252ab)
>
> Thus, it is proclaimed in both the Sāraśāstra (Trikasāra) and the Yāmala of the goddess (devyāh) (i.e., the Devyāyāmala) that this (goddess,) who is Parā Herself and abides in the midst of the three (Trika) goddesses, undivided (from them), is said to be essentially pure, unlimited consciousness alone. (with 15.252cd–253)
