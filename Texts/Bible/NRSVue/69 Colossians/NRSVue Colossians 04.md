# Colossians 4 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Colossians 03|← Colossians 3]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · —

Compare: [[Colossians 04|BSB and SBLGNT]] · [[DRB Colossians 04|Douay-Rheims]] · [[JANT Colossians 04|JANT notes]]

**4:1** Masters, treat your slaves justly and fairly, for you know that you also have a Master in heaven. ^4-1

## Further instructions

**4:2** Devote yourselves to prayer, keeping alert in it with thanksgiving. ^4-2

**4:3** At the same time, pray for us as well, that God will open to us a door for the word, that we may declare the mystery of Christ, for which I am in prison, ^4-3

**4:4** so that I may reveal it clearly, as I should. ^4-4

**4:5** Conduct yourselves wisely toward outsiders, making the most of the time.^[Or *opportunity*] ^4-5

**4:6** Let your speech always be gracious, seasoned with salt, so that you may know how you ought to answer everyone. ^4-6

## Final greetings and benediction

**4:7** Tychicus will tell you all the news about me; he is a beloved brother, a faithful minister, and a fellow servant in the Lord. ^4-7

**4:8** I have sent him to you for this very purpose, so that you may know how we are^[Other ancient authorities read *that I may know how you are*] and that he may encourage your hearts; ^4-8

**4:9** he is coming with Onesimus, the faithful and beloved brother, who is one of you. They will tell you about everything here. ^4-9

**4:10** Aristarchus my fellow prisoner greets you, as does Mark the cousin of Barnabas, concerning whom you have received instructions; if he comes to you, welcome him. ^4-10

**4:11** And Jesus who is called Justus greets you. These are the only ones of the circumcision among my coworkers for the kingdom of God, and they have been a comfort to me. ^4-11

**4:12** Epaphras, who is one of you, a servant of Christ,^[Other ancient authorities add *Jesus*] greets you. He is always striving in his prayers on your behalf, so that you may stand mature and fully assured^[Other ancient authorities read *complete*] in everything that God wills. ^4-12

**4:13** For I testify for him that he has worked hard for you and for those in Laodicea and in Hierapolis. ^4-13

**4:14** Luke, the beloved physician, and Demas greet you. ^4-14

**4:15** Give my greetings to the brothers and sisters in Laodicea and to Nympha and the church in her^[Other ancient authorities read *his* or *their*] house. ^4-15

**4:16** And when this letter has been read among you, have it read also in the church of the Laodiceans, and see that you read also the letter from Laodicea. ^4-16

**4:17** And say to Archippus, “See that you complete the task that you have received in the Lord.” ^4-17

**4:18** I, Paul, write this greeting with my own hand. Remember my chains. Grace be with you.^[Other ancient authorities add *Amen*] ^4-18
