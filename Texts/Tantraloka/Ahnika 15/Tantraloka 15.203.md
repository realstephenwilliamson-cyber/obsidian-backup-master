## Mūla

दिग्विभागः स्थितो लोके शास्त्रेऽपि च तथोच्यते ।
क्रमात्सदाशिवाधीशः पञ्चमन्त्रतनुर्यतः ॥ ^mula

digvibhāgaḥ sthito loke śāstre'pi ca tathocyate |
kramātsadāśivādhīśaḥ pañcamantratanuryataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 05 - Chapter Fifteen, part 4#^pg143|p. 125]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=143>))
>
> This is how the division of the directions is present within this, the pure Light (of consciousness) (prakāśamātra), that is, Śiva, the supreme benefactor. (Now) we will now expound how it is according to the scriptures and in the world. (with 15.202cd–203ab)
>
> In due order (after the principles, Śiva and Śakti), Sadāśiva is the lord. His body consists of the Mantras of the five (Brahmās). This is because the directions are derived from the division (of these Five Brahmās), called Īśāna, Tatpuruṣa, Aghora, Vāmadeva, and Sadyojāta, (along with the) lower (face). (with 15.203cd–204ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* तत्र शास्त्रीयं दिग्विभागमाह--

> क्रमात्सदाशिवाधीशः पञ्चमन्त्रतनुर्यतः ॥
> ईशन्रघोरवामाख्यसद्योऽधोभेदतो दिशः ।

शिवशक्तिदशानन्तरं भगवान् पञ्चमन्त्रतनुः सदाशिवनाथो भवति यस्मादीशन्रदिवक्रभेदतः षोढा दिशः समुल्लसन्ति--इति वाक्यार्थः ॥
