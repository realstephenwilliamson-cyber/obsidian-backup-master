## Mūla

ईश्वरं च महाप्रेतं प्रहसन्तं सचेतनम् ।
कालाग्निकोटिवपुषमित्येवं सर्वमासनम्  ॥ ^mula

īśvaraṁ ca mahāpretaṁ prahasantaṁ sacetanam |
kālāgnikoṭivapuṣam ity evaṁ sarvam āsanam[^75] || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2025 – April 2025
> p. 19 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=33>))
>
> **Sadāśiva The Great Ghost (Mahāpreta)**
>
> And (above Pure Knowledge one should contemplate) Īśvara (and above him Sadāśiva), the Great Ghost, who is (full of) consciousness and laughing, His body (blazing like) millions of Fires of Time.[^76]

[^75]: *parātri tyetatsarvam*
[^76]: MV 8/68 is quoted in the TĀv ad 15/312. See TĀ 15/309-310.
