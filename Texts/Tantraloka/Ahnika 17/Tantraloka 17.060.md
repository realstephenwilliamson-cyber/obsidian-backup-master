## Mūla

जुहोमि पुनरस्त्रेण वौषडन्त इति क्षिपेत् ।
पुनः पूर्णां ततो मायामभ्यर्च्याथ विसर्जयेत् ॥ ^mula

juhomi punarastreṇa vauṣaḍanta iti kṣipet |
punaḥ pūrṇāṃ tato māyāmabhyarcyātha visarjayet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 08 - Chapter Seventeen#^pg185|p. 165]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=185>))
>
> The teacher should unite in this way (the disciple) with (his) location within (his own) Self, (just as he was) previously placed within that (reality level). Then, (the disciple’s) Self, purified from that much (of the Path), should be led (back) into the disciple’s heart. In order make that (awareness of purity) firm (and stable, he should offer) with (the Vidyā of) Parā a full (oblation) again, preceded by the great Pāśupata (weapon), in order to purify (whatever) is adverse (viloma). Then saying again “I offer etc.” with the Weapon (Mantra) and, (with the other exclamations) ending with the mantra VAUṢAT, he should cast another full (oblation into the fire). Then, having worshiped Māyā, he should dismiss her. (with 17.58–60)
