> **4. Cakra (the Wheel)**
>
> The fist of the left hand faces upwards, the right... cast the fist... is the Wheel and the Arrow... (with 7.7cd–8ab)

> **5. Vajra (the Thunderbolt)**
>
> Place the right face downwards above the left facing upwards. The little fingers and the thumbs adhere together and the rest (of the fingers touch) the wrist – this is said to be the gesture of the thunderbolt of Indra that gives satisfaction. (with 7.8cd–9)
