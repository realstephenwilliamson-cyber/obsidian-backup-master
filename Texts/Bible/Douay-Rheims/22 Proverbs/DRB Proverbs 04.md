# Proverbs 4 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Proverbs 03|← Proverbs 3]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Proverbs 05|Proverbs 5 →]]

**4:1** Hear, ye children, the instruction of a father, and attend that you may know prudence. ^4-1

**4:2** I will give you a good gift, forsake not my law. ^4-2

**4:3** For I also was my father’s son, tender and as an only son in the sight of my mother: ^4-3

**4:4** And he taught me, and said: Let thy heart receive my words, keep my commandments, and thou shalt live. ^4-4

**4:5** Get wisdom, get prudence: forget not, neither decline from the words of my mouth. ^4-5

**4:6** Forsake her not, and she shall keep thee: love her, and she shall preserve thee. ^4-6

**4:7** The beginning of wisdom, get wisdom, and with all thy possession purchase prudence. ^4-7

**4:8** Take hold on her, and she shall exalt thee: thou shalt be glorified by her, when thou shalt embrace her. ^4-8

**4:9** She shall give to thy head increase of graces, and protect thee with a noble crown. ^4-9

**4:10** Hear, O my son, and receive my words, that years of life may be multiplied to thee. ^4-10

**4:11** I will shew thee the way of wisdom, I will lead thee by the paths of equity: ^4-11

**4:12** Which when thou shalt have entered, thy steps shall not be straitened, and when thou runnest thou shalt not meet a stumblingblock. ^4-12

**4:13** Take hold on instruction, leave it not: keep it, because it is thy life. ^4-13

**4:14** Be not delighted in the paths of the wicked, neither let the way of evil men please thee. ^4-14

**4:15** Flee from it, pass not by it: go aside, and forsake it. ^4-15

**4:16** For they sleep not except they have done evil: and their sleep is taken away unless they have made some to fall. ^4-16

**4:17** They eat the bread of wickedness, and drink the wine of iniquity. ^4-17

**4:18** But the path of the just, as a shining light, goeth forwards and increaseth even to perfect day. ^4-18

**4:19** The way of the wicked is darksome: they know not where they fall. ^4-19

**4:20** My son, hearken to my words, and incline thy ear to my sayings. ^4-20

**4:21** Let them not depart from thy eyes, keep them in the midst of thy heart: ^4-21

**4:22** For they are life to those that find them, and health to all flesh. ^4-22

**4:23** With all watchfulness keep thy heart, because life issueth out from it. ^4-23

**4:24** Remove from thee a froward mouth, and let detracting lips be far from thee. ^4-24

**4:25** Let thy eyes look straight on, and let thy eyelids go before thy steps. ^4-25

**4:26** Make straight the path for thy feet, and all thy ways shall be established. ^4-26

**4:27** Decline not to the right hand, nor to the left: turn away thy foot from evil. For the Lord knoweth the ways that are on the right hand: but those are perverse which are on the left hand. But he will make thy courses straight, he will bring forward thy ways in peace. ^4-27
