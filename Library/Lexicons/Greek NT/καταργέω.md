# καταργέω

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · verb

**Dodson:** I bring to naught, sever, abolish — (a) I make idle (inactive), make of no effect, annul, abolish, bring to naught, (b) I discharge, sever, separate from. (Strong's 2673; headword καταργέω)
