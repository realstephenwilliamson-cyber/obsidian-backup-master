## Chapter 60

**60:1** Arise, shine, for your light has dawned;  
The Presence of the LORD has shone upon you! ^60-1

**60:2** Behold! Darkness shall cover the earth,  
And thick clouds the peoples;  
But upon you the LORD will shine,  
And His Presence be seen over you. ^60-2

**60:3** And nations shall walk by your light,  
Kings, by your shining radiance. ^60-3

**60:4** Raise your eyes and look about:  
They have all gathered and come to you.  
Your sons shall be brought from afar,  
Your daughters like babes on shoulders. ^60-4

**60:5** As you behold, you will glow;  
Your heart will throb and thrill—  
For the wealth of the sea[^1] shall pass on to you,  
The riches of nations shall flow to you. ^60-5

**60:6** Dust clouds of camels shall cover you,  
Dromedaries of Midian and Ephah.  
They all shall come from Sheba;  
They shall bear gold and frankincense,  
And shall herald the glories of the LORD. ^60-6

**60:7** All the flocks of Kedar shall be assembled for you,  
The rams of Nebaioth shall serve your needs;  
They shall be welcome offerings on My altar,  
And I will add glory to My glorious House. ^60-7

**60:8** Who are these that float like a cloud,  
Like doves to their cotes? ^60-8

**60:9** Behold, the coastlands await me,[^2]  
With ships of Tarshish[^3] in the lead,  
To bring your sons from afar,  
And their[^4] silver and gold as well—  
For the name of the LORD your God,  
For the Holy One of Israel, who has glorified you. ^60-9

**60:10** Aliens shall rebuild your walls,  
Their kings shall wait upon you—  
For in anger I struck you down,  
But in favor I take you back. ^60-10

**60:11** Your gates shall always stay open—  
Day and night they shall never be shut—  
To let in the wealth of the nations,  
With their kings in procession. ^60-11

**60:12** For the nation or the kingdom  
That does not serve you shall perish;  
Such nations shall be destroyed. ^60-12

**60:13** The majesty of Lebanon shall come to you—  
Cypress and pine and box—  
To adorn the site of My Sanctuary,  
To glorify the place where My feet rest. ^60-13

**60:14** Bowing before you, shall come  
The children of those who tormented you;  
Prostrate at the soles of your feet  
Shall be all those who reviled you;  
And you shall be called  
“City of the LORD,  
Zion of the Holy One of Israel.” ^60-14

**60:15** Whereas you have been forsaken,  
Rejected, with none passing through,  
I will make you a pride everlasting,  
A joy for age after age. ^60-15

**60:16** You shall suck the milk of the nations,  
Suckle at royal breasts.[^5]  
And you shall know  
That I the LORD am your Savior,  
I, The Mighty One of Jacob, am your Redeemer. ^60-16

**60:17** Instead of copper I will bring gold,  
Instead of iron I will bring silver;  
Instead of wood, copper;  
And instead of stone, iron.  
And I will appoint Well-being as your government,  
Prosperity as your officials. ^60-17

**60:18** The cry “Violence!”  
Shall no more be heard in your land,  
Nor “Wrack and ruin!”  
Within your borders.  
And you shall name your walls “Victory”  
And your gates “Renown.” ^60-18

**60:19** No longer shall you need the sun  
For light by day,  
Nor the shining of the moon  
For radiance [by night[^6]];  
For the LORD shall be your light everlasting,  
Your God shall be your glory. ^60-19

**60:20** Your sun shall set no more,  
Your moon no more withdraw;  
For the LORD shall be a light to you forever,  
And your days of mourning shall be ended. ^60-20

**60:21** And your people, all of them righteous,  
Shall possess the land for all time;  
They are the shoot that I planted,  
My handiwork in which I glory. ^60-21

**60:22** The smallest shall become a clan;  
The least, a mighty nation.  
I the LORD will speed it in due time. ^60-22

[^1]: Emendation yields “coastlands.”
[^2]: Emendation yields “The vessels of the coastlands are gathering.”
[^3]: See note at 2.16.
[^4]: I.e., of the people of the coastlands.
[^5]: Lit. “breasts of kings” or “breasts of kingdoms.”
[^6]: So 1QIsa, Septuagint, and Targum.
