## About

A passage in Karl Marx's *Grundrisse*, his rough draft of 1857–58 for a critique of political economy. Marx follows capital's drive to develop the productive forces to its end point: an automatic system of machinery in which the worker becomes a watchman and regulator, and science, absorbed into fixed capital, faces the worker as an alien power.

- **The Grundrisse:** *Grundrisse der Kritik der Politischen Ökonomie (Rohentwurf)*, seven notebooks written in London over the winter of 1857–58, prompted by the Panic of 1857. They were first published in German in Moscow in two volumes, 1939 and 1941; a one-volume edition followed in East Berlin in 1953.
- **Location:** in the Chapter on Capital, in the pages on fixed capital and machinery that run from Notebook VI into Notebook VII. The contents of the marxists.org text, which follows the Penguin translation, start this discussion at p. 690; the phrase *general intellect* falls in Notebook VII.
- **Argument:** labour time is the measure of value, so as direct labour shrinks, capital undermines its own basis and, Marx writes, works towards its own dissolution. Collective social knowledge, the general intellect, becomes a direct force of production, and wealth could come to be measured by disposable time.
- **English:** Martin Nicolaus's translation of the whole *Grundrisse* (Penguin with New Left Review, 1973); David McLellan published extracts in 1971.
- **Reception:** Wikipedia calls it one of the most widely discussed sections of the *Grundrisse*. Wikipedia says it has been particularly influential on post-war thinkers, especially in the autonomist Marxist tradition in Italy; Antonio Negri's *Marx Beyond Marx* treats the *Grundrisse* as the summit of Marx's revolutionary thought. It has also been used in theories of cognitive capitalism.
- **Criticism:** David Harvey (2023), as Wikipedia reports, notes that Marx uses the term general intellect only once in his corpus. Michael Heinrich (2013) argues that the passage's collapse thesis rests on concepts Marx had not yet worked out, such as relative surplus value, and that *Capital* overcomes it.
