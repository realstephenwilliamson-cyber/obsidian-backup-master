## Chapter 3

**3:1** These are the sons of David who were born to him in Hebron: the first-born Amnon, by Ahinoam the Jezreelite; the second Daniel, by Abigail the Carmelite; ^3-1

**3:2** the third Absalom, son of Maacah daughter of King Talmai of Geshur; the fourth Adonijah, son of Haggith; ^3-2

**3:3** the fifth Shephatiah, by Abital; the sixth Ithream, by his wife Eglah; ^3-3

**3:4** six were born to him in Hebron. He reigned there seven years and six months, and in Jerusalem he reigned thirty-three years. ^3-4

**3:5** These were born to him in Jerusalem: Shimea, Shobab, Nathan, and Solomon, four by Bath-shua daughter of Ammiel; ^3-5

**3:6** then Ibhar, Elishama, Eliphelet, ^3-6

**3:7** Nogah, Nepheg, Japhia, ^3-7

**3:8** Elishama, Eliada, and Eliphelet—nine. ^3-8

**3:9** All were David’s sons, besides the sons of the concubines; and Tamar was their sister. ^3-9

**3:10** The son of Solomon: Rehoboam; his son Abijah, his son Asa, his son Jehoshaphat, ^3-10

**3:11** his son Joram, his son Ahaziah, his son Joash, ^3-11

**3:12** his son Amaziah, his son Azariah, his son Jotham, ^3-12

**3:13** his son Ahaz, his son Hezekiah, his son Manasseh, ^3-13

**3:14** his son Amon, and his son Josiah. ^3-14

**3:15** The sons of Josiah: Johanan the first-born, the second Jehoiakim, the third Zedekiah, the fourth Shallum. ^3-15

**3:16** The descendants of Jehoiakim: his son Jeconiah, his son Zedekiah; ^3-16

**3:17** and the sons of Jeconiah, the captive: Shealtiel his son, ^3-17

**3:18** Malchiram, Pedaiah, Shenazzar, Jekamiah, Hoshama, and Nedabiah; ^3-18

**3:19** the sons of Pedaiah: Zerubbabel and Shimei; the sons of Zerubbabel: Meshullam and Hananiah, and Shelomith was their sister; ^3-19

**3:20** Hashubah, Ohel, Berechiah, Hasadiah, and Jushab-hesed—five. ^3-20

**3:21** And the sons of Hananiah: Pelatiah and Jeshaiah; the sons of [Jeshaiah]: Rephaiah; the sons of [Rephaiah]: Arnan; the sons of [Arnan]: Obadiah; the sons of [Obadiah]: Shecaniah. ^3-21

**3:22** And the sons of Shecaniah: Shemaiah; and the sons of Shemaiah: Hattush, and Igal, and Bariah, and Neariah, and Shaphat—six. ^3-22

**3:23** And the sons of Neariah: Elioenai, and Hizkiah, and Azrikam—three. ^3-23

**3:24** And the sons of Elioenai: Hodaviah, and Eliashib, and Pelaiah, and Akkub, and Johanan, and Delaiah, and Anani—seven. ^3-24
