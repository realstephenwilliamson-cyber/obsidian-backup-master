# Zohar 2.235a

← [[Zohar 2.234b]] · [[Zohar (Soncino)]] · [[Zohar 2.235b]] →

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 6.06 - Parashat Pequdei#^z2-235a|Parashat Pequdei, at 2:235a]]

*Volume 2, folio 235a* · Pekudei

*Passage Pekudei 27:275 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 2.234b#^pekudei-27-275|Pekudei 27:275]].*

**Pekudei 28:276** R. Isaac then followed with a discourse on the verses: “O Lord, in thy strength the king rejoiceth; and in thy salvation how greatly doth he exult! Thou hast given him his heart’s desire… he asked life of thee, thou gavest it to him, even length of days for ever and ever” (Ps. 21, 2-5). He said: ‘David intended in this psalm to sing the praises of the Community of Israel by pointing out that the Holy One, blessed be He, is gladdened by means of the Torah, which is called “strength”, as it is written, “The Lord will give strength unto the people” (*Ibid.* 21, 11). The “king” in this verse refers to the Holy One, blessed be He, ^pekudei-28-276

**Pekudei 28:277** and the salvation mentioned is that of the Right. ^pekudei-28-277

**Pekudei 28:278** The verse continues: “He asked life of thee, thou gavest it to him, even length of days for ever and ever”. From here we learn that King David was not endowed with any life-duration at all of his own, but it was Adam who made him a gift of a portion of his life, consisting of seventy years.[^1] He thus attained length of life, both in this world and in the world to come. ^pekudei-28-278

**Pekudei 28:279** “His glory is great”, because the Holy One, blessed be He, is the greater of “the two great lights” (Gen. 1, 16), but only “through thy salvation”. ^pekudei-28-279

**Pekudei 28:280** The Psalm continues: “For thou makest him most blessed (lit. blessings) for ever; thou makest him glad with joy in thy presence” (*Ibid.* 21, 7), because He is the blessing of the whole world, the source of all blessing; similarly, it was said to Abram, “and be thou a blessing” (Gen. 12, 2): ^pekudei-28-280

**Pekudei 28:281** “Thou wilt make him glad with joy”; this refers to the time when the Holy One, blessed be He, will raise the Community of Israel from the dust and renew Her with the renewal of the moon in joy; ^pekudei-28-281

**Pekudei 28:282** “in thy presence”, to wit, to abide joyfully in Thy presence, in the perfection which She will achieve in that time. For when the Sanctuary was destroyed it was emptied of all its fulness, as we read: “She that hath borne seven languisheth” (Jer. 15, 9), also, “I shall be filled with her that is laid waste” (Ezek. 26, 2).[^2] ^pekudei-28-282

**Pekudei 29:283** Observe that when Moses was about to rear up the Tabernacle, he first surveyed each several part to see if it had been made properly, and only then did he rear it up; the several parts were one by one brought to him, this being the recondite idea of the verse, “the virgins her companions in her train being brought unto thee” (Ps. 45, 15). So Scripture says: AND THEY BROUGHT THE TABERNACLE UNTO MOSES. ^pekudei-29-283

**Pekudei 29:284** They brought it to Moses as the time had come for his espousals; just as the bride is first brought to the bridegroom and then he enters unto her; so first they “brought the Tabernacle unto Moses” and then he entered into the tent of meeting. ^pekudei-29-284

**Pekudei 29:285** Indeed, it is written: AND MOSES WAS NOT ABLE TO ENTER INTO THE TENT OF MEETING, BECAUSE THE CLOUD ABODE THEREON, for the reason that *She* was then arraying herself in *Her* finery as a woman tires and bedecks herself to receive her husband; and at such a moment it is unseemly for the husband to enter unto her. Thus “Moses was not able to enter into the tent of meeting…”, and they had to bring “the Tabernacle unto Moses”. ^pekudei-29-285

[^1]: v. Yalkut Hadash, 5b; Zohar, Gen. 55a.
[^2]: v. T. B. [[Pesachim 72#72b|Pesahim, 72b]] *et passim*. There is at this point a lacuna in the text.
