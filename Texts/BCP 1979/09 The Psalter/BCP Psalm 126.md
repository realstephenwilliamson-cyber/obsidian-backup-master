# Psalm 126

← [[BCP Psalm 125]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 127]] →

*In convertendo* · Book Five · Twenty-seventh Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 126]]

**126:1** When the LORD restored the fortunes of Zion, \*  
then were we like those who dream. ^126-1

**126:2** Then was our mouth filled with laughter, \*  
and our tongue with shouts of joy. ^126-2

**126:3** Then they said among the nations, \*  
"The LORD has done great things for them." ^126-3

**126:4** The LORD has done great things for us, \*  
and we are glad indeed. ^126-4

**126:5** Restore our fortunes, O LORD, \*  
like the watercourses of the Negev. ^126-5

**126:6** Those who sowed with tears \*  
will reap with songs of joy. ^126-6

**126:7** Those who go out weeping, carrying the seed, \*  
will come again with joy, shouldering their sheaves. ^126-7
