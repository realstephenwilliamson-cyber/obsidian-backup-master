## Mūla

अन्तःस्थं कण्ठ्योष्ठ्यं चन्द्राद्व्याप्तिस्तथोन्मनान्तेयम् ।
एवं कर्मणि कर्मणि यत्र क्वापि स्मरन् व्याप्तिम् ॥ ^mula

antaḥsthaṃ kaṇṭhyoṣṭhyaṃ candrādvyāptistathonmanānteyam |
evaṃ karmaṇi karmaṇi yatra kvāpi smaran vyāptim || ^iast

## Translation

>
> Kṣayāntastha (HĀḤ (?)), and Kaṇṭhyoṣṭhya (HSRKṢMVYŪṀ). Such is this pervasion from the Half-Moon to the Transmental. (with 29.160cd–161ab)
>
> Recalling (this state of) pervasion in this way in every action, wherever (and in whatever situation) it may be, he is always free of the stain (of attachment) and, liberated in this life (fvanmukta), is Supreme Bhairava. (with 29.161cd–162ab)

> सततमलेपो जीवन्मुक्तः परभैरवीभवति ।
