## Mūla

वीरश्च वीरशक्तिश्चेत्येवमस्मद्गुरुक्रमः ।
ततोऽवदंशान्विविधान् मांसमत्स्यादिसंयुतान् ॥ ^mula

vīraśca vīraśaktiścetyevamasmadgurukramaḥ |
tato'vadaṃśānvividhān māṃsamatsyādisaṃyutān || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 04 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 2#^pg65|p. 48]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=65>))
>
> (According to some,) the sequence there is that libation should (first) be offered to the deities and then to (the Kaula adepts,) the heroes (vīra). The sequence our teacher (Śambhunātha) (has taught) is that the Kaula adept (should be worshipped first,) and (then) the adept’s Śakti."? (with 28.91cd–92ab)
>
> Then (once that has been done), the most excellent adept (sādhaka) should spread there before them many kinds of (fine) snacks, along with meat and fish etc., until they are satisfied. (with 28.92cd–93ab)
