# κοσμοκράτωρ

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · noun

**Dodson:** ruler of this world — ruler of this world, that is, of the world as asserting its independence of God; used of the angelic or demonic powers controlling the sublunary world. (Strong's 2888; headword κοσμοκράτωρ, ορος, ὁ)
