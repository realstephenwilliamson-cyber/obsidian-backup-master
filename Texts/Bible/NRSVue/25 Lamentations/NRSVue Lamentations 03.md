# Lamentations 3 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Lamentations 02|← Lamentations 2]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Lamentations 04|Lamentations 4 →]]

## God’s steadfast love endures

**3:1** I am one who has seen affliction under the rod of God’s^[Heb *his*] wrath; ^3-1

**3:2** he has driven and brought me into darkness without any light; ^3-2

**3:3** against me alone he turns his hand, again and again, all day long. ^3-3

**3:4** He has made my flesh and my skin waste away; he has broken my bones; ^3-4

**3:5** he has besieged and enveloped me with bitterness and tribulation; ^3-5

**3:6** he has made me sit in darkness like the dead of long ago. ^3-6

**3:7** He has walled me about so that I cannot escape; he has put heavy chains on me; ^3-7

**3:8** though I call and cry for help, he shuts out my prayer; ^3-8

**3:9** he has blocked my ways with hewn stones; he has made my paths crooked. ^3-9

**3:10** He is a bear lying in wait for me, a lion in hiding; ^3-10

**3:11** he led me off my way and tore me to pieces; he has made me desolate; ^3-11

**3:12** he bent his bow and set me as a mark for his arrow. ^3-12

**3:13** He shot into my vitals the arrows of his quiver; ^3-13

**3:14** I have become the laughingstock of all my people, the object of their taunt songs all day long. ^3-14

**3:15** He has filled me with bitterness; he has sated me with wormwood. ^3-15

**3:16** He has made my teeth grind on gravel; he has made me cower in ashes; ^3-16

**3:17** my soul is bereft of peace; I have forgotten what happiness is; ^3-17

**3:18** so I say, “Gone is my glory and all that I had hoped for from the LORD.” ^3-18

**3:19** The thought of my affliction and my homelessness is wormwood and gall! ^3-19

**3:20** My soul continually thinks of it and is bowed down within me. ^3-20

**3:21** But this I call to mind, and therefore I have hope: ^3-21

**3:22** The steadfast love of the LORD never ceases,^[Syr Tg: Heb *LORD, we are not cut off*] his mercies never come to an end; ^3-22

**3:23** they are new every morning; great is your faithfulness. ^3-23

**3:24** “The LORD is my portion,” says my soul, “therefore I will hope in him.” ^3-24

**3:25** The LORD is good to those who wait for him, to the soul that seeks him. ^3-25

**3:26** It is good that one should wait quietly for the salvation of the LORD. ^3-26

**3:27** It is good for one to bear the yoke in youth, ^3-27

**3:28** to sit alone in silence when the Lord^[Heb *he*] has imposed it, ^3-28

**3:29** to put one’s mouth to the dust (there may yet be hope), ^3-29

**3:30** to give one’s cheek to the smiter and be filled with insults. ^3-30

**3:31** For the Lord will not reject forever. ^3-31

**3:32** Although he causes grief, he will have compassion according to the abundance of his steadfast love; ^3-32

**3:33** for he does not willingly afflict or grieve anyone. ^3-33

**3:34** When all the prisoners of the land are crushed under foot, ^3-34

**3:35** when justice is perverted in the presence of the Most High, ^3-35

**3:36** when one’s case is subverted—does the Lord not see it? ^3-36

**3:37** Who can command and have it done, if the Lord has not ordained it? ^3-37

**3:38** Is it not from the mouth of the Most High that evil and good come? ^3-38

**3:39** Why should any who draw breath complain about the punishment of their sins? ^3-39

**3:40** Let us test and examine our ways and return to the LORD. ^3-40

**3:41** Let us lift up our hearts as well as our hands to God in heaven. ^3-41

**3:42** We have transgressed and rebelled, and you have not forgiven. ^3-42

**3:43** You have wrapped yourself with anger and pursued us, killing without pity; ^3-43

**3:44** you have wrapped yourself with a cloud so that no prayer can pass through. ^3-44

**3:45** You have made us filth and rubbish among the peoples. ^3-45

**3:46** All our enemies have opened their mouths against us; ^3-46

**3:47** panic and pitfall have come upon us, devastation and destruction. ^3-47

**3:48** My eyes flow with rivers of tears because of the destruction of my people.^[Heb *the daughter of my people*] ^3-48

**3:49** My eyes will flow without ceasing, without respite, ^3-49

**3:50** until the LORD from heaven looks down and sees. ^3-50

**3:51** My eyes cause me grief at the fate of all the young women in my city. ^3-51

**3:52** Those who were my enemies without cause have hunted me like a bird; ^3-52

**3:53** they flung me alive into a pit and hurled stones on me; ^3-53

**3:54** water closed over my head; I said, “I am lost.” ^3-54

**3:55** I called on your name, O LORD, from the depths of the pit; ^3-55

**3:56** you heard my plea, “Do not close your ear to my cry for help, but give me relief!” ^3-56

**3:57** You came near when I called on you; you said, “Do not fear!” ^3-57

**3:58** You have taken up my cause, O Lord; you have redeemed my life. ^3-58

**3:59** You have seen the wrong done to me, O LORD; judge my cause. ^3-59

**3:60** You have seen all their malice, all their plots against me. ^3-60

**3:61** You have heard their taunts, O LORD, all their plots against me. ^3-61

**3:62** The whispers and murmurs of my assailants are against me all day long. ^3-62

**3:63** Whether they sit or rise—see, I am the object of their taunt songs. ^3-63

**3:64** Pay them back for their deeds, O LORD, according to the work of their hands! ^3-64

**3:65** Give them anguish of heart; your curse be on them! ^3-65

**3:66** Pursue them in anger and destroy them from under the LORD’s heavens. ^3-66
