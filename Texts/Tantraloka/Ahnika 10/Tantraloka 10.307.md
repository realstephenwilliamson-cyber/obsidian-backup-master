## Mūla

स्वरूपं मन्त्रमाहेशी शक्तिर्मन्त्रमहेश्वरः ।
शक्तिः शंभुरिमाः पञ्च मन्त्रेशे पञ्चभेदके ॥ ^mula

svarūpaṃ mantramāheśī śaktirmantramaheśvaraḥ |
śaktiḥ śaṃbhurimāḥ pañca mantreśe pañcabhedake || ^iast

> (6) When the Lords of Mantra (become objects of knowledge), there are five divisions. They are these five - (the waking and other states correspond to one’s) Own Nature, power of the Great Lords of Mantra, the Great Lords of Mantra, power and Śiva.
