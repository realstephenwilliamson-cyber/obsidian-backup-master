## Chapter 149

**149:1** Hallelujah.  
Sing to the LORD a new song,  
His praises in the congregation of the faithful. ^149-1

**149:2** Let Israel rejoice in its maker;  
let the children of Zion exult in their king. ^149-2

**149:3** Let them praise His name in dance;  
with timbrel and lyre let them chant His praises. ^149-3

**149:4** For the LORD delights in His people;  
He adorns the lowly with victory. ^149-4

**149:5** Let the faithful exult in glory;  
let them shout for joy upon their couches, ^149-5

**149:6** with paeans to God in their throats  
and two-edged swords in their hands, ^149-6

**149:7** to impose retribution upon the nations,  
punishment upon the peoples, ^149-7

**149:8** binding their kings with shackles,  
their nobles with chains of iron, ^149-8

**149:9** executing the doom decreed against them.  
This is the glory of all His faithful.  
Hallelujah. ^149-9
