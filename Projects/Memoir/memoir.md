I’ve never liked the phrase “war hero.” I think of myself as a war survivor.

Not that great acts of heroism don’t take place on the battlefield.

One day I noticed the Bronze Star with a “V” device (a little metal ‘V’ pinned into the ribbon, indicating that it was earned[^1] via combat valor rather than meritorious service) on his dress uniform, but when I asked Corporal D how he got it, he said it was” for being in the wrong place at the wrong time.” Later my buddy told me they were ambushed on their last tour. He had seen Corporal D run to a burning truck[^2] and drag an unconscious comrade to cover while the rest of the unit was pinned down by enemy fire; a hail of bullets the good corporal just ignored. Heroism no doubt.

A few months later we were in Iraq. Corporal D was in the gunner’s turret of another truck, I sat behind the passenger. Someone had hacked their iPod into the background of the truck’s internal com channel. I don’t remember the song[^3] but in the middle of the hook shots rang out[^4] The convoy sped up immediately,[^5] Corporal D swore loudly and said, “I think I was hit by the ricochet.[^6]”

Now, the back seat of a Humvee is like an airplane row, but middle seat is elevated and pushed forward and everyone’s wearing more than half their body weight worth of gear[^7] I looked up and to the left[^8] as he rips the shoulder guard off his body armor. As he casts it aside and examines his arm he finds nothing. “How bad? asked a sergeant from the passenger seat. Obviously in pain he stripped out of his body armor[^9] and I start to see the blood on his top. As he went to pull his top and t-shirt over his head he growled “pretty bad” and blood from his armpit sprayed across my face

[^1]: Athletes may *win* medals, I don’t know, war on the other hand has vict(ors/ems) not *winners*

[^2]: Truck = Armed & Armored High Mobility Multipurpose Wheeled Vehicle = Humvee

[^3]: The playlist, like the 5 occupants, was ethnically diverse. Side note, I did play “Bombs Over Baghdad” while flying with bombs over Baghdad

[^4]: Gunshots aren’t that loud but when it hits the armor it can sound like anything from a rock to the spittoon sound effect from Westerns

[^5]: Ambushes incorporating IED’s and CW’s(Conventional Weapons) were common in the area

[^6]: Off the truck’s armor that is

[^7]: Body armor, med kit, 210 rounds, 3 liters of water, weapons, etc

[^8]: Corporal D’s right leg was about a foot from my head
