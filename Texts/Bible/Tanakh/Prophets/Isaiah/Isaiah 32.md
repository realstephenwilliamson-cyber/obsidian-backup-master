# Isaiah 32

Compare: [[NRSVue Isaiah 32|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 32

**32:1** Behold, a king shall reign in righteousness,  
And ministers shall govern with justice; ^32-1

**32:2** Every one of them shall be  
Like a refuge from gales,  
A shelter from rainstorms;  
Like brooks of water in a desert,  
Like the shade of a massive rock  
In a languishing land. ^32-2

**32:3** Then the eyes of those who have sight shall not be sealed,  
And the ears of those who have hearing shall listen; ^32-3

**32:4** And the minds of the thoughtless shall attend and note,  
And the tongues of mumblers shall speak with fluent eloquence. ^32-4

**32:5** No more shall a villain be called noble,  
Nor shall “gentleman” be said of a knave. ^32-5

**32:6** For the villain speaks villainy  
And plots treachery;  
To act impiously  
And to preach disloyalty against the LORD;  
To leave the hungry unsatisfied  
And deprive the thirsty of drink. ^32-6

**32:7** As for the knave, his tools are knavish.  
He forges plots  
To destroy the poor with falsehoods  
And the needy when they plead their cause. ^32-7

**32:8** But the noble has noble intentions  
And is constant in noble acts. ^32-8

**32:9** You carefree women,  
Attend, hear my words!  
You confident ladies,  
Give ear to my speech! ^32-9

**32:10** In little more than a year,[^1]  
You shall be troubled, O confident ones,  
When the vintage is over  
And no ingathering takes place. ^32-10

**32:11** Tremble, you carefree ones!  
Quake, O confident ones!  
Strip yourselves naked,  
Put the cloth about your loins! ^32-11

**32:12** Lament upon the breasts,[^2]  
For the pleasant fields,  
For the spreading grapevines, ^32-12

**32:13** For my people’s soil—  
It shall be overgrown with briers and thistles—  
Aye, and for all the houses of delight,  
For the city of mirth. ^32-13

**32:14** For the castle shall be abandoned,  
The noisy city forsaken;  
Citadel and tower shall become  
Bare places[^3] forever,  
A stamping ground for wild asses,  
A pasture for flocks[^4]— ^32-14

**32:15** Till a spirit from on high is poured out on us,  
And wilderness is transformed into farm land,  
While farm land rates as mere brush.[^5] ^32-15

**32:16** Then justice shall abide in the wilderness  
And righteousness shall dwell on the farm land. ^32-16

**32:17** For the work of righteousness shall be peace,  
And the effect of righteousness, calm and confidence forever. ^32-17

**32:18** Then my people shall dwell in peaceful homes,  
In secure dwellings,  
In untroubled places of rest. ^32-18

**32:19** [^6]And the brush shall sink and vanish,  
Even as the city is laid low. ^32-19

**32:20** Happy shall you be who sow by all waters,  
Who send out cattle and asses to pasture.[^7] ^32-20

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Emendation yields “for the fields.”
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain; emendation yields “Brushland, desert.”
[^4]: Emendation yields “onagers”; cf. Job 39.5.
[^5]: I.e., the transformed wilderness will surpass in fertility what is now used as farm land.
[^6]: Meaning of verse uncertain.
[^7]: Lit. “let loose the feet of cattle and asses”; cf. 7.25 end.
