## Mūla

परेहसंविदामात्रं परलोकेहलोकते ।
वस्तुतः संविदो देशः कालो वा नैव किञ्चन ॥ ^mula

parehasaṃvidāmātraṃ paralokehalokate |
vastutaḥ saṃvido deśaḥ kālo vā naiva kiñcana || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 03 - Chapter Eleven, part 2#^pg111|p. 91]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=111>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 11.104cd–105ab.
>
> It is only the notions (we have) of ‘here’ and ‘elsewhere’ that become this world and the next. (with 11.104ab)
>
> In fact, there is neither a location nor time of consciousness at all. (with 11.104cd)

> परेहसंविदामात्रं परलोकेहलोकते ।
