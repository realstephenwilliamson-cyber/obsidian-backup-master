## Mūla

दिनादिकल्पनोत्थे तु नैयत्ये सर्वनित्यता ।
दिनमासर्क्षवर्षादिनैयत्यादुच्यते तदा ॥ ^mula

dinādikalpanotthe tu naiyatye sarvanityatā |
dinamāsarkṣavarṣādinaiyatyāducyate tadā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 03 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 1#^pg21|p. 4]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=21>))
>
> As the fixity (of rites) is determined on the basis of the conceived (division of) day and the like (according to the nature of the rite), because the month, asterism, and year etc. (during which to perform occasional rites) are necessarily fixed (and applicable in all cases), then it (can be) said that (the status of) all (rites) is that of regular ones (which is clearly absurd).

## Viveka

> [!viveka]- Viveka in English (Dyczkowski, vol. X, pp. 4–5)
> ![[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 03 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 1#Viveka on 28.3]]

*Lead-in:* अथोच्यते दिनादिकल्पनानियमेन नित्यतेति यथाशंसं सायंप्रातरादावेव सन्ध्यावन्दनादि--इत्याह--

> दिनादिकल्पनोत्थे तु नैयत्ये सर्वनित्यता ।
> दिनमासर्क्षवर्षादिनैयत्यादुच्यते तदा ॥

एवं तर्हि सर्वत्र दिनादिकल्पनानैयत्यस्य भावात् सर्वमेव नित्यमुच्यते--इत्याह--सर्वेत्यादि ॥३ ॥
