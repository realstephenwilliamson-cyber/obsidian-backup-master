# Zohar 3.034a

← [[Zohar 3.033b]] · [[Zohar (Soncino)]] · [[Zohar 3.035b]] →

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 7.04 - Parashat Tsav#^z3-34a|Parashat Tsav, at 3:34a]]

*Volume 3, folio 34a* · Tzav

*Passage Tzav 29:187 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 3.033b#^tzav-29-187|Tzav 29:187]].*

**Tzav 29:188** Mark now. The vital soul is only kept up by smell, and this smell brings to mind another, for when Sabbath departs and the additional soul departs, the soul and the spirit are separated and sad until the smell comes and unites them and makes them glad. ^tzav-29-188

**Tzav 29:189** In the same way all (attributes) are united by the smell of the sacrifice and the lamps are kindled and gladdened. ^tzav-29-189

**Tzav 29:190** If one lamp is placed above another and the lower one is lit, the smoke as it ascends kindles the upper one also. So the smoke of the sacrifices, as it ascends, kindles the supernal lamps till they all flame together and unite through this smell, so that there is “a sweet savour to the Lord”. ^tzav-29-190

**Tzav 29:191** Thus the smell of the offering is the support of all and is produced by the priest who brings all together; and therefore seven days of fulfilment were completed in him in order that all may be blessed through his service, and there may be blessing and joy above and below. ^tzav-29-191

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Tzav 30:192–30:193.*
