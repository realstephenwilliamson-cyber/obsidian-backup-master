# Psalm 55 (NRSVue)

## Complaint about a friend’s treachery

*To the leader: with stringed instruments. A Maskil of David.*

**55:1** Give ear to my prayer, O God; do not hide yourself from my supplication. ^55-1

**55:2** Attend to me and answer me; I am troubled in my complaint. I am distraught ^55-2

**55:3** by the noise of the enemy, because of the clamor of the wicked. For they bring^[Cn Compare Gk: Heb *they cause to totter*] trouble upon me, and in anger they bear a grudge against me. ^55-3

**55:4** My heart is in anguish within me; the terrors of death have fallen upon me. ^55-4

**55:5** Fear and trembling come upon me, and horror overwhelms me. ^55-5

**55:6** And I say, “O that I had wings like a dove! I would fly away and be at rest; ^55-6

**55:7** truly, I would flee far away; I would lodge in the wilderness; *Selah* ^55-7

**55:8** I would hurry to find a shelter for myself from the raging wind and tempest.” ^55-8

**55:9** Confuse, O Lord, confound their speech, for I see violence and strife in the city. ^55-9

**55:10** Day and night they go around it on its walls, and iniquity and trouble are within it; ^55-10

**55:11** ruin is in its midst; oppression and fraud do not depart from its marketplace. ^55-11

**55:12** It is not enemies who taunt me—I could bear that; it is not adversaries who deal insolently with me—I could hide from them. ^55-12

**55:13** But it is you, my equal, my companion, my familiar friend, ^55-13

**55:14** with whom I kept pleasant company; we walked in the house of God with the throng. ^55-14

**55:15** Let death come upon them; let them go down alive to Sheol, for evil is in their homes and in their hearts. ^55-15

**55:16** But I call upon God, and the LORD will save me. ^55-16

**55:17** Evening and morning and at noon I utter my complaint and moan, and he will hear my voice. ^55-17

**55:18** He will redeem me unharmed from the battle that I wage, for many are arrayed against me. ^55-18

**55:19** God, who is enthroned from of old, *Selah* will hear and will humble them, because they do not change and do not fear God. ^55-19

**55:20** My companion laid hands on a friend and violated a covenant with me^[Heb lacks *with me*] ^55-20

**55:21** with speech smoother than butter but with a heart set on war, with words that were softer than oil but in fact were drawn swords. ^55-21

**55:22** Cast your burden^[Or *Cast what he has given you*] on the LORD, and he will sustain you; he will never permit the righteous to be moved. ^55-22

**55:23** But you, O God, will cast them down into the lowest pit; the bloodthirsty and treacherous shall not live out half their days. But I will trust in you. ^55-23
