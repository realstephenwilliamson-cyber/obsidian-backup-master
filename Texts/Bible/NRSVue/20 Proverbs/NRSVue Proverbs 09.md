# Proverbs 9 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Proverbs 08|← Proverbs 8]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Proverbs 10|Proverbs 10 →]]

## Wisdom’s feast

**9:1** Wisdom has built her house; she has hewn her seven pillars. ^9-1

**9:2** She has slaughtered her animals; she has mixed her wine; she has also set her table. ^9-2

**9:3** She has sent out her female servants; she calls from the highest places in the town, ^9-3

**9:4** “You who are simple, turn in here!” To those without sense she says, ^9-4

**9:5** “Come, eat of my bread and drink of the wine I have mixed. ^9-5

**9:6** Lay aside immaturity and live, and walk in the way of insight.” ^9-6

## General maxims

**9:7** Whoever corrects a scoffer wins abuse; whoever rebukes the wicked gets hurt. ^9-7

**9:8** Do not rebuke a scoffer, lest he hate you; rebuke the wise, and he will love you. ^9-8

**9:9** Give instruction^[Heb lacks *instruction*] to the wise, and they will become wiser still; teach the righteous, and they will gain in learning. ^9-9

**9:10** The fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom, and the knowledge of the Holy One is insight. ^9-10

**9:11** For by me your days will be multiplied, and years will be added to your life. ^9-11

**9:12** If you are wise, you are wise for yourself; if you scoff, you alone will bear it. ^9-12

## Folly’s invitation and promise

**9:13** The foolish woman is loud; she is ignorant and knows nothing. ^9-13

**9:14** She sits at the door of her house, on a seat at the high places of the town, ^9-14

**9:15** calling to those who pass by, who are going straight on their way, ^9-15

**9:16** “You who are simple, turn in here!” And to those without sense she says, ^9-16

**9:17** “Stolen water is sweet, and bread eaten in secret is pleasant.” ^9-17

**9:18** But they do not know that the dead^[Heb *shades*] are there, that her guests are in the depths of Sheol. ^9-18
