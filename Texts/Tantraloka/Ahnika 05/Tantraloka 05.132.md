## Mūla

अव्यक्तानुकृतिप्रायो ध्वनिर्वर्णः स कथ्यते ।
सृष्टिसंहारबीजं च तस्य मुख्यं वपुर्विदुः ॥ ^mula

avyaktānukṛtiprāyo dhvanirvarṇaḥ sa kathyate |
sṛṣṭisaṃhārabījaṃ ca tasya mukhyaṃ vapurviduḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 07 - Chapter Five, part 5#^pg195|p. 180]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=195>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 5.133.
>
> A phoneme (varṇa) is said to be the resonance (dhvani) similar to an indistinct (avyakta) murmur which, vibrant (with consciousness), (manifests) there in the ‘utterance’ described above. (with 5.131cd–132ab)
>
> (The wise) know that its principal form is the seed of emission (SAUH) and withdrawal (KHPHREM). (with 5.132cd)
