## Chapter 9

**9:1** [^1]The people that walked in darkness  
Have seen a brilliant light;  
On those who dwelt in a land of gloom  
Light has dawned. ^9-1

**9:2** You have magnified that nation,  
Have given it great joy;  
They have rejoiced before You  
As they rejoice at reaping time,  
As they exult  
When dividing spoil. ^9-2

**9:3** For the yoke that they bore  
And the stick on their back—  
The rod of their taskmaster—  
You have broken as on the day of Midian.[^2] ^9-3

**9:4** Truly, all the boots put on to stamp with[^3]  
And all the garments donned in infamy  
Have been fed to the flames,  
Devoured by fire. ^9-4

**9:5** For a child has been born to us,  
A son has been given us.  
And authority has settled on his shoulders.  
He has been named  
“The Mighty God is planning grace;[^4]  
The Eternal Father, a peaceable ruler”— ^9-5

**9:6** In token of abundant authority  
And of peace without limit  
Upon David’s throne and kingdom,  
That it may be firmly established  
In justice and in equity  
Now and evermore.  
The zeal of the LORD of Hosts  
Shall bring this to pass. ^9-6

**9:7** My Lord  
Let loose a word[^5] against Jacob  
And it fell upon Israel. ^9-7

**9:8** But all the people noted[^6]—  
Ephraim and the inhabitants of Samaria—  
In arrogance and haughtiness: ^9-8

**9:9** “Bricks have fallen—  
We’ll rebuild with dressed stone;  
Sycamores have been felled—  
We’ll grow cedars instead!” ^9-9

**9:10** So the LORD let the enemies of Rezin[^7]  
Triumph over it  
And stirred up its foes— ^9-10

**9:11** Aram from the east  
And Philistia from the west—  
Who devoured Israel  
With greedy mouths.  
Yet His anger has not turned back,  
And His arm is outstretched still. ^9-11

**9:12** For the people has not turned back  
To Him who struck it  
And has not sought  
The LORD of Hosts. ^9-12

**9:13** So the LORD will cut off from Israel  
Head and tail,  
Palm branch and reed,  
In a single day. ^9-13

**9:14** Elders and magnates[^8]—  
Such are the heads;  
Prophets who give false instruction,  
Such are the tails[^9] ^9-14

**9:15** That people’s leaders have been misleaders,  
So they that are led have been confused. ^9-15

**9:16** That is why my Lord  
Will not spare[^10] their youths,  
Nor show compassion  
To their orphans and widows;  
For all are ungodly and wicked,  
And every mouth speaks impiety. ^9-16

**9:17** Already wickedness has blazed forth like a fire  
Devouring thorn and thistle.  
It has kindled the thickets of the wood,  
Which have turned into billowing smoke.[^11]  
Yet His anger has not turned back,  
And His arm is outstretched still.[^12] ^9-17

**9:18** By the fury of the LORD of Hosts,  
The earth was shaken.[^13]  
Next, the people became like devouring fire:  
No man spared his countryman. ^9-18

**9:19** They snatched on the right, but remained hungry,  
And consumed on the left without being sated.  
Each devoured the flesh of his own kindred[^14]— ^9-19

**9:20** Manasseh Ephraim’s, and Ephraim Manasseh’s,[^15]  
And both of them against Judah![^16]  
Yet His anger has not turned back,  
And His arm is outstretched still. ^9-20

[^1]: See note j at 5.30.
[^2]: See Judg. 7–8.
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain; emendation yields “in wickedness”; cf. Targum.
[^4]: As in 25.1.
[^5]: Septuagint reads “Let loose pestilence”; cf. Amos 4.10. In vv. 7–20 Isaiah alludes to and builds upon Amos 4.10–12.
[^6]: 1QIsa reads “shouted.”
[^7]: Emendation yields “its enemies.”
[^8]: Emendation yields “who practice partiality.”
[^9]: Emendation yields “palm branches”; the elders and the prophets are the leaders, the people are the led; cf. 3.1–2, 12.
[^10]: Cf. Arabic samuḥa. 1QIsa reads yḥmw.
[^11]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^12]: Moved down from v. 16 for clarity.
[^13]: Cf. note at 5.25.
[^14]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain. Emendation yields “fellow”; cf. Targum.
[^15]: Alludes to the civil wars of 2 Kings 15.10, 14–16, 25.
[^16]: Cf. 7.1–9.
