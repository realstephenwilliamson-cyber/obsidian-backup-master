#### Viveka on 9.221–222

Surely (one may ask), it has not been proved that the nature of pleasure is light. Pleasure is just pleasure, not light. Thus it is stated that ‘light is said to be joy (hlāda)’. This is because (activity) is sequential, as it is both being (as what is being attained by acting) and nonbeing (as that which is yet to be attained or has passed).
