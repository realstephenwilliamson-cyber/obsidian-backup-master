## Mūla

अनहंयुतया प्रोज्झेत् ससङ्कोचोऽपि सोऽकलः ।
नन्वित्थं दुष्कृतं किंचिदात्मीयमभिसन्धितः ॥ ^mula

anahaṃyutayā projjhet sasaṅkoco'pi so'kalaḥ |
nanvitthaṃ duṣkṛtaṃ kiṃcidātmīyamabhisandhitaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 05 - Chapter Nine, part 3#^pg119|p. 102]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=119>))
>
> He who abandons the entire stream of Karma by disassociating himself from it (anahaṁyutayā) is, by this process, a Vijñānakevalin (akala), (who is free of Karma) even though (his consciousness is still) contracted. (with 9.109cd–110ab)
>
> (Someone may object that) surely in this way one can (transfer) whatever evil deed one has done (to someone else simply) by one’s own intention (abhisaṁdhitah) that it may (accrue) to another. (We reply that) no, (this is not possible); you have not understood the true meaning of (our) words. (with 9.110cd–111ab)
