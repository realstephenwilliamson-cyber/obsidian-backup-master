# Psalm 37 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 036|← Psalm 36]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 038|Psalm 38 →]]

## Exhortation to patience and trust

*Of David.*

**37:1** Do not fret because of the wicked; do not be envious of wrongdoers, ^37-1

**37:2** for they will soon fade like the grass and wither like the green herb. ^37-2

**37:3** Trust in the LORD and do good; live in the land and enjoy security. ^37-3

**37:4** Take delight in the LORD, and he will give you the desires of your heart. ^37-4

**37:5** Commit your way to the LORD; trust in him, and he will act. ^37-5

**37:6** He will make your vindication shine like the light and the justice of your cause like the noonday. ^37-6

**37:7** Be still before the LORD, and wait patiently for him; do not fret over those who prosper in their way, over those who carry out evil devices. ^37-7

**37:8** Refrain from anger and forsake wrath. Do not fret—it leads only to evil. ^37-8

**37:9** For the wicked shall be cut off, but those who wait for the LORD shall inherit the land. ^37-9

**37:10** Yet a little while, and the wicked will be no more; though you look diligently for their place, they will not be there. ^37-10

**37:11** But the meek shall inherit the land and delight themselves in abundant prosperity. ^37-11

**37:12** The wicked plot against the righteous and gnash their teeth at them, ^37-12

**37:13** but the LORD laughs at the wicked, for he sees that their day is coming. ^37-13

**37:14** The wicked draw the sword and bend their bows to bring down the poor and needy, to kill those who walk uprightly; ^37-14

**37:15** their sword shall enter their own heart, and their bows shall be broken. ^37-15

**37:16** Better is a little that the righteous person has than the abundance of many wicked. ^37-16

**37:17** For the arms of the wicked shall be broken, but the LORD upholds the righteous. ^37-17

**37:18** The LORD knows the days of the blameless, and their heritage will abide forever; ^37-18

**37:19** they are not put to shame in evil times; in the days of famine they have abundance. ^37-19

**37:20** But the wicked perish, and the enemies of the LORD are like the glory of the pastures; they vanish—like smoke they vanish away. ^37-20

**37:21** The wicked borrow and do not pay back, but the righteous are generous and keep giving; ^37-21

**37:22** surely those blessed by the LORD shall inherit the land, but those cursed by him shall be cut off. ^37-22

**37:23** Our steps^[Heb *A man’s steps*] are made firm by the LORD when he delights in our^[Heb *his*] way; ^37-23

**37:24** though we stumble, we^[Heb *he stumbles, he*] shall not fall headlong, for the LORD holds us^[Heb *him*] by the hand. ^37-24

**37:25** I have been young and now am old, yet I have not seen the righteous forsaken or their children begging bread. ^37-25

**37:26** They are ever giving liberally and lending, and their children become a blessing. ^37-26

**37:27** Depart from evil, and do good; so you shall abide forever. ^37-27

**37:28** For the LORD loves justice; he will not forsake his faithful ones. The righteous shall be kept safe forever, but the children of the wicked shall be cut off. ^37-28

**37:29** The righteous shall inherit the land and live in it forever. ^37-29

**37:30** The mouths of the righteous utter wisdom, and their tongues speak justice. ^37-30

**37:31** The law of their God is in their hearts; their steps do not slip. ^37-31

**37:32** The wicked watch for the righteous and seek to kill them. ^37-32

**37:33** The LORD will not abandon them to their power or let them be condemned when they are brought to trial. ^37-33

**37:34** Wait for the LORD and keep to his way, and he will exalt you to inherit the land; you will look on the destruction of the wicked. ^37-34

**37:35** I have seen the wicked oppressing and towering like a cedar of Lebanon.^[Gk: Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^37-35

**37:36** Again I^[Gk Syr Jerome: Heb *he*] passed by, and they were no more; though I sought them, they could not be found. ^37-36

**37:37** Mark the blameless and behold the upright, for there is posterity for the peaceable. ^37-37

**37:38** But transgressors shall be altogether destroyed; the posterity of the wicked shall be cut off. ^37-38

**37:39** The salvation of the righteous is from the LORD; he is their refuge in the time of trouble. ^37-39

**37:40** The LORD helps them and rescues them; he rescues them from the wicked and saves them because they take refuge in him. ^37-40
