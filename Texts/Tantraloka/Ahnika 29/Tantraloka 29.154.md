## Mūla

सृष्ट्यादिक्रममन्तः कुर्वंस्तुर्ये स्थितिं लभते ।
एतत्खेचरमुद्रावेशेऽन्योन्यस्य शक्तिशक्तिमतोः ॥ ^mula

sṛṣṭyādikramamantaḥ kurvaṃsturye sthitiṃ labhate |
etatkhecaramudrāveśe'nyonyasya śaktiśaktimatoḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 12 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 4#^pg351|p. 334]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=351>))
>
> Thus, he who, by means of the seal (mudrā) which is the union of Moon, Sun and Fire, is engaged in internalizing (into consciousness) the succession of emanation, (persistence and withdrawal) suddenly (jhatiti) attains stability (sthiti) in the (liberated) Fourth (state of consciousness). (with 29.153cd–154ab)
>
> The reflective awareness Śakti and her possessor have of one another, when penetrated by (the power of) the Seal of the Skyfarer (khecaramudrā), and (when they) drink (i.e. kiss each other), eat (enjoy sex) (upabhoga), play (erotic games with one another), laugh (together) and the like, the vitality of Mantra (is experienced through the eight phases of Sound, namely,) 1) an inarticulate (avyakta) (whisper), 2) the resonance (dhvani) (of a bell), 3) the rumble (rāva) (of storm clouds), 4) crackling (sphoṭa), 5) a voice (śruti), 6) sound (nāda), and 7) the end of sound (nādānta)ů, and 8) (all of which) are forms of the uninterrupted and unstruck (resonance of pure consciousness) (andhata),), (which is the eighth). (with 29.154cd–156ab)
