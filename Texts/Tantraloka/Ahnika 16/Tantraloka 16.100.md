## Mūla

ललाटान्तं वेदवसौ रन्ध्रान्तं रसरन्ध्रके ।
वसुखेन्दौ द्वादशान्तमित्येष त्रिविधो विधिः ॥ ^mula

lalāṭāntaṃ vedavasau randhrāntaṃ rasarandhrake |
vasukhendau dvādaśāntamityeṣa trividho vidhiḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 06 - Chapter Sixteen, part 2#^pg68|p. 48]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=68>))
>
> There are 1) eighty-four (finger breadths from the left toe) up to the forehead; 2) there are ninety-six up to the Cavity (of Brahmā), and 3) one hundred and eight up to the End of the Twelve (finger space above the head) (dvādaśānta). This threefold procedure, which (I have) seen in the scripture called Śrīpūrva (i.e. the Mālinīvijayottara),) will be explained (step by step,) in due order. (with 16.100–101ab)

## Viveka

> [!viveka]- Viveka in English (Dyczkowski, vol. IX, p. 48)
> ![[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 06 - Chapter Sixteen, part 2#Viveka on 16.100–101]]

*Lead-in:* कथं चास्य देहन्यासः--इत्याह--

> ललाटान्तं वेदवसौ रन्ध्रन्तं रसरन्ध्रके ।
> वसुखेन्दौ द्वादशान्तमित्येष त्रिविधो विधिः ॥
> क्रमेण कथ्यते दृष्टः शास्त्रे श्रीपूर्वसंज्ञिते ।

वेदवसाविति--चतुरशीतावंगुलानाम् । रसरन्ध्रक इति--षण्णवतौ । वसुखेन्दाविति--अष्टोत्तरे शते ॥
