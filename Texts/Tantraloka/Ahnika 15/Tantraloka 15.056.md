## Mūla

गोरजोवत्यनुद्रिक्ते वायौ ह्लादिनि मन्त्रवाक् ।
गत्यागतिप्रयोगे वा वायव्यं स्नानमाचरेत् ॥ ^mula

gorajovatyanudrikte vāyau hlādini mantravāk |
gatyāgatiprayoge vā vāyavyaṃ snānamācaret || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 03 - Chapter Fifteen, part 2#^pg63|p. 45]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=63>))
>
> One who utters (the appropriate) Mantras should perform the ablution with Air when entering into and coming out (of a place) where there is a pleasant breeze, which is not too strong and contains the dust of (dry) cow dung.
