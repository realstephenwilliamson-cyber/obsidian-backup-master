## Mūla

तत्राणोः सत एवास्ति स्वातन्त्र्यं कर्मतो हि तत् ।
ईश्वरस्य च या स्वात्मतिरोधित्सा निमित्तताम् ॥ ^mula

tatrāṇoḥ sata evāsti svātantryaṃ karmato hi tat |
īśvarasya ca yā svātmatirodhitsā nimittatām || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 06 - Chapter Thirteen, part 2#^pg210|p. 190]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=210>))
>
> When the innate luminosity of the individual soul is obscured, the freedom (to act) (svātantrya) the individual soul possesses there in that case bears its corresponding fruit, which is (as limited) as is (its freedom,) because it is (due to its) Karma (not the activity of the unobscured Śiva nature). (with 13.109cd–110ab)
>
> The Lord’s own will to obscure Himself becomes the instigating cause (nimitta) of Karma and Impurity, and so they have no beginning. (with 13.110cd–111ab)
