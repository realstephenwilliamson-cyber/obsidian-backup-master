# Psalms 94 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 93|← Psalms 93]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 95|Psalms 95 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 94|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**94:1** Come let us praise the Lord with joy: let us joyfully sing to God our saviour. ^94-1

**94:2** Let us come before his presence with thanksgiving; and make a joyful noise to him with psalms. ^94-2

**94:3** For the Lord is a great God, and a great King above all gods. ^94-3

**94:4** For in his hand are all the ends of the earth: and the heights of the mountains are his. ^94-4

**94:5** For the sea is his, and he made it: and his hands formed the dry land. ^94-5

**94:6** Come let us adore and fall down: and weep before the Lord that made us. ^94-6

**94:7** For he is the Lord our God: and we are the people of his pasture and the sheep of his hand. ^94-7

**94:8** Today if you shall hear his voice, harden not your hearts: ^94-8

**94:9** As in the provocation, according to the day of temptation in the wilderness: where your fathers tempted me, they proved me, and saw my works. ^94-9

**94:10** Forty years long was I offended with that generation, and I said: These always err in heart. ^94-10

**94:11** And these men have not known my ways: so I swore in my wrath that they shall not enter into my rest. ^94-11
