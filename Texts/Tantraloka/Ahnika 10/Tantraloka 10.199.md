## Mūla

एवं धरादिमूलान्तं प्रक्रिया प्राणगामिनी ।
गुरुपर्वक्रमात्प्रोक्ता भेदे पञ्चदशात्मके ॥ ^mula

evaṃ dharādimūlāntaṃ prakriyā prāṇagāminī |
guruparvakramātproktā bhede pañcadaśātmake || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 13 - Chapter Ten, part 4#^pg436|p. 417]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=436>))
>
> Such is said to be the configuration (prakriyā) of the fifteen aspects pertaining to the principles from Earth to Nature (Prakṛti) within the vital breath, according to the line of (our) teachers.
