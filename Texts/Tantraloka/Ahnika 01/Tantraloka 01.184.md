## Mūla

यद्विकल्पानपेक्षत्वसापेक्षत्वे निजात्मनि ।
निशीथेऽपि मणिज्ञानी विद्युत्कालप्रदर्शितान् ॥ ^mula

yadvikalpānapekṣatvasāpekṣatve nijātmani |
niśīthe'pi maṇijñānī vidyutkālapradarśitān || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. I
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 01 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v01 10 - Chapter One, part 9#^pg376|p. 362]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 01.pdf#page=376>))
>
> The fact that consciousness is independent within itself of thought constructs (and perceives the nature of things directly reflected within itself or else) depends upon them (in order to discern its objects as specific particulars differentiated from others and itself) is only this movement to and fro (vibhrama) (between its) state of purity (nairmalya) and its opposite. (with 1.183cd–184ab)
>
> In this way, exponents of all views have established that in the course of the business of daily life (vyavahāra) etc., consciousness is dependent on discursive ideation (vikalpa). In order to clarify (that) once (that dependence) is abandoned, (consciousness) is (realised to be essentially) independent of discursive ideation, a large number of gems that are illuminated (for just the very short) span of time of a lightning flash. (Similarly, the man of knowledge grasps the nature of the object in the flash of pure awareness that precedes the formation of thought constructs, as one with his own conscious nature). (with 1.184cd–185ab)

*Lead-in:* एवं संविदः सर्ववादिसिद्धं व्यवहारादौ विकल्पसापेक्षत्वं परिहृत्य विकल्पानपेक्षत्वमेव स्फुटीकर्तुमुदाहरति --

> निशीथेऽपि मणिज्ञानी विद्युत्कालप्रदर्शितान् ॥
> तांस्तान्विशेषांश्चिनुते रत्नानां भूयसामपि ।

वैकटिको हि अचिरस्थायिनि परिमितेऽपि आलोके भूयसामपि रत्नानाम् अतिसूक्ष्मान् परस्परविशेषान् अवसायं विनापि अनुभवातिशयादेव जानीते, येन "इदमल्पम्, इदं महत्,""इदमितोऽपि महद्रत्नम्"इत्यस्य विवेकः स्यात् ॥ १८४ ॥

किं चात्र अनुभवातिशये निमित्तं येन विकल्पनैरपेक्ष्येणापि वस्तुनः सिद्धिः स्यात् ?
