## Mūla

नित्यादित्रितयं कुर्याद्गुरुः साधक एव च ।
नित्यमेव द्वयं चान्यो यावज्जीवं शिवाज्ञया ॥ ^mula

nityāditritayaṁ kuryād guruḥ sādhaka eva ca |
nityam eva dvayaṁ cānyo yāvaj jīvaṁ śivājñayā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2022 – June 2022
> p. 48 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=24>))
>
> The teacher and the adept may perform (all) three kinds of rites, that is, the regular (obligatory) ones (*nitya*) and the rest. (However,) the other two (kinds of devotees can), in accord with Śiva's command, (perform) only regular (obligatory) ones (for themselves) as long as they live.
