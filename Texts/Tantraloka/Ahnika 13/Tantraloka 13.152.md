## Mūla

दीक्षा भवत्यसन्दिग्धा तिलाज्याहुतिवर्जिता ।
अदृष्टमण्डलोऽप्येवं यः कश्चिद्वेत्ति तत्त्वतः ॥ ^mula

dīkṣā bhavatyasandigdhā tilājyāhutivarjitā |
adṛṣṭamaṇḍalo'pyevaṃ yaḥ kaścidvetti tattvataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 07 - Chapter Thirteen, part 3#^pg239|p. 217]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=239>))
>
> “He who knows this in truth has (received) the (true) initiation, which, devoid of (outer offerings such as) sesame seeds, clarified butter (and the like), is sure, and leads to Nirvāṇa.” “He who knows this in reality, although he has not seen the Maṇḍala, always possesses (every) accomplishment (siddhi). He (is a true) yogi and (true) initiate, and, with respect to the sacrifice, (although) he does not know the procedure, he becomes one who (truly) does.” These and other things the Great Lord says in the Parātrīśikā clearly declare that insight (jñāna) is the true (and essential) nature of (all) the purifying rites, including initiation. (with 13.151cd–154)
