## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 12 - Chapter Six, part 5#^pg407|p. 392]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=407>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 6.182cd–183ab.
>
> Therefore, the essential state of cognitive consciousness is, by its very nature, (perpetually) pulsating (spandamāna). (It manifests) in the vital breath as the (countless) mergers and emergences (of all things). (Accordingly,) it is said to be the arising of sixty years (and the other periods of time within the cycle of the breath). (with 6.181cd–182ab)
>
> This deliberation (carcanā) on the wonderful diversity of action is established in the pure will alone, and is the power of Time. Thus, in the outer world, (the power of Time) has no fixed form. (with 6.182cd–183ab)
