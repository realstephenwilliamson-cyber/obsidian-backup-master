## Chapter 66

**66:1** For the leader. A song. A psalm.  
Raise a shout for God, all the earth; ^66-1

**66:2** sing the glory of His name,  
make glorious His praise. ^66-2

**66:3** Say to God,  
“How awesome are Your deeds,  
Your enemies cower before Your great strength; ^66-3

**66:4** all the earth bows to You,  
and sings hymns to You;  
all sing hymns to Your name.” *Selah*. ^66-4

**66:5** Come and see the works of God,  
who is held in awe by men for His acts. ^66-5

**66:6** He turned the sea into dry land;  
they crossed the river on foot;  
we therefore rejoice in Him. ^66-6

**66:7** He rules forever in His might;  
His eyes scan the nations;  
let the rebellious not assert themselves. *Selah*. ^66-7

**66:8** O peoples, bless our God,  
celebrate His praises; ^66-8

**66:9** who has granted us life,  
and has not let our feet slip. ^66-9

**66:10** You have tried us, O God,  
refining us, as one refines silver. ^66-10

**66:11** You have caught us in a net,  
caught us in trammels.[^1] ^66-11

**66:12** You have let men ride over us;  
we have endured fire and water,  
and You have brought us through to prosperity. ^66-12

**66:13** I enter Your house with burnt offerings,  
I pay my vows to You, ^66-13

**66:14** [vows] that my lips pronounced,  
that my mouth uttered in my distress. ^66-14

**66:15** I offer up fatlings to You,  
with the odor of burning rams;  
I sacrifice bulls and he-goats. *Selah*. ^66-15

**66:16** Come and hear, all God-fearing men,  
as I tell what He did for me. ^66-16

**66:17** I called aloud to Him,  
glorification on my tongue. ^66-17

**66:18** Had I an evil thought in my mind,  
the Lord would not have listened. ^66-18

**66:19** But God did listen;  
He paid heed to my prayer. ^66-19

**66:20** Blessed is God who has not turned away my prayer,  
or His faithful care from me. ^66-20

[^1]: Lit. “put a trammel on our loins.”
