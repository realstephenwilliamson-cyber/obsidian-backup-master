## Mūla

अधिष्ठायैव संवित्तिरधिष्ठानं करोत्यलम् ।
अतोऽधिष्ठेयमात्रस्य शरीरत्वेऽपि कुड्यतः ॥ ^mula

adhiṣṭhāyaiva saṃvittiradhiṣṭhānaṃ karotyalam |
ato'dhiṣṭheyamātrasya śarīratve'pi kuḍyataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 06 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 4#^pg161|p. 144]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=161>))
>
> Consciousness can sustain (the existence) of an object, such as (the colours) blue and yellow, after having sustained the body fashioned previously. (with 28.334cd–335ab)
>
> Thus, the body is just an entity sustained (by consciousness, as is any other object,) even if it is a (living) body; the difference between it and (an inert) wall is that it is the first of all things to be sustained (by consciousness). (with 28.335cd–336ab)

## Viveka

> अतोऽधिष्ठेयमात्रस्य शरीरत्वेऽपि कुड्यतः ॥
> देहस्यास्ति विशेषो यत्सर्वाधिष्ठेयपूर्वता ।
