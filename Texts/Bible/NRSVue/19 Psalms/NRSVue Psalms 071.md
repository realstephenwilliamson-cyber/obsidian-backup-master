# Psalm 71 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 070|← Psalm 70]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 072|Psalm 72 →]]

## Prayer for lifelong protection and help

**71:1** In you, O LORD, I take refuge; let me never be put to shame. ^71-1

**71:2** In your righteousness deliver me and rescue me; incline your ear to me and save me. ^71-2

**71:3** Be to me a rock of refuge, a strong fortress^[Gk: Heb *to come continually you have commanded*] to save me, for you are my rock and my fortress. ^71-3

**71:4** Rescue me, O my God, from the hand of the wicked, from the grasp of the unjust and cruel. ^71-4

**71:5** For you, O Lord, are my hope, my trust, O LORD, from my youth. ^71-5

**71:6** From my birth I have leaned upon you, my protector since my mother’s womb.^[Q ms Gk Jerome: MT *from my mother’s womb you took me*] My praise is continually of you. ^71-6

**71:7** I have been like a portent to many, but you are my strong refuge. ^71-7

**71:8** My mouth is filled with your praise and with your glory all day long. ^71-8

**71:9** Do not cast me off in the time of old age; do not forsake me when my strength is spent. ^71-9

**71:10** For my enemies speak concerning me, and those who watch for my life consult together. ^71-10

**71:11** They say, “Pursue and seize that person whom God has forsaken, for there is no one to deliver.” ^71-11

**71:12** O God, do not be far from me; O my God, make haste to help me! ^71-12

**71:13** Let my accusers be put to shame and consumed; let those who seek to hurt me be covered with scorn and disgrace. ^71-13

**71:14** But I will hope continually and will praise you yet more and more. ^71-14

**71:15** My mouth will tell of your righteous acts, of your deeds of salvation all day long, though their number is past my knowledge. ^71-15

**71:16** I will come praising the mighty deeds of the Lord GOD; I will praise your righteousness, yours alone. ^71-16

**71:17** O God, from my youth you have taught me, and I still proclaim your wondrous deeds. ^71-17

**71:18** So even to old age and gray hairs, O God, do not forsake me, until I proclaim your might to all the generations to come.^[Gk Compare Syr: Heb *to a generation, to all who come*] Your power ^71-18

**71:19** and your righteousness, O God, reach the high heavens. You who have done great things, O God, who is like you? ^71-19

**71:20** You who have made me see many troubles and calamities will revive me again; from the depths of the earth you will bring me up again. ^71-20

**71:21** You will increase my honor and comfort me once again. ^71-21

**71:22** I will also praise you with the harp for your faithfulness, O my God; I will sing praises to you with the lyre, O Holy One of Israel. ^71-22

**71:23** My lips will shout for joy when I sing praises to you; my soul also, which you have rescued. ^71-23

**71:24** All day long my tongue will talk of your righteous help, for those who tried to do me harm have been put to shame and disgraced. ^71-24
