# Mālinīvijayottara, chapter 3: Mantras and the Deities of the Alphabets

68 verses, 1–68. Text and translation by Mark Dyczkowski, from *Mālinī*, January–March 2023: [PDF pp. 35–53](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=35>).

Dyczkowski's introduction to the chapter: [[Malinivijayottara 03 - Introduction (Dyczkowski)]].

## Verses


### The Extraction of the Mantras

- [[Malinivijayottara 03.001|3.1]] evam uktvā mahādevī jagadānandakāriṇā
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.002|3.2]] evam etan mahādeva nānyathā samudāhṛtam
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.003|3.3]] śivādivasturūpāṇāṁ vācakān parameśvara
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.004|3.4]] ity uktaḥ sa maheśānyā jagadārtiharo haraḥ

### The Goddess of the Alphabet The Matrix and the Seeds

- [[Malinivijayottara 03.005|3.5]] yā sā śaktir jagaddhātuḥ kathitā samavāyinī
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.006|3.6]] saikāpi saty anekatvaṁ yathā gacchati tac chṛṇu
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.007|3.7]] jñāpayantī jagaty atra jñanaśaktir nigadyate
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.008|3.8]] jātā tadaiva tattadvat kurvaty atra kriyocyate
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.009|3.9]] arthopādhivaśād yāti cintāmaṇir iveśvarī
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.010|3.10]] dvidhā ca navadhā caiva pañcāśaddhā ca mālinī
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.011|3.11]] kādibhiś ca smṛtā yonir navadhā vargabhedataḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.012|3.12]] bījam atra śivaḥ śaktir yonir ity abhidhīyate

### The Eight Mothers

- [[Malinivijayottara 03.013|3.13]] vargāṣṭakam iha jñeyam aghorādyam anukramāt
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.014|3.14]] māheśī brāhmaṇī caiva kaumārī vaiṣṇavī tathā

### The Fifty Rudras of the Alphabet

- [[Malinivijayottara 03.015|3.15]] śatārdhabhedabhinnānāṁ tatsaṁkhyānāṁ varānane
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.016|3.16]] tadvad eva ca śatīnāṁ tatsaṁkhyāṁnam anukramāt

### The Sixteen Rudras of the Seeds of Nectar

- [[Malinivijayottara 03.017|3.17]] amṛto 'mṛtapūrṇaś ca amṛtābho 'mṛtadravaḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.018|3.18]] amṛtāṅgo 'mṛtavapur amṛtodgāra eva ca
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.019|3.19]] tanmūrtir amṛteśaś ca sarvāmṛtadharo 'paraḥ

### The Thirty-four Rudras of the Consonants

- [[Malinivijayottara 03.020|3.20]] jayaś ca vijayaś caiva jayantaś cāparājitaḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.021|3.21]] jayamūrtir jayotsāho jayado jayavardhanaḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.022|3.22]] balāvahaś ca balavān baladātā baleśvaraḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.023|3.23]] sumanāḥ spṛhaṇo durgā bhadrakālo manonugaḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.024|3.24]] ete yonisamudbhūtāś catustriṁśat prakīrtitāḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.025|3.25]] bījayonisamudbhūtā rudraśaktisamāśrayāḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.026|3.26]] sarvaśāstrārthagarbhiṇyā ity evaṁ vidhayānayā
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.027|3.27]] sa tayā saṁprabuddhaḥ san yoniṁ vikṣobhya śaktibhiḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.028|3.28]] te tair āliṅgitāḥ santaḥ sarvakāmaphalapradāḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.029|3.29]] tair idaṁ saṁtataṁ viśvaṁ sadevāsuramānuṣam

### The Three Energies Aghorā, Ghorā and Ghoratarā

- [[Malinivijayottara 03.030|3.30]] anantasyāpi bhedasya śivaśakter mahātmanaḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.031|3.31]] viṣayeṣv eva saṁlīnān adho 'dhaḥ pātayanty aṇūn
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.032|3.32]] miśrakarmaphalāsaktiṁ pūrvavaj janayanti yāḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.033|3.33]] pūrvavaj jantujātasya śivadhāmaphalapradāḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.034|3.34]] etāḥ sarvāṇusaṁghātam api niṣṭhā yathā sthitāḥ

### The Mālinī Alphabet

- [[Malinivijayottara 03.035|3.35]] asyā vācakabhedena bhedo 'nyaḥ saṁpracakṣyate
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.036|3.36]] viśeṣavidhihīneṣu nyāsakarmasu mantravit
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.037|3.37]] na śikhā ṛṛ ḷ ḷ ca śiromālā tha mastakam
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.038|3.38]] vakavarga-i-ā vaktradantajihvāsu vāci ca
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.039|3.39]] ṭho hastayor jhañau śākhā jraṭau śūlakapālake
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.040|3.40]] tatparaḥ kathitaḥ prāṇaḥ ṣakṣāv udaranābhigau

### Parāparā Vidyā

- [[Malinivijayottara 03.041|3.41]] e-aikārau tathā jaṅghe tatparau caraṇau daphau
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.042|3.42]] sabindukāṁ dakṣajaṅghāṁ tato vācaṁ prakalpayet
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.043|3.43]] dakṣajānuyutaṁ daṇḍaṁ prāṇaṁ daṇḍastham īryutam
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.044|3.44]] usthitaṁ binduyuk prāṇaṁ pūrvavad daśanaṁ tataḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.045|3.45]] dakṣajānuyutaṁ hṛc ca prāṇaṁ jīvātmanā yutam
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.046|3.46]] nitambaṁ dakṣamudretaṁ dvitīyaṁ jihvayā dvijam
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.047|3.47]] punas tathaiva śikharaṁ jaṭharaṁ kevalaṁ tataḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.048|3.48]] nitambaṁ kevalaṁ nyasya hṛdayaṁ jihvayā yutam
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.049|3.49]] śūladaṇḍacatuṣkaṁ ca tatrādyaṁ dvayasaṁsthimusthitam
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.050|3.50]] tataḥ paramaghorāntaṁ pādyakādye ca pūrvavat

### Aparā Vidyā

- [[Malinivijayottara 03.051|3.51]] aghorāntaṁ nyased ādau prā aṁ binduyutaṁ punaḥ

### Parā Vidyā

- [[Malinivijayottara 03.052|3.52]] apareyaṁ samākhyātā rudraśaktiṁ parāṁ śṛṇu
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.053|3.53]] kampate gātrayaṣṭiś ca drutaṁ cotpatanaṁ bhavet
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.054|3.54]] atītānāgatārthasya kuryād vā kathanādikam
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.055|3.55]] pareyam anayā siddhiḥ sarvakāmaphalapradā
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.056|3.56]] rudraś ca rudraśaktiś ca guruś ceti trayaṁ samam
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.057|3.57]] śiṣyeṇāpi tadā grāhyā yadā saṁtoṣito guruḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.058|3.58]] bodhitā tu yadā tena guruṇā hṛṣṭacetasā

### The Yoginīs, Limbs and Faces of the Vidyās

- [[Malinivijayottara 03.059|3.59]] parāparāṅgasaṁbhūtā yoginyo 'ṣṭau mahābalāḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.060|3.60]] jñeyāḥ saptaikādaśārṇā ekārdhārṇadvayānvitā

### The Five Limbs of the Vidyās (vidyāṅga)

- [[Malinivijayottara 03.061|3.61]] vidyātrayasya gātrāṇi hrasvairvaktrāṇi paṇcabhiḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.062|3.62]] oṁ amṛte tejomālini svāhāpadavibhūṣitam 
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.063|3.63]] vedavedini hū.m phaṭ ca praṇavādisamanvita 
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.064|3.64]] vajriṇe vajradharāya svāhāntaṁ praṇavādikam

### The Pāśupata Weapon

- [[Malinivijayottara 03.065|3.65]] ślīṁpadaṁ  paśuśabdaṁ ca hūṁ phaḍantaṁ bhavādikam

### The Guardians of the Quarters and their Weapons

- [[Malinivijayottara 03.066|3.66]] laraṭakṣavayair dīrghaiḥ sūmāyuktaiḥ  sabindukaiḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.067|3.67]] tadvan nāsāpayobhyāṁ tu kalpyau viṣṇuprajāpatī
- [[Malinivijayottara 03.068|3.68]] iti mātṛgaśaḥ  proktaḥ sarvakāmaphalapradaḥ

## Closing

This is the third chapter of the *Mālinīvijayottara Tantra* concerning the extraction of Mantras.
