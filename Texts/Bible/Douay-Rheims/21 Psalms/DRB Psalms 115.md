# Psalms 115 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 114|← Psalms 114]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 116|Psalms 116 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 115|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**115:1** I have believed, therefore have I spoken; but I have been humbled exceedingly. ^115-1

**115:2** I said in my excess: Every man is a liar. ^115-2

**115:3** What shall I render to the Lord, for all the things he hath rendered unto me? ^115-3

**115:4** I will take the chalice of salvation; and I will call upon the name of the Lord. ^115-4

**115:5** I will pay my vows to the Lord before all his people: ^115-5

**115:6** Precious in the sight of the Lord is the death of his saints. ^115-6

**115:7** O Lord, for I am thy servant: I am thy servant, and the son of thy handmaid. Thou hast broken my bonds: ^115-7

**115:8** I will sacrifice to thee the sacrifice of praise, and I will call upon the name of the Lord. ^115-8

**115:9** I will pay my vows to the Lord in the sight of all his people: ^115-9

**115:10** In the courts of the house of the Lord, in the midst of thee, O Jerusalem. ^115-10
