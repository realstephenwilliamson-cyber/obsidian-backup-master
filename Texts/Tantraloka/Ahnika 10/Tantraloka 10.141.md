## Mūla

तद्वलाद्वेद्यतायोग्यभावेनैवात्र वेद्यता ।
तथाहि गाढनिद्रेऽपि प्रियेऽनाशङ्कितागताम् ॥ ^mula

tadvalādvedyatāyogyabhāvenaivātra vedyatā |
tathāhi gāḍhanidre'pi priye'nāśaṅkitāgatām || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 12 - Chapter Ten, part 3#^pg401|p. 382]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=401>))
>
> (What is meant) here by perceptibility is a condition that is favourable to perception. Thus, the power (bala) of the competence these two (perceivers) possess to be awake (and conscious) (bodhayogyatā), which is (implicit) in their future awakening, makes it possible for (the Earth and the other principles) to be perceptible to them. (with 10.140cd–141ab)
>
> The same is the case with the lover who comes to meet her beloved in secret (abhisārikā). Even though (her) beloved is in deep sleep, she thinks: “(When he awakens,) he will see me (and know that) I have come with confidence (in our love)”, and cannot contain herself (for joy). (with 10.141cd–142ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* एतदेव निदर्शयति -

> तथाहि गाढनिद्रेऽपि प्रियेऽनाशङ्कितागताम् ॥
> मां द्रक्ष्यतीति नाङ्गेषु स्वेषु मात्यभिसारिका ।

प्रियस्य गाढनिद्रामूढत्वाद्भाविनीमपि स्वात्मनि तद्वेद्यतां संभाव्याभिसारिकाया एवं संमदातिशय इत्यस्य निद्रितत्वेऽपि भावबोधसंबन्धनिबन्धनात्मिकया योग्यतया वेदितृत्वमस्ति येनैवमुक्तम् ॥ १४१ ॥
