# John 9 (NRSVue)

## A man born blind receives sight

**9:1** As he walked along, he saw a man blind from birth. ^9-1

**9:2** His disciples asked him, “Rabbi, who sinned, this man or his parents, that he was born blind?” ^9-2

**9:3** Jesus answered, “Neither this man nor his parents sinned; he was born blind so that God’s works might be revealed in him. ^9-3

**9:4** We^[Other ancient authorities read *I*] must work the works of him who sent me^[Other ancient authorities read *us*] while it is day; night is coming, when no one can work. ^9-4

**9:5** As long as I am in the world, I am the light of the world.” ^9-5

**9:6** When he had said this, he spat on the ground and made mud with the saliva and spread the mud on the man’s eyes, ^9-6

**9:7** saying to him, “Go, wash in the pool of Siloam” (which means Sent). Then he went and washed and came back able to see. ^9-7

**9:8** The neighbors and those who had seen him before as a beggar began to ask, “Is this not the man who used to sit and beg?” ^9-8

**9:9** Some were saying, “It is he.” Others were saying, “No, but it is someone like him.” He kept saying, “I am he.” ^9-9

**9:10** But they kept asking him, “Then how were your eyes opened?” ^9-10

**9:11** He answered, “The man called Jesus made mud, spread it on my eyes, and said to me, ‘Go to Siloam and wash.’ Then I went and washed and received my sight.” ^9-11

**9:12** They said to him, “Where is he?” He said, “I do not know.” ^9-12

## The Pharisees investigate the healing

**9:13** They brought to the Pharisees the man who had formerly been blind. ^9-13

**9:14** Now it was a Sabbath day when Jesus made the mud and opened his eyes. ^9-14

**9:15** Then the Pharisees also began to ask him how he had received his sight. He said to them, “He put mud on my eyes. Then I washed, and now I see.” ^9-15

**9:16** Some of the Pharisees said, “This man is not from God, for he does not observe the Sabbath.” Others said, “How can a man who is a sinner perform such signs?” And they were divided. ^9-16

**9:17** So they said again to the blind man, “What do you say about him? It was your eyes he opened.” He said, “He is a prophet.” ^9-17

**9:18** The Jews did not believe that he had been blind and had received his sight until they called the parents of the man who had received his sight ^9-18

**9:19** and asked them, “Is this your son, who you say was born blind? How then does he now see?” ^9-19

**9:20** His parents answered, “We know that this is our son and that he was born blind, ^9-20

**9:21** but we do not know how it is that now he sees, nor do we know who opened his eyes. Ask him; he is of age. He will speak for himself.” ^9-21

**9:22** His parents said this because they were afraid of the Jews, for the Jews had already agreed that anyone who confessed Jesus^[Gk *him*] to be the Messiah^[Or *the Christ*] would be put out of the synagogue. ^9-22

**9:23** Therefore his parents said, “He is of age; ask him.” ^9-23

**9:24** So for the second time they called the man who had been blind, and they said to him, “Give glory to God! We know that this man is a sinner.” ^9-24

**9:25** He answered, “I do not know whether he is a sinner. One thing I do know, that though I was blind, now I see.” ^9-25

**9:26** They said to him, “What did he do to you? How did he open your eyes?” ^9-26

**9:27** He answered them, “I have told you already, and you would not listen. Why do you want to hear it again? Do you also want to become his disciples?” ^9-27

**9:28** Then they reviled him, saying, “You are his disciple, but we are disciples of Moses. ^9-28

**9:29** We know that God has spoken to Moses, but as for this man, we do not know where he comes from.” ^9-29

**9:30** The man answered, “Here is an astonishing thing! You do not know where he comes from, yet he opened my eyes. ^9-30

**9:31** We know that God does not listen to sinners, but he does listen to one who worships him and obeys his will. ^9-31

**9:32** Never since the world began has it been heard that anyone opened the eyes of a person born blind. ^9-32

**9:33** If this man were not from God, he could do nothing.” ^9-33

**9:34** They answered him, “You were born entirely in sins, and are you trying to teach us?” And they drove him out. ^9-34

## Spiritual blindness

**9:35** Jesus heard that they had driven him out, and when he found him he said, “Do you believe in the Son of Man?”^[Other ancient authorities read *the Son of God*] ^9-35

**9:36** He answered, “And who is he, sir?^[Or *Lord*] Tell me, so that I may believe in him.” ^9-36

**9:37** Jesus said to him, “You have seen him, and the one speaking with you is he.” ^9-37

**9:38** He said, “Lord,^[Or *Sir*] I believe.” And he worshiped him. ^9-38

**9:39** Jesus said, “I came into this world for judgment, so that those who do not see may see and those who do see may become blind.” ^9-39

**9:40** Some of the Pharisees who were with him heard this and said to him, “Surely we are not blind, are we?” ^9-40

**9:41** Jesus said to them, “If you were blind, you would not have sin. But now that you say, ‘We see,’ your sin remains. ^9-41
