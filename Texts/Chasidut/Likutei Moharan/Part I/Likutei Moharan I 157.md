# Likutei Moharan I 157

← [[Likutei Moharan I 156]] · [[Likutei Moharan]] · [[Likutei Moharan I 158]] →

*Part I · Lesson 157* · [[Likutei Moharan]]

## 1

If a person would cleave to the words of Torah that emerge from the tzaddik’s lips, I wonder how he could afterwards bear and desire life in this world.

This is the meaning of ([[Pirkei Avot 04#^4-5|Avot 4:5]]): Whoever benefits from the words of Torah removes his life from this world. Understand this well.
