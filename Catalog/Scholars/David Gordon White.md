# David Gordon White

Distinguished Emeritus Professor of Religious Studies at the University of California, Santa Barbara; his areas are South Asian religions and the connected histories of Eurasian religions, and his books cover tantra, yoga and medieval Indian alchemy ([UCSB](https://www.religion.ucsb.edu/people/david-gordon-white)).
