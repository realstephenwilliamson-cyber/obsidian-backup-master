## Chapter 16

**16:1** A *michtam*[^1] of David.  
Protect me, O God, for I seek refuge in You. ^16-1

**16:2** I say to the LORD,  
“You are my Lord, my benefactor;  
there is none above You.”[^2] ^16-2

**16:3** [^3]As to the holy and mighty ones that are in the land,  
my whole desire concerning them is that ^16-3

**16:4** those who espouse another [god]  
may have many sorrows!  
I will have no part of their bloody libations;  
their names will not pass my lips. ^16-4

**16:5** The LORD is my allotted share and portion;[^4]  
You control my fate. ^16-5

**16:6** Delightful country has fallen to my lot;  
lovely indeed is my estate. ^16-6

**16:7** I bless the LORD who has guided me;  
my conscience[^5] admonishes me at night. ^16-7

**16:8** I am ever mindful of the LORD’s presence;  
He is at my right hand; I shall never be shaken. ^16-8

**16:9** So my heart rejoices,  
my whole being exults,  
and my body rests secure. ^16-9

**16:10** For You will not abandon me to Sheol,  
or let Your faithful one see the Pit. ^16-10

**16:11** You will teach me the path of life.  
In Your presence is perfect joy;  
delights are ever in Your right hand. ^16-11

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Others “I have no good but in You.”
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain; “holy and mighty ones” taken as epithets for divine beings; cf. qedoshim in Ps. 89.6, 8, and ’addirim in 1 Sam. 4.8.
[^4]: Lit. “cup.”
[^5]: Lit. “kidneys.”
