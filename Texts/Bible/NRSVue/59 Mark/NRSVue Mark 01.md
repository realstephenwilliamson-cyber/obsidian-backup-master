# Mark 1 (NRSVue)

— · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Mark 02|Mark 2 →]]

Compare: [[Mark 01|BSB and SBLGNT]] · [[DRB Mark 01|Douay-Rheims]] · [[JANT Mark 01|JANT notes]]

## The proclamation of John the Baptist

**1:1** The beginning of the good news^[Or *gospel*] of Jesus Christ.^[Other ancient authorities add *Son of God*] ^1-1

**1:2** As it is written in the prophet Isaiah,^[Other ancient authorities read *in the prophets*] “See, I am sending my messenger ahead of you,^[Gk *before your face*] who will prepare your way, ^1-2

**1:3** the voice of one crying out in the wilderness: ‘Prepare the way of the Lord; make his paths straight,’ ” ^1-3

**1:4** so John the baptizer appeared^[Other ancient authorities read *John was baptizing*] in the wilderness, proclaiming a baptism of repentance for the forgiveness of sins. ^1-4

**1:5** And the whole Judean region and all the people of Jerusalem were going out to him and were baptized by him in the River Jordan, confessing their sins. ^1-5

**1:6** Now John was clothed with camel’s hair, with a leather belt around his waist, and he ate locusts and wild honey. ^1-6

**1:7** He proclaimed, “The one who is more powerful than I is coming after me; I am not worthy to stoop down and untie the strap of his sandals. ^1-7

**1:8** I have baptized you with^[Or *in*] water, but he will baptize you with^[Or *in*] the Holy Spirit.” ^1-8

## The baptism of Jesus

**1:9** In those days Jesus came from Nazareth of Galilee and was baptized by John in the Jordan. ^1-9

**1:10** And just as he was coming up out of the water, he saw the heavens torn apart and the Spirit descending like a dove upon him. ^1-10

**1:11** And a voice came from the heavens, “You are my Son, the Beloved;^[Or *my beloved Son*] with you I am well pleased.” ^1-11

## The testing of Jesus

**1:12** And the Spirit immediately drove him out into the wilderness. ^1-12

**1:13** He was in the wilderness forty days, tested by Satan, and he was with the wild beasts, and the angels waited on him. ^1-13

## The beginning of the Galilean ministry

**1:14** Now after John was arrested, Jesus came to Galilee proclaiming the good news^[Or *gospel*] of^[Other ancient authorities add *of the kingdom of*] God ^1-14

**1:15** and saying, “The time is fulfilled, and the kingdom of God has come near;^[Or *is at hand*] repent, and believe in the good news.”^[Or *gospel*] ^1-15

## Jesus calls the first disciples

**1:16** As Jesus passed along the Sea of Galilee, he saw Simon and his brother Andrew casting a net into the sea, for they were fishers. ^1-16

**1:17** And Jesus said to them, “Follow me, and I will make you fishers of people.” ^1-17

**1:18** And immediately they left their nets and followed him. ^1-18

**1:19** As he went a little farther, he saw James son of Zebedee and his brother John, who were in their boat mending the nets. ^1-19

**1:20** Immediately he called them, and they left their father Zebedee in the boat with the hired men and followed him. ^1-20

## The man with an unclean spirit

**1:21** They went to Capernaum, and when the Sabbath came, he entered the synagogue and taught. ^1-21

**1:22** They were astounded at his teaching, for he taught them as one having authority and not as the scribes. ^1-22

**1:23** Just then there was in their synagogue a man with an unclean spirit, ^1-23

**1:24** and he cried out, “What have you to do with us, Jesus of Nazareth? Have you come to destroy us? I know who you are, the Holy One of God.” ^1-24

**1:25** But Jesus rebuked him, saying, “Be quiet and come out of him!” ^1-25

**1:26** And the unclean spirit, convulsing him and crying with a loud voice, came out of him. ^1-26

**1:27** They were all amazed, and they kept on asking one another, “What is this? A new teaching—with authority! He^[Or *A new teaching! With authority he*] commands even the unclean spirits, and they obey him.” ^1-27

**1:28** At once his fame began to spread throughout the surrounding region of Galilee. ^1-28

## Jesus heals many at Simon’s house

**1:29** As soon as they^[Other ancient authorities read *he*] left the synagogue, they entered the house of Simon and Andrew, with James and John. ^1-29

**1:30** Now Simon’s mother-in-law was in bed with a fever, and they told him about her at once. ^1-30

**1:31** He came and took her by the hand and lifted her up. Then the fever left her, and she began to serve them. ^1-31

**1:32** That evening, at sunset, they brought to him all who were sick or possessed by demons. ^1-32

**1:33** And the whole city was gathered around the door. ^1-33

**1:34** And he cured many who were sick with various diseases and cast out many demons, and he would not permit the demons to speak, because they knew him. ^1-34

## A preaching tour in Galilee

**1:35** In the morning, while it was still very dark, he got up and went out to a deserted place, and there he prayed. ^1-35

**1:36** And Simon and his companions hunted for him. ^1-36

**1:37** When they found him, they said to him, “Everyone is searching for you.” ^1-37

**1:38** He answered, “Let us go on to the neighboring towns, so that I may proclaim the message there also, for that is what I came out to do.” ^1-38

**1:39** And he went throughout all Galilee, proclaiming the message in their synagogues and casting out demons. ^1-39

## Jesus cleanses a man with a skin disease

**1:40** A man with a skin disease came to him begging him, and kneeling^[Other ancient authorities lack *kneeling*] he said to him, “If you are willing, you can make me clean.” ^1-40

**1:41** Moved with pity,^[Other ancient authorities read *anger*] Jesus^[Gk *he*] stretched out his hand and touched him and said to him, “I am willing. Be made clean!” ^1-41

**1:42** Immediately the skin disease left him, and he was made clean. ^1-42

**1:43** After sternly warning him he sent him away at once, ^1-43

**1:44** saying to him, “See that you say nothing to anyone, but go, show yourself to the priest, and offer for your cleansing what Moses commanded as a testimony to them.” ^1-44

**1:45** But he went out and began to proclaim it freely and to spread the word, so that Jesus^[Gk *he*] could no longer go into a town openly but stayed out in the country, and people came to him from every quarter. ^1-45
