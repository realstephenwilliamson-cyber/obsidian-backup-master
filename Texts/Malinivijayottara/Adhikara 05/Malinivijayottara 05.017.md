## Mūla

एतत्पत्यष्टकं प्रोक्तमतो गुह्यातिगुह्यकम् ।
तत्र भैरवकेदारमहाकालाः समध्यमाः ॥ ^mula

etat patyaṣṭakaṁ proktam ato guhyātiguhyakam |
tatra bhairavakedāramahākālāḥ samadhyamāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, July 2023 – September 2023
> p. 72 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=77>))
>
> A) 1) Lakulin, and 2) Bhārabhūti, (the two) 3) Diṇḍin and 4) Āṣāḍhin, along with 5) Puṣkara, 6) Naimiṣa, and 7) Prabhāsa and Amareśa who is the eighth. That is said to be the group of eight Lords (pratyaṣṭaka).[^34] (with 5.16–17ab)

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, July 2023 – September 2023
> p. 72 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=77>))
>
> B) There (the next group of eight consists of)[^35] 1) Bhairava, 2) Kedāra, 3) Mahākāla (Great Time), along with 4) Madhyama (Middle One), 5) Āmrātakeśa (Lord of Āmrātaka), 6) Jalpeśa (Lord of Chatter), 7) Śrīśaila along with 8) Harīndu.'[^36] (with 5.17cd–18ab)

[^34]: Read patyāṣṭakam for pratyātmakam in accord with TĀ 8/441d. MV 5/16-17ab quoted in TĀv ad 8/204cd- 205ab (204).
[^35]: According to TĀ 8/442 and 443 this is the secret group of eight secrets, whereas the following one is the group of eight great secrets.
[^36]: MV 5/17cd-18ab quoted in TĀv ad 8/205cd-206 (205-206ab). It is paraphrased in TĀ 8/442abc (441cd-442a). In the TĀ this is called the Group of Eight Secrets (guhyāṣṭaka) and the following one the Group of Eight Great Secrets (mahāguhyāṣṭaka).
