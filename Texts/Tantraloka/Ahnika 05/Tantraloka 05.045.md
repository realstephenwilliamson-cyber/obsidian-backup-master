## Mūla

प्राणोदये प्रमेये तु परानन्दं विभावयेत् ।
तत्रानन्तप्रमेयांशपूरणापाननिर्वृतः ॥ ^mula

prāṇodaye prameye tu parānandaṃ vibhāvayet |
tatrānantaprameyāṃśapūraṇāpānanirvṛtaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 03 - Chapter Five, part 1#^pg56|p. 41]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=56>))

> and (2) when the object of knowledge (arises in the form of apāna, the inhaled downward moving breath), he should contemplate the Bliss of the Other (Parānanda). (The yogi rests) satisfied there by drinking the plenitude (pāraṇa) of the infinite aspects of objectivity (prameyāṁśa), and having attained (gata) the Bliss of the Other (Parānanda), he should abide (there), adorned with the Moon of the downward moving breath (apāna). (with 5.45–46ab)
