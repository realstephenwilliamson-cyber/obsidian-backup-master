## Mūla

नपुंसकाः स्त्रियः शूद्रा ये चान्येऽपि तदर्थिनः ।
ते दीक्षायां न मीमांस्या ज्ञानकाले विचारयेत् ॥ ^mula

napuṃsakāḥ striyaḥ śūdrā ye cānye'pi tadarthinaḥ |
te dīkṣāyāṃ na mīmāṃsyā jñānakāle vicārayet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 14 - Chapter Twenty-Three#^pg324|p. 304]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=324>))
>
> “And that is said in the Jāāānottara (where we read that): ‘Brahmins, kṣatriyas, vaiśyas,” eunuchs, women, śūdras, as well as others who wish (to receive) that (Śaiva teaching), should not be examined (as to their competence) when (they are given) initiation; (rather that) should be examined when knowledge is being given (to them).’ The teacher who is said to be the rooted in knowledge (jñānamūla) should encourage one who performs the seven sacrifices (saptasatrin) (regardless of other considerations). (with 23.20cd–22ab)
