## Mūla

तत्स्थाने वृत्तिमन्तीति मतङ्गे गुरवो मम ।
नन्वन्यान्यपि कर्माणि सन्ति भूयांसि तत्कृते ॥ ^mula

tatsthāne vṛttimantīti mataṅge guravo mama |
nanvanyānyapi karmāṇi santi bhūyāṃsi tatkṛte || ^iast

> Therefore, my teachers of the Mataṅgatantra (such as Aniruddha) say that the organs of action, like the skin, pervade (all the body), but function primarily in their (own particular) place. (with 9.260cd–261ab)
>
> Therefore, my teachers of the Mataṅgatantra (such as Aniruddha) say that the organs of action, like the skin, pervade (all the body), but function primarily in their (own particular) place. (with 9.260cd–261ab)
>
> Surely, (one may object that the number of) organs of action is not fixed, because there are many other actions apart from these. For that reason, one should say that there are as many instruments of action (as there are types of action). (with 9.261cd–262ab)

## Viveka

> नन्वन्यान्यपि कर्माणि सन्ति भूयांसि तत्कृते ॥
> करणान्यपि वाच्यानि तथा चाक्षेष्वनिष्ठितिः ।
