## Mūla

तत्राप्यौपाधिकाद्भेदाल्लये भेदं परे विदुः ।
एवं तात्त्वेश्वरे वर्गे लीने सृष्टौ पुनः परे ॥ ^mula

tatrāpyaupādhikādbhedāllaye bhedaṃ pare viduḥ |
evaṃ tāttveśvare varge līne sṛṣṭau punaḥ pare || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 12 - Chapter Six, part 5#^pg404|p. 389]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=404>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 6.176cd–177ab.
>
> (Then,) those who reside beyond Māyā on the Pure Path dissolve into the Supreme Śiva. But according to others, there is duality (bheda) (even in this form of) reabsorption also, because (some) secondary, adjunct duality (aupādhikād bhedād) (persists) there. (with 6.175cd–176ab)
>
> Once the (whole) group of the Lords of the Principles (tattva) has dissolved away in this way, (others) who have attained that (status, by spiritual discipline) or by Śiva’s will, assume their position (sthāna) again in the next (new) creation. (with 6.176cd–177ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* नन्वेवमपवृक्तेषु पुनः सृष्टौ तत्र केषामधिकारः ? -- इत्याशङ्क्याह --

> एवं तात्त्वेश्वरे वर्गे लीने सृष्टौ पुनः परे ॥
> तत्साधकाः शिवेष्टा वा तत्स्थानमधिशेरते ।

तत्साधका इति -

"लोकधर्मिणमारोप्य मते भुवनभर्तरि ।"

इत्यादिना निरूपिताश्चर्यादिक्रमेण प्रेप्सिततत्तद्भुवनैश्वर्याः । शिवेष्टा इति, तदिच्छामात्रानुगृहीताः ॥ १७६ ॥
