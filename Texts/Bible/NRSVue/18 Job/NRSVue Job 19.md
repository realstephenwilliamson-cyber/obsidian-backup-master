# Job 19 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Job 18|← Job 18]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Job 20|Job 20 →]]

## Job replies: I know that my vindicator lives

**19:1** Then Job answered: ^19-1

**19:2** “How long will you torment me and break me in pieces with words? ^19-2

**19:3** These ten times you have cast reproach upon me; are you not ashamed to wrong me? ^19-3

**19:4** And even if it is true that I have erred, my error remains with me. ^19-4

**19:5** If indeed you magnify yourselves against me and make my humiliation an argument against me, ^19-5

**19:6** know then that God has put me in the wrong and closed his net around me. ^19-6

**19:7** Even when I cry out, ‘Violence!’ I am not answered; I call aloud, but there is no justice. ^19-7

**19:8** He has walled up my way so that I cannot pass, and he has set darkness upon my paths. ^19-8

**19:9** He has stripped my glory from me and taken the crown from my head. ^19-9

**19:10** He breaks me down on every side, and I am gone; he has uprooted my hope like a tree. ^19-10

**19:11** He has kindled his wrath against me and counts me as his adversary. ^19-11

**19:12** His troops come on together; they have thrown up siegeworks^[Cn: Heb *their way*] against me and encamp around my tent. ^19-12

**19:13** “He has put my family far from me, and my acquaintances are wholly estranged from me. ^19-13

**19:14** My relatives and my close friends have failed me; ^19-14

**19:15** the guests in my house have forgotten me; my female servants count me as a stranger; I have become an alien in their eyes. ^19-15

**19:16** I call to my servant, but he gives me no answer; I must myself plead with him. ^19-16

**19:17** My breath is repulsive to my wife; I am loathsome to my own family. ^19-17

**19:18** Even young children despise me; when I rise, they talk against me. ^19-18

**19:19** All my intimate friends abhor me, and those whom I love have turned against me. ^19-19

**19:20** My bones cling to my skin and to my flesh, and I have escaped by the skin of my teeth. ^19-20

**19:21** Have pity on me, have pity on me, O you my friends, for the hand of God has touched me! ^19-21

**19:22** Why do you, like God, pursue me, never satisfied with my flesh? ^19-22

**19:23** “O that my words were written down! O that they were inscribed in a book! ^19-23

**19:24** O that with an iron pen and with lead they were engraved on a rock forever! ^19-24

**19:25** For I know that my vindicator^[Or *redeemer*] lives and that in the end he will stand upon the earth; ^19-25

**19:26** and after my skin has been destroyed, then in my flesh I shall see God, ^19-26

**19:27** whom I shall see on my side, and my eyes shall behold, and not another. My heart faints within me! ^19-27

**19:28** If you say, ‘How we will persecute him!’ and, ‘The root of the matter is found in him,’ ^19-28

**19:29** be afraid of the sword, for wrath brings the punishment of the sword, so that you may know there is a judgment.” ^19-29
