# LXX Psalms 69

← [[LXX Psalms 68]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 70]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 070]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 070]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**69:1** For the end, by David for a remembrance, that the Lord may save me.  ^69-1

**69:2** Draw nigh, O God, to my help.  ^69-2

**69:3** Let them be ashamed and confounded that seek my soul: let them be turned backward and put to shame, that wish me evil.  ^69-3

**69:4** Let them that say to me, Aha, aha, be turned back and put to shame immediately.  ^69-4

**69:5** Let all that seek thee exult and be glad in thee: and let those that love thy salvation say continually, Let God be magnified.  ^69-5

**69:6** But I am poor and needy; O God, help me: thou art my helper and deliverer, O Lord, delay not.  ^69-6
