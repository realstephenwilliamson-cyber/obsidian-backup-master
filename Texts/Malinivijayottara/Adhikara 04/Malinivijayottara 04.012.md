## Mūla

एवमुक्तो महादेव्या भैरवो भूरिभोगदः ।
स्फुरद्धिमांशुसंतानप्रकाशित दिगन्तरः ॥ ^mula

evam ukto mahādevyā bhairavo bhūribhogadaḥ |
sphuraddhimāṁśusaṁtānaprakāśita digantaraḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, April 2023 – June 2023
> p. 18 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=59>))
>
> Once the Great Goddess had said this, Bhairava who bestows many worldly benefits, he who illumines the regions with the spreading forth by (his) resplendent lunar rays and whose playful rule is garlanded with the crowned heads of gods and demons (bowing before him), uttered these sweet words (inspired by a pure) unhampered intention. (with 4.12–13)
