# 1 Enoch 95 (Charles)

[[1 Enoch 094|← 1 Enoch 94]] · [[1 Enoch|index]] · [[1 Enoch 096|1 Enoch 96 →]]

## XCV. Enoch’s Grief: fresh Woes against the Sinners.

**95:1** Oh that mine eyes were [a cloud of] waters
That I might weep over you,
And pour down my tears as a cloud †of† waters:
That so I might rest from my trouble of heart! ^95-1

**95:2** †Who has permitted you to practise reproaches and wickedness?
And so judgement shall overtake you, sinners.† ^95-2

**95:3** Fear not the sinners, ye righteous;
For again will the Lord deliver them into your hands,
That ye may execute judgement upon them according to your desires. ^95-3

**95:4** Woe to you who fulminate anathemas which cannot be reversed:
Healing shall therefore be far from you because of your sins. ^95-4

**95:5** Woe to you who requite your neighbour with evil;
For ye shall be requited according to your works. ^95-5

**95:6** Woe to you, lying witnesses,
And to those who weigh out injustice,
For suddenly shall ye perish. ^95-6

**95:7** Woe to you, sinners, for ye persecute the righteous;
For ye shall be delivered up and persecuted because of injustice,
And heavy shall its yoke be upon you. ^95-7
