## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. I
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 01 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v01 07 - Chapter One, part 6#^pg256|p. 242]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 01.pdf#page=256>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 1.92.
>
> (Śiva) manifests free of obscuration. He manifests having obscured His own nature. He manifests as both (partially) obscured and unobscured. By associating with duality (and diversity He manifests) (bhedasaṁgamāt) in many ways. Thus, there are three powers within the Lord (corresponding to these three conditions). (My venerable) teacher has clearly elucidated (their nature) as being those named will, (knowledge and action), otherwise (collectively) called (His) (creative) freedom (svātantrya). (with 1.93–94)
