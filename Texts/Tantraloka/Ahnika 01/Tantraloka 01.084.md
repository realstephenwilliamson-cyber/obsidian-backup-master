## Mūla

आत्मैव धर्म इत्युक्तः शिवामृतपरिप्लुतः ।
प्रकाशावस्थितं ज्ञानं भावाभावादिमध्यतः ॥ ^mula

ātmaiva dharma ityuktaḥ śivāmṛtapariplutaḥ |
prakāśāvasthitaṃ jñānaṃ bhāvābhāvādimadhyataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. I
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 01 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v01 07 - Chapter One, part 6#^pg239|p. 225]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 01.pdf#page=239>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 1.83.
>
> It is said that (the inherent) nature (dharma) (of the ‘group’) is the (unconditioned) Self itself, flooded with Śiva’s nectar. (with 1.84ab)
>
> One should know that the abiding (of consciousness) within its own abode is the knowledge (of reality) (jñāna) present in the Light (of consciousness that shines) in the centre between (any two things,) such as the existence and non-existence (of any one thing). (One should know that) it is (the pure) subjectivity (draṣṭṛtva) from which (every obscuring) covering has fallen away. Stainless by virtue of (that) pure consciousness, which is said to be a separate (transcendental) reality (viviktavastu), it is called (the state of) ‘abiding within the state of the nature of the (knowledge of the) group (grāmadharmavṛtti). He who possesses (that state) achieves everything. (with 1.84cd–86ab)
