## Chapter 70

**70:1** For the leader. Of David. *Lehazkir*.[^1] ^70-1

**70:2** [^2]Hasten, O God, to save me;  
O LORD, to aid me! ^70-2

**70:3** Let those who seek my life  
be frustrated and disgraced;  
let those who wish me harm,  
fall back in shame. ^70-3

**70:4** Let those who say, “Aha! Aha!”  
turn back because of their frustration. ^70-4

**70:5** But let all who seek You be glad and rejoice in You;  
let those who are eager for Your deliverance always say,  
“Extolled be God!” ^70-5

**70:6** But I am poor and needy;  
O God, hasten to me!  
You are my help and my rescuer;  
O LORD, do not delay. ^70-6

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Cf. Ps. 40.14–18.
