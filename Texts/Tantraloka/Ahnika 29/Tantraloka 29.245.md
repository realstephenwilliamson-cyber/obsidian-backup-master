## Mūla

हृल्लक्ष्ये वा महेशानि बिन्दुं ज्वालाकुलप्रभम् ।
तेन संबोधयेत्साध्यं बिन्द्वाख्योऽयं प्रकीर्तितः ॥ ^mula

hṛllakṣye vā maheśāni binduṃ jvālākulaprabham |
tena saṃbodhayetsādhyaṃ bindvākhyo'yaṃ prakīrtitaḥ || ^iast

> 3) The mind is present in the location of the Bulb (in the base of Suṣumnā). It is situated on the path between the eyebrows, or else, O Great Goddess, in the target within the Heart. (The teacher) should awaken (his) disciple (sādhya)) with that Drop (in these places), its light (brilliant with blazing) flames. (This) is said to be (the initiation) called (the Piercing with) the Point. (with 29.244cd–245)
