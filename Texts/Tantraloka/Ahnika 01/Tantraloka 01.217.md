## Mūla

एवं वैकल्पिकी भूमिः शाक्ते कर्तृत्ववेदने ।
यस्यां स्फुटे परं त्वस्यां सङ्कोचः पूर्वनीतितः ॥ ^mula

evaṃ vaikalpikī bhūmiḥ śākte kartṛtvavedane |
yasyāṃ sphuṭe paraṃ tvasyāṃ saṅkocaḥ pūrvanītitaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. I
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 01 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v01 11 - Chapter One, part 10#^pg409|p. 395]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 01.pdf#page=409>))
>
> Such is the plane of thought constructs (operant) in the Empowered (means), in which (the individual subject’s) agency (kartṛtva) and cognitive awareness (vedana) are clearly evident (to him). However, for the aforestated reasons, on this (plane, consciousness) is contracted. One who is intent in this way on dissolving away the burden of (this) contraction possesses that blazing power (of consciousness) that brings about the inner manifestation as desired. (with 1.217–218)
