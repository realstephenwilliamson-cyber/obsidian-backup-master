## Mūla

अकृत्वा तं समाचारं पुनश्चक्रं प्रपूजयेत् ।
अथ वच्मि गुरोः शास्त्रव्याख्याक्रममुदाहृतम् ॥ ^mula

akṛtvā taṃ samācāraṃ punaścakraṃ prapūjayet |
atha vacmi guroḥ śāstravyākhyākramamudāhṛtam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 07 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 5#^pg191|p. 174]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=191>))
>
> If such a one who is engaged in the common observance (of Vedic norms) (lokācāra) (happens to have) entered (the gathering, the officiant should interrupt the rite). Without completing that common (basic) practice (samācāra) (at that time), he should worship the assembly (cakra) again (later). (with 28.384cd–385ab)
>
> Now I will talk about the (systematic) manner (krama) in which the scripture is explained, (as I was) taught by (my) teacher, (and is expounded) in the Devyāyāāmalaśāstra and elsewhere by Śiva (Himself). (with 28.385cd–386ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* इदानीं क्रमप्राप्तं व्याख्याविधिं वक्तुं प्रतिजानीते--

> अथ वच्मि गुरोः शास्त्रव्याख्याक्रममुदाहृतम् ॥
> देव्यायामलशास्त्रादौ तुहिनाभीशुमौलिना ।
