## Mūla

तोयं विनिक्षिपेन्मूर्ध्नि मन्त्रस्नानाय मन्त्रवित् ।
रजसा गोधुतेनैव वायव्यं स्नानमाचरेत् ॥ ^mula

toyaṁ vinikṣipen mūrdhni mantrasnānāya mantravit |
rajasā godhutenaiva[^9] vāyavyaṁ snānam ācaret || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, October 2024 – December 2024
> p. 16 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=15>))
>
> 3) In order to (perform) the ablution with mantra, the knower of mantra should (first) have bathed in accordance with the common procedure, and should (then) throw on (his head) water that has been consecrated with the three Vidyās (of Parā, Parāparā and Aparā). (with 8.10cd–11ab)

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, October 2024 – December 2024
> p. 16 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=15>))
>
> 4) He should perform the ablution with Air by means of the dust shaken off (from the body of a) cow. Immersed in meditation and uttering the Great Weapon, he should walk seven paces and then again return back for that same (amount), recollecting (The Vidyā of) Parāparā.[^10] (with 8.11cd–12)

[^9]: taṁ; lo: kṛteti
[^10]: MV 8/11cd-12 quoted TĀv ad 15/56.
