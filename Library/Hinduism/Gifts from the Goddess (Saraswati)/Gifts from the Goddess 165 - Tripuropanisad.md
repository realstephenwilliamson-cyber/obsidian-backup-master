Tripurōpaniṣad

Pañcadaśī

kāmō ka yōniḥ ē kāmakalā i vajrapāṇir la guhā hrīm *(guhā = cave; the yoni is implied)*

hasā ha sa mātariśva ka abhram ha indraḥ la punarguhā hrīm *(again)*

sakalā sa ka la

Knowing this Tripurasundarī *vidyā*, people forget their inhibitions and enter into the Ocean of Nectar, which floods their genitals (*svapīṭhā*). They live in heavenly greatness in the ultimate abode of Tripura.
