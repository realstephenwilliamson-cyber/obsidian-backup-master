# Job 38

← [[Job 37]] · [[Tanakh]] · [[Job 39]] →

Compare: [[NRSVue Job 38|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 38

**38:1** Then the LORD replied to Job out of the tempest and said: ^38-1

**38:2** Who is this who darkens counsel,  
Speaking without knowledge? ^38-2

**38:3** Gird your loins like a man;  
I will ask and you will inform Me. ^38-3

**38:4** Where were you when I laid the earth’s foundations?  
Speak if you have understanding. ^38-4

**38:5** Do you know who fixed its dimensions  
Or who measured it with a line? ^38-5

**38:6** Onto what were its bases sunk?  
Who set its cornerstone ^38-6

**38:7** When the morning stars sang together  
And all the divine beings shouted for joy? ^38-7

**38:8** Who closed the sea behind doors  
When it gushed forth out of the womb, ^38-8

**38:9** When I clothed it in clouds,  
Swaddled it in dense clouds, ^38-9

**38:10** When I made breakers My limit for it,  
And set up its bar and doors, ^38-10

**38:11** And said, “You may come so far and no farther;  
Here your surging waves will stop”? ^38-11

**38:12** Have you ever commanded the day to break,  
Assigned the dawn its place, ^38-12

**38:13** So that it seizes the corners of the earth  
And shakes the wicked out of it? ^38-13

**38:14** It changes like clay under the seal  
Till [its hues] are fixed like those of a garment. ^38-14

**38:15** Their light is withheld from the wicked,  
And the upraised arm is broken. ^38-15

**38:16** Have you penetrated to the sources of the sea,  
Or walked in the recesses of the deep? ^38-16

**38:17** Have the gates of death been disclosed to you?  
Have you seen the gates of deep darkness? ^38-17

**38:18** Have you surveyed the expanses of the earth?  
If you know of these—tell Me. ^38-18

**38:19** Which path leads to where light dwells,  
And where is the place of darkness, ^38-19

**38:20** That you may take it to its domain  
And know the way to its home? ^38-20

**38:21** Surely you know, for you were born then,  
And the number of your years is many! ^38-21

**38:22** Have you penetrated the vaults of snow,  
Seen the vaults of hail, ^38-22

**38:23** Which I have put aside for a time of adversity,  
For a day of war and battle? ^38-23

**38:24** By what path is the west wind[^1] dispersed,  
The east wind scattered over the earth? ^38-24

**38:25** Who cut a channel for the torrents  
And a path for the thunderstorms, ^38-25

**38:26** To rain down on uninhabited land,  
On the wilderness where no man is, ^38-26

**38:27** To saturate the desolate wasteland,  
And make the crop of grass sprout forth? ^38-27

**38:28** Does the rain have a father?  
Who begot the dewdrops? ^38-28

**38:29** From whose belly came forth the ice?  
Who gave birth to the frost of heaven? ^38-29

**38:30** Water congeals like stone,  
And the surface of the deep compacts. ^38-30

**38:31** Can you tie cords to Pleiades  
Or undo the reins of Orion? ^38-31

**38:32** Can you lead out Mazzaroth[^2] in its season,  
Conduct the Bear with her sons? ^38-32

**38:33** Do you know the laws of heaven  
Or impose its authority on earth? ^38-33

**38:34** Can you send up an order to the clouds  
For an abundance of water to cover you? ^38-34

**38:35** Can you dispatch the lightning on a mission  
And have it answer you, “I am ready”? ^38-35

**38:36** Who put wisdom in the hidden parts?  
Who gave understanding to the mind?[^3] ^38-36

**38:37** Who is wise enough to give an account of the heavens?  
Who can tilt the bottles of the sky, ^38-37

**38:38** Whereupon the earth melts into a mass,  
And its clods stick together. ^38-38

**38:39** Can you hunt prey for the lion,  
And satisfy the appetite of the king of beasts? ^38-39

**38:40** They crouch in their dens,  
Lie in ambush in their lairs. ^38-40

**38:41** Who provides food for the raven  
When his young cry out to God  
And wander about without food? ^38-41

[^1]: As Aramaic ’urya.
[^2]: Evidently a constellation.
[^3]: Or “rooster”; meaning of Heb. uncertain.
