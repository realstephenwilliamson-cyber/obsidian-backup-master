# Alon Goshen-Gottstein

Founder and director of the Elijah Interfaith Institute, a scholar of Jewish studies who has held academic posts at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem and Tel Aviv University and directed the Center for the Study of Rabbinic Thought at Beit Morasha; he works on interreligious relations and Jewish theology of religions ([Wipf and Stock](https://wipfandstock.com/author/alon-goshen-gottstein); [Berkley Center, Georgetown](https://berkleycenter.georgetown.edu/people/alon-goshen-gottstein)).
