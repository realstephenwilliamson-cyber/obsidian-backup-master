# Psalm 106 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 105|← Psalm 105]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 107|Psalm 107 →]]

## A confession of Israel’s sins

**106:1** Praise the LORD! O give thanks to the LORD, for he is good, for his steadfast love endures forever. ^106-1

**106:2** Who can utter the mighty doings of the LORD or declare all his praise? ^106-2

**106:3** Happy are those who observe justice, who do righteousness at all times. ^106-3

**106:4** Remember us,^[Heb mss Gk: MT *me*] O LORD, when you show favor to your people; help us^[Heb mss Gk: MT *me*] when you deliver them, ^106-4

**106:5** that we may see the prosperity of your chosen ones, that we may rejoice in the gladness of your nation, that we may glory in your heritage. ^106-5

**106:6** Both we and our ancestors have sinned; we have committed iniquity, have done wickedly. ^106-6

**106:7** Our ancestors, when they were in Egypt, did not consider your wonderful works; they did not remember the abundance of your steadfast love but rebelled against the Most High^[Cn: Heb *rebelled at the sea*] at the Red Sea.^[Or *Sea of Reeds*] ^106-7

**106:8** Yet he saved them for his name’s sake, so that he might make known his mighty power. ^106-8

**106:9** He rebuked the Red Sea,^[Or *Sea of Reeds*] and it became dry; he led them through the deep as through a desert. ^106-9

**106:10** So he saved them from the hand of the foe and delivered them from the hand of the enemy. ^106-10

**106:11** The waters covered their adversaries; not one of them was left. ^106-11

**106:12** Then they believed his words; they sang his praise. ^106-12

**106:13** But they soon forgot his works; they did not wait for his counsel. ^106-13

**106:14** But they had a wanton craving in the wilderness and put God to the test in the desert; ^106-14

**106:15** he gave them what they asked but sent a wasting disease among them. ^106-15

**106:16** They were jealous of Moses in the camp and of Aaron, the holy one of the LORD. ^106-16

**106:17** The earth opened and swallowed up Dathan and covered the faction of Abiram. ^106-17

**106:18** Fire also broke out in their company; the flame burned up the wicked. ^106-18

**106:19** They made a calf at Horeb and worshiped a cast image. ^106-19

**106:20** They exchanged the glory of God^[Compare Gk mss: Heb *exchanged their glory*] for the image of an ox that eats grass. ^106-20

**106:21** They forgot God, their Savior, who had done great things in Egypt, ^106-21

**106:22** wondrous works in the land of Ham, and awesome deeds by the Red Sea.^[Or *Sea of Reeds*] ^106-22

**106:23** Therefore he said he would destroy them—had not Moses, his chosen one, stood in the breach before him, to turn away his wrath from destroying them. ^106-23

**106:24** Then they despised the pleasant land, having no faith in his promise. ^106-24

**106:25** They grumbled in their tents and did not obey the voice of the LORD. ^106-25

**106:26** Therefore he raised his hand and swore to them that he would make them fall in the wilderness ^106-26

**106:27** and would disperse^[Syr: Heb *cause to fall*] their descendants among the nations, scattering them over the lands. ^106-27

**106:28** Then they attached themselves to the Baal of Peor and ate sacrifices offered to the dead; ^106-28

**106:29** they provoked the LORD to anger with their deeds, and a plague broke out among them. ^106-29

**106:30** Then Phinehas stood up and interceded, and the plague was stopped. ^106-30

**106:31** And that has been reckoned to him as righteousness from generation to generation forever. ^106-31

**106:32** They angered the LORD^[Heb *him*] at the waters of Meribah, and it went ill with Moses on their account, ^106-32

**106:33** for they made his spirit bitter, and he spoke words that were rash. ^106-33

**106:34** They did not destroy the peoples, as the LORD had commanded them, ^106-34

**106:35** but they mingled with the nations and learned to do as they did. ^106-35

**106:36** They served their idols, which became a snare to them. ^106-36

**106:37** They sacrificed their sons and their daughters to the demons; ^106-37

**106:38** they poured out innocent blood, the blood of their sons and daughters, whom they sacrificed to the idols of Canaan, and the land was polluted with blood. ^106-38

**106:39** Thus they became unclean by their acts and prostituted themselves in their doings. ^106-39

**106:40** Then the anger of the LORD was kindled against his people, and he abhorred his heritage; ^106-40

**106:41** he gave them into the hand of the nations, so that those who hated them ruled over them. ^106-41

**106:42** Their enemies oppressed them, and they were brought into subjection under their power. ^106-42

**106:43** Many times he delivered them, but they were rebellious in their purposes and were brought low through their iniquity. ^106-43

**106:44** Nevertheless, he regarded their distress when he heard their cry. ^106-44

**106:45** For their sake he remembered his covenant and showed compassion according to the abundance of his steadfast love. ^106-45

**106:46** He caused them to be pitied by all who held them captive. ^106-46

**106:47** Save us, O LORD our God, and gather us from among the nations, that we may give thanks to your holy name and glory in your praise. ^106-47

**106:48** Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, from everlasting to everlasting. And let all the people say, “Amen.” Praise the LORD! *(Psalms 107–150)* ^106-48
