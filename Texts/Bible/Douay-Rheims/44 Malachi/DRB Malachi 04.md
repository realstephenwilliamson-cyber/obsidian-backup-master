# Malachi 4 (Douay-Rheims)

*Malachias 4 in the Douay-Rheims*

[[DRB Malachi 03|← Malachi 3]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · —

**4:1** For behold the day shall come kindled as a furnace: and all the proud, and all that do wickedly shall be stubble: and the day that cometh shall set them on fire, saith the Lord of hosts, it shall not leave them root, nor branch. ^4-1

**4:2** But unto you that fear my name, the Sun of justice shall arise, and health in his wings: and you shall go forth, and shall leap like calves of the herd. ^4-2

**4:3** And you shall tread down the wicked when they shall be ashes under the sole of your feet in the day that I do this, saith the Lord of hosts. ^4-3

**4:4** Remember the law of Moses my servant, which I commanded him in Horeb for all Israel, the precepts, and judgments. ^4-4

**4:5** Behold I will send you Elias the prophet, before the coming of the great and dreadful day of the Lord. ^4-5

**4:6** And he shall turn the heart of the fathers to the children, and the heart of the children to their fathers: lest I come, and strike the earth with anathema. ^4-6
