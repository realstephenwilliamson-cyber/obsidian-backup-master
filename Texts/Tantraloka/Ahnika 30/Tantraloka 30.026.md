## Mūla

देवताचक्रविन्यासः स बहुत्वान्न लिप्यते ।
माया विसर्गिणी हुं फट् चेति मन्त्रोऽपरात्मकः ॥ ^mula

devatācakravinyāsaḥ sa bahutvānna lipyate |
māyā visargiṇī huṃ phaṭ ceti mantro'parātmakaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 02 - Chapter Thirty, part 1#^pg37|p. 19]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=37>))
>
> The deposition of the assembly of deities corresponding to each letter (of the Parāparā Vidyā) is also described in the venerable Siddhayogīśvarīmata. It is very long, and so is not written (here). (with 30.25cd–26ab)
>
> The Mantra which is the nature of Aparā consists of (the seed syllable of) Māyā, that has visarga (HRĪṆ) (along with) HŪM and PHAT. (with 30.26cd)
