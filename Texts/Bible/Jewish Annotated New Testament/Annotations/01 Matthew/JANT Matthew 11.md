### 11.1–19: John questions Jesus

*[[NRSVue Matthew 11#^11-1|Text]], p. 31* ^11-1

**[[NRSVue Matthew 11#^11-1|1]]:** *Finished instructing*, Jesus does not send the disciples on an independent mission (contrast [[Mark 03#^3-13|Mk 3.13]]; [[Luke 07#^7-18|Lk 7.18–35]]). ^11-1-2

**[[NRSVue Matthew 11#^11-2|2–3]]:** *Prison*, Josephus locates John’s imprisonment at Macherus, five miles east of the Dead Sea (*Ant.* 18.116–19). *Messiah*, see 3.13–17. *By his disciples*, 9.14n.; 14.12. ^11-2

**[[NRSVue Matthew 11#^11-4|4–5]]:** *What you hear and see*, Jesus fulfills some messianic expectations, although beliefs concerning who the Messiah was and what would happen when he arrived varied ([[Isaiah 35#^35-4|Isa 35.4–6]],[[Isaiah 35#^35-9|9–10]]; see also [[Isaiah 26#^26-19|Isa 26.19]]; [[Isaiah 29#^29-18|29.18–19]]; cf. *2 Bar.* 29.6; 4Q521; 11QMelch 18). See “Messianic Movements,” p. 622. Matthew diseases e Or *Why then did you go out? To see someone* omits “freeing the captive” ([[Isaiah 61#^61-1|Isa 61.1]]). Jesus may avoid answering directly since a report to John in prison saying, “I am the Messiah” could lead to Jesus’ arrest as well. ^11-4

**[[NRSVue Matthew 11#^11-10|10]]:** See [[Malachi 3#^3-1|Mal 3.1]]. ^11-10

**[[NRSVue Matthew 11#^11-11|11]]:** *Born of women*, mortals; see [[Job 14#^14-1|Job 14.1]]; [[Job 15#^15-14|15.14]]; [[Job 25#^25-4|25.4]]; [[Shabbat 88#88b|*b. Shabb*. 88b]]. *Least in the kingdom*, i.e., all of Jesus’ followers who are in the kingdom of heaven. ^11-11

**[[NRSVue Matthew 11#^11-13|13]]:** *Prophets and the law*, Israel’s Scripture, regarded as anticipating the Messiah ([[Acts 03#^3-24|Acts 3.24]]; [[Berakhot 34#34b|*b. Ber*. 34b]], “All the prophets prophesied only for the days of the Messiah”; see also *b. Shabb* 63a; [[Sanhedrin 99#99a|*b. Sanh*. 99a]]). Tertullian (*Pud*. 8) understood that John the Baptist’s arrival negated Torah. ^11-13

**[[NRSVue Matthew 11#^11-14|14–15]]:** See [[John 01#^1-21|Jn 1.21]]. *Elijah*, forerunner of the messianic age but not, in [[Malachi 3#^3-23|Mal 4.4–5]] \[HB 3.23\], of a Messiah; see also [[LXX Sirach 48#^48-10|Sir 48.10]]. In [[Sanhedrin 98#98a|*b. Sanh.* 98a]], Elijah is questioned by the rabbis concerning his arrival as the expected Messiah. ^11-14

**[[NRSVue Matthew 11#^11-18|18]]:** *Neither eating nor drinking*, engaging in ascetic practices. ^11-18

**[[NRSVue Matthew 11#^11-19|19]]:** *Son of Man*, see 8.20n. *Glutton and a drunkard*, allusion to the rebellious son ([[Torah 49 - Ki Teitzei#^21-20|Deut 21.20]]). *Tax collectors and sinners*, see 5.46n.; 9.10n. Wisdom (Gk “Sophia”; Heb “hokhmah”), the feminine manifestation of the divine (Prov 1–9; [[LXX Wisdom of Solomon 07#^7-21|Wis 7.21–8.1]]). ^11-19

### 11.20–24: Jesus condemns three cities

*[[NRSVue Matthew 11#^11-20|Text]], p. 32* · ([[Luke 10#^10-13|Lk 10.13]]**–**15). ^11-20

**[[NRSVue Matthew 11#^11-21|21]]:** *Woe to you*, a prophetic formula ([[Isaiah 05#^5-11|Isa 5.11–17]]; [[Isaiah 23#^23-1|23.1–12]]; [[Isaiah 29#^29-15|29.15–21]]). *Chorazin . . . Bethsaida*, Jewish cities on the northern tip of the Sea of Galilee, near Capernaum (see [[Mark 06#^6-45|Mk 6.45]]; [[Mark 08#^8-22|8.22]]; [[Luke 09#^9-10|Lk 9.10–17]]; [[John 01#^1-44|Jn 1.44]]). *Tyre and Sidon*, Mediterranean cities rebuked for extravagant wealth (Isa 23; Ezek 28; cf. [[Jeremiah 25#^25-22|Jer 25.22]]; [[Jeremiah 27#^27-3|27.3]]; [[Jeremiah 47#^47-4|47.4]]; [[Joel 4#^4-4|Joel 3.4–7]]; [[Zechariah 09#^9-1|Zech 9.1–4]]). ^11-21

**[[NRSVue Matthew 11#^11-23|23]]:** *Capernaum*, see 4.13n. *Hades*, the realm of the dead, synonymous with Gehenna (see 5.22n.). *Sodom*, see 10.15n. ^11-23

### 11.25–30: Jesus’ authority

*[[NRSVue Matthew 11#^11-25|Text]], p. 32* · ([[Luke 10#^10-21|Lk 10.21–22]]). ^11-25

**[[NRSVue Matthew 11#^11-25|25]]:** See 10.42; 18.10. *Infants,* Jewish tradition speaks of teaching children (or the “simple”) ([[Torah 45 - Vaetchanan#^6-7|Deut 6.7]]; [[Psalms 008#^8-3|Ps 8.3]]; [[Psalms 019#^19-8|19.7]]; 114.6; [[Proverbs 01#^1-4|Prov 1.4]]; 1QH 7.21–22). ^11-25-2

**[[NRSVue Matthew 11#^11-27|27]]:** Jesus claims unique knowledge of the divine. ^11-27
