## Mūla

प्रकर्तव्या यथा दीक्षा श्रीसन्तत्यागमोदिता ।
कथ्यते हाटकेशानपातालाधिपचोदिता ॥ ^mula

prakartavyā yathā dīkṣā śrīsantatyāgamoditā |
kathyate hāṭakeśānapātālādhipacoditā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 03 - Chapter Thirty, part 2#^pg75|p. 57]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=75>))
>
> I will (now) teach how the initiation should be performed as enjoined by Haṭakeśāna, lord of the netherworlds, in the Śrīsantatyāgama, for those who do not have money but are truly devoted, and those who, because of the defect of space and time etc., do not understand (things) in this way (as they truly are). (with 30.100–101)

## Viveka

> [!viveka]- Viveka in English (Dyczkowski, vol. XI, p. 57)
> ![[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 03 - Chapter Thirty, part 2#Viveka on 30.100–101]]

> तुलामेलकयोगः श्रीतन्त्रसद्भावशासने ।
> य उक्तः शम्भुनाथेन स मया दर्शितः क्रमात् ॥
> अथ वित्तविहीनानां प्रपन्नानां च तत्त्वतः ।
> देशकालादिदोषेण न तथाध्यवसायिनाम् ॥
> प्रकर्तव्या यथा दीक्षा श्रीसन्तत्यागमोदिता ।
> कथ्यते हाटकेशानपातालाधिपचोदिता ॥
