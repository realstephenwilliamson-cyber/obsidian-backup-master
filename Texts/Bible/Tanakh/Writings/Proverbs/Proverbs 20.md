# Proverbs 20

← [[Proverbs 19]] · [[Tanakh]] · [[Proverbs 21]] →

Compare: [[NRSVue Proverbs 20|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 20

**20:1** Wine is a scoffer, strong drink a roisterer;  
He who is muddled by them will not grow wise. ^20-1

**20:2** The terror of a king is like the roar of a lion;  
He who provokes his anger risks his life. ^20-2

**20:3** It is honorable for a man to desist from strife,  
But every fool becomes embroiled.[^1] ^20-3

**20:4** In winter the lazy man does not plow;  
At harvesttime he seeks, and finds nothing. ^20-4

**20:5** The designs in a man’s mind are deep waters,  
But a man of understanding can draw them out. ^20-5

**20:6** He calls many a man his loyal friend,  
But who can find a faithful man? ^20-6

**20:7** The righteous man lives blamelessly;  
Happy are his children who come after him. ^20-7

**20:8** The king seated on the throne of judgment  
Can winnow out all evil by his glance. ^20-8

**20:9** Who can say, “I have cleansed my heart,  
I am purged of my sin”? ^20-9

**20:10** False weights and false measures,  
Both are an abomination to the LORD. ^20-10

**20:11** A child may be dissembling in his behavior  
Even though his actions are blameless and proper. ^20-11

**20:12** The ear that hears, the eye that sees—  
The LORD made them both. ^20-12

**20:13** Do not love sleep lest you be impoverished;  
Keep your eyes open and you will have plenty of food. ^20-13

**20:14** “Bad, bad,” says the buyer,  
But having moved off, he congratulates himself. ^20-14

**20:15** Gold is plentiful, jewels abundant,  
But wise speech is a precious object. ^20-15

**20:16** Seize his garment, for he stood surety for another;[^2]  
Take it as a pledge, [for he stood surety] for an unfamiliar woman. ^20-16

**20:17** Bread gained by fraud may be tasty to a man,  
But later his mouth will be filled with gravel. ^20-17

**20:18** Plans laid in council will succeed;  
Wage war with stratagems. ^20-18

**20:19** He who gives away secrets is a base fellow;  
Do not take up with a garrulous man. ^20-19

**20:20** One who reviles his father or mother,  
Light will fail him when darkness comes. ^20-20

**20:21** An estate acquired in haste at the outset  
Will not be blessed in the end. ^20-21

**20:22** Do not say, “I will requite evil”;  
Put your hope in the LORD and He will deliver you. ^20-22

**20:23** False weights are an abomination to the LORD;  
Dishonest scales are not right. ^20-23

**20:24** A man’s steps are decided by the LORD;  
What does a man know about his own way? ^20-24

**20:25** It is a snare for a man to pledge a sacred gift rashly[^3]  
And to give thought to his vows only after they have been made. ^20-25

**20:26** A wise king winnows out the wicked,  
And turns the wheel upon them. ^20-26

**20:27** The lifebreath of man is the lamp of the LORD  
Revealing all his inmost parts. ^20-27

**20:28** Faithfulness and loyalty protect the king;  
He maintains his throne by faithfulness. ^20-28

**20:29** The glory of youths is their strength;  
The majesty of old men is their gray hair. ^20-29

**20:30** Bruises and wounds are repayment[^4] for evil,  
Striking at one’s inmost parts. ^20-30

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Or “a stranger.”
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
