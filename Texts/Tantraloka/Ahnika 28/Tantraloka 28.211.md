## Mūla

सामर्थ्यं योगिनो यद्वद्विनापि सहकारिणम् ।
एकजन्या भ्रातरः स्युस्तत्सदृग्यस्तु कोऽपि सः ॥ ^mula

sāmarthyaṃ yogino yadvadvināpi sahakāriṇam |
ekajanyā bhrātaraḥ syustatsadṛgyastu ko'pi saḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 05 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 3#^pg112|p. 95]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=112>))
>
> (A teacher) possesses an inner (supernal and) venerable (udāra) power of consciousness, even without that outer (Śakti). It is like the yogi’s capacity (to produce objects) even without an attendant (cooperant) cause. Brothers are born from the same parents. Anyone who is similar to that because he is connected to a (common spiritual) lineage (with others) is said to be a member of the teacher’s family (varga). This is the main lineage (to which a person may belong), which merits worship and is always honoured. (with 28.210cd–212)
