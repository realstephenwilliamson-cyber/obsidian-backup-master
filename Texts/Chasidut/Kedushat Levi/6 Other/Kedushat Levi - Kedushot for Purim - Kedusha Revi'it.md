## Teaching 7

And now we will explain in pleasant and sweet words why drinking wine is a commandment on Purim, and even more that one must get inebriated on Purim, which is not the case with other holidays. Indeed, it appears that it is since the son of an evil man like Ahasuerus should give us the merit to build the temple. Even though Darius was pure from his mother's side, he was nevertheless impure from his father's side. And it appears to me that since Haman and his ten sons were hung according to Ahasuerus' decree and he avenged us from our enemies, and on account of the rest of the things [that he did], even though God [manipulated ] him to make this decree, that is why he [earned to] give us the merit to build the second temple. And there were certainly holy sparks in Ahasuerus, as is well-known from the words of Ari, may his memory be blessed. For there are holy sparks in everything, and they must be elevated from the husks with the secret of *the riches he swallows, he vomits* ([[Job 20#^20-15|Job 20:15]]). And by way of Ahasuerus commanding to hang Haman and his ten sons and great salvation happening through him, through this, there was the raising of holy sparks that were in Ahasuerus, and he gave us the merit to build the temple. So too was it with the acts of Haman. Even though his intention was bad, there was salvation through his deeds, since he himself prepared the wooden pole to hang him and his sons. And also that which he said to kill Vashti, even though his intention was that [Ahasuerus] should marry his daughter, there was a great salvation for the Jewish people through this. It came out that there was an elevation of the holy sparks within Haman, and as a result of that, his descendants taught Torah to the masses, and the were shining children [*bnei barak*]. So it comes out that there was an elevation of the holy sparks that were within Haman and Ahasuerus. And the major principle [here] is that a place where penitents stand, completely righteous people are unable to stand, since there is a greater delight from the raising of holy sparks from the husks than from the righteous themselves. To illustrate, the delight is greater from a son who comes once a year to his father and comes from a far place than from one who is always with his father. Hence the delight is greater from a penitent, who comes from a far place, than from a righteous person, who is with his Father. Hence Purim [is due to that which] God sent Ahasuerus thoughts to act according to the will of the Jewish people, and the sparks within Ahasheurus were greatly elevated, such that he built the temple and that the act of Haman turned into a salvation; and [also] that, because of that, the descendants of Haman taught Torah to the masses. And since there were elevations for the sparks that were in the husk[s], there is great joy, as we have written, as it is like [with] a penitent. Hence we should increase our joy, and it is well-known that wine is the secret of joy. Hence we should increase the drinking of wine. As wine hints to the seventy [members] of the great Sanhedrin, who were in the world of delight. Hence we should increase the drinking of wine on this day. And God has more words about the topics of Chanukah and Purim and they will, God willing, be explained if God allows me to merit to print more volumes. And there, God willing, many topics about the Scroll of Esther and the statements of our Sages, may their memory be blessed, will be explained, as well as on the homilies about Chanukah and Purim.
