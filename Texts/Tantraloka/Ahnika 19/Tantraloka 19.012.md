## Mūla

आग्नेयीं धारणां कृत्वा सर्वमर्मप्रतापनीम् ।
पूरयेद्वायुना देहमंगुष्ठान्मस्तकान्तकम् ॥ ^mula

āgneyīṃ dhāraṇāṃ kṛtvā sarvamarmapratāpanīm |
pūrayedvāyunā dehamaṃguṣṭhānmastakāntakam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 10 - Chapter Nineteen#^pg224|p. 204]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=224>))
>
> (It is said) in the venerable Mālinīmata (i.e. the Mālinīvijayottara) that ‘the aforementioned deposition, that shines like the Fire (at the end) of Time (kālānala), is performed first in (reverse, that is) in the sequence (of) withdrawal, along with the pair (of syllables) SRK and CHINDI. Then, having performed the meditation on the Fire (āgneyīm dhāranām) that heats up all the vital points, the body should be filled with (the vital) breath from the big toe to the top of the head. Then, having dragged (the vital breath along) from the big toe up to the top of (the disciple’s head, the teacher) should sever (his) vital centres (marman) with (the Vidyā of) Kālarātri (the Dark Night of Death), which will be taught later, and expel (the breath) from the extremity of the cavity (at the top of the head).’ Conjoined by applying this process (kramayoga), even a (common) initiate (samayin) who has not offered oblations (is conjoined to the supreme reality,) and attains (the fruit of) that initiation. (with 19.11–14)
