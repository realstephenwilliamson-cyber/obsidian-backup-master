### 19.1–6: Heavenly celebration

*[[NRSVue Revelation 19#^19-1|Text]], p. 569* · The concluding glorification of God’s judgment shifts the antiphonal chanting to beings at God’s throne. ^19-1

**[[NRSVue Revelation 19#^19-2|2]]:** *Judgments . . . just*, [[Psalms 019#^19-10|Ps 19.9]] \[HB v 10\]. *Avenged . . . blood*, [[Psalms 079#^79-10|Ps 79.10]]. *Great whore*, see 17.1. *Fornication*, see 14.8. ^19-2

**[[NRSVue Revelation 19#^19-4|4–5]]:** *Hallelujah*, Heb “praise Yah,” Ps 146–150; 106.1; etc. ^19-4

**[[NRSVue Revelation 19#^19-5|5]]:** That the voice *from* the throne calls for praises *to our God* implies that its source (on or in the throne) is not actually God but an aspect of God. As in Ezek 1, the heavenly world involves several aspects of God (see 15.8n. and “Christ as Visual Appearance of God,” p. 541). **19.7–10. Announcement of the marriage supper.** The antiphonal singing in heaven now shifts from the destruction of the *whore* (v 2, an image of abomination and pollution) to the celebration of the marriage of the Lamb (ch 5) and Jerusalem, his pure bride, to be revealed in 21.2. ^19-5

**[[NRSVue Revelation 19#^19-8|8]]:** *Fine linen*, see 15.6–7n. *Deeds*, see 14.13n.; 16.15n. ^19-8

**[[NRSVue Revelation 19#^19-9|9]]:** *Blessed are*, see 1.3n. *Supper*, parody of the great eschatological feast for the righteous ([[Isaiah 25#^25-6|Isa 25.6–8]]; *4 Ezra* \[2 Esd\] 6.52; *2 Bar*. 29.4; *1 En.* 62.14); the horrific nature of this feast is soon revealed (19.17–18). ^19-9

**[[NRSVue Revelation 19#^19-10|10]]:** See 22.8–9. *To worship him*, seers and heroes privy to extraordinary visions often misunderstand who is God and who is an angel of God. If in earlier times righteous heroes prostrated themselves before angels just as they would before God (see [[LXX Tobit 12#^12-16|Tob 12.16–22]]), in early Jewish and Christian texts the appearance of concerns about how one subordinates oneself before an angel suggests that some authors saw angel-veneration as inconsistent with monotheism (see *Apoc. Zeph*. 6.11–15; *Ascen. Isa.* 4.2–14). Here it is God specifically, *not* God along with the Lamb/Jesus, who is to be worshipped. Jesus is a separate, evidently subordinate being, the subject of *testimony*. In this grammatically ambiguous verse (*testimony of* ? *testimony* \[to\]?), John links traditions about Jesus to the very *spirit of prophecy* that communicates from God to John. ^19-10

### 19.11–16: The avenging warrior

*[[NRSVue Revelation 19#^19-11|Text]], p. 570* ^19-11

**[[NRSVue Revelation 19#^19-11|11]]:** *White horse . . . rider*, personification of God’s Word as a warrior appears in contemporaneous Jewish texts (esp. [[LXX Wisdom of Solomon 18#^18-15|Wis 18.15–16]]) and does not derive from the Gospel of John; the imagery draws on such traditions as [[Isaiah 63#^63-1|Isa 63.1–3]]; *Pss. Sol*. 17.24; and *4 Ezra* 13.4–11, and is analogous to depictions of Wisdom: Gk “Sophia”; Heb “Ḥochma” ([[Proverbs 08#^8-22|Prov 8.22–9.1]]). God’s personified and active Word had a rich afterlife as the Aramaic “Memra” in early Jewish Targumim (see “*Logos*,” p. 688). *Faithful and True*, epithets for the authoritative character of John’s prophetic words (21.5; 22.6) and of the “witness” of the risen Christ figure (3.14). ^19-11-2

**[[NRSVue Revelation 19#^19-13|13]]:** *Robe dipped in blood*, could represent military efficacy (see [[Isaiah 63#^63-3|Isa 63.3]]), purity (see [[Revelation 07#^7-14|Rev 7.14]]), or, least likely, identification with Christ. ^19-13

**[[NRSVue Revelation 19#^19-14|14]]:** *Armies of heaven*, see 12.7–9n.; see [[II Kings 02#^2-12|2 Kings 2.12]]. ^19-14

**[[NRSVue Revelation 19#^19-15|15]]:** *Mouth . . . sword*, see 1.16n. *Rod of iron*, see 12.5n. *Wine press*, see 14.19n.; 14.20n. The enemies imagined here are the *nations* (Gentiles). ^19-15

**[[NRSVue Revelation 19#^19-16|16]]:** *King . . . lords*, see [[Torah 46 - Eikev#^10-17|Deut 10.17]]. ^19-16

### 19.17–18: The gory marriage supper

*[[NRSVue Revelation 19#^19-17|Text]], p. 570* · . Cf. v 9. ^19-17

**[[NRSVue Revelation 19#^19-17|17]]:** *The great supper* is for birds, not the saints; it vividly recalls Ezekiel’s invocation of birds and beasts to feast on the forces of the defeated Gog ([[Ezekiel 39#^39-17|Ezek 39.17–20]]); see also [[Isaiah 34#^34-5|Isa 34.5–7]]. ^19-17-2

### 19.19–20.3: Punishment of the beast, false prophet, and Satan

*[[NRSVue Revelation 19#^19-19|Text]], p. 570* ^19-19

**[[NRSVue Revelation 19#^19-19|19.19–20]]:** *Beast*, see 13.1; 17.8. *False prophet*, see 16.13n. *Mark*, see 7.1.3n.; 13.16–17n. *Lake of fire . . . sulfur*, see 14.10n. ^19-19-2
