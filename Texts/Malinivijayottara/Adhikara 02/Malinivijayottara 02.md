- [[Malinivijayottara 02.006|2.6]] tadvan māyāpi vijñeyā navadhā jñānakevalāḥ

### The Signs of Rudra's Power

- [[Malinivijayottara 02.013|2.13]] rudraśaktisamāveśas tatra nityam pratiṣṭhitaḥ

### The Penetrations of Rudra's Power

- [[Malinivijayottara 02.017|2.17]] rudraśaktisamāveśaḥ pañcadhā paripaṭhyate
- [[Malinivijayottara 02.018|2.18]] pañcadhā bhūtasamjñas tu tathā trimśatidhā paraḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 02.019|2.19]] dvividhaḥ śaktisamjño 'pi jñātavyaḥ paramārthataḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 02.020|2.20]] āṇavo 'yam samākhyātaḥ śākto 'py evamvidhaḥ smṛtaḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 02.021|2.21]] uccārakaraṇadhyānavarṇasthānaprakalpanaiḥ 
- [[Malinivijayottara 02.022|2.22]] uccārarahitam vastu cetasaiva vicintayan
- [[Malinivijayottara 02.023|2.23]] akimciccintakasyaiva guruṇā pratibodhataḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 02.024|2.24]] sārdham etac chatam proktam bhedānām anupūrvaśaḥ

### The Four Eggs and the Six Paths

- [[Malinivijayottara 02.049|2.49]] pārthivam prākṛtam caiva māyīyam śāktam eva ca
- [[Malinivijayottara 02.050|2.50]] pṛthag dvayam asamkhyātam ekam ekam pṛthak pṛthak
- [[Malinivijayottara 02.051|2.51]] ekam ekam pṛthak kṣārṇam padārṇamanuṣu smaret
- [[Malinivijayottara 02.052|2.52]] puraṣoḍaśakam jñeyam ṣaḍvidho 'dhvā prakīrtitaḥ 
- [[Malinivijayottara 02.053|2.53]] trayovimśatyavādīni tadvad dhādyakṣarāṇi ca
- [[Malinivijayottara 02.054|2.54]] tattvāni sapta bodhinyā taccaturthā purāṇi ca
- [[Malinivijayottara 02.055|2.55]] utpūyinyā caturtham tu tatra tattvatrayam viduḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 02.056|2.56]] aṣṭādaśa vijānīyād bhuvanāni samāsataḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 02.057|2.57]] svaraṣoḍaśakam mantram padam caikam vilakṣayet
- [[Malinivijayottara 02.058|2.58]] śuddhāśuddham jagat sarvam brahmāṇḍaprabhavam yataḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 02.059|2.59]] brahmā viṣṇuś ca rudraś ca īśvaraś ceti suvrate
- [[Malinivijayottara 02.060|2.60]] yo hi yasmād guṇotkṛṣṭaḥ sa tasmād ūrdhva ucyate

## Closing

This is the second chapter of the *Mālinīvijayottara* Tantra concerning pervasion.

*iti śrīmālinīvijayottare tantre vyāptyadhikāro dvitīyaḥ* ||
