## Mūla

दीक्षाप्यूर्ध्वाधरानेकभेदयोजनिकावशात् ।
भिद्यमाना योगिनां स्याद्विचित्रफलदायिनी ॥ ^mula

dīkṣāpyūrdhvādharānekabhedayojanikāvaśāt |
bhidyamānā yogināṃ syādvicitraphaladāyinī || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 06 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 4#^pg147|p. 130]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=147>))
>
> The yogis (who die) in this way, that is, as if moving into another person’s body etc., and move within the bodies of this and that reality level, are not dull (and unawake). And those too should be known to be of two kinds: worldly (laukika) and initiated. The first gradually become one with Śiva. The others (become Śiva) by just experiencing (and hence exhausting) the consequences of (their Karma). The initiation yogis receive also varies, because it conjoins to many different (reality levels and worlds, both) above and below, and bestows varied and wonderful fruits (accordingly). (with 28.300–302)

## Viveka

> [!viveka]- Viveka in English (Dyczkowski, vol. X, p. 130)
> ![[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 06 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 4#Viveka on 28.300–302]]

> एवं परशरीरादिचारिणामिव योगिनाम् ।
> तत्तत्तत्त्वशरीरान्तश्चारिणां नास्ति मूढता ॥
> ते चापि द्विविधा ज्ञेया लौकिका दीक्षितास्तथा ।
> पूर्वे शिवाः स्युः क्रमशः परे तद्भोगमात्रतः ॥
> दीक्षाप्यूर्ध्वाधरानेकभेदयोजनिकावशात् ।
> भिद्यमाना योगिनां स्याद्विचित्रफलदायिनी ॥

ते इति--योगिनः । लौकिका इति--पातञ्जलादिनिष्ठाः ॥ ३०२ ॥
