## Mūla

अर्घीशो भारभूतिश्च स्थितिः स्थाणुर्हरस्तथा ।
झण्ठिभौतिकसद्योजानुग्रहक्रूरसैनिकाः ॥ ^mula

arghīśo bhārabhūtiśca sthitiḥ sthāṇurharastathā |
jhaṇṭhibhautikasadyojānugrahakrūrasainikāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 07 - Chapter Thirty-Three#^pg191|p. 173]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=191>))
>
> The sixteen (powerholders) are Śrīkaṇṭtha, Ananta and Sūkṣma, Trimūrti, Śaṁvareśvara, Arghīśa and Bhārabhūti, Sthiti, Sthāṇu and Hara, Jhanṭhīśa, Bhautika, Sadyoja, Anugraha, Krūra, and Mahāsena. Or else, the following may be (optionally) conjoined with that (wheel): Amrta, Pūrṇa, Abha, its Drava, Ogha, Ūrmi, Syandana, (722, Vapuh, Udgāra, Vaktra, Tanu, Secana, Mūrtī, 168, and Sarvāmrtadhara is the other one. By reading (their names) in the feminine, these are said to be (their corresponding) sixteen powers. (with 33.9cd–12)
