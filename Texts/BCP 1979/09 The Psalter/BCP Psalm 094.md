# Psalm 94

← [[BCP Psalm 093]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 095]] →

*Deus ultionum* · Book Four · Eighteenth Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 094]]

**94:1** O LORD God of vengeance, \*  
O God of vengeance, show yourself. ^94-1

**94:2** Rise up, O Judge of the world; \*  
give the arrogant their just deserts. ^94-2

**94:3** How long shall the wicked, O LORD, \*  
how long shall the wicked triumph? ^94-3

[p. 723] ^pg723

**94:4** They bluster in their insolence; \*  
all evildoers are full of boasting. ^94-4

**94:5** They crush your people, O LORD, \*  
and afflict your chosen nation. ^94-5

**94:6** They murder the widow and the stranger \*  
and put the orphans to death. ^94-6

**94:7** Yet they say, "The LORD does not see, \*  
the God of Jacob takes no notice." ^94-7

**94:8** Consider well, you dullards among the people; \*  
when will you fools understand? ^94-8

**94:9** He that planted the ear, does he not hear? \*  
he that formed the eye, does he not see? ^94-9

**94:10** He who admonishes the nations, will he not punish? \*  
he who teaches all the world, has he no knowledge? ^94-10

**94:11** The LORD knows our human thoughts; \*  
how like a puff of wind they are. ^94-11

**94:12** Happy are they whom you instruct, O Lord! \*  
whom you teach out of your law; ^94-12

**94:13** To give them rest in evil days, \*  
until a pit is dug for the wicked. ^94-13

**94:14** For the LORD will not abandon his people, \*  
nor will he forsake his own. ^94-14

**94:15** For judgment will again be just, \*  
and all the true of heart will follow it. ^94-15

**94:16** Who rose up for me against the wicked? \*  
who took my part against the evildoers? ^94-16

**94:17** If the LORD had not come to my help, \*  
I should soon have dwelt in the land of silence. ^94-17

[p. 724] ^pg724

**94:18** As often as I said, "My foot has slipped," \*  
your love, O LORD, upheld me. ^94-18

**94:19** When many cares fill my mind, \*  
your consolations cheer my soul. ^94-19

**94:20** Can a corrupt tribunal have any part with you, \*  
one which frames evil into law? ^94-20

**94:21** They conspire against the life of the just \*  
and condemn the innocent to death. ^94-21

**94:22** But the LORD has become my stronghold, \*  
and my God the rock of my trust. ^94-22

**94:23** He will turn their wickedness back upon them  
and destroy them in their own malice; \*  
the LORD our God will destroy them. ^94-23
