## Mūla

करावूर्ध्वमुखौ कार्यावन्योन्यान्तरिताङ्गुली ।
अनामे मध्यपृष्ठस्थे तर्जन्यौ मूलपवर्गे ॥ ^mula

karāvūrdhvamukhau kāryāvanyonyāntaritāṅgulī |
anāme madhyapṛṣṭhasthe tarjanyau mūlapavarge || ^iast

*Dyczkowski prints no Sanskrit for this chapter; the text is that of the KSTS edition (Muktabodha M00160).*

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, July 2024 – September 2024
> p. 28 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=7>))
>
> **18. Mudgara (the Mallet) and Triśikhaa (the Three Tufts of Hair)**
>
> The hands should be turned upwards and the fingers mutually concealed by one another. The two ring fingers are placed on the back of the middle (fingers) and the two index fingers on the two little fingers (*mūlaparva*).
