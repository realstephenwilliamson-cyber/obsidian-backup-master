## Chapter 5

**5:1** Hear this word which I intone  
As a dirge over you, O House of Israel: ^5-1

**5:2** Fallen, not to rise again,  
Is Maiden Israel;  
Abandoned on her soil  
With none to lift her up. ^5-2

**5:3** For thus said my Lord GOD  
About the House of Israel:  
The town that marches out a thousand strong  
Shall have a hundred left,  
And the one that marches out a hundred strong  
Shall have but ten left. ^5-3

**5:4** Thus said the LORD  
To the House of Israel:  
Seek Me, and you will live. ^5-4

**5:5** Do not seek Bethel,  
Nor go to Gilgal,  
Nor cross over[^1] to Beer-sheba;  
For Gilgal shall go into exile,  
And Bethel shall become a delusion. ^5-5

**5:6** Seek the LORD, and you will live,  
Else He will rush like fire upon[^2] the House of Joseph  
And consume Bethel[^3] with none to quench it. ^5-6

**5:7** [Ah,] you who turn justice into wormwood  
And hurl righteousness to the ground!  
[Seek the LORD,] ^5-7

**5:8** Who made the Pleiades and Orion,  
Who turns deep darkness into dawn  
And darkens day into night,  
Who summons the waters of the sea  
And pours them out upon the earth—  
His name is the LORD! ^5-8

**5:9** It is He who hurls destruction upon strongholds,  
So that ruin comes upon fortresses![^4] ^5-9

**5:10** They hate the arbiter in the gate,  
And detest him whose plea is just. ^5-10

**5:11** Assuredly,  
Because you impose a tax[^5] on the poor  
And exact from him a levy of grain,  
You have built houses of hewn stone,  
But you shall not live in them;  
You have planted delightful vineyards,  
But shall not drink their wine. ^5-11

**5:12** For I have noted how many are your crimes,  
And how countless your sins—  
You enemies of the righteous,  
You takers of bribes,  
You who subvert in the gate  
The cause of the needy! ^5-12

**5:13** Assuredly,  
At such a time the prudent man keeps silent,  
For it is an evil time. ^5-13

**5:14** Seek good and not evil,  
That you may live,  
And that the LORD, the God of Hosts,  
May truly be with you,  
As you think. ^5-14

**5:15** Hate evil and love good,  
And establish justice in the gate;  
Perhaps the LORD, the God of Hosts,  
Will be gracious to the remnant of Joseph. ^5-15

**5:16** Assuredly,  
Thus said the LORD,  
My Lord, the God of Hosts:  
In every square there shall be lamenting,  
In every street cries of “Ah, woe!”  
And the farm hand shall be  
Called to mourn,  
And those skilled in wailing  
To lament; ^5-16

**5:17** For there shall be lamenting  
In every vineyard, too,  
When I pass through your midst  
—said the LORD. ^5-17

**5:18** Ah, you who wish  
For the day of the LORD!  
Why should you want  
The day of the LORD?  
It shall be darkness, not light! ^5-18

**5:19** —As if a man should run from a lion  
And be attacked by a bear;  
Or if he got indoors,  
Should lean his hand on the wall  
And be bitten by a snake! ^5-19

**5:20** Surely the day of the LORD shall be  
Not light, but darkness,  
Blackest night without a glimmer. ^5-20

**5:21** I loathe, I spurn your festivals,  
I am not appeased by your solemn assemblies. ^5-21

**5:22** If you offer Me burnt offerings—or your meal offerings—  
I will not accept them;  
I will pay no heed  
To your gifts of fatlings. ^5-22

**5:23** Spare Me the sound of your hymns,  
And let Me not hear the music of your lutes. ^5-23

**5:24** But let justice well up like water,  
Righteousness like an unfailing stream. ^5-24

**5:25** Did you offer sacrifice and oblation to Me  
Those forty years in the wilderness,  
O House of Israel? ^5-25

**5:26** [^6]And you shall carry off your “king”—  
Sikkuth[^7] and Kiyyun,[^8]  
The images you have made for yourselves  
Of your astral deity— ^5-26

**5:27** As I drive you into exile beyond Damascus  
—Said the LORD, whose name is God of Hosts.[^9] ^5-27

[^1]: I.e., into Judah; cf. 1 Kings 19.3.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Septuagint reads “the House of Israel.”
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^5]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^6]: Vv. 26–27 would read well after 6.14.
[^7]: Two Akkadian names applied to Saturn, here deliberately pointed with the vowels of Heb. shiqquṣ, “detestable thing.”
[^8]: Two Akkadian names applied to Saturn, here deliberately pointed with the vowels of Heb. shiqquṣ, “detestable thing.”
[^9]: I.e., who is Lord of all the astral bodies.
