## Mūla

एतद्विपर्ययाद् ग्राह्यमवश्यं शिवशासनम् ।
द्वावाप्तौ तत्र च श्रीमच्छ्रीकण्ठलकुलेश्वरौ ॥ ^mula

etadviparyayād grāhyamavaśyaṃ śivaśāsanam |
dvāvāptau tatra ca śrīmacchrīkaṇṭhalakuleśvarau || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 11 - Chapter Thirty-Seven, part 1#^pg250|p. 232]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=250>))
>
> (The teachings) of inferior systems are afflicted by ignorance and the lack of (sound) teachings. It is necessary therefore to choose the doctrine preached by Śiva, which is the (exact) opposite of this. (with 37.13cd–14ab)
>
> (The teachers) qualified there (to teach it) are two, the venerable Śrīkaṇṭha and Lakulīśa. This scripture, which is the right (one) and bestows the supreme good, is divided into two (major) currents (pravāha), of which the first also bestows worldly benefits as desired (not just liberation). (with 37.14cd–15)
