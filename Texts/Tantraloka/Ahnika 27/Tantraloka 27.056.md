## Mūla

सर्वमासनपक्षे प्राङ् न्यस्य संपूजयेत्क्रमम् ।
शुद्धाशुद्धाध्वजाः सर्वे मन्त्राः सर्वः शिवान्तकः ॥ ^mula

sarvamāsanapakṣe prāṅ nyasya saṃpūjayetkramam |
śuddhāśuddhādhvajāḥ sarve mantrāḥ sarvaḥ śivāntakaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 18 - Chapter Twenty-Seven#^pg442|p. 422]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=442>))
>
> When a Liṅga has (already been) installed by others, whether it be made of precious stones (bāna) or (a natural) self-generated (svayahbhū) one, one should first of all deposit all (the mantras of the principles, as is done) in the rite of (Śiva’s) Throne, and then worship the liturgy (krama) (within it). (with 27.55cd–56ab)
>
> All Mantras are born from the pure and impure Paths. (Moreover,) it is said that the entire Path ending with Śiva is (present) here in (His) Throne. (Thus,) it should be worshiped (anyway,) everywhere (in all cases). The invocation and dismissal (of the deity) should be done (in the same way) as before. (with 27.56cd–57)
