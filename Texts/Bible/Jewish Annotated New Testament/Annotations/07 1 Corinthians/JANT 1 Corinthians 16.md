**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 16#^16-1|16.1–4]]:** **The collection.** ^16-1

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 16#^16-1|1]]:** *Collection*, for the Jerusalem church, the Jesus movement’s headquarters (see [[Galatians 02#^2-10|Gal 2.10]]; 2 Cor 8; 9). By accepting this offering, delivered by Paul himself, Jerusalem church leaders would signal their recognition of his Gentile communities ([[Acts 24#^24-17|Acts 24.17]]; [[Galatians 02#^2-10|Gal 2.10]]). Jewish tradition understood almsgiving as atoning for sin and divine sacrifice, which necessarily excluded idolaters ([[Proverbs 11#^11-4|Prov 11.4]]; [[Daniel 04#^4-24|Dan 4.27]]; [[LXX Tobit 04#^4-7|Tob 4.7–11]]; [[LXX Sirach 03#^3-30|Sir 3.30]], [[LXX Sirach 29#^29-12|29.12]]; Josephus, *Ant*. 20.2.5 \[50–51\]; [[Rosh Hashanah 6#6a|*b. Rosh Ha-Shanah* 6a]]; [[Sanhedrin 26#26b|*b. Sanh*. 26b]]). ^16-1-2

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 16#^16-2|2]]:** *First day*, Sunday, the day his followers proclaimed Jesus resurrected ([[Matthew 28#^28-1|Mt 28.1]]; [[Mark 16#^16-2|Mk 16.2]]; [[Luke 24#^24-1|Lk 24.1]]; [[John 20#^20-1|Jn 20.1]]). ^16-2

### 16.5–24: Plans and farewells

*[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 16#^16-5|Text]], p. 351* ^16-5

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 16#^16-8|8]]:** *Pentecost,* Feast of Weeks (Heb “Shavuot”), celebrated by Christians as the time of the giving of the Holy Spirit ([[Acts 02#^2-1|Acts 2.1–42]]); in postbiblical Judaism, it commemorates the giving of the Torah on Mt. Sinai. ^16-8

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 16#^16-10|10]]:** *Timothy,* see 4.17. ^16-10

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 16#^16-12|12]]:** *Apollos,* see 1.12. ^16-12

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 16#^16-13|13]]:** *Courageous . . . strong*, these verbs appear in LXX [[LXX 2 Kingdoms 10#^10-12|2 Sam 10.12]]; [[Psalms 027#^27-14|Ps 27.14]]; [[Psalms 031#^31-25|31.25]] (cf. LXX [[LXX Deuteronomy 31#^31-6|Deut 31.6]]; [[Joshua 01#^1-6|Josh 1.6–7]]). ^16-13

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 16#^16-15|15]]:** *Stephanas*, see 1.16. *Achaia*, Greek province whose capital was Corinth. ^16-15

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 16#^16-17|17]]:** *Coming*, visiting Paul in Ephesus. *Fortunatus and Achaicus*, Romans with church authority. ^16-17

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 16#^16-19|19]]:** *Aquila and Prisca*, co-founders of the Corinthian church ([[Acts 18#^18-2|Acts 18.2]]); married couple expelled from Rome when the emperor Claudius expelled the Jews, perhaps for disturbances regarding the proclamation of Jesus ([[Romans 16#^16-3|Rom 16.3–4]]; Suetonius, *Claud.* 25.4). *House*, or household, see 1.16. ^16-19

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 16#^16-20|20]]:** *Holy kiss*, greeting exchanged between believers, as at the Lord’s Supper. ^16-20

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 16#^16-22|22]]:** *The Lord*, Christ Jesus. *Accursed*, [[Jeremiah 17#^17-5|Jer 17.5]]; [[Matthew 23#^23-13|Mt 23.13–27]]; *Ep. Arist.* 311; *Jub.* 23.30; *1 En.* 5.5–7; *Pss. Sol.* 4.14–22; *Sib. Or.* 3.295–349; 492–519; 1QS 2.4–9. *Love* (Gk “phileō”), used by Paul only here (contrast 2.9,13). *Our Lord, come!*, transliterated Gk from Aram “marana tha,” a formulaic appeal for Jesus to return and judge unbelievers (*Did.* 10.6). read *Maran atha*, meaning *Our Lord has come* d Other ancient authorities add *Amen* ^16-22
