Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 9.11 - Parashat Pinehas#^z3-220a|Parashat Pineḥas, at 3:220a]]

*Volume 3, folio 220a* · Pinchas

*Passage Pinchas 24:151 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 3.219b#^pinchas-24-151|Pinchas 24:151]].*

**Pinchas 25:152** As for the second question, assuredly we are nearer to the supernal King than all other peoples. God has made Israel as it were the heart of all mankind, and as the limbs cannot endure for a moment without the heart, so the other nations cannot endure without Israel. ^pinchas-25-152

**Pinchas 25:153** And what the heart is among the limbs, such is Israel among the nations. The heart is tender and weak, and it alone feels sorrow and distress, since in it alone is intelligence. The other limbs are distant from the king, which is the wisdom and intelligence situate in the brain, but the heart is near. So Israel is near to the Holy King, while the other nations are far away. ^pinchas-25-153

**Pinchas 26:154** Similarly in regard to the third question, Israel being the heart, which is tender and delicate and king of the members, takes for its food only the most purified part of all the blood, and leaves the remnant for the other members, which are not particular. ^pinchas-26-154

**Pinchas 26:155** They are therefore strong, as we see, but they also suffer from boils and from other ailments from which the heart is quite free. So God takes to himself Israel, who are clean and pure without any blemish.’ ^pinchas-26-155

**Pinchas 27:156** NOW THE NAME OF THE MAN OF ISRAEL THAT WAS SLAIN, WHO WAS SLAIN WITH THE MIDIANITISH WOMAN. R. Isaac said: ‘We should have expected the text to run “whom Phineas slew”.’ ^pinchas-27-156

**Pinchas 27:157** The reason why it is put in this way is because God, having raised Phineas to the high priesthood, did not wish to associate his name with the killing of a man, which does not beseem a high priest.’ ^pinchas-27-157

**Pinchas 28:158** R. Simeon was once going from Cappadocia to Lydda with R. Judah. R. Phineas b. Jair was coming the other way with two men behind him. Suddeynly the ass of R. Phineas came to a stop and would not budge. Said R. Phineas: ‘Let him be, he must have scented some newcomers here, or a miracle is to be wrought for us.’ While they were there R. Simeon emerged from behind a rock, and the ass immediately began to move forward. ‘Did I not tell you’, said R. Phineas, ‘that he scented some newcomers?’ ^pinchas-28-158

**Pinchas 28:159** He got down and embraced him, saying: ‘I saw in a dream the Shekinah coming to me and giving me beautiful presents. Now I see it.’ R. Simeon said: ‘From the sound of your ass’s hoofs I know that you are wholly joyful.’ Said R. Phineas: ‘Let us sit down in a suitable spot, since the words of the Torah require coolness.’ They found a spring of water and a tree and sat down. ^pinchas-28-159

**Pinchas 28:160** R. Phineas said: ‘I was reflecting that, in the resurrection of the dead, God will reverse the way of this world so that what is now first will then be last. We know this from the bones which God revived by the hand of Ezekiel, as it is written first, “And the bones drew near, each bone to his fellow”, and then, “And I saw, and behold there were sinews on them and flesh came up and skin formed on them above, but there was no breath in them” (Ezek. 37, 7-8). Thus we see that what a man is divested of here first will there be last, for here the breath is lost first and then the skin and then the flesh and then the bones.’ ^pinchas-28-160
