# Amos 1

[[Tanakh]] · [[Amos 2]] →

Compare: [[NRSVue Amos 01|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 1

**1:1** The words of Amos, a sheepbreeder from Tekoa, who prophesied concerning Israel in the reigns of Kings Uzziah of Judah and Jeroboam son of Joash of Israel, two years before the earthquake.[^1] ^1-1

**1:2** He proclaimed:  
The LORD roars from Zion,  
Shouts aloud from Jerusalem;  
And the pastures of the shepherds shall languish,  
And the summit of Carmel shall wither. ^1-2

**1:3** Thus said the LORD:  
For three transgressions of Damascus,  
For four, I will not revoke it:[^2]  
Because they threshed Gilead  
With threshing boards of iron. ^1-3

**1:4** I will send down[^3] fire upon the palace of Hazael,  
And it shall devour the fortresses of Ben-hadad.[^4] ^1-4

**1:5** I will break the gate bars of Damascus,  
And wipe out the inhabitants from the Vale of Aven  
And the sceptered ruler of Beth-eden;  
And the people of Aram shall be exiled to Kir  
—said the LORD. ^1-5

**1:6** Thus said the LORD:  
For three transgressions of Gaza,  
For four, I will not revoke it:  
Because they exiled[^5] an entire population,  
Which they delivered to Edom.[^6] ^1-6

**1:7** I will send down fire upon the wall of Gaza,  
And it shall devour its fortresses; ^1-7

**1:8** And I will wipe out the inhabitants of Ashdod  
And the sceptered ruler of Ashkelon;  
And I will turn My hand against Ekron,  
And the Philistines shall perish to the last man  
—said the Lord GOD. ^1-8

**1:9** Thus said the LORD:  
For three transgressions of Tyre,  
For four, I will not revoke it:  
Because they handed over  
An entire population to Edom,[^7]  
Ignoring the covenant of brotherhood.[^8] ^1-9

**1:10** I will send down fire upon the wall of Tyre,  
And it shall devour its fortresses. ^1-10

**1:11** Thus said the LORD:  
For three transgressions of Edom,  
For four, I will not revoke it:  
Because he pursued his brother with the sword  
And repressed all pity,  
Because his anger raged unceasing  
And his fury stormed[^9] unchecked. ^1-11

**1:12** I will send down fire upon Teman,  
And it shall devour the fortresses of Bozrah. ^1-12

**1:13** Thus said the LORD:  
For three transgressions of the Ammonites,  
For four, I will not revoke it:  
Because they ripped open the pregnant women of Gilead  
In order to enlarge their own territory. ^1-13

**1:14** I will set fire to the wall of Rabbah,  
And it shall devour its fortresses,  
Amid shouting on a day of battle,  
On a day of violent tempest. ^1-14

**1:15** Their king and his officers shall go  
Into exile together  
—said the LORD. ^1-15

[^1]: See Zech. 14.5.
[^2]: I.e, the decree of punishment.
[^3]: Cf. Lam. 1.13.
[^4]: Cf. 2 Kings 13.22–25.
[^5]: I.e., they cooperated in the annexation of Israelite territory; cf. Jer. 13.19 with note.
[^6]: Emendation yields “Aram”; cf. Isa. 9.11.
[^7]: Emendation yields “Aram.”
[^8]: Cf. 1 Kings 5.26; 9.12–13.
[^9]: Cf. Akkadian shamaru and Jer. 3.5.
