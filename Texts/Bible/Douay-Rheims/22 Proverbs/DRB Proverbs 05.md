# Proverbs 5 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Proverbs 04|← Proverbs 4]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Proverbs 06|Proverbs 6 →]]

**5:1** My son, attend to my wisdom, and incline thy ear to my prudence. ^5-1

**5:2** That thou mayst keep thoughts, and thy lips may preserve instruction. Mind not the deceit of a woman. ^5-2

**5:3** For the lips of a harlot are like a honeycomb dropping, and her throat is smoother than oil. ^5-3

**5:4** But her end is bitter as wormwood, and sharp as a two-edged sword. ^5-4

**5:5** Her feet go down into death, and her steps go in as far as hell. ^5-5

**5:6** They walk not by the path of life, her steps are wandering, and unaccountable. ^5-6

**5:7** Now therefore, my son, hear me, and depart not from the words of my mouth. ^5-7

**5:8** Remove thy way far from her, and come not nigh the doors of her house. ^5-8

**5:9** Give not thy honour to strangers, and thy years to the cruel. ^5-9

**5:10** Lest strangers be filled with thy strength, and thy labours be in another man’s house, ^5-10

**5:11** And thou mourn it the last, when thou shalt have spent thy flesh and thy body, and say: ^5-11

**5:12** Why have I hated instruction, and my heart consented not to reproof, ^5-12

**5:13** And have not heard the voice of them that taught me, and have not inclined my ear to masters? ^5-13

**5:14** I have almost been in all evil, in the midst of the church and of the congregation. ^5-14

**5:15** Drink water out of thy own cistern, and the streams of thy own well: ^5-15

**5:16** Let thy fountains be conveyed abroad, and in the streets divide thy waters. ^5-16

**5:17** Keep them to thyself alone, neither let strangers be partakers with thee. ^5-17

**5:18** Let thy vein be blessed, and rejoice with the wife of thy youth: ^5-18

**5:19** Let her be thy dearest hind, and most agreeable fawn: let her breasts inebriate thee at all times; he thou delighted continually with her love. ^5-19

**5:20** Why art thou seduced, my son, by a strange woman, and art cherished in the bosom of another? ^5-20

**5:21** The Lord beholdeth the ways of man, and considereth all his steps. ^5-21

**5:22** His own iniquities catch the wicked, and he is fast bound with the ropes of his own sins. ^5-22

**5:23** He shall die, because he hath not received instruction, and in the multitude of his folly he shall be deceived. ^5-23
