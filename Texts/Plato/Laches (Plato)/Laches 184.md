Suppose you had a consultation as to what your son’s exercise should be for a coming contest, would you be guided by the majority of us, or by the one who happened to have trained and exercised under a good master? Mel. By the latter, naturally, Socrates. Soc. Would you be guided by him alone rather than the four of us? Mel. Very likely. Soc.
