# Isaiah 31 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Isaiah 30|← Isaiah 30]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Isaiah 32|Isaiah 32 →]]

## Alliance with Egypt is futile

**31:1** Woe to those who go down to Egypt for help and who rely on horses, who trust in chariots because they are many and in horsemen because they are very strong, but do not look to the Holy One of Israel or consult the LORD! ^31-1

**31:2** Yet he is wise and can bring disaster; he does not depart from his words but will rise against the house of the evildoers and against the helpers of those who work iniquity. ^31-2

**31:3** The Egyptians are human and not God; their horses are flesh and not spirit. When the LORD stretches out his hand, the helper will stumble, and the one helped will fall, and they will all perish together. ^31-3

**31:4** For thus the LORD said to me, “As a lion or a young lion growls over its prey and, when a band of shepherds is called out against it, is not terrified by their shouting or daunted at their noise, so the LORD of hosts will come down to fight upon Mount Zion and upon its hill. ^31-4

**31:5** Like birds hovering overhead, so the LORD of hosts will protect Jerusalem; he will protect and deliver it; he will spare and rescue it.” ^31-5

**31:6** Turn back to him whom you^[Heb *they*] have deeply betrayed, O people of Israel. ^31-6

**31:7** For on that day all of you shall throw away your idols of silver and idols of gold, which your hands have sinfully made for you. ^31-7

**31:8** “Then the Assyrian shall fall by a sword not of mortals, and a sword not of humans shall devour him; he shall flee from the sword, and his young men shall be put to forced labor. ^31-8

**31:9** His rock shall pass away in terror, and his officers desert the standard in panic,” says the LORD, whose fire is in Zion and whose furnace is in Jerusalem. ^31-9
