## Mūla

गतागतम्सुविक्षिप्तम्सम्गतम्सुसमाहितम् ।
चतुर्धा रूपसम्ज्ञम्तु ज्ञातव्यम्योगचिन्तकैः ॥ ^mula

gatāgatam suvikṣiptam samgatam susamāhitam |
caturdhā rūpasamjñam[^63] tu jñātavyam yogacintakaiḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, October 2022 – December 2022
> p. 24 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=31>))
>
> *Abiding in Place (padastha) is of four kinds, namely,* Gone and Come (*gatāgata*), Well Dispersed (*suvikṣipta*), Well Conjoined (*samgata*) and Well Concentrated (*susamāhita*).[^62] (with 2.43d–44ab)

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, October 2022 – December 2022
> p. 24 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=31>))
>
> *Those who think about yoga should know that Established in Form is of four kinds, namely,* Emergent (*udita*), Extensive (*vipula*), Peaceful (*śānta*) and Well Content (*suprasanna*), which is another.[^64] (with 2.44cd–45ab)

[^63]: Printed edition: samstham
[^62]: Quoted in TĀv ad 10/252cd-253ab; TĀ 10/253ab is a direct quote of MV 2/44ab.
[^64]: MV 2/44cd-45ab is quoted in TĀv ad 10/263-264ab. TĀ 10/264ab is MV 2/45ab.
