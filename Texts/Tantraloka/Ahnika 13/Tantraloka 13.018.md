## Mūla

नाणुरस्ति भवे ह्यस्मिन्ननादौ कोऽन्वयक्रमः ।
भाविनः प्रागभावश्च ज्ञानस्येति स्थिते सति ॥ ^mula

nāṇurasti bhave hyasminnanādau ko'nvayakramaḥ |
bhāvinaḥ prāgabhāvaśca jñānasyeti sthite sati || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 05 - Chapter Thirteen, part 1#^pg154|p. 134]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=154>))
>
> There is no individual soul of this kind in this beginningless world of transmigration that, like an (empty) boat, has ever been totally devoid of knowledge. What sense (anvaya) (then is there in affirming this)? (with 13.17cd–18ab)
>
> (Now,) if (ignorance) is the antecedent absence of (some form of) knowledge that there will be (at some future time, then in that case,) the liberated soul also possesses that (kind of ignorance, because) it precedes the birth (of knowledge). Again, if you reply that there is no knowledge to come for a liberated soul, then reflect on this (that I am about to tell you). (with 13.18cd–19)
