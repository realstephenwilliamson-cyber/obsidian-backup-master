## Chapter 27

**27:1** David said to himself, “Some day I shall certainly perish at the hands of Saul. The best thing for me is to flee to the land of the Philistines; Saul will then give up hunting me throughout the territory of Israel, and I will escape him.” ^27-1

**27:2** So David and the six hundred men with him went and crossed over to King Achish son of Maoch of Gath. ^27-2

**27:3** David and his men stayed with Achish in Gath, each man with his family, and David with his two wives, Ahinoam the Jezreelite and Abigail wife of Nabal the Carmelite. ^27-3

**27:4** And when Saul was told that David had fled to Gath, he did not pursue him anymore. ^27-4

**27:5** David said to Achish, “If you please, let a place be granted me in one of the country towns where I can live; why should your servant remain with you in the royal city?” ^27-5

**27:6** At that time Achish granted him Ziklag; that is how Ziklag came to belong to the kings of Judah, as is still the case. ^27-6

**27:7** The length of time that David lived in Philistine territory was a year and four months. ^27-7

**27:8** David and his men went up and raided the Geshurites, the Gizrites, and the Amalekites—who were the inhabitants of the region of Olam,[^1] all the way to Shur and to the land of Egypt.— ^27-8

**27:9** When David attacked a region, he would leave no man or woman alive; he would take flocks, herds, asses, camels, and clothing. When he returned and came[^2] to Achish, ^27-9

**27:10** Achish would ask, “Where[^3] did you raid today?” and David would reply, “The Negeb[^4] of Judah,” or “the Negeb of the Jerahmeelites,” or “the Negeb of the Kenites.” ^27-10

**27:11** David would leave no man or woman alive to be brought to Gath; for he thought, “They might tell about us: David did this.” Such was his practice as long as he stayed in the territory of the Philistines. ^27-11

**27:12** Achish trusted David. He thought: “He has aroused the wrath of[^5] his own people Israel, and so he will be my vassal forever.” ^27-12

[^1]: Septuagint reads “Telam” (cf. “Telaim” in 15.4; and “Telem” in Josh. 15.24).
[^2]: Change of vocalization yields “brought it”; cf. v. 11.
[^3]: So some mss. and Targum; Septuagint and 4QSama read “Whom.”
[^4]: I.e., the part of the Negeb occupied by these clans.
[^5]: Cf. note at 13.4.
