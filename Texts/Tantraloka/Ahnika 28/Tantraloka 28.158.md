## Mūla

प्रासादे यागगेहे च कारयेन्नवरङ्गिकम् ।
विद्यापीठे तु खशराः, प्रतिमालिङ्गपीठगम् ॥ ^mula

prāsāde yāgagehe ca kārayennavaraṅgikam |
vidyāpīṭhe tu khaśarāḥ, pratimāliṅgapīṭhagam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 04 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 2#^pg94|p. 77]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=94>))
>
> The Seat of Knowledge (where the scriptures are placed and worshipped), the moveable Liñṅga, the sacrificial surface, the teacher and his group, the bell, the large and small ladle (sruk and sruva), the disciples, the bearers of sectarian signs, the door and the lintel above it (toraṇa), one’s own body, and the sacred seat (of the pit of the sacrificial) fire - it is right (that all of these be invested with the sacred thread,) as is beautiful (and befitting). Of nine colours, it should also be placed on the terrace and in the house where the sacrifice is celebrated. (with 28.156cd–158ab)
>
> The knots (of the thread placed on) on the Seat of Knowledge (vidyāpīṭha) should be fifty; the one on the idol and the pedestal of the Liṅga, forty-eight, the one on the bell, twenty-five; the one on the little ladle, eighteen; and the one on the large ladle, twenty-four. (The thread) in the terrace has thirty-six (knots), the one in the pavilion, twelve. (The one) in the house (reserved for) the bathing (of the deities), sixteen. and (the one) in the shrine for meditation, twenty-four. (The one placed) on the teacher, seven. (The one) on the adepts, five. There are seven (knots in the thread placed) on the spiritual son, four in that for the regular initiate. It is said that (the thread placed) on the disciple who follows a different scripture has five (knots). (The thread) for those who bear sectarian signs has only one (knot). Fashion one with ten (knots for the one) on the lintel (above the door) (toraṇa). There are eight knots (in the threads placed) on the doors. (with 28.158cd–162ab)
