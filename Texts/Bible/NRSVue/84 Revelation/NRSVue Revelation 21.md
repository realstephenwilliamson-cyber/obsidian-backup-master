## The new heaven and the new earth

**21:1** Then I saw a new heaven and a new earth, for the first heaven and the first earth had passed away, and the sea was no more. ^21-1

**21:2** And I saw the holy city, the new Jerusalem, coming down out of heaven from God, prepared as a bride adorned for her husband. ^21-2

**21:3** And I heard a loud voice from the throne saying, “See, the home^[Gk *the tabernacle*] of God is among mortals. He will dwell^[Gk *will tabernacle*] with them; they will be his peoples,^[Other ancient authorities read *people*] and God himself will be with them and be their God;^[Other ancient authorities lack *and be their God*] ^21-3

**21:4** he will wipe every tear from their eyes. Death will be no more; mourning and crying and pain will be no more, for^[Other ancient authorities lack *for*] the first things have passed away.” ^21-4

**21:5** And the one who was seated on the throne said, “See, I am making all things new.” Also he said, “Write this, for these words are trustworthy and true.” ^21-5

**21:6** Then he said to me, “It is done! I am the Alpha and the Omega, the Beginning and the End. To the thirsty I will give water as a gift from the spring of the water of life. ^21-6

**21:7** Those who conquer will inherit these things, and I will be their God, and they will be my children. ^21-7

**21:8** But as for the cowardly, the faithless,^[Or *the unbelieving*] the polluted, the murderers, the sexually immoral,^[Or *prostitutes*] the sorcerers, the idolaters, and all liars, their place will be in the lake that burns with fire and sulfur, which is the second death.” ^21-8

## Vision of the new Jerusalem

**21:9** Then one of the seven angels who had the seven bowls full of the seven last plagues came and said to me, “Come, I will show you the bride, the wife of the Lamb.” ^21-9

**21:10** And in the spirit^[Or *in the Spirit*] he carried me away to a great, high mountain and showed me the holy city Jerusalem coming down out of heaven from God. ^21-10

**21:11** It has the glory of God and a radiance like a very rare jewel, like jasper, clear as crystal. ^21-11

**21:12** It has a great, high wall with twelve gates, and at the gates twelve angels, and on the gates are inscribed the names that are the names^[Other ancient authorities lack *that are the names*] of the twelve tribes of the Israelites: ^21-12

**21:13** on the east three gates, on the north three gates, on the south three gates, and on the west three gates. ^21-13

**21:14** And the wall of the city has twelve foundations, and on them are the twelve names of the twelve apostles of the Lamb. ^21-14

**21:15** The angel^[Gk *He*] who talked to me had a measuring rod of gold to measure the city and its gates and walls. ^21-15

**21:16** The city has four equal sides, its length the same as its width, and he measured the city with his rod, twelve thousand stadia; its length and width and height are equal. ^21-16

**21:17** He also measured its wall, one hundred forty-four cubits by human measurement, which the angel was using. ^21-17

**21:18** The wall is built of jasper, while the city is pure gold, clear as glass. ^21-18

**21:19** The foundations of the wall of the city are adorned with every jewel; the first was jasper, the second sapphire, the third agate, the fourth emerald, ^21-19

**21:20** the fifth onyx, the sixth carnelian, the seventh chrysolite, the eighth beryl, the ninth topaz, the tenth chrysoprase, the eleventh jacinth, the twelfth amethyst. ^21-20

**21:21** And the twelve gates are twelve pearls, each of the gates is a single pearl, and the street of the city is pure gold, transparent as glass. ^21-21

**21:22** I saw no temple in the city, for its temple is the Lord God the Almighty and the Lamb. ^21-22

**21:23** And the city has no need of sun or moon to shine on it, for the glory of God is its light, and its lamp is the Lamb. ^21-23

**21:24** The nations will walk by its light, and the kings of the earth will bring their glory into it. ^21-24

**21:25** Its gates will never be shut by day—and there will be no night there. ^21-25

**21:26** People will bring into it the glory and the honor of the nations. ^21-26

**21:27** But nothing unclean will enter it, nor anyone who practices abomination or falsehood, but only those who are written in the Lamb’s book of life. ^21-27
