## Mūla

मण्डलस्थोऽहमेवायं साक्षी चाखिलकर्मणाम् ।
शुद्धा हि द्रष्टृता शम्भोर्मण्डले कल्पिता मया ॥ ^mula

maṇḍalastho'hamevāyaṃ sākṣī cākhilakarmaṇām |
śuddhā hi draṣṭṛtā śambhormaṇḍale kalpitā mayā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 05 - Chapter Sixteen, part 1#^pg58|p. 38]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=58>))
>
> 1) “It is I myself who reside in the maṇḍala, and am this witness of all (ritual) actions.” (In other words,) within Śiva’s maṇḍala, I conceive myself to be a pure witness, (not an agent).
