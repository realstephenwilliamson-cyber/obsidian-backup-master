## Mūla

जप्यादौ होमपर्यन्ते यद्यप्येकैककर्मणि ।
उदेति रूढिः परमा तथापीत्थं निरूपितम् ॥ ^mula

japyādau homaparyante yadyapyekaikakarmaṇi |
udeti rūḍhiḥ paramā tathāpītthaṃ nirūpitam || ^iast

> The supremely stable state (rūḍhi) arises in each of these actions (karman), from the repetition of Mantra to the offering of oblation; even so, they have (each been) described (separately) in this way.
