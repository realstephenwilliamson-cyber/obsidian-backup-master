# Zohar 3.161a

← [[Zohar 3.160b]] · [[Zohar (Soncino)]] · [[Zohar 3.161b]] →

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 9.03 - Parashat Shelah Lekha#^z3-161a|Parashat Shelaḥ Lekha, at 3:161a]]

*Volume 3, folio 161a* · Sh'lach

*Passage Sh'lach 10:75 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 3.160b#^sh-lach-10-75|Sh'lach 10:75]].*

**Sh'lach 10:76** Observe how much evil was wrought by the evil tongue: it called forth the decree that our ancestors should not enter the land, those that uttered it died, and weeping was decreed for succeeding generations. Their calumny of the Holy Land was, as it were, a calumny of the Almighty, and therefore God was indignant on account of this, and all Israel would have been destroyed but for the prayer of Moses. ^sh-lach-10-76

**Sh'lach 10:77** AND THEY TOLD HIM: the word “told” (*sapper*) means “explaining in detail”. ^sh-lach-10-77

**Sh'lach 10:78** WE CAME: as much as to say, “We entered into that land of which you were always singing the praises’. AND SURELY IT FLOWETH WITH MILK AND HONEY: R. Isaac said that if a man wants to deceive he should first say something true so that he may be believed. ^sh-lach-10-78

**Sh'lach 10:79** R. Hiya, however, said that what they meant was, “We came into the land which you praised so much and which you said was flowing with milk and honey, and this is its fruit! If this is the inheritance which God is giving to Israel and for which they have endured such sufferings, there is fruit in Egypt twiceasgood. ^sh-lach-10-79

**Sh'lach 10:80** HOWBEIT THE PEOPLE THAT DWELL IN THE LAND ARE FIERCE: as a rule the best warriors are stationed outside the towns to protect the roads, but here even those who dwell in the cities are mighty men. AND THE CITIES ARE FORTIFIED: even if all the kings of the world were to assemble against them, they could make no impression on them. R. Jose said: ‘The worst of their calumnies was that AMALEK DWELLS IN THE LAND OF THE SOUTH: so when a man has been bitten by a snake, if people want to frighten him they say, There is a snake here. ^sh-lach-10-80

**Sh'lach 10:81** So they said: That one that made war on you before is here, and where? In the land of the south, by which you have to enter. Straightway ALL THE CONGREGATION LIFTED UP THEIR VOICE AND CRIED: they doomed that night to be one of weeping for all generations. ^sh-lach-10-81

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Sh'lach 10:82.*

**Sh'lach 10:83** AND NOW, I PRAY THEE, LET THE POWER OF THE LORD BE GREAT. Observe that when God created man, He formed him on the supernal pattern, and placed his strength and power in the middle of the body where the heart is. ^sh-lach-10-83

**Sh'lach 11:84** Now the heart is closely attached to a place above it, to wit, the brain. ^sh-lach-11-84
