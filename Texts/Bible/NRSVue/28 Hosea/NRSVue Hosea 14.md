# Hosea 14 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Hosea 13|← Hosea 13]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · —

## A plea for repentance

**14:1** Return, O Israel, to the LORD your God, for you have stumbled because of your iniquity. ^14-1

**14:2** Take words with you and return to the LORD; say to him, “Take away all guilt; accept that which is good, and we will offer the fruit^[Gk Syr: Heb *bulls*] of our lips. ^14-2

**14:3** Assyria shall not save us; we will not ride upon horses; we will say no more, ‘Our God,’ to the work of our hands. In you the orphan finds mercy.” ^14-3

## Assurance of forgiveness

**14:4** I will heal their disloyalty; I will love them freely, for my anger has turned from them. ^14-4

**14:5** I will be like the dew to Israel; he shall blossom like the lily; he shall strike root like the forests of Lebanon.^[Cn: Heb *like Lebanon*] ^14-5

**14:6** His shoots shall spread out; his beauty shall be like the olive tree and his fragrance like that of Lebanon. ^14-6

**14:7** They shall again live beneath my^[Heb *his*] shadow; they shall flourish as a garden;^[Cn: Heb *they shall grow grain*] they shall blossom like the vine; their fragrance shall be like the wine of Lebanon. ^14-7

**14:8** O Ephraim, what have I^[Or *What more has Ephraim*] to do with idols? It is I who answer and look after you.^[Heb *him*] I am like an evergreen cypress; your fruit comes from me. ^14-8

**14:9** Those who are wise understand these things; those who are discerning know them. For the ways of the LORD are right, and the upright walk in them, but transgressors stumble in them. ^14-9
