## Mūla

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 06 - Chapter Five, part 4#^pg143|p. 128]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=143>))
> Dyczkowski numbers 5.97cd as 5.98ab.
>
> And it is said in the venerable Brahmayāmala that the illustrious (śrīmān) Sound (rāva) is of ten types. It issues from the heart, neck and palate, (each of which is) gross, subtle and supreme. Again, present everywhere (in all these types) is the All-pervading (Sound) (vibhu) that bestows the state of pervasion (vibhutva). (with 5.97cd–98)
