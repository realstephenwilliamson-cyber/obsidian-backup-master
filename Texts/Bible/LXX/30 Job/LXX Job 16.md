# LXX Job 16

← [[LXX Job 15]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Job 17]] →

*Job · [[LXX]]*

**16:1** But Job answered and said,  ^16-1

**16:2** I have heard many such things: poor comforters are ye all.  ^16-2

**16:3** What! is there any reason in vain words? or what will hinder thee from answering?  ^16-3

**16:4** I also will speak as ye *do*: if indeed your soul were in my *soul's* stead, then would I insult you with words, and I would shake my head at you.  ^16-4

**16:5** And would there were strength in my mouth, and I would not spare the movement of my lips.  ^16-5

**16:6** For if I should speak, I shall not feel the pain of my wound: and if I should be silent, how shall I be wounded the less?  ^16-6

**16:7** But now he has made me weary, and a worn-out fool; and thou hast laid hold of me.  ^16-7

**16:8** My falsehood has become a testimony, and has risen up against me: it has confronted me to my face.  ^16-8

**16:9** In his anger he has cast me down; he has gnashed his teeth upon me: the weapons of his robbers have fallen upon me.  ^16-9

**16:10** He has attacked me with the keen glances of his eyes; with his sharp *spear* he has smitten me *down* upon my knees; and they have run upon me with one accord.  ^16-10

**16:11** For the Lord has delivered me into the hands of unrighteous men, and thrown me upon the ungodly.  ^16-11

**16:12** When I was at peace he distracted me: he took me by the hair of the head, and plucked it out: he set me up as a mark.  ^16-12

**16:13** They surrounded me with spears, aiming at my reins: without sparing *me* they poured out my gall upon the ground.  ^16-13

**16:14** They overthrew me with fall upon fall: they ran upon me in *their* might.  ^16-14

**16:15** They sewed sackcloth upon my skin, and my strength has been spent on the ground.  ^16-15

**16:16** My belly has been parched with wailing, and darkness is on my eyelids.  ^16-16

**16:17** Yet there was no injustice in my hands, and my prayer is pure.  ^16-17

**16:18** Earth, cover not over the blood of my flesh, and let my cry have no place.  ^16-18

**16:19** And now, behold, my witness is in heaven, and my advocate is on high.  ^16-19

**16:20** Let my supplication come to the Lord, and let mine eye weep before him.  ^16-20

**16:21** Oh that a man might plead before the Lord, even *as* the son of man with his neighbour!  ^16-21

**16:22** But my years are numbered and *their end* come, and I shall go by the way by which I shall not return.  ^16-22
