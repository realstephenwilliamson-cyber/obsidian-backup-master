## Mūla

अवेद्या एव ते संस्युर्ज्ञाने सत्यपि वर्णिते ।
यथाहि पृथुबुध्नादिरूपे कुम्भस्य सत्यपि ॥ ^mula

avedyā eva te saṃsyurjñāne satyapi varṇite |
yathāhi pṛthubudhnādirūpe kumbhasya satyapi || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 10 - Chapter Ten, part 1#^pg331|p. 312]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=331>))
>
> Our reply (to all this) is that, if perceptibility were not to be a property of things, they would be imperceptible even when the (state of) cognitive consciousness (jñāna) (inherent in individual consciousness) described above arises. (with 10.28cd–29ab)
>
> A jar is an object with a large round base etc. A cloth differs from it. It does not assume the form (of a jar with its) large round base and the rest, even if (a jar) is present (near it). Similarly, how can entities that do not possess the quality of perceptibility come to have it, even if the cognitive consciousness (vijñāna) inherent in the subject is present (near it)? (with 10.29cd–31ab)
