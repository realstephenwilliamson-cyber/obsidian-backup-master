# Zohar 1.219a

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 3.07 - Parashat Va-Yhi#^z1-219a|Parashat Va-Yḥi, at 1:219a]]

*Volume 1, folio 219a* · Vayechi

*Passage Vayechi 21:171 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 1.218b#^vayechi-21-171|Vayechi 21:171]].*

**Vayechi 21:172** We have learnt that after seven days the body begins to decay, and the soul goes in to its place. It enters the cave of Machpelah, where it is allowed in up to a certain point according to its deserts. It then reaches the place of the Garden of Eden and meets the Cherubim and the flashing sword which is in the lower Garden of Eden, and if it is worthy to enter, it enters. ^vayechi-21-172

**Vayechi 21:173** We have learnt that four pillars[^1] are waiting there with the form of a body in their hands, and with this it gleefully clothes itself and then remains in its appointed circle in the Garden of Eden for its allotted time. Then a herald makes proclamation ^vayechi-21-173

**Vayechi 21:174** and a pillar of three colours is brought forward, which is called “the habitation of Mount Zion” (Is. 4, 5). By means of this pillar it ascends to the gate of righteousness, in which are Zion and Jerusalem. ^vayechi-21-174

**Vayechi 21:175** If it is worthy to ascend further, then happy is its portion and lot that it becomes attached to the Body of the King. If it is not worthy to ascend further, then “he that is left in Zion and he that remaineth in Jerusalem shall be called holy” (*Ibid.* 3). But if it is privileged to ascend further, then it beholds the glory of the King, and enjoys the supernal delight from the place which is called Heaven. Happy he that is vouchsafed this grace. ^vayechi-21-175

**Vayechi 21:176** R. Jose said: ‘There is a superior grace and an inferior grace. The superior grace is above the heavens, as it is written: “For great *above* the heaven is thy kindness” (Ps. 108, 5). Of the inferior grace it is written: “For great *unto* the heaven is thy kindness” (*Ibid.* 57, 11), and to this class belong the “faithful kindnesses of David” (Is. 55, 3).’ ^vayechi-21-176

**Vayechi 22:177** R. Isaac once questioned R. Simeon with regard to the verse, “a joyful mother of children”, saying: ‘I know what is meant by the mother, but who are the children?’ R. Simeon answered: ‘There are two children to God, one male and one female. The male he gave to Jacob, as it is written: “Israel is my son, my firstborn” (Ex. 4, 22). The female he gave to Abraham. ^vayechi-22-177

**Vayechi 22:178** The mother sits on the young and gives them suck; whence the precept, “Thou shalt not take the mother with the young.” Our teachers have said: “A man should beware of sinfulness below lest thereby the mother should be parted from the children.” ^vayechi-22-178

**Vayechi 22:179** But when men repent and act virtuously then the mother returns and shelters the young, and this is called “repentance” (*t’shubah*, lit. returning). Then, too, it can be said, “the mother of the children is joyful”. Hence a man should not cease from propagating his kind till he has a son and a daughter.’ ^vayechi-22-179

**Vayechi 22:180** R. Isaac was not yet satisfied. ‘The righteous’, he said, ‘desire no more than to “behold the pleasantness of the Lord” (Ps. 27, 4).’ R. Simeon answered: ‘It is all one, since this pleasantness comes from the Holy Ancient One to this heaven, and the desire of the righteous is fixed on that.’ ^vayechi-22-180

**Vayechi 23:181** R. Simeon further said: ‘It is written: “He hath cast down the earth from the heavens” (Lam. 2, 1). For when the Almighty resolved to destroy the Temple and to banish Israel among the nations, He removed from before Him the upper earth, and when that earth was put away from Him, then the lower earth was laid waste and Israel were banished among the nations; whereupon the Community of Israel said: “My mother’s sons were incensed against me” (S. S. 1, 6), and that was the cause of my downfall.’ ^vayechi-23-181

**Vayechi 23:182** R. Jose was once walking with R. Hiya the son of Rab, and as they were going along he said to him: ‘Do you see something over there?’ ‘I see a man in the river’, he answered, ‘and a bird on his head with a piece of flesh which it is eating and tearing with its claws. The man is crying out something, but I cannot catch what he says.’ ^vayechi-23-182

[^1]: i.e. angels.
