# Śāmbhavopāya — The Divine Path (Āhnika 3)

> [!abstract] Definition
> The direct intuitive pathway in *Tantrāloka* (Āhnika 3), where consciousness rests in pure thought-free awareness (*nirvikalpa*) through the awakening of sovereign will (*icchā-śakti*), without mental discursive thought (*vikalpa*).

## Operational Mechanics
- **Sudden Immersion (*Samāveśa*):** The yogi enters the gap between thoughts, where the mind dissolves into its ground.
- **The Mātṛkā Descent:** Āhnika 3 explores the cosmic structure of the phonemes as the manifestation of Śāmbhava energy.
