# Proletarianization of Knowledge

> [!abstract] Definition
> In Stieglerian political economy (extending Marx), proletarianization is not merely the impoverishment of wage workers, but the structural expropriation of knowledge, skill, and autonomy from human subjects into technical machines.

## Historical Phases of Proletarianization
1. **19th Century (Gestural Proletarianization):** The machine tool extracts the artisan's physical gesture and craftsmanship (*savoir-faire*), turning the worker into a machine tender.
2. **20th Century (Affective Proletarianization):** The culture industry, broadcast television, and consumer advertising extract the consumer's way of living (*savoir-vivre*), standardizing desires and behaviors.
3. **21st Century (Noetic Proletarianization):** Computational capitalism and AI extract theoretical knowledge (*savoir-penser*) and reflective synthesis. Generative systems produce essays, code, and analyses without the human experiencing cognitive formation.

## Manifestation in Education
In [[Second Domestication outline 2026-07-07|The Second Domestication]], institutional panic over AI-generated student essays is recognized as a symptom of prior proletarianization: educational systems long ago reduced education to verifiable informational outputs rather than preserving dialectical formation.
