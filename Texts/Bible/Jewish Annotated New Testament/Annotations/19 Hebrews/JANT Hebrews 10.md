### 10.1–18: Christ, a single sacrifice for sin

*[[NRSVue Hebrews 10#^10-1|Text]], p. 479* · Hebrews makes the argument that sacrifices prescribed in the Torah are not efficacious for eradicating sin, evident by their never-ending repetition. It is a curious argument, because there was never an expectation that sacrifices could cure people of sin. Rather, the Tanakh and Jewish tradition generally assumes human beings are flawed by nature. Even the Gospels in places suggest Jesus affirmed the sacrificial system ([[Matthew 05#^5-23|Mt 5.23–24]]; [[Mark 01#^1-44|Mk 1.44]]). In distinction to this understanding of expiatory sacrifice, Hebrews portrays Jesus’ sacrifice as a cure for sin, which therefore renders sacrifice obsolete. See 9.25–26; 10.11–14. ^10-1

**[[NRSVue Hebrews 10#^10-1|1]]:** *Shadow*, cf. 8.5. *True form*, i.e., things in themselves, as opposed to earthly copies. Hebrews reflects a Platonic ontology. *These realities*, lit., “events, activities” (Gk “pragmata”). *Continually offered*, see 9.25. On why the Law cannot achieve perfection, see 7.18–19,28; 9.9–10. ^10-1-2

**[[NRSVue Hebrews 10#^10-3|3–4]]:** Hebrews claims ritual sacrifice is only a *reminder of sin*, not the cure for it. Sin here means willful defiance of God, not simply errant behavior. ^10-3

**[[NRSVue Hebrews 10#^10-5|5–9]]:** [[Psalms 040#^40-7|Ps 40.6–8]] (HB 7–9), in its context a general statement about the relative merit of sacrifice, here applied to the arrival of Christ, who is cast in the role of the Psalm’s speaker. For the idea that sacrifices do not substitute for repentance, see [[Isaiah 01#^1-10|Isa 1.10–17]]; [[Jeremiah 07#^7-21|Jer 7.21–26]]; [[Psalms 050#^50-8|Ps 50.8–15]]; [[Psalms 051#^51-18|51.18–19]]; *b. Yoma* 86a-b quotes various traditions about the tremendous efficacy of repentance, including R. Hama bar Hanina, “Great is repentance; it brings healing to the world, as it is said, ‘I will heal their turnings. I will love them freely’” ([[Hosea 14#^14-5|Hos 14.5]]). ^10-5

**[[NRSVue Hebrews 10#^10-5|5]]:** *Came into the world*, Jewish expression for birth; see e.g., *m. Rosh Ha-Shanah* 1.2; *Sifre Deut.* 312. *Body you have prepared for me*, the reference is to [[Psalms 040#^40-7|Ps 40.7]]\[LXX\]. The Heb reads “ears you have dug for me.” Hebrews understands Jesus to be speaking (see 2.13n.). ^10-5-2

**[[NRSVue Hebrews 10#^10-6|6]]:** *Burnt offerings and sin offerings*, see “Sacrifice and the Temple,” p. 658. ^10-6

**[[NRSVue Hebrews 10#^10-7|7]]:** *See, God . . . me*, Jesus is speaking of himself. ^10-7

**[[NRSVue Hebrews 10#^10-8|8–9]]:** *He said . . . he added*, referring back to Ps 40 as quoted in vv 5–7, which Hebrews takes to be Christ’s words. *First . . . second*, in reference to covenants established, respectively, by Moses and Jesus. The Psalm’s first line represents the first covenant (the practices of sacrifice as prescribed in the Torah), and the second line—a body prepared for me—refers to Jesus’ unique self-sacrifice, which stands for the second covenant. ^10-8

**[[NRSVue Hebrews 10#^10-10|10]]:** *Sanctified*, cleansed of sin. ^10-10

**[[NRSVue Hebrews 10#^10-12|12–13]]:** [[Psalms 110#^110-1|Ps 110.1]] (see 1.13n.); in both the Tanakh and its reuse here, making *enemies* into a *footstool* refers to vanquishing them. ^10-12

**[[NRSVue Hebrews 10#^10-14|14]]:** *Single offering*, see 9.25–26. *Perfected*, see 2.10; 5.8; 7.28; 12.2. ^10-14

**[[NRSVue Hebrews 10#^10-15|15]]:** *Holy Spirit*, see 3.7; 9.8. ^10-15

**[[NRSVue Hebrews 10#^10-16|16–17]]:** A paraphrase of [[Jeremiah 31#^31-33|Jer 31.33]]; see also [[Hebrews 08#^8-10|Heb 8.10]]. *With them* replaces “the house of Israel,” and *their lawless deeds* is added. ^10-16

### 10.19–25: Exhortation to faithful endurance

*[[NRSVue Hebrews 10#^10-19|Text]], p. 480* ^10-19

**[[NRSVue Hebrews 10#^10-19|19]]:** *Friends* (“adelphoi”), lit., “brothers.” *Sanctuary*, see 9.24. ^10-19-2

**[[NRSVue Hebrews 10#^10-20|20]]:** Cf. 9.8. *Curtain*, see 6.19; 9.3. *His flesh* refers to Jesus’ sacrificial death. ^10-20

**[[NRSVue Hebrews 10#^10-21|21]]:** *Great priest*, see 4.14. *House of God*, see 3.5. ^10-21

**[[NRSVue Hebrews 10#^10-22|22]]:** *Washed with pure water*, perhaps a baptismal allusion. Cleansing with water is a common practice for achieving purity (see “Baptism and Eucharist,” p. 674). ^10-22

**[[NRSVue Hebrews 10#^10-23|23]]:** *Confession of our hope*, faith, not confession of sin; see 3.1; 4.14. *He who has promised*, referring to God. ^10-23

**[[NRSVue Hebrews 10#^10-25|25]]:** *Meet together*, perhaps gathering for worship; the Greek is “episynagōgē,” same root as “synagogue,” though it refers to the congregation, not a building. *Day*, Jesus’ return (the Parousia), related to traditions of the Day of the Lord; see e.g., [[Isaiah 02#^2-12|Isa 2.12]]; [[Joel 1#^1-15|Joel 1.15]]; [[Zechariah 14#^14-1|Zech 14.1]]; [[Revelation 16#^16-14|Rev 16.14]]. ^10-25

### 10.26–39: Warning about the day of judgment

*[[NRSVue Hebrews 10#^10-26|Text]], p. 480* ^10-26

**[[NRSVue Hebrews 10#^10-26|26–27]]:** See 6.4–6n. ^10-26-2

**[[NRSVue Hebrews 10#^10-26|26]]:** *Willfully persist*, there is a long tradition of distinguishing between willful and unwitting sinning; see [[Torah 24 - Vayikra#^4-1|Lev 4.1–5.13]]; [[Torah 37 - Sh'lach#^15-22|Num 15.22–31]]; Philo, *Cher.* 75; *m. Shabb.* 7.1; 11.6; *m. Ker.* 1.2. ^10-26-3

**[[NRSVue Hebrews 10#^10-27|27]]:** *Fury of fire*, such language is often associated with God’s condemnation of the wicked; see 12.29; also [[Torah 45 - Vaetchanan#^4-24|Deut 4.24]]; [[Isaiah 66#^66-15|Isa 66.15–16]]; [[Zephaniah 1#^1-18|Zeph 1.18]]. ^10-27

**[[NRSVue Hebrews 10#^10-28|28–29]]:** *Witnesses*, [[Torah 48 - Shoftim#^17-6|Deut 17.6]]; [[Torah 48 - Shoftim#^19-15|19.15]]. ^10-28

**[[NRSVue Hebrews 10#^10-29|29]]:** *How much worse* refers not to Jews who have rejected Jesus, but to Jesus’ followers who commit apostasy; the form of this argument is “qal vahomer”; see 9.14n. *Profaned the blood of the covenant*, spurning Jesus. ^10-29

**[[NRSVue Hebrews 10#^10-30|30]]:** *Vengeance is mine*, [[Torah 53 - Ha'Azinu#^32-35|Deut 32.35]]. *Lord will judge*, [[Torah 53 - Ha'Azinu#^32-36|Deut 32.36]]. ^10-30

**[[NRSVue Hebrews 10#^10-31|31]]:** *Living God*, see 3.12. ^10-31

**[[NRSVue Hebrews 10#^10-33|33]]:** *Persecution*, no empire-wide persecution of Christians occurred until 250–51. ^10-33

**[[NRSVue Hebrews 10#^10-34|34]]:** *Prison*, some early Christians were imprisoned; see Acts 12; 16; [[Philippians 02#^2-25|Phil 2.25]]; [[Philippians 04#^4-14|4.14–18]]. Prisoners relied on friends and family to bring them provisions. ^10-34

**[[NRSVue Hebrews 10#^10-36|36]]:** *Need endurance*, Maimonides said it was a Jew’s obligation to wait for the Messiah (*Mishneh Torah, Laws of Kings* 11). *Promised*, see 11.13,39–40. ^10-36

**[[NRSVue Hebrews 10#^10-37|37–38]]:** A paraphrase of [[LXX Habakkuk 02#^2-3|Hab 2.3–4]]\[LXX\] (see also [[Romans 01#^1-17|Rom 1.17]]; [[Galatians 03#^3-11|Gal 3.11]]). *My righteous . . . My soul*, “my” is not in Heb or LXX of Hab. ^10-37

**[[NRSVue Hebrews 10#^10-39|39]]:** *Are saved*, from the coming judgment. ^10-39
