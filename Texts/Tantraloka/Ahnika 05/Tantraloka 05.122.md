## Mūla

अत्र प्रयासविरहात् सर्वोऽसौ देवतागणः ।
आनन्दपूर्णे धाम्न्यास्ते नित्योदितचिदात्मकः ॥ ^mula

atra prayāsavirahāt sarvo'sau devatāgaṇaḥ |
ānandapūrṇe dhāmnyāste nityoditacidātmakaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 06 - Chapter Five, part 4#^pg177|p. 162]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=177>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 5.123.
>
> Here (in the supreme unmanifest Liñga), all this group of deities, which is eternally (active and) emergent (nityodita) consciousness, resides in the abode full of bliss, because (they and it are) free of (all) exertion.
