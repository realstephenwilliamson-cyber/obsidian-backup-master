# Sirach 16 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Sirach 15|← Sirach 15]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Sirach 17|Sirach 17 →]]

## God’s punishment of sinners

**16:1** Do not desire a multitude of worthless^[Heb: Gk *unprofitable*] children, and do not rejoice in ungodly offspring. ^16-1

**16:2** If they multiply, do not rejoice in them, unless the fear of the Lord is in them. ^16-2

**16:3** Do not trust in their survival or rely on their numbers,^[Other ancient authorities add *for you will groan in untimely mourning, and will know of their sudden end*] for one can be better than a thousand, and to die childless is better than to have ungodly children. ^16-3

**16:4** For through one intelligent person a city can be filled with people, but through a clan of outlaws it becomes desolate. ^16-4

**16:5** Many such things my eye has seen, and my ear has heard things more striking than these. ^16-5

**16:6** In an assembly of sinners a fire is kindled, and in a disobedient nation wrath has blazed up. ^16-6

**16:7** He did not forgive the ancient giants^[Gk: Heb *princes*] who revolted in their might. ^16-7

**16:8** He did not spare the neighbors of Lot, whom he loathed on account of their arrogance. ^16-8

**16:9** He showed no pity on the doomed nation, on those dispossessed because of their sins,^[Other ancient authorities add *all these things he did to the hard-hearted nations, and by the multitude of his holy ones he was not appeased*] ^16-9

**16:10** or on the six hundred thousand foot soldiers who assembled in their stubbornness.^[Other ancient authorities add *chastising, showing mercy, striking, healing, the Lord persisted in mercy and discipline*] ^16-10

**16:11** Even if there were only one stiff-necked person, it would be a wonder if he remained unpunished. For mercy and wrath are with the Lord;^[Gk *him*] he acquits and forgives,^[Heb: Gk *a master of propitiations*] but he also pours out wrath. ^16-11

**16:12** Great as is his mercy, so also is his chastisement; he judges people according to their deeds. ^16-12

**16:13** The sinner will not escape with plunder, and the patience of the godly will not be frustrated. ^16-13

**16:14** He makes room for every act of almsgiving; everyone receives in accordance with his or her deeds.^[Other ancient authorities add 16.15–16: *15The Lord hardened Pharaoh so that he did not recognize him, in order that his works might be known under heaven. 16His mercy is manifest to the whole of creation, and he apportioned his light and darkness to Adam.*] ^16-14

**16:17** Do not say, “I am hidden from the Lord, and who from on high has me in mind? Among so many people I am unknown, for what am I in a boundless creation? ^16-17

**16:18** Look, heaven and the highest heaven, the abyss and the earth tremble at his visitation!^[Other ancient authorities add *The whole world past and present is in his will.*] ^16-18

**16:19** Together the mountains and the foundations of the earth shake and tremble when he looks upon them. ^16-19

**16:20** But no human mind can grasp this, and who can comprehend his ways? ^16-20

**16:21** Like a tempest that no one can see, so most of his works are concealed.^[Meaning of Gk uncertain: Heb Syr *If I sin, no eye can see me, and if I am disloyal all in secret, who is to know?*] ^16-21

**16:22** Who is to announce his acts of justice? Or who can await them? For his decree^[Heb: Gk *the covenant*] is far off.”^[Other ancient authorities add *and a scrutiny for all comes at the end*] ^16-22

**16:23** Such are the thoughts of one devoid of understanding; a senseless and misguided person thinks foolishly. ^16-23

## God’s wisdom seen in creation

**16:24** Listen to me, my child, and acquire knowledge, and pay close attention to my words. ^16-24

**16:25** I will impart discipline precisely^[Gk *by weight*] and declare knowledge accurately. ^16-25

**16:26** When the Lord created^[Heb: Gk *judged*] his works from the beginning and, in making them, determined their boundaries, ^16-26

**16:27** he arranged their works in an eternal order and their dominion^[Or *elements*] for all generations. They neither hunger nor grow weary, and they do not abandon their tasks. ^16-27

**16:28** They do not crowd one another, and they never disobey his word. ^16-28

**16:29** Then the Lord looked upon the earth and filled it with his good things. ^16-29

**16:30** With all kinds of living beings he covered its surface, and into it they must return. ^16-30
