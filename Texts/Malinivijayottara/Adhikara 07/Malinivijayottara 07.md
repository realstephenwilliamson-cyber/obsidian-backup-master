# Mālinīvijayottara, chapter 7: Concerning the Gestures

36 verses, 1–36. Translation by Mark Dyczkowski, from *Mālinī*, July–September 2024: [PDF pp. 1–10](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=1>).

The print is ten scanned pages with the English and a drawing of each gesture, and no Sanskrit. The English was read from the page images by Claude (9 Nov 2026) ; the drawings are in the PDF. A second reader compared it with the page images on 10 Nov 2026. The Sanskrit in the verse notes is the KSTS text.

## Verses

- [[Malinivijayottara 07.001|7.1]] athātaḥ saṃpravakṣyāmi mudrākhyāḥ śivaśaktayaḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 07.002|7.2]] triśūlaṃ ca tathā padmaṃ śaktiścakraṃ savajrakam
- [[Malinivijayottara 07.003|7.3]] mahodayā karālā ca khaṭvāṅgaṃ sakapālakam
- [[Malinivijayottara 07.004|7.4]] āvāhasthāpanīrodhā dravyadā natireva ca

### 1. Triśūla – the Trident Gesture

- [[Malinivijayottara 07.005|7.5]] tarjanīmadhyamānāmā dakṣiṇasya prasāritāḥ

### 2. Padma – the Lotus Gesture

- [[Malinivijayottara 07.006|7.6]] padmākārau karau kṛtvā padmamudrāṃ pradarśayet

### 4. Cakra (the Wheel)

- [[Malinivijayottara 07.007|7.7]] prasṛte madhyame lagne kaumāryāḥ śaktiriṣyate

### 5. Vajra (the Thunderbolt)

- [[Malinivijayottara 07.008|7.8]] * * * kṣayenmuṣṭiṃ cakraṃ nārācaṃ * * * *
- [[Malinivijayottara 07.009|7.9]] kaniṣṭhāṅguṣṭhakau śliṣṭau śeṣāḥ syurmaṇibandhagāḥ

### 6. Daṇḍa (the Stick)

- [[Malinivijayottara 07.010|7.10]] ūrddhvaprasārito muṣṭirdakṣiṇo'ṅguṣṭhagarbhagaḥ

### 7. Daṃṣṭra (the Fang)

- [[Malinivijayottara 07.011|7.11]] vāmato vaktragāṃ kuryādvāmamuṣṭeḥ kaniṣṭhikām

### Mahāpretā (the Great Ghost)

- [[Malinivijayottara 07.012|7.12]] vāmajānugataṃ pādaṃ hastau pṛṣṭhapralambinau

### 8. Mahāmudrā (the Great Gesture)

- [[Malinivijayottara 07.013|7.13]] sarvayogigaṇasyeṣṭā prītā yogīśvarī matā
- [[Malinivijayottara 07.014|7.14]] tiryagmukhāntamupari saṃmukhāvūrdhvagau nayet

### 9. Khageśvarī (the Skyfaring Goddess)

- [[Malinivijayottara 07.015|7.15]] sarvakarmakarī caiṣā yogināṃ yogasiddhaye
- [[Malinivijayottara 07.016|7.16]] daṇḍākāraṃ tu taṃ bhāvaṃ nayedyāvatkakhatrayam

### 10. Mahodayā (the Great Arising)

- [[Malinivijayottara 07.017|7.17]] etāṃ baddhvā mahāvīraḥ khegatiṃ pratipadyate
- [[Malinivijayottara 07.018|7.18]] anāmāmadhyame tasya vāmāṅguṣṭhena pīḍayet
- [[Malinivijayottara 07.019|7.19]] madhyamānāmikābhyāṃ ca tadaṅguṣṭhaṃ nipīḍayet

### 11. Karālā (the Gaping Mouth and Protruding Teeth)

- [[Malinivijayottara 07.020|7.20]] anāmikākaniṣṭhābhyāṃ sṛkvaṇyau pravidārayet

### 12. Khaṭvāṅga (the Ascetic's Staff)

- [[Malinivijayottara 07.021|7.21]] kruddhadṛṣṭiḥ karāleyaṃ mudrā duṣṭabhayaṅkarī

### 13. Kapālaka (the Skull)

- [[Malinivijayottara 07.022|7.22]] khaṭvāṅgākhyā smṛtā mudrā kapālamadhunā śṛṇu

### 14. Hala (the Plough)

- [[Malinivijayottara 07.023|7.23]] kapālamiti vijñeyamadhunā halamucyate

### 15. Pāśa (the Fetter)

- [[Malinivijayottara 07.024|7.24]] vakratarjaninā grastā halamudreti kīrtitā

### 16. Aṅkuśa (the Elephant Gaud)

- [[Malinivijayottara 07.025|7.25]] vāmāṅguṣṭhāgrasaṃlagnaṃ pāśaḥ prasṛtakuñcitaḥ

### 17. Ghaṇṭā (the Uvula)

- [[Malinivijayottara 07.026|7.26]] adhomukhasthite vāme dakṣiṇāṃ tarjanīṃ budhaḥ

### 18. Mudgara (the Mallet) and Triśikhaa (the Three Tufts of Hair)

- [[Malinivijayottara 07.027|7.27]] karāvūrdhvamukhau kāryāvanyonyāntaritāṅgulī
- [[Malinivijayottara 07.028|7.28]] madhyame dve yute kārye kaniṣṭhe paruṣāvadhi

### 19. Aavaha (Invocation)

- [[Malinivijayottara 07.029|7.29]] mudgarastriśikho hyeṣa kṣaṇādāveśakārakaḥ

### 20. Sthāpanī (Installation)

- [[Malinivijayottara 07.030|7.30]] aṅguṣṭhau kalpayedvidvānmantrāvāhanakarmaṇi

### 21. Rodhā (Fixing)

- [[Malinivijayottara 07.031|7.31]] dvāveva garbhagāṅguṣṭhau vijñeyā saṃnirodhinī

### 23. Nati (Salutation)

- [[Malinivijayottara 07.032|7.32]] hṛdaye saṃmukhau hastau saṃlagnau prasṛtāṅgulī

### 24. Amṛtā (Nectar)

- [[Malinivijayottara 07.033|7.33]] anyonyāntaritāḥ sardhāḥ karayoraṅgulīḥ sthitāḥ
- [[Malinivijayottara 07.034|7.34]] dakṣiṇe ca tathā vāmāṃ tarjanīmadhyame tathā

### 25. Yogamudrā

- [[Malinivijayottara 07.035|7.35]] dakṣiṇaṃ nābhimūle tu vāmasyopari saṃsthitam
- [[Malinivijayottara 07.036|7.36]] evaṃ mudrāgaṇaṃ mantrī badhnīyāddhṛdaye budhaḥ
