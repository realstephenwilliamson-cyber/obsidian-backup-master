## Mūla

अथ वित्तविहीनानां प्रपन्नानां च तत्त्वतः ।
देशकालादिदोषेण न तथाध्यवसायिनाम् ॥ ^mula

atha vittavihīnānāṃ prapannānāṃ ca tattvataḥ |
deśakālādidoṣeṇa na tathādhyavasāyinām || ^iast

> I will (now) teach how the initiation should be performed as enjoined by Haṭakeśāna, lord of the netherworlds, in the Śrīsantatyāgama, for those who do not have money but are truly devoted, and those who, because of the defect of space and time etc., do not understand (things) in this way (as they truly are).
