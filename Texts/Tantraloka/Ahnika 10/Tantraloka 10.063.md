## Mūla

अन्यं प्रति चकास्तीति वच एव न विद्यते ।
सर्वान्प्रति च तन्नीलं स घटश्चेति यद्वचः ॥ ^mula

anyaṃ prati cakāstīti vaca eva na vidyate |
sarvānprati ca tannīlaṃ sa ghaṭaśceti yadvacaḥ || ^iast

> We who are the votaries of the doctrine that only Śiva exists and is self-luminous never say that He manifests to another. (with 10.62cd–63ab)
>
> Those of dull intellect say: “that colour blue and this jar (are equally visible) to everyone”, without understanding what is meant by that. (with 10.63cd–64ab)

*Lead-in:* न केवलमेकप्रमातृसतत्त्ववस्तुवादेनैवं युज्यते यावत्प्रमातृप्रमेययोस्तत्त्वतो भेदेऽपि -- इत्याह -
