# Psalm 88 (NRSVue)

## Prayer for help in despondency

*A Song. A Psalm of the Korahites. To the leader: according to Mahalath Leannoth. A Maskil of*

*Heman the Ezrahite.*

**88:1** O LORD, God of my salvation, at night, when I cry out before you, ^88-1

**88:2** let my prayer come before you; incline your ear to my cry. ^88-2

**88:3** For my soul is full of troubles, and my life draws near to Sheol. ^88-3

**88:4** I am counted among those who go down to the Pit; I am like those who have no help, ^88-4

**88:5** like those forsaken among the dead, like the slain that lie in the grave, like those whom you remember no more, for they are cut off from your hand. ^88-5

**88:6** You have put me in the depths of the Pit, in the regions dark and deep. ^88-6

**88:7** Your wrath lies heavy upon me, and you overwhelm me with all your waves. *Selah* ^88-7

**88:8** You have caused my companions to shun me; you have made me a thing of horror to them. I am shut in so that I cannot escape; ^88-8

**88:9** my eye grows dim through sorrow. Every day I call on you, O LORD; I spread out my hands to you. ^88-9

**88:10** Do you work wonders for the dead? Do the shades rise up to praise you? *Selah* ^88-10

**88:11** Is your steadfast love declared in the grave or your faithfulness in Abaddon? ^88-11

**88:12** Are your wonders known in the darkness or your saving help in the land of forgetfulness? ^88-12

**88:13** But I, O LORD, cry out to you; in the morning my prayer comes before you. ^88-13

**88:14** O LORD, why do you cast me off? Why do you hide your face from me? ^88-14

**88:15** Wretched and close to death from my youth up, I suffer your terrors; I am desperate.^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^88-15

**88:16** Your wrath has swept over me; your dread assaults destroy me. ^88-16

**88:17** They surround me like a flood all day long; from all sides they close in on me. ^88-17

**88:18** You have caused friend and neighbor to shun me; my companions are in darkness. ^88-18
