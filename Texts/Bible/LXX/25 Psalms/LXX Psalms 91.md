# LXX Psalms 91

← [[LXX Psalms 90]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 92]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 092]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 092]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**91:1** A Psalm of a Song for the Sabbath-day.  ^91-1

**91:2** It is a good thing to give thanks to the Lord, and to sing praises to thy name, O thou Most High;  ^91-2

**91:3** to proclaim thy mercy in the morning, and thy truth by night,  ^91-3

**91:4** on a psaltery of ten strings, with a song on the harp.  ^91-4

**91:5** For thou, O Lord, hast made me glad with thy work: and in the operations of thy hands will I exult.  ^91-5

**91:6** How have thy works been magnified, O Lord! thy thoughts are very deep.  ^91-6

**91:7** A foolish man will not know, and a senseless man will not understand this.  ^91-7

**91:8** When the sinners spring up as the grass, and all the workers of iniquity have watched; *it is* that they may be utterly destroyed for ever.[^1]  ^91-8

**91:9** But thou, O Lord, art most high for ever.  ^91-9

**91:10** For, behold, thine enemies shall perish; and all the workers of iniquity shall be scattered.[^2]  ^91-10

**91:11** But my horn shall be exalted as *the horn* of a unicorn; and mine old age with rich mercy.[^3]  ^91-11

**91:12** And mine eye has seen mine enemies, and mine ear shall hear the wicked that rise up against me.  ^91-12

**91:13** The righteous shall flourish as a palm-tree: he shall be increased as the cedar in Libanus.  ^91-13

**91:14** They that are planted in the house of the Lord shall flourish in the courts of our God.  ^91-14

**91:15** Then shall they be increased in a fine old age; and they shall be prosperous; that they may declare[^4]  ^91-15

**91:16** that the Lord my God is righteous, and there is no iniquity in him.  ^91-16

[^1]: 91:8 Perhaps, 'come to light'.
[^2]: 91:10 *Alex.*+ for behold thine enemies, O Lord, as in *Heb.*
[^3]: 91:11 *Alex.* oil. See Ps. 88. 20.
[^4]: 91:15 *Gr.* fat.
