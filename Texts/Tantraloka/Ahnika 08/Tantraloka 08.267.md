## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 08 - Chapter Eight, part 6#^pg293|p. 275]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=293>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 8.267cd–268ab.
>
> (There we read that) ‘the wombs (yoni) are the Bestowers of Yoga who, beyond (that), reside within the section of the qualities. These are, in due order: 1) Akrta, 2) Krta, 3) Rbhu, 4) Brahmā, 5) Viṣṇu, 6) Kumāra and then (finally), 7) the Goddess. They are (divine beings who possess) sense organs, yogic powers, including the power to make themselves small at will (anima) and the rest, the products (of Nature), the extending expanse of perceptions (pratyayaprapañca), the qualities of sattva and the rest generated from unmanifest Nature (avyakta). From the qualities (guṇa in which they reside), the Bestowers of Yoga dispense righteousness (dharma), knowledge, dispassion, lordship and their various fruits to men. Beyond them is the world of Śrīkaṇṭhanātha. He is the Lord and god who, enthroned on the qualities of sattva and the rest, is the sustainer and sole experiencing subject of the entire universe.’ (with 8.263cd–267)

## Viveka

> योनीरतीत्य गौणे स्कन्धे स्युर्योगदातारः ।
> अकृतकृतविभुविरिञ्चा हरिर्गुहः क्रमवशात्ततो देवी ॥
> करणान्यणिमादिगुणाः कार्याणि प्रत्ययप्रपञ्चश्च ।
> अव्यक्तादुत्पन्ना गुणाश्च सत्त्वादयोऽमीषाम् ॥
> धर्मज्ञानविरागानैश्वर्यं तत्फलानि विविधानि ।
> यच्छन्ति गुणेभ्योऽमी पुरुषेभ्यो योगदातारः ॥
> तेभ्यः परतो भुवनं सत्त्वादिगुणासनस्य देवस्य ।
> सकलजगदेकमातुर्भर्तुः श्रीकण्ठनाथस्य ॥

"प्रत्ययप्रपञ्चः"इति विपर्ययादिः पञ्चाशदाद्यः । "अमीषाम्"अकृतादीनां योगदातॄणाम् । एतच्चैषां दातृत्वं यत्परेभ्योऽपि धर्मादि प्रयच्छन्तीति । "परतः"इत्यूर्ध्वम् ॥ २६७ ॥
