# Sirach 4 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Sirach 03|← Sirach 3]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Sirach 05|Sirach 5 →]]

## Duties toward the poor and the oppressed

**4:1** My child, do not cheat the poor of their living, and do not keep needy eyes waiting. ^4-1

**4:2** Do not grieve the hungry or anger one in need. ^4-2

**4:3** Do not add to the troubles of the angry or delay giving to the needy. ^4-3

**4:4** Do not reject a suppliant in distress or turn your face away from the poor. ^4-4

**4:5** Do not avert your eye from the needy, and give no one reason to curse you, ^4-5

**4:6** for if in bitterness of soul some should curse you, their Creator will hear their prayer. ^4-6

**4:7** Endear yourself to the congregation; bow your head low to the great. ^4-7

**4:8** Give a hearing to the poor, and return their greeting kindly. ^4-8

**4:9** Rescue the oppressed from the oppressor, and do not be hesitant in giving a verdict. ^4-9

**4:10** Be a father to orphans, and be like a husband to their mother; you will then be like a son of the Most High, and he will love you more than does your mother. ^4-10

## The rewards of wisdom

**4:11** Wisdom teaches^[Heb Syr: Gk *exalts*] her children and takes hold of those who seek her. ^4-11

**4:12** Whoever loves her loves life, and those who seek her from early morning will be filled with joy. ^4-12

**4:13** Whoever holds her fast inherits glory, and the Lord blesses the place she^[Or *he*] enters. ^4-13

**4:14** Those who serve her minister to the Holy One; the Lord loves those who love her. ^4-14

**4:15** Whoever obeys her will judge the nations, and whoever listens to her will live securely. ^4-15

**4:16** If they remain faithful, they will inherit her; their descendants will also obtain her. ^4-16

**4:17** For at first she will walk with them in disguise; she will bring fear and dread upon them and will torment them by her discipline until she trusts them,^[Or *until they remain faithful in their heart*] and she will test them with her ordinances. ^4-17

**4:18** Then she will come straight back to them again and gladden them and will reveal her secrets to them. ^4-18

**4:19** If they go astray, she will forsake them and hand them over to their ruin. ^4-19

**4:20** Watch for the opportune time, and beware of evil, and do not be ashamed to be yourself. ^4-20

**4:21** For there is a shame that leads to sin, and there is a shame that is glory and favor. ^4-21

**4:22** Do not show partiality to your own harm or deference to your downfall. ^4-22

**4:23** Do not refrain from speaking at the proper moment,^[Heb: Gk *at a time of salvation*] and do not hide your wisdom.^[Other ancient authorities lack *and do not hide your wisdom*] ^4-23

**4:24** For wisdom becomes known through speech and education through the words of the tongue. ^4-24

**4:25** Never speak against the truth, but be ashamed of your ignorance. ^4-25

**4:26** Do not be ashamed to confess your sins, and do not try to stop the current of a river. ^4-26

**4:27** Do not subject yourself to a fool or show partiality to a ruler. ^4-27

**4:28** Fight to the death for truth, and the Lord God will fight for you. ^4-28

**4:29** Do not be reckless in your speech or sluggish and remiss in your deeds. ^4-29

**4:30** Do not be like a lion in your home or suspicious of your slaves. ^4-30

**4:31** Do not let your hand be stretched out to receive and closed when it is time to give back. ^4-31
