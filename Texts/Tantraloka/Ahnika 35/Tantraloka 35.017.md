## Mūla

अपूर्णास्तु परे तेन न मोक्षफलभागिनः ।
उपजीवन्ति यावत्तु तावत्तत्फलभागिनः ॥ ^mula

apūrṇāstu pare tena na mokṣaphalabhāginaḥ |
upajīvanti yāvattu tāvattatphalabhāginaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 09 - Chapter Thirty-Five#^pg217|p. 199]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=217>))
>
> It due to this (certainty) alone that the wise, in the stream of the business of (their) daily life, depend from their childhood only on the Śaiva revelation (āgama), which is the first of all. The others (who do not do so) are incomplete (and imperfect), and so do not partake of the fruit of liberation. (with 35.16–17ab)
>
> Indeed, (these others) partake of the fruit corresponding to the degree (of elevation of the revelation) they take as their support. (with 35.17cd)
