## BIBLIOGRAPHY

Almost all the direct quotations in this book can be found in the various English editions of the fathers. Most of them, how¬ ever, have been checked and corrected on the basis of the original Greek and Latin, or newly translated by the present author. The major exception to this is, of course, the Epideixis or Proof of the Apostolic Preaching by Irenaeus, which is avail¬ able in an Armenian version and in two English translations based upon that, but in neither Greek nor Latin manuscripts.
