# Proverbs 22

← [[Proverbs 21]] · [[Tanakh]] · [[Proverbs 23]] →

Compare: [[NRSVue Proverbs 22|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 22

**22:1** Repute is preferable to great wealth,  
Grace is better than silver and gold. ^22-1

**22:2** Rich man and poor man meet;  
The LORD made them both. ^22-2

**22:3** The shrewd man saw trouble and took cover;  
The simple kept going and paid the penalty. ^22-3

**22:4** The effect of humility is fear of the LORD,  
Wealth, honor, and life. ^22-4

**22:5** Thorns and snares are in the path of the crooked;  
He who values his life will keep far from them. ^22-5

**22:6** Train a lad in the way he ought to go;  
He will not swerve from it even in old age. ^22-6

**22:7** The rich rule the poor,  
And the borrower is a slave to the lender. ^22-7

**22:8** He who sows injustice shall reap misfortune;  
His rod of wrath shall fail. ^22-8

**22:9** The generous man is blessed,  
For he gives of his bread to the poor. ^22-9

**22:10** Expel the scoffer and contention departs,  
Quarrel and contumely cease. ^22-10

**22:11** A pure-hearted friend,  
His speech is gracious;  
He has the king for his companion. ^22-11

**22:12** The eyes of the LORD watch the wise man;  
He subverts the words of the treacherous. ^22-12

**22:13** The lazy man says, “There’s a lion in the street;  
I shall be killed if I step outside.”[^1] ^22-13

**22:14** The mouth of a forbidden[^2] woman is a deep pit;  
He who is doomed by the LORD falls into it. ^22-14

**22:15** If folly settles in the heart of a lad,  
The rod of discipline will remove it. ^22-15

**22:16** To profit by withholding what is due to the poor  
Is like making gifts to the rich—pure loss. ^22-16

**22:17** Incline your ear and listen to the words of the sages;  
Pay attention to my wisdom. ^22-17

**22:18** It is good that you store them inside you,  
And that all of them be constantly on your lips, ^22-18

**22:19** That you may put your trust in the LORD.  
I let you know today—yes, you— ^22-19

**22:20** Indeed, I wrote down for you a threefold lore,[^3]  
Wise counsel, ^22-20

**22:21** To let you know truly reliable words,  
That you may give a faithful reply to him who sent you. ^22-21

**22:22** Do not rob the wretched because he is wretched;  
Do not crush the poor man in the gate; ^22-22

**22:23** For the LORD will take up their cause  
And despoil those who despoil them of life. ^22-23

**22:24** Do not associate with an irascible man,  
Or go about with one who is hot-tempered, ^22-24

**22:25** Lest you learn his ways  
And find yourself ensnared. ^22-25

**22:26** Do not be one of those who give their hand,  
Who stand surety for debts, ^22-26

**22:27** Lest your bed be taken from under you  
When you have no money to pay. ^22-27

**22:28** Do not remove the ancient boundary stone  
That your ancestors set up. ^22-28

**22:29** See a man skilled at his work—  
He shall attend upon kings;  
He shall not attend upon obscure men.[^4] ^22-29

[^1]: Lit. “in the square.”
[^2]: Lit. “strange.”
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
