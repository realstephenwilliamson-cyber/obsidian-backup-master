# Psalm 29

← [[BCP Psalm 028]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 030]] →

*Afferte Domino* · Book One · Fifth Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 029]]

**29:1** Ascribe to the LORD, you gods, \*  
ascribe to the LORD glory and strength. ^29-1

**29:2** Ascribe to the LORD the glory due his Name; \*  
worship the LORD in the beauty of holiness. ^29-2

**29:3** The voice of the LORD is upon the waters;  
the God of glory thunders; \*  
the LORD is upon the mighty waters. ^29-3

**29:4** The voice of the LORD is a powerful voice; \*  
the voice of the LORD is a voice of splendor. ^29-4

**29:5** The voice of the LORD breaks the cedar trees; \*  
the LORD breaks the cedars of Lebanon; ^29-5

**29:6** He makes Lebanon skip like a calf, \*  
and Mount Hermon like a young wild ox. ^29-6

**29:7** The voice of the LORD splits the flames of fire;  
the voice of the LORD shakes the wilderness; \*  
the LORD shakes the wilderness of Kadesh. ^29-7

**29:8** The voice of the LORD makes the oak trees writhe \*  
and strips the forests bare. ^29-8

**29:9** And in the temple of the LORD \*  
all are crying, "Glory!" ^29-9

**29:10** The LORD sits enthroned above the flood; \*  
the LORD sits enthroned as King for evermore. ^29-10

[p. 621] ^pg621

**29:11** The LORD shall give strength to his people; \*  
the LORD shall give his people the blessing of peace. ^29-11
