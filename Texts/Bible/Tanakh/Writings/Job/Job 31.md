# Job 31

← [[Job 30]] · [[Tanakh]] · [[Job 32]] →

Compare: [[NRSVue Job 31|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 31

**31:1** I have covenanted with my eyes  
Not to gaze on a maiden. ^31-1

**31:2** What fate is decreed by God above?  
What lot, by Shaddai in the heights? ^31-2

**31:3** Calamity is surely for the iniquitous;  
Misfortune, for the worker of mischief. ^31-3

**31:4** Surely He observes my ways,  
Takes account of my every step. ^31-4

**31:5** Have I walked with worthless men,  
Or my feet hurried to deceit? ^31-5

**31:6** Let Him weigh me on the scale of righteousness;  
Let God ascertain my integrity. ^31-6

**31:7** If my feet have strayed from their course,  
My heart followed after my eyes,  
And a stain sullied my hands, ^31-7

**31:8** May I sow, but another reap,  
May the growth of my field be uprooted! ^31-8

**31:9** If my heart was ravished by the wife of my neighbor,  
And I lay in wait at his door, ^31-9

**31:10** May my wife grind for another,  
May others kneel over her! ^31-10

**31:11** For that would have been debauchery,  
A criminal offense, ^31-11

**31:12** A fire burning down to Abaddon,  
Consuming the roots of all my increase. ^31-12

**31:13** Did I ever brush aside the case of my servants, man or maid,  
When they made a complaint against me? ^31-13

**31:14** What then should I do when God arises;  
When He calls me to account, what should I answer Him? ^31-14

**31:15** Did not He who made me in my mother’s belly make him?  
Did not One form us both in the womb? ^31-15

**31:16** Did I deny the poor their needs,  
Or let a[^1] widow pine away, ^31-16

**31:17** By eating my food alone,  
The fatherless not eating of it also? ^31-17

**31:18** Why, from my youth he grew up with me as though I were his father;  
Since I left my mother’s womb I was her[^2] guide. ^31-18

**31:19** I never saw an unclad wretch,  
A needy man without clothing, ^31-19

**31:20** Whose loins did not bless me  
As he warmed himself with the shearings of my sheep. ^31-20

**31:21** If I raised my hand against the fatherless,  
Looking to my supporters in the gate, ^31-21

**31:22** May my arm drop off my shoulder;  
My forearm break off at the elbow.[^3] ^31-22

**31:23** For I am in dread of God-sent calamity;  
I cannot bear His threat. ^31-23

**31:24** Did I put my reliance on gold,  
Or regard fine gold as my bulwark? ^31-24

**31:25** Did I rejoice in my great wealth,  
In having attained plenty? ^31-25

**31:26** If ever I saw the light shining,  
The moon on its course in full glory, ^31-26

**31:27** And I secretly succumbed,  
And my hand touched my mouth in a kiss, ^31-27

**31:28** That, too, would have been a criminal offense,  
For I would have denied God above. ^31-28

**31:29** Did I rejoice over my enemy’s misfortune?  
Did I thrill because evil befell him? ^31-29

**31:30** I never let my mouth[^4] sin  
By wishing his death in a curse. ^31-30

**31:31** (Indeed, the men of my clan said,  
“We would consume his flesh insatiably!”) ^31-31

**31:32** No sojourner spent the night in the open;  
I opened my doors to the road. ^31-32

**31:33** Did I hide my transgressions like Adam,  
Bury my wrongdoing in my bosom, ^31-33

**31:34** That I should [now] fear the great multitude,  
And am shattered by the contempt of families,  
So that I keep silent and do not step outdoors? ^31-34

**31:35** O that I had someone to give me a hearing;  
O that Shaddai would reply to my writ,  
Or my accuser draw up a true bill! ^31-35

**31:36** I would carry it on my shoulder;  
Tie it around me for a wreath. ^31-36

**31:37** I would give him an account of my steps,  
Offer it as to a commander. ^31-37

**31:38** If my land cries out against me,  
Its furrows weep together; ^31-38

**31:39** If I have eaten its produce without payment,  
And made its [rightful] owners despair, ^31-39

**31:40** May nettles grow there instead of wheat;  
Instead of barley, stinkweed!  
The words of Job are at an end. ^31-40

[^1]: Lit. “the eyes of a.”
[^2]: Viz., the widow’s.
[^3]: Lit. “from its shaft,” i.e., the humerus.
[^4]: Lit. “palate.”
