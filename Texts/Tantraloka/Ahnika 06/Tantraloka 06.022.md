## Mūla

द्विधा च सोऽध्वा क्रियया मूर्त्या च प्रविभज्यते ।
प्राण एव शिखा श्रीमत्त्रिशिरस्युदिता हि सा ॥ ^mula

dvidhā ca so'dhvā kriyayā mūrtyā ca pravibhajyate |
prāṇa eva śikhā śrīmattriśirasyuditā hi sā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 08 - Chapter Six, part 1#^pg252|p. 237]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=252>))
>
> Enough of what is not the subject (we set out to discuss). (Let us) now deliberate on the original subject (of this chapter). All this Path is established (and grounded) in the vital breath (prāṇa). As such, the Path is divided into two kinds – (one differentiated) by action (kriyā) and (the other) by form (mūrti). (with 6.21–22ab)
>
> It is said in the venerable Triśirasa that the Topknot (śikhā) is the vital breath (prāṇa) itself. Tied during the sacrifice (yāga) and other (such rites), it is formless (niṣkalā). Thus, it is (transcendental) Śiva by nature. (with 6.22cd–23ab)
