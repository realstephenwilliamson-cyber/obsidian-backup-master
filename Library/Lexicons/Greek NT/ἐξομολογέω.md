# ἐξομολογέω

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · verb

**Dodson:** I consent fully, confess — (a) I consent fully, agree out and out, (b) I confess, admit, acknowledge (cf. the early Hellenistic sense of the middle: I acknowledge a debt), (c) I give thanks, praise. (Strong's 1843; headword ἐξομολογέω)
