# Psalms 38 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 37|← Psalms 37]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 39|Psalms 39 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 38|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**38:1** Unto the end, for Idithun himself, a canticle of David. ^38-1

**38:2** I said: I will take heed to my ways: that I sin not with my tongue. I have set guard to my mouth, when the sinner stood against me. ^38-2

**38:3** I was dumb, and was humbled, and kept silence from good things: and my sorrow was renewed. ^38-3

**38:4** My heart grew hot within me: and in my meditation a fire shall flame out. ^38-4

**38:5** I spoke with my tongue: O Lord, make me know my end. And what is the number of my days: that I may know what is wanting to me. ^38-5

**38:6** Behold thou hast made my days measurable: and my substance is as nothing before thee. And indeed all things are vanity: every man living. ^38-6

**38:7** Surely man passeth as an image: yea, and he is disquieted in vain. He storeth up: and he knoweth not for whom he shall gather these things. ^38-7

**38:8** And now what is my hope? is it not the Lord? and my substance is with thee. ^38-8

**38:9** Deliver thou me from all my iniquities: thou hast made me a reproach to the fool. ^38-9

**38:10** I was dumb, and I opened not my mouth, because thou hast done it. ^38-10

**38:11** Remove thy scourges from me. The strength of thy hand hath made me faint in rebukes: ^38-11

**38:12** Thou hast corrected man for iniquity. And thou hast made his soul to waste away like a spider: surely in vain is any man disquieted. ^38-12

**38:13** Hear my prayer, O Lord, and my supplication: give ear to my tears. Be not silent: for I am a stranger with thee, and a sojourner as all my fathers were. ^38-13

**38:14** O forgive me, that I may be refreshed, before I go hence, and be no more. ^38-14
