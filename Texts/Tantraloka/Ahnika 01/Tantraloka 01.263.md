## Mūla

निर्विकल्पितमुद्देशो विकल्पो लक्षणं पुनः ।
परीक्षणं तथाध्यक्षे विकल्पानां परम्परा ॥ ^mula

nirvikalpitamuddeśo vikalpo lakṣaṇaṃ punaḥ |
parīkṣaṇaṃ tathādhyakṣe vikalpānāṃ paramparā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. I
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 01 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v01 12 - Chapter One, part 11#^pg453|p. 439]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 01.pdf#page=453>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 1.264cd–265ab.
>
> (In relation to direct perception,) enunciation (corresponds to the first moment of perception, which is) not conceptualized. Definition (corresponds to the initial) conceptualization (of the object perceived) (vikalpa). Examination (corresponds to) the series of conceptions (concerning what) has been perceived directly in this way (initially).
