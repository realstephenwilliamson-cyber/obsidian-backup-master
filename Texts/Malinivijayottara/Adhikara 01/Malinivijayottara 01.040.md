## Mūla

हेयोपादेयविज्ञानं कथयन्ति शिवोदितम् ।
ब्रह्मादिस्तम्बपर्यन्ते जातमात्रे जगत्यलम् ॥ ^mula

heyopādeyavijñānaṁ kathayanti śivoditam |
brahmādistambaparyante jātamātre jagaty alam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2022 – June 2022
> p. 46 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=22>))
>
> These, having assumed their own sphere (of authority) (*gocara*), dispense to those like Brahmā, who aspire to liberation and worldly benefits (*bhoga*), (their appropriate) fruit accord to their own power. They too subsequently instruct the seers (*ṛṣi*) and these great lords, the Manus, the knowledge of what should be adopted and discarded, revealed by Śiva. (with 1.38cd–40ab)

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2022 – June 2022
> p. 47 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=23>))
>
> When (all) the universe, beginning with Brahmā down to a pillar, had just come into being, thirty-five million Mantras were applied by Śiva (to their tasks) and, having graced the mass of souls, they went to the plane free of sickness (and death).[^77] (with 1.40cd–41)

[^77]: Quoted in TĀv ad 8/344cd-345ab (344).
