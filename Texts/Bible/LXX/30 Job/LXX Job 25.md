# LXX Job 25

← [[LXX Job 24]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Job 26]] →

*Job · [[LXX]]*

**25:1** Then Baldad the Sauchite answered and said,  ^25-1

**25:2** What beginning or fear is his—even he that makes all things in the highest?  ^25-2

**25:3** For let none think that there is a respite for robbers: and upon whom will there not come a snare from him?  ^25-3

**25:4** For how shall a mortal be just before the Lord? or who that is born of a woman shall purify himself?  ^25-4

**25:5** If he gives an order to the moon, then it shines not; and the stars are not pure before him.  ^25-5

**25:6** But alas! man is corruption, and the son of man a worm.  ^25-6
