## Chapter 7

**7:1** My son, heed my words;  
And store up my commandments with you. ^7-1

**7:2** Keep my commandments and live,  
My teaching, as the apple of your eye. ^7-2

**7:3** Bind them on your fingers;  
Write them on the tablet of your mind. ^7-3

**7:4** Say to Wisdom, “You are my sister,”  
And call Understanding a kinswoman. ^7-4

**7:5** She will guard you from a forbidden[^1] woman;  
From an alien woman whose talk is smooth. ^7-5

**7:6** From the window of my house,  
Through my lattice, I looked out ^7-6

**7:7** And saw among the simple,  
Noticed among the youths,  
A lad devoid of sense. ^7-7

**7:8** He was crossing the street near her corner,  
Walking toward her house ^7-8

**7:9** In the dusk of evening,  
In the dark hours of night. ^7-9

**7:10** A woman comes toward him  
Dressed like a harlot, with set purpose.[^2] ^7-10

**7:11** She is bustling and restive;  
She is never at home. ^7-11

**7:12** Now in the street, now in the square,  
She lurks at every corner. ^7-12

**7:13** She lays hold of him and kisses him;  
Brazenly she says to him, ^7-13

**7:14** “I had to make a sacrifice of well-being;  
Today I fulfilled my vows. ^7-14

**7:15** Therefore I have come out to you,  
Seeking you, and have found you. ^7-15

**7:16** I have decked my couch with covers  
Of dyed Egyptian linen; ^7-16

**7:17** I have sprinkled my bed  
With myrrh, aloes, and cinnamon. ^7-17

**7:18** Let us drink our fill of love till morning;  
Let us delight in amorous embrace. ^7-18

**7:19** For the man of the house is away;  
He is off on a distant journey. ^7-19

**7:20** He took his bag of money with him  
And will return only at mid-month.” ^7-20

**7:21** She sways him with her eloquence,  
Turns him aside with her smooth talk. ^7-21

**7:22** Thoughtlessly he follows her,  
Like an ox going to the slaughter,  
Like a fool to the stocks for punishment[^3]— ^7-22

**7:23** Until the arrow pierces his liver.  
He is like a bird rushing into a trap,  
Not knowing his life is at stake. ^7-23

**7:24** Now, sons, listen to me;  
Pay attention to my words; ^7-24

**7:25** Let your mind not wander down her ways;  
Do not stray onto her paths. ^7-25

**7:26** For many are those she has struck dead,  
And numerous are her victims. ^7-26

**7:27** Her house is a highway to Sheol  
Leading down to Death’s inner chambers. ^7-27

[^1]: Lit. “strange.”
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
