## Chapter 7

**7:1** The word of the LORD came to me: ^7-1

**7:2** You, O mortal, [say:] Thus said the Lord GOD to the land of Israel: Doom! Doom is coming upon the four corners of the land. ^7-2

**7:3** Now doom is upon you! I will let loose My anger against you and judge you according to your ways; I will requite you for all your abominations. ^7-3

**7:4** I will show you no pity and no compassion; but I will requite you for your ways and for the abominations in your midst. And you shall know that I am the LORD. ^7-4

**7:5** Thus said the Lord GOD: A singular disaster; a disaster[^1] is coming. ^7-5

**7:6** Doom is coming! The hour of doom is coming! It stirs against you; there it comes! ^7-6

**7:7** The cycle has come around for you, O inhabitants of the land; the time has come; the day is near. There is panic on the mountains, not joy.[^2] ^7-7

**7:8** Very soon I will pour out My wrath upon you and spend My anger on you; I will judge you according to your ways, and I will requite you for all your abominations. ^7-8

**7:9** I will show you no pity and no compassion; but I will requite you for your ways, and for the abominations in your midst. And you shall know it was I the LORD who punished. ^7-9

**7:10** Here is the day! See, the [^3]cycle has come round; it has appeared. The rod has blossomed; arrogance has budded, ^7-10

**7:11** lawlessness has grown into a rod of wickedness. Nothing comes of them, nor of their abundance, nor of their wealth; nor is there preeminence among them. ^7-11

**7:12** The time has come, the day has arrived. Let not the buyer rejoice nor the seller mourn—for divine wrath shall overtake all her multitude. ^7-12

**7:13** For the seller shall not return to what he sold so long as they remain among the living. For the vision concerns all her multitude, it shall not be revoked. And because of his guilt, no man shall hold fast to his life. ^7-13

**7:14** They have sounded the horn, and all is prepared; but no one goes to battle, for My wrath is directed against all her multitude. ^7-14

**7:15** The sword is outside and pestilence and famine are inside; he who is in the open shall die by the sword, he who is in the town shall be devoured by famine and pestilence. ^7-15

**7:16** And if any survive, they shall take to the mountains; they shall be like doves of the valley, moaning together[^4]—every one for his iniquity. ^7-16

**7:17** All hands shall grow weak, and all knees shall turn to water. ^7-17

**7:18** They shall gird on sackcloth, and horror shall cover them; every face shall betray shame, and every head shall be made bald. ^7-18

**7:19** They shall throw their silver into the streets, and their gold shall be treated as something unclean. Their silver and gold shall not avail to save them in the day of the LORD’s wrath—to satisfy their hunger or to fill their stomachs. Because they made them stumble into guilt— ^7-19

**7:20** for out of their beautiful adornments, in which they took pride, they made their images and their detestable abominations—therefore I will make them[^5] an unclean thing to them. ^7-20

**7:21** I will give them as spoil to strangers, and as plunder to the wicked of the earth; and they shall defile them. ^7-21

**7:22** I will turn My face from them, and My treasures shall be defiled; ruffians shall invade it and defile it. ^7-22

**7:23** Forge the chain,[^6] for the land is full of bloody crimes, and the city is full of lawlessness. ^7-23

**7:24** I will bring in the worst of the nations to take possession of their houses; so shall I turn to naught the pride of the powerful, and their sanctuaries shall be defiled. ^7-24

**7:25** Horror[^7] comes, and they shall seek safety, but there shall be none. ^7-25

**7:26** Calamity shall follow calamity, and rumor follow rumor. Then they shall seek vision from the prophet in vain; instruction shall perish from the priest, and counsel from the elders. ^7-26

**7:27** The king shall mourn, the prince shall clothe himself with desolation, and the hands of the people of the land shall tremble. I will treat them in accordance with their own ways and judge them according to their deserts. And they shall know that I am the LORD. ^7-27

[^1]: A number of mss. and editions, as well as Targum, read “Disaster after disaster.”
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^4]: Emendation yields “like moaning doves. All of them shall perish.”
[^5]: I.e., their adornments.
[^6]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^7]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
