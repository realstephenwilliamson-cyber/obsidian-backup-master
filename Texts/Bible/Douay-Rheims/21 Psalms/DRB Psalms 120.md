# Psalms 120 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 119|← Psalms 119]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 121|Psalms 121 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 120|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**120:1** I have lifted up my eyes to the mountains, from whence help shall come to me. ^120-1

**120:2** My help is from the Lord, who made heaven and earth. ^120-2

**120:3** May he not suffer thy foot to be moved: neither let him slumber that keepeth thee. ^120-3

**120:4** Behold he shall neither slumber nor sleep, that keepeth Israel. ^120-4

**120:5** The Lord is thy keeper, the Lord is thy protection upon thy right hand. ^120-5

**120:6** The sun shall not burn thee by day: nor the moon by night. ^120-6

**120:7** The Lord keepeth thee from all evil: may the Lord keep thy soul. ^120-7

**120:8** May the Lord keep thy going in and thy going out; from henceforth now and for ever. ^120-8
