## Mūla

तेनैव कुर्यात्पूजां स इति शम्भोर्विनिश्चयः ।
साधकानां बुभुक्षूणां विधिर्नियतियन्त्रितः ॥ ^mula

tenaiva kuryātpūjāṃ sa iti śambhorviniścayaḥ |
sādhakānāṃ bubhukṣūṇāṃ vidhirniyatiyantritaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 17 - Chapter Twenty-Five (continued) and Chapter Twenty-Six#^pg409|p. 389]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=409>))
>
> It is Śambhu’s settled opinion (viniścaya) that one should worship with whatever brings about (an) expansion (of consciousness) within his mind. (with 26.54cd–55ab)
>
> In the case of adepts (sādhaka) and those who desire worldly benefits, the (specific ritual) procedure (they should follow) (vidhi) is (rigorously) fixed by the constraint (niyati) (which determines that a particular action gives rise to a specific result). (However) in the case of those who know reality, and those who aspire for liberation, it is free of (any) restriction. (with 26.55cd–56ab)
