## Mūla

चक्रं कसेश्चकेः कृत्या करोतेश्च किलोदितम् ।
यागश्च तर्पणं बाह्ये विकासस्तच्च कीर्त्यते ॥ ^mula

cakraṃ kaseścakeḥ kṛtyā karoteśca kiloditam |
yāgaśca tarpaṇaṃ bāhye vikāsastacca kīrtyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 11 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 3#^pg319|p. 302]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=319>))
>
> The word ‘cakra’ is said to be derive from the roots ‘ksī’ (expansion), ‘caka’ (satisfaction), ‘kṛtī’ (severing), and ‘kṛ’ (acting). Thus ‘cakra’ is that which expands, satisfies, severs (bondage), and acts. (with 29.106cd–107ab)
>
> Moreover, externally, the sacrifice (yāga) is the offering of libation (that gratifies the goddesses of the senses, and that gratification) is praised as the expansion (of consciousness) (vikāsa). (with 29.107cd)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* इत्यादिनीत्या पूजोपयोगिनो द्रव्यजातस्य आनन्दसन्दोहित्वं लक्षणं सर्वत्र उक्तम् । इह पुनर्मुख्यचक्रादेः पूज्यस्यैव कथं तदुच्यते?-- इत्याशङ्क्य आह--

> यागश्च तर्पणं बाह्ये विकासस्तच्च कीर्त्यते ॥

बहिरपि यागो नाम तर्पणमुच्यते । तच्च चितो नैराकाङ्क्ष्योत्पादात् विकासः समुच्छलद्रूपत्वम्--इत्यर्थः ॥ १०७ ॥
