## Chapter 46

**46:1** Bel[^1] is bowed, Nebo[^2] is cowering,  
Their images are a burden for beasts and cattle;  
The things you[^3] would carry [in procession]  
Are now piled as a burden  
On tired [beasts]. ^46-1

**46:2** They cowered, they bowed as well,  
They could not rescue the burden,[^4]  
And they themselves went into captivity. ^46-2

**46:3** Listen to Me, O House of Jacob,  
All that are left of the House of Israel,  
Who have been carried since birth,  
Supported since leaving the womb: ^46-3

**46:4** Till you grow old, I will still be the same;  
When you turn gray, it is I who will carry;  
I was the Maker, and I will be the Bearer;  
And I will carry and rescue [you]. ^46-4

**46:5** To whom can you compare Me  
Or declare Me similar?  
To whom can you liken Me,  
So that we seem comparable? ^46-5

**46:6** Those who squander gold from the purse  
And weigh out silver on the balance,[^5]  
They hire a metal worker to make it into a god,  
To which they bow down and prostrate themselves. ^46-6

**46:7** They must carry it on their backs and transport it;  
When they put it down, it stands,  
It does not budge from its place.  
If they cry out to it, it does not answer;  
It cannot save them from their distress. ^46-7

**46:8** Keep this in mind, and stand firm![^6]  
Take this to heart, you sinners! ^46-8

**46:9** Bear in mind what happened of old;  
For I am God, and there is none else,  
I am divine, and there is none like Me. ^46-9

**46:10** I foretell the end from the beginning,  
And from the start, things that had not occurred.  
I say: My plan shall be fulfilled;  
I will do all I have purposed. ^46-10

**46:11** I summoned that swooping bird from the East;[^7]  
From a distant land, the man for My purpose.  
I have spoken, so I will bring it to pass;  
I have designed it, so I will complete it. ^46-11

**46:12** Listen to Me, you stubborn of heart,[^8]  
Who are far from victory: ^46-12

**46:13** I am bringing My victory close;  
It shall not be far,  
And My triumph shall not be delayed.  
I will grant triumph in Zion  
To Israel, in whom I glory. ^46-13

[^1]: Babylonian deities.
[^2]: Babylonian deities.
[^3]: Emendation yields “they.”
[^4]: Emendation yields “him who carried [them]”; cf. Targum.
[^5]: Lit. “beam [of the balance].”
[^6]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^7]: I.e., Cyrus; cf. 41.2–3; 44.28–45.1.
[^8]: Septuagint reads, “who have lost heart.”
