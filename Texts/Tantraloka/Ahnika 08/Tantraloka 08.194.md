## Mūla

न यज्ञदानतपसा प्राप्यं काल्याः पुरं जयम् ।
तद्भक्तास्तत्र गच्छन्ति तन्मण्डलसुदीक्षिताः ॥ ^mula

na yajñadānatapasā prāpyaṃ kālyāḥ puraṃ jayam |
tadbhaktāstatra gacchanti tanmaṇḍalasudīkṣitāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 07 - Chapter Eight, part 5#^pg235|p. 217]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=235>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 8.194cd–195ab.
>
> Above the hundred Rudras is (the world) of Bhadrakālī. (It is called) Jaya and is blue (the colour of sapphire). Jaya, Kālī’s abode, cannot be reached by performing sacrifices, charity or austerity; only those who have been properly initiated into Her maṇḍala and are devoted to Her go there. (with 8.193cd–194)
