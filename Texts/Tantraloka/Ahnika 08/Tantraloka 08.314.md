## Mūla

अपि सर्वसिद्धवाचः क्षीयेरन्दीर्घकालमुद्गीर्णाः ।
न पुनर्योन्यानन्त्यादुच्यन्ते स्रोतसां संख्याः ॥ ^mula

api sarvasiddhavācaḥ kṣīyerandīrghakālamudgīrṇāḥ |
na punaryonyānantyāducyante srotasāṃ saṃkhyāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 09 - Chapter Eight, part 7#^pg330|p. 312]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=330>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 8.314cd–315ab.
>
> The words of even all the accomplished yogis (siddha) (put together), uttered for a long time, would come to an end before they could recount the number of the currents (of creation), innumerable as are the wombs.
