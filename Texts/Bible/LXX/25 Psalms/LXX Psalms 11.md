# LXX Psalms 11

← [[LXX Psalms 10]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 12]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 012]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 012]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**11:1** For the end, a Psalm of David, upon the eighth.  ^11-1

**11:2** Save me, O Lord; for the godly man has failed; for truth is diminished from among the children of men.[^1]  ^11-2

**11:3** Every one has spoken vanity to his neighbour: their lips are deceitful, they have spoken with a double heart.  ^11-3

**11:4** Let the Lord destroy all the deceitful lips, and the tongue that speaks great words:  ^11-4

**11:5** who have said, We will magnify our tongue; our lips are our own: who is Lord of us?  ^11-5

**11:6** Because of the misery of the poor, and because of the sighing of the needy, now will I arise, saith the Lord, I will set *them* in safety; I will speak *to them* thereof openly.  ^11-6

**11:7** The oracles of the Lord are pure oracles; as silver tried in the fire, proved *in* a furnace of earth, purified seven times.[^2]  ^11-7

**11:8** Thou, O Lord, shalt keep us, and shalt preserve us, from this generation, and for ever.  ^11-8

**11:9** The ungodly walk around: according to thy greatness thou hast greatly exalted the sons of men.  ^11-9

[^1]: 11:2 *Gr.* truths are become rare.
[^2]: 11:7 *Gr.* the earth.
