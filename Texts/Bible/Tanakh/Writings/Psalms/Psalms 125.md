## Chapter 125

**125:1** A song of ascents.  
Those who trust in the LORD  
are like Mount Zion  
that cannot be moved,  
enduring forever. ^125-1

**125:2** Jerusalem, hills enfold it,  
and the LORD enfolds His people  
now and forever. ^125-2

**125:3** The scepter of the wicked shall never rest  
upon the land allotted to the righteous,  
that the righteous not set their hand to wrongdoing.[^1] ^125-3

**125:4** Do good, O LORD, to the good,  
to the upright in heart. ^125-4

**125:5** But those who in their crookedness act corruptly,[^2]  
let the LORD make them go the way of evildoers.  
May it be well with Israel! ^125-5

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
