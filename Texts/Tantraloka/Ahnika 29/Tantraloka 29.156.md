## Mūla

अव्युच्छिन्नानाहतरूपैस्तन्मन्त्रवीर्यं स्यात् ।
इति चक्राष्टकरूढः सहजं जपमाचरन् परे धाम्नि ॥ ^mula

avyucchinnānāhatarūpaistanmantravīryaṃ syāt |
iti cakrāṣṭakarūḍhaḥ sahajaṃ japamācaran pare dhāmni || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 12 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 4#^pg352|p. 335]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=352>))
>
> The reflective awareness Śakti and her possessor have of one another, when penetrated by (the power of) the Seal of the Skyfarer (khecaramudrā), and (when they) drink (i.e. kiss each other), eat (enjoy sex) (upabhoga), play (erotic games with one another), laugh (together) and the like, the vitality of Mantra (is experienced through the eight phases of Sound, namely,) 1) an inarticulate (avyakta) (whisper), 2) the resonance (dhvani) (of a bell), 3) the rumble (rāva) (of storm clouds), 4) crackling (sphoṭa), 5) a voice (śruti), 6) sound (nāda), and 7) the end of sound (nādānta)ů, and 8) (all of which) are forms of the uninterrupted and unstruck (resonance of pure consciousness) (andhata),), (which is the eighth). (with 29.154cd–156ab)
>
> Thus, he who has mounted onto (this) group of eight Wheels (and is focused on them), practicing (the innate) spontaneous (sahaja) repetition of Mantra (japa) within the Supreme Abode (of consciousness in the centre), attains the plane of the eight Bhairavas, which is differentiated by the energies (kalā) of the group of eight (stages of the development of Sound). (with 29.156cd–157ab)
