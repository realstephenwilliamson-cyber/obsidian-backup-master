## Mūla

सर्वेषामुत्तरो मेरुर्लोकालोकश्च दक्षिणः ।
उदयास्तमयावित्थं सूर्यस्य परिभावयेत् ॥ ^mula

sarveṣāmuttaro merurlokālokaśca dakṣiṇaḥ |
udayāstamayāvitthaṃ sūryasya paribhāvayet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 05 - Chapter Eight, part 3#^pg174|p. 156]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=174>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 8.110cd–111ab.
>
> (Meru) is to the north of all (the eight continents), while Lokāloka is to the south. (with 8.110ab)
>
> One perceives the same with respect to the rising and setting of the sun (in the east and the west). (with 8.110cd)
