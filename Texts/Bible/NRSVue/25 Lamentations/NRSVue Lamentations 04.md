## The punishment of Zion

**4:1** How the gold has grown dim; how the pure gold is changed! The sacred stones lie scattered at the head of every street. ^4-1

**4:2** The precious children of Zion, worth their weight in fine gold—how they are reckoned as earthen pots, the work of a potter’s hands! ^4-2

**4:3** Even the jackals offer the breast and nurse their young, but my people has become cruel, like the ostriches in the wilderness. ^4-3

**4:4** The tongue of the infant sticks to the roof of its mouth for thirst; the children beg for food, but there is nothing for them. ^4-4

**4:5** Those who feasted on delicacies perish in the streets; those who were brought up in purple cling to ash heaps. ^4-5

**4:6** For the chastisement of my people has been greater than the punishment of Sodom, which was overthrown in a moment, though no hand was laid on it.^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^4-6

**4:7** Her princes were purer than snow, whiter than milk; their bodies were more ruddy than coral, their form cut like sapphire.^[Or *lapis lazuli*] ^4-7

**4:8** Now their visage is blacker than soot; they are not recognized in the streets. Their skin has shriveled on their bones; it has become as dry as wood. ^4-8

**4:9** Happier were those pierced by the sword than those pierced by hunger, whose life drains away, deprived of the produce of the field. ^4-9

**4:10** The hands of compassionate women have boiled their own children; they became their food in the destruction of my people. ^4-10

**4:11** The LORD gave full vent to his wrath; he poured out his hot anger and kindled a fire in Zion that consumed its foundations. ^4-11

**4:12** The kings of the earth did not believe, nor did any of the inhabitants of the world, that foe or enemy could enter the gates of Jerusalem. ^4-12

**4:13** It was for the sins of her prophets and the iniquities of her priests, who shed the blood of the righteous in her midst. ^4-13

**4:14** Blindly they wandered through the streets, so defiled with blood that no one was able to touch their garments. ^4-14

**4:15** “Away! Unclean!” people shouted at them; “Away! Away! Do not touch!” So they became fugitives and wanderers; it was said among the nations, “They shall stay here no longer.” ^4-15

**4:16** The LORD himself has scattered them; he will regard them no more; no honor was shown to the priests, no favor to the elders. ^4-16

**4:17** Our eyes failed, ever watching vainly for help; we were watching eagerly for a nation that could not save. ^4-17

**4:18** They dogged our steps so that we could not walk in our streets; our end drew near; our days were numbered, for our end had come. ^4-18

**4:19** Our pursuers were swifter than the eagles in the heavens; they chased us on the mountains; they lay in wait for us in the wilderness. ^4-19

**4:20** The LORD’s anointed, the breath of our life, was taken in their pits—the one of whom we said, “Under his shadow we shall live among the nations.” ^4-20

**4:21** Rejoice and be glad, O daughter Edom, you that live in the land of Uz; but to you also the cup shall pass; you shall become drunk and strip yourself bare. ^4-21

**4:22** The punishment of your iniquity, O daughter Zion, is accomplished; he will keep you in exile no longer; but your iniquity, O daughter Edom, he will punish; he will uncover your sins. ^4-22
