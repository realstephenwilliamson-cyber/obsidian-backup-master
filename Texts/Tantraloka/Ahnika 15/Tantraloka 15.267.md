## Mūla

भास्यमानोऽत्र चाभेदः स्वात्मनो भेद एव च ।
भेदे विजृम्भिते माया मायामातुर्विजृम्भते ॥ ^mula

bhāsyamāno'tra cābhedaḥ svātmano bheda eva ca |
bhede vijṛmbhite māyā māyāmāturvijṛmbhate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 05 - Chapter Fifteen, part 4#^pg177|p. 159]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=177>))
>
> It manifests (both) oneness (abheda) here, as well as (the duality which is the sense of) separation from His own nature. (with 15.267ab)
>
> When duality (bheda) unfolds, Māyā unfolds in relation to the Māyā subject (who perceives Māyā) (māyāmātr). (Conversely,) when oneness unfolds, that is (the unfolding of) Śiva’s nature of that same Māyā subject. (with 15.267cd–268ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* ननु यदि एवं भेदेनैव अयमवभासते, तत् सदैव संसारः स्यात्, न तु कदाचिदपि कैवल्यम् ?--इत्याशङ्क्य आह--

> भास्यमानोऽत्र चाभेदः स्वात्मनो भेद एव च ।

अत्रेति--स्वारसिके तथाभासने ॥

एतन्निबन्ध एव च बन्धमोक्षविभागः--इत्याह--

> भेदे विजृम्भिते माया मायामातुर्विजृम्भते ॥
> अभेदे जृम्भतेऽस्यैव मायामातुः शिवात्मता ।
