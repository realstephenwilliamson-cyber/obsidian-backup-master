# LXX Job 09

← [[LXX Job 08]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Job 10]] →

*Job · [[LXX]]*

**9:1** Then Job answered and said,  ^9-1

**9:2** I know of a truth that it is so: for how shall a mortal man be just before the Lord?  ^9-2

**9:3** For if he would enter into judgment with him, *God* would not hearken to him, so that he should answer to one of his charges of a thousand.  ^9-3

**9:4** For he is wise in mind, and mighty, and great: who has hardened himself against him and endured?  ^9-4

**9:5** Who wears out the mountains, and *men* know it not: who overturns them in anger.  ^9-5

**9:6** Who shakes the *earth* under heaven from its foundations, and its pillars totter.  ^9-6

**9:7** Who commands the sun, and it rises not; and he seals up the stars.  ^9-7

**9:8** Who alone has stretched out the heavens, and walks on the sea as on firm ground.  ^9-8

**9:9** Who makes Pleias, and Hesperus, and Arcturus, and the chambers of the south.  ^9-9

**9:10** Who does great and unsearchable things; glorious also and excellent things, innumerable.  ^9-10

**9:11** If ever he should go beyond me, I shall not see him: if he should pass by me, neither thus have I known *it*.  ^9-11

**9:12** If he would take away, who shall turn him back? or who shall say to him, What hast thou done?  ^9-12

**9:13** For *if* he has turned away *his* anger, the whales under heaven have stooped under him.  ^9-13

**9:14** Oh then that he would hearken to me, or judge my cause.  ^9-14

**9:15** For though I be righteous, he will not hearken to me: I will intreat his judgment.  ^9-15

**9:16** And if I should call and he should not hearken, I cannot believe that he has listened to my voice.  ^9-16

**9:17** Let him not crush me with a dark storm: but he has made my bruises many without cause.  ^9-17

**9:18** For he suffers me not to take breath, but he has filled me with bitterness.  ^9-18

**9:19** For indeed he is strong in power: who then shall resist his judgment?  ^9-19

**9:20** For though I should seem righteous, my mouth will be profane: and though I should seem blameless, I shall be proved perverse.  ^9-20

**9:21** For even if I have sinned, I know it not *in* my soul: but my life is taken away.  ^9-21

**9:22** Wherefore I said, Wrath slays the great and mighty man.  ^9-22

**9:23** For the worthless die, but the righteous are laughed to scorn.  ^9-23

**9:24** For they are delivered into the hands of the unrighteous *man*: he covers the faces of the judges *of the earth*: but if it be not he, who is it?  ^9-24

**9:25** But my life is swifter than a post: *my days* have fled away, and they knew it not.  ^9-25

**9:26** Or again, is there a trace of *their* path *left* by ships? or is there one of the flying eagle as it seeks *its* prey?  ^9-26

**9:27** And if I should say, I will forget to speak, I will bow down my face and groan;  ^9-27

**9:28** I quake in all my limbs, for I know that thou wilt not leave me alone *as* innocent.  ^9-28

**9:29** But since I am ungodly, why have I not died?  ^9-29

**9:30** For if I should wash myself with snow, and purge myself with pure hands,  ^9-30

**9:31** thou hadst thoroughly plunged me in filth, and my garment had abhorred me.  ^9-31

**9:32** For thou art not man like me, with whom I could contend, that we might come together to judgment.  ^9-32

**9:33** Would that *he* our mediator were *present*, and a reprover, and one who should hear *the cause* between both.  ^9-33

**9:34** Let him remove *his* rod from me, and let not his fear terrify me:  ^9-34

**9:35** so shall I not be afraid, but I will speak: for I am not thus conscious *of guilt*.  ^9-35
