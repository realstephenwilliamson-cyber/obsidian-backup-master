## Mūla

पुष्पादि सर्वं तत्स्थं तदगाधाम्भसि निक्षिपेत् ।
उक्तः स्थण्डिलयागोऽयं नित्यकर्मणि शम्भुना ॥ ^mula

puṣpādi sarvaṃ tatsthaṃ tadagādhāmbhasi nikṣipet |
uktaḥ sthaṇḍilayāgo'yaṃ nityakarmaṇi śambhunā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 17 - Chapter Twenty-Five (continued) and Chapter Twenty-Six#^pg417|p. 397]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=417>))
>
> Then having cleaned (himself in this way), when he has bathed, he should worship (the Lord) with a flower. (Then,) whatever is there (on the sacrificial surface), that is, the flowers and the rest, should be thrown into deep water. (with 26.75cd–76ab)
>
> (Thus) Śambhu has taught this sacrifice offered to the sacrificial surface (sthandilayāga), which is part of the regular (daily) rite. (with 26.76cd)
