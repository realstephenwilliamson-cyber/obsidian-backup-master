## Mūla

तत्परिकल्पितचक्रस्थदेवताः प्राप्नुवन्ति विज्ञानम् ।
ते तत्र शक्तिचक्रे तेनैवानन्दरसमयेन बहिः ॥ ^mula

tatparikalpitacakrasthadevatāḥ prāpnuvanti vijñānam |
te tatra śakticakre tenaivānandarasamayena bahiḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 11 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 3#^pg337|p. 320]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=337>))
>
> Those who have not attained (complete) enlightenment (aprāptavibodha), (but) are well-established in the sacrifice that has blossomed by the ejaculate, the deities located in that Wheel, fashioned by that (consciousness) (parikalpita), attain discerning knowledge (of consciousness, and bestow its fruits to the less enlightened). (with 29.129cd–130ab)
>
> Those (who are not fully enlightened) worship everything externally there within the Wheel of Śakti, in the four directions in the aforestated order,, beginning with (Ganesa,, with that same (sacrificial substance) made from the juice of Bliss (āānandarasa). After that, the sage should worship the couple Kuleśa (and Kuleśvarī) in the centre, (then) the (three) goddesses on the three spokes (of a triangle), and outside (that), on each spoke, a set of four. Thus (he should worship in this way), in due order, the wheel of twelve rays, (that of the) group of eight (Mothers), and that of eight times eight (Yoginīs), which is of many kinds. (with 29.130cd–132)
