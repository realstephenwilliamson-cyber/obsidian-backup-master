## Mūla

इत्येतदागमेषूक्तं तत एव पुरे पुरे ।
क्षेत्रमानं ब्रुवे श्रीमत्सर्वज्ञानादिषूदितम् ॥ ^mula

ityetadāgameṣūktaṃ tata eva pure pure |
kṣetramānaṃ bruve śrīmatsarvajñānādiṣūditam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 05 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 3#^pg127|p. 110]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=127>))
>
> It is said in the scriptures (that the soul resides in the deity’s world, which is) ‘in a place full of many kinds of trees, strewn with swans and ducks’. (with 28.250cd–251a)
>
> Next I will state the measure of the sacred area (kṣetra) within each sacred place (pura) (where a Liṅga is installed), as taught in the Sarvajñāna and other (Āgamas). When installed by a teacher, the extent of the (sacred) area around a Liṅga is one hundred hand-spans; if it is a spontaneously generated (liñga) (svayambhūta), one thousand, and half that (measure) if established by a Vedic seer (rsi).°). If a Liñga is installed by one who knows reality (tattvavit), the fruit (of worshipping it) is similar to that of a spontaneously generated one. If a teacher who does not know reality installs a Liñga, then the procedure (should be) repeated, otherwise (if that is not done, the consequent) defect (doṣa) renders both (the teacher and the Liñga) defective. (with 28.251b–254ab)
