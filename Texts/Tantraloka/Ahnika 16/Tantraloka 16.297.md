## Mūla

स्वबलेनैव भोगं वा मोक्षं वा लभते बुधः ।
तेन विज्ञानयोगादिबली प्राक् समयी भवन् ॥ ^mula

svabalenaiva bhogaṃ vā mokṣaṃ vā labhate budhaḥ |
tena vijñānayogādibalī prāk samayī bhavan || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 07 - Chapter Sixteen, part 3#^pg152|p. 132]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=152>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 16.296cd–297ab.
>
> (The disciple’s) initiation should rightly serve to purify (him). (Once he is) purified (and empowered thereby), he, wise (as he is), obtains worldly benefits or (attains) liberation (as the case may be) by his own (inherent) strength (svabala) alone. (with 16.296cd–297ab)
>
> Thus, he who has the strength (in himself to sustain) insight (vijñāna), Yoga and the like, whether he is initially a regular (initiate) or a spiritual son, he should not (limit himself) to being this only, but rather to what is consonant with (his) own (inherent spiritual) strength. (with 16.297cd–298ab)
