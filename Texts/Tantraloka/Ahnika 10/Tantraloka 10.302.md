## Mūla

तत्र स्वरूपं शक्तिश्च सकलश्चेति तत्त्रयम् ।
इति जाग्रदवस्थेयं भेदे पञ्चदशात्मके ॥ ^mula

tatra svarūpaṃ śaktiśca sakalaśceti tattrayam |
iti jāgradavastheyaṃ bhede pañcadaśātmake || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 14 - Chapter Ten, part 5#^pg509|p. 490]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=509>))
>
> The Great Lord has clearly said this in the Śrīpūrvaśastra, (where we read): “(1) When the divisions are fifteen, this waking state corresponds to three of them, namely, (one’s) Own Nature, the Conditioned (sakala) (subject) and (his) power. Dreaming and deep sleep correspond to the two Deconditioned (subjects), the Fourth state to the Mantra (subjects) and the rest, and the state Beyond the Fourth to Śiva and His power.
