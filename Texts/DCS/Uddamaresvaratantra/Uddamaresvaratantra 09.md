# Uḍḍāmareśvaratantra — Chapter 9

[[Uddamaresvaratantra 08|← Chapter 8]] · [[Uddamaresvaratantra|← index]] · [[Uddamaresvaratantra 10|Chapter 10 →]]

athānyat sampravakṣyāmi auṣadhaṃ paramadurlabham

^s1

āsphoṭī nāma vikhyātā nāgadamanīti viśrutā

^s2

asyā vidhānaṃ vakṣyāmi durlabhaṃ tridivaukasām

^s3

viṣāḥ sarve vinaśyanti lepanād bhakṣaṇāt kṣaṇāt

^s4

pattrahaste tāṃ pralipya sarpo dhriyate dhṛtamātre sarpo na naśyati naiva kṣatāni pralipya nāgapūjitamantreṇa sarpo vinaśyati

^s5

nāgadamanīmūlaṃ khananān nāḍīlalāṭalepanān nāśayati samantataḥ āmavātaṃ pittavātaṃ śleṣmavātam ete vātā vinaśyanti bhakṣaṇān nātra saṃśayaḥ

^s6

atha kathayāmy oṣadhīkaraṇe karaṇakāraṇāni

^s7

putramaya vaśīkaraṇakārakaputraputraṃ kaṃsaṃ kātarāpi vaśaṃ paraṃ mahilājanasyaikaśo 'py asya dīyate sā patiṃ parityajya paśyatāṃ lokānāṃ nagnā bhūtvā bhramati

^s8

atha kathayāmi tāntrikavidhim

^s9

tāmravedīparora iti lokair ucyate śanivāre tām abhimantrya digambaro muktakeśo bhūtvānudite bhānau grahaṇaṃ kuryāt

^s10

piṣṭvā samyakprakāreṇa strīpañcamalena ca kāmātureṇa kṛtvā tāmbūlena saha bhaginīkṛtvā dīyate sā vaśyā bhavati nānyathā

^s11

mātāpi putraṃ parityajya tatparā bhūtvā pṛṣṭhato nagnā bhavati yatra kutrāpi tathā tam anuyāti na saṃśayaḥ

^s12

kākajaṅgheti vikhyātā mahauṣadhir grāme sarvatra tiṣṭhati śanivāre saṃdhyāsamaye tasyā abhimantraṇaṃ kuryāt tadantaraṃ brāhme muhūrte utthāyānudite bhānau puṣyarkṣe hastarkṣe vā yoge khadirakīlakena tāṃ samūlām utpāṭayet

^s13

punas tāṃ saptamyām aṣṭamyāṃ navamyāṃ vā etāsu tithiṣu punarvasupuṣyahastarkṣayuktāsu svapañcamalena saha piṣṭvā svavīryaṃ svaraktam api tasmin dattvā yasyai vanitāyai dīyate sā strī vaśyā bhavati satyam eva mantreṇānena mantrayet

^s14

uoṃ namo bhagavati tripure trailokyamohini aiṃ drāṃ śrīṃ klīṃ sauṃ amukanāmnīṃ śīghraṃ me vaśam ānaya svāhā

^s15

iti mantraḥ

^s16

atha guñjākalpo likhyate śvetaguñjāṃ śanivāre saṃdhyāsamaye 'bhimantritāṃ kṛtvā tato brāhme muhūrte utthāyānudite bhānau khadirakīlakena digambaro bhūtvā samūlām utpāṭayet

^s17

puṣyarkṣe hastarkṣe vā strīpuṣpeṇa saha gorocanakaśmīrakuṅkumaśvetacandanaraktacandanakastūrīkarpūrahastimadena sahābhimantrya tilakaṃ kuryāt tadā strī kāmabāṇavimohitā vihvalā bhavati

^s18

mantreṇānena mantrayet uoṃ aiṃ hrīṃ śrīṃ klīṃ hūṃ phaṭ svāhā

^s19

iti kāmabāṇatilakam

^s20

athānyatantroktaṃ vaśīkaraṇādikaṃ likhyate sāram uddhṛtya saṃkṣepād vaśīkaraṇamohane

^s21

kāmināṃ prītijanakaṃ kiṃcit tad api gadyate

^s22

tatrādau tilakavidhiḥ

^s23

lajjāṃ madhūkaṃ kavyaṃ ca nalinīmūlam eva ca

^s24

etān piṣṭvā svavīryeṇa yaḥ kuryāt tilakaṃ pumān

^s25

tatkṣaṇād eva nayati vaśyatāṃ bhuvanatrayam

^s26

vātsyāyanena muninā proktaṃ yogam anuttamam

^s27

sitāṣṭamūlamañjiṣṭhā vacā mustā sakuṣṭhakā

^s28

strīyoniśoṇite caitad ekīkṛtya lalāṭake

^s29

śubhaṃ tilakam ādhatte yaḥ sa lokatrayaṃ kramāt

^s30

kṛtajñaḥ svavaśaṃ kuryān modate ca ciraṃ bhuvi

^s31

tagaraṃ pippalīmūlaṃ meṣaśṛṅgī kaṇā jaṭā

^s32

etat samaṃ svapañcāṅgamale nītvaikatāṃ sudhīḥ

^s33

madhunā tilakaṃ kuryād yaḥ kṣoṇīsutavāsare

^s34

jagat sarvaṃ vaśīkuryāt sa pumān nātra saṃśayaḥ

^s35

gorocanaṃ ca saṃbhāvya svapuṃso rudhireṇa yā

^s36

kuryāt sā tilakaṃ bhāle patiṃ ca mohayed bhṛśam

^s37

athāñjanavidhiḥ

^s38

mahāṣṭamīdine yas tu śmaśāne naramastake

^s39

pātitaṃ kajjalaṃ viśvaṃ mohayen nayanāñjanāt

^s40

rocanāṃ kesaraṃ kanyāṃ śilāṃ ceti viśodhayan

^s41

yājayed dṛṣṭipathagaṃ sarvam eva vimohayet

^s42

tālīśakuṣṭhanāgaraiḥ kṛtvā kṣoṇīśavartikām

^s43

siddhārthataile niḥkṣipya kajjalaṃ naramastake

^s44

pātayed añjanaṃ tasya sarvadā bhuvanatraye

^s45

dṛṣṭigocaram āyātaḥ sarvo bhavati dāsavat

^s46

śilākiñjalkaphalinīrocanānāṃ tathāñjanam

^s47

puṣyarkṣayoge vihitaṃ dampatyor mohanaṃ param

^s48

iti atha cūrṇavidhiḥ

^s49

kākajaṅghā śilā pakṣau bhrāmarau kṛṣṇam utpalam

^s50

tagarajaṃ caiṣāṃ cūrṇaṃ kṣiptaṃ vimohane

^s51

vātapaittikadalaṃ puṃso malaṃ mālāsavasya ca

^s52

pakṣāvaler idaṃ cūrṇaṃ kṣiptaṃ śirasi mohanam

^s53

atha bhakṣaṇavidhiḥ

^s54

antrādi sarvaṃ niṣkāsya khañjarīṭodaraṃ kulaiḥ

^s55

pūrayitvā svavīryeṇa sārameyagale kṣipet

^s56

mudrāṃ kṛtvā tadekānte saptāhaṃ dhārayet sudhīḥ

^s57

paścān niṣkāsya saṃśodhya vaṭīṃ kuryād viśoṣayet

^s58

sā bhakṣaṇavidhānena dīpamālā parasparam

^s59

dampatyoḥ prītijananī kīrtitā niyamottamā

^s60

anyat sumatam

^s61

athānyat sampravakṣyāmi tilakaṃ sarvakāmikam

^s62

gorocanaṃ vaṃśalocanaṃ matsyapittaṃ kaśmīrakuṅkumakesarasvayambhūkusumasvavīryaśrīkhaṇḍaraktacandanakastūrīkarpūrakākajaṅghāmūlāni samabhāgāni kṛtvā kūpataḍāganadījalena mardayitvā kumārikāpārśvakāṃ guṭikāṃ kṛtvā chāyāṃ guṭikāṃ kārayet

^s63

tayā lalāṭe tilakaṃ kṛtvā yāṃ yāṃ striyaṃ paśyati sā sā vaśyā bhavati

^s64

drauṃ vāṃ dhāṃ kṣauṃ aṃ kaṃ chaḥ ity anena mantreṇa mahiṣāsthimayaṃ kīlakam ekonaviṃśatyaṅgulaṃ sahasreṇābhimantritaṃ yasya nāmnā kūpataṭe nikhanet sa mahiṣeṇa vadhyate

^s65

uoṃ cchaḥ cchaḥ ﾲṃ amukaṃ huṃ īṃ cchaḥ cchaḥ uoṃ iti

^s66

anena mantreṇa mayūrāsthimayaṃ kīlakaṃ tryaṅgulaṃ sahasreṇābhimantritaṃ yasya nāmnā catuṣpathe nikhanet sa tatra bhramati

^s67

tatrānenāvatiṣṭhati paryaṭati kṣaṇamātreṇa uttolanena śāntir bhavati

^s68

uoṃ śrīṃ śrīṃ vrāṃ vrīṃ īṃ īṃ cchaḥ cchaḥ svāhā

^s69

anena mantreṇa meṣāsthimayaṃ kīlakaṃ dvādaśāṅgulaṃ sahasreṇābhimantritaṃ kṛtvā yasya gṛhe nikhanet sarvasiddhir asiddhā tasya bhavati

^s70

drīṃ bandhūkapuṣpasaṃkāśaṃ dhyātavyaṃ mantradīpake

^s71

kumbhakena varārohe śṛṇu vakṣyāmi ṣaḍguṇam

^s72

dhyātvā tu māsam ekaṃ tu mahāstrīm ānayed dhruvam

^s73

māsenaikena manunā ānayen nāgakanyakām

^s74

devakanyāṃ tribhir māsaiḥ sāyāhne nānyathā bhavet

^s75

uoṃ haṃ haḥ iti

^s76

anenaiva tu mantreṇa dhyātavyāḥ krodhayājakāḥ

^s77

yājyasya rudrasaṃkāśaṃ rudrahastaṃ surāsuraiḥ

^s78

māsena mānuṣaṃ lokaṃ nāgalokaṃ dvimāsataḥ

^s79

tribhir māsais tu deveśi svargalokaṃ na saṃśayaḥ

^s80

ṣaṇmāsābhyāsayogena trailokyaṃ niścalaṃ kuru

^s81

uoṃ raktahaṭā raktagaṭā mukuṭadhāriṇī edhati svāhā

^s82

mantrarāja ulṭā vedhā

^s83

uoṃ hrīṃ śrīṃ dhrīṃ vikṛtānanā bāhye phaṭ svāhā

^s84

mantram imaṃ yantre likhitvā yasya īkṣitaṃ dīyate sa aikāhikadvyāhikatryāhikaviṣamajvareṇa gṛhyate tatkṣaṇād eva naśyati nātra saṃśayaḥ

^s85

uoṃ bandhuna kṣayaṃ drīṃ drīṃ amukasyātmānaṃ nityajvareṇa pattrībandhanamātrasya sphura svāhā

^s86

iti nityajvaraśodhanapattrikā

^s87

uoṃ kṣaḥ svāhā

^s88

anena mantreṇa mantritaṃ jalaṃ bhṛśaṃ kṛtvā jvaritāṅgaṃ secayet tena jvaravimuktir bhavati niścitam

^s89

kharavāhanasiṃhasya kramād evānuśāsanam

^s90

ṛkṣaturagagato vāyur jīmūta iva garjitaḥ

^s91

ravivāre ślokam imaṃ likhitvā śirasi nyaset

^s92

tena sthāpitamātreṇa traimāsikajvaraṃ haret

^s93

vāneyasya biḍālasya gṛhītvā rudhiraṃ tataḥ

^s94

padmasūtrotthavartiṃ ca bhāvayet saptavārakam

^s95

śivāgre tajjatailena pātayet kajjalaṃ tataḥ

^s96

tenāñjitalocanas tu adṛśyo bhavati dhruvam

^s97

hebhirihebherihe

^s98

anenodakam ādāyālokya sahasravāraṃ parijapya anenaivāñjanena trirātreṇa siddhiḥ

^s99

padmasūtravartikasya tailena kajjalaṃ pātayet tenāñjitanetras tu adṛśyo bhavati

^s100

tato gomūtreṇa cakṣuṣī prakṣālya punaḥ pratyakṣo bhavati

^s101

atha ḍākinīdamanamantraḥ akṣaḥ kṣāṃ kṣaukājasinau devatā tattvadhūlinī ghonāśālinī bhamantri bandhuśanādaivataṃ laghukaṇṭakena purum abhiśāsano devatāṃ mahābhairava maṇḍalam acala uoṃ cchaḥ cchaḥ cchaḥ ḍākinīmatabandhu namaḥ

^s102

uoṃ namo bhagavate vajrāya caṇḍeśvarāya īṃ īṃ phaṭ svāhā

^s103

iti sarvabhūtaḍākinīdamanamantraḥ

^s104

damana sarpalaṅga ebhalisim ajabandhaniśi nāgapāśam acalaḥ iti damanamantraṃ bandhanaṃ ca

^s105

uoṃ raṃstrīṃ śīghraṃ ha svāhā

^s106

tripurāṃ sindūraśaṅkhacūrṇābhyāṃ udanālapattre likhitvā śodhayitavyaḥ sa divasatrayeṇa jvareṇāgatya milati

^s107

uoṃ ahohaḥ amukanamnīṃ śīghram ānaya svāhā ity ākarṣaṇam

^s108

uoṃ strīṃ strīṃ valīṃ valīm īṃ ahaḥ phaṭ svāhā

^s109

majjanasvastho bhavati

^s110

apsaraṇīṃ amukīṃ jīva

^s111

tena anena mantreṇa saptavāraṃ jalaṃ prajapya kāminyai pānārthaṃ dātavyam

^s112

uoṃ namo jale mohe kule phalāni saṃkule svāhā

^s113

anena mantreṇa saptavārābhimantritaṃ yasya dīyate sa vaśyo bhavati

^s114

uoṃ namo jale mohe drāṃ abjini phaṭ svāhā

^s115

anena mantreṇa pratyūṣe pānīyena mukhaṃ prakṣālayet sarvajanapriyo bhavati sarvasiddhīśvaro bhavati

^s116

uoṃ bhūr bhuvaḥ svaḥ svāhā

^s117

tāmbūlaṃ mantrayitvā yasya dīyate sa vaśyo bhavati

^s118

uoṃ namo jale mohe hana hana daha daha paca paca matha matha amukaṃ me vaśam ānaya svāhā

^s119

atha yakṣiṇīmantrasādhanaṃ surasundarīmanohāriṇīkanakāvatīratikarīkāmeśvarīnaṭyanurāgiṇīpadminī etā aṣṭau yakṣiṇyaḥ kāmanāyāṃ sādhanam

^s120

atha surasundarīsādhanam uoṃ hrīṃ āgaccha āgaccha surasundari svāhā

^s121

vajrapāṇigṛhaṃ gatvā gugguladhūpaṃ dattvā trisaṃdhyaṃ pūjayet sahasraṃ trisaṃdhyaṃ māsaparyantaṃ japet tato māsābhyantare pratyakṣā bhavati antimadine raktacandanenārghyaṃ dadyāt

^s122

tata āgatya mātā bhaginī bhāryā vā bhavati tāsāṃ yāni karmāṇi tāny eva karoti

^s123

yadi mātā bhavati tadā siddhadravyāṇi rasāyanāni dadāti

^s124

yadi bhaginī bhavati tadā pūrvavad amūlyaṃ vastraṃ dadāti

^s125

yadi bhāryā bhavati tadā sarvam aiśvaryaṃ paripūrayati

^s126

paraṃ tu varjanīyam ihānyayā saha śayanaṃ sā ca maithunapriyā bhavati anyathā naśyati

^s127

atha manohāriṇīsādhanam uoṃ hīṃ āgaccha āgaccha manohari svāhā

^s128

iha nadīsaṃgame gatvā candanena maṇḍalaṃ kṛtvā agurudhūpaṃ dattvā sahasraikaṃ mantraṃ māsaparyantaṃ pratyahaṃ japet

^s129

tato māsānte candanodakenārghyaṃ dadyāt puṣpaphalenaikacittena tasyā arcanaṃ kartavyaṃ tato 'rdharātrasamaye niyatam āgacchati āgatā satī tadājñāṃ karoti suvarṇaśataṃ tasmai sādhakāya pratyahaṃ dadāti

^s130

atha kanakāvatīsādhanam uoṃ hrīṃ kanakāvati maithunapriye āgaccha āgaccha svāhā

^s131

vaṭavṛkṣasamīpe sthitvā madyamāṃsādinaivedyaṃ tasyai mūlamantreṇa dattvā śeṣaṃ svayam apy aṅgīkṛtya sahasram ekaṃ mūlamantraṃ japet

^s132

evaṃ saptadinaparyantaṃ kuryāt

^s133

aṣṭamyāṃ rātrāv ardharātrasamaye vastrālaṃkārabhūṣitā aṣṭau parivārān ādāyopagacchati

^s134

āgatā sā kāmayitavyā bhāryā vā bhavati dvādaśajanānāṃ vastrālaṃkārabhojanaṃ ca dadāti aṣṭau kalā nityaṃ sādhakāya prayacchati

^s135

atha kāmeśvarīsādhanam uoṃ hīṃ āgaccha āgaccha kāmeśvari svāhā

^s136

iha gorocanayā bhūrjapattropari strīrūpāṃ pratimāṃ saṃlikhya ṣoḍaśopacāraiḥ pañcopacārair vā sampūjya tataḥ śayyāyām ekākī ekānte upaviśya tanmanā bhūtvā sahasraṃ japet tato māsānte tadbuddhyā svakīyāṃ bhāryāṃ pūjayet

^s137

tato madhusarpirbhyāṃ pratirātraṃ dīpaṃ prajvālya paścān maunaṃ kṛtvā mūlamantraṃ sahasrasaṃkhyaṃ japet

^s138

tato 'rdharātrasamaye niyatam āgacchati paraṃ tv anyāḥ striyo varjanīyāḥ

^s139

atha ratipriyāsādhanam uoṃ hīṃ āgaccha āgaccha ratikari svāhā

^s140

atra paṭe citrarūpiṇī lekhyā vastrakanakālaṅkārabhūṣitā utpalahastā kumarī jātīpuṣpaiḥ prapūjanaṃ kuryāt guggulena dhūpaṃ dadyāt tato 'ṣṭasahasraṃ pratyahaṃ japet

^s141

māsānte tu vidhivat pūjanaṃ kuryāt

^s142

dhūpadīpau prajvālanīyau tato 'rdharātrasamaye 'vaśyam āgacchati āgatā sā strībhāvena kāmayitavyā bhāryā bhavati sādhakasya parivāraṃ pālayati divyaṃ kāmikaṃ bhojanaṃ ca dadāti

^s143

atha padminīsādhanam uoṃ hīṃ āgaccha āgaccha padmini svāhā

^s144

svagṛhe candanena maṇḍalaṃ kṛtvā śiraḥsthaṃ kārayet guggulena dhūpaṃ dattvā sahasram ekaṃ pratyahaṃ japet tato māsānte paurṇamāsyāṃ rātrau vidhivat pūjāṃ kṛtvā japet

^s145

japānte 'rdharātrasamaye niyatam āgacchati āgatā sā kāmayitavyā bhāryā bhūtvā sarvakāmapradā bhavati rasaṃ rasāyanaṃ siddhadravyaṃ pratyahaṃ sādhakāya prayacchati

^s146

atha naṭīsādhanam uoṃ hrīṃ āgaccha āgaccha naṭi svāhā

^s147

atrāśokatale gatvā matsyamāṃsādyāhāragandhapuṣpādidhūpadīpabaliṃ dattvā sahasraṃ pratyahaṃ japet tataḥ sā māsānte niyatam āgacchati āgatā sā mātā bhaginī bhāryā vā bhavati

^s148

yadi mātā bhavati tadā divyaṃ kāmikaṃ bhojanaṃ dadāti vastrasugandhisuvarṇaśataṃ dadāti ca

^s149

yadi bhaginī bhavati tadā śatayojanād uttamāṃ striyam ānīya dadāti

^s150

yadi bhāryā bhavati tadā divyaṃ vastraṃ rasāyanam aṣṭadināntareṇa dadāti

^s151

athānurāgiṇīsādhanam uoṃ hrīṃ anurāgiṇi āgaccha āgaccha svāhā

^s152

iha kaśmīrakuṅkumena bhūrjapattre strīsadṛśīṃ pratimāṃ vilikhyāvāhanādikaṃ kṛtvā gandhapuṣpadhūpadīpādikaṃ dattvā tāmbūlāni nivedya sahasraṃ pratyahaṃ japet

^s153

māsam ekaṃ trisaṃdhyaṃ japet māsānte paurṇamāsyāṃ vidhivat pūjāṃ kṛtvā ghṛtadīpaṃ prajvālya samagrarātrau mantraṃ prajapet

^s154

tataḥ prabhātasamaye niyatam āgacchati āgatā sā sarvakāmapradā bhavati divyarasāyanāni dadāti pratyahaṃ ca dīnārāṇāṃ sahasraṃ dadāti

^s155

tasyāḥ prasādena varṣasahasrāṇy āyuś ca bhavati

^s156

atha ṣaṭtriṃśad yakṣiṇyaḥ

^s157

vicitrā vibhramā haṃsī bhīṣaṇī janarañjinī

^s158

viśālā madanā ghaṇṭā kālakarṇī mahābhayā

^s159

māhendrī śaṅkhinī cāndrī śmaśānā vaṭayakṣiṇī

^s160

mekhalā vikalā lakṣmīr mālinī śatapattrikā

^s161

sulocanā suśobhāḍhyā kapālinī viśālinī

^s162

naṭī kāmeśvarī svarṇarekhā ca surasundarī

^s163

manoharā pramodānurāgiṇī nakhakeśikā

^s164

bhāminī padminī caiva svarṇāvatī ratipriyā

^s165

ṣaṭtriṃśad etā yakṣiṇyaḥ kathitāḥ siddhikāmadāḥ
