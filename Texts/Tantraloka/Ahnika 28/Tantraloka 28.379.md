## Mūla

स्थपुटस्पर्शवत्संविद्विजातीयतया स्थिते ।
अतश्चक्रार्चनाद्येषु विजातीयमतन्मयम् ॥ ^mula

sthapuṭasparśavatsaṃvidvijātīyatayā sthite |
ataścakrārcanādyeṣu vijātīyamatanmayam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 07 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 5#^pg188|p. 171]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=188>))
>
> However, if anyone present there is (not absorbed in the show, and so) not one with it, (the one collective, unified consciousness) is destroyed, because, like the touch of an uneven, rugged surface (sthapuṭa), (his) consciousness stands out as differing in kind (from that of the others). (with 28.378cd–379ab)
>
> Therefore, one should (take care) not allow (anyone) to enter a gathering (cakra) (of initiates thus) engaged in worship and the like, (whose consciousness) is not one (with the participants), differs in kind (from that of the others), and fetters (them) with the contraction (of their) consciousness (by his presence). (with 28.379cd–380ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* एवमेवंविधस्य मेलकादौ प्रवेश एव न दातव्यः--इत्याह--

> अतश्चक्रार्चनाद्येषु विजातीयमतन्मयम् ॥
> नैव प्रवेशयेत्संवित्सङ्कोचननिबन्धनम् ।

प्रवेशाभावे संवित्सङ्कोचनिबन्धनत्वं हेतुः ॥
