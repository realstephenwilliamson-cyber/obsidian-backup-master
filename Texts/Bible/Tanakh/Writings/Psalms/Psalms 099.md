## Chapter 99

**99:1** The LORD, enthroned on cherubim, is king,  
peoples tremble, the earth quakes.[^1] ^99-1

**99:2** The LORD is great in Zion,  
and exalted above all peoples. ^99-2

**99:3** They praise Your name as great and awesome;  
He is holy! ^99-3

**99:4** Mighty king[^2] who loves justice,  
it was You who established equity,  
You who worked righteous judgment in Jacob. ^99-4

**99:5** Exalt the LORD our God  
and bow down to His footstool;  
He is holy! ^99-5

**99:6** Moses and Aaron among His priests,  
Samuel, among those who call on His name—  
when they called to the LORD,  
He answered them. ^99-6

**99:7** He spoke to them in a pillar of cloud;  
they obeyed His decrees,  
the law He gave them. ^99-7

**99:8** O LORD our God, You answered them;  
You were a forgiving God for them,  
but You exacted retribution for their misdeeds. ^99-8

**99:9** Exalt the LORD our God,  
and bow toward His holy hill,  
for the LORD our God is holy. ^99-9

[^1]: Clauses transposed for clarity.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
