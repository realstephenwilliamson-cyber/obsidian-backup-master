# Proverbs 12

← [[Proverbs 11]] · [[Tanakh]] · [[Proverbs 13]] →

Compare: [[NRSVue Proverbs 12|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 12

**12:1** He who loves discipline loves knowledge;  
He who spurns reproof is a brutish man. ^12-1

**12:2** A good man earns the favor of the LORD,  
A man of intrigues, His condemnation. ^12-2

**12:3** A man cannot be established in wickedness,  
But the root of the righteous will not be shaken loose. ^12-3

**12:4** A capable wife is a crown for her husband,  
But an incompetent one is like rot in his bones. ^12-4

**12:5** The purposes of the righteous are justice,  
The schemes of the wicked are deceit. ^12-5

**12:6** The words of the wicked are a deadly ambush,  
But the speech of the upright saves them. ^12-6

**12:7** Overturn the wicked and they are gone,  
But the house of the righteous will endure. ^12-7

**12:8** A man is commended according to his intelligence;  
A twisted mind is held up to contempt. ^12-8

**12:9** Better to be lightly esteemed and have a servant  
Than to put on airs and have no food. ^12-9

**12:10** A righteous man knows the needs of his beast,  
But the compassion of the wicked is cruelty. ^12-10

**12:11** He who tills his land shall have food in plenty,  
But he who pursues vanities is devoid of sense. ^12-11

**12:12** The wicked covet the catch of evil men;  
The root of the righteous yields [fruit].[^1] ^12-12

**12:13** Sinful speech is a trap for the evil man,  
But the righteous escapes from trouble. ^12-13

**12:14** A man gets his fill of good from the fruit of his speech;  
One is repaid in kind for one’s deeds. ^12-14

**12:15** The way of a fool is right in his own eyes;  
But the wise man accepts advice. ^12-15

**12:16** A fool’s vexation is known at once,  
But a clever man conceals his humiliation. ^12-16

**12:17** He who testifies faithfully tells the truth,  
But a false witness, deceit. ^12-17

**12:18** There is blunt talk like sword-thrusts,  
But the speech of the wise is healing. ^12-18

**12:19** Truthful speech abides forever,  
A lying tongue for but a moment. ^12-19

**12:20** Deceit is in the minds of those who plot evil;  
For those who plan good there is joy. ^12-20

**12:21** No harm befalls the righteous,  
But the wicked have their fill of misfortune. ^12-21

**12:22** Lying speech is an abomination to the LORD,  
But those who act faithfully please Him. ^12-22

**12:23** A clever man conceals what he knows,  
But the mind of a dullard cries out folly. ^12-23

**12:24** The hand of the diligent wields authority;  
The negligent are held in subjection. ^12-24

**12:25** If there is anxiety in a man’s mind let him quash it,  
And turn it into joy with a good word. ^12-25

**12:26** A righteous man gives his friend direction,[^2]  
But the way of the wicked leads astray. ^12-26

**12:27** A negligent man never has game to roast;  
A diligent man has precious wealth.[^3] ^12-27

**12:28** The road of righteousness leads to life;  
By way of its path there is no death. ^12-28

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
