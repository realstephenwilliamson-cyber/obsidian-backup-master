# LXX Psalms 84

← [[LXX Psalms 83]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 85]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 085]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 085]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**84:1** For the end, a Psalm for the sons of Core.  ^84-1

**84:2** O Lord, thou hast taken pleasure in thy land: thou hast turned back the captivity of Jacob.  ^84-2

**84:3** Thou hast forgiven thy people their transgressions; thou hast covered all their sins. Pause.  ^84-3

**84:4** Thou hast caused all thy wrath to cease: thou hast turned from thy fierce anger.  ^84-4

**84:5** Turn us, O God of our salvation, and turn thine anger away from us.  ^84-5

**84:6** Wouldest thou be angry with us for ever? or wilt thou continue thy wrath from generation to generation?  ^84-6

**84:7** O God, thou wilt turn and quicken us; and thy people shall rejoice in thee.  ^84-7

**84:8** Shew us thy mercy, O Lord, and grant us thy salvation.  ^84-8

**84:9** I will hear what the Lord God will say concerning me: for he shall speak peace to his people, and to his saints, and to those that turn their heart toward him.  ^84-9

**84:10** Moreover his salvation is near them that fear him; that glory may dwell in our land.  ^84-10

**84:11** Mercy and truth are met together: righteousness and peace have kissed *each other*.  ^84-11

**84:12** Truth has sprung out of the earth; and righteousness has looked down from heaven.  ^84-12

**84:13** For the Lord will give goodness; and our land shall yield her fruit.  ^84-13

**84:14** Righteousness shall go before him; and shall set his steps in the way.  ^84-14
