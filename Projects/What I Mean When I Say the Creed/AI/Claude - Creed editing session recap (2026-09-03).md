## The introduction's closing paragraph — fully drafted

> *"Finally, nothing in this account can be read forward... This is not a defect I intend to repair. It is what Recognition is..."*

Final version:

> *"Finally, nothing in this account can be run forward. From my perspective, every disclosure I describe becomes legible only afterward. The third day is read back into Hosea; the cross is read back from the resurrection; the criterion I use to say what is unique about Christ is settled by something that happened after the criterion was applied. This backwardness, or any sequence really, is perspectival; the actual motion is spanda, the Sanskrit word for pulse or throb. The revealing and concealing are one simultaneous act. Key moves: reframed from a forward/backward temporal claim to a perspectival one (backwardness belongs to the observer, not the material); introduced **spanda** as the name for the actual (non-directional) motion; resolved via your own insight that from Jesus of Nazareth's perspective the reading was never backward regardless of foreknowledge — which relocated "backward" from a property of events to a cost of standpoint.
