# Psalm 127

← [[BCP Psalm 126]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 128]] →

*Nisi Dominus* · Book Five · Twenty-seventh Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 127]]

**127:1** Unless the LORD builds the house, \*  
their labor is in vain who build it. ^127-1

[p. 783]

**127:2** Unless the LORD watches over the city, \*  
in vain the watchman keeps his vigil. ^127-2

**127:3** It is in vain that you rise so early and go to bed so late; \*  
vain, too, to eat the bread of toil,  
for he gives to his beloved sleep. ^127-3

**127:4** Children are a heritage from the LORD, \*  
and the fruit of the womb is a gift. ^127-4

**127:5** Like arrows in the hand of a warrior \*  
are the children of one's youth. ^127-5

**127:6** Happy is the man who has his quiver full of them! \*  
he shall not be put to shame  
when he contends with his enemies in the gate. ^127-6
