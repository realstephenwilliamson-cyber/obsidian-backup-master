# Song of Solomon 1 (Douay-Rheims)

*Canticle of Canticles 1 in the Douay-Rheims*

— · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Song of Solomon 02|Song of Solomon 2 →]]

**1:1** Let him kiss me with the kiss of his mouth: for thy breasts are better than wine, ^1-1

**1:2** Smelling sweet of the best ointments. Thy name is as oil poured out: therefore young maidens have loved thee. ^1-2

**1:3** Draw me: we will run after thee to the odour of thy ointments. The king hath brought me into his storerooms: we will be glad and rejoice in thee, remembering thy breasts more than wine: the righteous love thee. ^1-3

**1:4** I am black but beautiful, O ye daughters of Jerusalem, as the tents of Cedar, as the curtains of Solomon. ^1-4

**1:5** Do not consider me that I am brown, because the sun hath altered my colour: the sons of my mother have fought against me, they have made me the keeper in the vineyards: my vineyard I have not kept. ^1-5

**1:6** Shew me, O thou whom my soul loveth, where thou feedest, where thou liest in the midday, lest I begin to wander after the flocks of thy companions. ^1-6

**1:7** If thou know not thyself, O fairest among women, go forth, and follow after the steps of the flocks, and feed thy kids beside the tents of the shepherds. ^1-7

**1:8** To my company of horsemen, in Pharao’s chariots, have I likened thee, O my love. ^1-8

**1:9** Thy cheeks are beautiful as the turtledove’s, thy neck as jewels. ^1-9

**1:10** We will make thee chains of gold, inlaid with silver. ^1-10

**1:11** While the king was at his repose, my spikenard sent forth the odour thereof. ^1-11

**1:12** A bundle of myrrh is my beloved to me, he shall abide between my breasts. ^1-12

**1:13** A cluster of cypress my love is to me, in the vineyards of Engaddi. ^1-13

**1:14** Behold thou art fair, O my love, behold thou art fair, thy eyes are as those of doves. ^1-14

**1:15** Behold thou art fair, my beloved, and comely. Our bed is flourishing. ^1-15

**1:16** The beams of our houses are of cedar, our rafters of cypress trees. ^1-16
