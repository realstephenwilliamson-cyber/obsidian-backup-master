# Zohar 3.067b

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 7.08 - Parashat Aharei Mot#^z3-67b|Parashat Aḥarei Mot, at 3:67b]]

*Volume 3, folio 67b* · Achrei Mot

*Passage Achrei Mot 33:203 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 3.067a#^achrei-mot-33-203|Achrei Mot 33:203]].*

**Achrei Mot 33:204** Alternatively, the Community of Israel says to the Holy One, blessed be He, “While I am in exile among the nations and withhold myself from all evil communication with them, my soul desires thee to restore me to my place, and although they subject my sons to all kinds of oppression the holy spirit does not depart from me nor do I cease to seek thee and do thy commandments.’ ^achrei-mot-33-204

**Achrei Mot 33:205** R. Isaac said: ‘Israel says before God: While my soul is still within me I desire thee at night (the exile), because at such time the soul is constrained to yearn for thee, and when the holy spirit awakens within me I shall seek thee early to do thy will.’ R. Hizkiah said that “soul” refers to the Community of Israel and “spirit” to the Holy One, blessed be He. ^achrei-mot-33-205

**Achrei Mot 33:206** When R. Abba was studying with R. Simeon, the latter once rose at midnight to study the Torah, and R. Eleazar and R. Abba rose with him. R. Simeon discoursed on the verse: “As a hind panteth after the water-courses so my soul panteth for thee, O Lord” (Ps. 42, 2). ‘Happy are lsrael,’ he said, ‘for that God gave them the Holy Law and caused them to inherit holy souls from a holy place, that they might keep His commandments and delight themselves in His Law. ^achrei-mot-33-206

**Achrei Mot 33:207** For the Torah is called a delight, and this is what is meant by the saying that God comes to delight Himself with the righteous in the Garden of Eden, to wit, to regale Himself from the selfsame stream as the righteous. And whoever studies the Torah is privileged to delight himself along with the righteous from the waters of this stream. ^achrei-mot-33-207
