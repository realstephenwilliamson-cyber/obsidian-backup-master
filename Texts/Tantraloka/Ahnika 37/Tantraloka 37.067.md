## Mūla

अन्ये पितृव्यतनयाः शिवशक्तिशुभ्राः
क्षेमोत्पलाभिनवचक्रकपद्मगुप्ताः ।
ये संपदं तृणममंसत शंभुसेवा-
संपूरितं स्वहृदयं हृदि भावयन्तः ॥ ^mula

anye pitṛvyatanayāḥ śivaśaktiśubhrāḥ
kṣemotpalābhinavacakrakapadmaguptāḥ |
ye saṃpadaṃ tṛṇamamaṃsata śaṃbhusevā-
saṃpūritaṃ svahṛdayaṃ hṛdi bhāvayantaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 11 - Chapter Thirty-Seven, part 1#^pg277|p. 259]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=277>))
>
> Others were the sons of his paternal uncle, bright with Śiva’s power. They were Kṣema, Utpala, Abhinava, Cakraka and Padmagupta, who considered worldly success (saṁpat) (to be as worthless as) grass, meditating in (their) heart on their own (true) heart, which was completely filled (by their devout) service to Śiva.
