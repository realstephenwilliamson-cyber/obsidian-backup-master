# χρηματίζω

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · verb

**Dodson:** I warn, am publicly called — (originally: I transact business), (a) act. of God: I warn; pass: I am warned by God (probably in response to an inquiry as to one's duty), (b) (I take a name from my public business, hence) I receive a name, am publicly called. (Strong's 5537; headword χρηματίζω)
