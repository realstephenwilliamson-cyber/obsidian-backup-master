15\. Citrā Nityā

aim hrīm 

aḥ ckaum śrī pādukām pūjayāmi śrīm

aḥ citrā tarpayāmi namaḥ

16\. Ṣōḍaśi (Śrī Rājarājēśvarī)

aim hrīm 

aḥm śrī pādukām pūjayāmi śrīm

ka ē ī la hrīm tarpayāmi namaḥ ha sa ka la hrīm sa ka la hrīm aḥm ṣōḍaśi

The Nityā Dēvatās are also identified with the 15-syllable *Pañcadaśī Mantra*, with each syllable of the same being recited on its associated day or *nityā*. Each syllable refers to the eternal, formless aspects of the cosmos (Shiva) or to the material universe and its maya (Shakti). It is considered appropriate to worship the Devi on the days associated with Shakti, and not on days associated with Shiva.
