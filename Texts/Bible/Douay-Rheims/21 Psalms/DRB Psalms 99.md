# Psalms 99 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 98|← Psalms 98]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 100|Psalms 100 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 99|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**99:1** A psalm of praise. ^99-1

**99:2** Sing joyfully to God, all the earth: serve ye the Lord with gladness. Come in before his presence with exceeding great joy. ^99-2

**99:3** Know ye that the Lord he is God: he made us, and not we ourselves. We are his people and the sheep of his pasture. ^99-3

**99:4** Go ye into his gates with praise, into his courts with hymns: and give glory to him. Praise ye his name: ^99-4

**99:5** For the Lord is sweet, his mercy endureth for ever, and his truth to generation and generation. ^99-5
