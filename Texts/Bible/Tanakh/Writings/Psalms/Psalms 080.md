## Chapter 80

**80:1** For the leader; on *shoshannim*, *eduth*. Of Asaph. A psalm. ^80-1

**80:2** Give ear, O shepherd of Israel  
who leads Joseph like a flock!  
Appear, You who are enthroned on the cherubim, ^80-2

**80:3** at the head of Ephraim, Benjamin, and Manasseh!  
Rouse Your might and come to our help! ^80-3

**80:4** Restore us, O God;  
show Your favor that we may be delivered. ^80-4

**80:5** O LORD, God of hosts,  
how long will You be wrathful  
toward the prayers of Your people? ^80-5

**80:6** You have fed them tears as their daily bread,  
made them drink great measures of tears. ^80-6

**80:7** You set us at strife with our neighbors;  
our enemies mock us at will. ^80-7

**80:8** O God of hosts, restore us;  
show Your favor that we may be delivered. ^80-8

**80:9** You plucked up a vine from Egypt;  
You expelled nations and planted it. ^80-9

**80:10** You cleared a place for it;  
it took deep root and filled the land. ^80-10

**80:11** The mountains were covered by its shade,  
mighty cedars by its boughs. ^80-11

**80:12** Its branches reached the sea,  
its shoots, the river. ^80-12

**80:13** Why did You breach its wall  
so that every passerby plucks its fruit, ^80-13

**80:14** wild boars gnaw at it,  
and creatures of the field feed on it? ^80-14

**80:15** O God of hosts, turn again,  
look down from heaven and see;  
take note of that vine, ^80-15

**80:16** the stock planted by Your right hand,  
the stem[^1] you have taken as Your own. ^80-16

**80:17** For it is burned by fire and cut down,  
perishing before Your angry blast. ^80-17

**80:18** Grant Your help[^2] to the man at Your right hand,  
the one You have taken as Your own. ^80-18

**80:19** We will not turn away from You;  
preserve our life that we may invoke Your name. ^80-19

**80:20** O LORD, God of hosts, restore us;  
show Your favor that we may be delivered. ^80-20

[^1]: Lit. “son.”
[^2]: Lit. “hand.”
