## Mūla

विज्ञानाकलपर्यन्तमात्मा विद्येश्वरान्तकम् ।
शेषे शिवस्त्रितत्त्वे स्यादेकतत्त्वे शिवः परम् ॥ ^mula

vijñānākalaparyantamātmā vidyeśvarāntakam |

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 02 - Chapter Eleven, part 1#^pg54|p. 34]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=54>))
>
> Now that the procedure concerning (the division into) five Principles has been explained, we will talk about (the division) into three (Principles). (All that exists up to the level) of the Consciousness Deconditioned (perceivers) (vijñānākala) is the Self (Ātman). (All that extends from the Mantra subjects) up to Īśvara is Knowledge (Vidyā)’, and the rest Śiva. When there is (just) one principle, it is (only) Śiva who is supreme. (with 11.34cd–35)
