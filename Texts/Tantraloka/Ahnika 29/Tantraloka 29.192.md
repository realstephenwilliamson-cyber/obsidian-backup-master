## Mūla

आकर्ष्याकर्षकत्वेन प्रेर्यप्रेरकभावतः ।
उक्तं श्रीरत्नमालायां नाभिं दण्डेन संपुटम् ॥ ^mula

ākarṣyākarṣakatvena preryaprerakabhāvataḥ |
uktaṃ śrīratnamālāyāṃ nābhiṃ daṇḍena saṃpuṭam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 12 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 4#^pg372|p. 355]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=372>))
>
> (This can take place) because (the wheel of goddesses is both) impelled and impelling as the pulled and the puller. (with 29.192ab)
>
> It is said in the venerable Ratnamālā: ‘(the teacher) should apply the weapon of the command (codanāstra) - consisting of the navel (K), encapsulated with the staff (R), adorned with the left ornament (Ū) and shank (AU) as well as with the hip (M) — on the disciple’s hand that holds the flower. (He should do this) until paralysis (stobha) ensues and the (hand) falls by itself on (his) head. This is itself Śiva’s Hand, that instils immediate conviction (pratyaya) (of its effectiveness). With this same procedure (prayoga), the teacher should make the disciple take hold of the sacrificial pap and the (cleaning) stick for the teeth. (The significance of) its fall (where and how it takes place) is as before. (with 29.192cd–195)

*Lead-in:* देवीचक्रं च अत्र कथमर्चयेत् ?--इत्याशङ्क्य आह--

> आकर्ष्याकर्षकत्वेन प्रेर्यप्रेरकभावतः ।

हस्तयोर्हि प्रेर्यत्वेन देवीचक्रमभ्यर्चयेत् मूर्ध्नि च प्रेरकत्वेन । यतस्तदाकर्षणीयं तच्च आकर्षकम् । एवं हि मूर्ध्नि पूजितस्य देवीचक्रस्य सामर्थ्येन आकृष्टं हस्तद्वयम् । तत्रैव पाततः शिवहस्ततां यायादिति । यदुक्तम्--

"ततोऽस्य मस्तके चक्रं हस्तयोश्चार्च्य योगवित् ।

तद्धस्तौ प्रेरयेच्छक्त्या यावन्मूर्धान्तमागतौ ॥

शिवहस्तविधिः प्रोक्तः सद्यःप्रत्ययकारकः ।"

इति ॥

यदा पुनरेवं शिवहस्तविधिर्न सिद्ध्येत्, तदा शास्त्रान्तरीयं क्रममनुतिष्ठेत्--इत्याह--
