## Mūla

स्वं स्वरूपं पञ्चदशं तद्भूः पञ्चदशात्मिका ।
तथाहि तिस्रो देवस्य शक्तयो वर्णिताः पुरा ॥ ^mula

svaṃ svarūpaṃ pañcadaśaṃ tadbhūḥ pañcadaśātmikā |
tathāhi tisro devasya śaktayo varṇitāḥ purā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 10 - Chapter Ten, part 1#^pg312|p. 293]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=312>))
>
> The powerholders are seven, namely, Śiva, the Great Lords of Mantra, the Lords, the Mantras, the deconditioned pair (of subjects) (akalayuk), and the Conditioned (perceiver) (kalin). Along with their powers, they number fourteen. The fifteenth is (their) own ‘Innate Nature’ (svarūpa). (Thus the ‘Innate Nature’ of) Earth has fifteen aspects. (with 10.6–7ab)
>
> In this way, the three powers of the Lord (namely, Parā, Parāparā and Aparā) described previously are established in this way in a three-fold form as subject, means and object of knowledge. (with 10.7cd–8ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* नन्वस्मद्दर्शने नरशक्तिशिवात्मकमेव विश्वमिति सर्वत्रोद्धोष्यते, तत्कथमिह सिद्धान्तदर्शनादिसमुचितं प्रमातृभेदमवलम्ब्यैतदुक्तम् ? -- इत्याशङ्कां गर्भीकृत्यैतदुपपादयति --

> तथाहि तिस्त्रो देवस्य शक्तयो वर्णिताः पुरा ॥
> ता एव मातृमानमेयत्रैरूप्येण व्यवस्थिताः ।
