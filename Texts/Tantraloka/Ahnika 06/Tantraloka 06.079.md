## Mūla

तौ कॢप्तौ यावति तया तावत्येव दिनक्षपे ।
यावत्येव हि संवित्तिरुदितोदितसुस्फुटा ॥ ^mula

tau kḷptau yāvati tayā tāvatyeva dinakṣape |
yāvatyeva hi saṃvittiruditoditasusphuṭā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 10 - Chapter Six, part 3#^pg320|p. 305]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=320>))
>
> Every moment, consciousness is associated with Light and Bliss, and so those two, Day and Night, are such, fashioned as they are by that (same consciousness). (with 6.78cd–79ab)
>
> To the degree in which emergent consciousness has emerged very clearly, to that degree a moment is a cosmic age (kalpa) (if clear), or (if unclear,) the batting of an eyelid — so too (is it with) that (Day and Night). (with 6.79cd–80ab)
