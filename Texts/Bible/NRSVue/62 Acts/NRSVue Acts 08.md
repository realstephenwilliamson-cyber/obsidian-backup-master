# Acts 8 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Acts 07|← Acts 7]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Acts 09|Acts 9 →]]

Compare: [[Acts 08|BSB and SBLGNT]] · [[DRB Acts 08|Douay-Rheims]] · [[JANT Acts 08|JANT notes]]

**8:1** And Saul approved of their killing him. That day a severe persecution began against the church in Jerusalem, and all except the apostles were scattered throughout the countryside of Judea and Samaria. ^8-1

## Saul persecutes the church

**8:2** Devout men buried Stephen and made loud lamentation over him. ^8-2

**8:3** But Saul was ravaging the church by entering house after house; dragging off both men and women, he committed them to prison. ^8-3

## Philip preaches in Samaria

**8:4** Now those who were scattered went from place to place proclaiming the word. ^8-4

**8:5** Philip went down to the city^[Other ancient authorities read *a city*] of Samaria and proclaimed the Messiah^[Or *the Christ*] to them. ^8-5

**8:6** The crowds with one accord listened eagerly to what was said by Philip, hearing and seeing the signs that he did, ^8-6

**8:7** for unclean spirits, crying with loud shrieks, came out of many who were possessed, and many others who were paralyzed or lame were cured. ^8-7

**8:8** So there was great joy in that city. ^8-8

**8:9** Now a certain man named Simon had previously practiced magic in the city and amazed the people of Samaria, saying that he was someone great. ^8-9

**8:10** All of them, from the least to the greatest, listened to him eagerly, saying, “This man is the power of God that is called Great.” ^8-10

**8:11** And they listened eagerly to him because for a long time he had amazed them with his magic. ^8-11

**8:12** But when they believed Philip, who was proclaiming the good news about the kingdom of God and the name of Jesus Christ, they were baptized, both men and women. ^8-12

**8:13** Even Simon himself believed. After being baptized, he stayed constantly with Philip and was amazed when he saw the signs and great miracles that took place. ^8-13

**8:14** Now when the apostles at Jerusalem heard that Samaria had accepted the word of God, they sent Peter and John to them. ^8-14

**8:15** The two went down and prayed for them that they might receive the Holy Spirit ^8-15

**8:16** (for as yet the Spirit had not come^[Gk *fallen*] upon any of them; they had only been baptized in the name of the Lord Jesus). ^8-16

**8:17** Then Peter and John^[Gk *they*] laid their hands on them, and they received the Holy Spirit. ^8-17

**8:18** Now when Simon saw that the Spirit was given through the laying on of the apostles’ hands, he offered them money, ^8-18

**8:19** saying, “Give me also this power so that anyone on whom I lay my hands may receive the Holy Spirit.” ^8-19

**8:20** But Peter said to him, “May your silver perish with you, because you thought you could obtain God’s gift with money! ^8-20

**8:21** You have no part or share in this, for your heart is not right before God. ^8-21

**8:22** Repent therefore of this wickedness of yours, and pray to the Lord that, if possible, the intent of your heart may be forgiven you. ^8-22

**8:23** For I see that you are in the gall of bitterness and the chains of wickedness.” ^8-23

**8:24** Simon answered, “Pray for me to the Lord, that nothing of what you^[The Greek word for *you* and the verb *pray* are plural] have said may happen to me.” ^8-24

**8:25** Now after Peter and John^[Gk *after they*] had testified and spoken the word of the Lord, they returned to Jerusalem, proclaiming the good news to many villages of the Samaritans. ^8-25

## Philip and the Ethiopian eunuch

**8:26** Then an angel of the Lord said to Philip, “Get up and go toward the south^[Or *go at noon*] to the road that goes down from Jerusalem to Gaza.” (This is a wilderness road.) ^8-26

**8:27** So he got up and went. Now there was an Ethiopian eunuch, a court official of the Candace, the queen of the Ethiopians, in charge of her entire treasury. He had come to Jerusalem to worship ^8-27

**8:28** and was returning home; seated in his chariot, he was reading the prophet Isaiah. ^8-28

**8:29** Then the Spirit said to Philip, “Go over to this chariot and join it.” ^8-29

**8:30** So Philip ran up to it and heard him reading the prophet Isaiah. He asked, “Do you understand what you are reading?” ^8-30

**8:31** He replied, “How can I, unless someone guides me?” And he invited Philip to get in and sit beside him. ^8-31

**8:32** Now the passage of the scripture that he was reading was this: “Like a sheep he was led to the slaughter, and like a lamb silent before its shearer, so he does not open his mouth. ^8-32

**8:33** In his humiliation justice was denied him. Who can describe his generation? For his life is taken away from the earth.” ^8-33

**8:34** The eunuch asked Philip, “About whom, may I ask you, does the prophet say this, about himself or about someone else?” ^8-34

**8:35** Then Philip began to speak, and starting with this scripture he proclaimed to him the good news about Jesus. ^8-35

**8:36** As they were going along the road, they came to some water, and the eunuch said, “Look, here is water! What is to prevent me from being baptized?”^[Other ancient authorities add all or most of 8.37, *And Philip said, “If you believe with all your heart, you may.” And he replied, “I believe that Jesus Christ is the Son of God.”*] ^8-36

**8:38** He commanded the chariot to stop, and both of them, Philip and the eunuch, went down into the water, and Philip^[Gk *he*] baptized him. ^8-38

**8:39** When they came up out of the water, the Spirit of the Lord snatched Philip away; the eunuch saw him no more and went on his way rejoicing. ^8-39

**8:40** But Philip found himself at Azotus, and as he was passing through the region he proclaimed the good news to all the towns until he came to Caesarea. ^8-40
