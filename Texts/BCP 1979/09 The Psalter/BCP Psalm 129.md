# Psalm 129

← [[BCP Psalm 128]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 130]] →

*Sæpe expugnaverunt* · Book Five · Twenty-seventh Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 129]]

**129:1** "Greatly have they oppressed me since my youth," \*  
let Israel now say; ^129-1

**129:2** "Greatly have they oppressed me since my youth, \*  
but they have not prevailed against me." ^129-2

**129:3** The plowmen plowed upon my back \*  
and made their furrows long. ^129-3

**129:4** The LORD, the Righteous One, \*  
has cut the cords of the wicked. ^129-4

**129:5** Let them be put to shame and thrown back, \*  
all those who are enemies of Zion. ^129-5

**129:6** Let them be like grass upon the housetops, \*  
which withers before it can be plucked; ^129-6

**129:7** Which does not fill the hand of the reaper, \*  
nor the bosom of him who binds the sheaves; ^129-7

**129:8** So that those who go by say not so much as,  
"The LORD prosper you. \*  
We wish you well in the Name of the LORD." ^129-8
