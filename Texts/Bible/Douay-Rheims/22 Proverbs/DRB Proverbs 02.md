# Proverbs 2 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Proverbs 01|← Proverbs 1]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Proverbs 03|Proverbs 3 →]]

**2:1** My son, if thou wilt receive my words, and wilt hide my commandments with thee, ^2-1

**2:2** That thy ear may hearken to wisdom: Incline thy heart to know prudence: ^2-2

**2:3** For if thou shalt call for wisdom, and incline thy heart to prudence: ^2-3

**2:4** If thou shalt seek her as money, and shalt dig for her as for a treasure: ^2-4

**2:5** Then shalt thou understand the fear of the Lord, and shalt find the knowledge of God. ^2-5

**2:6** Because the Lord giveth wisdom: and out of his mouth cometh prudence and knowledge. ^2-6

**2:7** He will keep the salvation of the righteous, and protect them that walk in simplicity. ^2-7

**2:8** Keeping the paths of justice, and guarding the ways of saints. ^2-8

**2:9** Then shalt thou understand justice, and judgment, and equity, and every good path. ^2-9

**2:10** If wisdom shall enter into thy heart, and knowledge please thy soul: ^2-10

**2:11** Counsel shall keep thee, and prudence shall preserve thee, ^2-11

**2:12** That thou mayst be delivered from the evil way, and from the man that speaketh perverse things: ^2-12

**2:13** Who leave the right way, and walk by dark ways: ^2-13

**2:14** Who are glad when they have done evil, and rejoice in most wicked things: ^2-14

**2:15** Whose ways are perverse, and their steps infamous. ^2-15

**2:16** That thou mayst be delivered from the strange women, and from the stranger, who softeneth her words: ^2-16

**2:17** And forsaketh the guide of her youth, ^2-17

**2:18** And hath forgotten the covenant of her God: for her house inclineth unto death, and her paths to hell. ^2-18

**2:19** None that go in unto her shall return again, neither shall they take hold of the paths of life, ^2-19

**2:20** That thou mayst walk in a good way: and mayst keep the paths of the just. ^2-20

**2:21** For they that are upright shall dwell in the earth, and the simple shall continue in it. ^2-21

**2:22** But the wicked shall be destroyed from the earth: and they that do unjustly shall be taken away from it. ^2-22
