## Mūla

य ऊर्ध्वे किल संबोधः कुण्डे स प्रतिबिम्बितः ।
वह्निः प्राणः स्रुक्स्रुवौ च स्नेहः सङ्कल्पचिद्रसः ॥ ^mula

ya ūrdhve kila saṃbodhaḥ kuṇḍe sa pratibimbitaḥ |
vahniḥ prāṇaḥ sruksruvau ca snehaḥ saṅkalpacidrasaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 08 - Chapter Fifteen, part 7#^pg283|p. 265]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=283>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 15.431cd–432ab.
>
> The consciousness that is above, reflected in the (sacrificial) pit, is the fire. The large and small ladles are the vital breath. The clarified butter is the juice of (the aesthetic delight of) consciousness of (the sacrificer’s sacred) intent (saṁkalpa-cidrasa). Having known (this) in this way from the start, and having firmly placed in the fire of consciousness (that burns) in the End of the Twelve, the fire pit, the large and small ladles, the butter and the Mantras, (the teacher) should cast the (final) full oblation (into the outer fire). (with 15.429–430)
