# Psalms 114 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 113|← Psalms 113]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 115|Psalms 115 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 114|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**114:1** I have loved, because the Lord will hear the voice of my prayer. ^114-1

**114:2** Because he hath inclined his ear unto me: and in my days I will call upon him. ^114-2

**114:3** The sorrows of death have encompassed me: and the perils of hell have found me. I met with trouble and sorrow: ^114-3

**114:4** And I called upon the name of the Lord. O Lord, deliver my soul. ^114-4

**114:5** The Lord is merciful and just, and our God sheweth mercy. ^114-5

**114:6** The Lord is the keeper of little ones: I was little and he delivered me. ^114-6

**114:7** Turn, O my soul, into thy rest: for the Lord hath been bountiful to thee. ^114-7

**114:8** For he hath delivered my soul from death: my eyes from tears, my feet from falling. ^114-8

**114:9** I will please the Lord in the land of the living. ^114-9
