## Chapter 45

**45:1** For the leader; on *shoshannim*.[^1] Of the Korahites. A *maskil*. A love song. ^45-1

**45:2** My heart is astir with gracious words;  
I speak my poem to a king;  
my tongue is the pen of an expert scribe. ^45-2

**45:3** You are fairer than all men;  
your speech is endowed with grace;  
rightly has God given you an eternal blessing. ^45-3

**45:4** Gird your sword upon your thigh, O hero,  
in your splendor and glory; ^45-4

**45:5** in your glory, win success;  
ride on in the cause of truth and meekness and right;  
and let your right hand lead you to awesome deeds.[^2] ^45-5

**45:6** Your arrows, sharpened,  
[pierce] the breast of the king’s enemies;  
peoples fall at your feet.[^3] ^45-6

**45:7** Your divine throne[^4] is everlasting;  
your royal scepter is a scepter of equity. ^45-7

**45:8** You love righteousness and hate wickedness;  
rightly has God, your God, chosen to anoint you  
with oil of gladness over all your peers. ^45-8

**45:9** All your robes [are fragrant] with  
myrrh and aloes and cassia;  
from ivoried palaces  
lutes entertain you. ^45-9

**45:10** Royal princesses are your favorites;  
the consort stands at your right hand,  
decked in gold of Ophir. ^45-10

**45:11** Take heed, lass, and note,  
incline your ear:  
forget your people and your father’s house, ^45-11

**45:12** and let the king be aroused by your beauty;  
since he is your lord, bow to him. ^45-12

**45:13** O Tyrian lass,  
the wealthiest people will court your favor with gifts, ^45-13

**45:14** [^5]goods of all sorts.  
The royal princess,  
her dress embroidered with golden mountings, ^45-14

**45:15** is led inside to the king;  
maidens in her train, her companions,  
are presented to you. ^45-15

**45:16** They are led in with joy and gladness;  
they enter the palace of the king. ^45-16

**45:17** Your sons will succeed your ancestors;  
you will appoint them princes throughout the land. ^45-17

**45:18** I commemorate your fame for all generations,  
so peoples will praise you forever and ever. ^45-18

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Order of Heb. clauses inverted for clarity.
[^4]: Cf. 1 Chron. 29.23.
[^5]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
