## Chapter 146

**146:1** Hallelujah.  
Praise the LORD, O my soul! ^146-1

**146:2** I will praise the LORD all my life,  
sing hymns to my God while I exist. ^146-2

**146:3** Put not your trust in the great,  
in mortal man who cannot save. ^146-3

**146:4** His breath departs;  
he returns to the dust;  
on that day his plans come to nothing. ^146-4

**146:5** Happy is he who has the God of Jacob for his help,  
whose hope is in the LORD his God, ^146-5

**146:6** maker of heaven and earth,  
the sea and all that is in them;  
who keeps faith forever; ^146-6

**146:7** who secures justice for those who are wronged,  
gives food to the hungry.  
The LORD sets prisoners free; ^146-7

**146:8** The LORD restores sight to the blind;  
the LORD makes those who are bent stand straight;  
the LORD loves the righteous; ^146-8

**146:9** The LORD watches over the stranger;  
He gives courage to the orphan and widow,  
but makes the path of the wicked tortuous. ^146-9

**146:10** The LORD shall reign forever,  
your God, O Zion, for all generations.  
Hallelujah. ^146-10
