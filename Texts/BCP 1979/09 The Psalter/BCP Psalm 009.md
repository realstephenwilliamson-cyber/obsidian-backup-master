# Psalm 9

← [[BCP Psalm 008]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 010]] →

*Confitebor tibi* · Book One · Second Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 009]]

**9:1** I will give thanks to you, O LORD, with my whole heart; \*  
I will tell of all your marvelous works. ^9-1

**9:2** I will be glad and rejoice in you; \*  
I will sing to your Name, O Most High. ^9-2

**9:3** When my enemies are driven back, \*  
they will stumble and perish at your presence. ^9-3

**9:4** For you have maintained my right and my cause; \*  
you sit upon your throne judging right. ^9-4

**9:5** You have rebuked the ungodly and destroyed the wicked; \*  
you have blotted out their name for ever and ever. ^9-5

**9:6** As for the enemy, they are finished, in perpetual ruin, \*  
their cities plowed under, the memory of them perished; ^9-6

**9:7** But the LORD is enthroned for ever; \*  
he has set up his throne for judgment. ^9-7

**9:8** It is he who rules the world with righteousness; \*  
he judges the peoples with equity. ^9-8

**9:9** The LORD will be a refuge for the oppressed, \*  
a refuge in time of trouble. ^9-9

**9:10** Those who know your Name will put their trust in you, \*  
for you never forsake those who seek you, O LORD. ^9-10

**9:11** Sing praise to the LORD who dwells in Zion; \*  
proclaim to the peoples the things he has done. ^9-11

**9:12** The Avenger of blood will remember them; \*  
he will not forget the cry of the afflicted. ^9-12

[p. 594] ^pg594

**9:13** Have pity on me, O LORD; \*  
see the misery I suffer from those who hate me,  
O you who lift me up from the gate of death; ^9-13

**9:14** So that I may tell of all your praises  
and rejoice in your salvation \*  
in the gates of the city of Zion. ^9-14

**9:15** The ungodly have fallen into the pit they dug, \*  
and in the snare they set is their own foot caught. ^9-15

**9:16** The LORD is known by his acts of justice; \*  
the wicked are trapped in the works of their own hands. ^9-16

**9:17** The wicked shall be given over to the grave, \*  
and also all the people that forget God. ^9-17

**9:18** For the needy shall not always be forgotten, \*  
and the hope of the poor shall not perish for ever. ^9-18

**9:19** Rise up, O LORD, let not the ungodly have the upper hand; \*  
let them be judged before you. ^9-19

**9:20** Put fear upon them, O LORD; \*  
let the ungodly know they are but mortal. ^9-20
