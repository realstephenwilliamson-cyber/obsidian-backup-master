## Mūla

अग्नीषोमसनातनमृत्पिण्डं जहिहि हे महाकाश! ।
एतद्भुवनसंख्यातैरार्य्यावाक्यं प्रकीर्तितम् ॥ ^mula

agnīṣomasanātanamṛtpiṇḍaṃ jahihi he mahākāśa! |
etadbhuvanasaṃkhyātairāryyāvākyaṃ prakīrtitam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 03 - Chapter Thirty, part 2#^pg67|p. 49]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=67>))
>
> 9) “O Brahmā! O Viṣṇu! O Rudra! You are Śiva! You are Vasudeva! O great sky! Abandon indeed the primeval body of earth (fashioned from) Fire and Soma.” (with 30.79cd–80ab)
>
> This declaration, set in Ārya metre, is said to be made of (fourteen words, as is) the number of worlds. In the Nihśvāsa, Mukuṭottaratantra and elsewhere (instead of the words sanātana (primeval), mrtpinda (body of earth) and mahākāśa (great sky), the three words are sanātma (individual existence), tripinda (triple body), and mahākośa (great container), (respectively). (with 30.80cd–81)
