# Psalms 48 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 47|← Psalms 47]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 49|Psalms 49 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 48|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**48:1** Unto the end, a psalm for the sons of Core. ^48-1

**48:2** Hear these things, all ye nations: give ear, all ye inhabitants of the world. ^48-2

**48:3** All you that are earthborn, and you sons of men: both rich and poor together. ^48-3

**48:4** My mouth shall speak wisdom: and the meditation of my heart understanding. ^48-4

**48:5** I will incline my ear to a parable; I will open my proposition on the psaltery. ^48-5

**48:6** Why shall I fear in the evil day? the iniquity of my heel shall encompass me. ^48-6

**48:7** They that trust in their own strength, and glory in the multitude of their riches, ^48-7

**48:8** No brother can redeem, nor shall man redeem: he shall not give to God his ransom, ^48-8

**48:9** Nor the price of the redemption of his soul: and shall labour for ever, ^48-9

**48:10** And shall still live unto the end. ^48-10

**48:11** He shall not see destruction, when he shall see the wise dying: the senseless and the fool shall perish together: And they shall leave their riches to strangers: ^48-11

**48:12** And their sepulchres shall be their houses for ever. Their dwelling places to all generations: they have called their lands by their names. ^48-12

**48:13** And man when he was in honour did not understand; he is compared to senseless beasts, and is become like to them. ^48-13

**48:14** This way of theirs is a stumblingblock to them: and afterwards they shall delight in their mouth. ^48-14

**48:15** They are laid in hell like sheep: death shall feed upon them. And the just shall have dominion over them in the morning; and their help shall decay in hell from their glory. ^48-15

**48:16** But God will redeem my soul from the hand of hell, when he shall receive me. ^48-16

**48:17** Be not thou afraid, when a man shall be made rich, and when the glory of his house shall be increased. ^48-17

**48:18** For when he shall die he shall take nothing away; nor shall his glory descend with him. ^48-18

**48:19** For in his lifetime his soul will be blessed: and he will praise thee when thou shalt do well to him. ^48-19

**48:20** He shall go in to the generations of his fathers: and he shall never see light. ^48-20

**48:21** Man when he was in honour did not understand: he hath been compared to senseless beasts, and made like to them. ^48-21
