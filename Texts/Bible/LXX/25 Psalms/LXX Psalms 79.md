# LXX Psalms 79

← [[LXX Psalms 78]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 80]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 080]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 080]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**79:1** For the end, for alternate *strains*, a testimony for Asaph, a Psalm concerning the Assyrian.[^1]  ^79-1

**79:2** Attend, O Shepherd of Israel, who guidest Joseph like a flock; thou who sittest upon the cherubs, manifest thyself;  ^79-2

**79:3** before Ephraim and Benjamin and Manasse, stir up thy power, and come to deliver us.  ^79-3

**79:4** Turn us, O God, and cause thy face to shine; and we shall be delivered.  ^79-4

**79:5** O Lord God of hosts, how long art thou angry with the prayer of thy servant?  ^79-5

**79:6** Thou wilt feed us with bread of tears; and wilt cause us to drink tears by measure.  ^79-6

**79:7** Thou hast made us a strife to our neighbours; and our enemies have mocked at us.  ^79-7

**79:8** Turn us, O Lord God of hosts, and cause thy face to shine; and we shall be saved. Pause.  ^79-8

**79:9** Thou hast transplanted a vine out of Egypt: thou hast cast out the heathen, and planted it.  ^79-9

**79:10** Thou madest a way before it, and didst cause its roots to strike, and the land was filled *with it*.  ^79-10

**79:11** Its shadow covered the mountains, and its shoots *equalled* the goodly cedars.[^2]  ^79-11

**79:12** It sent forth its branches to the sea, and its shoots to the river.  ^79-12

**79:13** Wherefore hast thou broken down its hedge, while all that pass by the way pluck it?  ^79-13

**79:14** The boar out of the wood has laid it waste, and the wild beast has devoured it.  ^79-14

**79:15** O God of hosts, turn, we pray thee: look on *us* from heaven, and behold and visit this vine;  ^79-15

**79:16** and restore that which thy right hand has planted: and look on the son of man whom thou didst strengthen for thyself.  ^79-16

**79:17** *It is* burnt with fire and dug up: they shall perish at the rebuke of thy presence.[^3]  ^79-17

**79:18** Let thy hand be upon the man of thy right hand, and upon the son of man whom thou didst strengthen for thyself.  ^79-18

**79:19** So will we not depart from thee: thou shalt quicken us, and we will call upon thy name.  ^79-19

**79:20** Turn us, O Lord God of hosts, and make thy face to shine; and we shall be saved.  ^79-20

[^1]: 79:1 See Ps. 44, and 68, titles.
[^2]: 79:11 *Gr.* Cedars of God, see Gen. 30. 8; Jon. 3. 3; Acts 7. 20.
[^3]: 79:17 *Gr.* See Ps. 20. 9.
