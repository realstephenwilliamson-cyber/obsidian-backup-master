# συνέχω

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · verb

**Dodson:** I press together, confine, compel, am afflicted with — (a) I press together, close, (b) I press on every side, confine, (c) I hold fast, (d) I urge, impel, (e) pass: I am afflicted with (sickness). (Strong's 4912; headword συνέχω)
