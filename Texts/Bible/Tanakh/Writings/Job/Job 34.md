# Job 34

← [[Job 33]] · [[Tanakh]] · [[Job 35]] →

Compare: [[NRSVue Job 34|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 34

**34:1** Elihu said in reply: ^34-1

**34:2** Listen, O wise men, to my words;  
You who have knowledge, give ear to me. ^34-2

**34:3** For the ear tests arguments  
As the palate tastes food. ^34-3

**34:4** Let us decide for ourselves what is just;  
Let us know among ourselves what is good. ^34-4

**34:5** For Job has said, “I am right;  
God has deprived me of justice. ^34-5

**34:6** I declare the judgment against me false;  
My arrow-wound is deadly, though I am free from transgression.” ^34-6

**34:7** What man is like Job,  
Who drinks mockery like water; ^34-7

**34:8** Who makes common cause with evildoers,  
And goes with wicked men? ^34-8

**34:9** For he says, “Man gains nothing  
When he is in God’s favor.” ^34-9

**34:10** Therefore, men of understanding, listen to me;  
Wickedness be far from God,  
Wrongdoing, from Shaddai! ^34-10

**34:11** For He pays a man according to his actions,  
And provides for him according to his conduct; ^34-11

**34:12** For God surely does not act wickedly;  
Shaddai does not pervert justice. ^34-12

**34:13** Who placed the earth in His charge?  
Who ordered the entire world? ^34-13

**34:14** If He but intends it,  
He can call back His spirit and breath; ^34-14

**34:15** All flesh would at once expire,  
And mankind return to dust. ^34-15

**34:16** If you would understand, listen to this;  
Give ear to what I say. ^34-16

**34:17** Would one who hates justice govern?  
Would you condemn the Just Mighty One? ^34-17

**34:18** Would you call a king a scoundrel,  
Great men, wicked? ^34-18

**34:19** He is not partial to princes;  
The noble are not preferred to the wretched;  
For all of them are the work of His hands. ^34-19

**34:20** Some die suddenly in the middle of the night;  
People are in turmoil and pass on;  
Even great men are removed—not by human hands. ^34-20

**34:21** For His eyes are upon a man’s ways;  
He observes his every step. ^34-21

**34:22** Neither darkness nor gloom offers  
A hiding-place for evildoers. ^34-22

**34:23** He has no set time for man  
To appear before God in judgment. ^34-23

**34:24** He shatters mighty men without number  
And sets others in their place. ^34-24

**34:25** Truly, He knows their deeds;  
Night is over, and they are crushed. ^34-25

**34:26** He strikes them down with the wicked  
Where people can see, ^34-26

**34:27** Because they have been disloyal to Him  
And have not understood any of His ways; ^34-27

**34:28** Thus He lets the cry of the poor come before Him;  
He listens to the cry of the needy. ^34-28

**34:29** When He is silent, who will condemn?  
If He hides His face, who will see Him,  
Be it nation or man? ^34-29

**34:30** The impious man rules no more,  
Nor do those who ensnare the people. ^34-30

**34:31** Has he said to God,  
“I will bear [my punishment] and offend no more. ^34-31

**34:32** What I cannot see You teach me.  
If I have done iniquity, I shall not do so again”? ^34-32

**34:33** Should He requite as you see fit?  
But you have despised [Him]!  
You must decide, not I;  
Speak what you know. ^34-33

**34:34** Men of understanding say to me,  
Wise men who hear me, ^34-34

**34:35** “Job does not speak with knowledge;  
His words lack understanding.” ^34-35

**34:36** Would that Job were tried to the limit  
For answers which befit sinful men. ^34-36

**34:37** He adds to his sin;  
He increases his transgression among us;  
He multiplies his statements against God. ^34-37
