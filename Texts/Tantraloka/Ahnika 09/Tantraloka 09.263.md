## Mūla

यत्साधनम् तदक्षं स्यात् किन्तु कस्यापि कर्मणः ।
एतन्नास्मत्कृतप्रश्नतृष्णासन्तापशान्तये ॥ ^mula

yatsādhanam tadakṣaṃ syāt kintu kasyāpi karmaṇaḥ |
etannāsmatkṛtapraśnatṛṣṇāsantāpaśāntaye || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 09 - Chapter Nine, part 7#^pg265|p. 246]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=265>))
>
> (Our reply is that,) surely Kheṭapāla and others have refuted (this objection by saying that) not every means of executing an action needs to be an organ of action. Rather, it is so (only) in the case of certain (specific) actions (such as walking or grasping). (with 9.262cd–263ab)
>
> (This reply however) is not good enough to quell the suffering that comes (from the intense) thirst (for the truth aroused) by the question put to us. Peacocks are not satisfied with a little dirty water! (with 9.263cd–264ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* न ह्येतदस्माकमावर्जकं साक्षात्समाधानम् -- इत्याह --

> एतन्नास्मत्कृतप्रश्नतृष्णासन्तापशान्तये ॥
> नह्यस्वच्छमितप्रायैर्जलैस्तृप्यन्ति बर्हिणः ।

एतदस्माकं परकृतप्रश्नतृष्णासंतापं शमयितुं नालम् -- इत्यर्थः । एवं हि पराभिमतानि कर्मान्तराणि करणान्तराणि च सन्तीत्यभ्युपगतं भवतीति ॥ २६३ ॥
