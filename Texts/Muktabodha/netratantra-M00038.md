# netratantra

**Catalog number:** M00038  
**Main title:** netratantra with commentary by kṣemarāja  
**Commentator:** kṣemarāja  
**Editor(s):** śāstrī m. k., malaviya sudhakar  
**Published:** Electronic publisher Muktabodha Indological Research Institu, United States, 2007

## Description

The e-text was converted from devanagari typesetting files of Dr. Sudhakar Malaviya. His files were based on the edition of the Kashmir Series of Texts and Studies volumes 46 and 61.

The devanagari files were converted to roman transliteration by computer programs created by Muktabodha.

The photographic facsimiles are of volumes 46 and 61 of the KSTS series.
