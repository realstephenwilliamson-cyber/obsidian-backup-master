# Psalm 114

← [[BCP Psalm 113]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 115]] →

*In exitu Israel* · Book Five · Twenty-third Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 114]]

**114:1** Hallelujah!  
When Israel came out of Egypt, \*  
the house of Jacob from a people of strange speech, ^114-1

**114:2** Judah became God's sanctuary \*  
and Israel his dominion. ^114-2

[p. 757] ^pg757

**114:3** The sea beheld it and fled; \*  
Jordan turned and went back. ^114-3

**114:4** The mountains skipped like rams, \*  
and the little hills like young sheep. ^114-4

**114:5** What ailed you, O sea, that you fled? \*  
O Jordan, that you turned back? ^114-5

**114:6** You mountains, that you skipped like rams? \*  
you little hills like young sheep? ^114-6

**114:7** Tremble, O earth, at the presence of the Lord, \*  
at the presence of the God of Jacob, ^114-7

**114:8** Who turned the hard rock into a pool of water \*  
and flint-stone into a flowing spring. ^114-8
