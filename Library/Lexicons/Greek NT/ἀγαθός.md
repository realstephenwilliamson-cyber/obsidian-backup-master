# ἀγαθός

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · adjective

**Dodson:** good — intrinsically good, good in nature, good whether it be seen to be so or not, the widest and most colorless of all words with this meaning. (Strong's 18; headword ἀγαθός, ή, όν)
