## Mūla

पदं च तत्समापत्ति पदस्थं योगिनो विदुः ।
वेद्यसत्तां बहिर्भूतामनपेक्ष्यैव सर्वतः ॥ ^mula

padaṃ ca tatsamāpatti padasthaṃ yogino viduḥ |
vedyasattāṃ bahirbhūtāmanapekṣyaiva sarvataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 14 - Chapter Ten, part 5#^pg477|p. 458]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=477>))
>
> Here also, as before, (this state has three names,) of which the first is the (common) daily one (laukika), namely, dreaming. A dream (svāpa) is considered to be the absence of the perception of things that are normally considered to be external. The ‘Place’ (pada) is (where) the entire Cosmic Path (is located). It is the vital breath, (or) the conception (saṁkalpa) which is (inner) understanding (avagama). (The corresponding) contemplative state (samāpatti) is known to yogis as ‘Abiding in Place’ (padastha). (with 10.253cd–255ab)
>
> Dreaming is (a type of conceptual) cognitive consciousness (jñāna) which, with respect to the object of knowledge, is creatively free. Independent in every way of external objectivity (vedyasattā), (for the men of knowledge,) it abides as the state (termed) ‘Pervasion’ (vyāpti). (with 10.255cd–256ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* एवं ज्ञानिविषयत्वेनापि संज्ञान्तरं योजयति --

> वेद्यसत्तां बहिर्भूतामनपेक्ष्यैव सर्वतः ॥
> वेद्ये स्वातन्त्र्यभाग् ज्ञानं स्वप्नं व्याप्तितया भजेत् ।

इह खलु बहीरूपतात्मपारतन्त्र्यमपहाय सर्वतः स्वात्ममात्रोल्लिखित एवार्थे स्वतन्त्रं संयोजनवियोजनकारि वैकल्पिकं ज्ञानमेव स्वप्नं ज्ञानिषु व्याप्तितया भजेत्तथा व्यवहरेत् -- इत्यर्थः ॥ २५५ ॥
