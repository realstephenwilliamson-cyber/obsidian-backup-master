# Psalm 77 (NRSVue)

## God’s mighty deeds recalled

*To the leader: according to Jeduthun. Of Asaph. A Psalm.*

**77:1** I cry aloud to God, aloud to God, that he may hear me. ^77-1

**77:2** In the day of my trouble I seek the Lord; in the night my hand is stretched out without wearying; my soul refuses to be comforted. ^77-2

**77:3** I think of God, and I moan; I meditate, and my spirit faints. *Selah* ^77-3

**77:4** You keep my eyelids from closing; I am so troubled that I cannot speak. ^77-4

**77:5** I consider the days of old and remember the years of long ago. ^77-5

**77:6** I commune^[Gk Syr: Heb *My music*] with my heart in the night; I meditate and search my spirit:^[Syr Jerome: Heb *my spirit searches*] ^77-6

**77:7** “Will the Lord spurn forever and never again be favorable? ^77-7

**77:8** Has his steadfast love ceased forever? Are his promises at an end for all time? ^77-8

**77:9** Has God forgotten to be gracious? Has he in anger shut up his compassion?” *Selah* ^77-9

**77:10** And I say, “It is my grief that the right hand of the Most High has changed.” ^77-10

**77:11** I will call to mind the deeds of the LORD; I will remember your wonders of old. ^77-11

**77:12** I will meditate on all your work and muse on your mighty deeds. ^77-12

**77:13** Your way, O God, is holy. What god is so great as our God? ^77-13

**77:14** You are the God who works wonders; you have displayed your might among the peoples. ^77-14

**77:15** With your strong arm you redeemed your people, the descendants of Jacob and Joseph. *Selah* ^77-15

**77:16** When the waters saw you, O God, when the waters saw you, they were afraid; the very deep trembled. ^77-16

**77:17** The clouds poured out water; the skies thundered; your arrows flashed on every side. ^77-17

**77:18** The crash of your thunder was in the whirlwind; your lightnings lit up the world; the earth trembled and shook. ^77-18

**77:19** Your way was through the sea, your path through the mighty waters, yet your footprints were unseen. ^77-19

**77:20** You led your people like a flock by the hand of Moses and Aaron. ^77-20
