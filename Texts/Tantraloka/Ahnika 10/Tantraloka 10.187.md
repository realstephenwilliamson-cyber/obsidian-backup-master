## Mūla

भेदोऽयं पाञ्चदश्यादिर्यथा श्रीशंभुरादिशत् ।
समस्तेऽर्थेऽत्र निर्ग्राह्ये तुटयः षोडश क्षणाः ॥ ^mula

bhedo'yaṃ pāñcadaśyādiryathā śrīśaṃbhurādiśat |
samaste'rthe'tra nirgrāhye tuṭayaḥ ṣoḍaśa kṣaṇāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 12 - Chapter Ten, part 3#^pg426|p. 407]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=426>))
>
> We will now discuss how this division into fifteen etc. is found in the group of principles present within the energy of the vital breath, as taught by the venerable Śambhunātha. (with 10.186cd–187ab)
>
> Sixteen time-units of the breath (tuṭi) (are as many) moments (kṣaṇa) (of perception, discernible) here in all that is perceivable. Each (one) is conceived to occupy two and a quarter finger space within the movement (of the breath), which is thirty-six fingers (long). (with 10.187cd–188ab)
