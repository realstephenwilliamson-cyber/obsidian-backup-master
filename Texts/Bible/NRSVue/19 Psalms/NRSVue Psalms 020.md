# Psalm 20 (NRSVue)

## Prayer for victory

*To the leader. A Psalm of David.*

**20:1** The LORD answer you in the day of trouble! The name of the God of Jacob protect you! ^20-1

**20:2** May he send you help from the sanctuary and give you support from Zion. ^20-2

**20:3** May he remember all your offerings and regard with favor your burnt sacrifices. *Selah* ^20-3

**20:4** May he grant you your heart’s desire and fulfill all your plans. ^20-4

**20:5** May we shout for joy over your victory and in the name of our God set up our banners. May the LORD fulfill all your petitions. ^20-5

**20:6** Now I know that the LORD will help his anointed; he will answer him from his holy heaven with mighty victories by his right hand. ^20-6

**20:7** Some take pride in chariots and some in horses, but our pride is in the name of the LORD our God. ^20-7

**20:8** They will collapse and fall, but we shall rise and stand upright. ^20-8

**20:9** Give victory to the king, O LORD; answer us when we call.^[Gk: Heb *give victory, OLORD; let the King answer us when we call*] ^20-9
