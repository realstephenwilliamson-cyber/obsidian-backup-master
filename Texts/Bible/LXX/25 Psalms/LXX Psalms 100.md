# LXX Psalms 100

← [[LXX Psalms 99]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 101]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 101]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 101]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**100:1** A Psalm of David. I will sing to thee, O Lord, of mercy and judgment; I will sing a psalm,  ^100-1

**100:2** and I will be wise in a blameless way. When wilt thou come to me? I walked in the innocence of my heart, in the midst of my house.  ^100-2

**100:3** I have not set before mine eyes any unlawful thing; I have hated transgressors.  ^100-3

**100:4** A perverse heart has not cleaved to me; I have not known an evil man, forasmuch as he turns away from me.  ^100-4

**100:5** Him that privily speaks against his neighbour, him have I driven from *me*: he that is proud in look and insatiable in heart,—with him I have not eaten.  ^100-5

**100:6** Mine eyes *shall be* upon the faithful of the land, that they may dwell with me: he that walked in a perfect way, the same ministered to me.  ^100-6

**100:7** The proud doer dwelt not in the midst of my house; the unjust speaker prospered not in my sight.  ^100-7

**100:8** Early did I slay all the sinners of the land, that I might destroy out of the city of the Lord all that work iniquity.  ^100-8
