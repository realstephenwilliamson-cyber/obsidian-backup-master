## Chapter 9

**9:1** A pronouncement: The word of the LORD.  
He will reside in the land of Hadrach and Damascus;  
For all men’s eyes will turn to the LORD—  
Like all the tribes of Israel— ^9-1

**9:2** Including Hamath, which borders on it,[^1]  
And Tyre and Sidon, though they are very wise. ^9-2

**9:3** Tyre has built herself a fortress;  
She has amassed silver like dust,  
And gold like the mud in the streets. ^9-3

**9:4** But my Lord will impoverish her;  
He will defeat her forces at sea,  
And she herself shall be consumed by fire. ^9-4

**9:5** Ashkelon shall see it and be frightened,  
Gaza shall tremble violently,  
And Ekron, at the collapse of her hopes.  
Kingship shall vanish from Gaza,  
Ashkelon shall be without inhabitants, ^9-5

**9:6** And a mongrel people[^2] shall settle in Ashdod.  
I will uproot the grandeur of Philistia. ^9-6

**9:7** But I will clean out the blood from its mouth,  
And the detestable things from between its teeth.  
Its survivors, too, shall belong to our God:  
They shall become like a clan in Judah,  
And Ekron shall be like the Jebusites. ^9-7

**9:8** And I will encamp in My House against armies,[^3]  
Against any that come and go,  
And no oppressor shall ever overrun them again;  
For I have now taken note with My own eyes.[^4] ^9-8

**9:9** Rejoice greatly, Fair Zion;  
Raise a shout, Fair Jerusalem!  
Lo, your king is coming to you.  
He is victorious, triumphant,  
Yet humble, riding on an ass,  
On a donkey foaled by a she-ass. ^9-9

**9:10** He[^5] shall banish chariots from Ephraim  
And horses from Jerusalem;  
The warrior’s bow shall be banished.  
He shall call on the nations to surrender,[^6]  
And his rule shall extend from sea to sea  
And from ocean to land’s end. ^9-10

**9:11** [^7]You, for your part, have released[^8]  
Your prisoners from the dry pit,[^9]  
For the sake of the blood of your covenant, ^9-11

**9:12** [Saying], “Return to Bizzaron,[^10]  
You prisoners of hope.”  
In return [I] announce this day:  
I will repay you double. ^9-12

**9:13** For I have drawn Judah taut,  
And applied [My hand] to Ephraim as to a bow,  
And I will arouse your sons, O Zion,  
Against your sons, O Javan,  
And make you like a warrior’s sword. ^9-13

**9:14** And the LORD will manifest Himself to them,[^11]  
And His arrows shall flash like lightning;  
My Lord GOD shall sound the ram’s horn  
And advance in a stormy tempest.[^12] ^9-14

**9:15** The LORD of Hosts will protect them:  
[^13][His] slingstones shall devour and conquer;  
They shall drink, shall rage as with[^14] wine,  
And be filled [with it] like a dashing bowl,  
Like the corners of an altar. ^9-15

**9:16** The LORD their God shall prosper them  
On that day;  
[He shall pasture] His people like sheep.  
[They shall be] like crown jewels glittering on His soil. ^9-16

**9:17** How lovely, how beautiful they shall be,  
Producing young men like new grain,  
Young women like new wine! ^9-17

[^1]: I.e., on the land of Hadrach and Damascus.
[^2]: Heb. mamzer; cf. note at Deut. 23.3.
[^3]: Change of vocalization yields “as a garrison.”
[^4]: Emendation yields “of their suffering”; cf. 1 Sam. 1.11.
[^5]: Heb. “I.”
[^6]: Cf. Deut. 20.10–12 and note.
[^7]: Exact meaning and connection of vv. 11–12 uncertain.
[^8]: Taking shillaḥti as a second-person singular feminine form, with Septuagint; cf. Judg. 5.7 with note.
[^9]: I.e., a pit that serves as a dungeon rather than a cistern (both are called bor in Heb.).
[^10]: Perhaps a nickname (“fortress”) for Samaria (Heb. Shomeron).
[^11]: I.e., Judah.
[^12]: Lit. “tempests of wind”; for teman in the sense of wind, cf. Job 9.9; 39.26.
[^13]: The meaning of much of the rest of the chapter is uncertain.
[^14]: Some Septuagint mss. read “drink blood like.”
