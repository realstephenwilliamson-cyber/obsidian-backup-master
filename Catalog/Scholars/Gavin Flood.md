# Gavin Flood

Professor Emeritus of Hindu Studies and Comparative Religion in Oxford's Faculty of Theology and Religion, Senior Research Fellow at Campion Hall and Piramal Dean of Academic Affairs at the Oxford Centre for Hindu Studies; he works on medieval Śaiva tantric texts (with an edition and translation of the Netra Tantra in progress), comparative religion and phenomenology ([Campion Hall](https://www.campion.ox.ac.uk/people/professor-gavin-flood-fba); [Faculty of Theology and Religion](https://www.theology.ox.ac.uk/node/3011661)).
