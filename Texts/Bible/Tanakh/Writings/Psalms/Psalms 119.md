## Chapter 119

**119:1** Happy are those whose way is blameless,  
who follow the teaching of the LORD. ^119-1

**119:2** Happy are those who observe His decrees,  
who turn to Him wholeheartedly. ^119-2

**119:3** They have done no wrong,  
but have followed His ways. ^119-3

**119:4** You have commanded that Your precepts  
be kept diligently. ^119-4

**119:5** Would that my ways were firm  
in keeping Your laws; ^119-5

**119:6** then I would not be ashamed  
when I regard all Your commandments. ^119-6

**119:7** I will praise You with a sincere heart  
as I learn Your just rules. ^119-7

**119:8** I will keep Your laws;  
do not utterly forsake me. ^119-8

**119:9** How can a young man keep his way pure?—  
by holding to Your word. ^119-9

**119:10** I have turned to You with all my heart;  
do not let me stray from Your commandments. ^119-10

**119:11** In my heart I treasure Your promise;  
therefore I do not sin against You. ^119-11

**119:12** Blessed are You, O LORD;  
train me in Your laws. ^119-12

**119:13** With my lips I rehearse  
all the rules You proclaimed. ^119-13

**119:14** I rejoice over the way of Your decrees  
as over all riches. ^119-14

**119:15** I study Your precepts;  
I regard Your ways; ^119-15

**119:16** I take delight in Your laws;  
I will not neglect Your word. ^119-16

**119:17** Deal kindly with Your servant,  
that I may live to keep Your word. ^119-17

**119:18** Open my eyes, that I may perceive  
the wonders of Your teaching. ^119-18

**119:19** I am only a sojourner in the land;  
do not hide Your commandments from me. ^119-19

**119:20** My soul is consumed with longing  
for Your rules at all times. ^119-20

**119:21** You blast the accursed insolent ones  
who stray from Your commandments. ^119-21

**119:22** Take away from me taunt and abuse,  
because I observe Your decrees. ^119-22

**119:23** Though princes meet and speak against me,  
Your servant studies Your laws. ^119-23

**119:24** For Your decrees are my delight,  
my intimate companions. ^119-24

**119:25** My soul clings to the dust;  
revive me in accordance with Your word. ^119-25

**119:26** I have declared my way, and You have answered me;  
train me in Your laws. ^119-26

**119:27** Make me understand the way of Your precepts,  
that I may study Your wondrous acts. ^119-27

**119:28** I am racked with grief;  
sustain me in accordance with Your word. ^119-28

**119:29** Remove all false ways from me;  
favor me with Your teaching. ^119-29

**119:30** I have chosen the way of faithfulness;  
I have set Your rules before me. ^119-30

**119:31** I cling to Your decrees;  
O LORD, do not put me to shame. ^119-31

**119:32** I eagerly pursue Your commandments,  
for You broaden my understanding. ^119-32

**119:33** Teach me, O LORD, the way of Your laws;  
I will observe them to the utmost.[^1] ^119-33

**119:34** Give me understanding, that I may observe Your teaching  
and keep it wholeheartedly. ^119-34

**119:35** Lead me in the path of Your commandments,  
for that is my concern. ^119-35

**119:36** Turn my heart to Your decrees  
and not to love of gain. ^119-36

**119:37** Avert my eyes from seeing falsehood;  
by Your ways preserve me. ^119-37

**119:38** Fulfill Your promise to Your servant,  
which is for those who worship You. ^119-38

**119:39** Remove the taunt that I dread,  
for Your rules are good. ^119-39

**119:40** See, I have longed for Your precepts;  
by Your righteousness preserve me. ^119-40

**119:41** May Your steadfast love reach me, O LORD,  
Your deliverance, as You have promised. ^119-41

**119:42** I shall have an answer for those who taunt me,  
for I have put my trust in Your word. ^119-42

**119:43** Do not utterly take the truth away from my mouth,  
for I have put my hope in Your rules. ^119-43

**119:44** I will always obey Your teaching,  
forever and ever. ^119-44

**119:45** I will walk about at ease,  
for I have turned to Your precepts. ^119-45

**119:46** I will speak of Your decrees,  
and not be ashamed in the presence of kings. ^119-46

**119:47** I will delight in Your commandments,  
which I love. ^119-47

**119:48** I reach out for Your commandments, which I love;  
I study Your laws. ^119-48

**119:49** Remember Your word to Your servant  
through which You have given me hope. ^119-49

**119:50** This is my comfort in my affliction,  
that Your promise has preserved me. ^119-50

**119:51** Though the arrogant have cruelly mocked me,  
I have not swerved from Your teaching. ^119-51

**119:52** I remember Your rules of old, O LORD,  
and find comfort in them. ^119-52

**119:53** I am seized with rage  
because of the wicked who forsake Your teaching. ^119-53

**119:54** Your laws are a source of strength to me[^2]  
wherever I may dwell. ^119-54

**119:55** I remember Your name at night, O LORD,  
and obey Your teaching. ^119-55

**119:56** This has been my lot,  
for I have observed Your precepts. ^119-56

**119:57** The LORD is my portion;  
I have resolved to keep Your words. ^119-57

**119:58** I have implored You with all my heart;  
have mercy on me, in accordance with Your promise. ^119-58

**119:59** I have considered my ways,  
and have turned back to Your decrees. ^119-59

**119:60** I have hurried and not delayed  
to keep Your commandments. ^119-60

**119:61** Though the bonds of the wicked are coiled round me,  
I have not neglected Your teaching. ^119-61

**119:62** I arise at midnight to praise You  
for Your just rules. ^119-62

**119:63** I am a companion to all who fear You,  
to those who keep Your precepts. ^119-63

**119:64** Your steadfast love, O LORD, fills the earth;  
teach me Your laws. ^119-64

**119:65** You have treated Your servant well,  
according to Your word, O LORD. ^119-65

**119:66** Teach me good sense and knowledge,  
for I have put my trust in Your commandments. ^119-66

**119:67** Before I was humbled I went astray,  
but now I keep Your word. ^119-67

**119:68** You are good and beneficent;  
teach me Your laws. ^119-68

**119:69** Though the arrogant have accused me falsely,  
I observe Your precepts wholeheartedly. ^119-69

**119:70** Their minds are thick like fat;  
as for me, Your teaching is my delight. ^119-70

**119:71** It was good for me that I was humbled,  
so that I might learn Your laws. ^119-71

**119:72** I prefer the teaching You proclaimed  
to thousands of gold and silver pieces. ^119-72

**119:73** Your hands made me and fashioned me;  
give me understanding that I may learn Your commandments. ^119-73

**119:74** Those who fear You will see me and rejoice,  
for I have put my hope in Your word. ^119-74

**119:75** I know, O LORD, that Your rulings are just;  
rightly have You humbled me. ^119-75

**119:76** May Your steadfast love comfort me  
in accordance with Your promise to Your servant. ^119-76

**119:77** May Your mercy reach me, that I might live,  
for Your teaching is my delight. ^119-77

**119:78** Let the insolent be dismayed, for they have wronged me without cause;  
I will study Your precepts. ^119-78

**119:79** May those who fear You,  
those who know Your decrees,  
turn again to me. ^119-79

**119:80** May I wholeheartedly follow Your laws  
so that I do not come to grief. ^119-80

**119:81** I long for Your deliverance;  
I hope for Your word. ^119-81

**119:82** My eyes pine away for Your promise;  
I say, “When will You comfort me?” ^119-82

**119:83** Though I have become like a water-skin dried in smoke,  
I have not neglected Your laws. ^119-83

**119:84** How long has Your servant to live?  
when will You bring my persecutors to judgment? ^119-84

**119:85** The insolent have dug pits for me,  
flouting Your teaching. ^119-85

**119:86** All Your commandments are enduring;  
I am persecuted without cause; help me! ^119-86

**119:87** Though they almost wiped me off the earth,  
I did not abandon Your precepts. ^119-87

**119:88** As befits Your steadfast love, preserve me,  
so that I may keep the decree You proclaimed. ^119-88

**119:89** The LORD exists forever;  
Your word stands firm in heaven. ^119-89

**119:90** Your faithfulness is for all generations;  
You have established the earth, and it stands. ^119-90

**119:91** They stand this day to [carry out] Your rulings,  
for all are Your servants. ^119-91

**119:92** Were not Your teaching my delight  
I would have perished in my affliction. ^119-92

**119:93** I will never neglect Your precepts,  
for You have preserved my life through them. ^119-93

**119:94** I am Yours; save me!  
For I have turned to Your precepts. ^119-94

**119:95** The wicked hope to destroy me,  
but I ponder Your decrees. ^119-95

**119:96** I have seen that all things have their limit,  
but Your commandment is broad beyond measure. ^119-96

**119:97** O how I love Your teaching!  
It is my study all day long. ^119-97

**119:98** Your commandments make me wiser than my enemies;  
they always stand by me. ^119-98

**119:99** I have gained more insight than all my teachers,  
for Your decrees are my study. ^119-99

**119:100** I have gained more understanding than my elders,  
for I observe Your precepts. ^119-100

**119:101** I have avoided every evil way  
so that I may keep Your word. ^119-101

**119:102** I have not departed from Your rules,  
for You have instructed me. ^119-102

**119:103** How pleasing is Your word to my palate,  
sweeter than honey. ^119-103

**119:104** I ponder Your precepts;  
therefore I hate every false way. ^119-104

**119:105** Your word is a lamp to my feet,  
a light for my path. ^119-105

**119:106** I have firmly sworn  
to keep Your just rules. ^119-106

**119:107** I am very much afflicted;  
O LORD, preserve me in accordance with Your word. ^119-107

**119:108** Accept, O LORD, my freewill offerings;  
teach me Your rules. ^119-108

**119:109** Though my life is always in danger,  
I do not neglect Your teaching. ^119-109

**119:110** Though the wicked have set a trap for me,  
I have not strayed from Your precepts. ^119-110

**119:111** Your decrees are my eternal heritage;  
they are my heart’s delight. ^119-111

**119:112** I am resolved to follow Your laws  
to the utmost[^3] forever. ^119-112

**119:113** I hate men of divided heart,  
but I love Your teaching. ^119-113

**119:114** You are my protection and my shield;  
I hope for Your word. ^119-114

**119:115** Keep away from me, you evildoers,  
that I may observe the commandments of my God. ^119-115

**119:116** Support me as You promised, so that I may live;  
do not thwart my expectation. ^119-116

**119:117** Sustain me that I may be saved,  
and I will always muse upon Your laws. ^119-117

**119:118** You reject all who stray from Your laws,  
for they are false and deceitful. ^119-118

**119:119** You do away with the wicked as if they were dross;  
rightly do I love Your decrees. ^119-119

**119:120** My flesh creeps from fear of You;  
I am in awe of Your rulings. ^119-120

**119:121** I have done what is just and right;  
do not abandon me to those who would wrong me. ^119-121

**119:122** Guarantee Your servant’s well-being;  
do not let the arrogant wrong me. ^119-122

**119:123** My eyes pine away for Your deliverance,  
for Your promise of victory. ^119-123

**119:124** Deal with Your servant as befits Your steadfast love;  
teach me Your laws. ^119-124

**119:125** I am Your servant;  
give me understanding,  
that I might know Your decrees. ^119-125

**119:126** It is a time to act for the LORD,  
for they have violated Your teaching. ^119-126

**119:127** Rightly do I love Your commandments  
more than gold, even fine gold. ^119-127

**119:128** Truly by all [Your] precepts I walk straight;[^4]  
I hate every false way. ^119-128

**119:129** Your decrees are wondrous;  
rightly do I observe them. ^119-129

**119:130** The words You inscribed give[^5] light,  
and grant understanding to the simple. ^119-130

**119:131** I open my mouth wide, I pant,  
longing for Your commandments. ^119-131

**119:132** Turn to me and be gracious to me,  
as is Your rule with those who love Your name. ^119-132

**119:133** Make my feet firm through Your promise;  
do not let iniquity dominate me. ^119-133

**119:134** Redeem me from being wronged by man,  
that I may keep Your precepts. ^119-134

**119:135** Show favor to Your servant,  
and teach me Your laws. ^119-135

**119:136** My eyes shed streams of water  
because men do not obey Your teaching. ^119-136

**119:137** You are righteous, O LORD;  
Your rulings are just. ^119-137

**119:138** You have ordained righteous decrees;  
they are firmly enduring. ^119-138

**119:139** I am consumed with rage  
over my foes’ neglect of Your words. ^119-139

**119:140** Your word is exceedingly pure,  
and Your servant loves it. ^119-140

**119:141** Though I am belittled and despised,  
I have not neglected Your precepts. ^119-141

**119:142** Your righteousness is eternal;  
Your teaching is true. ^119-142

**119:143** Though anguish and distress come upon me,  
Your commandments are my delight. ^119-143

**119:144** Your righteous decrees are eternal;  
give me understanding, that I might live. ^119-144

**119:145** I call with all my heart;  
answer me, O LORD,  
that I may observe Your laws. ^119-145

**119:146** I call upon You; save me,  
that I may keep Your decrees. ^119-146

**119:147** I rise before dawn and cry for help;  
I hope for Your word. ^119-147

**119:148** My eyes greet each watch of the night,  
as I meditate on Your promise. ^119-148

**119:149** Hear my voice as befits Your steadfast love;  
O LORD, preserve me, as is Your rule. ^119-149

**119:150** Those who pursue intrigue draw near;  
they are far from Your teaching. ^119-150

**119:151** You, O LORD, are near,  
and all Your commandments are true. ^119-151

**119:152** I know from Your decrees of old  
that You have established them forever. ^119-152

**119:153** See my affliction and rescue me,  
for I have not neglected Your teaching. ^119-153

**119:154** Champion my cause and redeem me;  
preserve me according to Your promise. ^119-154

**119:155** Deliverance is far from the wicked,  
for they have not turned to Your laws. ^119-155

**119:156** Your mercies are great, O LORD;  
as is Your rule, preserve me. ^119-156

**119:157** Many are my persecutors and foes;  
I have not swerved from Your decrees. ^119-157

**119:158** I have seen traitors and loathed[^6] them,  
because they did not keep Your word in mind. ^119-158

**119:159** See that I have loved Your precepts;  
O LORD, preserve me, as befits Your steadfast love. ^119-159

**119:160** Truth is the essence of Your word;  
Your just rules are eternal. ^119-160

**119:161** Princes have persecuted me without reason;  
my heart thrills at Your word. ^119-161

**119:162** I rejoice over Your promise  
as one who obtains great spoil. ^119-162

**119:163** I hate and abhor falsehood;  
I love Your teaching. ^119-163

**119:164** I praise You seven times each day  
for Your just rules. ^119-164

**119:165** Those who love Your teaching enjoy well-being;  
they encounter no adversity. ^119-165

**119:166** I hope for Your deliverance, O LORD;  
I observe Your commandments. ^119-166

**119:167** I obey Your decrees  
and love them greatly. ^119-167

**119:168** I obey Your precepts and decrees;  
all my ways are before You. ^119-168

**119:169** May my plea reach You, O LORD;  
grant me understanding according to Your word. ^119-169

**119:170** May my petition come before You;  
save me in accordance with Your promise. ^119-170

**119:171** My lips shall pour forth praise,  
for You teach me Your laws. ^119-171

**119:172** My tongue shall declare Your promise,  
for all Your commandments are just. ^119-172

**119:173** Lend Your hand to help me,  
for I have chosen Your precepts. ^119-173

**119:174** I have longed for Your deliverance, O LORD;  
Your teaching is my delight. ^119-174

**119:175** Let me live, that I may praise You;  
may Your rules be my help; ^119-175

**119:176** I have strayed like a lost sheep;  
search for Your servant,  
for I have not neglected Your commandments. ^119-176

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Or “songs for me.”
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^4]: Or “I declare all [Your] precepts to be just.”
[^5]: With Targum; or “The exposition of Your words gives”; meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^6]: Or “have contended with.”
