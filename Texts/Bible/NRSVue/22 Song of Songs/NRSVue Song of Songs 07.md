# Song of Songs 7 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Song of Songs 06|← Song of Songs 6]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Song of Songs 08|Song of Songs 8 →]]

## Expressions of praise

**7:1** How graceful are your feet in sandals, O queenly maiden! Your rounded thighs are like jewels, the work of a master hand. ^7-1

**7:2** Your navel is a rounded bowl; may it never lack mixed wine. Your belly is a heap of wheat, encircled with lilies. ^7-2

**7:3** Your two breasts are like two fawns, twins of a gazelle. ^7-3

**7:4** Your neck is like an ivory tower. Your eyes are pools in Heshbon, by the gate of Bath-rabbim. Your nose is like a tower of Lebanon, overlooking Damascus. ^7-4

**7:5** Your head crowns you like Carmel, and your flowing locks are like purple; a king is held captive in the tresses. ^7-5

**7:6** How fair and pleasant you are, O loved one, delectable maiden!^[Syr: Heb *in delights*] ^7-6

**7:7** You are stately^[Heb *This your stature is*] as a palm tree, and your breasts are like its clusters. ^7-7

**7:8** I said, “I will climb the palm tree and lay hold of its branches.” O may your breasts be like clusters of the vine, and the scent of your breath like apples, ^7-8

**7:9** and your kisses^[Heb *palate*] like the best wine that goes down^[Heb *to my lover*] smoothly, gliding over lips and teeth.^[Gk Syr Vg: Heb *lips of sleepers*] ^7-9

**7:10** I am my beloved’s, and his desire is for me. ^7-10

**7:11** Come, my beloved, let us go forth into the fields and lodge in the villages; ^7-11

**7:12** let us go out early to the vineyards; let us see whether the vines have budded, whether the grape blossoms have opened and the pomegranates are in bloom. There I will give you my love. ^7-12

**7:13** The mandrakes give forth fragrance, and over our doors are all choice fruits, new as well as old, which I have laid up for you, O my beloved. ^7-13
