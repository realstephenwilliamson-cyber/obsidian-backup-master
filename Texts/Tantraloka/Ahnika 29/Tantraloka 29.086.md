## Mūla

हस्ते च युग्मके चैव जपः सप्तविधः स्मृतः ।
नेत्रे गमागमे कर्णावास्यं गुह्यं च गुह्यकम् ॥ ^mula

haste ca yugmake caiva japaḥ saptavidhaḥ smṛtaḥ |
netre gamāgame karṇāvāsyaṃ guhyaṃ ca guhyakam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 10 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 2#^pg295|p. 278]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=295>))
>
> O fair lady, the repetition of Mantra which is of seven kinds is said to be (the one that takes place) 1) within (the sense of sight in) the eye, 2) in the going and coming (of the breath), 3) within the (sense of hearing in) the ear, 4) within Haṁsa, and 5) in the mouth, so too 6) in the hand, and 7) in the couple (of Śiva and Śakti). (with 29.85cd–86ab)
>
> O fair lady, the repetition of eleven hundred thousand (takes place) 1) in the eye, 2-3) the going and coming (of the breath), 4-5) in the ears, 6) the face, 7) the genitals, 8) in what is associated with the genitals, 9) in the hundred spokes, 10) in the one located in the centre, and 11) in the thousand rays. Here also the oblations (into the fire number) a tenth (of the number of repetitions). (with 29.86cd–87)
