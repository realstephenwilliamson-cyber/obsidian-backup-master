# Job 16 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Job 15|← Job 15]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Job 17|Job 17 →]]

## Job reaffirms his innocence

**16:1** Then Job answered: ^16-1

**16:2** “I have heard many such things; miserable comforters are you all. ^16-2

**16:3** Have windy words no limit? Or what provokes you that you keep on talking? ^16-3

**16:4** I also could talk as you do, if you were in my place; I could join words together against you and shake my head at you. ^16-4

**16:5** I could encourage you with my mouth, and the solace of my lips would assuage your pain. ^16-5

**16:6** “If I speak, my pain is not assuaged, and if I refrain, how much of it leaves me? ^16-6

**16:7** Surely now God has worn me out; he has^[Heb *you have*] made desolate all my company. ^16-7

**16:8** And he has^[Heb *you have*] shriveled me up, which is a witness against me; my leanness has risen up against me, and it testifies to my face. ^16-8

**16:9** He has torn me in his wrath and hated me; he has gnashed his teeth at me; my adversary sharpens his eyes against me. ^16-9

**16:10** They have gaped at me with their mouths; they have struck me insolently on the cheek; they mass themselves together against me. ^16-10

**16:11** God gives me up to the evil and casts me into the hands of the wicked. ^16-11

**16:12** I was at ease, and he broke me in two; he seized me by the neck and dashed me to pieces; he set me up as his target; ^16-12

**16:13** his archers surround me. He slashes open my kidneys and shows no mercy; he pours out my gall on the ground. ^16-13

**16:14** He bursts upon me again and again; he rushes at me like a warrior. ^16-14

**16:15** I have sewed sackcloth upon my skin and have laid my strength in the dust. ^16-15

**16:16** My face is red with weeping, and deep darkness is on my eyelids, ^16-16

**16:17** though there is no violence in my hands, and my prayer is pure. ^16-17

**16:18** “O earth, do not cover my blood; let my outcry find no resting place. ^16-18

**16:19** Even now my witness is in heaven, and my advocate is on high. ^16-19

**16:20** My friends scorn me; my eye pours out tears to God, ^16-20

**16:21** that he would maintain the right of a mortal with God, as^[Syr Vg Tg: Heb *and*] one does for a neighbor. ^16-21

**16:22** For when a few years have come, I shall go the way from which I shall not return. ^16-22
