# J.N. Mohanty

Jitendra Nath Mohanty (born 1928 in Orissa, died 2023), emeritus professor of philosophy at Temple University; his work was in logic, epistemology and phenomenology, drawing on classical Indian and modern Western philosophy, especially Husserl; he was founding editor of *Husserl Studies* ([Temple University memorial](https://liberalarts.temple.edu/news/2023/03/memoriam-remembering-jitendra-nath-mohanty-1928-2023)). The page gives no exact date of death.
