# 1 Corinthians 2 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB 1 Corinthians 01|← 1 Corinthians 1]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB 1 Corinthians 03|1 Corinthians 3 →]]

Compare: [[1 Corinthians 02|BSB and SBLGNT]]

**2:1** And I, brethren, when I came to you, came not in loftiness of speech or of wisdom, declaring unto you the testimony of Christ. ^2-1

**2:2** For I judged not myself to know anything among you, but Jesus Christ, and him crucified. ^2-2

**2:3** And I was with you in weakness, and in fear, and in much trembling. ^2-3

**2:4** And my speech and my preaching was not in the persuasive words of human wisdom, but in shewing of the Spirit and power; ^2-4

**2:5** That your faith might not stand on the wisdom of men, but on the power of God. ^2-5

**2:6** Howbeit we speak wisdom among the perfect: yet not the wisdom of this world, neither of the princes of this world that come to nought; ^2-6

**2:7** But we speak the wisdom of God in a mystery, a wisdom which is hidden, which God ordained before the world, unto our glory: ^2-7

**2:8** Which none of the princes of this world knew; for if they had known it, they would never have crucified the Lord of glory. ^2-8

**2:9** But, as it is written: That eye hath not seen, nor ear heard, neither hath it entered into the heart of man, what things God hath prepared for them that love him. ^2-9

**2:10** But to us God hath revealed them, by this Spirit. For the Spirit searcheth all things, yea, the deep things of God. ^2-10

**2:11** For what man knoweth the things of a man, but the spirit of a man that is in him? So the things also that are of God no man knoweth, but the Spirit of God. ^2-11

**2:12** Now we have received not the spirit of this world, but the Spirit that is of God; that we may know the things that are given us from God. ^2-12

**2:13** Which things also we speak, not in the learned words of human wisdom; but in the doctrine of the Spirit, comparing spiritual things with spiritual. ^2-13

**2:14** But the sensual man perceiveth not these things that are of the Spirit of God; for it is foolishness to him, and he cannot understand, because it is spiritually examined. ^2-14

**2:15** But the spiritual man judgeth all things; and he himself is judged of no man. ^2-15

**2:16** For who hath known the mind of the Lord, that we may instruct him? But we have the mind of Christ. ^2-16
