# Lamentations 5

Compare: [[NRSVue Lamentations 05|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 5

**5:1** Remember, O LORD, what has befallen us;  
Behold, and see our disgrace! ^5-1

**5:2** Our heritage has passed to aliens,  
Our homes to strangers. ^5-2

**5:3** We have become orphans, fatherless;  
Our mothers are like widows. ^5-3

**5:4** We must pay to drink our own water,  
Obtain our own kindling at a price. ^5-4

**5:5** We are hotly[^1] pursued;  
Exhausted, we are given no rest. ^5-5

**5:6** We hold out a hand to Egypt;  
To Assyria, for our fill of bread. ^5-6

**5:7** Our fathers sinned and are no more;  
And we must bear their guilt. ^5-7

**5:8** Slaves are ruling over us,  
With none to rescue us from them. ^5-8

**5:9** We get our bread at the peril of our lives,  
Because of the sword of the wilderness.[^2] ^5-9

**5:10** Our skin glows like an oven,  
With the fever of famine. ^5-10

**5:11** They[^3] have ravished women in Zion,  
Maidens in the towns of Judah. ^5-11

**5:12** Princes have been hanged by them;[^4]  
No respect has been shown to elders. ^5-12

**5:13** Young men must carry millstones,  
And youths stagger under loads of wood. ^5-13

**5:14** The old men are gone from the gate,  
The young men from their music. ^5-14

**5:15** Gone is the joy of our hearts;  
Our dancing is turned into mourning. ^5-15

**5:16** The crown has fallen from our head;  
Woe to us that we have sinned! ^5-16

**5:17** Because of this our hearts are sick,  
Because of these our eyes are dimmed: ^5-17

**5:18** Because of Mount Zion, which lies desolate;  
Jackals prowl over it. ^5-18

**5:19** But You, O LORD, are enthroned forever,  
Your throne endures through the ages. ^5-19

**5:20** Why have You forgotten us utterly,  
Forsaken us for all time? ^5-20

**5:21** Take us back, O LORD, to Yourself,  
And let us come back;  
Renew our days as of old! ^5-21

**5:22** For truly, You have rejected us,  
Bitterly raged against us.  
TAKE US BACK, O LORD, TO YOURSELF,  
AND LET US COME BACK;  
RENEW OUR DAYS AS OF OLD! ^5-22

[^1]: Lit. “on our neck”; meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Or “heat (cf. Deut. 28.22) of the wilderness”; meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: I.e., the slaves of v. 8.
[^4]: I.e., the slaves of v. 8.
