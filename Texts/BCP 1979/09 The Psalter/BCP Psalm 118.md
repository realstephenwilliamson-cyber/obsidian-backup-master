# Psalm 118

← [[BCP Psalm 117]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 119]] →

*Confitemini Domino* · Book Five · Twenty-fourth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 118]]

**118:1** Give thanks to the LORD, for he is good; \*  
his mercy endures for ever. ^118-1

**118:2** Let Israel now proclaim, \*  
"His mercy endures for ever." ^118-2

[p. 761]

**118:3** Let the house of Aaron now proclaim, \*  
"His mercy endures for ever." ^118-3

**118:4** Let those who fear the LORD now proclaim, \*  
"His mercy endures for ever." ^118-4

**118:5** I called to the LORD in my distress; \*  
the LORD answered by setting me free. ^118-5

**118:6** The LORD is at my side, therefore I will not fear; \*  
what can anyone do to me? ^118-6

**118:7** The LORD is at my side to help me; \*  
I will triumph over those who hate me. ^118-7

**118:8** It is better to rely on the LORD \*  
than to put any trust in flesh. ^118-8

**118:9** It is better to rely on the LORD \*  
than to put any trust in rulers. ^118-9

**118:10** All the ungodly encompass me; \*  
in the Name of the LORD I will repel them. ^118-10

**118:11** They hem me in, they hem me in on every side; \*  
in the name of the LORD I will repel them. ^118-11

**118:12** They swarm about me like bees;  
they blaze like a fire of thorns; \*  
in the name of the LORD I will repel them. ^118-12

**118:13** I was pressed so hard that I almost fell, \*  
but the LORD came to my help. ^118-13

**118:14** The LORD is my strength and my song, \*  
and he has become my salvation. ^118-14

**118:15** There is a sound of exultation and victory \*  
in the tents of the righteous: ^118-15

[p. 762]

**118:16** "The right hand of the LORD has triumphed! \*  
the right hand of the LORD is exalted!  
the right hand of the LORD has triumphed!" ^118-16

**118:17** I shall not die, but live, \*  
and declare the works of the LORD. ^118-17

**118:18** The LORD has punished me sorely, \*  
but he did not hand me over to death. ^118-18

**118:19** Open for me the gates of righteousness; \*  
I will enter them;  
I will offer thanks to the LORD. ^118-19

**118:20** "This is the gate of the LORD; \*  
he who is righteous may enter." ^118-20

**118:21** I will give thanks to you, for you answered me \*  
and have become my salvation. ^118-21

**118:22** The same stone which the builders rejected \*  
has become the chief cornerstone. ^118-22

**118:23** This is the LORD'S doing, \*  
and it is marvelous in our eyes. ^118-23

**118:24** On this day the LORD has acted; \*  
we will rejoice and be glad in it. ^118-24

**118:25** Hosanna, LORD, hosanna! \*  
LORD, send us now success. ^118-25

**118:26** Blessed is he who comes in the name of the Lord; \*  
we bless you from the house of the LORD. ^118-26

**118:27** God is the LORD; he has shined upon us; \*  
form a procession with branches up to the horns of the altar. ^118-27

[p. 763]

**118:28** "You are my God, and I will thank you; \*  
you are my God, and I will exalt you." ^118-28

**118:29** Give thanks to the LORD, for he is good; \*  
his mercy endures for ever. ^118-29
