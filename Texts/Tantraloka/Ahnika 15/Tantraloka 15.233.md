## Mūla

अंगुष्ठाग्रात्कालवह्निज्वालाभास्वरमुत्थितम् ।
अस्त्रं ध्यात्वा तच्छिखाभिर्बहिरन्तर्दहेत्तनुम् ॥ ^mula

aṃguṣṭhāgrātkālavahnijvālābhāsvaramutthitam |
astraṃ dhyātvā tacchikhābhirbahirantardahettanum || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 05 - Chapter Fifteen, part 4#^pg159|p. 141]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=159>))
>
> Having visualized (dhyātvā) that the Weapon, shining with the flames of the Fire of Time, has risen up from the tip of (his left) toe, he should burn the body inwardly and externally with its flames.
