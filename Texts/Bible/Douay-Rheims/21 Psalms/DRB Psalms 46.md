# Psalms 46 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 45|← Psalms 45]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 47|Psalms 47 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 46|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**46:1** Unto the end, for the sons of Core. ^46-1

**46:2** O clap your hands, all ye nations: shout unto God with the voice of Joy, ^46-2

**46:3** For the Lord is high, terrible: a great king over all the earth. ^46-3

**46:4** He hath subdued the people under us; and the nations under our feet. ^46-4

**46:5** He hath chosen for us his inheritance the beauty of Jacob which he hath loved. ^46-5

**46:6** God is ascended with jubilee, and the Lord with the sound of trumpet. ^46-6

**46:7** Sing praises to our God, sing ye: sing praises to our king, sing ye. ^46-7

**46:8** For God is the king of all the earth: sing ye wisely. ^46-8

**46:9** God shall reign over the nations: God sitteth on his holy throne. ^46-9

**46:10** The princes of the people are gathered together, with the God of Abraham: for the strong gods of the earth are exceedingly exalted. ^46-10
