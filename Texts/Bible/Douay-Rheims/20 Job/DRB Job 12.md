# Job 12 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Job 11|← Job 11]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Job 13|Job 13 →]]

**12:1** When Job answered, and said: ^12-1

**12:2** Are you then men alone, and shall wisdom die with you? ^12-2

**12:3** I also have a heart as well as you: for who is ignorant of these things, which you know? ^12-3

**12:4** He that is mocked by his friends as I, shall call upon God and he will hear him: for the simplicity of the just man is laughed to scorn. ^12-4

**12:5** The lamp despised in the thoughts of the rich, is ready for the time appointed. ^12-5

**12:6** The tabernacles of robbers abound, and they provoke God boldly; whereas it is he that hath given all into their hands: ^12-6

**12:7** But ask now the beasts, and they shall teach thee: and the birds of the air, and they shall tell thee. ^12-7

**12:8** Speak to the earth, and it shall answer thee: and the fishes of the sea shall tell. ^12-8

**12:9** Who is ignorant that the hand of the Lord hath made all these things? ^12-9

**12:10** In whose hand is the soul of every living thing, and the spirit of all flesh of man. ^12-10

**12:11** Doth not the ear discern words, and the palate of him that eateth, the taste? ^12-11

**12:12** In the ancient is wisdom, and in length of days prudence. ^12-12

**12:13** With him is wisdom and strength, he hath counsel and understanding. ^12-13

**12:14** If he pull down, there is no man that can build up: if he shut up a. man, there is none that can open. ^12-14

**12:15** If he withhold the waters, all things shall be dried up: and if he send them out, they shall overturn the earth. ^12-15

**12:16** With him is strength and wisdom: he knoweth both the deceiver, and him that is deceived. ^12-16

**12:17** He bringeth counsellors to a foolish end, and judges to insensibility. ^12-17

**12:18** He looseth the belt of kings, and girdeth their loins with a cord. ^12-18

**12:19** He leadeth away priests without glory, and overthroweth nobles. ^12-19

**12:20** He changeth the speech of the true speakers, and taketh away the doctrine of the aged. ^12-20

**12:21** He poureth contempt upon princes, and relieveth them that were oppressed. ^12-21

**12:22** He discovereth deep things out of darkness, and bringeth up to light the shadow of death. ^12-22

**12:23** He multiplieth nations, and destroyeth them, and restoreth them again after they were overthrown. ^12-23

**12:24** He changeth the heart of the princes of the people of the earth, and deceiveth them that they walk in vain where there is no way. ^12-24

**12:25** They shall grope as in the dark, and not in the light, and he shall make them stagger like men that are drunk. ^12-25
