## Chapter 113

**113:1** Hallelujah.  
O servants of the LORD, give praise;  
praise the name of the LORD. ^113-1

**113:2** Let the name of the LORD be blessed  
now and forever. ^113-2

**113:3** From east to west  
the name of the LORD is praised. ^113-3

**113:4** The LORD is exalted above all nations;  
His glory is above the heavens. ^113-4

**113:5** Who is like the LORD our God,  
who, enthroned on high, ^113-5

**113:6** sees what is below,  
in heaven and on earth? ^113-6

**113:7** He raises the poor from the dust,  
lifts up the needy from the refuse heap ^113-7

**113:8** to set them with the great,  
with the great men of His people. ^113-8

**113:9** He sets the childless woman among her household  
as a happy mother of children.  
Hallelujah. ^113-9
