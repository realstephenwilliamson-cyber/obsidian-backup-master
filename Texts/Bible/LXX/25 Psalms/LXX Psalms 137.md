# LXX Psalms 137

← [[LXX Psalms 136]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 138]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 138]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 138]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**137:1** A Psalm for David, of Aggæus and Zacharias. I will give thee thanks, O Lord, with my whole heart; and I will sing psalms to thee before the angels; for thou hast heard all the words of my mouth.  ^137-1

**137:2** I will worship toward thy holy temple, and give thanks to thy name, on account of thy mercy and thy truth; for thou hast magnified thy holy name above every thing.  ^137-2

**137:3** In whatsoever day I shall call upon thee, hear me speedily; thou shalt abundantly provide me with thy power in my soul.  ^137-3

**137:4** Let all the kings of the earth, O Lord, give thanks unto thee; for they have heard all the words of thy mouth.  ^137-4

**137:5** And let them sing in the ways of the Lord; for great is the glory of the Lord.  ^137-5

**137:6** For the Lord is high, and *yet* regards the lowly; and he knows high things from afar off.  ^137-6

**137:7** Though I should walk in the midst of affliction, thou wilt quicken me; thou hast stretched forth thine hands against the wrath of mine enemies, and thy right hand has saved me.  ^137-7

**137:8** O Lord, thou shalt recompense *them* on my behalf: thy mercy, O Lord, *endures* for ever: overlook not the works of thine hands.  ^137-8
