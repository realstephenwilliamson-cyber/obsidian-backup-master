## Mūla

तादृग्रूपनिरूढ्यर्थं मनोवाक्कायवर्त्मना ।
यद्यत्समाचरेद्वीरः कुलयागः स स स्मृतः ॥ ^mula

tādṛgrūpanirūḍhyarthaṃ manovākkāyavartmanā |
yadyatsamācaredvīraḥ kulayāgaḥ sa sa smṛtaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 09 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 1#^pg222|p. 205]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=222>))
>
> Whatever a hero (that is, a Kaula initiate,) does by way of (his) mind, speech or body in order to firmly establish (within himself a profound and constant awareness) of such a reality is said to be the Kaula sacrifice.
