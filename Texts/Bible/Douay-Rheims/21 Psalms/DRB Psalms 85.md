# Psalms 85 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 84|← Psalms 84]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 86|Psalms 86 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 85|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**85:1** A prayer for David himself. Incline thy ear, O Lord, and hear me: for I am needy and poor. ^85-1

**85:2** Preserve my soul, for I am holy: save thy servant, O my God, that trusteth in thee. ^85-2

**85:3** Have mercy on me, O Lord, for I have cried to thee all the day. ^85-3

**85:4** Give joy to the soul of thy servant, for to thee, O Lord, I have lifted up my soul. ^85-4

**85:5** For thou, O Lord, art sweet and mild: and plenteous in mercy to all that call upon thee. ^85-5

**85:6** Give ear, O Lord, to my prayer: and attend to the voice of my petition. ^85-6

**85:7** I have called upon thee in the day of my trouble: because thou hast heard me. ^85-7

**85:8** There is none among the gods like unto thee, O Lord: and there is none according to thy works. ^85-8

**85:9** All the nations thou hast made shall come and adore before thee, O Lord: and they shall glorify thy name. ^85-9

**85:10** For thou art great and dost wonderful things: thou art God alone. ^85-10

**85:11** Conduct me, O Lord, in thy way, and I will walk in thy truth: let my heart rejoice that it may fear thy name. ^85-11

**85:12** I will praise thee, O Lord my God: with my whole heart, and I will glorify thy name for ever: ^85-12

**85:13** For thy mercy is great towards me: and thou hast delivered my soul out of the lower hell. ^85-13

**85:14** O God, the wicked are risen up against me, and the assembly of the mighty have sought my soul: and they have not set thee before their eyes. ^85-14

**85:15** And thou, O Lord, art a God of compassion, and merciful, patient, and of much mercy, and true. ^85-15

**85:16** O look upon me, and have mercy on me: give thy command to thy servant, and save the son of thy handmaid. ^85-16

**85:17** Shew me a token for good: that they who hate me may see, and be confounded, because thou, O Lord, hast helped me and hast comforted me. ^85-17
