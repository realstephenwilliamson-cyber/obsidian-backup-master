# John 12 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB John 11|← John 11]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB John 13|John 13 →]]

Compare: [[John 12|BSB and SBLGNT]]

**12:1** Jesus therefore, six days before the pasch, came to Bethania, where Lazarus had been dead, whom Jesus raised to life. ^12-1

**12:2** And they made him a supper there: and Martha served: but Lazarus was one of them that were at table with him. ^12-2

**12:3** Mary therefore took a pound of ointment of right spikenard, of great price, and anointed the feet of Jesus, and wiped his feet with her hair; and the house was filled with the odour of the ointment. ^12-3

**12:4** Then one of his disciples, Judas Iscariot, he that was about to betray him, said: ^12-4

**12:5** Why was not this ointment sold for three hundred pence, and given to the poor? ^12-5

**12:6** Now he said this, not because he cared for the poor; but because he was a thief, and having the purse, carried the things that were put therein. ^12-6

**12:7** Jesus therefore said: Let her alone, that she may keep it against the day of my burial. ^12-7

**12:8** For the poor you have always with you; but me you have not always. ^12-8

**12:9** A great multitude therefore of the Jews knew that he was there; and they came, not for Jesus’ sake only, but that they might see Lazarus, whom he had raised from the dead. ^12-9

**12:10** But the chief priests thought to kill Lazarus also: ^12-10

**12:11** Because many of the Jews, by reason of him, went away, and believed in Jesus. ^12-11

**12:12** And on the next day, a great multitude that was to come to the festival day, when they had heard that Jesus was coming to Jerusalem, ^12-12

**12:13** Took branches of palm trees, and went forth to meet him, and cried: Hosanna, blessed is he that cometh in the name of the Lord, the king of Israel. ^12-13

**12:14** And Jesus found a young ass, and sat upon it, as it is written: ^12-14

**12:15** Fear not, daughter of Sion: behold, thy king cometh, sitting on an ass’s colt. ^12-15

**12:16** These things his disciples did not know at the first; but when Jesus was glorified, then they remembered that these things were written of him, and that they had done these things to him. ^12-16

**12:17** The multitude therefore gave testimony, which was with him, when he called Lazarus out of the grave, and raised him from the dead. ^12-17

**12:18** For which reason also the people came to meet him, because they heard that he had done this miracle. ^12-18

**12:19** The Pharisees therefore said among themselves: Do you see that we prevail nothing? behold, the whole world is gone after him. ^12-19

**12:20** Now there were certain Gentiles among them, who came up to adore on the festival day. ^12-20

**12:21** These therefore came to Philip, who was of Bethsaida of Galilee, and desired him, saying: Sir, we would see Jesus. ^12-21

**12:22** Philip cometh, and telleth Andrew. Again Andrew and Philip told Jesus. ^12-22

**12:23** But Jesus answered them, saying: The hour is come, that the Son of man should be glorified. ^12-23

**12:24** Amen, amen I say to you, unless the grain of wheat falling into the ground die, ^12-24

**12:25** Itself remaineth alone. But if it die, it bringeth forth much fruit. He that loveth his life shall lose it; and he that hateth his life in this world, keepeth it unto life eternal. ^12-25

**12:26** If any man minister to me, let him follow me; and where I am, there also shall my minister be. If any man minister to me, him will my Father honour. ^12-26

**12:27** Now is my soul troubled. And what shall I say? Father, save me from this hour. But for this cause I came unto this hour. ^12-27

**12:28** Father, glorify thy name. A voice therefore came from heaven: I have both glorified it, and will glorify it again. ^12-28

**12:29** The multitude therefore that stood and heard, said that it thundered. Others said: An angel spoke to him. ^12-29

**12:30** Jesus answered, and said: This voice came not because of me, but for your sakes. ^12-30

**12:31** Now is the judgment of the world: now shall the prince of this world be cast out. ^12-31

**12:32** And I, if I be lifted up from the earth, will draw all things to myself. ^12-32

**12:33** (Now this he said, signifying what death he should die.) ^12-33

**12:34** The multitude answered him: We have heard out of the law, that Christ abideth for ever; and how sayest thou: The Son of man must be lifted up? Who is this Son of man? ^12-34

**12:35** Jesus therefore said to them: Yet a little while, the light is among you. Walk whilst you have the light, that the darkness overtake you not. And he that walketh in darkness, knoweth not whither he goeth. ^12-35

**12:36** Whilst you have the light, believe in the light, that you may be the children of light. These things Jesus spoke; and he went away, and hid himself from them. ^12-36

**12:37** And whereas he had done so many miracles before them, they believed not in him: ^12-37

**12:38** That the saying of Isaias the prophet might be fulfilled, which he said: Lord, who hath believed our hearing? and to whom hath the arm of the Lord been revealed? ^12-38

**12:39** Therefore they could not believe, because Isaias said again: ^12-39

**12:40** He hath blinded their eyes, and hardened their heart, that they should not see with their eyes, nor understand with their heart, and be converted, and I should heal them. ^12-40

**12:41** These things said Isaias, when he saw his glory, and spoke of him. ^12-41

**12:42** However, many of the chief men also believed in him; but because of the Pharisees they did not confess him, that they might not be cast out of the synagogue. ^12-42

**12:43** For they loved the glory of men more than the glory of God. ^12-43

**12:44** But Jesus cried, and said: He that believeth in me, doth not believe in me, but in him that sent me. ^12-44

**12:45** And he that seeth me, seeth him that sent me. ^12-45

**12:46** I am come a light into the world; that whosoever believeth in me, may not remain in darkness. ^12-46

**12:47** And if any man hear my words, and keep them not, I do not judge him: for I came not to judge the world, but to save the world. ^12-47

**12:48** He that despiseth me, and receiveth not my words, hath one that judgeth him; the word that I have spoken, the same shall judge him in the last day. ^12-48

**12:49** For I have not spoken of myself; but the Father who sent me, he gave me commandment what I should say, and what I should speak. ^12-49

**12:50** And I know that his commandment is life everlasting. The things therefore that I speak, even as the Father said unto me, so do I speak. ^12-50
