# Toḍalatantra — Patala 4

[[Todalatantra 03|← Patala 3]] · [[Todalatantra|← index]] · [[Todalatantra 05|Patala 5 →]]

devī uvāca

^s1

śrutā pūjā kālikāyāstārāyā vada sāmpratam

^s2

yasyāḥ prasaṅgamātreṇa vācaś cittāyate nṛṇām

^s3

īśvara uvāca

^s4

śṛṇu cārvaṅgi subhage tārāyāḥ pūjanaṃ mahat

^s5

mantreṇācamanaṃ kṛtvā gurudevaṃ namettataḥ

^s6

jalaṃ saṃśodhya hastau ca pādau ca kṣālayettataḥ

^s7

mantreṇācamanaṃ kṛtvā tataḥ pīṭhaṃ vicintayet

^s8

śikhāṃ baddhvā tato devi trividhaṃ vighnanāśanam

^s9

bhūmyāsanaṃ ca saṃśodhya vastre granthiṃ vidhāya ca

^s10

kāyavākśodhanaṃ kṛtvā tataḥ puṣpaviśodhanam

^s11

yantraṃ kṛtvā sādhakendraḥ sāmānyārghyaṃ ca vinyaset

^s12

dvārapālāṃśca saṃpūjya pīṭhapūjāṃ samārabhet

^s13

pīṭhaśaktīśca lakṣmyādyāstataḥ pīṭhamanuṃ japet

^s14

bhūtaśuddhiṃ pravakṣyāmi viśeṣamiha yadbhavet

^s15

kūrcayuktena haṃsena pūrakeṇa sureśvari

^s16

kuṇḍalyā saha cātmānaṃ caturviṃśatibījakam

^s17

tatra līnāni deveśi paramātmani sādhakaḥ

^s18

māyayā saṃdahet pāpaṃ puruṣaṃ kajjalaprabham

^s19

kumbhakena varārohe bhasma kuryād vicakṣaṇaḥ

^s20

vadhūbījena deveśi bhasmanā saha recayet

^s21

pūrakeṇa tu kūrcena lalāṭe'mṛtasaṃcayam

^s22

cintayet parameśāni kumbhakenāmṛtāmbudhim

^s23

āṃ hrīṃ kroṃ vahnibījāntaṃ hṛdi caikādaśaṃ japet

^s24

tatastu cintayed dhīmān oṃkārād raktapaṅkajam

^s25

tasyopari punardhyāyet huṃkāraṃ nīlasaṃnibham

^s26

tasyopari punar dhyāyed bījabhūṣitakartṛkām

^s27

tasyopari paraṃ binduṃ śivarūpaṃ ca avyayam

^s28

vālukāyāḥ sahasraikabhāgaṃ binduṃ sudurlabham

^s29

bindumadhye maṇidvīpaṃ śatayojanavistṛtam

^s30

bindumadhye paraṃ jyotirbudbudākāramaṇḍalam

^s31

yathaiva budbude devi pratibimbaṃ prapaśyati

^s32

tathā nirīkṣaṇaṃ kāryaṃ prakuryājjñānacakṣuṣā

^s33

maṇidvīpaṃ tu tanmadhye suvarṇavālukāmayam

^s34

parito bhāvayenmantrī parikhāto manoharam

^s35

madhye kalpadrumaṃ dhyāyedātmānaṃ tāriṇīmayam

^s36

udyadādityasaṃkāśaṃ jyotirmaṇḍalamuttamam

^s37

caturdvārasamāyuktaṃ hemaprākārabhūṣitam

^s38

bahucāmaraghaṇṭādidevakanyāsuśobhitam

^s39

mandavāyusamāyuktaṃ gandhadhūpair alaṃkṛtam

^s40

tanmadhye vedikāṃ dhyāyennānāratnopaśobhitām

^s41

suvarṇasūtraracitaṃ cintayecchattram uttamam

^s42

tadadhaścintayenmantrī ratnasiṃhāsanaṃ priye

^s43

tatra devīṃ cintayecca yathoktadhyānayogataḥ

^s44

yogasāre yathoktaistu upacāraiḥ prapūjayet

^s45

prāṇāyāmaṃ tataḥ kṛtvā ṛṣyādinyāsamācaret

^s46

varṇanyāsaṃ tataḥ kṛtvā karāṅganyāsamācaret

^s47

tālatrayaṃ choṭikābhir daśadigbandhanaṃ caret

^s48

ṣoḍhānyāsaṃ tataḥ kṛtvā vyāpakaṃ tadanantaram

^s49

viśeṣārghyaṃ ca saṃsthāpya pañcatattvaṃ viśodhayet

^s50

sudhādevīṃ samānīya pañcīkaraṇamācaret

^s51

kumbhe puṣpaṃ samādāya trikoṇe triḥ prapūjayet

^s52

vāmadehaṃ tridhā coktvā khecarīṃ daśadhā japet

^s53

tathā cānandagāyatrīm ṛcaṃ ca trir japet sudhīḥ

^s54

brahmaśāpaṃ śukraśāpaṃ kṛṣṇaśāpaṃ sureśvari

^s55

digjapānnāśayeddhīmān tato mantratrayaṃ japet

^s56

churikāṃ cāmṛtaṃ caiva prītisaṃrakṣaṇīṃ tathā

^s57

pāvamānaṃ ca trir japtvā śoṣaṇādīn samācaret

^s58

vāruṇaṃ trir japeddevi cāmṛtaṃ saptadhā japet

^s59

vidyātattve dhenumudrāṃ pradarśya mūlamaṣṭadhā

^s60

kuṇḍalinīṃ samutthāpya śivo'haṃ bhāvayettataḥ

^s61

māṃsaṃ mīnaṃ śodhayitvā mudrāśodhanamācaret

^s62

śaktiṃ ca kulapuṣpaṃ ca śodhayet sādhakottamaḥ

^s63

devān pitṝn ṛṣīṃścaiva tarpayediṣṭadevatām

^s64

śodhitaṃ dravyamādāya viśeṣārghye vinikṣipet

^s65

tato brahmamayaṃ dhyātvā pūjādhyānaṃ samācaret

^s66

nāsārandhrāt samānīya pīṭhe puṣpaṃ nidhāya ca

^s67

tataścāvāhanaṃ kṛtvā pañcamudrāḥ pradarśayet

^s68

ṣaḍaṅgena ca sampūjya punarmudrāṃ pradarśayet

^s69

jīvanyāsaṃ tataḥ kṛtvā upacāraiḥ prapūjayet

^s70

ṣaḍaṅgāni ca sampūjya akṣobhyaṃ pūjayedadhaḥ

^s71

gurupaṅktiṃ pūjayitvā daśamūleṣu pūjayet

^s72

mahāpūrvāṃś ca kālyādīn yajedvairocanādikān

^s73

punardevīṃ prapūjyātha baliṃ dadyādvicakṣaṇaḥ

^s74

prāṇāyāmaṃ tataḥ kṛtvā mantradhyānaṃ samācaret

^s75

japaṃ kṛtvā maheśāni devyā haste samarpayet

^s76

prāṇāyāmaṃ punaḥ kṛtvā tattvasvīkāramācaret

^s77

tasmai dattvā svayaṃ nītvā prajapet sādhakāgraṇīḥ

^s78

pītvā pītvā japitvā ca muktaḥ koṭijanaiḥ saha

^s79

pratipātre japenmantramaṣṭottaraśataṃ sudhīḥ

^s80

stutiṃ ca kavacaṃ smṛtvā cāṣṭāṅgaṃ praṇamet sudhīḥ

^s81

viśeṣārghyaṃ pradātavyam ātmānaṃ ca samarpayet

^s82

rudrarūpī svayaṃ bhūtvā saṃhāreṇa visarjayet

^s83

yonimudrāṃ tato baddhvā kṣamasveti visarjayet

^s84

aṅganyāsaṃ maheśāni prāṇāyāmaṃ tataḥ param

^s85

aiśānyāṃ maṇḍalaṃ kṛtvā nirmālyena prapūjayet

^s86

nirmālyavāsinīṃ ṅe'ntāṃ caṇḍeśvaryai namo namaḥ

^s87

nirmālyaṃ dhārayet śīrṣe candanaṃ ca lalāṭake

^s88

gurusthāne likhed yantraṃ vicared bhairavo yathā

^s89

saṃkṣepapūjanaṃ devi mānasaṃ tattvavarjitam

^s90

atroktamācaredatra nānyat saṃcārayet sudhīḥ

^s91

anyatsaṃcāraṇāddevi kruddhā bhavati tāriṇī

^s92

tattvajñānaṃ ca mānaṃ ca śaktimātre hi pārvati

^s93

ityetat kathitaṃ devi kimanyat śrotumicchasi

^s94
