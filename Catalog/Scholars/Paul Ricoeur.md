# Paul Ricœur

A French philosopher of hermeneutics and phenomenology who held chairs at Strasbourg, the Sorbonne (1956) and Paris-Nanterre (1965), and from 1970 succeeded Paul Tillich as John Nuveen Professor of Philosophical Theology at the University of Chicago, teaching there part of each year until 1992 ([Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/ricoeur/)).
