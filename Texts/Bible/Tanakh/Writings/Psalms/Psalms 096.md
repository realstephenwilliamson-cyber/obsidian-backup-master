## Chapter 96

**96:1** [^1]Sing to the LORD a new song,  
sing to the LORD, all the earth. ^96-1

**96:2** Sing to the LORD, bless His name,  
proclaim His victory day after day. ^96-2

**96:3** Tell of His glory among the nations,  
His wondrous deeds, among all peoples. ^96-3

**96:4** For the LORD is great and much acclaimed,  
He is held in awe by all divine beings. ^96-4

**96:5** All the gods of the peoples are mere idols,  
but the LORD made the heavens. ^96-5

**96:6** Glory and majesty are before Him;  
strength and splendor are in His temple. ^96-6

**96:7** Ascribe to the LORD, O families of the peoples,  
ascribe to the LORD glory and strength. ^96-7

**96:8** Ascribe to the LORD the glory of His name,  
bring tribute and enter His courts. ^96-8

**96:9** Bow down to the LORD majestic in holiness;  
tremble in His presence, all the earth! ^96-9

**96:10** Declare among the nations, “The LORD is king!”  
the world stands firm; it cannot be shaken;  
He judges the peoples with equity. ^96-10

**96:11** Let the heavens rejoice and the earth exult;  
let the sea and all within it thunder, ^96-11

**96:12** the fields and everything in them exult;  
then shall all the trees of the forest shout for joy ^96-12

**96:13** at the presence of the LORD, for He is coming,  
for He is coming to rule the earth;  
He will rule the world justly,  
and its peoples in faithfulness. ^96-13

[^1]: Cf. 1 Chron. 16.23–33.
