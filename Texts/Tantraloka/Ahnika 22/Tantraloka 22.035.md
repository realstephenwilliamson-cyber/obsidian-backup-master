## Mūla

ततोऽस्य शुद्धिं प्राक्कृत्वा ततो दीक्षां समाचरेत् ।
अधोदर्शनसंस्थेन गुरुणा दीक्षितः पुरा ॥ ^mula

tato'sya śuddhiṃ prākkṛtvā tato dīkṣāṃ samācaret |
adhodarśanasaṃsthena guruṇā dīkṣitaḥ purā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 13 - Chapter Twenty-Two#^pg308|p. 288]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=308>))
>
> (The reason for this) is because he has repeated a Mantra devoid of vitality, that has come from the mouth of an ignorant teacher. Moreover, (that) teacher has no authority (to teach and impart Mantras), due to the aforementioned defect. Thus, (a true teacher) may initiate (him again only after) having first purified him. (with 22.34–35ab)
>
> One who has been previously initiated by a teacher who belongs to an inferior school of thought (darśana) may subsequently go to a good teacher, thanks to an intense descent of the power (of grace). Then, if (that happens), once that true teacher has thus purified his (new) disciple, he should (without misgivings) perform all the rites of initiation and the rest, in accord with the stated procedure. (with 22.35cd–37ab)
