# Psalm 69 (NRSVue)

## Prayer for deliverance from persecution

*To the leader: according to Lilies. Of David.*

**69:1** Save me, O God, for the waters have come up to my neck. ^69-1

**69:2** I sink in deep mire, where there is no foothold; I have come into deep waters, and the flood sweeps over me. ^69-2

**69:3** I am weary with my crying; my throat is parched. My eyes grow dim with waiting for my God. ^69-3

**69:4** More in number than the hairs of my head are those who hate me without cause; many are those who would destroy me, my enemies who accuse me falsely. What I did not steal, must I now restore? ^69-4

**69:5** O God, you know my folly; the wrongs I have done are not hidden from you. ^69-5

**69:6** Do not let those who hope in you be put to shame because of me, O Lord GOD of hosts; do not let those who seek you be dishonored because of me, O God of Israel. ^69-6

**69:7** It is for your sake that I have borne reproach, that shame has covered my face. ^69-7

**69:8** I have become a stranger to my kindred, an alien to my mother’s children. ^69-8

**69:9** It is zeal for your house that has consumed me; the insults of those who insult you have fallen on me. ^69-9

**69:10** When I humbled my soul with fasting,^[Gk Syr: Heb *I wept, with fasting my soul*, or *I made my soul mourn with fasting*] they insulted me for doing so. ^69-10

**69:11** When I made sackcloth my clothing, I became a byword to them. ^69-11

**69:12** I am the subject of gossip for those who sit in the gate, and the drunkards make songs about me. ^69-12

**69:13** But as for me, my prayer is to you, O LORD. At an acceptable time, O God, in the abundance of your steadfast love, answer me. With your faithful help ^69-13

**69:14** rescue me from sinking in the mire; let me be delivered from my enemies and from the deep waters. ^69-14

**69:15** Do not let the flood sweep over me or the deep swallow me up or the Pit close its mouth over me. ^69-15

**69:16** Answer me, O LORD, for your steadfast love is good; according to your abundant mercy, turn to me. ^69-16

**69:17** Do not hide your face from your servant, for I am in distress—make haste to answer me. ^69-17

**69:18** Draw near to me; redeem me; set me free because of my enemies. ^69-18

**69:19** You know the insults I receive and my shame and dishonor; my foes are all known to you. ^69-19

**69:20** Insults have broken my heart, so that I am in despair. I looked for pity, but there was none; and for comforters, but I found none. ^69-20

**69:21** They gave me poison for food, and for my thirst they gave me vinegar to drink. ^69-21

**69:22** Let their table be a trap for them, a snare for their allies. ^69-22

**69:23** Let their eyes be darkened so that they cannot see, and make their loins tremble continually. ^69-23

**69:24** Pour out your indignation upon them, and let your burning anger overtake them. ^69-24

**69:25** May their camp be a desolation; let no one live in their tents. ^69-25

**69:26** For they persecute those whom you have struck down, and those whom you have wounded they attack still more.^[Gk Syr: Heb *recount the pain of*] ^69-26

**69:27** Add guilt to their guilt; may they have no acquittal from you. ^69-27

**69:28** Let them be blotted out of the book of the living; let them not be enrolled among the righteous. ^69-28

**69:29** But I am lowly and in pain; let your salvation, O God, protect me. ^69-29

**69:30** I will praise the name of God with a song; I will magnify him with thanksgiving. ^69-30

**69:31** This will please the LORD more than an ox or a bull with horns and hoofs. ^69-31

**69:32** Let the oppressed see it and be glad; you who seek God, let your hearts revive. ^69-32

**69:33** For the LORD hears the needy and does not despise his own who are in bonds. ^69-33

**69:34** Let heaven and earth praise him, the seas and everything that moves in them. ^69-34

**69:35** For God will save Zion and rebuild the cities of Judah, and his servants shall live^[Syr: Heb *and they shall live*] there and possess it; ^69-35

**69:36** the children of his servants shall inherit it, and those who love his name shall live in it. ^69-36
