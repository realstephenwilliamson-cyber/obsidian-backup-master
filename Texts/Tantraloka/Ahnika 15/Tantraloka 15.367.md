## Mūla

बहिर्यागस्य मुख्यत्वे सिद्ध्यादिपरिकल्पिते ।
अन्तर्यागः संस्क्रियायै ह्यन्यथार्चयिता पशुः ॥ ^mula

bahiryāgasya mukhyatve siddhyādiparikalpite |
antaryāgaḥ saṃskriyāyai hyanyathārcayitā paśuḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 07 - Chapter Fifteen, part 6#^pg244|p. 226]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=244>))
>
> (Although) the external sacrifice is primarily conceived for (the attainment of) accomplishments and the like, (one should also perform) the inner sacrifice in order to purify (oneself). Otherwise, the worshipper (remains) a fettered soul. He who is averse to accomplishments and the like worships the Lord externally in order to (attain) firm stability in the great internal sacrifice, and by virtue of this (stability), attains his goal. (with 15.367–368)
