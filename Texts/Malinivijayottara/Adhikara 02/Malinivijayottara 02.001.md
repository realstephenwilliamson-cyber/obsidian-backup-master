## Mūla

अथैषामेव तत्त्वानाम्धरादीनामनुक्रमात् ।
प्रपञ्चः कथ्यते लेशाद्योगिनाम्योगसिद्धये ॥ ^mula

athaiṣām eva tattvānām dharādīnām anukramāt |
prapañcaḥ kathyate leśād yoginām yogasiddhaye || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, July 2022 – September 2022
> p. 28 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Malini (journal), July-September 2022.pdf#page=30>))
>
> **Concerning Pervasion**
>
> Now will be explained in brief the (unfolding) manifoldness (*prapañca*) of the principles (*tattva*) from Earth onwards, in order that yogis may attain success in Yoga.
