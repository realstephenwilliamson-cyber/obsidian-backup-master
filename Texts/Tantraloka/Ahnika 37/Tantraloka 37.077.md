## Mūla

यामग्रगे वयसि भर्तृवियोगदीना-
मन्वग्रहीत् त्रिनयनः स्वयमेव भक्त्या ।
भाविप्रभावरभसेषुजनेष्वनर्थः
सत्यं समाकृषति सोऽर्थपरम्पराणाम् ॥ ^mula

yāmagrage vayasi bhartṛviyogadīnā-
manvagrahīt trinayanaḥ svayameva bhaktyā |
bhāviprabhāvarabhaseṣujaneṣvanarthaḥ
satyaṃ samākṛṣati so'rthaparamparāṇām || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 11 - Chapter Thirty-Seven, part 1#^pg282|p. 264]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=282>))
>
> Saddened by the separation from her husband, who preceded (her in death) when he was (still) young, (Śiva) the Three Eyed One Himself (spontaneously) graced her by virtue of (her) devotion. In those who are zealous for future greatness, a misfortune drags with it a series of good fortunes.
