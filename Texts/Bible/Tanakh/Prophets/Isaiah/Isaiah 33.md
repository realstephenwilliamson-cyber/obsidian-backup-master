# Isaiah 33

## Chapter 33

**33:1** Ha, you ravager who are not ravaged,  
You betrayer who have not been betrayed!  
When you have done ravaging, you shall be ravaged;  
When you have finished betraying, you shall be betrayed. ^33-1

**33:2** O LORD, be gracious to us!  
It is to You we have looked;  
Be their arm[^1] every morning,  
Also our deliverance in time of stress. ^33-2

**33:3** At [Your] roaring, peoples have fled,  
Before Your majesty nations have scattered; ^33-3

**33:4** And spoil[^2] was gathered as locusts are gathered,  
It[^3] was amassed[^4] as grasshoppers are amassed.[^5] ^33-4

**33:5** The LORD is exalted,  
He dwells on high!  
[Of old] He filled Zion  
With justice and righteousness. ^33-5

**33:6** Faithfulness to Your charge[^6] was [her] wealth,  
Wisdom and devotion [her] triumph,  
Reverence for the LORD—that was her[^7] treasure. ^33-6

**33:7** Hark! The Arielites[^8] cry aloud;  
Shalom’s[^9] messengers weep bitterly. ^33-7

**33:8** Highways are desolate,  
Wayfarers have ceased.  
A covenant has been renounced,  
Cities[^10] rejected  
Mortal man[^11] despised. ^33-8

**33:9** The land is wilted and withered;  
Lebanon disgraced and moldering,  
Sharon is become like a desert,  
And Bashan and Carmel are stripped bare. ^33-9

**33:10** “Now I will arise,” says the LORD,  
“Now I will exalt Myself, now raise Myself high. ^33-10

**33:11** You shall conceive hay,  
Give birth to straw;  
My[^12] breath will devour you like fire. ^33-11

**33:12** Peoples shall be burnings of lime,[^13]  
Thorns cut down that are set on fire. ^33-12

**33:13** Hear, you who are far, what I have done;  
You who are near, note My might.” ^33-13

**33:14** Sinners in Zion are frightened,  
The godless are seized with trembling:  
“Who of us can dwell with the devouring fire:  
Who of us can dwell with the never-dying blaze?” ^33-14

**33:15** He who walks in righteousness,  
Speaks uprightly,  
Spurns profit from fraudulent dealings,  
Waves away a bribe instead of grasping it,  
Stops his ears against listening to infamy,  
Shuts his eyes against looking at evil— ^33-15

**33:16** Such a one shall dwell in lofty security,  
With inaccessible cliffs for his stronghold,  
With his food supplied  
And his drink assured. ^33-16

**33:17** When your eyes behold a king in his beauty,[^14]  
When they contemplate the land round about, ^33-17

**33:18** Your throat[^15] shall murmur in awe,  
“Where is one who could count? Where is one who could weigh?  
Where is one who could count [all these] towers?” ^33-18

**33:19** No more shall you see the barbarian folk,  
The people of speech too obscure to comprehend,  
So stammering of tongue that they are not understood. ^33-19

**33:20** When you gaze upon Zion, our city of assembly,  
Your eyes shall behold Jerusalem  
As a secure homestead,  
A tent not to be transported,  
Whose pegs shall never be pulled up,  
And none of whose ropes shall break. ^33-20

**33:21** For there the LORD in His greatness shall be for us  
Like a region of rivers, of broad streams,  
Where no floating vessels can sail  
And no mighty craft can travel—  
Their[^16] ropes are slack,  
They cannot steady the sockets of their masts,  
They cannot spread a sail.[^17] ^33-21

**33:22** For the LORD shall be our ruler,  
The LORD shall be our prince,  
The LORD shall be our king:  
He shall deliver us. ^33-22

**33:23** Then shall indeed much spoil be divided,[^18]  
Even the lame shall seize booty. ^33-23

**33:24** And none who lives there shall say, “I am sick”;  
It shall be inhabited by folk whose sin has been forgiven. ^33-24

[^1]: Emendation yields “You have been our help.”
[^2]: Heb. “your spoil.”
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain. Emendation yields “booty”; cf. v. 23.
[^4]: Taking ¾qq as a cognate of q¾¾.
[^5]: Apparently for food; cf. Lev. 11.22.
[^6]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^7]: Heb. “his.”
[^8]: So a few manuscripts; cf. 29.1.
[^9]: I.e., Jerusalem’s; cf. Salem (Heb. Shalem), Ps. 76.3.
[^10]: 1QIsa reads “A pact.”
[^11]: Emendation yields “an obligation.”
[^12]: Heb. “your.”
[^13]: Emendation yields “brambles”; cf. 32.13.
[^14]: Emendation yields “perfection of beauty”; cf. Ps. 50.2.
[^15]: As in 59.13 and elsewhere; others “heart.”
[^16]: Heb. “your.”
[^17]: Brought up from v. 23 for clarity. The passage means that the Lord will render Jerusalem as inaccessible to enemies as if it were surrounded by an impassable sea.
[^18]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain; emendation yields “even a blind man shall divide much spoil.”
