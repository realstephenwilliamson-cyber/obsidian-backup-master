## Chapter 24

**24:1** Why are times for judgment not reserved by Shaddai?  
Even those close to Him cannot foresee His actions.[^1] ^24-1

**24:2** People remove boundary-stones;  
They carry off flocks and pasture them; ^24-2

**24:3** They lead away the donkeys of the fatherless,  
And seize the widow’s bull as a pledge; ^24-3

**24:4** They chase the needy off the roads;  
All the poor of the land are forced into hiding. ^24-4

**24:5** Like the wild asses of the wilderness,  
They go about their tasks, seeking food;  
The wilderness provides each with food for his lads; ^24-5

**24:6** They harvest fodder in the field,  
And glean the late grapes in the vineyards of the wicked. ^24-6

**24:7** They pass the night naked for lack of clothing,  
They have no covering against the cold; ^24-7

**24:8** They are drenched by the mountain rains,  
And huddle against the rock for lack of shelter. ^24-8

**24:9** [^2]They snatch the fatherless infant from the breast,  
And seize the child of the poor as a pledge. ^24-9

**24:10** They go about naked for lack of clothing,  
And, hungry, carry sheaves; ^24-10

**24:11** Between rows [of olive trees] they make oil,  
And, thirsty, they tread the winepresses. ^24-11

**24:12** Men groan in the city;  
The souls of the dying cry out;  
Yet God does not regard it as a reproach. ^24-12

**24:13** They are rebels against the light;  
They are strangers to its ways,  
And do not stay in its path. ^24-13

**24:14** The murderer arises in the evening[^3]  
To kill the poor and needy,  
And at night he acts the thief. ^24-14

**24:15** The eyes of the adulterer watch for twilight,  
Thinking, “No one will glimpse me then.”  
He masks his face. ^24-15

**24:16** In the dark they break into houses;  
By day they shut themselves in;  
They do not know the light. ^24-16

**24:17** For all of them morning is darkness;  
It is then that they discern the terror of darkness. ^24-17

**24:18** [^4]May they be flotsam on the face of the water;  
May their portion in the land be cursed;  
May none turn aside by way of their vineyards. ^24-18

**24:19** May drought and heat snatch away their snow waters,  
And Sheol, those who have sinned. ^24-19

**24:20** May the womb forget him;  
May he be sweet to the worms;  
May he be no longer remembered;  
May wrongdoers be broken like a tree. ^24-20

**24:21** May he consort with a barren woman who bears no child,  
Leave his widow deprived of good. ^24-21

**24:22** Though he has the strength to seize bulls,  
May he live with no assurance of survival. ^24-22

**24:23** Yet [God] gives him the security on which he relies,  
And keeps watch over his affairs. ^24-23

**24:24** Exalted for a while, let them be gone;  
Be brought low, and shrivel like mallows,  
And wither like the heads of grain. ^24-24

**24:25** Surely no one can confute me,  
Or prove that I am wrong. ^24-25

[^1]: Lit. “days.”
[^2]: This verse belongs to the description of the wicked in vv. 2–4a.
[^3]: Cf. Mishnaic Heb. ‘or, Aramaic ‘orta, “evening”; others “with the light.”
[^4]: From here to the end of the chapter the translation is largely conjectural.
