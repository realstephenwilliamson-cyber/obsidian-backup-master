# Isaiah 44

## Chapter 44

**44:1** But hear, now, O Jacob My servant,  
Israel whom I have chosen! ^44-1

**44:2** Thus said the LORD, your Maker,  
Your Creator who has helped you since birth:  
Fear not, My servant Jacob,  
Jeshurun[^1] whom I have chosen, ^44-2

**44:3** Even as I pour water on thirsty soil,  
And rain upon dry ground,  
So will I pour My spirit on your offspring,  
My blessing upon your posterity. ^44-3

**44:4** And they shall sprout like[^2] grass,  
Like willows by watercourses. ^44-4

**44:5** One shall say, “I am the LORD’s,”  
Another shall use the name of “Jacob,”  
Another shall mark his arm “of the LORD”[^3]  
And adopt the name of “Israel.” ^44-5

**44:6** Thus said the LORD, the King of Israel,  
Their Redeemer, the LORD of Hosts:  
I am the first and I am the last,  
And there is no god but Me. ^44-6

**44:7** [^4]Who like Me can announce,  
Can foretell it—and match Me thereby?  
Even as I told the future to an ancient people,  
So let him foretell coming events to them. ^44-7

**44:8** Do not be frightened, do not be shaken!  
Have I not from of old predicted to you?  
I foretold, and you are My witnesses.  
Is there any god, then, but Me?  
“There is no other rock; I know none!” ^44-8

**44:9** The makers of idols  
All work to no purpose;  
And the things they treasure  
Can do no good,  
As they themselves can testify.  
They neither look nor think,  
And so they shall be shamed. ^44-9

**44:10** Who would fashion a god  
Or cast a statue  
That can do no good? ^44-10

**44:11** Lo, all its adherents shall be shamed;  
They are craftsmen, are merely human.  
Let them all assemble and stand up!  
They shall be cowed, and they shall be shamed. ^44-11

**44:12** [^5]The craftsman in iron, with his tools,  
Works it[^6] over charcoal  
And fashions it by hammering,  
Working with the strength of his arm.  
Should he go hungry, his strength would ebb;  
Should he drink no water, he would grow faint. ^44-12

**44:13** The craftsman in wood measures with a line  
And marks out a shape with a stylus;  
He forms it with scraping tools,  
Marking it out with a compass.  
He gives it a human form,  
The beauty of a man, to dwell in a shrine. ^44-13

**44:14** For his use he cuts down cedars;  
He chooses plane trees and oaks.  
He sets aside trees of the forest;  
Or plants firs, and the rain makes them grow. ^44-14

**44:15** All this serves man for fuel:  
He takes some to warm himself,  
And he builds a fire and bakes bread.  
He also makes a god of it and worships it,  
Fashions an idol and bows down to it! ^44-15

**44:16** Part of it he burns in a fire:  
On that part he roasts[^7] meat,  
He eats[^8] the roast and is sated;  
He also warms himself and cries, “Ah,  
I am warm! I can feel[^9] the heat!” ^44-16

**44:17** Of the rest he makes a god—his own carving!  
He bows down to it, worships it;  
He prays to it and cries,  
“Save me, for you are my god!” ^44-17

**44:18** They have no wit or judgment:  
Their eyes are besmeared, and they see not;  
Their minds, and they cannot think. ^44-18

**44:19** They do not give thought,  
They lack the wit and judgment to say:  
“Part of it I burned in a fire;  
I also baked bread on the coals,  
I roasted meat and ate it—  
Should I make the rest an abhorrence?  
Should I bow to a block of wood?” ^44-19

**44:20** He pursues ashes![^10]  
A deluded mind has led him astray,  
And he cannot save himself;  
He never says to himself,  
“The thing in my hand is a fraud!” ^44-20

**44:21** Remember these things, O Jacob  
For you, O Israel, are My servant:  
I fashioned you, you are My servant—  
O Israel, never forget Me.[^11] ^44-21

**44:22** I wipe away your sins like a cloud,  
Your transgressions like mist—  
Come back to Me, for I redeem you. ^44-22

**44:23** Shout, O heavens, for the LORD has acted;  
Shout aloud, O depths of the earth!  
Shout for joy, O mountains,  
O forests with all your trees!  
For the LORD has redeemed Jacob,  
Has glorified Himself through Israel. ^44-23

**44:24** Thus said the LORD, your Redeemer,  
Who formed you in the womb:  
It is I, the LORD, who made everything,  
Who alone stretched out the heavens  
And unaided[^12] spread out the earth; ^44-24

**44:25** Who annul the omens of diviners,  
And make fools of the augurs;  
Who turn sages back  
And make nonsense of their knowledge; ^44-25

**44:26** But confirm the word of My[^13] servant  
And fulfill the prediction of My[^14] messengers.  
It is I who say of Jerusalem, “It shall be inhabited,”  
And of the towns of Judah, “They shall be rebuilt;  
And I will restore their ruined places.” ^44-26

**44:27** [I,] who said to the deep, “Be dry;  
I will dry up your floods,” ^44-27

**44:28** Am the same who says of Cyrus, “He is My shepherd;[^15]  
He shall fulfill all My purposes!  
He shall say of Jerusalem, ‘She shall be rebuilt,’  
And to the Temple: ‘You shall be founded again.’” ^44-28

[^1]: A name for Israel; see note on Num. 23.10; cf. Deut. 32.15; 33.5, 26.
[^2]: Lit. “in among.”
[^3]: It was customary to mark a slave with the owner’s name.
[^4]: Meaning of verse uncertain.
[^5]: The meaning of parts of vv. 12–13 is uncertain.
[^6]: I.e., the image he is making.
[^7]: Transposing the Heb. verbs for clarity.
[^8]: Transposing the Heb. verbs for clarity.
[^9]: Lit. “see.”
[^10]: Lit. “He shepherds ashes.”
[^11]: Emendation yields “them,” these things.
[^12]: Lit. “with none beside me,” or (following many Heb. mss., kethib, and ancient versions) “who was with me?”
[^13]: Heb. “His.”
[^14]: Heb. “His.”
[^15]: I.e., the king whom I have designated.
