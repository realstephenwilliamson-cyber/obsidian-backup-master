# LXX Psalms 112

← [[LXX Psalms 111]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 113]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 113]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 113]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**112:1** Alleluia. Praise the Lord, ye servants *of his*, praise the name of the Lord.  ^112-1

**112:2** Let the name of the Lord be blessed, from this present time and for ever.  ^112-2

**112:3** From the rising of the sun to his setting, the name of the Lord is to be praised.  ^112-3

**112:4** The Lord is high above all the nations; his glory is above the heavens.  ^112-4

**112:5** Who is as the Lord our God? who dwells in the high places,  ^112-5

**112:6** and *yet* looks upon the low things in heaven, and on the earth:  ^112-6

**112:7** who lifts up the poor from the earth, and raises up the needy from the dunghill;  ^112-7

**112:8** to set him with princes, *even* with the princes of his people:  ^112-8

**112:9** who settles the barren *woman* in a house, *as* a mother rejoicing over children.  ^112-9
