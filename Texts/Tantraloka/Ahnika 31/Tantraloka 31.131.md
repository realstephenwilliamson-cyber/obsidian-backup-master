## Mūla

विषुवत्स्थेन विन्यासो मन्त्राणां मण्डलोत्तमे ।
कार्योऽस्मिन् पूजिते यत्र सर्वेश्वरपदं भजेत् ॥ ^mula

viṣuvatsthena vinyāso mantrāṇāṃ maṇḍalottame |
kāryo'smin pūjite yatra sarveśvarapadaṃ bhajet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 05 - Chapter Thirty-One, part 2#^pg136|p. 118]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=136>))
>
> (In other words,) after having set the motionless instrument all at once into motion and squeezed the root which is bliss, one should enter into the plane of the three powers, that should be worshipped there with effort. All the attainments arise (this way). (At the same time,) one should contemplate (paribhāvayet) the trident made of the thirty-six reality levels (tattva) with this kind of initial effort, by conjoining (there) all the Path by (power of) the pervasion of the Six Paths, along with all the Mantras and wheels etc. The Mantras should be deposited in this venerated and most excellent maṇḍala, where one who is established in the Equinox (viṣuvat) may attain the plane of the Lord of All.’ (with 31.128–131)

## Viveka

> [!viveka]- Viveka in English (Dyczkowski, vol. XI, pp. 118–119)
> ![[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 05 - Chapter Thirty-One, part 2#Viveka on 31.128–131]]

> अस्पन्दकरणं कृत्वा एकदा स्पन्दवर्तनम् ।
> मूलमानन्दमापीड्य शक्तित्रयपदं विशेत् ॥
> तत्र पूज्यं प्रयत्नेन जायन्ते सर्वसिद्धयः ।
> समस्ताध्वसमायोगात् षोढाध्वव्याप्तिभावतः ॥
> समस्तमन्त्रचक्राद्यैरेवमादिप्रयत्नतः ।
> षट्त्रिंशत्तत्त्वरचितं त्रिशूलं परिभावयेत् ॥
> विषुवत्स्थेन विन्यासो मन्त्राणां मण्डलोत्तमे ।
> कार्योऽस्मिन् पूजिते यत्र सर्वेश्वरपदं भजेत् ॥

मूलमिति--मत्तगन्धात्मकम् । विषुवत्स्थेनेति--प्राणसाम्येन--इत्यर्थः ॥ १३१ ॥
