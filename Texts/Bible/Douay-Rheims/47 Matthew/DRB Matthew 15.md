# Matthew 15 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Matthew 14|← Matthew 14]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Matthew 16|Matthew 16 →]]

Compare: [[Matthew 15|BSB and SBLGNT]]

**15:1** Then came to him from Jerusalem scribes and Pharisees, saying: ^15-1

**15:2** Why do thy disciples transgress the tradition of the ancients? For they wash not their hands when they eat bread. ^15-2

**15:3** But he answering, said to them: Why do you also transgress the commandment of God for your tradition? For God said: ^15-3

**15:4** Honour thy father and mother: And: He that shall curse father or mother, let him die the death. ^15-4

**15:5** But you say: Whosoever shall say to father or mother, The gift whatsoever proceedeth from me, shall profit thee. ^15-5

**15:6** And he shall not honour his father or his mother: and you have made void the commandment of God for your tradition. ^15-6

**15:7** Hypocrites, well hath Isaias prophesied of you, saying: ^15-7

**15:8** This people honoureth me with their lips: but their heart is far from me. ^15-8

**15:9** And in vain do they worship me, teaching doctrines and commandments of men. ^15-9

**15:10** And having called together the multitudes unto him, he said to them: Hear ye and understand. ^15-10

**15:11** Not that which goeth into the mouth defileth a man: but what cometh out of the mouth, this defileth a man. ^15-11

**15:12** Then came his disciples, and said to him: Dost thou know that the Pharisees, when they heard this word, were scandalized? ^15-12

**15:13** But he answering them, said: Every plant which my heavenly Father hath not planted, shall be rooted up. ^15-13

**15:14** Let them alone: they are blind, and leaders of the blind. And if the blind lead the blind, both will fall into the pit. ^15-14

**15:15** And Peter answering, said to him: Expound to us this parable. ^15-15

**15:16** But he said: Are you also yet without understanding? ^15-16

**15:17** Do you not understand, that whatsoever entereth into the mouth, goeth into the belly, and is cast out into the privy? ^15-17

**15:18** But the things which proceed out of the mouth, come forth from the heart, and those things defile a man. ^15-18

**15:19** For from the heart come forth evil thoughts, murders, adulteries, fornications, thefts, false testimonies, blasphemies. ^15-19

**15:20** These are the things that defile a man. But to eat with unwashed hands doth not defile a man. ^15-20

**15:21** And Jesus went from thence, and retired into the coasts of Tyre and Sidon. ^15-21

**15:22** And behold a woman of Canaan who came out of those coasts, crying out, said to him: Have mercy on me, O Lord, thou son of David: my daughter is grieviously troubled by the devil. ^15-22

**15:23** Who answered her not a word. And his disciples came and besought him, saying: Send her away, for she crieth after us: ^15-23

**15:24** And he answering, said: I was not sent but to the sheep that are lost of the house of Israel. ^15-24

**15:25** But she came and adored him, saying: Lord, help me. ^15-25

**15:26** Who answering, said: It is not good to take the bread of the children, and to cast it to the dogs. ^15-26

**15:27** But she said: Yea, Lord; for the whelps also eat of the crumbs that fall from the table of their masters. ^15-27

**15:28** Then Jesus answering, said to her: O woman, great is thy faith: be it done to thee as thou wilt: and her daughter was cured from that hour. ^15-28

**15:29** And when Jesus had passed away from thence, he came nigh the sea of Galilee. And going up into a mountain, he sat there. ^15-29

**15:30** And there came to him great multitudes, having with them the dumb, the blind, the lame, the maimed, and many others: and they cast them down at his feet, and he healed them: ^15-30

**15:31** So that the multitudes marvelled seeing the dumb speak, the lame walk, and the blind see: and they glorified the God of Israel. ^15-31

**15:32** And Jesus called together his disciples, and said: I have compassion on the multitudes, because they continue with me now three days, and have not what to eat, and I will not send them away fasting, lest they faint in the way. ^15-32

**15:33** And the disciples say unto him: Whence then should we have so many loaves in the desert, as to fill so great a multitude? ^15-33

**15:34** And Jesus said to them: How many loaves have you? But they said: Seven, and a few little fishes. ^15-34

**15:35** And he commanded the multitude to sit down upon the ground. ^15-35

**15:36** And taking the seven loaves and the fishes, and giving thanks, he brake, and gave to his disciples, and the disciples to the people. ^15-36

**15:37** And they did all eat, and had their fill. And they took up seven baskets full, of what remained of the fragments. ^15-37

**15:38** And they that did eat, were four thousand men, beside children and women. ^15-38

**15:39** And having dismissed the multitude, he went up into a boat, and came into the coasts of Magedan. ^15-39
