Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 8.11 - Parashat Be-Ha'alotekha#^z3-156b|Parashat Be-Ha’alotekha, at 3:156b]]

*Volume 3, folio 156b* · Sh'lach, Beha'alotcha

*Passage Beha'alotcha 27:150 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 3.156a#^beha-alotcha-27-150|Beha'alotcha 27:150]].*

**Sh'lach 1:1** AND THE LORD SPAKE UNTO MOSES SAYING, SEND THEE MEN TO SPY OUT THE LAND OF CANAAN, ETC. R. Hiya here cited the verse: “Hast thou caused the dayspring to know his place”, etc. (Job 38, 12). ^sh-lach-1-1

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Sh'lach 2:2–2:6.*

**Sh'lach 2:7** R. Hiya here cited the verse: “Hast thou caused the dayspring to know his place”, etc. (Job 38, 12). ‘Note’, he said, ‘that Moses was the sun, and when he desired to enter the land, God said to him, Moses, when the light of the sun arrives, the moon is embraced in it, but the sun and the moon cannot shine together; the moon cannot shine till the sun is gathered in. ^sh-lach-2-7

**Sh'lach 3:8** Now thou art not permitted to enter, but if thou wouldst fain know of the land, send thee men who will inform thee. ^sh-lach-3-8
