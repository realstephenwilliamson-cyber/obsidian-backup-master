## Mūla

कोणेषु चिन्तयेन्मन्त्री आग्नेयादिष्वनुक्रमात् ।
गात्रकाणां चतुष्कं च दिक्षु पूर्वादिषु स्मरेत् ॥ ^mula

koṇeṣu cintayenmantrī āgneyādiṣvanukramāt |
gātrakāṇāṁ catuṣkaṁ ca dikṣu pūrvādiṣu smaret || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2025 – April 2025
> p. 16 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=30>))
>
> **Māyā at the Uvula**
>
> The reciter of mantra should meditate on the four qualities (of the intellect), namely righteousness (dharma), knowledge (*jñāna*), dispassion (*vairāgya*) and lordship (*aiśvarya*), in the four (intermediate) directions, beginning with the southeast up to the north-east. Again, he should meditate on the four cornices (*gātraka*)[^63] in the four-directions, starting with the east. (with 8.58cd–59)

[^63]: The *gātrakas* are elevated cornices that adorn the corners of the throne. Cf. Kṣemarāja ad SvT 2/63 who defines them as *siṁhāsanapaṭṭikārūpāḥ* and ad NT 18/41: *gātrakāṇi koṇāntarālagā avayavaviśeṣāḥ.* On these cornices, which according to the SvT are white and according to the NT, yellow, are projected from the eastern to northern corner impiety (*adharma*), ignorance (*ajñāna*), lack of detachment (*avairāgya*) and lack of mastery (anaiśvarya).
