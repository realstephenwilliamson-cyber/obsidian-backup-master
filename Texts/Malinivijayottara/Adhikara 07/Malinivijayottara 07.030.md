## Mūla

अङ्गुष्ठौ कल्पयेद्विद्वान्मन्त्रावाहनकर्मणि ।
मुष्टी द्वावुन्नताङ्गुष्ठौ स्थापनी परिकीर्तिता ॥ ^mula

aṅguṣṭhau kalpayedvidvānmantrāvāhanakarmaṇi |
muṣṭī dvāvunnatāṅguṣṭhau sthāpanī parikīrtitā || ^iast

> **19. Aavaha (Invocation)**
>
> The learned man, having cupped the two hands together (*añjaliṃ kṛtvā*), should place the thumbs on the ring and little fingers (during) the act of invoking the mantra (deity). (with 7.29cd–30ab)

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, July 2024 – September 2024
> p. 29 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=8>))
>
> **20. Sthāpanī (Installation)**
>
> The thumbs of the two fists are raised up. (This is) said to be (the gesture for the) installation (of the deity). (with 7.30cd–30cd)
