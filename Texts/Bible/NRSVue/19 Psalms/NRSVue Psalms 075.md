## Thanksgiving for God’s wondrous deeds

*To the leader: Do Not Destroy. A Psalm of Asaph. A Song.*

**75:1** We give thanks to you, O God; we give thanks; your name is near. People tell of your wondrous deeds. ^75-1

**75:2** At the set time that I appoint, I will judge with equity. ^75-2

**75:3** When the earth totters, with all its inhabitants, it is I who keep its pillars steady. *Selah* ^75-3

**75:4** I say to the boastful, “Do not boast,” and to the wicked, “Do not lift up your horn; ^75-4

**75:5** do not lift up your horn on high or speak with insolent neck.” ^75-5

**75:6** For not from the east or from the west and not from the wilderness comes lifting up, ^75-6

**75:7** but it is God who executes judgment, putting down one and lifting up another. ^75-7

**75:8** For in the hand of the LORD there is a cup with foaming wine, well mixed; he will pour a draught from it, and all the wicked of the earth shall drain it down to the dregs. ^75-8

**75:9** But I will rejoice^[Gk: Heb *declare*] forever; I will sing praises to the God of Jacob. ^75-9

**75:10** All the horns of the wicked I will cut off, but the horns of the righteous shall be exalted. ^75-10
