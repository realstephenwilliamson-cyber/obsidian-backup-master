## Mūla

मर्त्येऽवतीर्य वा नो वा शिवं यान्त्यपुनर्भवाः ।
तत्र स्वयम्भुवो द्वेधा केऽप्यनुग्रहतत्पराः ॥ ^mula

martye'vatīrya vā no vā śivaṃ yāntyapunarbhavāḥ |
tatra svayambhuvo dvedhā ke'pyanugrahatatparāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 05 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 3#^pg123|p. 106]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=123>))
>
> There are those who die in the abodes (gṛha) of (Liṅgas) that have descended, in accord with Śiva’s command, from (higher) principles. These include, amongst others, (Liṅgas) that are spontaneously born of themselves (svayambhū), (as well as those installed) by ascetics (muni), Vedic seers (devarṣi) or by (pious) people. (Those who die there) attain their (corresponding) world, and are initiated by the Lord of (that) world, in due order. Whether they descend or not into the mortal (world), they are without further rebirth, and attain Śiva. (with 28.237cd–239ab)
>
> There (in that context, the Liṅgas that) are spontaneously born of themselves (svayambhū) are of two kinds. Some are intent on gracing (all living beings, irrespective of place) (anugrahatatpara). Some (others) reside only in the places where a partial incarnation (of a deity) (aṁśa)) has come (to carry out) its own task. The man who dies in those (places) where (those dwell,) whom (Śiva) has ordered (to be there) in order to grace (others), attains (their) clearly evident grace without (having) to descend (again) into the mortal (world). (with 28.239cd–241ab)
