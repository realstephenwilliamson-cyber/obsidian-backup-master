# Psalm 60 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 059|← Psalm 59]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 061|Psalm 61 →]]

## Prayer for national victory after defeat

*To the leader: according to the Lily of the Covenant. A Miktam of David; for instruction; when he*

*struggled with Aram-naharaim and with Aram-zobah, and when Joab on his return killed twelve*

*thousand Edomites in the Valley of Salt.*

**60:1** O God, you have rejected us, broken our defenses; you have been angry; now restore us! ^60-1

**60:2** You have caused the land to quake; you have torn it open; repair the cracks in it, for it is tottering. ^60-2

**60:3** You have made your people suffer hard things; you have given us wine to drink that made us reel. ^60-3

**60:4** You have set up a banner for those who fear you, to rally to it out of bowshot.^[Gk Syr Jerome: Heb *because of the truth*] *Selah* ^60-4

**60:5** Give victory with your right hand and answer us,^[Or *me*] so that those whom you love may be rescued. ^60-5

**60:6** God has promised in his sanctuary,^[Or *by his holiness*] “With exultation I will divide up Shechem and portion out the Vale of Succoth. ^60-6

**60:7** Gilead is mine, and Manasseh is mine; Ephraim is my helmet; Judah is my scepter. ^60-7

**60:8** Moab is my washbasin; on Edom I hurl my shoe; over Philistia I shout in triumph.” ^60-8

**60:9** Who will bring me to the fortified city? Who will lead me to Edom? ^60-9

**60:10** Have you not rejected us, O God? You do not go out, O God, with our armies. ^60-10

**60:11** O grant us help against the foe, for human help is worthless. ^60-11

**60:12** With God we shall do valiantly; it is he who will tread down our foes. ^60-12
