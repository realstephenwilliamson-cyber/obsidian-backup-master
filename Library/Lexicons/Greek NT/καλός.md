# καλός

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · adjective

**Dodson:** beautiful, good, worthy — beautiful, as an outward sign of the inward good, noble, honorable character; good, worthy, honorable, noble, and seen to be so. (Strong's 2570; headword καλός, ή, όν)
