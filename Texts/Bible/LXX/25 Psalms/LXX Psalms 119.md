# LXX Psalms 119

← [[LXX Psalms 118]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 120]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 120]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 120]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**119:1** A Song of Degrees. In mine affliction I cried to the Lord, and he hearkened to me.  ^119-1

**119:2** Deliver my soul, O Lord, from unjust lips, and from a deceitful tongue.  ^119-2

**119:3** What should be given to thee, and what should be added to thee, for *thy* crafty tongue?  ^119-3

**119:4** Sharpened weapons of the mighty, with coals of the desert.  ^119-4

**119:5** Woe is me, that my sojourning is prolonged; I have tabernacled among the tents of Kedar.[^1]  ^119-5

**119:6** My soul has long been a sojourner;  ^119-6

**119:7** I was peaceable among them that hated peace; when I spoke to them, they warred against me without a cause.  ^119-7

[^1]: 119:5 *Heb.* משך.
