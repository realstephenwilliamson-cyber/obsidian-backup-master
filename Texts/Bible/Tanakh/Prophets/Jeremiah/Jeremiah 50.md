## Chapter 50

**50:1** The word which the LORD spoke concerning Babylon, the land of the Chaldeans, through the prophet Jeremiah: ^50-1

**50:2** Declare among the nations, and proclaim;  
Raise a standard, proclaim;  
Hide nothing! Say:  
Babylon is captured,  
Bel[^1] is shamed,  
Merodach[^2] is dismayed.  
Her idols are shamed,  
Her fetishes dismayed. ^50-2

**50:3** For a nation from the north has attacked her,  
It will make her land a desolation.  
No one shall dwell in it,  
Both man and beast shall wander away. ^50-3

**50:4** In those days and at that time—declares the LORD—the people of Israel together with the people of Judah shall come, and they shall weep as they go to seek the LORD their God. ^50-4

**50:5** They shall inquire for Zion; in that direction their faces shall turn; they shall come[^3] and attach themselves to the LORD by a covenant for all time, which shall never be forgotten. ^50-5

**50:6** My people were lost sheep: their shepherds led them astray, they drove them out to the mountains, they roamed from mount to hill, they forgot their own resting place. ^50-6

**50:7** All who encountered them devoured them; and their foes said, “We shall not be held guilty, because they have sinned against the LORD, the true Pasture, the Hope of their fathers—the LORD.” ^50-7

**50:8** Flee from Babylon,  
Leave the land of the Chaldeans,  
And be like he-goats that lead the flock! ^50-8

**50:9** For see, I am rousing and leading  
An assemblage of great nations against Babylon  
From the lands of the north.  
They shall draw up their lines against her,  
There she shall be captured.  
Their arrows are like those of a skilled warrior[^4]  
Who does not turn back without hitting the mark. ^50-9

**50:10** Chaldea shall be despoiled,  
All her spoilers shall be sated  
—declares the LORD. ^50-10

**50:11** For you rejoiced, you exulted,  
You who plundered My possession;  
You stamped like a heifer treading grain,  
You neighed like steeds. ^50-11

**50:12** So your mother will be utterly shamed,  
She who bore you will be disgraced.  
Behold the end of the nations—  
Wilderness, desert, and steppe! ^50-12

**50:13** Because of the LORD’s wrath she shall not be inhabited;  
She shall be utterly desolate.  
Whoever passes by Babylon will be appalled  
And will hiss at all her wounds. ^50-13

**50:14** Range yourselves round about Babylon,  
All you who draw the bow;  
Shoot at her, don’t spare arrows,  
For she has sinned against the LORD. ^50-14

**50:15** Raise a shout against her all about!  
She has surrendered;[^5]  
Her bastions have fallen,  
Her walls are thrown down—  
This is the LORD’s vengeance.  
Take vengeance on her,  
Do to her as she has done! ^50-15

**50:16** Make an end in Babylon of sowers,  
And of wielders of the sickle at harvest time.  
Because of the deadly[^6] sword,  
Each man shall turn back to his people,  
They shall flee every one to his land. ^50-16

**50:17** Israel are scattered sheep, harried by lions. First the king of Assyria devoured them, and in the end King Nebuchadrezzar of Babylon crunched their bones. ^50-17

**50:18** Assuredly, thus said the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel: I will deal with the king of Babylon and his land as I dealt with the king of Assyria. ^50-18

**50:19** And I will lead Israel back to his pasture, and he shall graze in Carmel and Bashan, and eat his fill in the hill country of Ephraim and in Gilead. ^50-19

**50:20** In those days and at that time  
—declares the LORD—  
The iniquity of Israel shall be sought,  
And there shall be none;  
The sins of Judah,  
And none shall be found;  
For I will pardon those I allow to survive. ^50-20

**50:21** Advance against her—the land of Merathaim[^7]—  
And against the inhabitants of Pekod;  
Ruin and destroy after them to the last  
—says the LORD—  
Do just as I have commanded you. ^50-21

**50:22** Hark! War in the land  
And vast destruction! ^50-22

**50:23** How the hammer of the whole earth  
Has been hacked and shattered!  
How Babylon has become  
An appallment among the nations! ^50-23

**50:24** I set a snare for you, O Babylon,  
And you were trapped unawares;  
You were found and caught,  
Because you challenged the LORD. ^50-24

**50:25** The LORD has opened His armory  
And brought out the weapons of His wrath;  
For that is the task  
Of my Lord GOD of Hosts  
In the land of the Chaldeans. ^50-25

**50:26** Come against her from every quarter;[^8]  
Break open her granaries,  
Pile her up like heaps of grain,[^9]  
And destroy her, let her have no remnant! ^50-26

**50:27** Destroy all[^10] her bulls,  
Let them go down to slaughter.  
Alas for them, their day is come,  
The hour of their doom! ^50-27

**50:28** Hark! fugitives are escaping  
From the land of Babylon,  
To tell in Zion of the vengeance of the LORD our God,  
Vengeance for His Temple. ^50-28

**50:29** Summon archers against Babylon,  
All who draw the bow!  
Encamp against her round about,  
Let none of her people escape.  
Pay her back for her actions,  
Do to her just what she has done;  
For she has acted insolently against the LORD,  
The Holy One of Israel. ^50-29

**50:30** Assuredly, her young men shall fall in her squares,  
And all her warriors shall perish in that day  
—declares the LORD. ^50-30

**50:31** I am going to deal with you, O Insolence  
—declares the Lord GOD of Hosts—  
For your day is come, the time when I doom you: ^50-31

**50:32** Insolence shall stumble and fall,  
With none to raise her up.  
I will set her cities on fire,  
And it shall consume everything around her. ^50-32

**50:33** Thus said the LORD of Hosts:  
The people of Israel are oppressed,  
And so too the people of Judah;  
All their captors held them,  
They refused to let them go. ^50-33

**50:34** Their Redeemer is mighty,  
His name is LORD of Hosts.  
He will champion their cause—  
So as to give rest to the earth,  
And unrest to the inhabitants of Babylon. ^50-34

**50:35** A sword against the Chaldeans  
—declares the LORD—  
And against the inhabitants of Babylon,  
Against its officials and its wise men! ^50-35

**50:36** A sword against the diviners, that they be made fools of!  
A sword against the warriors, that they be dismayed! ^50-36

**50:37** A sword against its horses and chariots,  
And against all the motley crowd in its midst,  
That they become like women!  
A sword against its treasuries, that they be pillaged! ^50-37

**50:38** A drought[^11] against its waters, that they be dried up!  
For it is a land of idols;  
They are besotted by their dread images.[^12] ^50-38

**50:39** Assuredly,  
Wildcats and hyenas[^13] shall dwell [there],  
And ostriches shall dwell there;  
It shall never be settled again,  
Nor inhabited throughout the ages. ^50-39

**50:40** It shall be as when God overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah and their neighbors—declares the LORD; no man shall live there, no human shall sojourn there. ^50-40

**50:41** Lo, a people comes from the northland;  
A great nation and many kings are roused  
From the remotest parts of the earth. ^50-41

**50:42** They grasp the bow and javelin,  
They are cruel, they show no mercy;  
The sound of them is like the roaring sea.  
They ride upon horses,  
Accoutered like a man for battle,  
Against you, O Fair Babylon! ^50-42

**50:43** The king of Babylon has heard the report of them,  
And his hands are weakened;  
Anguish seizes him,  
Pangs like a woman in childbirth. ^50-43

**50:44** It shall be as when a lion comes out of the jungle of the Jordan against a secure pasture: in a moment I can harry them out of it and appoint over it anyone I choose.[^14] Then who is like Me? Who can summon Me? Who is the shepherd that can stand up against Me? ^50-44

**50:45** Hear, then, the plan that the LORD has devised against Babylon, and has purposed against the land of Chaldea:  
Surely the shepherd boys  
Shall drag them away;  
Surely the pasture shall be  
Aghast because of them. ^50-45

**50:46** At the sound of Babylon’s capture  
The earth quakes,  
And an outcry is heard among the nations. ^50-46
