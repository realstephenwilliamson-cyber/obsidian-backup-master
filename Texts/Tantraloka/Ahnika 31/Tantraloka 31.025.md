## Mūla

ततो द्वयेन कर्तव्या गण्डिकान्तः सुसङ्गता ।
द्वयेनाग्रगसूत्राभ्यां मध्यशृङ्गद्वयं भवेत् ॥ ^mula

tato dvayena kartavyā gaṇḍikāntaḥ susaṅgatā |
dvayenāgragasūtrābhyāṃ madhyaśṛṅgadvayaṃ bhavet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 04 - Chapter Thirty-One, part 1#^pg94|p. 76]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=94>))
>
> Then, making use of the two previously mentioned half-moons that are facing (each other), (the teacher should) make a good (and beautiful) inner alignment of the ‘pillow’ with these two (between them), and with the two threads in front of them, along with the two (prongs, above and below the two tridents on the southern and northern sides), there are (also) the two prongs in the middle (between the prongs). (with 31.24cd–25)

## Viveka

> ततो यदुन्मुखं खण्डचन्द्रयुग्मं पुरोदितम् ॥
> ततो द्वयेन कर्तव्या गण्डिकान्तः सुसङ्गता ।
> द्वयेनाग्रगसूत्राभ्यां मध्यशृङ्गद्वयं भवेत् ॥

पुरेति--पूर्वशूलवर्तनावसरे । सुसङ्गतेति--पार्श्वशृङ्गयोः ॥ २५ ॥
