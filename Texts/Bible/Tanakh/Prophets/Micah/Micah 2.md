## Chapter 2

**2:1** Ah, those who plan iniquity  
And design evil on their beds;  
When morning dawns, they do it,  
For they have the power. ^2-1

**2:2** They covet fields, and seize them;  
Houses, and take them away.  
They defraud men of their homes,  
And people of their land. ^2-2

**2:3** Assuredly, thus said the LORD: I am planning such a misfortune against this clan that you will not be able to free your necks from it. You will not be able to walk erect; it will be such a time of disaster. ^2-3

**2:4** In that day,  
One shall recite a poem about you,  
And utter a bitter lament,  
And shall say:  
“My people’s portion changes hands;  
How it slips away from me!  
Our field is allotted to a rebel.[^1]  
We are utterly ravaged.”[^2] ^2-4

**2:5** Truly, none of you  
Shall cast a lot cord[^3]  
In the assembly of the LORD! ^2-5

**2:6** “Stop preaching!” they preach.  
“That’s no way to preach;  
[^4]Shame shall not overtake [us]. ^2-6

**2:7** Is the House of Jacob condemned?  
Is the LORD’s patience short?  
Is such His practice?”  
To be sure, My words are friendly  
To those who walk in rectitude; ^2-7

**2:8** But an enemy arises against[^5] My people.  
You strip the mantle with the cloak[^6]  
Off such as pass unsuspecting,  
Who are turned away from war.[^7] ^2-8

**2:9** You drive the women of My people away  
From their pleasant homes;  
You deprive their infants  
Of My glory forever. ^2-9

**2:10** Up and depart!  
This is no resting place  
Because of [your] defilement.  
Terrible destruction shall befall.[^8] ^2-10

**2:11** If a man were to go about uttering  
Windy, baseless falsehoods:  
“I’ll preach to you in favor of wine and liquor”—  
He would be a preacher [acceptable] to that people. ^2-11

**2:12** [^9]I will assemble Jacob, all of you;  
I will bring together the remnant of Israel;  
I will make them all like sheep of Bozrah,[^10]  
Like a flock inside its pen[^11]—  
They will be noisy with people. ^2-12

**2:13** One who makes a breach  
Goes before them;  
They enlarge it to a gate  
And leave by it.  
Their king marches before them,  
The LORD at their head. ^2-13

[^1]: Emendation yields “ravager.”
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: On a piece of land, thus acquiring title to it; cf. Josh. 18.6 and Ps. 16.6.
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^5]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain; emendation yields “you arise as enemies against.”
[^6]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain; emendation yields “off peaceful folk.”
[^7]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^8]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^9]: Vv. 12–13 may be an example of such “acceptable” preaching.
[^10]: Emendation yields “in a fold [Arabic s™†rah].”
[^11]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
