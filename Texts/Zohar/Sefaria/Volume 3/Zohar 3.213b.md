# Zohar 3.213b

← [[Zohar 3.213a]] · [[Zohar (Soncino)]] · [[Zohar 3.214a]] →

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 9.11 - Parashat Pinehas#^z3-213b|Parashat Pineḥas, at 3:213b]]

*Volume 3, folio 213b* · Pinchas

*Passage Pinchas 3:10 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 3.213a#^pinchas-3-10|Pinchas 3:10]].*

**Pinchas 3:11** Note that if one is zealous for the name of the Holy One, blessed be He, even though he is not qualified for greatness, he nevertheless obtains it. Phineas at that time was not qualified for greatness, but because he was zealous for the name of his Master he rose to the greatest heights, and all was made right in him[^1], and he was invested with the high priesthood. Hence the word “son” is mentioned in connection with him twice.[^2] ^pinchas-3-11

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Pinchas 4:12–6:18.*

[^1]: i.e. the souls of Nadab and Abihu entered into him, s. *infra*.
[^2]: The last 52 lines of the Hebrew text are not found in our translation.
