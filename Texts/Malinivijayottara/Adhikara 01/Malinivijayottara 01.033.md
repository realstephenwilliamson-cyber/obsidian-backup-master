## Mūla

कलादिक्षितिपर्यन्तमेतत्संसारमण्डलम् ।
समुद्रादि जगत्कृत्स्नं परिवर्तयतीच्छया ॥ ^mula

kalādikṣitiparyantametatsaṁsāramaṇḍalam |
samudrādi[^70] jagatkṛtsnaṁ parivartayatīcchayā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2022 – June 2022
> p. 45 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=21>))
>
> (The Lord) by (his) will causes to change (incessantly) this, the sphere of transmigratory existence, ranging from the Force (of limited agency)[^71] up to Earth, and the entire universe rich with (many) oceans etc.

[^70]: kh, g: *samudrādyam* /
[^71]: The Force (of limited agency) is kalātattva, the principle that imparts limited agency (kiṁcitkartṛtva), which produces Karma, to the individual soul.
