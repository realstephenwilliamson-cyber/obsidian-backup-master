# Mark 15 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Mark 14|← Mark 14]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Mark 16|Mark 16 →]]

Compare: [[Mark 15|BSB and SBLGNT]]

**15:1** And straightway in the morning, the chief priests holding a consultation with the ancients and the scribes and the whole council, binding Jesus, led him away, and delivered him to Pilate. ^15-1

**15:2** And Pilate asked him: Art thou the king of the Jews? But he answering, saith to him: Thou sayest it. ^15-2

**15:3** And the chief priests accused him in many things. ^15-3

**15:4** And Pilate again asked him, saying: Answerest thou nothing? behold in how many things they accuse thee. ^15-4

**15:5** But Jesus still answered nothing; so that Pilate wondered. ^15-5

**15:6** Now on the festival day he was wont to release unto them one of the prisoners, whomsoever they demanded. ^15-6

**15:7** And there was one called Barabbas, who was put in prison with some seditious men, who in the sedition had committed murder. ^15-7

**15:8** And when the multitude was come up, they began to desire that he would do, as he had ever done unto them. ^15-8

**15:9** And Pilate answered them, and said: Will you that I release to you the king of the Jews? ^15-9

**15:10** For he knew that the chief priests had delivered him up out of envy. ^15-10

**15:11** But the chief priests moved the people, that he should rather release Barabbas to them. ^15-11

**15:12** And Pilate again answering, saith to them: What will you then that I do to the king of the Jews? ^15-12

**15:13** But they again cried out: Crucify him. ^15-13

**15:14** And Pilate saith to them: Why, what evil hath he done? But they cried out the more: Crucify him. ^15-14

**15:15** And so Pilate being willing to satisfy the people, released to them Barabbas, and delivered up Jesus, when he had scourged him, to be crucified. ^15-15

**15:16** And the soldiers led him away into the court of the palace, and they called together the whole band: ^15-16

**15:17** And they clothe him with purple, and platting a crown of thorns, they put it upon him. ^15-17

**15:18** And they began to salute him: Hail, king of the Jews. ^15-18

**15:19** And they struck his head with a reed: and they did spit on him. And bowing their knees, they adored him. ^15-19

**15:20** And after they had mocked him, they took off the purple from him, and put his own garments on him, and they led him out to crucify him. ^15-20

**15:21** And they forced one Simon a Cyrenian who passed by, coming out of the country, the father of Alexander and of Rufus, to take up his cross. ^15-21

**15:22** And they bring him into the place called Golgotha, which being interpreted is, The place of Calvary. ^15-22

**15:23** And they gave him to drink wine mingled with myrrh; but he took it not. ^15-23

**15:24** And crucifying him, they divided his garments, casting lots upon them, what every man should take. ^15-24

**15:25** And it was the third hour, and they crucified him. ^15-25

**15:26** And the inscription of his cause was written over: THE KING OF THE JEWS. ^15-26

**15:27** And with him they crucify two thieves; the one on his right hand, and the other on his left. ^15-27

**15:28** And the scripture was fulfilled, which saith: And with the wicked he was reputed. ^15-28

**15:29** And they that passed by blasphemed him, wagging their heads, and saying: Vah, thou that destroyest the temple of God, and in three days buildest it up again; ^15-29

**15:30** Save thyself, coming down from the cross. ^15-30

**15:31** In like manner also the chief priests mocking, said with the scribes one to another: He saved others; himself he cannot save. ^15-31

**15:32** Let Christ the king of Israel come down now from the cross, that we may see and believe. And they that were crucified with him reviled him. ^15-32

**15:33** And when the sixth hour was come, there was darkness over the whole earth until the ninth hour. ^15-33

**15:34** And at the ninth hour, Jesus cried out with a loud voice, saying: Eloi, Eloi, lamma sabacthani? Which is, being interpreted, My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me? ^15-34

**15:35** And some of the standers by hearing, said: Behold he calleth Elias. ^15-35

**15:36** And one running and filling a sponge with vinegar, and putting it upon a reed, gave him to drink, saying: Stay, let us see if Elias come to take him down. ^15-36

**15:37** And Jesus having cried out with a loud voice, gave up the ghost. ^15-37

**15:38** And the veil of the temple was rent in two, from the top to the bottom. ^15-38

**15:39** And the centurion who stood over against him, seeing that crying out in this manner he had given up the ghost, said: Indeed this man was the son of God. ^15-39

**15:40** And there were also women looking on afar off: among whom was Mary Magdalen, and Mary the mother of James the less and of Joseph, and Salome: ^15-40

**15:41** Who also when he was in Galilee followed him, and ministered to him, and many other women that came up with him to Jerusalem. ^15-41

**15:42** And when evening was now come, (because it was the Parasceve, that is, the day before the sabbath,) ^15-42

**15:43** Joseph of Arimathea, a noble counsellor, who was also himself looking for the kingdom of God, came and went in boldly to Pilate, and begged the body of Jesus. ^15-43

**15:44** But Pilate wondered that he should be already dead. And sending for the centurion, he asked him if he were already dead. ^15-44

**15:45** And when he had understood it by the centurion, he gave the body to Joseph. ^15-45

**15:46** And Joseph buying fine linen, and taking him down, wrapped him up in the fine linen, and laid him in a sepulchre which was hewed out of a rock. And he rolled a stone to the door of the sepulchre. ^15-46

**15:47** And Mary Magdalen, and Mary the mother of Joseph, beheld where he was laid. ^15-47
