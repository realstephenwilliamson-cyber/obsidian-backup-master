## Mūla

कृत्वाऽथ शिवहस्तेन हृदयं परिमर्शयेत् ।
ततः स्वनाडीमार्गेण हृदयं प्राप्य वै शिशोः ॥ ^mula

kṛtvā'tha śivahastena hṛdayaṃ parimarśayet |
tataḥ svanāḍīmārgeṇa hṛdayaṃ prāpya vai śiśoḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 08 - Chapter Seventeen#^pg175|p. 155]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=175>))
>
> After this, the teacher strikes the disciple with (the Mantra of) the Weapon, in the place where a (particular) principle (tattva) (is projected into the body). Having done that, he should touch (the disciple’s) heart with Śiva’s Hand. Then having reached the disciple’s heart by the path of his own channel (of the vital breath), and having becoming one with the disciple’s Self and seized it by means of the (mantra of) the Heart (SAUḤ) and encapsulated by it, he should place it, that is, the one called ‘Haṁsa’, there in his own heart by means of the gesture (mudrā) of withdrawal. Although (Haṁsa is located there,) it lights up the body of the disciple with its radiant flames in such a way that it is not separated from the light (raśmimātrā). (with 17.30cd–33)
