## Mūla

जयन्ति जगदानन्दविपक्षक्षपणक्षमाः ।
परमेशमुखोद्भूतज्ञानचन्द्रमरीचयः ॥ ^mula

jayanti jagadānandavipakṣakṣapaṇakṣamāḥ |
parameśamukhodbhūtajñānacandramarīcayaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2022 – June 2022
> p. 39 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=15>))
>
> The rays of the moon of consciousness that come forth from the mouth of the Supreme Lord, capable of shattering (all) that is contrary to Cosmic Bliss (*jagadānanda*),[^38] triumph!

[^38]: Concerning the nature of ‘cosmic bliss’, see TĀ 5/50cd-52ab.
