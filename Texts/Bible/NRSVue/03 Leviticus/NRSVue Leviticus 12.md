## Purification of women after childbirth

**12:1** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, ^12-1

**12:2** “Speak to the Israelites, saying: “If a woman conceives and bears a male child, she shall be unclean seven days; as at the time of her menstruation, she shall be unclean. ^12-2

**12:3** On the eighth day the flesh of his foreskin shall be circumcised. ^12-3

**12:4** Her time of blood purification shall be thirty-three days; she shall not touch any holy thing or come into the sanctuary until the days of her purification are completed. ^12-4

**12:5** If she bears a female child, she shall be unclean two weeks, as in her menstruation; her time of blood purification shall be sixty-six days. ^12-5

**12:6** “When the days of her purification are completed, whether for a son or for a daughter, she shall bring to the priest at the entrance of the tent of meeting a lamb in its first year for a burnt offering and a pigeon or a turtledove for a purification offering. ^12-6

**12:7** He shall offer it before the LORD and make atonement on her behalf; then she shall be clean from her flow of blood. This is the law for her who bears a child, male or female. ^12-7

**12:8** If she cannot afford a sheep, she shall take two turtledoves or two pigeons, one for a burnt offering and the other for a purification offering, and the priest shall make atonement on her behalf, and she shall be clean.” ^12-8
