# Zohar 3.214a

← [[Zohar 3.213b]] · [[Zohar (Soncino)]] · [[Zohar 3.216a]] →

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 9.11 - Parashat Pinehas#^z3-214a|Parashat Pineḥas, at 3:214a]]

*Volume 3, folio 214a* · Pinchas

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Pinchas 6:19.*

**Pinchas 6:20** [^1] Phineas merited reward in this world and in the next. He was granted to outlive all those who went forth from Egypt and won the high priesthood for himself and his descendants. ^pinchas-6-20

**Pinchas 6:21** Now it is a rule that a priest who kills a human being becomes disqualified for the priesthood, and therefore by rights Phineas should have been disqualified. But because he was jealous for the Holy One, blessed be He, the priesthood was assigned to him and to his descendants in perpetuity.’ ^pinchas-6-21

**Pinchas 6:22** As R. Eleazar and R. Jose were once walking in the wilderness, R. Jose said: ‘When it says of Phineas, “Behold I give him my covenant of peace”, it means peace from the Angel of Death, so that he should never have power over him and that he should not suffer his chastisements. As for the tradition that Phineas did not die, the truth is that he did not die like other men, and he outlived all his generation, because he kept hold of this supernal covenant, and when he departed from the world it was with celestial yearning and beauteous attachment.’ ^pinchas-6-22

**Pinchas 7:23** R. Eleazar cited the verse: “And he showed me Joshua the high priest standing before the angel of the Lord”, etc. (Zech. 3, 1). ‘The “filthy garments”, as explained elsewhere, are those with which the spirit was clad in this world. It has been asked: When a man is doomed to Gehinnom, what are the garments with which they invest him? ^pinchas-7-23

**Pinchas 7:24** And the answer is given in the words, “Now Joshua was clothed with filthy garments, and standing before the angel” (*Ibid.* 3). This was the angel appointed over Gehinnom, ^pinchas-7-24

**Pinchas 7:25** and from this we can learn that the evil deeds of a man make for him these filthy garments, and so God said afterwards to him: “Behold, I have caused thine iniquity to pass from thee and I have clothed thee with rich apparel”; he was clothed in other proper garments through which a man may see the glory of the Shekinah. So Phineas did not depart from this world until there were prepared for him other garments pleasing to the spirit for the next world.’ ^pinchas-7-25

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Pinchas 7:26.*

[^1]: The first 10 lines of the Hebrew text are not found in our translation.
