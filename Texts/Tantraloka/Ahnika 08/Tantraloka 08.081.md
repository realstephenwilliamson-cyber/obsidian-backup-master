## Mūla

संभवन्त्यप्यसंस्कारा भारतेऽन्यत्र चापि हि ।
दृढप्राक्तनसंस्कारादीशेच्छातः शुभाशुभम् ॥ ^mula

saṃbhavantyapyasaṃskārā bhārate'nyatra cāpi hi |
dṛḍhaprāktanasaṃskārādīśecchātaḥ śubhāśubham || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 04 - Chapter Eight, part 2#^pg147|p. 129]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=147>))
>
> Even so, in Bhārata there are some who have no (such) latent impressions. Just as also elsewhere, in (some) other place, there can be good and bad Karma (śubhāśubham), according to the intensity of previous impressions and the Lord’s will. (All) that is seen in the ancient (scriptures). In the Land of Bhārata, all the four Ages occur, whereas it is always the Tretā Age there (in the other places). (with 8.81–82)
