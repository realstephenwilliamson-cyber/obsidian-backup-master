## Mūla

गुरुं कुर्यात् तदभ्यासात्तत्सङ्कल्पमयो ह्यसौ ।
तत्समानाभिसञ्जल्पो यदा मन्त्रार्थभावनात् ॥ ^mula

guruṃ kuryāt tadabhyāsāttatsaṅkalpamayo hyasau |
tatsamānābhisañjalpo yadā mantrārthabhāvanāt || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 07 - Chapter Sixteen, part 3#^pg140|p. 120]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=140>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 16.270ab.
>
> By virtue of (his assiduous) practice (abhyāsa) of that, (the teacher) is identified with the intention (that initiates its use) (tatsankalpa). (with 16.268cd–270ab)
>
> When there is no teacher who knows the meaning (purpose, goal and ultimate nature of) Mantras (mantrārtha) in all respects, take as a teacher one who is identified with Mantra. (with 16.269cd–270a)
>
> If by (deep) contemplation (bhāvanā) of the meaning (purpose, goal and ultimate nature of) Mantras (mantrārtha), the teacher’s discourse becomes like that (of Mantra), then if it is the same in all respects, then pray tell, what is the difference (between them)? (with 16.270cd–271ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* अत एवाह--

> ...........टदभ्यासात्तत्सङ्कल्पमयो ह्यसौ ।

ननु दृढनिरूढमन्त्रार्थभावनाभाजो गुरोरपि अन्योऽभिजल्पो मन्त्रसमानमहिमैव, तत्कथमुक्तं मन्त्राणां सञ्जल्पान्तरतुल्यकक्ष्यत्वं नास्ति ?--इत्याशङ्क्याह--

> तत्समानाभिसञ्जल्पो यदा मन्त्रार्थभावनात् ॥
> गुरोर्भवेत्तदा सर्वसाम्ये को भेद उच्यताम् ।
