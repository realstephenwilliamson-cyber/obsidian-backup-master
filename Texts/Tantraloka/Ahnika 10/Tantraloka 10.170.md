## Mūla

प्रेर्यमाणास्तु मन्त्रेशा मन्त्रास्तद्वाचकाः स्फुटम् ।
धरातत्त्वगतं योगमभ्यस्य शिवविद्यया ॥ ^mula

preryamāṇāstu mantreśā mantrāstadvācakāḥ sphuṭam |
dharātattvagataṃ yogamabhyasya śivavidyayā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 12 - Chapter Ten, part 3#^pg416|p. 397]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=416>))
>
> The Lords of Mantra are the beings (the Great Lords of Mantra) impel, and (their own) Mantras clearly denote them. Those who are in the the Earth Principle. Their view is not the dualist one of the fettered, such as the followers of the Sāmkhya or Vaiṣṇavas, but (they have practiced this Yoga) in accord with Śaiva doctrine, without (however) reaching (Śiva’s) Stable Abode (dhruvadhāman). Thus, those who, after enjoying the experience of this principle, have dissolved away (in the Water and other principles) at the end of an aeon (kalpānte) and are immersed in deep sleep, are the Dissolution Deconditioned (pralayākala) (perceivers) here (atra) (of this principle). (with 10.170–172)
