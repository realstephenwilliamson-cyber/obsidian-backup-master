## Chapter 8

**8:1** The word of the LORD of Hosts came [to me]: ^8-1

**8:2** Thus said the LORD of Hosts: I am very jealous for Zion, I am fiercely jealous for her. ^8-2

**8:3** Thus said the LORD: I have returned to Zion, and I will dwell in Jerusalem. Jerusalem will be called the City of Faithfulness, and the mount of the LORD of Hosts the Holy Mount. ^8-3

**8:4** Thus said the LORD of Hosts: There shall yet be old men and women in the squares of Jerusalem, each with staff in hand because of their great age. ^8-4

**8:5** And the squares of the city shall be crowded with boys and girls playing in the squares. ^8-5

**8:6** Thus said the LORD of Hosts: Though it will seem impossible to the remnant of this people in those days, shall it also be impossible to Me?—declares the LORD of Hosts. ^8-6

**8:7** Thus said the LORD of Hosts: I will rescue My people from the lands of the east and from the lands of the west, ^8-7

**8:8** and I will bring them home to dwell in Jerusalem. They shall be My people, and I will be their God—in truth and sincerity. ^8-8

**8:9** Thus said the LORD of Hosts: Take courage, you who now hear these words which the prophets spoke when the foundations were laid for the rebuilding of the Temple, the House of the LORD of Hosts. ^8-9

**8:10** [^1]For before that time, the earnings of men were nil, and profits from beasts were nothing. It was not safe to go about one’s business on account of enemies; and I set all men against one another. ^8-10

**8:11** But now I will not treat the remnant of this people as before—declares the LORD of Hosts— ^8-11

**8:12** but what it sows shall prosper: The vine shall produce its fruit, the ground shall produce its yield, and the skies shall provide their moisture. I will bestow all these things upon the remnant of this people. ^8-12

**8:13** And just as you were a curse among the nations, O House of Judah and House of Israel, so, when I vindicate you, you shall become a blessing.[^2] Have no fear; take courage! ^8-13

**8:14** For thus said the LORD of Hosts: Just as I planned to afflict you and did not relent when your fathers provoked Me to anger—said the LORD of Hosts— ^8-14

**8:15** so, at this time, I have turned and planned to do good to Jerusalem and to the House of Judah. Have no fear! ^8-15

**8:16** These are the things you are to do: Speak the truth to one another, render true and perfect justice in your gates. ^8-16

**8:17** And do not contrive evil against one another, and do not love perjury, because all those are things that I hate—declares the LORD. ^8-17

**8:18** And the word of the LORD of Hosts came to me, saying, ^8-18

**8:19** Thus said the LORD of Hosts: The fast of the fourth month, the fast of the fifth month, the fast of the seventh month, and the fast of the tenth month[^3] shall become occasions for joy and gladness, happy festivals for the House of Judah; but you must love honesty and integrity. ^8-19

**8:20** Thus said the LORD of Hosts: Peoples and the inhabitants of many cities shall yet come— ^8-20

**8:21** the inhabitants of one shall go to the other and say, “Let us go and entreat the favor of the LORD, let us seek the LORD of Hosts; I will go, too.” ^8-21

**8:22** The many peoples and the multitude of nations shall come to seek the LORD of Hosts in Jerusalem and to entreat the favor of the LORD. ^8-22

**8:23** Thus said the LORD of Hosts: In those days, ten men from nations of every tongue will take hold—they will take hold of every Jew by a corner of his cloak and say, “Let us go with you, for we have heard that God is with you.” ^8-23

[^1]: Cf. Hag. 1.6.
[^2]: I.e., a standard by which men curse or bless; cf. Gen. 12.2 and note.
[^3]: Commemorating, respectively, the events of 2 Kings 25.3 ff. (Jer. 52.6 ff.); 2 Kings 25.8 ff. (Jer. 52.12 ff.); 2 Kings 25.25 ff. (Jer. 41); 2 Kings 25.1 ff. (Jer. 52.4 ).
