# Psalm 36

← [[BCP Psalm 035]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 037]] →

*Dixit injustus* · Book One · Seventh Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 036]]

**36:1** There is a voice of rebellion deep in the heart of the wicked; \*  
there is no fear of God before his eyes. ^36-1

**36:2** He flatters himself in his own eyes \*  
that his hateful sin will not be found out. ^36-2

**36:3** The words of his mouth are wicked and deceitful; \*  
he has left off acting wisely and doing good. ^36-3

**36:4** He thinks up wickedness upon his bed  
and has set himself in no good way; \*  
he does not abhor that which is evil. ^36-4

**36:5** Your love, O LORD, reaches to the heavens, \*  
and your faithfulness to the clouds. ^36-5

**36:6** Your righteousness is like the strong mountains,  
your justice like the great deep; \*  
you save both man and beast, O LORD. ^36-6

**36:7** How priceless is your love, O God! \*  
your people take refuge under the shadow of your wings. ^36-7

**36:8** They feast upon the abundance of your house; \*  
you give them drink from the river of your delights. ^36-8

**36:9** For with you is the well of life, \*  
and in your light we see light. ^36-9

**36:10** Continue your loving-kindness to those who know you, \*  
and your favor to those who are true of heart. ^36-10

[p. 633]

**36:11** Let not the foot of the proud come near me, \*  
nor the hand of the wicked push me aside. ^36-11

**36:12** See how they are fallen, those who work wickedness! \*  
they are cast down and shall not be able to rise. ^36-12
