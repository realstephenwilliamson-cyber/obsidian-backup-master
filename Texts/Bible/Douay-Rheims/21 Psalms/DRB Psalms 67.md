# Psalms 67 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 66|← Psalms 66]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 68|Psalms 68 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 67|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**67:1** Unto the end, a psalm of a canticle for David himself. ^67-1

**67:2** Let God arise, and let his enemies be scattered: and let them that hate him flee from before his face. ^67-2

**67:3** As smoke vanisheth, so let them vanish away: as wax melteth before the fire, so let the wicked perish at the presence of God. ^67-3

**67:4** And let the just feast, and rejoice before God: and be delighted with gladness. ^67-4

**67:5** Sing ye to God, sing a psalm to his name, make a way for him who ascendeth upon the west: the Lord is his name. Rejoice ye before him: but the wicked shall be troubled at his presence, ^67-5

**67:6** Who is the father of orphans, and the judge of widows. God in his holy place: ^67-6

**67:7** God who maketh men of one manner to dwell in a house: Who bringeth out them that were bound in strength; in like manner them that provoke, that dwell in sepulchres. ^67-7

**67:8** O God, when thou didst go forth in the sight of thy people, when thou didst pass through the desert: ^67-8

**67:9** The earth was moved, and the heavens dropped at the presence of the God of Sina, at the presence of the God of Israel. ^67-9

**67:10** Thou shalt set aside for thy inheritance a free rain, O God: and it was weakened, but thou hast made it perfect. ^67-10

**67:11** In it shall thy animals dwell; in thy sweetness, O God, thou hast provided for the poor. ^67-11

**67:12** The Lord shall give the word to them that preach good tidings with great power. ^67-12

**67:13** The king of powers is of the beloved, of the beloved; and the beauty of the house shall divide spoils. ^67-13

**67:14** If you sleep among the midst of lots, you shall be as the wings of a dove covered with silver, and the hinder parts of her back with the paleness of gold. ^67-14

**67:15** When he that is in heaven appointeth kings over her, they shall be whited with snow in Selmon. ^67-15

**67:16** The mountain of God is a fat mountain. A curdled mountain, a fat mountain. ^67-16

**67:17** Why suspect, ye curdled mountains? A mountain in which God is well pleased to dwell: for there the Lord shall dwell unto the end. ^67-17

**67:18** The chariot of God is attended by ten thousands; thousands of them that rejoice: the Lord is among them in Sina, in the holy place. ^67-18

**67:19** Thou hast ascended on high, thou hast led captivity captive; thou hast received gifts in men. Yea for those also that do not believe, the dwelling of the Lord God. ^67-19

**67:20** Blessed be the Lord day by day: the God of our salvation will make our journey prosperous to us. ^67-20

**67:21** Our God is the God of salvation: and of the Lord, of the Lord are the issues from death. ^67-21

**67:22** But God shall break the heads of his enemies: the hairy crown of them that walk on in their sins. ^67-22

**67:23** The Lord said: I will turn them from Basan, I will turn them into the depth of the sea: ^67-23

**67:24** That thy foot may be dipped in the blood of thy enemies; the tongue of thy dogs be red with the same. ^67-24

**67:25** They have seen thy goings, O God, the goings of my God: of my king who is in his sanctuary. ^67-25

**67:26** Princes went before joined with singers, in the midst of young damsels playing on timbrels. ^67-26

**67:27** In the churches bless ye God the Lord, from the fountains of Israel. ^67-27

**67:28** There is Benjamin a youth, in ecstasy of mind. The princes of Juda are their leaders: the princes of Zabulon, the princes of Nephthali. ^67-28

**67:29** Command thy strength, O God: confirm, O God, what thou hast wrought in us. ^67-29

**67:30** From thy temple in Jerusalem, kings shall offer presents to thee. ^67-30

**67:31** Rebuke the wild beasts of the reeds, the congregation of bulls with the kine of the people; who seek to exclude them who are tried with silver. Scatter thou the nations that delight in wars: ^67-31

**67:32** Ambassadors shall come out of Egypt: Ethiopia shall soon stretch out her hands to God. ^67-32

**67:33** Sing to God, ye kingdoms of the earth: sing ye to the Lord: Sing ye to God, ^67-33

**67:34** Who mounteth above the heaven of heavens, to the east. Behold he will give to his voice the voice of power: ^67-34

**67:35** Give ye glory to God for Israel, his magnificence, and his power is in the clouds. ^67-35

**67:36** God is wonderful in his saints: the God of Israel is he who will give power and strength to his people. Blessed be God. ^67-36
