## Mūla

ऊर्ध्वचक्रदशाऽलाभे पिशाचावेश एव सा ।
मन्त्रनादबिन्दुशक्तिभुजङ्गमपरात्मिका ॥ ^mula

ūrdhvacakradaśā'lābhe piśācāveśa eva sā |
mantranādabinduśaktibhujaṅgamaparātmikā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 13 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 5#^pg395|p. 378]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=395>))
>
> (However,) it is stated in the venerable Ratnamālā that if the state of the upper Wheels is not attained, that (accomplishment is in actual fact) just (the result of) possession (āveśa) by a demon (piśāca). (with 29.238d–239ab)
>
> The Supreme Lord has said in the venerable Gahvara that the initiation by piercing is of six kinds, namely: (piercing by) Mantra, Sound (nāda), the Drop (bindu), the empowered one (Sakta),), the serpent (bhujaṅga of Kuṇdalinī), and supreme (para). (with 29.239cd–240ab)

*Lead-in:* बहुधेत्युक्तं निरूपयति--

> मन्त्रनादबिन्दुशक्तिभुजङ्गमपरात्मिका ॥
> षोढा श्रीगह्वरे वेधदीक्षोक्ता परमेशिना ।

तदुक्तं तत्र--

"मन्त्रवेधं तु नादाख्यं बिन्दुवेधमतः परम् ।

शाक्तं भुजङ्गवेधं तु परं षष्ठमुदाहृतम् ॥"

इति । षोढात्वे च अत्र अध्वशुद्धिगर्भीकारः कारणम् । यदुक्तम्--

"षोढा वे वेधबोधेन अध्वानं शोधयेत्प्रिये ।"

इति ॥
