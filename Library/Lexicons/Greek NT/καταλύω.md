# καταλύω

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · verb

**Dodson:** I break up, overthrow, unharness, find a lodging — (lit: I loosen thoroughly), (a) trans: I break up, overthrow, destroy, both lit. and met., (b) I unyoke, unharness a carriage horse or pack animal; hence: I put up, lodge, find a lodging. (Strong's 2647; headword καταλύω)
