# Proverbs 21

Compare: [[NRSVue Proverbs 21|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 21

**21:1** Like channeled water is the mind of the king in the LORD’s hand;  
He directs it to whatever He wishes. ^21-1

**21:2** All the ways of a man seem right to him,  
But the LORD probes the mind. ^21-2

**21:3** To do what is right and just  
Is more desired by the LORD than sacrifice. ^21-3

**21:4** Haughty looks, a proud heart—  
The tillage of the wicked is sinful. ^21-4

**21:5** The plans of the diligent make only for gain;  
All rash haste makes only for loss. ^21-5

**21:6** Treasures acquired by a lying tongue  
Are like driven vapor, heading for extinction.[^1] ^21-6

**21:7** The violence of the wicked sweeps them away,  
For they refuse to act justly. ^21-7

**21:8** The way of a man may be tortuous and strange,  
Though his actions are blameless and proper. ^21-8

**21:9** Dwelling in the corner of a roof is better  
Than a contentious wife in a spacious house.[^2] ^21-9

**21:10** The desire of the wicked is set upon evil;  
His fellowman finds no favor in his eyes. ^21-10

**21:11** When a scoffer is punished, the simple man is edified;  
When a wise man is taught, he gains insight. ^21-11

**21:12** The Righteous One observes the house of the wicked man;  
He subverts the wicked to their ruin. ^21-12

**21:13** Who stops his ears at the cry of the wretched,  
He too will call and not be answered. ^21-13

**21:14** A gift in secret subdues anger,  
A present in private, fierce rage. ^21-14

**21:15** Justice done is a joy to the righteous,  
To evildoers, ruination. ^21-15

**21:16** A man who strays from the path of prudence  
Will rest in the company of ghosts. ^21-16

**21:17** He who loves pleasure comes to want;  
He who loves wine and oil does not grow rich. ^21-17

**21:18** The wicked are the ransom of the righteous;  
The traitor comes in place of the upright. ^21-18

**21:19** It is better to live in the desert  
Than with a contentious, vexatious wife. ^21-19

**21:20** Precious treasure and oil are in the house of the wise man,  
And a fool of a man will run through them. ^21-20

**21:21** He who strives to do good and kind deeds  
Attains life, success, and honor. ^21-21

**21:22** One wise man prevailed over a city of warriors  
And brought down its mighty stronghold. ^21-22

**21:23** He who guards his mouth and tongue  
Guards himself from trouble. ^21-23

**21:24** The proud, insolent man, scoffer is his name,  
Acts in a frenzy of insolence. ^21-24

**21:25** The craving of a lazy man kills him,  
For his hands refuse to work. ^21-25

**21:26** All day long he is seized with craving  
While the righteous man gives without stint. ^21-26

**21:27** The sacrifice of the wicked man is an abomination,  
The more so as he offers it in depravity. ^21-27

**21:28** A false witness is doomed,  
But one who really heard will testify with success. ^21-28

**21:29** The wicked man is brazen-faced;  
The upright man discerns his course. ^21-29

**21:30** No wisdom, no prudence, and no counsel  
Can prevail against the LORD. ^21-30

**21:31** The horse is readied for the day of battle,  
But victory comes from the LORD. ^21-31

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
