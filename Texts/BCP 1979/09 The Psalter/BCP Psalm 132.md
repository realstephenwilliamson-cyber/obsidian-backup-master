# Psalm 132

← [[BCP Psalm 131]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 133]] →

*Memento, Domine* · Book Five · Twenty-eighth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 132]]

**132:1** LORD, remember David, \*  
and all the hardships he endured; ^132-1

[p. 786]

**132:2** How he swore an oath to the LORD \*  
and vowed a vow to the Mighty One of Jacob: ^132-2

**132:3** "I will not come under the roof of my house, \*  
nor climb up into my bed; ^132-3

**132:4** I will not allow my eyes to sleep, \*  
nor let my eyelids slumber; ^132-4

**132:5** Until I find a place for the LORD, \*  
a dwelling for the Mighty One of Jacob." ^132-5

**132:6** "The ark! We heard it was in Ephratah; \*  
we found it in the fields of Jearim. ^132-6

**132:7** Let us go to God's dwelling place; \*  
let us fall upon our knees before his footstool." ^132-7

**132:8** Arise, O LORD, into your resting-place, \*  
you and the ark of your strength. ^132-8

**132:9** Let your priests be clothed with righteousness; \*  
let your faithful people sing with joy. ^132-9

**132:10** For your servant David's sake, \*  
do not turn away the face of your Anointed. ^132-10

**132:11** The LORD has sworn an oath to David; \*  
in truth, he will not break it: ^132-11

**132:12** "A son, the fruit of your body \*  
will I set upon your throne. ^132-12

**132:13** If your children keep my covenant  
and my testimonies that I shall teach them, \*  
their children will sit upon your throne for evermore." ^132-13

**132:14** For the LORD has chosen Zion; \*  
he has desired her for his habitation: ^132-14

**132:15** "This shall be my resting-place for ever; \*  
here will I dwell, for I delight in her. ^132-15

[p. 787]

**132:16** I will surely bless her provisions, \*  
and satisfy her poor with bread. ^132-16

**132:17** I will clothe her priests with salvation, \*  
and her faithful people will rejoice and sing. ^132-17

**132:18** There will I make the horn of David flourish; \*  
I have prepared a lamp for my Anointed. ^132-18

**132:19** As for his enemies, I will clothe them with shame; \*  
but as for him, his crown will shine." ^132-19
