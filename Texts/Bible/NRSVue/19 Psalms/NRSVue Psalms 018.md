# Psalm 18 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 017|← Psalm 17]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 019|Psalm 19 →]]

## Royal thanksgiving for victory

*To the leader. A Psalm of David the servant of the LORD, who addressed the words of this song to the*

*LORD on the day when the LORD delivered him from the hand of all his enemies and from the hand of*

*Saul. He said:*

**18:1** I love you, O LORD, my strength. ^18-1

**18:2** The LORD is my rock, my fortress, and my deliverer, my God, my rock in whom I take refuge, my shield, and the horn of my salvation, my stronghold. ^18-2

**18:3** I call upon the LORD, who is worthy to be praised, so I shall be saved from my enemies. ^18-3

**18:4** The cords of death encompassed me; the torrents of perdition assailed me; ^18-4

**18:5** the cords of Sheol entangled me; the snares of death confronted me. ^18-5

**18:6** In my distress I called upon the LORD; to my God I cried for help. From his temple he heard my voice, and my cry to him reached his ears. ^18-6

**18:7** Then the earth reeled and rocked; the foundations also of the mountains trembled and reeled because he was angry. ^18-7

**18:8** Smoke went up from his nostrils and devouring fire from his mouth; glowing coals flamed forth from him. ^18-8

**18:9** He bowed the heavens and came down; thick darkness was under his feet. ^18-9

**18:10** He rode on a cherub and flew; he came swiftly upon the wings of the wind. ^18-10

**18:11** He made darkness his covering around him, his canopy thick clouds dark with water. ^18-11

**18:12** Out of the brightness before him there broke through his clouds hailstones and coals of fire. ^18-12

**18:13** The LORD also thundered in the heavens, and the Most High uttered his voice.^[Gk: Heb adds *hailstones and coals of fire*] ^18-13

**18:14** And he sent out his arrows and scattered them; he flashed forth lightnings and routed them. ^18-14

**18:15** Then the channels of the sea were seen, and the foundations of the world were laid bare at your rebuke, O LORD, at the blast of the breath of your nostrils. ^18-15

**18:16** He reached down from on high; he took me; he drew me out of mighty waters. ^18-16

**18:17** He delivered me from my strong enemy and from those who hated me, for they were too mighty for me. ^18-17

**18:18** They confronted me in the day of my calamity, but the LORD was my support. ^18-18

**18:19** He brought me out into a broad place; he delivered me because he delighted in me. ^18-19

**18:20** The LORD rewarded me according to my righteousness; according to the cleanness of my hands he recompensed me. ^18-20

**18:21** For I have kept the ways of the LORD and have not wickedly departed from my God. ^18-21

**18:22** For all his ordinances were before me, and his statutes I did not put away from me. ^18-22

**18:23** I was blameless before him, and I kept myself from guilt. ^18-23

**18:24** Therefore the LORD has recompensed me according to my righteousness, according to the cleanness of my hands in his sight. ^18-24

**18:25** With the loyal you show yourself loyal; with the blameless you show yourself blameless; ^18-25

**18:26** with the pure you show yourself pure; and with the crooked you show yourself shrewd. ^18-26

**18:27** For you deliver a humble people, but the haughty eyes you bring down. ^18-27

**18:28** It is you who light my lamp; the LORD, my God, lights up my darkness. ^18-28

**18:29** By you I can outrun a troop, and by my God I can leap over a wall. ^18-29

**18:30** This God—his way is perfect; the promise of the LORD proves true; he is a shield for all who take refuge in him. ^18-30

**18:31** For who is God except the LORD? And who is a rock besides our God? ^18-31

**18:32** The God who has girded me with strength and made my way safe.^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^18-32

**18:33** He made my feet like the feet of a deer and set me secure on the heights. ^18-33

**18:34** He trains my hands for war, so that my arms can bend a bow of bronze. ^18-34

**18:35** You have given me the shield of your salvation, and your right hand has supported me; your help^[Or *gentleness*] has made me great. ^18-35

**18:36** You gave me a wide place for my steps under me, and my feet did not slip. ^18-36

**18:37** I pursued my enemies and overtook them and did not turn back until they were consumed. ^18-37

**18:38** I struck them down so that they were unable to rise; they fell under my feet. ^18-38

**18:39** For you girded me with strength for the battle; you made my assailants sink under me. ^18-39

**18:40** You made my enemies turn their backs to me, and those who hated me I destroyed. ^18-40

**18:41** They cried for help, but there was no one to save them; they cried to the LORD, but he did not answer them. ^18-41

**18:42** I beat them fine, like dust before the wind; I cast them out like the mire of the streets. ^18-42

**18:43** You delivered me from strife with the peoples;^[Gk Tg: Heb *people*] you made me head of the nations; people whom I had not known served me. ^18-43

**18:44** As soon as they heard of me, they obeyed me; foreigners came cringing to me. ^18-44

**18:45** Foreigners lost heart and came trembling out of their strongholds. ^18-45

**18:46** The LORD lives! Blessed be my rock, and exalted be the God of my salvation, ^18-46

**18:47** the God who gave me vengeance and subdued peoples under me, ^18-47

**18:48** who delivered me from my enemies; indeed, you exalted me above my adversaries; you delivered me from the violent. ^18-48

**18:49** For this I will extol you, O LORD, among the nations and sing praises to your name. ^18-49

**18:50** Great triumphs he gives to his king and shows steadfast love to his anointed, to David and his descendants forever. ^18-50
