## Mūla

संक्रान्तित्रितये वृत्ते भुंक्ते चाष्टादशाङ्गुले ।
मेषं प्राप्ते रवौ पुण्यं विषुवत्पारलौकिकम् ॥ ^mula

saṃkrāntitritaye vṛtte bhuṃkte cāṣṭādaśāṅgule |
meṣaṃ prāpte ravau puṇyaṃ viṣuvatpāralaukikam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 11 - Chapter Six, part 4#^pg357|p. 342]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=357>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 6.115cd–116ab.
>
> Once three transits (sarnkrānti) (from one sign of the zodiac to the next) have taken place and eighteen finger(-breadths) have been used up, when the Sun reaches Aries, the auspicious (spring) equinox occurs, (whose fruit) is supernal (pāralaukika).
