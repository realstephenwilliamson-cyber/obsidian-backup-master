## Mūla

इयतो भोगपर्यायात्स्यात्साम्यं कर्मणामिति ।
अनेन नयबीजेन मन्ये वैचित्र्यकारणम् ॥ ^mula

iyato bhogaparyāyātsyātsāmyaṃ karmaṇāmiti |
anena nayabījena manye vaicitryakāraṇam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 06 - Chapter Thirteen, part 2#^pg192|p. 172]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=192>))
>
> A variety (of temporal situations) would be possible (only if) any one of this group (of three) were to have (an ascertainable) beginning (in such a way that one could say that) this (soul, for example,) has undergone experiences for one eon (kalpa), and this (other one) for two. (Only) on the basis of this (kind of) ongoing worldly experience would a balance of Karmas be possible. (with 13.76–77ab)
>
> This (same) argument (nayabīja), I believe, (establishes that) Karma, (which some have) made out to be the cause of the variety of the universe, is not conceivable in that way. (with 13.77cd–78ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* एवं च न केवलमियदेव अवद्यं यावदन्यदपि -- इत्याह --

> अनेन नयबीजेन मन्ये वैचित्र्यकारणम् ॥
> जगतः कर्म यत्कॢप्तं तत्तथा नावकल्पते ।

अनेन इति समनन्तरोक्तेन कर्माद्यनादित्वाविशेषलक्षणेन -- इत्यर्थः । मन्ये इति संभावनायाम् ॥ ७७ ॥
