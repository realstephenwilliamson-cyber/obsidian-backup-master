# Bihani Sarkar

Senior Lecturer in Comparative Non-Western Thought in the School of Global Affairs at Lancaster University; she works on early Indian politics, religions and literature from Sanskrit and Prakrit sources, the Goddess in Indian religion (especially Śākta traditions and their links to kingship and the state), and Sanskrit poetry, drama and aesthetics ([Lancaster profile](https://www.lancaster.ac.uk/global-affairs/people/bihani-sarkar)). The profile names her two books, *Heroic Shāktism: the cult of Durgā in ancient Indian kingship* (OUP 2017) and *Classical Sanskrit Tragedy* (2021).
