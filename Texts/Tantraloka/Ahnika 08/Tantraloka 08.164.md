## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 06 - Chapter Eight, part 4#^pg209|p. 191]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=209>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 8.164cd–165ab.
>
> These, (the first) seven worlds, are made of the five specific (gross elements) called ‘abundant’ (prabhūta), fashioned by Brahmā from (outside) his own body during the first creation (ādisarga). The remaining worlds (are pervaded by) the subtle elements that (constitute and) are the foundation of the subtle body. The gross elements are born of the subtle (tanmātra), beginning with with smell and the rest. They are the obscuring covering of (these) worlds (loka), checking (and sustaining) one another. (with 8.163–164)

## Viveka

> [!viveka]- Viveka in English (Dyczkowski, vol. V, pp. 192–193)
> ![[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 06 - Chapter Eight, part 4#Viveka on 8.163–164]]

> ये ब्रह्मणादिसर्गे स्वशरीरान्निर्मिताः प्रभूताख्याः ।
> स्थूलाः पञ्च विशेषाः सप्तामी तन्मया लोकाः ॥
> परतो लिङ्गाधारैः सूक्ष्मैस्तन्मात्रजैर्महाभूतैः ।
> लोकानामावरणैर्विष्टभ्य परस्परेण गन्धाद्यैः ॥

लिङ्गाधारैः शरीराश्रयैः; अत एव लोकावरणै कारणभूतैः -- इत्यर्थः । "विष्टभ्य परस्परेण"इति सामान्यविशेषरूपतया परस्परावष्टम्भेन अवस्थितैः -- इत्यर्थः ॥ १६४ ॥
