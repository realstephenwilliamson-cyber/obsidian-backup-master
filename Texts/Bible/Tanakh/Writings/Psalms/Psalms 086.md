## Chapter 86

**86:1** A prayer of David.  
Incline Your ear, O LORD,  
answer me,  
for I am poor and needy. ^86-1

**86:2** Preserve my life, for I am steadfast;  
O You, my God,  
deliver Your servant who trusts in You. ^86-2

**86:3** Have mercy on me, O Lord,  
for I call to You all day long; ^86-3

**86:4** bring joy to Your servant’s life,  
for on You, Lord, I set my hope. ^86-4

**86:5** For You, Lord, are good and forgiving,  
abounding in steadfast love to all who call on You. ^86-5

**86:6** Give ear, O LORD, to my prayer;  
heed my plea for mercy. ^86-6

**86:7** In my time of trouble I call You,  
for You will answer me. ^86-7

**86:8** There is none like You among the gods, O Lord,  
and there are no deeds like Yours. ^86-8

**86:9** All the nations You have made  
will come to bow down before You, O Lord,  
and they will pay honor to Your name. ^86-9

**86:10** For You are great and perform wonders;  
You alone are God. ^86-10

**86:11** Teach me Your way, O LORD;  
I will walk in Your truth;  
let my heart be undivided in reverence for Your name. ^86-11

**86:12** I will praise You, O Lord, my God, with all my heart  
and pay honor to Your name forever. ^86-12

**86:13** For Your steadfast love toward me is great;  
You have saved me from the depths of Sheol. ^86-13

**86:14** O God, arrogant men have risen against me;  
a band of ruthless men seek my life;  
they are not mindful of You. ^86-14

**86:15** But You, O Lord, are a God  
compassionate and merciful,  
slow to anger, abounding in steadfast love and faithfulness. ^86-15

**86:16** Turn to me and have mercy on me;  
grant Your strength to Your servant  
and deliver the son of Your maidservant. ^86-16

**86:17** Show me a sign of Your favor,  
that my enemies may see and be frustrated  
because You, O LORD, have given me aid and comfort. ^86-17
