## Mūla

इत्येष यो गुरोः प्रोक्तो विधिस्तं पालयेद् गुरुः ।
अन्यथा न शिवं यायाच्छ्रीमत्सारे च वर्णितम् ॥ ^mula

ityeṣa yo guroḥ prokto vidhistaṃ pālayed guruḥ |
anyathā na śivaṃ yāyācchrīmatsāre ca varṇitam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 14 - Chapter Twenty-Three#^pg357|p. 337]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=357>))
>
> This is the (prescribed) procedure (vidhi) for the teacher, who should (take care) to observe it. Otherwise (if he does not), it is said in the venerable Sāra that he will not go to Śiva (when he dies).
