## Mūla

सोऽप्यभ्युपागमदभीप्सितमस्य यद्वा
स्वातोद्यमेव हि निनर्तिषतोऽवतीर्णम् ।
सोऽनुग्रहप्रवण एव हि सद्गुरूणा-
माज्ञावशेन शुभसूतिमहाङ्कुरेण ॥ ^mula

so'pyabhyupāgamadabhīpsitamasya yadvā
svātodyameva hi ninartiṣato'vatīrṇam |
so'nugrahapravaṇa eva hi sadgurūṇā-
mājñāvaśena śubhasūtimahāṅkureṇa || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 11 - Chapter Thirty-Seven, part 1#^pg279|p. 261]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=279>))
>
> He also accepted their request, or else (how could it be otherwise,) the required musical instrument is surely furnished to one who wants to dance. At the command of his excellent teachers, which is the great sprout that gives birth to (all that is) auspicious, he was (always) inclined to favour (them).
