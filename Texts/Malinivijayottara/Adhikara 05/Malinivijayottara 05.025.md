## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, July 2023 – September 2023

> **The Group of Eight Yoga (Rudras) is within Fundamental Nature (pradhāna)**
>
> *The Group of Eight Yoga (Rudras) is within Fundamental Nature (pradhāna)*.[^44] There the first is Akṛta (Unfashioned), and (then) 2) Kṛta (Fashioned), 3) Vaibhava (Magnificence), 4) Brāhma and 5) Vaiṣṇava is after that, 6) Kaumara, 7) Auma, and 8) Śraikaṇṭha. Such is the group of eight Yogas. (with 5.24–25ab)

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, July 2023 – September 2023
> p. 74 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=79>))
>
> **The Eleven Rudras who reside in the individual soul**
>
> In the same way (the eleven Rudras who reside) in the individual soul are Vāma, Bhīma, Ugra, Bhava, Īśāna, Ekavīraka, Pracaṇḍa, Mādhava, Aja,[^45] Ananta and Ekaśiva.[^46] (with 5.25cd–26ab)

[^44]: MV 5/24a is quoted in TĀv intro to 8/238cd-241 (238-241ab).
[^45]: Read mādhavo 'jaś ca for *mādhavājāś* ca which is clearly wrong.
[^46]: MV 5/25cd-26ab is quoted in TĀv ad 8/301 (300cd-301ab).
