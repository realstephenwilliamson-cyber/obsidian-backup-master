# παῖς

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · noun

**Dodson:** a boy or girl child — (a) a male child, boy, (b) a male slave, servant; thus: a servant of God, especially as a title of the Messiah, (c) a female child, girl. (Strong's 3816; headword παῖς, παιδός, ὁ, ἡ)
