## Mūla

अनुत्तरादिह प्रोक्तं महाप्रचयसंज्ञितम् ।
पूर्णत्वादेव भेदानामस्यां सम्भावना न हि ॥ ^mula

anuttarādiha proktaṃ mahāpracayasaṃjñitam |
pūrṇatvādeva bhedānāmasyāṃ sambhāvanā na hi || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 14 - Chapter Ten, part 5#^pg493|p. 474]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=493>))
>
> This state (rūpa) (can only be attained) by the power of intuition (prasaṁkhyāna). For this reason, and because it is full (and perfect) and is Anuttara (the Absolute), it is called here the ‘Great Accumulation’ (mahāpracaya). (with 10.280cd–281ab)
>
> There can be no question of any divisions within it, because it is perfectly complete (pārṇpa). Thus, in order to refute (this possibility, the Maālinīvijaya) does not mention any specific division within it. (with 10.281cd–282ab)
