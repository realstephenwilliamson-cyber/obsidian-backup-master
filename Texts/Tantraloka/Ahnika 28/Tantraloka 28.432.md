## Mūla

तज्जुष्टमथ तस्याज्ञा प्राप्याश्नीयात्स्वयं शिशुः ।
ततः प्रपूजयेच्चक्रं यथाविभवसम्भवम् ॥ ^mula

tajjuṣṭamatha tasyājñā prāpyāśnīyātsvayaṃ śiśuḥ |
tataḥ prapūjayeccakraṃ yathāvibhavasambhavam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 07 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 5#^pg215|p. 198]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=215>))
>
> (The worship of the teacher) that was taught before was as an ancillary to the sacrifice. This one is to satisfy him (personally). Now, once the disciple has received his permission (to do so), he should himself eat the remnants of (the teacher’s) meal. He should then worship the assembly (cakra) as (lavishly) as his financial means permits. (with 28.431cd–432)
