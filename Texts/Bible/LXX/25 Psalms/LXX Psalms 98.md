# LXX Psalms 98

← [[LXX Psalms 97]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 99]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 099]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 099]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**98:1** A Psalm of David. The Lord reigns;—let the people rage; *it is he* that sits upon the cherubs, let the earth be moved.  ^98-1

**98:2** The Lord is great in Sion, and is high over all the people.  ^98-2

**98:3** Let them give thanks to thy great name; for it is terrible and holy.  ^98-3

**98:4** And the king's honour loves judgment; thou hast prepared equity, thou hast wrought judgment and justice in Jacob.[^1]  ^98-4

**98:5** Exalt ye the Lord our God, and worship *at* his footstool; for he is holy.  ^98-5

**98:6** Moses and Aaron among his priests, and Samuel among them that call upon his name; they called upon the Lord, and he heard them.  ^98-6

**98:7** He spoke to them in a pillar of cloud; they kept his testimonies, and the ordinances which he gave them.  ^98-7

**98:8** O Lord our God, thou heardest them; O God, thou becamest propitious to them, though thou didst take vengeance on all their devices.  ^98-8

**98:9** Exalt ye the Lord our God, and worship at his holy mountain; for the Lord our God is holy.  ^98-9

[^1]: 98:4 *Gr.* equities.
