## Mūla

आनन्दसंततिमहार्णवकर्णधारः
सद्दैशिकैरकवरात्मजवामनाथः ।
श्रीनाथसंततिमहाम्बरघर्मकान्तिः
श्रीभूतिराजतनयः स्वपितृप्रसादः ॥ ^mula

ānandasaṃtatimahārṇavakarṇadhāraḥ
saddaiśikairakavarātmajavāmanāthaḥ |
śrīnāthasaṃtatimahāmbaragharmakāntiḥ
śrībhūtirājatanayaḥ svapitṛprasādaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 11 - Chapter Thirty-Seven, part 1#^pg272|p. 254]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=272>))
>
> 1) Vāmanātha, the son of the true teacher, the excellent Erakavara, was the helmsman (of the boat on which he crossed) the ocean of the Lineage of Bliss (ānandasaṁtati). 2) The venerable son of Bhūtirāja, who was favoured (with initiation) by his father, (was for him) a radiantly beautiful a sun (that illumined) the great sky of the lineage (santāna) of Śrīnātha.
