# Psalms 141 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 140|← Psalms 140]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 142|Psalms 142 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 141|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**141:1** Of understanding for David. A prayer when he was in the cave. [1 Kings 24] ^141-1

**141:2** I cried to the Lord with my voice: with my voice I made supplication to the Lord. ^141-2

**141:3** In his sight I pour out my prayer, and before him I declare my trouble: ^141-3

**141:4** When my spirit failed me, then thou newest my paths. ^141-4

**141:5** I looked on my right hand, and beheld, and there was no one that would know me. Flight hath failed me: and there is no one that hath regard to my soul. ^141-5

**141:6** I cried to thee, O Lord: I said: Thou art my hope, my portion in the land of the living. ^141-6

**141:7** Attend to my supplication: for I am brought very low. Deliver me from my persecutors; for they are stronger than I. ^141-7

**141:8** Bring my soul out of prison, that I may praise thy name: the just wait for me, until thou reward me. ^141-8
