## Mūla

कृत्वा रहस्यं कथयेन्नान्यथा कामिके किल ।
अन्यतन्त्राभिषिक्तेऽपि रहस्यं न प्रकाशयेत् ॥ ^mula

kṛtvā rahasyaṃ kathayennānyathā kāmike kila |
anyatantrābhiṣikte'pi rahasyaṃ na prakāśayet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 13 - Chapter Twenty-Two#^pg308|p. 288]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=308>))
>
> Even (if he is) up to the (level of a) teacher, the secret (teaching) is only to be revealed to a follower of inferior doctrines once this rite of purification (saṁskriyā) has been performed — not otherwise. Indeed, (it is said) in the Kāmika: ‘even if one is consecrated (as a teacher) in another Tantra, the secret (teaching) should not be revealed (to him)’. (with 22.31cd–32)
