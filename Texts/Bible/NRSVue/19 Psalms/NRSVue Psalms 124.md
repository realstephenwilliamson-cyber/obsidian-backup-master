## Thanksgiving for Israel’s deliverance

*A Song of Ascents. Of David.*

**124:1** If it had not been the LORD who was on our side—let Israel now say— ^124-1

**124:2** if it had not been the LORD who was on our side, when our enemies attacked us, ^124-2

**124:3** then they would have swallowed us up alive, when their anger was kindled against us; ^124-3

**124:4** then the flood would have swept us away; the torrent would have gone over us; ^124-4

**124:5** then over us would have gone the raging waters. ^124-5

**124:6** Blessed be the LORD, who has not given us as prey to their teeth. ^124-6

**124:7** We have escaped like a bird from the snare of the hunters; the snare is broken, and we have escaped. ^124-7

**124:8** Our help is in the name of the LORD, who made heaven and earth. ^124-8
