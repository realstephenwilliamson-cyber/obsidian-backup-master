## Mūla

यौ श्वेतशृङ्गिणौ मेरोर्वामे मध्ये हिरण्मयम् ।
तयोर्नवकविस्तीर्णमायुश्चार्धत्रयोदश ॥ ^mula

yau śvetaśṛṅgiṇau merorvāme madhye hiraṇmayam |
tayornavakavistīrṇamāyuścārdhatrayodaśa || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 04 - Chapter Eight, part 2#^pg142|p. 124]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=142>))
>
> In the middle of the two, Śṛṅga (Peak) and Śveta (White) (chain of mountains) situated to the north of Meru, lies (the land called) Hiraṇyamaya (Made of Gold), that extends for nine thousand leagues. (The creatures there) live for thirteen and a half thousand (years).
