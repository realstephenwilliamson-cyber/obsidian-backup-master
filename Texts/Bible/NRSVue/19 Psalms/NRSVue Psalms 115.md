# Psalm 115 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 114|← Psalm 114]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 116|Psalm 116 →]]

## The impotence of idols and the greatness of God

**115:1** ^[Psalms 114–115 are a single psalm in the earliest witnesses]Not to us, O LORD, not to us, but to your name give glory, for the sake of your steadfast love and your faithfulness. ^115-1

**115:2** Why should the nations say, “Where is their God?” ^115-2

**115:3** Our God is in the heavens; he does whatever he pleases. ^115-3

**115:4** Their idols are silver and gold, the work of human hands. ^115-4

**115:5** They have mouths, but they do not speak; they have eyes, but they do not see. ^115-5

**115:6** They have ears, but they do not hear; they have noses, but they do not smell. ^115-6

**115:7** They have hands, but they do not feel; they have feet, but they do not walk; they make no sound in their throats. ^115-7

**115:8** Those who make them are like them; so are all who trust in them. ^115-8

**115:9** O Israel, trust in the LORD! He is their help and their shield. ^115-9

**115:10** O house of Aaron, trust in the LORD! He is their help and their shield. ^115-10

**115:11** You who fear the LORD, trust in the LORD! He is their help and their shield. ^115-11

**115:12** The LORD has been mindful of us; he will bless us; he will bless the house of Israel; he will bless the house of Aaron; ^115-12

**115:13** he will bless those who fear the LORD, both small and great. ^115-13

**115:14** May the LORD give you increase, both you and your children. ^115-14

**115:15** May you be blessed by the LORD, who made heaven and earth. ^115-15

**115:16** The heavens are the LORD’s heavens, but the earth he has given to human beings. ^115-16

**115:17** The dead do not praise the LORD, nor do any who go down into silence. ^115-17

**115:18** But we will bless the LORD from this time on and forevermore. Praise the LORD! ^115-18
