## Mūla

देहान्ते बुध्यते नो चेत् स्यादन्यादृक्प्रबोधनम् ।
तथाह्यन्त्यक्षणे ब्रह्मविद्याकर्णनसंस्कृतः ॥ ^mula

dehānte budhyate no cet syādanyādṛkprabodhanam |
tathāhyantyakṣaṇe brahmavidyākarṇanasaṃskṛtaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 06 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 4#^pg178|p. 161]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=178>))
>
> Thus, this has been established, namely, that whatever (tendency) awakens (at the moment) when the body comes to an end, it corresponds to what one has pondered on (bhāvyate) (most deeply in life). If that were not so, (any latent impression of) any other kind would be awakened (randomly). (with 28.360cd–361ab)
>
> When it is said that a person who has been purified by hearing the Brahmavidyā in the last moment (of his life) is liberated, (that is so) in accord with the strength of the preceding latent impressions (of his past actions). (with 28.361cd–362ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* भावनापेक्षामेव उपोद्बलयति--

> तथाह्यन्त्यक्षणे ब्रह्मविद्याकर्णनसंस्कृतः ॥
> मुच्यते जन्तुरित्युक्तं प्राक्संस्कारबलत्वतः ।

असद्विषयायां सदातनायां भावनायां असद्गतिरेव भवति, तदपहस्तनाय सद्विषयायां च भावनायां अबलवत्यामपि बलवत्त्वापादनार्थम्

"अचिन्त्या मन्त्रशक्तिर्वै परमेशमुखोद्भवा ।"

इत्याद्युक्त्या महाप्रभावाणां ब्रह्मविद्यानामन्त्ये क्षणे संस्कारार्थं भगवता उपदेशः कृतो येन अस्य मुक्तिरेव स्यात् ॥
