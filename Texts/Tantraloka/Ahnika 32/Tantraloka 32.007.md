## Mūla

तासां बहुत्वामुख्यत्वयोगाभ्यां नेह वर्णनम् ।
श्रीखेचरीसमाविष्टो यद्यत्स्थानं समाश्रयेत् ॥ ^mula

tāsāṃ bahutvāmukhyatvayogābhyāṃ neha varṇanam |
śrīkhecarīsamāviṣṭo yadyatsthānaṃ samāśrayet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 06 - Chapter Thirty-Two#^pg157|p. 139]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=157>))
>
> Other (mudrās) (described) in the Mālinīmata that are its auxiliaries, such as Padmamudrā, are not described here, because they are many and are (only of) secondary application. (with 32.6cd–7ab)
>
> Whatever place one who is penetrated by the venerable (goddess) Khecarī (the Skyfarer) (may choose) to take up as his dwelling (samāśrayet), that (act by itself) is enough to make the goddesses draw close. So why (indulge) in useless chatter (about other mudrās)? (with 32.7cd–8ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* ननु श्रीमालिनीमते पद्ममुद्रादिसाहचर्येणैव श्रीखेचरी अपि निर्दिष्टा, तत् सैव प्रधानेति तु कुतस्त्यम् ?--इत्याशङ्क्य आह--

> श्रीखेचरीसमाविष्टो यद्यत्स्थानं समाश्रयेत् ॥
> देवीसंनिधये तत्स्यादलं किं डम्बरैर्वृथा ।

अलमिति--पर्याप्तम् ॥
