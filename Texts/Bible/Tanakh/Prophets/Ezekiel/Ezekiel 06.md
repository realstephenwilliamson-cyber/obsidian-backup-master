## Chapter 6

**6:1** The word of the LORD came to me: ^6-1

**6:2** O mortal, turn your face toward the mountains of Israel and prophesy to them ^6-2

**6:3** and say: O mountains of Israel, hear the word of the Lord GOD. Thus said the Lord GOD to the mountains and the hills, to the streams and the valleys: See, I will bring a sword against you and destroy your shrines. ^6-3

**6:4** Your altars shall be wrecked and your incense stands smashed, and I will hurl down your slain in front of your fetishes. ^6-4

**6:5** I will cast the corpses of the people of Israel in front of their fetishes, and scatter your bones around your altars ^6-5

**6:6** in all your settlements. The towns shall be laid waste and the shrines shall be devastated. Thus your altars shall be laid waste and bear their punishment;[^1] your fetishes shall be smashed and annihilated, your incense stands cut down, and your handiwork wiped out; ^6-6

**6:7** and the slain shall fall in your midst. Then you shall know that I am the LORD. ^6-7

**6:8** Yet I will leave a remnant, in that some of you shall escape the sword among the nations and be scattered through the lands. ^6-8

**6:9** And those of you that escape will remember Me among the nations where they have been taken captive, how I was brokenhearted through[^2] their faithless hearts which turned away from Me, and through their eyes which lusted after their fetishes. And they shall loathe themselves for all the evil they committed and for all their abominable deeds. ^6-9

**6:10** Then they shall realize it was not without cause that I the LORD resolved to bring this evil upon them. ^6-10

**6:11** Thus said the Lord GOD: Strike your hands together and stamp your feet and cry: Aha! over all the vile abominations of the House of Israel who shall fall by the sword, by famine, and by pestilence. ^6-11

**6:12** He who is far away shall die of pestilence, and he who is near shall fall by the sword, and he who survives and is protected shall die of famine. Thus I will spend My fury upon them. ^6-12

**6:13** And you shall know that I am the LORD, when your slain lie among the fetishes round about their altars, on every high hill, on all the mountaintops, under every green tree, and under every leafy oak—wherever they presented pleasing odors to all their fetishes. ^6-13

**6:14** I will stretch out My hand against them, and lay the land waste and desolate in all their settlements, from the wilderness as far as Diblah;[^3] then they shall know that I am the LORD. ^6-14

[^1]: Targum and other ancient versions read “shall be devastated.”
[^2]: Emendation yields “how I broke.”
[^3]: A few Heb. mss. read “Riblah”; cf. 2 Kings 23.33; 25.6 ff.
