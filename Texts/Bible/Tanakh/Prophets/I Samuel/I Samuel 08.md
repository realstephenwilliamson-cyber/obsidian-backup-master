## Chapter 8

**8:1** When Samuel grew old, he appointed his sons judges over Israel. ^8-1

**8:2** The name of his first-born son was Joel, and his second son’s name was Abijah; they sat as judges in Beer-sheba. ^8-2

**8:3** But his sons did not follow in his ways; they were bent on gain, they accepted bribes, and they subverted justice. ^8-3

**8:4** All the elders of Israel assembled and came to Samuel at Ramah, ^8-4

**8:5** and they said to him, “You have grown old, and your sons have not followed your ways. Therefore appoint a king for us, to govern us like all other nations.” ^8-5

**8:6** Samuel was displeased that they said “Give us a king to govern us.” Samuel prayed to the LORD, ^8-6

**8:7** and the LORD replied to Samuel, “Heed the demand of the people in everything they say to you. For it is not you that they have rejected; it is Me they have rejected as their king. ^8-7

**8:8** Like everything else they have done ever since I brought them out of Egypt to this day—forsaking Me and worshiping other gods—so they are doing to you. ^8-8

**8:9** Heed their demand; but warn them solemnly, and tell them about the practices of any king who will rule over them.” ^8-9

**8:10** Samuel reported all the words of the LORD to the people, who were asking him for a king. ^8-10

**8:11** He said, “This will be the practice of the king who will rule over you: He will take your sons and appoint them as his charioteers and horsemen, and they will serve as outrunners for his chariots. ^8-11

**8:12** He will appoint them as his chiefs of thousands and of fifties; or they will have to plow his fields, reap his harvest, and make his weapons and the equipment for his chariots. ^8-12

**8:13** He will take your daughters as perfumers, cooks, and bakers. ^8-13

**8:14** He will seize your choice fields, vineyards, and olive groves, and give them to his courtiers. ^8-14

**8:15** He will take a tenth part of your grain and vintage and give it to his eunuchs and courtiers. ^8-15

**8:16** He will take your male and female slaves, your choice young men,[^1] and your asses, and put them to work for him. ^8-16

**8:17** He will take a tenth part of your flocks, and you shall become his slaves. ^8-17

**8:18** The day will come when you cry out because of the king whom you yourselves have chosen; and the LORD will not answer you on that day.” ^8-18

**8:19** But the people would not listen to Samuel’s warning. “No,” they said. “We must have a king over us, ^8-19

**8:20** that we may be like all the other nations: Let our king rule over us and go out at our head and fight our battles.” ^8-20

**8:21** When Samuel heard all that the people said, he reported it to the LORD. ^8-21

**8:22** And the LORD said to Samuel, “Heed their demands and appoint a king for them.” Samuel then said to the men of Israel, “All of you go home.” ^8-22

[^1]: Septuagint reads “cattle.”
