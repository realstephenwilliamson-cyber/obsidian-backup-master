## Mūla

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 06 - Chapter Eight, part 4#^pg200|p. 182]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=200>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 8.149cd–150ab.
>
> In this way, there are one million and five hundred thousand leagues from the earth up to the Pole Star. Mahāloka (the Great World), beyond the celestial world, extends for twenty-eight million and five hundred thousand leagues. 55, Munis and Siddhas such as Mārkaṇdeya reside there, divine beings who, established in glory (mahat), have completed their term of office, and other beings who (still) hold office there within the intermediate space of Mahāloka. (with 8.149–151ab)
