## Mūla

तस्मादवश्यं दातव्या गुरवे दक्षिणा पुनः ।
पूर्वं हि यागाङ्गतया प्रोक्तं तत्तुष्टये त्विदम् ॥ ^mula

tasmādavaśyaṃ dātavyā gurave dakṣiṇā punaḥ |
pūrvaṃ hi yāgāṅgatayā proktaṃ tattuṣṭaye tvidam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 07 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 5#^pg214|p. 197]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=214>))
>
> According to the Skandayāmala, he who does not worship (his) teacher is reborn a hundred times, competent (to practice), to then (finally) attain liberation. Thus (in order to avoid that), it is necessary to give the teacher a donation (dakṣinā) again. (with 28.430–431ab)
>
> (The worship of the teacher) that was taught before was as an ancillary to the sacrifice. This one is to satisfy him (personally). Now, once the disciple has received his permission (to do so), he should himself eat the remnants of (the teacher’s) meal. He should then worship the assembly (cakra) as (lavishly) as his financial means permits. (with 28.431cd–432)
