## Mūla

स्वतन्त्रत्वात्प्रमातोक्ता विचित्रो ज्ञेयभेदतः ।
सोमांशदाह्यवस्तूत्थवैचित्र्याभासबृंहितः ॥ ^mula

svatantratvātpramātoktā vicitro jñeyabhedataḥ |

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. II
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 02 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v02 08 - Chapter Three, part 5#^pg244|p. 228]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 02.pdf#page=244>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 3.124cd–125ab.
>
> Consciousness itself, which by virtue of its freedom is at one with (all) that is to be known (vijñeya), and so is (in all respects) independent, is said to be the subject that is wonderfully various (vicitra) due to the diversity (bheda) of the object of knowledge. (with 3.123cd–124ab)
>
> The Great Lord has called Fire Citrabhānu (the Many Varied Rayed One), because it is intensified (brṛṁita) by the manifestation of variety (vaicitryābhāsa) derived from the entities that are its fuel as aspects of the Moon (of objectivity). (with 3.124cd–125ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* अत एवाग्निरपि तथा -- इत्याह --

> सोमांशदाह्यवस्तूत्थवैचित्र्याभासबृंहितः ॥
> तत एवाग्निरुदितश्चित्रभानुर्महेशिना ।

ततः प्रमातुर्विचित्रत्वादेव, प्रभुणाग्निरपिचित्रा भोक्तृभोग्यादिरूपा भानवो यस्य स तत्तन्मानमेयादिविचित्रस्फार उक्तः, यतो मेयात्मनः सोमस्य कलादिरूपा अंशा एव दाह्यानि आत्मसात्कार्याणि वस्तूनि तेभ्यः तारतम्यादिना उत्थितेन वैचित्र्याभासेन बृंहितः वैशिष्ट्यमापादितः इत्यर्थः ॥ १२४ ॥
