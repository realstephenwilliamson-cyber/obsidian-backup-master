## Mūla

कस्यापि वाथ ज्ञानस्य प्राच्यः पक्षस्त्वसंभवी ।
न किञ्चिद्यस्य विज्ञानमुदपादि तथाविधः ॥ ^mula

kasyāpi vātha jñānasya prācyaḥ pakṣastvasaṃbhavī |
na kiñcidyasya vijñānamudapādi tathāvidhaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 05 - Chapter Thirteen, part 1#^pg153|p. 133]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=153>))
>
> (Again,) is what is called ignorance (just) an absence of knowledge? If so, does this absence precede (the arising of knowledge) (prāgabhāva) or is it (subsequent to its) destruction? In the first case, is it an absence of all (forms of) cognitive consciousness (saṁvid) or of (just) some kind of knowledge? The former alternative is impossible. (with 13.16–17ab)
>
> There is no individual soul of this kind in this beginningless world of transmigration that, like an (empty) boat, has ever been totally devoid of knowledge. What sense (anvaya) (then is there in affirming this)? (with 13.17cd–18ab)

> न किञ्चिद्यस्य विज्ञानमुदपादि तथाविधः ॥
