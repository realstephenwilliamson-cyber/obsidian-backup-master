# Proverbs 20 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Proverbs 19|← Proverbs 19]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Proverbs 21|Proverbs 21 →]]

**20:1** Wine is a mocker, strong drink a brawler, and whoever is led astray by it is not wise. ^20-1

**20:2** The dread anger of a king is like the growling of a lion; anyone who provokes him to anger forfeits life itself. ^20-2

**20:3** It is honorable to refrain from strife, but every fool is quick to quarrel. ^20-3

**20:4** The lazy person does not plow in season; harvest comes, and there is nothing to be found. ^20-4

**20:5** The purposes in the human mind are like deep water, but the intelligent will draw them out. ^20-5

**20:6** Many proclaim themselves loyal, but who can find one worthy of trust? ^20-6

**20:7** The righteous walk in integrity—happy are the children who follow them! ^20-7

**20:8** A king who sits on the throne of judgment winnows all evil with his eyes. ^20-8

**20:9** Who can say, “I have made my heart clean; I am pure from my sin”? ^20-9

**20:10** Diverse weights and diverse measures are both alike an abomination to the LORD. ^20-10

**20:11** Even children make themselves known by their acts, by whether what they do is pure and right. ^20-11

**20:12** The hearing ear and the seeing eye—the LORD has made them both. ^20-12

**20:13** Do not love sleep, or else you will come to poverty; open your eyes, and you will have plenty of bread. ^20-13

**20:14** “Bad, bad,” says the buyer, then goes away and boasts. ^20-14

**20:15** There is gold and abundance of costly stones, but the lips informed by knowledge are a precious jewel. ^20-15

**20:16** Take the garment of one who has given surety for a stranger; seize the pledge given as surety for foreigners. ^20-16

**20:17** Bread gained by deceit is sweet, but afterward the mouth will be full of gravel. ^20-17

**20:18** Plans are established by taking advice; wage war by following wise guidance. ^20-18

**20:19** A gossip reveals secrets; therefore do not associate with a babbler. ^20-19

**20:20** If you curse father or mother, your lamp will go out in utter darkness. ^20-20

**20:21** An estate quickly acquired in the beginning will not be blessed in the end. ^20-21

**20:22** Do not say, “I will repay evil”; wait for the LORD, and he will help you. ^20-22

**20:23** Differing weights are an abomination to the LORD, and false scales are not good. ^20-23

**20:24** All our steps are ordered by the LORD; how then can we understand our own ways? ^20-24

**20:25** It is a snare for one to say rashly, “It is holy,” and begin to reflect only after making a vow. ^20-25

**20:26** A wise king winnows the wicked and drives the wheel over them. ^20-26

**20:27** The human spirit is the lamp of the LORD, searching every inmost part. ^20-27

**20:28** Loyalty and faithfulness preserve the king, and his throne is upheld by righteousness.^[Gk: Heb *loyalty*] ^20-28

**20:29** The glory of youths is their strength, but the beauty of the aged is their gray hair. ^20-29

**20:30** Blows that wound cleanse away evil; beatings make clean the innermost parts. ^20-30
