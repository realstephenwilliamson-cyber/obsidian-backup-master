# 2 Corinthians 6 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue 2 Corinthians 05|← 2 Corinthians 5]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue 2 Corinthians 07|2 Corinthians 7 →]]

Compare: [[2 Corinthians 06|BSB and SBLGNT]] · [[DRB 2 Corinthians 06|Douay-Rheims]] · [[JANT 2 Corinthians 06|JANT notes]]

**6:1** As we work together with him,^[Gk *As we work together*] we entreat you also not to accept the grace of God in vain. ^6-1

**6:2** For he says, “At an acceptable time I have listened to you, and on a day of salvation I have helped you.” Look, now is the acceptable time; look, now is the day of salvation! ^6-2

**6:3** We are putting no obstacle in anyone’s way, so that no fault may be found with our ministry, ^6-3

**6:4** but as servants of God we have commended ourselves in every way: in great endurance, afflictions, hardships, calamities, ^6-4

**6:5** beatings, imprisonments, riots, labors, sleepless nights, hunger; ^6-5

**6:6** in purity, knowledge, patience, kindness, holiness of spirit, genuine love, ^6-6

**6:7** truthful speech, and the power of God; with the weapons of righteousness for the right hand and for the left; ^6-7

**6:8** in honor and dishonor, in ill repute and good repute. We are treated as impostors and yet are true, ^6-8

**6:9** as unknown and yet are well known, as dying and look—we are alive, as punished and yet not killed, ^6-9

**6:10** as sorrowful yet always rejoicing, as poor yet making many rich, as having nothing and yet possessing everything. ^6-10

**6:11** We have spoken frankly to you Corinthians; our heart is wide open to you. ^6-11

**6:12** There is no restriction in our affections but only in yours. ^6-12

**6:13** In return—I speak as to children—open wide your hearts also. ^6-13

## The temple of the living God

**6:14** Do not be mismatched with unbelievers. For what do righteousness and lawlessness have in common? Or what partnership is there between light and darkness? ^6-14

**6:15** What agreement does Christ have with Beliar? Or what does a believer share with an unbeliever? ^6-15

**6:16** What agreement has the temple of God with idols? For we^[Other ancient authorities read *you*] are the temple of the living God, as God said, “I will live in them^[Or *in their midst*] and walk among them, and I will be their God, and they shall be my people. ^6-16

**6:17** Therefore come out from them, and be separate from them, says the Lord, and touch nothing unclean; then I will welcome you, ^6-17

**6:18** and I will be your father, and you shall be my sons and daughters, says the Lord Almighty.” ^6-18
