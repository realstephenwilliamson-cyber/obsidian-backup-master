## Mūla

ध्यानादि तु फलात्साध्यमिति सिद्धामतोदितम् ।
तुलाशुद्धिपरीक्षां वा कुर्यात्प्रत्यययोगिनीम् ॥ ^mula

dhyānādi tu phalātsādhyamiti siddhāmatoditam |
tulāśuddhiparīkṣāṃ vā kuryātpratyayayoginīm || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 11 - Chapter Twenty#^pg256|p. 236]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=256>))
>
> Knowledge is self-validating and so does not require another (ulterior) result (to establish its validity). It is said in the Siddhāmata (that, on the contrary, the efficacity of) visualization (dhyāna) and the like can (only) be established by its fruit. (with 20.10cd–11ab)
>
> Or else (the teacher may impart the initiation) the Supreme Lord has taught in the Tantrasadbhāva that examines (the disciple’s degree of) purification by (weighing him on) scales (and so) possesses an (outer) sign of (its) accomplishment, (with 20.11cd–12ab)
>
> This same (kind of initiation) has been mentioned by the Supreme Lord in the Śrīpūrvaśāstra (Mālinīvijayottara) also, where he speaks of the gradation of the (descent of the) power (of grace), as indicated by (the signs of its attainment). They are joy, an upward leap, trembling, sleep, and the rolling of the eyes (ghūrni), as the fifth. (with 20.11cd–13)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* एवमधिकारिपरीक्षामभिधाय तुलाविधिमभिधत्ते--

> तुलाशुद्धिपरीक्षां वा कुर्यात्प्रत्यययोगिनीम् ॥
> यथा श्रीतन्त्रसद्भावे कथिता परमेशिना ।
