# Numbers 29 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Numbers 28|← Numbers 28]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Numbers 30|Numbers 30 →]]

**29:1** The first day also of the seventh month shall be venerable and holy unto you; you shall do no servile work therein, because it is the day of the sounding and of trumpets. ^29-1

**29:2** And you shall offer a holocaust for a most sweet odour to the Lord, one calf of the herd, one ram and seven lambs of a year old, without blemish. ^29-2

**29:3** And for their sacrifices, three tenths of flour tempered with oil to every calf, two tenths to a ram, ^29-3

**29:4** One tenth to a lamb, which in all are seven lambs: ^29-4

**29:5** And a buck goat for sin, which is offered for the expiation of the people, ^29-5

**29:6** Besides the holocaust of the first day of the month with the sacrifices thereof, and the perpetual holocaust with the accustomed libations. With the same ceremonies you shall offer a burnt sacrifice for a most sweet odour to the Lord. ^29-6

**29:7** The tenth day also of this seventh month shall be holy and venerable unto you, and you shall afflict your souls: you shall do no servile work therein. ^29-7

**29:8** And you shall offer a holocaust to the Lord for a most sweet odour, one calf of the herd, one ram, and seven lambs of a year old, without blemish: ^29-8

**29:9** And for their sacrifices, three tenths of flour tempered with oil to every calf, two tenths to a ram, ^29-9

**29:10** The tenth of a tenth to every lamb, which are in all seven lambs: ^29-10

**29:11** And a buck goat for sin, besides the things that are wont to be offered for sin, for expiation, and for the perpetual holocaust with their sacrifice and libations. ^29-11

**29:12** And on the fifteenth day of the seventh month, which shall be unto you holy and venerable, you shall do no servile work, but shall celebrate a solemnity to the Lord seven days. ^29-12

**29:13** And you shall offer a holocaust for a most sweet odour to the Lord, thirteen calves of the herd, two rams, and fourteen lambs of a year old, without blemish: ^29-13

**29:14** And for their libations three tenths of flour tempered with oil to every calf, being in all thirteen calves: and two tenths to each ram, being two rams, ^29-14

**29:15** And the tenth of a tenth to every lamb, being in all fourteen lambs: ^29-15

**29:16** And a buck goat for sin, besides the perpetual holocaust, and the sacrifice and the libation thereof. ^29-16

**29:17** On the second day you shall offer twelve calves of the herd, two rams and fourteen lambs of a year old, without blemish: ^29-17

**29:18** And the sacrifices and the libations for every one, for the calves and for the rams and for the lambs you shall duly celebrate: ^29-18

**29:19** And a buck goat for a sin offering besides the perpetual holocaust, and the sacrifice and the libation thereof. ^29-19

**29:20** The third day you shall offer eleven calves, two rams, and fourteen lambs of a year old, without blemish: ^29-20

**29:21** And the sacrifices and the libations of every one for the calves and for the rams and for the lambs you shall offer according to the rite: ^29-21

**29:22** And a buck goat for sin, besides the perpetual holocaust, and the sacrifice, and the libation thereof. ^29-22

**29:23** The fourth day you shall offer tell calves, two rams, and fourteen lambs of a year old, without blemish: ^29-23

**29:24** And the sacrifices and the libations of every one for the calves and for the rams and for the lambs you shall celebrate in right manner: ^29-24

**29:25** And a buck goat for sin, besides the perpetual holocaust, and the sacrifice and the libation thereof. ^29-25

**29:26** The fifth day you shall offer nine calves, two rams, and fourteen lambs of a year old, without blemish: ^29-26

**29:27** And the sacrifices and the libations of every one for the calves and for the rams and for the lambs you shall celebrate according to the rite: ^29-27

**29:28** And a buck goat for sin, besides the perpetual holocaust, and the sacrifice and the libation thereof. ^29-28

**29:29** The sixth day you shall offer eight calves, two rams, and fourteen lambs of a year old, without blemish: ^29-29

**29:30** And the sacrifices and the libations of every one for the calves and for the rams and for the lambs you shall celebrate according to the rite: ^29-30

**29:31** And a buck goat for sin, besides the perpetual holocaust, and the sacrifice and the libation thereof. ^29-31

**29:32** The seventh day you shall offer seven calves and two rams, and fourteen lambs of a year old, without blemish: ^29-32

**29:33** And the sacrifices and the libations of every one for the calves and for the rams and for the lambs you shall celebrate according to the rite: ^29-33

**29:34** And a buck goat for sin, besides the perpetual holocaust, and the sacrifice and the libation thereof. ^29-34

**29:35** On the eighth day, which is moat solemn, you shall do no servile work: ^29-35

**29:36** But you shall offer a holocaust for a most sweet odour to the Lord, one calf, one ram, and seven lambs of a year old, without blemish: ^29-36

**29:37** And the sacrifices and the libations of every one for the calves and for the rams and for the lambs you shall celebrate according to the rite: ^29-37

**29:38** And a buck goat for sin, besides the perpetual holocaust, and the sacrifice and the libation thereof. ^29-38

**29:39** These things shall you offer to the Lord in your solemnities: besides your vows and voluntary oblations for holocaust, for sacrifice, for libation, and for victims of peace offerings. ^29-39
