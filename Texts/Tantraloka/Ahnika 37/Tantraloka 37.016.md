## Mūla

तच्च पञ्चविधं प्रोक्तं शक्तिवैचित्र्यचित्रितम् ।
पञ्चस्रोत इति प्रोक्तं श्रीमच्छ्रीकण्ठशासनम् ॥ ^mula

tacca pañcavidhaṃ proktaṃ śaktivaicitryacitritam |
pañcasrota iti proktaṃ śrīmacchrīkaṇṭhaśāsanam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 11 - Chapter Thirty-Seven, part 1#^pg250|p. 232]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=250>))
>
> Again, that (current), diversified by the variety of (Śiva’s five) powers, is said to be of five kinds. The teaching (śāsana) of the venerable Śrīkaṇṭha is called the Five Currents (srotas) (of scriptures). These five currents (combining variously are) ten and eighteen-fold, (as the common scriptures of the Siddhānta) and then (above them) also is the most excellent and complete (alam) (current) called Bhairava, which is divided into sixty-four (scriptures). (with 37.16–17)
