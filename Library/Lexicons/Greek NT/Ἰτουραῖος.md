# Ἰτουραῖος

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · adjective

**Dodson:** Ituraean — Ituraean, an adjective applied to a district also called Trachonitis, about 60 miles east of the Sea of Galilee, and partly inhabited by the nomad tribe called Ituraeans. (Strong's 2484; headword Ἰτουραῖος, α, ον)
