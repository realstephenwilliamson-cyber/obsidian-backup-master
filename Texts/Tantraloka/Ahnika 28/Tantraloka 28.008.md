## Mūla

शास्त्रव्याख्यापुरामध्यावसानानि क्रमोदयः ।
देवतादर्शनं स्वाप्नमाज्ञा समयनिष्कृतिः ॥ ^mula

śāstravyākhyāpurāmadhyāvasānāni kramodayaḥ |
devatādarśanaṃ svāpnamājñā samayaniṣkṛtiḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 03 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 1#^pg24|p. 7]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=24>))
>
> The venerable Tantrasāāra has defined (the following events), into twenty-three kinds, as ‘occasional’, that are associated with special rites of worship. 1) The acquisition of (spiritual) knowledge, 2) the acquisition of the scriptures, 3) (a visit) by the teacher and 4) by his brothers and 5) family, 6) (the teacher’s) birthday, 7) (the day of the teacher’s) consecration and 8) that of his union (abheda) (with Śiva when he died), 9) (the day of) one’s own birth, 10) a gathering for a festival, 11) offerings to the dead (śrāddha), 12) the countering of a calamity, 13) rejoicing, 14) the sight of a portent, 15) a meeting with the Yoginīs, 16) a reunion with one who belong to one’s own spiritual lineage and the like, 17) the beginning, 18) middle and 19) end of the explanation of scripture, 20) the invocation of the liturgy (kramodaya), 21) a vision of a deity in a dream, 22) the authorization (ājñā) (received during one’s own ordination as a teacher), 23) as reparation for an infringement of the rules. (with 28.6–9)
