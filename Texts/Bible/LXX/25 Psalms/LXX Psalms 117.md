# LXX Psalms 117

← [[LXX Psalms 116]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 118]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 118]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 118]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**117:1** Alleluia. Give thanks to the Lord; for *he is* good: for his mercy *endures* for ever.  ^117-1

**117:2** Let now the house of Israel say, that *he is* good: for his mercy *endures* for ever.  ^117-2

**117:3** Let now the house of Aaron say, that *he is* good: for his mercy *endures* for ever.  ^117-3

**117:4** Let now all that fear the Lord say, that *he is* good: for his mercy *endures* for ever.  ^117-4

**117:5** I called on the Lord out of affliction: and he hearkened to me, *so as to bring me* into a wide place.  ^117-5

**117:6** The Lord is my helper; and I will not fear what man shall do to me.  ^117-6

**117:7** The Lord is my helper; and I shall see *my desire* upon mine enemies.  ^117-7

**117:8** *It is* better to trust in the Lord than to trust in man.  ^117-8

**117:9** *It is* better to hope in the Lord, than to hope in princes.  ^117-9

**117:10** All nations compassed me about: but in the name of the Lord I repulsed them.  ^117-10

**117:11** They completely compassed me about: but in the name of the Lord I repulsed them.  ^117-11

**117:12** They compassed me about as bees *do* a honeycomb, and they burst into flame as fire among thorns: but in the name of the Lord I repulsed them.  ^117-12

**117:13** I was thrust, and sorely shaken, that I might fall: but the Lord helped me.  ^117-13

**117:14** The Lord is my strength and my song, and is become my salvation.  ^117-14

**117:15** The voice of exultation and salvation is in the tabernacles of the righteous: the right hand of the Lord has wrought mightily.  ^117-15

**117:16** The right hand of the Lord has exalted me: the right hand of the Lord has wrought powerfully.  ^117-16

**117:17** I shall not die, but live, and recount the works of the Lord.  ^117-17

**117:18** The Lord has chastened me sore: but he has not given me up to death.  ^117-18

**117:19** Open to me the gates of righteousness: I will go into them, and give praise to the Lord.  ^117-19

**117:20** This is the gate of the Lord: the righteous shall enter by it.  ^117-20

**117:21** I will give thanks to thee; because thou hast heard me, and art become my salvation.  ^117-21

**117:22** The stone which the builders rejected, the same is become the head of the corner.  ^117-22

**117:23** This has been done of the Lord; and it is wonderful in our eyes.  ^117-23

**117:24** This is the day which the Lord has made: let us exult and rejoice in it.  ^117-24

**117:25** O Lord, save now: O Lord, send now prosperity.  ^117-25

**117:26** Blessed is he that comes in the name of the Lord: we have blessed you out of the house of the Lord.  ^117-26

**117:27** God is the Lord, and he has shined upon us: celebrate the feast with thick *branches, binding the victims* even to the horns of the altar.[^1]  ^117-27

**117:28** Thou art my God, and I will give thee thanks: thou art my God, and I will exalt thee. I will give thanks to thee, for thou hast heard me, and art become my salvation.  ^117-28

**117:29** Give thanks to the Lord; for he is good: for his mercy *endures* for ever.  ^117-29

[^1]: 117:27 Possibly, the multitude, *q. d.* cœtu frequenti.
