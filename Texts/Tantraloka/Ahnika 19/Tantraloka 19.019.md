## Mūla

द्वादशान्ते ततः कृत्वा बिन्दुयुग्मगते क्षिपेत् ।
निर्लक्ष्ये वा परे धाम्नि संयुक्तः परमेश्वरः ॥ ^mula

dvādaśānte tataḥ kṛtvā binduyugmagate kṣipet |
nirlakṣye vā pare dhāmni saṃyuktaḥ parameśvaraḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 10 - Chapter Nineteen#^pg236|p. 216]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=236>))
>
> The Lord has said in the Gahvara: ‘After having meditated on the Trident of Knowledge intensely energized (with Mantras) (saṁdīpta) and blazing with three flaming wheels, (the teacher) should by means of this repeatedly pierce (vedhana), awaken (bodhana), spin (bhrama), ignite (dīpana), strike (tāḍana), impel (toda), and agitate (calana) the Bulb (kanda) and the other wheels, especially the Heart. Then having placed it in the End of the Twelve, in which the two Points (of exhalation and inhalation) have ceased, he should cast (an offering into the fire). (Thus, he is) the Supreme Lord and is conjoined into the Ineffable (nirlakṣya) or the Supreme Abode. (The teacher) should not perform any purifying rite (saṁskāra) for him.’ Of what use are the offerings to the ancestors (śrādha) and the like for one who is (already) sanctified (bhāvita) and is complete (and perfect)?’ (with 19.17–20)
