# Psalm 100

← [[BCP Psalm 099]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 101]] →

*Jubilate Deo* · Book Four · Nineteenth Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 100]]

**100:1** Be joyful in the LORD, all you lands; \*  
serve the LORD with gladness  
and come before his presence with a song. ^100-1

**100:2** Know this: The LORD himself is God; \*  
he himself has made us, and we are his;  
we are his people and the sheep of his pasture. ^100-2

[p. 730] ^pg730

**100:3** Enter his gates with thanksgiving;  
go into his courts with praise; \*  
give thanks to him and call upon his Name. ^100-3

**100:4** For the LORD is good;  
his mercy is everlasting; \*  
and his faithfulness endures from age to age. ^100-4
