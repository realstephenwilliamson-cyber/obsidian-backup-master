## Mūla

मृतास्ते तत्र तद्रुद्रसयुक्त्वं यान्ति कोविदाः ।
तेषां सयुक्त्वं यातानामपि संस्कारतो निजात् ॥ ^mula

mṛtāste tatra tadrudrasayuktvaṃ yānti kovidāḥ |
teṣāṃ sayuktvaṃ yātānāmapi saṃskārato nijāt || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 05 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 3#^pg132|p. 115]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=132>))
>
> Those skilful (learned ones who) abide in the knowledge of that (reality level), mantras, (disciplined) observance and the rest, enter into a state of union (sayuktva) with that (corresponding) Rudra when they die. (with 28.264cd–265ab)
>
> Although they have (all) entered (equally) into (this same) state of union, their descent (into the world) as partial incarnations (aṁśa)) (of Rudra) is diverse, as befits the residual traces (of their past Karma) (sanskāra). (with 28.265cd–266ab)
