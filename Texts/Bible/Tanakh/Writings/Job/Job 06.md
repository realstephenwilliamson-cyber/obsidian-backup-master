## Chapter 6

**6:1** Then Job said in reply: ^6-1

**6:2** If my anguish were weighed,  
My full calamity laid on the scales, ^6-2

**6:3** It would be heavier than the sand of the sea;  
That is why I spoke recklessly.[^1] ^6-3

**6:4** For the arrows of the Almighty are in me;  
My spirit absorbs their poison;  
God’s terrors are arrayed against me. ^6-4

**6:5** Does a wild ass bray when he has grass?  
Does a bull bellow over his fodder? ^6-5

**6:6** Can what is tasteless be eaten without salt?  
Does mallow juice[^2] have any flavor? ^6-6

**6:7** I refuse to touch them;  
They are like food when I am sick. ^6-7

**6:8** Would that my request were granted,  
That God gave me what I wished for; ^6-8

**6:9** Would that God consented to crush me,  
Loosed His hand and cut me off. ^6-9

**6:10** Then this would be my consolation,  
As I writhed in unsparing[^3] pains:  
That I did not suppress my words against the Holy One.[^4] ^6-10

**6:11** What strength have I, that I should endure?  
How long have I to live, that I should be patient? ^6-11

**6:12** Is my strength the strength of rock?  
Is my flesh bronze? ^6-12

**6:13** Truly, I cannot help myself;  
I have been deprived of resourcefulness. ^6-13

**6:14** A friend owes loyalty to one who fails,  
Though he forsakes the fear of the Almighty;[^5] ^6-14

**6:15** My comrades are fickle, like a wadi,  
Like a bed on which streams once ran. ^6-15

**6:16** They are dark with ice;  
Snow obscures them;[^6] ^6-16

**6:17** But when they thaw, they vanish;  
In the heat, they disappear where they are. ^6-17

**6:18** Their course twists and turns;  
They run into the desert and perish. ^6-18

**6:19** Caravans from Tema look to them;  
Processions from Sheba count on them. ^6-19

**6:20** They are disappointed in their hopes;  
When they reach the place, they stand aghast. ^6-20

**6:21** So you are as nothing:[^7]  
At the sight of misfortune, you take fright. ^6-21

**6:22** Did I say to you, “I need your gift;  
Pay a bribe for me out of your wealth; ^6-22

**6:23** Deliver me from the clutches of my enemy;  
Redeem me from violent men”? ^6-23

**6:24** Teach me; I shall be silent;  
Tell me where I am wrong. ^6-24

**6:25** How trenchant honest words are;[^8]  
But what sort of reproof comes from you? ^6-25

**6:26** Do you devise words of reproof,  
But count a hopeless man’s words as wind? ^6-26

**6:27** You would even cast lots over an orphan,  
Or barter away your friend. ^6-27

**6:28** Now be so good as to face me;  
I will not lie to your face. ^6-28

**6:29** Relent! Let there not be injustice;  
Relent! I am still in the right. ^6-29

**6:30** Is injustice on my tongue?  
Can my palate not discern evil? ^6-30

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain; others “deny the words of the Holy One.”
[^5]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^6]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^7]: Following kethib, with Targum; meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^8]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
