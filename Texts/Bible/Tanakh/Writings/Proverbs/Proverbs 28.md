# Proverbs 28

← [[Proverbs 27]] · [[Tanakh]] · [[Proverbs 29]] →

Compare: [[NRSVue Proverbs 28|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 28

**28:1** The wicked flee though no one gives chase,  
But the righteous are as confident as a lion. ^28-1

**28:2** When there is rebellion in the land, many are its rulers;  
But with a man who has understanding and knowledge, stability will last.[^1] ^28-2

**28:3** A poor man who withholds what is due to the wretched  
Is like a destructive rain that leaves no food. ^28-3

**28:4** Those who forsake instruction praise the wicked,  
But those who heed instruction fight them. ^28-4

**28:5** Evil men cannot discern judgment,  
But those who seek the LORD discern all things. ^28-5

**28:6** Better is a poor man who lives blamelessly  
Than a rich man whose ways are crooked. ^28-6

**28:7** An intelligent son heeds instruction,  
But he who keeps company with gluttons disgraces his father. ^28-7

**28:8** He who increases his wealth by loans at discount or interest  
Amasses it for one who is generous to the poor. ^28-8

**28:9** He who turns a deaf ear to instruction—  
His prayer is an abomination. ^28-9

**28:10** He who misleads the upright into an evil course  
Will fall into his own pit,  
But the blameless will prosper. ^28-10

**28:11** A rich man is clever in his own eyes,  
But a perceptive poor man can see through him. ^28-11

**28:12** When the righteous exult there is great glory,  
But when the wicked rise up men make themselves scarce. ^28-12

**28:13** He who covers up his faults will not succeed;  
He who confesses and gives them up will find mercy. ^28-13

**28:14** Happy is the man who is anxious always,  
But he who hardens his heart falls into misfortune. ^28-14

**28:15** A roaring lion and a prowling bear  
Is a wicked man ruling a helpless people. ^28-15

**28:16** A prince who lacks understanding is very oppressive;  
He who spurns ill-gotten gains will live long. ^28-16

**28:17** A man oppressed by bloodguilt will flee to a pit;  
Let none give him support. ^28-17

**28:18** He who lives blamelessly will be delivered,  
But he who is crooked in his ways will fall all at once. ^28-18

**28:19** He who tills his land will have food in plenty,  
But he who pursues vanities will have poverty in plenty. ^28-19

**28:20** A dependable man will receive many blessings,  
But one in a hurry to get rich will not go unpunished. ^28-20

**28:21** To be partial is not right;  
A man may do wrong for a piece of bread. ^28-21

**28:22** A miserly man runs after wealth;  
He does not realize that loss will overtake it. ^28-22

**28:23** He who reproves a man will in the end  
Find more favor than he who flatters him. ^28-23

**28:24** He who robs his father and mother and says, “It is no offense,”  
Is a companion to vandals. ^28-24

**28:25** A greedy man provokes quarrels,  
But he who trusts the LORD shall enjoy prosperity. ^28-25

**28:26** He who trusts his own instinct is a dullard,  
But he who lives by wisdom shall escape. ^28-26

**28:27** He who gives to the poor will not be in want,  
But he who shuts his eyes will be roundly cursed. ^28-27

**28:28** When the wicked rise up, men go into hiding,  
But when they perish the righteous increase. ^28-28

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
