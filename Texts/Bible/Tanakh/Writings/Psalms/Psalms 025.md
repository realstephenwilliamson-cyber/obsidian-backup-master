## Chapter 25

**25:1** Of David.  
O LORD, I set my hope on You; ^25-1

**25:2** my God, in You I trust;  
may I not be disappointed,  
may my enemies not exult over me. ^25-2

**25:3** O let none who look to You be disappointed;  
let the faithless be disappointed, empty-handed. ^25-3

**25:4** Let me know Your paths, O LORD;  
teach me Your ways; ^25-4

**25:5** guide me in Your true way and teach me,  
for You are God, my deliverer;  
it is You I look to at all times. ^25-5

**25:6** O LORD, be mindful of Your compassion  
and Your faithfulness;  
they are old as time. ^25-6

**25:7** Be not mindful of my youthful sins and transgressions;  
in keeping with Your faithfulness consider what is in my favor,  
as befits Your goodness, O LORD. ^25-7

**25:8** Good and upright is the LORD;  
therefore He shows sinners the way. ^25-8

**25:9** He guides the lowly in the right path,  
and teaches the lowly His way. ^25-9

**25:10** All the LORD’s paths are steadfast love  
for those who keep the decrees of His covenant. ^25-10

**25:11** As befits Your name, O LORD,  
pardon my iniquity though it be great. ^25-11

**25:12** Whoever fears the LORD,  
he shall be shown what path to choose. ^25-12

**25:13** He shall live a happy life,  
and his children shall inherit the land. ^25-13

**25:14** The counsel[^1] of the LORD is for those who fear Him;  
to them He makes known His covenant. ^25-14

**25:15** My eyes are ever toward the LORD,  
for He will loose my feet from the net. ^25-15

**25:16** Turn to me, have mercy on me,  
for I am alone and afflicted. ^25-16

**25:17** My deep distress[^2] increases;  
deliver me from my straits. ^25-17

**25:18** Look at my affliction and suffering,  
and forgive all my sins. ^25-18

**25:19** See how numerous my enemies are,  
and how unjustly they hate me! ^25-19

**25:20** Protect me and save me;  
let me not be disappointed,  
for I have sought refuge in You. ^25-20

**25:21** May integrity and uprightness watch over me,  
for I look to You. ^25-21

**25:22** O God, redeem Israel  
from all its distress. ^25-22

[^1]: Or “secret.”
[^2]: Lit. “The distress of my heart.”
