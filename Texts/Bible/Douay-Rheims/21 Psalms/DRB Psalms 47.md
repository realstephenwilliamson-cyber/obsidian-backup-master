# Psalms 47 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 46|← Psalms 46]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 48|Psalms 48 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 47|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**47:1** A psalm of a canticle, for the sons of Core, on the second day of the week. ^47-1

**47:2** Great is the Lord, and exceedingly to be praised in the city of our God, in his holy mountain. ^47-2

**47:3** With the joy of the whole earth is mount Sion founded, on the sides of the north, the city of the great king. ^47-3

**47:4** In her houses shall God be known, when he shall protect her. ^47-4

**47:5** For behold the kings of the earth assembled themselves: they gathered together. ^47-5

**47:6** So they saw, and they wondered, they were troubled, they were moved: ^47-6

**47:7** Trembling took hold of them. There were pains as of a woman in labour. ^47-7

**47:8** With a vehement wind thou shalt break in pieces the ships of Tharsis. ^47-8

**47:9** As we have heard, so have we seen, in the city of the Lord of hosts, in the city of our God: God hath founded it for ever. ^47-9

**47:10** We have received thy mercy, O God, in the midst of thy temple. ^47-10

**47:11** According to thy name, O God, so also is thy praise unto the ends of the earth: thy right hand is full of justice. ^47-11

**47:12** Let mount Sion rejoice, and the daughters of Juda be glad; because of thy judgments, O Lord. ^47-12

**47:13** Surround Sion, and encompass her: tell ye in her towers. ^47-13

**47:14** Set your hearts on her strength; and distribute her houses, that ye may relate it in another generation. ^47-14

**47:15** For this is God, our God unto eternity, and for ever and ever: he shall rule us for evermore. ^47-15
