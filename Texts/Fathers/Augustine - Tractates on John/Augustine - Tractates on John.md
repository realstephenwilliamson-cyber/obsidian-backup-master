# Augustine — Tractates on the Gospel of John

> [!external] Source
> Augustine of Hippo, *Tractates on the Gospel of John*, tr. Rev. John Gibb (Tractates 1–37) and Rev. James Innes (Tractates 38–124), in *Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers*, Series 1, Vol. 7, ed. Philip Schaff (1888). Public domain. <https://www.ccel.org/ccel/schaff/npnf107.iii.ii.html>

Augustine preached these 124 tractates on the Gospel of John, covering the entire Gospel sequentially. They are among the most significant patristic commentaries on John.
