# Proverbs 6 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Proverbs 05|← Proverbs 5]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Proverbs 07|Proverbs 7 →]]

## Practical admonitions

**6:1** My child, if you have given your pledge to your neighbor, if you have bound yourself to another,^[Or *a stranger*] ^6-1

**6:2** you are snared by the utterance of your lips,^[Cn Compare Gk Syr: Heb *the words of your mouth*] caught by the words of your mouth. ^6-2

**6:3** So do this, my child, and save yourself, for you have come into your neighbor’s power: go, hurry,^[Or *humble yourself*] and plead with your neighbor. ^6-3

**6:4** Give your eyes no sleep and your eyelids no slumber; ^6-4

**6:5** save yourself like a gazelle from the hunter,^[Cn: Heb *from the hand*] like a bird from the hand of the fowler. ^6-5

**6:6** Go to the ant, you lazybones; consider its ways and be wise. ^6-6

**6:7** Without having any chief or officer or ruler, ^6-7

**6:8** it prepares its food in summer and gathers its sustenance in harvest. ^6-8

**6:9** How long will you lie there, O lazybones? When will you rise from your sleep? ^6-9

**6:10** A little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands to rest, ^6-10

**6:11** and poverty will come upon you like a robber, and want, like an armed warrior. ^6-11

**6:12** A scoundrel and a villain goes around with crooked speech, ^6-12

**6:13** winking the eyes, shuffling the feet, pointing the fingers, ^6-13

**6:14** with perverted mind devising evil, continually sowing discord; ^6-14

**6:15** on such a one calamity will descend suddenly, in a moment, damage beyond repair. ^6-15

**6:16** There are six things that the LORD hates, seven that are an abomination to him: ^6-16

**6:17** haughty eyes, a lying tongue, and hands that shed innocent blood, ^6-17

**6:18** a heart that devises wicked plans, feet that hurry to run to evil, ^6-18

**6:19** a lying witness who testifies falsely, and one who sows discord in a family. ^6-19

**6:20** My child, keep your father’s commandment, and do not forsake your mother’s teaching. ^6-20

**6:21** Bind them upon your heart always; tie them around your neck. ^6-21

**6:22** When you walk, they^[Heb *it*] will lead you; when you lie down, they^[Heb *it*] will watch over you; and when you awake, they^[Heb *it*] will talk with you. ^6-22

**6:23** For the commandment is a lamp and the teaching a light, and the reproofs of discipline are the way of life, ^6-23

**6:24** to preserve you from the wife of another,^[Gk: MT *the evil woman*] from the smooth tongue of the adulteress.^[Heb *alien woman*] ^6-24

**6:25** Do not desire her beauty in your heart, and do not let her capture you with her eyelashes, ^6-25

**6:26** for a prostitute’s fee is only a loaf of bread,^[Cn Compare Gk Syr Vg Tg: Heb *for because of a harlot to a piece of bread*] but the wife of another stalks a man’s precious life. ^6-26

**6:27** Can fire be carried in the bosom without burning one’s clothes? ^6-27

**6:28** Or can one walk on hot coals without scorching the feet? ^6-28

**6:29** So is he who sleeps with his neighbor’s wife; no one who touches her will go unpunished. ^6-29

**6:30** Thieves are not despised who steal only to satisfy their appetite when they are hungry. ^6-30

**6:31** Yet if they are caught, they will pay sevenfold; they will forfeit all the goods of their house. ^6-31

**6:32** But he who commits adultery has no sense; he who does it destroys himself. ^6-32

**6:33** He will get wounds and dishonor, and his disgrace will not be wiped away. ^6-33

**6:34** For jealousy arouses a husband’s fury, and he will show no restraint when he takes revenge. ^6-34

**6:35** He will accept no compensation and will refuse a bribe no matter how great. ^6-35
