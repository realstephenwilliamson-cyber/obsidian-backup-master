## Chapter 6

**6:1** Ah, you who are at ease in Zion[^1]  
And confident on the hill of Samaria,  
You notables of the leading nation  
On whom the House of Israel pin their hopes:[^2] ^6-1

**6:2** Cross over to Calneh and see,  
Go from there to Great Hamath,  
And go down to Gath of the Philistines:  
Are [you] better than those kingdoms,  
Or is their territory larger than yours?[^3] ^6-2

**6:3** Yet you ward off [the thought of] a day of woe  
And convene a session of lawlessness.[^4] ^6-3

**6:4** They lie on ivory beds,  
Lolling on their couches,  
Feasting on lambs from the flock  
And on calves from the stalls. ^6-4

**6:5** They hum snatches of song  
To the tune of the lute—  
They account themselves musicians[^5] like David. ^6-5

**6:6** They drink [straight] from the wine bowls  
And anoint themselves with the choicest oils—  
But they are not concerned about the ruin of Joseph. ^6-6

**6:7** Assuredly, right soon  
They shall head the column of exiles;  
They shall loll no more at festive meals. ^6-7

**6:8** My Lord GOD swears by Himself:  
I loathe the Pride of Jacob,[^6]  
And I detest his fortresses.  
I will declare forfeit city and inhabitants alike  
—declares the LORD, the God of Hosts. ^6-8

**6:9** If ten people are left in one house, they shall die. ^6-9

**6:10** And if someone’s kinsman—who is to burn incense for him—comes to carry the remains out of a house,[^7] and he calls to the one at the rear of the house, “Are there any alive besides you?” he will answer, “No, none.” And he will say, “Hush!”—so that no one may utter the name of the LORD. ^6-10

**6:11** For the LORD will command,  
And the great house shall be smashed to bits,  
And the little house to splinters. ^6-11

**6:12** Can horses gallop on a rock?  
Can it be plowed with oxen?[^8]  
Yet you have turned justice into poison weed  
And the fruit of righteousness to wormwood. ^6-12

**6:13** [Ah,] those who are so happy about Lo-dabar,[^9]  
Who exult, “By our might  
We have captured Karnaim”![^10] ^6-13

**6:14** But I, O House of Israel,  
Will raise up a nation against you  
—declares the LORD, the God of Hosts—  
Who will harass you from Lebo-Hamath  
To the Wadi Arabah. ^6-14

[^1]: Emendation yields “Joseph,” cf. v. 6, and 5.6, 15, where “Joseph” denotes the northern kingdom.
[^2]: Taking ba’ l-as synonymous with ba’ ‘ad; see Isa. 45.24 and note i-i.
[^3]: Emendation yields “Or is your territory larger than theirs?”
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^5]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^6]: A poetic designation of the northern kingdom.
[^7]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^8]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain; emendation yields “Can one plow the sea with oxen?”
[^9]: Two towns east of the Jordan recovered for Israel by Jeroboam II (see 2 Kings 14.25). For Lo-dabar, cf. 2 Sam. 9.4, 5; 17.27; for Karnaim, cf. Gen. 14.5.
[^10]: Two towns east of the Jordan recovered for Israel by Jeroboam II (see 2 Kings 14.25). For Lo-dabar, cf. 2 Sam. 9.4, 5; 17.27; for Karnaim, cf. Gen. 14.5.
