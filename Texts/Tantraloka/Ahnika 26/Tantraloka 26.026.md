## Mūla

तथा कुर्याद् गुरुर्गुप्तिहानिर्दोषवती यतः ।
देवीनां त्रितयं शुद्धं यद्वा यामलयोगतः ॥ ^mula

tathā kuryād gururguptihānirdoṣavatī yataḥ |
devīnāṃ tritayaṃ śuddhaṃ yadvā yāmalayogataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 17 - Chapter Twenty-Five (continued) and Chapter Twenty-Six#^pg399|p. 379]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=399>))
>
> Just as one disciple does not know the religious practice (anuṣthāna) of another, so too the teacher should act (in secrecy), for a breach of secrecy is a (culpable) defect. (The mantras) he should tell (his disciple) may be that of the triad of Goddesses, who may be pure (i.e. solitary), or in union with (their consorts). He may tell him (the Mantra of just) one goddess, pure (i.e. alone), or else in union (with her Bhairava). (with 26.25cd–27ab)
