## Mūla

न चेन्न क्वापि मुख्यत्वं नोपचारोऽपि तत्क्वचित् ।
एतच्छ्रीपूर्वशास्त्रे च स्फुटमुक्तं महेशिना ॥ ^mula

na cenna kvāpi mukhyatvaṃ nopacāro'pi tatkvacit |
etacchrīpūrvaśāstre ca sphuṭamuktaṃ maheśinā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 14 - Chapter Ten, part 5#^pg508|p. 489]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=508>))
>
> Nor is this just a metaphor (upacāra); all things are there in reality (within the Lord). If this were not so, there could be no primary or metaphorical sense in any case. (with 10.300cd–301ab)
>
> The Great Lord has clearly said this in the Śrīpūrvaśastra, (where we read): “(1) When the divisions are fifteen, this waking state corresponds to three of them, namely, (one’s) Own Nature, the Conditioned (sakala) (subject) and (his) power. Dreaming and deep sleep correspond to the two Deconditioned (subjects), the Fourth state to the Mantra (subjects) and the rest, and the state Beyond the Fourth to Śiva and His power. (with 10.301cd–303abc)
