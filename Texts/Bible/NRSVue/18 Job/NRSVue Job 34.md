# Job 34 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Job 33|← Job 33]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Job 35|Job 35 →]]

## Elihu proclaims God’s justice

**34:1** Then Elihu continued and said: ^34-1

**34:2** “Hear my words, you wise men, and give ear to me, you who know, ^34-2

**34:3** for the ear tests words as the palate tastes food. ^34-3

**34:4** Let us choose what is right; let us determine among ourselves what is good. ^34-4

**34:5** For Job has said, ‘I am innocent, and God has taken away my right; ^34-5

**34:6** in spite of being right I am counted a liar; my wound is incurable, though I am without transgression.’ ^34-6

**34:7** Who is there like Job, who drinks up scoffing like water, ^34-7

**34:8** who goes in company with evildoers and walks with the wicked? ^34-8

**34:9** For he has said, ‘It profits one nothing to take delight in God.’ ^34-9

**34:10** “Therefore, hear me, you who have sense; far be it from God that he should do wickedness and from the Almighty^[Traditional rendering of Heb *Shaddai*] that he should do wrong. ^34-10

**34:11** For according to their deeds he will repay them, and according to their ways he will make it befall them. ^34-11

**34:12** Of a truth, God will not do wickedly, and the Almighty^[Traditional rendering of Heb *Shaddai*] will not pervert justice. ^34-12

**34:13** Who gave him charge over the earth, and who laid on him^[Heb lacks *on him*] the whole world? ^34-13

**34:14** If he should take back his spirit^[Heb *his heart his spirit*] to himself and gather to himself his breath, ^34-14

**34:15** all flesh would perish together, and all mortals return to dust. ^34-15

**34:16** “If you have understanding, hear this; listen to what I say. ^34-16

**34:17** Shall one who hates justice govern? Will you condemn one who is righteous and mighty, ^34-17

**34:18** who says to a king, ‘You scoundrel!’ and to princes, ‘You wicked men!’; ^34-18

**34:19** who shows no partiality to nobles, nor regards the rich more than the poor, for they are all the work of his hands? ^34-19

**34:20** In a moment they die; at midnight the people are shaken and pass away, and the mighty are taken away by no human hand. ^34-20

**34:21** “For his eyes are upon the ways of mortals, and he sees all their steps. ^34-21

**34:22** There is no gloom or deep darkness where evildoers may hide themselves. ^34-22

**34:23** For he has not appointed a time^[Cn: Heb *yet*] for anyone to go before God in judgment. ^34-23

**34:24** He shatters the mighty without investigation and sets others in their place. ^34-24

**34:25** Thus, knowing their works, he overturns them in the night, and they are crushed. ^34-25

**34:26** He strikes them for their wickedness while others look on, ^34-26

**34:27** because they turned aside from following him and had no regard for any of his ways, ^34-27

**34:28** so that they caused the cry of the poor to come to him, and he heard the cry of the afflicted— ^34-28

**34:29** When he is quiet, who can condemn? When he hides his face, who can behold him? Whether nation or person, it is the same— ^34-29

**34:30** so that the godless should not reign or those who ensnare the people. ^34-30

**34:31** “For has anyone said to God, ‘I have endured punishment; I will not offend any more; ^34-31

**34:32** teach me what I do not see; if I have done iniquity, I will do it no more’? ^34-32

**34:33** Will he then pay back to suit you because you reject it? For you must choose and not I; therefore declare what you know.^[Meaning of Heb of 34.29–33 uncertain] ^34-33

**34:34** Those who have sense will say to me, and the wise who hear me will say, ^34-34

**34:35** ‘Job speaks without knowledge; his words are without insight.’ ^34-35

**34:36** Would that Job were tried to the limit, because his answers are those of the wicked. ^34-36

**34:37** For he adds rebellion to his sin; he claps his hands among us and multiplies his words against God.” ^34-37
