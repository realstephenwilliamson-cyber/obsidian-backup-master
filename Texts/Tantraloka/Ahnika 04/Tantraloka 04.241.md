## Mūla

संवित्तादात्म्यमापन्नं सर्वं शुद्धमतः स्थितम् ।
श्रीमद्वीरावलौ चोक्तं शुद्ध्यशुद्धिनिरूपणे ॥ ^mula

saṃvittādātmyamāpannaṃ sarvaṃ śuddhamataḥ sthitam |
śrīmadvīrāvalau coktaṃ śuddhyaśuddhinirūpaṇe || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. III
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 03 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v03 10 - Chapter Four, part 9#^pg349|p. 336]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 03.pdf#page=349>))
>
> Moreover, it is said in the venerable Vīrāvali, in the course of describing (the nature of) purity and impurity, that life (jīva) is the vehicle (vāhaka) of all things. And there is nothing that is not alive. Know that whatever is devoid of life is impure. (with 4.241cd–242)
