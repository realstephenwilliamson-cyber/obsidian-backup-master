## Mūla

सन्ध्यानामाहुरेतच्च तान्त्रिकीयं न नो मतम् ।
यासौ कालाधिकारे प्राक् सन्ध्या प्रोक्ता चतुष्टयी ॥ ^mula

sandhyānāmāhuretacca tāntrikīyaṃ na no matam |
yāsau kālādhikāre prāk sandhyā proktā catuṣṭayī || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 17 - Chapter Twenty-Five (continued) and Chapter Twenty-Six#^pg401|p. 381]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=401>))
>
> Others say that (the officiant) should face east, north, west and south, (respectively,) in the four junctions. This is the view Tāntrikas (hold); it is not ours. (with 26.33cd–34ab)
>
> (The officiant) should attend to the prescribed (rite) for the junctions (of the day), inwardly contemplating the fourfold junction taught previously in the chapter concerning (the cycle of) time (projected into the cycle of the breath). Or else, the disciple who is dedicated to study, knowledge, the teacher and (his) duties etc. may, (if he so wishes to save time, condense) the operations to be executed in (all) four junctions into (just) one (of them). (with 26.34cd–36ab)
