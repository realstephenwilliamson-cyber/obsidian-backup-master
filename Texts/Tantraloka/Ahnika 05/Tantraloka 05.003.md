## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 03 - Chapter Five, part 1#^pg18|p. 3]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=18>))
>
> While for some, (enlightened) thought becomes firm and pure by virtue of the innate freedom of the Self independently of any other means, for others that thought requires another means to purify itself. The first possibility has been discussed there (in the previous chapter, and so we shall now deal with the other one). (with 5.3–4)
