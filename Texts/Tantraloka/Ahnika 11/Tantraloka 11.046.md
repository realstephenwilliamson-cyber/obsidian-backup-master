## Mūla

तथापि न विमर्शात्मरूपं त्यजति तेन सः ।
प्रमाणात्मविमर्शात्मा मानवत्क्षोभभाङ् न तु ॥ ^mula

tathāpi na vimarśātmarūpaṃ tyajati tena saḥ |
pramāṇātmavimarśātmā mānavatkṣobhabhāṅ na tu || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 02 - Chapter Eleven, part 1#^pg64|p. 44]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=64>))
>
> (Although the means of knowledge is in this introverted state,) it does not for this reason abandon its own (essential) form, which is reflective awareness (vimarśa); (however,) this reflective awareness, which is (still) essentially the means of knowledge, is not disturbed (by objectivity), as is the means of knowledge (in the Pada state, which is directed at objectivity). Thus, it is said in the Trikaśāsana that the essential nature (svan rūpam) of Mantras and Padas is the same (abhinna). Supreme, it is inactive and undisturbed. (with 11.46–47)
