# Psalm 107

← [[BCP Psalm 106]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 108]] →

** · Book Five · Twenty-second Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 107]]

**Part I** Confitemini Domino

**107:1** Give thanks to the LORD, for he is good, \*  
and his mercy endures for ever. ^107-1

**107:2** Let all those whom the LORD has redeemed proclaim \*  
that he redeemed them from the hand of the foe. ^107-2

**107:3** He gathered them out of the lands; \*  
from the east and from the west,  
from the north and from the south. ^107-3

**107:4** Some wandered in desert wastes; \*  
they found no way to a city where they might dwell. ^107-4

**107:5** They were hungry and thirsty; \*  
their spirits languished within them. ^107-5

**107:6** Then they cried to the LORD in their trouble, \*  
and he delivered them from their distress. ^107-6

**107:7** He put their feet on a straight path \*  
to go to a city where they might dwell. ^107-7

**107:8** Let them give thanks to the LORD for his mercy \*  
and the wonders he does for his children. ^107-8

**107:9** For he satisfies the thirsty \*  
and fills the hungry with good things. ^107-9

[p. 747]

**107:10** Some sat in darkness and deep gloom, \*  
bound fast in misery and iron; ^107-10

**107:11** Because they rebelled against the words of God \*  
and despised the counsel of the Most High. ^107-11

**107:12** So he humbled their spirits with hard labor; \*  
they stumbled, and there was none to help. ^107-12

**107:13** Then they cried to the LORD in their trouble, \*  
and he delivered them from their distress. ^107-13

**107:14** He led them out of darkness and deep gloom \*  
and broke their bonds asunder. ^107-14

**107:15** Let them give thanks to the LORD for his mercy \*  
and the wonders he does for his children. ^107-15

**107:16** For he shatters the doors of bronze \*  
and breaks in two the iron bars. ^107-16

**107:17** Some were fools and took to rebellious ways; \*  
they were afflicted because of their sins. ^107-17

**107:18** They abhorred all manner of food \*  
and drew near to death's door. ^107-18

**107:19** Then they cried to the LORD in their trouble, \*  
and he delivered them from their distress. ^107-19

**107:20** He sent forth his word and healed them \*  
and saved them from the grave. ^107-20

**107:21** Let them give thanks to the LORD for his mercy \*  
and the wonders he does for his children. ^107-21

**107:22** Let them offer a sacrifice of thanksgiving \*  
and tell of his acts with shouts of joy. ^107-22

[p. 748]

**107:23** Some went down to the sea in ships \*  
and plied their trade in deep waters; ^107-23

**107:24** They beheld the works of the LORD \*  
and his wonders in the deep. ^107-24

**107:25** Then he spoke, and a stormy wind arose, \*  
which tossed high the waves of the sea. ^107-25

**107:26** They mounted up to the heavens and fell back to the depths; \*  
their hearts melted because of their peril. ^107-26

**107:27** They reeled and staggered like drunkards \*  
and were at their wits' end. ^107-27

**107:28** Then they cried to the LORD in their trouble, \*  
and he delivered them from their distress. ^107-28

**107:29** He stilled the storm to a whisper \*  
and quieted the waves of the sea. ^107-29

**107:30** Then were they glad because of the calm, \*  
and he brought them to the harbor they were bound for. ^107-30

**107:31** Let them give thanks to the LORD for his mercy \*  
and the wonders he does for his children. ^107-31

**107:32** Let them exalt him in the congregation of the people \*  
and praise him in the council of the elders. ^107-32

**Psalm 107: Part II** Posuit flumina

**107:33** The LORD changed rivers into deserts, \*  
and water-springs into thirsty ground, ^107-33

**107:34** A fruitful land into salt flats, \*  
because of the wickedness of those who dwell there. ^107-34

**107:35** He changed deserts into pools of water \*  
and dry land into water-springs. ^107-35

[p. 749]

**107:36** He settled the hungry there, \*  
and they founded a city to dwell in. ^107-36

**107:37** They sowed fields, and planted vineyards, \*  
and brought in a fruitful harvest. ^107-37

**107:38** He blessed them, so that they increased greatly; \*  
he did not let their herds decrease. ^107-38

**107:39** Yet when they were diminished and brought low, \*  
through stress of adversity and sorrow, ^107-39

**107:40** (He pours contempt on princes \*  
and makes them wander in trackless wastes) ^107-40

**107:41** He lifted up the poor out of misery \*  
and multiplied their families like flocks of sheep. ^107-41

**107:42** The upright will see this and rejoice, \*  
but all wickedness will shut its mouth. ^107-42

**107:43** Whoever is wise will ponder these things, \*  
and consider well the mercies of the LORD. ^107-43
