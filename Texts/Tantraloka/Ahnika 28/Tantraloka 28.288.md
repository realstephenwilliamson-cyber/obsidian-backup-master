## Mūla

विपरीतैरपि ज्ञानदीक्षागुर्वादिदूषकैः ।
तिरोभावो न विज्ञेयो हृदये रूढ्यभावतः ॥ ^mula

viparītairapi jñānadīkṣāgurvādidūṣakaiḥ |
tirobhāvo na vijñeyo hṛdaye rūḍhyabhāvataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 06 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 4#^pg141|p. 124]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=141>))
>
> (The cause of these latent impressions of transmigratory existence are these) actions and words, enraged (and distorted) by a defect (in the balance of the body’s) constituent elements. They are reflections that have not attained certainty, similar to those of one who sleeps or is drunk. The (very) reverse (viparīta) (of truth), they corrupt (dūṣaka) knowledge, initiation, the (tradition of) the teachers, and the like. One should not consider that (they are the cause of the soul’s) obscuration (tirobhāva), because there is no stability (rūḍhi) in the heart (of the core of consciousness). (with 28.287–288)

## Viveka

> [!viveka]- Viveka in English (Dyczkowski, vol. X, p. 125)

> व्यापारव्याहृतैस्तेन धातुदोषप्रकोपितैः ।
> अप्राप्तनिश्चयामर्शैः सुप्तमत्तोपमानकैः ॥
> विपरीतैरपि ज्ञानदीक्षागुर्वादिदूषकैः ।
> तिरोभावो न विज्ञेयो हृदये रूढ्यभावतः ॥

अप्राप्तनिश्चयामर्शैरिति--अन्यथाद्धित्वात् । रूढ्यभावत इति--अस्य हि अन्यत्र अस्ति दृढतरमभ्यासः--इति भावः ॥२८८ ॥
