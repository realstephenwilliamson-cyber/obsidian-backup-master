# Sanskrit scratchpad

A page for handwriting with the pen. Put the cursor where you want to write and run *Ink: New handwriting section* (the command palette on any device, or Ink's ribbon button), write, then lock the section. Each section is its own file under `Study/Sanskrit/Attachments/Ink/Writing/`; the picture shows on every device, with or without the plugin.

One device per section, and let Sync deliver before the same section is opened on another device. While you write, strokes older than the last 200 fade to keep the pen quick; they come back when the section is locked.

The day's letter is in the day's note under *Today*, and its card note has a *Practice* heading of its own; this page is for anything else — words from the verses, the CIS exercises, a line of a mantra.
