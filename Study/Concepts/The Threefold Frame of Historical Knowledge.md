# The Threefold Frame of Historical Knowledge

> [!abstract] Definition
> Paul Ricoeur’s epistemological structure governing historical truth, comprising three interlinked, mutually corrective operations: documentary proof (the archive), causal/teleological explanation, and literary/narrative emplotment (*mise-en-intrigue*).

## The Architecture
In *Memory, History, Forgetting* (2004: 250), Ricoeur identifies the "threefold frame that remains the secret of historical knowledge":
1. **Documentary Phase (Proof):** The gathering, critical examination, and authentication of physical traces and archival testimony.
2. **Explanatory Phase (Causality):** The socioscientific analysis of *why* events unfolded, testing hypotheses against evidence.
3. **Representational Phase (Narrative Emplotment):** The synthesis of disparate heterogeneous incidents into a coherent story accessible to human reading.

## The Digital Breakdown (The Curation Crisis)
In [[Cybernetic Anamnesis text 2025-11-05|Cybernetic Anamnesis]], this frame serves as the diagnostic metric for digital information collapse:
- Manufactured traces (deepfakes, purged databases) poison **documentary proof**.
- Hyper-personalized recommendation feeds sever **causal explanations**, replacing causality with engagement correlation.
- Echo chambers and fragmented feeds destroy shared **narrative emplotment**, making collective historical synthesis impossible.
