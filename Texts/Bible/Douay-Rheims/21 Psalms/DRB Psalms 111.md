# Psalms 111 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 110|← Psalms 110]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 112|Psalms 112 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 111|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**111:1** Blessed is the man that feareth the Lord: he shall delight exceedingly in his commandments. ^111-1

**111:2** His seed shall be mighty upon earth: the generation of the righteous shall be blessed. ^111-2

**111:3** Glory and wealth shall be in his house: and his justice remaineth for ever and ever. ^111-3

**111:4** To the righteous a light is risen up in darkness: he is merciful, and compassionate and just. ^111-4

**111:5** Acceptable is the man that showeth mercy and lendeth: he shall order his words with judgment: ^111-5

**111:6** Because he shall not be moved for ever. ^111-6

**111:7** The just shall be in everlasting remembrance: he shall not hear the evil hearing. His heart is ready to hope in the Lord: ^111-7

**111:8** His heart is strengthened, he shall not be moved until he look over his enemies. ^111-8

**111:9** He hath distributed, he hath given to the poor: his justice remaineth for ever and ever: his horn shall be exalted in glory. ^111-9

**111:10** The wicked shall see, and shall be angry, he shall gnash with his teeth and pine away: the desire of the wicked shall perish. ^111-10
