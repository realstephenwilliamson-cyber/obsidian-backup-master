# Zohar folio index (Pritzker notes)

Every folio side marked in your Pritzker notes (volumes 1–12), grouped by note. Each link opens the note at the head of the printed page on which that folio begins; the turn itself, marked like [15a] in the text, usually comes one to three paragraphs further down. Zohar references are written volume:folio, so [[Zohar 1.07 - Parashat Be-Reshit#^z1-15a|Zohar 1:15a]] is Volume I, folio 15a. Where two texts share a folio (the main *Zohar* and *Midrash ha-Ne’lam*, *Heikhalot*, *Piqqudin* or *Raza de-Razin*), the folio is listed under both notes. *Zohar Ḥadash* (ZḤ, folio columns a–d) and the folios of manuscript T1, which volume 12 follows in *Zohar al ha-Parashot*, come at the end.

## Volume I

485 folio sides, 1:1a to 1:265a. Not marked in volumes 1–12: 1:22b–28b, 1:212a–215b, 1:252a–253a, 1:254b–264a.
