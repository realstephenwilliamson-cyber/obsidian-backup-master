## Mūla

गन्धैर्मण्डलकं कृत्वा ब्रह्मस्थाने विचक्षणः ।
तत्र संपूजयेत्षट्कं त्रिकं वाप्येकमेव वा ॥ ^mula

gandhair maṇḍalakaṁ[^121] kṛtvā brahmasthāne vicakṣaṇaḥ |
tatra saṁpūjayet ṣaṭkaṁ[^122] trikaṁ[^123] vāpy ekam eva vā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2025 – April 2025
> p. 31 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=45>))
>
> **The Fire Sacrifice**
>
> Once the skilled (practitioner) has fashioned the maṇḍala with scents (or unguents),[^124] in (the middle of the abode of the sacrifice, which is) the location of Brahmā, he should worship either the group of six or the triad (of goddesses) or just one.[^125]

[^121]: Variant reading: *gandhamaṇḍalakamiti; kh: manumaṇḍalakam*
[^122]: kh: *saṁpūjayecchaṁkham*
[^123]: Variant reading: *trike veti*
[^124]: TĀ 15/387cd (384cd) reads *gandhamaṇḍale* for *gandhair maṇḍalakaṁ.*
[^125]: MV 8/109 is quoted in TĀv ad 15/388cd-391ab (385cd-388ab). The place where the firepit is to be dug is purified in this way.
