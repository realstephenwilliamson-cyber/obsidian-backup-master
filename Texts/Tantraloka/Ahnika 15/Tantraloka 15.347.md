## Mūla

सैव च भूयः स्वस्मात्सङ्कर्षति कालमिह बहिष्कुरुते ।
सङ्कर्षिणीति कथिता मातृष्वेतेषु सद्भावः ॥ ^mula

saiva ca bhūyaḥ svasmātsaṅkarṣati kālamiha bahiṣkurute |
saṅkarṣiṇīti kathitā mātṛṣveteṣu sadbhāvaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 07 - Chapter Fifteen, part 6#^pg232|p. 214]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=232>))
>
> She is also called ‘Saṁkarșṣiṇ?’ because she extracts (saṁkarṣaṭi) time here out of herself and externalises it. (with 15.347abc)
>
> She is the true being (sadbhāva) within these perceivers (mātṛ); that is, she is their (fundamental) reality (tattva), Existence (sattā), and the attainment (of their own nature) (prāpti), and it is due to her that objectivity is present within perceivers. She is (that) ultimate reality which is just freedom (itself), within the energies who are the mothers of the universe. (with 15.347d–348)
