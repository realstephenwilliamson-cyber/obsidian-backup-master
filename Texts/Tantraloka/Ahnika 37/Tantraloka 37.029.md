## Mūla

यस्मिन्काले च गुरुणा निर्विकल्पं प्रकाशितम् ।
मुक्तस्तेनैव कालेन यन्त्रं तिष्ठति केवलम् ॥ ^mula

yasminkāle ca guruṇā nirvikalpaṃ prakāśitam |
muktastenaiva kālena yantraṃ tiṣṭhati kevalam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 11 - Chapter Thirty-Seven, part 1#^pg256|p. 238]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=256>))

> When the teacher reveals (the one reality) free of thought constructs, (the disciple) is liberated in that very moment; all that remains is the machine (of the body).
