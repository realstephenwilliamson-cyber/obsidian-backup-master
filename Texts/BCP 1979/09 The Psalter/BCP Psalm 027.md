# Psalm 27

← [[BCP Psalm 026]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 028]] →

*Dominus illuminatio* · Book One · Fifth Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 027]]

**27:1** The LORD is my light and my salvation;  
whom then shall I fear? \*  
the LORD is the strength of my life;  
of whom then shall I be afraid? ^27-1

**27:2** When evildoers came upon me to eat up my flesh, \*  
it was they, my foes and my adversaries, who stumbled and fell. ^27-2

**27:3** Though an army should encamp against me, \*  
yet my heart shall not be afraid; ^27-3

**27:4** And though war should rise up against me, \*  
yet will I put my trust in him. ^27-4

**27:5** One thing have I asked of the LORD;  
one thing I seek; \*  
that I may dwell in the house of the LORD all the days of my life; ^27-5

**27:6** To behold the fair beauty of the LORD \*  
and to seek him in his temple. ^27-6

[p. 618]

**27:7** For in the day of trouble he shall keep me safe in his shelter; \*  
he shall hide me in the secrecy of his dwelling  
and set me high upon a rock. ^27-7

**27:8** Even now he lifts up my head \*  
above my enemies round about me. ^27-8

**27:9** Therefore I will offer in his dwelling an oblation  
with sounds of great gladness; \*  
I will sing and make music to the LORD. ^27-9

**27:10** Hearken to my voice, O LORD, when I call; \*  
have mercy on me and answer me. ^27-10

**27:11** You speak in my heart and say, "Seek my face." \*  
Your face, LORD, will I seek. ^27-11

**27:12** Hide not your face from me, \*  
nor turn away your servant in displeasure. ^27-12

**27:13** You have been my helper;  
cast me not away; \*  
do not forsake me, O God of my salvation. ^27-13

**27:14** Though my father and my mother forsake me, \*  
the LORD will sustain me. ^27-14

**27:15** Show me your way, O LORD; \*  
lead me on a level path, because of my enemies. ^27-15

**27:16** Deliver me not into the hand of my adversaries, \*  
for false witnesses have risen up against me,  
and also those who speak malice. ^27-16

**27:17** What if I had not believed  
that I should see the goodness of the LORD \*  
in the land of the living! ^27-17

[p. 619]

**27:18** O tarry and await the LORD'S pleasure;  
be strong, and he shall comfort your heart; \*  
wait patiently for the LORD. ^27-18
