# Letters

The fifty letters of the *mātṛkā* in their order, one note each: the sixteen vowels (with *aṃ* and *aḥ*), the thirty-three consonants and *kṣa*. A vowel's note shows its sign as it is written after a consonant; there are no separate notes for the signs. Each note has the letter's place of articulation, its body place in Abhinavagupta's *mātṛkā-nyāsa* ([[Tantraloka 15.117|TĀ 15.117]]–120), the flashcards that show it, and, for a consonant, the conjuncts of the [[Conjunct cycle (Tantraloka)|conjunct cycle]] that contain it. Made 13 Oct 2026 from the [[Sanskrit 108 Flashcard Register]] (decision D18).
