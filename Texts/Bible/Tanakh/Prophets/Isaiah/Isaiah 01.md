## Chapter 1

**1:1** The prophecies of Isaiah son of Amoz, who prophesied concerning Judah and Jerusalem in the reigns of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah. ^1-1

**1:2** Hear, O heavens, and give ear, O earth,  
For the LORD has spoken:  
“I reared children and brought them up—  
And they have rebelled against Me! ^1-2

**1:3** An ox knows its owner,  
An ass its master’s crib:  
Israel does not know,  
My people takes no thought.” ^1-3

**1:4** Ah, sinful nation!  
People laden with iniquity!  
Brood of evildoers!  
Depraved children!  
They have forsaken the LORD,  
Spurned the Holy One of Israel,  
Turned their backs [on Him]. ^1-4

**1:5** Why do you seek further beatings,  
That you continue to offend?  
Every head is ailing,  
And every heart is sick. ^1-5

**1:6** From head to foot  
No spot is sound:  
All bruises, and welts,  
And festering sores—  
Not pressed out, not bound up,  
Not softened with oil. ^1-6

**1:7** Your land is a waste,  
Your cities burnt down;  
Before your eyes, the yield of your soil  
Is consumed by strangers—  
A wasteland as overthrown by strangers![^1] ^1-7

**1:8** Fair[^2] Zion is left  
Like a booth in a vineyard,  
Like a hut in a cucumber field,  
Like a city beleaguered. ^1-8

**1:9** Had not the LORD of Hosts  
Left us some survivors,  
We should be like Sodom,  
Another Gomorrah. ^1-9

**1:10** Hear the word of the LORD,  
You chieftains of Sodom;  
Give ear to our God’s instruction,  
You folk of Gomorrah! ^1-10

**1:11** “What need have I of all your sacrifices?”  
Says the LORD.  
“I am sated with burnt offerings of rams,  
And suet of fatlings,  
And blood of bulls;  
And I have no delight  
In lambs and he-goats. ^1-11

**1:12** That you come to appear before Me—  
Who asked that [^3]of you?  
Trample My courts ^1-12

**1:13** no more;  
Bringing oblations is futile,  
Incense is offensive to Me.  
New moon and sabbath,  
Proclaiming of solemnities,  
Assemblies with iniquity,[^4]  
I cannot abide. ^1-13

**1:14** Your new moons and fixed seasons  
Fill Me with loathing;  
They are become a burden to Me,  
I cannot endure them. ^1-14

**1:15** And when you lift up your hands,  
I will turn My eyes away from you;  
Though you pray at length,  
I will not listen.  
Your hands are stained with crime— ^1-15

**1:16** Wash yourselves clean;  
Put your evil doings  
Away from My sight.  
Cease to do evil; ^1-16

**1:17** Learn to do good.  
Devote yourselves to justice;  
Aid the wronged.[^5]  
Uphold the rights of the orphan;  
Defend the cause of the widow. ^1-17

**1:18** “Come, let us reach an understanding,[^6]  
—says the LORD.  
Be your sins like crimson,  
They can turn snow-white;  
Be they red as dyed wool,  
They can become like fleece.” ^1-18

**1:19** If, then, you agree and give heed,  
You will eat the good things of the earth; ^1-19

**1:20** But if you refuse and disobey,  
You will be devoured [by] the sword.[^7]—  
For it was the LORD who spoke. ^1-20

**1:21** Alas, she has become a harlot,  
The faithful city  
That was filled with justice,  
Where righteousness dwelt—  
But now murderers. ^1-21

**1:22** Your[^8] silver has turned to dross;  
Your wine is cut with water.[^9] ^1-22

**1:23** Your rulers are rogues  
And cronies of thieves,  
Every one avid for presents  
And greedy for gifts;  
They do not judge the case of the orphan,  
And the widow’s cause never reaches them. ^1-23

**1:24** Assuredly, this is the declaration  
Of the Sovereign, the LORD of Hosts,  
The Mighty One of Israel:  
“Ah, I will get satisfaction from My foes;  
I will wreak vengeance on My enemies! ^1-24

**1:25** I will turn My hand against you,  
And smelt out your dross as with lye,[^10]  
And remove all your slag: ^1-25

**1:26** I will restore your magistrates as of old,  
And your counselors as of yore.  
After that you shall be called  
City of Righteousness, Faithful City.” ^1-26

**1:27** [^11]Zion shall be saved in the judgment;  
Her repentant ones, in the retribution.[^12] ^1-27

**1:28** But rebels and sinners shall all be crushed,  
And those who forsake the LORD shall perish. ^1-28

**1:29** Truly, you[^13] shall be shamed  
Because of the terebinths you desired,  
And you shall be confounded  
Because of the gardens you coveted. ^1-29

**1:30** For you shall be like a terebinth  
Wilted of leaf,  
And like a garden  
That has no water, ^1-30

**1:31** Stored wealth[^14] shall become as tow,  
And he who amassed it a spark;  
And the two shall burn together,  
With none to quench. ^1-31

[^1]: Emendation yields “like Sodom overthrown.”
[^2]: Lit. “Daughter.”
[^3]: Others “To trample My courts? / 13Bring no more vain oblations.”
[^4]: Septuagint “Fast and assembly”; cf. Joel 1.14.
[^5]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^6]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^7]: Or “you will be fed the sword.”
[^8]: I.e., Jerusalem’s.
[^9]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^10]: Emendation yields “in a crucible”; cf. 48.10.
[^11]: Others “Zion shall be saved by justice, / Her repentant ones by righteousness.”
[^12]: For this meaning cf. 5.16; 10.22.
[^13]: Heb. “they.”
[^14]: Connecting ḥason with ḥasan, “to store” (23.18), and ḥosen, “treasure” (33.6).
