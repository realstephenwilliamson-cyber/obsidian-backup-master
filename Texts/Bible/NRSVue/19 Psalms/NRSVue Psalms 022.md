# Psalm 22 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 021|← Psalm 21]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 023|Psalm 23 →]]

## Plea for deliverance from suffering and hostility

*To the leader: according to The Deer of the Dawn. A Psalm of David.*

**22:1** My God, my God, why have you forsaken me? Why are you so far from helping me, from the words of my groaning? ^22-1

**22:2** O my God, I cry by day, but you do not answer; and by night but find no rest. ^22-2

**22:3** Yet you are holy, enthroned on the praises of Israel. ^22-3

**22:4** In you our ancestors trusted; they trusted, and you delivered them. ^22-4

**22:5** To you they cried and were saved; in you they trusted and were not put to shame. ^22-5

**22:6** But I am a worm and not human, scorned by others and despised by the people. ^22-6

**22:7** All who see me mock me; they sneer at me; they shake their heads; ^22-7

**22:8** “Commit your cause to the LORD; let him deliver—let him rescue the one in whom he delights!” ^22-8

**22:9** Yet it was you who took me from the womb; you kept me safe on my mother’s breast. ^22-9

**22:10** On you I was cast from my birth, and since my mother bore me you have been my God. ^22-10

**22:11** Do not be far from me, for trouble is near, and there is no one to help. ^22-11

**22:12** Many bulls encircle me; strong bulls of Bashan surround me; ^22-12

**22:13** they open wide their mouths at me, like a ravening and roaring lion. ^22-13

**22:14** I am poured out like water, and all my bones are out of joint; my heart is like wax; it is melted within my breast; ^22-14

**22:15** my mouth^[Cn: Heb *strength*] is dried up like a potsherd, and my tongue sticks to my jaws; you lay me in the dust of death. ^22-15

**22:16** For dogs are all around me; a company of evildoers encircles me; they bound my hands and feet.^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^22-16

**22:17** I can count all my bones. They stare and gloat over me; ^22-17

**22:18** they divide my clothes among themselves, and for my clothing they cast lots. ^22-18

**22:19** But you, O LORD, do not be far away! O my help, come quickly to my aid! ^22-19

**22:20** Deliver my soul from the sword, my life^[Heb *my only one*] from the power of the dog! ^22-20

**22:21** Save me from the mouth of the lion! From the horns of the wild oxen you have rescued^[Heb *answered*] me. ^22-21

**22:22** I will tell of your name to my brothers and sisters;^[Or *kindred*] in the midst of the congregation I will praise you: ^22-22

**22:23** You who fear the LORD, praise him! All you offspring of Jacob, glorify him; stand in awe of him, all you offspring of Israel! ^22-23

**22:24** For he did not despise or abhor the affliction of the afflicted; he did not hide his face from me^[Heb *him*] but heard when I^[Heb *he*] cried to him. ^22-24

**22:25** From you comes my praise in the great congregation; my vows I will pay before those who fear him. ^22-25

**22:26** The poor^[Or *afflicted*] shall eat and be satisfied; those who seek him shall praise the LORD. May your hearts live forever! ^22-26

**22:27** All the ends of the earth shall remember and turn to the LORD, and all the families of the nations shall worship before him.^[Gk Syr Jerome: Heb *you*] ^22-27

**22:28** For dominion belongs to the LORD, and he rules over the nations. ^22-28

**22:29** To him,^[Cn: Heb *They have eaten and*] indeed, shall all who sleep in^[Cn: Heb *all the fat ones*] the earth bow down; before him shall bow all who go down to the dust, and I shall live for him.^[Compare Gk Syr Vg: Heb *and he who cannot keep himself alive*] ^22-29

**22:30** Posterity will serve him; future generations will be told about the Lord ^22-30

**22:31** and^[Compare Gk: Heb *it will be told about the Lord to the generation,31they will come and*] proclaim his deliverance to a people yet unborn, saying that he has done it. ^22-31
