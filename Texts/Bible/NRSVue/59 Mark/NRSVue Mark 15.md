# Mark 15 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Mark 14|← Mark 14]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Mark 16|Mark 16 →]]

Compare: [[Mark 15|BSB and SBLGNT]] · [[DRB Mark 15|Douay-Rheims]] · [[JANT Mark 15|JANT notes]]

## Jesus before Pilate

**15:1** As soon as it was morning, the chief priests held a consultation with the elders and scribes and the whole council. They bound Jesus, led him away, and handed him over to Pilate. ^15-1

**15:2** Pilate asked him, “Are you the King of the Jews?” He answered him, “You say so.” ^15-2

**15:3** Then the chief priests accused him of many things. ^15-3

**15:4** Pilate asked him again, “Have you no answer? See how many charges they bring against you.” ^15-4

**15:5** But Jesus made no further reply, so that Pilate was amazed. ^15-5

## Pilate hands Jesus over to be crucified

**15:6** Now at the festival he used to release a prisoner for them, anyone for whom they asked. ^15-6

**15:7** Now a man called Barabbas was in prison with the insurrectionists who had committed murder during the insurrection. ^15-7

**15:8** So the crowd came and began to ask Pilate to do for them according to his custom. ^15-8

**15:9** Then he answered them, “Do you want me to release for you the King of the Jews?” ^15-9

**15:10** For he realized that it was out of jealousy that the chief priests had handed him over. ^15-10

**15:11** But the chief priests stirred up the crowd to have him release Barabbas for them instead. ^15-11

**15:12** Pilate spoke to them again, “Then what do you wish me to do^[Other ancient authorities read *what should I do*] with the man you call^[Other ancient authorities lack *the man you call*] the King of the Jews?” ^15-12

**15:13** They shouted back, “Crucify him!” ^15-13

**15:14** Pilate asked them, “Why, what evil has he done?” But they shouted all the more, “Crucify him!” ^15-14

**15:15** So Pilate, wishing to satisfy the crowd, released Barabbas for them, and after flogging Jesus he handed him over to be crucified. ^15-15

## The soldiers mock Jesus

**15:16** Then the soldiers led him into the courtyard of the palace (that is, the governor’s headquarters), and they called together the whole cohort. ^15-16

**15:17** And they clothed him in a purple cloak, and after twisting some thorns into a crown they put it on him. ^15-17

**15:18** And they began saluting him, “Hail, King of the Jews!” ^15-18

**15:19** They struck his head with a reed, spat upon him, and knelt down in homage to him. ^15-19

**15:20** After mocking him, they stripped him of the purple cloak and put his own clothes on him. Then they led him out to crucify him. ^15-20

## The crucifixion of Jesus

**15:21** They compelled a passer-by, who was coming in from the country, to carry his cross; it was Simon of Cyrene, the father of Alexander and Rufus. ^15-21

**15:22** Then they brought Jesus^[Gk *him*] to the place called Golgotha (which means Place of a Skull). ^15-22

**15:23** And they offered him wine mixed with myrrh, but he did not take it. ^15-23

**15:24** And they crucified him and divided his clothes among them, casting lots to decide what each should take. ^15-24

**15:25** It was nine o’clock in the morning when they crucified him. ^15-25

**15:26** The inscription of the charge against him read, “The King of the Jews.” ^15-26

**15:27** And with him they crucified two rebels, one on his right and one on his left.^[Other ancient authorities add 15.28: *And the scripture was fulfilled that says, “And he was counted among the lawless.”*] ^15-27

**15:29** Those who passed by derided^[Or *blasphemed*] him, shaking their heads and saying, “Aha! You who would destroy the temple and build it in three days, ^15-29

**15:30** save yourself, and come down from the cross!” ^15-30

**15:31** In the same way the chief priests, along with the scribes, were also mocking him among themselves and saying, “He saved others; he cannot save himself. ^15-31

**15:32** Let the Messiah,^[Or *the Christ*] the King of Israel, come down from the cross now, so that we may see and believe.” Those who were crucified with him also taunted him. ^15-32

## The death of Jesus

**15:33** When it was noon, darkness came over the whole land^[Or *earth*] until three in the afternoon. ^15-33

**15:34** At three o’clock Jesus cried out with a loud voice, “Eloi, Eloi, lema sabachthani?” which means, “My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?”^[Other ancient authorities read *made me a reproach*] ^15-34

**15:35** When some of the bystanders heard it, they said, “Listen, he is calling for Elijah.” ^15-35

**15:36** And someone ran, filled a sponge with sour wine, put it on a stick, and gave it to him to drink, saying, “Wait, let us see whether Elijah will come to take him down.” ^15-36

**15:37** Then Jesus gave a loud cry and breathed his last. ^15-37

**15:38** And the curtain of the temple was torn in two, from top to bottom. ^15-38

**15:39** Now when the centurion who stood facing him saw that in this way he^[Other ancient authorities add *cried out and*] breathed his last, he said, “Truly this man was God’s Son!”^[Or *a son of God*] ^15-39

**15:40** There were also women looking on from a distance. Among them were Mary Magdalene, and Mary the mother of James the younger and of Joses, and Salome, ^15-40

**15:41** who followed him when he was in Galilee and ministered to him, and there were many other women who had come up with him to Jerusalem. ^15-41

## The burial of Jesus

**15:42** When evening had come, and since it was the day of Preparation, that is, the day before the Sabbath, ^15-42

**15:43** Joseph of Arimathea, a respected member of the council who was also himself waiting expectantly for the kingdom of God, went boldly to Pilate and asked for the body of Jesus. ^15-43

**15:44** Then Pilate wondered if he were already dead, and summoning the centurion he asked him whether he had been dead for some time. ^15-44

**15:45** When he learned from the centurion that he was dead, he granted the body to Joseph. ^15-45

**15:46** Then Joseph^[Gk *he*] bought a linen cloth and, taking down the body,^[Gk *it*] wrapped it in the linen cloth and laid it in a tomb that had been hewn out of rock. He then rolled a stone against the door of the tomb. ^15-46

**15:47** Mary Magdalene and Mary the mother of Joses saw where the body^[Gk *it*] was laid. ^15-47
