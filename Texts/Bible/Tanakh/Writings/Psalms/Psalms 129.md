## Chapter 129

**129:1** A song of ascents.  
Since my youth they have often assailed me,  
let Israel now declare, ^129-1

**129:2** since my youth they have often assailed me,  
but they have never overcome me. ^129-2

**129:3** Plowmen plowed across my back;  
they made long furrows. ^129-3

**129:4** The LORD, the righteous one,  
has snapped the cords of the wicked. ^129-4

**129:5** Let all who hate Zion  
fall back in disgrace. ^129-5

**129:6** Let them be like grass on roofs  
that fades before it can be pulled up, ^129-6

**129:7** that affords no handful for the reaper,  
no armful for the gatherer of sheaves, ^129-7

**129:8** no exchange with passersby:  
“The blessing of the LORD be upon you.”  
“We bless you by the name of the LORD.” ^129-8
