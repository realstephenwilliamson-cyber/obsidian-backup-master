## Mūla

कुर्वन्नधिक्रियां शास्त्रलङ्घी निग्रहभाजनम् ।
शक्तिपातबलादेव ज्ञानयोग्यविचित्रता ॥ ^mula

kurvannadhikriyāṃ śāstralaṅghī nigrahabhājanam |
śaktipātabalādeva jñānayogyavicitratā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 10 - Chapter Thirteen, part 6#^pg348|p. 326]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=348>))
>
> The teacher who belongs to a lower tradition, and even though he knows of the existence of teachers belonging to higher ones, continues to assume the status (of a teacher), violates (the precepts of) the scriptures, and (so) deserves to be punished (nigrahabhājana). (with 13.325cd–326ab)
>
> The variety of competence (a person possesses to grasp the) knowledge (of Śiva) is determined by the strength of the descent of the power (of grace) (he has received). (with 13.326cd)

## Viveka

> शक्तिपातबलादेव ज्ञानयोग्यविचित्रता ॥
