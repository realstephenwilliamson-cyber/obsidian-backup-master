## Chapter 40

**40:1** In the twenty-fifth year of our exile,[^1] the fourteenth year after the city had fallen, at the beginning of the year, the tenth day of the month—on that very day—the hand of the LORD came upon me, and He brought me there. ^40-1

**40:2** He brought me, in visions of God, to the Land of Israel, and He set me down on a very high mountain[^2] on which there seemed to be the outline of a city on the south.[^3] ^40-2

**40:3** He brought me over to it, and there, standing at the gate, was a man who shone like copper. In his hand were a cord of linen and a measuring rod. ^40-3

**40:4** The man spoke to me: “Mortal, look closely and listen attentively and note well everything I am going to show you—for you have been brought here in order to be shown—and report everything you see to the House of Israel.” ^40-4

**40:5** Along the outside of the Temple [area] ran a wall on every side. The rod that the man held was six cubits long, plus one handbreadth for each cubit; and when he applied it to that structure, it measured one rod deep[^4] and one rod high. ^40-5

**40:6** He went up to the gate that faced eastward and mounted its steps. He measured the threshold of the gate; it was one rod deep[^5]—the one threshold was one rod deep. ^40-6

**40:7** Each recess was one rod wide and one rod deep, with [a partition of] 5 cubits between recesses; and the threshold of the gate, at the inner vestibule of the gate, was one rod deep. ^40-7

**40:8** For when he measured it at the inner vestibule of the gate, it was one rod [deep].[^6] ^40-8

**40:9** Next he measured the vestibule of the gate, and it measured 8 cubits and its supports 2 cubits; the vestibule of the gate was at its inner end. ^40-9

**40:10** [^7]On either side of this eastern gate there were three recesses, all three of the same size; of identical sizes were also the supports[^8] on either side. ^40-10

**40:11** He measured the opening of the gate and found it 10 cubits wide, while the gate itself measured 13 cubits across.[^9] ^40-11

**40:12** At the fronts of the recesses on either side were barriers of one cubit;[^10] the recesses on either side were 6 cubits [deep]. ^40-12

**40:13** Their openings faced each other directly across the gate passage, so that when he measured from rear[^11] of recess to rear[^12] of recess he obtained a width of 25 cubits.[^13] ^40-13

**40:14** He made the vestibule[^14]—60 cubits—and the gate next to the support on every side of the court.[^15] ^40-14

**40:15** And [the distance] from the front of the outer[^16] gate to the front of the inner vestibule of the gate was 50 cubits. ^40-15

**40:16** The recesses—and their supports—had windows with frames[^17] on the interior of the gate complex on both sides, and the interiors of the vestibules also had windows on both sides; and the supports were adorned with palms. ^40-16

**40:17** He took me into the outer court. There were chambers there, and there was a pavement laid out all around the court. There were 30 chambers on the pavement. ^40-17

**40:18** The pavements flanked the gates; the depth of the lower[^18] pavements paralleled that of the gates. ^40-18

**40:19** Then he measured the width of the lower[^19] court, from in front of the inner gate to in front of the outer gate[^20]—100 cubits.  
After the east [gate], the north [gate].[^21] ^40-19

**40:20** Next he measured the gate of the outer court that faced north: its length and its width, ^40-20

**40:21** its three recesses on either side and its supports, as also its vestibule. It measured, like the first gate, 50 cubits in length and 25 cubits in width. ^40-21

**40:22** Its windows and [those of] its vestibule, as also its palm trees, corresponded to those of the gate that faced east. [From the outside] one had to climb 7 steps to reach it, and its vestibule was ahead of them.[^22] ^40-22

**40:23** Like the east gate, the north gate faced a gate leading into the inner forecourt; and when he measured the distance from gate to gate, it was 100 cubits. ^40-23

**40:24** Then he took me to the south side. There was also a gate on the south side, and he got the same measurements as before for its supports and its vestibule. ^40-24

**40:25** Both it and its vestibule had windows like the aforementioned ones. It was 50 cubits long and 25 cubits wide. ^40-25

**40:26** Its staircase consisted of 7 steps; its vestibule was ahead of them,[^23] and its supports were decorated on both sides with palm trees. ^40-26

**40:27** The inner court likewise had a gate facing south; and on the south side, too, he measured a distance of 100 cubits from the [outer] gate to the [inner] gate. ^40-27

**40:28** He now took me into the inner forecourt through its south gate. When he measured this south gate, it had the same measurements as the foregoing. ^40-28

**40:29** Its recesses, its supports, and its vestibule had the same measurements. Both it and its vestibule had windows on both sides; it was 50 cubits long and 25 cubits wide— ^40-29

**40:30** vestibules on both sides, 25 cubits long, 5 cubits wide.[^24] ^40-30

**40:31** Its vestibule, however, gave on the outer court.[^25] Its supports were adorned on either side with palms, and its staircase consisted of 8 steps. ^40-31

**40:32** Then he took me to the eastern side of the inner forecourt; and when he measured the gate there, he got the same measurements: ^40-32

**40:33** its recesses, supports, and vestibule had the above measurements. Both it and its vestibule had windows on both sides; it was 50 cubits long and 25 cubits wide, ^40-33

**40:34** and its vestibule gave on the outer court. Its supports were decorated on both sides with palm trees, and its staircase consisted of 8 steps. ^40-34

**40:35** Then he took me to the north gate, and found its measurements to be identical, ^40-35

**40:36** with the same recesses, supports, vestibule, windows on both sides, and a length of 50 cubits and a width of 25 cubits. ^40-36

**40:37** Its supports[^26] gave on the outer court; its supports were decorated on both sides with palm trees; and its staircase consisted of eight steps. ^40-37

**40:38** A chamber opened into the gate;[^27] there the burnt offering would be washed. ^40-38

**40:39** And inside the vestibule of the gate, there were two tables on each side, at which the burnt offering, the sin offering, and the guilt offering were to be slaughtered; ^40-39

**40:40** while outside—as one goes up toward[^28] the opening of the north gate—there were two tables on one side, and there were two tables on the other side of the gate’s vestibule. ^40-40

**40:41** Thus there were four tables on either flank of the gate—eight tables in all—at which [the sacrifices] were to be slaughtered. ^40-41

**40:42** As for the four tables for the burnt offering[^29]—they were of hewn stone, one and a half cubits long, one and a half cubits wide, and one cubit high—on them were laid out the instruments with which burnt offerings and sacrifices were slaughtered.[^30] ^40-42

**40:43** Shelves,[^31] one handbreadth wide, were attached all around the inside; and the sacrificial flesh was [laid] on the tables. ^40-43

**40:44** There were chambers for singers[^32] in the inner forecourt: [one] beside the north gate facing south, and one beside the east[^33] gate facing north. ^40-44

**40:45** [The man] explained to me: “The chamber that faces south is for the priests who perform the duties of the Temple; ^40-45

**40:46** and the chamber that faces north is for the priests who perform the duties of the altar—they are the descendants of Zadok, who alone of the descendants of Levi may approach the LORD to minister to Him.” ^40-46

**40:47** He then measured the forecourt: 100 cubits long and 100 cubits broad—foursquare. In front of the Temple stood the altar. ^40-47

**40:48** He took me into the portico of the Temple and measured it. The jambs[^34] of the portico were 5 cubits deep on either side. The width of the gate-opening was [14 cubits, and the flanking wall of the gate was][^35] 3 cubits on either side. ^40-48

**40:49** The portico was 20 cubits wide[^36] and 11[^37] cubits deep, and it was by steps that it was reached.[^38] There were columns by the jambs on either side. ^40-49
