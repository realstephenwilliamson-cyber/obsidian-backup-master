# LXX Psalms 26

← [[LXX Psalms 25]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 27]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 027]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 027]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**26:1** *A Psalm* of David, before he was anointed. The Lord is my light and my Saviour; whom shall I fear? the Lord is the defender of my life; of whom shall I be afraid?  ^26-1

**26:2** When evil-doers drew nigh against me to eat up my flesh, my persecutors and mine enemies, they fainted and fell.  ^26-2

**26:3** Though an army should set itself in array against me, my heart shall not be afraid: though war should rise up against me, in this am I confident.  ^26-3

**26:4** One thing have I asked of the Lord, this will I earnestly seek: that I should dwell in the house of the Lord all the days of my life, that I should behold the fair beauty of the Lord, and survey his temple.  ^26-4

**26:5** For in the day of mine afflictions he hid me in his tabernacle: he sheltered me in the secret of his tabernacle; he set me up on a rock.  ^26-5

**26:6** And now, behold, he has lifted up mine head over mine enemies: I went round and offered in his tabernacle the sacrifice of joy; I will sing, even sing psalms to the Lord.[^1][^2]  ^26-6

**26:7** Hear, O Lord, my voice which I have uttered aloud: pity me, and hearken to me.  ^26-7

**26:8** My heart said to thee, I have diligently sought thy face: thy face, O Lord, I will seek.  ^26-8

**26:9** Turn not thy face away from me, turn not thou away from thy servant in anger: be thou my helper, forsake me not; and, O God my Saviour, overlook me not.  ^26-9

**26:10** For my father and my mother have forsaken me, but the Lord has taken me to himself.  ^26-10

**26:11** Teach me, O Lord, in thy way, and guide me in a right path, because of mine enemies.  ^26-11

**26:12** Deliver me not over to the desire of them that afflict me; for unjust witnesses have risen up against me, and injustice has lied within herself.  ^26-12

**26:13** I believe that I shall see the goodness of the Lord in the land of the living.[^3]  ^26-13

**26:14** Wait on the Lord: be of good courage, and let thy heart be strengthened: yea, wait on the Lord.  ^26-14

[^1]: 26:6 *Gr.* shouting.
[^2]: 26:6 *Or,* and play on a psaltery.
[^3]: 26:13 *Gr.* good *things.*
