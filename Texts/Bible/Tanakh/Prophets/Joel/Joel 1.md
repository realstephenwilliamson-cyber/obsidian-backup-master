# Joel 1

Compare: [[NRSVue Joel 01|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 1

**1:1** The word of the LORD that came to Joel son of Pethuel. ^1-1

**1:2** Listen to this, O elders,  
Give ear, all inhabitants of the land.  
Has the like of this happened in your days  
Or in the days of your fathers? ^1-2

**1:3** Tell your children about it,  
And let your children tell theirs,  
And their children the next generation! ^1-3

**1:4** What the cutter[^1] has left, the locust has devoured;  
What the locust has left, the grub has devoured;  
And what the grub has left, the hopper has devoured. ^1-4

**1:5** Wake up, you drunkards, and weep,  
Wail, all you swillers of wine—  
For the new wine that is denied you![^2] ^1-5

**1:6** For a nation has invaded my land,  
Vast beyond counting,  
With teeth like the teeth of a lion,  
With the fangs of a lion’s breed. ^1-6

**1:7** They have laid my vines waste  
And splintered my fig trees:  
They have stripped off their bark and thrown [it] away;  
Their runners have turned white. ^1-7

**1:8** Lament—like a maiden girt with sackcloth  
For the husband of her youth! ^1-8

**1:9** Offering and libation have ceased  
From the House of the LORD;  
The priests must mourn  
Who minister to the LORD. ^1-9

**1:10** The country is ravaged,  
The ground must mourn;  
For the new grain is ravaged,  
The new wine is dried up,  
The new oil has failed. ^1-10

**1:11** Farmers are dismayed  
And vine dressers wail  
Over wheat and barley;  
For the crops of the field are lost. ^1-11

**1:12** The vine has dried up,  
The fig tree withers,  
Pomegranate, palm, and apple—  
All the trees of the field are sear.  
And joy has dried up  
Among men. ^1-12

**1:13** Gird yourselves and lament, O priests,  
Wail, O ministers of the altar;  
Come, spend the night in sackcloth,  
O ministers of my God.  
For offering and libation are withheld  
From the House of your God. ^1-13

**1:14** Solemnize a fast,  
Proclaim an assembly;  
Gather the elders—all the inhabitants of the land—  
In the House of the LORD your God,  
And cry out to the LORD. ^1-14

**1:15** Alas for the day!  
For the day of the LORD is near;  
It shall come like havoc from Shaddai.[^3] ^1-15

**1:16** For food is cut off  
Before our very eyes,  
And joy and gladness  
From the House of our God. ^1-16

**1:17** The seeds have shriveled  
Under their clods.[^4]  
The granaries are desolate,  
Barns are in ruins,  
For the new grain has failed. ^1-17

**1:18** How the beasts groan!  
The herds of cattle are bewildered  
Because they have no pasture,  
And the flocks of sheep are dazed.[^5] ^1-18

**1:19** To You, O LORD, I call.  
For fire[^6] has consumed  
The pastures in the wilderness,  
And flame[^7] has devoured  
All the trees of the countryside. ^1-19

**1:20** The very beasts of the field  
Cry out to You;  
For the watercourses are dried up,  
And fire has consumed  
The pastures in the wilderness. ^1-20

[^1]: The Heb. terms translated “cutter, locust, grub, and hopper” are of uncertain meaning; they probably designate stages in the development of the locust.
[^2]: Lit. “cut off from your mouth.”
[^3]: Traditionally “the Almighty”; see Gen. 17.1.
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^5]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^6]: I.e., scorching heat.
[^7]: I.e., scorching heat.
