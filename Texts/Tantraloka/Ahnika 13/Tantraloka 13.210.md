## Mūla

तत्रापि तारतम्यादिवशाच्छीघ्रचिरादितः ।
देहपातो भवेदस्य यद्वा काष्ठादितुल्यता ॥ ^mula

tatrāpi tāratamyādivaśācchīghracirāditaḥ |
dehapāto bhavedasya yadvā kāṣṭhāditulyatā || ^iast

> There (in that case) also, according to the level (of intensity) etc. (of the descent of the power of grace), the body may die immediately or after some time, or (in a state of intense absorption, the body may look) like a (an old dried out) piece of wood.
