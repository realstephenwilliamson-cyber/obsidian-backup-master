# Ezekiel 4 (NRSVue)

## The siege of Jerusalem portrayed

**4:1** “And you, O mortal, take a brick and set it before you. On it portray a city, Jerusalem, ^4-1

**4:2** and put siegeworks against it, and build a siege wall against it, and cast up a ramp against it; set camps also against it, and plant battering rams against it all around. ^4-2

**4:3** Then take an iron plate and place it as an iron wall between you and the city; set your face toward it, and let it be in a state of siege, and press the siege against it. This is a sign for the house of Israel. ^4-3

**4:4** “Then lie on your left side and place the guilt of the house of Israel upon it; you shall bear their guilt for the number of the days that you lie there. ^4-4

**4:5** For I assign to you a number of days, three hundred ninety days, equal to the number of the years of their guilt, and so you shall bear the guilt of the house of Israel. ^4-5

**4:6** When you have completed these, you shall lie down a second time, but on your right side, and bear the guilt of the house of Judah; forty days I assign you, one day for each year. ^4-6

**4:7** You shall set your face toward the siege of Jerusalem, and with your arm bared you shall prophesy against it. ^4-7

**4:8** See, I am putting cords on you so that you cannot turn from one side to the other until you have completed the days of your siege. ^4-8

**4:9** “And you, take wheat and barley, beans and lentils, millet and spelt; put them into one vessel and make bread for yourself. During the number of days that you lie on your side, three hundred ninety days, you shall eat it. ^4-9

**4:10** The food that you eat shall be twenty shekels a day by weight; at fixed times you shall eat it. ^4-10

**4:11** And you shall drink water by measure, one-sixth of a hin; at fixed times you shall drink. ^4-11

**4:12** You shall eat it as a barley cake, baking it in their sight on human dung.” ^4-12

**4:13** The LORD said, “Thus shall the people of Israel eat their bread, unclean, among the nations to which I will drive them.” ^4-13

**4:14** Then I said, “Ah Lord GOD! I have never defiled myself; from my youth up until now I have never eaten what died of itself or was torn by animals, nor has carrion flesh come into my mouth.” ^4-14

**4:15** Then he said to me, “See, I will let you have cow’s dung instead of human dung, on which you may prepare your bread.” ^4-15

**4:16** Then he said to me, “Mortal, I am going to cut off the supply of bread^[Heb *staff of bread*] in Jerusalem; they shall eat bread by weight and with fearfulness, and they shall drink water by measure and in dismay. ^4-16

**4:17** Lacking bread and water, they will look at one another in dismay and waste away under their punishment. ^4-17
