## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 03 - Chapter Eleven, part 2#^pg115|p. 95]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=115>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 11.112cd–113ab.
>
> (Even) a person who is awake can have (many day-)dreams in the span of three and a half hands-breadths and (two and a half) hours (of a breathing cycle that represents an entire day and night), during which he believes that he experiences (many) varied places and periods of time.

## Viveka

> [!viveka]- Viveka in English (Dyczkowski, vol. VII, pp. 95–96)
> ![[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 03 - Chapter Eleven, part 2#Viveka on 11.112]]

*Lead-in:* न च मायापदेऽपि देशकालयोः कश्चिन्नियमः -- इत्याह --

> जागराभिमते सार्धहस्तत्रितयगोचरे ।
> प्रहरे च पृथक् स्वप्नाश्चित्रदिक्कालमानिनः ॥

जाग्रद्दशायामपि हि नियतपरिमाणे शरीरदेशे प्रहराद्यात्मनि काले च पृथगुर्वीपर्वताकाशादितया दिनमाससंवत्सरकल्पादितया च विचित्रदिक्कालाद्यभिमानभाजः स्वप्नदशावलम्बिनोऽवभासा उपलभ्यन्ते -- इति को नामानयोर्नियमः ॥ ११२ ॥
