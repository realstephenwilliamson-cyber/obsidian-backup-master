## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 05 - Chapter Sixteen, part 1#^pg56|p. 36]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=56>))
>
> Then, having tied a white turban (sitoṣṇīṣa) (on his head), he should worship (his) hands, one after the other reciprocally, in order to burn away the fetters and emanate the pure principles. He should (then) worship the Mantras on Śiva’s Hand, (blazing) in the form of (His) radiant energy (tejas). They are present in the innermost circle (garbhāvaraṇa) (just as they are,) without limbs, entourage or thrones (āsana). After that, he should contemplate (his own) Self as one (alone) and (as seemingly) many (bhinna) because of (the five) tasks (it performs), that serve as limiting adjuncts (that appear to divide up its unity). This is as happens with the moon, (which appears to be various according to the stretches of) the water (in which it is reflected). (However,) in actual fact, it is divided (only) into (the following) six modes. (with 16.78–80)
