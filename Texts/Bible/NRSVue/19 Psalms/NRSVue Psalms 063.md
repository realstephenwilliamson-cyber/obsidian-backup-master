## Comfort and assurance in God’s presence

*A Psalm of David, when he was in the Wilderness of Judah.*

**63:1** O God, you are my God; I seek you; my soul thirsts for you; my flesh faints for you, as in a dry and weary land where there is no water. ^63-1

**63:2** So I have looked upon you in the sanctuary, beholding your power and glory. ^63-2

**63:3** Because your steadfast love is better than life, my lips will praise you. ^63-3

**63:4** So I will bless you as long as I live; I will lift up my hands and call on your name. ^63-4

**63:5** My soul is satisfied as with a rich feast,^[Heb *with fat and fatness*] and my mouth praises you with joyful lips ^63-5

**63:6** when I think of you on my bed and meditate on you in the watches of the night, ^63-6

**63:7** for you have been my help, and in the shadow of your wings I sing for joy. ^63-7

**63:8** My soul clings to you; your right hand upholds me. ^63-8

**63:9** But those who seek to destroy my life shall go down into the depths of the earth; ^63-9

**63:10** they shall be given over to the power of the sword; they shall be prey for jackals. ^63-10

**63:11** But the king shall rejoice in God; all who swear by him shall exult, for the mouths of liars will be stopped. ^63-11
