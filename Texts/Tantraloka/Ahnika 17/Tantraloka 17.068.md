## Mūla

प्लुष्टो लीनस्वभावोऽसौ पाशस्तं प्रति शम्भुवत् ।
परमेशमहातेजःशेषमात्रत्वमश्नुते ॥ ^mula

pluṣṭo līnasvabhāvo'sau pāśastaṃ prati śambhuvat |
parameśamahātejaḥśeṣamātratvamaśnute || ^iast

## Translation

> (The fetter) present before that (fire) is, as if (present before) Śiva, burnt and dissolved away, (such that) all that remains of it is just the Supreme Lord’s great radiant energy (tejas).
