# Matthew 25 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Matthew 24|← Matthew 24]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Matthew 26|Matthew 26 →]]

Compare: [[Matthew 25|BSB and SBLGNT]]

**25:1** Then shall the kingdom of heaven be like to ten virgins, who taking their lamps went out to meet the bridegroom and the bride. ^25-1

**25:2** And five of them were foolish, and five wise. ^25-2

**25:3** But the five foolish, having taken their lamps, did not take oil with them: ^25-3

**25:4** But the wise took oil in their vessels with the lamps. ^25-4

**25:5** And the bridegroom tarrying, they all slumbered and slept. ^25-5

**25:6** And at midnight there was a cry made: Behold the bridegroom cometh, go ye forth to meet him. ^25-6

**25:7** Then all those virgins arose and trimmed their lamps. ^25-7

**25:8** And the foolish said to the wise: Give us of your oil, for our lamps are gone out. ^25-8

**25:9** The wise answered, saying: Lest perhaps there be not enough for us and for you, go ye rather to them that sell, and buy for yourselves. ^25-9

**25:10** Now whilst they went to buy, the bridegroom came: and they that were ready, went in with him to the marriage, and the door was shut. ^25-10

**25:11** But at last come also the other virgins, saying: Lord, Lord, open to us. ^25-11

**25:12** But he answering said: Amen I say to you, I know you not. ^25-12

**25:13** Watch ye therefore, because you know not the day nor the hour. ^25-13

**25:14** For even as a man going into a far country, called his servants, and delivered to them his goods; ^25-14

**25:15** And to one he gave five talents, and to another two, and to another one, to every one according to his proper ability: and immediately he took his journey. ^25-15

**25:16** And he that had received the five talents, went his way, and traded with the same, and gained other five. ^25-16

**25:17** And in like manner he that had received the two, gained other two. ^25-17

**25:18** But he that had received the one, going his way digged into the earth, and hid his lord’s money. ^25-18

**25:19** But after a long time the lord of those servants came, and reckoned with them. ^25-19

**25:20** And he that had received the five talents coming, brought other five talents, saying: Lord, thou didst deliver to me five talents, behold I have gained other five over and above. ^25-20

**25:21** His lord said to him: Well done, good and faithful servant, because thou hast been faithful over a few things, I will place thee over many things: enter thou into the joy of thy lord. ^25-21

**25:22** And he also that had received the two talents came and said: Lord, thou deliveredst two talents to me: behold I have gained other two. ^25-22

**25:23** His lord said to him: Well done, good and faithful servant: because thou hast been faithful over a few things, I will place thee over many things: enter thou into the joy of thy lord. ^25-23

**25:24** But he that had received the one talent, came and said: Lord, I know that thou art a hard man; thou reapest where thou hast not sown, and gatherest where thou hast not strewed. ^25-24

**25:25** And being afraid I went and hid thy talent in the earth: behold here thou hast that which is thine. ^25-25

**25:26** And his lord answering, said to him: Wicked and slothful servant, thou knewest that I reap where I sow not, and gather where I have not strewed: ^25-26

**25:27** Thou oughtest therefore to have committed my money to the bankers, and at my coming I should have received my own with usury. ^25-27

**25:28** Take ye away therefore the talent from him, and give it to him that hath ten talents. ^25-28

**25:29** For to every one that hath shall be given, and he shall abound: but from him that hath not, that also which he seemeth to have shall be taken away. ^25-29

**25:30** And the unprofitable servant cast ye out into the exterior darkness. There shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth. ^25-30

**25:31** And when the Son of man shall come in his majesty, and all the angels with him, then shall he sit upon the seat of his majesty. ^25-31

**25:32** And all nations shall be gathered together before him, and he shall separate them one from another, as the shepherd separateth the sheep from the goats: ^25-32

**25:33** And he shall set the sheep on his right hand, but the goats on his left. ^25-33

**25:34** Then shall the king say to them that shall be on his right hand: Come, ye blessed of my Father, possess you the kingdom prepared for you from the foundation of the world. ^25-34

**25:35** For I was hungry, and you gave me to eat; I was thirsty, and you gave me to drink; I was a stranger, and you took me in: ^25-35

**25:36** Naked, and you covered me: sick, and you visited me: I was in prison, and you came to me. ^25-36

**25:37** Then shall the just answer him, saying: Lord, when did we see thee hungry, and fed thee; thirsty, and gave thee drink? ^25-37

**25:38** And when did we see thee a stranger, and took thee in? or naked, and covered thee? ^25-38

**25:39** Or when did we see thee sick or in prison, and came to thee? ^25-39

**25:40** And the king answering, shall say to them: Amen I say to you, as long as you did it to one of these my least brethren, you did it to me. ^25-40

**25:41** Then he shall say to them also that shall be on his left hand: Depart from me, you cursed, into everlasting fire which was prepared for the devil and his angels. ^25-41

**25:42** For I was hungry, and you gave me not to eat: I was thirsty, and you gave me not to drink. ^25-42

**25:43** I was a stranger, and you took me not in: naked, and you covered me not: sick and in prison, and you did not visit me. ^25-43

**25:44** Then they also shall answer him, saying: Lord, when did we see thee hungry, or thirsty, or a stranger, or naked, or sick, or in prison, and did not minister to thee? ^25-44

**25:45** Then he shall answer them, saying: Amen I say to you, as long as you did it not to one of these least, neither did you do it to me. ^25-45

**25:46** And these shall go into everlasting punishment: but the just, into life everlasting. ^25-46
