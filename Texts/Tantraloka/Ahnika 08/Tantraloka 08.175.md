## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 06 - Chapter Eight, part 4#^pg218|p. 200]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=218>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 8.175cd–176ab.
>
> If one were not to say that (the Egg) is an aggregate of entities (vastupinda), each one of the metaphysical principles could (then be thought to) be an Egg (of Brahmā).“” Even so, (if one accepts that this is not so in the case of all the other principles), surely, (one may ask,) why should this (undesirable view) not apply to (Prakṛti), the principle which consists of the flux of gross and subtle elements and the qualities (guṇa). (In response to this objection,) it is said that the term ‘entity’ (vastu) refers to something that has a body, senses and world-order. This is the reason why the Egg of Brahmā is said to be an aggregate of that. (with 8.175–177ab)
