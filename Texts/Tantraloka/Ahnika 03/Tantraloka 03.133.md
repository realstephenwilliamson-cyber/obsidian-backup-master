## Mūla

प्रकाशरूपं तत्प्राहुराग्नेयं शास्त्रकोविदाः ।
अत्र प्रकाशमात्रं यत्स्थिते धामत्रये सति ॥ ^mula

prakāśarūpaṃ tatprāhurāgneyaṃ śāstrakovidāḥ |
atra prakāśamātraṃ yatsthite dhāmatraye sati || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. II
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 02 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v02 08 - Chapter Three, part 5#^pg254|p. 238]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 02.pdf#page=254>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 3.133cd–134ab.
>
> The first form of these (vowels) is short and is called the Sun. (The long form) (dīrgha) is called the Moon, because it is brought about by an extended (dīrgha) rest (in the external object) due to the arousal (of the subject out of himself), and (also because it is the source of) the bliss (ānanda) (of sense contact). The prolated form, which comes next, follows that of the bliss of the Moon. Supreme and of the nature of Light, those who know the scriptures call it Fire. (with 3.131cd–133ab)
>
> The pure Light (of consciousness prakāśamātra) present here when the three abodes (of subject, object and means of knowledge) manifest is referred to in the scripture as the ‘Point’, and is considered to be the Point of Śiva (śivabindu). (with 3.133cd–134ab)

*Lead-in:* एवमप्यत्र परस्य प्रकाशस्य न स्वस्वरूपात् प्रच्यावः, इति विन्दुरूपत्वमुक्तम् -- इत्याह --

> अत्र प्रकाशमात्रं यत्स्थिते धामत्रये सति ॥
> उक्तं विन्दुतया शास्त्रे शिवविन्दुरसौ मतः ।

न चायं विन्दुः परप्रमात्रेकरूपत्वान्मन्त्रप्रमेयान्तर्वर्ती इत्याह -- "शिवविन्दुरसौ मतः"इति । अविभागपरप्रकाशात्मकत्वेन विदिक्रियायां स्वतन्त्रः परः प्रमाता -- इत्यर्थः । यदुक्तं प्राक् --

"उदितायां क्रियाशक्तौ सोमसूर्याग्निधामनि ।

अविभागः प्रकाशो यः स विन्दुः परमो हि नः ॥"

इति ॥ १३३ ॥
