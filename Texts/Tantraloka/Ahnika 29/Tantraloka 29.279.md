## Mūla

निर्यातं रोमकूपैस्तु भ्रमन्तं सर्वकारणैः ।
मध्यं निर्लक्ष्यमास्थाय भ्रमयेद्विसृजेत्ततः ॥ ^mula

niryātaṃ romakūpaistu bhramantaṃ sarvakāraṇaiḥ |
madhyaṃ nirlakṣyamāsthāya bhramayedvisṛjettataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 13 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 5#^pg423|p. 406]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=423>))
>
> The external (breath that enters by inhalation and) exhalation (prāṇa), that (has flowed outwards,) is (also) external. By its association with (the limited perceiver,) whose form is darkness, it has exited by means of the pores of the skin. (But it) is made to wander (internally) by all the causal (deities) (in the Wheels over which they preside). (The yogi,) having firmly fixed (āsthāya) (his attention) on the imperceptible Centre, should make (the breath) circulate and then emit (it) (visṛjet). By being linked (yogena) to the upward movement of the union (of the breath), he should pierce through the five knots and contemplate (vicintayet) the pair (of breaths) in a state of (intimate) union within the Abode of the Centre. He should not attend to (and connect) (saṁdhāna) the (limited) Self, Void, the outer (world), Mantra and the body. This initiation bestows the attainment of Śivahood to all living beings. (with 29.278cd–281)
