## Mūla

वामो वायं विधिः कार्यो मुक्तिमार्गावलम्बिभिः ।
वर्णमन्त्रविभेदेन पृथग्वा तत्फलार्थिभिः ॥ ^mula

vāmo vāyaṁ vidhiḥ kāryo muktimārgāvalambibhiḥ |
varṇamantravibhedena pṛthag vā tatphalārthibhiḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, October 2024 – December 2024
> p. 27 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=26>))
>
> *Those who take their support from the path to liberation should (first) perform this procedure of the left (concerning Śakti).*[^47] Or else those who do not desire that fruit[^48] (should perform them) separately with (the correspondingly) different mantras and phonemes.

[^47]: MV 8/44-46ab is concisely paraphrased as TĀ 15/254-255ab. MV 8/46ab is quoted in TĀv ad 15/255cd.
[^48]: Read *vātatphalārthibhiḥ* for *vā tatphalārthibhiḥ.*
