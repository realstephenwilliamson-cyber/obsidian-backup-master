# LXX Psalms 09

← [[LXX Psalms 08]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 10]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 009]] and [[Psalms 010]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 009]] and [[BCP Psalm 010]] (Heb 9 = LXX 9:1–21; Heb 10 = LXX 9:22–39) · [[Psalms numbering]]

**9:1** For the end, a Psalm of David, concerning the secrets of the Son.  ^9-1

**9:2** I will give thanks to thee, O Lord, with my whole heart; I will recount all thy wonderful works.  ^9-2

**9:3** I will be glad and exult in thee: I will sing to thy name, O thou Most High.  ^9-3

**9:4** When mine enemies are turned back, they shall be feeble and perish at thy presence.  ^9-4

**9:5** For thou hast maintained my cause and my right; thou satest on the throne, that judgest righteousness.  ^9-5

**9:6** Thou hast rebuked the nations, and the ungodly one has perished; thou hast blotted out their name for ever, even for ever and ever.  ^9-6

**9:7** The swords of the enemy have failed utterly; and thou hast destroyed cities: their memorial has been destroyed with a noise,  ^9-7

**9:8** but the Lord endures for ever: he has prepared his throne for judgment.  ^9-8

**9:9** And he will judge the world in righteousness, he will judge the nations in uprightness.  ^9-9

**9:10** The Lord also is become a refuge for the poor, a seasonable help, in affliction.  ^9-10

**9:11** And let them that know thy name hope in thee: for thou, O Lord, hast not failed them that diligently seek thee.  ^9-11

**9:12** Sing praises to the Lord, who dwells in Sion: declare his dealings among the nations.  ^9-12

**9:13** For he remembered them, *in* making inquisition for blood: he has not forgotten the supplication of the poor.  ^9-13

**9:14** Have mercy upon me, O Lord; look upon my affliction *which I suffer* of mine enemies, thou that liftest me up from the gates of death:  ^9-14

**9:15** that I may declare all thy praises in the gates of the daughter of Sion: I will exult in thy salvation.  ^9-15

**9:16** The heathen are caught in the destruction which they planned: in the very snare which they hid is their foot taken.  ^9-16

**9:17** The Lord is known as executing judgments: the sinner is taken in the works of his hands. A song of Pause.  ^9-17

**9:18** Let sinners be driven away into Hades, *even* all the nations that forget God.  ^9-18

**9:19** For the poor shall not be forgotten for ever: the patience of the needy ones shall not perish for ever.  ^9-19

**9:20** Arise, O Lord, let not man prevail: let the heathen be judged before thee.  ^9-20

**9:21** Appoint, O Lord, a lawgiver over them: let the heathen know that they are men. Pause.  ^9-21

**9:22** Why standest thou afar off, O Lord? *why* dost thou overlook *us* in times of need, in affliction?[^1]  ^9-22

**9:23** While the ungodly one acts proudly, the poor is hotly pursued: *the wicked* are taken in the crafty counsels which they imagine.[^2]  ^9-23

**9:24** Because the sinner praises himself for the desires of his heart; and the unjust one blesses himself.  ^9-24

**9:25** The sinner has provoked the Lord: according to the abundance of his pride he will not seek after *him*: God is not before him.[^3]  ^9-25

**9:26** His ways are profane at all times; thy judgments are removed from before him: he will gain the mastery over all his enemies.[^4]  ^9-26

**9:27** For he has said in his heart, I shall not be moved, *continuing* without evil from generation to generation.  ^9-27

**9:28** Whose mouth is full of cursing, and bitterness, and fraud: under his tongue are trouble and pain.  ^9-28

**9:29** He lies in wait with rich *men* in secret places, in order to slay the innocent: his eyes are set against the poor.[^5]  ^9-29

**9:30** He lies in wait in secret as a lion in his den: he lies in wait to ravish the poor, to ravish the poor when he draws him *after him*: he will bring him down in his snare.  ^9-30

**9:31** He will bow down and fall when he has mastered the poor.  ^9-31

**9:32** For he has said in his heart, God has forgotten: he has turned away his face so as never to look.  ^9-32

**9:33** Arise, O Lord God; let thy hand be lifted up: forget not the poor.  ^9-33

**9:34** Wherefore has the wicked provoked God? for he has said in his heart, He will not require *it*.  ^9-34

**9:35** Thou seest *it*; for thou dost observe trouble and wrath, to deliver them into thy hands: the poor has been left to thee; thou wast a helper to the orphan.  ^9-35

**9:36** Break thou the arm of the sinner and wicked man: his sin shall be sought for, and shall not be found.  ^9-36

**9:37** The Lord shall reign for ever, even for ever and ever: ye Gentiles shall perish out of his land.  ^9-37

**9:38** The Lord has heard the desire of the poor: thine ear has inclined to the preparation of their heart;  ^9-38

**9:39** to plead for the orphan and afflicted, that man may no more boast upon the earth.  ^9-39

[^1]: 9:22 *Note.*—Here begins a change of numbers as compared with the *Heb.* which continues to Ps. 146, this psalm having no number, and the eleventh being called the tenth. The following verses 22-39 are numbered 1-18 in the original text.
[^2]: 9:23 *Gr.* inflamed, as in a fever.
[^3]: 9:25 *Or,* anger.
[^4]: 9:26 *Gr.* profaned.
[^5]: 9:29 *Gr.* look.
