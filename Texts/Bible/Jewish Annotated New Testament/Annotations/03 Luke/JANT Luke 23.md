### 23.1–5: Jesus before Pilate

*[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-1|Text]], p. 162* · ([[Matthew 27#^27-1|Mt 27.1–14]]; [[Mark 15#^15-1|Mk 15.1–5]]; [[John 18#^18-28|Jn 18.28–38]]). ^23-1

**[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-1|1]]:** *Pilate*, see 3.1n. ^23-1-2

**[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-2|2]]:** *Perverting our nation*, perhaps alluding to [[Torah 47 - Re'eh#^13-2|Deut 13.1–11]] on false prophets. *Forbidding us to pay taxes*, see 20.25. *A king*, see 19.38. ^23-2

**[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-3|3]]:** *You say so*, does not answer the question. ^23-3

**[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-4|4]]:** *I find no basis*, see 23.14,22,41; [[John 19#^19-4|Jn 19.4]],[[John 19#^19-6|6]]; [[Acts 13#^13-28|Acts 13.28]]. The Gospels portray Pilate as seeking to free Jesus but succumbing to pressure by the Jewish rulers and population. ^23-4

### 23.6–12: Jesus before Herod

*[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-6|Text]], p. 163* · An episode unique to Luke’s Gospel. Son of Herod the Great (see 3.1n.), *Herod* \[Antipas\] arrested (3.19–20) and beheaded (9.9) John the Baptist. ^23-6

**[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-7|7]]:** *Sent him off to Herod*, perhaps alluding to [[Psalms 002#^2-2|Ps 2.2]] (kings taking council against the “anointed”). ^23-7

**[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-8|8]]:** See 9.9; [[Acts 04#^4-27|Acts 4.27–28]]. ^23-8

**[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-9|9]]:** *Gave him no answer*, see [[Matthew 27#^27-12|Mt 27.12]]; [[Mark 15#^15-5|Mk 15.5]]. ^23-9

**[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-10|10]]:** *Chief priests and the scribes*, see 19.47n. ^23-10

**[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-11|11]]:** *Mocked him*, see [[John 19#^19-2|Jn 19.2–3]]. **23.13–16 \[17\]: Pilate declares Jesus innocent.** ^23-11

**[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-13|13]]:** *Chief priests* (see 19.47n.), together with the *people* condemn Jesus. ^23-13

**[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-14|14]]:** *Not found this man guilty*, see vv 4,22,41. ^23-14

**[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-15|15]]:** *Herod*, see vv 6–12. ^23-15

**[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-16|16]]:** *Have him flogged*, perhaps an attempt to pacify the crowd, which is seeking Jesus’ death. **\[17\]:** Scribal addition from [[Mark 15#^15-6|Mk 15.6]]. ^23-16

### 23.18–25: The people condemn Jesus

*[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-18|Text]], p. 163* · ([[Matthew 27#^27-15|Mt 27.15–26]]; [[Mark 15#^15-6|Mk 15.6–15]]; [[John 18#^18-39|Jn 18.39–40]]; [[John 19#^19-16|19.16]]). See [[Acts 03#^3-13|Acts 3.13–14]]. ^23-18

**[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-18|18]]:** *Barabbas* ([[Matthew 27#^27-15|Mt 27.15–23]]; [[Mark 15#^15-6|Mk 15.6–14]]; [[John 18#^18-39|Jn 18.39–40]]), Aramaic, “Son of the Father.” Luke lacks Mark’s reference to the (unlikely) custom of releasing a prisoner and so makes the crowds’ demand for Barabbas inexplicable. ^23-18-2

**[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-19|19]]:** *Insurrection*, Barabbas is guilty of sedition, the charge leveled against Jesus (23.38). ^23-19

**[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-20|20–25]]:** *Wanting to release Jesus*, Luke places the blame for Jesus’ death on the Jewish people (see [[Acts 02#^2-23|Acts 2.23]]; [[Acts 03#^3-13|3.13–15]]; [[Acts 04#^4-10|4.10]]; [[Acts 05#^5-30|5.30]]; [[Acts 07#^7-52|7.52]]; [[Acts 13#^13-27|13.27–29]]). ^23-20

**[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-25|25]]:** *Handed Jesus over*, the language can suggest that Pilate gave Jesus to the Jewish crowd to execute. ^23-25

### 23.26–31: Journey to the cross

*[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-26|Text]], p. 163* · ([[Matthew 27#^27-31|Mt 27.31b–32]]; [[Mark 15#^15-20|Mk 15.20b–21]]; [[John 19#^19-17|Jn 19.17a]]). ^23-26

**[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-26|26]]:** *Cyrene*, in Libya. *The cross*, the horizontal beam. ^23-26-2

**[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-27|27]]:** *Beating their breasts*, indicating mourning rather than repentance (23.48). add verse 17, *Now he was obliged to release someone for them at the festival* ^23-27

**[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-28|28–31]]:** See 19.41–44; 21.23–24. ^23-28

**[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-29|29]]:** *Blessed are the barren*, see 21.23. ^23-29

**[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-30|30]]:** Suggestive of [[Hosea 10#^10-8|Hos 10.8]]. ^23-30

**[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-31|31]]:** Either the analogy suggests that if the innocent Jesus suffers, the guilty Jerusalem will suffer more, or if evil occurs with Jesus present, much more will occur in his absence ([[1 Peter 04#^4-17|1 Pet 4.17–18]]). ^23-31

### 23.32–49: The crucifixion

*[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-32|Text]], p. 164* · ([[Matthew 27#^27-33|Mt 27.33–43]]; [[Mark 15#^15-22|Mk 15.22–32a]]; [[John 19#^19-17|Jn 19.17b–27]]). ^23-32

**[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-32|32]]:** *Criminals*, Luke does not specify their crimes. ^23-32-2

**[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-33|33]]:** *Skull* (Gk “kranion”), Matthew and Mark give the Aram “Golgotha.” ^23-33

**[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-34|34]]:** See translators, note *b*; cf. [[Acts 07#^7-60|Acts 7.60]]. *Cast lots*, alluding to [[Psalms 022#^22-19|Ps 22.18]]. ^23-34

**[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-36|36]]:** *Sour wine*, alluding to [[Psalms 069#^69-22|Ps 69.21]]. ^23-36

**[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-39|39–43]]:** See [[Matthew 27#^27-44|Mt 27.44]]; [[Mark 15#^15-32|Mk 15.32b]]. ^23-39

**[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-41|41]]:** *Has done nothing wrong*, a point affirmed by Pilate and Herod Antipas (vv 4,14,22). ^23-41

**[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-42|42]]:** *Remember*, a psalmic cry for aid ([[Psalms 074#^74-2|Ps 74.2]],[[Psalms 074#^74-18|18]],[[Psalms 074#^74-22|22]]). ^23-42

**[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-43|43]]:** *Paradise*, originally referring to the Garden of Eden ([[LXX Genesis 02#^2-8|Gen 2.8–10]]\[LXX\]); here the home of the righteous dead prior to the resurrection; see 16.22. *B. Avod. Zar.* 18a reports that R. Hanina b. Teradion, arrested for teaching Torah, is burned to death “wrapped in the Scroll of the Torah . . . His disciples called out, ‘Rabbi, what do you see?’ He answered, ‘The parchments are being burnt but the letters are soaring on high . . .’ The Executioner then said to him, ‘Rabbi, if I raise the flame and take away the tufts of wool from over thy heart, will you cause me to enter into the life to come?’ ‘Yes,’ he replied . . . The Executioner then jumped and threw himself into the fire. And a bath qol \[heavenly voice\] exclaimed: ‘R. Hanina b. Teradion and the Executioner have been assigned to the world to come.’ ” ^23-43

**[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-44|44–48]]:** See [[Matthew 27#^27-45|Mt 27.45–54]]; [[Mark 15#^15-33|Mk 15.33–39]]; Jn *Jesus . . . what they are doing* c Or *the Christ* d Other ancient authorities add *written in Greek and Latin and Hebrew* (that is, *Aramaic*) e Or *blaspheming* f Other ancient authorities read *in* g Or *earth* h Or *the sun was eclipsed.* Other ancient authorities read *the sun was darkened* i Or *righteous* 19.28–30. ^23-44

**[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-45|45]]:** *Sun’s light failed*, the translation is uncertain; see Am 8.9 for such eschatological signs. *Curtain of the temple*, see [[Torah 19 - Terumah#^26-31|Ex 26.31–35]]. *Torn in two*, indicating mourning ([[Torah 09 - Vayeshev#^37-29|Gen 37.29]],[[Torah 09 - Vayeshev#^37-34|34]]; [[Torah 10 - Miketz#^44-13|44.13]]; [[Torah 37 - Sh'lach#^14-6|Num 14.6]]; Josh 7.6; 11.35; etc.) and perhaps prefiguring the Temple’s destruction. The point is not, contrary to some readings, new access to God, since God had always been accessible outside of the Temple. ^23-45

**[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-46|46]]:** *Spirit* (Gk “pneuma”), not the particular “Holy Spirit” that accompanies Jesus but the spirit that marks life for all ([[LXX Genesis 06#^6-3|Gen 6.3]]\[LXX\]). ^23-46

**[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-47|47]]:** *Innocent*, Gk “dikaios,” lit., “righteous.” ^23-47

**[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-48|48]]:** *Beating their breasts*, see v 27n. ^23-48

**[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-49|49]]:** See [[Matthew 27#^27-55|Mt 27.55–56]]; [[Mark 15#^15-40|Mk 15.40–41]]; [[John 19#^19-25|Jn 19.25–27]]. *Including the women*, see 23.55; see also 8.1–3; 24.10. ^23-49

### 23.50–56: Jesus’ entombment

*[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-50|Text]], p. 165* · ([[Matthew 27#^27-57|Mt 27.57–60]]; [[Mark 15#^15-42|Mk 15.42–46]]; [[John 19#^19-38|Jn 19.38–42]]). ^23-50

**[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-50|50]]:** *Council*, see 22.66n. ^23-50-2

**[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-51|51]]:** *Arimathea* (likely from Heb “Ramataim,” cf. Ramoth, Ramah), near Jerusalem. ^23-51

**[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-53|53]]:** *Linen cloth*, although not the same term as the “swaddling clothes” or “bands of cloth” (2.12), the connection of those cloths to the shepherd’s “sign” and the linen cloths to the resurrection should not be discount-ed (24.12). *Laid it in a rock-hewn tomb*, Luke does not record who cared for the bodies of the other two crucified men. ^23-53

**[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-54|54]]:** *Day of Preparation* (for the Sabbath), Friday, before sunset. *Sabbath was beginning*, at sundown. ^23-54

**[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-55|55–56]]:** See [[Matthew 27#^27-61|Mt 27.61]]; [[Mark 15#^15-47|Mk 15.47–16.1]]; *m. Shabb*. 23.5. ^23-55

**[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-55|55]]:** See 24.10. ^23-55-2

**[[NRSVue Luke 23#^23-56|56]]:** *Spices and ointments*, were applied to the corpse; see [[Mark 16#^16-1|Mk 16.1]]; [[John 19#^19-40|Jn 19.40]]; *m. Shabb*. 23.5 (“They prepare all that is needed for a corpse. They anoint and rinse it . . . ”). Preparation of the corpse should precede burial. The women presume that Jesus will not be resurrected. *They rested*, see [[Torah 17 - Yitro#^20-10|Ex 20.10]]. ^23-56
