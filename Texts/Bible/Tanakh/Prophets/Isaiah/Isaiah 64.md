## Chapter 64

**64:1** As when fire kindles brushwood,  
And fire makes water boil—  
To make Your name known to Your adversaries  
So that nations will tremble at Your Presence, ^64-1

**64:2** When You did wonders we dared not hope for,  
You came down  
And mountains quaked before You. ^64-2

**64:3** Such things had never been heard or noted.  
No eye has seen [them], O God, but You,  
Who act for those who trust in You.[^1] ^64-3

**64:4** Yet you have struck him who would gladly do justice,  
And remember You in Your ways.  
It is because You are angry that we have sinned;  
We have been steeped in them from of old,  
And can we be saved?[^2] ^64-4

**64:5** We have all become like an unclean thing,  
And all our virtues like a filthy rag.  
We are all withering like leaves,  
And our iniquities, like a wind, carry us off. ^64-5

**64:6** Yet no one invokes Your name,  
Rouses himself to cling to You.  
For You have hidden Your face from us,  
And made us melt because of[^3] our iniquities. ^64-6

**64:7** But now, O LORD, You are our Father;  
We are the clay, and You are the Potter,  
We are all the work of Your hands. ^64-7

**64:8** Be not implacably angry, O LORD,  
Do not remember iniquity forever.  
Oh, look down to Your people, to us all! ^64-8

**64:9** Your holy cities have become a desert:  
Zion has become a desert,  
Jerusalem a desolation. ^64-9

**64:10** Our holy Temple, our pride,  
Where our fathers praised You,  
Has been consumed by fire:  
And all that was dear to us is ruined. ^64-10

**64:11** At such things will You restrain Yourself, O LORD,  
Will You stand idly by and let us suffer so heavily? ^64-11

[^1]: Heb. “Him.”
[^2]: Emendation yields “Because You have hidden Yourself we have offended.” For the thought cf. 63.17.
[^3]: Emendation yields “delivered us into the hands of…”
