## Mūla

नासौ विज्ञानविश्वस्तो नासत्यं भ्रष्ट एव सः ।
ज्ञानानाश्वस्तचित्तं तं वचोमात्रेण शास्त्रितम् ॥ ^mula

nāsau vijñānaviśvasto nāsatyaṃ bhraṣṭa eva saḥ |
jñānānāśvastacittaṃ taṃ vacomātreṇa śāstritam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 10 - Chapter Fifteen, part 9#^pg357|p. 339]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=357>))
> Dyczkowski's numbering differs here; his markers: (559-590) (586cd-588ab), (587-588) (584cd-586ab).
>
> When the ‘regular’ initiate, whose success depends solely on the teacher, also possesses a fully awakened consciousness (vibodha), he should consider (his) teacher to be (even) more than a teacher (gurutama), by virtue of the respect (he has) for that awakened consciousness. Thus, one who, after he has attained knowledge (vijñāna), (yet) does not have great veneration for his teacher, has no faith in the knowledge (he has received). He is not zealous. (Indeed) he is just a fallen soul. (with 15.584cd–586ab)
>
> (A teacher who) does not have faith in the knowledge (of the scriptures), and is said to know the scriptures only by (their) words (without knowing the reality they teach), and with (his) heart criticises (this) knowledge, should certainly not be venerated. One should not make such a teacher (one’s own, or) if one has done so, abandon him. One should not consider Vaiṣṇava and other such teachers to be the main ones (as one does true Śaiva teachers). (with 15.586cd–588ab)
