## Mūla

अधोमुखं सदा स्थाप्यं पूजितं पूजने पुनः ।
तत्पात्रमुन्मुखं तच्च रिक्तं कुर्यान्न तादृशम् ॥ ^mula

adhomukhaṃ sadā sthāpyaṃ pūjitaṃ pūjane punaḥ |
tatpātramunmukhaṃ tacca riktaṃ kuryānna tādṛśam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 18 - Chapter Twenty-Seven#^pg438|p. 418]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=438>))
>
> (The sacrificial vessel) should always be installed facing down and worshipped (like that when it is consecrated). However, in the course of worship, that vessel (should be placed in its normal position,) facing towards (the worshipper, taking care) that it should not be (completely) emptied. It should be just the same at the end of worship, (as the officiant drinks from it, and so) completely fills (and satiates) himself with (the aesthetic delight - rasa - of) its (sacrificial) fluids (rasa). (Then,) the (sagacious and) skilled (officiant,) having worshipped (again), should (again) place it upside down. (with 27.42–43)
