# Uḍḍāmareśvaratantra — Chapter 14

[[Uddamaresvaratantra 13|← Chapter 13]] · [[Uddamaresvaratantra|← index]] · [[Uddamaresvaratantra 15|Chapter 15 →]]

klīṃ kāmāture kāmamekhale viṣayiṇi vararati bhagavati amukaṃ me vaśaṃ kuru vaśaṃ kuru klīṃ namaḥ svāhā

^s1

anena mantreṇa bhojanakāle saptagrāsān saptavārābhimantritān bhuñjīta

^s2

saptame divase strī vā puruṣo vā vaśībhavati svaṃ ca dadāti

^s3

oṃ huṃ svāhā

^s4

imaṃ mantraṃ trisaṃdhyaṃ japet śatrunāśo bhavati

^s5

klīṃ kāli kāli mahākāli kole kinyā svāhā

^s6

imaṃ mantraṃ pūrvam ayutaṃ japtvā saṃdhyākāle sahasraikaṃ homayet tataḥ kaṅkālī varadā bhavati suvarṇacatuṣṭayaṃ pratyahaṃ dadāti

^s7

oṃ drīṃ drīṃ draṃ draiṃ drauṃ draḥ huṃ namaḥ svāhā

^s8

anena mantreṇa pūrvam evāyutaṃ japtvā kevalam ājyaṃ hunet asmād ākarṣaṇaṃ bhavati

^s9

oṃ drauṃ drauṃ hīṃ hīṃ huṃ namaḥ svāhā

^s10

anena mantreṇa pūrvavidhinā japtvārdhamāsād ākarṣaṇaṃ bhavati

^s11

oṃ haṃ oṃ hūṃ hūṃ hīṃ svāhā

^s12

iti pūrvakhaṅgabhedaḥ

^s13

sarvasaṃjīvanīmantraḥ oṃ huṃ drīṃ draṃ drauṃ draḥ huṃ huṃ

^s14

anena mantreṇa sarvajvaranāśanaṃ bhavati

^s15

dīṃ haṃ sini svāhā

^s16

anena mantreṇa sarvajanavaśīkaraṇam

^s17

oṃ hīṃ namaḥ hīṃ phaṭ svāhā

^s18

imaṃ mantraṃ sādhyanāmnāyutaṃ japet śavāsanasthito hṛdayaṃ na prakāśayet [... au4 Zeichenjh] amukīṃ tāṃ [... au4 Zeichenjh] saṃgṛhya guṭikāṃ kṛtvā mukhe prakṣipya vidyādharatvaṃ bhavati

^s19

oṃ hīṃ huṃ namaḥ

^s20

imaṃ mantraṃ pūrvavidhinā japet pādukāsiddhir bhavati

^s21

oṃ kṣaṃ kṣaṃ hrīṃ huṃ phaṭ svāhā

^s22

imaṃ mantraṃ pūrvakrameṇa japed vetālasiddhir bhavati

^s23

oṃ hrīṃ svāhā

^s24

anena mantreṇa narakapālaṃ gṛhītvā tasmin naratailaṃ dattvā tasmin vāyasacakṣuḥsaṃvardhinīṃ vartikāṃ prajvālayet kṛṣṇapakṣāmāvāsyāyāṃ śanivāre andhakūpe śmaśāne vā śūnyāyatane vā kajjalaṃ pātayitavyaṃ tāvat kālaṃ pūrvoktaṃ mantraṃ japet yāvatā kālena vartiśeṣaṃ prajvalati avasāne prabhūtabalidānaṃ kartavyaṃ tatra balistambham ādāya tena siddhāñjanenāñjitanayanaḥ surāsurair api na dṛśyate 'nyalokasya kā kathā

^s25

oṃ hraṃ hrīṃ hūṃ hraiṃ hrauṃ hraḥ hrīṃ hrīṃ iti śaṅkhinīvidyā

^s26

oṃ hīṃ huṃ iti puṣpāñjalivedhaḥ

^s27

huṃ huṃ iti huṃkāravedhaḥ

^s28

oṃ hrīṃ huṃ ity ālayavedhaḥ

^s29

hrāṃ śivāvedhamantraḥ

^s30

hrīṃ iti bhramarāvartasaṃghaṭṭavedhaḥ

^s31

oṃ drīṃ huṃ chaḥ chaḥ svāhā

^s32

lohatriśūlaṃ kṛtvā rudhireṇa viṣaṃ piṣṭvā tena triśūlaṃ liptvāyutenābhimantritaṃ kṛtvā yasya nāmnā bhūmau nikhanet tasya śīghraṃ mṛtyur bhavati

^s33

oṃ hrīṃkāri hūṃkāri kapāli samāvedhaṃ bandhuṃ napuṃsakaṃ mahāśaye abhayaṃkari amarākhyaṃ kuru kuru jvaraṃ hana hana ākrośāt kolāhalaṃ parāṃ śaktyākarṣiṇīṃ sarvaśaktiprasaṅginīṃ śāntike huṃ phaṭ svāhā

^s34

imāṃ mahāvidyāṃ śatruvaśaṃkarīṃ manasā smaret sa sarvatra nirbhayo bhavati

^s35

oṃ oṃ oṃ haṃ haṃ haṃ haṃ sāṃ sāṃ sāṃ sāṃ imaṃ mantraṃ japitvā sthāvarajaṅgamaviṣanāśanaṃ bhavati

^s36

oṃ asthi yaṃsthi vidrānidrā saṃnividyā rā ṭaṃ ṭīṃ drīṃ samāsaṃ maṭaṃṭīṃ chaṃ chaṃ

^s37

anena mantreṇa kākapakṣaṃ sahasraikaṃ hunet yasya nāmnā tam uccāṭayati

^s38

strīṃ haṃ anena mantreṇāyute japte sati kavitvavidyā bhavati strīmaṇiśakunavidyāṃ hi saṃjapet jhaṭiti kavitvaṃ karoti

^s39

haṃ aiṃ haṃ haṃ aiṃ vada vada vāgvādini svāhā

^s40

imaṃ mantraṃ saptavāraṃ japtvādhikādhikaṃ kavitvaṃ ca karoti

^s41

oṃ haṃ chaṃ chaṃ chaṃ aiṃ namaḥ svāhā

^s42

sahasrajapādadhikādhikaṃ kavitvado 'yaṃ mantraḥ

^s43

oṃ kaṃ khaṃ gaṃ ghaṃ caṃ chaṃ chaṃ avilambaṃ vaktuḥ stambhayati vācam ālokanāt

^s44

huṃ huṃ huṃ huṃ khaṃ khaṃ khaṃ khaṃ chaṃ chaṃ vācāṃ stambhinī vāyusaṃjīvanī vidyā

^s45

drīṃ ālokavedhaḥ parokṣavedhaḥ

^s46

hrīṃ sarvavedhanamantraḥ

^s47

draṃ drauṃ drauṃ drauṃ draṃ amukaṃ bheji bheji hrīṃ chaṃ chaṃ chaṃ iti visphoṭakasaṃjīvinī avalokanāt kāryasiddhikarī

^s48

oṃ drāṃ drīṃ pūrvarākṣasān nāśaya sarvāṇi bhañjaya saṃtuṣṭā mohaya mahāsvane huṃ huṃ phaṭ svāhā iti sarvabhūtamāraṇamantraḥ

^s49

oṃ hrīṃ saḥ drāṃ chaḥ chaḥ chaḥ dūrvākṣīrahomena sarvaśāntikarī vidyā

^s50

huṃ pañcāṇḍaṃ cāṇḍaṃ drīṃ phaṭ svāhā anena mantreṇa manuṣyāsthikīlakaṃ saptāṅgulaṃ sahasradhābhimantritaṃ yasya gehe nikhanet tasya kūṭam utsādinaṃ bhavati uddhṛte sati punaḥ svāsthyaṃ bhavati

^s51

huṃ kṣaṃ amukaṃ phaṭ svāhā anena mantreṇa pecakapakṣimāṃsaṃ kaṭutailena saṃyutaṃ homayet sahasrahomena śatruṃ nipātayati

^s52

oṃ śrīṃ śrīṃ hrīṃ hrīṃ dhuṃ dhuṃ haṃ haḥ svāhā

^s53

iyaṃ hi trailokyavijayānamnī vidyā

^s54

manasā smaret sarvakāmaprado 'yaṃ mantraḥ

^s55

oṃ aiṃ hrīṃ śrīṃ klīṃ viśvarūpiṇi piśācini bhūtabhaviṣyādikaṃ vada vada me karṇe kathaya kathaya huṃ phaṭ svāhā

^s56

imaṃ mantraṃ śuklapratipadam ārabhya pūrṇimāparyantaṃ sahasraikaṃ trisaṃdhyaṃ japet pratyahaṃ pūtaṃ jalaṃ saghṛtaṃ bhaktapiṇḍaṃ harmyopari rātrau dadyāt trailokye yādṛśī tādṛśī vārttā sādhakasya karṇe bhūtabhaviṣyādikaṃ ca kathayati

^s57

oṃ raṃ rāṃ saṃ sāṃ laṃ lāṃ haṃ haḥ saṃ saḥ khaṃ khaḥ taṃ taḥ dhaṃ saṃ sphuṃ sphaḥ hrīṃ huṃ huṃ huṃ kṣīṃ kṣīṃ kṣauṃ sauṃ saḥ chaṃ chaḥ dhaṃ saḥ sphuṃ sphaḥ hrīṃ huṃ huṃ huṃ kṣīṃ kṣīṃ kṣauṃ saṃ phaṃ phaḥ huṃ phaṭ svāhā

^s58

ayaṃ samastaviṣanāśanamantraḥ

^s59

sacarācare oṃ sacarācare oṃ huṃ huṃ haṃ haṃ haṃ haṃ haṃ haṃ haṃ haṃ haṃ haṃ kṣakṣakṣaḥ hasaḥ oṃ saṃ huṃ hrīṃ sarveśa viṣṇubalena śaṃkaradarpeṇa vāyuvegena ravitejasā candrakāntyā vairaṃ bāṇaśūrpaṇaṃ sarvaṃ viṣaharaṃ vada sarvarakṣāṃsi hi nāśaya 2 bhañjaya 2 sarvaduṣṭān mohaya 2 deva huṃ phaṭ svāhā huṃ phaṭ svāhā

^s60

imaṃ mantram aṣṭottarasahasraṃ śataṃ vā japtvā saptame divase siddhiḥ samākarṣaṇaṃ bhavati

^s61

oṃ hrīṃ amukaṃ chaḥ chaḥ anena mantreṇa mānuṣyāsthimayaṃ kīlakam ekādaśāṅgulaṃ sahasreṇābhimantritaṃ yasya gṛhe nikhanet tasya kūṭaṃ cotsādanaṃ bhavati uddhṛte punaḥ svāsthyaṃ bhavati

^s62

oṃ hrīṃ kāla kaṅkāla mahākāla karālavadana amukaṃ gṛhṇa triśūlena bhinddhi 2 khaḍgena chinddhi 2 huṃ phaṭ chaḥ chaḥ svāhā

^s63

anena mantreṇa vibhītakakāṣṭhakīlakam ekaviṃśatyaṅgulaṃ sahasreṇābhimantritaṃ yasya gṛhadvāre nikhanyate tasya sadyo dehanipātanaṃ bhavati

^s64

oṃ hrīṃ klīṃ śrīṃ huṃ amukaṃ chaḥ chaḥ

^s65

anena mantreṇa siddhikāṣṭhamayaṃ kīlakaṃ navāṅgulaṃ sahasreṇābhimantritaṃ yasya gṛhe nikhanyate sa vaśyo bhavati

^s66

oṃ hrīṃ śrīṃ klīṃ mātaṃgini aiṃ hrīṃ śrīṃ klīṃ svāhā

^s67

anena mantreṇa rājikāṃ lavaṇaghṛtamiśritāṃ yasya nāmnā saha homayet tāṃ striyaṃ puruṣaṃ vā vaśayaty ākarṣaṇaṃ ca karoti

^s68

oṃ hrīṃ hūṃ chaḥ chaḥ

^s69

anena mantreṇa vāḍavakāṣṭhamayaṃ kīlakaṃ trayodaśāṅgulaṃ sahasreṇābhimantritaṃ yasya gṛhe nikhanet sa cakṣurbhyām andho bhavati

^s70

oṃ chaḥ oṃ chaḥ chaḥ

^s71

anena mantreṇa bilvakāṣṭhasya kīlakaṃ daśāṅgulaṃ sahasreṇābhimantritaṃ yasya gṛhe nikhanet saparivārasya tasya pretatvaṃ bhavati

^s72

oṃ hrīṃ amukīṃ me prayaccha svāhā

^s73

anena mantreṇa pāṭalākāṣṭhamayaṃ pañcāṅgulaṃ kīlakaṃ sahasreṇābhimantritaṃ yasya nāmnā devatāyatane nikhanet sa śīghraṃ kanyāṃ labhate

^s74
