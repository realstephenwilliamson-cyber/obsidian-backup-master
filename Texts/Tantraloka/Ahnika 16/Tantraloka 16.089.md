## Mūla

अनुसन्धिबलान्ते च समासव्यासभेदतः ।
कुर्यादत्यन्तमभ्यस्तमन्यान्तर्भावपूरितम् ॥ ^mula

anusandhibalānte ca samāsavyāsabhedataḥ |
kuryādatyantamabhyastamanyāntarbhāvapūritam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 05 - Chapter Sixteen, part 1#^pg62|p. 42]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=62>))
>
> (The teacher) should practice intensely within the force (bala) (of his) unifying contemplation (anusandhāna) to make the inner state (of all these elements, both) singly and collectively, completely full one within the other. Then, once again, he should (contemplate and) unite (them) with his thought to (their essential) iva nature (in the following way). (with 16.89–90ab)
