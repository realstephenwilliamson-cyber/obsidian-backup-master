## Chapter 1

**1:1** The proverbs of Solomon son of David, king of Israel: ^1-1

**1:2** For learning wisdom and discipline;  
For understanding words of discernment; ^1-2

**1:3** For acquiring the discipline for success,  
Righteousness, justice, and equity; ^1-3

**1:4** For endowing the simple with shrewdness,  
The young with knowledge and foresight. ^1-4

**1:5** —The wise man, hearing them, will gain more wisdom;  
The discerning man will learn to be adroit; ^1-5

**1:6** For understanding proverb and epigram,  
The words of the wise and their riddles. ^1-6

**1:7** The fear of the LORD is the beginning[^1] of knowledge;  
Fools despise wisdom and discipline. ^1-7

**1:8** My son, heed the discipline of your father,  
And do not forsake the instruction of your mother; ^1-8

**1:9** For they are a graceful wreath upon your head,  
A necklace about your throat. ^1-9

**1:10** My son, if sinners entice you, do not yield; ^1-10

**1:11** If they say, “Come with us,  
Let us set an ambush to shed blood,  
Let us lie in wait for the innocent  
(Without cause!) ^1-11

**1:12** Like Sheol, let us swallow them alive;  
Whole, like those who go down into the Pit. ^1-12

**1:13** We shall obtain every precious treasure;  
We shall fill our homes with loot. ^1-13

**1:14** Throw in your lot with us;  
We shall all have a common purse.” ^1-14

**1:15** My son, do not set out with them;  
Keep your feet from their path. ^1-15

**1:16** For their feet run to evil;  
They hurry to shed blood. ^1-16

**1:17** In the eyes of every winged creature  
The outspread net means nothing. ^1-17

**1:18** But they lie in ambush for their own blood;  
They lie in wait for their own lives. ^1-18

**1:19** Such is the fate of all who pursue unjust gain;  
It takes the life of its possessor. ^1-19

**1:20** Wisdom[^2] cries aloud in the streets,  
Raises her voice in the squares. ^1-20

**1:21** At the head of the busy streets she calls;  
At the entrance of the gates, in the city, she speaks out: ^1-21

**1:22** “How long will you simple ones love simplicity,  
You scoffers be eager to scoff,  
You dullards hate knowledge? ^1-22

**1:23** You are indifferent to my rebuke;  
I will now speak my mind to you,  
And let you know my thoughts. ^1-23

**1:24** Since you refused me when I called,  
And paid no heed when I extended my hand, ^1-24

**1:25** You spurned all my advice,  
And would not hear my rebuke, ^1-25

**1:26** I will laugh at your calamity,  
And mock when terror comes upon you, ^1-26

**1:27** When terror comes like a disaster,  
And calamity arrives like a whirlwind,  
When trouble and distress come upon you. ^1-27

**1:28** Then they shall call me but I will not answer;  
They shall seek me but not find me. ^1-28

**1:29** Because they hated knowledge,  
And did not choose fear of the LORD; ^1-29

**1:30** They refused my advice,  
And disdained all my rebukes, ^1-30

**1:31** They shall eat the fruit of their ways,  
And have their fill of their own counsels. ^1-31

**1:32** The tranquillity of the simple will kill them,  
And the complacency of dullards will destroy them. ^1-32

**1:33** But he who listens to me will dwell in safety,  
Untroubled by the terror of misfortune.” ^1-33

[^1]: Or “best part.”
[^2]: In Proverbs, wisdom is personified as a woman.
