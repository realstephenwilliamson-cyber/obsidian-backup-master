# Psalm 110 (NRSVue)

## Assurance of victory for God’s priest-king

*Of David. A Psalm.*

**110:1** The LORD says to my lord, “Sit at my right hand until I make your enemies your footstool.” ^110-1

**110:2** The LORD sends out from Zion your mighty scepter. Rule in the midst of your foes. ^110-2

**110:3** Your people will offer themselves willingly on the day you lead your forces on the holy mountains.^[Heb mss Symmachus Jerome: MT *in holy splendor*] From the womb of the morning, like dew, your youth^[Cn: Heb *the dew of your youth*] will come to you. ^110-3

**110:4** The LORD has sworn and will not change his mind, “You are a priest forever according to the order of Melchizedek.”^[Or *forever, a rightful king by my edict*] ^110-4

**110:5** The Lord is at your right hand; he will shatter kings on the day of his wrath. ^110-5

**110:6** He will execute judgment among the nations, filling them with corpses; he will shatter heads over the wide earth. ^110-6

**110:7** He will drink from the stream by the path; therefore he will lift up his head. ^110-7
