## Mūla

सुमनाः स्पृहणो दुर्गा भद्रकालो मनोनुगः ।
कौशिकः कालविश्वेशौ सुशिवः कोप एव च ॥ ^mula

sumanāḥ spṛhaṇo durgā bhadrakālo manonugaḥ |
kauśikaḥ kālaviśveśau suśivaḥ kopa eva ca || ^iast

> **The Thirty-four Rudras of the Consonants**
>
> 1) Jaya and 2) Vijaya, 3) Jayānta and 4) Aparājita, 5) Sujaya and 6) Jayarudra, 7) Jayakīrti, 8) Jayāvaha, 9) Jayamurti, 10) Jayotsāha, 11) Jayada, 12) Jayavardhana, 13) Bala and 14) Atibala, 15) Balabhadra, 16) Balaprada, 17) Balāvaha and 18) Balavān, 19) Baladātā, 20) Baleśvara, 21) Nandana, 22) Sarvatobhadra, 23) Bhadramurti, 24) Śivaprada, 25) Sumanas, 26) Spṛhaṇa, 27) Durga, 28) Bhadrakāla, 29) Manonuga, 30) Kauśika, 31) Kāla, 32) Viśveśa, 33) Suśiva, and 34) Kopa. Born from the matrices (yoni), these are said to be thirty-four.[^19] (with 3.20–24ab)

[^19]: MV 3/20-24 quoted in TĀv ad 33/14cd-17ab, which is an abbreviated paraphrase of it.
