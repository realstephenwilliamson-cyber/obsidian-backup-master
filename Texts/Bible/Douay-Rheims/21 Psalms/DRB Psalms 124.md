# Psalms 124 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 123|← Psalms 123]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 125|Psalms 125 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 124|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**124:1** They that trust in the Lord shall be as mount Sion: he shall not be moved for ever that dwelleth ^124-1

**124:2** In Jerusalem. Mountains are round about it: so the Lord is round about his people from henceforth now and for ever. ^124-2

**124:3** For the Lord will not leave the rod of sinners upon the lot of the just: that the just may not stretch forth their hands to iniquity. ^124-3

**124:4** Do good, O Lord, to those that are good, and to the upright of heart. ^124-4

**124:5** But such as turn aside into bonds, the Lord shall lead out with the workers of iniquity: peace upon Israel. ^124-5
