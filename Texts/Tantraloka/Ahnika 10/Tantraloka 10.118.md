## Mūla

उपदेशो[श्यो]पदेष्टृत्वव्यवहारोऽन्यथा कथम् ।
सत्यं स तु तथा सृष्टः परमेशेन वेद्यताम् ॥ ^mula

upadeśo[śyo]padeṣṭṛtvavyavahāro'nyathā katham |
satyaṃ sa tu tathā sṛṣṭaḥ parameśena vedyatām || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 11 - Chapter Ten, part 2#^pg389|p. 370]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=389>))
>
> Surely, (the opponent objects), although Śiva is self-luminous, He is objectively perceivable (vedyatva) (to some degree). Otherwise, how (could) the common interaction (vyavahāra) like this between (Śiva) the teacher and the taught (be possible)? (with 10.117cd–118ab)
>
> (We say that) this is quite true. However, (the Śiva who is the object of instruction) is created in this way by the Supreme Lord. He is led (down) to an objectively perceivable state and is brought to rest on the planes of the Mantra (perceivers) and the Great Lords of Mantra etc. (with 10.118cd–119ab)
