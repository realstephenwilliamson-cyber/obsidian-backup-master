## Mūla

अघोरान्तं न्यसेदादौ प्राणं बिन्दुयुतं पुनः ।
वाममुद्रान्वितं न्यस्य पाद्यं काद्येन पूर्ववत् ॥ ^mula

aghorāntaṁ nyased ādau prā aṁ binduyutaṁ punaḥ |
vāmamudrānvitaṁ nyasya pādyaṁ kādyena[^40] pūrvavat || ^iast

*Muktabodha's e-text of the KSTS edition (M00160) has:* aghorāntaṃ nyasedādau prāṇaṃ binduyutaṃ punaḥ | vāmamudrānvitaṃ nyasya pādyaṃ kādyena pūrvavat ||

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2023 – March 2023
> p. 49 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=49>))
>
> **Aparā Vidyā**
>
> One must first deposit (the syllable) that comes at the end of AGHORE (i.e. HRĪḤ), the vital breath (H) along with the left earring (Ū), the Point (Ṁ) (to form HŪṀ), and the Foot (PH) along with the skull (Ṭ) as before (to form PHAṬ).[^41]

[^40]: g: *pādyakādye ceti*
[^41]: MV 3/51 is quoted in TĀv ad 30/15-28ab. Concerning this Vidyā see TĀ 30/26cd.
