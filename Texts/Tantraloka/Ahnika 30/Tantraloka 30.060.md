## Mūla

बिन्द्विन्द्वनलकूटाग्निमरुत्षष्ठस्वरैर्युतम् ।
आपादतलमूर्धान्तं स्मरेदस्त्रमिदं ज्वलत् ॥ ^mula

bindvindvanalakūṭāgnimarutṣaṣṭhasvarairyutam |
āpādatalamūrdhāntaṃ smaredastramidaṃ jvalat || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 03 - Chapter Thirty, part 2#^pg61|p. 43]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=61>))
>
> (The adept) should recollect this Weapon, which is conjoined with the Point (Ṁ) (at the end), Moon (S), Fire (R), the Peak (KṢ), Fire (R), Wind (Y), and the sixth vowel (Ū), flaming from the soles of the feet up to (the end of) the head. He should, (meditating in this way,) bend the fingers and then impel it (up) from the knees to the supreme wheel (in the End of the Twelve), and bend (each finger as he passes) from wheel to wheel. (Thus) this (Mantra) has been taught with (its) secret which, supreme, (bestows) liberation (nirvāṇa) immediately. (with 30.60–62ab)
