# Revised Common Lectionary — Index

**License:** Revised Common Lectionary Daily Readings © 2005 Consultation on Common Texts / Augsburg Fortress. Used for non-profit study.

**Source:** <https://lectionary.library.vanderbilt.edu/>

The Revised Common Lectionary (RCL) is a three-year cycle (Year A, B, C) of weekly scripture readings used by many Protestant and Catholic denominations. Notes marked **Fixed** contain readings that are the same every year. The exceptions: the Fixed All Saints and Thanksgiving notes hold the Year A readings (each year has its own note, in the Season after Pentecost table below), and the Easter Vigil note holds the Year A gospel. In the Season after Pentecost (Propers 4–29) each note gives both of the RCL's Old Testament tracks: track 1 (semicontinuous) under *Readings*, in the `first_reading` and `psalm` properties, and track 2 (complementary) under *Complementary track*, in `first_reading_complementary` and `psalm_complementary`.
