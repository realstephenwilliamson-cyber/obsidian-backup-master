# Numbers 28 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Numbers 27|← Numbers 27]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Numbers 29|Numbers 29 →]]

## Daily offerings

**28:1** The LORD spoke to Moses, saying, ^28-1

**28:2** “Command the Israelites, and say to them: My offering, the food for my offerings by fire,^[Or *my gifts*] my pleasing odor, you shall take care to offer to me at its appointed time. ^28-2

**28:3** And you shall say to them: This is the offering by fire^[Or *the gift*] that you shall offer to the LORD: two male lambs a year old without blemish, daily, as a regular offering. ^28-3

**28:4** One lamb you shall offer in the morning, and the other lamb you shall offer at twilight;^[Heb *between the two evenings*] ^28-4

**28:5** also one-tenth of an ephah of choice flour for a grain offering, mixed with one-fourth of a hin of beaten oil. ^28-5

**28:6** It is a regular burnt offering, ordained at Mount Sinai for a pleasing odor, an offering by fire^[Or *a gift*] to the LORD. ^28-6

**28:7** Its drink offering shall be one-fourth of a hin for each lamb; in the sanctuary you shall pour out a drink offering of strong drink to the LORD. ^28-7

**28:8** The other lamb you shall offer at twilight^[Heb *between the two evenings*] with a grain offering and a drink offering like the one in the morning; you shall offer it as an offering by fire,^[Or *a gift*] a pleasing odor to the LORD. ^28-8

## Sabbath offerings

**28:9** “On the Sabbath day: two male lambs a year old without blemish and two-tenths of an ephah of choice flour for a grain offering, mixed with oil, and its drink offering— ^28-9

**28:10** this is the burnt offering for every Sabbath, in addition to the regular burnt offering and its drink offering. ^28-10

## Monthly offerings

**28:11** “At the beginnings of your months you shall offer a burnt offering to the LORD: two young bulls, one ram, seven male lambs a year old without blemish; ^28-11

**28:12** also three-tenths of an ephah of choice flour for a grain offering, mixed with oil, for each bull; and two-tenths of choice flour for a grain offering, mixed with oil, for the one ram; ^28-12

**28:13** and one-tenth of choice flour mixed with oil as a grain offering for each lamb—a burnt offering of pleasing odor, an offering by fire^[Or *a gift*] to the LORD. ^28-13

**28:14** Their drink offerings shall be half a hin of wine for a bull, one-third of a hin for a ram, and one-fourth of a hin for a lamb. This is the burnt offering of every month throughout the months of the year. ^28-14

**28:15** And there shall be one male goat for a purification offering to the LORD; it shall be offered in addition to the regular burnt offering and its drink offering. ^28-15

## Offerings at Passover

**28:16** “On the fourteenth day of the first month there shall be a Passover offering to the LORD. ^28-16

**28:17** And on the fifteenth day of this month is a festival; seven days shall unleavened bread be eaten. ^28-17

**28:18** On the first day there shall be a holy convocation. You shall not work at your occupations. ^28-18

**28:19** You shall offer an offering by fire,^[Or *a gift*] a burnt offering to the LORD: two young bulls, one ram, and seven male lambs a year old; see that they are without blemish. ^28-19

**28:20** Their grain offering shall be of choice flour mixed with oil: three-tenths of an ephah shall you offer for a bull and two-tenths for a ram; ^28-20

**28:21** one-tenth shall you offer for each of the seven lambs; ^28-21

**28:22** also one male goat for a purification offering, to make atonement for you. ^28-22

**28:23** You shall offer these in addition to the burnt offering of the morning, which belongs to the regular burnt offering. ^28-23

**28:24** In the same way you shall offer daily, for seven days, the food of an offering by fire,^[Or *a gift*] a pleasing odor to the LORD; it shall be offered in addition to the regular burnt offering and its drink offering. ^28-24

**28:25** And on the seventh day you shall have a holy convocation; you shall not work at your occupations. ^28-25

## Offerings at the festival of weeks

**28:26** “On the day of the first fruits, when you offer a grain offering of new grain to the LORD at your Festival of Weeks, you shall have a holy convocation; you shall not work at your occupations. ^28-26

**28:27** You shall offer a burnt offering, a pleasing odor to the LORD: two young bulls, one ram, seven male lambs a year old. ^28-27

**28:28** Their grain offering shall be of choice flour mixed with oil: three-tenths of an ephah for each bull, two-tenths for the one ram, ^28-28

**28:29** one-tenth for each of the seven lambs, ^28-29

**28:30** with one male goat, to make atonement for you. ^28-30

**28:31** In addition to the regular burnt offering with its grain offering, you shall offer them and their drink offering. They shall be without blemish. ^28-31
