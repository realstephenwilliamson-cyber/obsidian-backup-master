# Frictionless Expropriation

> [!abstract] Definition
> The structural logic of contemporary digital design and artificial intelligence that systematically eliminates cognitive effort, temporal delay, and interpretive struggle from human tasks, converting active human capability into dependent consumer passivity.

## Diagnostic in Education and Culture
In [[Second Domestication outline 2026-07-07|The Second Domestication]]:
- Where classical technology sought to amplify bodily power (the lever, the wheel) while preserving human governance, frictionlessness in cognitive technology does the intellectual work *for* the user.
- **Active Expropriation:** Smoothing out cognitive friction is not a neutral convenience; it atrophies the psychic faculty that the labor of reflection was building.
- **The Hollow Student:** In higher education, the student using LLMs to bypass reading and synthesis produces an immaculate paper without acquiring cognitive or moral formation. The machine did not break education; it exposed an institutional system that had already reduced formation to dead deliverables.
