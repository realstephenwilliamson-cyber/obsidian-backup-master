## Mūla

सर्वस्य संस्क्रिया तत्त्वं तत्तस्मै यद्यतोऽमलम् ।
नवाहुतीरथो दद्यान्नवात्मसहितेन तु ॥ ^mula

sarvasya saṃskriyā tattvaṃ tattasmai yadyato'malam |
navāhutīratho dadyānnavātmasahitena tu || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 08 - Chapter Fifteen, part 7#^pg274|p. 256]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=274>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 15.416cd–417ab.
>
> Everything (whatever it may be) is purified by gazing (at it) with the firm conviction (abhimāna) that it is Śiva, for that which is reality for the one (who does so) is stainless (by its very nature). (with 15.413cd–414ab)
>
> Now, (according to other scriptures), one should offer nine oblations to Śiva’s fire with (the Mantra) Navātman preceded by OM and ending with SVĀHĀ. (This is how the fire’s) purification takes place. Then, as before, conjoin, the sustainer, the sustained, and the assembly of the goddesses (devīcakra) in (this) fire, which is the universal Space (sāmānyavyoman) of Śiva consciousness. (with 15.414cd–416ab)
