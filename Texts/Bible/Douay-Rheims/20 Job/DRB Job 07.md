# Job 7 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Job 06|← Job 6]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Job 08|Job 8 →]]

**7:1** The life of man upon earth is a warfare, and his days are like the days of a hireling. ^7-1

**7:2** As a servant longeth for the shade, as the hireling looketh for the end of his work; ^7-2

**7:3** So I also have had empty months, and have numbered to myself wearisome nights. ^7-3

**7:4** If I lie down to sleep, I shall say: When shall arise? and again I shall look for the evening, and shall be filled with sorrows even till darkness. ^7-4

**7:5** My flesh is clothed with rottenness and the filth of dust, my skin is withered and drawn together. ^7-5

**7:6** My days have passed more swiftly than the web is cut by the weaver, and are consumed without any hope. ^7-6

**7:7** Remember that my life is but wind, and my eyes shall not return to see good things. ^7-7

**7:8** Nor shall the sight of man behold me: thy eyes are upon me, and I shall be no more. ^7-8

**7:9** As a cloud is consumed, and passeth away: so he that shall go down to hell shall not come up. ^7-9

**7:10** Nor shall he return my more into his house, neither shall his place know him any more. ^7-10

**7:11** Wherefore I will not spare my month, I will speak in the affliction of my spirit: I will talk with the bitterness of my soul. ^7-11

**7:12** Am I a sea, or a whale, that thou hast enclosed me in a prison? ^7-12

**7:13** If I say: My bed shall comfort me, and I shall be relieved speaking with myself on my couch: ^7-13

**7:14** Thou wilt frighten me with dreams and terrify me with visions. ^7-14

**7:15** So that my soul rather chooseth hanging, and my bones death. ^7-15

**7:16** I have done with hope, I shall now live no longer: spare me, for my days are nothing. ^7-16

**7:17** What is a man that thou shouldst magnify him? or why dost thou set thy heart upon him? ^7-17

**7:18** Thou visitest him early in the morning, and thou provest him suddenly. ^7-18

**7:19** How long wilt thou not spare me, nor suffer me to swallow down my spittle? ^7-19

**7:20** I have sinned: what shall I do to thee, O keeper of men? why hast thou set me opposite to thee, and I am become burdensome to myself? ^7-20

**7:21** Why dost thou not remove my sin, and why dost thou not take away my iniquity? Behold now I shall sleep in the dust: and if thou seek me in the morning, I shall not be. ^7-21
