# Ezekiel 37 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Ezekiel 36|← Ezekiel 36]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Ezekiel 38|Ezekiel 38 →]]

## The valley of dry bones

**37:1** The hand of the LORD came upon me, and he brought me out by the spirit of the LORD and set me down in the middle of a valley; it was full of bones. ^37-1

**37:2** He led me all around them; there were very many lying in the valley, and they were very dry. ^37-2

**37:3** He said to me, “Mortal, can these bones live?” I answered, “O Lord GOD, you know.” ^37-3

**37:4** Then he said to me, “Prophesy to these bones and say to them: O dry bones, hear the word of the LORD. ^37-4

**37:5** Thus says the Lord GOD to these bones: I will cause breath^[Or *wind* or *spirit*] to enter you, and you shall live. ^37-5

**37:6** I will lay sinews on you and will cause flesh to come upon you and cover you with skin and put breath^[Or *wind* or *spirit*] in you, and you shall live, and you shall know that I am the LORD.” ^37-6

**37:7** So I prophesied as I had been commanded, and as I prophesied, suddenly there was a noise, a rattling, and the bones came together, bone to its bone. ^37-7

**37:8** I looked, and there were sinews on them, and flesh had come upon them, and skin had covered them, but there was no breath in them. ^37-8

**37:9** Then he said to me, “Prophesy to the breath, prophesy, mortal, and say to the breath:^[Or *wind* or *spirit*] Thus says the Lord GOD: Come from the four winds, O breath,^[Or *wind* or *spirit*] and breathe upon these slain, that they may live.” ^37-9

**37:10** I prophesied as he commanded me, and the breath came into them, and they lived and stood on their feet, a vast multitude. ^37-10

**37:11** Then he said to me, “Mortal, these bones are the whole house of Israel. They say, ‘Our bones are dried up, and our hope is lost; we are cut off completely.’ ^37-11

**37:12** Therefore prophesy and say to them: Thus says the Lord GOD: I am going to open your graves and bring you up from your graves, O my people, and I will bring you back to the land of Israel. ^37-12

**37:13** And you shall know that I am the LORD when I open your graves and bring you up from your graves, O my people. ^37-13

**37:14** I will put my spirit within you, and you shall live, and I will place you on your own soil; then you shall know that I, the LORD, have spoken and will act, says the LORD.” ^37-14

## The two sticks

**37:15** The word of the LORD came to me: ^37-15

**37:16** Mortal, take a stick and write on it, “For Judah and the Israelites associated with it”; then take another stick and write on it, “For Joseph (the stick of Ephraim) and all the house of Israel associated with it”; ^37-16

**37:17** and join them together into one stick, so that they may become one in your hand. ^37-17

**37:18** And when your people say to you, “Will you not show us what you mean by these?” ^37-18

**37:19** say to them, “Thus says the Lord GOD: I am about to take the stick of Joseph (which is in the hand of Ephraim) and the tribes of Israel associated with it, and I will put the stick of Judah upon it^[Heb *I will put them upon it*] and make them one stick, in order that they may be one in my hand.” ^37-19

**37:20** When the sticks on which you write are in your hand before their eyes, ^37-20

**37:21** then say to them, “Thus says the Lord GOD: I will take the people of Israel from the nations among which they have gone and will gather them from every quarter and bring them to their own land. ^37-21

**37:22** I will make them one nation in the land, on the mountains of Israel, and one king shall be king over them all. Never again shall they be two nations, and never again shall they be divided into two kingdoms. ^37-22

**37:23** They shall never again defile themselves with their idols and their detestable things or with any of their transgressions. I will save them from all the apostasies into which they have fallen^[Cn: MT *from all the settlements in which they have sinned*] and will cleanse them. Then they shall be my people, and I will be their God. ^37-23

**37:24** “My servant David shall be king over them, and they shall all have one shepherd. They shall follow my ordinances and be careful to observe my statutes. ^37-24

**37:25** They shall live in the land that I gave to my servant Jacob, in which your ancestors lived; they and their children and their children’s children shall live there forever, and my servant David shall be their prince forever. ^37-25

**37:26** I will make a covenant of peace with them; it shall be an everlasting covenant with them, and I will bless^[Tg: Heb *give*] them and multiply them and will set my sanctuary among them forevermore. ^37-26

**37:27** My dwelling place shall be over them, and I will be their God, and they shall be my people. ^37-27

**37:28** Then the nations shall know that I the LORD sanctify Israel, when my sanctuary is among them forevermore.” ^37-28
