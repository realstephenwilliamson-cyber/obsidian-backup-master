# LXX Psalms 149

← [[LXX Psalms 148]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 150]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 149]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 149]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**149:1** Alleluia. Sing to the Lord a new song: his praise is in the assembly of the saints.  ^149-1

**149:2** Let Israel rejoice in him that made him; and let the children of Sion exult in their king.  ^149-2

**149:3** Let them praise his name in the dance: let them sing praises to him with timbrel and psaltery.  ^149-3

**149:4** For the Lord takes pleasure in his people; and will exalt the meek with salvation.  ^149-4

**149:5** The saints shall rejoice in glory; and shall exult on their beds.  ^149-5

**149:6** The high praises of God shall be in their throat, and two-edged swords in their hands;[^1]  ^149-6

**149:7** to execute vengeance on the nations, *and* punishments among the peoples;  ^149-7

**149:8** to bind their kings with fetters, and their nobles with manacles of iron;  ^149-8

**149:9** to execute on them the judgment written: this honour have all his saints.[^2]  ^149-9

[^1]: 149:6 *Gr.* exaltations, *q. d.* extollings.
[^2]: 149:9 *Gr.* inscribed, *sc.* in the scripture.
