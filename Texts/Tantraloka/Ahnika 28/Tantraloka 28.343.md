## Mūla

न तद्देहान्तरासङ्गि न तदन्त्यं यतो भवेत् ।
कस्यापि तु शरीरान्ते वासना या प्रभोत्स्यते ॥ ^mula

na taddehāntarāsaṅgi na tadantyaṃ yato bhavet |
kasyāpi tu śarīrānte vāsanā yā prabhotsyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 06 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 4#^pg167|p. 150]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=167>))
>
> The memory (when close to death) of relatives, the beloved, sons, and drinking etc. is clearly not connected with the (new) subsequent body, as it is not the last (memory) of that (previous one). (with 28.342cd–343ab)
>
> (It may happen) that someone has a clear (direct) experience while (still) alive (dehasattve); that arises in accord with the latent impression that awakens at the point of death. (This is) as narrated in the (Puranas, (There we read of) some sage who, filled with affection for a young gazelle, was transformed into one. (with 28.343cd–345ab)
>
> (There) in that case, the cause of (his) taking birth again is not this experience. That happens purely by chance (kākatāliyavat). The cause of that is the latent impression (which is awakened at the point of death). (with 28.343cd–345cd)
