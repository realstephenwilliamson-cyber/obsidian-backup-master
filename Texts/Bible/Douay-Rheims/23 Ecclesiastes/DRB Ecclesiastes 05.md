# Ecclesiastes 5 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Ecclesiastes 04|← Ecclesiastes 4]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Ecclesiastes 06|Ecclesiastes 6 →]]

**5:1** Speak not any thing rashly, and let not thy heart be hasty to utter a word before God. For God is in heaven, and thou upon earth: therefore let thy words be few. ^5-1

**5:2** Dreams follow many cares: and in many words shall be found folly. ^5-2

**5:3** If thou hast vowed any thing to God, defer not to pay it: for an unfaithful and foolish promise displeaseth him: but whatsoever thou hast vowed, pay it. ^5-3

**5:4** And it is much better not to vow, than after a vow not to perform the things promised. ^5-4

**5:5** Give not thy mouth to cause thy flesh to sin: and say not before the angel: There is no providence: lest God be angry at thy words, and destroy all the works of thy hands. ^5-5

**5:6** Where there are many dreams, there are many vanities, and words without number: but do thou fear God. ^5-6

**5:7** If thou shalt see the oppressions of the poor, and violent judgments, and justice perverted in the province, wonder not at this matter: for he that is high hath another higher, and there are others still higher than these: ^5-7

**5:8** Moreover there is the king that reigneth over all the land subject to him. ^5-8

**5:9** A covetous man shall not be satisfied with money: and he that loveth riches shall reap no fruit from them: so this also is vanity. ^5-9

**5:10** Where there are great riches, there are also many to eat them. And what doth it profit the owner, but that he seeth the riches with his eyes? ^5-10

**5:11** Sleep is sweet to a labouring man, whether he eat little or much: but the fulness of the rich will not suffer him to sleep. ^5-11

**5:12** There is also another grievous evil, which I have seen under the sun: riches kept to the hurt of the owner. ^5-12

**5:13** For they are lost with very great affliction: he hath begotten a son, who shall be in extremity of want. ^5-13

**5:14** As he came forth naked from his mother’s womb, so shall he return, and shall take nothing away with him of his labour. ^5-14

**5:15** A most deplorable evil: as he came, so shall he return. What then doth it profit him that he hath laboured for the wind? ^5-15

**5:16** All the days of his life he eateth in darkness, and in many cares, and in misery, and sorrow. ^5-16

**5:17** This therefore hath seemed good to me, that a man should eat and drink, and enjoy the fruit of his labour, wherewith he hath laboured under the sun, all the days of his life, which God hath given him: and this is his portion. ^5-17

**5:18** And every man to whom God hath given riches, and substance, and hath given him power to eat thereof, and to enjoy his portion, and to rejoice of his labour: this is the gift of God. ^5-18

**5:19** For he shall not much remember the days of his life, because God entertaineth his heart with delight, ^5-19
