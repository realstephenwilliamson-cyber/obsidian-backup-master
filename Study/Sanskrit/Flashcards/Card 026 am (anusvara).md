# अं (a)ṃ

<span style="font-size:4em; line-height:1.2">अं</span>

**Card 26 of 108** (printed: Card 1-26 · Alpha 28) · special letter ‘anusvāra’ · the letter: [[Letter 15 am (anusvara)|अं (a)ṃ]]

## Front

- **Glyph:** अं
- **Example:** पुष्पं, अहंकार

> [!note]- Back
> - **Pronunciation:** As in ‘hu<u>m</u>’
> - **Variation:** ँ
> - **Note:** soft nasal, replaces final m when next word begins with consonant. (a) represents any vowel. Can pronounce as nasal in same mouth pos. as following consonant. Variation called anunāsika.
> - <u>अं</u> अः
> - **Example:** puṣpaṃ (flower) · ahaṃkāra (ego)

## Register

- **Classification:** Pure nasal resonance · **Sthāna:** Cerebral / Nasal
- **Body place** (*mātṛkā-nyāsa*, [[Tantraloka 15.117|TĀ 15.117]]–120): crown lock (*śikhā*)
