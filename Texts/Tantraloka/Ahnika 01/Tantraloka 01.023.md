## Mūla

मलमज्ञानमिच्छन्ति संसाराङ्कुरकारणम् ।
इति प्रोक्तं तथा च श्रीमालिनीविजयोत्तरे ॥ ^mula

malamajñānamicchanti saṃsārāṅkurakāraṇam |
iti proktaṃ tathā ca śrīmālinīvijayottare || ^iast

## Translation

>
> The same is said in the venerable Mālinīvijayottara (thus): ‘(The wise) prefer (the view that) Impurity (mala) is ignorance (not a material substance as the dualists maintain). It is the cause of (Karma, which is) the sprout of transmigratory existence (saṁsāra).’
