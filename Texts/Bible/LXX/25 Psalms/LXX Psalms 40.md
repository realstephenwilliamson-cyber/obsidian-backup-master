# LXX Psalms 40

← [[LXX Psalms 39]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 41]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 041]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 041]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**40:1** For the end, a Psalm of David.  ^40-1

**40:2** Blessed *is the man* who thinks on the poor and needy: the Lord shall deliver him in an evil day.  ^40-2

**40:3** May the Lord preserve him and keep him alive, and bless him on the earth, and not deliver him into the hands of his enemy.  ^40-3

**40:4** May the Lord help him upon the bed of his pain; thou hast made all his bed in his sickness.  ^40-4

**40:5** I said, O Lord, have mercy upon me; heal my soul; for I have sinned against thee.  ^40-5

**40:6** Mine enemies have spoken evil against me, *saying*, When shall he die, and his name perish?  ^40-6

**40:7** And if he came to see *me*, his heart spoke vainly; he gathered iniquity to himself; he went forth and spoke in like manner.  ^40-7

**40:8** All my enemies whispered against me; against me they devised my hurt.  ^40-8

**40:9** They denounced a wicked word against me, *saying*, Now that he lies, shall he not rise up again?  ^40-9

**40:10** For even the man of my peace, in whom I trusted, who ate my bread, lifted up *his* heel against me.  ^40-10

**40:11** But thou, O Lord, have compassion upon me, and raise me up, and I shall requite them.  ^40-11

**40:12** By this I know that thou hast delighted in me, because mine enemy shall not rejoice over me.  ^40-12

**40:13** But thou didst help me because of *mine* innocence, and hast established me before thee for ever.  ^40-13

**40:14** Blessed *be* the Lord God of Israel from everlasting, and to everlasting. So be it, so be it.  ^40-14
