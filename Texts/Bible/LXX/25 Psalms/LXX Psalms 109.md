# LXX Psalms 109

← [[LXX Psalms 108]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 110]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 110]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 110]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**109:1** A Psalm of David. The Lord said to my Lord, Sit thou on my right hand, until I make thine enemies thy footstool.  ^109-1

**109:2** The Lord shall send out a rod of power for thee out of Sion: rule thou in the midst of thine enemies.  ^109-2

**109:3** With thee is dominion in the day of thy power, in the splendours of thy saints: I have begotten thee from the womb before the morning.[^1]  ^109-3

**109:4** The Lord sware, and will not repent, Thou art a priest for ever, after the order of Melchisedec.  ^109-4

**109:5** The Lord at thy right hand has dashed in pieces kings in the day of his wrath.  ^109-5

**109:6** He shall judge among the nations, he shall fill up *the number of* corpses, he shall crush the heads of many on the earth.  ^109-6

**109:7** He shall drink of the brook in the way; therefore shall he lift up the head.  ^109-7

[^1]: 109:3 *Or,* holiness, *i. e.* holy things.
