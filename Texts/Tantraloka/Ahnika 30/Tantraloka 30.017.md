## Mūla

एष भैरवसद्भावश्चन्द्रार्धादिविभूषितः ।
मातृकामालिनीमन्त्रौ प्रागेव समुदाहृतौ ॥ ^mula

eṣa bhairavasadbhāvaścandrārdhādivibhūṣitaḥ |
mātṛkāmālinīmantrau prāgeva samudāhṛtau || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 02 - Chapter Thirty, part 1#^pg32|p. 14]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=32>))
>
> The letter JH, Withdrawal (KṢ) and the Vital Breath (H), along with the sixth vowel (Ū) and the Point – (JHKṢHŪṀ); this is (the mantra) Bhairavasadbhāva, adorned with the Half-Moon and the rest. The Mantras of Mātṛkā and Mālinī were taught previously. (with 30.16cd–17)
