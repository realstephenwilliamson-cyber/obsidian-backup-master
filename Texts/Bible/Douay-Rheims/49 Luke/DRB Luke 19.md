# Luke 19 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Luke 18|← Luke 18]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Luke 20|Luke 20 →]]

Compare: [[Luke 19|BSB and SBLGNT]]

**19:1** And entering in, he walked through Jericho. ^19-1

**19:2** And behold, there was a man named Zacheus, who was the chief of the publicans, and he was rich. ^19-2

**19:3** And he sought to see Jesus who he was, and he could not for the crowd, because he was low of stature. ^19-3

**19:4** And running before, he climbed up into a sycamore tree, that he might see him; for he was to pass that way. ^19-4

**19:5** And when Jesus was come to the place, looking up, he saw him, and said to him: Zacheus, make haste and come down; for this day I must abide in thy house. ^19-5

**19:6** And he made haste and came down; and received him with joy. ^19-6

**19:7** And when all saw it, they murmured, saying, that he was gone to be a guest with a man that was a sinner. ^19-7

**19:8** But Zacheus standing, said to the Lord: Behold, Lord, the half of my goods I give to the poor; and if I have wronged any man of any thing, I restore him fourfold. ^19-8

**19:9** Jesus said to him: This day is salvation come to this house, because he also is a son of Abraham. ^19-9

**19:10** For the Son of man is come to seek and to save that which was lost. ^19-10

**19:11** As they were hearing these things, he added and spoke a parable, because he was nigh to Jerusalem, and because they thought that the kingdom of God should immediately be manifested. ^19-11

**19:12** He said therefore: A certain nobleman went into a far country, to receive for himself a kingdom, and to return. ^19-12

**19:13** And calling his ten servants, he gave them ten pounds, and said to them: Trade till I come. ^19-13

**19:14** But his citizens hated him: and they sent an embassage after him, saying: We will not have this man to reign over us. ^19-14

**19:15** And it came to pass, that he returned, having received the kingdom: and he commanded his servants to be called, to whom he had given the money, that he might know how much every man had gained by trading. ^19-15

**19:16** And the first came, saying: Lord, thy pound hath gained ten pounds. ^19-16

**19:17** And he said to him: Well done, thou good servant, because thou hast been faithful in a little, thou shalt have power over ten cities. ^19-17

**19:18** And the second came, saying: Lord, thy pound hath gained five pounds. ^19-18

**19:19** And he said to him: Be thou also over five cities. ^19-19

**19:20** And another came, saying: Lord, behold here is thy pound, which I have kept laid up in a napkin; ^19-20

**19:21** For I feared thee, because thou art an austere man: thou takest up what thou didst not lay down, and thou reapest that which thou didst not sow. ^19-21

**19:22** He saith to him: Out of thy own mouth I judge thee, thou wicked servant. Thou knewest that I was an austere man, taking up what I laid not down, and reaping that which I did not sow: ^19-22

**19:23** And why then didst thou not give my money into the bank, that at my coming, I might have exacted it with usury? ^19-23

**19:24** And he said to them that stood by: Take the pound away from him, and give it to him that hath ten pounds. ^19-24

**19:25** And they said to him: Lord, he hath ten pounds. ^19-25

**19:26** But I say to you, that to every one that hath shall be given, and he shall abound: and from him that hath not, even that which he hath, shall be taken from him. ^19-26

**19:27** But as for those my enemies, who would not have me reign over them, bring them hither, and kill them before me. ^19-27

**19:28** And having said these things, he went before, going up to Jerusalem. ^19-28

**19:29** And it came to pass, when he was come nigh to Bethphage and Bethania, unto the mount called Olivet, he sent two of his disciples, ^19-29

**19:30** Saying: Go into the town which is over against you, at your entering into which you shall find the colt of an ass tied, on which no man ever hath sitten: loose him, and bring him hither. ^19-30

**19:31** And if any man shall ask you: Why do you loose him? you shall say thus unto him: Because the Lord hath need of his service. ^19-31

**19:32** And they that were sent, went their way, and found the colt standing, as he had said unto them. ^19-32

**19:33** And as they were loosing the colt, the owners thereof said to them: Why loose you the colt? ^19-33

**19:34** But they said: Because the Lord hath need of him. ^19-34

**19:35** And they brought him to Jesus. And casting their garments on the colt, they set Jesus thereon. ^19-35

**19:36** And as he went, they spread their clothes underneath in the way. ^19-36

**19:37** And when he was now coming near the descent of mount Olivet, the whole multitude of his disciples began with joy to praise God with a loud voice, for all the mighty works they had seen, ^19-37

**19:38** Saying: Blessed be the king who cometh in the name of the Lord, peace in heaven, and glory on high! ^19-38

**19:39** And some of the Pharisees, from amongst the multitude, said to him: Master, rebuke thy disciples. ^19-39

**19:40** To whom he said: I say to you, that if these shall hold their peace, the stones will cry out. ^19-40

**19:41** And when he drew near, seeing the city, he wept over it, saying: ^19-41

**19:42** If thou also hadst known, and that in this thy day, the things that are to thy peace; but now they are hidden from thy eyes. ^19-42

**19:43** For the days shall come upon thee, and thy enemies shall cast a trench about thee, and compass thee round, and straiten thee on every side, ^19-43

**19:44** And beat thee flat to the ground, and thy children who are in thee: and they shall not leave in thee a stone upon a stone: because thou hast not known the time of thy visitation. ^19-44

**19:45** And entering into the temple, he began to cast out them that sold therein, and them that bought. ^19-45

**19:46** Saying to them: It is written: My house is the house of prayer. But you have made it a den of thieves. ^19-46

**19:47** And he was teaching daily in the temple. And the chief priests and the scribes and the rulers of the people sought to destroy him: ^19-47

**19:48** And they found not what to do to him: for all the people were very attentive to hear him. ^19-48
