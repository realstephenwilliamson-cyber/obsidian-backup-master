## Mūla

पाशवानि तु शास्त्राणि वामशक्त्यात्मकान्यलम् ।
सृष्ट्यादिसिद्धये शंभोः शङ्का तत्फलकॢप्तये ॥ ^mula

pāśavāni tu śāstrāṇi vāmaśaktyātmakānyalam |
sṛṣṭyādisiddhaye śaṃbhoḥ śaṅkā tatphalakḷptaye || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII

> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 15.520cd–521ab.
>
> The scriptures of the fettered (where these matters are taught) are essentially the (evil) energy, Vāmā. Doubt (exists) so that Śiva may bring about the creation (srṣti) etc. (of daily life,) and to fashion the consequence of that.
