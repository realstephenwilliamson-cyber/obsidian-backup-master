## Mūla

स्यात् स एव परं ह्रस्वपञ्चस्वरखसंयुतः ।
ओङ्कारैः पञ्चभिर्मन्त्रो विद्याङ्गहृदयं भवेत् ॥ ^mula

syāt sa eva paraṃ hrasvapañcasvarakhasaṃyutaḥ |
oṅkāraiḥ pañcabhirmantro vidyāṅgahṛdayaṃ bhavet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 02 - Chapter Thirty, part 1#^pg44|p. 26]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=44>))
>
> The limbs (of Parā) are the letter S conjoined with the six long vowels, whereas the faces are also S, but conjoined with the five short vowels and the Void (M). (with 30.36cd–37ab)
>
> The mantra which is the Heart, amongst the limbs of the Vidyā is made of five OMs. (with 30.37cd)
