## Mūla

पृथिवी स्थिररूपास्य शिवरूपेण भाविता ।
स्थिरीकरोति तामेव भावनामिति शुध्यति ॥ ^mula

pṛthivī sthirarūpāsya śivarūpeṇa bhāvitā |
sthirīkaroti tāmeva bhāvanāmiti śudhyati || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 08 - Chapter Seventeen#^pg206|p. 186]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=206>))
>
> The Earth, whose form is firm, contemplated (by the teacher) as being Śiva, renders that same meditation (bhāvanā) firm within him, and is thus purified. Water nourishes this (meditation), Fire makes it shine, the Wind (induces its inner) blissful touch, and Space makes it extensive. (with 17.103–104)
