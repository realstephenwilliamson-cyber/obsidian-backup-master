## Mūla

मध्योर्ध्वाधःसमुद्वृत्तशरावचतुरश्रकः ।
भैरवीयं च तल्लिङ्गं धरणी चास्य पीठिका ॥ ^mula

madhyordhvādhaḥsamudvṛttaśarāvacaturaśrakaḥ |

## Translation

> In its middle, above and below, (there is a place that is) octagonal, and (one) above that is round (samudvrtta) and square (respectively). Its peak (is concave) like a plate (śŚarāāva). (with 8.45ab)
>
> And that (Mount Meru) is a Bhairava Liṅga, and its base pithika)) is the earth. All the gods reside there, and it is worshipped constantly. (On top of it,) 1) in the centre, is the Benefactor’s assembly of Meru (called Manovatī). 2) To the northeast of that is Crystal Peak (jyotiṣkaśikhara), the abode of Lord Śrīkanṇṭha, who is an aspect (aṁśa) of Śiva. Descending fourteen thousand leagues below (the assembly of) Manovafi, all around Meru, there is a circular terrace (cakravāṭa). The guardians of the quarters (reside) here. (with 8.45cd–48ab)
