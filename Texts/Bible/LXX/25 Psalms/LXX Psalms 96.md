# LXX Psalms 96

← [[LXX Psalms 95]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 97]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 097]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 097]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**96:1** For David, when his land is established. The Lord reigns, let the earth exult, let many islands rejoice.  ^96-1

**96:2** Cloud and darkness are round about him; righteousness and judgment are the establishment of his throne.  ^96-2

**96:3** Fire shall go before him, and burn up his enemies round about.  ^96-3

**96:4** His lightnings appeared to the world; the earth saw, and trembled.  ^96-4

**96:5** The mountains melted like wax at the presence of the Lord, at the presence of the Lord of the whole earth.  ^96-5

**96:6** The heavens have declared his righteousness, and all the people have seen his glory.  ^96-6

**96:7** Let all that worship graven images be ashamed, who boast of their idols; worship him, all ye his angels.  ^96-7

**96:8** Sion heard and rejoiced; and the daughters of Judea exulted, because of thy judgments, O Lord.  ^96-8

**96:9** For thou art Lord most high over all the earth; thou art greatly exalted above all gods.  ^96-9

**96:10** Ye that love the Lord, hate evil; the Lord preserves the souls of his saints; he shall deliver them from the hand of sinners.  ^96-10

**96:11** Light is sprung up for the righteous, and gladness for the upright in heart.  ^96-11

**96:12** Rejoice in the Lord, ye righteous; and give thanks for a remembrance of his holiness.  ^96-12
