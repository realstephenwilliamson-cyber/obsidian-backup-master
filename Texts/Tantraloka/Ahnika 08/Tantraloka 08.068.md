## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 04 - Chapter Eight, part 2#^pg135|p. 117]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=135>))
>
> Below the circular terrace all around (mount Meru) is (the land) called Tlā. It is square and it has no sun or moon. It extends (in all directions around Meru) for nine thousand leagues. (Its living beings) live for thirteen (thousand years).
