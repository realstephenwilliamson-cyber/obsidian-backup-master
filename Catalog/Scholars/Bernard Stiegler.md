# Bernard Stiegler

A French philosopher of technology (1952–2020) whose last post was director of the department of cultural development at the Centre Pompidou in Paris; he founded the Institute for Research and Innovation (IRI) there in 2006 and the association Ars Industrialis in 2005 ([Artforum](https://www.artforum.com/news/bernard-stiegler-1952-2020-83654)).
