## Mūla

वीर्यारुणपरीणामदेहाहन्ताप्रतिष्ठिताः ।
देहोपकारसन्ताना ज्ञातेये परिनिष्ठिताः ॥ ^mula

vīryāruṇaparīṇāmadehāhantāpratiṣṭhitāḥ |
dehopakārasantānā jñāteye pariniṣṭhitāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 05 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 3#^pg106|p. 89]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=106>))
>
> Lineages, (both) corporeal and those (of hereditary) service, are based on the arrogation of subjectivity to the body (dehāhantā), which is an evolute of (the male) seed and (female) blood. (In other words,) they are firmly fixed in objectivity.
