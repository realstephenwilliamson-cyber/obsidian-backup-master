## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 03 - Chapter Eight, part 1#^pg76|p. 58]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=76>))
>
> The teacher has said in the venerable Dīkṣottara: “These are the Lords of the Path. 1) Brahmā (who pervades and governs from) from Ananta'” to the material nature (pradhāna). 2) Viṣṇu (who pervades and governs the principles) from the individual soul up to the force (of limited agency). 3) Rudra (who pervades and governs) within the Knot of Māyā. 4) 159, who is in the sphere of Pure Knowledge (and in the principles Īśvara and Sadāśiva). 5) After that (comes) Anāśrita (who pervades the principle of Power above them), and (then finally,) 6) Śiva, the Supreme (Lord), Who pervades (all) that (is pervaded and governed by the other five causal forces), is their supreme pervader. (with 8.9–10)
