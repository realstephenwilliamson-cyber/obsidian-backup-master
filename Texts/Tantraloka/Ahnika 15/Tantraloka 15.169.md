## Mūla

आनन्दजननं पूजायोग्यं हृदयहारि यत् ।
अतः कुलक्रमोत्तीर्णत्रिकसारमतादिषु ॥ ^mula

ānandajananaṃ pūjāyogyaṃ hṛdayahāri yat |
ataḥ kulakramottīrṇatrikasāramatādiṣu || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 04 - Chapter Fifteen, part 3#^pg123|p. 105]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=123>))
>
> Thus, whatever captivates the heart (hrdayahārin), suppressing the predominance of (the mistaken identification with) the body etc. which is not consciousness, and (so) generates bliss, is a suitable (offering for) worship (pūjā). (with 15.168cd–169ab)
>
> This is why the glory (and power) of sacrificial substances, such as grape wine, Kādambarī and Śīdhu? and the like, is (considered to be) supreme in the Kula traditions (and liturgies) (kulakrama) as well as in the Trikasāra, the Mata, and other (such scriptures) that are superior to them. (with 15.169cd–170ab)

## Viveka

> अतः कुलक्रमोत्तीर्णत्रिकसारमतादिषु ॥
> मद्यकादम्बरीशीधुद्रव्यादेर्महिमा परम् ।

आदिशब्देन मांसमैथुनादि । तदुक्तम्--

ङ नद्यो मधुवाहिन्यो न पलं पर्वतोपमम् ।

स्त्रीमयं न जगत्सर्वं कुतः सिद्धिः कुलागमे ॥"इति ॥
