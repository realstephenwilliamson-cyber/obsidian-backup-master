## Mūla

षड्विधेऽपि कृते शाक्ते मूर्त्यादावपि चिन्तयेत् ।
विद्याङ्गपञ्चकं पश्चादाग्नेय्यादिषु विन्यसेत् ॥ ^mula

ṣaḍvidhe 'pi kṛte śākte mūrtyādāv api cintayet |
vidyāṅgapañcakaṁ paścād āgneyyādiṣu vinyaset || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2025 – April 2025
> p. 21 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=35>))
>
> **Parā and the Encompassing Host**
>
> Once performed the six-fold (deposition) of power, as is done with the Icon and the rest,[^81] one should think of Parā, the goddess who bears the form of the will and destroys the suffering of (her) devotees (praṇata). She is the goddess who nourishes (all) and shines with the light of billions of moons.[^82] (with 8.74–75ab)

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2025 – April 2025
> p. 21 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=35>))
>
> After that one should deposit the five limbs of the Vidyā in the south-east etc., that is, in the south-east, north-east, south-west, north-west and south, in due order.[^83] (75cd-76ab). (with 8.75cd–76ab)

[^81]: The meaning here is unclear. See TĀ 15/326ab. Read *mūrtāv iva* for *mūrtāv api*.
[^82]: TĀ 15/323cd-325 is a paraphrase of MV 8/72-75ab.
[^83]: TĀ 15/353cd-365 is a paraphrase of MV 8/75cd-84ab interspersed with direct quotations. MV 8/75cd-76ab is quoted in TĀv ad 15/357cd-358 (357-358ab).
