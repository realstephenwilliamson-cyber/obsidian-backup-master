## About the Author

Brian Gregor (PhD, Boston College) is Assistant Professor of Philosophy at California State University, Dominguez Hills. He is the author of *A Philosophical Anthropology of the Cross: The Cruciform Self*, as well as numerous articles on the philosophy of religion, ethics, and aesthetics.
