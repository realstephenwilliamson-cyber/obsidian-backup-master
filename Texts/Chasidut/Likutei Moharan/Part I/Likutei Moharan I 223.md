# Likutei Moharan I 223

← [[Likutei Moharan I 222]] · [[Likutei Moharan]] · [[Likutei Moharan I 224]] →

*Part I · Lesson 223* · [[Likutei Moharan]]

## 1

When the tzaddik needs to ask something of God, it may happen that [He] does not grant his request. This is because sometimes He listens and sometimes He does not… ([[Zohar 2.03 - Parashat Va-Yera#^z1-105b|Zohar I, 105b]]).

However, there is a tzaddik who is able to declare: I say that it will be so!

This is the aspect of “Thus shall you bless… Say to them” ([[Torah 35 - Nasso#^6-23|Numbers 6:23]]). That is, I say it will be so: “God bless you and protect you!…” Amen.
