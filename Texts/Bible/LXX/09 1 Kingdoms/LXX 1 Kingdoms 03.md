# LXX 1 Kingdoms 03

← [[LXX 1 Kingdoms 02]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX 1 Kingdoms 04]] →

*1 Kingdoms · [[LXX]]*

**3:1** And the child Samuel ministered to the Lord before Heli the priest: and the word of the Lord was precious in those days, there was no distinct vision.[^1]  ^3-1

**3:2** And it came to pass at that time that Heli was sleeping in his place; and his eyes began to fail, and could not see.  ^3-2

**3:3** And the lamp of God *was burning* before it was trimmed, and Samuel slept in the temple, where *was* the ark of God.  ^3-3

**3:4** And the Lord called, Samuel, Samuel; and he said, Behold, *here am* I.  ^3-4

**3:5** And he ran to Heli, and said, *Here am* I, for thou didst call me: and he said, I did not call thee; return, go to sleep; and he returned and went to sleep.  ^3-5

**3:6** And the Lord called again, Samuel, Samuel: and he went to Heli the second time, and said, Behold *here am* I, for thou didst call me: and he said, I called thee not; return, go to sleep.[^2]  ^3-6

**3:7** And *it was* before Samuel knew the Lord, and *before* the word of the Lord was revealed to him.  ^3-7

**3:8** And the Lord called Samuel again for the third time: and he arose and went to Heli, and said, Behold, I *am here*, for thou didst call me: and Heli perceived that the Lord *had* called the child.  ^3-8

**3:9** And he said, Return, child, go to sleep; and it shall come to pass if he shall call thee, that thou shalt say, Speak, for thy servant hears: and Samuel went and lay down in his place.  ^3-9

**3:10** And the Lord came, and stood, and called him as before: and Samuel said, Speak, for thy servant hears.[^3]  ^3-10

**3:11** And the Lord said to Samuel, Behold, I execute my words in Israel; whoever hears them, both his ears shall tingle.  ^3-11

**3:12** In that day I will raise up against Heli all things that I have said against his house; I will begin, and I will make an end.  ^3-12

**3:13** And I have told him that I will be avenged on his house perpetually for the iniquities of his sons, because his sons spoke evil against God, and he did not admonish them.  ^3-13

**3:14** And *it shall* not *go on* so; I have sworn to the house of Eli, the iniquity of the house of Eli shall not be atoned for with incense or sacrifices for ever.  ^3-14

**3:15** And Samuel slept till morning, and rose early in the morning, and opened the doors of the house of the Lord; and Samuel feared to tell *Heli* the vision.[^4]  ^3-15

**3:16** And Heli said to Samuel, Samuel, *my* son; and he said, Behold, *here am* I.  ^3-16

**3:17** And he said, What *was* the word that was spoken to thee? I pray thee hide it not from me: may God do these things to thee, and more also, if thou hide from me any thing of all the words that were spoken to thee in thine ears.[^5]  ^3-17

**3:18** And Samuel reported all the words, and hid them not from him. And Heli said, He *is* the Lord, he shall do that which is good in his sight.  ^3-18

**3:19** And Samuel grew, and the Lord was with him, and there did not fall one of his words to the ground.  ^3-19

**3:20** And all Israel knew from Dan even to Bersabee, that Samuel *was* faithful as a prophet to the Lord.  ^3-20

**3:21** And the Lord manifested himself again in Selom, for the Lord revealed himself to Samuel; and Samuel was accredited to all Israel as a prophet to the Lord from one end of the land to the other: and Heli *was* very old, and his sons kept advancing *in wickedness*, and their way *was* evil before the Lord.  ^3-21

[^1]: 3:1 *Lit.* distinguishing.
[^2]: 3:6 *Gr.* added and called.
[^3]: 3:10 *Gr.* once and once.
[^4]: 3:15 *Gr.* sleeps *or* lies down.
[^5]: 3:17 *Gr.* add these things.
