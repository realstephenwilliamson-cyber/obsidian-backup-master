# 1 Timothy 4 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB 1 Timothy 03|← 1 Timothy 3]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB 1 Timothy 05|1 Timothy 5 →]]

Compare: [[1 Timothy 04|BSB and SBLGNT]]

**4:1** Now the Spirit manifestly saith, that in the last times some shall depart from the faith, giving heed to spirits of error, and doctrines of devils, ^4-1

**4:2** Speaking lies in hypocrisy, and having their conscience seared, ^4-2

**4:3** Forbidding to marry, to abstain from meats, which God hath created to be received with thanksgiving by the faithful, and by them that have known the truth. ^4-3

**4:4** For every creature of God is good, and nothing to be rejected that is received with thanksgiving: ^4-4

**4:5** For it is sanctified by the word of God and prayer. ^4-5

**4:6** These things proposing to the brethren, thou shalt be a good minister of Christ Jesus, nourished up in the words of faith, and of the good doctrine which thou hast attained unto. ^4-6

**4:7** But avoid foolish and old wives’ fables: and exercise thyself unto godliness. ^4-7

**4:8** For bodily exercise is profitable to little: but godliness is profitable to all things, having promise of the life that now is, and of that which is to come. ^4-8

**4:9** A faithful saying and worthy of all acceptation. ^4-9

**4:10** For therefore we labor and are reviled, because we hope in the living God, who is the Saviour of all men, especially of the faithful. ^4-10

**4:11** These things command and teach. ^4-11

**4:12** Let no man despise thy youth: but be thou an example of the faithful in word, in conversation, in charity, in faith, in chastity. ^4-12

**4:13** Till I come, attend unto reading, to exhortation, and to doctrine. ^4-13

**4:14** Neglect not the grace that is in thee, which was given thee by prophesy, with imposition of the hands of the priesthood. ^4-14

**4:15** Meditate upon these things, be wholly in these things: that thy profiting may be manifest to all. ^4-15

**4:16** Take heed to thyself and to doctrine: be earnest in them. For in doing this thou shalt both save thyself and them that hear thee. ^4-16
