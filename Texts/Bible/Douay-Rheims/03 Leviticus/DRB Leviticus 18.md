# Leviticus 18 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Leviticus 17|← Leviticus 17]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Leviticus 19|Leviticus 19 →]]

**18:1** And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying: ^18-1

**18:2** Speak to the children of Israel, and thou shalt say to them: I am the Lord your God. ^18-2

**18:3** You shall not do according to the custom of the land of Egypt, in which you dwelt: neither shall you act according to the manner of the country of Chanaan, into which I will bring you, nor shall you walk in their ordinances. ^18-3

**18:4** You shall do my judgments, and shall observe my precepts, and shall walk in them. I am the Lord your God. ^18-4

**18:5** Keep my laws and my judgments, which if a man do, he shall live in them. I am the Lord. ^18-5

**18:6** No man shall approach to her that is near of kin to him, to uncover her nakedness. I am the Lord. ^18-6

**18:7** Thou shalt not uncover the nakedness of thy father, or the nakedness of thy mother: she is thy mother, thou shalt not uncover her nakedness. ^18-7

**18:8** Thou shalt not uncover the nakedness of thy father’s wife: for it is the nakedness of thy father. ^18-8

**18:9** Thou shalt not uncover the nakedness of thy sister by father or by mother, whether born at home or abroad. ^18-9

**18:10** Thou shalt not uncover the nakedness of thy son’s daughter, or thy daughter’s daughter: because it is thy own nakedness. ^18-10

**18:11** Thou shalt not uncover the nakedness of thy father’s wife’s daughter, whom she bore to thy father, and who is thy sister. ^18-11

**18:12** Thou shalt not uncover the nakedness of thy father’s sister: because she is the flesh of thy father. ^18-12

**18:13** Thou shalt not uncover the nakedness of thy mother’s sister: because she is thy mother’s flesh. ^18-13

**18:14** Thou shalt not uncover the nakedness of thy father’s brother: neither shalt thou approach to his wife, who is joined to thee by affinity. ^18-14

**18:15** Thou shalt not uncover the nakedness of thy daughter in law: because she is thy son’s wife, neither shalt thou discover her shame. ^18-15

**18:16** Thou shalt not uncover the nakedness of thy brother’s wife: because it is the nakedness of thy brother. ^18-16

**18:17** Thou shalt not uncover the nakedness of thy wife and her daughter. Thou shalt not take her son’s daughter or her daughter’s daughter, to discover her shame: because they are her flesh, and such copulation is incest. ^18-17

**18:18** Thou shalt not take thy wife’s sister for a harlot, to rival her, neither shalt thou discover her nakedness, while she is yet living. ^18-18

**18:19** Thou shalt not approach to a woman having her flowers, neither shalt thou uncover her nakedness. ^18-19

**18:20** Thou shalt not lie with thy neighbour’s wife, nor be defiled with mingling of seed. ^18-20

**18:21** Thou shalt not give any of thy seed to be consecrated to the idol Moloch, nor defile the name of thy God: I am the Lord. ^18-21

**18:22** Thou shalt not lie with mankind as with womankind, because it is an abomination. ^18-22

**18:23** Thou shalt not copulate with any beast, neither shalt thou be defiled with it. A woman shall not lie down to a beast, nor copulate with it: because it is a heinous crime. ^18-23

**18:24** Defile not yourselves with any of these things with which all the nations have been defiled, which I will cast out before you, ^18-24

**18:25** And with which the land is defiled: the abominations of which I will visit, that it may vomit out its inhabitants. ^18-25

**18:26** Keep ye my ordinances and my judgments, and do not any of these abominations: neither any of your own nation, nor any stranger that sojourneth among you. ^18-26

**18:27** For all these detestable things the inhabitants of the land have done, that; were before you, and have defiled it. ^18-27

**18:28** Beware then, lest in like manner, it vomit you also out, if you do the like things, as it vomited out the nation that was before you. ^18-28

**18:29** Every soul that shall commit any of these abominations, shall perish from the midst of his people. ^18-29

**18:30** Keep my commandments. Do not the things which they have done, that have been before you, and be not defiled therein. I am the Lord your God. ^18-30
