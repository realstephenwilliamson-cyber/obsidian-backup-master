**Track 3**

So what is the sovereign? Hobbes writes “For by art is created that great LEVIATHAN called a COMMONWEALTH, or STATE (in Latin, CIVITAS), which is but an artificial man, though of greater stature and strength than the natural, for whose protection and defence it was intended; and in which the sovereignty is an artificial soul.”

So if the Leviathan is a cyborg we must consider not where does the power reside trying to find the sovereign but rather we should focus on a description of the "artificial soul" such a \[mechanism or\] creature would create. Would she be the Borg Queen \[a Star Trek reference\] or a super computer named Deep Thought our collective has built to tell us the answer is 42? \[Hitchhiker's GTTG\]

What if it's something unthinkably precious, the Logos, perhaps, the Deus ex Machina of reality, that is the animating force of this CIVITAS mined from the biological, psychological, and technological, distinctivenesses of each of us. Dasein \["\[Dasein is\] that entity which in its Being has this very Being as an issue…" (Heidegger, trans. 1927/1962, p.68)\] manifest in our society built on libidinal economic interaction.

In other words, “But this Post-modern cybernetic capitalist Leviathan is Sublime,” means that the Sovereign is the artificial soul created by this artificial cybernetic creature (and make no mistake about the word artificial here, it in no way means unreal, Trump's hair is artificial but it too, unfortunately, really exists) that is the CIVITAS.

I think the reason I can get excited about Hobbes's authoritarianism is because everything he said was true, only when you consider "computerised society as a totality constitutes a machine" the political authority is not the sovereign but the program that is running the machine is.
