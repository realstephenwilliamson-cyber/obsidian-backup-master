## Mūla

कालसंख्या सुसूक्ष्मैकचारगा गण्यते बुधैः ।
संध्यापूर्वाह्णमध्याह्नमध्यरात्रादि यत्किल ॥ ^mula

kālasaṃkhyā susūkṣmaikacāragā gaṇyate budhaiḥ |
saṃdhyāpūrvāhṇamadhyāhnamadhyarātrādi yatkila || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 13 - Chapter Six, part 6#^pg436|p. 421]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=436>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 6.210cd–211ab.
>
> The wise compute within the Equal Breath (samāna) also (all) the measures of time, ranging from one fuṭi to sixty years, (explained) previously, present in a single (phase of its) very subtle motion. (with 6.209cd–210ab)
>
> The main (phases) to grasp (grāhya) are the conjunctions (saṁdhyā) of these interior transits (through the signs of the zodiac), namely, those of the beginning of the day, midday and midnight. (They are important) because (when they take place, they) give rise to (the desired) fruit. (with 6.210cd–211ab)
