## Mūla

एवं युक्तः परे तत्त्वे गुरुणा शिवमूर्तिना ।
न भूयः पशुतामेति दग्धमायानिबन्धनः ॥ ^mula

evaṃ yuktaḥ pare tattve guruṇā śivamūrtinā |
na bhūyaḥ paśutāmeti dagdhamāyānibandhanaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 08 - Chapter Seventeen#^pg198|p. 178]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=198>))
>
> ‘(The disciple,) united in this way with the supreme reality by the teacher who is Śiva incarnate (śivamūrti), the fetters of Māyā burnt away, he does not return again to (his former) fettered state’.
