## Mūla

विदार्यास्यं कनिष्ठाभ्यां मध्यमाभ्यां तु नासिकाम् ।
अनामे कुञ्चयेत्प्राज्ञो भ्रूभङ्गं तर्जनीद्वयम् ॥ ^mula

vidāryāsyaṃ kaniṣṭhābhyāṃ madhyamābhyāṃ tu nāsikām |
anāme kuñcayetprājño bhrūbhaṅgaṃ tarjanīdvayam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 06 - Chapter Thirty-Two#^pg163|p. 145]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=163>))
>
> Placing the hands below the collarbone (jatru) and the left foot on the right, and opened the mouth wide with the little fingers and the nose with the middle fingers, the wise man should bend the ring fingers. (Then) the reciter of Mantra should arch the eyebrows, move the two index fingers about and so too the tongue. (As he does this,) he should make the sound HĀ! HĀ! Having reached the Cavity of Brahmā by (thus) applying (the Gesture of) the Trident, abandoning the place which is just that much (as where he is), he immediately quits (the surface of) the earth. (with 32.13cd–16ab)
