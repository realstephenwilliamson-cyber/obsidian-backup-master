# Psalm 10 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 009|← Psalm 9]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 011|Psalm 11 →]]

## Prayer for deliverance from enemies

**10:1** ^[Psalms 9–10 were originally one psalm, as in the Greek and Latin traditions. In Hebrew, Psalms 9–10 formed an acrostic.]Why, O LORD, do you stand far off? Why do you hide yourself in times of trouble? ^10-1

**10:2** In arrogance the wicked persecute the poor—let them be caught in the schemes they have devised. ^10-2

**10:3** For the wicked boast of the desires of their heart; those greedy for gain curse and renounce the LORD. ^10-3

**10:4** In the pride of their countenance the wicked say, “God will not seek it out”; all their thoughts are, “There is no God.” ^10-4

**10:5** Their ways prosper at all times; your judgments are on high, out of their sight; as for their foes, they scoff at them. ^10-5

**10:6** They think in their heart, “We shall not be moved; throughout all generations we shall not meet adversity.” ^10-6

**10:7** Their mouths are filled with cursing and deceit and oppression; under their tongues are mischief and iniquity. ^10-7

**10:8** They sit in ambush in the villages; in hiding places they murder the innocent. Their eyes stealthily watch for the helpless; ^10-8

**10:9** they lurk in secret like a lion in its den; they lurk that they may seize the poor; they seize the poor and drag them off in their net. ^10-9

**10:10** They stoop, they crouch, and the helpless fall by their might. ^10-10

**10:11** They think in their heart, “God has forgotten; he has hidden his face; he will never see it.” ^10-11

**10:12** Rise up, O LORD; O God, lift up your hand; do not forget the oppressed. ^10-12

**10:13** Why do the wicked renounce God and say in their hearts, “You will not call us to account”? ^10-13

**10:14** But you do see! Indeed, you note trouble and grief, that you may take it into your hands; the helpless commit themselves to you; you have been the helper of the orphan. ^10-14

**10:15** Break the arm of the wicked and evildoers; seek out their wickedness until you find none. ^10-15

**10:16** The LORD is king forever and ever; the nations shall perish from his land. ^10-16

**10:17** O LORD, you will hear the desire of the meek; you will strengthen their heart; you will incline your ear ^10-17

**10:18** to do justice for the orphan and the oppressed, so that those from earth may strike terror no more.^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^10-18
