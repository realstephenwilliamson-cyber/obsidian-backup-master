# Jeremiah 28

← [[Jeremiah 27]] · [[Tanakh]] · [[Jeremiah 29]] →

Compare: [[NRSVue Jeremiah 28|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 28

**28:1** That year, early in the reign of King Zedekiah of Judah, in the fifth month of the fourth year, the prophet Hananiah son of Azzur, who was from Gibeon, spoke to me in the House of the LORD, in the presence of the priests and all the people. He said: ^28-1

**28:2** “Thus said the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel: I hereby break the yoke of the king of Babylon. ^28-2

**28:3** In two years, I will restore to this place all the vessels of the House of the LORD which King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon took from this place and brought to Babylon. ^28-3

**28:4** And I will bring back to this place King Jeconiah son of Jehoiakim of Judah, and all the Judean exiles who went to Babylon—declares the LORD. Yes, I will break the yoke of the king of Babylon.” ^28-4

**28:5** Then the prophet Jeremiah answered the prophet Hananiah in the presence of the priests and of all the people who were standing in the House of the LORD. ^28-5

**28:6** The prophet Jeremiah said: “Amen! May the LORD do so! May the LORD fulfill what you have prophesied and bring back from Babylon to this place the vessels of the House of the LORD and all the exiles! ^28-6

**28:7** But just listen to this word which I address to you and to all the people: ^28-7

**28:8** The prophets who lived before you and me from ancient times prophesied war, disaster, and pestilence against many lands and great kingdoms. ^28-8

**28:9** So if a prophet prophesies good fortune, then only when the word of the prophet comes true can it be known that the LORD really sent him.” ^28-9

**28:10** But the prophet Hananiah removed the bar from the neck of the prophet Jeremiah, and broke it; ^28-10

**28:11** and Hananiah said in the presence of all the people, “Thus said the LORD: So will I break the yoke of King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon from off the necks of all the nations, in two years.” And the prophet Jeremiah went on his way. ^28-11

**28:12** After the prophet Hananiah had broken the bar from off the neck of the prophet Jeremiah, the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah: ^28-12

**28:13** “Go say to Hananiah: Thus said the LORD: You broke bars of wood, but you shall[^1] make bars of iron instead. ^28-13

**28:14** For thus said the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel: I have put an iron yoke upon the necks of all those nations, that they may serve King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon—and serve him they shall! I have even given the wild beasts to him.” ^28-14

**28:15** And the prophet Jeremiah said to the prophet Hananiah, “Listen, Hananiah! The LORD did not send you, and you have given this people lying assurances. ^28-15

**28:16** Assuredly, thus said the LORD: I am going to banish you from off the earth. This year you shall die, for you have urged disloyalty to the LORD.” ^28-16

**28:17** And the prophet Hananiah died that year, in the seventh month. ^28-17

[^1]: Septuagint reads “I will.”
