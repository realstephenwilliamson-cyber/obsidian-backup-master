# ऽ avagraha (apostrophe)

<span style="font-size:4em; line-height:1.2">ऽ</span>

**Card 62 of 108** (printed: Card 3-2 · Alpha 0) · punctuation

`BUTTON[sr-note, sr-cram]`

## Front

- **Glyph:** ऽ
- **Example:** सोऽहम्

> [!note]- Back
> - **Sign above the name:** ' (a large straight apostrophe)
> - **Note:** Replaces an elided ‘a’ which began the following word. Two in a row replace an elided ‘ā’.
> - **Example:** so'ham (I am That)

## Register

- **Classification:** Elision marker · **Sthāna:** Orthographic mark
- **Body place** (*mātṛkā-nyāsa*, [[Tantraloka 15.117|TĀ 15.117]]–120): none (a sign, not a letter)
