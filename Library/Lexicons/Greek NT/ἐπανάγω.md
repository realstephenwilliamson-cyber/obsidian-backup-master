# ἐπανάγω

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · verb

**Dodson:** I put out, go up — (a) nautical: I put out (from the shore), (lit: I take up a ship on to the high seas), (b) I go up, possibly: I go up again, return. (Strong's 1877; headword ἐπανάγω)
