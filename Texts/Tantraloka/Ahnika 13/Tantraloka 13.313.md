## Mūla

तं च त्यजेत्पापवृत्तिं भवेत्तु ज्ञानतत्परः ।
यथा चौराद् गृहीत्वार्थं तं निगृह्णाति भूपतिः ॥ ^mula

taṃ ca tyajetpāpavṛttiṃ bhavettu jñānatatparaḥ |
yathā caurād gṛhītvārthaṃ taṃ nigṛhṇāti bhūpatiḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 10 - Chapter Thirteen, part 6#^pg341|p. 319]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=341>))
>
> When (the teacher) comes to know the perverse conduct (of his disciple), by (observing) the variety (vaicitrya) (of his devious behaviour), (the teacher) should withdraw his knowledge (jñāna) from him and abandon him as one who behaves sinfully, (so that by sincere penance) he may be dedicated to (salvific) knowledge. (with 13.312–313ab)
>
> Just as a king first confiscates the things a thief has stolen and then punishes him, similarly, the wise man should first take away from the Vaiṣṇava and others (who are not sincere Śaivites,) the knowledge of Śiva (he taught him). (with 13.313cd–314ab)

## Viveka

> यथा चौराद् गृहीत्वार्थं तं निगृह्णाति भूपतिः ॥
> वैष्णवादेस्तथा शैवं ज्ञानमाहृत्य सन्मतिः ।

नन्वस्य प्रथमं निर्विकारमेव प्रसन्नचेतसा गुरुणा ज्ञानमुपदिष्टं तावतैव चास्य

"यस्मिन्काले तु गुरुणा निर्विकल्पं प्रकाशितम् ।
