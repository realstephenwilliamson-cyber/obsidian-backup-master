## Mūla

भुञ्जीत पूजयेच्चक्रं परसंतानिना नहि ।
एतच्च मतशास्त्रेषु निषिद्धं खण्डना यतः ॥ ^mula

bhuñjīta pūjayeccakraṃ parasaṃtāninā nahi |
etacca mataśāstreṣu niṣiddhaṃ khaṇḍanā yataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. III
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 03 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v03 11 - Chapter Four, part 10#^pg388|p. 375]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 03.pdf#page=388>))
>
> (A Kaula initiate should know these things, because) in order to attain a state of oneness (with his deity, he) should remain faithful (samāśrayet) to his own lineage (santāna). He must not enjoy and worship the assembly (cakra) (of Kaula initiates and Yoginīs) with members of a different spiritual lineage. (with 4.268cd–269ab)
>
> (But all) this is rejected in the Mata scriptures (of the higher Trika Kaula), because by this relative distinction (bheda), division (khandanā) would (appear to) arise within the (one) supreme reality, although it is (truly) undivided. (with 4.269cd–270ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* एतच्च अन्यत्र निषिद्धमित्याह --

> एतच्च मतशास्त्रेषु निषिद्धं खण्डना यतः ॥
> अखण्डेऽपि परे तत्त्वे भेदेनानेन जायते ।

"अनेन"इति समनन्तरोक्तेन हेयोपादेयरूपेण स्वपरसंतानादिनेत्यर्थः । नहि अखण्डे परे तत्त्वे काचन एवंविधा हेयोपादेयरूपा खण्डना युज्यते -- इत्याशयः ॥ २६९ ॥
