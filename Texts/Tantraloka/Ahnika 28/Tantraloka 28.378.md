## Mūla

विकस्वरा निष्प्रतिघं संविदानन्दयोगिनी ।
अतन्मये तु कस्मिंश्चित्तत्रस्थे प्रतिहन्यते ॥ ^mula

vikasvarā niṣpratighaṃ saṃvidānandayoginī |
atanmaye tu kasmiṃścittatrasthe pratihanyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 07 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 5#^pg187|p. 170]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=187>))
>
> It is because the causes of the contraction (of consciousness), such as jealousy, envy and the like, are absent, that consciousness is here expanding without obstacles, and conjoins (each spectator to his or her own essential) bliss. (with 28.377cd–378ab)
>
> However, if anyone present there is (not absorbed in the show, and so) not one with it, (the one collective, unified consciousness) is destroyed, because, like the touch of an uneven, rugged surface (sthapuṭa), (his) consciousness stands out as differing in kind (from that of the others). (with 28.378cd–379ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* येषां पुनरीर्ष्यादिसङ्कोचाभावो नास्ति, तेषां किं संविन्मयीभावो भवेन्न वा?--इत्याशङ्क्य आह--

> अतन्मये तु कस्मिंश्चित्तत्रस्थे प्रतिहन्यते ॥
> स्थपुटस्पर्शवत्संविद्विजातीयतया स्थिते ।

अतन्मये इति--संविन्मयतामनापन्ने--इत्यर्थः । अत एव उक्तम्-- विजातीयतया स्थिते इति । स्थपुटस्पर्शवदिति--यथाहि निम्नोन्नतवस्तुनि निम्ने स्पर्शस्य प्रतिघातो भवेत्, तथा अत्रापि संविदः--इत्यर्थः ॥
