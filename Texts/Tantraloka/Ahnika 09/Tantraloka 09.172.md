## Mūla

कला हि शुद्धा तत्तादृक् कर्मत्वं संप्रसूयते ।
मितमप्याशु येनास्मात् संसारादेष मुच्यते ॥ ^mula

kalā hi śuddhā tattādṛk karmatvaṃ saṃprasūyate |
mitamapyāśu yenāsmāt saṃsārādeṣa mucyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 06 - Chapter Nine, part 4#^pg166|p. 149]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=166>))
>
> The pure Force (of limited agency) generates activity (such as meditation or worship), which is (pure) as it is. Although limited, (this action) quickly frees (the fettered soul) from this transmigratory existence.
