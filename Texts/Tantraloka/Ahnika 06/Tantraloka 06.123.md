## Mūla

पितामहान्तं रुद्राः स्युर्द्वादशाग्रेऽत्र भाविनः ।
प्राणे वर्षोदयः प्रोक्तो द्वादशाब्दोदयोऽधुना ॥ ^mula

pitāmahāntaṃ rudrāḥ syurdvādaśāgre'tra bhāvinaḥ |
prāṇe varṣodayaḥ prokto dvādaśābdodayo'dhunā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 11 - Chapter Six, part 4#^pg364|p. 349]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=364>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 6.123cd–124ab.
>
> The months within this yearly breathing cycle, starting with Kārtika, are each one presided over by a Rudra, from Dakṣa up to Pitāmahā, who will be mentioned further ahead. (with 6.122cd–123ab)
>
> The arising of the year in the breath has been explained. Now (follows an exposition) of the twelve-year cycle. This is (calculated) by attributing 60 lunar days to each finger space successively, and (for the transit of Jupiter from one sign to another), and those twelve arise starting from the month of Caitra, (giving each month the value of a year,) in the course of the emergence of twelve years. (with 6.123cd–124)
