# Complete vault audit

**Use when:** you want one independent, thorough look at the whole vault after a large piece of work (first written for the day after the October 2026 plan closed), or once a season. It is read-only and ends in a report; nothing is fixed. It is heavier than the [[Monthly check-up]]'s report; it covers the ground of the retired [[Vault analysis (micro and macro)]] (in `AI/Archive/`) and adds a verification of what the last AI sessions claim to have done.

**Before you start:**

- Close other AI tools; let any other AI work on the vault finish first.
- Start a new Cowork task in the project *Obsidian Assit* with the model you want (Opus, say), connect the vault folder, open Obsidian, and have the runner line from project doc 07 ready to paste (the task will ask).
- Expect a long run with many agents. The report is the only output.
- **Who runs it, model, size:** an AI session as lead with agents; Opus or Fable for the lead and every checker. L: a day or more (19 Oct 2026). Not for maintenance mode; the one still planned is the audit of the 26 Oct corrections in the week of 2 Nov 2026, scoped to that work.
