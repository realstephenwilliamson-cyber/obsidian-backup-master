# Psalms 97 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 96|← Psalms 96]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 98|Psalms 98 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 97|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**97:1** A psalm for David himself. Sing ye to the Lord anew canticle: because he hath done wonderful things. His right hand hath wrought for him salvation, and his arm is holy. ^97-1

**97:2** The Lord hath made known his salvation: he hath revealed his justice in the sight of the Gentiles. ^97-2

**97:3** He hath remembered his mercy his truth toward the house of Israel. All the ends of the earth have seen the salvation of our God. ^97-3

**97:4** Sing joyfully to God, all the earth; make melody, rejoice and sing. ^97-4

**97:5** Sing praise to the Lord on the harp, on the harp, and with the voice of a psalm: ^97-5

**97:6** With long trumpets, and sound of comet. Make a joyful noise before the Lord our king: ^97-6

**97:7** Let the sea be moved and the fulness thereof: the world and they that dwell therein. ^97-7

**97:8** The rivers shall clap their hands, the mountains shall rejoice together ^97-8

**97:9** At the presence of the Lord: because he cometh to judge the earth. He shall judge the world with justice, and the people with equity. ^97-9
