## Mūla

यथा यथा प्रकाशेत तत्तद्भाववपुः स्फुटम् ।
एवं च नीलता नाम यथा काचित्प्रकाशते ॥ ^mula

yathā yathā prakāśeta tattadbhāvavapuḥ sphuṭam |
evaṃ ca nīlatā nāma yathā kācitprakāśate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 11 - Chapter Ten, part 2#^pg349|p. 330]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=349>))
>
> Therefore, the nature of each individual thing (bhāvavapus) is clearly apparent (sphuṭa), in accord with the manner in which this, the Supreme Śiva, of whom we have spoken previously and is Light alone, shines. (with 10.55cd–56ab)
>
> Thus, perceptibility (vedyatva) manifests in the same way that something (which is blue) manifests (its) ‘blueness’. It is associated with an aspect of the object (bhāva) (and not with the subject). (with 10.56cd–57ab)
