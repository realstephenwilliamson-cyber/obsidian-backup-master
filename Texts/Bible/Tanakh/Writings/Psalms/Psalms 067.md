# Psalms 067

## Chapter 67

**67:1** For the leader; with instrumental music. A psalm. A song. ^67-1

**67:2** May God be gracious to us and bless us;  
may He show us favor, *selah* ^67-2

**67:3** that Your way be known on earth,  
Your deliverance among all nations. ^67-3

**67:4** Peoples will praise You, O God;  
all peoples will praise You. ^67-4

**67:5** Nations will exult and shout for joy,  
for You rule the peoples with equity,  
You guide the nations of the earth. *Selah*. ^67-5

**67:6** The peoples will praise You, O God;  
all peoples will praise You. ^67-6

**67:7** May the earth yield its produce;  
may God, our God, bless us. ^67-7

**67:8** May God bless us,  
and be revered to the ends of the earth. ^67-8
