## Mūla

दृढानुरागसुभगसंरम्भाभोगभागिनः ।
स्वोल्लासि स्मरसर्वस्वं दार्ढ्यायान्यत्र दृश्यते ॥ ^mula

dṛḍhānurāgasubhagasaṃrambhābhogabhāginaḥ |
svollāsi smarasarvasvaṃ dārḍhyāyānyatra dṛśyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 14 - Chapter Twenty-Three#^pg355|p. 335]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=355>))
>
> The wealth of passion that emerges spontaneously from one who enjoys the bliss of intercourse with a beautiful woman whose love is firm (and intense) is seen to make (the passion of others) elsewhere firm (and intense).
