## Mūla

सांमुख्यं चास्य शिष्यस्य तत्कृपास्पदतात्मकम् ।
तमाराध्येति वचनं कृपाहेतूपलक्षणम् ॥ ^mula

sāṃmukhyaṃ cāsya śiṣyasya tatkṛpāspadatātmakam |
tamārādhyeti vacanaṃ kṛpāhetūpalakṣaṇam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 12 - Chapter Twenty-One#^pg264|p. 244]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=264>))
>
> (It is said) in the Mālinīvijayottara that (the aspirant who is penetrated by Rudra’s power) ‘is led to a true teacher so that he can obtain both worldly benefits and liberation’, The word ‘to’ (prati) in this sentence denotes (the aspirant’s) ‘presence’ (and proclivity towards the teacher) (sāńnmukhya). The ‘presence’ (and proclivity) of the disciple is essentially that he has been made an object of (the teacher’s) compassion. (with 21.2–3ab)
>
> The expression ‘once he has worshipped him’ is an implied characterization (upalakṣana) of the cause of (his) compassion. (with 21.3cd)
