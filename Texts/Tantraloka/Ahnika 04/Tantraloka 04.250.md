## Mūla

वैष्णवं ब्रह्मसंभूतैर्नेत्यादि परिचर्चयेत् ।
बाधते यो वैपरीत्यात्स मूढः पापभाग्भवेत् ॥ ^mula

vaiṣṇavaṃ brahmasaṃbhūtairnetyādi paricarcayet |
bādhate yo vaiparītyātsa mūḍhaḥ pāpabhāgbhavet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. III
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 03 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v03 10 - Chapter Four, part 9#^pg359|p. 346]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 03.pdf#page=359>))
>
> (However, the reverse is not the case). One should not consider that Śaiva (scripture) can ever be invalidated by the words of Vaiṣṇava (scripture), nor the Vaiṣṇava by those (Vedic scriptures) born of Brahmā, and so on. (with 4.249cd–250ab)
>
> The fool who overrides this in the opposite order (that is, the words of superior scriptures by those of inferior ones) is a sinner. (with 4.250cd)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* ननु विपर्ययेणापि बाधे को दोषः ? -- इत्याशङ्क्याह --

> बाधते यो वैपरीत्यात्स मूढः पापभाग्भवेत् ॥

वैपरीत्यं पूर्वेणोत्तरस्य बाधः । यदुक्तं तत्र --

"यो हि बाधयते पापः स मूढो नष्टचेतनः ।

उत्तरोत्तरवैशिष्ट्यं सर्वेषं परिकीर्तितम् ॥"

इति ॥ २५० ॥
