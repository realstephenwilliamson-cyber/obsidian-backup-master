## Mūla

उच्चाररहितम्वस्तु चेतसैव विचिन्तयन् ।
यम्समावेशमाप्नोति शाक्तः सोऽत्राभिधीयते ॥ ^mula

uccārarahitam vastu cetasaiva vicintayan |
yam samāveśam āpnoti śāktaḥ so 'trābhidhīyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, July 2022 – September 2022
> p. 33 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Malini (journal), July-September 2022.pdf#page=35>))
>
> The penetration one attains whilst thinking of (the nature of) reality (*vastu*) with the mind alone, without (having to practice) the utterance of Mantra (*uccāra*) (or the rest),[^39] is here called Empowered (*śākta*).

[^39]: This is the common meaning of the term '*uccāra*'. See note to TĀ 1/169 for details.
