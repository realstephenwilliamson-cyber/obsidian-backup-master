## Mūla

सामान्यतेजोरूपान्तराहूता भुवनेश्वराः ।
तर्पिताः श्राविताश्चाणोर्नाधिकारं प्रतन्वते ॥ ^mula

sāmānyatejorūpāntarāhūtā bhuvaneśvarāḥ |
tarpitāḥ śrāvitāścāṇornādhikāraṃ pratanvate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 05 - Chapter Sixteen, part 1#^pg60|p. 40]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=60>))
>
> The Lords of the Worlds, invoked (in this way) within the (one) universal (sāmānya) radiant energy (tejas), (when they have been offered) libation and made to hear (the appropriate Mantras, no longer) exert (any) authority over the individual soul.
