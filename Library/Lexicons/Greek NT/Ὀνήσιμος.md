# Ὀνήσιμος

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · noun

**Dodson:** Onesimus — (originally adj: useful, hence the play upon words in Philemon 10, 11, and very common as slave name), Onesimus, a slave of Philemon, a Christian of Colossae. (Strong's 3682; headword Ὀνήσιμος, ου, ὁ)
