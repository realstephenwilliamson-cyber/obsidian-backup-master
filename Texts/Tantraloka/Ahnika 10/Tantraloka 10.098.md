## Mūla

अव्यक्तान्ता यतोऽस्त्येषां सकलं प्रति वेद्यता ।
यत्तूच्यते कलाद्येन धरान्तेन समन्विताः ॥ ^mula

avyaktāntā yato'styeṣāṃ sakalaṃ prati vedyatā |
yattūcyate kalādyena dharāntena samanvitāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 11 - Chapter Ten, part 2#^pg377|p. 358]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=377>))
>
> In this way, the Earth (principle) has fifteen aspects, as do those (principles) from Water to Unmanifest Nature, insofar as they are perceptible (vedyatā) to the Fully Conditioned (sakala) (subjects). (with 10.97cd–98ab)
>
> It is said (in the Mālinīvijayottara) that the Fully Conditioned (sakala) (subjects) possess (the principles) ranging from the Force (of limited agency) up to Earth. (But this is only) a secondary characterization (of these perceivers, which serves to) indicate that, (in their case, all) six coverings (kośa, i.e. kañcuka) have emerged (and are functioning). (with 10.98cd–99ab)
>
> The Fully Conditioned (sakala) (perceivers) are those in which the six coverings, namely, impure Knowledge, Attachment (rāga), the Force (of limited agency) (kalā) and the rest, have emerged (and function) (udbhūta). In the case of those called (Deconditioned by) Dissolution (Pralayākala) (perceivers), all the coverings are in abeyance (nyagbhūta), whereas (in the case of the) Deconditioned by Consciousness ((Vi)jñānākala) (perceivers), these coverings have been destroyed. This is the teaching. (with 10.98cd–100)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* इत्युक्त्या कलान्तं सकलस्यावस्थानं तत्कथमव्यक्तान्तमस्य प्रमातृत्वमुक्तम् ? -- इत्याशङ्क्याह --

> यत्तूच्यते कलाद्येन धरान्तेन समन्विताः ॥
> सकला इति तत्कोशषट्कोद्रेकोपलक्षणम् ।

नह्यनेन वाक्येन पुंसः कलान्तमवस्थानमुक्तं, तथात्वे हि "पुरुषः पञ्चविंशकः"इत्यादिश्रुतिविरोधः स्यात्, किंतु उद्रिक्तमस्य मायादिकञ्चुकषट्कमिति । तद्धि प्रलयाकलस्यानुद्रिक्तं विज्ञानाकलस्य ध्वस्तमिति ॥ ९८ ॥
