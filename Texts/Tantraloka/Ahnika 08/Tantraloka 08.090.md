## Mūla

अन्यवर्षेषु पशुवद् भोगात्कर्मातिवाहनम् ।
प्राप्यं मनोरथातीतमपि भारतजन्मनाम् ॥ ^mula

anyavarṣeṣu paśuvad bhogātkarmātivāhanam |
prāpyaṃ manorathātītamapi bhāratajanmanām || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 05 - Chapter Eight, part 3#^pg157|p. 139]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=157>))
>
> In other lands, one (helplessly) experiences like a fettered beast only the fruits (of action), (that is, one can only) bear (past) Karma. (However,) what can be attained by those who are born in Bhārata surpasses even (the most cherished) desire.
