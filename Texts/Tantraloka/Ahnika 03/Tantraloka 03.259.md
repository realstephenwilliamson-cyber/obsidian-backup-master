## Mūla

तासामेव स्थितं रूपं बहुधा प्रविभज्यते ।
उपाध्यतीतं यद्रूपं तद्द्विधा गुरवो जगुः ॥ ^mula

tāsāmeva sthitaṃ rūpaṃ bahudhā pravibhajyate |
upādhyatītaṃ yadrūpaṃ taddvidhā guravo jaguḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. II
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 02 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v02 11 - Chapter Three, part 8#^pg395|p. 379]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 02.pdf#page=395>))
>
> Those same (powers), stainless and pure, are called Aghorā. They are established in creation, persistence and withdrawal, as well as in the passing away of these three limiting adjuncts (upādhi), by generating the (powers) Ghorā and Ghoratarā, that are of their (same) nature. Their nature, which abides thus (evaṁ sthitan rūpam), is divided in many ways. (with 3.257cd–259ab)
>
> The teachers have said that the form which transcends (these) limiting adjuncts is of two types, according to whether limiting adjuncts do not emerge, or due to (their) cessation (praśama). (with 3.259cd–260ab)

> अनुल्लासादुपाधीनां यद्वा प्रशमयोगतः ।
