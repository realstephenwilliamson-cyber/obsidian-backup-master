# Jan Gonda

Professor of Sanskrit, Avestan, Old Persian and Indo-European linguistics at Utrecht from 1932 until he became emeritus in 1975; his fields were Indo-European and Sanskrit linguistics, the Veda, the history and concepts of religion, and Indonesian philology ([Who Was Who in Indology](https://whowaswho-indology.info/2332/gonda-jan/)).
