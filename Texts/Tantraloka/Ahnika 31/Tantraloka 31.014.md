## Mūla

कोष्ठकार्धेऽपरं चेति युग्ममन्तर्मुखं भवेत् ।
ब्रह्मसूत्राद् द्वितीयस्मिन् हस्ते मर्मणि निश्चलम् ॥ ^mula

koṣṭhakārdhe'paraṃ ceti yugmamantarmukhaṃ bhavet |
brahmasūtrād dvitīyasmin haste marmaṇi niścalam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 04 - Chapter Thirty-One, part 1#^pg90|p. 72]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=90>))
>
> One should then make within half a square another pair (of semi-circles, connected to the preceding ones,) facing inwards. Then, having (firmly) placed one’s hand without moving on the second intersection (marman) (starting) from the thread of Brahmā, one who is well versed (vicakṣana) should fashion two full moons (i.e. circles). (with 31.14–15ab)
