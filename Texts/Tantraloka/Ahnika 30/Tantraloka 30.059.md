## Mūla

व्योमस्विति शिवेनोक्तं तन्त्रसद्भावशासने ।
छेदिनी क्षुरिकेयं स्याद्यया योजयते परे ॥ ^mula

vyomasviti śivenoktaṃ tantrasadbhāvaśāsane |
chedinī kṣurikeyaṃ syādyayā yojayate pare || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 03 - Chapter Thirty, part 2#^pg60|p. 42]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=60>))

> Śiva has taught this in the Tantrasadbhāva, (according to which) this is the Razor that splits (the inner vital centres,) by which one is conjoined with the supreme (reality).
