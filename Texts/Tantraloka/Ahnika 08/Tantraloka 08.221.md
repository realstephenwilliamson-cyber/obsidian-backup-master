## Mūla

ततो वागादिकर्माक्षयुक्तं करणमण्डलम् ।
अग्नीन्द्रविष्णुमित्राः सब्रह्माणस्तेषु नायकाः ॥ ^mula

tato vāgādikarmākṣayuktaṃ karaṇamaṇḍalam |

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 07 - Chapter Eight, part 5#^pg261|p. 243]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=261>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 8.221cd–222ab.
>
> Then comes the sphere of the senses associated with speech and the other organs of action. The Lords (nāyaka) (residing there) within them are, respectively: Agni, Indra, Viṣṇu, Mitra and Brahmā. According to the scriptures, from that comes the sphere of light, consisting of the five organs of knowledge, namely, those of hearing and the rest, the gods of which are the Directions (dik), Lightning, the Sun, Varuṇa and the Earth. (with 8.221–222)
