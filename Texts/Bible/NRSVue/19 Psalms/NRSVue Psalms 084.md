## The joy of worship in the temple

*To the leader: according to The Gittith. Of the Korahites. A Psalm.*

**84:1** How lovely is your dwelling place, O LORD of hosts! ^84-1

**84:2** My soul longs, indeed it faints, for the courts of the LORD; my heart and my flesh sing for joy to the living God. ^84-2

**84:3** Even the sparrow finds a home and the swallow a nest for herself, where she may lay her young, at your altars, O LORD of hosts, my King and my God. ^84-3

**84:4** Happy are those who live in your house, ever singing your praise. *Selah* ^84-4

**84:5** Happy are those whose strength is in you, in whose heart are the highways to Zion.^[Heb lacks *to Zion*] ^84-5

**84:6** As they go through the valley of Baca, they make it a place of springs; the early rain also covers it with pools. ^84-6

**84:7** They go from strength to strength; the God of gods will be seen in Zion. ^84-7

**84:8** O LORD God of hosts, hear my prayer; give ear, O God of Jacob! *Selah* ^84-8

**84:9** Behold our shield, O God; look on the face of your anointed. ^84-9

**84:10** For a day in your courts is better than a thousand elsewhere. I would rather be a doorkeeper in the house of my God than live in the tents of wickedness. ^84-10

**84:11** For the LORD God is a sun and shield; he bestows favor and honor. No good thing does the LORD withhold from those who walk uprightly. ^84-11

**84:12** O LORD of hosts, happy is everyone who trusts in you. ^84-12
