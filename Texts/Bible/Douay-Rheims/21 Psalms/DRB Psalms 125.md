# Psalms 125 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 124|← Psalms 124]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 126|Psalms 126 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 125|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**125:1** When the lord brought back the captivity of Sion, we became like men comforted. ^125-1

**125:2** Then was our mouth filled with gladness; and our tongue with joy. Then shall they say among the Gentiles: The Lord hath done great things for them. ^125-2

**125:3** The Lord hath done great things for us: we are become joyful. ^125-3

**125:4** Turn again our captivity, O Lord, as a stream in the south. ^125-4

**125:5** They that sow in tears shall reap in joy. ^125-5

**125:6** Going they went and wept, casting their seeds. ^125-6

**125:7** But coming they shall come with joyfulness, carrying their sheaves. ^125-7
