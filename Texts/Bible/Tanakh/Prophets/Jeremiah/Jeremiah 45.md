## Chapter 45

**45:1** The word which the prophet Jeremiah spoke to Baruch son of Neriah, when he was writing these words in a scroll at Jeremiah’s dictation, in the fourth year of King Jehoiakim son of Josiah of Judah: ^45-1

**45:2** Thus said the LORD, the God of Israel, concerning you, Baruch: ^45-2

**45:3** You say, “Woe is me! The LORD has added grief to my pain. I am worn out with groaning, and I have found no rest.” ^45-3

**45:4** Thus shall you speak to him: “Thus said the LORD: I am going to overthrow what I have built, and uproot what I have planted—this applies to the whole land.[^1] ^45-4

**45:5** And do you expect great things for yourself? Don’t expect them. For I am going to bring disaster upon all flesh—declares the LORD—but I will at least grant you your life[^2] in all the places where you may go.” ^45-5

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Cf. note at 21.9.
