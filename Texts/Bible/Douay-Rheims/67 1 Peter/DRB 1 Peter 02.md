# 1 Peter 2 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB 1 Peter 01|← 1 Peter 1]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB 1 Peter 03|1 Peter 3 →]]

Compare: [[1 Peter 02|BSB and SBLGNT]]

**2:1** Wherefore laying away all malice, and all guile, and dissimulations, and envies, and all detractions, ^2-1

**2:2** As newborn babes, desire the rational milk without guile, that thereby you may grow unto salvation: ^2-2

**2:3** If so be you have tasted that the Lord is sweet. ^2-3

**2:4** Unto whom coming, as to a living stone, rejected indeed by men, but chosen and made honourable by God: ^2-4

**2:5** Be you also as living stones built up, a spiritual house, a holy priesthood, to offer up spiritual sacrifices, acceptable to God by Jesus Christ. ^2-5

**2:6** Wherefore it is said in the scripture: Behold, I lay in Sion a chief corner stone, elect, precious. And he that shall believe in him, shall not be confounded. ^2-6

**2:7** To you therefore that believe, he is honour: but to them that believe not, the stone which the builders rejected, the same is made the head of the corner: ^2-7

**2:8** And a stone of stumbling, and a rock of scandal, to them who stumble at the word, neither do believe, whereunto also they are set. ^2-8

**2:9** But you are a chosen generation, a kingly priesthood, a holy nation, a purchased people: that you may declare his virtues, who hath called you out of darkness into his marvellous light: ^2-9

**2:10** Who in time past were not a people: but are now the people of God. Who had not obtained mercy; but now have obtained mercy. ^2-10

**2:11** Dearly beloved, I beseech you as strangers and pilgrims, to refrain yourselves from carnal desires which war against the soul, ^2-11

**2:12** Having your conversation good among the Gentiles: that whereas they speak against you as evildoers, they may, by the good works, which they shall behold in you, glorify God in the day of visitation. ^2-12

**2:13** Be ye subject therefore to every human creature for God’s sake: whether it be to the king as excelling; ^2-13

**2:14** Or to governors as sent by him for the punishment of evildoers, and for the praise of the good: ^2-14

**2:15** For so is the will of God, that by doing well you may put to silence the ignorance of foolish men: ^2-15

**2:16** As free, and not as making liberty a cloak for malice, but as the servants of God. ^2-16

**2:17** Honour all men. Love the brotherhood. Fear God. Honour the king. ^2-17

**2:18** Servants, be subject to your masters with all fear, not only to the good and gentle, but also to the froward. ^2-18

**2:19** For this is thankworthy, if for conscience towards God, a man endure sorrows, suffering wrongfully. ^2-19

**2:20** For what glory is it, if committing sin, and being buffeted for it, you endure? But if doing well you suffer patiently; this is thankworthy before God. ^2-20

**2:21** For unto this are you called: because Christ also suffered for us, leaving you an example that you should follow his steps. ^2-21

**2:22** Who did no sin, neither was guile found in his mouth. ^2-22

**2:23** Who, when he was reviled, did not revile: when he suffered, he threatened not: but delivered himself to him that judged him unjustly. ^2-23

**2:24** Who his own self bore our sins in his body upon the tree: that we, being dead to sins, should live to justice: by whose stripes you were healed. ^2-24

**2:25** For you were as sheep going astray; but you are now converted to the shepherd and bishop of your souls. ^2-25
