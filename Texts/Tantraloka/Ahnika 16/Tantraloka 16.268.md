## Mūla

लक्षणं कथितं ह्येष मन्त्रतन्त्रविशारदः ।
तेन मन्त्रार्थसंबोधे मन्त्रवार्तिकमादरात् ॥ ^mula

lakṣaṇaṃ kathitaṃ hyeṣa mantratantraviśāradaḥ |
tena mantrārthasaṃbodhe mantravārtikamādarāt || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 07 - Chapter Sixteen, part 3#^pg139|p. 119]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=139>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 16.267cd–268.
>
> It is for this reason that when (scriptures define) the characteristics of a (true) teacher, (this one) characteristic is stated with (insistent) effort (yatnāt) in various places, namely, that he is an expert in Mantras and their application (mantratantraviśārada). (with 16.267cd–268ab)
>
> Therefore, in order to awaken (the disciple) to the meaning (purpose, goal and ultimate nature of) Mantras (mantrārtha), the teacher should with (great) respect make use in every way of the explanation of the mantras (mantravārttika) (found in the scriptures and transmitted by the tradition), or (when they are used for ritual purposes,) apply the methods of modification (ūha) and exclusion (apoha). (with 16.268cd–269ab)
>
> By virtue of (his assiduous) practice (abhyāsa) of that, (the teacher) is identified with the intention (that initiates its use) (tatsankalpa). (with 16.268cd–270ab)
