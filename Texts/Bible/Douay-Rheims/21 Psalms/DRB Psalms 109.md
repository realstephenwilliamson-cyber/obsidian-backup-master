# Psalms 109 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 108|← Psalms 108]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 110|Psalms 110 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 109|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**109:1** The Lord said to my Lord: Sit thou at my right hand: Until I make thy enemies thy footstool. ^109-1

**109:2** The Lord will send forth the sceptre of thy power out of Sion: rule thou in the midst of thy enemies. ^109-2

**109:3** With thee is the principality in the day of thy strength: in the brightness of the saints: from the womb before the day star I begot thee. ^109-3

**109:4** The Lord hath sworn, and he will not repent: Thou art a priest for ever according to the order of Melchisedech. ^109-4

**109:5** The Lord at thy right hand hath broken kings in the day of his wrath. ^109-5

**109:6** He shall judge among nations, he shall fill ruins: he shall crush the heads in the land of the many. ^109-6

**109:7** He shall drink of the torrent in the way: therefore shall he lift up the head. ^109-7
