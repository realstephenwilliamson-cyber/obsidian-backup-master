## Mūla

यः साक्षादभजच्छ्रीमाञ्श्रीकण्ठो मानुषीं तनुम् ।
अत्र वीर्यं पुरैवोक्तं सर्वत्रानुसरेद्गुरुः ॥ ^mula

yaḥ sākṣādabhajacchrīmāñśrīkaṇṭho mānuṣīṃ tanum |
atra vīryaṃ puraivoktaṃ sarvatrānusaredguruḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 03 - Chapter Thirty, part 2#^pg81|p. 63]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=81>))
>
> Bhūtirāja, who was the venerable Śrīkaṇṭtha (Himself), who, directly visible, had assumed a human body, explained these three Vidyās (to me). (with 30.120cd–121ab)
>
> (The nature of) the virility (of Mantras) has (already) been taught here previously. The teacher should proceed accordingly everywhere (in all instances) in accord with (their) purpose, (the form of their) seed syllables, the (manner in which they induce) penetration into (the supreme reality), and the (manner of their) utterance, etc. There is nothing about (all) this that has not already been explained. Repetition is fruitless and we do not like it at all. (with 30.121cd–123ab)
