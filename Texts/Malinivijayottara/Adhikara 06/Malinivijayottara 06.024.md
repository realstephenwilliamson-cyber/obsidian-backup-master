## Mūla

व्यापकं पदमन्यच्च *** तत्परिकीर्तितम् ।
अपरोऽयं विधिः प्रोक्तः परापरमतः शृणु ॥ ^mula

vyāpakaṁ padam anyac ca *** tat parikīrtitam |
aparo 'yaṁ vidhiḥ proktaḥ parāparam ataḥ śṛṇu || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2024 – April 2024
> p. 31 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Malini (journal), January-April 2024.pdf#page=33>))
>
> *The other (remaining) Part is pervasive*[^32] (and present in all the preceding ones) and that is said, as before, (not to be reckoned separately).[^33] *This is said to be the lower (apara) procedure. Listen (now) to the intermediate one (parāpara).*[^34]

[^32]: MV 6/24a quoted in TĀv ad 16/225-230ab (225cd-230).
[^33]: This is clearly the intended sense. The lacuna in the printed text is assumed to be pūrvavat – 'as before' – or the like.
[^34]: The lower one consists of eighty four finger breadths and the intermediate one of ninety-six. The earth takes up the first four fingers thus the divisions are 4 + 55 + 3 + 18 + 16 = 96.
