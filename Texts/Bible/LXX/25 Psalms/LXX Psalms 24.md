# LXX Psalms 24

← [[LXX Psalms 23]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 25]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 025]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 025]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**24:1** A Psalm of David. To thee, O Lord, have I lifted up my soul.  ^24-1

**24:2** O my God, I have trusted in thee: let me not be confounded, neither let mine enemies laugh me to scorn.  ^24-2

**24:3** For none of them that wait on thee shall in any wise be ashamed: let them be ashamed that transgress without cause.  ^24-3

**24:4** Shew me thy ways, O Lord; and teach me thy paths.  ^24-4

**24:5** Lead me in thy truth, and teach me: for thou art God my Saviour: and I have waited on thee all the day.  ^24-5

**24:6** Remember thy compassions, O Lord, and thy mercies, for they are from everlasting.  ^24-6

**24:7** Remember not the sins of my youth, nor *my sins* of ignorance: remember me according to thy mercy, for thy goodness' sake, O Lord.  ^24-7

**24:8** Good and upright is the Lord: therefore will he instruct sinners in *the* way.[^1]  ^24-8

**24:9** The meek will he guide in judgment: the meek will he teach his ways.  ^24-9

**24:10** All the ways of the Lord are mercy and truth to them that seek his covenant and his testimonies.  ^24-10

**24:11** For thy name's sake, O Lord, do thou also be merciful to my sin; for it is great.  ^24-11

**24:12** Who is the man that fears the Lord? he shall instruct him in the way which he has chosen.  ^24-12

**24:13** His soul shall dwell in prosperity; and his seed shall inherit the earth.  ^24-13

**24:14** The Lord is the strength of them that fear him; and his covenant is to manifest *truth* to them.[^2]  ^24-14

**24:15** Mine eyes are continually to the Lord; for he shall draw my feet out of the snare.  ^24-15

**24:16** Look upon me, and have mercy upon me; for I am an only child and poor.  ^24-16

**24:17** The afflictions of my heart have been multiplied; deliver me from my distresses.  ^24-17

**24:18** Look upon mine affliction and my trouble; and forgive all my sins.  ^24-18

**24:19** Look upon mine enemies; for they have been multiplied; and they have hated me with unjust hatred.  ^24-19

**24:20** Keep my soul, and deliver me: let me not be ashamed; for I have hoped in thee.  ^24-20

**24:21** The harmless and upright joined themselves to me: for I waited for thee, O Lord.  ^24-21

**24:22** Deliver Israel, O God, out of all his afflictions.  ^24-22

[^1]: 24:8 *sc.* as a law-giver.
[^2]: 24:14 *Or,*'it,' *sc.* what has just been stated.
