## Mūla

मायां विक्षोभ्य संसारं निर्मिमीते विचित्रकम् ।
माया च नाम देवस्य शक्तिरव्यतिरेकिणी ॥ ^mula

māyāṃ vikṣobhya saṃsāraṃ nirmimīte vicitrakam |
māyā ca nāma devasya śaktiravyatirekiṇī || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 06 - Chapter Nine, part 4#^pg143|p. 126]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=143>))
>
> The same is said in the scripture (śāstra) called Śrīpūrva (i.e. the Mālinīvijayottara): ‘The desire for worldly pleasure (bhoga) arises in him by the power of the Lord’s will. Thus, (Aghoreśa, alias Ananta,) the first Great Lord of Mantra, disturbs Māyā, and goes on to fashion the diverse world of transmigration (saṁsāra) in order to assist (the soul) who desires worldly (experience).’ (with 9.147–149ab)
>
> And Māyā is Gods power, which is not separate (from Him). It is (His) freedom to make duality (bheda) manifest. (The Great Lord of Mantra) is such (as He is), fashioned by that (power of Māyā). (with 9.149cd–150ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* न च मायापि तदतिरिक्ता काचित्संभवति -- इत्याह --

> माया च नाम देवस्य शक्तिरव्यतिरेकिणी ॥
> भेदावभासस्वातन्त्र्यं तथाहि स तया कृतः ।

यन्नाम हि निखिलजगदुल्लासनक्रीडाशालिनः परमेश्वरस्य भेदावभासने स्वातन्त्र्यं तदेवाव्यतिरेकिणी अपूर्णताप्रथनेन मीनाति हिनस्ति इति माया शक्तिरुच्यते । तथाहि तयैवायं भेदावभासः समुल्लासितः । अत एव कारणे कार्योपचारात्सैव भेदावभास इत्युच्यते ॥ १४९ ॥
