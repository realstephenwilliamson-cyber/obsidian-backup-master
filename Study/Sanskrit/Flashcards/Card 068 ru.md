# रु ru

<span style="font-size:4em; line-height:1.2">रु</span>

**Card 68 of 108** (printed: Card 3-8 · Alpha 84) · consonant + vowel

## Front

- **Glyph:** रु
- **Variation:** —
- **Example:** गुरु
- The card has no stroke order (*How to Write*).

## Back

- **Pronunciation:** As in ‘guru’ (teacher)
- **Class (as printed):** short syllable
- **Note (no printed label):** This is the only time the short u is not placed below the letter.
- **Grid:** a row of vowel signs (◌ा ◌ि ◌ी ◌ु ◌ू) above the five rows of stops + य र ल व; in bold: ◌ु, र
- **Example:** guru (teacher)

## Register

- **Classification:** Combined syllable (irregular) · **Sthāna:** Ligature ra + u
- **Body place** (*mātṛkā-nyāsa*, [[Tantraloka 15.117|TĀ 15.117]]–120): ra: blood (*rakta*) · u: right ear (*karṇa*)
