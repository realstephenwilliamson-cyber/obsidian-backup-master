# Deuteronomy 33 (NRSVue)

## Moses’s final blessing on Israel

**33:1** This is the blessing with which Moses, the man of God, blessed the Israelites before his death. ^33-1

**33:2** He said, “The LORD came from Sinai and dawned from Seir upon us;^[Gk Syr Vg Compare Tg: Heb *upon them*] he shone forth from Mount Paran. With him were myriads of holy ones,^[Cn Compare Gk Sam Syr Vg: MT *He came from Ribeboth-kodesh,*] at his right, a host of his own.^[Cn Compare Gk: Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^33-2

**33:3** Indeed, O favorite among^[Or *O lover of the*] peoples, all his holy ones were in your charge; they marched at your heels, accepted direction from you. ^33-3

**33:4** Moses charged us with the law as a possession for the assembly of Jacob. ^33-4

**33:5** There arose a king in Jeshurun when the leaders of the people assembled, the united tribes of Israel. ^33-5

**33:6** May Reuben live and not die out, even though his numbers are few.” ^33-6

**33:7** And this he said of Judah, “O LORD, give heed to Judah, and bring him to his people; strengthen his hands for him,^[Cn: Meaning of Heb uncertain] and be a help against his adversaries.” ^33-7

**33:8** And of Levi he said, “Give to Levi^[Q ms Gk: MT lacks *Give to Levi*] your Thummim and your Urim to your loyal one, whom you tested at Massah, with whom you contended at the waters of Meribah, ^33-8

**33:9** who said of his father and mother, ‘I regard them not’; he ignored his kin and did not acknowledge his children. For they observed your word and kept your covenant. ^33-9

**33:10** They teach Jacob your ordinances and Israel your law; they place incense before you and whole burnt offerings on your altar. ^33-10

**33:11** Bless, O LORD, his substance, and accept the work of his hands; crush the loins of his adversaries, of those who hate him, so that they do not rise again.” ^33-11

**33:12** Of Benjamin he said, “The beloved of the LORD rests in safety—the Most High^[Cn: Heb *above him*] surrounds him all day long—and he rests between his shoulders.” ^33-12

**33:13** And of Joseph he said, “Blessed by the LORD be his land, with the choice gifts of heaven above and of the deep that lies beneath, ^33-13

**33:14** with the choice fruits of the sun and the rich yield of the months, ^33-14

**33:15** with the finest produce of the ancient mountains and the abundance of the everlasting hills, ^33-15

**33:16** with the choice gifts of the earth and its fullness and the favor of the one who dwells on Sinai.^[Cn: Heb *in the bush*] Let these come on the head of Joseph, on the brow of the prince among his brothers. ^33-16

**33:17** A firstborn^[Q ms Gk Syr Vg: MT *His firstborn*] bull—majesty is his! His horns are the horns of a wild ox; with them he gores the peoples all together to the ends of the earth; such are the myriads of Ephraim, such the thousands of Manasseh.” ^33-17

**33:18** And of Zebulun he said, “Rejoice, Zebulun, in your going out, and Issachar, in your tents. ^33-18

**33:19** They call peoples to the mountain; there they offer the right sacrifices, for they suck the affluence of the seas and the hidden treasures of the sand.” ^33-19

**33:20** And of Gad he said, “Blessed be the enlargement of Gad! Like a lion he lives; he tears at arm and scalp. ^33-20

**33:21** He chose the best for himself, for there a commander’s allotment was reserved; he came at the head of the people; he executed the justice of the LORD and his ordinances with Israel.” ^33-21

**33:22** And of Dan he said, “Dan is a lion’s whelp that leaps forth from Bashan.” ^33-22

**33:23** And of Naphtali he said, “O Naphtali, sated with favor, full of the blessing of the LORD, possess the west and the south.” ^33-23

**33:24** And of Asher he said, “Most blessed of sons be Asher; may he be the favorite of his brothers, and may he dip his foot in oil. ^33-24

**33:25** Your bars are iron and bronze, and as your days, so is your strength.^[Gk: Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^33-25

**33:26** There is none like God, O Jeshurun, who rides through the heavens to your help, majestic through the clouds. ^33-26

**33:27** He subdues the ancient gods,^[Cn: Heb *The eternal God is a dwelling place*] shatters^[Cn: Heb *from underneath*] the forces of old;^[Or *the everlasting arms*] he drove out the enemy before you and said, ‘Destroy!’ ^33-27

**33:28** So Israel lives in safety, untroubled is Jacob’s abode^[Or *fountain*] in a land of grain and wine, where the heavens drop down dew. ^33-28

**33:29** Happy are you, O Israel! Who is like you, a people saved by the LORD, the shield of your help and the sword of your triumph! Your enemies shall come fawning to you, but you shall tread on their backs.” ^33-29
