## Mūla

इत्येतत् प्रथमोपायरूपं ध्यानं न्यरूपयत् ।
श्रीशम्भुनाथो मे तुष्टस्तस्मै श्रीसुमतिप्रभुः ॥ ^mula

ityetat prathamopāyarūpaṃ dhyānaṃ nyarūpayat |
śrīśambhunātho me tuṣṭastasmai śrīsumatiprabhuḥ || ^iast

> The venerable Śambhunātha, satisfied with me, described this visualization (to me), which is the first means to realisation, (imparted) to him by (his teacher and) lord, the venerable Lord Sumati.
