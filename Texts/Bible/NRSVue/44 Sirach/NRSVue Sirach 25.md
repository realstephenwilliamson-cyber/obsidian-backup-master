# Sirach 25 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Sirach 24|← Sirach 24]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Sirach 26|Sirach 26 →]]

## Those who are worthy of praise

**25:1** I take pleasure in three things, and they are beautiful in the sight of God and of mortals:^[Syr Lat: Gk *In three things I was beautiful, and I stood in beauty before the Lord and mortals*] agreement among brothers and sisters, friendship among neighbors, and a wife and a husband who live in harmony. ^25-1

**25:2** I hate three kinds of people, and I loathe their manner of life: a pauper who boasts, a rich person who lies, and an old adulterer who lacks sense. ^25-2

**25:3** If you gathered nothing in your youth, how can you find anything in your old age? ^25-3

**25:4** How attractive is sound judgment in gray-haired women and for aged men to possess good counsel! ^25-4

**25:5** How attractive is wisdom in the aged and understanding and counsel in the venerable! ^25-5

**25:6** Rich experience is the crown of the aged, and their boast is the fear of the Lord. ^25-6

**25:7** I can think of nine whom I would call happy, and a tenth my tongue proclaims: a man who can rejoice in his children, who lives to see the downfall of his foes. ^25-7

**25:8** Happy the man who lives with a sensible wife and who does not plow with ox and ass together,^[Heb Syr: Gk lacks *and the one who does not plow with ox and ass together*] who does not slip with the tongue and who has not become enslaved to an inferior. ^25-8

**25:9** Happy is the one who finds good sense and the one who speaks to attentive listeners. ^25-9

**25:10** How great is the one who finds wisdom! But none is superior to the one who fears the Lord. ^25-10

**25:11** Fear of the Lord surpasses everything; to whom can we compare the one who has it?^[Other ancient authorities add as 25.12: *The fear of the Lord is the beginning of love for him, and faith is the beginning of clinging to him.*] ^25-11

## Some extreme forms of evil

**25:13** Any wound, but not a wound of the heart! Any wickedness, but not the wickedness of a woman! ^25-13

**25:14** Any suffering, but not suffering from those who hate! And any vengeance, but not the vengeance of enemies! ^25-14

**25:15** There is no venom^[Syr: Gk *head*] worse than a snake’s venom^[Syr: Gk *head*] and no anger worse than a woman’s^[Other ancient authorities read *an enemy’s*] wrath. ^25-15

## The evil of a wicked woman

**25:16** I would rather live with a lion and a dragon than live with an evil woman. ^25-16

**25:17** A woman’s wickedness changes her appearance and darkens her face like that of a bear. ^25-17

**25:18** Her husband sits^[Heb Syr: Gk *loses heart*] among the neighbors, and he cannot help sighing^[Other ancient authorities read *and listening he sighs*] bitterly. ^25-18

**25:19** Any iniquity is small compared to a woman’s iniquity; may a sinner’s lot befall her! ^25-19

**25:20** A sandy ascent for the feet of the aged—such is a garrulous wife to a quiet husband. ^25-20

**25:21** Do not be ensnared by a woman’s beauty, and do not desire a woman for her possessions.^[Heb Syr: Gk lacks *for her possessions*] ^25-21

**25:22** There is wrath and impudence and great disgrace when a wife supports her husband. ^25-22

**25:23** Dejected mind, gloomy face, and wounded heart come from an evil wife. Drooping hands and weak knees come from the wife who does not make her husband happy. ^25-23

**25:24** From a woman sin had its beginning, and because of her we all die. ^25-24

**25:25** Allow no outlet to water and no boldness of speech to an evil wife. ^25-25

**25:26** If she does not go as you direct, separate her from yourself. ^25-26
