## Mūla

दोषश्चेह न लोकस्थो दोषत्वेन निरूप्यते ।
अज्ञानख्यापनायुक्तख्यापनात्मा त्वसौ मतः ॥ ^mula

doṣaśceha na lokastho doṣatvena nirūpyate |
ajñānakhyāpanāyuktakhyāpanātmā tvasau mataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 14 - Chapter Twenty-Three#^pg351|p. 331]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=351>))
>
> The defect described here as a defect is not a worldly one; rather, it is considered to be (the teacher’s) lack of knowledge, (his incapacity to) communicate it, and the communication (of matters that are logically) unsound. The disciple also (has other defects). His lack of faith (and confidence) in such knowledge (of Śiva and the scriptures etc.) is the main one, as (all) the other defects derive, indeed, from this one. (with 23.79–80)
