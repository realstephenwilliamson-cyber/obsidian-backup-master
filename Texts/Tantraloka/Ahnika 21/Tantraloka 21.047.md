## Mūla

भुक्तियोजनिकायां तु भूयोभिर्गुरुभिस्तथा ।
कृतायां भोगवैचित्र्यं हेतुवैचित्र्ययोगतः ॥ ^mula

bhuktiyojanikāyāṃ tu bhūyobhirgurubhistathā |
kṛtāyāṃ bhogavaicitryaṃ hetuvaicitryayogataḥ || ^iast

> The variety of fruits of an (initiation) that conjoins (the initiand) to the fruits (he desires), performed by many teachers, is the result of the association (of a corresponding) variety of means (hetu).
