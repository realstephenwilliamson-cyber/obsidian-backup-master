## Mūla

मन्त्रसिद्धिः सर्वतत्त्ववशित्वं कृत्यसंपदः ।
कवित्वं सर्वशास्त्रार्थबोद्धृत्वमिति तत्क्रमात् ॥ ^mula

mantrasiddhiḥ sarvatattvavaśitvaṃ kṛtyasaṃpadaḥ |
kavitvaṃ sarvaśāstrārthaboddhṛtvamiti tatkramāt || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 08 - Chapter Thirteen, part 4#^pg280|p. 258]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=280>))
>
> (The expression) ‘penetrated by Rudra’s power’ refers (implicitly) to the (six) signs (of realisation that manifest in a person who has received it), mentioned here (in the Mālinīvijayottara). These are, in due order, 1) sound unwavering devotion to Rudra, 2) accomplishment of Mantras, 3) dominion over all the principles, 4) success in all one’s projects, 5) the gift of poetry (kavitva), and a (perfect) knowledge of the meaning of all the scriptures. There (in that case) also, according to its own degree of intensity, these (signs) may be present all together or individually. Moreover, (some of these), as the wise man can see, are predominantly (related to the attainment of) liberation, (others for the attainment of) worldly benefits. (with 13.214–216)
