### 4.2–9: Final appeals

*[[NRSVue Philippians 04#^4-2|Text]], p. 405* ^4-2

**[[NRSVue Philippians 04#^4-2|2]]:** *Euodia . . . Syntyche*, whose dissension could destabilize this church, especially given the social esteem commonly accorded Macedonian women (see [[Acts 17#^17-4|Acts 17.4]],[[Acts 17#^17-12|12]]). These two may have sparked the discord prompting Paul’s emphasis on unity earlier (1.27; 2.2). Since Paul’s letter will be read aloud to the congregation, naming both women could aim at quashing the disagreements of these dissidents. *In the Lord*, specifying the gravitas involved (see similar usage in 2.29). ^4-2-2

**[[NRSVue Philippians 04#^4-3|3]]:** *My loyal companion*, some Philippian leader; possibly a proper name (i.e., my loyal “Syzygus”); or, plausibly, Epaphroditus—who is now hearing himself directly charged to settle this quarrel. *Beside me*, as co-evangelists with Paul in the founding of this church. *Book of life*, in which God records the names of every deserving person destined for heaven in the world to come ([[Daniel 12#^12-1|Dan 12.1]]; [[Revelation 03#^3-5|Rev 3.5]]; [[Revelation 20#^20-15|20.15]]; see [[Torah 21 - Ki Tisa#^32-32|Ex 32.32]]; [[Psalms 069#^69-29|Ps 69.28]]; [[Isaiah 04#^4-3|Isa 4.3]]; [[Luke 10#^10-20|Lk 10.20]]). ^4-3

**[[NRSVue Philippians 04#^4-5|5]]:** *The Lord is near*, coming soon, a motivation for the Philippians to live in a godly manner. ^4-5

**[[NRSVue Philippians 04#^4-9|9]]:** *God of peace*, freedom from anxiety imparted to those relying upon God. ^4-9

### 4.10–20: Thanking the Philippians

*[[NRSVue Philippians 04#^4-10|Text]], p. 405* ^4-10

**[[NRSVue Philippians 04#^4-14|14]]:** *Kind of you*, lest, in declaring his independence, Paul appear unappreciative of their gift. ^4-14

**[[NRSVue Philippians 04#^4-15|15]]:** *Except you alone*, see [[2 Corinthians 11#^11-9|2 Cor 11.9]]. ^4-15

**[[NRSVue Philippians 04#^4-18|18]]:** *Paid in full*, releasing them from further obligation. *Fragrant offering . . . sacrifice acceptable and pleasing*, see [[Torah 24 - Vayikra#^1-3|Lev 1.3–4]],[[Torah 24 - Vayikra#^1-9|9]]. ^4-18

### 4.21–23: Closing greetings

*[[NRSVue Philippians 04#^4-21|Text]], p. 406* ^4-21

**[[NRSVue Philippians 04#^4-22|22]]:** *Emperor’s household*, soldiers and staff whom Paul evangelized (see 1.13n.). ^4-22

**[[NRSVue Philippians 04#^4-23|23]]:** *The grace . . . spirit*, similar to the ending of Galatians (6.18). ^4-23
