# Antonia M. Ruppel

A classicist who teaches Sanskrit, Greek and Latin (at Cornell, Oxford and LMU Munich, and online for Yogic Studies), author of *The Cambridge Introduction to Sanskrit* (2017) and *An Introductory Sanskrit Reader* (2021) ([Yogic Studies](https://www.yogicstudies.com/skt-201)); earlier a researcher on Oxford's Uncovering Sanskrit Syntax project ([Oxford Faculty of Linguistics](https://www.ling-phil.ox.ac.uk/people/antonia-ruppel)).
