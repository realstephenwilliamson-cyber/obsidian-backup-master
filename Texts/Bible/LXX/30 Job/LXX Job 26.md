# LXX Job 26

← [[LXX Job 25]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Job 27]] →

*Job · [[LXX]]*

**26:1** But Job answered and said,  ^26-1

**26:2** To whom dost thou attach thyself, or whom art thou going to assist? is it not he that *has* much strength, and *he* who has a strong arm?  ^26-2

**26:3** To whom hast thou given counsel? is it not to him who has all wisdom? whom wilt thou follow? is it not one who has the greatest power?  ^26-3

**26:4** To whom hast thou uttered words? and whose breath is it that has come forth from thee?  ^26-4

**26:5** Shall giants be born from under the water and the inhabitants thereof?  ^26-5

**26:6** Hell is naked before him, and destruction has no covering.  ^26-6

**26:7** He stretches out the north wind upon nothing, and he upon nothing hangs the earth;  ^26-7

**26:8** binding water in his clouds, and the cloud is not rent under it.  ^26-8

**26:9** He keeps back the face of his throne, stretching out his cloud upon it.  ^26-9

**26:10** He has encompassed the face of the water by an appointed ordinance, until the end of light and darkness.  ^26-10

**26:11** The pillars of heaven are prostrate and astonished at his rebuke.  ^26-11

**26:12** He has calmed the sea with *his* might, and by *his* wisdom the whale has been overthrown.  ^26-12

**26:13** And the barriers of heaven fear him, and by a command he has slain the apostate dragon.  ^26-13

**26:14** Behold, these are parts of his way; and we will hearken to him at the least intimation of his word: but the strength of his thunder who knows, when he shall employ *it*?  ^26-14
