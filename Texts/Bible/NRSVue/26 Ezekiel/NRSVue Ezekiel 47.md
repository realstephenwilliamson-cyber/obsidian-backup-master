# Ezekiel 47 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Ezekiel 46|← Ezekiel 46]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Ezekiel 48|Ezekiel 48 →]]

## Water flowing from the temple

**47:1** Then he brought me back to the entrance of the temple; there water was flowing from below the entryway of the temple toward the east (for the temple faced east), and the water was flowing down from below the south side of the temple, south of the altar. ^47-1

**47:2** Then he brought me out by way of the north gate and led me around on the outside to the outer gate that faces toward the east,^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] and the water was trickling out on the south side. ^47-2

**47:3** Going on eastward with a cord in his hand, the man measured one thousand cubits and then led me through the water, and it was ankle-deep. ^47-3

**47:4** Again he measured one thousand and led me through the water, and it was knee-deep. Again he measured one thousand and led me through the water, and it was up to the waist. ^47-4

**47:5** Again he measured one thousand, and it was a river that I could not cross, for the water had risen; it was deep enough to swim in, a river that could not be crossed. ^47-5

**47:6** He said to me, “Mortal, have you seen this?” Then he led me back along the bank of the river. ^47-6

**47:7** As I came back, I saw on the bank of the river a great many trees on the one side and on the other. ^47-7

**47:8** He said to me, “This water flows toward the eastern region and goes down into the Arabah, and when it enters the sea, the sea of stagnant waters, the water will become fresh. ^47-8

**47:9** Wherever the river goes,^[Gk Syr Vg Tg: Heb *the two rivers go*] every living creature that swarms will live, and there will be very many fish once these waters reach there. It will become fresh, and everything will live where the river goes. ^47-9

**47:10** People will stand fishing beside the sea^[Heb *it*] from En-gedi to En-eglaim; it will be a place for the spreading of nets; its fish will be of a great many kinds, like the fish of the Great Sea. ^47-10

**47:11** But its swamps and marshes will not become fresh; they are to be left for salt. ^47-11

**47:12** On the banks, on both sides of the river, there will grow all kinds of trees for food. Their leaves will not wither nor their fruit fail, but they will bear fresh fruit every month, because the water for them flows from the sanctuary. Their fruit will be for food and their leaves for healing.” ^47-12

## The new boundaries of the land

**47:13** Thus says the Lord GOD: These are the boundaries by which you shall divide the land for inheritance among the twelve tribes of Israel. Joseph shall have two portions. ^47-13

**47:14** You shall divide it equally; I swore to give it to your ancestors, and this land shall fall to you as your inheritance. ^47-14

**47:15** This shall be the boundary of the land: On the north side, from the Great Sea by way of Hethlon to Lebo-hamath and on to Zedad,^[Gk: Heb *Lebo-zedad,16Hamath*] ^47-15

**47:16** Berothah, Sibraim (which lies between the border of Damascus and the border of Hamath), as far as Hazer-hatticon, which is on the border of Hauran. ^47-16

**47:17** So the boundary shall run from the sea to Hazar-enon, which is north of the border of Damascus, with the border of Hamath to the north.^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] This shall be the north side. ^47-17

**47:18** On the east side, between Hauran and Damascus; along the Jordan between Gilead and the land of Israel; to the eastern sea and as far as Tamar.^[Compare Syr: Heb *you shall measure*] This shall be the east side. ^47-18

**47:19** On the south side, it shall run from Tamar as far as the waters of Meribath-kadesh, from there along the Wadi of Egypt^[Heb lacks *of Egypt*] to the Great Sea. This shall be the south side. ^47-19

**47:20** On the west side, the Great Sea shall be the boundary to a point opposite Lebo-hamath. This shall be the west side. ^47-20

**47:21** So you shall divide this land among you according to the tribes of Israel. ^47-21

**47:22** You shall allot it as an inheritance for yourselves and for the aliens who reside among you and have fathered children among you. They shall be to you as native-born of Israel; with you they shall be allotted an inheritance among the tribes of Israel. ^47-22

**47:23** In whatever tribe aliens reside, there you shall assign them their inheritance, says the Lord GOD. ^47-23
