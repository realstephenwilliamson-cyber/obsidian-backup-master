## Chapter 5

**5:1** Call now! Will anyone answer you?  
To whom among the holy beings will you turn? ^5-1

**5:2** Vexation kills the fool;  
Passion slays the simpleton. ^5-2

**5:3** I myself saw a fool who had struck roots;  
Impulsively, I cursed his home: ^5-3

**5:4** May his children be far from success;  
May they be oppressed in the gate with none to deliver them; ^5-4

**5:5** May the hungry devour his harvest,  
Carrying it off in baskets;  
May the thirsty swallow their wealth.[^1] ^5-5

**5:6** Evil does not grow out of the soil,  
Nor does mischief spring from the ground; ^5-6

**5:7** For man is born to [do] mischief,  
Just as sparks fly upward. ^5-7

**5:8** But I would resort to God;  
I would lay my case before God, ^5-8

**5:9** Who performs great deeds which cannot be fathomed,  
Wondrous things without number; ^5-9

**5:10** Who gives rain to the earth,  
And sends water over the fields; ^5-10

**5:11** Who raises the lowly up high,  
So that the dejected are secure in victory; ^5-11

**5:12** Who thwarts the designs of the crafty,  
So that their hands cannot gain success; ^5-12

**5:13** Who traps the clever in their own wiles;  
The plans of the crafty go awry. ^5-13

**5:14** By day they encounter darkness,  
At noon they grope as in the night. ^5-14

**5:15** But He saves the needy from the sword of their mouth,  
From the clutches of the strong. ^5-15

**5:16** So there is hope for the wretched;  
The mouth of wrongdoing is stopped. ^5-16

**5:17** See how happy is the man whom God reproves;  
Do not reject the discipline of the Almighty. ^5-17

**5:18** He injures, but He binds up;  
He wounds, but His hands heal. ^5-18

**5:19** He will deliver you from six troubles;  
In seven no harm will reach you: ^5-19

**5:20** In famine He will redeem you from death,  
In war, from the sword. ^5-20

**5:21** You will be sheltered from the scourging tongue;  
You will have no fear when violence comes. ^5-21

**5:22** You will laugh at violence and starvation,  
And have no fear of wild beasts. ^5-22

**5:23** For you will have a pact with the rocks in the field,  
And the beasts of the field will be your allies. ^5-23

**5:24** You will know that all is well in your tent;  
When you visit your wife[^2] you will never fail. ^5-24

**5:25** You will see that your offspring are many,  
Your descendants like the grass of the earth. ^5-25

**5:26** You will come to the grave in ripe old age,[^3]  
As shocks of grain are taken away in their season. ^5-26

**5:27** See, we have inquired into this and it is so;  
Hear it and accept it. ^5-27

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Lit. “home.”
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
