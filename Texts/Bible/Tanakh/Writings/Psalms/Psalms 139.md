## Chapter 139

**139:1** For the leader. Of David. A psalm.  
O LORD, You have examined me and know me. ^139-1

**139:2** When I sit down or stand up You know it;  
You discern my thoughts from afar. ^139-2

**139:3** You observe[^1] my walking and reclining,  
and are familiar with all my ways. ^139-3

**139:4** There is not a word on my tongue  
but that You, O LORD, know it well. ^139-4

**139:5** You hedge me before and behind;  
You lay Your hand upon me. ^139-5

**139:6** It is beyond my knowledge;  
it is a mystery; I cannot fathom it. ^139-6

**139:7** Where can I escape from Your spirit?  
Where can I flee from Your presence? ^139-7

**139:8** If I ascend to heaven, You are there;  
if I descend to Sheol, You are there too. ^139-8

**139:9** If I take wing with the dawn  
to come to rest on the western horizon, ^139-9

**139:10** even there Your hand will be guiding me,  
Your right hand will be holding me fast. ^139-10

**139:11** If I say, “Surely darkness will conceal me,  
night will provide me with cover,”[^2] ^139-11

**139:12** darkness is not dark for You;  
night is as light as day;  
darkness and light are the same. ^139-12

**139:13** It was You who created my conscience;[^3]  
You fashioned me in my mother’s womb. ^139-13

**139:14** I praise You,  
for I am awesomely, wondrously made;  
Your work is wonderful;  
I know it very well. ^139-14

**139:15** My frame was not concealed from You  
when I was shaped in a hidden place,  
knit together in the recesses of the earth. ^139-15

**139:16** Your eyes saw my unformed limbs;  
they were all recorded in Your book;  
in due time they were formed,  
to the very last one of them.[^4] ^139-16

**139:17** How weighty Your thoughts seem to me, O God,  
how great their number! ^139-17

**139:18** I count them—they exceed the grains of sand;  
I end—but am still with You. ^139-18

**139:19** O God, if You would only slay the wicked—  
you murderers, away from me!— ^139-19

**139:20** who invoke You for intrigue,  
Your enemies who swear by You falsely.[^5] ^139-20

**139:21** O LORD, You know I hate those who hate You,  
and loathe Your adversaries. ^139-21

**139:22** I feel a perfect hatred toward them;  
I count them my enemies. ^139-22

**139:23** Examine me, O God, and know my mind;  
probe me and know my thoughts. ^139-23

**139:24** See if I have vexatious ways,  
and guide me in ways everlasting. ^139-24

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Cf. Rashi, Ibn Ezra; meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Lit. “kidneys.”
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^5]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
