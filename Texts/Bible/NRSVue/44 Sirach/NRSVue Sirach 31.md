# Sirach 31 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Sirach 30|← Sirach 30]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Sirach 32|Sirach 32 →]]

## Right attitude toward riches

**31:1** Wakefulness over wealth wastes away one’s flesh, and anxiety about it drives away sleep. ^31-1

**31:2** Wakeful anxiety prevents slumber, and a severe illness carries off sleep.^[Other ancient authorities read *sleep carries off a severe illness*] ^31-2

**31:3** Rich people toil to amass possessions, and when they rest, they fill themselves with their delicacies. ^31-3

**31:4** Poor people toil to make a meager living, and if ever they rest, they become needy. ^31-4

**31:5** Those who love gold will not be justified; those who pursue money will be led astray^[Heb Syr: Gk *pursues destruction will be filled*] by it. ^31-5

**31:6** Many have come to ruin because of gold, and their destruction has met them face to face. ^31-6

**31:7** It is a stumbling block to those who are avid for it, and every fool will be taken captive by it. ^31-7

**31:8** Blessed are the rich who are found blameless and who do not go after gold. ^31-8

**31:9** Who are they, that we may call them happy? For they have done wonders among their people. ^31-9

**31:10** Who has been tested by it and been found perfect? Let it be for them a ground for boasting. Who has had the power to transgress and did not transgress and to do evil and did not do it? ^31-10

**31:11** Their prosperity will be established,^[Other ancient authorities add *because of this*] and the assembly will proclaim their acts of charity. ^31-11

## Table etiquette

**31:12** ^[Some ancient authorities add the title *Concerning Food*]Are you seated at the table of the great?^[Heb Syr: Gk *at a great table*] Do not be greedy at it, and do not say, “How much food there is here!” ^31-12

**31:13** Remember that a greedy eye is a bad thing. What has been created more greedy than the eye? Therefore it covers the face with tears. ^31-13

**31:14** Do not reach out your hand for everything you see, and do not crowd your neighbor^[Gk *him*] at the dish. ^31-14

**31:15** Judge your neighbor’s feelings by your own, and in every matter be thoughtful. ^31-15

**31:16** Eat what is set before you like a well brought-up person,^[Heb: Gk *like a human being*] and do not chew greedily, or you will be despised. ^31-16

**31:17** Be the first to stop, as befits good manners, and do not be insatiable, or you will give offense. ^31-17

**31:18** If you are seated among many persons, do not help yourself^[Gk *reach out your hand*] before they do. ^31-18

**31:19** How ample a little is for the well-disciplined! They do not breathe heavily when in bed. ^31-19

**31:20** Healthy sleep depends on moderate eating; they rise early and feel fit. The distress of sleeplessness and of nausea and colic are with the glutton. ^31-20

**31:21** If you are overstuffed with food, get up to vomit, and you will have relief. ^31-21

**31:22** Listen to me, my child, and do not disregard me, and in the end you will appreciate my words. In everything you do be moderate,^[Heb Syr: Gk *industrious*] and no sickness will overtake you. ^31-22

**31:23** People bless those who are liberal with food, and the testimony to their generosity is trustworthy. ^31-23

**31:24** The city complains of those who are stingy with food, and the testimony to their stinginess is accurate. ^31-24

## Temperance in drinking wine

**31:25** Do not try to prove your strength by wine-drinking, for wine has destroyed many. ^31-25

**31:26** As the furnace tests the work of the smith,^[Heb: Gk *tests the hardening of steel by dipping*] so wine tests hearts when the insolent quarrel. ^31-26

**31:27** Wine is very life to humans if taken in moderation. What is life to one who is without wine? It has been created to make people happy. ^31-27

**31:28** Wine drunk at the proper time and in moderation is rejoicing of heart and gladness of soul. ^31-28

**31:29** Wine drunk to excess leads to bitterness of spirit, to quarrels and stumbling. ^31-29

**31:30** Drunkenness increases the anger of fools to their own hurt, reducing their strength and adding wounds. ^31-30

**31:31** Do not reprove your neighbors at a banquet of wine, and do not despise them in their merrymaking; speak no word of reproach to them, and do not distress them by making demands of them. ^31-31
