# Nehemiah 11

← [[Nehemiah 10]] · [[Tanakh]] · [[Nehemiah 12]] →

Compare: [[NRSVue Nehemiah 11|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 11

**11:1** The officers of the people settled in Jerusalem; the rest of the people cast lots for one out of ten to come and settle in the holy city of Jerusalem, and the other nine-tenths to stay in the towns. ^11-1

**11:2** The people gave their blessing to all the men who willingly settled in Jerusalem. ^11-2

**11:3** These are the heads of the province who lived in Jerusalem—in the countryside[^1] of Judah, the people lived in their towns, each on his own property, Israelites, priests, Levites, temple servants, and the sons of Solomon’s servants, ^11-3

**11:4** while in Jerusalem some of the Judahites and some of the Benjaminites lived:  
Of the Judahites: Athaiah son of Uzziah son of Zechariah son of Amariah son of Shephatiah son of Mahalalel, of the clan of Periz, ^11-4

**11:5** and Maaseiah son of Baruch son of Col-hozeh son of Hazaiah son of Adaiah son of Joiarib son of Zechariah son of the Shilohite. ^11-5

**11:6** All the clan of Periz who were living in Jerusalem—468 valorous men. ^11-6

**11:7** These are the Benjaminites: Sallu son of Meshullam son of Joed son of Pedaiah son of Kolaiah son of Maaseiah son of Ithiel son of Jesaiah. ^11-7

**11:8** After him, Gabbai and Sallai—928. ^11-8

**11:9** Joel son of Zichri was the official in charge of them, and Judah son of Hassenuah was the second-in-command of the city. ^11-9

**11:10** Of the priests: Jedaiah son of Joiarib, Jachin, ^11-10

**11:11** Seraiah son of Hilkiah son of Meshullam son of Zadok son of Meraioth son of Ahitub, chief officer of the House of God, ^11-11

**11:12** and their brothers, who did the work of the House—822; and Adaiah son of Jeroham son of Pelaliah son of Amzi son of Zechariah son of Pashhur son of Malchijah, ^11-12

**11:13** and his brothers, heads of clans—242; and Amashsai son of Azarel son of Ahzai son of Meshillemoth son of Immer, ^11-13

**11:14** and their brothers, valorous warriors—128. Zabdiel son of Haggedolim was the official in charge of them. ^11-14

**11:15** Of the Levites: Shemaiah son of Hasshub son of Azrikam son of Hashabiah son of Bunni, ^11-15

**11:16** and Shabbethai and Jozabad of the heads of the Levites were in charge of the external work of the House of God. ^11-16

**11:17** Mattaniah son of Micha son of Zabdi son of Asaph was the head; at prayer, he would lead off with praise; and Bakbukiah, one of his brothers, was his second-in-command; and Abda son of Shammua son of Galal son of Jeduthun. ^11-17

**11:18** All the Levites in the holy city—284. ^11-18

**11:19** And the gatekeepers: Akkub, Talmon, and their brothers, who stood watch at the gates—172. ^11-19

**11:20** And the rest of the Israelites, the priests, and the Levites in all the towns of Judah [lived] each on his estate. ^11-20

**11:21** The temple servants lived on the Ophel; Ziha and Gishpa were in charge of the temple servants. ^11-21

**11:22** The overseer of the Levites in Jerusalem was Uzzi son of Bani son of Hashabiah son of Mattaniah son of Micha, of the Asaphite singers, over the work of the House of God. ^11-22

**11:23** There was a royal order concerning them, a stipulation concerning the daily duties of the singers. ^11-23

**11:24** Petahiah son of Meshezabel, of the sons of Zerah son of Judah, advised the king concerning all the affairs of the people. ^11-24

**11:25** As concerns the villages with their fields: Some of the Judahites lived in Kiriath-arba and its outlying hamlets, in Dibon and its outlying hamlets, and in Jekabzeel and its villages; ^11-25

**11:26** in Jeshua, in Moladah, and in Beth-pelet; ^11-26

**11:27** in Hazar-shual, in Beer-sheba and its outlying hamlets; ^11-27

**11:28** and in Ziklag and in Meconah and its outlying hamlets; ^11-28

**11:29** in En-rimmon, in Zorah and in Jarmuth; ^11-29

**11:30** Zanoah, Adullam, and their villages; Lachish and its fields; Azekah and its outlying hamlets. They settled from Beer-sheba to the Valley of Hinnom. ^11-30

**11:31** The Benjaminites: from Geba, Michmash, Aija, and Bethel and its outlying hamlets; ^11-31

**11:32** Anathoth, Nob, Ananiah, ^11-32

**11:33** Hazor, Ramah, Gittaim, ^11-33

**11:34** Hadid, Zeboim, Neballat, ^11-34

**11:35** Lod, Ono, Ge-harashim. ^11-35

**11:36** Some of the Judahite divisions of Levites were [shifted] to Benjamin. ^11-36

[^1]: Lit. “towns.”
