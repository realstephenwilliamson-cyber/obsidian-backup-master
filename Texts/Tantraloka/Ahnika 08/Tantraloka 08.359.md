## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 10 - Chapter Eight, part 8#^pg365|p. 347]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=365>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 8.359cd–360ab.
>
> Above (the principle of) Īśvara is that of Sadāśiva, to which (the wise) say, belongs the ‘Pure Enclosure’, adorned with the pair knowledge and action. It is said that beyond the Pure Enclosure is the Enclosure of Knowledge, effulgent with the two powers of being and nonbeing. Then (above that), the scripture describes the Enclosure of Power (which is also called the Enclosure of) the Pramāṇas. (with 8.357cd–359ab)
>
> After the Enclosure of Power comes the Enclosure of Brilliant Energy (tejasvyāvaraṇna), which is adorned with Tejasvī (Energetically Radiant) and Dhruveśa (Lord of Stability). Then (comes) the Enclosure of Right Knowledge (mānāvrti), of which there are four worlds. Beyond the Enclosure of Right Knowledge is the Enclosure of Perfect Purity (suśuddhāvrt), beautiful with (its) three worlds. Above the Enclosure of Perfect Purity, there is that of Śiva, in which there is only one world. (with 8.359cd–361ab)
