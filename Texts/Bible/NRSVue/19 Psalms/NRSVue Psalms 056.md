# Psalm 56 (NRSVue)

## Trust in God under persecution

*To the leader: according to The Dove on Far-off Terebinths. Of David. A Miktam, when the*

*Philistines seized him in Gath.*

**56:1** Be gracious to me, O God, for people trample on me; all day long foes oppress me; ^56-1

**56:2** my enemies trample on me all day long, for many fight against me. O Most High, ^56-2

**56:3** when I am afraid, I put my trust in you. ^56-3

**56:4** In God, whose word I praise, in God I trust; I am not afraid; what can flesh do to me? ^56-4

**56:5** All day long they seek to injure my cause; all their thoughts are against me for evil. ^56-5

**56:6** They stir up strife; they lurk; they watch my steps, as they hoped to take my life. ^56-6

**56:7** Do not deliver them for any reason;^[Gk Compare Syr Jerome: Heb *deliver them because of their iniquity*] in wrath cast down the peoples, O God! ^56-7

**56:8** You have kept count of my tossings; put my tears in your bottle. Are they not in your record? ^56-8

**56:9** Then my enemies will retreat in the day when I call. This I know, that^[Or *because*] God is for me. ^56-9

**56:10** In God, whose word I praise, in the LORD, whose word I praise, ^56-10

**56:11** in God I trust; I am not afraid. What can a mere mortal do to me? ^56-11

**56:12** My vows to you I must perform, O God; I will render thank offerings to you. ^56-12

**56:13** For you have delivered my soul from death and my feet from falling, so that I may walk before God in the light of life. ^56-13
