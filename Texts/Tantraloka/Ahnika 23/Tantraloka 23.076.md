## Mūla

त्रिजगज्ज्योतिषो ह्यन्यत्तेजोऽन्यच्च निशाकृतः ।
ज्ञानमन्यत्त्रिकगुरोरन्यत्त्वधरवर्तिनाम् ॥ ^mula

trijagajjyotiṣo hyanyattejo'nyacca niśākṛtaḥ |
jñānamanyattrikaguroranyattvadharavartinām || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 14 - Chapter Twenty-Three#^pg350|p. 330]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=350>))
>
> One thing is the radiant energy of the light (of the sun that illumines) the three worlds, and another that of the moon. One thing is the knowledge of a Trika teacher, and another that of those (teachers) who belong to lower (schools).
