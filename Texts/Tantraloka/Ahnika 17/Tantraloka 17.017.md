## Mūla

नमस्कारान्ततायोगात्पूर्णां सत्तां प्रकल्पयेत् ।
ततः पूर्णस्वभावत्वं तद्रूपोद्रेकयोगतः ॥ ^mula

namaskārāntatāyogātpūrṇāṃ sattāṃ prakalpayet |
tataḥ pūrṇasvabhāvatvaṃ tadrūpodrekayogataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 08 - Chapter Seventeen#^pg168|p. 148]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=168>))
>
> Then, in order to establish consciousness within the insentient principles (like Earth) (jadābhāsa), the teacher (should form and utter their Mantras). (In order to do this, he) should first apply the case ending of an invocation, (that is, the vocative, to the name of the principle,) and then the case ending of the dative, and then, by adding the salutation (namaskāra) at the end, he should imagine the full (unconditioned) being (sattā) (of consciousness within it). (with 17.16–17ab)
>
> Thus, (the realization of their) full (and perfect) nature is the result of (their) association with the abounding excellence (udreka) of that nature (which is their full, unconditioned being). This is because the exultation (udreka) of (the deity who is) the object of meditation is due to (the humble reverence) of the meditator who bows before it (prahvībhāva). (with 17.17cd–18ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* एतदेवोपपादयति--

> ततः पूर्णस्वभावत्वं तद्रूपोद्रेकयोगतः ॥
> ध्येयोद्रेको भवेद् ध्यातृप्रह्वीभाववशाद्यतः ।

नमस्कारे हि नमस्कर्तृगुणीभावेन नमस्कार्यस्यैव मुख्यत्वं भवेत्— इति भावः ॥
