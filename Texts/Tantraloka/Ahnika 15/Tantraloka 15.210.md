## Mūla

ऊर्ध्वाभिव्यक्त्ययोग्यत्वाद्विष्णोर्धातुश्च पञ्चमम् ।
न वक्त्रं तौ भेदमयौ सृष्टिस्थितिप्रभू यतः ॥ ^mula

ūrdhvābhivyaktyayogyatvādviṣṇordhātuśca pañcamam |
na vaktraṃ tau bhedamayau sṛṣṭisthitiprabhū yataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 05 - Chapter Fifteen, part 4#^pg147|p. 129]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=147>))
>
> Brahmā and Viṣṇu are not fit to manifest above (in the zenith) and so they do not have a fifth face. These two are, respectively, the lords of emission and persistence and, as such, are made of duality (bheda).
