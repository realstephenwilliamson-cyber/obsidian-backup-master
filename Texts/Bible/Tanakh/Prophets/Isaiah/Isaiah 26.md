## Chapter 26

**26:1** In that day, this song shall be sung  
In the land of Judah:  
Ours is a mighty city;  
He makes victory our inner and outer wall. ^26-1

**26:2** Open the gates, and let  
A righteous nation enter,  
[A nation] that keeps faith. ^26-2

**26:3** The confident mind You guard in safety,  
In safety because it trusts in You. ^26-3

**26:4** Trust in the LORD for ever and ever,  
For in Yah the LORD you have an everlasting Rock. ^26-4

**26:5** For He has brought low those who dwelt high up,  
Has humbled the secure city,  
Humbled it to the ground,  
Leveled it with the dust— ^26-5

**26:6** To be trampled underfoot,  
By the feet of the needy,  
By the soles of the poor. ^26-6

**26:7** The path is level for the righteous man;  
O Just One, You make smooth the course of the righteous. ^26-7

**26:8** For Your just ways, O LORD, we look to You;  
We long for the name by which You are called. ^26-8

**26:9** At night I yearn for You with all my being,  
I seek You with all the spirit within me.[^1]  
For when Your judgments are wrought on earth,  
The inhabitants of the world learn righteousness. ^26-9

**26:10** But when the scoundrel is spared, he learns not righteousness;  
In a place of integrity, he does wrong—  
He ignores the majesty of the LORD. ^26-10

**26:11** O LORD!  
They see not Your hand exalted.  
Let them be shamed as they behold  
Your zeal for Your people  
And fire consuming Your adversaries. ^26-11

**26:12** [^2]O LORD!  
May You appoint well-being for us,  
Since You have also requited all our misdeeds. ^26-12

**26:13** O LORD our God!  
Lords other than You possessed us,  
But only Your name shall we utter. ^26-13

**26:14** They are dead, they can never live;  
Shades, they can never rise;  
Of a truth, You have dealt with them and wiped them out,  
Have put an end to all mention of them. ^26-14

**26:15** [^3]When You added to the nation, O LORD,  
When You added to the nation,  
Extending all the boundaries of the land,  
You were honored. ^26-15

**26:16** O LORD! In their distress, they sought You;  
Your chastisement reduced them  
To anguished[^4] whispered prayer. ^26-16

**26:17** Like a woman with child  
Approaching childbirth,  
Writhing and screaming in her pangs,  
So are we become because of You, O LORD. ^26-17

**26:18** We were with child, we writhed—  
It is as though we had given birth to wind;  
We have won no victory on earth;  
The inhabitants of the world have not come to life![^5] ^26-18

**26:19** Oh, let Your dead revive!  
Let corpses[^6] arise!  
Awake and shout for joy,  
You who dwell in the dust!—  
For Your dew is like the dew on fresh growth;  
You make the land of the shades come to life.[^7] ^26-19

**26:20** Go, my people, enter your chambers,  
And lock your doors behind you.  
Hide but a little moment,  
Until the indignation passes. ^26-20

**26:21** For lo!  
The LORD shall come forth from His place  
To punish the dwellers of the earth  
For their iniquity;  
And the earth shall disclose its bloodshed  
And shall no longer conceal its slain. ^26-21

[^1]: Emendation yields “my spirit in the morning.”
[^2]: Meaning of verse uncertain.
[^3]: Meaning of vv. 15–16 uncertain.
[^4]: Lit. “anguish”; taking ṣaqun as a noun formed like zadon and sason.
[^5]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^6]: Grammar of Heb. unclear.
[^7]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
