# 1 Chronicles 3 (Douay-Rheims)

*1 Paralipomenon 3 in the Douay-Rheims*

[[DRB 1 Chronicles 02|← 1 Chronicles 2]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB 1 Chronicles 04|1 Chronicles 4 →]]

**3:1** Now these were the sons of David that were born to him in Hebron: the firstborn Amnon of Achinoam the Jezrahelitess, the second Daniel of Abigail the Carmelitess. ^3-1

**3:2** The third Absalom the son of Maacha the daughter of Tolmai king of Gessur, the fourth Adonias the son of Aggith, ^3-2

**3:3** The fifth Saphatias of Abital, the sixth Jethrahem of Egla, his wife. ^3-3

**3:4** So six sons were born to him in Hebron, where he reigned seven years and six months. And in Jerusalem he reigned three and thirty years. ^3-4

**3:5** And these sons were born to him in Jerusalem: Simmaa, and Sobab, and Nathan, and Solomon, four of Bethsabee the daughter of Ammiel. ^3-5

**3:6** Jebaar also and Elisama, ^3-6

**3:7** And Eliphaleeh, and Noge, and Nepheg, and Japhia, ^3-7

**3:8** And Elisama, and Eliada, and Elipheleth, nine: ^3-8

**3:9** All these the sons of David, beside the sons of the concubines: and they had a sister Thamar. ^3-9

**3:10** And Solomon’s son was Roboam: whose son Abia beget Asa. And his son was Josaphat, ^3-10

**3:11** The father of Joram: and Joram begot Ochozias, of whom was born Joas: ^3-11

**3:12** And his son Amasias begot Azarias. And Joathan the son of Azarias ^3-12

**3:13** Beget Achaz, the father of Ezechias, of whom was born Manasses. ^3-13

**3:14** And Manasses beget Amen the father of Josias. ^3-14

**3:15** And the sons of Josias were, the firstborn Johanan, the second Joakim, the third Sedecias, the fourth Sellum. ^3-15

**3:16** Of Joakim was born Jechonias, and Sedecias. ^3-16

**3:17** The sons of Jechonias were Asir, Salathiel, ^3-17

**3:18** Melchiram, Phadaia, Senneser and Jecemia, Sama, and Nadabia. ^3-18

**3:19** Of Phadaia were born Zorobabel and Semei. Zorobabel beget Mosollam, Hananias, and Salomith their sister: ^3-19

**3:20** Hasaba also, and Ohol, and Barachias, and Hasadias, Josabhesed, five. ^3-20

**3:21** And the son of Hananias was Phaltias the father of Jeseias, whose son was Raphaia. And his son was Arnan, of whom was born Obdia, whose son was Sechenias. ^3-21

**3:22** The son of Sechenias, was Semeia, whose sons were Hattus, and Jegaal, and Baria, and Naaria, and Saphat, six in number. ^3-22

**3:23** The sons of Naaria, Elioenai, and Ezechias, and Ezricam, three. ^3-23

**3:24** The sons of Elioenai, Oduia, and Eliasub, and Pheleia, and Accub, and Johanan, and Dalaia, and Anani, seven. ^3-24
