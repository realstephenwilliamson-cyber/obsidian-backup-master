## Mūla

षोडशाख्ये द्वादशिते द्वानवत्यधिके शते ।
चारार्धेन समं प्रोक्तं शतं द्वादशकाधिकम् ॥ ^mula

ṣoḍaśākhye dvādaśite dvānavatyadhike śate |
cārārdhena samaṃ proktaṃ śataṃ dvādaśakādhikam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 02 - Chapter Seven#^pg51|p. 33]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=51>))
>
> (Once that is done,) in a cycle of twelve (syllables) that is multiplied by twelve, the vital breath will arise one hundred and fifty times (in a day), as twelve times twelve makes one hundred and forty-four. It is said that (in a day, the vital breath will arise) one hundred and twelve times and a half, in (a cycle) said to be of sixteen (syllables) multiplied by twelve, (as sixteen times twelve makes) one hundred and ninety-two. When a cycle of sixteen syllables is multiplied by sixteen, the wise say that the vital breath will arise eighty-four and three eighths times (in a day, as sixteen times sixteen equals) two hundred and fifty-six. When (a cycle) of eight times eight syllables is multiplied by twelve, the vital breath will arise twenty-eight and one eighth times (in a day), as (sixty-four times twelve equals) seven hundred and sixty-eight. (with 7.48–52ab)
