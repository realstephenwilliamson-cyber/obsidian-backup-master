# Psalms 119 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 118|← Psalms 118]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 120|Psalms 120 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 119|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**119:1** In my trouble I cried to the Lord: and he heard me. ^119-1

**119:2** O Lord, deliver my soul from wicked lips, and a deceitful tongue. ^119-2

**119:3** What shall be given to thee, or what shall be added to thee, to a deceitful tongue. ^119-3

**119:4** The sharp arrows of the mighty, with coals that lay waste. ^119-4

**119:5** Woe is me, that my sojourning is prolonged! I have dwelt with the inhabitants of cedar: ^119-5

**119:6** My soul hath been long a sojourner. ^119-6

**119:7** With them that hate peace I was peaceable: when I spoke to them they fought against me without cause. ^119-7
