## Mūla

तदैव किल मुक्तोऽसौ यन्त्रं तिष्ठति केवलम् ।
प्रारब्धृकर्मसंबन्धाद्देहस्य सुखिदुःखिते ॥ ^mula

tadaiva kila mukto'sau yantraṃ tiṣṭhati kevalam |
prārabdhṛkarmasaṃbandhāddehasya sukhiduḥkhite || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 08 - Chapter Thirteen, part 4#^pg289|p. 267]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=289>))
>
> As is said in the venerable Ratnamālā and Gamatantra: ‘when the teacher reveals (the one reality) free of thought constructs, (the disciple) is liberated at that very moment; all that remains (of the disciple) is the machine (of his body).’ (with 13.230–231ab)
>
> The body is subject to pleasure and pain because it is associated with Karma which is in the process of giving rise to its consequences (prārabdhrkarma). He should not doubt (that he is liberated). That is said in the Gamatantra. (with 13.231cd–232ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* ननु यदि तदानीं देहादेर्यन्त्रप्रायता, तत्कथं सुखदुःखे स्यातां स्यातां चेन्न मुक्तत्वम्? -- इत्याशङ्क्याह --

> प्रारब्धकर्मसंबन्धाद्देहस्य सुखिदुःखिते ॥
> न विशङ्केत तच्च श्रीगमशास्त्रे निरूपितम् ।

देहस्य देहबुद्ध्यादेरनात्मनः । न विशङ्केतेति सुखदुःखानुभवान्नास्मि मुक्त इति । ननु नेयं निर्मूला शङ्का, तत्कथमेवमुक्तम्? -- इत्याशङ्क्याह -- "तच्च श्रीगमशास्त्रे निरूपितम्"इति । चो ह्यर्थे ॥
