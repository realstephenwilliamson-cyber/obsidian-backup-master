# Esther (Greek) 8 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Esther (Greek) 07|← Esther (Greek) 7]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Esther (Greek) 16|Esther (Greek) 16 →]]

## Royal favor shown the Jews

**8:1** On that very day King Artaxerxes granted to Esther all the property of the adversary Haman. Mordecai was summoned by the king, for Esther had told the king^[Gk *him*] that he was related to her. ^8-1

**8:2** The king took his ring, which he had taken back from Haman, and gave it to Mordecai, and Esther set Mordecai over everything that had been Haman’s. ^8-2

**8:3** Then she spoke once again to the king, and, falling at his feet, she asked him to avert all the evil that Haman had planned against the Jews. ^8-3

**8:4** The king extended his golden scepter to Esther, and she rose and stood before the king. ^8-4

**8:5** Esther said, “If it pleases you, and if I have found favor, let an order be sent rescinding the letters that Haman wrote and sent to destroy the Jews in your kingdom. ^8-5

**8:6** How can I look on the ruin of my people? How can I be safe if my ancestral nation^[Gk *country*] is destroyed?” ^8-6

**8:7** The king said to Esther, “Now that I^[Gk *If I*] have granted all of Haman’s property to you and have hung him on a pole because he laid hands on the Jews, what else do you request? ^8-7

**8:8** Write in my name what you think best and seal it with my ring, for whatever is written at the king’s command and sealed with my ring cannot be contravened.” ^8-8

**8:9** The secretaries were summoned on the twenty-third day of the first month, that is, Nisan, in the same year, and all that she commanded with respect to the Jews was given in writing to the administrators and governors of the satrapies from India to Ethiopia, one hundred twenty-seven satrapies, to each province in its own language. ^8-9

**8:10** The edict was written^[Gk *It was written*] with the king’s authority and sealed with his ring, and they sent out the letters by couriers. ^8-10

**8:11** He ordered the Jews^[Gk *them*] in every city to observe their laws, to defend themselves, and to act as they wished against their opponents and enemies ^8-11

**8:12** on a single day, the thirteenth of the twelfth month, which is Adar, throughout all the kingdom of Artaxerxes. ^8-12

## Addition e

**8:13** “Let copies of the decree be posted conspicuously in all the kingdom, and let all the Jews be ready on that day to fight against their enemies.” ^8-13

**8:14** So the messengers on horseback set out with all speed to perform what the king had commanded, and the decree was posted also in Susa. ^8-14

**8:15** Mordecai went out dressed in the royal robe and wearing a gold crown and a turban of purple linen. The people in Susa rejoiced on seeing him. ^8-15

**8:16** And the Jews had light and gladness ^8-16

**8:17** in every city and province wherever the decree was published; wherever the proclamation was made, the Jews had joy and gladness, a banquet and a holiday. And many of the nations were circumcised and became Jews out of fear of the Jews. ^8-17
