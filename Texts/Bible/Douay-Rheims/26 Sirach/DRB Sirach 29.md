# Sirach 29 (Douay-Rheims)

*Ecclesiasticus 29 in the Douay-Rheims*

[[DRB Sirach 28|← Sirach 28]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Sirach 30|Sirach 30 →]]

**29:1** He that sheweth mercy, lendeth to his neighbour: and he that is stronger in hand, keepeth the commandments. ^29-1

**29:2** Lend to thy neighbour in the time of his need, and pay thou thy neighbour again in due time. ^29-2

**29:3** Reap thy word, and deal faithfully with him: and thou shalt always find that which is necessary for thee. ^29-3

**29:4** Many have looked upon a thing lent as a thing found, and have given trouble to them that helped them. ^29-4

**29:5** Till they receive, they kiss the hands of the lender, and in promises they humble their voice: ^29-5

**29:6** But when they should repay, they will ask time, and will return tedious and murmuring words, and will complain of the time: ^29-6

**29:7** And if he be able to pay, he will stand off, he will scarce pay one half, and will count it as if he had found it: ^29-7

**29:8** But if not, he will defraud him of his money, and he shall get him for an enemy without cause: ^29-8

**29:9** And he will pay him with reproaches and curses, and instead of honour and good turn will repay him injuries. ^29-9

**29:10** Many have refused to lend, not out of wickedness, but they were afraid to be defrauded without cause. ^29-10

**29:11** But yet towards the poor be thou more hearty, and delay not to shew him mercy. ^29-11

**29:12** Help the poor because of the commandment: and send him not away empty handed because of his poverty. ^29-12

**29:13** Lose thy money for thy brother and thy friend: and hide it not under a stone to be lost. ^29-13

**29:14** Place thy treasure in the commandments of the most High, and it shall bring thee more profit than gold. ^29-14

**29:15** Shut up alms in the heart of the poor, and it shall obtain help for thee against all evil. ^29-15

**29:16** Better than the shield of the mighty, and better than the spear: ^29-16

**29:17** It shall fight for thee against thy enemy. ^29-17

**29:18** A good man is surety for his neighbour: and he that hath lost shame, will leave him to himself. ^29-18

**29:19** Forget not the kindness of thy surety: for he hath given his life for thee. ^29-19

**29:20** The sinner and the unclean fleeth from his surety. ^29-20

**29:21** A sinner attributeth to himself the goods of his surety: and he that is of an unthankful mind will leave him that delivered him. ^29-21

**29:22** A man is surety for his neighbour: and when he hath lost all shame, he shall forsake him. ^29-22

**29:23** Evil suretyship hath undone many of good estate, and hath tossed them as a wave of the sea. ^29-23

**29:24** It hath made powerful men to go from place to place round about, and they have wandered in strange countries. ^29-24

**29:25** A sinner that transgresseth the commandment of the Lord, shall fall into an evil suretyship: and he that undertaketh many things, shall fall into judgment. ^29-25

**29:26** Recover thy neighbour according to thy power, and take heed to thyself that thou fall not. ^29-26

**29:27** The chief thing for man’s life is water and bread, and clothing, and a house to cover shame. ^29-27

**29:28** Better is the poor man’s fare under a roof of boards, than sumptuous cheer abroad in another man’s house. ^29-28

**29:29** Be contented with little instead of much, and thou shalt not hear the reproach of going abroad. ^29-29

**29:30** It is a miserable life to go as a guest from house to house: for where a man is a stranger, he shall not deal confidently, nor open his mouth. ^29-30

**29:31** He shall entertain and feed, and give drink to the unthankful, and moreover he shall hear bitter words. ^29-31

**29:32** Go, stranger, and furnish the table, and give others to eat what thou hast in thy hand. ^29-32

**29:33** Give place to the honourable presence of my friends: for I want my house, my brother being to be lodged with me. ^29-33

**29:34** These things are grievous to a man of understanding: the upbraiding of houseroom, and the reproaching of the lender. ^29-34
