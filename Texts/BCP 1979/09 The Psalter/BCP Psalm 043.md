# Psalm 43

← [[BCP Psalm 042]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 044]] →

*Judica me, Deus* · Book Two · Eighth Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 043]]

**43:1** Give judgment for me, O God,  
and defend my cause against an ungodly people; \*  
deliver me from the deceitful and the wicked. ^43-1

**43:2** For you are the God of my strength;  
why have you put me from you? \*  
and why do I go so heavily while the enemy oppresses me? ^43-2

**43:3** Send out your light and your truth, that they may lead me, \*  
and bring me to your holy hill  
and to your dwelling; ^43-3

**43:4** That I may go to the altar of God,  
to the God of my joy and gladness; \*  
and on the harp I will give thanks to you, O God my God. ^43-4

[p. 645] ^pg645

**43:5** Why are you so full of heaviness, O my soul? \*  
and why are you so disquieted within me? ^43-5

**43:6** Put your trust in God; \*  
for I will yet give thanks to him,  
who is the help of my countenance, and my God. ^43-6
