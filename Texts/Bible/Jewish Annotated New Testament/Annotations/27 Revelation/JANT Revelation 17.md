# Revelation 17 (JANT notes)

### 17.1–19.6: Vision and destruction of the great whore Babylon

*[[NRSVue Revelation 17#^17-1|Text]], p. 566* · In counterpoint to the pregnant woman (ch 12), this spectacle of an alluring but perverse whore (Gk “porne”), *Babylon*, is inspired by the description of the two lustful sisters, Oholah (Samaria) and Oholibah (Jerusalem), in Ezek 23. This figure represents Rome (17.2a,9), but the emphasis on her *fornication* (Gk “porneia”) allows it to condense broader issues of purity and the body (see 2.20–22; 14.4; 22.15). ^17-1

### 17.1–6: Her image

*[[NRSVue Revelation 17#^17-1|Text]], p. 566* ^17-1-2

**[[NRSVue Revelation 17#^17-1|1]]:** *On many waters,* resembles Bronze-Age images of enthroned Canaanite gods (see [[Psalms 029#^29-10|Ps 29.10]]; [[Psalms 046#^46-5|46.4–5]]). ^17-1-3

**[[NRSVue Revelation 17#^17-2|2]]:** *Fornication*, combines impure, chaotic sexuality with the idea of reprehensi-ble political alliances. *Wine . . . drunk*, see 14.20n.; 16.6. ^17-2

**[[NRSVue Revelation 17#^17-3|3–4]]:** *In the spirit*, an ecstatic state (see 1.10; 4.2). *Purple and scarlet*, Roman senators wore these colors. Her “throne” turns from a seat *on many waters* (v 1) to a beast that is identical to the Satan-dragon of 12.3, signifying the diabolical power behind her authority; her attire suggests success in seduction (unlike the shameful nakedness of Oholah and Oholibah in [[Ezekiel 23#^23-1|Ezek 23.1]],[[Ezekiel 23#^23-10|10]],[[Ezekiel 23#^23-18|18]],[[Ezekiel 23#^23-29|29]], etc.). *Heads and . . . horns*, see 12.3. *Cup*, recalls the cup of “horror and desolation” handed to Oholibah ([[Ezekiel 23#^23-31|Ezek 23.31–34]]) and the cup prepared by God for Babylon that intoxi-cates other nations ([[Jeremiah 51#^51-7|Jer 51.7]]), but John reimagines it as a symbol of liquid impurities, the results of fornication (see Lev 15; [[Torah 49 - Ki Teitzei#^23-11|Deut 23.10]]). ^17-3

**[[NRSVue Revelation 17#^17-5|5]]:** *Written a name*, as with those imprinted with the Lamb and beast’s marks, the name on her forehead pertains to her fate as well as her identity (see 9.9; 14.11). Alternately, this *name* resembles a brand with which Roman criminals and slaves were marked. ^17-5

**[[NRSVue Revelation 17#^17-6|6]]:** *Drunk with the blood of the saints*, casts the woman as a cannibalistic monster and extends the *impurities* in her *cup (*v 4) to *blood*, inverting one of the main Jewish dietary regulations ([[Torah 02 - Noach#^9-4|Gen 9.4–6]]; [[Torah 25 - Tzav#^7-26|Lev 7.26–27]]; [[Torah 29 - Achrei Mot#^17-10|17.10–14]]). ^17-6

### 17.7–18: Her interpretation

*[[NRSVue Revelation 17#^17-7|Text]], p. 566* ^17-7

**[[NRSVue Revelation 17#^17-7|7]]:** As is typical of visions in Jewish and Christian apocalyptic literature, the strange concatenation of figures and attributes constitutes a *mystery* that an angel must decode (e.g., [[Daniel 08#^8-15|Dan 8.15–25]]; *4 Ezra* 12.3–35; *2 Bar.* 55–56.2). ^17-7-2

**[[NRSVue Revelation 17#^17-8|8]]:** *Was, and is not, and is about to ascend*, a threefold temporal status parodying John’s epithets of God as having a threefold temporal status (see 1.4,8; 4.8; etc.). *Bottomless pit*, see 9.1. *Book of life*, see 3.5n. ^17-8

**[[NRSVue Revelation 17#^17-9|9]]:** *Mind that has wisdom*, suggests that the symbolism relates to something in the real world (similar codes appear in Dan 7–8 and *4 Ezra* 12). *Seven mountains*, usually identified with the city of Rome. *Seven kings*, likely Roman emperors, although scholars disagree on which are meant. ^17-9

**[[NRSVue Revelation 17#^17-11|11]]:** *Was and is not*, see v 8. ^17-11

**[[NRSVue Revelation 17#^17-14|14]]:** Roman history culminates in eschatological *war* against the Lamb, here given traditional messianic titles, and his followers (*1 En*. 9.4; see [[Revelation 19#^19-16|Rev 19.16]]). This eschatological war does not necessarily replicate that in 16.14–16. ^17-14

**[[NRSVue Revelation 17#^17-16|16–17]]:** As with Ezekiel’s naked figures Oholah and Oholibah, vengeance on the *whore* is performed by her lovers, who strip her *naked* (cf. [[Ezekiel 23#^23-10|Ezek 23.10]],[[Ezekiel 23#^23-25|25–26]],[[Ezekiel 23#^23-29|29]]) in order to condemn the sexually alluring body. Rather than being stabbed or stoned (see [[Ezekiel 23#^23-10|Ezek 23.10]],[[Ezekiel 23#^23-47|47]]), this *whore* is devoured and burned (the punishment for sexual crimes \[[[Torah 09 - Vayeshev#^38-24|Gen 38.24]]; [[Torah 31 - Emor#^21-9|Lev 21.9]]\]). ^17-16

**[[NRSVue Revelation 17#^17-17|17]]:** *Put it into their hearts*, in turning against the *whore*, even the Satan-dragon serves God’s purpose. ^17-17
