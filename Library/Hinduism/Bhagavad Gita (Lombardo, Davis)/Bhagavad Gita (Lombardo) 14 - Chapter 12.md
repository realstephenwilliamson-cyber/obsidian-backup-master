## Chapter Twelve

Arjuna then asked:

\[1\] “So, some committed devotees
worship You continually,
some the Eternal Absolute.
Which of these knows *Yoga* better?”

The Blessed Lord answered:

\[2\] “Those who fasten their minds on Me
who worship Me continually,
endowed with supreme conviction,
are for Me the most devoted.

\[3\] But those who worship the Eternal,
the Undefined, Unmanifest,
Omnipresent, Unthinkable,
the Constant and Immovable,

\[4\] In control of all their senses,
even-minded on every side,
rejoicing in all creatures’ welfare,
they indeed attain Me also.

\[5\] Those focused on the Unmanifest
do meet with greater hindrances.
It is hard for embodied beings
to attain the Unmanifest.

\[6\] But those who renounce all actions
in Me, holding Me as supreme,
who worship Me, meditating
on Me in unswerving *Yoga*,

[p. 73] \[7\] I will soon deliver them, those
whose consciousness has entered Me,
from the ocean of death and rebirth,
O Arjuna, Son of Pritha.

\[8\] So keep your mind on Me alone,
let your consciousness enter Me,
and then there is no doubt that you
will hereafter abide in Me.

\[9\] If you prove to be unable
to keep your mind steady on Me,
then you can seek to attain Me
through constant practice of *Yoga*.

\[10\] If you are unable to practice,
be intent on what my work is.
Even by performing actions
for My sake, you will be perfect.

\[11\] If you cannot do even this,
rely on my *yogic* power.
Abandon all fruits of action
and then act, but with self-restraint.

\[12\] Knowledge is better than practice,
meditation better than knowledge,
renunciation better still.
Peace follows renunciation.

\[13\] A person who hates no being,
kindly and compassionate,
free from “mine” and without ego,
the same in pain and pleasure, patient,

\[14\] A *yogin* always satisfied,
of firm resolve, with self-control,
[p. 74] whose mind is always fixed on Me
in devotion, is dear to Me.

\[15\] One who does not shrink from the world,
nor the world from him, freed from joy,
from impatience, distress, and fear—
such a one too is dear to Me.

\[16\] Whoever is impartial, pure,
able, free from anxiety,
without ambition, and is also
devoted to Me, is dear to Me.

\[17\] One without either joy or hate,
without sorrow or desire,
beyond pleasant and unpleasant,
and devoted, is dear to Me.

\[18\] The same toward friend and enemy,
the same in honor and disgrace,
in cold and heat, pain and pleasure,
liberated from attachments,

\[19\] Silent in both praise and censure,
content with anything at all,
homeless, steady, and devoted—
such a person is dear to Me.

\[20\] Those who honor the duteous way
to immortality just declared,
and who believe with devotion
I am Supreme, are most dear to Me.
