## Mūla

अथातः संप्रवक्ष्यामि भुवनाध्वानमीश्वरि ।
आदौ कालाग्निभुवनं शोधितव्यं प्रयत्नतः ॥ ^mula

athātaḥ saṁpravakṣyāmi bhuvanādhvānam īśvari |
ādau kālāgnibhuvanaṁ śodhitavyaṁ prayatnataḥ || ^iast

> **1) Kālāgni and the Netherworlds**
>
> *Initially one should purify the world of Kālāgni with effort*.[^29] Once the (nether) world of Kūṣmāṇḍa has been purified, Avīci and Kumbhīpāka along with Raurava, the third (hell), are all undoubtedly purified. (with 5.1cd–2)

[^29]: MV 5/1d TĀv ad 8/ (438-445) (437cd-445ab).
