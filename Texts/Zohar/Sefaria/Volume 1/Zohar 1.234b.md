# Zohar 1.234b

← [[Zohar 1.234a]] · [[Zohar (Soncino)]] · [[Zohar 1.235a]] →

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 3.07 - Parashat Va-Yhi#^z1-234b|Parashat Va-Yḥi, at 1:234b]]

*Volume 1, folio 234b* · Vayechi

*Passage Vayechi 53:517 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 1.234a#^vayechi-53-517|Vayechi 53:517]].*

**Vayechi 53:518** When Jacob said to his sons, “I will tell you what will befall you in the latter end of days”, a kind of sadness came over him and the Shekinah departed. His sons, however, raised their voices and said, “Hear, O Israel, etc.”, and Jacob answered, “Blessed be the name of his glorious kingdom for ever and ever”, and on this proclamation of the unity the Shekinah returned to its place. AND JACOB CALLED. The word ‘called’ signifies that he established them in their place above and below. ^vayechi-53-518

**Vayechi 53:519** Similarly, Moses “called Hosea son of Nun, Joshua” (Num. 13, 16) to establish him in his proper place. There is a similar significance in the expressions “And he called his name Jacob” (Gen. 25, 26) and “the God of Israel called him El” (Gen. 33, 20). ^vayechi-53-519

**Vayechi 53:520** So, too, “I called from my sorrow unto the Lord” (Jonah, 3, 7), signifying that one who praises his Master and addresses supplications to Him establishes his Master more firmly, by showing that all depends upon Him and not upon any other power. ^vayechi-53-520

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Vayechi 53:521.*

**Vayechi 53:522** AND HE SAID. It has been laid down that “saying” means “thinking”, as in the expression “And thou shalt say in thy heart” (Deut. 7, 17). ASSEMBLE YOURSELVES ; that is, in complete harmony. AND I SHALL TELL YOU. The word “tell” (*agidah*) contains an allusion to the esoteric wisdom. ^vayechi-53-522

**Vayechi 53:523** He sought to reveal to them their final destiny. ^vayechi-53-523

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Vayechi 53:524.*

**Vayechi 53:525** It may be asked, seeing that he did not reveal what he sought to reveal, why are his words, which were afterwards belied, recorded in the Scripture? The truth is that all that was needful to be revealed is completely stated, and there is a hidden meaning within, and so nothing in the Scripture is belied. ^vayechi-53-525

**Vayechi 53:526** In fact, everything is included in the Scripture, and there is no word or letter short in it. Jacob said all that was needful for him to say, but not all openly, and not a letter was short of what was required. ^vayechi-53-526

**Vayechi 53:527** R. Judah and R. Jose were one day sitting at the gate of Lydda. Said the latter: ‘We are told that Jacob blessed his sons, but what are the blessings?’ R. Judah answered: ‘He did indeed bless them, as, for instance, “Judah, thee shall thy brethren praise”, “Dan shall judge his people”, “Out of Asher his bread shall be fat”, and so forth; ^vayechi-53-527

**Vayechi 53:528** but what he sought to reveal to them he did not reveal, namely, the end. We have laid down that there is an end to the right and an end to the left, and he sought to reveal to them the end (of the left) in order that they might keep themselves pure from uncleanness. What he revealed to them referred only to the time when they were in the Holy Land; later things were not stated openly, but are only hinted at in this section and in these blessings.’ ^vayechi-53-528
