## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 10 - Chapter Six, part 3#^pg345|p. 330]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=345>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 6.103cd–104ab.
>
> The Sun is the means of knowledge, whereas the Moon is the object of knowledge. (The two, are, respectively,) knowledge and action. Rāhu is the (individual) Māyā subject, who is skilled in obscuring them (ācchādana) and so, as he is (only) darkness (famorūpa), he cannot destroy (them). (with 6.102–103ab)
>
> The one who does destroy them is the primary (most authentic) subject, who is the nondual outpouring (of pure consciousness that arises from) the (pulsing) union (sarrghatta) which is the fusion of these (three, that is, subject, object and means of knowledge). (with 6.103cd)

*Lead-in:* नन्वेवमेतद्विलापने कः क्षम ? -- इत्याशङ्क्याह --

> तत्सङ्घट्टाद्वयोल्लासो मुख्यो माता विलापकः ॥

तेषां मातृमानमेयानां शङ्घट्टः"सामरस्यं ततः समुल्लसितमद्वयमेव मुख्यः प्रमाता विलापकस्तत्त्रयसङ्घट्टनेन परसंविन्मात्रसार एवेत्यर्थः । यदाहुः --

"प्राणार्कमानहठघट्टितमेयचन्द्र विद्रावितामृतरसोत्सुकितः खमाता ।

स्वर्भानुरावृणुत एव रविं रसं तु पुण्ये ग्रहेऽत्र रसयेत्त्रयघट्टनज्ञः ॥"

इति ॥ १०३ ॥
