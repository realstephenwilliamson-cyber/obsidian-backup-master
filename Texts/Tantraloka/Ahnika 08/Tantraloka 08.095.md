## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 05 - Chapter Eight, part 3#^pg159|p. 141]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=159>))
>
> Svāyambhuva Manu had ten sons. Three of them renounced the world. Now (the first son) was a king called Agnīdhra, (who reigned) on Jambūdvīpa. He had nine sons, and so (Jambudvīpa) was divided into nine parts. Nābhi, the ninth of these sons, had a grandson called Ārṣabha, whose son was Bharata, who had eight sons and a daughter. They divided the continent into nine parts. A ninth part of that (is the land of Jambūdvīpa), which thus measures only one hundred thousand leagues (in diameter). (with 8.94–96)
