# Tanya (Kehot)

*Tanya* by Rabbi Schneur Zalman of Liadi in the Kehot Publication Society translation, one note per chapter, following Sefaria's five parts; front matter (title page, approbations, forewords) gets a note of its own.

*Lessons in Tanya* on Chabad.org (Rabbi Yosef Wineberg's commentary, chapter by chapter): [the whole book](https://www.chabad.org/library/tanya/tanya_cdo/aid/6237/jewish/Lessons-in-Tanya-English-Hebrew.htm). Each chapter note below links its own lesson.
