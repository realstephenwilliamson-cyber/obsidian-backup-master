# Zohar 3.052a

← [[Zohar 3.051b]] · [[Zohar (Soncino)]] · [[Zohar 3.052b]] →

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 7.06 - Parashat Tazri'a#^z3-52a|Parashat Tazri’a, at 3:52a]]

*Volume 3, folio 52a* · Tazria

*Passage Tazria 34:180 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 3.051b#^tazria-34-180|Tazria 34:180]].*

**Tazria 35:181** R. Eleazar was once going to see his father-in-law, accompanied by R. Abba. He said: ‘Let us discourse on the Torah as we go.’ R. Eleazar then took as his text the verse: “Say, I pray thee, thou art my sister” (Gen. 12, 13). ‘This’, he said, ‘is very strange. Can we believe that a God-fearing man like Abram should speak thus to his wife in order that he might be well treated? The truth is, however, that Abram relied on the merit of his wife, and not on his own merit, to procure for him the money of the heathen, for a man obtains money through the merit of his wife, as it is written: “House and riches are an inheritance from fathers, but a prudent wife is from the Lord” (Prov. 19, 14). ^tazria-35-181

**Tazria 35:182** Further, he saw an angel going before her, who said to him: “Fear not, Abram, God sent me to procure for her the money of the heathen and to protect her from all harm”. Hence Abram did not fear for his wife but for himself, since he saw the angel with her but not with himself. ^tazria-35-182

**Tazria 35:183** Therefore he said to her: “Say, I pray thee, that thou art my sister, that he (the angel) may do good to me in this world, and that my soul may live in the next world for thy sake, if thou departest not from the right way, for otherwise death awaits me in this world.” ^tazria-35-183

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Tazria 35:184–35:185.*

**Tazria 35:186** R. Abba discoursed on the verse: “As in the day of thy coming forth from the land of Egypt will I show unto him marvellous things” (Mic. 7, 15). He said: ‘God will one day bring deliverance to his sons as in the days when He sent to deliver Israel and inflicted plagues on Egypt for their sake. What is the difference between this deliverance and that of Egypt? The deliverance in Egypt was from one king and one country: here it will be from all the kings of the earth. ^tazria-35-186

**Tazria 35:187** Then God will be glorified in all the world, and all shall know His dominion, as it is written: “The Lord shall be king over the whole earth” (Zech. 14, 9). And they shall bring Israel as an offering to the Holy One, blessed be He, as it is written, “And they shall bring all your brethren”, etc. (Isa. 66, 20). Then the patriarchs shall rejoice to see the deliverance of their sons, and so it is written: “As in the days of thy going forth from Egypt I shall show him wonders.” ^tazria-35-187
