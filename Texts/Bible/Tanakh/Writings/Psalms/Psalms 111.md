## Chapter 111

**111:1** Hallelujah.  
I praise the LORD with all my heart  
in the assembled congregation of the upright. ^111-1

**111:2** The works of the LORD are great,  
within reach of all who desire them.[^1] ^111-2

**111:3** His deeds are splendid and glorious;  
His beneficence is everlasting; ^111-3

**111:4** He has won renown for His wonders.  
The LORD is gracious and compassionate; ^111-4

**111:5** He gives food to those who fear Him;  
He is ever mindful of His covenant. ^111-5

**111:6** He revealed to His people His powerful works,  
in giving them the heritage of nations. ^111-6

**111:7** His handiwork is truth and justice;  
all His precepts are enduring, ^111-7

**111:8** well-founded for all eternity,  
wrought of truth and equity. ^111-8

**111:9** He sent redemption to His people;  
He ordained His covenant for all time;  
His name is holy and awesome. ^111-9

**111:10** The beginning[^2] of wisdom is the fear of the LORD;  
all who practice it gain sound understanding.  
Praise of Him is everlasting. ^111-10

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Or “chief part.”
