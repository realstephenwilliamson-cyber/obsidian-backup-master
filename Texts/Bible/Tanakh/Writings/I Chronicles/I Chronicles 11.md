## Chapter 11

**11:1** [^1]All Israel gathered to David at Hebron and said, “We are your own flesh and blood. ^11-1

**11:2** Long before now, even when Saul was king, you were the leader of Israel; and the LORD your God said to you: You shall shepherd My people Israel; you shall be ruler of My people Israel.” ^11-2

**11:3** All the elders of Israel came to the king at Hebron, and David made a pact with them in Hebron before the LORD. And they anointed David king over Israel, according to the word of the LORD through Samuel. ^11-3

**11:4** David and all Israel set out for Jerusalem, that is Jebus, where the Jebusite inhabitants of the land lived. ^11-4

**11:5** David was told by the inhabitants of Jebus, “You will never get in here!” But David captured the stronghold of Zion; it is now the City of David. ^11-5

**11:6** David said, “Whoever attacks the Jebusites first will be the chief officer”; Joab son of Zeruiah attacked first, and became the chief. ^11-6

**11:7** David occupied the stronghold; therefore it was renamed the City of David. ^11-7

**11:8** David also fortified the surrounding area, from the Millo round about, and Joab rebuilt the rest of the city. ^11-8

**11:9** David kept growing stronger, for the LORD of Hosts was with him. ^11-9

**11:10** And these were David’s chief warriors who strongly supported him in his kingdom, together with all Israel, to make him king, according to the word of the LORD concerning Israel. ^11-10

**11:11** This is the list of David’s warriors: Jashobeam son of Hachmoni, the chief officer; he wielded his spear against three hundred and slew them all on one occasion. ^11-11

**11:12** Next to him was Eleazar son of Dodo, the Ahohite; he was one of the three warriors. ^11-12

**11:13** He was with David at Pas Dammim when the Philistines gathered there for battle. There was a plot of ground full of barley there; the troops had fled from the Philistines, ^11-13

**11:14** but they took their stand in the middle of the plot and defended it, and they routed the Philistines. Thus the LORD wrought a great victory. ^11-14

**11:15** Three of the thirty chiefs went down to the rock to David, at the cave of Adullam, while a force of Philistines was encamped in the Valley of Rephaim. ^11-15

**11:16** David was then in the stronghold, and a Philistine garrison was then at Bethlehem. ^11-16

**11:17** David felt a craving and said, “If only I could get a drink of water from the cistern which is by the gate of Bethlehem!” ^11-17

**11:18** So the three got through the Philistine camp, and drew water from the cistern which is by the gate of Bethlehem, and they carried it back to David. But David would not drink it, and he poured it out as a libation to the LORD. ^11-18

**11:19** For he said, “God forbid that I should do this! Can I drink the blood of these men who risked their lives?”—for they had brought it at the risk of their lives, and he would not drink it. Such were the exploits of the three warriors. ^11-19

**11:20** Abshai, the brother of Joab, was head of another three. He once wielded his spear against three hundred and slew them. He won a name among the three; ^11-20

**11:21** among the three he was more highly regarded than the other two, and so he became their commander. However, he did not attain to the other three. ^11-21

**11:22** Benaiah son of Jehoiada from Kabzeel was a brave soldier who performed great deeds. He killed the two [sons] of Ariel of Moab. Once, on a snowy day, he went down into a pit and killed a lion. ^11-22

**11:23** He also killed an Egyptian, a giant of a man five cubits tall. The Egyptian had a spear in his hand, like a weaver’s beam, yet [Benaiah] went down against him with a club, wrenched the spear out of the Egyptian’s hand, and killed him with his own spear. ^11-23

**11:24** Such were the exploits of Benaiah son of Jehoiada; and he won a name among the three warriors. ^11-24

**11:25** He was highly regarded among the thirty, but he did not attain to the three. David put him in charge of his bodyguard.[^2] ^11-25

**11:26** The valiant warriors: Asahel brother of Joab, Elhanan son of Dodo from Bethlehem, ^11-26

**11:27** Shammoth the Harorite, Helez the Pelonite, ^11-27

**11:28** Ira son of Ikkesh from Tekoa, Abiezer of Anathoth, ^11-28

**11:29** Sibbecai the Hushathite, Ilai the Ahohite, ^11-29

**11:30** Mahrai the Netophathite, Heled son of Baanah the Netophathite, ^11-30

**11:31** Ittai son of Ribai from Gibeah of the Benjaminites, Benaiah of Pirathon, ^11-31

**11:32** Hurai of Nahale-gaash, Abiel the Arbathite, ^11-32

**11:33** Azmaveth the Bahrumite, Eliahba of Shaalbon, ^11-33

**11:34** the sons of Hashem the Gizonite, Jonathan son of Shageh the Hararite, ^11-34

**11:35** Ahiam son of Sacar the Hararite, Eliphal son of Ur, ^11-35

**11:36** Hepher the Mecherathite, Ahijah the Pelonite, ^11-36

**11:37** Hezro the Carmelite, Naarai son of Ezbai, ^11-37

**11:38** Joel brother of Nathan, Mibhar son of Hagri, ^11-38

**11:39** Zelek the Ammonite, Naharai the Berothite—the arms-bearer of Joab son of Zeruiah— ^11-39

**11:40** Ira the Ithrite, Gareb the Ithrite, ^11-40

**11:41** Uriah the Hittite, Zabad son of Ahlai. ^11-41

**11:42** Adina son of Shiza the Reubenite, a chief of the Reubenites, and thirty with him; ^11-42

**11:43** Hanan son of Maacah, and Joshaphat the Mithnite; ^11-43

**11:44** Uzziah the Ashterathite, Shama and Jeiel sons of Hotham the Aroerite; ^11-44

**11:45** Jedaiael son of Shimri, and Joha his brother, the Tizite; ^11-45

**11:46** Eliel the Mahavite, and Jeribai and Joshaviah sons of Elnaam, and Ithmah the Moabite; ^11-46

**11:47** Eliel, Obed, and Jaassiel the Mezobaite. ^11-47

[^1]: With vv. 1–9, cf. 2 Sam. 5.1–10, and with vv. 11–41, cf. 2 Sam. 23.8–39.
