## Mūla

स्वकारणे तिरोभूतिर्ध्वंसो यत्तेन नाध्रुवम् ।
एवं रसादिशब्दान्ततन्मात्रेष्वपि योजना ॥ ^mula

svakāraṇe tirobhūtirdhvaṃso yattena nādhruvam |
evaṃ rasādiśabdāntatanmātreṣvapi yojanā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 09 - Chapter Nine, part 7#^pg281|p. 262]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=281>))
>
> Thus, it is said to be pervasive, and because it has a cause, it is not stable (and permanent). (However,) as destruction is (essentially) its disappearance into its cause, it is not unstable (and impermanent). (with 9.281cd–282ab)
>
> The same also applies to the other subtle elements, ranging from taste to sound. (with 9.282cd)
