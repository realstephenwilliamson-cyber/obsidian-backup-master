## Mūla

तथा पुरेष्वपीत्येवं तद्विशेषेण नोदितम् ।
श्रीत्रैयम्बकसन्तानवितताम्बरभास्करः ॥ ^mula

tathā pureṣvapītyevaṃ tadviśeṣeṇa noditam |
śrītraiyambakasantānavitatāmbarabhāskaraḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 10 - Chapter Six, part 3#^pg328|p. 313]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=328>))
>
> Just as within the bodies (of living beings) the Days and Nights (throughout the year) that decrease and increase (in the length) are not equal, the same (takes place) within the world orders (pura), however it has not been taught? in detail in this way (in their case). (with 6.87cd–88ab)
>
> The venerable Śambhu(nātha), who is the sun of the extensive sky of the lineage of the venerable Traiyambaka, taught me the procession of Day and Night in this way. (with 6.88cd–89ab)
