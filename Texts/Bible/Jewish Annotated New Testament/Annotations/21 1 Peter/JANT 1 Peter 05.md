### 5.1–14: Final instructions

*[[NRSVue 1 Peter 05#^5-1|Text]], p. 509* ^5-1

**[[NRSVue 1 Peter 05#^5-1|1]]:** *Elder*, Gk “sympresbyteros,” lit., “co-elder.” *Witness of the sufferings of Christ*, the author claims to have witnessed Christ’s suffering, establishing his authority as an apostle. *Elders*, for elders as pastors/shepherds, see Acts 20; [[1 Timothy 03#^3-1|1 Tim 3.1–7]]; [[Titus 01#^1-5|Titus 1.5–9]]; *1 Clem.* 4.43. According to 1 Peter, elders are shepherds of the people, compared to the *chief shepherd* (5.4) who is *shepherd and guardian of your souls* (2.25). In Jewish tradition also, the term “elder” was not only an indication of age, but also of wisdom and leadership, *t. Meg.* 3.24; *Sifra Kedoshim* 7.12. ^5-1-2

**[[NRSVue 1 Peter 05#^5-2|2]]:** *Not for sordid gain*, elders are caretakers, not masters, thus they should lead generously, with humility. ^5-2

**[[NRSVue 1 Peter 05#^5-4|4]]:** *Crown of glory*, see 1.4. ^5-4

**[[NRSVue 1 Peter 05#^5-5|5–6]]:** On being subject to the authority see 2.13,18; 3.1,5,22. *God opposes the proud, but gives grace to the humble*, [[LXX Proverbs 03#^3-34|Prov 3.34]]\[LXX\]. The previous verse ([[Proverbs 03#^3-33|Prov 3.33]]) shares the contrast between God’s house and those outside it as in, 1Pet 4.17. ^5-5

**[[NRSVue 1 Peter 05#^5-6|6]]:** *Humble yourselves*, [[Micah 6#^6-8|Mic 6.8]]; [[Psalms 022#^22-7|Ps 22.7]]; *m. Avot* 4.4 (“Rabbi Levitas *blasphemed, but on your part he is glorified* c Other ancient authorities lack *exercising the oversight* d Other ancient authorities lack *as God would have you do it* e Or *of those who are older* of Yavneh said, ‘Be very very humble, for the anticipated end of a mortal is worms’”). *Hand of God*, recognition of God’s power should lead to humility. ^5-6

**[[NRSVue 1 Peter 05#^5-8|8]]:** *Discipline yourselves*, lit., “be sober” (1.13; 4.7). *Keep alert*, [[Mark 13#^13-34|Mk 13.34]]; [[1 Thessalonians 05#^5-6|1 Thess 5.6]]. *Roaring lion*, [[Psalms 022#^22-14|Ps 22.13]]. *The devil*, signifying either the non-believing oppressors, or the inner desire to express anger, to sin, or to lose faith (see “Supernatural Beings,” p. 682). ^5-8

**[[NRSVue 1 Peter 05#^5-9|9]]:** *Brothers and sisters in all the world*, this community is not alone in their suffering, although there was no empire-wide persecution of Christians until ca. 250 under the emperor Decius**.** ^5-9

**[[NRSVue 1 Peter 05#^5-12|12]]:** *Silvanus* may have been Peter’s scribe and so editor, or the carrier, of the letter, see Acts 15–16; [[1 Corinthians 15#^15-21|1 Cor 15.21]]; [[Galatians 05#^5-11|Gal 5.11]]; [[Colossians 04#^4-18|Col 4.18]]; [[1 Thessalonians 01#^1-1|1 Thess 1.1]]. ^5-12

**[[NRSVue 1 Peter 05#^5-13|13]]:** *Sister church*, Gk “syneklekte,” lit., “she that was chosen together” or “co-elect”; the term “church” is not used, and some scholars suggest the author is referring to an actual woman (perhaps in charge of a house church). See 1.2. *Babylon*, a symbol for Rome, the capital of the empire (see [[Revelation 14#^14-8|Rev 14.8]]). With this reminder of the site of the exile of the Judeans, the author identifies with Christians whose suffering makes them feel that they are in exile (1.1). *My son Mark*, traditionally understood as the evangelist Mark, thought to be Peter’s disciple (Acts 12–13; 15). For disciple as son, see *m. B. Metz.* 2.11; *Sifre Deut.* 34.3; *Lev. Rab.* 11.7. ^5-13

**[[NRSVue 1 Peter 05#^5-14|14]]:** *Kiss*, [[Romans 16#^16-16|Rom 16.16]]; [[1 Corinthians 16#^16-20|1 Cor 16.20]]; [[2 Corinthians 13#^13-12|2 Cor 13.12]]; [[1 Thessalonians 05#^5-26|1 Thess 5.26]]. *Peace to all*, throughout this letter, the author called readers to create communities of love and peace (1.22; 2.17; 3.8; 4.7), see also [[Ephesians 06#^6-23|Eph 6.23–24]]. ^5-14
