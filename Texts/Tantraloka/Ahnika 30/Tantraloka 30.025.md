## Mūla

अघोर्यादौ सप्तके स्यात् पिवन्याः परिशिष्टकम् ।
प्रत्येकवर्णगोऽप्युक्तः सिद्धयोगीश्वरीमते ॥ ^mula

aghoryādau saptake syāt pivanyāḥ pariśiṣṭakam |
pratyekavarṇago'pyuktaḥ siddhayogīśvarīmate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 02 - Chapter Thirty, part 1#^pg35|p. 17]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=35>))
>
> The parts (pada) (of the Vidyā) pertaining to the seven, Aghorī and the rest, consists, respectively, of five, six, five, (four as are) the Vedas, (two as are) the eyes, (three as are the three) fires, and (two as are) the eyes. The remaining one (of eleven syllables) is that of Pibanī. (with 30.24cd–25ab)
>
> The deposition of the assembly of deities corresponding to each letter (of the Parāparā Vidyā) is also described in the venerable Siddhayogīśvarīmata. It is very long, and so is not written (here). (with 30.25cd–26ab)
