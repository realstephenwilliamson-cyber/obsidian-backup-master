## Chapter 30

**30:1** A psalm of David. A song for the dedication of the House.[^1] ^30-1

**30:2** I extol You, O LORD,  
for You have lifted me up,  
and not let my enemies rejoice over me. ^30-2

**30:3** O LORD, my God,  
I cried out to You,  
and You healed me. ^30-3

**30:4** O LORD, You brought me up from Sheol,  
preserved me from going down into the Pit. ^30-4

**30:5** O you faithful of the LORD, sing to Him,  
and praise His holy name. ^30-5

**30:6** For He is angry but a moment,  
and when He is pleased there is life.  
One may lie down weeping at nightfall;[^2]  
but at dawn there are shouts of joy. ^30-6

**30:7** When I was untroubled,  
I thought, “I shall never be shaken,” ^30-7

**30:8** for You, O LORD, when You were pleased,  
made [me][^3] firm as a mighty mountain.  
When You hid Your face,  
I was terrified. ^30-8

**30:9** I called to You, O LORD;  
to my Lord I made appeal, ^30-9

**30:10** “What is to be gained from my death,[^4]  
from my descent into the Pit?  
Can dust praise You?  
Can it declare Your faithfulness? ^30-10

**30:11** Hear, O LORD, and have mercy on me;  
O LORD, be my help!” ^30-11

**30:12** You turned my lament into dancing,  
you undid my sackcloth and girded me with joy, ^30-12

**30:13** that [my] whole being might sing hymns to You endlessly;  
O LORD my God, I will praise You forever. ^30-13

[^1]: I.e., the Temple.
[^2]: Or “Weeping may linger for the night.”
[^3]: Following Saadia, R. Isaiah of Trani; cf. Ibn Ezra.
[^4]: Lit. “blood.”
