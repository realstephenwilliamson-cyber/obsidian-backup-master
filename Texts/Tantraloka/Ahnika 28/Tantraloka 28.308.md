## Mūla

यथाहि जीवन्मुक्तानां स्थितौ नास्ति विचारणा ।
सुखिदुःखिविमूढत्वे, मृतावपि तथा न सा ॥ ^mula

yathāhi jīvanmuktānāṃ sthitau nāsti vicāraṇā |
sukhiduḥkhivimūḍhatve, mṛtāvapi tathā na sā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 06 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 4#^pg149|p. 132]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=149>))
>
> Just as there is no (such) consideration, with regards to those who are liberated in this life when they are alive, whether they are suffering, happy or deluded, similarly there is none when (they) die also.
