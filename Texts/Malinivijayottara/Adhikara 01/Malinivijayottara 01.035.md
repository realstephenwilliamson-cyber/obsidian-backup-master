## Mūla

इत्यनेन कलाद्येन धरान्तेन समास्थिताः  ।
पुमांसः सकला ज्ञेयास्तदवस्थाजिघांसुभिः ॥ ^mula

ity anena kalādyena dharāntena samāsthitāḥ[^72] |
pumāṁsaḥ sakalā jñeyās tadavasthājighāṁsubhiḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2022 – June 2022
> p. 46 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=22>))

> **The Fully Conditioned (sakala) Perceivers**
>
> The individual souls who possess (the principles) from the Force (of limited agency) (kalā) to Earth should be known to be Fully Conditioned (sakala) by those who desire to bring this condition to an end.[^73]

[^72]: kh: avasthātritaya iti /
