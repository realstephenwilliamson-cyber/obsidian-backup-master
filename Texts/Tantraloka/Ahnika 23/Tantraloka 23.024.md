## Mūla

सर्वोपकरणव्रातमर्पणीयं विपश्चिते ।
सोऽभिषिक्तो गुरुं पश्चाद्दक्षिणाभिः प्रपूजयेत् ॥ ^mula

sarvopakaraṇavrātamarpaṇīyaṃ vipaścite |
so'bhiṣikto guruṃ paścāddakṣiṇābhiḥ prapūjayet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 14 - Chapter Twenty-Three#^pg326|p. 306]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=326>))
>
> In the course of the rite of consecration, (the teacher) should entrust the learned (disciple) with the entire aggregate of ritual implements, such as the (sacrificial) knife and the chalk (khatikā) (to draw the sacred diagrams), and so forth. After that the (newly) consecrated (teacher) should worship (his) teacher by (offering him) the sacrificial fee (dakṣinā). (with 23.23cd–24)

तदुक्तम्--

ङिर्भर्त्स्यैवं विधानेन अभिषेकं प्रदापयेत् ।"

"उष्णीषमुकुटाद्यांश्च छत्रपादुकमासनम् ।

दीक्ष्यानुग्रहमार्गेण दीक्षा व्याख्या त्वया सदा ।

"गुरुं सम्पूजयेच्छिष्यो यथाविभवविस्तरैः ।"
