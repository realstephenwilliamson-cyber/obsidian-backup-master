# Likutei Moharan II 074

*Part II · Lesson 74* · [[Likutei Moharan]]

## 1

After Purim we read the Chapter of Parah, which is a preparation for Pesach. The Chapter of Parah is read so that people will be alerted to purify themselves from the impurity of a corpse, in order to be ritually pure for offering the Pesach sacrifice.

It begins as the concept of *pur* (lottery), for PuRim is named after the *PuR* ([[Esther 09#^9-26|Esther 9:26]]). But afterwards it becomes *PaRah* (cow), because Purim, too, is certainly an approach and path to Pesach.

This is the meaning of “His lips, roses dripping with flowing myrrh” ([[Song of Songs 5#^5-13|Song of Songs 5:13]]). “His lips” is the concept of PeSaCh—*Peh SaCh* (a mouth that speaks) (as is brought). *Shoshanah* (rose) is Esther (as is brought in the holy Zohar and the writings of the Ari, of blessed memory). “Dripping with flowing myrrh” alludes to MoRDeKhaI—*MoR D’ror* ([[Chullin 139#139b|Chulin 139b]]), which connotes freedom, the freedom of Pesach.

Therefore, the letters which spell Purim are hinted at in the verse about Pesach: “seven days you shall eat matzahs as I have commanded you, at the appointed time of the month of the standing grain, for in it you exited from Egypt, and you shall not appear in My presence empty-handed” ([[Torah 18 - Mishpatim#^23-15|Exodus 23:15]]). The first letters of *MeMitzraim V’lo Yeira’u Panai Reikam* (“from Egypt, and you shall not appear in My presence empty-handed”) spell PURYM.
