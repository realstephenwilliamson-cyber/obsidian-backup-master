## Mūla

मन्त्रो हि विश्वरूपः सन्नुपाश्रयवशात्तथा ।
व्यक्तरूपस्ततो वह्नौ पाशप्लोषविधायकः ॥ ^mula

mantro hi viśvarūpaḥ sannupāśrayavaśāttathā |
vyaktarūpastato vahnau pāśaploṣavidhāyakaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 08 - Chapter Seventeen#^pg189|p. 169]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=189>))
>
> Mantra is (in reality) omniform, and being so, its manifest form is such (as it is) due to its underlying ground (upāśraya) (in which it operates), and so (present) within the fire (in that way), it burns away the fetters.
