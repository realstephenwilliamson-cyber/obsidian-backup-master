Fon Saint Gregory’s same oration On the Nativity:

The Logos becomes thick.! {1285C}

When the God-bearing teacher says that the “Logos becomes thick,” I think he does so with the following ideas in mind. Either because the Logos, who is simple and incorporeal, and who spiritually nourishes all the divine powers in heaven according to rank, deemed it worthy to “become thick” through His manifestation in the flesh (which was taken from us, and for us, and is consistent with us, but without sin), so that He might instruct us, by means of words and examples suited to us, in mysteries that transcend the power of all human speech.
