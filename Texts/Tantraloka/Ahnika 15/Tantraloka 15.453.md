## Mūla

पश्यत्येवं शक्तिपातसंस्कृतो मन्त्रसन्निधिम् ।
चक्षुरादीन्द्रियाणां हि सहकारिणि तादृशे ॥ ^mula

paśyatyevaṃ śaktipātasaṃskṛto mantrasannidhim |
cakṣurādīndriyāṇāṃ hi sahakāriṇi tādṛśe || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 08 - Chapter Fifteen, part 7#^pg296|p. 278]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=296>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 15.455cd–456ab.
>
> Just as one who is in love (raktahrdaya) sees by himself (spontaneously each of the many) virtues of the beloved, so does one purified by a descent of the power (of grace) see in this way the presence of Mantras (in the Mandala). (with 15.452cd–453ab)
>
> When such an aid to sight and the other senses is present, they become fit (to perceive) even what was previously totally invisible. (with 15.453cd–454ab)

## Viveka

> चक्षुरादीन्द्रियाणां हि सहकारिणि तादृशे ॥
> सत्यत्यन्तमदृष्टे प्रागपि जायेत योग्यता ।

चक्षुरादीन्द्रियाणां हि तादृशे शक्तिपातलक्षणे सहकारिणि सति प्रागत्यन्तमदृष्टेऽपि अनुभवसंस्काराभावाद्विकल्पस्य ज्ञानस्य अविषयेऽपि मन्त्रादौ दृशिक्रियाकरणायां योग्यता जायेत तथा संभावनीयम्--इत्यर्थः ॥
