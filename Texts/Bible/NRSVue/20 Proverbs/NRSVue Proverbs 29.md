# Proverbs 29 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Proverbs 28|← Proverbs 28]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Proverbs 30|Proverbs 30 →]]

**29:1** One who is often reproved, yet remains stubborn, will suddenly be broken beyond healing. ^29-1

**29:2** When the righteous are in authority, the people rejoice, but when the wicked rule, the people groan. ^29-2

**29:3** A child who loves wisdom makes a parent glad, but a companion of prostitutes destroys wealth. ^29-3

**29:4** By justice a king gives stability to the land, but one who makes heavy exactions ruins it. ^29-4

**29:5** Whoever flatters a neighbor is spreading a net for the neighbor’s feet. ^29-5

**29:6** In the transgression of the evil there is a snare, but the righteous sing and rejoice. ^29-6

**29:7** The righteous know the rights of the poor; the wicked have no such understanding. ^29-7

**29:8** Scoffers set a city aflame, but the wise turn away wrath. ^29-8

**29:9** If the wise go to law with fools, there is ranting and ridicule without relief. ^29-9

**29:10** The bloodthirsty hate the blameless, and they seek the life of the upright. ^29-10

**29:11** A fool gives full vent to anger, but the wise quietly holds it back. ^29-11

**29:12** If a ruler listens to falsehood, all his officials will be wicked. ^29-12

**29:13** The poor and the oppressor have this in common: the LORD gives light to the eyes of both. ^29-13

**29:14** If a king judges the poor with equity, his throne will be established forever. ^29-14

**29:15** The rod and reproof give wisdom, but a mother is disgraced by a neglected child. ^29-15

**29:16** When the wicked are in authority, transgression increases, but the righteous will look upon their downfall. ^29-16

**29:17** Discipline your children, and they will give you rest; they will give delight to your heart. ^29-17

**29:18** Where there is no prophecy, the people cast off restraint, but happy are those who keep the law. ^29-18

**29:19** By mere words slaves are not disciplined, for though they understand, they will not give heed. ^29-19

**29:20** Do you see someone who is hasty in speech? There is more hope for a fool than for anyone like that. ^29-20

**29:21** A slave pampered from childhood will come to a bad end.^[Vg: Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^29-21

**29:22** One given to anger stirs up strife, and the hothead causes much transgression. ^29-22

**29:23** A person’s pride will bring humiliation, but one who is lowly in spirit will obtain honor. ^29-23

**29:24** To be a partner of a thief is to hate one’s own life; one hears the victim’s curse but discloses nothing.^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^29-24

**29:25** The fear of others^[Or *human fear*] lays a snare, but one who trusts in the LORD is secure. ^29-25

**29:26** Many seek the favor of a ruler, but it is from the LORD that one gets justice. ^29-26

**29:27** The unjust are an abomination to the righteous, but the upright are an abomination to the wicked. ^29-27
