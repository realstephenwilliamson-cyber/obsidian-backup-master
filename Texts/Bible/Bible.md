# Bible

The Bible editions in the vault, in `Texts/Bible`: one note per chapter (the Torah one per parashah), with a link target for every verse.

| Edition | What it is | Numbering |
|---|---|---|
| [[Torah]] · [[Rashi (Metsudah)\|Rashi]] | The five books by parashah, with Rashi | Hebrew |
| [[Tanakh]] | JPS 1985 | Hebrew |
| [[LXX]] | Brenton's English Septuagint | Septuagint |
| [[New Testament]] | BSB, with the SBLGNT Greek | English |
| [[Douay-Rheims]] | The Vulgate in English (1899) | Vulgate |
| [[NRSVue]] | NRSV Updated Edition (2021), with the Deuterocanonical/Apocryphal books | English |
| [[Jewish Annotated New Testament]] | Introductions, annotations by chapter and essays (2nd ed., 2017), keyed to the NRSVue | English |

- **Numbering:** [[Psalms numbering]] (Hebrew, Greek and English psalms side by side), [[NRSVue and JPS numbering]] (each Tanakh and Torah chapter has a *Compare* line to the NRSVue) and the JANT's table [[JANT Table - Chapter-Verse Differences|Chapter/verse differences]]. How references in the notes are linked across the numberings: [[Vault Guide#Cross-references|Cross-references]].
- **Greek:** [[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)|Greek NT lexicon]] (one note per word).
- **Pages:** the [[Torah View]] (Torah, Prophets and Writings) and the [[New Testament View]]: a passage laid out like a printed page, with its commentaries and every note that cites it.
- **Gospels side by side:** the [[Synoptic View]], from the table [[Synoptic parallels]].
- **Lectionaries:** [[RCL Index|RCL]], and the Daily Office in [[BCP 1979]].
