> **8. Mahāmudrā (the Great Gesture)**
>
> The hands facing down, the wise man should lead (his) two feet up to the heart and (then) lead them up the top of the face which (should be) tilted. The two feet, raised up, face each other. (This is) called Mahāmudrā (which is used) during the rite of the purification of the body. This (gesture) performs all the rites and serves yogis to accomplish yoga. (with 7.13cd–15ab)
