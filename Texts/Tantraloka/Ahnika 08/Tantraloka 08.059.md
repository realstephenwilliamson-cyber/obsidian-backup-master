## Mūla

मन्दरो गन्धमादश्च विपुलोऽथ सुपार्श्वकः ।
सितपीतनीलरक्तास्ते क्रमात्पादपर्वताः ॥ ^mula

mandaro gandhamādaśca vipulo'tha supārśvakaḥ |
sitapītanīlaraktāste kramātpādaparvatāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 04 - Chapter Eight, part 2#^pg127|p. 109]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=127>))
>
> (Around mount) Meru, (moving in a clockwise direction) from east to north, are the mountains that support it (viṣkambha). (These are,) mount Mandara, Gandhamāda, Vipula and Supārśvaka. White, yellow, blue and red, (respectively), these mountains are at the feet (of Mount Meru). Having fixed (itself into) the earth by means of them, it stands (erect and) immobile. (with 8.58cd–60ab)
