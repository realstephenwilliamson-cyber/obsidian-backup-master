## Mūla

स्मृतियोग्योऽप्यन्यथा वा भोग्यभावं न तूज्झति ।
गाढनिद्राविमूढोऽपि कान्तालिंगितविग्रहः ॥ ^mula

smṛtiyogyo'pyanyathā vā bhogyabhāvaṃ na tūjjhati |
gāḍhanidrāvimūḍho'pi kāntāliṃgitavigrahaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 12 - Chapter Ten, part 3#^pg404|p. 385]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=404>))
>
> Worldly experience (bhoga) is clearly evident or not, according to the type (and degree) of consciousness. Whether it can be recollected or otherwise, it does not give up its condition as an object of experience (bhogyabhāva). (with 10.144cd–145ab)
>
> He who is embraced by his beloved is said to be one who enjoys (her), although he is unconscious and in deep sleep. Indeed, (when he awakes) he also thinks (himself) to have been such before (when he slept). (with 10.145cd–146ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* अत्रैव दृष्टान्तयति --

> गाढनिद्राविमूढोऽपि कान्तालिड्गितविग्रहः ॥
> भोक्तैव भण्यते सोऽपि मनुते भोक्तृतां पुरा ।

भण्यते इति लोकैः । सोऽप्यर्थात्प्रबुद्धः । पुरेति गाढमूढदशायामपि -- इत्यर्थः ॥ १४५ ॥
