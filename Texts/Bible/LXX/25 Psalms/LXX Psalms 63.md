# LXX Psalms 63

← [[LXX Psalms 62]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 64]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 064]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 064]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**63:1** For the end, a Psalm of David.  ^63-1

**63:2** Hear my prayer, O God, when I make my petition to thee; deliver my soul from fear of the enemy.  ^63-2

**63:3** Thou hast sheltered me from the conspiracy of them that do wickedly; from the multitude of them that work iniquity;  ^63-3

**63:4** who have sharpened their tongues as a sword; they have bent their bow maliciously;  ^63-4

**63:5** to shoot in secret at the blameless; they will shoot him suddenly, and will not fear.  ^63-5

**63:6** They have set up for themselves an evil matter, they have given counsel to hide snares; they have said, Who shall see them?  ^63-6

**63:7** They have searched out iniquity; they have wearied themselves with searching diligently, a man shall approach and the heart is deep,  ^63-7

**63:8** and God shall be exalted, their wounds were *caused by* the weapon of the foolish children,  ^63-8

**63:9** and their tongues have set him at nought, all that saw them were troubled;  ^63-9

**63:10** and every man was alarmed, and they related the works of God, and understood his deeds.  ^63-10

**63:11** The righteous shall rejoice in the Lord, and hope on him, and all the upright in heart shall be praised.  ^63-11
