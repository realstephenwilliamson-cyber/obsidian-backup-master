# Ecclesiastes 3 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Ecclesiastes 02|← Ecclesiastes 2]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Ecclesiastes 04|Ecclesiastes 4 →]]

## Everything has its time

**3:1** For everything there is a season and a time for every matter under heaven: ^3-1

**3:2** a time to be born and a time to die; a time to plant and a time to pluck up what is planted; ^3-2

**3:3** a time to kill and a time to heal; a time to break down and a time to build up; ^3-3

**3:4** a time to weep and a time to laugh; a time to mourn and a time to dance; ^3-4

**3:5** a time to throw away stones and a time to gather stones together; a time to embrace and a time to refrain from embracing; ^3-5

**3:6** a time to seek and a time to lose; a time to keep and a time to throw away; ^3-6

**3:7** a time to tear and a time to sew; a time to keep silent and a time to speak; ^3-7

**3:8** a time to love and a time to hate; a time for war and a time for peace. ^3-8

## The God-given task

**3:9** What gain have the workers from their toil? ^3-9

**3:10** I have seen the business that God has given to everyone to be busy with. ^3-10

**3:11** He has made everything suitable for its time; moreover, he has put a sense of past and future into their minds, yet they cannot find out what God has done from the beginning to the end. ^3-11

**3:12** I know that there is nothing better for them than to be happy and enjoy themselves as long as they live; ^3-12

**3:13** moreover, it is God’s gift that all should eat and drink and take pleasure in all their toil. ^3-13

**3:14** I know that whatever God does endures forever; nothing can be added to it nor anything taken from it; God has done this so that all should stand in awe before him. ^3-14

**3:15** That which is already has been, that which is to be already is, and God seeks out what has gone by.^[Heb *what is pursued*] ^3-15

## Judgment and the future belong to God

**3:16** Moreover, I saw under the sun that, in the place of justice, wickedness was there, and in the place of righteousness, wickedness was there as well. ^3-16

**3:17** I said to myself, “God will judge the righteous and the wicked, for he has appointed a time for every matter and for every work.” ^3-17

**3:18** I said to myself with regard to humans that God is testing^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] them to show that they are but animals. ^3-18

**3:19** For the fate of humans and the fate of animals is the same; as one dies, so dies the other. They all have the same breath, and humans have no advantage over the animals, for all is vanity. ^3-19

**3:20** All go to one place, all are from the dust, and all turn to dust again. ^3-20

**3:21** Who knows whether the human spirit goes upward and the spirit of animals goes downward to the earth? ^3-21

**3:22** So I saw that there is nothing better than that all should enjoy their work, for that is their lot; who can bring them to see what will be after them? ^3-22
