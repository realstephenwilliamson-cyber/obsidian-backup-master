# Kedushat Levi (Munk)

*Kedushat Levi*, in Rabbi Eliyahu Munk's translation, following Sefaria's structure: one note per parasha, and one note for each section that is not on a parasha. Sefaria describes it as the "Seminal work of Rabbi Levi Yitzchak of Berditchev arranged according to the weekly Torah portion." Teachings are numbered as on Sefaria; where Sefaria links a teaching to a Torah verse, the verse is linked under the teaching, and scripture references in the text are linked where Sefaria links the same verse to that teaching.

Munk's translation covers the five books of the Torah (815 teachings). 32 more passages, mostly in sections Munk did not translate (the Chanukah and Purim homilies, *Likutim*, *The Rules of Miracles*), are in the Sefaria Community Translation (CC0) and are marked where they appear. Sections with no English on Sefaria: Exodus, Parah; Leviticus, Shir HaShirim; Numbers, Homily for Shavuot; Deuteronomy, For Shabbat Nachamu; Deuteronomy, Haftarah of Nitzavim; Deuteronomy, V'Zot HaBerachah; Likutim on Avot; Haggadah MiSavi De'vey Atuni; Kedushot for Purim, Introduction; Kedushot for Purim, Kedusha Rishona; Kedushot for Purim, Kedusha Shniya; The Rules of Festivals; Chidushei Agadot; Chidushei Halachot; Kedushot for Chanukah, Kedusha Shniya; Kedushot for Chanukah, Kedusha Shlishit; Kedushot for Chanukah, Kedusha Revi'it; Explanations of Aggadot.
