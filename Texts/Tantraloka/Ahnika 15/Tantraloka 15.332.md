## Mūla

कालसङ्कर्षिणी घोरा शान्ता मिश्रा च सर्वतः ।
सिद्धातन्त्रे च सैकार्णा परा देवीति कीर्तिता ॥ ^mula

kālasaṅkarṣiṇī ghorā śāntā miśrā ca sarvataḥ |
siddhātantre ca saikārṇā parā devīti kīrtitā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 06 - Chapter Fifteen, part 5#^pg220|p. 202]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=220>))
>
> The goddess in the middle is (essentially) Mātrsadbhāva, who is Kālasaṁkarṣiṇī. She is fierce, tranquil, and both together. In the Siddhātantra she is called the goddess Parā, who consists of one syllable (SAUH). (with 15.331cd–332)
