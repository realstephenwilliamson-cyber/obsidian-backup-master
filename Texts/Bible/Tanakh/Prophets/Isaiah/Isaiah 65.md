## Chapter 65

**65:1** I responded to[^1] those who did not ask,  
I was at hand to those who did not seek Me;  
I said, “Here I am, here I am,”  
To a nation that did not invoke My name. ^65-1

**65:2** I constantly spread out My hands  
To a disloyal people,  
Who walk the way that is not good,  
Following their own designs; ^65-2

**65:3** The people who provoke My anger,  
Who continually, to My very face,  
Sacrifice in gardens and burn incense on tiles; ^65-3

**65:4** Who sit inside tombs  
And pass the night in secret places;  
Who eat the flesh of swine,  
With broth of unclean things in their bowls; ^65-4

**65:5** Who say, “Keep your distance! Don’t come closer!  
For I would render you consecrated.”[^2]  
Such things make My anger rage,  
Like fire blazing all day long. ^65-5

**65:6** See, this is recorded before Me;  
I will not stand idly by, but will repay,  
Deliver their[^3] sins[^4] into their bosom, ^65-6

**65:7** And the sins of their fathers as well  
—said the LORD—  
For they made offerings upon the mountains  
And affronted Me upon the hills.  
I will count out their recompense in full,[^5]  
Into their bosoms. ^65-7

**65:8** Thus said the LORD:  
As, when new wine is present in the cluster,  
One says, “Don’t destroy it; there’s good in it,”  
So will I do for the sake of My servants,  
And not destroy everything. ^65-8

**65:9** I will bring forth offspring from Jacob,  
From Judah heirs to My mountains;  
My chosen ones shall take possession,  
My servants shall dwell thereon. ^65-9

**65:10** Sharon[^6] shall become a pasture for flocks,  
And the Valley of Achor a place for cattle to lie down,  
For My people who seek Me. ^65-10

**65:11** But as for you who forsake the LORD,  
Who ignore My holy mountain,  
Who set a table for Luck[^7]  
And fill a mixing bowl for Destiny:[^8] ^65-11

**65:12** I will destine you for the sword,  
You will all kneel down, to be slaughtered—  
Because, when I called, you did not answer,  
When I spoke, you would not listen.  
You did what I hold evil,  
And chose what I do not want. ^65-12

**65:13** Assuredly, thus said the Lord GOD:  
My servants shall eat, and you shall hunger;  
My servants shall drink, and you shall thirst;  
My servants shall rejoice, and you shall be shamed; ^65-13

**65:14** My servants shall shout in gladness,  
And you shall cry out in anguish,  
Howling in heartbreak. ^65-14

**65:15** You shall leave behind a name  
By which My chosen ones shall curse:  
“So may the Lord GOD slay you!”  
But His servants shall be given a different name.[^9] ^65-15

**65:16** For whoever blesses himself in the land  
Shall bless himself by the true God;  
And whoever swears in the land  
Shall swear by the true God.  
The former troubles shall be forgotten,  
Shall be hidden from My eyes. ^65-16

**65:17** For behold! I am creating  
A new heaven and a new earth;  
The former things shall not be remembered,  
They shall never come to mind. ^65-17

**65:18** Be glad, then, and rejoice forever  
In what I am creating.  
For I shall create Jerusalem as a joy,  
And her people as a delight; ^65-18

**65:19** And I will rejoice in Jerusalem  
And delight in her people.  
Never again shall be heard there  
The sounds of weeping and wailing. ^65-19

**65:20** No more shall there be an infant or graybeard  
Who does not live out his days.  
He who dies at a hundred years  
Shall be reckoned a youth,  
And he who fails to reach a hundred  
Shall be reckoned accursed. ^65-20

**65:21** They shall build houses and dwell in them,  
They shall plant vineyards and enjoy their fruit. ^65-21

**65:22** They shall not build for others to dwell in,  
Or plant for others to enjoy.  
For the days of My people shall be  
As long as the days of a tree,  
My chosen ones shall outlive[^10]  
The work of their hands. ^65-22

**65:23** They shall not toil to no purpose;  
They shall not bear children for terror,[^11]  
But they shall be a people blessed by the LORD,  
And their offspring shall remain with them. ^65-23

**65:24** Before they pray, I will answer;  
While they are still speaking, I will respond. ^65-24

**65:25** The wolf and the lamb shall graze together,  
And the lion shall eat straw like the ox,  
And the serpent’s food shall be earth.  
In all My sacred mount[^12]  
Nothing evil or vile shall be done  
—said the LORD. ^65-25

[^1]: Lit. “I let Myself be inquired of…”
[^2]: Taking qedashtikha as equivalent to qiddashtikha, cf. Ezek. 44.19; others “For I am holier than thou.”
[^3]: Heb. “your.”
[^4]: Brought up from v. 7 for clarity.
[^5]: Taking rishonah as equivalent to beroshah; cf. Lev. 5.24; Jer. 16.18. Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^6]: Emendation yields “Jeshimon,” the bleak southeast corner of the Jordan Valley; cf. Num. 21.20; 23.8.
[^7]: Names of heathen deities.
[^8]: Names of heathen deities.
[^9]: I.e., a name to be used in blessing.
[^10]: Lit. “wear out.”
[^11]: Emendation yields “in vain.”
[^12]: See note at 11.9.
