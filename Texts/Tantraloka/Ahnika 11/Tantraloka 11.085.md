## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 03 - Chapter Eleven, part 2#^pg94|p. 74]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=94>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 11.85cd–86ab.
>
> If he is not able to include the others in it, he should purify the (progressively more) subtle (forms of it). As (that) is the seed state (of the Path), once that has been purified, it does not give birth to the subsequent series (of binding conditions any longer). (with 11.84cd–85ab)
>
> The process of purification is (essentially one of detachment, which is of) many kinds (that develop in stages). These are: (1) (the individual is initially) one-pointedly attentive (ekatānatā) on acquiring this and that worldly experience. (2) (He then attains) the competence (ādhipatya) for that. (3) (Once he has attained it,) it is abandoned (tyāāga). (4) (This is followed by) the intuition (vedana) that (the individual soul) is Śiva. (5) (The individual, knowing his true identity, is then) immersed (līnatā) in that (worldly experience again), and (6) (then) it is (finally definitively) removed (nirāsa). All this (can take place) both in a fixed sequence or (instantaneously) without. (with 11.85cd–86)
