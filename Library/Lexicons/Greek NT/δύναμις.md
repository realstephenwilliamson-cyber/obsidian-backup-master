# δύναμις

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · noun

**Dodson:** might, power, marvelous works — (a) physical power, force, might, ability, efficacy, energy, meaning (b) plur: powerful deeds, deeds showing (physical) power, marvelous works. (Strong's 1411; headword δύναμις, εως, ἡ)
