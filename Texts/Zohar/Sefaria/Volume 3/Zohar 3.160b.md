# Zohar 3.160b

← [[Zohar 3.160a]] · [[Zohar (Soncino)]] · [[Zohar 3.161a]] →

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 9.03 - Parashat Shelah Lekha#^z3-160b|Parashat Shelaḥ Lekha, at 3:160b]]

*Volume 3, folio 160b* · Sh'lach

*Passage Sh'lach 10:67 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 3.160a#^sh-lach-10-67|Sh'lach 10:67]].*

**Sh'lach 10:68** “Neither fear ye the people of the land”: for if a broken body will study the Torah, it will find healing therein. “For they are bread for us”: those accusers themselves provide every day food for those who study the Torah.’ ^sh-lach-10-68

**Sh'lach 10:69** AND THEY CAME UNTO THE VALLEY OF ESHCOL. R. Abba said: ‘They cut off the cluster, and when they came to raise it they were not able, nor even to move it, but Caleb and Joshua came and lifted it. While joined to the tree it was called a “branch”, but afterwards a “staff’. ^sh-lach-10-69

**Sh'lach 10:70** Through this Joshua and Caleb knew that they were destined to enter into the land and obtain a portion in it. On the way the others plotted against them, so Caleb addressed the branch, saying: Fruit, fruit, if for thee we are to be killed, why should we have thee? Straightway it lightened itself and they gave it to them.’ ^sh-lach-10-70

**Sh'lach 10:71** R. Eleazar said: ‘They did not give it to any other, but when they came back to the Israelites they gave it to them, and withdrew into the background.’ ^sh-lach-10-71

**Sh'lach 10:72** R. Isaac said: ‘When they came into the presence of the Anakim they displayed before them the staff of Moses and so were delivered.’ R. Judah said: ‘We have a tradition that Moses transmitted to them the Holy Name, and through this they were delivered.’ ^sh-lach-10-72

**Sh'lach 10:73** R. Hiya said: ‘The giants were called by three names: *Neflim, Anakim, Refaim*. Their original name was Nefilim, and when they associated with the daughters of men they were called Anakim, and then when they went about the world and neglected the Heavenly One they were called Refaim. ^sh-lach-10-73

**Sh'lach 10:74** They lived to a great age until at last half their body became paralysed while the other half remained vigorous. They would then take a certain herb and throw it into their mouths and die, and because they thus killed themselves they were called Refaim.’ R. Isaac said that they used to drown themselves in the sea, as it is written, “The Refaim are slain beneath the waters” (Job 26, 5). ^sh-lach-10-74

**Sh'lach 10:75** R. Simeon said: ‘Had Israel entered the land under the sign of the evil tongue, they would not have endured an instant. ^sh-lach-10-75
