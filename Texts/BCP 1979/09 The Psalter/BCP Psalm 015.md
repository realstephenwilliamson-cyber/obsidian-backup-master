# Psalm 15

← [[BCP Psalm 014]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 016]] →

*Domine, quis habitabit?* · Book One · Third Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 015]]

**15:1** LORD, who may dwell in your tabernacle? \*  
who may abide upon your holy hill? ^15-1

**15:2** Whoever leads a blameless life and does what is right, \*  
who speaks the truth from his heart. ^15-2

**15:3** There is no guile upon his tongue;  
he does no evil to his friend; \*  
he does not heap contempt upon his neighbor. ^15-3

**15:4** In his sight the wicked is rejected, \*  
but he honors those who fear the LORD. ^15-4

**15:5** He has sworn to do no wrong \*  
and does not take back his word. ^15-5

**15:6** He does not give his money in hope of gain, \*  
nor does he take a bribe against the innocent. ^15-6

**15:7** Whoever does these things \*  
shall never be overthrown. ^15-7
