## Mūla

जिज्ञासुस्तत एवेदमवधारयितुं क्षमः ।
वयं तूक्तानुवचनमफलं नाद्रियामहे ॥ ^mula

jijñāsustata evedamavadhārayituṃ kṣamaḥ |
vayaṃ tūktānuvacanamaphalaṃ nādriyāmahe || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 11 - Chapter Thirty-Seven, part 1#^pg257|p. 239]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=257>))
>
> I have discussed the nature of these (five) currents (of scripture), as beginning from the immovable (dhruva) plane of Anuttara, in detail in the Mālinīślokavārtika. He who wants to know (about it), can understand it from there. We do not sanction fruitless repetition of what has (already) been said. (with 37.30–31)
