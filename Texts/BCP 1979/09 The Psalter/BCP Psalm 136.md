# Psalm 136

← [[BCP Psalm 135]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 137]] →

*Confitemini* · Book Five · Twenty-eighth Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 136]]

**136:1** Give thanks to the LORD, for he is good, \*  
for his mercy endures for ever. ^136-1

**136:2** Give thanks to the God of gods, \*  
for his mercy endures for ever. ^136-2

[p. 790] ^pg790

**136:3** Give thanks to the Lord of lords, \*  
for his mercy endures for ever. ^136-3

**136:4** Who only does great wonders, \*  
for his mercy endures for ever; ^136-4

**136:5** Who by wisdom made the heavens, \*  
for his mercy endures for ever; ^136-5

**136:6** Who spread out the earth upon the waters, \*  
for his mercy endures for ever; ^136-6

**136:7** Who created great lights, \*  
for his mercy endures for ever; ^136-7

**136:8** The sun to rule the day, \*  
for his mercy endures for ever; ^136-8

**136:9** The moon and the stars to govern the night, \*  
for his mercy endures for ever. ^136-9

**136:10** Who struck down the firstborn of Egypt, \*  
for his mercy endures for ever; ^136-10

**136:11** And brought out Israel from among them, \*  
for his mercy endures for ever; ^136-11

**136:12** With a mighty hand and a stretched-out arm, \*  
for his mercy endures for ever; ^136-12

**136:13** Who divided the Red Sea in two, \*  
for his mercy endures for ever; ^136-13

**136:14** And made Israel to pass through the midst of it, \*  
for his mercy endures for ever; ^136-14

**136:15** But swept Pharaoh and his army into the Red Sea, \*  
for his mercy endures for ever; ^136-15

[p. 791] ^pg791

**136:16** Who led his people through the wilderness, \*  
for his mercy endures for ever. ^136-16

**136:17** Who struck down great kings, \*  
for his mercy endures for ever; ^136-17

**136:18** And slew mighty kings, \*  
for his mercy endures for ever; ^136-18

**136:19** Sihon, king of the Amorites, \*  
for his mercy endures for ever; ^136-19

**136:20** And Og, the king of Bashan, \*  
for his mercy endures for ever; ^136-20

**136:21** And gave away their lands for an inheritance, \*  
for his mercy endures for ever; ^136-21

**136:22** An inheritance for Israel his servant, \*  
for his mercy endures for ever. ^136-22

**136:23** Who remembered us in our low estate, \*  
for his mercy endures for ever; ^136-23

**136:24** And delivered us from our enemies, \*  
for his mercy endures for ever; ^136-24

**136:25** Who gives food to all creatures, \*  
for his mercy endures for ever. ^136-25

**136:26** Give thanks to the God of heaven, \*  
for his mercy endures for ever. ^136-26
