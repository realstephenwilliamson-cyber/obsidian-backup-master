## Mūla

साध्यो दाता दमनो ध्यानो भस्मेति बिन्दवः पञ्च ।
पञ्चार्थगुह्यरुद्राङ्कुशहृदयलक्षणं च सव्यूहम् ॥ ^mula

sādhyo dātā damano dhyāno bhasmeti bindavaḥ pañca |
pañcārthaguhyarudrāṅkuśahṛdayalakṣaṇaṃ ca savyūham || ^iast

## Translation

> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 8.328cd–329ab.
>
> The five Bindus are: Sādhya (Goal), Dhātr (Benefactor), Damaneśa (Lord of the Garland), Dhyāna (Meditation) and Bhasma (Ash). The group of eight (Pāśupata) scriptures (pramāna) (consists of): Pañcārtha, Guhya, Rudrāṇkuśa, Hrdaya, Lakṣaṇa, Vyūha, Ākarṣa and Ādarśa. (with 8.328–329ab)
