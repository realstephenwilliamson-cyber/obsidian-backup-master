# Sirach 8 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Sirach 07|← Sirach 7]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Sirach 09|Sirach 9 →]]

## Prudence and common sense

**8:1** Do not contend with the powerful, or you may fall into their hands. ^8-1

**8:2** Do not quarrel with the rich, in case their resources outweigh yours, for gold has ruined many and has perverted the minds of kings. ^8-2

**8:3** Do not argue with the loud of mouth, and do not heap wood on their fire. ^8-3

**8:4** Do not make fun of one who is uneducated, or your ancestors may be insulted. ^8-4

**8:5** Do not reproach one who is turning away from sin; remember that we all deserve punishment. ^8-5

**8:6** Do not disdain one who is old, for some of us are also growing old. ^8-6

**8:7** Do not rejoice over anyone’s death; remember that we must all die. ^8-7

**8:8** Do not slight the discourse of the sages, but turn to their maxims, because from them you will learn discipline and how to serve the great. ^8-8

**8:9** Do not ignore the discourse of the aged, for they themselves learned from their parents;^[Or *ancestors*] from them you learn how to understand and to give an answer when the need arises. ^8-9

**8:10** Do not kindle the coals of sinners, or you may be burned^[Heb: Gk *do not get burned*] in their flaming fire. ^8-10

**8:11** Do not let the insolent bring you to your feet, or they may lie in ambush against your words. ^8-11

**8:12** Do not lend to one who is stronger than you, but if you do lend anything, count it as a loss. ^8-12

**8:13** Do not give surety beyond your means, but if you give surety be prepared to pay. ^8-13

**8:14** Do not go to law against a judge, for the decision will favor him because of his standing. ^8-14

**8:15** Do not travel on the road with the reckless, or your troubles may become burdensome, for they will act as they please, and through their folly you will perish with them. ^8-15

**8:16** Do not pick a fight with the quick-tempered, and do not journey with them through a deserted region, because bloodshed means nothing to them, and where no help is at hand they will strike you down. ^8-16

**8:17** Do not consult with fools, for they cannot keep a secret. ^8-17

**8:18** In the presence of strangers do nothing that is to be kept secret, for you do not know what they will divulge.^[Or *it will bring forth*] ^8-18

**8:19** Do not reveal your thoughts to anyone, or you may drive away your happiness.^[Heb: Gk *and let him not return a favor to you*] ^8-19
