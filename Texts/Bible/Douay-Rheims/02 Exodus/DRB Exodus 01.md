# Exodus 1 (Douay-Rheims)

— · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Exodus 02|Exodus 2 →]]

**1:1** These are the names of the children of Israel, that went into Egypt with Jacob: they went in, every man with his household: ^1-1

**1:2** Ruben, Simeon, Levi, Juda, ^1-2

**1:3** Issachar, Zabulon, and Benjamin, ^1-3

**1:4** Dan, and Nephtali, Gad and Aser. ^1-4

**1:5** And all the souls that came out of Jacob’s thigh, were seventy: but Joseph was in Egypt. ^1-5

**1:6** After he was dead, and all his brethren, and all that generation, ^1-6

**1:7** The children of Israel increased, and sprung up into multitudes, and growing exceedingly strong they filled the land. ^1-7

**1:8** In the mean time there arose a new king over Egypt, that knew not Joseph: ^1-8

**1:9** And he said to his people: Behold the people of the children of Israel are numerous and stronger than we. ^1-9

**1:10** Come, let us wisely oppress them, lest they multiply: and if any war shall rise against us, join with our enemies, and having overcome us, depart out of the land. ^1-10

**1:11** Therefore he set over them masters of the works, to afflict them with burdens, and they built for Pharao cities of tabernacles, Phithom and Ramesses. ^1-11

**1:12** But the more they oppressed them, the more they were multiplied, and increased: ^1-12

**1:13** And the Egyptians hated the children of Israel, and afflicted them and mocked them: ^1-13

**1:14** And they made their life bitter with hard works in clay, and brick, and with all manner of service, wherewith they were overcharged in the works of the earth. ^1-14

**1:15** And the king of Egypt spoke to the midwives of the Hebrews: of whom one was called Sephora, the other Phua, ^1-15

**1:16** Commanding them: When you shall do the office of midwives to the Hebrew women, and the time of delivery is come: if it be a man child, kill it: if a woman, keep it alive. ^1-16

**1:17** But the midwives feared God, and did not do as the king of Egypt had commanded, but saved the men children. ^1-17

**1:18** And the king called for them and said: What is that you meant to do, that you would save the men children? ^1-18

**1:19** They answered: The Hebrew women are not as the Egyptian women: for they themselves are skillful in the office of a midwife; and they are delivered before we come to them. ^1-19

**1:20** Therefore God dealt well with the midwives: and the people multiplied and grew exceedingly strong. ^1-20

**1:21** And because the midwives feared God, he built them houses. ^1-21

**1:22** Pharao therefore charged all his people, saying: Whatsoever shall be born of the male sex, ye shall cast into the river: whatsoever of the female, ye shall save alive. ^1-22
