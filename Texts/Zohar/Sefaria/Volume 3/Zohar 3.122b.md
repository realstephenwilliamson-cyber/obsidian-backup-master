# Zohar 3.122b

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 8.08 - Parashat Naso#^z3-122b|Parashat Naso, at 3:122b]]

*Volume 3, folio 122b* · Nasso

*Passage Nasso 4:24 begins on the previous side: [[Zohar 3.122a#^nasso-4-24|Nasso 4:24]].*

**Nasso 4:25** For this reason, the Holy One, blessed be He, although he has sent Rigour into the world, desires that Israel should repent so that He may do good to them in this world and in the world to come. For nothing can withstand the power of repentance. ^nasso-4-25

**Nasso 4:26** Proper repentance is only effected by a surrender of one’s soul, so that she is taken away in a state of repentance. ^nasso-4-26

**Nasso 4:27** One sinner in the world brings about the destruction of many. Woe to the sinner, woe to his neighbour! We see this in the case of Jonah. Through his refusing to carry the message of his Master, how many people would have been destroyed on his account in the sea! So they all turned on him and carried out on him the sentence of sea-drowning, whereby they were all saved. The Holy One, blessed be He, however, had mercy on him and so brought about the deliverance of multitudes of people. This happened after Jonah returned to his Master out of the midst of his affliction, as we read: “I called out of my affliction unto the Lord, and he answered me” (Jonah 2, 3).’ ^nasso-4-27

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Nasso 4:31–4:38.*
