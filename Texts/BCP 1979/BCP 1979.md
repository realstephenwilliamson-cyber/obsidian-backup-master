# BCP 1979

The Book of Common Prayer (1979) of the Episcopal Church, one note per service or section in the order of the book, and one note per psalm. `[p. N]` marks where printed page N begins, and every page has an alias (“BCP 358”) for search and link autocompletion. Collects, numbered prayers and thanksgivings, catechism questions, Articles of Religion and psalm verses carry block IDs.
