## Mūla

षष्ठे गरुत्मानन्यस्मिङ्गङ्गान्यत्र वृषो विभुः ।
दक्षस्तु नवमे ब्रह्मशक्त्या समधिति[नि]ष्ठितः ॥ ^mula

ṣaṣṭhe garutmānanyasmiṅgaṅgānyatra vṛṣo vibhuḥ |
dakṣastu navame brahmaśaktyā samadhiti[ni]ṣṭhitaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 06 - Chapter Eight, part 4#^pg193|p. 175]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=193>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 8.141cd–142ab.
>
> 2) In the second (path of the winds) reside the Mothers and the daughters of fire, presided over by Rudra’s power. 3) Beyond that are the Siddhacaraṇa, born (in this state) by virtue of their deeds. 4) In the fourth are the eight gods of the divine weapons. 5) In the fifth are the elephants of the quarters. 6) Again, in the sixth, is Garuda. 7) In the next one is the (celestial) Ganges. 8) (Nandin, Śiva’s divine mount), the bull is elsewhere (in the eighth). 9) Dakṣa, rightly presided by Brahmā’s power, is in the ninth, and 10) the Vasus, Rudras and lidityas are in the tenth. (with 8.139cd–142ab)
