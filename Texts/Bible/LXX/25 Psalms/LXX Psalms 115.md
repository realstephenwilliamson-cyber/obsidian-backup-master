# LXX Psalms 115

← [[LXX Psalms 114]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 116]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 116]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 116]] (Heb 116:10–19 = LXX 115) · [[Psalms numbering]]

**115:1** Alleluia. I believed, wherefore I have spoken: but I was greatly afflicted.  ^115-1

**115:2** And I said in mine amazement, Every man is a liar.  ^115-2

**115:3** What shall I render to the Lord for all the things wherein he has rewarded me?  ^115-3

**115:4** I will take the cup of salvation, and call upon the name of the Lord. 4a I will pay my vows to the Lord, in the presence of all his people.  ^115-4

**115:6** Precious in the sight of the Lord is the death of his saints.  ^115-6

**115:7** O Lord, I am thy servant; I am thy servant, and the son of thine handmaid: thou hast burst my bonds asunder.  ^115-7

**115:8** I will offer to thee the sacrifice of praise, and will call upon the name of the Lord.  ^115-8

**115:9** I will pay my vows unto the Lord, in the presence of all his people,  ^115-9

**115:10** in the courts of the Lord's house, in the midst of thee, Jerusalem.  ^115-10
