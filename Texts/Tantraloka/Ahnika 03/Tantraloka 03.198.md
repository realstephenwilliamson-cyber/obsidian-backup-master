## Mūla

एकामर्शस्वभावत्वे शब्दराशिः स भैरवः ।
आमृश्यच्छायया योगात्सैव शक्तिश्च मातृका ॥ ^mula

ekāmarśasvabhāvatve śabdarāśiḥ sa bhairavaḥ |

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. II
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 02 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v02 10 - Chapter Three, part 7#^pg331|p. 315]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 02.pdf#page=331>))
>
> Bhairava Who is the Assembly of Sounds (śabdarāśi), (abides) within (the ‘T’ consciousness which is) the state of the essential nature (svabhāvatva) of the one reflective awareness (of pure ‘I’ consciousness). And (His) power, that is, Mātrkā, is (the state which arises) by (His) association with (just) the shade (that is, a subtle trace,) of the object of awareness. This (power) becomes Mālinī, in which the (seed vowels) are mixed in with the matrices (of the consonants), by coupling with the Assembly of Sounds. (Then again) she (assumes) the nature (of the nine) individual classes of letters by the application, as before, of a nine-fold act of reflective awareness. When a reflective awareness of each individual (letter finally) affirms itself, this same (power assumes) the nature of the fifty (forms of reflective awareness). (with 3.198–200ab)
