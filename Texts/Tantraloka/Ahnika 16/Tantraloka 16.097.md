## Mūla

शोध्याध्वनि च विन्यस्ते तत्रैव परिशोधकम् ।
न्यसेद्यथेप्सितं मन्त्रं शोध्यौचित्यानुसारतः ॥ ^mula

śodhyādhvani ca vinyaste tatraiva pariśodhakam |
nyasedyathepsitaṃ mantraṃ śodhyaucityānusārataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 05 - Chapter Sixteen, part 1#^pg65|p. 45]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=65>))
>
> Once the Path that is to be purified (śodhya) has been deposited (on the disciple’s body) according to what is (most) appropriate for it, the preferred Mantra that purifies it should be deposited there (in the same place). (If) in some cases (the Path) to be purified has not been deposited, then just by depositing the purifying (Mantra alone) it is purified by itself, by virtue of the (Mantra’s) purifying power. (with 16.97–98)
