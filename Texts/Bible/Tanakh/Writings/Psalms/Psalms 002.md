## Chapter 2

**2:1** Why do nations assemble,  
and peoples plot[^1] vain things; ^2-1

**2:2** kings of the earth take their stand,  
and regents intrigue together  
against the LORD and against His anointed? ^2-2

**2:3** “Let us break the cords of their yoke,  
shake off their ropes from us!” ^2-3

**2:4** He who is enthroned in heaven laughs;  
the Lord mocks at them. ^2-4

**2:5** Then He speaks to them in anger,  
terrifying them in His rage, ^2-5

**2:6** “But I have installed My king  
on Zion, My holy mountain!” ^2-6

**2:7** Let me tell of the decree:  
the LORD said to me,  
“You are My son,  
I have fathered you this day.[^2] ^2-7

**2:8** Ask it of Me,  
and I will make the nations your domain;  
your estate, the limits of the earth. ^2-8

**2:9** You can smash them with an iron mace,  
shatter them like potter’s ware.” ^2-9

**2:10** So now, O kings, be prudent;  
accept discipline, you rulers of the earth! ^2-10

**2:11** Serve the LORD in awe;  
tremble with fright,[^3] ^2-11

**2:12** pay homage in good faith,[^4]  
lest He be angered, and your way be doomed  
in the mere flash of His anger.  
Happy are all who take refuge in Him. ^2-12

[^1]: Lit. “utter.”
[^2]: Compare 2 Sam. 7.14, and Ps. 89.27 ff.
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain; others “rejoice with trembling.”
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
