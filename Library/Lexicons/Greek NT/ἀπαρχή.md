# ἀπαρχή

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · noun

**Dodson:** the first-fruits — the first-fruits, the earliest crop of the year, hence also met., for example, of the earliest converts in a district; there is evidence in favor of rendering in some passages merely by: sacrifice, gift. (Strong's 536; headword ἀπαρχή, ῆς, ἡ)
