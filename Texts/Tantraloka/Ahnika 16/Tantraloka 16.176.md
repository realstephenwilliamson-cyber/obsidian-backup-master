## Mūla

तथाहि वक्त्रैर्यस्याध्वा शुद्धस्तैरेव योजितः ।
भोक्तुमिष्टे क्वचित्तत्त्वे स भोक्ता तद्बलान्वितः ॥ ^mula

tathāhi vaktrairyasyādhvā śuddhastaireva yojitaḥ |
bhoktumiṣṭe kvacittattve sa bhoktā tadbalānvitaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 06 - Chapter Sixteen, part 2#^pg98|p. 78]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=98>))
>
> It is just in this way that the one who experiences worldly experience (bhoktṛ), whose Path has been purified by the (Mantra’s) faces and is conjoined by them somewhere to the principle (tattva) of (his) choice in order to have (worldly) experience, possesses its power (which is its essence or nature).
