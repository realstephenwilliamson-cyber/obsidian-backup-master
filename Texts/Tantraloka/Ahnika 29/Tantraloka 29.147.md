## Mūla

सूर्यसोमौ तु संरुध्य लयविक्षेपमार्गतः ।
एवं त्रिविधविमर्शावेशसमापत्तिधाम्नि य उदेति ॥ ^mula

sūryasomau tu saṃrudhya layavikṣepamārgataḥ |
evaṃ trividhavimarśāveśasamāpattidhāmni ya udeti || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 12 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 4#^pg346|p. 329]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=346>))
>
> He should practice the consciousness of Being (bhāvasarivitti) by turning away from all (these) states of being (bhāva), having restrained the Sun and Moon from the paths of merger and projection (vikṣepa). (with 29.146cd–147ab)
>
> The Resonance (dhvani), which is the reflective awareness of consciousness, that arises in this way (by practicing the consciousness of Being) in the abode of the contemplative attainment which is penetration (āveśasamāpatti) into the three-fold reflective awareness (of emission), is here (according to us,) the vitality of Mantra. He indeed knows the arising of Mantra (mantrodaya) if, eager to obtain such fruit that arises there, constantly contemplates his own mantra. (with 29.147cd–149ab)
