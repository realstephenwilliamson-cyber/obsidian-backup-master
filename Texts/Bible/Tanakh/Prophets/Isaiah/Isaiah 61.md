## Chapter 61

**61:1** The spirit of the Lord GOD is upon me,  
Because the LORD has anointed me;  
He has sent me as a herald of joy to the humble,  
To bind up the wounded of heart,  
To proclaim release to the captives,  
Liberation to the imprisoned; ^61-1

**61:2** To proclaim a year of the LORD’s favor  
And a day of vindication by our God;  
To comfort all who mourn— ^61-2

**61:3** To provide for[^1] the mourners in Zion—  
To give them a turban instead of ashes,  
The festive ointment instead of mourning,  
A garment of splendor instead of a drooping spirit.  
They shall be called terebinths of victory,  
Planted by the LORD for His glory. ^61-3

**61:4** And they shall build the ancient ruins,  
Raise up the desolations of old,  
And renew the ruined cities,  
The desolations of many ages. ^61-4

**61:5** Strangers shall stand and pasture your flocks,  
Aliens shall be your plowmen and vine-trimmers; ^61-5

**61:6** While you shall be called “Priests of the LORD,”  
And termed “Servants of our God.”  
You shall enjoy the wealth of nations  
And revel[^2] in their riches. ^61-6

**61:7** Because your shame was double—  
Men cried, “Disgrace is their portion”[^3]—  
Assuredly,  
They shall have a double share in their land,  
Joy shall be theirs for all time. ^61-7

**61:8** For I the LORD love justice,  
I hate robbery with a burnt offering.[^4]  
I will pay them their wages faithfully,  
And make a covenant with them for all time. ^61-8

**61:9** Their offspring shall be known among the nations,  
Their descendants in the midst of the peoples.  
All who see them shall recognize  
That they are a stock the LORD has blessed. ^61-9

**61:10** I greatly rejoice in the LORD,  
My whole being exults in my God.  
For He has clothed me with garments of triumph,  
Wrapped me in a robe of victory,  
Like a bridegroom adorned with a turban,  
Like a bride bedecked with her finery. ^61-10

**61:11** For as the earth brings forth her growth  
And a garden makes the seed shoot up,  
So the Lord GOD will make  
Victory and renown shoot up  
In the presence of all the nations. ^61-11

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Emendation yields “They inherited disgrace as their portion.”
[^4]: Emendation yields “the robbing of wages.”
