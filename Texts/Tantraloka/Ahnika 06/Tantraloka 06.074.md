## Mūla

ते चोक्ताः परमेशेन श्रीमद्वीरावलीकुले ।
सितासितौ दीर्घह्रस्वौ धर्माधर्मौ दिनक्षपे ॥ ^mula

te coktāḥ parameśena śrīmadvīrāvalīkule |
sitāsitau dīrghahrasvau dharmādharmau dinakṣape || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 10 - Chapter Six, part 3#^pg311|p. 296]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=311>))
>
> Liberation (is attained) by pervasion, meditation and yoga when Night and Day end (at sunset and dawn). The Lord has taught them in the venerable Virāvalīkula. (with 6.73cd–74ab)
>
> If (the pair of) bright and dark (lunar fortnights) (sitāsitau), long and short, righteousness (dharma) and unrighteousness (adharma), day and night, were to cease, that initiation (takes place by the experience of) pervasion, meditation and yoga. Day and Night in the movement of the breath has (thus) been taught. The month will be explained (next). (with 6.74cd–75)
