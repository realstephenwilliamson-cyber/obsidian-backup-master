# LXX Psalms 36

← [[LXX Psalms 35]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 37]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 037]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 037]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**36:1** *A Psalm* of David. Fret not thyself because of evil-doers, neither be envious of them that do iniquity.  ^36-1

**36:2** For they shall soon be withered as the grass, and shall soon fall away as the green herbs.  ^36-2

**36:3** Hope in the Lord, and do good; and dwell on the land, and thou shalt be fed with the wealth of it.  ^36-3

**36:4** Delight *thyself* in the Lord; and he shall grant thee the requests of thine heart.  ^36-4

**36:5** Disclose thy way to the Lord, and hope in him; and he shall bring *it* to pass.  ^36-5

**36:6** And he shall bring forth thy righteousness as the light, and thy judgment as the noon-day.  ^36-6

**36:7** Submit thyself to the Lord, and supplicate him: fret not thyself because of him that prospers in his way, at the man that does unlawful deeds.  ^36-7

**36:8** Cease from anger, and forsake wrath: fret not thyself so as to do evil.  ^36-8

**36:9** For evil-doers shall be destroyed: but they that wait on the Lord, they shall inherit the land.  ^36-9

**36:10** And yet a little while, and the sinner shall not be, and thou shalt seek for his place, and shalt not find *it*.  ^36-10

**36:11** But the meek shall inherit the earth; and shall delight *themselves* in the abundance of peace.  ^36-11

**36:12** The sinner will watch for the righteous, and gnash his teeth upon him.  ^36-12

**36:13** But the Lord shall laugh at him: for he foresees that his day will come.  ^36-13

**36:14** Sinners have drawn their swords, they have bent their bow, to cast down the poor and needy one, *and* to slay the upright in heart.  ^36-14

**36:15** Let their sword enter into their *own* heart, and their bows be broken.  ^36-15

**36:16** A little is better to the righteous than abundant wealth of sinners.  ^36-16

**36:17** For the arms of sinners shall be broken; but the Lord supports the righteous.  ^36-17

**36:18** The Lord knows the ways of the perfect; and their inheritance shall be for ever.  ^36-18

**36:19** They shall not be ashamed in an evil time; and in days of famine they shall be satisfied.  ^36-19

**36:20** For the sinners shall perish; and the enemies of the Lord at the moment of their being honoured and exalted have utterly vanished like smoke.  ^36-20

**36:21** The sinner borrows, and will not pay again: but the righteous has compassion, and gives.  ^36-21

**36:22** For they that bless him shall inherit the earth; and they that curse him shall be utterly destroyed.  ^36-22

**36:23** The steps of a man are rightly ordered by the Lord: and he will take pleasure in his way.  ^36-23

**36:24** When he falls, he shall not be ruined: for the Lord supports his hand.  ^36-24

**36:25** I was *once* young, indeed I am now old; yet I have not seen the righteous forsaken, nor his seed seeking bread.  ^36-25

**36:26** He is merciful, and lends continually; and his seed shall be blessed.[^1]  ^36-26

**36:27** Turn aside from evil, and do good; and dwell for ever.  ^36-27

**36:28** For the Lord loves judgment, and will not forsake his saints; they shall be preserved for ever: the blameless shall be avenged, but the seed of the ungodly shall be utterly destroyed.[^2]  ^36-28

**36:29** But the righteous shall inherit the earth, and dwell upon it for ever.  ^36-29

**36:30** The mouth of the righteous will meditate wisdom, and his tongue will speak of judgment.  ^36-30

**36:31** The law of his God is in his heart; and his steps shall not slide.[^3]  ^36-31

**36:32** The sinner watches the righteous, and seeks to slay him.  ^36-32

**36:33** But the Lord will not leave him in his hands, nor by any means condemn him when he is judged.  ^36-33

**36:34** Wait on the Lord, and keep his way, and he shall exalt thee to inherit the land: when the wicked are destroyed, thou shalt see *it*.  ^36-34

**36:35** I saw the ungodly very highly exalting himself, and lifting himself up like the cedars of Libanus.  ^36-35

**36:36** Yet I passed by, and lo! he was not: and I sought him, but his place was not found.  ^36-36

**36:37** Maintain innocence, and behold uprightness: for there is a remnant to the peaceable man.  ^36-37

**36:38** But the transgressors shall be utterly destroyed together: the remnants of the ungodly shall be utterly destroyed.  ^36-38

**36:39** But the salvation of the righteous is of the Lord; and he is their defender in the time of affliction.  ^36-39

**36:40** And the Lord shall help them, and deliver them: and he shall rescue them from sinners, and save them, because they have hoped in him.  ^36-40

[^1]: 36:26 *Gr.* for a blessing.
[^2]: 36:28 *Or,* cleared in judgement.
[^3]: 36:31 *Gr.* be tripped up, or supplanted.
