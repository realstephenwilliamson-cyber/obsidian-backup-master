# Psalms 69 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 68|← Psalms 68]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 70|Psalms 70 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 69|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**69:1** Unto the end, a psalm for David, to bring to remembrance that the Lord saved him. ^69-1

**69:2** O God, come to my assistance; O Lord, make haste to help me. ^69-2

**69:3** Let them be confounded and ashamed that seek my soul: ^69-3

**69:4** Let them be turned backward, and blush for shame that desire evils to me: Let them be presently turned away blushing for shame that say to me: Tis well, tis well. ^69-4

**69:5** Let all that seek thee rejoice and be glad in thee; and let such as love thy salvation say always: The Lord be magnified. ^69-5

**69:6** But I am needy and poor; O God, help me. Thou art my helper and my deliverer: O Lord, make no delay. ^69-6
