# LXX Psalms 19

← [[LXX Psalms 18]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 20]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 020]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 020]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**19:1** For the end, a Psalm of David.  ^19-1

**19:2** The Lord hear thee in the day of trouble; the name of the God of Jacob defend thee.  ^19-2

**19:3** Send thee help from the sanctuary, and aid thee out of Sion.  ^19-3

**19:4** Remember all thy sacrifice, and enrich thy whole-burnt-offering. Pause.[^1]  ^19-4

**19:5** Grant thee according to thy heart, and fulfil all thy desire.  ^19-5

**19:6** We will exult in thy salvation, and in the name of our God shall we be magnified: the Lord fulfil all thy petitions.  ^19-6

**19:7** Now I know that the Lord has saved his Christ: he shall hear him from his holy heaven: the salvation of his right hand is mighty.  ^19-7

**19:8** Some *glory* in chariots, and some in horses: but we will glory in the name of the Lord our God.[^2]  ^19-8

**19:9** They are overthrown and fallen: but we are risen, and have been set upright.  ^19-9

**19:10** O Lord, save the king: and hear us in whatever day we call upon thee.  ^19-10

[^1]: 19:4 *Or,* meat offering.
[^2]: 19:8 *Gr.* be magnified.
