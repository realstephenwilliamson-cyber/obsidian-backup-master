# Proverbs 30 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Proverbs 29|← Proverbs 29]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Proverbs 31|Proverbs 31 →]]

## Sayings of Agur

**30:1** The words of Agur son of Jakeh. An oracle. Thus says the man: I am weary, O God; I am weary, O God, and am wasting away.^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^30-1

**30:2** Surely I am too stupid to be human; I do not have human understanding. ^30-2

**30:3** I have not learned wisdom, nor have I knowledge of the holy ones.^[Or *Holy One*] ^30-3

**30:4** Who has ascended to heaven and come down? Who has gathered the wind in the hollow of the hand? Who has wrapped up the waters in a garment? Who has established all the ends of the earth? What is the person’s name? And what is the name of the person’s child? Surely you know! ^30-4

**30:5** Every word of God proves true; he is a shield to those who take refuge in him. ^30-5

**30:6** Do not add to his words, lest he rebuke you, and you be found a liar. ^30-6

**30:7** Two things I ask of you; do not deny them to me before I die: ^30-7

**30:8** Remove far from me falsehood and lying; give me neither poverty nor riches; feed me with the food that I need, ^30-8

**30:9** lest I be full and deny you and say, “Who is the LORD?” or I be poor and steal and profane the name of my God. ^30-9

**30:10** Do not slander a servant to a master, lest the servant curse you, and you be held guilty. ^30-10

**30:11** There are those who curse their fathers and do not bless their mothers. ^30-11

**30:12** There are those who are pure in their own eyes, yet are not cleansed of their filthiness. ^30-12

**30:13** There are those—how lofty are their eyes, how high their eyelids lift!— ^30-13

**30:14** there are those whose teeth are swords, whose teeth are knives to devour the poor from off the earth, the needy from among mortals. ^30-14

**30:15** The leech has two daughters; “Give, give,” they cry. Three things are never satisfied; four never say, “Enough”: ^30-15

**30:16** Sheol, the barren womb, the earth ever-thirsty for water, and the fire that never says, “Enough.” ^30-16

**30:17** The eye that mocks a father and scorns to obey a mother will be pecked out by the ravens of the valley and eaten by the vultures. ^30-17

**30:18** Three things are too wonderful for me; four I do not understand: ^30-18

**30:19** the way of an eagle in the sky, the way of a snake on a rock, the way of a ship on the high seas, and the way of a man with a woman. ^30-19

**30:20** This is the way of an adulteress: she eats and wipes her mouth and says, “I have done no wrong.” ^30-20

**30:21** Under three things the earth trembles; under four it cannot bear up: ^30-21

**30:22** a slave when he becomes king and a fool when glutted with food, ^30-22

**30:23** a contemptible woman when she gets a husband and a maid when she supplants her mistress. ^30-23

**30:24** Four things on earth are small, yet they are exceedingly wise: ^30-24

**30:25** the ants are a people without strength, yet they provide their food in the summer; ^30-25

**30:26** the badgers are a people without power, yet they make their homes in the rocks; ^30-26

**30:27** the locusts have no king, yet all of them march in rank; ^30-27

**30:28** the lizard^[Or *spider*] can be grasped in the hand, yet it is found in kings’ palaces. ^30-28

**30:29** Three things are stately in their stride; four are stately in their gait: ^30-29

**30:30** the lion, which is mightiest among wild animals and does not turn back before any; ^30-30

**30:31** the strutting rooster,^[Gk Syr Tg Compare Vg: Meaning of Heb uncertain] the he-goat, and a king against whom none can stand. ^30-31

**30:32** If you have been foolish, exalting yourself, or if you have been devising evil, put your hand on your mouth. ^30-32

**30:33** For as pressing milk produces curds and pressing the nose produces blood, so pressing anger produces strife. ^30-33
