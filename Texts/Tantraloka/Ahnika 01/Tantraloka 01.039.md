## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. I
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 01 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v01 05 - Chapter One, part 4#^pg164|p. 150]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 01.pdf#page=164>))
>
> If the notion (dhī) that ascertains (adhyavāsayanī) (the nature of an entity as) “I know this (to be) thus” arises, (generated) from the reflection (of the light of the Self) in the (mirror of the intellect) of the individual soul (aṇu) who is sullied by the six obscuring coverings (kañcuka), (but does not develop further,) then that kind of (intellectual) knowledge is said to be ignorance. Its (underlying corresponding spiritual ignorance,) which is that of the individual soul (itself), is nourished by it, and (the spiritual ignorance nourished by it in its turn) nourishes it (intellectual ignorance). (Thus, spiritual and intellectual ignorance feed on one another). (with 1.39–40)
