## Mūla

द्विगुणोऽस्य स संस्कारो नेत्थं शुद्धे घटे विधिः ।
इत्थं श्रीशक्तिपातोऽयं निरपेक्ष इहोदितः ॥ ^mula

dviguṇo'sya sa saṃskāro netthaṃ śuddhe ghaṭe vidhiḥ |
itthaṃ śrīśaktipāto'yaṃ nirapekṣa ihoditaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 09 - Chapter Thirteen, part 5#^pg320|p. 298]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=320>))
>
> A jar (that smells bad) is perfumed with flowers in order to eliminate the bad smell. The purification that it requires is double. Such is not the procedure when a jar is clean. (with 13.282cd–283ab)
>
> Thus, this venerable descent of the power (of grace) is said here to be independent (of all worldly causes). (with 13.283cd)
