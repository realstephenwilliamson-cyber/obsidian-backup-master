## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 11 - Chapter Six, part 4#^pg387|p. 372]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=387>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 6.154cd–155ab.
>
> The lifespan of the Lord Śrikaṇṭha is one hundred years, made of the days and nights in Fundamental (Nature) (pradhāna). It is equivalent to a day of (the Rudras,) who reside in the Obscuring Coverings (kañcuka). These, namely, Necessity (niyati), Time (kāla), Attachment (rāga), (Power of Limited) Knowledge (vidyā) and (Power of Limited) Action (kalā), dissolve successively into one another. The life of those (Rudras who reside there) is equal to a day of Gahaneśa. (with 6.153–154)
