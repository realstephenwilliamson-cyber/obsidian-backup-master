# Likutei Moharan I 182

← [[Likutei Moharan I 181]] · [[Likutei Moharan]] · [[Likutei Moharan I 183]] →

*Part I · Lesson 182* · [[Likutei Moharan]]

## 1

Know! all that the world speaks about during the *Sefirah* (*Omer* Counting period), throughout the entire *Sefirah* period, is only about the *sefirah* (the divine emanation) associated with that day.

Someone who understands, is able to hear and know this. If he listens very carefully to their conversations, he will hear that they speak only of the divine emanation of that day.
