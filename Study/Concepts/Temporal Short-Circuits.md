# Temporal Short-Circuits

> [!abstract] Definition
> Bernard Stiegler's term for the collapse of the temporal interval required for human consciousness to critically reflect upon, synthesize, and assimilate primary experience into secondary memory, caused by real-time, speed-of-light automated data flows.

## Cognitive and Social Mechanism
In organic human time:
$$\text{Primary Perception} \longrightarrow [\text{Temporal Delay / Latency}] \longrightarrow \text{Critical Reflection} \longrightarrow \text{Long-term Memory}$$

In machine-speed digital transmission:
- Algorithmic feeds, notifications, and predictive auto-completions operate faster than human biological reflex (*hyper-solicitation*).
- By removing latency, technical systems bypass deliberative consciousness and trigger immediate affective, nervous, or compulsive reactions.
- The outcome is a structural incapacity for **long-term temporal synthesis**, eroding both personal individuation and collective trans-individuation.
