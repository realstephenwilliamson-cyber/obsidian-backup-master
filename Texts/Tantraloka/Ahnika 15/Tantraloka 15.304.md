## Mūla

दक्षान्यावर्ततो न्यस्येच्छक्तीनां नवकद्वयम् ।
विद्यापद्मेऽत्र तच्चोक्तमपि प्राग्दर्श्यते पुनः ॥ ^mula

dakṣānyāvartato nyasyecchaktīnāṃ navakadvayam |
vidyāpadme'tra taccoktamapi prāgdarśyate punaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 06 - Chapter Fifteen, part 5#^pg198|p. 180]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=198>))

> Here, on (this) Lotus of Knowledge, one should place the two (groups of) nine powers: (the first) rotating (clockwise to one’s) right, and (then the second) the other way around. Although we have (already) mentioned (these powers) previously, we list (them here) again.
