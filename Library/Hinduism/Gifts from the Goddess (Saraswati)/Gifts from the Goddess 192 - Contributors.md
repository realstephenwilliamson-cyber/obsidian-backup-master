## Contributors

Many visitors to Devipuram are struck by the large number of Mexican and Central and South American devotees drawn to Guruji and his legacy. Many first discovered him through the efforts of Kirin Mishra, whom Guruji initiated as Uma-Parvathinanda Natha Saraswati. Born into a Brahmin lineage in India’s state of Bihar, Mishra grew up in the northeastern United States, practicing and later teaching Vedanta, Tantra, yoga and meditation. The deep reflection and care she invested into accurately representing Guruji’s philosophy and methods to non-English-speaking audiences won his trust and affection. Mishra also teaches in the United States, Canada and Western Europe. With a background in both aerospace and medical consulting, Balasingam Janahan is director of operations at Quantum Neuromonitoring in Los Angeles. His deepest passion, however, is Sri Vidya *upāsanā*. Trained and experienced in complex Vedic and Tantric ritual, Jana is widely acknowledged as a “priest’s priest.” Guruji initiated Jana into Sri Vidya as a teenager, and the two remained close until Guruji’s death.

Nishtala Maheswari Bala Tripura Sundari Amma is Guruji’s sister-in-law (she is married to his younger brother) and in 1977 became the first person ever initiated into Sri Vidya by Guruji. Over the ensuing decades, she became renowned for her *satsangs*—gatherings of people united for spiritual discussion and

A former paratrooper in the Russian army, Sergey Babkin is an accomplished martial artist and Moscow-based specialist in rehabilitation and therapeutic physical training. Certified by the Institute of Traditional Systems of Health and the Russian State University of Physical Culture, Sport and Tourism, Babkin’s personal yoga practice evolved from the study of the late heritage of Krishnamacharya (TKV Deshik-achar, Sribhashyam, AG Mohan, Shrivats Ramaswamy) and “its comprehension through the prism of modern knowledge about the theory and methodology of physical culture.” He has also translated a number of important works on Eastern spirituality into Russian. Venu Syama (Mani), a direct disciple of Guruji, was personally trained by him in the proper performance of Sri Vidya ritual. She and her husband Prasanna established the Kāmākhyāmṛtā Mission to spread Guruji’s methodologies,  focusing  particularly on the *Kalāvāhanā* and *Siri Jyōtī Pūjās*, meditation and *hōmās*; and to propagate his interest in empowering women and youth, building happy families and enjoyable old age; and to cultivate a loving world. Her guiding principle is: “Sri Vidya for all, Sri Vidya is for everyone.”

William Thomas is a Rotterdam-based teacher of media entertainment management at Inholland University of Applied Sciences. He previously headed the audiovisual department at Rijksakademie van Beeldende Kunsten and has taught film and audio production at the Rochester Institute of Technology. For two decades, Thomas has taught yoga and Sri Vidya in Western Europe. He was one of Guruji’s earliest Western disciples and did much to document Guruji’s early work through photography and video. Janice Craig is a California-based *yōginī*, healer and ritual artist. She is the founder of the Sri Vidya Shakta Tantra School. She is an Ayurvedic practitioner, certified yoga instructor, licensed massage therapist and temple dancer.

UmaPrabha Menon, of Trivandrum, Kerala, South India, has students across India and around the world.
