## Mūla

इतश्च क्रतुहोत्रादि कृत्वा ज्ञानविवर्जिताः ।
स्वर्यान्ति तत्क्षये लोकं मानुष्यं पुण्यशेषतः ॥ ^mula

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 06 - Chapter Eight, part 4#^pg199|p. 181]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=199>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 8.148cd–149ab.
>
> From here, those who are devoid of knowledge but have performed sacrifices and offered oblations to fire and the like go to Svar (the heavenly region). When (the fruits of their actions) are exhausted, (they are reborn) in the world of men by virtue of what remains of their merit.
