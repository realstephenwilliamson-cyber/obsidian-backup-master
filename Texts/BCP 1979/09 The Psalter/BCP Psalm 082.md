# Psalm 82

← [[BCP Psalm 081]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 083]] →

*Deus stetit* · Book Three · Sixteenth Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 082]]

**82:1** God takes his stand in the council of heaven; \*  
he gives judgment in the midst of the gods: ^82-1

**82:2** "How long will you judge unjustly, \*  
and show favor to the wicked? ^82-2

**82:3** Save the weak and the orphan; \*  
defend the humble and needy; ^82-3

**82:4** Rescue the weak and the poor; \*  
deliver them from the power of the wicked. ^82-4

**82:5** They do not know, neither do they understand;  
they go about in darkness; \*  
all the foundations of the earth are shaken. ^82-5

**82:6** Now I say to you, 'You are gods, \*  
and all of you children of the Most High; ^82-6

**82:7** Nevertheless, you shall die like mortals, \*  
and fall like any prince.'" ^82-7

[p. 706] ^pg706

**82:8** Arise, O God, and rule the earth, \*  
for you shall take all nations for your own. ^82-8
