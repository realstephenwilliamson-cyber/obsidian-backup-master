## Mūla

अहेतुनापि रागो हि व्याप्तो विद्यादिना स्फुटम् ।
तद्विना न भवेद्यत्तद् व्याप्तमित्युच्यते यतः ॥ ^mula

ahetunāpi rāgo hi vyāpto vidyādinā sphuṭam |
tadvinā na bhavedyattad vyāptamityucyate yataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 09 - Chapter Nine, part 7#^pg299|p. 280]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=299>))
>
> The principle of Attachment (for example) is pervaded by that of (Limited) Knowledge, although it is clearly not its cause. (with 9.308ab)
>
> (An entity) which would not exist without (another entity, and so depends on it for its existence,) is said to be pervaded by it. This is the reason why (there is no mention) of ‘subtleness’ (lāghava) (or ‘grossness’) at all (even) by name here in our system (darśana). It is clear therefore that the relationship between the pervader and the pervaded is based on the progressive excellence of the qualities (of the members of this relationship). (with 9.308cd–309)
