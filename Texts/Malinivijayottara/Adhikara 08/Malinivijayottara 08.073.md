## Mūla

ज्ञानरूपधरां ध्यात्वा किंचिदुग्रां न भीषणाम् ।
अपरां वामशृङ्गे तु भीषणां कृष्णपिङ्गलाम् ॥ ^mula

jñānarūpadharāṁ dhyātvā kiṁcid ugrāṁ na bhīṣaṇām |

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2025 – April 2025
> p. 20 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=34>))
>
> Then, (one should contemplate) the supreme power (Parā) in the middle and the (other) two on the right and the left. Parāparā (on the right) is red by nature and very powerful. She bears the form of knowledge and is somewhat fierce (but) not frightening (*bhīṣaṇa*). Having visualized (her, meditate on) Aparā on the left prong, who is frightening and tawny-black. (with 8.72–73)
