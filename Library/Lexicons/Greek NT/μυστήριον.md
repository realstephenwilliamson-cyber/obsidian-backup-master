# μυστήριον

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · noun

**Dodson:** anything hidden, a mystery — a mystery, secret, of which initiation is necessary; in the NT: the counsels of God, once hidden but now revealed in the Gospel or some fact thereof; the Christian revelation generally; particular truths or details of the Christian revelation. (Strong's 3466; headword μυστήριον, ου, τό)
