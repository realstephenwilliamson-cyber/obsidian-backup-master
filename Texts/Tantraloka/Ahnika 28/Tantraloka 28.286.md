## Mūla

ये तु कैवल्यभागीयाः प्रत्ययास्ते न जातुचित् ।
अभ्यस्ताः संसृतेर्भावात्तेनैते शक्तिपाततः ॥ ^mula

ye tu kaivalyabhāgīyāḥ pratyayāste na jātucit |
abhyastāḥ saṃsṛterbhāvāttenaite śaktipātataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 06 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 4#^pg141|p. 124]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=141>))
>
> The reason for this is that the latent predispositions to transmigratory existence (to which the fettered soul is subject) have been fashioned by prior intense practice. This is what (Patañjali), the Lord of the Snakes, has stated in brief, (saying) ‘in those breaks . . . ’. However, those (sound) notions (pratyaya) pertaining to liberation have hardly been practiced at all, because of the condition of transmigratory existence (to which the fettered soul is subject). Thus, they (can only be) due to a descent of the power (of grace). (with 28.285–286)
