## Chapter 98

**98:1** A psalm.  
Sing to the LORD a new song,  
for He has worked wonders;  
His right hand, His holy arm,  
has won Him victory. ^98-1

**98:2** The LORD has manifested His victory,  
has displayed His triumph in the sight of the nations. ^98-2

**98:3** He was mindful of His steadfast love and faithfulness toward the house of Israel;  
all the ends of the earth beheld the victory of our God. ^98-3

**98:4** Raise a shout to the LORD, all the earth,  
break into joyous songs of praise! ^98-4

**98:5** Sing praise to the LORD with the lyre,  
with the lyre and melodious song. ^98-5

**98:6** With trumpets and the blast of the horn  
raise a shout before the LORD, the King. ^98-6

**98:7** Let the sea and all within it thunder,  
the world and its inhabitants; ^98-7

**98:8** let the rivers clap their hands,  
the mountains sing joyously together ^98-8

**98:9** at the presence of the LORD,  
for He is coming to rule the earth;  
He will rule the world justly,  
and its peoples with equity. ^98-9
