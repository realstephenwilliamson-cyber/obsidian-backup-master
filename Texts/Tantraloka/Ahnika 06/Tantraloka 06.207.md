## Mūla

तदर्हति च यः कालो विषुवत्तदिहोदितः ।
विषुवत्प्रभृति ह्रासवृद्धी ये दिनरात्रिगे ॥ ^mula

tadarhati ca yaḥ kālo viṣuvattadihoditaḥ |
viṣuvatprabhṛti hrāsavṛddhī ye dinarātrige || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 13 - Chapter Six, part 6#^pg432|p. 417]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=432>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 6.207cd–208ab.
>
> (The root) viṣ signifies ‘pervasion’ because its condition (vṛtti) is one of equality, and that is said to be pervasion. This is why the time that merits this (designation) is here called ‘viṣuvat’ (‘equinox’). (with 6.206cd–207ab)
>
> The increase or decrease of (the time it takes for) the transits (to take place) during the day and at night (of the yearly cycle projected into the breath) is in accord with the sequence of the increase or decrease of the days and nights beginning with the equinox. (with 6.207cd–208ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* ननु विषुवत्संक्रान्तौ यद्येवं नियमस्तत्तदितरासु का व्यवस्था ? -- इत्याशङ्क्याह --

> विषुवत्प्रभृति ह्रासवृद्धी ये दिनरात्रिगे ॥
> तत्क्रमेणैव संक्रान्तिह्रासवृद्धी दिवानिशोः ।

तत्क्रमेणेति, रात्रिदिनवृद्धिह्रास्यपरिपाट्या तेन पञ्चत्रिंशद्घटिके दिने चतुर्दश संक्रान्तयः, पञ्चविंशतिघटिकायां रात्रौ दशेति ॥ २०७ ॥
