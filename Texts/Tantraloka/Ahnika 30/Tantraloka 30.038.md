## Mūla

प्रणवश्चामृते तेजोमालिनी स्वाहया सह ।
एकादशाक्षरं ब्रह्मशिरस्तन्मालिनीमते ॥ ^mula

praṇavaścāmṛte tejomālinī svāhayā saha |
ekādaśākṣaraṃ brahmaśirastanmālinīmate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 02 - Chapter Thirty, part 1#^pg45|p. 27]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=45>))

> The Brahmaśiras (the Head of Brahmā), according to the Mālinīmata, consists of eleven syllables, that is, pranava (OM) and AMRTE TEJOMĀLINI, along with SVĀHĀ.
