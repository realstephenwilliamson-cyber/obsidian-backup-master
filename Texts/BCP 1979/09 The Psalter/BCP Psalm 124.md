# Psalm 124

← [[BCP Psalm 123]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 125]] →

*Nisi quia Dominus* · Book Five · Twenty-seventh Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 124]]

**124:1** If the LORD had not been on our side, \*  
let Israel now say; ^124-1

**124:2** If the LORD had not been on our side, \*  
when enemies rose up against us; ^124-2

**124:3** Then would they have swallowed us up alive \*  
in their fierce anger toward us; ^124-3

**124:4** Then would the waters have overwhelmed us \*  
and the torrent gone over us; ^124-4

**124:5** Then would the raging waters \*  
have gone right over us. ^124-5

**124:6** Blessed be the LORD! \*  
he has not given us over to be a prey for their teeth. ^124-6

**124:7** We have escaped like a bird from the snare of the fowler; \*  
the snare is broken, and we have escaped. ^124-7

**124:8** Our help is in the Name of the LORD, \*  
the maker of heaven and earth. ^124-8
