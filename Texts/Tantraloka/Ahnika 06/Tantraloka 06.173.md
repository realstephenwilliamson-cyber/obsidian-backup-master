## Mūla

तत्सृष्टौ सृष्टिसंहारा निःसंख्या जगतां यतः ।
अन्तर्भूतास्ततः शाक्ती महासृष्टिरुदाहृता ॥ ^mula

tatsṛṣṭau sṛṣṭisaṃhārā niḥsaṃkhyā jagatāṃ yataḥ |

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 12 - Chapter Six, part 5#^pg403|p. 388]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=403>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 6.173cd–174ab.
>
> The emanations and reabsorptions of the universe contained in that (one) emanation are countless. This is why Śakti’s creation is called the Great Emanation (mahāsrṣṭi).

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* न केवलं शक्त्यन्तं प्रलयस्यैव महत्त्वं यावत्सृष्टेरपीत्याह --

> तत्सृष्टौ सृष्टिसंहारा निःसंख्या जगतां यतः ।
> अन्तर्भूतास्ततः शाक्ती महासृष्टिरुदाहृता ॥
