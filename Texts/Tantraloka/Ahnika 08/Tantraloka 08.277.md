## Mūla

भग्नानि महाप्रलये सृष्टौ नोत्पादितानि लीनानि ।

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 08 - Chapter Eight, part 6#^pg298|p. 280]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=298>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 8.277cd–278ab.
>
> 1) The unconscious (senses) (mdha) are those presided over by Akrta (‘what has not been done’). As such, they have no power to perform their function. 2) According to the scriptures (that is, the ivatanu), the fully active senses (vivrtta) (operate) in their specific fixed field of operation in a clear, fully evident manner (sphuṭa). 3) The senses that have merged (into consciousness) (lna) are those that were destroyed during the Great Dissolution (mahāpralaya) and were not regenerated in (the subsequent) creation. 4) Again, (those senses) that are called ‘transcendental’ (vikaraṇa) are (perfectly) obedient to the will. The objects of action (of these four types of senses) are also such (as they are). (with 8.276–277)

## Viveka

> [!viveka]- Viveka in English (Dyczkowski, vol. V, pp. 280–281)
> ![[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 08 - Chapter Eight, part 6#Viveka on 8.276–277]]

> अकृताधिष्ठानतया कृत्याशक्तानि मूढानि ।
> प्रतिनियतविषयभाञ्जि स्फुटानि शास्त्रे विवृत्तानि ॥
> भग्नानि महाप्रलये सृष्टौ नोत्पादितानि लीनानि ।
> इच्छाधीनानि पुनर्विकरणसंज्ञानि कार्यमप्येवम् ॥

"कृत्यम्"शब्दाद्यालोचनम्; अत एव बाधिर्यादिविशिष्टवृत्तित्वे हेतुः "प्रतिनियतविषयभाञ्जि"इति । सृष्टावनुत्पाद एव लीनत्वे हेतुः । "इच्छाधीनानि"इति स्वात्मायत्तानि -- इत्यर्थः । अत एव श्रीकण्ठादीनां स्वतन्त्रेन्द्रियत्वम् । यदुक्तमत्रैव --

"तेषूमापतिरेव प्रभुः स्वतन्त्रेन्द्रियो विकरणात्मा ।"(तं. ८ ।२२९)

एवमिति, मूढादिभेदाच्चतुर्धा ॥ २७७ ॥
