## Mūla

प्रकर्तव्या विधानेन दीक्षा सर्वफलप्रदा ॥ ^mula

prakartavyā vidhānena dīkṣā sarvaphalapradā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2024 – April 2024
> p. 33 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Malini (journal), January-April 2024.pdf#page=35>))
>
> Such (is the procedure with regards to this present) elemental body (*bhūtaśarīra*), by means of which the teacher, who is (the living) icon of Śiva (himself), should perform the initiation that bestows all desired fruits. (with 6.29cd–30ab)
