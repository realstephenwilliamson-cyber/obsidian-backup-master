## Mūla

भीमश्च भीषणश्चैव वमनः पिवनस्तथा ।
एतानष्टौ स्थितिध्वंसरक्षानुग्रहकारिणः ॥ ^mula

bhīmaś ca bhīñaṇaś caiva vamanaṭ pivanas tathā |
etān aṣṭau sthitidhvaṁsarakṣānugrahakāriṇaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2022 – June 2022
> p. 42 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=18>))
>
> (Their names are) Aghora, Paramaghora (Supreme Ghora), Ghorarūpa, Ghoramukha, Bhīma, Bhīṣaṇa, Vamana, and Pibana. These eight (Aghoras, that constitute the group of eight Aghoras (*aghorāṣṭaka*), maintain, destroy, protect and (dispense) grace. (with 1.19cd–20)
