## Mūla

न शङ्केत तथा शङ्का विलीयेतावहेलया ।
श्रीसर्वाचारवीरालीनिशाचरक्रमादिषु ॥ ^mula

na śaṅketa tathā śaṅkā vilīyetāvahelayā |

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 04 - Chapter Twelve#^pg135|p. 115]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=135>))
>
> There is no (confusing) mix-up (between what should and should not be done); thus (one should reflect), “what is this doubt (I have about using these sacrificial substances)? (It is nothing at all!) Even if there is (some), clearly one should not be in doubt.” In this way doubt would easily cease. (with 12.22cd–23ab)
>
> All this has been extensively stated in various places in scriptures such as the venerable Sarvācāra, Viīrāvalī, and Niśācāra, and in the Krama (teachings) and others. (with 12.23cd–24ab)
