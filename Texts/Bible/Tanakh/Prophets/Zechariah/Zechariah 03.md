## Chapter 3

**3:1** He further showed me Joshua, the high priest, standing before the angel of the LORD, and the Accuser[^1] standing at his right to accuse him. ^3-1

**3:2** But [the angel of] the LORD said to the Accuser, “The LORD rebuke you, O Accuser; may the LORD who has chosen Jerusalem rebuke you! For this is a brand plucked from the fire.”[^2] ^3-2

**3:3** Now Joshua was clothed in filthy garments when he stood before the angel. ^3-3

**3:4** The latter spoke up and said to his attendants, “Take the filthy garments off him!” And he said to him, “See, I have removed your guilt from you, and you shall be clothed in [priestly] robes.” ^3-4

**3:5** Then he[^3] gave the order, “Let a pure[^4] diadem be placed on his head.” And they placed the pure diadem on his head and clothed him in [priestly] garments,[^5] as the angel of the LORD stood by. ^3-5

**3:6** And the angel of the LORD charged Joshua as follows: ^3-6

**3:7** “Thus said the LORD of Hosts: If you walk in My paths and keep My charge, you in turn will rule My House and guard My courts, and I will permit you to move about among these attendants. ^3-7

**3:8** Hearken well, O High Priest Joshua, you and your fellow priests sitting before you! For those men are a sign that I am going to bring My servant the Branch.[^6] ^3-8

**3:9** For mark well this stone which I place before Joshua, a single stone with seven eyes.[^7] I will execute its engraving—declares the LORD of Hosts—and I will remove that country’s guilt in a single day. ^3-9

**3:10** In that day—declares the LORD of Hosts—you will be inviting each other to the shade of vines and fig trees.” ^3-10

[^1]: Others “Satan.”
[^2]: Joshua’s father (Hag. 1.1; 1 Chron. 5.40–41) was exiled and his grandfather executed (2 Kings 25.18–21) by the Babylonians, but Joshua returned.
[^3]: Heb. “I.”
[^4]: I.e., ritually pure.
[^5]: Joshua has now been rendered fit to associate with the heavenly beings (v. 7); cf. Isa. 6.6–8.
[^6]: I.e., the future king of David’s line. See 6.12; Jer. 23.5–6; 33.15–16; cf. Isa. 11.1.
[^7]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain. The stone apparently symbolizes the God-given power of the future Davidic ruler; see below 4.6–7.
