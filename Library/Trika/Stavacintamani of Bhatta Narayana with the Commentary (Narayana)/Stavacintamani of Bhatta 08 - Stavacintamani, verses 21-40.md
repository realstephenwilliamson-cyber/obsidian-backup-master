In this context, the word rasa means ‘love’ or ‘devotion’ (abhinivesa). Therefore, let my love, which exists for You, multiply in thousands of currents.

é! The word pratyabhijñā refers to the recognition of one’s own essential nature (ātman) and its identity with Śiva which grants liberation. In the recognition of one’s own Self there is no negating the world; however, at the moment of recognition, the world gets a new interpretation, a new understanding. The difference betwcen ‘recollection’ (smrti) and ‘recognition’ is: recollection requires the impressions of past experiences, while recognition, in addition to these impressions, requires the presence of the object whose real nature will be recognized. As Abhinavagupta puts it, “Recognition consists of the unification of what appeared once with what appears now, as the judgment: ‘This is the same Caitra’. It is the cognition which refers to an object that is directly present. It is reached through the unification of the experiences.” (IPV comm. 1:1:1)
