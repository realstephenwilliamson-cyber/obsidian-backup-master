# LXX Psalms 80

← [[LXX Psalms 79]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 81]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 081]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 081]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**80:1** For the end, a Psalm for Asaph, concerning the wine-presses.[^1]  ^80-1

**80:2** Rejoice ye in God our helper; shout aloud to the God of Jacob.  ^80-2

**80:3** Take a psalm, and produce the timbrel, the pleasant psaltery with the harp.  ^80-3

**80:4** Blow the trumpet at the new moon, in the glorious day of your feast.[^2]  ^80-4

**80:5** For *this* is an ordinance for Israel, and a statute of the God of Jacob.  ^80-5

**80:6** He made it *to be* a testimony in Joseph, when he came forth out of the land of Egypt: he heard a language which he understood not.[^3]  ^80-6

**80:7** He removed his back from burdens: his hands slaved in making the baskets.  ^80-7

**80:8** Thou didst call upon me in trouble, and I delivered thee; I heard thee in the secret place of the storm: I proved thee at the water of Strife. Pause.[^4]  ^80-8

**80:9** Hear, my people, and I will speak to thee, O Israel; and I will testify to thee: if thou wilt hearken to me;  ^80-9

**80:10** there shall be no new god in thee; neither shalt thou worship a strange god.  ^80-10

**80:11** For I am the Lord thy God, that brought thee out of the land of Egypt: open thy mouth wide, and I will fill it.  ^80-11

**80:12** But my people hearkened not to my voice; and Israel gave no heed to me.  ^80-12

**80:13** So I let them go after the ways of their own hearts: they will go on in their own ways.  ^80-13

**80:14** If my people had hearkened to me, if Israel had walked in my ways,  ^80-14

**80:15** I should have put down their enemies very quickly, and should have laid my hand upon those that afflicted them.  ^80-15

**80:16** The Lord's enemies *should have* lied to him: but their time shall be for ever.[^5]  ^80-16

**80:17** And he fed them with the fat of wheat; and satisfied them with honey out of the rock.  ^80-17

[^1]: 80:1 *Alex.* David.
[^2]: 80:4 *Alex.* our.
[^3]: 80:6 *Gr.* him, *sc.* Israel.
[^4]: 80:8 *Lit.* contradiction.
[^5]: 80:16 *sc.* of the others.
