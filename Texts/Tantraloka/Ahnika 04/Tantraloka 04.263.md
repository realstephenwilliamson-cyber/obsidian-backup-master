## Mūla

निर्मर्यादं स्वसंबोधं संपूर्णं बुद्ध्यतामिति ।
परकीयमिदं रूपं ध्येयमेतत्तु मे निजम् ॥ ^mula

nirmaryādaṃ svasaṃbodhaṃ saṃpūrṇaṃ buddhyatāmiti |
parakīyamidaṃ rūpaṃ dhyeyametattu me nijam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. III
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 03 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v03 11 - Chapter Four, part 10#^pg380|p. 367]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 03.pdf#page=380>))
>
> It is taught (generally in the Śaiva scriptures) that the essence of the observance of the Rule (samayācāāra) should be maintained (once one has received initiation). (However, the observance of) the Rule and the rest is prohibited in the Mata scriptures. This is because (the gradual) abandonment of this and that, so that the principles (which constitute the microcosmic and macrocosmic order) may be purified, derives its existence from relative distinctions (bhedaprānatayāā). (But these scriptures declare that knowing this,) ‘one should realize one’s own consciousness to be (perfect and) all-embracing in all respects (sarapūrna) and unconfined (by such rules and regulations) (nirmaryāda).’ (with 4.261cd–263ab)
>
> (Similarly, members of a Kula and others distinguish between their own deities and those of others, reflecting that) “that (iconic) form (rūpa) belongs to another (school), whereas this is my own, upon which I should meditate.” “A trident and the like (are the) sectarian marks (liṅga) of a different (tradition), whereas the skull and the like are (those of) my own.” (with 4.263cd–264ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* अथ "परस्वरूपलिङ्गादि नामगोत्रादिकं च यत्"इति व्याख्यातुकामः क्रमेण परकीयं स्वकीयं च रूपाद्याचष्टे --

> परकीयमिदं रूपं ध्येयमेतत्तु मे निजम् ॥
> ज्वालादिलिङ्गं चान्यस्य कपालादि तु मे निजम् ।

"एतत्"इति ध्यातृस्वभावम् । "ज्वालादि"इति बाह्यैषणादिसमुत्थत्वात् परकीयम् । ङिजम्"इति स्वशरीरावस्थितम् ॥ २६३ ॥
