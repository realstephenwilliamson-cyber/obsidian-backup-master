## Mūla

यो हि यस्माद्गुणोत्कृष्टः स तस्मादूर्ध्व उच्यते ।
एतत्ते कथितं सर्वं किमन्यत्परिपृच्छसि ॥ ^mula

yo hi yasmād guṇotkṛṣṭaḥ sa tasmād ūrdhva ucyate |
etat te kathitam sarvam kim anyat paripṛcchasi || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, October 2022 – December 2022
> p. 27 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=34>))
>
> O Lady of Good Vows, one should know that the four Lords (who reside) individually within these (four Eggs and preside over them,) that is, Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Rudra and Īśvara, are (all) tranquil. Whatever excels something else in quality is said to be above it.[^76] And so I have told you everything about this. What else are you enquiring about? (with 2.59–60)

[^76]: MV 2/60ab is quoted in TĀ 9/310ab and in TĀv ad 8/22cd-23ab, and ad 5/157cd-158 (156cd-157).
