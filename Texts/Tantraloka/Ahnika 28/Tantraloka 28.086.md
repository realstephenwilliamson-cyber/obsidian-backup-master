## Mūla

षड्योगिनीः सप्तकं च सप्तारेऽष्टाष्टके च वा ।
अन्यद्वा तादृशं तत्र चक्रे तादृक्स्वरूपिणि ॥ ^mula

ṣaḍyoginīḥ saptakaṃ ca saptāre'ṣṭāṣṭake ca vā |
anyadvā tādṛśaṃ tatra cakre tādṛksvarūpiṇi || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 04 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 2#^pg63|p. 46]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=63>))
>
> In the circle with one spoke, one should recollect (and apply) the prescribed procedure (for the worship of the teacher as) a ‘solitary hero’. The couple (yāmala) (is worshipped in the circle) with two spokes. Elsewhere (in the following circle), the triad (trika) (is worshipped). In the same way, the six Yoginīs (are worshipped) in (the circle) with six spokes, the group of seven in (the circle with) seven spokes, or in the eight (circles) of eight, (the sixty-four Yoginīs,) or whatever else that is like those (groupings), there (in an appropriate) circle that is like (the others). (with 28.85–86)
