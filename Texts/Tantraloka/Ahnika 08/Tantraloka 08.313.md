## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 09 - Chapter Eight, part 7#^pg329|p. 311]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=329>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 8.313cd–314ab.
>
> The (number and) extent of those extremely subtle (wombs) is not mentioned in the prescribed procedures (vidhi). Just as many surrounding lines of defence are constructed in a single (fortified) enclosure, similarly, there are many vaginal cavities (yonibila) in the one enclosure at the summit of Māyā. Hidden one from another by the walls of the subtle divisions (paṭala) within Māyā, innumerable Rudras, blissful and invisible, reside in each cavity. There, within (that) place, (they have variously) entered into a state of union (sāyujya) (with Śiva), proximity (sāmīpya) (to him, while) others (pare) abide in the same world with him (saloka). (with 8.310cd–313ab)
>
> The teacher (Bṛhaspati) has taught this in the same way (evam) with regards to each world of (their) inhabitants. (with 8.313cd)
