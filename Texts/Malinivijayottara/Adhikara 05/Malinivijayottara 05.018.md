## Mūla

आम्रातिकेशजल्पेशश्रीशैलाः सहरीन्दवः ।
भीमेश्वरमहेन्द्राट्टहासाः सविमलेश्वराः ॥ ^mula

āmrātikeśajalpeśaśrīśailāḥ saharīndavaḥ |
bhīmeśvaramahendrāṭṭahāsāḥ savimaleśvarāḥ || ^iast

*Muktabodha's e-text of the KSTS edition (M00160) has:* āmrātikeśajalpeśaśrīśailāḥ saharīndavaḥ | bhīmeśvaramahendrādṛhāsāḥ savimaleśvarāḥ ||

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, July 2023 – September 2023
> p. 72 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=77>))
>
> B) There (the next group of eight consists of)[^35] 1) Bhairava, 2) Kedāra, 3) Mahākāla (Great Time), along with 4) Madhyama (Middle O ne), 5) Āmrātakeśa (Lord of Āmrātaka), 6) Jalpeśa (Lord of Chatter), 7) Śrīśaila along with 8) Harīndu.'[^36] (with 5.17cd–18ab)

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, July 2023 – September 2023
> p. 72 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=77>))
>
> C) 1) Bhīmeśvara, 2) Mahendra, 3) Aṭṭahāsa, along with 4) Vimaleśvara, 5) Kanakhala, 6) Nākhala, 7) Kurukṣetra and 8) Gayā[^37] - that is the third secret (group of eight). (with 5.18cd–19ab)

[^35]: According to TĀ 8/442 and 443 this is the secret group of eight secrets, whereas the following one is the group of eight great secrets.
[^36]: MV 5/17cd-18ab quoted in TĀv ad 8/205cd-206 (205-206ab). It is paraphrased in TĀ 8/442abc (441cd-442a). In the TĀ this is called the Group of Eight Secrets (guhyāṣṭaka) and the following one the Group of Eight Great Secrets (mahāguhyāṣṭaka).
[^37]: MV 5/18cd-19ab quoted in TĀv ad 8/205cd-208 (205-208ab).
