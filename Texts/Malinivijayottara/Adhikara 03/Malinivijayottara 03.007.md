## Mūla

ज्ञापयन्ती जगत्यत्र ज्ञनशक्तिर्निगद्यते ।
एवंभूतमिदं वस्तु भवत्विति यदा पुनः ॥ ^mula

jñāpayantī jagaty atra jñanaśaktir nigadyate |
evaṁbhūtam idaṁ[^5] vastu bhavatv iti yadā punaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2023 – March 2023
> p. 40 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=40>))
>
> (When that power instils in consciousness) here in this world, the certainty that: 'this is such and should not be known to be otherwise', then (She) is called the power of knowledge. When assuming a state of intent towards Her task of the form – 'may all this be such' and that very instant makes it so, She is called activity here (in this world). (with 3.6cd–8ab)

[^5]: SpPra *evaṁbhūtamidaṁ sarvam iti kāryonmukhī yadā*
