## Mūla

भावस्य रूपमित्युक्ते केयमस्थानवैधुरी ।
अनेन नीतिमार्गेण निर्मूलमपसारिता ॥ ^mula

bhāvasya rūpamityukte keyamasthānavaidhurī |
anena nītimārgeṇa nirmūlamapasāritā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 11 - Chapter Ten, part 2#^pg358|p. 339]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=358>))
>
> (I fail to understand) what this misplaced aversion is (on your part to our) saying that (perceptibility) is an integral part (rūpa) of anything (which is perceivable). (with 10.70ab)
>
> This line of reasoning totally precludes (the possibility) of an infinite regress (which would be the case if ‘perceptibility’ were to be itself perceptible). (with 10.70cd–71a)
