## Mūla

विकार उपजायेत तत्तत्त्वं तत्त्वमुत्तमम् ।
प्राणे प्रतिष्ठितः कालस्तदाविष्टा च यत्तनुः ॥ ^mula

vikāra upajāyeta tattattvaṃ tattvamuttamam |
prāṇe pratiṣṭhitaḥ kālastadāviṣṭā ca yattanuḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 02 - Chapter Seven#^pg62|p. 44]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=62>))
>
> It is said in the venerable Mālinītantra that ‘the reality principle, (which is the foundation of) any (pulse of sensory) acti (vikāra) in the body, wherever it may occur, is the most excellent reality.’ (with 7.64cd–65ab)
>
> Time is based on the vital breath, and the body is penetrated (and pervaded) by that. Accordingly, the nature of that (vital breath), which is established in the body, will be described. (with 7.65cd–66ab)

*Lead-in:* इति । (मा.वि. १८ ।४२) ननु भवतु नामैतत् तत्राप्यस्य प्राणस्य देहनिष्ठत्वेन कस्माद्रूपमुच्यते ? -- इत्याशङ्क्याह --

> देहे प्रतिष्ठितस्यास्य ततो रूपं निरूप्यते ।
