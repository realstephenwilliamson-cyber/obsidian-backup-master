> [!abstract] Scope & Objective
> Missing volumes in the library's sets (revised 4 Oct 2026 against the current catalog, section 2), and a research dossier on **Moshe Idel**—the preeminent scholar whose work on linguistic mysticism, Abraham Abulafia, the Golem/Artificial Anthropoid, and Hasidism provides the bridge between your Kabbalistic, Stieglerian/Hobbesian, and Tantric research streams.

## 1. Moshe Idel: Research Dossier & Vault Integration

Moshe Idel’s scholarship is the primary Jewish counterpart to Abhinavagupta’s *Mātṛkā* theory and Thomas Hobbes’s *Artificial Man*.

### A. Core Works Matrix & Intersections

### B. High-Impact Conceptual Bridge: The Golem and the Leviathan
In *Golem*, Idel demonstrates that Jewish mystics did not create an artificial anthropoid for physical servitude, but as an ecstatic ritual proof that the mystic has purified consciousness to participate in the creative power of the 22 letters. 

This presents an astonishing conceptual polarity with Thomas Hobbes:
* **Hobbes’s *Leviathan*:** The "Artificial Man" is constructed through political covenant to enforce secular order against the terror of civil war (*the sovereign monster*).
* **Idel’s *Golem*:** The "Artificial Man" is constructed through the sacred letters to embody divine language in human consciousness.
* **The Cybernetic Leviathan:** Digital society constructs a *cybernetic Golem/Leviathan*—an artificial entity composed of binary alphabetic code that now rules over its creators.
