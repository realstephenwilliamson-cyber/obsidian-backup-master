# Psalm 137

← [[BCP Psalm 136]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 138]] →

*Super flumina* · Book Five · Twenty-eighth Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 137]]

**137:1** By the waters of Babylon we sat down and wept, \*  
when we remembered you, O Zion. ^137-1

**137:2** As for our harps, we hung them up \*  
on the trees in the midst of that land. ^137-2

**137:3** For those who led us away captive asked us for a song,  
and our oppressors called for mirth: \*  
"Sing us one of the songs of Zion." ^137-3

**137:4** How shall we sing the LORD'S song \*  
upon an alien soil? ^137-4

**137:5** If I forget you, O Jerusalem, \*  
let my right hand forget its skill. ^137-5

**137:6** Let my tongue cleave to the roof of my mouth  
if I do not remember you, \*  
if I do not set Jerusalem above my highest joy. ^137-6

**137:7** Remember the day of Jerusalem, O LORD,  
against the people of Edom, \*  
who said, "Down with it! down with it!  
even to the ground!" ^137-7

**137:8** O Daughter of Babylon, doomed to destruction, \*  
happy the one who pays you back  
for what you have done to us! ^137-8

**137:9** Happy shall he be who takes your little ones, \*  
and dashes them against the rock! ^137-9
