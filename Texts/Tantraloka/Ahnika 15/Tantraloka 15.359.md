## Mūla

नाभिहृत्कण्ठतालूर्ध्वकुण्डे ज्वलनवत्स्मरन् ।
मन्त्रचक्रं तत्र विश्वं ज्वह्वन्संपादयेद्धुतिम् ॥ ^mula

nābhihṛtkaṇṭhatālūrdhvakuṇḍe jvalanavatsmaran |
mantracakraṃ tatra viśvaṃ jvahvansaṃpādayeddhutim || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 07 - Chapter Fifteen, part 6#^pg239|p. 221]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=239>))

> (The teacher) should offer the oblation (in this way) as he recollects the wheel of Mantras like a fire in the navel, the heart, the throat, the palate and in the upper fire pit (of Kundalinī), offering all things there (to her) as an oblation.
