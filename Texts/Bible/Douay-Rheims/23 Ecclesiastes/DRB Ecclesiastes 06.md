# Ecclesiastes 6 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Ecclesiastes 05|← Ecclesiastes 5]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Ecclesiastes 07|Ecclesiastes 7 →]]

**6:1** There is also another evil, which I have seen under the sun, and that frequent among men: ^6-1

**6:2** A man to whom God hath given riches, and substance, and honour, and his soul wanteth nothing of all that he desireth: yet God doth not give him power to eat thereof, but a stranger shall eat it up. This is vanity and a great misery. ^6-2

**6:3** If a man beget a hundred children, and live many years, and attain to a great age, and his soul make no use of the goods of his substance, and he be without burial: of this man I pronounce, that the untimely born is better than he. ^6-3

**6:4** For he came in vain, and goeth to darkness, and his name shall be wholly forgotten. ^6-4

**6:5** He hath not seen the sun, nor known the distance of good and evil: ^6-5

**6:6** Although he lived two thousand years, and hath not enjoyed good things: do not all make haste to one place? ^6-6

**6:7** All the labour of man is for his mouth, but his soul shall not be filled. ^6-7

**6:8** What hath the wise man more than the fool? and what the poor man, but to go thither, where there is life? ^6-8

**6:9** Better it is to see what thou mayst desire, than to desire that which thou canst not know. But this also is vanity, and presumption of spirit. ^6-9

**6:10** He that shall be, his name is already called: and it is known, that he is man, and cannot contend in judgment with him that is stronger than himself. ^6-10

**6:11** There are many words that have much vanity in disputing. ^6-11
