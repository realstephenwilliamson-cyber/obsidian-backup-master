## Chapter 1

**1:1** The word of the LORD that came to Micah the Morashtite, who prophesied concerning Samaria and Jerusalem in the reigns of Kings Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah of Judah. ^1-1

**1:2** Listen, all you peoples,  
Give heed, O earth, and all it holds;  
And let my Lord GOD be your accuser—  
My Lord from His holy abode. ^1-2

**1:3** For lo! the LORD  
Is coming forth from His dwelling-place,  
He will come down and stride  
Upon the heights of the earth. ^1-3

**1:4** The mountains shall melt under Him  
And the valleys burst open—  
Like wax before fire,  
Like water cascading down a slope. ^1-4

**1:5** All this is for the transgression of Jacob,  
And for the sins of the House of Israel.  
What is the transgression of Jacob  
But Samaria,  
And what the shrines[^1] of Judah  
But Jerusalem? ^1-5

**1:6** So I will turn Samaria  
Into a ruin in open country,  
Into ground for planting vineyards;  
For I will tumble her stones into the valley  
And lay her foundations bare. ^1-6

**1:7** All her sculptured images shall be smashed,  
And all her harlot’s wealth be burned,  
And I will make a waste heap of all her idols,  
For they were amassed from fees for harlotry,  
And they shall become harlots’ fees again. ^1-7

**1:8** Because of this I will lament and wail;  
I will go stripped and naked!  
I will lament as sadly as the jackals,  
As mournfully as the ostriches. ^1-8

**1:9** For her[^2] wound is incurable,  
It has reached Judah,  
It has spread to the gate of my people,  
To Jerusalem. ^1-9

**1:10** [^3]Tell it not in Gath,  
Refrain from weeping;[^4]  
In Beth-leaphrah,  
Strew dust[^5] over your [head]. ^1-10

**1:11** Pass on, inhabitants of Shaphir!  
Did not the inhabitants of Zaanan  
Have to go forth naked in shame?  
There is lamentation in Beth-ezel—  
It will withdraw its support from you. ^1-11

**1:12** Though the inhabitants of Maroth  
Hoped for good,  
Yet disaster from the LORD descended  
Upon the gate of Jerusalem. ^1-12

**1:13** Hitch the steeds to the chariot,  
Inhabitant of Lachish!  
It is the beginning  
Of Fair Zion’s guilt;  
Israel’s transgressions  
Can be traced to you! ^1-13

**1:14** Truly, you must give a farewell gift  
To Moresheth-gath.  
The houses of Achzib are[^6]  
To the kings of Israel  
Like a spring that fails. ^1-14

**1:15** A dispossessor will I bring to you  
Who dwell in Mareshah;  
At Adullam the glory  
Of Israel shall set. ^1-15

**1:16** [^7]Shear off your hair and make yourself bald  
For the children you once delighted in;  
Make yourself as bald as a vulture,  
For they have been banished from you. ^1-16

[^1]: Emendation yields “sins.”
[^2]: I.e., the nation’s.
[^3]: Meaning of much of vv. 10–13 uncertain. They may refer to the transfer of part of western Judah to Philistine rule by Sennacherib of Assyria in the year 701 b.c.e.
[^4]: So that enemies may not gloat; cf. 2 Sam. 1.20.
[^5]: Heb. ‘aphar, a play on Beth-leaphrah; vv. 10–15 contain several similar puns.
[^6]: Emendation yields “Fair Achzib is.”
[^7]: A common rite of mourning; cf. Jer. 7.29.
