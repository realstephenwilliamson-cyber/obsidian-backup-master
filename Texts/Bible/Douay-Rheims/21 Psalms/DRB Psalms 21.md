# Psalms 21 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 20|← Psalms 20]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 22|Psalms 22 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 21|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**21:1** Unto the end, for the morning protection, a psalm for David. ^21-1

**21:2** O God my God, look upon me: why hast thou forsaken me? Far from my salvation are the words of my sins. ^21-2

**21:3** O my God, I shall cry by day, and thou wilt not hear: and by night, and it shall not be reputed as folly in me. ^21-3

**21:4** But thou dwellest in the holy place, the praise of Israel. ^21-4

**21:5** In thee have our fathers hoped: they have hoped, and thou hast delivered them. ^21-5

**21:6** They cried to thee, and they were saved: they trusted in thee, and were not confounded. ^21-6

**21:7** But I am a worm, and no man: the reproach of men, and the outcast of the people. ^21-7

**21:8** All they that saw me have laughed me to scorn: they have spoken with the lips, and wagged the head. ^21-8

**21:9** He hoped in the Lord, let him deliver him: let him save him, seeing he delighteth in him. ^21-9

**21:10** For thou art he that hast drawn me out of the womb: my hope from the breasts of my mother. ^21-10

**21:11** I was cast upon thee from the womb. From my mother’s womb thou art my God, ^21-11

**21:12** Depart not from me. For tribulation is very near: for there is none to help me. ^21-12

**21:13** Many calves have surrounded me: fat bulls have besieged me. ^21-13

**21:14** They have opened their mouths against me, as a lion ravening and roaring. ^21-14

**21:15** I am poured out like water; and all my bones are scattered. My heart is become like wax melting in the midst of my bowels. ^21-15

**21:16** My strength is dried up like a potsherd, and my tongue hath cleaved to my jaws: and thou hast brought me down into the dust of death. ^21-16

**21:17** For many dogs have encompassed me: the council of the malignant hath besieged me. They have dug my hands and feet. ^21-17

**21:18** They have numbered all my bones. And they have looked and stared upon me. ^21-18

**21:19** They parted my garments amongst them; and upon my vesture they cast lots. ^21-19

**21:20** But thou, O Lord, remove not thy help to a distance from me; look towards my defence. ^21-20

**21:21** Deliver, O God, my soul from the sword: my only one from the hand of the dog. ^21-21

**21:22** Save me from the lion’s mouth; and my lowness from the horns of the unicorns. ^21-22

**21:23** I will declare thy name to my brethren: in the midst of the church will I praise thee. ^21-23

**21:24** Ye that fear the Lord, praise him: all ye the seed of Jacob, glorify him. ^21-24

**21:25** Let all the seed of Israel fear him: because he hath not slighted nor despised the supplication of the poor man. Neither hath he turned away his face from me: and when I cried to him he heard me. ^21-25

**21:26** With thee is my praise in a great church: I will pay my vows in the sight of them that fear him. ^21-26

**21:27** The poor shall eat and shall be filled: and they shall praise the Lord that seek him: their hearts shall live for ever and ever. ^21-27

**21:28** All the ends of the earth shall remember, and shall be converted to the Lord: And all the kindreds of the Gentiles shall adore in his sight. ^21-28

**21:29** For the kingdom is the Lord’s; and he shall have dominion over the nations. ^21-29

**21:30** All the fat ones of the earth have eaten and have adored: all they that go down to the earth shall fall before him. ^21-30

**21:31** And to him my soul shall live: and my seed shall serve him. ^21-31

**21:32** There shall be declared to the Lord a generation to come: and the heavens shall shew forth his justice to a people that shall be born, which the Lord hath made. ^21-32
