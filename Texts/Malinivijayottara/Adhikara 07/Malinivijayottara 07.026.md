## Mūla

अधोमुखस्थिते वामे दक्षिणां तर्जनीं बुधः ।
चालयेन्मध्यदेशस्थां घण्टामुद्रा प्रिया मता ॥ ^mula

adhomukhasthite vāme dakṣiṇāṃ tarjanīṃ budhaḥ |
cālayenmadhyadeśasthāṃ ghaṇṭāmudrā priyā matā || ^iast

>
> **17. Ghaṇṭā (the Uvula)**
>
> When the left hand is facing downwards, the wise man should wiggle the index finger of the right (hand) located in the middle (above of the left one). This the Gesture of the Uvula, which is considered to be dear (and pleasant) (*priyā*).
