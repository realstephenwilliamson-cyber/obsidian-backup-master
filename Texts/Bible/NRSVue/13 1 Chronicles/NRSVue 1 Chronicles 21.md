# 1 Chronicles 21 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue 1 Chronicles 20|← 1 Chronicles 20]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue 1 Chronicles 22|1 Chronicles 22 →]]

## The census and plague

**21:1** Satan stood up against Israel and incited David to count the people of Israel. ^21-1

**21:2** So David said to Joab and the commanders of the army, “Go, number Israel, from Beer-sheba to Dan, and bring me a report, so that I may know their number.” ^21-2

**21:3** But Joab said, “May the LORD increase the number of his people a hundredfold! Are they not, my lord the king, all of them my lord’s servants? Why then should my lord require this? Why should he bring guilt on Israel?” ^21-3

**21:4** But the king’s word prevailed against Joab. So Joab departed and went throughout all Israel and came back to Jerusalem. ^21-4

**21:5** Joab gave the total count of the people to David. In all Israel there were one million one hundred thousand men who drew the sword, and in Judah four hundred seventy thousand who drew the sword. ^21-5

**21:6** But he did not include Levi and Benjamin in the numbering, for the king’s command was abhorrent to Joab. ^21-6

**21:7** But God was displeased with this thing, and he struck Israel. ^21-7

**21:8** David said to God, “I have sinned greatly in that I have done this thing. But now, please remove the guilt of your servant, for I have done very foolishly.” ^21-8

**21:9** The LORD spoke to Gad, David’s seer, saying, ^21-9

**21:10** “Go and say to David: Thus says the LORD: Three things I offer you; choose one of them, so that I may do it to you.” ^21-10

**21:11** So Gad came to David and said to him, “Thus says the LORD: Take your choice: ^21-11

**21:12** either three years of famine; or three months of devastation by your foes, while the sword of your enemies overtakes you; or three days of the sword of the LORD, pestilence on the land, and the angel of the LORD destroying throughout all the territory of Israel. Now decide what answer I shall return to the one who sent me.” ^21-12

**21:13** Then David said to Gad, “I am in great distress; let me fall into the hand of the LORD, for his mercy is very great, but let me not fall into human hands.” ^21-13

**21:14** So the LORD sent a pestilence on Israel, and seventy thousand persons fell in Israel. ^21-14

**21:15** And God sent an angel to Jerusalem to destroy it, but when he was about to destroy it, the LORD took note and relented concerning the calamity; he said to the destroying angel, “Enough! Stay your hand.” The angel of the LORD was standing by the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite. ^21-15

**21:16** David looked up and saw the angel of the LORD standing between earth and heaven and in his hand a drawn sword stretched out over Jerusalem. Then David and the elders, clothed in sackcloth, fell on their faces. ^21-16

**21:17** And David said to God, “Was it not I who gave the command to count the people? It is I who have sinned and done very wickedly. But these sheep, what have they done? Let your hand, I pray, O LORD my God, be against me and against my father’s house, but do not let your people be plagued!” ^21-17

## David’s altar and sacrifice

**21:18** Then the angel of the LORD commanded Gad to tell David that he should go up and erect an altar to the LORD on the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite. ^21-18

**21:19** So David went up following Gad’s instructions, which he had spoken in the name of the LORD. ^21-19

**21:20** Ornan turned and saw the king,^[Heb ms Gk: MT *angel*] and while his four sons who were with him hid themselves, Ornan continued to thresh wheat. ^21-20

**21:21** As David came to Ornan, Ornan looked and saw David; he went out from the threshing floor and prostrated himself before David with his face to the ground. ^21-21

**21:22** David said to Ornan, “Give me the site of the threshing floor that I may build on it an altar to the LORD—give it to me at its full price—so that the plague may be averted from the people.” ^21-22

**21:23** Then Ornan said to David, “Take it, and let my lord the king do what seems good to him; see, I present the oxen for burnt offerings and the threshing sledges for the wood and the wheat for a grain offering. I give it all.” ^21-23

**21:24** But King David said to Ornan, “No, but I will buy them for the full price. I will not take for the LORD what is yours nor offer burnt offerings that cost me nothing.” ^21-24

**21:25** So David paid Ornan six hundred shekels of gold by weight for the site. ^21-25

**21:26** David built there an altar to the LORD and presented burnt offerings and offerings of well-being. He called upon the LORD, and he answered him with fire from heaven on the altar of burnt offering. ^21-26

**21:27** Then the LORD commanded the angel, and he put his sword back into its sheath. ^21-27

## The place chosen for the temple

**21:28** At that time, when David saw that the LORD had answered him at the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite, he made his sacrifices there. ^21-28

**21:29** For the tabernacle of the LORD that Moses had made in the wilderness and the altar of burnt offering were at that time in the high place at Gibeon, ^21-29

**21:30** but David could not go before it to inquire of God, for he was afraid of the sword of the angel of the LORD. ^21-30
