## Mūla

एवं क्रमेण मूर्धाद्यैरङ्गैरेतत्पुनः पुनः ।
यतश्चिद्धर्म एवासौ शान्त्याद्यात्मा द्विजन्मता ॥ ^mula

evaṃ krameṇa mūrdhādyairaṅgairetatpunaḥ punaḥ |
yataściddharma evāsau śāntyādyātmā dvijanmatā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 09 - Chapter Fifteen, part 8#^pg329|p. 311]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=329>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 15.513cd–514ab.
>
> (The Mantra used for the sacraments consists), in due order, of OM, the Heart, the name (of the disciple) followed by ‘I purify’ (śodhayāmi) along with (SVĀHĀ), the beloved of the fire (agnivallabhā), repeated again and again with the limbs beginning with the Heart. (with 15.510cd–511ab)
>
> As the condition of being ‘twice-born’ is a quality of consciousness, and consists of (the virtues of) tranquillity and the rest, (it comes first); (the disciple’s state of) consciousness as that of Rudra arises successively (after that). (with 15.511cd–512ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* ननु अयं द्विजत्वापत्त्या रुद्रांशापादनाय योग्यो भवेदिति कस्मादुक्तम्?--इत्याशङ्क्य आह--

> यतश्चिद्धर्म एवासौ शान्त्याद्यात्मा द्विजन्मता ॥
> तेन रुद्रतया संवित्तत्क्रमेणैव जायते ।
> येन असौ द्विजन्मता नाम

"शान्तो दान्तो जितक्रोध...................... ।"

इत्यादिदृष्ट्या शान्त्याद्यात्मकत्वादुद्भवन्त्याश्चितः समुपचितिरूप एव धर्मः, तेन यथायथमुपचितत्वेन हेतुना द्विजत्वापादनप्रथनमेव संवित् रुद्रतया जायते तदंशापादनयोग्योऽयं भवेत्--इति भावः ॥
