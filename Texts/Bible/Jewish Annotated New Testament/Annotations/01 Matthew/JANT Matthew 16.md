### 16.1–4: Demand for a sign

*[[NRSVue Matthew 16#^16-1|Text]], p. 41* · ([[Mark 08#^8-11|Mk 8.11–13]]; [[Luke 11#^11-29|Lk 11.29–32]]; [[Luke 12#^12-54|12.54–56]]). ^16-1

**[[NRSVue Matthew 16#^16-1|1]]:** *Sadducees*, aristocrats often linked to the Temple (see “Jewish Movements of the NT Period,” p. 614). Rivals of Pharisees (Josephus, *J.W.* 2.164–66; *Ant*. 13.171–73, 297–98; 18.11,16–17), the pairing here suggests Jesus’ rejection by all Jewish leaders. *Test*, see 4.7n. *Sign from heaven*, divine validation of Jesus’ claims ([[Luke 15#^15-18|Lk 15.18]]; [[Romans 01#^1-18|Rom 1.18]]; see also [[Jeremiah 10#^10-2|Jer 10.2]]; [[Daniel 04#^4-23|Dan 4.26]]; *1 En*. 6.2; 13.8; *t. B. Kamma* 7.5, referencing a voice from Mount Sinai, citing [[Torah 32 - Behar#^25-25|Lev 25.25]]). See 12.38n. ^16-1-2

**[[NRSVue Matthew 16#^16-4|4]]:** *Evil and adulterous generation*, see 12.39n. *Sign of Jonah*, see 12.40n. ^16-4

### 16.5–12: Yeast of the Pharisees and Sadducees

*[[NRSVue Matthew 16#^16-5|Text]], p. 41* · ([[Mark 08#^8-14|Mk 8.14–21]]; [[Luke 12#^12-1|Lk 12.1]]). ^16-5

**[[NRSVue Matthew 16#^16-5|5]]:** *Forgotten to bring any bread*, ironic, given two miraculous multiplications of loaves. ^16-5-2

**[[NRSVue Matthew 16#^16-6|6]]:** *Yeast*, see 13.33n.; here a negative reference to Pharisaic teaching (16.11–12). ^16-6

**[[NRSVue Matthew 16#^16-8|8]]:** *Little faith,* see 6.30n. ^16-8

**[[NRSVue Matthew 16#^16-9|9]]:** *Five loaves*, see 14.30–31. ^16-9

**[[NRSVue Matthew 16#^16-10|10]]:** *Seven loaves*, see 15.32–38. ^16-10

### 16.13–20: Keys of the kingdom

*[[NRSVue Matthew 16#^16-13|Text]], p. 41* · ([[Mark 08#^8-27|Mk 8.27–33]]; [[Luke 09#^9-18|Lk 9.18–22]]). See *Gos. Thom*. 13. ^16-13

**[[NRSVue Matthew 16#^16-13|13]]:** *Caesarea Philippi*, a predominantly Gentile city about twenty-five miles north of the Sea of Galilee. *Son of Man*, see 8.20n. ^16-13-2

**[[NRSVue Matthew 16#^16-14|14]]:** *John the Baptist*, see 14.2. *Elijah*, see 11.14–15n.; [[Malachi 3#^3-23|Mal 3.23–24]]; [[LXX Sirach 48#^48-10|Sir 48.10]]. ^16-14

**[[NRSVue Matthew 16#^16-16|16]]:** *Messiah*, see 1.1n. *Living God*, see 15.31n. ^16-16

**[[NRSVue Matthew 16#^16-17|17]]:** *Flesh and blood*, faith in Jesus is based in revelation, not argument or sign. ^16-17

**[[NRSVue Matthew 16#^16-18|18]]:** *Peter*, a play on Gk “petra,” “rock”; the underlying Aram, *Kepha*, becomes Gk Cephas ([[John 01#^1-42|Jn 1.42]]; [[1 Corinthians 01#^1-12|1 Cor 1.12]], and elsewhere). *This rock*, Christian traditions disagree on whether the “rock” is Peter (leading to claims for the papacy), or his faith. *Church*, Gk “ekklēsia” (see 18.18). The LXX utilizes “ekklēsia” (“assembly”) for the Heb “qahal” (“congregation”); see LXX [[LXX Deuteronomy 04#^4-10|Deut 4.10]]; [[Torah 46 - Eikev#^9-10|9.10]]; [[Torah 48 - Shoftim#^18-16|18.16]]; [[Torah 52 - Vayeilech#^31-30|31.30]]; 2 Sam 7; 1 Chr 17; 1QM 4.10). Matthew is the only canonical Gospel to use this term. *Gates of Hades*, see 11.21n. ^16-18

**[[NRSVue Matthew 16#^16-19|19]]:** *Keys*, linked to knowledge and authority ([[Isaiah 22#^22-22|Isa 22.22]]; [[Luke 11#^11-52|Lk 11.52]]; [[Revelation 03#^3-7|Rev 3.7]]; *2 Bar*. 10.18; *3 Bar*. 11.2; *3 En*. 18.18; *Sifre Deut.* 32.25; [[Shabbat 31#31a|*b. Shabb*. 31a]]-b). *Bound . . . loose*, indicating “forbid and permit” (in a legal sense), according to rabbinic teachings (*m. Pesah.* 4.5; *b. Ter.* 5.4; cf. [[Matthew 18#^18-18|Mt 18.18]]); see 9.17n. ^16-19

**[[NRSVue Matthew 16#^16-20|20]]:** *Not to tell anyone*, messianic secret; see 8.4n. *Magdalan* b Gk *him* c Other ancient authorities lack 2*When it is . . . of the times* d Or *the Christ* e Gk *Petros* f Gk *petra* ^16-20

### 16.21–23: First Passion prediction

*[[NRSVue Matthew 16#^16-21|Text]], p. 42* · ([[Luke 09#^9-22|Lk 9.22]]) — on the term “passion” see 26.1–28.20n. See also 17.22–23; 20.17–19; 26.2. ^16-21

**[[NRSVue Matthew 16#^16-21|21]]:** *Great suffering*, precedes the time of redemption (Isa 52–53; [[Hosea 06#^6-2|Hos 6.2]]; [[Zechariah 13#^13-7|Zech 13.7–9]]; see also [[Daniel 07#^7-23|Dan 7.23]], the fourth beast will devour the earth before such a redemption). *Elders . . . chief priests . . . scribes*, Matthew attributes Jesus’ suffering to Jewish, not Roman, authorities. *Third day* ([[Hosea 06#^6-2|Hos 6.2]]; Jon 1.17; 2.10). *B. Sanh.* 97a, “in the third day, he will raise us up, and we shall live in his sight,” suggests that God would resurrect humanity on the third day following the end of the present age. ^16-21-2

**[[NRSVue Matthew 16#^16-22|22]]:** *God forbid*, Peter does not expect the Messiah to die (see 1.1n.; 3.14n.; see also [[Zechariah 09#^9-9|Zech 9.9]]; [[Daniel 09#^9-26|Dan 9.26]]). ^16-22

**[[NRSVue Matthew 16#^16-23|23]]:** *Satan*, Peter is cast in the role of tempter, as is Satan (4.10). *Stumbling block*, Gk “skandalon.” ^16-23

### 16.24–28: Jesus calls the disciples again

*[[NRSVue Matthew 16#^16-24|Text]], p. 42* · ([[Mark 08#^8-34|Mk 8.34–9.1]]; [[Luke 09#^9-23|Lk 9.23–27]]; [[John 12#^12-25|Jn 12.25]]). See 4.18–22; 10.38. ^16-24

**[[NRSVue Matthew 16#^16-24|24]]:** See [[Luke 14#^14-27|Lk 14.27]]. *Take up their cross*, see 10.38n. ^16-24-2

**[[NRSVue Matthew 16#^16-25|25]]:** *Will find it*, eternal life. The statement is a paradox: holding tightly to something risks losing it; letting it go preserves it. A similar parallel regarding life and death is found in rabbinic literature (see *Avot de R. Natan* B 35, “Everyone who preserves one thing from the Torah preserves his life, and everyone who loses one thing from the Torah will lose his life”). ^16-25

**[[NRSVue Matthew 16#^16-27|27]]:** *Son of Man*, see 8.20n. ^16-27

**[[NRSVue Matthew 16#^16-28|28]]:** *Will not taste death*, the messianic era is imminent ([[Mark 09#^9-1|Mk 9.1]]; [[Luke 09#^9-27|Lk 9.27]]; see also [[John 08#^8-51|Jn 8.51–52]]; [[1 Thessalonians 04#^4-15|1 Thess 4.15]]; see 28.20n.). ^16-28
