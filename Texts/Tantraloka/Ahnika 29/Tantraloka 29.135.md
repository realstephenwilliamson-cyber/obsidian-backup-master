## Mūla

तिष्ठत्युपरतवृत्तिः शून्यालम्बी निरानन्दः ।
योऽप्यनुचक्रदृगादिस्वरूपभाक् सोऽपि यत्तदायत्तः ॥ ^mula

tiṣṭhatyuparatavṛttiḥ śūnyālambī nirānandaḥ |
yo'pyanucakradṛgādisvarūpabhāk so'pi yattadāyattaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 11 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 3#^pg340|p. 323]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=340>))
>
> By attaining that tranquil state, the activity of the entire troupe of deities that resides in the (central) Wheel ceases, and abiding (thus) suspended in the Void, is devoid of (outer) bliss (nirānanda). (with 29.134cd–135ab)
>
> That which resides in the subordinate Wheels of sight and the rest (of the senses) possesses (their same) nature, and it too depends on that (primary Wheel of consciousness); (immobile,) it is immersed in (that state) devoid of (outer) bliss, yearning for bliss. (with 29.135cd–136ab)

*Lead-in:* न केवलं मध्यचक्रस्थ एव देवतागण एवमास्ते, यावदनुचक्रस्थोऽपि--इत्याह--

> योऽप्यनुचक्रदृगादिस्वरूपभाक् सोऽपि यत्तदायत्तः ॥
> तेनानन्दे मग्नस्तिष्ठत्यानन्दसाकींङ्क्षः ।

अनुचक्रदृगादिस्वरूपभागिति--अर्थात् देवतागणः । तदायत्त इति-- मुख्यचक्रस्थदेवतागणवशः । तेनेति--तदायत्तत्वेन ॥
