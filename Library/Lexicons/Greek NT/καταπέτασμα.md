# καταπέτασμα

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · noun

**Dodson:** a veil, curtain — (lit: that which is spread out downwards, that which hangs down), a curtain, veil, of that which separated the Holy of Holies from the outer parts of the temple at Jerusalem, also of an outer curtain at the entrance to the Holy Place in the same temple. (Strong's 2665; headword καταπέτασμα, ατος, τό)
