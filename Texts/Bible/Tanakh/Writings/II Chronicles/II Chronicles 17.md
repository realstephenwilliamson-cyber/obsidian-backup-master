# II Chronicles 17

Compare: [[NRSVue 2 Chronicles 17|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 17

**17:1** His son Jehoshaphat succeeded him as king, and took firm hold of Israel. ^17-1

**17:2** He stationed troops in all the fortified towns of Judah, and stationed garrisons throughout the land of Judah and the cities of Ephraim which his father Asa had captured. ^17-2

**17:3** The LORD was with Jehoshaphat because he followed the earlier ways of his father David, and did not worship the Baalim, ^17-3

**17:4** but worshiped the God of his father and followed His commandments—unlike the behavior of Israel. ^17-4

**17:5** So the LORD established the kingdom in his hands, and all Judah gave presents to Jehoshaphat. He had wealth and glory in abundance. ^17-5

**17:6** His mind was elevated in the ways of the LORD. Moreover, he abolished the shrines and the sacred posts from Judah. ^17-6

**17:7** In the third year of his reign he sent his officers Ben-hail, Obadiah, Zechariah, Nethanel, and Micaiah throughout the cities of Judah to offer instruction. ^17-7

**17:8** With them were the Levites, Shemaiah, Nethaniah, Zebadiah, Asahel, Shemiramoth, Jehonathan, Adonijah, Tobijah and Tob-adonijah the Levites; with them were Elishama and Jehoram the priests. ^17-8

**17:9** They offered instruction throughout Judah, having with them the Book of the Teaching of the LORD. They made the rounds of all the cities of Judah and instructed the people. ^17-9

**17:10** A terror of the LORD seized all the kingdoms of the lands around Judah, and they did not go to war with Jehoshaphat. ^17-10

**17:11** From Philistia a load of silver was brought to Jehoshaphat as tribute. The Arabs, too, brought him flocks: 7,700 rams and 7,700 he-goats. ^17-11

**17:12** Jehoshaphat grew greater and greater, and he built up fortresses and garrison towns in Judah. ^17-12

**17:13** He carried out extensive works in the towns of Judah, and had soldiers, valiant men, in Jerusalem. ^17-13

**17:14** They were enrolled according to their clans. Judah: chiefs of thousands, Adnah the chief, who had 300,000 valiant men; ^17-14

**17:15** next to him was Jehohanan the captain, who had 280,000; ^17-15

**17:16** next to him was Amasiah son of Zichri, who made a freewill offering to the LORD. He had 200,000 valiant men. ^17-16

**17:17** Benjamin: Eliada, a valiant man, who had 200,000 men armed with bow and buckler; ^17-17

**17:18** next to him was Jehozabad, who had 180,000 armed men. ^17-18

**17:19** These served the king, besides those whom the king assigned to the fortified towns throughout Judah. ^17-19
