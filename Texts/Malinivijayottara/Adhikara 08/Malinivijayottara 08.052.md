## Mūla

ना चासंशोधितं वस्तु किंचिदप्यत्र कल्पयेत् ।
तेन शुद्धं तु सर्वं यदशुद्धमपि तच्छुचि ॥ ^mula

nā cāsaṁśodhitaṁ vastu kiṁcidapyatra[^55] kalpayet |
tena śuddhaṁ tu sarvaṁ[^56] yadaśuddhamapi tacchuci || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, October 2024 – December 2024
> p. 29 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=28>))
>
> One should not think (kalpayet) that any substance, whatever it be (that is offered) here (in this rite), has not been purified (by it). Purified by that, everything, even what is impure, (becomes) pure.[^57]

[^55]: Variant reading: *kiṁcidapyupakalpayed*
[^56]: k: *pu0 saṁśodhitaṁ ca yatsarvam*
[^57]: MV 8/52 is quoted in TĀ 15/162cd-163ab. The reading in the KSTS edition of the MV 8/52b is *atra kalpayet* whereas in TĀ 15/162d it is *upakalpayet.*
