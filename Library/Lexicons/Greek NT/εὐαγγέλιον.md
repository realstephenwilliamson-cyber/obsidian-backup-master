# εὐαγγέλιον

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · noun

**Dodson:** the good news, the gospel — the good news of the coming of the Messiah, the gospel; the gen. after it expresses sometimes the giver (God), sometimes the subject (the Messiah, etc.), sometimes the human transmitter (an apostle). (Strong's 2098; headword εὐαγγέλιον, ου, τό)
