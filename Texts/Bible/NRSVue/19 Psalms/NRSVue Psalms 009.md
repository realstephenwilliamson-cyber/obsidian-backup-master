## God’s power and justice

*To the leader: according to Muth-labben. A Psalm of David.*

**9:1** ^[Psalms 9–10 were originally one psalm, as in the Greek and Latin traditions. In Hebrew, Psalms 9–10 formed an acrostic.]I will give thanks to the LORD with my whole heart; I will tell of all your wonderful deeds. ^9-1

**9:2** I will be glad and exult in you; I will sing praise to your name, O Most High. ^9-2

**9:3** When my enemies turned back, they stumbled and perished before you. ^9-3

**9:4** For you have maintained my just cause; you have sat on the throne giving righteous judgment. ^9-4

**9:5** You have rebuked the nations; you have destroyed the wicked; you have blotted out their name forever and ever. ^9-5

**9:6** The enemies have vanished in everlasting ruins; their cities you have rooted out; the very memory of them has perished. ^9-6

**9:7** But the LORD sits enthroned forever; he has established his throne for judgment. ^9-7

**9:8** He judges the world with righteousness; he judges the peoples with equity. ^9-8

**9:9** The LORD is a stronghold for the oppressed, a stronghold in times of trouble. ^9-9

**9:10** And those who know your name put their trust in you, for you, O LORD, have not forsaken those who seek you. ^9-10

**9:11** Sing praises to the LORD, who dwells in Zion. Declare his deeds among the peoples. ^9-11

**9:12** For he who avenges blood is mindful of them; he does not forget the cry of the afflicted. ^9-12

**9:13** Be gracious to me, O LORD. See what I suffer from those who hate me; you are the one who lifts me up from the gates of death, ^9-13

**9:14** so that I may recount all your praises and, in the gates of daughter Zion, rejoice in your deliverance. ^9-14

**9:15** The nations have sunk in the pit that they made; in the net that they hid has their own foot been caught. ^9-15

**9:16** The LORD has made himself known; he has executed judgment; the wicked are snared in the work of their own hands. *Higgaion. Selah* ^9-16

**9:17** The wicked shall depart to Sheol, all the nations that forget God. ^9-17

**9:18** For the needy shall not always be forgotten, nor the hope of the poor perish forever. ^9-18

**9:19** Rise up, O LORD! Do not let mortals prevail; let the nations be judged before you. ^9-19

**9:20** Put them in fear, O LORD; let the nations know that they are only human. *Selah* ^9-20
