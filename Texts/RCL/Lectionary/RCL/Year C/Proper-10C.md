## Complementary track

> [!note]- About the two tracks
> After Pentecost the Revised Common Lectionary offers two sets of Old Testament readings and psalms. The set under Readings is the semicontinuous one, which follows a book from Sunday to Sunday (Track 1 in the Episcopal Church). The complementary set below is chosen to go with the gospel (Track 2). The second reading and the gospel are the same in both. Once a set has been chosen, it should be followed to the end of the season.
