## Mūla

शूलदण्डचतुष्कं च तत्राद्यं द्वयसंस्थिमुस्थितम् ।
वामपादं च तस्यान्ते कपालं पतितं न्यसेत् ॥ ^mula

śūladaṇḍacatuṣkaṁ ca tatrādyaṁ dvayasaṁsthimusthitam |
vāmapādaṁ ca tasyānte kapālaṁ patitaṁ nyaset || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2023 – March 2023
> p. 48 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=48>))
>
> (Extract) four staffs (R) of the trident, the first pair of which is united with the right ornament (U)[^36] (whereas the second pair is not) (RU RU RA RA). (Then,) at the end of it, deposit the left foot (PH) (onto which) the skull (Ṭ) has fallen (PHAṬ). Then (finally the syllable) that comes after PARAMAGHORE (i.e., HUṀ),[^37] and as before, the foot (PH) with the skull (Ṭ) (PHAṬ). (with 3.49–50ab)

[^36]: Read *dvayam usthitam* for *dvayasaṁsthi(mutthi)tam*.
[^37]: The following HAḤ seems to have been omitted.
