# Job 40 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Job 39|← Job 39]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Job 41|Job 41 →]]

**40:1** And the Lord answering Job out of the whirlwind, said: ^40-1

**40:2** Gird up thy loins like a man: I will ask thee, and do thou tell me. ^40-2

**40:3** Wilt thou make void my judgment: and condemn me, that thou mayst be justified? ^40-3

**40:4** And hast thou an arm like God, and canst thou thunder with a voice like him? ^40-4

**40:5** Clothe thyself with beauty, and set thyself up on high and be glorious, and put on goodly garments. ^40-5

**40:6** Scatter the proud in thy indignation, and behold every arrogant man, and humble him. ^40-6

**40:7** Look on all that are proud, and confound them, and crush the wicked in their place. ^40-7

**40:8** Hide them in the dust together, and plunge their faces into the pit. ^40-8

**40:9** Then I will confess that thy right hand is able to save thee. ^40-9

**40:10** Behold behemoth whom I made with thee, he eateth grass like an ox. ^40-10

**40:11** His strength is in his loins, and his force in the navel of his belly. ^40-11

**40:12** He setteth up his tail like a cedar, the sinews of his testicles are wrapped together. ^40-12

**40:13** His bones are like pipes of brass, his gristle like plates of iron. ^40-13

**40:14** He is the beginning of the ways of God, who made him, he will apply his sword. ^40-14

**40:15** To him the mountains bring forth grass: there all the beasts of the field shall play. ^40-15

**40:16** He sleepeth under the shadow, in the covert of the reed, and in moist places. ^40-16

**40:17** The shades cover his shadow, the willows of the brook shall compass him about. ^40-17

**40:18** Behold, he will drink up a river, and not wonder: and he trusteth that the Jordan may run into his mouth. ^40-18

**40:19** In his eyes as with a hook he shall take him, and bore through his nostrils with stakes. ^40-19

**40:20** Canst thou draw out the leviathan with a hook, or canst thou tie his tongue with a cord? ^40-20

**40:21** Canst thou put a ring in his nose, or bore through his jaw with a buckle? ^40-21

**40:22** Will he make many supplications to thee, or speak soft words to thee? ^40-22

**40:23** Will he make a covenant with thee, and wilt thou take him to be a servant for ever? ^40-23

**40:24** Shalt thou play with him as with a bird, or tie him up for thy handmaids? ^40-24

**40:25** Shall friends cut him in pieces, shall merchants divide him? ^40-25

**40:26** Wilt thou fill nets with his skin, and the cabins of fishes with his head? ^40-26

**40:27** Lay thy hand upon him: remember the battle, and speak no more. ^40-27

**40:28** Behold his hope shall fail him, and in the sight of all he shall be cast down. ^40-28
