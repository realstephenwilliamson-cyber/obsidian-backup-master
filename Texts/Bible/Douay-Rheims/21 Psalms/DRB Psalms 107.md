# Psalms 107 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 106|← Psalms 106]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 108|Psalms 108 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 107|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**107:1** A canticle of a psalm for David himself. ^107-1

**107:2** My heart is ready, O God, my heart is ready: I will sing, and will give praise, with my glory. ^107-2

**107:3** Arise, my glory; arise, psaltery and harp: I will arise in the morning early. ^107-3

**107:4** I will praise thee, O Lord, among the people: and I will sing unto thee among the populations. ^107-4

**107:5** For thy mercy is great above the heavens: and thy truth even unto the clouds. ^107-5

**107:6** Be thou exalted, O God, above the heavens, and thy glory over all the earth: ^107-6

**107:7** That thy beloved may be delivered. Save with thy right hand and hear me. ^107-7

**107:8** God hath spoken in his holiness. I will rejoice, and I will divide Sichem and I will mete out the vale of tabernacles. ^107-8

**107:9** Galaad is mine, and Manasses is mine and Ephraim the protection of my head. Juda is my king: ^107-9

**107:10** Moab the pot of my hope. Over Edom I will stretch out my shoe: the aliens are become my friends. ^107-10

**107:11** Who will bring me into the strong city? who will lead me into Edom? ^107-11

**107:12** Wilt not thou, O God, who hast cast us off? and wilt not thou, O God, go forth with our armies? ^107-12

**107:13** O grant us help from trouble: for vain is the help of man. ^107-13

**107:14** Through God we shall do mightily: and he will bring our enemies to nothing. ^107-14
