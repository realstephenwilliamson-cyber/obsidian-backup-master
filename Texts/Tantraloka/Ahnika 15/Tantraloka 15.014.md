## Mūla

यस्य त्वीशप्रसादेन दिव्या काचन योग्यता ।
गुरोः शिशोश्च तौ नैव प्रति दीक्षोपयोगिता ॥ ^mula

yasya tvīśaprasādena divyā kācana yogyatā |
guroḥ śiśośca tau naiva prati dīkṣopayogitā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII

> (If) by the Lord’s grace, a teacher and disciple possess a certain (extraordinary) divine competence (yogitā) (to realise oneness), initiation is of no use to them.
