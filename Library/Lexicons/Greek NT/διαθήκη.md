# διαθήκη

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · noun

**Dodson:** a covenant, will, testament — (a) a covenant between two parties, (b) (the ordinary, everyday sense [found a countless number of times in papyri]) a will, testament. (Strong's 1242; headword διαθήκη, ης, ἡ)
