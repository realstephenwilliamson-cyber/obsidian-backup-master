## Chapter 77

**77:1** For the leader; on *Jeduthun*.  
Of Asaph. A psalm. ^77-1

**77:2** I cry aloud to God;  
I cry to God that He may give ear to me. ^77-2

**77:3** In my time of distress I turn to the Lord,  
with my hand [uplifted];  
[my eyes] flow all night without respite;[^1]  
I will not be comforted. ^77-3

**77:4** I call God to mind, I moan,  
I complain, my spirit fails. *Selah*. ^77-4

**77:5** You have held my eyelids open;  
I am overwrought, I cannot speak. ^77-5

**77:6** My thoughts turn to days of old,  
to years long past. ^77-6

**77:7** I recall at night their jibes at me;  
I commune with myself;  
my spirit inquires, ^77-7

**77:8** “Will the Lord reject forever  
and never again show favor? ^77-8

**77:9** Has His faithfulness disappeared forever?  
Will His promise be unfulfilled for all time? ^77-9

**77:10** Has God forgotten how to pity?  
Has He in anger stifled His compassion?” *Selah*. ^77-10

**77:11** And I said, “It is my fault  
that the right hand of the Most High has changed.”[^2] ^77-11

**77:12** I recall the deeds of the LORD;  
yes, I recall Your wonders of old; ^77-12

**77:13** I recount all Your works;  
I speak of Your acts. ^77-13

**77:14** O God, Your ways are holiness;  
what god is as great as God? ^77-14

**77:15** You are the God who works wonders;  
You have manifested Your strength among the peoples. ^77-15

**77:16** By Your arm You redeemed Your people,  
the children of Jacob and Joseph. *Selah*. ^77-16

**77:17** The waters saw You, O God,  
the waters saw You and were convulsed;  
the very deep quaked as well. ^77-17

**77:18** Clouds streamed water;  
the heavens rumbled;  
Your arrows flew about; ^77-18

**77:19** Your thunder rumbled like wheels;  
lightning lit up the world;  
the earth quaked and trembled. ^77-19

**77:20** Your way was through the sea,  
Your path, through the mighty waters;  
Your tracks could not be seen. ^77-20

**77:21** You led Your people like a flock  
in the care of Moses and Aaron. ^77-21

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
