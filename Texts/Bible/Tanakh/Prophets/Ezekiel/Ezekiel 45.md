## Chapter 45

**45:1** When you allot the land as an inheritance, you shall set aside from the land, as a gift sacred to the LORD, an area[^1] 25,000 [cubits] long and 10,000[^2] wide: this shall be holy through its entire extent. ^45-1

**45:2** Of this, a square measuring a full 500 by 500 shall be reserved for the Sanctuary,[^3] and 50 cubits for an open space all around it. ^45-2

**45:3** Of the aforesaid area, you shall measure off, as most holy and destined to include the Sanctuary, [a space] 25,000 long by 10,000 wide; ^45-3

**45:4** it is a sacred portion of the land; it shall provide space for houses for the priests, the ministrants of the Sanctuary who are qualified to minister to the LORD, as well as holy ground for the Sanctuary. ^45-4

**45:5** Another [space], 25,000 long by 10,000 wide, shall be the property of the Levites, the servants of the Temple—twenty chambers.[^4] ^45-5

**45:6** Alongside the sacred reserve, you shall set aside [a space] 25,000 long by 5,000 wide, as the property of the city; it shall belong to the whole House of Israel. ^45-6

**45:7** And to the prince shall belong, on both sides of the sacred reserve and the property of the city and alongside the sacred reserve and the property of the city, on the west extending westward and on the east extending eastward, a portion[^5] corresponding to one of the [tribal] portions that extend from the western border to the eastern border ^45-7

**45:8** of the land.[^6] That shall be his property in Israel; and My princes shall no more defraud My people, but shall leave the rest of the land to the several tribes of the House of Israel. ^45-8

**45:9** Thus said the Lord GOD: Enough, princes of Israel! Make an end of lawlessness and rapine, and do what is right and just! Put a stop to your evictions of My people—declares the Lord GOD. ^45-9

**45:10** Have honest balances, an honest *ephah*, and an honest *bath*.[^7] ^45-10

**45:11** The *ephah* and the *bath* shall comprise the same volume, the *bath* a tenth of a *ḥomer* and the *ephah* a tenth of a *ḥomer*; their capacity shall be gauged by the *ḥomer*. ^45-11

**45:12** And the shekel shall weigh 20 *gerah*s. 20 shekels, 25 shekels [and] 10 plus 5 shekels shall count with you as a *mina*.[^8] ^45-12

**45:13** This is the contribution you shall make: One-sixth of an *ephah* from every *ḥomer* of wheat and one-sixth of an *ephah* from every *ḥomer* of barley, ^45-13

**45:14** while the due from the oil—the oil being measured by the *bath*[^9]—shall be one-tenth of a *bath* from every *kor*.—As 10 *bath*s make a *ḥomer*, so 10 *bath*s make a *ḥomer*.[^10]— ^45-14

**45:15** And [the due] from the flock shall be one animal from every 200. [All these shall be contributed] from Israel’s products[^11] for meal offerings, burnt offerings, and offerings of well-being, to make expiation for them—declares the Lord GOD. ^45-15

**45:16** In this contribution, the entire population must join with the prince in Israel. ^45-16

**45:17** But the burnt offerings, the meal offerings, and the libations on festivals, new moons, sabbaths—all fixed occasions—of the House of Israel shall be the obligation of the prince; he shall provide the sin offerings, the meal offerings, the burnt offerings, and the offerings of well-being, to make expiation for the House of Israel. ^45-17

**45:18** Thus said the Lord GOD: On the first day of the first month, you shall take a bull of the herd without blemish, and you shall cleanse the Sanctuary. ^45-18

**45:19** The priest shall take some of the blood of the sin offering and apply it to the doorposts of the Temple, to the four corners of the ledge[^12] of the altar, and to the doorposts of the gate of the inner court. ^45-19

**45:20** You shall do the same on the seventh day of the month[^13] to purge the Temple from uncleanness caused by unwitting or ignorant persons. ^45-20

**45:21** On the fourteenth day of the first month you shall have the passover sacrifice; and during a festival of seven days unleavened bread shall be eaten. ^45-21

**45:22** On that day, the prince shall provide a bull of sin offering on behalf of himself and of the entire population; ^45-22

**45:23** and during the seven days of the festival, he shall provide daily—for seven days—seven bulls and seven rams, without blemish, for a burnt offering to the LORD, and one goat daily for a sin offering. ^45-23

**45:24** He shall provide a meal offering of an *ephah*[^14] for each bull and an *ephah* for each ram, with a *hin* of oil to every *ephah*. ^45-24

**45:25** So, too, during the festival of the seventh month, for seven days from the fifteenth day on, he shall provide the same sin offerings, burnt offerings, meal offerings, and oil. ^45-25

[^1]: Lit. “length.”
[^2]: Septuagint reads 20,000; cf. vv. 3–5.
[^3]: Cf. 42.15 ff.
[^4]: Septuagint reads “for towns to dwell in.”
[^5]: Lit. “length.”
[^6]: Cf. for all the foregoing 48.1 ff.
[^7]: The ephah is used for dry measure and the bath for liquid measure.
[^8]: The Mesopotamian mina of 60 shekels; but meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^9]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^10]: The Vulgate reads “kor”; ḥomer and kor are synonyms.
[^11]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^12]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^13]: Septuagint reads “in the seventh month.”
[^14]: Of choice flour.
