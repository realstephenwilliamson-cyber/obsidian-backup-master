# εἰρήνη

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · noun

**Dodson:** peace, peace of mind — peace, peace of mind; invocation of peace a common Jewish farewell, in the Hebraistic sense of the health (welfare) of an individual. (Strong's 1515; headword εἰρήνη, ης, ἡ)
