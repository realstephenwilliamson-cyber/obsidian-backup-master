## Mūla

तत्त्वानि सप्त बोधिन्या तच्चतुर्था पुराणि च ।
तृतीये सप्त वर्णाः स्युः पदमन्त्रद्वयम्द्वयम् ॥ ^mula

tattvāni sapta bodhinyā taccaturthā purāṇi ca |
tṛtīye sapta varṇāḥ syuḥ padamantradvayam dvayam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, October 2022 – December 2022
> p. 26 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=33>))
>
> C) Seven principles (from Puruṣa to Māyā) are (pervaded by) the Enlightening One, and four times that (the number of) worlds. In the third (Egg) there are seven phonemes, and there are two Padas and two Mantras.[^73]

[^73]: The seven phonemes are Ñ, Jh, J, Ch, C, Y, Gh. The two Padas and two Mantras are of five and two syllables each i.e *ñajhajachaca, ṅagha.*
