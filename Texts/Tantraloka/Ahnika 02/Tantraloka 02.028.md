## Mūla

न सन्न चासत्सदसन्न च तन्नोभयोज्झितम् ।

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. II
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 02 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v02 03 - Chapter Two, part 2#^pg51|p. 35]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 02.pdf#page=51>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 2.27cd–28ab.
>
> Neither (empirical) existence nor non-existence, neither both existence and non-existence nor devoid of both – that state is hard to know (indeed) – what could this (undefinable) Absolute (Anuttara) be? Manifestation (in the form of) ‘this’, which is limited by nature, is an existing thing (bhāva) in the world, as is a jar, and is (what) exists – this Bhairava is not such. (with 2.28–29)
