## Mūla

शेषं वामकरेणैव पूजाहोमजपादिकम् ।
एवमानन्दसंपूर्णं सर्वौन्मुख्यविवर्जितम् ॥ ^mula

śeṣaṃ vāmakareṇaiva pūjāhomajapādikam |
evamānandasaṃpūrṇaṃ sarvaunmukhyavivarjitam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 06 - Chapter Fifteen, part 5#^pg186|p. 168]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=186>))
>
> It is said in the venerable Nandisikhā and Ānandaśāsana that one must use the two sacrificial ladles, large (sruk) and small (sruva), (held together with both hands,) to make the full oblation (pārṇāhuti) of clarified butter. All other (rites, including) worship, oblation, repetition of Mantra and the rest (should be done) only with the left hand. (with 15.282–283ab)
>
> After having in this way generated a body for the sacrifice (yāgena) which is completely full of bliss and devoid of all propensity (to move out of itself), (the adept) should meditate on it is as Śiva (śivātmakam). (with 15.283cd–284ab)
