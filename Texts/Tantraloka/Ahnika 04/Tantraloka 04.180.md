## Mūla

क्रमाक्रमकथातीतं संवित्तत्त्वं सुनिर्मलम् ।
तदस्याः संविदो देव्या यत्र क्वापि प्रवर्तनम् ॥ ^mula

kramākramakathātītaṃ saṃvittattvaṃ sunirmalam |
tadasyāḥ saṃvido devyā yatra kvāpi pravartanam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. III
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 03 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v03 09 - Chapter Four, part 8#^pg288|p. 275]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 03.pdf#page=288>))
>
> In this way, consciousness, that abides twelve-fold within all (perceiving) subjects (mātr), is one only, and so is not subject in any way to succession (krama). Again, because there is no succession (of differing elements), there is also no simultaneity (between the appearance of differing elements), and as there is no (simultaneity), there is also no succession. The principle of consciousness, extremely pure, transcends all talk of succession and its absence (kramākramakathātīta). (with 4.178cd–180ab)
>
> Worship is complete (and perfect) (pūrnā) by virtue of the union (yoga) of the oneness (of all that is perceived within consciousness as one with it), wherever this Goddess of Consciousness operates. (with 4.180cd–181ab)
