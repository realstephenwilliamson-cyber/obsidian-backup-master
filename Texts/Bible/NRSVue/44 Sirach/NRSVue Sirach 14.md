# Sirach 14 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Sirach 13|← Sirach 13]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Sirach 15|Sirach 15 →]]

**14:1** Happy are those who do not blunder with their lips and are not stricken with grief for sin. ^14-1

**14:2** Happy are those whose hearts do not condemn them and who have not given up their hope. ^14-2

## Responsible use of wealth

**14:3** Riches are inappropriate for the small-minded, and of what use is wealth to misers? ^14-3

**14:4** What they deny themselves they collect for others, and others will live in luxury on their goods. ^14-4

**14:5** If they are mean to themselves, to whom will they be generous? They will not enjoy their own riches. ^14-5

**14:6** No one is worse than those who are grudging to themselves; this is the repayment for their meanness. ^14-6

**14:7** If ever they do good, it is by mistake,^[Syr: Gk *through forgetfulness*] and in the end they reveal their meanness. ^14-7

**14:8** Misers are evil people; they turn away and disregard people. ^14-8

**14:9** The eyes of the greedy are not satisfied with their share; greedy injustice withers the soul. ^14-9

**14:10** Misers begrudge bread, and it is lacking at their tables. ^14-10

**14:11** My child, treat yourself well, according to your means, and present worthy offerings to the Lord. ^14-11

**14:12** Remember that death does not tarry, and the decree^[Heb Syr: Gk *covenant*] of Hades has not been shown to you. ^14-12

**14:13** Do good to friends before you die, and reach out and give to them as much as you can. ^14-13

**14:14** Do not deprive yourself of a day’s enjoyment; do not let your share of desired good pass by you. ^14-14

**14:15** Will you not leave the fruit of your labors to another, and what you acquired by toil to be divided by lot? ^14-15

**14:16** Give and take and indulge yourself, because in Hades one cannot look for luxury. ^14-16

**14:17** All living beings become old like a garment, for the decree^[Heb: Gk *covenant*] from of old is, “You must die!” ^14-17

**14:18** Like abundant leaves on a spreading tree that sheds some and puts forth others, so are the generations of flesh and blood: one dies, and another is born. ^14-18

**14:19** Every work decays and ceases to exist, and the one who made it will pass away with it. ^14-19

## The happiness of seeking wisdom

**14:20** Happy are those who meditate on^[Other ancient authorities read *die in*] wisdom and who reason intelligently, ^14-20

**14:21** who reflect in their hearts on her ways and ponder her secrets, ^14-21

**14:22** pursuing her like a hunter and lying in wait on her paths; ^14-22

**14:23** who peer through her windows and listen at her doors; ^14-23

**14:24** who camp near her house and fasten their tent pegs to her walls; ^14-24

**14:25** who pitch their tents near her and so occupy an excellent lodging place; ^14-25

**14:26** who place their children under her shelter and lodge under her boughs; ^14-26

**14:27** who are sheltered by her from the heat and dwell in the midst of her glory. ^14-27
