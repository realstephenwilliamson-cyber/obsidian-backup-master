## Mūla

तथाप्यतत्त्वविद्वर्गानुग्रहाय तथा चरेत् ।
श्रीपिचौ च स्मृतेरेव पापघ्नत्वे कथं विभो ॥ ^mula

tathāpyatattvavidvargānugrahāya tathā caret |
śrīpicau ca smṛtereva pāpaghnatve kathaṃ vibho || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 07 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 5#^pg206|p. 189]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=206>))
>
> A special rite of worship (pūjana) should be performed when one has been acquitted from (a transgression) of the rules. Although no rites of reparation (prāyaścitta) (are necessary or have any significance) for one whose mind is free of thought, nonetheless he should perform them, so as to grace in that way (the many) who do not know the true nature of things (tattva). (with 28.408cd–409ab)
>
> Indeed, the goddess asked in the venerable Picu: “O Lord, as recollection alone (is sufficient) to destroy sin, why is the procedure for the rite of reparation (prāyaścitta) taught (here)?” (The Lord replied saying) “(That is) true. Here (in this world) just the recollection (of Mantra), repeated once, liberates one who knows the power of the true nature of recollection from all the net of Karma. Even so, the enjoined procedure must be observed, (not just for oneself, but) in order to protect the stability (of the world).” (with 28.409cd–411)
