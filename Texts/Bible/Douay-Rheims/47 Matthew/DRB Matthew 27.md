# Matthew 27 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Matthew 26|← Matthew 26]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Matthew 28|Matthew 28 →]]

Compare: [[Matthew 27|BSB and SBLGNT]]

**27:1** And when morning was come, all the chief priests and ancients of the people took counsel against Jesus, that they might put him to death. ^27-1

**27:2** And they brought him bound, and delivered him to Pontius Pilate the governor. ^27-2

**27:3** Then Judas, who betrayed him, seeing that he was condemned, repenting himself, brought back the thirty pieces of silver to the chief priests and ancients, ^27-3

**27:4** Saying: I have sinned in betraying innocent blood. But they said: What is that to us? look thou to it. ^27-4

**27:5** And casting down the pieces of silver in the temple, he departed: and went and hanged himself with an halter. ^27-5

**27:6** But the chief priests having taken the pieces of silver, said: It is not lawful to put them into the corbona, because it is the price of blood. ^27-6

**27:7** And after they had consulted together, they bought with them the potter’s field, to be a burying place for strangers. ^27-7

**27:8** For this cause the field was called Haceldama, that is, The field of blood, even to this day. ^27-8

**27:9** Then was fulfilled that which was spoken by Jeremias the prophet, saying: And they took the thirty pieces of silver, the price of him that was prized, whom they prized of the children of Israel. ^27-9

**27:10** And they gave them unto the potter’s field, as the Lord appointed to me. ^27-10

**27:11** And Jesus stood before the governor, and the governor asked him, saying: Art thou the king of the Jews? Jesus saith to him: Thou sayest it. ^27-11

**27:12** And when he was accused by the chief priests and ancients, he answered nothing. ^27-12

**27:13** Then Pilate saith to him: Dost not thou hear how great testimonies they allege against thee? ^27-13

**27:14** And he answered him to never a word; so that the governor wondered exceedingly. ^27-14

**27:15** Now upon the solemn day the governor was accustomed to release to the people one prisoner, whom they would. ^27-15

**27:16** And he had then a notorious prisoner, that was called Barabbas. ^27-16

**27:17** They therefore being gathered together, Pilate said: Whom will you that I release to you, Barabbas, or Jesus that is called Christ? ^27-17

**27:18** For he knew that for envy they had delivered him. ^27-18

**27:19** And as he was sitting in the place of judgment, his wife sent to him, saying: Have thou nothing to do with that just man; for I have suffered many things this day in a dream because of him. ^27-19

**27:20** But the chief priests and ancients persuaded the people, that they should ask Barabbas, and make Jesus away. ^27-20

**27:21** And the governor answering, said to them: Whether will you of the two to be released unto you? But they said, Barabbas. ^27-21

**27:22** Pilate saith to them: What shall I do then with Jesus that is called Christ? They say all: Let him be crucified. ^27-22

**27:23** The governor said to them: Why, what evil hath he done? But they cried out the more, saying: Let him be crucified. ^27-23

**27:24** And Pilate seeing that he prevailed nothing, but that rather a tumult was made; taking water washed his hands before the people, saying: I am innocent of the blood of this just man; look you to it. ^27-24

**27:25** And the whole people answering, said: His blood be upon us and our children. ^27-25

**27:26** Then he released to them Barabbas, and having scourged Jesus, delivered him unto them to be crucified. ^27-26

**27:27** Then the soldiers of the governor taking Jesus into the hall, gathered together unto him the whole band; ^27-27

**27:28** And stripping him, they put a scarlet cloak about him. ^27-28

**27:29** And platting a crown of thorns, they put it upon his head, and a reed in his right hand. And bowing the knee before him, they mocked him, saying: Hail, king of the Jews. ^27-29

**27:30** And spitting upon him, they took the reed, and struck his head. ^27-30

**27:31** And after they had mocked him, they took off the cloak from him, and put on him his own garments, and led him away to crucify him. ^27-31

**27:32** And going out, they found a man of Cyrene, named Simon: him they forced to take up his cross. ^27-32

**27:33** And they came to the place that is called Golgotha, which is the place of Calvary. ^27-33

**27:34** And they gave him wine to drink mingled with gall. And when he had tasted, he would not drink. ^27-34

**27:35** And after they had crucified him, they divided his garments, casting lots; that it might be fulfilled which was spoken by the prophet, saying: They divided my garments among them; and upon my vesture they cast lots. ^27-35

**27:36** And they sat and watched him. ^27-36

**27:37** And they put over his head his cause written: THIS IS JESUS THE KING OF THE JEWS. ^27-37

**27:38** Then were crucified with him two thieves: one on the right hand, and one on the left. ^27-38

**27:39** And they that passed by, blasphemed him, wagging their heads, ^27-39

**27:40** And saying: Vah, thou that destroyest the temple of God, and in three days dost rebuild it: save thy own self: if thou be the Son of God, come down from the cross. ^27-40

**27:41** In like manner also the chief priests, with the scribes and ancients, mocking, said: ^27-41

**27:42** He saved others; himself he cannot save. If he be the king of Israel, let him now come down from the cross, and we will believe him. ^27-42

**27:43** He trusted in God; let him now deliver him if he will have him; for he said: I am the Son of God. ^27-43

**27:44** And the selfsame thing the thieves also, that were crucified with him, reproached him with. ^27-44

**27:45** Now from the sixth hour there was darkness over the whole earth, until the ninth hour. ^27-45

**27:46** And about the ninth hour Jesus cried with a loud voice, saying: Eli, Eli, lamma sabacthani? that is, My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me? ^27-46

**27:47** And some that stood there and heard, said: This man calleth Elias. ^27-47

**27:48** And immediately one of them running took a sponge, and filled it with vinegar; and put it on a reed, and gave him to drink. ^27-48

**27:49** And the others said: Let be, let us see whether Elias will come to deliver him. ^27-49

**27:50** And Jesus again crying with a loud voice, yielded up the ghost. ^27-50

**27:51** And behold the veil of the temple was rent in two from the top even to the bottom, and the earth quaked, and the rocks were rent. ^27-51

**27:52** And the graves were opened: and many bodies of the saints that had slept arose, ^27-52

**27:53** And coming out of the tombs after his resurrection, came into the holy city, and appeared to many. ^27-53

**27:54** Now the centurion and they that were with him watching Jesus, having seen the earthquake, and the things that were done, were sore afraid, saying: Indeed this was the Son of God. ^27-54

**27:55** And there were there many women afar off, who had followed Jesus from Galilee, ministering unto him: ^27-55

**27:56** Among whom was Mary Magdalen, and Mary the mother of James and Joseph, and the mother of the sons of Zebedee. ^27-56

**27:57** And when it was evening, there came a certain rich man of Arimathea, named Joseph, who also himself was a disciple of Jesus. ^27-57

**27:58** He went to Pilate, and asked the body of Jesus. Then Pilate commanded that the body should be delivered. ^27-58

**27:59** And Joseph taking the body, wrapped it up in a clean linen cloth. ^27-59

**27:60** And laid it in his own new monument, which he had hewed out in a rock. And he rolled a great stone to the door of the monument, and went his way. ^27-60

**27:61** And there was there Mary Magdalen, and the other Mary sitting over against the sepulchre. ^27-61

**27:62** And the next day, which followed the day of preparation, the chief priests and the Pharisees came together to Pilate, ^27-62

**27:63** Saying: Sir, we have remembered, that that seducer said, while he was yet alive: After three days I will rise again. ^27-63

**27:64** Command therefore the sepulchre to be guarded until the third day: lest perhaps his disciples come and steal him away, and say to the people: He is risen from the dead; and the last error shall be worse than the first. ^27-64

**27:65** Pilate saith to them: You have a guard; go, guard it as you know. ^27-65

**27:66** And they departing, made the sepulchre sure, sealing the stone, and setting guards. ^27-66
