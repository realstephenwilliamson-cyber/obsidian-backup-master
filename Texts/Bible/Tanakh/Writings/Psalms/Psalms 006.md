## Chapter 6

**6:1** [^1]For the leader; with instrumental music on the *sheminith*.[^2] A psalm of David. ^6-1

**6:2** O LORD, do not punish me in anger,  
do not chastise me in fury. ^6-2

**6:3** Have mercy on me, O LORD, for I languish;  
heal me, O LORD, for my bones shake with terror. ^6-3

**6:4** My whole being is stricken with terror,  
while You, LORD—O, how long! ^6-4

**6:5** O LORD, turn! Rescue me!  
Deliver me as befits Your faithfulness. ^6-5

**6:6** For there is no praise of You among the dead;  
in Sheol, who can acclaim You? ^6-6

**6:7** I am weary with groaning;  
every night I drench my bed,  
I melt my couch in tears. ^6-7

**6:8** My eyes are wasted by vexation,  
worn out because of all my foes. ^6-8

**6:9** Away from me, all you evildoers,  
for the LORD heeds the sound of my weeping. ^6-9

**6:10** The LORD heeds my plea,  
the LORD accepts my prayer. ^6-10

**6:11** All my enemies will be frustrated and stricken with terror;  
they will turn back in an instant, frustrated. ^6-11

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
