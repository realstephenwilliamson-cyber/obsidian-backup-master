## Mūla

प्रसिद्धा सा न सङ्कोचं विनात्मनि मलश्च सः ।
विचित्रं हि फलं भिन्नं भोग्यत्वेनाभिमन्यते ॥ ^mula

prasiddhā sā na saṅkocaṃ vinātmani malaśca saḥ |
vicitraṃ hi phalaṃ bhinnaṃ bhogyatvenābhimanyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 05 - Chapter Nine, part 3#^pg113|p. 96]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=113>))
>
> The Karmic state (of the soul) is well known (to all) because it gives rise to the various consequences of its Karma, and that (never occurs) without the contraction (of consciousness) within the soul, which is (the soul’s fundamental) Impurity. (with 9.99cd–100ab)
>
> The subject who experiences the various and diverse consequences (of Karma) considers within himself that this is what he must experience. This state of duality is due to this. (with 9.100cd–101ab)
