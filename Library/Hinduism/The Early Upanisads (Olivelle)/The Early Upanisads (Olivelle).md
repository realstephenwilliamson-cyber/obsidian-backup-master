Translated by Patrick Olivelle. Oxford University Press, New York, 1998.

> [!note] Fresh OCR of the page images
> This text was read from the page images by OCR (Tesseract: the Latin model, and the Sanskrit model on the pages of Devanagari text). The English translation is reliable; transliterated words in the introduction and notes keep most diacritics, though some are misread. The Devanagari of the edition is not reliable: roughly one word in three has a misread letter or conjunct, and verse numbers are often garbled. In the notes, the lemma numbers are sometimes displaced. For the Sanskrit text, read the PDF (the *Open the PDF* button on the catalog note).
