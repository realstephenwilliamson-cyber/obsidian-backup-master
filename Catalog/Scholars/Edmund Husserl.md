# Edmund Husserl

The founder of phenomenology, professor at Göttingen (associate 1901, full 1906) and from 1916 full professor at Freiburg, where he retired in 1928 and was succeeded by Heidegger; he died in 1938 ([Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, Spring 2006 edition](https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/spr2006/entries/husserl/)).
