## Mūla

इत्युक्तः स महेशान्या जगदार्तिहरो हरः ।
वाचकानवदन्मन्त्रान्पारम्पर्यक्रमागतान् ॥ ^mula

ity uktaḥ sa maheśānyā jagadārtiharo haraḥ |
vācakān avadan mantrān pāramparyakramāgatān || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2023 – March 2023
> p. 39 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=39>))
>
> Thus spoke the Great Goddess. Hara, who removes (*hara*) the suffering of the universe, told (her, as she desired,) the Mantras that are the denotators (of (these realities), that have come (down) through sequence of the tradition (from master to disciple).
