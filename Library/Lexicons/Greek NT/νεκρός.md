# νεκρός

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · adjective

**Dodson:** dead, a corpse — (a) adj: dead, lifeless, subject to death, mortal, (b) noun: a dead body, a corpse. (Strong's 3498; headword νεκρός, ά, όν)
