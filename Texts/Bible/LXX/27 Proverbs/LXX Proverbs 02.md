# LXX Proverbs 02

← [[LXX Proverbs 01]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Proverbs 03]] →

*Proverbs · [[LXX]]*

**2:1** *My* son, if thou wilt receive the utterance of my commandment, and hide it with thee;  ^2-1

**2:2** thine ear shall hearken to wisdom; thou shalt also apply thine heart to understanding, and shalt apply it to the instruction of thy son.  ^2-2

**2:3** For if thou shalt call to wisdom, and utter thy voice for understanding;  ^2-3

**2:4** and if thou shalt seek it as silver, and search diligently for it as for treasures;  ^2-4

**2:5** then shalt thou understand the fear of the Lord, and find the knowledge of God.  ^2-5

**2:6** For the Lord gives wisdom; and from his presence *come* knowledge and understanding,  ^2-6

**2:7** and he treasures up salvation for them that walk uprightly: he will protect their way;  ^2-7

**2:8** that he may guard the righteous ways: and he will preserve the way of them that fear him.  ^2-8

**2:9** Then shalt thou understand righteousness, and judgment; and shalt direct all thy course aright.[^1]  ^2-9

**2:10** For if wisdom shall come into thine understanding, and discernment shall seem pleasing to thy soul,  ^2-10

**2:11** good counsel shall guard thee, and holy understanding shall keep thee;  ^2-11

**2:12** to deliver thee from the evil way, and from the man that speaks nothing faithfully.  ^2-12

**2:13** Alas *for those* who forsake right paths, to walk in ways of darkness;  ^2-13

**2:14** who rejoice in evils, and delight in wicked perverseness;  ^2-14

**2:15** whose paths are crooked, and their courses winding;[^2]  ^2-15

**2:16** to remove thee far from the straight way, and to estrange thee from a righteous purpose. *My* son, let not evil counsel overtake thee,  ^2-16

**2:17** *of her* who has forsaken the instruction of her youth, and forgotten the covenant of God.  ^2-17

**2:18** For she has fixed her house near death, and *guided* her wheels near Hades with the giants.[^3]  ^2-18

**2:19** None that go by her shall return, neither shall they take hold of right paths, for they are not apprehended of the years of life.[^4]  ^2-19

**2:20** For had they gone in good paths, they would have found the paths of righteousness easy.[^5]  ^2-20

**2:21** For the upright shall dwell in the earth, and the holy shall be left behind in it.  ^2-21

**2:22** The paths of the ungodly shall perish out of the earth, and transgressors shall be driven away from it.  ^2-22

[^1]: 2:9 *Lit.* all good a les.
[^2]: 2:15 *Lit.* wheel tracks. Compare Heb. 12. 13.
[^3]: 2:18 See *Heb.*
[^4]: 2:19 Singular variation from *Heb.*
[^5]: 2:20 *Gr.* smooth.
