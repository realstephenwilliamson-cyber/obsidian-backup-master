## Mūla

कृत्वा जपं ततः सर्वं देवतायै समर्पयेत् ।
तच्चोक्तं कर्तृतातत्त्वनिरूपणविधौ पुरा ॥ ^mula

kṛtvā japaṃ tataḥ sarvaṃ devatāyai samarpayet |
taccoktaṃ kartṛtātattvanirūpaṇavidhau purā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 17 - Chapter Twenty-Five (continued) and Chapter Twenty-Six#^pg414|p. 394]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=414>))
>
> After that, he should display the ritual gesture (mudrā) (with his hands), or with the mind by means of Yoga, with speech by applying Mantras (mantrayoga), and with the body by its posture (saṁniveśa). Then having repeatedly recited (the root) Mantra (japa), he should offer everything to the deity. (with 26.67–68ab)
>
> This has been said before in the course of the procedure of describing (and perceiving) the true (and essential) nature (tattva) of the agency (of consciousness). (with 26.68cd)
