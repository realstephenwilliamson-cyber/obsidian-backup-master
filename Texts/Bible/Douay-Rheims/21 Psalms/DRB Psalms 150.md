# Psalms 150 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 149|← Psalms 149]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · —

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 150|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**150:1** Praise ye the Lord in his holy places: praise ye him in the firmament of his power. ^150-1

**150:2** Praise ye him for his mighty acts: praise ye him according to the multitude of his greatness. ^150-2

**150:3** Praise him with sound of trumpet: praise him with psaltery and harp. ^150-3

**150:4** Praise him with timbrel and choir: praise him with strings and organs. ^150-4

**150:5** Praise him on high sounding cymbals: praise him on cymbals of joy: ^150-5

**150:6** let every spirit praise the Lord. Alleluia. ^150-6
