# Psalm 145

← [[BCP Psalm 144]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 146]] →

*Exaltabo te, Deus* · Book Five · Thirtieth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 145]]

**145:1** I will exalt you, O God my King, \*  
and bless your Name for ever and ever. ^145-1

**145:2** Every day will I bless you \*  
and praise your Name for ever and ever. ^145-2

**145:3** Great is the LORD and greatly to be praised; \*  
there is no end to his greatness. ^145-3

**145:4** One generation shall praise your works to another \*  
and shall declare your power. ^145-4

**145:5** I will ponder the glorious splendor of your majesty \*  
and all your marvelous works. ^145-5

**145:6** They shall speak of the might of your wondrous acts, \*  
and I will tell of your greatness. ^145-6

**145:7** They shall publish the remembrance of your great goodness; \*  
they shall sing of your righteous deeds. ^145-7

[p. 802]

**145:8** The LORD is gracious and full of compassion, \*  
slow to anger and of great kindness. ^145-8

**145:9** The LORD is loving to everyone \*  
and his compassion is over all his works. ^145-9

**145:10** All your works praise you, O LORD, \*  
and your faithful servants bless you. ^145-10

**145:11** They make known the glory of your kingdom \*  
and speak of your power; ^145-11

**145:12** That the peoples may know of your power \*  
and the glorious splendor of your kingdom. ^145-12

**145:13** Your kingdom is an everlasting kingdom; \*  
your dominion endures throughout all ages. ^145-13

**145:14** The LORD is faithful in all his words \*  
and merciful in all his deeds. ^145-14

**145:15** The LORD upholds all those who fall; \*  
he lifts up those who are bowed down. ^145-15

**145:16** The eyes of all wait upon you, O LORD, \*  
and you give them their food in due season. ^145-16

**145:17** You open wide your hand \*  
and satisfy the needs of every living creature. ^145-17

**145:18** The LORD is righteous in all his ways \*  
and loving in all his works. ^145-18

**145:19** The LORD is near to those who call upon him, \*  
to all who call upon him faithfully. ^145-19

**145:20** He fulfills the desire of those who fear him; \*  
he hears their cry and helps them. ^145-20

**145:21** The LORD preserves all those who love him, \*  
but he destroys all the wicked. ^145-21

[p. 803]

**145:22** My mouth shall speak the praise of the LORD; \*  
let all flesh bless his holy Name for ever and ever. ^145-22
