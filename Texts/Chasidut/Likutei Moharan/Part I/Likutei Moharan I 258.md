# Likutei Moharan I 258

← [[Likutei Moharan I 257]] · [[Likutei Moharan]] · [[Likutei Moharan I 259]] →

*Part I · Lesson 258* · [[Likutei Moharan]]

## 1

When a person encounters opposition, [his opponents] are able to turn him away from God’s path and knock him down from his level, God forbid. King David, of blessed memory, took pride in this: “Many are my persecutors and my enemies; from Your statutes I have not strayed” ([[Psalms 119#^119-157|Psalms 119:157]]). Although he encountered much opposition, he still did not stray from God’s path.
