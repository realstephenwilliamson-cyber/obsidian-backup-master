## Chapter 91

**91:1** O you who dwell in the shelter of the Most High  
and abide in the protection of Shaddai— ^91-1

**91:2** I say of the LORD, my refuge and stronghold,  
my God in whom I trust, ^91-2

**91:3** that He will save you from the fowler’s trap,  
from the destructive plague. ^91-3

**91:4** He will cover you with His pinions;  
you will find refuge under His wings;  
His fidelity is an encircling shield. ^91-4

**91:5** You need not fear the terror by night,  
or the arrow that flies by day, ^91-5

**91:6** the plague that stalks in the darkness,  
or the scourge that ravages at noon. ^91-6

**91:7** A thousand may fall at your left side,  
ten thousand at your right,  
but it shall not reach you. ^91-7

**91:8** You will see it with your eyes,  
you will witness the punishment of the wicked. ^91-8

**91:9** Because you took the LORD—my refuge,  
the Most High—as your haven, ^91-9

**91:10** no harm will befall you,  
no disease touch your tent. ^91-10

**91:11** For He will order His angels  
to guard you wherever you go. ^91-11

**91:12** They will carry you in their hands  
lest you hurt your foot on a stone. ^91-12

**91:13** You will tread on cubs and vipers;  
you will trample lions and asps. ^91-13

**91:14** “Because he is devoted to Me I will deliver him;  
I will keep him safe, for he knows My name. ^91-14

**91:15** When he calls on Me, I will answer him;  
I will be with him in distress;  
I will rescue him and make him honored; ^91-15

**91:16** I will let him live to a ripe old age,  
and show him My salvation.” ^91-16
