# LXX Sirach 16

← [[LXX Sirach 15]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Sirach 17]] →

*Sirach · [[LXX]]*

**16:1** Desire not a multitude of unprofitable children, neither delight in ungodly sons.  ^16-1

**16:2** Though they multiply, rejoice not in them, except the fear of the Lord be with them.  ^16-2

**16:3** Trust not thou in their life, neither respect their multitude: for one that is just is better than a thousand; and better it is to die without children, than to have them that are ungodly.  ^16-3

**16:4** For by one that hath understanding shall the city be replenished: but the kindred of the wicked shall speedily become desolate.  ^16-4

**16:5** Many such things have I seen with mine eyes, and mine ear hath heard greater things than these.  ^16-5

**16:6** In the congregation of the ungodly shall a fire be kindled; and in a rebellious nation wrath is set on fire.  ^16-6

**16:7** He was not pacified toward the old giants, who fell away in the strength of their foolishness.  ^16-7

**16:8** Neither spared he the place where Lot sojourned, but abhorred them for their pride.  ^16-8

**16:9** He pitied not the people of perdition, who were taken away in their sins:  ^16-9

**16:10** nor the six hundred thousand footmen, who were gathered together in the hardness of their hearts.  ^16-10

**16:11** And if there be one stiffnecked among the people, it is marvel if he escape unpunished: for mercy and wrath are with him; he is mighty to forgive and to pour out displeasure.  ^16-11

**16:12** As his mercy is great, so is his correction also: he judgeth a man according to his works.  ^16-12

**16:13** The sinner shall not escape with his spoils: and the patience of the godly shall not be frustrate.  ^16-13

**16:14** Make way for every work of mercy: for every man shall find according to his works.  ^16-14

**16:17** Say not thou, I will hide myself from the Lord: shall any remember me from above? I shall not be remembered among so many people: for what is my soul among such an infinite number of creatures?  ^16-17

**16:18** Behold, the heaven, and the heaven of heavens, the deep, and the earth, and all that therein is, shall be moved when he shall visit.  ^16-18

**16:19** The mountains also and foundations of the earth shall be shaken with trembling, when he looketh upon them.  ^16-19

**16:20** No heart can think upon these things worthily: and who is able to conceive his ways?  ^16-20

**16:21** It is a tempest which no man can see: for the most part of his works are hid.  ^16-21

**16:22** Who can declare the works of his justice? or who can endure them? for his covenant is afar off.  ^16-22

**16:23** He that wanteth understanding will think upon vain things: and a foolish man erring imagineth follies.  ^16-23

**16:24** My son, hearken unto me, and learn knowledge, and mark my words with thy heart.  ^16-24

**16:25** I will shew forth doctrine in weight, and declare his knowledge exactly.  ^16-25

**16:26** The works of the Lord are done in judgment from the beginning: and from the time he made them he disposed the parts thereof.  ^16-26

**16:27** He garnished his works for ever, and the chief of them unto all generations: they neither labour, nor are weary, nor cease from their works.  ^16-27

**16:28** None of them hindereth another, and they shall never disobey his word.  ^16-28

**16:29** After this the Lord looked upon the earth, and filled it with his blessings.  ^16-29

**16:30** With all manner of living things hath he covered the face thereof; and they shall return into it again.  ^16-30
