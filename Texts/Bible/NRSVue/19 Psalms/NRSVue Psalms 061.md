## Assurance of God’s protection

*To the leader: with stringed instruments. Of David.*

**61:1** Hear my cry, O God; listen to my prayer. ^61-1

**61:2** From the end of the earth I call to you, when my heart is faint. Lead me to the rock that is higher than I, ^61-2

**61:3** for you are my refuge, a strong tower against the enemy. ^61-3

**61:4** Let me abide in your tent forever, find refuge under the shelter of your wings. *Selah* ^61-4

**61:5** For you, O God, have heard my vows; you have given me the heritage of those who fear your name. ^61-5

**61:6** Prolong the life of the king; may his years endure to all generations! ^61-6

**61:7** May he be enthroned forever before God; appoint steadfast love and faithfulness to watch over him! ^61-7

**61:8** So I will always sing praises to your name, as I pay my vows day after day. ^61-8
