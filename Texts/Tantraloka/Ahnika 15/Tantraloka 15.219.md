## Mūla

अधिष्ठिता महेशेन चित्रतद्रूपधारिणा ।
किं वातिबहुना योऽसौ यष्टा तत्संमुखादितः ॥ ^mula

adhiṣṭhitā maheśena citratadrūpadhāriṇā |
kiṃ vātibahunā yo'sau yaṣṭā tatsaṃmukhāditaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 05 - Chapter Fifteen, part 4#^pg151|p. 133]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=151>))
>
> In this way, (each) direction is relative to the sun, (and so,) even though it is relative (vicitrā), it is such (in relation to the sun). (Each direction is) presided over by Śiva (maheśa), who bears their (diverse and mutually) relative form (citratadrūpa). (with 15.218cd–219ab)
>
> What is the use of extensive (explanations)? The directions are divided into eastern, southern, northern and western in relation to the sacrificer (yaṣṭṛ), according to whether (a place is) in front of him (behind) and so on. (with 15.219cd–220ab)

> किं वातिबहुना योऽसौ यष्टा तत्संमुखादितः ॥
> दिशोऽपि प्रविभज्यन्ते प्राक्सव्योत्तरपश्चिमाः ।
