## Chapter 84

**84:1** For the leader; on the *gittith*. Of the Korahites. A psalm. ^84-1

**84:2** How lovely is Your dwelling-place,  
O LORD of hosts. ^84-2

**84:3** I long, I yearn for the courts of the LORD;  
my body and soul shout for joy to the living God. ^84-3

**84:4** Even the sparrow has found a home,  
and the swallow a nest for herself  
in which to set her young,  
near Your altar, O LORD of hosts,  
my king and my God. ^84-4

**84:5** Happy are those who dwell in Your house;  
they forever praise You. *Selah*. ^84-5

**84:6** Happy is the man who finds refuge in You,  
whose mind is on the [pilgrim] highways. ^84-6

**84:7** They pass through the Valley of Baca,  
regarding it as a place of springs,  
as if the early rain had covered it with blessing.[^1] ^84-7

**84:8** They go from rampart to rampart,[^2]  
appearing before God in Zion. ^84-8

**84:9** O LORD, God of hosts,  
hear my prayer;  
give ear, O God of Jacob. *Selah*. ^84-9

**84:10** O God, behold our shield,  
look upon the face of Your anointed. ^84-10

**84:11** Better one day in Your courts than a thousand [anywhere else];  
I would rather stand at the threshold of God’s house  
than dwell in the tents of the wicked. ^84-11

**84:12** For the LORD God is sun[^3] and shield;  
the LORD bestows grace and glory;  
He does not withhold His bounty from those who live without blame. ^84-12

**84:13** O LORD of hosts,  
happy is the man who trusts in You. ^84-13

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Others “strength to strength.”
[^3]: Or “bulwark,” with Targum; cf. Isa. 54.12.
