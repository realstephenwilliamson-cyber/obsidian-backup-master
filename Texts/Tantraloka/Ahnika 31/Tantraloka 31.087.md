## Mūla

मध्यस्थं तं त्रिभागं च तदन्ते भ्रमयेदुभौ ।
भागमेकं परित्यज्य तन्मध्ये भ्रमयेत्पुनः ॥ ^mula

madhyasthaṃ taṃ tribhāgaṃ ca tadante bhramayedubhau |
bhāgamekaṃ parityajya tanmadhye bhramayetpunaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 05 - Chapter Thirty-One, part 2#^pg122|p. 104]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=122>))
>
> It is said in the Devyāyāmala that ‘half of a square field of four (handspans) is divided into twelve (squares), horizontally and vertically. Then the teacher should turn from the square located in the middle towards his own suitable side, upwards by one square and three quarters, making contact with the thread of Brahmā. Omitting one square, he should rotate (the thread) again in the middle of that (fanmadhye) to draw two half-moons, after that he should draw two others, toward the top, in the third square, until (it touches) the part above. (Then,) starting from the fourth square, he should again conjoin it (to corner of the field) above that, below and above. Starting from that measure, he should, having rotated upwards, connect well (the whole) with the fourth (pair of half-moons).’ (with 31.85cd–89ab)
