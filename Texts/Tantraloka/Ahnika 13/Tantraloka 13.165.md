## Mūla

इति प्रश्ने कृते देव्या श्रीमाञ्छंभुर्न्यरूपयत् ।
उपायोऽत्र विवेकैकः स हि हेयं विहापयन् ॥ ^mula

iti praśne kṛte devyā śrīmāñchaṃbhurnyarūpayat |
upāyo'tra vivekaikaḥ sa hi heyaṃ vihāpayan || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 07 - Chapter Thirteen, part 3#^pg246|p. 224]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=246>))
>
> The goddess asked: ‘iva cannot be described (or taught) (anirdeśya), (50) pray tell me what is the means there (in that case to attain him)?’ To which the venerable Śambhu (replied,) saying (the following). (with 13.164cd–165ab)
>
> ‘The sole means (to realise Him) here (in this case) is discrimination (viveka). Causing to abandon what should be abandoned, O fair-hipped one, it bestows on one the most excellent knowledge (born of) intuition (prātibha)’ (with 13.165cd–166ab)

*Lead-in:* किं न्यरूपयत् ? -- इत्याह --

> उपायोऽत्र विवेकैकः स हि हेयं विहापयन् ॥
> ददात्यस्य च सुश्रोणि प्रातिभं ज्ञानमुत्तमम् ।

विवेकैक इति विवेक एव एकः प्रधानं स्वपरामर्शात्मा विवेकः -- इत्यर्थः । हेयमिति मायीयभावादि । अस्येति साधकस्य ॥ १६५ ॥
