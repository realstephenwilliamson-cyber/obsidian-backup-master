## Mūla

आवृत्तिशतयोगेन पशोर्निर्वापणं भवेत् ।
कृत्वा कतिपयं कालं तत्राभ्यासमनन्यधीः ॥ ^mula

āvṛttiśatayogena paśornirvāpaṇaṃ bhavet |
kṛtvā katipayaṃ kālaṃ tatrābhyāsamananyadhīḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 05 - Chapter Sixteen, part 1#^pg41|p. 21]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=41>))
>
> By resting (in this practice) in this way, and by repeating (the Mantra) one hundred times, in the course of twelve minutes the animal is offered as a libation (nirvāpanam). (with 16.49cd–50ab)
>
> Having practiced there for a while, his mind undistracted, as is taught in the Cintāmanṇi, ‘he who knows Yoga and is free of doubt attains success, by that (supreme) nature (in which he rests). Clearly, that should be kept secret (and well guarded), as is the vital breath (itself). (with 16.50cd–51)
