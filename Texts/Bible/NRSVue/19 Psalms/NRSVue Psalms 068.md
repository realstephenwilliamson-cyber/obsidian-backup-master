# Psalm 68 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 067|← Psalm 67]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 069|Psalm 69 →]]

## Praise and thanksgiving

*To the leader. Of David. A Psalm. A Song.*

**68:1** Let God rise up; let his enemies be scattered; let those who hate him flee before him. ^68-1

**68:2** As smoke is driven away, so drive them away; as wax melts before the fire, let the wicked perish before God. ^68-2

**68:3** But let the righteous be joyful; let them exult before God; let them be jubilant with joy. ^68-3

**68:4** Sing to God; sing praises to his name; lift up a song to him who rides upon the clouds^[Or *cast up a highway for him who rides through the deserts*]—his name is the LORD—be exultant before him. ^68-4

**68:5** Father of orphans and protector of widows is God in his holy habitation. ^68-5

**68:6** God gives the desolate a home to live in; he leads out the prisoners to prosperity, but the rebellious live in a parched land. ^68-6

**68:7** O God, when you went out before your people, when you marched through the wilderness, *Selah* ^68-7

**68:8** the earth quaked, the heavens poured down rain at the presence of God, the God of Sinai, at the presence of God, the God of Israel. ^68-8

**68:9** Rain in abundance, O God, you showered abroad; you restored your heritage when it languished; ^68-9

**68:10** your flock found a dwelling in it; in your goodness, O God, you provided for the needy. ^68-10

**68:11** The Lord gives the command; great is the company of those^[Or *company of the women*] who bore the tidings: ^68-11

**68:12** “The kings of the armies, they flee, they flee!” The women at home divide the spoil, ^68-12

**68:13** though they stay among the sheepfolds—the wings of a dove covered with silver, its pinions with green gold. ^68-13

**68:14** When the Almighty^[Traditional rendering of Heb *Shaddai*] scattered kings there, snow fell on Zalmon. ^68-14

**68:15** O mighty mountain, mountain of Bashan; O many-peaked mountain, mountain of Bashan! ^68-15

**68:16** Why do you look with envy, O many-peaked mountain, at the mount that God desired for his abode, where the LORD will reside forever? ^68-16

**68:17** With mighty chariotry, twice ten thousand, thousands upon thousands, the Lord came from Sinai into the holy place.^[Cn: Heb *The Lord among them Sinai in the holy* (place)] ^68-17

**68:18** You ascended the high mount, leading captives in your train and receiving gifts from people, even from those who rebel against the LORD God’s abiding there. ^68-18

**68:19** Blessed be the Lord, who daily bears us up; God is our salvation. *Selah* ^68-19

**68:20** Our God is a God of salvation, and to GOD, the Lord, belongs escape from death. ^68-20

**68:21** But God will shatter the heads of his enemies, the hairy crown of those who walk in their guilty ways. ^68-21

**68:22** The Lord said, “I will bring them back from Bashan; I will bring them back from the depths of the sea, ^68-22

**68:23** so that you may bathe^[Gk Syr Tg: Heb *shatter*] your feet in blood, so that the tongues of your dogs may have their share from the foe.” ^68-23

**68:24** Your solemn processions are seen,^[Or *have been seen*] O God, the processions of my God, my King, into the sanctuary— ^68-24

**68:25** the singers in front, the musicians last, between them young women playing tambourines: ^68-25

**68:26** “Bless God in the great congregation, the LORD, O you who are of Israel’s fountain!” ^68-26

**68:27** There is Benjamin, the least of them, in the lead, the princes of Judah in a body, the princes of Zebulun, the princes of Naphtali. ^68-27

**68:28** Summon your might, O God; show your strength, O God, as you have done for us before. ^68-28

**68:29** Because of your temple at Jerusalem, kings bear gifts to you. ^68-29

**68:30** Rebuke the wild animals that live among the reeds, the herd of bulls with the calves of the peoples. Trample^[Cn: Heb *Trampling*] under foot those who lust after tribute; scatter the peoples who delight in war.^[Meaning of Heb of 68.30 is uncertain] ^68-30

**68:31** Let bronze be brought from Egypt; let Cush hasten to stretch out its hands to God. ^68-31

**68:32** Sing to God, O kingdoms of the earth; sing praises to the Lord, *Selah* ^68-32

**68:33** O rider in the heavens, the ancient heavens; listen, he sends out his voice, his mighty voice. ^68-33

**68:34** Ascribe power to God, whose majesty is over Israel and whose power is in the skies. ^68-34

**68:35** Awesome is God in his^[Gk: Heb *from your*] sanctuary, the God of Israel; he gives power and strength to his people. Blessed be God! ^68-35
