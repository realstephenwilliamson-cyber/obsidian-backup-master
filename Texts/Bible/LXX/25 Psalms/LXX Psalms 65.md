# LXX Psalms 65

← [[LXX Psalms 64]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 66]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 066]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 066]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**65:1** For the end, a Song of a Psalm of resurrection. Shout unto God, all the earth.  ^65-1

**65:2** O sing praises to his name; give glory to his praise.  ^65-2

**65:3** Say unto God, How awful are thy works! through the greatness of thy power thine enemies shall lie to thee.  ^65-3

**65:4** Let all the earth worship thee, and sing to thee; let them sing to thy name. Pause.  ^65-4

**65:5** Come and behold the works of God; *he is* terrible in *his* counsels beyond the children of men.  ^65-5

**65:6** Who turns the sea into dry land; they shall go through the river on foot; there shall we rejoice in him,  ^65-6

**65:7** who by his power is Lord over the age, his eyes look upon the nations; let not them that provoke *him* be exalted in themselves. Pause.[^1]  ^65-7

**65:8** Bless our God, ye Gentiles, and make the voice of his praise to be heard;  ^65-8

**65:9** who quickens my soul in life, and does not suffer my feet to be moved.  ^65-9

**65:10** For thou, O God, hast proved us; thou hast tried us with fire as silver is tried.  ^65-10

**65:11** Thou broughtest us into the snare; thou laidest afflictions on our back.  ^65-11

**65:12** Thou didst mount men upon our heads; we went through fire and water; but thou broughtest us out into *a place of* refreshment.  ^65-12

**65:13** I will go into thine house with whole-burnt-offerings; I will pay thee my vows,  ^65-13

**65:14** which my lips framed, and my mouth uttered in my affliction.  ^65-14

**65:15** I will offer to thee whole-burnt-sacrifices full of marrow, with incense and rams; I will sacrifice to thee oxen with goats. Pause.  ^65-15

**65:16** Come, hear, and I will tell, all ye that fear God, how great things he has done for my soul.  ^65-16

**65:17** I cried to him with my mouth, and exalted him with my tongue.  ^65-17

**65:18** If I have regarded iniquity in my heart, let not the Lord hearken *to me*.  ^65-18

**65:19** Therefore God has hearkened to me; he has attended to the voice of my prayer.  ^65-19

**65:20** Blessed be God, who has not turned away my prayer, nor his mercy from me.  ^65-20

[^1]: 65:7 *Or,* has dominion for ever.
