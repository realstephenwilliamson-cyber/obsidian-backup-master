## Chapter 44

**44:1** For the leader. Of the Korahites. A *maskil*. ^44-1

**44:2** We have heard, O God,  
our fathers have told us  
the deeds You performed in their time,  
in days of old. ^44-2

**44:3** With Your hand You planted them,  
displacing nations;  
You brought misfortune on peoples,  
and drove them out. ^44-3

**44:4** It was not by their sword that they took the land,  
their arm did not give them victory,  
but Your right hand, Your arm, and Your goodwill,  
for You favored them. ^44-4

**44:5** You are my king, O God;  
decree victories for Jacob! ^44-5

**44:6** Through You we gore our foes;  
by Your name we trample our adversaries; ^44-6

**44:7** I do not trust in my bow;  
it is not my sword that gives me victory; ^44-7

**44:8** You give us victory over our foes;  
You thwart those who hate us. ^44-8

**44:9** In God we glory at all times,  
and praise Your name unceasingly. *Selah*. ^44-9

**44:10** Yet You have rejected and disgraced us;  
You do not go with our armies. ^44-10

**44:11** You make us retreat before our foe;  
our enemies plunder us at will. ^44-11

**44:12** You let them devour us like sheep;  
You disperse us among the nations. ^44-12

**44:13** You sell Your people for no fortune,  
You set no high price on them. ^44-13

**44:14** You make us the butt of our neighbors,  
the scorn and derision of those around us. ^44-14

**44:15** You make us a byword among the nations,  
a laughingstock[^1] among the peoples. ^44-15

**44:16** I am always aware of my disgrace;  
I am wholly covered with shame ^44-16

**44:17** at the sound of taunting revilers,  
in the presence of the vengeful foe. ^44-17

**44:18** All this has come upon us,  
yet we have not forgotten You,  
or been false to Your covenant. ^44-18

**44:19** Our hearts have not gone astray,  
nor have our feet swerved from Your path, ^44-19

**44:20** though You cast us, crushed, to where the sea monster[^2] is,  
and covered us over with deepest darkness. ^44-20

**44:21** If we forgot the name of our God  
and spread forth our hands to a foreign god, ^44-21

**44:22** God would surely search it out,  
for He knows the secrets of the heart. ^44-22

**44:23** It is for Your sake that we are slain all day long,  
that we are regarded as sheep to be slaughtered. ^44-23

**44:24** Rouse Yourself; why do You sleep, O Lord?  
Awaken, do not reject us forever! ^44-24

**44:25** Why do You hide Your face,  
ignoring our affliction and distress? ^44-25

**44:26** We lie prostrate in the dust;  
our body clings to the ground. ^44-26

**44:27** Arise and help us,  
redeem us, as befits Your faithfulness. ^44-27

[^1]: Lit. “a wagging of the head.”
[^2]: Heb. tannim = tannin, as in Ezek. 29.3 and 32.2.
