## Mūla

षोडशांशे लिखेत्पद्मं द्वादशांगुललोपनात् ।
तदूर्ध्वं मध्यभागे तु वारिजन्म समालिखेत् ॥ ^mula

ṣoḍaśāṃśe likhetpadmaṃ dvādaśāṃgulalopanāt |
tadūrdhvaṃ madhyabhāge tu vārijanma samālikhet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 05 - Chapter Thirty-One, part 2#^pg123|p. 105]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=123>))
>
> Having taken from the field (gocara) one part from both, (the thread) should be rotated (to form the shape of a) leaf like that of a banyan (ficus indica). This is the drawing of the bottom (part of the staff). (Next) one should draw a lotus in (a space consisting of) sixteen parts by removing twelve finger-breadths. (with 31.91–92ab)
>
> One should draw a lotus above that (staff) in the middle part (of the mandala). A third one should then be drawn at the end of the middle prong. In the same way, one should draw two other lotuses (of the same size) on the wall (kaṭi), to the right and the left. (with 31.92cd–93)

*Lead-in:* तच्च कुत्र लिखेत्--इत्याह--

> तदूर्ध्वं मध्यभागे तु वारिजन्म समालिखेत् ॥
> मध्यशृङ्गावसाने तु तृतीयं विलिखेत्ततः ।

तदूर्ध्वम्--दण्डोपरि । मध्यभागे इति--मण्डलापेक्षया । न केवलमत्रैव पद्मं लिखेत्, यावदरोपर्यपि--इत्याह--मध्येत्यादि । तृतीयशब्दार्थमेव घटयति--मध्येत्यादिना ॥
