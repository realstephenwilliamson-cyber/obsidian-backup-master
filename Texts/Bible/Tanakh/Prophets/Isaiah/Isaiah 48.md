## Chapter 48

**48:1** Listen to this, O House of Jacob,  
Who bear the name Israel  
And have issued from the waters[^1] of Judah,  
Who swear by the name of the LORD  
And invoke the God of Israel—  
Though not in truth and sincerity— ^48-1

**48:2** For you[^2] are called after the Holy City[^3]  
And you[^4] do lean on the God of Israel,  
Whose name is LORD of Hosts: ^48-2

**48:3** Long ago, I foretold things that happened,  
From My mouth they issued, and I announced them;  
Suddenly I acted, and they came to pass. ^48-3

**48:4** Because I know how stubborn you are  
(Your neck is like an iron sinew  
And your forehead bronze), ^48-4

**48:5** Therefore I told you long beforehand,  
Announced things to you ere they happened—  
That you might not say, “My idol caused them,  
My carved and molten images ordained them.” ^48-5

**48:6** You have heard all this; look, must you not acknowledge it?[^5]  
As of now, I announce to you new things,  
Well-guarded secrets you did not know. ^48-6

**48:7** Only now are they created, and not of old;  
Before today[^6] you had not heard them;  
You cannot say, “I knew them already.” ^48-7

**48:8** You had never heard, you had never known,  
Your ears were not opened of old.  
Though I know that you are treacherous,  
That you were called a rebel from birth, ^48-8

**48:9** For the sake of My name I control My wrath;  
To My own glory, I am patient[^7] with you,  
And I will not destroy you. ^48-9

**48:10** See, I refine you, but not as silver;  
I test you in the furnace of affliction. ^48-10

**48:11** For My sake, My own sake, do I act—  
Lest [My name][^8] be dishonored!  
I will not give My glory to another. ^48-11

**48:12** Listen to Me, O Jacob,  
Israel, whom I have called:  
I am He—I am the first,  
And I am the last as well. ^48-12

**48:13** My own hand founded the earth,  
My right hand spread out the skies.  
I call unto them, let them stand up. ^48-13

**48:14** Assemble, all of you, and listen!  
Who among you[^9] foretold these things:  
“He whom the LORD loves  
Shall work His will against Babylon,  
And, with His might, against Chaldea”?[^10] ^48-14

**48:15** I, I predicted, and I called him;  
I have brought him and he shall succeed in his mission. ^48-15

**48:16** Draw near to Me and hear this:  
From the beginning, I did not speak in secret;  
From the time anything existed, I was there.[^11]  
“And now the Lord GOD has sent me, endowed with His spirit.”[^12] ^48-16

**48:17** Thus said the LORD your Redeemer,  
The Holy One of Israel:  
I the LORD am your God,  
Instructing you for your own benefit.  
Guiding you in the way you should go. ^48-17

**48:18** If only you would heed My commands!  
Then your prosperity would be like a river,  
Your triumph like the waves of the sea. ^48-18

**48:19** Your offspring would be as many as the sand,  
Their issue as many as its grains.[^13]  
Their name would never be cut off  
Or obliterated from before Me. ^48-19

**48:20** Go forth from Babylon,  
Flee from Chaldea!  
Declare this with loud shouting,  
Announce this,  
Bring out the word to the ends of the earth!  
Say: “The LORD has redeemed  
His servant Jacob!” ^48-20

**48:21** They have known no thirst,  
Though He led them through parched places;  
He made water flow for them from the rock;  
He cleaved the rock and water gushed forth. ^48-21

**48:22** There is no safety—said the LORD—for the wicked. ^48-22

[^1]: Emendation yields “loins.”
[^2]: Heb. “they.”
[^3]: Emendation yields “the holy people.”
[^4]: Heb. “they.”
[^5]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^6]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^7]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^8]: These words are supplied in some ancient versions; cf. v. 9.
[^9]: Heb. “them.”
[^10]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^11]: I.e., I foretold it through prophets.
[^12]: Lit. “and His spirit.”
[^13]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
