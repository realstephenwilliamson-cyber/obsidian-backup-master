# Judith 3 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Judith 02|← Judith 2]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Judith 04|Judith 4 →]]

## Entreaties for peace

**3:1** They therefore sent messengers to him to sue for peace in these words: ^3-1

**3:2** “We, the servants of Nebuchadnezzar, the Great King, lie prostrate before you. Do with us whatever you will. ^3-2

**3:3** See, our buildings and all our land and all our wheat fields and our flocks and herds and all our encampments^[Gk *all the sheepfolds of our tents*] lie before you; do with them as you please. ^3-3

**3:4** Our towns and their inhabitants are also your slaves; come and deal with them as you see fit.” ^3-4

**3:5** The men came to Holofernes and told him all this. ^3-5

**3:6** Then he went down to the seacoast with his army and stationed garrisons in the fortified towns and took picked men from them as auxiliaries. ^3-6

**3:7** These people and all in the countryside welcomed him with garlands and dances and tambourines. ^3-7

**3:8** Yet he demolished all their shrines^[Syr: Gk *borders*] and cut down their sacred groves, for he had been commissioned to destroy all the gods of the land, so that all nations should worship Nebuchadnezzar alone and that all their dialects and tribes should call upon him as a god. ^3-8

**3:9** Then he came toward Esdraelon, near Dothan, facing the great ridge of Judea; ^3-9

**3:10** he camped between Geba and Scythopolis and remained for a whole month in order to collect all the supplies for his army. ^3-10
