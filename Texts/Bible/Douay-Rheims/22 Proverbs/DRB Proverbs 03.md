# Proverbs 3 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Proverbs 02|← Proverbs 2]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Proverbs 04|Proverbs 4 →]]

**3:1** My son, forget not my law, and let thy heart keep my commandments. ^3-1

**3:2** For they shall add to thee length of days, and years of life and peace. ^3-2

**3:3** Let not mercy and truth leave thee, put them about thy neck, and write them in the tables of thy heart: ^3-3

**3:4** And thou shalt And grace and good understanding before God and men. ^3-4

**3:5** Have confidence in the Lord with all thy heart, and lean not upon thy own prudence. ^3-5

**3:6** In all thy ways think on him, and he will direct thy steps. ^3-6

**3:7** I Be not wise in thy own conceit: fear God, and depart from evil: ^3-7

**3:8** For it shall be health to thy navel, and moistening to thy bones. ^3-8

**3:9** Honour the Lord with thy substance, and give him of the first of all thy fruits: ^3-9

**3:10** And thy barns shall be filled with abundance, and thy presses shall run over with wine. ^3-10

**3:11** My son, reject not the correction of the Lord: and do not faint when thou art chastised by him: ^3-11

**3:12** For whom the Lord loveth, he chastiseth: and as a father in the son he pleaseth himself. ^3-12

**3:13** Blessed is the man that findeth wisdom and is rich in prudence: ^3-13

**3:14** The purchasing thereof is better than the merchandise of silver, and her fruit than the chiefest and purest gold: ^3-14

**3:15** She is more precious than all riches: and all the things that are desired, are not to be compared with her. ^3-15

**3:16** Length of days is in her right hand, and in her left hand riches and glory. ^3-16

**3:17** Her ways are beautiful ways, and all her paths are peaceable. ^3-17

**3:18** She is a tree of life to them that lay hold on her: and he that shall retain her is blessed. ^3-18

**3:19** The Lord by wisdom hath founded the earth, hath established the heavens by prudence. ^3-19

**3:20** By his wisdom the depths have broken out, and the clouds grow thick with dew. ^3-20

**3:21** My son, let not these things depart from thy eyes: keep the law and counsel: ^3-21

**3:22** And there shall be life to thy soul, and grace to thy mouth. ^3-22

**3:23** Then shalt thou walk confidently in thy way, and thy foot shall not stumble: ^3-23

**3:24** If thou sleep, thou shalt not fear: thou shalt rest, and thy sleep shall be sweet. ^3-24

**3:25** Be not afraid of sudden fear, nor of the power of the wicked falling upon thee. ^3-25

**3:26** For the Lord will be at thy side, and will keep thy foot that thou be not taken. ^3-26

**3:27** Do not withhold him from doing good, who is able: if thou art able, do good thyself also. ^3-27

**3:28** Say not to thy friend: Go, and come again: and tomorrow I will give to thee: when thou canst give at present. ^3-28

**3:29** Practise not evil against thy friend, when he hath confidence in thee. ^3-29

**3:30** Strive not against a man without cause, when he hath done thee no evil. ^3-30

**3:31** Envy not the unjust man, and do not follow his ways: ^3-31

**3:32** For every mocker is an abomination to the Lord, and his communication is with the simple. ^3-32

**3:33** Want is from the Lord in the house of the wicked: but the habitations of the just shall be blessed. ^3-33

**3:34** He shall scorn the scorners, and to the meek he will give grace. ^3-34

**3:35** The wise shall possess glory: the promotion of fools is disgrace. ^3-35
