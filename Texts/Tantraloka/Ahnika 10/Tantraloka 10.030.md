## Mūla

अतदात्मा पटो नैति पृथुबुध्नादिरूपताम् ।
तथा सत्यपि विज्ञाने विज्ञातृसमवायिनि ॥ ^mula

atadātmā paṭo naiti pṛthubudhnādirūpatām |
tathā satyapi vijñāne vijñātṛsamavāyini || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 10 - Chapter Ten, part 1#^pg332|p. 313]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=332>))
>
> A jar is an object with a large round base etc. A cloth differs from it. It does not assume the form (of a jar with its) large round base and the rest, even if (a jar) is present (near it). Similarly, how can entities that do not possess the quality of perceptibility come to have it, even if the cognitive consciousness (vijñāna) inherent in the subject is present (near it)? (with 10.29cd–31ab)

## Viveka

> [!viveka]- Viveka in English (Dyczkowski, vol. VI, pp. 313–314)
> ![[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 10 - Chapter Ten, part 1#Viveka on 10.29–31]]

> यथाहि पृथुबुध्नादिरूपे कुम्भस्य सत्यपि ॥
> अतदात्मा पटो नैति पृथुबुध्नादिरूपताम् ।
> तथा सत्यपि विज्ञाने विज्ञातृसमवायिनि ॥
> अवेद्यधर्मका भावाः कथं वेद्यत्वमाप्नुयुः ।

यथा खलु घटस्य पृथुबुध्नोदराकारत्वमस्ति इत्यातानवितानवत्त्वात् अपृथु बुध्नोदराकारः पटस्ताद्रूप्यं न यायात्, तथा ज्ञातृसमवायिन्यर्थप्रकाशात्मकेऽपि ज्ञाने स्वयमप्रकाशरूपा भावा नैव प्रकाशमाना भवेयुः-इति वाक्यार्थः ॥ ३० ॥
