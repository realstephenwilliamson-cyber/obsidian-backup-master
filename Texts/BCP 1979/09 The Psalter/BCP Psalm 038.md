# Psalm 38

← [[BCP Psalm 037]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 039]] →

*Domine, ne in furore* · Book One · Eighth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 038]]

**38:1** O LORD, do not rebuke me in your anger; \*  
do not punish me in your wrath. ^38-1

**38:2** For your arrows have already pierced me, \*  
and your hand presses hard upon me. ^38-2

[p. 637]

**38:3** There is no health in my flesh,  
because of your indignation; \*  
there is no soundness in my body, because of my sin. ^38-3

**38:4** For my iniquities overwhelm me; \*  
like a heavy burden they are too much for me to bear. ^38-4

**38:5** My wounds stink and fester \*  
by reason of my foolishness. ^38-5

**38:6** I am utterly bowed down and prostrate; \*  
I go about in mourning all the day long. ^38-6

**38:7** My loins are filled with searing pain; \*  
there is no health in my body. ^38-7

**38:8** I am utterly numb and crushed; \*  
I wail, because of the groaning of my heart. ^38-8

**38:9** O Lord, you know all my desires, \*  
and my sighing is not hidden from you. ^38-9

**38:10** My heart is pounding, my strength has failed me, \*  
and the brightness of my eyes is gone from me. ^38-10

**38:11** My friends and companions draw back from my affliction; \*  
my neighbors stand afar off. ^38-11

**38:12** Those who seek after my life lay snares for me; \*  
those who strive to hurt me speak of my ruin  
and plot treachery all the day long. ^38-12

**38:13** But I am like the deaf who do not hear, \*  
like those who are mute and who do not open their mouth. ^38-13

**38:14** I have become like one who does not hear \*  
and from whose mouth comes no defense. ^38-14

**38:15** For in you, O LORD, have I fixed my hope; \*  
you will answer me, O Lord my God. ^38-15

[p. 638]

**38:16** For I said, "Do not let them rejoice at my expense, \*  
those who gloat over me when my foot slips." ^38-16

**38:17** Truly, I am on the verge of falling, \*  
and my pain is always with me. ^38-17

**38:18** I will confess my iniquity \*  
and be sorry for my sin. ^38-18

**38:19** Those who are my enemies without cause are mighty, \*  
and many in number are those who wrongfully hate me. ^38-19

**38:20** Those who repay evil for good slander me, \*  
because I follow the course that is right. ^38-20

**38:21** O LORD, do not forsake me; \*  
be not far from me, O my God. ^38-21

**38:22** Make haste to help me, \*  
O Lord of my salvation. ^38-22
