## Mūla

तस्यादित उदात्तं तत्कथितं पदवेदिना ।
इत्थं संविदियं याज्यस्वरूपामर्शरूपिणी ॥ ^mula

tasyādita udāttaṃ tatkathitaṃ padavedinā |
itthaṃ saṃvidiyaṃ yājyasvarūpāmarśarūpiṇī || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 07 - Chapter Thirty-Three#^pg199|p. 181]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=199>))
>
> A half vowel also exists, as (the vowels) allow (division) into half measures, wherefore (Pānini), who knows grammar (padavedin), has said that the half measure at the beginning of a middle accent (svarita) is acute (udātta).' (with 33.27bcd–28ab)
>
> In this way, this consciousness is the reflective awareness of the essential nature of the object of worship. And that surrounding circle of wheels (around the centre) is one with consciousness. Many are the ways in which that may be applied (by the devotee), in accord with the various surrounding enclosures (of the attendant deities) of the Lord. (with 33.28cd–29)
