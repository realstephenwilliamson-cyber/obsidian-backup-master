## Mūla

विकल्पनिर्ह्रासवशेन याति विकल्पवन्ध्या परमार्थसत्या ।
संवित्स्वरूपप्रकटत्वमित्थं तत्रावधाने यततां सुबुद्धिः ॥ ^mula

vikalpanirhrāsavaśena yāti vikalpavandhyā paramārthasatyā |
saṃvitsvarūpaprakaṭatvamitthaṃ tatrāvadhāne yatatāṃ subuddhiḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 13 - Chapter Ten, part 4#^pg439|p. 420]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=439>))
>
> Ultimate reali devoid of ideation, becomes the clearly evident state of the essential nature of consciousness in this way by (the progressive) decrease of ideation. The wise man should make an effort to pay close attention there (to that process).
