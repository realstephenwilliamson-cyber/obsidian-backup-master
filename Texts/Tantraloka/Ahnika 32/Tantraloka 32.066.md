## Mūla

यागादौ तन्मध्ये तदवसितौ ज्ञानयोगपरिमर्शे ।
विघ्नप्रशमे पाशच्छेदे मुद्राविधेः समयः ॥ ^mula

yāgādau tanmadhye tadavasitau jñānayogaparimarśe |
vighnapraśame pāśacchede mudrāvidheḥ samayaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 06 - Chapter Thirty-Two#^pg185|p. 167]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=185>))
>
> The (right) time (to apply) the procedure (by which) the Seals (are made) is at the beginning, middle and end of the sacrifice, when contemplating knowledge and Yoga, when pacifying obstacles, and when severing (the disciple’s) fetters (in the course of initiation).
