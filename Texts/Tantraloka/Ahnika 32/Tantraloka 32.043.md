## Mūla

नादं वै शक्तिसद्गर्भं सद्गर्भात्कौलिनीपदम् ।
बीजपञ्चकचारेण शूलभेदक्रमेण तु ॥ ^mula

nādaṃ vai śaktisadgarbhaṃ sadgarbhātkaulinīpadam |
bījapañcakacāreṇa śūlabhedakrameṇa tu || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 06 - Chapter Thirty-Two#^pg173|p. 155]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=173>))
>
> ‘(The Mālinī alphabet) from N to PH is uttered (first). It is the Lord of Kula (Kauleśa), who is like the body. It is the Sound (nāda) that contains the Being of Power (śaktisat). (Then, once uttered and) having traversed into the first wheel, which is squeezed with the foot within the organ (yantra) of the Void (khe yantre), the plane of Kaulinī (that is, Kulakuṇḍalinī) (arises) from the womb of Being. (with 32.42–43ab)
>
> (There,) consciousness should awaken Rudra’s power by moving through the five seeds, by progressively breaking through the Trident, along with the various knots of the trident of the heart. (with 32.43–44ab)
