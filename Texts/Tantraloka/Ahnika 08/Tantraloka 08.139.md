## Mūla

महापरिवहान्तोऽयमृतर्द्धेः प्राङ्मरुत्पथः ।
अग्निकन्या मातरश्च रुद्रशक्त्या त्वधिष्ठिताः ॥ ^mula

mahāparivahānto'yamṛtarddheḥ prāṅmarutpathaḥ |
agnikanyā mātaraśca rudraśaktyā tvadhiṣṭhitāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 06 - Chapter Eight, part 4#^pg192|p. 174]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=192>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 8.139cd–140ab.
>
> This (first) series of winds (thus starts) with Rtarddhi and ends with Mahāparivaha (Great (Conveyor).). (with 8.139ab)
>
> 2) In the second (path of the winds) reside the Mothers and the daughters of fire, presided over by Rudra’s power. 3) Beyond that are the Siddhacaraṇa, born (in this state) by virtue of their deeds. 4) In the fourth are the eight gods of the divine weapons. 5) In the fifth are the elephants of the quarters. 6) Again, in the sixth, is Garuda. 7) In the next one is the (celestial) Ganges. 8) (Nandin, Śiva’s divine mount), the bull is elsewhere (in the eighth). 9) Dakṣa, rightly presided by Brahmā’s power, is in the ninth, and 10) the Vasus, Rudras and lidityas are in the tenth. (with 8.139cd–142ab)

*Lead-in:* एतदेव उपसंहरति --

> महापरिवहान्तोऽयमृतर्द्धेः प्राङ्मरुत्पथः ।

एवमत्र योजनानां सहस्रदशकात् ऋतर्द्धेरारभ्य महापरिवहान्तं षोडशानां वायूनामन्तरालेषु यथोक्तक्रमेण सार्धं शतसप्तकं परिसंख्याय शिष्टं विशेषश्रुत्यभावात् सममेव विभजनीयम्, येन प्रत्येकं शतपञ्चकं सार्धक्रोशा चाष्टसप्ततिर्मानं स्यात् ॥
