## Mūla

विकासं तत्त्वमायाति प्रातिभं तदुदाहृतम् ।
भस्मच्छन्नाग्निवत्स्फौट्यं प्रातिभे गौरवागमात् ॥ ^mula

vikāsaṃ tattvamāyāti prātibhaṃ tadudāhṛtam |
bhasmacchannāgnivatsphauṭyaṃ prātibhe gauravāgamāt || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 07 - Chapter Thirteen, part 3#^pg253|p. 231]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=253>))
>
> That (inner) reality (fattvam) of one who is purified by contemplation (bhāāvanābhāvita), that expands (within him) because his bonds have been severed by the sword of initiation, is said to be this intuitive (knowledge). (with 13.174cd–175ab)
>
> The clarity of (this) intuitive (knowledge) is due to the (excellence) of the teaching that has come down from the teachers (gauravāgamāt). Just as a fire hidden by ashes, (stoked by the wind, blazes, or) a seed sown and watered at the right time grows (abundantly), (this) intuitive (knowledge similarly) should become clear and brilliant (sphuṭet) by (the practice of) Yoga, the sacrificial rite (yāga), and the repeated recitation of Mantra, as taught by the teacher. (with 13.175cd–176)
