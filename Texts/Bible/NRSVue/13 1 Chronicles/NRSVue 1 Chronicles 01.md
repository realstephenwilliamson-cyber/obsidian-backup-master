# 1 Chronicles 1 (NRSVue)

— · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue 1 Chronicles 02|1 Chronicles 2 →]]

## From Adam to Abraham

**1:1** Adam, Seth, Enosh; ^1-1

**1:2** Kenan, Mahalalel, Jared; ^1-2

**1:3** Enoch, Methuselah, Lamech; ^1-3

**1:4** Noah, Shem, Ham, and Japheth. ^1-4

**1:5** The descendants of Japheth: Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Tubal, Meshech, and Tiras. ^1-5

**1:6** The descendants of Gomer: Ashkenaz, Diphath, and Togarmah. ^1-6

**1:7** The descendants of Javan: Elishah, Tarshish, Kittim, and Rodanim. ^1-7

**1:8** The descendants of Ham: Cush and Egypt, Put, and Canaan. ^1-8

**1:9** The descendants of Cush: Seba, Havilah, Sabta, Raama, and Sabteca. The descendants of Raama: Sheba and Dedan. ^1-9

**1:10** Cush became the father of Nimrod; he was the first to be a mighty one on the earth. ^1-10

**1:11** Egypt became the father of Ludim, Anamim, Lehabim, Naphtuhim, ^1-11

**1:12** Pathrusim, Casluhim, and Caphtorim, from whom the Philistines come.^[Heb *Casluhim, from which the Philistines come, Caphtorim*] ^1-12

**1:13** Canaan became the father of Sidon his firstborn, and Heth, ^1-13

**1:14** and the Jebusites, the Amorites, the Girgashites, ^1-14

**1:15** the Hivites, the Arkites, the Sinites, ^1-15

**1:16** the Arvadites, the Zemarites, and the Hamathites. ^1-16

**1:17** The descendants of Shem: Elam, Asshur, Arpachshad, Lud, and Aram. The sons of Aram:^[Cn: Heb omits *and Aram. The sons of*] Uz, Hul, Gether, and Meshech. ^1-17

**1:18** Arpachshad became the father of Shelah, and Shelah became the father of Eber. ^1-18

**1:19** To Eber were born two sons: the name of the one was Peleg (for in his days the earth was divided), and the name of his brother was Joktan. ^1-19

**1:20** Joktan became the father of Almodad, Sheleph, Hazarmaveth, Jerah, ^1-20

**1:21** Hadoram, Uzal, Diklah, ^1-21

**1:22** Ebal, Abimael, Sheba, ^1-22

**1:23** Ophir, Havilah, and Jobab; all these were the descendants of Joktan. ^1-23

**1:24** Shem, Arpachshad, Shelah; ^1-24

**1:25** Eber, Peleg, Reu; ^1-25

**1:26** Serug, Nahor, Terah; ^1-26

**1:27** Abram, that is, Abraham. ^1-27

## From Abraham to Jacob

**1:28** The sons of Abraham: Isaac and Ishmael. ^1-28

**1:29** These are their genealogies: the firstborn of Ishmael was Nebaioth, then Kedar, Adbeel, Mibsam, ^1-29

**1:30** Mishma, Dumah, Massa, Hadad, Tema, ^1-30

**1:31** Jetur, Naphish, and Kedemah. These are the sons of Ishmael. ^1-31

**1:32** The sons of Keturah, Abraham’s concubine: she bore Zimran, Jokshan, Medan, Midian, Ishbak, and Shuah. The sons of Jokshan: Sheba and Dedan. ^1-32

**1:33** The sons of Midian: Ephah, Epher, Hanoch, Abida, and Eldaah. All these were the descendants of Keturah. ^1-33

**1:34** Abraham became the father of Isaac. The sons of Isaac: Esau and Israel. ^1-34

**1:35** The sons of Esau: Eliphaz, Reuel, Jeush, Jalam, and Korah. ^1-35

**1:36** The sons of Eliphaz: Teman, Omar, Zephi, Gatam, Kenaz, Timna, and Amalek. ^1-36

**1:37** The sons of Reuel: Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah. ^1-37

**1:38** The sons of Seir: Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah, Dishon, Ezer, and Dishan. ^1-38

**1:39** The sons of Lotan: Hori and Homam; and Lotan’s sister was Timna. ^1-39

**1:40** The sons of Shobal: Alian, Manahath, Ebal, Shephi, and Onam. The sons of Zibeon: Aiah and Anah. ^1-40

**1:41** The son of Anah: Dishon. The sons of Dishon: Hamran, Eshban, Ithran, and Cheran. ^1-41

**1:42** The sons of Ezer: Bilhan, Zaavan, and Jaakan.^[Or *and Akan*] The sons of Dishan:^[Heb *Dishon*] Uz and Aran. ^1-42

**1:43** These are the kings who reigned in the land of Edom before any king reigned over the Israelites: Bela son of Beor, whose city was called Dinhabah. ^1-43

**1:44** When Bela died, Jobab son of Zerah of Bozrah succeeded him. ^1-44

**1:45** When Jobab died, Husham of the land of the Temanites succeeded him. ^1-45

**1:46** When Husham died, Hadad son of Bedad, who defeated Midian in the country of Moab, succeeded him, and the name of his city was Avith. ^1-46

**1:47** When Hadad died, Samlah of Masrekah succeeded him. ^1-47

**1:48** When Samlah died, Shaul^[Or *Saul*] of Rehoboth on the River succeeded him. ^1-48

**1:49** When Shaul^[Or *Saul*] died, Baal-hanan son of Achbor succeeded him. ^1-49

**1:50** When Baal-hanan died, Hadad succeeded him; the name of his city was Pai, and his wife’s name was Mehetabel daughter of Matred daughter of Me-zahab. ^1-50

**1:51** And Hadad died. The clans^[Or *chiefs*] of Edom were: clans^[Or *chiefs*] Timna, Aliah,^[Or *Alvah*] Jetheth, ^1-51

**1:52** Oholibamah, Elah, Pinon, ^1-52

**1:53** Kenaz, Teman, Mibzar, ^1-53

**1:54** Magdiel, and Iram; these are the clans^[Or *chiefs*] of Edom. ^1-54
