# Psalm 149 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 148|← Psalm 148]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 150|Psalm 150 →]]

## Praise for God’s goodness to Israel

**149:1** Praise the LORD! Sing to the LORD a new song, his praise in the assembly of the faithful. ^149-1

**149:2** Let Israel be glad in its Maker; let the children of Zion rejoice in their King. ^149-2

**149:3** Let them praise his name with dancing, making melody to him with tambourine and lyre. ^149-3

**149:4** For the LORD takes pleasure in his people; he adorns the humble with victory. ^149-4

**149:5** Let the faithful exult in glory; let them sing for joy on their couches. ^149-5

**149:6** Let the high praises of God be in their throats and two-edged swords in their hands, ^149-6

**149:7** to execute vengeance on the nations and punishment on the peoples, ^149-7

**149:8** to bind their kings with fetters and their nobles with chains of iron, ^149-8

**149:9** to execute on them the judgment decreed. This is glory for all his faithful ones. Praise the LORD! ^149-9
