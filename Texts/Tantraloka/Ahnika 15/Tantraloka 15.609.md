## Mūla

तैर्भुक्ते न भवेद्दोषो जलजैः पूर्वदीक्षितैः ।
अवश्यपालनीयत्वात्परतत्त्वेन सङ्गमात् ॥ ^mula

tairbhukte na bhaveddoṣo jalajaiḥ pūrvadīkṣitaiḥ |
avaśyapālanīyatvātparatattvena saṅgamāt || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 10 - Chapter Fifteen, part 9#^pg364|p. 346]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=364>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 15.611cd–612ab.
>
> The food offering (naivedya) should be eaten, and the remnants (Seṣa) thrown into the water of a river. There is no defect if it is eaten by the fish, (for they must have been) previously initiated (in another life). (with 15.608cd–609ab)
>
> They are called pledges (samaya) because they must be observed, because (by means of them one) is connected (saṁgama) with the supreme reality, and because they are the means to attain knowledge. (with 15.609cd–610ab)
