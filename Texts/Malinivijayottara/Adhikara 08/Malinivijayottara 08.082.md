## Mūla

बद्ध्वापि खेचरीं मुद्रां नोत्पतत्यवनीतलात् ।
इत्येतच्छाम्भवं प्रोक्तमष्टान्तं शाक्तमिष्यते ॥ ^mula

baddhvāpi khecarīṁ mudrāṁ notpataty avanītalāt |
ity etac chāmbhavaṁ proktam aṣṭāntaṁ śāktam iṣyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2025 – April 2025
> p. 24 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=38>))
>
> This is Trident, which is it said has one staff and three powers. O fair faced (goddess), without knowing the Trident of (the three) powers (to be such) in this way, although he forms the gesture of the Skyfaring Goddess, he does not fly up from the surface of the earth.[^85] (with 8.81–82ab)

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2025 – April 2025
> p. 24 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=38>))
>
> Thus, this is said to be the Divine (śāmbhava) (Trident). The Empowered one (śākta) is considered (to reach up only to) the end of eight (fingers' space above the Cavity of Brahmā). One should know that the Individual (Trident) ends four (fingers' space above it). This is considered to be the triple Trident.[^86] (with 8.82cd–83ab)

[^85]: MV 8/81cd-82ab is quoted in TĀv ad 15/361cd-363ab (361-362). TĀ 15/(361cd-365ab) (361-364) expands and explains MV 8/81-83ab, 84ab.
[^86]: MV 8/82cd-83ab quoted in TĀv ad 15/363cd-364ab (363) of which it is a paraphrase. See TĀ 15/361cd-363ab (361-362).
