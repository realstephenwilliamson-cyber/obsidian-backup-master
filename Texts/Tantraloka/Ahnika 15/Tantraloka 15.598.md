## Mūla

एवमन्येऽप्युदाहार्याः कुलगह्वरवर्त्मना ।
अतत्स्वभावे ताद्रूप्यं दर्शयन्नवशेऽपि यः ॥ ^mula

evamanye'pyudāhāryāḥ kulagahvaravartmanā |
atatsvabhāve tādrūpyaṃ darśayannavaśe'pi yaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 10 - Chapter Fifteen, part 9#^pg361|p. 343]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=361>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 15.600cd–601ab.
>
> In the same way, other (errors) can be exemplified in relation to the path (taught in the) Kulagahvara. (with 15.598ab)
>
> A tenacious error (graha) is that which, causing one to perceive, even if involuntarily, something that is of a certain nature as not being of that nature, obscures its own (true) nature. (Thus) it is said to be like a demon (graha) (that takes possession of people). (with 15.598cd–599ab)
