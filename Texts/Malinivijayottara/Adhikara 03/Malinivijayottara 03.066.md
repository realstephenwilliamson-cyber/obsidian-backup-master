## Mūla

लरटक्षवयैर्दीर्घैः सूमायुक्तैः सबिन्दुकैः ।

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2023 – March 2023
> p. 52 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=52>))
>
> **The Guardians of the Quarters and their Weapons**
>
> The skillful (adept) should fashion (the mantras of) Indra and the other (guardians of the quarters) with (the phonemes) L R Ṭ KṢ V Y along with SŪ and MĀ[^57] (for the other two, Kubera and Īśāna) with (the six) long (vowels Ā Ī Ū AI AU AḤ) and (a final) Point (Ṁ) and their weapons with short (vowels).

[^56]: Read with TĀ 30/42b *samāyuktaiḥ* ‘conjoined with’ for *sūmāyuktaiḥ*
[^57]: This is also the reading of the printed etext, but it is corrected by the editor Kaula in his list of corrections (*śodhapatra*) printed as an appendix at the end of the edition. The mantras of the guardians of the quarters are given in note to TĀ 30/42-43ab.
