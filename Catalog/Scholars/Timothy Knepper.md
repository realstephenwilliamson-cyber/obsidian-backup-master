# Timothy Knepper

Professor of Philosophy at Drake University, where he directs The Comparison Project, a public program in comparative philosophy of religion; he has written on the future of the philosophy of religion, on Pseudo-Dionysius, and a "global-critical" introduction to philosophies of religion ([Drake University](https://www.drake.edu/artsci/faculty/profiles/timothy-knepper)).
