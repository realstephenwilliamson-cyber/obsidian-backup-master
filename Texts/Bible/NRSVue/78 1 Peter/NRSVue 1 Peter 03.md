# 1 Peter 3 (NRSVue)

## Wives and husbands

**3:1** Wives, in the same way, be subject to your husbands, so that, even if some of them do not obey the word, they may be won over without a word by their wives’ conduct, ^3-1

**3:2** when they see the purity and respect of your conduct. ^3-2

**3:3** Do not adorn yourselves outwardly by braiding your hair and by wearing gold ornaments or fine clothing; ^3-3

**3:4** rather, let your adornment be the inner self with the lasting beauty of a gentle and quiet spirit, which is very precious in God’s sight. ^3-4

**3:5** It was in this way long ago that the holy women who hoped in God used to adorn themselves by being subject to their husbands. ^3-5

**3:6** Thus Sarah obeyed Abraham and called him lord. You have become her daughters as long as you do what is good and never let fears alarm you. ^3-6

**3:7** Husbands, in the same way, show consideration for your wives in your life together, paying honor to the woman—though the weaker vessel,^[Or *body*] they are joint heirs of the gracious gift of life—so that nothing may hinder your prayers. ^3-7

## Suffering for doing right

**3:8** Finally, all of you, have unity of spirit, sympathy, love for one another, a tender heart, and a humble mind. ^3-8

**3:9** Do not repay evil for evil or abuse for abuse, but, on the contrary, repay with a blessing. It is for this that you were called—that you might inherit a blessing. ^3-9

**3:10** For “Those who desire to love life and to see good days, let them keep their tongues from evil and their lips from speaking deceit; ^3-10

**3:11** let them turn away from evil and do good; let them seek peace and pursue it. ^3-11

**3:12** For the eyes of the Lord are on the righteous, and his ears are open to their prayer. But the face of the Lord is against those who do evil.” ^3-12

**3:13** Now who will harm you if you are eager to do what is good? ^3-13

**3:14** But even if you do suffer for doing what is right,^[Or *for righteousness’ sake*] you are blessed. Do not fear what they fear,^[Gk *their fear*] and do not be intimidated, ^3-14

**3:15** but in your hearts sanctify Christ as Lord. Always be ready to make your defense to anyone who demands from you an accounting for the hope that is in you, ^3-15

**3:16** yet do it with gentleness and respect. Maintain a good conscience so that, when you are maligned,^[Other ancient authorities read *when they malign you as evildoers*] those who abuse you for your good conduct in Christ may be put to shame. ^3-16

**3:17** For it is better to suffer for doing good, if suffering should be God’s will, than to suffer for doing evil. ^3-17

**3:18** For Christ also suffered^[Other ancient authorities read *died*] for sins once for all, the righteous for the unrighteous, in order to bring you^[Other ancient authorities read *us*] to God. He was put to death in the flesh but made alive in the spirit, ^3-18

**3:19** in which also he went and made a proclamation to the spirits in prison, ^3-19

**3:20** who in former times did not obey, when God waited patiently in the days of Noah, during the building of the ark, in which a few, that is, eight lives, were saved through water. ^3-20

**3:21** And baptism, which this prefigured, now saves you—not as a removal of dirt from the body but as an appeal to God for^[Or *a pledge to God from*] a good conscience, through the resurrection of Jesus Christ, ^3-21

**3:22** who has gone into heaven and is at the right hand of God, with angels, authorities, and powers made subject to him. ^3-22
