## Mūla

श्रोत्राकाशगतस्यास्ति दूरादूरस्वभावता ।
न चासौ प्रथमः शब्दस्तावद्व्यापीति युज्यते ॥ ^mula

śrotrākāśagatasyāsti dūrādūrasvabhāvatā |
na cāsau prathamaḥ śabdastāvadvyāpīti yujyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 09 - Chapter Nine, part 7#^pg295|p. 276]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=295>))
>
> Once the echo of a sound, whether distant or near, enters the space of the (cavity of the) auditory organ, it would be neither distant nor near. (with 9.302cd–303ab)
>
> Moreover, it makes no sense (to say) (na yujyate) that the first (original) sound is thus pervasive. (If that were true,) then why should those who are there (close to where the sound originates) hear that it is very loud, and (those at a distance), along with them, perceive it as soft? (The same object) cannot possess differing (conflicting) qualities. (with 9.303cd–304)
