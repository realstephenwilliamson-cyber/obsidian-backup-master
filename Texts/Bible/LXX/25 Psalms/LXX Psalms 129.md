# LXX Psalms 129

← [[LXX Psalms 128]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 130]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 130]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 130]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**129:1** A Song of Degrees. Out of the depths have I cried to thee, O Lord.  ^129-1

**129:2** O Lord, hearken to my voice; let thine ears be attentive to the voice of my supplication.  ^129-2

**129:3** If thou, O Lord, shouldest mark iniquities, O Lord, who shall stand?  ^129-3

**129:4** For with thee is forgiveness: for thy name's sake[^1][^2]  ^129-4

**129:5** have I waited for thee, O Lord, my soul has waited for thy word.  ^129-5

**129:6** My soul has hoped in the Lord; from the morning watch till night.  ^129-6

**129:7** Let Israel hope in the Lord: for with the Lord is mercy, and with him is plenteous redemption.  ^129-7

**129:8** And he shall redeem Israel from all his iniquities.  ^129-8

[^1]: 129:4 *Or,* propitiation.
[^2]: 129:4 See Gen. 31. 53.
