# The State as a Technical System

> [!abstract] Definition
> The realization that the state does not merely *use* technologies, but is itself structurally a technical megamachine—an organized system of [[Tertiary Retention|tertiary retentions]], archival infrastructures, and legal algorithms that manage the temporal horizon of its subjects.

## Conceptual Synthesis
Synthesizing:
1. **Thomas Hobbes:** The body politic as a mechanical automaton (see [[Hobbesian Artificial Man]]).
2. **Bernard Stiegler:** Society as the stabilization and governance of a technical system.
3. **Norbert Wiener:** Cybernetics as the science of control and communication in the animal and the machine (see [[Wiener - Cybernetics|Wiener (1948)]]).

The modern nation-state survived industrialization by harmonizing its technical system (steam, rail, telegraph) with its legal and educational institutions. Today's crisis occurs because digital computation develops at light speed while legislative and juridical structures operate at geological speed, creating a catastrophic structural desynchronization.
