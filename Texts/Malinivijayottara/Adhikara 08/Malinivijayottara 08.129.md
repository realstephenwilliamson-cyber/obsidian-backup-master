## Mūla

देवतादर्शनं साक्षात्तथा विष्ठानुलेपनम् ।
एवंविधं शुभं दृष्ट्वा सिद्धिं प्राप्नोत्यभीप्सिताम् ॥ ^mula

devatādarśanaṁ sākṣāt tathā viṣṭhānulepanam |
evaṁvidhaṁ śubhaṁ dṛṣṭvā siddhiṁ prāpnoty abhīpsitām || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2025 – April 2025
> p. 36 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=50>))
>
> Auspicious dreams such those of flowers, fruits and the like, women with beautiful clothes and ornaments, escape from a calamity, ascent[^144] into auspicious lands, the drinking of liquor, decapitation, the eating of raw meat, the direct vision of deities, the smearing with faces – having seen such an auspicious (dream), he obtains the accomplishment (*siddhi*) he desires. (with 8.127cd–129)

[^144]: Read with TĀ 15/48cd *adhirohaṇam* for *avarohaṇam.*
