## Mūla

तथा शिवोऽहं नान्योऽस्मीत्येवं भावयतस्तथा ।
एतदेवोच्यते दार्ढ्यं विमर्शहृदयङ्गमम् ॥ ^mula

tathā śivo'haṃ nānyo'smītyevaṃ bhāvayatastathā |
etadevocyate dārḍhyaṃ vimarśahṛdayaṅgamam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 05 - Chapter Fifteen, part 4#^pg179|p. 161]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=179>))
>
> Just as a person who thinks intensely that he is a sinner becomes one, he who thinks that he is Śiva and is none other than him, becomes such. (with 15.269cd–270ab)
>
> This itself is said to be the stability (dārdhya) (of insight) that is taken to heart by (the adept’s) reflective awareness (as true and certain), namely, the state free of thought constructs (that arises) by means of the flux of thought constructs (concerning his) oneness with Śiva. (with 15.270cd–271ab)

*Lead-in:* ननु प्राग्विकल्पाभ्यासपाटवात् निर्विकल्पकता भवतीत्युक्तं, तत् कथमिह शिवत्वं भवेदित्युच्यते?--इत्याशङ्क्य आह--

> एतदेवोच्यते दार्ढ्यं विमर्शहृदयङ्गमम् ॥
