## Mūla

श्रीब्रह्मयामलेऽप्युक्तं पात्रं गोमुखमुत्तमम् ।
गजकूर्मतलं कुम्भवृत्तशक्तिकजाकृति ॥ ^mula

śrībrahmayāmale'pyuktaṃ pātraṃ gomukhamuttamam |
gajakūrmatalaṃ kumbhavṛttaśaktikajākṛti || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 18 - Chapter Twenty-Seven#^pg433|p. 413]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=433>))
>
> Tt is also said in the venerable Brahmayāmala that the best vessel (i.e. a skull) is (one that is shaped like) the head of a cow, its base (round like) a tortoise or elephant, and form like the female organ (śaktikaja),it (should be) round like an elephant’s forehead (kumbha).
