## Mūla

ते एव शक्ती ताद्रूप्यभागिन्यौ नान्यथास्थिते ।
नन्वनुत्तरतानन्दौ स्वात्मना भेदवर्जितौ ॥ ^mula

te eva śaktī tādrūpyabhāginyau nānyathāsthite |
nanvanuttaratānandau svātmanā bhedavarjitau || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. II
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 02 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v02 07 - Chapter Three, part 4#^pg220|p. 204]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 02.pdf#page=220>))
>
> Although, the aroused state (kṣobha) of the (powers of) the Will and Expansion (previously) explained (to be Ī and Ū) may be associated (again) with those same energies (of the Absolute (A) and Bliss (Ā)), they assume this same form (namely, E, AI, O, AU), not some other state. (with 3.97cd–98ab)
>
> (One may ask): “Surely, the Absolute and Bliss are, in themselves, devoid of differences (bheda); how then is this wonderfully diverse emanation within them (possible)?” (with 3.98cd–99ab)
