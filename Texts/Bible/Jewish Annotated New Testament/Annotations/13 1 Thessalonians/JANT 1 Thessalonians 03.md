# 1 Thessalonians 3 (JANT notes)

### 3.1-5: Timothy’s mission to the Thessalonians

*[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 03#^3-1|Text]], p. 423* ^3-1

**[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 03#^3-1|1]]:** *Athens*, suggesting that Paul was writing from Athens. ^3-1-2

**[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 03#^3-2|2]]:** *Timothy*, 1.1n. *Brother*, a common form of address expressing biological or spiritual kinship, e.g., [[Torah 33 - Bechukotai#^26-36|Lev. 26.36]]; [[Acts 13#^13-15|Acts 13.15]]; 1QS 6.10. *Co*-*worker*, Paul’s term for his closest collaborators ([[Romans 16#^16-9|Rom 16.9]],[[Romans 16#^16-21|21]]; [[2 Corinthians 08#^8-23|2 Cor 8.23]]). *Strengthen and encourage*, see Philo, *Dreams* 1.158; 2.11. ^3-2

**[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 03#^3-3|3]]:** *Destined for*, the idea of predestination typifies the DSS, e.g., 1QS 3.15–16. ^3-3

**[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 03#^3-4|4]]:** *Suffer persecution* (Gk “thlibesthai”) or “undergo tribulation.” See 1.10n. ^3-4

**[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 03#^3-5|5]]:** *Tempter . . . tempted*, the Gk word means “test, put to the proof” (see 2.18 as well as [[1 Corinthians 05#^5-5|1 Cor 5.5]]; [[1 Corinthians 07#^7-5|7.5]]; [[1 Corinthians 10#^10-10|10.10]]; [[2 Corinthians 02#^2-11|2 Cor 2.11]]; [[2 Corinthians 04#^4-4|4.4]]; [[2 Corinthians 06#^6-15|6.15]]; [[2 Corinthians 12#^12-7|12.7]]). Satan tempts or goads humans to sin, see [[Job 01#^1-6|Job 1.6–12]]; some rabbis identified Satan with the serpent in Gen 3 (*b. Sot.* [[Sotah 9#9b|9b]]). ^3-5

### 3.6–10: Paul expresses relief and elation

*[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 03#^3-6|Text]], p. 423* ^3-6

**[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 03#^3-6|6]]:** *Timothy* likely brought a letter to Paul from the Thessalonians containing questions that arose in the community. ^3-6-2

**[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 03#^3-9|9]]:** *Joy that we feel*, lit., “joy that we rejoice,” a Hebraism (see [[LXX Isaiah 66#^66-10|Isa 66.10]]\[LXX\]; [[Matthew 02#^2-10|Mt 2.10]]). ^3-9

**[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 03#^3-10|10]]:** *Night and day*, in Judaism the day begins at sunset, see *m. Hul*. 5.5: “Just as one day stated with reference to the works of creation means the day with the preceding night, so one day stated with reference to it and its young \[means\] the day with the preceding night.” *Lacking in your faith*, perhaps based on Timothy’s report. ^3-10

### 3.11–13: Prayer concluding first part of the letter

*[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 03#^3-11|Text]], p. 423* · The themes in the following chs—sanctification (4.3–8), love (4.9–12), and the return of the Messiah (4.15–5.11)—first appear in this prayer. ^3-11

**[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 03#^3-11|11]]:** *Father,* see 1.1n. ^3-11-2

**[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 03#^3-12|12]]:** *Lord*, see 1.1 ^3-12

**[[NRSVue 1 Thessalonians 03#^3-13|13]]:** *Strengthen your hearts*, see [[LXX Psalms 111#^111-8|Ps 111.8]]\[LXX\]; [[LXX Sirach 06#^6-37|Sir 6.37]]; [[LXX Sirach 22#^22-16|22.16]]. *Holiness*, through faith and proper behavior. *Coming . . . saints*, believers (Gk “hagioi,” lit., “holy ones”) who have died (see 4.14); see [[Daniel 07#^7-18|Dan 7.18]],[[LXX Daniel (Greek) 07#^7-22|22]]\[LXX\] and 1QM 3.5. ^3-13
