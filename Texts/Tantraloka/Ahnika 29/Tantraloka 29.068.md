## Mūla

बीजं सा पीडयते रसशल्कविभागतोऽत्र कुण्डलिनी ।
अध्युष्टपीठनेत्री कन्दस्था विश्वतो भ्रमति ॥ ^mula

bījaṃ sā pīḍayate rasaśalkavibhāgato'tra kuṇḍalinī |
adhyuṣṭapīṭhanetrī kandasthā viśvato bhramati || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 10 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 2#^pg279|p. 262]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=279>))
>
> Here (Cakriṇī) is Kuṇdalinī, who crushes the seed, separating the juice from the husk. She is the leader of the sacred seat (called) ‘three and a half’, and residing within the Bulb (in the foundation of the body — kanda) wanders everywhere (throughout the universe). °
