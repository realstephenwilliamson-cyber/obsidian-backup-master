## Mūla

हृच्चक्रे प्रतिसंधत्ते बलात्पूर्णकृशात्मकौ ।
हठनिर्मलचन्द्रार्कप्रकाशः सत्यमीक्षते ॥ ^mula

hṛccakre pratisaṃdhatte balātpūrṇakṛśātmakau |
haṭhanirmalacandrārkaprakāśaḥ satyamīkṣate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 09 - Chapter Fifteen, part 8#^pg311|p. 293]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=311>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 15.481cd–482ab.
>
> When the disciple has (just) fallen asleep, he gathers together (and contemplates) forcefully in the wheel of (his) heart the pure Moon and Sun (of inhalation and exhalation) that are full and depleted. (with 15.478cd–479ab)
>
> (Possessing as his) light that of the Moon and Sun, pure (by the practice of this) forceful (katha yoga,) he sees a dream that is true and is capable of clearly reflecting on future (events), both auspicious and otherwise. (with 15.479cd–480ab)
