# Psalm 141

← [[BCP Psalm 140]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 142]] →

*Domine, clamavi* · Book Five · Twenty-ninth Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 141]]

**141:1** O LORD, I call to you; come to me quickly; \*  
hear my voice when I cry to you. ^141-1

**141:2** Let my prayer be set forth in your sight as incense, \*  
the lifting up of my hands as the evening sacrifice. ^141-2

**141:3** Set a watch before my mouth, O LORD,  
and guard the door of my lips; \*  
let not my heart incline to any evil thing. ^141-3

**141:4** Let me not be occupied in wickedness with evildoers, \*  
nor eat of their choice foods. ^141-4

**141:5** Let the righteous smite me in friendly rebuke;  
let not the oil of the unrighteous anoint my head; \*  
for my prayer is continually against their wicked deeds. ^141-5

**141:6** Let their rulers be overthrown in stony places, \*  
that they may know my words are true. ^141-6

**141:7** As when a plowman turns over the earth in furrows, \*  
let their bones be scattered at the mouth of the grave. ^141-7

**141:8** But my eyes are turned to you, Lord GOD; \*  
in you I take refuge;  
do not strip me of my life. ^141-8

**141:9** Protect me from the snare which they have laid for me \*  
and from the traps of the evildoers. ^141-9

**141:10** Let the wicked fall into their own nets, \*  
while I myself escape. ^141-10
