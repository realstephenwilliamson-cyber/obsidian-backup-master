## Mūla

सांख्यवेदादिसंसिद्धाञ्छ्रीकण्ठस्तदहर्मुखे ।
सृजत्येव पुनस्तेन न सम्यङ् मुक्तिरीदृशी ॥ ^mula

sāṃkhyavedādisaṃsiddhāñchrīkaṇṭhastadaharmukhe |
sṛjatyeva punastena na samyaṅ muktirīdṛśī || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 11 - Chapter Six, part 4#^pg386|p. 371]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=386>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 6.152cd–153ab.
>
> At the beginning of a (new) day (of creation), Śrīkantha again emits those who have mastered the Sāṁṅkhya or the Veda and the like. Therefore, this kind of liberation is not proper (and complete) (samyak).
