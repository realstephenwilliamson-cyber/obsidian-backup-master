## Mūla

अतत्त्ववेदिनो ये हि चर्यामात्रैकनिष्ठिताः ।
तेषां दोलायिते चित्ते ज्ञानहानिः प्रजायते ॥ ^mula

atattvavedino ye hi caryāmātraikaniṣṭhitāḥ |
teṣāṃ dolāyite citte jñānahāniḥ prajāyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 07 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 5#^pg207|p. 190]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=207>))
>
> Those who do not know reality and are entirely dedicated to observance (caryā) alone, when their mind is made to vacillate (by uncertainty), their knowledge is destroyed.
