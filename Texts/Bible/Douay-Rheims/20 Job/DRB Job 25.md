# Job 25 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Job 24|← Job 24]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Job 26|Job 26 →]]

**25:1** Then Baldad the Suhite answered, and I said: ^25-1

**25:2** Power and terror are with him, who maketh peace in his high places. ^25-2

**25:3** Is there any numbering of his soldiers? and upon whom shall not his light arise? ^25-3

**25:4** Can man be justified compared with God, or he that is born of a woman appear clean? ^25-4

**25:5** Behold even the moon doth not shine, and the stars are not pure in his sight. ^25-5

**25:6** How much less man that is rottenness and the son of man who is a worm? ^25-6
