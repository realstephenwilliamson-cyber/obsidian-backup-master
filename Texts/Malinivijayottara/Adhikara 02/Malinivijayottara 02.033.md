## Mūla

शिवश्चेति परिज्ञेयाः पञ्चैव वरवर्णिनि ।
स्वम्शक्तिः सनिजेशाना शक्तिशम्भू च पञ्चके ॥ ^mula

śivaś ceti parijñeyāḥ pañcaiva varavarṇini |
svam śaktiḥ sanijeśānā[^48] śaktiśambhū ca pañcake || ^iast

> (E) O fair-hued Lady, again, when (the Mantra perceivers are the object and so) the division is into seven, the five (states) should be known to be (divided) as before as (1) (their) innate nature, (2) the Lords of Mantra, (3) (their) Lords (the Great Lords of Mantra), (4) Śakti and (5) Śiva. (with 2.32cd–33ab)
