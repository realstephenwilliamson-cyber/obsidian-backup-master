# 1 Chronicles 8 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue 1 Chronicles 07|← 1 Chronicles 7]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue 1 Chronicles 09|1 Chronicles 9 →]]

## Descendants of Benjamin

**8:1** Benjamin became the father of Bela his firstborn, Ashbel the second, Aharah the third, ^8-1

**8:2** Nohah the fourth, and Rapha the fifth. ^8-2

**8:3** And Bela had sons: Addar, Gera, Abihud,^[Or *father of Ehud*] ^8-3

**8:4** Abishua, Naaman, Ahoah, ^8-4

**8:5** Gera, Shephuphan, and Huram. ^8-5

**8:6** These are the sons of Ehud (they were heads of ancestral houses of the inhabitants of Geba, and they were carried into exile to Manahath): ^8-6

**8:7** Naaman,^[Heb *and Naaman*] Ahijah, and Gera, that is, Heglam,^[Or *he carried them into exile*] who became the father of Uzza and Ahihud. ^8-7

**8:8** And Shaharaim had sons in the country of Moab after he had sent away his wives Hushim and Baara. ^8-8

**8:9** He had sons by his wife Hodesh: Jobab, Zibia, Mesha, Malcam, ^8-9

**8:10** Jeuz, Sachia, and Mirmah. These were his sons, heads of ancestral houses. ^8-10

**8:11** He also had sons by Hushim: Abitub and Elpaal. ^8-11

**8:12** The sons of Elpaal: Eber, Misham, and Shemed, who built Ono and Lod with its towns, ^8-12

**8:13** and Beriah and Shema (they were heads of ancestral houses of the inhabitants of Aijalon who put to flight the inhabitants of Gath), ^8-13

**8:14** and Ahio, Shashak, and Jeremoth. ^8-14

**8:15** Zebadiah, Arad, Eder, ^8-15

**8:16** Michael, Ishpah, and Joha were the sons of Beriah. ^8-16

**8:17** Zebadiah, Meshullam, Hizki, Heber, ^8-17

**8:18** Ishmerai, Izliah, and Jobab were the sons of Elpaal. ^8-18

**8:19** Jakim, Zichri, Zabdi, ^8-19

**8:20** Elienai, Zillethai, Eliel, ^8-20

**8:21** Adaiah, Beraiah, and Shimrath were the sons of Shimei. ^8-21

**8:22** Ishpan, Eber, Eliel, ^8-22

**8:23** Abdon, Zichri, Hanan, ^8-23

**8:24** Hananiah, Elam, Anthothijah, ^8-24

**8:25** Iphdeiah, and Penuel were the sons of Shashak. ^8-25

**8:26** Shamsherai, Shehariah, Athaliah, ^8-26

**8:27** Jaareshiah, Elijah, and Zichri were the sons of Jeroham. ^8-27

**8:28** These were the heads of ancestral houses, according to their generations, chiefs. These lived in Jerusalem. ^8-28

**8:29** Jeiel^[Heb lacks *Jeiel*] the father of Gibeon lived in Gibeon, and the name of his wife was Maacah. ^8-29

**8:30** His firstborn son: Abdon, then Zur, Kish, Baal, Nadab, ^8-30

**8:31** Gedor, Ahio, Zecher, ^8-31

**8:32** and Mikloth, who became the father of Shimeah. Now these also lived opposite their kindred in Jerusalem, with their kindred. ^8-32

**8:33** Ner became the father of Kish, Kish of Saul,^[Or *Shaul*] Saul^[Or *Shaul*] of Jonathan, Malchishua, Abinadab, and Esh-baal; ^8-33

**8:34** and the son of Jonathan was Merib-baal; and Merib-baal became the father of Micah. ^8-34

**8:35** The sons of Micah: Pithon, Melech, Tarea, and Ahaz. ^8-35

**8:36** Ahaz became the father of Jehoaddah; and Jehoaddah became the father of Alemeth, Azmaveth, and Zimri; Zimri became the father of Moza. ^8-36

**8:37** Moza became the father of Binea; Raphah was his son, Eleasah his son, Azel his son. ^8-37

**8:38** Azel had six sons, and these are their names: Azrikam, Bocheru,^[Or *his firstborn*] Ishmael, Sheariah, Obadiah, and Hanan; all these were the sons of Azel. ^8-38

**8:39** The sons of his brother Eshek: Ulam his firstborn, Jeush the second, and Eliphelet the third. ^8-39

**8:40** The sons of Ulam were mighty warriors, archers, having many children and grandchildren, one hundred fifty. All these were Benjaminites. ^8-40
