# Job 30 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Job 29|← Job 29]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Job 31|Job 31 →]]

**30:1** “But now they make sport of me, those who are younger than I, whose fathers I would have disdained to set with the dogs of my flock. ^30-1

**30:2** What could I gain from the strength of their hands? All their vigor is gone. ^30-2

**30:3** Through want and hard hunger they gnaw the dry and desolate ground; ^30-3

**30:4** they pick mallow and the leaves of bushes and to warm themselves the roots of broom. ^30-4

**30:5** They are driven out from society; people shout after them as after a thief. ^30-5

**30:6** In the gullies of wadis they must live, in holes in the ground and in the rocks. ^30-6

**30:7** Among the bushes they bray; under the nettles they huddle together. ^30-7

**30:8** A senseless, disreputable brood, they have been whipped out of the land. ^30-8

**30:9** “And now they mock me in song; I am a byword to them. ^30-9

**30:10** They abhor me; they keep aloof from me; they do not hesitate to spit at the sight of me. ^30-10

**30:11** Because God has loosed my bowstring and humbled me, they have cast off restraint in my presence. ^30-11

**30:12** On my right hand the rabble rise up; they send me sprawling and build roads for my ruin. ^30-12

**30:13** They break up my path; they promote my calamity; no one restrains^[Cn: Heb *helps*] them. ^30-13

**30:14** As through a wide breach they come; amid the crash they roll on. ^30-14

**30:15** Terrors are turned upon me; my honor is pursued as by the wind, and my prosperity has passed away like a cloud. ^30-15

**30:16** “And now my soul is poured out within me; days of affliction have taken hold of me. ^30-16

**30:17** The night racks my bones, and the pain that gnaws me takes no rest. ^30-17

**30:18** With violence he seizes my garment;^[Gk: Heb *my garment is disfigured*] he grasps me by^[Heb *like*] the collar of my tunic. ^30-18

**30:19** He has cast me into the mire, and I have become like dust and ashes. ^30-19

**30:20** I cry to you, and you do not answer me; I stand, and you merely look at me. ^30-20

**30:21** You have turned cruel to me; with the might of your hand you persecute me. ^30-21

**30:22** You lift me up on the wind, you make me ride on it, and you toss me about in the roar of the storm. ^30-22

**30:23** I know that you will bring me to death, to the house appointed for all living. ^30-23

**30:24** “Surely one does not turn against the needy,^[Heb *ruin*] when in disaster they cry for help.^[Cn: Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^30-24

**30:25** Did I not weep for those whose day was hard? Was not my soul grieved for the poor? ^30-25

**30:26** But when I looked for good, evil came, and when I waited for light, darkness came. ^30-26

**30:27** My inward parts are in turmoil and are never still; days of affliction come to meet me. ^30-27

**30:28** I go about in sunless gloom; I stand up in the assembly and cry for help. ^30-28

**30:29** I am a brother of jackals and a companion of ostriches. ^30-29

**30:30** My skin turns black and falls from me, and my bones burn with heat. ^30-30

**30:31** My lyre is turned to mourning and my pipe to the voice of those who weep. ^30-31
