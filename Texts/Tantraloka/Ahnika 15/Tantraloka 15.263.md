## Mūla

कृते शान्ते शिवे रूढः पुनः किमवरोहति ।
इति ते दूरतो ध्वस्ताः परमार्थं हि शांभवम् ॥ ^mula

kṛte śānte śive rūḍhaḥ punaḥ kimavarohati |
iti te dūrato dhvastāḥ paramārthaṃ hi śāṃbhavam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 05 - Chapter Fifteen, part 4#^pg176|p. 158]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=176>))
>
> Thus, those foolish people are totally refuted who ask how it is that after having shaken off the fetters by burning them, and Śiva (assumes his) tranquil (śānta) (state), (the officiant) who is immersed in Him descends again. Devoid of the essence of the effulgence of their own consciousness, they do not know the ultimate reality (paramārtha) of Śiva. (with 15.262cd–264ab)
