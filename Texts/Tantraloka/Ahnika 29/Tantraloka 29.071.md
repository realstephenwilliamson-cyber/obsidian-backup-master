## Mūla

त्रिकं त्रिकं यजेदेतद्भाविस्वत्रिकसंयुतम् ।
हृत्कुण्डली भ्रुवोर्मध्यमेतदेव क्रमात् त्रयम् ॥ ^mula

trikaṃ trikaṃ yajedetadbhāvisvatrikasaṃyutam |
hṛtkuṇḍalī bhruvormadhyametadeva kramāt trayam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 10 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 2#^pg283|p. 266]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=283>))
>
> (In each direction, that is,) the northeast, east, southeast, south, southwest (kravyād), west (ka), northwest and north, one should worship (the sacred seats) three (at a time,) commencing with Aṭṭahāsa, along with their own (corresponding) triad, as will be (taught). (with 29.70cd–71ab)
>
> (One should worship) this triad in due order, (namely) the Heart, Kundali, and the centre between the eyebrows. (It corresponds to the three) beginning with the cremation ground, (the wish-granting) tree and the host of true (and good) Yoginīs, that has arisen from the (sacred) site. (with 29.71cd–72ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* तदेव आह--

> हृत्कुण्डली भ्रुवोर्मध्यमेतदेव क्रमात् त्रयम् ॥
> श्मशानानि क्रमात्क्षेत्रभवं सद्योगिनीगणम् ।

यजेदिति पूर्वेण अत्र सम्बन्धः । क्षेत्रभवमियत्तत्पीठजातम्-- इत्यर्थः । तदुक्तम्--

".........................ॠइशकोणादितः क्रमात् ।

पूर्वदक्षिणवारुण्यः सौम्या याश्च दिशः प्रिये ॥"

इति,

"श्मशानं हृत्प्रदेशः स्यात्कल्पवृक्षस्तु कुण्डली ।

भ्रूमध्यं योगिनीक्षेत्रं ज्ञातव्यं योगिनीकुले ॥"

इति च ॥
