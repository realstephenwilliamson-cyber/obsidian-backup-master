# Psalm 113

← [[BCP Psalm 112]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 114]] →

*Laudate, pueri* · Book Five · Twenty-third Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 113]]

**113:1** Hallelujah!  
Give praise, you servants of the LORD; \*  
praise the Name of the LORD. ^113-1

**113:2** Let the Name of the LORD be blessed, \*  
from this time forth for evermore. ^113-2

**113:3** From the rising of the sun to its going down \*  
let the Name of the LORD be praised. ^113-3

**113:4** The LORD is high above all nations, \*  
and his glory above the heavens. ^113-4

**113:5** Who is like the LORD our God, who sits enthroned on high, \*  
but stoops to behold the heavens and the earth? ^113-5

**113:6** He takes up the weak out of the dust \*  
and lifts up the poor from the ashes. ^113-6

**113:7** He sets them with the princes, \*  
with the princes of his people. ^113-7

**113:8** He makes the woman of a childless house \*  
to be a joyful mother of children. ^113-8
