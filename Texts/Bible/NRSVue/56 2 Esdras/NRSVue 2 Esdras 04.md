# 2 Esdras 4 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue 2 Esdras 03|← 2 Esdras 3]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue 2 Esdras 05|2 Esdras 5 →]]

## Limitations of the human mind

**4:1** Then the angel that had been sent to me, whose name was Uriel, answered ^4-1

**4:2** and said to me, “Your understanding has utterly failed regarding this world, and do you think you can comprehend the way of the Most High?” ^4-2

**4:3** Then I said, “Yes, my lord.” And he replied to me, “I have been sent to show you three ways and to put before you three problems. ^4-3

**4:4** If you can solve one of them for me, then I will show you the way you desire to see and will teach you why the heart is evil.” ^4-4

**4:5** I said, “Speak, my lord.” And he said to me, “Go, weigh for me the weight of fire, or measure for me a blast^[Syr Ethiop Arab 1 Arab 2 Georg *a measure*] of wind, or call back for me the day that is past.” ^4-5

**4:6** I answered and said, “Who of those that have been born can do that, that you should ask me about such things?” ^4-6

**4:7** And he said to me, “If I had asked you, ‘How many dwellings are in the heart of the sea, or how many streams are at the source of the deep, or how many streams are above the firmament, or which are the exits of Hades, or which are the entrances^[Syr Compare Ethiop Arab 2 Arm: Lat lacks *of Hades, or which are the entrances*] of paradise?’ ^4-7

**4:8** perhaps you would have said to me, ‘I never went down into the deep, nor as yet into Hades, neither did I ever ascend into heaven.’ ^4-8

**4:9** But now I have asked you only about fire and wind and the day, things that you have experienced and from which you cannot be separated, and you have given me no answer about them.” ^4-9

**4:10** He said to me, “You cannot understand the things with which you have grown up; ^4-10

**4:11** how, then, can your mind comprehend the way of the Most High?^[Syr Ethiop add *For the way of the Most High is created immeasurable*] And how can one who is already worn out by the corrupt world understand incorruption?”^[Syr Ethiop *the way of the incorruptible?*] When I heard this, I fell on my face^[Syr Ethiop Arab 1: Meaning of Lat uncertain] ^4-11

**4:12** and said to him, “It would have been better for us not to be here than to come here and live in ungodliness and to suffer and not understand why.” ^4-12

## Parable of the forest and the sea

**4:13** He answered me and said, “I went into a forest of trees of the plain, and they made a plan ^4-13

**4:14** and said, ‘Come, let us go and make war against the sea, so that it may recede before us and so that we may make for ourselves more forests.’ ^4-14

**4:15** In like manner the waves of the sea also made a plan and said, ‘Come, let us go up and subdue the forest of the plain so that there also we may gain more territory for ourselves.’ ^4-15

**4:16** But the plan of the forest was in vain, for the fire came and consumed it; ^4-16

**4:17** likewise also the plan of the waves of the sea was in vain,^[Lat lacks *was in vain*] for the sand stood firm and blocked it. ^4-17

**4:18** If now you were a judge between them, which would you undertake to justify and which to condemn?” ^4-18

**4:19** I answered and said, “Each made a foolish plan, for the land has been assigned to the forest and the locale of the sea a place to carry its waves.” ^4-19

**4:20** He answered me and said, “You have judged rightly, but why have you not judged so in your own case? ^4-20

**4:21** For as the land has been assigned to the forest and the sea to its waves, so also those who inhabit the earth can understand only what is on the earth, and he who is^[Arab 2 Arm Georg *those who are*] above the heavens can understand what is above the height of the heavens.” ^4-21

## The new age will make all things clear

**4:22** Then I answered and said, “I implore you, my lord, why^[Syr Ethiop Arm Georg: Meaning of Lat uncertain] have I been endowed with the power of understanding? ^4-22

**4:23** For I did not wish to inquire about the ways above but about those things that we daily experience: why Israel has been given over to the nations in disgrace; why the people whom you loved has been given over to godless tribes and the law of our ancestors has been brought to destruction and the written covenants no longer exist. ^4-23

**4:24** We pass from the world like locusts, and our life is like a mist,^[Lat mss Syr Ethiop Arab 1 Georg: Lat *terror*] and we are not worthy to obtain mercy. ^4-24

**4:25** But what will he do for his^[Ethiop adds *holy*] name that is invoked over us? It is about these things that I have asked.” ^4-25

**4:26** He answered me and said, “If you are alive, you will see, and if you live long,^[Syr: Lat *live*] you will often marvel, because the age is hurrying swiftly to its end. ^4-26

**4:27** It will not be able to bring the things that have been promised to the righteous in their appointed times, because this age is full of sadness and infirmities. ^4-27

**4:28** For the evil about which you ask me has been sown, but the harvest of it has not yet come. ^4-28

**4:29** If therefore that which has been sown is not reaped, and if the place where the evil has been sown does not pass away, the field where the good has been sown will not come. ^4-29

**4:30** For a grain of evil seed was sown in Adam’s heart from the beginning, and how much ungodliness it has produced until now—and will produce until the time of threshing comes! ^4-30

**4:31** Consider now for yourself how much fruit of ungodliness a grain of evil seed has produced. ^4-31

**4:32** When heads of grain without number are sown, how great a threshing floor they will fill!” ^4-32

## When will the new age come?

**4:33** Then I answered and said, “How long?^[Syr Ethiop: Meaning of Lat uncertain] When will these things be? Why are our years few and evil?” ^4-33

**4:34** He answered me and said, “Do not be in a greater hurry than the Most High. You, indeed, are in a hurry for yourself,^[Syr Ethiop Arab Arm: Meaning of Lat uncertain] but the Highest is in a hurry on behalf of many. ^4-34

**4:35** Did not the souls of the righteous in their chambers ask about these matters, saying, ‘How long are we to remain here?^[Syr Ethiop Arab 2 Georg: Lat *How long do I hope thus?*] And when will the harvest of our reward come?’ ^4-35

**4:36** And the archangel Jeremiel answered and said, ‘When the number of those like yourselves^[Syr Ethiop Arab 2: Lat *number of seeds*] is completed, for he has weighed the age in the balance, ^4-36

**4:37** and measured the times by measure, and numbered the times by number, and he will not move or arouse them until that measure is fulfilled.’ ” ^4-37

**4:38** Then I answered and said, “But, O sovereign Lord, all of us also are full of ungodliness. ^4-38

**4:39** It is perhaps on account of us that the time of threshing is delayed for the righteous—on account of the sins of those who inhabit the earth.” ^4-39

**4:40** He answered me and said, “Go and ask a pregnant woman whether, when her nine months have been completed, her womb can keep the fetus within her any longer.” ^4-40

**4:41** And I said, “No, lord, it cannot.” He said to me, “In Hades the chambers of the souls are like the womb. ^4-41

**4:42** For just as a woman who is in labor makes haste to escape the pangs of birth, so also do these places hasten to give back those things who were committed to them from the beginning. ^4-42

**4:43** Then the things that you desire to see will be disclosed to you.” ^4-43

## How much time remains?

**4:44** I answered and said, “If I have found favor in your sight, and if it is possible, and if I am worthy, ^4-44

**4:45** show me this also: whether more time is to come than has passed or whether for us the greater part has gone by. ^4-45

**4:46** For I know what has gone by, but I do not know what is to come.” ^4-46

**4:47** And he said to me, “Stand at my right side, and I will show you the interpretation of a parable.” ^4-47

**4:48** So I stood and looked, and a flaming furnace passed by before me, and when the flame had gone by I looked, and the smoke remained. ^4-48

**4:49** And after this a cloud full of water passed before me and poured down a heavy and violent rain, and when the violent rainstorm had passed, drops still remained in it. ^4-49

**4:50** He said to me, “Consider it for yourself, for just as the rain is more than the drops and the fire is greater than the smoke, so the quantity that passed was far greater, but drops and smoke remained.” ^4-50

**4:51** Then I prayed and said, “Do you think that I shall live until those days? Or who will be alive in those days?” ^4-51

**4:52** He answered me and said, “Concerning the signs about which you ask me, I can tell you in part, but I was not sent to tell you concerning your life, for I do not know. ^4-52
