## Mūla

अथवा पुस्तकं तादृग्रहःशास्त्रक्रमोम्भितम् ।
सुशुद्धं दीक्षितकृतं तत्राप्येष विधिः स्मृतः ॥ ^mula

athavā pustakaṃ tādṛgrahaḥśāstrakramombhitam |
suśuddhaṃ dīkṣitakṛtaṃ tatrāpyeṣa vidhiḥ smṛtaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 18 - Chapter Twenty-Seven#^pg440|p. 420]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=440>))
>
> Whereas for one who has no desire, (his mantras) sever the fetters (of the soul) and nourish the reality levels that have been purified etc., (operating variously) according to the limiting adjuncts associated with each particular place (on the Path onto which they are projected). Or else, the same kind of procedure is said (to apply) to (a sacred) book that, very pure and composed by an initiate in which the liturgies of the secret scriptures are woven together (umbhita) (can serve as a support on which to worship). (with 27.48–49)

## Viveka

> [!viveka]- Viveka in English (Dyczkowski, vol. IX, pp. 420–421)
> ![[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 18 - Chapter Twenty-Seven#Viveka on 27.48–49]]

> अकामस्य तु ते तत्तत्स्थानोपाधिवशाद् ध्रुवम् ।
> पाशकर्तनसंशुद्धतत्त्वाप्यायादिकारिणः ॥
> अथवा पुस्तकं तादृग्रहःशास्त्रक्रमोम्भितम् ।
> सुशुद्धं दीक्षितकृतं तत्राप्येष विधिः स्मृतः ॥

अथवेति--पक्षान्तरे । तादृग्रहःशास्त्रक्रमोम्भितत्वेन च अस्य सर्वंसहत्वात् सर्वकर्मस्वपि आनुगुण्यं कटाक्षितम् । कृतमिति-- लिखितम् ॥ ४८-४९ ॥
