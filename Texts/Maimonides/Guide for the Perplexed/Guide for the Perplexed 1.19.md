# Guide for the Perplexed 1.19

← [[Guide for the Perplexed 1.18]] · [[Guide for the Perplexed]] · [[Guide for the Perplexed 1.20]] →

*Part 1, Chapter 19* · [[Guide for the Perplexed]]

THE term male is a homonym which denotes that one substance enters another, and fills it, as “And she filled (va-temalle) her pitcher” ([[Torah 05 - Chayei Sara#^24-16|Gen. 24:16]]); “An omer-full (melo) for each” ([[Torah 16 - Beshalach#^16-32|Exod. 16:32]]), and many other instances. Next, it signifies the expiration or completion of a fixed period of time, as “And when her days to be delivered were fulfilled (va-yimleü)” ([[Torah 06 - Toldot#^25-24|Gen. 25:24]]); “And forty days were completed (va-yimleü) for him” ([[Torah 01 - Bereshit#^1-3|Gen. 1. 3]]). It further denotes attainment of the highest degree of excellency, as “Full (male) with the blessing of the Lord” ([[Torah 54 - V'Zot HaBerachah#^33-23|Deut. 33:23]]); “Them hath he filled (mille) with wisdom of heart” ([[Torah 22 - Vayakhel#^35-35|Exod. 35:35]]) He was filled (va-yimmale) with wisdom, and understanding, and cunning” ([[I Kings 07#^7-14|1 Kings 7:14]]).

In this sense it is said “The whole earth is full (melo) of his glory” ([[Isaiah 06#^6-4|Isa. 6:4]]), “All the earth gives evidence of his perfection,” i.e. leads to a knowledge of it. Thus also” The glory of the Lord filled (male) the tabernacle” ([[Torah 23 - Pekudei#^40-34|Exod. 40:34]]); and, in fact, every application of the word to God must be interpreted in this manner; and not that He has a body occupying space. If, on the other hand, you prefer to think that in this passage by “the glory of the Lord,” a certain light created for the purpose is to be understood, that such light is always termed “glory,” and that such light “filled the tabernacle,” we have no objection.
