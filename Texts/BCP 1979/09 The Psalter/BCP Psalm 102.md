# Psalm 102

← [[BCP Psalm 101]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 103]] →

*Domine, exaudi* · Book Four · Twentieth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 102]]

**102:1** LORD, hear my prayer, and let my cry come before you; \*  
hide not your face from me in the day of my trouble. ^102-1

**102:2** Incline your ear to me; \*  
when I call, make haste to answer me, ^102-2

**102:3** For my days drift away like smoke, \*  
and my bones are hot as burning coals. ^102-3

**102:4** My heart is smitten like grass and withered, \*  
so that I forget to eat my bread. ^102-4

**102:5** Because of the voice of my groaning \*  
I am but skin and bones. ^102-5

**102:6** I have become like a vulture in the wilderness, \*  
like an owl among the ruins. ^102-6

**102:7** I lie awake and groan; \*  
I am like a sparrow, lonely on a house-top. ^102-7

**102:8** My enemies revile me all day long, \*  
and those who scoff at me have taken an oath against me. ^102-8

**102:9** For I have eaten ashes for bread \*  
and mingled my drink with weeping. ^102-9

**102:10** Because of your indignation and wrath \*  
you have lifted me up and thrown me away. ^102-10

**102:11** My days pass away like a shadow, \*  
and I wither like the grass. ^102-11

**102:12** But you, O LORD, endure for ever, \*  
and your Name from age to age. ^102-12

[p. 732]

**102:13** You will arise and have compassion on Zion,  
for it is time to have mercy upon her; \*  
indeed, the appointed time has come. ^102-13

**102:14** For your servants love her very rubble, \*  
and are moved to pity even for her dust. ^102-14

**102:15** The nations shall fear your Name, O LORD, \*  
and all the kings of the earth your glory. ^102-15

**102:16** For the LORD will build up Zion, \*  
and his glory will appear. ^102-16

**102:17** He will look with favor on the prayer of the homeless; \*  
he will not despise their plea. ^102-17

**102:18** Let this be written for a future generation, \*  
so that a people yet unborn may praise the LORD. ^102-18

**102:19** For the LORD looked down from his holy place on high; \*  
from the heavens he beheld the earth; ^102-19

**102:20** That he might hear the groan of the captive \*  
and set free those condemned to die; ^102-20

**102:21** That they may declare in Zion the Name of the LORD, \*  
and his praise in Jerusalem; ^102-21

**102:22** When the peoples are gathered together, \*  
and the kingdoms also, to serve the LORD. ^102-22

**102:23** He has brought down my strength before my time; \*  
he has shortened the number of my days; ^102-23

**102:24** And I said, "O my God,  
do not take me away in the midst of my days; \*  
your years endure throughout all generations. ^102-24

**102:25** In the beginning, O LORD, you laid the foundations of the earth, \*  
and the heavens are the work of your hands; ^102-25

[p. 733]

**102:26** They shall perish, but you will endure;  
they all shall wear out like a garment; \*  
as clothing you will change them,  
and they shall be changed; ^102-26

**102:27** But you are always the same, \*  
and your years will never end. ^102-27

**102:28** The children of your servants shall continue, \*  
and their offspring shall stand fast in your sight." ^102-28
