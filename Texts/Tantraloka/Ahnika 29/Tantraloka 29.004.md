## Mūla

कुलं च परमेशस्य शक्तिः सामर्थ्यमूर्ध्वता ।
स्वातन्त्र्यमोजो वीर्यं च पिण्डः संविच्छरीरकम् ॥ ^mula

kulaṃ ca parameśasya śaktiḥ sāmarthyamūrdhvatā |
svātantryamojo vīryaṃ ca piṇḍaḥ saṃviccharīrakam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 09 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 1#^pg220|p. 203]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=220>))
>
> 1) Kula is the Supreme Lord’s power (Śakti),), (which is) 2) (His) capacity (sāmarthya) (to generate and withdraw all things), 3) (His) eminence (ārdhvatā) (as the universal cause of everything), 4) (His) freedom (svātantrya) (to know and do everything as He wishes), 5) (Kula is His) vigour (ojas), 6) vitality (vīrya), 7) the aggregate (piṇḍa) (of His energies), 8) consciousness and 9) the body.
