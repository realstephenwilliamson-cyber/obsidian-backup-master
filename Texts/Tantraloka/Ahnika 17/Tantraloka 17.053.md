## Mūla

ततः पूर्णाहुतिं दत्त्वा परया वौषडन्तया ।
अपरामन्त्रतः शिष्यमुद्धृत्यात्महृदं नयेत् ॥ ^mula

tataḥ pūrṇāhutiṃ dattvā parayā vauṣaḍantayā |
aparāmantrataḥ śiṣyamuddhṛtyātmahṛdaṃ nayet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 08 - Chapter Seventeen#^pg183|p. 163]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=183>))
>
> In the same way, the expert (teacher) should offer three oblations each to the Forces (kalā), Mantras, parts of Mantra (pada) and phonemes, either (to each one) separately, or else (all of them) together as a whole. Then, after having offered a full oblation with Parā and VAUṢAT at the end, and having extracted the disciple with (the same) Aparā Mantra (by which the principle has been purified), he should lead him into his own heart. (with 17.52–53)

## Viveka

> एवं कलामन्त्रपदवर्णेष्वपि विचक्षणः ।
> तिस्रस्तिस्रो हुतीर्दद्यात् पृथक् सामस्त्यतोऽपि वा ॥
> ततः पूर्णाहुतिं दत्त्वा परया वौषडन्तया ।
> अपरामन्त्रतः शिष्यमुद्धृत्यात्महृदं नयेत् ॥

पृथगिति--एकैकध्येन । सामस्त्यत इति--इतराध्वपञ्चकं शोधयामीति । उद्धृत्येत्यर्थाच्छोधितात् तत्त्वात् । आत्महृदमिति-- गुरोः ॥५३ ॥
