# LXX Psalms 143

← [[LXX Psalms 142]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 144]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 144]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 144]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**143:1** *A Psalm* of David concerning Goliad. Blessed *be* the Lord my God, who instructs my hands for battle, *and* my fingers for war.  ^143-1

**143:2** My mercy, and my refuge; my helper, and my deliverer; my protector, in whom I have trusted; who subdues my people under me.  ^143-2

**143:3** Lord, what is man, that thou art made known to him? or the son of man, that thou takest account of him?  ^143-3

**143:4** Man is like to vanity: his days pass as a shadow.  ^143-4

**143:5** O Lord, bow thy heavens, and come down: touch the mountains, and they shall smoke.  ^143-5

**143:6** Send lightning, and thou shalt scatter them: send forth thine arrows, and thou shalt discomfit them.  ^143-6

**143:7** Send forth thine hand from on high; rescue me, and deliver me out of great waters, out of the hand of strange children;[^1]  ^143-7

**143:8** whose mouth has spoken vanity, and their right hand is a right hand of iniquity.  ^143-8

**143:9** O God, I will sing a new song to thee: I will play to thee on a psaltery of ten strings.  ^143-9

**143:10** *Even* to him who gives salvation to kings: who redeems his servant David from the hurtful sword.[^2]  ^143-10

**143:11** Deliver me, and rescue me from the hand of strange children, whose mouth has spoken vanity, and their right hand is a right hand of iniquity;  ^143-11

**143:12** whose children are as plants, strengthened in their youth: their daughters are beautiful, sumptuously adorned after the similitude of a temple.  ^143-12

**143:13** Their garners are full, and bursting with one kind of store after another; their sheep are prolific, multiplying in their streets.  ^143-13

**143:14** Their oxen are fat: there is no falling down of a hedge, nor going out, nor cry in their folds.[^3]  ^143-14

**143:15** Men bless the people to whom this lot belongs, *but* blessed is the people whose God is the Lord.  ^143-15

[^1]: 143:7 *Gr.* many.
[^2]: 143:10 *Or,* victory.
[^3]: 143:14 *Or,* habitations.
