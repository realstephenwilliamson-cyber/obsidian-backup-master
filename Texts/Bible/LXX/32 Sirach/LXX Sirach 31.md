# LXX Sirach 31

← [[LXX Sirach 30]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Sirach 32]] →

*Sirach · [[LXX]]*

**31:1** The hopes of a man void of understanding are vain and false: and dreams lift up fools.  ^31-1

**31:2** Whoso regardeth dreams is like him that catcheth at a shadow, and followeth after the wind.  ^31-2

**31:3** The vision of dreams is the resemblance of one thing to another, even as the likeness of a face to a face.  ^31-3

**31:4** Of an unclean thing what can be cleansed? and from that thing which is false what truth can come?  ^31-4

**31:5** Divinations, and soothsayings, and dreams, are vain: and the heart fancieth, as a woman's heart in travail.  ^31-5

**31:6** If they be not sent from the most High in thy visitation, set not thy heart upon them.  ^31-6

**31:7** For dreams have deceived many, and they have failed that put their trust in them.  ^31-7

**31:8** The law shall be found perfect without lies: and wisdom is perfection to a faithful mouth.  ^31-8

**31:9** A man that hath travelled knoweth many things: and he that hath much experience will declare wisdom.  ^31-9

**31:10** He that hath no experience knoweth little: but he that hath travelled is full of prudence.  ^31-10

**31:11** When I travelled, I saw many things; and I understand more than I can express.  ^31-11

**31:12** I was oft-times in danger of death: yet I was delivered because of these things.  ^31-12

**31:13** The spirit of those that fear the Lord shall live; for their hope is in him that saveth them.  ^31-13

**31:14** Whoso feareth the Lord shall not fear nor be afraid; for he is his hope.  ^31-14

**31:15** Blessed is the soul of him that feareth the Lord: to whom doth he look? and who is his strength?  ^31-15

**31:16** For the eyes of the Lord are upon them that love him, he is their mighty protection and strong stay, a defence from heat, and a cover from the sun at noon, a preservation from stumbling, and an help from falling;  ^31-16

**31:17** he raiseth up the soul, and lighteneth the eyes: he giveth health, life, and blessing.  ^31-17

**31:18** He that sacrificeth of a thing wrongfully gotten, his offering is ridiculous; and the gifts of unjust men are not accepted.  ^31-18

**31:19** The most High is not pleased with the offerings of the wicked; neither is he pacified for sin by the multitude of sacrifices.  ^31-19

**31:20** Whoso bringeth an offering of the goods of the poor doeth as one that killeth the son before his father's eyes.  ^31-20

**31:21** The bread of the needy is their life: he that defraudeth him thereof is a man of blood.  ^31-21

**31:22** He that taketh away his neighbour's living slayeth him; and he that defraudeth the labourer his hire is a bloodshedder.  ^31-22

**31:23** When one buildeth, and another pulleth down, what profit have they then but labour?  ^31-23

**31:24** When one prayeth, and another curseth, whose voice will the Lord hear?  ^31-24

**31:25** He that washeth himself after the touching of a dead body, if he touch it again, what availeth his washing?  ^31-25

**31:26** So is it with a man that fasteth for his sins, and goeth again, and doeth the same: who will hear his prayer? or what doth his humbling profit him?  ^31-26
