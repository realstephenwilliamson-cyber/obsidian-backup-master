# Judges 4 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Judges 03|← Judges 3]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Judges 05|Judges 5 →]]

## Deborah and Barak

**4:1** The Israelites again did what was evil in the sight of the LORD, after Ehud died. ^4-1

**4:2** So the LORD sold them into the hand of King Jabin of Canaan, who reigned in Hazor; the commander of his army was Sisera, who lived in Harosheth-ha-goiim. ^4-2

**4:3** Then the Israelites cried out to the LORD for help, for he had nine hundred chariots of iron and had oppressed the Israelites cruelly twenty years. ^4-3

**4:4** At that time Deborah, a prophet, wife of Lappidoth, was judging Israel. ^4-4

**4:5** She used to sit under the palm of Deborah between Ramah and Bethel in the hill country of Ephraim, and the Israelites came up to her for judgment. ^4-5

**4:6** She sent and summoned Barak son of Abinoam from Kedesh in Naphtali and said to him, “The LORD, the God of Israel, commands you, ‘Position yourself at Mount Tabor, taking ten thousand from the tribe of Naphtali and the tribe of Zebulun. ^4-6

**4:7** I will draw out Sisera, the general of Jabin’s army, to meet you by the Wadi Kishon with his chariots and his troops, and I will give him into your hand.’ ” ^4-7

**4:8** Barak said to her, “If you will go with me, I will go, but if you will not go with me, I will not go.” ^4-8

**4:9** And she said, “I will surely go with you; nevertheless, the road on which you are going will not lead to your glory, for the LORD will sell Sisera into the hand of a woman.” Then Deborah got up and went with Barak to Kedesh. ^4-9

**4:10** Barak summoned Zebulun and Naphtali to Kedesh, and ten thousand warriors went up behind him, and Deborah went up with him. ^4-10

**4:11** Now Heber the Kenite had separated from the other Kenites,^[Heb *from the Kain*] that is, the descendants of Hobab the father-in-law of Moses, and had encamped as far away as Elon-bezaanannim, which is near Kedesh. ^4-11

**4:12** When Sisera was told that Barak son of Abinoam had gone up to Mount Tabor, ^4-12

**4:13** Sisera called out all his chariots, nine hundred chariots of iron, and all the troops who were with him, from Harosheth-ha-goiim to the Wadi Kishon. ^4-13

**4:14** Then Deborah said to Barak, “Up! For this is the day on which the LORD has given Sisera into your hand. Has not the LORD gone out before you?” So Barak went down from Mount Tabor with ten thousand warriors following him. ^4-14

**4:15** And the LORD threw Sisera and all his chariots and all his army into a panic^[Heb adds *to the sword*] before Barak; Sisera got down from his chariot and fled away on foot, ^4-15

**4:16** while Barak pursued the chariots and the army to Harosheth-ha-goiim. All the army of Sisera fell by the sword; not one was left. ^4-16

**4:17** Now Sisera had fled away on foot to the tent of Jael wife of Heber the Kenite, for there was peace between King Jabin of Hazor and the clan of Heber the Kenite. ^4-17

**4:18** Jael came out to meet Sisera and said to him, “Turn aside, my lord, turn aside to me; have no fear.” So he turned aside to her into the tent, and she covered him with a rug. ^4-18

**4:19** Then he said to her, “Please give me a little water to drink, for I am thirsty.” So she opened a skin of milk and gave him a drink and covered him. ^4-19

**4:20** He said to her, “Stand at the entrance of the tent, and if anybody comes and asks you, ‘Is anyone here?’ say, ‘No.’ ” ^4-20

**4:21** But Jael wife of Heber took a tent peg and took a hammer in her hand and went softly to him and drove the peg into his temple, until it went down into the ground—he was lying fast asleep from weariness—and he died. ^4-21

**4:22** Then, as Barak came in pursuit of Sisera, Jael went out to meet him and said to him, “Come, and I will show you the man whom you are seeking.” So he went into her tent, and there was Sisera lying dead, with the tent peg in his temple. ^4-22

**4:23** So on that day God subdued King Jabin of Canaan before the Israelites. ^4-23

**4:24** Then the hand of the Israelites bore harder and harder on King Jabin of Canaan, until they destroyed King Jabin of Canaan. ^4-24
