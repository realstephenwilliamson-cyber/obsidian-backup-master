## Mūla

यत्प्रमाणात्मकं रूपमध्वनो मातृभागगम् ।
पदं ह्यवगमात्मत्वसमावेशात्तदुच्यते ॥ ^mula

yatpramāṇātmakaṃ rūpamadhvano mātṛbhāgagam |
padaṃ hyavagamātmatvasamāveśāttaducyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 02 - Chapter Eleven, part 1#^pg63|p. 43]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=63>))
>
> The form of the Path associated with the subjective aspect as the means of knowledge is called Pada, because it is penetrated by the capacity to comprehend (avagamatva). When Pada is freed of the disturbance (brought about by the means of knowledge directed at an outer object), it becomes Mantra. This is because the subject whose speech is hidden is silent. (with 11.44–45)
