## Mūla

नान्या काचिदपेक्षाऽस्य कृतकृत्यस्य सर्वतः ।
यथा च शिवनाथेन स्वातन्त्र्याद्भास्यते भिदा ॥ ^mula

nānyā kācidapekṣā'sya kṛtakṛtyasya sarvataḥ |
yathā ca śivanāthena svātantryādbhāsyate bhidā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 11 - Chapter Ten, part 2#^pg364|p. 345]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=364>))
>
> If objectivity thus abides at rest (in this way) in the light (of consciousness), it depends on nothing else, and has accomplished its task in every respect. (with 10.76cd–77ab)
>
> Just as Lord Śiva freely makes (the many objects) manifest as divided off (from one another) (bhidā), it is said that, in the same way, (he makes) this quality, perceptibility, (manifest), as (He does) blue and other (qualities). (with 10.77cd–78ab)
