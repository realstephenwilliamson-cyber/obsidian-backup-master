Pelikan's five-volume history of doctrine; vols. 1–3 held as PDF, each with a Markdown copy. (Vols. 4–5 are not in the vault; see [[Incomplete Sets and Scholar Research Guide]].)

Pelikan's *The Shape of Death: Life, Death and Immortality in the Early Fathers* is a separate, unrelated book by the same author — catalogued on its own.

**Checked against the file** on 7 Oct 2026: the title pages of the three volumes confirm Jaroslav Pelikan's The Christian Tradition: A History of the Development of Doctrine, vol. 1 The Emergence of the Catholic Tradition (100-600), vol. 2 The Spirit of Eastern Christendom (600-1700) and vol. 3 The Growth of Medieval Theology (600-1300), University of Chicago Press (vol. 1 1971, confirmed from the new scan on 13 Nov 2026; vol. 2 1974; vol. 3 1978), so the city (Chicago) was added and the subtitle property, which listed the volume titles rather than a printed subtitle, was emptied, while year and pages stay empty because the note has three files.

**Vol. 1's file** was replaced on 13 Nov 2026 by the Internet Archive's 2018 scan of a Boston Public Library copy (identifier christiantraditi00peli_0; its copyright page reads © 1971, published 1971). The file it replaced was a coarse black-and-white scan that lacked pp. 190 and 317 and had p. 71 twice. The new scan has the whole book; a previous reader's pencil underlining is on some pages, mostly in chapter 1. Vol. 1's Markdown copy (13 Nov 2026) is a fresh OCR of this scan.
