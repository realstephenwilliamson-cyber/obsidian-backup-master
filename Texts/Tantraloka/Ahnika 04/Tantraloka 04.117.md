## Mūla

सितभस्मनि देहस्य मज्जनं स्नानमुच्यते ।
इत्थं च विहितस्नानस्तर्पितानन्तदेवतः ॥ ^mula

sitabhasmani dehasya majjanaṃ snānamucyate |
itthaṃ ca vihitasnānastarpitānantadevataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. III
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 03 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v03 05 - Chapter Four, part 4#^pg149|p. 136]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 03.pdf#page=149>))
>
> C) (The true, inner) ablution is said to be the immersion of the body in the white ash (produced) when the fire (that devours offerings), which is outpouring (ullāsin) (awakened) consciousness, has arisen, and the fuel of the universe has been burnt away. (with 4.116cd–117ab)
>
> D) He who has performed the prescribed ablution in this way and offered libations (tarpita) to the deities of bliss (that govern the senses) should then proceed to also purify the metaphysical principles (tattva) that constitute the body. (with 4.117cd–118ab)
