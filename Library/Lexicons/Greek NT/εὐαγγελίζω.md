# εὐαγγελίζω

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · verb

**Dodson:** I bring good news, preach good tidings — I bring good news, preach good tidings, with or without an object, expressing either the persons who receive the good news or the good news itself (the good news being sometimes expressed as a person). (Strong's 2097; headword εὐαγγελίζω)
