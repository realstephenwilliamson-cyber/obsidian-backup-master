# Judith 12 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Judith 11|← Judith 11]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Judith 13|Judith 13 →]]

## Judith as a guest of Holofernes

**12:1** Then he commanded them to bring her in where his silver dinnerware was kept and ordered them to set a table for her with some of his own delicacies and with some of his own wine to drink. ^12-1

**12:2** But Judith said, “I cannot partake of them, or it will be an offense, but I will have enough with the things I brought with me.” ^12-2

**12:3** Holofernes said to her, “If your supply runs out, where can we get you more of the same? For none of your people are here with us.” ^12-3

**12:4** Judith replied, “As surely as you live, my lord, your servant will not use up the supplies I have with me before the Lord carries out by my hand what he has determined.” ^12-4

**12:5** Then the attendants of Holofernes brought her into the tent, and she slept until midnight. Toward the morning watch she got up ^12-5

**12:6** and sent this message to Holofernes: “Let my lord now give orders to allow your servant to go out and pray.” ^12-6

**12:7** So Holofernes commanded his guards not to hinder her. She remained in the camp three days. She went out each night to the valley of Bethulia and bathed at the spring in the camp.^[Other ancient authorities lack *in the camp*] ^12-7

**12:8** After bathing, she prayed the Lord God of Israel to direct her way for the triumph of his^[Other ancient authorities read *her*] people. ^12-8

**12:9** Then she returned purified and stayed in the tent until she ate her food toward evening. ^12-9

## Judith attends Holofernes’s banquet

**12:10** On the fourth day Holofernes held a banquet for his slaves only and did not invite any of his officers. ^12-10

**12:11** He said to Bagoas, the eunuch who had charge of his personal affairs, “Go and persuade the Hebrew woman who is in your care to join us and to eat and drink with us. ^12-11

**12:12** For it would be a disgrace if we let such a woman go without being intimate with her. If we do not seduce her, she will laugh at us.” ^12-12

**12:13** So Bagoas left the presence of Holofernes and approached her and said, “Let this pretty servant not hesitate to come to my lord to be honored in his presence and to enjoy drinking wine with us and to become today like one of the Assyrian women who serve in the palace of Nebuchadnezzar.” ^12-13

**12:14** Judith replied, “Who am I to refuse my lord? Whatever pleases him I will do at once, and it will be a joy to me until the day of my death.” ^12-14

**12:15** So she proceeded to dress herself in all her woman’s finery. Her maid went ahead and spread for her on the ground before Holofernes the lambskins she had received from Bagoas for her daily use in reclining. ^12-15

**12:16** Then Judith came in and lay down. Holofernes’s heart was ravished with her and his passion was aroused, for he had been waiting for an opportunity to be with her from the day he first saw her. ^12-16

**12:17** So Holofernes said to her, “Have a drink and be merry with us!” ^12-17

**12:18** Judith said, “I will gladly drink, my lord, because today is the greatest day in my whole life.” ^12-18

**12:19** Then she took what her maid had prepared and ate and drank before him. ^12-19

**12:20** Holofernes was greatly pleased with her and drank a great quantity of wine, much more than he had ever drunk in any one day since he was born. ^12-20
