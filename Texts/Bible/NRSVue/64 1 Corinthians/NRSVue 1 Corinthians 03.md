## On divisions in the corinthian church

**3:1** And so, brothers and sisters, I could not speak to you as spiritual people but rather as fleshly, as infants in Christ. ^3-1

**3:2** I fed you with milk, not solid food, for you were not ready for solid food. Even now you are still not ready, ^3-2

**3:3** for you are still fleshly. For as long as there is jealousy and quarreling^[Other ancient authorities add *and dissensions*] among you, are you not fleshly and behaving according to human inclinations? ^3-3

**3:4** For when one says, “I belong to Paul,” and another, “I belong to Apollos,” are you not all too human? ^3-4

**3:5** What then is Apollos? What is Paul? Servants through whom you came to believe, as the Lord assigned to each. ^3-5

**3:6** I planted, Apollos watered, but God gave the growth. ^3-6

**3:7** So neither the one who plants nor the one who waters is anything, but only God who gives the growth. ^3-7

**3:8** The one who plants and the one who waters have one purpose, and each will receive wages according to their own labor. ^3-8

**3:9** For we are God’s coworkers, working together; you are God’s field, God’s building. ^3-9

**3:10** According to the grace of God given to me, like a wise master builder I laid a foundation, and someone else is building on it. Let each builder choose with care how to build on it. ^3-10

**3:11** For no one can lay any foundation other than the one that has been laid; that foundation is Jesus Christ. ^3-11

**3:12** Now if anyone builds on the foundation with gold, silver, precious stones, wood, hay, straw— ^3-12

**3:13** the work of each builder will become visible, for the day^[Or *the Day*] will disclose it, because it will be revealed with fire, and the fire will test what sort of work each has done. ^3-13

**3:14** If the work that someone has built on the foundation survives, the builder will receive a wage. ^3-14

**3:15** If the work is burned up, the builder will suffer loss; the builder will be saved, but only as through fire. ^3-15

**3:16** Do you not know that you are God’s temple and that God’s Spirit dwells in you?^[In 3.16 and 3.17 the Greek word for *you* is plural] ^3-16

**3:17** If anyone destroys God’s temple, God will destroy that person. For God’s temple is holy, and you are that temple. ^3-17

**3:18** Do not deceive yourselves. If you think that you are wise in this age, you should become fools so that you may become wise. ^3-18

**3:19** For the wisdom of this world is foolishness with God. For it is written, “He catches the wise in their craftiness,” ^3-19

**3:20** and again, “The Lord knows the thoughts of the wise, that they are futile.” ^3-20

**3:21** So let no one boast about people.^[Or *about human things*] For all things are yours, ^3-21

**3:22** whether Paul or Apollos or Cephas or the world or life or death or the present or the future—all are yours, ^3-22

**3:23** and you are Christ’s, and Christ is God’s. ^3-23
