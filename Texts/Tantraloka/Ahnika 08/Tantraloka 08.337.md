## Mūla

न प्ररोहेत्पुनर्नान्यो हेतुस्तच्छेदनं प्रति ।
महामायोर्ध्वतः शुद्धा महाविद्याथ मातृका ॥ ^mula

na prarohetpunarnānyo hetustacchedanaṃ prati |
mahāmāyordhvataḥ śuddhā mahāvidyātha mātṛkā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 09 - Chapter Eight, part 7#^pg344|p. 326]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=344>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 8.337cd–338ab.
>
> Once (Māyā) is severed by the sword of Śaiva initiation and the sword of the knowledge of Śiva, it does not grow again. There is no other means but this to cut through it. (with 8.336cd–337ab)
>
> Now, above Mahāmāyā is Great Knowledge which, pure, is the Little Mother (Mātrkā) who is the Goddess of Speech. There are nine existing (sat) worlds there (governed by) Vāmā and the rest. (with 8.337cd–338ab)

## Viveka

> महामायोर्ध्वतः शुद्धा महाविद्याथ मातृका ॥
> वागीश्वरी च तत्रस्थं वामादिनवसत्पुरम् ।

शुद्धत्वादेव च अस्या महत्त्वमित्युक्तं "महाविद्या"इति ॥ ३३७ ॥
