# Hosea 13

← [[Hosea 12]] · [[Tanakh]] · [[Hosea 14]] →

Compare: [[NRSVue Hosea 13|NRSVue]] (NRSVue 13:16 = Heb 14:1)

## Chapter 13

**13:1** When Ephraim spoke piety,  
He was exalted in Israel;  
But he incurred guilt through Baal,[^1]  
And so he died. ^13-1

**13:2** And now they go on sinning;  
They have made them molten images,  
Idols, by their skill, from their silver,  
Wholly the work of craftsmen.  
Yet for these they appoint men to sacrifice;[^2]  
They are wont to kiss calves! ^13-2

**13:3** Assuredly,  
They shall be like morning clouds,  
Like dew so early gone;  
Like chaff whirled away from the threshing floor.  
And like smoke from a lattice. ^13-3

**13:4** Only I the LORD have been your God  
Ever since the land of Egypt;  
You have never known a [true] God but Me,  
You have never had a helper other than Me. ^13-4

**13:5** I looked after you in the desert,  
In a thirsty land. ^13-5

**13:6** When they grazed, they were sated;  
When they were sated, they grew haughty;  
And so they forgot Me. ^13-6

**13:7** So I am become like a lion to them,  
Like a leopard I lurk on the way; ^13-7

**13:8** Like a bear robbed of her young I attack them  
And rip open the casing of their hearts;  
I will devour them there like a lion,[^3]  
The beasts of the field shall mangle them. ^13-8

**13:9** You are undone, O Israel!  
You had no help but Me.[^4] ^13-9

**13:10** Where now is your king?  
Let him save you!  
Where are the chieftains in all your towns  
From whom you demanded:  
“Give me a king and officers”? ^13-10

**13:11** I give you kings in my ire,  
And take them away in My wrath. ^13-11

**13:12** Ephraim’s guilt is bound up,  
His sin is stored away.[^5] ^13-12

**13:13** Pangs of childbirth assail him,  
And the babe is not wise—  
For this is no time to survive  
At the birthstool of babes.[^6] ^13-13

**13:14** [^7]From Sheol itself I will save them,  
Redeem them from very Death.  
Where, O Death, are your plagues?  
Your pestilence where, O Sheol?  
Revenge shall be far from My thoughts.[^8] ^13-14

**13:15** For though he flourish among reeds,  
A blast, a wind of the LORD,  
Shall come blowing up from the wilderness;  
His fountain shall be parched,  
His spring dried up.  
That [wind] shall plunder treasures,  
Every lovely object. ^13-15

[^1]: I.e., Baal-peor; cf. 9.10.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Emendation yields “There dogs shall devour them”; cf. Septuagint.
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^5]: I.e., for future retribution.
[^6]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^7]: This verse would read well before 14.5.
[^8]: Lit. “Satisfaction (for this meaning of nḥm see Deut. 32.36; Isa. 1.24) shall be hidden from My eyes.”
