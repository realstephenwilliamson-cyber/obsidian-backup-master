## Mūla

पशुमात्रस्य सालोक्यं सामीप्यं दीक्षितस्य तु ।
तत्परस्य तु सायुज्यमित्युक्तं परमेशिना ॥ ^mula

paśumātrasya sālokyaṃ sāmīpyaṃ dīkṣitasya tu |
tatparasya tu sāyujyamityuktaṃ parameśinā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 05 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 3#^pg136|p. 119]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=136>))
>
> (Thus, in brief,) the Supreme Lord has said that one who is just a common fettered soul (who has not received initiation but has died in one of these places attains a state) of cohabitation in the same world (as the deity who governs it) (sālokya); one who has been initiated, proximity (to it) (sāmīpya), and one who is other than these, union (with it) (sāyujya).

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* एवमियता किं पर्यवसितम्?--इत्याशङ्क्य आह--

> पशुमात्रस्य सालोक्यं सामीप्यं दीक्षितस्य तु ।
> तत्परस्य तु सायुज्यमित्युक्तं परमेशिना ॥

दीक्षितस्येति--लोकधर्मिदीक्षया । तत्परस्येति--एवं दीक्षितत्वेऽपि तत्तत्स्थानादौ मृतस्य ॥
