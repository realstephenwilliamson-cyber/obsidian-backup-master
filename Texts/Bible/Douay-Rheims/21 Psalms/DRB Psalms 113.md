# Psalms 113 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 112|← Psalms 112]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 114|Psalms 114 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 113|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**113:1** When Israel went out of Egypt, the house of Jacob from a barbarous people: ^113-1

**113:2** Judea made his sanctuary, Israel his dominion. ^113-2

**113:3** The sea saw and fled: Jordan was turned back. ^113-3

**113:4** The mountains skipped like rams, and the hills like the lambs of the flock. ^113-4

**113:5** What ailed thee, O thou sea, that thou didst flee: and thou, O Jordan, that thou wast turned back? ^113-5

**113:6** Ye mountains, that ye skipped like rams, and ye hills, like lambs of the flock? ^113-6

**113:7** At the presence of the Lord the earth was moved, at the presence of the God of Jacob: ^113-7

**113:8** Who turned the rock into pools of water, and the stony hill into fountains of waters. ^113-8

**113:9** Not to us, O Lord, not to us; but to thy name give glory. ^113-9

**113:10** For thy mercy, and for thy truth’s sake: lest the gentiles should say: Where is their God? ^113-10

**113:11** But our God is in heaven: he hath done all things whatsoever he would. ^113-11

**113:12** The idols of the gentiles are silver and gold, the works of the hands of men. ^113-12

**113:13** They have mouths and speak not: they have eyes and see not. ^113-13

**113:14** They have ears and hear not: they have noses and smell not. ^113-14

**113:15** They have hands and feel not: they have feet and walk not: neither shall they cry out through their throat. ^113-15

**113:16** Let them that make them become like unto them: and all such as trust in them. ^113-16

**113:17** The house of Israel hath hoped in the Lord: he is their helper and their protector. ^113-17

**113:18** The house of Aaron hath hoped in the Lord: he is their helper and their protector. ^113-18

**113:19** They that fear the Lord hath hoped in the Lord: he is their helper and their protector. ^113-19

**113:20** The Lord hath been mindful of us, and hath blessed us. He hath blessed the house of Israel: he hath blessed the house of Aaron. ^113-20

**113:21** He hath blessed all that fear the Lord, both little and great. ^113-21

**113:22** May the Lord add blessings upon you: upon you, and upon your children. ^113-22

**113:23** Blessed be you of the Lord, who made heaven and earth. ^113-23

**113:24** The heaven of heaven is the Lord’s: but the earth he has given to the children of men. ^113-24

**113:25** The dead shall not praise thee, O Lord: nor any of them that go down to hell. ^113-25

**113:26** But we that live bless the Lord: from this time now and for ever. ^113-26
