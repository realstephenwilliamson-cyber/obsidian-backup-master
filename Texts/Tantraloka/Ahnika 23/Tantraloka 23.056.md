## Mūla

ननु विज्ञानमात्मस्थं कथं हर्तुं क्षमं भवेत् ।
अतो विज्ञानहरणं कथं श्रीपूर्व उच्यते ॥ ^mula

nanu vijñānamātmasthaṃ kathaṃ hartuṃ kṣamaṃ bhavet |
ato vijñānaharaṇaṃ kathaṃ śrīpūrva ucyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX

> Surely (one may ask), how can one remove the knowledge that is present in the Self? So how is it that the removal of knowledge (from a deceitful disciple’s) soul is taught in the Śrīpūrva (Mālinīvijayottara)?
