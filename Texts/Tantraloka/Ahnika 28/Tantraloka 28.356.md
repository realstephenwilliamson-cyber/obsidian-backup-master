## Mūla

स्वप्ने नास्ति स इत्येषा वाक्प्रमाणविवर्जिता ।
य एवैते तु दृश्यन्ते जाग्रत्येते मयेक्षिताः ॥ ^mula

svapne nāsti sa ityeṣā vākpramāṇavivarjitā |
ya evaite tu dṛśyante jāgratyete mayekṣitāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 06 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 4#^pg174|p. 157]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=174>))
>
> When a multitude of other perceivers, known by (their various) activities and dealings, (are seen) in a dream, the affirmation that that does not (really) exist is devoid of (any supporting) means of knowledge (pramānṇa). (with 28.355cd–356ab)
>
> (The affirmation) that “the things I have seen in a dream are those seen when awake” is false is (sustained only) on the strength of the words of those perceivers (first seen in the dream and then when awake). (with 28.356cd–357ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* ननु एवमपि जाग्रत्स्वप्नयोर्दार्ढ्यादार्ढ्याभ्यां सत्यत्वमसत्यत्वं च सर्वत्र प्रसिद्धं कथमपह्नोतुं शक्यम् ?--इत्याशङ्क्य आह--

> य एवैते तु दृश्यन्ते जाग्रत्येते मयेक्षिताः ॥
> स्वप्न इत्यस्तु मिथ्यैतत्तत्प्रमातृवचोबलात् ।

इत्येतदिति--एकत्वेन अभिमननम्--इत्यर्थः । तत्प्रमातृवचोबलादिति--ते हि जाग्रत्प्रमातारो मत्समक्षं ह्यः स्वप्ने भवद्भिः किं दृष्टमिति पृष्टा नेत्येव परं ब्रूयुरिति ॥
