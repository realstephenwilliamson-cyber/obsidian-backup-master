# ξύλον

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · noun

**Dodson:** a staff, cross, anything made of wood — anything made of wood, a piece of wood, a club, staff; the trunk of a tree, used to support the cross-bar of a cross in crucifixion. (Strong's 3586; headword ξύλον, ου, τό)
