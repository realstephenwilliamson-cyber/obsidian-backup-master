# Job 20 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Job 19|← Job 19]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Job 21|Job 21 →]]

**20:1** Then Sophar the Naamathite answered, and said: ^20-1

**20:2** Therefore various thoughts succeed one another in me, and my mind is hurried away to different things. ^20-2

**20:3** The doctrine with which thou reprovest me, I will hear, and the spirit of my understanding shall answer for me. ^20-3

**20:4** This I know from the beginning, since man was placed upon the earth, ^20-4

**20:5** That the praise of the wicked is short, and the joy of the hypocrite but for a moment. ^20-5

**20:6** If his pride mount up even to heaven, and his head touch the clouds: ^20-6

**20:7** In the end he shall be destroyed like a dunghill, and they that has seen him shall say: Where is he? ^20-7

**20:8** As a dream that fleeth away he shall not be found, he shall pass as a vision of the night: ^20-8

**20:9** The eyes that had seen him, shall see him no more, neither shall his place any more behold him. ^20-9

**20:10** His children shall be oppressed with want, and his hands shall render him his sorrow. ^20-10

**20:11** His bones shall be filled with the vices of his youth, and they shall sleep with him in the dust. ^20-11

**20:12** For when evil shall be sweet in his mouth, he will hide it under his tongue. ^20-12

**20:13** He will spare it, and not leave it, and will hide it in his throat. ^20-13

**20:14** His bread in his belly shall be turned into the gall of asps within him. ^20-14

**20:15** The riches which he hath swallowed; he shall vomit up, and God shall draw them out of his belly. ^20-15

**20:16** He shall suck the head of asps, and the viper’s tongue shall kill him. ^20-16

**20:17** (Let him not see the streams of the river, the brooks of honey and of butter.) ^20-17

**20:18** He shall be punished for all that he did, and yet shall not be consumed: according to the multitude of his devices so also shall he suffer. ^20-18

**20:19** Because he broke in and stripped the poor: he hath violently taken away a house which he did not build. ^20-19

**20:20** And yet his belly was not filled: and when he hath the things he coveted, he shall not be able to possess them. ^20-20

**20:21** There was nothing left of his meat, and therefore nothing shall continue of his goods: ^20-21

**20:22** When he shall be filled, he shall be straitened, he shall burn, and every sorrow shall fall upon him. ^20-22

**20:23** May his belly be filled, that God may send forth the wrath of his indignation upon him, and rain down his war upon him. ^20-23

**20:24** He shall flee from weapons of iron, and shall fall upon a bow of brass. ^20-24

**20:25** The sword is drawn out, and cometh forth from its scabbard, and glittereth in his bitterness: the terrible ones shall go and come upon him. ^20-25

**20:26** All darkness is hid in his secret places: a fire that is not kindled shall devour him, he shall be afflicted when left in his tabernacle. ^20-26

**20:27** The heavens shall reveal his iniquity, and the earth shall rise up against him. ^20-27

**20:28** The offspring of his house shall be exposed, he shall be pulled down in the day of God’s wrath. ^20-28

**20:29** This is the portion of a wicked man from God, and the inheritance of his doings from the Lord. ^20-29
