# Arthur Avalon

"Arthur Avalon" was the pen name of Sir John Woodroffe (1865–1936), a judge of the Calcutta High Court from 1904 and later its chief justice, who after retiring was Reader in Indian Law at Oxford; under the pen name he translated and wrote on the Tantras ([Encyclopedia.com, from Gale](https://www.encyclopedia.com/science/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/woodroffe-sir-john-1865-1936)).
