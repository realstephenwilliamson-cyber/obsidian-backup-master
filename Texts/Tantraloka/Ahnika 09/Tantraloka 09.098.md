## Mūla

कर्मणो हेतुतामेतु मलः कथमिवोच्यताम् ।
किं च कर्मापि न मलाद्यतः कर्म क्रियात्मकम् ॥ ^mula

karmaṇo hetutāmetu malaḥ kathamivocyatām |
kiṃ ca karmāpi na malādyataḥ karma kriyātmakam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 05 - Chapter Nine, part 3#^pg112|p. 95]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=112>))
>
> Thus, pray tell, once its power has been rendered into a (mere) residue (khilībhūta) by its incipient destruction, how can Impurity (i.e. āṇavamala) become a cause of Karma? (with 9.97cd–98ab)
>
> Moreover, Karma also is not (in itself) due to Impurity, because Karma is action, and the cause of action is certainly not Impurity, but the freedom (of the subject), which is agency. (with 9.98cd–99ab)
