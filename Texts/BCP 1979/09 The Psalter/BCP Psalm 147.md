# Psalm 147

← [[BCP Psalm 146]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 148]] →

*Laudate Dominum* · Book Five · Thirtieth Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 147]]

**147:1** Hallelujah!  
How good it is to sing praises to our God! \*  
how pleasant it is to honor him with praise! ^147-1

**147:2** The LORD rebuilds Jerusalem; \*  
he gathers the exiles of Israel. ^147-2

**147:3** He heals the brokenhearted \*  
and binds up their wounds. ^147-3

**147:4** He counts the number of the stars \*  
and calls them all by their names. ^147-4

**147:5** Great is our LORD and mighty in power; \*  
there is no limit to his wisdom. ^147-5

**147:6** The LORD lifts up the lowly, \*  
but casts the wicked to the ground. ^147-6

**147:7** Sing to the LORD with thanksgiving; \*  
make music to our God upon the harp. ^147-7

**147:8** He covers the heavens with clouds \*  
and prepares rain for the earth; ^147-8

**147:9** He makes grass to grow upon the mountains \*  
and green plants to serve mankind. ^147-9

**147:10** He provides food for flocks and herds \*  
and for the young ravens when they cry. ^147-10

**147:11** He is not impressed by the might of a horse; \*  
he has no pleasure in the strength of a man; ^147-11

**147:12** But the LORD has pleasure in those who fear him, \*  
in those who await his gracious favor. ^147-12

[p. 805] ^pg805

**147:13** Worship the LORD, O Jerusalem; \*  
praise your God, O Zion; ^147-13

**147:14** For he has strengthened the bars of your gates; \*  
he has blessed your children within you. ^147-14

**147:15** He has established peace on your borders; \*  
he satisfies you with the finest wheat. ^147-15

**147:16** He sends out his command to the earth, \*  
and his word runs very swiftly. ^147-16

**147:17** He gives snow like wool; \*  
he scatters hoarfrost like ashes. ^147-17

**147:18** He scatters his hail like bread crumbs; \*  
who can stand against his cold? ^147-18

**147:19** He sends forth his word and melts them; \*  
he blows with his wind, and the waters flow. ^147-19

**147:20** He declares his word to Jacob, \*  
his statutes and his judgments to Israel. ^147-20

**147:21** He has not done so to any other nation; \*  
to them he has not revealed his judgments.  
Hallelujah! ^147-21
