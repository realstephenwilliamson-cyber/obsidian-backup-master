# Job 14 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Job 13|← Job 13]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Job 15|Job 15 →]]

**14:1** “A mortal, born of woman, few of days and full of trouble, ^14-1

**14:2** comes up like a flower and withers, flees like a shadow and does not last. ^14-2

**14:3** Do you fix your eyes on such a one? Do you bring me into judgment with you? ^14-3

**14:4** Who can bring a clean thing out of an unclean? No one can. ^14-4

**14:5** Since their days are determined, and the number of their months is known to you, and you have appointed the bounds that they cannot pass, ^14-5

**14:6** look away from them and desist,^[Cn: Heb *that they may desist*] that they may enjoy, like laborers, their days. ^14-6

**14:7** “For there is hope for a tree, if it is cut down, that it will sprout again and that its shoots will not cease. ^14-7

**14:8** Though its root grows old in the earth and its stump dies in the ground, ^14-8

**14:9** yet at the scent of water it will bud and put forth branches like a young plant. ^14-9

**14:10** But mortals die and are laid low; humans expire, and where are they? ^14-10

**14:11** As waters fail from a lake and a river wastes away and dries up, ^14-11

**14:12** so mortals lie down and do not rise again; until the heavens are no more, they will not awake or be roused out of their sleep. ^14-12

**14:13** O that you would hide me in Sheol, that you would conceal me until your wrath is past, that you would appoint me a set time and remember me! ^14-13

**14:14** If mortals die, will they live again? All the days of my service I would wait until my release should come. ^14-14

**14:15** You would call, and I would answer you; you would long for the work of your hands. ^14-15

**14:16** For then you would not^[Syr: Heb lacks *not*] number my steps; you would not keep watch over my sin; ^14-16

**14:17** my transgression would be sealed up in a bag, and you would cover over my iniquity. ^14-17

**14:18** “But the mountain falls and crumbles away, and the rock is removed from its place; ^14-18

**14:19** the waters wear away the stones; the torrents wash away the soil of the earth; so you destroy the hope of mortals. ^14-19

**14:20** You prevail forever against them, and they pass away; you change their countenance and send them away. ^14-20

**14:21** Their children come to honor, and they do not know it; they are brought low, and it goes unnoticed. ^14-21

**14:22** They feel only the pain of their own bodies and mourn only for themselves.” ^14-22
