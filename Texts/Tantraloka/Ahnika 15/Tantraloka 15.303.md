## Mūla

तच्च तत्त्वं स्थितं भाव्यं लम्बिकाब्रह्मरन्ध्रयोः ।
प्रकाशयोगो ह्यत्रैवं दृक्श्रोत्ररसनादिकः ॥ ^mula

tacca tattvaṃ sthitaṃ bhāvyaṃ lambikābrahmarandhrayoḥ |
prakāśayogo hyatraivaṃ dṛkśrotrarasanādikaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 06 - Chapter Fifteen, part 5#^pg194|p. 176]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=194>))
>
> On the plane (just) above the Knot of Māyā and (immediately) below the three prongs (of the Trident), one should meditate of the square pedestal (catuṣkikā), which consists of Pure Knowledge and possesses the two coverings. One should meditate on this principle between the uvula and the Cavity of Brahmā. (with 15.302–303ab)
>
> The link with the light (of the intellect) (prakāśayoga) takes place here in this way especially (in the head), as (the senses of) sight, hearing, taste and the rest. (with 15.303cd)
