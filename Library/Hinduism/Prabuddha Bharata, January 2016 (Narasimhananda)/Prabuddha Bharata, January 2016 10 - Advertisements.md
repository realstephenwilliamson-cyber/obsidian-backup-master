Stories from The Bhagavatam 

—By Swami Bodhasarananda 

The Bhagavata Purana has for centuries been one of the favourite religious texts of India, and this is mostly because of its charming and attractive stories, like the story of Prahlada, of Dhruva, of King Bharata, and many more. What the Bhagavatam is most known for, however, are the stories of Krishna’s life. Almost all his stories are included in this volume—his childhood in Vrindavan, his youth in Mathura, and his later years in Dwaraka.
