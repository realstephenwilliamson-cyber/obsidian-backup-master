# Genesis 10 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Genesis 09|← Genesis 9]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Genesis 11|Genesis 11 →]]

**10:1** These are the generations of the sons of Noe: Sem, Cham, and Japheth: and unto them sons were born after the flood. ^10-1

**10:2** The sons of Japheth: Gomer, and Magog, and Madai, and Javan, and Thubal, and Mosoch, and Thiras. ^10-2

**10:3** And the sons of Gomer: Ascenez and Riphath and Thogorma. ^10-3

**10:4** And the sons of Javan: Elisa and Tharsis, Cetthim and Dodanim. ^10-4

**10:5** By these were divided the islands of the Gentiles in their lands, every one according to his tongue and their families in their nations. ^10-5

**10:6** And the sons of Cham: Chus, and Mesram, and Phuth, and Chanaan. ^10-6

**10:7** And the sons of Chus: Saba, and Hevila, and Sabatha, and Regma, and Sabatacha. The sons of Regma: Saba and Dadan. ^10-7

**10:8** Now Chus begot Nemrod: he began to be mighty on the earth. ^10-8

**10:9** And he was a stout hunter before the Lord. Hence came a proverb: Even as Nemrod the stout hunter before the Lord. ^10-9

**10:10** And the beginning of his kingdom was Babylon, and Arach, and Achad, and Chalanne in the land of Sennaar. ^10-10

**10:11** Out of that land came forth Assur, and built Ninive, and the streets of the city, and Chale. ^10-11

**10:12** Resen also between Ninive and Chale: this is the great city. ^10-12

**10:13** And Mesraim begot Ludim, and Anamim, and Laabim, Nepthuim, ^10-13

**10:14** And Phetrusim, and Chasluim; of whom came forth the Philistines, and the Capthorim. ^10-14

**10:15** And Chanaan begot Sidon, his firstborn, the Hethite, ^10-15

**10:16** And the Jebusite, and the Amorrhite, and the Gergesite, ^10-16

**10:17** The Hevite and the Aracite: the Sinite, ^10-17

**10:18** And the Aradian, the Samarite, and the Hamathite: and afterwards the families of the Chanaanites were spread abroad. ^10-18

**10:19** And the limits of Chanaan were from Sidon as one comes to Gerara even to Gaza, until thou enter Sodom and Gomorrha, and Adama, and Seboim even to Lesa. ^10-19

**10:20** These are the children of Cham in their kindreds, and tongues, and generations, and lands, and nations. ^10-20

**10:21** Of Sem also, the father of all the children of Heber, the elder brother of Japheth, sons were born. ^10-21

**10:22** The sons of Sem: Elam and Assur, and Arphaxad, and Lud, and Aram. ^10-22

**10:23** The sons of Aram: Us and Hull, and Gether, and Mess. ^10-23

**10:24** But Arphaxad begot Sale, of whom was born Heber. ^10-24

**10:25** And to Heber were born two sons: the name of the one was Phaleg, because in his days the earth was divided: and his brother’s name Jectan. ^10-25

**10:26** Which Jectan begot Elmodad, and Saleph, and Asarmoth, Jare, ^10-26

**10:27** And Aduram, and Uzal, and Decla, ^10-27

**10:28** And Ebal, and Abimael, Saba, ^10-28

**10:29** And Ophir, and Hevila, and Jobab. All these were the sons of Jectan. ^10-29

**10:30** And their dwelling was from Messa as we go on as far as Sephar, a mountain in the east. ^10-30

**10:31** These are the children of Sem according to their kindreds and tongues, and countries in their nations. ^10-31

**10:32** These are the families of Noe, according to their peoples and nations. By these were the nations divided on the earth after the flood. ^10-32
