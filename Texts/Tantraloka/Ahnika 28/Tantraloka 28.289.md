## Mūla

अत एव प्रबुद्धोऽपि कर्मोत्थान्भोगरूपिणः ।
यमकिङ्करसर्पादिप्रत्ययान्देहगो भजेत् ॥ ^mula

ata eva prabuddho'pi karmotthānbhogarūpiṇaḥ |
yamakiṅkarasarpādipratyayāndehago bhajet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 06 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 4#^pg142|p. 125]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=142>))
>
> Thus, even one who is awakened (and enlightened, but still) resides in the body, may have notions (pratyaya) that (he is beset by demons who are) the servants of Yama (the lord of the dead), snakes and the like, that are (in actual fact just) experiences (bhoga) born from (his past) Karma.
