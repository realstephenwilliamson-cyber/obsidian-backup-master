The Third *Āvaraṇa*: The Eight Erotic Sentiments

The third *āvaraṇa* (*tṛtīyāvaraṇam*) is the eight-petaled lotus (*aṣṭadalapadma*). It is the *sarvasaṁkṣōbhaṇa cakra*, the “wheel that agitates all.”

Upon completing the evolutionary process, we begin experiencing interactions. The cosmic wealth of our experience is encompassed by the Anaṅga Dēvatās,[^413] who are eight in number. They take the form of wealth—the wealth of experience, of God, of the cosmos. The eight-petaled lotus encompasses the eight forms of *aiśvarya*.[^414] The mantra *klīm* means attraction. Expanding oneself into the other and into the whole world is called *sarva ākarṣaṇa*.[^415] Dissolving the entire world into oneself is a function of Manmatha’s shaktis,[^416] who represent all forms of intercourse.

[^413]: Literally, formless deities; deities without bodies.
[^414]: Wealth, prosperity.
[^415]: All-attracting.
[^416]: The energies of Kāma, the God of Love.
