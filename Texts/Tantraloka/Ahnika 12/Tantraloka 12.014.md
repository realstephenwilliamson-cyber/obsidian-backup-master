## Mūla

अत्र पूजाजपाद्येषु बहिरन्तर्द्वयस्थितौ ।
द्रव्यौघे न विधिः कोऽपि न कापि प्रतिषिद्धता ॥ ^mula

atra pūjājapādyeṣu bahirantardvayasthitau |
dravyaughe na vidhiḥ ko'pi na kāpi pratiṣiddhatā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 04 - Chapter Twelve#^pg128|p. 108]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=128>))
>
> Here (in this tradition), worship, the repetition of mantra and the rest, (and so too) the flood of sacrificial substances, be they internal or external, are neither enjoined nor are (they) prohibited at all.
