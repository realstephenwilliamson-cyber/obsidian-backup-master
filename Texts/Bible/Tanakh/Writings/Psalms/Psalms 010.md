## Chapter 10

**10:1** Why, O LORD, do You stand aloof,  
heedless in times of trouble? ^10-1

**10:2** The wicked in his arrogance hounds the lowly—  
may they be caught in the schemes they devise! [^1] ^10-2

**10:3** [^2]The wicked crows about his unbridled lusts;  
the grasping man reviles and scorns the LORD. ^10-3

**10:4** The wicked, arrogant as he is,  
in all his scheming [thinks],  
“He does not call to account;  
God does not care.”[^3] ^10-4

**10:5** His ways prosper at all times;  
Your judgments are far beyond him;  
he snorts at all his foes. ^10-5

**10:6** He thinks, “I shall not be shaken,  
through all time never be in trouble.” ^10-6

**10:7** His mouth is full of oaths, deceit, and fraud;  
mischief and evil are under his tongue. ^10-7

**10:8** He lurks in outlying places;  
from a covert he slays the innocent;  
his eyes spy out the hapless. ^10-8

**10:9** He waits in a covert like a lion in his lair;  
waits to seize the lowly;  
he seizes the lowly as he pulls his net shut; ^10-9

**10:10** he stoops, he crouches,  
and the hapless fall prey to his might.[^4] ^10-10

**10:11** He thinks, “God is not mindful,  
He hides His face, He never looks.” ^10-11

**10:12** Rise, O LORD!  
Strike at him,[^5] O God!  
Do not forget the lowly. ^10-12

**10:13** Why should the wicked man scorn God,  
thinking You do not call to account? ^10-13

**10:14** You do look!  
You take note of mischief and vexation!  
To requite is in Your power.[^6]  
To You the hapless can entrust himself;  
You have ever been the orphan’s help. ^10-14

**10:15** O break the power of the wicked and evil man,  
so that when You look for[^7] his wickedness  
You will find it no more. ^10-15

**10:16** The LORD is king for ever and ever;  
the nations will perish from His land. ^10-16

**10:17** You will listen to the entreaty of the lowly, O LORD,  
You will make their hearts firm;  
You will incline Your ear ^10-17

**10:18** to champion the orphan and the downtrodden,  
that men who are of the earth tyrannize no more.[^8] ^10-18

[^1]: Or “they (i.e., the lowly) are caught by the schemes they devised.”
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Lit. “There is no God.”
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^5]: Lit. “Lift Your hand.”
[^6]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^7]: A play on darash, which in vv. 4 and 13 means “to call to account.”
[^8]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
