## Chapter 15

**15:1** The LORD said to me, “Even if Moses and Samuel were to intercede with Me,[^1] I would not be won over to that people. Dismiss them from My presence, and let them go forth! ^15-1

**15:2** And if they ask you, ‘To what shall we go forth?’ answer them, ‘Thus said the LORD:  
Those destined for the plague, to the plague;  
Those destined for the sword, to the sword;  
Those destined for famine, to famine;  
Those destined for captivity, to captivity. ^15-2

**15:3** And I will appoint over them four kinds[^2] [of punishment]—declares the LORD—the sword to slay, the dogs to drag, the birds of the sky, and the beasts of the earth to devour and destroy. ^15-3

**15:4** I will make them a horror to all the kingdoms of the earth, on account of King Manasseh son of Hezekiah of Judah, and of what he did in Jerusalem.’” ^15-4

**15:5** But who will pity you, O Jerusalem,  
Who will console you?  
Who will turn aside to inquire  
About your welfare? ^15-5

**15:6** You cast Me off  
—declares the LORD—  
You go ever backward.  
So I have stretched out My hand to destroy you;  
I cannot relent. ^15-6

**15:7** I will scatter them as with a winnowing fork  
Through the settlements of the earth.  
I will bereave, I will destroy My people,  
For they would not turn back from their ways. ^15-7

**15:8** Their widows shall be more numerous  
Than the sands of the seas.  
I will bring against them—  
Young men and mothers together[^3]—  
A destroyer at noonday.  
I will bring down suddenly upon them  
Alarm[^4] and terror. ^15-8

**15:9** She who bore seven is forlorn,  
Utterly disconsolate;  
Her sun has set while it is still day,  
She is shamed and humiliated.  
The remnant of them I will deliver to the sword,  
To the power of their enemies  
—declares the LORD. ^15-9

**15:10** Woe is me, my mother, that you ever bore me—  
A man of conflict and strife with all the land!  
I have not lent,  
And I have not borrowed;  
Yet everyone curses me. ^15-10

**15:11** The LORD said:  
Surely, a mere remnant of you  
Will I spare for a better fate![^5]  
By the enemy from the north[^6]  
In a time of distress and a time of disaster,  
Surely, I will have you struck down! ^15-11

**15:12** Can iron break iron and bronze?[^7] ^15-12

**15:13** [^8]I will hand over your wealth and your treasures  
As a spoil, free of charge,  
Because of all your sins throughout your territory. ^15-13

**15:14** And I will bring your enemies  
By way of a land you have not known.  
For a fire has flared in My wrath,  
It blazes against you. ^15-14

**15:15** O LORD, you know—  
Remember me and take thought of me,  
Avenge me on those who persecute me;  
Do not yield to Your patience,  
Do not let me perish![^9]  
Consider how I have borne insult  
On Your account. ^15-15

**15:16** When Your words were offered, I devoured them;  
Your word brought me the delight and joy  
Of knowing that Your name is attached to me,  
O LORD, God of Hosts. ^15-16

**15:17** I have not sat in the company of revelers  
And made merry!  
I have sat lonely because of Your hand upon me,  
For You have filled me with gloom. ^15-17

**15:18** Why must my pain be endless,  
My wound incurable,  
Resistant to healing?  
You have been to me like a spring that fails,  
Like waters that cannot be relied on. ^15-18

**15:19** Assuredly, thus said the LORD:  
If you turn back, I shall take you back  
And you shall stand before Me;  
If you produce what is noble  
Out of the worthless,  
You shall be My spokesman.  
They shall come back to you,  
Not you to them. ^15-19

**15:20** Against this people I will make you  
As a fortified wall of bronze:  
They will attack you,  
But they shall not overcome you,  
For I am with you to deliver and save you  
—declares the LORD. ^15-20

**15:21** I will save you from the hands of the wicked  
And rescue you from the clutches of the violent. ^15-21

[^1]: Lit. “stand before Me,” as Jeremiah is doing now; cf. 18.20.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^5]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^6]: Moved up from v. 12 for clarity.
[^7]: Emendation yields “He shall shatter iron—iron and bronze!”
[^8]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^9]: Lit. “Do not take me away.”
