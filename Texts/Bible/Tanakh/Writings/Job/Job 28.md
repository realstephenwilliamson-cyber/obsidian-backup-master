# Job 28

← [[Job 27]] · [[Tanakh]] · [[Job 29]] →

Compare: [[NRSVue Job 28|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 28

**28:1** There is a mine for silver,  
And a place where gold is refined. ^28-1

**28:2** Iron is taken out of the earth,  
And copper smelted from rock. ^28-2

**28:3** He sets bounds for darkness;  
To every limit man probes,  
To rocks in deepest darkness. ^28-3

**28:4** They open up a shaft far from where men live,  
[In places] forgotten by wayfarers,  
Destitute of men, far removed.[^1] ^28-4

**28:5** Earth, out of which food grows,  
Is changed below as if into fire. ^28-5

**28:6** Its rocks are a source of sapphires;  
It contains gold dust too. ^28-6

**28:7** No bird of prey knows the path to it;  
The falcon’s eye has not gazed upon it. ^28-7

**28:8** The proud beasts have not reached it;  
The lion has not crossed it. ^28-8

**28:9** Man sets his hand against the flinty rock  
And overturns mountains by the roots. ^28-9

**28:10** He carves out channels through rock;  
His eyes behold every precious thing. ^28-10

**28:11** He dams up the sources of the streams  
So that hidden things may be brought to light. ^28-11

**28:12** But where can wisdom be found;  
Where is the source of understanding? ^28-12

**28:13** No man can set a value on it;  
It cannot be found in the land of the living. ^28-13

**28:14** The deep says, “It is not in me”;  
The sea says, “I do not have it.” ^28-14

**28:15** It cannot be bartered for gold;  
Silver cannot be paid out as its price. ^28-15

**28:16** The finest gold of Ophir cannot be weighed against it,  
Nor precious onyx, nor sapphire. ^28-16

**28:17** Gold or glass cannot match its value,  
Nor vessels of fine gold be exchanged for it. ^28-17

**28:18** Coral and crystal cannot be mentioned with it;  
A pouch of wisdom is better than rubies. ^28-18

**28:19** Topaz from Nubia cannot match its value;  
Pure gold cannot be weighed against it. ^28-19

**28:20** But whence does wisdom come?  
Where is the source of understanding? ^28-20

**28:21** It is hidden from the eyes of all living,  
Concealed from the fowl of heaven. ^28-21

**28:22** Abaddon and Death say,  
“We have only a report of it.” ^28-22

**28:23** God understands the way to it;  
He knows its source; ^28-23

**28:24** For He sees to the ends of the earth,  
Observes all that is beneath the heavens. ^28-24

**28:25** When He fixed the weight of the winds,  
Set the measure of the waters; ^28-25

**28:26** When He made a rule for the rain  
And a course for the thunderstorms, ^28-26

**28:27** Then He saw it and gauged it;  
He measured it and probed it. ^28-27

**28:28** He said to man,  
“See! Fear of the Lord is wisdom;  
To shun evil is understanding.” ^28-28

[^1]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
