## Mūla

तदवश्यग्रहीतव्ये शास्त्रे स्वांशोपदेशिनि ।
मनाक्फलेऽभ्युपादेयतमं तद्विपरीतकम् ॥ ^mula

tadavaśyagrahītavye śāstre svāṃśopadeśini |
manākphale'bhyupādeyatamaṃ tadviparītakam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 11 - Chapter Thirty-Seven, part 1#^pg246|p. 228]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=246>))
>
> (Although) the scriptures, that teach their own (partial) aspects (of reality) and (so yield) limited fruits, must necessarily be chosen (by those who want these fruits), (the scripture) which is much more worthy of selection is one that (yields unlimited fruit, and so) is the opposite of this.
