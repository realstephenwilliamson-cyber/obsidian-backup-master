# LXX Psalms 89

← [[LXX Psalms 88]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 90]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 090]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 090]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**89:1** A Prayer of Moses the man of God. Lord, thou hast been our refuge in all generations.  ^89-1

**89:2** Before the mountains existed, and *before* the earth and the world were formed, even from age to age, Thou art.  ^89-2

**89:3** Turn not man back to *his* low place, whereas thou saidst, Return, ye sons of men?  ^89-3

**89:4** For a thousand years in thy sight are as the yesterday which is past, and as a watch in the night.  ^89-4

**89:5** Years shall be vanity to them: let the morning pass away as grass.[^1]  ^89-5

**89:6** In the morning let it flower, and pass away: in the evening let it droop, let it be withered and dried up.  ^89-6

**89:7** For we have perished in thine anger, and in thy wrath we have been troubled.  ^89-7

**89:8** Thou hast set our transgressions before thee: our age is in the light of thy countenance.  ^89-8

**89:9** For all our days are gone, and we have passed away in thy wrath: our years have spun out their tale as a spider.[^2]  ^89-9

**89:10** *As for* the days of our years, in them are seventy years; and if *men should be* in strength, eighty years: and the greater part of them would be labour and trouble; for weakness overtakes us, and we shall be chastened.[^3]  ^89-10

**89:11** Who knows the power of thy wrath?  ^89-11

**89:12** and *who knows how* to number *his days* because of the fear of thy wrath? So manifest thy right hand, and those that are instructed in wisdom in the heart.  ^89-12

**89:13** Return, O Lord, how long? and be intreated concerning thy servants.  ^89-13

**89:14** We have been satisfied in the morning with thy mercy; and we did exult and rejoice:  ^89-14

**89:15** let us rejoice in all our days, in return for the days wherein thou didst afflict us, the years wherein we saw evil.  ^89-15

**89:16** And look upon thy servants, and upon thy works; and guide their children.  ^89-16

**89:17** And let the brightness of the Lord our God be upon us: and do thou direct for us the works of our hands.[^4][^5]  ^89-17

[^1]: 89:5 *Gr.* their vanities or rejections.
[^2]: 89:9 *Gr.* mediated.
[^3]: 89:10 *Or,* what is more than these.
[^4]: 89:17 *Or,* prosper.
[^5]: 89:17 *Alex.*+ yea, prosper thou the work of our hands.
