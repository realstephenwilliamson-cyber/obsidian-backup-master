# Psalm 112 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 111|← Psalm 111]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 113|Psalm 113 →]]

## Blessings of the righteous

**112:1** Praise the LORD! Happy are those who fear the LORD, who greatly delight in his commandments. ^112-1

**112:2** Their descendants will be mighty in the land; the generation of the upright will be blessed. ^112-2

**112:3** Wealth and riches are in their houses, and their righteousness endures forever. ^112-3

**112:4** They rise in the darkness as a light for the upright; they are gracious, merciful, and righteous. ^112-4

**112:5** It is well with those who deal generously and lend, who conduct their affairs with justice. ^112-5

**112:6** For the righteous will never be moved; they will be remembered forever. ^112-6

**112:7** They are not afraid of evil tidings; their hearts are firm, secure in the LORD. ^112-7

**112:8** Their hearts are steady; they will not be afraid; in the end they will look in triumph on their foes. ^112-8

**112:9** They have distributed freely; they have given to the poor; their righteousness endures forever; their horn is exalted in honor. ^112-9

**112:10** The wicked see it and are angry; they gnash their teeth and melt away; the desire of the wicked comes to nothing. ^112-10
