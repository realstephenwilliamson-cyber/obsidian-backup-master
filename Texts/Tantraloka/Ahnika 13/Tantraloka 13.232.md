## Mūla

न विशङ्केत तच्च श्रीगमशास्त्रे निरूपितम् ।
अविद्योपासितो देहो ह्यन्यजन्मसमुद्भुवा ॥ ^mula

na viśaṅketa tacca śrīgamaśāstre nirūpitam |
avidyopāsito deho hyanyajanmasamudbhuvā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 09 - Chapter Thirteen, part 5#^pg292|p. 270]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=292>))
>
> The body is subject to pleasure and pain because it is associated with Karma which is in the process of giving rise to its consequences (prārabdhrkarma). He should not doubt (that he is liberated). That is said in the Gamatantra. (with 13.231cd–232ab)
>
> ‘Indeed, the body is attended (upāsita) by ignorance (avidyā) that arose (due to Karma) in another life. Thus, even realised souls (jñānin) are bound by the Karma (of previous lives), so long as the body persists.’ (with 13.232cd–233ab)
