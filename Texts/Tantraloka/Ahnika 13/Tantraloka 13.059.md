## Mūla

अथास्य पाको नामैष स्वशक्तिप्रतिबद्धता ।
सर्वान्प्रति तथैष स्याद् रुद्धशक्तिर्विषाग्निवत् ॥ ^mula

athāsya pāko nāmaiṣa svaśaktipratibaddhatā |
sarvānprati tathaiṣa syād ruddhaśaktirviṣāgnivat || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 06 - Chapter Thirteen, part 2#^pg180|p. 160]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=180>))
>
> Now, (the opponent replies that,) this (process whereby Impurity) matures (pāka) is the paralysis (pratibaddhatā) of its powers (not its destruction). (In that case, we would say) that (Impurity) should then be like that, namely, with its power paralyzed for everybody, like fire (that burns everything that is inflammable placed in it) or poison (that affects everybody who takes it). Moreover, when its power awakens, it would (give rise to its) own effect again, as does fire (that has gone out and is then relit,) or poison (ingested a second time). Thus, those who have been liberated would become bound (again). Moreover, we fail to understand what this power (of Impurity) could be. (with 13.59–60)
