ये तु तीत्रतमोद्रिक्तशक्तिनिर्मलताजुषः ।। २७५ ॥
न ते दीक्षामनुन्यासकारिणश्चेति वर्णितम् ।

ye tu tīvratamodriktaśaktinirmalatājuṣah ॥ 275 ॥
na te dīikṣāmanunyāsakāriṇaś ceti varnṇitam | 

It has (already) been said that those who possesses the purity which results from the most intense descent of the power (of grace) do not undergo the rites of initiation, (repeat) Mantras, or (perform) the depositions. (275cd-276ab) 

‘It has (already) been said’ in Chapter Two (which deals with Anupāya). 

Having talked about that by the way, he (goes on to discuss) the main point: 

The Worship of the Body 

The Officiant Who by the Depositions Has Become 

All Things Should Worship his Body 

एवं विश्वशरीर: सच्विश्वात्मत्वं गत; स्फुटम् ।। २७६ ॥
न्यासमात्रात् तथाभूतं देहं पष्पादिनार्चयेत् ।
पृथङ्मनर्विस्तरेण संक्षेपान्ूलमनत्रतः ॥। २७७ ॥
धूपनेवेदयतृप्तयाद्यैस्तथा व्याससमासतः ।

evar viśvaśarīrah san viśvātmatvaṁ gatah sphuṭam | 276 ॥
nyāsamātrāt tathābhūtaṁ dehan puṣpādinārcayet |
prthaṁ mantrair vistareṇa sarùṁkṣepān mūlamantratah || 277 |
dhūpanaivedyatrptyādyais tathā vyāsasamāsatah |
