*Part I · Lesson 256* · [[Likutei Moharan]]

## 1

The Name *ATaH* is propitious at sea for calming the waves. This is the inner meaning of “In the raising of its waves, *Atah* (You) quiet them” ([[Psalms 089#^89-10|Psalms 89:10]]).
