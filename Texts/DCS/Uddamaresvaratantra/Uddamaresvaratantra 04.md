# Uḍḍāmareśvaratantra — Chapter 4

[[Uddamaresvaratantra 03|← Chapter 3]] · [[Uddamaresvaratantra|← index]] · [[Uddamaresvaratantra 05|Chapter 5 →]]

athātaḥ sampravakṣyāmi bhūtavāde sudurlabham

^s1

yena vijñānamātreṇa sarvasiddhiḥ prajāyate

^s2

uoṃ hrīṃ kālī kaṅkālī kila kila svāhā

^s3

idaṃ mantraṃ pūrvaṃ sahasradaśakaṃ japet

^s4

tataḥ sahasraṃ juhuyāt kaṅkālī varadā bhavati suvarṇamāṣacatuṣṭayaṃ pratyahaṃ dadāti

^s5

atha sarvajanamukhastambhanam antaḥ uoṃ hrīṃ namo bhagavatī durvacatī kili vācābhañjanī sarvajanamukhastambhinī hrāṃ hrīṃ hraiṃ hrauṃ hraḥ svāhā

^s6

anena mantreṇa bilvamaricaṃ ghṛtāktaṃ sahasrahavanaṃ kuryāt samastajanapadāḥ kiṃkarā bhavanti

^s7

etanmantreṇa yadi nyagrodhasamidhaṃ ghṛtāktāṃ sahasraikaṃ homayet tadā strīvaśyaṃ bhavati

^s8

uoṃ rauṃ hrīṃ hūṃ hūṃ eṃ vada vada vāgvādinī vāgīśvarī namaḥ svāhā

^s9

anena mantreṇa sahasrajaptena kavitvaṃ karoti nātra saṃdehaḥ

^s10

uoṃ uoṃ uoṃ īṃ īṃ īṃ aiṃ aiṃ aiṃ namaḥ svāhā

^s11

anena mantreṇa daśasahasrajaptena kavitvaṃ karoti

^s12
