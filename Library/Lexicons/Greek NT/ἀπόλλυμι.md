# ἀπόλλυμι

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · verb

**Dodson:** I destroy, lose, am perishing — (a) I kill, destroy, (b) I lose, mid: I am perishing (the resultant death being viewed as certain). (Strong's 622; headword ἀπόλλυμι)
