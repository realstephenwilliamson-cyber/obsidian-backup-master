## Mūla

शूलदण्डान्तमध्यस्थशूलमध्यान्तगोचरम् ।
प्रविशेन्मूलमध्यान्तं प्रान्तान्ते शक्तिवेश्मनि ॥ ^mula

śūladaṇḍāntamadhyasthaśūlamadhyāntagocaram |
praviśenmūlamadhyāntaṃ prāntānte śaktiveśmani || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 05 - Chapter Thirty-One, part 2#^pg135|p. 117]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=135>))
>
> ‘After repeatedly casting upwards the area (gocara) from the middle of the the end of the trident that is located in the middle of the end of the staff, by means of the energy (kalā) (that comes forth) from the one’s own essential nature in the middle of the body, he should enter into the end of the middle of the base within the Abode of Power in the end of (its) extremity. (with 31.126cd–127)
