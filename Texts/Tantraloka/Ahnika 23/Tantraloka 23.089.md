## Mūla

यतस्तदप्रियं नैष शृणुयादिति भाषितम् ।
श्रीमातङ्गे तदुक्तं च नाधीतं भूमभीतितः ॥ ^mula

yatastadapriyaṃ naiṣa śṛṇuyāditi bhāṣitam |
śrīmātaṅge taduktaṃ ca nādhītaṃ bhūmabhītitaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 14 - Chapter Twenty-Three#^pg354|p. 334]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=354>))
>
> (A defect) like farming (infertile) saline soil or wandering around at night, does not entail a defect in the teacher’s status. (A significant defect) steals away the vital breath and material wealth (of others). But although fearful of its displeasing (nature), even so, (the disciple) should behave in such a way that he may hear what is displeasing (nor should he tell others about it). This is what is said (in the Mālinīvijayottara). (with 23.87cd–89ab)
>
> The same is said in the venerable Mataṁga. It is not cited (here) for fear of being too verbose. (with 23.89cd)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* न च एतदस्मच्छास्त्र एवोक्तम्--इत्याह--

> श्रीमातङ्गे तदुक्तं च नाधीतं भूमभीतितः ॥

भूमभीतित इति--ग्रन्थविस्तरभयात्--इत्यर्थः । तत् ततः स्वयमेव चर्यापादादेरनुसर्तव्यम् ॥८९ ॥
