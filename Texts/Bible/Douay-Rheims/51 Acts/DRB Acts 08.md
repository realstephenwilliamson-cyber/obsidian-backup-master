# Acts 8 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Acts 07|← Acts 7]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Acts 09|Acts 9 →]]

Compare: [[Acts 08|BSB and SBLGNT]]

**8:1** And at that time there was raised a great persecution against the church which was at Jerusalem; and they were all dispersed through the countries of Judea, and Samaria, except the apostles. ^8-1

**8:2** And devout men took order for Stephen’s funeral, and made great mourning over him. ^8-2

**8:3** But Saul made havock of the church, entering in from house to house, and dragging away men and women, committed them to prison. ^8-3

**8:4** They therefore that were dispersed, went about preaching the word of God. ^8-4

**8:5** And Philip going down to the city of Samaria, preached Christ unto them. ^8-5

**8:6** And the people with one accord were attentive to those things which were said by Philip, hearing, and seeing the miracles which he did. ^8-6

**8:7** For many of them who had unclean spirits, crying with a loud voice, went out. ^8-7

**8:8** And many, taken with the palsy, and that were lame, were healed. ^8-8

**8:9** There was therefore great joy in that city. Now there was a certain man named Simon, who before had been a magician in that city, seducing the people of Samaria, giving out that he was some great one: ^8-9

**8:10** To whom they all gave ear, from the least to the greatest, saying: This man is the power of God, which is called great. ^8-10

**8:11** And they were attentive to him, because, for a long time, he had bewitched them with his magical practices. ^8-11

**8:12** But when they had believed Philip preaching of the kingdom of God, in the name of Jesus Christ, they were baptized, both men and women. ^8-12

**8:13** Then Simon himself believed also; and being baptized, he adhered to Philip. And being astonished, wondered to see the signs and exceeding great miracles which were done. ^8-13

**8:14** Now when the apostles, who were in Jerusalem, had heard that Samaria had received the word of God, they sent unto them Peter and John. ^8-14

**8:15** Who, when they were come, prayed for them, that they might receive the Holy Ghost. ^8-15

**8:16** For he was not as yet come upon any of them; but they were only baptized in the name of the Lord Jesus. ^8-16

**8:17** Then they laid their hands upon them, and they received the Holy Ghost. ^8-17

**8:18** And when Simon saw, that by the imposition of the hands of the apostles, the Holy Ghost was given, he offered them money, ^8-18

**8:19** Saying: Give me also this power, that on whomsoever I shall lay my hands, he may receive the Holy Ghost. But Peter said to him: ^8-19

**8:20** Keep thy money to thyself, to perish with thee, because thou hast thought that the gift of God may be purchased with money. ^8-20

**8:21** Thou hast no part nor lot in this matter. For thy heart is not right in the sight of God. ^8-21

**8:22** Do penance therefore for this thy wickedness; and pray to God, that perhaps this thought of thy heart may be forgiven thee. ^8-22

**8:23** For I see thou art in the gall of bitterness, and in the bonds of iniquity. ^8-23

**8:24** Then Simon answering, said: Pray you for me to the Lord, that none of these things which you have spoken may come upon me. ^8-24

**8:25** And they indeed having testified and preached the word of the Lord, returned to Jerusalem, and preached the gospel to many countries of the Samaritans. ^8-25

**8:26** Now an angel of the Lord spoke to Philip, saying: Arise, go towards the south, to the way that goeth down from Jerusalem into Gaza: this is desert. ^8-26

**8:27** And rising up, he went. And behold a man of Ethiopia, an eunuch, of great authority under Candace the queen of the Ethiopians, who had charge over all her treasures, had come to Jerusalem to adore. ^8-27

**8:28** And he was returning, sitting in this chariot, and reading Isaias the prophet. ^8-28

**8:29** And the Spirit said to Philip: Go near, and join thyself to this chariot. ^8-29

**8:30** And Philip running thither, heard him reading the prophet Isaias. And he said: Thinkest thou that thou understandest what thou readest? ^8-30

**8:31** Who said: And how can I, unless some man shew me? And he desired Philip that he would come up and sit with him. ^8-31

**8:32** And the place of the scripture which he was reading was this: He was led as a sheep to the slaughter; and like a lamb without voice before his shearer, so openeth he not his mouth. ^8-32

**8:33** In humility his judgment was taken away. His generation who shall declare, for his life shall be taken from the earth? ^8-33

**8:34** And the eunuch answering Philip, said: I beseech thee, of whom doth the prophet speak this? of himself, or of some other man? ^8-34

**8:35** Then Philip, opening his mouth, and beginning at this scripture, preached unto him Jesus. ^8-35

**8:36** And as they went on their way, they came to a certain water; and the eunuch said: See, here is water: what doth hinder me from being baptized? ^8-36

**8:37** And Philip said: If thou believest with all thy heart, thou mayest. And he answering, said: I believe that Jesus Christ is the Son of God. ^8-37

**8:38** And he commanded the chariot to stand still; and they went down into the water, both Philip and the eunuch: and he baptized him. ^8-38

**8:39** And when they were come up out of the water, the Spirit of the Lord took away Philip; and the eunuch saw him no more. And he went on his way rejoicing. ^8-39

**8:40** But Philip was found in Azotus; and passing through, he preached the gospel to all the cities, till he came to Caesarea. ^8-40
