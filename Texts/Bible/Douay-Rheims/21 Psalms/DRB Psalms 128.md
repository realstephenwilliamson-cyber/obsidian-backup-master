# Psalms 128 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 127|← Psalms 127]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 129|Psalms 129 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 128|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**128:1** Often have they fought against me from my youth, let Israel now say. ^128-1

**128:2** Often have they fought against me from my youth: but they could not prevail over me. ^128-2

**128:3** The wicked have wrought upon my back: they have lengthened their iniquity. ^128-3

**128:4** The Lord who is just will cut the necks of sinners: ^128-4

**128:5** Let them all be confounded and turned back that hate Sion. ^128-5

**128:6** Let them be as grass on the tops of houses: which withered before it be plucked up: ^128-6

**128:7** Wherewith the mower filleth not his hand: nor he that gathereth sheaves his bosom. ^128-7

**128:8** And they that have passed by have not said: The blessing of the Lord be upon you: we have blessed you in the name of the Lord. ^128-8
