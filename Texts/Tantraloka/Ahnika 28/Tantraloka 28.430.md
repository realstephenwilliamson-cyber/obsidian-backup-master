## Mūla

गुरुपूजामकुर्वाणः शतं जन्मानि जायते ।
अधिकारी ततो मुक्तिं यातीति स्कन्दयामले ॥ ^mula

gurupūjāmakurvāṇaḥ śataṃ janmāni jāyate |
adhikārī tato muktiṃ yātīti skandayāmale || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 07 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 5#^pg214|p. 197]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=214>))
>
> According to the Skandayāmala, he who does not worship (his) teacher is reborn a hundred times, competent (to practice), to then (finally) attain liberation. Thus (in order to avoid that), it is necessary to give the teacher a donation (dakṣinā) again. (with 28.430–431ab)
