## Mūla

सोमसूर्याग्निभासात्म रूपं समवतिष्ठते ।
एवं शब्दादिविषये श्रोत्रादिव्योमवर्त्मना ॥ ^mula

somasūryāgnibhāsātma rūpaṃ samavatiṣṭhate |
evaṃ śabdādiviṣaye śrotrādivyomavartmanā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 03 - Chapter Five, part 1#^pg46|p. 31]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=46>))
>
> (Reality in) the form (rūpa) (of consciousness) as the Moon, Sun, Fire and Illumination (bhāsā) is well established there in the object, in accord with the succession of emission, persistence and withdrawal, by the rays of that Wheel. (with 5.27cd–29ab)
>
> Whatever be the object of sense — sound and the rest – onto which (consciousness) falls, by means of this cycle (cakra), in this way, along the path of the emptiness of the sense of hearing and the other (senses), should be contemplated as one (with it). (with 5.29cd–30ab)
