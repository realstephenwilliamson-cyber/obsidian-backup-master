## Mūla

शिव एव तदभ्यासफलं न्यासादि कीर्तितम् ।
यथाहि दुष्टकर्मास्मीत्येवं भावयतस्तथा ॥ ^mula

śiva eva tadabhyāsaphalaṃ nyāsādi kīrtitam |
yathāhi duṣṭakarmāsmītyevaṃ bhāvayatastathā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 05 - Chapter Fifteen, part 4#^pg179|p. 161]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=179>))
>
> The Māyā subject becomes Śiva Himself by the intense exercise of the thought (vikalpa) that he is of that nature. The fruit of the depositions and the like is said to be that practice. (with 15.268cd–269ab)
>
> Just as a person who thinks intensely that he is a sinner becomes one, he who thinks that he is Śiva and is none other than him, becomes such. (with 15.269cd–270ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* ननु एवमभ्यासमात्रादेव कथं शिवत्वं भवेत्?--इत्याशङ्कां गर्भीकृत्य दृष्टान्तयति--

> यथाहि दुष्टकर्मास्मीत्येवं भावयतस्तथा ॥
> तथा शिवोऽहं नान्योऽस्मीत्येवं भावयतस्तथा ।

यथाहि दुष्टं कर्म करोमीत्येवं भावयतस्तथा दुष्टकर्मत्वं भवेत्, न तु सुकृतकर्मत्वं तथा "शिवोऽहमद्वितीयोऽहम्"इत्यादि भावयतस्तथा शिवत्वमेव--इत्यर्थः ॥
