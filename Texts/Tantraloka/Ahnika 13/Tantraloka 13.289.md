## Mūla

निर्मलश्चापि तु प्राप्तावच्छित्कर्माद्यपेक्षकः ।
यया यान्ति परां सिद्धिमित्यस्येदं तु जीवितम् ॥ ^mula

nirmalaścāpi tu prāptāvacchitkarmādyapekṣakaḥ |
yayā yānti parāṃ siddhimityasyedaṃ tu jīvitam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 09 - Chapter Thirteen, part 5#^pg324|p. 302]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=324>))
>
> Although the cause there (of their devotion) is indeed Śiva, it is not (Śiva Himself directly who is) aloof (from all bondage) and stainless. Rather, it is (Śiva) who, assuming a limited (divine form such as Viṣṇu), depends on Karma and the rest. (with 13.288cd–289ab)
>
> This is the essence (jīvita) of (the statement) ‘due to that . . . they attain the supreme accomplishment.’ (with 13.289cd)
