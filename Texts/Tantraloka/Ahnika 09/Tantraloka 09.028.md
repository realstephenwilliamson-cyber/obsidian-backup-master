## Mūla

ततो यावति याद्रूप्यान्नियमो बाधवर्जितः ।
भाति तावति ताद्रूप्याद् दृढहेतुफलात्मता ॥ ^mula

tato yāvati yādrūpyānniyamo bādhavarjitaḥ |
bhāti tāvati tādrūpyād dṛḍhahetuphalātmatā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 03 - Chapter Nine, part 1#^pg54|p. 37]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=54>))
>
> Thus, the relationship between cause and effect is firmly established in a particular form in whatever way and in whatever form a necessary, (and invariable relationship) (niyama) manifests which is not (subsequently) invalidated (bādha) (as a false causal relationship).
