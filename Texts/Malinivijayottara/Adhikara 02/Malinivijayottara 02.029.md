## Mūla

शक्तिशम्भू परिज्ञेयौ तुर्यातीते वरानने ।
त्रयोदशात्मके भेदे स्वरूपमकलावुभौ  ॥ ^mula

śaktiśambhū parijñeyau turyātīte varānane |
trayodaśātmake bhede svarūpam akalāv ubhau[^46] || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, October 2022 – December 2022
> p. 19 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=26>))
>
> (A) There (in that case), the triad consisting of (1) the Conditioned (perceivers), (their) energy and (their) innate nature constitutes the level of wakefulness in the fifteen-fold division. (2 and 3) The two Deconditioned (subjects) should be rightly known to be in the states of dreaming and deep (dreamless) sleep (respectively). (4) The category of Mantra (perceivers) and the rest, that is, their Lords and their Lords (i.e. the Lords of Mantras and the Great Lords of Mantra), are considered to be in the Fourth State. O fair-faced Lady, (5) Śakti and Śiva should be known to be in the state Beyond the Fourth. (with 2.27–29ab)

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, October 2022 – December 2022
> p. 20 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=27>))
>
> (B) When the division is thirteen-fold (1) (the waking state corresponds to) the innate nature. (2-5) Both the two Deconditioned (subjects),[^47] Mantras, Lords of Mantras, (their) Lords (the Great Lords Mantra) as well as Śakti and Śiva are as before. (with 2.29cd–31ab)

[^46]: MS G: *svarūpamakalāv*
[^47]: Read with MS G *svarūpam akalau* for *svarūpasakalau.*
