## Mūla

स्वायत्तत्वे तयोर्व्यक्तिपूगे किं स्यात्तयोर्गतिः ।
प्रत्यक्षमपि नेत्रात्मदीपार्थादिविशेषजम् ॥ ^mula

svāyattatve tayorvyaktipūge kiṃ syāttayorgatiḥ |
pratyakṣamapi netrātmadīpārthādiviśeṣajam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 09 - Chapter Thirty-Five#^pg209|p. 191]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=209>))
>
> (The ascertainment of) both positive and negative concomitance (anvaya – vyatireka) depends on (this) a priori belief (prasiddhi). If they were to be self-dependent, what would become of them in the midst of a mass of individual entities (vyakti)? (with 35.2cd–3ab)
>
> Direct perception also is the result of (an aggregate of) specific (factors. In the case of sight these) include the eye, the Self, light and the object (it illumines) etc. There, at (its very) root, it also depends on that (same) a priori belief the nature of which is such (as stated). (with 35.3cd–4ab)

*Lead-in:* न केवलमनुमाने एव प्रसिद्धिर्निबन्धनं यावत् प्रत्यक्षेऽपि--इत्याह--

> प्रत्यक्षमपि नेत्रात्मदीपार्थादिविशेषजम् ॥
> अपेक्षते तत्र मूले प्रसिद्धिं तां तथात्मिकाम् ।

इन्द्रियादिसामग्रीजन्यं प्रत्यक्षमपि तत्र इन्द्रियादिरूपे मूले तथात्मिकां ताद्रूप्यावमर्शमयीं तां सर्वव्यवहारनिबन्धनभूतां प्रसिद्धिमपेक्षते तां विना इन्द्रियादिप्रेरणाभावे न किञ्चित् सिध्येत्--इत्यर्थः ॥
