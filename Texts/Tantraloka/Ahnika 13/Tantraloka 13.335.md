## Mūla

आमोदार्थी यथा भृङ्गः पुष्पात्पुष्पान्तरं व्रजेत् ।
विज्ञानार्थी तथा शिष्यो गुरोर्गुर्वन्तरं व्रजेत् ॥ ^mula

āmodārthī yathā bhṛṅgaḥ puṣpātpuṣpāntaraṃ vrajet |
vijñānārthī tathā śiṣyo gurorgurvantaraṃ vrajet || ^iast

## Translation

> ‘Just as a bee desiring fine scent goes from flower to flower, so too, a (good) student who desires knowledge should go from teacher to teacher.’
