## Mūla

पाट्टसूत्रं तु कौशेयं कार्पासं क्षौममेव च ।
चातुराश्रमिकाणां तु सुभ्रुवा कर्तितोक्षितम् ॥ ^mula

pāṭṭasūtraṃ tu kauśeyaṃ kārpāsaṃ kṣaumameva ca |
cāturāśramikāṇāṃ tu subhruvā kartitokṣitam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 04 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 2#^pg93|p. 76]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=93>))
>
> The thread of cloth (pāffasūtra) for those who belong to the four grades (of initiates) should be made of silk (kauśeya), cotton and flax, cut (kartita) and sprinkled (and coloured) (uksita)) by (an attractive woman) with beautiful eyebrows.
