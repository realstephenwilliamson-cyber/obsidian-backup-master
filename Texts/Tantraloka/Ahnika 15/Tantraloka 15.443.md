## Mūla

भोग्यस्य परमं सारं भोग्यं नर्नर्ति यत्नतः ।
सममेकानुसन्धानात्पाततो भोक्तृभोग्ययोः ॥ ^mula

bhogyasya paramaṃ sāraṃ bhogyaṃ narnarti yatnataḥ |
samamekānusandhānātpātato bhoktṛbhogyayoḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 08 - Chapter Fifteen, part 7#^pg291|p. 273]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=291>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 15.445cd–446ab.
>
> This is the (empowering) purification that comes about by the oblation (which is the ‘falling together’ of the clarified butter into the fire and onto the sacrificial pap) (sarnpātasahskāra) He who eats the sacrificial pap (i.e., the subject) is the (fundamental) sustaining state (of the rite) (adhiṣṭhiti), and so is the supreme essence of (what is) eaten (i.e., the object). (It is due to him, thus inwardly sustained and sustaining, that) the eaten (i.e., objectivity) (vibrates, as if) dancing intensely (narnarti) without any effort (at all). (with 15.442cd–443ab)
>
> (This sacrament is called an ‘equal fall’,) because the eater (i.e., the perceiver) and the eaten (i.e., the perceived) fall (pāta) (i.e., are experienced simultaneously) together, due to the single (synthetic) contemplation (ekānusandhāna) (of both of them) equally (samam) (together). Again, it is called this because by mutually falling (that is, combining) together they come together. (with 15.443cd–444ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* संपातशब्दस्य अन्वर्थतां दर्शयति--

> सममेकानुसन्धानात्पाततो भोक्तृभोग्ययोः ॥
> अन्योऽन्यत्र च संपातात्सङ्गमाच्चेत्थमुच्यते ।

अत्र तुल्यकालार्थत्वं च अवयवार्थः, सङ्गमार्थत्वं तु समुदायार्थः ॥
