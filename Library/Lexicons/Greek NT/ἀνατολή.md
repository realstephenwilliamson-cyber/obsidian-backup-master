# ἀνατολή

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · noun

**Dodson:** rising of the sun, the East — (a) rising of the sun, hence (b) (sing. and plur.) the quarter whence the sun rises, the East. (Strong's 395; headword ἀνατολή, ῆς, ἡ)
