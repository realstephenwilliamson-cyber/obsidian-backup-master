## Mūla

ज्ञानयोग्यास्तथा केचिच्चर्यायोग्यास्तथापरे ।
श्रीमन्नन्दिशिखातन्त्रे वितत्यैतन्निरूपितम् ॥ ^mula

jñānayogyāstathā keciccaryāyogyāstathāpare |
śrīmannandiśikhātantre vitatyaitannirūpitam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 07 - Chapter Thirteen, part 3#^pg245|p. 223]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=245>))
>
> It has been said in this same way in the Kiraṇā (that knowledge is acquired) from the teacher, scripture and oneself. So also, some are suited (to seek) knowledge, whereas some to (the practice of) religious observance (caryā). (with 13.162cd–163ab)
>
> This has been explained at length in the venerable Nandiśsikhātantra by means of (a series of) questions and answers, which will (now) be stated in full. (with 13.163cd–164ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* ननु इदं सिद्धसाधनं यद्गुरुशास्त्रापेक्षं ज्ञानमुदियादिति । एतद्धि लोकत एव सिद्धम्, किमत्र प्रमाणोपन्यासेन । स्वत एव केषाञ्चिदेतदुदेति इत्यत्र पुनः किं प्रमाणम्? -- इत्याशङ्क्याह --

> श्रीमन्नन्दिशिखातन्त्रे वितत्यैतन्निरूपितम् ॥
> प्रश्नोत्तरमुखेनेति तदभग्नं निरूप्यते ।

अभग्नमिति अखण्डम् ॥ १६३ ॥
