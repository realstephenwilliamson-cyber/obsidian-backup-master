## Chapter 16

**16:1** [^1]They brought in the Ark of God and set it up inside the tent that David had pitched for it, and they sacrificed burnt offerings and offerings of well-being before God. ^16-1

**16:2** When David finished sacrificing the burnt offerings and the offerings of well-being, he blessed the people in the name of the LORD. ^16-2

**16:3** And he distributed to every person in Israel—man and woman alike—to each a loaf of bread, a cake made in a pan, and a raisin cake.[^2] ^16-3

**16:4** He appointed Levites to minister before the Ark of the LORD, to invoke, to praise, and to extol the LORD God of Israel: ^16-4

**16:5** Asaph the chief, Zechariah second in rank, Jeiel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Mattithiah, Eliab, Benaiah, Obed-edom, and Jeiel, with harps and lyres, and Asaph sounding the cymbals, ^16-5

**16:6** and Benaiah and Jahaziel the priests, with trumpets, regularly before the Ark of the Covenant of God. ^16-6

**16:7** Then, on that day, David first commissioned Asaph and his kinsmen to give praise to the LORD: ^16-7

**16:8** [^3]“Praise the LORD;  
call on His name;  
proclaim His deeds among the peoples. ^16-8

**16:9** Sing praises unto Him;  
speak of all His wondrous acts. ^16-9

**16:10** Exult in His holy name;  
let all who seek the LORD rejoice. ^16-10

**16:11** Turn to the LORD, to His might;[^4]  
seek His presence constantly. ^16-11

**16:12** Remember the wonders He has done;  
His portents and the judgments He has pronounced, ^16-12

**16:13** O offspring of Israel, His servant,  
O descendants of Jacob, His chosen ones. ^16-13

**16:14** He is the LORD our God;  
His judgments are throughout the earth. ^16-14

**16:15** Be ever mindful of His covenant,  
the promise He gave for a thousand generations, ^16-15

**16:16** that He made with Abraham,  
swore to Isaac, ^16-16

**16:17** and confirmed in a decree for Jacob,  
for Israel, as an eternal covenant, ^16-17

**16:18** saying, ‘To you I will give the land of Canaan  
as your allotted heritage.’ ^16-18

**16:19** You were then few in number,  
a handful, merely sojourning there, ^16-19

**16:20** wandering from nation to nation,  
from one kingdom to another. ^16-20

**16:21** He allowed no one to oppress them;  
He reproved kings on their account, ^16-21

**16:22** ‘Do not touch My anointed ones;  
do not harm My prophets.’ ^16-22

**16:23** [^5]“Sing to the LORD, all the earth.  
proclaim His victory day after day. ^16-23

**16:24** Tell of His glory among the nations,  
His wondrous deeds among all peoples. ^16-24

**16:25** For the LORD is great and much acclaimed,  
He is held in awe by all divine beings. ^16-25

**16:26** All the gods of the peoples are mere idols,  
but the LORD made the heavens. ^16-26

**16:27** Glory and majesty are before Him;  
strength and joy are in His place. ^16-27

**16:28** “Ascribe to the LORD, O families of the peoples,  
ascribe to the LORD glory and strength. ^16-28

**16:29** Ascribe to the LORD the glory of His name,  
bring tribute and enter before Him,  
bow down to the LORD majestic in holiness. ^16-29

**16:30** Tremble in His presence, all the earth!  
The world stands firm; it cannot be shaken. ^16-30

**16:31** Let the heavens rejoice and the earth exult;  
let them declare among the nations, “The LORD is King!” ^16-31

**16:32** Let the sea and all within it thunder,  
the fields and everything in them exult; ^16-32

**16:33** then shall all the trees of the forest shout for joy  
at the presence of the LORD,  
for He is coming to rule the earth. ^16-33

**16:34** Praise the LORD for He is good;  
His steadfast love is eternal. ^16-34

**16:35** [^6]Declare:  
Deliver us, O God, our deliverer,  
and gather us and save us from the nations,  
to acclaim Your holy name,  
to glory in Your praise. ^16-35

**16:36** Blessed is the LORD, God of Israel, from eternity to eternity.” And all the people said, “Amen” and “Praise the LORD.” ^16-36

**16:37** He left Asaph and his kinsmen there before the Ark of the Covenant of the LORD to minister before the Ark regularly as each day required, ^16-37

**16:38** as well as Obed-edom with their kinsmen—68; also Obed-edom son of Jedithun and Hosah as gatekeepers; ^16-38

**16:39** also Zadok the priest and his fellow priests before the Tabernacle of the LORD at the shrine which was in Gibeon; ^16-39

**16:40** to sacrifice burnt offerings to the LORD on the altar of the burnt offering regularly, morning and evening, in accordance with what was prescribed in the Teaching of the LORD with which He charged Israel. ^16-40

**16:41** With them were Heman and Jeduthun and the other selected men designated by name to give praise to the LORD, “For His steadfast love is eternal.” ^16-41

**16:42** Heman and Jeduthun had with them trumpets and cymbals to sound, and instruments for the songs of God; and the sons of Jeduthun were to be at the gate. ^16-42

**16:43** Then all the people went every one to his home, and David returned to greet his household. ^16-43

[^1]: With vv. 1–3, cf. 2 Sam. 6.17–19.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: With vv. 8–22, cf. Ps. 105.1–15.
[^4]: I.e., the Ark; cf. Pss. 78.61; 132.8.
[^5]: With vv. 23–33, cf. Ps. 96.1–13.
[^6]: Cf. Ps. 106.47–48.
