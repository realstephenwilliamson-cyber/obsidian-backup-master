## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 09 - Chapter Six, part 2#^pg302|p. 287]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=302>))
>
> The results (of practice in) the eight praharas are 1) minor accomplishment (siddhi), 2) liberation, 3) magic, 4) (perfection) in the next world, and 5) in this world, 6) extremely distant and 7) proximate excellence. (with 6.67cd–68ab)
>
> Abhijit bestows liberation and worldly benefits at midday and midnight. (with 6.68cd)
