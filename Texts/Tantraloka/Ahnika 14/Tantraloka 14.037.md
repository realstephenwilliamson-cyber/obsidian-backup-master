## Mūla

श्रीपूर्वशास्त्रे तूक्तं यदुत्क्रान्तेर्लक्षणं न तत् ।
मुक्त्युपायतया किन्तु भोगहान्यै तथैषणात् ॥ ^mula

śrīpūrvaśāstre tūktaṃ yadutkrānterlakṣaṇaṃ na tat |
muktyupāyatayā kintu bhogahānyai tathaiṣaṇāt || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 12 - Chapter Fourteen, and Appendices to Chapters Eleven and Twelve#^pg391|p. 369]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=391>))
>
> The definition stated in the Mālinīvijayottara of (the voluntary) exit (from the body) is not as a means to liberation rather, (it is understood to be) motivated by the desire to avoid (further painful) worldly experience (bhoga) in that way.
