## Mūla

षड्वधः स्ववपुःशुद्धौ शुद्धिं सोऽध्वाधिगच्छति ।
एकेन वपुषा शुद्धौ तत्रैवान्यप्रकारताम् ॥ ^mula

ṣaḍvadhaḥ svavapuḥśuddhau śuddhiṃ so'dhvādhigacchati |
ekena vapuṣā śuddhau tatraivānyaprakāratām || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 03 - Chapter Eleven, part 2#^pg94|p. 74]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=94>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 11.83cd–84ab.
>
> This six-fold Path manifests in this way as noetic consciousness, subject, object and means of knowledge. It is (all) purified if (any one) of its own bodies is purified. (with 11.82cd–83ab)
>
> The teacher (who) contemplates (and unites the Path) (anusaṁdhānavat) should purify (the entire Path of his disciple) by purifying (just) the body (of just) one (of them), in such a way that the others are also included within it. (with 11.83cd–84ab)
