# περισσεύω

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · verb

**Dodson:** I exceed, am left over — (a) intrans: I exceed the ordinary (the necessary), abound, overflow; am left over, (b) trans: I cause to abound. (Strong's 4052; headword περισσεύω)
