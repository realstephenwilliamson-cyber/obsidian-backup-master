## Mūla

भूयोभूयः समावेशं निर्विकल्पमिमं श्रितः ।
अभ्येति भैरवीभावं जीवन्मुक्त्यपराभिधम् ॥ ^mula

bhūyobhūyaḥ samāveśaṃ nirvikalpamimaṃ śritaḥ |
abhyeti bhairavībhāvaṃ jīvanmuktyaparābhidham || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. II
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 02 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v02 12 - Chapter Three, part 9#^pg411|p. 395]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 02.pdf#page=411>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 3.271cd–272ab.
>
> He who enters repeatedly into this state of penetration (samāveśa) (into pure Bhairava consciousness) free of thought constructs, attains Bhairava’s state (bhairavībhāva), otherwise called liberation in this life.
