## Mūla

स्थित्वा मन्त्रादि संगृह्य त्यजेत्सोऽस्य तिरोभवः ।
श्रीसारशास्त्रे भगवान्वस्त्वेतत्समभाषत ॥ ^mula

sthitvā mantrādi saṃgṛhya tyajetso'sya tirobhavaḥ |
śrīsāraśāstre bhagavānvastvetatsamabhāṣata || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII

> (The consciousness of) one who behaves as if he were enlightened, although he is not, and having taken up (the practice of reciting) Mantras and the like, gives it up, is in a state of obscuration (tirobhāva). (with 13.120cd–121ab)
