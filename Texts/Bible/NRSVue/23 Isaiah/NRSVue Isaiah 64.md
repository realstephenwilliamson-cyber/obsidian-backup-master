# Isaiah 64 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Isaiah 63|← Isaiah 63]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Isaiah 65|Isaiah 65 →]]

**64:1** O that you would tear open the heavens and come down, so that the mountains would quake at your presence— ^64-1

**64:2** ^[64.1 in Heb]as when fire kindles brushwood and the fire causes water to boil—to make your name known to your adversaries, so that the nations might tremble at your presence! ^64-2

**64:3** When you did awesome deeds that we did not expect, you came down; the mountains quaked at your presence. ^64-3

**64:4** From ages past no one has heard, no ear has perceived, no eye has seen any God besides you, who works for those who wait for him. ^64-4

**64:5** You meet those who gladly do right, those who remember you in your ways. But you were angry, and we sinned; because you hid yourself we transgressed.^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^64-5

**64:6** We have all become like one who is unclean, and all our righteous deeds are like a filthy cloth. We all fade like a leaf, and our iniquities, like the wind, take us away. ^64-6

**64:7** There is no one who calls on your name or attempts to take hold of you, for you have hidden your face from us and have delivered^[Gk Syr OL Tg: Heb *melted*] us into the hand of our iniquity. ^64-7

**64:8** Yet, O LORD, you are our Father; we are the clay, and you are our potter; we are all the work of your hand. ^64-8

**64:9** Do not be exceedingly angry, O LORD, and do not remember iniquity forever. Now consider, we are all your people. ^64-9

**64:10** Your holy cities have become a wilderness; Zion has become a wilderness, Jerusalem a desolation. ^64-10

**64:11** Our holy and beautiful house, where our ancestors praised you, has been burned by fire, and all our pleasant places have become ruins. ^64-11

**64:12** After all this, will you restrain yourself, O LORD? Will you keep silent and punish us so severely? ^64-12
