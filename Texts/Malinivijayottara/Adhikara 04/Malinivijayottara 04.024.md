## Mūla

सार्धेनाण्डद्वयं व्याप्तमेकैकेन पृथग्द्वयम् ।
अपरायाः समाख्याता व्याप्तिरेषा विलोमतः ॥ ^mula

sārdhenāṇḍadvayaṁ vyāptam ekaikena pṛthag dvayam |
aparāyāḥ samākhyātā vyāptir eṣā vilomataḥ || ^iast

> **Aparā**
>
> Two Eggs (that extend up to Nature) are pervaded by the syllable and a half (PHAṬ) and the two others by the two syllables (HŪṀ and HrĪḤ). Such, in reverse, is (the extent of) the pervasion of Aparā.
