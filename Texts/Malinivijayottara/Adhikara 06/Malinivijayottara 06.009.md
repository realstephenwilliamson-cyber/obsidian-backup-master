> **2) The Deposition of the Five Ele- ments onto the Body**
>
> How the five principles are (distributed) in the body will now be explained. (The body) is pervaded by the Earth principle up to the ankles (for a space of four fingers), the rest, in due order, by Water up to a space of six fingers above the navel. Twenty-two units above that are covered by Fire.[^14] The pervasion of Air is said to (extend for) twelve units from that. Recollect the end of Space which is the supreme, tranquil (reality) that pervades (them) all.[^15] (with 6.6cd–9ab)

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, October 2023 – December 2023
> p. 19 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapter 6 (2023).pdf#page=7>))
>
> The same (method) is recommended in the case of those procedures that concern the division of the Path into five powers and other (such pentads also).[^16] (with 6.9cd)

[^14]: Read with the MV *tejasāvṛtam* for *tejasā vṛtam.*

[^15]: The deposition of five principles: Earth takes up four fingers, Water forty-six, Fire twenty-two, Air twelve, and Space, like Śiva, is pervasive. Thus, 4 + 46 + 22 + 12 = 84 finger breadths. Other depsitions of Cf. TĀ 16/109-110ab (109-110ab): (Taking this deposition as his model), the (teachers) can (easily) figure out by his own intelligence the depositions of nine, five, four or (just) one principle, by bearing in mind that the rest of the principles are included within (each one). The deposition of the five Forces (*kalā*) and the four Eggs (*aṇḍa*) is characterized by this (same procedure as that of the five and of the four principles, respectively).' The deposition of five principles is taught here in MV 6/6cd-9ab and that of three in MV 6/10. Jayaratha explains in his commentary that the other two are as follows: 'The deposition of nine principles: Earth to Nature take up fifty fingers, the individual soul three, Necessity (*niyati*) nine, time (*kāla*) six, Māyā, Śuddhavidyā, Īśvara and Sadāśiva four fingers each.' This makes 50 + 3 + 9 + 6 + 16 (4 x 4) = 84 fongr breadths. The deposition of the four principles: These are Earth, Nature, Māyā and Power (*śakti*), which correspond to the four Eggs (*aṇḍa*) (see TĀ 11/8 ff.). They measure 4, 46, 22 and 12 fingers respectively, making 84 finger breadths altogether.

[^16]: A noteworthy example is the projection of the Five Elements taught in the Kālottaras that equate them with the five Forces (kalā).
