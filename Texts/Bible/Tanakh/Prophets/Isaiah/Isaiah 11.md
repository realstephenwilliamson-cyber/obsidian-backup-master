## Chapter 11

**11:1** But a shoot shall grow out of the stump of Jesse,  
A twig shall sprout from his stock. ^11-1

**11:2** The spirit of the LORD shall alight upon him:  
A spirit of wisdom and insight,  
A spirit of counsel and valor,  
A spirit of devotion and reverence for the LORD. ^11-2

**11:3** He shall sense the truth[^1] by his reverence for the LORD:  
He shall not judge by what his eyes behold,  
Nor decide by what his ears perceive. ^11-3

**11:4** Thus he shall judge the poor with equity  
And decide with justice for the lowly of the land.  
He shall strike down a land[^2] with the rod of his mouth  
And slay the wicked with the breath of his lips. ^11-4

**11:5** Justice shall be the girdle of his loins,  
And faithfulness the girdle of his waist. ^11-5

**11:6** The wolf shall dwell with the lamb,  
The leopard lie down with the kid;  
The calf, the beast of prey, and the fatling[^3] together,  
With a little boy to herd them. ^11-6

**11:7** The cow and the bear shall graze,  
Their young shall lie down together;  
And the lion, like the ox, shall eat straw. ^11-7

**11:8** A babe shall play  
Over a viper’s hole,  
And an infant pass[^4] his hand  
Over an adder’s den. ^11-8

**11:9** In all of My sacred mount[^5]  
Nothing evil or vile shall be done;  
For the land shall be filled with devotion to the LORD  
As water covers the sea. ^11-9

**11:10** In that day,  
The stock of Jesse that has remained standing  
Shall become a standard to peoples—  
Nations shall seek his counsel  
And his abode shall be honored. ^11-10

**11:11** In that day, my Lord will apply His hand again to redeeming the other part[^6] of His people from Assyria—as also from Egypt, Pathros, Nubia, Elam, Shinar, Hamath, and the coastlands. ^11-11

**11:12** He will hold up a signal to the nations  
And assemble the banished of Israel,  
And gather the dispersed of Judah  
From the four corners of the earth. ^11-12

**11:13** Then Ephraim’s envy shall cease  
And Judah’s harassment shall end;  
Ephraim shall not envy Judah,  
And Judah shall not harass Ephraim. ^11-13

**11:14** They shall pounce on the back of Philistia to the west,  
And together plunder the peoples of the east;  
Edom and Moab shall be subject to them  
And the children of Ammon shall obey them. ^11-14

**11:15** The LORD will dry up the tongue of the Egyptian sea.—He will raise His hand over the Euphrates with the might[^7] of His wind and break it into seven wadis, so that it can be trodden dry-shod. ^11-15

**11:16** Thus there shall be a highway for the other part[^8] of His people out of Assyria, such as there was for Israel when it left the land of Egypt. ^11-16

[^1]: Lit. “His sensing [shall be]”; meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^2]: Emendation yields “the ruthless.”
[^3]: 1QIsa reads: “The calf and the beast of prey shall feed”; so too the Septuagint.
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^5]: I.e., the Holy Land; cf. Exod. 15.17; Ps. 78.54.
[^6]: I.e., the part outside the Holy Land; lit. “the rest that will remain.”
[^7]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^8]: I.e., the part outside the Holy Land; lit. “the rest that will remain.”
