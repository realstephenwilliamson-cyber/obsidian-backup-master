# Ezekiel 29

## Chapter 29

**29:1** In the tenth year, on the twelfth day of the tenth month, the word of the LORD came to me: ^29-1

**29:2** O mortal, turn your face against Pharaoh king of Egypt, and prophesy against him and against all Egypt. ^29-2

**29:3** Speak these words:  
Thus said the Lord GOD:  
I am going to deal with you, O Pharaoh king of Egypt,  
Mighty monster, sprawling in your[^1] channels,  
Who said,  
My Nile is my own;  
I made it for myself. ^29-3

**29:4** I will put hooks in your jaws,  
And make the fish of your channels  
Cling to your scales;  
I will haul you up from your channels,  
With all the fish of your channels  
Clinging to your scales. ^29-4

**29:5** And I will fling you into the desert,  
With all the fish of your channels.  
You shall be left lying in the open,  
Ungathered and unburied:  
I have given you as food  
To the beasts of the earth  
And the birds of the sky. ^29-5

**29:6** Then all the inhabitants of Egypt shall know  
That I am the LORD.  
Because you[^2] were a staff of reed  
To the House of Israel: ^29-6

**29:7** When they grasped you with the hand, you would splinter,  
And wound all their shoulders,[^3]  
And when they leaned on you, you would break,  
And make all their loins unsteady.[^4] ^29-7

**29:8** Assuredly, thus said the Lord GOD: Lo, I will bring a sword against you, and will cut off man and beast from you, ^29-8

**29:9** so that the land of Egypt shall fall into desolation and ruin. And they shall know that I am the LORD—because he boasted, “The Nile is mine, and I made it.” ^29-9

**29:10** Assuredly, I am going to deal with you and your channels, and I will reduce the land of Egypt to utter ruin and desolation, from Migdol to Syene, all the way to the border of Nubia.[^5] ^29-10

**29:11** No foot of man shall traverse it, and no foot of beast shall traverse it; and it shall remain uninhabited for forty years. ^29-11

**29:12** For forty years I will make the land of Egypt the most desolate of desolate lands, and its cities shall be the most desolate of ruined cities. And I will scatter the Egyptians among the nations and disperse them throughout the countries. ^29-12

**29:13** Further, thus said the Lord GOD: After a period of forty years I will gather the Egyptians from the peoples among whom they were dispersed. ^29-13

**29:14** I will restore the fortunes of the Egyptians and bring them back to the land of their origin, the land of Pathros,[^6] and there they shall be a lowly kingdom. ^29-14

**29:15** It shall be the lowliest of all the kingdoms, and shall not lord it over the nations again. I will reduce the Egyptians,[^7] so that they shall have no dominion over the nations. ^29-15

**29:16** Never again shall they be the trust of the House of Israel, recalling its guilt in having turned to them. And they shall know that I am the Lord GOD. ^29-16

**29:17** In the twenty-seventh year, on the first day of the first month, the word of the LORD came to me: ^29-17

**29:18** O mortal, King Nebuchadrezzar of Babylon has made his army expend vast labor on Tyre; every head is rubbed bald and every shoulder scraped. But he and his army have had no return for the labor he expended on Tyre. ^29-18

**29:19** Assuredly, thus said the Lord GOD: I will give the land of Egypt to Nebuchadrezzar, king of Babylon. He shall carry off her wealth and take her spoil and seize her booty; and she shall be the recompense of his army. ^29-19

**29:20** As the wage for which he labored, for what they did for Me, I give him the land of Egypt—declares the Lord GOD. ^29-20

**29:21** On that day I will endow the House of Israel with strength, and you shall be vindicated[^8] among them. And they shall know that I am the LORD. ^29-21

[^1]: Lit. “its.”
[^2]: Lit. “they.”
[^3]: Septuagint and Syriac read “palms”; cf. 2 Kings 18.21; Isa. 36.6.
[^4]: Taking ‘amad as a byform of ma‘ad; cf. Syriac translation.
[^5]: I.e., the length of Egypt, from north to south. Syene is modern Aswan.
[^6]: I.e., southern Egypt.
[^7]: Heb. “them.”
[^8]: Lit. “cause a horn to sprout for the House of Israel, and I will grant you opening of the mouth.”
