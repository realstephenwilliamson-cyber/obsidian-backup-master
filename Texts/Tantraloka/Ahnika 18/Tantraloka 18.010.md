## Mūla

अतन्मयीभूतमिति विक्षिप्तं कर्म सन्दधत् ।
क्रमात्तादात्म्यमेतीति विक्षिप्तं विधिमाचरेत् ॥ ^mula

atanmayībhūtamiti vikṣiptaṃ karma sandadhat |
kramāttādātmyametīti vikṣiptaṃ vidhimācaret || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 09 - Chapter Eighteen#^pg217|p. 197]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=217>))
>
> If (the disciple) is not identified (with iva), (the teacher) should apply the brief rite (of initiation). (Thus,) he gradually attains a state of identification. Accordingly, he should perform (this) brief procedure (vidhi).
