# Psalm 62

← [[BCP Psalm 061]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 063]] →

*Nonne Deo?* · Book Two · Twelfth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 062]]

**62:1** For God alone my soul in silence waits; \*  
from him comes my salvation. ^62-1

**62:2** He alone is my rock and my salvation, \*  
my stronghold, so that I shall not be greatly shaken. ^62-2

**62:3** How long will you assail me to crush me,  
all of you together, \*  
as if you were a leaning fence, a toppling wall? ^62-3

**62:4** They seek only to bring me down from my place of honor; \*  
lies are their chief delight. ^62-4

**62:5** They bless with their lips, \*  
but in their hearts they curse. ^62-5

**62:6** For God alone my soul in silence waits; \*  
truly, my hope is in him. ^62-6

**62:7** He alone is my rock and my salvation, \*  
my stronghold, so that I shall not be shaken. ^62-7

**62:8** In God is my safety and my honor; \*  
God is my strong rock and my refuge. ^62-8

**62:9** Put your trust in him always, O people, \*  
pour out your hearts before him, for God is our refuge. ^62-9

[p. 670]

**62:10** Those of high degree are but a fleeting breath, \*  
even those of low estate cannot be trusted. ^62-10

**62:11** On the scales they are lighter than a breath, \*  
all of them together. ^62-11

**62:12** Put no trust in extortion;  
in robbery take no empty pride; \*  
though wealth increase, set not your heart upon it. ^62-12

**62:13** God has spoken once, twice have I heard it, \*  
that power belongs to God. ^62-13

**62:14** Steadfast love is yours, O Lord, \*  
for you repay everyone according to his deeds. ^62-14
