# Isaiah 47 (NRSVue)

## The humiliation of Babylon

**47:1** Come down and sit in the dust, virgin daughter Babylon! Sit on the ground without a throne, daughter Chaldea! For you shall no more be called tender and delicate. ^47-1

**47:2** Take the millstones and grind meal; remove your veil; strip off your robe; uncover your legs; pass through the rivers. ^47-2

**47:3** Your nakedness shall be uncovered, and your shame shall be seen. I will take vengeance, and I will spare no one.^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^47-3

**47:4** Our Redeemer—the LORD of hosts is his name—is the Holy One of Israel. ^47-4

**47:5** Sit in silence, and go into darkness, daughter Chaldea! For you shall no more be called the mistress of kingdoms. ^47-5

**47:6** I was angry with my people; I profaned my heritage; I gave them into your hand; you showed them no mercy; on the aged you made your yoke exceedingly heavy. ^47-6

**47:7** You said, “I shall be mistress forever,” so that you did not lay these things to heart or remember their end. ^47-7

**47:8** Now therefore hear this, you lover of pleasures, who sit securely, who say in your heart, “I am, and there is no one besides me; I shall not sit as a widow or know the loss of children”— ^47-8

**47:9** both these things shall come upon you in a moment, in one day: the loss of children and widowhood shall come upon you in full measure, in spite of your many sorceries and the great power of your enchantments. ^47-9

**47:10** You felt secure in your wickedness; you said, “No one sees me.” Your wisdom and your knowledge led you astray, and you said in your heart, “I am, and there is no one besides me.” ^47-10

**47:11** But evil shall come upon you, which you cannot charm away; disaster shall fall upon you, which you will not be able to ward off, and ruin shall come on you suddenly, of which you know nothing. ^47-11

**47:12** Stand fast in your enchantments and your many sorceries, with which you have labored from your youth; perhaps you may be able to succeed; perhaps you may inspire terror. ^47-12

**47:13** You are wearied with your many consultations; let those who study^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] the heavens stand up and save you, those who gaze at the stars and at each new moon predict what^[Gk Syr Compare Vg: Heb *from what*] shall befall you. ^47-13

**47:14** See, they are like stubble; the fire consumes them; they cannot deliver themselves from the power of the flame. No coal for warming oneself is this, no fire to sit before! ^47-14

**47:15** Such to you are those with whom you have labored, who have trafficked with you from your youth; they all wander about in their own paths; there is no one to save you. ^47-15
