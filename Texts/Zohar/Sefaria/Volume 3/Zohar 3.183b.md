# Zohar 3.183b

← [[Zohar 3.183a]] · [[Zohar (Soncino)]] · [[Zohar 3.184a]] →

Pritzker translation (Matt): [[Zohar 9.07 - Parashat Huqqat#^z3-183b|Parashat Ḥuqqat, at 3:183b]]

*Volume 3, folio 183b* · Chukat

**Chukat 10:71** [^1]AND THE PEOPLE SPAKE AGAINST GOD AND AGAINST MOSES. That is to say, they spoke against God and wrangled with Moses. WHEREFORE HAVE YE BROUGHT US UP: they put all faces [i.e. God and Moses] on the same level. Therefore there were sent among them serpents that burnt them like fire, and the fire entered their bodies and they fell dead. ^chukat-10-71

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Chukat 10:72.*

**Chukat 11:73** THAT IS THE WELL WHEREOF THE LORD SAID UNTO MOSES, GATHER THE PEOPLE, ETC. This well never left them. ^chukat-11-73

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Chukat 11:74.*

**Chukat 11:75** It may be asked, How could they all draw from it? It issued into twelve streams and a channel went forth in every direction, and when Israel encamped and required water they used to stand by it and recite this song: “Ascend, O well, bring up thy waters to provide water for all, so that they may be watered from thee”. ^chukat-11-75

*Not translated in the Soncino edition: Chukat 11:76.*

**Chukat 11:77** So, too, they sang the praises of the well: “The well which the princes digged”, etc. All that they said in its praise was true, ^chukat-11-77

**Chukat 11:78** All people go to synagogue to influence the powers above, but few know how to do it. God is near to all who know how to call upon Him and to set powers in motion in the proper manner, but if they do not know how to call upon Him He is not near. So here, the Israelites spoke words of truth so as to set in motion[^2] ^chukat-11-78

**Chukat 11:79** this well, and before they did this it would not move. So also with magicians who get the “evil species” to serve them, if they do not employ the right formulas to draw them, they do not stir. ^chukat-11-79

[^1]: The first 11 lines of the Hebrew text do not appear in our translation.
[^2]: The translation of this page does not conform entirely to the Hebrew text.
