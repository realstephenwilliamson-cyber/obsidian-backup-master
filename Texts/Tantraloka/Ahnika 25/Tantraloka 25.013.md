## Mūla

पुत्रकोऽपि यदा कस्मैचन स्यादुपकारकः ।
तदा मातुः पितुः शक्तेर्वामदक्षान्तरालगाः ॥ ^mula

putrako'pi yadā kasmaicana syādupakārakaḥ |
tadā mātuḥ pituḥ śaktervāmadakṣāntarālagāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 17 - Chapter Twenty-Five (continued) and Chapter Twenty-Six#^pg381|p. 361]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=381>))
>
> If a spiritual son is also to help someone (who has died), he should make the channels (of the vital breath) on the left, right and middle, of (his) mother, father and consort (śakti) (respectively), flow (by blowing into them), and offer (the food) presented (niveditam) (to them), to the deity (deva). (with 25.13–14ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* अत्रैव विशेषमाह--

> पुत्रकोऽपि यदा कस्मैचन स्यादुपकारकः ।
> तदा मातुः पितुः शक्तेर्वामदक्षान्तरालगाः ॥
> नाडीः प्रवाहयेद्देवायार्पयेत निवेदितम् ।

अपिशब्दात् न केवलं गुरुः साधको वा । मातुः पितुरिति-- गुरोस्तत्पत्न्या अपि ॥
