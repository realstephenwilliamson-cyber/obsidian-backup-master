## Chapter 72

**72:1** Of Solomon.  
O God, endow the king with Your judgments,  
the king’s son with Your righteousness; ^72-1

**72:2** that he may judge Your people rightly,  
Your lowly ones, justly. ^72-2

**72:3** Let the mountains produce well-being for the people,  
the hills, the reward of justice. ^72-3

**72:4** Let him champion the lowly among the people,  
deliver the needy folk,  
and crush those who wrong them. ^72-4

**72:5** Let them fear You as long as the sun shines,  
while the moon lasts, generations on end. ^72-5

**72:6** Let him be like rain that falls on a mown field,  
like a downpour of rain on the ground, ^72-6

**72:7** that the righteous may flourish in his time,  
and well-being abound, till the moon is no more. ^72-7

**72:8** Let him rule from sea to sea,  
from the river to the ends of the earth. ^72-8

**72:9** Let desert-dwellers kneel before him,  
and his enemies lick the dust. ^72-9

**72:10** Let kings of Tarshish and the islands pay tribute,  
kings of Sheba and Seba offer gifts. ^72-10

**72:11** Let all kings bow to him,  
and all nations serve him. ^72-11

**72:12** For he saves the needy who cry out,  
the lowly who have no helper. ^72-12

**72:13** He cares about the poor and the needy;  
He brings the needy deliverance. ^72-13

**72:14** He redeems them from fraud and lawlessness;  
the shedding of their blood weighs heavily upon him.[^1] ^72-14

**72:15** So let him live, and receive gold of Sheba;  
let prayers for him be said always,  
blessings on him invoked at all times. ^72-15

**72:16** [^2]Let abundant grain be in the land, to the tops of the mountains;  
let his crops thrive like the forest of Lebanon;  
and let men sprout up in towns like country grass. ^72-16

**72:17** May his name be eternal;  
while the sun lasts, may his name endure;  
let men invoke his blessedness upon themselves;  
let all nations count him happy. ^72-17

**72:18** Blessed is the LORD God, God of Israel,  
who alone does wondrous things; ^72-18

**72:19** Blessed is His glorious name forever;  
His glory fills the whole world.  
Amen and Amen. ^72-19

**72:20** End of the prayers of David son of Jesse. ^72-20

[^1]: Or “their life is precious in his sight.”
[^2]: Meaning of some Heb. phrases in these verses uncertain.
