# D31c, Finn's leftover notes (18-19 Oct 2026)

The 15 Oct run (`../finn/`) left 266 notes on 152 pages as numbered paragraphs (`N\. text`), because the place of their call could not be found in the PDF's text layer. `leftovers.py` lists them (notes in Courier-Oblique with a margin number in the layer, no definition on the page, a `N\.` paragraph in the Markdown); `bands.py` renders the pages; five subagents read, for each, the words before the raised call and the note's first and last words (`readings/`). `link.py` turns a note into a footnote only if: the reading is certain and the note does not cross a page; its paragraph starts with `N\.` once on the page and its first words match; its last words are found before the next numbered paragraph (the definition ends there); the call's words are found once in the text above, the word carries no footnote already, and the Markdown word differs from the printed one only by digits or junk (letters and quote marks are never removed). A footnote's edits go in all together or not at all.

Result: 201 footnotes (1,100 in the book). Not converted 65: Markdown text did not match (call word 17, first words 10, label paragraph 8, last words 3, call words 4, ambiguous 2), note crosses a page 8, call word already has a footnote from 15 Oct 7 (the earlier footnote or the reading is off; not touched), uncertain or no call 6, overlap 1.
