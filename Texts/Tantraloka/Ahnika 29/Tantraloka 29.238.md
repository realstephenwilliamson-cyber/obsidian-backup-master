## Mūla

शिष्यस्य चक्रसंभेदप्रत्ययो जायते ध्रुवः ।
येनाणिमादिका सिद्धिः श्रीमालायां च चोदिता ॥ ^mula

śiṣyasya cakrasaṃbhedapratyayo jāyate dhruvaḥ |
yenāṇimādikā siddhiḥ śrīmālāyāṃ ca coditā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 13 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 5#^pg395|p. 378]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=395>))
>
> The initiation by piercing (vedha) has been described in various places (in the scriptures) as being of many kinds. It must be performed by (a teacher) who is well-practiced, by whom the disciple is made to enter (progressively) ever higher (levels) and an (outer) validatory sign (pratyaya) certainly (dhruvam) arises, (testifying to) the piercing of (the disciple’s) cakras (cakrasabheda). (with 29.237–238ab)
>
> (However,) it is stated in the venerable Ratnamālā that if the state of the upper Wheels is not attained, that (accomplishment is in actual fact) just (the result of) possession (āveśa) by a demon (piśāca). (with 29.238d–239ab)

*Lead-in:* एवंविधेन अस्य किं स्यात् ?--इत्याशङ्क्य आह--

> येनाणिमादिका सिद्धिः ........

अन्यथा पुनरूर्ध्वोर्ध्वप्रवेशाभावे विघ्नादिभाजनत्वं भवेत्-- यदागमः--इत्याह--

> ...........श्रीमालायां च चोदिता ॥
> ऊर्ध्वचक्रदशाऽलाभे पिशाचावेश एव सा ।

यदुक्तं तत्र--

"अधोऽवस्था यदा ऊर्ध्वं सङ्क्रमन्ति वरानने ।

सैव मोक्षपदावस्था सैव ज्ञानस्य भाजनम् ॥

ऊर्ध्वचक्रगतावस्था यदाधः संभवन्ति च ।

तदा पैशाच आवेशः स वै विघ्नस्य कारणम् ॥"

इति ॥
