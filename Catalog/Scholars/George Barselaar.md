# George Barselaar

Author of *The 12 Kalis in Kashmir Shaivism*, drawn from the teachings of Swami Lakshmanjoo; his bio on the page says he lived in Kashmir and studied for ten years with Swami Lakshmanjoo, worked with John Hughes on transcriptions of Lakshmanjoo's recorded teachings, and for 14 years has officiated at the annual yagya marking Lakshmanjoo's Mahasamadhi in Los Angeles ([Stillness Speaks](https://www.stillnessspeaks.com/kashmir-shaivism-12-kalis-george-barselaar/)).
