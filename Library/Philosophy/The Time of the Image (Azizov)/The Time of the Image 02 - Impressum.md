Zeigam Azizov *The Time of the Image.* 

*A Philosophical Exploration of the Image in the Work of Bernard Stiegler* Klagenfurter Beiträge zur Visuellen Kultur, 9 Köln: Halem, 2020

This book is based on a doctoral thesis entitled *Time and Image* (Goldsmiths University of London and Alpen-Adria-University of Klagenfurt/Austria) for which the author received a Doctor of Philosophy degree.

Zeigam Azizov (\*1963 in the Republic of Azerbaijan, currently living in London, UK) is an artist and a philosopher. He has exhibited, lectured, and published internationally.

The series *Klagenfurter Beiträge zur Visuellen Kultur* is published by Jörg Helbig and Rainer Winter.

Acknowledgments

This research started as a doctoral thesis entitled *Time and Image* at Goldsmiths University of London and was later completed at Klagenfurt University, as a result of which I received the degree of Doctor of Philosophy. I would like to thank everyone who supported this project.

The work of the late Stuart Hall and wonderful discussions on culture and politics with him played an important role in deciding to start this research. I will never forget him: a magnificent theorist and my great friend. I will always miss him.

Thanks go to Bernard Stiegler for approving the idea of this work, to Mark Nash for advising to consider the notion of ›suture‹ again and to Isaac Julien for his continued support for my work. I extend my thanks to Matthew Hyland for his comments and suggestions and to Sarat Maharaj for his wisdom.

Viktor Misiano published some articles based on ideas from this book in *Moscow Art Magazine*. I am very grateful to Christian Kravagna, who several times invited me to give lectures at the Vienna Academy of Arts.

Thanks go to my friends at Klagenfurt University Matthias Wieser, Sebastian Nestler and Andreas Hudelist for their friendship and hospitality, to Professor Brigitte Hilfie for her generosity and to Irmgard and Sandra for their continues assistance.

I also thank my son Filipp and his mother Anastasia for everything and to Filipp again for his drawing *An Island*.

My special thanks go to Professor Rainer Winter for supervising this project, for his invitations to teach at the Klagenfurt University and for his friendship.

London, 2020
