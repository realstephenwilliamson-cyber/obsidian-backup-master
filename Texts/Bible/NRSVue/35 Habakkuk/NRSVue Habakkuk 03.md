# Habakkuk 3 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Habakkuk 02|← Habakkuk 2]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · —

**3:1** A prayer of the prophet Habakkuk according to Shigionoth. ^3-1

## The prophet’s prayer

**3:2** O LORD, I have heard of your renown, and I stand in awe, O LORD, of your work. In our own time revive it; in our own time make it known; in wrath may you remember mercy. ^3-2

**3:3** God came from Teman, the Holy One from Mount Paran. *Selah* His glory covered the heavens, and the earth was full of his praise. ^3-3

**3:4** The brightness was like the sun; rays came forth from his hand, where his power lay hidden. ^3-4

**3:5** Before him went pestilence, and plague followed close behind. ^3-5

**3:6** He stopped and shook the earth; he looked and made the nations tremble. The eternal mountains were shattered; along his ancient pathways the everlasting hills sank low. ^3-6

**3:7** I saw the tents of Cushan under affliction; the tent curtains of the land of Midian trembled. ^3-7

**3:8** Was your wrath against the rivers,^[Or *against River*] O LORD, or your anger against the rivers^[Or *against River*] or your rage against the sea,^[Or *against Sea*] when you drove your horses, your chariots to victory? ^3-8

**3:9** You brandished your naked bow; sated^[Heb mss: MT *oaths*] were the arrows at your command.^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] *Selah* You split the earth with rivers. ^3-9

**3:10** The mountains saw you and writhed; a torrent of water swept by; the deep gave forth its voice. The sun raised high its hands; ^3-10

**3:11** the moon stood still in its exalted place, at the light of your arrows speeding by, at the gleam of your flashing spear. ^3-11

**3:12** In fury you marched on the earth; in anger you trampled nations. ^3-12

**3:13** You came forth to save your people, to save your anointed. You crushed the head of the wicked house, laying it bare from foundation to roof.^[Heb *neck*] *Selah* ^3-13

**3:14** You pierced with their^[Heb *his*] own arrows the head of his warriors,^[Gk Vg Syr: Meaning of Heb uncertain] who came like a whirlwind to scatter us,^[Heb *me*] gloating as if ready to devour the poor who were in hiding. ^3-14

**3:15** You trampled the sea with your horses, churning the mighty waters. ^3-15

**3:16** I hear, and I tremble within; my lips quiver at the sound. Rottenness enters into my bones, and my steps tremble^[Cn Compare Gk: Meaning of Heb uncertain] beneath me. I wait quietly for the day of calamity to come upon the people who attack us. ^3-16

## Trust and joy in the midst of trouble

**3:17** Though the fig tree does not blossom and no fruit is on the vines; though the produce of the olive fails and the fields yield no food; though the flock is cut off from the fold and there is no herd in the stalls, ^3-17

**3:18** yet I will rejoice in the LORD; I will exult in the God of my salvation. ^3-18

**3:19** GOD, the Lord, is my strength; he makes my feet like the feet of a deer and makes me tread upon the heights.^[Heb *my heights*] To the leader: with stringed^[Heb *my stringed*] instruments. ^3-19
