# 1 Enoch 2 (Charles)

[[1 Enoch 001|← 1 Enoch 1]] · [[1 Enoch|index]] · [[1 Enoch 003|1 Enoch 3 →]]

**2:1** Observe ye every thing that takes place in the heaven, how they do not change their orbits, ⌜and⌝ the luminaries which are in the heaven, how they all rise and set in order each in its season, and transgress not against their appointed order. ^2-1

**2:2** Behold ye the earth, and give heed to the things which take place upon it from first to last, ⌜how **steadfast** they are⌝, how ⌜none of the things upon earth⌝ change, ⌜but⌝ all the works of God appear ⌜to you⌝. ^2-2

**2:3** Behold the summer and the winter, 〚how the whole earth is filled with water, and clouds and dew and rain lie upon it〛. ^2-3
