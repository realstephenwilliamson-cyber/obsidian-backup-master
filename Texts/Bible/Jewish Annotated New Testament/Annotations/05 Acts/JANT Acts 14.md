### 14.1–28: Preaching in Lycaonia

*[[NRSVue Acts 14#^14-1|Text]], p. 252* ^14-1

**[[NRSVue Acts 14#^14-1|1–7]]:** Events in Iconium.  ^14-1-2

**[[NRSVue Acts 14#^14-1|1]]:** *Iconium*, see 13.51n. *Synagogue*, despite announcing that he is turning to the Gentiles (13.46), Paul continues to preach to Jews (16.13; 17.1,10,17; 18.4,19,26; 19.8), and some become believers. *Jews*, see 13.43. *Greeks*, see 13.16. **3**: *Speaking boldly*, see 4.13. ^14-1-3

**[[NRSVue Acts 14#^14-4|4]]:** *Jews*, those not persuaded to join Paul. *Apostles*, title applied to Paul only here and v 14. Paul claimed the title for himself ([[Romans 01#^1-1|Rom 1.1]]; [[1 Corinthians 01#^1-1|1 Cor 1.1]]; [[1 Corinthians 09#^9-1|9.1]]; [[2 Corinthians 01#^1-1|2 Cor 1.1]]; [[Galatians 01#^1-1|Gal 1.1]]), although some objected ([[1 Corinthians 09#^9-2|1 Cor 9.2]]). ^14-4

**[[NRSVue Acts 14#^14-5|5]]:** *Rulers*, synagogue leaders for Jews, civic officials for Greeks. *Stone them*, either legal punishment or mob action; see 7.58n. ^14-5

**[[NRSVue Acts 14#^14-6|6]]:** *Lystra* . . . *Derbe*, smaller towns in Lycaonia. ^14-6

**[[NRSVue Acts 14#^14-8|14.8–20]]:** **Events in Lystra.** The healing parallels Peter’s act (3.1–10). ^14-8

**[[NRSVue Acts 14#^14-9|9]]:** *Intently*, see 3.4. *Faith to be healed*, faith often accompanies healing (3.16; [[Luke 05#^5-20|Lk 5.20]]; [[Luke 07#^7-50|7.50]]; [[Luke 08#^8-48|8.48]]). ^14-9

**[[NRSVue Acts 14#^14-10|10]]:** Paul often performs miracles (13.11; 19.11; 28.3–5). ^14-10

**[[NRSVue Acts 14#^14-11|11–12]]:** *Lycaonian language*, if an independent language or local Greek dialect, it has not survived. That gods could appear in human form was widely accepted (Homer, *Od.* 17.483–87; Ovid, *Metam.* 8.611–724). Zeus was the supreme deity in the Greek pantheon; no explanation is given why the people identified Barnabas as such. Hermes was a messenger God, and therefore an appropriate identification for someone recognized as the chief speaker. ^14-11

**[[NRSVue Acts 14#^14-13|13]]:** *Wanted to offer sacrifice*, to Paul and Barnabas, and essentially mocking the religious practices not only of the people of Iconium but polytheists in general. ^14-13

**[[NRSVue Acts 14#^14-14|14]]:** *Tore their clothes*, showing deep distress ([[Torah 09 - Vayeshev#^37-29|Gen 37.29]]; [[Judges 11#^11-35|Judg 11.35]]; [[II Kings 02#^2-12|2 Kings 2.12]]; [[LXX Judith 14#^14-16|Jdt 14.16]]; [[LXX 1 Maccabees 13#^13-45|1 Macc 13.45]]). ^14-14

**[[NRSVue Acts 14#^14-15|15]]:** *Worthless things*, idols ([[Psalms 096#^96-5|Ps 96.5]]; [[Psalms 097#^97-7|97.7]]; [[Isaiah 57#^57-13|Isa 57.13]]; [[LXX Wisdom of Solomon 13#^13-10|Wis 13.10–19]]). *To the living God*, rejecting idols required turning to the true God. ^14-15

**[[NRSVue Acts 14#^14-16|16]]:** *Follow their own ways*, Jews could accept the use of idols by Gentiles (Philo, *Spec*. *Laws* 1.53; *Life of Moses* 2.205; Josephus, *Ant.* 4.207; *Ag. Ap.* 2.237), and legitimize that use by describing it as ancestral customs (*b. Hul.* 13b). ^14-16

**[[NRSVue Acts 14#^14-17|17]]:** *Rains . . . fruitful seasons*, God can be known through the natural world ([[Jeremiah 05#^5-24|Jer 5.24]]; [[Hosea 06#^6-3|Hos 6.3]]; [[Psalms 147#^147-8|Ps 147.8]]; Philo, *Leg. all.* 3.97–103; *Rewards* 41–42; *Spec. Laws* 3.189; *m. Ber.* 9.2; *Gen. Rab.* 39.1). ^14-17

**[[NRSVue Acts 14#^14-19|19]]:** *Jews*, traveling as much as 100 miles to denounce Paul reinforces their deep hatred. *Stoned*, see [[2 Corinthians 11#^11-25|2 Cor 11.25]]. ^14-19

**[[NRSVue Acts 14#^14-21|21–28]]:** **Return to Syrian Antioch.** Paul revisits communities in Lycaonia, Pisidia, and Pamphylia. ^14-21

**[[NRSVue Acts 14#^14-22|22]]:** *Strengthened the souls*, see 15.41; 16.5; 18.23; 20.2. In [[Luke 21#^21-12|Lk 21.12–19]], Jesus instructs his disciples that suffering is a precursor to the kingdom of God; Jewish texts often associate violence and suffering with the beginning of the end-time ([[Daniel 11#^11-32|Dan 11.32–35]]; *Sib*. *Or*. 3.635–51; *4 Ezra*\[2 Esd\] 6.18–25; *2 Bar*. 70; *m. Sot.* 9.15). ^14-22

**[[NRSVue Acts 14#^14-23|23]]:** *Elders*, see 11.30. *Lord*, Jesus, see 1.21; 9.5. ^14-23

**[[NRSVue Acts 14#^14-25|25]]:** *Attalia*, seaport of Perga; see 13.13n. ^14-25

**[[NRSVue Acts 14#^14-27|27]]:** *Church*, assembly of believers. *Faith for the Gentiles*, although Acts mentions Paul’s success among Jews, it is primarily interested in Gentile believers. ^14-27
