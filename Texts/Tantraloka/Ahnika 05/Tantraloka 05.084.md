## Mūla

महासाहससंयोगविलीनाखिलवृत्तिकः ।

mahāsāhasasaṃyogavilīnākhilavṛttikaḥ |

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 05 - Chapter Five, part 3#^pg120|p. 105]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=120>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 5.84cd–85ab.
>
> He who, united with the Great Audacity (mahāsāhasa), all the activity (of his senses) dissolved away, abides in a state of plenitude, the flux of the rays of his (senses) gathered together (within his own nature) and free of thought, there, (his) intuitive insight lucid (spaṣṭadṛk), attains to that consciousness whose (mere) sparks are the sole means by which (the abode of) transmigratory existence (samsāra) is burnt to ashes. (with 5.84–85)
