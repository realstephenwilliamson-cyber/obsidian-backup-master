Complacency Kills

There is a sentence in a Wikipedia article that says, “From February 2007 until April 2008, the 2nd Battalion, 32nd Field Artillery, Task Force Patriot, as part of the [2nd Brigade Combat Team, 1st Infantry Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2nd_Brigade_Combat_Team,_1st_Infantry_Division_(United_States)) served in northwest [Baghdad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baghdad) where it conducted dismounted [infantry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infantry) operations to secure the populace and defeat extremist, [insurgent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insurgent), and [criminal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Criminal) threats to security.” I wonder how many people get to see their most traumatic and formative life events summed up with such cold efficiency on the internet.

It was a cold February at Fort Riley Kansas

When I was told to bring only what I could fit in a duffle bag, rucksack, and day bag to survive for a year
