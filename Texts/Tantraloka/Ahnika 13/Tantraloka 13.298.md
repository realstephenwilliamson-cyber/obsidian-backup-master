## Mūla

भोगव्यवधिना कोऽपि साधकश्चिरशीघ्रतः ।
कश्चित्संपूर्णकर्तव्यः कृत्यपञ्चकभागिनि ॥ ^mula

bhogavyavadhinā ko'pi sādhakaściraśīghrataḥ |
kaścitsaṃpūrṇakartavyaḥ kṛtyapañcakabhāgini || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 09 - Chapter Thirteen, part 5#^pg328|p. 306]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=328>))
>
> 4) Finally, the teacher (guru) possesses (all) types of worldly benefits and liberation. He has completely accomplished all that needs to be done, and abides in the (divine) nature that has (the power to accomplish) the five (divine) tasks (of creation, persistence, destruction, grace and obscuration). The four grades, beginning with the regular (initiate) and the rest, (may be bestowed) sequentially or otherwise, all together or separately; (such is the) wonderful variety of the descent of the power (of grace). (with 13.295cd–300ab)
