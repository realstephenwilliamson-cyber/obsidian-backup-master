## Mūla

नियमश्च तथारूपभासनामात्रसारकः ।
बीजादङ्कुर इत्येवं भासनं नहि सर्वदा ॥ ^mula

niyamaśca tathārūpabhāsanāmātrasārakaḥ |
bījādaṅkura ityevaṃ bhāsanaṃ nahi sarvadā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 03 - Chapter Nine, part 1#^pg51|p. 34]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=51>))
>
> Essentially, (causal) necessity (niyama) consists solely of (the process of) manifestation (which takes place) in that form. The manifestation in this way (of) a sprout (emerging) from a seed does not always (take place, rather it happens at some particular time, when the conditions are right, and the seed is prompted to do so).
