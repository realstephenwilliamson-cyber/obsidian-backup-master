## Mūla

येन संदृष्टमात्रेति सिद्धमात्रपदद्वयात् ।
आकृतिर्दीप्तरूपा या मन्त्रस्तद्वत्सुदीप्तिकः ॥ ^mula

yena saṃdṛṣṭamātreti siddhamātrapadadvayāt |
ākṛtirdīptarūpā yā mantrastadvatsudīptikaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 12 - Chapter Twenty-One#^pg276|p. 256]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=276>))
>
> 1) The rite (should be) very abundant. 2) (The offering consists of) flowers and the like, that possess the most excellent characteristics. 3) The (sacred) place is (the location of) a solitary Liñga and the like (listed in the scriptures), where the Self is satisfied (and propitious). 4) The mandala is the lotus wheel of the triple Trident, (which is so powerful that) once seen, the condition of a regular initiate is attained, even without having offered oblations to the assembly of Mantras (mantramandala). That is stated in the Mālinītantra in two words, namely, ‘accomplished’ and ‘only’, where we read: ‘The mandala of the Rule is accomplished by just seeing which...’ 5) The image (of the deceased should be) energized (dīpta). 6) In the same way, the Mantra is well energized. The rest is clear, therefore it is not elaborated at length here. (with 21.18cd–22ab)

## Viveka

> क्रियातिभूयसी पुष्पाद्युत्तमं लक्षणान्वितम् ॥
> एकलिङ्गादि च स्थानं यत्रात्मा संप्रसीदति ।
> मण्डलं त्रित्रिशूलाब्जचक्रं यन्मन्त्रमण्डले ॥
> अनाहूतेऽपि दृष्टं सत्समयित्वप्रसाधनम् ।
> यदुक्तम् मालिनीतन्त्रे सिद्धं समयमण्डलम् ॥
> येन संदृष्टमात्रेति सिद्धमात्रपदद्वयात् ।
> आकृतिर्दीप्तरूपा या मन्त्रस्तद्वत्सुदीप्तिकः ॥
> शिष्टं स्पष्टमतो नेह कथितं विस्तरात्पुनः ।

मात्रेति--पूजादिव्यवच्छेदात् ॥
