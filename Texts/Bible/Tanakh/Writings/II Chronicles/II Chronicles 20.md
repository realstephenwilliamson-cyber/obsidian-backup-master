## Chapter 20

**20:1** After that, Moabites, Ammonites, together with some Ammonim,[^1] came against Jehoshaphat to wage war. ^20-1

**20:2** The report was brought to Jehoshaphat: “A great multitude is coming against you from beyond the sea, from Aram, and is now in Hazazon-tamar”—that is, Ein-gedi. ^20-2

**20:3** Jehoshaphat was afraid; he decided to resort to the LORD and proclaimed a fast for all Judah. ^20-3

**20:4** Judah assembled to beseech the LORD. They also came from all the towns of Judah to seek the LORD. ^20-4

**20:5** Jehoshaphat stood in the congregation of Judah and Jerusalem in the House of the LORD at the front of the new court. ^20-5

**20:6** He said, “LORD God of our fathers, truly You are the God in heaven and You rule over the kingdoms of the nations; power and strength are Yours; none can oppose You. ^20-6

**20:7** O our God, you dispossessed the inhabitants of this land before Your people Israel, and You gave it to the descendants of Your friend Abraham forever. ^20-7

**20:8** They settled in it and in it built for You a House for Your name. They said, ^20-8

**20:9** ‘Should misfortune befall us—the punishing sword, pestilence, or famine, we shall stand before this House and before You—for Your name is in this House—and we shall cry out to You in our distress, and You will listen and deliver us.’ ^20-9

**20:10** Now the people of Ammon, Moab, and the hill country of Seir, into whose [land] You did not let Israel come when they came from Egypt, but they turned aside from them and did not wipe them out, ^20-10

**20:11** these now repay us by coming to expel us from Your possession which You gave us as ours. ^20-11

**20:12** O our God, surely You will punish them, for we are powerless before this great multitude that has come against us, and do not know what to do, but our eyes are on You.” ^20-12

**20:13** All Judah stood before the LORD with their little ones, their womenfolk, and their children. ^20-13

**20:14** Then in the midst of the congregation the spirit of the LORD came upon Jahaziel son of Zechariah son of Benaiah son of Jeiel son of Mattaniah the Levite, of the sons of Asaph, ^20-14

**20:15** and he said, “Give heed, all Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem and King Jehoshaphat; thus said the LORD to you, ‘Do not fear or be dismayed by this great multitude, for the battle is God’s, not yours. ^20-15

**20:16** March down against them tomorrow as they come up by the Ascent of Ziz; you will find them at the end of the wadi in the direction of the wilderness of Jeruel. ^20-16

**20:17** It is not for you to fight this battle; stand by, wait, and witness your deliverance by the LORD, O Judah and Jerusalem; do not fear or be dismayed; go forth to meet them tomorrow and the LORD will be with you.’” ^20-17

**20:18** Jehoshaphat bowed low with his face to the ground, and all Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem threw themselves down before the LORD to worship the LORD. ^20-18

**20:19** Levites of the sons of Kohath and of the sons of Korah got up to extol the LORD God of Israel at the top of their voices. ^20-19

**20:20** Early the next morning they arose and went forth to the wilderness of Tekoa. As they went forth, Jehoshaphat stood and said, “Listen to me, O Judah and inhabitants of Jerusalem: Trust firmly in the LORD your God and you will stand firm; trust firmly in His prophets and you will succeed.” ^20-20

**20:21** After taking counsel with the people, he stationed singers to the LORD extolling the One majestic in holiness as they went forth ahead of the vanguard, saying, “Praise the LORD, for His steadfast love is eternal.” ^20-21

**20:22** As they began their joyous shouts and hymns, the LORD set ambushes for the men of Ammon, Moab, and the hill country of Seir, who were marching against Judah, and they were routed. ^20-22

**20:23** The Ammonites and Moabites turned against the men of the hill country of Seir to exterminate and annihilate them. When they had made an end of the men of Seir, each helped to destroy his fellow. ^20-23

**20:24** When Judah reached the lookout in the wilderness and looked for the multitude, they saw them lying on the ground as corpses; not one had survived. ^20-24

**20:25** Jehoshaphat and his army came to take the booty, and found an abundance of goods, corpses, and precious objects, which they pillaged, more than they could carry off. For three days they were taking booty, there was so much of it. ^20-25

**20:26** On the fourth day they assembled in the Valley of Blessing—for there they blessed the LORD; that is why that place is called the Valley of Blessing to this day. ^20-26

**20:27** All the men of Judah and Jerusalem with Jehoshaphat at their head returned joyfully to Jerusalem, for the LORD had given them cause for rejoicing over their enemies. ^20-27

**20:28** They came to Jerusalem to the House of the LORD, to the accompaniment of harps, lyres, and trumpets. ^20-28

**20:29** The terror of God seized all the kingdoms of the lands when they heard that the LORD had fought the enemies of Israel. ^20-29

**20:30** The kingdom of Jehoshaphat was untroubled, and his God granted him respite on all sides. ^20-30

**20:31** [^2]Jehoshaphat reigned over Judah. He was thirty-five years old when he became king, and he reigned in Jerusalem for twenty-five years. His mother’s name was Azubah daughter of Shilhi. ^20-31

**20:32** He followed the course of his father Asa and did not deviate from it, doing what was pleasing to the LORD. ^20-32

**20:33** However, the shrines did not cease; the people still did not direct their heart toward the God of their fathers. ^20-33

**20:34** As for the other events of Jehoshaphat’s reign, early and late, they are recorded in the annals of Jehu son of Hanani, which were included in the book of the kings of Israel. ^20-34

**20:35** Afterward, King Jehoshaphat of Judah entered into a partnership with King Ahaziah of Israel, thereby acting wickedly. ^20-35

**20:36** He joined with him in constructing ships to go to Tarshish; the ships were constructed in Ezion-geber. ^20-36

**20:37** Eliezer son of Dodavahu of Mareshah prophesied against Jehoshaphat, “As you have made a partnership with Ahaziah, the LORD will break up your work.” The ships were wrecked and were unable to go to Tarshish. ^20-37

[^1]: Probably for m‘nym “Meunites” (1 Chron. 4.41); cf. Kimhi.
[^2]: With vv. 31–37, cf. 1 Kings 22.41–49.
