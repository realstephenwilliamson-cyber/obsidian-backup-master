# Psalm 33

← [[BCP Psalm 032]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 034]] →

*Exultate, justi* · Book One · Sixth Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 033]]

**33:1** Rejoice in the LORD, you righteous; \*  
it is good for the just to sing praises. ^33-1

**33:2** Praise the LORD with the harp; \*  
play to him upon the psaltery and lyre. ^33-2

**33:3** Sing for him a new song; \*  
sound a fanfare with all your skill upon the trumpet. ^33-3

**33:4** For the word of the LORD is right, \*  
and all his works are sure. ^33-4

**33:5** He loves righteousness and justice; \*  
the loving-kindness of the LORD fills the whole earth. ^33-5

**33:6** By the word of the LORD were the heavens made, \*  
by the breath of his mouth all the heavenly hosts. ^33-6

**33:7** He gathers up the waters of the ocean as in a water-skin \*  
and stores up the depths of the sea. ^33-7

**33:8** Let all the earth fear the LORD; \*  
let all who dwell in the world stand in awe of him. ^33-8

**33:9** For he spoke, and it came to pass; \*  
he commanded, and it stood fast. ^33-9

**33:10** The LORD brings the will of the nations to naught; \*  
he thwarts the designs of the peoples. ^33-10

**33:11** But the LORD'S will stands fast for ever, \*  
and the designs of his heart from age to age. ^33-11

**33:12** Happy is the nation whose God is the LORD! \*  
happy the people he has chosen to be his own! ^33-12

[p. 627]

**33:13** The LORD looks down from heaven, \*  
and beholds all the people in the world. ^33-13

**33:14** From where he sits enthroned he turns his gaze \*  
on all who dwell on the earth. ^33-14

**33:15** He fashions all the hearts of them \*  
and understands all their works. ^33-15

**33:16** There is no king that can be saved by a mighty army; \*  
a strong man is not delivered by his great strength. ^33-16

**33:17** The horse is a vain hope for deliverance; \*  
for all its strength it cannot save. ^33-17

**33:18** Behold, the eye of the LORD is upon those who fear him, \*  
on those who wait upon his love, ^33-18

**33:19** To pluck their lives from death, \*  
and to feed them in time of famine. ^33-19

**33:20** Our soul waits for the LORD; \*  
he is our help and our shield. ^33-20

**33:21** Indeed, our heart rejoices in him, \*  
for in his holy Name we put our trust. ^33-21

**33:22** Let your loving-kindness, O LORD, be upon us, \*  
as we have put our trust in you. ^33-22
