# Micah 7

← [[Micah 6]] · [[Tanakh]]

Compare: [[NRSVue Micah 07|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 7

**7:1** Woe is me![^1]  
I am become like leavings of a fig harvest,  
Like gleanings when the vintage is over,  
There is not a cluster to eat,  
Not a ripe fig I could desire. ^7-1

**7:2** The pious are vanished from the land,  
None upright are left among men;  
All lie in wait to commit crimes,  
One traps the other in his net. ^7-2

**7:3** They are eager to do evil:  
The magistrate makes demands,  
And the judge [judges] for a fee;  
The rich man makes his crooked plea,  
And they grant it.[^2] ^7-3

**7:4** The best of them is like a prickly shrub;  
The [most] upright, worse than a barrier of thorns.  
On the day you waited for,[^3] your doom has come—  
Now their confusion shall come to pass. ^7-4

**7:5** Trust no friend,  
Rely on no intimate;  
Be guarded in speech  
With her who lies in your bosom. ^7-5

**7:6** For son spurns father,  
Daughter rises up against mother,  
Daughter-in-law against mother-in-law—  
A man’s own household  
Are his enemies. ^7-6

**7:7** Yet I will look to the LORD,  
I will wait for the God who saves me,  
My God will hear me. ^7-7

**7:8** Do not rejoice over me,  
O my enemy![^4]  
Though I have fallen, I rise again;  
Though I sit in darkness, the LORD is my light. ^7-8

**7:9** I must bear the anger of the LORD,  
Since I have sinned against Him,  
Until He champions my cause  
And upholds my claim.  
He will let me out into the light;  
I will enjoy vindication by Him. ^7-9

**7:10** When my enemy[^5] sees it,  
She shall be covered with shame,  
She who taunts me with “Where is He,  
The LORD your God?”  
My eyes shall behold her [downfall];  
Lo, she shall be for trampling  
Like mud in the streets. ^7-10

**7:11** A day for mending your walls[^6]—  
That is a far-off day. ^7-11

**7:12** This is rather a day when to you  
[Tramplers] will come streaming  
From Assyria and the towns of Egypt—  
From [every land from] Egypt to the Euphrates,  
From sea to sea and from mountain to mountain— ^7-12

**7:13** And your[^7] land shall become a desolation—  
Because of those who dwell in it—  
As the fruit of their misdeeds. ^7-13

**7:14** Oh, shepherd Your people with Your staff,  
Your very own flock.  
May they who dwell isolated  
In a woodland surrounded by farmland[^8]  
Graze[^9] Bashan and Gilead  
As in olden days. ^7-14

**7:15** I will show him[^10] wondrous deeds  
As in the days when You sallied forth from the land of Egypt. ^7-15

**7:16** Let nations behold and be ashamed  
Despite all their might;  
Let them put hand to mouth;  
Let their ears be deafened! ^7-16

**7:17** Let them lick dust like snakes,  
Like crawling things on the ground!  
Let them come trembling out of their strongholds[^11]  
To the LORD our God;  
Let them fear and dread You! ^7-17

**7:18** Who is a God like You,  
Forgiving iniquity  
And remitting transgression;  
Who has not maintained His wrath forever  
Against the remnant of His own people,  
Because He loves graciousness! ^7-18

**7:19** He will take us back in love;  
He will cover up our iniquities,  
You will hurl all our[^12] sins  
Into the depths of the sea. ^7-19

**7:20** You will keep faith with Jacob,  
Loyalty to Abraham,  
As You promised on oath to our fathers  
In days gone by. ^7-20

[^1]: The speaker is feminine (cf. ’elohayikh, v. 10), probably Samaria personified; cf. note l-l at 6.16.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^4]: Heb. feminine, apparently referring to Damascus.
[^5]: Heb. feminine, apparently referring to Damascus.
[^6]: To keep out tramplers (end of preceding verse); cf. Isa. 5.5; Ps. 80.13–14.
[^7]: Heb. “the.”
[^8]: I.e., the land west of the Jordan, which is represented as far less fertile than adjacent regions.
[^9]: Emendation yields “possess.”
[^10]: Emendation yields “Show us.”
[^11]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^12]: Heb. “their.”
