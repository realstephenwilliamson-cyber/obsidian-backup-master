## Mūla

केऽपि स्वकृत्यायातांशस्थानमात्रोपसेविनः ।
येऽनुग्रहार्थमाज्ञप्तास्तेषु यो म्रियते नरः ॥ ^mula

ke'pi svakṛtyāyātāṃśasthānamātropasevinaḥ |
ye'nugrahārthamājñaptāsteṣu yo mriyate naraḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 05 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 3#^pg123|p. 106]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=123>))
>
> There (in that context, the Liṅgas that) are spontaneously born of themselves (svayambhū) are of two kinds. Some are intent on gracing (all living beings, irrespective of place) (anugrahatatpara). Some (others) reside only in the places where a partial incarnation (of a deity) (aṁśa)) has come (to carry out) its own task. The man who dies in those (places) where (those dwell,) whom (Śiva) has ordered (to be there) in order to grace (others), attains (their) clearly evident grace without (having) to descend (again) into the mortal (world). (with 28.239cd–241ab)
