# Micah 7 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Micah 06|← Micah 6]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · —

## The total corruption of the people

**7:1** Woe is me! For I have become like one who, after the summer fruit has been gathered, after the vintage has been gleaned, finds no cluster to eat; there is no first-ripe fig for which I hunger. ^7-1

**7:2** The faithful have disappeared from the land, and there is no one left who is upright; they all lie in wait for blood, and they hunt each other with nets. ^7-2

**7:3** Their hands are skilled to do evil; the official and the judge ask for a bribe, and the powerful dictate what they desire; thus they pervert justice.^[Cn: Heb *they weave it*] ^7-3

**7:4** The best of them is like a brier, the most upright of them a thorn hedge. The day of their^[Heb *your*] sentinels, of their^[Heb *your*] punishment, has come; now their confusion is at hand. ^7-4

**7:5** Put no trust in a friend; have no confidence in a loved one; guard the doors of your mouth from her who lies in your embrace, ^7-5

**7:6** for the son treats the father with contempt, the daughter rises up against her mother, the daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law; your enemies are members of your own household. ^7-6

**7:7** But as for me, I will look to the LORD; I will wait for the God of my salvation; my God will hear me. ^7-7

## Penitence and trust in God

**7:8** Do not rejoice over me, my enemies;^[Heb *enemy*] when I fall, I shall rise; when I sit in darkness, the LORD will be a light to me. ^7-8

**7:9** I must bear the indignation of the LORD because I have sinned against him, until he takes my side and executes judgment for me. He will bring me out to the light; I shall see his vindication. ^7-9

**7:10** Then my enemies^[Heb *enemy*] will see, and shame will cover those^[Heb *she*] who said to me, “Where is the LORD your God?” My eyes will see their^[Heb *her*] downfall;^[Heb lacks *downfall*] now they^[Heb *she*] will be trodden down like the mire of the streets. ^7-10

## A prophecy of restoration

**7:11** A day for the building of your walls! On that day the boundary shall be far extended. ^7-11

**7:12** On that day they will come to you from Assyria to^[Heb ms: MT *Assyria and cities of*] Egypt and from Egypt to the River, from sea to sea and from mountain to mountain. ^7-12

**7:13** But the earth will be desolate because of its inhabitants, for the fruit of their doings. ^7-13

**7:14** Shepherd your people with your staff, the flock that belongs to you, which lives alone in a forest in the midst of a garden land; let them feed in Bashan and Gilead as in the days of old. ^7-14

**7:15** As in the days when you came out of the land of Egypt, show us^[Cn: Heb *I will show him*] marvelous things. ^7-15

**7:16** The nations shall see and be ashamed of all their might; they shall lay their hands on their mouths; their ears shall be deaf; ^7-16

**7:17** they shall lick dust like a snake, like the crawling things of the earth; they shall come trembling out of their fortresses; they shall turn in dread to the LORD our God, and they shall stand in fear of you. ^7-17

## God’s compassion and steadfast love

**7:18** Who is a God like you, pardoning iniquity and passing over the transgression of the remnant of his possession? He does not retain his anger forever because he delights in showing steadfast love. ^7-18

**7:19** He will again have compassion upon us; he will tread our iniquities under foot. You will cast all our^[Gk Syr Vg Tg: Heb *their*] sins into the depths of the sea. ^7-19

**7:20** You will show faithfulness to Jacob and steadfast love to Abraham, as you have sworn to our ancestors from the days of old. ^7-20
