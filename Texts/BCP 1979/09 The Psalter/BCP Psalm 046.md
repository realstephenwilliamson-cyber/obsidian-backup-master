# Psalm 46

← [[BCP Psalm 045]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 047]] →

*Deus noster refugium* · Book Two · Ninth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 046]]

**46:1** God is our refuge and strength, \*  
a very present help in trouble. ^46-1

**46:2** Therefore we will not fear, though the earth be moved, \*  
and though the mountains be toppled into the depths of the sea; ^46-2

**46:3** Though its waters rage and foam, \*  
and though the mountains tremble at its tumult. ^46-3

**46:4** The LORD of hosts is with us; \*  
the God of Jacob is our stronghold. ^46-4

**46:5** There is a river whose streams make glad the city of God, \*  
the holy habitation of the Most High. ^46-5

**46:6** God is in the midst of her;  
she shall not be overthrown; \*  
God shall help her at the break of day. ^46-6

**46:7** The nations make much ado, and the kingdoms are shaken; \*  
God has spoken, and the earth shall melt away. ^46-7

**46:8** The LORD of hosts is with us; \*  
the God of Jacob is our stronghold. ^46-8

**46:9** Come now and look upon the works of the LORD, \*  
what awesome things he has done on earth. ^46-9

**46:10** It is he who makes war to cease in all the world; \*  
he breaks the bow, and shatters the spear,  
and burns the shields with fire. ^46-10

[p. 650]

**46:11** "Be still, then, and know that I am God; \*  
I will be exalted among the nations;  
I will be exalted in the earth." ^46-11

**46:12** The LORD of hosts is with us; \*  
the God of Jacob is our stronghold. ^46-12
