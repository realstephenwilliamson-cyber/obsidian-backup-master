## Mūla

रुन्धनी रोधनी रोद्ध्री ज्ञानबोधा तमोपहा ।
एताः पञ्च कलाः प्राहुर्निरोधिन्यां गुरूत्तमाः ॥ ^mula

rundhanī rodhanī roddhrī jñānabodhā tamopahā |
etāḥ pañca kalāḥ prāhurnirodhinyāṃ gurūttamāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 10 - Chapter Eight, part 8#^pg378|p. 360]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=378>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 8.384cd–385ab.
>
> Above the Point is the Half Moon (Ardhacandra). It possesses five energies (kalā), namely, Jyotsnā (Light), Jyotisnāvatī (Endowed with Light), Kānti (Beauty), Suprabhā (Beautiful Light) and Vimalā (Pure). Then come the (energies that) obstruct (rodhikā). The best teachers say that these are the five energies within the Obstructress, namely, Rundhanī, Rodhanī, Roddhrī, Jñānabodhā (Awakening of Knowledge), and Tamopahā (Remover of Darkness). (with 8.383–384)
