# Psalms 43 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 42|← Psalms 42]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 44|Psalms 44 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 43|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**43:1** Unto the end, for the sons of Core, to give understanding. ^43-1

**43:2** We have heard, O God, with our ears: our fathers have declared to us, The work, thou hast wrought in their days, and in the days of old. ^43-2

**43:3** Thy hand destroyed the Gentiles, and thou plantedst them: thou didst afflict the people and cast them out. ^43-3

**43:4** For they got not the possession of the land by their own sword: neither did their own arm save them. But thy right hand and thy arm, and the light of thy countenance: because thou wast pleased with them. ^43-4

**43:5** Thou art thyself my king and my God, who commandest the saving of Jacob. ^43-5

**43:6** Through thee we will push down our enemies with the horn: and through thy name we will despise them that rise up against us. ^43-6

**43:7** For I will not trust in my bow: neither shall my sword save me. ^43-7

**43:8** But thou hast saved us from them that afflict us: and hast put them to shame that hate us. ^43-8

**43:9** In God shall we glory all the day long: and in thy name we will give praise for ever. ^43-9

**43:10** But now thou hast cast us off, and put us to shame: and thou, O God, wilt not go out with our armies. ^43-10

**43:11** Thou hast made us turn our back to our enemies: and they that hated us plundered for themselves. ^43-11

**43:12** Thou hast given us up like sheep to be eaten: thou hast scattered us among the nations. ^43-12

**43:13** Thou hast sold thy people for no price: and there was no reckoning in the exchange of them. ^43-13

**43:14** Thou hast made us a reproach to our neighbours, a scoff and derision to them that are round about us. ^43-14

**43:15** Thou hast made us a byword among the Gentiles: a shaking of the head among the people. ^43-15

**43:16** All the day long my shame is before me: and the confusion of my face hath covered me, ^43-16

**43:17** At the voice of him that reproacheth and detracteth me: at the face of the enemy and persecutor. ^43-17

**43:18** All these things have come upon us, yet we have not forgotten thee: and we have not done wickedly in they covenant. ^43-18

**43:19** And our heart hath not turned back: neither hast thou turned aside our steps from thy way. ^43-19

**43:20** For thou hast humbled us in the place of affliction: and the shadow of death hath covered us. ^43-20

**43:21** If we have forgotten the name of our God, and if we have spread forth our hands to a strange god: ^43-21

**43:22** Shall not God search out these things: for he knoweth the secrets of the heart. Because for thy sake we are killed all the day long: we are counted as sheep for the slaughter. ^43-22

**43:23** Arise, why sleepest thou, O Lord? arise, and cast us not off to the end. ^43-23

**43:24** Why turnest thou face away? and forgettest our want and our trouble? ^43-24

**43:25** For our soul is humbled down to the dust: our belly cleaveth to the earth. ^43-25

**43:26** Arise, O Lord, help us and redeem us for thy name’s sake. ^43-26
