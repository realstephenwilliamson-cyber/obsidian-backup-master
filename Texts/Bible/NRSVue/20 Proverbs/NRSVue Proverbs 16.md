# Proverbs 16 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Proverbs 15|← Proverbs 15]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Proverbs 17|Proverbs 17 →]]

**16:1** The plans of the mind belong to mortals, but the answer of the tongue is from the LORD. ^16-1

**16:2** All one’s ways may be pure in one’s own eyes, but the LORD weighs the spirit. ^16-2

**16:3** Commit your work to the LORD, and your plans will be established. ^16-3

**16:4** The LORD has made everything for its purpose, even the wicked for the day of trouble. ^16-4

**16:5** All those who are arrogant are an abomination to the LORD; be assured, they will not go unpunished. ^16-5

**16:6** By loyalty and faithfulness iniquity is atoned for, and by the fear of the LORD one avoids evil. ^16-6

**16:7** When the ways of people please the LORD, he causes even their enemies to be at peace with them. ^16-7

**16:8** Better is a little with righteousness than large income with injustice. ^16-8

**16:9** The human mind plans the way, but the LORD directs the steps. ^16-9

**16:10** Inspired decisions are on the lips of a king; his mouth does not sin in judgment. ^16-10

**16:11** Honest balances and scales are the LORD’s; all the weights in the bag are his work. ^16-11

**16:12** It is an abomination to kings to do evil, for the throne is established by righteousness. ^16-12

**16:13** Righteous lips are the delight of a king, and he loves those who speak what is right. ^16-13

**16:14** A king’s wrath is a messenger of death, and whoever is wise will appease it. ^16-14

**16:15** In the light of a king’s face there is life, and his favor is like the clouds that bring the spring rain. ^16-15

**16:16** How much better to get wisdom than gold! To get understanding is to be chosen rather than silver. ^16-16

**16:17** The highway of the upright avoids evil; those who guard their way preserve their lives. ^16-17

**16:18** Pride goes before destruction and a haughty spirit before a fall. ^16-18

**16:19** It is better to be of a lowly spirit among the poor than to divide the spoil with the proud. ^16-19

**16:20** Those who are attentive to a matter will prosper, and happy are those who trust in the LORD. ^16-20

**16:21** The wise of heart is called perceptive, and pleasant speech increases persuasiveness. ^16-21

**16:22** Wisdom is a fountain of life to one who has it, but folly is the punishment of fools. ^16-22

**16:23** The mind of the wise makes their speech judicious and adds persuasiveness to their lips. ^16-23

**16:24** Pleasant words are like a honeycomb, sweetness to the soul and health to the body. ^16-24

**16:25** Sometimes there is a way that seems to be right, but in the end it is the way to death. ^16-25

**16:26** The appetite of workers works for them; their hunger urges them on. ^16-26

**16:27** Scoundrels concoct evil, and their speech is like a scorching fire. ^16-27

**16:28** A perverse person spreads strife, and a whisperer separates close friends. ^16-28

**16:29** The violent entice their neighbors and lead them in a way that is not good. ^16-29

**16:30** One who winks the eyes plans^[Gk Syr Vg Tg: Heb *to plan*] perverse things; one who compresses the lips brings evil to pass. ^16-30

**16:31** Gray hair is a crown of glory; it is gained in a righteous life. ^16-31

**16:32** One who is slow to anger is better than the mighty, and one whose temper is controlled than one who captures a city. ^16-32

**16:33** The lot is cast into the lap, but the decision is the LORD’s alone. ^16-33
