## Mūla

समावेशोक्तिवद्योगस्त्रिविधः समुदाहृतः ।
तत्र प्राप्तोपदेशस्तु पारम्पर्यक्रमेण यः ॥ ^mula

samāveśoktivad yogas trividhaḥ samudāhṛtaḥ |
tatra prāptopadeśas tu pāramparyakrameṇa yaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, April 2023 – June 2023
> p. 22 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 1-5 (2022-2023).pdf#page=63>))
>
> Yoga is said to be of three kinds like the teachings (concerning the three) penetrations. There (in that context), the one who has acquired the teachings in accord with the transmission of the sequence (of teachers), the wise, should be known to be one who has acquired yoga, and he too is of three kinds. (with 4.34–35ab)
