## Mūla

द्वारप्राकारकोणेषु नेत्रानलशरानृतून् ।
नाडयो ब्रह्मवंशस्य लोप्या नेत्राद्रसस्थिताः ॥ ^mula

dvāraprākārakoṇeṣu netrānalaśarānṛtūn |
nāḍayo brahmavaṃśasya lopyā netrādrasasthitāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 05 - Chapter Thirty-One, part 2#^pg140|p. 122]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=140>))
>
> ‘(Then beginning from) the inside, (he should remove), in the corners of the walls (prākāra) of the doors, with the exception of the inner threads (nādivivarjitān), (squares numbering the two) eyes, (three sacred) fires, (five) arrows, and (the six) seasons.’ (with 31.137d–138ab)
>
> ‘Six squares (nādī) should be deleted, (beginning) from the second (square on the right side of) the square of Brahmā, from the third, five, from the fourth, four, and beginning from the sixth, two. (The same should be done also) on the left, where (the equivalent squares should similarly) be deleted beginning from the second, the third, the fourth and the sixth (squares).’ (with 31.138cd–139)

*Lead-in:* एवं दिक्चतुष्टये वर्तनामभिधाय कोणेषु अपि आह--

> द्वारप्राकारकोणेषु नेत्रानलशरानृतून् ।

नेत्रे द्वे, ऋतवः षट् । एवं द्वारकोणेषु एकैकभागपरिहारेण द्वित्रिपञ्चसंख्याकान् भागानन्तरारभ्य लोपयेत्, ऋतुसंख्याकांस्तु पृथगुपादानादेव निरवशेषान् यदुभयदिगुद्भूतशोभाद्वयसंभेदात् कोणेषु गोमूत्रिकाबन्धप्रायः संनिवेश उदियात्--इति द्वारसन्धिः ॥

इदानीं वीथीं वर्तयितुमाह--
