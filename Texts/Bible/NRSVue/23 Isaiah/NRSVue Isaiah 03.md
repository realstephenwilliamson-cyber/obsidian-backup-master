# Isaiah 3 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Isaiah 02|← Isaiah 2]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Isaiah 04|Isaiah 4 →]]

**3:1** For now the Sovereign, the LORD of hosts, is taking away from Jerusalem and from Judah support and staff—all support of bread and all support of water— ^3-1

**3:2** warrior and soldier, judge and prophet, diviner and elder, ^3-2

**3:3** captain of fifty and dignitary, counselor and skillful magician and expert enchanter. ^3-3

**3:4** And I will make youths their princes, and children shall rule over them. ^3-4

**3:5** The people will be oppressed, everyone by another and everyone by a neighbor; the youth will be insolent to the elder and the base to the honorable. ^3-5

**3:6** Someone will seize a relative, a member of the clan, saying, “You have a cloak; you shall be our leader, and this heap of ruins shall be under your rule.” ^3-6

**3:7** But the other will cry out on that day, saying, “I will not be a healer; in my house there is neither bread nor cloak; you shall not make me leader of the people.” ^3-7

**3:8** For Jerusalem has stumbled, and Judah has fallen, because their speech and their deeds are against the LORD, defying his glorious presence. ^3-8

**3:9** The look on their faces bears witness against them; they proclaim their sin like Sodom; they do not hide it. Woe to them, for they have brought evil on themselves. ^3-9

**3:10** Tell the innocent how fortunate they are, for they shall eat the fruit of their labors. ^3-10

**3:11** Woe to the guilty! How unfortunate they are, for what their hands have done shall be done to them. ^3-11

**3:12** My people—their oppressors extort them, and creditors^[Gk: Heb *women*] rule over them. O my people, your leaders mislead you and confuse the course of your paths. ^3-12

**3:13** The LORD rises to argue his case; he stands to judge the peoples. ^3-13

**3:14** The LORD enters into judgment with the elders and princes of his people: It is you who have devoured the vineyard; the spoil of the poor is in your houses. ^3-14

**3:15** What do you mean by crushing my people, by grinding the face of the poor? says the Lord GOD of hosts. ^3-15

**3:16** The LORD said: Because the daughters of Zion are haughty and walk with outstretched necks, glancing wantonly with their eyes, mincing along as they go, tinkling with their feet; ^3-16

**3:17** the Lord will afflict with scabs the heads of the daughters of Zion, and the LORD will lay bare their scalps and heads. ^3-17

**3:18** On that day the Lord will take away the finery of the anklets, the headbands, and the crescents; ^3-18

**3:19** the pendants, the bracelets, and the scarfs; ^3-19

**3:20** the headdresses, the armlets, the sashes, the perfume boxes, and the amulets; ^3-20

**3:21** the signet rings and nose rings; ^3-21

**3:22** the festal robes, the mantles, the cloaks, and the handbags; ^3-22

**3:23** the garments of gauze, the linen garments, the turbans, and the veils. ^3-23

**3:24** Instead of perfume there will be a stench; and instead of a sash, a rope; and instead of well-styled hair, baldness; and instead of a rich robe, a binding of sackcloth; instead of beauty, shame.^[Q ms: MT lacks *shame*] ^3-24

**3:25** Your men shall fall by the sword and your warriors in battle. ^3-25

**3:26** And her gates shall lament and mourn; desolate, she shall sit upon the ground. ^3-26
