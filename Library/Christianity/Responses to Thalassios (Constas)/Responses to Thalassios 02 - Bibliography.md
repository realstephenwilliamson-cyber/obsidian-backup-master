> - The first letter of each Question's opening word (and of the Prologue and the Introduction) is a picture in the PDF and is missing here ("AS IT BY chance" for "Was it by chance").
> - The PDF's Greek font lacks some letters (often μ and ι), so Greek words show gaps ("θέλη α" for θέλημα), as they do in the PDF's page images.
> - The PDF lacks printed pages 222 (the last scholia of Question 37) and 252 (the last scholia of Question 44), and repeats pages 305–315 (see the note for Question 51).
> - About ten note lines that hold only numbers (such as "276–78.") are missing.
> - The indexes run entries together and lose some lines; the three-column Index of Holy Scripture and Index of Greek Words are read across the columns, so their entries are out of order.
