## Mūla

अमले गगने व्यापिन्येकाग्रीभूतदृष्टिकः ।
स्मरन्मन्त्रं यदासीत काऽन्या निर्मलता ततः ॥ ^mula

amale gagane vyāpinyekāgrībhūtadṛṣṭikaḥ |
smaranmantraṃ yadāsīta kā'nyā nirmalatā tataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 03 - Chapter Fifteen, part 2#^pg64|p. 46]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=64>))
>
> If you view the pervasive sky, (staring at it) with (intense) concentration whilst recollecting the (appropriate) Mantra, what other (greater) purity can there be than this? Or else, this kind of ablution with Space (can also be performed) when one, intent on repeating the (appropriate) Mantra, touches (and wets his) body with the water that falls from a stainless sky. (with 15.57–58)
