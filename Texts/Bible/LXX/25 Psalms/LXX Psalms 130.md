# LXX Psalms 130

← [[LXX Psalms 129]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 131]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 131]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 131]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**130:1** A Song of Degrees. O Lord, my heart is not exalted, neither have mine eyes been *haughtily* raised: neither have I exercised myself in great *matters*, nor in things too wonderful for me.[^1]  ^130-1

**130:2** *I shall have sinned* if I have not been humble, but have exalted my soul: according to *the relation of* a weaned child to his mother, so wilt thou recompense my soul.  ^130-2

**130:3** Let Israel hope in the Lord, from henceforth and for ever.  ^130-3

[^1]: 130:1 *Alex.*+ 'for David.'
