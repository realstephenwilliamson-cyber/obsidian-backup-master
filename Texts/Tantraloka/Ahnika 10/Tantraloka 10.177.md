## Mūla

मायाकर्मसमुल्लाससंमिश्रितमलाबिलाः ।
धराधिरोहिणो ज्ञेयाः सकला इह पुद्गलाः ॥ ^mula

māyākarmasamullāsasaṃmiśritamalābilāḥ |
dharādhirohiṇo jñeyāḥ sakalā iha pudgalāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 12 - Chapter Ten, part 3#^pg419|p. 400]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=419>))
>
> The souls (pudgala), sullied by impurity (mala) mixed with the outpouring of Māyā and Karma, who reside here in the Earth Principle, should be known as Conditioned (sakala) (perceivers).
