# Hosea 10

## Chapter 10

**10:1** Israel is a ravaged vine  
And its fruit is like it.  
When his fruit was plentiful,  
He made altars aplenty;  
When his land was bountiful,  
Cult pillars abounded. ^10-1

**10:2** Now that his boughs[^1] are broken up,  
He feels his guilt;  
He himself pulls apart his altars,  
Smashes his pillars. ^10-2

**10:3** Truly, now they say,  
“We have no king;  
For, since we do not fear the LORD,  
What can a king do to us?” ^10-3

**10:4** So they conclude agreements and make covenants  
With false oaths,  
And justice degenerates into poison weeds,  
Breaking out[^2] on the furrows of the fields. ^10-4

**10:5** The inhabitants of Samaria fear  
For the calf of Beth-aven;[^3]  
Indeed, its people and priestlings,  
Whose joy it once was,[^4]  
Mourn over it for the glory  
That is departed from it. ^10-5

**10:6** It too shall be brought to Assyria  
As tribute to a patron[^5] king;  
Ephraim shall be chagrined,  
Israel shall be dismayed  
Because of his plans.[^6] ^10-6

**10:7** Samaria’s monarchy[^7] is vanishing  
Like foam upon water, ^10-7

**10:8** Ruined shall be the shrines of [Beth-]aven,[^8]  
That sin of Israel.  
Thorns and thistles  
Shall grow on their altars.  
They shall call to the mountains, “Bury us!”  
To the hills, “Fall on us!” ^10-8

**10:9** You have sinned more, O Israel,  
Than in the days of Gibeah.[^9]  
[^10]There they stand [as] at Gibeah!  
Shall they not be overtaken  
By a war upon scoundrels ^10-9

**10:10** As peoples gather against them?  
When I chose [them], I broke them in,  
Harnessing them for two furrows. ^10-10

**10:11** Ephraim became a trained heifer,  
But preferred to thresh;  
I placed a yoke  
Upon her sleek neck.[^11]  
I will make Ephraim do advance plowing;[^12]  
Judah[^13] shall do [main] plowing!  
Jacob shall do final plowing! ^10-11

**10:12** “Sow righteousness for yourselves;  
Reap the fruits of[^14] goodness;  
Break for yourselves betimes fresh ground  
Of seeking the LORD,  
So that you may obtain a teacher[^15] of righteousness.” ^10-12

**10:13** You have plowed wickedness,  
You have reaped iniquity—  
[And] you shall eat the fruits of treachery—  
Because you relied on your way,[^16]  
On your host of warriors. ^10-13

**10:14** But the din of war shall arise in your own people,  
And all your fortresses shall be ravaged  
As Beth-arbel was ravaged by Shalman[^17]  
On a day of battle,  
When mothers and babes were dashed to death together. ^10-14

**10:15** This is what Bethel has done to you[^18]  
For your horrible wickedness:  
At dawn[^19] shall Israel’s monarchy  
Utterly perish. ^10-15

[^1]: Cf. 2 Sam. 18.14, where the word is rendered “thick growth.”
[^2]: Cf. Amos 6.12; lit. “breaks out like poison weeds.”
[^3]: See note m at 4.15.
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^5]: See note j at 5.13.
[^6]: Emendation yields “image,” referring to the calf.
[^7]: The Heb. verb agrees with this word, not with “Samaria.”
[^8]: See note m at 4.15.
[^9]: See note at 9.9.
[^10]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^11]: Lit. “passed over the comeliness of its neck.”
[^12]: Taking rkb in the sense of the Arabic krb.
[^13]: Emendation yields “Israel.”
[^14]: Lit. “according to.”
[^15]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain; Septuagint reads “the fruits.”
[^16]: Septuagint reads “chariots.”
[^17]: Perhaps identical with the Shallum of 2 Kings 15.10 ff.; cf. the atrocities of Shallum’s rival, ibid., v. 16.
[^18]: Emendation yields “will I do to you, O House of Israel.”
[^19]: Meaning, perhaps, “swiftly as the dawn”; cf. v. 7 above, “like foam upon water.”
