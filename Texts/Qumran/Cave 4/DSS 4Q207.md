### Frag. 1 (= 1 Enoch 86:1-3)

[… Again I was]

**1** [lifting my eyes in the dream and I saw the heaven] above [and behold] a star [fell from heaven in the midst] ^f1-1

**2** [of the great bulls and ate and grazed] in the midst of them. Behold, then [I] saw [those bulls, large] ^f1-2

**3** [and black; all of them exchanged their feeds,] their pens and th[eir bu]llock[s and began to live with each other.] ^f1-3

**4** [I looked again in my dream and watched the sky] and behold many stars [came down and fell from the sky] ^f1-4

**5** [in the middle of the ﬁrst star and were turned into bu]lls in the mid[st of those bullocks and grazed with them and among them] ^f1-5
