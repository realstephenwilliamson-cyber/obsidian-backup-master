# LXX Psalms 29

← [[LXX Psalms 28]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 30]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 030]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 030]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**29:1** For the end, a Psalm and Song at the dedication of the house of David.[^1]  ^29-1

**29:2** I will exalt thee, O Lord; for thou hast lifted me up, and not caused mine enemies to rejoice over me.  ^29-2

**29:3** O Lord my God, I cried to thee, and thou didst heal me.  ^29-3

**29:4** O Lord, thou hast brought up my soul from Hades, thou hast delivered me from *among* them that go down to the pit.  ^29-4

**29:5** Sing to the Lord, ye his saints, and give thanks for the remembrance of his holiness.  ^29-5

**29:6** For anger is in his wrath, but life in his favour: weeping shall tarry for the evening, but joy shall be in the morning.  ^29-6

**29:7** And I said in my prosperity, I shall never be moved.  ^29-7

**29:8** O Lord, in thy good pleasure thou didst add strength to my beauty: but thou didst turn away thy face, and I was troubled.  ^29-8

**29:9** To thee, O Lord, will I cry; and to my God will I make supplication.  ^29-9

**29:10** What profit is there in my blood, when I go down to destruction? Shall the dust give praise to thee? or shall it declare thy truth?  ^29-10

**29:11** The Lord heard, and had compassion upon me; the Lord is become my helper.  ^29-11

**29:12** Thou hast turned my mourning into joy for me: thou hast rent off my sackcloth, and girded me with gladness;  ^29-12

**29:13** that my glory may sing praise to thee, and I may not be pierced *with sorrow*. O Lord my God, I will give thanks to thee for ever.  ^29-13

[^1]: 29:1 *Gr.* of a song.
