TRANSLATION 

(The Devi puts a further question) 

Is the nature of parāparā śakti (transcendent-cum-immanent Energy) and aparā śakti (immanent Energy) sakala i.e. consisting of parts or is the nature of parā śakti (transcendent Energy) also sakala ? Paratva or transcendence cannot be consistent with the division of letters and colour or of bodies (ną hi varṇavibhedena, dehabhedena vā bhavet paratvam) ; paratva or transcendence consists only in indivisibility (niṣskalatvena); it (transcendence) cannot co-exist with sakala (a composite of parts) (sakalatve na tadbhavet).?
