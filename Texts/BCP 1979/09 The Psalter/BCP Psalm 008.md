# Psalm 8

← [[BCP Psalm 007]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 009]] →

*Domine, Dominus noster* · Book One · First Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 008]]

**8:1** O LORD our Governor, \*  
how exalted is your Name in all the world! ^8-1

**8:2** Out of the mouths of infants and children \*  
your majesty is praised above the heavens. ^8-2

**8:3** You have set up a stronghold against your adversaries, \*  
to quell the enemy and the avenger. ^8-3

**8:4** When I consider your heavens, the work of your fingers, \*  
the moon and the stars you have set in their courses, ^8-4

**8:5** What is man that you should be mindful of him? \*  
the son of man that you should seek him out? ^8-5

**8:6** You have made him but little lower than the angels; \*  
you adorn him with glory and honor; ^8-6

**8:7** You give him mastery over the works of your hands; \*  
you put all things under his feet: ^8-7

**8:8** All sheep and oxen, \*  
even the wild beasts of the field, ^8-8

**8:9** The birds of the air, the fish of the sea, \*  
and whatsoever walks in the paths of the sea. ^8-9

**8:10** O LORD our Governor, \*  
how exalted is your Name in all the world! ^8-10
