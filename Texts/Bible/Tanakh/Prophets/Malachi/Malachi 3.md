## Chapter 3

**3:1** Behold, I am sending My messenger to clear the way before Me, and the Lord whom you seek shall come to His Temple suddenly. As for the angel of the covenant[^1] that you desire, he is already coming. ^3-1

**3:2** But who can endure the day of his coming, and who can hold out when he appears? For he is like a smelter’s fire and like fuller’s lye. ^3-2

**3:3** He shall act[^2] like a smelter and purger of silver; and he shall purify the descendants of Levi and refine them like gold and silver, so that they shall present offerings in righteousness. ^3-3

**3:4** Then the offerings of Judah and Jerusalem shall be pleasing to the LORD as in the days of yore and in the years of old. ^3-4

**3:5** But [first] I will step forward to contend against you, and I will act as a relentless accuser against those who have no fear of Me: Who practice sorcery, who commit adultery, who swear falsely, who cheat laborers of their hire, and who subvert [the cause of] the widow, orphan, and stranger, said the LORD of Hosts. ^3-5

**3:6** [^3]For I am the LORD—I have not changed; and you are the children of Jacob—you have not ceased to be. ^3-6

**3:7** From the very days of your fathers you have turned away from My laws and have not observed them. Turn back to Me, and I will turn back to you—said the LORD of Hosts. But you ask, “How shall we turn back?” ^3-7

**3:8** Ought man to defraud[^4] God? Yet you are defrauding Me. And you ask, “How have we been defrauding You?” In tithe and contribution.[^5] ^3-8

**3:9** You are suffering under a curse, yet you go on defrauding Me—the whole nation of you. ^3-9

**3:10** Bring the full tithe into the storehouse,[^6] and let there be food in My House, and thus put Me to the test—said the LORD of Hosts. I will surely open the floodgates of the sky for you and pour down blessings on you; ^3-10

**3:11** and I will banish the locusts[^7] from you, so that they will not destroy the yield of your soil; and your vines in the field shall no longer miscarry—said the LORD of Hosts. ^3-11

**3:12** And all the nations shall account you happy, for you shall be the most desired of lands—said the LORD of Hosts. ^3-12

**3:13** You have spoken hard words against Me—said the LORD. But you ask, “What have we been saying among ourselves against You?” ^3-13

**3:14** You have said, “It is useless to serve God. What have we gained by keeping His charge and walking in abject awe of the LORD of Hosts? ^3-14

**3:15** And so, we account the arrogant happy: they have indeed done evil and endured; they have indeed dared God and escaped.” ^3-15

**3:16** In this vein have those who revere the LORD been talking to one another. The LORD has heard and noted it, and a scroll of remembrance has been written at His behest concerning those who revere the LORD and esteem His name. ^3-16

**3:17** And on the day that I am preparing, said the LORD of Hosts, they shall be My treasured possession; I will be tender toward them as a man is tender toward a son who ministers to him. ^3-17

**3:18** And you shall come to see the difference between the righteous and the wicked, between him who has served God and him who has not served Him. ^3-18

**3:19** For lo! That day is at hand, burning like an oven. All the arrogant and all the doers of evil shall be straw, and the day that is coming—said the LORD of Hosts—shall burn them to ashes and leave of them neither stock nor boughs. ^3-19

**3:20** But for you who revere My name a sun of victory shall rise to bring healing.[^8] You shall go forth and stamp like stall-fed calves, ^3-20

**3:21** and you shall trample the wicked to a pulp, for they shall be dust beneath your feet on the day that I am preparing—said the LORD of Hosts. ^3-21

**3:22** Be mindful of the Teaching of My servant Moses, whom I charged at Horeb with laws and rules for all Israel. ^3-22

**3:23** Lo, I will send the prophet Elijah to you before the coming of the awesome, fearful day of the LORD. ^3-23

**3:24** He shall reconcile parents with children and children with their parents, so that, when I come, I do not strike the whole land with utter destruction.  
LO, I WILL SEND THE PROPHET ELIJAH TO YOU BEFORE THE COMING OF THE AWESOME, FEARFUL DAY OF THE LORD. ^3-24

[^1]: Apparently the messenger of the previous sentence is regarded as Israel’s tutelary angel.
[^2]: Lit. “sit.”
[^3]: Vv. 6–12 resume the thought of 1.2–5.
[^4]: Heb. qaba‘, a play on the name of Jacob (v. 6); cf. Gen. 27.36.
[^5]: I.e., the contributions to the priests from the new grain, oil, and wine; see Num. 18.12.
[^6]: I.e., the public storehouse; see Neh. 13.10–13.
[^7]: Lit. “devourer.”
[^8]: Lit. “with healing in the folds of its garments”; others “with healing in its wings.”
