## Mūla

उक्ता तथाप्यप्रतिघे नास्मिन्नावृतिसंभवः ।
नन्वेवं धरणीं मुक्त्वा शक्तौ प्रकृतिमाययोः ॥ ^mula

uktā tathāpyapratighe nāsminnāvṛtisaṃbhavaḥ |
nanvevaṃ dharaṇīṃ muktvā śaktau prakṛtimāyayoḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 02 - Chapter Eleven, part 1#^pg27|p. 7]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=27>))
>
> (The Egg of Power does not extend further) because even though a series of world-orders was previously said to be present in the principle called Śiva, it offers no resistance to penetration (apratigha), and so no enveloping covering is possible within it. (with 11.13cd–14ab)
>
> (Someone may object) that none of the Principles (that correspond to the four cosmic Eggs) except Earth, namely, Nature, Māyā and Power, offer resistance to penetration. So how is it possible for a Cosmic Egg to be within them? (with 11.14cd–15ab)
