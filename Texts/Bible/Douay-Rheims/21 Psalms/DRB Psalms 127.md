# Psalms 127 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 126|← Psalms 126]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 128|Psalms 128 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 127|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**127:1** Blessed are all they that fear the Lord: that walk in his ways. ^127-1

**127:2** For thou shalt eat the labours of thy hands: blessed art thou, and it shall be well with thee. ^127-2

**127:3** Thy wife as a fruitful vine, on the sides of thy house. ^127-3

**127:4** Behold, thus shall the man be blessed that feareth the Lord. ^127-4

**127:5** May the Lord bless thee out of Sion: and mayest thou see the good things of Jerusalem all the days of thy life. ^127-5

**127:6** And mayest thou see thy children’s children, peace upon Israel. ^127-6
