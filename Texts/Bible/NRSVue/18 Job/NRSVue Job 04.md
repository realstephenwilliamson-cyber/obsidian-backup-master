# Job 4 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Job 03|← Job 3]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Job 05|Job 5 →]]

## Eliphaz speaks: Job has sinned

**4:1** Then Eliphaz the Temanite answered: ^4-1

**4:2** “If one ventures a word with you, will you be offended? But who can keep from speaking? ^4-2

**4:3** See, you have instructed many; you have strengthened the weak hands. ^4-3

**4:4** Your words have supported those who were stumbling, and you have made firm the feeble knees. ^4-4

**4:5** But now it has come to you, and you are impatient; it touches you, and you are dismayed. ^4-5

**4:6** Is not your fear of God your confidence and the integrity of your ways your hope? ^4-6

**4:7** “Think now, who that was innocent ever perished? Or where were the upright cut off? ^4-7

**4:8** As I have seen, those who plow iniquity and sow trouble reap the same. ^4-8

**4:9** By the breath of God they perish, and by the blast of his anger they are consumed. ^4-9

**4:10** The roar of the lion, the voice of the fierce lion, and the teeth of the young lions are broken. ^4-10

**4:11** The strong lion perishes for lack of prey, and the whelps of the lioness are scattered. ^4-11

**4:12** “Now a word came stealing to me; my ear received the whisper of it. ^4-12

**4:13** Amid thoughts from visions of the night, when deep sleep falls on mortals, ^4-13

**4:14** dread came upon me and trembling, which made all my bones shake. ^4-14

**4:15** A spirit glided past my face; the hair of my flesh bristled. ^4-15

**4:16** It stood still, but I could not discern its appearance. A form was before my eyes; there was silence; then I heard a voice: ^4-16

**4:17** ‘Can mortals be righteous before^[Or *more righteous than*] God? Can humans be pure before^[Or *more pure than*] their Maker? ^4-17

**4:18** Even in his servants he puts no trust, and his angels he charges with error; ^4-18

**4:19** how much more those who live in houses of clay, whose foundation is in the dust, who are crushed like a moth. ^4-19

**4:20** Between morning and evening they are destroyed; they perish forever without any regarding it. ^4-20

**4:21** Their tent cord is plucked up within them, and they die devoid of wisdom.’ ^4-21
