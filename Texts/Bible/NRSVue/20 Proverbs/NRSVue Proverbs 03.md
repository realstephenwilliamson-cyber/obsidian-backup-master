# Proverbs 3 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Proverbs 02|← Proverbs 2]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Proverbs 04|Proverbs 4 →]]

## Admonition to trust and honor God

**3:1** My child, do not forget my teaching, but let your heart keep my commandments, ^3-1

**3:2** for length of days and years of life and abundant welfare they will give you. ^3-2

**3:3** Do not let loyalty and faithfulness forsake you; bind them around your neck; write them on the tablet of your heart. ^3-3

**3:4** Then you will find favor and high regard in the sight of God and of people. ^3-4

**3:5** Trust in the LORD with all your heart, and do not rely on your own insight. ^3-5

**3:6** In all your ways acknowledge him, and he will make straight your paths. ^3-6

**3:7** Do not be wise in your own eyes; fear the LORD and turn away from evil. ^3-7

**3:8** It will be a healing for your flesh and a refreshment for your body. ^3-8

**3:9** Honor the LORD with your substance and with the first fruits of all your produce; ^3-9

**3:10** then your barns will be filled with plenty, and your vats will be bursting with wine. ^3-10

**3:11** My child, do not despise the LORD’s discipline or be weary of his reproof, ^3-11

**3:12** for the LORD reproves the one he loves, as a father the son in whom he delights. ^3-12

## The true wealth

**3:13** Happy are those who find wisdom and those who get understanding, ^3-13

**3:14** for her income is better than silver and her revenue better than gold. ^3-14

**3:15** She is more precious than jewels, and nothing you desire can compare with her. ^3-15

**3:16** Long life is in her right hand; in her left hand are riches and honor. ^3-16

**3:17** Her ways are ways of pleasantness, and all her paths are peace. ^3-17

**3:18** She is a tree of life to those who lay hold of her; those who hold her fast are called happy. ^3-18

## God’s wisdom in creation

**3:19** The LORD by wisdom founded the earth; by understanding he established the heavens; ^3-19

**3:20** by his knowledge the deeps broke open, and the clouds drop down the dew. ^3-20

## The true security

**3:21** My child, do not let these escape from your sight: keep sound wisdom and prudence, ^3-21

**3:22** and they will be life for your soul and adornment for your neck. ^3-22

**3:23** Then you will walk on your way securely, and your foot will not stumble. ^3-23

**3:24** If you sit down,^[Gk: Heb *lie down*] you will not be afraid; when you lie down, your sleep will be sweet. ^3-24

**3:25** Then you will not be afraid of sudden panic or of the storm that strikes the wicked, ^3-25

**3:26** for the LORD will be your confidence and will keep your foot from being caught. ^3-26

**3:27** Do not withhold good from those to whom it is due,^[Heb *from its owners*] when it is in your power to do it. ^3-27

**3:28** Do not say to your neighbor, “Go and come again; tomorrow I will give it,” when you have it with you. ^3-28

**3:29** Do not plan harm against your neighbor who lives trustingly beside you. ^3-29

**3:30** Do not quarrel with anyone without cause, when no harm has been done to you. ^3-30

**3:31** Do not envy the violent, and do not choose any of their ways, ^3-31

**3:32** for the perverse are an abomination to the LORD, but the upright are in his confidence. ^3-32

**3:33** The LORD’s curse is on the house of the wicked, but he blesses the abode of the righteous. ^3-33

**3:34** Toward the scorners he is scornful, but to the humble he shows favor. ^3-34

**3:35** The wise will inherit honor, but stubborn fools, disgrace. ^3-35
