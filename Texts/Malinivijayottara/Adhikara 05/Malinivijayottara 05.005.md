## Mūla

तदूर्ध्वं पृथिवी ज्ञेया सप्तद्वीपार्णवान्विता ।
देवानामाश्रयो मेरुस्तन्मध्ये संव्यवस्थितः ॥ ^mula

tadūrdhvaṁ pṛthivī jñeyā saptadvīpārṇavānvitā |
devānām āśrayo merus tanmadhye saṁvyavasthitaḥ || ^iast

> **2) The Earth**
>
> One should know that Earth is above that with (its) seven islands and seven oceans. Located in the middle of them is Meru, the abode (and refuge) of the gods.
