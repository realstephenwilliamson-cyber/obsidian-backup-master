## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 02 - Chapter Eleven, part 1#^pg56|p. 36]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=56>))
>
> There are countless types of initiation, according to the predominant application (upayoga) (of the Path), wherever (within it) the worldly enjoyment (bhoga) (the disciple) desires (is found) and also because of the inclusion of other (realities) contemplated within it. (with 11.38cd–39ab)
>
> Thus, the Lord has taught the Path of the Principles, (both) extensively and in brief, starting with (the division into) thirty-six up to the procedure for (just) one principle. (with 11.39cd–40ab)

## Viveka

स्थितिरेकेन वा कार्या परापरविभूतये ॥"
