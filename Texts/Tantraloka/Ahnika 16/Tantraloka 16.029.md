## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 05 - Chapter Sixteen, part 1#^pg34|p. 14]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=34>))
>
> (The sacrificial animal may be) of eight kinds which are, respectively, of a progressively more elevated order, namely 1) ‘seen’ (dṛṣṭa), 2) ‘sprinkled’ (prokṣita), 3) ‘a witness’ (saṁdraṣṭṛ), 4) ‘deceived’ (prālabdha), 5) ‘appropriated’ (upātta), 6) ‘conjoined’ (yojita), 7) ‘offered as oblation’ (nirvāpita) and 8) ‘heroic’ (vīrapaśu). The more elevated kinds should not be given to those (beings) who are not qualified (to receive them). Indeed, not everybody is fit to eat the oblation that is appropriate for Śiva. (with 16.29–30)
