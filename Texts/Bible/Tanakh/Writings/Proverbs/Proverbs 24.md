# Proverbs 24

← [[Proverbs 23]] · [[Tanakh]] · [[Proverbs 25]] →

Compare: [[NRSVue Proverbs 24|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 24

**24:1** Do not envy evil men;  
Do not desire to be with them; ^24-1

**24:2** For their hearts[^1] talk violence,  
And their lips speak mischief. ^24-2

**24:3** A house is built by wisdom,  
And is established by understanding; ^24-3

**24:4** By knowledge are its rooms filled  
With all precious and beautiful things. ^24-4

**24:5** A wise man is strength;  
A knowledgeable man exerts power; ^24-5

**24:6** For by stratagems you wage war,  
And victory comes with much planning. ^24-6

**24:7** Wisdom is too lofty for a fool;  
He does not open his mouth in the gate. ^24-7

**24:8** He who lays plans to do harm  
Is called by men a schemer. ^24-8

**24:9** The schemes of folly are sin,  
And a scoffer is an abomination to men. ^24-9

**24:10** If you showed yourself slack in time of trouble,  
Wanting in power, ^24-10

**24:11** If you refrained from rescuing those taken off to death,  
Those condemned to slaughter— ^24-11

**24:12** If you say, “We knew nothing of it,”  
Surely He who fathoms hearts will discern [the truth],  
He who watches over your life will know it,  
And He will pay each man as he deserves. ^24-12

**24:13** My son, eat honey, for it is good;  
Let its sweet drops be on your palate. ^24-13

**24:14** Know: such is wisdom for your soul;  
If you attain it, there is a future;  
Your hope will not be cut off. ^24-14

**24:15** Wicked man! Do not lurk by the home of the righteous man;  
Do no violence to his dwelling. ^24-15

**24:16** Seven times the righteous man falls and gets up,  
While the wicked are tripped by one misfortune. ^24-16

**24:17** If your enemy falls, do not exult;  
If he trips, let your heart not rejoice, ^24-17

**24:18** Lest the LORD see it and be displeased,  
And avert His wrath from him. ^24-18

**24:19** Do not be vexed by evildoers;  
Do not be incensed by the wicked; ^24-19

**24:20** For there is no future for the evil man;  
The lamp of the wicked goes out. ^24-20

**24:21** Fear the LORD, my son, and the king,  
And do not mix with dissenters, ^24-21

**24:22** For disaster comes from them suddenly;  
The doom both decree who can foreknow? ^24-22

**24:23** These also are by the sages:  
It is not right to be partial in judgment. ^24-23

**24:24** He who says to the guilty, “You are innocent,”  
Shall be cursed by peoples,  
Damned by nations; ^24-24

**24:25** But it shall go well with them who decide justly;  
Blessings of good things will light upon them. ^24-25

**24:26** Giving a straightforward reply  
Is like giving a kiss. ^24-26

**24:27** Put your external affairs in order,  
Get ready what you have in the field,  
Then build yourself a home. ^24-27

**24:28** Do not be a witness against your fellow without good cause;  
Would you mislead with your speech? ^24-28

**24:29** Do not say, “I will do to him what he did to me;  
I will pay the man what he deserves.” ^24-29

**24:30** I passed by the field of a lazy man,  
By the vineyard of a man lacking sense. ^24-30

**24:31** It was all overgrown with thorns;  
Its surface was covered with chickweed,  
And its stone fence lay in ruins. ^24-31

**24:32** I observed and took it to heart;  
I saw it and learned a lesson. ^24-32

**24:33** A bit more sleep, a bit more slumber,  
A bit more hugging yourself in bed, ^24-33

**24:34** And poverty will come calling upon you,[^2]  
And want, like a man with a shield. ^24-34

[^1]: See note to 15.28.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
