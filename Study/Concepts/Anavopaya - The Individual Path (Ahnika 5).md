# Āṇavopāya — The Individual Path (Āhnika 5)

> [!abstract] Definition
> The embodied, ritual pathway in *Tantrāloka* (Āhnika 5), designed for the individual (*aṇu*) who still experiences identification with the finite physical body, utilizing sensory supports, breath (*prāṇa*), and external liturgy (*kriyā-śakti*).

## The Modalities of Āṇavopāya
1. **Prāṇa (Breath):** The circulation of breath through the subtle channels (*nāḍīs*) and cakras.
2. **Uccāra (Sonic Utterance):** The resonance of mantras moving through the stages of sound.
3. **Karaṇa (Physical Postures & Mudrās):** Bodily gestures aligning subtle energies.
4. **Dhyāna (Visual Contemplation):** Visualizing forms of deities and geometric mandalas.
