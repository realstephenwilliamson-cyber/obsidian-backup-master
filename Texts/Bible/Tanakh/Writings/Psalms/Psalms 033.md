## Chapter 33

**33:1** Sing forth, O you righteous, to the LORD;  
it is fit that the upright acclaim Him. ^33-1

**33:2** Praise the LORD with the lyre;  
with the ten-stringed harp sing to Him; ^33-2

**33:3** sing Him a new song;  
play sweetly with shouts of joy. ^33-3

**33:4** For the word of the LORD is right;  
His every deed is faithful. ^33-4

**33:5** He loves what is right and just;  
the earth is full of the LORD’s faithful care. ^33-5

**33:6** By the word of the LORD the heavens were made,  
by the breath of His mouth, all their host. ^33-6

**33:7** He heaps up the ocean waters like a mound,  
stores the deep in vaults. ^33-7

**33:8** Let all the earth fear the LORD;  
let all the inhabitants of the world dread Him. ^33-8

**33:9** For He spoke, and it was;  
He commanded, and it endured. ^33-9

**33:10** The LORD frustrates the plans of nations,  
brings to naught the designs of peoples. ^33-10

**33:11** What the LORD plans endures forever,  
what He designs, for ages on end. ^33-11

**33:12** Happy the nation whose God is the LORD,  
the people He has chosen to be His own. ^33-12

**33:13** The LORD looks down from heaven;  
He sees all mankind. ^33-13

**33:14** From His dwelling-place He gazes  
on all the inhabitants of the earth— ^33-14

**33:15** He who fashions the hearts of them all,  
who discerns all their doings. ^33-15

**33:16** Kings are not delivered by a large force;  
warriors are not saved by great strength; ^33-16

**33:17** horses are a false hope for deliverance;  
for all their great power they provide no escape. ^33-17

**33:18** Truly the eye of the LORD is on those who fear Him,  
who wait for His faithful care ^33-18

**33:19** to save them from death,  
to sustain them in famine. ^33-19

**33:20** We set our hope on the LORD,  
He is our help and shield; ^33-20

**33:21** in Him our hearts rejoice,  
for in His holy name we trust. ^33-21

**33:22** May we enjoy, O LORD, Your faithful care,  
as we have put our hope in You. ^33-22
