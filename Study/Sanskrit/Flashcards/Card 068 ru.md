## Front

> [!note]- Back
> - **Pronunciation:** As in ‘guru’ (teacher)
> - **Class:** short syllable · This is the only time the short u is not placed below the letter.

> - **Example:** guru (teacher)

## Register

- **Classification:** Combined syllable (irregular) · **Sthāna:** Ligature ra + u
- **Body place** (*mātṛkā-nyāsa*, [[Tantraloka 15.117|TĀ 15.117]]–120): ra: blood (*rakta*) · u: right ear (*karṇa*)
