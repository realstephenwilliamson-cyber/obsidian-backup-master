# Eight Chapters (Gorfinkle)

Maimonides' *Eight Chapters* (*Shemonah Perakim*) in Joseph I. Gorfinkle's translation, public domain. Sefaria describes it as "Maimonides’ 12th-century philosophical introduction to Pirkei Avot, analyzing the soul, human purpose, and free will." See also [[Pirkei Avot]].
