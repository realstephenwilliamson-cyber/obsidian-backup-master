# Job 8 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Job 07|← Job 7]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Job 09|Job 9 →]]

## Bildad speaks: Job should repent

**8:1** Then Bildad the Shuhite answered: ^8-1

**8:2** “How long will you say these things and the words of your mouth be a great wind? ^8-2

**8:3** Does God pervert justice? Or does the Almighty^[Traditional rendering of Heb *Shaddai*] pervert the right? ^8-3

**8:4** If your children sinned against him, he delivered them into the power of their transgression. ^8-4

**8:5** If you will seek God and make supplication to the Almighty,^[Traditional rendering of Heb *Shaddai*] ^8-5

**8:6** if you are pure and upright, surely then he will rouse himself for you and restore to you your rightful place. ^8-6

**8:7** Though your beginning was small, your latter days will be very great. ^8-7

**8:8** “For inquire now of bygone generations and consider what their ancestors have found, ^8-8

**8:9** for we are but of yesterday, and we know nothing, for our days on earth are but a shadow. ^8-9

**8:10** Will they not teach you and tell you and utter words out of their understanding? ^8-10

**8:11** “Can papyrus grow where there is no marsh? Can reeds flourish where there is no water? ^8-11

**8:12** While yet in flower and not cut down, they wither before any other plant. ^8-12

**8:13** Such are the paths of all who forget God; the hope of the godless shall perish. ^8-13

**8:14** Their confidence is gossamer, a spider’s house their trust. ^8-14

**8:15** If one leans against its house, it will not stand; if one lays hold of it, it will not endure. ^8-15

**8:16** The wicked thrive^[Heb *He thrives*] before the sun, and their shoots spread over the garden. ^8-16

**8:17** Their roots twine around the stoneheap; they live among the rocks.^[Gk Vg: Meaning of Heb uncertain] ^8-17

**8:18** If they are destroyed from their place, then it will deny them, saying, ‘I have never seen you.’ ^8-18

**8:19** See, these are their happy ways,^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] and out of the earth still others will spring. ^8-19

**8:20** “See, God will not reject the blameless, nor take the hand of evildoers. ^8-20

**8:21** He will yet fill your mouth with laughter and your lips with shouts of joy. ^8-21

**8:22** Those who hate you will be clothed with shame, and the tent of the wicked will be no more.” ^8-22
