## Mūla

तदास्वादभरावेशबृंहितां मतिषट्पदीम् ।
गुरोर्लक्ष्मणगुप्तस्य नादसंमोहिनीं नुमः ॥ ^mula

tadāsvādabharāveśabṛṃhitāṃ matiṣaṭpadīm |
gurorlakṣmaṇaguptasya nādasaṃmohinīṃ numaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. I
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 01 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v01 03 - Chapter One, part 2#^pg84|p. 70]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 01.pdf#page=84>))
>
> We salute the bee-like mind of the teacher Lakṣmaṇagupta, whose sound is the attractive (resonance of consciousness) intensified by (his) penetration (āāveśa) into the copious relish of that (Lotus, his teacher Utpaladeva)."
