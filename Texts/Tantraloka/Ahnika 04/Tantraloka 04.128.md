## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. III
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 03 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v03 06 - Chapter Four, part 5#^pg180|p. 167]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 03.pdf#page=180>))
>
> That second one is said to be the two red (circles of twelve spokes), which are like enclosures, outside that (wheel of sixteen spokes). In (the iris of each eye,) between (the black pupils and the white circles of the two eyes,) there are two (other) Wheels (of the two eyes), which are (partially) black and (partially) white (and have eight spokes each). One should know that within them (in the pupils) there are two (other) pure circles, (their) colour like that of (freshly) sliced black collyrium, with four spokes (each). O beloved, the two (subject and object, the male and female partners,) abide (united) as a couple in the two Wheels of the Fire and Moon (of eight and sixteen spokes, respectively). O Supreme Goddess, they expand and contract one another (when the yogi and yoginī are engaged in union). (with 4.128–130)
