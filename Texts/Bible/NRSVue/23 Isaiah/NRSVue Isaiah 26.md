# Isaiah 26 (NRSVue)

## Judah’s song of victory

**26:1** On that day this song will be sung in the land of Judah: We have a strong city; he sets up walls and bulwarks as a safeguard. ^26-1

**26:2** Open the gates, so that the righteous nation that maintains faithfulness may enter in. ^26-2

**26:3** Those of steadfast mind you keep in peace, in peace because they trust in you. ^26-3

**26:4** Trust in the LORD forever, for in the LORD GOD^[Heb *in Yah, theLORD*] you have an everlasting rock. ^26-4

**26:5** For he has brought low the inhabitants of the height; the lofty city he lays low. He lays it low to the ground, casts it to the dust. ^26-5

**26:6** The foot tramples it, the feet of the poor, the steps of the needy. ^26-6

**26:7** The way of the righteous is level; straight is the path of the righteous that you clear. ^26-7

**26:8** In the path of your judgments, O LORD, we have placed hope; your name and your renown are the soul’s desire. ^26-8

**26:9** My soul yearns for you in the night; my spirit within me earnestly seeks you. For when your judgments are in the earth, the inhabitants of the world learn righteousness. ^26-9

**26:10** If favor is shown to the wicked, they do not learn righteousness; they corrupt what is upright on the earth and do not see the majesty of the LORD. ^26-10

**26:11** O LORD, your hand is lifted up, but they do not see it. Let them see your zeal for your people and be ashamed. Let the fire for your adversaries consume them. ^26-11

**26:12** O LORD, may you ordain peace for us, for indeed, all that we have done, you have done for us. ^26-12

**26:13** O LORD our God, other lords besides you have ruled over us, but we acknowledge your name alone. ^26-13

**26:14** The dead do not live; shades do not rise because you have punished and destroyed them and wiped out all memory of them. ^26-14

**26:15** But you have increased the nation, O LORD; you have increased the nation; you are glorified; you have enlarged all the borders of the land. ^26-15

**26:16** O LORD, in distress they sought you; they poured out a prayer^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] when your chastening was on them. ^26-16

**26:17** Like a woman with child about to give birth writhes and cries out in her pain, so were we because of you, O LORD; ^26-17

**26:18** we were with child; we writhed, but we gave birth only to wind. We have won no victories on earth, and no one is born to inhabit the world. ^26-18

**26:19** Your dead shall live; their corpses^[Cn Compare Syr Tg: Heb *my corpse*] shall rise. Those who dwell in the dust will awake and shout for joy!^[Q ms Compare Gk Syr: MT *dust, awake and shout for joy!*] For your dew is a radiant dew, and the earth will give birth to those long dead.^[Heb *to the shades*] ^26-19

**26:20** Come, my people, enter your chambers, and shut your doors behind you; hide yourselves for a little while until the wrath is past. ^26-20

**26:21** For the LORD comes out from his place to punish the inhabitants of the earth for their iniquity; the earth will disclose the blood shed on it and will no longer cover its slain. ^26-21
