# Sirach 30 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Sirach 29|← Sirach 29]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Sirach 31|Sirach 31 →]]

## CONCERNING CHILDREN^[This heading is included in the Gk text.]

**30:1** He who loves his son will whip him often, so that he may rejoice at the way he turns out. ^30-1

**30:2** He who disciplines his son will profit by him and will boast of him among acquaintances. ^30-2

**30:3** He who teaches his son will make his enemies envious and will glory in him among his friends. ^30-3

**30:4** When the father dies, he will not seem to be dead, for he has left behind him one like himself, ^30-4

**30:5** whom in his life he looked upon with joy and at death, without grief. ^30-5

**30:6** He has left behind him an avenger against his enemies and one to repay the kindness of his friends. ^30-6

**30:7** Whoever cherishes his son will bind up his wounds, and at every cry his heart will be troubled. ^30-7

**30:8** An unbroken horse turns out stubborn, and an unchecked son turns out headstrong. ^30-8

**30:9** Pamper a child, and he will terrorize you; play with him, and he will grieve you. ^30-9

**30:10** Do not laugh with him, or you will have sorrow with him, and in the end you will gnash your teeth. ^30-10

**30:11** Give him no freedom in his youth;^[Other ancient authorities add *and do not ignore his errors. Bow down his neck in his youth,*] ^30-11

**30:12** beat his sides while he is young, or else he will become stubborn and disobey you.^[Other ancient authorities add *and you will have sorrow of soul from him*] ^30-12

**30:13** Discipline your son and make his yoke heavy,^[Heb: Gk *take pains with him*] so that you may not be offended by his shamelessness. ^30-13

**30:14** Better off poor, healthy, and fit than rich and afflicted in body. ^30-14

**30:15** Health and fitness are better than any gold and a robust body^[Gk: Syr Heb read *spirit*] than countless riches. ^30-15

**30:16** There is no wealth better than health of body and no gladness above joy of heart. ^30-16

**30:17** Death is better than a life of misery and eternal sleep^[Other ancient authorities lack *eternal sleep*] than chronic sickness. ^30-17

## CONCERNING FOODS^[This heading is included in the Gk text; other ancient authorities place the heading before 30.16]

**30:18** Good things poured out upon a mouth that is closed are like offerings of food placed upon a grave. ^30-18

**30:19** Of what use to an idol is a sacrifice? For it can neither eat nor smell. So is the one punished by the Lord; ^30-19

**30:20** he sees with his eyes and groans as a eunuch groans when embracing a young woman.^[Other ancient authorities add *So is the person who does right under compulsion*] ^30-20

**30:21** Do not give yourself over to sorrow, and do not distress yourself deliberately. ^30-21

**30:22** A joyful heart is life itself, and rejoicing lengthens one’s life span. ^30-22

**30:23** Indulge yourself^[Other ancient authorities read *Beguile yourself*] and take comfort, and remove sorrow far from you, for sorrow has destroyed many, and no advantage ever comes from it. ^30-23

**30:24** Jealousy and anger shorten life, and anxiety brings on premature old age. ^30-24

**30:25** Those who are cheerful and merry at table will benefit from their food. ^30-25
