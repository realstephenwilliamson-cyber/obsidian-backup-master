4\) viśāvaḥ: 1st DU of √viś ‘to enter’ –› ‘we both enter’

8\) muñcathaḥ: 2nd DU of √muc ‘to release’ –› ‘you both release’
9\) bharāmi: 1st SG of √bhṛ ‘to carry’ –› ‘I carry’

11\) kṣipataḥ: 3rd DU of √kṣip ‘to throw’ –› ‘they both throw’

3\)
1\) I find [it] and I am happy.
2\) Do you carry [it] or does he carry [it]?
3\) I love, but you don’t love.
4\) Why do the two of them enter here?
5\) But you don’t understand.
6\) We both enter here and you both enter there.
7\) Do you remember again?
8\) I enter, but you don’t see [it/me].
9\) We enter. Why don’t you see [us]?
