# LXX Psalms 53

← [[LXX Psalms 52]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 54]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 054]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 054]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**53:1** For the end, among the Hymns of instruction by David,  ^53-1

**53:2** when the Ziphites came and said to Saul, Lo, is not David hid with us?  ^53-2

**53:3** Save me, O God, by thy name, and judge me by thy might.  ^53-3

**53:4** O God, hear my prayer; hearken to the words of my mouth.  ^53-4

**53:5** For strangers have risen up against me, and mighty men have sought my life: they have not set God before them. Pause.  ^53-5

**53:6** For lo! God assists me; and the Lord is the helper of my soul.  ^53-6

**53:7** He shall return evil to mine enemies; utterly destroy them in thy truth.  ^53-7

**53:8** I will willingly sacrifice to thee: I will give thanks to thy name, O Lord; for *it is* good.  ^53-8

**53:9** For thou hast delivered me out of all affliction, and mine eye has seen *my desire* upon mine enemies.  ^53-9
