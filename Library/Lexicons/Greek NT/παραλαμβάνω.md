# παραλαμβάνω

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · verb

**Dodson:** I take, receive — I take from, receive from, or: I take to, receive (apparently not used of money), admit, acknowledge; I take with me. (Strong's 3880; headword παραλαμβάνω)
