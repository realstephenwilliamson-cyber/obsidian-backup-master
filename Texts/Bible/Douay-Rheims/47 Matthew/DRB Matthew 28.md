# Matthew 28 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Matthew 27|← Matthew 27]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · —

Compare: [[Matthew 28|BSB and SBLGNT]]

**28:1** And in the end of the sabbath, when it began to dawn towards the first day of the week, came Mary Magdalen and the other Mary, to see the sepulchre. ^28-1

**28:2** And behold there was a great earthquake. For an angel of the Lord descended from heaven, and coming, rolled back the stone, and sat upon it. ^28-2

**28:3** And his countenance was as lightning, and his raiment as snow. ^28-3

**28:4** And for fear of him, the guards were struck with terror, and became as dead men. ^28-4

**28:5** And the angel answering, said to the women: Fear not you; for I know that you seek Jesus who was crucified. ^28-5

**28:6** He is not here, for he is risen, as he said. Come, and see the place where the Lord was laid. ^28-6

**28:7** And going quickly, tell ye his disciples that he is risen: and behold he will go before you into Galilee; there you shall see him. Lo, I have foretold it to you. ^28-7

**28:8** And they went out quickly from the sepulchre with fear and great joy, running to tell his disciples. ^28-8

**28:9** And behold Jesus met them, saying: All hail. But they came up and took hold of his feet, and adored him. ^28-9

**28:10** Then Jesus said to them: Fear not. Go, tell my brethren that they go into Galilee, there they shall see me. ^28-10

**28:11** Who when they were departed, behold some of the guards came into the city, and told the chief priests all things that had been done. ^28-11

**28:12** And they being assembled together with the ancients, taking counsel, gave a great sum of money to the soldiers, ^28-12

**28:13** Saying: Say you, His disciples came by night, and stole him away when we were asleep. ^28-13

**28:14** And if the governor shall hear this, we will persuade him, and secure you. ^28-14

**28:15** So they taking the money, did as they were taught: and this word was spread abroad among the Jews even unto this day. ^28-15

**28:16** And the eleven disciples went into Galilee, unto the mountain where Jesus had appointed them. ^28-16

**28:17** And seeing them they adored: but some doubted. ^28-17

**28:18** And Jesus coming, spoke to them, saying: All power is given to me in heaven and in earth. ^28-18

**28:19** Going therefore, teach ye all nations; baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost. ^28-19

**28:20** Teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you: and behold I am with you all days, even to the consummation of the world. ^28-20
