## Mūla

पञ्चधाद्यन्तगं चैक्यमित्युपान्त्याक्षगो विधिः ।
शक्तितद्वत्प्रभेदेन तत्र द्वैरूप्यमुच्यते ॥ ^mula

pañcadhādyantagaṃ caikyamityupāntyākṣago vidhiḥ |
śaktitadvatprabhedena tatra dvairūpyamucyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 18 - Chapter Twenty-Seven#^pg434|p. 414]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=434>))
>
> The penultimate procedure (concerning the rosary with twenty-seven) beads relates to (Sadāśiva’s) five faces. (Each of these) is associated (with the five powers of) consciousness, vibration (spanda), knowledge, will, and action, and so is five-fold (thus making twenty-five. Moreover, there are two other phases corresponding to) the oneness that precedes (the unfolding differentiation of these phases) and the subsequent oneness (when it ends. Thus, making twenty-seven in all). (with 27.32cd–33ab)
