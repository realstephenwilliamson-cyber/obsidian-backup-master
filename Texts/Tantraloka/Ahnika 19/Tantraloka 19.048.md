## Mūla

इत्येवंपरमेतन्नादीक्षिताग्रे पठेदिति ।
यथा च समयी काष्ठे लोष्टे वा मन्त्रयोजनाम् ॥ ^mula

ityevaṃparametannādīkṣitāgre paṭhediti |
yathā ca samayī kāṣṭhe loṣṭe vā mantrayojanām || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 10 - Chapter Nineteen#^pg247|p. 227]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=247>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 19.47cd–48ab.
>
> But (someone may say) that the aim of saying that one should not recite (the scriptures) in front of the uninitiated is so that the scriptures may not be criticized (and profaned), which causes both to fall. (with 19.47cd–48ab)
>
> Just as a regular initiate applying Mantras causes (a piece of) wood or (clod of) earth to move, and does not thereby transgress (any rule), such is the case here also. (with 19.48cd–49ab)

*Lead-in:* अत्र च सविचिकित्सं परं स्वकञ्चुकानुप्रवेशेनैव प्रबोधयितुमाह--

> यथा च समयी काष्ठे लोष्टे वा मन्त्रयोजनाम् ॥
> कुर्वंस्तस्मिंश्चलत्येति न लोपं तद्वदत्र हि ।

काष्ठ इति--समिधादौ । लोष्ट इति--मृल्लिङ्गादावुपादेये । अत्रेति-- मुमूर्षौ ॥
