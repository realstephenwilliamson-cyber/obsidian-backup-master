## Mūla

महिमा चेदयं तौ किं नासमञ्जस्यभागिनौ ।
ईश्वरेच्छानपेक्षा तु भेदहेतुर्न कल्पते ॥ ^mula

mahimā cedayaṃ tau kiṃ nāsamañjasyabhāginau |
īśvarecchānapekṣā tu bhedaheturna kalpate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 06 - Chapter Thirteen, part 2#^pg194|p. 174]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=194>))
>
> If (you were to say) that it is due to the variety of the latent traces (left by) beginningless Karma, then one would again have to explain how that variety (of latent traces) arises. If (you say) that it is due to the power of Necessity (niyati) and Attachment (rāga), then why should these two not be liable to (the same) objection? (with 13.80–81ab)
>
> (ls it due then to God’s will? No, because) the Lord’s will is (free. It does) not depend (on any causes or conditions, and so) cannot be conceived to be the cause of (any biased) distinctions. (with 13.81cd)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* अथ ईश्वरेच्छा कर्मवैचित्र्ये निमित्तमित्युच्यते, तदपि न -- इत्याह --

> ईश्वरेच्छानपेक्षा तु भेदहेतुर्न कल्पते ॥

सा न तावत्सापेक्षा मलकर्मातिरिक्तस्य अपेक्षणीयस्य अभावात् कर्मवैचित्र्ये च कार्ये तदयोगात्, नापि अनपेक्षा, ईश्वरस्य वीतरागद्वेषत्वात् सर्वान्प्रति अविशिष्टतया तद्वैचित्र्यानुपपत्तेः ॥ ८१ ॥
