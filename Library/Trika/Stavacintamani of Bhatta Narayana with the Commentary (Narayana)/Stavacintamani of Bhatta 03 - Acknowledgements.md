## Acknowledgements

I wish to express my deep appreciation to Prof. Shri Narayana Mishra with whom I spent countless hours, over the period of 12 years, reading various Sanskrit philosophical and other texts. It was under his guidance that I was able to gain grounding in the six systems of Indian philosophy and acquire some competence in reading sastric Sanskrit. My deep gratitude also goes to Michael Huff whose editing skills have greatly contributed to the publication of this book. I wish to state my appreciation to Pandit Hemendranath Chakravarti, a close disciple of Mahāmahopādhyāya Gopinath Kaviraj, with whom I read Abhinavagupta’s Tantrasāra and several other Kashmir Śaiva texts. 

I also owe deep gratitude to Swami Chetanananda and Sharon Ward of Nityananda Institute who have supported me in many different ways, including financially, during the period of several years, while I was engaged in the translation of the present work and of Abhinavagupta’s Tantrasāra. 

I owe special acknowledgements to Dr. Navjivan Rastogi, Professor Emeritus of Lucknow University, for taking time of his busy schedule to read this manuscript and offer Ifis suggestions. His thoughts in the form of his ‘Foreword’ to this book are highly appreciated. I am very grateful to Prof. Fredrick Smith of the University of Iowa who has made several books, Ph.D. theses and other materials available to me, which otherwise I would have been unable to access. 

I want to express my thanks to Alvaro Enterria and Dilip Kumar of Indica Books for their decision to publish this book. 

Finally, I want to thank my wife, Kyung Hwa Ro, for her patience and assistance not only with typing and re-typing of this book but her help that has taken various forms during our lokayātrā together.
