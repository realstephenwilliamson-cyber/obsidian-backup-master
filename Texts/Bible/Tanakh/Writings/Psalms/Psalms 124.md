## Chapter 124

**124:1** A song of ascents. Of David.  
Were it not for the LORD, who was on our side,  
let Israel now declare, ^124-1

**124:2** were it not for the LORD, who was on our side  
when men assailed us, ^124-2

**124:3** they would have swallowed us alive  
in their burning rage against us; ^124-3

**124:4** the waters would have carried us off,  
the torrent would have swept over us; ^124-4

**124:5** over us would have swept  
the seething waters. ^124-5

**124:6** Blessed is the LORD, who did not let us  
be ripped apart by their teeth. ^124-6

**124:7** We are like a bird escaped from the fowler’s trap;  
the trap broke and we escaped. ^124-7

**124:8** Our help is the name of the LORD,  
maker of heaven and earth. ^124-8
