### TĀ 28.79–82

केवलो यामलो मिश्रशक्रयुग्वीरसड्धरः ।
केवलः केवठैरेव गुरुभिर्मिश्रित पुनः ॥ ७९ ॥

The subject of the passage that begins with this verse is Cakrayāga. As it is said to be the ‘main one’, it is clearly a form of Ādiyāga. The Ādiyāga taught in Chapter Twenty-nine focuses on just one couple, as does the Ādyayāga of JY 4/53, and clearly involves ritual intercourse. The rite in Cakrayāga is meant for an assembly of initiates who perform the rite with their partners. These may be the male practitioners’ wives, Śaktis (Tantric consorts), or low-caste prostitutes who are paid to participate. At the beginning of this passage, cakrayāga was identified with Mūrtiyāga as the Anuyāga of the Ad iyāga necessary to make it successful. They are essentially the same rite (see above, note 28,95). Ādiyāga is for a single couple, the Mārti- / Cakra / -yāga is for the assembly. Tt is a supplementary Yāga ~ anuyāga required to complete the Ādiyāga. In this way, individual practice is co-ordinated with that of the assembly.
