# Psalms 24 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 23|← Psalms 23]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 25|Psalms 25 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 24|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**24:1** Unto the end, a psalm for David. To thee, O Lord, have I lifted up my soul. ^24-1

**24:2** In thee, O my God, I put my trust; let me not be ashamed. ^24-2

**24:3** Neither let my enemies laugh at me: for none of them that wait on thee shall be confounded. ^24-3

**24:4** Let all them be confounded that act unjust things without cause. Shew, O Lord, thy ways to me, and teach me thy paths. ^24-4

**24:5** Direct me in thy truth, and teach me; for thou art God my Saviour; and on thee have I waited all the day long. ^24-5

**24:6** Remember, O Lord, thy bowels of compassion; and thy mercies that are from the beginning of the world. ^24-6

**24:7** The sins of my youth and my ignorances do not remember. According to thy mercy remember thou me: for thy goodness’ sake, O Lord. ^24-7

**24:8** The Lord is sweet and righteous: therefore he will give a law to sinners in the way. ^24-8

**24:9** He will guide the mild in judgment: he will teach the meek his ways. ^24-9

**24:10** All the ways of the Lord are mercy and truth, to them that seek after his covenant and his testimonies. ^24-10

**24:11** For thy name’s sake, O Lord, thou wilt pardon my sin: for it is great. ^24-11

**24:12** Who is the man that feareth the Lord? He hath appointed him a law in the way he hath chosen. ^24-12

**24:13** His soul shall dwell in good things: and his seed shall inherit the land. ^24-13

**24:14** The Lord is a firmament to them that fear him: and his covenant shall be made manifest to them. ^24-14

**24:15** My eyes are ever towards the Lord: for he shall pluck my feet out of the snare. ^24-15

**24:16** Look thou upon me, and have mercy on me; for I am alone and poor. ^24-16

**24:17** The troubles of my heart are multiplied: deliver me from my necessities. ^24-17

**24:18** See my abjection and my labour; and forgive me all my sins. ^24-18

**24:19** Consider my enemies for they are multiplied, and have hated me with an unjust hatred. ^24-19

**24:20** Keep thou my soul, and deliver me: I shall not be ashamed, for I have hoped in thee. ^24-20

**24:21** The innocent and the upright have adhered to me: because I have waited on thee. ^24-21

**24:22** Deliver Israel, O God, from all his tribulations. ^24-22
