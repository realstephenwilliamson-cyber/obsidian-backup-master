In its third meaning, where physical seduction is present, Tārā is a Buddhist mantra of the Mahayana or Vajrayana sect. Here her mantra takes the form *ōm hrīm strīm hum phaṭ*. The meaning is the same as above. Seduction is a situation in which maximum sensory inputs are present, physically and mentally. So gaining control here is vital.

The sound symbol *phaṭ*[^447] is very important, too. It is an explosive sound and symbolizes the piercing of the darkness by light. The Fourier transform[^448] of a sharp transition contains all frequencies within it. When the frequencies fall into the visible range, they become an explosion of light in meditation. At the lowest frequencies, the sound is a current or a vibration in the body. As the frequency rises, the following transformations occur: feelings, sound, heat, the silence of radio waves, light, ultra-violet, and then silence again.
