# Micah 2 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Micah 01|← Micah 1]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Micah 03|Micah 3 →]]

## Social evils denounced

**2:1** Woe to those who devise wickedness and evil deeds^[Cn: Heb *work evil*] on their beds! When the morning dawns, they perform it, because it is in their power. ^2-1

**2:2** They covet fields and seize them, houses and take them away; they oppress householder and house, people and their inheritance. ^2-2

**2:3** Therefore thus says the LORD: Now, I am devising against this family an evil from which you cannot remove your necks, and you shall not walk arrogantly, for it will be an evil time. ^2-3

**2:4** On that day they shall take up a taunt song against you and wail with bitter lamentation and say, “We are utterly ruined; the LORD^[Heb *he*] alters the inheritance of my people; how he removes it from me! Among our captors^[Cn: Heb *the rebellious*] he parcels out our fields.” ^2-4

**2:5** Therefore you will have no one in the LORD’s assembly to allot you a piece of land. ^2-5

**2:6** “Do not preach”—thus they preach—“one should not preach of such things; disgrace will not overtake us.” ^2-6

**2:7** Should this be said, O house of Jacob? Is the LORD’s patience exhausted? Are these his doings? Do not my words do good to one who walks uprightly? ^2-7

**2:8** But you rise up against my people^[Cn: Heb *But yesterday my people rose*] as an enemy; you strip the robe from the peaceful,^[Cn: Heb *from before a garment*] from those who pass by trustingly with no thought of war. ^2-8

**2:9** The women of my people you drive out from their pleasant houses; from their young children you take away my glory forever. ^2-9

**2:10** Arise and go, for this is no place to rest, because of uncleanness that destroys with a violent destruction. ^2-10

**2:11** If someone were to go about uttering empty falsehoods, saying, “I will preach to you of wine and strong drink,” such a one would be the preacher for this people! ^2-11

## A promise for the remnant of Israel

**2:12** I will surely gather all of you, O Jacob; I will gather the survivors of Israel; I will set them together like sheep in a fold, like a flock in its pasture; it will resound with people. ^2-12

**2:13** The one who breaks out will go up before them; they will break through and pass the gate, going out by it. Their king will pass on before them, the LORD at their head. ^2-13
