## Mūla

पद्मत्रय्यौन्मनसी तदिदं स्यादासनत्वेन ।
ता एवान्योन्यात्मकभेदावच्छेदनाजिहासुतया ॥ ^mula

padmatrayyaunmanasī tadidaṃ syādāsanatvena |
tā evānyonyātmakabhedāvacchedanājihāsutayā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 07 - Chapter Fifteen, part 6#^pg228|p. 210]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=228>))
>
> As duality is in the process of coming to an end with the (Great) Ghost, those (energies) gradually unfold, and (remaining) well distinct from one another, (manifest as) the three prongs (of the Trident). Once their duality has been completely destroyed, they then (manifest in the form of) the three lotuses, consisting of the Transmental (unmanasa). (All) this serves as (the Lord’s) Throne. (with 15.342–343ab)
>
> These same (powers), intending to abandon the differences that distinguish them from one another, assume the state of an object of worship, as the diverse powers and their possessors etc. (in the ascending hierarchy of subjects). (with 15.343cd–344ab)
