# Psalm 50

← [[BCP Psalm 049]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 051]] →

*Deus deorum* · Book Two · Tenth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 050]]

**50:1** The LORD, the God of gods, has spoken; \*  
he has called the earth from the rising of the sun to its setting. ^50-1

**50:2** Out of Zion, perfect in its beauty, \*  
God reveals himself in glory. ^50-2

**50:3** Our God will come and will not keep silence; \*  
before him there is a consuming flame,  
and round about him a raging storm. ^50-3

**50:4** He calls the heavens and the earth from above \*  
to witness the judgment of his people. ^50-4

**50:5** "Gather before me my loyal followers, \*  
those who have made a covenant with me  
and sealed it with sacrifice." ^50-5

**50:6** Let the heavens declare the rightness of his cause; \*  
for God himself is judge. ^50-6

**50:7** Hear, O my people, and I will speak:  
"O Israel, I will bear witness against you; \*  
for I am God, your God. ^50-7

**50:8** I do not accuse you because of your sacrifices; \*  
your offerings are always before me. ^50-8

**50:9** I will take no bull-calf from your stalls, \*  
nor he-goats out of your pens; ^50-9

**50:10** For all the beasts of the forest are mine, \*  
the herds in their thousands upon the hills. ^50-10

[p. 655]

**50:11** I know every bird in the sky, \*  
and the creatures of the fields are in my sight. ^50-11

**50:12** If I were hungry, I would not tell you, \*  
for the whole world is mine and all that is in it. ^50-12

**50:13** Do you think I eat the flesh of bulls, \*  
or drink the blood of goats? ^50-13

**50:14** Offer to God a sacrifice of thanksgiving \*  
and make good your vows to the Most High. ^50-14

**50:15** Call upon me in the day of trouble; \*  
I will deliver you, and you shall honor me." ^50-15

**50:16** But to the wicked God says: \*  
Why do you recite my statutes,  
and take my covenant upon your lips; ^50-16

**50:17** Since you refuse discipline, \*  
and toss my words behind your back? ^50-17

**50:18** When you see a thief, you make him your friend, \*  
and you cast in your lot with adulterers. ^50-18

**50:19** You have loosed your lips for evil, \*  
and harnessed your tongue to a lie. ^50-19

**50:20** You are always speaking evil of your brother \*  
and slandering your own mother's son. ^50-20

**50:21** These things you have done, and I kept still, \*  
and you thought that I am like you." ^50-21

**50:22** "I have made my accusation; \*  
I have put my case in order before your eyes. ^50-22

**50:23** Consider this well, you who forget God, \*  
lest I rend you and there be none to deliver you. ^50-23

[p. 656]

**50:24** Whoever offers me the sacrifice of thanksgiving honors me; \*  
but to those who keep in my way will I show the salvation of God." ^50-24
