# LXX Psalms 46

← [[LXX Psalms 45]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 47]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 047]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 047]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**46:1** For the end, a Psalm for the sons of Core.  ^46-1

**46:2** Clap your hands, all ye nations; shout to God with a voice of exultation.  ^46-2

**46:3** For the Lord most high is terrible; *he is* a great king over all the earth.  ^46-3

**46:4** He has subdued the peoples under us, and the nations under our feet.  ^46-4

**46:5** He has chosen out his inheritance for us, the beauty of Jacob which he loved. Pause.  ^46-5

**46:6** God is gone up with a shout, the Lord with a sound of a trumpet.  ^46-6

**46:7** Sing praises to our God, sing praises: sing praises to our King, sing praises.  ^46-7

**46:8** For God is king of all the earth: sing praises with understanding.  ^46-8

**46:9** God reigns over the nations: God sits upon the throne of his holiness.  ^46-9

**46:10** The rulers of the people are assembled with the God of Abraam: for God's mighty ones of the earth have been greatly exalted.  ^46-10
