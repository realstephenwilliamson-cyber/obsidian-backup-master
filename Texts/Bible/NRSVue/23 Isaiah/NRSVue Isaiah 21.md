# Isaiah 21 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Isaiah 20|← Isaiah 20]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Isaiah 22|Isaiah 22 →]]

## Oracles concerning Babylon, Edom, and Arabia

**21:1** The oracle concerning the wilderness of the sea. As whirlwinds in the Negeb sweep on, it comes from the desert, from a terrible land. ^21-1

**21:2** A stern vision is told to me; the betrayer betrays, and the destroyer destroys. Go up, O Elam; lay siege, O Media; all the sighing she has caused I bring to an end. ^21-2

**21:3** Therefore my loins are filled with anguish; pangs have seized me like the pangs of a woman in labor; I am bowed down so that I cannot hear; I am dismayed so that I cannot see. ^21-3

**21:4** My mind reels; horror has appalled me; the twilight I longed for has been turned for me into trembling. ^21-4

**21:5** They prepare the table; they spread the rugs; they eat; they drink. Rise up, commanders; oil the shield! ^21-5

**21:6** For thus the Lord said to me: “Go, post a lookout; let him announce what he sees. ^21-6

**21:7** When he sees riders, horsemen in pairs, riders on donkeys, riders on camels, let him watch closely, very closely.” ^21-7

**21:8** Then the watcher^[Q ms Syr Vg: MT *a lion*] called out: “Upon a watchtower I stand, O Lord, continually by day, and at my post I am stationed throughout the night. ^21-8

**21:9** Look, there they come, riders, horsemen in pairs!” Then he responded, “Fallen, fallen is Babylon, and all the images of her gods lie shattered on the ground.” ^21-9

**21:10** O my threshed and winnowed one, what I have heard from the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, I announce to you. ^21-10

**21:11** The oracle concerning Dumah. One is calling to me from Seir, “Sentinel, what of the night? Sentinel, what of the night?” ^21-11

**21:12** The sentinel says: “Morning comes and also the night. If you will inquire, inquire; come back again.” ^21-12

**21:13** The oracle concerning the desert plain. In the scrub of the desert plain you will lodge, O caravans of Dedanites. ^21-13

**21:14** Bring water to the thirsty, O inhabitants of the land of Tema; meet the fugitive with bread. ^21-14

**21:15** For they have fled from the swords, from the drawn sword, from the bent bow, and from the stress of battle. ^21-15

**21:16** For thus the Lord said to me: “Within a year, according to the years of a hired worker, all the glory of Kedar will come to an end, ^21-16

**21:17** and the remaining bows of Kedar’s warriors will be few, for the LORD, the God of Israel, has spoken.” ^21-17
