# ἄρχων

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · noun

**Dodson:** a ruler, prince, leader — a ruler, governor, leader, leading man; with the Jews, an official member (a member of the executive) of the assembly of elders. (Strong's 758; headword ἄρχων, οντος, ὁ)
