## Mūla

नादो नादान्तवृत्त्या तु भित्त्वा ब्रह्मबिलं हठात् ।
शक्तितत्त्वे लयं याति निजकालपरिक्षये ॥ ^mula

nādo nādāntavṛttyā tu bhittvā brahmabilaṃ haṭhāt |
śaktitattve layaṃ yāti nijakālaparikṣaye || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IV
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 04 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v04 12 - Chapter Six, part 5#^pg392|p. 377]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 04.pdf#page=392>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 6.162cd–163ab.
>
> (When) Sound (Nāda) (comes to the end of its period of existence, it dissolves into the End of Sound – Nādānta). (Then,) having forcefully pierced through the Aperture of Brahmā by the dynamism of the End of Sound (nādāntavrttyā), (it dissolves) into the Principle of Energy (Śaktitattva) when its time comes to an end. One should know that a Day and Night in the Principle of Energy is this much (i.e. Sadāśiva’s lifespan). (with 6.162–163ab)
