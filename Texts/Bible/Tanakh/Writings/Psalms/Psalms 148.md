## Chapter 148

**148:1** Hallelujah.  
Praise the LORD from the heavens;  
praise Him on high. ^148-1

**148:2** Praise Him, all His angels,  
praise Him, all His hosts. ^148-2

**148:3** Praise Him, sun and moon,  
praise Him, all bright stars. ^148-3

**148:4** Praise Him, highest heavens,  
and you waters that are above the heavens. ^148-4

**148:5** Let them praise the name of the LORD,  
for it was He who commanded that they be created. ^148-5

**148:6** He made them endure forever,  
establishing an order that shall never change. ^148-6

**148:7** Praise the LORD, O you who are on earth,  
all sea monsters and ocean depths, ^148-7

**148:8** fire and hail, snow and smoke,  
storm wind that executes His command, ^148-8

**148:9** all mountains and hills,  
all fruit trees and cedars, ^148-9

**148:10** all wild and tamed beasts,  
creeping things and winged birds, ^148-10

**148:11** all kings and peoples of the earth,  
all princes of the earth and its judges, ^148-11

**148:12** youths and maidens alike,  
old and young together. ^148-12

**148:13** Let them praise the name of the LORD,  
for His name, His alone, is sublime;  
His splendor covers heaven and earth. ^148-13

**148:14** He has exalted the horn of His people  
for the glory of all His faithful ones,  
Israel, the people close to Him.  
Hallelujah. ^148-14
