# Psalm 4

← [[BCP Psalm 003]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 005]] →

*Cum invocarem* · Book One · First Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 004]]

**4:1** Answer me when I call, O God, defender of my cause; \*  
you set me free when I am hard-pressed;  
have mercy on me and hear my prayer. ^4-1

[p. 588]

**4:2** "You mortals, how long will you dishonor my glory; \*  
how long will you worship dumb idols  
and run after false gods?" ^4-2

**4:3** Know that the LORD does wonders for the faithful; \*  
when I call upon the LORD, he will hear me. ^4-3

**4:4** Tremble, then, and do not sin; \*  
speak to your heart in silence upon your bed. ^4-4

**4:5** Offer the appointed sacrifices \*  
and put your trust in the LORD. ^4-5

**4:6** Many are saying,  
"Oh, that we might see better times!" \*  
Lift up the light of your countenance upon us, O LORD. ^4-6

**4:7** You have put gladness in my heart, \*  
more than when grain and wine and oil increase. ^4-7

**4:8** I lie down in peace; at once I fall asleep; \*  
for only you, LORD, make me dwell in safety. ^4-8
