## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. V
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 05 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v05 05 - Chapter Eight, part 3#^pg160|p. 142]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 05.pdf#page=160>))
>
> The ocean of salt outside (that surrounds Jambūdvīpa) measures just one hundred thousand leagues. 1) The mountains, Rṣabha (Bull), Dundubhi (Kettle Drum) and Dhūmra (Smoke), are outside it to the east. 2) To the north are Kañka (Gold), Droṇa (Soma Vessel) and Indu (Moon). 3) To the west are Varāha (Boar), Nandana (Delightful) and Aśoka (Without Suffering). 4) In the south along with Balāhaka, are Cakra (Wheel) and Maināka. Vādava, the submarine fire, is located between these (last) two. (with 8.97–98)
