## Mūla

ज्ञानमन्त्रक्रियाध्यानबलात्कर्तुं भवेत्प्रभुः ।
अनयोत्क्रम्यते शिष्यो बलादेवैककं क्षणम् ॥ ^mula

jñānamantrakriyādhyānabalātkartuṃ bhavetprabhuḥ |
anayotkramyate śiṣyo balādevaikakaṃ kṣaṇam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 10 - Chapter Nineteen#^pg239|p. 219]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=239>))
>
> Although he has not practiced Yoga, the teacher is (the Lord,) who is capable (prabhu) of performing (the initiation) that bestows immediate exit (from the body), by the strength of (his) knowledge, Mantras, ritual action, and visualization (dhyāna). (with 19.26cd–27ab)
>
> By this (initiation), the disciple quits (the body) forcefully (balāt) in just a single moment of time. It may well be (that he must still) experience (the consequences of residual Karma, but that) lasts for (just) an instant —so what of that? There is another (initiation) that bestows immediate exit (from the body), in which (the teacher) should offer a full oblation, (but) that should be imparted (only) when the vital breaths (of the disciple) are firmly intent on quitting (the body). (with 19.27cd–29ab)
