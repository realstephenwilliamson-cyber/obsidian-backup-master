## Mūla

शिष्यदेहे च तत्पाशशिथिलत्वप्रसिद्धये ।
स हि स्वेच्छावशात्पाशान्विधुन्वन्निव वर्तते ॥ ^mula

śiṣyadehe ca tatpāśaśithilatvaprasiddhaye |
sa hi svecchāvaśātpāśānvidhunvanniva vartate || ^iast

## Translation

> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 05 - Chapter Sixteen, part 1#^pg60|p. 40]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=60>))

> 5) “And (I also) reside in the body of the disciple, in order to loosen his fetters.” He abides (thus), as if shaking off (his disciple’s) fetters by the power of his own will.
