# Ἕλλην

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · noun

**Dodson:** a Hellene, a Greek — a Hellene, the native word for a Greek; it is, however, a term wide enough to include all Greek-speaking (i.e. educated) non-Jews. (Strong's 1672; headword Ἕλλην, ηνος, ὁ)
