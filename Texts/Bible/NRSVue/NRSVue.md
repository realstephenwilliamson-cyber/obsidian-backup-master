# NRSVue

The New Revised Standard Version Updated Edition (2021), with the Deuterocanonical/Apocryphal books, converted on 13 October 2026 from Stephen's copy of the eBible edition (Friendship Press, e-ISBN 978-1-7330759-4-7). It is here for reading the Gospels side by side and beside the *Jewish Annotated New Testament*, whose text is the NRSV. One note per chapter, a link target for each verse (`[[NRSVue Mark 01#^1-1]]`), in the edition's order: 39 books of the Old Testament, 18 Deuterocanonical/Apocryphal books, 27 of the New Testament. 1,398 chapters, 37,760 verses.

- **Numbering.** English chapter and verse numbers, as in the BSB New Testament; each New Testament chapter links the BSB and Douay-Rheims chapter of the same number. A psalm's superscription stands, unnumbered and in italics, before verse 1. *Esther (Greek)* keeps the edition's order with the King James chapter numbers (11, 12, 1, 2 …); the edition's note: "(The Greek Version Containing the Additional Chapters) Note: The deuterocanonical portions of the book of Esther are several additional passages found in the Greek translation of the Hebrew book of Esther, a translation that differs also in other respects from the Hebrew text (the latter is translated in the NRSV Old Testament). The disordered chapter numbers come from the displacement of the additions to the end of the canonical book of Esther by Jerome in his Latin translation and from the subsequent division of the Bible into chapters by Stephen Langton, who numbered the additions consecutively as though they formed a direct continuation of the Hebrew text. So that the additions may be read in their proper context, the whole of the Greek version is here translated, though certain familiar names are given according to their Hebrew rather than their Greek form, for example, Mordecai and Vashti instead of Mardocheus and Astin. The order followed is that of the Greek text, but the chapter and verse numbers conform to those of the King James, or Authorized, Version. The additions, conveniently indicated by the letters A–F, are located as follows: A before 1.1; B after 3.13; C and D after 4.17; E after 8.12; F after 10.3." *Sirach* begins with its Prologue (`NRSVue Sirach Prologue`).
- **Text notes.** The edition's lettered notes (other readings, literal renderings, meaning uncertain) are footnotes at the word they belong to, 6,143 in all. The cross-references are not in the notes.
- **Headings** are the edition's section headings, put into sentence case (the eBible prints them in capitals). Where the edition sets a heading inside a verse (84 places, Genesis 2:4 among them), the verse is kept whole and the heading follows it.
- **Brackets.** Double brackets mark what the edition treats as later additions (1 Kings 4:20–34 and other places; Mark 16:9–20; John 7:53–8:11), as printed. Mark's intermediate ending follows 16:8, unnumbered (`^16-intermediate`); so does the Hebrew addition after Sirach 51:12 (`^51-heb`).
- **Verses this text leaves out** and gives only in a note (Matthew 17:21, 18:11, 23:14; Mark 7:16, 9:44, 9:46, 11:26, 15:28; Luke 17:36, 23:17; John 5:4; Acts 8:37, 15:34, 24:7, 28:29; Romans 16:24) have no note of their own; the edition's note stands at the verse before.
