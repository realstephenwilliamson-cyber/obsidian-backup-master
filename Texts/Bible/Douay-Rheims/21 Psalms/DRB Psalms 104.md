# Psalms 104 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 103|← Psalms 103]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 105|Psalms 105 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 104|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**104:1** Alleluia. Give glory to the Lord, and call upon his name: declare his deeds among the Gentiles. ^104-1

**104:2** Sing to him, yea sing praises to him: relate all his wondrous works. ^104-2

**104:3** Glory ye in his holy name: let the heart of them rejoice that seek the Lord. ^104-3

**104:4** Seek ye the Lord, and be strengthened: seek his face evermore. ^104-4

**104:5** Remember his marvellous works which he hath done; his wonders, and the judgments of his mouth. ^104-5

**104:6** O ye seed of Abraham his servant; ye sons of Jacob his chosen. ^104-6

**104:7** He is the Lord our God: his judgments are in all the earth. ^104-7

**104:8** He hath remembered his covenant for ever: the word which he commanded to a thousand generations. ^104-8

**104:9** Which he made to Abraham; and his oath to Isaac: ^104-9

**104:10** And he appointed the same to Jacob for a law, and to Israel for an everlasting testament: ^104-10

**104:11** Saying: To thee will I give the land of Chanaan, the lot of your inheritance. ^104-11

**104:12** When they were but a small number: yea very few, and sojourners therein: ^104-12

**104:13** And they passed from nation to nation, and from one kingdom to another people. ^104-13

**104:14** He suffered no man to hurt them: and he reproved kings for their sakes. ^104-14

**104:15** Touch ye not my anointed: and do no evil to my prophets. ^104-15

**104:16** And he called a famine upon the land: and he broke in pieces all the support of bread. ^104-16

**104:17** He sent a man before them: Joseph, who was sold for a slave. ^104-17

**104:18** They humbled his feet in fetters: the iron pierced his soul, ^104-18

**104:19** Until his word came. The word of the Lord inflamed him. ^104-19

**104:20** The king sent, and he released him: the ruler of the people, and he set him at liberty. ^104-20

**104:21** He made him master of his house, and ruler of all his possession. ^104-21

**104:22** That he might instruct his princes as himself, and teach his ancients wisdom. ^104-22

**104:23** And Israel went into Egypt: and Jacob was a sojourner in the land of Cham. ^104-23

**104:24** And he increased his people exceedingly: and strengthened them over their enemies, ^104-24

**104:25** He turned their heart to hate his people: and to deal deceitfully with his servants. ^104-25

**104:26** He sent Moses his servant: Aaron the man whom he had chosen. ^104-26

**104:27** He gave them power to shew his signs, and his wonders in the land of Cham. ^104-27

**104:28** He sent darkness, and made it obscure: and grieved not his words. ^104-28

**104:29** He turned their waters into blood, and destroyed their fish. ^104-29

**104:30** Their land brought forth frogs, in the inner chambers of their kings. ^104-30

**104:31** He spoke, and there came divers sorts of flies and sciniphs in all their coasts. ^104-31

**104:32** He gave them hail for rain, a burning fire in the land. ^104-32

**104:33** And he destroyed their vineyards and their fig trees: and he broke in pieces the trees of their coasts. ^104-33

**104:34** He spoke, and the locust came, and the bruchus, of which there was no number. ^104-34

**104:35** And they devoured all the grass in their land, and consumed all the fruit of their ground. ^104-35

**104:36** And he slew all the firstborn in their land: the firstfruits of all their labour. ^104-36

**104:37** And he brought them out with silver and gold: and there was not among their tribes one that was feeble. ^104-37

**104:38** Egypt was glad when they departed: for the fear of them lay upon them. ^104-38

**104:39** He spread a cloud for their protection, and fire to give them light in the night. ^104-39

**104:40** They asked, and the quail came: and he filled them with the bread of heaven. ^104-40

**104:41** He opened the rock, and waters flowed: rivers ran down in the dry land. ^104-41

**104:42** Because he remembered his holy word, which he had spoken to his servant Abraham. ^104-42

**104:43** And he brought forth his people with joy, and his chosen with gladness. ^104-43

**104:44** And he gave them the lands of the Gentiles: and they possessed the labours of the people: ^104-44

**104:45** That they might observe his justifications, and seek after his law. ^104-45
