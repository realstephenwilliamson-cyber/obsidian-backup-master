## Mūla

अन्यशक्तितिरोभावे कस्याश्चित्सुस्फुटोदये ।
भेदान्तरमपि ज्ञेयं वीणावादकदृष्टिवत् ॥ ^mula

anyaśaktitirobhāve kasyāścitsusphuṭodaye |
bhedāntaramapi jñeyaṃ vīṇāvādakadṛṣṭivat || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 06 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 12 - Chapter Ten, part 3#^pg412|p. 393]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 06.pdf#page=412>))
>
> Again, another type (bheda) is determined by the obscuration of one power and the (concomitant) clearly evident emergence of another, as happens, for example, to the sense of sight when someone plays the vīnā.

## Viveka

> [!viveka]- Viveka in English (Dyczkowski, vol. VI, p. 394)
> ![[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v06 12 - Chapter Ten, part 3#Viveka on 10.162]]

*Lead-in:* तदेवान्वयव्यतिरेकगर्भं दृष्टान्तद्वारेण द्रढयति --

> अन्यशक्तितिरोभावे कस्याश्चित्सुस्फुटोदये ।
> भेदान्तरमपि ज्ञेयं वीणावादकदृष्टिवत् ॥

वीणावादकस्य हि दृगादिशक्तिन्यग्भावे श्रोत्रशक्तेरेवोद्भवः ॥ १६२ ॥
