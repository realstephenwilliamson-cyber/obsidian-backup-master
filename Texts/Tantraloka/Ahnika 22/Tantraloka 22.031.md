## Mūla

श्रीदेव्या यामलीयोक्तितत्त्वसम्यक्प्रवेदकः ।
गुर्वन्तस्याप्यधोदृष्टिशायिनः संस्क्रियामिमाम् ॥ ^mula

śrīdevyā yāmalīyoktitattvasamyakpravedakaḥ |
gurvantasyāpyadhodṛṣṭiśāyinaḥ saṃskriyāmimām || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 13 - Chapter Twenty-Two#^pg306|p. 286]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=306>))
>
> The initiation (that bestows) liberation to those who previously bore the sectarian signs (of other traditions), does not carry with it the competence (to become a teacher). Converts are not competent to be on the path of an adept (sādhaka) or teacher (ācārya). The teacher Īśānaśiva, who correctly explains the true meaning of the teaching of the venerable Devyāyāmala, has declared in his Paddhati that ‘converts enflamed by knowledge occupy the office of a teacher (only) to liberate (their disciples, but) not to also confer worldly benefits (on them). (It is only by the use of) the appropriate means that (he can act so that they may) also (enjoy) worldly benefits. (with 22.28cd–31ab)
>
> Even (if he is) up to the (level of a) teacher, the secret (teaching) is only to be revealed to a follower of inferior doctrines once this rite of purification (saṁskriyā) has been performed — not otherwise. Indeed, (it is said) in the Kāmika: ‘even if one is consecrated (as a teacher) in another Tantra, the secret (teaching) should not be revealed (to him)’. (with 22.31cd–32)
