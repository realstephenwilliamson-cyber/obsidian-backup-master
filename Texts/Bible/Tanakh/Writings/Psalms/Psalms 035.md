## Chapter 35

**35:1** Of David.  
O LORD, strive with my adversaries,  
give battle to my foes, ^35-1

**35:2** take up shield and buckler,  
and come to my defense; ^35-2

**35:3** ready the spear and javelin  
against my pursuers;  
tell me, “I am your deliverance.” ^35-3

**35:4** Let those who seek my life  
be frustrated and put to shame;  
let those who plan to harm me  
fall back in disgrace. ^35-4

**35:5** Let them be as chaff in the wind,  
the LORD’s angel driving them on. ^35-5

**35:6** Let their path be dark and slippery,  
with the LORD’s angel in pursuit. ^35-6

**35:7** For without cause they hid a net to trap me;  
without cause they dug a pit[^1] for me. ^35-7

**35:8** Let disaster overtake them unawares;  
let the net they hid catch them;  
let them fall into it when disaster [strikes]. ^35-8

**35:9** Then shall I exult in the LORD,  
rejoice in His deliverance. ^35-9

**35:10** All my bones shall say,  
“LORD, who is like You?  
You save the poor from one stronger than he,  
the poor and needy from his despoiler.” ^35-10

**35:11** Malicious witnesses appear  
who question me about things I do not know. ^35-11

**35:12** They repay me evil for good,  
[seeking] my bereavement. ^35-12

**35:13** Yet, when they were ill,  
my dress was sackcloth,  
I kept a fast—  
may what I prayed for happen to me![^2] ^35-13

**35:14** I walked about as though it were my friend or my brother;  
I was bowed with gloom, like one mourning for his mother. ^35-14

**35:15** But when I stumble, they gleefully gather;  
wretches gather against me,  
I know not why;  
[^3]they tear at me without end. ^35-15

**35:16** With impious, mocking grimace  
they gnash their teeth at me. ^35-16

**35:17** O Lord, how long will You look on?  
Rescue me from their attacks,[^4]  
my precious life, from the lions, ^35-17

**35:18** that I may praise You in a great congregation,  
acclaim You in a mighty throng. ^35-18

**35:19** Let not my treacherous enemies rejoice over me,  
or those who hate me without reason wink their eyes. ^35-19

**35:20** For they do not offer amity,  
but devise fraudulent schemes against harmless folk. ^35-20

**35:21** They open wide their mouths at me,  
saying, “Aha, aha, we have seen it!” ^35-21

**35:22** You have seen it, O LORD;  
do not hold aloof!  
O Lord, be not far from me! ^35-22

**35:23** Wake, rouse Yourself for my cause,  
for my claim, O my God and my Lord! ^35-23

**35:24** Take up my cause, O LORD my God, as You are beneficent,  
and let them not rejoice over me. ^35-24

**35:25** Let them not think,  
“Aha, just what we wished!”  
Let them not say,  
“We have destroyed him!” ^35-25

**35:26** May those who rejoice at my misfortune  
be frustrated and utterly disgraced;  
may those who vaunt themselves over me  
be clad in frustration and shame. ^35-26

**35:27** May those who desire my vindication  
sing forth joyously;  
may they always say,  
“Extolled be the LORD  
who desires the well-being of His servant,” ^35-27

**35:28** while my tongue shall recite Your beneficent acts,  
Your praises all day long. ^35-28

[^1]: Transferred from first clause for clarity.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain; lit. “my prayer returns upon my bosom.”
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
