# Psalm 78 (NRSVue)

## God’s goodness and Israel’s ingratitude

*A Maskil of Asaph.*

**78:1** Give ear, O my people, to my teaching; incline your ears to the words of my mouth. ^78-1

**78:2** I will open my mouth in a parable; I will utter dark sayings from of old, ^78-2

**78:3** things that we have heard and known, that our ancestors have told us. ^78-3

**78:4** We will not hide them from their children; we will tell to the coming generation the glorious deeds of the LORD and his might and the wonders that he has done. ^78-4

**78:5** He established a decree in Jacob and appointed a law in Israel, which he commanded our ancestors to teach to their children, ^78-5

**78:6** that the next generation might know them, the children yet unborn, and rise up and tell them to their children, ^78-6

**78:7** so that they should set their hope in God, and not forget the works of God, but keep his commandments; ^78-7

**78:8** and that they should not be like their ancestors, a stubborn and rebellious generation, a generation whose heart was not steadfast, whose spirit was not faithful to God. ^78-8

**78:9** The Ephraimites, armed with^[Heb *armed with shooting*] the bow, turned back on the day of battle. ^78-9

**78:10** They did not keep God’s covenant and refused to walk according to his law. ^78-10

**78:11** They forgot what he had done and the miracles that he had shown them. ^78-11

**78:12** In the sight of their ancestors he worked marvels in the land of Egypt, in the fields of Zoan. ^78-12

**78:13** He divided the sea and let them pass through it and made the waters stand like a heap. ^78-13

**78:14** In the daytime he led them with a cloud and all night long with a fiery light. ^78-14

**78:15** He split rocks open in the wilderness and gave them drink abundantly as from the deep. ^78-15

**78:16** He made streams come out of the rock and caused waters to flow down like rivers. ^78-16

**78:17** Yet they sinned still more against him, rebelling against the Most High in the desert. ^78-17

**78:18** They tested God in their heart by demanding the food they craved. ^78-18

**78:19** They spoke against God, saying, “Can God spread a table in the wilderness? ^78-19

**78:20** Even though he struck the rock so that water gushed out and torrents overflowed, can he also give bread or provide meat for his people?” ^78-20

**78:21** Therefore, when the LORD heard, he was full of rage; a fire was kindled against Jacob, his anger mounted against Israel, ^78-21

**78:22** because they had no faith in God and did not trust his saving power. ^78-22

**78:23** Yet he commanded the skies above and opened the doors of heaven; ^78-23

**78:24** he rained down on them manna to eat and gave them the grain of heaven. ^78-24

**78:25** Mortals ate of the bread of angels; he sent them food in abundance. ^78-25

**78:26** He caused the east wind to blow in the heavens, and by his power he led out the south wind; ^78-26

**78:27** he rained flesh upon them like dust, winged birds like the sand of the seas; ^78-27

**78:28** he let them fall within their camp, all around their dwellings. ^78-28

**78:29** And they ate and were well filled, for he gave them what they craved. ^78-29

**78:30** But before they had satisfied their craving, while the food was still in their mouths, ^78-30

**78:31** the anger of God rose against them, and he killed the strongest of them and laid low the flower of Israel. ^78-31

**78:32** In spite of all this they still sinned; they did not believe in his wonders. ^78-32

**78:33** So he made their days vanish like a breath and their years in terror. ^78-33

**78:34** When he killed them, they searched for him; they repented and sought God earnestly. ^78-34

**78:35** They remembered that God was their rock, the Most High God their redeemer. ^78-35

**78:36** But they flattered him with their mouths; they lied to him with their tongues. ^78-36

**78:37** Their heart was not steadfast toward him; they were not true to his covenant. ^78-37

**78:38** Yet he, being compassionate, forgave their iniquity and did not destroy them; often he restrained his anger and did not stir up all his wrath. ^78-38

**78:39** He remembered that they were but flesh, a wind that passes and does not come again. ^78-39

**78:40** How often they rebelled against him in the wilderness and grieved him in the desert! ^78-40

**78:41** They tested God again and again and provoked the Holy One of Israel. ^78-41

**78:42** They did not keep in mind his power or the day when he redeemed them from the foe, ^78-42

**78:43** when he displayed his signs in Egypt and his miracles in the fields of Zoan. ^78-43

**78:44** He turned their rivers to blood, so that they could not drink of their streams. ^78-44

**78:45** He sent among them swarms of flies that devoured them and frogs that destroyed them. ^78-45

**78:46** He gave their crops to the caterpillar and the fruit of their labor to the locust. ^78-46

**78:47** He destroyed their vines with hail and their sycamores with frost. ^78-47

**78:48** He gave over their cattle to the hail and their flocks to thunderbolts. ^78-48

**78:49** He let loose on them his fierce anger, wrath, indignation, and distress, a company of destroying angels. ^78-49

**78:50** He made a path for his anger; he did not spare them from death but gave their lives over to the plague. ^78-50

**78:51** He struck all the firstborn in Egypt, the first issue of their strength in the tents of Ham. ^78-51

**78:52** Then he led out his people like sheep and guided them in the wilderness like a flock. ^78-52

**78:53** He led them in safety so that they were not afraid, but the sea overwhelmed their enemies. ^78-53

**78:54** And he brought them to his holy hill, to the mountain that his right hand had won. ^78-54

**78:55** He drove out nations before them; he apportioned them for a possession and settled the tribes of Israel in their tents. ^78-55

**78:56** Yet they tested the Most High God and rebelled against him. They did not observe his decrees ^78-56

**78:57** but turned away and were faithless like their ancestors; they twisted like a treacherous bow. ^78-57

**78:58** For they provoked him to anger with their high places; they moved him to jealousy with their idols. ^78-58

**78:59** When God heard, he was full of wrath, and he utterly rejected Israel. ^78-59

**78:60** He abandoned his dwelling at Shiloh, the tent where he dwelt among mortals, ^78-60

**78:61** and delivered his power to captivity, his glory to the hand of the foe. ^78-61

**78:62** He gave his people to the sword and vented his wrath on his heritage. ^78-62

**78:63** Fire devoured their young men, and their young women had no marriage song. ^78-63

**78:64** Their priests fell by the sword, and their widows made no lamentation. ^78-64

**78:65** Then the Lord awoke as from sleep, like a warrior shouting because of wine. ^78-65

**78:66** He put his adversaries to rout; he put them to everlasting disgrace. ^78-66

**78:67** He rejected the tent of Joseph; he did not choose the tribe of Ephraim, ^78-67

**78:68** but he chose the tribe of Judah, Mount Zion, which he loves. ^78-68

**78:69** He built his sanctuary like the high heavens, like the earth, which he has founded forever. ^78-69

**78:70** He chose his servant David and took him from the sheepfolds; ^78-70

**78:71** from tending the nursing ewes he brought him to be the shepherd of his people Jacob, of Israel, his inheritance. ^78-71

**78:72** With upright heart he tended them and guided them with skillful hand. ^78-72
