# Zechariah 2 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Zechariah 01|← Zechariah 1]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Zechariah 03|Zechariah 3 →]]

## Third vision: The man with a measuring line

**2:1** ^[2.5 in Heb]I looked up and saw a man with a measuring line in his hand. ^2-1

**2:2** Then I asked, “Where are you going?” He answered me, “To measure Jerusalem, to see how wide and how long it is.” ^2-2

**2:3** Then the angel who spoke with me came forward, and another angel came forward to meet him ^2-3

**2:4** and said to him, “Run, say to that young man: Jerusalem shall be inhabited like unwalled villages because of the multitude of people and animals in it. ^2-4

**2:5** For I will be a wall of fire all around it, says the LORD, and I will be the glory within it.” ^2-5

## Interlude: An appeal to the exiles

**2:6** Up, up! Flee from the land of the north, says the LORD, for I have spread you abroad like the four winds of heaven, says the LORD. ^2-6

**2:7** Up! Escape to Zion, you who live with daughter Babylon. ^2-7

**2:8** For thus said the LORD of hosts after his glory sent me to the nations who plundered you: Truly, one who touches you touches the apple of my eye.^[Heb *his eye*] ^2-8

**2:9** For I am going to raise^[Or *wave*] my hand against them, and they shall become plunder for their own slaves. Then you will know that the LORD of hosts has sent me. ^2-9

**2:10** Sing and rejoice, O daughter Zion! For I will come and dwell in your midst, says the LORD. ^2-10

**2:11** Many nations shall join themselves to the LORD on that day and shall be my people, and I will dwell in your midst. And you shall know that the LORD of hosts has sent me to you. ^2-11

**2:12** The LORD will inherit Judah as his portion in the holy land and will again choose Jerusalem. ^2-12

**2:13** Be silent, all flesh, before the LORD, for he has roused himself from his holy dwelling. ^2-13
