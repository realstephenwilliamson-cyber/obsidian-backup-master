# Psalm 86 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 085|← Psalm 85]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 087|Psalm 87 →]]

## Supplication for help against enemies

*A Prayer of David.*

**86:1** Incline your ear, O LORD, and answer me, for I am poor and needy. ^86-1

**86:2** Preserve my life, for I am devoted to you; save your servant who trusts in you. You are my God; ^86-2

**86:3** be gracious to me, O Lord, for to you do I cry all day long. ^86-3

**86:4** Gladden the soul of your servant, for to you, O Lord, I lift up my soul. ^86-4

**86:5** For you, O Lord, are good and forgiving, abounding in steadfast love to all who call on you. ^86-5

**86:6** Give ear, O LORD, to my prayer; listen to my cry of supplication. ^86-6

**86:7** In the day of my trouble I call on you, for you will answer me. ^86-7

**86:8** There is none like you among the gods, O Lord, nor are there any works like yours. ^86-8

**86:9** All the nations you have made shall come and bow down before you, O Lord, and shall glorify your name. ^86-9

**86:10** For you are great and do wondrous things; you alone are God. ^86-10

**86:11** Teach me your way, O LORD, that I may walk in your truth; give me an undivided heart to revere your name. ^86-11

**86:12** I give thanks to you, O Lord my God, with my whole heart, and I will glorify your name forever. ^86-12

**86:13** For great is your steadfast love toward me; you have delivered my soul from the depths of Sheol. ^86-13

**86:14** O God, the insolent rise up against me; a band of ruffians seeks my life, and they do not set you before them. ^86-14

**86:15** But you, O Lord, are a God merciful and gracious, slow to anger and abounding in steadfast love and faithfulness. ^86-15

**86:16** Turn to me and be gracious to me; give your strength to your servant; save the child of your maidservant. ^86-16

**86:17** Show me a sign of your favor, so that those who hate me may see it and be put to shame, because you, LORD, have helped me and comforted me. ^86-17
