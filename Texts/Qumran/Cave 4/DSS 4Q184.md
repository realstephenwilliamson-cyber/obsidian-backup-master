### Frag. 1

**1** She […] utters futility and in […]. She is always looking for depravities, [and] whets the word[s of her mouth, …] ^f1-1

**2** and implies insult, and is [bu]sy leading the community astray with non[sense.] Her heart weaves traps, her kidneys n[ets. Her eyes] ^f1-2

**3** have been deﬁled with evil, her hands grasp the pit, her feet descend to act wickedly and to walk in crimes. […] ^f1-3

**4** (are) foundations of darkness, and there are plenty of sins in her wings. Her […] (are) night gloom and her clothes [(are) …] ^f1-4

**5** Her veils are shadows of the twilight and her adornments diseases of the pit. Her beds {her couches} are couches of the pit, [ …] ^f1-5

**6** (are) deep ditches. Her lodgings are couches of darkness and in the heart of the nigh[t] are her tents. In the foundations of gloom ^f1-6

**7** she sets up her dwelling, and camps in the tents of silence, in the midst of eternal ﬁre. She has no inheritance among all ^f1-7

**8** those who shine {.} brightly. She is the start of all the ways of wickedness. Alas! She is the ruination of all who inherit her, and the calamity of a[ll ] ^f1-8

**9** who grasp her. For her paths are paths of death, and her roads are tracks to sin. Her trails lead astray ^f1-9

**10** towards wickedness, and her pathways to the guilt of transgression. Her gates are the gates of death, in the entrance to her house Sheo[l] proceeds. ^f1-10

**11** A[l]l [those who go to her will not] come back, and all those who inherit her will descend to the pit. S[h]e hides in ambush in secret places, […] ^f1-11

**12** al[l …] In the city squares she veils herself, and in the gates of the village she stations herself, and there is no-one who can ke[ep her] ^f1-12

**13** from (her) incessant [fornicat]ing. Her eyes scan hither and thither, and she raises her eyebrows impudently, to spot a ^f1-13

**14** just ma[n] and overtake him, and a [no]ble man, to trip him up; the upright to turn (from) the path, the righteous chosen ones ^f1-14

**15** from keeping the precept, to make those with a steady [mind] ridiculous with recklessness, and those who walk uprightly to alter the ordi[na]nce. To make ^f1-15

**16** the simple rebel against God, to turn their steps off the paths of justice, to bring presumpt[uous]ness into their [hea]rt, so that they do not walk ^f1-16

**17** in the paths of uprightness. To sidetrack man into the ways of the pit, and seduce the sons of men with smooth words. ^f1-17
