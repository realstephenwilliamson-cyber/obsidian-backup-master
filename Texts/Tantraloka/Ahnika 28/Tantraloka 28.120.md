## Mūla

वृथा दीक्षा वृथा ज्ञानं गुर्वाराधनमेव च ।
विना पवित्राद्येनैतद्धरेन्नागः शिवाज्ञया ॥ ^mula

vṛthā dīkṣā vṛthā jñānaṃ gurvārādhanameva ca |
vinā pavitrādyenaitaddharennāgaḥ śivājñayā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 04 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 2#^pg80|p. 63]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=80>))
>
> ‘Stunned (at that time) by the sleep induced by the poison produced by churning of the milky ocean, (Vāsuki,) the king of the Nāgas, did not reside in his own world during the rainy season. Only this (Nāga called) Pavitra (was there). He worshipped Lord Bhairava and fasted for ten times a hundred divine years. He then said the following to the Lord, who was (thus) satisfied (by him): “I cannot remain in my own world during the rainy season.” (And so) that Supreme Lord (parameśvara) then took that Nāga and placed him in the sacred seat (on the top) of (his) matted hair. Then, all the hosts of gods (also) placed him on their own heads. It is not surprising if great and famous beings are honoured by everybody. Therefore, after having completed (the appropriate) sacrifice, (the teacher etc.) should always place a sacred thread on Maheśvara’s head and on that of (all the other) deities, as well as on his own. Ten times ten million rites of worship are not equal to (one) offering of the sacred thread. Without (offering) the sacred thread, initiation, knowledge, and the worship of the teacher are (all) in vain. It is due to that (omission) that the Nāga steals one away. Thus at Śiva’s command those who know Kula (doctrine) must perform (this rite of the sacred thread) with all effort.’ (with 28.114–121ab)

## Viveka

> [!viveka]- Viveka in English (Dyczkowski, vol. X, pp. 63–64)
> ![[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 04 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 2#Viveka on 28.114–121]]

> क्षीराब्धिमथनोद्भूतविषनिद्राविमूर्च्छितः ।
> नागराजः स्वभुवने मेघकाले स्म नावसत् ॥
> केवलं तु पवित्रोऽयं वायुभक्षः समाः शतम् ।
> दिव्यं दशगुणं नाथं भैरवं पर्यपूजयत् ॥
> व्यजिज्ञपच्च तं तुष्टं नाथं वर्षास्वहं निजे ।
> पाताले नासितुं शक्तः सोऽप्येनं परमेश्वरः ॥
> नागं निजजटाजूटपीठगं पर्यकल्पयत् ।
> ततः समस्तदेवौघैर्धारितोऽसौ स्वमूर्धनि ॥
> महतां महितानां हि नाद्भुता विश्वपूज्यता ।
> तस्मान्महेशितुर्मूर्ध्नि देवतानां च सर्वशः ॥
> आत्मनश्च पवित्रं तं कुर्याद्यागपुरःसरम् ।
> दश कोट्यो न पूजानां पवित्रारोहणे समाः ॥
> वृथा दीक्षा वृथा ज्ञानं गुर्वाराधनमेव च ।
> विना पवित्राद्येनैतद्धरेन्नागः शिवाज्ञया ॥
> तस्मात्सर्वप्रयत्नेन स कार्यः कुलवेदिभिः ।

पवित्रोऽयमिति तच्छब्दव्यपदेश्यः--इत्यर्थः । यदुक्तम्--

"तेनास्म्याराधितो देवि पवित्रेण महात्मना ।"

इति,

"पवित्रो नाम नागेन्द्रो ज्येष्ठो भ्रतास्ति वासुकेः ।"

इति च । पवित्रेणेति पाठे तु पञ्चगव्यादिनेति--व्याख्येयम् । समाः शतं दशगुणमिति वर्षसहस्रम्--इत्यर्थः । तदुक्तम्--

"दिव्यवर्षसहस्रं तु वायुभक्षो महाबलः ।"

इति । कुर्यादिति--गुर्वादिः । दशेत्यादिना प्रयोजनमुक्तम् ॥
