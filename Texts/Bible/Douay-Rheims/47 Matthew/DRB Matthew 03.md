# Matthew 3 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Matthew 02|← Matthew 2]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Matthew 04|Matthew 4 →]]

Compare: [[Matthew 03|BSB and SBLGNT]]

**3:1** And in those days cometh John the Baptist preaching in the desert of Judea. ^3-1

**3:2** And saying: Do penance: for the kingdom of heaven is at hand. ^3-2

**3:3** For this is he that was spoken of by Isaias the prophet, saying: A voice of one crying in the desert, Prepare ye the way of the Lord, make straight his paths. ^3-3

**3:4** And the same John had his garment of camels’ hair, and a leathern girdle about his loins: and his meat was locusts and wild honey. ^3-4

**3:5** Then went out to him Jerusalem and all Judea, and all the country about Jordan: ^3-5

**3:6** And were baptized by him in the Jordan, confessing their sins. ^3-6

**3:7** And seeing many of the Pharisees and Sadducees coming to his baptism, he said to them: Ye brood of vipers, who hath shewed you to flee from the wrath to come? ^3-7

**3:8** Bring forth therefore fruit worthy of penance. ^3-8

**3:9** And think not to say within yourselves, We have Abraham for our father. For I tell you that God is able of these stones to raise up children to Abraham. ^3-9

**3:10** For now the axe is laid to the root of the trees. Every tree therefore that doth not yield good fruit, shall be cut down, and cast into the fire. ^3-10

**3:11** I indeed baptize you in the water unto penance, but he that shall come after me, is mightier than I, whose shoes I am not worthy to bear; he shall baptize you in the Holy Ghost and fire. ^3-11

**3:12** Whose fan is in his hand, and he will thoroughly cleanse his floor and gather his wheat into the barn; but the chaff he will burn with unquenchable fire. ^3-12

**3:13** Then cometh Jesus from Galilee to the Jordan, unto John, to be baptized by him. ^3-13

**3:14** But John stayed him, saying: I ought to be baptized by thee, and comest thou to me? ^3-14

**3:15** And Jesus answering, said to him: Suffer it to be so now. For so it becometh us to fulfill all justice. Then he suffered him. ^3-15

**3:16** And Jesus being baptized, forthwith came out of the water: and lo, the heavens were opened to him: and he saw the Spirit of God descending as a dove, and coming upon him. ^3-16

**3:17** And behold a voice from heaven, saying: This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased. ^3-17
