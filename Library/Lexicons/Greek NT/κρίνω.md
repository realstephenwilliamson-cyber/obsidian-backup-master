# κρίνω

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · verb

**Dodson:** I judge, decide, think good — (a) I judge, whether in a law-court or privately: sometimes with cognate nouns emphasizing the notion of the verb, (b) I decide, I think (it) good. (Strong's 2919; headword κρίνω)
