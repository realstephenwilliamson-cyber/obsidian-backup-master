# Job 36 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Job 35|← Job 35]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Job 37|Job 37 →]]

## Elihu exalts God’s goodness

**36:1** Elihu continued and said: ^36-1

**36:2** “Bear with me a little, and I will show you, for I have yet something to say on God’s behalf. ^36-2

**36:3** I will bring my knowledge from far away and ascribe righteousness to my Maker. ^36-3

**36:4** For truly my words are not false; one who is perfect in knowledge is with you. ^36-4

**36:5** “Surely God is mighty and does not despise any; he is mighty in strength of understanding. ^36-5

**36:6** He does not keep the wicked alive but gives the afflicted their right. ^36-6

**36:7** He does not withdraw his eyes from the righteous, but with kings on the throne he sets them forever, and they are exalted. ^36-7

**36:8** And if they are bound in fetters and caught in the cords of affliction, ^36-8

**36:9** then he declares to them their work and their transgressions, that they are behaving arrogantly. ^36-9

**36:10** He opens their ears to instruction and commands that they return from iniquity. ^36-10

**36:11** If they listen and serve him, they complete their days in prosperity and their years in pleasantness. ^36-11

**36:12** But if they do not listen, they shall perish by the sword and die without knowledge. ^36-12

**36:13** “The godless in heart cherish anger; they do not cry for help when he binds them. ^36-13

**36:14** They die in their youth, and their life ends in shame.^[Heb *ends among the prostitutes*] ^36-14

**36:15** He delivers the afflicted by their affliction and opens their ear by adversity. ^36-15

**36:16** He also allured you out of distress into a broad place where there was no constraint, and what was set on your table was full of fatness. ^36-16

**36:17** “But you are obsessed with the case of the wicked; judgment and justice seize you. ^36-17

**36:18** Beware that wrath does not entice you into scoffing, and do not let the greatness of the ransom turn you aside. ^36-18

**36:19** Will your cry avail to keep you from distress, or will all the force of your strength? ^36-19

**36:20** Do not long for the night, when peoples are cut off in their place. ^36-20

**36:21** Beware! Do not turn to iniquity; because of that you have been tried by affliction. ^36-21

**36:22** See, God is exalted in his power; who is a teacher like him? ^36-22

**36:23** Who has prescribed for him his way, or who can say, ‘You have done wrong’? ^36-23

## Elihu proclaims God’s majesty

**36:24** “Remember to extol his work, of which mortals have sung. ^36-24

**36:25** All people have looked on it; everyone watches it from far away. ^36-25

**36:26** Surely God is great, and we do not know him; the number of his years is unsearchable. ^36-26

**36:27** For he draws up the drops of water; he distills^[Cn: Heb *they distill*] his mist in rain, ^36-27

**36:28** which the skies pour down and drop upon mortals abundantly. ^36-28

**36:29** Can anyone understand the spreading of the clouds, the thunderings of his pavilion? ^36-29

**36:30** See, he scatters his lightning around him and covers the roots of the sea. ^36-30

**36:31** For by these he governs peoples; he gives food in abundance. ^36-31

**36:32** He covers his hands with the lightning and commands it to strike the mark. ^36-32

**36:33** Its crashing^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] tells about him; he is jealous^[Meaning of Heb uncertain] with anger against iniquity. ^36-33
