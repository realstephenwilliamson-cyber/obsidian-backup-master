## Mūla

तस्यैतद्वासना हेतुः काकतालीयवत् स तु ।
ननु कस्मात्तदेवैष स्मरति इत्याह-यत्सदा ॥ ^mula

tasyaitadvāsanā hetuḥ kākatālīyavat sa tu |
nanu kasmāttadevaiṣa smarati ityāha-yatsadā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 06 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 4#^pg169|p. 152]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=169>))
>
> Creative imagination (bhāvana) is said to be a means of gradually rendering evident (unmanifest realities). A thing that is clearly evident is experienced (directly). It is clear that this is not imagination (bhāvana). (with 28.345cd–349ab)
>
> Surely (one may ask), how is it that he recollects just that (and not something else)? (with 28.346c)
>
> (The Lord) has said, it is sufficient that he be constantly steeped in that state (fadbhāvabhāvita) (to explain why) he recollects just that (and not anything else at the moment of death) sufficiently (to be reborn as he desires). (with 28.346d–347ab)
