## Chapter 114

**114:1** When Israel went forth from Egypt,  
the house of Jacob from a people of strange speech, ^114-1

**114:2** Judah became His holy one,[^1]  
Israel, His dominion. ^114-2

**114:3** The sea saw them and fled,  
Jordan ran backward, ^114-3

**114:4** mountains skipped like rams,  
hills like sheep. ^114-4

**114:5** What alarmed you, O sea, that you fled,  
Jordan, that you ran backward, ^114-5

**114:6** mountains, that you skipped like rams,  
hills, like sheep? ^114-6

**114:7** Tremble, O earth, at the presence of the Lord,  
at the presence of the God of Jacob, ^114-7

**114:8** who turned the rock into a pool of water,  
the flinty rock into a fountain. ^114-8

[^1]: Or “sanctuary.”
