## Mūla

देहपाते पुनः प्रेप्सेद्यदि तत्त्वेषु कुत्रचित् ।
भोगान् समस्तव्यस्तत्वभेदैरन्ते परं पदम् ॥ ^mula

dehapāte punaḥ prepsedyadi tattveṣu kutracit |
bhogān samastavyastatvabhedairante paraṃ padam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 08 - Chapter Seventeen#^pg199|p. 179]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=199>))
>
> If, on the contrary, (the disciple) were to desire to obtain worldly benefits after death somewhere within the reality levels (tattva), whether in general or in particular, and (then) finally the supreme place, (the teacher), deliberating (saṁkhyā) on the plane of that reality level, with his mind intent on nothing else, should perform the rite that conjoins (the disciple) with it by means of another full oblation. (with 17.92–93)
