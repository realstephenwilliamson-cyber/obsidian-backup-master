## Mūla

तदेव तर्पणं मुख्यं भोग्यभोक्त्रात्मतैव सा ।
अग्निसंपुटफुल्लार्णत्र्यश्रकालात्मको महान् ॥ ^mula

tadeva tarpaṇaṃ mukhyaṃ bhogyabhoktrātmataiva sā |
agnisaṃpuṭaphullārṇatryaśrakālātmako mahān || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 05 - Chapter Sixteen, part 1#^pg40|p. 20]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=40>))
>
> The (animal’s) soul should be made one (samarasa) with the wheel of the goddesses by imagining (it to be so) (bhāvanāt). That itself is the principal (and supreme) libation (tarpaṇa), which is a state of (oneness) of the enjoyer and the object of enjoyment. (with 16.47cd–48ab)
>
> The great syllabic Mantra (piṇḍa) (that is used to) move and draw out (ākarṣaṇa) etc. the flux of the (vital) essences, such as blood and the like (in the animal’s body), consists of three letters – the Blooming Phoneme (PH) encapsulated by Fire (R), the Triangle (E) and Time (Ṁ) (i.e RPHREṀ). (with 16.48cd–49ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* ननु किमेतत्समन्त्रकं कार्यं न वा?--इत्याशङ्क्याह--

> अग्निसंपुटफुल्लार्णत्र्यश्रकालात्मको महान् ॥
> पिण्डो रक्तादिसारौघचालनाकर्षणादिषु ।

अग्निः रेफः, फुल्लार्णः फकारः, त्र्यश्रमेकारः, कालो मकारः, एवं फ्रेम् । तदुक्तम्--

"क्रोधो वह्निपुटान्तस्थस्त्र्यश्रकालविभेदितः ।

सेयं रक्तादिसारौधकर्षणे क्षुरिका मता ॥"

इति ॥
