## Mūla

विकल्पः किल सञ्जल्पमयो यत्स विमर्शकः ।
मन्त्रात्मासौ विमर्शश्च शुद्धोऽपाशवतात्मकः ॥ ^mula

vikalpaḥ kila sañjalpamayo yatsa vimarśakaḥ |
mantrātmāsau vimarśaśca śuddho'pāśavatātmakaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 07 - Chapter Sixteen, part 3#^pg129|p. 109]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=129>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 16.249cd–250ab.
>
> Indeed, thought (vikalpa) is made of (inner) discourse (saṁjalpamaya) which reflects (on the nature of its object) (vimarśaka). And that is the essential nature of Mantra and reflective awareness (vimarśa), which is pure, and not the fettered state. Eternal, it is conceived (upakalpita) to be one with Śiva, Who, without beginning, is the dispenser of Crey) boon; by union (yoga) with that, the teacher’s thought also attains Śiva’s state. (with 16.250–251)
