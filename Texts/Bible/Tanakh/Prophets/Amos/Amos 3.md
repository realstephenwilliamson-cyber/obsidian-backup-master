# Amos 3

Compare: [[NRSVue Amos 03|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 3

**3:1** Hear this word, O people of Israel,  
That the LORD has spoken concerning you,  
Concerning the whole family that I brought up from the land of Egypt: ^3-1

**3:2** You alone have I singled out  
Of all the families of the earth—  
That is why I will call you to account  
For all your iniquities. ^3-2

**3:3** Can two walk together  
Without having met? ^3-3

**3:4** Does a lion roar in the forest  
When he has no prey?  
Does a great beast let out a cry from its den  
Without having made a capture? ^3-4

**3:5** Does a bird drop on the ground—in a trap—  
With no snare there?  
Does a trap spring up from the ground  
Unless it has caught something? ^3-5

**3:6** When a ram’s horn is sounded in a town,  
Do the people not take alarm?  
Can misfortune come to a town  
If the LORD has not caused it? ^3-6

**3:7** Indeed, my Lord GOD does nothing  
Without having revealed His purpose  
To His servants the prophets. ^3-7

**3:8** A lion has roared,  
Who can but fear?  
My Lord GOD has spoken,  
Who can but prophesy? ^3-8

**3:9** Proclaim in the fortresses of Ashdod[^1]  
And in the fortresses of the land of Egypt!  
Say:  
Gather on the hill[^2] of Samaria  
And witness the great outrages within her  
And the oppression in her midst. ^3-9

**3:10** They are incapable of doing right  
—declares the LORD;  
They store up lawlessness and rapine  
In their fortresses. ^3-10

**3:11** Assuredly,  
Thus said my Lord GOD:  
An enemy, all about the land!  
He shall strip you of your splendor,  
And your fortresses shall be plundered. ^3-11

**3:12** Thus said the LORD:  
As a shepherd rescues from the lion’s jaws  
Two shank bones or the tip of an ear,  
So shall the Israelites escape  
Who dwell in Samaria—  
With the leg[^3] of a bed or the head[^4] of a couch. ^3-12

**3:13** Hear [this], and warn the House of Jacob  
—says my Lord GOD, the God of Hosts— ^3-13

**3:14** That when I punish Israel for its transgressions,  
I will wreak judgment on the altar[^5] of Bethel,  
And the horns of the altar shall be cut off  
And shall fall to the ground. ^3-14

**3:15** I will wreck the winter palace  
Together with the summer palace;  
The ivory palaces shall be demolished,  
And the great houses shall be destroyed  
—declares the LORD. ^3-15

[^1]: Septuagint reads “Assyria.”
[^2]: Heb. plural; but cf. 4.1; 6.1.
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^4]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^5]: Heb. plural, but cf. “altar” in next line.
