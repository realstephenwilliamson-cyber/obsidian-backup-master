## Chapter 81

**81:1** For the leader; on the *gittith*. Of Asaph. ^81-1

**81:2** Sing joyously to God, our strength;  
raise a shout for the God of Jacob. ^81-2

**81:3** Take up the song,  
sound the timbrel,  
the melodious lyre and harp. ^81-3

**81:4** Blow the horn on the new moon,  
on the full moon for our feast day. ^81-4

**81:5** For it is a law for Israel,  
a ruling of the God of Jacob; ^81-5

**81:6** He imposed it as a decree upon Joseph  
when he went forth from[^1] the land of Egypt;  
I heard a language that I knew not. ^81-6

**81:7** I relieved his shoulder of the burden,  
his hands were freed from the basket. ^81-7

**81:8** In distress you called and I rescued you;  
I answered you from the secret place of thunder[^2]  
I tested you at the waters of Meribah. *Selah*. ^81-8

**81:9** Hear, My people, and I will admonish you;  
Israel, if you would but listen to Me! ^81-9

**81:10** You shall have no foreign god,  
you shall not bow to an alien god. ^81-10

**81:11** I the LORD am your God  
who brought you out of the land of Egypt;  
open your mouth wide and I will fill it. ^81-11

**81:12** But My people would not listen to Me,  
Israel would not obey Me. ^81-12

**81:13** So I let them go after their willful heart  
that they might follow their own devices. ^81-13

**81:14** If only My people would listen to Me,  
if Israel would follow My paths, ^81-14

**81:15** then would I subdue their enemies at once,  
strike their foes again and again. ^81-15

**81:16** Those who hate the LORD shall cower before Him;  
their doom shall be eternal. ^81-16

**81:17** He fed them[^3] the finest wheat;  
I sated you with honey from the rock. ^81-17

[^1]: Or “He went forth against.”
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Lit. “him,” i.e., Israel.
