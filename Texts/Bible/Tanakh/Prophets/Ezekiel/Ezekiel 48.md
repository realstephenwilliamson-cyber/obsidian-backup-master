# Ezekiel 48

← [[Ezekiel 47]] · [[Tanakh]]

Compare: [[NRSVue Ezekiel 48|NRSVue]]

## Chapter 48

**48:1** These are the names of the tribes:  
At the northern end, along the Hethlon road, [from] Lebo-hamath to Hazar-enan—which is the border of Damascus, with Hamath to the north—from the eastern border to the Sea: Dan—one [tribe]. ^48-1

**48:2** Adjoining the territory of Dan, from the eastern border to the western border: Asher—one. ^48-2

**48:3** Adjoining the territory of Asher, from the eastern border to the western border: Naphtali—one. ^48-3

**48:4** Adjoining the territory of Naphtali, from the eastern border to the western border: Manasseh—one. ^48-4

**48:5** Adjoining the territory of Manasseh, from the eastern border to the western border: Ephraim—one. ^48-5

**48:6** Adjoining the territory of Ephraim, from the eastern border to the western border: Reuben—one. ^48-6

**48:7** Adjoining the territory of Reuben, from the eastern border to the western border: Judah—one. ^48-7

**48:8** Adjoining the territory of Judah, from the eastern border to the western border, shall be the reserve that you set aside: 25,000 [cubits] in breadth and in length equal to one of the portions from the eastern border to the western border; the Sanctuary shall be in the middle of it. ^48-8

**48:9** The reserve that you set aside for the LORD shall be 25,000 long and 10,000[^1] wide. ^48-9

**48:10** It shall be apportioned to the following: The sacred reserve for the priests shall measure 25,000 [cubits] on the north, 10,000 on the west, 10,000 on the east, and 25,000 on the south,[^2] with the LORD’s Sanctuary in the middle of it. ^48-10

**48:11** This consecrated area shall be for the priests of the line of Zadok, who kept My charge and did not go astray, as the Levites did when the people of Israel went astray. ^48-11

**48:12** It shall be a special reserve for them out of the [total] reserve from the land, most holy, adjoining the territory of the Levites. ^48-12

**48:13** Alongside the territory of the priests, the Levites shall have [an area] 25,000 long by 10,000 wide; the total length shall be 25,000 and the breadth 10,000.[^3] ^48-13

**48:14** None of it—the choicest of the land—may be sold, exchanged, or transferred; it is sacred to the LORD. ^48-14

**48:15** The remaining 5,000 in breadth by 25,000 shall be for common use—serving the city for dwellings and pasture. The city itself shall be in the middle of it; ^48-15

**48:16** and these shall be its measurements: On the north side 4,500 cubits, on the south side 4,500, on the east side 4,500, and on the west side 4,500. ^48-16

**48:17** The pasture shall extend 250 cubits to the north of the city, 250 to the south, 250 to the east, and 250 to the west. ^48-17

**48:18** As for the remaining 10,000 to the east and 10,000 to the west, adjoining the long side[^4] of the sacred reserve, the produce of these areas adjoining the sacred reserve shall serve as food for the workers in the city; ^48-18

**48:19** the workers in the city from all the tribes of Israel shall cultivate it. ^48-19

**48:20** The entire reserve, 25,000 square, you shall set aside as the sacred reserve plus the city property. ^48-20

**48:21** What remains on either side of the sacred reserve and the city property shall belong to the prince. The prince shall own [the land] from the border of the 25,000 of the reserve[^5] up to the eastern boundary, and from the border of the 25,000 on the west up to the western boundary, corresponding to the [tribal] portions. The sacred reserve, with the Temple Sanctuary in the middle of it ^48-21

**48:22** and the property of the Levites and the city property as well, shall be in the middle of the [area belonging] to the prince; [the rest of the land] between the territory of Judah and the territory of Benjamin shall belong to the prince. ^48-22

**48:23** As for the remaining tribes:[^6] From the eastern border to the western border: Benjamin—one. ^48-23

**48:24** Adjoining the territory of Benjamin, from the eastern border to the western border: Simeon—one. ^48-24

**48:25** Adjoining the territory of Simeon, from the eastern border to the western border: Issachar—one. ^48-25

**48:26** Adjoining the territory of Issachar, from the eastern border to the western border: Zebulun—one. ^48-26

**48:27** Adjoining the territory of Zebulun, from the eastern border to the western border: Gad—one. ^48-27

**48:28** The other border of Gad shall be the southern boundary. This boundary shall run from Tamar to the waters of Meribath-kadesh, to the Wadi [of Egypt], and to the Great Sea. ^48-28

**48:29** That is the land which you shall allot as a heritage to the tribes of Israel, and those are their portions—declares the Lord GOD. ^48-29

**48:30** And these are the exits from the city: On its northern side, measuring 4,500 cubits, ^48-30

**48:31** the gates of the city shall be—three gates on the north—named for the tribes of Israel: the Reuben Gate: one; the Judah Gate: one; the Levi Gate: one. ^48-31

**48:32** On the eastern side, [measuring] 4,500 cubits—there shall be three gates: the Joseph Gate: one; the Benjamin Gate: one; and the Dan Gate: one. ^48-32

**48:33** On the southern side, measuring 4,500 cubits, there shall be three gates: the Simeon Gate: one; the Issachar Gate: one; and the Zebulun Gate: one. ^48-33

**48:34** And on the western side, [measuring] 4,500 cubits—there shall be three gates: the Gad Gate: one; the Asher Gate: one; the Naphtali Gate: one. ^48-34

**48:35** Its circumference [shall be] 18,000 [cubits]; and the name of the city from that day on shall be “The LORD Is There.” ^48-35

[^1]: Emendation yields “25,000”; cf. 45.3–6.
[^2]: Lit. “10,000 in breadth on the west; 10,000 in breadth on the east; and 25,000 in length on the south.”
[^3]: Septuagint reads “20,000”; cf. note a.
[^4]: I.e., the south side.
[^5]: Emendation yields “on the east.”
[^6]: The tribes not provided for in vv. 1–7, and lying south of the sacred gift.
