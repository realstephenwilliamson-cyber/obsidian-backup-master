## Mūla

कुर्याद्योग्येषु शिष्येषु नायोग्येषु कदाचन ।
रहस्ये योजयेद्विप्रं परीक्ष्य विपरीततः ॥ ^mula

kuryādyogyeṣu śiṣyeṣu nāyogyeṣu kadācana |
rahasye yojayedvipraṃ parīkṣya viparītataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 14 - Chapter Twenty-Three#^pg336|p. 316]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=336>))
>
> (A teacher who) has completed the Vow of the Observance of Mantra should thereafter do (all that is required) in all respects, such as (impart) initiation and explain (the scriptures) to qualified disciples, (but) never to those who are not. (with 23.41cd–42ab)
>
> According to the Ūrmiśāsana ‘a Brahmin should be introduced (yojayet) to the secret (teaching), and so too (his) partner (śakti), (only) after having examined whether their conduct is contrary (to the norms) - not otherwise!’ (with 23.42cd–43ab)

## Viveka

> [!viveka]- Viveka in English (Dyczkowski, vol. IX, p. 317)

>
> ![[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 14 - Chapter Twenty-Three#Viveka on 23.42–43]]

*Lead-in:* योग्यायोग्यपरीक्षायां च अस्य उदाहरणदिशा युक्तिं दर्शयति--

> रहस्ये योजयेद्विप्रं परीक्ष्य विपरीततः ॥
> आचाराच्छक्तिमप्येव नान्यथेत्यूर्मिशासने ।

विपरीतत आचारादिति--श्रुतिस्मृतिविरुद्धात् मद्यपानादेः । एवमिति-- विपरीतादेव आचारात् लोकविरुद्धात् निधुवनादेः, इतरथा हि लोभलौल्यादिना प्रवर्तयेतामित्युक्तं नान्यथेति ॥
