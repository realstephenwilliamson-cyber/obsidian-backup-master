## Mūla

दीक्षान्ते दीपकान् पक्त्वा समस्तैः साधकैः सह ।
चरुः प्राश्यः कुलाचार्यैर्महापातकनाशनः ॥ ^mula

dīkṣānte dīpakān paktvā samastaiḥ sādhakaiḥ saha |
caruḥ prāśyaḥ kulācāryairmahāpātakanāśanaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 13 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 5#^pg424|p. 407]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=424>))
>
> At the end of the initiation, after having cooked the ‘lamps’ (of meat), the sacrificial pap that destroys great sin should be eaten by the Kula teachers along with all the adepts. This is (said) in the venerable Ratnamālā. A ritual procedure which is less (than required) or excessive is indeed a sin. (Doing) this is said to quell that (sin). (with 29.282–283)
