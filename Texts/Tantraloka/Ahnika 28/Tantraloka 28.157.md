## Mūla

घण्टायां स्रुक्स्रुवे शिष्यलिङ्गिषु द्वारतोरणे ।
स्वदेहे वह्निपीठे च यथाशोभं तदिष्यते ॥ ^mula

ghaṇṭāyāṃ sruksruve śiṣyaliṅgiṣu dvāratoraṇe |
svadehe vahnipīṭhe ca yathāśobhaṃ tadiṣyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 04 - Chapter Twenty-Eight, part 2#^pg94|p. 77]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=94>))
>
> The Seat of Knowledge (where the scriptures are placed and worshipped), the moveable Liñṅga, the sacrificial surface, the teacher and his group, the bell, the large and small ladle (sruk and sruva), the disciples, the bearers of sectarian signs, the door and the lintel above it (toraṇa), one’s own body, and the sacred seat (of the pit of the sacrificial) fire - it is right (that all of these be invested with the sacred thread,) as is beautiful (and befitting). Of nine colours, it should also be placed on the terrace and in the house where the sacrifice is celebrated. (with 28.156cd–158ab)

## Viveka

> विद्यापीठे चले लिङ्गे स्थण्डिले च गुरोर्गणे ॥
> घण्टायां स्रुक्स्रुवे शिष्यलिङ्गिषु द्वारतोरणे ।
> स्वदेहे वह्निपीठे च यथाशोभं तदिष्यते ॥
> प्रासादे यागगेहे च कारयेन्नवरङ्गिकम् ।

वह्निपीठ इति--कुण्डे । नवरङ्गिकमिति--नानावर्णमित्यर्थः ॥
