## The divine shepherd

*A Psalm of David.*

**23:1** The LORD is my shepherd; I shall not want. ^23-1

**23:2** He makes me lie down in green pastures; he leads me beside still waters;^[Heb *waters of rest*] ^23-2

**23:3** he restores my soul.^[Or *life*] He leads me in right paths^[Or *paths of righteousness*] for his name’s sake. ^23-3

**23:4** Even though I walk through the darkest valley,^[Or *the valley of the shadow of death*] I fear no evil, for you are with me; your rod and your staff, they comfort me. ^23-4

**23:5** You prepare a table before me in the presence of my enemies; you anoint my head with oil; my cup overflows. ^23-5

**23:6** Surely^[Or *Only*] goodness and mercy^[Or *kindness*] shall follow me all the days of my life, and I shall dwell in the house of the LORD my whole life long.^[Heb *for length of days*] ^23-6
