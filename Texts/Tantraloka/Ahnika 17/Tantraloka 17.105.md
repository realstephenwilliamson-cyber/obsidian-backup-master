## Mūla

एवं तन्मात्रवर्गोऽपि शिवतामय इष्यते ।
परानन्दमहाव्याप्तिरशेषमलविच्युतिः ॥ ^mula

evaṃ tanmātravargo'pi śivatāmaya iṣyate |
parānandamahāvyāptiraśeṣamalavicyutiḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 08 - Chapter Seventeen#^pg206|p. 186]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=206>))
>
> In the same way, the subtle elements are also felt to be (iṣyate) Śiva’s state of being (śivatā). (The pure states of the five organs of action are as follows). The Great Pervasion of supreme bliss (is the ejaculation of the genitals), the falling away of all Impurity (mala) (is excretion), the movement within Śiva (is the locomotion of the legs), the laying hold of him (is the grasping of the hands), and the hymn to Śiva who is to be taken up (is the activity of the voice). (The smelling of) the fragrance of Śiva, tasting, seeing, touching and hearing Him — such is the pure state of the senses. (with 17.105–107ab)
