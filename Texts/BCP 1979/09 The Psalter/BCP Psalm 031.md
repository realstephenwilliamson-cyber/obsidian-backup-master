# Psalm 31

← [[BCP Psalm 030]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 032]] →

*In te, Domine, speravi* · Book One · Sixth Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 031]]

**31:1** In you, O LORD, have I taken refuge;  
let me never be put to shame; \*  
deliver me in your righteousness. ^31-1

**31:2** Incline your ear to me; \*  
make haste to deliver me. ^31-2

**31:3** Be my strong rock, a castle to keep me safe,  
for you are my crag and my stronghold; \*  
for the sake of your Name, lead me and guide me. ^31-3

**31:4** Take me out of the net that they have secretly set for me, \*  
for you are my tower of strength. ^31-4

**31:5** Into your hands I commend my spirit, \*  
for you have redeemed me,  
O LORD, O God of truth. ^31-5

**31:6** I hate those who cling to worthless idols, \*  
and I put my trust in the LORD. ^31-6

**31:7** I will rejoice and be glad because of your mercy; \*  
for you have seen my affliction;  
you know my distress. ^31-7

[p. 623]

**31:8** You have not shut me up in the power of the enemy; \*  
you have set my feet in an open place. ^31-8

**31:9** Have mercy on me, O LORD, for I am in trouble; \*  
my eye is consumed with sorrow,  
and also my throat and my belly. ^31-9

**31:10** For my life is wasted with grief,  
and my years with sighing; \*  
my strength fails me because of affliction,  
and my bones are consumed. ^31-10

**31:11** I have become a reproach to all my enemies and even to my neighbors,  
a dismay to those of my acquaintance; \*  
when they see me in the street they avoid me. ^31-11

**31:12** I am forgotten like a dead man, out of mind; \*  
I am as useless as a broken pot. ^31-12

**31:13** For I have heard the whispering of the crowd;  
fear is all around; \*  
they put their heads together against me;  
they plot to take my life. ^31-13

**31:14** But as for me, I have trusted in you, O LORD. \*  
I have said, "You are my God. ^31-14

**31:15** My times are in your hand; \*  
rescue me from the hand of my enemies,  
and from those who persecute me. ^31-15

**31:16** Make your face to shine upon your servant, \*  
and in your loving-kindness save me." ^31-16

**31:17** LORD, let me not be ashamed for having called upon you; \*  
rather, let the wicked be put to shame;  
let them be silent in the grave. ^31-17

[p. 624]

**31:18** Let the lying lips be silenced which speak against the righteous, \*  
haughtily, disdainfully, and with contempt. ^31-18

**31:19** How great is your goodness, O LORD!  
which you have laid up for those who fear you; \*  
which you have done in the sight of all  
for those who put their trust in you. ^31-19

**31:20** You hide them in the covert of your presence from those who slander them; \*  
you keep them in your shelter from the strife of tongues. ^31-20

**31:21** Blessed be the LORD! \*  
for he has shown me the wonders of his love in a besieged city. ^31-21

**31:22** Yet I said in my alarm,  
I have been cut off from the sight of your eyes." \*  
Nevertheless, you heard the sound of my entreaty  
when I cried out to you. ^31-22

**31:23** Love the LORD, all you who worship him; \*  
the LORD protects the faithful,  
but repays to the full those who act haughtily. ^31-23

**31:24** Be strong and let your heart take courage, \*  
all you who wait for the LORD. ^31-24
