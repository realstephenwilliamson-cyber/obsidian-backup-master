## Mūla

ततो मायादितत्त्वानि चत्वारि सुसमाहितः ।
चतुरङ्गुलया व्याप्त्या सकलान्तानि भावयेत् ॥ ^mula

tato māyāditattvāni catvāri susamāhitaḥ |
caturaṅgulayā vyāptyā sakalāntāni bhāvayet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, October 2023 – December 2023
> p. 18 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapter 6 (2023).pdf#page=6>))
>
> Then one who is well attentive should meditate on the four principles, beginning with Māyā and ending with Sakala (i.e., Sadāśiva), as extending for four fingers (each).
