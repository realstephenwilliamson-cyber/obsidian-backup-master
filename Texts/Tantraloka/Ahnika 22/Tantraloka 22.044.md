## Mūla

तद्दीक्षाश्चापि गृह्णीयादभिषेचनपश्चिमाः ।
ज्ञानोपोद्वलिकास्ता हि तत्तज्ज्ञानवता कृताः ॥ ^mula

taddīkṣāścāpi gṛhṇīyādabhiṣecanapaścimāḥ |
jñānopodvalikāstā hi tattajjñānavatā kṛtāḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 13 - Chapter Twenty-Two#^pg312|p. 292]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=312>))
>
> So too, one who, impelled by Maheśvara, who resides in his heart, aspires to a very extensive knowledge of the scriptures, may go to many teachers and also take their initiations that end in (his) consecration (as a teacher). Indeed, performed by (various teachers) who (individually) possess the knowledge this and that each one (of the scriptures), strengthen (and reinforce his) knowledge. (with 22.43–44)

## Viveka

> योऽपि हृत्स्थमहेशानचोदनातः सुविस्तृतम् ।
> शास्त्रज्ञानं समन्विच्छेत्सोऽपि यायाद् बहून् गुरून् ॥
> तद्दीक्षाश्चापि गृह्णीयादभिषेचनपश्चिमाः ।
> ज्ञानोपोद्वलिकास्ता हि तत्तज्ज्ञानवता कृताः ॥
