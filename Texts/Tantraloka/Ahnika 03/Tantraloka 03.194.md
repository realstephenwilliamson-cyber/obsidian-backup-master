## Mūla

विश्वशक्ताववच्छेदवन्ध्ये जातमुपासनम् ।
इत्येष महिमैतावानिति तावन्न शक्यते ॥ ^mula

viśvaśaktāvavacchedavandhye jātamupāsanam |
ityeṣa mahimaitāvāniti tāvanna śakyate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. II
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 02 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v02 10 - Chapter Three, part 7#^pg328|p. 312]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 02.pdf#page=328>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 3.194cd–195ab.
>
> As everything (viśva) is encompassed by that (triad), when this is properly worshipped, He who is the possessor of all powers and free of limitation is (truly) worshipped. (with 3.193cd–194ab)
>
> (However,) it is not possible (to say) that (even) this is the (full) extent of (His) glory, for it is impossible to determine its extent. (with 3.194cd)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* सर्वं चैतदाक्षेप्यमेत्स्फारसारमपरिच्छेद्यं चेत्याह --

> इत्येष महिमैतावानिति तावन्न शक्यते ॥
> अपरिच्छिन्नशक्तेः कः कुर्याच्छक्तिपरिच्छिदाम् ।

न शक्यते इति -- अर्थात्परिच्छेत्तुम् ॥ १९४ ॥
