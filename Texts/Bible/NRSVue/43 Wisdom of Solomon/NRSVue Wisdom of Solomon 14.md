# Wisdom of Solomon 14 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Wisdom of Solomon 13|← Wisdom of Solomon 13]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Wisdom of Solomon 15|Wisdom of Solomon 15 →]]

## Folly of a navigator praying to an idol

**14:1** Again, one preparing to sail and about to voyage over raging waves calls upon a piece of wood more fragile than the ship that carries him. ^14-1

**14:2** For it was desire for gain that planned that vessel, and wisdom was the artisan who built it, ^14-2

**14:3** but it is your providence, O Father, that steers its course, because you have given it a path in the sea and a safe way through the waves, ^14-3

**14:4** showing that you can save from every danger, so that even a person who lacks skill may put to sea. ^14-4

**14:5** It is your will that works of your wisdom should not be without effect; therefore people trust their lives even to the smallest piece of wood, and passing through the billows on a raft they come safely to land. ^14-5

**14:6** For even in the beginning, when arrogant giants were perishing, the hope of the world took refuge on a raft and guided by your hand left to the world the seed of a new generation. ^14-6

**14:7** For blessed is the wood by which righteousness comes. ^14-7

**14:8** But the idol made with hands is accursed, and so is the one who made it—he for having made it, and the perishable thing because it was named a god. ^14-8

**14:9** For equally hateful to God are the ungodly and their ungodliness, ^14-9

**14:10** for what was done will be punished together with the one who did it. ^14-10

**14:11** Therefore there will be a visitation also upon the idols of the nations, because, though part of what God created, they became an abomination, stumbling blocks for human souls and a trap for the feet of the foolish. ^14-11

## The origin and evils of idolatry

**14:12** For the idea of making idols was the beginning of sexual immorality, and the invention of them was the corruption of life, ^14-12

**14:13** for they did not exist from the beginning, nor will they last forever. ^14-13

**14:14** For through human vanity they entered the world, and therefore their speedy end has been planned. ^14-14

**14:15** For a father, consumed with grief at an untimely bereavement, made an image of his child, who had been suddenly taken from him; he now honored as a god what was once a dead human being and handed on to his dependents secret rites and initiations. ^14-15

**14:16** Then the ungodly custom, grown strong with time, was kept as a law, and at the command of monarchs carved images were worshiped. ^14-16

**14:17** When people could not honor monarchs^[Gk *them*] in their presence, since they lived at a distance, they imagined their appearance far away and made a visible image of the king whom they honored, so that by their zeal they might flatter the absent one as though present. ^14-17

**14:18** Then the ambition of the artisan impelled even those who did not know the king^[Gk lacks *the king*] to intensify their worship. ^14-18

**14:19** For he, perhaps wishing to please his ruler, skillfully forced the likeness to take more beautiful form, ^14-19

**14:20** and the multitude, attracted by the charm of his work, now regarded as an object of worship the one whom shortly before they had honored as a human. ^14-20

**14:21** And this became a hidden trap for humankind, because people, in bondage to misfortune or to royal authority, bestowed on objects of stone or wood the name that ought not to be shared. ^14-21

**14:22** Then it was not enough for them to err about the knowledge of God, but though living in great strife due to ignorance, they call such great evils peace. ^14-22

**14:23** For whether they kill children in their initiations or celebrate secret mysteries or hold frenzied revels with strange customs, ^14-23

**14:24** they no longer keep either their lives or their marriages pure, but they either treacherously kill one another or grieve one another by adultery, ^14-24

**14:25** and all is a raging riot of blood and murder, theft and deceit, corruption, faithlessness, tumult, perjury, ^14-25

**14:26** confusion over what is good, forgetfulness of favors, defiling of souls, sexual perversion, disorder in marriages, adultery, and debauchery. ^14-26

**14:27** For the worship of idols not to be named is the beginning and cause and end of every evil. ^14-27

**14:28** For their worshipers^[Gk *they*] either rave in exultation or prophesy lies or live unrighteously or readily commit perjury, ^14-28

**14:29** for because they trust in lifeless idols they swear wicked oaths and expect to suffer no harm. ^14-29

**14:30** But just penalties will overtake them on two counts: because they thought wrongly about God in devoting themselves to idols and because in deceit they swore unrighteously through contempt for holiness. ^14-30

**14:31** For it is not the power of the things by which people swear^[Or *of the oaths people swear*] but the just penalty for those who sin that always pursues the transgression of the unrighteous. ^14-31
