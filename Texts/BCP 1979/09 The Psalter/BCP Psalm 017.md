# Psalm 17

← [[BCP Psalm 016]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 018]] →

*Exaudi, Domine* · Book One · Third Day: Morning Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 017]]

**17:1** Hear my plea of innocence, O LORD;  
give heed to my cry; \*  
listen to my prayer, which does not come from lying lips. ^17-1

[p. 601]

**17:2** Let my vindication come forth from your presence; \*  
let your eyes be fixed on justice. ^17-2

**17:3** Weigh my heart, summon me by night, \*  
melt me down; you will find no impurity in me. ^17-3

**17:4** I give no offense with my mouth as others do; \*  
I have heeded the words of your lips. ^17-4

**17:5** My footsteps hold fast to the ways of your law; \*  
in your paths my feet shall not stumble. ^17-5

**17:6** I call upon you, O God, for you will answer me; \*  
incline your ear to me and hear my words. ^17-6

**17:7** Show me your marvelous loving-kindness, \*  
O Savior of those who take refuge at your right hand  
from those who rise up against them. ^17-7

**17:8** Keep me as the apple of your eye; \*  
hide me under the shadow of your wings, ^17-8

**17:9** From the wicked who assault me, \*  
from my deadly enemies who surround me. ^17-9

**17:10** They have closed their heart to pity, \*  
and their mouth speaks proud things. ^17-10

**17:11** They press me hard,  
now they surround me, \*  
watching how they may cast me to the ground, ^17-11

**17:12** Like a lion, greedy for its prey, \*  
and like a young lion lurking in secret places. ^17-12

**17:13** Arise, O LORD; confront them and bring them down; \*  
deliver me from the wicked by your sword. ^17-13

**17:14** Deliver me, O LORD, by your hand \*  
from those whose portion in life is this world; ^17-14

[p. 602]

**17:15** Whose bellies you fill with your treasure, \*  
who are well supplied with children  
and leave their wealth to their little ones. ^17-15

**17:16** But at my vindication I shall see your face; \*  
when I awake, I shall be satisfied, beholding your likeness. ^17-16
