## Mūla

शक्रवारुणदिक्स्थाश्च याम्यसौम्यगतास्तथा ।
एकोनत्रिंशद्वंशाः स्युरृजुतिर्यग्गतास्तथा ॥ ^mula

śakravāruṇadiksthāśca yāmyasaumyagatāstathā |
ekonatriṃśadvaṃśāḥ syurṛjutiryaggatāstathā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 05 - Chapter Thirty-One, part 2#^pg138|p. 120]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=138>))
>
> (The teacher) should first of all install the channels (nādikā), numbering thirty, that are located in the east and west and in the north and south. (with 31.132cd–133ab)
>
> (In this way,) there should be twenty-nine parts (varnśa) that extend vertically and horizontally. (with 31.133cd)
