## Mūla

तदेवं खेचरीचक्ररूढौ यद्रूपमुल्लसेत् ।
तदेव मुद्रा मन्तव्या शेषः स्याद्देहविक्रिया ॥ ^mula

tadevaṃ khecarīcakrarūḍhau yadrūpamullaset |
tadeva mudrā mantavyā śeṣaḥ syāddehavikriyā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 06 - Chapter Thirty-Two#^pg184|p. 166]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=184>))
>
> The configuration (rūpam) (of consciousness) that pours forth in this way, when one is firmly established in the Wheel of the Skyfarers, is considered to be (the one true) Seal. The rest are (just) deformations of the body.
