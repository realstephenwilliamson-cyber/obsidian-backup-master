## Jesus the true vine

**15:1** “I am the true vine, and my Father is the vinegrower. ^15-1

**15:2** He removes every branch in me that bears no fruit. Every branch that bears fruit he prunes^[The same Greek root refers to pruning and cleansing] to make it bear more fruit. ^15-2

**15:3** You have already been cleansed^[The same Greek root refers to pruning and cleansing] by the word that I have spoken to you. ^15-3

**15:4** Abide in me as I abide in you. Just as the branch cannot bear fruit by itself unless it abides in the vine, neither can you unless you abide in me. ^15-4

**15:5** I am the vine; you are the branches. Those who abide in me and I in them bear much fruit, because apart from me you can do nothing. ^15-5

**15:6** Whoever does not abide in me is thrown away like a branch and withers; such branches are gathered, thrown into the fire, and burned. ^15-6

**15:7** If you abide in me and my words abide in you, ask for whatever you wish, and it will be done for you. ^15-7

**15:8** My Father is glorified by this, that you bear much fruit and become^[Or *be*] my disciples. ^15-8

**15:9** As the Father has loved me, so I have loved you; abide in my love. ^15-9

**15:10** If you keep my commandments, you will abide in my love, just as I have kept my Father’s commandments and abide in his love. ^15-10

**15:11** I have said these things to you so that my joy may be in you and that your joy may be complete. ^15-11

**15:12** “This is my commandment, that you love one another as I have loved you. ^15-12

**15:13** No one has greater love than this, to lay down one’s life for one’s friends. ^15-13

**15:14** You are my friends if you do what I command you. ^15-14

**15:15** I do not call you servants^[Gk *slaves*] any longer, because the servant^[Gk *slave*] does not know what the master is doing, but I have called you friends, because I have made known to you everything that I have heard from my Father. ^15-15

**15:16** You did not choose me, but I chose you. And I appointed you to go and bear fruit, fruit that will last, so that the Father will give you whatever you ask him in my name. ^15-16

**15:17** I am giving you these commands so that you may love one another. ^15-17

## The world’s hatred

**15:18** “If the world hates you, be aware that it hated me before it hated you. ^15-18

**15:19** If you belonged to the world, the world would love you as its own. Because you do not belong to the world, but I have chosen you out of the world, therefore the world hates you. ^15-19

**15:20** Remember the word that I said to you, ‘Slaves are not greater than their master.’ If they persecuted me, they will persecute you; if they kept my word, they will keep yours also. ^15-20

**15:21** But they will do all these things to you on account of my name, because they do not know him who sent me. ^15-21

**15:22** If I had not come and spoken to them, they would not have sin, but now they have no excuse for their sin. ^15-22

**15:23** Whoever hates me hates my Father also. ^15-23

**15:24** If I had not done among them the works that no one else did, they would not have sin. But now they have seen and hated both me and my Father. ^15-24

**15:25** It was to fulfill the word that is written in their law, ‘They hated me without a cause.’ ^15-25

**15:26** “When the Advocate^[Or *Helper* or *Comforter*] comes, whom I will send to you from the Father, the Spirit of truth who comes from the Father, he will testify on my behalf. ^15-26

**15:27** You also are to testify, because you have been with me from the beginning. ^15-27
