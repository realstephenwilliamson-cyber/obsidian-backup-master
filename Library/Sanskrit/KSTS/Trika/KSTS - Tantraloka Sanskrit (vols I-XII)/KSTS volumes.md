# KSTS Tantrāloka: the volumes

This is Abhinavagupta's *Tantrāloka* with Jayaratha's *Viveka* commentary, edited in the Kashmir Series of Texts and Studies (KSTS) in twelve volumes between 1918 and 1938. Vol. I was edited by Mukund Ram Shastri and vols. II–XII by Madhusudan Kaul Shastri. The series numbers are not in volume order.

**All twelve volumes are now in the vault, in this folder** (copied on 6 Oct 2026 from scans Stephen downloaded from archive.org; 274 MB together). The catalog entry is [[Abhinavagupta - Tantraloka (KSTS, vols I-XII)]]. [[Tantraloka verse-page map]] links every verse to its page in these files, and [[Library/Sanskrit/KSTS/Trika/KSTS - Tantraloka Sanskrit (vols I-XII)/tantraloka-pages.tsv|tantraloka-pages.tsv]] has the same data as a table.

**Checked on 6 Oct 2026** (phase 2 of the October 2026 plan):
- The series numbers, years and editors come from each volume's title page.
- The āhnikas and verse ranges come from the running heads and from matching every verse's text against the OCR of every page.
- This corrects the earlier version of this note: vol. X covers āhnikas 16–27 (not 16–23), vol. XI covers 28–29 (not 24–30), and vol. XII covers all of āhnika 30. Vol. X is from 1933.

The verse notes' root text (Mūla) follows this edition; the exceptions are listed in the verse-page map. The other transcription, GRETIL's (Takashima's), is the same text as the Digital Corpus of Sanskrit's (2,529 of 2,726 verses identical). It was used as a witness in phase 2 and not added to the vault (Stephen's instruction: keep one of two matching copies).

## Text layers and OCR

- The scans' own text layers are poor. Vols. I–VI were run through an English OCR model, and their text is gibberish. Vols. VIII and X have no text. Vols. VII, IX, XI and XII have noisy Devanagari OCR.
- In phase 2, every page was read with Tesseract 5 and its Sanskrit model, at 300 dpi for vols. I–IV and 200 dpi for the rest. That text was used to build the page map; it is not stored in the PDFs.
