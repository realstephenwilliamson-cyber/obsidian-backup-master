## Mūla

पैशाचं राक्षसं याक्षं गान्धर्वं चैन्द्रमेव च ।
तथा सौम्यं सप्राजेशं ब्राह्ममष्टममिष्यते ॥ ^mula

paiśācaṁ rākṣasaṁ yākṣaṁ gāndharvaṁ caindram eva ca |
tathā saumyaṁ saprājeśaṁ brāhmam aṣṭamam iṣyate || ^iast

## Translation

> **The Eight Types of Divine Beings in the Intellect**
>
> (The eight types of divine beings are) Piśācas, Rākṣasas, Yakṣas, Gandharavas, as well those associated with Indra, so too Somas along with Prajāpatis, and Brahmās who are considered to be the eighth (type).[^43]

[^43]: MV 5/23 quoted in TĀv ad 8/227ab (226cd), MV KSTS reads in the last line iṣyate for ucyate. Cf. SvT 10/971cd-971ab, and above, 8/119-120. TĀ 8/225cd-228ab (225-227) is based on MV 5/21-23.
