## Chapter Ten

The Blessed Lord continued:

\[1\] “Once again, Mighty Warrior,
hear from Me a sublime teaching
that I will give you, beloved one,
out of concern for your welfare.

\[2\] Neither the multitudes of gods
nor the great seers know My origin.
I am the universal source
of all the great seers and the gods.

\[3\] He who knows Me, the beginningless,
birthless Sovereign of the universe,
is undeluded among mortals
and released from every evil.

\[4\] Acumen, knowledge, clarity,
patience, truth, self-control, calmness,
pleasure, pain, becoming, passing,
terror, and also fearlessness,

\[5\] Nonviolence, fairness, contentment,
ardor, charity, fame, and shame—
all the various states of beings
come to exist from Me alone.

\[6\] The seven great seers of antiquity
and the four ancestral Manus
from whom the world’s creatures were born
came forth from Me, born of My mind.

[p. 59] \[7\] Anyone who truly understands
My manifested *Yogic* power,
is without doubt united with Me
in unwavering, steady *Yoga*.

\[8\] I am the origin of all,
everything comes forth from Me.
Knowing this the awakened ones
worship Me in meditation.

\[9\] Concentrating their breath on Me,
awakening one another,
speaking about Me constantly,
they are content and full of joy.

\[10\] Those who are steadfast in practice
and worship Me with affection,
I give to them *Buddhi Yoga*,
by means of which they come to Me.

\[11\] Out of compassion for them I,
who dwell within their souls, destroy
the darkness born of ignorance
with the shining lamp of knowledge.”

Then Arjuna said:

\[12\] “Supreme *Brahman*, highest abode,
Purifier supreme, O Thou,
Spirit eternal and divine,
Primal God, unborn, pervasive:

\[13\] Thus they call You, all the seers,
the divine seer Narada too,
Asita, Devala, and Vyasa,
and You Yourself tell me also.

[p. 60] \[14\] I believe that all You tell me
is the truth, O Handsome-haired One.
None of the gods or demons know
Your manifestation, Blessed One.

\[15\] You know Your Self through Your Self alone,
O Spirit Supreme, Lord of beings,
source of welfare for all beings,
God of gods, O Lord of the world.

\[16\] Please describe, for You are able,
the divine self-manifestations
by which You permeate the worlds
and maintain Your presence in them.

\[17\] How may I know You, O *Yogin*,
through my constant meditation?
In what various modes of being
should I think of You, Blessed One?

\[18\] Explain further Your *Yogic* power
and its manifestation, Krishna.
For I can never get enough
of hearing your immortal words.”

And the Blessed One said:

\[19\] “Listen! I will explain to you
My divine self-manifestations,
but only the main ones, Arjuna.
There is no end to My extent.

\[20\] I am the Self, O Thick-haired One,
indwelling the hearts of all beings.
I am their beginning, and I am
their middle and their end as well.

[p. 61] \[21\] Of the Adityas I am Vishnu.
Of lights I am the shining sun.
I am Marichi of the Maruts.
In the zodiac I am the moon.

\[22\] Of the *Vedas* I am the Sama,
of the gods I am Vasava,
of the senses I am the mind,
beings’ thoughts, and consciousness.

\[23\] Of the Rudras I am Shankara,
the Yakshas’ and Rakshas’ Vittesha,
I am Agni of the Vasus,
of the mountains I am Meru.

\[24\] Of household priests, know that I am
Brihaspati, O Arjuna.
Of commanders I am Skanda.
Of waters I am the ocean.

\[25\] Of the great seers I am Bhrigu.
Of words I am the syllable *Om*.
Of rituals I am *japa*.
Of the unmoving, the Himalayas.

\[26\] Of trees I am the sacred fig;
of divine seers I am Narada;
of the Gandharvas, Chitraratha;
of the perfected, wise Kapila.

\[27\] Know that among horses I am
Ucchaishravas, born of nectar;
Airavata among elephants;
and among men I am the king.

\[28\] Of weapons I am the thunderbolt;
I am Kamadhuk among cows;
[p. 62] I am Kama, the god of love;
of serpents I am Vasuki.

\[29\] I am Ananta of the Nagas,
Varuna of aquatic creatures.
Of ancestors I am Aryaman;
of subduers I am Yama.

\[30\] Of demons I am Prahlada;
of the reckoners I am Time.
Of animals I am the lion;
and of birds I am Garuda.

\[31\] Of purifiers I am the wind;
of warriors I am Rama,
Makara among sea monsters,
and of rivers I am the Ganges.

\[32\] I am the beginning and the end
and the middle of all creations.
Of knowing I am supreme Self-knowing,
I am the speech of those who speak.

\[33\] Of letters I am the letter A,
the compound of compounded words.
I alone am infinite Time,
and I face in all directions.

\[34\] I am death and the origin
of future things. Of feminines
I am fame, speech, prosperity,
memory, wisdom, courage, patience.

\[35\] Of chants I am the Brihatsaman,
of meters I am the Gayatri.
Of months I am the Marga-shirsha,
of seasons the flowering spring.

[p. 63] \[36\] I am the gambling of the cheats
and the brilliance of the brilliant.
I am victory and exertion,
the goodness of those who are good.

\[37\] Of the Vrishnis I am Vasudeva,
of Pandu’s sons I am Arjuna.
Of the sages I am Vyasa,
of poets I am Ushana.

\[38\] Of kings I am their ruling power,
of the ambitious I am their counsel.
I am the silence of secrets
and the knowledge of those who know.

\[39\] I am also what is the seed
of every being, Arjuna.
There is nothing, living or not,
that could exist except through Me.

\[40\] There is no end to my divine
manifestations, Arjuna.
What I have listed are examples
of My manifestations’ extent.

\[41\] Every manifested being
that is powerful or glorious,
understand that all originate
from a fraction of My splendor.

\[42\] But what good will all this knowledge
do you, Arjuna? I support
the entire universe constantly
with a single fraction of Myself.”
