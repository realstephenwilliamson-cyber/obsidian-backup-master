### 6.1–23: The faithful obedience of life in Christ

*[[NRSVue Romans 06#^6-1|Text]], p. 297* · The argument is continued in diatribe style, though the speakers are sometimes not clear (see “Diatribe,” p. 289). Gentiles who have turned to God because of Christ’s faithfulness are obliged to live faithfully; although they are not under Torah, since they are not Israelites/Jews, they are to dedicate themselves to righteousness. The issue may revolve around how these Gentile men are to signify their righteous standing apart from circumcision. ^6-1

**[[NRSVue Romans 06#^6-1|1]]:** *What then are we to say?* It may be that the Gentile interlocutor responds to Paul’s argument in ch 5. ^6-1-2

**[[NRSVue Romans 06#^6-2|2]]:** *Died to sin*, see v 10. ^6-2

**[[NRSVue Romans 06#^6-3|3]]:** *Baptized . . . death,* the baptized person symbolically dies as did the Christ, is now dead to the power of sin (v 10), and will be raised as he is raised. ^6-3

**[[NRSVue Romans 06#^6-5|5]]:** *For if*, or “For since.” *Death like his*, see v 10. ^6-5

**[[NRSVue Romans 06#^6-7|7]]:** *Freed from sin*, lit., “justified from sin (or) by a sin offering” — could indicate being legitimated from standing as a sinner, or by way of a sin offering. ^6-7

**[[NRSVue Romans 06#^6-8|8]]:** *Believe*, or “trust.” ^6-8

**[[NRSVue Romans 06#^6-10|10]]:** *Died to sin . . . lives to God*, as far as sin is concerned, Christ is as if dead and therefore impervious; as far as God is concerned, Christ is alive. *Once for all,* though the death was a past event, it is operative (in a way not specified) on behalf of everyone now. ^6-10

**[[NRSVue Romans 06#^6-11|11]]:** Cf. [[LXX 4 Maccabees 07#^7-19|4 Macc 7.19]], “Since they believe that they, like our patriarchs Abraham and Isaac and Jacob, do not die to God, but live to God.” ^6-11

**[[NRSVue Romans 06#^6-12|12]]:** *To make you obey their passions*, or “to listen obediently \[to\] its intense lusts.” Paul thinks the body needs to be tamed; cf. 1.24; [[1 Corinthians 05#^5-1|1 Cor 5.1–7.40]]; [[1 Thessalonians 04#^4-5|1 Thess 4.5]]. ^6-12

**[[NRSVue Romans 06#^6-13|13]]:** *Members*, lit., “limbs”; by extension, “organs” ([[1 Corinthians 12#^12-14|1 Cor 12.14]]). Paul may be euphemistically personifying the “limbs” to refer to the body part that has been in dispute, and thus, to the implications for Gentiles; see Philo, *Migr*. 92: “because the rite of circumcision is an emblem of the excision of pleasures and of all the passions”; *Spec. Laws* 1.8–10; *Virtues* 214. ^6-13

**[[NRSVue Romans 06#^6-14|14]]:** *Not under law*, not become circumcised, and thus are not Jews subject to Torah. ^6-14

**[[NRSVue Romans 06#^6-16|16]]:** *Slaves*, the choice is not slavery vs. freedom, but to whom one will be enslaved; the choice is which master one obeys. [[Torah 32 - Behar#^25-42|Lev 25.42]] and other biblical and rabbinic texts (see esp. *Avot* 1.3) depict Israel as God’s slaves. ^6-16

**[[NRSVue Romans 06#^6-17|17]]:** *Form of teaching*, Paul may be alluding to a specific code applicable to Gentile Christ-followers, such as the Apostolic Decree ([[Acts 15#^15-19|Acts 15.19–21]],[[Acts 15#^15-27|27–32]]; [[Acts 16#^16-4|16.4–5]]) in lieu of the instruction not to undertake proselyte conversion. *From the heart*, with deep intention. ^6-17

**[[NRSVue Romans 06#^6-19|19]]:** *In human terms*, using an analogy to the human institution of slavery; rabbinic literature often notes that the Bible speaks in human language (Heb “dibrah torah kilshon bnai adam”— e.g., [[Nedarim 3#3a|*b. Ned.* 3a]]). *Your natural limitations*, lit., the weakness of your flesh, i.e., habits of behaving sinfully, see 1.18–32; *iniquity*, lit., “lawlessness,” as described in 1.18–32. ^6-19

**[[NRSVue Romans 06#^6-20|20]]:** *Slaves*, see 6.16n. *Free in regard to righteousness*, not obligated to do the right things as defined by Israel’s God. ^6-20

**[[NRSVue Romans 06#^6-21|21]]:** *Advantage*, lit., “fruit.” ^6-21

**[[NRSVue Romans 06#^6-22|22]]:** *Enslaved to God*, [[Torah 32 - Behar#^25-55|Lev 25.55]]; *sanctification,* being set apart to God and behaving faithfully because of this new status; *eternal life*, see 2.7n. ^6-22

**[[NRSVue Romans 06#^6-23|23]]:** *Free gift*, benefaction, see 1.5n.; “free” not in Gk. *In Christ Jesus*, identified with Christ Jesus. ^6-23
