# pauṣkaravṛtti

**Catalog number:** M00196  
**Author:** jñānaprakāśācārya  
**Manuscript:** IFP transcript 110  
**Published:** Muktabodha Indological Research Institute, Pondicherry, India

## Description

Copied from a manuscript belonging to Tondaimandalam jñānaprakāśaswamigal maṭham, Kanchipuram, Chi. Dt.This is a palm leaf manuscript of size 4 c.m. x 36 c.m with an average of ten lines per page. The hand is fairly good. The manuscript contains daṣakāryam, pauṣkarajñānapadaḥ, and prāsādaṣaṭślokī. It contains 1 to 219 leaves, and the transcript covers 936 pages.
