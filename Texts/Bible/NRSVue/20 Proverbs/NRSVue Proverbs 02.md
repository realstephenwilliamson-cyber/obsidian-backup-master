## The value of wisdom

**2:1** My child, if you accept my words and treasure up my commandments within you, ^2-1

**2:2** making your ear attentive to wisdom and inclining your heart to understanding, ^2-2

**2:3** if you indeed cry out for insight and raise your voice for understanding, ^2-3

**2:4** if you seek it like silver and search for it as for hidden treasures— ^2-4

**2:5** then you will understand the fear of the LORD and find the knowledge of God. ^2-5

**2:6** For the LORD gives wisdom; from his mouth come knowledge and understanding; ^2-6

**2:7** he stores up sound wisdom for the upright; he is a shield to those who walk blamelessly, ^2-7

**2:8** guarding the paths of justice and preserving the way of his faithful ones. ^2-8

**2:9** Then you will understand righteousness and justice and equity, every good path, ^2-9

**2:10** for wisdom will come into your heart, and knowledge will be pleasant to your soul; ^2-10

**2:11** prudence will watch over you, and understanding will guard you. ^2-11

**2:12** It will save you from the way of evil, from those who speak perversely, ^2-12

**2:13** who forsake the paths of uprightness to walk in the ways of darkness, ^2-13

**2:14** who rejoice in doing evil and delight in the perverseness of evil, ^2-14

**2:15** those whose paths are crooked and who are devious in their ways. ^2-15

**2:16** You will be saved from the loose woman,^[Heb *strange woman*] from the adulteress^[Heb *alien woman*] with her smooth words, ^2-16

**2:17** who forsakes the partner of her youth and forgets her sacred covenant, ^2-17

**2:18** for her way^[Cn: Heb *house*] leads down to death and her paths to the shades; ^2-18

**2:19** those who go to her never come back, nor do they regain the paths of life. ^2-19

**2:20** Therefore walk in the way of the good, and keep to the paths of the just. ^2-20

**2:21** For the upright will abide in the land, and the innocent will remain in it, ^2-21

**2:22** but the wicked will be cut off from the earth, and the treacherous will be rooted out of it. ^2-22
