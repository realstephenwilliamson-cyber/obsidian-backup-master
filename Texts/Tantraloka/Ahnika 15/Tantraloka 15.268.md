## Mūla

अभेदे जृम्भतेऽस्यैव मायामातुः शिवात्मता ।
मायाप्रमाता तद्रूपविकल्पाभ्यासपाटवात् ॥ ^mula

abhede jṛmbhate'syaiva māyāmātuḥ śivātmatā |
māyāpramātā tadrūpavikalpābhyāsapāṭavāt || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VIII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 08 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v08 05 - Chapter Fifteen, part 4#^pg178|p. 160]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 08.pdf#page=178>))
>
> When duality (bheda) unfolds, Māyā unfolds in relation to the Māyā subject (who perceives Māyā) (māyāmātr). (Conversely,) when oneness unfolds, that is (the unfolding of) Śiva’s nature of that same Māyā subject. (with 15.267cd–268ab)
>
> The Māyā subject becomes Śiva Himself by the intense exercise of the thought (vikalpa) that he is of that nature. The fruit of the depositions and the like is said to be that practice. (with 15.268cd–269ab)

*Lead-in:* ननु कथमस्य मायाप्रमातृत्वे सति अभेदो यन्नान्तरीयकं शिवात्मत्वं स्यात् ?--इत्याशङ्क्य आह--

> मायाप्रमाता तद्रूपविकल्पाभ्यासपाटवात् ॥
> शिव एव तदभ्यासफलं न्यासादि कीर्तितम् ।

ननु यदि एवं तत् किमनेन न्यासादिना?--इत्याशङ्क्य आह-- तदभ्यासफलमिति तदभ्यास एवं फलं प्रयोजनमस्य--इत्यर्थः ॥
