### 2.1–17: Christology

*[[NRSVue 1 Timothy 02#^2-1|Text]], p. 434* ^2-1

**[[NRSVue 1 Timothy 02#^2-2|2]]:** *For kings*, [[LXX Baruch 01#^1-11|Bar 1.11]] already exhorts, “pray for the life of King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon”; [[LXX 1 Maccabees 07#^7-33|1 Macc 7.33]] speaks of “the burnt offering that was being offered for the king”; and in *m. Avot* 3.2, “R. Hananiah, Prefect of the Priests, says, ‘Pray for the welfare of the government . . . ’” (see [[Jeremiah 27#^27-7|Jer 27.7]]). In the Second Temple period, a sacrifice on behalf of the emperor’s health was offered in the Jerusalem Temple. Paul ([[Romans 13#^13-1|Rom 13.1–2]]) and 1 Peter (2.13–14) advocate deference to the governing authority; Revelation does not. ^2-2

**[[NRSVue 1 Timothy 02#^2-3|3]]:** *God our Savior*, see 1.1n.; 4.10. ^2-3

**[[NRSVue 1 Timothy 02#^2-4|4]]:** *Saved . . . knowledge of the truth*, salvation is linked with right doctrine. ^2-4

**[[NRSVue 1 Timothy 02#^2-5|5–6]]:** The passage is set in poetical form to indicate that it might be an early creedal formula. *One God*, [[Torah 45 - Vaetchanan#^6-4|Deut 6.4]]. *Mediator*, in [[Galatians 03#^3-20|Gal 3.20]] Paul states that “a mediator \[Paul is referring to Moses\] involves more than one party; but God is one” although 1 Tim and Heb 8.6; 9.15; 12.34 describe Jesus as mediating between God and humanity. *Himself human*, Gk “anthropos”; Jesus mediates because he is a fellow human. ^2-5

**[[NRSVue 1 Timothy 02#^2-6|6]]:** *Ransom* (Gk “antilutron,” only place in NT where this form occurs; “lutron” in LXX for Heb “pidyon” \[“redemption”; see [[Torah 18 - Mishpatim#^21-30|Ex 21.30]], as payment for a life\]); “geʾulah” (“redemption,” e.g., regarding land, [[Torah 32 - Behar#^25-24|Lev 25.24]]); “kopher” (“ransom” in light of a census; see [[Torah 21 - Ki Tisa#^30-12|Ex 30.12]]); “hefdah” (for freeing slaves \[[[Torah 18 - Mishpatim#^21-8|Ex 21.8]]; [[Torah 30 - Kedoshim#^19-20|Lev 19.20]]\]): a payment for redemption or liberation, presumably here from sin; see [[Matthew 20#^20-28|Mt 20.28]]; [[Mark 10#^10-45|Mk 10.45]]. The author does not explain to whom the ransom is paid (God? Satan?). ^2-6

**[[NRSVue 1 Timothy 02#^2-7|7]]:** *Apostle*, see 1.1n. *Teacher of the Gentiles*, Paul’s commission ([[Galatians 01#^1-16|Gal 1.16]]; [[Galatians 02#^2-2|2.2]],[[Galatians 02#^2-7|7]]). ^2-7

### 2.8–15: Church practice

*[[NRSVue 1 Timothy 02#^2-8|Text]], p. 435* ^2-8

**[[NRSVue 1 Timothy 02#^2-8|8]]:** *Men should pray*, Gk “andras,” “men,” suggests that only males are so instructed. *Lifting up holy hands*, a gesture of prayer ([[Psalms 028#^28-2|Ps 28.2]]; [[Psalms 063#^63-5|63.4]] \[HB v 5\]; 134.2; 141.2. Exhortations to prayer are common Jewish sources, e.g., *m. Ber*. 4.3, “R. Gamaliel says, ‘Each day a man should pray the Eighteen \[Benedictions\].’” ^2-8-2

**[[NRSVue 1 Timothy 02#^2-9|9–10]]:** *Dress themselves modestly*, concern for female modesty, also found elsewhere in the Pastorals (see 5.11–15; [[Titus 02#^2-5|Titus 2.5]]). ^2-9

**[[NRSVue 1 Timothy 02#^2-11|11–15]]:** Part of a larger discussion in the Pauline and post-Pauline writings about women’s roles. The instructions are consistent with those of Roman moralists (see Plutarch’s *Advice to a Bride and Groom* 11.31–33). Whereas [[Romans 16#^16-1|Rom 16.1–3]],[[Romans 16#^16-7|7]] mentions women in church leadership, [[1 Corinthians 14#^14-33|1 Cor 14.33–36]], which many scholars regard as a non-Pauline addition, expresses reservations similar to those found here. ^2-11

**[[NRSVue 1 Timothy 02#^2-13|13–15]]:** Whereas [[1 Corinthians 14#^14-33|1 Cor 14.33b–36]] could be specific to the Corinthian church, 1 Timothy grounds female subordination in creation. *Adam was formed first*, the view that the earlier creation is the more important appears also in *Sifre Deut.* 11.10. Other rabbinic passages, following from [[Torah 01 - Bereshit#^1-27|Gen 1.27]] and 5.2, highlight an equal creation in which humanity was created as androgynous, and then God separated them (*Gen. Rab.* 8.1). *Not deceived*, Adam knowingly gave up Eden for the sake of his deceived wife; Gen 3 suggests that both the man and the woman are culpable (see [[Torah 01 - Bereshit#^3-17|Gen 3.17]]). *Will be saved through childbearing*, the Greek indicates salvation by bearing children, not being saved from death in childbirth. The author condemns women, including married women, who choose celibacy (see [[1 Corinthians 07#^7-7|1 Cor 7.7]]) as part of their life in Christ. ^2-13

**[[NRSVue 1 Timothy 02#^2-15|15]]:** *Modesty*, Gk “sophrosyne,” has the sense of self-control or prudence. The NRSV translates “sophrosyne” as “modesty” for [[1 Timothy 02#^2-9|1 Tim 2.9]],[[1 Timothy 02#^2-15|15]] (in relation to women) but as “sober truth” in [[Acts 26#^26-25|Acts 26.25]] (in relation to Paul) and “self-control” in relation to “young men” (the infinitive form of the term) in [[Titus 02#^2-6|Titus 2.6]]. ^2-15
