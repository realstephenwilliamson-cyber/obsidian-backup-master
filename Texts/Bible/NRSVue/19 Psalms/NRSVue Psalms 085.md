## Prayer for the restoration of God’s favor

*To the leader. Of the Korahites. A Psalm.*

**85:1** LORD, you were favorable to your land; you restored the fortunes of Jacob. ^85-1

**85:2** You forgave the iniquity of your people; you pardoned all their sin. *Selah* ^85-2

**85:3** You withdrew all your wrath; you turned from your hot anger. ^85-3

**85:4** Restore us again, O God of our salvation, and put away your indignation toward us. ^85-4

**85:5** Will you be angry with us forever? Will you prolong your anger to all generations? ^85-5

**85:6** Will you not revive us again, so that your people may rejoice in you? ^85-6

**85:7** Show us your steadfast love, O LORD, and grant us your salvation. ^85-7

**85:8** Let me hear what God the LORD will speak, for he will speak peace to his people, to his faithful, to those who turn to him in their hearts.^[Gk: Heb *but let them not turn back to folly*] ^85-8

**85:9** Surely his salvation is at hand for those who fear him, that his glory may dwell in our land. ^85-9

**85:10** Steadfast love and faithfulness will meet; righteousness and peace will kiss each other. ^85-10

**85:11** Faithfulness will spring up from the ground, and righteousness will look down from the sky. ^85-11

**85:12** The LORD will give what is good, and our land will yield its increase. ^85-12

**85:13** Righteousness will go before him and will make a path for his steps. ^85-13
