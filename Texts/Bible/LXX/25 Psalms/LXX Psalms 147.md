# LXX Psalms 147

← [[LXX Psalms 146]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 148]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 147]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 147]] (Heb 147:12–20 = LXX 147) · [[Psalms numbering]]

**147:1** Alleluia, *a Psalm* of Aggæus and Zacharias. Praise the Lord, O Jerusalem; praise thy God, O Sion.[^1]  ^147-1

**147:2** For he has strengthened the bars of thy gates; he has blessed thy children within thee.  ^147-2

**147:3** He makes thy borders peaceful, and fills thee with the flour of wheat.[^2]  ^147-3

**147:4** He sends his oracle to the earth: his word will run swiftly.  ^147-4

**147:5** He gives snow like wool: he scatters the mist like ashes.  ^147-5

**147:6** Casting *forth* his ice like morsels: who shall stand before his cold?  ^147-6

**147:7** He shall send out his word, and melt them: he shall blow *with* his wind, and the waters shall flow.  ^147-7

**147:8** He sends his word to Jacob, his ordinances and judgments to Israel.  ^147-8

**147:9** He has not done so to any *other* nation; and he has not shewn them his judgments.[^3]  ^147-9

[^1]: 147:1 In the original text, the following verses are numbered 12 to 20.
[^2]: 147:3 *Lit.* fat of wheat.
[^3]: 147:9 *Gr.* every.
