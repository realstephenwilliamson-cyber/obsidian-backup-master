# Psalm 107 (NRSVue)

[[NRSVue Psalms 106|← Psalm 106]] · [[NRSVue|index]] · [[NRSVue Psalms 108|Psalm 108 →]]

## Thanksgiving for deliverance from many troubles

**107:1** O give thanks to the LORD, for he is good, for his steadfast love endures forever. ^107-1

**107:2** Let the redeemed of the LORD say so, those he redeemed from trouble ^107-2

**107:3** and gathered in from the lands, from the east and from the west, from the north and from the south.^[Cn: Heb *sea*] ^107-3

**107:4** Some wandered in desert wastes, finding no way to an inhabited town; ^107-4

**107:5** hungry and thirsty, their soul fainted within them. ^107-5

**107:6** Then they cried to the LORD in their trouble, and he delivered them from their distress; ^107-6

**107:7** he led them by a straight way, until they reached an inhabited town. ^107-7

**107:8** Let them thank the LORD for his steadfast love, for his wonderful works to humankind. ^107-8

**107:9** For he satisfies the thirsty, and the hungry he fills with good things. ^107-9

**107:10** Some sat in darkness and in gloom, prisoners in misery and in irons, ^107-10

**107:11** for they had rebelled against the words of God and spurned the counsel of the Most High. ^107-11

**107:12** Their hearts were bowed down with hard labor; they fell down, with no one to help. ^107-12

**107:13** Then they cried to the LORD in their trouble, and he saved them from their distress; ^107-13

**107:14** he brought them out of darkness and gloom, and broke their bonds apart. ^107-14

**107:15** Let them thank the LORD for his steadfast love, for his wonderful works to humankind. ^107-15

**107:16** For he shatters the doors of bronze and cuts in two the bars of iron. ^107-16

**107:17** Some were sick^[Cn: Heb *fools*] through their sinful ways and because of their iniquities endured affliction; ^107-17

**107:18** they loathed any kind of food, and they drew near to the gates of death. ^107-18

**107:19** Then they cried to the LORD in their trouble, and he saved them from their distress; ^107-19

**107:20** he sent out his word and healed them and delivered them from destruction. ^107-20

**107:21** Let them thank the LORD for his steadfast love, for his wonderful works to humankind. ^107-21

**107:22** And let them offer thanksgiving sacrifices and tell of his deeds with songs of joy. ^107-22

**107:23** Some went down to the sea in ships, doing business on the mighty waters; ^107-23

**107:24** they saw the deeds of the LORD, his wondrous works in the deep. ^107-24

**107:25** For he commanded and raised the stormy wind, which lifted up the waves of the sea. ^107-25

**107:26** They mounted up to heaven; they went down to the depths; their courage melted away in their calamity; ^107-26

**107:27** they reeled and staggered like drunkards and were at their wits’ end. ^107-27

**107:28** Then they cried to the LORD in their trouble, and he brought them out from their distress; ^107-28

**107:29** he made the storm be still, and the waves of the sea were hushed. ^107-29

**107:30** Then they were glad because they had quiet, and he brought them to their desired haven. ^107-30

**107:31** Let them thank the LORD for his steadfast love, for his wonderful works to humankind. ^107-31

**107:32** Let them extol him in the congregation of the people and praise him in the assembly of the elders. ^107-32

**107:33** He turns rivers into a desert, springs of water into thirsty ground, ^107-33

**107:34** a fruitful land into a salty waste, because of the wickedness of its inhabitants. ^107-34

**107:35** He turns a desert into pools of water, a parched land into springs of water. ^107-35

**107:36** And there he lets the hungry live, and they establish a town to live in; ^107-36

**107:37** they sow fields and plant vineyards and get a fruitful yield. ^107-37

**107:38** By his blessing they multiply greatly, and he does not let their cattle decrease. ^107-38

**107:39** When they are diminished and brought low through oppression, trouble, and sorrow, ^107-39

**107:40** he pours contempt on princes and makes them wander in trackless wastes, ^107-40

**107:41** but he raises up the needy out of distress and makes their families like flocks. ^107-41

**107:42** The upright see it and are glad, and all wickedness stops its mouth. ^107-42

**107:43** Let those who are wise pay attention to these things and consider the steadfast love of the LORD. ^107-43
