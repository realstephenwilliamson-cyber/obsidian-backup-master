## Mūla

स्वभ्यस्तज्ञानिनं सन्तं बुभूषुमथ भाविनम् ।
योग्यं ज्ञात्वा स्वाधिकारं गुरुस्तस्मै समर्पयेत् ॥ ^mula

svabhyastajñāninaṃ santaṃ bubhūṣumatha bhāvinam |
yogyaṃ jñātvā svādhikāraṃ gurustasmai samarpayet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 14 - Chapter Twenty-Three#^pg315|p. 295]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=315>))
>
> This initiation that is called that of (the teacher’s spiritual) son possesses (the power) to conjoin (yukti) (fettered souls) with those principles that are the objects of (their) enjoyment, and bestows authority to be botha teacher and an adept (sādhaka). Once the teacher has ascertained in due order that (his disciple) is a well-exercised man of knowledge, and is competent (to be a teacher) at present, wishes to be so (bubhūṣu), and will be so (in the future), should bestow (the consecration) upon him as an offering, along with his own competence (adhikāra) (to be a teacher). (with 23.2–3)

## Viveka

> [!viveka]- Viveka in English (Dyczkowski, vol. IX, pp. 295–296)
> ![[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 14 - Chapter Twenty-Three#Viveka on 23.2–3]]

> सैवाधिकारिणी भोग्यतत्त्वयुक्तिमती क्रमात् ॥
> स्वभ्यस्तज्ञानिनं सन्तं बुभूषुमथ भाविनम् ।
> योग्यं ज्ञात्वा स्वाधिकारं गुरुस्तस्मै समर्पयेत् ॥

यैषेति--सबीजा, अभिषेकाच्चानयोरधिकारः, स च परीक्ष्य दातव्यः इति आचार्यस्य तावत् परीक्षां कर्तुमारभते-- क्रमादित्यादिना, क्रमादिति--श्रुतचिन्तादिप्रमुखमित्यर्थः । एतच्च सर्वत्र संबन्धनीयम् । बुभूषुमिति--भाविनमिति—च स्वभ्यस्तज्ञानितायाम् ॥ २-३ ॥
