# Psalm 22

← [[BCP Psalm 021]] · [[BCP 1979]] · [[BCP Psalm 023]] →

*Deus, Deus meu* · Book One · Fourth Day: Evening Prayer

Hebrew text and JPS translation: [[Psalms 022]]

**22:1** My God, my God, why have you forsaken me? \*  
and are so far from my cry  
and from the words of my distress? ^22-1

**22:2** O my God, I cry in the daytime, but you do not answer; \*  
by night as well, but I find no rest. ^22-2

**22:3** Yet you are the Holy One, \*  
enthroned upon the praises of Israel. ^22-3

**22:4** Our forefathers put their trust in you; \*  
they trusted, and you delivered them. ^22-4

**22:5** They cried out to you and were delivered; \*  
they trusted in you and were not put to shame. ^22-5

**22:6** But as for me, I am a worm and no man, \*  
scorned by all and despised by the people. ^22-6

**22:7** All who see me laugh me to scorn; \*  
they curl their lips and wag their heads, saying, ^22-7

**22:8** "He trusted in the LORD; let him deliver him; \*  
let him rescue him, if he delights in him." ^22-8

**22:9** Yet you are he who took me out of the womb, \*  
and kept me safe upon my mother's breast. ^22-9

**22:10** I have been entrusted to you ever since I was born; \*  
you were my God when I was still in my mother's womb. ^22-10

**22:11** Be not far from me, for trouble is near, \*  
and there is none to help. ^22-11

[p. 611] ^pg611

**22:12** Many young bulls encircle me; \*  
strong bulls of Bashan surround me. ^22-12

**22:13** They open wide their jaws at me, \*  
like a ravening and a roaring lion. ^22-13

**22:14** I am poured out like water;  
all my bones are out of joint; \*  
my heart within my breast is melting wax. ^22-14

**22:15** My mouth is dried out like a pot-sherd;  
my tongue sticks to the roof of my mouth; \*  
and you have laid me in the dust of the grave. ^22-15

**22:16** Packs of dogs close me in,  
and gangs of evildoers circle around me; \*  
they pierce my hands and my feet;  
I can count all my bones. ^22-16

**22:17** They stare and gloat over me; \*  
they divide my garments among them;  
they cast lots for my clothing. ^22-17

**22:18** Be not far away, O LORD; \*  
you are my strength; hasten to help me. ^22-18

**22:19** Save me from the sword, \*  
my life from the power of the dog. ^22-19

**22:20** Save me from the lion's mouth, \*  
my wretched body from the horns of wild bulls. ^22-20

**22:21** I will declare your Name to my brethren; \*  
in the midst of the congregation I will praise you. ^22-21

**22:22** Praise the LORD, you that fear him; \*  
stand in awe of him, O offspring of Israel;  
all you of Jacob's line, give glory. ^22-22

[p. 612] ^pg612

**22:23** For he does not despise nor abhor the poor in their poverty;  
neither does he hide his face from them; \*  
but when they cry to him he hears them. ^22-23

**22:24** My praise is of him in the great assembly; \*  
I will perform my vows in the presence of those who worship him. ^22-24

**22:25** The poor shall eat and be satisfied,  
and those who seek the LORD shall praise him: \*  
"May your heart live for ever!" ^22-25

**22:26** All the ends of the earth shall remember and turn to the LORD, \*  
and all the families of the nations bow before him. ^22-26

**22:27** For kingship belongs to the LORD; \*  
he rules over the nations. ^22-27

**22:28** To him alone all who sleep in the earth bow down in worship; \*  
all who go down to the dust fall before him. ^22-28

**22:29** My soul shall live for him;  
my descendants shall serve him; \*  
they shall be known as the LORD'S for ever. ^22-29

**22:30** They shall come and make known to a people yet unborn \*  
the saving deeds that he has done. ^22-30
