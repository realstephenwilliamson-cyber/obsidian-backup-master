## Mūla

दीक्षा हि नाम संस्कारो न त्वन्यत्सोऽस्ति चास्य हि ।
अत एव निजं शास्त्रं पठति क्वापि सामये ॥ ^mula

dīkṣā hi nāma saṃskāro na tvanyatso'sti cāsya hi |
ata eva nijaṃ śāstraṃ paṭhati kvāpi sāmaye || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 10 - Chapter Nineteen#^pg246|p. 226]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=246>))
> Dyczkowski numbers this verse 19.45cd–46ab.
>
> Once he has awakened, he is not said to be uninitiated. Initiation is (essentially) nothing but (an empowering and purifying) sacrament (saṁskāra), and he does indeed possess it. (with 19.45cd–46ab)
>
> Thus, if (some) fortunate man is liberated (on his death bed), having heard his own (appropriate) (nija) scripture recited somewhere by (even) a common initiate, he is not (guilty of) that defect (kṣati). (with 19.46cd–47ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* एवं शास्त्रपाठादिनापि परोपकृतावस्य न कश्चित्समयलोपजन्मा दोषः--इत्याह--

> अत एव निजं शास्त्रं पठति क्वापि सामये ॥
> तच्छ्रुत्वा कोऽपि धन्यश्चेन्मुच्यते नास्य सा क्षतिः ।

सामय इति--समयिनि--इत्यर्थः ॥
