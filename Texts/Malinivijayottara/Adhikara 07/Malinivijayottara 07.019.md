## Mūla

मध्यमानामिकाभ्यां च तदङ्गुष्ठं निपीडयेत् ।
मुद्रा महोदयाख्येयं महोदयकरी नृणाम् ॥ ^mula

madhyamānāmikābhyāṃ ca tadaṅguṣṭhaṃ nipīḍayet |
mudrā mahodayākhyeyaṃ mahodayakarī nṛṇām || ^iast

## Translation

>
> **10. Mahodayā (the Great Arising)**
>
> The left (hand) faces upwards, middle and ring fingers of the right (hand) that faces downwards should be pressed with the thumb of the left (hand), the little finger with the index, and the index with the little finger, and that thumb should be pressed with the middle and ring finger. This is the gesture called the Great Arising which greatly elevates men. (with 7.17cd–19)
