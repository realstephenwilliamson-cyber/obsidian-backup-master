### 21.1–4: The widow’s offering

*[[NRSVue Luke 21#^21-1|Text]], p. 158* · ([[Mark 12#^12-41|Mk 12.41–44]]). ^21-1

**[[NRSVue Luke 21#^21-1|1]]:** *Treasury*, receptacle for free-will offerings (*m. Seqal*. 2.1; 6.1; in [[John 08#^8-20|Jn 8.20]] the term refers to a room in the Temple). ^21-1-2

**[[NRSVue Luke 21#^21-2|2]]:** *Copper coins* (Gk “lepton”), see 12.59n. ^21-2

**[[NRSVue Luke 21#^21-4|4]]:** *All she had*, Jesus praises the women’s charity (see 6.30n.; *Lev. Rab*. 107a offers the illustrative story of a priest who rejects a woman’s offering of a handful of meal, and is rebuked by God). Jesus does not, contrary to some commentaries, condemn the Temple for exploitation. Had Jesus believed the woman was exploited, he could have advised her to keep her coins. ^21-4

### 21.5–6: Predicting the Temple’s destruction

*[[NRSVue Luke 21#^21-5|Text]], p. 158* · ([[Matthew 24#^24-1|Mt 24.1–2]]; [[Mark 13#^13-1|Mk 13.1–2]]). ^21-5

**[[NRSVue Luke 21#^21-5|5]]:** *Beautiful stones*, Josephus, *Ant*. 15.416 notes, “and that front was all of polished stone, insomuch that its fineness, to such as had not seen it, was incredible, and to such as had seen it, was greatly amazing” (cf. 15.392; *J.W*. 223). ^21-5-2

**[[NRSVue Luke 21#^21-6|6]]:** See 19.43–44n. ^21-6

### 21.7–11: End-time signs

*[[NRSVue Luke 21#^21-7|Text]], p. 158* · ([[Matthew 24#^24-3|Mt 24.3–8]]; [[Mark 13#^13-3|Mk 13.3–8]]). ^21-7

**[[NRSVue Luke 21#^21-7|7]]:** *When will this be*, see 17.20–21n.; [[Acts 01#^1-6|Acts 1.6]]. ^21-7-2

**[[NRSVue Luke 21#^21-10|10]]:** *Nation will rise against nation*, conventional imagery associated with the appearance of divine justice ([[Isaiah 19#^19-2|Isa 19.2]]). ^21-10

**[[NRSVue Luke 21#^21-11|11]]:** *Earthquakes . . . famines . . . plagues*, conventional apocalyptic language (e.g., Rev passim*; 4 Ezra* \[2 Esd\] 9.3). ^21-11

### 21.12–19: Disciples’ persecution

*[[NRSVue Luke 21#^21-12|Text]], p. 158* · ([[Matthew 24#^24-9|Mt 24.9–14]]; [[Mark 13#^13-9|Mk 13.9–13]]). ^21-12

**[[NRSVue Luke 21#^21-12|12]]:** *Arrest and persecute*, localized persecution (e.g., Nero’s persecution in 64 CE in Rome) occurred, but the first empire-wide persecution did not occur until the Decian persecution of 250 CE. *Synagogues*, see 12.11n. ^21-12-2

**[[NRSVue Luke 21#^21-14|14–15]]:** See 12.11–12. ^21-14

**[[NRSVue Luke 21#^21-16|16]]:** *Parents and brothers*, see 12.51n. ^21-16

**[[NRSVue Luke 21#^21-18|18]]:** *Hair of your head*, indicating immunity; see 12.7; [[I Samuel 14#^14-45|1 Sam 14.45]]; [[II Samuel 14#^14-11|2 Sam 14.11]]; [[I Kings 01#^1-52|1 Kings 1.52]]; [[Acts 27#^27-34|Acts 27.34]]. ^21-18

**[[NRSVue Luke 21#^21-19|19]]:** *Gain your souls*, have eternal life. ^21-19

### 21.20–24: Jerusalem’s

*[[NRSVue Luke 21#^21-20|Text]], p. 159* · **desolation** ([[Matthew 24#^24-15|Mt 24.15–22]]; [[Mark 13#^13-14|Mk 13.14–20]]). ^21-20

**[[NRSVue Luke 21#^21-20|20]]:** *Surrounded by armies*, see 19.43–44n. ^21-20-2

**[[NRSVue Luke 21#^21-22|22]]:** *Days of vengeance*, see [[Isaiah 63#^63-4|Isa 63.4]]; [[Hosea 09#^9-7|Hos 9.7]]. ^21-22

**[[NRSVue Luke 21#^21-23|23]]:** *Pregnant . . . nursing*, see 12.51n. ^21-23

**[[NRSVue Luke 21#^21-24|24]]:** *Times of the Gentiles*, perhaps referring to the Gentile mission (see [[Romans 11#^11-25|Rom 11.25]]) or to the time following the destruction of Jerusalem in 70 CE; the period has no distinguishing markers or temporal limits. ^21-24

### 21.25–28: Coming of the Son of Man

*[[NRSVue Luke 21#^21-25|Text]], p. 159* · ([[Matthew 24#^24-29|Mt 24.29–31]]; [[Mark 13#^13-24|Mk 13.24–27]]). ^21-25

**[[NRSVue Luke 21#^21-25|25–27]]:** Apocalyptic elements drawn from [[Joel 3#^3-3|Joel 3.3–4]]; [[LXX Isaiah 24#^24-19|Isa 24.19]]\[LXX\]; [[Psalms 046#^46-4|Ps 46.4]]; [[Psalms 065#^65-8|65.8]]; [[Psalms 089#^89-10|89.10]]; [[LXX Wisdom of Solomon 05#^5-22|Wis 5.22]]. ^21-25-2

**[[NRSVue Luke 21#^21-25|25]]:** [[Isaiah 13#^13-10|Isa 13.10]]; [[Joel 2#^2-10|Joel 2.10]]; [[Zephaniah 1#^1-15|Zeph 1.15]]. ^21-25-3

**[[NRSVue Luke 21#^21-26|26]]:** *Powers of the heavens*, celestial bodies or/and supernatural beings ([[Romans 08#^8-38|Rom 8.38]]; [[Ephesians 06#^6-12|Eph 6.12]]; [[Colossians 01#^1-16|Col 1.16]]; [[Hebrews 06#^6-4|Heb 6.4]]; [[1 Peter 03#^3-22|1 Pet 3.22]]). ^21-26

**[[NRSVue Luke 21#^21-27|27]]:** *Son of Man*, see 5.24n. *Cloud*, see [[Daniel 07#^7-13|Dan 7.13]]; [[Acts 01#^1-9|Acts 1.9]]. ^21-27

### 21.29–33: Lesson of the fig tree

*[[NRSVue Luke 21#^21-29|Text]], p. 159* · ([[Matthew 24#^24-32|Mt 24.32–36]]; [[Mark 13#^13-28|Mk 13.28–32]]). ^21-29

**[[NRSVue Luke 21#^21-32|32]]:** See 9.27n. ^21-32

**[[NRSVue Luke 21#^21-33|33]]:** See 16.17. *My words will not pass away*, combination of themes from Isaiah about God’s reliable and permanent word (e.g., [[Isaiah 40#^40-8|Isa 40.8]] \[“The word of God will stand forever”\]; 54.10). ^21-33

### 21.34–36: Admonition to alertness

*[[NRSVue Luke 21#^21-34|Text]], p. 159* · (See [[Matthew 24#^24-43|Mt 24.43–51]]; [[Matthew 25#^25-13|25.13]]; [[Mark 13#^13-33|Mk 13.33–37]]; see also [[Luke 12#^12-35|Luke 12.35–48]]). ^21-34

**[[NRSVue Luke 21#^21-34|34]]:** *Drunkenness*, see Tob 4.15; Sir 31.30; [[Romans 13#^13-13|Rom 13.13]]; [[Galatians 05#^5-21|Gal 5.21]]; [[1 Peter 04#^4-3|1 Pet 4.3]]. ^21-34-2

**[[NRSVue Luke 21#^21-35|35]]:** *Like a trap*, as with the image of the “thief in the night,” one can take precautions, but one cannot predict the timing of the eschaton. **36**: *Son of Man*, see 5.24n.; 21.25–28; the images recollect 18.1–8. ^21-35

**[[NRSVue Luke 21#^21-37|37]]:** *Mount of Olives*, see 19.29n. ^21-37-2
