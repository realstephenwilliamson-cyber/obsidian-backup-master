## Mūla

तयोरेव विभागे तु शक्तितद्वत्प्रकल्पने ।
शब्दराशिर्मालिनी च क्षोभात्म वपुरीदृशम् ॥ ^mula

tayoreva vibhāge tu śaktitadvatprakalpane |
śabdarāśirmālinī ca kṣobhātma vapurīdṛśam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 07 - Chapter Thirty-Three#^pg196|p. 178]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=196>))
>
> This consciousness, which is the essential nature (ātman) (of all things), is one alone, and its essence, the reflective awareness of all things. (Proceeding) from this is power and its possessor, which are (respectively) said to be Mātṛ (Mother) and Śabdarāśi. When these two divide (further) and are conceived to be power and its possessor, (they are then) Śabdarāśi and Mālinī, whose essential nature is the arousal (into manifestation of Mātṛkā,) and body is like this. (with 33.20–21)

## Viveka

> [!viveka]- Viveka in English (Dyczkowski, vol. XI, pp. 178–179)
> ![[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 07 - Chapter Thirty-Three#Viveka on 33.20–21]]

> एक एव चिदात्मैष विश्वामर्शनसारकः ।
> शक्तिस्तद्वानतो माता शब्दराशिः प्रकीर्तितौ ॥
> तयोरेव विभागे तु शक्तितद्वत्प्रकल्पने ।
> शब्दराशिर्मालिनी च क्षोभात्म वपुरीदृशम् ॥

अत इति--शक्तितद्वद्विभागस्य आसूत्रणात् । माता मातृका । तयोरिति - -मातृकाशब्दराश्योः । ननु मालिन्याः शक्तित्वे किं निमित्तम्?-- इत्याशङ्क्य आह--क्षोभात्म वपुरीदृशमिति ॥ २१ ॥
