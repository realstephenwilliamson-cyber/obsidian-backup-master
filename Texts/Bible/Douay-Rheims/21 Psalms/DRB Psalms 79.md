# Psalms 79 (Douay-Rheims)

[[DRB Psalms 78|← Psalms 78]] · [[Douay-Rheims|index]] · [[DRB Psalms 80|Psalms 80 →]]

Compare: [[LXX Psalms 79|LXX (Brenton), same numbering]] · the Hebrew numbering: [[Psalms numbering]]

**79:1** Unto the end, for them that shall he changed, a testimony for Asaph, a psalm. ^79-1

**79:2** Give ear, O thou that rulest Israel: thou that leadest Joseph like a sheep. Thou that sittest upon the cherubims, shine forth ^79-2

**79:3** Before Ephraim, Benjamin, and Manasses. Stir up thy might, and come to save us. ^79-3

**79:4** Convert us, O God: and shew us thy face, and we shall be saved. ^79-4

**79:5** O Lord God of hosts, how long wilt thou be angry against the prayer of thy servant? ^79-5

**79:6** How long wilt thou feed us with the bread of tears: and give us for our drink tears in measure? ^79-6

**79:7** Thou hast made us to be a contradiction to our neighbours: and our enemies have scoffed at us. ^79-7

**79:8** O God of hosts, convert us: and shew thy face, and we shall be saved. ^79-8

**79:9** Thou hast brought a vineyard out of Egypt: thou hast cast cut the Gentiles and planted it. ^79-9

**79:10** Thou wast the guide of its journey in its sight: thou plantedst the roots thereof, and it filled the land. ^79-10

**79:11** The shadow of it covered the hills: and the branches thereof the cedars of God. ^79-11

**79:12** It stretched forth its branches unto the sea, and its boughs unto the river. ^79-12

**79:13** Why hast thou broken down the hedge thereof, so that all they who pass by the way do pluck it? ^79-13

**79:14** The boar out of the wood hath laid it waste: and a singular wild beast hath devoured it. ^79-14

**79:15** Turn again, O God of hosts, look down from heaven, and see, and visit this vineyard: ^79-15

**79:16** And perfect the same which thy right hand hath planted: and upon the son of man whom thou hast confirmed for thyself. ^79-16

**79:17** Things set on fire and dug down shall perish at the rebuke of thy countenance. ^79-17

**79:18** Let thy hand be upon the man of thy right hand: and upon the son of man whom thou hast confirmed for thyself. ^79-18

**79:19** And we depart not from thee, thou shalt quicken us: and we will call upon thy name. ^79-19

**79:20** O Lord God of hosts, convert us: and shew thy face, and we shall be saved. ^79-20
