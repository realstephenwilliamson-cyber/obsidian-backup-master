## Mūla

अत्र क्रमे भेदतरोः समूलमुन्मूलनादासनपक्षचर्चा ।
पृथङ् न युक्ता परमेश्वरो हि स्वशक्तिधाम्नीव विशंश्रमीति ॥ ^mula

atra krame bhedataroḥ samūlamunmūlanādāsanapakṣacarcā |
pṛthaṅ na yuktā parameśvaro hi svaśaktidhāmnīva viśaṃśramīti || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 10 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 2#^pg293|p. 276]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=293>))
>
> Here in (this Kaula) liturgy (krama), the tree of duality has been pulled up root and all, and so it is unsound to consider (the Lord’s) throne to be an aspect (of reality that is) separate (from Him). (For here in this world,) the Supreme Lord is as if resting within the abode of His own powers.
