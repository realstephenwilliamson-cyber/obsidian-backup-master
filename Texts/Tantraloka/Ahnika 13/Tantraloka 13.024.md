## Mūla

अथाज्ञानं नह्यभावो मिथ्याज्ञानं तु तन्मतम् ।
तदेव कर्मणां स्वस्मिन्कर्तव्ये सहकारणम् ॥ ^mula

athājñānaṃ nahyabhāvo mithyājñānaṃ tu tanmatam |
tadeva karmaṇāṃ svasminkartavye sahakāraṇam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 05 - Chapter Thirteen, part 1#^pg157|p. 137]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=157>))
>
> Now (you may say that) ignorance (jñāna) is not (just) the absence (of knowledge), (rather) it is considered to be false knowledge (mithyājñāna). When actions give rise to their consequences, that (false knowledge) is (just) an ancillary cause.
