## Mūla

निराकारे हि चिद्धाम्नि विश्वाकृतिमये सति ।
फलार्थिनां काचिदेव ध्येयत्वेनाकृतिः स्थिता ॥ ^mula

nirākāre hi ciddhāmni viśvākṛtimaye sati |
phalārthināṃ kācideva dhyeyatvenākṛtiḥ sthitā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. III
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 03 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v03 09 - Chapter Four, part 8#^pg313|p. 300]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 03.pdf#page=313>))

> For those (adepts) who aspire to (particular) fruits, some (particular) form (through which they may attain them) is present within the formless abode of consciousness, omniform as it is, as the object of their meditation (dhyeya).
