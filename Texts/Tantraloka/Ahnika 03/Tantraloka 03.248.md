## Mūla

तत्परं त्रितयं तत्र शिवः परचिदात्मकः ।
विभागाभासनायां च मुख्यास्तिस्रोऽत्र शक्तयः ॥ ^mula

tatparaṃ tritayaṃ tatra śivaḥ paracidātmakaḥ |
vibhāgābhāsanāyāṃ ca mukhyāstisro'tra śaktayaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. II
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 02 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v02 11 - Chapter Three, part 8#^pg387|p. 371]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 02.pdf#page=387>))
>
> The triad that precedes these three, which is free of limiting adjuncts, is the supreme triad. Śiva, who is supreme consciousness, (manifests) there (at that level). (with 3.247cd–248ab)
>
> There are three principal powers here that are responsible for the manifestation of (these) divisions. They are the Absolute (Anuttarā), which is the Supreme (Parā power), the will, which abides as the Middle One (Parāparā), and the power of expansion called knowledge, which is said to be the Lower (Aparā power). (with 3.248cd–249)
