# ἀναφαίνω

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · verb

**Dodson:** I sight, I appear — (a) a nautical term: I sight (a place), (b) mid: I appear (as it were, out of the unseen), (c) I bring to light, make to appear. (Strong's 398; headword ἀναφαίνω)
