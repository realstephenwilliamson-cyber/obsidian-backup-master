# 1 Corinthians 8 (JANT notes)

[[JANT 1 Corinthians 07|← 1 Corinthians 7]] · [[Jewish Annotated New Testament|JANT index]] · [[JANT 1 Corinthians 09|1 Corinthians 9 →]]

Text: [[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 08|NRSVue]] · [[1 Corinthians 08|BSB and SBLGNT]] · Introduction: [[JANT 1 Corinthians - Introduction]]

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 08#^8-1|8.1–13]]:** **Dietary concerns.** Paul considers the final topic of purity for the Corinthian community: how believers should avoid any kind of association with idolatry. ^8-1

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 08#^8-1|1]]:** *Food sacrificed to idols*, a derogatory word (Gk “eidōlothutos”) used by Jews and Christians for pagan sacrifices. Some believers may have eaten the food for reasons relating to social status and familial loyalties. Jews generally avoided such fare and pagan ceremonial meals (Philo, *Dreams* 2.123; *Joseph* 154; *Spec. Laws* 3.126, and elsewhere); Roman authorities allowed them to bring “suitable food” (Gk “trophē epitēdeia”) into local marketplaces (Josephus, *Ant.* 14.259–61). *All of us possess knowledge*, 1.5n.; the phrase is likely another Corinthian slogan. *Puffs up*, see 5.6n.; [[Isaiah 02#^2-11|Isa 2.11–12]],[[Isaiah 02#^2-17|17]] construe idolatry as hubris. *Love* (Gk “agape”) *builds up,* rather than fractures communities (see 8.10). *Love,* epitomizes the law ([[Romans 13#^13-8|Rom 13.8]],[[Romans 13#^13-10|10]]; [[Galatians 05#^5-14|Gal 5.14]]; see [[Matthew 22#^22-37|Mt 22.37–40]]). ^8-1-2

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 08#^8-2|2]]:** *Know,* from scripture (see 8.4). *Knowledge* of the one God (see 8.7n.; [[Daniel 12#^12-3|Dan 12.3]]). ^8-2

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 08#^8-4|4]]:** *No idol . . . exists*, see Jon 2.8; Ps 115; [[II Chronicles 13#^13-9|2 Chr 13.9]]; Wis 13. *There is no God but one*, a core Jewish belief ([[Torah 45 - Vaetchanan#^6-4|Deut 6.4]]; [[Mark 12#^12-29|Mk 12.29]]). Following prophetic tradition (e.g., [[Jeremiah 02#^2-28|Jer 2.28]]), Paul combines the scriptural prohibitions against idolatry and polytheism ([[Torah 17 - Yitro#^20-3|Ex 20.3–4]]), prompting his twofold concern about Corinthian participation in pagan cultic meals: the statues are merely empty vessels (8.7), and the worship of many gods is false because there is only one God worthy of veneration (8.5). ^8-4

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 08#^8-5|5]]:** *In fact*, Paul does not deny the existence of multiple heavenly and earthly powers; he claims only that these are not proper objects of worship ([[Torah 45 - Vaetchanan#^4-19|Deut 4.19]]; [[Torah 48 - Shoftim#^17-3|17.3]]). Jewish cosmology envisions myriads of supernatural beings with various functions ([[I Kings 22#^22-19|1 Kings 22.19]]; [[Isaiah 06#^6-3|Isa 6.3]]; [[Psalms 148#^148-2|Ps 148.2]]; [[LXX Tobit 08#^8-3|Tob 8.3]]; [[LXX Baruch 04#^4-7|Bar 4.7]]; *Jub.* 1.20; 2.2,18; 3.15; 10.7–9; 18.9). Alternatively, Paul may be citing beliefs held by the Corinthians in order to refute them in v 6. *Many gods and many lords*, titles given to the *so-called gods* of the Gentiles. ^8-5

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 08#^8-6|6]]:** *Father*, see 1.3n. *Exist*, people were created by God and should live for God’s sake; Jesus is the vehicle by which *all things* . . . *exist*, some Jewish literature depicts God’s attribute of Wisdom as the means by which the world was created ([[Proverbs 08#^8-22|Prov 8.22]]; [[LXX Sirach 01#^1-4|Sir 1.4]]; Philo, *Drunkenness* 30–31; cf. [[Colossians 01#^1-15|Col 1.15–17]] \[probably not Pauline\], perhaps recapitulating [[LXX Wisdom of Solomon 07#^7-22|Wis 7.22–8.1]]). ^8-6

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 08#^8-7|7]]:** *Knowledge,* of the one God and therefore that *idols* are not gods. *Defiled*, see [[Ezekiel 20#^20-18|Ezek 20.18]]; [[Ezekiel 23#^23-30|23.30]]. ^8-7

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 08#^8-8|8–9]]:** Paul favorably quotes the Corinthian viewpoint that what one eats does not impact one’s relationship *to God*. *Stumbling block*, by analogy with the Lord’s Supper, through which believers affirmed belief in Jesus, the weak might be misled by pagan meal-sharing into affirming pagan deities (11.20–34). ^8-8

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 08#^8-10|10]]:** *Temple of an idol*, banquet areas within temple complexes. *Conscience*, awareness of God and conviction that idols are not gods. Rabbinic strictures prohibited even the appearance of misconduct because of its potential influence on others (see *y. Demai* 6.2; see also the example of Eleazar in [[LXX 2 Maccabees 06#^6-21|2 Macc 6.21–28]]). ^8-10

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 08#^8-12|12]]:** *Sin*, mislead believers ([[Isaiah 09#^9-15|Isa 9.16]]; [[Ezekiel 13#^13-9|Ezek 13.9–10]]; [[Micah 3#^3-5|Mic 3.5]]). ^8-12

**[[NRSVue 1 Corinthians 08#^8-13|13]]:** *Never eat meat*, since meat sold in the marketplace may have been used in pagan sacrifice, Corinthians could avoid sin by abstaining from meat. Paul’s theory parallels the rabbinic concept of “lifnei meshurat ha-din,” extending the boundaries of the law to ensure fidelity ([[Bava Metzia 24#24b|*b. B. Metz.* 24b]]). ^8-13
