# LXX Psalms 57

← [[LXX Psalms 56]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 58]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 058]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 058]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**57:1** For the end. Destroy not: by David, for a memorial.  ^57-1

**57:2** If ye do indeed speak righteousness, *then* do ye judge rightly, ye sons of men.  ^57-2

**57:3** For ye work iniquities in *your* hearts in the earth: your hands plot unrighteousness.  ^57-3

**57:4** Sinners have gone astray from the womb: they go astray from the belly: they speak lies.  ^57-4

**57:5** Their venom is like *that* of a serpent; as *that* of a deaf asp, and that stops her ears;  ^57-5

**57:6** which will not hear the voice of charmers, nor *heed* the charm prepared skilfully by the wise.[^1]  ^57-6

**57:7** God has crushed their teeth in their mouth: God has broken the cheek-teeth of the lions.  ^57-7

**57:8** They shall utterly pass away like water running through: he shall bend his bow till they shall fail.  ^57-8

**57:9** They shall be destroyed as melted wax: the fire has fallen and they have not seen the sun.  ^57-9

**57:10** Before your thorns feel the white thorn, he shall swallow you up as living, as in his wrath.  ^57-10

**57:11** The righteous shall rejoice when he sees the vengeance of the ungodly: he shall wash his hands in the blood of the sinner.  ^57-11

**57:12** And a man shall say, Verily then there is a reward for the righteous: verily there is a God that judges them in the earth.[^2]  ^57-12

[^1]: 57:6 *Gr.* poison.
[^2]: 57:12 *Gr.* fruit.
