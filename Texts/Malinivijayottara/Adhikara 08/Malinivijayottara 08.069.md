## Mūla

तस्य नाभ्युत्थितं शक्तिशूलशृङ्गत्रयं स्मरेत् ।
कखत्रयेण निर्यातं द्वादशान्तावसानकम् ॥ ^mula

tasya nābhyutthitaṁ śaktiśūlaśṛṅgatrayaṁ smaret |
kakhatrayeṇa niryātaṁ dvādaśāntāvasānakam || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, *Mālinī*, January 2025 – April 2025
> p. 19 ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Malinivijayottaratantra, chapters 7-8 (2024-2025).pdf#page=33>))
>
> **The Three Prongs**
>
> One should (now) recollect the three prongs of the Trident of Energy (śaktiśūla) that have arisen from (Sadāśiva's) navel. They exit as three (rays from) within the Cavity of Brahmā[^77] (on the crown of the head) and end in the End of the Twelve. Then one should think that on its prongs there are three lotuses empowered (by the three powers), white and presiding over all. This is the supreme throne.[^78] (with 8.69–70)

[^77]: Read *kakhe trayeṇa* for *kakhatayeṇa.*
[^78]: MV 8/69-70 are quoted in TĀv ad 15/313-314, which is an explanatory paraphrase of them.
