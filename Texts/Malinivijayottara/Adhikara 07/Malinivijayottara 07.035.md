## Mūla

दक्षिणं नाभिमूले तु वामस्योपरि संस्थितम् ।
तर्जन्यङ्गुष्ठकौ लग्नौ उच्छ्रितौ योगकर्मणि ॥ ^mula

dakṣiṇaṃ nābhimūle tu vāmasyopari saṃsthitam |
tarjanyaṅguṣṭhakau lagnau ucchritau yogakarmaṇi || ^iast

*Dyczkowski prints no Sanskrit for this chapter; the text is that of the KSTS edition (Muktabodha M00160).*

> **25. Yogamudrā**
>
> The right (hand), placed on top of the left one, is (placed) at the base (*mūla*) of the navel with the index and thumbs connected together and upraised – (this gesture is made) when practicing yoga (*yogakarman*).
