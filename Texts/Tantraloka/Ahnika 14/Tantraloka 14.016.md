## Mūla

तत्रापि चेच्छावैचित्र्यादिहामुत्रोभयात्मकः ।
दुःखस्यापि विभेदोऽस्ति चिरशैघ्र्यकृतस्तथा ॥ ^mula

tatrāpi cecchāvaicitryādihāmutrobhayātmakaḥ |
duḥkhasyāpi vibhedo'sti ciraśaighryakṛtastathā || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 12 - Chapter Fourteen, and Appendices to Chapters Eleven and Twelve#^pg375|p. 353]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=375>))
>
> Due to the variety (of forms) of the (divine) will, there (in that case) also, (as with grace,) there are various types of suffering, according to whether it is of this world, the other or both or whether it (lasts) a long (time) or (passes) quickly.
