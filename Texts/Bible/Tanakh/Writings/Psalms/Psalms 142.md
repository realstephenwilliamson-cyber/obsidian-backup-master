## Chapter 142

**142:1** A maskil of David, while he was in the cave.[^1] A prayer. ^142-1

**142:2** I cry aloud to the LORD;  
I appeal to the LORD loudly for mercy. ^142-2

**142:3** I pour out my complaint before Him;  
I lay my trouble before Him ^142-3

**142:4** when my spirit fails within me.  
You know my course;  
they have laid a trap in the path I walk. ^142-4

**142:5** Look at my right and see—  
I have no friend;  
there is nowhere I can flee,  
no one cares about me. ^142-5

**142:6** So I cry to You, O LORD;  
I say, “You are my refuge,  
all I have in the land of the living.” ^142-6

**142:7** Listen to my cry, for I have been brought very low;  
save me from my pursuers,  
for they are too strong for me. ^142-7

**142:8** Free me from prison,  
that I may praise Your name.  
The righteous shall glory in me[^2]  
for Your gracious dealings with me. ^142-8

[^1]: Cf. 1 Sam. 24.3–4.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
