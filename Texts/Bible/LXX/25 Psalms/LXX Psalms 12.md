# LXX Psalms 12

← [[LXX Psalms 11]] · [[LXX]] · [[LXX Psalms 13]] →

*Psalms · [[LXX]]*

Hebrew numbering (JPS): [[Psalms 013]] · Prayer Book (1979): [[BCP Psalm 013]] · [[Psalms numbering]]

**12:1** For the end, a Psalm of David.  ^12-1

**12:2** How long, O Lord, wilt thou forget me? for ever? how long wilt thou turn away thy face from me?  ^12-2

**12:3** How long shall I take counsel in my soul, *having* sorrows in my heart daily? how long shall my enemy be exalted over me?  ^12-3

**12:4** Look on me, hearken to me, O Lord my God: lighten mine eyes, lest I sleep in death;  ^12-4

**12:5** lest at any time mine enemy say, I have prevailed against him: my persecutors will exult if ever I should be moved.  ^12-5

**12:6** But I have hoped in thy mercy; my heart shall exult in thy salvation. I will sing to the Lord who has dealt bountifully with me, and I will sing psalms to the name of the Lord most high.  ^12-6
