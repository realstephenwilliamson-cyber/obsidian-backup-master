## Mūla

विचिकित्सा गलत्यन्तस्तथाऽसौ यत्नवान्भवेत् ।
धीकर्माक्षगता देवीर्निषिद्धैरेव तर्पयेत् ॥ ^mula

vicikitsā galatyantastathā'sau yatnavānbhavet |
dhīkarmākṣagatā devīrniṣiddhaireva tarpayet || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. VII
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 07 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v07 04 - Chapter Twelve#^pg131|p. 111]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 07.pdf#page=131>))
>
> (The Kaula adept) should exert himself in whatever way and by whatever means (his) inner uncertainty passes away, be it gradually or instantaneously. (with 12.18cd–19ab)
>
> (The adept) should offer libation to the goddesses who reside in the senses of knowledge and action (with sacrificial offerings that would otherwise be) prohibited. (with 12.19cd)
