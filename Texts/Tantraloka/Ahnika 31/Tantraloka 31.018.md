## Mūla

सुतीक्ष्णकुटिलाग्रं तदेकं शृङ्गं प्रजायते ।
द्वितीयस्मिन्नपि प्रोक्तः शृङ्ग एष विधिः स्फुटः ॥ ^mula

sutīkṣṇakuṭilāgraṃ tadekaṃ śṛṅgaṃ prajāyate |
dvitīyasminnapi proktaḥ śṛṅga eṣa vidhiḥ sphuṭaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 04 - Chapter Thirty-One, part 1#^pg92|p. 74]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=92>))

> Then omitting (another) two squares horizontally, one should fashion from (the last) half-moon in the west up to the corner, two semi-circles. One prong, which is sharp and point curved, is created (in this way). This same clear procedure, (is applied) to (draw) the second prong also. (with 31.17–18)
