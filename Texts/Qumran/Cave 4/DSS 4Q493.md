### Text

**1** the war. And the priests, sons of Aaron, shall take position in front of [the] lines ^t-1

**2** and blow the trumpets of memorial. And afterwards, they shall open the g[ate]s to the ^t-2

**3** infantrymen. The priests shall blow the battle trumpets [to strike] the lines ^t-3

**4** of the nations. The priests shall go out from among the slain and take posi[tion on one side and] on the other of … ^t-4

**5** on the side of the catapult and the ballista, and they shall not desecrate the oil of their priesthood [with the blood of the s]lai[n,] ^t-5

**6** nor shall they approach any of the lines of the infantrymen. They shall blow a shrill note so that the m[en] ^t-6

**7** of war go out to attack between the lines of the trumpets of [the slain.] And [t]he[y] shall b[e]gin ^t-7

**8** to stretch out the hand for battle. When their task is accomplished, they shall blow for them the trum[p]ets of withdr[a]wal ^t-8

**9** so that they enter the *[p. 985]* gates; and the second line shall go out. In accordance with this entire rule the le[vites] shall blow ^t-9

**10** for them during its task: when they go out [t]he[y] shall blow the tr[umpets of recall] for them, ^t-10

**11** and when [they] comple[te] (them), the trumpets [of alarm, and on] their withdrawal, [t]he[y] shall bl[ow for them the trumpets] ^t-11

**12** of asse[mbly.] According to [this] ordin[ance] shall they blow for a[ll the li]nes. Blank […] ^t-12

**13** […] on the trumpet[s] of the Sabbaths […] ^t-13

**14** and [… over the] perpetual [sacriﬁce] and the burnt-offerings is written «… […] ^t-14
