## Mūla

ध्यात्वा तेनास्य हृच्चक्रवेधनान्मन्त्रवेधनम् ।
आकारं नवधा देहे न्यस्य संक्रमयेत्ततः ॥ ^mula

dhyātvā tenāsya hṛccakravedhanānmantravedhanam |
ākāraṃ navadhā dehe nyasya saṃkramayettataḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. X
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 10 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v10 13 - Chapter Twenty-Nine, part 5#^pg397|p. 380]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 10.pdf#page=397>))
>
> 1) Once (the teacher) has visualized the wheel, full of flames, of eight spokes (or whatever number) is taught in his own scripture, he pierces the Wheel of (the disciple’s) heart with that. Thus, it is (called) piercing by Mantra. (with 29.240cd–241ab)
>
> Having deposited the letter A nine times on (his) body, (the teacher) should then transmit it, shining with great lustre, to the disciple by depositing it (on his body). Then (the disciple’s) bonds are paralysed and so he is united with the Supreme Reality. This is the procedure (I have) seen in the Dīkṣottara, taught to me by Sambhunātha. 241cd-243ab (with 29.241cd–243ab)
