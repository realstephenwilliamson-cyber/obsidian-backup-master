# Daniel C. Matt

A scholar and teacher of Kabbalah who translated and annotated *The Zohar: Pritzker Edition* (vols. 1–9, 2004–2016) after twenty years as a professor at the Center for Jewish Studies of the Graduate Theological Union in Berkeley; he now teaches online ([his site](https://www.danielcmatt.com/about.html)).
