## Mūla

ज्ञानहीनो गुरुः कर्मी स्वाधिकारं समर्प्य नो ।
दीक्षाद्यधिकृतिं कुर्याद्विना तस्याज्ञया पुनः ॥ ^mula

jñānahīno guruḥ karmī svādhikāraṃ samarpya no |
dīkṣādyadhikṛtiṃ kuryādvinā tasyājñayā punaḥ || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. IX
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 09 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v09 14 - Chapter Twenty-Three#^pg327|p. 307]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 09.pdf#page=327>))
>
> Once the teacher who is (dedicated to the performance of) rituals (karmin), (but is) devoid of knowledge, has transmitted his authority (to his disciple), he should not perform the functions for which he was (previously) authorized again, such as initiate (others), without his (disciple’s) consent. Thus, he should tell him (what he intends to do,) and (the disciple), having bowed (to his teacher), should approve. (with 23.25–26ab)

## Viveka

*Lead-in:* नन्वेवमभिषेकमस्मै दत्त्वा गुरुणा अनन्तरं कथं वर्तितव्यम् ?-- इत्याशङ्क्याह--

> ज्ञानहीनो गुरुः कर्मी स्वाधिकारं समर्प्य नो ।
> दीक्षाद्यधिकृतिं कुर्याद्विना तस्याज्ञया पुनः ॥
> इत्येवं श्रावयेत्सोऽपि नमस्कृत्याभिनन्दयेत् ।

तस्येति--स्वयमभिषिक्तस्य । अयं च श्लोकः क्वचित् "पालयेत्सप्तसत्रकम्"इत्यनन्तरं भ्रमात् लेखकैर्लिखित इति तदुपेक्ष्यम् ॥
