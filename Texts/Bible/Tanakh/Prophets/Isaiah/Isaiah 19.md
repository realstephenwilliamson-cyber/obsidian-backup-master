# Isaiah 19

## Chapter 19

**19:1** The “Egypt” Pronouncement.  
Mounted on a swift cloud,  
The LORD will come to Egypt;  
Egypt’s idols shall tremble before Him,  
And the heart of the Egyptians shall sink within them. ^19-1

**19:2** “I will incite Egyptian against Egyptian:  
They shall war with each other,  
Every man with his fellow,  
City with city  
And kingdom with kingdom.[^1] ^19-2

**19:3** Egypt shall be drained of spirit,  
And I will confound its plans;  
So they will consult the idols and the shades  
And the ghosts and the familiar spirits. ^19-3

**19:4** And I will place the Egyptians  
At the mercy of a harsh master,  
And a ruthless king shall rule them”  
—declares the Sovereign, the LORD of Hosts. ^19-4

**19:5** Water shall fail from the seas,  
Rivers dry up and be parched, ^19-5

**19:6** Channels turn foul as they ebb,  
And Egypt’s canals run dry.  
Reed and rush shall decay, ^19-6

**19:7** And the Nile papyrus by the Nile-side[^2]  
And everything sown by the Nile  
Shall wither, blow away, and vanish. ^19-7

**19:8** The fishermen shall lament;  
All who cast lines in the Nile shall mourn,  
And those who spread nets on the water shall languish. ^19-8

**19:9** The flax workers, too, shall be dismayed,  
Both carders and weavers chagrined.[^3] ^19-9

**19:10** [^4]Her foundations shall be crushed,  
And all who make dams shall be despondent. ^19-10

**19:11** Utter fools are the nobles of Tanis;  
The sagest of Pharaoh’s advisers  
[Have made] absurd predictions.  
How can you say to Pharaoh,  
“I am a scion of sages,  
A scion of Kedemite kings”?[^5] ^19-11

**19:12** Where, indeed, are your sages?  
Let them tell you, let them discover  
What the LORD of Hosts has planned against Egypt. ^19-12

**19:13** The nobles of Tanis have been fools,  
The nobles of Memphis deluded;  
Egypt has been led astray  
By the chiefs of her tribes. ^19-13

**19:14** The LORD has mixed within her  
A spirit of distortion,  
Which shall lead Egypt astray in all her undertakings  
As a vomiting drunkard goes astray; ^19-14

**19:15** Nothing shall be achieved in Egypt  
By either head or tail,  
Palm branch or reed.[^6] ^19-15

**19:16** In that day, the Egyptians shall be like women, trembling and terrified because the LORD of Hosts will raise His hand against them. ^19-16

**19:17** And the land of Judah shall also be the dread of the Egyptians; they shall quake whenever anybody mentions it to them, because of what the LORD of Hosts is planning against them. ^19-17

**19:18** In that day, there shall be several[^7] towns in the land of Egypt speaking the language of Canaan and swearing loyalty to the LORD of Hosts; one[^8] shall be called Town of Heres.[^9] ^19-18

**19:19** In that day, there shall be an altar to the LORD inside the land of Egypt and a pillar to the LORD at its border.[^10] ^19-19

**19:20** They shall serve as a symbol and reminder of the LORD of Hosts in the land of Egypt, so that when [the Egyptians] cry out to the LORD against oppressors, He will send them a savior and champion to deliver them. ^19-20

**19:21** For the LORD will make Himself known to the Egyptians, and the Egyptians shall acknowledge the LORD in that day, and they shall serve [Him] with sacrifice and oblation and shall make vows to the LORD and fulfill them. ^19-21

**19:22** The LORD will first afflict and then heal the Egyptians; when they turn back to the LORD, He will respond to their entreaties and heal them. ^19-22

**19:23** In that day, there shall be a highway from Egypt to Assyria. The Assyrians shall join with the Egyptians and Egyptians with the Assyrians, and then the Egyptians together with the Assyrians shall serve [the LORD]. ^19-23

**19:24** In that day, Israel shall be a third partner with Egypt and Assyria as a blessing[^11] on earth; ^19-24

**19:25** for the LORD of Hosts will bless them, saying, “Blessed be My people Egypt, My handiwork Assyria, and My very own Israel.” ^19-25

[^1]: I.e., the various districts of Egypt, which in Isaiah’s time were governed by hereditary princes.
[^2]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^3]: Meaning of Heb. uncertain.
[^4]: Meaning of verse uncertain; emendation yields “Her drinkers shall be dejected, / And all her brewers despondent.”
[^5]: Or “advisers.” The wisdom of the Kedemites was proverbial; cf. 1 Kings 5.10.
[^6]: I.e., a man of either high or low station; cf. 9.13, 14.
[^7]: Lit. “five.”
[^8]: Or “each one.”
[^9]: Meaning uncertain. Many Heb. mss. read ḥeres, “sun,” which may refer to Heliopolis, i.e., Sun City, in Egypt. Targum’s “Beth Shemesh” (cf. Jer. 43.13) has the same meaning.
[^10]: As a symbol of the Lord’s sovereignty over Egypt.
[^11]: I.e., a standard by which blessing is invoked; cf. Gen. 12.2 with note.
