## Mūla

इत्येवं बहुभेदेयं श्रीखेचर्येव गीयते ।
अन्यास्तदङ्गभूतास्तु पद्माद्या मालिनीमते ॥ ^mula

ityevaṃ bahubhedeyaṃ śrīkhecaryeva gīyate |
anyāstadaṅgabhūtāstu padmādyā mālinīmate || ^iast

## Translation

> [!source] Dyczkowski, vol. XI
> [[Library/Trika/Tantraloka (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka vol 11 (Dyczkowski)/Tantraloka Dyczkowski v11 06 - Chapter Thirty-Two#^pg156|p. 138]] ([pdf](<Library/Trika/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka translation/Dyczkowski - Tantraloka, vol. 11.pdf#page=156>))
>
> There, (in this regard, of all the mudrās,) the main (and most fundamental) one is the venerable Skyfarer (khecarī). (In her) ‘undifferentiated’ form (niṣkalatvena), she is well known to be Deity by nature. In (Her) differentiated form (sākalya) (She is the mudrās called) Triśūlinī (the Trident), Karaṅkinī (the Corpse), Krodhanā (Rage), Bhairavī, Lelihānikā (She Who Destroys by Licking up), Mahāpretā (the Great Ghost), Yogamudrā (the Gesture of Yoga), Jvālinī (the Burning One), Kṣobhinī (the Disturbing One), and Dhruvā (Firmly Fixed). This venerable Skyfarer itself, that is and has many varieties, is praised in this way. (with 32.4–6ab)
>
> Other (mudrās) (described) in the Mālinīmata that are its auxiliaries, such as Padmamudrā, are not described here, because they are many and are (only of) secondary application. (with 32.6cd–7ab)

## Viveka

> अन्यास्तदङ्गभूतास्तु पद्माद्या मालिनीमते ॥
> तासां बहुत्वामुख्यत्वयोगाभ्यां नेह वर्णनम् ।
