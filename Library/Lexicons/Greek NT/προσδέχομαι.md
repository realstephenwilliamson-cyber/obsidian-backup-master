# προσδέχομαι

[[Greek NT Lexicon (MorphGNT)]] · verb

**Dodson:** I expect, welcome, accept — (a) I await, expect, (b) I receive, welcome (originally: to my house), (c) I accept. (Strong's 4327; headword προσδέχομαι)
